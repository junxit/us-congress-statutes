# House Concurrent Resolution 35

> “revenue act of 1938”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed twenty thousand additional copies each of the bill (H. R. 9682) and the report (H. Report No. 1860), current session, entitled “Revenue Act of 1938”, of which fifteen thousand copies shall be for the use of the House document room and five thousand copies shall be for the use of the Senate document room.


*Passed, March 3, 1938.*

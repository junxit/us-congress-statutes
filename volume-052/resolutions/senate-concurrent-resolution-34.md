# Senate Concurrent Resolution 34

> juvenile court, district of columbia

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (H. R. 4276) to amend an Act entitled “An Act to create a Juvenile Court in and for the District of Columbia”, and for other purposes, the Clerk of the House is authorized and directed to renumber the sections beginning with section 26 so that the last section of the bill will be numbered 43, and to make necessary changes in references to sections.


*Passed, May 16, 1938.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 29

> joint meeting

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Monday, the 3d day of January 1938, at 1:30 o’clock postmeridian, for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed, January 3, 1938.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 29

> return of engrossed bill

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the action of the Speaker of the House of Representatives and of the President of the Senate in signing the enrolled bill (H. R. 7158) to except yachts, tugs, towboats, and unrigged vessels from certain provisions of the Act of June 25, 1936, as amended, be, and it is hereby, rescinded; and that the House of Representatives be, and it is hereby, requested to return to the Senate the engrossed bill.


*Passed, April 5, 1938.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 67

> adjournment

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Thursday the 16th day of June 1938, and that when they adjourn on said day they stand adjourned sine die.


*Passed, June 16, 1938.*

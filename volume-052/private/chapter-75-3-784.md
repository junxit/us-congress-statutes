# Chapter 784

> AN ACT For the relief of William E. Rich.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers William E. Rich, who was a member of the United States Army, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowances shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 28, 1938.*

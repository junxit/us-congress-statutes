# Chapter 551

> AN ACT For the relief of Alpha T. Johnson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is authorized and directed to credit the accounts of Alpha T. Johnson, postmaster at Fort Ransom, North Dakota, in the amount of $179.45, representing stamp and postal funds and stamp stock stolen from the Fort Ransom post office on August 18, 1936.

## § 2.

The Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to the said Alpha T. Johnson a sum equal to the total sum of any amounts which have been paid by her to the United States in settlement of such funds and stamp stock.


*Approved, June 20, 1938.*

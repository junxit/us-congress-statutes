# Chapter 112

> AN ACT For the relief of Milton S. Merrill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General is hereby authorized and directed to credit the accounts of Milton S. Merrill, former postmaster at Mount Hebron, California, in the amount of $392, such amount representing surplus postal funds remitted to his Central Accounting Post Office and lost in a mailtruck robbery in San Francisco, California, on April 7, 1924.


*Approved, April 7, 1938.*

# Chapter 731

> AN ACT To extend to Chief Quartermaster Clerk David C. Buscall, United States Marine Corps (retired), the benefits of the Act of May 7, 1932, providing highest World War rank to retired warrant officers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Chief Quarter-master Clerk David C. Buscall, United States Marine Corps (retired), is hereby placed on the retired list of the United States Marine Corps with the rank of captain: Provided further, That no increase in active or retired pay or allowances shall result from the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 25, 1938.*

# Chapter 727

> AN ACT For the relief of David B. Monroe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 1118 of the Revised Statutes, as amended, the Secretary of War is hereby authorized to reenlist in the United States Army David B. Monroe (Army serial number XXXX). now a sergeant, Signal Corps, United States Army, upon the expiration of the present enlistment and upon the expiration of any future enlistment of said David B. Monroe.


*Approved, June 25, 1938.*

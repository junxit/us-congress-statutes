# Chapter 272

> AN ACT Granting a pension to Mary Lord Harrison.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Administrator of Veterans’ Affairs be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Lord Harrison, widow of Benjamin Harrison, late a President of the United States, and pay her a pension at the rate of $5,000 per annum.


*Approved, May 24, 1938.*

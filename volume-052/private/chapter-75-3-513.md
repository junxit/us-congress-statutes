# Chapter 513

> AN ACT For the relief of Adolph Arendt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the order of deportation and warrant of arrest of Adolph Arendt be canceled, and that his entry at the port of Philadelphia as of December 26, 1923, be declared a legal entry.


*Approved, June 16, 1938.*

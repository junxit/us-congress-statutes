# Chapter 581

> AN ACT Granting six months’ pay to Mrs. Vallie M. Current.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of the current appropriation “Pay, Subsistence, and Transportation of Naval Personnel”, to Mrs. Vallie M. Current, mother of the late John R. Chandler, engineman, second-class, United States Navy, an amount equal to six months’ pay at the rate the late John R. Chandler was receiving at the date of his death: Provided, That Mrs. Vallie M. Current shall establish to the satisfaction of the Secretary of the Navy that she was actually dependent upon her late son at the time of his death, and the determination of the fact of such dependency by the Secretary of the Navy shall be final and conclusive upon the accounting officers of the Government.


*Approved, June 22, 1938.*

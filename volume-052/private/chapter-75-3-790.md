# Chapter 790

> AN ACT For the relief of James J. Coyne.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws the alien James J. Coyne, first sergeant, Eleventh Signal Company, United States Army, shall be considered to have been, at Detroit, Michigan, on May 28, 1924, lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence.


*Approved, June 28, 1938.*

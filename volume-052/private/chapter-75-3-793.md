# Chapter 793

> AN ACT For the relief of Isaac Friedlander.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration or immigration laws Isaac Friedlander, husband of a citizen of the United States, shall not be denied an immigration visa, nor denied admission to the United States for permanent residence, because of the fact that he is alleged to have concealed certain material facts in his application for a visa to enter the United States.


*Approved, June 28, 1938.*

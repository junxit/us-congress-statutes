# Chapter 569

> AN ACT For the relief of John B. Jones.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers John B. Jones, who served as a first lieutenant, One Hundred and Forty-third Infantry, United States Army, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged under honorable conditions from the military service of the United States on the 8th day of June 1918: Provided, That no back pay, compensation, benefit, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 22, 1938.*

# Chapter 116

> AN ACT For the relief of G. F. Flanders and J. W. Talbert.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the account of G. F. Flanders, former United States marshal for the southern district of Georgia, in the amount of $87.50, being the amount paid by him to J. W. Talbert for stenographic service in reporting the testimony in the case of United States against Frank Debbs Ware in July 1933, said sum having been disallowed as compensation in excess of the amount allowed by the Act of March 10, 1916 (39 Stat. 120).


*Approved, April 7, 1938.*

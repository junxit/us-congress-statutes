# Chapter 840

> AN ACT For the relief of Harry J. Somerville.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers Harry J. Somerville, who served as a private, Troop K. Eleventh Regiment United States Cavalry, shall be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the United States Army in the rank of private on July 26, 1918: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, benefit, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 29, 1938.*

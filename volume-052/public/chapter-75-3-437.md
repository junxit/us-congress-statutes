# Chapter 437

> AN ACT To amend an Act entitled “An Act extending the homestead laws and providing for right of way for railroads in the District of Alaska, and for other purposes”, approved May 14, 1898 (30 Stat. 409, 414).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 11 of an Act entitled “An Act extending the homestead laws and providing for right of way for railroads in the District of Alaska, and for other purposes”, be, and the same is hereby, amended by adding thereto the following: “and may permit such use by churches, hospitals, and charitable institutions in Alaska for firewood, fencing, buildings, and for domestic purposes”.


*Approved, June 15, 1938.*

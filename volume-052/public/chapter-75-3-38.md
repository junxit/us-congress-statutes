# Chapter 38

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making an additional appropriation for relief purposes for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1938.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That to continue to provide relief, and work relief on useful public projects, as authorized in the Emergency Relief Appropriation Act of 1937, and subject to all the provisions thereof, there is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $250,000,000, which amount shall be added to, and proportionately increase the specified amounts of the limitations prescribed under, the appropriation made in such Act.


*Approved, March 2, 1938.*

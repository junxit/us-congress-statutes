# Chapter 213

> AN ACT To amend the Act of May 27, 1908, authorizing settlement of accounts of deceased officers and enlisted men of the Navy and Marine Corps.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of May 27, 1908 (35 Stat. 373; U. S. C., 1934 edition, title 34, sec. 941), is hereby amended by inserting in line 34, page 373, Thirty-fifth Statutes at Large, after the words “Marine Corps” the words “Coast Guard, and of deceased commissioned officers of the Public Health Service”.


*Approved, May 13, 1938.*

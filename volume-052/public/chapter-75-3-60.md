# Chapter 60

> AN ACT Limiting the duties of the chief clerk and chief inspector of the Health Department of the District of Columbia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That hereafter neither the chief clerk nor the chief inspector of the Health Department of the District of Columbia shall act as a deputy to the health officer of said District.


*Approved, April 2, 1938.*

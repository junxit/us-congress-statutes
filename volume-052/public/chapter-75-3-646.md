# Chapter 646

> AN ACT To extend the time for completing the construction of a bridge across the Mississippi River at or near a point between Cherokee and Osage Streets, Saint Louis, Missouri.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time for completing the construction of a bridge across the Mississippi River, at or near a point between Cherokee and Osage Streets, Saint Louis, Missouri, authorized to be built by H. C. Brenner Realty and Finance Corporation, its successors and assigns, by an Act of Congress approved February 13, 1931, and revived and reenacted by an Act of August 30, 1935, is hereby extended three years from August 30, 1938.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, June 24, 1938.*

# Chapter 25

> AN ACT To amend an Act to provide for the retirement of Justices of the Supreme Court.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act to provide for retirement of Justices of the Supreme Court approved March 1, 1937, be, and is hereby, amended by adding thereto the following:

> ## § 2.
>
> The term ‘judicial circuit’ as used in this Act includes the District of Columbia.”


*Approved, February 11, 1938.*

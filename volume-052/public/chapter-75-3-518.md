# Chapter 518

> JOINT RESOLUTION To prescribe the acreage allotments for wheat for 1939.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 333 of the Agricultural Adjustment Act of 1938, as amended, is amended by adding at the end thereof a sentence to read as follows: “The national acreage allotment for wheat for 1939 shall be not less than fifty-five million acres.”


*Approved, June 20, 1938.*

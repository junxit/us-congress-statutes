# Chapter 520

> JOINT RESOLUTION Providing for the filling of a vacancy in the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution of the class other than Members of Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the vacancy in the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution, of the class other than Members of Congress, caused by the expiration of the term of R. Walton Moore, June 29, 1938, be filled by the appointment of R. Walton Moore, a citizen of Virginia, to succeed himself, for the statutory term of six years.


*Approved, June 20, 1938.*

# Chapter 47

> AN ACT To provide for the appointment of one additional United States district judge for the eastern district of Louisiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized to appoint, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, one additional United States district judge for the eastern district of Louisiana.


*Approved, March 18, 1938.*

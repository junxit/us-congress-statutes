# Chapter 340

> JOINT RESOLUTION For the designation of a street to be known as “Oregon Avenue”, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in honor of the State of Oregon, Daniel Road Northwest, in the District of Columbia, is hereby renamed “Oregon Avenue” and shall hereafter bear the name of “Oregon Avenue”.

## § 2.

The street in the District of Columbia running through squares 132 and 152, known as “Oregon Avenue” prior to the enactment of this joint resolution is hereby renamed “Swann Street” and shall be a part of the street heretofore designated as “Swann Street”.


*Approved, June 11, 1938.*

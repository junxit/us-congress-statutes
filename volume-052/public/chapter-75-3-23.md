# Chapter 23

> AN ACT To provide that residence requirements for judges shall not be held to apply to judges who have retired.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That no provision of law requiring any judge of any court of the United States to reside in any district or circuit shall be held or considered to apply to any such judge after he shall have retired.


*Approved, February 11, 1938.*

# Chapter 283

> AN ACT To amend Public Law Numbered 692, Seventy-fourth Congress, second, session.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That nothing in the Act approved June 19, 1936 (Public, Numbered 692, Seventy-fourth Congress, second session), known as the Robinson-Patman Antidiscrimination Act, shall apply to purchases of their supplies for their own use by schools, colleges, universities, public libraries, churches, hospitals, and charitable institutions not operated for profit.


*Approved, May 26, 1938.*

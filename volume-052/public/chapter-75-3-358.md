# Chapter 358

> AN ACT Amending the Act authorizing the collection and publication of cotton statistics by requiring a record to be kept of bales ginned by counties.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That an Act authorizing the Director of the Census to collect and publish statistics of cotton, approved April 2, 1924, be amended by adding at the end of section 4 thereof the following:

> “It shall also be the duty of every cotton ginner to keep a record of the county or parish in which each bale of cotton ginned by him is grown and to report at the March canvass of each year a segregation of the total number of bales ginned by counties or parishes in which grown.”


*Approved, June 14, 1938.*

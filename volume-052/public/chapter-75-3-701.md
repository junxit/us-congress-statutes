# Chapter 701

> AN ACT To amend the Act entitled “An Act to establish a Civilian Conservation Corps, and for other purposes”, approved June 28, 1937.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 9 of the Act entitled “An Act to establish a Civilian Conservation Corps, and for other purposes”, approved June 28, 1937 (50 Stat. 319), is amended by inserting in the first proviso thereof, after the word “Indians”, the following: “and enrollees in the Territories and insular possessions of the United States”.


*Approved, June 25, 1938.*

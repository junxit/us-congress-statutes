# Chapter 350

> AN ACT To provide for holding terms of the District Court of the United States for the Eastern District of Virginia at Newport News, Virginia.

*Be it enacted by Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That terms of the District Court of the United States for the Eastern District of Virginia shall be held at Newport News, Virginia, on the first Monday in February and the second Monday in July.


*Approved, June 13, 1938.*

# Chapter 133

> AN ACT To amend the Act entitled “An Act authorizing the Secretary of the Treasury to convey to the city of Wilmington, North Carolina, Marine Hospital Reservation”, being chapter 93, United States Statutes at Large, volume 42, part 1, page 1260, approved February 17, 1923.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That chapter 93, United States Statutes at Large, volume 42, part 1, page 1260, approved February 17, 1923, being an Act authorizing the Secretary of the Treasury to convey to the city of Wilmington, North Carolina, Marine Hospital Reservation, be, and the same is hereby, amended by striking out the last twenty-eight words thereof and inserting in lieu thereof the following, to wit: “one hundred and ninety-eight feet south of the south line of Church Street”.


*Approved, April 9, 1938.*

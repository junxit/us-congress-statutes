# Chapter 528

> AN ACT To repeal section 2 of the Act of June 16, 1936, authorizing the appointment of an additional district judge for the eastern district of Pennsylvania.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2 of the Act entitled “An Act authorizing the appointment of an additional district judge for the eastern district of Pennsylvania”, approved June 16, 1936 (49 Stat. 1523), be, and it is hereby, repealed.


*Approved, June 20, 1938.*

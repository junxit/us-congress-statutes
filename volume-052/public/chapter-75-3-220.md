# Chapter 220

> AN ACT To authorize an appropriation for reconstruction at Fort Niagara, New York, to replace loss by fire.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $75,000, or so much thereof as may be necessary, for the purpose of reconstructing and repairing at Fort Niagara, New York, barracks buildings designated as 50 N and 50 S, destroyed by fire March 4, 1938.


*Approved, May 16, 1938.*

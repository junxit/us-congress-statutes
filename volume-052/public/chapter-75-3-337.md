# Chapter 337

> AN ACT To authorize an increase in the basic allotment of enlisted men to the Air Corps within the total enlisted strength provided in appropriations for the Regular Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate. and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 13a of the Act approved June 3, 1916, as amended by the Act approved July 2, 1926 (44 Stat. 780), be, and the same is hereby, amended by striking out the words “sixteen thousand” in line 5 and inserting in lieu thereof the words “twenty-one thousand five hundred”.


*Approved, June 11, 1938.*

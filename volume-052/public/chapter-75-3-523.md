# Chapter 523

> AN ACT For the relief of the State of Georgia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the State of Georgia and Major Leroy Cowart, United States property and disbursing officer for Georgia, are hereby relieved from accountability for certain property belonging to the United States, of the total money value of $4,491.65, which property was loaned to such State for use by the Georgia National Guard and was unavoidably lost or destroyed when issued for emergency relief work made necessary by tornadoes at Cordele, Washington, and Gainesville, Georgia, in April 1936.


*Approved, June 20, 1938.*

# Chapter 273

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making an additional appropriation for grants to States for unemployment compensation administration, Social Security Board, for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1938.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $3,500,000 as an additional amount for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1938, for grants to States for unemployment compensation administration, as authorized in title III of the Social Security Act, approved August 14, 1935, and under the limitations in the appropriation for this purpose in the Independent Offices Appropriation Act, 1938.


*Approved, May 25, 1938.*

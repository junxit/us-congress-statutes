# Private Law 49

> AN ACT For the Relief of Mrs. Ingeborg Ruth Sattler McLaughlin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of the eleventh category of section 3 of the Immigration Act of 1917, as amended, Mrs. Ingeborg Ruth Sattler McLaughlin may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved May 15, 1951.*

# Private Law 373

> AN ACT For the relief of William Lund Main.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the minor child, William Lund Main, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. William H. Main, citizens of the United States.


*Approved October 27, 1951.*

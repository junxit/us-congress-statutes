# Private Law 212

> AN ACT For the Relief of Shizu Fujii and her son, Suenori Fujii.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, Shizu Fujii and her son, Suenori Fujii, the mother and brother, respectively, of United States citizens, may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if they are found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved August 17, 1951.*

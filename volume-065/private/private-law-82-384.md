# Private Law 384

> AN ACT For the relief of Meiko Shindo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of that Act, the minor child, Meiko Shindo, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien minor child of Captain and Mrs. Elmer F. Reavis, citizens of the United States.


*Approved October 29, 1951.*

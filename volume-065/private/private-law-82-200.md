# Private Law 200

> AN ACT For the Relief of Wong Thew Hor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, the provisions of section 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, pertaining to unmarried children under twenty-one years of age of a citizen of the United States, shall be held to be applicable to Wong Thew Hor, minor child of Wong Chung Gong, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 15, 1951.*

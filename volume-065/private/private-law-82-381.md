# Private Law 381

> AN ACT For the relief of Adelheid Wichman (now Adelheid Waitschies).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the minor child Adelheid Wichman (now Adelheid Waitschies) shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien minor child of Mr. and Mrs. Otto Waitschies, citizens of the United States.


*Approved October 29, 1951.*

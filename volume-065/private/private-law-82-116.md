# Private Law 116

> AN ACT For the Relief of Mrs. Johanna Hampton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the eleventh category of section 3 of the Immigration Act of February 5, 1917, as amended, Mrs. Johanna Hampton, of Wiesbaden, Germany, the wife of Mr. Chester L. Hampton, a citizen of the United States, may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved June 29, 1951.*

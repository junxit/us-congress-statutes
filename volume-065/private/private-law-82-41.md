# Private Law 41

> AN ACT For the Relief of Pietro Giannettino.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, Pietro Giannettino shall be held and considered to be the natural-born minor alien child of Mr. Pasquale Giannettino, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved May 14, 1951.*

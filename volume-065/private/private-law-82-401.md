# Private Law 401

> AN ACT For the relief of Ark Ping Jee Nong (Ngon).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, the provisions of section 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, shall be held to be applicable to the alien Ark Ping Jee Nong (Ngon), the minor unmarried child of Jee Tung Nong (Ngon), a citizen of the United States.


*Approved October 30, 1951.*

# Private Law 38

> AN ACT For the Relief of Mrs. Sadako Kawamura Lawton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, which exclude from admission to the United States aliens who are ineligible to citizenship, shall not hereafter apply to Mrs. Sadako Kawamura Lawton, the wife of Earle Evans Lawton, a citizen of the United States and an honorably discharged veteran of World War I.


*Approved May 14, 1951.*

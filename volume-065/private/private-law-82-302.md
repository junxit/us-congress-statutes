# Private Law 302

> AN ACT To provide for the admission of Janet and Daisy Wong to the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration laws, the provisions of section 4 (a) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, shall be held to be applicable in the case of Janet and Daisy Wong, natural-born children of Mrs. Jennie Wong, deceased United States citizen.


*Approved October 10, 1951.*

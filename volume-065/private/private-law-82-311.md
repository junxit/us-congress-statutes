# Private Law 311

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Giulia Di Gaetano Coccia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of the eleventh category of section 3 of the Immigration Act of 1917, as amended, Mrs. Giulia Di Gaetano Coccia may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the immigration laws.


*Approved October 10, 1951.*

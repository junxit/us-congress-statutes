# Private Law 356

> AN ACT For the relief of Dr. Charles Gordon Rennick Sell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Doctor Charles Gordon Rennick Sell, who was born in India of British parents, shall be deemed to have been born in Great Britain.


*Approved October 25, 1951.*

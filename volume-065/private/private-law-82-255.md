# Private Law 255

> AN ACT Authorizing the Secretary of State to continue Hervé J. L’Heureux to serve as Chief of the Visa Division for an additional year commencing September 1, 1951.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of State is authorized to continue the assignment of Hervé J. L’Heureux, a Foreign Service officer, class 1, to serve as Chief of the Visa Division for an additional year commencing September 1, 1951, if deemed to be desirable in the national interest.


*Approved September 13, 1951.*

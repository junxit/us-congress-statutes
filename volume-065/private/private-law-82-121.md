# Private Law 121

> AN ACT For the Relief of Bernard Spielmann.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the pur- poses of section 12 (a) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, Bernard Spielmann shall be deemed to have been born in Ecuador.


*Approved June 29, 1951.*

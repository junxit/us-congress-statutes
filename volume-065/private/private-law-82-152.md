# Private Law 152

> AN ACT For the Relief of Betty Minoru Kawachi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, shall not apply to Betty Minoru Kawachi, the minor child of Mrs. James J. Leatherman, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved July 18, 1951.*

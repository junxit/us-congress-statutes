# Private Law 44

> AN ACT For the Relief of Elena Erbez.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924. as amended, the minor child, Elena Erbez, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Robert Priester, citizens of the United States.


*Approved May 14, 1951.*

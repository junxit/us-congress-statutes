# Private Law 8

> AN ACT For the Relief of Edulji Dinshaw and his sister, Mrs. Bachoo Dinsha Woronzow.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Edulji Dinshaw and his sister, Mrs. Bachoo Dinsha Woronzow, shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fees and head taxes. Upon the granting of permanent residence to such aliens as provided for in this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct two numbers from the appropriate quotas for the first year that such quotas are available.


*Approved March 27, 1951.*

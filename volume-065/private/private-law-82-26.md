# Private Law 26

> AN ACT For the Relief of Marcelle Lecomte.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, Marcelle Lecomte shall be held to have physically resided continuously in the United States for a period of one year following her entry for permanent residence on December 16, 1945.


*Approved May 11, 1951.*

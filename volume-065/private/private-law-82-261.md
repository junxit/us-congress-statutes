# Private Law 261

> AN ACT For the relief of Rosita Anita Navarro and Ramona Alicia Navarro.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, which exclude from admission to the United States aliens who are ineligible to citizenship, shall not hereafter apply to Rosita Anita Navarro and Ramona Alicia Navarro, the minor children of Clara Fusako Takemoto Navarro, a citizen of the United States, and Francisco R. Navarro, a legal resident of the United States.


*Approved September 26, 1951.*

# Private Law 1

> AN ACT For the Relief of Vernon Crudge.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Vernon Crudge, who was born in China of British parents, shall be deemed to have been born in Great Britain.


*Approved March 1, 1951.*

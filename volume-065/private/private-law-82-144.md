# Private Law 144

> AN ACT For the Relief of Mrs. Clara Raffioer Droesse.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstand- ing the provisions of section 401 (e) of the Nationality Act of 1940, as amended, Mrs. Clara Raffloer Droesse shall be held and considered to have retained her United States citizenship.

# Private Law 47

> AN ACT For admission to the United States of Mrs. Margot Kazerski.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of the eleventh category of section 3 of the Immigration Act of 1917, as amended, Mrs. Margot Kazerski, 13 Karls Platz, Ludwigsburg, Germany, wife of serviceman Master Sergeant Frank Kazerski, 47 Sunnyside Avenue, Arlington, Massachusetts, may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved May 15, 1951.*

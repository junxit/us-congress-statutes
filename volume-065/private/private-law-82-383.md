# Private Law 383

> AN ACT For the relief of Leo Kieve.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Leo Kieve, a United States citizen veteran of World War II, shall not be held to have lost United States citizenship under any of the provisions of the Nationality Act of 1940 providing for loss of citizenship through continuous residence in a foreign state: Provided, That the said Leo Kieve returns to the United States for permanent residence within a period of one year following the effective date of this Act.


*Approved October 29, 1951.*

# Private Law 82

> AN ACT For the Relief of Tsung Hsien Hsu.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the minor child, Tsung Hsien Hsu. shall be held and considered to be the natural born alien child of Lieutenant Richard Corsa, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved June 14, 1951.*

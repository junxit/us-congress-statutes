# Private Law 241

> AN ACT For the Relief of Mrs. Tjitske Bandstra Van Der Velde.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provision of the second category of section 3 of the Immigration Act of 1917, as amended, Mrs. Tjitske Bandstra Van Der Velde may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence, if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved September 13, 1951.*

# Private Law 43

> AN ACT For the Relief of Jacquelyn Shelton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding any provision of law excluding from admission to the United States persons of race ineligible to citizenship, Jacquelyn Shelton, a minor child under the care of Staff Sergeant and Mrs. E. H. Shelton, Junior, both citizens of the United States residing temporarily in Japan, shall be held and considered for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, to be the natural-born alien child of the said Staff Sergeant and Mrs. E. H. Shelton, Junior.


*Approved May 14, 1951.*

# Private Law 266

> AN ACT For the relief of Madeleine Quarez.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, Madeleine Quarez, who is presently residing in Belgium and who was born in China of Belgian parents, shall be deemed to have been born in Belgium.


*Approved September 27, 1951.*

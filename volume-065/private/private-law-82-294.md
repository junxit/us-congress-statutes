# Private Law 294

> AN ACT For the relief of David Lee Harrigan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the minor child, David Lee Harrigan, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Thomas Y. Harrigan, citizens of the United States. Notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of the said Act, the said David Lee Harrigan may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if he is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved October 9, 1951.*

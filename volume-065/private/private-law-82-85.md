# Private Law 85

> AN ACT For the Relief of Irmgard Kohler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That solely for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, Irmgard Kohler shall be held and considered to be the minor child of her father, Alois Kohler, a naturalized United States citizen.


*Approved June 14, 1951.*

# Private Law 184

> AN ACT For the Relief of Florence Grace Pond Whitehill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is authorized to appoint Florence Grace Pond Whitehill, former nurse Navy Nurse Corps, an ensign in the Navy Nurse Corps, and place her on the retired list of the officers of the Navy with retired pay equal to 75 per centum of the active duty pay of the grade of ensign with credit for purposes of pay for all service to which she was entitled on February 24, 1929: Provided, That no back pay, allowances, or emoluments shall become due as a result of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved August 1, 1951.*

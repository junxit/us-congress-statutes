# Private Law 50

> AN ACT For the Relief of Sook Kat.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, Sook Kat, minor Chinese orphan residing in Hong Kong, China, shall be held and considered to be the alien natural-born daughter of Huie Mon, of Minneapolis, Minnesota, a natural-born United States citizen, and the provisions of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, pertaining to unmarried children under twenty-one years of age of a citizen of the United States, shall apply to the said Sook Kat.


*Approved May 15, 1951.*

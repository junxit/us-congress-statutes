# Private Law 123

> AN ACT For the Relief of Ethel Martha Quinn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 404 (b) of the Nationality Act of 1940, as amended, Ethel Martha Quinn shall be held and considered to have retained her United States citizenship.


*Approved June 29, 1951.*

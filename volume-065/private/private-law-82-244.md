# Private Law 244

> AN ACT For the Relief of Hoshi Kazuo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, shall not apply to Hoshi Kazuo, half-Japanese minor child in the care of Sergeant John B. Humberd and Mrs. Thelma Humberd, citizens of the United States. For the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the said Hoshi Kazuo shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of the said Sergeant John B. Humberd and the said Mrs. Thelma Humberd.


*Approved September 13, 1951.*

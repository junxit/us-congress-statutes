# Private Law 403

> AN ACT To authorize Rear Admiral Emory D. Stanley, United States Navy, retired, to accept employment with the Government of Peru.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Congress hereby grants its consent to Rear Admiral Emory D. Stanley, Supply Corps, United States Navy, retired, to accept, subject to the approval of the Secretary of the Navy, civil employment with, and compensation therefor from, the Government of the Republic of Peru. Acceptance of this employment and compensation therefor shall not affect the status of Rear Admiral Stanley on the retired list of the Navy or his entitlement to retired pay and other benefits arising therefrom.


*Approved October 30, 1951.*

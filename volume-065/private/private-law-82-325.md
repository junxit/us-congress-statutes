# Private Law 325

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Doris Ellen Young.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of the eighth category of section 3 of the Immigration Act of 1917, as amended, Mrs. Doris Ellen Young may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if sue is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved October 11, 1951.*

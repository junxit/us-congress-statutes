# Private Law 198

> AN ACT For the Relief of Elizabeth Jean Clarke.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, Elizabeth Jean Clarke shall be considered to be the natural-born alien child of Brigadier General and Mrs. Bruce C. Clarke, citizens of the United States.


*Approved August 15, 1951.*

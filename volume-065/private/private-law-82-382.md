# Private Law 382

> AN ACT For the relief of Sharon A. Gates.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the minor child, Sharon A. Gates, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Major and Mrs. William M. Gates, citizens of the United States.


*Approved October 29, 1951.*

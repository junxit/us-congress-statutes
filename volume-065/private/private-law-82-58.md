# Private Law 58

> AN ACT For the Relief of Concetta Santagati Giordano.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of the eleventh category of section 3 of the Immigration Act of 1917, as amended, Concetta Santagati Giordano, of Santo Stefano, Messina, Italy, Italian wife of Joseph Giordano, a citizen of the United States, may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved May 16, 1951.*

# Private Law 321

> AN ACT For the relief of Hanoh Sarapanovschi (also known as Hanoh Charat), Gizela (Gizele) Sarapanovschi (nee Levy) and Philippe Sarapanovschi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Hanoh Sarapanovschi, also known as Hanoh Charat, shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon the payment of the required visa fee and head tax. Upon the granting of permanent residence to Hanoh Sarapanovschi the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the number of displaced persons who shall be granted the status of permanent residence pursuant to section 4 of the Displaced Persons Act, as amended (62 Stat. 1011; 64 Stat. 219; 50 U. S. C. App. 1953).

## § 2.

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Gizela (Gizele) Sarapanovschi (nee Levy) and Philippe Sarapanovschi shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved October 11, 1951.*

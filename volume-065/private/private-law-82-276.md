# Private Law 276

> AN ACT For the relief of Davis Min Lee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, the provisions of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, shall be held to be applicable to the alien, Davis Min Lee, the minor unmarried child of Lee Tung Sing, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved September 27, 1951.*

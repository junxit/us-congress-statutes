# Private Law 220

> AN ACT For the Relief of Cecil Lennox Elliott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, Cecil Lennox Elliott shall not be held to have lost United States citizenship under any of the provisions of the Nationality Act of 1940 providing for loss of citizenship through continuous residence in a foreign state.


*Approved August 22, 1951.*

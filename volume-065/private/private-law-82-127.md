# Private Law 127

> AN ACT For the Relief of Kimi Hatano.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of the immigration laws relating to the exclusion of aliens inadmissible because of race shall not hereafter apply to Kimi Hatano, the Japanese fiancée of Roy B. Erickson, a citizen of the United States and an honorably discharged veteran of World War II, and that the said Kimi Hatano shall be eligible for a visa as a nonimmigrant temporary visitor for a period of three months: Provided, That the administrative authorities find that the said Kimi Hatano is coming to the United States with a bona fide intention of being married to the said Roy B. Erickson, and that she is found otherwise admissible under the immigration laws. In the event the marriage between the above-named parties does not occur within three months after the entry of the said Kimi Hatano, she shall be required to depart from the United States, and upon failure to do so shall be deported in accordance with the provisions of sections 19 and 20 of the Immigration Act of 1917, as amended (U. S. C. title 8, secs. 155 and 156). In the event that the marriage between the above-named parties shall occur within three months after the entry of the said Kimi Hatano, the Attorney General is authorized and directed to record the lawful admission for permanent residence of the said Kimi Hatano as of the date of the payment by her of the required visa fee and head tax.


*Approved June 29, 1951.*

# Private Law 355

> AN ACT For the relief of Emma Burr.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of the eleventh category, section 3, of the Immigration Act of 1917, as amended, Emma Burr may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved October 25, 1951.*

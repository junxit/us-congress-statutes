# Private Law 99

> AN ACT For the Relief of Tomas J. Zafiriadis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Tomas J. Zafiriadis shall be deemed to be a native of Uruguay.


*Approved June 27, 1951.*

# Private Law 375

> AN ACT For the relief of Patricia Ann Eddings.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding any provision of law excluding from admission to the United States persons of race ineligible to citizenship, the alien Patricia Ann Eddings, a minor child under the care of First Lieutenant and Mrs. James C. Eddings, Junior, both citizens of the United States residing temporarily in Japan, shall be held and considered to be the natural- born child of the First Lieutenant and Mrs. James C. Eddings, Junior.


*Approved October 29, 1951.*

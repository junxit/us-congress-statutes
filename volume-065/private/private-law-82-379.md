# Private Law 379

> AN ACT For the relief of Myrtle Harding.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the eleventh category of section 3 of the Immigration Act of 1917, as amended, Myrtle Harding may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved October 29, 1951.*

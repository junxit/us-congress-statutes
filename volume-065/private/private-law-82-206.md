# Private Law 206

> AN ACT For the Relief of Jane and Martha Clark.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of that Act, the minor children, Jane and Martha Clark, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien children of Eric B. Clark, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 16, 1951.*

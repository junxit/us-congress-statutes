# Private Law 326

> AN ACT For the relief of Inooka Kazumi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and not withstanding any provisions of law excluding persons of races ineligible to citizenship from admission to the United States, the minor child, Inooka Kazumi, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Henry Frazer Harris, Junior, citizens of the United States.


*Approved October 12, 1951.*

# Private Law 330

> AN ACT to effect entry of a minor child adopted or to be adopted by a United States citizen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the minor child, Tina Bentsen, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Lloyd M. Bentsen, Junior, citizens of the United States.


*Approved October 15, 1951.*

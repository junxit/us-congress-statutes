# Private Law 34

> AN ACT For the Relief of John Yan Chi Gee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, the provisions of sections 4(a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, shall be held to be applicable to the alien John Yan Chi Gee, the minor, unmarried child of James Kwong Gee, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved May 14, 1951.*

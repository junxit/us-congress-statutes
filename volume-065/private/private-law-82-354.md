# Private Law 354

> AN ACT For the relief of Moy Chin Shee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, Moy Chin Shee, a former resident of the United States and the mother of seven United States citizen children, shall be deemed to be a returning resident under the provisions of section 4 (b) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, if otherwise admissible to the United States.


*Approved October 25, 1951.*

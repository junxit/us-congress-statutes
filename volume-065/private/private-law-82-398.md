# Private Law 398

> AN ACT For the relief of Susa Yukiko Thomason.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, solely for the purposes of section 4 (a) and section 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, and notwithstanding any provisions excluding from admission to the United States persons of races ineligible to citizenship, Susa Yukiko Thomason, a minor Japanese child, shall be considered the alien natural-born child of Henry A. Thomason, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved October 29, 1951.*

# Private Law 132

> AN ACT For the Relief of Chin Yuen Ling, minor unmarried Chinese child of a United States citizen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, the provisions of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, shall be held to be applicable to the alien Chin Yuen Ling, the minor unmarried child of Chin Yick Goon, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved June 30, 1951.*

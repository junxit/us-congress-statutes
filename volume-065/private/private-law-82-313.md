# Private Law 313

> AN ACT For the relief of Shizu Terauchi Parks.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of the immigration laws relating to the exclusion of aliens inadmissible because of race shall not hereafter apply to Shizu Terauchi Parks, the Japanese fiancée of Henry Francis Parks, a citizen of the United States, and that the said Shizu Terauchi Parks shall be eligible for a visa as a nonimmigrant temporary visitor for a period of three months: Provided, That the administrative authorities find that the said Shizu Terauchi Parks is coming to the United States with a bona fide intention of being married to the said Henry Francis Parks, and that she is found otherwise admissible under the immigration laws. In the event the marriage between the above-named parties does not occur within three months after the entry of the said Shizu Terauchi Parks, she shall be required to depart from the United States, and upon failure to do so shall be deported in accordance with the provisions of sections 19 and 20 of the Immigration Act of 1917, as amended (U. S. C. title 8, secs. 155 and 156). In the event that the marriage between the above-named parties shall occur within three months after the entry of the said Shizu Terauchi Parks, the Attorney General is authorized and directed to record the lawful admission for permanent residence of the said Shizu Terauchi Parks as of the date of the payment by her of the required visa fee and head tax.


*Approved October 10, 1951.*

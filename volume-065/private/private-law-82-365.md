# Private Law 365

> AN ACT For the relief of Hans Lank.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Hans Lenk shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act: Provided, That there be given a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, in such amount and containing such conditions as he may prescribe, to the United States and to all States, Territories, counties, towns, municipalities, and districts thereof holding the United States and all States, Territories, counties, towns, municipalities, and districts thereof harmless against Hans Lenk becoming a public charge.


*Approved October 26, 1951.*

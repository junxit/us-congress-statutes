# Private Law 253

> AN ACT For the Relief of Shozo Ichiwawa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, shall not apply to Shozo Ichiwawa, Japanese minor child in the care of Staff Sergeant and Mrs. Robert Yung. For the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the said Shozo Ichiwawa shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of said Staff Sergeant and Mrs. Robert Yung.


*Approved September 13, 1951.*

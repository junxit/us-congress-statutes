# Private Law 299

> AN ACT For the relief of Maiku Suzuki.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding any provisions excluding from admission to the United States persons of races ineligible to citizenship, the minor child, Maiku Suzuki, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Captain and Mrs. Andrew A. Miller, citizens of the United States.


*Approved October 10, 1951.*

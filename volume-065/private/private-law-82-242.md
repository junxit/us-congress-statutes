# Private Law 242

> AN ACT For the Relief of Mrs. Shizuko Yamane.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, Mrs. Shizuko Yamane, the wife of Kanichi John Yamane, a United States citizen, may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved September 13, 1951.*

# Private Law 70

> AN ACT For the Relief of Jose Encarnacion Ortiz.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, Jose Encarnacion Ortiz, an honorably discharged veteran of World War II, now a patient at the Birmingham Veterans Facility, Van Nuys, California, shall be held to be lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fee and head tax. Upon the granting of permanent residence to such alien as provided for in this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the appropriate quota for the first year that such quota is available.


*Approved May 21, 1951.*

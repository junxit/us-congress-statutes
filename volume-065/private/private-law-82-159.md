# Private Law 159

> AN ACT For the Relief of Kay Adel Snedeker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, solely for the purposes of section 4 (a) and section 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, and notwithstanding any provisions excluding from admission to the United States persons of races ineligible to citizenship, Kay Adel Snedeker, a minor Japanese child, shall be considered the alien natural-born child of Charles W. Snedeker and his wife, Amelia Mary Snedeker, citizens of the United States.


*Approved July 27, 1951.*

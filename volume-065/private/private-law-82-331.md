# Private Law 331

> AN ACT For the relief of John R. Willoughby.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the eleventh category of section 3 of the Immigration Act of 1917, as amended, John R. Willoughby may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if he is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved October 16, 1951.*

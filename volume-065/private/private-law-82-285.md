# Private Law 285

> AN ACT For the relief of Helmuth Russow and Volker Harpe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Helmuth Assmas Balthasar Russow and Volker Harpe shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fees and head taxes. Upon the granting of permanent residence to such aliens as provided for in this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct the required numbers from the appropriate quota or quotas for the first year that such quota or quotas are available.


*Approved September 28, 1951.*

# Private Law 100

> AN ACT For the Relief of Aenny Blank.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Aenny Blank, who was born in Turkey of German parents, shall be held and considered to have been born in Germany and the visa application of the above-named Aenny Blank shall be deemed to have been filed under the German quota as of October 1, 1948.


*Approved June 27, 1951.*

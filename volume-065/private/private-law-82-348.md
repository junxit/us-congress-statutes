# Private Law 348

> AN ACT For the relief of Joji Ikeda, a minor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of that Act, the minor child, Joji Ikeda, shall be held and considered the natural-born alien child of Technical Sergeant Albert A. Lund, United States Air Force, AF–19067211, and his wife, Dorothy M. Lund, citizens of the United States.


*Approved October 22, 1951.*

# Private Law 118

> AN ACT For the Relief of Gabriele Gildo Falvo Citrigno.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the minor child, Gabriele Gildo Falvo Citrigno, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Carmen Citrigno, citizen of the United States.


*Approved June 29, 1951.*

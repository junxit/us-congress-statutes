# Private Law 360

> AN ACT For the relief of Rhoda Akiko Nishiyama.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, Rhoda Akiko Nishiyama, the minor daughter of a permanent resident of the United States, may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if otherwise admissible under the immigration laws.


*Approved October 25, 1951.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 83

> ADJOURNMENT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Thursday, March 22, 1951, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Monday, April 2, 1951.


*Passed March 21, 1951.*

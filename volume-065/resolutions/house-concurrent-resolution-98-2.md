# House Concurrent Resolution 98

> PUBLICATION ON COMMUNISM

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the publication of the Committee on Un-American Activities entitled “100 Things You Should Know About Communism Series, including Spotlight on Spies”, be printed as a House document and that there be printed five hundred thousand additional copies, of which three hundred and seventy-five thousand copies shall be for the use of the Committee on Un-American Activities and one hundred and twenty-five thousand copies shall be for the use of the Members of the House of Representatives.


*Passed May 14, 1951.*

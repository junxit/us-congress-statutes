# House Concurrent Resolution 170

> “REVENUE ACT OF 1951”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed thirty-five thousand five hundred additional copies of the public law enacted during the Eighty-second Congress, First Session, entitled “Revenue Act of 1951”, of which three thousand copies shall be for the Senate Document room, thirty thousand copies for the House Document room, one thousand copies for the Senate Committee on Finance, and one thousand five hundred copies for the House Committee on Ways and Means.


*Passed October 20, 1951.*

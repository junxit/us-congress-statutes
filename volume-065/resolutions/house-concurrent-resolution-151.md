# House Concurrent Resolution 151

> ADJOURNMENT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Thursday, August 23, 1951, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian Wednesday, September 12, 1951.


*Passed August 21, 1951.*

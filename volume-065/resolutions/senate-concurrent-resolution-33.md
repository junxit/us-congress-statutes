# Senate Concurrent Resolution 33

> CIVIL AERONAUTICS ACT OF 1938, AMENDMENT

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Secretary of the Senate be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, in the enrollment of the bill [S. 435] to amend the Civil Aeronautics Act of 1938, as amended, and for other purposes, to make the following changes:

On page 2, after line 9, of the engrossed bill, insert the following: “(d) The terms ‘insurance company’ and ‘insurance carrier’ in sections 1305 (a) and (b) and in section 1307 (d) shall include any mutual or stock insurance company, reciprocal insurance association, and any group or association authorized to do an aviation insurance business in any state of the United States.”

On page 6, line 14, of the engrossed bill, strike out the word “of”, where it occurs the first time, and in lieu thereof insert the word “to”.

On page 7, lines 6 and 7, strike out the words “Federal Security Administrator” and in lieu thereof insert the words “Secretary of Labor”.


*Agreed to June 12, 1951.*

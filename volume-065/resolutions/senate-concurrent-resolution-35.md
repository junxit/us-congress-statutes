# Senate Concurrent Resolution 35

> INDEPENDENT OFFICES APPROPRIATION ACT, 1952

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Secretary of the senate be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to reengross the amendments of the Senate to the bill (H. R. 3880) making appropriations for the Executive Office and sundry independent executive bureaus, boards, commissions, corporations, agencies, and offices for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1952, and for other purposes; and to reengross Senate amendment numbered 79 so as to read as follows:

(79) On page 35, line 23, strike out [$875,163,335] and insert: $873,105,770


*Agreed to June 27, 1951.*

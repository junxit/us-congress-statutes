# House Concurrent Resolution 98

> “GUIDE TO SUBVERSIVE ORGANIZATIONS AND PUBLICATIONS”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the publication of the Committee on Un-American Activities entitled “Guide to Subversive Organizations and Publications (and Appendix)” be printed as a House Document, and that sixty-five thou-sand additional copies be printed, of which forty thousand copies shall be for the use of the Committee on Un-American Activities and twenty-five thousand copies shall be for the use of the Members of the House of Representatives.


*Passed May 14, 1951.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 52

> CERTAIN POSTAL EMPLOYEES

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Secretary of the Senate be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, in the enrollment of the bill [S. 355) to adjust the salaries of postmasters, supervisors, and employees in the field service of the Post Office Department, to make the following correction, namely: In the language contained in the conference report on page 2, table of grades and salaries of employees in the automatic grades, under “Examiners of Equipment and Supplies”, strike out “4,470” and insert in lieu thereof “5,470”.


*Agreed to October 19, 1951.*

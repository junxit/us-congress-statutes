# House Concurrent Resolution 1

> JOINT MEETING

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Monday, the 8th day of January 1951, at 1 o’clock postmeridian, for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make.


*Passed January 5, 1951.*

# Public Law 169

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making an appropriation for the Veterans’ Administration for the fiscal year 1952.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1952, the following sum:

## Veterans’ Administration

## servicemen’s indemnities

For payment of liabilities under the Servicemen’s Indemnity Act of 1951, $5,000,000, to remain available until expended.


*Approved October 11, 1951.*

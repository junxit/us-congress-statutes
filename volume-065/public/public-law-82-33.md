# Public Law 33

> JOINT RESOLUTION To provide for continuation of authority for regulation of exports.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 12 of the Export Control Act of 1949 is hereby amended by striking out “1951” and inserting in lieu thereof “1953”.


*Approved May 16, 1951.*

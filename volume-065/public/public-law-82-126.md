# Public Law 126

> AN ACT Authorizing the President of the United States to issue a proclamation designating 1951 as Audubon Centennial Year.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States is hereby authorized to issue a proclamation designating 1951 as Audubon Centennial Year in observance of the one-hundredth anniversary of the death of John James Audubon.


*Approved August 28, 1951.*

# Public Law 82

> AN ACT To limit the retroactive application of the income tax to employees of the United States working in the possessions or in the Canal Zone.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 220 of Revenue Act of 1950 is amended by striking out “effective with respect to taxable years beginning after December 31, 1949” and inserting in lieu thereof “effective with respect to taxable years beginning after December 31, 1950”.


*Approved July 23, 1951.*

# Public Law 161

> AN ACT To amend section 2801 (c) (1) of the Internal Revenue Code.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph (1), subsection (c), section 2801, of the Internal Revenue Code is amended by striking out, wherever they appear, the words “ninety proof” and substituting in lieu thereof the words “eighty proof”.


*Approved October 10, 1951.*

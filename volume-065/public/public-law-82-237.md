# Public Law 237

> AN ACT To amend section 10 of the Flood Control Act of 1946.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subparagraph (q) under the subtitle “Lower Mississippi River” in section 10 of the Flood Control Act of 1946 (Public Law 526, Seventy-ninth Congress), is hereby amended by inserting after the words “Saint Francis River Basin” a comma and the words “the White River Backwater Area”.


*Approved October 30, 1951.*

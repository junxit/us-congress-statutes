# Public Law 80

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making additional appropriations for disaster relief for the fiscal year 1952, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1952, the following sum:

## Disaster Relief

For an additional amount for “Disaster relief”, $25,000,000, to be expended without regard to the limitation in section 8 of the Act of September 30, 1950 (Public Law 875).


*Approved July 18, 1951.*

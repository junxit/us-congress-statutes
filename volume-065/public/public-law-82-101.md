# Public Law 101

> AN ACT To amend the existing law to provide the privilege of renewing expiring five-year level-premium-term policies of United States Government life insurance.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second proviso of the first paragraph of section 301 of the World War Veterans’ Act, 1924, as amended, is hereby amended to read as follows: “Provided further, That at the expiration of any five-year period a five-year level-premium-term policy may be renewed for a successive five-year period at the premium rate for the attained age without medical examination.”


*Approved August 2, 1951.*

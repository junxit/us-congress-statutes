# Public Law 19

> AN ACT To authorize the Secretary of Agriculture to convey certain lands in Ogden, Utah, to the Ogden Chamber of Commerce.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of Agriculture be authorized and directed to convey by quitclaim deed to the Ogden Chamber of Commerce, Ogden, Utah, all rights, titles, and interests of the United States in and to lots 1 to 48, inclusive, in block 7, Fairmount Park Annex Addition to Ogden City, Weber County, State of Utah.


*Approved April 18, 1951.*

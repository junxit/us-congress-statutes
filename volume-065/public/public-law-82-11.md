# Public Law 11

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making additional appropriations for the District of Columbia for the fiscal year 1951, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury to the credit of the general fund of the District of Columbia not otherwise appropriated, for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1951, the following sum:

## office of administrator of rent control

For an additional amount for “Office of Administrator of Rent Control”, $21,250: Provided, That the limitation of $34,000 for payment of terminal leave only, in the appropriation of $113,100 for necessary expenses for “Office of Administrator of Rent Control”, contained in the Supplemental Appropriation Act, 1951 (Public Law 843, 81st Congress), is hereby repealed.


*Approved March 24, 1951.*

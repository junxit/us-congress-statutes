# Public Law 156

> JOINT RESOLUTION Amending an Act making temporary appropriations for the fiscal year 1952, and for other purposes.

*Resolved, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That clause (c) of section 4 of the joint resolution of July 1, 1951 (Public Law 70), as amended, is hereby amended by striking out “September 30, 1951” and inserting in lieu thereof “October 31, 1951”.


*Approved September 28, 1951.*

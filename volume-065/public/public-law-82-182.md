# Public Law 182

> AN ACT To amend the Migratory Bird Hunting Stamp Act of March 16, 1934 (48 Stat. 451; 16 U. S. C. 718d), as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (a) of section 4 of the Migratory Bird Hunting Stamp Act of March 16, 1934 (48 Stat. 451; 16 U. S. C. 718d), as amended is further amended by deleting the number “90” and inserting in lieu thereof the number “85” and subsection (b) of said section 4 is amended by inserting the words “in enforcing and” immediately after the words “The remainder shall be available for expenses” as they appear therein.


*Approved October 20, 1951.*

# Public Law 92

> AN ACT To correct an error In section 1 of the Act of June 28, 1947, “to stimulate volunteer enlistments in the Regular Military Establishment of the United States.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1 of the Act of June 28, 1947 (61 Stat. 191), is amended by deleting the words “last paragraph of section 127a of this Act” and inserting in lieu thereof the words “last paragraph of section 127a of the National Defense Act, as amended (10 U. S. C. 634)”.


*Approved July 30, 1951.*

# Public Law 125

> AN ACT Relating to the time for publication of the Official Register of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2 of the Act entitled “An Act to provide for the compiling and publishing of the Official Register of the United States”, approved August 28, 1935 (49 Stat. 957; 5 U. S. C. 654), is amended by striking out “as early as practicable after the first of June”, and inserting in lieu thereof “on or before December 31”.


*Approved August 27, 1951.*

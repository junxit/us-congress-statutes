# Public Law 244

> JOINT RESOLUTION Fixing the date of the meeting of the second regular session of the Eighty-second Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second regular session of the Eighty-second Congress shall begin at noon on Tuesday, January 8, 1952.


*Approved October 30, 1951.*

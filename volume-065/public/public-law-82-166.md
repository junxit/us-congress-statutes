# Public Law 166

> AN ACT Amending section 437 (c) of the Internal Revenue Code.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 437 (c) of the Internal Revenue Code is amended by striking out “(other than mutual and other than life or marine)” and inserting in lieu thereof “taxable under the provisions of section 204”.

## § 2.

The amendment made by section 1 shall have the same effect as if it had been a part of the said section 437 (c) on January 3, 1951.


*Approved October 10, 1951.*

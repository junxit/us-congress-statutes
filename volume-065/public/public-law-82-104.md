# Public Law 104

> AN ACT To amend subsection 602 (f) of the National Service Life Insurance Act of 1940, as amended, to authorize renewals of level premium term Insurance for successive five-year periods.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first proviso of subsection (f) of section 602 of the National Service Life Insurance Act of 1940, as amended, is hereby amended to read as follows: “Provided, That at the expiration of any term period any national service life insurance policy which has not been exchanged or converted to a permanent plan of insurance, may be renewed as level premium term insurance for a successive period of five years at the premium rate for the then attained age without medical examination, provided the required premiums are tendered prior to the expiration of such term”.


*Approved August 2, 1951.*

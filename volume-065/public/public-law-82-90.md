# Public Law

> JOINT RESOLUTION To provide that an aircraft carrier shall he named the Forrestal.

*Resolved by the Senate and House Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That when and if the United States completes construction of the aircraft carrier known as the United States, the construction of which was discontinued on April 23, 1949, or the aircraft carrier authorized in Public Law 3, Eighty-second Congress, first session, it shall be named the Forrestal.


*Approved July 30, 1951.*

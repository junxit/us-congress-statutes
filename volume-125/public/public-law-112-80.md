# Public Law 112–80

> An Act To amend title 39, United States Code, to extend the authority of the United States Postal Service to issue a semipostal to raise funds for breast cancer research.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. EXTENSION OF POSTAGE STAMP FOR BREAST CANCER RESEARCH.

Section 414(h) of title 39, United States Code, is amended by striking “2011” and inserting “2015”.


*Approved December 23, 2011.*

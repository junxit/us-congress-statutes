# Public Law 112–67

> Joint Resolution Making further continuing appropriations for fiscal year 2012, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Continuing Appropriations Act, 2012 (Public Law 112–36) is further amended by striking the date specified in section 106(3) and inserting “December 17, 2011”.


*Approved December 16, 2011.*

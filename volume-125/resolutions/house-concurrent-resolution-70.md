# House Concurrent Resolution 70

> ENROLLMENT CORRECTIONS—S. 365

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That in the enrollment of S. 365, the Secretary of the Senate shall amend the title so as to read: “An Act to provide for budget control.”.


*Agreed to August 2, 2011.*

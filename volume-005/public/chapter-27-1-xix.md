# Chapter XIX

> An Act authorizing the transmission of letters and packets to and from Mrs. Harrison, free of postage.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all letters and packets, carried by post to and from Mrs. Harrison, relict of the late William Henry Harrison, be conveyed free of postage during her natural life.


*Approved, September 9, 1841.*

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the above routes shall go into operation on the first day of July, eighteen hundred and forty-five, or sooner, should the funds of the department justify the same: Provided, That as soon as a responsible, contractor shall offer to transport the mails over any portion of the routes included in this bill, or in that approved on the thirty-first of August, eighteen hundred and forty-two, entitled “An act establishing certain post roads,” for the revenue derived from the new offices to be established thereon, the Postmaster General shall have the power forthwith to put them into operation.


*Approved, March 3, 1845.*

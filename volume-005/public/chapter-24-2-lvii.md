# Chapter LVII

> An Act making a further appropriation for suppressing Indian hostilities in Florida.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of one million of dollars be, and the same is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, in addition to former appropriations, for suppressing Indian hostilities in Florida, and that die same shall be expended in the manner already provided for by law.


*Approved, April 29, 1836.*

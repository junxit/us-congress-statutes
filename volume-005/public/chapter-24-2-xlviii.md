# Chapter XLVIII

> An Act to suspend the operation of the second proviso, third section of “An act making appropriations for the civil and diplomatic expenses of Government for the year one thousand eight hundred and thirty-five.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That so much of the third section of the act, entitled “An act making appropriations for the civil and diplomatic expenses of Government for the year 1835,” as provides “that the whole number of custom-house officers in the United States, on the 1st of January, 1834, shall not be increased until otherwise allowed by Congress,” be, and the same is hereby suspended to the end of the present session of Congress.


*Approved, April 9, 1836.*

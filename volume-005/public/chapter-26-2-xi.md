# Chapter XI

> An Act to make further provision for the expenses of an exploration and survey of that part of the northeastern boundary line of the United States which separates the States of Maine and New Hampshire from the British provinces.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of seventy-five thousand dollars, in addition to the sum heretofore provided, be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to enable the President of the United States to cause to be made and completed an exploration and survey of that part of the northeastern boundary line of the United States and the adjacent country, which separates the States of Maine and New Hampshire from the British provinces.


*Approved, February 27, 1841.*

# Chapter XXXVII

> An Act to continue in force for a limited time the act entitled “An act to carry into effect a convention between the United States and Spain.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of the act, entitled “An act to carry into effect a convention between the United States and Spain,” approved on the seventh day of June, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-six, be, and the same are hereby, extended until the first day of February, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-eight; and the commissioner appointed under the said act, shall, on or before the said day, terminate his duties.


*Approved, March 3, 1837.*

# Chapter CCLXIV

> An Act authorizing the printing of the Madison papers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Joint Committee on the Library be authorized to cause the Madison papers to be printed and published; and that a sum not exceeding five thousand dol- lars be appropriated for that purpose out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, July 9, 1838.*

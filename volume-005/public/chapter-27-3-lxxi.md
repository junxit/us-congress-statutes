# Chapter LXXI

> An Act declaring Robbinston in the State of Maine, to be a port of delivery.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the town of Robbinston, in the State of Maine, shall be a port of delivery, to be annexed to the district of Passamaquoddy, and shall be subject to the same regulations as other ports of delivery in the United States.


*Approved, March 3, 1843.*

# Chapter LX

> An Act making appropriations for certain improvements on the western shore of Lake Michigan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of twelve thousand five hundred dollars be, and is hereby appropriated, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the construction of a harbor at the town of Southport, in the Territory of Wisconsin.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the corps of topographical engineers shall select, from actual examination and survey, the point of location of said harbor.

## § 3.

And be it further enacted, That the money hereby appropriated shall be expended under the direction of the War Department.


*Approved, June 15, 1844.*

# Chapter XLVI

> An Act making a further appropriation for the suppression of Indian hostilities in Florida.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of five hundred thousand dollars be, and the same is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, in addition to former appropriations, for suppressing Indian hostilities in Florida.


*Approved, April 1, 1836.*

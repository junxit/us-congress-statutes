# Chapter III

> An Act to amend the act establishing a district court of the United States at Wheeling, Virginia.(a)

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That hereafter two annual terms of the district court for the western district of Virginia be holden at the city of Wheeling, commencing on the twenty-fifth March and the twenty-fifth of October, in lieu of the one term of the said district court now directed to be held at Wheeling.


*Approved, January 20, 1843.*

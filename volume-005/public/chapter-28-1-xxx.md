# Chapter XXX

> An Act relating to the port of entry in the district of Passamaquoddy, in the State of Maine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the port of entry and delivery which now is, or hereafter may be, constituted by virtue of the fourth section of the act approved March third, one thousand eight hundred and three, shall, while the same shall remain such, be also a port of entry for ships and vessels and their cargoes, arriving from the Cape of Good Hope and from places beyond the same.


*Approved, May 31, 1844.*

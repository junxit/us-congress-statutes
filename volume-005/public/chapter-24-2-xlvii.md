# Chapter XLVII

> An Act amendatory of “the act for the relief of the sufferers by fire in the city of New York,” passed March 19th, 1836.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the operation of the act entitled “An act for the relief of the sufferers by fire in the city of New York,” passed the nineteenth day of March last past, shall be, and hereby is limited and confined exclusively to such bonds of the description set forth in said act, as were made and entered into at the custom-house in the city of New York prior to the sixteenth day of December last past.


*Approved, April 5, 1836.*

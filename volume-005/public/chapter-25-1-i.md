# Chapter I

> An Act to postpone the fourth instalment of deposite with the States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the transfer of the fourth instalment of deposites directed to be made with the States, under the thirteenth section of the act of June twenty-third, eighteen hundred and thirty-six, be and the same is hereby postponed till the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-nine; Provided, That the three first instalments under the said act shall remain on deposite with the States, until otherwise directed by Congress.


*Approved, October 2, 1837.*

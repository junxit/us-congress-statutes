# Chapter CXVIII

> An Act to remove the Land Office from Clinton to Jackson, in the State of Mississippi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Land Office at present established at Clinton in the State of Mississippi be hereafter kept at Jackson, in the same State.


*Approved, June 23, 1836.*

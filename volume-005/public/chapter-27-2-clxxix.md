# Chapter CLXXIX

> An Act to change the name of the part of entry on Lake Erie, known as Portland, to that of Sandusky.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the first day of October, in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and forty-two, the port of Portland on Lake Erie, in the district of Sandusky, in the State of Ohio, shall be called the port of Sandusky.


*Approved, August 16, 1842.*

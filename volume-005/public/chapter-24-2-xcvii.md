# Chapter XCVII

> An Act repealing the fourteenth section of the “Act to incorporate the subscribers to the Bank of the United States,” approved, April tenth, eighteen hundred and sixteen.(a)

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the fourteenth section of the act entitled “An act to incorporate the subscribers to the Bank of the United States,” approved April tenth, eighteen hundred and six-teen, shall be, and the same is hereby, repealed.


*Approved, June 15, 1836.*

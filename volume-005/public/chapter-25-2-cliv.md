# Chapter CLIV

> An Act to extend the charter of the Bank of Alexandria, in the city of Alexandria.(a)

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That so much of the act entitled “An act to extend the charters of the Bank of Columbia, in Georgetown, and the Bank of Alexandria, in the city of Alexandria,” approved the twenty-fifth February, eighteen hundred and thirty-six, as relates to the Bank of Alexandria in the city of Alexandria, be, and the same is hereby, extended in its duration to the fourth of March, eighteen hundred and forty-one.


*Approved, July 5, 1838.*

# Resolution 12

> A Resolution to continue two clerks in the business of reservations and grants under Indian treaties.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the authority given to the Secretary of War by the joint resolution, approved May eighteenth, one thousand eight hundred and forty-two, to continue for two years the employment of two clerks in the business of reservations and grants under Indian treaties, is hereby extended and continued till otherwise directed by Congress.


*Approved, June 12, 1844.*

# Resolution 5

> A Resolution to disapprove and disaffirm an act of the Legislative Council of the Wisconsin Territory chartering a bank.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That Congress do, hereby, dissent from, disapprove, and disaffirm the act of the Legislative Council of Wisconsin, entitled “An act to incorporate the stockholders of the State Bank of Wisconsin at Prairie du Chien,” a copy of which said act has, during the present session of Congress, been presented for its action, and for confirmation or disapproval; and the said act of the Legislative Council of the said Territory is hereby declared to be null and void, and to have no force or effect whatsoever as a law of the said Territory.


*Approved, June 12, 1838.*

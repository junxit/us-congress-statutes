# Resolution 2

> Joint Resolution of respect for the memory of the donor of the Camp Chest of General Washington.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Senate and House of Representatives take pleasure in recognising to the widow and family of the late William Sydney Winder, their high sense of the value of the bequest contained in his will, and in expressing their respect for the memory of the donor.


*Approved, April 30, 1844.*

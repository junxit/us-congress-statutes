# Resolution 2

> Resolution to establish certain post roads in Missouri and Arkansas.

*Be it Resolved, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby authorized to establish the following post roads; From Fort Towson, in the Territory of Arkansas, to Fort Gibson, and from Fort Gibson, by Fayette, in Arkansas Territory, Barry court-house, Van Buren court-house, Jackson court-house, Fort Leavenworth, Liberty, in Clay county, Plattsburgh, in Clinton county, Fort Des Moines, to the town of Dubuque on the Mississippi river. And the same shall be continued until otherwise provided for by law.


*Approved, March 19, 1836.*

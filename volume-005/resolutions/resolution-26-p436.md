# Resolution

> Joint Resolution to present incorporated universities, colleges, &c., with copies of the catalogue of the Library of Congress.

*Resolved, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That one copy of the catalogue of the Library of Congress be presented to each of the incorporated universities, colleges, athenaeums, and historical societies in the United States, not exceeding three hundred in number, and to the American Antiquarian Society.


*Approved, January 14, 1841.*

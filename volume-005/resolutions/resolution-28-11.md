# Resolution 11

> A Resolution suspending the joint resolution providing for the printing of additional copies of the journals and public documents.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the resolution “providing for the printing of additional copies of the journals and public documents,” shall be suspended and take effect only from the commencement of the next session of Congress.


*Approved, June 12, 1844.*

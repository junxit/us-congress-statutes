# Public Resolution 6

> Joint Resolution for continuing an additional clerk in the Second Auditors office.

*Resolved, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provision contained in the act of twenty-sixth August, one thousand eight hundred and forty-two, “legalizing and making appropriations for such necessary objects as have been usually included in the general appropriation bills without other authority of law, and for other purposes,” for one additional clerk in the Second Auditor’s office at one thousand dollars, be, and the same is, continued until the thirtieth day of June, one thousand eight hundred and forty-four.


*Approved, March 3, 1843.*

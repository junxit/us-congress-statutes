# Chapter XCIX

> An Act allowing further time for entering donation rights to lands in the district of Detroit.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the claimants to certain donation rights to land in the district of Detroit, granted by the second [section] of an act, entitled “An act to authorize the granting of patents for land, according to the surveys that have been made, and to grant donation rights to certain claimants of land in the district of Detroit, and for other purposes,” passed the twenty-third of April, one thousand eight hundred and twelve, be, and they are hereby, allowed until the first day of December, one thousand eight hundred and eighteen, to file their claims with the register of the land office, for the district aforesaid.


*Approved, March 3, 1817.*

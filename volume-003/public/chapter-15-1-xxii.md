# Chapter XXII

> An Act for altering the time for holding the district court for the district of Virginia.(a)

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the terms of the district court for the district of Virginia, which are now directed by law to be holden on the twelfth day of April, in each year, shall hereafter be holden, for the said district, on the second day of April, in each year, except where such day shall occur on Sunday, when the term of the said court shall commence and be holden on the next succeeding day.


*Approved, March 19, 1818.*

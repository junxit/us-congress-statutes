# Chapter XXIX

> An Act making an appropriation for finishing the Senate Chamber and repairing the roof of the north wing of the Capitol.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a sum not exceeding nine thousand five hundred dollars be, and the same is hereby appropriated, to be applied under the direction of the President of the United States, to finishing the Senate Chamber, and repairing the roof of the north wing of the Capitol; which sum shall be paid out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, July 26, 1813.*

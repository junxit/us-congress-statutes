# Chapter CI

> An Act fixing the time for the next meeting of Congress.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That after the adjournment of the present session, the next meeting of Congress shall be on the second Monday of November next.


*Approved, May 13, 1820.*

# Chapter XL

> An Act to amend an act laying duties on licenses to retailers of wines, spirituous liquors, and foreign merchandise.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That nothing contained in the first section of the act laying duties on licenses to retailers of wines, spirituous liquors, and foreign merchandise, shall be construed to extend to vine dressers who sell at the place where the same is made, wine of their own growth, nor shall any vine dresser for vending solely at the place where the same is made, wine of his own growth, be compelled to take out license as a retailer of wine.


*Approved, February 8, 1815.*

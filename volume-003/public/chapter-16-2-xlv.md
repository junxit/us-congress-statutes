# Chapter XLV

> An Act to continue in force, for a further time, the act, entitled “An act for establishing trading-houses with the Indian tribes.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the act, entitled “An act for establishing trading-houses with the Indian tribes,” passed on the second day of March, one thousand eight hundred and eleven, and which was, by subsequent acts, continued in force until the first day of March, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-one, shall be, and the same is hereby, further continued in force until the third day of June, one thou sand eight hundred and twenty-two, and no longer.


*Approved, March 3, 1821.*

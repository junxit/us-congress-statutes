# Chapter LXXV

> An Act further to regulate the medical department of the army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the apothecary general and assistant apothecaries general, shall severally give bonds to the United States, with good and sufficient security, for the faithful performance of their duties, in such sums as shall be required by the Surgeon General of the Army, under the direction of the War Department.


*Approved, May 8, 1820.*

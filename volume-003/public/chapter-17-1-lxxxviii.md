# Chapter LXXXVIII

> An Act to repeal the fourteenth section of “An act to reduce and fix the military peace establishment,” passed the second day of March, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-one.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the fourteenth section of the act, entitled “An act to reduce and fix the military peace establishment,” passed the second day of March, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-one, be, and the same is hereby, repealed.


*Approved, May 7, 1822.*

# Chapter LXXXII

> An Act concerning the naval establishment.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That in addition to the sums heretofore appropriated for that purpose, the sum of two hundred thousand dollars be, and the same is hereby appropriated annually for three years, towards the purchase and supply of a stock of every description of timber, required for ship building, and other naval purposes, to be paid out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 3, 1815.*

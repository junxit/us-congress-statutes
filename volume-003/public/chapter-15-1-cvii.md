# Chapter CVII

> An Act to continue in force, from and after the thirtieth of June, one thousand eight hundred and nineteen, until the thirtieth of June, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-six, the fourth paragraph of the first section of the act, entitled “An act to regulate the duties on imports and tonnage.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the fourth paragraph of the first section of the act, entitled “An act to regulate the duties on imports and tonnage,” passed the twenty-seventh of April, one thousand eight hundred and sixteen, shall, from and after the thirtieth of June, one thousand eight hundred and nineteen, continue to operate in the same manner, and to have the same effect, until the thirtieth of June, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-six, that the above mentioned fourth paragraph now has, and will continue to have, until the thirtieth of June, one thousand eight hundred and nineteen, any law to the contrary notwithstanding.


*Approved, April 20, 1818.*

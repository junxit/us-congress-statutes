# Chapter LXIV

> An Act to repeal certain acts therein mentioned.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the act, entitled “An act to authorize the President of the United States to accept the services of state troops and of volunteers,” a nd the act entitled “An act to authorize the raising a corps of sea fencibles,” be, and the same are hereby repealed.


*Approved, February 27, 1815.*

# Chapter V

> An Act to continue the present mode of supplying the army of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the seventh, eighth, ninth and tenth sections of the act, entitled “An act regulating the staff of the army,” passed April fourteenth, eighteen hundred and eighteen, be, and the same are hereby, continued in force for the term of five years, and until the end of the next session of Congress thereafter.


*Approved, January 23, 1823.*

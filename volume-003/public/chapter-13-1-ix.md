# Chapter IX

> An Act freeing from postage all letters and packets to and from the superintendent general of military supplies.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all letters and packets to and from the superintendent general of military supplies, which relate to his official duties, shall be free from postage.


*Approved, July 13, 1813.*

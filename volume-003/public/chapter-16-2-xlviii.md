# Chapter XLVIII

> An Act to establish a part of entry in the district of Sandusky, in the state of Ohio, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That, from and after the first day of May next, the town of Portland in the district of Sandusky, in the state of Ohio, shall be the port of entry for that district; and that from and after that time the present port of entry established at Danbury shall cease to be the port of entry for said district.


*Approved, March 3, 1821.*

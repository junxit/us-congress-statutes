# Chapter XV

> An Act to continue in force an act to raise ten additional companies of rangers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the act, entitled “An act to raise ten additional companies of rangers,” passed the twenty-fifth day of February, one thousand eight hundred and thirteen, be and the same is hereby continued in force for one year from and after the date of the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 24, 1814.*

# Chapter CV

> An Act to continue in force an act, entitled “An act relating to settlers on lands of the United States.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That an act, entitled “An act relating to settlers on the lands of the United States,” passed the twenty-fifth of March, one thousand eight hundred and sixteen, be, and the same is hereby, continued in force for one year from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1817.*

# Chapter CLXX

> An Act fixing the compensation of the chaplains of Congress.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the chaplains of Congress shall be allowed and paid five hundred dollars per annum, each, as a compensation for their services, to commence with the present session of Congress, any law to the contrary notwithstanding.


*Approved, April 30, 1816.*

# Chapter XXXI

> An Act fixing the time for the next meeting of Congress.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That after the adjournment of the present session, the next meeting of Congress shall be on the first Monday in December next.


*Approved, July 27, 1813.*

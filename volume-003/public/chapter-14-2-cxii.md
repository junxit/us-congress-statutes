# Chapter CXII

> An Act for the relief of certain officers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the paymaster general be authorized, and is hereby required, to pay the general staff (the volunteer aids excepted) of the governor of the Illinois territory, while in service in the year one thousand eight hundred and twelve; and that to each one be allowed the pay and emoluments of a major of infantry.


*Approved, March 3, 1817.*

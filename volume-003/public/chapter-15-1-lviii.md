# Chapter LVIII

> An Act to repeal part of the act, entitled “An act to provide for surveying the coasts of the United States.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That so much of the third section of the act, passed the tenth day of February, one thousand eight hundred and seven, entitled “An act to provide for surveying the coasts of the United States,” as authorizes the employment of other persons in the execution of said act, than the persons belonging to the army and navy, be, and the same is hereby, repealed.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That all instruments and property of the United States, and all surveys, drafts, notes, charts, maps, and documents, in any wise belonging to the survey of the coasts, be deposited in such place as the President of the United States shall direct.


*Approved, April 14, 1818.*

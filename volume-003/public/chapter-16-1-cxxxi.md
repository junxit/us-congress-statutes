# Chapter CXXXI

> An Act to continue in force the act, entitled “An act to provide for reports of the decisions of the Supreme Court,” approved the third of March, one thousand eight hundred and seventeen.(a)

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the act, entitled “An act to provide for reports of decisions of the Supreme Court,” approved the third day of March, one thousand eight hundred and seventeen, be, and the same is hereby, continued in force for three years and no longer.


*Approved, May 15, 1820.*

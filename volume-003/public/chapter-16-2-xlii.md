# Chapter XLII

> An Act to extend the time for unlading vessels arriving from foreign ports, in certain cases.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That when the capacity of any vessel, arriving with a cargo from a foreign port, shall exceed three hundred tons, the term for unlading such vessel shall hereafter be twenty days from the report of arrival, Sundays excepted.


*Approved, March 3, 1821.*

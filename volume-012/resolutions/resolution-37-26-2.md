# Resolution 26

> A Resolution to facilitate the Payment of side and wounded Soldiers in the Hospitals and Convalescent Camps.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the paymaster-general be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to take immediate measures for the prompt payment of the sick and wounded soldiers in the convalescent camps, hospitals, and elsewhere, so that they may be fully paid within sixty days from and after the passage hereof.


*Approved, March 3, 1863.*

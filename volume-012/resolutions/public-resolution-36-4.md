# Public Resolution 4

> A Joint Resolution for the Relief of the Contractors of the Post-Office Department.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the accruing revenues of the Post-Office Department be, and the same are hereby, appropriated, in part payment of contractors for carrying the mails, and of route agents, during the quarter ending thirty-first March, eighteen hundred and sixty, under the provisions of the act of July second, eighteen hundred and thirty-six.


*Approved, March 28, 1860.*

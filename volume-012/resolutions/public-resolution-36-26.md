# Public Resolution 26

> A Resolution explanatory of the eighth Section of the Act of Congress approved February twenty-eighth, eighteen hundred and fifty-nine.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the repeal of [by] the eighth section of the act of Congress, approved the twenty-eighth day of February eighteen hundred and fifty-nine, of so much of the act of Congress entitled “An act to regulate trade and intercourse with Indian tribes, and to preserve peace on the frontiers,” approved June thirtieth eighteen hundred and thirty-four, as provides that the United States shall make indemnification out of the treasury for property taken or destroyed in certain cases by Indians trespassing on white men, as described in said act, shall not be construed to destroy or impair any right to indemnity which existed at the date of said repeal.


*Approved, June 25, 1860.*

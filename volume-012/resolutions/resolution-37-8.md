# Resolution 8

> A Resolution for Payment of the Expenses of the Joint Committee of Congress appointed to inquire into the Conduct of the War.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of ten thousand dollars, or so much thereof as may be necessary, be and the same is hereby appropriated, to pay the expenses of the Joint Committee of Congress, appointed to inquire into the conduct of the present war; and that said sum shall be drawn from the Treasury, upon the order of the Secretary of the Senate, as the same shall be required from time to time by the committee having such investigation in charge.


*Approved, January 27, 1862.*

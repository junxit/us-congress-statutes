# Resolution 2

> A Resolution relative to the Exhibition of the Industry of all Nations to be holden in London in the year eighteen, hundred and sixty-two.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he hereby is, authorized to take such measures as shall to him seem best to facilitate a proper representation of the industrial interests of the United States at the exhibition of the industry of all nations to be holden at London in the year eighteen hundred and sixty-two; and the sum of two thousand dollars is hereby appropriated for the incidental expenses thereof.


*Approved, July 27, 1861.*

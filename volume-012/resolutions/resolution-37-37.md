# Resolution 37

> A Resolution to encourage Enlistments in the Regular Army and Volunteer Forces.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That so much of the ninth section of the act approved August third, eighteen hundred and sixty-one, entitled, “An act [providing] for the better organization of the military establishment,” as abolishes the premium paid for bringing accepted recruits to the rendezvous, be and the same is hereby repealed, and hereafter a premium of two dollars shall be paid to any citizen, non-commissioned officer or soldier for such accepted recruit for the regular army he may bring to the rendezvous. And every soldier who hereafter enlists, either in the regular army or the volunteers, for three years or during the war, may receive his first month’s pay in advance, upon the mustering of his company into the service of the United States, or after he shall have been mustered into and joined a regiment already in the service.


*Approved, June 21, 1862.*

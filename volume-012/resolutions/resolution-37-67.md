# Resolution 67

> A Resolution to repeal and modify Sections two and three of an Act entitled “An Act to settle the Titles to certain Lands set apart for the Use of certain Half-breed Kansas Indians in Kansas Territory,” approved May twenty-six, eighteen hundred and sixty, and to repeal part of section one of said Act.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections two and three of an act entitled “An act to settle the titles to certain lands set apart for the use of certain Half-breed Kansas Indians in Kansas Territory,” approved May twenty-six, one thousand eight hundred and sixty, and so much of the first section as authorizes the Secretary of the Interior to decide what persons are heirs to deceased reservees as mentioned therein be and the same are hereby, repealed.


*Approved, July 17, 1862.*

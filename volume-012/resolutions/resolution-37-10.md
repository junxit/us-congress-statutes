# Resolution 10

> A Resolution for the Collection of War Statistics.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in order to develope, concentrate, and bring into effective action the mechanical and other resources of the United States, for the suppression of the rebellion and the future defence of this Government, the Superintendent of the Census be authorized to furnish the Secretary of War with such war statistics, as from time to time, the Secretary may judge necessary for the use of his Department.


*Approved, February 22, 1862.*

# Resolution 24

> Joint Resolution authorizing the Appointment of a Commissioner to revise and codify the Naval Laws of the United States.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized to appoint (by and with the consent of the Senate) a commissioner, whose duty it shall be to revise and codify the naval laws of the United States, and report such revised code to Congress at its next session. The annual salary of said commissioner shall be three thousand dollars.


*Approved, March 3, 1863.*

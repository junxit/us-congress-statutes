# Resolution 68

> Joint Resolution further to provide for the Compensation of Members of Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, until the further order of Congress, the Secretary of the Senate and the Sergeant-at-arms of the House are directed to receive, as a valid excuse for absence from duty in Congress, active employment in military service for the suppression of the rebellion without pay.

## § 2.

And be it further resolved, That when any senator or representative shall hereafter withdraw from his seat in anticipation of the adjournment of Congress and before the adjournment and does not return, he shall, in addition to the sum now deducted for each day, forfeit a further sum equal to the mileage now allowed by law for his return home, and it shall be deducted from his compensation, unless where said withdrawal is with the leave of the Senate or House of Representatives respectively.


*Approved, July 17, 1862.*

# Resolution 19

> Joint Resolution to revive “An Act to secure to the Officers and Men actually employed in the Western Department, or Department of Missouri, their Pay, Bounty, and Pension, and for other Purposes.”

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of a joint resolution entitled “A resolution to suspend all payments under the act approved the twenty-fifth of March, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, entitled ‘An act to secure to the officers and men actually employed in the Western Department, or Department of Missouri, their pay, bounty, and pension, and for other purposes,’” approved July twelfth, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, be, and they are hereby, revived, and the commissioners therein provided for shall be allowed six months from the passage of this resolution within which to make their report.


*Approved, February 16, 1863.*

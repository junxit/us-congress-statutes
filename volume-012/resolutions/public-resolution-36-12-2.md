# Public Resolution 12

> A Resolution for the Appointment of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the vacancies in the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution of the class “other than members of Congress,” be filled by the appointment of William L. Dayton, of New Jersey, in place of Richard Rush, deceased; William B. Astor, of New York, in place of Gideon Hawley, whose term has expired; and that Cornelius C. Felton, of Massachusetts, whose term hits expired, be reappointed.


*Approved, March 2, 1861.*

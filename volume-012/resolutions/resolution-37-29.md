# Resolution 29

> Joint Resolution to supply the Smithsonian Institution with Volumes of Wilkes’ Exploring Expedition.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the officer in charge of the Library of Congress be, and he is hereby, directed to furnish to the officer in charge of the Smithsonian Institution a copy of each of the volumes of the Exploring Expedition of Captain Wilkes, now in the possession of the Library.


*Approved, April 24, 1862.*

# Resolution 18

> Joint Resolution to compensate the Sailors on the Gunboat “Cairo” for Loss of Clothing.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, authorized, in settling the accounts of the petty officers, seamen, sailors, and others of the crew of the United States gunboat “Cairo,” to allow to each a sum not exceeding fifty dollars as a remuneration for the damage they may have sustained in the loss of their clothing by the destruction of said vessel in December last.


*Approved, February 13, 1863.*

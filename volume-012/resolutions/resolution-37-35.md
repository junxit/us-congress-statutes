# Resolution 35

> Joint Resolution to change the Name of the Barque “Quebec” to the “General Burnside.”

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of the barque “Quebec,” late of Kingston, Canada, be changed to that of “General Burnside,” of Clayton, New York, and that the Secretary of the Treasury be authorized to issue a register therefor.


*Approved, June 17, 1862.*

# Resolution 72

> A Resolution suspending the Sale by sealed Bids, of the Lands of the Kansas and Sac and Fox Indians.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sales of the lands of the Kansas and Sac and Fox tribes of Indians of the State of Kansas, by sealed bids, be, and the same are postponed until the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, any treaty or law to the contrary notwithstanding.


*Approved, July 17, 1862.*

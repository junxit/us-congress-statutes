# Public Resolution 2

> Joint Resolution making an Appropriation for inaugurating the Equestrian Statue of Washington.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of ten thousand dollars be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to be expended by the Secretary of the Interior in transporting and placing Mills’ Statue of Washington on the pedestal designated by the proper authorities, and in the ceremonies appropriate to the occasion.


*Approved, February 24, 1860.*

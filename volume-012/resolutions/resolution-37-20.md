# Resolution 20

> A Resolution providing for the Custody of the Letter and Gifts from the King of Siam.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the letter from His Majesty the Major King of Siam to the President of the United States and the accompanying gifts, be deposited in the collection of curiosities at the Department of the Interior.


*Approved, March 15, 1862.*

# Chapter XCIX

> An Act for the Relief of Eliza B. Mills, Widow of Robert Mills.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Eliza B. Mills, widow of Robert Mills, the sum of five hundred dollars, in payment for services of the said Robert Mills as architect in preparing and furnishing plans and estimates for a marine hospital at New Orleans, and a public printing office in the city of Washington.


*Approved, March 2, 1861.*

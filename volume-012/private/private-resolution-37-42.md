# Private Resolution 42

> Joint Resolution to confirm the adverse Decision of the Court of Claims in the Case of R. R. Ward and others, Assignees of Jacob Barker.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the adverse decision of the Court of Claims in the case of R. R. Ward and others, assignees of Jacob Barker, be, and the same is hereby, confirmed.


*Approved, March 3, 1863.*

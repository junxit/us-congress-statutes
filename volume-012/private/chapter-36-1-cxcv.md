# Chapter CXCV

> An Act for the Relief of Mary Preston, Widow of George Preston.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension rolls, at the rate of eight dollars per month, the name of Mary Preston, widow of George Preston, late an ordnance sergeant in the service of the United States, from the seventh of February, eighteen hundred and fifty-nine.


*Approved, June 22, 1860.*

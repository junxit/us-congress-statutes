# Private Resolution 8

> Joint Resolution authorizing the proper Accounting Officers of the Treasury to revise and adjust the Account of John Randolph Clay, United States Minister to Peru.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the Treasury be authorized and required to revise and adjust the account of John Randolph Clay, as minister to Peru.


*Approved, February 20, 1861.*

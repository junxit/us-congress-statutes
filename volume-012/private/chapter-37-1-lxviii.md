# Chapter LXVIII

> An Act to provide for the Payment of Arnold and Willett for Bread, and Henry North, for Wood, supplied to the United States Volunteers in Maryland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized to cause the account of G. W. Arnold and Charles S. Willett, for bread, and the account of Henry North, for wood, furnished to the United States volunteers in the State of Maryland to be audited and adjusted on terms of equity and justice.


*Approved, August 6, 1861.*

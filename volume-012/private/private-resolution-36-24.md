# Private Resolution 24

> A Resolution for the Restoration of Lieutenant Augustus S. Baldwin to the Active List, from the Leave-of-absence List of the Navy.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be authorized to nominate, and, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, to appoint Lieutenant Augustus S. Baldwin, first lieutenant and executive officer of the flag-ship Lancaster, to the active list of the navy.


*Approved, June 22, 1860.*

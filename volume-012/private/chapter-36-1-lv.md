# Chapter LV

> An Act for the Relief of Samuel A. West, George McCullough, Hiram McCullough, and Charles Pendergrast.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Samuel A. West, George McCullough, Hiram McCullough, and Charles Pendergrast be, and they are hereby, released from their liability on a contract entered into with the United States on the seventeenth day of November, eighteen hundred and fifty-six, for the delivery of stone at the navy yard at Gosport, in the State of Virginia.


*Approved, May 22, 1860.*

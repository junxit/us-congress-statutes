# Chapter XCVII

> An Act for the Relief of Katherine K. Russell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of Katherine Kirby Russell be, and the same is hereby, placed upon the pension list of the United States of America.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to pay to said Katherine K. Russell the sum of twenty-five dollars per month, and this sum to commence and to be computed from and after the first day of January, eighteen hundred and fifty-five, and the same to continue during her widowhood.


*Approved, March 2, 1861.*

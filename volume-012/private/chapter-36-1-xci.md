# Chapter XCI

> An Act for the Relief of John Dixon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to issue a bounty land-warrant for one hundred and sixty acres, to John Dixon, of Dixon’s Ferry, in the State of Illinois, for services rendered in the Black Hawk war.


*Approved, June 9, 1860.*

# Private Resolution 4

> Joint Resolution extending the Time for taking Testimony on the Application of Cyrus H. McCormick, for the Extension of his Patent.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the further time of ninety days be given to parties interested in resisting the extension of the patent of Cyrus H. McCormick, for his patent reaper, or his improvements or modifications of the same, to enable them to take testimony in opposition to said extension, to be used before the Commissioner of Patents, on the hearing of the application of said McCormick for an extension of said patent.


*Approved, February 9, 1861.*

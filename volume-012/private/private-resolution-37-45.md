# Private Resolution 45

> Joint Resolution to confirm the adverse Decision of the Court of Claims in the Case of C. J. Jenkins and W. W. Mann, Assignees of John McKinnie.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the adverse decision of the Court of Claims in the case of C. J. Jenkins and W. W. Mann, assignees of John McKinnie, be, and the same is hereby, confirmed.


*Approved, July 11, 1862.*

# Chapter CXXXI

> An Act granting a Pension to Lawrence P. N. Landrum, of Taylor County, Kentucky.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to place the name of Lawrence P. N. Landrum, of Taylor County, State of Kentucky, on the roll of invalid pensions of the United States, and pay to him the sum of ninety-six dollars per annum, to be computed from the twenty-third day of March, in the year one thousand eight hundred and sixty-two.


*Approved, July 2, 1862.*

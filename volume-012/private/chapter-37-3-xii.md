# Chapter XII

> An Act for the Relief of John B. Motley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of two hundred and ninety-eight dollars and eighty-seven Cents be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to be paid to John B. Motley, of Omaha City, in the Territory of Nebraska, for compensation in full for his services as acting secretary of said Territory from March twenty-three, eighteen hundred and fifty-eight, to July twelve, eighteen hundred and fifty-eight.


*Approved, January 23, 1863.*

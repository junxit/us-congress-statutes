# Chapter LXXXI

> An Act for the Relief of John Scott, Hill W. House, and Samuel O. House.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That John Scott, principal, and Hill W. House and Samuel O. House, sureties, be, and they are hereby, released from a judgment recovered against them by the United States, on the fourth day of April, eighteen hundred and fifty-five, in the district court for the northern district of Florida, upon a contract awarded to said John Scott, for carrying the mail upon route number three thousand five hundred and three, from New Orleans to Key West, from the fifteenth day of January, eighteen hundred and fifty-three, to the thirtieth day of June, eighteen hundred and fifty-five.


*Approved, June 7, 1860.*

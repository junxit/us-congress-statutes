# Chapter CV

> An Act for the Relief of Thomas G. Corbin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the Treasury pay to Lieutenant Thomas G. Corbin, United States navy, the sum of two hundred and ninety-three dollars and fifteen cents, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 2, 1861.*

# Chapter CLXXVI

> An Act granting an Invalid Pension to Hugh H. Howard, of Hockingport, State of Ohio.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Hugh H. Howard, of Hockingport, in the State of Ohio, a soldier in the Oregon Indian war, in eighteen hundred and fifty-five and eighteen hundred and fifty-six, upon the pension roll, and allow him a pension at the rate of eight dollars a month from the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and sixty.


*Approved, July 14, 1862.*

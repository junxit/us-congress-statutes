# Chapter XXXIV

> An Act for the Relief of Aaron H. Palmer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid to Aaron H. Palmer, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of three thousand dollars, in full compensation for his labor and research in collecting information, and preparing the same for the use of the government, relative to the Oriental nations, and particularly Japan.


*Approved, February 13, 1861.*

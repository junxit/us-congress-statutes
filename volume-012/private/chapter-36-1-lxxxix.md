# Chapter LXXXIX

> An Act granting a Pension to Adelaide Adams, Widow of Commander George Adams, United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Adelaide Adams, widow of the late Commander George Adams, of the United States navy, on the pension roll, at the rate of thirty dollars per month, and to pay her a pension at that rate from the ninth day of June, Anno Domini eighteen hundred and fifty-eight, and continue during her widowhood.


*Approved, June 9, 1860.*

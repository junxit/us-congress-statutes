# Chapter XCII

> An Act granting an Invalid Pension to Charles Appleton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Charles Appleton on the invalid pension rolls of the United States, at the rate of eight dollars per month, to commence on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and sixty, and to continue during his lifetime.


*Approved, March 2, 1861.*

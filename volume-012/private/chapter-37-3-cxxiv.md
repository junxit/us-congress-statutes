# Chapter CXXIV

> An Act for the Relief of the Administrator of Captain Richard Stevenson, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be allowed a credit to the administrator of the estate of Captain Richard Stevenson, deceased, late an assistant quartermaster of volunteers in the army of the United States, the sum of three thousand dollars, being the amount of money belonging to the government of the United States in his custody as such officer, seized and appropriated in Kentucky by guerillas while in the course of transmission by express on railroad trains from Indianapolis, Indiana, to Nashville, Tennessee, where said Stevenson was on duty.


*Approved, March 3, 1863.*

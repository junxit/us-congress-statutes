# Chapter CXXVI

> An Act granting an Invalid Pension to Beriah Wright, of New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Beriah Wright, of New York, upon the roll of invalid pensioners of the United States, and pay him a pension, at the rate of four dollars per month, in addition to the pension he is now receiving, from the sixteenth day of February, one thousand eight hundred and fifty-eight, during his natural life.


*Approved, June 13, 1860.*

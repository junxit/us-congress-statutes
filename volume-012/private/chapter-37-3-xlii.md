# Chapter XLII

> An Act for the Relief of Ignatius C. Mattingly, Postmaster at Bardstown, Kentucky.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Sixth Auditor of the Treasury in the settlement of the accounts of Ignatius C. Mattingly, postmaster at Bardstown, Kentucky, be directed to allow to said Mattingly the sum of three hundred and ninety dollars, the amount of postage stamps and money accrued from postages belonging to the Post-Office Department, forcibly taken from him by a rebel force under the command of John Morgan, upon said Mattingly exhibiting satisfactory proof that the said amount was taken from him without complicity, negligence, or deficiency of duty on his part.


*Approved, February 19, 1863.*

# Chapter CI

> An Act granting a Pension to James Lacey, of Grainger County, Tennessee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of James Lacey, of Grainger county, Tennessee, upon the invalid pension roll at eight dollars per month, beginning on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and sixty, and to continue during the existence of his present disability.


*Approved, June 9, 1860.*

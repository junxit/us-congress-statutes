# Chapter XCIX

> An Act for the Relief of Shade Calloway.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers allow and pay out of the treasury to Shade Calloway the sum of one thousand three hundred and fifty dollars, for work done by him on the Tennessee River, under his contract with Brevet Lieutenant-Colonel J. McClelland, dated the sixteenth of September, eighteen hundred and fifty-three, according to the account approved and certified by the agent placed in charge of said work at the death of the said officer.


*Approved, June 9, 1860.*

# Chapter LXV

> An Act granting a Pension to Prentis Champlain.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and instructed to place the name of Prentis Champlain on the invalid pension rolls of the United States, at the rate of eight dollars per month, to commence on the twenty-third day of March, eighteen hundred and sixty, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, March 1, 1861.*

# Private Resolution 5

> Joint Resolution to confirm the adverse Decision of the Court of Claims in the Case of Arthur Edwards and others.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the adverse decision of the Court of Claims in the case of Arthur Edwards and others be, and the same is hereby, confirmed.


*Approved, January 16, 1863.*

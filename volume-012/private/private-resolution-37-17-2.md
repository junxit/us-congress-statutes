# Private Resolution 17

> A Resolution to confirm the adverse Decision of the Court of Claims in the Case of Alexander D. Anderson, Administrator of John Anderson.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the adverse decision of the Court of Claims in the case of Alexander D. Anderson, administrator of John Anderson be, and the same is hereby, confirmed.


*Approved, February 10, 1863.*

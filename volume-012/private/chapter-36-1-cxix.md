# Chapter CXIX

> An Act for the Relief of Valentine Wehrheim.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Valentine Wehrheim on the roll of invalid pensioners, and pay him at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month, from and after the twenty-sixth day of January, eighteen hundred and sixty.


*Approved, June 12, 1860.*

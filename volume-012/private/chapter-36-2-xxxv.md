# Chapter XXXV

> An Act granting a Pension to Gregory Patti.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Gregory Patti on the list of invalid pensions, at the rate of seven dollars and fifty cents per month, and pay him a pension at that rate, from the first day of January, eighteen hundred and sixty, and continue during his natural life.


*Approved, February 13, 1861.*

# Chapter CLII

> An Act for the Relief of D. G. Farragut.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, authorized and instructed to allow to Captain D. G. Farragut, United States navy, in the settlement of his accounts, the sum of four hundred and seven dollars and nineteen cents, being the amount paid by him as wages to two master’s mates on board the United States ship Warren, employed at Mare Island, California.


*Approved, July 11, 1862.*

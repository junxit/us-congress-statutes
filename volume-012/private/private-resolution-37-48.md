# Private Resolution 48

> Joint Resolution to confirm the adverse Decision of the Court of Claims in the Case of Abraham King, Administrator of John Mandeville.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the adverse decision of the Court of Claims in the case of Abraham King, administrator of John Mandeville, be, and the same is hereby, confirmed.


*Approved, July 11, 1862.*

# Private Resolution 10

> A Resolution for the Relief of the legal Representatives of John A. Frost, deceased.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of the first section of the act entitled “An act for the relief of the forward officers of the late exploring expedition,” approved February first, eighteen hundred and forty-nine, shall be construed to embrace the claim of John A. Frost, who was acting boatswain of the United States brig “Porpoise” in said expedition, from January first, eighteen hundred and thirty-nine, to July seventh, eighteen hundred and forty-two, and that the amount found due to him shall be paid to his legal representatives.


*Approved, May 9, 1860.*

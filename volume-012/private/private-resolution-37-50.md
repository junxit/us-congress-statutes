# Private Resolution 50

> Joint Resolution to confirm the adverse Decision of the Court of Claims in the Case of T. S. J. Johnson.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the adverse decision of the Court of Claims in the case of T. S. J. Johnson be, and the same is hereby, confirmed.


*Approved, July 11, 1862.*

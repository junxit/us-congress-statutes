# Chapter CI

> An Act granting a Pension to Eliza M. Plympton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be directed to place the name of Eliza M. Plympton, widow of the late Colonel Joseph Plympton of the United States Army on the pension roll, at the rate of thirty dollars a month, from the fifth day of June, one thousand eight hundred and sixty, for and during her natural life.


*Approved, March 2, 1861.*

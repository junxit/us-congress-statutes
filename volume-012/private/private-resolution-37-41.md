# Private Resolution 41

> Joint Resolution to confirm the adverse Decision of the Court of Claims in the Case of Brig. General Armstrong.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the adverse decision of the Court of Claims in the case of Brigadier-General Armstrong be, and the same is hereby, confirmed.


*Approved, March 3, 1863.*

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

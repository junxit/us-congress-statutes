# Chapter XXXII

> An Act for the Relief of Angelina C. Bowman, Widow of Francis L. Bowman, late Captain United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place the name of Mrs. Angelina C. Bowman, widow of the late Captain Francis L. Bowman of the ninth regiment of United States infantry, upon the pension roll, and to pay her at the rate of thirty dollars per month, from the thirteenth day of January, eighteen hundred and fifty-nine, to continue for life.


*Approved, April 19, 1860.*

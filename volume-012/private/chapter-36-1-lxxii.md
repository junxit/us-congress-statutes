# Chapter LXXII

> An Act for the Relief of William P. Bowhay.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of eight hundred dollars be, and the same is hereby, allowed to William P. Bowhay, for severe personal injuries received by him whilst employed by the United States on board the frigate Congress, and which rendered him a cripple for life; the same to be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 1, 1860.*

# Chapter LXXIV

> An Act for the Relief of Mrs. Jane M. McCrabb, Widow of the late Captain John W. McCrabb, Assistant Quartermaster United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of five thousand two hundred and ninety-three dollars and ninety-six cents be paid to Mrs. Jane M. McCrabb, as administratrix of John W. McCrabb, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the same being allowance of commissions for disbursements of special appropriations by her late husband, Captain John W. McCrabb, prior to the thirtieth of September, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-eight; and that in the settlement of the accounts of said John W. McCrabb, the above amount be credited as of the date of its first charge.


*Approved, June 1, 1860.*

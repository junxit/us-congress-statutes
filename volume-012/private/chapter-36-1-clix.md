# Chapter CLIX

> An Act granting an Invalid Pension to Ezekiel Jones.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Ezekiel Jones on the invalid pension roll of the United States, at the rate of sixteen dollars per month, commencing from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 19, 1860.*

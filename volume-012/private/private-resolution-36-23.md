# Private Resolution 23

> A Resolution for the Relief of Arthur Edwards and his Associates.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster-General be, and he hereby is, authorized to examine and adjust the claim of Arthur Edwards and his associates for carrying the through mails on their boats between Cleveland and Detroit, Sandusky and Detroit, and Toledo and Detroit, during the years eighteen hundred and forty-nine and eighteen hundred and fifty-three, and the intervening years, and to pay to the said Arthur Edwards and his associates such sum of money as shall be found to be justly and equitably due to them for carrying the mails as aforesaid, and the sum thus found due shall be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 22, 1860.*

# Chapter CCXIII

> An Act for the Relief of Elizabeth Smith, of Coffee County, Tennessee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to cause to be issued to Elizabeth Smith, the wife of Joseph B. Smith, of Coffee county, Tennessee, a warrant for one hundred and sixty acres of land, for services rendered in the army of the United States, during the war with Mexico, by her father, Colonel Archibald Yell, of Arkansas.


*Approved, June 25, 1860.*

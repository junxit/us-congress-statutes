# Chapter XXI

> An Act for the Relief of Alice Hunt, Widow of Thomas Hunt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Alice Hunt, widow of Captain Thomas Hunt, on the pension roll, and to pay her, at the rate of thirty-five dollars per month, from the fifth day of January, eighteen hundred and sixty, for life or widowhood.


*Approved, April 11, 1860.*

## Marginal notes

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

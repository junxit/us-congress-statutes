# Chapter CCIX

> An Act for the Relief of E. George Squier, of New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to E. George Squier, of New York, from any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of nine thousand nine hundred and thirty-seven dollars, in full, for outfits to the republics of Central America, and balance of salary due him as minister of the United States to said republics.


*Approved, June 23, 1860.*

# Private Resolution 16

> A Resolution to confirm the adverse Decision of the Court of Claims in the Case of Thomas Hicks, Executor of Isaac Hicks.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the adverse decision of the Court of Claims in the case of Thomas Hicks, executor of Isaac Hicks be, and the same is hereby, confirmed.


*Approved, February 10, 1863.*

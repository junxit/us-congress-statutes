# Private Resolution 10

> A Resolution to repeal the Joint Resolution approved June fifteenth, eighteen hundred and sixty, for the Relief of William H. Degroot.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the joint resolution, approved June fifteen, eighteen hundred and sixty, for the relief of William H. Degroot, be, and the same is hereby, repealed; and that the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to transmit all the papers in his department, relating to the case of the said William H. Degroot, to the Court of Claims for examination.


*Approved, February 21, 1861.*

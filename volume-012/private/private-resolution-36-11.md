# Private Resolution 11

> Joint Resolution for the Relief of Thomas C. Ware.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid to Thomas C. Ware, of Cincinnati, Ohio, out of any money in the treasury of the United States not otherwise appropriated, the sum of fifteen hundred dollars, in full for services rendered by him to the government of the United States, at the request of D. O. Morton, late District Attorney of the United States in the case of the United States versus Lyman Cole, and others, indicted and tried in the Circuit Court of the United States for the District of Ohio, at the October term, A. D. eighteen hundred and fifty-three.


*Approved, May 16, 1860.*

# Chapter XLII

> An Act for the Relief of Madison Sweetzer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior cause to be paid to Madison Sweetzer the sum of eleven hundred dollars and ninety-seven cents, the balance of his account against the Sissetowan and Wah-pay-toan bands of Sioux Indians, of Minnesota, for necessary supplies heretofore furnished, examined, and verified by the honorable Governor W. A. Gorman, superintendent, and Agent H. W. Murphy, as commissioners, and the same be deducted from the annuities payable to said Indians for the year commencing on the first of July, eighteen hundred and sixty.


*Approved, May 9, 1860.*

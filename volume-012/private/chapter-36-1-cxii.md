# Chapter CXII

> An Act for the Relief of Charles W. Brooks, of New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Charles W. Brooks, of the State of New York, on the invalid pension roll at the rate of eight dollars per month, and to pay him at that rate from the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and sixty, and continue during his natural life.


*Approved, June 9, 1860.*

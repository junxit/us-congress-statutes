# Chapter CLXXVI

> An Act for the Relief of Chauncey W. Fuller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Chauncey W. Fuller upon the invalid pension rolls, at the rate of eight dollars per month, to commence on the first day of April, eighteen hundred and fifty-eight, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, June 21, 1860.*

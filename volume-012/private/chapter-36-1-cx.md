# Chapter CX

> An Act for the Relief of R. K. Doebler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the assignment made by Samuel H. Dill on land-warrant for one hundred and sixty acres of land, number ten thousand one hundred and seventeen, issued fourth November, eighteen hundred and fifty-one, which assignment was made on the twenty-eighth day of November, eighteen hundred and fifty-one, to R. K. Doebler, be, and the same is hereby, legalized and made valid.


*Approved, June 9, 1860.*

# Chapter LXXXIX

> An Act for the Relief of Richard Chenery.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of Customs be, and he is hereby, directed to settle the account of Richard Chenery, assignee of Horace P. Russ, for the construction by the latter of the granite paving on Battery street, in front of the United States custom-house, at San Francisco, and to pay him such sum as may be shown to be legally and equitably due out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated: Provided, The said sum shall not exceed three thousand eight hundred and ninety dollars.


*Approved, March 2, 1861.*

# Private Resolution 33

> A Resolution to confirm the Opinion of the Court of Claims adverse to the Claim of Thomas B. Graham.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the opinion of the Court of Claims, adverse to the claim of Thomas B. Graham, be, and hereby is, confirmed.


*Approved, May 21, 1862.*

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

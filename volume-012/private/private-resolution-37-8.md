# Private Resolution 8

> Joint Resolution to confirm the adverse Decision of the Court of Claims in the Case of J. W. Knaggs, Administrator of Whitmore Knaggs.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the adverse decision of the Court of Claims in the case of James W. Knaggs, administrator of Whitmore Knaggs be, and the same is hereby, confirmed.


*Approved, January 16, 1863.*

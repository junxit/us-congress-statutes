## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the Postmaster General is hereby authorized to change the terminus of existing mail routes connecting with or intersecting railroads when the postal service can be thereby improved.

## § 3.

And be it further enacted, That the Postmaster General be and he is hereby authorized to fix the salaries of special agents of the Post Office Department, employed on temporary service, at any sum less than the amount designated for such service by law.


*Approved, June 2, 1862.*

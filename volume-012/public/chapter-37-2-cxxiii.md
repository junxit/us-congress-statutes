# Chapter CXXIII

> An Act to provide for the Appointment of an Indian Agent in Colorado Territory.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and is hereby, authorized to appoint, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, or during the recess thereof and until the end of its next session after such appointment, an agent for the Grand River and Wintah bands of Indians, in the Territory of Colorado, at a salary of fifteen hundred dollars per annum.


*Approved, July 1, 1862.*

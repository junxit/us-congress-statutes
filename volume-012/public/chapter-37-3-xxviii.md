# Chapter XXVIII

> An Act to make the State of Wisconsin a Part of the Ninth Judicial Circuit.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the State of Wisconsin shall be, and is hereby, made a part of the ninth judicial circuit of the United States.


*Approved, February 9, 1863.*

# Chapter CLXXXIX

> An Act in Relation to Mission Claims at Sault Ste. Marie, Michigan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the missionary claims referred to in the fourth section of the act of twenty-sixth September, eighteen hundred and fifty, entitled “An act providing for the examination and settlement of claims for land at the Sault Ste. Marie, Michigan,” and reported upon by the register and receiver at Sault Ste. Marie, pursuant to said act, shall be entitled to recognition and confirmation in the same manner and on the same terms as claims of individuals therein provided for; and upon the final approval of the said claims, as provided in the eighth section of the act aforesaid, patents shall be issued therefor.


*Approved, June 22, 1860.*

# Chapter LXXV

> An Act declaring the Value of the new Silver Florin of Austria.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the new silver florin of Austria shall, in all computations at the custom-house, be estimated at forty-six cents and nineteen-hundredths of a cent.


*Approved, March 2, 1861.*

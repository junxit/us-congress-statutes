# Chapter CX

> An Act making Appropriations for Postal Service on Post Routes established at the present Session of Congress.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of one hundred and fifty thousand dollars is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for postal service on such mail routes established by the present Congress as the Postmaster General may deem necessary and expedient.


*Approved, June 18, 1862.*

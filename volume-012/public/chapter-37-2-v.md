# Chapter V

> An Act making an Appropriation for Gunboats on the Western Rivers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of one million of dollars be, and the same is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for gunboats on the Western rivers.


*Approved, December 24, 1861.*

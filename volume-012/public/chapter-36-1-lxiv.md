# Chapter LXIV

> An Act authorizing the “Domestic and Foreign Missionary Society of the Protestant Episcopal Church in the United States” to enter a certain Tract of Land in the State of Wisconsin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the “Domestic and Foreign Missionary Society of the Protestant Episcopal Church in the United States” be, and is hereby, authorized to enter, at the rate of one dollar and twenty-five cents per acre, a certain tract of land known as the “Mission Farm,” and numbered as lot number eighteen, on the east bank of Fox River, near Green Bay, State of Wisconsin, having a front on Fox River of six chains, and running eastwardly back from the river, between parallel lines, one hundred and fifty-four and sixty-nine hundreths chains, and containing ninety-two and eighty-one hundredths superficial acres.


*Approved, June 1, 1860.*

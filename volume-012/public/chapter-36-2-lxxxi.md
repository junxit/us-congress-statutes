# Chapter LXXXI

> An Act to amend the Provisions of the fifty-sixth Section of “An Act to regulate the Collection of Duties on Imports and Tonnage,” approved the second Day of March, one thousand seven hundred and ninety-nine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That whenever any goods, wares, or merchandise, shall be imported into any port of the United States from any foreign port, in any ship or vessel, at the expiration of eight working days, if the ship or vessel shall be less than three hundred tons burden, and within twelve working days, if it be of three hundred tons burden and less than eight hundred, and within fifteen days, if it be of eight hundred tons burden and upwards, after the time within which the report of the master or person having charge or command of any ship or vessel is required to be made to the collector of the district, if there shall be found any goods, wares, or merchandise other than shall have been reported for some other district, or some foreign port or place, the collector shall take possession thereof; but with the consent of the owner or consignee of any goods, wares, or merchandise, or with the consent of the owner or master of the vessel in which the same may be imported, the said goods, wares, or merchandise may be taken possession of by the collector, after one day’s notice to the collector of the district.


*Approved, March 2, 1861.*

# Chapter CXIV

> An Act to provide for issuing an American Register to the Steam-vessel “Maple Leaf."

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be issued, under the direction of the Secretary of the Treasury, an American register to the British colonial built steam-vessel “Maple Leaf” now owned by Charles Spear and J. H. B. Lang, of Boston, in the State of Massachusetts.


*Approved, March 3, 1863.*

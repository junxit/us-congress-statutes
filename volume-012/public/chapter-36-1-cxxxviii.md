# Chapter CXXXVIII

> An Act making Appropriation for the Payment of the Expenses of the Legislative Assembly of the Territory of Minnesota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of twenty-six thousand dollars, or so much thereof as has not been heretofore paid, appropriated by the act of March third, Anno Domini eighteen hundred and fifty-seven, for the payment of the expenses of the legislative assembly of the Territory of Minnesota, be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to be paid to the State of Minnesota.


*Approved, June 16, 1860.*

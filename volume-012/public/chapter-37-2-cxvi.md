# Chapter CXVI

> An Act to change the Part of Entry for the District of Brunswick, Georgia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the first day of July, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, the port of entry for the district of Brunswick, Georgia, shall be Brunswick, and that Darien shall be abolished as the port of entry.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That there shall be a deputy collector appointed, according to law, to reside at Darien, and to exercise such powers as the Secretary of the Treasury, under the revenue laws, may prescribe.


*Approved, June 20, 1862.*

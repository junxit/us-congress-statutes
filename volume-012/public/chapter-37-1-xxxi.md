# Chapter XXXI

> An Act to increase the Medical Corps of the Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the medical corps of the Navy shall consist of eighty surgeons, and one hundred and twenty passed and other assistant-surgeons.


*Approved, July 31, 1861.*

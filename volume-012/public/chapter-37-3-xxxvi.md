# Chapter XXXVI

> An Act to issue an American Register to the Steamship Karnak.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby directed to issue an American register to the steamship or vessel known as the Karnak, of the collection district of the port of New York, the same being a British built vessel, but now owned by American citizens.


*Approved, February 16, 1863.*

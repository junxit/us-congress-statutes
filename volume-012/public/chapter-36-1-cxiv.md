# Chapter CXIV

> An Act to liquidate the unadjusted Contracts of the Tennessee River Improvement.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all contracts made by the duly authorized officers of the Government, appointed under the act of thirty-second Congress making an appropriation of fifty thousand dollars for the improvement of the Tennessee River, shall be audited by the accounting officers of the treasury, and paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 12, 1860.*

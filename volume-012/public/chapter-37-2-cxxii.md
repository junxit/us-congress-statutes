# Chapter CXXII

> An Act changing the Name of the American-built Schooner “Tom Dyer” to that of “Dolphin.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the collector of the customs of the port of Detroit be, and he is hereby, authorized, under the direction of the Secretary of the Treasury, to issue a new register, license, or enrolment, to the American-built schooner “Tom Dyer,” in the name of the “Dolphin,” the same being owned by H. N. Strong.


*Approved, July 1, 1862.*

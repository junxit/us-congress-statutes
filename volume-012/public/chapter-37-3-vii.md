# Chapter VII

> An Act to improve the Organization of the Cavalry Forces.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That hereafter each regiment of cavalry organized in the United States service, may have two assistant surgeons, and each company or troop of cavalry shall have from sixty to seventy-eight privates.


*Approved, January 6, 1863.*

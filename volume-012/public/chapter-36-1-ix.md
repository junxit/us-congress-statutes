# Chapter IX

> An Act to establish Mail Routes in the Territory of Kansas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be, and are hereby, established in the Territory of Kansas, the following mail routes:

- A mail route from Denver City to Missouri City, via Arrapahoe, Golden City, Golden Gate, and Mountain City, a distance of forty miles.
- A mail route from Denver City to Breckenridge, via Baden and Tarryall, a distance of one hundred miles.
- A mail route from Denver City to Boulder, in Nebraska Territory, a distance of forty miles.
- A mail route from Denver City, down the Platte River, to Julesburg, a distance of two hundred and forty miles.
- A mail route from Denver City to Colorado City, a distance of sixty-eight miles, via Belmont.


*Approved, March 27, 1860.*

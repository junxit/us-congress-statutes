# Chapter II

> An Act to amend the fourth Section of the Act for the Admission of Oregon into the Union, so as to extend the Time for selecting Salt Springs and contiguous Lands in Oregon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time for selecting the salt springs and contiguous lands, according to the provisions of the fourth section of the act entitled “An act for the admission of Oregon into the Union,” approved February fourteenth, eighteen hundred and fifty-nine, be extended to any time within three years from the passage of this act, anything in said section to the contrary notwithstanding.


*Approved, December 17, 1860.*

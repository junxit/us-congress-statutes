# Chapter XI

> An Act to continue in Force an Act therein mentioned, relating to the Port of Baltimore.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the act passed the seventeenth day of March, one thousand eight hundred, entitled “An act declaring the assent of Congress to certain acts of the States of Maryland and Georgia,” and which by subsequent acts have been continued in force until the third day of March, one thousand eight hundred and sixty-one, be and the same, so far as it relates to the act of the State of Maryland, is hereby revived and continued in force until the third day of March, one thousand eight hundred and seventy-one: Provided, That the duty shall not be levied on any vessel more than once in any month.


*Approved, January 19, 1861.*

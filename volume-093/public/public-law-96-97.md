# Public Law 96–97

> An Act To amend section 15(d) of the Tennessee Valley Authority Act of 1933 to increase the amount of debt which may be incurred by the Tennessee Valley Authority.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first sentence of subsection (a) of section 15(d) of the Tennessee Valley Authority Act of 1933, as amended (16 U.S.C. 831n–4), is amended by striking out “$15,000,000,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$30,000,000,000”.


*Approved October 31, 1979.*

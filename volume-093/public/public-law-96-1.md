# Public Law 96–1

> Joint Resolution To extend the time for filing the Economic Report

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Public Law 95–594 is amended by striking out “January 22, 1979” the second time it appears in section 2, and inserting in lieu thereof “January 29, 1979”.


*Approved January 22, 1979.*

# Public Law 96–139

> An Act To designate the building known as the Federal Building, at 211 Main Street, in Scott City, Kansas, as the “Henry D. Parkinson Federal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the building at 211 Main Street, in Scott City, Kansas (commonly known as the Federal Building) shall hereafter be known and designated as the “Henry D. Parkinson Federal Building”. Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to that building shall be held to be a reference to the Henry D. Parkinson Federal Building.


*Approved December 12, 1979.*

# Public Law 96–176

> An Act To establish an improved program for extra long staple cotton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, effective with respect to the 1980 and subsequent crops of extra long staple cotton, section 101(f) of the Agricultural Act of 1949, as amended, is amended by—

- **(1)** striking out “1968” and inserting in lieu thereof “1980”;
- **(2)** striking out “50” and inserting in lieu thereof “85”;
- **(3)** striking out “100” and inserting in lieu thereof “135”; and
- **(4)** striking out “65” and inserting in lieu thereof “55”.


*Approved December 31, 1979.*

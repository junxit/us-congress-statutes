# Public Law 96–141

> An Act To designate the building known as the Department of Labor Building in Washington, District of Columbia, as the “Frances Perkins Department of Labor Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the building at 200 Constitution Avenue, Northwest, in Washington, District of Columbia (commonly known as the Department of Labor Building) shall hereafter be known and designated as the “Frances Perkins Department of Labor Building”. Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to that building shall be held to be a reference to the “Frances Perkins Department of Labor Building”.


*Approved December 12, 1979.*

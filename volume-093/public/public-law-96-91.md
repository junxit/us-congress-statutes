# Public Law 96–91

> An Act To amend title 28 of the United States Code to allow the United States attorney and assistant United States attorneys for the Eastern District of New York to reside within twenty miles of the district.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 545(a) of title 28, United States Code, is amended by striking out “Columbia and the Southern District of New York” and inserting in lieu thereof “Columbia, the Southern District of New York, and the Eastern District of New York”.


*Approved October 25, 1979.*

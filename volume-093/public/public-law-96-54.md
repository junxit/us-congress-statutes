# Public Law 96–54

> An Act To make certain technical and clerical amendments to title 5, United Stales Code.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. references to title 5, united states code

Whenever in this Act, any reference, amendment, or repeal is expressed in terms of a reference or amendment to, or repeal of, a section or other provision, it shall be considered to be made to a section or other provision of title 5, United States Code.

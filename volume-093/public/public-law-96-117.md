# Public Law 96–117

> Joint Resolution To amend the Act of October 21, 1978 (92 Stat. 1675; Public Law 95–498).

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of October 21, 1978 (92 Stat. 1675; Public Law 95–498) is hereby amended by inserting in section 1(b)(2), before the phrase “Section 5:”, the heading “Township 13 North, Range 4 East”.


*Approved November 16, 1979.*

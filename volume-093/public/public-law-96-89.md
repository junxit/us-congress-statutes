# Public Law 96–89

> An Act To amend the Act of July 2, 1940, as amended, to increase the amount authorized to be appropriated for the Canal Zone Biological Area.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 7 of the Act of July 2, 1940 (20 U.S.C 79e), is amended by striking out “$350,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$750,000”.

## § 2.

The provision in section 1 of this Act shall take effect on October 1, 1979.


*Approved October 19, 1979.*

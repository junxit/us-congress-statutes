# Public Law 96–120

> An Act To designate the United States Federal Courthouse Building located at 655 East Durango, San Antonio, Texas, as the “John H. Wood, Jr., Federal Courthouse”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States Federal Courthouse Building located at 655 East Durango, San Antonio, Texas, shall hereafter be called and designated as the “John H. Wood, Jr., Federal Courthouse”. Any reference in law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to such building shall be held to be reference to the John H. Wood, Jr., Federal Courthouse.


*Approved November 16, 1979.*

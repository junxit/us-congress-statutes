# House Concurrent Resolution 99

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Tuesday, April 10, 1979, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian on Monday, April 23, 1979, and that when the Senate recesses on Tuesday, April 10, 1979, it stand in recess until 12 o’clock meridian on Monday, April 23, 1979.


*Agreed to April 10, 1979.*

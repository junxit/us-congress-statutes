# House Concurrent Resolution 66

> “OUR FLAG”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed as a House document a revised edition of “Our Flag”, revised under the direction of the Joint Committee on Printing.

## § 2.

In addition to the usual number of copies, there shall be printed two hundred and seventy-eight thousand additional copies, of which two hundred and twenty-one thousand five hundred copies shall be for the use of the House of Representatives, fifty-one thousand five hundred copies shall be for the use of the Senate, and five thousand copies shall be for the use of the Joint Committee on Printing.


*Agreed to May 14, 1979.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 1

> JOINT MEETING

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Tuesday, January 23, 1979, at 9 o’clock postmeridian for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed January 18, 1979.*

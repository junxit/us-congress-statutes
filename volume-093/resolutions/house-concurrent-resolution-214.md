# House Concurrent Resolution 214

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Tuesday, November 20, 1979, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian on Monday, November 26, 1979, and that when the Senate recesses on Tuesday, November 20, 1979, it stand in recess until 10 o’clock a.m. on Monday, November 26, 1979.


*Agreed to November 20, 1979.*

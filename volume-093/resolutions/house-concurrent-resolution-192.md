# House Concurrent Resolution 192

> CORRECTION OF ENROLLED BILL H.R. 3996

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (H.R. 3996), to amend the Rail Passenger Service Act to extend the authorization of appropriations for Amtrak for three additional years, and for other purposes, the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall make the following correction: in section 102 of the bill, insert “Act (45 U.S.C. 502) is amended—” immediately after “Rail Passenger Service”.


*Agreed to September 28, 1979.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 55

> CORRECTION OF ENROLLED BILL S. 1871

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (S. 1871) the Secretary of the Senate shall correct the title of the bill by striking out “for 2 years” where it appears therein.


*Agreed to November 29, 1979.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 44

> CORRECTIONS OF ENROLLED BILL S. 1030

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

In section 103(c)(1) strike out “42 U.S.C. 6361(f)

That, in the enrollment of the bill (S. 1030) to authorize the President to create an emergency program to conserve energy, and for other purposes, the Secretary of the Senate shall make the following corrections;

In section 101 strike out “(a) Findings.—”.

In section 103(b)(1) strike out “42 U.S.C. 6361(e)” and insert in lieu thereof “42 U.S.C. 6261(e)”.

In section 103(b)(2)(C) insert between the words “other” and “ pursuant” the word “than”.

” and insert in lieu thereof “42 U.S.C. 6261(f)”.

In section 105(b)(3) strike out “42 U.S.C. 6261” and insert in lieu thereof “42 U.S.C. 6263”.

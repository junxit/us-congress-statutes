# House Concurrent Resolution 184

> “7TH EDITION OF THE IMMIGRATION AND NATIONALITY ACT WITH AMENDMENTS AND NOTES ON RELATED LAWS”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That at such time as the committee print entitled “7th Edition of the Immigration and Nationality Act with Amendments and Notes on Related Laws”, is printed, there shall be printed an additional twelve thousand copies, of which nine thousand copies shall be for the use of the House Committee on the Judiciary and three thousand copies for the use of the Senate Committee on the Judiciary.


*Agreed to December 6, 1979.*

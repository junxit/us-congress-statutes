# Private Law 96–26

> An Act For the relief of Caroline M. Babcock.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Caroline M. Babcock, supervisory accounting technician for the Twelfth Coast Guard District, San Francisco, California, is relieved of liability for the payment to the United States of the sum of $8,000. Such sum represents the amount of funds missing on the morning of November 9, 1974, from a safe for which Caroline M. Babcock, as supervisory accounting technician, was responsible. In the audit and settlement of the accounts of any certifying or disbursing officer of the United States, full credit shall be given to Caroline M. Babcock for the amount for which liability is relieved by this Act.

## § 2.

- **(a)** If any part of the amount specified in the first section of this Act has been received or withheld from Caroline M. Babcock in connection with the liability relieved under the first section of this Act, the Secretary of the Treasury shall pay to her such amount out of any funds in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated.
- **(b)** No amount in excess of 10 per centum of any appropriation made under subsection (a) of this section shall be paid to or received by any attorney for services rendered in connection with such appropriation. Any person violating this subsection shall be fined not more than $1,000.


*Approved December 21, 1979.*

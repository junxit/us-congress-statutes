# Private Law 96–34

> An Act For the relief of Maryrose and Rosemary Evangelista.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Maryrose and Rosemary Evangelista may be classified as children within the meaning of section l01(b)(1)(F) of the Act, upon approval of petitions filed in their behalf by Frank and Noemi Quijano, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of the Act: Provided, That the natural parents or brothers or sisters of the beneficiaries shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved December 28, 1979.*

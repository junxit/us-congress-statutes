# Private Law 96–14

> An Act For the relief of Patrick A. and Wayne L. Thomas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Wayne Lloyd Thomas and Patrick Anthony Thomas may be classified as children within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F)of the Act, upon approval of petitions filed in their behalf by Mr. and Mrs. Keith Stewart, a lawful resident alien and a citizen of the United States, respectively, pursuant to section 204 of the Act: Provided, That the natural parents or brothers or sisters of the beneficiaries shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved October 19, 1979.*

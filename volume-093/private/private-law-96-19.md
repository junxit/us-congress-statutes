# Private Law 96–19

> An Act For the relief of Dirk Vierkant.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Dirk Vierkant shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act upon payment of the required visa fee. Upon the granting of permanent residence to such alien as provided for in this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper officer to reduce by the required number, during the current fiscal year or the fiscal year next following, the total number of immigrant visas and conditional entries which are made available to natives of the country of the alien’s birth under paragraphs (1) through (8) of section 203(a) of the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved December 5, 1979.*

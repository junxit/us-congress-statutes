# Private Law 96–40

> An Act For the relief of Eileen Ferraren Fair.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Eileen Ferraren Fair may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of the Act (8 U.S.C. 1101(b)(1)(F)), upon approval of a petition filed in her behalf by Ilia Fair and Clarence E. Fair, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of the Act (8 U.S.C. 1154): Provided, That the natural parents or brothers or sisters of the beneficiary shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved December 28, 1979.*

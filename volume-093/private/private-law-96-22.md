# Private Law 96–22

> An Act For the relief of Jung-Sook Mun.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1101 et seq.), Jung-Sook Mun shall be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of such Act, upon approval of a petition filed in her behalf by Robert White and Patricia White, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of such Act. The natural parents, brothers, and sisters of Jung-Sook Mun shall not, by virtue of their relationship to her, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under such Act.


*Approved December 5, 1979.*

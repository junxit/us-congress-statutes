# Chapter 393

> An Act relating to the registration of trademarks.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That nothing contained in the law entitled “An act to authorize the registration of trademarks and protect the same,” approved March third, eighteen hundred and eighty-one, shall prevent the registry of any lawful trademark rightfully used by the applicant in foreign commerce or commerce with Indian tribes at the time of the passage of said act.


*Approved, August 5, 1882.*

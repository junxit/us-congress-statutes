# Chapter 478

> An Act to donate two condemned cast-iron cannon and twelve cannon-balls to the A. E. Burnside Post Number One hundred and nine of the Grand Army of the Republic of South Chicago, Illinois

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to donate two condemned cast- iron cannon and twelve cannonballs to the A. E. Burnside Post Number One hundred and nine of the Grand Army of the Republic, at South Chicago, Illinois


*Approved, August 8, 1882.*

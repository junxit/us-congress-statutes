# Chapter 118

> An Act to amend the laws with reference to elections in West Virginia

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That on the second Tuesday of October, eighteen hundred and eighty-two, there shall be elected in each Congressional District in the State of West Virginia, one representative to represent said State of West Virginia in the Forty-eighth Congress.

## § 2.

That said election shall be conducted according to the laws now in force, except so far as the same relate to and fix the time of such election.


*Approved, May 4, 1882.*

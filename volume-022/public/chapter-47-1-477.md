# Chapter 477

> An Act to authorize the transfer of the property of the National Soldiers and Sailors' Orphan Home to the Garfield Memorial Hospital.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the board of trustees of the National Soldiers anti Sailors’ Orphan Home of the District of Columbia arc hereby empowered to transfer and convey all the property, real, personal and mixed, of the said National Soldiers and Sailors’ Orphan Home to the Garfield Memorial Hospital, located in said district; and the said Garfield Memorial Hospital is hereby empowered to sell and convey the same and apply the proceeds to the object for which it was incorporated: Provided: That this act shall not be construed, to make the United States liable in any way on account of said transfer, or the changing of the direction of the trust.


*Approved, August 8, 1882.*

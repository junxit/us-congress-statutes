# Chapter 85

> An Act to provide a deficiency for the subsistence of the Arapahoe. Cheyenne, Kiowa, Comanche, Apache and Wichita Indians.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of eighty thousand dollars or so much thereof as may be necessary, is hereby appropriated out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated to be expended under the direction of the Secretary of the Interior for the subsistence of the Arapahoes, Cheyennes, Apaches, Kiowas, Comanches, and Wiehitas, in the Indian Territory, the same being a deficiency for the fiscal year of eighteen hundred and eighty-two.


*Approved, April 21, 1882.*

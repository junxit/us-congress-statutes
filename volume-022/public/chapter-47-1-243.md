# Chapter 243

> An Act to vacate a certain part of Rock street, in Georgetown, in the District of Columbia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the portion of Rock street, in Georgetown, in the District of Columbia, lying between Bridge street and the intersection of said Rock street with Monroe street be, and the same is hereby, vacated.


*Approved, June 27, 1882.*

# Chapter 155

> An Act to donate iron cannon to the township of Milan, Ohio.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to deliver to the mayor of Milan, Ohio, four condemned east iron cannon, if the same can be done without serious detriment to the government, for the adornment of the monument erected in the village of Milan commemorating the names of soldiers who devoted and lost their lives in the service of the United States during the war of the rebellion.


*Approved, May 17, 1882.*

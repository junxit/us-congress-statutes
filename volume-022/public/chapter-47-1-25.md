# Chapter 25

> An Act in reference to the Trustees of the Lincoln Monument Association

Whereas, owing to the large number of Trastees named in the “Act to incorporate the Lincoln Monument Association” approved March thirtieth, eighteen hundred and sixty-seven, it proves to be impracticable for a majority of said Trastees to meet for the transaction of the business of said association: Therefore, Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

That hereafter five of the Trustees of said association, whether named in said act, or subsequently appointed, shall constitute a legal quorum and may exercise all the powers conferred by law upon said association: Provided, That each of said trustees shall be notified by the President or Secretary twenty days in advance of any meeting of said trustees.


*Approved, February 25, 1882.*

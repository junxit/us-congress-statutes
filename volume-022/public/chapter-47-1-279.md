# Chapter 279

> An Act granting four condemned cannon to Otis Chapman Post Number One hundred and three of the Grand Army of the Republic, at Chicopee, Massachusetts, for monumental purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War is hereby authorized to deliver to Otis Chapman Post Number Oue hundred and three of the Grand Army of the Republic, at Chicopee, Massachusetts, four condemned cast-iron cannon for monumental purposes.


*Approved, July 7, 1882.*

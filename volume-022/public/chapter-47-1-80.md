# Chapter 80

> An Act to provide additional accommodations for the Department of the Interior.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of twenty thousand dollars be, and hereby is, appropriated, out of any moneys in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the purpose of enabling the Secretary of the Interior to rent or lease from year to year a suitable building or buildings for additional accommodations for the Pension Office and Land Office Department of the Interior.


*Approved, April 15, 1882.*

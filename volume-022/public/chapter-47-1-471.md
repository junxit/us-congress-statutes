# Chapter 471

> An Act authorizing the deputy collector of customs stationed at San Juan Island, in the Puget Sound district, to enter and clear vessels and collect duties.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the deputy collector of customs stationed at San Juan Island, in the Puget Sound district, in Washington Territory is, with the approval of the Secretary of the Treasury, empowered to enter and clear vessels and collect duties.


*Approved, August 8, 1882.*

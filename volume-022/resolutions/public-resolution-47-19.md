# Public Resolution 19

> JOINT RESOLUTION providing for the erection of a monument over the grave of Thomas Jefferson.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be and is hereby appropriated out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of ten thousand dollars, or so much thereof as may be necessary, for the erection of a suitable monument and to make other suitable improvements over the grave of Thomas Jefferson, at Monticello, Virginia, and that said sum be expended under the direction of the Secretary of State.


*Approved, April 18, 1882.*

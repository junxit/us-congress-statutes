# Public Resolution 11

> JOINT RESOLUTION to print certain eulogies delivered in Congress upon the late Michael P. O’Connor.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be printed of the eulogies delivered in Congress upon the late Michael P.O’Connor, a member-elect to the Forty-seventh Congress from the State of South Carolina, twelve thousand copies, of which three thousand shall be for the use of the Senate and nine thousand for the use of the House of Representatives; and the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby directed to have printed a portrait of the said Michael P. O’Connor to accompany said eulogies; and for the purpose of engraving and printing said portrait the sum of five hundred dollars, or so much thereof as may be necessary, be, and the same is hereby, appropriated out of any moneys in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 15, 1882.*

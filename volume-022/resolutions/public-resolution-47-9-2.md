# Public Resolution 9

> Joint resolution to admit free of duty a monument to General Washington.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to allow the State Society of the Cincinnati of Pennsylvania to import free of duty a monument, or the parts thereof as they may be completed, proposed to be erected as a memorial of General Washington in Fairmount Park, Philadelphia.


*Approved, February 17, 1883.*

# Public Resolution 12

> Joint resolution to print certain eulogies delivered in Congress upon the lute William M. Lowe.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be printed of the eulogies delivered in Congress upon the late, William M. Lowe, a member of the Forty-seventh Congress, from the State of Alabama, twelve thousand copies, of which three thousand shall be for the use of the Senate, and nine thousand for the use of the House of Representatives; and the Secretary of the Treasury be, and be is hereby directed to have printed a portrait of the said William M. Lowe, to accompany said eulogies; and for the purpose of engraving or printing said portrait, the sum of five hundred dollars, or so much thereof as may be necessary, be, and the same is hereby appropriated, out of any moneys iu the Treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, February 23, 1883.*

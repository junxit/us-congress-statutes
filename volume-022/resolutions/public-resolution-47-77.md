# Public Resolution 77

> JOINT RESOLUTION providing one Month’s extra pay for certain employés of the Senate.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Acting Secretary of the Senate be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to pay to all committee-clerks, pages, laborers and other employés of the Senate who do not now receive annual salaries a sum equal to one Month’s pay at the adjournment of the present session of Congress; and the amount necessary to pay the same is hereby appropriated out of any moneys in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated and shall be immediately available.


*Approved, August 8, 1882.*

# Public Resolution 24

> Joint resolution authorizing the sale of the Congressional Directory and the current numbers of the Congressional Record.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That it shall be lawful for the Public Printer, under the direction of the Joint Committee of the Senate and House of Representatives on Printing, to print for sale, at a price sufficient to reimburse the expenses of such printing, the current Congressional Directory and the current numbers of the Congressional Record. The. money derived from such sales shall be paid into the Treasury monthly to the credit of the appropriation for public printing, and no sales shall be made on credit.


*Approved, March 3, 1883.*

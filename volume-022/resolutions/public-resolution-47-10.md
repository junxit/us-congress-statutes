# Public Resolution 10

> Joint resolution to provide for the binding of the compendium of the Tenth Census.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Public Printer is hereby authorized and directed to bind the compendium of the Tenth Census in two volumes, of as nearly equal size as practicable, together with a complete index in each volume, The volumes of the reports of the Tenth Census assigned to the House of Representatives, shall be distributed upon the orders of the Representatives and Delegates of the Forty seventh Congress, in accordance with “An act to provide for the publication of the Tenth Census” approved August seventh, eighteen hundred and eighty two.


*Approved, February 17, 1883.*

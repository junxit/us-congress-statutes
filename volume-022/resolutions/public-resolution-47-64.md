# Public Resolution 64

> JOINT RESOLUTION providing for the publication at the Government Printing office of certain information in aid of the Society of the Red Cross.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of one thousand dollars, or so much thereof as may be necessary, is hereby appropriated out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to be expended under the direction of the Secretary of State in the printing and publication of necessary information concerning the Societies of the Red Cross for the amelioration of the sufferings of soldiers in time of war and the recent accession of the United States to the Geneva Convention for the organization of said Societies, and giving a brief history of such organizations and the results thereof.


*Approved, August 3, 1882.*

# Public Resolution 21

> JOINT RESOLUTION making an appropriation to enable the Light House Board to meet the extraordinary expenses consequent upon the flood in the Mississippi River.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of ten thousand dollars, be, and the same is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated to enable the Light House Board to pay the extraordinary expenses consequent upon the flood in the Mississippi River, said sum to be immediately available


*Approved, May 1, 1882.*

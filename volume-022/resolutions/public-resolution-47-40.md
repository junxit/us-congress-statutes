# Public Resolution 40

> JOINT RESOLUTION to print Doctor John L. Hayes’ pamphlet upon the husbandry of the Angora goat.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of Agriculture be and hereby is authorized and directed to employ Doctor John L. Hayes to prepare for publication his pamphlet upon the husbandry of the Angora goat and to have ten thousand copies of the same printed for distribution, three thousand by the Senate and seven thousand by the House of Representatives.


*Approved, July 1, 1882.*

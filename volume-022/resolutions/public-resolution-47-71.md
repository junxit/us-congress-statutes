# Public Resolution 71

> JOINT RESOLUTION authorizing and directing the Secretary of the Interior to distribute copies of the Journals of the Senate and House of Representatives to public and law libraries.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be authorized and directed, from the Journals of the Senate and House of Representatives now in his charge, to distribute copies to such public and law libraries, not already supplied with the same, as shall be designated, in equal number, by each Senator, Representative, and Delegate in Congress.


*Approved, August 5, 1882.*

# Public Resolution 29

> Joint resolution to pay the Capitol Police one month’s extra pay.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Clerk of the House be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to all those of the Capitol Police on the roll July first, eighteen hundred and eighty two, one month’s extra pay, at the same compensation now paid them by law; and an amount sufficient to pay the same is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the same to be made immediately available.


*Approved, March 3, 1883.*

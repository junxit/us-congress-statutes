# Public Resolution 68

> JOINT RESOLUTION authorizing the Secretary of War to deliver to the Society of the Fifty-first Regiment Pennsylvania Veteran Volunteers the stand of colors presented to it by citizens of Norristown, Pennsylvania.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to deliver to the Society of the Fifty-first Regiment Pennsylvania Veteran Volunteers the stand of colors presented to said regiment by the citizens of Norristown Pennsylvania, which was captured by the confederate forces on May twelfth, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, at the battle of Spottsylvania, Virginia, and subsequently retaken by the United States troops at the fall of Richmond.


*Approved, August 4, 1882.*

# Public Resolution 25

> Joint resolution providing for additional copies of the Revised Statutes for the use of the Interior Department.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of State be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to deliver to the Secretary of the Interior, for the use of the Department of the Interior and its subordinate bureaus and offices, one hundred copies of the second edition of the Revised Statutes of the United States.


*Approved, March 3, 1883.*

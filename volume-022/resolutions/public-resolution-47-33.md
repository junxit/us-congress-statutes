# Public Resolution 33

> JOINT RESOLUTION extending the fishing season in the Potomac River in the District of Columbia, to the fifteenth of June, for the year eighteen hundred and eighty-two.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the “fishing season” for the catching of shad and herring in the waters of the Potomac River, in the District of Columbia, and the exposing of the same for sale, for the year eighteen hundred and eighty-two, be, and the same is hereby, ex- tended to the fifteenth day of June, subject to the restrictions and provisions of the act of Congress approved April sixth, anno Domini eighteen hundred and eighty, entitled: “An act for protection of the Potomac fisheries in the District of Columbia, and for the preservation of shad and herring in the Potomac River.” And this resolution shall apply to the year eighteen hundred and eighty-two only.


*Approved, June 7, 1882.*

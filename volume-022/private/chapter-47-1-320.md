# Chapter 320

> An Act granting a pension to Martha Westervelt

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Martha Westervelt, mother of William Westervelt, formerly a private in Company K., Third New York Cavalry.


*Approved, July 22, 1882.*

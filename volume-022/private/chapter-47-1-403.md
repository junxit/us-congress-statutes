# Chapter 403

> AN ACT for the relief of James Burke.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of one hundred and eighty dollars and forty cents be, and the same is hereby appropriated, out of any moneys in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to enable the Secretary of War to reimburse to James Burke, superintendent of the national cemetery at Salisbury, North Carolina, the amount of a judgment, costs, and disbursements, which judgment was obtained against said Burke in the circuit court of Pulaski County, Kentucky, at a suit of William H. Logan, for an alleged trespass by said Burke, committed while in discharge of his duty as said superintendent, which said Burke defended, but was by said court condemned in damages and costs for the aforesaid sum of one hundred and eighty dollars and forty cents.


*Approved, August 5, 1882.*

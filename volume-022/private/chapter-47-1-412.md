# Chapter 412

> AN ACT granting a pension to Ann Leddy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a pension be, and the same is hereby, granted to Ann Leddy, widow of Thomas Leddy, late of Company B., Sixty-ninth New York Volunteers, United States Army, subject to the rules of the office of the Commissioner of Pensions adopted in conformity with the laws.


*Approved, August 5, 1882.*

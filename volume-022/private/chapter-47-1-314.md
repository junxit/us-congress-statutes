# Chapter 314

> An Act granting a pension to Jacob. R. McFarren.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions* and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jacob R. McFarren, late a private in Company F, of the Eighty-sixth Regiment of Illinois Volunteers.


*Approved, July 22, 1882.*

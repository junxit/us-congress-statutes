# Chapter 33

> An Act for the relief of Paulina Jones, widow of Alexander Jones, deceased, late of Company E, Second North Carolina Infantry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War is hereby authorized and directed to place the name of Alexander Jones upon the rolls of Company E, Second North Carolina Mounted Infantry to date September first-eighteen hundred and sixty-three.


*Approved, March 15, 1882.*

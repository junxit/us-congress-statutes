# Chapter 143

> An Act granting a pension to Sally Hall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Sally Hall, of South New Market, New Hampshire, widow of Andrew Hall, late it private in Captain Hersey’s company of New Hampshire Militia in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve, to take effect from the time of the filing of her application for a pension, the same being numbered one hundred and eighty-six thousand five hundred and sixteen, but in other respects subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, May 11, 1882.*

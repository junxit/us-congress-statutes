# Chapter 34

> An Act for the relief of Charles Collins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of three hundred and thirty-seven dollars and thirty-six cents be, and hereby is, appro- priated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to reimburse Charles Collins, postmaster at Berlin, Worcester County, Maryland, for stamps stolen from the post office at that place on the night of November twenty-third, eighteen hundred and seventy-seven.


*Approved, March 15, 1882.*

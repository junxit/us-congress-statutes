# Chapter 83

> An act to rerate the pension of Frank S. Sowers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to rerate the pension of Frank S. Sowers, formerly a first lieutenant in the Third Regiment of Ohio Cavalry Volunteers; the said rerating to be made according to the discretion of the Secretary of the Interior.


*Approved, March 2, 1883.*

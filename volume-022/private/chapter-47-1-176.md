# Chapter 176

> An Act for the relief of Thomas J. Wharton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of two hundred and fifty dollars be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any moneys in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the benefit of Thomas J. Wharton, of Jackson, Mississippi, as compensation for certain professional services rendered by said Wharton in the southern district of Mississippi, under appointment of the then district attorney of the United States for the southern district of Mississippi, in accordance with the statute in such case made and provided.

## § 2.

That this act be in force from and after its passage.


*Approved, May 23, 1882.*

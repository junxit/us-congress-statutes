# Chapter 251

> An Act granting an increase of pension to George Gans.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of George Gans, late a sergeant of Company D, Thirty-sixth Wisconsin Volunteers, so as to pay him twenty-four dollars per month instead of eighteen dollars, as at present, to commence from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 27, 1882.*

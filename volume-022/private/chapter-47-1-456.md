# Chapter 456

> AN ACT increasing the pension of John F. Ellis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of John F. Ellis, late of Company H. eightieth Ohio Volunteers, to thirty-six dollars per month, from and after the passage of this act, certificate numbered one hundred and eight thousand, four hundred and, sixty-six.


*Approved, August 7, 1882.*

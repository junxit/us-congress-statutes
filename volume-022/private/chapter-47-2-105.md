# Chapter 105

> An act granting a pension to Daniel M. Morley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Daniel M Morley, late a private in Company E. Twenty-ninth Regiment Ohio Volunteers.


*Approved, March 3, 1883.*

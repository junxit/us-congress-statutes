# Chapter 164

> An Act for the relief of Louisa Bainbridge Hoff.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the passage of this act there be paid, out of the Navy pension fund, to Mrs. Louisa Bainbridge Hoff, widow of the late Rear-Admiral Henry K. Hoff, United States Navy, the sum of fifty dollars per mouth during her widowhood, the same to be in lieu of her present pension.


*Approved, May 17, 1882.*

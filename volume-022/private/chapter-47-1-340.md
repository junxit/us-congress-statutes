# Chapter 340

> An Act to restore the name of Eliza M. Bass to the pension-roll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to restore to the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Eliza M. Bass, of Fort Wayne, Indiana, widow of Sion S. Bass, late Colonel of the Thirtieth Regiment of Indiana Volunteers, from and after the passage of this act. -


*Approved, July 22, 1882.*

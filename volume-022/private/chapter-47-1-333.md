# Chapter 333

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Lizzie M. Mitchell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Lizzie M. Mitchell, widow of John Mitchell, deceased, late a captain in the United States Army, who died of wounds received and disease contracted while in the service.


*Approved, July 22, 1882.*

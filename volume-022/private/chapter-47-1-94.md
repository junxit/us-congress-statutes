# Chapter 94

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Rosetta L. McKay.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Rosetta L. McKay, mother of Fred. A. McKay, who was second lieutenant of Company E., Forty-first Regiment Ohio Volunteers, and who died December sixth, eighteen hundred and seventy-one, from disease contracted in the line of duty in the military service of the United States.


*Approved, April 25, 1882.*

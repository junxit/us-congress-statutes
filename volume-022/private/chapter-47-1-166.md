# Chapter 166

> An Act granting a pension to Earl S. Rathbun.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Earl 8. Rathbun, said pension to be at the rate of eight dollars per month, from and after the date of the passage of this act, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, May 17, 1882.*

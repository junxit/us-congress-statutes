# Chapter 417

> AN ACT to remove the political disabilities of Frank C. Armstrong of Maryland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two thirds of each House concurring therein,),*

That all the political disabilities imposed upon Frank 0. Armstrong of Maryland, by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States, by reason of his participation in the rebellion; are hereby removed.


*Approved, August 5, 1882.*

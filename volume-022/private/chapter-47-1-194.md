# Chapter 194

> An Act authorizing Samuel W. Dabney, United States consul at Fayal, to accept a gold medal from the French Republic.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Samuel W. Dabney, United States consul at Fayai, be, and is hereby, authorized to accept from the President of the French Republic a life-saving testimonial gold medal for gallantry in rescuing four seamen of the.French bark Jacques Couer, wrecked at Fayai on the night of November thirtieth, eighteen hundred and eighty.


*Approved, May 26, 1882.*

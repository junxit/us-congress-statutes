# Chapter 229

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Rebecca Reynolds.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, to increase the pension of Rebecca Reynolds, widow of the late Rear-Admiral William Reynolds, from thirty dollars to fifty dollar’s a month, said increase to take effect from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 16, 1882.*

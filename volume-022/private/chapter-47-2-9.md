# Chapter 9

> An act granting a pension to George Foster

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George Foster, late a private in Company C, Tenth United States Cavalry.


*Approved, December 27, 1882.*

# Chapter 335

> An Act granting a pension to Betty Taylor Dandridge.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Betty Taylor Dandridge, daughter of the late General Zachary Taylor, and widow of William W. S. Bliss, late a lieu ten ant colonel in the Army of the United States, and to pay to her the sum of fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, July 22, 1882.*

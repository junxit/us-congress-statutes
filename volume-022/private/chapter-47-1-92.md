# Chapter 92

> An Act granting a pension to George Andrews.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George Andrews, late a private in the Sixth Maine Battery of Artillery in the late war of the rebellion ; this act to take effect and be in force from and after its passage.


*Approved, April 25, 1882.*

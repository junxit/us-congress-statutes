# Chapter 201

> An Act granting a pension to Frank Kitzmiller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representative^ of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Frank Kitzmiller, late a corporal of Company II, Thirteenth Regiment Kansas Volunteers.


*Approved, June 10, 1882.*

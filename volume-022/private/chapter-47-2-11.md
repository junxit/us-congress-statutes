# Chapter 11

> An act to remove the political disabilities of James. I. Waddell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all political disabilities imposed upon James. I. Waddell by the Fourteenth Amendment to the Constitution of the United States, be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, January 5, 1883.*

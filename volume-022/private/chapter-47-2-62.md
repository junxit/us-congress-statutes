# Chapter 62

> An act referring to the Court of Claims the claim of Gallus Kirchner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the claim of Gallus Kirchner, of the State of Indiana, as specified in his memorial presented to the Senate at the Forty-sixth Congress, for stone supplied to the United States at Indianapolis, in said State, be, and the same is hereby, referred to the Court of Claims, to hear, try, and determine the same according to law and equity, as if the said claim were not barred by the statute of limitations, or any former judgment of said court in this case, and to render judgment for the fair and reasonable value of the stone used by the United States, if any: Provided, That no judgment shall be rendered for any such stone used in the construction of the Main Arsenal Building.


*Approved, March first, 1883.*

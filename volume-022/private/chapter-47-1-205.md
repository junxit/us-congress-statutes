# Chapter 205

> An Act granting a pension to Mary Blowers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized to place on the pension-roll the name of Mary Blowers, widow of James Blowers, late a private in Battery B, First Regiment of West Virginia Volunteer Artillery.


*Approved, June 10, 1882.*

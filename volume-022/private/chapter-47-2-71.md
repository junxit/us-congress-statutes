# Chapter 71

> An act granting a pension to D. D. Edwards.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of D. D. Edwards, late a private in Company G of the Seventh Regiment of Ohio Volunteer Cavalry.


*Approved, March 2, 1883.*

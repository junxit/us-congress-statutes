# Chapter 341

> An Act granting a pension to Amelia Ann Wilson and her minor child.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Amelia Ann Wilson, widow of the late Marcellus Wilson, who was a private in the war with Mexico, and pay her a pension of eight dollars per month, and two dollars per month for her daughter Alice A. Wilson, until she arrives at the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, July 22, 1882.*

# Chapter 66

> An Act granting a pension to P. B. Perry, senior.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of P. B. Perry, senior, a soldier of the Mexican war.


*Approved, April 1, 1882.*

# Chapter 135

> An Act granting a pension to Francis Reichert.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Francis Reichert, late of Company 1, Fifth Missouri Volunteers, and Company K, Fourteenth Illinois Volunteers.


*Approved, May 11, 1882.*

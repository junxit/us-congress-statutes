# Chapter 155

> An act granting a pension to Clara Wible

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Clara Wible, widow of John M. Wible, deceased on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, March 3, 1883.*

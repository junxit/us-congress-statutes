# Chapter 175

> An Act for the relief of Mrs Almira Farnsworth

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie hereby is, authorized and directed to put the name of Mrs Almira Farnsworth dependent mother of Edward Farnsworth, late a private in Company E, Twentieth Indiana Volunteer Infantry, on the pension-rolls, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, May 23, 1882.*

# Chapter 102

> An Act granting an increase of pension to John F. Chase.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension of twenty-four dollars a month now paid to John F. Chase, late a private in the Fifth Maine Battery, be increased to thirty-six dollars a month, such increase to commence from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, April 25, 1882.*

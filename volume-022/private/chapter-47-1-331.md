# Chapter 331

> An Act for the relief of Dicey Bobbitt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,'That,*

the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll at the rate of eight dollars per month, and to continue at that rate during her life, the name of Dicey Bobbitt, widow of Robert Bobbitt, deceased, who was a member of Captain Benjamin Cooley’s company of Virginia Militia Cavalry in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve.


*Approved, July 22, 1882.*

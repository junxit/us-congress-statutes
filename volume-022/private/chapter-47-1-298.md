# Chapter 298

> An Act granting a pension to Emma A. Ramsey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws the name of Emma A. Ramsey, widow of J. Allen Ramsey, late surgeon of the one hundred and twenty first Regiment of Pennsylvania Volunteers, to take effect from the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 15, 1882.*

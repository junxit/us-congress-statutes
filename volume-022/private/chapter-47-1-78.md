# Chapter 78

> An Act for the relief of Peter Gallagher.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of tie United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of eighty-nine dollars and fifty-seven cents to Peter Gallagher of Washington, District of Columbia, amount due him in consequence of joint resolution approved February twenty-eighth, eighteen hundred and. sixty-seven.


*Approved, April 11, 1882.*

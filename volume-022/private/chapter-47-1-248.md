# Chapter 248

> An Act granting a pension to Alice McMahon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Alice McMahon, widow of the late Private Daniel McMahon, of Company L, Second New York Artillery Volunteers.


*Approved, June 27, 1882.*

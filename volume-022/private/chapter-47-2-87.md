# Chapter 87

> An act granting a pension to George C. Rust.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of George C. Rust, of Weakley County, Tennessee, be, and the same is hereby, placed on the pension-roll of the soldiers of the war of eighteen hundred and twelve, at the rate allowed by law to the private soldiers of said war.


*Approved, March 2, 1883.*

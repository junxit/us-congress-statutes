# Chapter 72

> An act granting a pension to James B. White.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of James B. White, late a private in Company B, Sixty-second Regiment of Ohio Volunteers, on the invalid pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws of the United States.


*Approved, March 2, 1883.*

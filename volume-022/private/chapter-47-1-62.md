# Chapter 62

> An Act for the relief of Cyrus C. Clark.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the accounting officers of the Treasury shall allow to Cyrus C. Clark, late an additional paymaster in the Army, in the settlement of his account, a credit for the sum of fifteen thousand nine hundred and seventy-nine dollars and eighty-seven cents, as of the date of April sixth, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, the same being for money lost by him in the discharge of his official duty, without fault or neglect on his part, as decided heretofore by the Court of Claims.


*Approved, April 1, 1882.*

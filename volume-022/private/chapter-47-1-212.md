# Chapter 212

> An Act granting a pension to Mary C. Murray.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll. subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary C. Murray, widow of Lieutenant-Colonel Edward Murray, late of the Fifth New York Heavy Artillery.


*Approved, June 10, 1882.*

# Chapter 461

> AN ACT for the relief of Major W. R. King.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Howie of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the accounting officers of the Treasury are hereby authorized to pass to the official credit of Major W. Li. King, disbursing officer for the Tennessee River improvement, the sum of three thousand nine hundred and seventy dollars, and eighteen cents, that being the unrecovered balance of five thousand two hundred and forty dollars and eighteen cents of public funds forcibly taken from A. G. Smith, receiver of materials, on Muscle Shoals Canal, in the State of Alabama, by William Ryan and two other armed robbers, on the eleventh day of March, eighteen hundred and eighty-one and for which sum the said W. R. King is accountable.


*Approved, August 7, 1882.*

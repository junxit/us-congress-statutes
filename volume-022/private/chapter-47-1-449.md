# Chapter 449

> AN ACT for the relief of Joseph Conrad, of Missouri.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in view of the long and faithful services of Captain and Brevet Colonel Joseph Conrad, of the Eleventh Infantry, his total disability by reason of gunshot wounds received in the line of duty and action, while holding the commission of a colonel at the, time he was wounded, the President be, and he is hereby, authorized to place that officer on the retired list of the Army as a colonel, with the pay and emoluments of a retired officer of that grade.


*Approved, August 7, 1882.*

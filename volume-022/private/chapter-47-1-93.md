# Chapter 93

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Charles H. Ordway.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Blouse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Charles II. Ordway, late a private in Company H, Seventh Regiment New Hampshire Volunteer Infantry, at the rate of thirty-six dollars per month, in lieu of the pension lie is now receiving.


*Approved, April 25, 1882.*

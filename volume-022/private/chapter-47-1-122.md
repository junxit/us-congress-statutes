# Chapter 122

> An Act for the relief of Chaplain M. J. Kelly and others.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the Treasury of the United States be, and are hereby, authorized and directed to settle with M. J. Kelly, late a chaplain in the Unit d States Army, and with all commissioned and non-commissioned officers and privates of Company “B” of the Twentieth Infantry Regiment of the United States army who lost their personal effects by fire at Fort Ripley, Minnesota, on the fourteenth day of January, eighteen hundred and seventy seven, at a sum not to exceed in the aggregate, seven hundred and nine dollars and thirty-four cents, and a sufficient sum therefor is hereby appropriated out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, May 4, 1882.*

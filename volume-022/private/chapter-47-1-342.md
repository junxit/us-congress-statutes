# Chapter 342

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Bernard Brady.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Bernard Brady, formerly a private in Company 1, Fourth Regiment United States Infantry, to the sum of fifty dollars per month, for the loss of left leg and part of right foot.


*Approved, July 24, 1882.*

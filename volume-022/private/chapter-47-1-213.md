# Chapter 213

> An Act granting a pension to Lewis Lewis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll. subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lewis Lewis, late a private in Company F, Seventy-eighth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers.


*Approved, June 10, 1882.*

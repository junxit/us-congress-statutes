# Chapter 330

> An Act granting an increase of pension to George J. Webb.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension Jaws, the name of George J. Webb, late of the One hundredth New York Volunteers, for increase of pension to twenty dollars per month, such increase to commence from the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 22, 1882.*

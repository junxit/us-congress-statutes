# Chapter 329

> An Act for the relief of John H. Jackson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of John H. 'Jackson, formerly of Company G, One hundred and forty-ninth Regiment Indiana State Volunteers.


*Approved, July 22, 1882.*

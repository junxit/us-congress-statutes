# Chapter 137

> An Act for the relief of William H. Hill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William II. Hill, late a lieutenant in the one hundred and tenth Regiment of Pennsylvania Infantry Volunteers, at the rate of thirty-six dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, May 11, 1882.*

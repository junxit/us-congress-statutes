# Chapter 22

> An act granting a pension to John V. Bovell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and ho is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John V. Bovell, late captain Company C One hundred and fiftieth Regiment Illinois Infantry Volunteers


*Approved, January 11, 1883.*

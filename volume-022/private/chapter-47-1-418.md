# Chapter 418

> AN ACT for the relief of Elizabeth Leebrick

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Elizabeth Leebrick shall have her name placed upon the pension roll by the Secretary of the Interior, for her service as nurse in the Army of the late war, at the rate of sixteen dollars per month.


*Approved, August 5, 1882.*

# Chapter 306

> An Act granting a pension to Ellen Lardner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ellen Lardner, widow of the late Rear-Admiral James L Lardner, United States Navy, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars a month.


*Approved, July 15, 1882.*

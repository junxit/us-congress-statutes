# Chapter 297

> An Act for the relief of Rebecca Wright, widow of James Wright, a soldier in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to put the name of Rebecca Wright, widow of James Wright, who was a private in Captain Hosier's company, war of eighteen hundred and twelve, on the pension rolls, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, July 15, 1882.*

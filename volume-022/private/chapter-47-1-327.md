# Chapter 327

> An Act to increase the pension of Elijah W. Penny.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Elijah W. Penny, late lieutenant-colonel of the One hundred and thirtieth Regiment of Indiana Volunteers to the sum of thirty-six dollars per month, said increase to take effect from the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 22, 1882.*

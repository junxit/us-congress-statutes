# Chapter 305

> An Act granting a pension to George C. Quick.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George C. Quick, who served in Captain Solomon Miller’s company of Mounted Bangers in the Black Hawk war.


*Approved, July 15, 1882.*

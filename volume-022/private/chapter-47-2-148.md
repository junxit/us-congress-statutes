# Chapter 148

> An act granting a pension to Mrs. Elizabeth A. Hendrickson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Mrs. Elizabeth A. Hendrickson, widow or Thomas Hendrickson, late a retired colonel in the United States Army, and to pay her at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month from and after the passage of this act as other pensions are paid.


*Approved, March 3, 1883.*

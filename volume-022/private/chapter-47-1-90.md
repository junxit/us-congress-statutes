# Chapter 90

> An Act granting a pension to Ann Atkinson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ann Atkinson, widow of Hopeful Toler, a soldier in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve to eighteen hundred and fifteen.


*Approved, April 25, 1882.*

# Chapter 96

> An Act granting a pension to Susan R. Johnson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Susan R. Johnson, mother by adoption of George Davis, late a private in Company G. Twenty-eighth Regiment of Pennsylvania Volunteers


*Approved, April 25, 1882.*

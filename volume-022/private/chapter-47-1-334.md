# Chapter 334

> An Act for the relief of Robert P. Walker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and instructed to restore to the pension roll the name of Robert P. Walker, late of Company n, Ninety-fourth Regiment Ohio Volunteers.


*Approved, July 22, 1882.*

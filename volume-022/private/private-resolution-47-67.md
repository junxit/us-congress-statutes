# Private Resolution 67

> Joint resolution authorizing the Secretary of War to loan tents to the Washington Light Infantry Corps.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized to loan to the Washington Light Infantry Corps of the District of Columbia, if they can be conveniently spared, seventy tents to be used by them for encampment purposes during the month of August eighteen hundred and eighty-two; Provided, That ample security be given to the Secretary of War, to be judged of by him, that the said tents shall be returned in good condition immediately’ after their use as aforesaid.


*Approved, August 3, 1882.*

# Chapter 387

> AN ACT for the relief of Ella Carroll, formerly Ella Long.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all the right, title, and interest of the United States in and to the following described real estate, situate, being,’ and lying in the city of Washington and District of Columbia, known and designated on the public plat or plan of said city as lot numbered three, in square numbered five hundred and thirty, be, and the same hereby are, granted and conveyed to Ella Carroll, formerly Ella Long, illegitimate child of Daniel Long, deceased, her heirs and assigns forever.


*Approved, August 4, 1882.*

# Chapter 38

> An Act granting a pension to Olive Stephenson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Olive Stephenson, widow of Caleb Stephenson, a soldier of the war of eighteen hundred and twelve, and to pay and allow to her the pension allowed by law to the widows of such soldiers.


*Approved, March 15, 1882.*

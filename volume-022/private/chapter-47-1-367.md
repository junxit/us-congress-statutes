# Chapter 367

> AN ACT for the relief of Patrick Sullivan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension from eighteen to twenty-four dollars per month of Patrick Sullivan, late of Company K, eighty second Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, August 1, 1882.*

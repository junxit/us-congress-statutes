# Chapter 82

> An act providing for the pay of Rear-Admiral Roger N. Stembel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Rear-Admiral Roger N. Stembel, United States Navy, be paid, out of any unexpended moneys in the Treasury, the pay and compensation of a rear-admiral on the retired-list from and after June fifth, eighteen hundred and seventy-four, that being the date of his promotion to the retired-list as a rear-admiral.


*Approved, March 2, 1883.*

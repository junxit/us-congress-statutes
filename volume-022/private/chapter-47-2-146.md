# Chapter 146

> An act for the relief of the German National Bank of Louisville Kentucky.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid out of the Treasury of the United States, out of any moneys not otherwise appropriated, the sum of three hundred and thirteen dollars and fifty cents to the German National Bank of Louisville, Kentucky, being the amount of United States five-twenty coupons inclosed in a registered letter and destroyed while in custody of the postal authorities: Provided, however, That a satisfactory bond of indemnity shall be filed with the Secretary of the Treasury before said payment shall be made.


*Approved, March 3, 1883.*

# Chapter 317

> An Act increasing the pension of Emma H. Collins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Emma II. Collins, widow of Frederick Collins, late a Lieutenant in the United States Navy, a pension at the rate of forty dollars a month during her widowhood, and from the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 22, 1882.*

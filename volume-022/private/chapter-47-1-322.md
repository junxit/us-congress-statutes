# Chapter 322

> An Act granting a pension to Elizabeth Vernor Henry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America hi Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elizabeth Vernor Henry, orphan sister of the late Commander Edmund W. Henry, of the United States Navy, and to pay her a pension at the rate of twenty-live dollars per month.


*Approved, July 22, 1882.*

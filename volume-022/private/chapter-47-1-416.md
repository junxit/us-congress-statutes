# Chapter 416

> AN ACT granting a pension to Mary E. Matthews.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary E. Matthews, widow of Edward S. Matthews, late a surgeon in the United States Navy, having the rank of lieutenant-commander.


*Approved, August 5, 1882.*

# Chapter 163

> An act for the relief of C. H. Miller

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury, under the regulations of the Treasury Department, be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to O. H. Miller the sum of three hundred and eighteen dollars, being the amount of three Oregon war bonds, numbered eight hundred and sixty, eight hundred and sixty one, and eight hundred and sixty two, for one hundred dollars each, and the accrued interest, purchased by him of Patrick Miller, of Portland, Oregon, in eighteen hundred and seventy, as to which there has been an alleged defective assignment


*Approved, March 3, 1883.*

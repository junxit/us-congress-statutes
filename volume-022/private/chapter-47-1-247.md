# Chapter 247

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Rose M. Wood.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Rose M. Wood, widow of William Maxwell Wood, late Surgeon-General United States Navy, who died in the naval service, and pay her a pension of fifty dollars per month from and after the passage of this act, out of the naval pension fund.


*Approved, June 27, 1882.*

# Chapter 74

> An act granting a pension to Esther M. Carey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Esther M. Carey, mother of William B. Carey, deceased, late a private in Company H. Twelfth Regiment Ohio Volunteers, war of eighteen hundred and sixty-one.


*Approved, March 2, 1883.*

# Chapter 211

> An Act for the relief of Charles H. Frank

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Charles H. Frank to thirty-six dollars a month, to date from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 10, 1882.*

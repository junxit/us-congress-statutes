# Chapter 134

> An Act granting a pension to David G. Hutchinson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of David G. Hutchinson, of Lincoln, in the State of Maine, dependent father of William H. Hutchinson, late a corporal of Company E, Seventeenth United States Infantry.


*Approved, May 11, 1882.*

# Private Resolution 60

> Joint resolution for the relief of Sarah J. S. Garnet, widow of Henry H. Garnet, late minister to Liberia.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to be paid to Sarah J. S. Garnet, widow of Dr. Henry H. Garnet, late minister of the United States to Liberia, one year’s salary as said minister in addition to all legal allowances, deducting the amount of salary by said Henry H. Garnet, since the date of his appointment.


*Approved, August 1, 1882.*

# Chapter 408

> AN ACT granting a pension to Sarah Shea.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah Shea, widow of William Shea, late a private in Company A. Second Regiment United States Maryland Volunteers.


*Approved, August 5, 1882.*

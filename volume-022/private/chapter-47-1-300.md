# Chapter 300

> An Act for the relief of Ellen Moore, widow of Allen Moore, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That tho Secretary of the Interior be authorized to inscribe upon the pension rolls the name of Ellen Moore, widow of Allen Moore, deceased, of Company C. Palmetto Regiment, South Carolina Volunteers, Mexican war, and pay her a pension from and after the passage of this act at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, July 15, 1882.*

# Chapter 228

> An Act granting a pension to William H. H. Anderson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,That the.,*

Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William H. H. Anderson, late a private in Company B. First Indiana Heavy Artillery Volunteers.


*Approved, June 16, 1882.*

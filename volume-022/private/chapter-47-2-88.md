# Chapter 88

> An act granting a pension to Kate Quilligan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, the name of Kate Quilligan, daughter of Patrick Quilligan, late of Company B, Fourth United States Cavalry, and to pay her a pension, through her legally constituted guardian, at the rate of eight dollars per month, during the insanity of said Kate Quilligan


*Approved, March 2, 1883.*

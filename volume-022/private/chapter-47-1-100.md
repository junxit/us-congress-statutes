# Chapter 100

> An Act granting a pension to Elizabeth S. M. Finley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Elizabeth S. M. Finley, widow of Clement A. Finley, late Surgeon-General of the United States Army, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, April 25, 1882.*

# Chapter 584

> AN ACT For the relief of Agnes Tarjani.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purpose of the immigration and naturalization laws Agnes Tarjani, a native of Hungary, shall be considered the natural-born daughter of her adoptive parents, Mr. and Mrs. Dezidir Ziegler, citizens of the United States.


*Approved September 19, 1949.*

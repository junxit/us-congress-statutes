# Chapter 56

> AN ACT For the relief of Charles L. Bishop.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General is authorized and directed (1) to cancel all claims against Charles L. Bishop for payment of the sum of $350 representing the aggregate amount of money orders erroneously paid by the said Charles L. Bishop in 1944 while acting as assistant Navy mail clerk at the naval air station, Alameda, California, to a person then in the armed forces of the United States who had stolen such money orders and fraudulently endorsed them in his own favor; and (2) to pay to the said Charles L. Bishop, out of any money available for the pay of employees in the postal service, an amount equal to any portion of such sum heretofore paid by him pursuant to such claims.


*Approved April 14, 1949.*

# Chapter 750

> AN ACT To authorize the sale of certain allotted inherited land on the Pine Ridge Reservation, South Dakota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to sell the trust allotment numbered 645 of Returns Warrior, deceased Pine Ridge allottee, described as the south half of section 2, township 37 north, range 41 west, sixth principal meridian, South Dakota, containing three hundred and twenty acres, conveyance to be made by deed or the issuance of a patent in fee to the purchaser and to disburse the proceeds of such sale to Jessie American Horse for her benefit.


*Approved October 26, 1949.*

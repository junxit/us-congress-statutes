# Chapter 482

> AN ACT For the relief of J. W. Greenwood, Junior.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to credit the accounts of J. W. Greenwood, Junior, former regional fiscal officer for the Central Administrative Services Division, Office for Emergency Management, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, and he is hereby relieved from any liability to refund or pay to the United States the sum of $615.41, such sum being vouchers issued to enlisted personnel for travel expenses at the same rate as commissioned officers, whereas they were entitled to a lower rate under the Army regulations, and for other purposes.


*Approved August 18, 1949.*

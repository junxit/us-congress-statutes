# Chapter 373

> AN ACT For the relief of Rudolf A. V. Raff.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration laws Rudolf A. V. Raff shall, upon application at a port of entry of the United States, be admitted for permanent residence without an immigration visa, provided he meets all the other requirements of the immigration laws. Upon his admission in to the United States, the Secretary of State shall deduct one number from the quota for Austria for the year in which the admission occurs or from such quota for the first succeeding year.


*Approved August 1, 1949.*

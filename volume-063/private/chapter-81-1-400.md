# Chapter 400

> AN ACT To record the lawful admission to the United States for permanent residence of Karl Frederick Kucker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Attorney General be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to record the lawful admission for permanent residence of Karl Frederick Kucker, who entered the United States at New York, in April 1925, and that he shall, for all purposes under the immigration and naturalization laws, be deemed to have been lawfully admitted for permanent residence only as of that date and nullifying and canceling all other departures and entries which he might have made thereafter.


*Approved August 4, 1949.*

# Chapter 205

> AN ACT Authorizing the Secretary of the Interior to issue a patent in fee to Clarence M. Scott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is authorized and directed to issue a patent in fee to Clarence M. Scott for the following-described lands in the State of Montana: Lots 3, 4, 5, 6, 11, 12, 13, 14 in section 2, and lots 1, 2, 7, 8, 9, 16 in section 3, township 3 south, range 28 east, Montana principal meridian, and containing four hundred and forty and fifty-two one-hundredths acres.


*Approved June 14, 1949.*

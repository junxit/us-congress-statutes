# Chapter 345

> AN ACT For the relief of Colonel Wlodzimierz Onacewicz.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws Wlodzimierz Onacewicz, colonel, Polish Army, retired, who was awarded by the President of the United States the Legion of Merit, degree of officer, shall be deemed to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of April 29, 1941, the date on which he was admitted to the United States as the military attaché of the Polish Embassy.


*Approved July 18, 1949.*

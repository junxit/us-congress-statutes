# Chapter 748

> AN ACT For the relief of George Seeman Jensen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, George Seeman Jensen, a native of Denmark, shall be admitted into the United States upon application made notwithstanding the provisions of the Act of March 4, 1929, relating to entry after deportation (8 U. S. C. 180).


*Approved October 26, 1949.*

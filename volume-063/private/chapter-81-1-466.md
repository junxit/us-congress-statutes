# Chapter 466

> AN ACT For the relief of Louis Esposito.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Attorney General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to cancel the outstanding order and warrant of deportation in the case of Louis Esposito, any provision of existing law to the contrary notwithstanding. From and after the date of the approval of this Act, Louis Esposito shall not again be subject to deportation by reason of the same facts upon which the present outstanding proceedings rest.


*Approved August 17, 1949.*

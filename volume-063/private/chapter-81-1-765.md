# Chapter 765

> AN ACT To provide for the advancement of commissioned Warrant Officer Chester A. Davis, United States Marine Corps (retired) to the rank of lieutenant colonel on the retired list.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That commissioned Warrant Officer Chester A. Davis, United States Marine Corps (retired), is hereby advanced on the retired list of the United States Marine Corps to the grade of lieutenant colonel, the highest grade held by him during World War I: Provided, That no increase of active or retired pay or allowances shall result from the passage of this Act.


*Approved October 27, 1949.*

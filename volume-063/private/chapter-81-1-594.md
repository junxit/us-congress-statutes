# Chapter 594

> AN ACT For the relief of Anthony Charles Bartley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Attorney General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to record the lawful admission for permanent residence of Anthony Charles Bartley as of September 1, 1948, the date of his lawful admission into the United States.

Upon the enactment of this Act the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the quota for India of the first year that such quota number is available.


*Approved October 5, 1949.*

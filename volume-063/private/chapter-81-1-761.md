# Chapter 761

> AN ACT To legalize the admission into the United States of Edmea Pacho.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Attorney General is hereby directed to record the entry into the United States of Edmea Pacho at New York, New York, on November 5, 1947, as a lawful admission to the United States for permanent residence for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws. The Secretary of State shall thereupon reduce by one the immigration quota for Italy for the first year that such quota may be available.


*Approved October 26, 1949.*

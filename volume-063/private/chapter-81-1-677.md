# Chapter 677

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Hilda De Silva.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions relating to illiteracy in section 3 of the Immigration Act of 1917, as amended, Mrs. Hilda De Silva, of Bermuda, four of whose children are residents and citizens of the United States, may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence, if she is otherwise admissible under the immigration laws.


*Approved October 11, 1949.*

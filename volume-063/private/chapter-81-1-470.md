# Chapter 470

> AN ACT For the relief of John B. Boyle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of the eleventh category of section 3 of the Immigration Act of 1917, as amended (39 Stat. 875–878; 8 U. S. C. 136). John B. Boyle, a native of Canada, may be admitted into the United States for permanent residence if he is found otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved August 17, 1949.*

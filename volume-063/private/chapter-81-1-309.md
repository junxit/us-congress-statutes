# Chapter 309

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Sonia Kaye Johnston.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws the provision of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, which excludes from the United States persons who are ineligible to citizenship, shall not hereafter apply to Mrs. Sonia Kaye Johnston, a native of England and citizen of Japan, now residing in London, England, and who is the wife of Thomas F. Johnston, a citizen of the United States. If otherwise admissible under the immigration laws, said Mrs. Sonia Kaye Johnston shall be granted the status of a nonquota immigrant.


*Approved July 13, 1949.*

# Chapter 368

> AN ACT To provide for the admission to, and the permanent residence in, the United States of Poon Lim.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the Immigration and Naturalization laws Poon Lim shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted into the United States for permanent residence on November 29, 1943, the date upon which he was temporarily admitted into the United States, upon the payment by him of the visa fee and head tax. Upon the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the appropriate quota for the first year in which such quota is available.


*Approved July 27, 1949.*

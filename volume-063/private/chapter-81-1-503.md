# Chapter 503

> AN ACT For the relief of William (Vasilios) Kotsakis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of immigration and naturalization laws, William (Vasilios) Kotsakis shall be considered to be the natural-born son of Mr. and Mrs. Nick Canelis, of Billings, Montana, citizens of the United States.


*Approved August 24, 1949.*

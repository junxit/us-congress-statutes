# Chapter 115

> AN ACT For the relief of Ralph Martin Elzingre, also known as Ralph Seawell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, the alien Ralph Martin Elzingre, also known as Ralph Seawell, of Mill Valley, Marin County, California, shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted at San Pedro, California, on May 2, 1945, to the United States for permanent residence, upon payment of the visa fee and head tax.

## § 2.

Upon the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of State shall authorize the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the nonpreference category of the first available quota for nationals of the Philippine Islands.


*Approved May 14, 1949.*

# Chapter 684

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Toshiko Keyser.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, Mrs. Toshiko Keyser, a Canadian citizen of Japanese descent, who is the spouse of Donald E. Keyser, a United States citizen and an honorably discharged veteran of World War II, shall be admitted to the United States for permanent residence upon application hereafter filed if she is otherwise admissible under the immigration laws.


*Approved October 12, 1949.*

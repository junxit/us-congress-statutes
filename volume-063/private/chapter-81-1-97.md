# Chapter 97

> AN ACT Conferring United States citizenship posthumously upon Vaso B. Benderach.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Vaso B. Benderach, a Yugoslav flier, who served honorably with the Fifteenth Air Force of the United States, and who died on May 7, 1948, while a patient at Fitzsimons General Hospital, Colorado, shall be held and considered to have been a citizen of the United States at the time of his death.


*Approved May 10, 1949.*

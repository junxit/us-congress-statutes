# Chapter 573

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Justa G. Vda. de Guido, Belen de Guido, Mulia de Guido, and Oscar de Guido.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws the Attorney General is hereby authorized and directed to record the last lawful admission into the United States of Mrs. Justa G. Vda. de Guido, Belen de Guido, Mulia de Guido, and Oscar de Guido, as a lawful admission for permanent residence.

## § 2.

Upon the enactment of this Act, Mrs. Justa G. Vda. de Guido and Mulia de Guido shall be held to be subject to the provisions of section 4 (b) of the Immigration Act of May 26, 1924, as amended; and the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct four numbers from the quota for the Philippine Islands of the first year that such quota numbers are available.


*Approved September 7, 1949.*

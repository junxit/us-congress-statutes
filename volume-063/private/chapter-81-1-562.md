# Chapter 562

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Giustina Schiano Lomoriello.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Mrs. Giustina Schiano Lomoriello, a citizen of the United States, who lost citizenship by voting in an Italian election, may be naturalized by taking prior to one year from the enactment of this Act, before any diplomatic or consular officer of the United States abroad, the oaths prescribed by section 335 of the Nationality Act of 1940, as amended.

## § 2.

From and after naturalization under this Act Mrs. Lomoriello shall have the same citizenship status as that which existed immediately prior to its loss.


*Approved September 7, 1949.*

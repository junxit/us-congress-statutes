# Chapter 132

> AN ACT For the relief of Emma Armstrong.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to credit the account of Emma Armstrong, former acting postmaster at Maple Shade, New Jersey, in the amount of $1,473.86, such sum being postal and money order funds lost in a burglary of the Maple Shade, New Jersey, post office, between closing time on Saturday, October 30, and opening time Monday morning, November 1, 1943, and she is hereby relieved from any liability therefor.


*Approved May 19, 1949.*

# Chapter 17

> AN ACT For the relief of Engebert Axer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws the Attorney General of the United States shall record the lawful admission for permanent residence of Reverend Engebert Axer, Society of Jesus, as of September 26, 1937, the date on which he was originally admitted to the United States as a student.


*Approved March 10, 1949.*

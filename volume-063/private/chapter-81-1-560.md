# Chapter 560

> AN ACT For the relief of Annie Balaz.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws Annie Balaz shall be considered to be the natural-born daughter of Mr. and Mrs. Andrej Balaz, United States citizens.


*Approved September 7, 1949.*

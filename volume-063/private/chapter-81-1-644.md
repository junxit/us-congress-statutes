# Chapter 644

> AN ACT For the relief of Zora B. Vulich.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Mrs. Zora B. Vulich, the widow of Captain Borivoje Vulich, who was killed in action on August 22, 1944, while serving with the United States Army Air Forces, who would be entitled to nonquota immigration status but for the death of her husband, shall, if otherwise found admissible to the United States, be deemed to be a nonquota immigrant.


*Approved October 7, 1949.*

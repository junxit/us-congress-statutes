# Chapter 53

> AN ACT For the relief of Jose Babace.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Attorney General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to cancel any deportation proceedings which might arise in the case of Jose Babace (Irigoyen), Minden, Nevada, legally admitted but who has remained in the United States longer than permitted by law and regulations, and that this alien shall be considered as having been admitted for permanent entry as of the date of his actual entry on the payment of the visa fee of $10 and the head tax of $8.

Upon enactment of this Act the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the Spanish quota for the first year that the said Spanish quota is available.


*Approved April 14, 1949.*

# Chapter 16

> AN ACT To authorize the advance on the retired list of Lieutenant John T. McDermott, United States Navy (retired), to the grade of lieutenant commander.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding any other provision of law, Lieutenant John T. McDermott, United States Navy (retired), shall be advanced to the grade of lieutenant commander on the retired list effective for all purposes from the date of his retirement.


*Approved March 10, 1949.*

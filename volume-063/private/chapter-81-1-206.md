# Chapter 206

> AN ACT Authorizing the Secretary of the Interior to issue a patent in fee to Irene Scott Bassett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is authorized and directed to issue a patent in fee to Irene Scott Bassett for the following-described lands in the State of Montana: Northeast quarter of section 22; south half of the northwest quarter and the south half of section 23; east half of the northeast quarter of section 26, all in township 6 south, range 32 east, Montana principal meridian, containing approximately six hundred and forty acres.


*Approved June 14, 1949.*

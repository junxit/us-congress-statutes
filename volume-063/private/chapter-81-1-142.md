# Chapter 142

> AN ACT Authorizing the Secretary of the Interior to sell the land of George Peters under existing regulations.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That upon the filing of a written application by George Peters, Crow Indian allottee numbered 1292, the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized to sell to a Crow Indian, under existing regulations, the homestead and other land of said George Peters, described as all of section 20; the north half of the north half; the north half of the north half of the south half of the north half of section 29, township 4 south, range 37 east, Montana principal meridian, containing eight hundred and forty acres, the status of such land with respect to taxability to remain unchanged.


*Approved May 25, 1949.*

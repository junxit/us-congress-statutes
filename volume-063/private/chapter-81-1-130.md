# Chapter 130

> AN ACT For the relief of Theodore Papachristopoulos.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Theodore Papachristopoulos shall be considered to be the natural-born son of his stepfather, Roland Stanger, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved May 19, 1949.*

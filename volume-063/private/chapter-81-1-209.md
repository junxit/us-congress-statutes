# Chapter 209

> AN ACT Authorizing the issuance of a patent in fee to Ethel M. Pearson George.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, upon application in writing, the Secretary of the Interior is authorized and directed to issue to Ethel M. Pearson George, Crow allottee numbered 1330, of Phoenix, Arizona, a patent in fee to the following-described lands allotted to her on the Crow Indian Reservation, Montana: The east half of section 25, and the northeast quarter of section 36, township 2 south, range 29 east, Montana principal meridian, containing four hundred and eighty acres.


*Approved June 14, 1949.*

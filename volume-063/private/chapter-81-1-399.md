# Chapter 399

> AN ACT For the relief of Franz Eugene Laub.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of the eleventh category of section 3 of the Immigration Act of 1917, as amended (8 U. S. C. 136 (e)), Franz Eugene Laub, a native and citizen of Germany whose wife is a citizen and resident of the United States, may be considered as having been lawfully admitted for permanent residence as of May 24, 1935, the date he last entered the United States.


*Approved August 4, 1949.*

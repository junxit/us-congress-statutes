# Chapter 293

> AN ACT For the relief of Lee F. Bertuccioli.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of the first category of section 3 of the Immigration Act of 1917, as amended, shall not hereafter be applicable to Lee F. Bertuccioli, Farnborough, Kent, England, who served in the armed forces of the United States in World War II under the name of Lee F. Johnson, and who, because of a physical disability arising out of such service, is unable to obtain an immigration visa for admission into the United States.


*Approved July 2, 1949.*

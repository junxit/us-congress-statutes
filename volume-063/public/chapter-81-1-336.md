# Chapter 336

> AN ACT To provide for further contributions to the International Children’s Emergency Fund.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in order to enable the President through June 30, 1950, to carry out the purpose of the International Children’s Emergency Fund Assistance Act of 1948 (title II of the Foreign Assistance Act of 1948) the date “1949”, wherever it appears in such Act, is hereby amended to read “1950”, and in section 206 of such Act the word “through” is hereby substituted for the words “for the fiscal year ending”: Provided however, That in authorizing such continued extension of United States participation in the International Emergency Children’s Fund, it is the expressed intention of the Congress that such participation by the United States shall cease on June 30, 1950.

## § 2.

Funds appropriated by the second paragraph of title I of the Foreign Aid Appropriation Act, 1949, shall remain available through June 30, 1950.


*Approved July 14, 1949.*

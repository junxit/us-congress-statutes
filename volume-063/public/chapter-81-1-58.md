# Chapter 58

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making an additional appropriation for the Veterans’ Administration for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1949, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the following sum:

## Independent Offices

## veteran’s administration

For an additional amount for “Readjustment benefits”, 1949, $595,890,000, to remain available until expended.


*Approved April 15, 1949.*

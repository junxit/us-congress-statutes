# Chapter 340

> AN ACT To amend section 2680 of title 28, United States Code.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2680 of title 28, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end thereof the following new subparagraph:

> - **(m)** Any claim arising from the activities of the Panama Railroad Company.”


*Approved July 16, 1949.*

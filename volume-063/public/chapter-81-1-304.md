# Chapter 304

> AN ACT To transfer the trawlers Alaska and Oregon from the Reconstruction Finance Corporation to the Fish and Wildlife Service

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Reconstruction Finance Corporation is hereby authorized and directed to transfer the trawlers Alaska and Oregon, and all their equipment, to the Fish and Wildlife Service of the Department of the Interior. Such transfer shall be without reimbursement or transfer of funds.

## § 2.

There is hereby authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary for the maintenance, repair, alteration, improvement, equipment, and operation of the vessels transferred pursuant to the first section of this Act.


*Approved July 13, 1949.*

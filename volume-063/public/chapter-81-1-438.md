# Chapter 438

> AN ACT To authorize the Commissioners of the District of Columbia to appoint contracting officers to make contracts in amounts not exceeding $3,000.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first paragraph of section 2 of the Act entitled “An Act to grant additional powers to the Commissioners of the District of Columbia, and for other purposes”, approved December 20, 1944 (58 Stat. 821, 822), is amended by striking out therefrom “$1,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$3,000”.


*Approved August 16, 1949.*

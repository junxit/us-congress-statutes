# Chapter 274

> AN ACT To amend the Nationality Act of 1940.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That that part of subparagraph (7) of paragraph (b) of section 324A of the Nationality Act of 1940, as amended (54 Stat. 1137; 8 U. S. C. 907), reading: “Section 334 (e)” is hereby amended to read: “Section 334 (c)”.


*Approved June 29, 1949.*

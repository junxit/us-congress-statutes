# Chapter 248

> AN ACT To amend section 312 of the Officer Personnel Act of 1947, as amended, so as to provide for the retention of certain officers of the Medical and Dental Corps of the Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Officer Personnel Act of 1947, as amended, is hereby further amended by deleting in the third proviso to subsection (b) of section 312 the words “twenty-two in the Medical Corps,” and the words “and twelve in the Dental Corps”; by inserting in the said proviso after the comma following the words “Civil Engineer Corps” the words “in the Medical Corps and in the Dental Corps a number as determined necessary by the Secretary of the Navy to meet the needs of the service”; by deleting in the said subsection the words “And provided further” and substituting in lieu thereof the words “Provided further”; and by adding at the end of the said subsection the following proviso: “And provided further, That until June 30, 1952, no captain of the Medical Corps or of the Dental Corps shall be subject to involuntary retirement pursuant to this subsection prior to reaching the age of sixty-two”.


*Approved June 25, 1949.*

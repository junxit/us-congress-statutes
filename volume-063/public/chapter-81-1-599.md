# Chapter 599

> AN ACT To amend section 5 of the Act approved July 10, 1890, as amended, relating to the admission into the Union of the State of Wyoming, so as to permit the leasing of school lands within such State for mineral purposes for terms in excess of ten years.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 5 of the Act entitled “An Act to provide for the admission of the State of Wyoming into the Union, and for other purposes”, approved July 10, 1890, as amended (48 Stat. 350), is hereby further amended by striking out the words “mineral leases including leases for exploration for oil and gas and the extraction thereof for a term not longer than ten years;”.


*Approved October 5, 1949.*

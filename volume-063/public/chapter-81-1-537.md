# Chapter 537

> AN ACT To authorize the Secretary of the Army to purchase certain property in Morgan County.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Army is hereby authorized and directed to acquire through purchase, condemnation, or otherwise, and to pay compensation, for all right, title, and interest, including easements and any and all other appurtenant rights and privileges which the Morgan County Farm Bureau Cooperative Association has in and to a tract of land, comprising seven and twenty-nine one-hundredths acres, more or less, located in Morgan township, Morgan County, State of Ohio; said property consisting of a mill building, appurtenant works, together with certain rights and water privileges granted to Robert McConnell, a predecessor in title, by the State of Ohio.

## § 2.

In his discretion, the Secretary of the Army may authorize the owner to remove any improvements on the property: Provided, That appropriate reduction of the salvage value thereof shall be made from the purchase price.

## § 3.

Funds appropriated for the maintenance and improvement of river and harbor works may be expended to carry out the purposes of this Act.


*Approved September 7, 1949.*

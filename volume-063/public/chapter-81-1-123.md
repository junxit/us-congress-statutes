# Chapter 123

> AN ACT To exempt artificial limbs from duty if imported for personal use and not for sale.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That title II of the Tariff Act of 1930 (relating to the free list) is hereby amended by adding at the end thereof the following new paragraph:

> - **(1816)** Artificial limbs and limb braces imported solely for the personal use of a specified person and not for sale otherwise than for the use of such person.”


*Approved May 17, 1949.*

# Chapter 473

> JOINT RESOLUTION Amending an Act making temporary appropriations for the fiscal year 1950, as amended, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Public Law 154 (Eighty-first Congress), making temporary appropriations for the fiscal year 1950, and for other purposes, as amended, is hereby amended by striking out, in section (c) thereof, “or (3) August 15, 1949,” and inserting in lieu thereof the following: “or (3) with respect to paragraph (b) August 15, 1949, and with respect to paragraph (a) September 15, 1949”.


*Approved August 18, 1949.*

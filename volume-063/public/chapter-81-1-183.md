# Chapter 183

> JOINT RESOLUTION Authorizing the President to proclaim the week in which June 6, 1949, occurs as Patrick Henry Week in commemoration of the sesquicentennial anniversary of the death of Patrick Henry.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating the week in which June 6, 1949. occurs as Patrick Henry Week, inviting the people of the United States to observe such week, and particularly the day June 6, 1949, as the one hundred fiftieth anniversary of the death of Patrick Henry, in accordance with their religious faith, with appropriate ceremonies which will serve to recall his great contribution to the cause of our national independence and to the establishment of the liberties of the people of the United States.


*Approved June 8, 1949.*

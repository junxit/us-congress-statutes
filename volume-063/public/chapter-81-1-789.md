# Chapter 789

> AN ACT To provide for parity in awards of disability compensation.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That any person otherwise eligible for disability compensation under the World War Veterans’ Act, 1924, as restored with limitations by Public Law Numbered 141, Seventy-third Congress, March 28, 1934, as amended, shall be entitled to monthly compensation of $150 if and while the disability is rated as total, and if and while partially disabled an amount having the same ratio to $150 as the degree of his disability bears to the total disability.

## § 2.

The increases provided by this Act shall be effective December 1, 1949.


*Approved October 29, 1949.*

# Chapter 539

> AN ACT To extend the time for completing the construction of a bridge across the Mississippi River at or near a point between Delmar Boulevard and Cole Street in the city of Saint Louis, Missouri, and a point opposite thereto in the city of East Saint Louis, Illinois.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time for completing the construction of a bridge across the Mississippi River, at or near a point between Delmar Boulevard and Cole Street in the city of Saint Louis, Missouri, and a point opposite thereto in the city of East Saint Louis, Illinois, authorized to be built by the city of East Saint Louis, Illinois, by an Act of Congress approved August 7, 1946, is hereby extended three years from August 7, 1949.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved September 7, 1949.*

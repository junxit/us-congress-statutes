# Chapter 788

> AN ACT To increase the retired pay of certain members of the former Lighthouse Service.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the annual rate of retired pay received by any person who was retired on or before June 29, 1949, under section 6 of the Act of June 20, 1918, as amended and supplemented (33 U. S. C., 1946 edition, 763–765), shall be increased by $360 effective on the first day of the calendar month following enactment of this Act.


*Approved October 29, 1949.*

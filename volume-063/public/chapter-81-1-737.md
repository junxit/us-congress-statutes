# Chapter 737

> JOINT RESOLUTION To clarify the status of the Architect of the Capitol under the Federal Property and Administrative Services Act of 1949.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the term “the Senate and the House of Representatives”, as used in the Federal Property and Administrative Services Act of 1949, shall be construed to include the Architect of the Capitol and any activities under his direction, and any of the services authorized by such Act shall (as far as practicable) be made available to the Architect of the Capitol, upon his request.


*Approved October 26, 1949.*

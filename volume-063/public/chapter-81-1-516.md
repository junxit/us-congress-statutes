# Chapter 516

> AN ACT To discontinue divisions of the court in the district of Kansas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 96 of title 28 of the United States Code is amended to read as follows:

> “§ 96. Kansas
>
> “Kansas constitutes one judicial district.
>
> “Court shall be held at Kansas City, Leavenworth, Salina, Topeka, Hutchinson, Wichita, Dodge City, and Fort Scott.”


*Approved August 27, 1949.*

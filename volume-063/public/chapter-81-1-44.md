# Chapter 44

> AN ACT To suspend certain import taxes on copper.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the import tax imposed under section 3425 of the Internal Revenue Code shall not apply with respect to articles (other than copper sulfate and other than composition metal provided for in paragraph 1657 of the Tariff Act of 1930, as amended, which is suitable both in its composition and shape, without further refining or alloying, for processing into castings, not including as castings ingots or similar cast forms) entered for consumption or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption during the period beginning April 1, 1949, and ending with the close of June 30, 1950.


*Approved March 31, 1949.*

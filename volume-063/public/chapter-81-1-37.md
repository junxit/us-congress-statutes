# Chapter 37

> AN ACT To provide for the appointment of an additional district judge for the middle district of Georgia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is authorized to appoint, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, one additional district judge for the United States District Court for the Middle District of Georgia. When a vacancy shall occur in the office of the existing district judge for said district such vacancy shall not be filled.


*Approved March 29, 1949.*

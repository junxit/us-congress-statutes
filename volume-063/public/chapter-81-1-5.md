# Chapter 5

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making a further appropriation for disaster relief, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the following sum:

## funds appropriated to the president

Disaster relief: A further amount for disaster relief, $500,000, subject to the limitations and restrictions under this head in the Second Deficiency Appropriation Act, 1948.


*Approved February 7, 1949.*

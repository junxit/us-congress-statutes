# Chapter 484

> AN ACT To provide for the preparation of a plan for the celebration of the one hundredth anniversary of the building of the Soo Locks.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby created a commission to be known as the Soo Locks Centennial Celebration Commission (hereinafter referred to as the “Commission”) and to be composed of nine Commissioners to be appointed by the President. The Commissioners shall serve without compensation and shall select a Chairman from among their number.

## § 2.

- **(a)** It shall be the duty of the Commission to prepare a comprehensive plan for the celebration in the year 1955 of the one hundredth anniversary of the building of the Soo Locks.
- **(b)** The Commission shall make a report of its progress to the President at least twice a year, and shall submit to the President prior to the beginning of the celebration a final report setting forth the plan prepared pursuant to subsection (a) of this section and containing such recommendations for carrying out such plan as it deems advisable. The Commission shall cease to exist thirty days after the date of the submission of the final report.


*Approved August 19, 1949.*

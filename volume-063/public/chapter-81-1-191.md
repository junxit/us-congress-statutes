# Chapter 191

> AN ACT To amend the Agricultural Act of 1948.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1 (a) of title I of the Agricultural Act of 1948 (Public Law 897, Eightieth Congress) is amended by inserting, following the date “June 30, 1950,” a parenthetical clause reading as follows: “(September 30, 1950, in the case of Maryland and the cigar-leaf types of tobacco)”.


*Approved June 10, 1949.*

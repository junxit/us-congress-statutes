# Chapter 602

> AN ACT To amend section 4 (e) of the Civil Service Retirement Act of May 29, 1930, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4 (e) of the Civil Service Retirement Act of May 29, 1930, as amended, is amended by substituting a colon for the period at the end thereof and adding the following: “ Provided, That in cases of officers and employees paid on a fee basis, the maximum ‘basic salary, pay, or com-pensation’ which may be used for the purposes of this Act shall be10,000 per annum. ”

## § 2.

This amendment shall become effective April 1, 1948.


*Approved October 5, 1949.*

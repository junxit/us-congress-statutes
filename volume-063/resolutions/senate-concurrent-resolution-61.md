# Senate Concurrent Resolution 61

> certain indian lands held in trust by united states

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That, in the enrollment of the bill (S. 1323) to declare that the United States holds certain lands in trust for the Pueblo Indians and the Canoncita Navajo group in New Mexico, and for other purposes, the Secretary of the Senate be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to strike out the word “Canoncita”, where it appears on page 2, line 12, of the Senate engrossed bill and in the title of the bill, respectively, and in lieu thereof insert “Canoncito”.


*Agreed to August 4, 1949.*

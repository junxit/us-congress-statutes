# House Concurrent Resolution 22

> legislative budget

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the date of reporting the legislative budget, as set forth in section 138 (a) of the Legislative Reorganization Act of 1946, as it may apply to the budget for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1950, is hereby postponed until May 1, 1949.


*Passed February 10, 1949.*

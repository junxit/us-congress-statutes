# House Concurrent Resolution 56

> adjournment

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Thursday, April 14, 1949, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian Monday, April 25, 1949.


*Passed April 14, 1949.*

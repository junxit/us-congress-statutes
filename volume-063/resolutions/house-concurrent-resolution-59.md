# House Concurrent Resolution 59

> joint meeting

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Thursday, the 19th day of May 1949, at 12:30 o’clock postmeridian, for the purpose of hearing an address by His Excellency Eurico Gaspar Dutra, President of the United States of Brazil.


*Passed May 12, 1949.*

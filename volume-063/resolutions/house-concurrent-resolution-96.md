# House Concurrent Resolution 96

> national bank act and bretton woods agreements act, amendment

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Clerk of the House of Representatives, in the enrollment of the bill (H. R. 4332) entitled “An Act to amend the National Bank Act and the Bretton Woods Agreements Act, and for other purposes”, is authorized and directed, in the second sentence of section 3 of the Act, after the word “Act” to insert the word “and”.


*Passed June 23, 1949.*

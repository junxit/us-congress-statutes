# House Concurrent Resolution 147

> atomic energy commission

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Joint Committee on Atomic Energy be authorized to have printed for its use fifty thousand copies of Senate Report 1169, entitled “Report on Investigation into the United States Atomic Energy Commission”, and which was introduced in the Senate on October 13, 1949.


*Passed October 19, 1949.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 18

> “the strategy and tactics of world communism”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed as a House document and that there be printed in addition fifteen thousand copies of Supplement III (Country Studies A, B, and C) of the report of the Subcommittee on National and International Movements of the Committee on Foreign Affairs entitled “The Strategy and Tactics of World Communism” for the use of the Committee on Foreign Affairs.


*Passed March 22, 1949.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 103

> expenses of joint committee

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the expenses of the joint committee created pursuant to H. Con. Res. 102, not to exceed $5,000, shall be paid by the Clerk of the House of Representatives out of the contingent fund of the House of Representatives, such contingent fund to be reimbursed from the contingent fund of the Senate in the amount of one-half of the disbursements so made.


*Passed August 25, 1949.*

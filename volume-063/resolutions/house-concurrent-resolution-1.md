# House Concurrent Resolution 1

> joint meeting

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Wednesday, the 5th day of January 1949, at 1 o’clock postmeridian for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed January 5, 1949.*

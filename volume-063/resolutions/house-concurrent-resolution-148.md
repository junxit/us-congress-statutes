# House Concurrent Resolution 148

> adjournment sine die

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Wednesday, October 19, 1949, and that when they adjourn on said day, they stand adjourned sine die.


*Passed October 19, 1949.*

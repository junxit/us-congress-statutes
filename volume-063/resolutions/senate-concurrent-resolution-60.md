# Senate Concurrent Resolution 60

> “a decade of american foreign policy: basic documents, 1941–1949”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the manuscript entitled “A Decade of American Foreign Policy: Basic Documents, 1941–1949”, prepared at the request of the Senate Foreign Relations Committee by the staff of the committee and the Department of State, be printed as a Senate document, and that one thousand additional copies shall be printed for the use of the Committee on Foreign Relations of the Senate.


*Agreed to October 18, 1949.*

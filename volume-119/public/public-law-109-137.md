# Public Law 109–137

> An Act To amend the Federal Water Pollution Control Act to extend the authorization of appropriations for Long Island Sound.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. LONG ISLAND SOUND AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS.

Section 119(f) of the Federal Water Pollution Control Act (33 U.S.C. 1269(f)) is amended by striking “2005” each place it appears and inserting “2010”.


*Approved December 22, 2005.*

# Public Law 109–158

> An Act To amend Public Law 107–153 to modify a certain date.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. SETTLEMENT OF TRIBAL CLAIMS.

Section 1(a) of Public Law 107–153 (25 U.S.C. 4044 note; 116 Stat. 79) is amended by striking “December 31, 1999” and inserting “December 31, 2000”.


*Approved December 30, 2005.*

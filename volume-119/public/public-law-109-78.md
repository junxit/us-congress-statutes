# Public Law 109–78

> An Act To extend the waiver authority of the Secretary of Education with respect to student financial assistance during a war or other military operation or national emergency.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. EXTENSION OF WAIVER AUTHORITY.

Section 6 of the Higher Education Relief Opportunities for Students Act of 2003 (20 U.S.C. 1070, note) is amended by striking “September 30, 2005” and inserting “September 30, 2007”.


*Approved September 30, 2005.*

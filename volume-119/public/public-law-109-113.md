# Public Law 109–113

> An Act To amend part E of title IV of the Social Security Act to provide for the making of foster care maintenance payments to private for-profit agencies.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. SHORT TITLE.

This Act may be cited as the “Fair Access Foster Care Act of 2005”.

## § 2. FOSTER CARE MAINTENANCE PAYMENTS TO PRIVATE FOR-PROFIT AGENCIES.

Section 472(b) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 672(b)) is amended by striking “nonprofit” each place it appears.


*Approved November 22, 2005.*

# Public Law 109–128

> Joint Resolution Making further continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 2006, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Public Law 109–77 is further amended by striking the date specified in section 106(3) and inserting the following: “December 31, 2005”.

## § 2.

Section 114(b) of Public Law 109–77 is amended by striking “and December 1, 2005,” and inserting “December 1, 2005, and January 1, 2006”.


*Approved December 18, 2005.*

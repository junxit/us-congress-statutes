# Public Law 109–5

> An Act To extend the existence of the Nazi War Crimes and Japanese Imperial Government Records Interagency Working Group for 2 years.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. TWO-YEAR EXTENSION OF WORKING GROUP.

Section 802(b)(1) of the Japanese Imperial Government Disclosure Act of 2000 (Public Law 106–567; 114 Stat. 2865) is amended by striking “4 years” and inserting “6 years”.


*Approved March 25, 2005.*

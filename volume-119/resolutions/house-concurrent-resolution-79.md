# House Concurrent Resolution 79

> JACKIE ROBINSON, POSTHUMOUS CONGRESSIONAL GOLD MEDAL—CAPITOL ROTUNDA AUTHORIZATION

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the rotunda of the Capitol is authorized to be used on March 2, 2005, for a ceremony to award a Congressional gold medal to Jackie Robinson (posthumously), in recognition of his many contributions to the Nation. Physical preparations for the ceremony shall be carried out in accordance with such conditions as the Architect of the Capitol may prescribe.


*Agreed to March 1, 2005.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 276

> RE-ENROLLMENT—H.R. 3765

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President is requested to return to the House of Representatives the enrollment of H.R. 3765. When the bill is returned by the President, the actions of the presiding officers of the two Houses in signing the bill shall be rescinded, and the Clerk of the House shall re-enroll the bill in accordance with the action of the two Houses.


*Agreed to October 27, 2005.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 68

> adjournment

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Saturday, July 21, 1945, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian on Monday, October 8, 1945, or until 12 o’clock meridian on the third day after Members are notified to reassemble in accordance with section 3 of this concurrent resolution, whichever occurs first.Senate.

## § 2.

That the consent of the House of Representatives is hereby given to an adjournment of the Senate at any time during the month of August or September 1945, until 12 o’clock meridian on Monday, October 8, 1945, or until 12 o’clock meridian on the third day after Members are notified to reassemble in accordance with section 3 of this concurrent resolution, whichever occurs first.

## § 3.

The President pro tempore of the Senate and the Speaker of the House of Representatives shall notify the Members of the Senate and the House, respectively, to reassemble whenever in their opinion legislative expediency shall warrant it or whenever the Majority Leader of the Senate and the Majority Leader of the House, acting jointly, or the Minority Leader of the Senate and the Minority Leader of the House, acting jointly, file a written request with the Secretary of the Senate and the Clerk of the House that the Congress reassemble for the consideration of legislation.


*Passed July 21, 1945.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 45

> pearl harbor attack

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the limit of time required, under the provisions of Senate Concurrent Resolution 27, for the submission to the Senate and House of Representatives of the report of the Joint Committee on the Investigation of the Pearl Harbor Attack be, and the same is hereby, extended to February 15, 1946.


*Agreed to December 20, 1945.*

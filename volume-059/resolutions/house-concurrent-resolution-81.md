# House Concurrent Resolution 81

> highway-construction program

*Be it resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That for the purposes only as specified in section 2 of the Federal-Aid Highway Act of 1944 (Public Law 521, Seventy-eighth Congress), it is hereby found as a fact that the war emergency has been relieved to an extent that will justify proceeding with the highway-construction program provided for by said Act, and for the purposes of said Act the first postwar fiscal year referred to therein shall be the fiscal year ending June 30, 1946.


*Passed October 2, 1945.*

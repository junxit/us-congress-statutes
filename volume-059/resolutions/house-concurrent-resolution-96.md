# House Concurrent Resolution 96

> joint meeting

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Tuesday, October 23, 1945, at 12:30 o’clock postmeridian, for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed October 18, 1945.*

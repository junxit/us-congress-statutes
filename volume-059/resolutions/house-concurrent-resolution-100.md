# House Concurrent Resolution 100

> address of the president presenting his recommendations with respect to universal military training

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed three hundred thousand copies of House Document 359, entitled “Address of the President of the United States, before a joint session of the Senate and House of Representatives, presenting his recommendations with respect to universal military training”, of which seventy-five thousand copies shall be for the use of the Senate, two hundred and twenty-one thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives, two thousand copies for the Senate document room, and two thousand copies for the House document room.


*Passed November 1, 1945.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 47

> adjournment sine die

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Friday, December 21, 1945, and that when they adjourn on said day they stand adjourned sine die.


*Agreed to December 21, 1945.*

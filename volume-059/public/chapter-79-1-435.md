# Chapter 435

> AN ACT To transfer Ben Hill County, Georgia, from the Waycross division of the southern judicial district of Georgia to the Americus division of the middle judicialdistrict of Georgia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Ben HillCounty, Georgia, of the Waycross division of the southern judicialdistrict of Georgia be, and it is hereby, detached from said judicialdistrict and attached to the Americus division of the middlejudicial district of Georgia.


*Approved October 29, 1945.*

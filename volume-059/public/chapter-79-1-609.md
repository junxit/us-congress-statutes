# Chapter 609

> JOINT RESOLUTION Fixing the date of meeting of the second session of the Seventy-ninth Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second session of the Seventy-ninth Congress shall begin at noon on Monday, January 14, 1946.


*Approved December 28, 1945.*

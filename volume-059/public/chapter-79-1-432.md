# Chapter 432

> JOINT RESOLUTION To extend in the case of aluminum plants and facilities the time during which disposition of such plants and facilities is prohibited under the Surplus Property Act of 1944, as amended.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (c) of section 19 of the Surplus Property Act of 1944, as amended, is amended by inserting after the words “thirty days ” the following: “(or sixty days in the case of aluminum plants and facilities)”.


*Approved October 22, 1945.*

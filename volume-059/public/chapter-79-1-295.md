# Chapter 295

> AN ACT For the relief of the Truckee-Carson Irrigation District.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proposed contract approved as to form by the Secretary of the Interior on January 9, 1945, between the United States of America and the Truckee-Carson Irrigation District is approved and, after said contract shall have been duly executed for and in behalf of the Truckee-Carson Irrigation District, the said Secretary is hereby authorized to execute it on behalf of the United States.


*Approved July 14, 1945.*

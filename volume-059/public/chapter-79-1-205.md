# Chapter 205

> AN ACT To continue in effect section 6 of the Act of July 2, 1940 (54 Stat. 714), as amended, relating to the exportation of certain commodities.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 6 of the Act of July 2, 1940 (54 Stat. 714), as amended by the Act ofJune 30, 1942 (56 Stat. 463), and the Act of July 1, 1944 (Public Law 397, Seventy-eighth Congress), is hereby further amended by deleting from subsection (d) thereof the words “June 30, 1945” and substituting therefor the words “June 30, 1946”.


*Approved June 30, 1945.*

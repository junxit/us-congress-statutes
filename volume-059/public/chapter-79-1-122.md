# Chapter 122

> AN ACT To amend section 3 (b) of the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, so as to permit exemption of security issues not exceeding $300,000 from the provisions of such Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (b) of section 3 of the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, is amended by striking out “$100,000” where it appears in such subsection, and inserting in lieu thereof “$300,000”.


*Approved May 15, 1945.*

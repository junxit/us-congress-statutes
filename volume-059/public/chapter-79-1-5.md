# Chapter 5

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making an additional appropriation for the fiscal year 1945 for the Census of Agriculture.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the amount named in the appropriation contained in the Department of Commerce Appropriation Act, 1945, for preparing for, taking, compiling, and publishing the quinquennial Census of Agriculture of the United States is hereby increased by $6,784,000.


*Approved February 28, 1945.*

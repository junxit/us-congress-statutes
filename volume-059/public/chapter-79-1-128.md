# Chapter 128

> AN ACT To amend section 409 of the Interstate Commerce Act, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 409 of the Interstate Commerce Act, as amended, is amended by striking out the words “thirty-six months” wherever they appear therein and inserting in lieu thereof the words “forty-five months”.


*Approved May 16, 1945.*

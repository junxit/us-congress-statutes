# Chapter 417

> AN ACT To revive and reenact the Act entitled “An Act to authorize the city of Duluth, in the State of Minnesota, to construct a toll bridge across the Saint Louis River, between the States of Minnesota and Wisconsin, and for other purposes”, approved August 7, 1939.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act approved August 7, 1939 (heretofore amended and extended by an Act of Congress approved April 30, 1940, and further extended and amended by an Act of Congress approved May 9, 1941), authorizing the city of Duluth, in the State of Minnesota, to construct a toll bridge across the Saint Louis River between the States of Minnesota and Wisconsin, and for other purposes, be, and is hereby, revived and reenacted: Provided, That this Act shall be null and void unless the actual construction of the bridge herein referred to be commenced within one year and completed within three years from the date of approval hereof: Provided further, That no toll shall be charged to any vehicle owned by the United States Government.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved October 16, 1945.*

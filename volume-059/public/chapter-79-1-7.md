# Chapter 7

> AN ACT To repeal the Act entitled “An Act to authorize the conveyance of the old lighthouse keeper’s residence in Manitowoc, Wisconsin, to the Otto Oas Post, Numbered 659, Veterans of Foreign Wars of the United States, Manitowoc, Wisconsin”, approved June 16, 1938.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of June 16, 1938, ch. 472 (52 Stat. 756), entitled “An Act to authorize the conveyance of the old lighthouse keeper’s residence in Manitowoc, Wisconsin, to the Otto Oas Post, Numbered 659, Veterans of Foreign Wars of the United States, Manitowoc, Wisconsin”, is hereby repealed.


*Approved February 28, 1945.*

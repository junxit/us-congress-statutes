# Chapter 298

> AN ACT To amend an Act entitled “An Act to provide for the purchase of public lands for home and other sites”, approved June 1, 1938 (52 Stat. 609).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act entitled “An Act to provide for the purchase of public lands for home and other sites”, approved June 1, 1938 (52 Stat. 609), is hereby amended by striking out the words “prescribed: Provided further, That this Act shall not apply to any lands in the Territory of Alaska”, and by inserting in lieu thereof the words “prescribe: Provided further, That any employee of the Department of the Interior, stationed in Alaska, notwithstanding such employment, may, in the discretion of the Secretary, purchase or lease one such tract in the Territory of Alaska, except business sites, under this Act”.


*Approved July 14, 1945.*

# Chapter 225

> AN ACT To extend five-year-level-premium-term policies for an additional three years.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the five-year-term period under all national service life insurance issued on or before December 31, 1945, and not exchanged or converted prior to that date to a plan other than five-year-level-premium-term insurance is hereby extended for an additional period of three years. The premiums actually chargeable for such additional period shall be the same as during the original five-year period notwithstanding that the premiums due under section 602 (e) of the National Service LifeInsurance Act of 1940 are those for a three-year-level-premium-term insurance at the attained age of the insured at the commencement date of such three-year period: Provided, That the Administrator of Veterans Affairs is authorized to make such adjustments as he may determine to be proper in reserves and any dividends.


*Approved July 2, 1945.*

# Chapter 505

> AN ACT For the relief of Florence Zimmerman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 15 to 20, inclusive, of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for the employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes”, approved September 7, 1916, as amended (U. S. C., 1934 edition, title 5, secs. 767 and 770), are hereby waived in favor of Florence Zimmerman, who is alleged to have sustained injuries to her back in the line of her duties on June 23, 1942, while employed in the Navy medical supply depot, Brooklyn, New York, and her claim for compensation is authorized to be considered and acted upon under the remaining provisions of such Act, as amended, if she files such claim with the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission not later than sixty days after the date of enactment of this Act.

## § 2.

The monthly compensation which the said Florence Zimmerman may be entitled to receive by reason of the enactment of this Act shall commence on the first day of the month during which this Act is enacted.


*Approved November 30, 1945.*

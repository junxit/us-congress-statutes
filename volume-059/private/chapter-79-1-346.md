# Chapter 346

> AN ACT For the relief of John F. Davis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions of any laws or orders, regulations or rulings, made thereunder by any department, agency, or officer of the United States, John F. Davis, principal fiscal accounting clerk, naval air station, Tillamook, Oregon, is hereby released from any indebtedness to the United States arising from the claim of the United States for the cost of transportation of household effects of said John F. Davis from Palo Alto, California, to Tillamook, Oregon; it being the intent of this Act that the cost of such transportation shall be borne by the United States.


*Approved July 31, 1945.*

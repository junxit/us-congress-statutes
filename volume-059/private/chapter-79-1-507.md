# Chapter 507

> AN ACT For the relief of Leslie O. Allen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limitations of time in sections 15 to 20, both inclusive, of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes”, approved September 7, 1916, as amended, are hereby waived in favor of Leslie O. Allen, of Natchez, Mississippi, and the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission is hereby authorized and directed to receive and consider under the remaining provisions of said Act his claim on account of injury of his knee alleged to have been incurred in May 1939, while serving with the Civilian Conservation Corps at Meadville, Mississippi: Provided,That claim hereunder shall be filed within six months from the approval of this Act: Provided further, That no benefits shall accrue prior to the approval of this Act.


*Approved November 30, 1945.*

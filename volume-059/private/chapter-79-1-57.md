# Chapter 57

> AN ACT For the relief of the Southern Bitumen Company, of Ensley, Alabama.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Administrator of the Federal Works Agency is hereby authorized and directed to relieve the Southern Bitumen Company, of Ensley, Alabama, of all claims of the Federal Works Agency, amounting to $13,500, covering liquidated damages for delays due to unusual labor shortages, low priorities, failure to obtain rights-of-way, and exceptional inclement weather conditions, in connection with the completion of work under a contract entered into between the Federal Works Agency and the Southern Bitumen Company for the construction of an outfall sewer at Anniston, Alabama, project numbered Ala. 1–160 (F).


*Approved April 16, 1945.*

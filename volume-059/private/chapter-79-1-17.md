# Chapter 17

> AN ACT For the relief of John T. Cooper.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General is authorized and directed to credit the account of John T. Cooper, postmaster at Hartselle, Alabama, in the sum of $179. Such sum represents a shortage in such account caused by the loss of such sum from the money-order cash drawer at such post office during the night of October 24, 1940.


*Approved March 1, 1945.*

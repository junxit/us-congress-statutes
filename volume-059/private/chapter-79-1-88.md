# Chapter 88

> AN ACT For the relief of Benjamin D. Lewis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 15 to 20, inclusive, of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes”, approved September 7, 1916, as amended (U. S. C., 1940 edition, title 5, secs. 767 and 770) are hereby waived in favor of Benjamin D. Lewis, who is alleged to have contracted tuberculosis while employed at the Normoyle Quartermaster Motor Base, San Antonio, Texas, prior to 1942, and his claim for compensation is authorized to be considered and acted upon under the remaining provisions of such Act, as amended, if he files such claim with the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission not later than sixty days after the date of the enactment of this Act: Provided, That no benefits hereunder shall accrue prior to the approval of this Act.


*Approved April 20, 1945.*

# Chapter 96

> AN ACT To grant the honorary rank of colonel to Edward J. Kelly, major and superintendent of the Metropolitan Police force of the District of Columbia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in appreciation and recognition of Edward J. Kelly’s thirty-nine years of devoted service to the District of Columbia and its residents, that he shall have the honorary rank of colonel during the remaining period of his incumbency in such office.


*Approved April 27, 1945.*

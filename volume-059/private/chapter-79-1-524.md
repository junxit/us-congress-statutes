# Chapter 524

> AN ACT For the relief of Candler Cobb.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Candler Cobb, New York, New York, the sum of $506.10. The payment of such sum shall reimburse the said Candler Cobb for the expenditure of a like amount in payment of hospital and nurses’ bills incurred in connection with an emergency operation undergone by him during May 1943, while a member of the United States Army.


*Approved December 3, 1945.*

# Chapter 259

> AN ACT For the relief of Nelson R. Park.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Nelson R. Park, American Foreign Service officer, formerly assigned as consul at Barranquilla, Colombia, is hereby relieved from accounting for 2,023 pesos, the equivalent of $1,191.77, which were stolen from the safe in the consulate at Barranquilla during the night of June 3, 1942.


*Approved July 2, 1945.*

# Chapter 587

> AN ACT To amend the Act entitled “An Act for the relief of certain settlers in the town site of Ketchum, Idaho”, approved July 11, 1940, so as to extend for three years the time for making application for benefits thereunder.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Act entitled “An Act for the relief of certain settlers in the town site of Ketchum, Idaho”, approved July 11, 1940, is amended by striking out the words “three years” and inserting in lieu thereof the words “six years”.


*Approved December 21, 1945.*

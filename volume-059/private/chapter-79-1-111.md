# Chapter 111

> AN ACT Granting a franking privilege to Anna Eleanor Roosevelt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all mail matter sent by the post by Anna Eleanor Roosevelt, widow of the late Franklin Delano Roosevelt, under her written autograph signature or facsimile thereof, be conveyed free of postage during her natural life.


*Approved May 7, 1945.*

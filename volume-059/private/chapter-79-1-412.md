# Chapter 412

> AN ACT For the relief of the Oregon Caves Resort.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Oregon Caves Resort, Grants Pass, Oregon, is hereby relieved from its liability for franchise fees for the years 1934 and 1935 under contract with the Department of Agriculture in the amount and to the extent that these fees exceed those that would have been due for the years 1934 and 1935, as determined by the Secretary of the Interior, under the terms of the renegotiated contract between the National Park Service, Department of the Interior, and the Oregon Caves Resort, dated January 1, 1936, had this latter contract been in full force and effect on January 1, 1934.


*Approved October 13, 1945.*

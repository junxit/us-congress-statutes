# House Concurrent Resolution 172

> “OUR AMERICAN GOVERNMENT”—HOUSE PRINT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That a revised edition of the booklet entitled “Our American Government” shall be printed as a House document. In addition to the usual number, there shall be printed 560,000 copies of the document, of which 443,000 copies shall be for the use of the House of Representatives, 103,000 copies shall be for the use of the Senate, and 14,000 copies shall be for the use of the Joint Committee on Printing.


*Agreed to October 4, 1991.*

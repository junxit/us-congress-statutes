# House Concurrent Resolution 151

> “COLUMBUS IN THE CAPITOL”—HOUSE PRINT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the volume entitled “Columbus in the Capitol” prepared pursuant to House Concurrent Resolution 275, One Hundred First Congress, shall be printed as a House document. In addition to the usual number, there shall be printed 123,000 copies of the document, of which 88,000 copies shall be for the use of the House of Representatives, 20,000 copies shall be for the use of the Senate, and 15,000 copies shall be for the use of the Joint Committee on Printing.


*Agreed to August 2, 1991.*

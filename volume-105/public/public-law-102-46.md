# Public Law 102–46

> An Act To correct an error in the Solar, Wind, Waste, and Geothermal Power Production Incentives Act of 1990.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3(17)(E) of the Federal Power Act, as amended, is further amended by striking “, and which would otherwise not qualify as a small power production facility because of the power production capacity limitation contained in subparagraph (A)(ii)”.


*Approved May 17, 1991.*

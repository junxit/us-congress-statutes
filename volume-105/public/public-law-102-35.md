# Public Law 102–v

> Joint Resolution Designating the Week of April 21–27, 1991, as “National Crime Victims’ Rights Week”.

Whereas thirty-five million individuals in the United States are victimized by crime each year, with six million failing prey to violence; Whereas the Department of Justice estimates that five out of six individuals will be the victim or intended victim of crime during their lifetimes; Whereas many victims suffer severe psychological, physical, and emotional hardships as a result of victimizations; Whereas the Nation must commit its collective energies to improving the criminal justice and social services response to victims; and Whereas, as a Nation committed to justice and liberty for all, efforts must be continued to remove the inequities victims face and to protect and restore individual rights: Now, therefore, be it Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

That April 21 through April 27, 1991, is designated as “National Crime Victims’ Rights Week”, and the President is authorized and requested to issue a proclamation calling upon the people of the United States to observe the week with appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Approved April 24, 1991.*

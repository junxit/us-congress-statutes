# Public Law 102–187

> Joint Resolution To make a technical correction in Public Law 101–549.

Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

That in section 112(b)(1) of the Clean Air Act, as amended by section 301 of Public Law 101–549, strike out the term “7783064 Hydrogen sulfide” in the list of pollutants.


*Approved December 4, 1991.*

# Public Law 102–221

> An Act To improve the operational efficiency of the James Madison Memorial Fellowship Foundation, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. AMENDMENT OF JAMES MADISON MEMORIAL FELLOWSHIP ACT.

The James Madison Memorial Fellowship Act (20 U.S.C. 4501 et seq.) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (b) of section 803, by adding at the end the following new paragraph:
  > - **(3)** A member of the Board whose term has expired may continue to serve until the earlier of—
  >   - **(A)** the date on which a successor has taken office; or
  >   - **(B)** the date on which the Congress adjourns sine die to end the session of Congress that commences after the date on which the member’s term expired.”; and

- **(2)** in subsection (a) of section 811—
  - **(A)** in paragraph (1)—
    - **(i)** by striking “an other” and inserting “and other”; and
    - **(ii)** by striking “(1)”; and
  - **(B)** by striking paragraph (2).


*Approved December 11, 1991.*

# Public Law 102–129

> An Act To designate the building located at 6600 Lorain Avenue in Cleveland, Ohio, as the “Patrick J. Patton United States Post Office Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The building located at 6600 Lorain Avenue in Cleveland, Ohio, is designated as the “Patrick J. Patton United States Post Office Building”.

## § 2. LEGAL REFERENCES.

Any reference in any law, regulation, document, record, map, or other paper of the United States to the building referred to in section 1 is deemed to be a reference to the “Patrick J. Patton United States Post Office Building”.


*Approved October 15, 1991.*

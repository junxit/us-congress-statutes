# Public Law 102–112

> Joint Resolution To authorize the President to issue a proclamation designating each of the weeks beginning on November 24, 1991, and November 22, 1992, as “National Family Week”.

Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

That the President is hereby authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating each of the weeks beginning on November 24, 1991, and November 22, 1992, as “National Family Week”.


*Approved October 3, 1991.*

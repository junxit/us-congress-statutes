# Public Law 102–216

> An Act To lengthen from five to seven years the expiration period applicable to legislative authority relating to construction of commemorative works on Federal land in the District of Columbia and its environs.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 10(b) of the Act entitled “An Act to provide standards for placement of commemorative works on certain Federal lands in the District of Columbia and its environs, and for other purposes” (40 U.S.C. 1010(b)) is amended by striking out “five-year period” and inserting in lieu thereof “seven-year period”.

## § 2. EFFECTIVE DATE.

The amendment made by this Act shall take effect on October 1, 1991.


*Approved December 11, 1991.*

# Public Law 102–v

> Joint Resolution To designate the week beginning April 21, 1991, and the week beginning April 19, 1992, each as “National Organ and Tissue Donor Awareness Week”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the week beginning April 21, 1991, and the week beginning April 19, 1992, are each designated “National Organ and Tissue Donor Awareness Week”, and the President is authorized and requested to issue a proclamation calling upon the people of the United States to observe such weeks with appropriate programs, ceremonies, and activities.


*Approved April 26, 1991.*

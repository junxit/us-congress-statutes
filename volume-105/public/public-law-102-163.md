# Public Law 102–163

> Joint Resolution Making further continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 1992, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 106(c) of Public Law 102–145 is amended by striking out “November 14, 1991” and inserting in lieu thereof “November 26, 1991”.


*Approved November 15, 1991.*

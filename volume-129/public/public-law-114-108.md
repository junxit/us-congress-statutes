# Public Law 114–108

> Joint Resolution Appointing the day for the convening of the second session of the One Hundred Fourteenth Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second regular session of the One Hundred Fourteenth Congress shall begin at noon on Monday, January 4, 2016.


*Approved December 18, 2015.*

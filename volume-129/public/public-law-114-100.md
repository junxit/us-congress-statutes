# Public Law 114–100

> Joint Resolution Making further continuing appropriations for fiscal year 2016, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Continuing Appropriations Act, 2016 (Public Law 114–53) is further amended by striking the date specified in section 106(3) and inserting “December 22, 2015”.


*Approved December 16, 2015.*

# Public Law 100–489

> An Act To designate the United States courthouse located at 445 Broadway in Albany, New York, as the “James T. Foley United States Courthouse”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The United States courthouse located at 445 Broadway in Albany, New York, shall be known and designated as the “James T. Foley United States Courthouse”.

## § 2. LEGAL REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to the courthouse referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “James T. Foley United States Courthouse”.


*Approved October 14, 1988.*

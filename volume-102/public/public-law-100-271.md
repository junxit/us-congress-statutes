# Public Law 100–271

> An Act To amend the Department of Defense Authorization Act, 1985, to extend medical benefits for certain former spouses.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § I. EXTENSION OF MEDICAL BENEFITS FOK CERTAIN FORMER SPOUSES.

Section 645(c) of the Department of Defense Authorization Act, 1985 (Public Law 98–525; 98 Stat. 2549), is amended by striking out “April 1, 1988,” and inserting in lieu thereof “December 31, 1988,”.


*Approved March 29, 1988.*

# Public Law 100–258

> An Act To designate Morgan and Lawrence Counties in Alabama as a single metropolitan statistical area.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the counties of Morgan and Lawrence in Alabama shall be considered for designation as a single metropolitan statistical area by the Director of the Office of Management and Budget without regard to the portion of the Bankhead National Forest located within Lawrence County.


*Approved March 14, 1988.*

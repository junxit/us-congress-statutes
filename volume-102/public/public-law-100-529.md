# Public Law 100–529

> An Act To quiet title and possession with respect to a certain private land claim in Sumter County, Alabama.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding any other provision of law, the limitation provision of section 1 of the Act of December 22, 1928 (45 Stat. 1069; 43 U S C. 1068), popularly known as the Color-of-Title Act, that restricts conveyances under that Act to not more than 160 acres, shall not apply to any claim for a patent that may be filed under the Color-of-Title Act for a parcel of land described as section 33, township 20 north, range 1 west, Sumter County, Alabama.

## § 2.

Except as otherwise provided in section 1 of this Act, all provisions of the Color-of-Title Act shall apply to any claim for a patent under the Color-of-Title Act for the parcel of land described in the first section of this Act.


*Approved October 25, 1988.*

# Public Law 100–536

> An Act To authorize appropriations to carry out title I of the Marine Protection, Research, and Sanctuaries Act of 1972 during fiscal years 1989, 1990, and 1991.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 111 of the Marine Protection, Research, and Sanctuaries Act of 1972 (33 U.S.C. 1420) is amended to read as follows:

> ## § 111. “authorization of appropriations
>
> There are authorized to be appropriated, for purposes of carrying out this title, not to exceed $12,000,000 for each of fiscal years 1989, 1990, and 1991.”.


*Approved October 28, 1988.*

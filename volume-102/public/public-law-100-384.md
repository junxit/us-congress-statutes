# Public Law 100–384

> An Act To designate the Federal building located at 50 Spring Street, Southwest, Atlanta, Georgia, as the “Martin Luther King, Jr. Federal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The Federal building located at 50 Spring Street, Southwest, Atlanta, Georgia, shall be known and designated as the “Martin Luther King, Jr. Federal Building”.

## § 2. LEGAL REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to the Federal building referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Martin Luther King, Jr. Federal Building”.


*Approved August 10, 1988.*

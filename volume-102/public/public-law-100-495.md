# Public Law 100–495

> An Act To amend the Job Training Partnership Act to make a technical change.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 161(b) of the Job Training Partnership Act is amended—

- **(1)** by inserting “(1)” after “(b)”; and
- **(2)** by adding at the end thereof the following new paragraph:
- **(2)** Notwithstanding paragraph (1), funds obligated for any program year for programs authorized by sections 452 through 455 of part D of title IV of this Act shall remain available until expended.”.

## § 2.

The amendments made by this Act shall apply with respect to funds available for expenditure on or after June 30, 1988.


*Approved October 17, 1988.*

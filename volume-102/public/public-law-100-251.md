# Public Law 100–251

> An Act To rescind certain budget authority recommended in Public Law 100–202.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That of the funds made available in the fiscal year 1988 Foreign Operations, Export Financing and Related Programs Appropriations Act (Public Law 100–202), under the heading Migration and Refugee Assistance, $8,000,000 for the construction of educational facilities for North African Jewish refugees in France is rescinded.


*Approved February 16, 1988.*

# Public Law 100–255

> An Act To make a technical correction to section 8103 of title 46, United States Code.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 8103(b)(3) of title 46, United States Code, is amended by striking “subsection,” and substituting “section,”.


*Approved March 4, 1988.*

# Public Law 100–273

> Joint Resolution To authorize and request the President to issue a proclamation designating April 24 through April 30, 1988, as “National Organ and Tissue Donor Awareness Week”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating April 24 through April 30, 1988, as “National Organ and Tissue Donor Awareness Week”.


*Approved March 30, 1988.*

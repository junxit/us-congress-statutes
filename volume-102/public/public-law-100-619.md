# Public Law 100–619

> An Act To amend the Hunger Prevention Act of 1988 to make a technical correction.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. TECHNICAL CORRECTION.

In section 701(b)(4) strike out “and sections 310 through 352” and insert in lieu thereof “sections 310 through 343, and sections 345 through 352”.


*Approved November 5, 1988.*

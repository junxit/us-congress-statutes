# Private Law 100–10

> An Act For the relief of John H. Teele.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for purposes of sections 5724 and 5724a of title 5 of the United States Code, the expenses incurred by John H. Teele to relocate himself, his immediate family, and his household goods and personal effects incident to beginning employment with the Department of the Army at his official station in Huntsville, Alabama, shall be treated as expenses incurred by an employee transferred in the interest of the Government from one official station or agency to another for permanent duty.


*Approved May 5, 1988.*

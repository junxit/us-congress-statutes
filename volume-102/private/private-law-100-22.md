# Private Law 100–22

> An Act For the relief of Helen Lannier.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for purposes of section 8341 of title 5, United States Code, Helen Lannier of Alexandria, Virginia, shall be considered to be the widow of the late Delbert Lannier.


*Approved October 25, 1988.*

# Private Law 100–20

> An Act For the relief of Irma Purisch and Daniel Purisch.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Irma Purisch and Daniel Purisch, shall each be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(E) of such Act, upon approval of a petition filed on their behalf by Carolyn Weiss Purisch, a citizen of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of such Act. No natural parent, brother, or sister, if any, of Irma Purisch or Daniel Purisch shall, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved October 4, 1988.*

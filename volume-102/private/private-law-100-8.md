# Private Law 100–8

> An Act For the relief of Tracey McFarlane.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding her age, Tracey McFarlane may be naturalized under section 322 of the Immigration and Nationality Act if a petition is filed on her behalf pursuant to that section not later than 2 years after the date of enactment of this Act.


*Approved April 12, 1988.*

# Private Law 100–30

> An Act For the relief of John M. Gill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury shall pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to John M. Gill (Social Security Numbered xxxxxxxxxxx) of Merced, California, the sum of $15,000. Payment of such sum shall be in full settlement of all claims of John M. Gill against the United States for the value of personal property that was lost in Vietnam as a result of the fall of Saigon in 1975.

## § 2.

Not more than 10 per centum of the sum appropriated in the first section of this Act shall be paid to or received by any agent or attorney for services rendered in connection with the claim specified in such action. Any person violating this section shall be fined not more than $1,000.


*Approved October 31, 1988.*

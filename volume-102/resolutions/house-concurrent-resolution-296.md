# House Concurrent Resolution 296

> HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES ELECTION LAW GUIDEBOOK, 1988—HOUSE PRINT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the House of Representatives Election Law Guidebook, 1988, shall be printed as a House document. In addition to the usual number, two thousand copies of the document shall be printed for the use of the Committee on House Administration of the House of Representatives.


*Agreed to June 23, 1988.*

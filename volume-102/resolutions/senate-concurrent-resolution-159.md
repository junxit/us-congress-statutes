# Senate Concurrent Resolution 159

> ENROLLMENT CORRECTIONS—H.R. 4174

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (H.R. 4174), the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall make the following corrections:

- **(1)** At the end of subparagraph (b)(3) of section 132, strike out ‘conference,”; and’ and insert “conference.”
- **(2)** At the end of subparagraph (b)(2) of section 132, strike out the semicolon and insert “; and”.
- **(3)** In section 132, strike out subparagraph (b)(4).


*Agreed to October 19, 1988.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 110

> ENROLLMENT CORRECTIONS—S. 854

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That, in the enrollment of the bill (S. 854) Nevada-Florida Land Exchange Authorization Act of 1988, the Secretary of the Senate shall make the following corrections:

- **(1)** In subsection (c) of section 5, strike “International Power Project” and insert in lieu thereof: “Intermountain Power Project”.
- **(2)** In subsection (g)(2) of section 5, after “Secretary under” insert: “any”.


*Agreed to March 29, 1988.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 98

> “DEVELOPMENTS IN AGING: 1987” REPORT—SENATE PRINT

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed as a Senate document “Developments in Aging: 1987”, a three volume report, as prepared by the Special Committee on Aging of the Senate.

## § 2.

Such document shall be in such style, form, manner, and binding as directed by the Joint Committee on Printing after consultation with the Special Committee on Aging.

## § 3.

There shall be printed 3,000 copies of volume I of the report, 1,000 copies of volume II of the report, and 5,000 copies of volume III of the report.


*Agreed to April 19, 1988.*

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

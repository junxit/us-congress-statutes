# Senate Concurrent Resolution 137

> NATIONAL COUNCIL OF RETURNED PEACE CORPS VOLUNTEERS—CAPITOL ROTUNDA CEREMONY

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That permission is conferred on the National Council of Returned Peace Corps Volunteers to use the Rotunda of the Capitol, from 12:00 noon, November 21, 1988, until 12:00 noon, November 22, 1988, for a vigil of readings from personal Peace Corps journals by Re turned Peace Corps Volunteers in honor of John F. Kennedy, the founder of the Peace Corps, on the 25th anniversary of his death.


*Agreed to October 4, 1988.*

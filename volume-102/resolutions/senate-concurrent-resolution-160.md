# Senate Concurrent Resolution 160

> ENROLLMENT CORRECTION—S. 508

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the text of the bill (S. 508) to amend title 5, United States Code, to strengthen the protections available to Federal employees against prohibited personnel practices, and for other purposes, the Secretary of the Senate is authorized and directed in the enrollment of such bill to make the following correction: in section 1219(a)(3) of title 5, United States Code, as added by section 3(a) of the bill, strike out “subsection (f)” and insert in lieu thereof “subsection (e)”.


*Agreed to October 12, 1988.*

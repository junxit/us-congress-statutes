# House Concurrent Resolution 270

> KATYN FOREST MASSACRE REPORT—HOUSE PRINT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That House Report Number 2505, Eighty-second Congress, dated December 22, 1952, the final report of the Select Committee to Conduct an Investigation and Study of the Facts, Evidence, and Circumstances of the Katyn Forest Massacre, shall be printed as a House document. In addition to the usual number, 25,000 copies of the report shall be printed for the use of the House of Representatives.


*Agreed to April 22, 1988.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 398

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House and Senate adjourn on Saturday, October 22, 1988, they stand adjourned sine die.


*Agreed to October 22, 1988.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 106

> ENROLLMENT CORRECTION—S. 854

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President of the United States is requested to return to the Senate the enrolled bill (S. 854) entitled “An Act entitled the ‘Nevada-Florida Land Exchange Authorization Act of 1988’.”. The Secretary of the Senate is authorized to receive such bill if it is returned when the Senate is not in session. Upon the return of such bill, the action of the Speaker of the House of Representatives and the Deputy President pro tempore of the Senate in signing it shall be deemed rescinded and the Secretary of the Senate shall reenroll the bill with the following correction:

In subsection (a) of section 3, strike “conveyance of” and insert in lieu thereof the words “conveyance to”.


*Agreed to March 23, 1988.*

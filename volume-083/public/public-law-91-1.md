# Public Law 91–1

> AN ACT To increase the per annum rate of compensation of the President of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 102 of title 3, United States Code, is amended by striking out “$100,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$200,000”.

## § 2.

The amendment made by this Act shall take effect at noon on January 20, 1969.


*Approved January 17, 1969.*

# Public Law 91–180

> AN ACT To continue for two additional years the duty-free status of certain gifts by members of the Armed Forces serving in combat zones.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** item 915.25 of the appendix to the Tariff Schedules of the United States (19 U.S.C. 1202) is amended by striking out “On or before 12/31/69” and inserting in lieu thereof “On or before 12/31/71”.
- **(b)** The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply with respect to articles entered, or withdrawn from warehouse, for consumption on or after January 1, 1970.


*Approved December 30, 1969.*

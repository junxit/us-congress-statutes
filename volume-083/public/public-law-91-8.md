# Public Law 91–8

> AN ACT To increase the public debt limit set forth in section 21 of the Second Liberty Bond Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first sentence of section 21 of the Second Liberty Bond Act (31 U.S.C. 757b) is amended by striking out “$358,000,000,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$365,000,000,000”.

## § 2.

During the period beginning on the date of the enactment of this Act and ending on June 30, 1970, the public debt limit set forth in the first sentence of section 21 of the Second Liberty Bond Act shall be temporarily increased by $12,000,000,000. Section 3 of the Act of June 30, 1967 (Public Law 90–39; 81 Stat. 99), is repealed.


*Approved April 7, 1969.*

# Public Law 91–125

> JOINT RESOLUTION To authorize appropriations for expenses of the National Council on Indian Opportunity.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated not to exceed $300,000 annually for the expenses of the National Council on Indian Opportunity, established by Executive Order Numbered 11399 of March 6, 1968.

## § 2.

The National Council on Indian Opportunity shall terminate five years from the date of this Act unless it is extended by an Act of Congress.


*Approved November 26, 1969.*

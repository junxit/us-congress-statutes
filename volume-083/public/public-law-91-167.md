# Public Law 91–167

> AN ACT To change the limitation on the number of apprentices authorized to be employees of the Government Printing Office, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of October 22, 1968 (ch. 3, sec. 305, 82 Stat. 1240) (44 U.S.C. 305), is amended by deleting in the second sentence the words “nor more than two hundred apprentices at one time” and substituting therefor the words “nor more than four hundred apprentices at one time”.


*Approved December 26, 1969.*

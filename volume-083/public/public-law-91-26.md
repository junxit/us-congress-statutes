# Public Law 91–26

> AN ACT To extend through December 31, 1970, the suspension of duty on electrodes for use in producing aluminum.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the matter appearing in the effective period column for item 909.25 of the Tariff Schedules of the United States (19 U.S.C. 1202) is amended by striking out “7/15/69” and inserting in lieu thereof “12/31/70”.

## § 2.

The amendment made by the first section of this Act shall apply with respect to articles entered, or withdrawn from warehouse, for consumption after July 15, 1969.


*Approved June 13, 1969.*

# Public Law 91–137

> AN ACT To extend for one year the authorization for research relating to fuels and vehicles under the provisions of the Clean Air Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first sentence of section 104(c) of the Clean Air Act (42 U.S.C. 1857b–1(c)) is amended by striking out “and”, and by striking out the period at the end thereof and inserting in lieu thereof “, and for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1970, $45,000,000.”.


*Approved December 5, 1969.*

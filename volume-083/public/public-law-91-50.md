# Public Law 91–50

> AN ACT To continue for a temporary period the existing interest equalization tax.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That effective with respect to acquisitions made after July 31, 1969, section 4911(d) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1954 (relating to termination of interest equalization tax) is amended by striking out “July 31, 1969” and inserting in lieu thereof “August 31, 1969”.


*Approved August 2, 1969.*

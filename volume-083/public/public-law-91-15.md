# Public Law 91–15

> AN ACT To amend the Marine Resources and Engineering Development Act of 1966 to continue the National Council on Marine Resources and Engineering Development, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (f) of section 8 of the Marine Resources and Engineering Development Act of 1966 (88 U.S.C. 1102(f)) is amended by striking out “June 30, 1969” and inserting in lieu thereof “June 80, 1970”.

## § 2.

Section 9 of such Act (88 U.S.C. 1108) is amended by striking out “$1,500,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$1,200,000”.


*Approved May 23, 1969.*

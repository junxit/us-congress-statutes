# Public Law 91–131

> AN ACT To authorize appropriations to carry out the Standard Reference Data Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there are hereby authorized to be appropriated to the Department of Commerce such sums as may be necessary for the fiscal years 1970 and 1971, but not to exceed a total of $6,000,000, to carry out the purposes of the Standard Reference Data Act (Public Law 90–396; 82 Stat. 339).


*Approved December 1, 1969.*

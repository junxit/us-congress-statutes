# Public Law 91–182

> JOINT RESOLUTION Establishing that the second regular session of the Ninety-first Congress convene at noon on Monday, January 19, 1970.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second regular session of the Ninety-first Congress shall begin at noon on Monday, January 19, 1970.


*Approved December 30, 1969.*

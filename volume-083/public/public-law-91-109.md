# Public Law 91–109

> AN ACT To amend the Act entitled “An Act to provide for the establishment of the Frederick Douglass home as a part of the park system in the National Capital, and for other purposes”, approved September 5, 1962.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4 of the Act entitled “An Act to provide for the establishment of the Frederick Douglass home as a part of the park system in the National Capital, and for other purposes”, approved September 5, 1962 (76 Stat. 435), is amended to read as follows:

> ## § 4.
>
> There are authorized to be appropriated such sums, but not more than $413,000, as may be needed for the restoration and development of buildings and grounds at Cedar Hill.”


*Approved November 6, 1969.*

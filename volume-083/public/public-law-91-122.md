# Public Law 91–122

> AN ACT To amend section 358a(a) of the Agricultural Adjustment Act of 1938, as amended, to extend the authority to transfer peanut acreage allotments.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 358a (a) of the Agricultural Adjustment Act of 1938, as amended, is amended by changing “and 1969” to read “, 1969, and 1970”.


*Approved November 21, 1969.*

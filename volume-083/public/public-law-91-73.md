# Public Law 91–73

> AN ACT Relating to age limits in connection with appointments to the United States Park Police.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions of Public Law 89–554 (80 Stat. 419, 5 U.S.C. 3307) the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized to determine and fix the minimum and maximum limits of age within which original appointments to the United States Park Police may be made.


*Approved September 26, 1969.*

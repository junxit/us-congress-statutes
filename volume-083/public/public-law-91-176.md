# Public Law 91–176

> JOINT RESOLUTION To authorize appropriations for expenses of the President’s Council on Youth Opportunity.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary for the expenses of the President’s Council on Youth Opportunity, established by Executive Order Numbered 11330 of March 5, 1967.


*Approved December 30, 1969.*

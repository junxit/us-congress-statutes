# Public Law 91–105

> JOINT RESOLUTION To provide for a temporary extension of the authority conferred by the Export Control Act of 1949.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 12 of the Export Control Act of 1949, as amended (50 U.S.C. App. 2032), is amended by striking out “October 31, 1969” and inserting in lieu thereof “December 31, 1969”.

## § 2.

The last paragraph under the heading “Senate” in the First Deficiency Act, fiscal year 1926 (2 U.S.C. 64a) is amended to read as follows:

> “In the event of the death, resignation, or disability of the Secretary of the Senate, the Comptroller of the Senate shall be deemed his successor as a disbursing officer, under his bond as Comptroller, and he shall serve as such disbursing officer until the end of the quarterly period during which a new Secretary shall have been elected and qualified, or such disability shall have been ended.”


*Approved October 31, 1969.*

# Public Law 91–141

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making further continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 1970, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That clause (c) of section 102 of the joint resolution of November 14, 1969 (Public Law 91–117), is hereby amended by striking out “December 6, 1969” and inserting in lieu thereof “the sine die adjournment of the first session of the Ninety-first Congress”.


*Approved December 5, 1969.*

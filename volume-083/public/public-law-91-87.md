# Public Law 91–87

> JOINT RESOLUTION To authorize the President to designate the period beginning November 16, 1969, and ending November 22, 1969, as “National Family Health Week”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Members of the Ninety-first Congress request the President of the United States officially to proclaim the week of November 16 to 22 as National Family Health Week as a means of focusing national attention during the year upon the accomplishments of the American health care system and the central role played by the family physician in the maintenance of superior medical care for Americans of all ages and from all walks of life.


*Approved October 15, 1969.*

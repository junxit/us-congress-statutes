# Public Law 91–107

> AN ACT To amend the provisions of the Perishable Agricultural Commodities Act, 1930, to authorize an increase in license fee, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph (6) of the first section of the Perishable Agricultural Commodities Act, 1930, as amended (7 U.S.C. 499a(6)), is amended by striking out “$90,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$100,000”.

## § 2.

Paragraph (7) of the first section of such Act (7 U.S.C. 499a (7)) is amended by striking out “$90,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$100,000”.

## § 3.

The third sentence of section 3(b) of such Act (7 U.S.C. 499c(b)) is amended by striking out “$50” and inserting in lieu thereof “$100”.


*Approved November 4, 1969.*

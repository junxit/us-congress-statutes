# Public Law 91–146

> AN ACT To authorize the appropriation of funds for Fort Donelson National Battlefield in the State of Tennessee, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding any other provision of law, there are hereby authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to satisfy the final net judgments rendered against the United States in civil actions numbered 3371 and 3397 in the United States District Court for the Middle District of Tennessee, Nashville Division, for the acquisition of lands for the Fort Donelson National Battlefield, totaling $12,721.25, plus interest as provided by law.


*Approved December 16, 1969.*

# Public Law 91–90

> AN ACT To amend the John F. Kennedy Center Act to authorize additional funds for such Center.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** section 8 of the John F. Kennedy Center Act (Public Laws 85–874, 88–260) is amended by striking out “$15,500,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$23,000,000”.
- **(b)** Section 9 of the John F. Kennedy Center Act is amended by striking out “$15,400,000” each of the two places it appears and inserting in lieu thereof in each such place “$20,400,000”.


*Approved October 17, 1969.*

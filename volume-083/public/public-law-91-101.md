# Public Law 91–101

> AN ACT To amend title 38 of the United States Code in order to eliminate the six-month limitation on the furnishing of nursing home care in the case of veterans with service-connected disabilities.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That next to the last sentence of subsection (a) of section 620 of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking out “except where in the judgment of the Administrator a longer period is warranted in the case of any veteran” and inserting in lieu thereof “except (A) in the case of the veteran whose hospitalization was primarily for a service-connected disability, or (B) where in the judgment of the Administrator a longer period is warranted in the case of any other veteran”.


*Approved October 30, 1969.*

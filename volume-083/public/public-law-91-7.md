# Public Law 91–7

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making a supplemental appropriation for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1969, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the following sum is appropriated out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to supply a supplemental appropriation for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1969, and for other purposes; namely:

## DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE

## Commodity Credit Corporation

For partial restoration of capital impairment of the Commodity Credit Corporation for costs heretofore incurred, $1,000,000,000.


*Approved April 1, 1969.*

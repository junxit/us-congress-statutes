# Public Law 91–36

> AN ACT To continue for a temporary period the existing suspension of duty on heptanoic acid, and to continue for one month the existing rates of withholding of income tax.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That item 907.30 of the Tariff Schedules of the United States (19 U.S.C. 1202) is amended by striking out “On or before 8/8/69” and inserting in lieu thereof “On or before 12/31/70”.

## § 2.

- **(a)** Section 3402 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1954 (relating to income tax collected at source) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking out “June 30, 1969” in subsection (a) (1) and inserting in lieu thereof “July 31, 1969”;
  - **(2)** by striking out “July 1, 1969” in subsection (a) (2) and inserting in lieu thereof “August 1, 1969”; and
  - **(3)** by striking out “July 1, 1969” in subsection (c) (6) and inserting in lieu thereof “August 1, 1969”.
- **(b)** The amendments made by subsection (a) shall apply with respect to wages paid after June 30, 1969.


*Approved June 30, 1969.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 315

> ADJOURNMENT--HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses shall adjourn on Wednesday, August 13, 1969, and that when they adjourn on said day they stand adjourned until 12 o’clock noon on Wednesday, September 3, 1969.


*Passed August 12, 1969.*

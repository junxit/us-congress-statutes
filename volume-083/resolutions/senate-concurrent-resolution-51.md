# Senate Concurrent Resolution 51

> CORRECTION OF ENROLLED BILL S. 3016

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Secretary of the Senate, in the enrollment of the bill (S. 3016) to provide for the continuation of programs authorized under the Economic Opportunity Act of 1964, to authorize advance funding of such programs, and for other purposes, is hereby authorized and directed to make the following correction:

In section 114 strike out “section 620(d)” and insert “section 602(d)”.

## § 2.

That the Senate recede and concur in the House amendment to the title of S. 3016.


*Agreed to December 20, 1969.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 77

> JOINT MEETING

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Tuesday, January 14, 1969, at 9 o’clock p.m., for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed January 13, 1969.*

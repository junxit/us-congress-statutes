# House Concurrent Resolution 296

> ADJOURNMENT--HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two houses adjourn on Wednesday, July 2, 1969, they stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Monday, July 7, 1969.


*Passed July 1, 1969.*

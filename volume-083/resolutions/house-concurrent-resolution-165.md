# House Concurrent Resolution 165

> DIAMOND JUBILEE YEAR OF THE AMERICAN MOTION PICTURE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Congress of the United States do hereby designate 1969 as the Diamond Jubilee Year of the American Motion Picture.


*Passed May 5, 1969.*

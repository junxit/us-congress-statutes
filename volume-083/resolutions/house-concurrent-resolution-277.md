# House Concurrent Resolution 277

> ADJOURNMENT--HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Wednesday, May 28, 1969, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Monday, June 2, 1969.


*Passed May 27, 1969.*

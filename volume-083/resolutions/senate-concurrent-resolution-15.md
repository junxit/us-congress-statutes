# Senate Concurrent Resolution 15

> SURVEY OF THE ALLIANCE FOR PROGRESS-COMPILATION OF STUDIES

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there shall be printed as a Senate document a compilation of the studies on the Survey of the Alliance for Progress prepared at the request of the Subcommittee on American Republics Affairs, Committee on Foreign Relations, and the hearings held relating thereto, with illustrations.

## § 2.

There shall be printed three thousand additional copies of such Senate document. Such additional copies shall be for the use of the Committee on Foreign Relations.


*Agreed to April 29, 1969.*

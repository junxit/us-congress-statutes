# Senate Concurrent Resolution 48

> ADJOURNMENT--HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That when the Senate adjourns on Wednesday, November 26, 1969, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. Monday, December 1, 1969; and that when the House adjourns on Wednesday, November 26, 1969, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock noon on Monday, December 1, 1969.


*Agreed to November 25, 1969.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 17

> SAINT LAWRENCE SEAWAY--TENTH ANNIVERSARY

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Congress recognizes the great benefits the Saint Lawrence Seaway has provided in stimulating economic development and prosperity not only within the region of the Great Lakes-Saint Lawrence Seaway system, but throughout the entire United States and the North American Continent, and commends the celebration, during the period from June 26, 1969, through July 7, 1969, of the tenth anniversary of the opening of the Saint Lawrence Seaway to all Americans.


*Agreed to June 26, 1969.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 345

> “A GUIDE TO STUDENT ASSISTANCE”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed as a House document “A Guide to Student Assistance,” prepared by the Committee on Education and Labor; and that sixty-two thousand two hundred additional copies be printed, of which forty-three thousand nine hundred shall be for the use of the House of Representatives, ten thousand three hundred shall be for the use of the Senate, four thousand copies shall be for the use of the Committee on Education and Labor of the House, and four thousand copies shall be for the use of the Committee on Labor and Public Welfare of the Senate.

## § 2.

Copies of such document shall be prorated to Members of the Senate and the House of Representatives for a period of sixty days, after which the unused balance shall revert to the respective Senate and House Documents Room.


*Passed December 11, 1969.*

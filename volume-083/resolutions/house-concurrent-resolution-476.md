# House Concurrent Resolution 476

> SIGNING OF ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That notwithstanding the sine die adjournment of the two Houses, the Speaker of the House of Representatives and the President of the Senate, or the President pro tempore of the Senate, or the acting President pro tempore of the Senate be, and they are hereby, authorized to sign enrolled bills and joint resolutions duly passed by the two Houses and found truly enrolled.


*Passed December 23, 1969.*

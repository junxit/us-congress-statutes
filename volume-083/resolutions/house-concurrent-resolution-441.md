# House Concurrent Resolution 441

> ADJOURNMENT--HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Thursday, November 6, 1969, it stand adjourned until 12:00 meridian, Wednesday, November 12, 1969.


*Passed November 6, 1969.*

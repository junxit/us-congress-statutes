# House Concurrent Resolution 209

> “THE ANALYSIS AND EVALUATION OF PUBLIC EXPENDITURES: THE PPB SYSTEM”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Joint Economic Committee five thousand additional copies of volumes 1, 2, and 3 of its joint committee print of the Ninety-first Congress, first session, entitled “The Analysis and Evaluation of Public Expenditures: The PPB System”.


*Passed July 24, 1969.*

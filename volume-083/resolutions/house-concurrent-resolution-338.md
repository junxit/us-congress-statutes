# House Concurrent Resolution 338

> “STRATEGY AND SCIENCE: TOWARD A NATIONAL SECURITY POLICY FOR THE 1970’S”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the document entitled “Strategy and Science: Toward a National Security Policy for the 1970’s”, hearings by the Subcommittee on National Security Policy and Scientific Developments, dated April 27, 1969, be printed as a House document and that an additional three thousand copies be printed for the use of the Committee on Foreign Affairs of the House of Representatives.


*Passed October 16, 1969.*

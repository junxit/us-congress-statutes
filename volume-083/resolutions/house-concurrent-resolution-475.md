# House Concurrent Resolution 475

> ADJOURNMENT SINE DIE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Tuesday, December 23, 1969, and that when they adjourn on said day, they stand adjourned sine die.


*Passed December 23, 1969.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 191

> ADJOURNMENT-HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Thursday, April 3, 1969, they stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Monday, April 14, 1969.


*Passed April 1, 1969.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 114

> DARTMOUTH COLLEGE-TWO HUNDREDTH ANNIVERSARY

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Congress sends congratulations and greetings to Dartmouth College on the occasion of the two hundredth anniversary of its founding, and extends the hope of the people of the United States that Dartmouth College will continue to grow and prosper in centuries yet to come.


*Passed June 19, 1969.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 473

> CORRECTION OF ENROLLED BILL H. R. 14751

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Clerk of the House of Representatives, in the enrollment of the bill (H.R. 14751) making appropriations for military construction for the Department of Defence for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1970, and for other purposes, is hereby authorized and directed to make the following correction in the text of the House engrossed bill:

On page two of the House engrossed bill, strike out “Public Law 91-____” and insert “Public Law 91–121.”


*Passed December 19, 1969.*

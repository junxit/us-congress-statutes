# House Concurrent Resolution 35

> VETERANS’ BENEFITS CALCULATOR

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That after the conclusion of the second session of the Ninety-first Congress there shall be printed fifty thousand and seventy copies of a Veterans’ Benefits (Calculator prepared by the Veterans’ Affairs Committee of which two thousand copies shall be for the use of the Veterans’ Affairs Committee, two thousand copies for the use of the Committee on Finance, thirty-seven thousand three hundred and fifteen copies for the use of the House of Representatives, and eight thousand seven hundred and fifty-five copies for the use of the Senate.

## § 2.

Copies of such document shall be prorated to Members of the Senate and House of Representatives for a period of sixty days, after which the unused balance shall revert to the respective Senate and House document rooms.


*Passed May 23, 1969.*

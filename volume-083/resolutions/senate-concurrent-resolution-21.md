# Senate Concurrent Resolution 21

> THERMAL POLLUTION--1968 HEARINGS

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Public Works, one thousand additional copies of part I, and seven hundred additional copies of part 2, thermal pollution, 1968 hearings, held during the second session of the Ninetieth Congress.


*Agreed to July 1, 1969.*

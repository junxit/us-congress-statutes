# House Concurrent Resolution 208

> “SUBVERSIVE INFLUENCES IN RIOTS, LOOTING, AND BURNING”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the House Committee on Internal Security three thousand additional copies each of parts 1; 2, and 3 of the publication “Subversive influences in Riots, Looting, and Burning”, Ninetieth Congress.


*Passed July 24, 1969.*

# Private Law 91–55

> AN ACT For the relief of Nickolas George Polizos.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Nickolas George Polizos shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of June 16, 1957.


*Approved October 17, 1969.*

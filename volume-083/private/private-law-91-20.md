# Private Law 91–20

> AN ACT For the relief of George Tilson Weed.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, George Tilson Weed shall be held and considered to have complied with the provisions of section 316 of that Act as they relate to residence and physical presence.


*Approved July 1, 1969.*

# Private Law 91–5

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Roberto de la Caridad Miquel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Roberto de la Caridad Miquel shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of September 8, 1961.


*Approved May 15, 1969.*

# Private Law 91–15

> AN ACT To confer United States citizenship posthumously upon Specialist Four Klaus Josef Strauss.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Specialist Four Klaus Josef Strauss, a native of Germany, who served honorably in the United States Army from November 15, 1966, until his death on February 8, 1968, shall be held and considered to have been a citizen of the United States at the time of his death.


*Approved June 30, 1969.*

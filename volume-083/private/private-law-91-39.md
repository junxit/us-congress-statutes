# Private Law 91–39

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Vita Cusumano.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Vita Cusumano shall be deemed to have a priority date of August 25, 1954, on the fifth preference foreign state limitation for Italy.


*Approved August 20, 1969.*

# Private Law 91–60

> AN ACT For the relief of Mr. and Mrs. Wong Yui.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mr. and Mrs. Wong Yui shall be held and considered to have been born in China.


*Approved December 2, 1969.*

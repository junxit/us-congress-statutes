# Private Law 91–11

> AN ACT For the relief of Noriko Susan Duke (Nakano).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Noriko Susan Duke (Nakano) shall be held and considered to be within the purview of section 323 (c) of such Act.


*Approved June 13, 1969.*

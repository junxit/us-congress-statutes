# Private Law 91–46

> AN ACT For the relief of Cheng-huai Li.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Cheng-huai Li shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of July 7, 1962.


*Approved September 26, 1969.*

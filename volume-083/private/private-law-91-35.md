# Private Law 91–35

> AN ACT For relief of Romeo de la Torre Sanano and his sister, Julieta de la Torre Sanano.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Romeo de la Torre Sanano and his sister, Julieta de la Torre Sanano, may be classified as children within the meaning of section 101 (b)(1) (F) of the Act, upon approval of petitions filed in their behalf by Captain and Mrs. Andres S. Sanano. citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of the Act: Provided, That the natural brothers or sisters of the beneficiaries shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved August 18, 1969.*

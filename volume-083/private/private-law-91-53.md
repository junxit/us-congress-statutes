# Private Law 91–53

> AN ACT For the relief of John (Giovanni) Denaro.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, John (Giovanni) Denaro shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of October 23, 1962.


*Approved October 17, 1969.*

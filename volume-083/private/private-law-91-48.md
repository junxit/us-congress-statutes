# Private Law 91–48

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Jagir Singh Randhawa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Jagir S. Randhawa shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of September 4, 1957.


*Approved September 29, 1969.*

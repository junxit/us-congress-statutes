# Private Law 91–50

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Konstantinos Nicholaos Babaliaros.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Konstantinos Nicholaos Babaliaros shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of July 27, 1962.


*Approved October 14, 1969.*

# Private Law 91–17

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Marjorie J. Hottenroth.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Marjorie J. Hottenroth, the widow of a United States citizen, shall be deemed to be an immediate relative and the provisions of section 204 of the Act shall be inapplicable in her case.


*Approved July 1, 1969.*

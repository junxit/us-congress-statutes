# Private Law 91–62

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Irene G. Queja.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Irene G. Queja shall be held and considered to be within the purview of section 203(a) (2) of that Act and the provisions of section 204 of the said Act shall not be applicable in this case.


*Approved December 15, 1969.*

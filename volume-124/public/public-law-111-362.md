# Public Law 111–362

> An Act To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 47 South 7th Street in Indiana, Pennsylvania, as the “James M. ‘Jimmy’ Stewart Post Office Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. JAMES M. “JIMMY” STEWART POST OFFICE BUILDING.

- **(a)** *Designation.—* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 47 South 7th Street in Indiana, Pennsylvania, shall be known and designated as the “James M. ‘Jimmy’ Stewart Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References.—* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “James M. ‘Jimmy’ Stewart Post Office Building”.


*Approved January 4, 2011.*

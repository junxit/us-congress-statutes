# Public Law 111–222

> An Act To amend the National Law Enforcement Museum Act to extend the termination date.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. NATIONAL LAW ENFORCEMENT MUSEUM ACT.

Section 4(f) of the National Law Enforcement Museum Act (Public Law 106–492) is amended by striking “10 years” and inserting “13 years”.


*Approved August 6, 2010.*

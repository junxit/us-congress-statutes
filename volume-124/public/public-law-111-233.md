# Public Law 111–233

> An Act To reauthorize State agricultural mediation programs under title V of the Agricultural Credit Act of 1987.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. SHORT TITLE.

This Act may be cited as the “Agricultural Credit Act of 2010”.

## § 2. AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS.

Section 506 of the Agricultural Credit Act of 1987 (7 U.S.C. 5106) is amended by striking “2010” and inserting “2015”.


*Approved August 16, 2010.*

# Public Law 111–290

> Joint Resolution Making further continuing appropriations for fiscal year 2011, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Continuing Appropriations Act, 2011 (Public Law 111–242) is amended by striking the date specified in section 106(3) and inserting “December 18, 2010”.


*Approved December 4, 2010.*

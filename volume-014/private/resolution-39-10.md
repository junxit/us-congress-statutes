# Resolution 10

> Joint Resolution for the Relief of William D. Nelson.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid to William D. Nelson one thousand dollars for his services in recruiting for the Union army in East Tennessee during the years eighteen hundred and sixty-one and eighteen hundred and sixty-two.


*Approved, January 31, 1867.*

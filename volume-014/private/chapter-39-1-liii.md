# Chapter LIII

> An Act granting a Pension to Mrs. Elizabeth York, widow of Shubal York, late a Surgeon in the Fifty-fourth Regiment Illinois Infantry, Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Elizabeth York, widow of Shubal York, late a surgeon in the Fifty-fourth regiment Illinois volunteer infantry, on the pension rolls, at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month, to commence from and after the passage of this act, and to continue during her widowhood.


*Approved, April 17, 1866.*

# Chapter XCI

> An Act granting [a] Pension to Spencer Kellogg.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Spencer Kellogg, of Oswego county, State of New York, on the roll of invalid pensions, and pay or cause to be paid to his legally appointed guardian the sum of twenty dollars per month, until he, the said Spencer Kellogg, shall have attained the age of sixteen years; this act to take effect from the sixth day of September, eighteen hundred and sixty-five.


*Approved, May 22, 1866.*

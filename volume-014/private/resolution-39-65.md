# Resolution 65

> Joint Resolution providing for the Settlement of Accounts of W. H. Hamrick.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the Treasury Department be directed to settie the accounts of the late Wyatt H. Hamrick, lieutenant and quartermaster of the thirty-ninth Ohio volunteers, upon equitable terms, and upon the best evidence available.


*Approved, July 3, 1866.*

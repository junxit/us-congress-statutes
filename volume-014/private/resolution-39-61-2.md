# Resolution 61

> Joint Resolution to pay Lieutenant John H. Hamlin for Military Services.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Paymaster-General of the United States army be, and he is hereby, directed to pay to John H. Hamlin, of Michigan, late a first lieutenant in the seventh regiment of Michigan cavalry, the full pay and allowances of a second lieutenant of cavalry for the time from the first day of July, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, to the twenty-eighth day of March, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, when he was mustered in as a first lieutenant.


*Approved, March 2, 1867.*

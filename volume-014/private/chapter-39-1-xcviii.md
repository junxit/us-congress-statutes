# Chapter XCVIII

> An Act to grant a Pension to Leonard St. Clair.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to place the name of Leonard St. Clair on the pension rolls of the United Suites as a pensioner, at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, May 24, 1866.*

# Chapter LXXVIII

> An Act for the Relief of R. L. B. Clarke.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Treasurer of the United States be, and he is hereby, directed to pay, out of any money in the treasury of the United States not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one thousand five hundred dollars to R. L. B. Clarke, in full for the time and expense incurred by him in contesting the seat of Augustus Hall, from the first district of Iowa, in the thirty-fourth Congress.


*Approved, May 9, 1866.*

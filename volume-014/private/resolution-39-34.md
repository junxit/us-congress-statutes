# Resolution 34

> Joint Resolution to permit Captain John A. Webster, Jr. of the Steamer “Mahoning,” to receive from the Government of Great Britain a Gold Chronometer.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That John A. Webster, Jr. of the revenue-cutter service, captain of the steamer “Mahoning,” be and he is hereby, permitted to receive from the government of Great Britain a gold chronometer, which that government is desirous to award to him, in token of its appreciation of valuable services rendered by Captain Webster to several British vessels in distress on our coast.


*Approved, February 28, 1867.*

# Chapter CXXXIX

> An Act changing the Name of Emil Cohen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Emil Cohen, of the city of Washington, in the District of Columbia, be, and he is hereby, authorized, from and after the passage of this act, to take and use the surname of Cornely, and that his name hereafter be Emil Comely; and all acts done and entered into by that name shall have the same effect and operation in law as if his name had originally been Emil Cornely, of Washington, in the District of Columbia.


*Approved, June 23, 1866.*

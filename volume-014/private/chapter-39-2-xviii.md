# Chapter XVIII

> An Act for the Relief of Catharine Welsh.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Catharine Welsh, widow of private John Welsh, late of company E, twenty-sixth regiment of Illinois volunteer infantry, be authorized to receive the bounty, back pay, and allowances due to said John Welsh, without being required to make other or further proof of his death than that already furnished.


*Approved, January 31, 1867.*

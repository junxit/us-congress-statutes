# Chapter LXXXVII

> An Act for the Benefit of Mrs. Jerusha Page.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Mrs. Jerusha Page, of the State of Missouri, the widow of the late Thomas C. Page, deceased, on the pension roll, at the rate of eight dollars per month, to commence from the passage of this act and to continue during her widowhood.


*Approved, February 25, 1867.*

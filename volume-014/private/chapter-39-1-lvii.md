# Chapter LVII

> An Act granting Pension to Lewis W. Dietrich.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Lewis W. Dietrich, late a second lieutenant of company “E,” thirtieth regiment U. S. colored troops, on the pension list, and to pay him a pension at the rate of fifteen dollars per month; this act to take effect from and after its passage.


*Approved, April 17, 1866.*

# Chapter XCI

> An Act for the Relief of Mary A. Smith, of Johnson County, Tennessee, Widow of Alexander D. Smith, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, instructed to place the name of Mary A. Smith of Johnson county, Tennessee, on the pension rolls, at the rate of thirty dollars per month, to commence from the fifth day of November, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, and to continue during her widowhood, upon satisfactory proof that she was and is the widow of Alexander D. Smith, late a lieutenant-colonel of the thirteenth regiment Tennessee cavalry volunteers.


*Approved, February 25, 1867.*

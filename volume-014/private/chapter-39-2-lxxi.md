# Chapter LXXI

> An Act for the Relief of Daniel Frederick Bakeman, a Revolutionary Soldier.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, directed to place the name of Daniel Frederick Bakeman, of Sandusky, New York, upon the pension roll, and there be paid to said Daniel Frederick Bakeman, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of five hundred dollars per annum during his natural life, payable semi-annually, commencing on the first day of July, eighteen hundred and sixty-six.


*Approved, February 22, 1867.*

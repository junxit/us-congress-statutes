# Chapter CLXXXVII

> An Act for the Relief of Joel Farley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Joel Farley, late a private in company F eleventh Iowa volunteer infantry, on the pension rolls, at the rate of fifteen dollars per month.


*Approved, July 13, 1866.*

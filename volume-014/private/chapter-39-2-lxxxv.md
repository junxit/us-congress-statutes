# Chapter LXXXV

> An Act granting a Pension to Charles N. Weiss.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Charles N. Weiss, of the District of Columbia, on the pension roll, at the rate of fifteen dollars per month, to commence from the passage of this act and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, February 25, 1867.*

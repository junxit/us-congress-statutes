# Chapter CXXXIII

> An Act for the Relief of Mary A. Cross.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to cause to be paid to Mary A. Cross, of Fremont, Ohio, widow of Hubbard II. Cross, late private in company F, seventy-second regiment Ohio volunteer infantry, a pension from the twelfth day of June, anno Domini eighteen hundred and sixty-two, (the date of the death of her husband,) to the date at which the pension heretofore granted to her commenced, and at the same rate.


*Approved, February 28, 1867.*

# Chapter XCIV

> An Act for the Relief of Martha J. Willey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Martha J. Willey, widow of George W. Willey, late a corporal in company F, seventh regiment New Hampshire volunteers, on the pension rolls, at the rate of eight dollars per month, said pension to commence on the eighteenth day of April, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, and to continue during her widowhood; and in the event of the marriage or death of said Martha J. Willey, then to the minor children of George W. Willey, subject to the limitations and restrictions of the pension laws.


*Approved, May 22, 1866.*

# Chapter CXXXII

> An Act granting a Pension to John Rogers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of John Rogers, a soldier in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve, and a private in Captain James Payne’s company of Virginia militia, on the pension rolls, at the rate of eight dollars per mouth.


*Approved, February 28, 1867.*

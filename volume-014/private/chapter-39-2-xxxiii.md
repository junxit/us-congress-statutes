# Chapter XXXIII

> An Act for the Relief of Captain James Starkey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of one hundred dollars be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to pay to James Starkey, late captain of the St. Paul light cavalry, the amount by him paid to Richard Postel, for the loss of a horse killed in a fight with Indians, in eighteen hundred and fifty-seven.


*Approved, February 5, 1867.*

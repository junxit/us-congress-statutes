# Chapter XXXVIII

> An Act for the Relief of Emma J. Hall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to place the name of Emma Jane Hall, widow of Perry Hall, deceased, late a chaplain in the seventy-ninth regiment, Indiana volunteers, upon the pension rolls, at the rate now prescribed by law for officers of his rank, and to be continued during her widowhood.


*Approved, April 10, 1866.*

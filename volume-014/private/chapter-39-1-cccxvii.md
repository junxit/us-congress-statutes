# Chapter CCCXVII

> An Act granting a Pension to Daniel Lucas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Daniel Lucas, of Plymouth, Massachusetts, formerly a private of company E. third U. S. infantry, on the roll of invalid pensions, and pay to him monthly such a sum as he may be entitled [to] under the limitations and restrictions regulating the payment of pensions.


*Approved, July 28, 1866.*

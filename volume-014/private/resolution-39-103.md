# Resolution 103

> Joint Resolution to reimburse Mrs. Mary Phelps, of Missouri.

*Re it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid to Mrs. Mary Phelps, of Missouri, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of twenty thousand dollars to reimburse her for expenditures made by her in raising and equipping troops for the United States in the late rebellion, and also for her expenditures made in behalf of the soldiers of the Union wounded in battle, and of the orphan children of soldiers of the Union.


*Approved, July 28, 1866.*

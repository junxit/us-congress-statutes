# Resolution 39

> Joint Resolution for the Relief of Obadiah Aderton.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be directed to place the name of Obadiah Aderton, of the State of Maine, on the list of pensions at the rate of eight dollars per month, payment to commence with the date of the passage of this joint resolution.


*Approved, February 28, 1867.*

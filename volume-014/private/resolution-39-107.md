# Resolution 107

> Joint Resolution authorizing the Secretary of the Interior to pay Charles M. Pott a Pension of fifteen Dollars per Month.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay or cause to be paid to Charles M. Pott, late of company K, one hundred and seventy-ninth Pennsylvania militia, now on the pension roll, the same pension provided for, for persons having lost one hand in the military service of the United States, as provided in section one of an act entitled “An act supplementary to the several acts relating to pensions,” approved June sixth, eighteen hundred and sixty-six.


*Approved, July 28, 1866.*

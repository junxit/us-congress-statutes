# Chapter LXXXIV

> An Act for the Relief of Kennedy O’Brien.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Kennedy O’Brien, late a private in company K, fifth regiment Indiana volunteers, from eight dollars per month to twenty-five dollars per month, and to pay him such increased pension from the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 25, 1867.*

# Chapter CXVIII

> An Act to confirm the Title of José Serafin Ramirez to certain Lands in New Mexico.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the grant to José Serafin Ramirez of the Canon del Agua, as approved by the surveyor-general of New Mexico January twenty, eighteen hundred and sixty, and designated as number seventy in the transcript of private land claims in New Mexico, transmitted to Congress by the Secretary of the Interior January eleven, eighteen hundred and sixty-one, is hereby confirmed: Provided, however, That this confirmation shall only be construed as a relinquishment on the part of the United States, and shall not affect the adverse rights of any persons whomsoever.


*Approved, June 12, 1866.*

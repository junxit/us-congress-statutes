# Chapter XIX

> An Act for the Relief of Solomon P. Smith.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there shall be paid to Solomon P. Smith, late a captain in the one hundred and fifteenth regiment of New York volunteers, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of two hundred and sixty dollars for his pension from the fourteenth day of January, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, when he was mustered out of the service, until the fifteenth day of February, eighteen hundred and sixty-six, the date of tho filing of his application for a pension with the commissioner of pensions.


*Approved, January 31, 1867.*

# Chapter CCXXVIII

> An Act for the Relief of Liston H. Pearce.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, directed to pay to the Rev. Liston H. Pearce the sum of five hundred and forty dollars, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, in full for his services as chaplain of the one hundred and thirty-second regiment of Illinois volunteers, during the recent rebellion.


*Approved, July 23, 1866.*

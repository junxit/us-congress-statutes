# Chapter CCX

> An Act for the Relief of Frank Pugsley, late a Private Soldier in Company I, of the Third Regiment of New Hampshire Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the final settlement of the accounts of Frank Pugsley, as a private soldier in Company I, of the third regiment of New Hampshire volunteers, the accounting officers of the treasury are authorized and required to regard the date of his discharge from the service of the United States as of the twenty-fourth day of October, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, and to compute his pay and allowances as such soldier to that time.


*Approved, March 2, 1867.*

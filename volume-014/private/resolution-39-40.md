# Resolution 40

> Joint Resolution for the Relief of the Mother of Charles O. Rowohl.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, or cause to be paid, to the mother of Charles O. Rowohl, deceased, late of company I, eighth regiment Kansas volunteers, eight dollars per month, from the date of the death of her son Charles O. Rowohl to the time when she commenced receiving a pension under the general pension laws.


*Approved, February 28, 1867.*

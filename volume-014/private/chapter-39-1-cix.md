# Chapter CIX

> An Act for the Relief of Cordelia Murray.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to pay to Cordelia Murray, widow of George W. Murray, a pension equal in amount to the pension granted to the said George W. Murray, by an act of Congress approved December twentieth, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, entitled “An act for the relief of George W. Murray.”


*Approved, June 6, 1866.*

# Resolution 19

> A Resolution for the Relief of Charles Clark, Marshal of the United States for the District of Maine.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized, in the settlement of the accounts of Charles Clark, marshal of the United States for the district of Maine, to allow him credit for such sum of public money as was in his charge as marshal, not to exceed three thousand and twenty-eight dollars, as he may be satisfied was burned in said marshal’s office, in the custom-house building at Portland, Maine, on the fourth day of July last.


*Approved, February 18, 1867.*

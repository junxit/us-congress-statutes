# Chapter CLXXXV

> An Act amendatory of an Art entitled “At Act granting a Pension to Mrs. Emerance Gouler.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That an act approved April eighteen, eighteen hundred and sixty-six, granting a pension to Mrs. Emerance Gouler, be so amended as to continue the pension granted to Mrs. Gouler, in the event of her death or remarriage, to her minor children under the age of sixteen years, had by her late husband, Charles Gouler, a private in company F, ninth New Hampshire volunteers.


*Approved, July 13, 1866.*

# Chapter CLXXXVIII

> An Act for the Relief of James L. Perham.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the. United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to James L. Perham, late of company “G” tenth regiment Maine volunteers, a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month, from February fourth, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, to November seventeenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, amouuting to one hundred and seventy-one dollars and thirty-six cents.


*Approved, July 13, 1866.*

# Resolution 25

> Joint Resolution for the Relief of Stephen E. Jones.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the paymaster-general is hereby directed to pay to Stephen E. Jones the full pay and allowances of a first lieutenant of cavalry for the period during which he actually served as aid-de-camp on the staff of General Nelson and General George H. Thomas, prior to his muster into the United States service.


*Approved, February 22, 1867.*

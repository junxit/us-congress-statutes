# Chapter CCV

> An Act granting [a] Pension to Jonathan W. Beach.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place or have placed upon the pension rolls the name of Jonathan W. Beach, and pay to him the sum of twenty-five dollars per month during his blindness. This act to take effect from and after its passage.


*Approved, July 18, 1866.*

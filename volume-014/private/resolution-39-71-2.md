# Resolution 71

> Joint Resolution for the Relief of J. H. Riley.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of two hundred dollars be allowed and paid to J. H. Riley for services rendered by him as clerk to the house committee on the Pacific railroad during the first and second sessions of the present Congress.


*Approved, March 2, 1867.*

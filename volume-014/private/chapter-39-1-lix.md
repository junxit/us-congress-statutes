# Chapter LIX

> An Act granting a Pension to Mrs. Sarah E. Wilson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Mrs. Sarah E. Wilson, widow of William II. Wilson, late acting surgeon United States volunteers, on the pension roll, at the rate of seventeen dollars per month, to commence from the passage of this act, and to continue during her widowhood.


*Approved, April 18, 1866.*

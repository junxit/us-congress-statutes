# Chapter CXI

> An Act for the Relief of James Riddle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, aut[h]orized and directed to place the name of James -Riddle, a resident of the city of New York, and late of company “G,” eighth United States infantry, on the pension rolls, at the rate of eight dollars per month, to commence from the twenty-ninth day of July, eighteen hundred and sixty-three.


*Approved, February 28, 1867.*

# Resolution 89

> A Resolution authorizing the Secretary of the Treasury to audit and settle the Accounts of Caleb T. Fay and William Y. Patch, late Assessor and Collector of Internal Revenue at San Francisco.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be authorized to audit and settle the accounts of Caleb T. Fay, and William Y. Patch, late assessor and collector of internal revenue at San Francisco, as to him may appear just and equitable.


*Approved, July 26, 1866.*

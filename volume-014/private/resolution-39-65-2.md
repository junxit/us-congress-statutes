# Resolution 65

> Joint Resolution referring the Papers in the Case of F. A. Gibbons and F. X. Kelley to the Court of Claims.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the papers in the case of Francis A. Gibbons and F. X. Kelley be referred back to the court of claims, and the court shall be authorized, if of opinion that the ends of justice will be subserved thereby, to grant a rehearing of the case on an amended petition, without prejudice to the claim by reason of the former decision of said court.


*Approved, March 2, 1867.*

# Chapter XC

> An Act for the Relief [of] Charles Appleton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Charles Appleton, late a soldier in the United States army, from eight dollars per month to fifteen dollars per month, and to pay him such increased pension from the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 25, 1867.*

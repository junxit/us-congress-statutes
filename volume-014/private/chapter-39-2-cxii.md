# Chapter CXII

> An Act for the Relief of J. and O. P. Cobb & Co.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the treasurer of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to J. and O. P. Cobb & Co., of Aurora, Indiana, the sum of seven thousand eight hundred and ninety dollars and eighteen cents, out of any money heretofore or hereafter appropriated and applicable to the payment of claims against the quartermaster’s bureau, in full discharge of all claim of the said J. and O. P. Cobb & Co. for bay taken and destroyed by order of General Boyle, on the Ohio river, in July, eighteen hundred and sixty-three.


*Approved, February 28, 1867.*

# Resolution 108

> Joint Resolution to authorize the Payment of Rev. C. B. Boynton, as Chaplain of the House of Representatives of the thirty-ninth Congress.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Rev. Charles B. Boynton is authorized to draw the amount appropriated to the payment of the chaplain of the House for the Thirty-ninth Congress.


*Approved, July 28, 1866.*

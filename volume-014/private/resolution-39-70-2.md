# Resolution 70

> A Resolution for the Relief of Dyer B. Pettijohn.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to cause to be paid to Dyer B. Pettijohn the full pay and emoluments of a second lieutenant of infantry, of the first company of sharpshooters, Minnesota volunteers, from the thirtieth day of May, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, to the twentieth day of May, eighteen hundred and sixty-live, deducting therefrom any amount he may have received as an enlisted man during the time specified.


*Approved, March 2, 1867.*

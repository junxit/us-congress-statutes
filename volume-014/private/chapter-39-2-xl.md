# Chapter XL

> An Act for the Relief of the Sureties of James T. Pollock, late Receiver at Crawfordsville, Indiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Robert C. Gregory, Henry Crawford, William Galey, and the other sureties of James T. Pollock, late receiver at Crawfordsville, Indiana, by bond to the United States dated January thirtieth, in the year of our Lord eighteen hundred and thirty-seven, be, and they are hereby, released from their said liability arising from any defalcation, omission, or misconduct of the said James T. Pollock as such receiver, and the proper officer of the Treasury Department be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to dismiss any and all suits that may have been instituted, and are now pending in favor of the United States, against the sureties aforesaid, growing out of the default of the said receiver, James T. Pollock.


*Approved, February 15, 1867.*

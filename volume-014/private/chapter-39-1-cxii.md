# Chapter CXII

> An Act granting a Pension to Mrs. Martha Stevens.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Mrs. Martha Stevens, widow of John F. Stevens, late deputy provost-marshal of the fourth congressional district of the State of Indiana, on the pension roll, at the rate of seventeen dollars per month, to commence from the tenth day of June, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, and to continue during her widowhood.


*Approved, June 8, 1866.*

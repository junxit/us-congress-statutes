# Chapter LIII

> An Act for the Relief of Hiram Hedrick.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Hiram Hedrick, of Peoria, Illinois, late a private in company D, eleventh regiment, Illinois cavalry, on the pension rolls, at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month, and to pay him at this rate in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 18, 1867.*

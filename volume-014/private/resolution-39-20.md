# Resolution 20

> Joint Resolution for the Relief of James Keenan.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is directed, in the settlement of the accounts of James Keenan, late consul at Hong Kong, China, to pay his legal representatives the amount of exchange to which he would have been entitled for loss, had he drawn the several balances due him on the adjustment of his accounts.


*Approved, February 18, 1867.*

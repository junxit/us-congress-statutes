# Chapter CXXXVII

> An Act granting a Pension to William Gleason.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of William Gleason, who enlisted on the fifth day of November, eighteen hundred and sixty, at Newport barracks, State of Kentucky, and was discharged on the fourteenth day of October, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, on the pension rolls, at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, February 28, 1867.*

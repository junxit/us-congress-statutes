# Chapter CCVI

> An Act granting a Pension to Mrs. Charlotte E. Reed.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized to issue to Mrs. Charlotte E. Reed, widow of John D. Reed, late of Falls Church, Fairfax county, Virginia, a pension certificate, and place her name on the roll of pensioners, with the pay and under the conditions and limitations of a widow’ of a private of infantry.


*Approved, July 18, 1866.*

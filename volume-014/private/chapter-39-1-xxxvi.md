# Chapter XXXVI

> An Act for the Relief of F. A. Patterson, late Captain of the Third Virginia Cavalry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay Captain F. A. Patterson full amount of pay and emoluments as a captain of the third Virginia cavalry from the twenty-fifth day of November, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, the date of his commission by the governor of Virginia, to the date of muster out of his regiment.


*Approved, April 10, 1866.*

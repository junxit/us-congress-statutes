# Chapter CVI

> An Act granting [a] Pension to Mrs. Sally Andrews.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Sally Andrews, of Buxton, York county, Maine, widow of the late Elisha Andrews, quarter gunner on board the “Levetta Adams,” on the pension list of invalid pensioners, and pay or cause to be paid to her the sum of eight dollars per month during her widowhood, the pension to commence from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 6, 1866.*

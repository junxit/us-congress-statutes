# Resolution 106

> Joint Resolution to pay Colonel Lewis F. Fix.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the United States are hereby directed to pay to Lewis Ferdinand Fix of Ohio, the pay and emoluments of a lieutenant-colonel of infantry, for the time from the first day of March, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, to the twenty-ninth of July, eighteen hundred and sixty-five.


*Approved, July 28, 1866.*

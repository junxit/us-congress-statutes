# Chapter CXVI

> An Act for the Relief of Thomas Glasgow.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Thomas Glasgow, who was in the service of the United States from November, eighteen hundred and fourteen, until June, eighteen hundred and fifteen, on the pension rolls, at the rate of eight dollars per month, commencing March fourth, eighteen hundred and sixty-one, and to continue the same during his natural life.


*Approved, February 28, 1867.*

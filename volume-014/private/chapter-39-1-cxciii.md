# Chapter CXCIII

> An Act for the Relief of John Gordon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of John Gordon, late of company “G,” ninth United States infantry, upon the pension rolls at the rate of eight dollars per month, and pay him the same from and after the passage of this act, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, July 13, 1866.*

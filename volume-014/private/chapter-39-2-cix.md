# Chapter CIX

> An Act for the Relief of Hiram Paulding, Rear-Admiral United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, directed to audit the accounts of Hiram Paulding while a captain in the United States navy, covering his expenditures in the entertainment of foreign officers and people who visited the frigate “Saint Lawrence,” under his command, at Southampton, not exceeding the sum of nine hundred and sixty-three dollars and ninety-two cents; and also his expenditures in entertaining the officers of the governments of Bremerhaven and Stockholm, in the years eighteen hundred and forty-eight, eighteen hundred and forty-nine, and eighteen hundred and fifty, not exceeding the sum of two thousand six hundred and ninety dollars.


*Approved, February 28, 1867.*

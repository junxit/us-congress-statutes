# Chapter XCII

> An Act granting a Pension to Mrs. Ernestine Becker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Mrs. Ernestine Becker, widow of Leopold Becker, late captain of company D, twenty-fourth regiment Illinois infantry volunteers, on the pension roll, at the rate of twenty dollars per month, to commence from the fifth day of May, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, and to continue during her widowhood.


*Approved, February 25, 1867.*

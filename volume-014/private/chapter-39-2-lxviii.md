# Chapter LXVIII

> An Act for the Relief of John Gray, a Revolutionary Soldier.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of John Gray, of Noble county, Ohio, upon the pension roil, and that there be paid to said John Gray, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of five hundred dollars per annum during his natural life, payable semi-annually, commencing on the first day of July, eighteen hundred and sixty-six.


*Approved, February 22, 1867.*

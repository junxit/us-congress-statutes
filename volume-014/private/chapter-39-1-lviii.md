# Chapter LVIII

> An Act for the Relief of James G. Clarke.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby directed, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to pay to James G. Clarke the sum of six thousand four hundred and eighty-three dollars and ninety-six cents (S G,483 96), in full for services as acting charge d’affaires of the United States at Brussels from June eleventh, eighteen hundred and fifty-seven, to September twenty-seventh, eighteen hundred and fifty-eight.


*Approved, April 18, 1866.*

# Chapter CXXVI

> An Act for the Relief of Daniel McMahon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Daniel McMahon, late a captain in the twentieth regiment New York State militia, a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month, from June twenty-ninth, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, to February twenty-seventh, eighteen hundred and sixty-six.


*Approved, February 28, 1867.*

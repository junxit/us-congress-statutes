# Chapter CXXI

> An Act for the Relief of Levisa Daniel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Levisa Daniel, widow of Joseph Daniel, late of the first Tennessee volunteers, a pension, at the rate of eight dollars per month, from the ninth day of May, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, to the eleventh day of September, eighteen hundred and sixty-five.


*Approved, February 28, 1867.*

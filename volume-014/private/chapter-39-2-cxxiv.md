# Chapter CXXIV

> An Act for the Relief of Mary B. Fowler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Mary B. Fowler, of Cleveland, Ohio, on the pension rolls, at the rate of eight dollars per month, subject to the provisions of the law relative to dependent mothers of deceased soldiers.


*Approved, February 28, 1867.*

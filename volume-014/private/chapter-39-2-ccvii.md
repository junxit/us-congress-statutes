# Chapter CCVII

> An Act for the Relief of Reverend Samuel M. Beatty, of Ohio.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid to Samuel M. Beatty, of Ohio, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of four hundred and thirteen dollars and sixty cents, the pay and allowance due to him as chaplain from January twelfth, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, to April twenty-fifth, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, at the United States hospital at Cleveland, Ohio.


*Approved, March 2, 1867.*

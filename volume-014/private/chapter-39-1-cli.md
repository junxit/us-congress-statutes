# Chapter CLI

> An Act granting a Pension to Mrs. Harriet B. Crocker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Mrs. Harriet B. Crocker, mother of Henry B. Crocker, late a private in company G, one hundred and fifteenth regiment Ohio volunteer infantry, on the pension roll, at the rate of eight dollars per month, to commence from the fourth day of October, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, and to continue during her widowhood.


*Approved, June 27, 1866.*

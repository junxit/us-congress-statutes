# Chapter LII

> An Act granting Pension to Virginia K. V. Moore.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Virginia K. V. Moore, daughter of Richard D. Moore, deceased, late of Co. “K,” seventy-second regiment Illinois Volunteers, on the list of invalid pensioners, and pay to her, or her legally appointed guardian, the sum of eight dollars per month, from December third, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, the date of the death of Richard D. Moore, until she shall have attained the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, April 17, 1866.*

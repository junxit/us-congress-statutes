# Chapter CXXXIV

> An Act granting Arrears of Pension to Sally Allen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representaliues of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to cause to be paid to Sally Allen, widow of Isaac Allen, of Maine, twenty-five dollars and thirty-three cents, being the amount due her husband by special act of April twenty-sixth, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, at the time of his death.


*Approved, February 28, 1867.*

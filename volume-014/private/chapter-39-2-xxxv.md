# Chapter XXXV

> An Act for the Relief of E. J. Curley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to pay, or cause to be paid, E. J. Curley, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of thirty-four thousand two hundred and forty-eight dollars and fifty-two cents, as compensation in full for corn purchased of him by Captain E. B. W. Reslieaux, assistant-quartermaster, on the part of the government.


*Approved, February 8, 1867.*

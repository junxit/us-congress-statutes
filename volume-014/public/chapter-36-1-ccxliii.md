# Chapter CCXLIII

> An Act to change the Place of holding Court in the Northern District of Georgia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the district court furtile northern district of Georgia shall hereafter be held at Atlanta, instead of Marietta; and the clerk of said northern district is hereby required to remove all the books, papers, and records belonging to his office from Marietta to Atlanta.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That all process made returnable to the court heretofore held at Marietta shall be taken and considered returnable to the court tit Atlanta.


*Approved, July 25, 1866.*

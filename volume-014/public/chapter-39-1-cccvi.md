# Chapter CCCVI

> An Act supplemental to the Act to appropriate Money for the Postal Services.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be, and hereby is, appropriated, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, for carrying the mail upon the post roads established by acts of Congress passed during the first session of the Thirty-ninth Congress, for the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, eighteen hundred and sixty-seven, the sum of four hundred and eighty-six thousand five hundred and twenty-five dollars.


*Approved, July 28, 1866.*

# Chapter LXXXVIII

> An Act amendatory of “An Act to provide for the Reports of Decisions of the Supreme Court of the United States.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the reporter of the decisions of the Supreme Court of the United States shall hereafter be allowed the term of eight months for the publication of his reports instead of six, as provided by the act of August twenty-nine, eighteen hundred and forty-two.


*Approved, May 21, 1866.*

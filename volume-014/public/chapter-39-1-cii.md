# Chapter CII

> An Act to repeal Section twenty-three of Chapter seventy-nine of the Acts of the Third Session of the Thirty-Seventh Congress, relating to Passports.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section twenty-three of an act entitled “An act making appropriations for sundry civil expenses of the government for the year ending June thirty, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, and for the year ending the thirtieth June, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, and for other purposes,” be, and the same is hereby, repealed. And hereafter passports shall be issued only to citizens of the United States.


*Approved, May 30, 1866.*

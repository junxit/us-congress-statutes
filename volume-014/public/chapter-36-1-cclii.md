# Chapter CCLII

> An Act to change the port of Entry in Puget’s Sound.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the first day of October, eighteen hundred and sixty-six, the port of Port Angelos, in the district of Puget’s Sound, in Washington Territory, is hereby abolished as a port of entry, and that Port Townsend be, and is hereby, established as the port of entry and delivery for the said district, from and after said date.


*Approved, July 25, 1866.*

# Chapter CLVII

> An Act to amend an Act entitled “An Act to continue, alter, and amend the Charter of the City of Washington,” approved May seventeen, eighteen hundred and forty-eight.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second section of the act entitled “An act to continue, alter, and amend the charter of the city of Washington,” approved May seventeen, eighteen hundred and forty-eight, shall be so amended as to read, “To license, tax, and regulate agencies of all kinds of insurance companies: Provided, That the tax or license shall not exceed one per centum upon the cash premiums received.”

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That all acts and parts of acts inconsistent with this act are hereby repealed.


*Approved, March 2, 1867.*

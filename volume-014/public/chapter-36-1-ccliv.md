# Chapter CCLIV

> An Act in Relation to the unlawful Tapping of Government Water Pipes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the unlawful tapping of any water pipe laid down in the District of Columbia by authority of the United States is hereby declared to be a misdemeanor and an indictable offence; and any person who may be indicted for and convicted of such offence in the criminal court of the District of Columbia shall be subject to such fine as the court may think proper to impose, not exceeding five hundred dollars, or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding one year. And it is hereby made the special duty of the commissioner of public buildings to bring to the notice of the attorney of the United States for the District of Columbia, or to the grand jury, any infraction of tins law.


*Approved, July 25, 1866.*

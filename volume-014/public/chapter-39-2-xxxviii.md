# Chapter XXXVIII

> An Act to fix the Pay of the Quartermaster-Sergeant of the Battalion of Engineers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the passage of this act, the pay and allowances of the quartermaster-sergeant of the battalion of engineers of the army of the United States shall be the same as those allowed by law to the sergeant-major of that battalion.


*Approved, February 12, 1867.*

# Chapter CLXXX

> An Act to provide for making the Town of Whitehall, New York, a Port of Delivery.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the town of Whitehall, in the State of New York, which by existing law is a port through which imported merchandise may be exported in bond and for drawback to the adjacent British North American provinces, be, and the same is hereby, constituted a port of delivery within the collection district of Champlain, and that a deputy collector, as now authorized by law, shall there reside, who shall receive the same compensation as is now paid to the deputy collector now stationed at that port.


*Approved, July 13, 1866.*

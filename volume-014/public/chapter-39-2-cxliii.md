# Chapter CXLIII

> An Act to quiet Title to Land in the Towns of Santa Clara and Petaluma, in the State of California.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all the right and title of the United States to the land situated within the corporate limits of the towns of Santa Clara and Petaluma, in the State of California, as defined in the acts of the legislature of that State incorporating said towns, be, and the same are hereby, relinquished and granted to the corporate authorities of said towns and their successors, in trust, for and with authority to convey so much of said land as is in the bona fide occupancy of parties upon the passage of this act, by themselves or tenants, to such parties: Provided, That this grant shall not extend to any reservation of the United States, nor prejudice any valid adverse right or claim, if such exist, to said land or any part thereof, nor preclude a judicial examination and adjustment thereof.


*Approved, March 1, 1867.*

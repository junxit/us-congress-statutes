# Chapter LXXXIV

> An Act to amend “An Act to establish the Grade of Vice-Admiral in the United States Navy.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second section of an act to establish the grade of vice-admiral in the United States navy, approved December twenty-first, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, be, and the same is hereby, amended by adding thereto the following: “And he shall be allowed a Secretary, with the rank and sea pay and allowances of a lieutenant in the navy.”


*Approved, May 16, 1866.*

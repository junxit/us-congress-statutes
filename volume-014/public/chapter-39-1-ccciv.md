# Chapter CCCIV

> An Act directing a District Court to be held at the City of Erie, in the State of Pennsylvania.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, besides the terms of the district court of the United States, directed by law to be held at Pittsburgh, in the county of Alleghany, and at Williamsport, in the county of Lycoming, for the western district of the State of Pennsylvania, the judge of said western district shall hold two terms in every year, at the city of Erie, in the county of Erie, which shall commence the first Monday of July and January in each and every year, beginning in the July or January which shall first immediately follow the passage of this act, and be continued and adjourned from time to time, as the court may deem expedient, for the despatch of the business thereof.


*Approved, July 28, 1866.*

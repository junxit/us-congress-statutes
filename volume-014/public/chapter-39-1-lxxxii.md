# Chapter LXXXII

> An Act imposing a Duty on live Animals.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That on and after the passage of this act, there shall be levied, collected, and paid, on all horses, mules, cattle, sheep, hogs, and other live animals imported from foreign countries, a duty of twenty per centum ad valorem: Provided, That any such animals now bona fide owned by resident citizens of the United States, and now in any of the provinces of British America, may be imported into the United States free of duty until the expiration of ten days next after the passage of this act.


*Approved, May 16, 1866.*

# Chapter LXXI

> An Act to remit and refund certain Duties.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby authorized and empowered to remit, or if paid to refund, any duties levied on produce shipped from a port of the United States to a port of the United States, via Canada, if the said produce was actually in transit and detained by ice when the recent reciprocity treaty with Canada expired.


*Approved, May 2, 1866.*

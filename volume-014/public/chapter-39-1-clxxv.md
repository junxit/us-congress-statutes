# Chapter CLXXV

> An Act to provide for the Payment of the sixth, eighth, and eleventh Regiments of Ohio Volunteer Militia of Cincinnati, Bard’s Company of Cavalry, and Paulsen’s Battery, during the Time they were in the Service of the United States, in 1862.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the officers and men of the sixth, eighth, and eleventh regiments of Ohio volunteer militia, and of Captain S. W. Bard’s company of cavalry, and of Captain August Paulsen’s battery, of Cincinnati, ordered into the service of the United States, at Cincinnati, Ohio, on the second day of September, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, notwithstanding any irregularity in their muster into the service of the United States, be paid for the time the officers and men were actually in the service, respectively, not however to exceed the period of thirty-one days.


*Approved, July 5, 1866.*

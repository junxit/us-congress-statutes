# Chapter LXVII

> An Act fixing the Compensation for the Bailiffs and Criers of the Courts of the District of Columbia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the bailiffs and criers, who are required by the marshal or courts of the District of Columbia to attend upon the district, circuit, or criminal court of said District, shall be paid by said marshal three dollars and fifty cents per day for each day’s attendance, (instead of two dollars, as now provided by law,) commencing with the first of January, eighteen hundred and sixty-six.


*Approved, February 22, 1867.*

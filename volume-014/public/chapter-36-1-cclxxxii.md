# Chapter CCLXXXII

> An Act to authorize, the Refunding of certain Taxes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That where the license tax imposed upon any wholesale dealer has been calculated upon the amount of such dealer’s sales for the previous year, in accordance with the terms of the seventy-ninth section of an act approved June thirtieth, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, and it shall be proved to the satisfaction of the commissioner of internal revenue that the sales made under such license did not equal in amount the sales of such previous year, it shall be lawful for said commissioner to refund to such wholesale dealer so much of the amount paid for such licence as may be in excess of the proper tax chargeable upon the amount of sales actually made under such license during the year for which the same was issued.


*Approved, July 27, 1866.*

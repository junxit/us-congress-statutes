# Chapter XI

> An Act in Regard to the Compensation of Route Agents in the Post-Office Department.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster-General be authorized to pay route agents in the service of the Post-Office Department any sum not less than nine hundred dollars nor more than twelve hundred dollars per annum.


*Approved, January 22, 1867.*

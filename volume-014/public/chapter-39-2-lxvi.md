# Chapter LXVI

> An Act to restore Lieutenant Joseph P. Fyffe to his Grade in Active Service of the Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be authorized to nominate, and with the advice and consent of the Senate to appoint, Lieutenant Joseph P. Fyffe to the active list of the navy, and to restore him to the rank to which he may be entitled thereon.


*Approved, February 22, 1867.*

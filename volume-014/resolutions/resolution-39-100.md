# Resolution 100

> Joint Resolution authorizing the Transmission through the Mails, free of Postage, of certain Certificates, by the Adjutant-General of New Jersey.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the adjutant-general of New Jersey be authorized to transmit through the mails, free of postage, certain certificates of thanks awarded by the legislature to the soldiers of that State, under such regulations as the postmaster-general may direct.


*Approved, July 28, 1866.*

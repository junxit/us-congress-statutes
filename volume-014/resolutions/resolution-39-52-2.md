# Resolution 52

> A Resolution thanking the Chambers of Senators and Deputies of Brazil for their Resolutions of Sorrow and Sympathy on the Death of President Lincoln.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Congress of the United States has received with respect and gratitude the resolutions of sorrow and sympathy which the Chambers of Senators and Deputies of Brazil have adopted on the death of President Lincoln, and hereby tenders to those Chambers the thanks of the people of the United States.

## § 2.

And be it further resolved, That it shall be the duty of the President of the United States to forward a copy of this resolution to the Emperor of Brazil, with a request that it be communicated to the two chambers.


*Approved, March 2, 1867.*

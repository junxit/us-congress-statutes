# Resolution 33

> Joint Resolution authorizing the Secretary of the Navy to grant the Use of Guns for Trial of Ridgway's Battery.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he hereby is, authorized to grant the use of two eleven-inch Dahlgren guns to John Ridgway of Boston, for use in experimental trials of Ridgway's patent revolving battery.


*Approved, February 28, 1867.*

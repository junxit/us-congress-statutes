# Resolution 22

> Joint Resolution for the Restoration of Lieutenant-Commander S. L. Breese, U. S. Navy, to the Active List from the Retired List.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be authorized to nominate, and by and with the advice and consent of the Senate to appoint, Lieutenant-Commander S. L. Breese to the active list of the navy, with the rank to which he may be entitled thereon.


*Approved, February 22, 1867.*

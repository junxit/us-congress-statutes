# Resolution 4

> Joint Resolution granting certain public Property to the Soldiers’ Orphans’ Home of Iowa.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the buildings, sheds, furniture, and other property, now at camp Kinsman, near Davenport, Scott county, Iowa, be, and the same are hereby, donated to the Soldiers’ Orphans’ Home of Iowa.


*Approved, January 22, 1866.*

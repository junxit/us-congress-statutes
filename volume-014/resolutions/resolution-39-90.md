# Resolution 90

> A Resolution to change the Place of holding the Terms of the Circuit Court for the District of West Virginia.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the terms of the circuit court for the district of West Virginia, heretofore held at Lewisburg, in the county of Greenbrier, shall be hereafter held at the city of Parkersburg, at the time now fixed by law.


*Approved, July 27, 1866.*

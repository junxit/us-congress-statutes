# Resolution 20

> Joint Resolution giving Construction to the Law in Relation to Bounties payable to Soldiers discharged for Wounds.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the true intent and meaning of the words “or in the line of duty,” used in the fourth section of the act approved March third, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, entitled “An act to amend the several acts heretofore passed to provide for the enrolling and calling out the national forces, and for other purposes,” requires that the benefit of the provision of said section shall be extended to any enlisted man or other person entitled by law to bounty who has been or may be discharged by reason of a wound received while actually in service under military orders, not at the time on furlough or leave of absence, nor engaged in any unlawful or unauthorized act or pursuit.


*Approved, April 12, 1866.*

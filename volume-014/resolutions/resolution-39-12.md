# Resolution 12

> Joint Resolution giving the Consent of Congress to the Transfer of the Counties of Berkeley and Jefferson to the State of West Virginia.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Congress hereby recognizes the transfer of the counties of Berkeley and Jefferson from the State of Virginia to West Virginia; and consents thereto.


*Approved, March 10, 1866.*

# Resolution 49

> A Resolution in Relation to National Banking Associations.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That in all cases where a national bank has paid or may pay in excess of what may be, or has been found due from said bank, on account of the duty required to be paid to the treasurer of the United States, the bank so having paid or paying such excess of duty may state an account therefor, which, on being certified by the treasurer of the United States, and found correct by the first comptroller of the treasury, shall be refunded in the ordinary manner by warrant on the treasury.


*Approved, March 2, 1867.*

# Resolution 44

> A Resolution for printing additional Copies of the Appendix to the Diplomatic Correspondence of eighteen hundred and sixty-five.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That in addition to the number of copies of papers relating to Foreign Affairs now authorized by law, there shall be printed for distribution by the Department of State, on fine paper, with wide margin, a sufficient number of copies of the Appendix to the Diplomatic Correspondence of eighteen hundred and sixty-five, to supply one copy to each Senator and each Representative of the Thirty-Ninth Congress, and to each Foreign Government, and one copy to each corporation, association, or public body whose expressions of condolence or sympathy are published in said volume; one hundred of these copies to be bound in full Turkey morocco, full gilt, and the remaining copies to be bound in half Turkey morocco, marble-edged.


*Approved, March 2, 1867.*

# Resolution 2

> A Resolution for increasing the Bond of the Superintendent of public Printing.

Whereas the amount of money which can be advanced to the superintendent of public printing under existing law is not sufficient to enable him to meet the current expenditures of his office: Therefore, Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

That the said superintendent be requested to furnish a new bond, in the penal sum of eighty thousand dollars.


*Approved, January 12, 1866.*

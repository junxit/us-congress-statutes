# Resolution 18

> Joint Resolution in Relation to the Public Lands appertaining to the Armory at Springfield.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first section of a joint resolution approved June seventeenth, eighteen hundred and forty-four, entitled “A resolution relating to the public lands appertaining to the armories at Springfield and Harper’s Ferry,” is hereby revived, re-enacted, and continued in force.


*Approved, April 4, 1866.*

# Resolution 59

> Joint Resolution authorizing the Secretary of State to present to Captain James G. Smith, of the British Brig “Victoria,” a Grid Chronometer, in Token of Appreciation of his Services in rescuing from Death the Master, Officers, and Crew, and Passengers on Board of the American Brig “E. H. Fitler.”

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of State be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to cause to be procured and presented to Captain James G. Smith, master of the brig “Victoria,” of Yarmouth, Nova Scotia, a gold chronometer, in token of appreciation by Hie government of the United States of his humane and successful efforts in rescuing from death the master, officers, crew, and passengers on board the brig E. H. Filler, of Philadelphia, wrecked at sea on the morning of January twenty-second, eighteen hundred and sixty-seven.


*Approved, March 2, 1867.*

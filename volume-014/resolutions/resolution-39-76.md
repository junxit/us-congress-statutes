# Resolution 76

> Joint Resolution authorizing the Commissioner of Public Buildings to employ three additional Watchmen in the Smithsonian Grounds.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the commissioner of public buildings and grounds be, and the same is hereby, authorized to employ three additional watchmen for the Smithsonian grounds.


*Approved, July 25, 1866.*

# Resolution 48

> A Resolution to facilitate the Settlement of Accounts of disbursing Officers.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That so much of the act entitled “An act to provide for the more prompt settlement of the accounts of disbursing officers,” approved July seventeen, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, as provides that “such accounts with the vouchers necessary to the correct and prompt settlement thereof, shall be rendered direct to the proper accounting officers of the treasury,” be, and the same is hereby, repealed; and all such accounts and vouchers shall hereafter be sent to the bureau to which they pertain, and, after examination there, shall be passed to the proper accounting officer of the treasury for settlement.


*Approved, March 2, 1867.*

# Resolution 25

> A Resolution for the temporary Relief of destitute People in the District of Columbia.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of twenty-five thousand dollars be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the temporary relief of the destitute population in the District of Columbia, to be expended under the direction of the Commissioner of Freedmen’s Bureau.


*Approved, April 17, 1866.*

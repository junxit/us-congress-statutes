# Resolution 45

> Joint Resolution authorizing [the] Postmaster-General to pay additional Salary to Letter-Carriers in San Francisco.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster-General be authorized to pay such additional salary to letter-carriers in San Francisco, above that provided by law, as may be necessary to secure competent persons for such service.


*Approved, June 6, 1866.*

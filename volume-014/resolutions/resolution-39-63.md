# Resolution 63

> A Resolution to authorize the Hiring of a Building or Buildings for the temporary Accommodation of the Department of State.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of State be authorized to hire a suitable building or buildings for the temporary accommodation of the Department of State, and that such sum of money, not exceeding twenty-five thousand dollars, as may be necessary towards defraying the expense of such hiring, the transfer of the public archives, and the fitting up of the building or buildings, be, and the same is hereby, appropriated out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, July 3, 1866.*

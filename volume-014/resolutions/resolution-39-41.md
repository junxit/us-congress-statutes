# Resolution 41

> A Resolution to authorize certain Medals to be distributed to Veteran Soldiers free of Postage.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the adjutant-general of the State of Ohio is hereby authorized to distribute through the mails free of postage, to veteran soldiers re-enlisted in Ohio, certain medals furnished by the general assembly of that State, and in such case the envelope enclosing the same shall be franked by such adjutant-general in the mode prescribed by the Postmaster-General.


*Approved, May 26, 1866.*

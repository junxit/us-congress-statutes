# Resolution 6

> A Resolution appropriating Money to defray the Expenses of the Joint Select Committee on Retrenchment.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of fifteen thousand dollars, or as much thereof as may be necessary, be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to defray the expenses of the “Joint Committee on Retrenchment,” and that sum shall be drawn from the treasury, upon the order of the secretary of the Senate, as the same shall be required; and any portion of the amount hereby appropriated, that shall be allowed by the said joint committee to witnesses attending before it, or persons employed in its service, for per diem, travelling, or other necessary expenses, and paid by the secretary of the Senate, in pursuance of the orders of said joint committee, shall be accordingly credited and allowed by the accounting officers of the Treasury Department.


*Approved, January 22, 1867.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 91

> HEARINGS ON AMENDING THE ATOMIC ENERGY ACT OF 1954

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be reprinted for the use of the Joint Committee on Atomic Energy two thousand additional copies of the hearings before the Subcommittee on Agreements for Cooperation of the Joint Committee on Atomic Energy on Amending the Atomic Energy Act of 1954 with respect to exchange of military information and material with allies during the second session of the Eighty-fifth Congress.


*Agreed to August 31, 1960.*

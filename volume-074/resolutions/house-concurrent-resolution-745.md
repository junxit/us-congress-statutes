# House Concurrent Resolution 745

> ADJOURNMENT SINE DIE

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Thursday, September 1, 1960, and that when they adjourn on said day, they stand adjourned sine die.


*Passed September 1, 1960.*

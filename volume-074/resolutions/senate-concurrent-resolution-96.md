# Senate Concurrent Resolution 96

> PRINTING OF INTERNAL SECURITY MANUAL

*Resolved, by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed as a Senate document a revised edition of the Internal Security Manual; and that five thousand additional copies he printed for the use of the Committee on the Judiciary.


*Agreed to August 31, 1960.*

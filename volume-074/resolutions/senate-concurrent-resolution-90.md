# Senate Concurrent Resolution 90

> IMPROPER ACTIVITIES IN THE LABOR OR MANAGEMENT FIELD

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Government Operations not to exceed three thousand additional copies each of parts 2 and 3 of the final report of the Select Committee on Improper Activities in the Labor or Management Field (S. Rept. 1139, Eighty-sixth Congress, second session), pursuant to S. Res. 44 and S. Res. 249, Eighty-sixth Congress.


*Agreed to August 31, 1960.*

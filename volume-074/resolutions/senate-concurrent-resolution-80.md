# Senate Concurrent Resolution 80

> PRINTING OF HEARINGS ON SATELLITE AND MISSILE PROGRAMS

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Armed Services one thousand additional copies of part 1 of the hearings held by that committee on an inquiry into the satellite and missile programs.


*Agreed to March 30, 1960.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 101

> TRANSPORTATION SYSTEM FOR DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Joint Committee on Washington Metropolitan Problems, created by House Concurrent Resolution 172, agreed to August 29, 1957, is hereby authorized to hold public hearings on the bills S. 3193 and H.R. 11135, and to furnish transcripts of such hearings, and make such recommendations as it sees fit, to the Committees on the District of Columbia of the Senate and House of Representatives, respectively.


*Agreed to April 21, 1960.*

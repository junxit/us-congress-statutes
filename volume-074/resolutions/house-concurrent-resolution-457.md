# House Concurrent Resolution 457

> “NOMINATION AND ELECTION OF THE PRESIDENT AND VICE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES, ETC.”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed as a House document the publication entitled “Nomination and Election of the President and Vice President of the United States, Including the Manner of Selecting Delegates to Political National Conventions”; and that there be printed an additional fifty thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives, said comes to be prorated to the Members and Resident Commissioner.


*Passed February 9, 1960.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 660

> GRANTING OF PERMANENT RESIDENCE TO CERTAIN ALIENS

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Congress favors the granting of the status of permanent residence in the case of each alien hereinafter named, in which case the Attorney General has determined that such alien is qualified under the provisions of section 4 of the Displaced Persons Act of 1948, as amended (62 Stat. 101; 64 Stat. 219):

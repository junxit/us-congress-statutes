# Private Law 86–448

> AN ACT For the relief of Ida Exle (nee Ida Sterio).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203(a)(2) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Ida Exle (nee Ida Sterio) shall be held and considered to be the natural mother of Charles Klippel, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved July 14, 1960.*

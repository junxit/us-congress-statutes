# Private Law 86–326

> AN ACT For the relief of Margherita Pino Zordan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a)(27)(A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Margherita Pino Zordan, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Vittorio Attilio Zordan, citizens of the United States: Provided, That no natural parent of Margherita Pino Zordan, by virtue of such parentage, shall be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved June 11, 1960.*

# Private Law 86–386

> AN ACT For the relief of Preciolita V. Corliss (nee Preciolita Valera).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Mrs. Preciolita V. Corliss (nee Preciolita Valera), the widow of a United States citizen, shall be deemed to be within the purview of section 101 (a)(27)(A) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, and the provisions of section 205 of that Act shall not be applicable in this case.


*Approved July 5, 1960.*

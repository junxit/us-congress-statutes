# Private Law 86–366

> AN ACT For the relief of Low Wing Quey (Kwai).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a)(27)(A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Low Wing Quey (Kwai) shall be held and considered to be the natural-born minor alien child of Low Shiu Hong, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved June 30, 1960.*

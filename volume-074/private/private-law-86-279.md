# Private Law 86–279

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. E. Christine Williams.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. E. Christine Williams shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of March 5, 1953, upon payment of the required visa fee: Provided, That unless she is entitled to care under the Dependents’ Medical Care Act (70 Stat. 250), a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved May 13, 1960.*

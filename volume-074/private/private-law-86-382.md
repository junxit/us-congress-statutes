# Private Law 86–382

> AN ACT For the relief of Stamatina Kalpaka.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, the Attorney General is authorized and directed to cancel any outstanding orders and warrants of deportation, warrants of arrest, and bond, which may have issued in the case of Stamatina Kalpaka. From and after the date of the enactment of this Act, the said Stamatina Kalpaka shall not again be subject to deportation by reason of the same facts upon which such deportation proceedings were commenced or any such warrants and orders have issued.


*Approved July 5, 1960.*

# Private Law 86–349

> AN ACT For the relief of Jesus Cruz-Figueroa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, the Attorney General is authorized and directed to cancel any outstanding orders and warrants of deportation, warrant of arrest, and bonds, which may have issued in the case of Jesus Cruz-Figueroa.


*Approved June 12, 1960.*

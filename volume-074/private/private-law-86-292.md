# Private Law 86–292

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Alice Anderson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** notwithstanding the expiration of the period prescribed by Public Law 615, Eighty-third Congress (68 Stat. 759), for the settlement of claims authorized under subsection (e) of section 6 of the War Claims Act of 1948, as amended, the Foreign Claims Settlement Commission of the United States is authorized and directed to determine the validity and amount of the claim (numbered XXXXXX of Mrs. Alice Anderson as surviving mother of Orvill F. Linebaugh, deceased.
- **(b)** Any award made by the Foreign Claims Settlement Commission of the United States with respect to such claims shall be certified to the Secretary of the Treasury for payment, in accordance with applicable provisions of the War Claims Act of 1948, as amended, out of any money in the Treasury of the United States not otherwise appropriated.

## § 2.

There is hereby authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary for the payment of any award or awards that may be certified by the Foreign Claims Settlement Commission of the United States pursuant to this Act, including amounts required by such Commission and the Secretary of the Treasury for the payment of their respective administrative expenses in connection therewith.


*Approved May 13, 1960.*

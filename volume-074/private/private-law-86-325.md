# Private Law 86–325

> AN ACT For the relief of Nobuko Stickels.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Nobuko Stickels, the widow of Charles F. Stickels, a deceased United States citizen who served honorably in the Armed Forces of the United States, shall be deemed to be a non-quota immigrant.


*Approved June 11, 1960.*

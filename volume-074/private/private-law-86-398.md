# Private Law 86–398

> AN ACT For the relief of Fumie Yoshioka.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 101(a)(27)(B) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Fumie Yoshioka shall be deemed to be a returning resident alien.


*Approved July 7, 1960.*

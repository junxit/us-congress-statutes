# Private Law 86–368

> AN ACT For the relief of Boris Priestley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Boris Priestley shall be deemed to have been born in Great Britain.


*Approved June 30, 1960.*

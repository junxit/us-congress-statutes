# Private Law 86–352

> AN ACT For the relief of Anne-Marie Stehlin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Anne-Marie Stehlin, a naturalized citizen of the United States, shall be permitted to reside in France until May 31, 1964, without losing her United States citizenship under section 352(a) of such Act.


*Approved June 29, 1960.*

# Private Law 86–384

> AN ACT For the relief of Francis M. Haischer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the account of Francis M. Haischer, of Albany, New York, of the sum of $2,037.72. Such sum represents an overpayment of retired pay which was paid by the Department of the Navy which he received in good faith.


*Approved July 5, 1960.*

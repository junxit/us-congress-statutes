# Private Law 86–443

> AN ACT For the relief of Gloria Anne Loveday.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Gloria Anne Loveday shall be deemed to have been born in Great Britain.


*Approved July 14, 1960.*

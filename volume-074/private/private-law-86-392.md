# Private Law 86–392

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Ethel B. Morgan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 202 of the Social Security Act, Mrs. Ethel B. Morgan of 253 East Market Street, Bethlehem, Pennsylvania, shall be held and considered to have been the wife and subsequently the widow (as those terms are defined in section 216 of that Act) of Elias Robons Morgan (social security account numbered XXXXXXXXXXX). Social security benefits to commence as of June 1952.


*Approved July 5, 1960.*

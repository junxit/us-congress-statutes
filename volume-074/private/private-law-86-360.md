# Private Law 86–360

> AN ACT For the relief of Emiko Nagamine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Emiko Nagamine shall be deemed to be within the purview of section 101(a)(27)(B) of that Act.


*Approved June 30, 1960.*

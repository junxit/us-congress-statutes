# Private Law 86–328

> AN ACT For the relief of Leila Finlay Bohin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 301(a)(7) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Leila Finlay Bohin shall be held and considered to have been physically present in the United States for a period of 5 years after attaining the age of 14 years.


*Approved June 11, 1960.*

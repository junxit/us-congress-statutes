# Private Law 86–454

> AN ACT For the relief of Michiko (Hirai) Christopher.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a)(27)(A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Michiko (Hirai) Christopher shall lie held and considered to be the natural born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Adolphus Christopher, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents of Michiko Christopher shall not by virtue of such parentage be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved September 2, 1960.*

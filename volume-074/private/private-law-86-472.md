# Private Law 86–472

> AN ACT For the relief of Robert J. Reeves.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Robert J. Reeves is hereby relieved of all liability to the United States to refund the amount of $935.20, but not in excess thereof, resulting from an overpayment of compensation he received, through administrative error, as an employee of Wright Air Development Division, Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, Ohio, in the period between August 24, 1958, and January 23, 1960.


*Approved September 6, 1960.*

# Private Law 86–421

> AN ACT For the relief of Joseph Lue Fan and Aura Joan Lue Fan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a)(27)(A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Joseph Lue Fan and Aura Joan Lue Fan, shall be held and considered to be the minor alien children of King Lue Fan, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved July 14, 1960.*

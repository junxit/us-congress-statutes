# Private Law 86–358

> AN ACT For the relief of Manuel Alves de Carvalho.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Manuel Alves de Carvalho shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of March 13, 1957.


*Approved June 30, 1960.*

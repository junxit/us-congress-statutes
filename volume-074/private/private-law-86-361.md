# Private Law 86–361

> AN ACT For the relief of John Lipset.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, John Lipset shall be deemed to have retained the status conferred upon him under Private Law 844 of the Eighty-fourth Congress, and the provisions of section 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act shall not be applicable in this case.


*Approved June 30, 1960.*

# Private Law 86–251

> AN ACT For the relief of Oscar A. LaBranche.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Oscar A. LaBranche of San Diego, California, is hereby relieved of all liability to pay to the United States the sum of $3,503.68. Amounts aggregating such sum were paid as compensation to the said Oscar A. LaBranche during the period from August 12, 1940, to December 19, 1942, as an employee of the Post Office Department in contravention of prohibitions which were included in annual Appropriation Acts for such period against the payment by the United States of compensation to certain persons not citizens of the United States. In the audit and settlement of the accounts of any certifying or disbursing officer of the United States, full credit shall be given for all amounts for which liability is relieved by this Act.


*Approved April 8, 1960.*

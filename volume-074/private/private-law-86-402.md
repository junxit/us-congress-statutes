# Private Law 86–402

> AN ACT For the relief of Pierre R. DeBroux.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of section 316(a) of the Immigration and Nationality Act as they relate to residence and physical presence requirements for naturalization, shall be inapplicable in the case of Pierre R. DeBroux: Provided, That nothing in this Act shall be held to waive the six-month residence requirement within the State in which the petition for naturalization is filed.


*Approved July 7, 1960.*

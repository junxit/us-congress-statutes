# Private Law 86–307

> AN ACT For the relief of Josette A. M. Stanton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of section 301(b) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, and section 16 of the Act of September 11, 1957, Josette A. M. Stanton shall be held and considered to have complied with the provisions thereof if she establishes residence in the United States prior to attaining the age of twenty-five years.


*Approved June 8, 1960.*

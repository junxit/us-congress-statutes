# Private Law 86–265

> AN ACT For the relief of Frank Podany.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Frank Podany shall be deemed to have been born in Czechoslovakia.


*Approved April 29, 1960.*

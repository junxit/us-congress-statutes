# Private Law 86–461

> AN ACT For the relief of Chauncey A. Ahalt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Chauncey A. Ahalt, Dayton, Ohio, is hereby relieved of all liability to repay to the United States a sum of $1,097.11, which was erroneously paid to him by the Department of the Air Force as salary between September 26, 1954, and January 23, 1960. In the audit and settlement of the accounts of any certifying or disbursing officer of the United States, full credit shall be given for the amount for which liability is relieved by this Act.


*Approved September 2, 1960.*

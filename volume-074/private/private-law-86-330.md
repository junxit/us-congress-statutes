# Private Law 86–330

> AN ACT For the relief of John George Sarkis Lindell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a)(27)(A) and 205, of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, John George Sarkis Lindell shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Albert J. Lindell, both citizens of the United States.


*Approved June 11, 1960.*

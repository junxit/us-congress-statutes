# Private Law 86–394

> AN ACT For the relief of Peter Leo Bahr, John Trevor Jefferies, Charmian Candy Jefferies, and Stephen Reid Jefferies.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the provisions of sections 201(a), 202(a)(5), and 202(b) of that Act shall not be applicable in the case of Peter Leo Bahr.

## § 2.

For the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, John Trevor Jefferies, Charmian Candy Jefferies, and Stephen Reid Jefferies shall be held to have been born in Great Britain.


*Approved July 7, 1960.*

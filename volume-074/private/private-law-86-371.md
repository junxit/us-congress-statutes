# Private Law 86–371

> AN ACT For the relief of Ah See Lee Chin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Ah See Lee Chin shall be held and considered to be within the purview of the first proviso to section 312(1) of that Act and may be naturalized upon compliance with all of the other requirements of title III of that Act.


*Approved June 30, 1960.*

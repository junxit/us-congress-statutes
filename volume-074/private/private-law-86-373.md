# Private Law 86–373

> AN ACT For the relief of Jung Hi Pak.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a)(27)(A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Jung Hi Pak, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Captain and Mrs. William S. Herrington, citizens of the United States: Provided, That no natural parent of Jung Hi Pak, by virtue of such parentage, shall be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved June 30, 1960.*

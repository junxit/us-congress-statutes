# Private Law 86–395

> AN ACT For the relief of Ming-Chen Hsu.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Ming-Chen Hsu shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of September 12, 1948.


*Approved July 7, 1960.*

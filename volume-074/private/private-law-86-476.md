# Private Law 86–476

> AN ACT For the relief of Major Edmund T. Coppinger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of the Act entitled “An Act providing for the barring of claims against the United States”, approved October 9, 1940 (31 U.S.C. 71a), are hereby waived in favor of Major Edmund T. Coppinger, of Harrisburg, Pennsylvania, with respect to his claim for lump sum payment under section 2 of the Act of June 16, 1936, as amended (10 U.S.C., 1952 edition, sec. 300a), if such claim is filed with the General Accounting Office within the one-year period which begins on the date of enactment of this Act.


*Approved September 8, 1960.*

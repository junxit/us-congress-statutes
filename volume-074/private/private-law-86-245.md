# Private Law 86–245

> AN ACT For the relief of Carl J. Fisher, captain, United States Army, retired.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Carl J. Fisher, captain, United States Army, retired, is hereby relieved of all liability to refund to the United States the sum of $2,384.86, representing overpayments of retired pay received by him for the period July 1, 1952, to September 30, 1958, inclusive.

## § 2.

The Secretary of the Treasury is hereby authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Carl J. Fisher, the sum of any amounts withheld from him on account of the overpayments referred to in the first section of this Act.


*Approved April 4, 1960.*

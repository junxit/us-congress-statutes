# Private Law 86–441

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Humiko Ross.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Mrs. Humiko Ross, the widow of a United States citizen, shall be deemed to be within the purview of section 101(a)(27)(A) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, and the provisions of section 205 of that Act shall not be applicable in this case.


*Approved July 14, 1960.*

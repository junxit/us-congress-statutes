# Private Law 86–362

> AN ACT For the relief of Mo Tong Lui.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 101 (a)(27)(A) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mo Tong Lui shall be held and considered to be under 21 years of age: Provided, That a petition is filed in his behalf under section 205 of the said Act within one year from the date of enactment of this Act.


*Approved June 30, 1960.*

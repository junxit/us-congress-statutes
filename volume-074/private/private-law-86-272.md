# Private Law 86–272

> AN ACT For the relief of Gim Bong Wong.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a)(27)(A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Gim Bong Wong, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Chin Get Wong and Yee Yoke Ying Wong, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents of the said Gim Bong Wong shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved May 13, 1960.*

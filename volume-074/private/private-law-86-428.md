# Private Law 86–428

> AN ACT For the relief of Ireneo D. Brodit and Antonio D. Brodit.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 101(a)(27)(A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Ireneo D. Brodit and Antonio D. Brodit shall be held and considered to be the natural-born minor alien children of Vicente Brodit, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved July 14, 1960.*

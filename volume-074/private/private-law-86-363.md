# Private Law 86–363

> AN ACT For the relief of Lloyd C. Kimm.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Lloyd C. Kimm shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of July 31, 1925.


*Approved June 30, 1960.*

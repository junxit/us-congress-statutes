# Private Law 86–350

> AN ACT For the relief of Angela Maria.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a)(27)(A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Angela Maria shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien minor child of Larry Keighley, a citizen of the United States, and the provisions of section 212(a)(7) and (15) of the said Act shall be held to be inapplicable in this case: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the said Act: And provided further, That the exemptions granted herein shall apply only to grounds for exclusion of which the Department of State or the Department of Justice have knowledge prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved June 12, 1960.*

# Private Law 86–281

> AN ACT For the relief of Jean K. Simmons.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 15 to 20, inclusive, of the Federal Employees’ Compensation Act are hereby waived in favor of Jean K. Simmons, of Levittown, Pennsylvania, and her claim for compensation for disease alleged to have been contracted in the performance of duty as an employee of the United States Veterans’ Administration Hospital, Castle Point, New York, shall be acted upon under the remaining provisions of such Act if she files such claim with the Bureau of Employees’ Compensation, Department of Labor, within six months after the date of enactment of this Act: Provided, That no benefits shall accrue by reason of the enactment of this Act for any period prior to the date of its enactment except reasonable medical and hospital expenditures.


*Approved May 13, 1960.*

# Private Law 86–437

> AN ACT For the relief of Wong Gee Sing.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a)(27)(A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Wong Gee Sing shall be held and considered to be the minor alien child of Wong Len Chong, citizen of the United States.


*Approved July 14, 1960.*

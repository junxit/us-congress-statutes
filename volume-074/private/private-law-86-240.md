# Private Law 86–240

> AN ACT For the relief of Eleanor Constan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of the War Claims Act of 1948. as amended, limiting the period of time within which claims may be filed thereunder, the Foreign Claims Settlement Commission of the United States shall have jurisdiction to receive and to determine the validity and amount of the claim of Eleanor Constan of West Newton, Massachusetts, for civilian detention benefits under subsections (a) through (e) of section 5 of that Act of 1948, as amended, and shall certify to the Secretary of the Treasury for payment out of the War Claims Fund any award made thereunder. The Secretary of the Treasury shall pay to the said Eleanor Constan the amount of any such award so certified by the Commission.


*Approved February 25, 1960.*

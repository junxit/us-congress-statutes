# Private Law 86–242

> AN ACT For the relief of Candace Elizabeth Lee Johnson (Kyung Hee Lee).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a)(27)(A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Candace Elizabeth Lee Johnson (Kyung Hee Lee), shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Jack H. Johnson, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents of the said Candace Elizabeth Lee Johnson (Kyung Hee Lee) shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved February 25, 1960.*

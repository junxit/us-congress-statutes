# Public Law 86–471

> AN ACT To authorize the construction of modern naval vessels.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is authorized to undertake the construction of not to exceed eight thousand tons of amphibious warfare vessels and landing craft, not to exceed four thousand tons of patrol vessels, and not to exceed twenty thousand tons of auxiliary vessels.

## § 2.

There is hereby authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary for the construction of the foregoing vessels.


*Approved May 14, 1960.*

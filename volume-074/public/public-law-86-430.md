# Public Law 86–430

> AN ACT To amend the Act of July 19, 1954, to exempt from taxation certain additional property of the Veterans of Foreign Wars of the United States in the District of Columbia, and to provide that the tax exemption granted the property of the Veterans of Foreign Wars of the United States in the District of Columbia shall be effective with respect to taxable years beginning on and after July 1, 1959.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** the Act entitled “An Act to exempt from taxation certain property of the Veterans of Foreign Wars of the United States in the District of Columbia”, approved July 19, 1954 (Public Law 510, Eighty-third Congress), as amended, is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking out “lots 38, 20, and 19” and inserting in lieu thereof “lots 38, 20, 19, and 841”; and
  - **(2)** by inserting “with respect to taxable years beginning on and after July 1, 1959,” immediately after “exempt”.


*Approved April 22, 1960.*

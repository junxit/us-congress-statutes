# Public Law 86–647

> JOINT RESOLUTION Authorizing and requesting the President to issue a proclamation with respect to the 1960 Pacific Festival, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is authorized and requested to issue a proclamation inviting foreign nations to participate in the 1960 Pacific Festival which is being held at San Francisco, California, from September 9, 1960, to September 18, 1960, inclusive.


*Approved July 14, 1960.*

# Public Law 86–597

> AN ACT To authorize a continuation of flight instruction for members of the Reserve Officers’ Training Corps until August 1, 1964.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 32 of the Act of September 2, 1958, Public Law 85–861 (72 Stat. 1564), is amended by striking out the words “four years” and inserting the words “eight years” in place thereof.


*Approved July 7, 1960.*

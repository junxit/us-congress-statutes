# Public Law 86–483

> JOINT RESOLUTION To authorize and request the President to issue a proclamation in connection with the centennial of the birth of General of the Armies John J. Pershing.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States is authorized and requested to proclaim September 13, 1960, as a day upon which all Americans should pay honor and respect to General of the Armies John J. Pershing and the men who served under him.

## § 2.

All departments and agencies of the Government are hereby authorized to cooperate with any civic and patriotic organizations which may be conducting ceremonies in commemoration of the birth of General Pershing. The Secretary of Defense will be responsible for coordination between such civic and patriotic organizations and the departments and agencies of the Government.


*Approved June 1, 1960.*

# Public Law 86–635

> AN ACT To amend the Career Compensation Act of 1949 with respect to Incentive pay for certain submarine service.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection 204(a) (2) of the Career Compensation Act of 1949, as amended (37 U.S.C. 235(a) (2)), be amended to read, “(2) duty on board a submarine, including, in the case of nuclear-powered submarines, periods of training and rehabilitation after assignment thereto as determined by the Secretary concerned, and including submarines under construction from the time builders’ trials commence, and duty as an operator or crew member of an operational, self-propelled submersible, including undersea exploration and research vehicles;”.


*Approved July 12, 1960.*

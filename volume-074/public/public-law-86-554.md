# Public Law 86–554

> AN ACT To change the title of the Assistant Director of the Cast and Geodetic Survey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 8 of the Act of January 19, 1942 (56 Stat. 8), as amended, is further amended by striking the words “Assistant Director” wherever they appear and substituting in lieu thereof the words “Deputy Director”.

## § 2.

All laws and orders relating or referring to the Assistant Director of the Coast and Geodetic Survey shall be deemed to relate or refer to the Deputy Director of the Coast and Geodetic Survey.


*Approved June 30, 1960.*

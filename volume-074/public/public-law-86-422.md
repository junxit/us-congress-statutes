# Public Law 86–422

> AN ACT To reduce the cabaret tax from 20 percent to 10 percent.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph (6) of section 4231 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1954 (relating to imposition of tax with respect to roof gardens, cabarets, and other similar places) is amended by striking out “20 percent” and inserting in lieu thereof “10 percent”.

## § 2.

The amendment made by the first section of this Act shall apply only with respect to periods after 10 antemeridian on the first day of the first month which begins more than 10 days after the date of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved April 9, 1960.*

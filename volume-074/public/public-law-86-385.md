# Public Law 86–385

> AN ACT To provide a two-year extension of the existing provision for a minimum wheat acreage allotment in the Tulelake area of California.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first sentence of section 334(i) of the Agricultural Adjustment Act of 1938, as amended (7 U.S.C. 1334 (i)), is amended by striking out “1958 and 1959” and inserting “1958 through 1961”.


*Approved February 20, 1960.*

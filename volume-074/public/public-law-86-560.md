# Public Law 86–560

> AN ACT To extend the Defense Production Act of 1950, as amended, for an additional two years.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first sentence of subsection (a) of section 717 of the Defense Production Act of 1950, as amended (50 U.S.C. app. 2166), is amended by striking out “June 30, 1960” and inserting in lieu thereof “June 30, 1962”.

## § 2.

The second proviso to the first sentence of subsection (b) of section 304 is amended by striking out the word “quarter” and inserting in lieu thereof the words “six months”.


*Approved June 30, 1960.*

# Public Law 86–551

> AN ACT To repeal the Act of May 29, 1958, which authorized and directed the Administrator of General Services to provide for the release of restrictions and reservations contained in an instrument conveying certain land by the United States to the State of Wisconsin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of May 29, 1958 (72 Stat. 149), is hereby repealed.


*Approved June 29, 1960.*

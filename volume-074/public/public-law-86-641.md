# Public Law 86–641

> AN ACT To amend section 2771 of title 10, United States Code, to authorize certain payments of deceased members’ final accounts without the necessity of settlement by General Accounting Office.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the last sentence of section 2771(c) of title 10, United States Code, is amended to read as follows:

> “Payment under clause (6) of subsection (a) shall be made
> - **(1)** upon settlement by the General Accounting Office; or “(2) as otherwise authorized by the Comptroller General.”


*Approved July 12, 1960.*

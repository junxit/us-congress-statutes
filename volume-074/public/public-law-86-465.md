# Public Law 86–465

> AN ACT To repeal certain retirement promotion authority of the Coast and Geodetic Survey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of June 6, 1942 (ch. 383, 56 Stat. 328, as amended (33 U.S.C. 864e)), is hereby repealed.

## § 2.

This Act becomes effective on November 1, 1959.


*Approved May 13, 1960.*

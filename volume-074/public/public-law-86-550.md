# Public Law 86–550

> AN ACT To make American nationals eligible for scholarships and fellowships authorized by the National Science Foundation Act of 1950.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second sentence of section 10 of the National Science Foundation Act of 1950 (64 Stat. 149, 152; 42 U.S.C., sec. 1869) is amended by adding the words “or nationals” after the word “citizens”.


*Approved June 29, 1960.*

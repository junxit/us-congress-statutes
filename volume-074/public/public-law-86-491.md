# Public Law 86–491

> AN ACT To amend title 38, United States Code, to make uniform the marriage date requirements for service-connected death benefits.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That item (1) of subsection 302(a) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking out “ten years” and inserting in lieu thereof “fifteen years”.


*Approved June 8, 1960.*

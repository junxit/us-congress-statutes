# Public Law 86–512

> AN ACT To amend the Act of August 1, 1956 (70 Stat. 898)

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of August 1, 1956 (70 Stat. 898), is hereby amended by adding section 5 thereto, reading as follows:

> ## § 5.
>
> For the purposes of this Act, the words ‘restored lands’ include, without limiting the meaning thereof, those lands at Big Delta and Tok Junctions that are withdrawn by public land orders numbered 808 and 975 and that lie between the centerline of the Richardson and Glenn Highways and the land included within United States surveys 2727, 2728, 2770, 2771, 2772, 2773, 2774, 2723, 2724, 2725, and 2726.”


*Approved June 11, 1960.*

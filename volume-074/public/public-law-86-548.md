# Public Law 86–548

> AN ACT To provide that certain funds shall be paid to the Kickapoo Tribal Council of Oklahoma.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior shall pay to the Kickapoo Tribal Council of Oklahoma the unexpended balances of funds in the Treasury of the United States under the following symbols and titles: The funds paid the Kickapoo Tribal Council of Oklahoma under this Act shall be used for the rebuilding and repair of the Kickapoo tribal community house near McLoud, Oklahoma, and for other tribal business purposes.

- **(1)** 14X7053 Kickapoos in Oklahoma Fund;
- **(2)** 14X7553 Interest and Accruals on Interest, Kickapoos in Oklahoma Fund.


*Approved June 29, 1960.*

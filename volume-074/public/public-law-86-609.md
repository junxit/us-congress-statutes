# Public Law 86–609

> AN ACT To amend the Communications Act of 1934 with respect to certain rebroadcasting activities.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 318 of the Communications Act of 1934 (47 U.S.C. 318) is amended by striking out “(3) stations engaged in broadcasting, and” and insert in lieu thereof the following: “(3) stations engaged in broadcasting (other than those engaged solely in the function of rebroadcasting the signals of television broadcast stations), and”.

## § 2.

Section 319(d) of the Communications Act of 1934 (47 U.S.C. 319(d)) is amended by inserting after the period at the end thereof the following: “If the Commission finds that the public interest, convenience, and necessity would be served thereby, it may waive the requirement of a permit for construction of a station that is engaged solely in rebroadcasting television signals if such station was constructed on or before the date of enactment of this sentence.”


*Approved July 7, 1960.*

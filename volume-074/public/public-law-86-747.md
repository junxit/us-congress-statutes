# Public Law 86–747

> AN ACT Authorizing the President of the United States of America to present a gold medal to Robert Frost, a New England poet.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is authorized to present, in the name of Congress, an appropriate gold medal to Robert Frost in recognition of his poetry, which has enriched the culture of the United States and the philosophy of the world. For such purpose, the Secretary of the Treasury shall cause to be struck a gold medal with suitable emblems, devices, and inscriptions to be determined by the Secretary.

## § 2.

There is authorized to be appropriated the sum of $2,500 to carry out the provisions of this Act.


*Approved September 13, 1960.*

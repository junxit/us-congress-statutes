# Public Law 86–479

> AN ACT To extend until June 30, 1963, the suspension of duty on imports of crude chicory and the reduction in duty on ground chicory.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 1 and 3 of the Act entitled “An Act to suspend for two years the duty on crude chicory and to amend the Tariff Act of 1930 as it relates to chicory”, approved April 16, 1958, as amended (72 Stat. 87; 19 U.S.C. 1001, par. 776 and note; Public Law 86–441), are each amended by striking out “July 16, 1960” and inserting in lieu thereof “June 30, 1963”.


*Approved June 1, 1960.*

# Public Law 86–799

> AN ACT To establish a price support level for milk and butterfat.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (c) of section 201 of the Agricultural Act of 1949 (7 U.S.C. 1446), as amended, is further amended by adding the following new sentence to be inserted immediately after the first sentence: “Notwithstanding the foregoing provisions, for the period beginning with the enactment of this sentence and ending March 31, 1961, the price of milk for manufacturing purposes and the price of butterfat shall be supported at not less than $3.22 per hundredweight and 59.6 cents per pound, respectively.”


*Approved September 16, 1960.*

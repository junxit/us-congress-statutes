# Public Law 86–464

> AN ACT To extend the Export Control Act of 1949 for two additional years.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 12 of the Export Control Act of 1949 (63 Stat. 7), as amended by Public Law 33, Eighty-second Congress (65 Stat. 43), Public Law 62, Eighty-third Congress (67 Stat. 62), Public Law 631, Eighty-fourth Congress (70 Stat. 407), and Public Law 85–466, Eighty-fifth Congress (72 Stat. 220), is hereby amended by striking out “1960” and inserting in lieu thereof “1962”.


*Approved May 13, 1960.*

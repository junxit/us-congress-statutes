# Public Law 86–698

> JOINT RESOLUTION To authorize and request the President to issue a proclamation in connection with the centennial of the birth of Jane Addams, founder and leader of Chicago’s Hull House.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States is authorized and requested to proclaim September 6, 1960, as a day upon which all Americans should pay honor and respect to Jane Addams, founder and leader of Chicago’s Hull House.


*Approved September 2, 1960.*

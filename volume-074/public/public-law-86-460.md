# Public Law 86–460

> AN ACT To provide for the designation of a portion of the District of Columbia as the “Plaza of the Americas”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the portion of the District of Columbia located between Constitution Avenue and C Street, Northwest, and between Nineteenth and Seventeenth Streets, Northwest, is hereby designated as “Plaza of the Americas”.


*Approved May 13, 1960.*

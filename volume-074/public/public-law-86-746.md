# Public Law 86–746

> AN ACT To make permanent law the provisions of section 408 of the National Housing Act regulating savings and loan holding companies.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (g) of section 408 of the National Housing Act (12 U.S.C. 1730a(g)) is hereby repealed.


*Approved September 13, 1960.*

# Public Law 86–623

> AN ACT To grant the right, title, and interest of the United States in and to certain lands to the city of Crawford, Nebraska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior shall convey to the city of Crawford, Nebraska, upon the payment by the city of the sum of $500, which is the estimated fair market value thereof, all the right, title, and interest of the United States in and to the tract of land which is enclosed within the following boundary:

Starting at a point where the north line of the corporate limits of the city of Crawford, Dawes County, Nebraska, intercepts the east line of the tract of land granted, subject to certain conditions, to the village of Crawford, Nebraska, by the Vet of June 25, 1906 (34 Stat. 461), and running thence due west a distance of 660 feet, thence due north a distance of 660 feet, thence due east a distance of 660 feet, thence due south a distance of 660 feet to the place of origin, containing 10 acres more or less.


*Approved July 12, 1960.*

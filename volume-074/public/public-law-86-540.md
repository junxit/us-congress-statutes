# Public Law 86–540

> AN ACT To amend section 3(b) of the Act of May 9, 1958 (72 Stat. 105), relating to the preparation of a roll of the members of the Otoe and Missouria Tribe and to per capita distribution of judgment funds.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3(b) of the Act of May 9, 1958 (72 Stat. 105), is hereby amended by changing the period to a comma and by adding “or in accordance with such procedures as the Secretary determines will adequately protect the best interests of such persons.”


*Approved June 29, 1960.*

# Public Law 86–411

> AN ACT To amend section 2734 of title 10, United States Code, to extend the statute of limitations in the case of certain foreign claims.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2734(b) (1) of title 10, United States Code, is amended by striking out the words “one year” and inserting the words “two years” in place thereof.


*Approved April 8, 1960.*

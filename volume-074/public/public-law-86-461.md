# Public Law 86–461

> AN ACT To amend the Act relating to the Commission of Fine Arts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2 of the Act entitled “An Act Establishing a Commission of Fine Arts,” as amended (40 U.S.C. 106), is amended to read as follows:

> ## § 2.
>
> There are hereby authorized to be appropriated such amounts as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act.”


*Approved May 13, 1960.*

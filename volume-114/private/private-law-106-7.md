# Private Law 106–7

> An Act For the relief of Kerantha Poole-Christian.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. CLASSIFICATION AS A CHILD UNDER THE IMMIGRATION AND NATIONALITY ACT.

- **(a)** *In General.—* In the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Kerantha Poole-Christian shall be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(E) of such Act, upon approval of a petition filed on her behalf by Clifton or Linette Christian, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of such Act.
- **(b)** *Limitation.—* No natural parent, brother, or sister, if any, of Kerantha Poole-Christian shall, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved October 13, 2000.*

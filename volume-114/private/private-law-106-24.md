# Private Law 106–24

> An Act For the relief of Jose Guadalupe Tellez Finales.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. PERMANENT RESIDENCE.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, for purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1101 et seq.), Jose Guadalupe Tellez Pinales shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act upon payment of the required visa fee.


*Approved November 22, 2000.*

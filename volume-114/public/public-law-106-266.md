# Public Law 106–266

> An Act To designate the Federal facility located at 1301 Emmet Street in Charlottesville, Virginia, as the “Pamela B. Gwin Hall”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The Federal facility located at 1301 Emmet Street in Charlottesville, Virginia, shall be known and designated as the “Pamela B. Gwin Hall”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Federal building referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Pamela B. Gwin Hall”.


*Approved September 22, 2000.*

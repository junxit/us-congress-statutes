# Public Law 106–508

> An Act To provide for increased penalties for violations of the Export Administration Act of 1979, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 20 of the Export Administration Act of 1979 (50 U.S.C. App. 2419) is amended by striking “August 20, 1994” and inserting “August 20, 2001”.


*Approved November 13, 2000.*

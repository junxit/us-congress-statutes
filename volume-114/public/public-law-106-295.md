# Public Law 106–295

> An Act To designate the bridge on United States Route 231 that crosses the Ohio River between Maceo, Kentucky, and Rockport, Indiana, as the “William H. Natcher Bridge”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The bridge on United States Route 231 that crosses the Ohio River between Maceo, Kentucky, and Rockport, Indiana, shall be known and designated as the “William H. Natcher Bridge”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the bridge referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “William H. Natcher Bridge”.


*Approved October 13, 2000.*

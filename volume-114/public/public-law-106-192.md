# Public Law 106–192

> An Act To amend the Wild and Scenic Rivers Act to extend the designation of a portion of the Lamprey River in New Hampshire as a recreational river to include an additional river segment.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. SHORT TITLE.

This Act may be cited as the “Lamprey Wild and Scenic River Extension Act”.

## § 2. LAMPREY RECREATIONAL RIVER, NEW HAMPSHIRE.

- **(a)** *Additional Segment.—* The paragraph entitled “Lamprey River, New Hampshire” in section 3(a) of the Wild and Scenic Rivers Act (16 U.S.C. 1274(a)) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “11.5-mile segment extending from the southern Lee town line” and inserting “23.5-mile segment extending from the Bunker Pond Dam in Epping”; and
  - **(2)** by striking “towns of” and inserting “towns of Epping,”.
- **(b)** *Management.—* Section 405 of division 1 of the Omnibus Parks and Public Lands Management Act of 1996 (Public Law 104–333; 110 Stat. 4149; 16 U.S.C. 1274 note) is amended—
  - **(1)** in subsection (b)(2), by inserting “Epping,” before “Durham”; and
  - **(2)** by striking subsection (c).


*Approved May 2, 2000.*

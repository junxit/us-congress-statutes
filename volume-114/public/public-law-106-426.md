# Public Law 106–426

> Joint Resolution Making further continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 2001, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Public Law 106–275, is further amended by striking the date specified in section 106(c) and inserting “November 3, 2000”, and by adding, at the end, the following new section:

> ## § 120.
>
> Notwithstanding any other provision of this joint resolution, except section 107, $7,100,000 shall be available for obligation by the Administrator of General Services for expenses necessary to carry out the Presidential Transition Act of 1963 (3 U.S.C. 102 note).”.


*Approved November 3, 2000.*

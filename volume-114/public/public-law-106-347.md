# Public Law 106–347

> An Act To designate the post office and courthouse located at 2 Federal Square, Newark, New Jersey, as the “Frank R. Lautenberg Post Office and Courthouse”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The post office and courthouse located at 2 Federal Square, Newark, New Jersey, shall be known and designated as the “Frank R. Lautenberg Post Office and Courthouse”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the post office and courthouse referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Frank R. Lautenberg Post Office and Courthouse”.


*Approved October 23, 2000.*

# Public Law 106–306

> Joint Resolution Making further continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 2001, and for other purposes.

Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

That Public Law 106–275 is further amended by striking “October 14, 2000” in section 106(c) and inserting in lieu thereof “October 20, 2000”.

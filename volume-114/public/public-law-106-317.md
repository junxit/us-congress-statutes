# Public Law 106–317

> An Act To make technical corrections to title X of the Energy Policy Act of 1992.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DATE EXTENSIONS.

Section 1001 of the Energy Policy Act of 1992 (42 U.S.C. 2296a) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (b)(1)(B)(i), by striking “2002” and inserting “2007”;
- **(2)** in subsection (b)(1)(B)(ii), by striking “placed in escrow not later than December 31, 2002,” and inserting “incurred by a licensee after December 31, 2007,”; and
- **(3)** in subsection (b)(2)(E)(i) by striking “July 31, 2005” and inserting “December 31, 2008”.


*Approved October 19, 2000.*

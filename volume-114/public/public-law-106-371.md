# Public Law 106–371

> An Act To increase the amount authorized to be appropriated for the north side pumping division of the Minidoka reclamation project, Idaho.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 5 of the Act of September 30, 1950 (chapter 1114; 64 Stat, 1085), authorizing appropriations for the north side pumping division of the Minidoka reclamation project, Idaho, is amended by striking “$11,395,000” and inserting “$14,200,000”.


*Approved October 27, 2000.*

# Public Law 472

> AN ACT To provide that horticultural commodities shall be included within the tern) “agricultural commodities” for the purpose of the agricultural exemption for motor carriers in the interstate Commerce Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That clauses (4a) and (6) of subsection (b) of section 203 of the Interstate Commerce Act are amended by inserting after “agricultural” in each such clause the following: “(including horticultural)”.


*Approved July 9, 1952.*

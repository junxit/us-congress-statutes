# Public Law 323

> JOINT RESOLUTION To designate the lake to be formed by the waters impounded by the Wolf Creek Dam in the State of Kentucky as Lake Cumberland.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the lake to be formed by the waters impounded by the Wolf Creek Dam in the State of Kentucky shall hereafter be known as Lake Cumberland, and any law, regulation, document, or record of the United States in which such lake is designated or referred to shall be held to refer to such lake under and by the name of Lake Cumberland.


*Approved April 17, 1952.*

# Public Law 508

> AN ACT To authorize the Administrator of General Services to transfer to the Department of the Navy, without reimbursement, certain property at Fort Worth, Texas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Administrator of General Services is authorized to transfer to the Navy Department, without reimbursement, the Globe Aircraft plant, Fort Worth, Texas, consisting of approximately one hundred fifty-six and six-tenths acres of land and all improvements thereon.


*Approved July 11, 1952.*

# Public Law 500

> JOINT RESOLUTION To change the name of the South Coulee Dam in the Columbia Basin project to Dry Falls Dam.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the South Coulee Dam in the Columbia Basin project shall hereafter be known as Dry Falls Dam and any law, regulation, document, or record of the United States in which such dam is designated or referred to under the name South Coulee Dam shall be held to refer to such dam under and by the name of Dry Falls Dam.


*Approved July 10, 1952.*

# Public Law 386

> AN ACT To repeal the Alaska railroads tax.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, effective with respect to taxable years ending after the date of the enactment of this Act, chapter 8 of the Internal Revenue Code (imposing the Alaska railroads tax) is hereby repealed.


*Approved June 10, 1952.*

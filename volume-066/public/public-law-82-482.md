# Public Law 482

> AN ACT To amend sections 213 (b), 213 (c), and 215 of title II of the Hawaiian Homes Commission Act, 1920, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 213 (b) of the Hawaiian Homes Commission Act, 1920, as amended, is hereby further amended by increasing the amount of “$2,000,000” appearing therein to “$5,000,000”.

## § 2.

That section 213 (c) of the Hawaiian Homes Commission Act, 1920, as amended, is hereby further amended by increasing the amount, of “$400,000” appearing therein to “$800,000”.

## § 3.

That paragraph (1) of section 215 of title II of the Hawaiian Homes Commission Act, 1920, as amended, is hereby further amended in the following respects:

- **(a)** By amending the figure “$5,000” therein to read “$12,000”.
- **(b)** By amending the figure “$3,000” therein to read “$6,000”.

## § 4.

That paragraphs (2) and (3) of section 215 of title II of the Hawaiian Homes Commission Act, 1920, as amended, are hereby further amended by changing the phrase “3 per centum” wherever it appears in said paragraphs to read “2½ per centum”.


*Approved July 9, 1952.*

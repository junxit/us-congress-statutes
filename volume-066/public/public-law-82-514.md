# Public Law 514

> AN ACT To confer Federal jurisdiction to prosecute certain common-law crimes of violence when such crimes are committed on an American airplane in flight over the high seas or over waters within the admiralty and maritime jurisdiction of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 7 of title 18, United States Code, is hereby amended by adding at the end thereof a new subsection reading as follows:

> - **(5)** Any aircraft belonging in whole or in part to the United States, or any citizen thereof, or to any corporation created by or under the laws of the United States, or any State, Territory, district, or possession thereof, while such aircraft is in flight over the high seas, or over any other waters within the admiralty and maritime jurisdiction of the United States and out of the jurisdiction of any particular State.”


*Approved July 12, 1952.*

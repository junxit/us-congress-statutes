# Public Law 329

> AN ACT To extent the provisions of the Federal Credit Union Act, as amended, to the Virgin Islands.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 22 of the Federal Credit Union Act, as amended, is hereby amended to read as follows:

> ## § 22.
>
> The provisions of this Act shall be extended to and include the Panama Canal Zone and the Virgin Islands.”


*Approved May 8, 1952.*

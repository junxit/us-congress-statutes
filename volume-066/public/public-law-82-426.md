# Public Law 426

> AN ACT To amend the Act of January 12, 1951 (64 Stat. 1257), amending and extending title II of the First War Powers Act, 1943.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, section 2 of the Act of January 12, 1951 (64 Stat. 1257), is hereby amended by striking out “1952” and inserting in lieu thereof “1953”.


*Approved June 30, 1952.*

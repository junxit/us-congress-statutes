# Public Law 373

> AN ACT To amend the Act entitled “An Act to provide for the recording and releasing of Hens by entries on certificates of title for motor vehicles arid trailers, and for other purposes”, approved July 2, 1940, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4 of the Act entitled “An Act to provide for the recording and releasing of liens by entries on certificates of title for motor vehicles and trailers, and for other purposes”, approved July 2, 1940, as amended (54 Stat. 736; title 40, ch. 7, D. C. Code, 1940 edition), is amended by inserting after the word “engine” a comma and the words “serial or identification”.

## § 2.

Section 8 of said Act is amended by inserting after the word “engine” a comma and the words “serial or identification”.

## § 3.

Section 13 of said Act is amended by inserting after the word “engine” a comma and the words “serial or identification”.


*Approved June 4, 1952.*

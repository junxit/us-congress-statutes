# Public Law 460

> AN ACT To provide for the further development of cooperative agricultural extension work.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That appropriations available for agricultural extension work in the fiscal year ending June 30, 1953 (except the amount apportioned pursuant to section 23 (b) (2) of the Bankhead-Jones Act, as amended (7 U. S. C. 343d–1)), shall be paid to the States, Alaska, Hawaii, and Puerto Rico in the same proportions as appropriations available for such work in the fiscal year ending June 30, 1952.


*Approved July 7, 1952.*

# Public Law 420

> AN ACT To amend section 218 if) of the Social Security Act with respect to effective dates of agreements entered into with States before January 1, 1954.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 218 (f) of the Social Security Act, as amended (relating to the effective dates of voluntary agreements for coverage of State and local employees), is hereby amended by striking out “January 1, 1953” and inserting in lieu thereof “January 1, 1954”.


*Approved June 28, 1952.*

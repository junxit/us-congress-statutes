# Public Law 462

> JOINT RESOLUTION To amend the Act of July 1, 1947 (61 Stat. 242).

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first section of the Act of July 1, 1947 (61 Stat. 242), is amended by striking out “Marine Corps League, Incorporated,” and inserting “Marine Corps War Memorial Foundation”.

That section 3 of said Act be amended by striking out “five years” and inserting “ten years”.


*Approved July 7, 1952.*

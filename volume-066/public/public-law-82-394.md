# Public Law 394

> AN ACT To amend the Act entitled “An Act to create a board of accountancy for the District of Columbia, and for other purposes”, approved February 17, 1923.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the third sentence of section 8 of the Act entitled “An Act to create a board of accountancy for the District of Columbia, and for other purposes”, approved February 17, 1923 (42 Stat. 1263, ch. 94; sec. 2–908 D. C. Code, 1940 edition), is amended by striking therefrom “of $10 for each examination”, and inserting in lieu thereof “to be fixed by the Board of Accountancy, not exceeding $20 for each such examination”.


*Approved June 16, 1952.*

# Public Law 404

> AN ACT To extend the Rubber Act of 194S (Public Law 469, Eightieth Congress), as amended, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** subsection (a) of section 9 of the Rubber Act of 1948 (Public Law 469, Eightieth Congress), as amended, is further amended (1) by striking out “April 1, 1951” and inserting in lieu thereof “March 1, 1953”, and (2) by striking out “January 15, 1952” and inserting in lieu thereof “April 15, 1953”.
- **(b)** Section 20 of such Act, as amended, is further amended by striking out “June 30, 1952” and inserting in lieu thereof “March 31, 1954”.


*Approved June 23, 1952.*

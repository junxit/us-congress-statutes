# Public Law 355

> AN ACT To amend section 2800 (a) (5) of the Internal Revenue Code.

*Be it enacted by the Senate awl House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2800 (a) (5) of the Internal Revenue Code is amended by deleting the period at the end thereof and adding the following: “or to vodka produced from pure spirits in the manner authorized at registered distilleries.”

## § 2.

The amendment made by this Act shall take effect on the first day of the first month which begins more than ten days after the date of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved May 22, 1952.*

# Public Law 335

> AN ACT To authorize and direct the Administrator of General Services to transfer to the Department of the Nary the Government-owned magnesium foundry at Teterboro, New Jersey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Administrator of General Services is authorized and directed to transfer to the Department of the Navy, without reimbursement or exchange of funds, the facility at Teterboro, New Jersey, known as the Government-owned magnesium foundry, comprising Plancors 8 and 132.


*Approved May 13, 1952.*

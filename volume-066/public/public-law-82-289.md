# Public Law 289

> AN ACT To amend the Hawaiian Organic Act relating to qualification of jurors.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section S3 of the Hawaiian Organic Act (31 Stat. 141, 157; 48 U. SC., 1946 edition, see. 635) is hereby amended by deleting the word “male”.

## § 2.

This Act shall take effect as of the opening day of the term of the circuit courts of the several circuits in the Territory of Hawaii which follows the approval of this Act.


*Approved April 1, 1952.*

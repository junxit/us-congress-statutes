# Public Law 393

> JOINT RESOLUTION To continue the effectiveness of certain statutory provisions until June 30, 1952.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the joint resolution entitled “Joint resolution to continue the effectiveness of certain statutory provisions until June 1, 1952”, approved April 14, 1952 (Public Law 313, Eighty-second Congress), as amended, is amended by striking out “June 15, 1952” wherever it appears in such joint resolution, as amended, and inserting in lieu thereof “June 30, 1952”.


*Approved June 14, 1952.*

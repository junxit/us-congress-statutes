# Public Law 262

> AN ACT To extend to screen vehicle contractors benefits accorded star-route contractors with respect to the renewal of contracts and adjustment of contract pay.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** clause (1) of the next to last paragraph Of section 3951 of the Revised Statutes, as amended (U. S. C., title 39, sec. 434), is amended by inserting after the words “star-route” the words “or screen vehicle Service”.
- **(b)** Clause (2) of such paragraph is amended by inserting after the word “route” wherever it appears in such clause the words “or contract”.
- **(c)** The last paragraph of such section is amended by inserting after the words “star-route” the words “or screen vehicle service”.


*Approved February 29, 1952.*

# Public Law 438

> AN ACT To amend section 709 of title 18 of the United States Code.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 709 of title 18 of the United States Code is amended by inserting immediately before the last paragraph thereof a new paragraph as follows: “This section shall not make unlawful the use of the word ‘national’ as part of the name of any business or firm engaged in the insurance or indemnity business, whether such firm was engaged in the insurance or indemnity business prior or subsequent to the date of enactment of this paragraph.”


*Approved July 3, 1952.*

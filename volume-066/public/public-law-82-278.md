# Public Law 278

> AN ACT To correct a typographical error in Public Law 204, Eighty-second Congress, relating to assistant superintendents in the Motor Vehicle Service of the Post Office Department.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That section 13 (a) of the Act of July 6, 1945 (Public Law 134, Seventy-ninth Congress, as amended by section 8 of Public Law 204, Eighty-second Congress), is hereby further amended by changing the heading “Assistant superintendent” in the table therein to read “Assistant superintendents”.

## § 2.

This Act shall be effective as of July 1, 1951.


*Approved March 12, 1952.*

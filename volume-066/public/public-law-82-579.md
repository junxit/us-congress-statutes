# Public Law 579

> AN ACT To extend the duration of the Water Pollution Control Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the words “each of the five fiscal years during the period beginning July 1, 1948, and ending June 30, 1953” where they occur in section 7 and subsections (a), (c), (d), and (e) of section 8 of the Water Pollution Control Act (Public Law 845, Eightieth Congress), are hereby amended to read “each of the eight fiscal years during the period beginning July 1, 1948, and ending June 30, 1956”.


*Approved July 17, 1952.*

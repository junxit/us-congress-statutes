# Public Law 446

> AN ACT To amend the Act entitled “An Act to provide books for the adult blind”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first sentence of the Act of June 13, 1944 (58 Stat. 276), is amended by striking out the word “adult”.


*Approved July 3, 1952.*

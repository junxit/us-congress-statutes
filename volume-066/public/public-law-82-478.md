# Public Law 478

> AN ACT To amend the Act entitled “An Act to provide for the establishment of the Coronado International Memorial, in the State of Arizona“, approved August 18.1SM1 (W Stat. 030).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the words “Coronado National Memorial” are hereby substituted in lieu of the words “Coronado International Memorial” wherever such words occur in the Act of August 18, 1941 (55 Stat. 630).

## § 2.

That section 1 of the aforesaid Act is hereby amended by striking out “: Provided, That said proclamation shall not be issued until the President of the United States shall have been advised through official channels that the Government of Mexico has established, or provided for the establishment of, an area of similar type and size adjoining the area described herein”.


*Approved July 9, 1952.*

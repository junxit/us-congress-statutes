# Public Law 424

> AN ACT To amend the Act of August 7, 1946, providing for the establishment of a modern, adequate, and efficient hospital center in the District of Columbia, as amended, so as to extend to June 30, 11155, the period for author I art ion for appropriations for carrying out the purposes of the Act as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 6 of the Act entitled “An Act to provide for the establishment of a modern, adequate, and efficient hospital center in the District of Columbia”, approved August 7, 1946, as amended, is hereby amended by striking out “June 30, 1952” and inserting in lieu thereof “June 30, 1955”.


*Approved June 28, 1952.*

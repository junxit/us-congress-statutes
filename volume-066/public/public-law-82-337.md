# Public Law 337

> AN ACT To amend the Federal Credit Union Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph 7(d) of section 7 of the Federal Credit Union Act, as amended (12 U. S. C. 1757), be amended to read as follows: “or in shares or accounts of Federal savings and loan associations and in shares or accounts of any other institution, the accounts of which are insured by the Federal Savings and Loan Insurance Corporation”.


*Approved May 13, 1952.*

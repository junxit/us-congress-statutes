# Senate Concurrent Resolution 60

> KIM SONG NORE

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the action of the two Houses in connection with the passage of the bill (S. 1236) for the relief of Kim Song Nore be rescinded, and that the said bill be postponed indefinitely.


*Agreed to February 7, 1952.*

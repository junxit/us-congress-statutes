# Senate Concurrent Resolution 86

> GENERAL MEETING OF COMMONWEALTH PARLIAMENTARY ASSOCIATION

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Vice President and the Speaker of the House of Representatives are authorized to appoint four Members of the Senate and four Members of the House of Representatives, respectively, to attend the next general meeting of the Commonwealth Parliamentary Association to be held in Canada on the invitation of the Canadian branch of the association and to designate the chairmen of the delegations from each of the Houses to be present at such meeting. The expenses incurred by the members of the delegations and staff appointed for the purpose of carrying out this concurrent resolution shall not exceed $4,500 for each of the delegations and shall be reimbursed to them from the contingent, fund of the House of which they are Members, upon submission of vouchers approved by the chairman of the delegation of which they are members.


*Agreed to July 2, 1952.*

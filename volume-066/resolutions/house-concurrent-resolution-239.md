# House Concurrent Resolution 239

> ENROLLMENT OF GENERAL APPROPRIATION BILLS

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That, in the enrollment of general appropriation bills enacted during the remainder of the second session of the Eighty-second Congress, the Clerk of the House may correct chapter, title, and section numbers.


*Passed July 5, 1952.*

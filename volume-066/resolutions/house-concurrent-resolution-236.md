# House Concurrent Resolution 236

> IMMIGRATION AND NATIONALITY ACT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed thirteen thousand additional copies of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Public Law 414, Eighty-second Congress, second session, of which ten thousand copies shall be for the House document room and three thousand copies for the Senate document room.


*Passed July 1, 1952.*

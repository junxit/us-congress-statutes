# Senate Concurrent Resolution 88

> FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM, STATE BANKS

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Secretary of the Senate be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, in the enrollment of the bill (S. 2938) to amend section 9 of the Federal Reserve Act, as amended, and section 5155 of the Revised Statutes, as amended, and for other purposes, to make the following correction, namely:

On page 2, line 19, of the Senate engrossed bill, in lieu of the language “(b) Subsection (c) or section” insert “(b) Subsection (c) of section”.


*Agreed to July 3, 1952.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 240

> ADJOURNMENT SINE DIE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Monday, July 7, 1952, and that when they adjourn on said day they stand adjourned sine die.


*Passed July 7, 1952.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 208

> ADJOURNMENT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Thursday, April 10, 1952, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Tuesday, April 22, 1952.


*Passed April 10, 1952.*

# Private Law 906

> AN ACT For the relief of Iwanna Pryjma and Roma Pryjma.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the adminis- tration of the immigration laws, Iwanna Pryjma and Roma Pryjma shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted into the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required head taxes and visa fees.

## § 2.

The Secretary of State is authorized and directed to instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct two numbers from the non-preference category of the appropriate immigration quota for the first year such quota is available.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

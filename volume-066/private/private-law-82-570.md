# Private Law 570

> AN ACT For the relief of Mark Yoke Lun and Mark Seep Ming.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, the provisions of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, shall be held to be applicable to the aliens, Mark Yoke Lun and Mark Seep Ming, the minor, unmarried children of Mark Teung Quong, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved April 18, 1952.*

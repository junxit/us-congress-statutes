# Private Law 934

> AN ACT For the relief of Kenji Kusumoto.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, Kenji Kusumoto, Japanese husband of Mrs. Grace Y. Kusumoto, a United States citizen, may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if he is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

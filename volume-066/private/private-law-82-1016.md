# Private Law 1016

> AN ACT For the Relief of Harris A. Bakken.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Harris A. Bakken, of Wilmington, California, formerly sales officer at Vancouver Barracks, Washington, is relieved of all liability to refund to the United States the sum of $3,692.02, plus interest which has since accrued. Such sum represents the amount for which he is accountable because of a shortage in his accounts. The Comptroller General is authorized and directed to allow credit in the settlement of the accounts of the said Harris A. Bakken in the sum of $3,692.02, phis interest on such sum.


*Approved July 17, 1952.*

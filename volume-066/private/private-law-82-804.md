# Private Law 804

> AN ACT For the relief of Maria Weiland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the minor child, Maria Weiland, shall be held and considered to be natural-born alien child of First Lieutenant and Mrs. John P. Fowler, citizens of the United States.


*Approved July 9, 1952.*

# Private Law 434

> AN ACT For the relief of Robert William Lauber.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United states of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of that Act, the minor child, Robert William Lauber, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien minor child of Sergeant and Mrs. William J. Lauber, citizens of the United States.


*Approved February 12, 1952.*

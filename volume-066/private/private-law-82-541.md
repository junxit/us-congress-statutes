# Private Law 541

> AN ACT For the relief of Hanne Lore Hart.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the minor child, Hanne Lore Hart, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Master Sergeant and Mrs. Steven L. Hart, citizens of the United States.


*Approved April 10, 1952.*

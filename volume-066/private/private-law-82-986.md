# Private Law 986

> AN ACT For the relief of Ruth Ann Holecek.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of that Act, the minor child, Ruth Ami Holecek. shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Frank G. Holecek, citizens of the United States.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

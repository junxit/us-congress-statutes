# Private Law 679

> AN ACT For the relief of Sifters Maria Salerno, Eufrasisa Binotto, Marla Ballatore, and Giovanna Buziol.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Sisters Maria Salerno. Eufrasisa Binotto, Maria Ballatore, and Giovanna Buziol shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fees and head taxes. Upon the granting of permanent residence to such aliens as provided for in this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct four numbers from the appropriate quota for the first year or years that such quota is available.


*Approved June 11, 1952.*

# Private Law 569

> AN ACT For the relief of Leda Taft.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, the provisions of sections 4(a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, shall be held to be applicable, to Leda Taft and for this purpose she shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of William Taft, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved April 18, 1952.*

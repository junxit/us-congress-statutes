# Private Law 859

> AN ACT For the relief of Panagiotes Itounieliotis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, Panagiotes Roumeliotis shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted into the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required head tax and visa fee.

## § 2.

The Secretary of State is authorized and directed to instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the nonpreference category of the appropriate immigration quota for the first year such quota is available.


*Approved July 14, 1952.*

# Private Law 590

> AN ACT For the relief of Gylda Haydel Wagner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, solely for the purposes of section 4 (a) and section 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, and notwithstanding any provisions excluding from admission to the United States persons of races ineligible to citizenship. Gylda Raydel Wagner, a minor half-Japanese child, shall be considered’ the alien natural-born child of Technical Sergeant George F. Wagner and his wife, Lloyce P. Wagner, citizens of the United States.


*Approved May 9, 1952.*

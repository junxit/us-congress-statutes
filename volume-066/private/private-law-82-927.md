# Private Law 927

> AN ACT For the relief of Nahan Abdo Haj Moussa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the minor child Nahari Abdo Haj Moussa shall be held and considered to be the natural born alien child of Nageeb Abdo, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

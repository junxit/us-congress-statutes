# Private Law 606

> AN ACT For the relief of Lieselotte Maria Kuebler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, the alien Lieselotte Maria Kuebler (the German fiancée of Joseph J. Nikisher, a United States citizen and an honorably discharged veteran of the United States Armed Forces) shall be eligible for admission to the United States as a non-immigrant temporary visitor for a period of three months if the appropriate administrative authorities find that the said Lieselotte Maria Kuebler is coming to the United States with a bona fide intention of being married to the said Joseph J. Nikisher and that she is otherwise admissible under the immigration laws. If the marriage between the above-named parties does not occur wit hin three months after the entry of said Lieselotte Maria Kuebler. she shall be required to depart from the United States and upon failure to do so shall be deported in accordance with sections 19 and 20 of the Immigration Act of February 5, 1917, as amended (U. S. C., 1946 edition, title 8, secs. 155 and 156). If the above-named parties are married within three months after the entry of said Lieselotte Maria Kuebier, the Attorney General is authorized and directed to record the lawful admission for permanent residence of the said Lieselotte Maria Kuebier upon the payment of the required visa fee and head tax.


*Approved May 13, 1952.*

# Private Law 443

> AN ACT For the relief of the minor child, Pengsiu Mei.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 2 of the Act of December 17, 1943, as amended (57 Stat. 601; 60 Stat. 975, 8 U. S. C. 212 (a)), the minor child, Pengsiu Mei, may be admitted to the United States as a non-quota immigrant if such alien is otherwise admissible under the immigration laws.


*Approved February 14, 1952.*

# Private Law 697

> AN ACT For the relief of Kathleen Cowley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding any provision of law excluding a aliens inadmissible to the United States because of race, the minor child, Kathleen Cowley, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Captain and Mrs. James E. Cowley, citizens of the United States.


*Approved June 12, 1952.*

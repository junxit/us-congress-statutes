# Private Law 657

> AN ACT For the relief of Dulcie Ann Steinhardt Sherlock.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 201 (g) of the Nationality Act of 1940, as amended (8 U. S. C. 601 (g)), Dulcie Ann Steinhardt Sherlock, daughter of the late Ambassador Laurence A. Steinhardt and Mrs. Steinhardt, shall be held and considered to have been residing in the United States during all the time she was residing abroad with her parents during her minority when her father was an Ambassador in the Foreign Service of the United States.


*Approved May 29, 1952.*

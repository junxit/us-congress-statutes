# Private Law 501

> AN ACT For the relief of Tory Lee Eakin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the minor child, Tory Lee Eakin, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Lieutenant and Mrs. John B. Eakin, citizens of the United States.


*Approved March 28, 1952.*

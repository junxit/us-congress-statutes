# Private Law 857

> AN ACT For the relief of Peter Rousetos, also known as Panagiotis Roussetos, also known as Panagiotis Roussetos Metritikas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the minor child, Peter Rousetos, also known as Panagiotis Roussetos, also known as Panagiotis Roussetos Metritikas, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and, Mrs. John Metritikas, citizens of the United States.


*Approved July 12, 1952.*

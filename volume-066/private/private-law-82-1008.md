# Private Law 1008

> AN ACT For the Relief of Ai-Ling Tung Tsou and her son, Moody Tsou.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 2 of the Act of December 17, 1943, as amended (8 U, S, C. 212 (a)), Ai-Ling Tung Tsou and her son, Moody Tsou, may be admitted to the United States as preferential quota immigrants in accordance with section 6(a) (2) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, if they are otherwise admissible to the United States.


*Approved July 16, 1952.*

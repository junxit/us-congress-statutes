# Private Law 550

> AN ACT For the relief of Julie Bettelheim and Evelyn Lang Hirsch.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Julie Bettelheim and Evelyn Lang Hirsch shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent, residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon the payment of the required visa fee and head tax. Upon the granting of permanent residence to such aliens as provided for in this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct two numbers from the number of displaced persons who shall be granted the status of permanent residence pursuant to section 4 of the Displaced Persons Act, as amended (62 Stat. 1011; 64 Stat. 219; 50 U. S. C. App, 1953).


*Approved April 11, 1952.*

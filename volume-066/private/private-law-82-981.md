# Private Law 981

> AN ACT For the relief of Raymond Scott Hill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the minor child, Haymond Scott Hill, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Frank Johnson Hill, Junior, citizens of the United States. Notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of the said Act, the said Raymond Scott Hill may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if he is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

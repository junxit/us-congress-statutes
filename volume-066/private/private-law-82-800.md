# Private Law 800

> AN ACT For the relief of Alevtina Olson and Tatiana Snejina.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Alevtina Olson and Tatiana Snejina shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fees and head taxes. Upon the granting of permanent residence to each such alien as provided for in this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota officer to deduct two numbers from the appropriate quota for the first year that such quota is available.


*Approved July 9, 1952.*

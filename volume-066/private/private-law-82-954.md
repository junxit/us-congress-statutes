# Private Law 954

> AN ACT For the relief of Linda Lee Converse.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the minor child, Linda Lee Converse, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Staff Sergeant and Mrs. Arthur K. Converse, Junior, citizens of the United States.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

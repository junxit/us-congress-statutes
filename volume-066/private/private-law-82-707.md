# Private Law 707

> AN ACT For the relief of Susan Jeanne Kerr.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purpose of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, Susan Jeanne Kerr shall be considered to be the alien natural-born daughter of her adoptive parents, Captain and Mrs. John K. Kerr, United States citizens.


*Approved June 13, 1952.*

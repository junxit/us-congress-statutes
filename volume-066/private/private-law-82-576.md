# Private Law 576

> AN ACT For the relief of Kazumi Yamashito.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and, notwithstanding the provision of section 13 (c) of that Act, the minor child, Kazumi Yamashito, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Technical Sergeant and Mrs. Edward W. Gentry, citizens of the United States.


*Approved April 18, 1952.*

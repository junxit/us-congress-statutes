# Private Law 589

> AN ACT For the relief of Kunio Itoh.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of that Act, the minor child, Kunio Itoh, shall be held and considered to be the natural-horn alien child of Staff Sergeant and Mrs. Herman W. Robertson, citizens of the United States.


*Approved May 9, 1952.*

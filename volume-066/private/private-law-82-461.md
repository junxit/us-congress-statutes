# Private Law 461

> AN ACT For the relief of Wong Woo, also known as William Curtis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, solely for the purpose of section 4 (a) and section 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, Wong Woo, also known as William Curtis, a Chinese child, shall be considered the alien natural-born child of Captain and Mrs. Ralph Archer, citizens of the United States.


*Approved February 29, 1952.*

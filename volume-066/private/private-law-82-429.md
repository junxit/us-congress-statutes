# Private Law 429

> AN ACT For the relief of Constance Chin Hung.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, the provisions of sections 4(a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, shall be held to be applicable to the alien Constance Chin Hung, the minor, unmarried child of George Chin Hung, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved February 12, 1952.*

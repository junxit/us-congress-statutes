# Private Law 672

> AN ACT For the relief of Joachim Volk, also known as Steven Craig Delano.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, solely for the purposes of section 4 (a) and section 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, Joachim Volk, also known as Steven Craig Delano, shall be considered the alien natural-born child of Warrant Officer Junior Grade and Mrs. Norman Delano, citizens of the United States.


*Approved June 11, 1952.*

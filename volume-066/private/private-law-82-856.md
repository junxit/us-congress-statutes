# Private Law 856

> AN ACT For the relief of Leopold Laufer and Elfriede Laufer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the minor children Leopold Laufer and Elfriede Laufer shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien children of Mr. and Mrs. Adam Roth, citizens of the United States.


*Approved July 12, 1952.*

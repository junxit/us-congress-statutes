# Private Law 710

> AN ACT For the relief of Pauline W. Goodyear.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of the eleventh category of section 3 of (he Immigration Act of 1917, as amended, Mrs. Pauline W. Goodyear may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved June 13, 1952.*

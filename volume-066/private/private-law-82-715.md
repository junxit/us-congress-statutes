# Private Law 715

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Ann Morrison.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the national service life insurance in the amount, of $10,000 (N–3187200) granted to the late Leonard Morrison, who died on June 25, 1948, shall be held and considered to have been in effect at the time of his death. The Administrator of Veterans’ Affairs shall pay such insurance in accordance with the National Service Life Insurance Act of 1940, as amended, except that any payments made as a result of enactment of this Act shall be made directly from the national service life insurance appropriation.


*Approved June 19, 1952.*

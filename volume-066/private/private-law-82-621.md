# Private Law 621

> AN ACT For the relief of Janice Justina King.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purpose of section 4 (a) and section 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, and notwithstanding any provisions excluding from admission to the United States persons of races ineligible to citizenship, Janice Justina King, a minor half-Japanese child, shall be considered the alien natural-born child of Captain Reuben R. King, a citizen of the United States, and Mrs. Reuben R. King.


*Approved May 16, 1952.*

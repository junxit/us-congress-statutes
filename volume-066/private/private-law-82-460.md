# Private Law 460

> AN ACT For the relief of Mitsuko Sakata Lord.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, Mitsuko Sakata Lord, the wife of Peter J. Lord, a United States citizen, may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved February 29, 1952.*

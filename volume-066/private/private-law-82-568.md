# Private Law 568

> AN ACT For the relief of Kazuyoshi Hino and Yasuhiko Hino.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, Kazuyoshi Hino and Yasuhiko Hino, the minor unmarried children of a citizen of the United States, may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if they are found to be otherwise admissible under the immigration laws.


*Approved April 18, 1952.*

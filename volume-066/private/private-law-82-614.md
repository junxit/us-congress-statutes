# Private Law 614

> AN ACT For the relief of Maria Seraphenia Egawa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 4 (a) and section 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, and notwithstanding any provisions excluding from admission to the United States persons of races ineligible to citizenship, Maria Seraphenia Egawa, a minor half-Japanese child, shall be considered the alien natural-born child of Sergeant and Mrs. Elmer F. Grant, citizens of the United States.


*Approved May 15, 1952.*

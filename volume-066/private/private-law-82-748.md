# Private Law 748

> AN ACT For the relief of Takae Nomura,

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, relating to the exclusion of aliens inadmissible because of race, shall not hereafter apply to Takae Nomura, the Japanese fiancée of Isamu H. Yamaki, a citizen of the United States and a civilian employee of the United States Government serving in Japan, and that the said Takae Nomura may be eligible for a nonquota immigration visa if she is found otherwise admissible under the immigration laws; Provided, That the administrative authorities find that marriage between the above-named parties occurred within three months immediately succeeding the enactment date of this Act.


*Approved June 28, 1952.*

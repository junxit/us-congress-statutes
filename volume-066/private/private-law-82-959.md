# Private Law 959

> AN ACT For the relief of Margarite Mary Fujita,

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the, purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of such Act, the minor child Margarite Mary Fujita shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Harold H. Fujita, citizens of the United States.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

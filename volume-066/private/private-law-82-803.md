# Private Law 803

> AN ACT For the relief of Heidi Geraldine Connelly.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, us amended, the minor child, Heidi Geraldine Connelly, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Captain and Mrs. Chester C. Connelly, citizens of the United States.


*Approved July 9, 1952.*

# Private Law 879

> AN ACT For the relief of Walter Duschinsky.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding those provisions of section 4 of the Displaced Persons Act of 1948, as amended, relating to date of application for an adjustment of immigration status, the alien, Walter Duschinsky, may, at any time within six months following the effective date of this Act, apply the Attorney General for the adjustment of his immigration status, and notwithstanding the provisions of subsection (b) of the said section 4, other than those relating to the status at the time of entry, such alien shall, if he is otherwise qualified under the provisions of said section 4, be deemed to be a displaced person within the meaning of said section 4.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

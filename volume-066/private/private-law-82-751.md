# Private Law 751

> AN ACT For the relief of Angela Monte McCracken.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of the eleventh category of section 3 of the Immigration Act of 1917, as amended, Angela Moniz McCracken may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible tinder the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved June 28, 1952.*

# Private Law 425

> AN ACT For the relief of Francesco Fratalia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of the eleventh category of section 3 of the Immigration Act of 1917, as amended, Francesco Fratalia may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved February 5, 1952.*

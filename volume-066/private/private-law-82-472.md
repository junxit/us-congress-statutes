# Private Law 472

> AN ACT For the relief of Mieko Takamine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, solely for the purposes of section 4 (a) and section 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding any provisions excluding from admission to the United States persons of races ineligible to citizenship, Mieko Takamine, a minor Japanese child, shall be considered the alien natural-born child of Corporal Joseph A. Gruber, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved February 29, 1952.*

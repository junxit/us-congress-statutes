# Private Law 423

> AN ACT For the relief of Carl Weitlanner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the naturalization and immigration laws, section 307 (a) (1) of the Nationality Act of 1940, as amended, shall not apply to Carl Weitlanner.


*Approved February 5, 1952.*

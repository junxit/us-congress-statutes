# Private Law 459

> AN ACT For the relief of Edward Chi-Kan Lam.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, the provisions of section 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, shall be held to be applicable to the alien Edward Chi-Kan Lam, the husband of Errancies Lew, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved February 29, 1952.*

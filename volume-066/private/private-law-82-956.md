# Private Law 956

> AN ACT For the relief of Deborah Anita Hudson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, shall not apply to Deborah Anita Hudson (nee Kazuko Hirayanagi), minor half-Japanese child in the care of Sergeant James E. Hudson (a member of the Armed Forces of the United States now serving in Japan) and Mrs. Catherine J. Hudson, citizens of the United States. For the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the said Deborah Anita Hudson shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of the said Sergeant James E. Hudson and Mrs. Catherine J. Hudson.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

# Private Law 974

> AN ACT For the relief of Tracy Ann Corley (Elisabeth Lecorehe).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, Tracy Ann Corley (Elisabeth Lecorehe) shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien minor child of Technical Sergeant and Mrs. Robert C. Corley, citizens of the United States.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

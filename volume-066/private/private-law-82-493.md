# Private Law 493

> AN ACT For the relief of Oswald A. Drica-Minieris.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, the Attorney General of the United States is authorized and directed to record the lawful admission for permanent residence in the United States of Oswald A. Drica-Minieris as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fee and head tax.

## § 2.

Upon enactment of this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the appropriate quota for the first year the said quota is available.


*Approved March 28, 1952.*

# Private Law 970

> AN ACT For the relief of Samuele Rossi (also known as Renato Rossi).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the alien Samuele Rossi (also known as Renato Rossi) shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien minor child of Mr. and Mrs. John Goldassio, citizens of the United States.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

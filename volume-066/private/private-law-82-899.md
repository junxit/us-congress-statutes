# Private Law 899

> AN ACT For the relief of Hsieh Ta-Chuan or Der Ott-Kuan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 2 of the Act of December 17, 1943, as amended (57 Stat. 601; 60 Stat. 975, 8 U. S. C. 212 (a)), Hsieh Ta-Chuan or Der Ott-Kuan, alien minor child of William Shao-Tang Hsieh, a United States citizen, may be admitted to the United States as a nonquota immigrant in accordance with sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, if such alien is otherwise admissible under the immigration laws.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

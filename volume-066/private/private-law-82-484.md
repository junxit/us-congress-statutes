# Private Law 484

> AN ACT For the relief of Hai Soon Lee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the naturalization laws, section 303 of the Nationality Act of 1940, as amended (8 U. S. C., sec. 703), shall not apply to Hai Soon Lee, wife of Han Ken Lee, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved March 5, 1952.*

# Private Law 696

> AN ACT For the relief of Eugene Richard Sushko.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 if the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, Eugene Richard Sushko shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of First Lieutenant and Mrs. Albert R. Sushko, citizens of the United States.


*Approved June 12, 1952.*

# Private Law 427

> AN ACT For the relief of Ai Mei Yu and Ai Mei Chen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the minor children, Ai Mei Yu and Ai Mei Chen, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien children of Adelia L. Eggestein, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved February 12, 1952.*

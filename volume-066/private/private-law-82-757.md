# Private Law 757

> AN ACT For the relief of Chizuko Nakagami.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, section 13 (e) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, shall not apply to Chizuko Nakagami, Japanese minor child in the care of Mr. and Mrs. George W. Uderitz, citizens of the United States. For the purposes of sections 4(a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the said Chizuko Nakagami shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien minor child of the said Mr. and Mrs. George W. Uderitz.


*Approved June 28, 1952.*

# Private Law 473

> AN ACT For the relief of Carol Ann Hutchins (Sybille Schubert).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Rouse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the minor child, Carol Ann Hutchins (Sybille Schubert), shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Captain and Mrs. La Vern C. Hutchins, citizens of the United States.


*Approved February 29, 1952.*

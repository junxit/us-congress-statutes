# Private Law 763

> AN ACT For the relief of Gordon Uglow.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of the eleventh category of section 3 of the Immigra- tion Act of 1917, as amended, Gordon Uglow may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if he is found to be otherwise admissible tinder the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved June 28, 1952.*

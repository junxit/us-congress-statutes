# Private Law 772

> AN ACT To fix the seniority rights and service of Albert O. Raeder as sergeant in the District of Columbia Fire Department.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purpose of determining the seniority rights and service of Albert O. Raeder, in the rank of sergeant in the District of Columbia Fire Department he shall be held to have been promoted to such rank as of May 1, 1947.


*Approved July 1, 1952.*

# Private Law 507

> AN ACT For the relief of Calcedonio Tagliarini.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of the eleventh category of section 3 of the immigration Act of 1917, as amended, Calcedonio Tagliarini may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if he is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved April 3, 1952.*

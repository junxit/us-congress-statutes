# Private Law 567

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Carla Mulligan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of the eleventh category of section 3 of the Immigration Act of 1917, as amended, Mrs. Carla Mulligan, German wife of William J, Mulligan, a citizen of the United States and World War II veteran, may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved April 18, 1952.*

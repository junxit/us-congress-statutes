# Private Law 559

> AN ACT For the relief of Rumi Takemura.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of such Act, the minor child, Rumi Takemura, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Sergeant Juan M. Valdez, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved April 17, 1952.*

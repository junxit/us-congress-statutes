# Private Law 925

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Tomiko Munakata Millhollin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, excluding from the United States aliens ineligible to citizenship, shall not apply to Mrs. Tomiko Munakata Millhollin, Japanese wife of Lorrin Millhollin, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

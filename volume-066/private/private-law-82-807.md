# Private Law 807

> AN ACT For the relief of Karen Christ one Eisen Murdock.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the minor child, Karen Christene Eisen Murdock, shall be held and considered to be the natural born alien child of Master Sergeant and Mrs. David L. Murdock, citizens of the United States.


*Approved July 9, 1952.*

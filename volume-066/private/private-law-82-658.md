# Private Law 658

> AN ACT For the relief of Matheos Alafouzos.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 4 (a) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the marriage between Martha Vlahos, a United States citizen, and Matheos Alafouzos, a citizen of Greece, shall be deemed to have occurred prior to January 1, 1948.


*Approved May 29, 1952.*

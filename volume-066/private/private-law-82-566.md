# Private Law 566

> AN ACT For the relief of Hisamitsu Kodani.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, Hisamitsu Kodani, the minor unmarried child of lawfully resident aliens of the United States, may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if lie is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved April 18, 1952.*

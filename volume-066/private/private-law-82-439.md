# Private Law 439

> AN ACT For the relief of Ritsuko Chojin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (e) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, Ritsuko Chojin, the wife of Masakatsu Chojin, a United States citizen, may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is fourni to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved February 13, 1952.*

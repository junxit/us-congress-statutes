# Private Law 720

> AN ACT For the relief of Rosarina Garofalo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purposes of section 6 (a) (2) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, Rosarina Garofalo shall be held and considered to be under twenty-one years of age.


*Approved June 27, 1952.*

# Private Law 946

> AN ACT For the relief of Antonio and Francesco Lo Schiavo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act. of 1924, as amended, the minor children, Antonio and Francesco Lo Schiavo, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien children of Mrs. Margaret Lo Schiavo, citizen of the United States.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

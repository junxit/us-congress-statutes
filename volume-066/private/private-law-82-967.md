# Private Law 967

> AN ACT For the relief of Paul Gust Williams.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the minor child, Paul Gust Williams (formerly Paavo Luomaranta), shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Clarke M. Williams, citizens of the United States.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

# Private Law 575

> AN ACT For the relief of Joint Michael Jurecek.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4(a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding; the provisions of section 13 (c) of that Act, the minor child, John Michael Jurecek, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Technical Sergeant and Mrs. Forrest C. Jurecek.


*Approved April 18, 1952.*

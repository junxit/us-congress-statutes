# Private Law 953

> AN ACT For the relief of Mark Yen Hui.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration Laws, sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, shall be held to be applicable in the case of Mark Yen Hui, a minor alien child of Mark Jung, citizen of the United States.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

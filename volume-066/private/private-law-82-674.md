# Private Law 674

> AN ACT For the relief of Debra Elaine Evans.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the minor child, Debra Elaine Evans, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Technical Sergeant and Mrs. Charles E. Evans, citizens of the United Slates.


*Approved June 11, 1952.*

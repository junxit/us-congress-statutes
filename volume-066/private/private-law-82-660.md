# Private Law 660

> AN ACT For the relief of Sharon Elaine Frankovich.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the minor child, Sharon Elaine Frankovich, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Sergeant First Class and Mrs. Steve Frankovich, citizens of the United States.


*Approved June 4, 1952.*

# Private Law 991

> AN ACT For the relief of Hildegard Hohmeier.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, the alien Hildegard Hohmeier, the German fiancée of Melvin C. Fehnel, sergeant in the United States Armed Forces and a citizen of the United States, shall be eligible for a visa as a nonimmigrant, temporary visitor for a period of three months: Provided, That the administrative authorities find that the said Hildegard Hobmeier is coining to the United States with a bona fide intention of being married to the said Melvin C. Fehnel, and that she is found otherwise admissible under the immigration laws. In the event that the marriage between the above-named parties does not occur within three months after the entry of the said Hildegard Hobmeier, she shall be required to depart from the United States, and upon failure to do so shall be deported in accordance with the provisions of sections 19 and 20 of the Immigration Act of 1917, as amended (U. S. C., title 8, secs. 155 and 156). In the event that, the marriage between the above-named parties shall occur within three months after the entry of the said Hildegard Hobmeier, the Attorney General is authorized and directed to record the lawful admission for permanent residence of the said Hildegard Hobmeier as of the date of the payment by her of the required visa fee and head tax.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

# Private Law 979

> AN ACT For the relief of Wong Yang Yee and Wong Sue Chee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, the provisions of section 4 (a) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, pertaining to unmarried children under twenty-one years of age of a citizen of the United States, shall be held to be applicable to the aliens Wong Yang Yee and Wong Sue Chee, minor children of Eddie Huie, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

# Private Law 750

> AN ACT For the relief of Samuel Thomas Wong.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, the provisions of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, shall be held to be applicable to the alien, Samuel Thomas Wong, the minor, unmarried child of Samuel Eugene Wong, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved June 28, 1952.*

# Private Law 673

> AN ACT For the relief of Midori Sugimoto.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of that Act, the minor child. Midori Sugimoto, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Lieutenant and Mrs. Thomas H. Malini, citizens of the United States.


*Approved June 11, 1952.*

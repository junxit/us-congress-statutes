# Private Law 949

> AN ACT For the relief of Toshiko Nakamuta Takimoto and her minor son.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended. Toshiko Nakamuta Takimoto, the wife of Kiyoshi Takimoto, a United States citizen, and their minor son, may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if they are found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the Immigration laws.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

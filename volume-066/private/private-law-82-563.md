# Private Law 563

> AN ACT For the relief of Isao Ishimoto.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That solely for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding any provision of law excluding from the United States persons of races ineligible to citizenship, Isao Ishimoto, minor half-Japanese child, shall be held and considered to be the alien natural-born child of Sergeant and Mi’s. William H. Yoder, United States citizens.


*Approved April 18, 1952.*

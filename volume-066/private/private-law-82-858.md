# Private Law 858

> AN ACT For the relief of William J. Martin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to cancel the indebtedness of William J. Martin, in the amount of $146.86, arising out of the transportation of his household goods from Leland, Mississippi, to Arlington, Virginia, while he was an employee of the United States Department of Agriculture in September 1947.


*Approved July 12, 1952.*

# Private Law 801

> AN ACT For the relief of Rhee Song Wu.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding any provision of law excluding aliens inadmissible to the United States because of race, the minor child, Rhee Song Wu, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Captain and Mrs. Sylvester W. Booker, citizens of the United States.


*Approved July 9, 1952.*

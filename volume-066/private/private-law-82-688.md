# Private Law 688

> AN ACT For the relief of Stephan Joseph Horvath and Lucas Albert Horvath.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding any provision of law excluding from admission to the United States persons of race ineligible to citizenship, Stephan Joseph Horvath and Lucas Albert Horvath, minor children under the care of Technical Sergeant and Mrs. S. J. Horvath, Junior, both citizens of the United States residing temporarily in Japan, shall be held and considered for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, to be the natural-born alien children of the said Technical Sergeant and Mrs. S. J. Horvath, Junior.


*Approved June 11, 1952.*

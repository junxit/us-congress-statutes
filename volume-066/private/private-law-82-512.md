# Private Law 512

> AN ACT For the relief of Anneliese Barbara Vollrath and Mrs. Margarete Elise Vollrath.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Anneliese Barbara Vollrath and Mrs. Margarete Elise Vollrath shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon the payment of the required visa fee and head tax. Upon the granting of permanent residence to such aliens as provided for in this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct two numbers from the number of displaced persons who shall be granted the status of permanent residence pursuant to section 4 of the Displaced Persons Act, as amended (62 Stat. 1011; 64 Stat. 219: 50 U. S. C. 1953).


*Approved April 3, 1952.*

# Private Law 881

> AN ACT For the relief of Michiko Yamamori Wilder and her minor child.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, Michiko Yamamori Wilder, the wife of Nola Wilder, a United States citizen, and her minor child may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if they are found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

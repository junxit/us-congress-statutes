# Private Law 602

> AN ACT For the relief of Minghan Hammerlind.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the minor child, Minghan Hammerlind, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Miss Elsa Hammerlind, citizen of the United States.


*Approved May 13, 1952.*

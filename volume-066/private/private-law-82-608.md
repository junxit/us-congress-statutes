# Private Law 608

> AN ACT For the relief of Motoko Sakurada.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, Motoko Sakurada, the minor child of Shizue Sakurada, a United States citizen, may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved May 13, 1952.*

# Private Law 929

> AN ACT For the relief of Naoki and Yoshiki Sakamoto.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, Naoki and Yoshiki Sakamoto, the minor children of Takeyo Sakamoto, a United States citizen, may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if they are found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

# Private Law 437

> AN ACT For the relief of Stein S. Ransier.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, Stela S. Ransier, the wife of Otis Ransier, a citizen of the United States, may he admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved February 13, 1952.*

# Private Law 474

> AN ACT For the relief of Albert Walton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 331 of the Nationality Act of 1940, as amended, and if otherwise eligible under all other provisions of the said Act, Albert Walton may file the petition for naturalization prescribed by law.


*Approved February 29, 1952.*

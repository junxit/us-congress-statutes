# Private Law 903

> AN ACT For the relief of Brenda Marie Gray (Akemi).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of that Act, the minor child, Brenda Marie Gray (Akemi), shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Captain and Mrs. Ralph J. Gray, citizens of the United States.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

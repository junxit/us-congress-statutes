# Private Law 634

> AN ACT For the relief of Ruth Obre Dubonnet.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Ruth Obre Dubonnet, if found otherwise admissible to citizenship, may be naturalized under this Act upon compliance with all the requirements of the naturalization laws, except that— (a) no declaration of intention, no certificate of arrival, and no period of residence within the United States or any State shall be required; and (b) the petition for naturalization shall be filed with any court having naturalization jurisdiction prior to the expiration of six months immediately following the date of enactment of this Act,


*Approved May 23, 1952.*

# Private Law 904

> AN ACT For the relief of Deborah Jayne Engelman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 4 (a) and section 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding any provisions excluding from admission to the United States persons of races ineligible to citizenship, Deborah Jayne Engelman, a minor alien child, shall be considered the natural-born alien child of Captain and Mrs. Edward Engelman, citizens of the United States.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

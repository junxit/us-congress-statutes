# Private Law 945

> AN ACT For the relief of Sandra E. Dennett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of the eleventh category of section 3 of the Immigra- tion Act of 1917, as amended, Sandra E. Dennett may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

# Private Law 671

> AN ACT To authorize the President of the United Stales tn present the Distinguished Flying Cross to Colonel Roscoe Turner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is hereby authorized to present in the name of the Congress the Distinguished Flying Cross to Colonel Boscoe Turner in recognition of his meritorious achievements and contribution toward the advancement of the science of aerial flight.


*Approved June 11, 1952.*

# Private Law 515

> AN ACT For the relief of Elvira Zachmann.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the minor child, Elvira Zachmann, shall be held and considered to be the, natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. John P. Poole, citizens of the United States.


*Approved April 3, 1952.*

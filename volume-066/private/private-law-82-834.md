# Private Law 834

> AN ACT For the relief of Thorvald Nin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 404 of the Nationality Act of 1940, Thorvald Nin shall be held and considered to have retained his United States citizenship regardless of any period of residence outside the United States prior to the date of enactment of this Act.


*Approved July 11, 1952.*

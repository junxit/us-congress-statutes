# Private Law 517

> AN ACT For the relief of Anna Maria Krause.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of the eleventh category of section 3 of the Immigration Act of 1917, as amended (8 U. S. C. 136 (e)), Anna Maria Krause may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence providing she is otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved April 3, 1952.*

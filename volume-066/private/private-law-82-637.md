# Private Law 637

> AN ACT For the relief of Robert Wendell Tadlock.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, solely for the purposes of section 4 (a) and section 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of that Act, Robert Wendell Tadlock, a minor child, born in Japan, shall be considered the alien natural-born child of Lieutenant Colonel and Mrs. Marion C. Tadlock, citizens of the United States.


*Approved May 26, 1952.*

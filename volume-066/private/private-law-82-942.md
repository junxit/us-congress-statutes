# Private Law 942

> AN ACT For the relief of Wong See Sun,

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, the provisions of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, shall be held to be applicable to the alien Wong See Sun, the minor unmarried daughter of Y. 8. Wong aka Wong Yung Sau, a citizen of the United States,


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

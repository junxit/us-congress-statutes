# Private Law 976

> AN ACT For the relief of Hitomi Matsushita.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of that Act, the minor child, Hitomi Matsushita, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Paul C. Henry, a citizen of the United States.-


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

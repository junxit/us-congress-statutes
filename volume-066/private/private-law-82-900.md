# Private Law 900

> AN ACT For the relief of Fumiko Ito Stewart.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, Fumiko Ito Stewart, the wife of Theodore M. Stewart, a United States citizen, may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

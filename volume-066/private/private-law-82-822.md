# Private Law 822

> JOINT RESOLUTION Authorizing the President to issue posthumously to the late William S. Cox a commission as third lieutenant, United States Navy, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is authorized to issue posthumously to the late William S. Cox, late a third lieutenant, United States Navy, a commission as a third lieutenant as of the date of his death in 1874.

## § 2.

The Secretary of the Navy is authorized and requested to amend the records of the Navy Department so as to carry the said William S. Cox as a third lieutenant, United States Navy, at the time of his death on October 17, 1874.


*Approved July 10, 1952.*

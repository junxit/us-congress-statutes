# Private Law 885

> AN ACT For the relief of Tom Tateki Iriye.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, Tom Tateki Iriye may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence provided he is otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

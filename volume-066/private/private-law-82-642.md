# Private Law 642

> AN ACT For file relief of Mrs, Shu-Ting Liu Hsin and her daughter, Lucia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Mrs. Shu-Ting Liu Hsia and her daughter, Lucia, shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fees and head taxes. Upon the granting of permanent residence to such aliens as provided for in this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct appropriate numbers from the first available appropriate quota or quotas.


*Approved May 29, 1952.*

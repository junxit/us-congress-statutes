# Private Law 560

> AN ACT For the relief of Ronald Yee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, shall apply to Ronald Yee, alien minor unmarried son of Stanford (Stanley) Yee. a United States citizen.


*Approved April 18, 1952.*

# Private Law 617

> AN ACT For the relief of Carl Himura.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of such Act, the minor child, Carl Himura, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Corporal Dalton C. Boster, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved May 15, 1952.*

# Private Law 758

> AN ACT For the relief of Veronica Merita Kitson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended and notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of the said Act, the minor child, Veronica Merita Ritson, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Sergeant and Mrs. George A. Ritson, citizens of the United States.


*Approved June 28, 1952.*

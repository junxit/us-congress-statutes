# Private Law 478

> AN ACT For the relief of Cindy Eberhardt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, solely for the purpose of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding any provisions excluding from admission to the United States persons of races ineligible to citizenship, Cindy Eberhardt, a minor Japanese child, shall be considered the alien natural-born daughter of Marjorie M. Eberhardt.


*Approved March 5, 1952.*

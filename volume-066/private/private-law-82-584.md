# Private Law 584

> AN ACT For the relief of Nobuko Hiramoto.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act. of 1924, as amended, Nobuko Hiramoto, the minor child of a United States citizen, may be admitted to the United States if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved May 8, 1952.*

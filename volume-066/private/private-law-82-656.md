# Private Law 656

> AN ACT For the relief of Theresa Hatcher.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for tile purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding any provisions of law excluding persons of races ineligible to citizenship from admission to the United States, the minor child, Theresa Hatcher, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Master Sergeant and Mrs. Richard G. Hatcher, citizens of the United States.


*Approved May 29, 1952.*

# Private Law 914

> AN ACT For the relief of Robert Royce Farkas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the minor child, Robert Royce Farkas, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Sergeant and Mrs. William W. Farkas, citizens of the United States.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

# Private Law 431

> AN ACT For the relief of Joseph Emanuel Winger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding any provisions excluding from admission to the United States persons of races ineligible to citizenship, Joseph Emanuel Winger shall be deemed to be the natural-born alien child of Sergeant and Mrs. R. L. Winger, citizens of the United States.


*Approved February 12, 1952.*

# Private Law 817

> AN ACT For the relief of Silas B. Morris.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General is hereby directed to waive the collection of $505.46 from Silas B. Morris, Route 3, Temple, Texas, which is the amount paid him, less taxes, as a substitute rural mail carrier. Mr. Morris is a permanent employee at the Veterans’ Administration Hospital, Temple, Texas, and was advised by officials of the Post Office Department that he could act as a substitute rural mail carrier notwithstanding the fact his salary with the Veterans’ Administration exceeds $2,000.


*Approved July 10, 1952.*

# Private Law 648

> AN ACT For the relief of George Georgacopoulos.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, George Georgacopoulos shall be held and considered Io be the minor child of his father. Reverend Elias Georgacopoulos, a lawfully admitted legal resident of the United States.


*Approved May 29, 1952.*

# Private Law 726

> AN ACT For the relief of Edward Charles Cleverley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration laws, the provisions of the eleventh category of section 3 of the Immigration Act of 1917, as amended (8 U. S. C,, sec. 136 (e)), shall not hereafter apply to Edward Charles Cleverley. Windsor, Ontario, Dominion of Canada, with respect to any conviction or admission of the commission of any crime in his case of which the Department of Justice has knowledge on the date of enactment of this Act.


*Approved June 27, 1952.*

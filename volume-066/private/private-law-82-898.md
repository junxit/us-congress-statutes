# Private Law 898

> AN ACT For the relief of Miki Takano.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding any provisions of law excluding persons of races ineligible to citizenship from admission to the United States, the minor child, Miki Takano, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Master Sergeant Edward J. McGarvey, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

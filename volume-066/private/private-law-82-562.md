# Private Law 562

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Michi Masaoka.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, Mrs. Michi Masaoka, the mother of Susumu Masaoka, a United States citizen, may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved April 18, 1952.*

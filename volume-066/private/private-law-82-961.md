# Private Law 961

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Katharina Luise Trenye.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provision of the eleventh category of section 3 of the Immigration Act of 1917, as amended, Mrs. Katharina Luise Trenye may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

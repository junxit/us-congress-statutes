# Private Law 806

> AN ACT For the relief of Jimmy Lee Davis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the minor child, Jimmy Lee Davis, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Sergeant, and Mrs. Billie Davis, citizens of the United States and notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the said Jimmy Lee Davis may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if he is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved July 9, 1952.*

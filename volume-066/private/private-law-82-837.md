# Private Law 837

> AN ACT To effect the entry into the United States of Yukio Niimura, a minor Japanese national.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of the said Act, the minor child, Yukio Niimura, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Technical Sergeant Donald E. Wilson, citizen of the United States.


*Approved July 11, 1952.*

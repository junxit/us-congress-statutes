# Private Law 894

> AN ACT For the relief of Charles Michell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of the eleventh category of section 3 of the Immigration Act of 1917, as amended, Charles Michell may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence provided he is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

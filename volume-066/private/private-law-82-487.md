# Private Law 487

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Margherita Caroli.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, Mrs. Margherita Caroli may be admitted to the United States as a nonquota immigrant in accordance with section 4 (b) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, if otherwise admissible to the United States.


*Approved March 5, 1952.*

# Private Law 926

> AN ACT For the relief of Fong Bat Woon and Fong Get Nan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, shall apply to the aliens Fong Bat Woon and Fong Get Nan, minor unmarried children of Frank Y. Fong, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

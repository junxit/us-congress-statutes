# Private Law 687

> AN ACT For the relief of Ying Chee Jung.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, the provisions of sections 4(a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, shall be held to be applicable to the alien, Ying Chee Jung, the minor, unmarried child of Phillip Jung, a native-born citizen of the United States.


*Approved June 11, 1952.*

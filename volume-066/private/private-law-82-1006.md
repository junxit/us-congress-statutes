# Private Law 1006

> AN ACT to authorize payment to the Empire District Electric Co. for reasonable costs of protecting its Ozark Beach power plant from the backwater of Bull Shoals Dam.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Chief of Engineers be. and he is hereby authorized to make such payment, not to exceed $700,000 to the Empire District Electric Company of Joplin, Missouri, as in his judgment will cover the reasonable cost of protective works to prevent physical damage to the said company’s Ozark Beach Dam and power plant and appurtenant facilities by backwater from the Bull Shoals Dam.


*Approved July 16, 1952.*

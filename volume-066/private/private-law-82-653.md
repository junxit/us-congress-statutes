# Private Law 653

> AN ACT For the relief of Charlotte Elizabeth Cason.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding any provision of law excluding aliens inadmissible to the United States because of race, the minor child, Charlotte Elizabeth Cason, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Sergeant and Mrs. Wesley Cason, citizens of the United States.


*Approved May 29, 1952.*

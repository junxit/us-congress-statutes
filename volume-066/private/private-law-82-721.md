# Private Law 721

> AN ACT For the relief of Doreen Iris Neal.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Doreen Iris Neal shall be deemed to have been born in Great Britain.


*Approved June 27, 1952.*

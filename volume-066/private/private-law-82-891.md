# Private Law 891

> AN ACT For the relief of Margit Stolz Bohm and Klaus Seigfreid Bohm.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of the eleventh category of section 3 of the Immigration Act of 1917, as amended, Margit Stolz Bohm may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.

## § 2.

That for the purposes of sections 4(a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the minor child, Klaus Seigfreid Bohm, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Sergeant First Class Denzil Kay Bohm, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

# Private Law 599

> AN ACT For the relief of Jan Yee Young.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, the provisions of sections 4(a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, shall be held to be applicable to the alien Jan Yee Young, the minor unmarried child of Tim Oy Young, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved May 13, 1952.*

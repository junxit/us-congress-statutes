# Private Law 918

> AN ACT For the relief of Chiu But Yue.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, Chiu But Yue, the minor child of a United States citizen father, shall be deemed to be a nonquota immigrant if otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

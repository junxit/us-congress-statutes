# Private Law 603

> AN ACT For the relief of Nigel C. 8. Salter-Mathieson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 307 (a) (1) and 331 of the Nationality Act of 1940, as amended. Nigel C. S. Salter-Mathieson may file a petition for naturalization in accordance with the requirements of section 332 of that Act.


*Approved May 13, 1952.*

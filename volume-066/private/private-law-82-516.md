# Private Law 516

> AN ACT For the relief of Fusako Terao Scogin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, Fusako Terao Scogin shall be held to be a nonquota immigrant and may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved April 3, 1952.*

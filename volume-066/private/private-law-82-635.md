# Private Law 635

> AN ACT For the relief of Harumi Kamiaka.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 4 (a) and section 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding any provisions excluding from admission to the United States persons of races ineligible to citizenship, Harumi Kamiaka, a minor half-Japanese child, shall be considered the alien natural-born child of Sergeant and Mrs. James B. C. Wells, citizens of the United States.


*Approved May 23, 1952.*

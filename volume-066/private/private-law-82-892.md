# Private Law 892

> AN ACT For the relief of Jo Anti Fosberg.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the pur- poses of section 4 (a) and section 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding any provisions excluding from admission to the United States persons of races ineligible to citizenship, Jo Ann Fosberg, a minor child, shall be considered the natural-born alien child of Sergeant Raymond O. Fosberg, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved July 15, 1952.*

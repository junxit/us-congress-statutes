# Private Law 686

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Setsnyo Sumida.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration laws, the provisions of section 4 (b) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended (8 U. S. C., sec. 204 (b)), shall be held and considered to be applicable to Mrs. Setsuyo Sumida.


*Approved June 11, 1952.*

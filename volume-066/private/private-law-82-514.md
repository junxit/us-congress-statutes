# Private Law 514

> AN ACT For the relief of William Grant Braden, Junior.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, and notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of that Act, the minor child, William Grant Braden, Junior, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. William Grant Braden, citizens of the United States.


*Approved April 3, 1952.*

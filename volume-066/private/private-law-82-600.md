# Private Law 600

> AN ACT For the relief of Lydia Daisy Jessie Greene.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of the eleventh category of section 3 of the Immigration Act of 1917, as amended, Lydia Daisy Jessie Greene may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved May 13, 1952.*

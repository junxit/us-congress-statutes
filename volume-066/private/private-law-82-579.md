# Private Law 579

> AN ACT For the relief of Kimberly Ann Cibulski, also known as Belle Lee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the minor child, Kimberly Ann Cibulski, also known as Belle Lee, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Fred C. Cibulski, citizens of the United States.


*Approved April 18, 1952.*

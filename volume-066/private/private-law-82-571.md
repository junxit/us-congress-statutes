# Private Law 571

> AN ACT for the relief of Mrs. Aiko Eijima Phillips.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended. Mrs. Aiko Eijima Phillips, the Japanese wife of Roscoe H. Phillips, a United States citizen, may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence, if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved April 18, 1952.*

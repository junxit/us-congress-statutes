# Private Law 663

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Madelaine Viale Moore.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, the provisions of the eleventh category of section 3 of the Immigration Act of 1917, as amended, shall not hereafter apply to Mrs. Madelaine Viale Moore insofar as is involved any conviction or admission of a crime by her of which the Department of Justice and the Department of State have knowledge on the date of enactment hereof.


*Approved June 9, 1952.*

# Private Law 698

> AN ACT For the relief of Barbara Ann Sheppard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the minor child, Barbara Ann Sheppard, shall be held and considered to he the natural-born alien child of Master Sergeant and Mrs. Gordon B. Sheppard, citizens of the United States.


*Approved June 12, 1952.*

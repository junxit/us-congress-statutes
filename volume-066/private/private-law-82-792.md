# Private Law 792

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Alexander Symeonidis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the laws relating to immigration and naturalization, Doctor Alexander Symeonidis shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of August 25, 1947, the date of his first entry into the United States,


*Approved July 8, 1952.*

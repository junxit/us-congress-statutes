# Senate Concurrent Resolution 10

> statue of general william henry harrison beadle

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the statue of General William Henry Harrison Beadle, presented by the State of South Dakota, to be placed in Statuary Hall, is accepted in the name of the United States, and that the thanks of the Congress be tendered said State for the contribution of the statue of one of its most eminent citizens, illustrious for his valor as a soldier and his distinguished service as an educator; and be it further

That a copy of these resolutions, suitably engrossed and duly authenticated, be transmitted to the Governor of South Dakota.


*Passed, July 19, 1937.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 21

> technological trends and national policy, etc.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Report of the Subcommittee on Technology, submitted to the National Resources Committee, entitled “Technological Trends and National Policy, Including the Social Implications of the New Inventions”, be printed as a House document; and that ten thousand additional copies shall be printed, of which two thousand nine hundred copies shall be for the use of the Senate and seven thousand one hundred copies shall be for the use of the House.


*Passed, August 6, 1937.*

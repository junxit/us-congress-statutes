# House Concurrent Resolution 14

> independent offices appropriation bill, 1938

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That, in the enrollment of the bill (H. R. 4064) making appropriations for the Executive Office and sundry independent executive bureaus, boards, commissions, and offices, for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1938, and for other purposes, the Clerk of the House of Representatives is hereby authorized and directed to change the amount of “$150,000,000” in the third paragraph under the heading “Social Security Board” to the sum of “$132,000,000” and to change the total accordingly.


*Passed, May 28, 1937.*

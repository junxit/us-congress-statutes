# House Concurrent Resolution 9

> charles m. perkins

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the action of the Speaker of the House of Representatives and of the President of the Senate in signing the enrolled bill (H. R. 1089) entitled “An Act for the relief of Charles M. Perkins”, be rescinded, and that in the re-enrollment of the said bill the Clerk of the House of Representatives be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to make the following correction, to wit:

Strike out the figures “$14,987.66” and insert in lieu thereof the figures “$14,897.66.”


*Passed, April 9, 1937.*

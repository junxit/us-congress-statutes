# Chapter 283

> AN ACT To reserve certain lands in the State of Utah for the Koosharem Band of Paiute Indians.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the boundary of the Koosharem Indian Reservation in Utah is hereby extended to include the east half of section 8, township 27 south, range 1 west, Salt Lake meridian. Valid rights in the above lands initiated prior to the approval hereof shall not be affected by this Act.


*Approved, May 28, 1937.*

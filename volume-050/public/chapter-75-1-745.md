# Chapter 745

> AN ACT To amend subsection (e) of section 9 of the Trading with the Enemy Act, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (e) of section 9 of the Trading with the Enemy Act, as amended, is amended by inserting after the words “unless such nation in like case extends reciprocal rights to citizens of the United States” a colon and the following: “ Provided, That any arrangement made by a foreign nation for the release of money and other property of American citizens and certified by the Secretary of State to the Attorney General as fair and the most advantageous arrangement obtainable shall be regarded as meeting this requirement. ”


*Approved, August 24, 1937.*

# Chapter 59

> JOINT RESOLUTION To amend Public Law Numbered 780, Seventy-fourth Congress, to authorize the acquisition of lands in the city of Alameda, county of Alameda, State of California, as a site for a naval air station and to authorize the construction and installation of a naval air station thereon, for the purpose of making a correction therein.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1 of Public Law Numbered 780, entitled “An Act to authorize the acquisition of lands in the city of Alameda, county of Alameda, State of California, as a site for a naval air station and to authorize the construction and installation of a naval air station thereon”, approved June 24, 1936, is amended by inserting after the words “free from all”, before the colon and preceding the proviso, the word “encumbrances”.


*Approved, March 29, 1937.*

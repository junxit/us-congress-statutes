# Chapter 278

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making an appropriation to enable the Social Security Board to make payments of grants to States for old-age assistance for the fiscal year 1937.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $18,000,000, to continue available until June 30, 1938, to enable the Social Security Board to make payments of grants to States for old-age assistance, including the same objects specified under this head in the First Deficiency Appropriation Act, fiscal year 1936.


*Approved, May 28, 1937.*

# Chapter 12

> JOINT RESOLUTION To extend for a period of two years the guarantee by the United States of debentures issued by the Federal Housing Administrator.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 204 (b) of the National Housing Act, as amended, is amended by striking out “July 1, 1937” and inserting in lieu thereof “July 1, 1939”.


*Approved, February 19, 1937.*

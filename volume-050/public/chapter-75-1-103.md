# Chapter 103

> AN ACT For the relief of the State of Pennsylvania.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions of section 3646, as amended, of the Revised Statutes of the United States, the chief disbursing officer of the Treasury Department is authorized and directed to issue, without the requirement of an indemnity bond, a duplicate of original check numbered 65451, symbol number 79088, drawn January 25, 1935, in favor of “State Treasurer of Pennsylvania, trust fund”, for $11,315.93 and lost, stolen, or miscarried in the mails.


*Approved, April 15, 1937.*

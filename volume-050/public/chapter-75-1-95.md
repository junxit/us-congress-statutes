# Chapter 95

> AN ACT To authorize any Government department to exchange used parts of certain types of equipment for new or reconditioned parts of the same equipment.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That any Government department is authorized to exchange used parts of mechanical refrigerators, hermetically sealed refrigerating units, temperature control devices, and watchmen’s clocks as payment, in full or in part, for new or reconditioned parts to be used for the same purpose as those proposed to be exchanged.


*Approved, April 15, 1937.*

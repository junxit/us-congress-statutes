# Chapter 222

> AN ACT To provide for the establishment of a Coast Guard station at or near Pass-a-Grille Beach, Florida.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized to establish a Coast Guard station at or near Pass-a-Grille Beach, Florida, at such point as the Commandant of the Coast Guard may recommend.


*Approved, May 18, 1937.*

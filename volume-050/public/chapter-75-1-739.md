# Chapter 739

> AN ACT To extend the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Delaware River between the village of Barry ville, New York, and the village of Shohola, Pennsylvania.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of the highway bridge across the Delaware River between points in the village of Barryville, Sullivan County, New York, and the village of Shohola, Pike County, Pennsylvania, authorized to be built by the Interstate Bridge Commission of the State of New York and the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, by an Act of Congress approved June 19, 1936, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from June 19, 1937.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, August 23, 1937.*

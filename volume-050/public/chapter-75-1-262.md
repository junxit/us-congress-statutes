# Chapter 262

> AN ACT To provide for the establishment of a Coast Guard station on the coast of Georgia at or near Tybee Island.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized to establish a Coast Guard station on the coast of Georgia, at or near Tybee Island, at such point as the Commandant of the Coast Guard may recommend.


*Approved, May 25, 1937.*

# Chapter 19

> JOINT RESOLUTION To amend the Act entitled “An Act to levy an excise tax upon carriers and an income tax upon their employees, and for other purposes”, approved August 29, 1935.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 12 of the Act entitled “An Act to levy an excise tax upon carriers and an income tax upon their employees, and for other purposes”, approved August 29, 1935, is amended by striking out “February 28, 1937” and inserting in lieu thereof “June 30, 1938”.


*Approved, February 27, 1937, 12 noon.*

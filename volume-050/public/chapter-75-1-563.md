# Chapter 563

> AN ACT Relating to the citizenship of certain classes of persons born in the Canal Zone or the Republic of Panama.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That any person born in the Canal Zone on or after February 26, 1904, and whether before or after the effective date of this Act, whose father or mother or both at the time of the birth of such person was or is a citizen of the United States, is declared to be a citizen of the United States.

## § 2.

Any person born in the Republic of Panama on or after February 26, 1904, and whether before or after the effective date of this Act, whose father or mother or both at the time of the birth of such person was or is a citizen of the United States employed by the Government of the United States or by the Panama Railroad Company, is declared to be a citizen of the United States.


*Approved, August 4, 1937.*

# Chapter 154

> AN ACT Authorizing an appropriation for payment to the Government of China for the account of Li Po-tien.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for payment to the Government of China for the account of Li Po-tien, a citizen of China, as compensation for personal injuries sustained as a result of an assault committed by Anthony R. Tofil, private, United States marines, at Tientsin, China, on January 2, 1929, the sum of $300.


*Approved, May 6, 1937.*

# Chapter 303

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making an appropriation for expenses of the Marine Band in attending the United Confederate Veterans Reunion at Jackson, Mississippi, in 1937.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for carrying out the provisions of the Act entitled “An Act to authorize the attendance of the Marine Band at the United Confederate Veterans’ 1937 Reunion at Jackson, Mississippi, June 9, 10, 11, and 12, 1937”, approved May 24, 1937, there is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $5,300.


*Approved, June 8, 1937.*

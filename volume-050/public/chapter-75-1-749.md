# Chapter 749

> JOINT RESOLUTION To authorize an appropriation for the expenses of participation by the United States in the Eighth International Road Congress in 1938.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $5,000, or so much thereof as may be necessary, for the expenses of participation by the United States in the Eighth International Road Congress, to be held in The Netherlands in 1938, including personal services in the District of Columbia and elsewhere, without reference to the Classification Act of 1923, as amended; stenographic reporting, translating, and other services, by contract if deemed necessary, without regard to section 3709 of the Revised Statutes (U. S. C., title 41, sec. 5); rent; traveling expenses; purchase of necessary books, documents, newspapers, and periodicals; official cards; printing and binding; preparation, installation, transportation, and operation of an appropriate exhibit; entertainment; local transportation; the payment of expenses incident to travel by steamer, rail, or motorbus on the official congress inspection trip; and such other expenses as may be authorized by the Secretary of State, including the reimbursement of other appropriations from which payment may have been made for any of the purposes herein specified.


*Approved, August 24, 1937.*

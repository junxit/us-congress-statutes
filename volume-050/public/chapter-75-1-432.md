# Chapter 432

> AN ACT To amend the Interstate Commerce Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 22 (1) of Part I of the Interstate Commerce Act is amended by inserting after the word “guide” the words “or seeing-eye dog or other guide dog specially trained and educated for that purpose”.


*Approved, July 5, 1937.*

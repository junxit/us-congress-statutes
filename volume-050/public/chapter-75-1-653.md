# Chapter 653

> AN ACT To extend the provisions of the forty-hour law for postal employees to watchmen and messengers in the Postal Service.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first section of the Act entitled “An Act to fix the hours of duty of postal employees, and for other purposes”, approved August 14, 1935, is amended by striking out the words “and laborers” and inserting in lieu thereof the following: “laborers, watchmen, and messengers”.


*Approved, August 16, 1937.*

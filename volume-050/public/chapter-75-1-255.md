# Chapter 255

> AN ACT For the relief of the State of Maine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the State of Maine is hereby relieved from accountability for certain property belonging to the United States, of the value of $175, which was loaned by the United States property and disbursing officer of the State of Maine, at the request of the municipal officers of the city of Ellsworth, Maine, for emergency relief work at the fire which destroyed a part of the city of Ellsworth, Maine, on May 8, 1933, such property having been unavoidably lost or destroyed in the course of such work, and listed as property shortages in the report of survey dated June 26, 1933.


*Approved, May 25, 1937.*

# Chapter 631

> AN ACT To amend section 3528 of the Revised Statutes relating to the purchase of metal for minor coins of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, section 3528 of the Revised Statutes, as amended (U. S. C., 1934 edition, title 31, sec. 340), is hereby further amended by striking out the figures “$400,000” and inserting in lieu thereof the figures “$600,000”.


*Approved, August 14, 1937.*

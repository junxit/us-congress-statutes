# Chapter 428

> AN ACT To transfer Crawford County, Iowa, from the southern judicial district of Iowa to the northern judicial district of Iowa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Crawford County, Iowa, of the western division of the southern judicial district of Iowa be, and it is hereby, detached from said judicial district and attached to the western division of the northern judicial district of Iowa.


*Approved, July 5, 1937.*

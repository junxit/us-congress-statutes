# Chapter 73

> JOINT RESOLUTION To make funds available to carry out the provisions of existing law authorizing the purchase and distribution of products of the fishing industry.

*Resolved, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That not to exceed $1,000,000 of the funds available to the Federal Surplus Commodities Corporation may be used by such Corporation for the purpose of diverting surplus fish (including shellfish) and the products thereof from the normal channels of trade and commerce by the acquisition and distribution thereof in accordance with the provisions of the Act entitled “An Act to authorize the purchase and distribution of products of the fishing industry”, approved March 5, 1937.


*Approved, April 12, 1937.*

# Chapter 590

> AN ACT Granting a renewal of patent numbered 60731 relating to the badge of the Girl Scouts, Incorporated.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a certain design patent issued by the United States Patent Office of date of March 28, 1922, being patent numbered 60731, is hereby renewed and extended for a period of fourteen years from and after the date of approval of this Act, with all the rights and privileges pertaining to the same, being generally known as the badge of the Girl Scouts, Incorporated.


*Approved, August 12, 1937.*

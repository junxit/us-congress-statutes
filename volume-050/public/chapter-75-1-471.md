# Chapter 471

> AN ACT To amend Public Act Numbered 467, Seventy-third Congress, entitled “Federal Credit Union Act.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Federal Credit Union Act is amended by inserting at the end thereof the following new section:

> ## § 21.
>
> Upon application by any credit union organized under State law or by any Federal credit union organized in accordance with the terms of this Act, the membership of which is composed exclusively of Federal employees and members of their families, which application shall be addressed to the officer or agency of the United States charged with the allotment of space in the Federal buildings in the community or district in which said credit union or Federal credit union does business, such officer or agency may in his or its discretion allot space to such credit union if space is available without charge for rent or services.”


*Approved, July 9, 1937.*

# Chapter 771

> AN ACT To authorize the appointment of an additional judge for southern district of Ohio.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President hereby is authorized to appoint by and with the advice and consent of the Senate one district judge for the southern district of Ohio in addition to those now authorized by law. Said additional judge shall be entitled to receive the same salary payable in the same manner as is now provided for district judges in said district This additional district judge shall reside within said district and shall be subject to the general provisions of law relating to district judges of the United States.

## § 2.

This Act shall take effect immediately upon the approval thereof by the President of the United States.


*Approved, August 25, 1937.*

# Chapter 833

> AN ACT To authorize an exchange of lands at the New Cumberland General Depot, Pennsylvania.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War is hereby authorized to convey to the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania all right, title, and interest of the United States in and to that portion of the New Cumberland General Depot, New Cumberland, Pennsylvania, consisting of that portion of the said depot lying in the northwest corner thereof and now occupied by the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania under a lease dated June 2, 1936, and to accept in exchange therefor a conveyance from the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania of the fee-simple title to a tract of land adjoining New Cumberland General Depot of approximately the same area, both conveyances to be under such terms and conditions as may be prescribed by the Secretary of War.


*Approved, August 26, 1937.*

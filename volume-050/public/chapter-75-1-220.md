# Chapter 220

> AN ACT To create the office of Counselor of the Department of State.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there shall be in the Department of State a Counselor of the Department of State, to be appointed by the President, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, with a salary of $10,000 per annum.


*Approved, May 18, 1937.*

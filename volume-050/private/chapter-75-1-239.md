# Chapter 239

> AN ACT For the relief of James M. Winter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and, House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limitations of time in sections 15 to 20, both inclusive, of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes”, approved September 7, 1916, as amended, are hereby waived in favor of James M. Winter, a former employee of the Army Transport Service, for disability alleged to have been incurred between September 16, 1918, and June 30, 1920, and the United States Employeesʼ Compensation Commission is hereby authorized to receive and consider his claim under the remaining provisions of said Act: Provided, That claim hereunder shall be made within six months after the approval of this Act: Provided further, That no benefits shall accrue prior to the approval, of this Act.


*Approved, May 22, 1937.*

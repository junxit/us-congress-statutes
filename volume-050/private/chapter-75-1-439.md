# Chapter 439

> AN ACT For the relief of James Philip Coyle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged sailors James Philip Coyle, who enlisted in the United States Navy on June 16, 1898, as a fireman, second-class, serving on the United States ship Franklin (service number XXXX XXXX), shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the naval service of the United States on September 21, 1922: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, July 6, 1937.*

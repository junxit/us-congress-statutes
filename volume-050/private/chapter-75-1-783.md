# Chapter 783

> AN ACT For the relief of F. P. Delahanty.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the accounts of Lieutenant Commander F. P. Delahanty, Supply Corps, United States Navy, with the sum $820.80, representing the amount of payments made by him in good faith to Lieutenant Commander C. K. Osborne, United States Navy, for rental and subsistence allowance of his dependent mother during the period July 1 to December 31, 1923.


*Approved, August 25, 1937.*

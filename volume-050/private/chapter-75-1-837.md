# Chapter 837

> AN ACT For the relief of John A. Flagg.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws or any laws conferring rights, privileges, or benefits upon persons who have served in the military forces of the United States the period spent by John A. Flagg as mess sergeant, Company G, Ninth Regiment Massachusetts Volunteer Infantry, from March 25, 1917, to July 25, 1917, shall be included in computing the time spent by said John A. Flagg in active service in the United States Army: Provided, That no back pay, pension, bounty, benefit, or other emolument shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act, except for the period March 25, 1917, to July 25, 1917, inclusive.


*Approved, August 26, 1937.*

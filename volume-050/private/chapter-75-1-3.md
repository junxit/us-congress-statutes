# Chapter 3

> AN ACT Granting a pension to Grace G. Coolidge.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Administrator of Veterans’ Affairs is authorized and directed to place on the pension rolls the name of Grace G. Coolidge, widow of Calvin Coolidge, late a President of the United States, and to pay her a pension at the rate of $5,000 per annum.


*Approved, January 14, 1937.*

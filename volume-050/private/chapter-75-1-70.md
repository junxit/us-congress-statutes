# Chapter 70

> AN ACT For the relief of the Baker-Whiteley Coal Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to extend to the Baker-Whiteley Coal Company, of Baltimore, Maryland, the provisions and benefits of the Act entitled “An Act to provide relief to Government contractors whose costs of performance were increased as a result of compliance with the Act approved June 16, 1933, and for other purposes”, approved June 16, 1934, with respect to its contract numbered Tpr–40, entered into on August 10, 1933, for the furnishing of coal to the Government fuel yards at Washington, District of Columbia, to the same extent and in the same manner as if said contract had been entered into prior to August 10, 1933.


*Approved, April 6, 1937.*

# Chapter 437

> AN ACT For the relief of Angelo and Auro Cattaneo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions of the Act approved October 16, 1918 (40 Stat. 1012), as amended by the Act approved June 5, 1920 (41 Stat. 1008; U. S. C., title 8, sec. 137), the Secretary of Labor is hereby authorized and directed to cancel the order and warrant of deportation heretofore issued under the provisions of that Act against Angelo and Auro Cattaneo.


*Approved, July 5, 1937.*

# Chapter 641

> AN ACT For the relief of Vincent Chicco.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Vincent Chicco, Charleston, South Carolina, is hereby relieved of all liability as surety on the bond in the sum of $5,000, filed in the United States District Court for the Eastern District of South Carolina, for the appearance of one Morris Grossman for trial on a charge of conspiracy to violate the National Prohibition Act.


*Approved, August 14, 1937.*

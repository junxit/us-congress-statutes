# Chapter 711

> AN ACT For the relief of W. H. Lenneville.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the postal-savings account of W. H. Lenneville, postmaster at Dickinson, North Dakota, in the sum of $504.90, on account of the loss of postal savings funds resulting from the failure of the Dakota National Bank of Dickinson, Dickinson, North Dakota, prior to April 1, 1924.


*Approved, August 19, 1937.*

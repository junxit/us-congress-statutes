# Chapter 450

> AN ACT For the relief of Timothy Joseph McCarthy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Timothy Joseph McCarthy, late of the United States Navy, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged under honorable conditions from the naval service on February 13, 1919: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, July 8, 1937.*

# Chapter 492

> AN ACT Authorizing John Monroe Johnson, Assistant Secretary of Commerce, to accept the decoration tendered him by the Belgian Government.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That John Monroe Johnson, Assistant Secretary of Commerce, is authorized to accept the decoration which has been tendered him by the Belgian Government.


*Approved, July 13, 1937.*

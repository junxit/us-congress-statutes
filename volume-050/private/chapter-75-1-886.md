# Chapter 886

> AN ACT For the relief of F. A. Gross and others.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to allow credit in the accounts of F. A. Gross, Superintendent of the Fort Hall Indian Agency; Donald H. Biery, Superintendent of the Sherman Institute; Lem A. Towers, Superintendent of the Southern Pueblos Indian Agency; and G. F. Allen, Chief Disbursing Officer of the Treasury Department, for expenditures made for travel expense, compensation, and per diem of certain Indian employees of the Indian Service while attending the fourth seminar in education at Yale University during the fiscal year 1935, under authorities issued by the Commissioner of Indian Affairs.


*Approved, August 28, 1937.*

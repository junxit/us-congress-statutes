# Chapter 380

> AN ACT To amend Private Act Numbered 210, approved August 13, 1935, by substituting as payee therein the Clark Dredging Company in lieu of the Bowers Southern Dredging Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Private Act Numbered 210, approved August 13, 1935, for the relief of the Bowers Southern Dredging Company, be, and the same is hereby, amended by substituting as payee therein the Clark Dredging Company as transferee or assignee of said Bowers Southern Dredging Company.


*Approved, June 24, 1937.*

# Chapter 112

> AN ACT For the relief of G. A. Trotter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to allow credit in the accounts of G. A. Trotter, former superintendent and special disbursing agent of the Zuni Indian Agency, for payments aggregating $102.40 made to Will Halloran as mileage for the use of his personally owned automobile while performing his official duties as road supervisor in the Indian Service.


*Approved, April 17, 1937.*

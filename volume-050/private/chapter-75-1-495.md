# Chapter 495

> AN ACT Validating homestead entry Billings 029004 of Lillian J. Glinn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the stock-raising homestead entry, Billings 029004, made by Lillian J. Glinn on June 28, 1927, as amended, for all of section 32, township 7 south, range 54 east, Montana principal meridian, is hereby validated, and the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized to accept the final proof submitted by the entrywoman, now Lillian J. Castleberry, in support of said homestead entry on December 20, 1934, and to issue patent for the entry in regular course.


*Approved, July 13, 1937.*

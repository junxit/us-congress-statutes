# Chapter 208

> AN ACT For the relief of Captain Benjamin Dutton, Junior, Captain C. H. J. Keppler, Commander Leo H. Thebaud, and Lieutenant Commander Gordon S. Bower, Supply Corps, United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to credit the accounts of Captain Benjamin Dutton, Junior, United of States Navy, in the amount of $401.43; Captain C. H. J. Keppler, United States Navy, in the amount of $1,078.76; Commander Leo H. Thebaud, United States Navy, in the amount of $540.08; and Lieutenant Commander Gordon S. Bower, Supply Corps, United States Navy, in the amount of $150.74, which sums represent payments of exchange relief made by these four officers to Captain Joseph J. A. McMullin, Medical Corps, United States Navy, in the amount of $1,119.61; to Lieutenant Commander Lloyd E. Clifford, United States Navy, in the amount of $540.08; and to Lieutenant (Junior Grade) F. P. Kreuz, Medical Corps., United States Navy, in the amount of $511.32, and disallowed in their accounts by the Comptroller General of the United States.


*Approved, May 15, 1937.*

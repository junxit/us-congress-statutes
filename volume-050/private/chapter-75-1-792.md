# Chapter 792

> AN ACT For the relief of Leah P. Rice.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to allow credit in the accounts of Leah P. Rice, former postmaster at Harrison, Nebraska, in the amount of $172.55, representing funds lost in the failure of the First National Bank of Harrison, Nebraska, February 5, 1924.


*Approved, August 25, 1937.*

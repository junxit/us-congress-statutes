# Chapter 365

> AN ACT Awarding a Navy Cross to John W. Thomason and Robert Slover.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That John W. Thomason, Major, United States Marine Corps, and Robert Slover, gunnery sergeant, United States Marine Corps, be awarded, and each is hereby authorized to receive, a Navy Cross, to be prepared under the direction of the Secretary of the Navy, for extraordinary heroism in the battle of Soissons on July 18, 1918, in destroying a machine-gun nest and capturing two machine guns.


*Approved, June 18, 1937.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 20

> holiday recess.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Saturday, December 21, 1929, they stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian Monday, January 6, 1930.


*Passed, December 12, 1929.*

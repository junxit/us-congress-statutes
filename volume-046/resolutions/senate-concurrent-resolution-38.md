# Senate Concurrent Resolution 38

> investigation of communist propaganda.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed twelve thousand additional copies of House Report Numbered 2290, Seventy-first Congress, being a report of the special committee to investigate communist activities in the United States, of which not to exceed four thousand copies shall be printed for the use of the House, five thousand copies for the document room of the House, and three thousand copies for the document room of the Senate.


*Passed, February 14, 1931.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 35

> north dakota judicial district.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the action of the Speaker of the House of Representatives and of the Vice President in signing the bill (H. R. 185) entitled “An Act to amend section 180, title 28, United States Code, as amended,” be rescinded, and that in the reenrollment of said bill the word “Richmond” be stricken out and the word “Richland” be inserted in lieu thereof.


*Passed, May 27, 1930.*

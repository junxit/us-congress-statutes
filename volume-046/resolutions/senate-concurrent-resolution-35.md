# Senate Concurrent Resolution 35

> holiday recess.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That when the two Houses of Congress adjourn on the legislative day of Saturday, December 20, 1930, they stand adjourned until twelve o’clock meridian Monday, January 5, 1931.


*Passed, December 20, 1930.*

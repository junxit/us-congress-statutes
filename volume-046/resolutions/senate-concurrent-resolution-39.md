# Senate Concurrent Resolution 39

> “washington, the national capital.”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there shall be printed with illustrations and bound, as may be directed by the Joint Committee on Printing, four thousand copies of the manuscript entitled “Washington, the National Capital,” prepared by H. P. Caemmerer, of which one thousand copies shall be for the use of the Senate and two thousand seven hundred copies for the use of the House of Representatives, and the remaining copies shall be available for distribution as the Joint Committee on Printing may direct.


*Passed, March 3, 1931.*

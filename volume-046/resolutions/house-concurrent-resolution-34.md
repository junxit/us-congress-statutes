# House Concurrent Resolution 34

> federal probation officers.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the House of Representatives the bill (H. R. 3975) entitled “An Act to amend sections 726 and 727 of title 18, United States Code, with reference to Federal probation officers, and to add a new section thereto.”


*Passed, May 26, 1930.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 44

> adjournment.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Thursday, the 3d day of July, 1930, and that when they adjourn on said day they stand adjourned sine die.


*Passed, July 3, 1930.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 47

> mesa verde national park, colo.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President is requested to return to the House of Representatives the bill (H. R. 15876) entitled “An Act to provide for the addition of certain lands to the Mesa Verde National Park, Colorado, and for other purposes,” for the purpose of permitting the correction of an error in the enrolled bill.


*Passed, February 16, 1931.*

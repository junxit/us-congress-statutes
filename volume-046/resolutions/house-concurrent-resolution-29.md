# House Concurrent Resolution 29

> battles of king’s mountain and cowpens, s. c.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That three thousand additional copies of House Document Numbered 328, Seventieth Congress, first session, entitled “Historical Statements Concerning the Battle of King’s Mountain and the Battle of the Cowpens in South Carolina,” by Lieutenant Colonel H. L. Landers, be printed with illustrations and bound for the use of the Committee on Printing of the House of Representatives.


*Passed, April 10, 1930.*

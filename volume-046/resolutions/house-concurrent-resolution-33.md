# House Concurrent Resolution 33

> north dakota judicial district.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the House of Representatives the bill (H. R. 185) entitled “An Act to amend section 180, title 28, United States Code, as amended.”


*Passed, May 26, 1930.*

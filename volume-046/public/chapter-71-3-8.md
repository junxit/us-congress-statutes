# Chapter 8

> An Act To change the name of Iowa Circle in the city of Washington to Logan Circle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of the circle now known as Iowa Circle, in the city of Washington, is hereby changed to Logan Circle in recognition of the services rendered the United States by General John A. Logan during the Civil War and in civil life, and the surveyor of the District of Columbia is hereby directed to enter such change on the records of his office.


*Approved, December 11, 1930.*

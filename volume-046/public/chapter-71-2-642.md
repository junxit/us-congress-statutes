# Chapter 642

> An Act Authorizing the repayment of rents and royalties in excess of requirements made under leases executed in accordance with the General Leasing Act of February 25, 1020.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of the Act of Congress approved December 11, 1919 (41 Stat. L. 366), entitled “An Act to amend an Act approved March 26, 1908, entitled ‘An Act to provide for the repayment of certain commissions, excess payments, and purchase moneys paid under the public land laws,’” is hereby made applicable to all payments in excess of lawful requirements made under the Act of Congress approved February 25, 1920 (41 Stat. L. 437), and under any statute relating to the sale, entry, lease, or other disposition of the public lands.


*Approved, June 27, 1930.*

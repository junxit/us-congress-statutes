# Chapter 362

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the immediate appropriation of certain amounts authorized to be appropriated by the Settlement of War Claims Act of 1928.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sums authorized by subsection (p) of section 3 of the Settlement of War Claims Act of 1928 to be appropriated after the date on which the awards of the war claims arbiter under section 3 of such Act are certified to the Secretary of the Treasury, are hereby authorized to be appropriated at any time, but shall not be available until after such date.


*Approved, May 29, 1930.*

# Chapter 4

> Joint Resolution Amending Public Resolution Numbered 108, Seventieth Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the joint resolution establishing a Joint Commission on Insular Reorganization, being Public Resolution Numbered 108, Seventieth Congress, approved March 4, 1929, is hereby amended by striking out “December 16, 1929,” and inserting in lieu thereof “January 16, 1930.”


*Approved, December 16, 1929.*

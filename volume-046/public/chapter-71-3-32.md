# Chapter 32

> An Act To extend the time for construction of a free highway bridge across the Red River at or near Moncla, Louisiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge authorized by Act of Congress approved March 10, 1928, to be built by the Louisiana Highway Commission of the State of Louisiana across the Red River, at or near Moncla, Avoyelles Parish, Louisiana, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from March 10, 1931.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, January 15, 1931.*

# Chapter 332

> An Act To authorize the Commissioners of the District of Columbia to transfer to the trustees of Howard University title to certain property in the District of Columbia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioners of the District of Columbia be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to transfer to the trustees of Howard University title to lots 821 and 822, in square 3064, with all improvements thereon, upon payment to the District of Columbia of the sum of $15,000; and the said commissioners are further authorized and directed to transfer to the trustees of Howard University title to lot 800 in square 3068, including all improvements thereon, upon payment to the District of Columbia for credit to the water revenues of the sum of $22,500.


*Approved, February 28, 1931.*

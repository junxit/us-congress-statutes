# Chapter 83

> An Act To extend the time for the construction of a bridge across the Rio Grande at or near Rio Grande City, Texas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge authorized by Act of Congress approved February 15, 1929, to be built by the Rio Grande City-Camargo Bridge Company, across the Rio Grande at or near Rio Grande City, Texas, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from February 15, 1931.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, January 31, 1931.*

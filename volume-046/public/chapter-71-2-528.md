# Chapter 528

> An Act To extend the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Des Moines River at or near Croton, Iowa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of the bridge across the Des Moines River at or near Croton, Iowa, authorized to be built by Henry Horsey, Winfield Scott, A. L. Ballegoin, and Frank Schee, their heirs, legal representatives, and assigns, by the Act of Congress approved May 22, 1928, and heretofore extended by Act of Congress approved March 2, 1929, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from May 22, 1930.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, June 18, 1930.*

# Chapter 839

> An Act To authorize an appropriation for construction at Carlisle Barracks, Pennsylvania.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated not to exceed $37,000 for the construction of a stable at Carlisle Barracks, Pennsylvania.


*Approved, July 3, 1930.*

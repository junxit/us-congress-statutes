# Chapter 118

> An Act Granting the consent of Congress to the State of Massachusetts to construct, maintain, and operate a free highway bridge across the Merrimack River at or near Tyngsboro, Massachusetts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the consent of Congress is hereby granted to the State of Massachusetts to construct, maintain, and operate a free highway bridge and approaches thereto across the Merrimack River, at a point suitable to the interests of navigation, at or near Tyngsboro, Massachusetts, in accordance with the provisions of an Act entitled “An Act to regulate the construction of bridges over navigable waters,” approved March 23, 1906.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, April 8, 1930.*

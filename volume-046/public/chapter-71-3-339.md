# Chapter 339

> An Act To extend the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Missouri River at or near Rulo, Nebraska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Missouri River at or near Rulo, Nebraska, authorized to be built by John C. Mullen, T. L. Davies, John II. Hutchings, and Virgil Falloon, all of Falls City, Nebraska, their heirs, legal representatives, and assigns, by Act of Congress approved March 29, 1928, heretofore extended by Act of Congress approved June 10, 1930, are hereby further extended one and three years, respectively, from March 29, 1931.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, February 28, 1931.*

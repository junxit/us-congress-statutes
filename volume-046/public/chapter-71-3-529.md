# Chapter 529

> Joint Resolution To provide an additional appropriation for hospital, domiciliary, and out-patient dispensary facilities for the Veterans’ Administration.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of $5,000,000 is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for carrying out the provisions of the Act entitled “An Act to authorize an appropriation to provide additional hospital, domiciliary, and out-patient dispensary facilities for persons entitled to hospitalization under the World War Veterans Act, 1924, as amended, and for other purposes,” approved March 4, 1931.


*Approved, March 4, 1931.*

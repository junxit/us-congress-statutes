# Chapter 30

> An Act To extend the time for constructing a bridge across the Atchafalaya River at or near Morgan City, Louisiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge authorized by Act of Congress approved March 10, 1928, to be built by the Louisiana Highway Commission across the Atchafalaya River, at or near Morgan City, Saint Mary Parish, Louisiana, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from the date of approval hereof.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, January 15, 1931.*

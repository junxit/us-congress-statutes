# Chapter 493

> An Act To authorize an appropriation of tribal funds to purchase certain privately owned lands within the Fort Apache Indian Reservation, Arizona.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated not to exceed the sum of $1,300 of funds on deposit to the credit of the Indians of the Fort Apache Reservation, Arizona, for the purchase of land and appurtenances thereto, exclusive of mineral rights, located within the exterior boundaries of that reservation, and belonging to the Aztec Land and Cattle Company, title thereto to be taken in the name of the United States in trust for said Indians.


*Approved, March 4, 1931.*

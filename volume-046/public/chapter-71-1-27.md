# Chapter 27

> An Act To amend the Act entitled “An Act making appropriations for the Department of the Interior for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1930, and for other purposes.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the paragraph of the Act entitled “An Act making appropriations for the Department of the Interior for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1930, and for other purposes” (Public, Numbered 1033, Seventieth Congress), referring to the Milk River project, Montana, be amended to read as follows:

> “Milk River project, Montana: For operation and maintenance, Chinook, Malta, and Glasgow divisions, $17,000; continuation of construction, $17,000; in all, $34,000.”


*Approved, June 17, 1929.*

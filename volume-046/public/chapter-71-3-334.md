# Chapter 334

> An Act Authorizing the construction of a bridge across the Mahoning River at Edinburg, Lawrence County, Pennsylvania.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the consent of Congress is hereby granted to the State Highway Department of Pennsylvania to construct, maintain, and operate a free highway bridge and approaches thereto on Pennsylvania State Highway Route Numbered 444, across the Mahoning River at Edinburg, Lawrence County, Pennsylvania, at a point suitable to the interests of navigation, in accordance with the provisions of an Act entitled “An Act to regulate the construction of bridges over navigable water,” approved March 23, 1906.

## § 2.

That the right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, February 28, 1931.*

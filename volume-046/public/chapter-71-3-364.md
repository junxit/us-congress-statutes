# Chapter 364

> An Act To authorize the Leo N. Levi Memorial Hospital Association to mortgage its property in Hot Springs National Park.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Leo N. Levi Memorial Hospital Association is hereby authorized, with the approval of the Secretary of the Interior, to execute mortgages upon its rights in and properties upon lots numbered 1, 2, 3, and 4 in block numbered 114 in the city of Hot Springs, Arkansas, and such mortgages, together with the approval of said Secretary of the Interior, may be filed for record in the office of the Secretary of the Interior, and when so recorded shall have all the effect of a public record.


*Approved, March 2, 1931.*

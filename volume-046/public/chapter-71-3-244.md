# Chapter 244

> An Act To provide for the appointment of an additional district judge for the southern district of Illinois.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to appoint, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, an additional judge of the District Court of the United States for the Southern District of Illinois.


*Approved, February 20, 1931.*

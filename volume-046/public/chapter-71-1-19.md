# Chapter 19

> Joint Resolution To make available funds for carrying into effect the public resolution of February 20, 1929, as amended, concerning the cessions of certain islands of the Samoan group to the United States.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for carrying into effect the provisions of Public Resolution Numbered 89, Seventieth Congress, entitled “Joint resolution to provide for accepting, ratifying, and confirming the cessions of certain islands of the Samoan group to the United States, and for other purposes,” approved February 20, 1929, as amended, there is hereby made available until expended the sum of $25,000, from the unobligated balance of the continuing appropriation “Naval Emergency Fund,” Navy Department, to be expended in the discretion of the President by such agency or agencies as he may direct.


*Approved, June 13, 1929.*

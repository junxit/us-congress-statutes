# Chapter 447

> An Act To legalize a quay in Milburn Creek at Baldwin Harbor, New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the quay owned by Daniel S. Quigley, located in Milburn Creek at Baldwin Harbor, Nassau County, New York, be, and the same is hereby, legalized to the same extent and with like effect as to all existing or future laws and regulations of the United States as if the permit required by the existing laws of the United States in such cases made and provided had been regularly obtained prior to the erection of said quay: Provided, That any changes in said quay which the Secretary of War may deem necessary and order in the interest of navigation shall be promptly made by the owner thereof.

## § 2.

That the right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, March 3, 1931.*

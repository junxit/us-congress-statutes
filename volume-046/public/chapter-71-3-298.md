# Chapter 298

> An Act To legalize bridgea across the Staunton River at Brookneal, Route Numbered 18, Campbell County, and at Clover, Halifax County, Route Numbered 12, State of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the highway bridges built by the authorities of the State of Virginia across the Staunton River at Brookneal, on Route Numbered 18, Campbell County, and at Clover, Halifax County, on Route Numbered 12, are hereby legalized and the consent of Congress is hereby given to their maintenance by the said State for the use of the general public: Provided, That any changes in said bridges which the Secretary of War may deem necessary and order in the interest of navigation shall be promptly made by the said State.

## § 2.

That the right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, February 25, 1931.*

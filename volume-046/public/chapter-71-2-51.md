# Chapter 51

> An Act To extend the time for constructing a bridge across the Santa Rosa Sound, Florida.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of the bridge across the Santa Rosa Sound at or near Deer Point, Santa Rosa County, Florida, authorized to be built by the counties of Escambia and Santa Rosa, by the Act of Congress approved May 26, 1928, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from May 26, 1930.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, February 19, 1930.*

# Chapter 90

> Joint Resolution Amending section 1 of the Act entitled “An Act authorizing the construction, repair, and preservation of certain public works on rivers and harbors, and for other purposes,” approved July 3, 1930, relating to the Monongahela River, Pennsylvania.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provision relating to the Monongahela River, Pennsylvania, in section 1 of the Act entitled “An Act authorizing the construction, repair, and preservation of certain public works on rivers and harbors, and for other purposes,” approved July 3, 1930, is hereby amended to read as follows:

> “Monongahela River, Pennsylvania, in accordance with the report submitted in Rivers and Harbors Committee Document Numbered 22, Seventieth Congress, second session: Provided, That the Chief of Engineers may, in his discretion, locate the new locks and dam above the existing structure and on such site as he may deem most desirable.”


*Approved, January 31, 1931.*

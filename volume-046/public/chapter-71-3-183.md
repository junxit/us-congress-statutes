# Chapter 183

> An Act For the relief of the State of Maine and the city of Portsmouth, New Hampshire.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $34,773.11, the sum being the unexpended balance of an appropriation heretofore made by Congress to aid in the construction of a bridge between Kittery, Maine, and Portsmouth, New Hampshire, one-half to be paid to the State of Maine and the other half to the city of Portsmouth, New Hampshire.


*Approved, February 14, 1931.*

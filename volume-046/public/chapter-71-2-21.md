# Chapter 21

> Joint Resolution Making an appropriation for participation by the United States in the celebration of the one thousandth anniversary of the Althing, the National Parliament of Iceland.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of $55,000 is appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to continue available until June 30, 1931, for expenses of participation by the United States in the celebration of the one thousandth anniversary of the Althing, the National Parliament of Iceland, as authorized by Public Resolution Numbered 18, Seventy-first Congress, approved June 21, 1929, including the procurement of a suitable statue or other memorial of Leif Ericsson as a gift of the American people to the people of Iceland, transportation, subsistence or per diem in lieu thereof (notwithstanding the provisions of any other Act), contract services without regard to section 3709 of the Revised Statutes (United States Code, title 41, section 5), sculptors’ fees, and such other expenses as the President shall deem appropriate.


*Approved, January 20, 1930.*

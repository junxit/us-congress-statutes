# Chapter 26

> An Act To extend the time for completing the construction of a bridge across the Mississippi River at or near the city of Prairie du Chien, Wisconsin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time for completing the construction of a bridge across the Mississippi River, at or near the city of Prairie du Chien, Wisconsin, authorized to be built by J. H. Peacock, F. G. Bell, S. V. Taylor, E. C. Amann, and C. E. Ferris, their heirs, legal representatives, and assigns, by an Act of Congress approved March 7, 1928, is hereby extended one year from March 7, 1931.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, January 12, 1931.*

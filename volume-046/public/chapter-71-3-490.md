# Chapter 490

> An Act To authorize the collection of annual statistics relating to crime and to the defective, dependent, and delinquent classes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Director of the Census be, and hereby is, authorized to compile and publish annually statistics relating to crime and to the defective, dependent, and delinquent classes.


*Approved, March 4, 1931.*

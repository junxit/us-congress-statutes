# Chapter 644

> An Act To amend an Act entitled “An Act to promote the safety of employees and travelers upon railroads by compelling common carriers engaged in interstate commerce to equip their locomotives with safe and suitable boilers and appurtenances thereto,” approved February 17, 1911, as amended March 4, 1915, June 26, 1918, and June 7, 1924.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3 of the Act entitled “An Act to promote the safety of employees and travelers upon railroads by compelling common carriers engaged in interstate commerce to equip their locomotives with safe and suitable boilers and appurtenances thereto,” approved February 17, 1911, as amended March 4, 1915, June 26, 1918, and June 7, 1924, is hereby amended so as to provide that the salary of the chief inspector shall be $7,500 per year, and the salary of each assistant chief inspector shall be $6,000 per year.

## § 2.

That section 4 of said Act, as amended, is hereby amended so as to provide that the salary of each district inspector appointed under said Act shall be $4,000 per year.


*Approved, June 27, 1930.*

# Chapter 560

> An Act To transfer certain lands to the Ouachita National Forest, Arkansas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the southwest quarter southeast quarter of section 24, township 4 north, range 28 west, fifth principal meridian, be, and the same is hereby, transferred to and made a part of the Ouachita National Forest, in the State of Arkansas, and shall hereafter be administered subject to the laws and regulations relating to the national forest.


*Approved, June 21, 1930.*

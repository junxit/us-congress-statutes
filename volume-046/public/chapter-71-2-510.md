# Chapter 510

> An Act To authorize the Secretary of War to donate a bronze cannon to the city of Martins Ferry, Ohio.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War is authorized and directed to donate, without expense to the United States, to the city of Martins Ferry, Ohio, a bronze fieldpiece, twelve pounder, cast muzzle loading, diameter of bore four and five-eighths inches, now located at Watervliet Arsenal, Watervliet, New York.


*Approved, June 17, 1930.*

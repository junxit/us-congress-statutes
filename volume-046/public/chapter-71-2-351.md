# Chapter 351

> An Act To extend the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Illinois River, at or near Peoria, Illinois.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of the bridge across the Illinois River, at or near Peoria, Illinois, authorized to be built by the city of Peoria, Peoria County, Illinois, by the Act of Congress approved March 29, 1928, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from March 29, 1930.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, May 29, 1930.*

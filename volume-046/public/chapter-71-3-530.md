# Chapter 530

> Joint Resolution Making an appropriation for expenses of the United States Marine Band in attending the national convention of the United Spanish-American War Veterans in New Orleans, Louisiana, in September, 1931.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $8,171 for the fiscal year 1932, for expenses of the United States Marine Band in attending the national convention of the United Spanish-American War Veterans, to be held at New Orleans, Louisiana, on September 6 to 10, inclusive, 1931, as authorized by the Act approved March 3, 1931.


*Approved, March 4, 1931.*

# Chapter 454

> An Act To increase the salaries of certain postmasters of the first class.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second paragraph under the heading “Reclassification of postal salaries,” in section 1 of title 1 of the Act of February 28, 1925, reclassifying the salaries of postmasters, be, and the same is hereby, amended to read as follows:

> “First class, $40,000, but less than $50,000, $3,200; $50,000, but less than $60,000, $3,300; $60,000 but less than $75,000, $3,400; $75,000 but less than $90,000, $3,500 ; $90,000 but less than $120,000, $3,600; $120,000 but less than $150,000, $3,700; $150,000 but less than $200,000, $3,800; $200,000 but less than $250,000, $3,900; $250,000 but less than $300,000, $4,000; $300,000 but less than $400,000, $4,200; $400,000 but less than $500,000, $4,500 ; $500,000 but less than $600,-000, $5,000; $600,000 but less than $1,500,000, $6,000; $1,500,000 but less than $3,000,000, $7,000; $3,000,000 but less than $7,000,000, $8,000; $7,000,000 but less than $10,000,000, $9,000; $10,000,000 but less than $20,000,000, $10,000; $20,000,000 but less than $40,000,000, $11,000; $40,000,000 and upwards, $12,000.”


*Approved, June 11, 1930.*

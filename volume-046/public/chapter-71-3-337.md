# Chapter 337

> An Act To extend the time for completing the construction of a bridge across the Mississippi River at or near the city of Lansing, Iowa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time for completing the construction of a bridge across the Mississippi River at or near the city of Lansing, Iowa, authorized to be built by the Interstate Bridge Company, its successors and assigns, by an Act of Congress approved March 10, 1928, is hereby extended three years from March 10, 1931.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, February 28, 1931.*

# Chapter 210

> An Act To amend an Act entitled “An Act to readjust the commissioned personnel of the Coast Guard, and for other purposes,” approved March 2, 1929.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second proviso of section 1 of the Act entitled “An Act to readjust the commissioned personnel of the Coast Guard, and for other purposes,” approved March 2, 1929, is hereby amended by striking out the words “total service” in said proviso and substituting therefor the words “total commissioned service.”


*Approved, April 23, 1930.*

# Chapter 10

> Joint Resolution Extending until June 30, 1930, the availability of the appropriation for enlarging and relocating the Botanic Garden.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the appropriation “Enlarging and relocating Botanic Garden, 1928 and 1929,” contained in the Deficiency Appropriation Act approved December 22, 1927, is hereby continued and made available for the same purposes during the fiscal year ending June 30, 1930.


*Approved, June 5, 1929.*

# Chapter 437

> An Act For the appointment of an additional circuit judge for the fifth judicial circuit.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized to appoint, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, an additional circuit judge for the fifth judicial circuit.


*Approved, June 10, 1930.*

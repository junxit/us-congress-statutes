# Chapter 855

> An Act To increase the salary of the Commissioner of Customs.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of Customs shall receive a salary at the rate of $10,000 per annum, effective on and after the date of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved, July 3, 1930.*

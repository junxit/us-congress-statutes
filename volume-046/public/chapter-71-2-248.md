# Chapter 248

> An Act To authorize an appropriation for construction at Fort McKinley, Portland, Maine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated not to exceed $50,000 for the construction of barracks at Fort McKinley, Portland, Maine.


*Approved, May 13, 1930.*

# Chapter 39

> An Act To extend, the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the water between the mainland at or near Cedar Point and Dauphin Island, Alabama.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the water between the mainland at or near Cedar Point and Dauphin Island, Alabama, authorized to be built by Dauphin Island Railway and Harbor Company by an Act of Congress approved February 25, 1927, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from February 25, 1930.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, February 7, 1930.*

# Chapter 103

> Joint Resolution Making an appropriation to complete the restoration of the frigate Constitution.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $300,000, to remain available until June 30, 1931, for completing the repair, equipment, and restoration of the frigate Constitution, as authorized by the Act approved March 4, 1925 (Forty-third Statutes at Large, page 1278).


*Approved, April 3, 1930.*

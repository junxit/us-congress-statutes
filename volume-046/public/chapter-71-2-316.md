# Chapter 316

> An Act To authorize the Secretary of the Navy to dispose of material no longer needed by the Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized in his discretion to dispose of, without charge, except for transportation and delivery, to properly accredited schools, colleges, and universities, for use in courses of vocational training and instruction, such machinery, mechanical equipment, and tools as may be obsolete or no longer needed by the Navy.


*Approved, May 23, 1930.*

# Chapter 852

> An Act To make permanent the additional office of district judge created for the eastern district of Illinois by the Act of September 14, 1922.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the additional office of district judge for the eastern district of Illinois, created by the Act entitled “An Act for the appointment of an additional circuit judge for the fourth judicial circuit, for the appointment of additional district judges for certain districts, providing for an annual conference of certain judges, and for other purposes,” approved September 14, 1922, shall not be subject to the provisions of the third paragraph of section 1 of such Act, prohibiting the filling of vacancies.


*Approved, July 3, 1930.*

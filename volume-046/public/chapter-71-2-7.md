# Chapter 7

> An Act Continuing the powers and authority of the Federal Radio Commission under the Radio Act of 1927, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all the powers and authority vested in the Federal Radio Commission by the Radio Act of 1927, as amended, shall continue to be vested in and exercised by the commission until otherwise provided for by law; and wherever any reference is made in such Act to the period of one year after the first meeting of the commission, such period of one year is hereby extended until such time as is otherwise provided for by law.

## § 2.

The period during which the members of the commission shall receive compensation at the rate of $10,000 per annum is hereby extended until such time as is otherwise provided for by law.

## § 3.

The commission is authorized to appoint a chief engineer who shall receive a salary of $10,000 per annum, and not to exceed two assistants to such chief engineer at salaries not to exceed $7,500 each per annum. It may appoint such other technical assistants as it may from time to time find necessary for the proper performance of its duties and as from time to time may be appropriated for by Congress.


*Approved, December 18, 1929.*

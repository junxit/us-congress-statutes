# Chapter 82

> An Act To extend the time for the construction of the bridge across the Rio Grande at or near San Benito, Texas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge authorized by Act of Congress approved February 15, 1929, to be built by the Rio Grande del Norte Investment Company, across the Rio Grande at or near San Benito, Texas, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from February 15, 1931.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, January 31, 1931.*

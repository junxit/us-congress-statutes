# Chapter 110

> An Act To change the limit of cost for the construction of the Coast Guard Academy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limit of cost fixed in the Act approved February 16, 1929, entitled “An Act to provide for the acquisition of a site and the construction thereon and equipment of buildings and appurtenances for the Coast Guard Academy,” is hereby increased from $1,750,000 to $2,500,000.


*Approved, April 7, 1930.*

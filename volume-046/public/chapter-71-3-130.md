# Chapter 130

> An Act Authorizing the Secretary of the Navy, in his discretion, to deliver to the custody of the Rosenberg Library, in the city of Galveston, Texas, the silver service presented to the United States for the cruiser Galveston.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy is authorized, in his discretion, to deliver to the custody of the Rosenberg Library in the city of Galveston, Texas, for preservation and exhibition, the silver service which was presented to the United States for the cruiser Galveston by the citizens of Galveston, Texas: Provided, That no expense shall be incurred by the United States for the delivery of such silver service.


*Approved, February 13, 1931.*

# Chapter 492

> An Act Authorizing the Secretary of the Navy, in his discretion, to deliver to the custody of the Princeton Club of Philadelphia, the bowl and ladle formerly in use on the United States ship Princeton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy is authorized, in his discretion, to deliver to the custody of the Princeton Club of Philadelphia, for preservation and exhibition, the bowl and ladle formerly in use on the United States ship Princeton: Provided, That no expense shall be incurred by the United States for the delivery of such bowl and ladle.


*Approved, March 4, 1931.*

# Chapter 784

> An Act To further amend section 37 of the National Defense Act of June 4, 1920, as amended by section 2 of the Act of September 22, 1922, so as to more clearly define the status of reserve officers not on active duty or on active duty for training only.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 37 of the National Defense Act of June 4, 1920, as amended by section 2 of the Act of September 22, 1922 (Forty-second Statutes, page 1033; sections 351, 352, 353, 356, and 360, title 10, United States Code), be, and the same is hereby, amended by adding thereto another sentence as follows: “Reserve officers while not on active duty shall not, by reason solely of their appointments, oaths, commissions, or status as reserve officers, or any duties or functions performed or pay or allowances received as reserve officers, be held or deemed to be officers or employees of the United States, or persons holding any office of trust or profit or discharging any official function under or in connection with any department of the Government of the United States. ”


*Approved, July 1, 1930.*

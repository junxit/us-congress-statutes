# Chapter 537

> An Act To provide for the appointment of two additional justices of the Supreme Court of the District of Columbia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is authorized to appoint, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, two additional justices of the Supreme Court of the District of Columbia, who shall have the same tenure of office, pay, and emoluments, powers, and duties as the present justices of that court.


*Approved, June 19, 1930.*

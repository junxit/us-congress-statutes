# Chapter 135

> An Act To extend the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Ohio River at Cairo, Illinois.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of the bridge across the Ohio River at or near Cairo, Illinois, authorized to be built by the Cairo Association of Commerce by the Act of Congress approved March 6, 1928, and extended for one year by the Act of Congress approved March 2, 1929, and again extended one year by the Act of Congress approved May 26, 1930, are hereby further extended one and three years, respectively, from March 6, 1931.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, February 13, 1931.*

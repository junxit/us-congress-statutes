# Chapter 567

> An Act To amend the Act of May 29, 1928, pertaining to certain War Department contracts by repealing the expiration date of that Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That so much of an Act entitled “An Act to require certain contracts entered into by the Secretary of War or by officers authorized by him to make them, to be in writing, and for other purposes,” approved May 29, 1928 (45 Stat. L. 985), as provides that said Act shall cease to be in effect after June 30, 1930, is hereby repealed.


*Approved, June 21, 1930.*

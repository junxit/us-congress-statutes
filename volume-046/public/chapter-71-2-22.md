# Chapter 22

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the appointment of an ambassador to Poland.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized to appoint, as the representative of the United States, an ambassador to the Republic of Poland, who shall receive as compensation the sum of $17,500 per annum.


*Approved, January 22, 1930.*

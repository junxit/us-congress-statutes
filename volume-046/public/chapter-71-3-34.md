# Chapter 34

> An Act To extend the time for construction of a free highway bridge across the Red River at or near Coushatta, Louisiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge authorized by Act of Congress approved February 3, 1928, as amended by Act approved February 13, 1929, to be built by the State Highway Commission of Louisiana, across the Red River, at or near Coushatta, Red River Parish, Louisiana, are hereby further extended one and three years, respectively, from February 13, 1931.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, January 15, 1931.*

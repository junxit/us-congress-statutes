# Chapter 170

> An Act Providing for the sale of isolated tracts in the former Crow Indian Reservation, Montana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of section 2455 of the Revised Statutes of the United States, as amended by the Act of March 9, 1928 (45 Stat. L. 253; U. S. C., 2d supp., title 43, ch. 28, sec. 1171), be, and the same are hereby, extended and made applicable to lands within the portion of the Crow Indian Reservation, Montana, ceded by the Act of March 3, 1891 (26 Stat. L. 1040).


*Approved, February 14, 1931.*

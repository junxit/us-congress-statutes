# Chapter 11

> Joint Resolution To provide an appropriation for payment to the widow of John J. Casey, late a Representative from the State of Pennsylvania.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of $10,000 is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for payment to the widow of John J. Casey, late a Representative from the State of Pennsylvania. Such sum to be disbursed by the Sergeant at Arms of the House of Representatives.


*Approved, June 5, 1929.*

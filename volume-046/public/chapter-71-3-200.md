# Chapter 200

> An Act To facilitate work of the Department of Agriculture in the Territory of Alaska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of Agriculture be, and he is hereby, authorized to furnish subsistence to employees of the United States Department of Agriculture in the Territory of Alaska, and to purchase personal equipment and sup-plies for them, and to make deductions to meet the cost thereof from any money appropriated for salary payments or otherwise due such employees.


*Approved, February 16, 1931.*

# Chapter 23

> Joint Resolution To amend the Act entitled “An Act to incorporate the American Hospital of Paris,” approved January 30, 1913.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proviso contained in section 2 of the Act entitled “An Act to incorporate the American Hospital of Paris,” approved January 30, 1913, is amended to read as follows: “ Provided, That the total value of the property owned at any one time by the said corporation shall not exceed $8,000,000.”


*Approved, June 14, 1929.*

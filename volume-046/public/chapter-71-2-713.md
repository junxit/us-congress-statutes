# Chapter 713

> An Act For the relief of the State of Vermont.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the State of Vermont be, and it is hereby, relieved from all responsibility and accountability for certain quartermaster property to the approximate value of $1,758.87, the property of the War Department which was lost, destroyed, or used for relief work incident to the flood of November, 1927, while in the possession of the troops of the National Guard, State of Vermont; and the Secretary of War is hereby authorized and directed to terminate all further accountability for said property.


*Approved, June 28, 1930.*

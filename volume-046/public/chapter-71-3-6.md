# Chapter 6

> An Act To extend the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Grand Calumet River at East Chicago, Indiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Grand Calumet River at or near East Chicago, Indiana, authorized to be built by the Chicago South Shore and South Bend Railroad and its successors and assigns, by an Act of Congress approved February 13, 1929, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from February 13, 1930.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, December 10, 1930.*

# Chapter 233

> An Act To extend the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Ohio River at or near Cannelton, Indiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of the bridge across the Ohio River at or near Cannelton, Indiana, authorized to be built by the Hawesville and Cannelton Bridge Company, by the Act of Congress approved March 1, 1929, heretofore extended by Act of Congress approved May 13, 1930, are hereby further extended one and three years, respectively, from March 1, 1931.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, February 20, 1931.*

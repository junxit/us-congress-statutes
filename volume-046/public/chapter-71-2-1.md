# Chapter 1

> An Act To extend the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Mississippi River at or near Wabasha, Minnesota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of the bridge authorized by Act of Congress, approved March 10, 1928, to be built across the Mississippi River at or near Wabasha, Minnesota, by the Wabasha Bridge Committee, Wabasha, Minnesota, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from the date of approval hereof.

## § 2.

That the right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, December 13, 1929.*

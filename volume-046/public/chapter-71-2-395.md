# Chapter 395

> An Act To repeal section 144, Title II, of the Act of March 3, 1899, chapter 429 (section 2253 of the Compiled Laws of Alaska).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 144, Title II, of the Act approved March 3, 1899, chapter 429, 30 Statutes, 1301 (section 2253 of the Compiled Laws of Alaska), be, and the same is hereby, repealed.


*Approved, June 3, 1930.*

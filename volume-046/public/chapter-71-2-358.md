# Chapter 358

> An Act To amend section 1112 of the Code of Law for the District of Columbia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first paragraph of section 1112 of the Act of March 3, 1901, chapter 854, entitled “An Act to establish a code of law for the District of Columbia,” be, and it is hereby, amended to read as follows:

> ## § “Sec. 1112. Marshal’s fees.—
>
> For each return on any warrant, attachment, summons, capias, or other writ (except execution, venire, or a summons or subpoena for a witness), whether or not service has been made, $1 for each person: Provided, however, That for the return on any citation, summons, notice, or rule issued by the probate court the fee shall be 50 cents for each person.”


*Approved, May 29, 1930.*

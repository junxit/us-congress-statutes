# Chapter 76

> Joint Resolution To authorize an appropriation for the expenses of official delegates to the fourth World’s Poultry Congress, to be held in England in 1930.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That an appropriation is authorized of $15,000 for the expenses of official delegates of the United States to the World’s Fourth Poultry Congress, to be held in England in 1930, in addition to funds heretofore appropriated for the purpose of a United States Government exhibit at such congress (Forty-fifth Statutes at Large, page 1635).


*Approved, March 10, 1930.*

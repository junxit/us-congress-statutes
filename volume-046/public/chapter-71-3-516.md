# Chapter 516

> An Act To fix the annual compensations of the secretary and the Governor of the Territory of Alaska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the date of passage of this Act the salary of the secretary of the Territory of Alaska is fixed at $5,600 per annum, and that of the governor at $10,000 per annum.


*Approved, March 4, 1931.*

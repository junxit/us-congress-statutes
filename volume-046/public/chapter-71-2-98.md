# Chapter 98

> An Act To increase the appropriation for the acquisition of a site for the new House Office Building.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the appropriation “House Office Building,” contained in the First Deficiency Act, fiscal year 1929, is hereby made available for the payment of not to exceed $1,077,745.74 for the acquisition of such site notwithstanding the limit of cost for site named in such appropriation and in section 1 of the Act entitled “An Act to provide for the acquisition of a site and the construction thereon of a fireproof office building or buildings for the House of Representatives, approved January 10, 1929.


*Approved, March 28, 1930.*

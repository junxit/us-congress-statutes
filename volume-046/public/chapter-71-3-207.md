# Chapter 207

> An Act To authorize appropriations for construction of a storehouse for ammunition at Fort Benjamin Harrison.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated a sum not to exceed $4,500 for the purpose of constructing two magazines in which to store ammunition at Fort Benjamin Harrison, Indiana.


*Approved, February 17, 1931.*

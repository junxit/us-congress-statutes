# Chapter 283

> An Act For the relief of Drinkard B. Milner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,,*

That in the administration of the Emergency Officers’ Retirement Act Captain Drinkard B. Milner shall be considered as coming within the provisions of said Act and entitled to the benefits thereof.


*Approved, May 14, 1930.*

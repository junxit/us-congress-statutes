# Chapter 194

> An Act For the relief of Sterling S. Ball.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is authorized and directed to credit the accounts of Sterling S. Ball, former postmaster at Kahoka, Missouri, with the sum of $177.98, representing the value of war-savings stamps and other property lost from the post office at Kahoka, Missouri, on December 19, 1918.

## § 2.

The sureties on the bond of Sterling S. Ball as such postmaster are relieved from any liability on account of such loss.


*Approved, February 14, 1931.*

# Chapter 259

> An Act For the relief of William L. Wiles.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers William L. Wiles, late a private in Company K, Second Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service as a private of that organization on the 10th day of August, 1862: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 20, 1931.*

# Chapter 349

> An Act For the relief of Helen F. Griffin and Ada W. Allen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission shall be, and it is hereby, authorized and directed to extend to Helen F. Griffin, widow of Alfred A. Griffin, and to Ada W. Allen, widow of G. F. Allen, the provisions of an Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes,” approved September 7, 1916, as amended, compensation to commence from and after the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 28, 1931.*

# Chapter 589

> An Act Granting to the Butte Anglers’ Club, of Butte, Montana, a patent to lot 1, section 5, township 2 south, range 9 west, and a patent to the Northern Pacific Railway Company of lot 2 in said section 5.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby granted, subject to vested existing rights, to the Butte Anglers’ Club, of Butte, Montana, lot 1 of section 5, township 2 south, range 9 west, and to the Northern Pacific Railway Company lot 2 in said section, upon its filing with the Secretary of the Interior a duly certified copy of a conveyance of the said land to the said Butte Anglers’ Club. In each case the patent, in evidence of the grant herein made, shall provide that the land conveyed shall revert to the United States if and when after two years from the date thereof the premises cease to be used for the propagation of game fish and that any and all mineral of any kind or character within the granted lands reserved to the United States.


*Approved, March 5, 1931.*

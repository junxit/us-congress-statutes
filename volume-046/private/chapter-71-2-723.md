# Chapter 723

> An Act For the relief of Jesse A. Frost.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated and in full settlement against the Government to Jesse A. Frost the sum of $175 for the total loss of personal effects by fire while in charge of the checking station at Grand Canyon National Park, Arizona, on October 15, 1923.


*Approved, June 28, 1930.*

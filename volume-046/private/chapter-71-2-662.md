# Chapter 662

> An Act For the relief of T. B. Cowper.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to adjust and settle the claim of T. B. Cowper for legal services rendered to the United States in connection with the extradition from Canada, in May, 1929, of one “Red” Stevens, alias “Ife” Stevens, charged with a violation of the United States narcotic law at Buffalo, New York, and to allow in full and final settlement of said claim not to exceed the sum of $175. There is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $175, or so much thereof as may be necessary, to pay said claim.


*Approved, June 27, 1930.*

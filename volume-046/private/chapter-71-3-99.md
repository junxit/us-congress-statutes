# Chapter 99

> An Act For the relief of Jeremiah F. Mahoney.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers Jeremiah F. Mahoney, who was a member of Company K, First Regiment Montana Volunteer Infantry, Spanish-American War, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a member of that organization on the 17th day of October, 1899: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, January 31, 1931.*

# Chapter 744

> An Act For the relief of Myrtle M. Hitzing.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the accounts of Myrtle M. Hitzing, postmaster at Danville, Florida, in the sum of $170.97, due to the United States on account of money and postage stamps stolen from the safe of the post office at Danville when burglarized on the 13th day of May, 1929.


*Approved, June 28, 1930.*

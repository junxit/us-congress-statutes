# Chapter 696

> An Act To authorize an appropriation for the relief of Joseph K. Munhall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $116.25 for payment to Joseph K. Munhall, of Corona, California, in full compensation for the value of equipment belonging to him destroyed in the burning of the Oak Grove ranger station house, Cleveland National Forest, California, on March 25, 1927.


*Approved, June 27, 1930.*

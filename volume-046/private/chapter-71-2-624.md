# Chapter 624

> An Act For the relief of George W. Burgess.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the account of George W. Burgess, postmaster at Pawtucket, Rhode Island, in the sum of $254,272.11, the value of postage-stamp stock lost in the burglary of the post office at Pawtucket, Rhode Island, February 1, 1926.


*Approved, June 26, 1930.*

# Chapter 450

> An Act For the relief of Bessie Blaker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the funds of the Alaska Railroad, the sum of $300 to Bessie Blaker, for loss of four log buildings, with furnishings, located on her homestead about one mile south of Fox, Alaska, by fire from sparks of locomotives of the Alaska Railroad, in May, 1927.


*Approved, June 10, 1930.*

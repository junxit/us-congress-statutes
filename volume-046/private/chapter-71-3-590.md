# Chapter 590

> An Act For the relief of Mrs. Herman M. Warr.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission shall be, and it is hereby, authorized and directed to extend to Mrs. Herman M. Warr, widow of Herman M. Warr, a former employee of the Bureau of Prohibition, the provisions of an Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes,” approved September 7, 1916, and the pay of the said Herman M. Warr, at the time of his death, shall be deemed to have been at the rate of $150 per month: Provided, That no benefits hereunder shall accrue prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved, March 5, 1931.*

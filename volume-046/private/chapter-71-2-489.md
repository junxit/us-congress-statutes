# Chapter 489

> Joint Resolution Concerning a bequest made to the Government of the United States by S. A. Long, late of Shinnston, West Virginia.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the bequest made to the Government of the United States by S. A. Long, late of Shinnston, West Virginia, in his last will and testament dated August 27, 1927, and recorded in book 14, page 308, of the records of the county court of Harrison County, West Virginia, be declined by the Government of the United States and that the estate of the said S. A. Long be forever discharged from any obligation to the United States growing out of said last will and testament.


*Approved, March 3, 1931.*

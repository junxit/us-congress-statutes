# Chapter 456

> An Act For the relief of Rebecca Green.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the accounts of Rebecca Green, postmaster at Barbourville, Knox County, Kentucky, in the sum of $597.56 due to the United States on account of postal funds embezzled by Frederick Custer Miller, former assistant postmaster at Barbourville, Kentucky, in the year 1927.


*Approved, March 3, 1931.*

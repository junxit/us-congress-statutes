# Chapter 285

> An Act For the relief of Jessie Axton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Jessie Axton the sum of $165, in full settlement of her claim against the Government for services rendered in reporting and transcribing the record in the case of the United States against Horace Alderman.


*Approved, February 23, 1931.*

# Chapter 669

> An Act For the relief of Gustav J. Braun.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and hereby directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Gustav J. Braun the sum of $389.92 in full settlement of his claim on account of the loss, while he was in the American Expeditionary Forces, of baggage and personal effects stored under orders.


*Approved, June 27, 1930.*

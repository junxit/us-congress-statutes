# Chapter 150

> An Act For the relief of Charles F. Reilly.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws Charles F. Reilly, late of Company D, Ninth Regiment United States Infantry, and Army Service Detachment, West Point, New York, shall hereafter be held to have been honorably discharged from service in the military forces of theUnited States on December 18, 1902, and July 25, 1917, respectively: Provided, That no pension, bounty, pay, or other emolument shall accrue prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved, February 13, 1931.*

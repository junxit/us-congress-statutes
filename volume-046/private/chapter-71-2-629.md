# Chapter 629

> An Act For the relief of Charles E. Anderson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay Charles E. Anderson, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $40 representing loss sustained by him in the purchase of horses for the United States Geological Survey in April, 1910.


*Approved, June 26, 1930.*

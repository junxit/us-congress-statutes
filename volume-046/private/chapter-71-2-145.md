# Chapter 145

> An Act For the relief of Clarence L. Stevens.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to issue a patent in fee to Clarence L. Stevens, Crow allottee numbered 1259, for land allotted to him under the provisions of the Act of June 4, 1920 (Forty-first Statutes at Large, page 751), and designated as homestead.


*Approved, April 12, 1930.*

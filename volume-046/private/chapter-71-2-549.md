# Chapter 549

> An Act For the relief of Charles E. Byron, alias Charles E. Marble.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws Charles E. Byron, alias Charles E. Marble, shall be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the naval service of the United States on May 6, 1900: Provided, That no pension, bounty, or other allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 19, 1930.*

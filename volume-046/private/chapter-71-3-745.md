# Chapter 745

> An Act For the relief of Prentice O’Rear.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to allow credit in the account of Prentice O’Rear, postmaster at Columbus, Mississippi, in the sum of $27,003.08, claimed to be the amount of public funds and property committed to his care and lost through burglary on April 12, 1923, the granting of which relief is recommended by the Postmaster General.


*Approved, June 28, 1930.*

# Chapter 768

> An Act For the relief of the Oregon Short Line Railroad Company, Salt Lake City, Utah.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be authorized and directed to allow the Oregon Short Line Railroad Company, Salt Lake City, Utah, the sum of $567.50 in payment of transportation charges on a shipment of twenty-five automobile truck bodies from Fort Bliss, Texas, to Boise, Idaho, on Government bill of lading numbered WQ/A-1068213, issued March 26, 1921, by the Quartermaster General’s Office at Fort Bliss, Texas. There is hereby appropriated out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $567.50 for payment of the claim.


*Approved, June 30, 1930.*

# Chapter 256

> An Act For the relief of William Parish.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers William Parish, who was a member of Company L, Fourteenth Regiment United States Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a member of that organization on the 15th day of January, 1903: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 20, 1931.*

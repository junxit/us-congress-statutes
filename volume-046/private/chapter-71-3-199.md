# Chapter 199

> An Act To reimburse Charles Thompson for expenses incurred as an authorized delegate of the Fort Peck Indians.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Charles Thompson from the fund designated “Indian Moneys, Proceeds of Labor, Fort Peck Indians,” the sum of $94.10 as reimbursement for actual expenses incurred as a delegate to the Sioux council at Rosebud Agency, South Dakota, on October 21 and 22, 1926, under authority of the Commissioner of Indian Affairs dated October 7, 1926.


*Approved, February 14, 1931.*

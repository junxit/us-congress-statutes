# Chapter 740

> An Act For the relief of Dalton G. Miller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $16.90 to Dalton G. Miller, senior drainage engineer of the Bureau of Public Roads, in full settlement of all payments made by him for repairs to a truck belonging to the University of Minnesota, which was loaned to the Bureau of Public Roads for use in conducting a cooperative investigation during the fiscal year ending June 30, 1929, to determine the effect of soil alkali and acid upon drain tile.


*Approved, June 28, 1930.*

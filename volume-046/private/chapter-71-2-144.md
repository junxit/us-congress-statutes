# Chapter 144

> An Act For the relief of Josephine Laforge (Sage Woman).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to issue a patent in fee to Josephine Laforge (Sage Woman), Crow allottee numbered 1254, for land allotted to her under the provisions of the Act of June 4, 1920 (Forty-first Statutes at Large, page 751), and designated as homestead.


*Approved, April 12, 1930.*

# Chapter 294

> An Act For the relief of the widow of Robert Graham Moss.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the settlement of all claims of Mrs. Robert G. Moss as the widow of the late Robert Graham Moss, captain of Infantry, United States Army, who died at Columbus, Georgia, on April 6, 1928, and who was entitled to promotion to the grade of major on March 24, 1928, and whose nomination for such promotion was pending in the United States Senate at the time of his death, said Robert Graham Moss shall be regarded as having been promoted to the grade of major on March 24, 1928, and as having been entitled, from and including that date, to the pay and allowances authorized for an officer in the grade or major with his length of service.


*Approved, February 24, 1931.*

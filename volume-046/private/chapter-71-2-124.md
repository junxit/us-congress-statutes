# Chapter 124

> An Act For the relief of Frank Yarlott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to issue a patent in fee to Frank Yarlott, Crow allottee numbered 1695, for land allotted to him under the provisions of the Act of June 4, 1920 (Forty-first Statutes at Large, page 751), and designated as a homestead, except as to the northwest quarter of section 32, township 7 south, range 38 east, Montana meridian, Montana, which one hundred and sixty acres shall remain inalienable in accordance with section 13 of said Act of June 4, 1920, cited.


*Approved, April 8, 1930.*

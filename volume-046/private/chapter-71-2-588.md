# Chapter 588

> An Act For the relief of First Lieutenant John R. Bailey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the accounting officers credit the account of First Lieutenant John R. Bailey, Quartermaster Corps, United States Army, in the sum of $1,438.50 public funds, to wit, embezzled by Technical Sergeant John Beusch, Quartermaster Corps, United States Army.


*Approved, June 23, 1930.*

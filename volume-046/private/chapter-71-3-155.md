# Chapter 155

> An Act For the relief of Samuel W. Greer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers Samuel W. Greer, who was a member of Company M, Third Regiment Kentucky Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a private of that organization on the 1st day of December, 1898: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 13, 1931.*

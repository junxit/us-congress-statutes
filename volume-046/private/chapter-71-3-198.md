# Chapter 198

> An Act To reimburse William Whitright for expenses incurred as an authorized delegate of the Fort Peck Indians.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to William Whitright from the fund designated “ Indian Moneys, Proceeds of Labor, Fort Peck Indians,” the sum of $94.10 as reimbursement for actual expenses incurred as a delegate to the Sioux council at Rosebud Agency, South Dakota, on October 21 and 22, 1926, under authority of the Commissioner of Indian Affairs dated October 7, 1926.


*Approved, February 14, 1931.*

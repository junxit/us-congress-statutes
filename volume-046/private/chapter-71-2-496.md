# Chapter 496

> An Act For the relief of Frank Storms.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Frank Storms, formerly of the United States Navy, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the naval service of the United States on the 20th day of March, 1909: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance be held to have accrued by the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 16, 1930.*

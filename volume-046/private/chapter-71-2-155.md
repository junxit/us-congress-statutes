# Chapter 155

> An Act For the relief of Captain Chester G. Mayo, Supply Corps, United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the General Accounting Office is hereby authorized and directed to credit the accounts of Captain Chester G. Mayo, Supply Corps, United States Navy, in the amount of $2,994.38, which sum represents the aggregate of payments made by said officer on voucher numbered 8419 for $2,400 paid February 23, 1922, under department contract numbered 3069 (Yards and Docks numbered 4301); on voucher numbered 3334 for $164 paid March 3, 1922; on voucher numbered 162 for $3 paid September 5, 1922; on voucher numbered 5182 for $275 paid July 3, 1922; on voucher numbered 3820 for $15 paid August 11, 1920; and on voucher numbered 4708 for $137.38 paid August 28, 1922, which payments were subsequently disallowed by the Comptroller General.


*Approved, April 14, 1930.*

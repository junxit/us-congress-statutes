# Chapter 159

> An Act For the relief of Lieutenant Henry Guilmette, Supply Corps, United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the General Accounting Office is hereby authorized and directed to credit the accounts of Lieutenant Henry Guilmette, Supply Corps, United States Navy, in the amount of $49.80, which sum represents a payment made by said officer to Joseph Daniel Morrison, machinist’s mate (first class), United States Navy, as a travel allowance upon transfer to the Fleet Naval Reserve, Class F–4–C, after sixteen years’ service.


*Approved, April 14, 1930.*

# Chapter 98

> An Act For the relief of George Press.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers George Press, who was a member of Company B, Sixteenth Regiment United States Infantry, shall here after be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a private of that organization on the 24th day of March, 1899: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, January 31, 1931.*

# Chapter 163

> An Act For the relief of Captain John H. Merriam, Supply Corps, United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the General Accounting Office is hereby authorized and directed to credit the accounts of Captain John H. Merriam, Supply Corps, United States Navy, in the amount of $310, which sum represents a payment made by said officer to the J. H. Nolan Construction Company April 12, 1916, on public bill numbered 1028, contract numbered 2180, disallowed by the Comptroller General in the final settlement of the accounts of said officer.


*Approved, April 14, 1930.*

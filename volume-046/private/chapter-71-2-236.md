# Chapter 236

> An Act Authorizing the heirs of Elijah D. Myers to purchase land in section 7, township 28 south, range 11 west, Willamette meridian, county of Coos, State of Oregon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the widow and heirs of Elijah D. Myers, deceased, formerly of Coos County, State or Oregon, are hereby given the preference right to purchase the west half northeast quarter and the northeast quarter northeast quarter section 7, township 28 south, range 11 west, Willamette meridian, county of Coos, State of Oregon, at the price of $2.50 per acre, plus all taxes that have been paid on said land by the United States, subject to the right of James A. Hobson, of Myrtle Point, Oregon, his heirs or assigns, to remove prior to March 4, 1936, the timber thereon: Provided, That no purchase herein shall be effected until the land shall be restored from power-site withdrawal or released for disposition with a reservation of power rights under section 24 of the Federal Water Power Act of June 10, 1920 (Forty-first Statutes, page 1063).


*Approved, May 9, 1930.*

# Chapter 705

> An Act For the relief of Mrs. W. M. Kittle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Mrs. W. M. Kittle, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $30.63 per month from August 23, 1926, until her remarriage or death, on account of the death of her husband, W. M. Kittle, caused by the airplane accident at Langin Field, Moundsville, West Virginia, on July 10, 1921.


*Approved, June 27, 1930.*

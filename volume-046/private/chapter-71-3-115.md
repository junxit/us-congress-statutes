# Chapter 115

> An Act Providing for the promotion of Chief Boatswain Edward Sweeney, United States Navy, retired, to the rank of lieutenant (junior grade) on the retired list of the Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is hereby authorized to advance Chief Boatswain Edward Sweeney, United States Navy, retired, to the rank of lieutenant (junior grade) on the retired list of the Navy, with the retired pay and allowances of that rank: Provided, That no back pay, bounty, pension, or other emolument shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 9, 1931.*

# Chapter 593

> An Act For the relief of William. L. Bruhn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of Amenca in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers William L. Bruhn, who was a member of Battery I, Second Regiment United States Artillery, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a private of that organization on the 11th day of November, 1899: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 5, 1931.*

# Chapter 367

> An Act To authorize the President to appoint Captain Charles H. Harlow a commodore on the retired list.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized to appoint, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, Captain Charles H. Harlow (retired), United States Navy, a commodore on the retired list of the Navy: Provided, That nothing contained herein shall entitle Captain Charles H. Harlow to any back pay or allowances.


*Approved, May 29, 1930.*

# Chapter 532

> An Act For the relief of C. O. Crosby.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the account of C. O. Crosby, postmaster at Walterboro, South Carolina, with the sum of $3,765.01, covering a shortage in his accounts believed to be due in large part to the destruction of paid money orders in a fire in the post office on March 28, 1926, and to some extent to the embezzlement of funds by a former clerk in the post office.


*Approved, June 18, 1930.*

# Chapter 460

> An Act For the relief of John T. Doyle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to issue a patent in fee to John T. Doyle, Crow allottee numbered 1660, for land allotted to him under the provisions of the Act of June 4, 1920 (41 Stat. L. 751), and designated as homestead.


*Approved, March 3, 1931.*

# Chapter 603

> An Act For the relief of Ruban W. Riley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 17 and 20 of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes,” approved September 7, 1916, as amended, are hereby waived in favor of Ruban W. Riley, who lost the sight of his right eye as a result of a fall from a cliff while in the performance of his duties as United States surveyor.


*Approved, June 24, 1930.*

# Chapter 387

> An Act For the relief of Rolla Duncan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General is authorized and directed to credit the account of Rolla Duncan, former United States marshal for the district of Montana, in the sum of $195.50, which amount was paid by said Rolla Duncan as United States marshal to the county of Yellowstone, Montana, for the care and maintenance of Federal prisoners.


*Approved, June 2, 1930.*

# Chapter 55

> An Act For the relief of Guy Braddock Scott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged sailors Guy Braddock Scott, formerly a coal passer, United States Navy, shall hereafter he held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the naval service of the United States as a coal passer on the 26th day of December, 1898: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, January 29, 1931.*

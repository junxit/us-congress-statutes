# Chapter 493

> An Act Granting a franking privilege to Helen H. Taft.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all mail matter sent by post by Helen H. Taft, widow of the late William Howard Taft, under her written autograph signature, be conveyed free of postage during her natural life.


*Approved, June 14, 1930.*

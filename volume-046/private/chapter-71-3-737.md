# Chapter 737

> An Act For the relief of Fred S. Thompson.

*Be it enacted by the, Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the accounts of Fred S. Thompson, postmaster at Superior, Wisconsin, in the sum of $71,225.74 due the United States on account of postal funds, postage stamps, and war tax revenue stamps lost as a result of burglary at the post office at Superior, Wisconsin, November 21, 1925.


*Approved, June 28, 1930.*

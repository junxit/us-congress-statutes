# Chapter 360

> An Act To provide an appropriation for the payment of claims of persons who suffered property damage, death, or personal injury due to the explosion at the naval ammunition depot, Lake Denmark, New Jersey, July 10, 1926.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of $33,346.56 is appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to enable the Comptroller General of the United States to make payment of claims for property damage, death, or personal injury due to the explosion at the naval ammunition depot, Lake Denmark, New Jersey, July 10, 1926, to the respective persons and in the respective amounts as recommended by the Comptroller General of the United States and as fully set forth in House Document Numbered 321, Seventy-first Congress, second session, pursuant to the Act of March 2, 1927 (Forty-fourth Statutes, part 3, page 1800).


*Approved, February 28, 1931.*

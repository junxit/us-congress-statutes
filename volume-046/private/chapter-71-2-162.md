# Chapter 162

> An Act For the relief of Captain William L. F. Simonpietri, Supply Corps, United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the General Accounting Office is hereby authorized and directed to credit the accounts of Captain William L. F. Simonpietri, Supply Corps, United States Navy, in the amount of $220, which amount represents payments to M. W. Doolan Company for personal services as food inspectors under proposal and acceptance dated May 12, 1926.


*Approved, April 14, 1930.*

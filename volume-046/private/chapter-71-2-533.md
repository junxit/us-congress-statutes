# Chapter 533

> An Act For the relief of Second Lieutenant Burgo D. Gill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $54.75 to Second Lieutenant Burgo D. Gill, in full settlement of damage to his car caused through collision with a Government team.


*Approved, June 18, 1930.*

# Chapter 586

> An Act For the relief of James C. Fritzen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the accounts of James C. Fritzen, postmaster at Neenah, Wisconsin, in the sum of $250.75, on account of loss of twenty-five coils of 2-cent postage stamps.


*Approved, June 23, 1930.*

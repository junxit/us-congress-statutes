# Chapter 535

> An Act For the relief of John Donahue.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws or any laws conferring rights, privileges, or benefits upon persons honorably discharged from the United States Navy John Donahue shall be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the United States Navy on March 19, 1903: Provided, That no pension, pay, or bounty shall be held to have accrued by reason of this Act prior to its passage.


*Approved, March 4, 1931.*

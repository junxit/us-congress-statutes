# Chapter 627

> An Act For the relief of Vida T. Layman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to adjust and settle the claim of Vida T. Layman for traveling expenses incurred by her in going from Arlington, Kansas, to Southern Navajo Indian Agency, Arizona, and return in connection with her appointment as a teacher at the Indian Agency, which was not consummated, and to allow in full and final settlement of said claim an amount not in excess of $66.01. There is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $66.01 for the payment of such claim.


*Approved, June 26, 1930.*

# Chapter 380

> An Act For the relief of Mary Altieri.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $1,000 to Mary Altieri as compensation for personal injuries to said Mary Altieri, who was injured February 11, 1917, by a United States automobile which was carrying mail in the city of Chicago, Illinois, at the time driven by an unidentified person.


*Approved, March 2, 1931.*

# Chapter 485

> An Act For the relief of George W. Posey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws George W. Posey, late of Company A, Twentieth Regiment, and of Company B, Thirty-fifth Regiment, Wisconsin Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a private of Company A, Twentieth Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer Infantry, on the 24th day of August, 1862, and as a private of Company B, Thirty-fifth Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer Infantry on the 24th day of July, 1865: Provided, That no back pay, bounty, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 13, 1930.*

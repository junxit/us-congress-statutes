# Chapter 471

> An Act For the relief of Mabel L. Brown.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Mabel L. Brown, mother of Walter L. Brown, late of Battery A, Eighteenth Regiment United States Field Artillery, shall be regarded as the duly designated beneficiary and dependent of the late Private Brown under the Act approved December 17, 1919 (Forty-first Statutes, page 367).


*Approved, March 3, 1931.*

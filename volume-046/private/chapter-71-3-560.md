# Chapter 560

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Jessie R. Greene.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Administrator of Veterans’ Affairs be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jessie R. Greene, widow of Captain Frank L. Greene, late of Company B, First Regiment Vermont Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of $150 per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 4, 1931.*

# Chapter 164

> An Act For the relief of Lieutenant Commander Thomas Cochran, Supply Corps, United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the General Accounting Office is hereby authorized and directed to credit the accounts of Lieutenant Commander Thomas Cochran, Supply Corps, United States Navy, in the amount of $200, which amount represents payments to M. W. Doolan Company for services performed in connection with inspection of canned fruits and vegetables under accepted proposal dated April 20, 1926.


*Approved, April 14, 1930.*

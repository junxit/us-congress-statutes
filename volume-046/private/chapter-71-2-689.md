# Chapter 689

> An Act For the relief of Clifford J. Turner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of April 12, 1928 (45 Stat. L. 1720), validating homestead entry, Crookston 018072, now Cass Lake 013632, made by Clifford J. Turner, August 31, 1923, upon payment of the appraised price of the timber thereon, be, and the same is hereby, amended so as to validate the said entry upon payment by the entryman of the sum of $242.74 for the timber on the land described in said Act, which amount shall be placed to the credit of the Chippewa Indians of Minnesota, and the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby authorized to transfer to the credit of said Chippewa Indians of Minnesota the sum of $184.48, representing the difference between the original appraised value and the actual amount paid by the entryman.


*Approved, June 27, 1930.*

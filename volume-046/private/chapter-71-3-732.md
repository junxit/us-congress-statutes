# Chapter 732

> An Act For the relief of Homer Elmer Cox.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated not to exceed $147.73, to reimburse former Coxswain Homer Elmer Cox, United States Navy, for losses of clothing and other personal effects sustained by him when the United States ship San Diego was sunk off Fire Island Light on July 19, 1918: Provided, That such reimbursement shall be made under regulations to be prescribed by the Secretary of the Navy and upon vouchers to be approved by him.


*Approved, June 28, 1930.*

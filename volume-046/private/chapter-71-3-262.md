# Chapter 262

> An Act For the relief of Lillian N. Lakin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized, notwithstanding the provisions of the Act of May 22, 1928 (Forty-fifth Statutes, page 710), to settle, adjust, and certify the claim of Lillian N. Lakin as a person standing in loco parentis to the late Private Raymond N. Lakin, United States Marine Corps, for the sums of $126 as six months’ death gratuity and $40.65 as arrears of pay.


*Approved, February 20, 1931.*

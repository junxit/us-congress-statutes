# Chapter 466

> An Act For the relief of Frank D. Peck.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws Frank D. Peck shall be hereafter held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a private of Company M, Third Regiment United States Infantry, on November 22, 1900: Provided, That no back pension, back pay, or back allowance shall accrue by virtue of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 3, 1931.*

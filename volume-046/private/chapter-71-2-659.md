# Chapter 659

> An Act For the relief of the Gulf Refining Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to adjust and settle the claim of the Gulf Refining Company for refund of rent erroneously paid on premises known as 401 Fifteenth Street northwest, Washington, District of Columbia, after claimant had vacated said premises on or before April 15, 1928, and to allow said claim in the sum of not to exceed $200. There is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $200 for payment of said claim.


*Approved, June 27, 1930.*

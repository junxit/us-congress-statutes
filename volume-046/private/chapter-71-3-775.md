# Chapter 775

> An Act For the relief of Alice Hipkins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 17 and 20 of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes,” approved September 7, 1916, as amended, are hereby waived in favor of Alice Hipkins, widow of S. Otho Hipkins, late filter engineer. United States Public Health Service, at Perry Point, Maryland, who died as a result of chlorine-gas poisoning while in the performance of his duties.


*Approved, June 30, 1930.*

# Chapter 53

> An Act For the relief of David A. Dehart.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, sailors, and marines David A. Dehart, who was a private of the United States Marine Corps, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a private of the United States Marine Corps on the 20th day of June, 1900: Provided, That no bounty, pay, or allowance shall be held as accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, January 29, 1931.*

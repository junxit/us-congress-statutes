# Chapter 395

> An Act For the relief of Thomas W. Bath.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers Thomas W. Bath, who was first lieutenant and assistant surgeon in the Second Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, Spanish War, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States April 26, 1899, credited with ninety days’ service, and eligible to all benefits accruing under the law of May 1, 1926, and all Acts amendatory thereto: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 2, 1931.*

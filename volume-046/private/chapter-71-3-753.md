# Chapter 753

> An Act To reimburse Lieutenant Colonel Frank J. Killilea.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General is hereby authorized and directed to credit the accounts of the United States property and disbursing officer for the State of Massachusetts, Lieutenant Colonel Frank J. Killilea, the sum of $632.


*Approved, June 28, 1930.*

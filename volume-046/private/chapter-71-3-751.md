# Chapter 751

> An Act For the relief of Fred Schwarz, junior.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the director of the United States Veterans’ Bureau be and he hereby is, authorized and directed to pay to Fred Schwarz, junior, coroner of the county of Philadelphia, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, out of appropriations for “salary and expenses,” the sum of $31.25 in full satisfaction of all claims against the United States for stenographic transcript of the coroner’s inquest to ascertain the cause of death of Joseph P. Barlow, an employee of the United States Veterans’ Bureau.


*Approved, June 28, 1930.*

# Resolution II

> Resolution for distributing returns of the last census.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the librarian of Congress be authorized to deliver to each member of the present Congress, who was not a member of the last Congress, two copies of the return of the last census of the inhabitants of the United States, and that it may be lawful for the said members to transmit the said copies, free of postage, by mail.


*Approved, June 19, 1834.*

# Resolution III

> Resolution in relation to Charles Carroll, of Carrollton.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That Charles Carroll, of Carrollton, the only surviving signer of the Declaration of Independence, be, and he is hereby, authorized to receive and transmit letters and packages by the mail free of postage.


*Approved, May 23, 1828.*

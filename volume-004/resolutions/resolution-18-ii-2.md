# Resolution II

> Resolution directing an inventory of the furniture in the President’s house to be taken.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the commissioner of the public buildings be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to take an inventory of the furniture of the President’s house, at such time as may be convenient to the President, and to deliver a copy thereof to the President elect, and one copy to each House of Congress.


*Approved, March 3, 1825.*

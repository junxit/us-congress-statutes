# Resolution III.

> Resolution respecting the pay of the marine corps.

*Resolved by the Senate and If mise of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the pay, subsistence, emoluments, and allowances of officers, non-commissioned officers, musicians and privates of the United States marine corps, shall be the same as they were previously to the first of April, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-nine, and shall so continue until they shall be altered by law.


*Approved, May 25, 1832.*

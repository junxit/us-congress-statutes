# Resolution I

> Resolution in relation to the transmission of public documents printed by order of either House of Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That nothing contained in the act to reduce into one the several acts establishing and regulating the Post-office Department, approved March third, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-five, shall be construed to repeal or limit the operation of the act authorizing the transmission of certain documents free of postage, approved December nineteenth, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-one.


*Approved, January 13, 1831.*

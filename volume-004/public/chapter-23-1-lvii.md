# Chapter LVII

> An Act to extend the time allowed for the discharge of the duties of the commission for carrying into effect the convention with France.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That so much of an act entitled “An act to carry into effect the convention between the United States and his majesty the king of the French, concluded at Paris on the fourth day of July, eighteen hundred and thirty-one,” approved July thirteenth, eighteen hundred and thirty-two, as limits the duration of the commission created by the said act to two years, be, and the same is hereby, repealed, and that a period of three years, commencing on the first Monday of August, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-two, be allowed for the discharge of the duties prescribed by the said act.


*Approved, June 19, 1834.*

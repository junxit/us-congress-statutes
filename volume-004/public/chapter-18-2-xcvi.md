# Chapter XCVI

> An Act to establish the city of Hudson and the city of Troy, in the state of New York, Bowdoinham, in the state of Maine, and Fairport, in the state of Ohio, ports of delivery, and to abolish Topsham as a port of delivery.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the city of Hudson and the city of Troy, in the state of New York, Bowdoinham, in the district of Bath, in the state of Maine, and Fairport, in the district of Cuyahoga, in the state of Ohio, be, and the same are hereby, severally, made, a port of delivery.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the port of delivery established at Topsham, in the state of Maine, be, and the same is hereby, abolished.


*Approved, March 3, 1825.*

# Chapter XXX

> An Act to change the terms of the district court of the United States for the Kentucky district.(a)

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, from and after the passage of this act, the sessions of the district court of the United States, in and for the Kentucky district, shall commence and be holden on the first Monday of May and of November, in each year, instead of the terms now appointed by law.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That all motions, process, pleas, and suits, returnable to the term of said court, heretofore appointed to be holden in April next, shall stand adjourned and continued over to the May term, next appointed by this act, and shall be as effectual in law as if the said April term had not been abolished.


*Approved, March 24, 1824.*

# Chapter LVII

> An Act making appropriation to satisfy certain balances due to the commissioners and secretaries of land claims in Florida.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That there be and hereby is, appropriated out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of three thousand three hundred and eighty-six dollars and five cents, to satisfy certain balances due to the commissioners of land claims in Florida and their several secretaries, prior to the thirty-first day of December, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-four.


*Approved, March 3, 1825.*

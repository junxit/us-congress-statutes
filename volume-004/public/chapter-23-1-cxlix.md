# Chapter CXLIX

> An Act to mark and open a road from Columbia to Little Rock, in the territory of Arkansas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of ten thousand dollars be, and the same hereby is, appropriated, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the purpose of marking and cutting out a road from Columbia, in Chicot county, to Little Rock, in the territory of Arkansas, to be expended under the direction of the President of the United States.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

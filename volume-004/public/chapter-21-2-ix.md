# Chapter IX

> An Act to allow a salary to the marshal of the eastern district of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of two hundred dollars be, and the same hereby is, allowed, annually, as a salary to the marshal of the eastern district of Virginia.


*Approved, January 21, 1829.*

# Chapter CLXXXIX

> An Act to reduce the duty on salt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That, the duty on salt be fifteen cents per bushel of fifty-six pounds, from the thirty-first of December next, until the thirty-first of December, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-one; and, after that time, ten cents per bushel, and no more.


*Approved, May 29, 1830.*

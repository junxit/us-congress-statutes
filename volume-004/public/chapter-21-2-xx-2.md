# Chapter XX

> An Act to repeal the charges imposed on passports and clearances.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That, so much of the act of the first of June, one thousand seven hundred and ninety-six, entitled “An act providing passports for the ships and vessels of the United States,” as imposes a charge of ten dollars for passports, and of (bur dollars for a clearance, to any ship or vessel bound on a voyage to any foreign country, be, and the same is hereby repealed, to take effect from and after the thirty-first day of March of the present year.


*Approved, February 12, 1831.*

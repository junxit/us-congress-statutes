# Chapter CLV

> An Act for the relief of the Florida Indians.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of twenty thousand dollars be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to be expended under the directions of the President, in affording sustenance to the suffering Florida Indians.


*Approved, May 22, 1826.*

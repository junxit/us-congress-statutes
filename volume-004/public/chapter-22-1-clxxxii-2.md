# Chapter CLXXXII

> An Act to alter the times of holding the district court of the United States for the state of Illinois.(a)

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the term of the district court of the United States for the district of Illinois, which is now directed by law to be held on the first Monday of Alay, shall hereafter be held on the fourth Monday of May in each year; and all process which may have issued, or which may hereafter issue, returnable on the said first Monday of May, as heretofore directed, shall be held returnable, and be returned, on the fourth Monday of May in each year.


*Approved, July 9, 1832.*

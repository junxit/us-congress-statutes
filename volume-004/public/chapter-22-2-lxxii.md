# Chapter LXXII

> An Act prolonging the second session of the fifth legislative council of the territory of Michigan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the legislative council of the territory of Michigan, now in session, be, and is hereby authorized to continue its present session thirty days beyond the time now limited by law.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That, for the purpose of defraying the incidental expenses and for paying the members of said legislative council for the additional time employed as aforesaid, the sum of two thousand dollars be, and is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 2, 1833.*

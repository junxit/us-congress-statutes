# Chapter XCVIII

> An Act to establish a port of delivery at Delaware City.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That Delaware City, in the district of Philadelphia, shall be a port of delivery; and a surveyor shall be appointed, who shall reside at said city.


*Approved, May 20, 1830.*

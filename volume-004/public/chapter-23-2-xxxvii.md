# Chapter XXXVII

> An Act to authorize the removal of the land office at Wapaughkoneta to Lima, in the state of Ohio.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the land office at present established at Wapaughkoneta, in the state of Ohio, be removed to Lima, in Allen county, in the same state.


*Approved, March 3, 1835.*

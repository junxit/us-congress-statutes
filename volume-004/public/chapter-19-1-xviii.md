# Chapter XVIII

> An Act to confirm the supplementary report of the commissioners of the western district of Louisiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the claims marked B, described in the supplementary report of the commissioners of the western district of the state of Louisiana, dated the 11th of May, one thousand eight hundred and fifteen, and recommended by them for confirmation., be, and the same are hereby, confirmed in the same manner, and under the same restrictions, as the report, to which this was supplementary, was confirmed, by the act of the twenty-ninth of April, one thousand eight hundred and sixteen.


*Approved, March 31, 1826.*

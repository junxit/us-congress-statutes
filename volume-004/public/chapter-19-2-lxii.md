# Chapter LXII

> An Act to increase the salary of the Postmaster General.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That, from the first day of the present year, there shall be paid, annually, to the Postmaster General, two thousand dollars, in addition to his present salary.


*Approved, March 2, 1827.*

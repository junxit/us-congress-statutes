# Chapter L

> An Act changing the residence of the collector in the district of Burlington, in the state of New Jersey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That hereafter, the collector of the district of Burlington, in the state of New Jersey, shall reside at Lamberton instead of Burlington, in said district.


*Approved, March 31, 1830.*

# Chapter LXXV

> An Act making an additional appropriation for defraying the expenses of bringing to the seat of government the votes for President and Vice President of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That there be, and there hereby is, appropriated, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of two thousand nine hundred and sixty-nine dollars and fifty cents, for the payment of the expenses of bringing to the seat of government the votes for President and Vice President of the United States, in addition to the sum of three thousand three hundred dollars, heretofore appropriated for the same object.


*Approved, March 3, 1825.*

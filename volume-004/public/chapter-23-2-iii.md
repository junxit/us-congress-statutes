# Chapter III

> An Act making an appropriation for the completion of the military barracks at New Orleans.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of one hundred and seven thousand five hundred dollars be, and the same is hereby appro-priated and made payable out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the completion of the barracks at New Orleans, under the direction of the Secretary at War.


*Approved, January 27th, 1835.*

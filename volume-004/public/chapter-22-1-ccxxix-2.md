# Chapter CCXXIX

> An Act for the erection of barracks, quarters, and store-houses, and the purchase of a site, in the vicinity of New Orleans.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of one hundred and twelve thousand dollars, to be paid out of any money in the Trea-sury not otherwise appropriated, be, arid the saine is hereby appropriated for the erection of barracks, quarters, and store-houses, and the purchase of a site, in the vicinity of New Orleans, for a garrison of four companies of United States’ troops.


*Approved, July 14, 1832.*

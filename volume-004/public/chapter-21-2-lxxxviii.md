# Chapter LXXXVIII

> An Act to repeal the act to establish the district of Blakely.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the act entitled “An act to establish the district of Blakely,” approved the seventeenth day of April, eighteen hundred and twenty-two, be, and the same is hereby, repealed.


*Approved, March 2, 1831.*

# Chapter CII

> An Act limiting the times of advertising the sales of the public lands.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That hereafter the public lands which shall be exposed to public sale by order of the President of the United States, shall be advertised for a period of not less than three nor more than six months prior to the day of sale, any thing in any law heretofore enacted to the contrary notwithstanding.


*Approved, June 28, 1834.*

# Chapter CCXL

> An Act to authorize the sale of certain public lands in the state of Ohio.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the lands heretofore reserved tor certain Indian tribes in the state of Ohio, and which were ceded to the United States by treaties ratified on the twenty-fourth day of March, in the year one thousand eight hundred and thirty-one, and the sixth day of April, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-two, be, and the same are hereby attached to, and made to form part of, the land districts in which they are respectively situated, and liable to be sold as other public lands in the state of Ohio.


*Approved, July 14, 1832.*

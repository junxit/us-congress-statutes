# Chapter VIII

> An Act concerning the transportation of the mail between Vincennes and St. Louis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That so much of the act, entitled “An act to alter and establish certain post-roads,” approved March third, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-one, as directs that “the mail from Vincennes, Indiana, to St. Louis, Missouri, shall pass by Vandalia,” shall be, and the same hereby is, repealed.


*Approved, March 3, 1826.*

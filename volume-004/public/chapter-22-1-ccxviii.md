# Chapter CCXVIII

> An Act making a re-appropriation of a sum heretofore appropriated for the suppression of the slave trade.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the unexpended balance of the sum of thirty thousand dollars, appropriated by the act, entitled “An act making an appropriation for the suppression of the slave trade,” approved May twenty-fourth, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-eight, be re-appropriated to the same object, pursuant to the act of Congress of the third of March, one thousand eight hundred and nineteen.


*Approved, May 31, 1830.*

# Chapter LXXIV

> An Act establishing a port of entry and delivery at the village of Fall River in Massachusetts, and discontinuing the office at Dighton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That there shall, from and after the first day of April next, be established in the village of Fall River, near the mouth of Taunton Great river, in Massachusetts, a port of entry and delivery.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the office of the collection of the customs in Dighton, Massachusetts, shall, from and after the first day of April next, be discontinued.


*Approved, March 2, 1833.*

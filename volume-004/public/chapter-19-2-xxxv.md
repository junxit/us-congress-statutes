# Chapter XXXV

> An Act to extend the time of issuing and locating military land warrants to officers and soldiers of the revolutionary army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the time limited by the second section of the act, approved the twenty-fourth day of February, one thousand eight hundred and nineteen, for issuing military land warrants to the officers and soldiers of the revolutionary army, shall be extended till the fourth day of March, one thousand eight hundred and thirty; and the time for locating the unlocated warrants shall be extended till the first day of October thereafter.


*Approved, March 2, 1827.*

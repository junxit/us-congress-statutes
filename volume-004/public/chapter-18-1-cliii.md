# Chapter CLIII

> An Act malting appropriations for deepening the channel leading into the harbour of Presque isle, and for repairing Plymouth Beach.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the following sums of money be, and the same are hereby, appropriated, out of any money sin the treasury not otherwise appropriated, and placed at the disposition of the President of the United States, for the purpose of accomplishing the objects hereinafter mentioned, to wit: the sum of twenty thousand dollars, for making or deepening the channel, leading into the harbour of Presque Isle, in the state of Pennsylvania; and lire sum of twenty thousand dollars, to repair Plymouth Beach, in the state of Massachusetts, and thereby prevent the harbour, at that place, from being destroyed.


*Approved, May 26, 1824.*

# Chapter XCVII

> An Act to authorize the removal of the custom-house from Magnolia, to St. Marks in Florida.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That, the President of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized to remove the custom-house, now established at Magnolia on the St. Marks river in Florida, to the town of St. Marks or some other point on St. Marks harbour which may be deemed suitable, when in his judgment the public interests and convenience may require it, and after the removal aforesaid the office of surveyor at St. Marks shall be abolished.


*Approved, June 28, 1834.*

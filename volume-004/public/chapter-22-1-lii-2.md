# Chapter LII

> An Act to amend the several acts establishing a territorial government in Florida.(a)

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That there shall be elected one member of the legislative council in the territory of Florida, from the counties of Madison and Hamilton, and one from the county of Walton, in said territory.


*Approved, March 22, 1832.*

# Chapter CLXXIII

> An Act for changing the term of the district court, for the western district of Louisiana.(a)

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the term of the district court of the United States for the western district of Louisiana, shall hereafter commence and be holden on the third Monday of March in each and every year, instead of the time heretofore appointed by law, and the court shall then be holden under the powers, duties, rules, and regulations heretofore prescribed by law.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That all process, pleadings, motions, suits and business, heretofore begun, pending, and undetermined, shall stand continued, valid and adjourned over to the said next term of said court to be holden on the said third Monday in March next, then to be proceeded in, as if regularly returnable to, notified, or set, for that term.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

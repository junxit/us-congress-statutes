# Chapter CL

> An Act allowing appeals and writs of error from the decisions in the district court in the northern district of New York, in certain cases.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That appeals and writs of error shall lie from decisions in the district court for the northern district of New York, when exercising the powers of a circuit court; and from decisions which may be made by the circuit court for the southern district of said state, in causes heretofore removed to said circuit court, from the said district court sitting as a circuit court, to the Supreme Court of the United States, in the same manner as from circuit courts.


*Approved, May 22, 1826.*

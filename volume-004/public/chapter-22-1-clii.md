# Chapter CLII

> An Act to repeal the proviso in “An act to authorize masters of vessels in certain cases to clear out either at the custom-house of Petersburg, or that of Richmond.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the proviso of an act, passed May twenty-sixth, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-four, entitled “An act to authorize masters of vessels in certain cases to dear out either at the custom-house of Petersburg, or that of Richmond,” be, and the same is hereby, repealed.


*Approved, May 28, 1830.*

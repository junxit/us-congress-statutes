# Chapter XXXII

> An Act to authorize the appointment of a sub-agent to the Winnebago Indians, on Rock river.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That an additional sub-agent be allowed to the Winnebago tribe of Indians, to reside on the waters of Rock river; and that the said agent shall be appointed as like officers are appointed, and receive the same amount of compensation.


*Approved, February 25, 1831.*

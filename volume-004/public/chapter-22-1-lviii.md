# Chapter LVIII

> An Act to add a part of the southern to the northern district of Alabama.(a)

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That all that part of the country lying within the limits of Alabama, and now in the occupancy of the Cherokee and Chicasaw tribes of Indians, shall be added to, and constitute a part of, the northern judicial district of Alabama, instead of the southern district of said state as now arranged.


*Approved, March 31, 1832.*

# Chapter XX

> An Act concerning the entry of vessels at the port of Fairfield, in Connecticut.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That it shall be lawful to make entry of any foreign ship or vessel, and of the cargo which may be on board the same, and to unlade such cargo, or any part thereof, at the port of Fairfield, in the state of Connecticut, under the regulations in such cases by law provided.


*Approved, February 22, 1827.*

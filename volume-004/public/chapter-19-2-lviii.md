# Chapter LVIII

> An Act establishing a port of delivery at the town of Marshfield, in the district of Plymouth, and a port of delivery at Rhinebeck Landing, in the district of New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the town of Marshfield, in the district of Plymouth, and Rhinebeck Landing, in the district of New York, shall each be a port of delivery.


*Approved, March 2, 1827.*

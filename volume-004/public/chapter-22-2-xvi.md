# Chapter XVI

> An Act for making Calais and Pembroke, in the state of Maine, ports of delivery.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the towns of Calais and Pembroke, in the state of Maine, shall be ports of delivery, to be annexed to the district of Passamaquoddy.


*Approved, February 5, 1833.*

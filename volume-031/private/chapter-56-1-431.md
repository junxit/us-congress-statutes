# Chapter 431

> An Act Granting a pension to Catharine A. Schwunger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject, to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Catharine A. Schwunger, widow of Henry Schwunger, late private Company A, Fifth Regiment, Pennsylvania Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, May 12, 1900.*

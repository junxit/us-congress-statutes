# Chapter 108

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to John Roop.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Roop, late first assistant engineer. United States Navy, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, January 23, 1901.*

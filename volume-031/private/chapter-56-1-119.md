# Chapter 119

> An Act For the relief of Louis Miller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to correct the military record of Louis Miller, late a member of the Sixty-second New York Infantry Volunteers, and to grant him an honorable discharge as of date August fourth, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, from Company I of said regiment: Provided, That no pay, bounty, or allowances shall be allowed by reason of this Act.


*Approved, March 30, 1900.*

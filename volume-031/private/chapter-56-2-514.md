# Chapter 514

> An Act Granting a pension to Benoni A. McConnell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Benoni A. McConnell, late of Company E, One hundredth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1901.*

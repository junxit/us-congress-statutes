# Chapter 399

> An Act Granting a pension to Fanny Healy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Fanny Healy, widow of Chester Healy, late of Company B, Fourth Regiment Iowa Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 20, 1901.*

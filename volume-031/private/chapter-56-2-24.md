# Chapter 24

> An Act For the relief of Henry O. Morse.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to remove the charge of desertion now standing against the name of Henry O. Morse, late of the United States ship Alleghany, and to issue to said Henry O. Morse a certificate of discharge from the service: Provided, That said Morse shall not by the passage of this Act be entitled to any bounty or back pay of any kind.


*Approved, January 14, 1901.*

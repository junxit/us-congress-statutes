# Chapter 632

> An Act Granting a pension to Rosa L. Couch.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Rosa L. Couch, widow of Simon A. Couch, late captain Company D, Thirteenth Regiment Wisconsin Infantry Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, June 4, 1900.*

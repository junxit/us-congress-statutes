# Chapter 306

> An Act Granting a pension to Joseph E. Baldwin.

*Be it enacted the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Joseph E. Baldwin, late of Company C, First Regiment Enrolled Missouri Militia, Company C, Seventh Regiment Enrolled Missouri Militia, and Company E, Thirty-second Regiment Enrolled Missouri Militia, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, April 23, 1900.*

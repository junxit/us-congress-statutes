# Chapter 42

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Major A. Northrop.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Major A. Northrop, late of Company H. Fifteenth Regiment New Hampshire Volunteer Infantry, and of Company K, Eighteenth Regiment New Hampshire Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, January 14, 1901.*

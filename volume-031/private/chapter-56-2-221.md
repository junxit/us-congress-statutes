# Chapter 221

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Americus V. Rice.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Ho use of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Americus V. Rice, late colonel Fifty-seventh Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and brigadier-general, United States Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of one hundred dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 7, 1901.*

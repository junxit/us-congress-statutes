# Chapter 885

> An Act To authorize the appointment of Thomas Lutz Stitt as an officer in the Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, to appoint Thomas Lutz Stitt as a line officer in the Navy as ensign, subject to physical, moral, and professional examination, to take rank and position at the foot of the list, but he shall not receive pay or allowances for the time he has been out of the naval service.


*Approved, March 3, 1901.*

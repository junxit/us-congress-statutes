# Chapter 165

> An Act Granting a pension to Jane E. Augur.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jane E. Augur, widow of Christopher C. Augur, ate brigadier-general of the United States Army, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, April 4, 1900.*

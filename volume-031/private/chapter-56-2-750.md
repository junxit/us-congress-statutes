# Chapter 750

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Lucie M. Mabry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lucie M. Mabry, widow of Woodford H. Mabry, late colonel First Regiment Texas Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 1, 1901.*

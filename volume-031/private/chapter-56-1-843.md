# Chapter 843

> An Act Granting a pension to Mary E. Reynolds.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary E. Reynolds, widow of Joseph J. Reynolds, late major-general, United States Volunteers, and colonel, United States Army, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, June 6, 1900.*

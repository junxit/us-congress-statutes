# Chapter 731

> An Act Granting a pension to Joseph H. Hamrick and Ella G. Hamrick.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the names of Joseph H. Hamrick and Ella G. Hamrick, the blind and helpless children of Thomas H. Hamrick, late of Company K, Forty-fifth Regiment Kentucky Volunteer Infantry, and pay them each a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, June 5, 1900.*

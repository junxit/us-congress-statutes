# Chapter 399

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Lillian Capron.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lillian Capron, widow of Allyn K. Capron, late captain Troop L, First Regiment United States Volunteer Cavalry, war with Spain, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty-five dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, May 12, 1900.*

# Chapter 641

> Ac Act Granting a pension to Rosa Cox.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Rosa Cox, the dependent and helpless daughter of Evin O. Cox, late of Company K, Thirty-fourth Regiment Iowa Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 28, 1901.*

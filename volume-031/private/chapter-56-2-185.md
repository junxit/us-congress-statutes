# Chapter 185

> An Act Granting a pension to William Lyman Chittenden.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William Lyman Chittenden, late of Captain L. J. Powell’s company Minute Men Oregon Volunteers of eighteen hundred and fifty-six, and pay him a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, January 30, 1901.*

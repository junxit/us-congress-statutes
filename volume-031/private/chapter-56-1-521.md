# Chapter 521

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Katharine Taylor Dodge.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Katharine Taylor Dodge, widow of Charles Dodge, junior, late a captain and brevet major, Company C, Twenty-fourth United States Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that she now receives and two dollars per month additional on account of the minor child of said Charles Dodge, junior, until such child shall arrive at the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, May 22, 1900.*

# Chapter 168

> An Act Granting a pension to J. J. Groff.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of J. J. Groff, late scout and guide in the United States Army, and pay him a pension at such rate as his disability from wound of neck and head may entitle him to.


*Approved, January 25, 1901.*

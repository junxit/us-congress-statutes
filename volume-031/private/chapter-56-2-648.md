# Chapter 648

> Ac Act Granting an increase of pension to Augustus L. Chetlain.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior he, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Augustus L. Chetlain, late brigadier and brevet major-general, United States Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 28, 1901.*

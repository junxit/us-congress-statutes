# Chapter 197

> An Act To correct the military record of George J. Titcomb.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to remove the charge of desertion standing against George J. Titcomb, late of the Sixth Battery Massachusetts Volunteer Light Artillery, and to substitute there-for “found dead in Boston Harbor May twenty-first, eighteen hundred and sixty-four.”


*Approved, February 4, 1901.*

# Chapter 425

> An Act Granting a pension to Julia S. Goodfellow.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Julia S. Goodfellow, widow of Edward Goodfellow, ate, captain Company E. Forty-fifth Regiment United States Colored Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 20, 1901.*

# Chapter 322

> An Act Granting a pension to Ambrose Brisett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ambrose Brisett, late of the United States Marine Corps in the war with Mexico, and allow him a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 7, 1901.*

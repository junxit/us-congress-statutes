# Chapter 661

> Ac Act Granting an increase of pension to Condy Menalis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Condy Menalis, late of Company A, Two hundred and second Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 28, 1901.*

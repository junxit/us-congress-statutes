# Chapter 203

> An Act Granting a pension to Lutheria H. Maynard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lutheria H. Maynard, widow of John Tye, late a private of Company A, Second Regiment Minnesota Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars a month.


*Approved, April 17, 1900.*

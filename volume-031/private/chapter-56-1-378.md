# Chapter 378

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Caroline B. Bradford.

*Be it enacted by the Senate an d House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Caroline B. Bradford, widow of Robert F. Bradford, late captain, United States Navy, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month in lieu of that sue is now receiving.


*Approved, May 5, 1900.*

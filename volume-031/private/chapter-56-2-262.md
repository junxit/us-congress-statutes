# Chapter 262

> An Act Granting a pension to Joseph Crawford.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the inline of Joseph Crawford, late of Company C, One hundred and thirty-third Regiment West Virginia State Militia, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 7, 1901.*

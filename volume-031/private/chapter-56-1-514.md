# Chapter 514

> An Act Granting a pension to Edward L. Ruby.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Edward L. Ruby, alias Edward Cassall, late a member of Company F, Nineteenth Regiment Kansas Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him it pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month.


*Approved, May 22, 1900.*

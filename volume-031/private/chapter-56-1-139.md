# Chapter 139

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to James J. McMains.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of James J. McMains, late, of Company F. Thirty-sixth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay him it pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, April 2, 1900.*

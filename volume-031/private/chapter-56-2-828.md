# Chapter 828

> An Act For the relief of Charles M. Kennerly.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the Treasury of the United States pay to Charles M. Kennerly, of Henry County, Tennessee, the sum of six hundred dollars, in full for property taken or seized by United States officers, belonging to the said Charles M. Kennedy, on or about the fifteenth day of March, eighteen hundred and seventy-four; and said sum of six hundred dollars is hereby appropriated for that purpose out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 2, 1901.*

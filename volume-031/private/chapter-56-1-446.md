# Chapter 446

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Clare H. Burleigh.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be. is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Clare H. Burleigh, widow of Henry M. Burleigh, late captain Company D, First Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and assistant adjutant-general, United States Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, May 12, 1900.*

# Chapter 683

> Ac Act Granting a pension to Fidillar White, alias William Johnson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Fidillar White, alias William Johnson, late of Company G, Fifty-fifth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 1, 1901.*

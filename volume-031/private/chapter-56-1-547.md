# Chapter 547

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Margaret B. Shipp.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Margaret B. Shipp, widow of William E. Shipp, late first lieutenant, Tenth Regiment United States Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving, and two dollars per month additional on account of each of the minor children of said William E. Shipp until they reach the age of sixteen year.


*Approved, May 24, 1900.*

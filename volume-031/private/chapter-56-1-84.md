# Chapter 84

> An Act Granting a pension to Louise Donath.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Louise Donath, permanently helpless and dependent child of John G. Donath, late a hospital steward in the, military service of the United States, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month, payable to her legally constituted guardian.


*Approved, March 22, 1900.*

# Chapter 338

> An Act Granting a pension to Ashel C. Aulick.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ashel C. Aulick, Company H, Third Ohio Infantry, war with Spain, and pay him a pension at the rate of fifteen dollars per month.


*Approved, February 7, 1901.*

# Chapter 633

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Harriet V. Gridley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Harriet V. Gridley, widow of Charles V. Gridley, late captain, United States Navy, and pay her a pension tit the rate of fifty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, June 4, 1900.*

# Chapter 756

> An Act Granting a pension to Christopher Costello.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pen-sion laws, the name of Christopher Costello, late ordinary seaman, United States Navy, and pay him a pension rated according to the degree of his disability from injury of head and epilepsy.


*Approved, June 5, 1900.*

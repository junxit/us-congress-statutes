# Chapter 33

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Emma L. Du Bois.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Emma L. Du Bois, widow of Wilbur F. Du Bois, late of Company A, Twentieth Regiment Michigan Volunteer Infantry and first lieutenant Company F, One hundred and eighth Regiment United States Colored Troops, and pay her a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month, in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, January 14, 1901.*

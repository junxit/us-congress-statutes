# Chapter 127

> An Act Granting a pension to Nellie L. Parsons.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Nellie L. Parsons, widow of H. Chester Parsons, late captain Company L, First Regiment Vermont Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, January 23, 1901.*

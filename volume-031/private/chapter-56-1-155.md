# Chapter 155

> An Act For the relief of Joseph Bacigaluppi and others.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all real estate lying in the District of Columbia heretofore purchased by and conveyed to Joseph Bacigaluppi, Margaretha Riehl, alias Margaret Riehl, Christiana Dengler, John A, Narges, and Smith Lee, all of the District of Columbia, and of Francesco Perna, of Montgomery County, State of Maryland, be, and the same is hereby, relieved and exempted from the operations of an Act entitled “An Act to restrict the ownership of real estate in the Territories to American citizens, and so forth,” approved March third, eighteen hundred and eighty-seven; and that all forfeitures incurred by force of said Act in respect to such real estate be, and the same are hereby, remitted.


*Approved, April 3, 1900.*

# Chapter 367

> An Act Granting a pension to Eliza L. Reese.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Eliza L. Reese, widow of James T. Reese, late of Captain William Butt's company, Creek Indian war of eighteen hundred and thirty-six, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 12, 1901.*

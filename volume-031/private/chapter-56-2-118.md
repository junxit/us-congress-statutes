# Chapter 118

> An Act Granting a pension to Eva Clark.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Eva Clark, former widow of Joseph L. C. Hill, late of Company C, Thirtieth Regiment Indiana Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, January 23, 1901.*

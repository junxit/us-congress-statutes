# Chapter 850

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Winefred M. Goins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Winefred M. Goins, widow of John J. Goins, late of Captains Irwin and Mercer’s companies, Georgia Volunteers, war of eighteen hundred and twelve, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, June 6, 1900.*

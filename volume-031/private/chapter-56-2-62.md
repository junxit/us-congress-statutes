# Chapter 62

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Charles Claussen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Charles Claussen, late private, Company D, Eighth United States Infantry, and to pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, January 14, 1901.*

# Chapter 767

> An Act Granting a pension to Sophia A. Lane.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sophia A. Lane, widow of James Sterling Lane, late private in Captain John J. Floyd’s company of Georgia Volunteers, Creek Indian war, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, June 5, 1900.*

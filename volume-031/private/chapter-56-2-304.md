# Chapter 304

> An Act Granting a pension to Catherine A. McClanathan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Catherine A. McClanathan, formerly the widow of William P. Cox, late of Company L, Sixth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 7, 1901.*

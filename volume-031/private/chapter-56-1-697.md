# Chapter 697

> An Act Granting a pension to Hellen Lang.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Hellen Lang, widow of James Lang, late of Company C, Eleventh Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifteen dollars per month.


*Approved, June 4, 1900.*

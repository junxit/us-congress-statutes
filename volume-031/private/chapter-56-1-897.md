# Chapter 897

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Evan M. Woodward.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Evan M. Woodward, late adjutant Second Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Reserve Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per mouth in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, June 7, 1900.*

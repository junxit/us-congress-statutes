# Chapter 113

> An Act Granting a pension to Samuel Nichols.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Samuel Nichols, late of Company E, One hundred and Fifty-third Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month, in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, January 23, 1901.*

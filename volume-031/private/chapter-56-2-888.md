# Chapter 888

> An Act For the relief of Isaac R. Dunkelberger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be, and is hereby, authorized to nominate, and, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, appoint Isaac R. Dunkelberger, late a captain, First United States Cavalry, and brevet lieutenant-colonel, United States Army, a captain of cavalry in the Army of the United States, and when so appointed he shall be placed on the retired list of officers of the Army.


*Approved, March 3, 1901.*

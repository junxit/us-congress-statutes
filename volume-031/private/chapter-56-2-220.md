# Chapter 220

> An Act For the relief of the devisees and legal representatives of D. L. Huskey, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the stun of one hundred and thirty-nine dollars and nineteen cents be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any moneys in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to pay the devisees and legal representatives of D. L. Huskey, deceased, being the balance due D. L. Huskey, as shown by the records of the Post-Office Department, for services from July first, eighteen hundred and sixty-one, to January nineteenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, as contractor on route numbered ten thousand four hundred and five, Missouri.


*Approved, February 6, 1901.*

# Chapter 210

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Catherine A. Young.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representative of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Catherine A. Young, widow of John L. Young, late of Captain Preston’s company, First Regiment Virginia Volunteers, war with Mexico, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 5, 1901.*

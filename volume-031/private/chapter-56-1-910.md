# Chapter 910

> An Act Granting a pension to Virginia Hull.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Virginia Hull, helpless daughter of Egbert B. Hull, late first lieutenant of Company E, One hundred and twenty-fifth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, June 7, 1900.*

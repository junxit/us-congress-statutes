# Chapter 64

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Annie B. Sharrard.

*Be it enacted the Senate and Hawse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Annie B. Sharrard, widow of Samuel R. Sharrard, late captain Company C, Twenty-first Regiment Kentucky Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, January 14, 1901.*

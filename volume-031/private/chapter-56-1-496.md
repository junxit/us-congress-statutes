# Chapter 496

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Juliet Gregory.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Juliet Gregory, widow of Felix G. Gregory, late of Company I, Third Regiment Missouri Mounted Volunteers, in the Mexican war, and pay her a pension of twenty dollars per month in lieu of the pension she is now receiving.


*Approved, May 22, 1900.*

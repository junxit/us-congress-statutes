# Chapter 201

> An Act For the promotion and retirement of Passed Assistant Surgeon John F. Bransford, of the United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and is hereby, authorized and empowered to appoint Passed Assistant Surgeon John F. Bransford a surgeon in the Navy of the United States, and then place the name of said Bransford on the retired list thereof.


*Approved, February 5, 1901.*

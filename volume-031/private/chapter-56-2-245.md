# Chapter 245

> An Act Granting a pension to Agnes B. Hoffman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the tension roil, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Agnes B. Hoffman, widow of William G. Hoffman, ate first lieutenant, Third United States Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 7, 1901.*

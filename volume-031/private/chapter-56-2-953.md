# Chapter 953

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Francis M. Thompson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Francis M. Thompson, late of Company D, Twenty-first Regiment Iowa Volunteer Infantry, and and pay him a pension at the rate of sixty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 3, 1901.*

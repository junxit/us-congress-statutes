# Chapter 495

> An Act Granting a pension to Caroline M. H. Searing.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Caroline M. H. Searing, widow of William M. Searing, late colonel Thirtieth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1901.*

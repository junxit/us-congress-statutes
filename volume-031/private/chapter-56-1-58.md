# Chapter 58

> An Act Granting a pension to Silas B. Hensley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Silas B. Hensley, late private, Company K, Third North Carolina Mounted Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, March 20, 1900.*

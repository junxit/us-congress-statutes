# Chapter 829

> An Act Granting a pension to Clarence S. Hall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Clarence S. Hall, the blind and dependent son of Andrew L. Hall, late of Company C, First Battalion Maine Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, June 6, 1900.*

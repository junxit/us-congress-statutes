# Chapter 423

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Elizabeth A. Hyatt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elizabeth A. Hyatt, widow of Asa W. Hyatt, late of Company K, Fourth Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eighteen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, May 12, 1900.*

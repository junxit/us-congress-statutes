# Chapter 517

> An Act Granting a pension to Felix G. Sitton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Felix G. Sitton, late of Company H, First Regiment, Doniphan’s Missouri Mounted Volunteer Infantry, war with Mexico, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, May 22, 1900.*

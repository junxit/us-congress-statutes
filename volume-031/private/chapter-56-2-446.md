# Chapter 446

> An Act Granting a pension to Eliza N. Lord.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Eliza N. Lord, dependent mother of Horace W. Lord, late of Company G. Eighth Regiment New Hampshire Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 20, 1901.*

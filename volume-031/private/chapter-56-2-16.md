# Chapter 16

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Sarah E. Tate.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah E. Tate, widow of Samuel A. Tate, late of Company I, Fortieth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving: Provided, That in the event of the death of Florence Tate, the helpless and dependent child of said Samuel A. Tate, the additional pension herein granted on account of such child shall cease and determine.


*Approved, January 14, 1901.*

# Chapter 443

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Maria Egan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Maria Egan, widow of Michael Egan, late captain Company G, Eighty-eighth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, May 12, 1900.*

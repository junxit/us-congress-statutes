# Chapter 71

> An Act Granting a pension to Augusta Ullman.

*Be it enacted be the Senate and House. of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Augusta Ullman, widow of Charles Ullman, late of Company H, Second United States Dragoons, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, January 14, 1901.*

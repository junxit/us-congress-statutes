# Chapter 147

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to John Coombs.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the, name of John Coombs, late of Company H, Second Regiment Illinois Volunteer Heavy Artillery, and pay him a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, January 23, 1901.*

# Chapter 503

> An Act Granting a pension to Taylor Hux.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Taylor Hux, the dependent and helpless son of John Hux, late of Company B, Thirty-third Regiment Iowa Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1901.*

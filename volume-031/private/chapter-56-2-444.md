# Chapter 444

> An Act Granting a pension to Lizzie Barrett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lizzie Barrett, widow of Michael Barrett, alias Michael Lynch, late of Company G, Sixty-first Regiment Massachusetts Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month, and two dollars per month additional on account of each of the minor children of said Michael Barrett, alias Michael Lynch, until they reach the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, February 20, 1901.*

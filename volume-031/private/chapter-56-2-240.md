# Chapter 240

> An Act Granting a pension to Edward Hounsom.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Edward Hounsom, late of Company E, Twenty-ninth Regiment Enrolled Missouri Militia, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month.


*Approved, February 7, 1901.*

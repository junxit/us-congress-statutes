# Chapter 239

> An Act Granting a pension to John C. Ray.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the general pension law, the name of John C. Ray, late pilot of the United States steamers Champion and Springfield, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month.


*Approved, April 17, 1900.*

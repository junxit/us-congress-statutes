# Chapter 449

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Pleasant H. McBride.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Pleasant H. McBride, late sergeant in Captain L. D. Newman’s company, First Tennessee Mounted Volunteers, war with Mexico, and pay him a pension tit the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, May 12, 1900.*

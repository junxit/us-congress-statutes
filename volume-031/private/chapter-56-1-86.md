# Chapter 86

> An Act Granting a pension to Margarethe Bomke.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Margarethe Bomke, widow of Edmund Bomke, late quartermaster-sergeant of Eleventh Regiment of New York Militia, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month, and two dollars per month additional for each of the two minor children of the soldier.


*Approved, March 22, 1900.*

# Chapter 403

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Charles C. Doolittle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he, is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Charles C. Doolittle, late colonel Eighteenth Regiment Michigan Volunteer Infantry, and brigadier-general, United States Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, May 12, 1900.*

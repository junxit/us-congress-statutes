# Chapter 915

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Florence L. Stuart.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Florence L. Stuart, widow of Sidney E. Stuart, late a captain, Ordnance Department, United States Army, and that she be paid a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving, and two dollars per month additional for each of her minor children until they shall arrive at the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, March 3, 1901.*

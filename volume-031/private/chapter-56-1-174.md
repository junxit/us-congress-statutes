# Chapter 174

> An Act Granting a pension to Sarah Claggett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah Claggett, former widow of Michael Dye, late of Company A. Seventy-eighth Regiment Indiana Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, April 4, 1900.*

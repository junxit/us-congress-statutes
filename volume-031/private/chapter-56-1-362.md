# Chapter 362

> An Act Granting a pension to Sarah Ann Fletcher.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah Ann Fletcher, widow of Archer Fletcher, late of Company C, Seventh Battalion. District of Columbia Volunteer Militia Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, May 5, 1900.*

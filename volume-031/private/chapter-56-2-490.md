# Chapter 490

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Louisa N. Godfrey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Louisa N. Godfrey, widow of John W. Godfrey, late acting master, United States Navy, and pay her a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 25, 1901.*

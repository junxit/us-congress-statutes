# Chapter 694

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Kate B. Warren.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Kate B. Warren, widow of Charles E. Warren, late assistant surgeon Ninety-fifth Regiment United States Colored Troops and surgeon Ninetieth Regiment United States Colored Troops, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, June 4, 1900.*

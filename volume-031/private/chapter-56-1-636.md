# Chapter 636

> An Act Granting a pension to Lydia F. Wiley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lydia F. Wiley, widow of John Wiley, late surgeon of the Sixth Regiment New Jersey Infantry Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, June 4, 1900.*

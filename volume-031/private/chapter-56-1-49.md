# Chapter 49

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to William Hamley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William Hamley, late private in Company B, Forty-first Wisconsin Volunteers, at the rate of fifty dollars per month in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 20, 1900.*

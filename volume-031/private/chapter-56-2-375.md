# Chapter 375

> An Act To correct the military record of William Elkert.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to set aside the findings of the court-martial in the case of William Elkert, late of Company H, Forty-fifth Wisconsin Infantry Volunteers, and to grant to said William Elkert an honorable discharge from said company and regiment: Provided, That no pay, bounty, or other emolument shall become due or payable by virtue of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 15, 1901.*

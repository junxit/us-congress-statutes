# Chapter 312

> An Act Granting a pension to Pattie D. McCown.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Pattie D. McCown, formerly the widow of John Buford, late major-general, United States Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 7, 1901.*

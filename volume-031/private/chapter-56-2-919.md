# Chapter 919

> An Act To pay to J. P. Ouzts two hundred and nine dollars and fifty cents for services as deputy collector internal revenue for district of South Carolina.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of two hundred and nine dollars and fifty cents is hereby appropriated, out of any unexpended balance in the Treasury, to pay Joseph P. Ouzts for services rendered during the years eighteen hundred and ninety-four and eighteen hundred and ninety-five as deputy collector of internal revenue in the district of South Carolina.


*Approved, March 3, 1901.*

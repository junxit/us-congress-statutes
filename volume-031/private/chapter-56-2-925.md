# Chapter 925

> An Act Granting a pension to Sallie B. Wilson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sallie B. Wilson, widow of Samuel B. Wilson, late of Company C, Eighth Regiment United States Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month, and two dollars per month additional for each of the two minor children of said soldier, until such children shall have arrived at the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, March 3, 1901.*

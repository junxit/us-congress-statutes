# Chapter 311

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Frank Blair.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Frank Blair, late private in Captain S. H. M Walker's company of Texas Mounted Volunteers, war with Mexico, and pay him a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 7, 1901.*

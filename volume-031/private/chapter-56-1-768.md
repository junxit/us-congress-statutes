# Chapter 768

> An Act Granting a pension to Margaret Thornberry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Margaret Thornberry, widow of Samuel Milligan, late of Company I, Eleventh Regiment Indiana Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, June 5, 1900.*

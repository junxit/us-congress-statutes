# Chapter 87

> An Act Granting pension to Philip Yoe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Philip Yoe, late of Company C, Third Potomac Home Brigade, Maryland Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 22, 1900.*

# Chapter 60

> An Act Granting a pension to Mary Jackman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Jackman, dependent mother of James M. Jackman, late of Company E, First Regiment Heavy Artillery New Hampshire Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 20, 1900.*

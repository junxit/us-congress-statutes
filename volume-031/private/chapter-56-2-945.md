# Chapter 945

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Cornelia Hays.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Cornelia Hays, widow of William Hays, late brigadier-general, United States Volunteers, and colonel in the United States Army, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 3, 1901.*

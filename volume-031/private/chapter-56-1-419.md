# Chapter 419

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Adolphus Lavine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject, to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Adolphus Lavine, late of Company I, One hundred and sixty-ninth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, May 12, 1900.*

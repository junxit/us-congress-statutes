# Chapter 602

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Margaret E. Van Horn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Margaret E. Van Horn, widow of James J. Van Horn, late colonel Eighth Regiment United States Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving, and two dollars per month additional on account of each of the two minor children of said James J. Van Horn until such children shall arrive at the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, June 1, 1900.*

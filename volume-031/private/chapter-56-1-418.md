# Chapter 418

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Julia A. Floyd.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Julia A. Floyd, widow of Joel G. Floyd, late private, Company A, Twenty-third Regiment, and sergeant, Company H, Sixty-first Regiment, Massachusetts Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty-two dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving: Provided, however, That in the case of the death of the epileptic child, T. Gilbert Floyd, on whose account the pension of Juba A. Floyd is increased, the pension of said Julia A. Floyd shall continue only at the rate of eight dollars per month and two dollars per month for the minor child Zoe, now on the roll, from and after the date of death of said epileptic child.


*Approved, May 12, 1900.*

# Chapter 309

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Frances D. Best.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the tension roll, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws, the name, of Frances D. Best, widow of Joseph G. Best, late lieutenant-colonel Twenty-first Regiment Missouri Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, April 23, 1900.*

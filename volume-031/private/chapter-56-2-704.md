# Chapter 704

> An Act Granting a pension to Mamie Craig Lawton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mamie Craig Lawton. widow of Henry W. Lawton, late lieutenant-colonel. United States Army, and major-general, United States Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per mouth.


*Approved, March 1, 1901.*

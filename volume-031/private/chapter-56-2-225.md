# Chapter 225

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Mattie Otis Dickinson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mattie Otis Dickinson, widow of Captain Walter Mason Dickinson, late of the Seventeenth Regiment of Infantry, United States Army, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars a month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 7, 1901.*

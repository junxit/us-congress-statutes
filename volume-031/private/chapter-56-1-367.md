# Chapter 367

> An Act Granting a pension to Charles Williamson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Charles Williamson, late assistant surgeon Twelfth Regiment Kansas State Volunteer Militia, and pay him a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month.


*Approved, May 5, 1900.*

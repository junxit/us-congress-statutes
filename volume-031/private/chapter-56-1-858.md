# Chapter 858

> An Act For the relief of Fred Weddle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Fred Weddle, having served in the Quartermaster’s Department of the United States Army during the war of the rebellion, and with the Regular Army before the late war, be. and he is hereby, relieved from any disability under the laws of the United States from any defect of naturalization, and is hereby authorized to prosecute Indian depredation claim numbered six hundred and eighty-six, now pending in the United States Court of Claims, and to receive judgment therein the same as if he had been naturalized under the laws of the United States at the date of the loss.


*Approved, June 6, 1900.*

# Chapter 656

> An Act Granting a pension to Elvira Hunter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elvira Hunter, widow of Nicholas Hunter, late of Company F, First Regiment Arkansas Mounted Gunners in the war known as the Sabine disturbance, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, June 4, 1900.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 55

> john m. smith.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the House the bill of the House numbered (H. R. 5156). “Granting an increase of pension to John M. Smith.”

Passed the House May 15, 1900.

Passed the Senate May 16, 1900.

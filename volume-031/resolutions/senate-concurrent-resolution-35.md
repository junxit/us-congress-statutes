# Senate Concurrent Resolution 35

> report of director of geological survey.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed 25,000 extra copies of the report of the Director of the Geological Survey submitted to the Secretary of the Interior relating to the Cape Nome district, in Alaska, with maps accompanying the same. 5,000 copies for the use of the Senate, 10,000 copies for the use of the House of Representatives, and 10,000 copies for distribution by the (Geological Survey.

Passed the Senate March 8, 1900.

Passed the House March 13, 1900. With amendments.

(Amendments incorporated in the foregoing, agreed to in the Senate March 15.)

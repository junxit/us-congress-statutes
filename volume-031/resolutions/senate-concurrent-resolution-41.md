# Senate Concurrent Resolution 41

> “crimes, misdemeanors, and penalties.”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed 4,500 copies of the. report transmitted by the Secretary of State of the commissioner for the United States on the International Prison Commission on “Crimes, misdemeanors, and penalties” in the United States; of which 1,000 shall be for the use of the Senate, 2,000 for the use of the House of Representatives, and 1.500 for distribution by the Department of State.

Passed the Senate January 9, 1900.

Passed the House April 7, 1900.

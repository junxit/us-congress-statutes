# Senate Concurrent Resolution 78

> george g. kemp.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate the bill of the Senate No. 1226, “Granting an increase of pension to George G. Kemp.”

Passed the Senate January 8, 1901.

Passed the House of Representatives January 9, 1901.

# Public Resolution 34

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the President of the United States to appoint David Bagley as an additional cadet at the Naval Academy, Annapolis, Maryland.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States he, and is hereby, authorized to appoint as an additional cadet at the Naval Academy, Annapolis, Maryland, David Bagley, brother of Ensign Worth Bagley, United States Navy, killed in battle at Cardenas, Cuba, May eleventh, eighteen hundred and ninety-eight.


*Approved, June 6, 1900.*

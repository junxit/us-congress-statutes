# Senate Concurrent Resolution 101

> lewis s. horsey.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate the bill of the Senate No. 1203 “Granting an increase of pension to Lewis S. Horsey.”

Passed the Senate February 18, 1901.

Passed the House of Representatives February 18, 1901.

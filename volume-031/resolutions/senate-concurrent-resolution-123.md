# Senate Concurrent Resolution 123

> reports on plants poisonous to stock in montana.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed ten thousand copies additional to the usual number of the report of Doctor V. K. Chestnut and Doctor E. V. Wilcox of their investigation of plants poisonous to stock in Montana, of which one thousand copies shall be for the use of the Senate, three thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives, and six thousand copies for the use of the Department of Agriculture.

Passed the Senate February 22, 1901.

Passed the House of Representatives March 1, 1901.

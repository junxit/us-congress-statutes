# Senate Concurrent Resolution 125

> army.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate and House of Representatives six thousand copies of the Legislative History of the General Staff of the Army of the United States, of which one thousand five hundred copies shall be for the use of the Senate, three thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives, and one thousand five hundred copies for the use of the War Department.

Passed the Senate February 28, 1901.

Passed the House of Representatives March 1, 1901.

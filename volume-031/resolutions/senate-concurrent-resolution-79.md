# Senate Concurrent Resolution 79

> lock etc., brennecke’s shoals, osage river, mo.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Secretary of War be directed to transmit to the Senate an additional estimate of the amount necessary to be appropriated for the completion of the work upon the lock and dam at Brennecke’s shoals, on the Osage River in the State of Missouri.

Passed the Senate January 21, 1901.

Passed the House of Representatives January 22, 1901.

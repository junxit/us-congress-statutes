# House Concurrent Resolution 44

> national banking laws.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed in black cloth binding and wrapped for mailing five thousand copies of the National banking laws for the use of the office of the Comptroller of the Currency.

Passed the House April 9, 1900.

Passed the Senate April 11, 1900.

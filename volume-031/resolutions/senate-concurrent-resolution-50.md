# Senate Concurrent Resolution 50

> elender herring.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate the bill of the Senate numbered 1265, “Granting a pension to Elender Herring.”

Passed the Senate April 14, 1900.

Passed the House April 24. 1900.

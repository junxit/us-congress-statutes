# Senate Concurrent Resolution 59

> andrew f. dinsmore.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate the bill of the Senate (S. 3215) “Granting an increase of pension to Andrew F. Dinsmore.”

Passed the Senate May 22, 1900.

Passed the House May 22, 1900.

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 91

> “history, etc., united states census.”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed and bound in cloth for the use of the Census Bureau, five hundred copies of Senate Document Numbered One hundred and ninety-four, first session, Fifty-sixth Congress, entitled “The History and Growth of the United States Census.”

Passed the Senate June 2, 1900.

Passed the House of Representatives February 9, 1901.

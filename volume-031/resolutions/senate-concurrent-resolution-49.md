# Senate Concurrent Resolution 49

> “the louisiana purchase.”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed nine thousand copies of the work entitled “The Louisiana Purchase,” by the honorable Commissioner of the General Land Office of the United States, three thousand copies for the use of the Senate and six thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives.

Passed the Senate March 28, 1900.

Passed the House April 14, 1900, with the following amendments:

(Amendments incorporated in the foregoing.)

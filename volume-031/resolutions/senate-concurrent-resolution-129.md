# Senate Concurrent Resolution 129

> report on hand and machine labor.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed and bound in cloth eight thousand extra copies of the report of the Commissioner of Labor on hand and machine labor, known as his Thirteenth Annual Report, of which five thousand shall be for the use of the Department of Labor, one thousand copies for the use of the Senate, and two thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives.

Passed the Senate May 8, 1900.

Passed the House of Representatives March 1, 1901.

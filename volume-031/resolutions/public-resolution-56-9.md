# Public Resolution 9

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the President to appoint one woman com- missioner to represent the United States and the National Society of the Daughters of the American Revolution at the unveiling of the statue of Lafayette at the exposition in Paris, France, in nineteen hundred.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President may appoint one woman commissioner to represent the United States and the National Society of the Daughters of the American Revolution at the, unveiling of the statue of Lafayette and the presentation of a tablet for said statue at Paris, France, in nineteen hundred, and at the exposition there to be held.


*Approved, February 23, 1900.*

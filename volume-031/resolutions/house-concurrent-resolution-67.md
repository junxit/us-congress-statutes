# House Concurrent Resolution 67

> enrolling bill.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That during the remainder of the present session of Congress the engrossing and enrolling of bills and joint resolutions by printing, as provided by act of Congress, approved March second, eighteen hundred and ninety-five, may be suspended, and said bills and joint resolutions may be written by hand.

Passed the House, June 5, 1900.

Passed the Senate, June 5, 1900.

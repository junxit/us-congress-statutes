# House Concurrent Resolution 120

> st. joseph, mo., river improvements.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Secretary of War is hereby instructed to send to the House of Representatives information as to the information and recommendations contained in the supplemental report of the Missouri River Commission as to river improvements at St. Joseph, Missouri.

Passed the House of Representatives March 1, 1901.

Passed the Senate March 2, 1901.

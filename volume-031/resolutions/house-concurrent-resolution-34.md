# House Concurrent Resolution 34

> report of philippine commission.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed and bound of the Report of the Philippine Commission fifteen thousand copies, ten thousand for the use of the House and five thousand for the use of the Senate.

Passed the House February 12, 1900.

Passed the Senate February 23, 1900.

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 60

> alice v. cook, pension bill.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate the bill of the Senate (S. 2344) “Granting a pension to Alice V. Cook.”

Passed the Senate, May 24, 1900.

Passed the House, May 24, 1900.

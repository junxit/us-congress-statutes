# Senate Concurrent Resolution 54

> the smithsonian institution documents.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed of “The Smithsonian Institution Documents relative to its Origin and History,” seven thousand copies, of which fifteen hundred copies shall be for the use of the Senate, three thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives, and two thousand five hundred copies for the use of the Smithsonian Institution.

Passed the Senate April 26, 1900.

Passed the House May 11, 1900, with the following amendments:

(Amendments incorporated in the foregoing.)

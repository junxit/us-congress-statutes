# Public Resolution 2

> Joint Resolution To fill a vacancy in the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the vacancy in the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institutions of the class other than members of Congress, caused by the death of William Lyne Wilson, of Virginia, shall be filled by the appointment of George Gray, a resident of Delaware.


*Approved, January 14, 1901.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 43

> reports of the director of the mint.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

Resolved,

That there be six thousand additional copies of the report of the Director of the Mint on the production of the precious metals for the calendar year eighteen hundred and ninety-eight, bound in cloth and wrapped, two thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives, one thousand for the use of the Senate, and thrqe thousand copies for the use of the Director of the Mint.

That there also be printed seven thousand five hundred additional copies of the report of the Director of the Mint covering the operations of the mints and assay offices of the United States for the fiscal year ended June, thirtieth, eighteen hundred and ninety-nine, to be bound in cloth and wrapped, three thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives, one thousand five hundred for the use of the Senate, and three, thousand for the use of the Director of the Mint.

Passed the House April 7, 1900.

Passed the Senate April 11, 1900.

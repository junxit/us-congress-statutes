# House Concurrent Resolution 108

> alexander f. hartford.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the House the bill of the House (H. R. 8998) “Granting an increase of pension to Alexander F. Hartford.”

Passed the House of Representatives February 25, 1901.

Passed the Senate February 26, 1901.

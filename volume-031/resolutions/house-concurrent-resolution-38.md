# House Concurrent Resolution 38

> bureau of american republics.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Bureau of American Republics two thousand five hundred copies of the Annual Report of the Director of the Bureau of American Republics.

Passed the Senate December 14, 1899.

Passed the House March 23, 1900.

# House Concurrent Resolution 30

> holiday recess, 1899-1900.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Wednesday, the twentieth day of December, they stand adjourned until twelve o’clock, meridian, on Wednesday. January third, nineteen hundred.

Passed the House December 13, 1899.

Passed the Senate December 14. 1899.

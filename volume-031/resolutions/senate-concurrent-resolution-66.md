# Senate Concurrent Resolution 66

> senate bill no. 2483.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate the bill of the Senate (S. 2483) “Granting an increase of pension to Lewis C. Beard.”

Passed the Senate, June 5, 1900.

Passed the House, June 5, 1900.

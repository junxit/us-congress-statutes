# Senate Concurrent Resolution 47

> margaret h. kent.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate the bill of the Senate numbered 2332, “Granting an increase of pension to Margaret H. Kent.”

Passed the Senate May 7, 1900.

Passed the House May 7, 1900.

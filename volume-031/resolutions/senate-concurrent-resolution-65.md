# Senate Concurrent Resolution 65

> senate bill no. 2962.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate the bill of the Senate (S. 2962) “Granting an increase of pension to William Blades.”

Passed the Senate, June 4, 1900.

Passed the House, June 5, 1900.

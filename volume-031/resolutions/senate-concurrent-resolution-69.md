# Senate Concurrent Resolution 69

> digest of decisions, etc., senate, etc.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed three thousand additional copies of Senate Miscellaneous Document Two hundred and seventy-eight, second session, Fifty-third Congress, the same being a “Digest of decisions and precedents of the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States, relating to their powers and privileges respecting their members and officers and to investigations, contempts, libels, contumacious witness, expulsions, etc.,” as revised and new matter added, together with decisions of the Supreme Court of the United States and other courts, of which one thousand copies shall be for the use of the Senate and two thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives.

Passed the Senate, January 15, 1900.

Passed the House June 5, 1900.

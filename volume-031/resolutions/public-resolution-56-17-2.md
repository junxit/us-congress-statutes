# Public Resolution 17

> Joint Resolution Concerning printing of additional copies of the Annual Report of the Geological Survey.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That of the volumes or parts of the Annual Report of the Geological Survey which relate to hydrography, forestry, and mining and mineral resources there shall here-after be published one thousand copies in addition to the number now published, for distribution by the Geological Survey.


*Approved, March 2, 1901.*

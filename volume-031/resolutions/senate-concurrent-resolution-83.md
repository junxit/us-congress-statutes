# Senate Concurrent Resolution 83

> brunswick river, n. c.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Secretary of War be directed to transmit to the Senate an estimate of the cost of removing the obstructions at the mouth of Brunswick River, North Carolina.

Passed the Senate January 25, 1901.

Passed the House of Representatives January 25, 1901.

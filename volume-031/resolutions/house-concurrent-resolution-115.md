# House Concurrent Resolution 115

> atlas chickamauga and chattanooga battlefields.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed four thousand extra copies of the Atlas of the Chickamauga and Chattanooga Battlefields, with such additional positions as have been identified, two thousand copies of which shall be for the use of the House of Representatives, one thousand for the use of the Senate, and one thousand for the use of the Secretary of War and the Chickamauga and Chattanooga National Military Park Commission.

Passed the House of Representatives February 9, 1901.

Passed the Senate February 28, 1901.

# House Concurrent Resolution 113

> employees house of representatives.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the committee of conference on the disagreeing votes of the two Houses on the amendments of the Senate to the bill (H. R. 12291) making appropriations for legislative, executive, and judicial expenses are authorized to include in their report such alterations, changes, and recommendations as they may deem proper with reference to so much of the text of said bill as relates to the officers and employees of the House of Representatives.

Passed the House of Representatives February 26, 1901.

Passed the Senate February 27, 1901.

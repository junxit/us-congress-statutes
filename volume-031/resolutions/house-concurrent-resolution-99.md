# House Concurrent Resolution 99

> hawaiian islands report.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That five thousand copies of the report of the investigations of the agricultural resources and capabilities of the Hawaiian Islands transmitted by a message of the President to Congress, dated January twenty-fourth, nineteen hundred and one, be printed, two thousand copies for the use of the House, and one thousand copies for the use of the Senate, and two thousand copies for the use of the Department of Agriculture.

Passed the House of Representatives February 9, 1901.

Passed the Senate February 15, 1901.

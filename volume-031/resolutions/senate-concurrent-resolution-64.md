# Senate Concurrent Resolution 64

> john g. b. masters, pension bill.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return the bill of the Senate (S. 1551) “Granting a pension to John G. B. Masters.”

Passed the Senate, June 4, 1900

Passed the House, June 5, 1900.

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

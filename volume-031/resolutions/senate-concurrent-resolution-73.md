# Senate Concurrent Resolution 73

> alaska civil government bill.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Committee on Enrolled Bills be, and it is hereby, authorized and directed to insert the word “low” after the word “below” in line 26, page eleven of the enrolled bill, entitled “An Act making further provision for a civil government for Alaska, and for other purposes,” the same having been omitted in the enrollment of the bill.

Passed the Senate, June 6, 1900.

Passed the House, June 5, 1900.

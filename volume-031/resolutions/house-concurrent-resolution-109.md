# House Concurrent Resolution 109

> charles e. churchill.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the House the bill of the House (H R 4963) “Granting an increase of pension to Charles E. Churchill.”

Passed the House of Representatives February 25, 1901.

Passed the Senate February 26, 1901.

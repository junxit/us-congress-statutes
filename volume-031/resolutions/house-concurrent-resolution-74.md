# House Concurrent Resolution 74

> senate bill no. 3106.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate the bill of the Senate (S. 3106) “Relating to accounts of United States marshals and the clerks of the District Courts for the Territory of Utah.”

Passed the Senate, June 7, 1900.

Passed the House, June 7, 1900.

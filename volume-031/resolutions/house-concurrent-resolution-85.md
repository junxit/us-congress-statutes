# House Concurrent Resolution 85

> oliver h. cram.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the House the bill (H. R. 10761) “Granting an increase of pension to Oliver H. Cram.”

Passed the House of Representatives February 1, 1901.

Passed the Senate February 1, 1901.

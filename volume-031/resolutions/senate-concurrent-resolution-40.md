# Senate Concurrent Resolution 40

> diplomatic and consular appropriation bill.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (H. R. 7941) “An Act making appropriation for the diplomatic and consular service for the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, nineteen hundred and one,” the, Clerk of the House of Representatives be directed to omit the word “Ottawa,” in line twenty-two, page eleven, of the engrossed bill.

Passed the Senate March 31, 1900.

Passed the House March 31, 1900.

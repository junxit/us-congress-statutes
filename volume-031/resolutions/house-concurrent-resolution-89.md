# House Concurrent Resolution 89

> orville h. cram.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Committees on Enrolled Bills of the two Houses be authorized and directed to correct the enrolled bill of the House (H. R. 10761) entitled “An Act granting an increase of pension to Oliver H. Cram,” by striking out the words “Oliver H. Cram,” wherever they occur in title and text and inserting “Orville H. Cram.”

Passed the House February 5, 1901.

Passed the Senate February 6, 1901.

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 92

> documentary history of the constitution of the united states.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That of the document known as the Documentary History of the Constitution of the United States seven thousand copies be printed, of which number two thousand shall be for the use of the Senate, four thousand shall be for the use of the House of Representatives, and one thousand for the use of the Department of State.

Passed the Senate January 24, 1901.

Passed the House of Representatives February 9, 1901.

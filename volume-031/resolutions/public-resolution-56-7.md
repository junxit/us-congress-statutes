# Public Resolution 7

> Joint Resolution Increasing the limit of cost of the new building for the Government Printing Office, to meet the increased prices of building materials, and to permit of making the south end of the power house extension of the same height as the main building.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limit of cost of the new building for the Government Printing Office, authorized by the sundry civil act approved March third, eighteen hundred and ninety- nine, be. and hereby is, increased by four hundred and twenty-nine thousand dollars, or so much thereof as may be necessary, to meet the increased prices of building materials, and to permit of making the south end of the power house extension, for a depth of about forty-five feet from G street, northwest, of the same height as the main building.


*Approved, February 17, 1900.*

# Public Resolution 30

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the printing of additional copies of the annual report upon the improvement and care of public buildings and grounds.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be printed each year hereafter, in addition to the number of copies now authorized by law. two hundred additional copies of the annual report upon the improvement and care of public buildings and grounds, and the care and maintenance of the Washington Monument, in the District of Columbia, for the use of the officer in charge of public buildings and grounds.


*Approved, June 2, 1900.*

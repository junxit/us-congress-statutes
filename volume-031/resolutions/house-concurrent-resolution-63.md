# House Concurrent Resolution 63

> house bill no. 9083.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President of the United States be, and he is, respectfully requested to return to the House of Representatives House bill 9083.

Passed the House, June 2, 1900

Passed the Senate, June 4, 1900.

# House Concurrent Resolution 117

> reports director of mint.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be six thousand additional copies of the Report of the Director of the Mint on the production of the precious metals for the calendar year eighteen hundred and ninety-nine bound, in cloth and wrapped, two thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives, one thousand for the use of the Senate, and three thousand copies for the use of the Director of the Mint.

that there also be printed eight thousand additional copies of the Report of the Director of the Mint, covering the operations of the mints and assay offices of the United States for the fiscal year ended June thirtieth, nineteen hundred, to be bound in cloth and wrapped, two thousand copies for the use of the Senate, three thousand for the use of the House of Representatives, and three thousand for the use of the Director of the Mint.

Passed the House of Representatives February 25, 1901.

Passed the Senate March 1, 1901.

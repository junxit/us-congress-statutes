# Senate Concurrent Resolution 104

> lewis s. horsey.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Committee on Enrolled Bills is hereby authorized to correct the enrollment of the bill (S. 1203) entitled “An Act granting an increase of pension to Lewis S. Horsey,” by striking out the words “Company K, Nineteenth Regiment Iowa Infantry Volunteers” and inserting in lieu thereof the words “Company D, Fourth Regiment Iowa Volunteer Cavalry.”

Passed the Senate February 23, 1901.

Passed the House of Representatives February 25, 1901.

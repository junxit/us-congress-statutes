# Chapter 101

> An Act Relating to the accounts of United States marshals and clerks of the district courts of the Territory of Utah.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States marshals and the clerks of the district courts of the Territory of Utah prior to its admission to the Union as a State shall be held accountable only for fees earned in United States cases, in accordance with a decision of the Attorney-General dated December second, eighteen hundred and ninety-one, and all unclosed accounts of such officers shall be settled and closed accordingly, and the fees earned in United States cases, and withheld from them, shall be paid to them out of any money not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, January 19, 1901.*

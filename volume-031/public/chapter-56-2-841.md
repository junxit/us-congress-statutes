# Chapter 841

> An Act To provide an American register for the steam yacht May.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of Navigation be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to cause the foreign-built steam yacht May, owned by a citizen of the United States, to be registered as a vessel of the United States.


*Approved, March 3, 1901.*

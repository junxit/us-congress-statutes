# Chapter 6

> An Act To amend an Act authorizing the construction of a bridge across the Mississippi River at Dubuque, Iowa, approved March sixth, nineteen hundred.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section two of an Art authorizing the construction of a bridge across the Mississippi River at Dubuque, Iowa, approved March sixth, nineteen hundred, is hereby amended by striking out the words “two hundred” and inserting in lieu thereof the words “one hundred and seventy-five.”


*Approved, December 21, 1900.*

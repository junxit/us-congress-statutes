# Chapter 811

> An Act To authorize the detail of an officer of the retired list of the Army as adjutant-general of the District of Columbia militia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States may detail as adjutant-general of the District of Columbia militia any retired officer of the Army who may be nominated to the President by the brigadier-general commanding the District of Columbia militia, said retired officer while so detailed to have the active service pay and allowances of his rank in the Regular Army.


*Approved, June 6, 1900.*

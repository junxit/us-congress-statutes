# Chapter 351

> An Act To extend the privileges of the seventh section of the immediate transportation Act to Saginaw, Michigan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, the privileges of the seventh section of the Act approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, governing the immediate transportation of dutiable merchandise without appraisement be, and they are hereby, extended to Saginaw, Michigan.


*Approved, February 11, 1901.*

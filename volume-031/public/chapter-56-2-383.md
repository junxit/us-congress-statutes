# Chapter 383

> An Act To extend the privileges of the seventh section of the immediate transportation Act to New Bedford, Massachusetts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the privileges of the seventh section of the Act approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, governing the immediate transportation of dutiable merchandise without appraisement, be, and they are hereby, extended to the port of New Bedford, Massachusetts.


*Approved, February 20, 1901.*

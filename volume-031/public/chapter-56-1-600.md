# Chapter 600

> An Act To facilitate the entry of steamships engaged in the coasting trade between Porto Rico and the Territory of Hawaii and the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of the Act of June fifth, eighteen hundred and ninety-four, entitled “An Act to facilitate the entry of steamships,” are hereby extended to steamships engaged in trading between ports of Porto Rico and the Territory of Hawaii and those of the United States.


*Approved, May 31, 1900.*

# Chapter 392

> An Act To authorize the establishment, at some point in North Carolina, of a station for the investigation of problems connected with marine fishery interests of the middle and south Atlantic coast.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of Fish and Fisheries be, and he is hereby, authorized, empowered, and directed to establish a station for the investigation of problems connected with the marine fishery interests of the middle and south Atlantic States at some point in North Carolina.

## § 2.

That for necessary surveys, erection of buildings and other structures, and for the proper equipment of said station, the sum of twelve thousand five hundred dollars, or so much thereof as may be necessary, be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, May 12, 1900.*

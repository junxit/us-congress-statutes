# Chapter 671

> An Act For the relocation of certain tracks of street railways in the District of Columbia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That whenever the Banker Hill road or Wisconsin avenue is improved by the Commissioners of the District of Columbia, the said Commissioners are authorized to permit the street railroad tracks upon said highways to.be located in the middle of the roadway, should such location be considered for the best interests of the public.


*Approved, March 1, 1901.*

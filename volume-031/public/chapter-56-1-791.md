# Chapter 791

> An Act Making appropriations for sundry civil expenses of the Government, for the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, nineteen hundred and one, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the following sums be, and the same are hereby, appropriated, for the objects hereinafter expressed, for the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, nineteen hundred and one, namely:

## coast and geodetic survey.

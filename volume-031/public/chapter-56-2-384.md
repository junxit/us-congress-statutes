# Chapter 384

> An Act To establish a light and fog station at Point Dume, Los Angeles County, California.

*Be it enacted by the Seriate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be established at Point Dume, Los Angeles County, California, a suitable light and fog-signal station, at a cost not to exceed sixty-three thousand dollars.


*Approved, February 20, 1901.*

# Chapter 485

> An Act To establish light-house and fog signal in State of Washington.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a light-house and fog signal be established and constructed at Slip Point, Clallam Bay, State of Washington; said light-house not to exceed the cost of twelve thousand five hundred dollars.


*Approved, May 19, 1900.*

# Chapter 35

> An Act Authorizing the health officer of the District of Columbia to issue a permit for the removal of the remains of the late Major-General E. O. C. Ord from Oak Hill Cemetery, District of Columbia, to the United States National Cemetery at Arlington, Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the health officer of the District of Columbia be, and he is hereby, authorized to issue a permit for the removal of the remains of the late Major-General E. O. C. Ord from Oak Hill Cemetery, District of Columbia, to the United States National Cemetery at Arlington, Virginia.


*Approved, March 8, 1900.*

# Chapter 589

> An Act To provide for officers in the customs district of Hawaii.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there shall be in the customs district of Hawaii one collector, who shall reside at Honolulu, and who shall receive a salary of four thousand dollars per annum, and such deputy collectors and other customs officers as the Secretary of the Treasury shall deem necessary.


*Approved, May 26, 1900.*

# Chapter 112

> An Act To establish light and fog stations to mark the main southern entrance of the new breakwater at Buffalo, New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be established, to mark the main southern entrance of the new breakwater at Buffalo, New York, suitable light and fog-signal stations, at a cost not to exceed forty-five thousand dollars.


*Approved, March 28, 1900.*

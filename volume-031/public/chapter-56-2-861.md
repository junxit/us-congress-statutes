# Chapter 861

> An Act To establish Lowelltown, Maine, a subport of entry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Lowelltown, Maine, be, and is hereby, established as a subport of entry in the customs collection district of Bangor, Maine.


*Approved, March 3, 1901.*

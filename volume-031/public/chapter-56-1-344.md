# Chapter 344

> An Act To amend an Act authorizing the terms of the district court of the United States for the southern district of Mississippi to be held hereafter at Biloxi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first section of chapter three hundred and fifty-one of the United States Statutes at Large, entitled “An Act authorizing the terms of the district court of the United States for the southern district of Mississippi to be held here-after at Biloxi,” approved March second, eighteen hundred and ninety-nine, be amended as follows, to wit: After the words “district court” insert the words “and circuit court.”

## § 2.

That this Act take effect from and after its passage.


*Approved, May 3, 1900.*

# Chapter 178

> An Act To establish light and fog signal at Browns Point, in Puget Sound.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a light and fog signal be established and constructed at Browns Point, on Commencement Bay, in Puget Sound, being at the entrance of the harbor of the city of Tacoma; said light and fog signal not to exceed the cost of six thousand dollars.


*Approved, April 5, 1900.*

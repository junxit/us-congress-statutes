# Chapter 817

> An Act For reestablishing the range lights on the Delaware River known as Finns Point range, Reedy Island range, and Port Penn range.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to reestablish the range lights on the Delaware River known as Finns Point range, Reedy Island range, and Port Penn range, at a cost not exceeding ninety thousand dollars.


*Approved, June 6, 1900.*

# Chapter 779

> An Act To amend an Act granting to the Muscle Shoals Power Company right to erect and construct canal and power stations at Muscle Shoals, Alabama.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

Muscle Shoals PowerCompany canal and power stations.

Vol. 30, p. 1351.

That section two of an Act entitled “An Act granting to the Muscle Shoals Power Company right to erect and construct canal and power stations at Muscle Shoals, Alabama,” approved March third, eighteen hundred and eighty-nine, be, and the same is hereby, amended so as to read as follows:

> ## § 2.
>
> That unless the work herein authorized be commenced within two years, and completed within four years from the date hereof, the privileges hereby granted shall cease and be determined.”


*Approved, June 6, 1900.*

# Chapter 489

> An Act To constitute Durham, North Carolina, a port of delivery in the customs collection district of Pamlico, and to extend the privileges of the seventh section of the Act of Congress approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, to said port.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Durham, North Carolina, be, and is hereby, constituted a port of delivery in the customs collection district of Pamlico, and the privileges of the seventh section of the Act of Congress approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, governing the immediate transportation of dutiable merchandise without appraisement, are hereby extended to said port of Durham.


*Approved, May 22, 1900.*

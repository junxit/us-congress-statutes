# Chapter 463

> An Act For the establishment of a light-house and fog signal at Point No Point, in Chesapeake Bay, between Cove Point and Smiths Point.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a light-house and fog signal be established at Point No Point, in the Chesapeake Bay, between Cove Point and Smiths Point, at a cost not to exceed the sum of sixty-five thousand dollars.


*Approved, February 23, 1901.*

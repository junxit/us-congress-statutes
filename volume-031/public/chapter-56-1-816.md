# Chapter 816

> An Act To provide an American register for the ships Star of Italy and Star of Bengal.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of Navigation is hereby authorized and directed to cause the foreign-built ship Star of Italy and foreign-built ship Star of Bengal, owned by citizens of the United States or citizens of Hawaii, to be registered as vessels of the United States.


*Approved, June 6, 1900.*

# Chapter 857

> An Act Extending to the city of Everett, Washington, a subport of entry, the privileges of the Act approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, governing the immediate transportation of dutiable merchandise.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the privileges of the Act approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, governing the immediate transportation of dutiable merchandise without appraisement, be, and the same are hereby, extended to Everett, a subport of entry in the customs collection district of Puget Sound.


*Approved, March 3, 1901.*

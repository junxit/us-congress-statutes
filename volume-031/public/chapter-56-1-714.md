# Chapter 714

> An Act To extend the privilege of immediate transportation of dutiable goods to the port of Astoria, Oregon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the privilege of immediate transportation of dutiable goods, in accordance with the provisions of an Act entitled “An Act to amend the statutes in relation to the immediate transportation of dutiable goods, and for other purposes.” approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, and the amendments thereto, be, and the same is hereby, extended to the port of Astoria, Oregon.


*Approved, June 5, 1900.*

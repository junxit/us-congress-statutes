# Chapter 92

> An Act To provide for the holding of the circuit and district courts of the United States for the eastern district of Arkansas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the regular terms of the circuit and district courts for the eastern district of Arkansas shall be held at the times and places as follows, to wit:

For the western division, at Little Rock on the first Monday in April and the third Monday in October;

For the eastern division, at Helena on the second Mondays in March and October;

For the northern division, at Batesville on the fourth Monday in May and the second Monday in December.

## § 2.

That this Act shall take effect and be in force from and after its passage.


*Approved, January 16, 1901.*

# Chapter 588

> An Act To amend an Act regulating the inspection of flour in the District of Columbia, approved December twenty-first, eighteen hundred and ninety-eight.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section six of the Act entitled “An Act regulating the inspection of flour in the District of Columbia,” approved December twenty-first, eighteen hundred and ninety-eight, be amended by striking out the last clause of said section and inserting in lieu thereof the following:

> “And no barrel, half barrel, or sack of flour not examined and branded by the inspector as aforesaid shall be sold within the District under fine of one dollar for each and every barrel, half barrel, or sack, to be collected as other fines and penalties are collected.”


*Approved, May 26, 1900.*

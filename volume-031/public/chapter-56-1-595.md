# Chapter 595

> An Act To change the characteristic of Cape Cod light, Massachusetts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to change the characteristic of Cape Cod light, situated at North Truro, Massachusetts, from a fixed white to a flashing white light, at a cost not exceeding fifteen thousand dollars.


*Approved, May 29, 1900.*

# Chapter 837

> An Act To extend the privileges of the seventh section of the immediate transportation Act to Fall River, Massachusetts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the privileges of the seventh section of the Act approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, governing the immediate transportation of dutiable merchandise without appraisement be, and they are hereby, extended to the port of Fall River, Massachusetts.


*Approved, March 3, 1901.*

# Chapter 107

> An Act To supply a deficiency in the appropriation for transcripts of records and plats in the General Land Office.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of nine thousand three hundred and fifty dollars is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to supply a deficiency in the appropriation made for the fiscal year nineteen hundred and one, for furnishing transcripts of records and plats, General Land Office, to be expended under the direction of the Secretary of the Interior: Provided, That copyists employed under this appropriation shall be selected by the Secretary of the Interior at a compensation of two dollars per day while actually employed, at such times and for such periods as exigencies of the work may demand.


*Approved, January 22, 1901.*

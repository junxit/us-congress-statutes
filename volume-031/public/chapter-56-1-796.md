# Chapter 796

> Ac Act To extend the coal land laws to the district of Alaska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United State of America in Congress assembled,*

That so much of the public land laws of the United States are hereby extended to the district of Alaska as relate to coal lands, namely, sections twenty-three hundred And forty-seven to twenty-three hundred and fifty-two. inclusive, of the Revised Statutes.


*Approved, June 6, 1900.*

# Chapter 614

> An Act An Act To amend section forty-four hundred and fourteen, title fifty-two, Revised Statutes of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section forty-four hundred and fourteen, title fifty-two, of the Revised Statutes of the United States, as amended by the Acts of Congress approved March first and second, eighteen hundred and ninety-five, be amended by inserting therein the words “Toledo, Ohio.” after the words “Mobile, Alabama.”


*Approved, June 2, 1900.*

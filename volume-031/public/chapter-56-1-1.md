# Chapter 1

> An Act To extend the time for examination of monthly accounts by bureaus and offices of the War Department.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time for examination of monthly accounts by the bureaus and offices of the War Department after the date of actual receipt and before transmitting the same to the Auditor for the War Department, as limited by section twelve of the legislative, executive, and judicial appropriation Act, approved July thirty-first, eighteen hundred and ninety-four, is hereby extended from twenty days to sixty days for the period of one year from the date of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, December 20, 1899.*

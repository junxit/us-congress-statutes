# Public Law 104–128

> An Act To eliminate the Board of Tea Experts by repealing the Tea Importation Act of 1897.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. SHORT TITLE.

This Act may be cited as the “Federal Tea Tasters Repeal Act of 1996”.

## § 2. REPEAL OF TEA IMPORTATION ACT OF 1897.

The Tea Importation Act (21 U.S.C. 41 et seq.) is repealed.

## § 3. EFFECTIVE DATE.

This Act shall take effect on the date of enactment of this Act.


*Approved April 9, 1996.*

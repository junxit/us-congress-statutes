# Public Law 104–233

> An Act To reauthorize the Indian Environmental General Assistance Program Act of 1992, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. REAUTHORIZATION.

Section 502(h) of the Indian Environmental General Assistance Program Act of 1992 (42 U.S.C. 4368b(h)) is amended by striking “$15,000,000” and inserting “such sums as may be necessary”.


*Approved October 2, 1996.*

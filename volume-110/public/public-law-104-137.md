# Public Law 104–137

> An Act To designate the United States Post Office-Courthouse located at South 6th and Rogers Avenue, Port Smith, Arkansas, as the “Judge Isaac C. Parker Federal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The United States Post Office-Courthouse located at South 6th and Rogers Avenue, Fort Smith, Arkansas, shall be known and designated as the “Judge Isaac C. Parker Federal Building”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the United States Post Office-Courthouse referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Judge Isaac C. Parker Federal Building”.


*Approved April 30, 1996.*

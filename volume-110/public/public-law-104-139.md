# Public Law 104–139

> An Act To redesignate the Federal building located at 345 Middlefield Road in Menlo Park, California, and known as the Earth Sciences and Library Building, as the “Vincent E. McKelvey Federal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. REDESIGNATION.

The Federal building located at 345 Middlefield Road, in Menlo Park, California, and known as the Earth Sciences and Library Building, shall be known and designated as the “Vincent E. McKelvey Federal Building”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Federal building referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Vincent E. McKelvey Federal Building”.


*Approved April 30, 1996.*

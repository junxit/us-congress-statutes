# Public Law 104–133

> An Act To amend the Indian Self-Determination and Education Assistance Act to extend for two months the authority for promulgating regulations under the Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. EXTENSION OF AUTHORITY TO PROMULGATE REGULATIONS.

Section 107(a)(2)(B) of the Indian Self-Determination and Education Assistance Act (25 U.S.C. 450k(a)(2)(B)) is amended by striking “18 months” and inserting “20 months”.


*Approved April 25, 1996.*

# Public Law 104–189

> An Act To redesignate the Dunning Post Office in Chicago, Illinois, as the “Roger P. McAuliffe Post Office”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. REDESIGNATION.

The Dunning Post Office, located at 6441 West Irving Park Road, Chicago, Illinois, shall be redesignated and known as the “Roger P. McAuliffe Post Office”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the United States Post Office referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Roger P. McAuliffe Post Office”.


*Approved August 20, 1996.*

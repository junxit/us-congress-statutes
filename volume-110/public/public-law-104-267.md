# Public Law 104–267

> An Act To waive temporarily the Medicaid enrollment composition rule for certain health maintenance organizations.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. WAIVER OF 75/25 MEDICAID ENROLLMENT RULE FOR CERTAIN MANAGED CARE ORGANIZATIONS.

The requirement of section 1903(m)(2)(A)(ii) of the Social Security Act is waived—

- **(1)** with respect to Catholic Health Services Plan of Brooklyn and Queens, Inc. (doing business as Fidelis Health Plan) and Managed Healthcare Systems of New York, Inc., for contract periods through January 1, 1999, and
- **(2)** with respect to Health Partners of Philadelphia, Inc., for contract periods through December 31, 1999.


*Approved October 9, 1996.*

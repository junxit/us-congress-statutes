# House Concurrent Resolution 123

> OFFICE OF COMPLIANCE PROVISIONAL REGULATIONS—APPROVAL

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

## § 1. APPROVAL OF REGULATIONS.

The regulations applicable to employing offices which are not the House of Representatives or the Senate and covered employees who are not the employees of the House of Representatives or the Senate which are to be issued by the Office of Compliance before January 23, 1996, are hereby approved on a provisional basis until such time as such regulations are approved in accordance with section 304(c) of the Congressional Accountability Act of 1995 (2 U.S.C. 1384(c)).


*Agreed to January 22, 1996.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 49

> ENROLLMENT CORRECTIONS—H.R. 2854

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Clerk of the House of Representatives, in the enrollment of the bill (H.R. 2854) to modify the operation of certain agricultural programs, shall make the following corrections:

In section 215—

- **(1)** in paragraph (1), insert “and” at the end;
- **(2)** in paragraph (2), strike “and” at the end and insert a period; and
- **(3)** strike paragraph (3).


*Agreed to March 28, 1996.*

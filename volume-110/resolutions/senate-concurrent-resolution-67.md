# Senate Concurrent Resolution 67

> COMMISSION ON PROTECTING AND REDUCING GOVERNMENT SECRECY REPORT—SENATE PRINT

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there shall be printed as a Senate document the report of the Commission on Protecting and Reducing Government Secrecy.

## § 2.

The document referred to in the first section shall

- **(1)** published under the supervision of the Secretary of the Senate; and
- **(2)** in such style, form, manner, and binding as directed by the Joint Committee on Printing, after consultation with the Secretary of the Senate. The document shall include illustrations.

## § 3.

In addition to the usual number of copies of the document, there shall be printed the lesser of—

- **(1)** 5,000 copies for the use of the Secretary of the Senate; or
- **(2)** such number of copies as does not exceed a total production and printing cost of $45,000.


*Agreed to September 26, 1996.*

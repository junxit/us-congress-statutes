# Senate Concurrent Resolution 63

> LIVESTOCK PRODUCERS—DISASTER ASSISTANCE

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

## § 1. USE OF DISASTER RESERVE FOR ASSISTANCE TO LIVE-STOCK PRODUCERS.

In light of the prolonged drought and other adverse weather conditions existing in certain areas of the United States, the Secretary of Agriculture should promptly dispose of all commodities in the disaster reserve maintained under section 813 of the Agricultural Act of 1970 (7 U.S.C. 1427a) to relieve the distress of livestock producers whose ability to maintain livestock is adversely affected by disaster conditions, such as prolonged drought or flooding.


*Agreed to June 12, 1996.*

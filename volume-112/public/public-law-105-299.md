# Public Law 105–299

> An Act To designate a Federal building located in Florence, Alabama, as the “Justice John McKinley Federal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION OF JUSTICE JOHN MCKINLEY FEDERAL BUILDING.

The Federal building located at 210 North Seminary Street in Florence, Alabama, shall be known and designated as the “Justice John McKinley Federal Building”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Federal building referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Justice John McKinley Federal Building”.


*Approved October 27, 1998.*

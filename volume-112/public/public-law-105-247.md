# Public Law 105–247

> An Act To correct a provision relating to termination of benefits for convicted persons.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. CORRECTION.

Section 8148(a) of title 5, United States Code, is amended by striking “a receipt” and inserting “or receipt”.


*Approved October 9, 1998.*

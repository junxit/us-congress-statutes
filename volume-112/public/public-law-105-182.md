# Public Law 105–182

> An Act To extend the legislative authority for the Board of Regents of Gunston Hall to establish a memorial to honor George Mason.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,,*

## § 1. EXTENTION OF LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY FOR MEMORIAL ESTABLISHMENT.

The legislative authority for the Board of Regents of Gunston Hall to establish a commemorative work (as defined by section 2 of the Commemorative Works Act (40 U.S.C. 1002)) shall expire August 10, 2000, notwithstanding the time period limitation specified in section 10(b) of the Commemorative Works Act (40 U.S.C. 1010(b)).


*Approved June 19, 1998.*

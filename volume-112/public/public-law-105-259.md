# Public Law 105–259

> An Act To extend the date by which an automated entry–exit control system must be developed.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. EXTENSION OF DATE FOR DEVELOPMENT OF AUTOMATED ENTRY–EXIT CONTROL SYSTEM.

Section 110 of division C of Public Law 104–208 is amended by striking “2 years after the date of enactment of this Act” and inserting “October 15, 1998”.


*Approved October 15, 1998.*

# Public Law 105–345

> An Act To amend the Omnibus Parks and Public Lands Management Act of 1996 to extend the legislative authority for the Black Patriots Foundation to establish a commemorative work.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. BLACK REVOLUTIONARY WAR PATRIOTS MEMORIAL.

Section 506 of the Omnibus Parks and Public Lands Management Act of 1996 (40 U.S.C. 1003 note; 110 Stat. 4155) is amended by striking “1998” and inserting “2000”.


*Approved November 2, 1998.*

# Chapter 446

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Meshack L. Jones.

*Be it enacted by the Seriate and House of Representatives of the United Staten of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Meshack L. Jones, late of Company F, Seventh Regiment Illinois Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 19, 1906.*

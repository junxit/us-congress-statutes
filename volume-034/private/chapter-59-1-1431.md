# Chapter 1431

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Thomas Patterson.

*Be it exacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisons and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Thomas Patterson, late of Company A, Tenth Regiment Iowa Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, April 11, 1906.*

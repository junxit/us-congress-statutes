# Chapter 1917

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Isaiah Perkins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representative of the United Staten of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws. the name of Isaiah Perkins, late of Company E. Second Regiment New York Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 26, 1907.*

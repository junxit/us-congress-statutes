# Chapter 529

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Sabastian Laudner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sabastian Laudner, late of Company H, Fifth Regiment, and Company II, Sixth Regiment, West Virginia Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 7, 1906.*

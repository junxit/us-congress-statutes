# Chapter 1710

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Frank Osterberg, alias William McKay.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Frank Osterberg, alias William McKay, late of the U. S. S. Dacotah, Alleghany, and Cyane, United States Navy, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lien of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, April 23, 1906.*

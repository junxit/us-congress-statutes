# Chapter 1011

> An Act Granting a pension to Mary Louise McLean.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Louise McLean, widow of Nathaniel C. McLean, late brigadier-general, United States Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 19, 1907.*

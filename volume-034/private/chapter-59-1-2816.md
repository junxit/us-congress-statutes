# Chapter 2816

> An Act Granting a pension to Isaac Cope.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Isaac Cope, helpless and dependent son of Edmund Cope, late of Company K, Forty-seventh Regiment Indiana Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, June 6, 1906.*

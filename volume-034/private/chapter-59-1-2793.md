# Chapter 2793

> An Act Granting a pension to Catharine Arnold.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Catharine Arnold, widow of Aiming W. Arnold, ate of Company B, Forty-fourth Regiment, and Company B, Seventy-third Regiment, New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, June 6, 1906.*

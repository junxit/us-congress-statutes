# Chapter 1985

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Mary Evans.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Evans, widow of Henry J. Evans, late of Company G, Twenty-seventh Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving: Provided, That in the event of the death of Jessie F. Evans, helpless and dependent child of said Henry J. Evans, the additional pension herein granted shall cease and determine: And provided further, That in the event of the death of Mary Evans the name of said Jessie F. Evans shall be placed on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, at the rate of twelve dollars per month from and after the date of death of said Mary Evans.


*Approved, February 26, 1907.*

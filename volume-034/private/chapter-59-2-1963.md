# Chapter 1963

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Luther W. Harris.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Luther W. Harris, late of Company B, Seventh Regiment Missouri Volunteer Cavalry, and Company II, Seventy- second Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per mouth in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 26, 1907.*

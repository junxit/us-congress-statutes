# Chapter 2093

> An Act Granting a pension to Robert Kennish.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Robert Kennish, late mate United States ship Restless, United States Navy, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, May 7, 1906.*

# Chapter 1278

> An Act Granting a pension to Jesse Harral.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jesse Harral, late of Captain H. W. Cooke’s company, Third Battalion Tennessee Infantry, Cherokee Indian disturbances, and pay him a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1907.*

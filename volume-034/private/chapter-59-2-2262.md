# Chapter 2262

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Henrietta Teague.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Henrietta Teague, widow of George H. Teague, late captain Company B, First Regiment Massachusetts Volunteer Cavalry, and Company B, Fifth Regiment Massachusetts Colored Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 28, 1907.*

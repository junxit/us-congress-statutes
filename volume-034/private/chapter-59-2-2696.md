# Chapter 2696

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to James M. Dick.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of James M. Dick, late of Company II, Second Regiment Iowa Volunteer Infantry, and captain Company A, Twenty-fifth Regiment Iowa Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 2, 1907.*

# Chapter 697

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Joseph L. Topham.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Joseph L. Topham, late of Company G, Fourth Regiment Connecticut Volunteer Infantry, commissary sergeant Forty- third Regiment Pennsylvania Emergency Militia, and second lieutenant Company A, Second Regiment New Jersey Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 6, 1907.*

# Chapter 3078

> An Act To correct the military record of Benjamin F. Graham.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to grant to Benjamin F. Graham, an assistant surgeon of the One hundred and ninety-fourth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, an honorable discharge as of date October eighteenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-five: Provided, That no pay, bounty, or other emoluments shall become due or payable by virtue of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 11, 1906.*

# Chapter 2889

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to North Ann Dorman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of North Ann Dorman, widow of Ephraim D. Dorman, late of Company G, Second Regiment Maine Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, June 6, 1906.*

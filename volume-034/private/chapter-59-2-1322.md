# Chapter 1322

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Anna F. Burlingame.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Anna F. Burlingame, former widow of Walter H. Johnson, late of Company D, Third Regiment Rhode Island Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 25, 1907.*

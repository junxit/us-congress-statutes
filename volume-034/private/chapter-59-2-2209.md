# Chapter 2209

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Harvey Key.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Harvey Key, late of Company F, Sixth Regiment Kentucky Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him it pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 28, 1907.*

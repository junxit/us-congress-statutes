# Chapter 1965

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Anna Frances Hall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Anna Frances Hall, widow of Caleb Hall, late of Company E, Seventh Regiment Rhode Island Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, April 26, 1906.*

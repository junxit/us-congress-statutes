# Chapter 1649

> An Act For the relief of Zenas Parker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster-General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to refund to Zenas Parker, late postmaster at Stewart, Houston County, Tennessee, the sum of one hundred and sixty-one dollars and seventy-three cents, being the amount of funds belonging to the Post-Office Department of the United States stolen from the said Zenas Parker by burglars December twenty-fourth, nineteen hundred and two.


*Approved, April 21, 1906.*

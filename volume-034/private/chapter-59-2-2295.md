# Chapter 2295

> An Act Authorizing the Secretary of the Navy to grant a discharge to Peter O’Neil.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to grant a discharge to Peter O’Neil, late a member of United States Navy.


*Approved, March 1, 1907.*

# Chapter 1914

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Alexander Esler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Alexander Esler, late of Captain Boyd’s company, District of Columbia Volunteer Militia, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, April 26, 1906.*

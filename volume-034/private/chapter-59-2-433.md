# Chapter 433

> An Act For the relief of Matthew J. Davis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of one thousand five hundred and eighty-eight dollars and twenty-four cents be, and is hereby, appropriated to Matthew J. Davis, for damages to his schooner Lillie, caused by fire on the twenty-first day of January, nineteen hundred and two. while she was in charge of the United States quarantine officer at Ship Island, near Biloxi, Mississippi.


*Approved, January 29, 1907.*

# Chapter 759

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Charles Washburn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Charles Washburn, late first lieutenant Company A, Thirty-fifth Regiment Iowa Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that be is now receiving.


*Approved, March 12, 1906.*

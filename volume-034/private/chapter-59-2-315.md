# Chapter 315

> An Act Granting a pension to Emily J. Sherman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Emily J. Sherman, helpless and dependent daughter of Nathan P. Sherman, late of Company E, Fourteenth Regiment Vermont Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, January 21, 1907.*

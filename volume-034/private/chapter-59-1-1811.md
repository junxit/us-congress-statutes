# Chapter 1811

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Zacur P. Pott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Zacur P. Pott, late of Company M, First Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Cavalry, and captain Company C, One hundred and ninety-fourth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, April 28, 1906.*

# Chapter 1282

> An Act Granting a pension to Maria Elizabeth Posey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Maria Elizabeth Posey, helpless and dependent daughter of Carnot Posey, late lieutenant, Company B, First Regiment Mississippi Volunteers, war with Mexico, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, March 26, 1906.*

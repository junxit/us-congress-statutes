# Chapter 3028

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Mary H. Scott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name, of Mary H. Scott, widow of Albert B. Scott, late captain, Thirteenth Regiment United States Infantry, war with Spain, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving and two dollars per month additional on account of each of the minor children of said Albert B. Scott until they reach the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, June 6, 1906.*

# Chapter 2104

> An Act For the relief of Wiley Corbett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Treasurer of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to pay, out of any funds in the United States Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of three hundred and three dollars and thirty-eight cents to Wiley Corbett on account of unused revenue stamps for whisky produced by the said Corbett and destroyed by fire before such stamps were issued.


*Approved, February 27, 1907.*

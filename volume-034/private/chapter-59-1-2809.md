# Chapter 2809

> An Act Granting a pension to Harriet A. Morton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House- of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Harriet A. Morton, widow of Robert S. Morton, late chaplain Seventeenth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, June 6, 1906.*

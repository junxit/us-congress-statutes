# Chapter 2897

> An Act To provide for the repayment of certain customs dues.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of one hundred and eleven dollars and seventy cents is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the repayment to John Effinger, or his legal representative, of the customs duties wrongfully collected at the port of Honolulu, in the Territory of Hawaii, on entries numbered one hundred and eighty-five, three hundred and fifty-seven, four hundred and eighty, eight hundred and ninety-six, eleven hundred and fifty-four, and twenty-seven hundred and five, in the months of February, April, July. August, September, and October, of the year nineteen hundred and one, made by John Effinger.


*Approved, March 2, 1907.*

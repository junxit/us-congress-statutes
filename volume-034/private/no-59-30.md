# No. 30

> Joint Resolution Granting permission to Rear-Admiral B. H. McCalla to accept a medal from the King of Great Britain, and the Order of the Red Eagle from the Emperor of Germany.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That permission is hereby granted to Rear-Admiral B. H. McCalla, United States Navy, to accept the China war medal, with Pekin clasp, tendered to him by the, King of Great Britain, and the Order of the Red Eagle, with swords, tendered to him by the Emperor of Germany.


*Approved, March 4, 1907.*

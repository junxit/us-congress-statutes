# Chapter 2836

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Elizabeth Wolfe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elizabeth Wolfe, widow of Joshua B. Wolfe, late of Company A, Battalion of Missouri Volunteer Light Artillery, war with Mexico, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 2, 1907.*

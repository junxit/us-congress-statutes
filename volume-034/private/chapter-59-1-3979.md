# Chapter 3979

> An Act Granting a pension to Mary Whisler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Whisler, helpless and dependent daughter of George W. Whisler, late of Company F, Second Regiment Missouri Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month.


*Approved, June 30, 1906.*

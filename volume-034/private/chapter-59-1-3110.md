# Chapter 3110

> An Act Granting a pension to Harriet M. Smithers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Harriet M. Smithers, former widow of William E. Wood, late musician in the band of the Seventieth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, June 11, 1906.*

# Chapter 572

> An Act Granting a pension to Elizabeth Cleaver.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elizabeth Cleaver, widow of Shesh B. Cleaver, late deputy provost marshal first district of Iowa, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 6, 1907.*

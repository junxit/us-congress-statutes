# Chapter 1217

> An Act Granting a pension to Helen Goll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Helen Goll, widow of Charles Goll, late sergeant, first class, Hospital Corps, United States Army, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month, and two dollars per month additional on account of each of the minor children of said Charles Goll until they reach the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, February 25, 1907.*

# Chapter 3978

> An Act For the relief of Taylor Ware.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Taylor Ware be held and considered to have been honorably discharged as a private of Company D, Fifty-second United States Colored Infantry Volunteers, as of date of July seventeenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, and the Secretary of War is hereby authorized to issue a discharge in accordance with this Act: Provided, That no pay, bounty, or other emoluments shall accrue by virtue of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 30, 1906.*

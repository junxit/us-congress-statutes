# Chapter 2604

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Maria Lorch.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Maria Lorch, widow of Henry Lorch, late of Company C, Fifty-second Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and Company A, Twenty-eighth Regiment New York National Guard Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 2, 1907.*

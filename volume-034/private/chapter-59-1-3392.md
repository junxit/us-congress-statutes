# Chapter 3392

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Henry Clay Sloan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Henry Clay Sloan, late first lieutenant Company I. Forty-eighth Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer Infantry, and first lieutenant, Fourth Regiment United States Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, June 18, 1906.*

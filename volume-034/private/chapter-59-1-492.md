# Chapter 492

> An Act Granting a pension to Marion H. Long.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Marion II. Long, widow of John C. Long, late first lieutenant and adjutant, One hundred and forty-seventh Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month.


*Approved, February 19, 1906.*

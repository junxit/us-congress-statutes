# Chapter 2585

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to James C. Strong.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of James C. Strong, late lieutenant-colonel Thirty-eighth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of seventy-two dollars per month in lieu of that be is now receiving.


*Approved, March 2, 1907.*

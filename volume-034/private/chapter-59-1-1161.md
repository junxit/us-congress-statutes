# Chapter 1161

> An Act Granting a pension to Emma C. Anderson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Emma C. Anderson, widow of Carl A. Anderson, late of Company G, Fourteenth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 26, 1906.*

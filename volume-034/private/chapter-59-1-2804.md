# Chapter 2804

> An Act Granting a pension to Rhoda Munsil.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Rhoda Munsil, widow of Aaron Munsil, late of First Independent Company Michigan Volunteer Sharpshooters, attached to Twenty-seventh Regiment Michigan Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, June 6, 1906.*

# Chapter 1149

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Jonathan Daughenbaugh.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jonathan Daughenbaugh, late of Company D, Forty-sixth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 26, 1906.*

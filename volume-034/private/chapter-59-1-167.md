# Chapter 167

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to George H. Hodges.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior he, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George 11. Hodges, late captain Company G, One hundred and eighty-seventh Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 10, 1900.*

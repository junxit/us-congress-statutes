# Chapter 1136

> An Act Granting a pension to Galon S. Clevenger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Galon S. Clevenger, late chaplain Third Regiment United States Volunteer Cavalry, war with Spain.


*Approved, March 26, 1906.*

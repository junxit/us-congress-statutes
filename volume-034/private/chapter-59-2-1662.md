# Chapter 1662

> An Act Granting a pension to Nannie T. Johnson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Nannie T. Johnson, dependent mother of Cave Johnson, late first lieutenant Company H, First Regiment Tennessee Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain, and pay her a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month.


*Approved, February 26, 1907.*

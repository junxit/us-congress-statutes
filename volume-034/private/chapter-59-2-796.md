# Chapter 796

> An Act Granting a pension to Howard William Archer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Howard William Archer, helpless and dependent son of Oscar F. Archer, late of Company E, Eighth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 6, 1907.*

# Chapter 902

> An Act For the payment of Robert D. Benedict for services rendered.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid to Robert D. Benedict, of the Borough of Brooklyn, city of New York, State of New York, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of two hundred and fifty dollars in full payment and discharge of the claim of said Robert D. Benedict, for legal services rendered by him in a legal proceeding taken by the clerk of the district court of the United States for the southern district of New York, for an injunction to prevent said clerk from being deprived of, the necessary accommodations for the records of said court.


*Approved, February 8, 1907.*

# Chapter 1338

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Nancy F. Shelton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Nancy F. Shelton, widow of William A. Shelton, ate captain Company D, First Regiment Missouri State Militia Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 26, 1906.*

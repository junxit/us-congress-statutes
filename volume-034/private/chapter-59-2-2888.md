# Chapter 2888

> An Act For the relief of Captain William N. Hughes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Captain William N. Hughes, United States Army, retired, detailed July fourteenth, nineteen hundred and two, for duty at East Florida Seminary, Gainesville, Florida, shall be held and considered as having been detailed under the Act of November third, eighteen hundred and ninety-three.


*Approved, March 2, 1907.*

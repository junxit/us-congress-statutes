# Chapter 707

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Sarah E. Hoffman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah E. Hoffman, widow of Lafayette Hoffman, late of Company I, One hundred and seventy-ninth Regiment, and Company F, Eighteenth Regiment, Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving: Provided, That in the event of the death of Josephine Hoffman, helpless and dependent child of said Lafayette Hoffman, the additional pension herein granted shall cease and determine: And provided further, That in the event of the death of Sarah E. Hoffman, the name of said Josephine Hoffman shall be placed on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, at the rate of twelve dollars per month from and after the date of death of said Sarah E. Hoffman.


*Approved, February 6, 1907.*

# Chapter 1917

> An Act Granting a pension to Kate E. Young.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Kate E. Young, widow of George W. Young, late of ram Lioness, Mississippi Marine Brigade, United States Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month, and two dollars per month additional on account of the minor child of said George W. Young until she reaches the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, April 26, 1906.*

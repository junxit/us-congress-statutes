# Chapter 882

> An Act For the relief of J. J. L. Peel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to pay to J. J. L. Peel, of Spokane, Washington, the sum of one thousand and forty- one dollars and seventy-six cents, being the amount advanced by him for necessary clerk hire and expenses while acting as postmaster at Spokane, Washington.


*Approved, February 7, 1907.*

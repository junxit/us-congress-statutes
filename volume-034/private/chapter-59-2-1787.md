# Chapter 1787

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Elizabeth Balew.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elizabeth Balew, widow of George M. Balew, late of Captain Angel’s company, North Carolina Volunteers, Cherokee Indian disturbance, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she in now receiving.


*Approved, February 26, 1907.*

# Chapter 2034

> An Act Granting a pension to Hannah E. Wilmer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Hannah E. Wilmer, widow of Edwin Wilmer, late colonel Sixth Regiment Delaware Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, April 27, 1906.*

# Chapter 535

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Amos Snyder.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby; authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Amos Snyder, late of Company B, Seventy-sixth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 6, 1907.*

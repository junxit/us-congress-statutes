# Chapter 2814

> An Act Granting a pension to Emily Compton.

*Be it enacted try the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Emily Compton, widow of Henry Compton, late of Company F, Third Regiment Missouri Volunteer Infantry, war with Mexico, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, June 6, 1906.*

# Chapter 355

> An Act Granting a pension to David Wilborn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roil, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of David Wilborn, late of Company A, Ninth Battalion Ohio Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain, and pay him a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, January 21, 1907.*

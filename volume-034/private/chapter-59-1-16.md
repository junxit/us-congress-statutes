# Chapter 16

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Benjamin F. Bean.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior he, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Benjamin F. Bean, late captain Company I, One hundred and twenty-ninth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and Company B, Thirty-fourth Regiment Pennsylvania Emergency Militia, and pay him a pension of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 2, 1906.*

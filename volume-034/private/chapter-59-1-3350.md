# Chapter 3350

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Sarah A. Petherbridge.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah A. Petherbridge, widow of Edward R. Pether-bridge, late of Captain Tilghman’s company, Maryland Light Artillery of District of Columbia and Maryland Volunteers, war with Mexico, and major, Purnell Legion. Maryland Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, June 18, 1906.*

# Chapter 3835

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Mary M. Stone.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary M. Stone, widow of Roy Stone, late colonel One hundred and forty-ninth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry. and brigadier general, United States Volunteers, war with Spain, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, June 29, 1906.*

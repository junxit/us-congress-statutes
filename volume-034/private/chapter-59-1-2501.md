# Chapter 2501

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Harlan P. Cobb.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Staten of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Harlan P. Cobb, late of Company B. Battalion United States Engineers, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, May 21, 1906.*

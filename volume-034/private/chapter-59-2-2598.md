# Chapter 2598

> An Act Granting a pension to Emil S. Weisse.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Emil S. Weisse, late of Company G, Second Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain.


*Approved, March 2, 1907.*

# Chapter 687

> An Act Granting a pension to Morris Osborn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Morris Osborn, dependent father of William L. Osborn, late first lieutenant Company B. Eleventh Regiment Michigan Volunteer Cavalry, and Company G, Eighth Regiment Michigan Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 12, 1906.*

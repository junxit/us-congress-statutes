# Chapter 958

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Anna Williams.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Anna Williams, widow of John C. Williams, late of Company C, One hundredth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and Company B, Third Regiment Veteran Reserve Corps, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 18, 1907.*

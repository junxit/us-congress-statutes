# Chapter 840

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to George G. Brail.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George G. Brail, late of Company G, Sixty-fourth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that tie is now deceiving.


*Approved, March 12, 1906.*

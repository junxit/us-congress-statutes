# Chapter 1076

> An Act Granting a pension to Oscar F. Renick.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Oscar F. Renick, acting assistant surgeon, United States Army, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 19, 1906.*

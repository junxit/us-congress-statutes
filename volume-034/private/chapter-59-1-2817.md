# Chapter 2817

> An Act Granting a pension to Margaret Stevens.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Margaret Stevens, widow of Joshua Stevens, late of Company I, Ninety-eighth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, June 6, 1906.*

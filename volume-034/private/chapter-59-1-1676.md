# Chapter 1676

> An Act Granting a pension to Cora F. Mitchell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Cora F. Mitchell, helpless and dependent daughter of Seth W. Mitchell, late of Company C, Thirty-eighth Regiment Massachusetts Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, April 23, 1906.*

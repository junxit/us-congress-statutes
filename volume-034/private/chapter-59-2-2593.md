# Chapter 2593

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Mary S. Platt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary S. Platt, widow of Henry A. Platt, late first lieutenant and quartermaster First Territorial Regiment United States Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving, and two dollars per month for the minor child of the said officer until she shall attain the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, March 2, 1907.*

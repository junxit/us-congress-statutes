# Chapter 1286

> An Act Granting a pension to Emma C. Aikin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the [tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Emma C. Aikin, widow of Calvin N. Aikin. late of Company B. Twenty-fourth Regiment Michigan Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1907.*

# Chapter 1740

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to S. Amanda Mansfield.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of S. Amanda Mansfield, widow of Henry O. Mansfield, late captain Company E, Fifty-second Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, April 23, 1906.*

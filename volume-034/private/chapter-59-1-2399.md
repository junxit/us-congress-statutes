# Chapter 2399

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to William Hoaglin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roil, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William Hoaglin, late of Company G, Second Regiment New York Veteran Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, May 8, 1906.*

# Chapter 3132

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Eliza Rogers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Eliza Rogers, widow of Michael Rogers, late of United States Marine Corps, and pay her a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lien of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, June 11, 1906.*

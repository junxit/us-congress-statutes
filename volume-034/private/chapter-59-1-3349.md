# Chapter 3349

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to George W. Hyde.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George W. Hyde, late of Company K, First Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer Infantry, and One hundred and Forty-ninth Company, Second Battalion, Veteran Reserve Corps, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, June 18, 1906.*

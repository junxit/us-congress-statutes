# Chapter 989

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Bettie May Vose.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Bettie May Vose, widow of William P. Vose, late colonel, Artillery Corps, and brigadier-general, United States Army, retired, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 18, 1907.*

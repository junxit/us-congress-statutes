# Chapter 3405

> An Act Granting a pension to Florence H. Godfrey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Florence H. Godfrey, widow of Guy C. M. Godfrey, late captain and assistant surgeon. United States Army, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month, and two dollars per month additional on account of the minor child of the said Guy C. M. Godfrey until he reaches the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, June 18, 1906.*

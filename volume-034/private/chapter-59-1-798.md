# Chapter 798

> An Act Granting a pension to George Larson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George Larson, late of Company D, Fifth Regiment United States Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 12, 1906.*

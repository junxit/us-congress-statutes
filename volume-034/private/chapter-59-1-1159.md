# Chapter 1159

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Joseph Baker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject, to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Joseph Baker, late of Battery B, First Regiment New Jersey Volunteer Light Artillery, and pay him a pension at the rate, of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 26, 1906.*

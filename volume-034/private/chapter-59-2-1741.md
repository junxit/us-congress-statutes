# Chapter 1741

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Theron Cross.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Theron Cross, late first lieutenant Company B, One hundred and thirty-sixth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, anti pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 26, 1907.*

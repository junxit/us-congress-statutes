# Chapter 259

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Ezekiel Morrill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ezekiel Morrill, late assistant surgeon Thirteenth Regiment New Hampshire Volunteer Infantry and surgeon First Regiment New Hampshire Volunteer Heavy Artillery, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, January 18, 1907.*

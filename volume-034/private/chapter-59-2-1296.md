# Chapter 1296

> An Act Granting a pension to Celestia E. Outlaw.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Celestia E. Outlaw, widow of Young P. Outlaw, late of Captain Graham’s company, Georgia Volunteers, Florida Seminole Indian war. and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1907.*

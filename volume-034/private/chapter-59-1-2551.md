# Chapter 2551

> An Act Granting a pension to Lorenzo E. Johnson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lorenzo E. Johnson, late acting assistant surgeon, United States Army, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, May 21, 1906.*

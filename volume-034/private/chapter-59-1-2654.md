# Chapter 2654

> An Act Granting a pension to August Crome.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of August Crome, late of Company G, Tenth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, June 6, 1906.*

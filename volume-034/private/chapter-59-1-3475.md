# Chapter 3475

> An Act Granting a pension to Cornelius Sullivan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Cornelius Sullivan, late of United States steamship Iris, United States Navy, war with Mexico, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, June 20, 1906.*

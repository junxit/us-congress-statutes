# Chapter 1743

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to George W. Cassie.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the [tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George W. Cassie, late of Company E, Sixty-sixth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of forty-live dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 26, 1907.*

# Chapter 1021

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Sarah A. Tyler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of American Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior he, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah A. Tyler, widow of William H. H. Tyler, late of Company G, Third Regiment New Hampshire Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 19, 1906.*

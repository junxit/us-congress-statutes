# Chapter 1595

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Anna H. Wagner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and, House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Anna H. Wagner, widow of Arthur L. Wagner, late colonel and military secretary, General Staff, United States Army, and pay her a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving, and two dollars per month additional on account of each of the minor children of said Arthur L. Wagner until they reach the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, April 11, 1906.*

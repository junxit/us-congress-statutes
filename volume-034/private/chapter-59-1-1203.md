# Chapter 1203

> An Act Granting a pension to Alice Rourk.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Alice Rourk, widow of Francis Rourk, late acting assistant surgeon. United States Army, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, March 26, 1906.*

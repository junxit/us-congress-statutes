# Chapter 2117

> An Act For the relief of Second Lieutenant Gouverneur V. Packer, Twenty-fourth United States Infantry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to cause to be credited to Second Lieutenant Gouverneur V. Packer, battalion quartermaster and commissary, Twenty-fourth Regiment United States Infantry, on his accounts as acting commissary of subsistence of the post of Fort Missoula, Montana, with the sum of forty-eight dollars and eighty-nine cents, the amount of subsistence funds, for which he was responsible, stolen by Commissary-Sergeant Walter E. Smith, United States Army, who deserted from the service September first, nineteen hundred and three.


*Approved, February 28, 1907.*

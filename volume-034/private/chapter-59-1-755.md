# Chapter 755

> An Act Granting a pension to Eva B. Koch.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the penson laws, the name of Eva B. Koch, widow of Christian Koch, alias Christian Cook, late of Company E, Seventh Regiment United States Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 12, 1906.*

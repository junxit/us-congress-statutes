# Chapter 3980

> An Act To indemnify Edgar P. Swett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to issue to Edgar P. Swett a patent for one hundred and sixty acres of contiguous, nonmineral public land, subject to homestead entry, to be selected by the said Edgar P. Swett within two years of the passage of this Act, to indemnify said Swett on account of a previous entry made by him which was held invalid.


*Approved, June 30, 1906.*

# Chapter 1505

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Eliza A. Hughes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Eliza A. Hughes, widow of John A. Hughes, late captain Company H, Forty-sixth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 25, 1907.*

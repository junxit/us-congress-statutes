# Chapter 770

> An Act Granting a pension to Glawvina A. Pinnell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Glawvina A. Pinnell, widow of Willis O. Pinnell, late captain Company H, Seventy-ninth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, March 12, 1906.*

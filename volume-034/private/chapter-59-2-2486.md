# Chapter 2486

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Maria L. Philbrick.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Maria L. Philbrick, widow of Chase Philbrick, late lieutenant-colonel Fifteenth Regiment Massachusetts Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 1, 1907.*

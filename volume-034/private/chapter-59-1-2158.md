# Chapter 2158

> An Act Granting a pension to William I. Lusch.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William I. Lusch, late of Company B, Eighth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty-six dollars per month.


*Approved, May 7, 1906.*

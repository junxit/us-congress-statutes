# Chapter 724

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Sallie H. Murphy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sallie H. Murphy, widow of John Murphy, late first lieutenant Company D, Fortieth Regiment Indiana Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 12, 1906.*

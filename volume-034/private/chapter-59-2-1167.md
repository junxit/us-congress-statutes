# Chapter 1167

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Jackson Mays.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jackson Mays, late of Company E, Sixth Regiment Kansas Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twentyfour dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 20, 1907.*

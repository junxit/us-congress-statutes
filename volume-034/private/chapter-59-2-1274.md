# Chapter 1274

> An Act Granting a pension to Mary Kaisted.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Kaisted, widow of Jasper Kaisted, late unassigned,


*Approved, February 25, 1907.*

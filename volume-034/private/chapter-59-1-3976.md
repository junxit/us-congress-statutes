# Chapter 3976

> An Act Granting an honorable discharge to Seth Davis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Seth Davis shall here-after be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a first lieutenant, Company E, Fourth Regiment Michigan Volunteer Infantry, on April twenty-third, eighteen hundred and sixty-six: Provided, That no pay, bounty, or other emoluments shall become due or payable by virtue of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 30, 1906.*

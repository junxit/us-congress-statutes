# Chapter 2882

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to David Ross.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, anti he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roil, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of David Ross, late of Company D, First Regiment Kentucky Volunteer Cavalry, war with Mexico, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, June 6, 1906.*

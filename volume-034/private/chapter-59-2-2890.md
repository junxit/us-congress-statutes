# Chapter 2890

> An Act Authorizing the President to appoint Webb C. Maglathlin a second assistant engineer in the Revenue-Cutter Service.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be, and is hereby, authorized to appoint Webb C. Maglathlin a second assistant engineer in the United States Revenue-Cutter Service upon the occurring of the first vacancy after the passage of this Act in the grade of second assistant engineer in said Service: Provided, That the said Webb C. Maglathlin passes satisfactorily the physical examination required by the regulations of that Service.


*Approved, March 2, 1907.*

# Chapter 242

> An Act Granting a pension William I. Heed.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William I. Heed, late of Troop I, Second Regiment United States Cavalry.


*Approved, February 10, 1906.*

# Chapter 778

> An Act Granting a pension to John J. Meeler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John J. Meeler, late of Company L, Second Regiment Arkansas Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain.


*Approved, March 12, 1906.*

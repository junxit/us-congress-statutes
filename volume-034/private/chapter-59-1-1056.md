# Chapter 1056

> An Act Granting a pension to Marcelina S. Groff.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Marcelina S. Groff, widow of J. J. Groff, late scout and guide. United States Army, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month and two dollars per month additional on account of the minor child of said J. J. Groff until he reaches the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, March 19, 1906.*

# Chapter 2204

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Susan Mahany.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Susan Mahany, widow of Michael Mahany, late of Company M, Tenth Regiment New York Volunteer Cavalry, and Signal Corps, United States Army, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving, and two dollars per month additional on account of each of the minor children of the said Michael Mahany until they reach the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, February 28, 1907.*

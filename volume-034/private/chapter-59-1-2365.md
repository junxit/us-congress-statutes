# Chapter 2365

> An Act Granting a pension to Clara F Leslie.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Clara F. Leslie, widow of John A. Leslie, late first lieutenant and adjutant Two hundred and third Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, May 8, 1906.*

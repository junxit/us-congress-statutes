# Chapter 3304

> An Act Granting a pension to Annis Bailey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Annis Bailey, former widow of Abram R. Ward, ate of Company K, Thirteenth Regiment Michigan Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, June 14, 1906.*

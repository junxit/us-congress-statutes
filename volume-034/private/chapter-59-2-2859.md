# Chapter 2859

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Polly Ann Bowman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension, laws, the name of Polly Ann Bowman, widow of John Bowman, late of Captain H. Garmeny’s company, First Regiment Georgia Mounted Volunteers. Creek Indian war, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 2, 1907.*

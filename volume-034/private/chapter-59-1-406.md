# Chapter 406

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Hiram E. Monroe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Hiram E. Monroe, late of Company E, Eleventh Regiment Michigan Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 19, 1906.*

# Chapter 114

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Sophia A. Knapp.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sophia A. Knapp, widow of Sylvester Knapp, late of the Twenty-fourth Battery Indiana Volunteer Light Artillery, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 5, 1906.*

# Chapter 2294

> An Act For the relief of John C. Ray, assignee of John Gafford, of Arkansas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of eight hundred and fifty-three dollars and twenty-five cents be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated for the relief of John C. Ray, assignee of John Gafford, deceased, mail contractor on route numbered seventy-nine hundred and fifty-six, in the State of Arkansas, for carrying the United States mails to May thirty-first, eighteen hundred and sixty-one, this being the amount of pay due to John Gafford to that date as found by the Auditor for the Post-Office Department and reported to the Secretary of the Treasury for appropriation.


*Approved, March 1, 1907.*

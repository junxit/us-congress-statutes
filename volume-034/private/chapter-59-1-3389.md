# Chapter 3389

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Laura Hentig.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Laura Hentig, widow of Edmund C. Hentig, late captain Troop D, Sixth Regiment United States Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, June 18, 1906.*

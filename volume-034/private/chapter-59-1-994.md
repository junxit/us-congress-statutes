# Chapter 994

> An Act Granting a pension to Ulrika Bottcher.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ulrika Bottcher, widow of John Bottcher, late of Company B, Forty-fourth Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, March 19, 1906.*

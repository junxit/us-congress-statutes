# Chapter 339

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Zelinda E. Odenbaugh.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Zelinda E. Odenbaugh, former widow of John N. Thompson, late of Company K, Twenty-fourth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, January 21, 1907.*

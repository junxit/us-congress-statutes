# Chapter 2351

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to John Bowen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior he, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the. provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Bowen, late second lieutenant Companies D and A, First Regiment Oregon Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 1, 1907.*

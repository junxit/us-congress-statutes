# Chapter 368

> An Act Granting a pension to Ella T. Hapeman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ella T. Hapeman, widow of Douglas Hapeman, late lieutenant-colonel One hundred and fourth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 19, 1906.*

# Chapter 3658

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Leah Bedford.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Leah Bedford, widow of John Bedford, late second lieutenant Company C, Second Regiment Veteran Reserve Corps, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, June 29, 1906.*

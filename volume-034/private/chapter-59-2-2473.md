# Chapter 2473

> An Act Granting a pension to Jeremiah Bowman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jeremiah Bowman, late of Troop D, Ninth Regiment United States Cavalry, and Company B, Twenty-fourth Regiment United States Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 1, 1907.*

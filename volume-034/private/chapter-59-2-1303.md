# Chapter 1303

> An Act Granting a pension to Mary Murray.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Murray, widow of William Murray, late first sergeant, Fourteenth Regiment United States Army, retired, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1907.*

# Chapter 339

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Myron E. Billings.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Myron E. Billings, late of Company L, First Regiment United States Volunteer Sharpshooters; Company L, Fifth Regiment Iowa Volunteer Cavalry, and captain, One hundred and twentieth Regiment United States Colored Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 19, 1906.*

# Chapter 3069

> An Act To correct the military record of Henry Gude.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Henry Gude shall be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from Company K, Seventeenth Missouri Infantry Volunteers, as of date of August twenty-sixth, eighteen hundred and sixty-live: Provided, That no pay, bounty, or other emoluments shall become due or payable by virtue of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 9, 1906.*

# Chapter 2023

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Rose Sevin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Rose Sevin, widow of Charles Julius Sevin, late hospital steward United States ships Hartford, Colorado, and Pensacola, United States Navy, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 26, 1907.*

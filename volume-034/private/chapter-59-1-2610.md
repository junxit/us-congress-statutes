# Chapter 2610

> An Act Granting a pension to Laura Hilgeman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Laura Hilgeman, widow of William Hilgeman, late of Company C, First Regiment Missouri Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, June 6, 1906.*

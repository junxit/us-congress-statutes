# Chapter 2266

> An Act Granting a pension to Reuben R. Ballenger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roil, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Reuben R. Ballenger, late of Company G, Fourth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, May 7, 1906.*

# Chapter 2507

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Ann J. Thompson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

. That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ann J. Thompson, widow of Robert M. Thompson, ate major, Sixth Regiment Tennessee Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, May 21, 1906.*

# Chapter 3244

> An Act Granting a pension to Benjamin F. Tudor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Benjamin F. Tudor, late of Company B, Santa Fe Battalion, Missouri Mounted Volunteers, war with Mexico, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, June 11, 1906.*

# Chapter 1295

> An Act Granting a pension to F. Medora Johnson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of F. Medora Johnson, widow of Thomas Johnson, late of Company G, One hundred and seventeeth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1907.*

# Chapter 2834

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Drucillar A. Massey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Drucillar A. Massey, widow of Berrel J. Massey, ate of Company B, Third Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, war with Mexico, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, June 6, 1906.*

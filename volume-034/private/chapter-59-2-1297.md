# Chapter 1297

> An Act Granting a pension to Louisa Bartlett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Louisa Bartlett, helpless and dependent daughter of Elijah Bartlett, late of Company D, Sixtieth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1907.*

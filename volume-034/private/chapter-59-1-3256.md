# Chapter 3256

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Allamanza M. Harrison.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Allamanza M. Harrison, widow of William Harrison, late of Company C, Santa Fe Battalion Missouri Mounted Volunteers, war with Mexico, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, June 11, 1906.*

# Chapter 435

> An Act For the relief of P. S. Corbett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the accounts of P. S. Corbett, formerly United States marshal for the State of Nevada, be readjusted by the Secretary of the Treasury, and the said Secretary is hereby directed to allow and credit to the said Corbett, under the head of “bees and expenses of marshals. United States courts, eighteen hundred and eighty-three and eighteen hundred and eighty-four,” the sum of one thousand five hundred and thirty-three dollars and sixty- four cents now charged against said Corbett on the books of the Treasury Department.


*Approved, January 30, 1007.*

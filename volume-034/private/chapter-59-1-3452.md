# Chapter 3452

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Thomas McGowan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Thomas McGowan, late second-class firemen. United States steamers North Carolina, Shamrock, and Commodore Hull, United States Navy, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, June 20, 1906.*

# Chapter 3150

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Catharine Myers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Catharine Myers, widow of Adelbert Myers, late of Company A, One hundred and forty-third Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, June 11, 1906.*

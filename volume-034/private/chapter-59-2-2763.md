# Chapter 2763

> An Act Granting a pension to Lauraette La Fleur.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lauraette La Fleur, widow of Asher La Fleur, late of Company H, Fourth Regiment Michigan Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 2, 1907.*

# Chapter 1252

> An Act Granting a pension to Margarette R. Bacon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Margarette R. Bacon, widow of George A. Bacon, late colonel Fifteenth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifteen dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1907.*

# Chapter 887

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Thomas Clark.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Thomas Clark, late of Company C, Second Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers, war with Mexico, and Company D, Second Regment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 12, 1906.*

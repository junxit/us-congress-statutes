# Chapter 1523

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to William Ralston.

*Be, it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William Ralston, late of Company D, Twenty-fifth Regiment Missouri Volunteer Infantry, and Company B. First Regiment Missouri Volunteer Engineers, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, April 11, 1906.*

# Chapter 1332

> An Act Granting a pension to Lyman Critchfield, junior.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House, of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior he, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lyman Critchfield, junior, late of Company D, Eighth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain.


*Approved, March 26, 1906.*

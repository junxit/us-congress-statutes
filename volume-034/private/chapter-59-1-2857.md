# Chapter 2857

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to John Misner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Misner, late of Company G, Seventh Regiment United States Infantry, war with Mexico, and Company L, Second Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lien of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, June 6, 1906.*

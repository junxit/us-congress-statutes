# Chapter 943

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to William A. Southworth.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William A. Southworth, late of Company A, One hundred and fourteenth Regiment, and Company E, Eighty-ninth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 12, 1906.*

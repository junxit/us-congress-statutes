# Chapter 3450

> An Act Granting an honorable discharge to Henry G. Thomas, deceased, Company C, Second Kentucky Cavalry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to correct the military record of, and grant an honorable discharge to, Henry G. Thomas, deceased, late captain of Company C, Second Kentucky Cavalry: Provided, That no pay, bounty, or other emoluments shall accrue by virtue of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 20, 1906.*

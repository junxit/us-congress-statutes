# Chapter 479

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Sarah A. J. Tayman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension taws, the name of Sarah A. J. Tayman, widow of Jacob Tayman, alias Jacob Tarman, late of Captain Trysinger’s company, Pennsylvania Militia, war of eighteen hundred and twelve, and pay her a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 19, 1906.*

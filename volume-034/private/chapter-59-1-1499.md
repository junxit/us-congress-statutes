# Chapter 1499

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Arthur R. Dreppard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Arthur R. Dreppard, late of Company M, Ninth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain, and pay him a pension at the rate of eighteen dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, April 11, 1906.*

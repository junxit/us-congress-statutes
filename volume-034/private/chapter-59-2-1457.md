# Chapter 1457

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to John Bryant.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place, on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Bryant, late of Captains Tucker’s, McClellan’s, and Roberts’s companies, Florida Volunteers, Florida Indian war, and pay him a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that he, is now receiving.


*Approved, February 25, 1907.*

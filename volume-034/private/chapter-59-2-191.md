# Chapter 191

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Aaron Daniels.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Aaron Daniels, late of Captain Roberts’ company, Florida Mounted Volunteers, Seminole. Indian war, and pay him a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that be is now receiving.


*Approved, January 18, 1907.*

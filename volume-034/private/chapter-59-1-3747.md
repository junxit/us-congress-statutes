# Chapter 3747

> An Act Granting a pension to Sophie M. Staab.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sophie M. Staab, helpless and dependent daughter of Frederick Staab, late of Company K, Twelfth Regiment New Jersey Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, June 29, 1906.*

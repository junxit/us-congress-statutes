# Chapter 647

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Mary Gramberg.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Gramberg, widow of George Gramberg, late of Company B, Twentieth Regiment Massachusetts volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 6, 1907.*

# Chapter 1653

> An Act Granting a pension to Ida E. G. Pierce.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ida E. G. Pierce, widow of Ebenezer W. Pierce, late colonel Twenty-ninth Regiment Massachusetts Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifteen dollars per month.


*Approved, February 26, 1907.*

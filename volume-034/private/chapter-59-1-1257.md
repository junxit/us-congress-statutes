# Chapter 1257

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Henry Crandell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the [tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Henry Crandell, late of Company C, Seventieth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and Troop I), Second Regiment United States Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 26, 1906.*

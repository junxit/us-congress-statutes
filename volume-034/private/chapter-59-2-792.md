# Chapter 792

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Sarah A. Galloway.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah A. Galloway, widow of Simpson R. Galloway, late of Captain Killian’s company, Third Regiment North Carolina Volunteers, Cherokee Indian disturbances, and ay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 6, 1907.*

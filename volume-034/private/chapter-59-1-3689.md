# Chapter 3689

> An Act Granting a pension to Edna Buchanan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Edna Buchanan, widow of William P. Buchanan, ate of Company A, Ninth Regiment Indiana Legion, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, June 29, 1906.*

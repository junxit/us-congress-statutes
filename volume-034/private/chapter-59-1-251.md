# Chapter 251

> An Act Granting a pension to Emeline S. Hayner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Emeline S. Hayner, former widow of William H. Gosline, late captain Company A, One hundred and seventy-seventh Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month, commencing March third, nineteen hundred and five.


*Approved, February 10, 1906.*

# Chapter 2337

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Mary E. Dougherty.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary E. Dougherty, widow of John C. Dougherty, late first lieutenant Company M, One hundredth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 1, 1907.*

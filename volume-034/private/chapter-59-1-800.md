# Chapter 800

> An Act Granting a pension to Harriet E. St. John.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Harriet E. St. John, widow of Thomas H. St. John, late of Company D, Fifteenth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 12, 1906.*

# Chapter 1615

> An Act Granting a pension to Mary Palmer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Palmer, widow of Stephen J. Palmer, late of Captain Morgan’s independent company Iowa Volunteers, war with Mexico, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, April 11, 1906.*

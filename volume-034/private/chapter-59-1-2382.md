# Chapter 2382

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Myra R. Daniels.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension taws, the name of Myra R. Daniels, widow of William II. Daniels, late of Company D. First Regiment District of Columbia Volunteer Cavalry, and Company F, First Regiment Maine Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, May 8, 1906.*

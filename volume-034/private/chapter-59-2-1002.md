# Chapter 1002

> An Act Granting a pension to Hansford G. Gilkeson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Hansford G. Gilkeson, late of Company K, Second Regiment Virginia Volunteer Infantry, and Hospital Corps, United States Army, war with Spain, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 19, 1907.*

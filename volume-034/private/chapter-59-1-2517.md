# Chapter 2517

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to George H. Woodard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

Hint the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George H. Woodard, late of Company H, Eighty-third Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and Troop A, Second Regiment United States Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, May 21, 1906.*

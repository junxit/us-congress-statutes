# Chapter 1699

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Caroline Dehlendorf.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place, on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Caroline Dehlendorf, widow of William Dehlendorf, late of Company B, Fifth Regiment United States Reserve Corps, Missouri Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, April 23, 1906.*

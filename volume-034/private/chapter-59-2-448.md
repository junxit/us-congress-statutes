# Chapter 448

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Elizabeth B. Boyle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elizabeth 15. Boyle, widow of Henry G. Boyle, alias Henry B. Miller, late of Company 0, Mormon Battalion, Iowa Volunteers, war with Mexico, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 1, 1907.*

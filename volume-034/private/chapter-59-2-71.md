# Chapter 71

> An Act Granting a pension to Allen M. Cameron.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Allen M. Cameron, late of Troop M, Fifth Regiment United States Cavalry, war with Spain, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, January 12, 1907.*

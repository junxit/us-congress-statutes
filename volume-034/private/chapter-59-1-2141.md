# Chapter 2141

> An Act Granting a pension to Alba B. Bean.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Alba B. Bean, late of Company C, Twentieth Regiment United States Infantry, and Hospital Corps, United States Army, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty-six dollars per month.


*Approved, May 7, 1906.*

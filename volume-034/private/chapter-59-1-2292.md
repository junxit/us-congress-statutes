# Chapter 2292

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Truman Aldrich.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Truman Aldrich, late of Company F, One hundred and forty-first Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay in a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, May 7, 1906.*

# Chapter 1449

> An Act Granting a pension to Ray E. Kline.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ray E. Kline, widow of Daniel L. Kline, late of Brigade Band First Brigade, First Division Sixteenth Army Corps, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, April 11, 1906.*

# Chapter 889

> An Act For the relief of George H. Chase.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That George H. Chase, late second-class fireman, United States steamship New York, be held and considered as one and the same person as George II. Eaton, and to have been granted an ordinary discharge from the United States service as of date of July twenty-sixth, eighteen hundred and ninety-six.


*Approved, February 7, 1907.*

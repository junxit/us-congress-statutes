# Chapter 2862

> An Act Granting a pension to William McCraney.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William McCraney, late of Captain Morgan’s company, Iowa Mounted Volunteers, war with Mexico, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 2, 1907.*

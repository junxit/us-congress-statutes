# Chapter 1869

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Solomon Stanfield.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Solomon Stanfield, late of Company H. Fifth Regiment Indiana Volunteers, war with Mexico, and Company K, Fifty-first Regiment Indiana Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that lie is now receiving.


*Approved, February 26, 1907.*

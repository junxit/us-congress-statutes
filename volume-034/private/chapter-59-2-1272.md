# Chapter 1272

> An Act Granting a pension to Ritty M. Lane.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ritty M. Lane, widow of James Lane, late of Company I, Third Regiment Illinois Volunteers, war with Mexico, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1907.*

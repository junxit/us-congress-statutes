# Chapter 1900

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Harriet Williams.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Harriet Williams, widow of James E. Williams, late major Third Regiment United States Colored Volunteer Heavy Artillery, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, April 26, 1906.*

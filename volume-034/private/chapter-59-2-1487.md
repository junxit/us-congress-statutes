# Chapter 1487

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Henry Utter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Henry Utter, late of Company I, Fifth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty- four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 25, 1907.*

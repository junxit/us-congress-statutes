# Chapter 893

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Letitia D. Watkins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the [tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Letitia D. Watkins, widow of James Watkins, late of Company D, Fourth Regiment Tennessee Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 12, 1906.*

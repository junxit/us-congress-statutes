# Chapter 1504

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Thomas J. Prouty.

*Be it enacted by the Semite and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name or Thomas J. Prouty, late of Company B, Forty-fifth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 25, 1907.*

# Chapter 1094

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Susan E. Israel.

*Beit enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Susan E. Israel, widow of Jeptha M. Israel, late first lieutenant Company K. First Regiment North Carolina Volunteers, war with Mexico, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 19, 1906.*

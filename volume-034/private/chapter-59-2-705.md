# Chapter 705

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Emmagene Bronson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Emmagene Bronson, widow of Nelson Bronson, late first lieutenant Company E, Eighth Regiment Connecticut Volunteer Infantry, and Eighty-ninth Company. Second Battalion Veteran Reserve Corps, and pay her a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 6, 1907.*

# Chapter 915

> An Act For the relief of John C. Lynch.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Court of Claims shall have and possess jurisdiction and authority to inquire into and finally adjudicate the claim of John C. Lynch, a resident of Shackelford County, Texas, for property taken or destroyed by Indians, and for the purpose of this action it shall be assumed he was a citizen of the United States at the time of the injury.


*Approved, February 9, 1907.*

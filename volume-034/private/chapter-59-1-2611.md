# Chapter 2611

> An Act Granting a pension to Albert M. Ryan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension taws, the name of Albert M. Ryan, late of Captain Michael M. Gilbreath’s company, Alabama Scouts and Guides, and pay him a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, June 6, 1906.*

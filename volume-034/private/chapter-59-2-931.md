# Chapter 931

> An Act For the relief of F. Kraut, of Leon Springs, Texas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to F. Kraut, of Leon Springs, Texas, the sum of three hundred and twenty- five dollars, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, in full settlement of claims for damage to property incident to the target practice of the Army of the United States near Leon Springs, Texas.


*Approved, February 16, 1907.*

# Chapter 2710

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to William Freeman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William Freeman, late of Company H, Second Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, war with Mexico, and Company A, Ninety-seventh Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 2, 1907.*

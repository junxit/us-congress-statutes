# Chapter 2441

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Laura B. Ihrie.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Laura B. Ihrie, widow of George P. Ihrie, late captain’s clerk, United States ship Ohio, United States Navy, war with Mexico, lieutenant-colonel, Third Regiment California Volunteer Infan try, colonel and additional aidede-camp, United States Volunteers, and major and paymaster, United States Army, and pay her a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, May 10, 1906.*

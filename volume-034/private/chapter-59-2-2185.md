# Chapter 2185

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Mary Jane Miller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Jane Miller, widow of Worth Miller, late of Company H, One hundred and sixty-sixth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and unassigned, Seventh Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 28, 1907.*

# Chapter 2808

> An Act Granting a pension to Lizzie H. Prout.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lizzie II. Prout, widow of William D. Prout, late captain’s clerk United States ship Relief, United States Navy, war with Mexico, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, June 6, 1906.*

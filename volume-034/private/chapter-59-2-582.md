# Chapter 582

> An Act Granting a pension to Ann Lytle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ann Lytle, widow of George Lytle, late of Company F, Seventy-seventh Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 6, 1907.*

# Chapter 2883

> An Act To complete the naval record of Patrick Naddy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy is hereby directed to so amend the naval record of Patrick Naddy, late first-class boy, United States Navy, on United States receiving ship North Carolina and United States ships Galena and Princeton, as to show him honorably discharged, to date from June thirtieth, eighteen hundred and sixty-three.


*Approved, March 2, 1907.*

# Chapter 1515

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Sibby Barnhill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Hawse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sibby Barnhill, widow of William Barnhill, late of Captain Cunningham’s company, Third Regiment North Carolina Militia. Cherokee Indian disturbances, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 25, 1907.*

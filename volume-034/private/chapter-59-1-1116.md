# Chapter 1116

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Barney McGirl.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior he, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Barney McGirl, late of Company 1, Second Regiment Kansas Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 19, 1906.*

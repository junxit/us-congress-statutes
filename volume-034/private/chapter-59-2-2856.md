# Chapter 2856

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Adeline Brown.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Adeline Brown, widow of James M. Brown, late captain Company K, Seventh Regiment New Jersey Volunteer Infantry, and major Fifteenth Regiment New Jersey Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 2, 1907.*

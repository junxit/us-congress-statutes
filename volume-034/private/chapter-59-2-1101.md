# Chapter 1101

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Augusta T. Eichholtz.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Augusta T. Eichholtz, widow of Hugh Eichholtz, late of Company L. Fifteenth Regiment, New York Volunteer Heavy Artillery, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 19, 1907.*

# Chapter 3798

> An Act Granting a pension to Caroline E. Perry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Caroline E. Perry, widow of Louis E. Perry, late of Company A, Second Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month, and two dollars per month additional on account of each of the minor children of said Louis E. Perry, until they reach the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, June 29, 1906.*

# Chapter 1503

> An Act Granting a pension to Isaac Baker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension vs, the name of Isaac Baker, late of Company K, Forty-third Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, April 11, 1906.*

# Chapter 361

> An Act Granting a pension to Florence Greeley De Veaux.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Florence Greeley De Veaux, widow of James G. De Veaux, late acting assistant surgeon, United States Army, war with Spain, and pay her a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month and two dollars per month additional on account of the minor child of the said James G. De Veaux until she reaches the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, February 19, 1906.*

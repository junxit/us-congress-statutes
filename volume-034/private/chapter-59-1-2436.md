# Chapter 2436

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Matilda C. Frizelle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Matilda C. Frizelle. widow of Daniel C. Frizelle, late of Company F, Fourteenth Regiment United States Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving, and two dollars per month additional on account of the minor child of the said Daniel C. Frizelle until she reaches the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, May 8, 1906.*

# Chapter 883

> An Act For the relief of William H. Beall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the records as having been honorably discharged the name of William H. Beall, late a paymaster’s steward on gunboat Fairplay, and issue to him a discharge to bear date of August fifteenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-three.


*Approved, February 7, 1907.*

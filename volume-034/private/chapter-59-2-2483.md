# Chapter 2483

> An Act Granting a pension to Ella B. Morrow.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ella B. Morrow, widow of Carlos Morrow, late of Company G, Fourth Regiment Vermont Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month, and two dollars per month additional on account of each of the minor children of the said Carlos Morrow until they reach the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, March 1, 1907.*

# Chapter 803

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Mary Wilson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Wilson, widow of Robert Wilson, late of Seventh Independent Battery New York Volunteer Light Artillery, and pay her a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 6, 1907.*

# Chapter 1360

> An Act For the relief of Major George E. Pickett, paymaster, United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Major George E. Pickett, paymaster, United States Army, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one thousand four hundred and fifty-six dollars and seventeen cents.


*Approved, March 31, 1906.*

# Chapter 1205

> An Act To correct the military record of George H. Keating.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That George H. Keating be held and considered to have been mustered into the United States military service as a private in Company G, Ninth New York Infantry Volunteers, as of date of May eighteenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-one, and honorably discharged from said service as of date of May twentieth, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, and that an honorable discharge be issued in accordance with this Act: Provided, That no pay, bounty, or other emoluments shall become due or payable by virtue of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 25, 1907.*

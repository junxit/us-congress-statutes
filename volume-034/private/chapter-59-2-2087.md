# Chapter 2087

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Francis M. Hatten.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Francis M. Hatten, late of Company I, Ninth Regiment West Virginia Volunteer Infantry, and Company D, First Regiment West Virginia Veteran Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that be is now receiving.


*Approved, February 27, 1907.*

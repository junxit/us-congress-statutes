# Chapter 231

> An Act Granting a pension to Mary Mitchell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Mitchell, widow of Thomas Mitchell, alias John Grove, late of Company I, First Regiment Potomac Home Brigade, Maryland Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 10, 1906.*

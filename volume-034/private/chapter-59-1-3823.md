# Chapter 3823

> An Act Granting a pension to Catherine Bausman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Catherine Bailsman, widow of Rinehart A. Bausman, late of United States ship Louisville, and acting master, United States Navy, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, June 29, 1906.*

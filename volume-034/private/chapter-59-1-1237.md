# Chapter 1237

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Hermann Lieb.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Hermann Lieb, late major Eighth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, colonel Fifth Regiment United States Colored Volunteer Heavy Artillery, and brevet brigadier-general, United States Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 26, 1906.*

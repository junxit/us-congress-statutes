# Chapter 1818

> An Act Granting a pension to Thomas Cheek.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Thomas Cheek, late second-class boy, ship Iroquois, United States Navy, and pay him a pension at the rate of six dollars per month.


*Approved, April 23, 1906.*

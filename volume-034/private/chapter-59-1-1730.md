# Chapter 1730

> An Act Granting a pension to William C. Estill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and I louse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension taws, the name of William C. Estill, helpless and dependent son of William J. Estill, late of Company F, Twenty-eighth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, April 23, 1906.*

# Chapter 2501

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Ann Hudson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ann Hudson, widow of Christopher C. Hudson, late ensign Captain Tatum’s company, Tennessee Volunteers, Creek Indian war, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 1, 1907.*

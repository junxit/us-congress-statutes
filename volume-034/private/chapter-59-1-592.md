# Chapter 592

> An Act Granting a pension to Clara T. Leathers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Clara T. Leathers, widow of Alexander T. Leathers, late of Company C, Fourth Regiment United States Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 7, 1906.*

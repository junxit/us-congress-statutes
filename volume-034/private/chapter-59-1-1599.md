# Chapter 1599

> An Act Granting a pension to Orlando W. Frazier.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Orlando W. Frazier, helpless and dependent son of Orlando W. Frazier, late captain Company G, One hundred and forty-fourth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, April 11, 1906.*

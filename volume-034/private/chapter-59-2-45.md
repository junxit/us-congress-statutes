# Chapter 45

> An Act To remove the charge of absence without leave and reported desertion from the military record of J. F. Wisnewski.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That J. F. Wisnewski, late captain of Company K, Second Kentucky Infantry, be held and considered to have been honorably discharged said service on August eighteenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, and that the Secretary of War be authorized to issue an honorable discharge in accordance with this Act: Provided, That no pay, bounty, or other emoluments shall become due or payable by virtue of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, January 12, 1907.*

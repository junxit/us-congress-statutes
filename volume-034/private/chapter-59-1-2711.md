# Chapter 2711

> An Act Granting a pension to Sarah Ellen Dickens.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah Ellen Dickens, widow of Thomas Dickens, late of Company A, Sixty-second Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, June 6, 1906.*

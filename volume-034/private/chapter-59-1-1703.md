# Chapter 1703

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Caroline Neilson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Caroline Neilson, widow of Thomas Neilson, late sergeant of Captain J. H. Stewart’s company, Maryland Militia Volunteers, war of eighteen hundred and twelve, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per mouth in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, April 23, 1906.*

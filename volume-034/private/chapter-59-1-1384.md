# Chapter 1384

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Katherine F. Wainwright.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Katherine F. Wainwright, widow of George A. Wain-wright, late first lieutenant Company A and major First Regiment New Hampshire Volunteer Heavy Artillery, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, April 11, 1906.*

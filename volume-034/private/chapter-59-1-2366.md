# Chapter 2366

> An Act Granting a pension to Juliet K. Phillips.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Juliet K. Phillips, widow of Jesse J. Phillips, late lieutenant-colonel Ninth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and brevet brigadier-general, United States Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, May 8, 1906.*

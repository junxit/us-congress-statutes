# Chapter 1797

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Daniel Sullivan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Daniel Sullivan, late of Company G, Twelfth Regiment Massachusetts Volunteer Infantry, and U. S. S. Ohio, Ethan Allen, and Savannah, U. S. Navy, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 26, 1907.*

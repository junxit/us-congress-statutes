# Chapter 2163

> An Act Granting a pension to William Berry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William Berry, late of Captain Gilbreath’s company, Alabama Scouts and Guides, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, May 7, 1906.*

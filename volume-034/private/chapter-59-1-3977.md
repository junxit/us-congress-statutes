# Chapter 3977

> An Act For the relief of Daniel B. Murphy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Daniel B. Murphy, late adjutant of the Twenty-fifth Regiment New Jersey Volunteer Infantry, shall be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from said regiment on February eighteenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, and that the Secretary of War shall issue to him an honorable discharge as of that date: Provided, That no pay, bounty, or other emoluments shall become due or payable by virtue of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 30, 1906.*

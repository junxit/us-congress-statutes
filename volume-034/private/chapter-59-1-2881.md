# Chapter 2881

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Andrew Dorn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Andrew Dorn, late of Company D. One hundred and forty-sixth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay him it pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, June 6, 1906.*

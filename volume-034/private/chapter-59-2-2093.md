# Chapter 2093

> An Act For the relief of W. B. Sutter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster-General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to allow on the accounts of W. B. Sutter, postmaster at Lindsey, Pennsylvania, a credit of two hundred and eighteen dollars and nineteen cents, for postage stamps and money stolen from said post-office by burglars March fifteenth-sixteenth, eighteen hundred and ninety-eight.


*Approved, February 27, 1907.*

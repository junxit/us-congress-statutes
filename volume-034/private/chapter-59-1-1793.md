# Chapter 1793

> An Act Granting a pension to Louis Grossman.

*Be it enacted by the Semite and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Louis Grossman, late of Company D, Fifteenth Regiment United States Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, April 23, 1906.*

# Chapter 1546

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Frank Schadler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior he, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Frank Schadler, late of Company D, Fourth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and U. S. S. Dumbarton, United States Navy, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lien of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 25, 1907.*

# Chapter 2003

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Jacob Palmer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior he. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jacob Palmer, late first lieutenant Company F, Eighty-sixth Regiment Indiana Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 26, 1907.*

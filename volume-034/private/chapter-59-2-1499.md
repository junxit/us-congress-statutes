# Chapter 1499

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Libbie D. Lowry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Libbie D. Lowry, widow of Morrow P. Lowry, late acting assistant paymaster, United States Navy, and pay her a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 25, 1907.*

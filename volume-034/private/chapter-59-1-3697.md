# Chapter 3697

> An Act Granting a pension to Lucy A. Thomas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lucy A. Thomas, widow of Harvey B. Thomas, late of band, First Brigade, First Division, Seventh Army Corps, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, June 29, 1906.*

# Chapter 3851

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Frances Becker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions anti limitations of the pension laws, the name of Frances Becker, widow of Ferdinand Becker, late of Company E, Twenty-third Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and Company G, Third Regiment New York Volunteer Light Artillery, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, June 29, 1906.*

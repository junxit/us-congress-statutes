# Chapter 3963

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Ella E. Kenney.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject, to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ella E. Kenney, widow of (filbert A. Kenney, late of Company K, Tenth Regiment Rhode Island Volunteer Infantry, and Company C, Second Regiment, Rhode Island Volunteer Cavalry and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, June 30, 1906.*

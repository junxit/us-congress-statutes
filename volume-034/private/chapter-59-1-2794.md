# Chapter 2794

> An Act Granting a pension to Will E. Kayser.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Will E. Kayser, late of Company I, Second Regiment United States Volunteer Engineers, war with Spain, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, June 6, 1906.*

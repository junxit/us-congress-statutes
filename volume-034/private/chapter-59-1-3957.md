# Chapter 3957

> An Act Granting a pension to Helen G. Hibbard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Helen G. Hibbard, widow of James M. Edminster, late of Company E, Ninth Regiment New Hampshire Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, June 30, 1906.*

# Chapter 223

> An Act Granting a pension to Harriet O’Donald.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Harriet O’Donald, widow of John S. O’Donald, late first lieutenant Company K, Eighty-fifth Regiment Indiana Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, January 18, 1907.*

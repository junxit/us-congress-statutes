# Chapter 1804

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to William R. Wilkins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place, on the tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William R. Wilkins, late of Company 1, Second Regiment West Virginia Volunteer Infantry, and Company I, Fifth Regiment West Virginia Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 26, 1907.*

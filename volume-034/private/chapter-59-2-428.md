# Chapter 428

> An Act For the relief of the Postal Telegraph Cable Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to the Postal Telegraph Cable Company, a corporation incorporated under the laws of the State, of New York, having its principal office at number two hundred and fifty-three Broadway, New York City, the sum of two thousand one hundred and fifty-five dollars and nineteen cents, for telegraph service rendered to the Post-Office Department from July first, eighteen hundred and eighty-nine, to June thirtieth, eighteen hundred and ninety-three.


*Approved, January 28, 1907.*

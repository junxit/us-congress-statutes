# Chapter 204

> An Act Granting a pension to Else C. Isachsen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Else C. Isachsen, dependent mother of Alfred C. Isachsen, late of Company F, First Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 10, 1906.*

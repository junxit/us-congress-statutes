# Chapter 1207

> An Act To correct the military record of James H. Davis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That James H. Davis, of Company B, East Tennessee National Guard, war of the rebellion, be held and considered to have been enlisted in said organization September fourteenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-three: Provided, That no pay, bounty, or other emoluments shall become due or payable by virtue of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 25, 1907.*

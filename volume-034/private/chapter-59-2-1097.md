# Chapter 1097

> An Act Granting a pension to Henry W. Blair.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and ho is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Henry W. Blair, late lieutenant-colonel Fifteenth Regiment New Hampshire Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of seventy-two dollars per month.


*Approved, February 19, 1907.*

# Chapter 3133

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Jesse M. Auchmuty.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jesse M. Auchmuty, late of Company C, One hundred and thirty-first Regiment, and Company D, Two hundred and eighth Regiment. Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, June 11, 1906.*

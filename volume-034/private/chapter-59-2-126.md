# Chapter 126

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Anna Lamar Walker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Anna Lamar Walker, widow of Nathaniel G. W. Walker, late of Captain Allen’s company, Colonel Brisbane’s Regiment South Carolina Volunteers, Florida Indian war, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, January 12, 1907.*

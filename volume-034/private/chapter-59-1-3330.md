# Chapter 3330

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Louisa Carr.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Louisa Carr, widow of John T. Carr, late of Company B, Third Regiment West Virginia Volunteer Infantry, and Company D, Sixth Battalion West Virginia Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, June 14, 1906.*

# Chapter 320

> An Act Granting a pension to Eliza J. Ingle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the. name of Eliza J. Ingle, widow of William T. Ingle, late a member of Company E, Fourteenth Regiment Iowa Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, January 21, 1907.*

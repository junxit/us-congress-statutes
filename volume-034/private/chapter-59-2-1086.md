# Chapter 1086

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Adline Mabry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Adline Mabry, widow of Isaiah Mabry, late of Company G, Twelfth Regiment United States Infantry, war with Mexico, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 19, 1907.*

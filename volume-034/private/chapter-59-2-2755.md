# Chapter 2755

> An Act Granting a pension to Sadie E. Hawthorn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sadie E. Hawthorn, widow of Aldus F. Hawthorn, late captain Company D, One hundred and fifty-seventh Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, March 2, 1907.*

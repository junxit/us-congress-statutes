# Chapter 1063

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Delia A. Hooker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate an d House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Delia A. Hooker, widow of Ambrose E. Hooker, late captain, Ninth Regiment United States Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that, she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 19, 1906.*

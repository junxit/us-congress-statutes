# Chapter 2343

> An Act Granting a pension to Mary F. Johnson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary F. Johnson, widow of Gilman B. Johnson, ate first lieutenant and quartermaster. Eleventh Regiment New Hampshire Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month.


*Approved, March 1, 1907.*

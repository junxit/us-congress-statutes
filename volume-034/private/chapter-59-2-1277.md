# Chapter 1277

> An Act Granting a pension to Delia S. Humphrey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Delia S. Humphrey, widow of John H. Humphrey, late lieutenant-colonel Forty-fifth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1907.*

# Chapter 795

> An Act Granting a pension to Charles T. Hesler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Charles T. Hesler, late of Company C, Fifteenth Regiment Minnesota Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain.


*Approved, March 12, 1906.*

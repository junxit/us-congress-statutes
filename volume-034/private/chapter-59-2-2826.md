# Chapter 2826

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Virginia C. Galloway.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Virginia C. Galloway, widow of John Enos Galloway, ate of Captain Fitzhugh’s company, Texas Mounted Volunteers, war with Mexico, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 2, 1907.*

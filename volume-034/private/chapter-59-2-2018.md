# Chapter 2018

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Alonzo Foster.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Alonzo Foster, late of Company F, Sixth Regiment New York Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 26, 1907.*

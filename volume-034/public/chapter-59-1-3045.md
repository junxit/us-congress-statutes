# Chapter 3045

> An Act For the relief of the State of Rhode Island.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby directed to resettle and readjust the claim of the State of Rhode Island for and on account of its expenditures in raising volunteers during the war with Spain under the provisions of the Acts of Congress approved July eighth, eighteen hundred and ninety- eight, March third, eighteen hundred and ninety-nine, and April twenty-seventh, nineteen hundred and four.


*Approved, June 7, 1906.*

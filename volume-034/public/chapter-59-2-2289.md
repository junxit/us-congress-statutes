# Chapter 2289

> An Act Ratifying and confirming chapter fifty-eight of the twenty-third legislative assembly of the Territory of Arizona, providing for repair of the Territorial bridge at Florence, Pinal County, Arizona.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That chapter fifty-eight of session laws of the twenty-third legislative assembly of the Territory of Arizona, “providing for the repair of the Territorial bridge at Florence, Arizona.” approved March sixteenth, nineteen hundred and five. be. and the same is hereby, ratified and approved.


*Approved, March 1, 1907.*

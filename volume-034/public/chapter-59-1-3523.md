# Chapter 3523

> An Act To provide for the traveling expenses of the President of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That hereafter there may be expended for or on account of the traveling expenses of the President of the United States such sum as Congress may from time to time appropriate, not exceeding twenty-five thousand dollars per annum, such sum when appropriated to be expended in the discretion of the President and accounted for on his certificate solely.

There is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the purposes authorized by this Act for the fiscal year nineteen hundred and seven, the sum of twenty-five thousand dollars.


*Approved, June 23, 1906.*

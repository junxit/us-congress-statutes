# Chapter 150

> An Act To establish a life-saving station at the Isles of Shoals, off Portsmouth, New Hampshire.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to establish a life-saving station at such point on the Isles of Shoals, off Portsmouth, New Hampshire, as the General Superintendent of the Life-Saving Service may recommend.


*Approved, January 17, 1907.*

# Chapter 3549

> An Act Authorizing Captain Ejnar Mikkelsen to act as master of an American vessel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Captain Ejnar Mikkelsen is hereby authorized to act as master of any vessel of the United States purchased by him while on an expedition in her to the Beaufort Sea, any Act of Congress to the contrary notwithstanding.


*Approved, June 26, 1906.*

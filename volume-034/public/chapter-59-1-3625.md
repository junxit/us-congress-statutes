# Chapter 3625

> An Act To extend the privileges of the seventh section of the Act approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, to the subport of Superior, Wisconsin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the privileges of the seventh section of the Act approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, entitled “An Act to amend the statutes in relation to immediate transportation of dutiable goods, and for other purposes,” be, and the same are hereby, extended to the subport of Superior, in the State of Wisconsin.


*Approved, June 29, 1906.*

# Chapter 2941

> An Act For the relief of Harold D. Childs.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized to nominate and, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, to appoint Harold D. Childs, late midshipman, an ensign in the United States Navy, and to place him upon the retired list as such with three-fourths the pay of his grade.


*Approved, March 4, 1907.*

# Chapter 2552

> An Act To change the line of the reservation at Hot Springs, Arkansas, and of Reserve avenue.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the line of the Government reservation at Hot Springs. Arkansas, and of Reserve avenue, be changed so as to run from stone monument twenty-six to stone monument twenty-eight on a direct line, instead of running from twenty-six to twenty-seven and thence to twenty-eight, as it now does: Provided, That the tract of land thus excluded from the reservation by changing the lines as above, be ceded to the city of Hot Springs, to become a part of Reserve avenue and to be used for street purposes only: to be accepted by the city without change of the opposite (southerly) boundary line of said avenue.


*Approved, May 23, 1906.*

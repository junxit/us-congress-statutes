# Chapter 470

> An Act To amend an Act entitled “An Act to amend the statutes in relation to immediate transportation of dutiable goods, and for other purposes,” approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, by extending the provisions of the first section thereof to the port of Brunswick, Georgia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the privileges of the first section of the Act approved June tenth, anno Domini eighteen hundred and eighty, entitled “An Act to amend the statutes in relation to immediate transportation of dutiable goods, and for other purposes.” be, and the same are hereby, extended to the port of Brunswick, Georgia.


*Approved, February 6, 1907.*

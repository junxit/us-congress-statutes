# Chapter 893

> An Act For an additional term of court at Quincy, Illinois.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That an additional term of the circuit and district courts in and for said southern district of Illinois shall be held at the city of Quincy on the first Monday of March of each year.


*Approved, February 8, 1907.*

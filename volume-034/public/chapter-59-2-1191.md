# Chapter 1191

> An Act To provide for a land district in Valley County, in the State of Montana, to be known as the Glasgow land district.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all that portion of the State of Montana included within the present boundaries of Valley County is hereby constituted a new land district, and that the land office for said district shall be located at Glasgow, in said county.


*Approved, February 25, 1907.*

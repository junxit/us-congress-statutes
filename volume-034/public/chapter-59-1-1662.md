# Chapter 1662

> An Act Making an appropriation to supply a deficiency in the appropriation for bringing home remains of officers and men of the Navy and Marine Corps who die abroad.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That to supply a deficiency in the appropriation for “bringing home the remains of officers and men, Navy and Marine Corps, who die abroad” on account of the fiscal year nineteen hundred and six. there is hereby appropriated out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated the sum of four thousand dollars.


*Approved, April 23, 1906.*

# Chapter 3507

> An Act Changing the name of Douglas street to Clifton street.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the passage of this Act the street extending from Fourteenth street, west, to University place, in the District of Columbia, now known as Douglas street, shall be known and designated as Clifton street.


*Approved, June 21, 1906.*

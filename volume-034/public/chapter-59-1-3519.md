# Chapter 3519

> An Act To amend an act entitled “An act to establish a port of delivery at Salt Lake City, Utah.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate. and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section two of an Act entitled “An Act to establish a port of delivery at Salt Lake City. Utah,” approved March eighteenth, nineteen hundred and four, be, and the same is hereby, amended to read as follows:

> ## § 2.
>
> That there shall be appointed a surveyor of customs to reside at said port, whose salary shall be one thousand five hundred dollars per annum, in lieu of all fees and commissions of every kind whatsoever.”


*Approved, June 23, 1906.*

# Chapter 2553

> An Act To extend the privileges of the seventh section of the Act approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, to the port of Oswego, New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the privileges of the seventh section of the Act approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, entitled “An Act to amend the statutes in relation to immediate transportation of dutiable goods, and for other purposes,” be, and the same are hereby. extended to the port of Oswego, in the State of New York.


*Approved, May 23, 1906.*

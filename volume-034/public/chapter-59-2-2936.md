# Chapter 2936

> Ac Act To amend an Act entitled “An Act to authorize the Ox Bow Power Company of South Dakota to construct a dam across the Missouri River.”

*Beit enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section two of chapter eighteen hundred and twenty-one of the laws of eighteen hundred and ninety-four, approved April twenty-eighth, nineteen hundred and four, is hereby amended to read as follows:

> ## § 2.
>
> That this Act shall be null and void unless the structures herein authorized shall be commenced within one year and completed within three years from the date of approval thereof.”


*Approved, March 4, 1907, 11 a. m.*

# Chapter 1619

> An Act To amend and reenact section one of chapter seventy-seven of volume twenty-seven of the United States Statutes at Large, being “An Act to provide for a term of the United States circuit and district courts at Evanston, Wyoming,” approved May twenty-third, eighteen hundred and ninety-two.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section one of chapter seventy-seven of volume twenty-seven of the United States Statutes at Large, being “An Act to provide for a term of the United States circuit and district courts at Evanston. Wyoming.” approved May twenty-third, eighteen hundred and ninety-two, be, and the same is hereby, amended and reenacted so as to read as follows:

> “That hereafter and until otherwise provided by law there shall be held annually, on the second Tuesday in July each year, a term of the circuit and district courts for the district of Wyoming at the town of Evanston, in said district, said term to be in addition to the terms now required by law to be held at the city of Cheyenne, in said district.”


*Approved, April 13, 1906.*

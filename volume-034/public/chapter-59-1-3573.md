# Chapter 3573

> An Act To provide a seal for United States commissioners.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That each United States commissioner shall provide himself with an official impression seal, to be prescribed by the Attorney-General, which said seal shall be affixed to each jurat or certificate of the official acts of said commissioner, but no increase of fees shall be allowed by reason thereof.


*Approved, June 28, 1906.*

# Chapter 2582

> An Act To amend an Act entitled “An Act to amend an Act entitled ‘An Act to incorporate the Masonic Mutual Relief Association of the District of Columbia,’” approved February fifth, nineteen hundred and one.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proviso at the end of section five of the Act of Congress entitled “An Act to amend an Act entitled ‘An Act to incorporate the Masonic Mutual Relief Association of the District of Columbia,’ “approved February fifth, nineteen hundred and one, be, and the same is hereby, amended so that it will read as follows: “Provided, however, That no certificate of membership shall be issued by said corporation for an amount exceeding five thousand dollars.”


*Approved, June 6, 1906.*

# Chapter 2572

> An Act To grant American registry to the German bark Mariechen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of Navigation be, and he is hereby, authorized to register as an American vessel the German bark Mariechen: Provided, however, That such registry shall not give to said vessel the right to engage in the coastwise trade. And this Act is based upon the express condition that said bark is to be used as a barge, and that no application shall be hereafter made for the right for said vessel to engage in the coastwise trade.


*Approved, March 2, 1907.*

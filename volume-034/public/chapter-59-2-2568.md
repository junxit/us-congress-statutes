# Chapter 2568

> An Act For the relief of homestead entrymen who have paid more than the lawful purchase money.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in all cases in which homestead entrymen upon final proof or commutation shall have been required to pay more than the lawful purchase money for their lands, the Secretary of the Interior shall cause the excess to be repaid to the entryman or to his heirs or assigns.


*Approved, March 2, 1907.*

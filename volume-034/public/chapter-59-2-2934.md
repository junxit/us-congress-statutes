# Chapter 2934

> Ac Act To authorize the legislature of Oklahoma to dispose of a certain section of school land.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the legislature of the State of Oklahoma, when the State of Oklahoma shall have been admitted, is hereby authorized to grant section sixteen, in township fourteen, north of range four, east of the Indian meridian, Lincoln County, Oklahoma, to the board of education of the city of Chandler, in said county, for school purposes upon such terms as the said legislature may impose.


*Approved, March 4, 1907, 11 a. m.*

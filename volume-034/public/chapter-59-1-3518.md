# Chapter 3518

> An Act Providing for the retirement of petty officers and enlisted men of the Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in computing the necessary thirty years’ time for the retirement of petty officers and enlisted men of the Navy, all service in the Army, Navy, or Marine Corps shall be credited.


*Approved, June 22, 1906.*

# Chapter 3930

> An Act To grant American registry to the bark Homeward Bound.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of Navigation be, and he is hereby, authorized to register as an American vessel the bark Homeward Bound, now sailing under a limited American register.


*Approved, June 30, 1906.*

# Chapter 2554

> An Act Authorizing the Secretary of the Interior to have a survey made of unsurveyed public lands in the State of Louisiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That pursuant to section twenty-two hundred and eighteen of the Revised Statutes of the United States the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and instructed to cause a survey to be made of all of the unsurveyed public lands in the State of Louisiana lying north of the Louisiana base line and thirty-first degree of north latitude, together with such resurveys and retracements as may be found necessary in order to complete the original surveys herein provided for.


*Approved, May 25, 1906.*

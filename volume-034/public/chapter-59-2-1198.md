# Chapter 1198

> An Act Providing for a United States judge for the northern judicial district of Alabama.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, shall appoint a district judge for the northern judicial district of Alabama, who shall possess and exercise all the powers conferred by existing law upon the judges of the district courts of the United States, and who snail possess the same powers and perform the same duties within the said northern judicial district of Alabama as are now possessed by and performed by the district judge of the United States in any of the judicial districts established by law, and he shall receive the same compensation now or hereafter prescribed by law in respect to other district judges of the United States: And provided, That after appointment the judge appointed under this Act shall reside at Birmingham, in said district.


*Approved, February 25, 1907.*

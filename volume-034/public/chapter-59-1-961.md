# Chapter 961

> An Act Extending the public-land laws to certain lands in Wyoming.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the public-land laws of the United States be, and the same are hereby, extended to the lands embraced within the territory ten miles square ceded to the United States by the Shoshone and Arapaho Indians by the agreement ratified by the Act approved June seventh, eighteen hundred and ninety-seven.


*Approved, March 19, 1906.*

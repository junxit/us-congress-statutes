# Chapter 3577

> An Act To amend an Act to provide for circuit and district courts of the United States at Albany, Georgia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the word “southwestern,” wherever it appears in said Act, be stricken out, and the word “Albany” be inserted in lieu thereof.

## § 2.

That the counties of Tift, Turner, and Crisp be assigned to said division of the southern district of Georgia.

## § 3.

That the county of Colquitt, now in the southwestern division of the southern district of Georgia, be detached from said southwestern division and attached to the Albany division of said district.

## § 4.

That the county of Miller, now in the northern district of Georgia, be detached from said district and attached to the Albany division of the southern district of Georgia.


*Approved, June 28, 1906.*

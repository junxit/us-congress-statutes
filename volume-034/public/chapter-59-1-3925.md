# Chapter 3925

> An Act To authorize the Lake Schutte Cemetery Corporation to convey lands heretofore granted to it.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Lake Schutte Cemetery Corporation, of Dunseith, North Dakota, be, and the same is hereby, authorized and empowered to sell and convey in fee simple all or any part of the south half of the northwest quarter of section thirty, township one hundred and sixty-two north, range seventy-two west, in the State of North Dakota, and that the person or persons to whom the same shall be conveyed shall take and hold the lands so conveyed free and clear of any limitation placed on the use thereof by the Act under which said lands were granted to said corporation.


*Approved, June 30, 1906.*

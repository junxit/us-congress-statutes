# Chapter 1194

> An Act Authorizing the sale of certain lands to the city of Buffalo, Wyoming.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to sell to the city of Buffalo, Johnson County, Wyoming, for the sum of one dollar and twenty live cents an acre, for use as a public park and fair grounds, the following described lands: Lot twelve and the southeast quarter of the southwest quarter of section twenty-seven and the northeast quarter of the north-west quarter, and lot five of section thirty-four, in township fifty-one north of range eighty-two west of the sixth principal meridian.


*Approved, February 25, 1907.*

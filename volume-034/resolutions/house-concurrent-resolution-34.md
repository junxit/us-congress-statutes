# House Concurrent Resolution 34

> johningram.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President be requested to return the bill (H. R. 18214) entitled "An Act granting an increase of pension to John Ingram.”


*Passed January 11, 1907.*

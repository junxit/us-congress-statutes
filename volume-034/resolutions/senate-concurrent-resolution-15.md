# Senate Concurrent Resolution 15

> official misconduct.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Committee of Conference on the disagreeing votes of the two Houses on the amendments of the Senate to the bill (H R 10129) “To amend section fifty-five hundred and one of the Revised Statutes of the United States,” be, and the same is hereby, authorized to agree to an amendment on page 2, line 14, of the bill, by inserting after the word “thereof” the words “and every member of Congress.”


*Passed April 13, 1906.*

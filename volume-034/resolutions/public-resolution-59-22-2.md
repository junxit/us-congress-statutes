# Public Resolution 22

> Joint Resolution Relating to securing a channel of six feet depth over Foys Flats in the Trent River, North Carolina, about four miles above Newbern.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War is authorized to expend such portion of the appropriation made in the river and harbor Act of this session for the Neuse and Trent rivers, North Carolina, as may be necessary for securing a channel of six feet depth over Foys Flats in the Trent River, North Carolina, about four miles above Newbern.


*Approved, March 4, 1907, 10 a. m.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 19

> harbor at duluth, minn.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Secretary of War be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to cause an examination and survey to be made of the harbor at Duluth, Minnesota, including the entrance thereto, with a view to determining what modifications of the present plan, if any, are desirable.


*Passed June 20, 1906.*

# Public Resolution 4

> Joint Resolution Authorizing temporary leaves of absence for homestead settlers.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That homestead settlers upon the public domain, in North Dakota, South Dakota, Wyoming, Minnesota, and Montana, are hereby granted a leave of absence from their land for a period of three months from the date of the approval of this resolution: Provided, That the period of actual absence under this resolution shall not be deducted from the full time of residence required by law.


*Approved, January 18, 1907.*

# Public Resolution 50

> Joint Resolution Providing for the printing of reports ordered by the river and harbor Act of March third, nineteen hundred and five.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That at any time prior to the assembling of Congress in December, nineteen hundred and six, all reports of preliminary examinations and surveys ordered in the river and harbor Act of March third, nineteen hundred and live, that may be ready for printing shall, in the discretion of the Secretary of War, be printed by the Public Printer as documents of the Fifty-ninth Congress.


*Approved, June 30, 1906.*

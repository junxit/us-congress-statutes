# House Concurrent Resolution 8

> kiowa, chomanche, and apache indian reservations, orla.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President be, and hereby is, requested to return to the House the bill (H. R. 431) “To open for settlement five hundred and five thousand acres of land in the Kiowa, Comanche, and Apache Indian Reservations, in Oklahoma Territory.”


*Passed March 26, 1906.*

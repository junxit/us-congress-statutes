# Public Resolution 29

> Joint Resolution To supply a deficiency in an appropriation for the postal service.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of eighty thousand dollars, to supply a deficiency in the appropriation for the manufacture of stamped envelopes and newspaper wrappers for the fiscal year nineteen hundred and six.


*Approved, June 13, 1906.*

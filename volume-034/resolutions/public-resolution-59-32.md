# Public Resolution 32

> Joint Resolution Granting condemned cannon for a statue to Governor Stevens T. Mason, of Michigan.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War is hereby authorized and directed to deliver to the governor of the State of Michigan six bronze or brass condemned cannon, to be used to make a life-size statue of Stevens T. Mason, late governor of Michigan: Provided, That the Government shall be at no expense in connection with this gift.


*Approved, June 23, 1906.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 3

> elijah spangler.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President of the United States be requested to return to the House of Representatives (H. R. 1059) “Granting an increase of pension to Elijah Spangler.”


*Passed February 20, 1906.*

# Public Resolution 28

> Joint Resolution Providing for the printing of five thousand copies of House Document Numbered Six hundred and fifty-one, Fifty-ninth Congress, second session.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be printed and bound in paper covers five thousand copies of House Document Numbered Six hundred and fifty-one, Fifty-ninth Congress, second session, relating to second-class mail matter, one thousand copies for the use of the Senate, two thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives, and two thousand copies for distribution by the Post-Office Department.


*Approved March 4, 1907, 11 a. m.*

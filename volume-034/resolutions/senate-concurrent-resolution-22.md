# Senate Concurrent Resolution 22

> helen c. sanderson.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return the bill (S 3028) “granting an increase of pension to Helen C. Sanderson.”


*Passed June 26, 1906.*

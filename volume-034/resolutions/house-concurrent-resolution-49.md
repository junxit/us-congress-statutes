# House Concurrent Resolution 49

> felix g. morrison.

*Resolved by the House of Representative (the Senate concurring),*

That the President be requested to return the bill (H. R. 21606), entitled “An Act granting an increase of pension to Felix G. Morrison.”


*Passed February 28, 1907.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 18

> byron k. may.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return the bill (S 1510) entitled “An Act granting an increase of pension to Byron K. May.”


*Passed June 14, 1906.*

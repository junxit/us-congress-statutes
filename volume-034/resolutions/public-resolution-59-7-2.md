# Public Resolution 7

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the Secretary of War to furnish two three-inch wrought-iron muzzle-loading cannon, with their carriages, limbers, and accessories, to the State of South Dakota.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized to deliver to the State of South Dakota, if the same can be done without detriment to the public service, two three-inch wrought-iron muzzle-loading cannon, now in South Dakota, with the carriages, limbers, and accessories belonging to them, to be the property of the said State: Provided, That no expense shall be incurred by the United States in the delivery of said cannon, carriages, and accessories.


*Approved, January 23, 1907.*

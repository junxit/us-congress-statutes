# Public Resolution 40

> Joint Resolution To print the Fourth Annual Report of the United States Reclamation Service.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Director of the Geological Survey be, and he is hereby, authorized to print four thousand copies of the Fourth Annual Report of the United States Reclamation Service, the cost thereof to be paid out of the reclamation fund.


*Approved, June 29, 1906.*

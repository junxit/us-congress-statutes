# Senate Concurrent Resolution 41

> john mckinnon, alias john mack.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return the bill (S. 1160) entitled “An Act to correct the military record of John McKinnon, alias John Mack.”


*Passed February 5, 1907.*

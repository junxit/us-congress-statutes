# Public Resolution 38

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the Secretary of War to furnish a certain gun carriage to the mayor of the city of Ripley, Lauderdale County, Tennessee.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and is hereby, authorized to deliver to the mayor of the city of Ripley, Lauderdale County. Tennessee, if the same can be done without detriment to the public service, one ten-inch carriage for Rodman gun now at New York Arsenal. Governors Island. New York Harbor, the same to be used for the mounting thereon of a ten-inch columbiad cannon recovered from the ruins of Fort Pillow. Lauderdale County, Tennessee, by the said municipal corporation of Ripley and the county of Lauderdale, Tennessee, acting through their respective agents and officers, to be used and mounted as a monument in the court-house yard or on the public square in said town of Ripley: Provided, That the donation shall be without expense to the United States.


*Approved, June 28, 1906.*

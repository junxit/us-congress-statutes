# Public Resolution 21

> Joint Resolution For appointment of members of Board of Managers of the National Home for Disabled Volunteer Soldiers.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Charles M. Anderson, of Ohio; William Warner, of Missouri; Franklin Murphy, of New Jersey, and James W. Wadsworth, of New York, be, and the same hereby are, appointed as members of the Board of Managers of the National Home for Disabled Volunteer Soldiers of the United States; Charles M. Anderson, William Warner, and Franklin Murphy to succeed themselves, their terms of service expiring April twenty-first, nineteen hundred and six; James W. Wadsworth to succeed General Martin T. McMahon, deceased, whose term of office expires April twenty-first, nineteen hundred and ten.


*Approved, May 7, 1906.*

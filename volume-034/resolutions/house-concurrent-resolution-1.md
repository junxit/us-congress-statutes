# House Concurrent Resolution 1

> holiday recess.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Thursday, December twenty-first, they stand adjourned until twelve o’clock meridian Thursday, January fourth, nineteen hundred and six.


*Passed December 18, 1905.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 14

> hazing at naval academy.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed five thousand copies of the hearings before the subcommittee of the Committee on Naval Affairs of the House of Representatives. Fifty-ninth Congress, at the United States Naval Academy, Annapolis, Maryland, on the subject of hazing at the Naval Academy, with accompanying report, three thousand five hundred copies for the House of Representatives and one thousand five hundred for the Senate.


*Passed April 10, 1906.*

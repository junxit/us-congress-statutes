# House Concurrent Resolution 32

> holiday recess.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Thursday. December twentieth, they stand adjourned until twelve o’clock Meridian, January third, nineteen hundred and seven.


*Passed December 17, 1906.*

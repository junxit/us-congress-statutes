# House Concurrent Resolution 27

> susan c. smith.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President be requested to return the bill (H. R. 19047) entitled “An Act granting an increase of pension to Susan C. Smith”


*Passed June 29, 1906.*

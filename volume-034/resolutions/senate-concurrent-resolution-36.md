# Senate Concurrent Resolution 36

> louis catinette.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return the bill (S. 3671) entitled “An Act granting an increase of pension to Louis Castinette.”


*Passed January 21, 1907.*

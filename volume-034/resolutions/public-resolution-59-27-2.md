# Public Resolution 27

> Joint Resolution To provide for the printing of two hundred and fifty thousand copies of the special report on the diseases of horses.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be printed and bound in cloth two hundred and fifty thousand copies of the special report on diseases of horses, with accompanying illustrations, the same to be first revised and brought to date under the supervision of the Secretary of Agriculture, one hundred and seventy-five thousand for the use of the House of Representatives, sixty thousand for the use of the Senate, and fifteen thousand for the use of the Department of Agriculture.


*Approved, March 4, 1907, 11 a. m.*

# Public Resolution 29

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the Attorney-General to print eight hundred and fifty copies of the Session Laws.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Attorney-General be, and he is hereby, authorized to have printed, for distribution by the Department of Justice, eight hundred and fifty copies of the Session Laws, and of the Statutes at Large, in lieu of five hundred copies of each as now provided by law.


*Approved, March 4, 1907, 11 a. m.*

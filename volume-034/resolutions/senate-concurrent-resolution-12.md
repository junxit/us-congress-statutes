# Senate Concurrent Resolution 12

> japanese naval medical and sanitary features of russojapanese war.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed four thousand seven hundred and fifty copies of the “Report on the Japanese Naval Medical and Sanitary Features of the Russo-Japanese War to the Surgeon-General, United States Navy.” by Surgeon William C. Braisted, United States Navy, the same to include the illustrations, of which one thousand two hundred and fifty copies shall be for the use of the Senate, two thousand five hundred copies shall be for the use of the House of Representatives and one thousand copies for the use of the Bureau of Medicine and Surgery of the Navy Department.


*Passed April 7, 1906.*

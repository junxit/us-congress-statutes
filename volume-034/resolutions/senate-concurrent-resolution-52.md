# Senate Concurrent Resolution 52

> william n. bronson.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return the bill (S. 7822) entitled “An Act granting an increase of pension to William N. Bronson.”


*Passed March 2, 1907.*

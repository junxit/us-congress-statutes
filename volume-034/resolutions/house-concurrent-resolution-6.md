# House Concurrent Resolution 6

> david a jones.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the House of Representatives the bill (H. R. 8494) “Granting an increase of pension to David A. Jones.”


*Passed, March 12, 1906.*

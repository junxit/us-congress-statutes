# Public Resolution 18

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the wearing of the distinctive badge adopted by the Army and Navy Union upon all occasions of ceremony.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the distinctive badge adopted by the Army and Navy Union of the United States may be worn, in their own right, upon all public occasions of ceremony by officers and enlisted men of the Army and Navy of the United States who are members of said organization.


*Approved, March 2, 1907.*

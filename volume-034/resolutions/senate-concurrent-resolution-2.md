# Senate Concurrent Resolution 2

> dams and power stations on tennessee river, muscle shoals, ala.

*Resolved by the Senate (the Home of Representatives concurring),*

That the President is requested to return to the House of Representatives House Bill 297, “To authorize the construction of dams and power stations on the Tennessee River at Muscle Shoals, Alabama,” for the purpose of amendment.


*Passed February 13, 1906.*

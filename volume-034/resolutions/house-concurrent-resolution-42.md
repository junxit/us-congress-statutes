# House Concurrent Resolution 42

> reuben a. george.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President be requested to return the bill (H. R. 20928) entitled “An Act granting an increase of pension to Reuben A. George.”


*Passed February 6, 1907.*

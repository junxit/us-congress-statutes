# Senate Concurrent Resolution 40

> lorenzo f. harmon.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return the bill (S 1879) entitled “An Act granting an increase of pension to Lorenzo F. Harmon.”


*Passed February 1, 1907.*

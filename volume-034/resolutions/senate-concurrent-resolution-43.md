# Senate Concurrent Resolution 43

> john w. mcwilliams.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate the bill (S. 5854) “Granting an increase of pension to John W. McWilliams,” the beneficiary being dead.


*Passed February 15, 1907.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 47

> marcus wood.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President be requested to return the bill (H. R. 21121), entitled “An Act granting an increase of pension to Marcus Wood.”


*Passed February 25, 1907.*

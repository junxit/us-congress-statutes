# Senate Concurrent Resolution 23

> five civilized tribes.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed one thousand copies of the “Topical index to the twelve Annual Reports of the Commission to the Five Civilized Tribes to the Secretary of the Interior"; two hundred copies for the use of the Senate, four hundred copies for the use of the House of Representatives, and four hundred copies for the use of the Department of the Interior.


*Passed June 28, 1906.*

# Public Resolution 9

> Joint Resolution Extending protection of second proviso of Section one of the Act of December twenty-first, nineteen hundred and four, to certain entrymen.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That where entries were allowed by the local land office prior to December twenty-first, nineteen hundred and four, of hinds purchased from persons who were bona fide settlers on March fifth, nineteen hundred and four, such entrymen shall be entitled to the protection extended by the second proviso of section one of the Act of December twenty-first, nineteen hundred and four, if they have continued to comply in good faith with the requirements of the settlement laws.


*Approved, January 29, 1907.*

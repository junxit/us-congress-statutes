# Public Resolution 2

> Joint Resolution Relating to the contingent expenses of the House of Representatives.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the appropriation made for the miscellaneous items of the contingent expenses of the House of Representatives for the fiscal year nineteen hundred and six is hereby made available for the payment of salaries and labor heretofore or hereafter specifically ordered by the House of Representatives of the present Congress, in addition to the purposes for which said appropriation is otherwise available.


*Approved, January 17, 1906.*

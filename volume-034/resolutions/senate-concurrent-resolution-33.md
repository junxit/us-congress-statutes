# Senate Concurrent Resolution 33

> charles e. henry.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate the bill (S. 6197) entitled “An Act granting an increase of pension to Charles E. Henry.”


*Passed December 20, 1906.*

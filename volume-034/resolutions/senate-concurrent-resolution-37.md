# Senate Concurrent Resolution 37

> daniel g. smith.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return the hill (S. 5073) entitled “An Act granting an increase of pension to Daniel G. Smith.”


*Passed January 21, 1907.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 38

> district of columbia code.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the House of Representatives the bill (H. R. 16944) “An Act to amend section eight hundred and seventy-eight of the Code of law for the District of Columbia.”


*Passed January 28, 1907.*

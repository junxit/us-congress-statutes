# Senate Concurrent Resolution 11

> rochambeau state.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the concurrent resolution passed February two, nineteen hundred and four, providing for the publication of the proceedings on the occasion of the unveiling of the Rochambeau statue is hereby continued in force, and excepted from the limitation of one year, as provided in section eighty of the Act of January twelfth, eighteen hundred and ninety-five, providing for the public printing and binding and the distribution of public documents.


*Passed April 7, 1906.*

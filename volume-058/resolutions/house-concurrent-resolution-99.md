# House Concurrent Resolution 99

> amendment of nationality act of 1940

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Clerk of the House of Representatives, in the enrollment of the bill (H. R. 4271) to amend the Nationality Act of 1940 to preserve the nationality of citizens residing abroad, is authorized and directed to strike out the word “six” which appears in the proviso and insert in lieu thereof the word “five”.


*Passed September 15, 1944.*

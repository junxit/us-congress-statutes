# House Concurrent Resolution 75

> adjournment

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Saturday, April 1, 1944, they stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Wednesday, April 12, 1944.


*Passed March 31, 1944.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 58

> adjournment sine die

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Tuesday, December 19, 1944, and that when they adjourn on said day they stand adjourned sine die.


*Agreed to December 19, 1944.*

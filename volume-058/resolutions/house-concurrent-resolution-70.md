# House Concurrent Resolution 70

> “revenue act of 1943”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed forty-eight thousand additional copies of Public Law Numbered 235, current session, entitled “Revenue Act of 1943”, of which forty thousand copies shall be for the use of the House document room, four thousand copies shall be for the use of the Senate document room, three thousand copies for the use of the Committee on Ways and Means of the House, and one thousand copies for the use of the Committee on Finance of the Senate.


*Passed March 7, 1944.*

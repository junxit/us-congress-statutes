# Senate Concurrent Resolution 47

> signing of enrolled bills, etc.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That notwithstanding the adjournment of the two Houses, as authorized by Senate Concurrent Resolution 46, the Acting President pro tempore of the Senate and the Speaker of the House of Representatives be, and they are hereby, authorized to sign enrolled bills and joint resolutions duly passed by the two Houses which have been examined by the Committee on Enrolled Bills of each House and found truly enrolled.


*Agreed to June 23, 1944.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 51

> evasion of military and naval service

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Clerk of the House of Representatives, in the enrollment of the bill (H. R. 4257) to expatriate or exclude certain persons for evading military and naval service, be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to strike out on page 1, line 7 “(h)” and insert in lieu thereof “(i)”; and on page 1, line 8, strike out “(i)” and insert in lieu thereof “(j)”.


*Agreed to September 18, 1944.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 90

> individual income tax act of 1944

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the manuscript prepared by Representative Daniel A. Reed, containing an analysis of questions and answers relative to the individual income tax payment Act of 1944, be printed as a House document; and that thirty-four thousand additional copies shall be printed for the use of the House document room.


*Passed June 12, 1944.*

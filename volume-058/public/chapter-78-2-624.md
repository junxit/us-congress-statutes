# Chapter 624

> AN ACT To amend the Act entitled “An Act to authorize the use for war purposes of silver held or owned by the United States”, approved July 12, 1943.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2 of the Act entitled “An Act to authorize the use for war purposes of silver held or owned by the United States”, approved July 12, 1943 (Public Law 137, Seventy-eighth Congress), is amended to read as follows:

> ## § 2.
>
> This Act shall expire on December 31, 1945.”


*Approved December 20, 1944.*

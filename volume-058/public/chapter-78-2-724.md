# Chapter 724

> AN ACT To amend section 119 of the Judicial Code.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 119 of the Judicial Code (U. S. C., title 28, sec. 215) is hereby amended by adding at the end thereof the following:

> “For the purposes of this section, the District of Columbia shall be deemed to be a judicial circuit.”


*Approved December 23, 1944.*

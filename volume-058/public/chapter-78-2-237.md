# Chapter 237

> AN ACT To provide for the disposition of tribal funds of the Minnesota Chippewa Tribe of Indians.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding any other provision of existing law, the tribal funds now on deposit or hereafter placed to the credit of the “Minnesota Chippewa Tribe of Indians”, in the United States Treasury, shall be available for such purposes as may be designated by the tribal council of said tribe and approved by the Secretary of the Interior.


*Approved June 7, 1944.*

# Chapter 361

> AN ACT To provide for the transportation to their homes of persons discharged from the naval service because of under age at time of enlistment.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That appropriations which provide for pay, allowances, and transportation of enlisted personnel of the Navy, Marine Corps, and Coast Guard, including reserve components thereof, shall be available for the payment of pay and allowances to and including the date of termination of the enlistment contract, and for transportation home of any enlisted person whose enlistment contract is terminated by cancelation or discharge while under the minimum statutory or administrative age limit by reason of having falsely stated his age on his application for enlistment.


*Approved July 1, 1944.*

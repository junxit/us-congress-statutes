# Chapter 155

> AN ACT To fix the annual compensation of the secretary of the Territory of Alaska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the date of passage of this Act the salary of the secretary of the Territory of Alaska is fixed at $7,500 per annum.

## § 2.

All Acts and parts of Acts in conflict herewith are repealed to the extent of such conflict.


*Approved April 3, 1944.*

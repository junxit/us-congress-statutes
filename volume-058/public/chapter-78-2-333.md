# Chapter 333

> AN ACT To extend the Civilian Pilot Training Act of 1939.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 7 of the Civilian Pilot Training Act of 1939, as amended, is amended by striking out the date “July 1, 1944” in the second sentence of such section and inserting in lieu thereof the date “July 1, 1946”.


*Approved June 30, 1944.*

# Chapter 240

> AN ACT To increase the debt limit of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That this Act may be cited as the Public Debt Act of 1944.

## § 2.

That section 21 of the Second Liberty Bond Act, as amended, is further amended to read as follows:

> ## § 21.
>
> The face amount of obligations issued under the authority of this Act shall not exceed in the aggregate $260,000,000,000 outstanding at any one time.”

## § 3. REDUCTION OF WAR TAX RATE ON CABARETS, ROOF GARDENS, ETC.

- **(a)** *Reduction of Rate.—* Section 1650 of the Internal Revenue Code is amended by striking out “30 per centum” where it appears in the table therein as the war tax rate on cabarets, roof gardens, and so forth, and inserting in lieu thereof “20 per centum”.
- **(b)** *Effective Date.—* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall be applicable only with respect to the period beginning at 10 antemeridian on the first day of the first month following the date of enactment of this Act.


*Approved June 9, 1944.*

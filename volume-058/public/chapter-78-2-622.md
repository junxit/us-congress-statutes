# Chapter 622

> AN ACT To provide that the transmountain tunnel constructed in connection with the Colorado-Big Thompson project shall be known as the “Alva B. Adams tunnel”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the transmountain tunnel constructed in connection with the Colorado-Big Thompson reclamation project shall hereafter be known as the “Alva B. Adams tunnel”.


*Approved December 20, 1944.*

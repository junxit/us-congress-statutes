# Chapter 417

> AN ACT To authorize the Secretary of the Interior to accept property for the Moores Creek National Military Park, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Repesentatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized, in his discretion, to accept in behalf of the United States donations of lands, buildings, structures, and other property, or interests therein, which he may determine to be of historical interest in connection with the Moores Creek National Military Park, the title to such property or interests to be satisfactory to the Secretary of the Interior: Provided, That the area to be accepted pursuant to this Act shall not exceed one hundred acres. All such property and interests, upon acquisition by the Federal Government, shall be a part of the Moores Creek National Military Park and shall be subject to all laws and regulations applicable thereto.


*Approved September 27, 1944.*

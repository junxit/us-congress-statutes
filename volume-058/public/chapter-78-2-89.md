# Chapter 89

> AN ACT To extend for one year the date of termination of Public Law 22, dated April 1, 1943, entitled “To provide for a temporary increase in compensation for certain employees of the District of Columbia Government and the White House Police Force”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2 of Public Law 22, approved April 1, 1943, be amended to read as follows:

> “This Act shall take effect as of December 1, 1942, and shall terminate on June 30, 1945, or such earlier date as the Congress by concurrent resolution may prescribe.”


*Approved March 11, 1944.*

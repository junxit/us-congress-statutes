# Chapter 334

> AN ACT To amend the National Housing Act, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 603 (a) of the National Housing Act, as amended, is hereby amended by (1) striking out “$1,600,000,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$1,700,000,000”; and (2) inserting in the third proviso after the word “except” the figure “(1)” and striking the period at the end of the third proviso and inserting in lieu thereof a semicolon and the following: “or (2) a mortgage that is given to refinance an existing mortgage insured under this title and which does not exceed the original principal amount and unexpired term of such existing mortgage.”


*Approved June 30, 1944.*

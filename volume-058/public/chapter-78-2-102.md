# Chapter 102

> AN ACT To change the name of “laborer” in the Postal Service to that of “mail handler”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the classification “laborer” in the Postal Service is hereby abolished, and in lieu thereof there is hereby created the classification of “mail handler” to perform the same duties and receive the same compensation as laborers.


*Approved March 20, 1944.*

# Chapter 148

> AN ACT To amend the Act approved March 2, 1895, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 5 of the Act of Congress approved March 2, 1895 (28 Stat. 807), as amended by an Act approved March 8, 1928 (45 Stat. 247), is further amended by inserting in the third line of the proviso as it appears on page 247 of volume 45 of the United States Statutes at Large, after the word “employees” the following: “officers and employees of other civilian agencies of the United States and bonded officers and enlisted men of the Army, Navy, Marine Corps, and Coast Guard.”


*Approved March 31, 1944.*

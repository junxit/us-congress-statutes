# Chapter 295

> AN ACT To amend section 12 (b) of the Act of May 29, 1930, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 12 (b) of the Act of May 29, 1930, as amended, is amended by inserting, immediately following the word “employee” where it appears at the end of the first proviso, the following: “Provided further, That no such interest shall be allowed on any separation unless the service covered thereby aggregates more than one year.”


*Approved June 28, 1944.*

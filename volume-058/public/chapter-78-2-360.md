# Chapter 360

> AN ACT To amend section 6 of the Act of July 2, 1940 (54 Stat. 714), relating to the exportation of certain commodities, and to continue said Act in effect.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 6 of the Act of July 2, 1940 (54 Stat. 714), as amended by the Act of June 30, 1942 (56 Stat. 463), is hereby amended by deleting from subsection (b) thereof the words “Board of Economic Warfare” and substituting therefor the words “Foreign Economic Administration” and by deleting from subsection (d) thereof the words “June 30, 1944” and substituting therefor the words “June 30, 1945”.


*Approved July 1, 1944.*

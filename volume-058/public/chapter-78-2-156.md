# Chapter 156

> JOINT RESOLUTION Providing for the employment of Government employees for folding speeches and pamphlets, House of Representatives.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions of the Act of May 10, 1916, as amended by the Act of August 29, 1916, the Sergeant at Arms of the Senate and the Doorkeeper of the House of Representatives are hereby respectively authorized, during the Seventy-eighth Congress, to employ, whenever necessary, the services of Government employees for folding speeches and pamphlets at the prevailing rates provided by law.


*Approved April 3, 1944.*

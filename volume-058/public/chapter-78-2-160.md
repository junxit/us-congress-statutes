# Chapter 160

> AN ACT To enable the Secretary of the Interior to complete payment of awards in connection with the war minerals relief statutes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in order to enable the Secretary of the Interior to complete payment of awards under the Act entitled “An Act to amend section 5 of the Act of March 2, 1919, generally known as the ‘war minerals relief statutes’”, approved May 18, 1936 (49 Stat. 1355), there is hereby authorized to be appropriated the sum of $54,775.82, in addition to the sum authorized by such Act of May 18, 1936.


*Approved April 4, 1944.*

# Chapter 276

> AN ACT Relating to the computation of interest on contributions to the civil service retirement fund returned to employees upon their separation from the service.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 12 (b), as amended, of the Civil Service Retirement Act of May 29, 1930, as amended (U. S. C., title 5, sec. 724 (b)), is amended by inserting at the end thereof the following: “In computing interest under this subsection, a fractional part of a month in the total service of an officer or employee shall be disregarded.”


*Approved June 26, 1944.*

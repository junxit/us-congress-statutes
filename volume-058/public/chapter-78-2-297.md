# Chapter 297

> AN ACT To remove restrictions on establishing post-office branches and stations.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the third proviso in the Act of June 9, 1896, entitled “An Act making appropriations for the service of the Post Office Department for the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, eighteen hundred and ninety-seven” (29 Stat. 313; 39 U. S. C. 160), is hereby suspended for the duration of the present war.

## § 2.

This Act shall remain in effect for the duration of the present war and for six months thereafter.


*Approved June 28, 1944.*

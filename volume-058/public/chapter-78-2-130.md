# Chapter 130

> AN ACT To provide titles for heads of staff departments of the United States Marine Corps, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That hereafter officers serving as heads of the Paymaster’s Department and the Quartermaster’s Department of the United States Marine Corps shall have the title of “The Paymaster General of the Marine Corps” and “The Quartermaster General of the Marine Corps”, respectively.

## § 2.

All laws or parts of laws now in force relating to the staff departments of the United States Marine Corps, except as provided in section 1 of this Act, shall remain in full force and effect.


*Approved March 24, 1944.*

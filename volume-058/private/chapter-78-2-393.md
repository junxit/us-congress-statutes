# Chapter 393

> AN ACT Confirming the claim of the heirs of Monroe Johnson, deceased, to certain lands in the State of Mississippi, county of Adams.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all the right, title, and interest of the United States in and to section 47, township 7 north, range 1 west, in Adams County, Mississippi, consisting of approximately sixty-two acres, be, and the same is hereby, granted, released, relinquished, and conveyed in fee simple by the United States of America to the heirs of Monroe Johnson, deceased, as such heirs may be determined according to the laws of descent and distribution of the State of Mississippi; said land being the same land conveyed by homestead entry numbered 34838 of Monroe Johnson, deceased, late of Washington, Mississippi.


*Approved July 1, 1944.*

# Chapter 43

> AN ACT For the relief of Tom S. Steed.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limitations of time in sections 15 to 20, both inclusive, of the Act entitled, “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes”, approved September 7, 1916, as amended, are hereby waived in favor of Tom S. Steed, a former employee of the Department of Agriculture, Soil Conservation Service, Winnsboro, Texas, for disability alleged to have resulted from an injury sustained by him on April 19, 1937, and the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission is hereby authorized to consider and determine the merits of his claim under the remaining provisions of said Act.


*Approved February 22, 1944.*

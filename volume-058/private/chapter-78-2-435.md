# Chapter 435

> AN ACT For the relief of Paul Szeliga.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws the Attorney General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to cancel the warrant of arrest and the order of deportation heretofore issued against Paul Szeliga. Hereafter, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, such alien shall be deemed to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence on June 1, 1923.


*Approved September 27, 1944.*

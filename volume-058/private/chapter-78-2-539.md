# Chapter 539

> AN ACT For the relief of Victoria Cormier.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Mrs. Victoria Cormier, Charlestown, Maryland, the sum of $250, in full payment of all claims against the United States for injuries sustained, and expenses in connection therewith, as a result of an accident on July 3, 1943, near Charlestown, Maryland, when Navy station wagon numbered 6963 collided with an automobile in which she was riding.


*Approved December 7, 1944.*

# Chapter 487

> AN ACT To amend the Act entitled “An Act for the confirmation of the title to the Saline Lands in Jackson County, State of Illinois, to D. H. Brush, and others”, approved March 2, 1861.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act entitled “An Act for the confirmation of the title to the Saline Lands in Jackson County, State of Illinois, to D. H. Brush, and others”, approved March 2, 1861 (12 Stat. 891), is amended by striking out so much thereof as reads as follows: “To Stephen Holliday, the southwest quarter of the southeast quarter of the southeast quarter of section thirty-one, township eight, of range two:”, and inserting in lieu thereof the following: “To Stephen Holliday, the southeast quarter of the southeast quarter of section thirty-one, township eight, of range two:”.

## § 2.

The amendment made by the first section of this Act shall be effective as of March 2, 1861.


*Approved November 29, 1944.*

# Chapter 431

> AN ACT For the relief of Freda Utley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, the Attorney General shall not consider as applicable to Freda Utley the provisions of the Act of October 16, 1918 (40 Stat. 1012), as amended by the Acts of June 5, 1920 (41 Stat. 1008), and June 28, 1940 (Public, Numbered G70, Seventy-sixth Congress, ch. 439).


*Approved September 27, 1944.*

# Public Law 86–305

> JOINT RESOLUTION Establishing that the second regular session of the Eighty-sixth Congress convene at noon on Wednesday, January 6, 1960.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second regular session of the Eighty-sixth Congress shall begin at noon on Wednesday, January 6, 1960.


*Approved September 21, 1959.*

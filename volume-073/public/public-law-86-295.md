# Public Law 86–295

> AN ACT To amend the Mineral Leasing Act of February 25, 1920.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph (2) of section 6(b) of the Federal Airport Act (49 U.S.C., sec. 1105(b) (2)) is amended to read as follows:

> - **(2)** Such discretionary fund shall be available for such approved projects in the several States, Alaska, and Hawaii as the Administrator may deem most appropriate for carrying out the national airport plan, regardless of the location of such projects. The Administrator shall give consideration, in determining the projects for which such fund is to be so used, to the existing airport facilities in the several States, Alaska, and Hawaii, and to the need for or lack of development of airport facilities in the several States, Alaska, and Hawaii.”


*Approved September 21, 1959.*

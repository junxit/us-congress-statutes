# Public Law 86–154

> AN ACT Granting the consent of Congress to interstate compacts for the development or operation of airport facilities.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the consent of Congress is hereby given to each of the several States to enter into any agreement or compact, not in conflict with any law of the United States, with any other State or States for the purpose of developing or operating airport facilities. The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is expressly reserved.


*Approved August 11, 1959.*

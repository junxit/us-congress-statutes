# Public Law 86–202

> AN ACT To amend the Act entitled “An Act to incorporate St. Ann’s Infant Asylum, in the District of Columbia”, approved March 3, 1863, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first section of the Act entitled “An Act to incorporate St. Ann’s Infant Asylum, in the District of Columbia”, approved March 3, 1863 (12 Stat. 798), as amended by the Act of October 3, 1942 (56 Stat. 768), is further amended (1) by striking out “Saint Ami’s Infant Asylum” and inserting in lieu thereof “Saint Ann’s Infant and Maternity Home”; (2) by striking out “in the city of Washington, in the District of Columbia,”; and (3) by striking out “not exceeding in value at any one time $1,000,000,”.


*Approved August 25, 1959.*

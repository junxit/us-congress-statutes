# Public Law 86–358

> AN ACT To amend the Agricultural Adjustment Act of 1938 to extend for two years the definition of “peanuts” which is now in effect.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the last paragraph of the Act entitled “An Act to amend the peanut marketing quota provisions of the Agricultural Adjustment Act of 1938, as amended, and for other purposes”, approved August 13, 1957 (7 U.S.C. 1359 note), is amended by striking the word “and” and inserting after the figure “1959” the words “, 1960 and 1961”.


*Approved September 22, 1959.*

# Public Law 86–67

> AN ACT To direct the Secretary of the Interior to administer certain acquired lands as revested Oregon and California railroad grant lands.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior shall hereafter administer the east half, section 24, township 21 south, range 8 west, Willamette Meridian, in the State of Oregon, as revested Oregon and California railroad grant lands under the Act of August 28, 1937 (50 Stat. 874), as amended (43 U.S.C. 1181a–1181j), and all other laws which are or may become applicable to such lands.


*Approved June 23, 1959.*

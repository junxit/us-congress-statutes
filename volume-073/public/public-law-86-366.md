# Public Law 86–366

> AN ACT To extend the duration of the Federal air pollution control law, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limitations and restrictions contained in section 142, title 28, of the United States Code, shall be waived insofar as pertains to holding court by the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Oklahoma at Durant, Oklahoma.


*Approved September 22, 1959.*

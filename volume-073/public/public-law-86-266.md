# Public Law 86–266

> JOINT RESOLUTION To change the name of Roosevelt Dam, Reservoir, and Power Plant in Arizona to Theodore Roosevelt Dam, Lake, and Power Plant.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the dam, reservoir, and power plant in Arizona, known as Roosevelt Dam, Reservoir, and Power Plant, shall hereafter be known as Theodore Roosevelt Dam, Lake, and Power Plant, and any law, regulation, document, or record of the United States in which such dam, reservoir, and power Slant are designated or referred to under the name Roosevelt Dam, Reservoir, and Power Plant shall be held to refer to such dam, reservoir, and power plant under and by the name of Theodore Roosevelt Dam, Lake, and Power Plant.


*Approved September 14, 1959.*

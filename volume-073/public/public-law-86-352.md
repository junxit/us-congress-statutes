# Public Law 86–352

> JOINT RESOLUTION To authorize the designation of the period of October 17 to October 24, 1959, as National Olympic Week.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, effective January 1, 1960, the Act of May 18, 1928 (36 U.S.C. 143), is amended by striking out “May 1” and inserting in lieu thereof “the first Monday in October”.


*Approved September 22, 1959.*

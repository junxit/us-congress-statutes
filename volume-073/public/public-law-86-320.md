# Public Law 86–320

> AN ACT To amend section 1915 of title 28, United States Code, relating to proceedings in forma pauperis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1915(a) of title 28, United States Code, is amended by deleting the word “citizen” and inserting in place thereof the word “person”.


*Approved September 21, 1959.*

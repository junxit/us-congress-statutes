# Public Law 86–83

> AN ACT To amend the Act of March 3, 1901, to eliminate the requirement that certain District of Columbia corporations be managed by not more than fifteen trustees.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 608 of the Act entitled “An Act to establish a code of law for the District of Columbia”, approved March 3, 1901, as amended (D.C. Code, sec. 29–204), is amended by striking out “nor more than fifteen”.


*Approved July 13, 1959.*

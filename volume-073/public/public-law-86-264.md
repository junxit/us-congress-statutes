# Public Law 86–264

> AN ACT To authorize the erection of a plaque in honor of the late Honorable Sam D. McReynolds on or near the site of the Chickamauga Dam.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Sam D. McReynolds Memorial Committee is hereby authorized to place, at no cost to the United States, on or near the site of the Chickamauga Dam a suitable memorial plaque in honor and appreciation of the late Honorable Sam D. McReynolds, formerly a Member of the House of Representatives, for his contributions to the erection of such dam and for his interest in and contributions to the success of the Tennessee Valley Authority and the purposes for which such Authority was created. The suitability of the size, design, and exact location of such plaque shall be subject to the approval of the Board of Directors of the Tennessee Valley Authority.


*Approved September 14, 1959.*

# Public Law 86–333

> AN ACT To provide that the tax exemption heretofore accorded the Veterans of Foreign Wars with respect to certain property in the District of Columbia, formerly owned by such organization but never used for its Intended purpose, shall apply instead to other property subsequently acquired and used for that purpose.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act en-titled “An Act to exempt from taxation certain property of the Veterans of Foreign Wars of the United States in the District of Columbia”, approved July 19, 1954 (Public Law 510, Eighty-third Con- gress), is amended by striking out “the property situated in square 724 in the city of Washington, District of Columbia, described as lots 819, 820, 821, 822, 823, and 824” and inserting in lieu thereof “the property situated in square 757 in the city of Washington, District of Columbia, described as lots 38, 20, and 19”.


*Approved September 21, 1959.*

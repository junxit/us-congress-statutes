# Public Law 86–347

> AN ACT To repeal the Act of May 27, 1912, which authorized and directed the Secretary of the Treasury to sell certain land to the First Baptist Church of Plymouth, Massachusetts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of May 27, 1912 (37 Stat. 117, ch. 134) is hereby repealed.


*Approved September 22, 1959.*

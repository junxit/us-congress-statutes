# Public Law 86–103

> AN ACT To extend the authority of the Administrator of Veterans’ Affairs to maintain offices in the Republic of the Philippines.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection 230 (b) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking out “1960” and inserting in lieu thereof “1970”.


*Approved July 23, 1959.*

# Public Law 86–131

> AN ACT To amend the Federal Crop Insurance Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 508(a) of the Federal Crop Insurance Act (7 U.S.C. 1508(a)), as amended, is amended by amending the eleventh sentence thereof to read as follows: “Insurance shall not be provided on any agricultural commodity in any county in which the Board determines that the income from such commodity constitutes an unimportant part of the total agricultural income of the county, except that insurance may be provided for producers on farms situated in a local producing area bordering on a county with a crop-insurance program.”


*Approved August 4, 1959.*

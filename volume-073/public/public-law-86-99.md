# Public Law 86–99

> AN ACT To extend for a period of two years the privilege of free importation of gifts from members of the armed forces of the United States on duty abroad.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2 of the Act of December 5, 1942, entitled “An Act to accord free entry to bona fide gifts from members of the armed forces of the United States on duty abroad”, as amended (U.S.C., title 50 App., sec. 847), is amended by striking out “July 1, 1959” and inserting in lieu thereof “July 1, 1961”.


*Approved July 17, 1959.*

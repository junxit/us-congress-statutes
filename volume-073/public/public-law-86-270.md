# Public Law 86–270

> JOINT RESOLUTION Authorizing and requesting the President to issue a proclamation with respect to the 1950 Pacific Festival, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is authorized and requested to issue a proclamation inviting foreign nations to participate in the 1959 Pacific Festival which is being held at San Francisco, California, from September 18, 1959, to September 27, 1959, inclusive.


*Approved September 14, 1959.*

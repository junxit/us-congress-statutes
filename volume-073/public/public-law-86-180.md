# Public Law 86–180

> AN ACT To amend the Act entitled “An Act for the relief of the city of Fort Myers, Florida, and Lee County, Florida”, approved July 22, 1958.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph (a) of the first section of the Act entitled “An Act for the relief of the city of Fort Myers, Florida, and Lee County, Florida”, approved July 22, 1958 (72 Stat. 401), is amended by striking out “$137,997.64” and inserting in lieu thereof “$141,997.64”.


*Approved August 24, 1959.*

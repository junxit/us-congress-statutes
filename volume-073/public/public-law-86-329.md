# Public Law 86–329

> AN ACT To amend section 35 of chanter III of the Act of June 19, 1934, entitled “An Act to regulate the business of life insurance in the District of Columbia”, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 35 of chapter III of the Act of June 19, 1934, entitled “An Act to regulate the business of life insurance in the District of Columbia” (sec. 35–535, D.C. Code 1951 edition), as amended, is amended by striking out the figure “40” in the first clause of subsection (5) (a) and inserting in lieu thereof the figure “33⅓”.


*Approved September 21, 1959.*

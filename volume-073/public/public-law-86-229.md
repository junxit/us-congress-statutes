# Public Law 86–229

> AN ACT To amend the law relating to the distribution of the funds of the Creek Tribe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 5 of the Act of August 1, 1955 (69 Stat. 431), is amended by changing “$200,000” to “$325,000”.


*Approved September 8, 1959.*

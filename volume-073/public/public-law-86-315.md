# Public Law 86–315

> AN ACT To authorize the use of Great Lakes vessels on the oceans.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of the Merchant Ship Sales Act of 1946, as amended (60 Stat. 41, 50 U.S.C. 1735, as amended by Public Law 856, Eighty-first Congress), and contracts executed thereunder, vessels purchased from the United States for exclusive use on the Great Lakes, including the Saint Lawrence River and Gulf, and their connecting waterways, may be operated in any trades and in any manner permitted to other vessels documented under the laws of the United States.


*Approved September 21, 1959.*

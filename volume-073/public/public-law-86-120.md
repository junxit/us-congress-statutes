# Public Law 86–120

> AN ACT To extend the provisions of title XII of the Merchant Marine Act, 1936, relating to war risk insurance, for an additional five years, ending September 7, 1965.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1214 of title XII of the Merchant Marine Act, 1936, as amended (U.S.C., title 46, sec. 1294), is amended by striking out “10 years” and inserting in lieu thereof “15 years”.


*Approved July 31, 1959.*

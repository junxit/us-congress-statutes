# Public Law 86–259

> AN ACT To amend section 4161 of title 18, United States Code, relating to computation of good time allowances for prisoners.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first paragraph of section 4161 of title 18, United States Code, is amended by striking out the words “to be credited as earned and computed monthly”.


*Approved September 14, 1959.*

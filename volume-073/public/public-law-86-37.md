# Public Law 86–37

> AN ACT To suspend temporarily the tax on the processing of palm oil, palm-kernel oil, and fatty acids, salts, and combinations, or mixtures thereof.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the tax imposed under section 4511(a) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1954 shall not apply with respect to the first domestic processing of palm oil, palm-kernel oil, fatty acids derived therefrom, or salts thereof, or of any combination or mixture solely because such combination or mixture contains a substantial quantity of one or more of such oils, fatty acids, or salts, during the period beginning with the first day of the first month which begins more than 10 days after the date of the enactment of this Act and ending with the close of June 30, 1960.


*Approved May 29, 1959.*

# Public Law 86–56

> AN ACT To revise the minimum charge on pieces of mail of odd sizes and shapes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the third proviso contained in section 3 of the Act of October 30, 1951, as amended (39 U.S.C. 290a–1), is amended by striking out “6 cents” and inserting in lieu thereof “3½ cents”.


*Approved June 23, 1959.*

# Public Law 86–203

> AN ACT To designate the Coyote Valley Reservoir in California as Lake Mendocino.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the body of water created by the Coyote Valley Dam in Mendocino County, California, and known as the “Coyote Valley Reservoir” shall hereafter be known and designated as “Lake Mendocino”. Any law, regulation, document, or record of the United States in which such body of water is designated or referred to under and by the name of “Coyote Valley Reservoir” shall be held and considered to refer to such body of water under and by the name of “Lake Mendocino”.


*Approved August 25, 1959.*

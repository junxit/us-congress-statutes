# Public Law 86–224

> JOINT RESOLUTION Amending a Joint resolution making temporary appropriations for the fiscal year 1960, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1 of the Act of July 31, 1959 (Public Law 86–118), is hereby amended by striking out “August 31, 1959” and inserting in lieu thereof “September 30, 1959”.

## § 2.

The amounts appropriated by section 2 of Public Law 86–118 are hereby increased as follows: Mutual security programs from “$300 000,000” to “$430 000,000”.


*Approved September 3, 1959.*

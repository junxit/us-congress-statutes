# Public Law 86–335

> AN ACT To improve the active duty promotion opportunity of Air Force officers from the grade of captain to the grade of major.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, during the period beginning on the date of enactment of this Act and ending at the close of June 30, 1961, any authorized strength prescribed for the grade of major by or under section 8202 of title 10, United States Code, may be exceeded by not more than three thousand.


*Approved September 21, 1959.*

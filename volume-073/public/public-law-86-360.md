# Public Law 86–360

> AN ACT To repeal section 217 of the Merchant Marine Act, 1936, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 217 of the Merchant Marine Act, 1936, as amended (56 Stat. 171; 46 U.S.C. 1127), is hereby repealed.


*Approved September 22, 1959.*

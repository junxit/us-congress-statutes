# Public Law 86–119

> AN ACT To extend the voluntary home mortgage credit program.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 610(a) of the Housing Act of 1954 is amended by striking out “July 31, 1959” and inserting in lieu thereof “September 30, 1959”.


*Approved July 31, 1959.*

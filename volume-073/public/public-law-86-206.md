# Public Law 86–206

> AN ACT To amend the Uniform Narcotic Drug Act of the District of Columbia, as amended, to permit paregoric to be dispensed by oral as well as written prescription.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Howe of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 10 of the Uniform Narcotic Drug Act of the District of Columbia, as amended (52 Stat. 790; sec. 33–410, D.C. Code, 1951 edition), is amended by striking out in the third sentence of the last paragraph thereof “without a written prescription” and inserting in lieu thereof “without a written or oral prescription”.


*Approved August 25, 1959.*

# Public Law 86–33

> AN ACT To provide for the training of postmasters under the Government Employees Training Art.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4(a) (5) of the Government Employees Training Act (72 Stat. 329; 5 U.S.C. 2303(a)(5)) is amended by inserting “(other than a postmaster)” immediately following the word “Senate”.


*Approved May 26, 1959.*

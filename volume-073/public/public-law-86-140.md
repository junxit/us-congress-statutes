# Public Law 86–140

> AN ACT To amend title 10, United States Code, to eliminate the requirement that each chaplain make an annual report to the Secretary of the Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (d) of section 6031 of title 10, United States Code, is hereby repealed.


*Approved August 7, 1959.*

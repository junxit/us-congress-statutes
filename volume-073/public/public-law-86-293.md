# Public Law 86–293

> AN ACT To amend the Bankruptcy Act in regard to the verification of pleadings.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subdivision c of section 18 of the Bankruptcy Act is amended to read as follows: “c. Petitions for both voluntary and involuntary bankruptcy shall be verified under oath.”


*Approved September 21, 1959.*

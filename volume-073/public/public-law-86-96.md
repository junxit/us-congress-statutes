# Public Law 86–96

> AN ACT To extend the special enlistment programs provided by section 262 of the Armed Forces Reserve Act of 1952, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 262 of the Armed Forces Reserve Act of 1952, as amended (50 U.S.C. 1013), is further amended by deleting the date “August 1, 1959” in the first sentence of section 262(a) and inserting in lieu thereof the date “August 1, 1963”.


*Approved July 17, 1959.*

# Public Law 86–187

> AN ACT To amend title 38 of the United States Code to provide that multiple sclerosis developing a 10 per centum or more degree of disability within three years after separation from active service shall be presumed to be service connected.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 312(4) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking out “two” and inserting in lieu thereof “three”.


*Approved August 25, 1959.*

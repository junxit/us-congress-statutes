# Private Law 86–68

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Radboud Louwrens Beukenkamp.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of title III of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Radboud Louwrens Beukenkamp (XXXXXXXXX) shall be held and considered to have been admitted to the United States for permanent residence on September 1, 1959, and to have complied with the residential and physical presence requirements of section 316 of the said Act.


*Approved July 13, 1959.*

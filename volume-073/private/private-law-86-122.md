# Private Law 86–122

> AN ACT For the relief of certain aliens.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Surma, David, Esther, Theodor, Rowena, Sargon, Marie, Plarim, Elishwa, Sulty, Paul, Sophia, Surma (daughter of Paul and Sophia), Eshaya, Virginia, George and Mersina D-Mar Shimum and Mrs. Khanna Zecharia, shall be held and considered to be classifiable as non-quota immigrants under the provisions of section 101(a) (27) (F) and section 204 of that Act shall not be applicable in their cases.


*Approved August 24, 1959.*

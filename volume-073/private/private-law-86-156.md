# Private Law 86–156

> AN ACT To exempt from all taxation certain property of the Association For Childhood Education International In the District of Columbia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the real prop-erty situated in square 1908 in the city of Washington, District of Columbia, described as lots 11, 801, 806, and 807, owned by the Association For Childhood Education International, a District of Columbia corporation, and all personal property located thereon, is hereby exempt from all taxation so long as the same is owned, occupied and used by the Association For Childhood Education International for its educational and other corporate purposes and is not used for commercial or income producing purposes, subject to the provisions of sections 2, 3, and 5 of the Act entitled “An Act to define the real property exempt from taxation in the District of Columbia”, approved December 24, 1942 (56 Stat. 1089; D.C. Code, secs. 47–801b, 47–801c and 47–801e).


*Approved September 9, 1959.*

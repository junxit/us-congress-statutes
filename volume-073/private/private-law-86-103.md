# Private Law 86–103

> AN ACT For the relief of Tse Man Chan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Tse Man Chan shall be held and considered to be the minor alien child of Mrs. Alice Lee Chan, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 18, 1959.*

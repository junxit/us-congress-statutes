# Private Law 86–135

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Clare M. Ash.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for purposes of section 202(3) of the Social Security Act, Mrs. Clare M. Ash, of Racine, Wisconsin, shall be held and considered to be the widow (as defined in section 216(c) of such Act) of Frank S. Ash (social security account number XXXXXXXXXXX.


*Approved September 1, 1959.*

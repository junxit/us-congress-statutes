# Private Law 86–96

> AN ACT For the relief of Abraham Fye.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (d) of section 16 of the War Claims Act of 1948 and section 105 of the War Claims Act amendments of 1954 are each hereby waived in favor of Abraham Fye, of Brooklyn, New York, and his claim for benefits under section 16 of the War Claims Act of 1948 is hereby authorized and directed to be acted upon under the remaining provisions of the War Claims Act of 1948, if he files claim for such benefits with the Foreign Claims Settlement Commission within the six-month period which begins on the date of enactment of this Act.


*Approved August 11, 1959.*

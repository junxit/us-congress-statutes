# Private Law 86–5

> AN ACT For the relief of Logan Duff.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 212 of the Act of June 30, 1932 (5 U.S.C. 59a), is waived for the period beginning February 16, 1946, and ending May 23, 1954, both dates inclusive, insofar as it applied to First Lieutenant Logan Duff, retired (Army serial number XXXXXXX), and he is relieved of liability to repay to the United States the sum of $6,677.08, which was erroneously paid to him as retired pay and as civilian salary during such period while he was employed by the officers’ open mess, Fort Benning, Georgia. In the audit and settlement of the accounts of any certifying or disbursing officer of the United States, full credit shall be given for all amounts for which liability is relieved by this Act.


*Approved May 13, 1959.*

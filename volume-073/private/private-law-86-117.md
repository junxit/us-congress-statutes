# Private Law 86–117

> AN ACT For the relief of Alan Alfred Coleman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstand-ing the provision of section 212(a)(4) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Alan Alfred Coleman may be issued a visa and be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if he is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the said Act: And provided further, That the exemption granted herein shall apply only to a ground for exclusion of which the Department of Justice or the Department of State has knowledge prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved August 21, 1959.*

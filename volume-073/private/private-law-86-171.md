# Private Law 86–171

> AN ACT For the relief of Pauline D. Kimbrough.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Mrs. Pauline D. Kimbrough, widow of Colonel J. Claude Kimbrough, deceased, is hereby relieved of all liability to repay to the United States the sum of $5,577.89, representing gratuity pay received by her upon the death of her husband; the Judge Advocate General of the Army having determined subsequent to such payment that the said Colonel Kimbrough’s status at the time of his death was not that of a retired officer on active duty and that no authority existed for payment of such gratuity.


*Approved September 16, 1959.*

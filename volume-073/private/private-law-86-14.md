# Private Law 86–14

> AN ACT For the relief of Lois K. Alexander.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Lois K. Alexander of New York, New York, be relieved of all liability to repay to the United States the sum of $105.95, representing reimbursement of actual transportation costs incurred by Lois K. Alexander in transporting her household effects from Washington, District of Columbia, to New York City as a result of her being transferred by the Housing and Home Finance Agency from Washington, District of Columbia, to a new duty station in New York City.


*Approved May 13, 1959.*

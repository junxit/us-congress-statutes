# Private Law 86–145

> AN ACT For the relief of Chung Ching Wei.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Chung Ching Wei shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of March 18, 1947.


*Approved September 8, 1959.*

# Private Law 86–39

> AN ACT For the relief of Stanislawa Siedlecka (Rejman).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child Stanislawa Siedlecka (Rejman) shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mrs. Katarzyna Siedlecka, a citizen of the United States: Provided, That no natural parent of the beneficiary, by virtue of such parentage, shall be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved July 6, 1959.*

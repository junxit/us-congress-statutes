# Private Law 86–26

> AN ACT For the relief of Miss Mame E. Howell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the designation on April 2, 1940, by Lucy Howell Netherton, deceased former employee of the Veterans’ Administration, of Miss Mame E. Howell. Louisville, Kentucky, as the sole beneficiary entitled to payment of the amount of $4,954.85 in the civil service retirement and disability fund to the credit of the said Lucy Howell Netherton, shall be held and considered to be, and at all times on and after April 2, 1940, to have been, in full force and effect.


*Approved June 23, 1959.*

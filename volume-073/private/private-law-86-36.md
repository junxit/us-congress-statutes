# Private Law 86–36

> AN ACT For the relief of Ben Chassin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, as amended, Ben Chassin shall be held and considered to be within the purview of section 354(5) of that Act.


*Approved July 6, 1959.*

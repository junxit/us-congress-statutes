# Private Law 86–111

> AN ACT For the relief of Theopi Englezos.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Theopi Englezos shall be held and considered to have been born in Turkey.


*Approved August 21, 1959.*

# Private Law 86–60

> AN ACT For the relief of Jessie Isobel Foster.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Attorney General is authorized and directed to cancel any outstanding order and warrant of deportation, warrant of arrest, and bonds, which may have issued in the case of Jessie Isobel Foster. From and after the date of the enactment of this Act, the said Jessie Isobel Foster shall not again be subject to deportation by reason of the same facts upon which such deportation proceedings were commenced or any such warrants and orders have issued: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved July 6, 1959.*

# Private Law 86–41

> AN ACT For the relief of Umeko Parker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Umeko Parker, the widow of a United States citizen, shall be deemed to be within the purview of section 101(a) (27) (A) of that Act, and the provisions of section 205 of the said Act shall not be applicable in this case.


*Approved July 6, 1959.*

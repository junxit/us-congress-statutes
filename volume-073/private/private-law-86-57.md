# Private Law 86–57

> AN ACT For the relief of Saeko Higa and Masako Higa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Saeko Higa and Masako Higa shall be deemed to be within the purview of section 202(b)(3) of that Act


*Approved July 6, 1959.*

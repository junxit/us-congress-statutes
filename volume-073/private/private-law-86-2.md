# Private Law 86–2

> AN ACT For the relief of Elizabeth Lucie Leon (also known as Lucie Noel).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Elizabeth Lucie Leon (also known as Lucie Noel) shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence on January 2, 1951, upon payment of the required visa fee, and shall be permitted to file a petition for naturalization and to be naturalized notwithstanding the provisions of section 316(a) (1) and (2) and section 316(b) of that Act.


*Approved May 4, 1959.*

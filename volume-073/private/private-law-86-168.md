# Private Law 86–168

> AN ACT For the relief of Ourania Ben Bilkas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the pur-poses of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Ourania Ben Blikas shall be held and considered to be the natural-born minor-alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Ben John Blikas, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural mother of the beneficiary shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved September 14, 1959.*

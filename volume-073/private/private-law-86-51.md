# Private Law 86–51

> AN ACT For the relief of Giovanni Malara.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Giovanni Malara, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Giovanni and Margherita Malara, citizens of the United States: Provided, That no natural parent of Giovanni Malara, by virtue of such parentage, shall be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved July 6, 1959.*

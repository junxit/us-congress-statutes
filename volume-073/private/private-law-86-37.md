# Private Law 86–37

> AN ACT For the relief of Yaeko Inouye.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Yaeko Inouye shall be held and considered to be a returning resident alien within the purview of section 101(a) (27) (B) of that Act.


*Approved July 6, 1959.*

# Private Law 86–118

> AN ACT For the relief of Herman Luchner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of section 212(a)(9) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Herman Luchner may be issued a visa and be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act: Provided, That this exemption shall apply only to a ground for exclusion or which the Department of State or the Department of Justice has knowledge prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved August 21, 1959.*

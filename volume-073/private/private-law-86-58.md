# Private Law 86–58

> AN ACT For the relief of Anthony Elio Monacelli.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203(a) (3) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Anthony Elio Monacelli, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Elio Monacelli, lawful permanent residents of the United States: Provided, That the natural mother of Anthony Elio Monacelli, by virtue of such parentage, shall not be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved July 6, 1959.*

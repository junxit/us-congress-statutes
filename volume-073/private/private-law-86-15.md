# Private Law 86–15

> AN ACT For the relief of Hilary W. Jenkins, Junior.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Hilary W. Jenkins, Junior, Falls Church, Virginia. Mrs. Lillian Hawes, Mrs. Mable L. Jenkins, and Mrs. Minnie N. Graham are hereby relieved of liability to pay to the United States the sum of $627.42. Such sum represents the amount of indebtedness of the said Hilary W. Jenkins, Junior, to the United States resulting from the non-deduction, without fault on his part, of certain amounts of his voluntary allotments and statutory contribution, as a soldier in the Army of the United States, during the period from October 1, 1942, to September 30, 1945, both dates inclusive and paid to the other persons named in this Act. In the audit and settlement of the accounts of any certifying or disbursing officer, full credit shall be given for the amount for which liability is relieved by this Act.


*Approved May 13, 1959.*

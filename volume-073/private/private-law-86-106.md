# Private Law 86–106

> AN ACT For the relief of certain officers of the Public Health Service.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the following-named retired officers of the United States Public Health Service are hereby relieved of all liability for payment to the United States of the following stated sums, such sums representing overpayments of retired pay as a result of unauthorized recomputations of their retired pay under the provisions of the Career Compensation Act of 1949: Doctor Charles V. Akin, $9,705.12; Doctor Richard H. Creel, $10,928.94; Doctor Marshall C. Guthrie, $10,928.94; Doctor John W. Kerr, $10,928.94; Doctor Allan J. McLaughlin, $10,928.94; Doctor John McMullen, $10,928.94; Doctor Roy P. Sandidge, $10,039.74; Doctor Frederick C. Smith, $10,928.94; Doctor Walter J. Treadway, $10,928.94; Doctor Clifford E. Waller, $8,701.22, and Doctor Mark J. White, $10,928.94.


*Approved August 21, 1959.*

# Private Law 86–189

> AN ACT Conferring United States citizenship posthumously upon Gerardo Rafael Dobarganes y Torres.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Airman Second Class Gerardo Rafael Dobarganes y Torres, AF14605811, a native of Cuba, who served honorably in the United States Air Force from September 6, 1956, until his death on June 12, 1958, shall be held and considered to have been a citizen of the United States at the time of his death.


*Approved September 21, 1959.*

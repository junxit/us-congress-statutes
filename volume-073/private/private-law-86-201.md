# Private Law 86–201

> AN ACT For the relief of Rachel Nethery.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 15 to 20, inclusive, of the Federal Employees’ Compensation Act are hereby waived in favor of Rachel Nethery, Seattle, Washington, and her claim for compensation benefits arising out of the death of her daughter, Maud L. Nethery, who was fatally injured on March 29, 1919, while employed at the United States Navy Yard, Puget Sound, Washington, shall be acted upon under the remaining provisions of such Act if she files such claim with the Bureau of Employees’ Compensation, Department of Labor, within the six-month period which commences on the date of enactment of this Act. No benefits shall accrue by reason of the enactment of this Act for any period prior to the date of its enactment.


*Approved September 21, 1959.*

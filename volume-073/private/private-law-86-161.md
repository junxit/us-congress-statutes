# Private Law 86–161

> AN ACT To exempt from taxation certain property of the United Spanish War Veterans. Incorporated, in the District of Columbia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That that part of the real property described as lot 886, in square 677, situated in the city of Washington, District of Columbia, which comprises the undivided one-half interest owned by the United Spanish War Veterans, Incorporated, is hereby exempt from all taxation so long as the same is owned and occupied by the United Spanish War Veterans, Incorporated, and is not used for commercial purposes, subject to the provisions of sections 2, 3, and 5 of the Act entitled “An Act to define the real property exempt from taxation in the District of Columbia”, approved December 24, 1942 (56 Stat. 1089).


*Approved September 9, 1959.*

# Private Law 86–193

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Ellen Leschner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and nationality laws, section 352(a) of the Immigration and Nationality Act shall not be held to have been or to be applicable to Mrs. Ellen Leschner, a citizen of the United States, provided she returns to the United States prior to July 1, 1961.


*Approved September 21, 1959.*

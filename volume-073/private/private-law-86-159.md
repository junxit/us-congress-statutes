# Private Law 86–159

> AN ACT For the relief of Concetta Meglio Meglio.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Concetta Meglio Meglio, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Marianna Marcella, a citizen of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents of Concetta Meglio Meglio shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved September 9, 1959.*

# Private Law 86–120

> AN ACT For the relief of Kum Hung Seeto and Kum Wo Seeto.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor children, Kum Hung Seeto and Kum Wo Seeto, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien children of Earnest S. Joe, a United States citizen: Provided, That the natural parents of Kum Hung Seeto and Kum Wo Seeto shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved August 24, 1959.*

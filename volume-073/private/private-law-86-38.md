# Private Law 86–38

> AN ACT For the relief of Wong Bick Quon (Marla Wong).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the pur-poses of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Wong Bick Quon (Maria Wong) shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mrs. Mary Fong Chan, a citizen of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents of Wong Bick Quon (Maria Wong) shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved July 6, 1959.*

# Private Law 86–109

> AN ACT For the relief of Yee You Gee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Yee You Gee, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. John M. Yee, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 21, 1959.*

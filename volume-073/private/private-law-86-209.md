# Private Law 86–209

> AN ACT For the relief of Christopher J. Mulligan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of title III of the Immigration and Nationality Act, section 315 of the said Act shall not be applicable to Christopher J. Mulligan.


*Approved September 21, 1959.*

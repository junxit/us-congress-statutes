# Private Law 86–158

> AN ACT For the relief of Nettie Korn and Manfred Korn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Nettie Korn and Manfred Korn shall be deemed to have been born in Austria.


*Approved September 9, 1959.*

# Private Law 86–213

> AN ACT For the relief of Andrew Choa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for all purposes of section 316(a)(1) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Andrew Choa shall be held and considered to have been physically present in the United States from May 28, 1956, to June 30, 1956.


*Approved September 21, 1959.*

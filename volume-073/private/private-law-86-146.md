# Private Law 86–146

> AN ACT For the relief of William James Harkins and Thomas Lloyd Harkins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor children, William James Harkins and Thomas Lloyd Harkins, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien children of Mr. and Mrs. Lewis James Harkins, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural mother of William James Harkins and Thomas Lloyd Harkins shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved September 8, 1959.*

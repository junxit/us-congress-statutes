# Private Law 86–164

> AN ACT To authorize Colonel Philip M. Whitney, United States Army, retired, to accept and wear the decoration tendered him by the Government of the Republic of France.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Colonel Philip M. Whitney, United States Army, retired, is authorized to accept and wear the decoration known as the Croix de Guerre with palm, and to accept any supporting documents tendered him by the Government of the Republic of France. The Secretary of State shall deliver such decoration and any such supporting documents to the said Colonel Philip M. Whitney.


*Approved September 14, 1959.*

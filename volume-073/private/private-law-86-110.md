# Private Law 86–110

> AN ACT For the relief of Ho Rim Yoon Holsman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the pur-poses of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Ho Rim Yoon Holsman shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Noel and Helen Holsman, citizens of the United States.


*Approved August 21, 1959.*

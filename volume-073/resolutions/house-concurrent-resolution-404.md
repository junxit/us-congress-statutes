# House Concurrent Resolution 404

> “COMPENDIUM OF PAPERS SUBMITTED ON REVISION OF THE FEDERAL INCOME TAX LAWS”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Ways and Means, House of Representatives, five thousand additional copies of the compendium of papers entitled “Compendium of Papers Submitted on Revision of the Federal Income Tax Laws.”


*Passed September 11, 1959.*

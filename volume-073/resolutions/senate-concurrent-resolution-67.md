# Senate Concurrent Resolution 67

> REENROLLMENT OF 8. 1928

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (S. 1928) to provide for the participation of the United States in the Inter-American Development Bank, the Secretary of the Senate be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to make the following change, namely: On page 8, lines 11 and 12, of the engrossed bill, in the phrase “section 4(a)(6) of the Bretton Woods Agreements Act”, strike out “(a)” and insert “(b)”.


*Agreed to July 29, 1959.*

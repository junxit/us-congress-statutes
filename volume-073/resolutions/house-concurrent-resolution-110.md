# House Concurrent Resolution 110

> ADJOURNMENT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Thursday, March 26, 1959, they stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Tuesday, April 7, 1959.


*Passed March 25, 1959.*

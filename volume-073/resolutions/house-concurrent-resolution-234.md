# House Concurrent Resolution 234

> “ORGANIZATION AND MANAGEMENT OF MISSILE PROGRAMS”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Government Operations two thousand additional copies of the hearing, entitled “Organization and Management of Missile Programs”, held by that committee during the current session.


*Passed September 9, 1959.*

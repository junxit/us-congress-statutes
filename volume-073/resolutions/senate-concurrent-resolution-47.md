# Senate Concurrent Resolution 47

> PRINTING OF HEARINGS ON AUTOMATION AND TECHNOLOGICAL CHANGE

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Joint Economic Committee one thousand additional copies of the hearings on automation and technological change held by that committee during the Eighty-fourth Congress.


*Agreed to August 18, 1959.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 72

> “BIOLOGICAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL EFFECTS OF NUCLEAR WAR”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Joint Committee on Atomic Energy be authorized to have printed for its use ten thousand additional copies of the public hear-ings on “Biological and Environmental Effects of Nuclear War,” held by the Special Subcommittee on Radiation during the Eighty-sixth Congress, first session.


*Passed September 7, 1959.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 64

> “THE PRAYER ROOM IN THE UNITED STATES CAPITOL”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed fifty-four thousand two hundred additional copies of House Document Numbered 234, Eighty-fourth Congress, first session, entitled “The Prayer Room in the United States Capitol”, of which forty-four thousand one hundred copies shall be for the use of the House of Representatives and ten thousand one hundred copies for the use of the Senate.


*Passed April 10, 1959.*

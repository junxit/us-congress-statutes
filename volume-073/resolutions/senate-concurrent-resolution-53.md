# Senate Concurrent Resolution 53

> “FALLOUT FROM NUCLEAR WEAPONS TESTING”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Joint Committee on Atomic Energy be authorized to have printed for its use ten thousand additional copies of the public hearings on “Fallout From Nuclear Weapons Testing”, held by the Special Subcommittee on Radiation during the Eighty-sixth Congress, first session.


*Agreed to September 7, 1959.*

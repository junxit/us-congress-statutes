# House Concurrent Resolution 378

> COMMISSION OF FINE ARTS REPORT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Sixteenth Report of the Commission of Fine Arts, transmitted by the President to the Congress of the United States on August 5, 1959, be printed as a House document with illustrations.


*Passed September 9, 1959.*

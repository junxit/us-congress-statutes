# House Concurrent Resolution 440

> ADJOURNMENT SINE DIE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Tuesday, September 15, 1959, and that when they adjourn on said day, they stand adjourned sine die.


*Passed September 15, 1959.*

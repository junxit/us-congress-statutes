# Senate Concurrent Resolution 38

> CONGRESSIONAL HEARINGS ON ADMINISTERED PRICES

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on the Judiciary, United States Senate, two thousand five hundred additional copies each of parts 1, 2, 3, and 4 of the hearings conducted by the committee during the Eighty-fifth Congress, first session, on administered prices.


*Agreed to August 18, 1959.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 185

> MIAMI UNIVERSITY, OXFORD, OHIO

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Congress hereby extends its felicitations and best wishes to Miami University, Oxford, Ohio, on the occasion of the one hundred and fiftieth anniversary of such university.


*Passed May 28, 1959.*

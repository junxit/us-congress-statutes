# House Concurrent Resolution 405

> “HOSPITAL, NURSING HOME, AND SURGICAL BENEFITS OF OASI BENEFICIARIES”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Ways and Means, House of Representatives, four thousand additional copies of the hearings entitled “Hospital, Nursing Home, and Surgical Benefits of OASI Beneficiaries”.


*Passed September 11, 1959.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 17

> NATIONAL LITTLE LEAGUE BASEBALL WEEK

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President is authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating the week beginning the second Monday in June of each year, as National Little League Baseball Week in recognition of the national and community benefits resulting from Little League activity, and inviting the people of the United States to observe such week in schools, parks, athletic fields, and other suitable places with appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Passed June 1, 1959.*

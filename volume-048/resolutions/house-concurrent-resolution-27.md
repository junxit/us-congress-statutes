# House Concurrent Resolution 27

> united states roanoke colony commission

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the House concurrent resolution of the Seventy-second Congress establishing the United States Roanoke Colony Commission is continued in full force and effect during the Seventy-third Congress, and, as continued, section 2 of such resolution is amended by striking out “1934” and inserting in lieu thereof “1937.”


*Passed, January 27, 1934.*

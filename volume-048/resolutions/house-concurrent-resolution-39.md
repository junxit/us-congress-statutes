# House Concurrent Resolution 39

> centennial of death of la fayette

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be compiled, and printed with illustrations, in such form and style as may be directed by the Joint Committee on Printing, seven thousand copies of the proceedings at the joint session of the two Houses of Congress in the Hall of the House of Representatives on May 20, 1934, held in commemoration of the centennial anniversary of the death of Gilbert du Motier, Marquis de La Fayette, together with such other matter as the committee may deem pertinent, of which one thousand copies shall be for the use of the Senate, four thousand copies shall be for the House of Representatives, and two thousand copies for the use of the Joint Committee on Arrangements.


*Passed, June 16, 1934.*

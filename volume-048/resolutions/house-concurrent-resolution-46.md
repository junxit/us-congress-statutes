# House Concurrent Resolution 46

> alcoholic beverages in canal zone

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the provisions of S. 3696, entitled “An Act authorizing the President to make rules and regulations in respect to alcoholic beverages in the Canal Zone, and for other purposes”, shall, with necessary editorial changes, be incorporated and printed in the proper place in the “Canal Zone Code” (H.R. 8700) prior to its enrollment and signature.


*Passed, June 15, 1934.*

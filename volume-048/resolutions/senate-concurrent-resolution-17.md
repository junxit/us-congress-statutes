# Senate Concurrent Resolution 17

> daniel boone bicentennial

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate the bill (S. 3355) to authorize the coinage of 50-cent pieces in commemoration of the two-hundredth anniversary of the birth of Daniel Boone, to correct an error therein.


*Passed, May 21, 1934.*

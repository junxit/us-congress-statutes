# House Concurrent Resolution 47

> adjournment

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the Senate shall adjourn as of the legislative day of June 6, 1934, and the House of Representatives shall adjourn as of the legislative day of June 15, 1934, they shall stand adjourned sine die.


*Passed, June 18, 1934.*

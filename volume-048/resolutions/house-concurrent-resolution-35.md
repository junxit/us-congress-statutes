# House Concurrent Resolution 35

> fees in naturalization proceeding

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President is requested to return to the House of Representatives the bill (H.R. 3521, Seventy-third Congress, second session) entitled “An Act to reduce certain fees in naturalization proceedings, and for other purposes”, for the purpose of correcting an error in said bill.


*Passed, April 12, 1934.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 25

> joint meeting

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the. House of Representatives on Wednesday, the 3d day of January 1934, at 1:30 o’clock in the afternoon, for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed, January 3, 1934.*

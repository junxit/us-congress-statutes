# Senate Concurrent Resolution 16

> national motor vehicle theft act

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Secretary of the Senate is authorized and directed, in the enrollment of the bill (S. 2845) entitled “An Act to extend the provisions of the National Motor Vehicle Theft Act to other stolen property”, to strike out “1929” where it appears in section 7 thereof and insert in lieu thereof “1919.”


*Passed, May 17, 1934.*

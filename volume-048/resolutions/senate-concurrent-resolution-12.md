# Senate Concurrent Resolution 12

> alaska prohibition repeal

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the action of the Vice President and of the Speaker of the House of Representatives in signing the enrolled bill (S. 2729) entitled “An Act to repeal an Act of Congress entitled ‘An Act to prohibit the manufacture or sale of alcoholic liquors in the Territory of Alaska, and for other purposes’, approved February 14, 1917, and for other purposes”, be rescinded, and that in the reenrollment of such bill the last proviso of section 1 reading as follows: “ Provided, That the Governor of the Territory of Alaska, from and after the passage and approval of this Act, shall have the power and authority to grant pardons to persons theretofore convicted of violations of the aforesaid Act of February 14, 1917.”, be stricken out.


*Passed, April 5, 1934.*

# Chapter 437

> AN ACT For the relief of R. S. Howard Company, Incorporated.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That jurisdiction is hereby conferred upon the Court of Claims to hear and adjudicate, without regard to existing statutes of limitations, the claim of R. S. Howard Company, for just compensation, arising out of the service upon said company of United States Navy Commandeer Order Numbered N-3255, dated June 18, 1918, with the same right as in other cases to either party to apply to the Supreme Court of the United States for writ of certiorari to review any judgment that may be rendered.


*Approved, June 8, 1934.*

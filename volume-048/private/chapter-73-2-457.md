# Chapter 457

> AN ACT For relief of the Western Montana Clinic, Missoula, Montana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay to the Western Montana Clinic, of Missoula, Montana, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $5,022.30 in full satisfaction of its claim against the United States for surgical and medical services rendered prior to May 30, 1932, to Indians on the Flathead Indian Reservation, Montana.


*Approved, June 11, 1934.*

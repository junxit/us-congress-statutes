# Chapter 529

> AN ACT For the relief of Sue Hall Erwin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Sue Hall Erwin, mother of Marcus Erwin, Junior, ensign, United States Navy, who was killed by an explosion on the United States ship Mississippi, June 12, 1924, is hereby allowed an amount equal to six months’ pay at the rate Marcus Erwin, Junior, was receiving at the time of his death: Provided, That the said Sue Hall Erwin shall establish to the satisfaction of the Secretary of the Navy her dependency upon her son, the late Marcus Erwin, Junior.


*Approved, June 14, 1934.*

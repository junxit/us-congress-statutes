# Chapter 704

> AN ACT For the relief of Paul Jelna.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, their widows or dependent relatives, Paul Jelna, who was a private of Company A, Twenty-ninth Regiment United States Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a private of that organization on November 30, 1902: Provided, That no back pay, pension, or other emolument shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 21, 1934.*

# Chapter 791

> AN ACT For the relief of B. E. Dyson, former United States marshal, southern district of Florida.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the General Accounting Office is hereby authorized and directed to credit the accounts of B. E. Dyson, former United States marshal, southern district of Florida, in the amount of $1,060 disallowed by certificate of settlement numbered F-22358-J, dated December 18, 1931, representing payments made to Frank A. Kopp for services rendered as bailiff while also holding an appointment as deputy marshal at a compensation of $175 per annum.


*Approved, June 26, 1934.*

# Chapter 330

> AN ACT For the relief of Frank Ferst.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers Frank Ferst, who was a member of Battery B, Fourth Regiment United States Field Artillery, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a member of that organization on the 6th day of November 1905: Provided, That no bounty, pay, pension, or allowance shall accrue because of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, May 21, 1934.*

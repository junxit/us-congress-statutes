# Chapter 811

> AN ACT For the relief of Erik Nylin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission is hereby authorized to consider and determine, in the same manner and to the same extent as if application for the benefits of the Employees’ Compensation Act had been made within the one-year period required by sections 17 and 20 thereof, the claim of Erik Nylin, on account of disability caused by his employment in the service of the United States at Elim, Alaska: Provided, That no benefits shall accrue prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved, June 26, 1934.*

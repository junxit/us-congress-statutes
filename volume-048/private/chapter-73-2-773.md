# Chapter 773

> AN ACT For the relief of Henry Poole.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers Henry Poole, who was a member of Company D, Seventeenth Regiment United States Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a member of that organization on the 8th day of April 1899: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 26, 1934.*

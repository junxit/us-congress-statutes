# Chapter 359

> AN ACT For the relief of C. A. Dickson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is authorized and directed to credit the account of C. A. Dickson, postmaster at Cleburne, Texas, in the sum of $72.45. Such sum represents the amount of United States postal funds lost by reason of the failure of the Home National Bank and the Farmers and Merchants National Bank of Cleburne, Texas, and charged in the account of the said postmaster as a balance due the United States after the payment of final dividends in respect of such deposits.


*Approved, May 26, 1934.*

# Chapter 399

> AN ACT For the relief of Anna H. Jones.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized, notwithstanding the provisions of the Act of June 4, 1920 (41 Stat. 824; 34 U. S. C., 943), to settle, adjust, and certify the claim of Anna H. Jones as a person standing in loco parentis to the late Marine Gunner Walter G. Jones, United States Marine Corps, for the sum of $1,110 as six months’ death gratuity.


*Approved, June 5, 1934.*

# Chapter 272

> AN ACT For the relief of Bonnie S. Baker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $100.29 to Bonnie S. Baker, former postmaster at Gore, Georgia, to reimburse her for currency and coin in that amount stolen from said post office by burglary on November 18, 1930, which said loss was sustained without negligence on the part of said postmaster and was by her repaid to the Government from her private funds.


*Approved, May 9, 1934.*

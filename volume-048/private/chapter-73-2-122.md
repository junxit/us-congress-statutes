# Chapter 122

> AN ACT For the relief of Manuel Merritt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized, and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated and in full settlement against the Government, the sum of $40.20 to Manuel Merritt in payment of amount of loss sustained in postal funds by the failure and closing of the First National Bank of Roff, Oklahoma.


*Approved, April 13, 1934.*

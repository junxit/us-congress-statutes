# Chapter 132

> AN ACT For the relief of George G. Slonaker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission is hereby authorized to consider and determine, in the same manner and to the same extent as if application for the benefits of the Employees’ Compensation Act had been made within the one-year period required by sections 17 and 20 thereof, the claim of George G. Slonaker, on account of injury to his left eye, and subsequent blindness, alleged to have been proximately caused by his employment as an incinerator operator by the United States Government at Camp Colt, Pennsylvania, from March 1918 to November 1918: Provided, That he shall file a notice of such injury and claim for compensation therefor not later than sixty days from the date of enactment of this Act: And provided further, That no benefits shall accrue prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved, April 13, 1934.*

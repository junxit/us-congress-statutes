# Chapter 776

> AN ACT For the relief of Howell K. Stephens.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers Howell K. Stephens, who was a private, Medical Department, United States Army, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States on the 25th day of October 1919: Provided, That no back pay, compensation, benefit, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 26, 1934.*

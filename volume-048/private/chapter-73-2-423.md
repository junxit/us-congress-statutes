# Chapter 423

> AN ACT For the relief of John S. Cathcart.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to John S. Cathcart, of Hartsville, South Carolina, the sum of $87.80 for money expended for the Post Office Department.


*Approved, June 6, 1934.*

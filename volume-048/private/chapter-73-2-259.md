# Chapter 259

> AN ACT For the relief of Elbert L. Grove.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged sailors Elbert L. Grove, late of United States Navy, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the naval service of the United States as a member of that organization on the 31st day of March 1901: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall accrue by virtue of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, May 7, 1934.*

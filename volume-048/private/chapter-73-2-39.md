# Chapter 39

> AN ACT For the relief of William C. Campbell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay to William C. Campbell, of Pawhuska, Oklahoma, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $64.64 in full satisfaction of his claim against the United States for one half of his deceased son’s share in payment made to the Santee Sioux Indians in 1924, which was erroneously paid to another Indian of the same name.


*Approved, March 2, 1934.*

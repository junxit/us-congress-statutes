# Chapter 560

> AN ACT Granting a franking privilege to Grace G. Coolidge.

*Be it enacted the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all mail matter sent by post by Grace G. Coolidge, widow of the late Calvin Coolidge, under her written autograph signature, be conveyed free of postage during her natural life.


*Approved, June 16, 1934.*

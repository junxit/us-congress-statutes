# Chapter 647

> JOINT RESOLUTION Authorizing the President of the United States to present the Distinguished Flying Cross to Emory B. Bronte.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States is authorized to present the Distinguished Flying Cross to Emory B. Bronte, of San Francisco, California, in recognition of his heroic courage and great skill as a navigator on the second successful airplane flight from California to the Hawaiian Islands, made under extremely adverse weather conditions in twenty- five hours, two minutes, on July 14 and 15, 1927.


*Approved, June 18, 1934.*

# Chapter 787

> AN ACT For the relief of Harry Lee Shaw.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws or any laws conferring rights, privileges, or benefits upon persons honorably discharged from the United States Army, Harry Lee Shaw shall be held and considered to have been honorably discharged as a captain, Medical Corps, United States Army, on December 5, 1918: Provided, That no compensation, retirement pay, back pay, pension, or other benefit shall be held to have accrued by reason of this Act prior to its passage.


*Approved, June 26, 1934.*

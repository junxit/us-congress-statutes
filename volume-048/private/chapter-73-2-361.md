# Chapter 361

> AN ACT For the relief of R. Gilbertsen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the accounts of R. Gilbertsen, postmaster at Glenburn, North Dakota, in the sum of $250.30 due the United States on account of the loss of postal funds resulting from the failure of the Glenburn State Bank at Glenburn, North Dakota: Provided, That the said R. Gilbertsen shall assign to the United States any and all claims he may have to dividends arising from the liquidation of said bank.


*Approved, May 26, 1934.*

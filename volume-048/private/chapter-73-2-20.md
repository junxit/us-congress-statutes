# Chapter 20

> AN ACT For the relief of Captain L. P. Worrall, Finance Department, United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit in the accounts of L. P. Worrall, Captain, Finance Department, United States Army, the sum of $956.40, said amount being public funds for which he is accountable and which were lost when a safe in the Finance Office at Fort Douglas, Utah, was dynamited and robbed at approximately 11 o’clock postmeridian, October 28, 1932.


*Approved, February 20, 1934.*

# Chapter 67

> AN ACT For the relief of the Neill Grocery Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to the Neill Grocery Company, Wheeling, West Virginia, the sum of $2,531.97. Such sum represents the amount of a fine and court costs paid on such date, by such company, pursuant to a conviction for violating provisions of the Lever Act of August 10, 1917, as amended, prior to the declaration by the Supreme Court of the United States of the invalidity of such provisions.


*Approved, March 13, 1934.*

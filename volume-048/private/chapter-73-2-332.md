# Chapter 332

> AN ACT For the relief of the Playa de Flor Land and Improvement Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That jurisdiction is hereby conferred upon the District Court of the Canal Zone to hear and determine, but subject to the provisions for appeal as in other cases provided by the Panama Canal Act, as amended, the claim of the Playa de Flor Land and Improvement Company against the United States on account of property taken by the United States in the Canal Zone.


*Approved, May 21, 1934.*

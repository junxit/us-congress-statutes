# Chapter 246

> AN ACT For the relief of Edward J. Devine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Administrator of Veterans’ Affairs is authorized and directed to pay, out of the appropriation “ Medical and hospital services ”, to Edward J. Devine the sum of $65.50. The payment of such sum shall be in full settlement of all claims against the United States for undertaking services performed by Edward J. Devine in connection with the burial of Patrick J. Murtagh.


*Approved, May 7, 1934.*

# Chapter 721

> AN ACT To grant permission to the Willard Family Association to erect a tablet at Fort Devens, Massachusetts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War is authorized and directed to grant permission to the Willard Family Association to erect an appropriate tablet at Fort Devens, Massachusetts, on the site of the farm formerly owned by Major Simon Willard, but the United States shall not be put to any expense in or by the erection thereof.


*Approved, June 22, 1934.*

# Chapter 187

> AN ACT For the relief of Lewis E. Green.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission is hereby authorized and instructed to receive and determine the claim of Lewis E. Green, a former employee in the United States Arsenal at Tullytown, Pennsylvania, without regard to the limitation of time within which such claims are to be filed under the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes ”, approved September 7, 1916, as amended.


*Approved, April 30, 1934.*

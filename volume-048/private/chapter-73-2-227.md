# Chapter 227

> AN ACT For the relief of William J. Nowinski.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in computation of service for pay purposes, Lieutenant (Junior Grade) William J. Nowinski, Supply Corps, United States Navy, shall be held and considered to have entered a commissioned status in the Navy on April 19, 1926.


*Approved, May 7, 1934.*

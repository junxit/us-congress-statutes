# Chapter 135

> AN ACT For the relief of Mary Elizabeth O’Brien.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 17 and 20 of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes ”, are hereby waived in favor of Mary Elizabeth O’Brien, a former employee of the United States Veterans’ Bureau: Provided, That no benefits shall accrue prior to the approval of this Act.


*Approved, April 13, 1934.*

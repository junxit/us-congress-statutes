# Chapter 769

> AN ACT For the relief of the Edward F. Gruver Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is authorized to adjust and settle the claim of the Edward F. Gruver Company in an amount not to exceed $200 for leather labels furnished the Federal Radio Commission, notwithstanding any provision of law requiring such supplies to be obtained from the Government Printing Office.


*Approved, June 26, 1934.*

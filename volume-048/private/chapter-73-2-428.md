# Chapter 428

> AN ACT For the relief of George A. Fox.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States is hereby authorized to appoint George A. Fox, chief pharmacist’s mate, United States Navy, a chief pharmacist in the Navy, subject to qualification on physical examination, with the pay and allowances provided by law for chief warrant officers after twenty years’ commissioned service and with creditable record: Provided, That no back pay, allowances, or emoluments shall become due prior to the date of this Act.


*Approved, June 7, 1934.*

# Chapter 128

> AN ACT For the relief of David I. Brown.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, their widows, or dependent relatives, David I. Brown, formerly a private of Company E, Twenty-eighth Regiment United States Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a member of said company and regiment on the 17th day of January 1903: Provided, That no pay, pension, bounty, or other emoluments shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, April 13, 1934.*

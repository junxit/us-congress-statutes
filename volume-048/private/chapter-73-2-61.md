# Chapter 61

> AN ACT Authorizing adjustment of the claims of John T. Lennon and George T. Flora.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to allow John T. Lennon and George T. Flora $25 each in full and final settlement of their claims for blood furnished May 4 and May 11, 1926, respectively, for transfusion to Harvey J. Shoppe, a patient in a Government hospital. There is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $50 for the payment of such claim.


*Approved, March 13, 1934.*

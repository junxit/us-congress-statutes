# Chapter 542

> AN ACT To change the name of the retail liquor dealers’ stamp tax in the case of retail drug stores or pharmacies.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in. Congress assembled,*

That the first paragraph of subdivision “Fourth” of section 3244 of the Revised Statutes, as amended (U.S.C., title 26, see. 205 (a)), is amended by adding at the end thereof a new sentence to read as follows: “The tax required to be paid by this paragraph shall, in case of a retail drug store or pharmacy making sales of liquors through a duly licensed pharmacist, be designated as a ‘medicinal spirits stamp tax.’ ”


*Approved, June 15, 1934.*

# Chapter 552

> AN ACT To amend the District of Columbia Alcoholic Beverage Control Act to permit the issuance of retailers’ licenses of class B in residential districts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first paragraph of section 15 of the District of Columbia Alcoholic Beverage Control Act is amended to read as follows:

> ## § 15.
>
> No retailer’s licenses except of classes B or E shall be issued for any business conducted in a residential-use district as defined in the zoning regulations and shown in the official atlases of the Zoning Commission, except for a restaurant or tavern conducted in a hotel, apartment house, or club, and then only when the entrance to such restaurant or tavern is entirely inside of the hotel, apartment house, or club and no sign or display is visible from the outside of the building.”


*Approved, June 16, 1934.*

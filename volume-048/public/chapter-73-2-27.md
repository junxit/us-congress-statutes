# Chapter 27

> AN ACT To extend the time for completing the construction of a bridge across the Missouri River at or near South Omaha, Nebraska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time for the construction of a bridge across the Missouri River at or near South Omaha, Nebraska, authorized by section 4 of the Act of Congress approved June 10, 1930, heretofore extended by an Act of Congress approved February 20, 1931, is hereby further extended one year from June 10, 1934.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is reserved.


*Approved, February 24, 1934.*

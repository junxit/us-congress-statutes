# Chapter 56

> JOINT RESOLUTION To provide an appropriation to carry into effect the Act entitled “An Act to provide for loans to farmers for crop production and harvesting during the year 1934, and for other purposes”, approved February 23, 1934.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That to enable the Governor of the Farm Credit Administration to carry into effect the provisions of the Act entitled “An Act to provide for loans to farmers for crop production and harvesting during the year 1934, and for other purposes”, approved February 23, 1934 (Public Act Numbered 97, Seventy-third Congress), including personal services and rent in the District of Columbia and elsewhere; paper, printing and binding; supplies and services, without regard to section 3709 of the Revised Statutes (U.S.C., title 41, sec. 5) when the aggregate amount involved does not exceed $50, and such other expenses as may be necessary, there is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $40,000,000, to remain available until June 30, 1935.


*Approved, March 10, 1934.*

# Chapter 692

> AN ACT To amend section 5 of Public Act Numbered 2 of the Seventy-second Congress, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 5 of Public Act Numbered 2 of the Seventy-second Congress, as amended, be amended by striking out the period at the end of the second paragraph thereof and inserting in lieu thereof a colon and the following: “ Provided, That such limitation shall not apply to advances to receivers or other liquidating agents of closed banks when made for the purpose of liquidation or reorganization. ”


*Approved, June 21, 1934.*

# Chapter 660

> AN ACT Donating bronze trophy guns to the Cohoes Historical Society, Cohoes, New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War, in his discretion, is hereby authorized to deliver to the order of the Cohoes Historical Society two bronze trophy guns stored in the Watervliet Arsenal at Watervliet, New York, and marked “W. A. 240” and “W. A. 241”, caliber, four and one hundred and twenty-five thousandths: Provided, That the United States shall be put to no expense in connection with the delivery of said guns.


*Approved, June 19, 1934.*

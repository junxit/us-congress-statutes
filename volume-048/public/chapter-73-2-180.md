# Chapter 180

> AN ACT To extend the time for the construction of a bridge across the Wabash River at a point in Sullivan County, Indiana, to a point opposite on the Illinois shore.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge authorized by Act of Congress approved February 10, 1932, to be built by Sullivan County, Indiana, or any board or commission of said county which is or may be created or established for the purpose, across the Wabash River, extending from some point in the county across said river to a point opposite on the Illinois shore, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from the date of approval hereof.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, April 30, 1934.*

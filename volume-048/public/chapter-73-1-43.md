# Chapter 43

> AN ACT To authorize the Comptroller General to allow claim of district numbered 13, Choctaw County, Oklahoma, for payment of tuition for Indian pupils.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General is hereby authorized and directed to allow payment of claims of the public school district numbered 13, Choctaw County, Oklahoma, for tuition of Indian pupils during the fiscal year 1931, in the sum not to exceed $3,435.61 from the appropriation entitled “Indian Schools, Five Civilized Tribes, Oklahoma, 1931.”


*Approved, May 29, 1933.*

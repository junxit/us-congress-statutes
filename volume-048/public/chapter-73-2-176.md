# Chapter 176

> AN ACT To legalize a bridge across Saint Francis River at or near Lake City, Arkansas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the bridge now being constructed across Saint Francis River at or near Lake City, Arkansas, by the Arkansas State Highway Commission, if completed in accordance with the plans accepted by the Chief of Engineers and the Secretary of War as providing suitable facilities for navigation and operated as a free bridge, shall be a lawful structure, and shall be subject to the conditions and limitations of the Act entitled “An Act to regulate the construction of bridges over navigable waters”, approved March 23, 1906.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, April 30, 1934.*

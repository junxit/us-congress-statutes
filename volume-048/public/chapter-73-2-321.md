# Chapter 321

> AN ACT Repealing certain sections of the Revised Code of Laws of the United States relating to the Indians.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 2111, 2112, 2113, 2120, 2134, 2147, 2148, 2149, 2150, 2151, 2152, and 2153 of the Revised Statutes (U.S.C., title 25, secs. 171, 172, 173, 186, 219, 220, 221, 222, 223, 224, 225, and 226) are hereby repealed.


*Approved, May 21, 1934.*

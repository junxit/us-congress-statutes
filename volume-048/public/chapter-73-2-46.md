# Chapter 46

> JOINT RESOLUTION To amend Public Act Numbered 81 of the Seventy-third Congress, relating to the sale of timber on Indian land.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the last proviso in the Act of June 16, 1933 (Public, Numbered 81, Seventy-third Congress, first session; 48 Stat.L. 311), relating to the sale of timber on Indian lands, be, and the same hereby is, amended to read as follows: “And provided further, That the authority granted herein shall terminate on the 4th day of September 1934.”


*Approved, March 5, 1934.*

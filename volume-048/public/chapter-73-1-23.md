# Chapter 23

> JOINT RESOLUTION To amend section 2 of the Act approved February 4, 1933, to provide for loans to farmers for crop production and harvesting during the year 1933, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2 of the Act of February 4, 1933 (Public, Numbered 327), be, and the same is hereby, amended by adding at the end of the first sentence thereof, the words: “and in the case of summer fallowing or winter wheat, a first lien, or an agreement to give a first lien on crops to be harvested in 1934, shall, in the discretion of the Secretary of Agriculture, be deemed sufficient security.”


*Approved, May 1, 1933.*

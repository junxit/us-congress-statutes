# Chapter 20

> JOINT RESOLUTION To provide for the payment of pages for the Senate and House of Representatives for the first session of the Seventy-third Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the payment of pages from April 1, 1933, until the end of the first session of the Seventy-third Congress, as follows:

For twenty-one pages for the Senate Chamber at the rate of pay provided by law, so much as may be necessary.

For forty-one pages for the House of Representatives, including ten pages for duty at the entrances to the Hall of the House, at the rate of pay provided by law, so much as may be necessary.


*Approved, April 14, 1933.*

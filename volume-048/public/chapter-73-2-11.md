# Chapter 11

> AN ACT To provide for extension of time for making deferred payments on homestead entries in the abandoned Fort Lowell Military Reservation, Arizona.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time within which a homestead entryman for lands in the abandoned Fort Lowell Military Reservation, in the State of Arizona, shall make deferred payments be, and it is hereby, extended for a period of two years from the 1933 anniversary of the date of the acceptance of his proof tendered on his entry.


*Approved, February 14, 1934.*

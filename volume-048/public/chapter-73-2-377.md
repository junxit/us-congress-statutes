# Chapter 377

> AN ACT To change the designation of Four-and-a-half Street southwest to Fourth Street.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the street designated as “ Four-and-a-half Street ” running south from the center of the Mail to P Street south be, and the same is hereby, changed to Fourth Street, thereby giving this street for its entire length from Pennsylvania Avenue northwest to P Street south the designation of Fourth Street.


*Approved, June 4, 1934.*

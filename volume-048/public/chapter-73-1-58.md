# Chapter 58

> AN ACT To amend existing law in order to obviate the payment of one year’s sea pay to surplus graduates of the Naval Academy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That so much of the Act of August 5, 1882 (22 Stat. 285, ch. 391), as is contained in the proviso at the end of section 1057, title 34, United States Code, is hereby amended by repealing the words “and one year’s sea pay”, so that the said proviso will read as follows: “ Provided, That if there be a surplus of graduates, those who do not receive such appointments shall be given a certificate of graduation and an honorable discharge.”


*Approved, June 10, 1933.*

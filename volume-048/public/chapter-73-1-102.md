# Chapter 102

> AN ACT To remove the limitation on the filling of the vacancy in the office of senior circuit judge for the ninth judicial circuit.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is authorized, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, to appoint a circuit judge to fill the vacancy in the United States Circuit Court of Appeals for the Ninth Judicial Circuit occasioned by the death of Honorable William B. Gilbert. A vacany 1 occurring at any time in the office of circuit judge referred to in this section is authorized to be filled.


*Approved, June 16, 1933, 4 p.m.*

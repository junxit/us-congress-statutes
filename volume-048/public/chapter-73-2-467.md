# Chapter 467

> AN ACT To amend the Act of June 15, 1933, amending the National Defense Act of June 3, 1916, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 37 of the National Defense Act of June 3, 1916, as amended, be, and the same is hereby, further amended by inserting after the words “United States”, in the seventh sentence of said section, the words “or of the Philippine Islands”.


*Approved, June 12, 1934.*

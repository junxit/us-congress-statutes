# Chapter 581

> AN ACT Authorizing the control of floods in the Salmon River, Alaska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the project of prevention and control of floods in the Salmon River, Alaska, recommended in the report of the Chief of Engineers, United States Army, in House Document Numbered 228, Seventy-second Congress, is hereby adopted and authorized and shall be prosecuted under the direction of the Secretary of War and the supervision of the Chief of Engineers in accordance with the plan recommended in such report and subject to the conditions set forth therein.


*Approved, June 18, 1934.*

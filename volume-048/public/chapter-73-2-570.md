# Chapter 570

> AN ACT To amend the Act approved June 28, 1932 (47 Stat.L. 337).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Aet approved June 28, 1932 (47 Stat.L. 337), be, and the same is hereby, amended by striking out in the eighth line thereof the word “three” and substituting therefor the word “five”.


*Approved, June 18, 1934.*

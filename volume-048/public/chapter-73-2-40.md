# Chapter 40

> AN ACT To provide for the care and transportation of seamen from shipwrecked fishing and whaling vessels.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3 (relating to the care and transportation of shipwrecked merchant seamen) of the Act approved December 21, 1898 (U.S.C., title 46, sec. 593), entitled “An Act to amend the laws relating to American seamen, for the protection of such seamen, and to promote commerce”, shall apply to fishing and whaling vessels, notwithstanding the provisions of section 26 of such Act.


*Approved, March 5, 1934.*

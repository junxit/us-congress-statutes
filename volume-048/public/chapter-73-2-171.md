# Chapter 171

> AN ACT To extend the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Columbia River near The Dalles, Oregon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Columbia River near The Dalles, Oregon, authorized to be built by The Dalles Bridge Company, a Washington corporation, by the Act of Congress approved March 4, 1933, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from March 4, 1934.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, April 30, 1934.*

# Chapter 80

> AN ACT Amending section 74 of the Judicial Code, as amended (U.S.C., Annotated title 28, sec. 147).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the State of Connecticut shall constitute one judicial district to be known as the “district of Connecticut.” Terms of the district court shall be held at New Haven on the second Tuesday in February and the third Tuesday in September; at Hartford on the second Tuesday in May and the first Tuesday in December; at Norwalk on the third Tuesday in April; and at Columbia on the first Tuesday in September: Provided, That suitable rooms and accommodations shall be furnished for the holdings of said court and for the use of the officers of said court at Norwalk and Columbia free of expense to the Government of the United States.


*Approved, June 15, 1933.*

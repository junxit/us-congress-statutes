# Chapter 51

> AN ACT To amend an Act (Public, Numbered 431, Seventy-second Congress) to identify The Dalles Bridge Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That an Act to authorize the construction of certain bridges over navigable waters of the United States, approved March 4, 1933 (Public, Numbered 431, Seventy-second Congress), be amended by adding to section 2a the words “a Washington corporation”, immediately following the words “The Dalles Bridge Company.”


*Approved, June 9, 1933.*

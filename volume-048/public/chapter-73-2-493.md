# Chapter 493

> AN ACT To exempt from taxation certain property of The American Legion in the District of Columbia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and. House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the property situated in square 185 in the city of Washington, District of Columbia, described as lots 32 and 33, owned, occupied, and used by The American Legion, is hereby exempt from all taxation so long as the same is so owned and occupied, and not used for commercial purposes, subject to the provisions of section 8 of the Act of March 3, 1877, as amended and supplemented (D.C. Code, title 20, sec. 712), providing for exemptions of church and school property.


*Approved, June 13, 1934.*

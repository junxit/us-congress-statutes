# Chapter 94

> AN ACT To transfer Bedford County from the Nashville division to the Winchester division of the middle Tennessee judicial district.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Bedford County of the Nashville division of the middle district of the State of Tennessee is hereby detached from the Nashville division and attached to and made a part of the Winchester division of the middle district of such State.


*Approved, June 16, 1933, 12:55 p.m.*

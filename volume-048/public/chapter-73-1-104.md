# Chapter 104

> AN ACT To amend Public Act Numbered 435 of the Seventy-second Congress, relating to sales of timber on Indian land.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Public Act Numbered 435 of the Seventy-second Congress entitled “An Act to authorize the Secretary of the Interior to modify the terms of existing contracts for the sale of timber on Indian land when it is in the interest of the Indians so to do” is hereby amended by adding to the first section thereof the following proviso: “Provided, That the restrictions as to reducing prices below the basic sales prices shall not apply to the Klamath Indian Reservation in Oregon: And provided further, That the authority granted herein shall terminate one year from the date of enactment of this Act.”


*Approved, June 16, 1933, 5 p.m.*

# Chapter 26

> AN ACT Granting the consent of Congress to the State of Oregon to maintain a bridge already constructed across Youngs Bay near the city of Astoria, Oregon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the consent of Congress is hereby granted to the State of Oregon, and its successors and assigns, to maintain and operate, in accordance with the provisions of the Act entitled “An Act to regulate the construction of bridges over navigable water”, approved March 23, 1906, a bridge and approaches thereto already constructed across Youngs Bay near the city of Astoria, Oregon, which bridge is hereby declared to be a lawful structure to the same extent and in the same manner as if it had been constructed in accordance with the provisions of said Act of March 23, 1906.

## § 2.

That the right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, February 24, 1934.*

# Chapter 219

> AN ACT To authorize the Department of Agriculture to issue a duplicate check in favor of the Mississippi State treasurer, the original check having been lost.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions of sections 3646, as amended, of the Revised Statutes of the United States, the disbursing clerk of the Department of Agriculture is authorized and directed to issue, without the requirement of an indemnity bond, a duplicate of original check numbered 534971 drawn April 3, 1929, in favor of the Mississippi State treasurer for $1,871.02, and lost, stolen, or miscarried in the mails.


*Approved, May 7, 1934.*

# Chapter 26

> AN ACT To extend the time for completing the construction of a bridge across the Missouri River at or near Kansas City, Kansas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives. of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time for completing the construction of a bridge across the Missouri River at or near Kansas City, Kansas, authorized to be built by the Interstate Bridge Company, its successors and assigns, by an Act of Congress approved May 22, 1928, heretofore extended by Acts of Congress approved March 2, 1929, and June 30, 1930, is hereby further extended two years from May 22, 1933.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, May 12, 1933.*

# Chapter 34

> AN ACT To amend section 207 of the Bank Conservation Act with respect to bank reorganizations.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 207 of the Bank Conservation Act is amended by striking out “national banking association” wherever it appears therein and inserting in lieu thereof the word “bank.”


*Approved, May 20, 1933.*

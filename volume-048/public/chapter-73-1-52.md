# Chapter 52

> AN ACT To extend the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Ohio River at or near Owensboro, Kentucky.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Ohio River at or near Owensboro, Kentucky, authorized to be built by the State Highway Commission of Kentucky by an Act of Congress approved June 9, 1932, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from June 9, 1933.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, June 9, 1933.*

# Chapter 353

> AN ACT To establish a department of physics at the United States Military Academy, West Point, New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That hereafter there is authorized one professor of physics at the United States Military Academy, with the same status, rank, pay, and allowances of other professors at said Military Academy.


*Approved, May 26, 1934.*

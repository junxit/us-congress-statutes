# Chapter 37

> AN ACT To confer the degree of bachelor of science upon graduates of the Naval, the Military, and the Coast Guard Academies.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the superintendents of the United States Naval Academy, the United States Military Academy, and the United States Coast Guard Academy may, under such rules and regulations as the Secretary of the Navy, the Secretary of War, and the Secretary of the Treasury may prescribe, confer the degree of bachelor of science upon all graduates of their respective academies, from and after the date of the accrediting of said academies by the Association of American Universities.


*Approved, May 25, 1933.*

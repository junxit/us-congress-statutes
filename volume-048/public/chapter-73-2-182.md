# Chapter 182

> AN ACT Providing for settlement of claims of officers and enlisted men for extra pay provided by Act of January 12, 1899.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the General Accounting Office is authorized and directed to receive and settle claims of officers and enlisted men who were appointed or enlisted in the Army under the Act of March 2, 1899 (30 Stat. L. 979), for one or two months’ extra pay provided by the Act of January 12, 1899, as amended (30 Stat. L. 784), notwithstanding the disallowance of their claims for such extra pay by the former accounting officers of the Treasury.


*Approved, April 30, 1934.*

# Chapter 446

> AN ACT To amend section 32 of the Emergency Farm Mortgage Act of 1933.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the fifth sentence of section 32 of the Emergency Farm Mortgage Act of 1933 is amended by striking out “which has been foreclosed at any time between July 1, 1931, and the date of the enactment of this Act, or which is foreclosed after the enactment of this Act”.


*Approved, June 11, 1934.*

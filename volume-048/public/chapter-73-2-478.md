# Chapter 478

> AN ACT To further extend the times for commencement and completing the construction of a bridge across the Missouri River at or near Garrison, North Dakota.

*Be, it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Missouri River, at or near Garrison, North Dakota, authorized to be built by the State of North Dakota, by the Acts of Congress approved February 10, 1932, and February 14, 1933, are hereby further extended one and three years, respectively, from February 14, 1934.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, June 12, 1934.*

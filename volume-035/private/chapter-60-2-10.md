# Chapter 10

> An Act For the relief of Jabez Burchard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to transfer Jabez Burchard, assistant engineer, United States Navy (retired), from the half-pay list to the seventy-five per centum pay list of retired officers under section fifteen hundred and eighty-eight of the Revised Statutes of the United States, to take effect from the date of his retirement.


*Approved, January 5, 1909.*

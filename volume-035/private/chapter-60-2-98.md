# Chapter 98

> An Act For the relief of Thomas J. Shocker.

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Thomas J. Shocker shall hereafter be held and considered to be entitled to all of the rights and benefits that he would be entitled to on account of military service, except pay, bounty, and other emoluments, if he had been continuously in the military service of the United States as a private of Company B, Twelfth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Cavalry, from the twelfth day of September, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, to the first day of July, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, and had been honorably discharged on the date last named herein.


*Approved, February 6, 1909.*

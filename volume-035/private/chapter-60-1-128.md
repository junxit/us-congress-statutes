# Chapter 128

> An Act For the relief of A. E. Couch.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of forty two dollars and eighty-five cents be, and the same is, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to pay to A. E. Couch, of Carterton, Russell County, Virginia, being the amount of costs deposited by the .said A. E. Conch with the Honorable L. P. Summers, collector of the sixth collection district of Virginia, and inadvertently by the said collector deposited to the credit of the Treasurer of the United States, when said sum should have been deposited with the Secretary of the Treasury as an offer of compromise of a certain criminal prosecution then pending in the United States district court for the western district of Virginia, against the said A. E. Couch, the said sum having been deposited in the Treasury of the United States on the seventeenth day of May, nineteen hundred and five.


*Approved, April 2, 1908.*

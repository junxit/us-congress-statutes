# Chapter 31

> An Act To reimburse Anna B. Moore, late postmaster at Rhyolite, Nevada, for money expended for clerical assistance.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America-in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Anna B. Moore, late postmaster at Rhyolite, Nevada, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one thousand and ninety-two dollars, to reimburse her for money expended for necessary clerical assistance.


*Approved, January 21, 1909.*

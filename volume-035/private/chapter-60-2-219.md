# Chapter 219

> An Act For the relief of E. J. Reed.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House, of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to E. J. Reed, postmaster at Danby, Vermont, the sum of three hundred and one dollars and twenty cents, the sum of money and stamps stolen from tho post-office at Danby, Vermont, on the twenty-ninth day of April, nineteen hundred and six, without any fault on his part.


*Approved, February 26, 1909.*

# Chapter 24

> An Act To correct the naval record of Edward T. Lincoln.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to correct the record of service of Edward T. Lincoln, late an acting master’s mate in the Navy during the war of the rebellion, to show that he was honorably discharged the service on January eighth, eighteen hundred and sixty-five.


*Approved, January 20, 1909.*

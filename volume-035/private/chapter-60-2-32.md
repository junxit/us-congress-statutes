# Chapter 32

> An Act For the relief of Theodore F. Northrop.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Theodore F. Northrop, late first lieutenant, Second Regiment New York Cavalry Volunteers, and who commanded a body of mounted military scouts in the army of General Sherman from January third, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, to March thirty-first, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, shall be held and considered to have been an officer of the Volunteer Army during that time, for the purpose of an application for a medal of honor: Provided, That no pay, bounty, or other allowance shall become due or payable by virtue of this Act.


*Approved, January 21, 1909.*

# Chapter 337

> An Act For the relief of certain officers of the United States Signal Corps.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the expenses of operating and keeping in repair the northwestern section of the United States military telegraph lines (from Bismarck to Fort Ellis and connections), constructed under Act of Congress approved June twentieth, eighteen hundred and seventy-eight, which, between the twentieth day of June, eighteen hundred and seventy-eight, and the thirtieth day of June, eighteen hundred and eighty-three, may have been paid out of moneys received from dispatches sent over said section, be, and the same are hereby, authorized and allowed; and the several officers making such payments are entitled to and shall receive proper credit, therefor upon examination of their respective accounts: Provided, That said accounts conform in all other respects to the rules of the War and Treasury Departments governing the disbursements of public moneys.


*Approved, March 4, 1909.*

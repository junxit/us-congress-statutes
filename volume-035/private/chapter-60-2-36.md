# Chapter 36

> An Act To reimburse Ulysses G. Winn for money erroneously paid into the Treasury of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be, and is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one hundred and thirty-two dollars and eighty cents, to reimburse Ulysses G. Winn for said amount, erroneously deposited by him while United States commissioner at Center and Stonewall, in the .southern district of the Indian Territory, in the years nineteen hundred and nineteen hundred and one, and which were paid into the Treasury of the United States.


*Approved, January 22, 1909.*

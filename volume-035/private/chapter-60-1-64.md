# Chapter 64

> An Act Granting a pension to Calesta Clark.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior he, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Calesta Clark, widow of Joseph Clark, late of Company G, Twenty-fifth Regiment United States Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 7, 1908.*

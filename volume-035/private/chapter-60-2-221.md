# Chapter 221

> An Act To reimburse Agnes M. Harrison, postmaster at Wheeler, Mississippi, for loss of money-order remittance.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury of the United States not otherwise appropriated, for the reimbursement of Agnes M. Harrison, postmaster at Wheeler, in the State of Mississippi, for money-order remittance lost in transit November twelfth, nineteen hundred and seven, without fault on her part, the sum of fifty-six dollars.


*Approved, February 26, 1909.*

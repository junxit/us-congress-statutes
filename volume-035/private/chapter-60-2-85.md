# Chapter 85

> An Act For the relief of Patrick Conlin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Patrick Conlin shall hereafter be held and considered to be entitled to all of the rights and benefits that he would be entitled to on account of military service, except pay, bounty, and other emoluments, if he had been continuously in the military service of the United States as a private of Company I, Fifty-seventh Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, from the twenty-seventh day of January, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, to the eighth day of August, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, and had been honorably discharged on the date last named herein.


*Approved, February 6, 1909.*

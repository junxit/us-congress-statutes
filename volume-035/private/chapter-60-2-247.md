# Chapter 247

> An Act Granting pensions and increase of pensions to certain soldiers and sailors of the civil war and to the widows and dependent and helpless relatives of such soldiers and sailors.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

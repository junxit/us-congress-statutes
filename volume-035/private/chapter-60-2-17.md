# Chapter 17

> An Act For the relief of William Radcliffe.

*Be it enacted by the Semite and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of twenty-five thousand dollars be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to pay William Radcliffe, a British subject, for damages caused by destruction of his fish hatchery and property in Delta, Colorado, by a mob in nineteen hundred and one.


*Approved, January 9, 1909.*

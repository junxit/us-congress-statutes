# Chapter 187

> An Act To grant to John T. Rivett privilege to make commutation of his homestead entry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That John T. Rivett be, and he is hereby, granted the privilege at his option to make commutation of his homestead entry of the southwest quarter of section twenty-eight, in township twenty-two north, range fifty west, sixth principal meridian, in the State of Nebraska, as provided by law for the making of commutation of homestead entries.


*Approved, February 24, 1909.*

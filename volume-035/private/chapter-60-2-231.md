# Chapter 231

> An Act To repeal the Act of June twenty-ninth, nineteen hundred and six, granting a pension to Jackson Adkins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act approved June twenty-ninth, nineteen hundred and six, entitled “An Act granting a pension to Jackson Adkins,” be, and the same is hereby, repealed.


*Approved, February 27, 1909.*

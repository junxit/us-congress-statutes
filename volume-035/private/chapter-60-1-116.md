# Chapter 116

> An Act For the relief of John V. Johnson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the said John V. Johnson (doing business under the name of the Brooklyn Architectural Iron Works) be, and he hereby is, relieved from any penalty, forfeiture, or claim for liquidated damages under his certain contract with the United States of America, dated on or about the twenty-second day of December, nineteen hundred and six, to install a steel book stack and gallery in the record vault at the United States Immigration Station, Ellis Island. New York Harbor.

## § 2.

Unit the said John V. Johnson (doing business under the name, of the Brooklyn Architectural Iron Works) shall be paid the sum of one thousand eight hundred dollars from the permanent appropriation “Expenses of regulating immigration,” in full of the amount due him under said contract, without deduction of any penalty, forfeiture, or claim for liquidated damages or otherwise.


*Approved, March 31, 1908.*

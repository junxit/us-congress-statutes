# Chapter 50

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Jane C. Stingley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jane C. Stingley, widow of James D. Stingley, late of Captain John Mathis’s company. South Carolina Volunteers, Florida Indian war, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 25, 1908.*

# Chapter 331

> An Act Waiving the statute of limitations as to the claim of the Nestler Brewing Company, and authorizing the Commissioner of Internal Revenue to adjudicate the same.

*Be it enacted by the Senate a nd House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time, within which the, Nestler Brewing Company, a corporation organized under the laws of the State of Utah, or its agents or legal representatives, may file a claim for the redemption of revenue stamps is hereby extended for a period of ninety days from and after the approval of this Act, notwithstanding the provisions of the Act of May twelfth, nineteen hundred, amended by the Act of June thirtieth, nineteen hundred and two; and the Commissioner of Internal Revenue is authorized to consider and adjudicate said claim upon its merits, notwithstanding any prior determination thereof.


*Approved, March 4, 1909.*

# Chapter 60

> An Act To amend an Act to provide for the reorganization of the consular service of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act entitled “An Act to provide for the reorganization of the consular service of the United States,” approved April fifth, nineteen hundred and six, as heretofore amended, is further amended as follows: By striking out, in class nine, consuls, the word “Messina,” and by inserting after the word “Carlsbad,” in class seven, consuls, the word “Catania.”


*Approved, February 3, 1909.*

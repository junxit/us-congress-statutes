# Chapter 305

> An Act Relative to outward alien manifests on certain vessels.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Represen tali res of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That until the provisions of section twelve of the immigration Act of February twentieth, nineteen hundred and seven, relating to outward alien manifests, shall be made applicable to passengers going out of the United States to Canada by land carriage, said provisions shall not apply to passengers going by vessels employed exclusively in the trade between the ports of the United States and the Dominion of Canada and the Republic of Mexico.


*Approved, March 4, 1909.*

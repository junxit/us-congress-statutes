# Chapter 308

> An Act To grant two cannons to Ohio Northern University, at Ada, Ohio.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized to donate to the Ohio Northern University, at Ada, Ohio, two muzzle-loading cannons, now in possession of said university, to be used by the students of said university in military drill: Provided, That no expense shall be incurred by the United States.


*Approved, March 4, 1909.*

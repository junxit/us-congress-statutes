# Chapter 101

> An Act To validate certain entries of public lands in the State of Colorado.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That no entries or filings for lands in township five and one-half south, of ranges forty-two, forty-three, forty-four, forty-five, and forty-six west, in the State of Colorado, shall be canceled or held invalid because they were not allowed, made, or perfected in the proper land district.


*Approved, March 26, 1908.*

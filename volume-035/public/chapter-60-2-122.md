# Chapter 122

> An Act Prescribing what shall constitute a legal cord of wood in the District of Columbia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That hereafter a legal cord of wood in the District of Columbia shall consist of and contain one hundred and twenty-eight cubic feet.

## § 2.

That all Acts or parts of Acts in conflict with or inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed in so far and only in so far as they conflict or are inconsistent herewith.


*Approved, April 2, 1908.*

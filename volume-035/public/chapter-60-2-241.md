# Chapter 241

> An Act Authorizing the Secretary of Commerce and Labor to exchange property.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of Commerce and Labor be, and he is hereby, authorized to exchange property now occupied by the Light-House Board at Ashtabula, Ohio, for other property of not less value at another location and to make a contract for such exchange.


*Approved, March 2, 1909.*

# Chapter 13

> An Act Granting a right of way to the Southern Pacific Railroad Company across the Fort Mason Military Reservation in California.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the consent of the United States is hereby given to the Southern Pacific Company, a corporation created and existing under the laws of the State of Kentucky, to locate, construct, maintain, and operate a railroad and tunnel upon and across the property belonging to the United States at Fort Mason, in the State of California, upon such location and under such regulations as shall be approved by the Secretary of War.


*Approved, January 8, 1909.*

# Chapter 267

> An Act To change the name of the Washington Hospital for Foundlings.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act entitled “An Act for incorporating a hospital for foundlings in the city of Washington,” approved April twenty-second, eighteen hundred and seventy, be, and the same is hereby, amended by changing the name of said institution to the Washington Home for Foundlings.


*Approved, March 3, 1909.*

# Chapter 79

> An Act To place John Crowley on the retired list of the United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in consideration of services rendered in the United States Navy during a period of thirty-two years, from April eleventh, eighteen hundred and sixty-live, to June twentieth, eighteen hundred and ninety-seven, that the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place John Crowley on the retired list of the navy with the retired pay of the rating in which he wasserving at the time of his last honorable discharge.


*Approved, February 6, 1909.*

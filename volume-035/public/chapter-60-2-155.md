# Chapter 155

> An Act Granting to the city of Woodward, in the State of Oklahoma, lot two, in block forty-eight, for park and other public purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That lot numbered two, in block numbered forty-eight, in the city of Woodward and State of Oklahoma, be, and the same is hereby, granted to said city of Woodward, for the use and benefit of the inhabitants of said city, for park and other public purposes.


*Approved, April 30, 1908.*

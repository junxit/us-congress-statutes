# Chapter 76

> An Act Authorizing the Secretary of the Interior to sell isolated tracts of land within the Nez l’erces Indian Reservation.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the law providing for the sale of any isolated or disconnected tract or parcel of the public domain is hereby extended and made applicable to any isolated and unappropriated public lands embraced within the Nez Perces Indian Reservation: Provided, That for agricultural lands purchasers under this Act shall pay not less than three dollars and seventy-five cents per acre, and for lands valuable for stone and timber they shall pay not less than five dollars per acre.


*Approved, February 6, 1909.*

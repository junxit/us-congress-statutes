# Chapter 89

> An Act To provide for the extension of time within which homestead entrymen may establish their residence upon certain lands within the limits of the Huntley irrigation project, in the county of Yellowstone, in the State of Montana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the homestead entrymen on lands within the limits of the Huntley irrigation project, in the county of Yellowstone, in the State of Montana, opened to entry July twenty-second, nineteen hundred and seven, be, and they are hereby, granted an extension of time in which to establish their residence upon the lands so opened and filed upon until the fifteenth day of May, nineteen hundred and eight: Provided, however, That this Act shall in no manner affect the regularity or validity of such filings, or any of them, so made by the said entry men on the lands aforesaid; and it is only intended hereby to extend the time for the establishment of such residence as herein provided.


*Approved, March 16, 1908.*

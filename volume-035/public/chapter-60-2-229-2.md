# Chapter 229

> An Act To amend an Act to authorize a bridge across the Missouri River at or near Council Bluffs, Iowa, approved February first, nineteen hundred and eight.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United, States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Act to authorize a bridge across the Missouri River at or near Council Bluffs, Iowa, approved February first, nineteen hundred and eight, is hereby amended so as to give to the Central Railroad and Bridge Company, of Council Bluffs, Iowa, all the authority and rights granted under said Act if the actual construction, of the bridge authorized by said Act is commenced within one year and completed within three years from the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 27, 1909.*

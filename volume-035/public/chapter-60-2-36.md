# Chapter 36

> An Act To extend immediate transportation privileges to the subport of Alburg, in the customs collection district of Vermont.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the privileges of the first section of the Act approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, relating to the transportation of dutiable merchandise without appraisement, be, and the same are hereby, extended to the subport of Alburg, in the customs collection district of Vermont.


*Approved, February 24, 1908.*

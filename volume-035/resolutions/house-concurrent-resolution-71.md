# House Concurrent Resolution 71

> harry kimmel.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the House of Representatives the bill (H. R. 17214) for the relief of Harry Kimmel, a commander on the retired list of the United States Navy.


*Passed February 26, 1909.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 78

> criminal code.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives Senate concurring),*

That the Secretary of the Senate be authorized to renumber the sections consecutively; to strike out the head-notes at the beginning of each chapter to sections which have been omitted and to renumber the head-notes to correspond to the numbers given the sections; to correct the references in one section to other sections; to correct typographical errors: and to correct punctuation as indicated by the Committee on Conference.


*Passed February 23, 1909.*

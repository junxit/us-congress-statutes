# Public Resolution 28

> Providing for additional lands for Idaho under the provisions of the Carey Act.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That an additional one million acres of arid lands within the State of Idaho be made available and subject to the terms of section four of an Act of Congress entitled “An Act making appropriations for sundry civil expenses of the Government for the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, eighteen hundred and ninety-five, and for other purposes,” approved August eighteenth, eighteen hundred and ninety-four, and by amendments thereto, and that the State of Idaho be allowed, under the provisions of said Acts, said additional area, or so much thereof as may be necessary for the purposes and under the provisions of said Acts.


*Approved, May 25, 1908.*

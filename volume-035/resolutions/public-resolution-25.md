# Public Resolution 25

> To amend an Act authorizing the construction of bridges across navigable waters, and so forth.

*Resolved by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the bill (S. 4809) entitled “An Act authorizing the construction of bridges across navigable waters, and to extend the time for the construction of bridges across navigable waters, and to legalize the construction of bridges across navigable waters,” be, and the, same is hereby, corrected so that the name Saint Louis, Brownsville and Mexico Railway Company, as used therein, be changed to the Brownsville and Gulf Railway Company.


*Approved, May 22, 1908.*

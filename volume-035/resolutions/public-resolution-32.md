# Public Resolution 32

> Authorizing the employment of clerical services in the Department of Justice.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Attorney-General is authorized to continue the employment of clerical services during the fiscal year nineteen hundred and nine, under the appropriation for “Defense in Indian Depredation Claims” and to pay therefor out of said appropriation, not to exceed the sum of six thousand dollars.


*Approved, May 30, 1908.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 92

> g. s. bingham.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representative concurring),*

That the President he requested to return to the Senate the bill (S. 689l) for the relief of Major G. S. Bingham.


*Passed February 16, 1909.*

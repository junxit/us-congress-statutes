# Public Resolution 26

> Authorizing the transfer of the statue of President Washington, now located in the Capitol grounds, to the Smithsonian Institution.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the statue of President Washington, now located in the Capitol grounds east of the Capitol, be, and the same is hereby, transferred to the custody of the Smithsonian Institution.


*Approved, May 22, 1908.*

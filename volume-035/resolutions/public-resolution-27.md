# Public Resolution 27

> Providing for the printing of the Special Report on the Diseases or Cattle.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be printed and bound one hundred thousand copies of the Special Report on the Diseases of Cattle, the same to be first revised and brought to date under the supervision of the Secretary of Agriculture; thirty thousand copies for the use of the Senate, sixty thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives, and ten thousand copies for distribution by the Department of Agriculture.


*Approved, May 23, 1908.*

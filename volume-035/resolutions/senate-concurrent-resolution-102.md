# Senate Concurrent Resolution 102

> customs tariff.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representative concurring),*

That there be printed fifteen thousand additional copies of Senate document five hundred and forty-seven, Sixtieth Congress. Second session, relating to customs tariffs and consisting of Senate and House reports of eighteen hundred and eighty-eight, eighteen hundred and ninety, eighteen hundred and ninety-four and eighteen hundred and ninety-seven; five thousand copies for the use of the Senate and ten thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives.


*Passed March 3, 1909.*

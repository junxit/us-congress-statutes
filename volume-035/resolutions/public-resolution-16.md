# Public Resolution 16

> For the relief of Archibald G. Stirling, recently midshipman, United States Navy.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized to appoint former Midshipman Archibald G. Stirling to be an ensign in the United States Navy to take the position he is entitled to, by his order of merit, as shown by his examination for final graduation.


*Approved, April 3, 1908.*

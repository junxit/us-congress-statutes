# Public Resolution 14

> Authorizing the Secretary of War to establish harbor lines in Wilmington Harbor, California.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War is hereby authorized to fix and establish pierhead and bulkhead lines, either or both, in the inner harbor of San Pedro, otherwise known as Wilmington Harbor, California, beyond which no piers, wharves, bulkheads, or other works shall be extended or deposits made except under such regulations as shall be prescribed from time to time by the Secretary of War.


*Approved, March 26, 1908.*

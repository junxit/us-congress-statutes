# House Concurrent Resolution 46

> holiday recess.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Saturday, December nineteenth, they stand adjourned until twelve o’clock, meridian, Monday, January fourth, nineteen hundred and nine.


*Passed December 15, 1908.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 11

> holiday recess.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

Thatwhen the two Houses adjourn on Saturday. December twenty-first, they stand adjourned until twelve o’clock meridian, Monday, January sixth, nineteen hundred and eight.


*Passed December 17, 1907.*

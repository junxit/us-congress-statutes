# House Concurrent Resolution 75

> additional judges, etc.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the House of Representatives (H. R. 21896), Sixtieth Congress, “An Act to amend section eighty-six of an act to provide a government for the Territory of Hawaii, and to provide for additional judges, and for other judicial purposes.”


*Passed March 1, 1909.*

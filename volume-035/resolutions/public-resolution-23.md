# Public Resolution 23

> Authorizing the widening of the channel of Michigan City Harbor.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Sta tes of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized, in his discretion, to use any unexpended balance of money heretofore appropriated or that may be hereafter appropriated for the improvement or maintenance of the harbor at Michigan City, Indiana, for the rebuilding of the western revetment of said harbor upon a new alignment, with the view of widening the channel of said harbor.


*Approved, May 13, 1908.*

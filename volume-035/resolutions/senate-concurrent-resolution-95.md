# Senate Concurrent Resolution 95

> C. DeW. WILCOX.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of .Representatives concurring,*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate the hill (S. 5969) authorizing the Department of State to deliver to Major C. DeW. Wileox decoration and diploma presented by Government of France.


*Passed February 16, 1909.*

# Public Resolution 8

> For the appointment of a member of the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in. Congress assembled,*

That the vacancy in the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution of the class “other than Members of Congress” shall be tilled by the appointment of Charles F. Choate, junior, a citizen of Massachusetts.


*Approved, February 24, 1908.*

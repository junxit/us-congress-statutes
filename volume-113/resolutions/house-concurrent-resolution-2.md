# House Concurrent Resolution 2

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on the legislative day of Wednesday. January 6, 1999, it stand adjourned until 2 p.m. on Tuesday January 19, 1999.


*Agreed to January 6, 1999.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 196

> CONGRESSIONAL GOLD MEDAL PRESENTATIONCEREMONY FOR PRESIDENT AND MRS. GERALD R. FORD—CAPITOL ROTUNDA AUTHORIZATION

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Rotunda of the Capitol is authorized to be used on October 27, 1999, for the presentation of the Congressional Gold Medal to President and Mrs. Gerald R. Ford. Physical preparations for the ceremony shall be carried out in accordance with such conditions as the Architect of the Capitol may prescribe.


*Agreed to October 26, 1999.*

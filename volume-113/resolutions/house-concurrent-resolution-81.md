# House Concurrent Resolution 81

> NATO 50TH ANNIVERSARY CEREMONY—CAPITOL GROUNDS AUTHORIZATION

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Rotunda of the United States Capitol is authorized to be used on April 23, 1999, for a ceremony in honor of the 50th Anniversary of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO) and welcoming the three newest members of NATO, the Republic of Poland, the Republic of Hungary, and the Czech Republic, into NATO. Physical preparations for the ceremony shall be carried out in accordance with such conditions as the Architect of the Capitol may prescribe.


*Agreed to April 19, 1999.*

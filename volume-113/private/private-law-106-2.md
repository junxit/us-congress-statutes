# Private Law 106–2

> An Act To direct the Secretary of the Interior to transfer to the personal representative of the estate of Fred Steffens of Big Horn County, Wyoming, certain land comprising the Steffens family property.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. TRANSFER OF STEFFENS FAMILY PROPERTY.

- **(a)** *Conveyance.—* Subject to subsection (b) and valid existing rights, the Secretary of the Interior shall issue, without consideration, a quitclaim deed to Marie Wambeke of Big Horn County, Wyoming, the personal representative of the estate of Fred Steffens, to the land described in subsection (c).
- **(b)** *Reservation of Minerals.—* All minerals underlying the land described in subsection (c) are reserved to the United States.
- **(c)** *Land Description.—* The land described in this subsection is the parcel comprising approximately 80 acres and known as “Farm Unit C” in the E½NW¼ of Section 27 in Township 57 North, Range 97 West, 6th Principal Meridian, Wyoming.
- **(d)** *Revocation of Withdrawal.—* The withdrawal for the Shoshone Reclamation Project made by the Bureau of Reclamation under Secretarial Order dated October 21, 1913, is revoked with respect to the land described in subsection (c).


*Approved August 2, 1999.*

# Public Law 106–70

> An Act To extend for 9 additional months the period for which chapter 12 of title 11,United States Code, is reenacted.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. AMENDMENTS.

Section 149 of title Iof division C of Public Law 105–277, as amended by Public Law 106–5, is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “October 1, 1999”each place it appears and inserting “July 1, 2000”and
- **(2)** in subsection (a)—
  - **(A)** by striking “March 31, 1999” and inserting “September 30, 1999” and
  - **(B)** by striking “April 1, 1999”and inserting “October 1, 1999.”

## § 2. EFFECTIVE DATE.

The amendments made by section 1 shall take effect on October 1, 1999.


*Approved October 9, 1999.*

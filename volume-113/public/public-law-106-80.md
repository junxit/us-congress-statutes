# Public Law 106–80

> An Act To amend title 4, United States Code, to add the Martin Luther King Jr. holiday to the list of days on which the flag should especially be displayed.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. ADDITION OF MARTIN LUTHER KING JR. HOLIDAY TOLIST OF DAYS.

Section 6(d) of title 4, United States Code, is amended by inserting “Martin Luther King Jr.’s birthday, third Monday in January;” after “January 20;”.


*Approved October 25, 1999.*

# Public Law 106–122

> An Act To amend the Federal Reserve Act to broaden the range of discount window loans which may be used as collateral for Federal reserve notes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the third sentence of the second undesignated paragraph of section 16 of the Federal Reserve Act (12 U.S.C. 412) is amended by striking “acceptances acquired under the provisions of section 13 of this Act” and inserting “acceptances acquired under section 10A, 10B, 13, or 13A of this Act”.


*Approved December 6, 1999.*

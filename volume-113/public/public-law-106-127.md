# Public Law 106–127

> Joint Resolution Appointing the day for the convening of the second session of the One Hundred Sixth Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DAY FOR CONVENING OF SECOND SESSION OF HONE HUNDRED SIXTH CONGRESS.

The second regular session of the One Hundred Sixth Congress shall begin at noon on Monday, January 24, 2000.

## § 2. ADDITIONAL SESSION PRIOR TO CONVENING.

If the Speaker of the House of Representatives and the Majority Leader of the Senate, acting jointly after consultation with the Minority Leader of the House of Representatives and the Minority Leader of the Senate, determine that it is in the public interest for the Members of the House of Representatives and the Senate to reassemble prior to the convening of the second regular session of the One Hundred Sixth Congress as provided in section 1—

- **(1)** the Speaker and Majority Leader shall so notify their respective Members; and
- **(2)** Congress shall reassemble at noon on the second day after the Members are so notified.


*Approved December 6, 1999.*

# Public Law 106–130

> An Act To provide for the holding of court at Natchez, Mississippi, in the same manner as court is held at Vicksburg, Mississippi, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. HOLDING OF COURT AT NATCHEZ, MISSISSIPPI.

Section 104(b)(3) of title 28, United States Code, is amended in the second sentence by striking all beginning with the colon through “United States”.

## § 2. HOLDING OF COURT AT WHEATON, ILLINOIS

Section 93(a)(1) of title 28, United States Code, is amended by adding after Chicago “and Wheaton”.


*Approved December 6, 1999.*

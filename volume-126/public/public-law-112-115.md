# Public Law 112–115

> An Act To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 260 California Drive in Yountville, California, as the “Private First Class Alejandro R. Ruiz Post Office Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. PRIVATE FIRST CLASS ALEJANDRO R. RUIZ POST OFFICE BUILDING.

- **(a)** *Designation.—* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 260 California Drive in Yountville, California, shall be known and designated as the “Private First Class Alejandro R. Ruiz Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References.—* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Private First Class Alejandro R. Ruiz Post Office Building”.


*Approved May 15, 2012.*

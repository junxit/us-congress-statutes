# Public Law 112–184

> An Act To designate the new United States courthouse in Buffalo, New York, as the “Robert H. Jackson United States Courthouse”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The United States courthouse at 2 Niagara Square, Buffalo, New York shall be known and designated as the “Robert H. Jackson United States Courthouse”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the United States courthouse referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Robert H. Jackson United States Courthouse”.


*Approved October 5, 2012.*

# Public Law 112–221

> An Act To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 6083 Highway 36 West in Rose Bud, Arkansas, as the “Nicky ‘Nick’ Daniel Bacon Post Office”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. NICKY “NICK” DANIEL BACON POST OFFICE.

- **(a)** *Designation.—* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 6083 Highway 36 West in Rose Bud, Arkansas, shall be known and designated as the “Nicky ‘Nick’ Daniel Bacon Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References.—* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Nicky ‘Nick’ Daniel Bacon Post Office”.


*Approved December 28, 2012.*

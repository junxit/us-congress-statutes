# House Concurrent Resolution 147

> PARCHMENT PRINTING REQUIREMENT—WAIVER

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That pursuant to the last sentence of section 106 of title 1, United States Code, the requirement of section 107 of such title that the enrollment of any bill or joint resolution be printed on parchment is waived for the duration of the One Hundred Twelfth Congress, and the enrollment of any such bill or joint resolution shall be in such form as may be certified by the Clerk of the House of Representatives or the Secretary of the Senate (as applicable) to be a truly enrolled bill or joint resolution (as the case may be).


*Agreed to January 1, 2013.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 58

> ENROLLMENT CORRECTION—H.R. 4240

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That, in the enrollment of the bill (H.R. 4240) an Act to reauthorize the North Korean Human Rights Act of 2004, and for other purposes, the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall make the following correction: in section 7, insert “is amended” before “by striking”.


*Agreed to August 7, 2012.*

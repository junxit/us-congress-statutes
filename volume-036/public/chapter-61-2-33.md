# Chapter 33

> An Act To declare Big Tarkio River, in Holt and Atchison counties, nonnavigable.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Big Tarkio River, in the counties of Holt and Atchison, in the State of Missouri, be, and the same is hereby, declared to be not a navigable water of the United States within the meaning of the laws enacted by Congress for the preservation and protection of such waters.

## § 2.

That the right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, February 15, 1910.*

# Chapter 152

> AN ACT For establishing a light and fog-signal station on the San Pedro breakwater, California.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of Commerce and Labor be, and he is hereby, authorized to establish a light and fog-signal station on the San Pedro breakwater, California, at a cost not to exceed thirty-six thousand dollars.


*Approved, February 24, 1911.*

# Chapter 282

> An Act To require that all inmates of the workhouse and reformatory for the District of Columbia shall be returned to and released in said District.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That hereafter all inmates of the workhouse and reformatory for the District of Columbia shall be returned to and released in said District on the day of the expiration of sentence.


*Approved, June 10, 1910.*

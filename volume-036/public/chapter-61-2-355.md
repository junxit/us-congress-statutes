# Chapter 355

> An Act Changing the name of the Saint Johns collection district, in the State of Florida, to the Jacksonville collection district.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of the collection district in the State of Florida now known as the Saint Johns collection district be, and the same is hereby, changed to the Jacksonville collection district.


*Approved, June 23, 1910.*

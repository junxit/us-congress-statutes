# Chapter 11

> An Act To attach Ben Hill County to the Albany division of the southern district of Georgia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the county of Ben Hill, in the State of Georgia, be attached to the Albany division of the southern district of Georgia.


*Approved, August 5, 1909.*

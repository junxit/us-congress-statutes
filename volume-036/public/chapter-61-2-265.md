# Chapter 265

> An Act Extending the time in which to file adverse claims and institute adverse suits against mineral entries in the district of Alaska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the district of Alaska adverse claims authorized and provided for in sections twenty-three hundred and twenty-five and twenty-three hundred and twenty-six, United States Revised Statutes, may be filed at any time during the sixty days period of publication or within eight months thereafter, and the adverse suits authorized and provided for in section twenty-three hundred and twenty-six, United States Revised Statutes, may be instituted at any time within sixty days after the filing of said claims in the local land, office.


*Approved, June 7, 1910.*

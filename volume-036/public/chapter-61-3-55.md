# Chapter 55

> AN ACT Authorizing the Secretary of Commerce and Labor to exchange the site for the immigrant station at the port of Boston.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of Commerce and Labor be, and he is hereby, authorized to exchange the site heretofore acquired for an immigrant stationat Boston, Massachusetts, for another suitable site,the additional costnot to exceed thirty thousand dollars.


*Approved, February 13, 1911.*

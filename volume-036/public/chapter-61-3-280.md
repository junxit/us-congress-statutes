# Chapter 280

> AN ACT For the protection of game in the Territory of Alaska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the passage of this Actit shall be lawful to kill grouse, ptarmigan, shore birds, and waterfowl from September first to Marchfirst, both inclusive, anywhere in the Territory of Alaska.


*Approved, March 4, 1911.*

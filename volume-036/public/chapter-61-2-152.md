# Chapter 152

> An Act To amend section seven hundred and seventy-three of the Revised Statutes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section seven hundred and seventy-three of the Revised Statutes be, and the same is hereby, amended to read as follows:

> ## § 773.
>
> It shall be the duty of the United States district attorneys to make and forward to the Solicitor of the Treasury, for his information and the purposes of a permanent record, such reports relating to suits in which the United States is a party as may be required by the Solicitor of the Treasury with the approval of the Attorney-General.”


*Approved, April 9, 1910.*

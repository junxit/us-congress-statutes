# Chapter 38

> AN ACT To transfer Saint Joseph Bay, of the Pensacola collection district, in the State of Florida, to the Apalachicola collection district.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Saint Joseph Bay Harbor, in the State of Florida, be transferred from the Pensacola collection district,and the same is hereby added to the Apalachicola collection district.


*Approved, February 6, 1911.*

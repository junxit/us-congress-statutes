# Chapter 114

> AN ACT For the relief of the sufferers from famine in China.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of Waris hereby authorized to transport, under the supervision of the American National Red Cross Society,the supplies donated by the people of the United States for the relief of the sufferers from famine in China, and for this purpose may order one of the Army transports to make a trip from Seattle, Washington, to China: Provided, That the total expense thus incurred shall not exceed fifty thousand dollars.And the sum of fifty thousand dollars is hereby appropriated and authorized to be paid out of any moneys in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the purpose specified in this Act.


*Approved, February 18, 1911.*

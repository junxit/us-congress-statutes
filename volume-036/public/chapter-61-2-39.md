# Chapter 39

> An Act To extend the time for the completion of bridge across the Mississippi River at Saint Louis, Missouri, by the Saint Louis Electric Bridge Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time for completing the construction of the bridge authorized by the Act entitled “An Act providing for the construction of a bridge across the Mississippi River,” approved February fifteenth, nineteen hundred and seven, is hereby extended to one year from the date of the passage of this Act.

## § 2.

That the right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, February 16, 1910.*

# Chapter 157

> AN ACT Providing for aids to navigation along the Livingstone Channel, Detroit River, Michigan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of Commerce and Labor be, and be is hereby, authorized to establish and provide such lights and buoys as may, in his judgment, be necessary to properly mark the Livingstone Channel in the Detroit River, Michigan, at an expense not to exceed two hundred and ten thousand dollars.


*Approved, February 24, 1911.*

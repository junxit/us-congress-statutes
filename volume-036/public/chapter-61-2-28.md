# Chapter 28

> An Act To abolish the United States land office at Des Moines, Iowa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the land office at Des Moines, Iowa, shall be, and is hereby, abolished from and after the twenty-eighth day of February, nineteen hundred and ten; and the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized to transfer to the State of Iowa such of the transcripts, documents, and records of the office as are not required for the use of the United States and as the State may desire to preserve.


*Approved, February 15, 1910.*

# Chapter 105

> An Act To change the name of Twenty-third street north of Calvert street, in the District of Columbia, to Woodley place.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Twenty-third street north of Calvert street, in the District of Columbia, shall hereafter be known as Woodley place.


*Approved, March 23, 1910.*

# Chapter 411

> An Act Providing for an increase of salary for the United States marshal for the eastern district of Louisiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, commencing with the fiscal year beginning July first, nineteen hundred and ten, the salary of the United States marshal for the eastern district of Louisiana be fixed at the rate of four thousand dollars per annum.


*Approved, June 25, 1910.*

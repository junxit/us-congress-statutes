# Chapter 151

> AN ACT Providing for the naturalization of the wife and minor children of insane aliens, making homestead entries under the land laws of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That when any alien, who has declared his intention to become a citizen of the United States, becomes insane before he is actually naturalized, and his wife shall thereafter make a homestead entry under the land laws of the United States, she and their minor children may, by complying with the other provisions of the naturalization laws be naturalizedwithout making any declaration of intention.


*Approved, February 24, 1911.*

# Chapter 75

> AN ACT To amend an Act entitled “An Act to authorize the construction of a bridge across the Monongahela River, in the State of Pennsylvania, by the Liberty Bridge Company,” approved March second, nineteen hundred and seven.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section two of an Act entitled ‘‘An Act to authorize the construction of a bridge across the Monongahela River, in the State of Pennsylvania, by the Liberty Bridge Company,”approved March second, nineteen hundred and seven, as amended by the Acts approved March sixteenth, nineteen hundred and eight, February eighteenth, nineteen hundred and nine,and April second, nineteen hundred and ten, be, and is hereby, further amended to read as follows:

## § 2.

That this Act shall be null and void if actual construction of the bridge herein authorized be not commenced within one year and completed within three years from March fifteenth, nineteen hundred and eleven.”


*Approved, February 15, 1911.*

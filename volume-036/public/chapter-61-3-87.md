# Chapter 87

> AN ACT To authorize the employment of letter carriers at certain post-offices.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That hereafter when two or more post-offices situated within the corporate limits of any city, village, or borough are consolidated by authority of the PostmasterGeneral, and the said offices together produced a gross revenue for the preceding fiscal year of not less than ten thousand dollars, letter carriers may be employed for the free delivery of mail matterin like manner as if any one of such post-offices had produced such revenue in said fiscal year.


*Approved, February 16, 1911.*

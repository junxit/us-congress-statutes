# Chapter 216

> AN ACT For the relief of Passed Assistant Paymaster Edwin M. Hacker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navybe, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to restore Passed Assistant Paymaster Edwin M. Hacker, United States Navy, to a place on the list of pay officers of the Navy, next after Passed Assistant Paymaster Thom Williamson, junior, United States Navy.


*Approved, March 3, 1911.*

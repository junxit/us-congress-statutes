# Chapter 398

> An Act Amending the statutes in relation to the immediate transportation of dutiable goods and merchandise.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the privileges of the first section of the Act approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, entitled “An Act to amend the statutes in relation to immediate transportation of dutiable goods, and for other purposes,” be, and the same are hereby, extended to the port of New London, in the customs collection district of New London, Connecticut.


*Approved, June 25, 1910.*

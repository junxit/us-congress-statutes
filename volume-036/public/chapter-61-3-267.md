# Chapter 267

> AN ACT To amend section thirty-five hundred and thirty-six of the Revised Statutes of the United States, relating to the weighing of silver coins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section thirty-five hundred and thirty-six of the Revised Statutes of the United Statesbe, and the same is hereby, amended so as to read as follows:

## § 3536.

In adjusting the weight of the silver coins the following deviationsshall not be exceeded in any single piece: In the dollar, the half and quarter dollar, and in the dime, one and one-half grams.”


*Approved, March 4, 1911.*

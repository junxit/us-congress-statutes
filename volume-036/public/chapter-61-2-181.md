# Chapter 181

> An Act To grant authority to the Crosby Transportation Company, of Milwaukee, Wisconsin, to change the name of the steamer Naomi to E. G. Crosby.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of Navigation is hereby authorized and directed, upon application by the owners, the Crosby Transportation Company, of Milwaukee, Wisconsin, to change the name of the steamer Naomi, official number eighty thousand eight hundred and sixty-one, to E. G. Crosby.


*Approved, April 21, 1910.*

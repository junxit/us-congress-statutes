# Chapter 160

> AN ACT To provide for sittings of the United States circuit and district courts of the northern district of Mississippi at the city of Clarksdale, in said district.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there shall be held at the city of Clarksdale, in the northern districtof Mississippi,a term of both the circuit and district courts of said district on the second Monday in June and the second Monday in December of each year: Provided, That suitable rooms and accommodations shall be furnished for the holding of said court without expenseto the Government of the United States.


*Approved, February 24, 1911.*

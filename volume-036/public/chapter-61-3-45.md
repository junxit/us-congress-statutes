# Chapter 45

> AN ACT For the relief of John M. Blankenship.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, to appoint John M. Blankenship an ensign in the United States Navy on the retired list.


*Approved, February 13, 1911.*

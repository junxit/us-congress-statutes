# Chapter 362

> An Act To authorize the appointment of Frank de l. Carrington as a major on the retired list of the United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized to nominate, and, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, to appoint Frank de I. Carrington, late a major of infantry in the United States Army, to be a major on the retired list, United States Army, as of date of the approval of this Act.


*Approved, June 23, 1910.*

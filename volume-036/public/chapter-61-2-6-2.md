# Chapter 6

> An Act To extend the time for Ashley County, Arkansas, to construct a bridge across Bayou Bartholomew, at a point near Morrell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge, authorized by the Act approved February twentieth, nineteen hundred and eight, entitled “An Act to authorize the county of Ashley, State of Arkansas, to construct a bridge across Bayou Bartholomew, at a point above Morrell, in said county and State, the dividing line between Drew and Ashley counties,” is hereby extended to one year and three years, respectively, from the date of approval of this Act.

## § 2.

That the right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, January 27, 1910.*

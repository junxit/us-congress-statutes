# Chapter 57

> AN ACT Extending the provisions of the Act approved March tenth, nineteen hundred and eight, entitled “An Act to authorize A. J. Smith and his associates to erect a dam across the Choctawhatchee River in Dale County, Alabama.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time for completing the construction of the dam authorized by the Act entitled “An Act to authorize A. J. Smith and his associates to erect a dam across the Choctawhatchee River, in Dale County, Alabama,” approved March tenth, nineteen hundred and eight, is hereby extended to one year from and after the passage of this Act.

## § 2.

That the right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, February 13, 1911.*

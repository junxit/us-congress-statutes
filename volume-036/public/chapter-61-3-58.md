# Chapter 58

> AN ACT For the relief of Edward Forbes Greene.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized to nominateand, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, to appoint Edward Forbes Greene, late lieutenant, a lieutenant in the United States Navy, and to place him upon the retired list as such with three-fourths the pay of his grade: Provided,That the said Edward Forbes Greene shall not, by the passage of this Act, be entitled to back payof any kind, including bounty or emoluments.


*Approved, February 13, 1911.*

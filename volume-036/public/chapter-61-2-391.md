# Chapter 391

> An Act Transferring swamp lands to the State of Wisconsin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be authorized to transfer to the State of Wisconsin the following-described swamp lands: The west half of the southeast quarter, the northeast quarter of the southwest quarter, and the southeast quarter of the northwest quarter, all in section thirty-one, in township twelve north, range fourteen east.


*Approved, June 25, 1910.*

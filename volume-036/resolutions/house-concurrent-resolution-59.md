# House Concurrent Resolution 59

> captain evan m. johnson.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (H. R. 14729) “ For the relief of Captain Evan M. Johnson, United States Army,” the enrolling clerk of the House be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to strike out, in line ten, of the engrossed bill, the words “March twenty-fourth, nineteen hundred and two,” and insert in lieu thereof the words “May sixteenth, eighteen hundred and ninety-nine,” the latter being the date of the sinking of the transport Meade mentioned in the bill in question.


*Passed February 11, 1911.*

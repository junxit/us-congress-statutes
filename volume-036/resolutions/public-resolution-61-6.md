# Public Resolution 6

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the printing of reports upon preliminary examinations and surveys, and so forth.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That at any time prior to the assembling of Congress in December, nineteen hundred and nine, all reports of preliminary examinations and surveys heretofore authorized by Congress that may be prepared and ready for printing shall, in the discretion of the Secretary of War, be printed by the Public Printer as Documents of the Sixty-first Congress.


*Approved, August 5, 1909.*

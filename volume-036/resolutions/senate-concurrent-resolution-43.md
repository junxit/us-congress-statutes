# Senate Concurrent Resolution 43

> judicial code.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (S. 7031) “To codify, revise, and amend the laws relating to the judiciary,” the enrolling clerk is directed to strike out from the bill as reported from conference that part of section One hundred and fifty-one thereof which reads as follows: “In any proceeding under this section, the court shall determine as a preliminary inquiry the question of limitation, delay, or laches; and if it shall be of opinion that the delay in presenting the claim is not excusable, and that the bar of the statute of limitations should not be removed, it shall not proceed further to find the existence of loyalty, liability, or the extent thereof, in such case, but shall report such finding in bar to the House by which the claim or matter was referred.


*Passed March 2, 1911.*

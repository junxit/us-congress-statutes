# House Concurrent Resolution 61

> helen s. hogan.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President of the United States be, and is hereby, requested to return to the House the bill (H. R. 25081) “For the relief of Helen S. Hogan.”


*Passed February 21, 1911.*

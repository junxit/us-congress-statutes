# House Concurrent Resolution 49

> john a. brown.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the House of Representatives the bill (H. R. 2272) “For the relief of John A. Brown.”


*Passed June 21, 1910.*

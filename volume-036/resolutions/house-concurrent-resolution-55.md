# House Concurrent Resolution 55

> holiday recess.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Wednesday, December twenty-first, they stand adjourned until twelve o’clock meridian, Thurdsay, January fifth, nineteen hundred and eleven.


*Passed December 19, 1910.*

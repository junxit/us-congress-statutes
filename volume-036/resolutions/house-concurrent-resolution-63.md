# House Concurrent Resolution 63

> homestead entries in national forests.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President of the United States be, and hereby is, requested to return to the House the bill (H. R. 26290) “Providing for the validation of certain homestead entries.”


*Passed March 2, 1911.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 26

> holiday recess.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Tuesday, December twenty-first, they stand adjourned until twelve o’clock, meridian, Tuesday, January fourth, nineteen hundred and ten.


*Passed December 16, 1909.*

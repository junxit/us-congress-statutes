# Public Resolution 34

> Joint Resolution To enable the States of Wisconsin, Illinois, Indiana, and Michigan to determine the jurisdiction of crimes committed on Lake Michigan.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the consent of the Congress of the United States is hereby given to the States of Wisconsin, Illinois, Indiana, and Michigan, or any two or more of them, by such agreement or compact as they may deem desirable or necessary or otherwise, not in conflict with the Constitution of the United States or any law thereof, to determine and settle the jurisdiction to be exercised by said States, respectively, over offenses arising out of the violation of the laws of any of said States upon the waters of Lake Michigan.


*Approved, June 22, 1910.*

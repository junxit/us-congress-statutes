# Senate Concurrent Resolution 33

> statue of francis el pierpont.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed and bound the proceedings in Congress, together with the proceedings at the unveiling in Statuary Hall upon the acceptance of the statue of Francis H. Pierpont, presented by the State of West Virginia, sixteen thousand five hundred copies, of which five thousand shall be for the use of the Senate and ten thousand for the use of the House of Representatives and the remaining one thousand five hundred copies shall be for the use and distribution of the Senators and Representatives in Congress from the State of West Virginia.

The Joint Committee on Printing is hereby authorized to have the copy prepared for the Public Printer, who shall procure suitable copper-process plates to be bound with these proceedings.


*Passed June 6, 1910.*

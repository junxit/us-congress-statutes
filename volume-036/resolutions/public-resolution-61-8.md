# Public Resolution 8

> Joint Resolution Providing for the filling of vacancies, which occurred on January twenty-seventh, nineteen hundred and ten, in the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution of the class other than Members of Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the vacancies which occurred on January twenty-seventh, nineteen hundred and ten, in the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution of the class other than Members of Congress, shall be filled by the reappointment of John B. Henderson and Alexander Graham Bell, residents of the city of Washington, whose terms of office expired on that date.


*Approved, February 11, 1910.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 29

> edward d. gilbert.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be, and he is hereby, requested to return to the Senate the bill (S. 6089) “To correct the military record of Edward D. Gilbert.”


*Passed April 1, 1910.*

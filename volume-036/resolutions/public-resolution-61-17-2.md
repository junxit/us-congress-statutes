# Public Resolution 17

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the printing of one hundred thousand copies of the Special Report on the Diseases of the Horse.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be printed and bound in cloth one hundred thousand copies of the Special Report on the Diseases of the Horse, the same to be first revised and brought to date under the supervision of the Secretary of Agriculture; seventy thousand copies for use of the House of Representatives, thirty thousand copies for use of the Senate.


*Approved, March 4, 1911.*

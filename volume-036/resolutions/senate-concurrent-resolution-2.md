# Senate Concurrent Resolution 2

> [No. 28.] Joint Resolution For the relief of Lafayette L. McKnight.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of Pensions is authorized and directed to consider Lafayette L. McKnight to have been in the military service of the United States throughout the period specified in an Act approved February ninth, nineteen hundred and nine, entitled “An Act for the relief of Lafayette L. McKnight.”


*Approved, May 18, 1910.*

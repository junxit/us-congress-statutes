# Public Resolution 4

> Joint Resolution Providing for the printing of two thousand copies of Senate Document Numbered Three hundred and fifty-seven, for use of the Department of State.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be printed for use of the Department of State two thousand copies of Senate Document Numbered Three hundred and fifty-seven, Sixty-first Congress, second session, “Treaties, Conventions, International Acts, Protocols, and Agreements between the United States of America and other powers.”


*Approved, February 14, 1911.*

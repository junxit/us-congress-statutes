# House Concurrent Resolution 33

> statue of george l. shoup.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the statue of George L. Shoup, presented by the State of Idaho and now in place in Statuary Hall, is hereby accepted in the name of the United States, and the thanks of Congress tendered the State for this contribution of the statue of one of its most eminent citizens, illustrious for his distinguished military and civil services.

## § 2.

That a copy of these resolutions, suitably engrossed and duly authenticated, be transmitted to the governor of the State of Idaho.


*Passed January 17, 1910.*

# Public Resolution 10

> Joint Resolution Changing the title of the sealer of weights and measures of the District of Columbia to superintendent of weights, measures, and markets.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sealer of weights and measures of the District of Columbia shall hereafter be known and designated as the “superintendent of weights, measures, and markets.”


*Approved, February 19, 1910.*

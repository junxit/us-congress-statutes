# Public Resolution 23

> Joint Resolution Providing for certain printing and binding for the International Bureau of American Republics.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the allotment for printing and binding for Congressfor the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, nineteen hundred and ten, be, and the same is hereby, made available for the printingand binding of the International Bureau of American Republics in the sum of three thousand four hundred and seventyeight dollars and eighty-three cents.


*Approved, May 7, 1910.*

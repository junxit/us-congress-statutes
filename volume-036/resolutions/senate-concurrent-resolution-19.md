# Senate Concurrent Resolution 19

> statue of general lewis wallace.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives. concurring),*

That the thanks of Congress be presented to the State of Indiana for providing the statue of General Lewis Wallace, a citizen of Indiana, distinguished as a soldier, diplomat, and author.

That the statue be accepted and placed in the National Statuary Hall in the Capitol, and that a copy of these resolutions duly authenticated be transmitted to the governor of the State of Indiana.


*Passed February 26, 1910.*

# Public Resolution 4

> Joint Resolution To remove ice gorges in the Ohio River.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That to enable the Secretary of War to take such steps as he may deem to be necessary in the removal of ice gorges in the Ohio River, which are threatening the destruction of life and property, there is appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of ten thousand dollars, or so much thereof as may be necessary.


*Approved, January 19, 1910.*

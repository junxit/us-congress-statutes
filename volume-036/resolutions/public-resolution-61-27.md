# Public Resolution 27

> Joint Resolution To provide for the printing as a House document of five hundred thousand copies of Farmers’ Bulletin Numbered Three hundred and ninety-one.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be printed as a House document five hundred thousand copies of Farmers’ Bulletin Numbered Three hundred and ninety-one, entitled “Economical Use of Meats in the Home,” four hundred thousand copies thereof for the use of the House of Representatives and one hundred thousand copies thereof for the use of the Senate.


*Approved, May 18, 1910.*

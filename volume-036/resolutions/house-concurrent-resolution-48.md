# House Concurrent Resolution 48

> james c. johnson.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the House of Representatives the bill (H. R. 1386) “To correct the naval record of James C. Johnson.”


*Passed June 21, 1910.*

# Public Resolution 33

> Joint Resolution Making the Act entitled “An Act to provide for the appropriate marking of the graves of the soldiers and sailors of the confederate army and navy who died in northern prisons and were buried near the prisons where they died, and for other purposes,” apply to the confederate mound in Oakwood Cemetery, at Chicago.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act entitled “An Act to provide for the appropriate marking of the graves of the soldiers and sailors of the confederate army and navy who died in northern prisons and were buried near the prisons where they died, and for other purposes,” approved March ninth, nineteen hundred and six, as heretofore continued in full force and effect, shall hereafter be construed to apply to the confederate mound in Oakwood Cemetery, at Chicago, anything in joint resolutions heretofore passed by Congress to the contrary notwithstanding.


*Approved, June 11, 1910.*

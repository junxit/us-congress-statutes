# House Concurrent Resolution 62

> helen s. hogan.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Speaker of the House of Representatives and the President of the Senate be, and hereby are, directed to erase their signatures to the bill (H. R. 25081) “For the relief of Helen S. Hogan,” and that the said bill be reenrolled with the words “Act of February twenty-sixth” changed to “Act of February twenty-fifth.”


*Passed February 24, 1911.*

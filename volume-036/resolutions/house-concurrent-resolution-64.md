# House Concurrent Resolution 64

> homestead entries in national forests.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the action of the Speaker of the House of Representatives and of the Vice President of the United States and President of the Senate in signing the enrolled bill (H. R. 26290) “Providing for the validation of certain homestead entries,” be, and hereby is, rescinded, and that the bill be reenrolled with the first word in section 2 changed from “than” to “that”.


*Passed March 3, 1911.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 37

> jonesboro, arkansas, courts.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate the bill (S. 6719) “To provide for the sittings of the United States circuit and district courts of the eastern division of the eastern district of Arkansas at the city of Jonesboro in said district.


*Passed June 23, 1910.*

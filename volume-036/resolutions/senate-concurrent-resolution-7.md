# Senate Concurrent Resolution 7

> stephenson grand army memorial.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed and bound, in the form of eulogies, including illustrations, seven thousand copies of the proceedings on the occasion of the dedication of the Stephenson Grand Army Memorial, in Washington, July third, nineteen hundred and nine, of which one thousand five hundred shall be for the use of the Senate, three thousand five hundred for the use of the House of Representatives, and two thousand to be delivered to the Stephenson Grand Army Memorial Committee.


*Passed March 2, 1911.*

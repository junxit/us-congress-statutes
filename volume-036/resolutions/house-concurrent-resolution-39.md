# House Concurrent Resolution 39

> comparison of tariff acts.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed and bound ten thousand copies of a compilation entitled “Comparison of the tariff Acts of July twenty-fourth, eighteen hundred and ninety-seven, and of August fifth, nineteen hundred and nine, showing the rates of duty, respectively, in parallel columns,” as prepared by William W. Evans, a clerk of the Committee on Ways and Means, and authorized by said committee to be printed, seven thousand five hundred copies for the use of the House and two thousand five hundred copies for the use of the Senate.


*Passed June 2, 1910.*

# Chapter 2

> An Act For the relief of Aaron Cornish.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws Aaron Cornish, who was assistant surgeon, Ninety-seventh Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a member of said organization on the eighth day of September, eighteen hundred and sixty-two: Provided, That no pension shall accrue prior to the approval of this Act.


*Approved, December 15, 1910.*

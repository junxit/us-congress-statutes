# Chapter 99

> An Act For the relief of George W. Flack.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws and the laws governing the National Home for Disabled Volunteer Soldiers, or any branch thereof, George W. Flack, now a resident of Pennsylvania, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a private of Company I, Fifth Regiment United States Veteran Volunteer Infantry, on the sixteenth day of June, eighteen hundred and sixty-six: Provided, That no pension shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 15, 1910.*

# Chapter 66

> An Act To reimburse Charles K. Darling for moneys necessarily expended by him as clerk of the court of appeals for the first circuit.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Charles K. Darling, clerk of the court of appeals for the first circuit, the sum of three hundred and seventy-nine dollars and seventeen cents, and said sum of three hundred and seventy-nine dollars and seventeen cents is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, being moneys necessarily expended by him and personal compensation in his official capacity as said clerk from May fourth, nineteen hundred and eight, to June thirtieth, nineteen hundred and eight.


*Approved, February 13, 1911.*

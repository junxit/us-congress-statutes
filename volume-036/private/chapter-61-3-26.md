# Chapter 26

> An Act For the relief of John H. Willis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws and the laws governing the National Home for Disabled Volunteer Soldiers, or any branch thereof, John H. Willis shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a private of Battery D, Fouith United States Artillery, on the twenty-sixth day of May, eighteen hundred and sixty-five: Provided,That no pension shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, January 23, 1911.*

# Chapter 294

> An Act For the relief of Valentine Fraker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws Valentine Fraker be held and considered to have been a member of Company D, Seventh Regiment Provisional Enrolled Missouri Militia, from April first, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, to July fifteenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, when he was honorably discharged: Provided, That no pension shall accrue or become payable prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 4, 1911.*

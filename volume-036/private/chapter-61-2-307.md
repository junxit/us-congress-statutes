# Chapter 307

> An Act For the relief of Demon S. Decker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, or benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers Demon S. Decker, who was a first lieutenant of Company G, Fifty-sixth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a first lieutenant of said company and regiment on the third day of April, eighteen hundred and sixty-four: Provided, That, other than as above set forth, no bounty, pay, pension, or other emolument shall accrue prior to or by reason of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 17, 1910.*

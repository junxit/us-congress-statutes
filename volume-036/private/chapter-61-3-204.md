# Chapter 204

> An Act For the relief of John Marshall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws John Marshall, late of Company F, Sixty-fifth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, the Third Independent Battery New York Volunteer Light Artillery, and the United States Navy, shall be deemed to have been honorably discharged April twenty-fourth, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, from the United States Navy: Provided,That no pension shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 2, 1911.*

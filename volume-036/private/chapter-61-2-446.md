# Chapter 446

> An Act For the relief of John A. Brown.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws John A. Brown, who served on the United States ship New Ironsides until March thirty-first, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the naval service on that date.


*Approved, June 25, 1910.*

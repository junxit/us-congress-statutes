# Chapter 273

> An Act Granting pensions and increase of pensions to certain soldiers and sailors of the Regular Army and Navy, and certain soldiers and sailors of wars other than the civil war, and to widows and dependent relatives of such soldiers and sailors.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in, Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws—

The name of John Howard, late of Troop F, Sixth Regiment United States Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.

The name of Walter S. Hall, alias Walter McLaughlin, late of Company D, Twelfth Regiment, United States Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.

The name of Sophie M. Guard, widow of Alexander McC. Guard, late first lieutenant, Nineteenth Regiment United States Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty-live dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.

The name of August Pfefferle, late of Company C, Eleventh Regiment United States Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.

The name of Joel W. Adkins, late of Captain Patrick H. Rogers’s Company F, Texas Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.

The name of Anthony L. Houston, late of Captain John Williams’s first company, Texas Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.

The name of John C. Bonner, late of Captain C. E. Travis’s Company E, Mounted Batallion Texas Volunteers, Texas and New Mexico Indian wars, and pay him a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.

The name of Jacob Burleson, late of Captain John S. Ford’s second company, Texas Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.

The name of James Conner, late of John S. Ford’s second company, Texas Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.

The name of Nannie C. Potter, widow of Joseph A. Potter, late major and quartermaster, United States Army, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty-five dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.

The name of Helen W. Wilson, widow of Van C. Wilson, late of Company B, Third Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month, and two dollars per month additional on account of the minor child of said Van C. Wilson until he reaches the age of sixteen years.

The name of Louis H. Pennington, late of band, Fourth Regiment Virginia Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.

The name of Margaret C. Fury, widow of William O. Fury, late of Company L, Third Regiment New Jersey Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month, and two dollars per month additional on account of each of the four minor children of said William O. Fury until they reach the age of sixteen years.

The name of Albert Steinhauser, late captain Company B, Forty-fifth Regiment United States Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain, and pay him a pension al the rate of forty-six dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.

The name of Stephen A. Kennedy, late of Company E, Third Regiment Indiana Volunteers, war with Mexico, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.

The name of Welcome N. Bender, late of Company F, Twentieth Regiment Kansas Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.

The name of Sarah F. McKee, widow of Warren M. McKee, late of Thirteenth Company, Signal Corps, United States Volunteers, war with Spain, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.

The name of John Hiet, late of Troop G, Fifth Regiment United States Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.

The name of Schofield Henderson, late of Company K, Second Regiment Tennessee Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.

The name of Sarah A. Salter, widow of Horace Salter, late of Morgan’s independent company, Iowa Mounted Volunteers, war with Mexico, and pay her a pension at the rate, of twelve dollars per month.

The name of Louisa Jacobs, widow of Martin Jacobs, late of Captain B. W. Ballard’s company, Kentucky Militia, war of eighteen hundred and twelve, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty- four dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.

The name of Hannah Kelley, dependent mother of John D. Kelley, late of Company L, Eighth Regiment Massachusetts Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.

The name of Celia Cornell, widow of Francis Cornell, late of Company I, Fourteenth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.

The name of Charles E. Woodward, late of Hospital Corps, United States Army, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.

The name of Mary Ryan, dependent mother of Peter Ryan, late of Company I, Second Regiment United States Infantry, war with Spain, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.

The name of Thomas J. D. Pinnick, late unassigned recruit, Third Regiment United States Dragoons, war with Mexico, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.

The name of Jennie M. Huntington, widow of Henry D. Huntington, late first lieutenant, Second Regiment United States Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty-five- dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.

The name of Frank MaCulley, late of Battery C, Pennsylvania Volunteer Light Artillery, war with Spain, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.

The name of Katherine Lawrence, widow of Harry B. Lawrence, late of Company E, Fifth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month, and two dollars per month additional to each of the two minor children of said Harry B. Lawrence until they reach the age of sixteen years.

The name of Sarah E. Hooper, widow of James Hooper, late of United States ship “Comet,” United States Navy, war of eighteen hundred and twelve, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.

The name of Thomas J. Seay, late of Company M, Second Regiment Arkansas Volunteer Infantry, and Company K, Third Regi-ment United States Engineers, and pay him a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month.

The name of Lewis W. Avant, late of Captain G. H. Nelson’s company, Texas Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.

The name of John H. Alexander, late of Captain G. H. Nelson’s company, Texas Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.

The name of Jeddie E. Rabidoux, late of Hospital Corps, United States Army, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, June 7, 1910.*

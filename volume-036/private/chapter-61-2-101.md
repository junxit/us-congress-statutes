# Chapter 101

> An Act For the relief of William H. Hawley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws William H. Hawley, alias Henry Hawley, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as private in Company E, Twenty-first Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, on the sixth day of April, eighteen hundred and sixty-three: Provided, That no pension shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 15, 1910.*

# Chapter 214

> An Act For the relief of Samuel W. Campbell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of six hundred and ninety-six dollars to the credit of Samuel W. Campbell, Indian agent at Lapointe Indian Agency, Wisconsin, on account of money paid by said Campbell to Delia M. Rabideau for clerical services, and the Secretary of the Interior is authorized to make such payment, and said sum of six hundred and ninety-six dollars is hereby appropriated for said purpose.


*Approved, May 6, 1910.*

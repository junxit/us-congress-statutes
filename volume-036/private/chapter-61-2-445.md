# Chapter 445

> An Act For the relief of James C. Johnson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws James C. Johnson, who served in the United States Navy until May fifth, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the naval service on that date.


*Approved, June 25, 1910.*

# Chapter 251

> An Act For the relief of Edward D. Gilbert.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, or benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers Edward D. Gilbert, now a resident of Haddam, Connecticut, who was a private in Company I, Twenty-first Regiment Connecticut Volunteer, Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a member of said company and regiment on the seventeenth day of March, eighteen hundred and sixty-three: Provided, That no pension or bounty shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, May 19, 1910.*

# Chapter 339

> An Act For the relief of Joseph Dobson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, or benefits upon persons who have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States, Joseph Dobson, who was a member of Company K, Twenty-first Regiment United States Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from said company and regiment on the eighteenth day of November, eighteen hundred and seventy-five.


*Approved, June 22, 1910.*

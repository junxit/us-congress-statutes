# Chapter 476

> An Act Repealing part of the Act of March fifth, nineteen hundred and ten, relating to an increase of pension to Jacob Whitlock.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That so much of the Act of Congress approved March fifth, nineteen hundred and ten (Private, Numbered Nineteen), increasing the pension of Jacob Whitlock, late of Company B, First Regiment New Jersey Volunteer Infantry, and unassigned, First Battalion Veteran Reserve Corps, be, and the same is hereby, repealed.


*Approved, June 25, 1910.*

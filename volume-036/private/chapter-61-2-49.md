# Chapter 49

> An Act For the relief of Thomas B. Pope.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws, Thomas B. Pope shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as first lieutenant of Company A, Fifty-sixth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, on the fourth day of September, eighteen hundred and sixty-two.


*Approved, February 19, 1910.*

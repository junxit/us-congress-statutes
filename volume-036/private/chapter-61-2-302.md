# Chapter 302

> An Act For the relief of Elizabeth G. Martin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of one thousand two hundred dollars, for the aid and support of Elizabeth G. Martin, widow of James P. Martin, who lost his life as the result of injuries received on the sixth day of April, nineteen hundred and six, by being run over by an engine of the Isthmian Canal Commission at Paraiso, belonging to the United States, being operated on the Panama Railroad at Paraiso, in the Canal Zone, be hereby appropriated, out of tiny money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the same to be paid to the said Elizabeth G. Martin by the Secretary of the Treasury immediately upon the approval of this Act.


*Approved, June 17, 1910.*

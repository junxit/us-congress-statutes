# Chapter 481

> An Act For the relief of Sanford A. Pinyan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, or benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Sanford A. Pinyan, who was a private in Company A, First Georgia Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been mustered in as a member of said company and regiment on August thirty-first, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, and honorably discharged therefrom on November fourth, eighteen hundred and sixty-four: Provided,That, other than as above set forth, no bounty, pay, pension, or other emolument shall accrue prior to or by reason of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 25, 1910.*

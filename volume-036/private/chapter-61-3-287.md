# Chapter 287

> An Act For the relief of Arthur G. Fisk.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster-General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the postal account of Arthur G. Fisk, postmaster at San Francisco, California, with the sum of thirteen thousand two hundred and twenty-nine dollars and seventy-nine cents for postal funds, stamps, and other stamped paper, and with the further sum of eight hundred and twenty-five dollars and eighty-six cents for money-order funds, on account of losses resulting from earthquakes and fire April eighteenth, nineteen hundred and six.

## § 2.

That there be, and there is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of fourteen thousand and fifty-five dollars and sixty-five cents for the purposes specified in this Act.


*Approved, March 4, 1911.*

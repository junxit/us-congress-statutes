# Chapter 308

> An Act To appropriate the sum of two hundred dollars for Fenton T. Ross, of Loudoun County, Virginia, whose horse was permanently injured by employees of the Agricultural Department in making experiments authorized by law.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Fenton T. Ross, of Loudoun County, Virginia, the sum of two hundred dollars, which sum is hereby appropriated out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to reimburse the said Fenton T. Ross for the loss of a horse which was permanently injured on May eighteenth, nineteen hundred and nine, by becoming entangled in a piece of steel wire which had fallen across his pasture, in which said horse was grazing, said wire having become detached from certain kites living from the United States Weather Bureau at Mount Weather, Virginia.


*Approved, June 17, 1910.*

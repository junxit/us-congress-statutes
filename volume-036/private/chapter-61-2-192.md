# Chapter 192

> An Act For the relief of Dewitt Eastman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, or benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Dewitt Eastman, who was a private of Battery I, Fourth Regiment United States Artillery, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a member of said battery and regiment on the thirteenth day of June, eighteen hundred and sixty-five: Provided, That, other than as above set forth, no bounty, pay, pension, or other emolument shall accrue prior to or by reason of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, April 26, 1910.*

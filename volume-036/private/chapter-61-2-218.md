# Chapter 218

> An Act For the relief of Theodore F. Colgrove.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws Theodore F. Colgrove shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a lieutenant-colonel of the One hundred and forty-seventh Regiment Indiana Infantry, on the thirtieth day of September, eighteen hundred and sixty-five: Provided, That no pension shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, May 9, 1910.*

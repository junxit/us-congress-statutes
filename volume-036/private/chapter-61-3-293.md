# Chapter 293

> An Act For the relief of David F. Wallace.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws, David F. Wallace, who was a private of Company M, Fifth Tennessee Volunteer Cavalry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a member of said company and regiment on the fourth day of June, eighteen hundred and sixty-four: Provided, That no pension shall accrue prior to the approval of this Act.


*Approved, March 4, 1911.*

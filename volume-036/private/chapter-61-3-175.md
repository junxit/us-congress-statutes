# Chapter 175

> An Act For the relief of James Jones.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws and the laws governing the National Home for Disabled Volunteer Soldiers, or any branch thereof, James Jones shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a private of Company E, Thirty-third Regiment New York Veteran Volunteers, on the second day of May, eighteen hundred and sixty-three: Provided,That no pension shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 27, 1911.*

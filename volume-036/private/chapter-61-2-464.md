# Chapter 464

> An Act For the relief of Matthew McCarthy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, or benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers Matthew McCarthy, who was a private of Company H, Eighteenth Regiment Indiana Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a member of said company and regiment on the eighth day of July, eighteen hundred and sixty-four: Provided,That, other than as above set forth, no bounty, pay, pension, or other emolument shall accrue prior to or by reason of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 25, 1910.*

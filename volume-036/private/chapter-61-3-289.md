# Chapter 289

> An Act For the relief of Alexander Wilkie.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws and of all laws granting relief and privileges to honorably discharged soldiers who served during the Civil War, Alexander Wilkie, formerly second lieutenant Company C, Tenth Regiment Vermont Volunteer Infantry, shall be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a member of said organization to date December thirty-first, eighteen hundred and sixty-four: Provided, That other than as above set forth, no bounty, pay, pension, or other emolument shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 4, 1911.*

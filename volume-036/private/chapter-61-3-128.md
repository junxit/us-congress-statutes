# Chapter 128

> An Act Authorizing the issuance of a patent to certain lands to Charles E. Miller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to issue a patent to Charles E. Miller, for the benefit of his grantees and their successors, for the land embraced in his homestead entry, serial number nought two hundred and eighty-seven, Lander, Wyoming, according to description to be determined by special survey or otherwise as the Secretary of the Interior may find necessary, corresponding as nearly as possible to the entry as made, upon the payment of all reclamation charges against such lands, and the usual commissions and also the cost of survey, if any.


*Approved, February 18, 1911.*

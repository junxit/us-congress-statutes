# Chapter 125

> An Act For the relief of William P. Drummon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That William P. Drummon shall hereafter be held and considered to have been mustered into the service of the United States as a private of Company H, Seventeenth Regiment New York State Militia Volunteer Infantry, on the eighth day of July, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, and to have remained continuously in said service until honorably discharged therefrom on the thirteenth day of August, eighteen hundred and sixty-three: Provided, however,That no pay, bounty, or other allowances shall become due or payable by virtue of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 18, 1911.*

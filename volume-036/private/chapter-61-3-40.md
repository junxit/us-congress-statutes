# Chapter 40

> An Act For the relief of Cornelius Cahill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws Cornelius Cahill shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a corporal in Company G, Twenty-sixth Regiment Iowa Volunteer Infantry, on the fifth day of June, eighteen hundred and sixty-three: Provided,That no pension shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 8, 1911.*

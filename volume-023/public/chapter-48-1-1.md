# Chapter 1

> An act to fix the time for holding the District Court in the District of Maine at Bangor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the regular term of the District Court of the District of Maine now held at Bangor on the Fourth Tuesday shall hereafter be held on the First Tuesday of June.


*Approved, January 18th, 1884.*

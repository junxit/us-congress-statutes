# Chapter 80

> An act in relation to the legislature of Dakota Territory.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the legislature of the Territory of Dakota shall hereafter consist of twenty-four members of the council and forty-eight members of the house of representatives, and that there shall be elected at the next general election in said Territory two members of the council and four members of the house of representatives in each of the twelve legislative districts provided for in chapter seven of the Territorial statutes of eighteen hundred and eighty-three of said Territory.


*Approved, June 12, 1884.*

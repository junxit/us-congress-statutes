# Chapter 1

> An Act to provide statuary and historical tablets for the Saratoga monument.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of forty thousand dollars be, and the same hereby is, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to be expended by the Saratoga Monument Association in statuary and historical tablets within and upon the monument now erected at Schuylerville, New York, commemorative of Burgoyne’s surrender.

## § 2.

That said money shall be paid by the Secretary of the Treasury to the said association upon the certificate and receipt of the president of the association.


*Approved, December 18th, 1884.*

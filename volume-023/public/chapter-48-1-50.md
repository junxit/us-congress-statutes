# Chapter 50

> An act to repeal section eight of an act entitled “Au act to accept and ratify the agreement submitted by the confederated bands of Ute Indians in Colorado for the sale of their reservation in said State, and for other purposes, and to make the necessary appropriations for carrying out the same,” approved June fifteenth, eighteen hundred and eighty.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, section eight of an act entitled “An act to accept and ratify the agreement submitted by the confederated bands of Ute Indians in Colorado for the sale of their reservation in said State, and for other purposes, and to make the necessary appropriations for carrying out the same,” approved June fifteenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, be, and the same is hereby, repealed; and that the lands referred to in said section are hereby restored to the public domain.


*Approved, May 14, 1884.*

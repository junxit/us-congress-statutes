# Chapter 45

> An Act to release the reversionary right of the Government of the United States to certain lands in the city of Detroit and State of Michigan,

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That any right, of reversion or otherwise which the United States may be supposed to have in the space on which the high-school building belonging to the board of education of the city of Detroit is situtated being a part of the old town of Detroit, laid out by the governor and judges of the Territory of Michigan under an act of Congress approved April twenty-first, eighteen hundred and six, entitled “An act to provide for the adjustment of the titles of land in the town of Detroit and Territory of Michigan, and for other purposes,” he, and the same is hereby, granted and released to the said board of education.


*Approved, January 31st, 1885.*

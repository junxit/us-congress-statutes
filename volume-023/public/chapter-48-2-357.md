# Chapter 357

> An act to increase the salary of the clerk of the Court of Alabama Claims

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the salary of the clerk of the Court of Commissioners, of Alabama Claims shall hereafter be at the rate of four thousand four hundred dollars per annum,


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

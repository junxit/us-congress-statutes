# Chapter 353

> An Act amending section seven hundred and sixty-four of the Revised Statutes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section seven hundred and sixty four of the Revised Statutes be amended so that the same shall read as follows:

> “From the final decision of such circuit court an appeal may be taken to the Supreme Court in the cases described in the preceding section”.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

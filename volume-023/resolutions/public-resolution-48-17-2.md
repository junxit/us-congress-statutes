# Public Resolution 17

> Joint resolution providing for printing monograph two of the publications of the United States Geological Survey.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be printed at the Government Printing Office the usual number of monograph second of the publications of the United States Geological Survey, with the necessary illustrations, and to conform to the editions already issued by the Survey.


*Approved, March 2d, 1885.*

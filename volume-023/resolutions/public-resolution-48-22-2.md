# Public Resolution 22

> Joint resolution to provide for the printing of additional copies of the report of the committee on Military Affairs on the investigation of the National Home for disabled volunteer soldiers.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in addition to the authorized number five thousand copies of the report of the Committee on Military Affairs on the investigation of the National Home for disabled volunteers be printed one thousand for the use of the Senate, and four thousand for the use of the House of Representatives.


*Approved, March 3, 1885.*

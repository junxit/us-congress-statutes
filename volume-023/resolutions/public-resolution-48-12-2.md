# Public Resolution 12

> Joint resolution to print two thousand additional copies of Lieutenant P. H. Ray’s report of the International Polar Expedition to Point Barrow, Alaska.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That two thousand additional copies of Lieutenant P. H. Ray’s report of the International Polar Expedition to Point Barrow, Alaska, be printed for distribution by the Chief Signal Officer of the Army.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

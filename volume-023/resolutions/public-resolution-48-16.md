# Public Resolution 16

> Joint resolution providing for printing the sixth and seventh annual reports of the Director of the Bureau of Ethnology.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be printed at the Government Printing Office fifteen thousand five hundred copies each of the sixth and seventh annual reports of the Director of the Bureau of Ethnology, with accompanying papers and illustrations, and uniform with the preceding volumes of the series; of which three thousand five hundred shall be for the use of the Senate, seven thousand for the use of the House of Representatives, and five thousand for distribution by the Bureau of Ethnology.


*Approved, March 2, 1885.*

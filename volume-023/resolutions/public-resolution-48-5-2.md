# Public Resolution 5

> Joint resolution providing for the payment of laborers in Government employ for certain holidays.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the employees of the Navy Yard, Government Printing Office, Bureau of Printing and Engraving, and all other per diem employees of the Government on duty at Washington, or elsewhere in the United States, shall be allowed the following holidays, to wit: The first day of January, the twenty-second day of February, the fourth day of July, the twenty-fifth day of December, and such days as may be designated by the President as day’s for national thanksgiving, and shall receive the same pay as on other days.


*Approved, Jan. 6, 1885.*

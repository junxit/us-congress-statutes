# Public Resolution 19

> Joint resolution providing for the addition of ten thousand dollars to the contingent fund of the Senate.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of ten thousand dollars, or so much thereof as may be necessary, is hereby appropriated out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated to be added to the contingent fund of the Senate for the payment of the current expenses of special and select.committees for the fiscal year ending June the thirtieth, eighteen hundred and eighty-four.


*Approved, March 31st, 1884.*

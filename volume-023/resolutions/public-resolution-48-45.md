# Public Resolution 45

> Joint resolution authorizing the printing and binding of additional copies of the reports of the National Board of Health.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Public Printer be, and he is hereby, authorized to print and bind, for the use of the National Board of Health one thousand copies of each of its annual reports beginning with the year eighteen hundred and eighty.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

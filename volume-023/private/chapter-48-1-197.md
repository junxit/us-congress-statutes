# Chapter 197

> AN ACT granting a pension to Ira McNair.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ira McNair, late a private in Company D, Eighteenth Regiment Iowa Volunteers.


*Approved, July 4, 1884.*

# Chapter 235

> An Act granting a pension to Charles W. Baldwin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions of the pension laws, the name of Charles W. Baldwin, late a private in Company C, Nineteenth Regiment Illinois Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

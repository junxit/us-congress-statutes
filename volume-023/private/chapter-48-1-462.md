# Chapter 462

> An Act for the relief of William Stansberry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of William Stansberry, late of Company A, Third West Virginia Cavalry, on the pension-roll, subject to the rules and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

# Chapter 177

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Merlin C, Harris.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Merlin C, Harris, late Captain of Company C, Ninety-sixth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, from the amount now paid him as sergeant to the amount paid a first lieutenant.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

# Chapter 494

> An Act granting a pension to W. H. H. Coleman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, directed and authorized to place on the pension-roll, the name of W. H. H. Coleman, late a private of Company B, Eleventh Regiment Pennsylvania Reserve Corps, subject to the pro visions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

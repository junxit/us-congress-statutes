# Chapter 244

> An Act granting a pension to John Galloup

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of John Gallonp, late a corporal of Company F, Sixty ninth Regiment Enrolled Missouri Militia, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, and pay him a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

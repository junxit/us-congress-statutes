# Chapter 496

> An Act granting a pontoon to Elizabeth Kaler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the mime of Elizabeth Kaler, foster-mother of George W, Kaler, late a private in Company E, Eighty-seventh Regiment Indiana Volunteers, and that the rate of her pension be the same as if she had been the mother of the deceased soldier,


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

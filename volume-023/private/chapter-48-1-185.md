# Chapter 185

> An act granting a pension to Joseph T. Caldwell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Joseph T. Caldwell, late of Company G, Sixty-ninth Regiment Enrolled Missouri Militia, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, and pay him a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 4, 1884.*

# Chapter 475

> An Act granting a pension to Joseph Raible,

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Joseph Raible of Indiana, who lost an arm above the elbow, during the war, while firing a salute, at the city of Indianapolis, Indiana, under the direction of the quartermaster general of Indiana; and that the said Secretary be, and he is hereby, directed to pay him at the rate of thirty dollars per month, in lieu of all other pensions now paid him.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

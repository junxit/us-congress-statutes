# Chapter 196

> An Act for the relief of William C. H. Bowman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William C. H. Bowman, late a private in Company D, Second Regiment Missouri Cavalry.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

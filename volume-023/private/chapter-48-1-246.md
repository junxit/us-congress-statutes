# Chapter 246

> An Act granting a pension to Reuben Marshall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Reuben Marshall late a private in the Kansas Volunteer Militia.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

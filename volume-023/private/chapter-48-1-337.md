# Chapter 337

> An Act for the relief of Henrietta M. Sands

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That from and after the passage of this act there be paid, out of the naval pension fund, to Henrietta M. Sauds, widow of the late Rear-Admiral Benjamin F. Sands,United States Navy, the sum of fifty dollars per month during her widowhood, the same to be in lieu of her present pension


*Approved, July 7, 1884.*

# Chapter 141

> An act granting a pension to Isabella J. Ramsdell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, at the rate of eight dollars per month, to date from the passage of this act, the name of Isabella J Ramsdell, on account of the death of her husband, David D. Ramsdell, a sailor and marine of the United States service for over thirty years, who died of disease super-induced by said service.


*Approved, July 1, 1884.*

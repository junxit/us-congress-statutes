# Chapter 132

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Charlotte Hackett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Charlotte Hackett, mother of Samuel F. Hackett, late a private in Company E, Second Regiment Wisconsin Volunteers, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 19th, 1885.*

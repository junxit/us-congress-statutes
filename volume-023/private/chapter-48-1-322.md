# Chapter 322

> An Act granting a pension to Catherine Hunter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Catherine Hunter, widow of Levi M. Hunter, late a private in Company A, One hundred and twentieth Indiana Volunteers, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

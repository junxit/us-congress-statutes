# Chapter 211

> AN ACT granting a pension to Mrs Mary Morris Husband.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America-in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Mary Morris Husband, a volunteer nurse in the late war, giving her the sum of twenty-live dollars a month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 4, 1884.*

# Chapter 131

> An Act granting a pension to Sarah Denny Ripley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Sarah Denny Ripley, widow of Brigadier-General James W. Ripley, deceased, late Chief of Ordnance, United States Army, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 19th, 1885.*

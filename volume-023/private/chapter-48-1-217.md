# Chapter 217

> An Act granting a pension to Eliza Pigeon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior he, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Eliza Pigeon, widow of Joseph Pigeon, late a private in Company B, Third Regiment New York Artillery Volunteers in the war of the rebellion.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

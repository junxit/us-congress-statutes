# Chapter 240

> An Act granting a pension to John Robbins

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Robbins, late a private in Company A, Second Illinois Volunteers (Mexican War) at the rate of twenty four dollars per mouth; the same to be in lieu of any pension now received,


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

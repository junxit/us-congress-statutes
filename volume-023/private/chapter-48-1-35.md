# Chapter 35

> An act granting a pension to Laurena C. P. Haskins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Laurena 0. P. Haskins, widow of H D. Haskins, as a lieutenant in the one hundred and tenth Regiment New York Volunteers and to pay her pension as a lieutenants widow from and after the passage of this act, in lieu of the pension now granted to her.


*Approved, April 28, 1884.*

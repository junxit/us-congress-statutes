# Chapter 301

> An Act for the relief of Otto Leissring.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Otto Leissring, late first lieutenant Company G, Eighteenth Regiment United States Colored Troops, and pay him a pension of thirty dollars per month, in lieu of all pensions now paid him,


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

# Chapter 250

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Merritt Lewis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is, hereby, authorized and directed to increase to the first grade, namely, fifty dollars per month, the pension now granted to Merritt Lewis by certificate numbered thirty-three thousand six hundred and thirty-one.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

# Chapter 187

> An act granting a pension to Patrick Droney.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Patrick Droney, son of Mathew Droney, late a private marine in the United States Navy, and pay him a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month, to take effect from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 4, 1884.*

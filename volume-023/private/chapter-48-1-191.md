# Chapter 191

> An act granting a pension to Mrs. Mary McLaughlin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Mary Mc.Langhlin, widow of Barney McLaughlin, late of Company I, Thirty-fourth Regiment New Jersey Volunteers.


*Approved, July 4, 1884.*

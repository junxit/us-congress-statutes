# Chapter 44

> An Act to remove the political disabilities of W. H. Ward, of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of eaeh House concurring therein),*

That all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment to the constitution of the United States be, and the same are hereby, removed from W. H. Ward, of Virginia.


*Approved, January 30th, 1885.*

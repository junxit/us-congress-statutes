# Chapter 325

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Rowland Ward.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Rowland Ward, late a private in Company I, Fourth Regiment New York Heavy Artillery to forty dollars per month in lieu of the pension now received by him, the increase hereby granted to commence from the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

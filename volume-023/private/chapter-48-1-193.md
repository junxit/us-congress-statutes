# Chapter 193

> An act granting a pension to Catharine Henry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Catharine Henry, widow of Hiram Henry, late a private in Company F, Sixty-first New York Volunteers.


*Approved, July 4, 1884.*

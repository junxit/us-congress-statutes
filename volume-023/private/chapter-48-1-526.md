# Chapter 526

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs Lucy Parr.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed toplace the name of Lucy Parr, widow of Samuel B. Parr, late of Company I, Fourth Regiment Iowa Volunteer Infantry, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

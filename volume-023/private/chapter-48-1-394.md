# Chapter 394

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Ann Cornelia Lan man.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ann Cornelia Lanman, and pay her a pension of fifty dollars a month from and after the passage of this act, in lieu of the pensiou now received by her.


*Approved, March 3, 1885.*

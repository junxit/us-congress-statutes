# Chapter 347

> An Act granting a pension to John C. F. Beyland, of New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of pensions be, and he is hereby, authorized to place on the pension-roll subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John 0. F. Beyland, of New York, wounded at the post of Clarksburg, West Virginia.


*Approved, July 7, 1884.*

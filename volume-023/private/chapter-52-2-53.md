# Chapter 53

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Mrs. Mary K. S. Eaton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension of thirty dollars per mouth heretofore granted and now allowed to Mrs. Mary K. S. Eaton, widow of General Amos B. Eaton, late Commissary-General of the Army of the United States, be, and the same hereby is, increased to the sum of fifty dollars per mouth; and that the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, directed to pay to her the sum last mentioned per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, February tenth, 1885.*

# Chapter 527

> An Act granting a pension to Addie L. Moore.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll of the United States the name of Addie L. Moore, widow of Camillus A. Moore, late a private in Company E, Seventy-fourth Regiment Illinois Volunteers, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension-laws, to take effect from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

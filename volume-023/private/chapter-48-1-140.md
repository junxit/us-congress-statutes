# Chapter 140

> An act granting a pension to Louisa V. de Kilpatrick, widow of Major-General Judson Kilpatrick.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Louisa V. de Kilpatrick, widow of Major-General Judson Kilpatrick, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, July 1, 1884.*

# Chapter 200

> An Act granting a pension to Margaret A, Ringwait.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place ou the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Margaret A. Ringwait, sister of Lewis Ringwait, late of Company F, Seventeenth Pennsylvania Cavalry, and to pay her the pension allowed by law to the dependent relatives of deceased soldiers.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

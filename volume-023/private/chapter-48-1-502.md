# Chapter 502

> An Act granting a pension to Sylvester Greenough,

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Sylvester Greenough, a soldier of the Mexican war, Black Hawk war, and Florida war, and late a. private in Company E, Thirty-seventh Regiment Iowa Volunteer Infantry (Gray Beard Regiment), at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

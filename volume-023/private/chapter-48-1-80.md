# Chapter 80

> An Act for the relief of Mrs Jane Young.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs Jane Young, mother of Henry Young, alias Frank Lowe, late a member of Company G, Sixteenth Regiment of Massachusetts Volunteers.


*Approved, February 14th, 1885.*

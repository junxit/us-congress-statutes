# Chapter 175

> An act for the relief of John Kane.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll of the United States the name of John Kane, late a private in Company A, Eleventh Regiment Iowa Volunteer Infantry, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws; such pension to commence from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 3, 1884.*

# Chapter 203

> AN ACT granting a pension to Alexander Saint Bernard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Alexander Saint Bernard, late pilot on the steamer Michigan, United States Navy, and pay him a pension, from and after the passage of this act, at the rate of fifteen dollars per month.


*Approved, July 4, 1884.*

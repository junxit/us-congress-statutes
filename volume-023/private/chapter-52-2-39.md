# Chapter 39

> An Act to remove the political disabilities of Gabriel H. Hill of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (tico-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all political disabilities imposed by the Fourteenth Amendment to the Constitution of the United States be, and the same are hereby, removed from Gabriel H. Hill, of Virginia.


*Approved, January 27th, 1885.*

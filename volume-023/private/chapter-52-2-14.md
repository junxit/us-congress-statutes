# Chapter 14

> An Act granting a pension to Albert Brant.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, at eight dollars per month, the name of Albert Brant, late a private iu Company A, Fourth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Cavalry.


*Approved, January tenth, 1885.*

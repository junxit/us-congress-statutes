# Chapter 115

> An act granting a pension to William McClure.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws the name of William McClure, late a private in Company A, of the Third Illinois Light Artillery and to pay him a pension from the date of the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 20, 1884.*

# Chapter 285

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Mary Anna Egan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Mary Anna Egan, widow of the ¡ate James Egan, a Captain in the United States Army, be, and she is hereby, granted and allowed, from and after the passage of this act, a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month; and the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of said Mary Anna Egan on the pension roll at said rate, in lieu of the pension now paid her.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

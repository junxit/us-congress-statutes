# Chapter 433

> An Act for the relief of Edward Kraemer

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Edward Kraemer, formerly a member of Company F, Twelfth Regiment Illinois State Volunteers, to twenty-four dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

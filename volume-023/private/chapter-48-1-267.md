# Chapter 267

> An Act granting a pension to Margaret Wiggins

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the laws, the name of Margaret Wiggins, mother of Henry Wiggins, late a private in the Ninth Regiment Kansas Volunteer Cavalry.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

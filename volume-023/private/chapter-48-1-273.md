# Chapter 273

> An Act granting a pension to Arthur I. McConnell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Arthur I. McConnell, late of Company M, One hundred and second Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

# Chapter 183

> An act granting a pension to Mrs. Mary C. Jones.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Mrs. Mary C. Jones, widow of Doctor Thomas M, Jones, late surgeon of the One hundred and thirtieth Regiment Indiana Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws. This act to take effect from and after its passage.


*Approved, July 4, 1884.*

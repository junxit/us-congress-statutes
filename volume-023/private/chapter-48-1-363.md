# Chapter 363

> An Act to remove certain disabilities of John L. Cowardin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That his disabilities under said section be, and they are hereby, removed.


*Approved, July 7, 1884.*

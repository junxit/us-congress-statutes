# Chapter 252

> An Act granting a pension to Reuben J Chewning

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a pension be, and is hereby, granted to Reuben J Chewning, late a private in the Fourth Regiment Minnesota Infantry Volunteers; and that the Commissioner of Pensions be, and is hereby, instructed to place the name of said Reuben J Chewning on the pension roll.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

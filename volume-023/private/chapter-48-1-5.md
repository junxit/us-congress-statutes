# Chapter 5

> An act to remove the political disabilities of Samuel H. Lockett, of Alabama.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States be, and the same are hereby, removed from Samuel H. Lockett, of Alabama.


*Approved, January 31st, 1884.*

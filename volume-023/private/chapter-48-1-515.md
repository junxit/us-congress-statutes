# Chapter 515

> An Act granting a pension to Henry Bierderbick.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Henry Bierderbick, late a hospital steward in the United States Army, and one of the survivors of the late Greely expedition to Lady Franklin Bay.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

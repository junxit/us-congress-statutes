# Chapter 238

> AN ACT granting a pension to Theresa Crosby Watson

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll of the United States the name of Theresa Crosby Watson, widow of James M. Watson late a commodore in the United States Navy, and to pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars a month from and after the passage of this act, subject to all the other provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

# Chapter 478

> An Act granting a pension to David N. Bryan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of David N. Bryan, late a private in Company D, Seventy-eighth Regiment Ohio Volunteers


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

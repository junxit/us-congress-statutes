# Chapter 13

> An Act for the relief of Jacob Hoerth

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, au.horized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jacob Iloerth, late of Company C, Twenty-eighth Regiment of Kentucky Volunteers.


*Approved, January tenth, 1885.*

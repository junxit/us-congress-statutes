# Chapter 514

> An Act granting a pension to Matilda Cody

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Matilda Cody widow of John Cody late of Company L, Fourteenth Regiment Kentucky Cavalry,


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

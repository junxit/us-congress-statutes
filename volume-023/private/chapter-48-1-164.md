# Chapter 164

> An act for the relief Catherine Terry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Catherine Terry, widow of Mitchell Terry late a private in Company A, One hundred and eighteenth New York Volunteers, upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, July 3, 1884.*

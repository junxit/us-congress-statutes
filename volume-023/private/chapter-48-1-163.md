# Chapter 163

> An act restoring to the pension-roll the name of John Allee

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to restore to the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of John Allee, formerly of Company K, Twenty-second Regiment Indiana Volunteers.


*Approved, July 3, 1884.*

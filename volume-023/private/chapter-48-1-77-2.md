# Chapter 77

> An Act for the relief of Hugh Dougherty.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Hugh Dougherty, late first lieutenant of Company K, Thirteenth Regiment Kansas Infantry.


*Approved, February 14th, 1885.*

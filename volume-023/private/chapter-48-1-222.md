# Chapter 222

> An Act granting a pension to Mary C. Axline.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to place the name of Mary C Axline, widow of Jacob Axline, late a first lieutenant in Captain Hickman Mills’s Company of Missouri Enrolled Militia, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

# Chapter 450

> An Act for the relief of Rose Dougherty,

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Rose Dougherty, widow of John Dougherty, late of Company B, Thirty-seventh Regiment New York Volunteers,


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

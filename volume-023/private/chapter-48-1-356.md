# Chapter 356

> An Act granting a pension to Sarah C. Hall

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United. States of America in Congress assembled, That,*

the Secretary of the interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to restore to the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah C. Hall, widow of John Hall, late a captain in the Fourth New York Cavalry, with pay from the date her name was dropped from the pension-roll


*Approved, July 7, 1884.*

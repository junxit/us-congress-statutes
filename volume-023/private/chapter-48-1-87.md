# Chapter 87

> An act granting an increase of pension to George F. Dresser.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of George F. Dresser, formerly a private in Company A, Thirty fifth Regiment New York State Volunteers, at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, June 12, 1884.*

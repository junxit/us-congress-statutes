# Chapter 300

> An Act granting a pension to Phillip Wiggins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Phillip Wiggins, late a private in Company F, Thirty-fifth United States Colored Troops, and wounded at the battle of Olustee, on the twentieth day of February, eighteen hundred and sixty-four.


*Approved, February 28, 1885.*

# Chapter 499

> An Act to inereaae the pension of Jane D. Brom.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be and he is hereby, directed to increase the pension of Jane. 1). Brent, widow of Thomas L. Brent, late captain of Company, Fourth Regiment Artillery of the Regular Army of the United States, to a rating of fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

# Chapter 384

> An Act granting a pension to Mary B. Holmes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary B. Holmes, widow of Henry W. Holmes, late a lieutenant of Company F, Seventy-second Regiment New York Volunteers, and allow her the same pension drawn by her husband during his life.


*Approved, March 3, 1885.*

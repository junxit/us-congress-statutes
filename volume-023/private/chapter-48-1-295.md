# Chapter 295

> An Act granting a pension to Simon E. Lewis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of Américain Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Simon E. Lewis, late a private in Company H, Sixteenth United States I ii fan try.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

# Chapter 96

> An Act granting a pension to William Herring

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of William Herring, late a private in Company A, Thirteenth United States Colored Heavy Artillery, on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 14th, 1885.*

# Chapter 10

> An Act granting a pension to Frederick Corfe

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject, to the provisions and limitations of the pension- laws, the name of Frederick Corfe, late an acting staff surgeon in the United States Army.


*Approved, January tenth, 1885.*

# Chapter 41

> An act for the relief of Columbus F. Perry and Elizabeth H. Gilmer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby directed, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to pay to Columbus F. Perry and Elizabeth H. Gilmer three thousand dollars.


*Approved, May 7, 1884.*

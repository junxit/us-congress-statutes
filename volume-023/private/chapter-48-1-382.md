# Chapter 382

> An Act granting a pension to Robert Sheridan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Robert Sheridan, late a private in Company D, First Rhode Island Light Artillery.


*Approved, March 3, 1885.*

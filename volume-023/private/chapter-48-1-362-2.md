# Chapter 362

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Elijah W. Penny.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject, to the provisions of the pension laws, the name of Elijah W. Penny, late lieutenant-colonel of the One hundred and thirtieth Regiment of Indiana Volunteers, at the rate of forty-two dollars per month, in lieu of the thirty-six dollars per month heretofore allowed him, as specified in pension certificate seventy-six thousand one hundred and forty-four.


*Approved, March 3, 1885.*

# Chapter 276

> An Act granting a pension to Andrew Franklin, alias Andrew McKee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Andrew Franklin, alias Andrew McKee, late a private in Captain M. Armstrong’s company of Ohio militia, from August twenty-second, eighteen hundred and twelve, to February twenty-second, eighteen hundred and thirteen, and from July twenty-eighth, eighteen hundred and thirteen, to August eighteenth, eighteen hundred and thirteen, in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve, to take effect from the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 28, 1885.*

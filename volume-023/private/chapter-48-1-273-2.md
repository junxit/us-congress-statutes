# Chapter 273

> An Act granting an increase of pension to George A. Washburn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of George A. Washburn, late major of the Sixteenth Regiment Connecticut Volunteers, and brevet brigadier-general, to forty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, February 28, 1885.*

# Chapter 155

> An Act to remove the political disabilities of Alexander W. Stark.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment to the constitution of the United States be, and the same are hereby, removed from Alexander W. Stark, of Virginia.


*Approved, February 25th, 1885.*

# Chapter 191

> An Act granting a pension William L. Sloan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William L. Sloan, late a corporal of Company B, Third Regiment Minnesota Volunteers.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

# Chapter 292

> An Act for the relief of Sherman C. Perry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Sherman C. Perry, late of Company B, Sixteenth New York Volunteers, a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month, in lieu of the pension now paid to him, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 28, 1885.*

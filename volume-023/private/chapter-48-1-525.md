# Chapter 525

> An Act to restore to the pension-roll the name of Rachael A. Queen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, directed to restore to the pension-roll the name of Rachael A Queen, dependent sister of Jonathan Queen, late of Company F, Fifth California Volunteers.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

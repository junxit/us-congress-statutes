# Chapter 307

> An Act granting a pension to Alonzo Cooper,

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place the name of Alonzo Cooper, late first lieutenant of Company F. Twelfth New York Cavalry, war of the rebellion, on the pension-roll subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

# Chapter 321

> An Act for the relief of Maria L, Lee,

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Maria L. Lee, widow of Abel H, Lee, who was a scout in the Army of the Potomac during the war of the rebellion, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws of the United States.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

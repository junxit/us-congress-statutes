# Chapter 338

> An Act granting a pension to Wesley Morford.

*Be it enacted by the Seriate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Wesley Morford, late a private in Company K. Twelfth Regiment of Iowa Volunteers, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 7, 1884.*

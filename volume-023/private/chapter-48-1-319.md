# Chapter 319

> An Act granting a pension to P. W. Bradbury.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be authorized to place the name of P. W. Bradbury, of Saint Louis, Missouri, and formerly connected with the command of General John C. Fremont, as a scout, on the pension list, at the rate of twenty dollars per mouth.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

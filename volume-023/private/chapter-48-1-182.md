# Chapter 182

> An Act granting a pension to Hugh Ryan,

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Hugh Ryan, late assistant surgeon of the Thirty-fourth Regiment of Kentucky Volunteer Infantry,


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

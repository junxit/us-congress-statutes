# Chapter 400

> An Act to increase the pension of Mrs Martha C. Breese.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension now granted to Mrs. Martha C. Breese, widow of Kidder Randolph Breese, late a captain in the United States Navy, be, and the same is hereby increased to fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1885.*

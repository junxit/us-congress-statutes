# Chapter 442

> An Act for the relief of Lieutenant Nathaniel Johnson Coffin,

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension now granted to Nathaniel Johnson Coffin, late first lieutenant of Company K, Thirteenth New Hampshire Volunteer Infantry in the late war of the rebellion, be, and the same is hereby, increased to twenty dollars per month,


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

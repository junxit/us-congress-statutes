# Chapter 447

> An Act for the relief of Mary Royal,

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Mary Royal, widow of William Royal, deceased, late a private in Company I, Fifty-ninth Regiment of Indiana Volunteer Infantry, on the pension roll, subject to the limitations and conditions of the pension laws.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

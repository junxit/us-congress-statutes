# Chapter 280

> An Act granting a pension to William H. H. Gilley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William H. H. Gilley, late a private in Company G, Forty-second Regiment Indiana Infantry Volunteers, said pension to commence from the date of the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 28, 1885.*

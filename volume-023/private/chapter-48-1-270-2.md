# Chapter 270

> An Act act granting pension to Newton J. Burris.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Newton J. Burris, late a private in Company I, Sixty-eighth Regiment Indiana Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, February 28, 1885.*

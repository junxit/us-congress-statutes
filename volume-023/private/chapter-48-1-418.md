# Chapter 418

> An Act granting a pension to John Boyle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of John Boyle, late a private in the Tenth Battery Indiana State Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws. This act to take effect from and after its passage,


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

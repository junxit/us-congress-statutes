# Chapter 315

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Anna J. Foster

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That the Secre ary of the Interior be, ami he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Mrs. Anna J. Foster, «idow of Major General John G. Foster, on the pension-roll, and pay her a pension of fifty dollars a month from and after the passage of this act, in lieu of the pension now paid her by law.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

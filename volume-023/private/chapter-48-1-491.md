# Chapter 491

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Jonathan C. Harrison.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, at the rate of thirty dollars per month, the name of Jonathan C. Harrison, late a private in Company B, Fifty-second Ohio Volunteers.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

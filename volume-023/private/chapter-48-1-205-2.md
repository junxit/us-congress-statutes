# Chapter 205

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs Mary J, Stotts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Mrs Mary J. Stotts, widow of Green C. Stotts, late captain of Company D, Seventy-sixth Missouri Militia, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

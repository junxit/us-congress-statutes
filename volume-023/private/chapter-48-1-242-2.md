# Chapter 242

> An Act to allow a pension to George F, West.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That George F, West, late a corporal Company I, Fifth Regiment of Wisconsin Infantry Volunteers, now on the pension roll at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month, from and after the passage of this act shall be entitled to receive the same pension as a soldier now receives, or that may hereafter be allowed by law, who has lost one arm at or above the elbow or one leg at or above the knee.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

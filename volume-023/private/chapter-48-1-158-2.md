# Chapter 158

> An Act for the relief of A. B. Montgomery.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all political disabilities imposed upon A. B. Montgomery, a citizen of Georgia, by the fourteenth article of amendments to the Constitution of the United States, be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, February 25th, 1885.*

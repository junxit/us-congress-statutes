# Chapter 105

> An Act granting a pension to John Maloney.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of John Maloney, late a private in Company K, Seventieth Indiana Volunteers subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 14th, 1885.*

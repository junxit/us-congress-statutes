# Chapter 426

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs Lydia S Huggins

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Mrs Lydia S Huggins, mother of Rufus A Huggins, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

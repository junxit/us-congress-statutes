# Chapter 125

> An act to remove the disabilities of Robert D. Thorburn of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That the disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States upon, and incurred by Robert D. Thorburn of Virginia, shall be, and are hereby, removed.


*Approved, June 26, 1884.*

# Chapter 419

> An Act granting a pension to Elizabeth Connor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elizabeth Connor, widow of William Connor, deceased, late a private in Captain Paterson Bain’s company in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

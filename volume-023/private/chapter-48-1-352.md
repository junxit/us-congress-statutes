# Chapter 352

> An Act granting a pension to Captain Israel Stough.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Israel Stough, late captain of Company F, Forty-fourth Regiment Ohio Volunteers, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitation of the pension laws.


*Approved, July 7, 1884.*

# Chapter 109

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs Sarah S, Sampson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized to place on the pension-roll the name of Mrs Sarah 8. Sampson, an Army nurse, widow of Lieutenant-Colonel Charles A. L. Sampson, late of the Maine Infantry Volunteers, at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, February 14th, 1885.*

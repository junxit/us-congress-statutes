# Chapter 281

> An Act granting a pension to Patrick Furlong.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Patrick Furlong, late a private in Company G, Fourteenth Regiment Vermont Volunteers.


*Approved, February 28, 1885.*

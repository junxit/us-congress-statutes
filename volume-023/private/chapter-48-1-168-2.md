# Chapter 168

> An Act for the relief of James Bedell, senior.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior, be and is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of James Bedell, senior, as dependent father of Samuel Umstead subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

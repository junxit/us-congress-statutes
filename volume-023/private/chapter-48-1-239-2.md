# Chapter 239

> An Act granting a pension to Eliza M, Byers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Eliza M, Byers, widow of Doctor William J. Byers, and pay her the pension of the widow of a surgeon of volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

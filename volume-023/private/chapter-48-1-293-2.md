# Chapter 293

> An Act for the relief of Mary Mulholland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to restore to the pension-roll the name of Mary Mulholland, under certificate numbered one hundred and ninety-three thousand four hundred and three.


*Approved, February 28, 1885.*

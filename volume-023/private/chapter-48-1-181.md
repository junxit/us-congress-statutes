# Chapter 181

> An Act granting a pension to Helen M, Harrison.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Helen M, Harrison, widow of Alexander R, Harrison, late a private in Company F, Tenth Regiment Minnesota Volunteers.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

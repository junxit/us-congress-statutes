# Chapter 171

> An act granting a pension to Sarah E. Price.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Sarah E. Price, widow of Nathan Price, late captain of Company F, Tenth Kansas Volunteers.


*Approved, July 3, 1884.*

# Chapter 513

> An Act granting a pension to Thomas M. McChesney.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Thomas M McChesney, of he township of Cranbury, in the county of Middlesex and State of New Jersey, shall be placed upon the list of invalid pensioners, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, as though he had been regularly mustered into the service as an enlisted man in Company M Ninth New Jersey Volunteers.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

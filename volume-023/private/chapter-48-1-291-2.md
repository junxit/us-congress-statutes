# Chapter 291

> An Act granting a pension to Noah Caton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be directed to place on the pension-roll the name Noah Caton, late of Company H, Eighty-fifth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws.


*Approved, February 28, 1885.*

# Chapter 153

> An act granting a pension to Sarah Dayton, widow of Erastus G. Dayton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Sarah Dayton, widow of Erastus G. Dayton, late acting ensign under General J.G. Foster, who received a grapeshot wound during the rebellion and died from the effects thereof; and that she be paid the pension of a widow of an ensign in the Navy.


*Approved, July 3, 1884.*

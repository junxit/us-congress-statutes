# Chapter 522

> An Act granting a pension to Lina J. Stearns.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lina J. Stearns, widow of Oliver F. Stearns, late a private in Company F, Sixteenth Regiment New Hampshire Volunteers.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

# Chapter 501

> An Act granting a pension to John Sparr.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Sparr, of Batavia, New York, late of Company C, Third Regiment New York Cavalry.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

# Chapter 6

> An Act for the relief of Juliet H. Palmer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the passage of this act there be paid, out of the Navy pension fund, to Juliet H. Palmer, widow of the late Surgeon-General James C. Palmer, United States Navy, the sum of fifty dollars per month during her widowhood, the same to be in lieu of her present pension.


*Approved, December twenty fifth, 1884.*

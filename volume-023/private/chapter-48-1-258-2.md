# Chapter 258

> An Act granting an increase of pension to George S. Hawley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That George S. Hawley, late a first lieutenant in the Twenty-third Regiment United States Colored Troops, now on the pension-roll at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month, from and after the passage of this act shall be entitled to receive a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

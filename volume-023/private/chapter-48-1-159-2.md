# Chapter 159

> An Act for the relief of William M. Gardner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all political disabilities imposed upon William M. Gardner, a citizen of the State of Georgia, by the fourteenth article of amendments to the Constitution of the United States, be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, February 25th, 1885.*

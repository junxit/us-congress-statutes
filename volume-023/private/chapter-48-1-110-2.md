# Chapter 110

> An Act granting a pension to Nelson Gammons.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Nelson Gammons, late a private in Company K, Thirty-third Regiment Massachusetts Volunteers,


*Approved, February 14th, 1885.*

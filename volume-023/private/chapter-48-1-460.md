# Chapter 460

> An Act for the relief of Mrs Ida P. Belcher.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Ida P. Belcher, widow of the late Captain Edwin Belcher, of Company E, Seventy-third Pennsylvania Volunteers, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws as applicable to-widows of deceased officers and soldiers,


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

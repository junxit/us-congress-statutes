# Chapter 487

> An Act granting a pension to Octavia A, Newhall

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary ot the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Octavia A. Newhall, widow of Everett Newhall, late a private in the Fifth Regiment of Massachusetts Volunteers, Company F.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

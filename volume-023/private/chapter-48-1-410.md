# Chapter 410

> An Act granting a pension to Sarah M. Bissell,

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah M. Bissell, widow of Commodore Simon B. Bissell, late of the United States Navy, and pay her a pension of fifty dollars per month, from and after the passage of this act,


*Approved, March 3, 1885.*

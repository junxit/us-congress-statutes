# Chapter 524

> An Act granting a pension to David Sears.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of David Sears, late a private in Company D, Eighty-first New York Volunteers, on the pension-roll of the United States, under the rules and regulations of the pension laws.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

# Chapter 184

> An act to increase the pension of Joseph B. Sellers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Joseph B Sellers, who now holds pension-certificate numbered thirty-four thousand two hundred and seventy-five, to thirty-six dollars per month, to take effect from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 4, 1884.*

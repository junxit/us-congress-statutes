# Chapter 176

> An Act granting a pension to James H. Reid.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of James H. Reid, of Dorsey’s regiment of Illinois volunteers in the Black Hawk war, and pay him at the rate of eight dollars per month on and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

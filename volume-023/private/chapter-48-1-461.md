# Chapter 461

> An Act for the relief of Christian Arndt

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Christian Arndt, late of Company H, Ninth Minnesota Infantry, on the pension-roll, subject to the rules and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

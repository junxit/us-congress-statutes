# Chapter 296

> An Act granting a pension to Mary A. Henry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary A Henry, widow of William Henry, late a private in Company K, One hundred and fifth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

# Chapter 391

> An Act to place J Washington Brank on the muster-rolls of Company B, Second North Carolina Mounted Infantry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of J Washington Brank. on the muster-rolls of Company B, Second North Carolina Mounted Infantry, to date from September twenty-fifth eighteen hundred and sixty-three.


*Approved, March 3rd, 1885.*

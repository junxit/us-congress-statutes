# Chapter 486

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Augustus Jones,

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension now allowed to Augustus Jones, a soldier of the war of eighteen hundred and twelve, be increased from eight dollars per month to the sum of twenty dollars per month, to take effect from and after the passage of this act,


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

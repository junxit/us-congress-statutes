# Chapter 421

> An Act granting a pension to Rachel Nickell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name Rachel Nickell, widow of Asbury Nickell, late of Company I, Forty-seventh Regiment Kentucky Mounted Infantry.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

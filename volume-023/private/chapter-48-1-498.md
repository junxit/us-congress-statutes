# Chapter 498

> An Act granting an increase of pension to John O. Gardner,

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of John O. Gardner, formerly of Company A, Ninth Maine Volunteers to twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

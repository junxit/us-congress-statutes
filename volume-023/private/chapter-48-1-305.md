# Chapter 305

> An Act increasing the pension of Charles A. Fuller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension heretofore granted to said Charles A. Fuller be, and the same is hereby, increased to the sum of forty-five dollars per month, and he shall hereafter be paid at that rate, according to the rules- and practice of the Pension Department.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

# Chapter 210

> An Act granting a pension to James Frazier.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of James Frazier, late a private in Company B, Fifteenth Ohio Volunteers.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

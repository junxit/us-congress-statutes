# Chapter 251

> An Act granting a pension to John C. Fenscke.

*Be it enacted by the Senate- and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a pension be, and is hereby, granted to John C. Fenscke, late an employee of the United States Government, on account of wounds received while in the discharge of his duties as such employee; and that the Commissioner of Pensions be, and he is hereby, instructed to place the name of said John C. Fenscke on the pension-roll, with a pension at the rate of a private soldier, according to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

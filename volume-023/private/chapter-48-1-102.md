# Chapter 102

> An Act granting a pension to Louis D. Petty.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Louis D, Petty, late a private in Company E, Eighth Missouri Volunteer Cavalry, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitar tions of the pension Jaws.


*Approved, February 14th, 1885.*

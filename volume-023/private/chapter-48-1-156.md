# Chapter 156

> An act granting a pension to Mary Eliza Dickson, widow of Doctor Thomas Dickson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Eliza Dickson, widow of Doctor Thomas Dickson, and pay her the pension of a widow of an assistant Surgeon.


*Approved, July 3, 1884.*

# Chapter 504

> An Act granting a pension to Alexander Weide

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Alexander Weide, late captain of Company C, Third West Virginia Cavalry, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

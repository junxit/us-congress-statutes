# Chapter 219

> An Act granting a pension to Catharine S. Edmondson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Catharine S. Edmondson, dependent mother of William 0. Edmondson, deceased, late a private in Company I of the Eighty-second Regiment Pennsylvania Infantry Volunteers, and pay her a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

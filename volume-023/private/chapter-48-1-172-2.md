# Chapter 172

> An Act granting a pension to William Robinson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William Robinson, late a scout in the United States Army.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

# Chapter 95

> An act for the relief of Isaac Reed.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions, regulations, and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Isaac Reed, late a private in Company F, Twenty-sixth Indiana Volunteer Infantry, and now a resident of the town of Cannelton and county of Perry, in the State of Indiana.


*Approved, June 12, 1884.*

# Chapter 497

> An Act granting a pension to William H. Kinman,

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the restrictions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William H. Kinman, formerly of Company F, Thirty-fourth Ohio Volunteers,


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

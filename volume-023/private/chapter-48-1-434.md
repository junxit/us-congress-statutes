# Chapter 434

> An Act for the relief of Harrison Mitchell, late of Company K, Forty-eight Indiana Volunteers,

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is. authorized and directed to place the name of Harrison Mitchell, late of Company K, Forty-eighth Indiana Volunteers, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws,


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

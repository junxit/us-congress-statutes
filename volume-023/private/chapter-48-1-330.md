# Chapter 330

> An Act to increase the pension of Richard Jobes

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Richard Jobes late of Company D. Sixteenth Regiment of Connecticut Volunteers, and to pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

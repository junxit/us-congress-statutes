# Chapter 89

> An Act granting a pension to John T. Marshall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John T. Marshall, late a seaman in the United States Navy.


*Approved, February 14th, 1885.*

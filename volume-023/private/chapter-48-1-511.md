# Chapter 511

> An Act granting a pension to Rosanna Riley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place the name of Rosanna Riley on the pension-roll and pay her a pension of eighteen dollars a month.

## § 2.

That this act shall take effect upon its passage.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

# Chapter 119

> An Act granting a pension to Henry Davis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Henry Davis, late lieutenant colonel of the Eighty-second Regiment Indiana Volunteer Infantry, on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations and conditions of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 14th, 1885.*

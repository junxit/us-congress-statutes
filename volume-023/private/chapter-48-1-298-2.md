# Chapter 298

> An Act for the relief of Morris Geld.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Morris Geld, late of the general mounted service, United States Army be, and he hereby is, placed on the pension-roll of the United States, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 28, 1885.*

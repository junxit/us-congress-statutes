# Chapter 76

> An act to increase the pension of John Algoe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of John Algoe, formerly captain of Company A, Tenth Regiment Michigan Infantry State Volunteers, to forty-five dollars per month from the date of this act.


*Approved, June 11, 1884.*

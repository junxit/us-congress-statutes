# Chapter 173

> An Act granting pensions to Frederick Nelson, T Caine, and Henry C. Sanders.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the names of Frederick Nelson, T Caine, and Henry C, Sanders, of Wyoming Territory, late employees of the Quartermaster’s Department of the United States Army, who were severely wounded and disabled for life, while connected with Major Thornburg’s expedition, in the engagement with the Ute Indians, September twenty-ninth, eighteen hundred and seventy-nine.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

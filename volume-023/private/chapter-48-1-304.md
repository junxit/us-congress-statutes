# Chapter 304

> An Act granting a pension to Solon L. Simonds.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Solon L. Simonds, of Littleton, New Hampshire, lately a private in Company A, Eighth Regiment of Vermont Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

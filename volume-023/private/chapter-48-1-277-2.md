# Chapter 277

> An Act granting a pension to Anna Ginn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Anna Ginn, widow of Benjamin Ginn, late a private in Company F, Fiftieth Regiment Enrolled Missouri Militia.


*Approved, February 28, 1885.*

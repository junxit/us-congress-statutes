# Chapter 15

> An Act granting a pension to Henry Alden.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Henry Alden, late a private iu Company E, Eighteenth Massachusetts Volunteers.


*Approved, January tenth, 1885.*

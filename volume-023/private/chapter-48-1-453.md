# Chapter 453

> An Act for the relief of John F. Chase.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of John F. Chase, formerly a member of Company B, Eighteenth Regiment New York State Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

# Chapter 390

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Sophia A Morgan, widow of the kite Charles H. Morgan, a brevet brigadier-general in the United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs Sophia A. Morgan, widow of Brevet Brigadier-General and Brigadier-General of Volunteers Charles H. Morgan, at the rate of fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1885.*

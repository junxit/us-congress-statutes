# Chapter 254

> An Act granting a pension to Mark Spencer Van Loan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mark Spencer Van Loan, of Catskill New York, late a private in Company K, Eightieth New York Volunteers,


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

# Chapter 270

> An Act granting a pension to Eliza A. Shealey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Eliza A. Shealey, widow of Daniel Shealey, late second lieutenant of Company E, Second Maryland Regiment of Cavalry, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws, and to pay lier a pension during her widowhood from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

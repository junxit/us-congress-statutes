# Chapter 248

> An Act granting a pension to Louisa A. Estes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Louisa A. Estes, widow of John Estes, late of Company E, Seventy-third Regiment of Indiana Volunteers, subject to the conditions and regulations of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

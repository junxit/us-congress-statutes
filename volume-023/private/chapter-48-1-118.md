# Chapter 118

> An Act granting a pension to Maria H. Sargent.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Maria H. Sargent, and to pay her a pension of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 14th, 1885.*

# Chapter 317

> An Act to pension Julia A. Marcum.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Julia A. Marcum, subject to the rules and regulations of the Department.


*Approved, March 2, 1885.*

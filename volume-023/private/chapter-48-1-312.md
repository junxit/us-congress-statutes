# Chapter 312

> An Act for the relief of Catherine H. Glick.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Catherine H. Glick, widow of Doctor Elias B. Glick, late a Surgeon in the Fortieth Regiment Indiana Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

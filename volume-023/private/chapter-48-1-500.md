# Chapter 500

> An Act granting a pension to Judson Bostwick.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Judson Bostwick, of Arcade, New York, late of Company F, Fifth Regiment New York, Cavalry Volunteers,


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

# Chapter 204

> An Act granting a pension to Charles H, Phillips.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Charles H, Phillips, late a teamster in the First Division, Fifteenth Army Corps, and pay him a pension of fifteen dollars per month in lieu of the present pension.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

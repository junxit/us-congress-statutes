# Chapter 298

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs Sarah E. E. Seelye, alias Franklin Thompson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Sarah E E. Seelye, alias Franklin Thompson, who was late a private in Company F, Second Regiment of Michigan Infantry Volunteers, at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

# Chapter 429

> An Act granting a pension to Annie E, Bailey,

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Annie E, Bailey, widow of First Lieutenant William 0, Bailey, of Company B, Fourth Regiment West Virginia Infantry,


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

# Chapter 232

> An Act increasing the pension of Elmina P. Spencer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension of Mrs Elmina P. Spencer, be, and the same is hereby, increased to twenty dollars per month; and the Commissioner of Pensions is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of said Elmina P. Spencer, on the pension-roll as a pensioner of the United States for the sum of twenty dollars per month, said twenty dollars per month being in lieu of all other pensions heretofore granted.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

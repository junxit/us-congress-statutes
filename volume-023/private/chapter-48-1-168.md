# Chapter 168

> An act granting a pension to Emma A. Porch.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Emma A, Porch, of Cole County, Missouri and that she be paid at the rate of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, July 3, 1884.*

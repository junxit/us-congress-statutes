# Chapter 221

> An Act granting a pension to Benjamin P, Lowell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized to place on the pension-roll, subject to the pension laws, the name of Benjamin P. Lowell, late of Company I, First Regiment Maine Cavalry Volunteers.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

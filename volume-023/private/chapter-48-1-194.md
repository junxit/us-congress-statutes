# Chapter 194

> An act granting a pension to Michael Mack

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Michael Mack, late a private in Company A, Twenty-seventh Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry; also late a private in Company G, First Battalion, Eighteenth Regiment United States Infantry.


*Approved, July 4, 1884.*

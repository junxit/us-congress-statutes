# Chapter 180

> An Act granting a pension to Elizabeth Fowler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Elizabeth Fowler, the widow of Philo Fowler, late of Company A. Second Battalion, Seventeenth United States Infantry Volunteers of the State of Maine.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

# Chapter 493

> An Act granting a pension to Edward Wilcox.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension.roll the name of Edward Wilcox, an imbecile son of Leonard Wilcox, late of Company A, Twenty-first Regiment Connecticut Volunteer Infantry, and pay his legally appointed conservator, for bis use and benefit, a pensiou of eighteen dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

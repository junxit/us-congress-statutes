# Chapter 366

> An Act granting a pension to the widow and children of the late Byram Pitney.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the names of the widow and children of Byram Pitney, late of Company K, Twenty sixth Regiment New Jersey Volunteers.


*Approved, March 3, 1885.*

# Chapter 268

> An Act granting a pension to Hugh O’Neil

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, instructed to place the name of Hugh O’Neil on the pension-roll, said Hugh O’Neil being the dependent father of John O’Neil, late a private in Company I, Mounted Riflemen of the Indian wars.


*Approved, February 28, 1885.*

# Chapter 311

> An Act granting a pension to Julia M. Reynolds.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Sta tes of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to piece on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws, the name of Julia M, Reynolds, widow of James S. Reynolds, deceased, late second lieutenant of Company H, Tenth Regiment New York Cavalry.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

# Chapter 381

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Polly Young.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Polly Young, widow of Jesse Young, late a soldier in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve, and pay her a pension, from and after the passage of this act, at the rate of thirty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 3, 1885.*

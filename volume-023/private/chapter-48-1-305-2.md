# Chapter 305

> An Act granting a pension to Leonard King

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to place the name of Leonard King, of Farmington, Van-Buren County, Iowa, upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, as dependent father of Miles King, late of Company B, Third Iowa Cavalry Volunteers in the late war, to take effect from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 28, 1885.*

# Chapter 69

> An Act to increase the pension of Simpson Harris.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Simpson Harris, a soldier of the war of eighteen hundred and twelve, and who now holds pension-certificate numbered thirty-seven hundred and six, to the sum of fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 14th, 1885.*

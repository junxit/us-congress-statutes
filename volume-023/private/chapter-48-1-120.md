# Chapter 120

> An act granting a pension to A. M. Wilson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of A. M. Wilson, father of James Wilson, deceased, late a private in the Fourteenth Regiment of Kansas Volunteer Cavalry.


*Approved, June 24, 1884.*

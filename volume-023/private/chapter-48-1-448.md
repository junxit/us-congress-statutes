# Chapter 448

> An Act for the relief of Charles Milk.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to place on the pension-roll the name of Charles Milk, late a member of Company B, One hundred and fifty-second Regiment of Indiana Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

# Chapter 365

> An Act granting a pension to James Bond.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll of the United States the name of James Bond, who was a private in Company B, Fifty-second Regiment of Ohio Volunteer Infantry in the late war of the rebellion; the pension under this act to date from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1885.*

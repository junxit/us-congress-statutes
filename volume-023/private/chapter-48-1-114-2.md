# Chapter 114

> An Act granting a pension to Margaret A. Maguire

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Margaret A. Maguire, of Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, widow of George R. Maguire, deceased, late a lieutenant in the Thirteenth Pennsylvania Cavalry, United States Volunteers.


*Approved, February 14th, 1885.*

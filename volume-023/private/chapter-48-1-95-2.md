# Chapter 95

> An Act or the relief of Major W. W. Frybarger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to put Major W. W. Frybarger, of Connersville, Indiana, on the pension-roll at the rate of forty dollars per month ; said pension to begin at the date of the approval of this act, and to be in lieu of all other pensions granted the said Frybarger.


*Approved, February 14th, 1885.*

# Chapter 512

> An Act granting a pension to Margaret Flaherty,

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, at the rate of eight dollars per month the name of Margaret Flaherty, dependent stepmother of Bartlett Flaherty, late a private in Company F, Third Maine Volunteers,


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

# Chapter 238

> An Act granting a pension to Chloe A. Whipple,

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Chloe A, Whipple, of Batavia, New York, mother of Charles A, Whipple, late of Company L, Eighth New York Heavy Artillery,


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

# Chapter 209

> An Act granting a pension to Isaac R, H. Caldwell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed and authorized to put the name of Isaac R. H. Caldwell, late captain of Company G, Thirteenth Kentucky Volunteers, on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

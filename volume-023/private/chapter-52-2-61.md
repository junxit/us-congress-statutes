# Chapter 61

> An Act granting a pension to Catharine Schools.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Catharine Schools, widow of Thomas Schools, deceased, late a private in Company C, First Regiment of Delaware Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, February 13th, 1885.*

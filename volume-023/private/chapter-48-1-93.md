# Chapter 93

> An act granting a pension to Robert M. Flack

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Robert M. Flack, late captain of Company F, Eighty-fourth Pennsylvania Volunteers.


*Approved, June 12, 1884.*

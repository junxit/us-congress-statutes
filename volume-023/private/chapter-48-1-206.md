# Chapter 206

> AN ACT granting a pension to Temple Golden.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Temple Golden, late a private in Company K, Seventy-sixth Regiment Missouri State Militia, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, July 4, 1884.*

# Chapter 271

> An Act granting a pension to Almira B. Kaler,

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to restore to the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Almira B. Kaier, mother of John Dexter Kaier, deceased, late a private in Company B, Eighth Regiment Maine Volunteers,


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

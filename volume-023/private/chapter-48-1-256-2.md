# Chapter 256

> An Act granting an increase of pension to George W. Clark.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of George W. Clark, late a private in Company E, Twelfth Regiment New Hampshire Volunteers, to fifty dollars per month, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

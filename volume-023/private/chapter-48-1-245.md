# Chapter 245

> An Act granting a pension to H. E. Van Trees

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of H. E. Van Trees, late a first lieutenant in the Kansas Volunteer Militia.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

# Chapter 252

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Edward P. Quinn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Edward P. Quinn, of the city of Albany, late a lieutenant of Company D, One hundred and twenty-third New York Volunteers, to the sum of forty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

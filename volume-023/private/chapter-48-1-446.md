# Chapter 446

> An Act for the relief of Elizabeth W. Creighton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Elizabeth W. Creighton, widow of J. Blakely Creighton, late a rear-admiral, on the pension-roll, and to allow her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

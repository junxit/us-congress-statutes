# Chapter 244

> An Act to grant a pension to Harriet M. Baily.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Harriet M. Baily, widow of William P. Baily, late a Colonel in the service of the United States, of the Second Regiment of Delaware Volunteers.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

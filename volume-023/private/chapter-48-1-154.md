# Chapter 154

> An act granting a pension to Solomon K. Ruggles.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Solomon K. Ruggles, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, July 3, 1884.*

# Chapter 275

> An Act for increase of pension of Martha T. Stribling, widow of Cornelius K. Stribling, late a rear-admiral in the United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Represen tatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to cause the pen- sion of Martha T. Stribling, widow of Cornelius K. Stribling late a rear- admiral in the United States Navy, now on the pension-roll of the United States, to be increased, and to pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

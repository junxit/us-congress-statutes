# Chapter 336

> An Act granting a pension to Elizabeth J. Colbert.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elizabeth J. Colbert, widow of Joseph C. Colbert, late a private in Company K, one hundred and twenty-third Illinois Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, July 7, 1884.*

# Chapter 367

> An Act granting a pension to Anne T. Dicks.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Anne T. Dicks, widow of John IT. Dicks, late an acting master in the United States Navy


*Approved, March 3, 1885.*

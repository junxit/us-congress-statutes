# Chapter 302

> An Act for the relief of John H. Johnson

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John H. Johnson, late a private in Company E, Fourteenth Kentucky Volunteers.


*Approved, February 28, 1885.*

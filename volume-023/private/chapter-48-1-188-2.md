# Chapter 188

> An Act to restore the name of Warren Sams to the pension roll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to restore the name of Warren Sams, late a private in Company K, First Regiment North Carolina Volunteers in the war with Mexico, to the pension-roll, to take effect from the time the soldier was dropped from the rolls, on the sixteenth of May, eighteen hundred and seventy-seven,


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

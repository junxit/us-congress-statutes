# Chapter 88

> An act granting a pension to Caroline Lauffer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Caroline Lauffer, widow of Jacob Lauffer, late of Company 0, Sixth Connecticut Volunteers.


*Approved, June 12, 1884.*

# Chapter 96

> An act for the relief of Charlotte Day.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Charlotte Day, mother of Charles E. Day, formerly of Company F, First New Jersey Militia (Pennsylvania Emergency).


*Approved, June 12, 1884.*

# Chapter 508

> An Act restoring John Snyder to the pension roll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to restore to the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Snyder, formerly of Company T, One hundred and twenty-ninth Regiment of Indiana Infantry.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

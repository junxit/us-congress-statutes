# Chapter 521

> An Act granting a pension co Catharine Helton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Catharine Helton, dependent mother of Calvin Helton, late of Company I, Twenty-fourth Kentucky Volunteers.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

# Chapter 280

> An Act for the relief of Sarah Becktel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Sarah A. Bechtel, widow of Isaac Bechtel, late of Company B. Twenty-sixth Ohio Regiment, and Company E. Third Ohio Cavalry, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

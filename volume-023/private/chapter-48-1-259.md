# Chapter 259

> An Act granting a pension to William T. McCoy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, That tha,*

Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William T. McCoy, late of Company B, One hundred and Tenth Regiment of Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

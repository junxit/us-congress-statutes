# Chapter 33

> An act granting a pension to Caroline M. McDougal, widow of Rear-Admiral David S. McDougal.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

. That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Caroline M. McDougal, widow of Rear-Admiral David S. McDougal, late of the United States Navy, and pay her a pension of fifty dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, April 28, 1884.*

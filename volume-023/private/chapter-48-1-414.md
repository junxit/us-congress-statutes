# Chapter 414

> An Act granting a pension to the widow of the late Commander S. Dana Greene, United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll at the rate of fifty dollars per month the name of Mary A. Greene, widow of the late Commander S. Dana Greene, United States Navy.


*Approved, March 3, 1885.*

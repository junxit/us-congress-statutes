# Chapter 313

> An Act for the relief of Asa Dye,

*Be it enacted by the Senate and. House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Asa Dye, formerly of Company I, Tenth Regiment New York Volunteers, and pay him a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

# Chapter 97

> An act granting a pension to Julia A. Ross.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Julia A. Ross, widow of Michael M. Ross, late a private in Company I, First United States Artillery, and who died in said service August tenth, eighteen hundred and fifty-two.


*Approved, June 12, 1884.*

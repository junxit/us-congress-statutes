# Chapter 84

> An Act for the relief John Johnson

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the act of March third, eighteen hundred and seventy-nine, entitled “An act for the relief of certain pensioners” (who have lost a leg at the hip-joint), be so construed as to include John Johnson, who had his right arm and a part of the shoulder-blade shot away at the battle of Fredericksburg, Virginia, December thirteenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-two.


*Approved, February 14th, 1885.*

# Chapter 362

> An Act granting a pension to James King

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of James King, late a private in Company B, Forty eighth Regiment Kentucky Volunteers.


*Approved, July 7, 1884.*

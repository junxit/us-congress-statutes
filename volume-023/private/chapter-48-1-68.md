# Chapter 68

> An act to increase the pension of Francis Scott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Francis Scott, late a private in Company B, Twenty-third Regiment of Wisconsin Infantry Volunteers, now’ on the pension-roll at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month, shall, from and after the passage of this act, be entitled to receive the same pension as a soldier now receives or which shall be allowed hereafter by law to one who has lost one arm at or above the elbow or one leg at or above the knee.


*Approved, June 3, 1884.*

# Chapter 85

> An Act restoring Rebecca Walcott to the pension-roll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to restore to the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Rebecca Walcott, mother of James A. Walcott, late a corporal of Company E, Second Vermont Volunteers.


*Approved, February 14th, 1885.*

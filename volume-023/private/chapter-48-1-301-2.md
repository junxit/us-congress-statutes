# Chapter 301

> An Act granting a pension to Margaret A. Berry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Margaret A. Berry, widow of William M. Berry, late private in Company I, Tenth Indiana Cavalry.


*Approved, February 28, 1885.*

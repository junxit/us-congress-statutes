# Chapter 310

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Captain H. D. F. Young.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to increase the pension heretofore granted Harrison D. F. Young, late captain of Company F, Second Regiment of New Hampshire Volunteers, from ten dollars per month to twenty dollars per month, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 5, 1884.*

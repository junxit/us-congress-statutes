# Chapter 173

> An act for the relief of Mrs. Rebecca J. Pierce.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Mrs. Rebecca J. Pierce, widow of David A. Pierce, late of Company I, Twenty-fourth Missouri Volunteer Infantry, be placed on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, July 3, 1884.*

# Chapter 137

> An act granting a pension to Miles B. Farlin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Miles B. Farlin, late a private in Company C, Thirty-first Regiment Massachusetts Volunteers in the war of the rebellion, and pay him a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 1, 1884.*

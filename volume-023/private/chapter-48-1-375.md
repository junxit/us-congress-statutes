# Chapter 375

> An Act for the relief of Robert J. Ballort

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Robert J. Ballort, late a private in Company F, Eighth Regiment Michigan Cavalry, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws.


*Approved, March 3, 1885.*

# Chapter 240

> An Act to restore the name of Lewis J, Blair to the pension-roll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to reinstate on the pension-roll, the name of Lewis J. Blair, late Lieutenant-colonel of the Eighty-eighth Regiment Indiana Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 26, 1885.*

# Chapter 107

> An Act to restore the name of Walter H. Crow, to the pension-roll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and is hereby, authorized and directed to restore the name of Walter H. Crow, late a sergeant of Company K, Thirty-third Indiana Volunteers, to the pension roll, and pay him a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 14th, 1885.*

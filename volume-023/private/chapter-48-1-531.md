# Chapter 531

> An Act to grant a pension to James Dye,

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of James Dye, late an assistant surgeon in the service of the United States, of the Twenty-first Regiment of Missouri Volunteers,


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

# Chapter 312

> An Act to grant a pension to Joseph R. Dodds.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to place on the pension-roll the name of Joseph R. Dodds, late a private in Company K, Seventh Regiment of Iowa Cavalry Volunteers, subject to the conditions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 28, 1885.*

# Chapter 528

> An Act to remove the political disabilities of W. H. Murdaugh of Virginia

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two thirds of each House concurring therein)*

That all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States be and the same are hereby, removed from W. H. Murdaugh of Virginia.


*Approved, March 3d, 1885.*

# Chapter CLXXX

> An Act for the Benefit of William L. Oliver.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be and he is hereby directed to place the name of William L. Oliver, of Davis county, in the State of Iowa, upon the roll of invalid pensioners, and pay him from the first day of January, eighteen hundred and fifty-six, the sum of ten dollars per month during his natural life, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 3, 1857.*

## Marginal notes

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

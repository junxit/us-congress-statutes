# Chapter CLXXVII

> An Act for the Relief of William Walton, a Soldier of the War of eighteen hundred and twelve.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be and he is hereby authorized and required to place the name of William Walton, of the State of Indiana, on the invalid pension roll, and that he be entitled to receive a pension at the rate of four dollars per month as an invalid, from the third day of December, eighteen hundred and fifty-five, during his life.


*Approved, March 3, 1857.*

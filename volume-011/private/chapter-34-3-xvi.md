# Chapter XVI

> An Act for the Relief of Amos B. Carwine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That That the Secretary of the Treasury pay, from any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Amos B. Corwine, the sum of twenty-seven thousand eight hundred and four dollars and thirty-three cents, in full compensation for moneys expended by him in forwarding destitute citizens of the United States from Panama to San Francisco.


*Approved, January 17, 1857.*

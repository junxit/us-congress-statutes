# Chapter CL

> An Act to confirm to Charles Waterman his Title to certain Lots in Milwaukie, Wisconsin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the title of Charles Waterman to lots numbered one and two, in block numbered ninety-eight, in the third ward of the city of Milwaukie, in the State of Wisconsin, extending to Lake Michigan, formerly occupied by the United States light-house keeper, be and the same is hereby confirmed, and that a patent shall issue therefor as in other cases: Provided, That this confirmation shall only operate as a relinquishment of title on the part of the United States, and shall in no way affect the rights of third parties.


*Approved, March 3, 1857.*

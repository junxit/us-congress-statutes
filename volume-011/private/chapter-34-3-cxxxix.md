# Chapter CXXXIX

> An Act for the Relief of William Burdell, Samuel Medary, and William T. Martin, Administrator of the Estate of Edgar Gale, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That William Burdell and Samuel Medary, and the estate of Edgar Gale, deceased, who was co-surety with said Burdell and Medary of John T. Arthur, in his official bond executed eighth of December, eighteen hundred and forty-seven, as assistant quartermaster of the army of the United States, be relettsed from their liabilities on said bond, and from any suits brought or judgment obtained thereon against said Burdell, Medary, and William T. Martin, as administrator of the estate of said Gale, all or any of them.


*Approved, March 3, 1857.*

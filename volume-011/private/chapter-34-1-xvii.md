# Chapter XVII

> An Act for the Relief of Mary E. Tillman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War cause to be paid to Mary E. Tillman, of South Carolina, a pension of twenty dollars per month, during her natural life, commencing on the fourth day of March, one thousand eight hundred and fifty-seven.


*Approved, April 23, 1856.*

# Chapter XXXIII

> An Act for the Relief of Ransdell Pegg.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Bansdell Pegg the sum of one hundred and eight dollars and fifty cents out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, being the amount of difference of pay received by him as a watchman at the east wing of the Patent-Office and that received by the other watchman of said building from the twenty-third July eighteen hundred and forty-nine to first October eighteen hundred and fifty.


*Approved, February 5, 1857.*

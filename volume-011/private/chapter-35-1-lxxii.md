# Chapter LXXII

> An Act for the Relief of Stephen R. Rowan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the attorney of the United States of America for the southern district of Illinois be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to enter satisfaction of a judgment rendered by the district court of the United States tor the said southern district of Illinois, at its June term, anno Domini eighteen hundred and fifty-six, in favor of the United States of America against Stephen R. Rowan, on his paying all the costs in said ease.


*Approved, June 1, 1858.*

# Chapter CLXXXIX

> An Act granting a Pension to Franklin W. Armstrong, of Hardin County, Kentucky.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be and he is hereby authorized and directed to place the name of Franklin W. Armstrong, of Hardin county, Kentucky, upon the invalid pension roll, at sixteen dollars per month, beginning on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and fifty-six, and to continue during the existence of his present disability.


*Approved, March 3, 1857.*

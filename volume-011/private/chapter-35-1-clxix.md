# Chapter CLXIX

> An Act granting an invalid Pension to William Randolph.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of William Randolph on the invalid pension roll, at the rate of four dollars per month, and pay him at that rate from the twelfth day of May, one thousand eight hundred and fifty-eight, during his natural life.


*Approved, June 14, 1858.*

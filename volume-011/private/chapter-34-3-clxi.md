# Chapter CLXI

> An Act for the Relief of Mary Hooker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and required to place the name of Mary Hooker, widow of James Hooker, who was military or hospital storekeeper in the State of Connecticut, during the war of the Revolution, from February, seventeen hundred and seventy-seven, to November, seventeen hundred and and eighty, on the pension roll of said State, under act of seventh July, eighteen hundred and thirty-eight, and subsequent acts, at the rate of pension due for said service; and the amount so found due lobe paid to the children of said Mary Hooker, deceased.


*Approved, March 3, 1857.*

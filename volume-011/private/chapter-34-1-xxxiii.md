# Chapter XXXIII

> An Act for the Relief of John Crawford.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That John Crawford be, and he is hereby, authorized to locate certificate number three thousand one hundred and fifty, for eight hundred and ninety dollars and ninety cents, forfeited land-stock in the State of Mississippi, on any public lands in the United States subject to private entry, at not exceeding one dollar and twenty-five cents per acre.


*Approved, May 29, 1856.*

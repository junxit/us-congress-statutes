# Chapter XLI

> An Act for the Relief of the Legal Representatives of Edmund H. McCabe, Assignee of Antoine Soulard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That the legal representatives of Edmund H. McCabe, assignee of Antoine Soulard, be and they are hereby authorized to enter, of the public lands of the United States subject to entry, at not exceeding one dollar and twenty-five cents per acre, such quantity of land as has been sold by the United States within the boundaries of the claim of Antoine Soulard, confirmed by the Supreme Court, of the United States, at January term, eighteen hundred and thirty-six, from the time of filing the petition on which said confirmation was made to the issue of the patent under such confirmation, and that a patent or patents shall issue therefor.


*Approved, February 7, 1857.*

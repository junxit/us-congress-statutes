# Chapter LIII

> An Act to increase the Pension of John Richmond.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the two third pension heretofore allowed to John Richmond, of the State of Massachusetts, a private in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve, be, and the same is hereby, raised to a full pension.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted by the authority aforesaid, That the benefits accruing to the said John Richmond under and by virtue of this act shall commence January one, eighteen hundred and fifty-five, and continue for and during his natural life.


*Approved, May 24, 1858.*

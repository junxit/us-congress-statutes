# Chapter XXXII

> An Act for the Relief of Captain Langdon C. Euston, Assistant Quartermaster United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the War Department be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to credit Captain Langdon C. Easton, assistant quartermaster United States army, on settlement of his accounts, with the sum of seventeen thousand six hundred and fifty dollars, being the amount of which he was defrauded by bis late clerk, Gustavus McIIarcourt, while stationed at Santa Fe, New Mexico.


*Approved, May 29, 1856.*

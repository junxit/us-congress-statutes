# Chapter CLXVIII

> An Act for the Relief of Richard J. Murray, a Soldier in the Seminole War of eighteen hundred and eighteen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of Richard J. Murray be placed on the roll of invalid pensions, at the rate of eight dollars per month, to commence on the first day of December, eighteen hundred and fifty-five, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, March 3, 1857.*

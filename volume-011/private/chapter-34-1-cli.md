# Chapter CLI

> An Act for the Relief of Josiah S. Little.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid to Josiah S. Little, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one thousand dollars, in full remuneration for a piece of land to which he lost title by the operation of the fourth article of the Treaty to settle and define the boundries between the Territories of the United States and the possessions of her British Majesty,” &c., of the ninth of August, eighteen hundred and forty-two.


*Approved, August 18, 1856.*

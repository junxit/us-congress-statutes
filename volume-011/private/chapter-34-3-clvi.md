# Chapter CLVI

> An Act providing an Increase of Pension to Isaac Phillips.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior cause the pension of Isaac Phillips, of Pennsylvania, to be changed on the pension roll from twenty dollars a year to eight dollars per month, commencing from the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-one, and to continue during his natural life, deducting former payments.


*Approved, March 3, 1857.*

# Chapter XXVII

> An Act for the Relief of Charlotte Turner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Mrs. Charlotte Turner be, and is hereby, authorized to enter, at the minimum price of one dollar and twenty-five cents per acre, the east half of the southeast quarter of section twenty-one, and the west half of the southwest quarter of section twenty-two, in township three, of range eight east, in the southeastern district, Louisiana, in virtue of her long settlement and valuable improvements thereon.


*Approved, January 28, 1857.*

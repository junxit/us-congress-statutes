# Chapter XCII

> An Act for the Relief of Mary B. Dusenbery.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name, of Mary B. Dusenbery be placed on the pension rolls at the sum of thirty dollars per month, to commence on the third day of December, eighteen hundred and fifty-five, and continue for the term of five years.


*Approved, March 3, 1859.*

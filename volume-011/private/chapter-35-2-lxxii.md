# Chapter LXXII

> An Act for the Relief of Anthony Caslo, a Soldier in the War of eighteen hundred and twelve.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to allow and pay to Anthony Caslo, otherwise known as Anthony Castle, an invalid pensioner, an amount equal to two dollars and sixty-six cents per month, from the twenty-fifth day of May, one thousand eight hundred and sixteen, to the twenty-fifth day of May, one thousand eight hundred and fifty-one, the date at which his name was entered on the roll of invalid pensioners.


*Approved, March 2, 1859.*

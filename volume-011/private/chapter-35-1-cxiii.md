# Chapter CXIII

> An Act for the Relief of Shove Chase, of New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Shove Chase, of New York, upon the invalid pension list, at the rate of eight dollars per month, commencing on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and fifty-six, to continue during his natural fife.


*Approved, June 7, 1858.*

## Marginal notes

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

# Chapter CXXXIV

> An Act for the Relief of Wyatt Griffith.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Wyatt Griffith, of Tennessee, on the invalid pension roll, at the rate of eight dollars per month, from the twentieth day of June, anno Domini eighteen hundred and fifty-four, and pay him at that rate during the term of his natural life.


*Approved, June 9, 1858.*

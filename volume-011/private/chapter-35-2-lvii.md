# Chapter LVII

> An Act for the Relief of John F. Cannon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster-General be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to pay to John F. Cannon, at the rate of one hundred and twenty dollars per annum, for and during the time he carried said mail, according to his contract, in addition to the amount already paid to him, for additional expense incurred and extra service performed by him on mail route number two thousand six hundred and twenty-seven.


*Approved, February 23, 1859.*

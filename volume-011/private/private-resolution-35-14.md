# Private Resolution 14

> A Resolution for the Relief of John Grayson.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, directed, in adjusting the account of John Grayson, pension agent at Pittsburg, Pennsylvania, to place to the credit of the said John Grayson the amount of five hundred and twenty-six dollars and thirteen cents, paid by him to George De Camp, one of the surviving children and heirs of Susannah Stokely, deceased, widow of Nehemiah Stokely, a captain in the revolutionary war; the same having been paid in conformity with the directions of the Secretary of the Interior, as conveyed upon the face of a certificate of pension issued by the Commissioner of Pensions to said George De Camp.


*Approved, June 1, 1858.*

# Chapter LXII

> An Act explanatory of an Act entitled “An Act for the Relief of Dempsey Pittman,” approved August sixteenth, eighteen hundred and fifty-six.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the act approved August sixteenth, eighteen hundred and fifty-six, entitled “An act tor the relief of Dempsey Pittman,” be so construed as to authorize and direct the Secretary of War to pay to the said Dempsey Pittman the compensation and allowances of a colonel of infantry, for the period of five months, in full consideration for his services in Florida in eighteen hundred and thirty-eight.


*Approved, June 1, 1858.*

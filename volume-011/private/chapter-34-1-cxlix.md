# Chapter CXLIX

> An Act for the Relief of Nancy Bowen and Sarah Larrabee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Nancy Bowen, formerly the widow of Robert Brice, upon the roll of pensioners, and pay to her the sum of ninety-six dollars per annum, from the fourth day of July, eighteen hundred and fifty, during her natural life; also, that he be, and is hereby, directed to place the name of Sarah Larrabee, formerly the widow of Barstow Newell, upon the pension rolls and pay to her the sum of ninety-six dollars per annum, commencing on the first day of March, eighteen hundred and fifty-four, and continuing during her natural life.


*Approved, August 18, 1856.*

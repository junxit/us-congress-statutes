# Chapter VII

> An Act to admit to Register or Enrolment the Schooner Zadock Pratt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be and hereby is authorized and directed to issue a register or enrolment to the schooner “ Zadock Pratt,” now owned by William Peck and James B. Smith, citizens of Buffalo, in the State of New York: Provided, it shall be proved, to the satisfaction of said secretary, that two thirds of the cost of the construction of said vessel was put upon her in the United Stales.


*Approved, March 13, 1856.*

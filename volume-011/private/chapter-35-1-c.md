# Chapter C

> An Act for the Relief of Benjamin Wakefield.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Benjamin Wakefield, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the difference of pay between that of master’s mate and boat swain, from the first day of January, eighteen hundred and forty-eight, to the nineteenth day of January, eighteen hundred and fifty.


*Approved, June 5, 1858.*

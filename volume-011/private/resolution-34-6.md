# Resolution 6

> A Resolution for the Relief of the Southwestern and Muscogee Railroad Companies.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster-General be and he is hereby authorized to correct the error in the bid of R. R. Cuyler, for the President of the Southwestern and Muskogee Railroad Companies, on mail route six thousand three hundred and three; and that he be required to allow them fifteen thousand three hundred dollars per annum for the double daily service, from the commencement of their service to the expiration of the contract, or so long as the New York and New Orleans mail may continue to be transported over said route.


*Approved, May 9, 1856.*

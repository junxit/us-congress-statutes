# Chapter XI

> An Act for the Relief of Doctor Charles D. Maxwell, a Surgeon in the United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the accounting officers of the treasury be, and are hereby, required to allow and pay to Doctor Charles D. Maxwell, surgeon in the navy of the United States, the difference of pay between that of a passed assistant surgeon and a surgeon, from the twenty-second day of December, eighteen hundred and forty-five, to the seventh day of July, eighteen hundred and forty-eight, being the period during which he performed the duties of a surgeon and assistant surgeon on board the United States Ship Cyane, and that the same be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 20, 1858.*

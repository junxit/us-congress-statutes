# Chapter XIII

> An Act for the Relief of Edmund Mitchell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of Edmund Mitchell, of Carrol county, in the State of Kentucky, be placed upon the pension roll, at the rate of eight dollars per month, to commence on the first day of March, eighteen hundred and fifty-four.


*Approved, August 23, 1856.*

## Marginal notes

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

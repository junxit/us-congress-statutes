# Chapter LII

> An Act to amend an Act entitled “An Act granting a Pension to Ansel Wilkinson,” approved August thirteenth, eighteen hundred and fifty-six.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the “act granting a pension to Ansel Wilkinson,” approved August thirteenth, eighteen hundred and fifty-six, be so amended that the word “Ansel” shall read Asel wherever the same occurs in the said act.


*Approved, May 24, 1858.*

# Chapter CLXXXII

> An Act for the Relief of George Bond.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be and he is hereby directed to place the name of George Bond on the roll of invalid pensions for the State of Georgia, at the rate of eight dollars per month, to commence on the fourth day of December, eighteen hundred and fifty-five, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, March 3, 1857.*

# Chapter XLIV

> An Act for the Relief of Myra Clark Gaines.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be directed to place the name of Myra Clark Gaines, widow of the late Major-General Edmund P. Gaines, on the pension roll, at the rate of half the pay per month to which the said Gaines was entitled at his death, to commence from the sixth day of June, eighteen hundred and forty-nine, and to continue during her natural life.


*Approved, February 18, 1859.*

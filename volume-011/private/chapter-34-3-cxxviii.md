# Chapter CXXVIII

> An Act for the Relief of Amos Armstrong, of Ohio.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby required to place the name of Amos Armstrong, of Summit county, Ohio, upon the pension roll, and cause to be paid him the sum of eight dollars per month for and during the term of his natural life, commencing on the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and forty-seven.


*Approved, March 3, 1857.*

# Chapter CXLIII

> An Act for the Relief of James Davidson, of Kentucky.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in lieu of the pension now paid to James Davidson of Kentucky, the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to pay to said James Davidson twenty dollars per month from the day his present pension commenced, for and during his natural life.


*Approved, August 18, 1856.*

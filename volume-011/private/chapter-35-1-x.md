# Chapter X

> An Act for the Relief of John Hamilton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 9nited States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to pay out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of two hundred dollars with interest from the first day of June, eighteen hundred and fifty-two, to John Hamilton, of Champaign County, Ohio, in full compensation for his time and services, &c., during his imprisonment with the Indians, in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve with Great Britain.


*Approved, March 16, 1858.*

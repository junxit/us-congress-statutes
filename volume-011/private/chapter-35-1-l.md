# Chapter L

> An Act for the Relief of the Heirs and legal Representatives of Pierre Broussard, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the heirs and legal representatives of Pierre Broussard, deceased, late of Louisiana, be, and they are hereby, confirmed in their title to a certain tract of land situated on the Bayou Teche, in the parish of St. Martin, in said State of Louisiana, and known on the recognized public surveys as section thirty-six (36), in township eight (8) south, of range five (5) east, containing about one hundred and seventy acres: Provided, That this confirmation shall only operate as a relinquishment of title on the part of the United States, and shall not affect any adverse rights, if any such there be.


*Approved, May 24, 1858.*

# Chapter LXIV

> An Act for the Relief of John C. McConnell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of nine hundred and ninety-three dollars and forty-two cents be paid to John C. McConnell, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the same being for fresh beef furnished by him to General Quitman’s brigade of volunteers in the Mexican war in April, eighteen hundred and forty-seven.


*Approved, March 2, 1857.*

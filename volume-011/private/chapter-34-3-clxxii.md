# Chapter CLXXII

> An Act for the Relief of Robert H. Stevens.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be and he is hereby authorized and directed to place the name of Robert H. Stevens, of the State of New York, on the invalid pension roll, at the rate of eight dollars per month, to commence on the first day of March, eighteen hundred and fifty-four, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, March 3, 1857.*

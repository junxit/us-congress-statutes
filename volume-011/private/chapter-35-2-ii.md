# Chapter II

> An Act recognizing the Assignment on Land Warrant Number Thirty-five Thousand Nine Hundred and Fifty-six, (35,956,) issued to John Davis, as valid.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the assignment upon land warrant number thirty-five thousand nine hundred and fifty-six, (35,956,) issued to John Davis, under the act of September twenty-eight, eighteen hundred and fifty, for forty acres, be, and is hereby recognized, as valid.


*Approved, December 21, 1858.*

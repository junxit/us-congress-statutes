# Chapter LV

> An Act for the Relief of Robinson Gammon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to place the name of Robinson Gammon, of Roxbury, in the county of Oxford, and State of Maine, upon the roll of invalid pensions, at the rate of eight dollars per month, from the third day of December, in the year eighteen hundred and fifty-six, during his life.


*Approved, February 23, 1859.*

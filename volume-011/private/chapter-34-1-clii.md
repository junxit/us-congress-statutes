# Chapter CLII

> An Act for the Relief of Nathan M. Lounsbury.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, required and authorized to pay to Nathan M. Lounsbury, of Rutland county, Vermont, the sum of seven hundred and fifty-six dollars, being at the rate of eight dollars per month, for arrears of pension, from the eighteenth day of March, eighteen hundred and eighteen, to the fourth day of February, eighteen hundred and twenty-six, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, August 18, 1856.*

# Private Resolution 19

> A Resolution devolving upon the Secretary of War the Execution of the Act of Congress entitled “An Act supplemental to an Act therein mentioned,” approved December twenty-two, eighteen hundred and fifty-four.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the duties imposed, or required to be performed, by the act of Congress entitled “An act supplemental to an act therein mentioned,” approved December twenty-two, eighteen hundred and fifty-four, including the act to which it is supplemental, be, and the same are hereby, transferred to the Secretary of War, who shall proceed de novo to execute the same in their plain and obvious meaning: Provided, nevertheless, That from any amount which may be found just and equitably due to the legal representatives of George Fisher, deceased, there shall be deducted all sums which may have been heretofore allowed and paid by the United States.


*Approved, June 3, 1858.*

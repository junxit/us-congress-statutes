# Private Resolution 11

> Joint Resolution for the Relief of Michael Pappreniza.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized to extend the provisions of the joint resolution approved March three, eighteen hundred and fifty-three, entitled “A resolution for the relief of the Spanish consul and other subjects of Spain residing at Key West by indemnity for losses occasioned in the year eighteen hundred and fifty-one,” to the case of one Michael Pappreniza, an Austrian subject, who, it is alleged, sustained losses at the same time in consequence of his being supposed to be a Spaniard: Provided, That the amount allowed as indemnity to said Pappreniza shall not exceed the sum of two hundred dollars.


*Approved, February 26, 1859.*

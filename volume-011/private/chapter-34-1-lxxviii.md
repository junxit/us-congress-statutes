# Chapter LXXVIII

> An Act for the Relief of Joseph Hill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension allowed to Joseph Hill, an invalid pensioner, be increased to twelve dollars per month, from June twenty-five, eighteen hundred and fifty-six, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, August 6, 1856.*

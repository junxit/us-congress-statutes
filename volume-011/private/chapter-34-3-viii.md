# Chapter VIII

> An Act to authorize the Postmaster-General to execute a Contract with Messrs. Garman, Wigle, and Benford, for carrying the Mail from Cumberland, Maryland, to Greensburg, Pennsylvania.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster-General be and he is hereby authorized to execute a contract with Messrs. Garman, Wigle, and Benford, accepted bidders at the late lettings of mail contracts on route number three thousand three hundred and thirty-one, from Cumberland, Maryland, to Greensburg, Pennsylvania, at the sum of forty-three hundred and twenty dollars per annum.


*Approved, January 13, 1857.*

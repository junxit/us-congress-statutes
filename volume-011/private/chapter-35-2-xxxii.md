# Chapter XXXII

> An Act for the Relief of the Heirs and legal Representatives of Olivier Landry, of the State of Louisiana:

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the heirs and legal representatives and assigns of Olivier Landry be, and they are hereby, confirmed in their title to a certain tract of land situated in township ten south, range five east, in the southwestern district of Louisiana, containing two hundred and thirty acres and eighty-four hundredths of an acre, being the tract on the township map of said township marked forty-nine, bounded on one side by a tract belonging to the heirs of Rene Trahan, and on the other by land formerly confirmed to said Olivier Landry: Provided, That this act shall only be construed as a relinquishment of any title that the United States may have to said lands, and shall not affect any title that any third person may have in and to said lands.


*Approved, February 9, 1859.*

# Chapter XCV

> An Act for the Relief of Isaac Body and Samuel Fleming.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Isaac Body be allowed to enter at the land-office at Springfield, Illinois, at the minimum price, at any time within one year after the date of this act, the southeast quarter of section number nineteen of township number twenty-six north, of range twelve west; and that Samuel Fleming be allowed to enter, at the same land-office, and on the same terms and conditions, the northwest quarter of section twenty, township twenty-six north, range twelve west: Provided, however, That this act shall only operate as a relinquishment of title on the part of the United States.


*Approved, June 5, 1858.*

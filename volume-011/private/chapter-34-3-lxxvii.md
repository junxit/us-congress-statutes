# Chapter LXXVII

> An Act for the Relief of William Kendall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the accounting officers of the treasury be and they are hereby directed to settle the account of William Kendall, sutler at Fort Columbus, for all articles furnished by him to soldiers there recruited or stationed during the late Mexican war; and in all cases where the several amounts claimed were regularly entered on the original muster or descriptive rolls which accompanied the detachments from said fort, and were thus charged against the respective soldiers, but were not transferred to the company rolls, nor retained out of the soldiers’ pay and allowed to the said Kendall, the said amounts shall be allowed and paid to him out of any moneys in the treasury not other wise appropriated.


*Approved, March 2, 1857.*

# Chapter CLV

> An Act for the Relief of F. A. Cunningham, Paymaster United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the Treasury Department be, and they are hereby authorized and directed, in the settlement of the accounts of F. A. Cunningham, paymaster of the United States army, to allow him a credit in the sum of thirty-six thousand and eighty-five dollars, of which sum of public money he was robbed at Santa Fe, New Mexico, on the eighteenth of January, eighteen hundred and fifty-five.


*Approved, August 18, 1856.*

# Chapter LXXVI

> An Act for the Relief of Lyman N. Cook.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of Lyman N. Cook be placed on the pension roll of the United States, and that he receive an annual pension of twenty-two dollars and fifty cents per month during his natural life, to be paid to him out of the treasury of the United States.


*Approved, March 2, 1857.*

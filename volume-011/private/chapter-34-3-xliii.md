# Chapter XLIII

> An Act to extend the Time for selling the Lands granted to the Kentucky Asylum for teaching the Deaf and Dumb.

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the further time of five years, from and after the expiration of the time heretofore allowed, be and the same is hereby allowed and permitted the trustees of the Centre College of Kentucky, who are also the trustees of said Kentucky asylum for teaching the deaf and dumb, to sell the lands heretofore granted said asylum by acts of Congress heretofore passed and confirmed to said trustees.


*Approved, February 7, 1857.*

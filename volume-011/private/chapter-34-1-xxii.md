# Chapter XXII

> An Act to continue Half-pay to Mrs. Lewright Browning for a further term of five Years.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to continue to Mrs. Lewright Browning, widow of Robert L. Browning, late of the navy, half the monthly pay of her said deceased husband, for a further term of five years, from the time when the half-pay granted her by act of Congress, approved September twenty, eighteen hundred and fifty, shall cease.


*Approved, April 26, 1856.*

# Chapter CXXXII

> An Act for the Relief of Abraham Kintdng.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Abraham Kintzing, late special examiner of drugs at Philadelphia, the sum of three hundred and nineteen dollars and fifty cents, being the difference between his salary as special examiner, and that of the assistant appraiser, for three months and twenty-five days, the period during which he performed the duties of the latter office, in addition to his own, in consequence of the death of its incumbent.


*Approved, August 18, 1856.*

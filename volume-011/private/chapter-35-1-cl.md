# Chapter CL

> An Act for the Relief of William S. Bradford.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, from and after the passage of this act, the pension now paid to William S. Bradford be increased from the present amount received by him to twenty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, June 11, 1858.*

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

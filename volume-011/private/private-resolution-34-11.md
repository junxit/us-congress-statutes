# Private Resolution 11

> Joint Resolution for the Relief of Israel B. Bigelow.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a pension of fifteen dollars per month during his natural life, be allowed to Israel B. Bigelow, of Texas, to commence from the fourteenth day of January, eighteen hundred and forty-seven, the day on which he was severely wounded and disabled by the enemy, while bearing important despatches from Major-General Patterson to General Quitman, during the march of the division of volunteers from Victoria to Tampico, in Mexico.


*Approved, March 2, 1857.*

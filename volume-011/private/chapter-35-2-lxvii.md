# Chapter LXVII

> An Act for the Relief of Kennedy O’Brien.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, directed to place the name of Kennedy O’Brien on the list of invalid pensioners, and that said O’Brien be paid a pension, at the rate of eight dollars per month, from the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and fifty-four.


*Approved, February 28, 1859.*

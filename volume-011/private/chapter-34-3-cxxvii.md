# Chapter CXXVII

> An Act for the Relief of Charles Parish, a Soldier of the War of eighteen hundred and twelve.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of Charles Parish be placed on the roll of invalid pensions, at the rate of four dollars per month, commencing the third day of December, eighteen hundred and fifty-five, and continuing during his natural life.


*Approved, March 3, 1857.*

## Marginal notes

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

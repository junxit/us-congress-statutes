# Chapter CXXXVIII

> An Act for the Relief of Stephen Fellows.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Stephen Fellows on the invalid pension list, at the rate of lour dollars per month, from the twentieth day of January, eighteen hundred and fifty-eight, and continue during life.


*Approved, June 9, 1858.*

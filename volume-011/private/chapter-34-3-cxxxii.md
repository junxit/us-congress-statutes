# Chapter CXXXII

> An Act for the Relief of Henry Stewart.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of Henry Stewart be placed on the roll of invalid pensioners, at the rate of eight dollars per month, commencing the first of December, eighteen hundred and fifty-five, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, March 3, 1857.*

# Chapter CXXI

> An Act for the Construction of a Road in the Territory of Nebraska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of thirty thousand dollars be and the same is hereby appropriated out of any money in the treasury, for the construction of a road from the Platte River, via the Omaha Reserve and Dahkota City, to the Running Water River, in the Territory of Nebraska; said road to be constructed under the direction of the Secretary of the Interior.


*Approved, March 3, 1857.*

# Chapter LXVIII

> An Act to authorize the Enrollment, Registry, and License of certain Steamboats, or Vessels, owned by the Buffalo and Lake Huron Railroad Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That certificates of enrollment, register, or license, shall issue for any American-built steamboat or steam vessel, now owned, or which shall hereafter be owned, by the Buffalo and Lake Huron Railway Company, a corporation created under and by virtue of the laws of the State of New York, in the name of said company, on the president or secretary of said company, taking the oath required by the fourth section of the act entitled “An act to authorize the register, or enrollment and license to be issued in the name of the president or secretary of any incorporated company owning a steamboat or vessel,” passed March three eighteen hundred and twenty-five. And nothing contained in said act shall be construed to prevent the oath required by the fourth section of said act from being taken by said president, or secretary, of the said Buffalo and Lake Huron Railway Company, by reason of any personal disability, or otherwise.


*Approved, March 1, 1859.*

# Chapter VIII

> An Act to repeal an Act entitled “An Act authorizing the Secretary of the Treasury to change the Names of Vessels in certain Cases,” approved the fifth of March, one thousand eight hundred and fifty-six.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the act entitled, “An act authorizing the Secretary of the Treasury to change the names of vessels in certain cases,” approved fifth March, one thousand eight hundred and fifty-six, be, and the same is hereby, repealed.


*Approved, January 17, 1859.*

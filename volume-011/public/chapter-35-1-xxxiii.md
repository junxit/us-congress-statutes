# Chapter XXXIII

> An Act to enlarge the Detroit and Saginaw Land Districts in Michigan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all that part of the present Cheboygan district, in the State of Michigan, which lies south of the line dividing townships twenty-eight and twenty-nine north, and east of the line dividing ranges two and three west, shall be attached to and form a part of the present Saginaw district, and all that part of the said Cheboygan district which lies north of the line dividing townships twenty-eight and twenty-nine north, and east of the line dividing ranges one and two west, including the island of Mackinac, be attached to and form a part of the Detroit district, in said State.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That this act take effect from and after the first day of July next.


*Approved, May 11, 1858.*

# Chapter XXXII

> An Act amendatory of an Act entitled “An Act to establish two additional Land Districts in the Territory of Minnesota,” approved July eighth, eighteen hundred and fifty-six.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That so much of an act entitled “An act to establish two additional land districts in the Territory of Min-nesota,” approved July eighth, anno Domini eighteen hundred and fifty six, as defines the southern boundary of the northwestern land district, on the west side of the Mississippi River, be, and the same is hereby, repealed, and in lieu thereof the following boundaries are established, to wit: Commencing at the point on the eastern side of the Mississippi river where the present south line touches the river; thence down said River to the point opposite the intersection with the river of the eighth standard parallel; thence along said parallel to the point of intersection of guide meridian number four; thence along said guide meridian to the seventh standard parallel; thence west along said seventh parallel to the Sioux Wood river; thence north to the line heretofore established.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the line dividing ranges twenty-three and twenty-four be the boundary line between the northwestern and northeastern land districts in lieu of the range line between eighteen and nineteen, as heretofore established in the above-recited act.


*Approved, May 11, 1858.*

# Chapter CLXI

> An Act making an Appropriation for the Completion of the Military Road from Astoria to Salem, in Oregon Territory.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of thirty thousand dollars ($30,000) be, and the same is hereby, appropriated for the completion of the military road from Astoria to Salem, to be completed under the direction of the Secretary of War.


*Approved, June 14, 1858.*

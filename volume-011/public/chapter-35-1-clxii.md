## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the Postmaster-General be authorized to make such arrangements for the transmission of the great through mails between Portland and New Orleans, as will insure the most speedy and certain connection, including in the route for one of the daily mails, as many of the seaboard commercial cities as may be consistent with the greatest despatch.


*Approved, June 14, 1858.*

# Resolution 20

> Joint Resolution authorizing Commander M. F. Maury to accept a Gold Medal awarded to him by the Emperor of Austria.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Commander M. F. Maury, of the United States navy, be, and he is hereby, authorized to accept the great gold medal of the arts and sciences recently presented to him by his Majesty the Emperor of Austria.


*Approved, June 5, 1858.*

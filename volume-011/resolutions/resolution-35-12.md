# Resolution 12

> Joint Resolution for paying the Compensation of Stenographers employed by Committees of the House of Representatives.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to allow and pay, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the compensation of stenographers employed by the committees of the House of Representatives, as audited under the direction of said House.


*Approved, May 24, 1858.*

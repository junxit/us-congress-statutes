# Resolution 2

> Joint Resolution authorizing Townsend Harris, United States Consul-General at Japan, and H. C. J. Heusken, his interpreter, respectively, to accept a Snuffbox from her Majesty, the Queen of England.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Townsend Harris, United States Consul-General in Japan, and H. C. J. Heusken be, and they are hereby authorized, to accept, respectively, from the Queen of England a snuffbox bearing her Majesty’s cypher, which it is the intention of the Queen to offer to each as a mark of her gratitude for assistance rendered by them to Lord Elgin in his negotiation of a treaty between Great Britain and Japan.


*Approved, January 25, 1859.*

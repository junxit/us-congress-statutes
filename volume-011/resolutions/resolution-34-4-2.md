# Resolution 4

> A Resolution authorizing Alexander D. Bache to accept a Medal presented to him by the King of Sweden.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Alexander D. Bache, Superintendent of the Coast Survey, be and he is hereby authorized to accept the gold medal recently presented to him by the King of Sweden.


*Approved, August 30, 1856.*

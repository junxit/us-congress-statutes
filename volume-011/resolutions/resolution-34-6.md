# Resolution 6

> A Resolution for the Appointment of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the vacancies in the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution, of the class “other than members of Congress,” be filled by the reappointment, of the late incumbents, viz: Richard Rush, of Philadelphia, and Joseph G. Totten, of Washington.


*Approved, January 28, 1857.*

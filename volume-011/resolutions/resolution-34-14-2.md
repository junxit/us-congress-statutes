# Resolution 14

> A Resolution relating to the Compensation of the Chaplains of Congress.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the chaplains of the two Houses of Congress, be paid on the last day of each month during the regular sessions of Congress, at the rate of seven hundred and fifty dollars per annum, beginning with the present Congress, and at the end of each regular session, they shall be paid the residue of said annual salary.


*Approved, March 3, 1857.*

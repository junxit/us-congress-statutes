# Resolution 23

> A Resolution authorizing the Secretary of War to expend the Appropriation made July eighth, eighteen hundred and fifty-six, upon such Channel of the St. Mary’s River as he may select.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the appropriation of July eighth, eighteen hundred and fifty-six, made for the improvement of St. Mary’s River, in the State of Michigan, may be expended in excavating the channel as, from the evidence which he may obtain, the Secretary of War shall deem best.


*Approved, June 9, 1858.*

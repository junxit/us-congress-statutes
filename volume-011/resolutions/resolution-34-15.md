# Resolution 15

> Joint Resolution for the Presentation of Medals to Dr. Kane, his Officers and Men.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy shall cause to be struck and presented to Dr. Kane, his officers and men, respectively, such appropriate medals as in the judgment of the said Secretary shall express the high estimation in which Congress hold their respective merits and services.


*Approved, March 3, 1857.*

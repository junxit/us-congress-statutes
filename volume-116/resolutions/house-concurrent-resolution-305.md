# House Concurrent Resolution 305

> RONALD AND NANCY REAGAN CONGRESSIONAL GOLD MEDAL PRESENTATION CEREMONY—CAPITOL ROTUNDA AUTHORIZATION

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Rotunda of the Capitol is authorized to be used on May 16, 2002, for a ceremony to present a gold medal on behalf of Congress to former President Ronald Reagan and his wife Nancy Reagan. Physical preparations for the ceremony shall be carried out in accordance with such conditions as the Architect of the Capitol may prescribe.


*Agreed to March 6, 2002.*

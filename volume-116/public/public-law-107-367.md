# Public Law 107–367

> An Act To reauthorize the Mni Wiconi Rural Water Supply Project.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. MNI WICONI RURAL WATER SUPPLY PROJECT, SOUTH DAKOTA.

Section 10(a) of the Mni Wiconi Project Act of 1988 (Public Law 100–516; 102 Stat. 2571), as amended by section 813 of the Mni Wiconi Act Amendments of 1994 (Public Law 103–434; 108 Stat. 4545), is amended—

- **(1)** in the first sentence, by inserting “(based on October 1, 1992, price levels) and $58,800,000 (based on October 1, 1997, price levels)” after “$263,241,000”;
- **(2)** in the second sentence, by striking “2003” and inserting “2008”; and
- **(3)** in the last sentence, by inserting “(with respect to the $263,241,000), and October 1, 1997 (with respect to the $58,800,000)” after “1992”.


*Approved December 19, 2002.*

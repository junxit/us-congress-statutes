# Public Law 107–177

> An Act To designate the Federal building and United States courthouse located at 501 Bell Street in Alton, Illinois, as the “William L. Beatty Federal Building and United States Courthouse”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The Federal building and United States courthouse located at 501 Bell Street in Alton, Illinois, shall be known and designated as the “William L. Beatty Federal Building and United States Courthouse”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Federal building and United States courthouse referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “William L. Beatty Federal Building and United States Courthouse”.


*Approved May 17, 2002.*

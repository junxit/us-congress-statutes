# Public Law 107–328

> Joint Resolution Relative to the convening of the first session of the One Hundred Eighth Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first regular session of the One Hundred Eighth Congress shall begin at noon on Tuesday, January 7, 2003.


*Approved December 4, 2002.*

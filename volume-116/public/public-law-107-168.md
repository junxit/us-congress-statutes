# Public Law 107–168

> An Act To extend the authority of the Export-Import Bank until May 31, 2002.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. EXTENSION OF EXPORT-IMPORT BANK.

Notwithstanding the dates specified in section 7 of the Export-Import Bank Act of 1945 (12 U.S.C. 635f) and section 1(c) of Public Law 103-428, the Export-Import Bank of the United States shall continue to exercise its functions in connection with and in furtherance of its objects and purposes through May 31, 2002.


*Approved May 1, 2002.*

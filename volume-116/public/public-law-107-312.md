# Public Law 107–312

> An Act To reduce preexisting PAYGO balances, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. REDUCTION OF PREEXISTING PAYGO BALANCES.

Upon the enactment of this Act, the Director of the Office of Management and Budget shall reduce any balances of direct spending and receipts legislation for all fiscal years under section 252 of the Balanced Budget and Emergency Deficit Control Act of 1985 to zero.


*Approved December 2, 2002.*

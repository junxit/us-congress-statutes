# Public Law 107–375

> An Act To extend the periods of authorization for the Secretary of the Interior to implement capital construction projects associated with the endangered fish recovery implementation programs for the Upper Colorado and San Juan River Basins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. AMENDMENTS TO EXISTING PROVISIONS.

Public Law 106–392 (114 Stat. 1602) is amended as follows:

- **(1)** Section 2(1)is amended by inserting “and extended by the Extension of the Cooperative Agreement dated December 6, 2001,” after “September 29, 1987,”.
- **(2)** Section 3(a)(2) is amended by striking “fiscal year 2005” and inserting “fiscal year 2008”.
- **(3)** Section 3(a)(3) is amended by striking “fiscal year 2007” and inserting “fiscal year 2008”.
- **(4)** Section 3(b) is amended—
  - **(A)** in paragraph (1) by striking “fiscal year 2005” and inserting “fiscal year 2008”; and
  - **(B)** in paragraph (2) by striking “fiscal year 2007” and inserting “fiscal year 2008”.
- **(5)** Section 3(c)(1) is amended by striking “with” and inserting “within”.


*Approved December 19, 2002.*

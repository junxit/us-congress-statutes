# Chapter 15

> AN ACT For the relief of Charles H. LeGay.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions of section 1118, Revised Statutes, the Secretary of War is hereby authorized to reenlist, in the United States Army, Charles H. LeGay, Medical Department, Fort McIntosh, Texas, at the expiration of said Charles H. LeGay’s present period of enlistment, and on such future dates as the said Charles H. LeGay may make application for reenlistment.


*Approved, January 26, 1940.*

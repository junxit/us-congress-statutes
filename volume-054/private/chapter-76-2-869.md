# Chapter 869

> AN ACT For the relief of Howard Mondt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions of section 1118, Revised Statutes, the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized to reenlist in the United States Army Howard Mondt, Air Corps, Hamilton Field, California, at the expiration of the said Howard Mondt’s present period of enlistment on November 9, 1910, and on such future dates as the said Howard Mondt may make application for reenlistment: Provided, That he meets the other requirements for enlistment in the Army.


*Approved, October 14, 1940.*

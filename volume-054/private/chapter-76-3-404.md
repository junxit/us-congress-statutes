# Chapter 404

> AN ACT For the relief of Kurt Wessely.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws Kurt Wessely shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence on August 3, 1937, at Ellis Island, New York.

Upon the enactment of this Act the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the nonpreference category of the quota during the current year.


*Approved, June 20, 1940.*

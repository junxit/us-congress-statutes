# Chapter 547

> AN ACT Providing for the extension of nonquota status to Frederick Beck.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding section 4 (d) which limits the right of a male minister or professor to secure a nonquota status for his wife and unmarried children, the Secretary of State is hereby authorized and directed to issue a nonquota visa to Frederick Beck, the husband of Professor Vera F. Beck.


*Approved, July 2, 1940.*

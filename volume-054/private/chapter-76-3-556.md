# Chapter 556

> AN ACT For the relief of Ethel G. Hamilton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions and limitations of sections 15 to 20, both inclusive, of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes”, approved September 7, 1916, as amended, the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission be, and the same is hereby, authorized and directed to receive and consider the claim of Ethel G. Hamilton for disability alleged to have been incurred by her on or about November 11, 1937, while she was employed at the Veterans’ Administration facility, Biloxi, Mississippi, and to determine said claim upon its merits under the provisions of said Act: Provided, That no benefits shall accrue prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved, July 8, 1940.*

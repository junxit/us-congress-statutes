# Chapter 87

> AN ACT For the relief of George Louis Artick.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws George Louis Artick, sometimes known as George Attick, of Danbury, Connecticut, shall be held and considered to nave been legally admitted to the United States for permanent residence during July 1925.

## § 2.

The Secretary of Labor is authorized and directed to cancel any warrants of arrest or orders of deportation which may have been issued in the case of said George Louis Artick, sometimes known as George Attick, upon the ground of unlawful residence in the United States.


*Approved, April 11, 1940.*

# Chapter 585

> AN ACT For the relief of Edward Hagenson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions and limitations of sections 15 to 20, both inclusive, of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes,” approved September 7, 1916, as amended, are hereby waived in the case of Edward Hagenson, formerly employed by the Alaska Railroad during the period 1932 to May 3, 1934; the General Manager of the Alaska Railroad is authorized and directed to hear, consider and determine any claim filed with said railroad, within one year after the date of enactment of this Act, by said Edward Hagenson or by his heirs, executors, or administrators for compensation under the provisions of such Act of September 7, 1916, as amended, for disability due to silicosis: Provided, That no benefits hereunder shall accrue prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved, July 11, 1940.*

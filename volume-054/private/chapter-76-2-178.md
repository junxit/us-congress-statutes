# Chapter 178

> AN ACT For the relief of Esther Cottingham Grab.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Esther Cottingham Grab, widow of Frederic Daniel Grab, late American commercial attache at Caracas, Venezuela, the sum of $5,000, such sum representing one year’s salary of her deceased husband, who died May 2, 1937, in an airplane crash, while in the Foreign Service of the United States of America.


*Approved, April 30, 1940.*

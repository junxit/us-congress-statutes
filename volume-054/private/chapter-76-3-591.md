# Chapter 591

> AN ACT Authorizing the naturalization of Thomas A. Lambie.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding any other provision of law, at any time within one year after the date of enactment of this Act, Thomas A. Lambie, of Owings Mills, Maryland, may be naturalized as a citizen of the United States by taking the naturalization oath of allegiance before any court having jurisdiction of the naturalization of aliens.


*Approved, July 11, 1940.*

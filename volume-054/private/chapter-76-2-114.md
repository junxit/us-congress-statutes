# Chapter 114

> AN ACT For the relief of Howard Daury.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 17 to 20, inclusive, of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes”, approved September 7, 1916, as amended (U. S. C., 1934 edition, title 5, secs. 767 and 770), are hereby waived in favor of Howard Daury, of Pittsfield, Massachusetts, who allegedly sustained injuries in line of duty on April 27, 1934, at Pittsfield, Massachusetts, while employed by the Civil Works Administration, and his claim for compensation is authorized to be considered and acted upon under the remaining provisions of such Act, as amended, if he files such claim with the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission not later than six months after the date of enactment of this Act: Provided, That no benefits hereunder shall accrue prior to the approval of this Act.


*Approved, April 18, 1940.*

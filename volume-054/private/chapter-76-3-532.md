# Chapter 532

> AN ACT For the relief of Malachy Ryan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the account of Malachy Ryan, former acting postmaster at Caledonia, Minnesota, with the sum of $109, lost by reason of burglary occurring in his office May 8, 1933.


*Approved, July 2, 1940.*

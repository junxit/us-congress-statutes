# Chapter 208

> AN ACT For the relief of Luise Ehrenfeld.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, the Secretary of Labor is hereby authorized and directed to permit registration of Luise Ehrenfeld as having entered the United States for permanent residence. Upon the enactment of this Act the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the nonpreference category of the quota during the current year.


*Approved, May 24, 1940.*

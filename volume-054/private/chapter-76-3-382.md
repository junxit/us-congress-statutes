# Chapter 382

> AN ACT To provide for the advancement of John L. Hines on the retired list of the Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That John L. Hines, formerly Chief of Staff of the Army of the United States and now major general on the retired list of the Army, shall, beginning with the date of the enactment of this Act, have the rank of a general on the retired list of the Army: Provided, That the said John L. Hines shall receive no increase in retired pay, allowances, or other pecuniary benefits by reason of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved, June 15, 1940.*

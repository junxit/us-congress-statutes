# Chapter 9

> AN ACT For the relief of George E. Miller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to allow credit in the account of George E. Miller, disbursing clerk, London, England, in the sum of $424.47, representing the amount disallowed by the Comptroller General in connection with the shipment of personal effects of Sam E. Woods, American commercial attach from Prague, Czechoslovakia, to Berlin, Germany, in steel moving vans which, although increasing the weight to an amount in excess of that allowed by the regulations, was considerably cheaper than any other method of packing and transportation. Said travel order for removal of personal effects being dated August 16, 1937.


*Approved, January 17, 1940.*

# Chapter 603

> AN ACT For the relief of Guy F. Allen, chief disbursing officer. Division of Disbursement, Treasury Department.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to allow credit in the February 1937 account of Guy F. Allen, chief disbursing officer, Division of Disbursement, Treasury Department, without charge against the certifying officer of the Department of the Interior, for voucher 16–28071, $149.94.


*Approved, July 11, 1940.*

# Chapter 92

> AN ACT For the relief of Dane Goich.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws Dane Goich shall be held and considered to have been legally admitted to the United States for permanent residence on June 15, 1925.

## § 2.

The Secretary of Labor is authorized and directed to cancel any warrants of arrest or orders of deportation which may have been issued in the case of the said Dane Goich upon the ground of unlawful residence in the United States.


*Approved, April 11, 1940.*

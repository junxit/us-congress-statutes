# Chapter 816

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Hama Torii Emerson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the date of the approval of this Act, Mrs. Hama Torii Emerson, who was admitted into the Territory of Hawaii on June 27, 1931, for a temporary stay and is the Japanese wife of a citizen of the United States, shall be deemed to have been lawfully admitted as an immigrant for permanent residence.


*Approved, October 9, 1940.*

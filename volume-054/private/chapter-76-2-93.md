# Chapter 93

> AN ACT For the relief of Mary Nouhan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, Mary Nouhan, of Paterson, New Jersey, shall be held and considered to have been legally admitted to the United States for permanent residence on April 17, 1925.

## § 2.

The Secretary of Labor is authorized and directed to cancel any warrants of arrest or orders of deportation which may have been issued in the case of the said Mary Nouhan upon the ground of unlawful entry into the United States.


*Approved, April 11, 1940.*

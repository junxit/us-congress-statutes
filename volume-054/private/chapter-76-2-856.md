# Chapter 856

> AN ACT For the relief of James Robert Harman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, or benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers James Robert Harman, who was a member of the Sixty-fifth Service Squadron, Air Corps, shall be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States on May 15, 1931: Provided, That no back pay or pension shall be held to have accrued prior to the approval of this Act.


*Approved, October 10, 1940.*

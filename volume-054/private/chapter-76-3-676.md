# Chapter 676

> AN ACT For the relief of May C. Taylor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Home Owners’ Loan Corporation is hereby authorized and directed to pay to May C. Taylor, of Newark, New Jersey, the sum of $497.50, in full settlement of all claims against the Home Owners’ Loan Corporation for salary due her for duties performed while employed by the Home Owners’ Loan Corporation: Provided, That no part of the amount appropriated in this Act in excess of 10 per centum thereof shall be paid or delivered to or received by any agent or attorney on account of services rendered in connection with this claim, and the same shall be unlawful, any contract to the contrary notwithstanding. Any person violating the provisions of this Act shall be deemed guilty of a misdemeanor and upon conviction thereof shall be fined in any sum not exceeding $1,000.


*Approved, August 13, 1940.*

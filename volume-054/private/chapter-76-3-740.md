# Chapter 740

> AN ACT For the relief of Louis A. Charland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions and limitations of sections 15 to 20, both inclusive, of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes”, approved September 7, 1916, as amended, the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission is hereby authorized and directed to receive and consider, when filed, the claim of Louis A. Charland for disability alleged to have been incurred by him on or about November 11, 1935, when engaged in authorized activities while an employee of the Veterans’ facility at Sunmount, New York, and to determine said claim upon its merits and under the provisions of said Act: Provided, That such claim be filed within six months after the passage of this Act: And provided further, That no benefits shall accrue prior to the approval of this Act.


*Approved, October 4, 1940.*

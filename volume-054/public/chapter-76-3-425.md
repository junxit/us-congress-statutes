# Chapter 425

> AN ACT To allow moving expenses to employees in the Railway Mail Service.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That hereafter officers and regular clerks in the Railway Mail Service, when arbitrarily transferred under orders of the Department from one official station to another for permanent duty, and who actually have to change their residence, may be allowed their actual and necessary transportation expenses for moving their household goods, including packing and drayage, not in excess of three thousand five hundred pounds.


*Approved, June 25, 1940.*

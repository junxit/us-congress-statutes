# Chapter 134

> AN ACT To change the name of a portion of Twenty-fourth Street Northwest to Williamsburg Lane.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of that portion of the street in the District of Columbia now known as Twenty-fourth Street Northwest, which begins at Porter Street and extends one block in a northerly direction to Rock Creek Park, is hereby changed to Williamsburg Lane.


*Approved, April 22, 1940.*

# Chapter 941

> JOINT RESOLUTION To extend the date for filing a report by the United States Commission for the Celebration of the Two Hundredth Anniversary of the Birth of Thomas Jefferson.

*Resolved by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 7 of the joint resolution approved September 24, 1940 (Public Resolution Numbered 100, Seventy-sixth Congress), is hereby amended to read as follows:

> ## § 7.
>
> The Commission shall, on or before the 15th day of February 1941, make a report to the Congress in order that enabling legislation may be enacted.”


*Approved, January 9, 1941.*

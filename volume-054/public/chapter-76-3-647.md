# Chapter 647

> AN ACT To provide for the rank and title of lieutenant general of the Regular Army in the military departments of Panama and Hawaii.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act entitled “An Act to provide for the rank and title of lieutenant general of the Regular Army”, approved August 5, 1939, is hereby amended to include the major generals of the Regular Army specifically assigned by the Secretary of War to command the Panama Canal and Hawaiian Departments.


*Approved, July 31, 1940.*

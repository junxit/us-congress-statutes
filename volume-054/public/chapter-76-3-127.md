# Chapter 127

> AN ACT To amend the District of Columbia Unemployment Compensation Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1 (b) of the District of Columbia Unemployment Compensation Act, approved August 28, 1935, as amended, is further amended by adding a new paragraph:

> - **(9)** Service performed by an individual under the age of eighteen in the delivery or distribution of newspapers or shopping news, not including delivery or distribution to any point for subsequent delivery or distribution.”

## § 2.

This amendment shall be effective January 1, 1940.


*Approved, April 22, 1940.*

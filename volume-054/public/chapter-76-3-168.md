# Chapter 168

> AN ACT Amending Acts extending the franking privilege to widows of ex-Presidents of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Acts of February 1, 1909 (ch. 55, 35 Stat. 591), October 27, 1919 (ch. 84, 41 Stat. 1449), March 4, 1924 (ch. 45, 43 Stat. 1359), June 14, 1930 (ch. 493, 46 Stat. 1906), and June 16, 1934 (ch. 560, 48 Stat. 1395), extending the franking privilege to Frances F. Cleveland (Preston), Mary Lord Harrison, Edith Carow Roosevelt, Edith Bolling Wilson, Helen H. Taft, and Grace G. Coolidge, respectively, arc hereby amended by inserting in each of said Acts the words “or facsimile thereof” after the words “under her written autograph signature”.


*Approved, April 30, 1940.*

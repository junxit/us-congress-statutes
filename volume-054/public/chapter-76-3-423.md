# Chapter 423

> AN ACT To amend section 301 (a) of the Sugar Act of 1937.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (a) of section 301 of the Sugar Act of 1937 is amended by adding at the end thereof the following: “The Secretary is authorized to make payments, notwithstanding a failure to comply with the conditions provided in this subsection, but the payments made with respect to any crop shall be subject to a deduction of $10 for each child for each day, or a portion of a day, during which such child was employed or permitted to work contrary to the foregoing provisions of this subsection, in the 1937,1938, and 1939 crops.”.


*Approved, June 25, 1940.*

# Chapter 19

> AN ACT To amend the Emergency Farm Mortgage Act of 1933, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the tenth sentence of section 32 of the Emergency Farm Mortgage Act of 1933, as amended (U. S. C., title 12, sec. 1016), is further amended by striking out “February 1, 1940” in the two places in which it appears and inserting in lieu thereof “June 1, 1942”.


*Approved, February 1, 1940.*

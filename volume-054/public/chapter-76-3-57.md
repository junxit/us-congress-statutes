# Chapter 57

> AN ACT To amend section 23 of the Act of March 4, 1909, relating to copyrights.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 23 of the Act of March 4, 1909 (35 Stat. 1080; U. S. C., title 17, sec. 23), is hereby amended by deleting from the second proviso clause thereof the words “when such contribution has been separately registered”.


*Approved, March 15, 1940.*

# Chapter 284

> AN ACT To repeal certain laws with respect to manifests and vessel permits.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 4349, 4350, 4351, 4352, 4353, 4354, 4355, 4356, 4357, 4359, and 4360 of the Revised Statutes of the United States, and the Act of July 12, 1876 (U. S. C., 1934 edition, title 46, sec. 302), are hereby repealed.


*Approved, June 8, 1940.*

# Chapter 192

> AN ACT To provide allowances for uniforms and equipment for certain officers of the Officers’ Reserve Corps of the Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That officers of the Officers’ Reserve Corps of the Army shall be entitled to an allowance for uniforms and equipment of $50 per annum upon completion, in separate fiscal years, of each of their first three periods of active-duty training of three months or less, following their original appointment, during which periods the uniform is required to he worn.


*Approved, May 14, 1940.*

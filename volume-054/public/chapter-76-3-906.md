# Chapter 906

> AN ACT To amend the Act entitled “An Act to establish a Civilian Conservation Corps, and for other purposes”, approved June 28, 1937, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 8 of the Act entitled “An Act to establish a Civilian Conservation Corps, and for other purposes”, approved June 28, 1937 (50 Stat. 319), as amended by the Act of May 12, 1938 (52 Stat. 349), is further amended by adding after the words “War veterans” in the second proviso of said section the following: “and Indians”.


*Approved, October 21, 1940.*

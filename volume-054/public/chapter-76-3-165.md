# Chapter 165

> AN ACT Providing for sick leave for substitute postal employees.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 1 and 2 of Public Law Numbered 194, first session of the Seventy-sixth Congress, be amended to read as follows:

> “That hereafter substitutes in the Postal Service shall be rated as employees and each substitute postal employee in the classified civil service shall be granted the same rights and benefits with respect to annual and sick leave that accrue to regular employees in proportion to the time actually employed.
>
> ## § 2.
>
> No substitute shall be entitled to sick leave for an illness or disability incurred at a time when such substitute is on leave of absence, granted at his own request, other than annual leave, or when such substitute is not available for duty.”


*Approved, April 30, 1940.*

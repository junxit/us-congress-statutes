# Chapter 522

> AN ACT To extend the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Susquehanna River at or near the city of Harrisburg, Pennsylvania.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Susquehanna Elver, at or near the city of Harrisburg, Pennsylvania, authorized to be built by the Dauphin County (Pennsylvania) Authority by an Act of Congress approved August 7, 1939, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from May 1, 1940.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, July 2, 1940.*

# Chapter 762

> AN ACT Relating to the statue of retired officers of the Army, Navy, Marine Corps, and Coast Guard of the United States, and to amend section 113 of the Criminal Code.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 113 of the Criminal Code is hereby amended by inserting at the end thereof the following:

> “Retired officers of the Army, Navy, Marine Corps, and Coast Guard of the United States, while not on active duty, shall not by reason of their status as such be subject to the provisions of this section: Provided, That nothing herein shall be construed to allow any retired officer to represent any person in the sale of anything to the Government through the department in whose service he holds a retired status.”


*Approved, October 8, 1940.*

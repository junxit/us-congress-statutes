# Chapter 252

> AN ACT To transfer Hardeman County, Texas, from the Fort Worth division to the Wichita Falls division of the northern judicial district of Texas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, effective thirty days after the date of the enactment of this Act, the territory embraced in Hardeman County, Texas, shall be withdrawn from the Fort Worth division of the northern judicial district of Texas and shall constitute a portion of the Wichita Falls division of such district.


*Approved, June 6, 1940.*

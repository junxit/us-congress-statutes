# Chapter 251

> AN ACT Authorizing the Secretary of the Navy to accept on behalf of the United States a gift of the yacht Freedom from Sterling Morton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United, States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized to accept on behalf of the United States, without expense to the Government, the yacht Freedom and her equipment as a gift from her owner, Sterling Morton, to the United States Naval Academy.


*Approved, June 6, 1940.*

# Chapter 203

> AN ACT To amend section 24e, National Defense Act, as amended, so as to add an alternative requirement for appointment in the Dental Corps.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the next to the last sentence of section 24e, of the National Defense Act, as amended by section 7 of the Act of April 3, 1939 (Public, Numbered 18, Seventy-sixth Congress), be, and the same is hereby amended to read as follows:

> “To be eligible for appointment in the Dental Corps, a candidate must be a graduate of a recognized dental college, and have been engaged in the practice of his profession for at least two years subsequent to graduation, or must have, after such graduation, satisfactorily completed a dental internship of not less than one year in a hospital or dispensary.”


*Approved, May 15, 1940.*

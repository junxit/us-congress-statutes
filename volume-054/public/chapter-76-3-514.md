# Chapter 514

> AN ACT To amend section 5 of the Act of Congress approved June 26, 1906, relative to the Alaska Salmon fishery.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 5 of the Act of Congress approved June 26, 1906, entitled “An Act for the protection and regulation of the fisheries of Alaska”, as amended is further amended by inserting after the word “barter” in the first sentence of said section a comma and the following: “and except by hook and line for either personal or commercial use,”.


*Approved, July 2, 1940.*

# Chapter 649

> AN ACT To increase the credit resources of Commodity Credit Corporation.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act approved March 8, 1938 (52 Stat. 107), as amended by the Act of March 4, 1939 (53 Stat. 510), be amended as follows: In section 4 delete the figure “$900,000,000” and insert in lieu thereof the figure “$1,400,000,000”.


*Approved, August 9, 1940.*

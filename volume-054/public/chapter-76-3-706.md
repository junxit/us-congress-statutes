# Chapter 706

> AN ACT To increase the number of midshipmen at the United States Naval Academy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That until September 14, 1940, the President is authorized to appoint as additional midshipmen at large at the Naval Academy those competitive and alternate candidates designated for admission in the calendar years 1939 and 1940 who were found mentally qualified therefor prior to the date of this Act but were not accepted for reasons other than physical disqualification: Provided, That no such candidate shall be eligible for admission who was more than twenty years of age on April 1, 1940.


*Approved, September 4, 1940.*

# Chapter 934

> JOINT RESOLUTION Authorizing the President to invite foreign countries to participate in the Pan American Cotton Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States is authorized and requested, by proclamation or in such manner as he may deem proper, to invite all foreign countries and nations to the Pan American Cotton Congress to be held at Memphis, Tennessee, during the year 1941, with a request that they participate therein.


*Approved, December 17, 1940.*

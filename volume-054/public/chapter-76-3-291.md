# Chapter 291

> AN ACT To extend the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Mississippi River at or near Winona, Minnesota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Mississippi River, at or near Winona, Minnesota, authorized to be built by the States of Minnesota and Wisconsin, jointly or separately, by an Act of Congress approved June 28, 1938, as extended by an Act of Congress approved July 25, 1939, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from the date of approval of this Act.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, June 8, 1940.*

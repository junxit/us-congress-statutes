# Chapter 794

> AN ACT To authorize the maintenance and operation of fish hatcheries in connection with the Grand Coulee Dam project.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in connection with fish hatcheries built or to be built as a part of the fish-protection program required on the Grand Coulee Dam project, the Secretary of the Interior is authorized to contract with the State of Washington for the maintenance and operation of any of them at the expense of said State.


*Approved, October 9, 1940.*

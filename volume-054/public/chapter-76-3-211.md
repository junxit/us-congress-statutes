# Chapter 211

> AN ACT To designate the lock and dam at Alton, Illinois, as the Henry T. Rainey Dam.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in honor of the late Henry T. Rainey, former Speaker of the House of Representatives, the navigation lock and dam at Alton, Illinois, otherwise identified as Mississippi River Lock and Dam Numbered 26, shall hereafter be known as the Henry T. Rainey Dam.


*Approved, May 27, 1940.*

# Chapter 355

> AN ACT To donate to the city of Seattle a totem pole carved by the Alaskan native Civilian Conservation Corps.

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United, States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Director of the Civilian Conservation Corps, through the regional forester, United States Forest Service, Juneau, Alaska, is hereby authorized to donate to the city of Seattle, Washington, the duplicate of the pioneer place totem pole which has been carved by Alaskan native Civilian Conservation Corps enrollees.


*Approved, June 13, 1940.*

# Chapter 412

> AN ACT To authorize the sale of lumber and other forest products obtained from the forests on Indian reservations by Indian enterprises.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the lumber and other forest products produced by Indian enterprises from the forests on Indian reservations may be sold under such regulations as the Secretary of the Interior may prescribe, without compliance with section 3709 of the Revised Statutes.


*Approved, June 24, 1940.*

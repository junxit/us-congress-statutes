# Chapter 172

> AN ACT To amend the Subsistence Expense Act of 1926, as amended by the Act of June 30, 1932 (ch. 314, sec. 209, 47 Stat. 405).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 209 of the Act of June 30, 1932 (5 U. S. C. 823a), limiting the transportation of automobiles at Government expense, be, and the same is hereby, amended by adding thereto the following: “Provided further, That funds available to the Department of State may be expended for the transportation of a personally owned automobile in any case where the Secretary of State shall determine that ocean transportation is necessary for any part of the distance between points of origin and destination, except that this authorization shall not be extended to any Ambassador or Minister when proceeding to a post of duty where a Government-owned automobile shall have been provided tor his use.”


*Approved, April 30, 1940.*

# Chapter 390

> AN ACT To extend the time limit for cooperation between the Bureau of Reclamation and the Farm Security Administration in the development of farm units on public lands under Federal reclamation projects.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of August 7, 1939 (Public, Numbered 307, Seventy-sixth Congress, first session), is hereby amended by striking out “during the fiscal year 1940,” and by inserting “during the fiscal year 1941,”,


*Approved, June 17, 1940.*

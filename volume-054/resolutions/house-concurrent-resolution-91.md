# House Concurrent Resolution 91

> second excess profits tax act of 1940

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed sixty-five thousand additional copies of Public Law Numbered 801, current session, entitled “Second Excess Profits Tax Act of 1940”, of which ten thousand copies shall be for the use of the Senate document room, fifty thousand copies shall be for the use of the House document room, three thousand for the Committee on Ways and Means of the House, and two thousand for the Committee on Finance of the Senate.


*Passed, October 11, 1940.*

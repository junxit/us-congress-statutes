# House Concurrent Resolution 45

> supreme court, sesquicentennial

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the proceedings at the various ceremonies in commemoration of the one hundred and fiftieth anniversary of the commencement of the first session of the Supreme Court of the United States, together with such additional matter as the Joint Committee on Arrangements in charge of these ceremonies may deem fitting and appropriate, in connection with this historical event, be printed, with illustrations, as a document; and that two hundred thousand additional copies be printed, of which fifty thousand shall be for the use of the Senate and one hundred and fifty thousand shall be for the use of the House of Representatives.


*Passed, March 4, 1940.*

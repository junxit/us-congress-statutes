# Senate Concurrent Resolution 53

> u. s. district court, hawaii

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Secretary of the Senate be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, in the enrollment of the bill (S. 1114) to extend the jurisdiction of the United States District Court, Territory of Hawaii, over the Midway Islands, Wake Island, Johnston Island, Sand Island, Kingman Reef, Kure Island, Baker Island, Howland Island, Jarvis Island, Canton Island, Enderbury Island, and for other purposes, to make the following changes, namely: On page 1, lines 7 and 8, of the engrossed bill, after “Howland Island,” insert “and”.

Amend the title so as to read: “An Act to extend the jurisdiction of the United States District Court, Territory of Hawaii, over the Mid-way Islands, Wake Island, Johnston Island, Sand Island, Kingman Reef, Kure Island, Baker Island, Howland Island, and Jarvis Island, and for other purposes.”


*Passed, August 5, 1940.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 88

> transportation act of 1940

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed sixty thousand additional copies of Public Law Numbered 785, current Congress, entitled “Transportation Act of 1940”, of which ten thousand copies shall be for the use of the Senate document room and fifty thousand copies shall be for the use of the House document room.


*Passed, September 19, 1940.*

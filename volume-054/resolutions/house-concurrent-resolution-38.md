# House Concurrent Resolution 38

> joint meeting

*Resolved by the House of Representatives. (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Wednesday, the 3d day of January 1940, at 2 o’clock in the afternoon for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed, January 3, 1940.*

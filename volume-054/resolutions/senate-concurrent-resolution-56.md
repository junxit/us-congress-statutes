# Senate Concurrent Resolution 56

> inaugural ceremonies, 1941

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That, in order to conform to the seating accommodations for the inaugural ceremonies in 1937, the Committee on Arrangements for the inauguration of the President-elect of the United States on the East Plaza of the Capitol on January 20, 1941, be, and it is hereby, authorized to have erected, under the supervision of the Architect of the Capitol, an additional stand, the expenses of which shall be paid one-half from the contingent fund of the Senate and one-half from the contingent fund of the House of Representatives.


*Passed, December 5, 1940.*

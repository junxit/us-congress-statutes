# Senate Concurrent Resolution 51

> kurt wessely

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Secretary of the Senate be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, in the enrollment of the bill (S. 2598) for the relief of Kurt Wessely, to make the following change, namely: In line 9 of the engrossed bill strike out “numbers” and insert “number”.


*Passed, June 12, 1940.*

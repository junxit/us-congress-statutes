# Senate Concurrent Resolution 31

> adjournment

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Friday, the 3d day of November, 1939, and that when they adjourn on said day they stand adjourned sine die.


*Passed, November 3, 1939.*

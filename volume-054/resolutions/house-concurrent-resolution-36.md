# House Concurrent Resolution 36

> joint meeting

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Thursday, the 21st day of September 1939, at 2 o’clock in the afternoon, for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed, September 21, 1939.*

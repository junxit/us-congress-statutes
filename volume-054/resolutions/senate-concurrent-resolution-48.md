# Senate Concurrent Resolution 48

> public schools, d. c.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Clerk of the House of Representatives be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, in the enrollment of the bill (H. R. 9326) to provide educational employees of the public schools of the District of Columbia with leave of absence, with part pay, for purposes of educational improvement, and for other purposes, to make the following change, namely: On page 4, lines 8 and 9 of the engrossed bill, strike out “July 1, 1938” and insert “July 1, 1940”.


*Passed, June 7, 1940.*

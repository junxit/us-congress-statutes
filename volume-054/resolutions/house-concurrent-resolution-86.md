# House Concurrent Resolution 86

> recess of congress

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Thursday, July 11, 1940, they stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Monday, July 22, 1940.


*Passed, July 11, 1940.*

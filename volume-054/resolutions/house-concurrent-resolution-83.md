# House Concurrent Resolution 83

> recess of congress

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Saturday, June 22, 1940, they stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian Monday, July 1, 1940.


*Passed, June 22, 1940.*

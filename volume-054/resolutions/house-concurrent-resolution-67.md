# House Concurrent Resolution 67

> joint meeting

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Thursday, the 16th day of May 1940, at 1 o’clock postmeridian, for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed, May 16, 1940.*

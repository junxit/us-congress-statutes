# House Concurrent Resolution 58

> intermediate report of special committee to investigate thenational labor relations board

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed twenty-five thousand additional copies of House Report Numbered 1902, current Congress, entitled “Intermediate Report of Special Committee to Investigate the National Labor Relations Board”, of which three thousand copies shall be for the use of the Senate document room and twenty-two thousand copies shall be for the use of the House document room.


*Passed, April 11, 1940.*

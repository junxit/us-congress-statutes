# Senate Concurrent Resolution 50

> prison-made goods

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the action of the Speaker of the House of Representatives in signing the enrolled joint resolution (S. J. Res. 59) authorizing the Bureau of Labor Statistics to collect information as to amount and value of all goods produced in State and Federal prisons be, and it is hereby, rescinded; and be it further

That the Secretary of the Senate be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, in the reenrollment of the said joint resolution, to make the following change, namely: In line 15 of the engrossed joint resolution strike out “May 1, 1940” and insert “May 1, 1941”.


*Passed, June 11, 1940.*

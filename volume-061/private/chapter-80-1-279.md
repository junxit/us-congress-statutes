# Chapter 279

> AN ACT For the relief of Arsenio Acacio Lewis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Attorney General be, and he is hereby, directed to record the lawful admission for permanent residence of Arsenio Acacio Lewis as of June 21, 1945, the date on which he was temporarily admitted to the United States. Upon the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the Philippine quota of the first year that such quota is available.


*Approved July 21, 1947.*

# Chapter 362

> AN ACT For the relief of Julian M. Thomas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Julian M. Thomas, of Oakland, California, for legal services and expenses in connection with Pershing Hall, a memorial in Paris, France, the sum of $14,717.


*Approved July 30, 1947.*

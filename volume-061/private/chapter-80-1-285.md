# Chapter 285

> AN ACT For the relief of Anna Malama Mark.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Anna Malama Mark, of Honolulu, Territory of Hawaii, shall be considered to have been lawfully admitted on September 7, 1928, to the United States for permanent residence.


*Approved July 21, 1947.*

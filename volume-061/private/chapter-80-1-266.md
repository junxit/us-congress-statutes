# Chapter 266

> AN ACT For the relief of the alien Michael Soldo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, the alien Michael Soldo, of West Palm Beach, Florida, whose wife and minor child are citizens and residents of the United States, shall be considered to have been lawfully admitted, at Detroit, Michigan, on October 15, 1936, to the United States for permanent residence.


*Approved July 18, 1947.*

# Chapter 363

> AN ACT For the relief of William D. McCormick.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States o f America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration laws William D. McCormick shall, upon application at a port of entry into the United States, be admitted for permanent residence without an immigration visa, provided he meets all the other requirements of the immigration laws. Upon his admission into the United States, the Secretary of State shall deduct one number from the quota for India for the year in which the admission occurs or from the quota of the first available succeeding year.


*Approved July 30, 1947.*

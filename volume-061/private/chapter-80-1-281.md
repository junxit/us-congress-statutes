# Chapter 281

> AN ACT For the relief of Antonio Belaustegui.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws the Attorney General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to record the lawful admission for permanent residence of Antonio Belaustegui as of December 21, 1929, at New York City from the Steamship Cabo Sta. Maria, the date and place he entered the United States. Upon the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the Spanish quota of the first year that the Spanish quota is hereafter available.


*Approved July 21, 1947.*

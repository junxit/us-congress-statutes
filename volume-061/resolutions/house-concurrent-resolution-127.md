# House Concurrent Resolution 127

> ADJOURNMENT SINE DIE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of S. Con. Res. 33, Eightieth Congress, the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Friday, December 19, 1947, and that when they adjourn on said day, they stand adjourned sine die.


*Passed December 19, 1947.*

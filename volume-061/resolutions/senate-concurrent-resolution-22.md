# Senate Concurrent Resolution 22

> CONTROL OF DANGEROUS WEAPONS IN THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President of the United States be, and he is hereby, requested to return to the House of Representatives the enrolled bill (H. R. 493) to amend section 4 of the Act entitled “An Act to control the possession, sale, transfer, and use of pistols and other dangerous weapons in the District of Columbia”, approved July 8, 1932 (sec. 22, 3204 D. C. Code, 1940 edition); that if and when the said bill is returned by the President, the action of the Presiding Officers of the two Houses in signing the said bill be deemed to be rescinded; and that the House engrossed bill be returned to the Senate.


*Agreed to July 1, 1947.*

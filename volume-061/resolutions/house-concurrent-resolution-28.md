# House Concurrent Resolution 28

> JOINT MEETING

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Wednesday, March 12, 1947, at 1 o’clock post meridian, for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed March 10, 1947.*

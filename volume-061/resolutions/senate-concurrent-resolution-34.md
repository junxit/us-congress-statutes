# Senate Concurrent Resolution 34

> SIGNING OF ENROLLED BILLS, ETC.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That notwithstanding the adjournment of the two Houses until January 2, 1948, the Speaker of the House of Representatives and the President pro tempore of the Senate be, and they are hereby, authorized to sign enrolled bills and joint resolutions duly passed by the two Houses and found truly enrolled.


*Agreed to July 26, 1947.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 49

> REORGANIZATION PLAN NO. 2 OF 1947

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Congress does not favor the Reorganization Plan Numbered 2 of May 1, 1947, transmitted to Congress by the President on the 1st day of May 1947.


*Passed June 30, 1947.*

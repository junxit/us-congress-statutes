# Senate Concurrent Resolution 17

> STATUE OF THE LATE WILLIAM E. BORAH

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the statue of the late William E. Borah, presented by the State of Idaho, now in the Capitol Building, is accepted in the name of the United States, and that the thanks of Congress be tendered to the State for the contribution of the statue of one of its most eminent citizens, illustrious for his historic renown and distinguished civic services.

That a copy of these resolutions, suitably engrossed and duly authenticated, be transmitted to the Governor of Idaho.


*Agreed to June 6, 1947.*

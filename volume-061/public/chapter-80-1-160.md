# Chapter 160

> AN ACT To extend the period of validity of the Act to facilitate the admission into the United States of the alien fiancees or fiances of members of the armed forces of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the authority conferred upon the Secretary of State and the Attorney General under the provisions of the Act approved June 29, 1946 (60 Stat. 339), shall be extended to December 31, 1947, midnight.


*Approved June 28, 1947.*

# Chapter 104

> AN ACT To amend sections 3533 and 3536 of the Revised Statutes with respect to deviations in standard of ingots and weight of silver coins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3533 of the Revised Statutes (U. S. C., title 31, sec, 346) is amended by striking out the word “three-thousandths” and inserting in lieu thereof the word “six-thousandths”.

## § 2.

Section 3536 of the Revised Statutes, as amended (U. S. C., title 31, sec, 349), is amended to read as follows:

> “In adjusting the weight of silver coins the following deviations shall not be exceeded in any single piece: In the dollar, six grains; in the half-dollar, four grains; in the quarter-dollar, three grains; and in the dime, one and one-half grains.”


*Approved June 14, 1947.*

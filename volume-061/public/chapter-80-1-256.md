# Chapter 256

> AN ACT To repeal the Post Roads Act of 1866, as amended, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Post Roads Act of 1866, as amended (Revised Statutes, secs. 5263–5269, inclusive; U. S. C., title 47, secs. 1–6, inclusive, and 8), is hereby repealed.

## § 2.

Nothing in this Act shall limit the authority of the Federal Communications Commission under the provisions of the Communications Act of 1934, as amended, to prescribe charges, classifications, regulations, and practices, including priorities, applicable to Government communications.

## § 3.

This Act shall take effect on the tenth day following the enactment date thereof.


*Approved July 16, 1947.*

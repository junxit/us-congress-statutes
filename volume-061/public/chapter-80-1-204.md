# Chapter 204

> AN ACT To authorize the transfer to the Panama Canal of property which is surplus to the needs of the War Department or Navy Department.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the War Department and the Navy Department are authorized to transfer to the Panama Canal, regardless of present location and without charge to the Panama Canal, materials, supplies, tools, and equipment of every character, including structures, vessels, and floating equipment, which are surplus to the needs of the department having title thereto and which may be certified by the Governor of the Panama Canal as necessary for the care, maintenance, operation, improvement, sanitation, and government of the Panama Canal and Canal Zone.


*Approved July 2, 1947.*

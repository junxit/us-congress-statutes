# Chapter 152

> AN ACT To amend section 2 (a) of the National Housing Act, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2 (a) of the National Housing Act, as amended, is hereby amended by striking out in the first sentence “1947” and inserting “1949”.


*Approved June 26, 1947.*

# Chapter 98

> AN ACT To amend section 3539 of the Revised Statutes, relating to taking trial pieces of coins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate House of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3539 of the Revised Statutes, as amended (U. S. C., title 31, sec. 352), is amended by striking out the word “two” wherever it appears therein and inserting in lieu thereof the word “ten”.


*Approved June 5, 1947.*

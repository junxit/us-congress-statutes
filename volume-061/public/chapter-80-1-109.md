# Chapter 109

> AN ACT To amend section 17 of the Pay Readjustment Act of 1942, so as to increase the pay of cadets and midshipmen at the service academies, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section of the Pay Readjustment Act of 1942 (66 Stat. 368; 37 U. S. G. 117), is hereby amended by striking therefrom the figures “$780” and substituting therefor the figures “$936”.

## § 2.

The increases in pay provided by this Act shall become effective on the first day of the first month following its enactment, and no increase in pay for any period prior thereto shall accrue by reason of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved June 20, 1947.*

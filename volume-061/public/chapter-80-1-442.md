# Chapter 442

> AN ACT To amend the District of Columbia rent control law so as to provide that schools and universities may recover possession of housing accommodations in certain cases.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (b) of section 5 of the Act entitled “An Act to regulate rents in the District of Columbia, and for other purposes”, approved December 2, 1941, as amended, is amended by (1) striking out the period at the end of paragraph (5) and inserting in lieu thereof a comma and the word “or”, and (2) adding at the end thereof a new paragraph as follows:

> - **(6)** The landlord, being a recognized school or an accredited nonprofit university, has a bona fide need for the premises for educational, research, administrative, or dormitory use.”


*Approved August 1, 1947.*

# Chapter 483

> JOINT RESOLUTION Fixing the date of meeting of the second regular session of the Eightieth Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate House of Representatives of the United, States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second regular session of the Eightieth Congress shall begin at noon on Tuesday, January 6, 1948.


*Approved August 4, 1947.*

# Chapter 63

> AN ACT Authorizing and directing the removal of stone piers in West Executive Avenue between the grounds of the White House and the Department of State Building.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Director of the National Park Service is hereby authorized and directed to remove the stone piers from West Executive Avenue between the grounds of the White House and the Department of State Building.


*Approved May 15, 1947.*

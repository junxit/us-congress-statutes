# Chapter 429

> AN ACT To extend for three months the provisions of the District of Columbia Emergency Rent Act, approved December 2, 1941, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1 (b) of the Act entitled “An Act to regulate rents in the District of Columbia, and for other purposes”, approved December 2, 1941, as amended (D. C. Code, 1940 edition, sec. 45–1601), is hereby amended by striking out “December 31, 1947” and inserting in lieu thereof “March 31, 1948”.


*Approved August 1, 1947.*

# Chapter 419

> AN ACT To authorize the Secretary of the Navy to establish a postgraduate school at Monterey, California.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy is authorized to acquire six hundred and six and five hundred and ninety-two one-thousandths acres of land upon which the United States of America now has an option with buildings thereon at Monterey, California, for the establishment of a naval postgraduate school, including the necessary construction and alterations to provide school facilities, quarters, and collateral facilities and equipment, including the acquisition of the necessary land, at a cost not to exceed $2,500,000: Provided, That contracts may be entered into without regard to the provisions of 3709 Revised Statutes.


*Approved July 31, 1947.*

# Chapter 236

> AN ACT Declaring Kenduskeag Stream, Penobscot County, Maine, to be a nonnavigable waterway.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United, States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, the Kenduskeag Stream, a minor tributary of the Penobscot River, located in Penobscot County, in the State of Maine, be, and the same is hereby, declared to be a nonnavigable waterway within the meaning of the Constitution and laws of the United States of America.

## § 2.

That the right of Congress to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved July 11, 1947.*

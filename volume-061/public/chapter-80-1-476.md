# Chapter 476

> AN ACT To amend the Act entitled “An Act for the retirement of public school teachers in the District of Columbia”, approved August 7, 1946.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 7 of the Act entitled “An Act for the retirement of public school teachers in the District of Columbia”, approved August 7, 1946, be, and the same hereby is, amended by striking out the word “twenty” and inserting in lieu thereof the word “fifty”.


*Approved August 4, 1947.*

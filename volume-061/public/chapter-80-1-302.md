# Chapter 302

> AN ACT To extend temporarily the time for filing applications for patents and for taking action in the United States Patent Office with respect thereto.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the period of extension of priority rights under section 1 of Public Law 690, Seventy-ninth Congress, approved August 8, 1946, and the time for the payment of any fee or the taking of any other action under section 3 of said Act, specified as expiring twelve months after the passage of that Act, shall be further extended to a date not later than February 29, 1948, in favor of citizens of the United States and citizens or subjects of countries which grant or shall grant before February 29, 1948, substantially reciprocal privileges to citizens of the United States for such extended term, subject to the provisions of said Public Law 690.


*Approved July 23, 1947.*

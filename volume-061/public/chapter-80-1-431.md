# Chapter 431

> AN ACT To amend the Federal Home Loan Bank Act, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (b) of section 10 of the Federal Home Loan Bank Act is hereby amended by striking the words “twenty years” appearing in the first sentence thereof, and inserting in lieu thereof the words “twenty-five years”.


*Approved August 1, 1947.*

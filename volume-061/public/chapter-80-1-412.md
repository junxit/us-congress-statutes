# Chapter 412

> An Act To amend title 1 of the Act entitled “An Act to provide for research into basic laws and principles relating to agriculture and to provide for the further development of cooperative agricultural extension work and the more complete endowment and support of land-grant colleges”, approved June 29, 1935 (the Bank-head-Jones Act).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 11 of title I of the Act entitled “An Act to provide for research into basic laws and principles relating to agriculture and to provide for the further development of cooperative agricultural extension work and the more complete endowment and support of land-grant colleges”, approved June 29, 1935 (the Bank-head-Jones Act), which was added by Public Law 733, Seventy-ninth Congress, is amended by striking out the words “authorized to be” wherever they appear in that section.


*Approved July 31, 1947.*

# Chapter 27

> JOINT RESOLUTION Authorizing the Commandant of the United States Coast Guard to waive compliance with the navigation and vessel-inspection laws administered by the Coast Guard.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That effective April 1, 1947, the Commandant, United States Coast Guard, is authorized to waive compliance with the navigation and vessel-inspection laws administered by the Coast Guard to the extent and in such manner and upon such terms as may be deemed necessary by him in the orderly reconversion of the merchant marine from wartime to peacetime operations.

## § 2.

The authority granted by this resolution shall remain in force only until April 1, 1948: Provided, That after June 1, 1947,the Commandant shall not waive compliance with those sections of the navigation and vessel-inspection laws requiring the employment of American citizens as officers and crew members and limiting the employment of aliens except insofar as such employment shall be in the steward’s department of vessels authorized to carry in excess of twelve passengers.


*Approved March 31, 1947.*

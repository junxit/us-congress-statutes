# Chapter 45

> AN ACT To suspend certain import taxes on copper.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the import tax imposed under section 3425 of the Internal Revenue Code shall not apply with respect to articles (other than copper sulphate) entered for consumption or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption during the period beginning with the day following the date of the enactment of this Act and ending with the close of March 31, 1949.


*Approved April 29, 1947.*

# Chapter 349

> AN ACT To authorize the issuance of a special series of commemorative stamps in honor of Gold Star Mothers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General is authorized and directed to prepare for issuance at as early a date as practicable, a special series of 3-cent postage stamps, of such design as he shall prescribe, in honor and commemoration of Gold Star Mothers.


*Approved July 30, 1947.*

# Private Law 100–7

> An Act For the relief of Helen Ying-Yu Lin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purpose of sections 203(a)(1) and 204 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Helen Ying-Yu Lin shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Gerald Christensen, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents or brothers or sisters of the beneficiary shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved December 23, 1987.*

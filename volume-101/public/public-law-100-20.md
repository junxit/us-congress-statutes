# Public Law 100–20

> An Act Making technical corrections relating to the Federal Employees’ Retirement System.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** section 302(a)(1)(D)(II) of the Federal Employees’ Retirement System Act of 1986 (Public Law 99–335; 100 Stat. 602) is amended by striking out “8442(b)(1)(B)” and inserting in lieu thereof “8442(b)(1), 8443(a)(1),”.
- **(b)** The first sentence of section 8432(b)(4)(A) of title 5, United States Code, is amended to read as follows: “
  > Notwithstanding paragraph (2)(A), an employee or Member who is an employee or Member on January 1, 1987, and continues as an employee or Member without a break in service through April 1, 1987, may make the first election for the purpose of subsection (a) during the election period prescribed for such purpose by the Executive Director.”.


*Approved April 7, 1987.*

# Public Law 100–100

> An Act To amend the Farm Disaster Assistance Act of 1987 to extend the reporting date for the ethanol cost effectiveness study.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. ETHANOL COST-EFFECTIVENESS STUDY.

- **(a)** In General.—Section 13(d) of the Farm Disaster Assistance Act of 1987 (Public Law 100–45; 101 Stat. 324) is amended by striking out “90 days” and inserting in lieu thereof “180 days”.
- **(b)** *Effective Date.—* The amendment made by subsection (a) shall become effective on May 27, 1987.


*Approved August 18, 1987.*

# Public Law 100–217

> An Act To amend the Export-Import Bank Act of 1945.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 15(c)(2) of the Export-Import Bank Act of 1945 (22 U.S.C. 635i–3(c)(2)) is amended by striking out “during fiscal year 1986” and inserting in lieu thereof “during fiscal years 1986, 1987, and 1988”.


*Approved December 29, 1987.*

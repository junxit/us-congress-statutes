# Public Law 100–1

> Joint Resolution Extending the time within which the President may transmit the Economic Report to the Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions of section 3 of the Act of February 20, 1946, as amended (15 U.S.C. 1022), the President shall transmit to the Congress not later than January 30, 1987, the Economic Report.


*Approved January 28, 1987.*

# Public Law 100–152

> An Act To designate the segment of Corridor V in the State of Alabama as the Robert E. (Bob) Jones, Jr. Highway.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The segment of the highway on the Appalachian development highway system known as “Corridor V” which is within the boundaries of the State of Alabama shall hereafter be known and designated as the “Robert E. (Bob) Jones, Jr. Highway”.

## § 2. REFERENCE.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, or other paper of the United States to the highway referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Robert E. (Bob) Jones, Jr. Highway”.


*Approved November 3, 1987.*

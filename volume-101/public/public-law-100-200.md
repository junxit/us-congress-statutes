# Public Law 100–200

> Joint Resolution To provide for the temporary extension of certain programs relating to housing and community development, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That each provision of law amended by Public Law 100–179 is amended by striking “December 16, 1987” each place it appears and inserting “March 15, 1988”.

# Public Law 100–80

> An Act To provide for a temporary extension of the public debt limit.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** subsection (a) of the first section of the Act of May 15, 1987, entitled “An Act to provide for a temporary increase in the public debt limit” (Public Law 100–40) is amended by striking out “July 17, 1987” and inserting in lieu thereof “August 6, 1987”.
- **(b)** The amendment made by subsection (a) shall take effect on the date of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved July 30, 1987.*

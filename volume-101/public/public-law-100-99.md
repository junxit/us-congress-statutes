# Public Law 100–99

> An Act To extend certain protections under title 11 of the United States Code, the Bankruptcy Code.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Public Law 100–41 is amended by striking out “September 15, 1987” and inserting in lieu thereof “October 15, 1987”.


*Approved August 18, 1987.*

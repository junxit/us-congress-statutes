# Public Law 100–186

> An Act To designate “The Stars and Stripes Forever” as the national march of the United States of America.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the composition by John Philip Sousa entitled “The Stars and Stripes Forever” is hereby designated as the national march of the United States of America.


*Approved December 11, 1987.*

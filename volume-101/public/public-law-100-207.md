# Public Law 100–207

> An Act To designate the Federal building located at 600 West Madison, Chicago, Illinois, as the “Harold Washington Social Security Center”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The Federal building known as the “Great Lakes Program Service Center” and located at 600 West Madison, Chicago, Illinois, shall be known and designated as the “Harold Washington Social Security Center”.

## § 2. LEGAL REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to the Federal building referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Harold Washington Social Security Center”.


*Approved December 23, 1987.*

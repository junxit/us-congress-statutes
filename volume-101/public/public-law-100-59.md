# Public Law 100–59

> An Act To designate the Federal Building located at 10 Causeway Street, Boston. Massachusetts, as the “Thomas P. O’Neill. Jr.. Federal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION OF BUILDING.

The Federal Building located at 10 Causeway Street, Boston, Massachusetts, shall be known and designated as the “Thomas P. O’Neill, Jr., Federal Building”.

## § 2. LEGAL REFERENCES.

Any reference to such building in any law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Thomas P. O’Neill, Jr., Federal Building”.


*Approved June 29, 1987.*

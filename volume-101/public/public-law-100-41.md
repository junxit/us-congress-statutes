# Public Law 100–41

> An Act To extend certain protections under title 11 of the United States Code, the Bankruptcy Code.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 608(a) of Public Law 99–591 (100 Stat. 3341–74), and section 2(a) of Public Law 99–656 (100 Stat. 3668) are amended by striking out “May 15, 1987” each place it appears and inserting in lieu thereof “September 15, 1987”.


*Approved May 15, 1987.*

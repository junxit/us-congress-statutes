# Public Law 100–197

> Joint Resolution Making further continuing appropriations for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1988, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 102(c) of Public Law 100–120, as amended by Public Law 100–162 and Public Law 100–193 is further amended by striking out “December 18, 1987” and inserting in lieu thereof “December 21, 1987”.

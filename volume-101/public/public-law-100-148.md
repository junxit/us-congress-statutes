# Public Law 100–148

> An Act To extend the Aviation Insurance Program for five years.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1312 of the Federal Aviation Act of 1958 (49 App. U.S.C. 1542) is amended by striking “1987” and inserting in lieu thereof “1992”.


*Approved October 30, 1987.*

# Public Law 100–30

> Joint Resolution To authorize and request the President to issue a proclamation designating April 26, through May 2, 1987, as “National Organ and Tissue Donor Awareness Week”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating April 26, through May 2, 1987 as “National Organ and Tissue Donor Awareness Week”.


*Approved April 29, 1987.*

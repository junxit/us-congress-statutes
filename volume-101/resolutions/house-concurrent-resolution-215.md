# House Concurrent Resolution 215

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Tuesday, November 10, 1987, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian on Monday, November 16, 1987.


*Agreed to November 10, 1987.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 154

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Wednesday, July 1, 1987, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian on Tuesday, July 7, 1987, and that when the Senate recesses on Wednesday, July 1, 1987, or Thursday, July 2, 1987, pursuant to a motion made by the Majority Leader, or his designee, in accordance with this resolution, it stand in recess until 10 o’clock ante meridiem on Tuesday, July 7, 1987.


*Agreed to July 1, 1987.*

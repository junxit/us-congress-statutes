# House Concurrent Resolution 127

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Thursday, May 21, 1987, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian on Wednesday, May 27, 1987, and that when the Senate adjourns on Thursday, May 21, 1987, it stand adjourned until 11 o’clock ante meridiem on Wednesday, May 27, 1987.


*Agreed to May 21, 1987.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 177

> “GUIDE TO RECORDS OF THE UNITED STATES HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AT THE NATIONAL ARCHIVES, 1789–1989: BICENTENNIAL EDITION”—HOUSE PRINT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the compilation of materials entitled “Guide to Records of the United States House of Representatives at the National Archives, 1789–1989: Bicentennial Edition” (prepared under the supervision of the Commission on the United States House of Representatives Bicentenary, with the editorial assistance of the Historian of the House of Representatives) shall be printed as a House document, with illustrations and suitable binding. In addition to the usual number, two thousand copies of such document shall be printed for the use of the Office for the Bicentennial of the House of Representatives.


*Agreed to November 4, 1987.*

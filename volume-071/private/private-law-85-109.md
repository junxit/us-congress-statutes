# Private Law 85–109

> AN ACT For the relief of Cbrisoula Antonios Chegaras.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A.) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Chrisoula Antonios Chegaras shall be held and considered to be the natural-born minor alien child of Antonios Chegaras, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 7, 1957.*

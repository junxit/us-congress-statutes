# Private Law 85–23

> AN ACT For the relief of Jew Gim Gee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Jew Gim Gee, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Jew Suey Thit, a United States citizen.


*Approved May 31, 1957.*

# Private Law 85–277

> AN ACT For the relief of John Panagiotou.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Alexander John Panagiotou shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Nick J. Dennery, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 31, 1957.*

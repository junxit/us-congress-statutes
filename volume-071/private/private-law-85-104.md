# Private Law 85–104

> AN ACT For the relief of Sheu Shei Lan and Chow Shong Yep.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of sections 201 (a) and 202 (b) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Sheu Shei Lan and Chow Shong Yep shall be deemed chargeable to the quota for the Asia Pacific Triangle.


*Approved July 26, 1957.*

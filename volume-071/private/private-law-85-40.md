# Private Law 85–40

> JOINT RESOLUTION To waive the provision of section 212 (a) (9) of the Immigration and Nationality Act in behalf of certain aliens.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of section 212 (a) (9) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Rose Maria Pucillo, Bertha Gabriel Y. Martinez Gomez, Francesco Pizzo, Bronislaw (John) Waz, Mrs. Elizabeth Helene Carter, George Liberates (Lymperatos), Hildegard Kaufmann, Mrs. Helene Dimitrowa Shiver, Berthilde Vogler Ganno, Herta Friedrichs Rockwood, Luis Torres, Ursula Anna Peterich von Rekowsky, Antonio Contreras, Francesco Di Ixirenzo, Beniamino Rocco Giordano, and Donato Severino Spensieri may be issued visas and admitted to the United States for permanent residence if they are found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act: Provided, That these exemptions shall apply only to grounds for exclusion of which the Department of State or the Department of Justice had knowledge prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved June 27, 1957.*

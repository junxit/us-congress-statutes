# Private Law 85–260

> AN ACT For the relief of Maria West.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Maria West, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Victor L. West, Junior, and Callie S. West, citizens of the United States.


*Approved August 30, 1957.*

# Private Law 85–67

> AN ACT For the relief of Z. A. Hardee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding any period of limitations or lapse of time, claims for credit or refund of overpayments of income taxes for the taxable years 1945 through 1948 made by Z. A. Hardee, of Enfield, North Carolina, may be filed at any time within one year after the date of the enactment of this Act. The provisions of sections 322 (b) 3774, and 3775 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1939 shall not apply to the refund or credit of any overpayment of tax for which a claim for credit or refund is filed under the authority of this Act within such one-year period.


*Approved July 18, 1957.*

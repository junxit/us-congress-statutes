# Private Law 85–78

> AN ACT For the relief of Miyako Ueda Osgood.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Miyako Ueda Osgood, widow of John David Osgood, a deceased United States citizen who served honorably in the Armed Forces of the United States, shall be deemed to be a nonquota immigrant.


*Approved July 26, 1957.*

# Private Law 85–269

> AN ACT For the relief of Victoria Galea.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, section 202 (c) (1) shall not be applicable in connection with the application for an immigrant visa by Victoria Galea.


*Approved August 30, 1957.*

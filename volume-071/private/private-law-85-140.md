# Private Law 85–140

> AN ACT For the relief of Poppy Catherine Hayakawa Merritt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, paragraph (5) of subsection (a) and subsection (b) of section 202 of such Act shall not apply in the case of Poppy Catherine Hayakawa Merritt.


*Approved August 14, 1957.*

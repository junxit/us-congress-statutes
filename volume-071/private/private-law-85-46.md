# Private Law 85–46

> AN ACT For the relief of Elda Mondillo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 1111 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act. Elda Mondillo, shall be held and considered to be the minor alien child of Mr. Giovanni Mondillo, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved June 27, 1957.*

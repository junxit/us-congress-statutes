# Private Law 85–15

> AN ACT For the relief of Nicola Marcello.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Nicola Marcello shall be classifiable as a returning resident alien as defined in section 101 (a) (27) (B) of that Act.


*Approved May 16, 1957.*

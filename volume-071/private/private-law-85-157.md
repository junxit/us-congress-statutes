# Private Law 85–157

> AN ACT For the relief of Yee Suey Nong.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 101 (a) (27) (B) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Yee Suey Nong shall be held and considered to be a returning resident alien.


*Approved August 14, 1957.*

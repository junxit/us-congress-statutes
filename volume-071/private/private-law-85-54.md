# Private Law 85–54

> AN ACT For the relief of Loutfie Kalil Noma (also known as Louttie Slemon Noma or Loutfie Noama ).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Ixmttie Kalil Noma (also known as I .out tie Slemon Noma or Loutfie Noama) shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fee. Upon the granting of permanent residence to such alien as provided for in this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the appropriate quota for the first year that such quota is available: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the said Act.


*Approved July 17, 1957.*

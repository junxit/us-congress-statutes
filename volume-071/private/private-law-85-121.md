# Private Law 85–121

> AN ACT For the relief of Leona C. Nash.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of all laws administered by the Veterans’ Administration, Leona C. Nash shall be held and considered to have validly married George J. Nash (Veterans’ Administration claim numbered XC 1342436).


*Approved August 14, 1957.*

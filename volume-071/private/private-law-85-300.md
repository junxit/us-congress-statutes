# Private Law 85–300

> AN ACT For the relief of Pedro Gonzales.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Pedro Gonzales shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence on June 15, 1935.


*Approved September 2, 1957.*

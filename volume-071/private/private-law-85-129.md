# Private Law 85–129

> AN ACT For the relief of Seol Bong Ryu.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Seol Bong Ryu, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Brooks Doran and Violet Risley Anderson, citizens of the United States.


*Approved August 14, 1957.*

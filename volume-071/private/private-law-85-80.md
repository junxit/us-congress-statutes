# Private Law 85–80

> AN ACT For the relief of Noeml Maria Vida Williams and Maria Loretta Vida.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of sections 212 (a) (9) and ( 12) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Noemi Maria Vida Williams may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of such Act: Provided, That this exemption shall apply only to a ground for exclusion of which the Department of Justice or the Department of State has knowledge prior to the enactment of this Act.

## § 2.

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Maria Loretta Vida shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act upon payment of the required visa fee.


*Approved July 26, 1957.*

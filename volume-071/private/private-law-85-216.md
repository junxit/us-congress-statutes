# Private Law 85–216

> AN ACT For the relief of Pasquale Pratola.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203 (a) (3) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Pasquale Pratola, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Otantonio Pratola, a legally resident alien of the United States.


*Approved August 28, 1957.*

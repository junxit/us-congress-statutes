# Private Law 85–324

> AN ACT For the relief of Delfina Cinco de Lopez.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of subsection (a) of section 201 and subsection (b) of section 202 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Delfina Cinco de Lopez shall be classified as an immigrant under the provisions of section 101 (a) (27) (C) of that Act.


*Approved September 4, 1957.*

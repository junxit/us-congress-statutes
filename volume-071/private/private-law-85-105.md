# Private Law 85–105

> AN ACT For the relief of Janos Schreiner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 101 (b) (2) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Janos Schreiner shall be held and considered to be the natural father of Miss Maria Lukats, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved July 26, 1957.*

# Private Law 85–89

> AN ACT For the relief of Georgina Mercedes Liera.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Georgina Mercedes Liera shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted (o the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fee.


*Approved July 26, 1957.*

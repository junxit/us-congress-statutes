# Private Law 85–209

> AN ACT For the relief of Dezrin Boswell (also known as Dezrin Boswell Johnson).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and ationality Act, Dezrin Boswell (also known as Dezrin Boswell Johnson) shall be held, and considered to be the natural-born minor alien child of Mrs. Martha Allen, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 28, 1957.*

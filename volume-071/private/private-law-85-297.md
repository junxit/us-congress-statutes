# Private Law 85–297

> AN ACT For the relief of Westfedlt Brothers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Westfeldt Brothers, of New Orleans, Louisiana, is hereby relieved of liability to pay to the United States the amount of $540,540.00 erroneously levied as increased customs duty on account of merchandise imported by them, because of a clerical error in the entry of such merchandise (New Orleans warehouse entry numbered 676 of October 3, 1945), such error being perpetuated by an appraisement based thereon which became conclusive with respect to all parties upon the lapse of sixty days after the date of such appraisement.


*Approved September 2, 1957.*

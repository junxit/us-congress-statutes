# Private Law 85–44

> AN ACT For the relief of Jeffrey Charles Medworth.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Jeffrey Charles Medworth, a British subject who was born in India, shall be deemed to have been born in Great Britain.


*Approved June 27, 1957.*

# Private Law 85–245

> AN ACT For the relief of Marjeta Winkle Brown.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Marjeta Winkle Brown, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Harold Darrel Brown, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 30, 1957.*

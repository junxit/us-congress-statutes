# Private Law 85–251

> AN ACT For the relief of Hideko Takiguchi Pulaski.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Hideko Takiguchi Pulaski, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Sergeant First Class John Pulaski, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 30, 1957.*

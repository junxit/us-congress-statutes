# Private Law 85–122

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Jennie B. Prescott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the one-year time limitation upon the filing of applications for waiver of premiums contained in the last proviso of the fourth sentence of section 602 (n) of the National Service Life Insurance Act of 1940 is hereby waived with respect to any application for waiver of premiums on national service life insurance policy numbered N–17603908 (issued on the life of Robert E. Prescott, Veterans’ Administration claim numbered XC–9016903) filed by Mrs. Jennie B. Prescott within the one-year period which begins on the date of enactment of this Act. Any payments made pursuant to such application shall be paid from the national service life insurance appropriation.


*Approved August 14, 1957.*

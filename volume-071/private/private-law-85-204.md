# Private Law 85–204

> AN ACT For the relief of Sylvia Ottila Tenyi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203 (a) (3) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Sylvia Ottila Tenyi shall be held and considered to be the child of Irene Tenyi Petercsak, a lawfully resident alien of the United States.


*Approved August 28, 1957.*

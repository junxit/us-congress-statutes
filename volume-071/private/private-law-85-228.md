# Private Law 85–228

> AN ACT For the relief of Angela Ferrini.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 101 (b) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Angela Ferrini shall be held and considered to be within the purview of section 101 (a) (27) (A) of the said Act.


*Approved August 29, 1957.*

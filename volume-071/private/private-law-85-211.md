# Private Law 85–211

> AN ACT For the relief of Mary Hummel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mary Hummel shall be held and considered to have been born in Great Britain.


*Approved August 28, 1957.*

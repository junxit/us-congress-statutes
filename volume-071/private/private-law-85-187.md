# Private Law 85–187

> AN ACT For the relief of Edward J. Moskot.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Edward J. Moskot, Captain, United States Army, retired (serial number 0499596), is relieved of all liability to the United States (1) for any unpaid balance of amounts paid to him as salary during the period February 9, 1954 to October 21, 1954, and from November 1, 1954, to November 15, 1954, inclusive, by the Anniston Ordnance Depot Officer’s Open Mess, Anniston, Alabama, a nonappropriated fund activity of the United States Army, and (2) any amounts paid to him as retired pay from the United States Army for the period for which he received a salary from such officer’s open mess.

## § 2.

In the audit and settlement of the accounts of any certifying or disbursing officer of the United States full credit shall be given for the amounts for which liability is relieved by this Act.


*Approved August 22, 1957.*

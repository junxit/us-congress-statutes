# Private Law 85–207

> AN ACT For the relief of Maria Sausa and Gregorio Sausa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor children, Maria Sausa and Gregorio Sausa, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien children of Mr. and Mrs. Pedro L. Porticos, citizens of the United States.


*Approved August 28, 1957.*

# Private Law 85–152

> AN ACT For the relief of Francesca Maria Arria.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203 (a) (3) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Francesca Maria Arria shall be held and considered to be the minor natural-born child of Mrs. Maria Arria, an alien lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence.


*Approved August 14, 1957.*

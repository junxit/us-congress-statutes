# Private Law 85–212

> AN ACT For the relief of Gordon Broderick.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Gordon Broderick shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of March 22, 1938.


*Approved August 28, 1957.*

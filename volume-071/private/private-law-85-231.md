# Private Law 85–231

> AN ACT For the relief of Sic Gun Chau (Tse) and Hing Man Chau.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of paragraph (6) of section 212 (a) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Sic Gun Chau (Tse) and Hing Man Chau may, if they are found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of such Act, be issued visas and be admitted to the United States for permanent residence under such conditions and controls as the Attorney General, after consultation with the Surgeon General of the United States Public Health Service, Department of Health, Education, and Welfare, deems necessary to impose. A suitable or £ roper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, shall 5 given by or on behalf of the said Sic Gun Chau (Tse) and Hing Man Chau in the same manner and subject to the same conditions as bonds or undertakings given under section 213 of such Act. This Act shall apply only to grounds for exclusion under paragraph (6) of section 212 (a) of such Act known to the Secretary of State or the Attorney General prior to the date of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved August 29, 1957.*

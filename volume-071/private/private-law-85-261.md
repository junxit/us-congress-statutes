# Private Law 85–261

> AN ACT For the relief of Bella Rodriguez Ternoir.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 212 (a) (12) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Belia Rodriguez Ternoir, wife of an American citizen, may be issued a visa and admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act: Provided, That the exemption provided for in this Act shall apply only to the grounds for exclusion of which the Department of State or the Department of Justice had knowledge prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved August 30, 1957.*

# Private Law 85–179

> AN ACT For the relief of Donald F. Thompson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the accounts of Donald F. Thompson, of Knoxville, Maryland, in the amount of $223.08. Such sum represents the overpayment of accrued leave after serving in the Armed Forces in Korea in 1951.


*Approved August 21, 1957.*

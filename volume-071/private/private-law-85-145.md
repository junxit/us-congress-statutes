# Private Law 85–145

> AN ACT For the relief of Panagiotis Tullos.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions of section 212 (a) (1) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Panagiotis Tulios, may be issued a visa and admitted to the United States if he is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of said Act: Provided further, That this exemption shall apply only to a ground for exclusion of which the Department of State or the Department of Justice has knowledge prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved August 14, 1957.*

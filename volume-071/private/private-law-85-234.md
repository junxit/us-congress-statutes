# Private Law 85–234

> AN ACT For the relief of Phillis Guyadeen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, sections 202 (a) (5) and 202 (b) (4) of that Act shall not be applicable in connection with the application for an immigrant visa by Phillis Guyadeen.


*Approved August 29, 1957.*

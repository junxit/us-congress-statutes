# Private Law 85–70

> AN ACT For the relief of Alessandro Renda.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Alessandro Renda shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fee. Upon the granting of permanent residence to such alien as provided for in this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the appropriate quota for the first year that such quota is available. A suitable or proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, snail be given by or on behalf of the said Alessandro Renda in the same manner and subject to the same conditions as bonds or undertakings given under section 213 of such Act.


*Approved July 26, 1957.*

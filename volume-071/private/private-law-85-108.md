# Private Law 85–108

> AN ACT For the relief of Maria Parisi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Maria Parisi shall be held and considered to be the minor alien child of Antonio Parisi, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 7, 1957.*

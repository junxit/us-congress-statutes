# Private Law 85–21

> AN ACT For the relief of Lee Chong Talk.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 101 (a) (27) (A) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Lee Chong Taik, shall be held and considered the natural-born alien child of Air. and Airs. Otto V. Byhre, citizens of the United States.


*Approved May 31, 1957.*

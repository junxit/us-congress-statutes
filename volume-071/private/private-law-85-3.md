# Private Law 85–3

> AN ACT For the relief of Duk Chang Cho.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Duk Chang Cho, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Master Sergeant and Mrs. Francis M. Bradshaw, citizens of the United States.


*Approved April 23, 1957.*

# Private Law 85–81

> AN ACT For the relief of Maria Caccomo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203 (a) (3) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Maria Caccomo shall be held and considered to be the minor child of Salvatore Caccomo, a lawful permanent resident of the United States.


*Approved July 26, 1957.*

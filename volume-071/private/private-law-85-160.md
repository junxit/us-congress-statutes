# Private Law 85–160

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Fusako Takai and Thomas Takai.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Fusako Takai and Thomas Takai shall be held and considered to be nonquota immigrants.


*Approved August 14, 1957.*

# Private Law 85–167

> AN ACT For the relief of Ralph Landolfi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the one-year limitation of time contained in the first proviso of section 303 (d) (1) of the Federal Employees’ Compensation Act Amendments of 1049 (63 Stat. 867) is hereby waived in favor of Ralph Landolfi, East Orange, New Jersey, with respect to his claim for compensation under the provisions of the Federal Employees’ Compensation Act for injuries alleged to have been sustained on June 17, 1949, while in the performance of his duties as acting assistant superintendent at the United States post office, Roseville Station. Newark, New Jersey, if he files a claim for such compensation with the Bureau of Employees’ Compensation, Department of Labor, within sixty days after the date of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved August 16, 1957.*

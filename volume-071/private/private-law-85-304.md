# Private Law 85–304

> AN ACT For the relief of Gilbert B. Mar.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Gilbert B. Mar snail be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of September 22, 1948.


*Approved September 4, 1957.*

# Private Law 85–72

> AN ACT For the relief of John G. Michael.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That John G. Michael, who lost United States citizenship under the provisions of section 404 (b) of the Nationality Act of 1940, may be naturalized by taking, prior to one year after the date of the enactment of this Act, before any court referred to in subsection (a) of section 310 of the Immigration and Nationality Act or before any diplomatic or consular officer of the United States abroad, an oath as prescribed by section 337 of such Act From and after naturalization under this Act, the said John G. Michael shall have the same citizenship status as that which existed immediately prior to its loss.


*Approved July 26, 1957.*

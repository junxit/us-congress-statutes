# Private Law 85–292

> AN ACT For the relief of Sandra Ann Scott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Sandra Ann Scott, shall be held and considered to be the naturalbom alien child of David W. Scott, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved September 2, 1957.*

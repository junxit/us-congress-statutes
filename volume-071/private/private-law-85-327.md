# Private Law 85–327

> AN ACT For the relief of Letizia Maria Arini.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of paragraphs (9) and (12) of section 212 (a) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Letizia Maria Arini may be issued a visa and be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of such Act. This Act shall apply only to grounds for exclusion under such paragraphs known to the Secretary of State or the Attorney General prior to the date of the enactment of this Act. For the relief of Sherwood Lloyd Pierce.


*Approved September 4, 1957.*

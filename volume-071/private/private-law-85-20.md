# Private Law 85–20

> AN ACT For the relief of Mary Bar me and her two minor children Steran Barme and Dinah Barme.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Alary Barme and her two minor children, Steran Barme and Dinah Barme, shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fees.


*Approved May 31, 1957.*

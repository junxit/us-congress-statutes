# Private Law 85–162

> AN ACT For the relief of William H. Barney.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That William H. Barney, of Los Angeles, California, is hereby relieved of all liability to the United States arising out of his failure to perform Navy Contract Numbered NOy–22897 (specification 25380; structural alterations, Building Numbered 2–69 and vault, Building Numbered 2–68, United States Naval Construction Battalion Center, Port Hueneme, California). The said William II. Barney submitted his bid on October 24, 1950, and accepted notice to proceed on November 6, 1950, but he did not sign the contract, or enter upon its performance, on account of certain previously unforeseen costs of performance which would have caused him irretrievable financial loss.


*Approved August 14, 1957.*

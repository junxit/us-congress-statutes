# Private Law 85–10

> AN ACT For the relief of Herbert James Bramley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstand-ing the provision of section 212 (a) (6) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the alien, Herbert James Bramley, may be granted a visa and be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if he is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act and upon compliance with such conditions and controls which the Attorney General, after consultation with the Surgeon General of the United States Public Health Service, Department of Health, Education. and Welfare, may deem necessary to impose: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the said Act.


*Approved April 25, 1957.*

# Private Law 85–7

> AN ACT For the relief of Victor Charles Hunt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Attorney General is authorized and directed to discontinue any deportation proceedings and to cancel any outstanding order and warrant of deportation, warrant of arrest, and bend, which may have been issued in the case of Victor Charles Hunt. From and after the date of enactment of this Act, the said Victor Charles Hunt shall not again be subject to deportation by reason of the same facts upon which such deportation proceedings were commenced or any such warrants and order have issued.


*Approved April 25, 1957.*

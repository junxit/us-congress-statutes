# Private Law 85–97

> AN ACT For the relief of Yun Wha Yoon Holsman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Yim Wha Yoon Holsman shall be held and con- sidered to be the natural-born alien child of Noel and Helen Holsman, citizens of the United States.


*Approved July 26, 1957.*

# Private Law 85–34

> AN ACT For the relief of Lydia Anne Foote.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That any period of time in which Lydia Anne Foote may reside in France within five years after the date of enactment of this Act shall not be deemed to be residence in a foreign state within the meaning of section 352 (a) (1) of the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved June 13, 1957.*

# Private Law 85–217

> AN ACT To clear the title to certain Indian land.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States hereby disclaims on behalf of itself and any Indian allottee, or his heirs or devisees, any interest in the eighty-six and eight one-hundredths acres of land in Miami Comity, Kansas, the title to which was quieted by judgment of the district court of Miami County, Kansas, in the case of Rutherford and others against Wah-Pon-Ge-Quah and others (numbered 15734).


*Approved August 28, 1957.*

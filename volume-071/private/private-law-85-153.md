# Private Law 85–153

> AN ACT For the relief of Triantafilia Antul.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Triantafilia Antul shall be held and considered to be the natural-born minor alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Charles Antul, citizens of the United States.


*Approved August 14, 1957.*

# Private Law 85–65

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Theodore (Nicole Xantho) Rousseau.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the admin-istration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Theodore (Nicole Xantho) Rousseau shall be deemed to be within the purview of section 354 (3) of the said Act, and to have had no nationality other than Rumanian prior to her naturalization as a United States citizen.


*Approved July 18, 1957.*

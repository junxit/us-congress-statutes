# Private Law 85–159

> JOINT RESOLUTION For the relief of certain aliens.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Ettore Penna, Mrs. Sirvart Minassian, Noel Kaiser, Sonja Spieker, David W. S. Ahn and Margaret Y. A. Ahn shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fees. Upon the granting of permanent residence to each alien as provided for in this Act, if such alien was classifiable as a quota immigrant at the time of the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to reduce by one the quota for the quota area to which the alien is chargeable for the first year that such quota is available: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the said Act in the case of Sonja Spieker.


*Approved August 14, 1957.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 62

> “PRICE DISCRIMINATION, THE ROBINSON-PATMAN ACT, AND THE ATTORNEY GENERAL’S NATIONAL COMMITTEE TO STUDY THE ANTITRUST LAWS”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed five thousand additional copies of the report of the Select Committee on Small Business of the House of Representatives entitled “Price Discrimination, the Robinson-Patman Act, and the Attorney General’s National Committee to Study the Antitrust Laws” (House Report Numbered 2966, Eighty-fourth Congress), of which four thousand copies shall be for the use of the Select Committee on Small Business of the House of Representatives and one thousand copies shall be for the use of the House Document Room.


*Passed March 8, 1957.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 24

> “TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Foreign Relations two thousand five hundred additional copies of Senate Report 139, current Congress, entitled “Technical Assistance”.


*Agreed to May 21, 1957.*

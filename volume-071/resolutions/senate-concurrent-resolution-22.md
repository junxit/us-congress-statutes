# Senate Concurrent Resolution 22

> “INTERNAL SECURITY ANNUAL REPORT FOR 1956”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on the Judiciary seventy-five thousand additional copies of Senate Report Numbered 131, Eighty-fifth Congress, entitled “Internal Security Annual Report for 1956”.


*Agreed to July 1, 1957.*

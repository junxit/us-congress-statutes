# House Concurrent Resolution 174

> DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE APPROPRIATIONS, 1958

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Appropriations, House of Representatives, one thousand additional copies of part 1 of the hearings held by that committee during the current session on the Department of Defense appropriations for 1958.


*Passed May 22, 1957.*

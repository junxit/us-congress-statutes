# House Concurrent Resolution 104

> EXCISE TAXES

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Ways and Means, House of Representatives, two thousand five hundred additional copies of the hearings on excise taxes held by that committee during the Eighty-fourth Congress, second session.


*Passed March 8, 1957.*

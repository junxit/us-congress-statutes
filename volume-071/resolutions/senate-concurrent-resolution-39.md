# Senate Concurrent Resolution 39

> COMMISSION ON GOVERNMENT SECURITY

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there shall be printed as a Senate document the report of the Commission on Government Security, submitted to the Congress June 21, 1957, pursuant to Public Law 304 of the Eighty-fourth Congress, as amended; and that there shall be printed five thousand additional copies, of which two thousand five hundred shall be for the use of the Senate and two thousand five hundred for the use of the House.


*Agreed to August 22, 1957.*

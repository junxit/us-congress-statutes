# House Concurrent Resolution 179

> EXCISE TAX CHANGES ACT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed twelve thousand four hundred additional copies of the report to accompany H. R. 7125, a bill to make technical changes in the Federal excise tax laws; of which seven thousand, five hundred copies shall be for the use of the Committee on Ways and Means; four thousand, two hundred copies for the House document room; five hundred copies for the Senate document room; and two hundred copies for the use of the Committee on Finance.


*Passed May 27, 1957.*

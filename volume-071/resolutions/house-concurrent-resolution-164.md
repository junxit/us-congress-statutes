# House Concurrent Resolution 164

> ADJOURNMENT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Thursday, April 18, 1957, they stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Monday, April 29, 1957.


*Passed April 18, 1957.*

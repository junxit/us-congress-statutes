# House Concurrent Resolution 229

> ADJOURNMENT SINE DIE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Friday, August 30, 1957, and that when they adjourn on said day, they stand adjourned sine die.


*Passed August 30, 1957.*

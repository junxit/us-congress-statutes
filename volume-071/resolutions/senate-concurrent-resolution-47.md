# Senate Concurrent Resolution 47

> “INVESTIGATION OF THE FINANCIAL CONDITION OF THE UNITED STATES”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Finance three thousand additional copies of part 1 of the hearings entitled “Investigation of the Financial Condition of the United States”, and five thousand additional copies of part 2 and subsequent parts of said hearings held by that committee during the Eighty-fifth Congress, first session.


*Agreed to August 30, 1957.*

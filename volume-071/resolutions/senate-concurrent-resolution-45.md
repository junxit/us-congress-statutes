# Senate Concurrent Resolution 45

> MUTUAL SECURITY PROGRAM

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Foreign Relations, United States Senate, one thousand additional copies of parts 1 and 2 of the hearings held by that committee during the current session on the mutual security program for fiscal year 1958.


*Agreed to August 30, 1957.*

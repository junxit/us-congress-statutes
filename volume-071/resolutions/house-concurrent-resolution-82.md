# House Concurrent Resolution 82

> NATIONAL HIGHWAY PROGRAM

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Public Works, House of Representatives, two thousand additional copies of the hearings held by said committee during the Eighty-fourth Congress, second session, on the national highway program.


*Passed March 8, 1957.*

# Public Law 85–54

> AN ACT To amend the Legislative Appropriation Act, 1955, with reference to official office expenses of Members of Congress.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the nineteenth paragraph under the subheading “Contingent Expenses of the House” under the heading “HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES” in the Legislative Appropriation Act, 1955 (2 U. S. C., sec. 122a; Public Law 470, Eighty-third Congress), is amended to read as follows:

> “The Clerk of the House is authorized and directed to reimburse each Member from the contingent fund in an amount not to exceed $150 quarterly, upon certification of a Member, for official office expenses incurred outside the District of Columbia.”


*Approved June 13, 1957.*

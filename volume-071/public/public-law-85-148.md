# Public Law 85–148

> AN ACT To amend the Act of March 4, 1938, to extend by ten years the period prescribed for determining the rates of toll to be charged for use of the bridge across the Missouri River near Rulo, Nebraska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (e) of section 5 of the Act entitled “An Act to authorize the construction of certain bridges and to extend the times for commencing and/or completing the construction of other bridges over the navigable waters of the United States”, approved March 4, 1933 (47 Stat. 1556), as amended by the Act of June 19, 1948 ( 62 Stat. 497), is further amended (1) by striking out “and financing” and inserting financing, and refinancing”, and (2) by striking out “thirty years” and inserting “forty years”.


*Approved August 16, 1957.*

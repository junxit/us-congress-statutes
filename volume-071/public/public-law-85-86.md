# Public Law 85–86

> AN ACT To repeal section 1157 of title 18 of the United States Code, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1157 of title 18 of the United States Code, as amended, is repealed.


*Approved July 10, 1957.*

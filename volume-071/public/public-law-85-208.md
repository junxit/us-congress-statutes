# Public Law 85–208

> AN ACT To amend the Career Compensation Act of 1949 to provide incentive pay for human test subjects.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 204 (a) of the Career Compensation Act of 1949, as amended (37 U. S. C. 235 (a) ), is further amended—

- **(1)** by striking out the word “and” at the end of clause (11);
- **(2)** by striking out the period at the end of clause (12) and inserting “; and” in lieu thereof; and
- **(3)** by adding the following clause: “(13) duty as human test subject in thermal stress experiments.”

## § 2.

Section 204 (c) of the Career Compensation Act of 1949, as amended (37 U. S. C. 235 (c) ), is further amended by striking out “(12)” and inserting “(13)” in lieu thereof.


*Approved August 28, 1957.*

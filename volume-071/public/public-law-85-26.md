# Public Law 85–26

> AN ACT To provide a temporary extension of certain special provisions relating to State plans for aid to the blind.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 344 (b) of the Social Security Act Amendments of 1950 (Public Law 734, Eighty-first Congress), as amended by section 302 of the Social Security Amendments of 1954 (Public Law 761, Eighty-third Congress), is amended by striking out “June 30, 1957” and inserting in lieu thereof “June 30, 1959”.


*Approved April 25, 1957.*

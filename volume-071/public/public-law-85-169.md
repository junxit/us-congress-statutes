# Public Law 85–169

> AN ACT To transfer ownership to Allegany County, Maryland, of a bridge loaned to such county by the Bureau of Public Roads.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all right, title, and interest of the United States to the Bailey bridge at Corriganville, Maryland, which was loaned to Allegany County, Maryland, in 1948 by the Bureau of Public Roads of the Department of Commerce, is hereby transferred to such county.


*Approved August 28, 1957.*

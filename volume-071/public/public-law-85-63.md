# Public Law 85–63

> AN ACT To amend the Act of August 3, 1950, as amended, to continue in effect the provisions relating to the authorized personnel strengths of the Armed Forces.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1 of the Act of August 3, 1950, chapter 537, as amended (68 Stat. 27), is further amended by striking out the words “July 31, 1957” and inserting the words “July 1, 1959” in place thereof.


*Approved June 27, 1957.*

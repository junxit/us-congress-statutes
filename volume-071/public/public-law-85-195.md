# Public Law 85–195

> AN ACT To modify section 3 of the Act of June 30, 1945 (59 Stat. 263).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

Notwithstanding the provisions of section 3 of the Act of June 30, 1945 (59 Stat. 265), the. Secretary of the Interior is directed to disburse the sums due to the heirs or devisees of deceased claimants who have been identified on or before June 30, 1957.


*Approved August 28, 1957.*

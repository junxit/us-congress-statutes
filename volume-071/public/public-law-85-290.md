# Public Law 85–290

> JOINT RESOLUTION Establishing that the second regular session of the 85th Congress convene at noon on Tuesday, January 7, 1958.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second regular session of the Eighty-fifth Congress shall begin at noon on Tuesday, January 7, 1958.


*Approved September 4, 1957.*

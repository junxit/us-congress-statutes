# Public Law 85–35

> AN ACT To provide for the reconveyance of certain land to the city of Spearfish, South Dakota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior shall transfer by quitclaim deed to the city of Spearfish, South Dakota, all right, title, and interest of the United States in and to the following described land, including improvements thereon, in such city: Lot 1-A of the northwest quarter of the southeast quarter of section fifteen in township six north, range two east, Black Hills meridian, containing one acre.


*Approved May 21, 1957.*

# Public Law 85–21

> JOINT RESOLUTION To implement further the Act of July 15, 1946, by approving the signature by the Secretary of the Treasury of an agreement amending the Anglo-American Financial Agreement of December 6, 1945.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1 of the Act of July 15, 1946 (60 Stat. 535; 22 U. S. C. 2867) is hereby amended by changing the period at the end thereof to a comma and adding the following, “and the action of the Secretary of the Treasury in signing the agreement dated March 6, 1957. amending said agreement is hereby approved.”


*Approved April 20, 1957.*

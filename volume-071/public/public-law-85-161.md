# Public Law 85–161

> AN ACT To amend Public Law 815, Eighty-first Congress, relating to school construction in federally affected areas, to make its provisions applicable to Wake Island.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 210 (14) of the Act of September 23, 1950 (Public Law 815, Eighty-first Congress), is amended by striking out “or the Virgin Islands” and inserting “the Virgin Islands, or Wake Island”.


*Approved August 21, 1957.*

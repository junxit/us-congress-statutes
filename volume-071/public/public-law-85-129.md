# Public Law 85–129

> AN ACT To amend the Act of July 11, 194, to increase the maximum rate of compensation which the director of the Metropolitan Police force band may be paid.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the last sen- tence of the first section of the Act entitled “An Act to authorize the establishment of a band in the Metropolitan Police force”, approved July 11, 1947 (D. C. Code 4–182), is amended by striking out “lieu-tenant” and inserting in lieu thereof “captain”.


*Approved August 14, 1957.*

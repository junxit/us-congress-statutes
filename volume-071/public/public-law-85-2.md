# Public Law 85–2

> JOINT RESOLUTION To extend the time for transmitting the economic report of the President for the first regular session of the Eighty-fifth Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions of section 3 (a) of the Employment Act of 1946, as amended (relating to the time for filing the economic report of the President), the economic report for the first regular session of the Eighty-fifth Congress shall be transmitted not later than January 23, 1957.


*Approved January 18, 1957.*

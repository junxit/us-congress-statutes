# Public Law 85–194

> AN ACT To increase the maximum amount payable by the Veterans’ Administration for mailing or shipping charges of personal property left by any deceased veteran on Veterans’ Administration property.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1907 of the Veterans’ Benefits Act of 1957 is amended by striking out “$10” and inserting “$25”.


*Approved August 28, 1957.*

# Public Law 85–82

> AN ACT To exempt the sale of materials for certain war memorials in the District of Columbia from the District of Columbia Sales Tax Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 128 of the District of Columbia Sales Tax Act, as amended (63 Stat. 112; D. C. Code 47–2605) is amended by adding at the end thereof the following:

> - **(r)** Sales of material to be incorporated permanently in any war memorial authorized by Congress to be erected on public grounds of the United States.”

## § 2.

The amendment made by the first section of this Act shall be effective only with respect to sales taking place on and after January 1, 1957.


*Approved July 3, 1957.*

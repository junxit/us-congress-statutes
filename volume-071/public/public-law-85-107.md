# Public Law 85–107

> AN ACT To amend Public Law 31, Eighty-fourth Congress, first session, to increase the authorization for appropriation to the Atomic Energy Commission for the construction of a modern office building in or near the District of Columbia to serve as its principal office.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Public Law 31, Eighty-fourth Congress, first session, is hereby amended, by striking the figure “$10,000,000” and inserting in lieu thereof the figure “$13,300,000”.


*Approved July 17, 1957.*

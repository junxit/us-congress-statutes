# Public Law 85–121

> AN ACT To extend the effectiveness of the Missing Persons Act, as extended, until April 1, 1958.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 15, Missing Persons Act (56 Stat. 147, 1093), as amended, is further amended by deleting “July 1, 1957” and inserting in lieu thereof “April 1, 1958”.


*Approved August 7, 1957.*

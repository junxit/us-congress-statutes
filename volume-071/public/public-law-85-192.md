# Public Law 85–192

> AN ACT Relating to the affairs of the Osage Tribe of Indians in Oklahoma.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 9 of the Act of Congress approved June 28, 1906 (34 Stat. 539), as amended by section 7 of the Act of March 2, 1929 (45 Stat. 1478), is hereby amended by striking therefrom the words “January 1, 1959” and substituting therefor the words “January 1, 1984”.


*Approved August 28, 1957.*

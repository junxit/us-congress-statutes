# Public Law 85–16

> AN ACT To provide that the Delegate from Alaska in the House of Representatives of the United States shall be a member of the Alaska International Rail and Highway Commission.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (a) of the first section of the Act entitled “An Act to Establish an Alaska International Rail and Highway Commission”, approved August 1, 1956 (Public Law 884, Eighty-fourth Congress; 70 Stat. 888), is amended—

- **(1)** by striking out “twelve members” and inserting in lieu thereof “thirteen members”;
- **(2)** by striking out “and” at the end o£ clause (2) thereof; and
- **(3)** by inserting “; and” in lieu of the period at the end thereof, and adding the following:
  > - **(4)** the Delegate from Alaska in the House of Representatives of the United States.”


*Approved April 20, 1957.*

# Public Law 85–3

> AN ACT To amend Public Law 954, Eighty-fourth Congress, approved August 3, 1956.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Public Law 954 of the Eighty-fourth Congress, approved August 3, 1956, entitled “An Act to provide for a President’s Advisory Commission on Presidential Office Space” be amended as follows: In the second sentence of section 1 strike out the words “within six months” and insert in lieu thereof the words “within ten months”.


*Approved January 25, 1957.*

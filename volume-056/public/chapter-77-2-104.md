# Chapter 104

> JOINT RESOLUTION Amending section 7 of the Neutrality Act of 1939.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 7 of the Neutrality Act of 1939 (Public Resolution Numbered 54, Seventy-sixth Congress) is amended by adding at the end thereof the following new paragraph:

> - **(e)** This section shall not be operative when the United States is at war.”


*Approved, February 21, 1942.*

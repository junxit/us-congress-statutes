# Chapter 531

> AN ACT To amend the Bankhead-Jones Farm Tenant Act to permit exchange of land with private owners.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in, Congress assembled,*

That subsection (c), section 32, of title III of the Bankhead-Jones Farm Tenant Act (Act of July 22, 1937) is hereby amended by adding the following proviso at the end of the first sentence thereof: “: Provided, however, That an exchange may be made with private owners and with subdivisions or agencies of State governments in any case where the Secretary of Agriculture finds that such exchange would not conflict with the purposes of the Act, and that the value of the property received in exchange is substantially equal to that of the property conveyed.”


*Approved, July 28, 1942.*

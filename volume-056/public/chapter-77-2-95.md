# Chapter 95

> AN ACT To extend the time for examination of quarterly accounts covering expenditures by disbursing officers of the United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time for examination of quarterly accounts covering expenditures by disburing officers of the United States Navy after the date of actual receipt in the Bureau of Supplies and Accounts, Navy Department, and before transmitting the same to the General Accounting Office, as limited by section 12 of the Act of July 31, 1894 (28 Stat. 209), as amended, is hereby extended from sixty to ninety days in time of war or during any emergency declared by Congress and for a period of eighteen months after such war or emergency shall have ceased to exist.


*Approved, February 20, 1942.*

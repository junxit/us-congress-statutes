# Chapter 571

> AN ACT To amend the Act of May 19, 1926, entitled “An Act to authorize the President to detail officers and enlisted men of the United States Army, Navy, and Marine Corps to assist the governments of the Latin-American republics in military and naval matters.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of May 19, 1926 (44 Stat. 565), as amended by the Act of May 14, 1935 (49 Stat. 218), be, and the same is hereby, amended by inserting after the words “and the Commonwealth of the Philippine Islands” the words “and during war or a declared national emergency, the governments of such other countries as the President deems it in the interest of national defense to assist,”.


*Approved, October 1, 1942.*

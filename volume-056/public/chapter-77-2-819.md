# Chapter 819

> AN ACT To amend the Nationality Act of 1940, to preserve the nationality of naturalized veterans of the Spanish-American War and of the World War, and of their wives, minor children, and dependent parents.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 'United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 406 of the Nationality Act of 1940, approved October 14, 1940 (54 Stat. 1170), be, and it hereby is, amended by adding thereto a new subsection to be lettered “(h)” and reading as follows:

> - **(h)** Who is a veteran of the Spanish-American War, or of the World War, his wife, minor children, or dependent parents.”


*Approved, December 24, 1942.*

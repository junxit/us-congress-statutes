# Chapter 320

> AN ACT To relieve disbursing and certifying officers of the United States of responsibility for overpayments made on transportation accounts under certain circumstances.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That hereafter no disbursing or certifying officer of the United States shall be held liable for overpayments made for transportation furnished on Government bills of lading or transportation requests when said overpayments are due to the use of improper transportation rates, classifications, or the failure to deduct, the proper amount under land-grant laws or equalization and other agreements.


*Approved, June 1, 1942.*

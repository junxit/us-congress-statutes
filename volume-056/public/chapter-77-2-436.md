# Chapter 436

> AN ACT To make permanently effective the Act regulating interstate and foreign commerce in petroleum and its products.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 13 of the Act entitled “An Act to regulate interstate and foreign commerce in petroleum and its products by prohibiting the shipment in such commerce of petroleum and its products produced in violation of State law, and for other purposes”, approved February 22, 1935 (49 Stat. 30), as amended, is hereby repealed.


*Approved, June 22, 1942.*

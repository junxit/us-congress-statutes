# Chapter 347

> AN ACT For the relief of the Tlingit and Haida Indians of Alaska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Home of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time within which suit or suits may be filed by the Tlingit and Haida Indians of Alaska under the terms of the Act of Congress of June 19, 1935 (ch. 275, 49 Stat. L. 388), is hereby extended for a period of three years from and after the date of the approval of this Act.


*Approved, June 5, 1942.*

# Chapter 34

> AN ACT To amend section 3 (a) of the Act entitled “An Act to authorize the Secretary of the Navy to proceed with the construction of certain public works, and for other purposes”, approved June 2, 1939 (53 Stat. 800), so as to transfer the administration of the Naval Supply Depot, Oakland, to the Commandant, Twelfth Naval District.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3 (a) of the Act entitled “An Act to authorize the Secretary of the Navy to proceed with the construction of certain public works, and for other purposes”, approved June 2, 1939 (53 Stat. 800), is hereby amended by striking out the first proviso and inserting in lieu thereof the following: “Provided, That such land shall be used only as a naval supply depot and for no other purpose, and such depot shall be a part of the Naval Operating Base, San Francisco, and shall be so administered by the Commandant, Twelfth Naval District:”.


*Approved, February 3, 1942.*

# Chapter 542

> AN ACT To amend article of war 50½.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the third and fifth paragraphs of article of war 501/2 (41 Stat. 797–799), as amended by the Act approved August 20, 1937 (50 Stat. 724), be further amended by adding a further proviso at the end of each of said paragraphs as follows: “Provided further, That whenever a branch of the office of the Judge Advocate General is established, under the provisions of the last paragraph of this article, with a distant command, such functions may be performed by the commanding general of such distant command in all cases in which the board of review in such branch office is empowered to act and in which the commanding general of such distant command is not the appointing or confirming authority.”


*Approved, August 1, 1942.*

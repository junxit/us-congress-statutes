# Chapter 36

> AN ACT To increase the period for which leases may be made of public lands granted to the State of Idaho for educational purposes by the Act of July 3, 1890.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That so much of section 5 of the Act entitled “An Act to provide for the admission of the State of Idaho into the Union” approved July 3, 1890, as reads “be leased for periods of not more than five years” is amended to read as follows: “be leased for periods of not more than ten years”.


*Approved, February 6, 1942.*

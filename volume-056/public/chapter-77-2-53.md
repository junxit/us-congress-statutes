# Chapter 53

> AN ACT To amend the Act approved October 24, 1941, entitled “An Act to authorize the Secretary of the Navy to provide salvage facilities, and for other purposes” (Public Law Numbered 280, Seventy-seventh Congress), so as to remove the limitation on the sum authorized to be appropriated annually to effectuate the purposes of the Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2 of the Act approved October 24, 1941, entitled “An Act to authorize the Secretary of the Navy to provide salvage facilities, and for other purposes” (Public Law Numbered 280, Seventy-seventh Congress), be, and the same is hereby, amended to read as follows:

> ## § 2.
>
> There is hereby authorized to be appropriated out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, such funds as may be necessary to effectuate the purposes of this Act.”


*Approved, February 10, 1942.*

# Chapter 827

> AN ACT To provide for the appointment of an additional district judge for the northern district of Alabama.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is authorized to appoint, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, an additional district judge for the District Court of the United States for the Northern District of Alabama.

Birmingham shall be the official place of residence of every person who may become a district judge for the District Court of the United States for the Northern District of Alabama by virtue of appointment and confirmation in accordance with the provisions of this Act.


*Approved, December 24, 1942.*

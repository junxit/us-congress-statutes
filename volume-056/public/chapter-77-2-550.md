# Chapter 550

> JOINT RESOLUTION To authorize the War Shipping Administration to sell or charter two merchant vessels to the Government of Ireland.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding any other provision of law, the War Shipping Administration is authorized, with the approval of the President, to sell or charter the merchant vessels West Neris and West Hematite to the Government of Ireland, upon such terms and conditions as the War Shipping Administration may deem necessary or desirable for the protection of the public interest.


*Approved, August 6, 1942.*

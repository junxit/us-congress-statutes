# Chapter 407

> AN ACT To add to the Cleveland National Forest, California, certain contiguous lands of the United States which can be most effectively and economically protected and administered as parts of said national forest.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subject to any valid subsisting rights which hitherto have been established and hereafter are maintained under any of the land laws of the United States, the lands hereinafter described hereby are added to and made parts of the Cleveland National Forest and hereafter shall be subject to all laws, rules, and regulations applicable to the national forests:

Township 15 south, range 6 east, San Bernardino meridian: Lots 3 and 4, section 7; lots 1 and 2, section 18.

Township 8 south, range 6 west, San Bernardino meridian: Sections 2, 11, and 14, exclusive of the parts thereof within the boundaries of the Santa Margarita grant.


*Approved, June 11, 1942.*

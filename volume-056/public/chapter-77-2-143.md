# Chapter 143

> AN ACT To transfer Blair County, Pennsylvania, from the western judicial district of Pennsylvania to the middle judicial district of Pennsylvania.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Blair County, Pennsylvania of the western judicial district of Pennsylvania, be. and it is hereby, detached from said judicial district and attached to the middle judicial district of Pennsylvania: Provided, That the transfer herein provided shall not affect any case or proceedings now pending.


*Approved, March 5, 1942.*

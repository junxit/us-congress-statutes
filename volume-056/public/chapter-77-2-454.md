# Chapter 454

> JOINT RESOLUTION Continuing the Federal Surplus Commodities Corporation as an agency of the United States.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Federal Surplus Commodities Corporation is hereby continued as an agency of the United States, under the direction of the Secretary of Agriculture, until June 30, 1945.


*Approved, June 27, 1942.*

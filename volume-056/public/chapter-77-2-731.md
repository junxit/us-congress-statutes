# Chapter 731

> AN ACT To provide for the appointment of an additional circuit judge for the fifth circuit.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is authorized to appoint, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, one additional circuit judge for the fifth circuit.


*Approved, December 14, 1942.*

# Chapter 381

> AN ACT To change the name of the Black Warrior National Forest to the William B. Bankhead National Forest.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the date of enactment of this Act the national forest situated in the State of Alabama known and designated as the “Black Warrior National Forest” shall be known and designated as the “William B. Bankhead National Forest”. All laws, regulations, and public documents and records of the United States in which such national forest is designated or referred to under the name of the “Black Warrior National Forest” shall be held to refer to such national forest under and by the name of the “William B. Bankhead National Forest”.


*Approved, June 6, 1942.*

# Chapter 41

> JOINT RESOLUTION To amend section 124 of the Internal Revenue Code to simplify the procedure in connection with amortization of certain facilities.

*Resolved by the Senate and. House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, effective as of October 8, 1940, section 124 (i), as amended, of the Internal Revenue Code, is hereby repealed.


*Approved, February 6, 1942.*

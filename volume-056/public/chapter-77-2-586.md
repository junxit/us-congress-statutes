# Chapter 586

> AN ACT To amend the Act approved August 27, 1940 (54 Stat. 864), entitled “An Act increasing the number of naval aviators in the line of the Regular Navy and Marine Corps, and for other purposes”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4 of the Act entitled “An Act increasing the number of naval aviators in the line of the Regular Navy and Marine Corps, and for other purposes”, approved August 27, 1940 (54 Stat. 864), is hereby amended by adding before the period at the end thereof the following: “ Provided further, That this section shall be effective from September 8, 1939”.


*Approved, October 10, 1942.*

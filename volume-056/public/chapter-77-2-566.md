# Chapter 566

> JOINT RESOLUTION Authorizing the President of the United States of America to proclaim October 11, 1942, General Pulaski’s Memorial Day for the observance and commemoration of the death of Brigadier General Casimir Pulaski.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States of America is authorized to issue a proclamation calling upon officials of the Government to display the flag of the United States on all governmental buildings on October 11, 1942, and inviting the people of the United States to observe the day in schools and churches, or other suitable places, with appropriate ceremonies in commemoration of the death of General Casimir Pulaski.


*Approved, September 26, 1942.*

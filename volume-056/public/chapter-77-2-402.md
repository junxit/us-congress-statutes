# Chapter 402

> AN ACT To increase the rate of pension to World War veterans from $30 to $40 per month and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph I (f), part III, Veterans Regulation Numbered 1 (a), as amended, is hereby amended to read:

> - **(I(f))** The amount of pension payable under the terms of part III shall be $40 monthly: Provided, That—”.


*Approved, June 10, 1942.*

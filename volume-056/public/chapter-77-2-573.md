# Chapter 573

> AN ACT To authorize a reduction in the course of instruction at the United States Military Academy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized to reduce, in his discretion, until the termination of the present war, the course of instruction at the United States Military Academy from four to three years and to graduate classes which have completed such reduced courses of instruction.


*Approved, October 1, 1942.*

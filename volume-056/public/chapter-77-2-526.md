# Chapter 526

> AN ACT Relating to the jurisdiction over certain lands in the Isle Royale National Park.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act entitled “An Act to provide for the addition of certain lands to the Isle Royale National Park, in the State of Michigan, and for other purposesS”, approved March 6, 1942, is hereby amended by striking out the words “Secretary of the Treasury”, wherever they appear in such Act, and inserting in lieu thereof the words “Secretary of the Navy”.


*Approved, July 27, 1942.*

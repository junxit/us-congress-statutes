# Chapter 206

> AN ACT To limit the initial base pay of $21 per month for enlisted men in the Army and Marine Corps to those of the seventh grade.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 12 (a) of the Selective Training and Service Act of 1940 (54 Stat. 885) be, and it is hereby, amended by adding the words “of the seventh grade” after the word “men” in line 7 of said section.


*Approved, March 28, 1942.*

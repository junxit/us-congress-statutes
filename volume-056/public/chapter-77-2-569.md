# Chapter 569

> JOINT RESOLUTION Authorizing extensions of time for filing return of capital stock tax in 1942.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1203 (b) (2) of the Internal Revenue Code (relating to power of the Commissioner to extend the time for filing capital stock tax returns) is amended by inserting at the end thereof the following new sentence: “With respect to the year ending June 30, 1942, the extension may be for not more than one hundred and twenty days.”


*Approved, September 29, 1942.*

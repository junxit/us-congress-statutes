# Senate Concurrent Resolution 29

> requisition of articles and materials for use by the united states

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Secretary of the Senate be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, in the enrollment of the bill (S. 2586) to amend sections 1 and 3 of the Act entitled “An Act to authorize the President to requisition articles and materials for the use of the United States, and for other purposes”, approved October 19, 1940 (54 Stat. 1090), to continue the Act in effect during the existing war, and for other purposes, to change the title so as to read: “An Act to amend sections 1 and 3 of the Act entitled ‘An Act to authorize the President to requisition certain articles and materials for the use of the United States, and for other purpose’, approved October 10, 1940 (54 Stat. 1090), to continue the Act in effect during the existing war, and for other purposes.”


*Agreed to June 29, 1942.*

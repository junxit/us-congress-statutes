# House Concurrent Resolution 80

> revenue act of 1942

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed seven thousand five hundred additional copies of House Report Numbered 2333, current session, on H. R. 7378, entitled “To provide revenue, and for other purposes”, of which five thousand copies shall be, for the use of the House document room, two hundred copies for the use of the Senate document room, two thousand copies for the use of the Committee on Ways and Means of the House of Representatives, and three hundred copies for the use of the Committee on Finance of the Senate.


*Passed August 13, 1942.*

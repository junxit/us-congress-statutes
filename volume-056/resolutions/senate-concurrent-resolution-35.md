# Senate Concurrent Resolution 35

> revenue act of 1942

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed thirty-seven thousand additional copies of Public Law Numbered 753, current session, entitled “Revenue Act of 1942”, of which five thousand copies shall be for the use of the Senate document room, twenty-five thousand copies shall be for the use of the House document room, five thousand copies for the use of the Committee on Ways and Means of the House, and two thousand copies for the use of the Committee on Finance of the Senate.


*Agreed to October 26, 1942.*

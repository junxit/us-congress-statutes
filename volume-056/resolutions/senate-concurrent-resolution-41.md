# Senate Concurrent Resolution 41

> adournment sine die

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Wednesday, the 16th day of December, 1942, and that when they adjourn on said day they stand adjourned sine die.


*Agreed to December 15, 1942.*

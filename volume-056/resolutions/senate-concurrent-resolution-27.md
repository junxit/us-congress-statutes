# Senate Concurrent Resolution 27

> second war powers act, 1942

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That in enrolling the bill (S. 2208) to further expedite the prosecution of the war, the Secretary of the Senate is authorized and directed to make all necessary corrections in title and section numbers and cross references as may be necessary by reason of the omission from the enrolled bill of title VIII.


*Agreed to March 23, 1942.*

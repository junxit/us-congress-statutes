# Senate Concurrent Resolution 24

> operation of national defense program

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed five thousand additional copies of the report (Report Numbered 480, part 5, current session) of the select committee of the Senate authorized and directed to make a complete study and investigation of the operation of the national defense program, of which two thousand copies shall be for the use of the select committee, one thousand for the use of the Senate document room, and two thousand copies for the use of the House document room.


*Agreed to February 6, 1942.*

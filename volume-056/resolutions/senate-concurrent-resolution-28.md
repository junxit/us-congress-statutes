# Senate Concurrent Resolution 28

> major paul a. larned, u. s. army

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the action of the Speaker of the House of Representatives and of the Vice President in signing the enrolled bill (S. 2202) to reinstate Paul A. Larned, a major, United States Army, retired, to the active list of Regular Army is hereby rescinded;

Resolved further, That the action of the Senate and the House of Representatives in passing the said bill is hereby vacated.


*Agreed to April 28, 1942.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 78

> “handbook for service men and women, world war ii, and their dependents”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the manuscript entitled “Handbook for Service Men and Women, World War II, and Their Dependents”, be printed as a public document, and that twenty-five thousand additional copies be printed, of which nineteen thousand five hundred copies shall be for the use of the House of Representatives and five thousand five hundred copies shall be for the use of the Senate.


*Passed July 23, 1942.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 62

> joint meeting

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Tuesday, the 6th day of January 1942, at 12:30 o’clock in the afternoon, for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed January 6, 1942.*

# Chapter 832

> AN ACT For the relief of John Sweeney.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purposes of all laws of the United States and all regulations thereunder, John Sweeney, of Paris, Tennessee, shall be deemed to be, and to have been since his birth, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved, December 24, 1942.*

# Chapter 245

> AN ACT For the relief of Jacques Hailpern, Max Hailpern, and Sally Hailpern Zaharia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws the Attorney General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to record the lawful admission for permanent residence of Jacques Hailpern, Max Hailpern, and Sally Hailpern Zaharia, as of December 23, 1939, the date on which they were admitted temporarily to the United States.


*Approved, April 24, 1942.*

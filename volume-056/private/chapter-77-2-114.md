# Chapter 114

> AN ACT For the relief of James Wood.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions and limitations of sections 17 to 20, both inclusive, of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes”, approved September 7, 1916, as amended, the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to receive and consider, when filed, the claim of James Wood, of Oswego, New York, for disability incurred by him while in the employment of the Civil Works Administration in February 1934, and to determine said claim upon its merits: Provided, That no benefits shall accrue prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved, February 24, 1942.*

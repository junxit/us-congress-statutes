# Chapter 655

> AN ACT For the relief of Earl Carbauh.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding any other provision of law Earl Carbauh, an employee of the Post Office Department retired under the provisions of section 8 (a) of the Act of June 16, 1933 (48 Stat. 305; U. S. C., title 5, sec. 736 (a)), shall not be required to reimburse the United States for any annuity payments received by him during the period of his employment at the United States Soldiers’ Home as a chorister from July 1, 1935, to April 1, 1942.


*Approved, December 1, 1942.*

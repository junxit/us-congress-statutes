# Chapter 698

> AN ACT For the relief of Leslie Charteris (Leslie Charles Bowyer Yin) and Patricia Ann Charteris.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws Leslie Charteris (Leslie Charles Bowyer Yin), the husband of a citizen of the United States, and his daughter Patricia Ann Charteris, shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence on September 8, 1939, the date the said Leslie Charteris and Patricia Ann Charteris were last admitted to the United States for temporary residence.


*Approved, December 8, 1942.*

# Chapter 498

> AN ACT For the relief of Doris Mongol Rai.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions of section 13–C of the Immigration Act of May 26, 1924, the Attorney General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to permit Doris Mongol Rai to remain permanently in the United States, provided she is otherwise admissible under the immigration laws.


*Approved, July 9, 1942.*

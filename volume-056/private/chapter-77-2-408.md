# Chapter 408

> AN ACT For the relief of Jerry McKinley Thompson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General is authorized and directed to cancel the amount of $3,820.19 entered on the accounts of Jerry McKinley Thompson, carrier in the post office at Hampton Beach, New Hampshire, by reason of disallowance by the General Accounting Office of payments made to the said Jerry McKinley Thompson by the Post Office Department for his services in delivery of mail at the Hampton Beach Post Office during periods from June 15 to September 15 of each year from 1929 to 1937, inclusive.


*Approved, June 11, 1942.*

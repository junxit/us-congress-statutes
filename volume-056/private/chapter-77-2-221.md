# Chapter 221

> AN ACT For the relief of Albert Edward Whiteside.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws the Attorney General be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to cancel the warrant of arrest and the order of deportation heretofore issued against Albert Edward Whiteside, and that the alien shall not hereafter become subject to deportation for the cause contained in the present warrant.


*Approved, April 7, 1942.*

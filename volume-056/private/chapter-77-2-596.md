# Chapter 596

> AN ACT For the relief of G. C. Barco and W. G. Knowles.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Attorney General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to cancel the judgment in favor of the United States against G. C. Barco and W. G. Knowles, of West Palm Beach, Florida, as sureties on a forfeited bail bond in a criminal proceeding against Courtney Hardin in the United States District Court for the Southern District of Florida.


*Approved, October 13, 1942.*

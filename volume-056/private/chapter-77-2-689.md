# Chapter 689

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Claud Tuck and Darrell Claud Tuck, a minor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 15 to 20, inclusive, of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes”, approved September 7, 1916, as amended, are hereby waived in favor of Mrs. Claud Tuck and Darrell Claud Tuck, a minor: Provided, That the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission is hereby authorized and directed to consider their claim under the provisions of the Act of February 15, 1934 (48 Stat. 351), as amended, in the same manner as it would if Claud Tuck, deceased, had, on May 23, 1938, been regularly employed by the Works Progress Administration: Provided further, That claim hereunder shall be filed within six months after the enactment of this Act.


*Approved, December 5, 1942.*

# Chapter 251

> AN ACT For the relief of Dora Thompson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to (1) rescind the cancelation of homestead entry Great Falls 073861 of Dora Thompson, (2) restore such entry to a pending status, and (3) extend for three years from the date of enactment of this Act the period during which said Dora Thompson may submit final proof with respect to compliance with residence requirements applicable to such entry.


*Approved, April 28, 1942.*

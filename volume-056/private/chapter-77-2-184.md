# Chapter 184

> AN ACT For the relief of Nell Mahoney.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to remove from the records of his office the debt which has been raised therein against Nell Mahoney, junior clerk-typist, Houston County, Texas, Rural Rehabilitation office of the Farm Security Administration, Department of Agriculture, at Crockett, Texas, in the sum of $125.75, together with interest due thereon from date of loss, public funds for which she is accountable and which were stolen from her desk in the Houston County Rural Rehabilitation office, Crockett, Texas, without her fault, on September 18, 1940.


*Approved, March 13, 1942.*

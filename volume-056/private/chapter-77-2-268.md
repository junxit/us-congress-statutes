# Chapter 268

> AN ACT For the relief of Bibiano L. Meer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to allow credit in the account of or otherwise to relieve Bibiano L. Meer, collector of internal revenue, Manila, Philippine Islands, for the value of certain series 108, class C, cigar 500’s internal-revenue stamps amounting to $250, which were charged to the collector of internal revenue at Manila and which for some unknown reason were not delivered to him or disappeared from his office, or for which because of an error in labeling he was not allowed credit upon their return to the Stamp Section, Accounts and Collections Unit, United States Bureau of Internal Revenue.


*Approved, April 29, 1942.*

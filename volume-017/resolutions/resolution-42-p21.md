# Resolution

> A Resolution authorizing the Appointment of a Commissioner to an international Congress on penitentiary and reformatory Discipline.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That, the President be, and he is hereby, authorized to appoint a commissioner to attend an international congress on penitentiary and reformatory discipline, proposed to be held in Europe; but the said appointment shall not authorize tiny expenditure of money from the treasury for salary or expenses, and must be accepted upon this express condition.


*Approved, March 20, 1871.*

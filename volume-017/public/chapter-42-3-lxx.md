# Chapter LXX

> An Act to authorize the President to appoint Frederick E. Upton, of Bath, Maine, a Master in the Navy of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and be is hereby, authorized to appoint Frederick E. Upton, of Bath, Maine, a master in the United States navy, with the grade in his rank that he held at the date of leaving the United States service.


*Approved, January 27, 1873.*

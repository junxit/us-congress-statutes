# Chapter CCLIX

> An Act to establish a Board of local Inspectors of Steam-vessels for the Customs District of Michigan, in the State of Michigan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there shall be a local board of inspectors designated and appointed for the customs district of Michigan, in the State of Michigan, and the inspector of hulls and the inspector of boilers, composing the said board, shall receive a salary of nine hundred dollars a year each.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

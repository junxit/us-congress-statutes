# Chapter CCCXLIII

> An Act to create an additional Land District in Florida.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That that portion of Florida lying east of the line between ranges fourteen and fifteen east shall constitute an additional land district, and shall be known as the East Florida district, the office for which shall be located at Jaynesville.

## § 2.

That there shall be appointed a register and a receiver for said land district, and who shall be entitled to the same compensation as is, or may hereafter be, prescribed by law for like officers of the other district in said State.


*Approved, June 8, 1872.*

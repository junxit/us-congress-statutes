# Chapter XXXIX

> An Act to fix the Compensation of Gaugers and Measurers at the Port of Boston.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the compensation of gaugers and measurers at the port of Boston, shall be the same as provided for the same class of officers at the port of New York under existing laws.


*Approved, January 17, 1873.*

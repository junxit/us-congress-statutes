# Chapter I

> An Act making an Appropriation for the Repairs, Furnishing, and Ornamentation of the United States Custom-house and Post-office at Bath, Maine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of live thousand dollars be, and the same is hereby, appropriated ont of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the repair, furnishing, and heating of the United States custom-house and post-office at Bath, in the State of Maine, and for grading the grounds, to be expended in accordance with existing laws.


*Approved, December 10, 1872.*

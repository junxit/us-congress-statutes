# Chapter LVII

> An Act to amend Section thirty-five of an Act entitled “An Act to reduce internal Taxes, and for other Purposes.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the privileges of an act entitled “Al Act to reduce internal taxes, and for other purposes,” approved July fourteenth, eighteen hundred aifl seventy, be, and are hereby extended to the port of Pittsburgh, in the State of Pennsylvania, with the same effect as if it had been inserted in the thirty-fifth section of said act.


*Approved, March 18, 1872.*

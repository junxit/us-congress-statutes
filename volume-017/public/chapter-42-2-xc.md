# Chapter XC

> An Act defining the Rights of Part Dieners of Vessels in certain Cases.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That any person or persons or body corporate having more than one-half ownership of any vessel shall have the same power to remove a captain, who is also part owner of such vessel, as such majority owners now have to remove a captain not an owner: Provided, That this act shall not apply where there is a valid written agreement subsisting, by virtue of which such captain would be entitled to possession, nor in any case where a captain has possession as part owner, obtained before the passage of this act.


*Approved, April 9, 1872.*

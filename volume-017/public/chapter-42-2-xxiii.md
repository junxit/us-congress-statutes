# Chapter XXIII

> An Act extending the Time for the Completion oftheGreen Bay and Sturgeon Bay and Lake Michigan Ship Canal, in the State of Wisconsin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time for the completion of the Green Bay and Sturgeon Bay and Lake Michigan ship canal be, and the same is hereby, extended to the tenth day of April, auno Domini eighteen hundred and seventy-four.


*Approved, March 1, 1872.*

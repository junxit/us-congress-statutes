# Chapter CLXIX

> An Act to regulate the Salary of the Consul at Tien Tsin, China.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the salary of the United States consul at Tien Tsin, China, shall, from and after the passage of tills act, be thirty-five hundred dollars; and the laws regulating the duties of salaried consuls shall govern said consul.


*Approved, May 17, 1872.*

# Chapter CLXXXII

> An Act to reduce the Limits of the military Reservation at Fort Stanton, New Mexico.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and be is hereby, authorized and instructed to reduce the limits of the military reservation at Fort Stanton, in New Mexico, to a tract not exceeding sixteen square miles. The new limits of said reservation shall embrace a strip of land eight miles in length, and shall extend one mile from each bank of the Rio Bonito; the boundaries thereof to be determined by the Secretary of War, and the balance of the reservation to be thrown open to entry and settlement under the laws of the United States.


*Approved, May 21, 1872.*

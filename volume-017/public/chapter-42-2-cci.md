# Chapter CCI

> An Act to provide for holding the United States District Court in the City of Toledo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there shall be two terms of the United States district court for the northern district of Ohio heldin the city of Toledo, Ohio, in each year from and after the passage of this act, the time and the length of the terms to be fixed by the judge of said court.


*Approved, May 23, 1872.*

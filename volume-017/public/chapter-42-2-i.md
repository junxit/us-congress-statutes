# Chapter I

> An Act to provide for a Deficiency in the Appropriations for the Expenses of taking the ninth Census of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, to supply a deficiency in the appropriations for defraying the expenses of taking the ninth census of the United States, the following sums be, and they are hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, viz.:—

For the payment of marshals and assistant and deputy marshals, the sum of two hundred and ninety-three thousand dollars.

For clerk-hire and miscellaneous expenses, the sum of one hundred and eighty-seven thousand dollars.


*Approved, December 16, 1871.*

# Chapter CCXXXIX

> An Act fixing the Time for the Election of Representatives from, the State of California to the forty-fourth Congress

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That on the first Wednesday in September, in the year eighteen hundred and seventy-four, there shall be elected in each congressional district in the State of California one representative to represent said State in the forty-fourth Congress of the United States.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

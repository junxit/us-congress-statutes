# Chapter CCXVIII

> An Act to provide for the Expenses of the Investigation of alleged Frauds in the recent senatorial Election in Kansas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of ten thousand dollars be, and the same hereby is appropriated, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to pay the expenses of the select committee, of the Senate, appointed to inquire into the charges of bribery and corruption in the recent senatorial election in Kansas.


*Approved, March 1, 1873.*

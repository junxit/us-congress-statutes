# Chapter CLXXIV

> An Act authorising the Nomination and Appointment to the retired List of the Navy of certain Volunteers on the active List of the Navy, who are disabled in Consequence of Wounds received during the late War.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be, and is hereby, authorized to nominate, and, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, to appoint upon the retired list of the navy with the rank of master, L. R. Chester, formerly an acting ensign in the navy.


*Approved, February 21, 1873.*

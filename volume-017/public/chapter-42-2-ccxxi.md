# Chapter CCXXI

> An Act to change the Name of the Schooner La Pette to La Petite.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of the schooner La Pette, of Huron, Erie county, State of Ohio, be, and is hereby, changed from its present name to that of La Petite.


*Approved, May 27, 1872.*

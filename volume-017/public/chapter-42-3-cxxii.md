# Chapter CXXII

> An Act making an Appropriation to defray the Expenses of the Investigations in Regard to Elections in Kansas, Louisiana, and Arkansas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Conyress assembled,*

That the sum of thirty thousand dollars is hereby appropriated to be paid out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated to defray the expenses of the investigations ordered by the Senate in regard to elections in Kansas, Louisiana, and Arkansas.


*Approved, February 8, 1873.*

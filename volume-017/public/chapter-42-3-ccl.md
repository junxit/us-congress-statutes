# Chapter CCL

> An Act restoring Captain Alonso J. Marsh to his Position in the Army, and that he be honorably mustered out of the Service as of the Date of the Mastering-out of his Regiment.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to restore Alonzo J. Marsh, late a captain of company H. eleventh regiment veteran reseiwe corps, to his position as such captain, and grant him an honorable muster out as of the date on which he was dismissed; to wit, ou March seventh, eighteen hundred and sixty-five.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

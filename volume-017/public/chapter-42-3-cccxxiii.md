# Chapter CCCXXIII

> An Act extending the Time for the Completion of the Portage Lake and Lake Superior Ship Canal.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time for the completion of the Portage Lake and Lake Superior ship canal be and the same is hereby extended to the first day of December, anno Domini eighteen hundred and seventy-three.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

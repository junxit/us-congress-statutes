# Chapter CCXVI

> An Act authorizing the President to appoint Prank M. Ashton a second assistant Engineer in the Navy of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and is hereby, authorized, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, to appoint Frank M. Ashton a second assistant engineer in the United States navy, subject to the usual examination.


*Approved, March 1, 1873.*

# Chapter CLXII

> An Act to provide that Minors shall not be enlisted in the military Service of the United States without the Consent of Parents or Guardians.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That no person under the age of twenty-one years shall be enlisted or mustered into the military service of the United States without the written consent of his parents or guardians :Provided, That such minor shall have such parents or guardians entitled to his custody and control.

## § 2.

That in case any officer knowingly violates the provisions of this act by the enlistment or muster of a minor, be shall be liable to be arrested and tried by a court-martial, and, upon conviction, shall be dismissed from the service, or suffer such other punishment as such court may direct.


*Approved, May 15, 1872.*

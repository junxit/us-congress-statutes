# Chapter CCCV

> An Act to correct an Error in the Act approved February twenty-fourth, eighteen hundred and seventy-one.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That so much of the act entitled “An act to provide for the disposition of useless military reservations,” approved February twenty-fourth, eighteen hundred and seventy-one, as locates the military reservation of Fort Walla-Walla, in “Oregon,” is hereby amended so as to read “Washington Territory,” the actual location of said reservation.


*Approved, June 5, 1872.*

# Chapter XXXVII

> An Act to amend an Act entitled “An Act relating to Members of Congress, Heads of Departments, and other Of leers of the Government,” approved June eleventh, eighteen hundred and sixty-four.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of an act entitled “An act relating to members of Congress, heads of Departments, and other officers of the government,” approved June eleventh, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, be so extended as to apply in all respects to Delegates from the Territories, and the District or Territory of Columbia.


*Approved, January 16, 1873.*

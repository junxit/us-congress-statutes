# Chapter LIV

> An Act authorizing the President of the United States to re-establish the Monroe Land District in the State of Louisiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized, if in his judgment the public interests would be subserved thereby, to re-establish the Monroe land district in the State of Louisiana, with the same boundaries that existed before the consolidation of said land district with the land district at New Orleans.


*Approved, March 16, 1872.*

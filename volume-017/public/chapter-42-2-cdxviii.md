# Chapter CDXVIII

> An Act for the Relief of John C. Beaumont.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, restore John C. Beaumont to the position of captain on the active list, to take position at the foot of the list of captains: Provided, That the passage of this act shall not entitle the said John C. Beaumont to draw any additional back pay.


*Approved, June 10, 1872.*

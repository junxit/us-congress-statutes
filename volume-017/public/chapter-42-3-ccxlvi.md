# Chapter CCXLVI

> An Act to restore Absalom Kirby as second assistant Engineer in the Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, to restore Absalom Kirby to his original position on the active list as a second assistant engineer in the navy of the United States.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

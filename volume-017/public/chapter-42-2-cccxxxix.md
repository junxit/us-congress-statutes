# Chapter CCCXXXIX

> An Act to amend an Act entitled “An Act to establish a Uniform System of Bankruptcy throughout the United States.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first proviso in section fourteen of an act approved March second, eighteen hundred and sixty-seven, entitled “An act to establish a uniform system of bankruptcy throughout the United States,” be amended by striking out the words “eighteen hundred and sixty-four,” and inserting in lieu thereof “eighteen hundred and seventy-one.”


*Approved, June 8, 1872.*

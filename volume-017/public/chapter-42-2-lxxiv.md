# Chapter LXXIV

> An Act to authorize the Restoration of Gilbert Morton to the naval Service.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of I,he United States be, and he is hereby, authorized to nominate, and, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, to re-appoint Gilbert Mortou an ensign in the navy.


*Approved, April 1, 1872.*

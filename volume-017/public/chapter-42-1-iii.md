# Chapter III

> An Act relating to condemned Cannon for Cemetery at San Francisco.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War is hereby authorized to deliver to the Grand Army of the Republic G*mctery Association, of San Francisco, California, six pieces of condemned iron cannon and thirty cannon balls, to be used in the embellishment of the burial-grounds of said association.


*Approved, March 24, 1871.*

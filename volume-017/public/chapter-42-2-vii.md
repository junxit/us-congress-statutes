# Chapter VII

> An Act to establish Post-roads.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the following be established as post-roads:—

## PENNSYLVANIA.

From Lebanon, via Fontana, Colebrook, Mastersonville, Old Line, and Sporting Hill, to Manheim.

From Annville, via Bellview and Water Works, to Jonestown.


*Approved, January 16, 1872.*

# Chapter CLXXXVIII

> An Act for the Relief of Settlers on the late Sioux Indian Resection, in the State of Minnesota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That all actual settlers, who have duly filed their declaratory statements under the pre-emption laws, with the register of the proper local land-office, upon the unsold lands now included within the limits of the late Sioux Indian reservation in the State of Minnesota, shall be allowed until the first day of March, anno Domini, eighteen hundred and seventy-four, in which to make proof and payment for their claims.


*Approved, February 24, 1873.*

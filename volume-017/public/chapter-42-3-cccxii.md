# Chapter CCCXII

> An Act providing for the Completion of the, military Road from Santa Fé to Taos, in the Territory of Neto Mexico.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of twenty-five thousand dollars be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the treasury of the United States not otherwise appropriated, for the completion of the military road from the city of Sarita he to Pernandez de Taos, in the Territory of New Mexico, to be expended under the direction of the Secretary of War.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

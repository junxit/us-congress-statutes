# Chapter XXXIII

> An Act Io repeal the Paragraphs of Schedule Cof the infernal Revenue Acts imposing Taxes on canned Meats, Fish, and certain other Articles,

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the passage of this act so much of Schedule C of the internal revenue acts as imposes a tax on any “can. bottle, or other single package containing meats, fish, shellfish, fruits, vegetables, sauces, sirups, prepared mustard, jams, or jellies,” be, and the same is hereby, repealed.


*Approved, March 5, 1872.*

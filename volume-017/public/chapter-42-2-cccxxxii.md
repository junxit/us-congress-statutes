# Chapter CCCXXXII

> An Act to amend an Act entitled “An Act to regulate the diplomatic and consular Systems of the United States,” approved August eighteenth, eighteen hundred and fifty-six.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Schedule C of section three of an act entitled “An act to regulate the diplomatic and consular systems of the United States,” approved August eighteenth, eighteen hundred and fifty-six, be amended so as to add to the consuls in Brazil a consul at Santarem.


*Approved, June 8, 1872.*

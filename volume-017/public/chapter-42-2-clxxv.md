# Chapter CLXXV

> An Act to allow the Pleasure Yacht “Tied Hot” to take the Name of “Addie Parker,” and be registered under that Name.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pleasure yacht registered at New Bedford, named “Red Hot,” may, instead thereof, take the name of “Addie Parker,” and be registered under that name.


*Approved, May 18, 1872.*

# Chapter LXIII

> An Act for the Relief of preemption Settlers in die State of California.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all bona-fide preemption claimants who may have filed their declaratory statements, prior to the passage of tins act., upon any of the public lands in the State of California, shall have one year from and after the passage of this act in which to make proof and payment of their respective claims.


*Approved, March 22, 1872.*

# Chapter VI

> An Act authorizing the President to nominate R. H. Lamson a Lieutenant in the United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be, and hereby is, authorized to nominate K. II. Lamson a lieutenant in the United States navy.


*Approved, March 27, 1871.*

# Chapter XLIX

> An Act establishing Post-routes in the State of Maine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the following be established as post-roads:

Maine: From East Baldwin, via North Baldwin, Sebago, and South Bridgeton, to Bridgeton. From Gray Depotto Gray Corner, in the county of Cumberland.


*Approved, January 23, 1873.*

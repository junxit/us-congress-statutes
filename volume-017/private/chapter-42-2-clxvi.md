# Chapter CLXVI

> An Act authorizing the Issue of Clothing to certain enlisted Men of Company B, fourteenth Regiment of Infantry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized to issue clothing in lieu of and equal in value to that lost by those enlisted men of company B, of the fourteenth regiment of infantry, whose quarters in the barracks at Fort D. A. Russell, Wyoming Territory, were destroyed by fire on the morning of the sixth day of February, eighteen hundred and seventy-one.


*Approved, May 15, 1872.*

# Chapter CLIII

> An Act granting a Pension to Eliza J. Fracker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Eliza J. Fracker, who adopted James A. Markland when he was sixteen months old, and who died of disease contracted in the service, as first lieutenant of company F, second regiment Ohio infantry volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month, from the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 17, 1873.*

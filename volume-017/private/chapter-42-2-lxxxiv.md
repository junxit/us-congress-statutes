# Chapter LXXXIV

> An Act granting a Pension to Margaret Nyce.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Margaret Nyce, mother of William Nyce, late a private in company “K,” of the one hundredth Ohio volunteers, to take effect from the first day of January, eighteen hundred and seventy.


*Approved, April 2, 1872.*

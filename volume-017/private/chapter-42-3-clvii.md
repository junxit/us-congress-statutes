# Chapter CLVII

> An Act to remove the Disabilities of certain Persons herein named.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each house concurring therein,)*

That all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution upon Lafayette McLaws, Alfred Iverson, Martin J. Crawford, Lucius J. Gartrell, Goode Bryan, Benjamin C. Yancey, and John W. H. Underwood, of Georgia; William N. H. Smith, Burton Craig, and Samuel A. Ashe, of North Carolina; John V. Wright, of Tennessee; F. W. Latham, of Texas; David Clopton, of Alabama; and Isaac N. Brown, of Mississippi, be, and they are hereby, removed.


*Approved, February 17, 1873.*

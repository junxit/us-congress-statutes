# Chapter CLXIV

> An Act granting a Pension to Mary A. Shoemaker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary A. Shoemaker, widow of Horace Shoemaker, late a captain in the Missouri State militia, in lieu of the pension now held by her.


*Approved, February 18, 1873.*

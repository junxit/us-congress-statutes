# Chapter CDVII

> An Act granting a Pension to William E. Farley and John M. Farley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the names of William E. Farley and John M. Farley, minor children of A. J. Farley, late a private in company F, fifth regiment West Virginia infantry, to take effect from the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

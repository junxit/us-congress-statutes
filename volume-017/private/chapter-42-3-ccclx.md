# Chapter CCCLX

> An Act for the Relief of Jennie E. Savage.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Jennie E. Savage, a minor child of William E. Savage, who was a con tract-surgeon in the United States army, on the pension-list, and to pay to her legally authorized guardian the pension due her said father’s rank as surgeon, from the sixteenth day of February, anno Domini eighteen hundred and seventy-one, until she arrives at the age of sixteen years, unless she should die before that date.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

# Chapter CCCLIX

> An Act for the Relief of Frederick Walter, Company A, fourth United States Artillery.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and required to place the name of Frederick Walter, late private company A, fourth United States artillery, on the list of invalid pensioners, and to cause him to be paid at the rate of eight dollars per month from the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

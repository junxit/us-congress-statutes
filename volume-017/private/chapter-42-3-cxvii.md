# Chapter CXVII

> An Act granting a Pension to Robert G. Wilson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Robert G. Wilson, late a private in company A, seventy-ninth regiment New York volunteers, at the. rate of twenty-five dollars per month, in lieu of the pension now held and enjoyed by him.


*Approved, February 5, 1873.*

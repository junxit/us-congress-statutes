# Chapter CCCLXXXII

> An Act for the Relief of Captain Charles McClure.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers, in set th ng and adjusting the accounts of Charles McClure, cap tain and commissary of subsistence, United States army, are hereby directed to credit said McClure with the sum of eight hundred and forty-four dollars and sixty-three cents, on account of loss of that amount in eighteen hundred and sixty-nine, and eighteen hundred and seventy, by the embezzlement of a clerk in bis employ, the amount being lost without neglect or fault of said McClure.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

# Chapter CXI

> An Act granting a Pension to Garrett Flemming.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Garrett Flemming, late a private in the forty-third regiment of Ohio volunteers.


*Approved, February 5, 1873.*

# Chapter CLII

> An Act granting a Pension to Jane Thompson and Margaret Thompson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the names of Jane Thompson and Margaret Thompson, only minor children of John Thompson, late a private in company G, forty-fifth United States infantry.


*Approved, February 17, 1873.*

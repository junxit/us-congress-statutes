# Chapter CCCIV

> An Act to refund certain Duties paid by the Winona and Saint Peters Railroad Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be refunded to the Winona and Saint Peters Railroad Company, in gold, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of three thousand four hundred and twenty-four dollars and twenty-eight cents, being the additional duties paid by them on railroad iron under the joint resolution entitled “A joint resolution to increase temporarily the duties on imports,” approved April twenty-nine, eighteen hundred and sixty-four.


*Approved, June 4, 1872.*

# Chapter CDLXXVIII

> An Act for the Relief of W. W. Griffin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury are hereby required to audit and pay to W. W. Griffin, of Santa Fe, New Mexico, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, the sum of four thousand three hundred and fifty dollars for services rendered as commissioner under the laws of the United States.


*Approved, June 10, 1872.*

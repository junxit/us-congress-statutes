# Chapter CDXIX

> An Act granting a Pension to John Davis, Corpral of the Nightwatch on board the United States, Ship “Octorara.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Davis, a corporal of the night-watch on board the United States ship “Octorara,” and pay him at the rate of fifteen dollars per month, to commence from the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

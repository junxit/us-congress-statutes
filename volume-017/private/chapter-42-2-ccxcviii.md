# Chapter CCXCVIII

> An Act granting a Pension to William Freshour.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William Freshour, late a captain in company B of the eighth regiment of Ohio cavalry, from the twelfth day of October, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, deducting the amount received by him on pension certificate now held by him, and this act to be in lieu of said certificate of pension.


*Approved, June 4, 1872.*

# Chapter CCCLXXXVI

> An Act for the Relief of Rufus M. Pickel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the Treasury Department be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to credit Rufus M. Pickel, late internal revenue stamp agent at Mount Pleasant, Iowa, with five hundred and seventy-two dollars and fifty cents in his account, being the value of revenue stamps mailed to him in the ordinary course of business and never received by said Pickel, on being satisfied that said stamps were irrecoverably lost without fault on his part.


*Approved, June 8, 1872.*

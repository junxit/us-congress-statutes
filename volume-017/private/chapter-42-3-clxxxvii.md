# Chapter CLXXXVII

> An Act to relieve from Disabilities W. R. Boggs, of Missouri.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each house concurring therein,)*

That all political disabilities imposed upon W. R. Boggs, a citizen of Missouri, by reason of the fourteenth article of the amendments to the Constitution of the United States, be, and the same are hereby removed.


*Approved, February 22, 1873.*

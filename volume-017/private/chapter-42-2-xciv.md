# Chapter XCIV

> An Act granting a Pension to Sarah Whiting.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah Whiting, widow of Joseph B. Whiting, late a second lieutenant in the twenty-sixth regiment Massachusetts volunteers.


*Approved, April 9, 1872.*

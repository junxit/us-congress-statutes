# Chapter LXXVI

> An Act relating to the Capture of the Albemarle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the prize case of the rebel iron-clad man-of-war Albemarle shall be taken up and re-examined by the proper United States court, that the captors may obtain an appraisal such as is required by the prize laws of Congress.


*Approved, April 1, 1872.*

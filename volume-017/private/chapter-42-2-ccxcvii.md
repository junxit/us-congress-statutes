# Chapter CCXCVII

> An Act granting a Pension to Priscilla Harrison.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Priscilla Harrison, widow of Samuel Harrison, deceased, late a private in company C, sixty-third regiment of Pennsylvania volunteers, and afterwards a private in the one hundred and fifth regiment of Pennsylvania infantry volunteers, and that she be paid the same from the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 4, 1872.*

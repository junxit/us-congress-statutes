# Chapter CCCXVIII

> An Act for the Relief of Harmon E. Wentworth, late Second Lieutenant fourteenth New York Heavy Artillery.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Paymaster-General, United States army, is hereby authorized and directed to pay to Harmon E. Wentworth, late second lieutenant, company D, fourteenth regiment New York heavy artillery, the full pay and emoluments of a second lieutenant of heavy artillery, from the seventh day of June, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, to the sixteenth day of April, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, and also to allow him what he may have refunded of the “three months’ pay proper,” paid to him under act of March third, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, deducting therefrom all pay he may have received from the government as an enlisted man for that period.


*Approved, June 6, 1872.*

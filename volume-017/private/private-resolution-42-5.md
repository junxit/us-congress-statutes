# Private Resolution 5

> Joint Resolution to amend the Resolution for the Relief of James Keenan, late Consul at Hong-Kong, China, approved February eighteenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-seven.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the joint resolution approved February eighteenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-seven, for the relief of James Keenan, deceased, late consul at Hong-Kong, China, be, and the same is hereby, amended by striking out all after the words " the several balances due him," and inserting in lieu thereof the words “on his accounts for relief-of seamen.”


*Approved, May 13, 1872.*

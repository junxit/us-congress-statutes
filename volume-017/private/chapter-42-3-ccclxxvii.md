# Chapter CCCLXXVII

> An Act for the Relief of John L. Bullard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury, in set th ng and adjusting the accounts of John L. Bullard, late captain and commissary of subsistence of United States volunteers, be, and are hereby, authorized and directed to credit said John L. Bullard, late captain and commissary of subsistence of United States volunteers, with the sum of nine thousand six hundred and uinety dollars and fifty cents, being the amount of government flmds which was disbursed by him, without his default, on forged certificates for commutations of rations of prisoners—


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

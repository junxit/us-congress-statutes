# Chapter LXXVII

> An Act to pay William P. Preston the Sum therein mentioned.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid out of the Treasury of the United States, to William P. Preston, the sum of fourteen hundred and thirty-seven dollars and ninety-three cents, the same being for his expenses in contesting the seat of J. M. Harris, of Maryland, in the thirty-sixth Congress.


*Approved, April 1, 1872.*

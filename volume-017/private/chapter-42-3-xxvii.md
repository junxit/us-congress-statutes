# Chapter XXVII

> An Act repealing an Act entitled “An Act granting a Pension to Joanna L. Shaw.”

*Be it enacted big the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the act approved July twenty-seventh, eighteen hundred and sixty-eight, entitled “An act granting a pension to Joanna L. Shaw,” be, and the same is hereby, repealed.


*Approved, January 10, 1873.*

# Chapter CDLXV

> An Act for the Relief of Doctor John H. McQuown, of Illinois.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Paymaster-General cause to be allowed and paid, out of any money subject to his order for the pay of the army, to Doctor John H. McQuown, of Illinois, the sum of one thousand and twenty dollars, in full satisfaction for all military and medical services rendered the United States by said John II. McQuown as acting assistant surgeon of the sixty-second regiment of Illinois volunteer infantry.


*Approved, June 10, 1872.*

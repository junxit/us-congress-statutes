# Chapter LXXIII

> An Act granting a Pension to Jerome E. Pampell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jerome E. Pampell, late a private in the third regiment of Maryland volunteers, to take effect from the passage of this act.


*Approved, January 28, 1873.*

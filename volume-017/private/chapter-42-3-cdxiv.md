# Chapter CDXIV

> An Act granting a Pension to Emily Booth.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place, upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Emily Booth, widow of George F. Booth, late a private of company A, eighteenth regiment of Massachusetts volunteers, from the passage of this act, during her widowhood.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

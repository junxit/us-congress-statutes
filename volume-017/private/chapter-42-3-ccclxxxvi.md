# Chapter CCCLXXXVI

> An Act granting a Pension to William B. Lilly.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William B. Lilly, late captain of company B, first South Carolina vol unteers, in the Mexican war, to take effect from and alter the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

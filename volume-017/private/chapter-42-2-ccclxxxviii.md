# Chapter CCCLXXXVIII

> An Act granting a Pension to Abigail Ryan, Widow of Thomas A. Ryan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Abigail Ryan, whose name is now upon the pension roll, and to pay her a pension of two dollars per month from the passage of this act for her minor child under sixteen years of age.


*Approved, June 8, 1872.*

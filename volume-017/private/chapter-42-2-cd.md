# Chapter CD

> An Act granting a Pension to George W. Parris.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of George W. Parris, of Dayton, Ohio, at the rate of fifteen dollars per month, to take effect from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 8, 1872.*

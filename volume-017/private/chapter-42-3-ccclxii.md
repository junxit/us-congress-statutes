# Chapter CCCLXII

> An Act for the Relief of George F. Tracy, Postmaster at Bridgeport, Connecticut

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be authorized, in adjusting the accounts, of George F. Tracy postmaster at Bridgeport, Connecticut, to allow and credit him the sum of two thousand and eighty-five dollars, stolen from the post-office at Bridgeport, Connecticut on the first day of December, eighteen hundred and seventy-one, without any fault or negligence on the part of the said George F. Tracy


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

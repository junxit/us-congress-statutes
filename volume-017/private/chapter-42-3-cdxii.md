# Chapter CDXII

> An Act granting a Pension to Julia Cosgrove.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Julia Cosgrove, widow of Edward Cosgrove, late a private in company K, twenty-fourth regiment veteran reserve corps. This act to take effect from and after its passage.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

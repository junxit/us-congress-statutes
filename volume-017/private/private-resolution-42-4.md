# Private Resolution 4

> Joint Resolution giving the consent of Congress to Professor Joseph Henry, Secretary of the Smithsonian Institution, to accept the Title and Regalia of a Commander of the Royal Norwegian Order of St. Olaf conferred upon him by the King of Sweden and Norway, Grand Master of said Order.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the consent of Congress is hereby given to Professor Joseph Henry, secretary of the Smithsonian Institution, to accept the title and regalia of a commander of the Royal Norwegian Order of St. Olaf, conferred upon him for his distinguished scientific service and character by the king of Sweden and Norway, grand master of said order.


*Approved, April 20, 1871*

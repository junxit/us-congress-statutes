# Chapter CCCXVII

> An Act for the Relief of Wilson Bowlby, Collector of Internal Revenue for Oregon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and hereby is, directed to credit Wilson Bowlby, collector of internal revenue for Oregon, with one thousand one hundred and four dollars, the value of internal revenue stamps alleged to have been sent by mail on the twenty-sixth of April, eighteen hundred and seventy-one, to said Bowlby, and which never reached him.


*Approved, June 6, 1872.*

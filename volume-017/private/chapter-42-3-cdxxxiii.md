# Chapter CDXXXIII

> An Act granting a Pension to Susan Davis, dependent Mother of William W. Davis, deceased, late a Private in Company E, one hundred and third Regiment of Pennsylvania Infantry Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Susan Davis, dependent mother of William W. Davis, late a private in company E, one hundred and third regiment of Pennsylvania infantry volunteers, and that she be paid the same from the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

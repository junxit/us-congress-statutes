# Chapter CDXLVII

> An Act for the Relief of Horace B. Shepard, of Indiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there shall be refunded and paid to Horace B. Shepard, late collector of internal revenue for the first collection district of Indiana, the sum of one thousand and six dollars and seventy-five cents for beer-stamps, amounting to that sum, intrusted to him for sale while acting as such collector, and destroyed by fire by the burning of his office on the twenty-ninth day of March, in the year eighteen hundred and sixty-seven, and which were charged to, and have been accounted for by, him in the final settlement of his accounts with the government.


*Approved, June 10, 1872.*

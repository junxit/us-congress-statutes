# Chapter L

> An Act granting a Pension to Elizabeth Kenyon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elizabeth Kenyon, dependent mother of John T. Kenyon, late a private in company A, first regiment Rhode Island cavalry.


*Approved, March 14, 1872.*

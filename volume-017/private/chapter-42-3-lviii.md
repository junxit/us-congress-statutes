# Chapter LVIII

> An Act for the Relief of Captain Lyman J. Hissong, of Henry County, Ohio.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid to Lyman J. Hissong, of Ohio, the pay of captain from the eighteenth day of April, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, to the first day of August, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, while he actually served as captain in the ninth regiment of Louisiana infantry volunteers, colored, after he was by leave mustered out of the sixty-eighth regiment Ohio volunteers, and before he was formally mustered into the said Louisiana regiment.


*Approved, January 23, 1873.*

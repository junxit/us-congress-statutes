# Chapter LXXXVII

> An Act granting a Pension to Charles B. Fairchild.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Charles B. Fairchild, late a first lieutenant in company “A,” first regiment New York veteran volunteer cavalry.


*Approved, January 31, 1873.*

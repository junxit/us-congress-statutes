# Chapter CCXV

> An Act granting a Pension to Elizabeth O’Neill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elizabeth O’Neill, dependent mother of John O’Neill, late a private in company I, United States mounted rifles, and pay her a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, May 25, 1872.*

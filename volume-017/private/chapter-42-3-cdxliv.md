# Chapter CDXLIV

> An Act for the removal of the political Disabilities of I. Ernest Meiere, of the State of Maryland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each house concurring therein,)*

That all political disabilities of I. Ernest Meiere, of the State of Maryland, incurred under the provisions of the fourteenth amend ment of the Constitution of the United States be, and the same are hereby removed.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

# Chapter CDLXX

> An Act for the Relief of William M. Stafford, late acting District Attorney for Montana Territory.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to pay, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to William M. Stafford, the sum of five hundred dollars, as compensation for his services rendered as acting United States district attorney for Montana Territory during the years one thousand eight hundred and sixty-six and seven.


*Approved, June 10, 1872.*

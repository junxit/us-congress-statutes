# Chapter LXVII

> An Act granting a Pension to George J. St. Louis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George J. St. Louis, late a private in company A, thirty-third regiment Illinois volunteer infantry, to take effect from the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 27, 1872.*

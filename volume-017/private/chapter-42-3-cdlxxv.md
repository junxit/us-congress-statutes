# Chapter CDLXXV

> An Act granting a Pension to Mrs. Mollie L. Roberts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Mrs. Mollie L. Roberts, widow of John S. Roberts, late first lieutenant in the twenty-second regiment of Indiana volunteers, on the pension roll, at the rate of fifteen dollars per month from the death of her husband, who at the tune of his death was receiving a pension as such lieutenant, and to continue during her widowhood.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

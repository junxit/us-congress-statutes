# Chapter CVI

> An Act to confirm the Title of John Boyer to certain Lands therein described.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the title of John Boyer to the south half of northwest quarter and northeast quarter of southwest quarter of section thirty-four, township thirty-three, range twenty-eight west, one hundred and twenty acres, in the district of lands subject to location at Springfield, Missouri, be, and is hereby, confirmed; and the Secretary of the Interior shall cause to be issued to the said John Boyer a patent for said land on bounty-land warrant location thirty-nine thousand five hundred and twenty-six, one hundred and twenty acres, act March third, eighteen hundred and fifty-five, first correcting the location papers and records to cover the aforesaid tracts.


*Approved, April 17, 1872.*

# Chapter LX

> An Act for the Relief of Harriet W. Pond.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in consideration of injuries received during the war of the rebellion, the sum of one thousand dollars be paid to Mrs. Harriet W. Pond, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, January 23, 1873.*

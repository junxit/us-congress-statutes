# Chapter CCCXCI

> An Act granting a Pension to Mary Mullins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Mullins, widow of Thomas Mullins, late of company B, first regiment Iowa cavalry volunteers, at the rate of eight dollars per month, from the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

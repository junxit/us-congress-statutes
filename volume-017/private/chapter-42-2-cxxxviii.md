# Chapter CXXXVIII

> An Act granting a Pension to the minor Children of Amanda M. Ritchey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the names of the minor children under sixteen years of age of Amanda M Ritchey, deceased, widow of Powers Ritchey, late a contract surgeon in the United States army.


*Approved, May 2, 1872.*

# Chapter CDLXV

> An Act for the Relief of Grace B. Peck.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from the fourteenth day of September, eighteen hundred and fifty-five to the nineteenth day of February, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, Grace B. Peck, widow of Captain Elisha Peck, shall be allowed the difference between the pay her late husband received as commander and the pay of a captain on the retired list of the United States navy, at that time.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

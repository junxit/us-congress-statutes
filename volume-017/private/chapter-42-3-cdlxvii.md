# Chapter CDLXVII

> An Act granting a Pension to Ann E. Boyle, Widow of Commodore Junius J. Boyle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the navy pension rolls the name of Ann E. Boyle, widow of Commodore Junius J. Boyle, late of the United States navy; to receive from and after the passage of this act, the pay of a lieutenant in the navy, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

# Chapter CCCXCVII

> An Act granting a Pension to Antoine Brossa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Antoine Brossa, late a private in company I, eighty-third regiment New York volunteers; this act to take efiect from and after its passage.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

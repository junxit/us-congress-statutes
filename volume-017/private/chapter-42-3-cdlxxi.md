# Chapter CDLXXI

> An Act to remove the political Disabilities of David Coleman, of Buncombe County, North Carolina.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Unitea States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each house concurring therein,)*

That the political disabilities imposed on David Coleman, of Buncombe county, North Carolina, by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution, be, and they are hereby, removed


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

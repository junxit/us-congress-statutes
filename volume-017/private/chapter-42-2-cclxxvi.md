# Chapter CCLXXVI

> An Act granting a Pension to Abraham Cooper.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Abraham Cooper, late a private in company K, first United States cavalry, to take effect from the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 1, 1872.*

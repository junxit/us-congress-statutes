# Chapter CCLXXI

> An Act granting a Pension to Andrew Reinfort.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Andrew Reinfort, late a private in company B, fifty-eighth regiment Indiana volunteers.


*Approved, June 1, 1872.*

# Chapter CDLVIII

> An Act for the Relief of Thomas Hollis, of Morgan County, Georgia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid to Thomas Hollis, of Morgan county, Georgia, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of two hundred and twenty-four dollars and fourteen cents, due him for earning the mails of the United States in the year eighteen hundred and sixfy-one, according to the records of the Post-office Department.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

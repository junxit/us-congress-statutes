# Chapter CDXXXIX

> An Act granting a Pension to Chancy J. Poore.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Chancy J. Poore, late a private in battery G, first New York light artillery; this act to take effect from and after its passage.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

# Chapter XVII

> An Act granting a Pension to Sarah Evans.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah Evans, mother of L. G. Evans, late sergeant company K, twenty-first United States infantry, and pay her a pension from the passage of this act, and to continue during life.


*Approved, February 7, 1872.*

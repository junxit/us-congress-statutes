# Chapter CXCIV

> An Act granting a Pension to John Greenwait.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Greenwait, late a private in company F, twelfth regiment of Illinois volunteers.


*Approved, February 24, 1873.*

# Chapter CDXLII

> An Act granting a Pension to Millie A. Powell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Millie A. Powell, widow of John Powell, late a private in company B, third regiment Indiana cavalry volunteers, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

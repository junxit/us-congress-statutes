# Chapter CCCLXXXIX

> An Act granting a Pension to Ann Woods

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ann Woods, widow of Bernard Woods, late a private in company D, twenty-eighth regiment of Massachusetts volunteers, to take effect from and after the passage of this act—


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

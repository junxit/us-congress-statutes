# Chapter CI

> An Act for the Relief of F. B. Brayton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be and hereby is, authorized and directed to credit (as of the twenty-fourth day of May, anno Domini eighteen hundred and sixty-eight) to F. B. Brayton, postmaster of Mount Morris, Ogle county, in the State of Illinois, the sum of three hundred and fifteen dollars, in consideration of the loss of that amount in postage-stamps robbed from his post-office in Mount Morris, without his fault or negligence.


*Approved, April 16, 1872.*

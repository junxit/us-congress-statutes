# Chapter XCII

> An Act granting a Pension to Frederick A. Garlick, of Cortland County New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Frederick A. Garlick, of Cortland county, New York, upon the pension roll, at the rate of fifteen dollars per month, to commence from the passage of this act.


*Approved, April 9, 1872.*

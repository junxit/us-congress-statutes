# Chapter CXVI

> An Act granting a Pension to Mrs. Abigail Chaplin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pen- sion roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Abigail Chaplin, who was the widow of Josiah Snelling, junior, late a colonel in the United States army, to take effect from and after the first day of January, eighteen hundred and seventy.


*Approved, February 5, 1873.*

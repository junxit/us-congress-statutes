# Chapter CDXV

> An Act granting a Pension to Ada Lois Harrison.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ada Lois Harrison, only minor child of Samuel Harrison, late a private of company I, eighty-ninth Indiana volunteers.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

# Chapter CCCLXXIV

> An Act for the Relief of P. W. Standifer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officer of the United States be authorized and directed to audit and allow the claim of P. W. Standefer, for services rendered as a telegraph operator by direction of the military authorities of the United States in the war of the rebellion, for a period not exceeding six months, at the rate of one hundred dollars per month, and that the same be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated: Provided, That it shall be satisfactorily proved that such services were actually rendered.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

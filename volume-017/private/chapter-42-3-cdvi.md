# Chapter CDVI

> An Act granting a Pension to Henry Riemann.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Henry Riemann, late a private in the twelfth regiment of Maine volunteers, to take effect from the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

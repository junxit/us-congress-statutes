# Chapter CDXLV

> An Act to remove Disabilities imposed by the fourteenth Amendment of the Constitution of the United States from Robert M T. Hunter of Virginia

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each house concurring therein,)*

That all disabilities imposed by the third section of the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States be, and the same are hereby, removed from Robert M. T. Hunter, of Virginia.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

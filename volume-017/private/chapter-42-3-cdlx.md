# Chapter CDLX

> An Act for the Relief of George R. Wilson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to George R. Wilson, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of twenty thousand dollars as payment in full for his patent in the construction of iron, or metallic, broad side carriages in the naval service the same to be in full for past and future use of said patent by the government of the United States.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

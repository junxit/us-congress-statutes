# Chapter CCCLXXVIII

> An Act to relieve Charles J. Faulkner, of West Virginia, from the legal and political Disabilities imposed by the fourteenth Amendment of the Constitution of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each house concurring therein,)*

That the disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States, by reason of participation in the late rebellion, be, and they hereby are, removed from Charles J. Faulkner, of West Virginia.


*Approved, June 8, 1872.*

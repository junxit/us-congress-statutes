# Chapter CLIV

> An Act granting a Pension to Mrs. Mary Davis, of Philadelphia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Mary Davis, mother of Charles B. Davis, deceased, late captain of company F, sixth Pennsylvania cavalry volunteers, on the pension roll, at the rate of twenty dollars per month, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 17, 1873.*

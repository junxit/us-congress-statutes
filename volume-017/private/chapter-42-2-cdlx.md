# Chapter CDLX

> An Act for the Relief of Harriet Spring, the Heir of Captain Williams Barker, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid to Harriet Spring, of Waterville, Maine, the heir of Captain Williams Barker, of Waterville, Maine, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the half-pay of a captain from the end of the revolutionary war to the death of Captain Barker, February nineteenth, eighteen hundred and nineteen; and that a warrant be issued to the said Harriet Spring for such bounty-land as she, in virtue of the services of the said Captain Williams Barker, deceased, as a private soldier in said war is entitled to receive.


*Approved, June 10, 1872.*

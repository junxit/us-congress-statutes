# Chapter XLI

> An Act to reimburse certain Soldiers for Loss of Clothing by Fire at Chicago.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War is hereby authorized to issue, without charge, to certain soldiers of the general mounted service, United States army, sufficient clothing, or the money value thereof, to replace the amount lost by each of them during their efforts to subdue the fire in Chicago: Provided, That the total value of such clothing shall not, in the aggregate, exceed the sum of one hundred and thirty dollars.


*Approved, March 11, 1872.*

# Chapter CCLXXV

> An Act granting a Pension to Margaret C. Gibson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Margaret C. Gibson, widow of Jacob Gibson, who was killed while on duty as a scout in the United States service, and pay her a pension at eight dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 1, 1872.*

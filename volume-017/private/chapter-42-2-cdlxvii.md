# Chapter CDLXVII

> An Act for the Relief of Seth M. Whitten, late Private of the fourth Regiment, Michigan Infantry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Adjutant-General of the army be, and he is hereby, directed to remove the charge of desertion from the name of Seth M. Whitten, late private company K, fourth regiment of Michigan infantry, and that the said Seth M. Whitten shall be allowed and paid the back pay and bounty due him, in the same manner and to the same extent as if the said charge of desertion had never been made.


*Approved, June 10, 1872.*

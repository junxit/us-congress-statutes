# Chapter XCI

> An Act granting a Pension to Lucretia B. Galloway.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lucretia B. Galloway, widow of John M. Galloway, late a corporal in company C of the seventh regiment of Missouri militia, to take effect from the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 1, 1873.*

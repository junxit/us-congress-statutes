# Chapter CXXIV

> An Act to remove the Disabilities of William A. Graham, a Citizen of North Carolina.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each house concurring therein,)*

That all legal and political disabilities imposed by the Constitution of the United States upon William A. Graham, a citizen of North Carolina, in consequence of participation in the recent rebellion, be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, February 8, 1873.*

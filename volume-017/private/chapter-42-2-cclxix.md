# Chapter CCLXIX

> An Act granting a Pension to Martha Elwell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Martha Elwell, the dependent widowed mother of Newell Elwell, a private in the late war of the rebellion.


*Approved, June 1, 1872.*

# Chapter CXXXVI

> An Act granting a Pension to John Carbaugh, a Private of Company A, forty-sixth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Carbaugh, a private of company A, in the forty-sixth regiment Pennsylvania volunteers.


*Approved, May 2, 1872.*

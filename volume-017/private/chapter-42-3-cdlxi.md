# Chapter CDLXI

> An Act for the Relief of Charity Lodge Number one hundred and eleven of Free and Accepted Masons of Harper’s Ferry West Virginia

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of seven hundred and sixty-seven dollars and thirty-nine cents be and the same is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appro priated to reimburse Charity Lodge number one hundred and eleven of Free and Accepted Masons of Harper’s Ferry West Virginia, for ex penses incurred by said lodge in refitting their lodge-room which said expense was made necessary by the occupation of the same by the United States troops from the year eighteen hundred and sixty-one to the year eighteen hundred and sixty-four.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

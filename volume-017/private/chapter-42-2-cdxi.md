# Chapter CDXI

> An Act for the Relief of John Milone, Postmaster at Uhrichsville, Ohio.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed, in settling the accounts of John Milone, postmaster at Uhrichsville, Ohio, to allow and credit to him the sum of two hundred and ten dollars, an amount of postage-stamps stolen from his office by burglars on the night of Octo her six, eighteen hundred and sixty-eight, without fault or neglect on his part.


*Approved, June 8, 1872.*

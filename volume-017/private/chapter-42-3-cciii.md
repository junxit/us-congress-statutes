# Chapter CCIII

> An Act for the Relief of Henry B. Mears.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Paymaster-General is hereby directed to pay to Henry B. Mears, late a first lieutenant in the thirty-sixth regiment of Indiana volunteers, out of any money appropriated or that may be appropriated for the pay of the army, the full pay and emoluments of a first lieutenant of infantry in the army of the United States, from the fourteenth day of December, eighteen hundred and sixty-one, to October nineteenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, deducting therefrom any sum received by him for services rendered during said period in the army of the United States.


*Approved, February 25, 1873.*

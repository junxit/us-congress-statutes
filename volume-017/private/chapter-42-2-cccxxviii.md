# Chapter CCCXXVIII

> An Act to remove the political Disabilities of the Persons therein named.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all legal and political dis- abilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States by reason of participation in the late rebellion be, and they are hereby, removed from the following persons, namely: Alexander R. Boteler, of West Virginia; John Young Brown, of Henderson, Kentucky.


*Approved, June 7, 1872.*

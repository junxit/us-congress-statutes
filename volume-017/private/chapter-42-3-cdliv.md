# Chapter CDLIV

> An Act for the Relief of Joseph Lanman

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid to Joseph Lanman, rear-admiral in the United States navy, the difference between the pay of a commodore and that of a rear-admiral of the United States navy, from December seventh, eighteen hundred and sixty-seven, to April fourteenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-eight.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

# Chapter CCCLXXXIII

> An Act for the Relief of Hugh McCormick.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to pay Hugh McCor mick, receiving and disbursing clerk of the Patent-Office, out of any moneys belonging to the Patent-Office, such sum, in addition towhat he may already have received since his appointment as a clerk of the fourth class, in eighteen hundred and tifty-five, as will make his entire compen sation, during the time he has performed the duties of receiving and disbursing clerk, equal to that of the other disbursing clerks in the De partment of the Interior.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

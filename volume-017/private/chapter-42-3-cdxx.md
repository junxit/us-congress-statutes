# Chapter CDXX

> An Act granting a Pension to Samuel B. Davis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Samuel B. Davis, late a corporal in company C, eighteenth regiment Indiana volunteers.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

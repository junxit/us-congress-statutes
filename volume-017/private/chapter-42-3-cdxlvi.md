# Chapter CDXLVI

> An Act to remove the Disabilities of Shelton F. Leake, of Albemarle County, Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each house concurring,)*

That all political disabilities imposed by the third section of the fourteenth article of amendments to the Constitution of the United States be, and they are hereby removed from Shelton F. Leake, of Albemarle county, in the State of Virginia.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

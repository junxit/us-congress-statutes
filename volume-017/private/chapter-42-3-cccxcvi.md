# Chapter CCCXCVI

> An Act granting a Pension to Harper Smith.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Harper Smith, late a private in company B, twenty-third regiment Pennsylvania volunteers, commencing from the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1873.*

# Chapter XCIII

> An Act granting a Pension to Sarah Baird.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah Baird, widow of Thomas Baird, late a sergeant in company C, fourth Pennsylvania cavalry, to take effect from the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 1, 1873.*

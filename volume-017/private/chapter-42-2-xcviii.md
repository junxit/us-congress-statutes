# Chapter XCVIII

> An Act granting a Pension to Margaret Lee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Margaret Lee, mother of David Lee, late a private in company B, fourteenth Ohio volunteers, and to pay her a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, April 12, 1872.*

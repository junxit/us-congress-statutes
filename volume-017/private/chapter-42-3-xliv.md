# Chapter XLIV

> An Act for the Relief of Theodore Adams.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid to Theodore Adams, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one hundred and twelve thousand seven hundred and forty dollars and seventy-six cents, in full for all claim under his contracts with the government of the United States for the construction of thirty-eight mortar-boats and eight steam-tugs.


*Approved, January 20, 1873.*

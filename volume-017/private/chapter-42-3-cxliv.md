# Chapter CXLIV

> An Act relating to the Claim of John B. Chapman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the commissioner of Indian affairs be, and be is hereby, authorized and required to investigate the claim of John B. Chapman against the Osage nation of Indians, and report to Congress what, if anything, is justly due said claimant from the Indians or the government; if anything, how much.


*Approved, February 14, 1873.*

# Public Law 88–488

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 1965, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House, of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, clause (c) of section 102 of the joint resolution of June 29, 1964 (Public Law 88–325), is hereby amended by striking out “August 31, 1964” and inserting in lieu thereof “September 30, 1964”.


*Approved August 22, 1964.*

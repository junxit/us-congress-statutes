# Public Law 88–296

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making a supplemental appropriation for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1964, for disaster relief, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the following sum is appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1964, namely:

## Funds Appropriated to the President

## disaster relief

For an additional amount for “Disaster relief“, $50,000,000, to remain available until expended: Provided, That not to exceed 3 per centum of the foregoing amount shall be available for administrative expenses.


*Approved April 7, 1964.*

# Public Law 88–637

> AN ACT To authorize removal of a flight hazard at the United States Naval Air Station, Norfolk, Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy is hereby authorized to remove an existing flight hazard at the Naval Air Station, Norfolk, Virginia; but no funds may be expended for such purpose unless specifically appropriated for such purpose.


*Approved October 8, 1964.*

# Public Law 88–529

> AN ACT To extend for three years the special milk programs for the Armed Forces and veterans hospitals.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 202 of the Agricultural Act of 1949, as amended (7 U.S.C. 1446a), is amended by striking in subsections (a) and (b) the words “December 31, 1964,” and inserting in lieu thereof “December 31, 1967”.


*Approved August 31, 1964.*

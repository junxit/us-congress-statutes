# Public Law 88–466

> AN ACT To amend the joint resolution approved August 20, 1958, granting the consent of Congress to the several States to negotiate and enter into compacts for the purpose of promoting highway traffic safety.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the joint resolution approved August 20, 1958 (72 Stat. 635), is amended by inserting in the resolving clause after the word “States” the phrase “, and one or more of the several States and the District of Columbia,”.


*Approved August 20, 1964.*

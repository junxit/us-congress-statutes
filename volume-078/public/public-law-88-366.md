# Public Law 88–366

> JOINT RESOLUTION To authorize the President to proclaim December 7, 1966, as Pearl Harbor Day in commemoration of the twenty-fifth anniversary of the attack on Pearl Harbor.

Whereas December 7, 1966, will mark the twenty-fifth anniversary of the attack on Pearl Harbor; and Whereas the steadfast heroism of American forces before the unforeseen onslaught was an inspiration throughout the grim and terrible struggle which followed; and Whereas the bright beacon of courage then ignited will burn forever in the hearts of freemen: Now, therefore, be it Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

That the President is hereby authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating December 7, 1966, as Pearl Harbor Day, and calling upon the people of the United States to observe such day with appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Approved July 9, 1964.*

# Public Law 88–480

> AN ACT To extend the authority of the Postmaster General to enter into leases of real property for periods not exceeding thirty years, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2109 of title 39, United States Code, is amended to read us follows:

> ## § 2109. Time limitations on agreements
>
> “Agreements may not be entered into under sections 2104 and 2105 of this title after July 22, 1964, and under section 2103 after December 31, 1966.”


*Approved August 22, 1964.*

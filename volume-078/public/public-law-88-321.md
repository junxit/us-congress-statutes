# Public Law 88–321

> JOINT RESOLUTION To increase the amount authorized to be appropriated for the work of the President’s Committee on Employment of the Physically Handicapped.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the joint resolution entitled “Joint Resolution authorizing an appropriation for the work of the President’s Committee on National Employ the Physically Handicapped Week”, approved July 11, 1949 (63 Stat. 409), as amended, is amended by striking out “$300,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$400,000”.


*Approved June 24, 1964.*

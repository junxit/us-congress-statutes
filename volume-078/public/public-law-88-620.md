# Public Law 88–620

> AN ACT To authorize the promotion of qualified Reserve officers of the Army arid the Air Force to existing unit vacancies.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That clauses (6) and (48), section 1, of the Act of June 30, 1960, Public Law 86–559 (74 Stat. 264), are each amended by striking out “July 1, 1964” in the last sentence and inserting “July 1, 1965” in place thereof.

## § 2.

Section 3383(e) of title 10, United States Code, is amended by striking out “July 1, 1964” and inserting “July 1, 1965” in place thereof.


*Approved October 3, 1964.*

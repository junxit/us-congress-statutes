# Public Law 88–649

> JOINT RESOLUTION Fixing the time of assembly of the Eighty-ninth Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Eighty-ninth Congress shall assemble at noon on Monday, January 4, 1965.


*Approved October 13, 1964.*

# Public Law 88–362

> AN ACT To continue for two years the existing suspensions of duty on certain alumina and bauxite.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** items 907.15, 909.30, and 911.05 of title I of the Tariff Act of 1930 (Tariff Schedules of the United States; 28 F.R., part II, pages 432 and 433, Aug. 17, 1963) are-each amended by striking out “On or before 7/15/64” and inserting in lieu thereof “On or before 7/15/66”.
- **(b)** The amendments made by subsection (a) shall apply with respect to articles entered, or withdrawn from warehouse, for consumption after July 15, 1964.


*Approved July 7, 1964.*

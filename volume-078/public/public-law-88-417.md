# Public Law 88–417

> AN ACT To repeal the Art of October 22, 1919 (41 Stat. 283; 43 U.S.C. 351–355–357–360).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, subject to any valid rights and obligations existing on the date of approval of this Act, the Act of October 22, 1919 (41 Stat. 293; 43 U.S.C. 351–355, 357–360), is hereby repealed.

## § 2.

Any valid application for permit under that Act, on file with the Secretary of the Interior on the effective date of this Act, may lie processed in the same manner as if this Act had not been enacted.


*Approved August 11, 1964.*

# Public Law 88–481

> AN ACT To provide hospital, domiciliary, and medical care for non-service-connected disabilities to recipients of the Medal of Honor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph (2) of section 601 of title 38, United States Code, is amended by inserting immediately after “Indian Wars” the following: “, or any veteran awarded the Medal of Honor”.


*Approved August 22, 1964.*

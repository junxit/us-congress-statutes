# Public Law 88–401

> AN ACT To amend section 801 of title 38, United States Code, to provide assistance in acquiring specialty adapted housing for certain blind veterans who have suffered the loss or loss of use of a lower extremity.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph (2) of section 801 of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking out “, and such permanent and total disability is such as to preclude locomotion without the aid of a wheelchair,” and inserting in lieu thereof a semicolon.


*Approved August 4, 1964.*

# Public Law 88–468

> AN ACT To enable the United States to contribute its share at the expenses of the International Commission for Supervision and Control in Laos as provided in article IS of the protocol to the declaration on the neutrality of Laos.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated to the Department of State such sums as may be necessary from time to time for the payment by the United States of its share of the costs of the operations of the International Commission for Supervision and Control in Laos as provided in article 18 of the protocol to the declaration on the neutrality of Laos dated July 23, 1982.


*Approved August 20, 1964.*

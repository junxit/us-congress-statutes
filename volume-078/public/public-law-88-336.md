# Public Law 88–336

> AN ACT To continue until the close of June 30, 1966, the existing suspension of duty on certain copying shoe lathes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** item 911.70 of title I of the Tariff Act of 1930 (Tariff Schedules of the United States; 28 F.R., part II, page 434, Aug. 17, 1963) is amended by striking out “On or before 8/7/64” and inserting in lieu thereof “On or before 6/30/66”.
- **(b)** The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply with respect to articles entered, or withdrawn from warehouse, for consumption, after August 7, 1964.


*Approved June 30, 1964.*

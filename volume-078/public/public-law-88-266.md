# Public Law 88–266

> AN ACT To authorize the transportation of privately owned motor vehicles of Government employees assigned to duty in Alaska, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1(f) of the Administrative Expenses Act of 1946 (5 U.S.C. 73b–l(f)) is amended by adding at the end thereof a new sentence as follows: “For the purposes of this subsection and subsection (e), Alaska shall be considered to be outside the continental limits of the United States.”


*Approved February 5, 1964.*

# Public Law 88–663

> AN ACT To provide for the disposition of judgment funds now on deposit to the credit of the Red Luke Band of Chippewa Indians.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the funds on deposit, in the Treasury of the United States to the credit of the Red Lake Band of Chippewa Indians that were appropriated by the Act of June 9, 1964, to pay a judgment by the Indian Claims Commission in docket 18A, and the interest thereon, after payment of attorney fees and expenses, may be advanced or expended for any purpose that is authorized by the tribal governing body and approved by the Secretary of the Interior. Any part of such funds that may be distributed per capita to the members of the tribe shall not be subject to Federal or State income tax.


*Approved October 13, 1964.*

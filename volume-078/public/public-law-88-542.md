# Public Law 88–542

> AN ACT To amend the Railway Labor Act to provide that the terms of office of members of the National Mediation Board shall expire on July 1.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the third sentence of the initial paragraph of section 4 of the Railway Labor Act is amended to read as follows: “Each member of the Mediation Board in office on January 1, 1965, shall be deemed to have been appointed for a term of office which shall expire on July 1 of the year his term would have otherwise expired.” Such paragraph is further amended by inserting at the end thereof the following new sentence: “Upon the expiration of bis term of office a member shall continue to serve until his successor is appointed and shall have qualified.”


*Approved August 31, 1964.*

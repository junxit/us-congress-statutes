# Public Law 88–530

> AN ACT To amend section 25 of title 13, United States Code, relating to the duties of enumerators of the Bureau of the Census, Department of Commerce.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, That,*

That subsection (c) of section 25 of title 13, United States Code (relating to certain duties of enumerators of the Bureau of the Census, Department of Commerce) , is hereby repealed.


*Approved August 31, 1964.*

# Public Law 88–580

> AN ACT To authorize the mint to inscribe the figure 1984 on all coins minted until adequate supplies of coins are available.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding section 3517 of the Revised Statutes (31 U.S.C. 324), all coins minted from the date of enactment of this Act until July 1 or January 1, whichever date first occurs after the date on which the Secretary of the Treasury determines that adequate supplies of coins are available, shall be inscribed with the figure “1964” in lieu of the year of the coinage.

## § 2.

The requirement of section 3550 of the Revised Statutes (31 U.S.C. 366) that the obverse working dies at each mint shall be destroyed at the end of each calendar year shall not be applicable during the period provided for in section 1 of this Act.


*Approved September 3, 1964.*

# Public Law 88–326

> AN ACT Authorizing: a utility of dust control measures at Long Island, Port Isabel, Texas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Chief of Engineers is hereby authorized to undertake a study of the adverse effects of dust storms from Long Island, Port Isabel, Texas, at a cost not to exceed $50,000, with a view toward establishing such remedial and protective measures as in his judgment may be deemed necessary to prevent said adverse effects.


*Approved June 29, 1964.*

# Public Law 88–651

> AN ACT To amend section 500 of title 38, United States Code, to permit the payment of special pension to holders of the Congressional Medal of Honor awarded such medal for actions not Involving conflict with an enemy, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 560(b) of title 38, United States Code, is amended (1) by striking out “fifty years” and inserting in lieu thereof “forty years” and (2) by striking out “beyond the call of duty” and all that follows through the end thereof and inserting in lieu thereof “beyond the call of duty while so serving.”


*Approved October 13, 1964.*

# Public Law 88–397

> AN ACT Granting a renewal of patent numbered D–102,975, relating to a medal of the American Legion.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a certain design patent issued by the United States Patent Office, dated April 17, 1951, being patent numbered D–162,975, is hereby renewed and extended for a period of fourteen years from and after the date of approval of this Act, with all the rights and privileges pertaining to the same, being generally known as a medal of the American Legion.


*Approved August 1, 1964.*

# Public Law 88–616

> AN ACT To authorize certain veterans’ benefits for disability or death resulting from injuries sustained prior to January 1, 1957, by reservists while proceeding directly to or returning directly from active duty for training or inactive duty training.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection 106(d)(2) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking out “after December 31, 1956,”.


*Approved October 2, 1964.*

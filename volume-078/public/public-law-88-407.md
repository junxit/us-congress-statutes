# Public Law 88–407

> AN ACT To change the name of the United States Olympic Association to the United States Olympic Committee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the corporation known as the United States Olympic Association, which was incorporated by the Act entitled “An Act to incorporate the United States Olympic Association,” approved September 21, 1950 (64 Stat. 899), shall be known and designated hereafter as the United States Olympic Committee and any reference to such corporation under the name of the United States Olympic Association shall be held to refer to such corporation under and by the name of the United States Olympic Committee.


*Approved August 10, 1964.*

# Public Law 88–404

> AN ACT Declaring a portion of Bayou Black and Bayou Terrebonne, Louisiana, non-navigable waterways of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** Bayou Black, Terrebonne Parish, Louisiana, between the proposed location of an earthen plug and dam (approximately 500 feet east from the city limits of Houma, Louisiana) and that point where the Houma Canal joins said stream; and (b) Bayou Terrebonne, Terrebonne Parish, Louisiana, between the point where Bar row Street crosses said stream and a line determined by prolonging and extending the eastern right-of-way line of New Orleans Boulevard in a southerly direction to the south bank of said stream; be, and the same are hereby, declared to be not navigable waters of the United States within the meaning of the laws of the United States.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved August 7, 1964.*

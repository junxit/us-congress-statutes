# Public Law 88–263

> JOINT RESOLUTION To amend the joint resolution of January 28, 1948, relating to membership and participation by the United States in the South Pacific Commission, so as to authorize certain appropriations thereunder for the fiscal years 1965 and 1966.

*Resolved by the Semite and, House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3(a) of the joint, resolution entitled “Joint resolution providing for membership and participation by the United States in the South Pacific Commission and authorizing an appropriation therefor”, approved January 28, 1948 (22 U.S.C. 280b(a)), is amended by striking out “$100,000 annually” and inserting in lieu thereof “$150,000 for the fiscal year 1965, and $150,000 for the fiscal year 1966,”.


*Approved January 31, 1964.*

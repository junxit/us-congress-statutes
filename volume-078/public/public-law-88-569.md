# Public Law 88–569

> AN ACT To amend the Act of March 10, 1964.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 6 of the Act of March 10, 1964 (78 Stat. 156), is hereby amended by substituting the figures “$3,200,000” for the figures “$2,000,000”.


*Approved September 2, 1964.*

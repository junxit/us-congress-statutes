# Public Law 88–628

> JOINT RESOLUTION To authorize the President to proclaim October 15 of each year as White Cane Safety Day.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is hereby authorized to issue annually a proclamation designating October 15 as White Cane Safety Day and calling upon the people of the United States to observe such day with appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Approved October 6, 1964.*

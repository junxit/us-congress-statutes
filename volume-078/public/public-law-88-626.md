# Public Law 88–626

> AN ACT To disclaim any title of the United States to certain real property in Modoc County, California.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States disclaims any right, title, or interest it may have, derived from its original ownership of the land as acquired by the Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo, including, without limitation, any right, title, or interest stemming from the doctrines of accretion, reliction, or lands omitted from survey, in or to real property situated in the bed or former bed of Pelican Lake, also known as Cowhead Lake, in township 47 north, range 17 east. Mount Diablo meridian, Modoc County, California.


*Approved October 3, 1964.*

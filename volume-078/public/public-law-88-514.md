# Public Law 88–514

> AN ACT To amend the District of Columbia Unemployment Compensation Act, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the third sentence of subsection (f) of section 13 of the District of Columbia Unemployment Compensation Act approved August 28, 1935 (49 Stat. 946), as amended (sec. 46–313 (f), D.C. Code, 1961 edition), is amended by inserting “, or the Department of Public Welfare of the government of the District of Columbia, or the United States Accounting Office” immediately after “public employment offices”.


*Approved August 30, 1964.*

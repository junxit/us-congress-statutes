# Private Law 88–315

> AN ACT For the relief of Moni Parvanoft Floroff.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Moni Parvanoff Floroff shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of April 22, 1958, upon payment of the required visa fee.


*Approved August 30, 1964.*

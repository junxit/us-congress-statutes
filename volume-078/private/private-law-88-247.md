# Private Law 88–247

> AN ACT For the relief of Elisabete Maria Fonseca.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203(a)(3) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Elisabete Maria Fonseca shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien daughter of Mr. Augusto Fonseca, a lawfully resident alien of the United States: Provided, That the legal husband of the beneficiary’s mother shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved June 29, 1964.*

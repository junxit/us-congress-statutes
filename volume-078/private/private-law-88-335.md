# Private Law 88–335

> AN ACT For the relief of Czeslaw (Chester) Kaluzny.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203(a)(2) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Czeslaw (Chester) Kaluzny shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien son of Mr. and Mrs. Joseph D. Malinowski, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural father of the said Czeslaw (Chester) Kaluzny shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved September 24, 1964.*

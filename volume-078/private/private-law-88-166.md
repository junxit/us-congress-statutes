# Private Law 88–166

> AN ACT For the relief of Agaram K. Sreekanth.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 2 of the Act entitled “An Act to facilitate the entry of alien skilled specialists and certain relatives of United States citizens, and for other purposes”, approved October 24, 1962 (76 Stat. 1247), Agaram K. Sreekanth shall be held and considered to be an alien eligible for a quota immigrant status under the provisions of section 203(a)(1) of the Immigration and Nationality Act on the basis of a petition filed with the Attorney Gene nil prior to April 1, 1962.


*Approved January 20, 1964.*

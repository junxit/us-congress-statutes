# Private Law 88–353

> AN ACT For the relief of Leobardo L. Gonzalez.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Leobardo L. Gonzalez may be naturalized upon compliance with all the requirements of title III of the Immigration and Nationality Act, except that no period of residence or physical presence within the United States or any State shall be required in addition to his residence and physical presence within the United States since September 2, 1961.


*Approved October 13, 1964.*

# Private Law 88–168

> AN ACT For the relief of Hai Yung Jung and Johnny Jung.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of the second sentence in section 205(c) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the petitions filed in behalf of Hai Yung Jung and Johnny Jung by Mr. and Mrs. Warren Hugh Cateron pursuant to the provisions of section 205(b) of that Act may be approved, subject to all the conditions in that section relating to eligible orphans.


*Approved January 20, 1964.*

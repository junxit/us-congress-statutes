# Private Law 88–220

> AN ACT For the relief of Leon Llanos.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of section 301(a)(7) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Leon Llanos, a citizen of the United States at birth, shall be considered to have resided in the United States for five years after attaining the age of fourteen years.


*Approved May 14, 1964.*

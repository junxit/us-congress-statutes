# Private Law 88–343

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Lois Graybill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the one-year time limitation upon the filing of applications for waiver of premiums provided in section 712(c), title 38, United States Code is hereby waived with respect to any application for waiver of premiums on national service life insurance policy numbered V 1421 04 08 (issued on the life of Albert C. Graybill, Veterans’ Administration claim numbered XC–9 671 272) filed by Mrs. Lois Graybill of Williamsport, Pennsylvania, within the one-year period which begins on the date of enactment of this Act. Any payments made pursuant to such application shall be paid from the national service life insurance appropriation.


*Approved October 2, 1964.*

# Private Law 88–194

> AN ACT For the relief of Constantine Theothoropoulos.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Constantine Theothoropoulos may be classified as an eligible orphan within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of the Act, upon approval of a petition filed in his behalf by Mr. and Mrs. Emile Pappas, citizens of the United States, pursuant, to section 205(b) of the Act, subject to all the conditions in that section relating to eligible orphans.


*Approved March 13, 1964.*

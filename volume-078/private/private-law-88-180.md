# Private Law 88–180

> AN ACT For the relief of Areti Siozos Paidas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Areti Siozos Paidas may be classified as an eligible orphan within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of the Act, upon approval of a petition filed in her behalf by Mr. and Mrs. James Paidas, citizens of the United States pursuant to section 205(b) of the Act, subject to all the conditions m that section relating to eligible orphans.


*Approved March 13, 1964.*

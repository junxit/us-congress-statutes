# Private Law 88–300

> AN ACT For the relief of Rolando de la Torre Arceo and John Anthony Arceo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Rolando de la Torre Arceo and John Anthony Arceo may be classified as eligible orphans within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of the Act, upon approval of petitions filed in their behalf by Mr. and Mrs. Ernest Villanes Arceo, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 205(b) of the Act, subject to all the conditions in that section relating to eligible orphans.


*Approved August 30, 1964.*

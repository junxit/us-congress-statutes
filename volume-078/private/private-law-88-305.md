# Private Law 88–305

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Guiseppa D’Aquanno, Maria D’Aquanno, and Benedicto D’Aquanno.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, Mrs. Guiseppa D’Aquanno, Maria D’Aquanno, and Benedicto D’Aquanno shall be deemed to be within the purview of section 1 of the Act of October 24, 1962 (76 Stat. 1247).


*Approved August 30, 1964.*

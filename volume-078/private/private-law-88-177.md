# Private Law 88–177

> AN ACT For the relief of Willy Sapuschnin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Act of July 14, 1960 (74 Stat. 504), Willy Sapuschnin shall be held and considered to have been paroled into the United States on the date of the enactment of this Act, as provided for in the said Act of July 14, 1960.


*Approved March 13, 1964.*

# Private Law 88–262

> AN ACT For the relief of Major Keith K. Lund.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Major Keith K. Lund (AO495754) is hereby relieved of liability to the United States for the outstanding balance of amounts owed to the Government by reason of erroneous allotments, and failure to deduct from his pay the amount of proper allotments, paid for his account, during the period from October 1, 1942, through January 31, 1946, inclusive. Such allotments were paid without fault or negligence on his part, while he was a prisoner of war. In the audit and settlement of the accounts of any certifying or disbursing officer of the United States, credit shall be given for any amount for which liability is relieved by this Act.


*Approved August 1, 1964.*

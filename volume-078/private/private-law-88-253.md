# Private Law 88–253

> AN ACT For the relief of Jackie Bergancia Smith.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, a petition filed in behalf of Jackie Bergancia Smith by Roy Edward Smith may be approved pursuant to the provisions of section 205(b) of the Act, subject to all the conditions in that section relating to eligible orphans.


*Approved July 11, 1964.*

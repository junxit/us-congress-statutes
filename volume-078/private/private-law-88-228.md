# Private Law 88–228

> AN ACT For the relief of Wolfgang Stresemann.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, the Act of September 13, 1961 (Private Law 87–190) is hereby amended by substituting the date “March 1, 1964” for “March 2, 1961”.


*Approved May 14, 1964.*

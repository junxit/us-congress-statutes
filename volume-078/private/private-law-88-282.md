# Private Law 88–282

> AN ACT To permit the vessel SC–1473 to engage in the fisheries.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the vessel SC–1473 may be documented as a vessel of the United States and may engage in the foreign and coastwise trade and in the fisheries as long as such vessel is owned by a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 13, 1964.*

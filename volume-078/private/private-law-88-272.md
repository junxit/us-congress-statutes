# Private Law 88–272

> AN ACT For the relief of Mr. and Mrs. Blanton Darbro.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the limitations of time prescribed in clause (1) subsection (b) of section 715 of title 32 of the United States Code, or any other provision or doctrine of law, any claim by Blanton Darbro and/or Lue Darbro, his wife, of Vevay, Indiana, for damages as a result of the crash of a Kentucky Air National Guard airplane on their property on or about October 30, 1957, shall be settled under the remaining provisions of the said section 715 if such claim is presented in writing within one year after the date of enactment of this Act.


*Approved August 4, 1964.*

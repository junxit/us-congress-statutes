# Private Law 88–356

> AN ACT For the relief of Kim Book Hee and Kim Lou.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the Administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, section 205(c), relating to the number of petitions which may be approved in behalf of eligible orphans, shall be inapplicable in the case of a petition filed in behalf of Kim Sook Hee by Mr. and Mrs. Anthony Caucutt, citizens of the United States.


*Approved October 13, 1964.*

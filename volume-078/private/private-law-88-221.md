# Private Law 88–221

> AN ACT For the relief of Ioanna Ganas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, a petition filed inbehalf of Ioanna Ganas by Tassia Ganas, a citizen of the United States, may be approved pursuant to the provisions of section 205(b) of the Act, subject to all the conditions in that section relating to eligible orphans.


*Approved May 14, 1964.*

# Private Law 88–316

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Miguel de Socarraz.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Miguel de Socarraz shall be held and considered to have been admitted to the United States for permanent residence on August 27, 1960.


*Approved August 30, 1964.*

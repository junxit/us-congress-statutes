# Private Law 88–227

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Salim Akyol.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Salim Akyol shall be held and considered to have been admitted to the United States for permanent residence on June 26, 1954, and to have complied with the requirements of section 316 of the Act as they relate to residence and physical presence.


*Approved May 14, 1964.*

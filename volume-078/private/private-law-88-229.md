# Private Law 88–229

> AN ACT For the relief of Henry Bang Williams.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203(a)(2) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Henry Bang Williams shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien son of Mr. and Mrs. Arthur Henry Williams, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural mother of the said Henry Bang Williams shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved June 6, 1964.*

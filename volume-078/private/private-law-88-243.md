# Private Law 88–243

> AN ACT For the relief of Chang In Wu.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 205(c) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, a petition may be filed in behalf of Chang In Wu by Mr. and Mrs. Robert Ainley, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 205(b) of the said Act.


*Approved June 24, 1964.*

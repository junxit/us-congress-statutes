# Private Law 88–355

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Jorge A. Picaza.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Doctor Jorge A. Picaza may be naturalized upon compliance with all of the requirements of title III of the Immigration and Nationality Act, except that no period of residence or physical presence within the United States or any State shall be required in addition to his residence and physical presence within the United States since June 19, 1944.


*Approved October 13, 1964.*

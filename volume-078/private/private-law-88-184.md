# Private Law 88–184

> AN ACT For the relief of Agnese Brienza.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, Agnese Brienza shall lie deemed to be within the purview of section 25(a) of the Act of September 26, 1961 (75 Stat. 657).


*Approved March 13, 1964.*

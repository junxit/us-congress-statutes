# Private Law 88–357

> AN ACT For the relief of Basilio King, his wife, and their children.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purpose of section 101(a)(27)(B) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Basilio King, his wife Monica Elisa Co Chia King, and their children Basilio King, Junior, Johnny King, Elizabeth King, Sylvia King, and Cynthia King shall be deemed to be returning resident aliens.


*Approved October 13, 1964.*

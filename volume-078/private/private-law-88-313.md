# Private Law 88–313

> AN ACT For the relief of Danny Hiromi Oyama.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Danny Hiromi Oyama, may be classified as an eligible orphan within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of the Act, upon approval of a petition filed in his behalf by Corporal Earnest J. Griffin, a citizen of the United States, pursuant to section 205(b) of the Act, subject to all the conditions in that section relating to eligible orphans.


*Approved August 30, 1964.*

# Private Law 88–301

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Maisie Magdalene Lim Ketchens.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Maisie Magdalene Lim Reichens (widow of Charles Ketchens, a citizen of the United States) shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fee.


*Approved August 30, 1964.*

# Private Law 88–286

> AN ACT For the relief of Helen Marghitsa Georgalas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, Helen Marghitsa Georgalas, who lost United States citizenship under the provisions of paragraph (5), subsection (a) of section 349 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, may be naturalized by taking, prior to one year after the date of the enactment of this Act, before any court referred to in subsection (a) of section 310 of the Immigration and Nationality Act or before any diplomatic or consular officer of the United States abroad, an oath as prescribed by section 337 of such Act. From and after naturalization under this Act, the said Helen Marghitsa Georgalas shall have the same citizenship status as that which existed immediately prior to its loss: Provided, That the beneficiary returns to the United States for permanent residence within one year following the effective date of this Act.


*Approved August 14, 1964.*

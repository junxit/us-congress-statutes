# Private Law 88–181

> AN ACT For the relief of Ewald Johan Consen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Ewald Johan Consen shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permament residence as of March 3, 1957.


*Approved March 13, 1964.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 372

> SIGNING OF ENROLLED BILLS, ETC.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That notwithstanding the sine die adjournment of the two Houses, the Speaker of the House of Representatives and the President pro tempore of the Senate be, and they are hereby, authorized to sign enrolled bills and joint resolutions duly passed by the two Houses and found truly enrolled.


*Passed October 2, 1964.*

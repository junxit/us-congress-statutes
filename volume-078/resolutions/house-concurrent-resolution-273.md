# House Concurrent Resolution 273

> STATUE OF FATHER EUSEBIO F. KINO—TEMPORARY PLACEMENT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the State of Arizona is hereby authorized to place temporarily in the rotunda of the Capitol a statue of the late Father Eusebio Francisco Kino, of Arizona, and to hold ceremonies in the rotunda on said occasion; and the Architect of the Capitol is hereby authorized to make the necessary arrangements therefor.


*Passed August 20, 1964.*

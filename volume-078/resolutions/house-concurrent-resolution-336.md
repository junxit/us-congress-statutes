# House Concurrent Resolution 336

> SCHOOL PRAYER HEARINGS

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on the Judiciary, House of Representatives, four thousand additional copies each, to be printed concurrently with the publications entitled “Proposed Amendments to the Constitution Relating to Prayers and Bible Reading in the Public Schools, Hearings Before the Committee on the Judiciary, House of Representatives, parts 1,2, and 3”, Eighty-eighth Congress, second session.


*Passed August 20, 1964.*

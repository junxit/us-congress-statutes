# Senate Concurrent Resolution 75

> GENERAL DOUGLAS MACARTHUR—FLORAL WREATH

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Sergeant at Arms of the Senate and the Sergeant at Arms of the House of Representatives are each hereby authorized and directed to purchase a floral wreath to be placed by the catafalque bearing the remains of late General of the Army of the United States, Douglas MacArthur, which are to lie in state in the rotunda of the Capitol of the United States from April 8 to April 9, 1964, the expenses of which shall be paid from the contingent funds of the Senate and the House of Representatives, respectively.


*Agreed to April 6, 1964.*

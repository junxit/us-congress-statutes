# Senate Concurrent Resolution 73

> ROBERT S. KERR WATER RESEARCH CENTER—DESIGNATION

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Southwest regional water laboratory of the Department of Health, Education, and Welfare at Ada, Oklahoma, should be known and designated as the “Robert S. Kerr Water Research Center” in honor of the late Senator Robert S. Kerr, of Oklahoma. Any law, regulation, document, or record of the United States in which such laboratory is referred to should be held to refer to such laboratory under and by the name of the “Robert S. Kerr Water Research Center.


*Passed June 19, 1964.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 274

> STATUE OF FATHER EUSEBIO F. KINO—ACCEPTANCE

*Resolved by the Rouse of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the statue of Father Eusebio Francisco Kino, presented by the State of Arizona, to be placed in the Statuary Hall collection, is accepted in the name of the United States, and that the thanks of the Congress be tendered said State for the contribution of the statue of one of its most eminent pioneers, illustrious for his early missionary work, exploration, and cartography; and be it further

Resolved, That a copy of these resolutions, suitably engrossed and duly authenticated, be transmitted to the Governor of Arizona.


*Passed August 20, 1964.*

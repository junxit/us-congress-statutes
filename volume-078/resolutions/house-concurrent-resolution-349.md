# House Concurrent Resolution 349

> “STATE TAXATION OF INTERSTATE COMMERCE”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed two thousand additional copies of House Report 1480, Eighty-eighth Congress, second session, entitled “State Taxation of Interstate Commerce,” with illustrations and maps, for the use of the House Committee on the Judiciary.


*Passed August 20, 1964.*

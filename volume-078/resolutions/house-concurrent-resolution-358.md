# House Concurrent Resolution 358

> IMMIGRATION HEARINGS

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That, there be printed for the use of the Committee on the Judiciary, House of Representatives, five thousand additional copies of part 1, “Immigration Hearings Before Subcommittee No. 1 of the Committee on the Judiciary, House of Representatives”; and five thousand additional copies, each, of parts 2 and 3 to be printed concurrently with the publications entitled “Immigration Hearings Before Sub-committee No. 1 of the Committee on the Judiciary, House of Representatives”, Eighty-eighth Congress, second session.


*Passed October 2, 1964.*

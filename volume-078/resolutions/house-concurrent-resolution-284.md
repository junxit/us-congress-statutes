# House Concurrent Resolution 284

> ADJOURNMENT–HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Thursday, March 26, 1964, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Monday, April 6, 1964.


*Passed March 25, 1964.*

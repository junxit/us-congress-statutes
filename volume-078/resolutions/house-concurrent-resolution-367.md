# House Concurrent Resolution 367

> CORRECTION IN ENROLLMENT OF H. R. 6593

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (H.R. 6593) entitled “An Act for the relief of Earnest O. Scott”, the Clerk of the House of Representatives is authorized and directed to make the following correction:

On page 2, line 7 of the engrossed House bill, strike out “some” and insert “any sums”.


*Passed September 29, 1964.*

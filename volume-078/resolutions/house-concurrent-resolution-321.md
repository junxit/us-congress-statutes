# House Concurrent Resolution 321

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Thursday, July 2, 1964, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock noon on Monday, July 20, 1964.

Resolved further, That when the Senate adjourns on Friday, July 10, 1964, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, July 20, 1964.


*Passed July 2, 1964.*

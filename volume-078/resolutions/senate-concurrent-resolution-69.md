# Senate Concurrent Resolution 69

> TRIBUTES TO PRESIDENT JOHN F. KENNEDY

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be primed with illustrations as a Senate document all remarks by Members of the Senate and the House of Representatives in the Hafts of Congress which constitute tributes to the life, character, and public service of the late President, John F. Kennedy.

## § 2.

There shall be printed and bound as directed by the Joint. Committee on Printing, thirty-two thousand two hundred and fifty additional copies of such document, of which ten thousand three hundred copies shall be for the use of the Senate and twenty-one thousand nine hundred and fifty copies shall be for the use of the House of Representatives.


*Agreed to January 29, 1964.*

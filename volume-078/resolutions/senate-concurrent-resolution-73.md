# Senate Concurrent Resolution 73

> ATOMIC ENERGY COMMISSION HEARINGS

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Joint Committee on Atomic Energy two thousand additional copies each of part 2 and part 3 of its hearings on the Atomic Energy Commission authorizing legislation, fiscal year 1965.


*Agreed to June 2, 1964.*

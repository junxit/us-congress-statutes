# House Concurrent Resolution 247

> “OUR FLAG”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed three hundred thousand additional copies of House Document Numbered 104, 1st session, 88th Congress, entitled “Our Flag”, of which two hundred thousand shall be for the use of the House of Representatives, and one hundred thousand shall be for the use of the Senate.


*Passed April 16, 1964.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 346

> “THE FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM AFTER FIFTY YEARS”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of tire Committee on Banking and Currency, House of Representatives, three thousand five hundred additional copies of volume III of the hearings before the Subcommittee on Domestic Finance of the Committee on Banking and Currency, House of Representatives, entitled “The Federal Reserve System After Fifty Years”, dated April 9,13,14,16, 22, and 29, 1964.


*Passed August 20, 1964.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 88

> “A REPORT OF A STUDY OF UNITED STATES FOREIGN AID IN TEN MIDDLE EASTERN AND AFRICAN COUNTRIES”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on Government Operations two thousand additional copies of its committee print of the Eighty-eighth Congress, first session, entitled “A Report of a Study of United States Foreign Aid in Ten Middle Eastern and African Countries,” a study made by Senator Ernest Gruening for the Subcommittee on Reorganization and International Organizations.


*Agreed to August 4, 1964.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 189

> TIN—DISPOSAL

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Congress expressly approves, pursuant to section 3(e) of the Strategic and Critical Materials Stock Piling Act (53 Stat. 811, as amended, 50 U.S.C. 98b(e)), the disposal from the national stockpile of approximately ninety-eight thousand long tons of pig tin.


*Passed July 2, 1964.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 83

> HEARINGS ON INTERAGENCY COORDINATION IN ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARDS

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Government Operations three thousand additional copies of its hearings on interagency coordination in environmental hazards (pesticides), part I (including exhibits).


*Agree to August 4, 1964.*

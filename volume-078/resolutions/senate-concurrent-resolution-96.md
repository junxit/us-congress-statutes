# Senate Concurrent Resolution 96

> “CATALOG OF FEDERAL AIDS TO STATE AND LOCAL GOVERNMENTS”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on Government Operations eighteen thousand additional copies of its committee print of the Eighty-eighth Congress, second session, entitled “Catalog of Federal Aids to State and Local Governments”, a study prepared by the Legislative Reference Service of the Library of Congress for the Subcommittee on Intergovernmental Relations.


*Agreed to September 30, 1964.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 359

> ADJOURNMENT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses shall adjourn on Friday, August 21, 1964, and that when they adjourn on said day they stand adjourned until 12 o’clock noon on Monday, August 31, 1964.


*Passed August 21, 1964.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 78

> REPORT OF INTERNATIONAL CONGRESS ON EDUCATION OF THE DEAF, 1963

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the report of the proceedings of the International Congress on Education of the Deaf, and of the forty-first meeting of the Convention of American Instructors of the Deaf, held in Washington, District of Columbia, in June 1963, be printed with illustrations as a Senate document; and that five thousand additional copies be printed for the use of the Joint Committee on Printing.


*Agreed to September 30, 1964.*

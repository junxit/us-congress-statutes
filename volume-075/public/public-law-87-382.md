# Public Law 87–382

> AN ACT To amend section 6(a) of the Virgin Islands Corporation Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (a) of section 6 of the Virgin Islands Corporation Act (63 Stat. 350, 353), as amended (48 U.S.C. 1407e(a)), is further amended by striking out the figure “$11,000,000” in both places where it appears therein and inserting in lieu thereof the figure “$15,000,000”.


*Approved October 4, 1961.*

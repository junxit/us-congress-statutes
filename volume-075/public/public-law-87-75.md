# Public Law 87–75

> AN ACT To amend Public Law 85–26, as amended by Public Law 86–542, relating to dual rate contract agreements.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

United States of America in Congress assembled, That section 2 of the Act entitled “To amend the Shipping Act, 1916”, approved August 12, 1958 (72 Stat. 574), as amended by an Act approved June 29, 1960 (74 Stat. 253), is further amended by striking out “June 30, 1961” and inserting in lieu thereof “September 15, 1961”.


*Approved June 30, 1961.*

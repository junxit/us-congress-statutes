# Public Law 87–52

> AN ACT To authorize construction of community support facilities at Los Alamos County, New Mexico.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Atomic Energy Commission is authorized with funds presently available or otherwise made available to it to construct (under the applicable provisions of chapter 14 of the Atomic Energy Act of 1954, as amended) community support facilities at White Rock, Los Alamos County, New Mexico, at a total cost not to exceed $300,000, and for that purpose there is authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary.


*Approved June 16, 1961.*

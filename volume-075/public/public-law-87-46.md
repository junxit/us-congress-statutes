# Public Law 87–46

> AN ACT To amend section 714 of title 32, United States Code, to authorize certain payments of decreased members’ final accounts without the necessity of settlement by General Accounting Office.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the last sentence of section 714(c) of title 32, United States Code, is amended to read as follows:

> “Payment under clause (6) of subsection (a) shall be made—
> - **(1)** upon settlement by the General Accounting Office; or
> - **(2)** as otherwise authorized by the Comptroller General.”


*Approved June 16, 1961.*

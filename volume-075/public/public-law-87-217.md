# Public Law 87–217

> AN ACT To authorize and direct the Secretary of the Treasury to cause the vessel Acadia, owned by Robert J. Davis of Port Clyde, Maine, to be documented as a vessel of the United States with coastwise privileges.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 4132 of the Revised Statutes of the United States, as amended (46 U.S.C. 11), the Secretary of the Treasury shall cause the vessel Acadia, owned by Robert J. Davis of Port Clyde, Maine, to be documented as a vessel of the United States, upon compliance with the usual requirements, with the privilege of engaging in the coastwise trade so long as such vessel is owned by a citizen of the United States.


*Approved September 13, 1961.*

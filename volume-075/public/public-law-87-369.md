# Public Law 87–369

> AN ACT To repeal section 791 of title 18 of the United States Code so as to extend the application of chapter 37 of title 18, relating to espionage and censorship.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 791 of title 18, United States Code, is repealed.

## § 2.

The analysis of chapter 37 of such title is amended by deleting the following:

> - “791. Scope of chapter.”


*Approved October 4, 1961.*

# Public Law 87–4

> JOINT RESOLUTION Relating to the Time for filing a Report on Renegotiation by the Joint Committee on Internal Revenue Taxation.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Section 4(b)(2) of Public Law 86–89, July 13, 1959, extending the Renegotiation Act of 1951, is amended by striking out “March 31, 1961”, relating to the time for filing a report on Renegotiation by the Joint Committee on Internal Revenue Taxation, and inserting in lieu thereof “June 30, 1961”.


*Approved March 22, 1961.*

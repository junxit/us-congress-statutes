# Public Law 87–340

> AN ACT To disclaim interest in certain rights in certain lands in the State of Nevada.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States hereby disclaims any interest in lands which it may have, prior to the date of approval of this Act, acquired by virtue of chapter 103 Stat., Nevada 1887, or by any revisions and reenactment thereof.


*Approved October 3, 1961.*

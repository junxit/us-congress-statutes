# Public Law 87–164

> AN ACT To provide travel and transportation allowances for members of the National Guard and reserve components when travel is performed in an active duty or inactive duty training status in compliance with Federal directives.

*Be it enacted by the Senate; and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 501(b) of the Career Compensation Act of 1949, as amended, is amended by striking out the words “furnished with transportation to and from such duty, with subsistence en route” in the first sentence and inserting the words “authorized the travel and transportation allowances prescribed in section 303(a) of this Act for travel performed to and from such duty” in place thereof.


*Approved August 25, 1961.*

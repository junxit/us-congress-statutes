# Public Law 87–339

> AN ACT To amend the Federal Employees’ Compensation Act of 1960.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first proviso of section 104 of the Federal Employees’ Compensation Act Amendments of 1960 (74 Stat. 906) is amended by adding immediately preceding the colon, the following: “, except that this section shall apply to employees of the government of the District of Columbia other than members of the police and fire departments who are pensioned or pensionable under the provisions of the Policemen and Firemen’s Retirement and Disability Act”.

## § 2.

This Act shall take effect October 1, 1960.


*Approved October 3, 1961.*

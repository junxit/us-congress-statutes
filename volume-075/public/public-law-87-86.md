# Public Law 87–86

> AN ACT To correct a technical inaccuracy in the Act of May 19, 1961 (Public Law 87–36).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3(e) of the Act of May 19, 1961 (Public Law 87–36), is amended by striking out “title 18” and inserting in lieu thereof “title 28”


*Approved July 11, 1961.*

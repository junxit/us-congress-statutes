# Public Law 87–97

> AN ACT To amend section 4004 of title 38, United States Code, to require that the Board of Veterans’ Appeals render findings of fact and conclusions of law in the opinions setting forth its decisions on appeals.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4004 of title 38, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end thereof the following:

> - **(d)** The decisions of the Board shall be in writing and shall contain findings of fact and conclusions of law separately stated.”

## § 2.

The amendment made by this Act shall take effect as of January 1, 1962.


*Approved July 20, 1961.*

# Public Law 87–181

> AN ACT To amend the Act granting the consent of Congress to the negotiation of certain compacts by the States of Nebraska, Wyoming, and South Dakota in order to extend the time for such negotiation.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the last sentence of the Act entitled “An Act granting the consent of Congress to the negotiation by the States of Nebraska, Wyoming, and South Dakota of certain compacts with respect to the use of waters common to two or more of said States”, approved August 5, 1953 (67 Stat. 365), as amended, is amended further by striking out “eight years” and inserting in lieu thereof “ten years”.


*Approved August 30, 1961.*

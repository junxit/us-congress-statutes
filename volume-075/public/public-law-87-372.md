# Public Law 87–372

> AN ACT To amend section 109 of the Federal Property and Administrative Services Act of 1949, as amended, so as to remove the limitation on the maximum capital of the General Supply Fund.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the third sentence of subsection (a) of section 109 of the Federal Property and Administrative Services Act of 1949 (63 Stat. 382), as amended (5 U.S.C. 630g(a)), is hereby deleted.


*Approved October 4, 1961.*

# Public Law 87–34

> JOINT RESOLUTION Authorizing the President to proclaim the week in May 1961 in which falls the third Friday of that month as National Transportation Week.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States is requested and authorized to officially proclaim the week in May of 1961 in which falls the third Friday of that month as National Transportation Week, and to issue a proclamation inviting the people of the United States to observe such period with appropriate ceremonies and activities, as a tribute to the men and women who, night and day, move goods and people throughout our land.


*Approved May 16, 1961.*

# Public Law 87–281

> AN ACT To amend the Act entitled “An Act to regulate the height of buildings in the District of Columbia”, approved June 1, 1910, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph 3 of section 5 of the Act entitled “An Act to regulate the height of buildings in the District of Columbia”, approved June 1, 1910, as amended (36 Stat. 452; 43 Stat. 961; sec. 5–405, D.C. Code, 1951 edition), is amended by striking “over eight stories in height or”.


*Approved September 22, 1961.*

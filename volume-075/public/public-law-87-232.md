# Public Law 87–232

> AN ACT To make certain funds available to the Nez Perce Tribe of Idaho.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior make available the unexpended balances of funds in the Treasury of the United States under the following symbols and titles: to the Nez Perce Tribe of Idaho for purposes the tribe requests and approved by the Secretary of the Interior.

- **(1)** 14X7063 Nez Perces of Idaho fund;
- **(2)** 14X7563 interests and accruals on interest, Nez Perces of Idaho fund;


*Approved September 14, 1961.*

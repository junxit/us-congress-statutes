# Public Law 87–390

> AN ACT To further amend section 201 (i) of the Federal Civil Defense Act of 1950, as amended, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 201 (i) of the Federal Civil Defense Act of 1950, as amended, is further amended by inserting after the first proviso the following: “Provided further, That retroactive, financial contributions which were otherwise approvable, approved and made to the States prior to June 30, 1960, to carry out the purposes of this subsection are hereby ratified and affirmed;”


*Approved October 4, 1961.*

# Public Law 87–170

> AN ACT To amend the Watershed Protection and Flood Prevention Act to permit certain new organizations to sponsor works of improvement thereunder.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the last paragraph of section 2 of the Watershed Protection and Flood Prevention Act is amended by inserting immediately before the period at the end thereof the following: “; or any irrigation or reservoir company, water users’ association, or similar organization having such authority and not being operated for profit that may be approved by the Secretary”.


*Approved August 30, 1961.*

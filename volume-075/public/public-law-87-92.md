# Public Law 87–92

> AN ACT To continue the authority of the President under title II of the Agricultural Trade Development and Assistance Act of 1954, as amended, to utilize surplus agricultural commodities to assist needy peoples and to promote economic development in underdeveloped areas of the world.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 601(a)(2) of the Mutual Security Act of 1960 is hereby repealed.


*Approved July 20, 1961.*

# Public Law 87–360

> AN ACT To amend the Act entitled “An Act for the organization, improvement, and maintenance of the National Zoological Park”, approved April 30, 1890.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2 of the Act entitled “An Act for the organization, improvement, and maintenance of the National Zoological Park”, approved April 30, 1890 (26 Stat. 78), is amended by inserting immediately after “administer” the following: “and improve”.


*Approved October 4, 1961.*

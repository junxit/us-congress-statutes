# Public Law 87–267

> AN ACT To repeal sections 1176 and 1177 of the Revised Statutes of the United States relating to the District of Columbia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 1176 and 1177 of the Revised Statutes of the United States relating to the District of Columbia (18 Stat. 136; D.C. Code, secs. 22–3401, 3402, and 3403) are hereby repealed.


*Approved September 21, 1961.*

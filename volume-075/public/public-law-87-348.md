# Public Law 87–348

> JOINT RESOLUTION Fixing the beginning of the second regular session of the Eighty-seventh Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second regular session of the Eighty-seventh Congress shall begin at noon on Wednesday, January 10, 1962.


*Approved October 4, 1961.*

# Public Law 87–89

> AN ACT To extend the provisions of title XIII of the Federal Aviation Act of 1958, relating to war risk insurance.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1312 of title XIII of the Federal Aviation Act of 1958 (49 U.S.C. 1542), is hereby amended by striking out “June 13, 1961” and inserting “June 13, 1966” in lieu thereof.


*Approved July 20, 1961.*

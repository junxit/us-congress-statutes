# Senate Concurrent Resolution 54

> SIGNING OF ENROLLED BILLS, ETC.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That notwithstanding the sine die adjournment of the two Houses, the Speaker pro tempore of the House of Representatives and the President of the Senate or the President pro tempore of the Senate be, and they are hereby, authorized to sign enrolled bills and joint resolutions duly passed by the two Houses and found truly enrolled.


*Agreed to September 27, 1961.*

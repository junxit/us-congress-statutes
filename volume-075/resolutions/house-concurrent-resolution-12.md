# House Concurrent Resolution 12

> PRINTING OF HOUSE DOCUMENT NO. 412

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed for the use of the House of Representatives five hundred thousand additional copies of House Document Numbered 412, Eighty-fifth Congress, second session, with recommendations.


*Passed September 15, 1961.*

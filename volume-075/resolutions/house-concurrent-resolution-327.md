# House Concurrent Resolution 327

> “THE COMMISSION ON INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed four thousand six hundred additional copies of House Document Numbered 198 of the Eighty-fourth Congress, first session, entitled “The Commission on Intergovernmental Relations”, of which four thousand shall be for the use of the House Committee on Government Operations, three hundred copies for the House document room, and three hundred copies for the Senate document room.


*Passed July 17, 1961.*

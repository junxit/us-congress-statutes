# Senate Concurrent Resolution 39

> COMPILATION OF HEARINGS, REPORTS, AND COMMITTEE PRINTS OF SUBCOMMITTEE ON NATIONAL POLICY MACHINERY

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on Government Operations three thousand copies each of volumes 1, 2, and 3 of a compilation of the hearings, reports, and committee prints of its Subcommittee on National Policy Machinery issued during the Eighty-sixth and Eighty-seventh Congresses.


*Agreed to September 7, 1961.*

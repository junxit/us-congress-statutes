# House Concurrent Resolution 309

> “THE NORTHERN CALIFORNIA DISTRICT OF THE COMMUNIST PARTY-STRUCTURE-OBJECTIVES-LEADERSHIP”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Un-American Activities ten thousand additional copies each of parts 1, 2, 3, and 4 of the hearings held by that committee in San Francisco entitled “The Northern California District of the Communist Party-Structure-Objectives-Leadership”, Eighty-sixth Congress, second session.


*Passed July 17, 1961.*

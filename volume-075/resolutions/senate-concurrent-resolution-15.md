# Senate Concurrent Resolution 15

> “A REPORT ON UNITED STATES FOREIGN OPERATIONS”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed with illustrations, as a Senate document, a report entitled “A Report on United States Foreign Operations”, submitted by Senator Allen J. Ellender to the Senate Committee on Appropriations on February 1, 1961; and that four thousand additional copies be printed for the use of that committee.


*Agreed to March 15, 1961.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 211

> ADJOURNMENT-HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Thursday, March 30, 1961, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian on Monday, April 10, 1961.


*Passed March 30, 1961.*

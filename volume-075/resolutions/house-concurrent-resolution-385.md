# House Concurrent Resolution 385

> “HISTORY OF THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the manuscript entitled “History of the House of Representatives”, by George B. Galloway, be printed under the supervision of the Committee on House Administration as a House document, and that there be printed two thousand additional copies for the use of the House of Representatives.


*Passed September 15, 1961.*

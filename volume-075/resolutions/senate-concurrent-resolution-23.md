# Senate Concurrent Resolution 23

> HEARING ON MIGRATORY LABOR

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring,*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Labor and Public Welfare two thousand additional copies of part I of the hearing on migratory labor held before its Subcommittee on Migratory Labor during the Eighty-sixth Congress, first session.


*Agreed to June 29, 1961.*

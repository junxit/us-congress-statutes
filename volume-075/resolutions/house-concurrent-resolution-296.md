# House Concurrent Resolution 296

> COMMANDER ALAN B. SHEPARD, JR., USN

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Congress hereby commends Commander Alan B. Shepard, Junior, United States Navy, of Derry, New Hampshire, for his out standing achievement and the courage and skill displayed by him in his flight into space on May 5, 1961, in the Mercury capsule known as Freedom 7.


*Passed May 8, 1961.*

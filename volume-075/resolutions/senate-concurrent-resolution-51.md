# Senate Concurrent Resolution 51

> REENROLLMENT OF S. 902

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the action of the Speaker pro tempore in signing the enrolled bill (S. 902) to amend the Small Business Investment Act of 1958, and for other purposes, is hereby rescinded; the Secretary of the Senate is hereby directed to reenroll said bill with the following corrections:

In the text of section 11(d) of the bill (1) strike out “(d), (e),” and insert in lieu thereof “(e)” and (2) strike out “(d)” the second time it appears and insert in lieu thereof “(e)”.


*Agreed to September 19, 1961.*

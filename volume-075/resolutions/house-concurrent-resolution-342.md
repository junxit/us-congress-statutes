# House Concurrent Resolution 342

> TRIBUTE TO THE HONORABLE SAM RAYBURN

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the tributes extended to the Speaker, the Honorable Sam Rayburn, in the House of Representatives on June 12, 1961, and any other relevant material, be printed, with illustrations, as a House document; and that one hundred and twenty-seven thousand additional copies shall be printed, of which one hundred and twenty-two thousand five hundred copies shall be for the use of the House of Representatives, two thousand five hundred copies, for the use of the Senate, and two thousand copies, bound in style to be determined by the Joint Committee on Printing, for the use of the Speaker.


*Passed July 20, 1961.*

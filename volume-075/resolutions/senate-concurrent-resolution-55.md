# Senate Concurrent Resolution 55

> ADJOURNMENT SINE DIE

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the two Houses shall adjourn on Wednesday, the 27th day of September, 1961, and that when they adjourn on said day they stand adjourned sine die.


*Agreed to September 27, 1961.*

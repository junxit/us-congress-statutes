# Private Law 137

> AN ACT For the relief of Mr. Louis Fischer, Feger Seafoods, and Mr. and Mrs. Thomas R. Stuart.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the one-year limitation of time set forth in the second sentence of section 401 (b) of the Act entitled “An Act to authorize certain construction at military and naval installations, and for other purposes”, approved July 14, 1952, is waived in favor of Mr. Louis Fischer, Cocoa, Florida; Feger Seafoods, New Smyrna Beach, Florida; and Mr. and Mrs. Thomas E. Stuart, Cocoa Beach, Florida, with respect to their claims for reimbursement (under section 40i(b) of such Act) arising out of the acquisition by the United States of tract numbered 946, Cape Canaveral Auxiliary Air Force Base, Florida.


*Approved July 31, 1961.*

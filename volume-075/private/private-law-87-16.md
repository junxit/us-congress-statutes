# Private Law 87–16

> AN ACT For the relief of Captain Ernest Mountain.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Captain Ernest Mountain shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of June 30, 1929, upon payment of the required visa fee.


*Approved May 25, 1961.*

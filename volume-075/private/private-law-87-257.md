# Private Law 87–257

> AN ACT For the relief of Compton Jones and Hulbert Jones.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a)(27)(A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Compton Jones and Hulbert Jones shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien minor children of Arthur F. Jones, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved September 26, 1961.*

# Private Law 87–15

> AN ACT For the relief of Andreas Rakintozis (also known as Andreas Rakintzis or Rakajes).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 4 of the Act entitled “An Act to provide for the entry of certain relatives of United States citizens and lawfully resident aliens”, approved September 22, 1959 (73 Stat. 644), Andreas Rakintozis (also known as Andreas Rakintzis or Rakajes) shall be held and considered to be eligible for a quota immigrant status under the provisions of section 203fa) (4) of the Immigration and Nationality Act on the basis of a petition approved by the Attorney General prior to January 1, 1959.


*Approved May 25, 1961.*

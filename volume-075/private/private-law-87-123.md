# Private Law 123

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Tome Takamoto.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Tome Takamoto shall be held to be classifiable as a nonquota returning resident under the provisions of section 101(a) (27) (B) of that Act.


*Approved July 26, 1961.*

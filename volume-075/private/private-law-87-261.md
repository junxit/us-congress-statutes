# Private Law 87–261

> AN ACT For the relief of Fernando Manni.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203(a)(2) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Fernando Manni shall be held and considered to be the parent of Renzo Grossi, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved September 26, 1961.*

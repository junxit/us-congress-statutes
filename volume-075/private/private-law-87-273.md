# Private Law 87–273

> AN ACT For the relief of Fares Salem Salman Hamarneh.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a)(27)(A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child. Fares Salem Salman Hamarneh, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Sami Khalaf Hamarneh, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents of the said Fares Salem Salman Hamarneh shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved October 3, 1961.*

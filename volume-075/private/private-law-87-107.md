# Private Law 107

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Tui Hing Tow Woo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Mrs. Tui Hing Tow Woo shall be held and considered to have complied with the provisions of section 312(1) of the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved July 11, 1961.*

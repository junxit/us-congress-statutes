# Private Law 166

> AN ACT For the relief of James D. Jalili.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, James D. Jalili shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of December 10, 1955, upon payment of the required visa fee.


*Approved August 30, 1961.*

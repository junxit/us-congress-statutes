# Private Law 87–72

> AN ACT For the relief of Fausto Lavari.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the periods of time Fausto Lavari has resided in the United States since March 13, 1921, shall be held and considered to meet the residence and physical presence requirements of section 316 of the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved June 29, 1961.*

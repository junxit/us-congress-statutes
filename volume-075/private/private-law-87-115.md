# Private Law 115

> AN ACT For the relief of Krste Angeloff.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

Krste Angeloff.

66 Stat. 166, 180.

8 USC 1101, 1155.

That, for the purposes of section 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Krste Angeloff shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien minor child of Mrs. Miles Angeloff, citizen of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents of the said Krste Angeloff shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved July 20, 1961.*

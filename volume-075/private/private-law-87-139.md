# Private Law 139

> AN ACT For the relief of Hyacinth Louise Miller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child. Hyacinth Louise Miller shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Robert Miller.


*Approved August 3, 1961.*

# Private Law 190

> AN ACT For the relief of Wolfgang Stresemann.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of section 352(a)(1) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Wolfgang Stresemann shall be held to have established residence in the country of his birth on March 2, 1961.


*Approved September 13, 1961.*

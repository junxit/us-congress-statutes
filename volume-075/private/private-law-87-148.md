# Private Law 148

> AN ACT For the relief of Jean Goedicke.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Jean Goedicke, of Casper, Wyoming, is relieved of liability to pay to the United States the sum of $628.25, representing the amount of rentals owed by the said Jean Goedicke as lessee of certain Federal lands under an oil and gas lease (Buffalo 038682) issued to her on August 1, 1945, as a result of her failure to file a timely surrender of such lease due to a misunderstanding as to her responsibilities under such lease.


*Approved August 17, 1961.*

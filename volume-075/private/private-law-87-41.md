# Private Law 87–41

> AN ACT For the relief of Anna B. Prokop.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Anna B. Prokop shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Miron Prokop, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents of the beneficiary shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved June 21, 1961.*

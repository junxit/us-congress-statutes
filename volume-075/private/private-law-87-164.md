# Private Law 164

> AN ACT For the relief of W. B. J. Martin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (b) of section 201 of the Act of January 27, 1948, as amended (62 Stat. 6; 66 Stat. 276; 70 Stat. 241), shall not be applicable in the case of W. B. J. Martin.


*Approved August 30, 1961.*

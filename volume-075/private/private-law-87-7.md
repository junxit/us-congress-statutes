# Private Law 87–7

> AN ACT For the relief of Alessandro Gellhorn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Alessandro Gellhorn shall be deemed to be within the purview of section 323 of the said Act.


*Approved May 25, 1961.*

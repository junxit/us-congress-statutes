# Private Law 220

> AN ACT To authorize the Secretary of the Interior to enter into an exchange of certain land in Madera County, California, with Mary Saunders Moses.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in order to bring about desirable land-use and ownership adjustments relating to certain private and federally owned land adjacent to the Madera equalizing reservoir, a feature of the Central Valley project, California, and in order to facilitate the administration of such reservoir, the Secretary of the Interior is authorized, in his discretion, to exchange lands of approximately equal value as provided in this Act.

# Private Law 87–81

> AN ACT For the relief of Martha Uchacz Barras.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child Martha Uchacz Barras shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Anne Barras, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved June 30, 1961.*

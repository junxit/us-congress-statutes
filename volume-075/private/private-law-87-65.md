# Private Law 87–32

> AN ACT For the relief of Jeno Becsey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Jeno Becsey shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of January 3, 1957: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the said Act.


*Approved June 29, 1961.*

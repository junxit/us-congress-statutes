# Private Law 87–13

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Elizabeth Clifford.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Elizabeth Clifford shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence on September 30, 1956: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved May 25, 1961.*

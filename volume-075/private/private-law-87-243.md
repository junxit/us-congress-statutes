# Private Law 87–243

> AN ACT For the relief of Adelina Benedict (nee Rosasco).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 101(a)(27)(B) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Adelina Benedict (nee Rosasco) shall be deemed to be a returning resident alien.


*Approved September 26, 1961.*

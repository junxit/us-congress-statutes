# Private Law 87–83

> AN ACT For the relief of Marian Walczyk and Marya Marek.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Marian Walczyk, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of John Walczyk, a citizen of the United States.

## § 2.

For the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Marya Marek, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. John Marek, citizens of the United States.

## § 3.

The natural parents of the beneficiaries of this Act shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved June 30, 1961.*

# Private Law 87–14

> AN ACT For the relief of Nicolaos A. Papadimitriou.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Nicolaos A. Papadimitriou shall be held and considered to be the natural-born minor alien child of Mr. and Mrs. James N. Demmette, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents of the said Nicolaos A. Papadimitriou shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved May 25, 1961.*

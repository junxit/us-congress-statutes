# Private Law 87–276

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor John Lopinto Arzaga.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor John Lopinto Arzaga shall be deemed to be within the purview of section 319(a) of that Act.


*Approved October 3, 1961.*

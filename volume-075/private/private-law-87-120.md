# Private Law 120

> AN ACT For the relief of Kejen Pi Corsa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Kejen Pi Corsa shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Captain and Mrs. Richard T. Corsa: Provided, That the natural parents of the beneficiary shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved July 26, 1961.*

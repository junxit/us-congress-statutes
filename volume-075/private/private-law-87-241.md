# Private Law 87–241

> AN ACT For the relief of Washington George Brodber Bryan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a)(27)(A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Washington George Brodber Bryan, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Clifford Randal Bryan, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved September 26, 1961.*

# Private Law 103

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Anneliese Franziska Guay.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 212(a)(3) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Anneliese Franziska Guay may be issued a visa and admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act: Provided, That, unless the beneficiary is entitled to care under the Dependents’ Medical Care Act (70 Stat. 250), a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the Immigration and Nationality Act: Provided further, That this exemption shall apply only to a ground for exclusion of which the Department of State or the Department of Justice had knowledge prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved July 11, 1961.*

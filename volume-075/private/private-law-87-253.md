# Private Law 87–253

> AN ACT For the relief of Richard Fordham.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 301(a)(7) and (b) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Richard Fordham shall be held to have come to the United States prior to July 1, 1960, and to have retained his United States citizenship acquired at birth.


*Approved September 26, 1961.*

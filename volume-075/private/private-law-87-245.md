# Private Law 87–245

> AN ACT For the relief of Alberto Luciano (Rocchi) Rosasco.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a)(27)(A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Alberto Luciano (Kocchi) Rosasco shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Albert Leopold Rosasco, citizens of the United States.


*Approved September 26, 1961.*

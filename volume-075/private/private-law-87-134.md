# Private Law 134

> AN ACT For the relief of Lee Shee Won.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Lee Shee Won shall be held and considered to be the natural-born minor alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Robert G. H. Lee, citizens of the United States.


*Approved July 31, 1961.*

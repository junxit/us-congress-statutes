# Private Law 87–92

> AN ACT For the relief of Hans Hangartner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Hans Hangartner shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of August 10, 1953.

## § 2.

For the purposes of title III of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the provisions of 315(a) thereof shall be held not to be applicable to the alien named in section 1 of this Act.


*Approved June 30, 1961.*

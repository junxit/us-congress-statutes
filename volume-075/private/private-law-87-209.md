# Private Law 209

> AN ACT For the relief of Constantinos Georgiou Stavropoulos.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and rationality Act, Constantinos Georgiou Stavropoulos shall be held and considered to be the minor natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. John D. Stavropoulos, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents of Constantinos Georgiou Stavropoulos shall not, by virtue of such parentage be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved September 26, 1961.*

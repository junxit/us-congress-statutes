# Private Law 87–71

> AN ACT For the relief of Hok Yuen Woo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 206 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Hok Yuen Woo shall be held and considered to be the minor natural-born alien child of Chew Yat Woo, a United States citizen.


*Approved June 29, 1961.*

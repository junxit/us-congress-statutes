# Private Law 196

> AN ACT For the relief of Andrew Telesfor Kostanecki.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Home of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Andrew Telesfor Kostanecki shall be deemed to have been within the purview of the Act of May 24, 1934 (48 Stat. 797), at the time of his birth.


*Approved September 14, 1961.*

# Private Law 87–46

> AN ACT For the relief of Mah Quock.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mah Quock shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to permanent residence as of July 8, 1935.


*Approved June 21, 1961.*

# Private Law 87–35

> AN ACT For the relief of In Fil Chung, In Ae Chung, In Sook Chung and In Ja Chung.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor children. In Fil Chung, In Ae Chung, In Sook Chung and In Ja Chung, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien children of Mr. and Mrs. Elmer Bussmann, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural father of the beneficiaries, by virtue of such parentage, shall not be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved June 16, 1961.*

# Private Law 87–70

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Nobutaka Azuma.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Nobutaka Azuma shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of March 18, 1951.


*Approved June 29, 1961.*

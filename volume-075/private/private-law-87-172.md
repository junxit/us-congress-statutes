# Private Law 172

> AN ACT For the relief of Mary Dawn Poison (Emmy Lou Kim) and Joseph King Polson (Sung Sang Moon).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mary Dawn Poison (Emmy Lou Kim) and Joseph King Poison (Sung Sang Moon) shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien children of Vernon and Dawn Poison, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents of the beneficiaries shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved August 31, 1961.*

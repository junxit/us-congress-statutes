# Private Law 163

> AN ACT For the relief of Roger Chong Yeun Dunne.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Roger Chong Yeun Dunne shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of January 10, 1950, upon payment of the required visa fee and head tax. Upon the granting of permanent residence to such alien as provided for in this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the appropriate quota for the first year that such quota is available.


*Approved August 30, 1961.*

# Private Law 87–275

> AN ACT For the relief of Kabalan Farris.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Act of September 22, 1959 (Public Law 86–363), Kabalan Farris shall be deemed to be within the purview of section 4 of that Act.


*Approved October 3, 1961.*

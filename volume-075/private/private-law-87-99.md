# Private Law 87–99

> AN ACT For the relief of Urszula Sikora, Radoslav Vulin, and Desanka Vulin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Urszula Sikora, shall be held and considered to be the natural-bom alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Benno Coster, citizens of the United States.

## § 2.

For the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor children, Radoslav Vulin and Desanka Vulin, shall be held and considered to be the naturalborn alien children of Mr. and Mrs. Dragutin Vulin, citizens of the United States.

## § 3.

The natural parents of the beneficiaries of this Act shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved June 30, 1961.*

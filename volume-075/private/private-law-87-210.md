# Private Law 210

> AN ACT For the relief of Guisseppe Glorioso.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Act of September 22, 1959 (Public Law 86–363), Guisseppe Glorioso shall be deemed to be within the purview of section 4 of that Act.


*Approved September 26, 1961.*

# Private Law 87–89

> AN ACT For the relief of Tomislav Lazarevich.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 4 of the Act of September 22, 1959, Tomislav lazarevich shall be deemed to have been, registered on a consular waiting list pursuant to section 203(c) of the Immigration and Nationality Act under a priority date earlier than December 31, 1953.


*Approved June 30, 1961.*

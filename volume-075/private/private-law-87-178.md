# Private Law 178

> AN ACT For the relief of Giuseppa Alonzi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purposes of section 4 of the Act of September 22, 1959, Giuseppa Alonzi shall be deemed to have been registered on a consular waiting list pursuant to section 203(c) of the Immigration and Nationality Act under a priority date earlier than December 31, 1953, and the visa petition approved in behalf of Giuseppa Alonzi shall be deemed to nave been approved prior to January 1, 1959.


*Approved September 6, 1961.*

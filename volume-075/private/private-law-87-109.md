# Private Law 109

> AN ACT For the relief of Jose Lauchengco, Junior.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

Jose Lauchengco, Jr.

66 Stat. 166, 180.

8 USC 1101, 1155.

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a)(27)(A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Jose Lauchengco, Junior, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born minor alien child of Mrs. Angeles Y. Sapota, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved July 11, 1961.*

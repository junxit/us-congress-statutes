# Private Law 87–77

> AN ACT For the relief of Charles Edward Pifer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 fa) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Charles Edward Pifer, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Joy J. Pifer and Griffin Watson Pifer, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents of the said Charles Edward Pifer shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved June 30, 1961.*

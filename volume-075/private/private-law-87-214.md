# Private Law 214

> AN ACT For the relief of Maria Trela Terpak.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Maria Trela Terpak, shall be held and considered to be the natural-bom alien child of Mr. and Mrs. George Terpak, Junior, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents of the said Maria Trela Terpak shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved September 26, 1961.*

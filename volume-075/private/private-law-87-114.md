# Private Law 114

> AN ACT For the relief of Henry Eugene Godderis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of title III of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Henry Eugene Godderis shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence on September 1, 1955.


*Approved July 14, 1961.*

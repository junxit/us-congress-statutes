# Private Law 160

> AN ACT For the relief of Huan-pin Tso.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child Huan-pin Tso shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Ting Hsien Wang, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents of the said Huan-pin Tso shall not, by virtue of such parentage be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved August 30, 1961.*

# Private Law 212

> AN ACT For the relief of Young Jei Oh and Soon Nee Lee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor children. Young Jei Oh and Soon Nee Lee, shall be held and considered to be the minor alien children of Mr. and Mrs. Robert J. Riddell, citizens of the United States: Provided, That no natural parent of the beneficiaries, by virtue of such parentage, shall be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved September 26, 1961.*

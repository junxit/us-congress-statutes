# Private Law 87–5

> AN ACT For the relief of Mico Delic.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child Mico Delic shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Eli Delich, citizens of the United States.


*Approved May 25, 1961.*

# Private Law 180

> AN ACT For the relief of Aideh Kobler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the provisions of sections 201(a), 202(a)(5) and 202(b)(2) of that Act shall not be applicable in the case of Mrs. Aideh Kobler, and she shall be considered to be accompanying her spouse within the meaning of section 202(a) (2) of the said Act if she files an application for a visa within six months from the date of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved September 6, 1961.*

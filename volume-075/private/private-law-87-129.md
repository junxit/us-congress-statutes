# Private Law 129

> AN ACT For the relief of Jung Ngon Woon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Jung Ngon Woon shall be held and considered to be the natural-born minor alien child of Thomas Wook Jung, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved July 31, 1961.*

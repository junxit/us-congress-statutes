# Private Law 87–73

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Keum Ja Asato (Mrs. Thomas R. Asato).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Keum Ja Asato, the widow of a United States citizen who served honorably in the Armed Forces of the United States, shall be held and considered to be within the purview of section 101(a) (27) (A) of that Act and the provisions of section 205 of that Act shall not be applicable in this case.


*Approved June 29, 1961.*

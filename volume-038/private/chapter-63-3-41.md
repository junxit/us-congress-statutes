# Chapter 41

> An Act For the relief of Isaac Bethurum.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Isaac Bethurum, who was a private of Company B, Fifteenth Regiment Kansas Volunteer Cavalry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a member of said company and regiment on the seventeenth day of October, eighteen hundred and sixty-five: Provided, That no back pay, bounty, or pension shall accrue to him prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 17, 1915.*

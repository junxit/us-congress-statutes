# Chapter 321

> An Act For the relief of Wilhelmina Rohe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws John Rohe shall be hereafter held and considered to have been drowned in Nagasaki Harbor, Japan, on the twentieth day of March, nineteen hundred and one, in line of duty and while in the service of the United States as a private in Company M, Twenty-sixth Regiment United States Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, October 14, 1914.*

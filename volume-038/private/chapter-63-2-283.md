# Chapter 283

> An Act To validate the homestead entry of William H. Miller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the homestead entry of William H. Miller, numbered naught ten thousand two hundred and twenty-four, made October twenty-eighth, nineteen hundred and nine, for the northwest quarter of section twenty-nine, township twenty north, range forty-nine west of the sixth principal meridian, in the State of Nebraska, be, and the same is hereby, validated.


*Approved, August 22, 1914.*

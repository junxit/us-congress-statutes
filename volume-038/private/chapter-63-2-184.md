# Chapter 184

> An Act For the relief of Charles R. Grant.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Charles R. Grant shall hereafter be held and considered to have been in the military service of the United States as a member of Company F, Twelfth Regiment Connecticut Volunteer Infantry, and to have been honorably discharged therefrom: Provided, however, That this Act shall not confer upon him any right to pay, annuities, pensions, or other emoluments which might otherwise accrue to him from such service.


*Approved, July 17, 1914.*

# Chapter 101

> An Act For the relief of Anna Miller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay. out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Anna Miller, of Glencoe, Wyoming, the sum of $116, as full reimbursement for sums paid by her for carrying the United States mail between the railroad station and the post office at Glencoe, Wyoming, from December twentieth, nineteen hundred and six, to January sixth, nineteen hundred and eight.


*Approved, March 3, 1915.*

# Chapter 305

> An Act For the relief of Philip Cook.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws Philip Cook, who was a private of Troop H, Sixth Regiment United States Cavalry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a member of said troop and regiment on the third day of August, eighteen hundred and sixty-five: Provided, That no back pay, bounty, pension, or other emolument shall accrue by reason of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, September 19, 1914.*

# Chapter 215

> An Act For the relief of Amato Castellano, Libero Baranello, and Michele Baranello.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, out of any money in the United States treasury not otherwise appropriated, to pay to Amato Castellano, Libero Baranello, and Michele Baranello, all Brooklyn, New York, the sum of $2,000, being the amount heretofore paid into the Treasury of the United States in settlement of their certain bail bond in the case of United States against Carmana Lobosco, convicted in the United States Circuit Court for the Eastern District of New York, and thereafter, while a fugitive from justice, rearrested through the efforts of said sureties.


*Approved, March 4, 1915.*

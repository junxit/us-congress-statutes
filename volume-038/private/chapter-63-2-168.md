# Chapter 168

> An Act For the relief of Marcus L. Pelham.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws Marcus L. Pelham shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a lieutenant in Company G, Eighth Regiment Connecticut Volunteer Infantry, on the twenty-ninth day of December, eighteen hundred and sixty-four: Provided, That no pension shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, July 17, 1914.*

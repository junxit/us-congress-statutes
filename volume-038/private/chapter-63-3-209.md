# Chapter 209

> An Act Granting relief to Jacob Barger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws. Jacob Barger shall hereafter be held and considered to have been mustered as a private in Company E, Fourteenth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Cavalry on the twentieth day of June, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, and to have been honorably discharged on the first day of March, eighteen hundred and sixty-five.


*Approved, March 4, 1915.*

# Chapter 93

> An Act To reinstate Frank Ellsworth McCorkle as a cadet at United States Military Academy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized to reappoint as cadet at the United States Military Academy, without regard to age or the existence of vacancies, Frank Ellsworth McCorkle.


*Approved, May 16, 1914.*

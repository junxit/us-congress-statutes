# Chapter 128

> An Act For the relief of John Q. Adams.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the homestead entry of John Q. Adams, made June twenty-third, nineteen hundred and nine, for farm unit A on the Bellefourche reclamation project, South Dakota, under the Act of June seventeenth, nineteen hundred and two, for lots one and two, section two, township nine north, range four east, Black Hills meridian, be, and the same is hereby, validated, subject to future compliance with the law.


*Approved, June 25, 1914.*

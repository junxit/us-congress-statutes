# Chapter 114

> An Act For the relief of Peter Scott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Peter Scott, who was a private of Company K, Fifty-sixth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a private of said company and regiment on the tenth day of July, eighteen hundred and sixty-four: Provided, That no pension, emoluments, or allowances of any description shall accrue by reason of this Act prior to the date of approval thereof.


*Approved, March 3, 1915.*

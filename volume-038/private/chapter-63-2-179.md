# Chapter 179

> An Act To authorize Louis Eder to enter lands under the homestead laws.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Louis Eder, of Laramie County, Wyoming, be, and he is hereby, authorized to enter, and secure title to the same under the homestead laws of the United States, one hundred and sixty acres of surveyed unreserved and unappropriated public lands in lieu of the north half of north half of section twenty-eight, township fourteen north, range seventy west, of sixth principal meridian, relinquished by him to the Government for inclusion in the withdrawal of lands for the protection of the water supply of a military post, and no charge shall be made for making the entry of record to cover the tract selected: Provided, That the requirements of the laws respecting settlement, residence, improvements, and so forth, are complied with on the new claim, credit being allowed for the time spent on the relinquished claim.


*Approved, July 17, 1914.*

# Chapter 281

> An Act For the relief of C. F. Jackson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the title of C. F. Jackson in and to the northwest quarter of section twenty-seven, township fifteen, range sixteen, Noxubee County, Mississippi, as assignee of the conveyance by McKee Folsom, be, and the same is hereby, quieted and confirmed, and patent therefor shall issue to the said C. F. Jackson.


*Approved, August 22, 1914.*

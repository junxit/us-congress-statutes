# Chapter 62

> An Act For the relief of George W. Hoyt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the accounts of George W. Hoyt, postmaster at Cheyenne, Wyoming, in the sum of $9,917.50, due to the United States on account of money-order funds embezzled by Joseph Kingman, late assistant postmaster at Cheyenne, Wyoming, in the year nineteen hundred and eleven; and the sum of $9,917.50 is hereby appropriated, out of any moneys in the Treasury of the United States not otherwise appropriated, for the payment of this claim.


*Approved, April 17, 1914.*

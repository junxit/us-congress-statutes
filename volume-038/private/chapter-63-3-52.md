# Chapter 52

> An Act Authorizing the issuance of patent to Arthur J. Floyd for section thirty-one, township twenty-two north, range twenty-two west of the sixth principal meridian, in the State of Nebraska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to issue patent to Arthur J. Floyd for section thirty-one, in township twenty-two north, range twenty-two west of the sixth principal meridian, in the State of Nebraska.


*Approved, February 23, 1915.*

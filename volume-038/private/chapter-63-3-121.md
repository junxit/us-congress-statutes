# Chapter 121

> An Act For the relief of John M. Gray.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, John M. Gray, who was a private of Company K, Eighty-sixth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a private of said company and regiment on the ninth day of December, eighteen hundred and sixty-three.


*Approved, March 3, 1915.*

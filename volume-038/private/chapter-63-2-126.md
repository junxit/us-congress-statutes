# Chapter 126

> An Act To reinstate Francis Graves Bonham as a cadet at the United States Military Academy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized to reappoint as a cadet at the United States Military Academy, without regard to age or the existence of vacancies, Francis Graves Bonham.


*Approved, June 24, 1914.*

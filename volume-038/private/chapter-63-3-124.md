# Chapter 124

> An Act For the relief of L. V. Thomas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to L. V. Thomas the sum of $1,680, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, for personal injuries received while working on the Panama Canal in the employ of the Panama Canal Commission as a carpenter.


*Approved, March 3, 1915.*

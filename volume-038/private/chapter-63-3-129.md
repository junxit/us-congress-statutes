# Chapter 129

> An Act To provide for the payment of the claim of J. O. Modisette for services performed for the Chickasaw Indians of Oklahoma.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of Indian Affairs is hereby authorized to investigate the claim of J. O. Modisette, of Jennings, Louisiana, amounting to $270, alleged to be due as tuition for instructing certain Chickasaw pupils, and, in his discretion, to pay such claim wholly or in part from funds on deposit in the Treasury of the United States to the credit of the Chickasaw Nation.


*Approved, March 3, 1915.*

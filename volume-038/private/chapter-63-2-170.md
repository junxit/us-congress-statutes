# Chapter 170

> An Act For the relief of Hosea Stone.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws and the laws governing the National Home for Disabled Volunteer Soldiers, or any branch thereof, Hosea Stone, late of Battery A, Fourth Regiment United States Artillery, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as of Battery A, Fourth Regiment United States Artillery, on the twenty-first day of July, eighteen hundred and sixty-five: Provided, That no pension shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, July 17, 1914.*

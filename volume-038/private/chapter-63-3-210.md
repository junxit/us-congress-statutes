# Chapter 210

> An Act For the relief of William Ham.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers William Ham, who was a private in Company F, Eleventh Regiment Veteran Reserve Corps, shall hereafter no held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a private of said company and regiment on the fourteenth day of November, eighteen hundred and sixty-five: Provided, That no back pension, back pay, back allowances, or other emoluments shall accrue by reason of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 4, 1915.*

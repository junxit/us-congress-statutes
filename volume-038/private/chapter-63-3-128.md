# Chapter 128

> An Act For the relief of William A. Wallace.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, William A. Wallace, who was a private in Company E, Thirteenth Regiment New York Volunteer Militia, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been mustered into service of the United States as a private of said company and regiment on the twenty-third day of April, eighteen hundred and sixty-one: Provided, That no back pay, bounty, pension, or other emolument shall accrue prior to the approval of this Act.


*Approved, March 3, 1915.*

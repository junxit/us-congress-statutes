# Chapter 319

> An Act Authorizing the health officer of the District of Columbia to issue a permit for the removal of the remains of the late Earl A. Bancroft from Glenwood Cemetery, District of Columbia, to Mantorville, Minnesota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the health officer of the District of Columbia be, and he is hereby, authorized to issue a permit for the removal of the remains of the late Earl A. Bancroft from Glenwood Cemetery, District of Columbia, to Mantorville, Minnesota.


*Approved, October 7, 1914.*

# Chapter 34

> An Act For the relief of Charles Richter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws and the laws governing the National Home for Disabled Volunteer Soldiers, or any branch thereof, Charles Richter, alias Herman Wittman, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a private of Company K, Thirty-fifth Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer Infantry: Provided, That no pension shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 17, 1915.*

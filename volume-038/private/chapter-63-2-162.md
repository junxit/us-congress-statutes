# Chapter 162

> An Act For the relief of George Gardells.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of pension laws, George Gardells shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as private in Company B Fifth Regiment New York Volunteer Cavalry, on the nineteenth day of April, eighteen hundred and sixty-four: Provided, That no pension shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, July 17, 1914.*

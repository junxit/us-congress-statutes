# Chapter 126

> An Act For the relief of Edward Whiteside

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Edward Whiteside, who was a musician in Company C, Fifth New York Infantry Volunteers, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a musician of said company and regiment on or about the fourteenth day of May, eighteen hundred and sixty-three: Provided, That no back pay, bounty, pension, or allowance shall be allowed by reason of this Act.


*Approved, March 3, 1915.*

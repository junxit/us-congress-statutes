# Chapter 221

> An Act For the relief of Lucien P. Rogers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers Lucien P. Rogers shall hereafter be held and considered to have been in the military service of the United States as a member of Company F, Seventy-fourth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, from August first, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, to June twenty-eighth, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, and to have been honorably discharged from that service on the last-named date.


*Approved, July 31, 1914.*

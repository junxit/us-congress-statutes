# Chapter 231

> An Act For the relief of Seth Watson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Seth Watson, who was a first lieutenant of Company B, Twenty-eighth Regiment United States Colored Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a member of said company and regiment on the twenty-fourth day of June, eighteen hundred and sixty-four: Provided, That no pay or bounty shall accrue or become payable by reason of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, August 6, 1914.*

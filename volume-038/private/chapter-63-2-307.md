# Chapter 307

> An Act For the relief of Mirick Burgess.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Mirick Burgess, who was a private of Company I, Third Regiment New Hampshire Volunteer Infantry, and of Company H, Twelfth Regiment United States Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a member of the last-named company and regiment on March twenty-eighth, eighteen hundred and sixty-three: Provided, That no pay nor bounty shall accrue or become payable by reason of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, September 19, 1914.*

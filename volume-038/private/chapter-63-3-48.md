# Chapter 48

> An Act Admitting to citizenship and fully naturalizing George Edward Lerrigo, of the city of Topeka, in the State of Kansas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That George Edward Lerrigo, the son of an American citizen, of the city of Topeka, in Shawnee County, State of Kansas, is hereby admitted and declared to be a citizen of the United States of America, and is fully naturalized as such citizen for all purposes from and alter the taking effect of this Act.


*Approved, February 23, 1915.*

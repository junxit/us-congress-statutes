# Chapter 174

> An Act For the relief of C. M. Hammond.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of $150 be, and the same hereby is, appropriated, out of any money in the United States Treasury not otherwise appropriated, and the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby directed to pay to C. M. Hammond, of New York City, New York, a deputy collector of internal revenue, in compensation for money paid by him into the Treasury of the United States for cigar stamps supposed to have been lost in the United States mails, but thereafter found and delivered to the collector of internal revenue at Albany, New York.


*Approved, July 17, 1914.*

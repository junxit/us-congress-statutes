# Chapter 118

> An Act For the relief of Daniel Hampton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws and the laws governing the National Home for Disabled Volunteer Soldiers, or any branch thereof, Daniel Hampton, who was a member of Company D, Twenty-fourth Regiment Massachusetts Volunteer Infantry, and of Company G, Twenty-fourth Veteran Reserve Corps, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a member of the last-named organization on the fourteenth day of August, eighteen hundred and sixty-five: Provided, That no pension shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 19, 1914.*

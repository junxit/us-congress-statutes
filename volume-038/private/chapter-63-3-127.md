# Chapter 127

> An Act For the relief of Joseph A. Powers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $256.17, as compensation to Joseph A. Powers, a letter carrier in the city of New York, New York, for loss of salary resulting from injuries received by him on February twenty-third, nineteen hundred and twelve, from the breaking of a stool which he was using in his employment.


*Approved, March 3, 1915.*

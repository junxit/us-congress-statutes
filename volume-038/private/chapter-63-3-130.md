# Chapter 130

> An Act For the relief of James W. McGreevey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, James W. McGreevey, who was a musician in Company A, Second Regiment United States Veteran Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a musician of said company and regiment on the second day of July, eighteen hundred and sixty-five.


*Approved, March 3, 1915.*

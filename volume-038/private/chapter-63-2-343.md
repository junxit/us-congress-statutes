# Chapter 343

> An Act For the relief of Patrick McGee, alias Patrick Gallagher.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws Patrick McGee, alias Patrick Gallagher, who served as a private in Company H, Tenth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, under the name of Patrick Gallagher, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a member of said company and regiment: Provided, That no back pay, bounty, pension, or allowance shall be allowed by reason of this Act.


*Approved, October 22, 1914.*

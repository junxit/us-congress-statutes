# Chapter 138

> An Act For the relief of George Richardson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the title of George Richardson in and to the northwest quarter of section thirty-three, township fifteen, range sixteen, Noxubee County, Mississippi, as assignee of the conveyance of John Victor, be, and the same is hereby, quieted and confirmed, and patent therefor shall issue to the said George Richardson.


*Approved, March 3, 1915.*

# Chapter 80

> An Act For the relief of Allen Edward O’Toole and others, who sustained damage by reason of accident at Rock Island Arsenal.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the relief of Allen Edward O’Toole, Bessie O’Toole, and Clifford O’Toole, whose father, Edward O’Toole, was fatally injured by an accident at the Rock Island Arsenal February twenty-fourth, nineteen hundred and five, the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the following sums, namely: To Allen Edward O’Toole, $333.33; to Bessie O’Toole, $333.33; to Clifford O’Toole, $333.34: Provided, That in case any of the beneficiaries herein named are minors the amount appropriated shall be paid to his or her duly appointed guardian.


*Approved, May 8, 1914.*

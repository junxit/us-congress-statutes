# Chapter 107

> An Act For the relief of Alonzo D. Cadwallader.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws Alonzo D. Cadwallader shall hereafter be held and considered to have performed ninety days actual military service as a private of Company K, Seventeenth Regiment Michigan Volunteer Infantry: Provided, That no pension shall accrue prior to the approval of this Act.


*Approved, March 3, 1915.*

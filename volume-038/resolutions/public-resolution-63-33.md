# Public Resolution 33

> Joint Resolution Granting authority to the American Red. Cross to charter a ship or snips of foreign register for the transportation of nurses and supplies and for all uses in connection with the work of that society.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That authority be granted to the American Red Cross, during the continuance of the present war, to charter a ship or ships of foreign register, to carry the American flag, for the transportation of nurses and supplies and for all uses in connection with the work of said society.


*Approved, August 20, 1914.*

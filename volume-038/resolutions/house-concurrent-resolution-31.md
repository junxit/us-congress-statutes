# House Concurrent Resolution 31

> statue of john gorrie.

*Resolved by the House, of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the State of Florida be, and is hereby, granted the privilege of placing in Statuary Hall of the Capitol the statue of John Gorrie, now deceased, who was a citizen of Florida, illustrious for distinguished civic services.


*Passed, February 6, 1914.*

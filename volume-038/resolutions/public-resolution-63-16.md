# Public Resolution 16

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the President to extend invitations to foreign Governments to participate in the International Congress of Americanists.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized to extend invitations to foreign Governments to be represented by delegates at the Nineteenth International Congress of Americanists, to be held at the city of Washington in October of the year nineteen hundred and fourteen: Provided, That no appropriation shall be granted for expenses of delegates or for other expenses incurred in connection with the said conference.


*Approved, May 9, 1914.*

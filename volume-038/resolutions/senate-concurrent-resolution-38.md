# Senate Concurrent Resolution 38

> texas coast oyster beds.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the action of the Speaker of the House of Representatives and of the Vice President of the United States and President of the Senate in signing the enrolled bill (S. 3362) “to authorize the Secretary of Commerce, through the Coast and Geodetic Survey and the Bureau of Fisheries, to make a survey of natural oyster beds, bars and rocks, and barren bottoms contiguous thereto in waters along the coast of and within the State of Texas” be, and is hereby, rescinded, and that in the reenrollment of the bill the words “and directed,” in line four of the bill, be stricken out and the words “in his discretion,” substituted therefor.


*Passed, March 3, 1915.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 28

> statue of george washington glick.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the statue of George Washington Glick, presented by the State of Kansas to be placed in Statuary Hall, is accepted in the name of the United States, and that the thanks of Congress be tendered the State for the contribution of the Statue of one of its most eminent citizens, illustrious for his distinguished civic services.

## Second.

That a copy of these resolutions, suitably engrossed and duly authenticated, be transmitted to the governor of the State of Kansas.


*Passed, February 12, 1915.*

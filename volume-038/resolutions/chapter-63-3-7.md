# Chapter 7

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the Secretary of Commerce to postpone the sale of furseal skins now in the possession of the Government until such time as in his discretion he may deem such sale advisable.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of Commerce be, and he hereby is, authorized to postpone the sale of all skins now in possession of the Government, taken from seals killed on the Pribilof Islands for food purposes, under section eleven of the Act of August twenty-fourth, nineteen hundred and twelve, until such time as, in his discretion, he shall deem advisable; and the proceeds of such sale shall be covered into the Treasury of the United States.


*Approved, February 24, 1915.*

# Public Resolution 8

> Joint Resolution To authorize the appointment of Thomas Green Peyton as a cadet in the United States Military Academy.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized to appoint Thomas Green Peyton a cadet in the United States Military Academy: Provided, That this shall not operate to increase the Corps of Cadets at said academy as now authorized by law.


*Approved, September 3, 1913.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 24

> joint meeting.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Tuesday, the second day of December, 1913, at one o’clock in the afternoon, for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make them.


*Passed, November 29, 1913.*

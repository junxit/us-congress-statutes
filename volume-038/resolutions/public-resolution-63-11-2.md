# Public Resolution 11

> Joint Resolution Reappropriating certain funds for expenditure at the Naval Station at New Orleans, Louisiana.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the unexpended balances of appropriations heretofore made for the Naval Station, New Orleans, Louisiana, and not yet turned back into the Treasury, are hereby reappropriated and made available for expenditure at that Station for such purpose as the Secretary of the Navy may direct.


*Approved, April 25, 1914.*

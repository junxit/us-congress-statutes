# Public Resolution 19

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the President to accept an invitation to participate in the Sixth International Congress of Chambers of Commerce and Commercial and Industrial Associations.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized to accept an invitation extended by the Government of the French Republic to the Government of the United States to participate by delegates in the Sixth International Congress of Chambers of Commerce and Commercial and Industrial Associations, to be held at Paris from the eighth to the tenth of June, nine-teen hundred and fourteen: Provided, That no appropriation shall be granted for expenses of delegates or for other expenses incurred in connection with the said conference.


*Approved, May 28, 1914.*

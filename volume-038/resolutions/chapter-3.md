# Chapter 3

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the Commissioner of Patents to exchange printed copies of United States patents with the Dominion of Canada.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of Patents of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized to exchange with the Dominion of Canada, under such terms of contract as may by him be deemed practicable, printed copies of patents now in the United States Patent Office and hereafter issued by the United States.


*Approved, January 14, 1915.*

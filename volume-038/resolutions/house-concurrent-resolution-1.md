# House Concurrent Resolution 1

> joint meeting

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Tuesday, the eighth day of April, nineteen hundred and thirteen, at 12:30 o’clock in the afternoon, for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed, April 7, 1913.*

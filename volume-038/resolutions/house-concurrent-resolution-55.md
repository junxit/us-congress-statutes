# House Concurrent Resolution 55

> holiday recess.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn December twenty-third, nineteen hundred and fourteen, they stand adjourned until twelve o’clock meridian on Tuesday, December twenty-ninth, nineteen hundred and fourteen.


*Passed, December 16, 1914.*

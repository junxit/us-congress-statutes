# Public Resolution 4

> Joint Resolution Extending the leave of absence of Mrs. A. E. Grant.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioners of the District of Columbia be, and they hereby are, authorized to further extend, for a period not exceeding six months, with pay the leave of absence to Airs. A. E. Grant, a clerk in the office of the assessor of the District of Columbia.


*Approved, May 29, 1913.*

# Chapter 13

> Joint Resolution Concerning estimates and expenditures for the Reclamation Service.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That until the meeting of the Sixty-fourth Congress, those members of the Committee on Appropriations of the House of Representatives, not less than five m number, who are members elect to the Sixty-fourth Congress, are authorized to conduct hearings at Washington, District of Columbia, and elsewhere concerning the work of the Reclamation Service and estimates for the various projects of that service.


*Approved, March 4, 1915.*

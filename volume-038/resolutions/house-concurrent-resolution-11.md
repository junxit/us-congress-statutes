# House Concurrent Resolution 11

> tariff bill.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed thirty thousand copies of the bill (H. R. 3321) with amendments, as reported in the Senate July eleventh, nineteen hundred and thirteen, twenty thousand copies for the use of the House and ten thousand copies for the use of the Senate.


*Passed, July 14, 1913.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 37

> joint meeting.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Monday, the twentieth day of April, nineteen hundred and fourteen, at three o’clock in the afternoon, for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make them.


*Passed, April 20, 1914.*

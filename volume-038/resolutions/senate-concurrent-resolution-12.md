# Senate Concurrent Resolution 12

> federal reserve act.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of Congress eighty-five thousand copies of the Federal Reserve Act, in pamphlet form, to be apportioned as follows: Forty thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives, twenty thousand copies for the use of the Senate, five thousand copies for the use of the Committee on Banking and Currency of the Senate, five thousand copies for the use of the Committee on Banking and Currency of the House, five thousand copies for the use of the document room of the Senate, and ten thousand copies for the use of the document room of the House.


*Passed, December 23, 1913.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 26

> holiday recess.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn December, the twenty-third, nineteen hundred and thirteen, they stand adjourned until twelve o’clock, meridian, on Monday, January the twelfth, nineteen hundred and fourteen.


*Passed, December 23, 1913.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 16

> joint meeting.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

Thatthe two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives, on Wednesday, the twenty-seventh day of August, nineteen hundred and thirteen, at twelve o’clock and forty-five minutes in the afternoon, for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make them.


*Passed, August 26, 1913.*

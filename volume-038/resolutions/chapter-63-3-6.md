# Chapter 6

> Joint Resolution Requesting the President of the United States to invite foreign Governments to participate in the International Congress on Education.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States is hereby authorized and requested to invite foreign Governments to appoint honorary vice presidents and otherwise participate in the International Congress on Education, to be held at Oakland, California, August sixteenth to twenty-seventh, nine-teen hundred and fifteen, in connection with the Panama-Pacific International Exposition: Provided, That no appropriation shall be granted at any time hereafter in connection with said congress.


*Approved, February 23, 1915.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 8

> prayers by the chaplain, house of representatives.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the House of Representatives, to be distributed through the folding room, fifty thousand copies of House Document Numbered Fourteen hundred and fifty-eight, Sixty-second Congress, same being “Prayers offered at the opening of the sessions of the Sixty-second Congress of the United States.”


*Passed, February 4, 1914.*

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

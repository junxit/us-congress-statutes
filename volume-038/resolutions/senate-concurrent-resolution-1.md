# Senate Concurrent Resolution 1

> concentration of control of money and credit.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed 6,000 additional copies of House Report No. 1593, 62d Congress, on the “Concentration of Control of Money and Credit,” of which 2,000 copies shall be for the use of the Senate Document Room and 4,000 copies for the use of the House Document Room.


*Passed, April 30, 1913.*

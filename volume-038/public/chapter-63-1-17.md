# Chapter 17

> An Act To fix the times and places of holding district court for the district of Arizona.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the State of Arizona shall constitute one judicial district, to be known as the district of Arizona.

## § 2.

That terms of the district court shall be held in Tucson on the first Mondays in May and November; at Phoenix on the first Mondays in April and October; at Prescott on the first Mondays in March and September; and at Globe on the first Mondays in June and December. Causes, civil and criminal, may be transferred by the court or judge thereof from any of the aforesaid places where court shall be held in said district to any of the places herein above mentioned in said district when the convenience of the parties or the ends of justice would be promoted by the transfer; and any interlocutory order may be made by the court or judge thereof in any of the herein above mentioned places.


*Approved, October 3, 1913.*

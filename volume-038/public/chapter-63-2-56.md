# Chapter 56

> An Act Making appropriations for the support of the Military Academy for the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, nineteen hundred and fifteen, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the following sums be, and the same are hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the support of the Military Academy for the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, nineteen hundred and fifteen.

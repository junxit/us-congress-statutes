# Chapter 54

> An Act To regulate details of majors in the Ordnance Department.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That majors may be detailed in the Ordnance Department, under section twenty-six of the Act approved February second, nineteen hundred and one, and Acts amendatory thereof, without a compulsory period of service out of that department.


*Approved, February 24, 1915.*

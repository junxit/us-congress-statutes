# Chapter 49

> An Act To increase the limit of cost for increased quarantine facilities at the port of Portland, Maine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limit of cost for increased quarantine facilities at the port of Portland, Maine, authorized by the Act approved August twenty-fourth, nineteen hundred and twelve, is hereby increased by the additional sum of $23,620.


*Approved, April 3, 1914.*

# Chapter 98

> An Act To place Barrow County, Georgia, in the eastern division of the northern district of Georgia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the county of Barrow, in the State of Georgia, is hereby attached to and made a part of the eastern division of the northern judicial district of said State.


*Approved, March 3, 1915.*

# Chapter 152

> An Act To increase the appropriation for the erection of an immigration station at Baltimore, Maryland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section twenty-nine of the public building Act approved March fourth, nineteen hundred and thirteen, providing for an immigration station at Baltimore, Maryland, is hereby amended so as to increase the limit of cost from $280,000 to $550,000.


*Approved, July 17, 1914.*

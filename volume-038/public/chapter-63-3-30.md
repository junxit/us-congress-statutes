# Chapter 30

> An Act To increase the limit of cost of the United States post-office building and site at Saint Petersburg, Florida.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limit of cost of the United States post-office building at Saint Petersburg, Florida, be, and the same is hereby, increased from $80,000 to $115,000, of which not to exceed $12,500 may be expended for additional land for the enlargement of the present site, payment for such additional land to be made from any sums heretofore appropriated for the construction of said building.


*Approved, February 17, 1915.*

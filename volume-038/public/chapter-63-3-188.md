# Chapter 188

> An Act To increase the limit of cost of the United States post-office building and site at Cohoes, New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limit of cost heretofore authorized by Congress for the purchase of a site and the erection of a post-office building at Cohoes, New York, be, and the same is hereby, increased from $100, 000 to $140, 000.


*Approved, March 4, 1915.*

# Chapter 185

> An Act To increase the appropriation for a public building at Elkins, West Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limit of cost heretofore authorized for the erection of a building for the use and accommodation of the post office and other offices of the Government at Elkins, West Virginia, be increased from $85,000 to $135,000.


*Approved, March 4, 1915.*

# Chapter 97

> An Act To amend an Act entitled “An Act providing that the State of Wyoming be permitted to relinquish to the United States certain lands heretofore selected and to select other lands from the public domain in lieu thereof,” approved April twelfth, nineteen hundred and ten.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of the Act entitled “An Act providing that the State of Wyoming be permitted to relinquish to the United States certain lands heretofore selected and to select other lands from the public domain in lieu thereof,” approved April twelfth, nineteen hundred and ten, be, and the same are hereby, extended so as to include and apply to the southeast quarter of section thirteen in township twenty-seven north, range eighty-five west.


*Approved, May 25, 1914.*

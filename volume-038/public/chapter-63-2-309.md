# Chapter 309

> An Act To relocate the headquarters of the customs district, of Florida.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That hereafter the headquarters of the customs district of Florida shall be at Tampa, in said State.


*Approved, September 24, 1914.*

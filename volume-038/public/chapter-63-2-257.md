# Chapter 257

> An Act To increase the limit of cost of public building at Shelbyville, Tennessee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limit of cost of the United States post-office building at Shelbyville, Tennessee, be, and the same is hereby, increased $5,000.


*Approved, August 20, 1914.*

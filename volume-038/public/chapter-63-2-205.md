# Chapter 205

> An Act To increase the limit of cost of the United States post-office building at Newcastle, Indiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limit of cost of the United States post-office building at Newcastle, Indiana, be, and the same is hereby, increased $15,000 or so much thereof as may be necessary to meet the additional cost of construction of said building by the substitution of stone instead of brick with stone trimmings, as specified in the existing specification.


*Approved, July 22, 1914.*

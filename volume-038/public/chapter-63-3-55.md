# Chapter 55

> An Act To make Nyando, New York, a port through which merchandise may be imported for transportation without appraisement.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the privileges of the first section of the Act approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, governing the immediate transportation of dutiable merchandise without appraisement, be, and the same are hereby, extended to the port of Nyando, New York.


*Approved, February 24, 1915.*

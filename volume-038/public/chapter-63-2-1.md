# Chapter 1

> An Act Authorizing the appointment of envoys extraordinary and ministers plenipotentiary to each Paraguay and Uruguay.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is hereby authorized to appoint, as the representative of the United States, an envoy extraordinary and minister plenipotentiary to Paraguay, who shall receive as his compensation the sum of $10,000 per annum.

## § 2.

That the President is hereby further authorized to appoint, as the representative of the United States, an envoy extraordinary and minister plenipotentiary to Uruguay, who shall receive as his compensation the sum of $10,000 per annum.


*Approved, December 6, 1913.*

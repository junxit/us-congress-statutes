# Chapter 295

> An Act To fix the time for holding the term of the district court in the Jonesboro division of the eastern district of Arkansas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That hereafter the terms of the United States district court for the Jonesboro division of the eastern district of Arkansas shall be held at Jonesboro on the first Monday in May and the fourth Monday in November.


*Approved, September 9, 1914.*

# Chapter 154

> An Act To increase the limit of cost of Federal building at Pendleton, Oregon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That to enable the Secretary of the Treasury to erect and complete the post-office building at Pendleton, State of Oregon, provided for in existing legislation, and to provide therein accommodations for the United States courts and court officials and other Government offices, the limit of cost hereto-fore fixed by Congress be, and the same is hereby, increased in and by the sum of $60,000, and the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby authorized to enter into contract for the erection and completion of said building within the limit of cost herein established.


*Approved, July 17, 1914.*

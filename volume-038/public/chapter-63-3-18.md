# Chapter 18

> An Act To increase the limit of cost of the United States public building at Grand Junction, Colorado.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limit of cost of the United States public building at Grand Junction, Colorado, be, and the same hereby is, increased from the sum of $100,000 to the sum of $175,000, said increase being necessary in order to complete the building according to the present plans and specifications.


*Approved, January 26, 1915.*

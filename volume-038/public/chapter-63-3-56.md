# Chapter 56

> An Act To increase the limit of cost of the United States post-office building at Seymour, Indiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limit of cost of the United States post-office building at Seymour, Indiana, be, and the same is hereby, increased $15,000, or so much thereof as may be necessary to meet the additional cost of construction of said building by the substitution of stone instead of brick with stone trimmings, as specified in the existing specification.


*Approved, February 24, 1915.*

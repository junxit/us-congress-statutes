# Chapter 3

> An Act To increase and fix the compensation of the collector of customs for the customs collection district of Omaha.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the compensation of the collector of customs for the customs collection district of Omaha shall be increased from $2,500 per annum, as provided in the plan of reorganization of the customs service promulgated by the President on March third, nineteen hundred and thirteen, to $3,500 per annum, and that compensation at the said rate of $3,500 per annum shall be paid to the said collector of customs from and after June thirtieth, nineteen hundred and thirteen.


*Approved, December 28, 1914.*

# Chapter 68

> An Act Providing an appropriation for the relief and transportation of American citizens in Mexico.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the relief of American citizens in Mexico, including transportation to their homes in the United States, there is appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $500,000, to be expended at the discretion of the President.


*Approved, April 24, 1914.*

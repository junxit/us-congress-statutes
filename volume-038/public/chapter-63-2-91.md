# Chapter 91

> An Act To authorize the appointment of an ambassador to Argentina.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is hereby authorized to appoint, as the representative of the United States, an ambassador to Argentina, who shall receive as his compensation the sum of $17,500 per annum.


*Approved, May 16, 1914.*

# Chapter 328

> An Act To increase the limit of cost of public building at La Junta, Colorado.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limit of cost of the United States post-office building at La Junta, Colorado, be, and the same is hereby, increased $10,000.


*Approved, October 20, 1914.*

# Chapter 10

> An Act Authorizing the appointment of an ambassador to Spain.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is hereby authorized to appoint, as the representative of the United States, an ambassador to Spain, who shall receive as his compensation the sum of SI7,500 per annum.


*Approved, September 4, 1913.*

# Private Law 105–2

> An Act For the relief of John Wesley Davis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. WAIVER OF TIME LIMITATIONS.

The time limitations set forth in sections 3702(c) and 3328(a)(1) of title 31, United States Code, shall not apply with respect to a claim by John Wesley Davis, of Forestville, Maryland, for the amounts due to him by the— The amounts due are represented by checks that were received but not negotiated by John Wesley Davis.

- **(1)** Department of the Navy in the amount of $42,123.84; and
- **(2)** Department of the Treasury in the amount of $12,508.20.

## § 2. DEADLINE.

Section 1 shall apply only if John Wesley Davis or his authorized representative submits a claim pursuant to such subsection before the expiration of the 6-month period beginning on the date of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved August 11, 1997.*

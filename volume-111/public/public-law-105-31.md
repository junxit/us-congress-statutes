# Public Law 105–31

> An Act To waive temporarily the Medicaid enrollment composition rule for the Better Health Plan of Amherst, New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. WAIVER OF 75/25 MEDICAID ENROLLMENT RULE FOR BETTER HEALTH PLAN, INC.

Effective July 1, 1997, the requirement of section 1903(m)(2)(A)(ii) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1396b(m)(2)(A)(ii)) is waived, for contract periods through December 31, 1998, with respect to the Better Health Plan, Inc. operating in New York.


*Approved July 25, 1997.*

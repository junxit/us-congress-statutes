# Public Law 105–11

> An Act To make a technical correction to title 28, United States Code, relating to jurisdiction for lawsuits against terrorist states.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, effective with respect to any cause of action arising, before, on, or after the date of the enactment of this Act, section 1605(a)(7)(B)(ii) of title 28, United States Code, is amended by striking “the claimant or victim was not” and inserting “neither the claimant nor the victim was”.


*Approved April 25, 1997.*

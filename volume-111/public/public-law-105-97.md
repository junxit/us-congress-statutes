# Public Law 105–97

> An Act To designate the United States Post Office located at 150 North 3rd Street in Steubenville, Ohio, as the “Douglas Applegate Post Office”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The United States Post Office located at 150 North 3rd Street in Steubenville, Ohio, shall be known and designated as the “Douglas Applegate Post Office”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the United States Post Office referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Douglas Applegate Post Office”.


*Approved November 19, 1997.*

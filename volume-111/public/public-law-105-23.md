# Public Law 105–23

> An Act To amend section 2118 of the Energy Policy Act of 1992 to extend the Electric and Magnetic Fields Research and Public Information Dissemination program.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. AMENDMENTS.

Section 2118 of the Energy Policy Act of 1992 (42 U.S.C. 13478) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsections (c)(5), (e)(5), (g)(3)(B), (j)(1), and (1) by striking “1997” each place it appears and inserting in lieu thereof “1998”; and
- **(2)** in subsection (i)(1), by striking “$65,000,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$46,000,000”.


*Approved July 3, 1997.*

# Public Law 105–110

> An Act To amend the Act incorporating the American Legion to make a technical correction.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 5 of the Act entitled “An Act to Incorporate the American Legion”, approved September 16, 1919 (41 Stat. 285; 36 U.S.C. 45) is amended by striking “December 22, 1961” and inserting “February 28, 1961”.


*Approved November 20, 1997.*

# Public Law 105–71

> Joint Resolution Making further continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 1998, and for other purposes.

Resolved, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

That section 106(3) of Public Law 105–46 is further amended by striking “November 10, 1997” and inserting in lieu thereof “November 14, 1997”, and each provision amended by sections 122 and 123 of such public law shall be applied as if “November 14, 1997” was substituted for “October 23, 1997”.


*Approved November 10, 1997.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 269

> mrs. warren d. cooper and son

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (H. R. 8008) for the relief of Mrs. Warren D. Cooper and her son, Teddy Devere Cooper, the Clerk of the House is authorized and directed to make the following correction :

On page 2, line 1 of the House engrossed bill, strike out the date “September 2, 1934” and insert “September 23, 1934”.


*Passed July 25, 1956.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 248

> “shortage of scientific and engineering manpower”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Joint Committee on Atomic Energy thirty thousand additional copies of the hearings held by the Research and Development Subcommittee of the said joint committee during the Eighty-fourth Congress entitled “Shortage of Scientific and Engineering Manpower.”


*Passed July 12, 1956.*

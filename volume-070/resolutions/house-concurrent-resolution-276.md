# House Concurrent Resolution 276

> adjournment sine die

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on July 27, 1956, and that when they adjourn on that day, they stand adjourned sine die.


*Passed July 27, 1956.*

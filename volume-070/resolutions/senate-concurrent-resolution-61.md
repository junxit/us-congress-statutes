# Senate Concurrent Resolution 61

> “federal tax policy for economic growth and stability”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Joint Committee on the Economic Report one thousand additional copies of the joint committee print, entitled “Federal Tax Policy for Economic Growth and Stability.”


*Agreed to February 21, 1956.*

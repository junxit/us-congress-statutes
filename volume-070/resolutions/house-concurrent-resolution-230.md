# House Concurrent Resolution 230

> u. s. membership in organization for trade cooperation

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed two thousand five hundred additional copies of the hearings on H. R. 5550 a bill authorizing the President to accept membership for the United States in the Organization for Trade Cooperation, held by the Committee on Ways and Means for the use of the said committee.


*Passed May 21, 1956.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 265

> membership of communist china in un

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That it is the sense of the Congress that its previous expressions should be and are hereby reemphasized that the Communist regime in China should not be admitted to membership in the United Nations or any of its specialized agencies as the representatives of China; and

That the Congress hereby expresses its conviction that such admission would gravely injure the United Nations and impair its effective functioning in accordance with the aims, principles, and provisions of the United Nations Charter.


*Passed July 23, 1956.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 60

> automation and technological change

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Joint Committee on the Economic Report one thousand additional copies of the hearings on automation and technological change, held before said joint committee during the Eighty-fourth Congress, first session.


*Agreed to February 21, 1956.*

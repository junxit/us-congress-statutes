# House Concurrent Resolution 254

> communist conspiracy

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Un-American Activities, House of Representatives, ten thousand additional copies each of House Reports Numbered 2240, 2241, 2242, 2243, and 2244, current session, all of which are reports on the Communist conspiracy.


*Passed July 26, 1956.*

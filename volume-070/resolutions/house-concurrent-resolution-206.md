# House Concurrent Resolution 206

> national highway program

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Public Works, House of Representatives, one thousand additional copies of parts 1 and 2 of the hearings held by said committee during the Eighty-fourth Congress, first session, on the national highway program.


*Passed April 24, 1956.*

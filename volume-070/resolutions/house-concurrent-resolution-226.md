# House Concurrent Resolution 226

> adjournment

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Thursday, March 29, 1956, they stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Monday, April 9, 1956.


*Passed March 28, 1956.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 51

> “stock market study”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Banking and Currency one thousand additional copies of the hearings entitled “Stock Market Study”, held before the above committee during the Eighty-fourth Congress.


*Agreed to February 21, 1956.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 80

> lumber indians of north carolina

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the action of the Speaker pro tempore of the House of Representatives and of the President of the Senate in signing the enrolled bill (H. R. 4656) relating to the Lumbee Indians of North Carolina be, and it is hereby, rescinded, and that the engrossed bill be returned to the Senate.


*Agreed to May 24, 1956.*

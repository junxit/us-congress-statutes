# House Concurrent Resolution 245

> “the great pretense”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed fifty thousand additional copies of the report entitled “The Great Pretense” (House Report Numbered 2169, Eighty-fourth Congress, second session), for the use of the Committee on Un-American Activities.


*Passed July 12, 1956.*

# Private Law 535

> AN ACT For the relief of Edmund Lowe and Richard Lowe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Edmund Lowe and Richard Lowe shall be held and considered to be the minor children of their mother, Mrs. Sam Lee Jue, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved March 13, 1956.*

# Private Law 823

> AN ACT For the relief of Clarence Christensen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 15 to 20 of the Federal Employees’ Compensation Act are hereby waived in favor of Clarence Christensen, Saint Paul, Minnesota, and his claim for compensation for personal injuries alleged to have been sustained on February 28, 1947, while he was employed as a supply inspector by the Department of the Air Force at Fort Snelling, Minnesota, shall be acted upon under the remaining provisions of such Act in the same manner as if such claim had been timely filed, if such claim is filed within sixty days after the date of the enactment of this Act: Provided, That no benefits shall acrue by reason of the enactment of this Act for any period prior to its enactment, except in the case of such medical or hospitalization expenditures which may be deemed reimbursable.


*Approved July 28, 1956.*

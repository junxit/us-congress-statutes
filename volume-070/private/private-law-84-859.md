# Private Law 859

> AN ACT To ratify and confirm the sale of certain real property of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

The conveyance of the real property of the United States evidenced by the deed dated April 4, 1947, conveying certain real property of the United States to Steve Larsson Homer, filed for record in the office of the United States Commissoner and ex-officio recorder of Skagway, Alaska, in volume 13 of deeds, pages 264 to 265, inclusive, is hereby ratified and confirmed as of April 4, 1947.


*Approved August 3, 1956.*

# Private Law 743

> JOINT RESOLUTION For the relief of certain relatives of United States citizens.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 212 (a) (6) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the aliens hereinafter named may be issued visas and be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if they are found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act under such conditions and controls which the Attorney General, after consultation w ith the Surgeon General of the United States Public Health Service, Department of Health, Education, and Welfare, may deem necessary to impose : Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the said Act: Etsuko I.

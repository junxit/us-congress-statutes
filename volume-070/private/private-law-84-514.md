# Private Law 514

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Esther Moreno.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purpose of section 202 (a) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Esther Moreno shall be held to be a native of France.


*Approved February 15, 1956.*

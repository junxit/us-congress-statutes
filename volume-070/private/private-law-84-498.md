# Private Law 498

> AN ACT For the relief of Simone Gilliland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Simone Gilliland shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act: Provided. That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the said Act.


*Approved January 25, 1956.*

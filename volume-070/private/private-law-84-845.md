# Private Law 845

> AN ACT For the relief of Elma Agnes Gibson Hollingsworth.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Elma Agnes Gibson Hollingsworth, administratrix of the estate of Virgil Thomas Gibson (Veterans’ Administration claim numbered XC 539 583), is hereby relieved of all liability to repay to the United States the sum of $1,209.44, together with interest and costs, which she was held liable to repay the United States by the United States Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit on September 13, 1955. Such sum represents the amount overpaid to the said Virgil Thomas Gibson by the Veterans’ Administration through an administrative error.


*Approved August 2, 1956.*

# Private Law 711

> AN ACT For the relief of Rumiko Fujiki Kirkpatrick.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Rumiko Fujiki Kirkpatrick shall lie held and considered to lie a nonquota immigrant.


*Approved June 27, 1956.*

# Private Law 508

> AN ACT For the relief of Leong Ding Foon Quon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Leong Ding Foon Quon shall be held to be a returning resident alien as defined in section 101 (a) (27) (B) of that Act.


*Approved February 2, 1956.*

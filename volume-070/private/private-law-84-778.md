# Private Law 778

> AN ACT To provide for the sale by the Secretary of the Interior of certain public lands of the United States which have not been used for the purpose for which acquired.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior shall, as soon as practicable after the date of the enactment of this Act, sell approximately forty-seven acres of land, more Sarticularly described in section 2, which was acquired by the United tates from Elvie Briggs as necessary for the Folsom Dam and Reservoir project and which is not being used for project purposes. Elvie Briggs shall have the right of first preference to purchase such real property at the full current appraised value as determined by the Secretary of the Army.

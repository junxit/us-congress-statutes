# Private Law 795

> AN ACT For the relief of Elizabeth M. A. de Cuevas Faure.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provision of paragraph (1) of subsection (a) of section 352 of the Immigration and Nationality Act shall not be held to be applicable to Elizabeth M. A. de Cuevas Faure: Provided, That she return to the United States for permanent residence within two years following the effective date of this Act.


*Approved July 24, 1956.*

# Private Law 850

> AN ACT To authorize certain members and former members of the Armed Forces of the United States to accept and wear decorations, and supporting documents conferred upon them by the Government of the Philippines.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

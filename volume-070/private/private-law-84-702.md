# Private Law 702

> AN ACT For the relief of Santiago Gonzalez Trigo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Attorney General is authorized and directed to discontinue any deportation proceeding and to cancel any outstanding order and warrant of deportation, any warrant of arrest and bond which may have been issued in the case of Santiago Gonzalez Trigo, and the said Santiago Gonzalez Trigo shall not again be subject to deportation by reason of the same facts upon which any such deportation proceedings were commenced or any such warrants of arrest have issued.


*Approved June 22, 1956.*

# Private Law 852

> AN ACT For the relief of Hal A. Marchant.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 27 of the Merchant Marine Act, 1920, as amended, the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to cause the gas screw vessel Jenny, official number 256330, now owned by Hal A. Marchant of Coulee Dam, Washington, to be documented as a vessel of the United States with full coastwise privileges, upon compliance with the usual requirements for documentation, so long as the vessel is owned, and shall continue to be owned, by a citizen of the United States and the said vessel Jenny shall be deemed to be entitled to engage in such trade so long as it is so documented.


*Approved August 3, 1956.*

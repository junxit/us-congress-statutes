# Private Law 749

> AN ACT For the relief of Maria del Carmen Gago Santana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Maria del Carmen Gago Santana shall be deemed to have been born in Cuba.


*Approved July 9, 1956.*

# Private Law 552

> AN ACT For the relief of Kevin Murphy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 202 of the Immigration and Nationality Act. Kevin Murphy shall be held to have been born in England.


*Approved March 29, 1956.*

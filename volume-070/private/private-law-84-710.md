# Private Law 710

> AN ACT For the relief of W. R. Zanes and Company of Louisiana, Incorporated.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the W. R. Zanes and Company of Louisiana, Incorporated, is hereby relieved of liability to pay to the United States the amount of $146,907.45 erroneously levied as increased customs duty on account of merchandise imported by them, because of a clerical error in the entry of such merchandise (New Orleans consumption entry numbered 3137 of January 29, 1952), such error being perpetuated by an appraisement based thereon which became conclusive with respect to all parties upon the lapse of thirty days after the date of such appraisement.


*Approved June 27, 1956.*

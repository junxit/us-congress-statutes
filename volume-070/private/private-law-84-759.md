# Private Law 759

> JOINT RESOLUTION For the relief of certain aliens.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Ida Kaganowicz, Wonona Wong Chang (or Yit Chen Wong), Giuseppe Rosario DiStefano, Albert Rossi, Mrs. Kama Asato, Mrs. Tomeko Kishi, Fusa Kimura, and Lau Geu shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fees. Upon the granting of permanent residence to each alien as provided for in this Act, if such alien was classifiable as a quota immigrant at the time of the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to reduce by one the quota for the quota area to which the alien is chargeable for the first year that such quota is available.

## § 2.

For the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Antonio Doncovio shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fee.

## § 3.

Within six months immediately following the effective date of this Act, Sirijo Tanfara may file an application for adjustment of his immigration status under the provisions of section 6 of the Refugee Relief Act of 1953, as amended (67 Stat. 403; 68 Stat. 1045), notwithstanding his status at the time of entry into the United States.


*Approved July 11, 1956.*

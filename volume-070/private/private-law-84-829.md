# Private Law 829

> AN ACT For the relief of Patricia May Stevens.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Patricia May Stevens shall be deemed to have been born in Great Britain.


*Approved July 30, 1956.*

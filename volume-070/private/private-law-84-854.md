# Private Law 854

> AN ACT To authorize the appointment and retirement of Darrell C. Williams as a lieutenant commander, United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is authorized to advance Darrell C. Williams, now a lieutenant, United States Navy, retired, to the rank of lieutenant commander, United States Navy, on the retired list of the United States Navy, with the retired pay and allowances of that grade; but no back pay or allowances shall be held to have accrued by reason of this Act, prior to its enactment.


*Approved August 3, 1956.*

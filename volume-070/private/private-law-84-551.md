# Private Law 551

> AN ACT For the relief of Ane Karlic Vlasich.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Ane Karlic Vlasich, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Charles Vlasich, citizens of the United States.


*Approved March 29, 1956.*

# Private Law 647

> AN ACT For the relief of Lieutenant Colonel George H. Cronin, United States Air Force.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Lieutenant Colonel George H. Cronin (Air Force service number AO–132251) is hereby relieved of all liability to refund to the United States any sums representing certain overpayments for longevity and flying pay made to him during the period beginning August 1, 1942, and ending June 1, 1949. In the audit and settlement of the accounts of any certifying or disbursing officer of the United States, full credit shall be given for any amount for which liability is relieved by this Act.


*Approved May 18, 1956.*

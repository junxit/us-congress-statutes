# Private Law 595

> AN ACT For the relief of Alice Duckett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Alice Duckett shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date, of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved April 10, 1956.*

# Private Law 558

> AN ACT For the relief of Jean Henri Buchet.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act. Jean Henri Buchet shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved March 29, 1956.*

# Private Law 683

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Ida Bifolchini Bosehetti.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Mrs. Ida Bifolchini Bosehetti. Adams, Massachusetts, the sum of $2,149.66. The payment of such sum shall be in full settlement of all claims of the said Mrs. Ida Bifolchini Bosehetti against the United States for death compensation payable to her as the widow of Marino D. Bifolchini (XC–3860207), a veteran of World War II, for the period beginning January 12, 1945, and ending April 14, 1948: Provided, That no part of the amount appropriated in this Act shall be paid or delivered to or received by any agent or attorney on account of services rendered in connection with this claim, and the same shall be unlawful, any contract to the contrary notwithstanding. Any person violating the provisions of this Act shall be deemed guilty of a misdemeanor and upon conviction thereof shall be fined in any sum not exceeding $1,000.


*Approved June 4, 1956.*

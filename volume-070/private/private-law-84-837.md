# Private Law 837

> AN ACT For the relief of Egbert Carlsson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of the Act entitled “An Act providing for the barring of claims against the United States”, approved October 9, 1940 (54 Stat. 1061), the Comptroller General of the United States is authorized and directed to accept and consider any claim filed within one year from the date of enactment of this Act by Egbert Carlsson, of McPherson, Kansas, for compensation for the use by the Department of the Navy, from about November 1, 1943, through March 31, 1945, of certain lands in Lake County, Oregon, as part of an aerial gunnery range.


*Approved August 1, 1956.*

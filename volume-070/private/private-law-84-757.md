# Private Law 757

> AN ACT For the relief of Maria Novak.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (b) (2) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the alien Maria Novak shall be held and considered to be the alien parent of Janvid John Staut, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved July 11, 1956.*

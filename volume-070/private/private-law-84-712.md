# Private Law 712

> AN ACT For the relief of Inna Hekker Grade.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Inna Hekker Grade shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence on September 14, 1948.


*Approved June 27, 1956.*

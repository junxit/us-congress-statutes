# Private Law 878

> AN ACT For the relief of Miroslav Slovak.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Federal Communications Commission is authorized to issue a license to Miroslav Slovak as an operator under subsection (L) of section 303 of the Communications Act of 1934 (48 Stat. 1082), notwithstanding the requirement of such subsection with respect to citizenship.


*Approved August 6, 1956.*

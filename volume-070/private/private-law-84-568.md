# Private Law 568

> AN ACT For the relief of Zabel Vartanian.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Zabel Vartanian shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for lawful residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fee. Upon granting of permanent residence to such alien, as provided in this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the appropriate quota for the first year such quota is available.


*Approved March 29, 1956.*

# Private Law 744

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Hose Amoresano and her children.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, Mrs. Rose Amoresano and her children, Rosa, Emilio, and Giovanna, shall be deemed to be eligible to apply for nonquota immigrant visas under section 4 (a) (6) of the Refugee Relief Act of 1953 (67 Stat. 401).


*Approved July 9, 1956.*

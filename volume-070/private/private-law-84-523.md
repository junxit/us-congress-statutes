# Private Law 523

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Donald A. Howard (nee Miss Elsa Ursula Kuchinke).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 212 (a) (9) and (12) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Donald A. Howard (nee Miss Elsa Ursula Kuchinke) may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act: Provided, That these exemptions shall apply only to grounds for exclusion of which the Department of State or the Department of Justice had knowledge prior to enactment of this Act.


*Approved February 15, 1956.*

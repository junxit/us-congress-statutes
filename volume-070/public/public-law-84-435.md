# Public Law 435

> AN ACT Authorizing a preliminary examination and survey of McGirts Creek, Florida, for flood control.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Army, acting through the Chief of Engineers, is hereby authorized and directed to make a preliminary examination and survey with a view to determining the advisability of making improvements for flood control in McGirts Creek, a tributary of Saint Johns River, in Florida.


*Approved March 24, 1956.*

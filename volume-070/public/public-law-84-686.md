# Public Law 686

> AN ACT To amend section 1 of the Act of August 9, 1955 (69 Stat. 555), authorizing the sale of certain land by the Pueblos of San Lorenzo and Pojoaque.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1 of the Act of August 9, 1955 (69 Stat. 555), is amended by inserting after “townships 6, 7, and 8 north”, the following: “range 14 west, townships 7 and 8 north,”.


*Approved July 11, 1956.*

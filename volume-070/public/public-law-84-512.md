# Public Law 512

> AN ACT To authorize renewals of a lease of the Annette Island Airport to the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Congress of the United States hereby approves the extension, from year to year, until June 30, 1999, of a lease of certain land comprising part of Annette Island, Alaska, for use by the Civil Aeronautics Administration as an airport, entered into by the United States of America and the Council of the Annette Island Reserve on December 13, 1948, section 5 of which lease provides that no renewal thereof shall extend beyond June 30, 1939, unless approved by Congress.


*Approved May 9, 1956.*

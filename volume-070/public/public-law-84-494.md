# Public Law 494

> AN ACT To designate the reservoir above the Monticello Dam in California as Lake Berryessa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the reservoir located above the Monticello Dam in Napa County, California, shall hereafter be known as Lake Berryessa, and any law, regulation, document, or record of the United States in which such reservoir is designated or referred to shall be held to refer to such reservoir under and by the name of Lake Berryessa.


*Approved April 27, 1956.*

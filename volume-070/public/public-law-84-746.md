# Public Law 746

> AN ACT To extend the provisions of title XIII of the Civil Aeronautics Act of 1938, as amended, relating to war risk insurance for an additional five years.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1312 of title XIII of the Civil Aeronautics Act of 1938, as amended (Act of June 23, 1938, ch. 601, 52 Stat. 977, as amended by the Act of June 14, 1951, ch. 123, 65 Stat. 69, 49 U. S. C. 722), is amended by striking out the word “five” and inserting in lieu thereof the word “ten”.


*Approved July 20, 1956.*

# Public Law 749

> AN ACT To continue the effectiveness of the Missing Persons Act, as extended, until July 1, 1957.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 15, Missing Persons Act (56 Stat. 147, 1093), as amended, is further amended by deleting “July 1, 1956" and inserting in lieu thereof “July 1, 1957”.


*Approved July 20, 1956.*

# Public Law 773

> AN ACT To amend the Act entitled “An Act to provide recognition for meritorious service by members of the police and fire departments of the District of Columbia”, approved March 4, 1929.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first section of the Act entitled “An Act to provide recognition for meritorious service by members of the police and fire departments of the District of Columbia, approved March 4, 1929, is amended to read as follows:

> “That for the official recognition of outstanding acts in the line of duty by the members of the police and fire departments of the District of Columbia there shall be awarded annually one gold medal and one or more silver medals, appropriately inscribed, to those members of each department who have by outstanding or conspicuous services earned such awards.”


*Approved July 24, 1956.*

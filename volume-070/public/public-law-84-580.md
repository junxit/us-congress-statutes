# Public Law 580

> AN ACT To amend the Act of June 19, 1948 (ch. 511, 62 Stat. 489), relating to the retention in the service of disabled commissioned officers and warrant officers of the Army and Air Force.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of June 19, 1948 (ch. 511, 62 Stat. 489), is hereby amended to read as follows:

> “That notwithstanding any other law, the President may retain on active duty disabled commissioned officers and warrant officers of the Army and Air Force until their physical reconstruction has reached a point where they will not be further benefited by retention in a military or Veterans’ Administration hospital, or until processed for physical disability benefits otherwise provided by law.”


*Approved June 15, 1956.*

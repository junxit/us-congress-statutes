# Public Law 1003

> JOINT RESOLUTION To extend the operation of the Emergency Ship Repair Act of 1954.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4 of the Emergency Ship Repair Act of 1954 (Public Law 608, Eighty-third Congress) is amended by striking out the words “within twenty-four months after date of enactment of this Act”, and inserting in place thereof “before July 1, 1958”.


*Approved August 6, 1956.*

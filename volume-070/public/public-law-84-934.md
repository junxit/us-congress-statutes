# Public Law 934

> AN ACT To provide additional time for the Tariff Commission to review the customs tariff schedules.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 101 (c) of the Customs Simplification Act of 1954 (Public Law 768, Eighty-third Congress) is amended by striking out “two years after the enactment of this Act” and inserting in lieu thereof “March 1, 1958,”.


*Approved August 2, 1956.*

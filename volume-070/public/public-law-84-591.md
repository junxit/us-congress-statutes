# Public Law 591

> AN ACT To amend the Employment Act of 1946, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3 (a) of the Employment Act of 1946, as amended (relating to the time for filing: the economic report of the President), is amended by striking out “at the beginning of each regular session (commencing with the year 1947)” and inserting in lieu thereof “not later than January 20 of each year”.

## § 2.

Section 5 (a) of such Act and the heading thereof are each amended by striking out “Joint Committee on the Economic Report” and inserting in lieu thereof “Joint Economic Committee”; and any other statute in which the name “Joint Committee on the Economic Report” appears is amended to conform to the foregoing change in the name of the Joint Committee.


*Approved June 18, 1956.*

# Public Law 724

> AN ACT To suspend for two years the duty on crude bauxite and on calcined bauxite.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That effective with respect to articles entered, or withdrawn from warehouse, for consumption during the two-year period beginning July 16, 1956, no duty shall be imposed upon bauxite, crude, not refined or otherwise advanced in condition in any manner, or upon calcined bauxite.


*Approved July 16, 1956.*

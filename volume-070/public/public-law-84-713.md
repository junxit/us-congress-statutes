# Public Law 713

> AN ACT To allow the use of certain property in Volusia County, Florida, for civil-defense purposes without payment of compensation to the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding any other provision of law, or any condition or restriction placed on the real property by the deed given by the United States to the Board of Public Instruction of Volusia County, Florida, on May 15, 1948, which can be found in book 386, page 27 of the public records of Volusia County, Florida, such real property may be used for civil-defense purposes by the Volusia County Civil Defense Control Center without the payment of compensation to the United States.


*Approved July 14, 1956.*

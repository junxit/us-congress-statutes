# Public Law 1012

> AN ACT To suspend for two years the import duties on certain lathes used for shoe last roughing or for shoe last finishing, and to permit substitution for drawback purposes in the case of printing papers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph 1643 of the Tariff Act of 1930 is amended by inserting immediately after “shoe machinery,” the following: “copying lathes used for making rough or finished shoe lasts from models of shoe lasts and, in addition, capable of producing more than one size shoe last from a single size model of a shoe last,”.

## § 2.

The amendment made by this Act shall apply only in the case of articles entered for consumption, or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after the date of enactment of this Act and prior to the expiration of two years after such date.

## § 313.

- **(b)** of the Tariff Act of 1930, as amended, is amended by inserting after “linseed oil,” wherever it appears the following:
  > “or printing papers, coated or uncoated,”.


*Approved August 6, 1956.*

# Public Law 866

> AN ACT To amend the Act of July 15, 1954, authorizing the sale of certain vessels to Brazil.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of Congress entitled “To authorize the sale of certain vessels to Brazil for use in the coastwise trade of Brazil”, approved July 15, 1954 (68 Stat. 481), is amended by inserting before the period at the end thereof a comma and the following: “except that, upon recommendation of the Maritime Administrator, and with the approval of the Secretary of Commerce, any such vessel may carry bulk grain and coal cargo while en route from the United States directly to Brazil after purchase hereunder for use thereafter only in the coastwise trade of Brazil”.


*Approved August 1, 1956.*

# Public Law 595

> AN ACT Authorizing the Administrator of General Services to convey certain property of the United States to the city of Roseburg, Oregon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subject to such reservations and restrictions as may be necessary to protect the interests of the United States, the Administrator of General Services is authorized and directed to quitclaim to the city of Roseburg, Oregon, all of the right, title, and interest of the United States in and to a tract of land containing one hundred and sixty-three acres, more or less, situated in the Veterans’ Administration hospital reservation in that city, the exact legal description of which shall be determined by the Administrator of General Services.


*Approved June 18, 1956.*

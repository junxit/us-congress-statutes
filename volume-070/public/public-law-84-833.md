# Public Law 833

> JOINT RESOLUTION Authorizing an appropriation for expenses of the Pan American games to be held in Cleveland, Ohio, in 1959.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated out of moneys in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated the sum of $5,000,000 for III Pan American Games (1959). The said appropriation shall be available for the purpose of promoting and insuring the success of the Pan American games to be held in Cleveland, Ohio, in 1959 and shall be expended in the discretion of the organization sponsoring said games, subject to such audit as may be prescribed by the Comptroller General of the United States.


*Approved July 30, 1956.*

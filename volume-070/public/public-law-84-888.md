# Public Law 888

> AN ACT To increase the minimum postal savings deposit, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 6 of the Act of June 25, 1910, as amended (39 U. S. C. 756), is hereby further amended by striking out “$1” wherever it appears therein, and by insetting in lieu thereof “$5”.


*Approved August 1, 1956.*

# Public Law 411

> AN ACT To extend through June 30, 1957, the duration of the Poliomyelitis Vaccination Assistance Act of 1955.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2, and subsections (a) and (b) of section 6, of the Poliomyelitis Vaccination Assistance Act of 1955 (Public Law 377, Eighty-fourth Congress) are each amended by striking out “February 15, 1956” and inserting in lieu thereof “the close of June 30, 1957”.


*Approved February 15, 1956.*

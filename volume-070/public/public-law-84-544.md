# Public Law 544

> AN ACT To amend title 18 of the United States Code, so as to provide for the punishment of persons who assist in the attempted escape of persons in Federal custody.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 752 of title 18, United States Code, is amended by inserting after the word “escape” in line 2 thereof the phrase “, or attempt to escape,”.


*Approved May 28, 1956.*

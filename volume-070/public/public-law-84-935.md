# Public Law 935

> AN ACT To amend the Federal Employees’ Group Life Insurance Act of 1954 to bring employees of Gallaudet College within its coverage.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2 (a) of the Federal Employees’ Group Life Insurance Act of 1954 is amended by striking out “(but not including” and inserting in lieu thereof “(including Gallaudet College but not including”.

## § 2.

This Act shall become effective on the first day of the first pay period which begins at least ten days after date of approval.


*Approved August 2, 1956.*

# Public Law 755

> AN ACT To exclude certain lands from Acadia National Park, Maine, and to authorize their disposal as surplus Federal property.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the tract of land in Acadia National Park, State of Maine, comprising approximately three hundred acres and identified as the “Green Lake Fish Hatchery Tract” is hereby excluded from Acadia National Park, and the said tract is authorized to be disposed of in accordance with the laws relating to the disposition of Federal property.


*Approved July 24, 1956.*

# Public Law 620

> AN ACT To amend the Career Compensation Act of 1949, as amended, in relation to the refund of reenlistment bonuses.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the fourth proviso of section 207 (a) of the Career Compensation Act of 1949, as amended (ch. 580, 65 Stat. 654; 37 U. S. C. 238 (a)), is further amended by deleting the phrase “, less any amount paid in Federal or State income taxes on such refundable part”.


*Approved June 25, 1956.*

# Public Law 457

> AN ACT To designate the lake created by Buford Dam in the State of Georgia as “Lake Sidney Lanier”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the lake created by the Buford Dam, now being constructed on the Chattahoochee River about thirty-five miles northeast of Atlanta, Georgia, shall be known and designated as Lake Sidney Lanier in honor of the late Sidney Lanier, author of the poem “Song of the Chattahoochee”. Any law, regulation, document, or record of the United States in which such lake is referred to under any other name or designation, shall be held to refer to such lake as Lake Sidney Lanier.


*Approved March 29, 1956.*

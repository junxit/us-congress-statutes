# Public Law 1019

> AN ACT To authorize Canadian vessels to be employed in the coastwise transportation of coal to Ogdensburg, New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That until June 30, 1957, notwithstanding the provisions of law of the United States restricting to vessels of the United States the transportation of merchandise, directly or indirectly, from any port in the United States to another port of the United States, Canadian vessels may transport coal to Ogdensburg, New York, from other points in the United States, on the Great Lakes, or their connecting or tributary waters.


*Approved August 7, 1956.*

# Public Law 670

> AN ACT To change the distribution of Coast and Geodetic Survey charts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 246 of title 44 of the United States Code be amended by striking from the last clause thereof the word “ten” and inserting in place thereof the words “one hundred”.


*Approved July 9, 1956.*

# Public Law 708

> AN ACT To amend the Act relating to cemetery associations.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, without regard to the provisions of section 27–114 of the District of Columbia Code (Act of March 3, 1901, 31 Stat. 1295, ch. 854, sec. 670), the Commissioners of the District of Columbia are hereby authorized to license for cemetery purposes any parcel of land in the District of Columbia which does not exceed one acre in size, and which, except for a one-side frontage of less than 100 feet on a public street or highway, is otherwise completely bounded by land dedicated to cemetery purposes.


*Approved July 14, 1956.*

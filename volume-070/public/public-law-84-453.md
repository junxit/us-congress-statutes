# Public Law 453

> AN ACT To provide for the relocation of the Trenton Massacre Canyon Monument presently located near Trenton, Nebraska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior shall relocate the Trenton Massacre Canyon Monument near Trenton, Nebraska, to a site near United States Highway Numbered 34 to be selected by the Chamber of Commerce of Trenton, Nebraska.

## § 2.

This Act shall not be construed to authorize the Secretary of the Interior to acquire real property.


*Approved March 29, 1956.*

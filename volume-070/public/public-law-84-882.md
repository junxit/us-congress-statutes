# Public Law 882

> JOINT RESOLUTION To authorize an additional position of Assistant Director in the Bureau of the Budget.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby established one additional position of Assistant Director in the Bureau of the Budget, for which the compensation shall be the same as is now or may hereafter be provided for the two such positions heretofore authorized by law (31 U. S. C. 16a).


*Approved August 1, 1956.*

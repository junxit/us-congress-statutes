# Public Law 712

> AN ACT To relieve the Secretary of the Interior of certain reporting requirements in connection with proposed National Park Service awards of concession leases and contracts, including renewals thereof.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second paragraph under the heading “National Park Service” in the Act of July 31, 1953 (67 Stat. 261, 271) is amended to read as follows: “The Secretary of the Interior shall hereafter report in detail all proposed awards of concession leases and contracts involving a gross annual business of $100,000 or more, or of more than five years in duration, including renewals thereof, sixty days before such awards are made, to the President of the Senate and Speaker of the House of Representatives for transmission to the appropriate committees.”


*Approved July 14, 1956.*

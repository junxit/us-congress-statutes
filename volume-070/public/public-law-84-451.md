# Public Law 451

> AN ACT To repeal the requirement for heads of departments and agencies to report to the Postmaster General the number of penalty envelopes and wrappers on hand at the close of each fiscal year.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 301 of the Penalty Mail Act of 1948, as amended (62 Stat. 1048; 39 U. S. C., sec. 321i), is hereby amended by striking out the second sentence thereof.


*Approved March 29, 1956.*

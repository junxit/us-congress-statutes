# Public Law 517

> AN ACT To terminate the prohibition against employment of Mongolian labor in the construction of reclamation projects.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4 of the Reclamation Act of June 17, 1902 (32 Stat. 389), is hereby amended by inserting a period in lieu of the comma after the word “work” in the proviso of that section and striking out the following language: “and no Mongolian labor shall be employed thereon.”.


*Approved May 10, 1956.*

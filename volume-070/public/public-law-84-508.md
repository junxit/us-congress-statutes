# Public Law 508

> AN ACT To provide for longer terms of office for the justices of the Supreme Court of Hawaii and the circuit courts of Hawaii.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first para-graph of section 80 of the Hawaiian Organic Act, as amended (48 U. S. C., sec. 633), is amended effective at noon, January 20, 1957, by striking out “That the President shall nominate and, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, appoint the chief justice and justices of the supreme court, the judges of the circuit courts, who shall hold their respective offices for the term of four years, unless sooner removed by the President” and inserting in lieu thereof the following: “The President shall nominate and, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, appoint the chief justice and justices of the supreme court, who shall hold office for the term of seven years unless sooner removed by the President, and the judges of the circuit courts, who shall hold office for the term of six years, unless sooner removed by the President”.

## § 2.

The amendment made by this Act shall apply notwithstanding the provisions of section 1864 of the Revised Statutes of the United States (48 U. S.C., sec. 1463a).


*Approved May 9, 1956.*

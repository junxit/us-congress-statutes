# Public Law 404

> AN ACT Authorizing the completion of the initial stage of development for flood control and other purposes in the Russian River Basin, California.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in addition to the authorization of $11,522,000 for accomplishment of the initial stage of the plan for flood control and other purposes in the Russian River Basin, California, authorized by the Flood Control Act of 1950, there is hereby authorized the completion of the initial stage at an estimated additional cost of $1,165,000.

## § 2.

There are hereby authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act.


*Approved February 10, 1956.*

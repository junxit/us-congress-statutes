# Public Law 990

> AN ACT To provide for the establishment of a fish hatchery in the State of West Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is authorized to establish, construct, equip, operate, and maintain a new fish hatchery in the State of West Virginia.

## § 2.

There are hereby authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out the purposes of this Act.


*Approved August 6, 1956.*

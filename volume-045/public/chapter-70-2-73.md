# Chapter 73

> An Act For the appointment of an additional circuit judge for the second judicial circuit.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized to appoint, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, an additional circuit judge for the second judicial circuit.


*Approved, January 17, 1929.*

# Chapter 625

> An Act To extend medical and hospital relief to retired officers and retired enlisted men of the United States Coast Guard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That hereafter retired officers and retired enlisted men of the United States Coast Guard shall be entitled to medical treatment at Marine hospitals and out-patient offices of the Public Health Service.


*Approved, May 18, 1928.*

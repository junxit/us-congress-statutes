# Chapter 240

> Joint Resolution to change the name of the Ancon Hospital in the Panama Canal Zone to the Gorgas Hospital.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in recognition of his distinguished services to humanity and as a fitting perpetuation of the name and memory of Major General William Crawford Gorgas, from and after the passage of this Act, the Government hospital within the Canal Zone, near the city of Panama, heretofore known as the Ancon Hospital, shall hereafter be known and designated on the public records as the Gorgas Hospital.

## § 2.

That the change in the name of the said hospital shall in no wise affect the rights of the Federal Government, or any municipality, corporation, association, or person; and all records, maps, and public documents of the United States in which said hospital is mentioned or referred to under the name of the Ancon Hospital or otherwise, shall be held to refer to the said hospital under and by the name of the Gorgas Hospital.


*Approved, March 24, 1928.*

# Chapter 873

> An Act To authorize an appropriation for the purchase of certain privately owned lands within the Fort Apache Indian Reservation, Arizona.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized an appropriation of $6,200, or as much thereof as might be required, from the tribal fund “Indian moneys proceeds of labor” on deposit in the Treasury of the United States to the credit of the Indians of the Fort Apache Reservation, Arizona, for the purchase of the land and appurtenances thereof located within the exterior boundaries of that reservation and belonging to the Aztec Land and Cattle Company, title thereto to be taken in the name of the United States in trust for said Indians.


*Approved, May 29, 1928.*

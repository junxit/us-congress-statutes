# Chapter 264

> An Act To authorize appropriations for construction at military posts, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated, not to exceed $80,000, to be expended for the construction, installation, and completion at Fort Humphreys, Virginia, of barracks and utilities and appurtenances thereto.


*Approved, March 28, 1928.*

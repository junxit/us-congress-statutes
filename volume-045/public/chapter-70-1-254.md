# Chapter 254

> An Act To authorize appropriations for construction at military posts, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated not to exceed $65,000, to be expended for the construction and installation at Schofield Barracks, Hawaii, of nurses’ quarters and utilities and appurtenances thereto.


*Approved, March 27, 1928.*

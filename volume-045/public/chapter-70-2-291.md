# Chapter 291

> An Act To amend the Trading with the Enemy Act so as to extend the time within which claims may be filed with the Alien Property Custodian.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (d) of section 25 of the Trading with the Enemy Act, as amended, is hereby amended by striking out the term “one year” in clause (1) of said subsection and inserting in lieu thereof the term “two years.”


*Approved, February 21, 1929.*

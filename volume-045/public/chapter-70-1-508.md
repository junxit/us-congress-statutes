# Chapter 508

> An Act For the appointment of an additional circuit judge for the sixth judicial circuit.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That hereafter there shall be in the sixth circuit four circuit judges, to be appointed and to have the powers, salary, and duties prescribed in section 118 of the Judicial Code, as amended.


*Approved, May 8, 1928.*

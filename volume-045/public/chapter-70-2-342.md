# Chapter 342

> An Act To extend the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Potomac River at or near Dahlgren, Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Potomac River from a point in the vicinity of Dahlgren, in the northeastern end of King George County, Virginia, to a point south of Popes Creek, Charles County, Maryland, authorized to be built by the George Washington-Wakefield Memorial Bridge, a corporation, its successors and assigns, by an Act of Congress approved May 5, 1926, heretofore extended by an Act of Congress approved February 16, 1928, are hereby further extended one and three years, respectively, from February 16, 1929.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, February 26, 1929.*

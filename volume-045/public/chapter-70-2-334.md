# Chapter 334

> An Act To provide for the appointment of three additional judges of the District Court of the United States for the Southern District of New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized to appoint, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, three additional judges of the District Court of the United States for the Southern District of New York, who shall reside in said district and who shall possess the same powers, perform the same duties, and receive the same compensation as the present district judges of said district.


*Approved, February 26, 1929.*

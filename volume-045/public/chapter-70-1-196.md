# Chapter 196

> An Act To amend an Act entitled “An Act for the relief of Indians occupying railroad lands in Arizona, New Mexico, or California,” approved March 4, 1913.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all of the provisions of an Act entitled “An Act for the relief of Indians occupying railroad lands in Arizona, New Mexico, or California,” approved March 4, 1913, and amended by the Act of April 11, 1916, and the Act of June 30, 1919, be, and the same are hereby, extended to March 4, 1931: Provided, That the provisions of this Act shall apply only in cases where it is shown that the lands were actually occupied in good faith by Indians prior to March 4, 1913, and the applicants are otherwise entitled to receive such tracts, in allotment under existing law but for the grant to the railroad company.


*Approved, March 10, 1928.*

# Chapter 306

> Joint Resolution To amend subdivisions (b) and (e) of section 11 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subdivisions (b) and (e) of section 11 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, are amended by striking out the figures “1928” and inserting in lieu thereof the figures “1929.”


*Approved, March 31, 1928.*

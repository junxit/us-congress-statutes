# Chapter 98

> An Act To extend the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Mississippi River at or near Baton Rouge, Louisiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of the bridge across the Mississippi River at or near Baton Rouge, Louisiana, authorized to be built by the Baton Rouge-Mississippi River Bridge Company, its successors and assigns, by the Act of Congress approved February 20, 1928, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from February 20, 1929.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, January 25, 1929.*

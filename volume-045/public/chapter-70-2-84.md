# Chapter 84

> An Act To authorize an increase in the limit of cost of two fleet submarines.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limitation of $5,300,000 each imposed in the Naval Appropriation Act for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1926, on construction and machinery expenditures on account of two fleet submarines is hereby increased to $6,650,000 each.


*Approved, January 19, 1929.*

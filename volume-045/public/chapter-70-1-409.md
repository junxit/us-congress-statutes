# Chapter 409

> An Act To authorize appropriations for construction at the Pacific Branch, Soldiers’ Home, Los Angeles County, California, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated not to exceed $2,100,000 to be expended for the construction and installment at the Pacific Branch, Soldiers’ Home, Los Angeles County, California, of twelve barrack buildings and utilities and appurtenances thereto.


*Approved, April 23, 1928.*

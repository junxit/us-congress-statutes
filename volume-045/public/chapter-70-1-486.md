# Chapter 486

> An Act To authorize appropriations for the construction of roads at the Presidio of San Francisco, California.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $47,200 for the purpose of completing the military roads at the Presidio of San Francisco, California.


*Approved, May 3, 1928.*

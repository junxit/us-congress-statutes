# Chapter 274

> An Act To amend an Act entitled “An Act making appropriations for the naval service for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1922, and for other purposes,” approved July 12, 1921.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second proviso under the heading “Contingent, Bureau of Ordnance,” contained in the Naval Appropriation Act approved July 12, 1921 (Forty-second Statutes at Large, page 128), is hereby amended to read as follows:

> “Provided further, That hereafter no money appropriated for ordnance or ordnance material or material purchased therewith shall be used for any other purpose than that for which the appropriation was made, except that this provision shall not prohibit the transfer to other bureaus or departments of used or obsolescent material which is no longer needed for the purpose for which originally acquired.”


*Approved, February 20, 1929.*

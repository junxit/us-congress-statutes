# Chapter 134

> An Act To increase the salary of the Librarian of Congress.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Librarian of Congress on and after July 1, 1928, shall receive salary at the rate of $10,000 per annum.


*Approved, March 6, 1928.*

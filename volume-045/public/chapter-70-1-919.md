# Chapter 919

> Joint Resolution To provide for the striking of a medal commemorative of the achievements of Thomas A. Edison in illumining the path of progress through the development and application of inventions that have revolutionized civilization in the last century.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in recognition of the achievements of Thomas A. Edison, the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to cause to be struck and presented to Thomas A. Edison a gold medal with suitable emblems, devices, and inscriptions to be determined by the Secretary of the Treasury. For such puroose there is authorized to be appropriated the sum of $1,000.


*Approved, May 29, 1928.*

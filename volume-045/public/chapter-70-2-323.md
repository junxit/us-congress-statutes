# Chapter 323

> An Act To repeal that portion of the Act of August 24, 1912, imposing a limit on agency salaries of the Indian Service.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provision in the Act of August 24, 1912 (Thirty-seventh Statutes at Large, page 521; United States Code, title 25, page 692, section 58), imposing a limitation of $15,000 on the amount which may be paid for salaries at any one Indian agency and $20,000 at a consolidated agency, be, and the same is hereby, repealed.


*Approved, February 26, 1929.*

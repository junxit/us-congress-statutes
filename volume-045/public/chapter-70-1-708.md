# Chapter 708

> An Act To provide for the construction or purchase of one heavy seagoing Air Corps retriever for the War Department.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated not to exceed $40,000 to be expended by the Secretary of War for the construction or purchase of one heavy seagoing Air Corps retriever for use at France Field, Canal Zone.


*Approved, May 23, 1928.*

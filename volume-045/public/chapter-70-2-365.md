# Chapter 365

> An Act To amend the Act of Congress of June 26, 1906, entitled “An Act for the protection of the fisheries of Alaska, and for other purposes.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 7 of of the Act of June 26, 1906, entitled “An Act for the protection of the fisheries of Alaska, and for other purposes,” is amended so that it will read as follows:

> ## § 7.
>
> It shall be unlawful to preserve for sale as food for human consumption any salmon unless it shall have been canned, salted, iced, frozen, smoked, or dried within forty-eight hours after being killed.”

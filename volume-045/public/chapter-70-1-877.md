# Chapter 877

> An Act Repealing existing law requiring the Postmaster General to report action taken on claims of postmasters.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2 of the “Act to amend the Act approved May 9, 1888, as amended by the Act of June 11, 1896,” approved January 21, 1914, which requires the Postmaster General to make an annual report to Con-gress of his action on claims of postmasters for loss by burglary, fire, or other unavoidable casualty, is hereby repealed.


*Approved, May 29, 1928.*

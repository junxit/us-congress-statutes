# Chapter 341

> An Act To extend the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Allegheny County, Pennsylvania.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of the bridge across the Allegheny River at Oil City, county of Venango, State of Pennsylvania, authorized to be built by The Pennsylvania Railroad Company, by the Act of Congress approved February 16, 1928, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from February 16, 1929.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, February 26, 1929.*

# Chapter 2

> Joint Resolution For the appointment of W. S. Albright, of Kansas, as a member of the Board of Managers of the National Homes for Disabled Volunteer Soldiers.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That W. S. Albright, of Kansas, be, and he is hereby, appointed a member of the Board of Managers of the National Homes for Disabled Volunteer Soldiers of the United States, to succeed W. S. Albright, whose term expires January 6, 1929.


*Approved, December 6, 1928.*

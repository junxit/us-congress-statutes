# Chapter 709

> An Act To provide for the construction or purchase of two motor mine yawls for the War Department.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated not to exceed $10,000 to be expended by the Secretary of War for the construction or purchase of two motor mine yawls for replacement purposes, at a cost not to exceed $5,000 each.


*Approved, May 23, 1928.*

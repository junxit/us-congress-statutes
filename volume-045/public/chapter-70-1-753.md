# Chapter 753

> An Act To authorize a per capita payment to the Pine Ridge Sioux Indians of South Dakota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to withdraw from funds on deposit in the Treasury of the United States to the credit of the Pine Ridge Sioux Indians of South Dakota a sum sufficient to make a $10 per capita payment to said Indians, under such rules and regulations as he may prescribe.


*Approved, May 26, 1928.*

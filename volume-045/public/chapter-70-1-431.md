# Chapter 431

> An Act Authorizing acceptance from Peter G. Gerry of the gift of the law library of the late Elbridge T. Gerry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Chief Justice of the United States is authorized to accept on behalf of the United States, for the use of the Supreme Court, the gift of Peter G. Gerry, a Senator of the United States from the State of Rhode Island, of the law library bequeathed to him by his father, the late Elbridge T. Gerry.


*Approved, April 25, 1928.*

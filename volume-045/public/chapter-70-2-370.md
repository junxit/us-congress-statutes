# Chapter 370

> An Act To amend section 1, rule 3, subdivision (e), of an Act to regulate navigation on the Great Lakes and their connecting and tributary waters, enacted February 8, 1895, as amended May 17, 1928.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That rule 3, subdivision (e), of an Act entitled “An Act to regulate navigation on the Great Lakes and their connecting and tributary waters,” enacted February 8, 1895, and being chapter 64, Twenty-eighth Statutes at Large, section 645, as amended May 17, 1928 (U. S. C. title 33, section 252), be amended by substituting the letter “a” in parentheses for the second letter “e” in parentheses in the seventh line thereof.


*Approved, February 28, 1929.*

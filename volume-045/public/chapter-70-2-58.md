# Chapter 58

> An Act Providing for the purchase of one thousand one hundred and twenty-four acres of land, more or less, in the vicinity of Camp Bullis, Texas, and authorizing an appropriation therefor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a sum not to exceed $15,000 is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the purchase by voluntary conveyance, or by condemnation proceedings, of one thousand one hundred and twenty-four acres of land, more or less, in the vicinity of and for use in connection with the present military reservation at Camp Bullis, Texas, and the Secretary of War is hereby authorized to make such purchase.


*Approved, January 12, 1929.*

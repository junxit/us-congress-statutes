# Chapter 502

> An Act To amend the Longshoremen’s and Harbor Workers’ Compensation Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in any court proceedings under section 21 or other provisions of the Longshoremen’s and Harbor Workers’ Compensation Act, it shall be the duty of the district attorney of the United States in the judicial district in which the case is pending to appear as attorney or counsel on behalf of the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission or its deputy commissioner when either is a party to the case or interested, and to represent such commission or deputy in any court in which such case may be carried on appeal.


*Approved, May 4, 1928.*

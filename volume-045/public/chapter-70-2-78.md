# Chapter 78

> An Act To enable electricians, radio electricians, chief electricians, and chief radio electricians to be appointed to the grade of ensign.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That electricians, radio electricians, chief electricians, and chief radio electricians shall be eligible for appointment to the grade of ensign under the restrictions imposed by law upon the appointment of boatswains, gunners, machinists, chief boatswains, chief gunners, and chief machinists to that grade.


*Approved, January 19, 1929.*

# Chapter 199

> An Act To authorize the sale of certain lands near Garden City, Kansas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the State of Kansas be, and it is hereby, authorized to sell all or any part of the following described land granted to said State under the provisions of the Act of Congress approved June 22, 1916, to wit: Sections 25, 26, and 35 in township 24 south, and sections 1 and 2 in township 25 south, all in range 33 west of the sixth principal meridian, notwithstanding the restrictions contained in said Act: Provided, That the proceeds of said sale shall be used to purchase land in sections 23 and 24 in township 24, range 33, and in sections 19 and 30 in township 24, range 32, all in Finney County, Kansas, to be used as a State game preserve.


*Approved, March 10, 1928.*

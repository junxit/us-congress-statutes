# Chapter 710

> An Act To provide for the construction or purchase of two L boats for the War Department.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated not to exceed $50,000 to be expended by the Secretary of War for the construction or purchase of two L boats, for replacing boats of a similar type destroyed, at a cost not to exceed $25,000 each.


*Approved, May 23, 1928.*

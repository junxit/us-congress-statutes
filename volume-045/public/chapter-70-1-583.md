# Chapter 583

> An Act Authorizing an appropriation for the repair and resurfacing of roads on the Fort Baker Military Reservation, California.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $35,000 for the repair and resurfacing of the roads on the Fort Baker Military Reservation, California.


*Approved, May 16, 1928.*

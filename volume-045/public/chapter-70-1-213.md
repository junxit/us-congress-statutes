# Chapter 213

> An Act Relative to the pay of certain retired warrant officers and enlisted men and warrant officers and enlisted men of the reserve forces of the Army, Navy, Marine Corps, and the Coast Guard, fixed under the terms of the Panama Canal Act, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4 of the Panama Canal Act, as amended, shall not be construed as requiring the deduction of the retired pay or allowances of any retired warrant officer or enlisted man of the Army, Navy, Marine Corps, or Coast Guard, or the training pay, retainer pay, or allowances of any warrant officer or enlisted man of the reserve forces of the Army, Navy, Marine Corps, or Coast Guard, from the amount of the salary or compensation provided by or fixed under the terms of the Panama Canal Act, as amended.


*Approved, March 12, 1928.*

# Chapter 198

> An Act Vacating the alley between lots 16 and 17, square 1083, District of Columbia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioners of the District of Columbia be, and they are hereby, authorized to close, vacate, and abandon so much of the fifteen-foot public alley in square 1083 as lies between lots 16 and 17; same to revert in equal proportion to the abutting lots.


*Approved, February 14, 1929.*

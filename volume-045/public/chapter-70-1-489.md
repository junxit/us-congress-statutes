# Chapter 489

> Joint Resolution To provide for the membership of the United States in the American International Institute for the Protection of Childhood.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That to enable the United States to become a member of the American International Institute for the Protection of Childhood at Montevideo, Uruguay, there is hereby authorized to be appropriated the sum of $2,000 per annum for the contribution by the United States toward the support of the institution.


*Approved, May 3, 1928.*

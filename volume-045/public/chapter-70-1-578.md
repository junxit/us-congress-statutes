# Chapter 578

> An Act To authorize the purchase of real estate by the War Department.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War, under such conditions as he shall determine, is author- ized to acquire by purchase or condemnation real estate at Opaeula Gulch and Gilbert, Hawaii, for the purpose of providing four sites for firing installations for heavy artillery, and there is hereby authorized to be appropriated for this purpose a sum not exceeding $10,125.


*Approved, May 16, 1928.*

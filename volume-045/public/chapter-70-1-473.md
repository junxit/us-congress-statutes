# Chapter 473

> An Act To provide for the conservation of fish, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Department of Commerce be, and it is hereby, authorized to study, investigate and determine the best means and methods of preventing the destruction of fish occasioned by ditches, canals, and other works constructed or maintained by the United States; and for this purpose such sums of money as may be necessary, not exceeding in the aggregate $25,000 are hereby authorized to be expended out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, May 1, 1928.*

# Chapter 511

> An Act Authorizing an appropriation of Crow tribal funds for payment of council and delegate expenses, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated, from funds to the credit of the Crow Tribe of Indians, the sum of $5,000, to be available for the payment of the expenses of the tribal council in holding meetings on the Crow Reservation, and for the expenses of delegates which such council may send to the city of Washington on tribal business when authorized by the Secretary of the Interior or the Commissioner of Indian Affairs and under such rules and regulations as may be prescribed by the Secretary of the Interior.


*Approved, March 2, 1929.*

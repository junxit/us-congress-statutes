# Chapter 579

> An Act To fix the pay and allowances of chaplain at the United States Military Academy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That so much of the Act of February 18, 1896, as provides that the chaplain at the United States Military Academy shall, while so serving, receive the same pay and allowances as a captain, mounted, is hereby amended to read as follows: “The chaplain at the United States Military Academy shall, while so serving, receive a salary of $4,000 per annum and be entitled to the same allowances with respect to public quarters, fuel, and light as those allowed to a captain.”


*Approved, May 16, 1928.*

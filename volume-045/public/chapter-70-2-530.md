# Chapter 530

> An Act Extending the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Mississippi River at or near Arkansas City, Arkansas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Mississippi River at or near Arkansas City, Arkansas, authorized to be built by Henry Thane, his heirs, legal representatives, and assigns, by the Act of Congress approved March 29, 1928, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from March 29, 1929.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, March 2, 1929.*

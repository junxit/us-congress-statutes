# Chapter 210

> An Act Authorizing the Secretary of the Navy, in his discretion, to deliver to the custody of Naval Post 110 of the American Legion the bell of the battleship Connecticut.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy is authorized, in his discretion, to deliver to the custody of Naval Post 110 of the American Legion, for preservation and exhibition the bell which was in use on the battleship Connecticut: Provided, That no expenses shall be incurred by the United States for the delivery of such bell.


*Approved, February 15, 1929.*

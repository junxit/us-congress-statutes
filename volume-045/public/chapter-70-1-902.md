# Chapter 902

> An Act To authorize officers of the Medical Corps to account certain service in computing their rights for retirement, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in computing length of service for purposes of retirement in the case of an officer of the Medical Corps of the Army, active duty performed as a member of the Medical Reserve Corps or as a contract surgeon, acting assistant surgeon, or contract physician, under a general contract obligating him to serve full time and to take station and change station as ordered, shall be credited to the same extent as service under a Regular Army commission.


*Approved, May 29, 1928.*

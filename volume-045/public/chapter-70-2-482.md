# Chapter 482

> An Act To increase the membership of the National Advisory Committee for Aeronautics.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the membership of the National Advisory Committee for Aeronautics is hereby increased from twelve members to fifteen members: Provided, That the three additional members to be appointed by the President shall be acquainted with the needs of aeronautical science, either civil or military, or skilled in aeronautical engineering or its allied sciences, and shall serve as such without compensation.


*Approved, March 2, 1929.*

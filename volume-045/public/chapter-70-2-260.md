# Chapter 260

> An Act To authorize the payment of certain salaries or compensation to Federal officials and employees by the treasurer of the Territory of Alaska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That any salaries to United States officials or employees of the United States Government in Alaska, appropriated by the Alaska Territorial Legislature, session of 1927, may be paid to such United States officials or employees of the United States by the treasurer of Alaska up to and including the date of March 31,1929, any Federal law to the contrary notwithstanding: Provided, That subsequent to March 31, 1929, all appropriations by the Alaska Territorial Legislature shall be in conformity with the provisions of the Act of Congress approved August 24, 1912, entitled “An Act to create a legislative assembly in the Territory of Alaska, to confer legislative powers thereon, and for other purposes,” and amendments thereto.


*Approved, February 18, 1929.*

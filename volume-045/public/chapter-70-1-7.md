# Chapter 7

> Joint Resolution To continue commissioners in the Court of Claims.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of sections 1 and 2 of the Act approved February 24, 1925 (February 24, 1925, chapter 301, paragraph 1, Forty-third Statutes, page 964; United States Code, page 899, section 269), providing for commissioners in the Court of Claims, their appointment, powers, and procedure, be and the same are hereby continued in full force and effect for the term of three years from the date of the approval of this joint resolution.


*Approved, January 11, 1928.*

# Chapter 494

> An Act To amend section 1440 of the Revised Statutes of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1440 of the Revised Statutes of the United States be amended by adding at the end thereof the following new sentence: “Provided, however, That the foregoing provision shall not apply to any officer of the Navy on the retired list.”


*Approved, March 2, 1929.*

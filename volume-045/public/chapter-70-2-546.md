# Chapter 546

> An Act To extend the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Tennessee River at or near the mouth of Clarks River.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of the bridge across the Tennessee River, at or near the mouth of Clarks River, authorized to be built by the State Highway Commission, Commonwealth of Kentucky, by the Act of Congress approved May 18, 1928, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from May 18, 1929.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, March 2, 1929.*

# Chapter 364

> An Act To repeal paragraphs 127 and 128 of the Act entitled “An Act to discontinue certain reports now required by law to be made to Congress,” approved May 29, 1928.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraphs 127 and 128 of the Act entitled “An Act to discontinue certain reports now required by law to be made to Congress,” approved May 29, 1928, are hereby repealed.

## § 2.

The reports of the acts of the Philippine Legislature, referred to in paragraph 127 of such Act of May 29, 1928, and the acts and resolutions of the Legislature of Porto Rico, referred to in paragraph 128 of such Act of May 29, 1928, shall be continued as if such Act of May 29, 1928, had not been enacted.


*Approved, February 28, 1929.*

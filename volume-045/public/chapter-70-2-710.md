# Chapter 710

> Joint Resolution To appoint Homer W. Hall a member of the subcommittee of the Committee on the Judiciary established under House Joint Resolution 431 to inquire into the official conduct of Grover M. Moscowitz, United States District Judge for the Eastern District of New York.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Homer W. Hall, a member of the Committee on the Judiciary of the House of Representatives be, and he is hereby, appointed a member of the subcommittee of the Committee on the Judiciary of the House of Representatives established by House Joint Resolution 431 to inquire into the official conduct of Grover M. Moscowitz, United States District Judge for the Eastern District of New York, vice Royal H. Welder, deceased.


*Approved, March 4, 1929.*

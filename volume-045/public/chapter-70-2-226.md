# Chapter 226

> An Act To provide for the acquisition of a site and the construction thereon and equipment of buildings and appurtenances for the Coast Guard Academy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary Site to be acquired of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to acquire in fee simple without cost to the United States a suitable site at New London, Connecticut, and to construct and equip thereon such buildings and appurtenances as he may deem necessary for the purpose of the United States Coast Guard Academy, all at a total cost not to exceed $1,750,000, which amount, or so much thereof as may be necessary, is hereby authorized to be appropriated.


*Approved, February 16, 1929.*

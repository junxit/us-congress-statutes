# Chapter 697

> An Act To extend the time for completing the construction of a bridge across the Mississippi River at or near Cairo, Illinois.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives. of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time for completing the construction of a bridge across the Mississippi Biver at or near Cairo, Illinois, authorized to be built by the Cairo Bridge and Terminal Company, its legal representatives, successors, or assigns, by the Act of Congress approved April 2, 1926, is hereby extended to April 2, 1930.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, March 4, 1929.*

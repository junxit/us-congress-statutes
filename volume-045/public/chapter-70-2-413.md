# Chapter 413

> An Act For the appointment of an additional circuit judge for the ninth judicial circuit.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and is hereby, authorized to appoint, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, an additional circuit judge for the ninth judicial circuit.

## § 2.

When a vacancy shall occur due to the death, resignation, or retirement of the present senior circuit judge of said circuit, such vacancy shall not be filled unless authorized by Congress.


*Approved, March 1, 1929.*

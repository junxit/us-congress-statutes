# Chapter 312

> An Act To authorize alterations and repairs to certain naval vessels.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purpose of modernizing the United States ships Pennsylvania and Arizona, alterations and repairs to such vessels are hereby authorized at a total cost not to exceed the sum of $14,800,000, in all. The alterations to the capital ships herein authorized shall be subject to the limitations prescribed in the treaty limiting naval armaments ratified August 17, 1923.


*Approved, February 25, 1929.*

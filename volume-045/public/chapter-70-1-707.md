# Chapter 707

> An Act To reserve certain lands on the public domain in Valencia County, New Mexico, for the use and benefit of the Acoma Pueblo Indians.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all vacant, unappropriated, and undisposed of lands within township 7 north, ranges 7 and 8 west, and fractional township 8 north, range 8 west, New Mexico principal meridian, in New Mexico, be, and they are hereby, reserved for the sole use and occupancy of the Indians of the Acoma Pueblo: Provided, That the rights and claims of any bona fide settler initiated under the public land laws prior to October 3, 1927, the date of withdrawal of the lands from all forms of entry, shall not be affected by this Act.


*Approved, May 23, 1928.*

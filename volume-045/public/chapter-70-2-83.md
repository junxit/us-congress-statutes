# Chapter 83

> An Act To authorize alterations and repairs to the United States ship California.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That alterations and repairs are hereby authorized to the United States ship California at a total cost not to exceed $350,000 in addition to the sum of $300,000 authorized to be expended under existing law. The alterations herein authorized shall be subject to the limitations prescribed in the treaty limiting naval armament ratified August 17, 1923.


*Approved, January 19, 1929.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 31

> oakland harbor, california.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the House of Representatives the joint resolution (H. J. Res. 244) entitled “Joint Resolution authorizing a modification of the adopted project for Oakland Harbor, California. ”


*Passed, April 20, 1928.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 22

> adjournment.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President of the Senate and the Speaker of the House of Representatives be authorized to close the first session of the Seventieth Congress by adjourning their respective Houses on the 29th day of May, 1928, at 5.30 o’clock post meridian.


*Passed, May 29, 1928.*

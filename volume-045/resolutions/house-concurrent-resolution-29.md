# House Concurrent Resolution 29

> statue of andrew jackson.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the statue of Andrew Jackson by Mrs. Belle Kinney Scholz, presented by the State of Tennessee, to be placed in Statuary Hall, is accepted in the name of the United States, and that the thanks of Congress be tendered the State for the contribution of the statue of one of its most eminent citizens, illustrious for his distinguished services to the country in war and in peace.

## § 2.

That a copy of these resolutions, suitably engrossed and duly authenticated, be transmitted to the Governor of Tennessee.


*Passed, April 11, 1928.*

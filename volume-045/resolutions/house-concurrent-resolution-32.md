# House Concurrent Resolution 32

> oakland harbor, california.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the action of the Speaker of the House of Representatives and the Vice President in signing the joint resolution (H. J. Res. 244) entitled “A jointresolution authorizing the modification of the adopted project for Oakland Harbor, California,” be rescinded and that in the reenrollment of said joint resolution the word “June” be stricken out and the word “January” be inserted in lieu thereof.


*Passed, April 24, 1928.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 32

> permit to new york city to enter federal property.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate the Joint Resolution (S. J. Res. 171) entitled “Joint Resolution granting the consent of Congress to the city of New York to enter upon certain United States property for the purpose of constructing a Rapid Transit Railway.”


*Passed, January 29, 1929.*

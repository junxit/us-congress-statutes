# Senate Concurrent Resolution 20

> war department surplus real property.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President of the United States be, and he is hereby, requested to return to the Senate the bill (S. 3752) to amend section 3 of an Act entitled “An Act authorizing the use for permanent construction at military posts of the proceeds from the sale of surplus War Department real property, and authorizing the sale of certain military reservations, and for other purposes,” approved March 12, 1926.


*Passed, May 21, 1928.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 15

> harriman geographic code system.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Secretary of the Senate and the Clerk of the House of Representatives hereby are authorized and directed to pay, out of the contingent funds of the Senate and House of Representatives, respectively, the sum of $2,000, or so much thereof as may be required, one-half of said sum to be paid by the said Secretary of the Senate, the remaining half by the said Clerk of the House upon vouchers duly approved by the Chairman of the Joint Committee of both Houses authorized by S. J. Res. 110 (Public Resolution Numbered 70, Sixty-ninth Congress), to consider the purchase of the right to an unrestricted use of the Harriman Geographic Code System under patents issued, or that may be issued, and also the unrestricted use of the copyrights issued, or that may be issued, in connection with the products of the Harriman Geographic Code System, for all governmental, administrative, or publication purposes for which the same may be desirable.


*Passed, February 25, 1929.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 3

> statue of alexander h. stephens.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the statue of Alexander H. Stephens, presented by the State of Georgia, to be placed in Statuary Hall, is accepted in the name of the United States, and that the thanks of Congress be tendered the State for the contribution of the statue of one of its most eminent citizens, illustrious for his distinguished humanitarian service.

That a copy of these resolutions, suitably engrossed and duly authenticated, be transmitted to the Governor of Georgia.


*Passed, December 7, 1927.*

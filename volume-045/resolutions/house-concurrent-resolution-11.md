# House Concurrent Resolution 11

> first deficiency act, 1928.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Clerk of the House be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed in the enrollment of H. R. 5800 (Seventieth Congress, first session), known as the First Deficiency Bill, to insert on page 38 in line 9 of the engrossed bill after the word “attorneys” the following words: “ (their heirs or their assignees now of record, as their interest may appear)”.


*Passed, December 19, 1927.*

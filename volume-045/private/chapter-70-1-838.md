# Chapter 838

> An Act To correct the naval record John Lewis Burns.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to correct the naval record of the late John Lewis Burns, gunner, United States Navy, to show that his death on August 6, 1918, while attached to the United States ship North Carolina, was incurred in line of duty and was not due to his own misconduct.


*Approved, May 28, 1928.*

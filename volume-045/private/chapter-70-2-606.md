# Chapter 606

> An Act For the relief of Frans Jan Wouters, of Antwerp, Belgium.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That an appropriation in the sum of $1,648 be, and the same is hereby, authorized to compensate Frans Jan Wouters for the death of his father, Jan Van Wouters, a Belgian national, as a result of being struck by a United States Navy automobile in the city of Antwerp, Belgium, on August 12, 1919.


*Approved, March 2, 1929.*

# Chapter 632

> An Act For the relief of George Heitkamp.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 17 and 20 of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes,” approved September 7, 1916, as amended, are hereby waived in favor of George Heitkamp, a civilian employee of the War Department, who was injured at Fort Sam Houston, Texas, on or about September 11, 1921, and his case is hereby authorized to be considered and acted upon under the remaining provisions of such Act.


*Approved, March 2, 1929.*

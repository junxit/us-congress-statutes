# Chapter 297

> An Act For the relief of Captain Will H. Gordon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Captain Will H. Gordon, of Plattsburg Barracks, New York, the sum of $472, in full compensation for stoppage against pay of said Captain Will H. Gordon, covering the loss of twenty pistols for which he was held pecuniarily responsible in accordance with investigation and recommendation of the inspector general.


*Approved, February 21, 1929.*

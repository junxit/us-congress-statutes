# Chapter 846

> An Act For the relief of Harry M. King.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission be, and it is hereby, directed to hear and determine the claim of Harry M. King, who claims to have suffered physical injuries received by him in line of duty while working for the Government at Muscle Shoals, Alabama, during the year 1918.


*Approved, May 28, 1928.*

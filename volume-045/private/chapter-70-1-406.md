# Chapter 406

> An Act For the relief of William F. Wheeler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That William F. Wheeler shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as captain of Company D, Forty-ninth New York Volunteer Infantry, as of the 4th day of October, 1862: Provided, That no back pay, bounty, pension, or other emolument shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, April 21, 1928.*

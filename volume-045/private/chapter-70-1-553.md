# Chapter 553

> An Act For the relief of William Thurman Enoch.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, and in full settlement against the Government, to William Thurman Enoch, of El Paso, Texas, the sum of $5,000, on account of permanent injury sustained by him through the negligence of Frederick W. Warner, an employee of the United States.


*Approved, May 14, 1928.*

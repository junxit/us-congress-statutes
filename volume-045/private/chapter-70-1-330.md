# Chapter 330

> An Act For the relief of Harriet K. Carey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the General Accounting Office is hereby authorized and directed to allow credit to Harriet K. Carey in the sum of $95.02 for travel and expenses incurred in the month of September, 1926, in connection with the investigations of matters pertaining to the Pension Bureau.


*Approved, April 9, 1928.*

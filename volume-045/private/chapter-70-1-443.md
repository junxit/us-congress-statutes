# Chapter 443

> An Act For the relief of Frank Hanley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of laws conferring rights and privileges upon honorably discharged soldiers or sailors, Frank Hanley shall be held to have been honorably discharged from the naval service as a landsman on board the United States ship Yantic on November 20, 1865: Provided, That no back pay, compensation, or other benefit shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, April 26, 1928.*

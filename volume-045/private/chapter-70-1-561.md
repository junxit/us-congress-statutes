# Chapter 561

> An Act For the relief of Clifford J. Sanghove.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is authorized and directed to cancel the claim of the United States against Clifford J. Sanghove, lieutenant, United States Naval Reserve Force, retired, in the sum of $1,067.46 erroneously paid to him.


*Approved, May 14, 1928.*

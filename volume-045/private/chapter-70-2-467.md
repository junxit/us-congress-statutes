# Chapter 467

> An Act For the relief of Cary Dawson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to allow credit in the settlement of the accounts of Cary Dawson, regional disbursing officer, special disbursing agent, United States Veterans’ Bureau, Cincinnati, in the sum of $330 for restoration of the premises which were occupied by the United States Veterans’ Bureau on the fourth floor of the Cleveland Life Building, Evansville, Indiana.


*Approved, March 1, 1929.*

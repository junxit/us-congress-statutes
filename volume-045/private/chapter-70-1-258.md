# Chapter 258

> An Act For the relief of A. S. Guffey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the accounts of A. S. Guffey, late postmaster at Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, in the sum of $2,237.50 due to the United States on account of postal funds embezzled by Henry C. Schuster, late superintendent of the north side station, Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, in the year 1920.


*Approved, March 27, 1928.*

# Chapter 269

> An Act For the relief of Charles J. Hunt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is authorized and directed to allow the claim of Charles J. Hunt for compensation in the sum of $1,228.33 for services as financial clerk in the office of the superintendent for the Five Civilized Tribes at Muskogee, Oklahoma, from April 25, 1926, to September 8, 1926, inclusive, which services were at the rate of $3,300 per annum.


*Approved, February 19, 1929.*

# Chapter 402

> An Act For the relief of Dennis W. Scott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Dennis W. Scott, who was a member of Company B, Thirty-second Regiment United States-Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a private of that organization on the 31st day of October, 1898: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, April 21, 1928.*

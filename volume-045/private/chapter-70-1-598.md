# Chapter 598

> An Act For the relief of John L. Nightingale.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the account of John L. Nightingale, postmaster at Fort Collins, Colorado, in the sum of $34,934.90, the value of funds and stamps lost in the burglary of the post office at Fort Collins, Colorado, July 24, 1927.


*Approved, May 16, 1928.*

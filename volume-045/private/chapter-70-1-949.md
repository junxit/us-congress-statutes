# Chapter 949

> An Act For the relief of Harry C. Bradley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the Employees’ Compensation Act of September 7, 1916, as amended by the Act of February 12, 1927, the Employees’ Compensation Commission is authorized and divested, in connection with any application that may be filed by Harry C. Bradley, now a clerk in the United States Land Office at Las Cruces, New Mexico, to consider that he contracted tuberculosis in the service of the United States.


*Approved, May 29, 1928.*

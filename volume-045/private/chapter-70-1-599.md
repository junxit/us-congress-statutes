# Chapter 599

> An Act For the relief of Marion Banta.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Marion Banta, of Carrollton, Carroll County, Kentucky, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $81.50, in full compensation for the amount of a check cashed and the proceeds thereof appropriated by the United States Government, which was given by said Marion Banta to guarantee the performance of a contract.


*Approved, May 16, 1928.*

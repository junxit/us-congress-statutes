# Chapter 719

> An Act For the relief of John H. and Avie D. Mathison, parents of Charles W. Mathison, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the World War Veterans’ Act, 1924 (chapter 10, title 38, United States Code), as amended, Charles William Mathison shall be held and considered to have been a private in the United States Marine Corps at the time of his death on May 28, 1919.


*Approved, March 4, 1929.*

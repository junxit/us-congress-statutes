# Chapter 331

> An Act For the relief of Winfield Scott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the General Accounting Office is hereby authorized and directed to allow credit to Winfield Scott in the sum of $278.14, to cover travel and expenses in the months of August and September, 1926, incurred in connection with matters pertaining to the Pension Bureau.


*Approved, April 9, 1928.*

# Chapter 89

> An Act For the relief of Joseph Jameson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the account of Joseph Jameson, postmaster at Lorain, Ohio, in the sum of $10,662.10, due the United States on account of public funds and property lost in the burglary of the post office on March 1, 1925.


*Approved, February 18, 1928.*

# Chapter 798

> An Act For the relief of Joseph S. Carroll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in consideration of his long and faithful service in various positions at the United States Naval Academy, where he is now employed as utility man for the midshipmen’s mess, and of his enlisted service in the regular Navy on various midshipmen’s cruises, extending, in all, over a continuous period of fifty years, the Secretary of the Navy is hereby authorized to enlist Joseph S. Carroll as a chief commissary steward in the United States Navy and to immediately thereafter transfer him to the retired list of the Navy with the retired pay of that rating: Provided, That the said Joseph S. Carroll shall not be entitled to any back pay or allowances prior to the date upon which he may be transferred to the retired list, as herein authorized: Provided further, That the enlistment of the said Joseph S. Carroll, as herein authorized, may be effected without regard to age or physical qualifications.


*Approved, May 26, 1928.*

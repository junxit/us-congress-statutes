# Chapter 789

> An Act For the relief of A. F. Gallagher.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay to A. F. Gallagher, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $190.40, in full satisfaction of all claims against the United States on account of stenographic services rendered in reporting certain hearings held in the Bureau of Internal Revenue on November 23, 1925, and January 15, 1926.


*Approved, May 26, 1928.*

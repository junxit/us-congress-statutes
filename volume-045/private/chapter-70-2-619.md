# Chapter 619

> An Act For the relief of Timothy Hanlon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Director of the United States Veterans’ Bureau be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to pay to Timothy Hanlon, out of the appropriation for medical and hospital services, the sum of $28.50 for services rendered in connection with the funeral and burial of Haymond F. Poore (C–541–245), deceased.


*Approved, March 2, 1929.*

# Chapter 238

> An Act To extend the benefits of the United States Employees’ Compensation Act of September 7, 1916, to John L. Jenifer, a former employee of the Government Printing Office, Washington, District of Columbia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission shall be, and it is hereby, authorized and directed to extend to John L. Jenifer, a former employee in the Government Printing Office, Washington, District of Columbia, the provisions of an Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes,” approved September 7, 1916.


*Approved, February 16, 1929.*

# Chapter 832

> An Act For the relief of Michael Ilitz.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Michael Ilitz, sergeant, first class, United States Army, retired, who had applied for retirement three months previous to June 3, 1916, but remained in the service to demonstrate his invention, the Army rolling kitchen, until June 28, 1916, be, and he is hereby, placed in the first grade.


*Approved, May 28, 1928.*

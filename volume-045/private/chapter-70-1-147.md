# Chapter 147

> An Act To authorize Colonel Charles A. Lindbergh, United States Army Air Corps Reserve, to accept decorations and gifts from foreign governments.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Colonel Charles A. Lindbergh, United States Army Air Corps Reserve, is hereby authorized and permitted to accept decorations, medals, certificates, or gifts which have been heretofore or may hereafter be tendered him in recognition of services, exploits, or achievements, by the government of any foreign state with which the Government of the United States was at the time of such tender and acceptance on friendly terms; and the consent of Congress required therefor by clause 8 of section 9 of Article I of the Constitution is hereby expressly granted.


*Approved, March 7, 1928.*

# Chapter 366

> An Act For the relief of Holger M. Trandum.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Holger M. Trandum is hereby allowed three years from the date of the passage of this Act within which to submit proof upon homestead entry 051441, Miles City series, embracing the north half section 24, township 9 north, range 58 east, principal meridian, Montana, which entry is hereby reinstated for the purposes of this Act.


*Approved, April 12, 1928.*

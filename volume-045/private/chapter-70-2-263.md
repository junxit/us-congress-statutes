# Chapter 263

> An Act For the relief of Charles H. Salley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury of the United States not otherwise appropriated, to Charles H. Salley, of Salley, South Carolina, the sum of $67 in full compensation for costs and disbursements as administrator of the estate of Jim Woodward, deceased.


*Approved, February 18, 1929.*

# Chapter 396

> An Act For the relief of Frank C. Messenger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is authorized to appoint Frank C. Messenger (retired), formerly chief gunner in the United States Navy, a lieutenant in the United States Navy, and place him upon the retired list of the Navy with the retired pay and allowance of that grade: Provided, That no back pay, allowance, or emoluments shall become due as a result of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 28, 1929.*

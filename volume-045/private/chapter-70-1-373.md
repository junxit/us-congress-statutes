# Chapter 373

> An Act For the relief of Charles A. Black, alias Angus Black.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in theadministration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers Charles A. Black, alias Angus Black, who was a member of Company B Eleventh Massachusetts Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a private of that organization on the 17th day of August, 1861: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowances shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, April 13, 1928.*

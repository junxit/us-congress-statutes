# Chapter 694

> An Act For the relief of Charles R. Sies.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Chief Pay Clerk Charles R. Sies, United States Navy, retired, shall be advanced on the retired list to passed assistant paymaster with the rank and retired pay and allowances of a lieutenant on the retired list of the Navy. Such rank shall take effect on March 4, 1925, and such pay and allowances shall be paid from and after such date.


*Approved, May 22, 1928.*

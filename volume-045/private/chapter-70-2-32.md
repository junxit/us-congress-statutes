# Chapter 32

> An Act For the relief of Russell White Bear.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to issue a patent in fee to Russell White Bear, Crow allottee numbered 822, for land allotted to him under the provisions of the Act of June 4, 1920 (Forty-first Statutes at Large, page 751), and designated. as homestead.


*Approved, December 15, 1928.*

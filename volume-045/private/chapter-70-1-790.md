# Chapter 790

> An Act For the relief of Frank E. Shults.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the account of Frank E. Shults, postmaster at Baraboo, Wisconsin, in the sum of $28,170.37 due to the United States on account of loss sustained in the burglary of the post office at Baraboo, Wisconsin, on June 30, 1926.


*Approved, May 26, 1928.*

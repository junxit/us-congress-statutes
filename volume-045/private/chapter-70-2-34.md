# Chapter 34

> Joint Resolution For the relief of Eloise Childers, Creek Indian, minor, roll numbered 354.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

Whereas Eloise Childers, Creek Indian, minor, roll numbered 354, having lost the allotment of land which the United States attempted to assign her and having received from the tribal funds no payments for the satisfaction of such rights; Therefore be it

That there is authorized to be appropriated the sum of $1,213.24, out of the tribal funds of the Creek Nation, in full satisfaction of her rights to an allotment of Creek tribal lands.


*Approved, December 15, 1928.*

# Chapter 924

> An Act For the relief of Oliver C. Sell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, or benefits, upon honorably discharged soldiers, Oliver C. Sell, recruiting officer, and in Company B, First Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been enrolled in the military service of the United States on July 24, 1898, instead of August 1, 1898, as shown on the official records of the War Department.


*Approved, May 29, 1928.*

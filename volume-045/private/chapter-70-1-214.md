# Chapter 214

> An Act For the relief of James W. Kingon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to James W. Kingon, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $170 in full settlement of all pay, bounty, and allowances due him for services in Company H, Forty-second Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, March 10, 1928.*

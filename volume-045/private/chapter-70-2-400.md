# Chapter 400

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Louise A. Wood.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Louise A. Wood, widow of Leonard Wood, late a major general in the United States Army, and pay her a pension at the rate of $5,000 per year in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 28, 1929.*

# Chapter 722

> An Act For the relief of T. L. Young and C. T. Cole.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to relieve T. L. Young and C. T. Cole from the payment of the judgment recovered on the bond for the sum representing the amount of the bond they signed as surety for C. A. Walters, who was subsequently rearrested and produced in court: Provided, That the defendants, T. L. Young and C. T. Cole, pay the costs in the case of the United States against C. A. Walters and others, being case Numbered 2566 of the term of the District Court of the United States held at Kansas City, in said district, beginning on the 6th day of October, 1924.


*Approved, March 4, 1929.*

# Chapter 49

> An Act For the relief of Joel T. Smith.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the account of Joel T. Smith, late postmaster at Chester, Montana, in the sum of $505, due the United States on account of the loss resulting from the closing of the First National Bank, of Chester, Montana.


*Approved, February 13, 1928.*

# Chapter 391

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the Secretary of War to award a duplicate Congressional Medal of Honor to Lieutenant Colonel William J. Sperry.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to issue a duplicate Congressional Medal of Honor to William J. Sperry, late lieutenant colonel, Sixth Vermont Infantry, Civil War, who was awarded a Congressional Medal of Honor for most distinguished gallantry at Petersburg, Virginia, April 2, 1865; and that the duplicate medal be presented to the widow of the above-mentioned officer.


*Approved, April 20, 1928.*

# Chapter 936

> An Act For the relief of the Near East Relief (Incorporated).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to cancel and abandon the claim in the sum of $632,669 with interest for United States property sold in 1919 to the Near East Relief (Incorporated), for use in relieving and supplying the pressing needs of the peoples of the countries involved in the late war: Provided, That the Near East Relief (Incorporated) shall first cancel all claims of whatever kind and description the said Near East Relief (Incorporated) now holds against the United States Government.


*Approved, May 29, 1928.*

# Chapter 788

> An Act For the relief of the widow of Surgeon Mervin W. Glover, United States Public Health Service, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to the widow of Mervin W. Glover, deceased, the sum of $4,725, being the amount of salary for one year.


*Approved, May 26, 1928.*

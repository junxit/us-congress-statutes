# Chapter 625

> An Act For the relief of Ella Mae Rinks.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, and in full settlement against the Government, the sum of $772.35 to Ella Mae Rinks for personal injury resulting from being run down by a truck belonging to the Sequoyah Orphan Training School.


*Approved, March 2, 1929.*

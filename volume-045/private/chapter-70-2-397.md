# Chapter 397

> An Act For the relief of Ruth B. Lincoln.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, and in full settlement against the United States Government, the sum of $200, to Kuth B. Lincoln, as reimbursement for personal injuries as a result of the crashing of a naval airplane on March 24, 1928.


*Approved, February 28, 1929.*

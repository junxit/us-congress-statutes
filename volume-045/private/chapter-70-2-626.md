# Chapter 626

> An Act For the relief of H. L. Redlingshafer for payments made in official capacity disallowed by the General Accounting Office.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed in the settlement of the accounts of H. L. Redlingshafer, a district fiscal agent of the Forest Service, Department of Agriculture, to allow the sum of $242.50, payments to Chester W. Cheatham, now deceased, of per diem in lieu of subsistence at Juneau, Alaska, during the period January 1 to April 30, 1925.


*Approved, March 2, 1929.*

# Chapter 234

> An Act For the relief of Florence P. Hampton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, and in full settlement against the Government, the sum of $5,000 to Florence P. Hampton, on account of the death of her husband, Henry Alfred Hampton, who was killed in Calhoun County, Florida, September 6, 1928, while deputized as a prohibition enforcement officer.


*Approved, February 16, 1929.*

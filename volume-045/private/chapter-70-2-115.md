# Chapter 115

> An Act For the relief of E. L. F. Auffurth.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, and in full settlement against the Government, the sum of $105.60 to reimburse E. L. F. Auffurth for loss of salary while employed in the United States post office and customhouse at New Orleans, Louisiana.


*Approved, January 29, 1929.*

# Chapter 848

> An Act Authorizing the Secretary of the Navy to make a readjustment of pay to Gunner W. H. Anthony, junior, United States Navy (retired).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy is hereby authorized to examine into the service performed by Gunner W. H. Anthony, junior, United States Navy (retired), and to pay him such amount as would be due him on January 14, 1920, for any active service performed while on the retired list.


*Approved, May 28, 1928.*

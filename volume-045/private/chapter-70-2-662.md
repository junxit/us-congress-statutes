# Chapter 662

> An Act For the relief of John Bowie.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury he, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to John Bowie, of Annapolis Junction, Maryland, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $2,610 for damage to his farm in Anne Arundel County, Maryland, on account of the occupation by the United States Army during the years 1917, 1918, and 1919.


*Approved, March 2, 1929.*

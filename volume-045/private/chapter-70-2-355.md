# Chapter 355

> An Act For the relief of Simon A. Richardson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws Simon A. Richardson, who enlisted in the Navy July 26, 1898, shall hereafter be held and considered to have enlisted on the 26th day of May, 1898: Provided, That no back pay, pension, bounty or allowances shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 27, 1929.*

# Chapter 801

> An Act For the relief of Charlie R. Pate.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of all laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Charlie R. Pate, who was a member of Company L, Twenty-seventh United States Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a private of that organization on November 30, 1902: Provided, That no back pay, bounty, pension, or other allowances shall be held as accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, May 26, 1928.*

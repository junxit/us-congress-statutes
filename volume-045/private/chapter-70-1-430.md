# Chapter 430

> An Act For the relief of Peter S. Kelly.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws and the laws conferring rights and privileges upon honorably discharged soldiers, their widows and dependent relatives, Peter S. Kelly, late of Company B, First Regiment Montana Volunteer Infantry, shall be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a member of the above organization on the 17th day of October, 1899: Provided, That no pay, pension, bounty, or other emoluments shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, April 24, 1928.*

# Chapter 975

> An Act For the relief of Ross F. Collins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States is hereby authorized to appoint, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, Ross F. Collins, now a lieutenant, United States Naval Reserve, a lieutenant commander, United States Naval Reserve, to rank next after Julian L. Woodruff, United States Naval Reserve, and to make an extra number in that grade.


*Approved, May 29, 1928.*

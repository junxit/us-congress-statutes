# Chapter 248

> An Act To allow credit in the accounts of William A. Schoenfeld.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed in the settlement of the accounts of William A. Schoenfeld, a special temporary disbursing agent of the Department of Agriculture, to allow the sum of $80 now standing as a disallowance in the accounts of said William A. Schoenfeld, being the difference between $110 each paid for two typewriting machines purchased in Vienna, Austria, and the limiting price for such machines, $70 each, fixed by Act of April 4, 1924 (Forty-third Statutes, page 67).


*Approved, February 16, 1929.*

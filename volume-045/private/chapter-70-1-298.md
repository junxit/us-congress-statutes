# Chapter 298

> An Act For the relief of John Rooks.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the General Accounting Office is hereby authorized to allow payments covering the salary of John Kooks for services actually rendered as United States marshal for the district of South Dakota from June 8, 1924, to October 30, 1925, inclusive.


*Approved, March 29, 1928.*

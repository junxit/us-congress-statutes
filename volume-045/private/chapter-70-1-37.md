# Chapter 37

> An Act For the relief of Thomas M. Ross.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws, Thomas M. Ross shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from Company F, Second Regiment United States Infantry, war withSpain, on April 11, 1900: Provided, That no back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 11, 1928.*

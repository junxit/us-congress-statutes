# Chapter 799

> An Act Granting six months’ pay to Vincentia V. Irwin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, such sum as may be necessary to pay to Vincentia V. Irwin, widow of the late Ensign Glendon Ward Irwin, United States Navy, an amount equal to six months pay at the rate said Glendon Ward Irwin was receiving at the date of his death.


*Approved, May 26, 1928.*

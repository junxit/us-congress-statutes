# Chapter 285

> An Act For the benefit of Morris Fox Cherry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Morris Fox Cherry, who was a member of Battery A, One hundred and thirty-second Field Artillery, Thirty-sixth Division, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a private of that organization on the 16th day of April, 1918. That no back pay or allowances shall accrue by reason of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 20, 1929.*

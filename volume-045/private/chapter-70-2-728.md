# Chapter 728

> An Act For the relief of William S. Shacklette.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That hereafter William S. Shacklette, who was retired for physical disabilities, incurred in line of duty at the time of the explosion of the boilers of the United States ship Bennington, at San Diego, California, July 21, 1905, and was awarded the Congressional Medal of Honor for “ extraordinary heroism displayed at the time of the accident,” shall receive the retired pay of chief pharmacist, equivalent to the maxi mum pay and allowance of that grade provided by law.


*Approved, March 4, 1929.*

# Chapter 971

> An Act For the relief of Captain Louis C. Brinton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Bouse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to allow credit in the account of the above named officer of the Army of the United States in the sum herein stated which now stands as disallowance on the books of the General Accounting Office: Captain Louis C. Brinton (now lieutenant colonel), Coast Artillery, the sum of $1,398.66, being the amount of erroneous payments made by him during the summer of 1916 while acting as finance officer at Brownsville, Texas.


*Approved, May 29, 1928.*

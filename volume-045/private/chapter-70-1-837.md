# Chapter 837

> An Act For the relief of Lieutenant Charles Thomas Wooten, United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized to settle the claim of Lieutenant Charles Thomas Wooten, United States Navy, in the sum of $518.25 for medical, hospital, and other expenses incident to an emergency operation upon the said Lieutenant Wooten on May 23, 1924.


*Approved, May 28, 1928.*

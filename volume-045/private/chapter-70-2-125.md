# Chapter 125

> An Act For the relief of Bertram Lehman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Bertram Lehman, of Tuscaloosa, Alabama, the sum of $1,257, as reimbursement for medical, nursing, and hospital expenses paid by him on account of a disability incurred while serving as a private in Company 9, Third Training Battalion, One hundred and fifty-third Depot Brigade, United States Army.


*Approved, January 30, 1929.*

# Chapter 663

> An Act For the relief of Dale S. Rice.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Dale S. Rice, of Okanogan, Washington, the sum of $606.52 as reimbursement for moneys paid by the said Dale S. Rice in purchase of lands under an additional homestead entry, serial numbered Spokane 013980, made by him December 15, 1919, and canceled July 2, 1927.

## § 2.

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, $606.52 for the payment of said sum to said Dale S. Rice, as provided in the foregoing section.


*Approved, March 2, 1929.*

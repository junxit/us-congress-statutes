# Chapter 90

> An Act For the relief of Walter D. Lovell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Walter D. Lovell, of Minneapolis, Minnesota, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $3,548.17, in full settlement for extra work, for which the Government has received the benefit and for which no payment has been made, and for losses suffered by reason of action of governmental agencies, by reason of increased cost of labor and materials, and other causes brought about by the late war with Germany, incurred by Walter D. Lovell, under contract for construction work at Kock Island Arsenal, Illinois, dated January 19, 1917, with the Chief of Ordnance, War Department, prior to the entrance of the United States into the late war with Germany, and thereafter completed.


*Approved, January 19, 1929.*

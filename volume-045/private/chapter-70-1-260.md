# Chapter 260

> An Act For the relief of Willis B. Cross.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws Willis B. Cross shall hereafter he held and considered to have been mustered into the military service of the United States as a private of Captain Miner’s Seventeenth Battery, Indiana Volunteer Light Artillery, and to have been honorably discharged therefrom after having served therein more than ninety days: Provided, That no back pay, bounty, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 27, 1928.*

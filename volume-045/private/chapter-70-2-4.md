# Chapter 4

> An Act For the relief of the State of South Carolina.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions of section 3646, as amended, of the Revised Statutes of the United States, the disbursing clerk of the Department of Agriculture is authorized and directed to issue, without the requirement of an indemnity bond, a duplicate of original check numbered 1369652, drawn May 10, 1927, in favor of “ State treasurer of South Carolina, Columbia, South Carolina,” for $2,152.07 and lost, stolen, or miscarried in the mails.


*Approved, December 6, 1928.*

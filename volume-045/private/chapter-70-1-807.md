# Chapter 807

> An Act For the relief of Elbert L. Cox.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Elbert L. Cox, who was a member of Headquarters Troop, One hundred and thirty-fifth Machine Gun Battalion, Thirty-seventh Division, United States Army, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a second lieutenant of that organization on the 19th day of August, 1918: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, May 26, 1928.*

# Chapter 646

> An Act For the relief of James J. Dower.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers and their dependents, James J. Dower, who was a member of Battery C, Second United States Artillery, Fourteenth Company Coast Artillery, Ninety-sixth Company Coast Artillery, Twentieth Company Coast Artillery Corps, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a private of that organization on the 17th day of August, 1907: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 2, 1929.*

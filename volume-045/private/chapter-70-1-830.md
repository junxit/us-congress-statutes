# Chapter 830

> An Act For the relief of William M. Sherman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, or their dependents, William M. Sherman who served as a private of Troop A, Eighth Regiment United States Cavalry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a member of said organization on the 7th day of March, 1901: Provided, That no bounty, pension, pay, or other emoluments shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, May 28, 1928.*

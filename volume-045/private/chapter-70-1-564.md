# Chapter 564

> An Act For the relief of George M. Browder and F. N. Browder.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to George M. Browder and F. N. Browder, as administrators of the estate of the late F. G. Browder, the sum of $7,500. Such sum shall be in full satisfaction of all claims against the United States for damages resulting from the death of the said F. G. Browder, who, on October 20, 1926, near the city of Montgomery, Alabama, was struck and killed by an airplane owned and operated by the United States.


*Approved, May 14, 1928.*

# Chapter 938

> An Act For the relief of Captain Clarence Barnard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Captain Clarence Barnard, Ordnance Department, the sum of $1,374.21, because of losses sustained by him while in the service.


*Approved, May 29, 1928.*

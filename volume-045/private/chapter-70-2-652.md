# Chapter 652

> An Act For the relief of John F O’Neil.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized to appoint John F. O’Neal a carpenter in the United States Naval Reserve Force, and place him upon the retired list with three-quarters pay of his grade: Provided, That the said John F. O’Neil shall not, by the passage of this Act, be entitled to back pay or allowances.


*Approved, March 2, 1929.*

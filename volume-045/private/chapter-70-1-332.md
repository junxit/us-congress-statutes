# Chapter 332

> An Act For the relief of W. Laurence Hazard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the General Accounting Office is hereby authorized and directed to allow credit to W. Laurence Hazard in the sum of $167.52, to cover travel and expenses incurred in September, 1926, in connection with investigations of matters pertaining to the Pension Bureau.


*Approved, April 9, 1928.*

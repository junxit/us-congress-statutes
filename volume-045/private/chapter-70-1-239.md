# Chapter 239

> Joint Resolution For the relief of Henry A. Bellows.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions of section 1761 of the Revised Statutes Henry A. Bellows shall be paid compensation at the rate of $10,000 per annum for the period during which he served as a member of the Federal Radio Commission.

## § 2.

The moneys made available for the fiscal year 1927 by the Act of February 23, 1927, and those appropriated for the Federal Radio Commission by the First Deficiency Act, fiscal year 1928, shall be available for the payment of such compensation.


*Approved, March 23, 1928.*

# Chapter 237

> An Act For the relief of G. W. Rogers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to G. W. Rogers, former captain, Quartermaster Corps, United States Army, the sum of $400, representing the amount of deductions, during the months of May, June, July, and August, 1919, from his pay as captain, Quartermaster Corps, toward the settlement of a shortage in his accounts as disbursing officer in France during the period from December 23, 1918, to April 26, 1919, such shortage having been subsequently credited in his accounts by certificate of settlement numbered M–19682–W.


*Approved, March 23, 1928.*

# Chapter 562

> An Act For the relief of Finch R. Archer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General is hereby authorized and directed to make settlement, upon the approval of the Attorney General, with Finch R. Archer, warden of the United States Penitentiary at McNeil Island, Washington, in an amount not exceeding $1,608.54, covering the total value of money and personal effects stolen September 26, 1922, from the office of the said penitentiary by escaping prisoners; payment to be made from the appropriation for the maintenance of the institution for the current fiscal year.


*Approved, May 14, 1928.*

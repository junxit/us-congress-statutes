# Chapter 644

> An Act For the relief of the Staunton Brick Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to the Staunton Brick Company, Staunton, Virginia, the sum of $2,000 in full compensation for loss and damage suffered by the said Staunton Brick Company by reason of the diversion of a car of coal for war purposes in December, 1917.


*Approved, March 2, 1929.*

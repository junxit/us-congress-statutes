# Chapter 296

> An Act For the relief of Arthur Waldenmeyer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws or any laws conferring rights, privileges, or benefits upon persons honorably discharged from the United States Army, Arthur Waldenmeyer shall be held and considered to have been honorably discharged as a private, Company F, Second Regiment Infantry, United States Army, on March 21, 1900; but no pension, pay, nor bounty shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 21, 1929.*

# Chapter 121

> An Act For the relief of Carl Holm.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Director of the United States Veterans’ Bureau be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to reimburse out of the appropriation for medical and hospital services to Carl Holm, of Livermore, California, the sum of $102, plus interest thereon at the rate of 6 per centum per annum from June 12, 1925, to the date of the enactment of this Act. Such sum represents the amount of loss sustained by the said Carl Holm as the result of damage to his barley crop caused by the drilling of a well at the United States Veterans’ Bureau Hospital, Livermore, California, on June 12, 1925.


*Approved, January 29, 1929.*

# Chapter 642

> An Act For the relief of Frank L. Merrifield.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws, Frank L. Merrifield shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States in Group B, repair unit numbered 306, Motor Transport Corps, on the 4th day of September, 1921: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 2, 1929.*

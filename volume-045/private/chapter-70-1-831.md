# Chapter 831

> An Act For the relief of the Randolph-Macon Academy, Front Royal, Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Randolph-Macon Academy, Front Royal, Virginia, be, and it is hereby, relieved from all responsibility and accountability for certain ordnance property, to the value of $5,605.30, the property of the War Department, which was destroyed by fire while in the possession of the academy for training purposes, and the Secretary of War is directed to terminate all further accountability for said property.


*Approved, May 28, 1928.*

# Chapter 635

> An Act For the relief of Robert S. Ament.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Robert S. Ament, of New York, New York, the sum of $6,301.33, in full compensation for damage to a pier at Provincetown, Massachusetts (of which he is lessee and which he has improved and decorated to the value of this claim), by the United States Coast Guard cutter Morrill, on November 16, 1926.


*Approved, March 2, 1929.*

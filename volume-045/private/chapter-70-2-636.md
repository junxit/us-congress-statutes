# Chapter 636

> An Act For the relief of Kirby Hoon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the account of Kirby Hoon, postmaster at Helena, Montana, in the sum of $218.40, on account of a shortage of stamped envelopes.


*Approved, March 2, 1929.*

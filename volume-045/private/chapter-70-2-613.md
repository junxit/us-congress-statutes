# Chapter 613

> An Act For the relief of N. P. Nelson and Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to N. P. Nelson and Company of Bozeman, Montana, as subcontractors of the Pioneer Construction Company, the sum of $1,760.31 in full settlement of all claims resulting from the requisitioning of the road construction crew of N. P. Nelson and Company employed on the Paola section of the Belton-Java, Montana, highway project, by the forest supervisor for fire suppression work in connection with the Lost Johnnie Creek forest fire in 1926.


*Approved, March 2, 1929.*

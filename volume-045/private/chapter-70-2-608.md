# Chapter 608

> An Act For the relief of Margaret Vaughn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Paymaster General of the Navy is authorized and directed to pay, out of funds appropriated for the pay of the Navy, to Margaret Vaughn, mother of Cornelius Vaughn, late boiler maker, first class, United States Navy, an amount equal to six months’ pay at the rate received by such Cornelius Vaughn at the time of his death.


*Approved, March 2, 1929.*

# Chapter 698

> An Act Granting six months’ pay to Constance D. Lathrop.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Constance D. Lathrop, widow of the late Commander Patrick Theodore Moore Lathrop, United States Navy, is hereby allowed an amount equal to six months’ pay at the rate said Patrick Theodore Moore Lathrop was receiving at the date of his death.

## § 2.

That the payment of the amount of money hereby allowed and authorized to be paid to said Constance D. Lathrop is authorized to be made from the appropriations for beneficiaries of officers who die while in the active service of the United States Navy.


*Approved, May 22, 1928.*

# Chapter 347

> An Act For the relief of Doctor Andrew J. Baker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States Compensation Commission be, and it is hereby, directed to hear and determine the claim of Doctor Andrew J. Baker, who claims to have been disabled in the line of his employment as physician at the Pacific Branch of the National Home for Disabled Volunteer Soldiers at Sawtelle, California, the benefit of the Act of September 7, 1916 (Thirty-ninth Statutes at Large, page 742), as amended, without regard to the time of the filing of his claim for such benefits.


*Approved, February 26, 1929.*

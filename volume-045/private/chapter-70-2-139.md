# Chapter 139

> An Act For the relief of Michael J. Fraher.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Michael J. Fraher, who was a member of Company H, Thirty-second Regiment United States Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably on November 4, 1899, from the military service of the United States: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 2, 1929.*

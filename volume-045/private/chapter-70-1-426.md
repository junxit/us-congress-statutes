# Chapter 426

> An Act For the relief of James A. DeLoach.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, from any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, and in full settlement against the Government, the sum of $2,500 to James A. DeLoach for injuries received by him while attending a citizens’ military training camp at Camp McClellan, Alabama.


*Approved, April 23, 1928.*

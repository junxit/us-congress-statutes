# Chapter 935

> An Act For the relief of Mattie Holcomb.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Mattie Holcomb, mother of Henry Grady Holcomb, alias Henry Grady Halcomb, late ship’s cook, second class, United States Navy, is hereby allowed an amount equal to six months’ pay at the rate said Henry Grady Holcomb was receiving at the date of his death: Provided, That the said Mattie Holcomb establishes to the satisfaction of the Secretary of the Navy the fact that she was dependent upon her son, the late Henry Grady Holcomb, at the time of his death.

## § 2.

That the payment of the amount of money allowed and authorized to be paid to the said Mattie Holcomb is authorized to be made from the appropriation “ Pay, subsistence, and transportation of naval personnel.”


*Approved, May 29, 1928.*

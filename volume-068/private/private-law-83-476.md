# Private Law 476

> AN ACT For the relief of Jacob Vandenbergh.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Jacob Vandenbergh shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fee.


*Approved June 28, 1954.*

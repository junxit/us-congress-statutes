# Private Law 632

> AN ACT For the relief of Sister Antonella Marie Gutterres (Thereza Maria Gutterres).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of American Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Sister Antonella Marie Gutterres (Thereza Maria Gutterres) shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fee. Upon the granting of permanent residence to such alien as provided for in this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the appropriate quota for the first year that such quota is available.


*Approved August 13, 1954.*

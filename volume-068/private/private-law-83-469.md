# Private Law 469

> AN ACT Authorizing the Secretary of the Interior to issue a patent in fee to Lucy Yarlott Othermedicine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to issue to Lucy Yarlott Othermedicine a patent in fee to the following-described lands allotted to her on the Crow Indian Reservation, Montana: The west half of the northeast quarter, and the southeast quarter of the northeast quarter of section 22, and lot 3 of section 10, township 9 south, range 34 east, Montana principal meridian. The prior disposition of the homestead land of Lucy Yarlott Othermedicine is hereby ratified and confirmed.


*Approved June 28, 1954.*

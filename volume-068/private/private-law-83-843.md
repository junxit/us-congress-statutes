# Private Law 843

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Erika (Hohenleitner) Stapleton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of section 212 (a) (9) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Erika (Hohenleitner) Stapleton may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act: Provided, That this exemption shall apply only to a ground for exclusion of which the Department of State or the Department of Justice had knowledge prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved August 26, 1954.*

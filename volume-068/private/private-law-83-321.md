# Private Law 321

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Ala Olejcak (nee Holubowa).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

notwithstanding the provisions of section 212 fa) (9) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Ala Olejcak (nee Holubowa) may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of such Act: Provided, That this exemption shall apply only to a ground for exclusion of which the Department of State or the Department of Justice have knowledge prior to enactment of this Act.


*Approved April 1, 1954.*

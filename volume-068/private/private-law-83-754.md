# Private Law 754

> AN ACT For the relief of June Ann Sakurai.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, June Ann Sakurai, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Donald Winfield Smith, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 23, 1954.*

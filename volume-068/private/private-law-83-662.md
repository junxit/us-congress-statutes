# Private Law 662

> AN ACT For the relief of Joseph Di Pasquale.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 212 (a) 9 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Joseph Di Pasquale may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if he is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of such Act: Provided, That this exemption shall apply only to a ground for exclusion of which the Department of State or the Department of Justice have knowledge prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved August 16, 1954.*

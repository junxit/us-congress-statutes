# Private Law 853

> AN ACT For the relief of Mr. Fu-Ho Chan, Mrs. Fu-Ho Chan, and their child, Richard Chan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, and notwithstanding the provision of section 202 (b) of the said Act, Mr. Fu-Ho Chan and Mrs. Fu-Ho Chan, and their child, Richard Chan, shall be held to be natives of Canada, the country of their present residence.


*Approved August 26, 1954.*

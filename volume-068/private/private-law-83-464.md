# Private Law 464

> AN ACT For the relief of Letizia Maria Genoveffa Lo Bianco.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Letizia Maria Genoveffa Lo Bianco, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Giuseppe Loverde, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved June 28, 1954.*

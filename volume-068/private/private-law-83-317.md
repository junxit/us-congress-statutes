# Private Law 317

> AN ACT For the relief of Esther Wagner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 212 (a) (9) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Esther Wagner may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act: Provided, That this exemption shall apply only to a ground of exclusion of which the Secretary of State or the Attorney General had knowledge prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved March 30, 1954.*

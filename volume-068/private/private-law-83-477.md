# Private Law 477

> AN ACT For the relief of Maria Teresa Rossi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Maria Teresa Rossi, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Paul Persechino.


*Approved June 28, 1954.*

# Private Law 976

> AN ACT For the relief of Elisa-Pompea Roppo (Elisa-Pompea Cardone).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Elisa-Pompea Roppo (Elisa-Pompea Cardone), shall be held and considered to be the natural born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Michael Roppo, citizens of the United States.


*Approved September 1, 1954.*

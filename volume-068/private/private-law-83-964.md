# Private Law 964

> AN ACT For the relief of James Arthur Cimino and Joan Cimino.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor children, James Arthur Cimino, and Joan Cimino shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien children of James N. Cimino and Barbara Scribner Cimino, citizens of the United States.


*Approved September 1, 1954.*

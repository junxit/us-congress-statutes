# Private Law 380

> AN ACT For the relief of Zdzislaw (Jerzy) Jazwinski.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Zdzislaw (Jerzy) Jazwinski shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act; and that his past membership in the classes defined in section 212 (a) (28) of the Immigration and Nationality Act shall not hereafter be a cause for his exclusion from the United States. Upon the granting of permanent residence to such alien as provided for in this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the appropriate quota for the first year that such quota is available.


*Approved May 27, 1954.*

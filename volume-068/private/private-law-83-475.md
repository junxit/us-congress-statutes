# Private Law 475

> AN ACT For the relief of Giorgio Salvini Thompson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That; for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Giorgio Salvini Thompson, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Richard Arlen Thompson, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved June 28, 1954.*

# Private Law 233

> AN ACT For the relief of Setsuko Motohara Kibler, widow of Robert Eugene Kibler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Setsuko Motohara Kibler shall be held and considered to be eligible for a nonquota immigrant status if she is found admissible to the United States under the provisions of that Act.


*Approved February 20, 1954.*

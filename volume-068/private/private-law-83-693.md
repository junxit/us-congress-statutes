# Private Law 693

> AN ACT For the relief of Adolfo L. Kalb, and his wife, Mrs. Eugenia G. Kalb.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Adolfo L. Kalb and his wife, Mrs. Eugenia G. Kalb, shall be held to have a nonquota status as defined by section 101 (a) (27) (B) of that Act.


*Approved August 21, 1954.*

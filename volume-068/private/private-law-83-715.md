# Private Law 715

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Gertrud Eckerl Strickland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of section 212 (a) (6) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Gertrud Eckerl Strickland may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 21.3 of the said Act.


*Approved August 21, 1954.*

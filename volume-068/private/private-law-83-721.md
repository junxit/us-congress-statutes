# Private Law 721

> AN ACT For the relief of Brunhilde Walburga Golomb Hartsworm.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of section 212 (a) (9) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Brunhilde Walburga Golomb Hartsworm may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act: Provided, That this exemption shall apply only to a ground for exclusion of which the Department of State or the Department of Justice had knowledge prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved August 23, 1954.*

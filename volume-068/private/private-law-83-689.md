# Private Law 689

> AN ACT For the relief of Ilona Elizabeth Carrier.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Ilona Elizabeth Carrier shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of July 16, 1952.


*Approved August 21, 1954.*

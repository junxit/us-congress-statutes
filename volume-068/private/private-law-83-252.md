# Private Law 252

> AN ACT Conferring United States citizenship posthumously upon Henry Litmanowitz (Litman).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Henry Litmanowitz (Litman), the son of Mr. and Mrs. Morris Litmanowitz (Litman), of Cleveland, Ohio, who was killed while serving with the United States Armed Forces in Korea, shall be held and considered to have been a citizen of the United States at the time of his death.


*Approved February 27, 1954.*

# Private Law 243

> AN ACT For the relief of John Abraham and Ann Abraham.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor children, John Abraham and Ann Abraham, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien children of Mr. and Mrs. Francis Buck, citizens of the United States.


*Approved February 20, 1954.*

# Private Law 626

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Robert Lee Slaughter, nee Elisa Ortiz Orat.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of American Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Robert Lee laughter, nee Elisa Ortiz Orat, shall he held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fee.


*Approved August 13, 1954.*

# Private Law 513

> AN ACT For the relief of Lydia Wickenfeld Butz.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of American Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of section 212 (a) (9) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Lydia Wickenfeld Butz may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act: Provided, That this exemption shall apply only to a ground for exclusion of which the Department of State or the Department of Justice has knowledge prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved July 14, 1954.*

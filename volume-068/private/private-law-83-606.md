# Private Law 606

> AN ACT For the relief of Joseph Flury Paluy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of American Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of section 212 (a) (9) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Joseph Flury Paluy may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if he is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act.


*Approved August 13, 1954.*

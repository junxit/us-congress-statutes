# Private Law 533

> AN ACT For the relief of Karl Ullstein.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of American Congress assembled,*

That, in the adminis- tration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Karl Ullstein, a naturalized citizen of the United States, shall be permitted to reside in Germany until January I960 without losing his United States citizenship under section 352 (a) of such Act.


*Approved July 15, 1954.*

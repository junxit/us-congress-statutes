# Private Law 406

> AN ACT For the relief of Harold Donaghy Bishop.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of paragraph (3) of section 212 (a) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Harold Donaghy Bishop may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if he is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking approved by the Attorney General be given as prescribed by section 213 of that Act.


*Approved June 11, 1954.*

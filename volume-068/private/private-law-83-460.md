# Private Law 460

> AN ACT For the relief of Juanita Andrada Lach and Leticia Androda Lach.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor children, Juanita Andrada Lach and Leticia Androda Lach, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien children of Sergeant and Mrs. Peter J. Lach, citizens of the United States.


*Approved June 28, 1954.*

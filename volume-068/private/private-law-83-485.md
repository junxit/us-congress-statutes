# Private Law 485

> AN ACT For the relief of Barbara Gene Coster.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Barbara Gene Coster, who lost United States citizenship under the provisions of section 401 (e) of the Nationality Act of 1940, as amended, may be naturalized by taking prior to one year after the effective date of this Act, before any court referred to in subsection (a) of section 310 of the Immigration and Nationality Act or before any diplomatic or consular officer of the United States abroad, the oaths prescribed by section 337 of the said Act. From and after naturalization under this Act, the said Barbara Gene Coster shall have the same citizenship status as that which existed immediately prior to its loss.


*Approved June 28, 1954.*

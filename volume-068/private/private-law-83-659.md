# Private Law 659

> AN ACT For the relief of Andreja Glusic.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203 (a) (3) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Andreja Glusic shall be held and considered to be the minor child of her parents Andrej Glusic and Marica Penca Glusic.


*Approved August 13, 1954.*

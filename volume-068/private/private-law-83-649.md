# Private Law 649

> AN ACT For the relief of Ingeborg Bogner Johnson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Ingeborg Bogner Johnson shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the en- actment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fee: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the said Act.


*Approved August 13, 1954.*

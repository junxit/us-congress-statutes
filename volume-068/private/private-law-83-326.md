# Private Law 326

> AN ACT For the relief of Manfred Singer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Manfred Singer, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Bertram Morton Singer, citizen of the United States.


*Approved April 15, 1954.*

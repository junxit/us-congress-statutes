# Private Law 334

> AN ACT For the relief of Margherita Di Meo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Margherita Di Meo, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Anthony Di Meo, citizens of the United States.


*Approved April 17, 1954.*

# Private Law 726

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Helen Stryk.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 212 (a) (6) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Helen Stryk may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of such Act: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the said Act.


*Approved August 23, 1954.*

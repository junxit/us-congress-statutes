# Private Law 735

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Wai-Jan Low Fong.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Wai-Jan Low Fong shall be held and considered to be a nonquota returning resident alien, as defined by section 101 (a) (27) (B) of that Act.


*Approved August 23, 1954.*

# Private Law 946

> AN ACT For the relief of Francesco Pugliese.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Francesco Pugliese, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Harold P. Koblens, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 31, 1954.*

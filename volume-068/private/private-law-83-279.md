# Private Law 279

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Gisela Walter Sizemore.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of section 212 (a) (6) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Gisela Walter Sizemore may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of that Act.


*Approved March 10, 1954.*

# Private Law 362

> AN ACT For the relief of Theresa Mire Piantoni.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Theresa Mire Piantoni, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Nicolas Piantoni, citizens of the United States.


*Approved May 17, 1954.*

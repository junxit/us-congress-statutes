# Private Law 241

> AN ACT For the relief of Rufin Manikowski.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 202 (a) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Rufin Manikowski shall be held and considered to have been born in Germany.


*Approved February 20, 1954.*

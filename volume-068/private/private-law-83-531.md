# Private Law 531

> AN ACT For the relief of Borivoje Vulich.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of American Congress assembled,*

That Borivoje Vulich, a Yugoslav flier, who served honorably with the Fifteenth Air Force of the United States, and who died August 22, 1944, while engaged in a bombing mission over enemy territory, shall be held and considered to have been a citizen of the United States at the time of his death.


*Approved July 15, 1954.*

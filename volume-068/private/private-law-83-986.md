# Private Law 986

> AN ACT For the relief of Bonita Lee Simpson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Bonita Lee Simpson shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Lester A. Simpson.


*Approved September 1, 1954.*

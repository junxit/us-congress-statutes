# Private Law 507

> AN ACT For the relief of Mr. and Mrs. Ivan S. Aylesworth.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of American Congress assembled,*

That Mr. and Mrs. Ivan S. Aylesworth, who lost United States citizenship under the provisions of section 401 (e) of the Nationality Act of 1940, as amended, may be naturalized by taking, prior to one year after the effective date of this Act, before any court referred to in subsection (a) of section 310 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, or before any diplomatic or consular officer of the United States abroad, the oath prescribed by section 337 of the Immigration and Nationality Act. From and after naturalization under this Act, the said Mr. and Mrs. Ivan S. Aylesworth shall have the same citizenship status as that which existed immediately prior to its loss.


*Approved July 14, 1954.*

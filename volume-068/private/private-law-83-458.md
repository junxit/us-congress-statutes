# Private Law 458

> AN ACT For the relief of Frank Bastinelle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Frank Bastinelle, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. Lorenzo Fortuna, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved June 28, 1954.*

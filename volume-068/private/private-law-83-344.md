# Private Law 344

> AN ACT For the relief of Dora Vida Lyew Seixas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of subsection (b) of section 202 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Dora Vida Lyew Seixas shall be classified as an immigrant under the provisions of section 101 (a) (27) (C) of that Act.


*Approved April 19, 1954.*

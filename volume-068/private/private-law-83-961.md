# Private Law 961

> AN ACT For the relief of Marla Busa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 203 (a) (3) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Maria Busa shall be held and considered to be the minor child of Giovanni Busa, an alien lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence.


*Approved September 1, 1954.*

# Private Law 777

> AN ACT For the relief of Michael Henry LaFleur.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Michael Henry LaFleur, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mrs. Electa D. LaFleur, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 24, 1954.*

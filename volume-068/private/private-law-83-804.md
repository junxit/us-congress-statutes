# Private Law 804

> AN ACT For the relief of Louie Ella Attaway.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Louie Ella Attaway, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of William R. Attaway, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 26, 1954.*

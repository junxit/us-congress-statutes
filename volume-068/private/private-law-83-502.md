# Private Law 502

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Olympia Cuc.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of American Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Olympia Cue shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fee.


*Approved July 6, 1954.*

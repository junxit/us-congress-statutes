# Private Law 351

> AN ACT For the relief of Petra Fumia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Petra Fumia, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Angelo Ruta, citizens of the United States.


*Approved May 6, 1954.*

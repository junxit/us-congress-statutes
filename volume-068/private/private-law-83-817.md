# Private Law 817

> AN ACT For the relief of Natale Joseph John Ratti.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the provisions of section 101 (a) (27) (B) should be held and considered to be applicable to Natale Joseph John Ratti.


*Approved August 26, 1954.*

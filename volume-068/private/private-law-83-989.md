# Private Law 989

> AN ACT For the relief of Cleophat Robert Joseph Caron.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of sections 212 (a) (9), (17) and (19) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Cleophat Robert Joseph Caron may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if he is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of such Act: Provided, That this exemption shall apply only to grounds for exclusion of which the Department of State or the Department of Justice has knowledge prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved September 1, 1954.*

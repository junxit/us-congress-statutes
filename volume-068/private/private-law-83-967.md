# Private Law 967

> AN ACT For the relief of John Enepekides, his wife, Anna, and his son, George.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provision in the second sentence of subsection (c) of section 316 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, modifying the requirements of subsection (b) for an uninterrupted period of at least one year of physical presence in the United States, shall be applicable to John Enepekides, his wife, Anna, and his son, George.


*Approved September 1, 1954.*

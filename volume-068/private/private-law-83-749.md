# Private Law 749

> AN ACT For the relief of Nicoletta Di Donato.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purpose of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Nicoletta Di Donato, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Donato Di Donato, citizens of the United States.


*Approved August 23, 1954.*

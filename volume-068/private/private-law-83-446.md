# Private Law 446

> AN ACT To authorize the advancement of certain lieutenants on the retired list of the Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding any other provision of law, Lieutenant Harold Edgar Peifer, United States Navy (retired), Lieutenant Jim Tom Acree, United States Navy (retired), Lieutenant Harold Coldwell, United States Navy (retired), Lieutenant Thomas Tingey Craven, United States Navy (retired), and Lieutenant Charles Samuel Boarman, United States Navy (retired), shall he advanced to the grade of lieutenant commander on the retired list effective for all purposes from the date of retirement in each case.


*Approved June 22, 1954.*

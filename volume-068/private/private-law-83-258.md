# Private Law 258

> AN ACT For the relief of Lina Anna Adelheid (Adam) Hoyer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Lina Anna Adelheid (Adam) Hoyer shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fee.


*Approved February 27, 1954.*

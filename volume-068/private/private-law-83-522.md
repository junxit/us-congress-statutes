# Private Law 522

> AN ACT For the relief of Walter Kuznicki.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of American Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of section 212 (a) (9) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Walter Kuznicki may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if he is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of such Act: Provided, That this exemption shall apply only to a ground for exclusion of which the Department of State or the Department of Justice has knowledge prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved July 14, 1954.*

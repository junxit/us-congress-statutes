# Private Law 980

> AN ACT For the relief of Kim Kwang Suk and Kim Woo Shik.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor children, Kim Kwang Suk and Kim Woo Shik, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien children of Mr. Kenneth N. Roberts, a citizen of the United States, and his wife.


*Approved September 1, 1954.*

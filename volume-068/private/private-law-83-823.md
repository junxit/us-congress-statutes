# Private Law 823

> AN ACT For the relief of Maria Teresa Lubiato.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Maria Teresa Lubiato, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Erminio Lubiato, citizens of the United States.


*Approved August 26, 1954.*

# Private Law 849

> AN ACT For the relief of Dana Evanovich.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Dana Evanovich shall be held and considered to be the minor child of Jim D. Evans, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 26, 1954.*

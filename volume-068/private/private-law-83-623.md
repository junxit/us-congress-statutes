# Private Law 623

> AN ACT For the relief of Michajlo Dzieczko.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of American Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Michajlo Dzieczko shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon the payment of the required visa fee.


*Approved August 13, 1954.*

# Private Law 651

> AN ACT For the relief of Jeannette Kalker and Abraham Benjamin Kalker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203 (a) (3) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor children, Jeannette Kalker and Abraham Benjamin Kalker, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien children of Mr. Abraham Benjamin Konijn, a lawful permanent resident of the United States, and his wife.


*Approved August 13, 1954.*

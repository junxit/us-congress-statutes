# Private Law 786

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Liang Nun Wang and his wife and child, Fa-chi Ling Wang and Eileen Wang.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Liang Nun Wang shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the elate of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fee. Upon the granting of permanent residence to such alien as provided for in this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the appropriate quota for the first year that such quota is available.

## § 2.

In the administration of the Refugee Relief Act of 1953, Fa-chi Ling Wang and her child, Eileen Wang, shall be held to be classifiable as refugees in accordance with the provisions of section 4 (a) (12) of that Act, and shall be exempt from the requirements of section 7 (d) thereof.


*Approved August 24, 1954.*

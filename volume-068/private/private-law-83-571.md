# Private Law 571

> AN ACT For the relief of Albina Sicas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of American Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 212 (a) (4) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Albina Sicas may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of such Act: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the said Act: And provided further, That the said Albina Sicas shall be held and considered to be the minor child of her mother, Mrs. Hilda Sicas.


*Approved July 28, 1954.*

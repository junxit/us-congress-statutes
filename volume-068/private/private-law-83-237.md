# Private Law 237

> AN ACT For the relief of Tamiko Nagae.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Tamiko Nagae, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of John C. Frame and Viola C. Frame, citizens of the United States.


*Approved February 20, 1954.*

# Private Law 850

> AN ACT For the relief of Paul James Patrie.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of section 212 (a) (6) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Paul James Patrie may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if he is round to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of such Act: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of such Act.


*Approved August 26, 1954.*

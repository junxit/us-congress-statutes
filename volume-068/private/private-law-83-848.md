# Private Law 848

> AN ACT For the relief of Ezio Bertoni.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstand-ing the provision of section 212 (a) (3) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Ezio Bertoni may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if he is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the said Act.


*Approved August 26, 1954.*

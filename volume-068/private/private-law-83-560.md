# Private Law 560

> AN ACT For the relief of Nicole Goldman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of American Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of section 212 (a) (1) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Nicole Goldman may lie admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the said Act.


*Approved July 26, 1954.*

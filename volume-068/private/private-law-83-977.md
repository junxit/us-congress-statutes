# Private Law 977

> AN ACT For the relief of Peter Julian Newbery and Prudence Ellen Newbery.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 203 (a) (1) (B) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Peter Julian Newbery and Prudence Ellen Newbery shall be held and considered to be the natural-born children of Charles Bruce Newbery and regarded as having accompanied him at the time of his admission for permanent residence under section 203 (a) (1) of the said Act: Provided, That they obtain appropriate immigrant visas and apply for admission into the United States within six months following the date of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved September 1, 1954.*

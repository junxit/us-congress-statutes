# Private Law 973

> AN ACT For the relief of Esther Joanne Potter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Esther Joanne Potter, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Gail M. Potter, citizens of the United States.


*Approved September 1, 1954.*

# Private Law 966

> AN ACT For the relief of Francesco Marinelli.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Francesco Marinelli shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Master Sergeant Russell A. Lachmiller, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved September 1, 1954.*

# Private Law 870

> AN ACT For the relief of Patricia A. Pembroke.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 15 to 20, inclusive, of the Federal Employees’ Compensation Act are hereby waived in favor of Patricia A. Pembroke, of San Francisco, California, and her claim for compensation for disability from disease allegedly contracted in 1944 while in the performance of her duty as a payroll clerk in the Department of the Army, Civilian Personnel Office, San Francisco, California, is hereby authorized and directed to be acted upon under the remaining provisions of such Act, if she files such claim with the Bureau of Employees’ Compensation, Department of Labor, within sixty days after the enactment of this Act: Provided, That no benefits shall accrue prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved August 28, 1954.*

# Private Law 665

> AN ACT For the relief of Fernando A. Rubio, Junior.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 315 of title III of the Immigration and Nationality Act, or any other provision of law making ineligible to become a citizen of the United States any alien who has applied for exemption or discharge from training or service in the Armed Forces or in the National Security Training Corps of the United States on the ground that he is an alien, Doctor Fernando A. Rubio, Junior, who is now serving as a captain in the United States Army, may be naturalized upon compliance with all other provisions of title III of such Act.


*Approved August 16, 1954.*

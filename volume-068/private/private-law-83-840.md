# Private Law 840

> AN ACT For the relief of Erika Schneider Buonasera.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of section 212 (a) (9) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Erika Schneider Buonasera may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provivions of that Act: Provided, That this exemption shall apply only to a ground for exclusion of which the Department of State or the Department of Justice had knowledge prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved August 26, 1954.*

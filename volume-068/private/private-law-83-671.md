# Private Law 671

> AN ACT For the relief of Fred and Bernice Ehlers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of section 322 of the Internal Revenue Code (relating to refunds and credits), the claim for refund of income tax for the taxable year ending December 31, 1948, mailed by Fred and Bernice Ehlers, of Hettinger, North Dakota, on March 15, 1952, and received in the office of the director of internal revenue, Fargo, North Dakota, on March 19, 1952, shall be held and considered to have been filed within the three-year period referred to in section 322 (b) (1) of the Internal Revenue Code.


*Approved August 18, 1954.*

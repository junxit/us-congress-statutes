# Private Law 313

> AN ACT For the relief of Silva Galjevscek.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Silva Galjevscek shall be held and considered to be the minor child of her parents, Franz and Leopolda Galjevscek, lawful permanent residents of the United States.


*Approved March 30, 1954.*

# Private Law 764

> AN ACT For the relief of Roger Feghali.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 5 (a) of the Refugee Relief Act of 1953, Roger Feghali shall be held and considered to be under ten years of age.


*Approved August 23, 1954.*

# Private Law 309

> AN ACT For the relief of Vera Lazaros and Cristo Lazaros.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor children, Vera Lazaros and Cristo Lazaros, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien children of Mr. and Mrs. Louis Lazaros, citizens of the United States.


*Approved March 30, 1954.*

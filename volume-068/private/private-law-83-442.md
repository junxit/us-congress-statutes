# Private Law 442

> AN ACT For the relief of Hildegard Monti.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 212 (a) (9) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Hildegard Monti may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of such Act: Provided, That this exemption shall apply only to a ground of exclusion of which the Department of State or the Department of Justice has knowledge prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved June 22, 1954.*

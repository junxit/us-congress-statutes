# Private Law 987

> AN ACT For the relief of Ingeborg Otto.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of section 212 (a) (7) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Ingeborg Otto may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited in accordance with section 213 of the said Act.


*Approved September 1, 1954.*

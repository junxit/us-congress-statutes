# Private Law 229

> AN ACT For the relief of Katina Panagioti Fifflis and Theodore Panagiotou Fifflis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor children, Katina Panagioti Fifflis and Theodore Panagiotou Fifflis, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien children of Nicholas Th. and Maria Fifths, citizens of the United States.


*Approved February 20, 1954.*

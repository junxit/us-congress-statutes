# House Concurrent Resolution 250

> “INTERNAL REVENUE CODE OF 1954”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed twelve thousand five hundred and ninety additional copies of the slip law for the Internal Revenue Code of 1954, of which two thousand four hundred and seventy-five copies shall be for the use of the Senate, five hundred copies for the use of the Committee on Finance, six thousand six hundred and fifteen copies for the use of the House of Representatives, and three thousand copies for the use of the Committee on Ways and Means.


*Passed July 14, 1954.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 89

> “INTERNAL REVENUE CODE OF 1954”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed seven thousand two hundred additional copies of Senate Report Numbered 1622, current Congress, entitled “Internal Revenue Code of 1954”, of which two thousand copies shall be for the Senate document room; fifteen hundred copies for the use of the Committee on Finance; two thousand copies for the House document room; fifteen hundred copies for the Committee on Ways and Means; and two hundred copies for the Joint Committee on Internal Revenue Taxation.


*Passed June 22, 1954.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 106

> ALASKA: FEDERAL DECLARATORY JUDGMENTS ACT

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the action of the Speaker of the House of Representatives in signing the enrolled bill (H. R. 1975) to amend section 2201 of title 28, United States Code, to extend the Federal Declaratory Judgments Act to the Territory of Alaska, be rescinded, and that the Clerk of the House be and he is hereby authorized and directed, in the reenrollment of the bill, to make the following correction:

On page 1, line 6 of the engrossed House bill, strike out the word “section” and in lieu thereof insert the word “sentence”.


*Agreed to August 17, 1954.*

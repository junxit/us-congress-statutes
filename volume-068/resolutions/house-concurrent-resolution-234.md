# House Concurrent Resolution 234

> CAPITOL ROTUNDA HISTORIC FRIEZE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the proceedings, held in the rotunda of the United States Capitol at the illumination of the new portion and dedication of the historic frieze, be printed, with appropriate illustrations, in such form and style as may be directed by the Joint Committee on Printing, as a House document; and that five thousand copies be printed, of which four thousand copies shall be for the use of the House of Representatives and one thousand copies shall be for the use of the Senate.


*Passed May 27, 1954.*

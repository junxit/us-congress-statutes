# House Concurrent Resolution 213

> HEALTH PROBLEMS

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Interstate and Foreign Commerce, House of Representatives, fifteen hundred additional copies each of parts 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5 of the hearings held by the said committee during the Eighty-third Congress relative to health problems.


*Passed May 27, 1954.*

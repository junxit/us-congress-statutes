# House Concurrent Resolution 210

> “ORGANIZED COMMUNISM IN THE UNITED STATES”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed thirty-five thousand additional copies or the report entitled “Organized Communism in the United States”; and that five thousand copies shall be for the use of the Committee on Un-American Activities and thirty thousand copies to be prorated to the Members of the House of Representatives for a period of ninety days, after which time the unused balance shall revert to the Committee on Un-American Activities.


*Passed May 27, 1954.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 224

> ADJOURNMENT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Thursday, April 15, 1954, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian Monday, April 26, 1954.


*Passed April 14, 1954.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 97

> STRATEGIC AND CRITICAL MATERIALS

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed one thousand additional copies of part 6 of the hearings conducted before a subcommittee of the Senate Committee on Interior and Insular Affairs pursuant to S. Res. 143, Eighty-third Congress, relative to stockpile and accessibility of strategic ana critical materials to the United States in time of war. Such additional copies shall be for the use of the Senate Committee on Interior and Insular Affairs.


*Agreed to August 4, 1954.*

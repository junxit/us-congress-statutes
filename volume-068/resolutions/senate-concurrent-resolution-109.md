# Senate Concurrent Resolution 109

> SIGNING OF ENROLLED BILLS, ETC.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That, notwithstanding the sine die adjournment of the House of Representatives and the temporary adjournment of the Senate, the Speaker of the House of Representatives and the President of the Senate be, and they are hereby, authorized to sign enrolled bills and joint resolutions duly passed by the two Houses and found truly enrolled.


*Agreed to August 20, 1954.*

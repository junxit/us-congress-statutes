# House Concurrent Resolution 232

> “INTERNAL REVENUE CODE OF 1954”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed one thousand additional copies of the hearings on H. R. 8300 (Internal Revenue Code of 1954) held by the Senate Committee on Finance for the use of the Committee on Ways and Means.


*Passed May 5, 1954.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 80

> “REVIEW OF THE UNITED NATIONS CHARTER-A COLLECTION OF DOCUMENTS”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed three thousand additional copies of Senate Document 87. Eighty-third Congress, second session, Review of the United Nations Charter—A Collection of Documents; one thousand copies for the use of the Committee on Foreign Relations and two thousand copies for the Members of the House of Representatives.


*Agreed to July 19, 1954.*

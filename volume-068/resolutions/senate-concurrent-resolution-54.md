# Senate Concurrent Resolution 54

> “TREATIES AND EXECUTIVE AGREEMENTS”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on the Judiciary two thousand additional copies of the hearings relative to Treaties and Executive Agreements held before a subcommittee of the above committee during the Eighty-third Congress, first session.


*Agreed to January 20, 1954.*

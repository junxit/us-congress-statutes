# Senate Concurrent Resolution 107

> TRANSFER OF LAND TO OREGON

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Clerk of the House of Representatives, in the enrollment of the bill (H. R. 8020), entitled “An Act authorizing the transfer of certain property of the United States Government (in Klamath County, Oregon) to the State of Oregon”, is authorized and directed to make the following correction: In the matter added by the Senate amendment, strike out “section 203 (d) (2) (D)” and insert in lieu thereof “section 203 (k) (2) (I))”.


*Agreed to August 17, 1954.*

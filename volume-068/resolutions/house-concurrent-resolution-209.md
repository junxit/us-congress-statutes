# House Concurrent Resolution 209

> COMMERCIAL-AND INDUSTRIAL-TYPE ACTIVITIES IN GOVERNMENT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Government Operations of the House of Representatives five hundred additional copies of parts 1 and 2 of the hearings held by the said committee during the Eighty-third Congress, first session, relative to commercial and industrial-type activities in the Federal Government, with, in part 1, a brief statement by the chairman of the full committee which was inadvertently omitted from the hearings.


*Passed May 27, 1954.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 267

> CONTRIBUTION OF ATOMIC ENERGY TO MEDICINE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Joint Committee on Atomic Energy twenty-five thousand additional copies of the hearings held by the said joint committee during the current Congress, relative to the contribution of atomic energy to medicine.


*Passed August 12, 1954.*

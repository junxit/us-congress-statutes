# Senate Concurrent Resolution 98

> “ACTIVITIES OF UNITED STATES CITIZENS EMPLOYED BY THE UNITED NATIONS”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on the Judiciary an additional twenty thousand copies of the second interim report entitled “Activities of United States Citizens Employed by the United Nations”, a report of hearings held before a subcommittee of the above committee during the Eighty-third Congress.


*Agreed to August 4, 1954.*

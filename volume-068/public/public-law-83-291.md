# Public Law 291

> AN ACT To continue the effectiveness of the Missing Persons Act, as extended, until July 1, 1955.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 15, Missing Persons Act (56 Stat. 147, 1093), as amended by subsection 1 (f). Act of April 4, 1953 (Public Law 16, Eighty-third Congress), is amended by deleting the word “February 1, 1954”, and inserting in lieu thereof “July 1, 1955”.


*Approved January 30, 1954.*

# Public Law 380

> AN ACT To honor veterans on the 11th day of November of each year, a day dedicated to world peace.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act entitled “An Act making the 11th day of November in each year a legal holiday”, approved May 13, 1938 (52 Stat. 351; 5 U. S. C., sec. 87a), is hereby amended by striking out the word “Armistice” and inserting in lieu thereof the word “Veterans”.


*Approved June 1, 1954.*

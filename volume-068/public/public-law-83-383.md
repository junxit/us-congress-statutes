# Public Law 383

> AN ACT To repeal section 307 of title III of the Federal Civil Defense Act of 1950. as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 307 of the Federal Civil Defense Act of 1950, as amended (50 U. S. C. App. 2297), is amended by striking out the date “June 30, 1954” and inserting in lieu thereof the date “June 30, 1958”.


*Approved June 3, 1954.*

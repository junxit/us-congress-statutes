# Public Law 411

> AN ACT To amend the Act of June 19, 1948, to provide for censuses of manufactures, mineral industries, and other businesses, relating to the year 1954.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1 (a) of the Act of June 19, 1948 (62 Stat. 478), is amended by the addition of the following proviso: “Provided further, That the censuses of manufactures, of mineral industries, and of other businesses, including the distributive trades and service establishments, directed to be taken in the year 1954 relating to the year 1953, shall be taken instead in the year 1955 relating to year 1954”.


*Approved June 18, 1954.*

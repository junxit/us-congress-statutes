# Public Law 509

> AN ACT To authorize the Secretary of the Army to convey to the Government’s grantors certain lands erroneously conveyed by them to the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Army is authorized to convey by quitclaim deed to each grantor of the United States all of the right, title, and interest of the United States in and to those lands which were, through mistake, misunderstanding, error, or inadvertence conveyed, with certain other lands, to the United States by such grantor in connection with the expansion of the military reservation now known as the Camp Drum Military Reservation, New York, upon request of each such grantor.


*Approved July 19, 1954.*

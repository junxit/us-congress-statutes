# Public Law 432

> AN ACT To authorize the appropriation of additional funds to complete the International Peace Garden, North Dakota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first section of the Act entitled “An Act to authorize an appropriation to complete the International Peace Garden, North Dakota”, approved October 25, 1949 (63 Stat. 888), is amended by striking out “$100,000” and by inserting in lieu thereof “$200,000”.


*Approved June 28, 1954.*

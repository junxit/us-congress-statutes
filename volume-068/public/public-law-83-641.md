# Public Law 641

> AN ACT To amend title 18 of the United States Code, so as to increase the penalties applicable to the smuggling of goods into the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 545 of title 18, United States Code, is amended by striking out “$5,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$10,000”, and by striking out “two years” and inserting in lieu thereof “five years”.

## § 2.

The amendments made by the first section of this Act shall apply only with respect to offenses committed on and after the date of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved August 24, 1954.*

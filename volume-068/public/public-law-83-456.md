# Public Law 456

> AN ACT To repeal section 4 of the Act of March 2, 1934, creating the Model Housing Board of Puerto Rico.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4 of the Act of March 2, 1934 (48 Stat. 361; 48 U. S. C. 793a), is hereby repealed.

## § 2.

Any moneys remaining in the revolving fund established by subsection (a) of section 4 of said Act of March 2, 1934, shall be covered into the treasury of the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico.


*Approved June 30, 1954.*

# Public Law 498

> AN ACT To amend section 12 of the Alaska Public Works Act, approved August 24, 1949 (63 Stat. 629).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 12 of the Alaska Public Works Act, approved August 24, 1949 (63 Stat. 629), is amended to read as follows:

> ## § 12.
>
> The authority of the Secretary under this Act to provide public works and to enter into agreements with applicants in connection therewith shall terminate on June 30, 1959, or on the date he obligates for such purposes the total amount authorized to be appropriated hereunder, whichever first occurs.”


*Approved July 15, 1954.*

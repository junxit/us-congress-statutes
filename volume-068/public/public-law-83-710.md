# Public Law 710

> AN ACT To change the name of Gavins Point Reservoir back of Gavins Point Dam to Lewis and Clark Lake.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Gavins Point Reservoir back of Gavins Point Dam, which is located on the Missouri River near Yankton, South Dakota, shall hereafter be known as Lewis and Clark Lake and any law, regulation, document, or record of the United States in which such reservoir is designated or referred to under the name of Gavins Point Reservoir shall be held to refer to such body of water under and by the name of Lewis and Clark Lake.


*Approved August 30, 1954.*

# Public Law 638

> AN ACT To extend the coverage of the Servicemen’s Indemnity Act to members of the Reserve Officers’ Training Corps when ordered to active training duty for periods in excess of fourteen days.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2 of the Servicemen’s Indemnity Act of 1951 is amended by inserting immediately after “including the National Guard when called or ordered to active duty or active training duty for fourteen days or more;” the following: “members of the Reserve Officers’ Training Corps, the Naval Reserve Officers’ Training Corps, and the Air Force Reserve Officers’ Training Corps, when called or ordered to active training duty for fourteen days or more while on such active training duty;”.

## § 2.

The amendment made by this Act shall take effect as of April 25, 1951.


*Approved August 24, 1954.*

# Public Law 520

> AN ACT To amend section 24 of the Federal Reserve Act, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the fourth paragraph of section 24 of the Federal Reserve Act, as amended (U. S. C., 1952 edition, title 12, sec. 371), is amended by adding in clause (d) the words “or the Small Business Administration” after the words “the Housing and Home Finance Administrator”, and by adding the words “or of the Small Business Act of 1953,” after the words “Housing Act of 1948, as amended,”.


*Approved July 22, 1954.*

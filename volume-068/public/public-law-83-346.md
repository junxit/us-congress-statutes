# Public Law 346

> AN ACT To extend the period of election under the Uniformed Services Contingency Option Act of 1953 for certain members of the uniformed services.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3 (a) of the Uniformed Services Contingency Option Act of 1953 is amended by deleting in the third sentence the words “one hundred and eighty days” and substituting therefor the words “one year”.


*Approved April 29, 1954.*

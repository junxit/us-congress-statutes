# Public Law 394

> JOINT RESOLUTION To extend the time for the erection of a memorial to the memory of Mohandas K. Gandhi.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of September 28, 1949 (63 Stat. 699, ch. 587), relating to the erection of a memorial to the memory of Mohandas K. Gandhi is hereby amended by striking out the words “within five years from the date of approval of this resolution” and substituting in lieu thereof the words “by September 28, 1959”.


*Approved June 10, 1954.*

# Public Law 439

> AN ACT To amend the Act of August 30, 1935 (49 Stat. 1049), authorizing the Chippewa Indians of Wisconsin to submit claims to the Court of Claims.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second proviso in section 3 of the Act of August 30, 1935 (49 Stat. 1049, 1050), entitled “An Act authorizing the Chippewa Indians of Wisconsin to submit claims to the Court of Claims”, is hereby amended by deleting “5 per centum” and by inserting in lieu thereof “10 per centum”.


*Approved June 29, 1954.*

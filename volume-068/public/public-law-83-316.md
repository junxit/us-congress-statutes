# Public Law 316

> AN ACT To repeal the act entitled “An Act to authorize the Director of the Census to collect and publish statistics of red cedar shingles.”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act entitled “An Act to authorize the Director of the Census to collect and publish statistics of red-cedar shingles,” approved May 25, 1937 (50 Stat. 204, 205) be, and it is hereby, repealed.


*Approved March 26, 1954.*

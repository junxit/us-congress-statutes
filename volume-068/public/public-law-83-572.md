# Public Law 572

> AN ACT Relating to the renewal of star-route and screen vehicle service contracts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first sentence of the next to last paragraph of section 3951 of the revised statutes, as amended (39 U. S. C. 434) is amended by striking out the words “one year” and inserting in lieu thereof the words “six months”.


*Approved August 10, 1954.*

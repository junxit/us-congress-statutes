# Public Law 418

> AN ACT To approve Act Numbered 27 of the Session Laws of 1951 of the Territory of Hawaii, entitled “An Act to amend Act 24 of the Session Laws of Hawaii 1927, as ratified by the Act of Congress of March 2, 1928, so as to extend the electric light and power franchise granted by said Act to cover the entire districts of Waimea and Koloa on the Island of Kauai, Territory of Hawaii”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Act Numbered 27 of the Session Laws of 1951 of the Territory of Hawaii, entitled “An Act to amend Act 24 of the Session Laws of Hawaii 1927, as ratified by the Act of Congress of March 2, 1928, so as to extend the electric light and power franchise granted by said Act to cover the entire districts of Waimea and Koloa on the Island of Kauai. Territory of Hawaii,” passed by the Legislature of the Territory of Hawaii and approved by the Governor of the Territory of Hawaii on May 4, 1951, is hereby approved.


*Approved June 18, 1954.*

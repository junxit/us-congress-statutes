# Public Law 592

> AN ACT To amend section 405 of the District of Columbia Law Enforcement Act of 1953, to make available to the judges of such District the psychiatric and psychological services provided for in such section.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 405 of the District of Columbia Law Enforcement Act of 1953 is amended by striking “(1) The probation officers” and inserting in lieu thereof “(1) In criminal cases, the judges of the district court and the probation officers”.


*Approved August 16, 1954.*

# Public Law 687

> AN ACT Authorizing the Secretary of the Interior to adjust or cancel certain charges on the Milk River project.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior may, in his discretion and notwithstanding the provision of any other law, adjust or cancel any charges, including penalties, which have accrued, or which will hereafter accrue, under Public Notice Numbered 5, Milk River project, Montana.


*Approved August 28, 1954.*

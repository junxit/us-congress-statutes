# Public Law 645

> AN ACT To repeal the requirement of section 3921 of the Revised Statutes that postmasters report to the Postmaster General failure to cancel postage stamps.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the part ofsection 3921 of the Revised Statutes which is codified in section 366 of title 39, United States Code, is hereby amended by striking out the comma following the word “them” and the words “and report the delinquent postmaster to the Postmaster General”.


*Approved August 24, 1954.*

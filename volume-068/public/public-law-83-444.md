# Public Law 444

> AN ACT To amend the Act of October 31, 1949 (63 Stat. 1049).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1, of subsection (b), of the Act of October 31, 1949 (63 Stat. 1049), is hereby amended by deleting the figure “1954”, wherever the same appears in the third and fourth provisos, and by inserting in lieu thereof the figure “1957”; and by deleting the figure “1953” in the fourth proviso, and by inserting in lieu thereof the figure “1956”.


*Approved June 29, 1954.*

# Public Law 735

> AN ACT Authorizing the payment of salary to any individual given a recess appointment as Comptroller General of the United States before the beginning of the Eighty-fourth Congress.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding section 1761, as amended, of the Revised Statutes, any individual appointed by the President to the office of Comptroller General of the United States before the beginning of the first regular session of the Eighty-fourth Congress may be paid the salary for such office as provided by law.

## § 2.

This Act shall cease to be in effect upon the forty-first day following the beginning of the first regular session of the Eighty-fourth Congress unless prior to such day the President shall have submitted to the Senate a nomination to fill such office.


*Approved August 31, 1954.*

# Public Law 644

> AN ACT To repeal a limitation on pay of certain officers of the Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That fleet admirals of the Navy, appointed under the Act of March 23, 1946 (60 Stat. 59), shall be considered to be retired naval officers for the purposes of the last proviso of the paragraph under the heading “Pay of the Navy” contained in the Act of June 10, 1896 (29 Stat. 361), as that proviso was amended by section 9 of the Act of July 22, 1935 (49 Stat. 490; 34 U. S. C. 883).


*Approved August 24, 1954.*

# Public Law 545

> AN ACT To amend the Act of June 29, 1935 (the Bankhead-Jones Act), as amended, to strengthen the conduct of research of the Department of Agriculture.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of June 29, 1935 (the Bankhead-Jones Act), as amended (7 U. S. C. 427–427j), is amended by adding at the end of section 10 thereof the following:

> - **(e)** Appropriations for research work in the Department of Agriculture shall be available for accomplishing such purposes by contract through the means provided in subsection (a) hereof.”


*Approved July 28, 1954.*

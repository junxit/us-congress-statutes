# Public Law 700

> JOINT RESOLUTION Fixing the time of assembly of the Eighty-fourth Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Eighty-fourth Congress shall assemble at noon on Wednesday, January 5, 1955.


*Approved August 28, 1954.*

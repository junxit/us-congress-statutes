# Public Law 293

> JOINT RESOLUTION Amending Public Law 207, Eighty-third Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limitation on the amount available for expenses of travel under the appropriation for “Salaries and expenses” of the Commission on Organization of the Executive Branch of the Government, contained in chapter VII of the Supplemental Appropriations Act of 1954 (Public Law 207, Eighty-third Congress, approved August 7, 1953) is hereby increased to $60,000.


*Approved February 9, 1954.*

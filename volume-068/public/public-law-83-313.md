# Public Law 313

> AN ACT To amend the Army-Navy Medical Service Corps Act of 1947 relating to the per centum of colonels in the Medical Service Corps, Regular Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Army-Navy Medical Services Corps Act of 1947 (61 Stat. 734), as amended, is hereby amended by deleting the proviso to section 101 and substituting a period for the colon immediately preceding such proviso.


*Approved March 23, 1954.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 14

> ADJOURNMENT—SENATE AND HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That when the Senate recesses on Thursday, February 9, 1989, at the conclusion of the joint session to receive a message from the President, it stand in recess until 2:15 post meridiem on Tuesday, February 21, 1989, or until 12 o’clock meridian on the second day after Members are notified to reassemble pursuant to section 2 of this resolution; and that when the House adjourns on Thursday, February 9, 1989, it stand adjourned until 12:00 o’clock meridian on Tuesday, February 21, 1989, or until 12 o’clock meridian on the second day after Members are notified to reassemble pursuant to section 2 of this resolution.

## § 2.

The Majority Leader of the Senate and the Speaker of the House, acting jointly after consultation with the Minority Leader of the Senate and the Minority Leader of the House, shall notify the Members of the Senate and the House, respectively, to reassemble whenever, in their opinion, the public interest shall warrant it.


*Agreed to February 9, 1989.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 23

> JOINT COMMITTEE OF THE CONGRESS ON THE LIBRARY—DESIGNATION OF MEMBER

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That effective for the One Hundred First Congress, the Chairman of the Committee on Rules and Administration of the Senate may designate another member of the Committee to serve on the Joint Committee of the Congress on the Library in place of the Chairman.


*Agreed to March 21, 1989.*

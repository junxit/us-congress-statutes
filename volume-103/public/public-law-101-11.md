# Public Law 101–11

> An Act To make permanent the authority provided under the Temporary Emergency Wildfire Suppression Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. SHORT TITLE.

This Act may be cited as the “Wildfire Suppression Assistance Act”

## § 2. PERMANENT AUTHORITY.

The Temporary Emergency Wildfire Suppression Act (Public Law 100–428) is amended by repealing section 5.


*Approved April 7, 1989.*

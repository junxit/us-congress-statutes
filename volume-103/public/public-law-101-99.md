# Public Law 101–99

> An Act To authorize appropriations for the American Folklife Center for fiscal years 1990, 1991, and 1992.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 8 of the American Folklife Preservation Act (20 U.S.C. 2107) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking out “and ” after “1988”; and
- **(2)** by inserting after “1989” the following: “, $998,000 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1990, $1,050,100 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1991, and $1,120,000 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1992”.


*Approved September 26, 1989.*

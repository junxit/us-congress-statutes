# Public Law 101–199

> An Act To designate the building located at 2562 Hylan Boulevard, Staten Island, New York, as the “Walter Edward Grady United States Post Office Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the building located at 2562 Hylan Boulevard, Staten Island, New York, known as the New Dorp Station, is designated as the “Walter Edward Grady United States Post Office Building”. Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to that building shall be deemed to be a reference to the Walter Edward Grady United States Post Office Building.


*Approved December 6, 1989.*

# Public Law 101–87

> An Act To extend by 1 year a program under which the Government is allowed to accept the voluntary services of private-sector executives.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3(a) of the Executive Exchange Program Voluntary Services Act of 1986 (5 U.S.C. 4103 note) is amended by striking “1989” and inserting “1990”.


*Approved August 16, 1989.*

# Public Law 101–120

> An Act To amend the Head Start Act to increase the amount authorized to be appropriated for fiscal year 1990.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. SHORT TITLE.

This Act may be cited as the “Head Start Supplemental Authorization Act of 1989”.

## § 2. AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS.

Section 639 of the Head Start Act (42 U.S.C. 9834) is amended by striking “$1,405,000,000” and inserting “$1,552,000,000”.


*Approved October 23, 1989.*

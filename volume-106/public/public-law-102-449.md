# Public Law 102–449

> An Act To designate the Department of Veterans Affairs medical center in Marlin, Texas, as the Thomas T. Connally Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The Department of Veterans Affairs medical center in Marlin, Texas, is designated as the “Thomas T. Connally Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center”.

## § 2. LEGAL REFERENCES.

Any reference in any law, regulation, document, record, map, or other paper of the United States to the medical center referred to in section 1 is deemed to be a reference to the “Thomas T. Connally Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center”.


*Approved October 23, 1992.*

# Public Law 102–310

> An Act To provide a 4-month extension of the transition rule for separate capitalization of savings associations’ subsidiaries.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 5(t)(5)(D)(ii) of the Home Owners’ Loan Act (12 U.S.C. 1464(t)(5)(D)(ii)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “June 30, 1992” and inserting “October 31, 1992”; and
- **(2)** by striking “July 1, 1992” and inserting “November 1, 1992”.


*Approved July 1, 1992.*

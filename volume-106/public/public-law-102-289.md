# Public Law 102–289

> An Act To provide flexibility to the Secretary of Agriculture to carry out food assistance programs in certain countries.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. USE OF FOREIGN CURRENCY PROCEEDS.

Section 416(b)(7)(D)(iv) of the Agricultural Act of 1949 (7 U.S.C. 1431(b)(7)(D)(iv)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “and (II)” and inserting “(II)”; and
- **(2)** by striking the final period and inserting “, and (III) in a country other than the country of origin, if such proceeds are generated in a currency generally accepted in such other country.”.


*Approved May 20, 1992.*

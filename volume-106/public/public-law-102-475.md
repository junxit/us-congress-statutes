# Public Law 102–475

> Joint Resolution Providing for the convening of the first session of the One Hundred Third Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first regular session of the One Hundred Third Congress shall begin at noon on Tuesday, January 5, 1993.


*Approved October 23, 1992.*

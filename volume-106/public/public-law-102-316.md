# Public Law 102–316

> Joint Resolution To extend through September 30, 1992, the period in which there remains available for obligation certain amounts appropriated for the Bureau of Indian Affairs for the school operations costs of Bureau-funded schools.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding Public Law 101–512, amounts appropriated in such Public Law for the Bureau of Indian Affairs for school operations costs of Bureau-funded schools shall remain available for obligation through September 30, 1992.


*Approved July 2, 1992.*

# Public Law 102–312

> An Act To designate the Federal building located at 1520 Market Street, St. Louis, Missouri, as the “L. Douglas Abram Federal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Federal building located at 1520 Market Street, St. Louis, Missouri, shall hereafter be known and designated as the “L. Douglas Abram Federal Building”. Any reference to such building in any law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States shall be deemed to be a reference to the “L. Douglas Abram Federal Building”.


*Approved July 2, 1992.*

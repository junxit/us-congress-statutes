# Public Law 102–261

> An Act To designate the Federal Building and the United States Courthouse located at 15 Lee Street in Montgomery, Alabama, as the “Frank M. Johnson, Jr. Federal Building and United States Courthouse”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The Federal Building and United States Courthouse located at 15 Lee Street in Montgomery, Alabama, shall be known and designated as the “Frank M. Johnson, Jr. Federal Building and United States Courthouse”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in any law. map, regulation, document, paper or other record of the United States to the Federal Building and United States Courthouse referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Frank M. Johnson, Jr. Federal Building and United States Courthouse”.


*Approved March 20, 1992.*

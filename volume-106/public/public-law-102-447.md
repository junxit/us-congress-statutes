# Public Law 102–447

> An Act To designate the Central Square facility of the United States Postal Service in Cambridge, Massachusetts, as the “Clifton Merriman Post Office Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The Central Square facility of the United States Postal Service located at 770 Massachusetts Avenue in Cambridge, Massachusetts, shall be known and designated as the “Clifton Merriman Post Office Building”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Clifton Merriman Post Office Building”.


*Approved October 23, 1992.*

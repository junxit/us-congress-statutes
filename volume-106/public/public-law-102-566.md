# Public Law 102–566

> An Act To amend the Agricultural Adjustment Act of 1938 to permit the acre-for-acre transfer of an acreage allotment or quota for certain commodities, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. ACRE-FOR-ACRE TRANSFER OF CERTAIN ACREAGE ALLOTMENTS.

Section 318 of the Agricultural Adjustment Act of 1938 (7 U.S.C. 1314d) is amended by striking subsection (e) and inserting the following new subsection:

> - **(e)** The transfer of an allotment or quota under this section shall be approved acre for acre.”.


*Approved October 28, 1992.*

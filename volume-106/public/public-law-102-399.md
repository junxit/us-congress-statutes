# Public Law 102–399

> An Act To authorize appropriations for the American Folklife Center for fiscal year 1993.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 8 of the American Folklife Preservation Act (20 U.S.C. 2107) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking out “1991, and” and inserting in lieu thereof “1991,”; and
- **(2)** by inserting after “1992” the following: “, and $1,120,000 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1993”.


*Approved October 7, 1992.*

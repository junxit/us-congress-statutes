# Public Law 102–317

> An Act To direct the Secretary of Health and Human Services to extend the waiver granted to the Tennessee Primary Care Network of the enrollment mix requirement under the medicaid program.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. EXTENSION OF MEDICAID WAIVER FOR TENNESSEE PRIMARY CARE NETWORK.

Section 6411(f) of the Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act of 1989 is amended by striking “June 30, 1992” and inserting “January 31, 1994”.


*Approved July 2, 1992.*

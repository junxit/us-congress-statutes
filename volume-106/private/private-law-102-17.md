# Private Law 102–17

> An Act For the relief of Anna C. Massari.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. WAIVER OF TIME LIMITATIONS.

- **(a)** *In General.—* The time limitations set forth in section 3702(b) of title 31, United States Code, shall not apply with respect to a claim for the disbursement of pay due by the Department of the Navy to Anna C. Massari, as represented by payroll checks that were issued to, but not negotiated by, Anna C. Massari prior to March 31, 199.
- **(b)** *Deadline.—* Subsection (a) shall apply only if Anna C. Massari or her authorized representative submits a claim pursuant to such subsection before the expiration of the 6-month period beginning on the date of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved October 23, 1992.*

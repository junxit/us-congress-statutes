# Private Law 102–18

> An Act For the relief of the Wilkinson County School District, in the State of Mississippi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to the Wilkinson County School District, in the State of Mississippi, an amount equal to $57,896 in full satisfaction of all claims of such School District relating to any overpayment of windfall profit tax for periods ending before January 1, 1983.


*Approved October 23, 1992.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 131

> ADJOURNMENT—SENATE AND HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That notwithstanding the provisions of section 132(a) of the Legislation Reorganization Act of 1946 (2 U.S.C. 198), as amended by section 461 of the Legislative Reorganization Act of 1970 (Public Law 91–510; 84 Stat. 1193), the Senate and the House of Representatives shall not adjourn for a period in excess of three days, or adjourn sine die, until both Houses of Congress have adopted a concurrent resolution providing either for an adjournment (in excess of three days) to a day certain, or for adjournment sine die.


*Agreed to July 31, 1992.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 328

> “YEAR OF THE AMERICAN INDIAN, 1992: CONGRESSIONAL RECOGNITION AND APPRECIATION” BOOK—HOUSE PRINT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the book entitled “Year of the American Indian, 1992: Congressional Recognition and Appreciation”, prepared under the direction of the Joint Committee on Printing, shall be printed as a House document, with illustrations and suitable binding. In addition to the usual number there shall be printed the lesser of—

- **(1)** 123,000 copies of the document, of which 88,000 copies shall be for the use of the House of Representatives, 20,000 copies shall be for the use of the Senate, and 15,000 copies shall be for the use of the Joint Committee on Printing; or
- **(2)** such number of copies as does not exceed a cost of $200,000, with distribution to be allocated in the same proportion as described in paragraph (1).


*Agreed to July 2, 1992.*

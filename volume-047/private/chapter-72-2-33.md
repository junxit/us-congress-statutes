# Chapter 33

> AN ACT Authorizing adjustment of the claim of Johnson and Higgins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized to settle and adjust the claim of Johnson and Higgins in a sum not exceeding $115.12 for a general average adjustment requested by the War Department to be made in August, 1922, and report of which was made in 1927. There is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $115.12 for payment of the claim.


*Approved, February 2, 1933.*

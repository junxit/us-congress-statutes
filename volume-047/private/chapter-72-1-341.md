# Chapter 341

> AN ACT For the relief of Agnes C. Reder.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to issue a patent to Agnes C. Reder for lands embraced in her enlarged homestead entry, Billings 029518, and her stock-raising homestead entry, Billings 029541, upon fulfillment of the usual requirements but without reference to the limitations of the Act of August 30, 1890 (26 Stat. 391).


*Approved, June 30, 1932.*

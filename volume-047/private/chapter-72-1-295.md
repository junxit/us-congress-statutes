# Chapter 295

> AN ACT For the relief of National Ben Franklin Fire Insurance Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $406.29 to National Ben Franklin Fire Insurance Company, in full reimbursement for the sale by the Government of a Buick automobile stolen April 21, 1920, and while operated by the thief for illegal purposes, was seized April 26, 1921, forfeited, and sold under the customs revenue laws, and the proceeds converted into the Treasury of the United States.


*Approved, June 28, 1932.*

# Chapter 302

> AN ACT For the relief of the Copper Ridge Mining Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay to the Copper Ridge Mining Company, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $515, in full satisfaction of the claims of said company against the United States for repayment of purchase money in connection with mineral entries Phoenix 056018 and 056019, such claims for repayment not having been submitted to the General Land Office within the time required by the Act entitled “An Act to amend an Act approved March 26, 1908, entitled ‘An Act to provide for the repayment of certain commissions, excess payments, and purchase moneys paid under the public land laws,’” approved December 11, 1919.


*Approved, June 28, 1932.*

# Chapter 224

> AN ACT For the relief of John E. Davidson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws and of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers and sailors, their widows and dependent relatives, John E. Davidson, seaman, second class, United States Navy, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged under honorable conditions from the naval service of the United States at Saint Elizabeths Hospital, Washington, District of Columbia, on the 16th day of July, 1918: Provided, That no compensation, retirement pay, back pay, pension, or other benefit shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 3, 1933.*

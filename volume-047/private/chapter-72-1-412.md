# Chapter 412

> AN ACT For the relief of Oscar R. Hahnel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Oscar R. Hahnel the sum of $300, to reimburse him for damages to his automobile caused by a collision with an Army truck near Bretton Woods, New Hampshire, on August 10, 1927.


*Approved, July 2, 1932.*

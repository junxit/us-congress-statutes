# Chapter 66

> AN ACT For the relief of Thomas C. LaForge.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to issue a patent in fee to Thomas C. LaForge, Crow allottee numbered 1257, for land allotted to him under the provisions of the Act of June 4, 1920 (41 Stat. L. 751), and designated as homestead.


*Approved, March 1, 1932.*

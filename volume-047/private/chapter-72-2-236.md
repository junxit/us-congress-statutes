# Chapter 236

> AN ACT For the relief of Harry E. Blomgren.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to allow and credit in the accounts of Harry E. Blomgren, postmaster at Fort Dodge, Iowa, the sum of $26, being the amount expended by him for vehicle hire, said sum having been disallowed by the General Accounting Office.


*Approved, March 3, 1933.*

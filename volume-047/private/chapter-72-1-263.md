# Chapter 263

> AN ACT For the relief of H. H. Lee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $233.75 to H. H. Lee as final settlement of his claim for loss by fire to his own property while assigned to protection of Government holdings during the Half Moon forest fire in Glacier National Park.


*Approved, June 14, 1932.*

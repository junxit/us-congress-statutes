# Chapter 38

> AN ACT Authorizing the President to transfer and appoint Lieutenant Morris Smellow, United States Navy, to the grade of passed assistant paymaster, with the rank of lieutenant, in the Supply Corps of the United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States is hereby authorized to transfer and appoint Lieutenant Morris Smellow, United States Navy, to the grade of passed assistant paymaster, with the rank of lieutenant, in the Supply Corps of the United States Navy, to rank next after Lieutenant Edmund T. Stewart, junior, Supply Corps, and with Lieutenant Charles H. Momm, of the line, as a running mate.


*Approved, February 6, 1933.*

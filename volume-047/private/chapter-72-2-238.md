# Chapter 238

> AN ACT For the relief of William A. Lester.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated and in full settlement against the Government, to William A. Lester the sum of $100, paid in connection with timber and stone application numbered 09583 to the Department of the Interior, on June 18, 1917. which application has been refused due to the fact that the land in question has been withdrawn from homestead entry.


*Approved, March 3, 1933.*

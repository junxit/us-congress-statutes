# Chapter 358

> AN ACT For the relief of Lieutenant M. A. Sprengel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the General Accounting Office is hereby authorized and directed to credit the accounts of Lieutenant M. A. Sprengel, Supply Corps, United States Navy, in the amount of $17.36, which amount represents payments made to Lieutenant C. T. Simard, United States Navy, for mileage performed under orders of the Bureau of Navigation of the Navy Department dated May 21, 1927.


*Approved, June 30, 1932.*

# Chapter 85

> AN ACT For the relief of C. N. Hildreth, junior.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to credit the accounts of C. N. Hildreth, junior, former collector customs for collection district numbered 18, with the sum of $89.10, representing the amount of a payment made to the A. A. A. Garage of Miami, Florida, on voucher 851 in his August, 1928, accounts, and heretofore disallowed by the Comptroller General.


*Approved, February 15, 1933.*

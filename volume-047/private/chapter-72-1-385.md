# Chapter 385

> AN ACT For the relief of Grover Cleveland Ballard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $246.75 to reimburse Grover Cleveland Ballard, an ex-service man of the World War, for money expended for medical and surgical service in reamputating his leg, which was shot off below the knee during the World War.


*Approved, July 1, 1932.*

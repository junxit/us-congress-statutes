# Chapter 458

> AN ACT For the relief of W. A. Peters.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is authorized and directed to credit the accounts of W. A. Peters, postmaster at Sallisaw, Oklahoma, in the sum of $614.80. Such sum represents the amount of a deficit in the accounts of the said W. A. Peters, caused by the loss by said W. A. Peters of postal funds deposited in the First National Bank of Sallisaw, Oklahoma, which failed on November 22, 1927.


*Approved, July 7, 1932.*

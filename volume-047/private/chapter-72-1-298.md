# Chapter 298

> AN ACT For the relief of Don C. Fees.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of American 1 in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to allow in the accounts of Don C. Fees, disbursing clerk, Department of Justice, the sum of $33.80, paid by him under authority and direction of said department for the purchase, in the open market, of two thousand six hundred manila envelopes, which was disallowed by said Comptroller General.


*Approved, June 28, 1932.*

# Chapter 79

> AN ACT For the relief of Anna Marie Sanford, widow of William Richard Sanford, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission is hereby authorized to consider and determine the claim of Anna Marie Sanford, widow of William Richard Sanford, deceased, former furnace man, navy yard, Washington, District of Columbia, in the same manner and to the same extent as if said William Richard Sanford had made application for the benefits of said Act within the one-year period required by sections 17 and 20 thereof: Provided, That no benefit shall accrue prior to the approval of this Act.


*Approved, March 15, 1932.*

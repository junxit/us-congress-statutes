# Chapter 400

> AN ACT For the relief of Edna M. Gilson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the accounts of Edna M. Gilson, postmaster at Steubenville, Ohio, in the sum of $1,389.80, due the United States on account of the loss of postal funds resulting from larceny and embezzlement.


*Approved, July 2, 1932.*

# Chapter 316

> AN ACT For the relief of Clive Sprouse and Robert F. Moore.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the homestead entries of Clive Sprouse (Salt Lake City serial 046562) and Robert F. Moore (Salt Lake City serial 048376), embracing land within the former Uintah Indian Reservation, are hereby validated.


*Approved, March 4, 1933.*

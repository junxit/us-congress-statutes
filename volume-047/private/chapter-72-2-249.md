# Chapter 249

> AN ACT For the relief of the Great Falls Meat Company, of Great Falls, Montana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to adjust and settle the claim of the Great Falls Meat Company, of Great Falls, Montana, arising out of the purchase by said company from the Department of Agriculture, in November, 1931, of a buffalo, the carcass of which spoiled before delivery, and to allow not to exceed $50 in full and final settlement of the claim. There is hereby appropriated, out of money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $50, or so much thereof as may be necessary, for the payment of said claim.


*Approved, March 3, 1933.*

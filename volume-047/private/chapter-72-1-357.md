# Chapter 357

> AN ACT For the relief of Harry Manning Lee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged sailors Harry Manning Lee, who served as private, United States Marine Corps, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the naval service of the United States on September 5, 1918: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 30, 1932.*

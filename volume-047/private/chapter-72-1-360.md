# Chapter 360

> AN ACT For the relief of Florence Northcott Hannas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress asembled,*

That Florence Northcott Hannas, mother of Walter William Northcott, late of the United States Navy, shall be regarded as the duly designated beneficiary and dependent of the late Walter William Northcott, under the Act approved June 4, 1920 (41 Stat. 824; U. S. C., title 34, sec. 943).


*Approved, June 30, 1932.*

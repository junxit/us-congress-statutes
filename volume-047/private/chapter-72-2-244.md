# Chapter 244

> AN ACT For the relief of Newport Contracting and Engineering Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to the Newport Contracting and Engineering Company, of Lee Hall, Virginia, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $2,490 in full settlement for penalty imposed for delay in completing contract Noy-673 dated October 25, 1929, with the Navy Department for submarine escape practice tank.


*Approved, March 3, 1933.*

# Chapter 292

> AN ACT For the relief of C. A. Cates.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in, Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $1,000 in full settlement of claims against the Government of the United States to C. A. Cates, who sustained injuries on or about February 5, 1929, at Dayton, Ohio, when struck by an Army truck operated by a private soldier then acting in the course of the performance of his duties as an employee of the Government.


*Approved, March 4, 1933.*

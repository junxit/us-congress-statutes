# Chapter 144

> AN ACT For the relief of Fireman’s Fund Insurance Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of the indefinite appropriation “Refunding moneys erroneously received and covered,” the sum of $121.44 to Fireman’s Fund Insurance Company, of San Francisco, California, which sum is the balance left after all costs had been deducted from the price obtained at auction in connection with libel filed against one Dodge coupe automobile engine numbered A–917–841.


*Approved, April 25, 1932.*

# Chapter 437

> AN ACT For the relief of John Heffron.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws or any laws conferring rights, privileges, or benefits upon persons honorably discharged from the United States Navy John Heffron shall be held and considered to have served honorably as a cook (first class), United States Navy, for more than ninety days during the war with Spain: Provided, That no pension, pay, or bounty shall be held to have accrued by reason of this Act prior to its passage.


*Approved, July 6, 1932.*

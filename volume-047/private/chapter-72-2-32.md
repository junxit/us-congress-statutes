# Chapter 32

> AN ACT For the relief of George T. Johnson and Sons.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to George T. Johnson and Sons, of Cambridge, Maryland, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $180 in full settlement of claim for repairs ordered by the collector of customs at Baltimore, Maryland, to wharf and boats of H. F. Brannock (Incorporated) due to damages done by seized power boats Hiawatha and Whippoorwill in charge of United States customs officers.


*Approved, February 1, 1933.*

# Chapter 485

> AN ACT For the relief of George H. Holman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers George H. Holman, late a private in Company B, Thirty-third United States Volunteer Infantry, and in Company E, Twelfth Regiment United States Infantry, shall be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States, as a member of said Company E, Twelfth Regiment United States Infantry, on the 27th day of February, 1904: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, July 14, 1932.*

# Chapter 415

> AN ACT For the relief of Octavia Gulick Stone.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to issue upon her application a patent in fee to Octavia Gulick Stone, Blackfeet allottee numbered 2133, for land allotted to her under the provisions of the Act of June 30, 1919 (41 Stat. L. 3–16), and designated as homestead.


*Approved, July 2, 1932.*

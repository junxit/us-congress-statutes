# Chapter 104

> AN ACT For the relief of Harriette Olsen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the account of Harriette Olsen, postmaster at Armstrong, Iowa, in the sum of $42.91, due the United States on account of the loss resulting from the closing of the First National Bank of Armstrong, Iowa.


*Approved, February 18, 1933.*

# Chapter 235

> AN ACT For the relief of Major O. S. McCleary, United States Army, retired.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is authorized and directed to allow to Major O. S. McCleary, United States Army, retired, the sum of $148.98, being difference between active-duty pay and allowances and retired pay for period from July 2 to 20, 1927, while he was on leave from active duty to which as a retired officer he was assigned.


*Approved, March 3, 1933.*

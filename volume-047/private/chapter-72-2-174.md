# Chapter 174

> AN ACT Granting six months’ pay to Ruth McCarn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, such sum as may be necessary to pay to Ruth McCarn, dependent mother of the late John Bush Watson, seaman, United States Navy, an amount equal to six months’ pay at the rate said John Bush Watson was receiving at the date of his death.


*Approved, March 1, 1933.*

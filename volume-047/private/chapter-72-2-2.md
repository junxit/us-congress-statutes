# Chapter 2

> AN ACT For the relief of John S. Shaw.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission is hereby authorized to consider and determine the claim of John S. Shaw, who purports to have suffered injury while employed as rural mail carrier some time in October, 1918, in the same manner and to the same extent as if said John S. Shaw had made application for the benefits of said Act within the one-year period required by sections 17 and 20 thereof: Provided, That no benefit shall accrue prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved, December 13, 1932.*

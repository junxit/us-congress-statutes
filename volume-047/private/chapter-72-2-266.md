# Chapter 266

> AN ACT Authorizing adjustment of the claim of Arthur R. Saffran.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to adjust and settle the claim of Arthur R. Saffran for $560 as the proceeds covered into the Treasury of the United States from a sale not in accordance with law of his automobile, a libel for the forfeiture of which for violation of internal revenue statutes was subsequently dismissed by the United States District Court for the Southern District of New York, and to allow not to exceed $560 in full and final settlement of the claim. There is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $560, or so much thereof as may be necessary, for the payment of the claim.


*Approved, March 3, 1933.*

# Chapter 267

> AN ACT For the relief of Sadie L. Kirby.

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to issue a patent to Sadie L. Kirby, of Silver City, New Mexico, for the east half of section 35, township 15 south, range 18 west, New Mexico principal meridian, embraced in her additional entry (Las Cruces serial 040099) under the Stock-Raising Homestead Act.


*Approved, March 3, 1933.*

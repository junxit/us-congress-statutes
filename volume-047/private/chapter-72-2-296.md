# Chapter 296

> AN ACT For the relief of J. R. Reimer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to allow credit in the accounts of J. R. Reimer, post-master at Clinton, Oklahoma, for $459.90 unlawfully paid to a substitute clerk, C. H. Markum, for vehicle hire.


*Approved, March 4, 1933.*

# Chapter 262

> AN ACT For the relief of William M. Sherman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws William M. Sherman, who served as a private in the following organizations, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a member of Troop A, Eighth Regiment United States Cavalry, on the 18th day of May, 1900, and from Company I, Eighth Regiment United States Infantry, on the 7th day of March, 1901: Provided, That no bounty, pension, pay, or other emoluments shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 3, 1933.*

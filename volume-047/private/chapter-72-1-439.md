# Chapter 439

> AN ACT For the relief of Lewis A. McDormott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers Lewis A. McDormott, deceased, late of the United States Marine Corps, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged under honorable conditions from the naval service of the United States as a member of the United States Marine Corps on the 25th day of April, 1899: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, July 6, 1932.*

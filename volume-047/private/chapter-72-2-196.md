# Chapter 196

> AN ACT For the relief of Jack C. Richardson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $143.04, to be paid to Lieutenant Jack C. Richardson, United States Navy, in full satisfaction of his claim for expenses incurred while traveling around the world on the German airship Graf Zeppelin under orders of August 5, 1929, issued by the Navy Department.


*Approved, March 2, 1933.*

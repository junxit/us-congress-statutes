# Chapter 350

> AN ACT For the relief of Walter S. West.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged marines Walter S. West, who was a member of Marine Guard, United States ship Marblehead, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a member of the United States Marine Corps on the 14th day of January, 1899: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 30, 1932.*

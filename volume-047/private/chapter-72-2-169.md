# Chapter 169

> AN ACT For the relief of the Dongji Investment Company (Limited).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Dongji Investment Company (Limited), an Hawaiian corporation, organized under the laws of the Territory of Hawaii, be, and it is hereby, released from any and all claims for damages, in excess of the amount of the performance bond given by such company, which the United States of America may have against it arising out of its breach of contract numbered N311S–2830 dated March 18, 1929, by and between the Dongji Investment Company (Limited), and the United States of America.


*Approved, March 1, 1933.*

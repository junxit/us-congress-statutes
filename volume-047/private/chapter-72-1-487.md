# Chapter 487

> AN ACT For the relief of David C. Jeffcoat.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission is hereby authorized to consider and determine the claim of David C. Jeffcoat as to whether he suffered an injury while employed in the Postal Service compensable under the Employees’ Compensation Act occurring after the date of its enactment, in the same manner and to the same extent as if said David C. Jeffcoat had made application for the benefits of said Act within the one-year period required by sections 17 and 20 thereof: Provided, That no benefit shall accrue prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved, July 14, 1932.*

# Chapter 143

> AN ACT For the relief of W. J. Shirley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to W. J. Shirley the sum of $60.77 in reimbursement for value of his personal property destroyed by fire in the military service of the United States at Brest, France, on the 21st day of July, 1919, and for which loss he was in no wise responsible.


*Approved, April 25, 1932.*

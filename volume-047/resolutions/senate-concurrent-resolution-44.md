# Senate Concurrent Resolution 44

> bridge, missuori river (h. r. 14500).

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the action of the Speaker of the House of Representatives and of the Vice President of the United States in signing the enrolled bill (H. R. 14500) to extend the time for completing the construction of a bridge across the Missouri River at or near Kansas City, Kansas, be, and the same is hereby, rescinded.


*Passed, February 28, 1933.*

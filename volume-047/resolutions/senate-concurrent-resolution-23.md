# Senate Concurrent Resolution 23

> jurisdiction in management of indian country.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President of the United States be, and he is hereby, requested to return to the Senate the enrolled bill (S. 3322) entitled “An Act to transfer certain jurisdiction from the War Department in the management of Indian country.”


*Passed, March 31, 1932.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 35

> war department appropriation bill.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Saturday, the 16th day of July, 1932, and that when they adjourn on said day they stand adjourned sine die.


*Passed, July 16, 1932.*

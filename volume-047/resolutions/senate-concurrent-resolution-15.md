# Senate Concurrent Resolution 15

> building, etc., associations.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the action of the Speaker of the House of Representatives and of the President pro tempore of the Senate in signing the enrolled bill (S. 2199) entitled “An Act exempting building and loan associations from being adjudged bankrupts ” be rescinded, and that in the reenrollment of the said bill the Secretary of the Senate be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to make the following corrections, namely: On page 1 of the engrossed bill, line 8, strike out the comma after the word except on page 1, line 9, insert a comma after the word “ association and on page 2, line 5, strike out the word “ of ” and insert in lieu thereof the word “ or ”.


*Passed, February 8, 1932.*

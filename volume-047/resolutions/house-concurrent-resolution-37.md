# House Concurrent Resolution 37

> war department appropriation bill.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Clerk of the House is hereby authorized and directed in the enrollment of the bill H. R. 11897, “An Act making appropriations for the military and nonmilitary activities of the War Department for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1933, and for other purposes,” to insert on page 9, line 15 of the bill the sum of $5,928,389, in lieu of the matter directed to be inserted by the concurrence of the House in Senate Amendment Numbered 14.


*Passed, July 13, 1932.*

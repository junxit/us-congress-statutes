# Senate Concurrent Resolution 43

> supreme court, rules of practice, etc.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Secretary of the Senate be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, in the enrollment of the bill (S. 4020) to give the Supreme Court of the United States authority to prescribe rules of practice and procedure with respect to proceedings in criminal cases after verdict, to strike out on page 1, lines 8 and 9, respectively, of the engrossed bill the words “Porto Rico ” and insert in lieu thereof “ Puerto Rico ”.


*Passed, February 22, 1933.*

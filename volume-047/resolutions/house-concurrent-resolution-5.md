# House Concurrent Resolution 5

> holiday recess.

*Resolved, by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Tuesday, December 22, 1931, they stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian Monday, January 4, 1932.


*Passed, December 22, 1931.*

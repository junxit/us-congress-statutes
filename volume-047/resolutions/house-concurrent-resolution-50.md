# House Concurrent Resolution 50

> congressional directory.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That an edition of the Congressional Directory for the first session of the Seventy-third Congress be compiled, prepared, indexed, and published under the direction of the Joint Committee on Printing, as provided for in section 73 of the Printing Act approved January 12, 1895.


*Passed, February 22, 1933.*

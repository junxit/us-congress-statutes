# House Concurrent Resolution 49

> canal zone civil code.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Clerk of the House is hereby authorized and directed in the enrollment of the bill (H. R. 7522) entitled “An Act to provide a new civil code for the Canal Zone and to repeal the existing civil code,” to omit Senate amendments numbered 15 to 23, inclusive.


*Passed, February 17, 1933.*

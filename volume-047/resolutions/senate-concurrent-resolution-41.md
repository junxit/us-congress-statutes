# Senate Concurrent Resolution 41

> loans to farmers for crop production, etc.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring,*

That the Secretary of the Senate be, and he is hereby authorized and directed, in the enrollment of the bill (S. 5160) to provide for loans to farmers for crop production and harvesting during the year 1933, and for other purposes, to insert on page 2, line 9, of the engrossed House amendment, after the figures “ 1933 ”, the following:“, or on livestock,”.


*Passed, January 26, 1933.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 29

> laws relating to veterans of various wars.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the letter of the Administrator of Veterans’ Affairs, dated May 12, 1932, transmitting, in response to S. Res. 412 (Seventy-first Congress), a compilation of all Federal laws relating to the veterans of our various wars, be printed, with illustrations, as a Senate document; and that ten thousand additional copies shall be printed for distribution by the Veterans’ Administration, of which there may be furnished, upon written application to the Administrator of Veterans’ Affairs, one copy to each post of the Grand Army of the Republic, the American Legion, and the Veterans of Foreign Wars of the United States, to each camp of the United Spanish War Veterans, to each camp of Veterans of the Indian Wars, each chapter of the American Red Cross, and to each chapter of the Disabled American Veterans of the World War.


*Passed, June 27, 1932.*

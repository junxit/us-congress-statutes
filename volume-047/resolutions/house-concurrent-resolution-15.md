# House Concurrent Resolution 15

> prayers by the chaplain of the house.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That five thousand copies of the prayers offered by the Reverend James Shera Montgomery, Chaplain of the House of Representatives, at the opening of the daily sessions of the House during the Seventieth and Seventy-first Congresses, be printed and bound for the use of the House of Representatives.


*Passed, January 29, 1932.*

# Chapter 42

> AN ACT Authorizing the modification of the existing project for the Willamette River between Oregon City and Portland, Oregon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the project for the improvement of the Willamette River between Oregon City and Portland, Oregon, authorized by the River and Harbor Act approved July 3, 1930, is hereby modified in accordance with the recommendation of the Chief of Engineers in the report submitted in House Document Numbered 748, Seventy-first Congress, third session.


*Approved, February 11, 1932.*

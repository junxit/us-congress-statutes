# Chapter 148

> AN ACT To extend the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Rio Grande at Boca Chica, Texas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge authorized by Act of Congress approved June 10, 1932, to be built by the Boca Chica Bridge Company, across the Rio Grande at Boca Chica, Texas, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from June 10, 1933.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, March 1, 1933.*

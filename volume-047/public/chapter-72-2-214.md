# Chapter 214

> JOINT RESOLUTION Authorizing the American National Red Cross and certain other organizations to exchange Government-owned cotton for articles containing wool.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the American National Red Cross or any other organization to which Government-owned cotton has been or shall hereafter be delivered pursuant to law is hereby authorized, if it shall be deemed advisable, to exchange any such cotton for cloth or wearing apparel or other articles of clothing containing wool.


*Approved, March 3, 1933.*

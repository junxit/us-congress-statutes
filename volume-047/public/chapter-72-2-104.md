# Chapter 104

> AN ACT To extend the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Missouri River at or near Saint Charles, Missouri.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for completing the construction of the bridge across the Missouri River at or near Saint Charles, Missouri, authorized to be built by the Saint Louis-Kansas City Short Line Railroad Company by the Act of Congress approved March 2, 1929, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from March 2, 1932.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, April 15, 1932.*

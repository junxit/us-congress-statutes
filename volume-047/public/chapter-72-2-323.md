# Chapter 323

> AN ACT To extend the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the east branch of the Niagara River at or near the city of Niagara Falls, New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge authorized by Act of Congress approved June 17, 1930, to be built by the Niagara Frontier Bridge Commission, a State commission created by act of the Legislature of the State of New York, chapter 594 of the Laws of 1929, across the east branch of the Niagara River, from the city of Niagara Falls, in the county of Niagara and State of New York, at a point east of Evershed Avenue in said city of Niagara Falls, to Grand Island, in the county of Erie and State of New York, are hereby extended two and five years, respectively, from June 17, 1932.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, June 30, 1932.*

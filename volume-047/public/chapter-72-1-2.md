# Chapter 2

> JOINT RESOLUTION Extending the time within which the War Policies Commission is required to submit its final report.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time within which the War Policies Commission, created by Public Resolution Numbered 98, Seventy-first Congress, approved June 27, 1930, was required to submit its final report to the Congress be, and the same is hereby, extended ninety days.


*Approved, December 21, 1931.*

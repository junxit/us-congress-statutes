# Chapter 73

> AN ACT Regulating the use of appropriations for the military and nonmilitary activities of the War Department.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the expenditure of appropriations for the military and nonmilitary activities of the War Department, the Secretary of War shall, unless in his discretion the interest of the Government will not permit, purchase or contract for, within the limits of the United States, only articles of the growth, production, or manufacture of the United States, notwithstanding that such articles of the growth, production, or manufacture of the United States may cost more, if such excess of cost be not unreasonable.


*Approved, March 8, 1932.*

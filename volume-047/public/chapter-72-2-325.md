# Chapter 325

> AN ACT Authorizing the Secretary of the Navy, in his discretion, to deliver to the custody of the Alabama Society of Fine Arts, the silver service presented to the United States for the United States ship Montgomery.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy is authorized, in his discretion, to deliver to the custody of the Alabama Society of Fine Arts, for preservation and exhibition, the silver service which was presented to the United States for the United States ship Montgomery by the citizens of that State: Provided, That no expense shall be incurred by the United States for the delivery of such silver service: Provided further, That said silver service shall be subject to recall when in the opinion of the Secretary of the Navy it may be of service to the Navy.


*Approved, June 30, 1932.*

# Chapter 87

> AN ACT To repeal an ordinance enacted by the Isthmian Canal Commission August 5, 1911, and approved by the Secretary of War August 22, 1911, establishing market regulations for the Canal Zone.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the ordinance enacted by the Isthmian Canal Commission on August 5, 1911, and approved by the Secretary of War on August 22, 1911, establishing market regulations for the Canal Zone be, and it is hereby, repealed.


*Approved, February 16, 1933.*

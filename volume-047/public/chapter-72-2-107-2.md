# Chapter 107

> AN ACT To amend section 98 of the Judicial Code, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proviso in the fifth paragraph of section 98 of the Judicial Code, as amended (U. S. C., title 28, sec. 179), is hereby amended to read as follows: “Provided, That the cities of Winston-Salem and Rockingham shall each provide and furnish at its own expense a suitable and convenient place for holding the district court until Federal buildings containing quarters for the court are erected at such places.”


*Approved, February 20, 1933.*

# Chapter 17

> AN ACT To extend the time for completing the construction of a bridge across Lake Champlain at or near Rouses Point, New York, and a point at or near Alburgh, Vermont.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time for completing the construction of a bridge across Lake Champlain at or near Rouses Point, New York, and a point at or near Alburgh, Vermont, authorized to be built by Elisha N. Goodsell, of Alburgh, Vermont, his heirs, legal representatives, and assigns, by an Act of Congress approved February 15, 1929, heretofore extended by Act of Congress approved April 19, 1930, is hereby extended three years from February 15, 1933.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby reserved.


*Approved, January 24, 1933.*

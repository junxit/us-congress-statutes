# Chapter 57

> AN ACT To extend the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Missouri River at or near Garrison, North Dakota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Missouri River, at or near Garrison, North Dakota, authorized to be built by the State of North Dakota, by an Act of Congress approved February 10, 1932, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from February 10, 1933.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, February 14, 1933.*

# Chapter 332

> AN ACT To add certain lands to the Idaho National Forest, Idaho.

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the following-described areas be, and the same are hereby, included in and made a part of the Idaho National Forest, subject to all prior adverse rights, and that said lands shall hereafter be subject to all laws affecting national forests: All township 23 north, ranges 2 and 3 east, and that part of the west half of township 24 north, range 4 east, which is not already included in the Nez Perce National Forest; all Boise meridian.


*Approved, June 30, 1932.*

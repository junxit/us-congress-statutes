# Chapter 31

> AN ACT To extend the time for completing the construction of a bridge across the Mississippi River near and above the city of New Orleans, Louisiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time for completing the construction of a bridge across the Mississippi River, near and above the city of New Orleans, authorized to be built by the city of New Orleans, a municipal corporation existing under the laws of the State of Louisiana, its successors and assigns, through its Public Belt Railroad Commission, by an Act of Congress approved April 17, 1924, heretofore extended by an Act of Congress approved May 24, 1928, is hereby further extended three years from May 24, 1933.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, February 10, 1932.*

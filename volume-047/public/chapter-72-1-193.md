# Chapter 193

> JOINT RESOLUTION Authorizing the Joint Committee on the Library to procure an oil portrait of former President Calvin Coolidge.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Joint Committee on the Library is hereby authorized to procure an oil portrait of former President Calvin Coolidge for the Executive Mansion, at a cost not to exceed $2,500.


*Approved, May 19, 1932.*

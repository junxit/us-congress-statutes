# Chapter 129

> AN ACT To extend the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Missouri River at or near Elbowoods, North Dakota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Missouri River, at or near Elbowoods, North Dakota, authorized to be built by the State of North Dakota, by an Act of Congress approved March 4, 1931, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from March 4, 1932.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, April 22, 1932.*

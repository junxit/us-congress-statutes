# Chapter 280

> AN ACT To provide for the free importation of certain articles exported temporarily for scientific or educational purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Title II of the Tariff Act of 1930 is amended by adding at the end thereof the following:

> ## “Par. 1815.
>
> Articles, when returned after having been loaned and exported for use temporarily abroad solely for exhibition, examination, or experimentation, for scientific or educational purposes, if imported by or for the account of the person who exported them from the United States, and not for sale, subject to such regulations as the Secretary of the Treasury shall prescribe.”


*Approved, March 4, 1933.*

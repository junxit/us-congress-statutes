# Chapter 149

> AN ACT To extend the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Mississippi River at or near Baton Rouge, Louisiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Mississippi River at or near Baton Rouge, Louisiana, authorized to be built by the Louisiana Highway Commission, and the Missouri Pacific Railroad Company, and the Louisiana and Arkansas Railway Company, by an Act of Congress approved February 10, 1932, are hereby extended two and four years, respectively, from February 10, 1933.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, March 1, 1933.*

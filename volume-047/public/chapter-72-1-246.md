# Chapter 246

> JOINT RESOLUTION To amend section 625 (a) of the Revenue Act of 1932;

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 625 (a) of the Revenue Act of 1932 is amended by striking out the words “or with any person other than a dealer”, and by adding at the end thereof a new sentence as follows: “If any article has, under a contract of the character above described, been delivered, prior to June 21, 1932, to any person (other than a dealer or other than a person intending to use the article as material in the manufacture or production of another article, or to sell it on or in connection with, or with the sale of, another article), no tax shall be collected under this title.”


*Approved, June 13, 1932.*

# Chapter 20

> JOINT RESOLUTION To exempt from the tax on admissions amounts paid for admission tickets sold by authority of the committee on inaugural ceremonies on the occasion of the inauguration of the President elect in March, 1933.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all amounts paid for admission tickets sold by authority of the committee on inaugural ceremonies of the inauguration of the President elect in March, 1933, shall be exempt from the tax on admissions imposed by section 500 of the Revenue Act of 1926, as amended, all the net proceeds from the sale of said tickets to be donated by the said committee to charity.


*Approved, January 24, 1933.*

# Chapter 186

> AN ACT Relating to the construction of a Federal building at Mangum, Oklahoma.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the construction of the Federal building at Mangum, Oklahoma, authorized by the Act of February 16, 1931 (Document Numbered 788, Seventy-first Congress), the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby authorized to provide facilities for the holding of terms of the District Court for the Western District of Oklahoma.


*Approved, March 2, 1933.*

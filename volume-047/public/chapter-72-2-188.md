# Chapter 188

> JOINT RESOLUTION For the relief of the State of Idaho.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the State of Idaho be, and is hereby, relieved of any obligation to replace the building on the Boise Barracks Military Reservation, Boise, Idaho, or to reimburse the United States for the amount of damage to the building destroyed by fire on January 8, 1928.


*Approved, March 2, 1933.*

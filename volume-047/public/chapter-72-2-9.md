# Chapter 9

> JOINT RESOLUTION Extending the time for filing the report of the joint committee to investigate the operation of the laws and regulations relating to the relief of veterans.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time within which the joint committee to investigate the operation of the laws and regulations relating to the relief of veterans, created by section 701 of the Legislative Appropriation Act for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1933, approved June 30, 1932, shall report to the Senate and the House of Representatives, is hereby extended to and including the 3d day of March, 1933.


*Approved, January 3, 1933.*

# Chapter 434

> AN ACT To amend the sixth exception in section 3 of the Immigration Act of 1924 with reference to nonimmigrant status of certain aliens.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3 (6) of the Immigration Act of 1924 be amended so as to read as follows:

> - **(6)** An alien entitled to enter the United States solely to carry on trade between the United States and the foreign state of which he is a national under and in pursuance of the provisions of a treaty of commerce and navigation, and his wife, and his unmarried children under twenty-one years of age, if accompanying or following to join him.”


*Approved, July 6, 1932.*

# Chapter 101

> JOINT RESOLUTION To provide for the naming of Montgomery Blair Portal.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the portion of Sixteenth Street and the adjacent park reservation lying within the District of Columbia at the intersection of Sixteenth Street, North Portal Drive, Eastern Avenue, and the District line, shall be known as Montgomery Blair Portal, in commemoration of the public service of the late Montgomery Blair, Postmaster General in the Cabinet of President Lincoln.


*Approved, April 14, 1932.*

# Chapter 48

> AN ACT To change the name of “Roosevelt Island” to “Theodore Roosevelt Island.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That an Act to establish a memorial to Theodore Roosevelt, approved May 21, 1932 (Public, Numbered 146, Seventy-second Congress), be amended as follows:

That wherever the name “Roosevelt Island” appears in sections 1, 2, and 3 of this Act it shall be stricken out and the name “Theodore Roosevelt Island” shall be inserted in lieu thereof.

## § 2.

In all public documents, records, and maps of the United States in which such island is designated or referred to it shall be designated as “Theodore Roosevelt Island.”


*Approved, February 11, 1933.*

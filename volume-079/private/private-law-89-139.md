# Private Law 89–139

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Orhan Metin Ozmat.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Orhan Metin temat shall be held and considered to have complied with the requirements of section 316 of the Act as they relate to residence and physical presence.


*Approved October 21, 1965.*

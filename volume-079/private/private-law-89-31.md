# Private Law 89–31

> AN ACT For the relief of Krystyna Stella Hancock.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act; Krystyna Stella Hancock may be classified as an eligible orphan within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of the Act, upon approval of a petition filed in her behalf by Mr. and Mrs. Charles E. Hancock, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 205(b) of the Act, subject to all the conditions in that section relating to eligible orphans.


*Approved August 4, 1965.*

# Private Law 89–74

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Jorge Rosendo Barahona.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Jorge Rosendo Barahona shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of October 3, 1960.


*Approved September 17, 1965.*

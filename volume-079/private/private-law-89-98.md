# Private Law 89–98

> AN ACT For the relief of Evangelia Moshonu Kantas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Evangelia Moshou Kantas may be classified as an eligible orphan within the meaning of section 101 (b)(F) of the said Act and a petition may be filed by Mr. and Mrs. Jerry Kantas, citizens of the United States, in behalf of the said Evangelia Moshou Kantas pursuant to section 205 (b) of the Immigration and Nationality Act subject to all the conditions in that section relating to eligible orphans.


*Approved September 25, 1965.*

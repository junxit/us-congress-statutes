# Private Law 89–189

> AN ACT For the relief of James D. W. Blyth, his wife, Jean Mary Blyth, and their daughter, Penelope Jean Blyth.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, James D. W. Blyth, is wife, Jean Mary Blyth, and their daughter, Penelope Jean Blyth, shall be held and considered to have complied with the requirements of section 316 of the Act as they relate to residence and physical presence.


*Approved October 31, 1965.*

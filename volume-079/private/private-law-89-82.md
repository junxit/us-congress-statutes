# Private Law 89–82

> AN ACT For the relief of Shu listen Chang.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Shu Hsien Chang shall a held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of August 7, 1950.


*Approved September 21, 1965.*

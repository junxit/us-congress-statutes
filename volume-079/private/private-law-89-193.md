# Private Law 89–193

> AN ACT To provide for the free entry of one mass spectrMueter and one split pole spectrograph for the use of the University of Rochester, Rochester, New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to admit free of duty one muss spectrometer (including all accompanying equipment, parts, accessories, and appurtenances) and one split pole spectrograph (including all accompanying equipment, parts, accessories, and appurtenances) for the use of the University of Rochester, Rochester, New York.
- **(b)** If the liquidation of the entry of any article described in subsection (a) of this section has become final, such entry shall be reliquidated and the appropriate refund of duty shall be made.


*Approved November 1, 1965.*

# Private Law 89–146

> AN ACT For the relief of Joseph Durante.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Joseph Durante shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to permanent residence as of October 11, 1954.


*Approved October 23, 1965.*

# Private Law 89–149

> AN ACT For the relief of Raffaella Achilli.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Raffaella Achilli may be. classified as an eligible orphan within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of that Act, upon approval of petitions filed in her liehalf by Mr. and Mrs. Armando Loretucci, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 205(b) of that Act, subject to all the conditions in that section relating to eligible orphans.


*Approved October 26, 1965.*

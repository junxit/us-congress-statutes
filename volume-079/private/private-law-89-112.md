# Private Law 89–112

> AN ACT For the relief of Efstahia Giannos.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Efstahia Giannos may be classified as an eligible orphan within the meaning of section 101 (b)(1)(F) of the Act, and a petition filed in her behalf by Mr. Theodore Giannos, a citizen of the United States, may be approved pursuant to section 205(b) of the Act, subject to all the conditions in that section relating to eligible orphans.


*Approved September 29, 1965.*

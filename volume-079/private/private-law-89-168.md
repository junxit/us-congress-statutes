# Private Law 89–168

> AN ACT For the relief of Paride Marchesan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Pari de Marchesan may lie classified as an eligible orphan within the meaning of section 1Û1 (b)(1)(F) of the said Act, and a petition may he filed in behalf of the said Paride Marchesan by Master Sergeant and Mrs. Harry Simpson, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 205 (b) of the Immigration and Nationality Act subject to all the conditions in that section relating to eligible orphans.


*Approved October 30, 1965.*

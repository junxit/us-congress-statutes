# Private Law 89–92

> AN ACT For the relief of Alva Arlington Garnes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a)(27)(A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Alva Arlington Garnes, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Cecil Edgar Taitt, citizens of the United States: Provided, That no natural parent of the beneficiary, by virtue of such parentage, shall be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved September 22, 1965.*

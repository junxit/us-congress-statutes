# Private Law 89–204

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Eleni Baeoia Ciaeco, doctor of medicine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Eleni Bacola Ciaeco, doctor of medicine, shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of December 19, 1957.


*Approved November 7, 1965.*

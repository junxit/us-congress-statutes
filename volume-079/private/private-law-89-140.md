# Private Law 89–140

> AN ACT For the relief of Maria del Rosario de Fatima Lopez Hayes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Maria del Rosario de Fatima Lopez Hayes may bo classified as an eligible orphan within the meaning of section 101 (b)(1)(F) of the Act, and a petition filed in her behalf by Mary E. Hayes, a citizen of the United States, may be approved pursuant to section 205(b) of the Act, subject to all the conditions in that section relating to eligible orphans.


*Approved October 23, 1965.*

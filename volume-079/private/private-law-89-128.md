# Private Law 89–128

> AN ACT For the relief of Ksenija Popovic.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Ksenija Popovic may be classified as an eligible orphan within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of the Act, and a petition filed in her behalf by Mrs. Joan Drinkwater Behan, a citizen of the United States, may lie approved pursuant to section 205(b) of the Act, subject to all the conditions in that section relating to eligible orphans.


*Approved October 9, 1965, 6:40 a.m.*

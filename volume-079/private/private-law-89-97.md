# Private Law 89–97

> AN ACT For the relief of Ming Chop Chau.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of section 101(b)(1)(E) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Ming Chup Chau shall be held and considered to be under twenty-one years of age.


*Approved September 25, 1965.*

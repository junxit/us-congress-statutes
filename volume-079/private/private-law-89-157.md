# Private Law 89–157

> AN ACT For the relief of Emilia Majka.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Mrs. Emilia Majka, the widow of a United States citizen, shall be deemed to be within the purview of section 101(a)(27)(A) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, and the provisions of section 205 of that Act shall be inapplicable in her case.


*Approved October 27, 1965.*

# Private Law 89–49

> AN ACT For the relief of Lewis H. Nelson III.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to settle the claim of Lewis H. Nelson ill, 48 Anderegg Drive, Rome, New York, for compensation for services rendered the Department of the Air Force, Gnffiss Air Force Base, subsequent to the termination of his term of appointment on August 1“, 1963, after having been erroneously informed that such appointment had been extended. There is hereby appropriated the sum of $255.33 in full and final settlement of said claim from which there shall be deducted and properly deposited Federal withholding tux $34.99, Federal Insurance Contributions Act $17.86, and State tax $2.90, leaving a balance of $199.58 to be paid to the claimant.


*Approved August 28, 1965.*

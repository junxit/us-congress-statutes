# Private Law 89–208

> AN ACT To provide for the free entry of a ninety-centimeter split-pole magnetic spectrograph system with orange-peel internal conversion spectrometer attached for the use of the University of Pittsburgh.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to admit free of duty one ninety-centimeter split-pole magnetic spectrograph system with orange-peel internal conversion spectrometer attached for the use of the University of Pittsburgh, Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania.
- **(b)** If the liquidation of the entry of any article described in subsection (a) has become final, such entry shall be reliquidated and the appropriate refund of duty shall be made.


*Approved November 8, 1965.*

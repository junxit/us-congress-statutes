# Private Law 89–152

> AN ACT For the relief of Miss Gloria Seborg.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of title III of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Miss Gloria Seborg, admitted to the United States for permanent residence on November 5, 1954, shall be held to have complied with the residential and physical presence requirements of section 316 of the said Act.


*Approved October 27, 1965.*

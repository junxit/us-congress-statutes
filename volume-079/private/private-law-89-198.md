# Private Law 89–198

> AN ACT To provide for the free entry of a mass spectrometer which was imported during May 1963 for the use of Stanford University, Stanford, California.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to admit free of duty the mass spectrometer (including all accompanying equipment, parts, accessories, and appurtenances) imported for the use of Stanford University, Stanford, California, which was entered during May 1963, pursuant to Consumption Entry 3970.
- **(b)** If the liquidation of the entry of any article described in subsection (a) has become final, such entry shall be reliquidated and the appropriate refund of duty shall be made.


*Approved November 2, 1965.*

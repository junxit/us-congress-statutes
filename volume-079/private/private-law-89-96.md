# Private Law 89–96

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Sedat M. Ayata.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the pur-poses of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Sedat M. Ay at a shall be held and considered to be lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of October 1, 1958.


*Approved September 25, 1965.*

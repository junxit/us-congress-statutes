# Private Law 89–194

> AN ACT For the relief of Margaret Elizabeth and Frederick Henry Todd.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Margaret Elizabeth Todd and Frederick Henry Todd shall be held and considered to have complied with the provisions of section 316 of that Act as they relate to residence and physical presence.


*Approved November 1, 1965.*

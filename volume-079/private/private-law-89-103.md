# Private Law 89–103

> AN ACT For the relief of Angelo lannuzzi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Angelo lannuzzi shall be held and considered to be within the purview of section 101(b)(1)(B) of that Act.


*Approved September 29, 1965.*

# Private Law 89–24

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Ana Cristina Rainforth.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Ana Cristina ainforth, the widow of Robert Ray Rainforth, a citizen of the United States, shall be held and considered to be within the purview of section 101(a)(27)(A) of that Act and the provisions of section 205 of the said Act shall not be applicable in this case.


*Approved August 2, 1965.*

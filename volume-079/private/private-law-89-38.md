# Private Law 89–38

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Jose S. Lastra.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Doctor Jose S. Lastra may be naturalized upon compliance with all of the requirements of title III of the Immigration and Nationality Act, except that no period of residence or physical presence within the United States or any State shall be required in addition to his residence and physical presence within the United States since October 30, 1961.


*Approved August 28, 1965.*

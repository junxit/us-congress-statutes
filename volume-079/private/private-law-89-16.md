# Private Law 89–16

> AN ACT For the relief of Alfred Estrada.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of title III of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Alfred Estrada shall be held and considered to have complied with the residence and physical presence requirements of section 316 of the said Act.


*Approved July 28, 1965.*

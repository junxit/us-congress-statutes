# Private Law 89–106

> AN ACT For the relief of Oh Wha Ja (Penny Korleen Doughty).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203(a)(2) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Oh Wha Ja (Penny Korleen Doughty) shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien daughter of Mr. and Mrs. Edwin Doughty, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents of the beneficiary shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved September 29, 1965.*

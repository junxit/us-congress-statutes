# Private Law 89–133

> AN ACT For the relief of Henryka Lyska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Henryka Lyska may be classified as an eligible orphan within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of that Act, and a petition may lie filed in behalf of the said Henryka Lyska. by Mr. and Mrs. Stanley Lyska, citizens of the United Slates, pursuant to section 205(b) of the Immigration and Nationality Act subject to all the conditions in that section relating to eligible orphans.


*Approved October 19, 1965.*

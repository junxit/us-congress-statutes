# Private Law 89–29

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Phoebe Thompson Neesham.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 301(a)(7) of the Immigration and Nationality’ Act, Mrs. Phoebe Thompson Neesham shall be held and considered to have been physically present in the United States for a period of five years after attaining the age of fourteen years.


*Approved August 3, 1965.*

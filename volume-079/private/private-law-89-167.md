# Private Law 89–167

> AN ACT For the relief of F. F. Hintze.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That F. F. Hintze, 112 University Street, Salt Lake City, Utah, is relieved of all liability to the United States with respect to accrued rentals in the amount of $1,280.00 claimed to be due the United States under oil and gas lease, serial Cheyenne 066038.


*Approved October 30, 1965.*

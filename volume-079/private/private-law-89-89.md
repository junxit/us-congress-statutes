# Private Law 89–89

> AN ACT For the relief of Maria Tsilis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Maria Tsi lis shall be deemed to be within the purview of section 101 (b)(1)(E) of that Act, and she shall be considered eligible for first preference quota status under section 203(a)(1) of the said Act as one following to join her adoptive parents, Mr. and Mrs. Stavros Manthos Tsi lis, and shall be accorded the same priority in the issuance of the visa as that, accorded her adoptive parents: Provided, That the said Maria Tsilis shall enter the United States within one year following the date of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved September 22, 1965.*

# Private Law 89–195

> AN ACT To provide for the free entry of one mass spectrometer-gas chromatograph for the use of Oklahoma State University, Stillwater, Oklahoma.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to admit free of duty a mass spectrometer-gas chromatograph (including all accompanying equipment, parts, accessories, and appurtenances) for the use of Oklahoma State University.
- **(b)** If the liquidation of the entry of the article described in subsection (a) has become final, such entry shall be reliquidated and the appropriate refund of duty shall be made.


*Approved November 1, 1965.*

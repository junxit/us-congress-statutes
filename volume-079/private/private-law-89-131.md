# Private Law 89–131

> AN ACT For the relief of Choy-Sim Mah.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Choy-Sim Mah, the widow of Poi Wong, a citizen of the United States, shall be held and considered to be within the purview of section 101 (a)(27)(A) of that Act and the provisions of section 205 of the said Act shall not be applicable in this case.


*Approved October 19, 1965.*

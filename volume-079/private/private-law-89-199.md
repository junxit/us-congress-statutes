# Private Law 89–199

> AN ACT To provide fur the free entry of one mass spectrometer for the use of the University of Chicago.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to admit free of duty one mass spectrometer (including all accompanying equipment, parts, accessories, and appurtenances) for the use of the 1 niversity of Chicago.
- **(b)** If the liquidation of the entry of any article descrilied in subsection (a) has become final, such entry shall be reliquidated and the appropriate refund of duty shall be made.


*Approved November 2, 1965.*

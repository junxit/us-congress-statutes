# Private Law 89–116

> AN ACT For the relief of Miss Rosa Basile DeSantis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Miss Rosa Basile DeSantis may be classified as an eligible orphan within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of the Act, and a petition filed in her behalf by Santa DeSantis, a citizen of the United States, may be approved pursuant to section 205(b) of the Act, subject to all the conditions in that section relating to eligible orphans.


*Approved October 1, 1965.*

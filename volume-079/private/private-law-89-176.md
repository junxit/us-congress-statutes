# Private Law 89–176

> AN ACT For the relief of Rifkin Textiles Corporation.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** notwithstanding the time limitation prescribed by section 520 of the Tariff Act of 1930 (19 U.S.C. 1520) or any other statute of limitation, the consumption entries made on behalf of Rifkin Textiles Corporation of New York, New York, and set out in subsection (b), may be reliquidated in accordance with the law applicable as of the date such entries where made and refund of duties may be made in accordance with such reliquidation if application for such reliquidation is made within one year after the date of enactment of this Act.
- **(b)** The consumption entries referred to in subsection (a) and the date on which each such entry was liquidated are as follows:

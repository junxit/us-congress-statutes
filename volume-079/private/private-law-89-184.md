# Private Law 89–184

> AN ACT For the relief Of Ping-Kwan Fong.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Ping-Kwan Fong shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of September 24, 1947.


*Approved October 31, 1965.*

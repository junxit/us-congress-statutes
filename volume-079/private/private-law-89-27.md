# Private Law 89–27

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Ignace D. Liu.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, Doctor Ignace D. Liu shall be deemed to be within the purview of section 2 of the Act of October 24, 1962 (76 Stat. 1247).


*Approved August 3, 1965.*

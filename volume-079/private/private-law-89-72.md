# Private Law 89–72

> AN ACT For the relief of Jose L. Rodriguez.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Jose L. Rodriguez shall be held and considered to have retained his priority on the quota waiting list as of March 24, 1952, the date on Which he first registered as an intending immigrant.


*Approved September 17, 1965.*

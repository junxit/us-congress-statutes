# Private Law 89–33

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Antonio R. Perez.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Antonio Perez shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of January 4, 1956.


*Approved August 4, 1965.*

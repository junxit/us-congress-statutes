# House Concurrent Resolution 454

> DIAMOND DIES AND BISMUTH ALLOYS–DISPOSAL

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Congress expressly approves, pursuant to section 3(e) of the Strategic and Critical Materials Stock Piling Act (50 U.S.C. 98b(e)), the disposal from the national stockpile of approximately eight thousand three hundred seventy-four pieces of subgrade small diamond dies and approximately thirty-six thousand five hundred eighty pounds of nonstockpile grade bismuth alloys.


*Passed August 23, 1965.*

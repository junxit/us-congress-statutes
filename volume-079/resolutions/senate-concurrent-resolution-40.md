# Senate Concurrent Resolution 40

> CORRECTION OF ENROLLED BILL S. 1229

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Secretary of the Senate, in the enrollment of the bill (S. 1229), to provide uniform policies with respect to recreation and fish and wildlife benefits and costs of Federal multiple-purpose water resource projects, and for other purposes, is authorized and directed to strike out the words “Act of 1965” in the last line of subsection (h) of section 6.


*Agreed to June 28, 1965.*

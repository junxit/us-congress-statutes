# House Concurrent Resolution 519

> HEARINGS TO AMEND THE IMMIGRATION AND NATIONALITY ACT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed for the use of the Committee on the Judiciary of the House of Representatives six thousand additional copies of the hearings before Subcommittee Numbered 1 of the Committee on the Judiciary of the House of Representatives on H.R. 2580, to amend the Immigration and Nationality Act, and for other purposes, dated March 3, 8, 10, 11, 18, and 31: April 6; May 18, 20, 26, and 27; and June 1, 1965.


*Passed October 22, 1965.*

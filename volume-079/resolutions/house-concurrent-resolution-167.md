# House Concurrent Resolution 167

> REAL PROPERTY ACQUISITION IN FEDERAL PROGRAMS, COMPENSATION STUDY

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Public Works, House of Representatives, one thousand additional copies of a study made by that committee of compensation and assistance for persons affected by real property acquisition in Federal and federally assisted programs.


*Passed March 29, 1965.*

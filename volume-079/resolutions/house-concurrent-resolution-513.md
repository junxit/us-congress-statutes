# House Concurrent Resolution 513

> HEARING ON “LOWER COLORADO RIVER BASIN PROJECT”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Interior and Insular Affairs, House of Representatives, twenty-five hundred additional copies of the committee hearing entitled “Lower Colorado River Basin Project”, Eighty-ninth Congress, first session.


*Passed October 22, 1965.*

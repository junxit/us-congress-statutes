# House Concurrent Resolution 442

> CORRECTION OF ENROLLED BILL H.R. 8371

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Clerk of the House of Representatives, in the enrollment of the bill (H.R. 8371) to reduce excise taxes, and for other purposes, is authorized and directed to strike out “gasoline” in the new section 6424(b)(2) added by section 202(b) of the bill and to insert in lieu thereof “lubricating oil”.


*Passed June 17, 1965.*

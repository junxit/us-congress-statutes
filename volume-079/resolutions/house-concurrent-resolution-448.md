# House Concurrent Resolution 448

> CRUSADE FOR SAFETY DAY–PROCLAMATION AUTHORIZATION

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President is authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating November 26, 1965. as Crusade for Safety Day and calling upon the people of the United States to observe such day with appropriate ceremonies and activities designed to reduce traffic accidents.


*Passed October 22, 1965.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 44

> “THE COMMISSION ON INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Government (Operations ten thousand additional copies of House Document 198 of the Eighty-fourth Congress entitled “The Commission on Intergovernmental Relations.”


*Agreed to August 26, 1965.*

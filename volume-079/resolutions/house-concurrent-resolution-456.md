# House Concurrent Resolution 456

> PUBLIC LAW 89–97–8OC1AL SECURITY AMENDMENTS OF 1965

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed thirty thousand additional copies of the public law containing the social security amendments enacted in 1965 pursuant to H.R. 6675; of which twenty-two thousand five hundred copies shall be for the House document room, five thousand copies shall be for the Senate document room, two thousand copies shall be for the use of the Committee on Ways and Means, and five hundred copies shall be for the use of the Committee on Finance.


*Passed July 30, 1965.*

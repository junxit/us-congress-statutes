# House Concurrent Resolution 411

> “COMMUNIST ACTIVITIES IN THE BUFFALO, NEW YORK, AREA”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Un-American Activities three thousand additional copies of the publication entitled “Communist Activities in the Buffalo, New York, Area,” Eighty-eighth Congress, first session.


*Passed July 7, 1965.*

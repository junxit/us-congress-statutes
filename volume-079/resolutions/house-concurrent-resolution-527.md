# House Concurrent Resolution 527

> ADJOURNMENT SINE DIE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring;,*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Saturday, October 23, 1965, and that when they adjourn on said day, they stand adjourned sine die.


*Passed October 23, 1965.*

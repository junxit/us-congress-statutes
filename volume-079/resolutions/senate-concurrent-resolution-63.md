# Senate Concurrent Resolution 63

> HEARINGS ON ECONOMIC CONCENTRATION, VOLUMES 2 AND 3

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on the Judiciary two thousand additional copies of volume 2 and volume 3 of the hearings held by its Subcommittee on Antitrust and Monopoly during the Eighty-ninth Congress, first session, on economic concentration.


*Agreed to October 22, 1965.*

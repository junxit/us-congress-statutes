# House Concurrent Resolution 509

> HEARINGS AND REPORT ON CRIME IN THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on the District of Columbia, House of Representatives, one thousand additional copies each of the hearings held by that committee in the Eighty-eighth Congress on crime legislation for the District of Columbia; the hearings held jointly by the House District of Columbia Committee and the Senate District of Columbia Committee in the Eighty-eighth Congress on crime in the District of Columbia; and House Report Numbered 176 of the Eighty-ninth Congress, entitled “District of Columbia Crime”.


*Passed October 22, 1965.*

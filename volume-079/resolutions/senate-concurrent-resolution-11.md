# Senate Concurrent Resolution 11

> SUBCOMMITTEE ON NATIONAL SECURITY STAFFING AND OPERATIONS HEARINGS

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on Government Operations three thousand additional copies of the hearings (including brief staff studies thereon) held by its Subcommittee on National Security Staffing and Operations during the Eighty-eighth Congress.


*Agreed to August 17, 1965.*

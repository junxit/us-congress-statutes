# Senate Concurrent Resolution 37

> HEARINGS ON ECONOMIC CONCENTRATION, ETC., PART 1

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on the Judiciary two thousand additional copies of part 1 of the hearings held by its Subcommittee on Antitrust and Monopoly during the Eighty-eighth Congress, second session, on economic concentration and its overall and conglomerate aspects.


*Agreed to August 17, 1965.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 451

> “THE PRAYER ROOM IN THE UNITED STATES CAPITOL”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed fifty-four thousand two hundred additional copies of House Document Numbered 234 of the Eighty-fourth Congress, entitled “The Prayer Room in the United States Capitol”, of which forty-three thousand nine hundred copies shall be for the use of the House of Representatives and ten thousand three hundred copies shall be for the use of the Senate.

## § 2.

Copies of such document shall be prorated to Members of the Senate and House of Representatives for a period of sixty days, after which the unused balance shall revert to the respective Senate and House Document Rooms.


*Passed September 10, 1965.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 512

> HEARING ON HOME RULE FOR THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate Concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on the District of Columbia two thousand additional copies of the hearing held by that committee during the current session on home rule for the District of Columbia.


*Passed October 22, 1965.*

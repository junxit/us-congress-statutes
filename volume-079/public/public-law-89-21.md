# Public Law 89–21

> AN ACT To authorize appropriations for procurement of small patrol cutters for the Coast Guard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in addition to any funds previously authorized to be appropriated, funds are hereby authorized to be appropriated for fiscal year 1966 for the use of the Coast Guard in the amount of $6,260,000 for the procurement of seventeen small patrol cutters.


*Approved May 21, 1965.*

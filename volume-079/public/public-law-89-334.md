# Public Law 89–334

> AN ACT To amend the Small Business Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4(c) of the Small Business Act is amended by striking out “$1,721,000,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$1,841,000,000”.


*Approved November 8, 1965.*

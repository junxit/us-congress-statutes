# Public Law 89–201

> AN ACT To provide an increase in the retired pay of certain members of the former Lighthouse Service.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the annual rate of retired pay of each person retired prior to January 1, 1963, under section 6 of the Act of June 20, 1918, as amended and supplemented, shall be increased by 6.5 per centum, effective on the first day of the first calendar month following the date of enactment of this Act.


*Approved September 25, 1965.*

# Public Law 89–78

> AN ACT To amend the Small Business Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4(c) of the Small Business Act (15 U.S.C. 633(c)) is amended—by striking out “$341,000,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$461,000,000.”


*Approved July 21, 1965.*

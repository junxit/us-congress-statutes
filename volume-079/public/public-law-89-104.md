# Public Law 89–104

> AN ACT To amend the Act of August 7, 1935, to increase the authorized annual share of the United States as an adhering member of the International Council of Scientific Unions and. Associated Unions.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of August 7, 1935 (22 U.S.C. 274), is amended by striking out “$65,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$100,000”.


*Approved August 3, 1965.*

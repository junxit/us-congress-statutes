# Public Law 89–1

> JOINT RESOLUTION Extending the date for transmission of the Budget and the Economic Report.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** notwithstanding the provisions of section 201 of the Act of June 10, 1922, as amended (31 U.S.C. 11), the President shall transmit to the Congress not later than January 25, 1965, the Budget for the Fiscal Year 1966; and (b) notwithstanding the provisions of section 3 of the Act of February 20, 1946, as amended (15 U.S.C. 1022), the President shall transmit to the Congress not later than January 28, 1965, the Economic Report.


*Approved January 13, 1965.*

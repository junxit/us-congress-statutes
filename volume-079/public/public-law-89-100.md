# Public Law 89–100

> AN ACT To amend the Organic Act of Guam to provide for the payment of legislative salaries and expenses by the government of Guam.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (e) of section 26 of the Organic Act of Guam (64 Stat. 384, 391; 48 U.S.C. 1421d(e)), is amended to read as follows:

> - **(e)** Each member of the legislature shall be paid such compensation and shall receive such additional allowances or benefits as may be fixed under the laws of Guam. Such compensation, allowances, or benefits, together with all other legislative expenses shall be appropriated by, and paid out of funds of, the government of Guam.”


*Approved July 30, 1965.*

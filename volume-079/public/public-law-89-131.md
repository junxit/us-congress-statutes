# Public Law 89–131

> AN ACT To amend the Act of June 23, 1949, relating to the telephone and telegraph service furnished Members of the House of Representatives.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That clause (1) of subsection (b) of section 2 of the Act entitled “An Act relating to telephone and telegraph service and clerk hire for Members of the House of Representatives”, approved June 23, 1949, as amended (2 U.S.C. 46g), is amended by striking out “five” and inserting in lien thereof “four”.

## § 2.

Tire amendment made by the first section of this Act shall take effect as of noon, January 3, 1965.


*Approved August 21, 1965.*

# Public Law 89–256

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 1966, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the joint resolution of September 30, 1965 (Public Law 89–221), is hereby amended by striking out “October 15, 1965” and inserting in lieu thereof “October 23, 1965”.


*Approved October 15, 1965.*

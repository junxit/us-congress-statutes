# Public Law 89–152

> AN ACT To amend the Universal Military Training and Service Act of 1951, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Universal Military’ Training and Service Act, as amended, is hereby further amended as follows:

> ## § 1.
>
> Section 12(b)(3) is amended to read as follows:
>
> - **(3)** who forges, alters, knowingly destroys, knowingly mutilates, or in any manner changes any such certificate or any notation duly and validly inscribed thereon: or”.


*Approved August 30, 1965.*

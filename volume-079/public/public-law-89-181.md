# Public Law 89–181

> AN ACT To increase the authorization of appropriations for the support of the Gorges Memorial Laboratory.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, effective for fiscal years ending after June 30, 1963, the first section of the Act entitled “An Act to authorize a permanent annual appropriation for the maintenance and operation of the Gorgas Memorial Laboratory”, approved May 7, 1928, as amended (45 Stat. 491; 22 U.S.C. 278), is amended by striking out “$250,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “not to exceed $500,000”.


*Approved September 11, 1965.*

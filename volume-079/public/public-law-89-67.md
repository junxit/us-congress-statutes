# Public Law 89–67

> AN ACT To amend provisions of law relating to the settlement of admiralty claims.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 4802 (c), 4803(c), 7622(c), 7623(c), 9802(c), and 9803(c) of title 10, United States Code, are each amended by striking out the figure “$1,000” and inserting the figure “$10,000” in place thereof.


*Approved July 7, 1965.*

# Public Law 89–69

> AN ACT To extend the Juvenile Delinquency and Youth Offenses Control Act of 1991.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 6 of the Juvenile Delinquency and Youth Offenses Control Act of 1961 is amended to read as follows:

> ## § 6. “authorization of appropriations
>
> For the purpose of carrying out the program provided for in the preceding sections of this Act during the period ending June 30, 1967, there is hereby authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1962, and each of the three succeeding fiscal years, the sum of $10,000,000, for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1966, the sum of $6,500,000, and for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1967, the sum of $10,000,000.”


*Approved July 8, 1965.*

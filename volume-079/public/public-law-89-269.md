# Public Law 89–269

> AN ACT To amend the Act of May 17, 1954 (68 Stat. 98), as amended, providing for the construction of the Jefferson National Expansion Manorial at the site of old Saint Louis, Missouri, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of May 17, 1954 (68 Stat. 98), entitled “An Act to provide for the construction of the Jefferson National Expansion Memorial at the site of old Saint Louis, Missouri, in general accordance with the plan approved by the United States Territorial Expansion Memorial Commission, and for other purposes,” as amended by the Act of September 6, 1958 (72 Stat. 1794), is hereby further amended by striking the figure “$17,250,000” from section 4 thereof and inserting in lieu thereof the figure “$23,250,000.”


*Approved October 19, 1965.*

# Public Law 89–159

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 19(56. and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the joint resolution of July 30, 1965 (Public Law 89–96), is hereby amended by striking out “August 31, 1965” and inserting in lieu thereof “September 30, 1965”.


*Approved September 1, 1965.*

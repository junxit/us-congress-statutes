# Public Law 89–89

> AN ACT To extend the provisions of title XII of the Merchant Marine Act, 1936, relating to war risk insurance, for an additional five years, ending September 7, 1970.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1214 of title XII of the Merchant Marine Act, 1936, as amended (U.S.C., title 46, sec. 1294), is amended by striking out “fifteen years” and inserting in lieu thereof “twenty years”.


*Approved July 27, 1965.*

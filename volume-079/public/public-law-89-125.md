# Public Law 89–125

> AN ACT To amend the National Arts and Cultural Development Act of 1964 with respect to the authorization of appropriations therein.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 10 of the National Arts and Cultural Development Act of 1964 is amended by inserting “per annum” after “$150,000”.


*Approved August 13, 1965.*

# Public Law 89–303

> AN ACT To amend the requirements relating to lumber under the Shipping Act, 1916.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 18(b)(1) of the Shipping Act, 1916 (46 U.S.C. 817(b)(1)), is amended by inserting before the word “lumber” wherever it appears in this section the word “softwood”.


*Approved October 30, 1965.*

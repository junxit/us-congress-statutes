# Public Law 89–221

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 196(3, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 101 of the joint resolution approved June 30, 1965 (Public Law 89–58), as amended, is amended by adding a new subsection as follows: “(e) Such amounts as may be necessary for continuing Civil Supersonic Aircraft Development Activities which have been conducted in the fiscal year 1966 but at a rate for operations not in excess of the rate provided in the supplemental estimate pending before the Congress until the enactment into law of the applicable appropriation”, and section 102 is further amended by striking out “September 30, 1965” and inserting in lieu thereof “October 15, 1965”, except as provided in section 101(e) hereof.


*Approved September 30, 1965.*

# Public Law 89–340

> JOINT RESOLUTION Establishing that the second regular session of the Eighty-ninth Congress convene at noon on Monday, January 10, 1966.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second regular session of the Eighty-ninth Congress shall begin at noon on Monday, January 10, 1966.


*Approved November 8, 1965.*

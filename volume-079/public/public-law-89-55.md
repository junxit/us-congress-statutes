# Public Law 89–55

> JOINT RESOLUTION To extend the Area Redevelopment Act for a period of two months.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (a) of section 29 of the Area Redevelopment Act is amended by striking out “June 30, 1965.” and inserting in lieu thereof “August 31, 1965.”


*Approved June 30, 1965.*

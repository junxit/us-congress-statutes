# Public Law 90–547

> AN ACT To amend the Water Resources Planning Act to revise the authorization of appropriations for administering the provisions of the Act, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United State# of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 401 of the Water Resources Planning Act (Public Law 89–80; 79 Stat. 244) is amended by deleting “$300,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$500,000”.


*Approved October 2, 1968.*

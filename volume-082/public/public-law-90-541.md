# Public Law 90–541

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 1969, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That clause (c) of section 102 of the joint resolution of June 29, 1968 (Public Law 90–366), is hereby further amended by striking out “September 30, 1968” and inserting in lieu thereof “October 12, 1968”.


*Approved October 1, 1968.*

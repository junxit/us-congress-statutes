# Public Law 90–330

> AN ACT To extend the authority to grant a special thirty-day leave for members of the uniformed services who voluntarily extend their tours of duty in hostile fire areas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 703(b) of title 10, United States Code, is amended by striking out “June 30, 1968”, and inserting in lieu thereof “June 30, 1970”.


*Approved June 5, 1968.*

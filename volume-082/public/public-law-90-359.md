# Public Law 90–359

> AN ACT To amend sections 3 and 4 of the Act approved September 22. UMM (78 Stat. 990), providing for an investigation and study to determine a site for the construction of a sea-level canal connecting the Atlantic and Pacific Oceans.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act approved September 22, 1864 (Public Law 88–609, 78 Stat. 990), as amended, is hereby further amended (1) by striking out “December 1, 1969” in section 3 and inserting in lieu thereof “December 1, 1970”, and (2) by striking out “$17,500,000” in section 4 and inserting in lien thereof “$24,000,000”.


*Approved June 22, 1968.*

# Public Law 90–603

> AN ACT To amend title 37, United States Code, to clarify the conditions under which physicians and dentists who extend their service on active duty in a uniformed service may be paid continuation pay.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 311 (a) (2) of title 37, United States Code, is amended to read as follows:

> - **(2)** has completed his initial active duty obligation; and”.

## § 2.

The amendment made by this Act becomes effective as of January 1, 1968.


*Approved October 18, 1968.*

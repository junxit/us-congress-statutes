# Public Law 90–253

> JOINT RESOLUTION Authorizing the President to proclaim the period February 11 through 17, 1908, as “LULAC Week”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is hereby authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating the period February 11 through 17, 1968, as “LULAC Week”, and calling upon the people of the United States to observe such week with appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Approved February 10, 1968.*

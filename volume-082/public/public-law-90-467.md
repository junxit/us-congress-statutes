# Public Law 90–467

> AN ACT To amend the Life Insurance Act of the District of Columbia, approved June 19, 1934 (48 Stat. 1125).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 11 of chapter II of the Life Insurance Act, approved June 19, 1934 (48 Stat, 1132), as amended (D.C. Code, sec. 35–410), is amended by adding at the end of the first paragraph thereof the following: “This paragraph shall not apply to an alien company which maintains in the United States as required by law, assets held in trust for the benefit of the United States policyholders in an amount, not less than the sum of its required capital deposit and the amount of its outstanding liabilities arising out of. its insurance transactions in the United States.”


*Approved August 8, 1968.*

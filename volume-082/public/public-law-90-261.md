# Public Law 90–261

> AN ACT To amend section 2 of the Migratory Bird Conservation Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2 of the Migratory Bird Conservation Act is amended by striking out “the Secretary of Commerce” and inserting in lieu thereof “the Secretary of Transportation”.


*Approved March 2, 1968.*

# Public Law 90–405

> AN ACT Authorizing the President to proclaim August 11, 1968, as “Family Reunion Day”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is hereby authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating August 11, 1968, as “Family Reunion Day”, and calling upon the people of the United States to observe, such day with appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Approved July 18, 1968.*

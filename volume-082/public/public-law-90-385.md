# Public Law 90–385

> AN ACT To increase the limitation on the number of officers for the Coast Guard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (a) of section 42 of title 14, United States Code, is amended by striking out “four” and inserting “five” in place thereof so that the subsection will read as follows:

> - **(a)** The total number of commissioned officers, excluding commissioned warrant officers, on active duty in the Coast Guard shall not exceed five thousand.”


*Approved July 5, 1968.*

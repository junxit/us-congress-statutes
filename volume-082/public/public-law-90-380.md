# Public Law 90–380

> AN ACT To amend section 11–341(b) of the District of Columbia Code which relates to the sales price for the retorts of the opinions of the United States Court of Appeals for the District of Columbia Circuit.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 11–341 (b) of the District of Columbia Code is amended by striking out “at not more than $6.50 per volume” and inserting in lieu thereof “at not more than $12 per volume”.


*Approved July 5, 1968.*

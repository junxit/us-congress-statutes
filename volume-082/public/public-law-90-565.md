# Public Law 90–565

> JOINT RESOLUTION To correct certain references in section 4(i) of the Act entitled “An Act to amend chapter 37 of title 36 of the United States Code with respect to the veterans’ home loan program, to amend the National Housing Act with respect to interest rates on insured mortgages, and for other purposes”, approved May 7, 1968.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4(i) of the Act of May 7, 1968 (Public Law 90–301), is amended—

- **(1)** by striking out “(a)”, each place it appears, and inserting in lieu thereof “(b)”;
- **(2)** by striking out “(4)” and inserting in lieu thereof “(5)”; and
- **(3)** by striking out “(5)” and inserting in lieu thereof “(6)”.


*Approved October 12, 1968.*

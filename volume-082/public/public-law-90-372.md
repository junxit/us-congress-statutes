# Public Law 90–372

> AN ACT To name the United States customhouse, Providence, Rhode Island, the “John E. Fogarty Federal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States customhouse, Providence. Rhode Island, shall, from and after the date of enactment of this Act, be known and designated as the “John E. Fogarty Federal Building”. Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to such United States customhouse shall be held to be a reference to the “John E. Fogarty Federal Building”.


*Approved July 2, 1968.*

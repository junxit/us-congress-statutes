# Public Law 90–536

> AN ACT To amend the Tennessee Valley Authority Act of 1933 with respect to certain provisions applicable to condemnation proceedings.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the last six paragraphs of section 25 of the Tennessee Valley Authority Act of 1933 (48 Stat. 70), as amended (16 U.S.C. 831x), are hereby repealed.

## § 2.

The amendment made by this Act shall be effective only with respect to condemnation proceedings initiated after thirty days following the date of enactment of this Act.


*Approved September 28, 1968.*

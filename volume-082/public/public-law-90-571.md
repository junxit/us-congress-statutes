# Public Law 90–571

> AN ACT To extend until July 15, 1969, the suspension of duty on electrodes for use in producing aluminum.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the matter appearing in the effective period column for item 909.25 of the Tariff Schedules of the United States (19 U.S.C., sec. 1202, item 909.25) is amended by striking out “7/15/68” and inserting in lieu thereof “7/15/69”.

## § 2.

The amendment made by the first section of this Act shall apply with respect to articles entered, or withdrawn from warehouse, for consumption, after July 15, 1968.


*Approved October 12, 1968.*

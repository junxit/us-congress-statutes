# Public Law 90–406

> JOINT RESOLUTION To authorize the President to issue a proclamation designating the week of October 13, 1968, as “Salute to Eisenhower Week”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States is hereby authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating the week of October 13, 1968, as “Salute to Eisenhower Week”, and calling upon the people of the United States to observe such week with appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Approved July 18, 1968.*

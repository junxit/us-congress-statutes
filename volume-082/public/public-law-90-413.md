# Public Law 90–413

> AN ACT To amend section 1730 of title 18, United States Code, to permit the uniform or badge of the letter-carrier branch of the postal service to be worn In theatrical, television, or motion-picture productions under certain circumstances.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1730 of title 18, United States Code, is hereby amended by adding thereto a new paragraph, immediately following the end of the present provision, which shall read as follows:

> “The provisions of the preceding paragraph shall not apply to an actor or actress in a theatrical, television, or motion-picture production who wears the uniform or budge of the letter-carrier branch of the Postal Service while portraying a member of that service, if the portrayal does not tend to discredit that service.”


*Approved July 21, 1968.*

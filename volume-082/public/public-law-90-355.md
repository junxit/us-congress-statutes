# Public Law 90–355

> AN ACT To amend the Act of August 9, 1955, to authorize longer term leases of Indian lands on the Hualapai Reservation in Arizona.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second sentence of section 1 of the Act of August 9, 1955 (69 Stat. 539), as amended (25 U.S.C. 415), is hereby further amended by inserting the words “the Hualapai Reservation,” after the words “the Fort Mojave Reservation,”.


*Approved June 20, 1968.*

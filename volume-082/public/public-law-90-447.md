# Public Law 90–447

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making continuing appropriations for the fiscal year i960, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That clause (c) of section 102 of the joint resolution of June 29, 1968 (Public Law 90–366), is hereby amended by striking out “July 31, 1968” and inserting in lieu thereof “September 30, 1968”.


*Approved July 31, 1968, 7:45 p.m.*

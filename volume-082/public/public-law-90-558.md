# Public Law 90–558

> JOINT RESOLUTION Recognizing the significant part which Harry S. Truman played in the creation of the United Nations.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States is hereby authorized and requested to issue on October 24, 1968, a proclamation recognizing the significant part which Harry S. Truman, as President of the United States, played in the creation of the United Nations.


*Approved October 11, 1968.*

# Public Law 90–604

> AN ACT To authorize the disposal of magnesium from the national stockpile.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America In Congress assembled,*

That the Administrator of General Services is hereby authorized to dispose of, by negotiation or otherwise, approximately fifty-five thousand short tons of magnesium now held in the national stockpile established pursuant to the Strategic and Critical Materials Stock Piling Act (50 U.S.C. 90–98h). Such disposition may be made without regard to the provisions of section 3 of the Strategic and Critical Materials Stock Piling Act: Provided, That the time and method of disposition shall be fixed with due regard to the protection of the United States against avoidable loss and the protection of producers, processors, and consumers against avoidable disruption of their usual markets.


*Approved October 18, 1968.*

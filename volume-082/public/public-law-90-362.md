# Public Law 90–362

> AN ACT To authorize the further amendment of the Peace Corps Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3(d) of the Peace Corps Act, as amended, which authorizes appropriations to curry out the purposes of that Act, is amended by striking out “1968” and “$115,700,000” and substituting “1969” and “$112,800,000”, respectively.


*Approved June 27, 1968.*

# Public Law 90–305

> JOINT RESOLUTION To designate May 20, 1968, as “Charlotte, North Carolina, Day”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That May 20, 1968, is hereby designated as “Charlotte, North Carolina, Day” in commemoration of the two hundredth anniversary of such city, and the President is authorized and requested to issue a proclamation inviting the people of the United States to observe such day with appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Approved May 13, 1968.*

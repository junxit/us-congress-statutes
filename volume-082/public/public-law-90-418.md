# Public Law 90–418

> AN ACT To amend the Commodity Exchange Act, as amended, to make frozen concentrated orange juice subject to the provisions of such Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Home of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the third sentence of section 2(a) of the Commodity Exchange Act, as amended (7 U.S.C. 2), is amended by striking out “and livestock products” and inserting in lieu thereof “, livestock products, and frozen concentrated orange juice”.


*Approved July 23, 1968.*

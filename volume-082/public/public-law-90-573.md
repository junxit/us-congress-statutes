# Public Law 90–573

> AN ACT To authorize the Commissioner of the District of Columbia to enter into contracts for the inspection, maintenance, and repair of fixed equipment in District-owned buildings for periods not to exceed three years.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of the District of Columbia is authorized to enter into contracts for periods not exceeding three years for the inspection, maintenance, and repair of fixed equipment in buildings owned by the District of Columbia.


*Approved October 12, 1968.*

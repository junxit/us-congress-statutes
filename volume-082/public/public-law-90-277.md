# Public Law 90–277

> JOINT RESOLUTION To provide for the designation of the second week of May of 1968 as “National School Safety Patrol Week”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second week of May of 1968 is hereby designated as “National School Safety Patrol Week” and the President is requested to issue a proclamation calling upon all people of the United States for the observance of such a week with appropriate proceedings and ceremonies.


*Approved March 29, 1968.*

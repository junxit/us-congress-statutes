# Public Law 90–294

> AN ACT To amend the Communications Act of 1934 by extending the authorization of appropriations for the Corporation for Public Broadcasting.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraphs (1) and (2) of section 396 (k) of the Communications Act of 1934 are each amended by striking out “1968” and inserting in lieu thereof “1969”.


*Approved April 26, 1968.*

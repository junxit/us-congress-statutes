# House Concurrent Resolution 614

> ANTI-CRIME PROGRAM HEARINGS

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on the Judiciary, House of Representatives, one thousand additional copies of hearings entitled “Anti-Crime Program, Hearings before Subcommittee No. 5 of the Committee on the Judiciary, House of Representatives, Ninetieth Congress, first session”.


*Passed June 3, 1968.*

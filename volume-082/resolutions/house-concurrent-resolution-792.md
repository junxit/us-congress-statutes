# House Concurrent Resolution 792

> ADJOURN WENT-HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Wednesday, July 3, 1968, they stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Monday, July 8, 1968.


*Passed July 2, 1968.*

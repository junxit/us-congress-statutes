# House Concurrent Resolution 702

> “SUMMARY OF VETERANS LEGISLATION REPORTED, NINETIETH CONGRESS”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed for the use of the Committee on Veterans’ Affairs of the House of Representatives fifty-six thousand one hundred copies of a publication entitled “Summary of Veterans Legislation Reported, Ninetieth Congress”, with an additional forty-three thou-sand nine hundred copies for the use of Members of the House of Representatives.


*Passed June 3, 1968.*

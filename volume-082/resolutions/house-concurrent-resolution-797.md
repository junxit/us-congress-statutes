# House Concurrent Resolution 797

> “COMMERCIAL BANKS AND THEIR TRUST ACTIVITIES: EMERGING INFLUENCE ON THE AMERICAN ECONOMY”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That these shall be printed for the use of the Committee on Banking and Currency of the House of Representatives two thousand copies of the two-volume subcommittee print entitled “Commercial Banks and Their Trust Activities: Emerging Influence on the American Economy,” a staff report prepared for the Subcommittee on Domestic Finance of the Committee on Banking and Currency.


*Passed October 11, 1968.*

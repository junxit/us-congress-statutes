# House Concurrent Resolution 44

> VETERANS’ BENEFITS CALCULATOR

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That after the conclusion of the second session of the Ninetieth Congress there shall be printed fifty thousand two hundred and forty copies of a Veterans7 Benefits Calculator prepared by the Veterans’ A flairs Committee of which two thousand copies shall be for the use of the Veterans’ Affairs Committee, two thousand copies for the use of the Committee on Finance, thirty-seven thousand four hundred and eighty-five copies for the use of the House of Representatives, and eight thousand seven hundred and fifty-five copies for the use of the Senate.


*Passed June 3, 1968.*

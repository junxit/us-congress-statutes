# House Concurrent Resolution 763

> “FEDERAL EDUCATIONAL POLICIES, PROGRAMS, AND PROPOSALS”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed as a House document a survey and hand-book entitled “Federal Educational Policies, Programs, and Proposals” and that two thousand copies be printed for the use of the Committee on Education and Labor, House of Representatives.


*Passed October 11, 1968.*

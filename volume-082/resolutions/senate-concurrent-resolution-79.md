# Senate Concurrent Resolution 79

> CORRECTIONS IN ENROLLMENT OF S. 827

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Secretary of the Senate, in the enrollment of the bill, S. 827, entitled “An Act to establish a nationwide system of trails, and for other purposes”, be authorized to make the fol hewing corrections: In section 4(a) (i) after the words “such trails are reasonably accessible to urban areas, and” add a comma and insert, the word “or” and in section 4(b) (i) at the end of the paragraph delete the comma after the word “and” and delete the word “or”.


*Agreed to September 19, 1968.*

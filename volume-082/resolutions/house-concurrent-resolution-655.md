# House Concurrent Resolution 655

> WARSAW GHETTO UPRISING-ANNIVERSARY COMMEMORATION

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That it is the sense of Congress to recognize and acknowledge the world significance of the Warsaw ghetto uprising as a reaffirmation of the ineradicable determination to fight for freedom from oppression and that Congress joins in commemorating on April 25 the twenty-fifth anniversary of the Warsaw ghetto uprising against the Nazi occupation forces by the beleaguered and outnumbered Jews of the Warsaw ghetto.


*Passed April 25, 1968.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 66

> “LONG-RANGE PROGRAM AND RESEARCH NEEDS IN AGING AND RELATED FIELDS”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Special Committee on Aging six thousand additional copies of its hearings of the Ninetieth Congress, entitled “Long-Range Program and Research Needs in Aging and Related Fields”.


*Agreed to May 23, 1968.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 213

> SELECTED LETTERS OF VICE ADMIRAL HYMAN G. RICKOVER

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed as a House document the letters of Vice Admiral Hyman G. Kickover, United States Navy, to the Speaker of the House of Representatives relating to the distinguished Americans in whose honor the United States Navy Polaris nuclear submarines were named. The copy for such House document shall be prepared under the super-vision of the Joint Committee on Printing.

## § 2.

In addition to the usual number, there shall be printed twelve thousand additional copies of such House document, of which four thousand copies shall be for the use of the Senate, and eight thousand copies shall be for the use of the House of Representatives.

## § 3.

That, notwithstanding any provision of the copyright laws and regulations with respect to publications in public domain, the letters shall be subject to copyright by the author thereof, Vice Admiral Hyman G. Rickover, United States Navy.

## § 4.

Copies of such document shall be prorated to Members of the Senate and House of Representatives for a period of sixty days, after which the unused balance shall revert to the respective Senate and House document rooms.


*Passed August 1, 1968.*

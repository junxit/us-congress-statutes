# Senate Concurrent Resolution 83

> ADJOURNMENT SINE DIE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Monday, October 14, 1968, and that when they adjourn on said day, they stand adjourned sine die.


*Agreed to October 14, 1968.*

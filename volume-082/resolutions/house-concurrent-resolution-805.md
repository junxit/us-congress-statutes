# House Concurrent Resolution 805

> ADJOURNMENT-HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the Congress adjourns on Friday, August 2, 1968, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Wednesday, September 4, 1968.


*Passed August 2, 1968.*

# Private Law 90–362

> AN ACT For the relief of Sondra D. Shaw.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Sondra D. Shaw, of San Diego, California, is hereby relieved of liability to the United States in the amount of $12,08.74, and any accrued interest thereon, representing income tax liability claimed by the United States on funds embezzled by her deceased husband from his employer over a five-year period, which resulted in an assessment of taxes for the years 1958 through 1963, inclusive.


*Approved October 24, 1968.*

# Private Law 90–237

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Edgar Reinaldo Nunez Baez.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the pur-poses of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Edgar Reinaldo Nunez Baez shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of February 9, 1963.


*Approved May 20, 1968.*

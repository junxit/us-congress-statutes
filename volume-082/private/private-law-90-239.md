# Private Law 90–239

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Margarita Lorigados.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Margarita Lorigados shall beheld and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of October 10, 1961.


*Approved May 21, 1968.*

# Private Law 90–296

> AN ACT For the relief of Miss Amalia Seresly.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203(a)(1) and 204 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Miss Amalia Seresly shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien unmarried daughter of Mr. Demosthenes Raptelis, a citizen of the United States: Provided, That no natural parents or brothers or sisters of the beneficiary, by virtue of such relationship, shall be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved July 23, 1968.*

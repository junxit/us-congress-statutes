# Private Law 90–342

> JOINT RESOLUTION To grant the status of permanent residence to Maria Mercedes Riewerts.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the case of Marin Mercedes Riewerts, in whose case deportation was suspended in accordance with the provisions of section 19(c) of the Immigration Act of February 5, 1917, as amended (39 Stat. 889; 54 Stat. 672–673), (he Commissioner of Immigration and Naturalization is authorized and directed to cancel deportation proceedings and, in accordance with the provisions of the said section 19(c) of the said Act, to record the alien’s lawful admission for permanent residence as of August 3, 1925, upon payment of a fee of $18 to the Commissioner.


*Approved September 28, 1968.*

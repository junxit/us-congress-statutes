# Private Law 90–274

> AN ACT For the relief of the estate of Charles C. Beaury.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, the estate of Charles C. Beaury, deceased is hereby relieved of liability to the United States in the amount of $288, representing the amount which the late Charles C. Beaury, a substitute carrier in the United States Post Office at Saint Louis, Missouri, was indebted to the United States by reason of the loss of money contained in an envelope known as registered mail which was apparently stolen on or about March 4, 1965, from a mail truck operated by the decedent.


*Approved July 1, 1968.*

# Private Law 90–223

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Anacleto C. Fernandez.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Anacleto C. Fernandez shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of May 31, 1961.


*Approved May 17, 1968.*

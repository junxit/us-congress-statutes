# Private Law 90–334

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Fang Luke Chiu.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Fang Luke Chiu shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of January 12, 1955.


*Approved September 24, 1968.*

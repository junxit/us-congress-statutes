# Private Law 90–229

> AN ACT For the relief of Susan Elizabeth (Cho) Long.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Susan Elizabeth (Cho) Long may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of the Act, upon approval of a petition filed in her behalf by Miss Joyce M. Long, a citizen of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of the said Act.


*Approved May 18, 1968.*

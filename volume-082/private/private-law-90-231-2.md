# Private Law 90–231

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor George S. Ioanndes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor George S. Ioannides shall lie held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of January 18, 1957.


*Approved August 11, 1968.*

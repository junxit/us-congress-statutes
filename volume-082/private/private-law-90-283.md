# Private Law 90–283

> AN ACT For the relief of Arthur Anderson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding any other provision of Jaw, if application is made by Arthur Anderson, of Detroit, Michigan, within ninety days after the date of enactment of this Act for any benefits under title 38, United States Code (as in effect on June 30, 1960), such application shall be deemed to have been filed on June 30, 1960.


*Approved July 2, 1968.*

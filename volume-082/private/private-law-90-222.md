# Private Law 90–222

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Jose Ernesto Garcia y Tojar.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Jose Ernesto Garcia y Tojar shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of November 23, 1961.


*Approved May 17, 1968.*

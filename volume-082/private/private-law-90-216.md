# Private Law 90–216

> AN ACT To authorize the use of the vessel Annie B. to the coastwise trade.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 27 of the Merchant Marine Act of 1920 and the provisions of section 4132 of the Revised Statutes as amended the vessel Annie B., owned by William M. Fifield of Stonington, Maine, may be used in the coastwise trade so long as such vessel is owned by a citizen of the United States and so long as it is used for the transportation of bait and supplies for the lobster and crab fisheries within the State of Maine.


*Approved May 4, 1968.*

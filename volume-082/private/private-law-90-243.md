# Private Law 90–243

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Heriberto Jose Hernandez-Suitrest.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Heriberto Jose Hernandez- shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of September 3, 1960.


*Approved May 21, 1968.*

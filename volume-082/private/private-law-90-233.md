# Private Law 90–233

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Alberto De Jongh.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Alberto De Jongh shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of June 12, 1961.


*Approved May 20, 1968.*

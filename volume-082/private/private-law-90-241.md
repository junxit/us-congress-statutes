# Private Law 90–241

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Jesus Adalberto Quevedo-Avila.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Jesus Adalberto Quevedo-Avila shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of June 7, 1961.


*Approved May 21, 1968.*

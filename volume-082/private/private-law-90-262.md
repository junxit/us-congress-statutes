# Private Law 90–262

> AN ACT For the relief of Lennart Gordon langhorne.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Lennart Gordon Langhorne shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of February 1, 1962.


*Approved June 22, 1968.*

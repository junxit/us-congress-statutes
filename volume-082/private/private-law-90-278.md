# Private Law 90–278

> AN ACT For the relief of Lloyd W. Corbisier.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the annual leave account of Lloyd W. Corbisier, postal employee of Sturgeon Bay, Wisconsin, there shall be added a separate account of three hundred and fifty hours of annual leave, in full settlement of all claims of the said Lloyd W. Corbisier against the United States for compensation for the loss of such leave which was earned by him in the period July 1, 1945, through February 28, 1948, inclusive, while he was employed in the United States Post Office in Sturgeon Bay, Wisconsin, and which, through administrative error, was not credited to his leave account.

## § 2.

Section 203(c) of the Annual and Sick Leave Act of 1951, as amended (85 Stat. 880, 67 Stat. 137; 5 U.S.C. 2082(c)), shall not apply with respect to the leave granted by this Act, and such leave likewise shall not affect the use or accumulation, pursuant to applicable law, of other annual leave earned by the said Lloyd W. Corbisier. None of the leave granted by this Act shall be settled by means of a cash payment in the event such leave or part thereof remains unused at. the time the said Lloyd W. Corbisier is separated by death or otherwise from (he Federal service.


*Approved July 1, 1968.*

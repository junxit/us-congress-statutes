# Private Law 90–205

> AN ACT For the relief of Roberto Perdomo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Roberto Perdomo shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of March 31, 1962.


*Approved January 27, 1968.*

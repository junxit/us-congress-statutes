# Private Law 90–298

> AN ACT For the relief of Amir U. Khan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the pur-poses of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Amir U. Khan shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence on July 9, 1948, and the periods of time he has resided in the United States since that date shall be held and considered to meet the residence and physical presence requirements of section 316 of the said Act.


*Approved July 23, 1968.*

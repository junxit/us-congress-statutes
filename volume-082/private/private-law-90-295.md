# Private Law 90–295

> AN ACT For the relief of Demetra Lani Augelopoulos.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Demetra Lani Angelopoulos may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of the said Act, upon approval of a petition filed in her behalf by Mr. Constantine Angelopoulos, a citizen of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of the said Act: Provided, That the brothers or sisters of the beneficiary shall not, by reason of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved July 21, 1968.*

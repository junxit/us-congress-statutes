# Private Law 90–217

> AN ACT For the relief of Nora Austin Hendrickson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwith-standing the provisions of section 3010 of title 38; United States Code, or any statute of limitations, the application hied by Nora Austin Hendrickson under the veterans’ benefits provisions of title 38 of the United States Code as the surviving widow of the late Edward Harold Hendrickson, who died September 30, 1962, shall be held and considered to have been filed with the Veterans Administration within one year after his death and shall be considered in accordance with the law applicable as of that date.


*Approved May 11, 1968.*

# Private Law 90–227

> AN ACT For the relief of Ana Jacalne.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Ana Jacalne may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of such Act, and a petition may be filed in behalf of the said Ana Jacalne by Mr. and Mrs. Steven Jacalne, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of such Act.


*Approved May 18, 1968.*

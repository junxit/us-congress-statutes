# Private Law 90–254

> AN ACT For the relief of Kap Rai Kim and Young Nam Kim.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, section 204(c), relating to the number of petitions which may be approved in behalf of adopted children, shall be inapplicable in the case of petitions filed in behalf of Kap Rai Kim and Young Nam Kim by the Reverend and Mrs. Elwood John Culp, citizens of the United States: Provided, That no brothers or sisters of the beneficiaries shall thereafter, by virtue of such relationship, lie accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved June 15, 1968.*

# Private Law 90–328

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Earl C. Chamberlayne.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Earl C. Chamberlayne shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of December 9, 1952.


*Approved September 24, 1968.*

# Private Law 90–341

> AN ACT For the relief Of Private Willy R. Michallk, RA 15924409.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Private Willy Michalik, a native of Germany, who served honorably in the United States Army from April 21, 1966, until his death on April 10, 1968, shall be held and considered to have been a citizen of the United States at the time of his death.


*Approved September 26, 1968.*

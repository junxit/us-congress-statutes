# Private Law 90–276

> AN ACT For the relief of Willard Herndon Rusk.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy is authorized and directed to pay, out of current appropriations available for the payment of severance pay to Lieutenant Willard Herndon Rusk, who was discharged from the United States Navy on March 24, 1966, an amount equal to the difference between (a) the amount of severance pay which would have been paid to said person upon his discharge from the United States Navy if the computation of such severance pay had been based upon his actual commissioned service in the United States Navy, and (b) the amount of severance pay actually paid to him.


*Approved July 1, 1968.*

# Private Law 90–263

> AN ACT For the relief of Private First Class John Patrick Collopy, US51616166.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Private First Class John Patrick Collopy, a native of Ireland, who served honorably in the United States Army from April 25, 1966, until his death on July 15, 1967, shall be held and considered to have been a citizen of the United States at the time of his death.


*Approved June 22, 1968.*

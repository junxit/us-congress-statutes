# Private Law 90–343

> AN ACT Conferring United States citizenship posthumously upon Staff Sergeant Ivan Claus King.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Staff Sergeant Ivan Claus King, a native of Germany, who served honorably in the United States Army from May 12, 1965, until his death on October 2, 1967, shall be held and considered to have been a citizen of the United States at the time of his death.


*Approved September 28, 1968.*

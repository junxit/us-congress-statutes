# Private Law 90–299

> AN ACT To confer United States citizenship posthumously upon Private First Class John R. Aneli.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, Private First Class John R. Aneli, a native of Italy, who served honorably in the United States Anny from August 29, 1966, until his death on August 7, 1967, shall be held and considered to have been a citizen of the United States at the time of his death.


*Approved July 23, 1968.*

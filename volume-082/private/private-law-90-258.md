# Private Law 90–258

> AN ACT For the relief of Yong Chin Sager.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Yong Chin Sager may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of the Act, upon approval of a petition tiled in his behalf by William Jesse Sager, a citizen of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of the Act: Provided, That the natural brothers or sisters of the beneficiary shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved June 22, 1968.*

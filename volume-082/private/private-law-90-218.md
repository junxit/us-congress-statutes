# Private Law 90–218

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Brandla Don (nee Praschnik).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Brandin Don (nee Prnschnik) shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of September 29, 1960.


*Approved May 17, 1968.*

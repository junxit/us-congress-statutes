# Private Law 90–207

> AN ACT For the relief of Kelley Michelle Auerbach.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Kelley Michelle Auerbach may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of the said Act, upon approval of a petition filed in her behalf by Mrs. Kay J. Auerbach, a citizen of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of the said Act.


*Approved March 29, 1968.*

# Private Law 90–256

> AN ACT For the relief of Angeliki Gianuakou.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Angeliki Giannakou may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of the Act, and a petition filed in her behalf by Miss Dorothy E. Hughes, a citizen of the United States, may be approved pursuant to section 204 of the said Act, subject to all the conditions in that section relating to orphans: Provided, That the brothers or sisters of the said Angeliki Giannakou shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved June 20, 1968.*

# Private Law 90–226

> AN ACT For the relief of Mariana Mantzios.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mariana Mantzios may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of such Act, subject to the proviso to such section, and a petition may be filed in behalf of the said Mariana Mantzios by Mr. and Mrs. Mike Mantzios, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204(a) of such Act.


*Approved May 18, 1968.*

# Chapter LIII

> An Act to alter the time for the next meeting of Congress.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That after the adjournment of the present session, the next meeting of Congress shall be on the first Monday of November next.


*Approved, April 22, 1808.*

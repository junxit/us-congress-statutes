## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That from and after the first day of June next, all post roads heretofore established by any act of Congress of the United States, shall be and the same are hereby discontinued: Provided, that nothing herein contained shall be construed so as to affect any existing contracts.


*Approved, April 28, 1810.*

# Chapter XXXIV

> An Act to extend the time for completing the third census, or enumeration of the inhabitants of the United States.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, That the assistants in the several states and territories, for which returns have not been completed, have, until the first Monday of June next, to make their returns to the marshals and secretaries; and that the marshals and secretaries have, until the first Monday of July next, to make and file their returns in the office of the Secretary of State, any law to the contrary notwithstanding.


*Approved, March 2, 1811.*

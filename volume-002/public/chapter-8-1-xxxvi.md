# Chapter XXXVI

> An Act altering the time for the next meeting of Congress.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, That after the expiration of the present session, the next meeting of Congress shall be on the first Monday of November next.


*Approved, March 26, 1804.*

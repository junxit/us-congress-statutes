# Chapter X

> An Act to alter the time for the next meeting of Congress.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives o f the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That after the adjournment of the present session, the next meeting of Congress shall be on the fourth Monday of May next.


*Approved, January 30, 1809.*

# Chapter XLI

> An Act further to prolong the continuance of the Mint at Philadelphia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the act, intituled “An act concerning the mint,” approved March the third, one thousand eight hundred and one, is hereby revived and continued in force and operation for the further term of five years after the fourth day of March, one thousand eight hundred and eight.


*Approved, April 1, 1808.*

# Chapter XXV

> An Act to revive, and continue in force, an act, intituled “An act to augment the salaries of the officers therein mentioned,” passed the second day of March, one thousand seven hundred and ninety-nine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the. United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That an act, intituled “An act to augment the salaries of the officers therein mentioned,” be, and the same is hereby revived, and continued in force, for and during the term of two years, from the commencement of the present year.


*Approved, April 14, 1802.*

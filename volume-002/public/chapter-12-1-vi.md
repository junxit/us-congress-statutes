# Chapter VI

> An Act allowing further time for completing the payments on certain lands held by right of preemption, in the Mississippi territory.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, That all the purchasers of public lands, by right of preemption in the Mississippi territory, who have made payment of their first instalment of the purchase money, be allowed until the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and thirteen, to complete the payments on their lauds, respectively, any law to the contrary notwithstanding.


*Approved, December 12, 1811.*

# Chapter XXIV

> An Act extending the right of suffrage in the Indiana territory.(a)

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That every free white male person in the Indiana territory, above the age of twenty-one years, having been a citizen of the United States, and resident in the said territory, one year next preceding an election of representatives, and who has a legal or equitable title to a tract of land of the quantity of fifty acres, or who may become the purchaser from the United States of a tract of land of the quantity of fifty acres, or who holds in his own right a town lot of the value of one hundred dollars, shall be entitled to vote for representatives to the general assembly of the said territory.


*Approved, February 26, 1808.*

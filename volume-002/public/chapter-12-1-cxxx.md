# Chapter CXXX

> An Act supplementary to the act entitled “An act respecting alien enemies.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That nothing in the proviso contained in the act, entitled “An act respecting alien enemies,” approved on the sixth day of July, one thousand seven hundred and ninety-eight, shall be extended or construed to extend to any treaty, or to any article of any treaty, which shall have expired, or which shall not be in force, at the time when the proclamation of the President shall issue.


*Approved, July 6, 1812.*

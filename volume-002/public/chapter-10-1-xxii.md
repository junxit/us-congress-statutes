# Chapter XXII

> An Act making additional compensation to the Marshals for the Districts of North Carolina and New Jersey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the last day of March next, there shall be paid annually to the marshal for the district of North Carolina, the sum of four hundred dollars; to the marshal for the district of New Jersey, the sum of two hundred dollars, in addition to the fees and emoluments heretofore allowed them by law.


*Approved, February 25, 1808.*

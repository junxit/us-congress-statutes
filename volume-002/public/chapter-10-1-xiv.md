# Chapter XIV

> An Act to continue in force for a limited time an act, intituled “An act continuing for a limited time the salaries of the officers of government therein mentioned.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That an act passed on the twentieth day of February, one thousand eight hundred and four, intituled “An act continuing for a limited time the salaries of the officers of government, therein mentioned,” shall be, and continue in force for the term of three years, and to the end of the next session of Congress thereafter, and no longer.


*Approved, January 27, 1808.*

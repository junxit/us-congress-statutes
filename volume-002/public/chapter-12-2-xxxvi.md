# Chapter XXXVI

> An Act to establish certain post roads in the State of Louisiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the following post roads be established; from Natchez, in the Mississippi territory, by Concordia to Catahoula, and from thence, by Rapids to Nachitoches; from St. Francisville, by St. Helena and St. Tammany to Madisonville.


*Approved, February 27, 1813.*

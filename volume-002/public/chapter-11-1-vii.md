# Chapter VII

> An Act to fix the time for the next meeting of Congress.

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That after the adjournment of the present session, the next meeting of Congress shall be on the fourth Monday of November next.


*Approved, June 24, 1809.*

# Chapter II

> An Act allowing an additional compensation to the Secretaries of the Mississippi, Indiana, Louisiana and Michigan Territories.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That each of the secretaries of the Mississippi, Indiana, Louisiana and Michigan territories, appointed under the authority of the United States, be entitled to the annual sum of one thousand dollars, in lieu of his present compensation, to commence on the first day of January next.


*Approved, December 5, 1807.*

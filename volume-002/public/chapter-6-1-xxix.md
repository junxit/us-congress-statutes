# Chapter XXIX

> An Act fixing the rank and pay of the commanding officer of the Corps of Marines.(a)

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a lieutenant-colonel commandant shall be appointed to command the corps of marines, and shall be entitled to the same pay and emoluments as a lieutenant-colonel in the army of the United States; any thing in the act for the establishing and organizing a marine corps to the contrary notwithstanding; and that the office of major of the said corps shall thereafter be abolished.


*Approved, April 22, 1800.*

# Resolution

> Resolution to publish the Report and Chart of the Survey of the coast of North Carolina.

*Resolved, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he hereby is authorized and directed, to cause to be published the report and chart of a survey of the coast of North Carolina, made by Thomas Coles and Jonathan Price, under the act of Congress passed at the last session for that purpose.


*Approved, March 2, 1807.*

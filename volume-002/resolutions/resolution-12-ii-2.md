# Resolution II

> Resolution requesting the President of the United States to cause to be prepared and laid before Congress a system if Military Discipline.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be, and he is hereby requested lo cause to lie prepared and laid before Congress, as soon as practicable, a military system of discipline for the infantry of the army and militia of the United States.


*Approved, March 3, 1813.*

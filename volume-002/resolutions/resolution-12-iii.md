# Resolution III

> Resolution requesting the state of Georgia to assent to the formation of two States of the Mississippi territory.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the legislature of the state of Georgia be, and they are hereby requested to give their assent by law to the formation of two states of the Mississippi territory: Provided, in the opinion of Congress a division of said territory for that purpose should hereafter be expedient.


*Approved, June 17, 1812.*

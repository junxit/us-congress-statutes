# Private Law 89–292

> AN ACT For the relief of the Moapa Valley Water Company, of Logandale, Nevada.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Moapa Valley Water Company, of Logandale, Nevada, is hereby relieved of all liability to pay to the United States the sum of $56,018.22 representing the interest accruing prior to January 1, 1965, on three loans made to the said water company by the Farmers Home Administration in the amounts of $200,000, $30,000, and $20,000.


*Approved September 12, 1966, 9:05 p.m.*

# Private Law 89–423

> AN ACT For the relief of Antonio Barquet Chediak.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Antonio Barquet Chediak shall be held and considered to be lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of May 26, 1961.


*Approved November 2, 1966.*

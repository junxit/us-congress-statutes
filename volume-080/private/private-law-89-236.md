# Private Law 89–236

> AN ACT For the relief of Manojlo Verzich.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, as amended, Manojlo Verzich may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of the Act, upon approval of a petition filed in his behalf by Mr. and Mrs. Merko Verzich, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of the said Act, subject to all the conditions in that section relating to orphans.


*Approved April 16, 1966.*

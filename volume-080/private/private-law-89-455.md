# Private Law 89–455

> AN ACT For the relief of Che Yim Loh.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Che Yim Loh may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of the Act, and a petition filed in behalf of the said Che Yim Loh by Margaret Lee Weil, a citizen of the United States, may be approved pursuant to section 204 of the Act.


*Approved November 6, 1966.*

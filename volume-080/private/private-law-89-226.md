# Private Law 89–226

> AN ACT For the relief of Jack Ralph Walker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Jack Ralph Walker shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence on September 16, 1930.


*Approved March 17, 1966.*

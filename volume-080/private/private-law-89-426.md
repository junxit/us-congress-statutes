# Private Law 89–426

> AN ACT To amend Private Law 86–203 to permit the use of the vessel John F. Drews in the coastwise trade while it is owned by a citizen of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Private Law 86–203, approved September 21, 1959 (73 Stat. A89), is amended by inserting immediately before the period at the end thereof the following: “or by any other citizen of the United States within the meaning of section 2 of the Shipping Act, 1916 (46 U.S.C. 802)”.


*Approved November 2, 1966.*

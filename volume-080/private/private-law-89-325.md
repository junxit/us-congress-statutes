# Private Law 89–325

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Jaime E. Condom Valera.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Jaime E. Condom Valera shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of August 24, 1960.


*Approved October 3, 1966.*

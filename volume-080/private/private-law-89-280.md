# Private Law 89–280

> AN ACT For the relief of Chung K. Won.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203(a)(1) and 204 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Chung K. Won shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien son of Mr. Won Wing, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 25, 1966.*

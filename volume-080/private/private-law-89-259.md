  - Each such employee or former employee who has at any time made any repayment to the United States on account of any such overpayments made to him (or, in the event of his death, the person who would be entitled thereto under the first section of the Act of August 3, 1950 (5 U.S.C. 61f)), shall be entitled to have an amount equal to all such repayments made by him refunded if application is made to the project manager, Columbia Basin project, within two years after the date of enactment of this Act.
- **(b)** For purposes of the Civil Service Retirement Act and the Federal Employees’ Group Life Insurance Act of 1954, each overpayment for which liability is relieved by subsection (a) of this section shall be deemed to have been a valid payment.

## § 2.

In the audit and settlement of the accounts of any certifying or disbursing officer of the United States full credit shall be given for any amounts for which liability is relieved by the first section of this Act.

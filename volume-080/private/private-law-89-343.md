# Private Law 89–343

> AN ACT For the relief of Georges Fraise.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of section 301(b) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, and section 16 of the Act of September 11, 1957, Georges Fraise shall be held and considered to have retained his United States citizenship if he complies with the provisions thereof prior to attaining the age of thirty-two years.


*Approved October 8, 1966.*

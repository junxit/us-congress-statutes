# Private Law 89–411

> AN ACT For the relief of Maria Karidakis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Maria Karidakis may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of that Act, upon approval of a petition filed in her behalf by Mr. and Mrs. George N. Karidakis, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of that Act, subject to all the conditions in that section relating to petitions in behalf of a child as defined in section 101(b)(1)(F).


*Approved November 2, 1966.*

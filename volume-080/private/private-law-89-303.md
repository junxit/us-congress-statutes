# Private Law 89–303

> AN ACT For the relief of Ronald Whelan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the limitations of paragraph (1) of subsection (b) of section 2733 of the United States Code or any other statute of limitations, the Secretary of the Army is herein’ authorized and directed to receive, consider, and settle the claim of Ronald Whelan, a minor, in accordance with the otherwise applicable provisions of section 2733 of title 10 of the United States Code based upon the injuries and disabilities he suffered as the result of being struck by a United States Army vehicle in Frankfurt, Germany, on or about September 26, 1960; and if found to be meritorious, the claim shall lie paid in accordance with the procedures provided in that section. The claim authorized to be considered by this Act shall be filed within one year of the date of approval of this Act.


*Approved September 16, 1966.*

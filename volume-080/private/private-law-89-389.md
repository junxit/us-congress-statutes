# Private Law 89–389

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Ricardo R. Fuste.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Ricardo R. Fuste shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of September 12, 1961.


*Approved November 2, 1966.*

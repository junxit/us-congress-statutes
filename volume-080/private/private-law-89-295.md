# Private Law 89–295

> AN ACT For the relief of Maria Anna Piotrowski, formerly Czeslawa Marek.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Maria Anna Piotrowski, formerly Czeslawa Marek, may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of the Act, upon approval of a petition filed in her behalf by Chester and Eugenia Piotrowski, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of the Act.


*Approved September 14, 1966.*

# Private Law 89–358

> AN ACT For the relief of Hazel Louise Schuman Strunk.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Hazel Louise Schuman Strunk shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of December 1, 1923.


*Approved October 15, 1966.*

# Private Law 89–319

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Arturo Victor Fajardo-Carpio.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Arturo Victor Fajardo-Carpio shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of December 7, 1960.


*Approved October 3, 1966.*

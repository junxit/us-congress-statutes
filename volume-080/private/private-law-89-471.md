# Private Law 89–471

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Luis Crespo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Luis Crespo shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of March 17, 1961.


*Approved November 9, 1966.*

# Private Law 89–273

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Anna Michalska Holoweckyj (formerly Mrs. Anna Zalewski).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the provisions of section 204(c) of that Act shall be inapplicable in the case of Mrs. Anna Michalska Holoweckyj (formerly Mrs. Anna Zalewski).


*Approved August 4, 1966.*

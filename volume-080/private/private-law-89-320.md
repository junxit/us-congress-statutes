# Private Law 89–320

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Felix Hurtado Perez.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Felix Hurtado Perez shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence on January 22, 1961.


*Approved October 3, 1966.*

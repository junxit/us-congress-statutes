# Private Law 89–237

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Panaglota Vastakis and Soteros Vastakis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Mrs. Panagiota Vastakis and Soteros Vastakis, the widow and son, respectively, of a United States citizen, shall be deemed to be within the purview of section 101 (a)(27)(A) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, and the provisions or section 204 of that Act shall not be applicable in their cases.


*Approved April 16, 1966.*

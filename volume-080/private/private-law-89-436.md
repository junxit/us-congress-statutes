# Private Law 89–436

> AN ACT For the relief of Vicente Fernandez Marino.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Vicente Fernandez Marino shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of November 7, 1961.


*Approved November 2, 1966.*

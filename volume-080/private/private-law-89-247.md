# Private Law 89–247

> AN ACT For the relief of Charlotte Schulz.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, the periods of time Charlotte Schulz has resided in the United States since her lawful admission for permanent residence on July 20, 1955, shall be held and considered to meet the residence and physical presence requirements of section 316 of the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved June 21, 1966.*

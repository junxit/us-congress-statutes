# Private Law 89–384

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Jose A. G. Mendoza.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Jose A. G. Mendoza shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of September 2, 1960.


*Approved November 2, 1966.*

# Private Law 89–296

> AN ACT For the relief of Robert Dean Ward.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limitations of time applicable to the filing of claims under subsection (f) of section 5 of the War Claims Act of 1948, as amended, are hereby waived in favor of Robert Dean Ward of Seaside, California, and his claim for benefits thereunder shall be acted upon, without regard to such time limitations, if he files such claim with the Bureau of Employees’ Compensation, Department of Labor, within the six-month period beginning on the elate of enactment of this Act: Provided, hat no benefits except hospital and medical expenses actually incurred shall accrue for any period prior to the date of enactment of this Act. Approved September 16, 1966.


*Approved September 16, 1966.*

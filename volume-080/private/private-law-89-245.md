# Private Law 89–245

> AN ACT For the relief of Virgilio Acosta-Martinez.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Virgilio Acosta-Martinez shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of June 22, 1960.


*Approved May 26, 1966.*

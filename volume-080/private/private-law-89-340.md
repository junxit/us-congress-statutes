# Private Law 89–340

> AN ACT To permit Edward C. Bower to serve as a director of the Virgin Islands National Bank prior to his obtaining United States citizenship.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 5146 of the Revised Statutes of the United States, as amended (12 U.S.C. 72), Edward C. Bower shall not. prior to January 1, 1971, be disabled by reason of his Canadian citizenship from serving as a director of the Virgin Islands National Bank.


*Approved October 4, 1966.*

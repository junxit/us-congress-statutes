# Private Law 89–277

> AN ACT For the relief of Jack L. Philippot.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of paragraph (7) of section 301(a) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Jack L. Philippot, a citizen of the United States, shall be held and considered to have been physically present in the United States during the time he was serving abroad in the employ of the United States.


*Approved August 4, 1966.*

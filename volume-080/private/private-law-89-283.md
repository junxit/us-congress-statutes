# Private Law 89–283

> AN ACT For the relief of Emanuel G. Topakas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the provisions of section 204(c) of that Act shall be inapplicable in the case of Emanuel G. Topakas.


*Approved September 7, 1966.*

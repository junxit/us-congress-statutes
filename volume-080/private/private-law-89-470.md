# Private Law 89–470

> AN ACT For the relief of Katherine M. Perakis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for purposes of determining the entitlement of Katherine M. Perakis to child’s insurance benefits under section 202(d) of the Social Security Act on the Basis of the wages and self-employment income of George Perakis (Social Security Account Number XXXXXXX), of Peabody, Massachusetts, the said Katherine M. Perakis shall be deemed to have been the legally adopted child of the said George Perakis, and to have been dependent upon him at the time of his death on August 20, 1962.


*Approved November 6, 1966.*

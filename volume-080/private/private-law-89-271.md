# Private Law 89–271

> AN ACT For the relief of Bryan George Simpson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Bryan George Simpson shall lie held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fee.


*Approved August 4, 1966.*

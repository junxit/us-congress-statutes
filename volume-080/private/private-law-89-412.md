# Private Law 89–412

> AN ACT For the relief of Muriel Williams.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 201(g) of the Nationality Act of 1940, Muriel Williams shall be held and considered to have been residing in the United States for a period of five years after attaining the age of sixteen years.


*Approved November 2, 1966.*

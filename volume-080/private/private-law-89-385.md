# Private Law 89–385

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Bienvenido Benach Carreras.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Bienvenido Benach Carreras shall be held and considered to have been admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of August 26, 1961.


*Approved November 2, 1966.*

# Private Law 89–252

> AN ACT For the relief of Haider Raza and his wife, Irene Raza, and their children, Afzal Anthony and Haider Raymond Raza.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Haider Raza and his wife, Irene Raza, and their children. Afzal Anthony and Haider Raymond Raza, shall be held and considered to have been admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of May 1, 1961.


*Approved June 23, 1966.*

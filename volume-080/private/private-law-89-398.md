# Private Law 89–398

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Antonio B. Santillano.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Antonio B. Santillano shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence on December 3, 1957, and the periods of time he has resided in this country since that date shall be held and considered to meet the residence and physical presence requirements of section 316 of the said Act.


*Approved November 2, 1966.*

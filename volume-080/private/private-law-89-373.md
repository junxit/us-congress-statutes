# Private Law 89–373

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Miguel Angel Martinez y Corpas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Miguel Angel Martinez y Corpas shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of October 25, 1960.


*Approved November 2, 1966.*

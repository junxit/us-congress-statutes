# Private Law 89–337

> AN ACT For the relief of Kim Kin Soon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, section 204(c), relating to the number of petitions which may be approved in behalf of orphans, shall be inapplicable in the case of a petition filed in behalf of Kim Kin Soon by Mr. and Mrs. Thomas H. Johnstone, citizens of the United States.


*Approved October 4, 1966.*

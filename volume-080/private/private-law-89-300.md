# Private Law 89–300

> AN ACT For the relief of Lourdes 8. (Delotavo) Matzke and Yusef Ali Chouman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203(a)(1) and 204 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Lourdes S. (Delotavo) Matzke shall be. held and considered to be the natural-born alien daughter of Dr. and Mrs. Howard Matzke, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural mother of the beneficiary shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.

## § 2.

In the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Yusef Ali Chouman may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of the said Act, upon approval of a petition filed in his behalf by Mr. and Mrs. Mohamad Schuman, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of the said Act.


*Approved September 16, 1966.*

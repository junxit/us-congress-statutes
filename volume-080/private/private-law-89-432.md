# Private Law 89–432

> AN ACT For the relief of Henry P. Leonhardy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the limitations contained in Section 33 of the Trading with the Enemy Act as amended (50 U.S.C. App. 33) with respect to the filing of claims for the return of property pursuant to Section 32 of such Act (50 U.S.C. App. 32), any notice of claim filed under such Act by Henry P. Leonhardy of GarmischPartenkirchen, Federal Republic of Germany, within one month of the enactment of this Act shall be deemed to be timely filed.


*Approved November 2, 1966.*

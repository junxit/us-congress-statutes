# Private Law 89–317

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Jorge G. Echenique.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purpose of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Jorge G. Echenique shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of April 1, 1961.


*Approved October 3, 1966.*

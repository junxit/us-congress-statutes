# Private Law 89–313

> AN ACT For the relief of Irene Snyder.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Irene Snyder may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of the said Act, and a petition may be filed in behalf of the said Irene Snyder by Sergeant and Mrs. Robert Snyder, a citizen and lawful alien resident, respectively, of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of the said Act.


*Approved September 27, 1966.*

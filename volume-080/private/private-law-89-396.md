# Private Law 89–396

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Luis E. Bencomo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Luis E. Bencomo shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of June 5, 1961.


*Approved November 2, 1966.*

# Private Law 89–453

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Olibio S. Rodriguez-Eiras.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Olibio S. Rodriguez-Eiras shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of September 6, 1961.


*Approved November 6, 1966.*

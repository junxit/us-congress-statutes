# Private Law 89–244

> AN ACT For the relief of Frantisek Vohryzka.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the periods of time Frantisek Vohryzka has resided in the United States since his lawful admission for permanent residence on March 28, 1955, shall be held and considered to meet the residence and physical presence requirements of section 316 of the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved May 11, 1966.*

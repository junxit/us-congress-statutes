# Private Law 89–415

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Mario Orlando Santos-Estevez.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Mario Orlando Santos-Estevez shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of September 21, 1961.


*Approved November 2, 1966.*

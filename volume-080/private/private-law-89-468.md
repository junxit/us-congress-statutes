# Private Law 89–468

> AN ACT For the relief of Theodora Bezates.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, as amended, Theodora Bezates may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of that Act, and a petition may be filed in her behalf by Mr. and Mrs. Gus Bezates, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of the Act.


*Approved November 6, 1966.*

# Public Law 89–598

> AN ACT Relating to the composition of the District of Columbia Court of General Sessions.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 11–902 (a) of the District of Columbia Code is amended by striking out “fifteen” and inserting in lieu thereof “twenty”.


*Approved September 21, 1966.*

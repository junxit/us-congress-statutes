# Public Law 89–410

> JOINT RESOLUTION To authorize the President to proclaim May 4, 1966, as a “Day of Recognition” for firefighters.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is hereby authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating May 4, 1966, as a “Day of Recognition” of the personal sacrifices and devotion to duty of firefighters in the United States of America in protecting lives and property in their communities; and calling upon the people of the United States to observe such day with appropriate ceremonies.


*Approved May 4, 1966.*

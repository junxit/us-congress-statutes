# Public Law 89–385

> JOINT RESOLUTION Designating April 9, 1966, as “Sir Winston Churchill Day”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That April 9, 1966, the anniversary of the conferring of honorary United States citizenship on Sir Winston Churchill, is hereby designated as “Sir Winston Churchill Day”. The President is authorized and requested to issue a proclamation calling on the people of the United States to honor the memory of Sir Winston Churchill by observing such day with appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Approved April 9, 1966.*

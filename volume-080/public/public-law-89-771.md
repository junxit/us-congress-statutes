# Public Law 89–771

> AN ACT To extend coverage of the State Technical Services Act of 1965 to the territory of Guam.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2(f) of the State Technical Services Act of 1965 (79 Stat. 680) be amended by inserting “Guam,” immediately after “Puerto Rico,”.


*Approved November 6, 1966.*

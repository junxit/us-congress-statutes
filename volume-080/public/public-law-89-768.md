# Public Law 89–768

> JOINT RESOLUTION Designating February, 1967 as American History Month.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That February, 1967 is hereby designated as American History Month, and the President of the United States is requested and authorized to issue a proclamation inviting the people of the United States to observe such month in schools and other suitable places with appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Approved November 5, 1966.*

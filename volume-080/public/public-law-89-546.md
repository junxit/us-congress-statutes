# Public Law 89–546

> JOINT RESOLUTION To authorize the President to proclaim the 8th day of September 1966 as “International Literacy Day”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is hereby authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating the 8th day of September 1966 as International Literacy Day, and calling upon the people of the United States to observe such day with appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Approved August 27, 1966.*

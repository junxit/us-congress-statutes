# Public Law 89–692

> AN ACT To continue for a temporary period certain existing rules relating to the deductibility of accrued vacation pay.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 97 of the Technical Amendments Act of 1958, as amended (26 U.S.C., sec. 162, note), is amended by striking out “January 1, 1967,” and inserting in lieu thereof “January 1, 1969,”.


*Approved October 15, 1966.*

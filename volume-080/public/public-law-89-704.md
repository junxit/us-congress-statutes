# Public Law 89–704

> JOINT RESOLUTION Fixing the time of assembly of the Ninetieth Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Ninetieth Congress shall assemble at noon on Tuesday, January 10, 1967.


*Approved November 2, 1966.*

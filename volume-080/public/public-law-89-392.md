# Public Law 89–392

> AN ACT To amend the Tariff Schedules of the United States to suspend the duty on certain tropical hardwoods.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subpart B of part 1 of the appendix to title I of the Tariff Act of 1930 (Tariff Schedules of the United States; 28 F.R., part II, Aug. 17, 1963; 77A Stat.; 19 U.S.C., sec. 1202) is amended by adding at the end thereof the following new items:

## § 2.

The amendment made by the first section of this Act shall apply to articles entered, or withdrawn from warehouse, for consumption after the date or the enactment of this Act.

# Public Law 89–727

> AN ACT To give the consent of Congress to the State of Massachusetts to become a party to the agreement relating to bus taxation proration and reciprocity as set forth in title II of the Act of April 14, 1965 (79 Stat. 60), and consented to by Congress in that Act and in the Act of November 1, 1965 (79 Stat. 1157).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the consent of Congress is given to the State of Massachusetts to become a party to the agreement relating to bus taxation proration and reciprocity as set forth in title II of the Act of April 14, 1965 (79 Stat. 60), and consented to by Congress in that Act and in the Act of November 1, 1965 (79 Stat. 1157).

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is expressly reserved.


*Approved November 2, 1966.*

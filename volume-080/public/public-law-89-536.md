# Public Law 89–536

> AN ACT To amend a limitation on the salary of the Academic Dean of the Naval Postgraduate School.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 7043(b) of title 10, United States Code, is amended by deleting the following, “prescribes, but not more than $13,500 a year.” and substituting in lieu thereof the following: “prescribes, but not more than the rate of compensation provided for grade 18 of the general schedule of the Classification Act of 1949, as amended.”


*Approved August 11, 1966.*

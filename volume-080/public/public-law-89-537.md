# Public Law 89–537

> AN ACT To amend section 15d of the Tennessee Valley Authority Act of 1933 to increase the amount of bonds which may be issued by the Tennessee Valley Authority.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first sentence of subsection (a) of section 15d of the Tennessee Valley Authority Act of 1933, as amended (16 U.S.C. 831n–4), is amended by striking out “$750,000,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$1,750,-000,000”.


*Approved August 12, 1966.*

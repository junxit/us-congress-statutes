# Public Law 89–585

> AN ACT To amend section 4 of the District of Columbia Income and Franchise Tax Act of 1947.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph (2) of section 4(h) of title I of the District of Columbia Income and Franchise Tax Act of 1947, as amended (D.C. Code, sec. 47–1551c(h)), is amended to read as follows:

> - **(2)** Sales of tangible personal property by a corporation or unincorporated business which— during the taxable year for the sole purpose of dealing with the United States for commercial or noncommercial purposes or of dealing with the District or persons for noncommercial purposes; but each such corporation and unincorporated business which does business in the District with the United States shall be subject to the licensing provisions in title XIV of this article.”
>   - **(A)** has or maintains an office, warehouse, or other place of business in the District, or
>   - **(B)** has an officer, agent, or representative having an office or other place of business in the District,

## § 2.

The amendment made by the first section of this Act shall apply with respect to taxable years ending on or after the date of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved September 19, 1966.*

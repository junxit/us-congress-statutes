# Public Law 89–539

> AN ACT To authorize the disposal of metallurgical grade manganese ore from the national stockpile and the supplemental stockpile.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

, That the Administrator of General Services is hereby authorized to dispose of, by negotiation or otherwise, approximately one million nine hundred thousand short dry tons of metallurgical grade manganese ore now held in the national stockpile established pursuant to the Strategic and Critical Materials Stock Piling Act (50 U.S.C. 98–98h) and the supplemental stockpile established pursuant to section 104(b) of the Agricultural Trade Development and Assistance Act of 1954, as amended (7 U.S.C. 1704(b)). Such disposition may be made without regard to the provisions of section 3 of the Strategic and Critical Materials Stock Piling Act: Provided, That the time and method of disposition shall be fixed with due regard to the protection of the United States against avoidable loss and the protection of producers, processors, and consumers against avoidable disruption of their usual markets.


*Approved August 19, 1966.*

# Public Law 89–588

> AN ACT To repeal certain provisions of the Act of January 21, 1929 (45 Stat. 1091), as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 3, 4, 5, 6, and 7 of the Act of January 21, 1929 (45 Stat. 1091), as amended, are hereby repealed.


*Approved September 19, 1966.*

# Public Law 89–411

> JOINT RESOLUTION To provide for the designation of the week beginning April 23, 1967, as “Youth Temperance Education Week”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating the week beginning April 23, 1967, as “Youth Temperance Education Week”, and inviting the people of the United States to cooperate during such week with programs of temperance education.


*Approved May 4, 1966.*

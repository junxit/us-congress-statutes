# Public Law 89–804

> AN ACT To amend section 208(c) to provide that certificates issued to motor common carriers of passengers pursuant to future applications shall not confer, as an incident to the grant of regular route authority, the right to transport special or chartered parties.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 208(c) of the Interstate Commerce Act is amended to read as follows:

> - **(c)** Any common carrier by motor vehicle transporting passengers under a certificate issued under this part pursuant to an application filed on or before January 1, 1967, or under any reissuance of the operating rights contained in such certificate, may transport in interstate or foreign commerce to any place special or chartered parties under such rules and regulations as the Commission shall have prescribed.”


*Approved November 10, 1966.*

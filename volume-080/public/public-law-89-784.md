# Public Law 89–784

> AN ACT To change the name of the Rolla Jewel Bearing Plant at Rolla, North Dakota, to the William Langer Jewel Bearing Plant.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Jewel Bearing Plant operated and maintained by the General Services Administration at Rolla, North Dakota, shall hereafter be known as the William Langer Jewel Bearing Plant, and any law, regulation, document, or record of the United States in which such plant is designated or referred to shall be held to refer to such plant under and by the name of the William Langer Jewel Bearing Plant.


*Approved November 6, 1966.*

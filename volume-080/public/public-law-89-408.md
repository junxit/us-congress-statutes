# Public Law 89–408

> AN ACT To amend the Indian Long-Term Leasing Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1 of the Act of August 9, 1955 (69 Stat. 539), as amended, is hereby amended as follows: After the words “Fort Mojave Reservation,” insert the words “the Pyramid Lake Reservation,”.


*Approved April 27, 1966.*

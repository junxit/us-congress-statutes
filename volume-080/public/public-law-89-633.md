# Public Law 89–633

> AN ACT To amend the Consolidated Farmers Home Administration Act of 1961 to authorize the Secretary of Agriculture to hold prepayments made to the Secretary by Insured loan borrowers and transmit them to the holder of the note In installments as they become due.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph (2) of subsection 309(f) of the Consolidated Farmers Home Administration Act of 1961 is amended by striking out the words “the due date of the annual installment” and inserting in lieu thereof the word “due”.


*Approved October 8, 1966.*

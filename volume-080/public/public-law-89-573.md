# Public Law 89–573

> AN ACT To extend for three years the period during which certain extracts suitable for tanning may be imported free of duty.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That item 907.80 of the Tariff Schedules of the United States (19 U. S. C., sec. 1202, item 907. 80) is amended by striking out “On or before 9/30/66” and inserting in lieu thereof “On or before 9/30/69”.


*Approved September 13, 1966.*

# Public Law 89–442

> AN ACT To retrocede to the Shite of Kansas concurrent jurisdiction over Haskell Institute.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby retroceded to the State of Kansas by the United States concurrent jurisdiction over the site of Haskell Institute, at Lawrence, Kansas.


*Approved June 8, 1966.*

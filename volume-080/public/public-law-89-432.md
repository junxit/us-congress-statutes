# Public Law 89–432

> AN ACT To continue for a temporary period the existing suspension of duty on heptanoic acid.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That item 907.30 of the Tariff Schedules of the United States (19 U.S.C., sec. 1202, item 907.30) is amended by striking out “On or before 8/8/66” and inserting in lieu thereof “On or before 8/8/69”.


*Approved May 31, 1966.*

# Public Law 89–455

> AN ACT To amend section 111 of title 38, United States Code, to authorize the prepayment of certain expenses associated with the travel of veterans to or from a Veterans’ Administration facility or other place, in connection with vocational rehabilitation or counseling, or for the purpose of examination, treatment, or care.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection 111(b) of title 38, United States Code, is amended to read as follows:

> - **(b)** Payment of the following expenses or allowances in connection with vocational rehabilitation, counseling, or upon termination of examination, treatment, or care, may be made before the completion of travel:
>   - **(1)** the mileage allowance authorized by subsection (a) hereof;
>   - **(2)** actual local travel expenses;
>   - **(3)** the expense of hiring an automobile or ambulance, or the fee authorized for the services of a nonemployee attendant.”


*Approved June 18, 1966.*

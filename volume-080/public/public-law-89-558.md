# Public Law 89–558

> AN ACT To provide that the United States District Court for the District of Connecticut shall also be held at New London, Connecticut.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second sentence of section 86 of title 28, United States Code, is amended to read as follows: “Court shall be held at Bridgeport, Hartford, New Haven, New London, and Waterbury.”


*Approved September 7, 1966.*

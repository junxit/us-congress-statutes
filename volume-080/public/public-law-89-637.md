# Public Law 89–637

> JOINT RESOLUTION To extend the authority of the Postmaster General to enter into leases of real property for periods not exceeding thirty years, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2109 of title 39, United States Code, is amended to read as follows:

> ## § 2109. Time limitations on agreements
>
> “Agreements may not be entered into under sections 2104 and 2105 of this title after July 22, 1964, and under section 2103 after April 30, 1967.”


*Approved October 10, 1966.*

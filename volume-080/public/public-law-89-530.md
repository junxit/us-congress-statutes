# Public Law 89–530

> AN ACT To increase the amount authorized to be appropriated for the development of the Arkansas Post National Memorial.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3 of the Act entitled “An Act to provide for the establishment of the Arkansas Post National Memorial, in the State of Arkansas”, approved July 6, 1960 (74 Stat. 334; Public Law 86–595), is amended by striking out “$125,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$550,000”.


*Approved August 11, 1966.*

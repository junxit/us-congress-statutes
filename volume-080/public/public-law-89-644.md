# Public Law 89–644

> AN ACT To amend the Connally Hot Oil Act by exempting States from certain provisions thereof.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2 of the Act of February 22, 1935, as amended (49 Stat. 30; 15 U.S.C. 715a), commonly referred to as the Connally Hot Oil Act, is amended by striking out the period at the end of paragraph (1) of such section and inserting in lieu thereof a comma and the following: “except petroleum or any of its constituent parts, title to which has been acquired by a State pursuant to its laws.”.


*Approved October 13, 1966.*

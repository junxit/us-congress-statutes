# Public Law 89–575

> AN ACT To continue for a temporary period the existing suspension of duty on certain istle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** item 903.90 of the Tariff Schedules of the United States (19 U.S.C., sec. 1202, item 903.90) is amended by striking out “9/5/69” and inserting in lieu thereof “9/5/69”.
- **(b)** The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply with respect to articles entered, or withdrawn from warehouse, for consumption, after September 5, 1966.


*Approved September 13, 1966.*

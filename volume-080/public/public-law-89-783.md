# Public Law 89–783

> AN ACT To provide fur the striking of medals in commemoration of the United States Naval Construction Battalions (SEABEES) twenty-fifth anniversary and the United States Navy Civil Engineers Corps (CEC) one hundredth anniversary.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury (hereinafter referred to as the “Secretary”) shaft strike and furnish for the United States Naval Facilities Engineering Command (hereinafter referred to as the “command”) for the celebration of the twenty-fifth anniversary of the United States Naval Construction Battalions (SEABEES) and the one-hundredth anniversary of the United States Navy Civil Engineer Corps (CEC). National medals in commemoration of such anniversaries.

## § 2.

Such medals shall be of such sizes, materials, and designs, and shall be so inscribed as the command may determine with the approval of the Secretary.

## § 3.

Not more than one hundred thousand of such medals may he produced. Production shall be in such quantities, not less than two thousand, as may be ordered by the command, but no work may be commenced on any order unless the Secretary has received security satisfactory to him for the payment of the cost of the production of such order. Such cost shall include labor, materials, dies, use of machinery, and overhead expenses, as determined by the Secretary. No medals may be produced pursuant to this Act after December 31, 1968.

## § 4.

Upon receipt of payment for such medals in the amount of the cost thereof as determined pursuant to Section 3, the Secretary shall deliver the medals as the command may request.


*Approved November 6, 1966.*

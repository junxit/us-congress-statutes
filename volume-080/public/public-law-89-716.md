# Public Law 89–716

> AN ACT To amend section 4339 of title 10. United States Code.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4339 of title 10, United States Code, is repealed.

## § 2.

The organist and choirmaster and the civilian instructors in the departments of foreign languages and tactics at the United States Military Academy who are serving under appointments made prior to January 17, 1963, are entitled to public quarters without charge, and to fuel and light without charge when they occupy public quarters.


*Approved November 2, 1966.*

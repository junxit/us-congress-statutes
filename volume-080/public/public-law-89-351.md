# Public Law 89–351

> JOINT RESOLUTION Authorizing the President to proclaim National Ski Week.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States is hereby authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating the period beginning January 21, 1966, and ending January 30, 1966, as “National Ski Week”, in recognition of the economic, recreational, and healthful aspects of the sport of skiing; and inviting the people of the United States to observe such week by participation in appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Approved January 21, 1966.*

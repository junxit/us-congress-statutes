# Public Law 89–592

> AN ACT To amend the law establishing the revolving fund for expert assistance loans to Indian tribes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the appropriation authorization in section 1 of the Act of November 4, 1963 (77 Stat. 301), is hereby amended by changing “$900,000” to “$1,800,000”.


*Approved September 19, 1966.*

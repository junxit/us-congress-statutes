# Public Law 89–433

> AN ACT To make permanent the existing suspension of duty on certain natural graphite.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** item 517.31 of the Tariff Schedules of the United States (19 U.S.C., sec. 1202, item 517.31) is repealed and there is inserted in lieu thereof the following:
| “ |   |   | Other: |   |   |   |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|   | 517.30 |   | If valued $50 per ton or less | Free | 10% ad val. |   |
|   | 517.33 |   | Other | 0.5% ad val. | 10% ad val. | ” |

- **(b)** Item 909.20 of such Schedules (19 U.S.C., sec. 1202, item 909.20) is repealed.
- **(c)** Subsections (a) and (b) shall apply with respect to articles entered, or withdrawn from warehouse, for consumption, after June 30, 1966.


*Approved May 31, 1966.*

# Public Law 89–350

> JOINT RESOLUTION Extending the date for transmission of the Economic Report.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions of section 3 of the Act of February 20, 1946, as amended (15 U.S.C. 1022), the President shall transmit to the Congress not later than January 27, 1966, the 1966 Economic Report.


*Approved January 19, 1966.*

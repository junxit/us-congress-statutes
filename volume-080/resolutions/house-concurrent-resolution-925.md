# House Concurrent Resolution 925

> “ISTHMIAN CANAL POLICY QUESTIONS, CANAL ZONE-PANAMA CANAL SOVEREIGNTY, PANAMA CANAL MODERNIZATION, NEW CANAL”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the document entitled “Isthmian Canal Policy Questions, Canal Zone—Panama Canal Sovereignty, Panama Canal Modernization, New Canal”, a compilation of addresses and remarks by Congressman Daniel J. Flood, be printed as a House document, and that an additional ten thousand five hundred copies be printed of which seven thousand five hundred copies shall be for the use of the House of Representatives and two thousand five hundred copies shall be for the use of the Senate.


*Passed August 18, 1966.*

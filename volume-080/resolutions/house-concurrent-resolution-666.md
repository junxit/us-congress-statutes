# House Concurrent Resolution 666

> “A STUDY OF FEDERAL CREDIT PROGRAMS”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Banking and Currency, House of Representatives, two thousand additional copies of the committee print entitled “A Study of Federal Credit Programs”, prepared by that committee during the Eighty-eighth Congress.


*Passed August 18, 1966.*

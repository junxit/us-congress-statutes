# House Concurrent Resolution 587

> WATERLOO, N.Y., BIRTHPLACE OF MEMORIAL DAY

*Resolved by the House of Representatives {the Senate concurring),*

That the Congress of the United States, in recognition of the patriotic tradition set in motion one hundred years ago in the village or Waterloo. New York, does hereby officially recognize Waterloo, New York, as the birthplace of Memorial Day, and authorizes and requests the President to issue, prior to May 30, 1966, an appropriate proclamation calling the attention of all citizens to the centennial anniversary of the first observance of Memorial Day in Waterloo, New York.


*Passed May 19, 1966.*

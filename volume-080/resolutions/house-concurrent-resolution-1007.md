# House Concurrent Resolution 1007

> “INTERNATIONAL EDUCATION: PAST, PRESENT, PROBLEMS AND PROSPECTS”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the document entitled “International Education: Past, Present, Problems and Prospects,” a report by the Task Force on International Education of the Committee on Education and Labor, House of Representatives, be printed as a House document and that an additional seven thousand copies be printed for the use of the Committee on Education and Labor of the House of Representatives.


*Passed October 17, 1966.*

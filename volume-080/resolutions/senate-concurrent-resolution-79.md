# Senate Concurrent Resolution 79

> “RECENT FEDERAL RESERVE ACTIONS AND ECONOMIC POLICY COORDINATION”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Joint Economic Committee five thousand additional copies of parts 1 and 2 of its hearings of the Eighty-ninth Congress, first session, entitled “Recent Federal Reserve Actions and Economic Policy Coordination”.


*Agreed to June 2, 1966.*

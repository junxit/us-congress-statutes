# House Concurrent Resolution 804

> ADJOURNMENT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Thursday, June 30, 1966, they stand adjourned until 12 o’clock noon on Monday, July 11, 1966.


*Passed June 30, 1966.*

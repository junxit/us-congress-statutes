# House Concurrent Resolution 805

> SIGNING OF ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That notwithstanding any adjournment of the two Houses until July 11, 1966, the Speaker of the House of Representatives and the President of the Senate be, and they are hereby, authorized to sign enrolled bills and joint resolutions duly passed by the two Houses and found truly enrolled.


*Passed June 30, 1966.*

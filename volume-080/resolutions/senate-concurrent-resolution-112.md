# Senate Concurrent Resolution 112

> CORRECTION IN ENROLLMENT OF BILL (S. 1310)

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (S. 1310) relating to the National Museum of the Smithsonian Institution, the Secretary of the Senate is authorized and directed to make section 1 read: “That this Act may be cited as the ‘National Museum Act of 1966’.”


*Agreed to October 11, 1966.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 634

> “SUMMARY OF VETERANS LEGISLATION REPORTED, EIGHTY-NINTH CONGRESS”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed for the use of the Committee on Veterans’ Affairs of the House of Representatives eighty-six thousand one hundred copies of a publication entitled “Summary of Veterans Legislation Reported, Eighty-ninth Congress”, with an additional forty-three thousand nine hundred copies for the use of the Members of the House of Representatives.


*Passed August 18, 1966.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 90

> REPORT ON RECREATIONAL OPPORTUNITIES IN THE STATE OF WASHINGTON

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Interior and Insular Affairs Committee two thousand five hundred additional copies of the Senate hearings of the Study Team Report on the Recreational Opportunities in the State of Washington, held in the Eighty-ninth Congress, second session.


*Agreed to August 24, 1966.*

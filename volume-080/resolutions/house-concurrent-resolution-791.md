# House Concurrent Resolution 791

> “UNITED STATES POLICY TOWARD ASIA”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the document entitled “United States Policy Toward Asia”, a report by the Subcommittee on the Far East and the Pacific of the Committee on Foreign Affairs, House of Representatives, together with hearings thereon held by that subcommittee, dated May 19, 1966, be printed as a House document and that an additional three thousand copies be printed for the use of the Committee on Foreign Affairs of the House of Representatives.


*Passed August 18, 1966.*

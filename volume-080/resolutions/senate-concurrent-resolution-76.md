# Senate Concurrent Resolution 76

> “DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA HOME RULE, DELEGATE TO HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on the District of Columbia two thousand five hundred additional copies of its hearings of the Eighty-ninth Congress, first session, entitled “District of Columbia Home Rule, Delegate to House of Representatives” (S. 268, S. 1118).


*Agreed to June 2, 1966.*

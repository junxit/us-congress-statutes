# House Concurrent Resolution 1028

> CORRECTION IN ENROLLMENT OF BILL (S. 3467)

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (S. 3467) to amend the National School Lunch Act, as amended, to strengthen and expand food service programs for children, the Secretary of the Senate be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to correct the title so as to read: “An Act to strengthen and expand food service programs for children.”


*Passed October 5, 1966.*

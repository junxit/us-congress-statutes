# Senate Concurrent Resolution 109

> SENATE; HEARINGS OF SUBCOMMITTEE ON ANTITRUST AND MONOPOLY

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on the Judiciary one thousand additional copies each of the hearings held by its Subcommittee on Antitrust and Monopoly on physician ownership in pharmacies and drug companies (Eighty-eighth Congress, second session), price discrimination legislation (Eighty-ninth Congress, first, session) and distribution problems affecting small business (Eighty-ninth Congress, first session).


*Agreed to October 19, 1966.*

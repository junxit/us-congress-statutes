# House Concurrent Resolution 1048

> ADJOURNMENT SINE DIE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Saturday, October 22, 1966, and that when they adjourn on said day, they stand adjourned sine die.


*Passed October 22, 1966.*

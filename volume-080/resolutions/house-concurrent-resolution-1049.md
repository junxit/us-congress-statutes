# House Concurrent Resolution 1049

> SIGNING OF ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That notwithstanding the sine die adjournment of the two Houses, the Speaker of the House of Representatives and the President of the Semite be, and they are hereby, authorized to sign enrolled bills and joint resolutions (Fitly passed by the two Houses and found truly enrolled.


*Passed October 22, 1966.*

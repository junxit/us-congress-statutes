# Senate Concurrent Resolution 77

> HEARINGS ON SUPPLEMENTAL FOREIGN ASSISTANCE, FISCAL YEAR 1966-VIETNAM

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Foreign Relations ten thousand additional copies of its hearings on supplemental foreign assistance, fiscal year 1966—Vietnam, of the Eighty-ninth Congress, second session.


*Agreed to September 12, 1966.*

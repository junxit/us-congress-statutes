# Senate Concurrent Resolution 91

> “UNITED STATES POLICY WITH RESPECT TO MAINLAND CHINA”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Foreign Relations five thousand additional copies of its hearings on “United States Policy With Respect to Mainland China”, of the Eighty-ninth Congress, second session.


*Agreed to June 2, 1966.*

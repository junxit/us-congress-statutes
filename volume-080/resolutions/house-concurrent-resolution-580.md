# House Concurrent Resolution 580

> HEARINGS ON H. R. 6991, THE PUBLIC WORKS AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT ACT OF 1965

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Public Works, House of Representatives, one thousand additional copies of the hearings of that committee on H.R. 6991, the Public Works and Economic Development Act of 1965.


*Passed August 18, 1966.*

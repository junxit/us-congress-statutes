# House Concurrent Resolution 52

> VETERANS’ BENEFITS CALCULATOR

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That after the conclusion of the second session of the Eighty-ninth Congress there shall be printed fifty thousand two hundred and forty copies of a Veterans’ Benefits Calculator prepared by the Veterans’ Affairs Committee of which two thousand copies shall be for the use of the Veterans’ Affairs Committee, two thousand copies for the use of the Committee on Finance, thirty-seven thousand four hundred and eighty-five copies for the use of the House of Representatives, and eight thousand seven hundred and fifty-five copies for the use of the Senate.


*Passed August 18, 1966.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 86

> “TWENTIETH ANNIVERSARY OF THE EMPLOYMENT ACT OF 1946 AN ECONOMIC SYMPOSIUM”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Joint Economic Committee five thousand additional copies of its hearings of the Eighty-ninth Congress, second session, entitled “Twentieth Anniversary of the Employment Act of 1946, an Economic Symposium”.


*Agreed to June 2, 1966.*

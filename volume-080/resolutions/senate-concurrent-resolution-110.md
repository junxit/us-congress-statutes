# Senate Concurrent Resolution 110

> “DETECTION AND PREVENTION OF CHRONIC DISEASE UTILIZING MULTIPHASIC HEALTH SCREENING TECHNIQUES”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Special Committee on Aging four thousand additional copies of hearings held September 20, 21, and 22, 1966, in Washington, District of Columbia, titled “Detection and Prevention of Chronic Disease Utilizing Multiphasic Health Screening Techniques.”


*Agreed to October 19, 1966.*

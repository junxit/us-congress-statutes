# House Concurrent Resolution 625

> ADJOURNMENT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Thursday, April 7, 1966, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Monday, April 18, 1966, and that when the Senate adjourns on Thursday, April 7, 1966, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Wednesday, April 13, 1966.


*Passed April 6, 1966.*

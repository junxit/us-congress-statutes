# House Concurrent Resolution 619

> “CONFLICTS BETWEEN THE FEDERAL RESEARCH PROGRAMS AND THE NATION’S GOALS FOR HIGHER EDUCATION”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Government Operations one thousand additional copies of part 2 of the committee print entitled “Conflicts Between the Federal Research Programs and the Nation’s Goals for Higher Education”, of the Eighty-ninth Congress, first session.


*Passed August 18, 1966.*

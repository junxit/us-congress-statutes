# Senate Concurrent Resolution 70

> MARBLE BUST OF CONSTANTINO BRUMIDI

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Joint Committee on the Library is authorized and directed to procure a marble bust of Constantino Brumidi, and to cause such bust to be placed in the corridor, known as the Brumidi corridor, on the first floor of the Senate wing of the Capitol.

## § 2.

Expenses incurred by the Joint Committee on the Library in carrying out this concurrent resolution, which shall not exceed $2,500, shall be paid out of the contingent fund of the Senate on vouchers approved by the chairman of the Joint Committee.


*Agreed to June 6, 1966.*

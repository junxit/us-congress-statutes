# House Concurrent Resolution 630

> HOUSE DOCUMENT 190, THE CONSTITUTION OF THE UNITED STATES

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed an additional fifty-four thousand two hundred copies of House Document Numbered 190 of the Eighty-ninth Congress in the style and format directed by the Joint Com-mittee on Printing. Not more than one hundred of such copies shall be delivered to each Member of Congress and to the Resident Commissioner of Puerto Rico.


*Passed August 18, 1966.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 373

> REPORT OF JOINT COMMITTEE ON ARRANGEMENTS TO COMMEMORATE THE ONE HUNDREDTH ANNIVERSARY OF THE SECOND INAUGURAL OF ABRAHAM LINCOLN

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed as a House document the report of the Joint Committee on Arrangements To Commemorate the One Hundredth Anniversary of the Second Inaugural of Abraham Lincoln and that said report may include such illustrations as the joint committee may include. The preparation of material for printing and the selection of an appropriate binding shall be under the supervision of the subcommittee of the joint committee on arrangements for the commemorative ceremony.


*Passed August 18, 1966.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 435

> CAPITOL VISITOR CENTER CEREMONIES—EMANCIPATION HALL AUTHORIZATION

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That Emancipation Hall may be used on December 2, 2008, for ceremonies and activities held in connection with the opening of the Capitol Visitor Center to the public. Physical preparations for such ceremonies and activities shall be carried out in accordance with such conditions as the Architect of the Capitol may prescribe.


*Agreed to November 20, 2008.*

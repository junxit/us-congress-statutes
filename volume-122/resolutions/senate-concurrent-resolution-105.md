# Senate Concurrent Resolution 105

> ENROLLMENT CORRECTIONS—H.R. 6063

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill H.R. 6063, an Act to authorize the programs of the National Aeronautics and Space Administration, and for other purposes, the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall make the following corrections:

In section 601(b)(2)(A)(iii) of the bill, strike “Orbiter”.

In section 611(d)(1) of the bill, strike “first President” and insert “President”.

In section 611(e)(3) of the bill, strike “correctly” and insert “currently”.

In section 611(e)(7) of the bill, strike “extention” and insert “extension”.

In section 612 of the bill, strike “operations” and insert “operational”.

In section 1119 of the bill, strike “The Report” and insert “The report”.


*Agreed to October 2, 2008.*

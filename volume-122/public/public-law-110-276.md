# Public Law 110–276

> An Act To designate the United States customhouse building located at 31 Gonzalez Clemente Avenue in Mayagüez, Puerto Rico, as the “Rafael Martínez Nadal United States Customhouse Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The United States customhouse building located at 31 Gonzalez Clemente Avenue in Mayagüez, Puerto Rico, shall be known and designated as the “Rafael Martínez Nadal United States Customhouse Building”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the United States customhouse building referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Rafael Martínez Nadal United States Customhouse Building”.


*Approved July 15, 2008.*

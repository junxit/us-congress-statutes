# Public Law 110–431

> An Act To authorize funding for the National Crime Victim Law Institute to provide support for victims of crime under Crime Victims Legal Assistance Programs as a part of the Victims of Crime Act of 1984.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. REAUTHORIZATION.

Section 103(b) of the Justice for All Act of 2004 (Public Law 108–405; 118 Stat. 2264) is amended in paragraphs (1) through (5) by striking “2006, 2007, 2008, and 2009” each place it appears and inserting “2010, 2011, 2012, and 2013”.


*Approved October 15, 2008.*

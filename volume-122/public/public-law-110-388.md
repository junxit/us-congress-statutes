# Public Law 110–388

> An Act To provide for the appointment of the Chief Human Capital Officer of the Department of Homeland Security by the Secretary of Homeland Security.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. APPOINTMENT OF THE CHIEF HUMAN CAPITAL OFFICER BY THE SECRETARY OF HOMELAND SECURITY.

Section 103(d) of the Homeland Security Act of 2002 (6 U.S.C. 113(d)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking paragraph (3); and
- **(2)** redesignating paragraphs (4) and (5) as paragraphs (3) and (4), respectively.


*Approved October 10, 2008.*

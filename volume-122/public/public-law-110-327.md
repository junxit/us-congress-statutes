# Public Law 110–327

> An Act To amend the Improving America’s Schools Act of 1994 to make permanent the favorable treatment of need-based educational aid under the antitrust laws.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. SHORT TITLE.

This Act may be cited as the “Need-Based Educational Aid Act of 2008”.

## § 2. AMENDMENT.

Section 568(d) of the Improving America’s Schools Act of 1994 (15 U.S.C. 1 note) is amended by striking “2008” and inserting “2015”.


*Approved September 30, 2008.*

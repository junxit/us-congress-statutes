# Public Law 110–182

> An Act To extend the Protect America Act of 2007 for 15 days.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. 15-DAY EXTENSION OF THE PROTECT AMERICA ACT OF 2007.

Section 6(c) of the Protect America Act of 2007 (Public Law 110–55; 121 Stat. 557; 50 U.S.C. 1803 note) is amended by striking “180 days” and inserting “195 days”.


*Approved January 31, 2008.*

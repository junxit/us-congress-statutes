# Public Law 110–395

> An Act To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 245 North Main Street in New City, New York, as the “Kenneth Peter Zebrowski Post Office Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. KENNETH PETER ZEBROWSKI POST OFFICE BUILDING.

- **(a)** *Designation.—* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 245 North Main Street in New City, New York, shall be known and designated as the “Kenneth Peter Zebrowski Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References.—* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Kenneth Peter Zebrowski Post Office Building”.


*Approved October 13, 2008.*

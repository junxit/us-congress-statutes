# Public Law 110–308

> An Act To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 219 East Main Street in West Frankfort, Illinois, as the “Kenneth James Gray Post Office Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. KENNETH JAMES GRAY POST OFFICE BUILDING.

- **(a)** *Designation.—* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 219 East Main Street in West Frankfort, Illinois, shall be known and designated as the “Kenneth James Gray Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References.—* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Kenneth James Gray Post Office Building”.


*Approved August 12, 2008.*

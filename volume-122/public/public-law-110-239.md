# Public Law 110–239

> An Act To amend title 4, United States Code, to encourage the display of the flag of the United States on Father’s Day.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. ADDITIONAL OCCASION FOR DISPLAY OF THE FLAG OF THE UNITED STATES.

Section 6(d) of title 4, United States Code, is amended by inserting after “Flag Day, June 14;” the following: “Father’s Day, third Sunday in June;”.


*Approved June 3, 2008.*

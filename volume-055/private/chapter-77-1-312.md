# Chapter 312

> AN ACT Granting a pension to Martha Pace.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Administrator of Veterans’ Affairs be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Martha Pace, widow of Abner Pace, late of Troop A, Second United States Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of $30 per month.


*Approved, July 21, 1941.*

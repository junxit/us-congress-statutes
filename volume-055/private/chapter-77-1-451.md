# Chapter 451

> AN ACT For the relief of Harry F. Baker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to credit the accounts of Harry F. Baker, of Rockland, Delaware, in the sum of $599.93, said amount representing payments made to Harry F. Baker for services rendered as postmaster while also holding an appointment with the Federal Housing Administration in contravention of the dual compensation statutes.


*Approved, October 16, 1941.*

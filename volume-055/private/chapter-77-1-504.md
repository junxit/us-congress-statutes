# Chapter 504

> AN ACT For the relief of Arma Lee Hogan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions and limitations of sections 15 to 20, both inclusive, of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes”, approved September 7, 1916, as amended, the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission is hereby authorized and directed to receive and consider, when filed, the claim of Arma Lee Hogan for disability alleged to have been incurred by her during December of 1937, when engaged in authorized activities while an employee of the Glenn Dale Sanatorium, Glenn Dale, Maryland, and to determine said claim upon its merits under the provisions of said Act: Provided, That said claim shall be filed with the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission not later than sixty days after the approval of this Act: And provided further, That no benefits shall accrue prior to the approval of this Act.


*Approved, November 21, 1941.*

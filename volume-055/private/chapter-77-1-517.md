# Chapter 517

> AN ACT For the relief of Tibor Hoffmann and Magda Hoffmann.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws Tibor Hoffmann and his wife Magda Hoffmann shall be held and considered to have been legally admitted to the United States for permanent residence on the date of their entry into the United States. Upon the enactment of this Act the Secretary of State is authorized and directed to instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct two numbers from the nonpreference category of the Hungarian quota.


*Approved, November 21, 1941.*

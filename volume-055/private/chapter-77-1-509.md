# Chapter 509

> AN ACT For the relief of Arthur G. Moyer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United. States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 17 and 20 of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes”, approved September 7, 1916, as amended (U. S. C., 1934 edition, title 5, secs. 767 and 770) are hereby waived in favor or Arthur G. Moyer, of Orwigsburg, Pennsylvania, who is alleged to have sustained an injury on or about February 21, 1939, while employed by the Works Progress Administration, and his case is authorized to be considered and acted upon under the remaining provisions of such Act, as amended and supplemented, if he files a claim for compensation with the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission not later than sixty days after the date of enactment of this Act: Provided, That no benefits shall accrue prior to the approval of this Act.


*Approved, November 21, 1941.*

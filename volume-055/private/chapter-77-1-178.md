# Chapter 178

> AN ACT For the relief of Frank E. Nichols.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions and limitations of sections 15 to 20, both inclusive, of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes”, approved September 7, 1916, as amended, the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission is hereby authorized and directed to receive and consider the claim of Frank E. Nichols for compensation for disability alleged to have been incurred by him about August 5, 1937, while in the employment of the National Youth Administration as supervisor at the Hampton Armory, Hampton, Virginia, and to determine said claim upon its merits under the remaining provisions of said Act: Provided, That claim hereunder shall be filed within six months from the approval of this Act.


*Approved, June 6, 1941.*

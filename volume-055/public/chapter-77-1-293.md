# Chapter 293

> AN ACT To extend the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Mississippi River at or near Friar Point, Mississippi, and Helena, Arkansas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Mississippi River at or near Friar Point, Mississippi, and Helena, Arkansas, authorized to be built by the Arkansas-Mississippi Bridge Commission and its successors and assigns by an Act of Congress, approved May 17, 1939, heretofore extended by an Act of Congress, approved May 27, 1940, are hereby further extended one and three years, respectively, from the date of approval of this Act.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, July 14, 1941.*

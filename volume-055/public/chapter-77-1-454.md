# Chapter 454

> AN ACT To authorize the Reconstruction Finance Corporation to issue notes, bonds, and debentures in the sum of $1,500,000,000 in excess of existing authority.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the amount of notes, bonds, debentures, and other such obligations which the Reconstruction Finance Corporation is authorized to issue and have outstanding at any one time under existing law is hereby increased by $1,500,000,000.


*Approved, October 23, 1941.*

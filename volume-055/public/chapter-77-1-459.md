# Chapter 459

> AN ACT To permit mining within the Organ Pipe Cactus National Monument in Arizona.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That within the Organ Pipe Cactus National Monument in Arizona all mineral deposits of the classes and kinds now subject to location, entry, and patent under the mining laws of the United States shall be, exclusive of the land containing them, subject to disposal under such laws, with right of occupation and use of so much of the surface of the land as may be required for all purposes reasonably incident to the mining or removal of the minerals and under such general regulations as may be prescribed by the Secretary of the Interior.


*Approved, October 27, 1941.*

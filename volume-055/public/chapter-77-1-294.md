# Chapter 294

> AN ACT To revive and reenact the Act entitled “An Act authorizing the village of Cassville, Wisconsin, or its assigns, to construct, maintain, and operate a toll bridge across the Mississippi River at or near Cassville, Wisconsin, and to a place at or near the village of Guttenberg, Iowa”, approved August 7, 1939.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act approved August 7, 1939, authorizing the village of Cassville, Wisconsin, or its assigns, to construct, maintain, and operate a toll bridge across the Mississippi River at or near Cassville, Wisconsin, and to a place at or near the village of Guttenberg, Iowa, be, and is hereby, revived and reenacted: Provided, That this Act shall be null and void unless the actual construction of the bridge herein referred to be commenced within one year and completed within three years from the date of approval hereof.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, July 14, 1941.*

# Chapter 398

> JOINT RESOLUTION Providing for the filling of a vacancy in the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution of the class other than Members of Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the vacancy in the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution, of the class other than Members of Congress, caused by the death of R. Walton Moore, be filled by the appointment of Frederic C. Walcott, a citizen of Connecticut, for the statutory term of six years.


*Approved, August 21, 1941.*

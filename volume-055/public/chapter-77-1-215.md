# Chapter 215

> AN ACT To authorize the use of certain lands for military purposes at the Fort McPherson Military Reservation, Georgia, and the Fort Du Pont Military Reservation, Delaware.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Atlanta National Guard target range, Georgia, comprising one hundred thirty-four and two tenths acres of land, being no longer required for National Guard purposes, is hereby incorporated in and made a part of the Fort McPherson Military Reservation, Georgia.

## § 2.

That the tract of land, comprising thirty-six one-hundredths of an acre, with dwelling house thereon, adjacent to the Fort Du Pont Military Reservation, Delaware, heretofore acquired as a part of the Chesapeake and Delaware Canal property, being no longer required for that purpose, is hereby incorporated in and made a part of the Fort Du Pont Military Reservation, Delaware.


*Approved, June 21, 1941.*

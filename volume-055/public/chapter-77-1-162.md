# Chapter 162

> AN ACT Authorizing a reduction in the course of instruction at the Naval Academy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized to reduce, in his discretion, until August 1, 1945, the course of instruction at the United States Naval Academy from four to three years and to graduate classes which have completed such reduced courses of instruction.


*Approved, June 3, 1941.*

# Chapter 278

> AN ACT To amend section 353 (b) of the Communications Act of 1934, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 353 (b) of the Communications Act of 1934, as amended (U. S. C., 1934 edition, Supp. V, title 47, sec. 353 (b)), is hereby amended by inserting before the period at the end thereof a comma and the following: “but during the emergency proclaimed by the President on September 8, 1939, to exist, but not after June 30, 1943, the aforesaid requirement of six months’ previous service may be suspended or modified by regulation or order of the Commission for successive periods of not more than six months’ duration”.


*Approved, July 8, 1941.*

# Chapter 1

> AN ACT To authorize major alterations to certain naval vessels.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purpose of improving antiaircraft defenses of combatant and auxiliary vessels of the United States, alterations to such vessels are hereby authorized, and expenditures therefor shall not be limited by the provisions of the Act approved July 18, 1935 (49 Stat. 482; 5 U. S. C., Supp. V, sec. 468a), but the total cost of such alterations shall not exceed $300,000,000.


*Approved, January 29, 1941.*

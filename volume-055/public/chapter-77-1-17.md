# Chapter 17

> AN ACT To amend the Act of May 4, 1898 (30 Stat. 380; U. S. C., title 34, sec. 21), as amended, to authorize the President to appoint additional acting assistant surgeons in time of national emergency.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of May 4, 1898, entitled “An Act making appropriations for the naval service for the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, eighteen hundred and ninety-nine, and for other purposes” (30 Stat. 380; U. S. C., title 34, sec. 21), as amended by the Act of March 18, 1940, Public, Numbered 440, Seventy-sixth Congress, is hereby further amended to read as follows: “The President is hereby authorized to appoint for temporary service one hundred acting assistant surgeons, who shall have the rank and compensation of assistant surgeons: Provided, That the Secretary of the Navy may appoint in time of war or national emergency declared by the President to exist, for temporary service, such acting assistant surgeons as the exigencies of the service may require, who shall receive the compensation of assistant surgeons.”


*Approved, March 17, 1941.*

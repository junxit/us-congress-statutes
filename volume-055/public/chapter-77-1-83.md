# Chapter 83

> AN ACT To provide for the appointment of one additional United States district judge for the northern district of Ohio.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized to appoint, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, one additional United States district judge for the northern district of Ohio: Provided, That the first vacancy occurring in the office of district judge in said district shall not be filled.


*Approved, May 1, 1941.*

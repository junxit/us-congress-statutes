# Chapter 477

> AN ACT To authorize an appropriation for the purpose of establishing a national cemetery at Honolulu, Territory of Hawaii.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $50,000 to establish a national cemetery at Honolulu, Territory of Hawaii: Provided, That a suitable location for such a cemetery, acceptable to the War Department, shall be made available without cost to the United States Government.


*Approved, November 21, 1941.*

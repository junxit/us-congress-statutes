# Chapter 280

> AN ACT Relating to the manning of certain sail vessels.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of section 13 of the Act of March 4, 1915, as amended (U. S. C., 1934 edition, Supp. V, title 46, sec. 672), relating to the manning of certain vessels, shall not apply to any sail vessel of less than five hundred tons registered tonnage, while not carrying passengers for hire, and while not operating outside the line dividing inland waters from the high seas, as defined in section 2 of the Act of February 19, 1895, as amended (U. S. C., 1934 edition, title 33, sec. 151).


*Approved, July 8, 1941.*

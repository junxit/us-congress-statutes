# Chapter 98

> AN ACT To amend the District of Columbia Motor Vehicle Financial Responsibility Act, approved May 3, 1935.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,,*

That section 2 of the District of Columbia Motor Vehicle Financial Responsibility Act of May 3, 1935 (49 Stat. 166), as amended, is amended by adding after the second subparagraph therein the following new subparagraph:

> “Reckless driving, as provided in section (9) (b) of such Traffic Acts, if personal injury occurs as a result thereof;”.


*Approved, May 9, 1941.*

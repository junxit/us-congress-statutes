# Chapter 430

> AN ACT Relating to allowances for rental quarters of certain naval officers stationed in the Canal Zone.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That any amounts paid to the Panama Canal or lessees thereof by any officer of the Navy or Marine Corps incident to his occupancy of quarters under the jurisdiction of the Panama Canal during the fiscal years 1935 and 1936, which were in excess of the amounts paid such officer as rental allowance, shall to the extent of such excess be refunded to such persons upon presentation of a claim therefor to the Comptroller General.


*Approved, October 13, 1941.*

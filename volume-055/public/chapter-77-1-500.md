# Chapter 500

> AN ACT To amend the District of Columbia Unemployment Compensation Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the District of Columbia Unemployment Compensation Act, approved August 28, 1935, as amended, is further amended to read as follows:

> In section 3 (c), line 2, after the word “year”, strike out the figures “1942” and insert in lieu thereof the figures “1943”.


*Approved, November 21, 1941.*

# Chapter 637

> AN ACT To amend the Organic Act of the Virgin Islands of the United States, approved June 22, 1936.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the last sentence of section 20 of the Organic Act of the Virgin Islands of the United States, approved June 22, 1936 (49 Stat. 1807), is hereby amended by striking out the word “shall” in said sentence and inserting in lieu thereof the word “may”, so that said sentence, as amended, will read as follows:

> “He may attend or may depute another person to represent him at the meetings of the legislative authorities herein established, and may give expression to his views on any matter before such bodies.”


*Approved, December 26, 1941.*

# Chapter 498

> AN ACT To provide for payments in advance to enlisted men of monetary allowance in lieu of quarters and subsistence under certain conditions.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 11 of the Act of June 10, 1922 (42 Stat. 630), be amended by adding thereto the following proviso: “Provided, That payments of allowance for quarters and subsistence may be made in advance to enlisted men under such regulations as the President may prescribe”.


*Approved, November 21, 1941.*

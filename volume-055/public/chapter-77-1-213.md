# Chapter 213

> AN ACT To amend section 3528 of the Revised Statutes, as amended, relating to the purchase of metal for minor coins of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3528 of the Revised Statutes, as amended (U. S. C., Supp. V, title 31, sec. 340), is hereby further amended by striking out the figure “$600,000” and inserting in lieu thereof the figure “$1,000,000”.


*Approved, June 21, 1941.*

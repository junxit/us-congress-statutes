# Chapter 424

> AN ACT To permit the steamship Port’ Saunders, official number 220150, and steamship Hawk, official number 220149, to engage in the fisheries.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the steamship Port Saunders, official number 220150, and the steamship Hawk, official number 220149, may be registered or enrolled and licensed as vessels of the United States for the purpose of engaging in the fisheries as long as such vessels are owned by a citizen of the United States, native born or fully naturalized, or a corporation which is a citizen of the United States and of which 75 per centum of the interest therein is owned by citizens of the United States, as defined in section 2 (c) of the Shipping Act, 1916, as amended: Provided, That neither of these vessels may engage in the coastwise trade under penalty of forfeiture.


*Approved, September 25, 1941.*

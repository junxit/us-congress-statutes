# Chapter 410

> AN ACT To amend the National Housing Act, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 603 of the National Housing Act is hereby amended by striking the figure “$100,000,000” appearing in subsection (a) thereof and inserting in lieu thereof the figure “$300,000,000”.


*Approved, September 2, 1941.*

# Chapter 647

> JOINT RESOLUTION Fixing the dates of meeting of the second session of the Seventy-seventh Congress and of the first session of the Seventy-eighth Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second session of the Seventy-seventh Congress shall begin at noon on Monday, January 5, 1942, and the first session of the Seventy-eighth Congress shall begin at noon Monday, January 4, 1943.


*Approved, January 2, 1942.*

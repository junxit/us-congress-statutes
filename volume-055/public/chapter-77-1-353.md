# Chapter 353

> AN ACT To authorize the Secretary of War to convey to the Territory of Hawaii certain lands on the island of Oahu, Territory of Hawaii, in consideration of the Governor of Hawaii having transferred to the United States certain lands in the Hawaiian Islands.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized to convey to the Territory of Hawaii all right, title, and interest of the United States in and to that portion of the military reservation known as Hickam Field, island of Oahu, Territory of Hawaii, containing about ninety-seven and thirteen one-hundredths acres, described in subsection (a) of this Act, in consideration of the Governor of Hawaii having transferred to the United States five tracts of land containing about seven hundred and fifty and four hundred and ninety-five one-thousandths acres in the Hawaiian Islands described in subsection (b) of this Act:

# Chapter 152

> AN ACT Authorizing the Secretary of the Interior to convey the right, title, and interest of the United States in certain property.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized, in his discretion, to convey all the right, title, and interest of the United States in lot three, block six of Wannassay’s Addition to Toppenish, Washington, to Cecelia Crow Arquette or her heirs or assigns.


*Approved, May 28, 1941.*

# Chapter 618

> AN ACT To permit fifteen-round championship boxing bouts in the Territories of Alaska and Hawaii.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 321 of the Act entitled “An Act to codify, revise, and amend the penal laws of the United States”, approved March 4, 1909, as amended, is amended by inserting at the end thereof the following new sentence: “In the case of championship bouts, the limitation on the number of rounds shall be fifteen in lieu of ten.”


*Approved, December 22, 1941.*

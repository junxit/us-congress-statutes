# House Concurrent Resolution 47

> classification act of 1923, amendments

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Clerk of the House of Representatives is authorized and directed in the enrollment of the bill H. R. 1073, entitled “An Act to amend the Classification Act of 1923, as amended”, to insert following the numeral “2” in line 16, page 5, of the engrossed bill the following: “of the Act”.


*Passed July 21, 1941.*

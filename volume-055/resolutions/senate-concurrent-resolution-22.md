# Senate Concurrent Resolution 22

> adjournment sine die

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the two houses of Congress shall adjourn on Friday, the second day of January 1942, and that when they adjourn on said day they stand adjourned sine die.


*Agreed to December 30, 1941.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 15

> coronado international memorial, arizona

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Secretary of the Senate be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, in the enrollment of the bill (S. 752) to provide for the establishment of the Coronado International Memorial, in the State of Arizona, to make the following change, viz: On page 2, line 7, of the Senate engrossed bill, after the figure “20” and the comma, strike out the words “west half” and insert in lieu thereof the following: “lots 3 and 4”.


*Agreed to August 13, 1941.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 26

> adjournment, house of representatives

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Thursday, April 3, 1941, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Monday, April 14, 1941.


*Passed April 2, 1941.*

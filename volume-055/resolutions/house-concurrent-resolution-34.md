# House Concurrent Resolution 34

> “our american government: what is it? how does it function?”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed as a House document one hundred and seventy-seven thousand copies of a revised edition of the pamphlet entitled “Our American Government: What is it? How does it function?” of which forty-five thousand copies shall be for the use of the Senate and one hundred and thirty-two thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives.


*Passed May 15, 1941.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 17

> “revenue act of 1941”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed thirty-five thousand additional copies of Public Law Numbered 250, current session, entitled “Revenue Act of 1941”, of which five thousand copies shall be for the use of the Senate document room, twenty-five thousand copies shall be for the use of the House document room, four thousand for the Committee on Ways and Means of the House, and one thousand for the Committee on Finance of the Senate.


*Agreed to September 22, 1941.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 28

> interstate migration of destitute citizens

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed nine thousand additional copies of House Report Numbered 369, current session, entitled “Report of the Select Committee to Investigate the Interstate Migration of Destitute Citizens”, which shall be for the use of said committee.


*Passed April 29, 1941*

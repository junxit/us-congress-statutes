# Chapter LIII

> An Act to suspend, in part, the act intituled “An act to alter and amend the act intituled An act laying certain duties upon Snuff and refined Sugar.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That so much of the act intituled “An act to alter and amend the act, intituled An act laying certain duties upon snuff and refined sugar,” as respects the duties therein laid upon mills and implements employed in the manufacture of snuff, and the drawbacks therein allowed upon the exportation of snuff manufactured within the United States, be suspended, from the passing of this act, until the end of the next session of Congress.


*Approved, June 1, 1796.*

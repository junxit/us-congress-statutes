# Chapter XIV

> An Act fixing the time for the next annual meeting of Congress.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, That after the third day of March next, the first annual meeting of Congress shall be on the fourth Monday of October next.


*Approved, March 2, 1791.*

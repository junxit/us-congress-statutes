# Chapter XIX

> An Act relative to the Rix-Dollar of Denmark.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, That so much of an act, intituled “An act to provide more effectually for the collection of the duties imposed by law on goods, wares and merchandise imported into the United States, and on the tonnage of ships or vessels,” as hath rated the rix-dollar of Denmark at one hundred cents, be, and the same is hereby repealed; and that this repeal shall be deemed to operate in respect to all duties which have already arisen or accrued, as well as to such as shall hereafter arise or accrue.


*Approved, March 3, 1791.*

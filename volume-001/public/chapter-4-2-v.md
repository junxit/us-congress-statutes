# Chapter V

> An Act to continue in force for a limited time, the act, in addition to the act, for the punishment of certain crimes against the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the act, intituled “An act, in addition to the act, for the punishment of certain crimes against the United States,” shall continue and be in force, for and during the term of two years, and from thence to the end of the next session of Congress, and no longer.


*Approved, March 2, 1797.*

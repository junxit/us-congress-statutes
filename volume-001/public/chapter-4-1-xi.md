# Chapter XI

> An Act to continue in force “An act to ascertain the fees in Admiralty proceedings in the District Courts of the United States, and for other purposes.”

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, That the act, intituled “An act to ascertain the fees in admiralty proceedings in the district courts of the United States, and for other purposes,” be, and is hereby continued in force, for the term of two years from the passing of this act, and from thence to the end of the next session of Congress thereafter, and no longer.


*Approved, March 31, 1796.*

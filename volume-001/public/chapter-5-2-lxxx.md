# Chapter LXXX

> An Act to suspend, for a further lime, the duties upon the manufacture of Snuff within the United States, and the drawbacks upon the exportation thereof.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all such parts of any law or laws heretofore passed, as impose duties upon mills and implements employed in the manufacture of snuff, or allow drawbacks upon the exportation of snuff manufactured within the United States, shall be, and the same hereby are suspended until the end of the first session of the sixth Congress of the United States.


*Approved, July 16, 1798.*

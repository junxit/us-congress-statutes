# Chapter XXXV

> An Act for allowing an additional compensation to the Judges of the districts of Rhode Island and Delaware.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, That there shall be allowed to the judges of the districts of Rhode Island and Delaware, each, two hundred dollars yearly, in addition to the compensation heretofore allowed, to commence on the first day of the present year, and to be paid at the treasury of the United States, in quarterly payments.


*Approved, February 27, 1795.*

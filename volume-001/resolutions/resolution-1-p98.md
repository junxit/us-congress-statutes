# Resolution

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That John White, late a commissioner to settle the accounts between the United States and the states of Pennsylvania, Delaware, and Maryland, and his clerks, John Wright, and Joshua Dawson, be considered as in office until the fourth day of February, one thousand seven hundred and eighty-nine.


*Approved, September 29, 1789.*

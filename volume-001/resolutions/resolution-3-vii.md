# Resolution VII

*Resolved United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That it shall be the duty of the respective clerks of the several district courts in the United States, to return true copies of the tables of fees payable in the supreme or superior courts of the state in which such clerk resides, to the Attorney General of the United States, on or before the fifth day of December next.


*Approved, June 9, 1794.*

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

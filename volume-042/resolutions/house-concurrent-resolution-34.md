# House Concurrent Resolution 34

> revenue law of 1921.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed sixty thousand additional copies of Public Law Numbered 98, known as the Revenue Law of 1921, of which ten thousand shall be for the Senate, forty-four thousand shall be for the House, one thousand shall be for the Senate document room, two thousand for the House document room, one thousand for the Committee on Finance of the Senate, and two thousand for the Committee on Ways and Means of the House of Representatives.


*Passed, December 12, 1921.*

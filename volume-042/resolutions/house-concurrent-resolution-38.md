# House Concurrent Resolution 38

> agricultural inquiry.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the time for the completion of the investigation by the Joint Commission of Agricultural Inquiry, created by a Senate concurrent resolution (S. Con. Res. 4) of the first session of the Sixty-seventh Congress, and the filing of the report to Congress therein directed to be made, be, and the same is hereby, extended to a date not later than the 15th of April, 1922.


*Passed, December 14, 1921.*

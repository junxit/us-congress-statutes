# House Concurrent Resolution 18

> vest-pocket congressional directory.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed and bound a vest-pocket edition of the Congressional Directory, of which there shall be one edition only during each session of Congress.

The preparation, number, style, and distribution of such Directory shall be under the control of the Joint Committee on Printing: Provided, however, That the usual number shall not be printed.


*Passed, June 24, 1921.*

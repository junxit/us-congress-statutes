# Senate Concurrent Resolution 22

> memorial to general grant.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Sergeant at Arms of the Senate and the Sergeant at Arms of the House of Representatives are hereby authorized and directed to purchase a floral wreath to be placed upon the memorial to General Ulysses S. Grant at the unveiling ceremonies on April 27, 1922, the expense of such wreath to be paid in equal proportions from the contingent funds of the Senate and House of Representatives.


*Passed, April 26, 1922.*

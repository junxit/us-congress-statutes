# Senate Concurrent Resolution 39

> washington’s birthday.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Sergeant at Arms of the Senate and the Sergeant at Arms of the House of Representatives are hereby authorized and directed to purchase a floral wreath to be placed at the base of the Washington Monument on Washington’s Birthday, February 22, 1923, on the occasion of the ceremonies attending upon the Silent Tribute, the expense of such wreath to be paid in equal proportions from the contingent funds of the Senate and House of Representatives.


*Passed, February 20, 1923.*

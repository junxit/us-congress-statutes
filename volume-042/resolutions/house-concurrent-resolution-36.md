# House Concurrent Resolution 36

> grand army encampment, 1922.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed as a House document one thousand five hundred copies of the Journal of the Fifty-sixth National Encampment of the Grand Army of the Republic for the year 1922, not to exceed $1,700 in cost, with illustrations, one thousand copies of which shall be for the use of the House and five hundred copies for the use of the Senate.


*Passed, June 30, 1922.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 53

> employment for federal prisoners.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That a special joint committee of the Senate and House of Representatives be created, composed of three members of the Committee on the Judiciary of the Senate and three members of the Committee on the Judiciary of the House of Representatives, to be designated by the President of the Senate and the Speaker of the House of Representatives, respectively, that shall investigate and report to Congress, not later than the first Monday in December, 1923, as to employment of prisoners and what articles it is desirable to produce or manufacture in the United States penitentiaries at Leavenworth, Kansas, and McNeil Island, Washington, the cost of erecting buildings and the equipment of such buildings with the necessary machinery for the production of any such articles, the probable cost of manufacture of such articles and the prices now paid under contract for such articles, and such other data as may be pertinent to the general inquiry. Such committee may employ clerical and stenographic assistance, and the expenses thereof and of the committee may be paid one-half out of the contingent fund of the Senate and one-half out of the contingent fund of the House of Representatives upon vouchers to be approved by the chairman of such joint committee, but such expenses shall not exceed $2,000.


*Passed, March 2, 1923.*

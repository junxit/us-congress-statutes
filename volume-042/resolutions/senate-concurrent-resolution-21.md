# Senate Concurrent Resolution 21

> tariff bill.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the bill (H. R. 7456) to provide revenue, to regulate commerce with foreign countries, to encourage the industries of the United States, and for other purposes, as reported to the Senate, be printed as a Senate document with an index, and that fifteen thousand additional copies be printed, of which five thousand shall be for the Senate document room, eight thousand for the House document room, one thousand for the Committee on Finance of the Senate, and one thousand for the Committee on Ways and Means of the House.


*Passed, April 20, 1922.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 37

> agricultural inquiry.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed fifty thousand additional copies of parts 1 and 2 of House Report numbered 408, being the report of the Joint Commission of Agricultural Inquiry, in four parts, of which ten thousand shall be for the Senate, thirty thousand for the House, one thousand shall be for the Senate document room, two thousand for the House document room, and seven thousand for the Joint Commission of Agricultural Inquiry.


*Passed, January 19, 1922.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 38

> crop failure, etc.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate the bill (S. 2023) defining the crop failure in the production of wheat, rye, or oats by those who borrowed money from the Government of the United States for the purchase of wheat, rye, or oats for seed, and for other purposes, to correct an error therein.


*Passed, February 12, 1923.*

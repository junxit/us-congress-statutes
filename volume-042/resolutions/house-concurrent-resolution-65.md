# House Concurrent Resolution 65

> tariff bill.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed six thousand additional copies of the bill (H. R. 7456) to provide revenue, to regulate commerce with foreign countries, to encourage the industries of the United States, and for other purposes, of which four thousand copies shall be for the House document room and two thousand copies for the Senate document room.


*Passed, August 23, 1922.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 8

> recess.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Wednesday, the 24th day of August, 1921, they stand adjourned until twelve o’clock meridian on Wednesday, the 21st day of September, 1921.


*Passed, August 24, 1921.*

## Marginal notes

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

- August 24, 1921. [S. Con. Res., No. 8.]
- Recess of Congress. Authorized from August 24th until September 21st, 1921.

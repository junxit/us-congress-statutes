# House Concurrent Resolution 23

> tariff bill.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the bill (H. R. 7456) “To provide revenue, to regulate commerce with foreign countries, to encourage the industries of the United States, and for other purposes,” be printed as a House document with an index, and that fifteen thousand additional copies be printed, of which nine thousand shall be for the use of the House, to be distributed through the folding room, four thousand for the Senate, one thousand for the Committee on Ways and Means of the House, and one thousand for the Committee on Finance of the Senate.


*Passed, July 11, 1921.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 67

> tariff bill.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (H. R. 7456) entitled “An Act to provide revenue, to regulate commerce with foreign countries, to encourage the industries of the United States, and for other purposes,” the Clerk of the House of Representatives is hereby authorized to strike out “coconuts,” appearing in paragraph 1626 of the bill, as agreed to in conference.


*Passed, September 20, 1922.*

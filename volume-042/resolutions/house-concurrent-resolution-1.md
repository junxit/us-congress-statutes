# House Concurrent Resolution 1

> joint meeting.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the hall of the House of Representatives on Tuesday, the 12th day of April, 1921, at 1 o’clock in the afternoon, for the purpose of receiving such communication as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed, April 12, 1921.*

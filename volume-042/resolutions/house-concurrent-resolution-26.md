# House Concurrent Resolution 26

> agricultural inquiry.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the time for the completion of the investigation by the Joint Commission of Agricultural Inquiry created by Senate Concurrent Resolution Number Four, of the present session, and the filing of the report to Congress therein directed to be made, be, and the same is hereby, extended to a date not later than the first Monday in January, 1922.


*Passed, August 4, 1921.*

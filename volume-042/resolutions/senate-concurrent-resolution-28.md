# Senate Concurrent Resolution 28

> floods and levees of the mississippi.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed one thousand copies of part 2 of House Report Numbered 300, Sixty-third Congress, second session, entitled “Floods and Levees of the Mississippi River,” for the use of the Senate document room.


*Passed, August 25, 1922.*

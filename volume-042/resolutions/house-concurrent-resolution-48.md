# House Concurrent Resolution 48

> joint meeting.

*Resolved by the House, of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the hall of the House of Representatives on Tuesday, the 28th day of February, 1922, at 12:30 o’clock in the afternoon, for the purpose of receiving such communication as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed, February 28, 1922.*

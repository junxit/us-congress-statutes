# House Concurrent Resolution 61

> agricultural inquiry.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed fifty thousand of parts 3 and 4 of House Report Numbered 408, being the report of the Joint Commission of Agricultural Inquiry, of which eight thousand shall be for the Senate, twenty-five thousand for the House, one thousand shall be for the Senate document room, two thousand for the House document room, and fourteen thousand for the Joint Commission of Agricultural Inquiry.


*Passed, June 29, 1922.*

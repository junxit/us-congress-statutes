# House Concurrent Resolution 22

> funeral of overseas soldiers.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Speaker is hereby authorized to appoint a committee of five Members, which committee, together with such as may be appointed by the President of the Senate, be, and are hereby, authorized to attend the funeral services of seven thousand one hundred and sixty-one soldiers from overseas, to be held on Pier Four, Hoboken, New Jersey, on the afternoon of Sunday, July tenth, at three o’clock.

The expense incident to such attendance to be paid one-half from the contingent fund of the House and one-half from the contingent fund of the Senate.


*Passed, July 6, 1921.*

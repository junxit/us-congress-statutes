# Senate Concurrent Resolution 20

> funeral of overseas soldiers.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

Whereas the United States transport Cambrai is expected to arrive in New York on or about March 30, bearing the last of the bodies of American soldiers from the battle fields of the World War Therefore be it

That a committee of five Senators, to be designated by the President of the Senate, and five members of the House of Representatives, to be designated by the Speaker, is authorized to represent the Congress at such services and ceremonies as may be determined upon as proper and appropriate. One half of the expenses of such committee shall be paid out of the contingent fund of the Senate and one-half out of the contingent fund of the House.


*Passed, March 21, 1922.*

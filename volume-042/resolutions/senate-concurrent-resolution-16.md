# Senate Concurrent Resolution 16

> holiday recess.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That when the two Houses complete the business of the calendar day of Thursday, December 22, 1921, they shall stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Tuesday, January 3, 1922.


*Passed, December 21, 1921.*

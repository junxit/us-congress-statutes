# Chapter 81

> An Act For the relief of Robert G. Whitfield.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Robert G. Whitfield, his heirs, assigns, or legal representatives, from any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $261.60 as a refund of a donation which Robert G. Whitfield made to the Government.


*Approved, February 25, 1922.*

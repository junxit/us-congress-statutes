# Chapter 239

> An Act For the relief of Joseph Zitek.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay to Joseph Zitek, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $75 as compensation for damage done to the wheat field of said Joseph Zitek, near Ulysses, Nebraska, May 29, 1921, by airplanes of the United States Air Mail Service.


*Approved, March 3, 1923.*

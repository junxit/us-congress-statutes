# Chapter 252

> An Act For the relief of John M. Green.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws John M. Green shall be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a private of Company G, Eighty-eighth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, on the 8th day of September, 1865: Provided, That no pension, bounty, or other allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 29, 1922.*

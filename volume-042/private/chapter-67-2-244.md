# Chapter 244

> An Act For the relief of Philip S. Everest.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury of the United States not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $300, and that the same be paid to Philip S. Everest, superintendent and special disbursing agent Bad River (Wisconsin) Indian Reservation, in full compensation on account of moneys expended by him for the benefit of an Indian allottee upon said reservation whose allotment had been erroneously located by said agent through misinformation given to him as to the location of a reservation corner.


*Approved, June 26, 1922.*

# Chapter 156

> An Act For the relief of William Collie Nabors.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to William Collie Nabors the sum of $3,000, in full settlement against the Government, for damages suffered by reason of his being struck and seriously injured by a Government motor cycle which was ridden or driven by a regularly enlisted soldier of the United States Army.


*Approved, February 28, 1923.*

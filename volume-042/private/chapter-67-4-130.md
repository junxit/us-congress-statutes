# Chapter 130

> An Act For the relief of Robert J. Ashe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Robert J. Ashe, who was a private in Troop G, Fifth Regiment United States Cavalry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a member of that organization on the 21st day of August, 1914: Provided, That no pay or other emoluments shall accrue by virtue of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 26, 1923.*

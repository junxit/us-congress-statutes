# Chapter 341

> An Act For the relief of James Kelly.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $3,029.46 to Warrant Officer James Kelly that has been paid by him from private funds in the replacement of public funds for which he was responsible when a major, Signal Corps, United States Army, and acting as a financial agent at Port Newark, New Jersey, and which was lost through theft.


*Approved, September 18, 1922.*

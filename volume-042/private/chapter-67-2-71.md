# Chapter 71

> An Act For the relief of Colonel Herbert Deakyne, Corps of Engineers, United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the accounting officers of the Treasury are hereby authorized and directed to allow and credit the accounts of Colonel Herbert Deakyne, Corps of Engineers, United States Army, the sum of $45.85 disallowed against him on the books of the Treasury.


*Approved, February 24, 1922.*

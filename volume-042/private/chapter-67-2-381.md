# Chapter 381

> An Act For the relief of Henry T. Hill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Henry T. Hill, formerly a private of Company D, Sixteenth Regiment United States Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a member of said company on the 19th day of May, 1902: Provided, That no pay and allowances due him on said date shall be allowed him, and that no pension shall accrue to him prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, September 21, 1922.*

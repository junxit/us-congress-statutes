# Chapter 196

> An Act For the relief of John N. Halladay.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the account of John N. Halladay, former postmaster at Oak, Alabama, with $8,012.13, and to certify said credit to the General Accounting Office, said sum being a balance due the United States which is chargeable to the embezzlement of funds and theft of money-order forms which were printed for and stolen from the post office at Oak, Alabama, and unlawfully uttered in Pensacola, Florida, and Mobile, Alabama, by Mr, and Mrs. Leon W. Mendel.


*Approved, March 2, 1923.*

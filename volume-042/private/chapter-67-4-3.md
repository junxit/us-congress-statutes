# Chapter 3

> An Act For the relief of the Cornwell Company, Saginaw, Michigan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to pay to the Cornwell Company, Saginaw, Michigan, successor to the Saginaw Beef Company, the sum of $8,000, which sum is hereby appropriated, being the amount of money collected from the Saginaw Beef Company by fine imposed by District Judge Sessions, at Grand Rapids, Michigan, on August 28, 1914.


*Approved, December 14, 1922.*

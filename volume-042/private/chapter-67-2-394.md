# Chapter 394

> An Act For the relief of D. V. Bussie.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to D. V. Bussie the sum of $1,000 on account of injury sustained by the said D. V. Bussie when struck by a United States mail truck as he was crossing Normal Boulevard at Sixty-first Street, in the city of Chicago, Illinois, on the 17th day of April, 1921.


*Approved, September 21, 1922.*

# Chapter 241

> An Act For the relief of John Calvin Starr.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to John Calvin Starr, the sum of $5,000 in full settlement against the Government for personal injuries sustained as result of being struck by runaway Government-owned mail truck.


*Approved, March 3, 1923.*

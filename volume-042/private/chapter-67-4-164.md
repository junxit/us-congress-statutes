# Chapter 164

> An Act For the relief of C. M. Rieves.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby relinquished to C. M. Rieves, of Marion, Arkansas, all right, title, and interest of the United States in the unsurveyed part of the east one-half of section eleven, lying south of the unsurveyed north half of the northeast quarter of said section, in township eight north of range eight east of the fifth principal meridian in the State of Arkansas.


*Approved, February 28, 1923.*

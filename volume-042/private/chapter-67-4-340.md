# Chapter 340

> An Act For the relief of Hugh Marshall Montgomery.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to issue patent for lot 4, containing fifty-four acres, of section 3, in township 17 north, and range 5 west, Choctaw meridian, in the State of Mississippi, to Hugh Marshall Montgomery.


*Approved, March 4, 1923.*

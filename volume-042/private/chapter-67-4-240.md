# Chapter 240

> An Act For the relief of Americus Enfield.

*Re it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the account of Americus Enfield, postmaster at Bedford, Pennsylvania, in the sum of $41,148.94, due to the United States on account of war saving stamps and postage stamps which were lost as the result of burglary of said post office on November 7, 1918.


*Approved, March 3, 1923.*

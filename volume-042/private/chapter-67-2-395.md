# Chapter 395

> An Act For the relief of William H. Slaine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to William H. Slaine the sum of $475 for damages to his automobile by a mail truck belonging to the Government.


*Approved, September 21, 1922.*

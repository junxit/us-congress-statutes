# Chapter 194

> An Act For the relief of Martin Cletner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Martin Cletner, who served under the name of Martin Cubbler as a member of Captain Wrigley’s independent company, Pennsylvania Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a member of that organization on or about the 24th day of November, 1862: Provided, That no pay, pension, bounty, or other allowance shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 2, 1923.*

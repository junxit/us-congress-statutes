# Chapter 376

> An Act For the relief of Hubert Reynolds.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Bouse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the accounts of Hubert Reynolds, postmaster at Greeley, Colorado, in the sum of $69,300, due to the United States on account of war-savings stamps, on account of losses resulting from burglary of said post office on December 26, 1918.


*Approved, September 21, 1922.*

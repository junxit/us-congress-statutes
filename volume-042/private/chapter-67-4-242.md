# Chapter 242

> An Act For the relief of Quincy R. Craft.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Quincy R. Craft, chief of office and fiscal agent, Forest Service, Department of Agriculture, the sum of $346.71, being the amount disallowed by the accounting officers of the Treasury in his account covering expenses incurred in the erection of a building at the Bessey Nursery of the Nebraska National Forest.


*Approved, March 3, 1923.*

# Chapter 152

> An Act For the relief of Harry E. Fiske.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $3,689.35 to Harry E. Fiske on account of injuries received at the Watertown Arsenal, through no fault of his own, while testing a gun carriage on January 6, 1916.


*Approved, February 28, 1923.*

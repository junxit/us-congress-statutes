# Chapter 176

> An Act For the relief of Monroe B. Shealy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Monroe B. Shealy, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $414.66 for damages to his automobile by an Army truck belonging to the Government.


*Approved, May 2, 1922.*

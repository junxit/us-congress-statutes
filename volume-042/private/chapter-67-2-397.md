# Chapter 397

> An Act For the relief of Edward A. Purdy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit Edward A. Purdy, as postmaster of Minneapolis, Minnesota, in the sum of $1,948.48, said sum being the balance due the United States Government after a collection of $1,000 had been made on the bond and applied to the shortage of certain postage stamps and cash from the money-order funds embezzled or stolen by Christian H. Mackeprang from one of the branch post offices of the said city of Minneapolis.


*Approved, September 21, 1922.*

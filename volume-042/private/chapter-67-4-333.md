# Chapter 333

> An Act For the relief of August Nelson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the homestead entry numbered 027376, Cheyenne, Wyoming, made by August Nelson on October 11, 1920, under the Act of February 19, 1909 (Thirty-fifth Statutes at Large, page 639), for lots three and four, east half of the southwest quarter, and southeast quarter of section 30, township 25 north; range 81 west, sixth principal meridian, be, and the same is hereby, validated, and the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized to issue patent thereon upon the submission of satisfactory proof of compliance with the law under which the entry was allowed.


*Approved, March 4, 1923.*

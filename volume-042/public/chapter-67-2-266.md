# Chapter 266

> An Act Declaring Tchula Lake, Holmes County, Mississippi, to be a nonnavigable stream.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That Tchula Lake, in Holmes County, in the State of Mississippi, be, and the same is hereby, declared to be a nonnavigable stream within the meaning of the Constitution and laws of the United States.

## § 2.

That the right of Congress to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, July 1, 1922.*

# Chapter 10

> An Act Amending subdivision (5) of section 302 of the War Risk Insurance Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subdivision (5) of section 302 of the War Risk Insurance Act is hereby amended to read as follows:

> ## “(5)
>
> If the disabled person is so helpless as to be in constant need of a nurse or attendant, such additional sum shall be paid, but not exceeding $20 per month, as the director may deem reasonable, and if the disabled person is blind, legless or armless and is in constant need of a nurse or attendant, such additional sum shall be paid, but not exceeding $50 per month, as the Director may deem reasonable.”


*Approved, December 18, 1922.*

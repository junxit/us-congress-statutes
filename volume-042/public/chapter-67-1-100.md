# Chapter 100

> An Act To permit the use in the post office at Cincinnati, Ohio, of special canceling stamps bearing the words “Public Health Exposition, Cincinnati, Ohio, October 15 to 22, 1921.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to permit the use in the post office at Cincinnati, Ohio, of special canceling stamps bearing the following words and figures: “Public Health Exposition, Cincinnati, Ohio, October 15 to 22, 1921.”


*Approved, October 5, 1921.*

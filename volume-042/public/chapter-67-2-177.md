# Chapter 177

> An Act To provide for the monthly payment of pensions.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That payment of pensions shall be made monthly, on the fourth day of each month, beginning not later than September, 1922: Provided, That the provisions of this Act shall not apply to civil pensions.

## § 2.

That all Acts or parts of Acts inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed.


*Approved, May 3, 1922.*

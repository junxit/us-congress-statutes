# Chapter 240

> An Act Providing for the appointment of Warrant Officer Herbert Warren Hardman as captain in the Quartermaster Corps, United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and hereby is, authorized to appoint Herbert Warren Hardman, formerly a major, Quartermaster Corps, a captain in the Quartermaster Corps, United States Army, to date from July 1, 1920, and to be placed on the promotion list in such place as is provided by section 24a of the Act of June 4, 1920, for persons appointed as captains under the provisions of section 24 of said Act.


*Approved, June 26, 1922.*

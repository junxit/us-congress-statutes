# Chapter 99

> An Act Providing for “Visit the Dunes, Michigan City,” canceling stamp to be used by Michigan City, Indiana, post office.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to permit the use in the post office at Michigan City, Indiana, a special canceling stamp bearing the following words and figures: “Visit the Dunes, Michigan City, Indiana, May 1, 1922, to November 1, 1922.”


*Approved, October 5, 1921.*

# Chapter 125

> An Act To provide that deferred grazing fees received prior to December 31, 1921, shall be considered as receipts of the fiscal year 1921.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purpose of apportioning the 25 per centum of the accrued receipts from national forests during the fiscal year ending June 30, 1921, which are due and payable to the States under the Act of May 23, 1908, and the 10 per centum of said receipts which may be expended by the Secretary of Agriculture under the Act of March 4, 1913 (Thirty-seventh Statutes, page 828), for the construction and maintenance of roads and trails within the national forests, all moneys which are received by the Secretary of Agriculture prior to December 31, 1921, as deferred grazing fees authorized to be so paid under the Act of March 3, 1921 (Public, Numbered 367, page 18), shall be considered as receipts of the fiscal year 1921.


*Approved, November 17, 1921.*

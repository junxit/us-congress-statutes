# Chapter 113

> Joint Resolution Extending the term of the National Screw Thread Commission for a period of five years from March 21, 1922.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the term of the National Screw Thread Commission, creat ed by an Act approved July 18, 1918, as amended by an Act approved March 3, 1919, is hereby extended for a period of five years from March 21, 1922.


*Approved, March 21, 1922.*

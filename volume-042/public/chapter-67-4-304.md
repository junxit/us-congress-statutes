# Chapter 304

> Joint Resolution To provide an additional appropriation for the Federal Farm Loan Board for the fiscal year 1924.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of $24,000 is appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1924, to provide payment for the following additional positions in the Federal Farm Loan Board: Two members at the rate of $10,000 each and two private secretaries at the rate of $2,000 each.


*Approved, March 4, 1923.*

# Chapter 244

> An Act To amend section 3142 of the Revised Statutes, to permit an increase in the number of collection districts for the collection of internal revenue and in the number of collectors of internal revenue from sixty-four to sixty-five.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3142 of the Revised Statutes is amended by adding at the end thereof a new paragraph to read as follows:

> “On and after July 1, 1921, the whole number of collection districts for the collection of internal revenue and the whole number of collectors of internal revenue shall not exceed sixty-five.”


*Approved, March 4, 1923.*

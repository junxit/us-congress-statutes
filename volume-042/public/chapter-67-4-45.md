# Chapter 45

> Joint Resolution For the appointment of three members of the Board of Managers of the National Home for Disabled Volunteer Soldiers.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That James W. Wadsworth, of New York; H. H. Markham, of California; and W. S. Albright, of Kansas, he, and they are hereby, appointed members of the Board of Managers of the National Home for Disabled Volunteer Soldiers of the United States, to succeed James W. Wadsworth, of New York; H. H. Markham, of California; and W. S. Albright, of Kansas, whose terms of office expire April 21, 1922.


*Approved, January 26, 1923.*

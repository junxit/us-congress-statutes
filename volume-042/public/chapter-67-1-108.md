# Chapter 108

> An Act To extend the time for the construction of a bridge across the Rio Grande, within or near the city limits of El Paso, Texas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge, authorized by Act of Congress approved October 6, 1917, to be built by the city of El Paso, Texas, across the Rio Grande, within or near the city limits of El Paso, Texas, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from the date of approval hereof.

## § 2.

That the right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, October 15, 1921.*

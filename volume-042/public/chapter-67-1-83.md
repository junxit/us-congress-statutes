# Chapter 83

> An Act Authorizing and directing the Postmaster General to permit the use of a special canceling stamp at the post office of Birmingham, Alabama, bearing the words “Birmingham semicentennial, October 24 to 29, 1921.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, ana he is hereby, authorized and directed to permit the use in the Birmingham, Alabama, post office of special canceling stamps bearing the following words: “Birmingham semicentennial, October 24 to 29.”


*Approved, August 24, 1921.*

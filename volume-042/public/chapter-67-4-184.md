# Chapter 184

> An Act Authorizing the Secretary of the Interior to approve indemnity selections in exchange for described granted school lands.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That upon the selection by the State of Wyoming under the provisions of sections 2275 and 2276, United States Revised Statutes, as amended by the Act of February 28, 1891 (Twenty-sixth Statutes, page 796), and in accordance with the regulations of the Department of the Interior governing such selections of other lands approximately equal in area in exchange for tract numbered sixty, township fifty-six north, of range sixty-nine west, of the sixth principal meridian in that State, which is a segregation by resurvey of granted school section thirty-six in said township, the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized to convey title to the State for the land so selected if found regular.


*Approved, March 2, 1923.*

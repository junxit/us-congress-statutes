# Chapter 114

> An Act Authorizing an appropriation for the construction of a road within the Fort Apache Indian Reservation, Arizona.

*Be it enacted by the senate and House of representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized an appropriation of $15,000 from any tribal funds on deposit in the Treasury to the credit of the Indians of the Fort Apache Indian Reservation, Arizona, to be immediately available, to pay one-half the cost of constructing a wagon road, within said reservation, between Cooley and the northeast boundary of said reservation: Provided, That no part of the appropriation herein authorized shall be expended until the Secretary of the Interior shall have obtained from the proper authorities of the County of Apache, Arizona, satisfactory guarantees of the payment by said county of one-half of the cost of the construction of said road.


*Approved, February 26, 1923.*

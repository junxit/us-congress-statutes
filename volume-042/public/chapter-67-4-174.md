# Chapter 174

> An Act To fix the annual salary of the collector of customs for the district of North Carolina.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the salary of the collector of customs for the district of North Carolina is hereby fixed at $5,000 per annum.


*Approved, March 2, 1923.*

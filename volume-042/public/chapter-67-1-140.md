# Chapter 140

> An Act To extend the time for constructing a bridge across the White River at or near the town of Des Arc, Arkansas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the bridge authorized by the Act of Congress approved February 19, 1920, to be built across the White River at or near the town of Des Arc, Arkansas, by Gordon N. Peay, junior, his heirs and assigns, are hereby extended one year and three years, respectively, from the date of approval hereof.

## § 2.

That the right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, November 23, 1921.*

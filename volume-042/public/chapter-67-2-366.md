# Chapter 366

> An Act Providing for the retention by the Government of the property in Nome, Alaska, known as the Detention Hospital Building, and its use by the Bureau of Education, Department of the Interior.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the property commonlyknown as the Detention Hospital Building, being situated on plot consisting of lot sixty-six, in block thirty, in the town of Nome, Territory of Alaska, according to the official plat thereof, and the easterly sixty-five feet of Second Avenue west, in said town of Nome, Territory of Alaska, vacated by said town of Nome and donated by said town of Nome to the Department of Justice on the 25th day of August, 1913, by ordinance numbered 232, and a portion and part of lot sixty-seven, in block thirty, in the town of Nome, Territory of Alaska, according to the official plat thereof, approximately eight feet in width and extending the full length of said lot sixty-seven alongside of and contiguous to said lot sixty-six, shall not be sold but shall be retained by the United States for use by the employees of the United States Bureau of Education, Department of the Interior, and for such other uses of the Government as the President may direct.

The Attorney General is authorized to transfer the custody of said building to the Secretary of the Interior for use as set forth above.

All laws or parts of laws to the extent they are in conflict with the provisions of this Act arc repealed.


*Approved, September 21, 1922.*

# Chapter 88

> An Act To control importations of dyes and chemicals.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subdivision (a) of section 501 of the Dye and Chemical Control Act, approved May 27, 1921, is amended by striking out the words “three months,” and inserting in lieu thereof the words “six months.”

## § 2.

That all furniture, file cases, typewriters, and other office appliances in use by the War Trade Section of the Department of State on May 28, 1921, shall be transferred to and become the property of the Treasury Department.


*Approved, August 24, 1921.*

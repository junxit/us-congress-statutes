# Chapter 6

> An Act To authorize the Secretary of the Interior to accept completion of Carey segregation numbered eleven and to issue patent therefor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of theInterior is hereby authorized and directed to accept work already performed and the proof submitted under Carey segregation numbered eleven, State of Colorado, and to issue patent to the State of Colorado for lands embraced therein.


*Approved, December 16, 1922.*

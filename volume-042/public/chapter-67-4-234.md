# Chapter 234

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the erection on public grounds, of a memorial to the late Joseph J. Darlington.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Chief of Engineers, United States Army, be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to select a suitable site and to grant permission for the erection on public grounds of the United States in the city of Washington, District of Columbia, other than those of the Capitol, the Library of Congress, the White House, and the Mall, of a memorial to the late Joseph J. Darlington, a leader of the Washington bar, as a gift to the people of the city of Washington: Provided, That the site chosen and the design or the memorial shall be approved by the Joint Library Committee of Congress, with the advice of the Commission of Fine Arts; that it shall be erected under the supervision of the Chief of Engineers, and that the United States shall be put to no expense in or by the erection or maintenance of the said memorial.


*Approved, March 3, 1923.*

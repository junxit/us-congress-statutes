# Chapter 131

> An Act For the relief of Richard P. McCullough.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized to restore Commander Richard P. McCullough, United States Navy, to a place on the list of commanders of the Navy to rank next after Commander Walter Albert Smead, United States Navy.


*Approved, April 11, 1922.*

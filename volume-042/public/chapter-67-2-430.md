# Chapter 430

> Joint Resolution Providing for an additional investigation of the tri-county irrigation project, Nebraska.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior, upon the payment to him in advance of the necessary funds to defray the expenses thereof, be, and he is hereby, authorized to make an additional investigation of the tri-county project in Nebraska, comprising the counties of Gosper, Phelps, and Kearney, in said State, and to extend said investigation into Adams County, Nebraska, with a view of ascertaining whether it is practicable to convey for irrigation purposes flood waters from the Platte River onto lands in said counties.


*Approved, September 22, 1922.*

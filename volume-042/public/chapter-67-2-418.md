# Chapter 418

> An Act To authorize the construction of a bridge across Pearl River at or near Tilton, Lawrence County, Mississippi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Natchez, Columbia and Mobile Railroad Company is hereby authorized to construct a bridge, across Pearl River, at a point suitable to the interests of navigation, at or near Tilton, Lawrence County, Mississippi, in accordance with the provisions of the Act entitled “An Act to regulate the construction of bridges over navigable waters,” approved March 23, 1906.

## § 2.

That the right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, September 22, 1922.*

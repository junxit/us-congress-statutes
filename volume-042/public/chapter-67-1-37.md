# Chapter 37

> An Act To extend the time for the construction of a bridge across the Arkansas River at a point near Webbers Falls, in Muskogee County, Oklahoma.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge, authorized by Act of Congress approved May 27, 1920, to be built by the county of Muskogee, State of Oklahoma, across the Arkansas River, at a point near Webbers Falls, in section eighteen, township twelve north, range twenty-one east, in the said county and State, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from the date of approval hereof.

## § 2.

That the right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, June 30, 1921.*

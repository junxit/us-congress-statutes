# Chapter 111

> An Act Providing for an International Aero Congress cancellation stamp to be used by the Omaha post office.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to permit the use in the Omaha post office of special canceling stamps bearing the following words and figures: “International Aero Congress, Omaha, November 3 to 5, 1921.”


*Approved, October 20, 1921.*

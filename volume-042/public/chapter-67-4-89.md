# Chapter 89

> An Act To revive and to reenact an Act entitled “An Act granting the consent of Congress for the construction of a bridge and approaches thereto across the Arkansas River between the cities of Little Rock and Argenta,” approved October 6, 1917.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act approved October 6, 1917, granting the consent of Congress for the county of Pulaski, in the State of Arkansas, its successors and assigns, to construct a bridge across the Arkansas River at the city of Little Rock on the site now occupied by the free highway bridge constructed by said county in the years 1896 and 1897 be, and the same is hereby, revived and reenacted: Provided, That this Act shall be null and void unless the actual construction of the bridge hereby authorized be commenced within one year and completed within three years from the date of approval thereof.

## § 2.

That the right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, February 15, 1923.*

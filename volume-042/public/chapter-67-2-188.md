# Chapter 188

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the transfer to the jurisdiction of the Joint Committee on the Library certain reservations in the District of Columbia for use in connection with the Botanic Garden.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Chief of Engineers, United States Army, is hereby authorized and directed to transfer to the jurisdiction of the Joint Committee on the Library the following United States reservations for use in connection with the Botanic Garden: Numbered 6–A, 6–B, 6–C, 6–D, 6–E, 6–F, 6–G, 6–H, 6–1, 6–K, 6–L, 6–M, 6–N, 6–0, 6–P, 6–Q, 6–R, 292, and 293, all of which lie along the site of the old James Creek Canal, from Maryland Avenue to P Street southwest, Washington, in the District of Columbia.


*Approved, May 11, 1922.*

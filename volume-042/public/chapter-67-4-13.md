# Chapter 13

> An Act To amend section 9 of the Trading with the Enemy Act as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 9 of the Trading with the Enemy Act as amended is amended by striking out the words “ eighteen months ” in such section and inserting in lieu thereof “ thirty months.”


*Approved, December 27, 1922.*

# Chapter 71

> An Act To amend an Act entitled “An Act to repeal section 3480 of the Revised Statutes of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act entitled “An Act to repeal section 3480 of the Revised Statutes of the United States,” approved July 6, 1914, be amended by adding after the word “Army” the words “Navy, and Marine Corps.”


*Approved, February 13, 1923.*

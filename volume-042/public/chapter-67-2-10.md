# Chapter 10

> An Act To add certain lands on the North Fork of the Shoshone River to the Shoshone National Forest.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the following-described lands are hereby added to the Shoshone National Forest, Wyoming, and made subject to all laws applicable to national forests: West half of section seventeen, all of sections eighteen and nineteen, west half of section twenty, west half of section twenty-nine, all of section thirty, township fifty-two north, range one hundred and five west, all of the sixth principal meridian, Wyoming.


*Approved, December 20, 1921.*

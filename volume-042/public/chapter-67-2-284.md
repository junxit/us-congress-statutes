# Chapter 284

> An Act To authorize the California Débris Commission to reimburse the city of Sacramento, California, for money expended by said city in the construction of the Sacramento weir.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from funds appropriated and contributed for the control of floods on the Sacramento River in pursuance of the Flood Control Act, approved March 1, 1917, the California Débris Commission is hereby authorized to pay to the city of Sacramento, California, the sum of $161,557.08, as equitable reimbursement of money expended by the said city in the construction of the weir at the head of the Sacramento by-pass leading into the Yolo by-pass, such structure being an essential part of the project adopted by the aforesaid Act as set forth in House Document Numbered 81, Sixty-second Congress, first session, as modified by the report of said commission submitted February 8, 1913, approved by the Chief of Engineers of the United States Army and the Board of Engineers for Rivers and Harbors, and printed in Rivers and Harbors Committee Document Numbered 5, Sixty-third Congress, first session, in so far as said plan provides for the rectification and enlargement of river channels and the construction of weirs.


*Approved, August 24, 1922.*

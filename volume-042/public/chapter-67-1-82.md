# Chapter 82

> An Act To extend the time for payment of grazing fees for the use of national forests during the calendar year 1921.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time for making payments of grazing fees for the use of national forests as provided by existing law is extended from the 1st day of September, 1921, to the 1st day of December, 1921.


*Approved, August 24, 1921.*

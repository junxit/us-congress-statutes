# House Concurrent Resolution 133

> AMERICAN MILITARY HEROISM CELEBRATION—CAPITOL ROTUNDA CEREMONIES

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

Whereas America can never forget the sacrifices of our brave military heroes: Now, therefore, be it

That the Rotunda of the Capitol may be used on Thursday, May 24, 1990, at 10:30 a.m., to allow the assembling of Members of Congress for ceremonies celebrating American Military Heroism.

## § 2.

The Architect of the Capitol may prescribe conditions for physical preparations with respect to the use of the Rotunda authorized by the first section.


*Agreed to May 23, 1990.*

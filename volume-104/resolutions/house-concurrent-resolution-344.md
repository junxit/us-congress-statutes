# House Concurrent Resolution 344

> HIS ALL HOLINESS PATRIARCH DIMITRIOS—CAPITOL ROTUNDA CEREMONY

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the rotunda of the Capitol is authorized to be used on July 10, 1990, from 6:30 p.m. to 7:30 p.m., to allow Members of Congress to greet and receive His All Holiness Patriarch Dimitrios, the 269th Ecumenical Patriarch of Constantinople. Physical preparations for the conduct of the ceremony shall be carried out in accordance with such conditions as may be prescribed by the Architect of the Capitol.


*Agreed to July 10, 1990.*

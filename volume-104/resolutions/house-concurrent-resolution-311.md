# House Concurrent Resolution 311

> “OUR FLAG” BOOKLET—HOUSE PRINT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed 108,000 additional copies of the booklet entitled “Our Flag” (House Document 100–247), of which 88,000 copies shall be for the use of the House of Representatives, and 20,000 copies shall be for the use of the Senate.


*Agreed to May 17, 1990.*

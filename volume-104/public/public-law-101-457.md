# Public Law 101–457

> An Act To designate the Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center in Albany, New York, as the “Samuel S. Stratton Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center in Albany, New York, shall be known and designated as the “Samuel S. Stratton Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Samuel S. Stratton Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center”.


*Approved October 24, 1990.*

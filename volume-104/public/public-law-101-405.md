# Public Law 101–405

> An Act To extend the temporary increase in the public debt limit.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. EXTENSION OF TEMPORARY INCREASE IN PUBLIC DEBT LIMIT.

Section 1 of Public Law 101–350 is amended by striking “October 2,1990” and inserting “October 6, 1990”.

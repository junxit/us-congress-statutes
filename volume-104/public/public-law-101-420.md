# Public Law 101–420

> An Act To amend section 5948 of title 5, United States Code, to reauthorize physicians comparability allowances.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 5948(d) of title 5, United States Code, is amended by striking out the second sentence and inserting in lieu thereof “No agreement shall be entered into under this section later than September 30, 1993, nor shall any agreement cover a period of service extending beyond September 30, 1995.”.


*Approved October 12, 1990.*

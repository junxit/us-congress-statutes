# Public Law 101–259

> An Act To designate the Federal building located at 360 South Main Street in Salt Lake City, Utah, as the “Frank E. Moss United States Courthouse”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The Federal building located at 350 South Main Street in Salt Lake City, Utah, shall be known and designated as the “Frank E. Moss United States Courthouse”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Federal building referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Frank E. Moss United States Courthouse”.


*Approved March 30, 1990.*

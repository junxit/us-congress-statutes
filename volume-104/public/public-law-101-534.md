# Public Law 101–534

> An Act To amend title 5 of the United States Code to increase the allowance for services of attendants.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. SHORT TITLE.

This Act may be cited as the “Attendant Allowance Adjustment Act”.

## § 2. INCREASE IN AMOUNT.

Subsection (a) of section 8111 of title 5, United States Code is amended by striking out “$500” and inserting in lieu thereof “$1,500”.

## § 3. EFFECTIVE DATE.

The amendment made by section 2 shall take effect October 1, 1990.


*Approved November 7, 1990.*

# Public Law 101–559

> An Act To designate the Federal Building at 88 West 100 North in Provo, Utah, as the “J. Will Robinson Federal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The Federal building located at 88 West 100 North in Provo, Utah, shall hereafter be known and designated as the “J. Will Robinson Federal Building”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, regulation, map, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Federal building referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “J. Will Robinson Federal Building”.


*Approved November 15, 1990.*

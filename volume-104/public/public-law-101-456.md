# Public Law 101–456

> An Act To redesignate the Federal building located at 1 Bowling Green in New York, New York, as the “Alexander Hamilton United States Custom House”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. REDESIGNATION.

The Federal building located at 1 Bowling Green in New York, New York, and known as the United States Custom House, shall be known and designated as the “Alexander Hamilton United States Custom House”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Federal building referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Alexander Hamilton United States Custom House”.


*Approved October 24, 1990.*

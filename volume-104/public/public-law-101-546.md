# Public Law 101–546

> An Act To designate the Post Office building at 222 West Center Street, in Orem, Utah, as the “Arthur V. Watkins Post Office Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Post Office located at 222 West Center Street, in Orem, Utah, shall hereafter be known and designated as the “Arthur V. Watkins Post Office Building”. Any reference in a law, regulation, map, document, record, or other paper of the United States to that building shall be deemed to be a reference to the Arthur V. Watkins Post Office Building.


*Approved November 14, 1990.*

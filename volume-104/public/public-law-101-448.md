# Public Law 101–448

> An Act To designate the Federal building located at 51 Southwest 1st Avenue in Miami, Florida, as the “Claude Pepper Federal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The Federal building located at 51 Southwest 1st Avenue in Miami, Florida, shall be known and designated as the “Claude Pepper Federal Building”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Federal building referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Claude Pepper Federal Building”.


*Approved October 22, 1990.*

# Public Law 101–598

> An Act To designate the Federal building located at 340 North Pleasant Valley Road in Winchester, Virginia, as the “J. Kenneth Robinson Postal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. REDESIGNATION.

The Federal building located at 340 North Pleasant Valley Road in Winchester, Virginia, and known as the Winchester Post Office, shall be known and designated as the “J. Kenneth Robinson Postal Building”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Federal building referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “J. Kenneth Robinson Postal Building”.


*Approved November 16, 1990.*

# Public Law 101–270

> An Act To suspend section 332 of the Agricultural Adjustment Act of 1938 for the 1991 crop of wheat.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 332 of the Agricultural Adjustment Act of 1938 (7 U.S.C. 1332) shall not be applicable to the 1991 crop of wheat.


*Approved April 10, 1990.*

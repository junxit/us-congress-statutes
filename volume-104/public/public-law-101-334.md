# Public Law 101–334

> An Act To amend the Ethics in Government Act of 1978 to increase the authorization of appropriations for the Office of Government Ethics.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. SHORT TITLE.

This Act may be cited as the “Ethics in Government Act Amendment of 1990”.

## § 2. INCREASE IN AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS.

Section 405(2) of the Ethics in Government Act of 1978 (5 U.S.C. App.) is amended by striking “$3,500,000” and inserting “$5,000,000”.


*Approved July 16, 1990.*

# Public Law 115–153

> An Act To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 100 Mathe Avenue in Interlachen, Florida, as the “Robert H. Jenkins, Jr. Post Office”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. ROBERT H. JENKINS, JR. POST OFFICE.

- **(a)** *Designation.—* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 100 Mathe Avenue in Interlachen, Florida, shall be known and designated as the “Robert H. Jenkins, Jr. Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References.—* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Robert H. Jenkins, Jr. Post Office”.


*Approved March 23, 2018.*

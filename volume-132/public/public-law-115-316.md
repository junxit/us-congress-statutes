# Public Law 115–316

> An Act To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 3s101 Rockwell Street in Warrenville, Illinois, as the “Corporal Jeffrey Allen Williams Post Office Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. CORPORAL JEFFREY ALLEN WILLIAMS POST OFFICE BUILDING.

- **(a)** *Designation.—* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 3s101 Rockwell Street in Warrenville, Illinois, shall be known and designated as the “Corporal Jeffrey Allen Williams Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References.—* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Corporal Jeffrey Allen Williams Post Office Building”.


*Approved December 14, 2018.*

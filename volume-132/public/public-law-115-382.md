# Public Law 115–382

> An Act To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 35 West Main Street in Frisco, Colorado, as the “Patrick E. Mahany, Jr., Post Office Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. PATRICK E. MAHANY, JR., POST OFFICE BUILDING.

- **(a)** *Designation.—* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 35 West Main Street in Frisco, Colorado, shall be known and designated as the “Patrick E. Mahany, Jr., Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References.—* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Patrick E. Mahany, Jr., Post Office Building”.


*Approved December 21, 2018.*

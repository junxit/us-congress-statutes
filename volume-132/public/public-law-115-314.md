# Public Law 115–314

> An Act To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 105 Duff Street in Macon, Missouri, as the “Arla W. Harrell Post Office”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. ARLA W. HARRELL POST OFFICE.

- **(a)** *Designation.—* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 105 Duff Street in Macon, Missouri, shall be known and designated as the “Arla W. Harrell Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References.—* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Arla W. Harrell Post Office”.


*Approved December 13, 2018.*

# Public Law 115–301

> An Act To repeal the Act entitled “An Act to confer jurisdiction on the State of Iowa over offenses committed by or against Indians on the Sac and Fox Indian Reservation”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of June 30, 1948, entitled “An Act to confer jurisdiction on the State of Iowa over offenses committed by or against Indians on the Sac and Fox Indian Reservation” (62 Stat. 1161, chapter 759) is repealed.


*Approved December 11, 2018.*

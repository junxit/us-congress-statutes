# Public Law 115–349

> An Act To designate the flood control project in Sedgwick County, Kansas, commonly known as the Wichita-Valley Center Flood Control Project, as the “M.S. ‘Mitch’ Mitchell Floodway”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The flood control project in Sedgwick County, Kansas, commonly known as the Wichita–Valley Center Flood Control Project, shall be known and designated as the “M.S. ‘Mitch’ Mitchell Floodway”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the flood control project referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “M.S. ‘Mitch’ Mitchell Floodway”.


*Approved December 21, 2018.*

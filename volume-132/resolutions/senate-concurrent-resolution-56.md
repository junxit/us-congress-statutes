# Senate Concurrent Resolution 56

> GEORGE HERBERT WALKER BUSH—FUNERAL SERVICES—CATAFALQUE AUTHORIZATION

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Architect of the Capitol is authorized and directed to transfer the catafalque which is situated in the Exhibition Hall of the Capitol Visitor Center to the rotunda of the Capitol so that such catafalque may be used in connection with services to be conducted there for the late George Herbert Walker Bush, 41st President of the United States.


*Agreed to December 3, 2018.*

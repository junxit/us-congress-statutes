# Senate Concurrent Resolution 46

> ENROLLMENT CORRECTION—H.R. 5895

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That, in the enrollment of the bill H.R. 5895, the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall make the following correction to the title so as to read: “Making consolidated appropriations for Energy and Water Development, the Legislative Branch, Military Construction, Veterans Affairs, and Related Agencies for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2019, and for other purposes.”.


*Agreed to September 13, 2018.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 99

> SELECT COMMITTEE ON NUTRITION AND HUMAN NEEDS-HEARINGS AND REPORTS

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Select Committee on Nutrition and Human Needs not to exceed three thousand additional copies of all parts of its hearings and reports on national policy.

## § 2.

The authorization conferred by section 1 of this concurrent resolution shall terminate on February 28, 1975.


*Agreed to December 12, 1974.*

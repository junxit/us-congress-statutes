# Senate Concurrent Resolution 83

> “SURGEON GENERAL’S REPORT BY THE SCIENTIFIC ADVISORY COMMITTEE ON TELEVISION AND SOCIAL BEHAVIO”—HEARINGS

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on Commerce one thousand additional copies of its hearings of the Ninety- second Congress, second session, entitled “Surgeon General’s Report by the Scientific Advisory Committee on Television and Social Behavior.”.


*Agreed to May 23, 1974.*

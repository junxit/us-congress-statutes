# Houses Concurrent Resolution 594

> JOINT MEETING

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Monday, August 12, 1974, at 9 p.m. for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed August 12, 1974.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 75

> ADJOURNMENT—SENATE

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That when the Senate completes its business today, Wednesday, March 13, 1974, it stand adjourned until noon, Tuesday, March 19, 1974.


*Agreed to March 13, 1974.*

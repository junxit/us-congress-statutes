# House Concurrent Resolution 611

> CORRECTION OF ENROLLED BILL H. R. 15842

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Clerk of the House of Representatives in the enrollment of the bill (H.R. 15842) to increase compensation for District of Columbia policemen, firemen, and teachers; to increase annuities payable to retired teachers in the District of Columbia; to establish an equitable tax on real property in the District of Columbia; to provide for additional revenue for the District of Columbia; and for other purposes, is directed to make the corrections described as follows:

In Section 202(2), Teachers and School Officers Salary Schedule (which is effective on the first pay period beginning on or after January 1, 1975), strike out in Class 15 “Group D—Doctor’s”, and insert in lieu thereof “Group D, master’s degree + 60 or doctor’s”.


*Passed August 22, 1974.*

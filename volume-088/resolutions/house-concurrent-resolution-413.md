# House Concurrent Resolution 413

> JOINT MEETING

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Wednesday, January 30, 1974. at 9:00 o’clock p.m., for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed January 22, 1974.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 106

> “PUBLIC FINANCING OF FEDERAL ELECTIONS”—HEARINGS

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on Rules and Administration one thousand additional copies of its hearings of the first session of the Ninety-third Congress entitled “Public Financing of Federal Elections”.


*Agreed to December 12, 1974.*

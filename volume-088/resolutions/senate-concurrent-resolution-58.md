# Senate Concurrent Resolution 58

> COMMISSION ON THE BANKRUPTCY LAWS OF THE UNITED STATES—REPORT

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on the Judiciary one thousand additional copies each of parts I and II of the Report of the Commission on the Bankruptcy Laws of the United States (House Document 93–137).


*Agreed to March 4, 1974.*

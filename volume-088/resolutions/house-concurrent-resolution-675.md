# House Concurrent Resolution 675

> CONSTITUTION OF THE UNITED STATES-POCKET-SIZE EDITION

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed as a House document the Constitution of the United States (pocket-size edition), as amended through July 5, 1971, and there be printed two hundred seventy-two thousand five hundred additional copies of such document, of which two hundred twenty-one thousand shall be for the use of the House of Representatives and fifty-one thousand five hundred shall be for the use of the Senate.


*Passed December 20, 1974.*

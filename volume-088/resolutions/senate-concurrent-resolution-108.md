# Senate Concurrent Resolution 108

> GERALD R. FORD—PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

Whereas Gerald R. Ford was a Member of Congress for twenty-five years; and Whereas he is known to the Congress as a good and faithful friend; and Whereas he assumes today the Office of President of the United States: Now, therefore, be it Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),

That the Congress extends to Gerald R. Ford its sincere best wishes, its assurances of firm cooperation and its fervent hopes for success in office.


*Agreed to August 9, 1974.*

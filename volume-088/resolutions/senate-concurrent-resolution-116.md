# Senate Concurrent Resolution 116

> “THE MARIHUANA-HASHISH EPIDEMIC AND ITS IMPACT ON UNITED STATES SECURITY”—HEARINGS

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on the Judiciary ten thousand additional copies of the hearings before its Subcommittee on Internal Security during the present session entitled “The Marihuana-Hashish Epidemic and Its Impact on United States Security”.


*Agreed to December 12, 1974.*

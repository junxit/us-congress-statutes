# House Concurrent Resolution 445

> “STATE POSTSECONDARY EDUCATION COMMISSIONS”—HEARINGS

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed for the use of the Committee on Education and Labor, House of Representatives, one thousand copies of the Oversight Hearings entitled “State Postsecondary Education Commissions”, held April 9, 11, and 12, 1973.


*Passed July 15, 1974.*

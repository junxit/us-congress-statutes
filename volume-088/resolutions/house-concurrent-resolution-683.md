# House Concurrent Resolution 683

> PORTRAIT OF HONORABLE LEONOR K. SULLIVAN-PRESENTATION

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the transcript of the proceedings in the Committee on Merchant Marine and Fisheries of September 24, 1974, incident to the presentation of a portrait of the Honorable Leonor K. Sullivan to the Committee on Merchant Marine and Fisheries be printed as a House document with illustrations and suitable binding.

## § 2.

In addition to the usual number, there shall be printed one thousand five hundred copies of such document for the use of the Committee on Merchant Marine and Fisheries.


*Passed December 18, 1974.*

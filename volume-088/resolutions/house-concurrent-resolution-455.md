# House Concurrent Resolution 455

> “OUR AMERICAN GOVERNMENT. WHAT IS IT? HOW DOES IT WORK?”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed, as a House document, a revised edition of the House document “Our American Government. What Is it? How Does It Work?”; and that five hundred and sixty-five thousand additional copies lie printed, of which four hundred and forty-two thousand shall be for the use of the House of Representatives and one hundred and three thousand shall be for the use of the Senate, and twenty thousand shall be for the use of the Joint Committee on Printing.


*Passed June 17, 1974.*

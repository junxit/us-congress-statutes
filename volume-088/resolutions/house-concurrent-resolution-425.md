# House Concurrent Resolution 425

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Thursday, February 7, 1974, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Wednesday, February 13, 1974, and that when the Senate adjourns on Friday, February 8, 1974, it stand adjourned until 12:00 o’clock meridian, Monday, February 18, 1974.


*Passed February 7, 1974.*

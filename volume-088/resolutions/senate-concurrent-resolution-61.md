# Senate Concurrent Resolution 61

> “CONFIDENCE AND CONCERN: CITIZENS VIEW AMERICAN GOVERNMENT—A SURVEY OF PUBLIC ATTITUDES”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on Government Operations two thousand additional copies of part I of its committee print entitled “Confidence and Concern: Citizens View American Government—A Survey of Public Attitudes”, dated December 3, 1973.


*Agreed to March 4, 1974.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 223

> “UNITED STATES SPACE WEEK”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President is requested to issue a proclamation designating the seven-day period of July 15 through 21, 1974, as “United States Space Week”, and calling upon the people of the United States to observe such period with appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Passed July 16, 1974.*

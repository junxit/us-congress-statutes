# Senate Concurrent Resolution 59

> “DISCLOSURE OF CORPORATE OWNERSHIP”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the compilation entitled “Disclosure of Corporate Ownership”, prepared by the Subcommittees on Intergovernmental Relations and Budgeting, Management and Expenditures, of the Senate Committee on Government Operations, be printed with illustrations as a Senate document; and that there be printed five thousand additional copies of such document for the use of that committee.


*Agreed to March 4, 1974.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 474

> “CONTROLLING THE DAMAGE: UNITED STATES POLICY OPTIONS FOR GREECE”—REPORT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed for use by the Committee on Foreign Affairs of the House of Representatives ten thousand copies of the report of a study mission to Greece entitled “Controlling the Damage: United States Policy Options for Greece”.


*Passed July 15, 1974.*

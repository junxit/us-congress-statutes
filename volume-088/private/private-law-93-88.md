# Private Law 93–88

> AN ACT For the relief of Lidia Myslinska Bukosky.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Lidia Myslinska Bokosky, the widow of a citizen of the United States, shall be held and considered to be within the purview of section 201(b) of that Act and the provisions of section 204 of such Act, shall not be applicable in this case.


*Approved August 17, 1974.*

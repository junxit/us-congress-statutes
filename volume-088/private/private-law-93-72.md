# Private Law 93–72

> AN ACT For the relief of Monroe A. Lucas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Monroe A. Lucas, of Monticello, Illinois, is hereby relieved of all liability to the United States for any loss in the Farmers Home Administration rural housing loan account of James A. Lewis, Junior, of Effingham County, Illinois, as a result of a fire which destroyed the house of the said James A. Lewis, Junior, on April IS, 1970, while the said Monroe A. Lucas was the Farmers Home Administration county supervisor for Effingham County.


*Approved June 8, 1974.*

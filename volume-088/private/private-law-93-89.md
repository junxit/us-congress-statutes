# Private Law 93–89

> AN ACT For the relief of Melissa Catambay Gutierrez and Milagros Catambay Gutierrez.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Melissa Catambay Gutierrez and Milagros Catambay Gutierrez may be classified as children within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of the Act, upon approval of petitions filed in their behalf by Mr. and Mrs. Ulpiano F. Gutierrez, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of the said Act: Provided, That the brothers or sisters of the beneficiaries shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved August 17, 1974.*

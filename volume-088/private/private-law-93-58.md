# Private Law 93–58

> AN ACT For the relief of Flora Datiles Tabayo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Flora Datiles Tabayo may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of the Act, upon approval of a petition filed in her behalf by Mr. and Mrs. Jack Lee, citizens of the United States, respectively, pursuant to section 204 of the Act: Provided, That the brothers or sisters of the beneficiary shall not by virtue of Such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved April 12, 1974.*

# Private Law 93–61

> AN ACT For the relief of Jorge Mario Bell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Jorge Mario Bell may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of such Act upon approval of a petition filed in his behalf by James Francis Bell III. a citizen of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of such Act. The natural brothers and sisters of the said Jorge Mario Bell shall not. by virtue of such relationship, be. accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved April 12, 1974.*

# Private Law 93–60

> AN ACT For the relief of Uhel D. Polly.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the patent laws of the United States, with respect to United States patent numbered 3.459,614 (Uhel I). Polly, of Fort Lauderdale, Florida, patentee) that the period in regard to public use or sale in this country as stated in section 102(b), title 35 of the United States (lode be enlarged to two years prior to the date of the application of aforesaid patent. Nothing contained in this Act shall bar any person from exercising any rights which vested prior to the effective date of this Act.


*Approved April 12, 1974.*

# Private Law 93–65

> AN ACT For the relief of Vo Thi Suong (Nini Anne Hoyt).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act. Vo Thi Suong (Nini Anne Hoyt) may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b) (1) (F) of the Act, upon approval of a petition filed in her behalf by Lieutenant Colonel and Mrs. Max B. Hoyt, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of the Act: Provided, That the brothers or sisters of the beneficiary shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved April 12, 1974.*

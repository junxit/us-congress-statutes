# Private Law 93–91

> AN ACT To confer United States citizenship posthumously upon Lance Corporal Federico Silva.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Lance Corporal Federico Silva, a native of Mexico, who was serving in the united States Marine Corps when he was killed in action on December 18, 1965, shall be held and considered to have been a citizen of the United States at the time of his death.


*Approved August 20, 1974.*

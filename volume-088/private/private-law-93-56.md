# Private Law 93–56

> AN ACT For the relief of Raphael Johnson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Raphael Johnson shall be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b) (1)(F) of such Act upon approval of a petition filed in his behalf, pursuant to section 204 of such Act, by Mrs. Howard M. Johnson, a citizen of the United States. The brothers and sisters of said Raphael Johnson shall not by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act. The provisions of section 245(c) of the Immigration and Nationality Act shall be inapplicable in this case.


*Approved March 29, 1974.*

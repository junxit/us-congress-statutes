# Private Law 93–55

> AN ACT For the relief of Robert J. Martin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, on such terms as it deems just, the United States Postal Service is authorized to compromise, release, or discharge in whole or in part the liability of Robert J. Martin, of Lake Carmel, New York, to the United States for the loss resulting from the theft of an amount of cash in his custody as a mailtruck driver, which was taken in a theft occurring on January 2, 1969.


*Approved March 29, 1974.*

# Private Law 93–102

> AN ACT For the relief of Anita Tomasi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Anita Tomasi may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of such Act upon approval of a petition filed in her behalf by Miss Theresa Tomasi, a citizen of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of such Act: Provided, That the brothers or sisters of the beneficiary shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved December 27, 1974.*

# Private Law 93–69

> AN ACT For the relief of Kamal Antoine Chalaby.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, the periods of time Kamal Antoine Chalaby has resided in the United States since his lawful admission for permanent residence on October 31, 1962, shall be held and considered to meet the residence and physical presence requirements of section 316 of the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved May 16, 1974.*

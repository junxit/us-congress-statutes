# Private Law 93–75

> AN ACT For the relief of Charito Fernandez Bautista.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203(a) (2) and 204 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Charito Fernandez Bautista shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Petronio D. Gonzalo, lawful resident aliens of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents or brothers and sisters of the beneficiary shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved June 22, 1974.*

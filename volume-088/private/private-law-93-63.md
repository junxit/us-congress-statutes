# Private Law 93–63

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Jozefa Sokolowska Domanski.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Jozefa Sokolowska Domanski shall he held and considered to be within the purview of section 203 (a) (2) of that Act and the provisions of section 204 of that Act shall not be applicable in this case.


*Approved April 12, 1974.*

# Private Law 93–122

> AN ACT For the relief of Judith E. Sterling.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 101 (a) (27) (B) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Judith E. Sterling shall be held and considered to be a returning resident alien.


*Approved January 2, 1975.*

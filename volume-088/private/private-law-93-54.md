# Private Law 93–54

> AN ACT For the relief of Georgina Henrietta Harris.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Georgina Henrietta Harris, the adopted daughter of Mrs. Esthyr Harris, a United States citizen, shall be held and considered to have met the residence and physical presence requirements of section 323 of such Act.


*Approved March 29, 1974.*

# Public Law 547

> An Act To amend section 3031 of title 10, United States Code, to increase the number of authorized Deputy Chiefs of Staff for the Army Staff.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3031(a) of title 10, United States Code, is amended by striking out “three” in clause (3) and inserting in place thereof “four”.


*Approved December 26, 1974.*

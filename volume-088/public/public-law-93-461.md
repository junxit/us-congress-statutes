# Public Law 93–461

> An Act To repeal the “cooly trade” laws.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 2158–2163, Revised Statutes, and sections 1, 2, and 4 of the Act of March 3, 1875 (ch. 141, 18 Stat. 477) (8 U.S.C. 331–339), are hereby repealed.


*Approved October 20, 1974.*

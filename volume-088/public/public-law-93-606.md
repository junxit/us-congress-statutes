# Public Law 93–606

> AN ACT To amend the Act of September 2, 1960, as amended, so as to authorize different minimum grade standards for packages of grapes and plums exported to different destinations.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1 of the Act of September 2, I960, as amended (74 Stat. 734J, is amended by inserting in the first sentence thereof “and destination” immediately following the words “such variety” and “to such destination” at the end of such sentence.


*Approved January 2, 1975.*

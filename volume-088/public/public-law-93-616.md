# Public Law 93–616

> AN ACT To designate a national laboratory as the “Holifield National Laboratory“.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Oak Ridge National Laboratory at Oak Ridge, Tennessee, shall hereafter be known and designated as the “Holifield National Laboratory”. Any reference in any law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to the Oak Ridge National Laboratory shall be held to be reference to the Holifield National Laboratory.


*Approved January 2, 1975.*

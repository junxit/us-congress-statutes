# Public Law 93–470

> An Act To suspend the duty on synthetic rutile until the close of June 30, 1977.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subpart B of part 1 of the Appendix to the Tariff Schedules of the United States (19 U.S.C. 1202) is amended by inserting after item 911.16 the following new item:

> | “ | 911.25 | Synthetic rutile (provided for in item 603.70, pt. 1, schedule 6) | Free | No Change | On or before 6/30/77 | ”. |
> |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## § 2.

The amendment made by the first section of this Act shall apply with respect to articles entered, or withdrawn from warehouse, for consumption on or after the date of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved October 26, 1974.*

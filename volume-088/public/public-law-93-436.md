# Public Law 436

> An Act To extend the appropriation authorization for reporting of weather modification activities.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 6 of the Act entitled “An Act to provide for the reporting of weather modification activities to the Federal Government”, approved December 18, 1971 (15 U.S.C. 330e), is amended by striking out “ending June 30, 1973, and June 30, 1974,” and inserting in lieu thereof “1973, 1974, 1975, 1976, and 1977,”.


*Approved October 5, 1974.*

# Public Law 551

> An Act To amend the Act to incorporate Little League Baseball to provide that the league shall be open to girls as well as to boys.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3 of the Act of July 16, 1964, entitled “An Act to incorporate the Little League Baseball, Incorporated” (Public Law 88–378), is amended by striking out “boys” each place it appears and inserting in lieu thereof “young people” and by striking out “citizenship, sportsmanship, and manhood” and inserting in lieu thereof “citizenship and sportsmanship”.


*Approved December 26, 1974.*

# Public Law 93–504

> AN ACT To eliminate discrimination based on sex in the youth programs offered by the Naval Sea Cadet Corps.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2 of the Act entitled “An Act to incorporate the Naval Sea Cadet Corps”, approved September 10, 1962 (36 U.S.C. 1042), is amended by striking out “boys” and inserting in lieu thereof “young people”.


*Approved November 29, 1974.*

# Public Law 367

> Joint Resolution To extend the expiration date of the Defense Production Act of 1950.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first sentence of section 717 (a) of the Defense Production Act of 1950 is amended by striking out “July 30” and inserting in lieu thereof “September 30”.


*Approved August 7, 1974.*

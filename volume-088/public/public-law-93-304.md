# Public Law 304

> An Act To authorize appropriations for United States participation in the International Ocean Exposition ’75.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That this Act may be cited as the “International Ocean Exposition Appropriations Authorization Act of 1973”.

## § 2.

There is authorized to be appropriated for the United States Information Agency for “Special International Exhibitions”, for United States participation in the International Ocean Exposition to be held in Okinawa, Japan, in 1975, as authorized by the Mutual Educational and Cultural Exchange Act of 1961, as amended (22 U.S.C. 2451 et seq.), $5,600,000, to remain available until expended: Provided, That the amount authorized to be appropriated herein shall be available without regard to section 3108 of title 5, United States Code.


*Approved June 8, 1974.*

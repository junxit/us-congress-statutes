# Public Law 93–534

> AN ACT To allow advance payment of subscription charges for publication for official use prepared for auditory as well as visual usage.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of June 12, 1930 (46 Stat. 580, 31 U.S.C. 530a), as amended, is hereby amended by adding at the end thereof the following new section:

> ## § 2.
>
> For the purposes of this Act, the term ‘other publications’ shall include any publication printed, microfilmed, photocopied, or magnetically or otherwise recorded for auditory or visual usage.”


*Approved December 22, 1974.*

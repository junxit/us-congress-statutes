# Public Law 553

> Joint Resolution Relative to the convening of the first session of the Ninety-fourth Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first regular session of the 94th Congress shall begin at 12 o’clock noon on Tuesday, January 14, 1975.


*Approved December 27, 1974.*

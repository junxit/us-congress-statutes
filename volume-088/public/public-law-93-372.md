# Public Law 372

> Joint Resolution To extend by sixty-two days the expiration date of the Export Administration Act of 1969.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 14 of the Export Administration Act of 1969 is amended by striking out “July 30” and inserting in lieu thereof “September 30”.


*Approved August 14, 1974.*

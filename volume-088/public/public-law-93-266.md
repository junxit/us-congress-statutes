# Public Law 93–266

> AN ACT To amend the Act entitled “An Act to incorporate the American Hospital of Paris”, approved January 30, 1913 (37 Stat. 654).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** section 4 of the Act entitled “An Act to incorporate the American Hospital of Paris”, approved January 30, 1913 (37 Stat. 654), is amended by deleting “nor more than twenty”.
- **(b)** Section 6 of such Act is amended by deleting “an equal number of” wherever it appears therein.


*Approved April 12, 1974.*

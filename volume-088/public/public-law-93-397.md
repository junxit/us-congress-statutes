# Public Law 93–397

> AN ACT To amend section 8202(a) of title 10, United States Code, to extend for two years the period during which the authorized number for the grades of lieutenant colonel and colonel in the Air Force are increased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That beginning with October 1, 1974, through September 30, 1976, the columns under the headings “For colonels” and “For lieutenant colonels” contained in the table in section 8202(a) of title 10, United States Code, are suspended. for such period such columns shall read as follows:

> | “For colonels | For lieutenant colonels |
> |---|---|
> | 3,395 | 6,370 |
> | 3,743 | 7,552 |
> | 4,091 | 8,733 |
> | 4,440 | 9,914 |
> | 4,788 | 1,095 |
> | 5,136 | 12,276 |
> | 5,484 | 13,452 |
> | 5,833 | 14,638 |
> | 6,181 | 15,819 |
> | 6,528 | 17,001 |
> | 6,876 | 18,162 |
> | 7,226 | 19,363 |
> | 7,573 | 20,544 |
> | 7,921 | 21,726”. |


*Approved August 29, 1974.*

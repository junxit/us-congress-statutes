# Public Law 93–520

> AN ACT To extend for one year the time for entering into a contract under section 106 of the Water Resources Development Act of 1974.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 106 of the Water Resources Development Act of 1974 (Public Law 93–251) is amended by striking out “June 1, 1974” and inserting in lieu thereof “June 1, 1975”.


*Approved December 13, 1974.*

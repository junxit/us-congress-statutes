# Public Law 93–472

> An Act To extend for one year the authorization for appropriations to implement title I of the Marine Protection, Research, and Sanctuaries Act of 1972.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 111 of the Marine Protection, Research, and Sanctuaries Act of 1972 (Public Law 92–532; 86 Stat. 1052) is amended by striking “fiscal year 1974,” and inserting in lieu thereof “fiscal years 1974 and 1975,”.


*Approved October 26, 1974.*

# Public Law 331

> Joint Resolution To extend by thirty days the expiration date of the Export-Import Bank Act of 1945.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 8 of the Export-Import Bank Act of 1945 is amended by striking out “June 30” and inserting in lieu thereof “July 30”.


*Approved July 4, 1974.*

# Public Law 327

> Joint Resolution To extend by thirty days the expiration date of the Export Administration Act of 1969.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 14 of the Export Administration Act of 1969 is amended by striking out “June 30” and inserting in lieu thereof “July 30”


*Approved June 30, 1974.*

# Public Law 93–453

> An Act To authorize the conveyance of certain lands to the New Mexico State University, Las Cruces, New Mexico.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the acreage limitation in the Act of June 14, 1926, as amended (43 U.S.C. 869–4), the Secretary of the Interior may convey to the New Mexico State University at Las Cruces. New Mexico, in accordance with the provisions of that Act, all or any part of the following described lands: All of the above-described lands lie in sections 13, 14, 15, 22, 23, 24, 25, 26, and 35, township 23 south, range 2 east, New Mexico principal meridian.

- **(1)** Those lands described in Public Land Order Numbered 2051, containing 1,393.19 acres;
- **(2)** Those lands described in Public Land Order Numbered 3685, containing 2,789.07 acres; and
- **(3)** Southwest quarter, section 14, township 23 south, range 2 east. New Mexico principal meridian, consisting of 160 acres.


*Approved October 18, 1974.*

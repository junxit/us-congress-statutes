# House Concurrent Resolution 63

> violations of antitrust acts.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That twenty-five thousand copies of the majority and minority reports of the committee authorized under H. Res. 148 to investigate violations of the antitrust Act of eighteen hundred and ninety and other Acts he printed for the use of the House, fifteen thousand to be distributed through the folding room and ten thousand through the document room.


*Passed August 26, 1912.*

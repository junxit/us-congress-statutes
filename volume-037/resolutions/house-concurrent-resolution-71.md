# House Concurrent Resolution 71

> tariff hearings.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed and bound in buckram two thousand five hundred copies of “ Tariff Hearings ” before the Committee on Ways and Means of the House of Representatives since the sixth day of January last, one thousand seven hundred copies for the use of the House and eight hundred copies for the use of the Senate.


*Passed March 1, 1913.*

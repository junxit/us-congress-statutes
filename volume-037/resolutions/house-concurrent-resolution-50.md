# House Concurrent Resolution 50

> congratulations to china.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the United States of America congratulates the people of China on their assumption of the powers, duties, and responsibilities of self-government, and expresses the confident hope that, in the adoption and maintenance of a republican form of government, the rights, liberties, and happiness or the Chinese people will be secure and the progress of the country insured.


*Passed April 17, 1912.*

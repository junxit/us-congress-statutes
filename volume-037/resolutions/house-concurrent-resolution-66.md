# House Concurrent Resolution 66

> holiday recess.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Thursday, December nineteenth, nineteen hundred and twelve, they stand adjourned until twelve o’clock M. on Thursday, January second, nineteen hundred and thirteen.


*Passed December 13, 1912.*

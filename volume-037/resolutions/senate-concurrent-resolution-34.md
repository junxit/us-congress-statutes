# Senate Concurrent Resolution 34

> judicial code.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed thirty thousand copies of the Judicial Code of the United States, prepared under the direction of the Judiciary Committee of the Senate, ten thousand copies of which shall be for the use of the Senate and twenty thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives.


*Passed February 18, 1913.*

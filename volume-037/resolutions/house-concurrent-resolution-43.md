# House Concurrent Resolution 43

> typhoid fever bulleting.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed fifty thousand copies of Public Health Bulletin numbered Fifty-one, with illustrations, entitled “The Causation and Prevention of Typhoid Fever,” thirty-five thousand for the use of the House of Representatives and fifteen thousand for the use of the Senate.


*Passed June 11, 1912.*

# Public Resolution 36

> Joint Resolution Providing for the payment of the expenses of the Senate in the impeachment trial of Robert W. Archbald.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be appropriated from any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated the sum of ten thousand dollars, or so much thereof as may be necessary, to defray the expenses of the Senate in the impeachment trial of Robert W. Archbald.


*Approved, August 1, 1912.*

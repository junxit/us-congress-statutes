# Senate Concurrent Resolution 36

> impeachment of robert w. archbald.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed and bound one thousand five hundred copies of the proceedings in the Senate of the United States and in the House of Representatives and before the Judiciary Committee thereof in the matter of the impeachment of Robert W. Archbald, additional circuit judge of the United States from the third judicial circuit, and designated a judge of the Commerce Court, of which five hundred shall be for the use of the Senate and one thousand for the use of the House of Representatives.


*Passed March 3, 1913.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 40

> fertilizer resources of the united states.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed ten thousand copies of Senate Document Numbered One hundred and ninety, “Fertilizer Resources of the United States,” message from the President of the United States, December eighteenth, nineteen hundred and eleven, for the use of the House.


*Passed March 11, 1912.*

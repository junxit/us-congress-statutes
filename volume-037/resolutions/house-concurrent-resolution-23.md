# House Concurrent Resolution 23

> cotton tare.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed and bound in volume form, with accompanying illustrations, one hundred thousand copies of the Special Consular Reports on Cotton Tare, submitted by the Department of State, in response to the request of Representative William G. Brantley, of which thirty thousand shall be for the use of the Senate and sixty-five thousand for the use of the House of Representatives, and five thousand to be delivered to the House document room for distribution.


*Passed February 27, 1912.*

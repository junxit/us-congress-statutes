# Senate Concurrent Resolution 39

> capital punishment, disctrict of columbia.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return the bill (S. 7162) to amend section eight hundred and one of the Code of Law for the District of Columbia.


*Passed January 30, 1913.*

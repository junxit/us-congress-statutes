# Senate Concurrent Resolution 27

> hookworm and soil pollution chart.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate document room two thousand copies on manilla paper of a wall chart on “Hookworm And Soil Pollution.”


*Passed August 24, 1912.*

# Public Resolution 10

> Joint Resolution To appoint George Gray a member of the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the vacancy in the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution, in the class other than Members of Congress, shall be filled by the reappointment of George Gray, a citizen of Delaware.


*Approved, February 7, 1913.*

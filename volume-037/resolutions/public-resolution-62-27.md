# Public Resolution 27

> Joint Resolution To appoint Andrew D. White a member of the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the vacancy in the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution, in the class other than Members of Congress, shall be filled by the reappointment of Andrew D. White, a citizen of New York.


*Approved, June 26, 1912.*

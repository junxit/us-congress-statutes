# Senate Concurrent Resolution 38

> alaska laws.

*Resolved by the Senate, (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed four thousand copies of the laws of the United States applicable to the Territory of Alaska, compiled by the Committee on Territories of the Senate and the Committee on Territories of the House of Representatives in compliance with Public Act Numbered Three hundred and thirty-four, fifteen hundred copies of which shall be for the use of the Senate, and two thousand five hundred copies for the use of the House of Representatives.


*Passed February 18, 1913.*

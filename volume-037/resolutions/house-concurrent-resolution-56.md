# House Concurrent Resolution 56

> omnibus pension bill, h. r. 23557.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Clerk of the House be authorized and directed in the enrollment of the bill (H. R. 23557) entitled “An Act granting pensions and increase of pensions to certain soldiers and sailors of the Civil War and certain widows and dependent children of soldiers and sailors of said war,” to strike out on page thirty-five, line twenty-three, the name “Moffitt” and insert name “Maffitt.”


*Passed June 13, 1912.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 28

> panama canal.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Committees on Enrolled Bills of the two Houses be authorized to correct the enrolled bill of the House (H. R. 21969) entitled “An act to provide for the opening, maintenance, protection, and operation of the Panama Canal, and the sanitation and government of the Canal Zone,” by inserting in section eleven, after the words “or elsewhere” where they occur the second time, the words “with which said railroad or other carrier aforesaid does or may compete for traffic.”


*Passed August 17, 1912.*

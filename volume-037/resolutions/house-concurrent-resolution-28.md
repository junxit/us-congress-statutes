# House Concurrent Resolution 28

> national monetary commission.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed, for the use of the House of Representatives, thirty thousand copies of the report, with accompanying bill, of the National Monetary Commission, to be delivered to the superintendent of the folding room of the House of Representatives for distribution.


*Passed March 11, 1912.*

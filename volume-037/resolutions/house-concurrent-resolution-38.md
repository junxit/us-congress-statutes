# House Concurrent Resolution 38

> soil survey, caddo parish, la.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be reprinted two thousand additional copies of the Soil Survey of Caddo Parish, Louisiana, for the use of the House document room.


*Passed August 24, 1912.*

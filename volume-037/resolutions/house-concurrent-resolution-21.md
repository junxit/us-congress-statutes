# House Concurrent Resolution 21

> holiday recess.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Thursday, December 21st, they stand adjourned until twelve o’clock meridian on Wednesday, January third, nineteen hundred and twelve.


*Passed December 11, 1911.*

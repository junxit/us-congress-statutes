# Chapter 76

> An Act For the relief of Isaac Thompson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws and the laws governing the National Home for Disabled Volunteer Soldiers, or any branch thereof, Isaac Thompson, now a resident of Columbus, Franklin County, Ohio, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a private in Company F, First Regiment Ohio Volunteer Cavalry, on the twenty-sixth day of February, eighteen hundred and sixty-five; Provided, That no pension shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 25, 1913.*

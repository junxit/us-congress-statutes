# Chapter 16

> An Act For the relief of Joshua H. Hutchinson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized to issue a patent to Joshua H. Hutchinson for the land embraced in his homestead entry, serial number naught twenty-two hundred and forty-five, Susanville, California, for the east half of the southwest quarter and the west half of the southeast quarter of section thirty-three, township forty-three north, range seventeen east, Mount Diablo meridian, upon submission of proof of residence upon and improvement and cultivation of said land as required by the homestead laws.


*Approved, January 27, 1913.*

# Chapter 12

> An Act To refund to the National Cartage and Warehouse Company, of New York City, New York, excess duty.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to refund to the National Cartage and Warehouse Company, of New York City, New York, the sum of ninety-five dollars and forty cents collected on two cases of majolica ware in excess of the actual duty thereon.


*Approved, January 24, 1913.*

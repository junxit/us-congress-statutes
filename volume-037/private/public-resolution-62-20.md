# Public Resolution 20

> Joint Resolution To authorize Captain John W. Gulick, United States Army, to accept a position under the Government of the Republic of Chile.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Captain John W. Gulick, Coast Artillery Corps, United States Army, be, and he is hereby, permitted to accept from the Government of the Republic of Chile the position of instructor of the Coast Artillery of the Chilean Army and the emoluments, rights, and privileges pertaining thereto.


*Approved, May 11, 1912.*

# Chapter 397

> An Act For the relief of James E. C. Covel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws and the laws governing the National Home for Disabled Volunteer Soldiers, or any branch thereof, James E. C. Covel, who was a second lieutenant of Company H, Sixteenth Regiment Iowa Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably as a member of said company and regiment on the fourteenth day of April, eighteen hundred and sixty-five Provided, That no pension shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, August 24, 1912.*

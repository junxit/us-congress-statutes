# Chapter 49

> An Act To authorize and direct the Commissioners of the District of Columbia to place the name of Annie M. Matthews on the pension roll of the police and firemen’s pension fund.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioners of the District of Columbia be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll of the police and firemen’s pension fund the name of Annie M. Matthews, mother of Hugh C. Matthews, late private, Metropolitan police force of the District of Columbia at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, August 22, 1911.*

# Chapter 283

> An Act For the relief of James S. Baer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws James S. Baer, late captain Company G, First Regiment Maryland Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a member of said company and regiment on the twenty-eighth day of September, eighteen hundred and sixty-four: Provided, That no pension shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, August 9, 1912.*

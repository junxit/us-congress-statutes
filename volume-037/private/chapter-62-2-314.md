# Chapter 314

> An Act For the relief of John M. Oak.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the accounts of John M. Oak, postmaster at Bangor, Maine, in the sum of one hundred and thirty-two thousand three hundred and eleven dollars and twelve cents, due to the United States on account of postal funds, key-deposit funds, money-order funds, postage stamps, and other stamped paper on account of losses resulting from fire April thirtieth, nineteen hundred and eleven.


*Approved, August 20, 1912.*

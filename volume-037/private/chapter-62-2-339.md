# Chapter 339

> An Act For the relief of John J. Troxell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws and the laws governing the National Home for Disabled Volunteer Soldiers, or any branch thereof, John J. Troxell shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably dis-charged from the military service of the United States as a lieutenant of Company H, Fifty-second Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, on the twenty-third day of October, eighteen hundred and sixty-three : Provided, That no pension shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, August 22, 1912.*

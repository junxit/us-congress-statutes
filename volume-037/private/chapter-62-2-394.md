# Chapter 394

> An Act For the relief of Ferdinand Tobe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, or benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Ferdinand Tobe, who was a private of Company A, Fifty-eighth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a member of said company and regiment on the thirtieth day of June, eighteen hundred and sixty three: Provided, That no pay, bounty, pension, or other emolument shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, August 24, 1912.*

# Chapter 103

> An Act For the relief of Robert Ross.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws Robert Ross, now a resident of Ryan, Kentucky, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a private of Company B, Sixty-first Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, on the twenty-eighth day of December, eighteen hundred and sixty-two: Provided, That no pension, emoluments, or allowances of any description shall accrue by reason of this Act prior to date of approval thereof.


*Approved, March 2, 1913.*

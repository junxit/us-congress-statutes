# Chapter 67

> An Act For the relief of John K. Wren.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws John K. Wren, who served in Company D, Sixty-sixth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, shall be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from said company and regiment on the sixteenth day of December, eighteen hundred and sixty-three: Provided, That no rights or benefits under any law shall accrue to the said John K. Wren prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 21, 1913.*

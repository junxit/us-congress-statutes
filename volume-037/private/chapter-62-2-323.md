# Chapter 323

> An Act For the relief of John Johnson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to John Johnson, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one thousand dollars, which sum is hereby appropriated, for permanent injuries sustained by said John Johnson at White Shoals Light Station, Lake Michigan, on June twenty-ninth, nineteen hundred and nine.


*Approved, August 21, 1912.*

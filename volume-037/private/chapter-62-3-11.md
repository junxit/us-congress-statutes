# Chapter 11

> An Act For the relief of Theodore N. Gates.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws Theodore N. Gates, late of Company K, Twenty-fifth. Regiment Massachusetts Volunteer Infantry, shall be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a member of said company and regiment on the twenty-ninth day of September, eighteen hundred and sixty-three: Provided, That no pension shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, January 24, 1913.*

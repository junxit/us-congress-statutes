# Public Resolution 34

> Joint Resolution To grant American citizenship to Eugene Prince.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Eugene Prince, son of John Henry Prince, an American citizen residing in Saint Petersburg, Russia, be, and he is hereby, unconditionally admitted to the character and privileges of a citizen of the United States.


*Approved, July 19, 1912.*

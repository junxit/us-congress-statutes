# Chapter 305

> An Act To extend the limits of the port of entry of Galveston, Texas, to include Port Bolivar, in that State.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limits of the port of entry of Galveston, Texas, be, and the same are hereby, extended to include Port Bolivar, in that State.


*Approved, August 20, 1912.*

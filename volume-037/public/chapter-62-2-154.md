# Chapter 154

> An Act To permit the American Academy in Rome to enlarge its purposes, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the American Academy in Rome incorporated by the Act of Congress approved March third, nineteen hundred and five, may by a resolution of its board of trustees enlarge its purposes so as to include the study and investigation of the archaeology, literature, and history of the classical and later periods: and that the said corporation may take and hold real and personal property to an amount not exceeding three million dollars.


*Approved, June 6, 1912.*

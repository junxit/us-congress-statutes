# Chapter 71

> An Act Providing for an increase of salary of the United States marshal for the district of Connecticut.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the passage of this Act the salary of the United States marshal for the district of Connecticut shall be at the rate of two thousand five hundred dollars a year.


*Approved, April 8, 1912.*

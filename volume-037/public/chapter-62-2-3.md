# Chapter 3

> An Act Extending the time for payment of balance due on purchase price of a certain tract of land.

*Beit enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the balance due the United States for the land purchased by the Grand Lodge of Ancient Free and Accepted Masons of the State of Oklahoma, under the provisions of the Act approved January thirty-first, nineteen hundred and ten (Public, Numbered Twenty-nine), may be paid in five equal annual installments, beginning at the time the second annual payment, under the original purchase, shall become due, with interest at five per centum per annum.


*Approved, December 21, 1911.*

# Chapter 114

> An Act Providing for publicity in taking evidence under Act of July second, eighteen hundred and ninety.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the taking of depositions of witnesses for use in any suit in equity brought by the United States under the Act entitled “An Act to protect trade and commerce against unlawful restraints and monopolies,” approved July second, eighteen hundred and ninety, and in the hearings before any examiner or special master appointed to take testimony therein, the proceedings shall be open to the public as freely as are trials in open court; and no order excluding the public from attendance on any such proceedings shall be valid or enforceable.


*Approved, March 3, 1913.*

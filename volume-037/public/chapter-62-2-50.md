# Chapter 50

> An Act Relative to members of the Female Nurse Corps serving in Alaska or at places without the limits of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the superintendent and members of the Female Nurse Corps when serving in Alaska or at places without the limits of the United States may be allowed the same privileges in regard to cumulative leaves of absence and method of computation of same as are now allowed by law to Army officers so serving.


*Approved, March 4, 1912.*

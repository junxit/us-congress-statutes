# Chapter 89

> An Act Authorizing the lease of school lands for public-park purposes by the State of Washington for a longer period than five years.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the southeast quarter and the southwest quarter, section thirty-six, township eighteen north, range ten west; and the southeast quarter, and the southeast quarter of the northeast quarter, section sixteen, township seventeen north, range nine west, in Chehalis County, granted to the State of Washington for educational purposes may, under such rules and regulations as the legislature of the said State shall prescribe, be leased for public-park purposes for such term as said legislature may fix, anything in the enabling act of said State to the contrary notwithstanding.


*Approved, April 24, 1912.*

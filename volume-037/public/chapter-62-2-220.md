# Chapter 220

> An Act Concerning unrigged vessels.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That upon affidavit by a reputable shipbuilder of the United States that an unrigged wooden vessel of the United States has been rebuilt, giving the date and place of such rebuilding, is sound and free from rotten or doted wood in structural parts, properly fastened and calked and in strength and seaworthiness as good as new, the Commissioner of Navigation shall include in the List of Merchant Vessels a notation to that effect.


*Approved, July 9, 1912.*

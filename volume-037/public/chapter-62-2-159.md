# Chapter 159

> An Act To designate Walhalla, Neche, and Saint John, in the State of North Dakota, subports of entry, and to extend the privileges of the first section of the Act of Congress approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, to said subports.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Walhalla, Neche, and Saint John, in the State of North Dakota, be, and the same are hereby, designated subports of entry in the customs collection district of North and South Dakota.

## § 2.

That the privileges of the first section of the Act approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, entitled “An Act to amend the statutes in relation to immediate transportation of dutiable goods, and for other purposes,” be, and the same are hereby, extended to the said subports.

## § 3.

That the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby authorized to discontinue the said subports of entry, or to withdraw the privileges of the first section of the Act of June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, therefrom, at any time when he shall be satisfied that the interests of commerce or of the revenue no longer require their continuance.


*Approved, June 7, 1912.*

# Chapter 119

> An Act To increase the limit of cost of the additions to the public building at Salt Lake City, Utah.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limit of cost of the additions to the public building to be erected at Salt Lake City in the State of Utah, is hereby increased from two hundred and five thousand dollars to two hundred and twenty-five thousand dollars.


*Approved, May 10, 1912.*

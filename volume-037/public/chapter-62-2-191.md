# Chapter 191

> An Act To increase the limit of cost of the United States post-office building at Huron, South Dakota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limit of cost of the United States post-office building at Huron, South Dakota, be, and the same is hereby, increased six thousand dollars, or so much thereof as may be necessary to finish the walls of said building with the stone specified in the existing contract.


*Approved, July 3, 1912.*

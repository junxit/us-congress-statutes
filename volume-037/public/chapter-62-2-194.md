# Chapter 194

> An Act To authorize the appointment of Harold Hancock Taintor to the grade of second lieutenant in the Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, to appoint Harold Hancock Taintor to the grade of second lieutenant in the United States Army, with lineal rank in accordance with his rating at the competitive examination held under the law by the War Department in January, nineteen hundred and twelve.


*Approved, July 3, 1912.*

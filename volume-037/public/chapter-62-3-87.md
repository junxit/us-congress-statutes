# Chapter 87

> An Act For the relief of Gibbes Lykes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized to nominate and, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, to appoint Gibbes Lykes, late a second lieutenant of cavalry in the United States Army, to be a second lieutenant of cavalry in the United States Army, to take rank at the foot of the list of second lieutenants of cavalry: Provided, That no back pay or allowances shall accrue by reason of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 28, 1913.*

# Chapter 48

> An Act To extend the time for constructing a bridge across the Mississippi River at Minneapolis, Minnesota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time for commencing and completing the construction of the bridge authorized by the Act of Congress approved January twenty-seventh, nineteen hundred and twelve, to be built across the Mississippi River, from the intersection of Nineteenth Avenue south and Bluff Street to the intersection of Tenth and University Avenues southeast, in the city of Minneapolis, Minnesota, is hereby extended to one year and three years, respectively, from date of approval hereof.

That the right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, February 13, 1913.*

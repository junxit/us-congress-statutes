# Chapter 159

> An Act To create a board of local inspectors, Steamboat-Inspection Service, for the port of Los Angeles, California.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section forty-four hundred and fourteen of the Revised Statutes of the United States be amended by inserting in the first paragraph thereof, after the words “New Orleans, Louisiana,” and before the words “Juneau, Alaska,” the words “Los Angeles, California”; and that the said section be further amended by inserting in the fifth paragraph thereof, after the words “Portland, Maine,” and before the words “Juneau, Alaska,” the words “Los Angeles, California.”


*Approved, March 4, 1913.*

# Chapter 171

> An Act To amend the Act approved January twenty-seventh, nineteen hundred and twelve, entitled “An Act to authorize the construction of a bridge across Caddo Lake, in Louisiana.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section one of the Act approved January twenty-seventh, nineteen hundred and twelve, entitled “An Act to authorize the construction of a bridge across Caddo Lake, in Louisiana,” be, and hereby is, amended so as to extend the time at which the construction of the bridge shall begin one year, and the time of the completion thereof be likewise extended one year.


*Approved, March 4, 1913.*

# Chapter 222

> An Act To fix the terms of the District Court for the Western District of Michigan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the terms of the District Court for the Western District of Michigan for the southern division shall be held at Grand Rapids, commencing on the first Tuesdays in March, June, October, and December; and for the northern division at Marquette, commencing on the second Tuesdays of April and September; and at Sault Sainte Marie, commencing on the second Tuesdays in January and July.


*Approved, July 9, 1912.*

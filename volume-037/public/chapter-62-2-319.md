# Chapter 319

> An Act Making a grant of lands for school purposes in block numbered thirty-one, town site of Powell, Shoshoni reclamation project, Wyoming.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to issue patent conveying block thirty-one, town site of Powell, on Shoshoni reclamation project, Wyoming, to school district numbered two, Park County, Wyoming.


*Approved, August 21, 1912.*

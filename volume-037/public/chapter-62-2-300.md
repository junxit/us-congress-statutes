# Chapter 300

> An Act To amend an Act entitled “An Act to regulate the liens of judgments and decrees of the courts of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section three of an Act entitled “An Act to regulate the liens of judgments and decrees of the courts of the United States,” approved August first, eighteen hundred and eighty-eight, be, and the same is hereby, repealed.

This Act shall take effect on and after January first, nineteen hundred and thirteen.


*Approved, August 17, 1912.*

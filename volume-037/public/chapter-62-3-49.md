# Chapter 49

> An Act To extend the time for constructing a bridge across the Mississippi River at Minneapolis, Minnesota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time for commencing anti completing the construction of the bridge authorized by the Act of Congress approved January twenty-seventh, nineteen hundred and twelve, to be built across the Mississippi River from Washington Avenue north, near its intersection with Lyndale Avenue north and Forty-second Avenue, to Thirty-seventh Avenue northeast, in the city of Minneapolis, Minnesota, is hereby extended to one year and three years, respectively, from date of approval hereof.

## § 2.

That the right to alter, amend, or repeal tins Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, February 13, 1913.*

# Chapter CL

> An Act for the Relief of Charles T. Jackson, M. D., of Boston.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, do pay unto Charles T. Jackson, M. D., of the city of Boston, the sum of four hundred and fifty dollars for making analyses of the digestive organs and their contents of the late Captain Israel S. Bunce, of the American barque Betsey Williams, for the purposes of the trial of Griffin and Lee, charged with the murder by poisoning of said Bunce, and for mileage and expenses of traveling twice to and from the city of Boston to the city of New York, as a witness at said trial on behalf of the United States.


*Approved, March 3, 1871.*

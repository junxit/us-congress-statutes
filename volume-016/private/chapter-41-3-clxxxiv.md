# Chapter CLXXXIV

> An Act granting a Pension to Leann Cox.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Leann Cox, mother of George C. Cox, late a private in company M, of the second regiment of California cavalry, upon the pension rolls, subject to the conditions and limitations of the pension laws, and to pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month from the date of the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1871.*

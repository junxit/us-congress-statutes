# Private Resolution 13

> Joint Resolution authorizing the Secretary of the Treasury to settle the Accounts of Richard R. Peebles, of Austin County, Texas, late Collector of the Port of Galveston, Texas.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the accounting officers of the treasury be, and are hereby, authorized to adjust and settle upon a proper and equitable basis the accounts of Richard R. Peebles, late collector of the port of Galveston, Texas.


*Approved, January 24, 1871.*

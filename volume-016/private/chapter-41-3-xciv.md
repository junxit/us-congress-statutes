# Chapter XCIV

> An Act granting a Pension to Mary W. Ensminger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary W. Ensminger, widow of Benjamin B. Ensminger, late a private in company B, one hundred and twentieth regiment Indiana volunteers, and to pay her a pension during her widowhood, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 27, 1871.*

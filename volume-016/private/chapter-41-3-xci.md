# Chapter XCI

> An Act for the Relief of Maria Lea.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension heretofore granted to Seth Lea, by virtue of an act of Congress entitled “An act for the relief of Seth Lea,” approved July twenty-seventh, eighteen hundred and sixty-eight, be, and the same is hereby, revived and continued to Maria Lea, his widow, at the rate of fifteen dollars per month, to commence from and after the passage of this act, and to continue during her widowhood.


*Approved, February 27, 1871.*

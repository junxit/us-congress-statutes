# Chapter CLXXXII

> An Act for the Benefit of Margaret Riddle, Widow and Executrix of George Read Riddle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be, and is hereby, appropriated to Margaret Riddle, widow and executrix of George Read Riddle, deceased, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, two thousand dollars, in full satisfaction for supplies for the army of the United States taken from the said Riddle during the late rebellion.


*Approved, June 30, 1870.*

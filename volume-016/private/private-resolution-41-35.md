# Private Resolution 35

> A Resolution for the Relief of Brigadier-General Gabriel R. Paul, of the United States Army.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Brigadier-General Gabriel R. Paul shall receive the full pay and allowance of a brigadier-general in the army of the United States, that being his rank when wounded, and on which he was retired.


*Approved, April 12, 1870.*

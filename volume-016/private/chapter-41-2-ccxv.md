# Chapter CCXV

> An Act for the Relief of John Tyler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the commissioner of patents, upon due application made to him, is authorized to extend the patent of John Tyler, of West Lebanon, New Hampshire, for improvements in water-wheels, issued to him July eight, eighteen hundred and fifty-six, and reissued in eighteen hundred and sixty-eight, and which will expire July eight, eighteen hundred and seventy, upon the same evidence and principles as if application had been made to him by said patentee in due time prior to said expiration of said patent.


*Approved, July 7, 1870.*

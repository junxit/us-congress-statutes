# Chapter XCIX

> An Act granting a Pension to Anna B. Snider.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Anna B. Snider, mother of Henry Snider, late a private in Company I, tenth Ohio cavalry, at the rate of eight dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, May 11, 1870.*

# Private Resolution 37

> Joint Resolution for the Relief of Mary A. Elliot.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States, in favor of the said Mary A. Elliot, hereby surrender, abandon, and forever release all right or claim they may have, by reason of any escheat, to lot numbered eleven, in square numbered four hundred and six, in the city of Washington, District of Columbia.


*Approved, February 17, 1871.*

# Chapter CLXXXVI

> An Act granting a Pension to Susannah Snyder.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Susannah Snyder, widow of Jacob Snyder, late first lieutenant company C, seventy-second regiment Ohio volunteers, at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1871.*

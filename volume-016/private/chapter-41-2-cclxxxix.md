# Chapter CCLXXXIX

> An Act to remove all legal and political Disabilities from George H. Bier, of Maryland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

(two thirds of each House concurring therein,) That all legal and political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States, by reason of participation in the late rebellion, be, and they hereby are, removed from George H. Bier, of Maryland.


*Approved, July 14, 1870.*

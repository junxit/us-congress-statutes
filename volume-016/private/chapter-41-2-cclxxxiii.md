# Chapter CCLXXXIII

> An Act to relieve A. E. Reynolds from civil and political Disabilities.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

(two thirds of each House concurring therein,) That all civil and political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States be, and the same are hereby, removed from A. E. Reynolds, of Corinth, in the State of Mississippi.


*Approved, July 14, 1870.*

# Chapter CCLXXXVI

> An Act repealing “An Act granting a Pension to Mrs. Ernestine Becker.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That “An act granting a pension to Mrs. Ernestine Becker,” approved February twenty-fifth, eighteen hundred and sixty-seven, be, and the same is hereby, repealed.


*Approved, July 14, 1870.*

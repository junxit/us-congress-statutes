# Chapter CLXXIII

> An Act for the Relief of Nancy S. Ledford.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Nancy S. Ledford, widow of Christopher M. Ledford, late a private in company F, second North Carolina mounted infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month, commencing from the passage of this act, with such further allowance as is provided by a law for her children under sixteen years of age; and in the event of the death or remarriage of said widow, the pension hereby granted her shall continue to her children under sixteen years of age by her said husband, until they severally attain that age.


*Approved, March 3, 1871.*

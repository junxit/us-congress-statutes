# Private Resolution 45

> Joint Resolution to enable the Court of Claims to hear and determine the Claim of E. Diekelman, a Subject of the King of Prussia.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the claim of E. Diekelman, a subject of the King of Prussia, for damages for an alleged detention of the ship “Essex” by the military authorities of the United States, at New Orleans, in the month of September, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, be, and is hereby, referred to the court of claims, for its decision in accordance with law, and to award such damages as may be just in the premises, if he may be found to be entitled to any damages.


*Approved, May 4, 1870.*

# Chapter CCCXVI

> An Act to remove the legal and political Disabilities of W. C. Whitthorne, of Maury County, Tennessee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all legal and political disabilities be, and are hereby, removed from W. C. Whitthorne, of Maury county, Tennessee.


*Approved, July 15, 1870.*

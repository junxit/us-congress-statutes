# Chapter CLXXIX

> An Act granting a Pension to Adam D. Myers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Adam D. Myers, late private company K, one hundred and sixty-sixth regiment Pennsylvania infantry, and to pay him a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1871.*

# Private Resolution 52

> A Resolution authorizing the President to strike from the Rolls of the Army the Names of certain Officers.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized to drop from the rolls of the army Lieutenant W. Barnhart, first lieutenant fourth cavalry, and W. J. Patterson, first lieutenant fifth artillery.


*Approved, May 5, 1870.*

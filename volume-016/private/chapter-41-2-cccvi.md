# Chapter CCCVI

> An Act for the Relief of Rittenhouse P. Finck.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster-General be, and is hereby, instructed to pay Rittenhouse P. Finck, out of any monies [moneys] heretofore appropriated for the use of the Post-Office Department, the sum of three hundred dollars, in full for all claims upon the United States for services as postal agent.


*Approved, July 15, 1870.*

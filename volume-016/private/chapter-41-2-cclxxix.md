# Chapter CCLXXIX

> An Act for the Relief of J. Alden Rathbun, Postmaster of Mystic Bridge, Connecticut.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid to J. Alden Rathbun, postmaster of Mystic Bridge, Connecticut, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of three hundred and sixty-five dollars, the amount of postal stamps deposited for safety in the First National Bank of Mystic Bridge, and stolen therefrom by burglars on the night of July twenty-ninth, eighteen hundred and sixty-seven, without fault or negligence on the part of said postmaster, and which he has accounted for to the proper accounting officers of the treasury.


*Approved, July 14, 1870.*

# Chapter CXLVI

> An Act for the Relief of W. B. Carpenter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officer of the treasury of the United States pay to W. B. Carpenter the pay and allowances of an assistant surgeon of the army of the United States from January one, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, to May twenty-seven, eighteen hundred and sixty-two.


*Approved, March 3, 1871.*

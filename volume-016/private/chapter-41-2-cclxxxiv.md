# Chapter CCLXXXIV

> An Act for the Relief of Charles A. Frazer, of Harrison County, Texas, and Joseph H. Sloss, of Colbert County, State of Alabama, of all political Disabilities.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

(two thirds of each House concurring therein.) That all political disabilities imposed by the provisions of the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States upon Charles A. Frazer, of Harrison county, State of Texas, and Joseph H. Sloss, of Colbert county, State of Alabama, for participation in the late rebellion, be, and the same is [are] hereby, removed.


*Approved, July 14, 1870.*

# Private Resolution 139

> Joint Resolution for the Relief of Chief Engineers George Sewell and William H. Shock, of the Navy.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the service of George Sewell and William H. Shock, as chief engineers of the United States navy, shall be considered as having commenced at the same date as that of Samuel Archbold, with whom they were examined as first assistant engineers, and passed for promotion to the grade of chief engineer.


*Approved, July 15, 1870.*

# Chapter CCCXIII

> An Act for the Relief of Sarah L. Goodrich.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Sarah L. Goodrich, widow of Sanford B. Goodrich, deceased, late band-master in the third regiment of Michigan cavalry, be allowed and paid the same bounty and additional bounty to which she would have been entitled as such widow, had the said Sanford B. Goodrich been an enlisted man in the army of the United States at the time of his death.


*Approved, July 15, 1870.*

# Chapter CCLXXVI

> An Act to authorize the Importation of a common Road Steam Locomotive free of Duty.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That G. W. Dick, of Butler county, Ohio, be, and he is hereby, authorized to import, free of duty, from Scotland, a common road steam locomotive, for experimental purposes, under such instructions as may be given by the Secretary of the Treasury in the case.


*Approved, July 14, 1870.*

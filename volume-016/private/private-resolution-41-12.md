# Private Resolution 12

> Joint Resolution providing for the Payment of James T. Elliott, late a Private of the eleventh Michigan Infantry, for Services as extra Duty Clerk.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the quartermaster-general be, and hereby is, directed to audit and allow the claim of James T. Elliott, late a private in the eleventh Michigan infantry, for pay as extra duty clerk from March third, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, to November fifteenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, at forty cents per day.


*Approved, January 24, 1871.*

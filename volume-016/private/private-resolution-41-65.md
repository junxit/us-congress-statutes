# Private Resolution 65

> A Resolution to authorise the Payment of the full Salary of Alvin P. Hovey, as Minister in Peru, without Deduction on Account of Absence from his Post.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Alvin P. Hovey, envoy extraordinary and minister plenipotentiary of the United States in Peru, who has been absent from his post as a witness in a judicial proceeding interesting to the United States, be paid the full amount of his salary, notwithstanding such absence, and the same shall be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, May 26, 1870.*

# Private Resolution 58

> A Resolution to compensate John L. Miles, late second Lieutenant of the second Indiana Battery, for one Horse lost in the Service.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, there be paid to John L. Miles, late second lieutenant second Indiana battery, one hundred and fifty dollars, for one horse lost May, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, at Fort Smith, Arkansas, by reason of an inadequate supply of forage while said Miles was acting under orders separate from said battery and from a change of position thereof.


*Approved, March 3, 1871.*

# Chapter CCCX

> An Act for the Relief of L. F. Larkin, of Weldon, North Carolina.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of one hundred and ninety-eight dollars [and] eighty-eight cents, from any sum not otherwise appropriated, be, and hereby is, allowed to L. F. Larkin, postmaster at Weldon, North Carolina, to reimburse him for losses sustained by the robbery of his office of stamps, etc.


*Approved, July 15, 1870.*

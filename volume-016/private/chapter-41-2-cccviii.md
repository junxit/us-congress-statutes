# Chapter CCCVIII

> An Act for the Relief of Francis M. Strong and Thomas Ross.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Francis M. Strong and Thomas Ross have leave to make application to the commissioner of patents for the extension of the letters-patent issued to them for improvement in platform scales, for the term of fourteen years from the fifteenth day of January, eighteen hundred and fifty-six, said letters-patent bearing date the fifteenth day of January, eighteen hundred and fifty-six, in the same manner as if the petition for said extension had been filed at least ninety days before the expiration of said patent; and that the commissioner be authorized to consider and determine said application in the same manner as if it had been filed ninety days before the expiration of said patent.


*Approved, July 15, 1870.*

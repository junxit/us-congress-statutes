# Private Resolution 19

> A Resolution amending an Act entitled “An Act for the Relief of Henry C. Noyes.”

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

Whereas the act entitled “An act for the relief of Henry C. Noyes,” approved March third, eighteen hundred and sixty-nine, contains a misrecital of the number of the bond therein referred to, whereby the said act is rendered ineffectual for the relief of the said Noyes: Therefore,

That the said act be, and hereby is, amended by striking therefrom the word “thirty-seven,” and inserting in lieu thereof the word “sixty-seven.”


*Approved, March 2, 1870.*

# Chapter CCI

> An Act granting a Pension to Henry C. Weatherby.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Henry C. Weatherby, father of Henry K. Weatherby, late a private in company I of the second Iowa cavalry, upon the pension rolls, and pay him a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month from the twenty-second day of February, eighteen hundred and sixty-four.


*Approved, July 1, 1870.*

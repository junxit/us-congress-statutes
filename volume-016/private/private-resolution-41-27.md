# Private Resolution 27

> Joint Resolution for the Relief of Francis S. McDonald.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to cause to be paid to Francis S. McDonald the full pay and emoluments of a second lieutenant of the third Minnesota volunteers from November the first, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, to June twenty-sixth, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, with travel, pay, and allowances from Snyder’s Bluff, Mississippi, to Fort Snelling, Minnesota.


*Approved, March 23, 1870.*

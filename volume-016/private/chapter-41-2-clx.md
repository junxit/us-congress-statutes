# Chapter CLX

> An Act granting a Pension to Mary Fryer, of Philadelphia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Mary Fryer, widow of John Fryer, deceased, late a seaman in the United States navy, and that she be paid at the rate of eight dollars per month, commencing from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 23, 1870.*

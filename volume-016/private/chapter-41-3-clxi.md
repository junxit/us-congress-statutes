# Chapter CLXI

> An Act granting a Pension to Zelica T. Dunlap.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Zelica T. Dunlap, widow of James E. Dunlap, late lieutenant-colonel twenty-ninth regiment Illinois volunteers, at the rate of thirty dollars per month, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1871.*

# Private Resolution 84

> A Resolution conferring upon the Court of Claims Jurisdiction to hear and determine the Claim of William B. Campbell.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That jurisdiction is hereby given to the court of claims to hear and determine the claim of William B. Campbell for the loss of an unfinished steamer, alleged to have been seized in the fall of eighteen hundred and sixty-one, by order of General Grant, while in command at Cairo, to prevent the same from falling into the hands of the rebels and being used by them to cross the river from the Kentucky to the Illinois shore, and which vessel is alleged to have been lost in consequence of that seizure. And said court is authorized to hear and determine the validity of said claim and the amount of said Campbell’s loss.


*Approved, June 23, 1870.*

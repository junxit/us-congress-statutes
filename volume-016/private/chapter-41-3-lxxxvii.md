# Chapter LXXXVII

> An Act granting a Pension to Orson R. Willard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Orson R. Willard, late a seaman on the United States steamer “Colorado,” and to grant him a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 27, 1871.*

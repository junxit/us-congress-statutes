# Chapter CLXX

> An Act granting a Pension to Ellis A. Haines.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ellis A. Haines, late a scout in second cavalry division, United States army, department West Virginia, and pay him a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1871.*

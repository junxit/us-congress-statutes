# Private Resolution 10

> A Resolution authorizing Commander Charles H. Baldwin, United States Navy, to accept a Gold Medal from the King of the Netherlands.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Commander Charles H. Baldwin, of the United States navy, be, and he is hereby, authorized to accept a gold medal from the King of the Netherlands, tendered him in appreciation of services rendered to a merchant vessel of that nation in distress.


*Approved, February 7, 1870.*

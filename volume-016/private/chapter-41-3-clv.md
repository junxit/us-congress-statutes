# Chapter CLV

> An Act increasing the Pension of Isabella Fogg.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension now paid to Mrs. Isabella Fogg, by virtue of a special act approved February twenty-eighth, eighteen hundred and sixty-seven, to thirty dollars per month, and to pay her the said sum of thirty dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1871.*

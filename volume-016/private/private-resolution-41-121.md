# Private Resolution 121

> Joint Resolution for the Relief of Jane Dean Bishop.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from the twenty-third day of May, eighteen hundred and fifty-seven, to the twenty-seventh day of March, eighteen hundred and sixty-six, Jane Dean Bishop, widow of the late Surgeon Wm. S. Bishop, United States navy, shall be allowed the difference of pay between her husband’s salary as a passed assistant surgeon and a surgeon in the navy, according to the established rates of pay during that period.


*Approved, July 14, 1870.*

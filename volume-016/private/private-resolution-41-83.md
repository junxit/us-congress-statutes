# Private Resolution 83

> A Resolution to admit free of Duty, certain printed Chromos.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That certain printed chromos of Indian paintings belonging to John M. Stanley, not exceeding twenty-one thousand copies, shall be admitted free of duty, under such rules and regulations as the Secretary of the Treasury may prescribe: Provided, That the permit so granted to John M. Stanley shall be in full settlement of all claim against the United States for the destruction by fire, of certain Indian paintings belonging to him, in January, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, at the time of the burning of the building of the Smithsonian Institution, in the city of Washington.


*Approved, June 23, 1870.*

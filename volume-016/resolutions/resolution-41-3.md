# Resolution 3

> A Resolution relative to consular Fees.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section three of chapter two hundred and thirty-three of the public acts of the Thirty-ninth Congress, approved July twenty-five, eighteen hundred and sixty-six, be, and is hereby, declared to take effect from and after January first, eighteen hundred and sixty-seven. And all fees which have been paid into the treasury in obedience to the provision of said section, and which accrued prior to said first day of January, eighteen hundred and sixty-seven, shall be refunded out of “consular receipts.”


*Approved, March 26, 1869.*

# Resolution 28

> A Resolution authorizing the Use of a naval Vessel to transport Breadstuffs to Europe.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be, and is hereby, authorized to cause to be stationed at the port of New York, and the ports of Boston and Philadelphia, if the same can be done without injury to the public service, one or more of our naval vessels, to be there held in readiness to receive on board for transportation such supplies as may be furnished by the people of the United States for the destitute and suffering people of France and Germany.


*Approved, February 10, 1871.*

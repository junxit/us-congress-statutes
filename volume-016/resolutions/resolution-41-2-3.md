# Resolution 2

> Joint Resolution to correct an Error in the Enrollment of the Law in Relation to Pay of grand and petit Jurors.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the third section of the act approved July fifteenth, eighteen hundred and-seventy, “to provide for the compensation of grand and petit jurors in the circuit and district courts of the United States, and for other purposes,” be, and the same is hereby, repealed ; the said section having been enrolled by mistake.


*Approved, December 22, 1870.*

# Resolution 134

> Joint Resolution granting condemned Cannon to the Delaware Soldiers' and Sailors' Monument Association.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War is hereby authorized to deliver to the Soldiers' and Sailors' Monument Association of Wilmington, Delaware, ten pieces of condemned brass cannon.


*Approved, July 15, 1870.*

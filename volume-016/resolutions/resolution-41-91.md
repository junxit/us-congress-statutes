# Resolution 91

> Joint Resolution Relative to the Champaign County, Ohio, Monumental Association.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be and is authorized to donate to the Champaign County (Ohio) Soldiers' Monumental Association one condemned bronze or brass gun, with which to make and place the statue of a soldier on the monument erected, or to be erected, by said association, out of any such guns which the government now has or may hereafter have, and not required for public use.


*Approved, June 30, 1870.*

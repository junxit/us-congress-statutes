# Resolution 90

> Joint Resolution in Relation to female Clerks in the internal Revenue Bureau.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby authorized to continue in employment, as now employed, the temporary female clerks in the bureau of internal revenue, for the period of fifteen days from the thirtieth of June instant, unless provision therefor shall be sooner made by law.


*Approved, June 29, 1870.*

# Resolution 128

> Joint Resolution giving condemned Ordnance to the National Asylum for Disabled Soldiers, Milwaukee, Wisconsin.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to transfer to the National Asylum for Disabled Soldiers in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, six pieces of condemned ordnance, to be placed in the soldiers' cemetery at Milwaukee.


*Approved, July 15, 1870.*

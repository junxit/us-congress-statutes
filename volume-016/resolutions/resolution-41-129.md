# Resolution 129

> Joint Resolution relative to the Pay of the Capitol Police and Watchmen.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the men discharged by the reduction of the Capitol police and watchmen force be paid for the current month of July, and that the same be paid out of the contingent fund of the Senate and House of Representatives in equal proportions.


*Approved, July 15, 1870.*

# Resolution 42

> Joint Resolution for the Relief of John E. Hagerty, of St. Louis, Missouri.

*Be it enacted [resolved] by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster-General be, and is hereby, directed to readvertise for the transportation of all mails in the city of St. Louis, now under contract with John E. Hagerty, and release him from his bond: Provided, That new service under said advertisement can be secured at a reasonable rate.


*Approved, February 24, 1871.*

# Resolution 55

> Joint Resolution granting condemned Guns for Soldiers' Monuments at Fairmount Cemetery, Newark, New Jersey.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be authorized to turn over to the managers of the Fairmount cemetery, at Newark, New Jersey, six pieces of condemned iron or bronze guns on hand, unfit for army purposes, for ornamentation of the soldiers' monuments therein.


*Approved, May 6, 1870.*

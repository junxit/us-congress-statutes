# Resolution 17

> A Resolution making San Diego, California, a Port of Delivery.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That San Diego, California, be, and is hereby, created a port of delivery in the customs collection district of San Francisco, and that the deputy collector of that port receive the same salary as other deputy collectors of the district.


*Approved, April 10, 1869.*

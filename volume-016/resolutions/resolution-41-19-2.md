# Resolution 19

> A Resolution granting the Hight of Way to the Pensacola and Barrancas Railroad Company through the naval and military Reservations near Pensacola, Florida.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the right of way is hereby granted to the Pensacola and Barrancas Railroad Company to construct their road upon and through the naval and military reservations near Pensacola, Florida, under such regulations, restrictions, and conditions as the Secretary of the Navy and the Secretary of War may prescribe.


*Approved, January 30, 1871..*

# Resolution 108

> A Resolution authorizing the Nomination and Appointment of Lieutenants Charles H. Pendleton and Richard P. Leary as Lieutenant-Commanders on the active List of the Navy.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be authorized to nominate and, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, to appoint Lieutenants Charles II. Pendleton and Richard P. Leary as lieutenant-commanders on the active list of the navy.


*Approved, July 12, 1870.*

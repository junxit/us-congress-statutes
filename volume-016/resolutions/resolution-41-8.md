# Resolution 8

> A Resolution respecting the Pay and Allowances of enlisted Men of the Army.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pay and allowances of the enlisted men of the army shall remain as now fixed by law until the thirtieth of June, eighteen hundred and seventy.


*Approved, April 6, 1869.*

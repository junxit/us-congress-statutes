# Resolution 14

> A Resolution providing for the Repair of the Inclosure of the Cemetery at Harper’s Ferry, West Virginia.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, out of the proceeds of the sale recently made by the Secretary of War of the public property at Harper’s Ferry, West Virginia, the sum of fifteen hundred dollars, or so much thereof as shall be necessary, is hereby appropriated to repair the inclosure of the cemetery at said Harper’s Ferry, to be expended for that purpose under the direction of the Secretary of War.


*Approved, January 25, 1871.*

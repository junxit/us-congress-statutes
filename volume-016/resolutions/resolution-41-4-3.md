# Resolution 4

> A Resolution to authorise the Restoration of Benjamin S. Richards to the active List of the Navy.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be, and he is, authorized, by and with the advise and consent of the Senate, to appoint Ensign Benjamin S. Richards a master on the active list of the navy, to date from the twenty-sixth day of March, eighteen hundred and sixty-nine, with the pay of that grade from that date.


*Approved, January 10, 1871.*

# Resolution 53

> A Resolution extending the Time for the Completion of the first Section of twenty Miles of the Cairo and Fulton Railroad.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in case the Cairo and Fulton Railroad Company shall complete the first section of twenty miles of said road by the twentieth day of December, eighteen hundred and seventy, and the Secretary of the Interior shall be satisfied of such completion, then the said company shall be entitled to its lands in all respects and to the same extent as it would have been had said twenty miles been completed by the twenty-eighth of April, eighteen hundred and seventy, as provided by law relating to said railroad company.


*Approved, May 6, 1870.*

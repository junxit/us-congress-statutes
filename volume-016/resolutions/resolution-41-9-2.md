# Resolution 9

> Joint Resolution for the Restoration of Captain Dominick Lynch, of the United States Navy, to the active List from the reserved List.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States of America be authorized to nominate, and by and with the advice and consent of the Senate to appoint, Captain Dominick Lynch to the active list of the navy, as a captain.


*Approved, January 20, 1871.*

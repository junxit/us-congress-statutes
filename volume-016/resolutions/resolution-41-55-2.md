# Resolution 55

> A Resolution relating to Balances of Appropriations far the Library of Congress and Botanic Garden.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all balances of appropriations under the control of the joint committee on the library, and unexpended at the expiration of the fiscal year ending June thirty, eighteen hundred and seventy, may be expended under direction of said committee, and not carried to the surplus fund, as provided by sections five and six of the act making appropriations for the legislative, executive, and judicial expenses of the government for the year ending the thirtieth of June, eighteen hundred and seventy-one, approved July twelve, eighteen hundred and seventy.


*Approved, March 3, 1871.*

# Resolution 20

> A Resolution for the Appointment of General William T. Sherman a Regent of the Smithsonian Institution.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That General William T. Sherman be, and he is hereby, appointed a member of the board of regents of the Smithsonian Institution, in the place of Richard Delafield, resigned.


*Approved, February 2, 1871.*

# Resolution 33

> A Resolution for the Relief of Lieutenant-Commander John N. Quackenbush.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be authorized to nominate, and by and with the advice and consent of the Senate to appoint, Lieutenant-Commander John N. Quackenbush to the active list of the navy, with the rank to which he may be entitled thereon.


*Approved, February 16, 1871.*

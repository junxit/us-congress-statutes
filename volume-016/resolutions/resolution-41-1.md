# Resolution 1

> A Resolution authorizing the Removal of the Public Stables, Steam Saw-Mill, and other Buildings from the Capitol Grounds.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the officer in charge of the Capitol extension be, and he is hereby, authorized to clear from the Capitol grounds the public stables, steam saw-mill, and such other buildings as are of no further use to the work on the Capitol extension.


*Approved, March 23, 1869.*

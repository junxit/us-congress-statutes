# Resolution 34

> A Resolution to authorize the President to permit William L. Hanscom, late a naval Constructor of the Navy of the United Slates, to withdraw his Resignation of that Office.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President, if he shall deem it expedient, be, and hereby is, authorized to permit William L. Hanscom, late naval constructor of the navy of the United States, to withdraw his resignation of that office, which was accepted by the Department of the Navy, January fifth, eighteen hundred and sixty-six, and to reinstate the said Hanscom in the office of naval constructor: Provided, however, That no pay, emoluments, or compensation in any form, shall be allowed or paid under authority of this resolution, except such as may become due under the laws for services which may be rendered from and after the time when the said Hanscom shall have been reinstated by order of the President.


*Approved, February 16, 1871.*

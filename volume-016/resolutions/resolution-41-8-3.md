# Resolution 8

> Joint Resolution granting condemned Ordnance to the Seventh Regiment Monument Association of Ohio.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War is hereby authorized to deliver to the Seventh Regiment Monument Association of Ohio four pieces of condemned iron cannon, to be used in ornamenting the grounds around a monument now being erected at Cleveland, Ohio, in memory of the officers and soldiers of the seventh regiment of Ohio volunteer infantry, who lost their lives in the late war.


*Approved, January 18, 1871.*

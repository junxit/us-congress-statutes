# Resolution 36

> A Resolution in Relation to the Printing, for the Use of the Department of State, of additional Copies of Documents emanating from that Department.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That hereafter, in addition to the number of copies of documents emanating from the Department of State which may be ordered to be printed by either house of Congress, it shall be the duty of the congressional printer to cause to be printed for the use of that department five hundred copies of every such document, when requested to do so by the Secretary of State : Provided, That the expense of the same shall in no single case exceed the sum of five hundred dollars.


*Approved, April 16, 1870.*

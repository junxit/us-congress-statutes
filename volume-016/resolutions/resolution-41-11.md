# Resolution 11

> Joint Resolution concerning Vacancies in the Adjutant-General’s Department.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the vacancies existing in the adjutant-general’s department at the time of the passage of the act approved March third, eighteen hundred and sixty-nine, “making appropriations for the support of the army for the year ending June thirtieth, eighteen hundred and seventy, and for other purposes,” be, and the same are hereby, exempted from the operation of said act.


*Approved, April 10, 1869.*

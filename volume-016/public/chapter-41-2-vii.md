# Chapter VII

> An Act repealing so much of the Act of August eighth, one thousand eight hundred and forty-six, as declared the Des Moines River, in the then Territory of Iowa, a public Highway.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That so much of the act of August eighth, one thousand eight hundred and forty-six, entitled “An act granting certain lands to the Territory of Iowa to aid in the improve-ment of the navigation of the Des Moines river, in said Territory," as makes said river a public highway be, and the same is hereby, repealed.


*Approved, January 20, 1870.*

# Chapter XCII

> An Act in Relation to the Iowa River in the State of Iowa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That so much of the Iowa river, in the State of Iowa, as lies north of the town of Wapello, be, and the same is hereby, declared not a navigable river or public highway.


*Approved, May 6, 1870.*

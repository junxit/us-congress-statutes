# Chapter CXXXIV

> An Act to restore Robert Boyd, junior, to the active List of the Navy of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be authorized to nominate, and, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, to appoint Robert Boyd, junior, now on the retired list, as a commander on the active list of the navy.


*Approved, March 3, 1871.*

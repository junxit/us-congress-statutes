# Chapter XXXIX

> An Act to authorize the Removal of the Custom-House from St. Mark's to Cedar Keys, Florida.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the port of entry of the collection district of St. Mark's, in the State of Florida, be, and the same is hereby, removed from the town of St. Mark's to the town of Cedar Keys in said district, and the said town of St. Mark's is hereby made and declared to be a port of delivery in said district.


*Approved, February 8, 1871.*

# Chapter CXL

> An Act to amend an Act entitled “An Act firing the Compensation for the Bailiffs and Criers of the Courts of the District of Columbia,” approved February twenty-two, eighteen hundred and sixty-seven.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the passage of this act the marshal shall pay to each bailiff and crier who shall be required to attend upon the district, circuit, [and] criminal courts of the District of Columbia, three dollars and fifty cents for each day such bailiff or crier shall attend upon either of said courts.


*Approved, June 21, 1870.*

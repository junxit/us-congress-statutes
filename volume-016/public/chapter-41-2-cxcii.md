# Chapter CXCII

> An Act to repeal certain Sections of Acts passed by the territorial Legislature of Wyoming.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the following sections of acts, passed by the legislature of the Territory of Wyoming, to wit: The fourth and sixth sections of an act entitled “An act providing for the collection of taxes heretofore assessed and levied in this Territory” ; also sections forty-nine to fifty-nine, both inclusive, of an act entitled “An act to create and regulate corporations,” be, and the same are hereby, disapproved, repealed, and declared null and void.


*Approved, July 1, 1870.*

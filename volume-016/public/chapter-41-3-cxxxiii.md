# Chapter CXXXIII

> An Act to authorize the Restoration of James Belger to the Rank of Major and Quartermaster in the Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Home of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized to restore James Belger to the rank of major and quartermaster in the army, to date from the day of his restoration to his said rank under this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1871.*

# Chapter XX

> An Act to declare and fix the Status of Judge Advocates of the Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the number of judge advocates of the army be, and the same is hereby, fixed at eight, and the President is hereby authorized, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, to fill all vacancies which have occurred or may hereafter occur therein.


*Approved, April 10, 1869.*

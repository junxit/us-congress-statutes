# Chapter CCLXV

> An Act to amend Section four of the Act of March thirty-one, eighteen hundred and sixty-eight.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there shall not be assessed or collected, under or by virtue of section four of an act approved March thirty-one, eighteen hundred and sixty-eight, entitled “An act to exempt certain manufacturers from internal tax, and for other purposes,” any internal tax upon pork-packers, lard-renderers, or those engaged in smoking hams, curing meats, or others known as in the provision trade, as manufacturers within the meaning of the said section; and if any such tax shall have been assessed, but not collected, the same is hereby remitted.


*Approved, July 14, 1870.*

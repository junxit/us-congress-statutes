# Chapter LVIII

> An Act to repeal an Act entitled “An Act to confirm Title to certain Lands in Illinois.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the description of the lands mentioned in said act be so amended as to read township two north, instead of “town[ship] one north.”


*Approved, February 18, 1871.*

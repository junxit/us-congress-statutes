# Chapter CXVII

> An Act to repeal the Preamble of an Act in Relation to the National Theological institute, approved April twenty-second, eighteen hundred and seventy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the preamble to an act entitled “An act declaring the construction of ‘An act to incorporate the National Theological Institute,’ approved May tenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-six, and also ‘An act to amend an act entitled ‘An act to incorporate the National Theological Institute, and to define and extend the powers of the same,’’” approved April twenty-second, eighteen hundred and seventy, be, and the same is hereby, repealed.


*Approved, June 1, 1870.*

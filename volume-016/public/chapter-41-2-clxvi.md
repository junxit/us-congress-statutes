# Chapter CLXVI

> An Act declaring the Bridge between Philadelphia and Camden a Post-Route.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Bouse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the bridge authorized to be constructed by “An act giving the consent of the United States to the erection of a bridge across the Delaware river, between Philadelphia and Camden,” approved April sixth, eighteen hundred and seventy, when erected, shall be, and is hereby, declared to be a post-route.


*Approved, June 28, 1870.*

# Chapter XXI

> An Act to repeal an Act of the Legislature of New Mexico imposing a Capitation Tax on bovine Cattle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the act of the legislature of the Territory of New Mexico, of February third, eighteen hundred and sixty-nine, and all other laws and parts of laws of said legislature imposing a capitation tax on bovine cattle introduced into the said Territory from other Territories or States, or the Republic of Mexico, be, and the same are hereby, disapproved and repealed.


*Approved, April 10, 1869.*

# Chapter LXXII

> An Act supplementary to an Act to establish the Department of Justice.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there shall be in the Department of Justice an additional assistant of the Attorney-General, who shall be appointed by the President, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, and shall hold his office until a successor shall be duly qualified, and who shall be paid the salary of other assistants of the Attorney-General.


*Approved, February 25, 1871.*

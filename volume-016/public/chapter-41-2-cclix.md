# Chapter CCLIX

> An Act to amend existing Laws relating to internal Revenue.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That on and after the passage of this act, in addition to the articles now exempt by law, the articles and products hereinafter enumerated shall be exempt from internal tax, namely: Canned and preserved fish, shell-fish, meats, fruits, and vegetables.

Ante, p. 257.


*Approved, July 14, 1870.*

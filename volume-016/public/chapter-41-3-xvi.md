# Chapter XVI

> An Act relating to the Printing of the annual Report of the Secretary of War.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That it shall be the duty of the congressional printer, in place of the twenty-five hundred copies of the abridgment of the annual report of the Secretary of War ordered by section one of the act of July twenty-seventh, eighteen hundred and sixty-six, to cause to be printed and bound for the use of the War Department eight hundred full copies of the last annual report and accompanying documents.


*Approved, January 10, 1871.*

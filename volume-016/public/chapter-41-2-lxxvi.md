# Chapter LXXVI

> An Act to provide, for the Survey of the Fort Gratiot military Reservation, in the State of Michigan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be, and is hereby, appropriated from any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of three thousand dollars, to complete the survey and subdivision of the Fort Gratiot military reservation in pursuance of law ; and that said money be returned to the treasury from the proceeds of the sale of any part of said military reservation.


*Approved, May 4, 1870.*

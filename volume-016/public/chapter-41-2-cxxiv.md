# Chapter CXXIV

> An Act to fix the Salary of the Bailiff of the Court of Claims.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the passage of this act the annual salary of the bailiff of the court of claims shall be at the rate of fifteen hundred dollars per annum, to be paid as no provided by law.


*Approved, June 7, 1870.*

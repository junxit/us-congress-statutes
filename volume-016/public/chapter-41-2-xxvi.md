# Chapter XXVI

> An Act to authorize the Secretary of War to place at the Disposal of the Soldiers’ Monument Committee of Rock Island County, Illinois, certain condemned Ordnance.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place at the disposal of the soldiers’ monument committee at Rock Island, Illinois, twelve pieces of condemned ordnance from the arsenal of Rock Island, for the purpose of completing the soldiers’ monument and appurtenances at said city of Rock Island.


*Approved, March 9, 1870.*

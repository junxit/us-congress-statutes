# Chapter CLXXI

> An Act to provide for changing the Boundaries of Land Districts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized and empowered to change and reestablish the boundaries of land districts whenever, in his opinion, the public interests will be subserved thereby, without authority to increase the number of land offices or land districts.


*Approved, June 29, 1870.*

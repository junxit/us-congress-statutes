# Chapter LXXXI

> An Act legalizing certain Locations of agricultural College Scrip therein designated.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That all locations of agricultural college scrip allowed prior to December first, eighteen hundred and sixty seven, at the several land offices in the State of Wisconsin, in excess of the maximum quantity authorized by the act entitled “An act donating public lands to the several States and Territories which may provide colleges for the benefit of agriculture and the mechanic arts,” approved July second, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, be and the same are hereby, legalized ; and the commissioner of the general land office is authorized to issue patents upon such locations: Provided, That the same shall be in all other respects legal and valid.


*Approved, May 5, 1870.*

# Chapter CLII

> An Act to amend the Act of the twenty-five December, eighteen hundred and sixty-one, entitled “An Act to further promote the Efficiency of the Navy.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first section of the act of the twenty-first of December, anno Domini eighteen hundred and sixty-one, entitled “An act to further promote the efficiency of the navy,” shall not be so construed as to retire any officer under the age of sixty-two years, and whose name shall not have been borne upon the navy register for a period of forty-five years after he had arrived at the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, June 25, 1864.*

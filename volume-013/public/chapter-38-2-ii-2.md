# Chapter II

> An Act authorizing the Holding of a special Session of the United States District Court for the District of Indiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a special session of the United States district court for the district of Indiana shall be holden at the usual place of holding said court on the first Tuesday in January, eighteen hundred and sixty-five.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That all suits and proceedings of a civil or criminal nature, now pending in or returnable to said court, shall be proceeded in, heard, tried, and determined by said court, at said special session, in the same manner as at a regular term of said court, and the judge thereof is hereby empowered to order the empaneling of a petit and grand jury for said session.


*Approved, December 20, 1864.*

# Chapter LXXVI

> An Act to establish the Office of Solicitor and Naval Judge-Advocate.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized to appoint, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, for service during the rebellion and one year thereafter, at, officer in the Navy Department, to be called the “Solicitor and Naval Judge-Advocate General,” at an annual salary of three thousand live hundred dollars, and that until the close of the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, eighteen hundred and sixty-six, the salary herein provided for shall be paid from any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the fees for record in naval courts-martial shall not in any one case exceed the sum of two hundred dollars.


*Approved, March 2, 1865.*

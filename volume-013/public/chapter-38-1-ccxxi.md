# Chapter CCXXI

> An Act in Relation to the Sale of Reservations of the Public Lands.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That whenever any reservation of public lands shall be brought into market under existing laws, it shall be lawful for the commissioner of the general land-office to fix a minimum price, not less than one dollar and twenty-five cents per acre, below which such lands shall not be disposed of.


*Approved, July 2, 1864.*

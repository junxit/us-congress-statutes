# Chapter LXV

> An Act to change the Name of the District and Port of Presque Isle to the District and Port of Erie.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the district of Presque Isle, in the state of Pennsylvania, shall hereafter be known as the district of Erie, and the port of Presque Isle shall hereafter be known as the port, of Erie.


*Approved, April 21, 1864.*

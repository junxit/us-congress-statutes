# Chapter CC

> An Act repealing so much of “An Act to supply Deficiencies in the Appropriations for the Service of the Fiscal Year ending the thirtieth of June, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, and for other Purposes,” approved March fourteen, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, as appropriates twenty-five thousand Dollars for erecting a Naval Hospital al Kittery, Maine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That so much of “An act to supply deficiencies in the appropriations for the service of the fiscal year ending the thirtieth of June, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, and for other purposes,” approved, March fourteen, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, as appropriates “for erecting naval hospital at Kittery, Maine, twenty-five thousand dollars,” be, and the same is hereby, repealed.


*Approved, July 1, 1864.*

# Chapter CXXVIII

> An Act to amend an Act entitled “An Act to extend the Time for the Withdrawal of Goods from Public Stores and Bonded Warehouses, and for other Purposes,” approved twenty-ninth February, eighteen hundred and sixty-four.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all goods, wares, and merchandise, in public stores or bonded warehouses, on which the duties are unpaid, and which shall have been in bond for more than one year and less than three years, may be entered for consumption and the bonds cancelled at any time before the first day of September next, on payment of duties and charges according to the laws in force at the time the goods shall be withdrawn.


*Approved, June 17, 1864.*

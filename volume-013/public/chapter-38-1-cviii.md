# Chapter CVIII

> An Act to repeal the first Section of the Joint Resolution relative to the Transfer of Persons in the Military Service to the Naval Service, approved February twenty-four, eighteen hundred and sixty-four.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first section of the joint resolution entitled a “Joint resolution relative to the transfer of persons in the military service to the naval service,” approved February twenty-four, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, be, and the same is hereby, repealed.


*Approved, June 3, 1864.*

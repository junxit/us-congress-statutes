# Chapter LXXI

> An Act concerning the Collection District of Salem and Beverly, in Massachusetts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the office of naval officer for district of Salem and Beverly, in the State of Massachusetts, be, and the same is hereby, abolished.

## § 2.

And be it further encoded, That the office of surveyor for said district, to reside at Beverly, be, and the same is hereby, abolished.

## § 3.

And be it further enacted, That the salary of surveyor of said district shall hereafter be four hundred dollars per annum.


*Approved, February 28, 1865.*

# Chapter LXXXV

> An Act to provide for a Chief of Staff to the Lieutenant-General commanding die Armies of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States may, by and with the advice and consent of the advice and consent of the Senate, appoint a chief of staff to the lieutenant-general commanding the armies of the United States, who shall have the rank, pay, and allowances of abrigadier-general in the United States army.


*Approved, March 3, 1865.*

# Chapter CI

> An Act to provide for the Payment of the Claims of Peruvian Citizens, under the Convention between the United States and Peru, of the twelfth of January, eighteen hundred and sixty-three.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purpose of discharging the obligations of the United States, under the convention with Peru, of the twelfth of January last, there he paid to Stephen G. Montano, or to his legal representatives, in the current money of the United States, the sum of forty-one thousand seven hundred and eighty-two dollars and thirty-eight cents; and to Juan del Carmen Vergel, or his legal representatives, the sum of one thousand one hundred and seventy dollars, in the silver money of the United States, or its equivalent, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 1, 1864.*

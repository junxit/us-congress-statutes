# Chapter XXXIV

> An Act in Addition to an Act to amend the Laws relating to the Post-Office Department, approved March three, eighteen hundred and sixty-three.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the franking privilege of the President and the Vice-President of the United States shall extend to and cover all mail matter sent from, or directed to, either of them.


*Approved, March 16, 1864.*

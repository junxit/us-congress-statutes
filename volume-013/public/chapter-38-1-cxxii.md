# Chapter CXXII

> An Act to abolish the Collection Districts of Port Orford and Cape Perpetua, in the State of Oregon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the collection districts of Cape Perpetua and Port Orford, heretofore established by law, are hereby abolished, and the same attached to the collection district of Oregon.


*Approved, June 11, 1864.*

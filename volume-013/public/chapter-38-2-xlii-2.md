# Chapter XLII

> An Act to enlarge the Port of Entry and Delivery for the District of Philadelphia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the port of entry and delivery for the district of Philadelphia shall be bounded on the river Delaware by Frankford Creek, on the north, and Broad Street, on the south.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That all acts or parts of acts conflicting with the provisions of this act be, and the same are hereby, repealed.


*Approved, February 20, 1865.*

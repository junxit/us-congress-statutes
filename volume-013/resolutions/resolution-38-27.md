# Resolution 27

> A Resolution to encourage the Employment of disabled and discharged Soldiers.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That persons honorably discharged from the military or naval service by reason of disability resulting from wounds or sickness incurred in the line of duty, should be preferred for appointments to civil offices, provided they shall be found to possess the business capacity necessary for the proper discharge of the duties of such offices.

## § 2.

And be it further resolved, That, in grateful recognition of the services, sacrifices, and sufferings of persons honorably discharged from the military and naval service of the country, by reason of wounds, disease, or the expiration of terms of enlistment, it is respectfully recommended to bankers, merchants, manufacturers, mechanics, farmers, and persons engaged in industrial pursuits, to give them the preference for appointments to remunerative situation[s] and employments.


*Approved, March 3, 1865.*

# Resolution

> A Resolution explanatory of an Act entitled “An Act to increase the Pay of Soldiers in the United States Army, and far other Purposes,” approved, June twentieth, eighteen hundred and sixty-four.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the word “musicians” in the first section of an act entitled “An act to increase the pay of soldiers in the United States army, and for other purposes,” approved June twentieth, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, is not to be construed to include musicians, (other than leaders,) employed as members of brigade and regimental bands; but such members of bands shall be paid as heretofore, one fourth of the members of each band thirty-four dollars per month, one fourth of them twenty dollars per month, and the remaining half of them seventeen dollars per month.


*Approved, July 2, 1864.*

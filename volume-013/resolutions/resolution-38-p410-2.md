# Resolution

> A Resolution for the Relief of Clerks at the Kittery and Philaddphia Navy Yards.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That in the settlement of the accounts of the Kittery and Philadelphia navy yards, the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, authorized to allow such sums as have already been appropriated by congress for the pay of clerks at said yards, from October, eighteen hundred and fifty-seven, to July, eighteen hundred and fifty-nine.


*Approved, June 25, 1864.*

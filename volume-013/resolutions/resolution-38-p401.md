# Resolution

> A Resolution expressive of the Thanks of Congress to Major-General Nathaniel P. Ranks and the Officers and Soldiers wider his Command at Port Hudson.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the thanks of congress are hereby tendered to Major-General Nathaniel P. Banks and the officers and soldiers under bis command for the skill, courage, and endurance which ’compelled the surrender of Port Hudson, and thus removed the last obstruction to the free navigation of the Mississippi River.


*Approved, January 28, 1864.*

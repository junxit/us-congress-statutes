# Resolution

> A Resolution relating to the Publication of the Decisions of the Supreme Court of the United States for December Term, eighteen hundred and sixty-three.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That so much of the act of congress, approved August twenty-nine, eighteen hundred and forty-two, as provides that the reporter of the decisions of the supreme court shall not be paid the compensation provided by the said act unless he print and publish the decisions within six months after such decisions shall be made, shall not be held to apply to the decisions of December term, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, the present reporter of the said court not having been appointed until near the close of the term: Provided, however, That such decisions be published within one year from the passage of this resolution.


*Approved, April 22, 1864.*

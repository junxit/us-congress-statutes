# Resolution

> A Resolution to compensate the Sailors on the Gunboat “Baron de Kalb” for Loss of Clothing.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, authorized, in settling the accounts of the petty officers, seamen, sailors, and others of the crew of the United States gunboat “Baron de Kalb,” to allow to each a sum not exceeding fifty dollars as a remuneration for the damage they may have sustained in the loss of their clothing by the destruction of said vessel, to be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 7, 1864.*

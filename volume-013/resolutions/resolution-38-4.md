# Resolution 4

> A Resolution tendering the Thanks of Congress to Lieutenant William B. Cushing, of the United States Navy, and to the Officers and Men who assisted him in his gallant and perilous Achievement in destroying the rebel Steamer “Albemarle,” in Compliance with the President’s Recommendation to Congress of the fifth of December, eighteen hundred [and] sixty-four.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the thanks of congress are due, and are hereby tendered, to Lieutenant William B. Cushing, of the United States navy, and to the officers and men under his command, for the skill and gallantry exhibited by them in the destruction of the rebel iron-clad steamer “Albemarle,” at Plymouth, North Carolina, on the night of the twenty-seventh of October, eighteen hundred and sixty-four.


*Approved, December 20, 1864.*

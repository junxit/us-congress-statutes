# Resolution

> Joint Resolution granting certain Privileges to the City of Des Moines, in the State of Iowa.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States hereby relinquish to the city of Des Moines, in the State of Iowa, a municipal corporation established under the laws of said state, all their right and interest in the coal-beds underlying the river Des Moines, within the limits of said city: Provided, That no disposition or use thereof shall be made which shall obstruct the free navigation of said river; nor shall any one grant of the privilege of mining the same extend for a longer period than ten years.


*Approved, June 15, 1864.*

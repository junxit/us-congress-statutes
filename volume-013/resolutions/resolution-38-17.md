# Resolution 17

> A Resolution to extend the Time for the Reversion to the United States of the Lands granted by Congress to aid in the Construction of a Railroad from Pare Marquette to Flint, and for the Completion of said Road.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time specified in the fourth section of the act of congress approved June three, eighteen hundred and fifty-six, entitled “An act making a grant of alternate sections of the public lands to the State of Michigan to aid in the construction of certain railroads in said state, and for other purposes,” for the reversion to the United States of the lands granted by said act to aid in the construction of a railroad from Pere Marquette to Flint, and for the completion of said road, be, and the same is hereby, extended for the term of five years.


*Approved, February 17, 1865.*

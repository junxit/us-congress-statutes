# Resolution 35

> A Resolution transferring Maps and other Documents relating to the Surveys of the Pacific Railroad to the Department of the Interior.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all maps, profiles, and other drawings, together with estimates and reports connected with explorations and surveys for the Pacific Railroad, made under the authority of the government, and all other information upon the subject of said road in the possession of any department of the government, be transferred to the Department of the Interior; and that the Secretary of the Interior be authorized to furnish copies of the same, free of charge, to the Union Pacific Railroad Company, so far as they may be useful in aiding said company in determining the proper route for said road.


*Approved, March 3, 1865.*

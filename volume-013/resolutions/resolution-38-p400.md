# Resolution

> Joint Resolution to provide for the Printing annually of the Report of the commissioner of Internal Revenue.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That it shall be the duty of the superintendent of the public printing annually to print, for the use of the commissioner of internal revenue, one thousand copies of his report to the Secretary of the Treasury.


*Approved, January 13, 1864.*

# Resolution

> Joint Resolution to increase temporarily the Duties on Imports.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That until the end of sixty days from the passage of this resolution, fifty per cent of the rates of duties and imposts now imposed by law on all goods, wares, merchandise, and articles imported, shall be added to the present duties and imposts now charged on the importation of such articles: Provided, That printing paper unsized, used for books and newspapers exclusively, shall be exempt from the operation of this resolution.


*Approved, April 29, 1864.*

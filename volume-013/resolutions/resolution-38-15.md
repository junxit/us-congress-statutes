# Resolution 15

> A Resolution providing for the Compilation of a Congressional Directory at each Session.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Congressional Directory be compiled under the direction of the joint committee on public printing, and published by the superintendent of public printing—the first edition for each session to be ready for distribution within one week after the commencement thereof.


*Approved, February 14, 1865.*

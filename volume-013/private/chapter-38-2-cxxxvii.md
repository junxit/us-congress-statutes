# Chapter CXXXVII

> An Act for the Relief of Jean M. Lander, Widow of F. W. Lander, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to audit and settle the account of Brigadier-General F. W. Lander, deceased, for services rendered and expenses incurred by him in making a reconnoissance for a railroad from Puget’s Sound to the Mississippi River, in eighteen hundred and fifty-four, and the amount the said Secretary shall find to be justly due shall be paid to the widow of the deceased, to wit, Jean M. Lander, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated: Provided, The same shall not exceed the sum of four thousand seven hundred and fifty dollars, and it shall be in full consideration for said services and expenses.


*Approved, March 3, 1865.*

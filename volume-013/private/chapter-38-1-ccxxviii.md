# Chapter CCXXVIII

> An Act for the Relief of the Widow of C. A. Haun.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to place the name of Mrs. C. A. Haun, of the county of Greene and State of Tennessee, on the pension roll at the rate of eight dollars per month, to commence on the eleventh day of December, eighteen hundred and sixty-one, and to continue during her widowhood.


*Approved, July 2, 1864.*

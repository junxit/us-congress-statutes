# Chapter LXII

> An Act for the Relief of Mary Shircliff.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Mary Shircliff, widow of John Shircliff, on the pension roll, and pay her a pension, at the rate of eight dollars per month, during her widowhood, from the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 25, 1865.*

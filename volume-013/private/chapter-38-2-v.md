# Chapter V

> An Act for the Relief of George W. Murray.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the commissioner of pensions be, and he is hereby, directed to pay to George W. Murray the pension to which he was entitled by the act of congress approved on the third of March, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-seven, entitled “An act for the more equitable administration of the navy pension fund.”


*Approved, December 20, 1864.*

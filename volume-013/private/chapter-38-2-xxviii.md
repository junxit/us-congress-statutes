# Chapter XXVIII

> An Act for the Relief of Mary Scales Accardi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, directed to place the name of Mary Scales Accardi, the widow of Salvador Accardi, upon the roll of widows of invalid pensioners, and that the said Mary Scales Accardi be paid a pension, at the rate of six dollars per month, commencing July first, eighteen hundred and sixty-two.


*Approved, January 30, 1865.*

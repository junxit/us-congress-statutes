# Private Resolution 40

> A Resolution for the Relief of Garrett R. Barry, a Paymaster in the United States Navy.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Paymaster Garrett R. Barry, United States navy, be, and he is hereby, released from his liability as surety of John De Bree, formerly paymaster in the navy of the United States.


*Approved, March 3, 1865.*

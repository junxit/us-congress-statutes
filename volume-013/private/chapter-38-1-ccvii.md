# Chapter CCVII

> An Act for the Relief of George F. Nesbitt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That George F. Nesbitt be, and he is hereby, authorized and empowered to put an end to his contract with the United States, entered into with the Postmaster-General on the twenty-seventh day of March, anno Domini eighteen hundred and sixty-two, for furnishing the Post-Office Department with stamped envelopes and newspaper wrappers, on giving to the Postmaster-General sixty days’ notice in writing.


*Approved, July 1, 1864.*

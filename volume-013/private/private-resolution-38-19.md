# Private Resolution 19

> Joint Resolution authorizing the Settlement of the Accounts of J. N. Carpenter, a Paymaster in the United States Navy.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, authorized, in settling the accounts of J. N. Carpenter, paymaster in the U. S. navy, to allow and credit him with the sum of two hundred dollars, the same being the value of articles of clothing stolen from the U. S. sloop “Saratoga,” after said vessel had been put out of commission at Philadelphia, in January, eighteen hundred and sixty-three.


*Approved, March 16, 1864.*

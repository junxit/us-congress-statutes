# Private Resolution 44

> A Joint Resolution for the Relief of Rev. W. B. Matchett.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the paymaster-general of the United States be authorized and directed to adjust and pay to W. B. Matchett the amount due him as chapl[a]in of the tenth regiment of New York volunteers, from the time up to which he was last paid to the time said regiment was mustered out of service.


*Approved, June 20, 1864.*

# Private Resolution 34

> Joint Resolution referring the Claim of J. H. Clark & Co. to the Court of Claims.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the claim of J. H. Clark & Co., heretofore pending before congress, be, and the same hereby is, referred to the court of claims.


*Approved, May 26, 1864.*

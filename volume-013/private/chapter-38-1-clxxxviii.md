# Chapter CLXXXVIII

> An Act for the Relief of the Estate of B. F. Kendall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the Treasury Department be, and they are hereby, authorized to allow, in the settlement of the accounts of B. F. Kendall, deceased, late superintendent of Indian affairs for Washington Territory, the sum of two thousand one hundred and eight dollars and thirty cents, the same being the amount shown to have been disbursed by him in his lifetime, and for which no vouchers were to be found.


*Approved, June 30, 1864.*

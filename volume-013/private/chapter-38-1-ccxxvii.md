# Chapter CCXXVII

> An Act for the Relief of Richard G. Murphy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Richard G. Murphy, of Minnesota, formerly Indian agent, for services and expenses in the removal of Sioux Indians, in the year one thousand eight hundred and fifty-three, the sum of six hundred dollars.


*Approved, July 2, 1864.*

# Chapter CXXXVI

> An Act granting a Pension to Ezekiel Darling.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to place the name of Ezekiel Darling on the pension rolls, at the rate of four dollars per month, during his natural life, said pension to commence from the passage of the bill.


*Approved, March 3, 1865.*

# Private Resolution 39

> A Resolution authorizing the Acceptance of a Sword of Honor from the Government of Great Britain, by Captain Henry S. Stellwagen, of the United States Navy.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Captain Henry S. Stellwagen, of the navy of the United States, be, and he hereby is, authorized to accept the sword of honor recently presented to him by the government of Great Britain as a mark of their gratitude for the assistance rendered by him, while in command of the frigate Constellation, to the British brigantine Mersey when in a disabled condition.


*Approved, March 3, 1865.*

# Chapter XV

> An Act for the Relief of George Mowry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to pay to George Mowry the sum of seventy-two dollars and twelve cents, in full payment for conveying two prisoners from Somerset jail, in Pennsylvania, to Pittsburg, in the year eighteen hundred and forty-one, by order of the United States deputy marshal for the western district of Pennsylvania.


*Approved, January 18, 1865.*

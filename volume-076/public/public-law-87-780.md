# Public Law 87–780

> JOINT RESOLUTION Providing for the establishment of an annual National School Lunch Week.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the seven-day period beginning on the second Sunday of October in each year is hereby designated as National School Lunch Week, and the President is requested to issue annually a proclamation calling on the people of the United States to observe such week with appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Approved October 9, 1962.*

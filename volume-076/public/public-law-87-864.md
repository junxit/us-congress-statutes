# Public Law 87–864

> JOINT RESOLUTION Fixing the time of assembly of the Eighty-eighth Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Eighty-eighth Congress shall assemble at noon on Wednesday, January 9,


*Approved October 23, 1962.*

# Public Law 87–746

> AN ACT To amend the Act providing for the economic and social development in the Ryukyu Islands.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4 of the Act of July 12, 1960 (Public Law 86–629; 74 Stat. 462), is amended by striking out the figure “$6,000,000” and inserting the figure “$12,000,000” in place thereof.


*Approved October 4, 1962.*

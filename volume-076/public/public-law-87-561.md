# Public Law 87–561

> AN ACT To postpone by three months the date on or before which the Securities and Exchange Commission shall report to the Congress the results of its study and investigation pursuant to section 19(d) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second sentence of subsection (d) of section 19 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (15 U.S.C. 78s(d)) is amended by striking out “January 3, 1963” and inserting “April 3, 1963” in lieu thereof. The last sentence of such subsection is amended by striking out “$750,000” and inserting “$950,000” in lieu thereof.


*Approved July 27, 1962.*

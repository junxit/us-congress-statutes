# Public Law 87–471

> AN ACT To change the name of Whitman National Monument to Whitman Mission National Historic Site.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That effective January 1, 1963, the Whitman National Monument, established pursuant to the Act of June 29, 1936 (49 Stat. 2028; 16 U.S.C. 433k–433m), shall be known as the Whitman Mission National Historic Site.


*Approved May 31, 1962.*

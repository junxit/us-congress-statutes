# Public Law 87–544

> AN ACT To change the classes of persons eligible to receive payments of benefits withheld during the lifetime of deceased veterans while being furnished hospital or domiciliary care.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3203 (a)(2)(A), title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking out the words “third, if no spouse or child” and all that follows down through “brothers and sisters in equal parts” and inserting in lieu thereof the following: “third, if no spouse or child, then to the dependent parents in equal parts”.

## § 2.

The amendment made by this Act shall also apply to cases in which pension eligibility is subject to the provisions of section 9(b) of the Veterans’ Pension Act of 1959.


*Approved July 25, 1962.*

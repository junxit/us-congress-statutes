# Public Law 87–405

> AN ACT To authorize an additional Assistant Secretary of Commerce.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there shall be in the Department of Commerce, in addition to the Assistant Secretaries now provided by law, one additional Assistant Secretary of Commerce who shall be appointed by the President by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, snail receive compensation at the rate prescribed by law for Assistant Secretaries of Commerce, and shall perform such duties as the Secretary of Commerce shall prescribe.


*Approved February 16, 1962.*

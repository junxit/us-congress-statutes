# Public Law 87–854

> AN ACT To amend the Tariff Act of 1930 to permit the designation of certain contract carriers as carriers of bonded merchandise.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 551 of the Tariff Act of 1930, as amended (19 U.S.C., sec. 1551), is amended to read as follows:

> ## § 551. BONDING OF CARRIERS.
>
> “Under such regulations and subject to such terms and conditions as the Secretary of the Treasury shall prescribe— upon application, may, in the discretion of the Secretary, be designated as a carrier of bonded merchandise for the final release of which from customs custody a permit has not been issued.”
>
> - **(1)** any common carrier of merchandise owning or operating a railroad, steamship, or other transportation line or route for the transportation of merchandise in the United States,
> - **(2)** any contract carrier authorized to operate as such by any agency of the United States, and
> - **(3)** any freight forwarder authorized to operate as such by any agency of the United States,


*Approved October 23, 1962.*

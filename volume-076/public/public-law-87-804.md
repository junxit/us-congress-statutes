# Public Law 87–804

> JOINT RESOLUTION Authorizing the President of the United States to designate the period from November 26, 1962, through December 2, 1962, as National Cultural Center Week.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States is hereby authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating the period from November 26, 1962, through December 2, 1962, as National Cultural Center Week; urging all persons, organizations, and governmental agencies involved in fostering the performing arts in this Nation to publicize and observe such week; and calling upon the Governors of the States to join in promoting the National Cultural Center campaign.


*Approved October 15, 1962.*

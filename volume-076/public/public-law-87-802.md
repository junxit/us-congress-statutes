# Public Law 87–802

> AN ACT To authorize the Commissioners of the District of Columbia to delegate the function of approving contracts not exceeding $50,000.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (b) of section 3 of Reorganization Plan Numbered 5 of 1952 is amended by striking “$25,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$50,000”.


*Approved October 11, 1962.*

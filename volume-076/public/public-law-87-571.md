# Public Law 87–571

> AN ACT To permit the use of certain construction tools actuated by explosive charges in construction activity on the United States Capitol Grounds.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 6 of the Act entitled “An Act to define the area of the United States Capitol Grounds, to regulate the use thereof, and for other purposes”, approved July 31, 1946 (60 Stat, 718; 40 U.S.C. 193f), is amended by adding at the end thereof the following new sentence: “Nothing contained in this Act shall prevent the use, in the construction of any structure or facility on the United States Capitol Grounds, of any construction tool actuated by or employing explosive charges, if (1) that tool is of a kind and design ordinarily used for such construction, (2) the Architect of the Capitol has authorized its use upon such grounds after determining that, its use will not endanger human life or safety, and (3) such use is in accordance with rules and regulations prescribed by the Architect of the Capitol.”


*Approved August 6, 1962.*

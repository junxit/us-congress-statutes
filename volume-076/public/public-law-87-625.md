# Public Law 87–625

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 1963, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the joint resolution of July 31, 1962 (Public Law 87–564), is hereby amended by striking out “August 31, 1962” and inserting in lieu thereof “September 30, 1962”.


*Approved August 31, 1962.*

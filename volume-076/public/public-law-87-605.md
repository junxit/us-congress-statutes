# Public Law 87–605

> JOINT RESOLUTION Authorizing the State of Arizona to place in the Statuary Hall collection at the United States Capitol the statue of Eusebio Francisco Kino.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the State of Arizona is hereby authorized and granted the privilege of placing in the Statuary Hall collection at the United States Capitol the statue of Eusebio Francisco Kino, pioneer missionary, explorer, and cartographer, the statue to be received as one of two statues furnished and provided by said State in accordance with the Act of July 2, 1864 (section 1814 of the Revised Statutes of the United States).


*Approved August 24, 1962.*

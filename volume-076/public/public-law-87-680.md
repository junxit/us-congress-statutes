# Public Law 87–680

> AN ACT To amend the Cooperative Forest Management Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Cooperative Forest Management Act (64 Stat. 473; 16 U.S.C. 568c, 568d) is amended by striking out of the first sentence of section 2 thereof “$2,500,000” and inserting “$5,000,000”.


*Approved September 25, 1962.*

# Public Law 87–824

> AN ACT To amend the Agricultural Adjustment Act of 1938 relating to the lease and, transfer of tobacco acreage allotments.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Agricultural Adjustment Act of 1938, as amended, is further amended (1) by changing the language enclosed in parentheses in the first sentence of section 316(a) to read “(other than a Burley tobacco acreage allotment, and for the 1963 crop year, other than a cigar-filler and cigar-binder (types 42, 43, 44, 53, 54, and 55) tobacco acreage allotment)”; and (2) by striking the period and inserting at the end of the second sentence of subsection 316(b) the following: “: Provided, That no such lease shall be renewed for 1963 for cigar-filler and cigar-binder (types 42,43,44, 53, 54, and 55) tobacco.”


*Approved October 15, 1962.*

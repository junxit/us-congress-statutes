# Public Law 87–406

> AN ACT Extending to Guam the power to enter into certain interstate compacts relating to the enforcement of the criminal laws and policies of the States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (b) of section 111 or title 4 of the United States Code is amended by inserting after the name “the Virgin Islands,” the name “Guam”.


*Approved February 16, 1962.*

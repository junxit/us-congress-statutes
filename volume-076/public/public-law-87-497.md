# Public Law 87–497

> AN ACT To continue for a temporary period the existing suspension of duty on certain amorphous graphite.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act entitled “An Act to suspend for two years the import duty on certain amorphous graphite”, approved May 13, 1960 (Public Law 86–453; 74 Stat. 103), is amended by striking out “during the two-year period beginning on the day after the date of the enactment of this Act” and inserting in lieu thereof “after May 13, 1960, and before July 1, 1964”.


*Approved June 25, 1962.*

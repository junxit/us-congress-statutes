# Public Law 87–801

> AN ACT To amend section 309 of the Food and Agriculture Act of 1962.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That item (7) of Public Law 74, Seventy-seventh Congress (7 U.S.C. 1340(7)), as amended by section 309 of the Food and Agriculture Act of 1962, is amended by changing the period at the end thereof to a comma and adding “or 1963.”


*Approved October 11, 1962.*

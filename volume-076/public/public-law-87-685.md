# Public Law 87–685

> AN ACT To amend section 9 of the Act of May 22, 1928, as amended, authorizing and directing a national survey of forest resources.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the last sentence of section 9 of the Act of May 22, 1928, as amended (45 Stat. 699,702; 16 U.S.C. 581h), is hereby amended by striking out “$1,500,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$2,500,000”.


*Approved September 25, 1962.*

# Public Law 87–812

> AN ACT To amend the Migratory Bird Conservation Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 18 of the Migratory Bird Conservation Act (16 U.S.C. 715q) is hereby amended by striking out “commisison” and inserting in lieu thereof “commission” and by striking out “$5,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$7,500”.


*Approved October 15, 1962.*

# Public Law 87–484

> AN ACT To amend title 30 of the United States Code to provide for additional writing or printing on third and fourth class mail.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the present section 4555 of title 39, United States Code, be designated subparagraph (a) and a new subparagraph (b) be added as follows:

> - **(b)** There may be enclosed with, attached to, or endorsed upon third and fourth class mail, either in writing or otherwise, the instructions and directions for the use thereof.”


*Approved June 15, 1962.*

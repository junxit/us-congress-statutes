# Public Law 87–494

> AN ACT To amend section 6 of the Agricultural Marketing Act, as amended, to reduce the revolving fund available for subscriptions to the capital stock of the banks for cooperatives.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 6 of the Agricultural Marketing Act, as amended (12 U.S.C. 1141d), is amended by adding the following sentence at the end thereof: “Effective upon enactment of this sentence the sum authorized to be appropriated for the aforesaid revolving fund is reduced from $500,000,000 to $150,000,000 and any amount in said fund in excess of $150,000,00(1 (including any amount thereof used to purchase capital stock in the central and regional banks for cooperatives) shall be credited to miscellaneous receipts of the Treasury.”


*Approved June 25, 1962.*

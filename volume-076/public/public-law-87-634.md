# Public Law 87–634

> AN ACT To make eligible for assistance under the public facility loan program certain areas where research or development installations of the National Aeronautics and Space Administration are located.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph (4) of section 202(b) of the Housing Amendments of 1955 is amended by inserting immediately after “Act” the following: “, or in the case of a community in or near which is located a research or development installation of the National Aeronautics and Space Administration”.


*Approved September 5, 1962.*

# Public Law 87–410

> AN ACT To amend subsection (h) of suction 124 of the Agricultural Enabling Amendments Act of 1961.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (h) of section 124 of the Agricultural Enabling Amendments Act of 1961 be amended by striking, following the word “subsection”, “(a) or”, and striking out the words “diverted acres” and inserting in lieu thereof “acres diverted from the 1962 allotment”.


*Approved March 3, 1962.*

# Public Law 87–466

> AN ACT To repeal section 409 of the Public Buildings Act of 1949, requiring the submission of a report to the Congress concerning eligible public building projects.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 409 of the Public Buildings Act of 1949 (40 U.S.C. 355) is hereby repealed.


*Approved May 31, 1962.*

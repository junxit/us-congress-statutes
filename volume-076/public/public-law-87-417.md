# Public Law 87–417

> AN ACT To amend the Act of June 4, 1953 (67 Stat. 41), entitled “An Act to authorize the Secretary of the Interior, or his authorized representative, to convey certain school properties to local school districts or public agencies.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second proviso in the Act of June 4, 1953 (67 Stat. 41), as amended, is amended to read: “Provided further, That no more than fifty acres of land shall be transferred under the terms of this Act in connection with any single school property conveyed to State or local governmental agencies or to local school authorities.”


*Approved March 16, 1962.*

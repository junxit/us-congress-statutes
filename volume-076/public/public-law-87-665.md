# Public Law 87–665

> AN ACT To amend title 18, United States Code, section 4163, relating to discharge of prisoners.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4163 of title 18, United States Code, is amended to read as follows: “Except as hereinafter provided a prisoner shall be released at the expiration of his term of sentence less the time deducted for good conduct. A certificate of such deduction shall be entered on the commitment by the warden or keeper. If such release date falls upon a Saturday, a Sunday, or on a Monday which is a legal holiday at the place of confinement, the prisoner may be released at the discretion of the warden or keeper on the preceding Friday. If such release date falls on a holiday which falls other than on a Saturday, Sunday, or Monday, the prisoner may be released at the discretion of the warden or keeper on the day preceding the holiday.”


*Approved September 19, 1962.*

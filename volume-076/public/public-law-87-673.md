# Public Law 87–673

> AN ACT To amend the Act of January 30, 1913, to provide that the American Hospital of Paris shall have perpetual succession.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 9 of the Act entitled “An Act to incorporate the American Hospital of Paris” approved January 30, 1913 (37 Stat. 655), is amended by striking out “for the term of fifty years” and inserting in lieu thereof “in perpetuity”.


*Approved September 19, 1962.*

# Public Law 87–551

> AN ACT To waive section 142, title 2S, United States Code, with respect to the United States District Court for the District of Connecticut for holding court at Bridgeport.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limitations and restrictions contained in section 142, title 28, United States Code, shall be waived with respect to the holding of court at Bridgeport, Connecticut, by the United States District Court for the District of Connecticut.


*Approved July 27, 1962.*

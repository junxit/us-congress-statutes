# Public Law 87–798

> AN ACT To amend the Consolidated Farmers Home Administration Act of 1961 in order to increase the limitation on the amount of loans which may be insured under subtitle A of such Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 308 of the Consolidated Farmers Home Administration Act of 1961 is amended by striking out the figure “$150,000,000” and inserting in lieu thereof the figure “$200,000,000”.


*Approved October 11, 1962.*

# Public Law 87–694

> AN ACT To repeal section 557 and to amend section 559 of the Act entitled “An Act to establish a code of law for the District of Columbia”, approved March 3, 1901.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 557 of the Act entitled “An Act to establish a code of law for the District of Columbia”, approved March 3, 1901 (31 Stat. 1189, 1279; D.C. Code, sec. 1–401) is repealed.

## § 2.

Section 559 of the Act entitled “An Act to establish a code of law for the District of Columbia”, approved March 3, 1901 (31 Stat. 1189, 1279; D.C. Code, sec. 1–402, 1–502), is amended by striking out “commissioners of deeds and”.


*Approved September 25, 1962.*

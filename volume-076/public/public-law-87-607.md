# Public Law 87–607

> AN ACT To continue for two years the existing suspension of duties on certain lathes used for shoe last roughing or for shoe last finishing.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first sentence of section 2 of Public Law 1012, Eighty-fourth Congress (70 Stat. 1076), approved August 6, 1956 (relating to suspension of duties on certain lathes used for shoe last roughing or for shoe last finishing), as amended, is amended by striking out “August 7, 1962” and inserting in lieu thereof “August 7, 1964”.


*Approved August 24, 1962.*

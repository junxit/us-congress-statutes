# Senate Concurrent Resolution 87

> “MILITARY COLD WAR EDUCATION AND SPEECH REVIEW POLICIES”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on Armed Services not to exceed six thousand additional copies of all parts of the hearings entitled “Military Cold War Education and Speech Review Policies,” held by the Special Preparedness Subcommittee during the current session, and not to exceed six thousand additional copies of the report. thereon to be made to the Senate by that committee.


*Agreed to August 30, 1962.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 415

> CUMULATIVE INDEX TO PUBLICATIONS OF COMMITTEE ON UNAMERICAN ACTIVITIES, 1938-1954

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Un-American Activities two thousand additional copies of the publication entitled “Cumulative Index to Publications of the Committee on Un-American Activities, 1938-1954”, Eighty-fourth Congress, first session.


*Passed July 18, 1962.*

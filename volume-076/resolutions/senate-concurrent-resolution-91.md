# Senate Concurrent Resolution 91

> POOLED COTTON ALLOTMENTS OF BILLIE SOL ESTES

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the (Committee on Government Operations two thousand additional copies of all parts of the hearing held by its Permanent Investigating Subcommittee during the current session on Department of Agriculture handling of pooled cotton allotments of Billie Sol Estes.


*Agreed to September 27, 1962.*

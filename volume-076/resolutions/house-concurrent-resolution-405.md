# House Concurrent Resolution 405

> CIVIL DEFENSE-1961, HEARINGS

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the House Committee on Government Operations five thousand additional copies of the committee print “Civil Defense—1961, Hearings Before a Subcommittee of the committee on Government Operations, House of Representatives, August 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 8, and 9, 1961,” issued by the Committee on Government Operations during the Eighty-seventh Congress, first session.


*Passed April 12, 1962.*

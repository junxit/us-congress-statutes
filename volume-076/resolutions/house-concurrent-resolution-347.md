# House Concurrent Resolution 347

> GENERAL OF THE ARMY DOUGLAS MAC ARTHUR

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the thanks and appreciation of the Congress and the American people are hereby tendered to General of the Army Douglas MacArthur, in recognition of his outstanding devotion to the American people, his brilliant leadership during and following World War II, and the unsurpassed affection held for him by the people of the Republic of the Philippines which has done so much to strengthen the ties of friendship between the people of that nation and the people of the United States.


*Passed July 23, 1962.*

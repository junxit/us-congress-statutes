# House Concurrent Resolution 413

> SUPPLEMENT TO CUMULATIVE INDEX TO PUBLICATIONS OF COMMITTEE ON UN-AMERICAN ACTIVITIES-1955-1960

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Un-American Activities four thousand additional copies of a publication entitled “Supplement to Cumulative Index to Publications of the Committee on Un-American Activities—1955 through 1960 (Eighty-fourth, Eighty-fifth, and Eighty-sixth Congresses)”, Eighty-seventh Congress, first session.


*Passed July 18, 1962.*

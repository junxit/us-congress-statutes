# Senate Concurrent Resolution 68

> HEARINGS ON REVENUE ACT OF 1962

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Finance not to exceed one thousand five hundred additional copies each of part 1 and all subsequent parts of hearings on the Revenue Act of 1962, held by that committee during the Eighty-seventh Congress.


*Agreed to May 23, 1962.*

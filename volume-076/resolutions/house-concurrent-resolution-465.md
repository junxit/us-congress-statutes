# House Concurrent Resolution 465

> ADJOURNMENT-HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Thursday, April 19, 1962, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Monday, April 30, 1962.


*Passed April 19, 1962.*

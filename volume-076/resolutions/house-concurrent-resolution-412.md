# House Concurrent Resolution 412

> “MANIPULATION OF PUBLIC OPINION BY ORGANIZATIONS UNDER CONCEALED CONTROL OF THE COMMUNIST PARTY”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Un-American Activities ten thousand additional copies each of parts 1 and 2 of the House Report Numbered 1282, Eignty-seventh Congress, first session, entitled “Manipulation of Public Opinion by Organizations Under Concealed Control of the Communist Party”.


*Passed April 12, 1962.*

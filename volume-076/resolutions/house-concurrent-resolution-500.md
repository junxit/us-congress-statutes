# House Concurrent Resolution 500

> IMPACT OF IMPORTS AND EXPORTS ON EMPLOYMENT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Education and Labor one thousand additional copies each of parts 2, 3, 6, and 7 of the hearings held by that committee on the impact of imports and exports on employment.


*Passed August 9, 1962.*

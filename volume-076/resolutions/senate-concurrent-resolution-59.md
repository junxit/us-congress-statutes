# Senate Concurrent Resolution 59

> “ADMINISTERED PRICES, DRUGS”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on the Judiciary two thousand additional copies of Senate Report Numbered 448, Eighty-seventh Congress, first session, entitled “Administered Prices, Drugs”, a report issued by the Committee on the Judiciary and made by its Subcommittee on Antitrust and Monopoly pursuant to S. Res. 52, Eighty-Seventh Congress.


*Agreed to March 15, 1962.*

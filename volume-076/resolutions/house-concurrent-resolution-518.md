# House Concurrent Resolution 518

> IRAN, EARTHQUAKE-EXPRESSION OF SYMPATHY

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Congress of the United States has learned with shock and sorrow of the devastation caused by the recent earthquake in Iran, which resulted in the death of thousands of its people and injuries and loss of homes to many tens of thousands. The Congress, on behalf of the people of the United States, hereby expresses its deepest sympathy for the people of Iran.


*Passed September 4, 1962.*

# Private Law 87–601

> AN ACT For the relief of Sebastians Santoro.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Sebastiana Santoro shall be held and considered to be a child of Giovanni Santoro, a citizen of the United States, as defined in section 101(b)(1)(A) of that Act.


*Approved October 10, 1962.*

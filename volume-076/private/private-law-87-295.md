# Private Law 87–295

> AN ACT To amend Private Law 85–699.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act entitled “An Act for the relief of Charles C. and George C. Finn”, approved August 27, 1958 (72 Stat. A157), is amended by striking out “on September 15, 1952” and inserting in lieu thereof “on or about September 18, 1952”.


*Approved February 16, 1962.*

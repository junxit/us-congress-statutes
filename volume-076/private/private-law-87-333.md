# Private Law 87–333

> AN ACT For the relief of Eugene C. Harter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Eugene C. Harter, Dayton, Ohio, is hereby relieved of all liability to repay to the United States a sum of $1,140.20, which was erroneously paid to him by the Department of the Air Force as salary between May 29, 1955, and February 27, 1960.

## § 2.

The Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, any amounts refunded by reason of the liability referred to in section 1 of this Act by the said Eugene C. Harter, or any amounts withheld by the United States from money otherwise due him. In the audit and settlement of the accounts of any certifying or disbursing officer of the United States, full credit shall be given for the amount for which liability is relieved by this Act.


*Approved April 10, 1962.*

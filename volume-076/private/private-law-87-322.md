# Private Law 87–322

> AN ACT For the relief of Saifook Chan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the provisions of the proviso to section 201(a) shall not be applicable in the case of Saifook Chan, a native of Malaya.


*Approved March 3, 1962.*

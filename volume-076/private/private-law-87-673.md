# Private Law 87–673

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Felix Nabor Sabates.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Felix Nabor Sabates shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence on January 16, 1956.

## § 2.

For the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Mehmet Vecihi Kalaycioglu shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of July 1, 1957, upon payment of the required visa fee. Upon the granting of permanent residence to such alien as provided for in this Act the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the appropriate quota for the first year that such quota is available.

## § 3.

For the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Ali Khosrowkhah shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of April 29, 1946.


*Approved October 23, 1962.*

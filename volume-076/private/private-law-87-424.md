# Private Law 87–424

> AN ACT For the relief of Franziska Aloisia Fuchs (nee Tercka).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of section 212(a)(3) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Franziska Aloisia Fuchs (nee Tercka), may be issued a visa and admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of this Act: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the said Act: Provided further, That this exemption shall apply only to a ground for exclusion of which the Department of State or the Department of Justice had knowledge prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved June 15, 1962.*

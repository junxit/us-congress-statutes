# Private Law 87–454

> AN ACT For the relief of Lee Hwa Sun.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Lee Hwa Sun, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. John Matthews, Junior, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents or the said Lee Hwa Sun shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved July 10, 1962.*

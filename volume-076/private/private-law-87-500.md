# Private Law 87–500

> AN ACT For the relief of Hanna Ghosn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Hanna Ghosn shall be held and considered to be the minor natural-born alien child of Mrs. Louisa Assaff. a United States citizen: Provided, That the natural parents of Hanna Ghosn shall not by virtue of such parentage be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved August 15, 1962.*

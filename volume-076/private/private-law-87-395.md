# Private Law 87–395

> AN ACT For the relief of Hendrikus Zoetmulder (Harry Combres).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Hendrikus Zoetmulder (Harry Combres) shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence pursuant to sections 1(B) and 3 of the Act of September 2, 1958, as amended (72 Stat. 1712–1713; 74 Stat. 505), as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fee.


*Approved May 31, 1962.*

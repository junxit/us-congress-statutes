# Private Law 87–649

> AN ACT For the relief of Jose Fuentes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Jose Fuentes of Santurce, Puerto Rico, be relieved of all liability to the United States for the return of salary and other payments made to him covering the period November 3, 1955, through March 3, 1961, said liability having been incurred as a result of an administrative error in the determination of his eligibility for appointment to a civilian position with the Housing and Home Finance Agency. In the audit and settlement of the accounts of any certifying or disbursing officer of the United States, credit shall be given for any amount for which liability is relieved by this Act.


*Approved October 15, 1962.*

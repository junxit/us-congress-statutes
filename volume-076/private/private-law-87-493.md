# Private Law 87–493

> AN ACT For the relief of Eladio Aris (also known as Eladio Aris Carvallo).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Eladio Aris (also known as Eladio Aris Carvallo) shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fee: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the said Act.


*Approved August 6, 1962.*

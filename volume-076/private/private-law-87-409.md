# Private Law 87–409

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Frances Mangiaracina.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 101(a) (27) (B) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Frances Mangiaracina shall be considered to be a returning resident alien.


*Approved June 8, 1962.*

# Private Law 87–309

> AN ACT For the relief of Haralambos Agourakls.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Haralambos Agourakis shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of John William and Effie Parashos, citizens of the United States.


*Approved March 3, 1962.*

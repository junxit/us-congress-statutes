# Private Law 87–315

> AN ACT For the relief of Brigitte Marie Ida Kroll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the periods of time Brigitte Marie Ida Kroll has resided in the United States since her admission as a lawful permanent resident on May 23, 1953, shall be held and considered to meet the residence and physical presence requirements of section 316 of the said Act, and the petition for naturalization may be filed with any court having naturalization jurisdiction.


*Approved March 3, 1962.*

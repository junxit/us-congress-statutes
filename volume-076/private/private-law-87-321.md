# Private Law 87–321

> AN ACT For the relief of Hugo Kolberg.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Hugo Kolberg, a naturalized citizen of the United States, shall be held not to lose nor to have lost his United States citizenship under section 352(a) (1) of such Act by residing in Germany: Provided, That he returns to the United States for permanent residence prior to September 1, 1964.


*Approved March 3, 1962.*

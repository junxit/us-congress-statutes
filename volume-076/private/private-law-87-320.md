# Private Law 87–320

> AN ACT For the relief of Lucia Bianca Cianto Rosa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Lucia Bianca Cianto Rosa shall be deemed to be within the purview of section 4 of Public Law 86–363, the Act of September 22, 1959.


*Approved March 3, 1962.*

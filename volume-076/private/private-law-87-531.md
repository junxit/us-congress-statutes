# Private Law 87–531

> AN ACT For the relief of Kim Hyung In Comstock.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Kim Hyung In Comstock shall be held and considered to be the natural-born minor alien child of Specialist First Class Carl D. Comstock and Mrs. Comstock, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents of the beneficiary shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privileges, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved September 6, 1962.*

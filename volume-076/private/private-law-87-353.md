# Private Law 87–353

> AN ACT For the relief of Edward Yin Liang.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Act of September 26, 1961 (75 Stat. 650), Edward Yin Liang shall be deemed to be within the purview of section 25(a) of that Act.


*Approved April 16, 1962.*

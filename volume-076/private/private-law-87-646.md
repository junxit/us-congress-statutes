# Private Law 87–646

> AN ACT For the relief of Rosa and Rita Quattrocchi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Rosa and Rita Quattrocchi may be classified as eligible orphans within the meaning of section 101(b)(1) (F) of the said Act and petitions may be filed by Mr. and Mrs. Samuel Canfora, citizens of the United States, in behalf of the said Rosa and Rita Quattrocchi, pursuant to section 205 (b) of the Immigration and Nationality Act subject to all the conditions in that section relating to eligible orphans.


*Approved October 15, 1962.*

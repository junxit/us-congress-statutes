# Private Law 87–548

> AN ACT For the relief of Stella Rosa Pagano.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Stella Rosa Pagano shall be held and considered to be the minor natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Luigi Pagano, citizens of the United States.


*Approved September 19, 1962.*

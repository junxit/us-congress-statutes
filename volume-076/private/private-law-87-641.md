# Private Law 87–641

> AN ACT For the relief of Gunter Heinz Hillebrand.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the periods of time Gunter Heinz Hillebrand has resided in the United States since his admission as a lawful permanent resident on March 5, 1956, shall be held and considered to meet the residence and physical presence requirements of section 316 of the said Act, and the petition for naturalization may be filed with any court having naturalization jurisdiction.


*Approved October 15, 1962.*

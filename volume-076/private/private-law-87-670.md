# Private Law 87–670

> AN ACT For the relief of Foug Yee Hin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Fong Yee Hin shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of July 25, 1940, upon payment of the required visa fee and head tax. Upon granting of permanent residence to such alien as provided for in this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the appropriate quota for the first year that such quota is available.


*Approved October 23, 1962.*

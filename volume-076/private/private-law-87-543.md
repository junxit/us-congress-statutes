# Private Law 87–543

> AN ACT For the relief of Fotios Sakelaropouloe Kaplan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act. the minor child, Fotios Sakelaropoulos Kaplan shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mrs. Helen Kaplan, a citizen of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents of Fotios Sakelaropoulos Kaplan shall not, by virtue of such parent age, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved September 19, 1962.*

# Private Law 87–298

> AN ACT For the relief of Francis Ainsworth.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States Civil Service Commission is directed to authorize payment, out of the civil service retirement and disability fund, to Francis Ainsworth, of Fall River, Massachusetts, an amount equal to interest at 3 per centum per annum compounded annually, on the refund of retirement deductions which was due him upon his separation from Government service, from the date of such separation to the day of payment of such refund, such payment having been delayed for a period of fifteen years because of an error in the computation of the length of his allowable service.


*Approved February 16, 1962.*

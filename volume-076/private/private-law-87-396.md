# Private Law 87–396

> AN ACT To repeal the Act of August 14, 1957 (Private Law 85–160).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of August 14, 1957 (Private Law 85–160), be, and the same is hereby, repealed.


*Approved May 31, 1962.*

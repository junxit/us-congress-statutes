# Private Law 87–378

> AN ACT For the relief of Spencer E. Hewitt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limitations of time applicable to filing of claims for benefits under subsections (a) through (e) of section 5 of the War Claims Act of 1948, as amended, are hereby waived in favor of Spencer E. Hewitt, of El Cajon, California (Foreign Claims Settlement Commission claim numbered 107737), and Iris claim for benefits under such subsections is authorized and directed to be acted upon by the Foreign Claims Settlement Commission if such claim is filed with the Commission within six months after the date of enactment of this Act.


*Approved May 31, 1962.*

# Private Law 87–612

> AN ACT For the relief of Mona Mclsaac Downey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mona Mclsaac Downey shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act: Provided, That if the said Mona Mclsaac Downey is not entitled to medical care under the Dependents’ Medical Care Act (70 Stat. 250), a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved October 11, 1962.*

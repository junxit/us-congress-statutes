# Private Law 87–529

> AN ACT To amend the Act of May 13, 1960 (Private Law 86–286).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Private Law 86–286, approved May 13, 1960, is hereby amended by substituting “twenty-eight years” in lieu of “twenty-five years”.


*Approved September 5, 1962.*

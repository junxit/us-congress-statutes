# Private Law 87–562

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. A. R. Lendian.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 201(g) of the Nationality Act of 1940, Mrs. A. R. Lendian shall be held and considered to have resided in the United States, prior to the birth of her daughter. Isabel Alicia Lendian, for at least five years after attaining the age of sixteen years.


*Approved September 25, 1962.*

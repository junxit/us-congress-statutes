# Private Law 87–340

> AN ACT For the relief of Teofllo Estoesta.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the limitation contained in section 2(b) of Public Law 217, Eighty-fifth Congress, approved August 29, 1957, or any other limiting statute, the claim of Teofilo Estoesta, Army serial number 10304217, for pay and allowances due him from April 1, 1943, to November 30, 1943, and February 1, 1944, to March 22, 1945, shall be regarded as having been timely filed and may be considered and paid m accordance with other applicable provisions of law.


*Approved April 10, 1962.*

# Private Law 87–430

> AN ACT For the relief of Ferdinand A. Hermens.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, section 352(a) of that Act shall be held not to be nor to have been applicable to any period of residence of Ferdinand A. Hermens, a naturalized citizen of the United States, in Germany after April 30, 1962, and prior to May 1, 1965.


*Approved June 19, 1962.*

# Private Law 87–448

> AN ACT For the relief of Joey Kim Purdy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Joey Kim Purdy shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Ralph B. Purdy, Junior, and Julia Purdy, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents of the said Joey Kim Purdy shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved July 10, 1962.*

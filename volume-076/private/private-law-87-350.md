# v

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Jeliza Prendie Milenovic.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Attorney General is authorized and directed to cancel any outstanding orders and warrants of deportation, warrants of arrest, and bond, which may have issued in the case of Mrs. Jeliza Prendie Milenovic. From and after the date of the enactment of this Act, the said Mrs. Jeliza Prendie Milenovic shall not again be subject to deportation by reason of the same facts upon which such deportation proceedings were commenced or any such warrants and orders have issued.


*Approved April 16, 1962.*

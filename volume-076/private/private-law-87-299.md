# Private Law 87–299

> AN ACT For the relief of Arlin David English.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Arlin David English, who lost United States citizenship under the provisions of section 349(a)(3) of the Immigration and Nationality Act of 1952, may be naturalized by taking prior to one year after the effective date of this Act, before any court referred to in subsection (2) of section 310 of the Immigration and Nationality Act or before any naturalization court in the United States, the oaths prescribed by section 337 of the said Act. From and after naturalization under this Act, the said Arlin David English shall have the same citizenship status as that which existed immediately prior to its loss.


*Approved February 16, 1962.*

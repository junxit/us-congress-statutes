# Private Law 87–416

> AN ACT For the relief of Kenneth David Wooden.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (Al and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Kenneth David Wooden, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Harold Hoover Wooden, a citizen of the United States: Provided, That no natural parent of Kenneth David Wooden, by virtue of such parentage, shall be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved June 12, 1962.*

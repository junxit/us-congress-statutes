# Private Law 87–659

> AN ACT For the relief of Concetta Maria, Rosetta, and Tomasino Mangiaracina.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Act of September 26, i961 (Public Law 87–301), Concetta Maria, Rosetta, and Tomasino Mangiaracina shall be deemed to be within the purview of section 25 of that Act.


*Approved October 15, 1962.*

# Private Law 87–408

> AN ACT For the relief of Sydney Gruson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, upon his admission for permanent residence in the United States, Sydney Gruson shall be held and considered to have complied with the residential and physical presence requirements of section 316 of the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved June 8, 1962.*

# Private Law 87–558

> AN ACT For the relief of Librande P. Caltagirone.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Librande P. Caltagirone shall be considered to have satisfied the service requirements of section 6(d) of the Civil Service Retirement Act, as amended (70 Stat. 749, as amended; 5 U.S.C. 2256(d)), so as to qualify for an annuity computed in accordance with the other applicable provisions of that Act as a consequence of his involuntary separation on June 8, 1962, from his position as a law clerk to a United States district judge.


*Approved September 19, 1962.*

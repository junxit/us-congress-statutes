# Private Law 87–639

> AN ACT For the relief of Michael (Mike) Bessler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the provisions of the proviso to section 201(a) shall not be applicable in the case of Michael (Mike) Bessler, a native of Malaya: Provided, That the natural mother of Michael (Mike) Bessler shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved October 15, 1962.*

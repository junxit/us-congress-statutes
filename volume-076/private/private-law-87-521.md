# Private Law 87–521

> AN ACT For the relief of Stepanida Losowskaja.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the provisions of section 312 of such Act shall be deemed not to be applicable in the case of Stepanida Losowskaja.


*Approved August 29, 1962.*

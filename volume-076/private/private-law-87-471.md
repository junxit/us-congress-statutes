# Private Law 87–471

> AN ACT For the relief of Claude Homann-Herimberg (nee Wagner).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of title III of the Immigration and Nationality Act, section 352(a)(2) of the said Act shall be deemed to have been and to be inapplicable in the case of Claude Homann-Herimberg (nee Wagner), a naturalized citizen of the United States: Provided, That the said Claude Homann-Herimberg (nee Wagner) establishes residence in the United States prior to the expiration of thirty-six months following the date of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved July 18, 1962.*

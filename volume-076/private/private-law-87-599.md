# Private Law 87–599

> AN ACT For the relief of Karl Heinz Agar.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Karl Heinz Agar shall be deemed to be within the purview of section 323 of that Act.


*Approved October 10, 1962.*

# Private Law 87–381

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Kikue Yamamoto Leghorn and her minor son, Yuichiro Yamamoto Leghorn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Kikue Yamamoto Leghorn and her minor son, Yuichiro Yamamoto Leghorn, shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fees.


*Approved May 31, 1962.*

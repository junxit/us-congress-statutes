# Private Law 87–591

> AN ACT To provide for the free entry of one nuclear magnetic resonance spectrometer and one mass spectrometer for the use of the University of Illinois.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to admit free of duty one nuclear magnetic resonance spectrometer, and one mass spectrometer, for the use of the University of Illinois.


*Approved October 5, 1962.*

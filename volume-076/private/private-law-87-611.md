# Private Law 87–611

> AN ACT For the relief of Benjamin Leach, Diogracias Leaeh, Rogelio Leach, and Maximo Leach.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Benjamin Leach, Diogracias Leach, Rogelio leach, and Maximo Leach shall be held and considered to be the natural-born minor alien children of Grady R. Leach, a citizen of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents of the beneficiaries shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved October 10, 1962.*

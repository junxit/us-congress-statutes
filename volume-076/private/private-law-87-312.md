# Private Law 87–312

> AN ACT For the relief of Nancie Ellen Williamson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Nancie Ellen Williamson, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Percy Williamson, citizens of the United States: Provided, That no natural parent of Nancie Ellen Williamson by virtue of such parentage shall be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved March 3, 1962.*

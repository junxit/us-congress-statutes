# Private Law 87–618

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Hassan M. Nouri.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Hassan M. Nouri shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence on July 3, 1957.


*Approved October 11, 1962.*

# Private Law 87–682

> AN ACT For the relief of Don C. Jensen and Bruce E. Woolner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Don C. Jensen and Bruce E. Woolner are hereby relieved of all liability to pay to the United States the amounts listed opposite their names, representing unaccounted for outstanding balances in their accounts while serving as disbursing officers of the United States: Don C. Jensen, $415.99; Bruce E. Woolner, $804.73.

## § 2.

The Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to the said Don C. Jensen and Bruce E. Woolner, the sum of any amounts received or withheld from them on account of the outstanding balances referred to in the first section of this Act.


*Approved October 23, 1962.*

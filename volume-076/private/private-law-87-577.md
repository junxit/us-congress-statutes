# Private Law 87–577

> AN ACT For relief of Anthony E. O’Sorlo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limitations of time applicable to the receipt, adjudication, and payment of claims for civilian detention benefits under section 5 (a) through (e) of the War Claims Act of 1948, as amended, are hereby waived in favor of Anthony E. O’Sorio, of Bronx, New York, and his claim for such benefits is authorized and directed to be acted upon by the Foreign Claims Settlement Commission if such claim is filed with the Commission within the six-month period which begins on the date of enactment of this Act.


*Approved October 1, 1962.*

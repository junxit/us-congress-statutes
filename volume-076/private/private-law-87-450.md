# Private Law 87–450

> AN ACT For the relief of Lise Marie Berthe Marguerite De Simone.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the provisions of sections 201(a), 202(a)(5), and 202(b) shall be inapplicable in the case of Lise Marie Berthe Marguerite De Simone.


*Approved July 10, 1962.*

# Private Law 87–437

> AN ACT Granting a renewal of patent numbered 92,187 relating to the badge of the Sons of the American Legion.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a certain design patent issued by the United States Patent Office of date of May 8, 1934, being patent numbered 92,187, is hereby renewed and extended for a period of fourteen years from and after the date of approval of this Act, with all the rights and privileges pertaining to the same, being generally known as “the badge of the Sons of the American Legion.”


*Approved June 25, 1962.*

# Private Law 87–443

> AN ACT For the relief of Kim Oarey (Timothy Mark Alt).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Kim Carey (Timothy Mark Alt) shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Raymond L. Alt, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents of Kim Carey (Timothy Mark Alt) shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved June 27, 1962.*

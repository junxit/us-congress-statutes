# Private Law 87–483

> AN ACT For the relief of George Ross Hutchins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of section 212(a) (3) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, George Ross Hutchins may be issued a visa and be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if he is otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act: Provided, That the exemption granted herein shall apply only to a ground for exclusion of which the Department of State or the Department of Justice has knowledge prior to the enactment of this Act: Provided, further, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the said Act.


*Approved August 6, 1962.*

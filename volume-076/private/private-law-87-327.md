# Private Law 87–327

> AN ACT For the relief of Jean L. Dunlop.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Jean L. Dunlop of Arlington, Virginia, is hereby relieved of all liability for repayment to the United States of the sum of $1,275, representing the amount of erroneous payments of a class Q allotment made to her by the Department of the Army for the period from August 1, 1951, through May 31, 1952, as a result of administrative error.

## § 2.

The Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to the said Jean L. Dunlop, the sum of any amounts received or withheld from her on account of the overpayments referred to in the first section of this Act.


*Approved March 31, 1962.*

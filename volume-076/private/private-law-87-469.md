# Private Law 87–469

> AN ACT For the relief of Noreen Joyce Baden.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Noreen Joyce Baden shall be deemed to be chargeable to the quota for Great Britain.


*Approved July 18, 1962.*

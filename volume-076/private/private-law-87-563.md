# Private Law 87–563

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Heghine Tomassian.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 1 through 4 of the Act of July 14, 1960 (74 Stat. 504, 505), shall be applicable in the case of Mrs. Heghine Tomassian.


*Approved September 27, 1962.*

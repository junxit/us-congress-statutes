# Private Law 87–511

> AN ACT For the relief of Helena M. Grover.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the limitations contained in section 202(h) of the Social Security Act, as amended, or any other limitation provided by law, the application of Helena M. Grover for parents insurance benefits and proof of support, filed on July 9, 1959, shall be considered to have been timely filed and acted upon in accordance with other applicable provisions of the Social Security Act.


*Approved August 24, 1962.*

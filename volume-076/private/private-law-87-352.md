# Private Law 87–352

> AN ACT For the relief of Mary Elizabeth Sidor Polkowska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203(a) (3) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Mary Elizabeth Sidor Polkowska shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mrs. Therese Polkowska, a lawful permanent resident of the United States.


*Approved April 16, 1962.*

# Private Law 87–490

> AN ACT For the relief of Ivy Gwendolyn Myers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Ivy Gwendolyn Myers shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien minor child of Egbert Myers, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 6, 1962.*

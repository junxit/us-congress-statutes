# Private Law 87–435

> AN ACT For the relief of Michael Najeeb Metry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Michael Najeeb Metry, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Doctor and Mrs. John M. Metry, citizens of the United States: Provided, That, the natural parents of Michael Najeeb Metry shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved June 25, 1962.*

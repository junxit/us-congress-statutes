# Private Law 87–376

> AN ACT For the relief of Fong Chun Hong.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Fong Chun Hong, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien minor child of Fong Houck Pong, a citizen of the United States: Provided, That the natural mother of the beneficiary shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved May 31, 1962.*

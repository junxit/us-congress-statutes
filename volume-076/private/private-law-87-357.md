# Private Law 87–357

> AN ACT For the relief of Aida Mary Sorino Bocealery.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Aida Mary Sorino Boccalery shall be deemed to be within the purview of section 353(3) of that Act.


*Approved April 16, 1962.*

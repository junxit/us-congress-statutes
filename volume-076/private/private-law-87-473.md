# Private Law 87–473

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Leslie M. Paterson, Janet Paterson, and Mary Paterson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the clause numbered “(1)” in section 202(c) of that Act shall be inapplicable in the cases of Mrs. Leslie M. Paterson, Janet Paterson and Mary Paterson.


*Approved July 25, 1962.*

# Private Law 87–316

> AN ACT For the relief of Frances E. Sarcone.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Frances E. Sarcone shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of September 18, 1951.


*Approved March 3, 1962.*

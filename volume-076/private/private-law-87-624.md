# Private Law 87–624

> AN ACT For the relief of Major William R. Cook.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Major William R. Cook, AO862571, United States Air Force, the sum of $842.76 in full settlement of his claim against the United States for travel performed by Major Cook and members of his family from Travis Air Force Base, California, Offutt Air Force Base, Nebraska, during May and June 1960. His claim for such expenses was denied because his permanent change of station orders were amended to show his permanent station as Sunnyvale, California, while he was on leave prior to the time his travel to the new permanent station was required.


*Approved October 11, 1962.*

# Private Law 87–318

> AN ACT For the relief of Susanne Rae Deremo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purpose of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Susanne Rae Deremo, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Charles E. Deremo and Elene Deremo, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents of the said Susanne Rae Deremo shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved March 3, 1962.*

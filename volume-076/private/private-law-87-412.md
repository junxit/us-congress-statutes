# Private Law 87–412

> AN ACT For the relief of Sonia Maria Smith.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purpose of sections 101(a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Sonia Maria Smith, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Doris and Cecil Smith, citizens of the United States.


*Approved June 8, 1962.*

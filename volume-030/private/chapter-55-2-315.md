# Chapter 315

> An Act Granting a pension to Annie Wittenmyer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Annie Wittenmyer, a hospital nurse during the late war of the rebellion, and to pay to her a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, May 14, 1898.*

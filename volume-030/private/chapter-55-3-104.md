# Chapter 104

> An Act Granting a pension to Ella Hayne Agnew.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ella Hayne Agnew, widow of Enoch Agnew, who served with the Palmetto Regiment of South Carolina in the Mexican war, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 4, 1899.*

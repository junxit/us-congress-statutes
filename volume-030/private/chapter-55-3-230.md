# Chapter 230

> An Act To remove the charge of desertion from the naval record of Horace G. Reed.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to amend the naval record of Horace G. Reed, deceased, late a first-class boy on board of the Grampus and Moose, in Admiral Porter’s fleet, by removing from said record the charge of desertion, and to issue in this behalf a certificate of discharge from the said naval service of the United States: Provided, That no pay or allowances shall become due or payable by reason of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 28, 1899.*

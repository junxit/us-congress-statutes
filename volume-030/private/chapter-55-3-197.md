# Chapter 197

> An Act Granting a pension to Isom Gibson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Isom Gibson, of Company B, commanded by Captain Bateman, Arkansas Volunteers, Sabine disturbances, and pay him a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1899.*

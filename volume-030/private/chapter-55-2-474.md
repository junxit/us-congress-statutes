# Chapter 474

> An Act Granting a pension to Thomas Edsall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Thomas Edsall, late of Company B, Ninth Regiment Illinois Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, June 18, 1898.*

# Chapter 666

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to George S. Walton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of George S. Walton, late of Company N, Second Missouri Mounted Volunteers, Mexican war, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, July 8, 1898.*

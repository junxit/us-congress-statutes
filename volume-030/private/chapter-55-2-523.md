# Chapter 523

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Levi K. Long.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Levi R. Long, late private of Company I, Seventeenth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension which he now receives.


*Approved, June 28, 1898.*

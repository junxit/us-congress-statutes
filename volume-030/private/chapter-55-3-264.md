# Chapter 264

> An Act Granting a pension to Julia Walke.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Julia Walke, widow of Henry Walke, late rear admiral, United States Navy, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per mouth.


*Approved, February 28, 1899.*

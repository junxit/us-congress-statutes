# Chapter 592

> An Act To remove the charge of desertion from the military record of Sebastian Beckerd.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to remove the charge of desertion now standing on the military record of Sebastian Beckerd, late of Company I, First Louisiana Volunteer Infantry, and to issue to him an honorable discharge: Provided, That no pay or emoluments be allowed or paid by reason of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

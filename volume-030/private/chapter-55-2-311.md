# Chapter 311

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Martha Ann Hynes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Martha Ann Hynes, of Little Rock, Arkansas, widow of Andrew R. Hynes, second lieutenant of Company C, Fifth Regiment Louisiana Volunteers, Mexican War, and to pay her the sum of twelve dollars per mouth, the same to be in lieu of the pension she is now receiving.


*Approved, May 14, 1898.*

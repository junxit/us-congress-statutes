# Chapter 303

> An Act Granting a pension to Simpson Everett Stilwell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Simpson Everett Stilwell, late scout and guide in the United States Army, at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, May 14, 1898.*

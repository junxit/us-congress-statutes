# Chapter 177

> An Act For the relief of Jerome A. Stanton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of .Jerome A. Stanton, a scout and master of transportation in the Seventh Corps of the armies of the United States, and pay him a pension of eighteen dollars per month for wounds and injuries received while in the discharge of his duties.


*Approved, April 15, 1898.*

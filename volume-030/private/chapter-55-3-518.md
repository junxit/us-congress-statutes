# Chapter 518

> An Act Granting a pension to Olivia T. Worden.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Olivia T. Worden, widow of the late John L. Worden, an admiral of the United States Navy, and pay her a pension, from the passage of this Act, at the rate of fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

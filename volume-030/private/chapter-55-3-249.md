# Chapter 249

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Solomon Kline.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Solomon Kline, late of Company I, Forty-sixth Indiana Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month in lieu of the pension which he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 28, 1899.*

# Chapter 184

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Millie A. Berry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the. Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Millie A. Berry, widow of R. C. Berry, late first lieutenant of Company F, Seventy seventh Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month in lieu of the pension she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 21, 1899.*

# Chapter 126

> An Act To remove the charge of desertion from the military record of Daniel Straw, late private, Company F, Twenty-sixth Massachusetts Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to amend the military record of Daniel Straw, late private of Company F, Twenty-sixth Massachusetts Volunteers, so as to remove the charge of desertion and grant him an honorable discharge: Provided, That no pay, bounty, or other emoluments shall become due or payable by virtue of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 8, 1899.*

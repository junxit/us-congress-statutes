# Chapter 535

> An Act To increase the pension of Mrs. Rebecca S. Foster.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Rebecca S. Foster, widow of the late Lieutenant-Colonel John A. Foster, of the One hundred and seventy-fifth Regiment of New York Volunteer Infantry, upon the pension roll and pay her a pension at the rate of forty dollars a month in lieu of the pension she now receives.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

# Chapter 134

> An Act Granting a pension to Jane B. Johnston.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jane B. Johnston, an army nurse at Government Hospital, Throggs Neck, New York, during the war of the rebellion, and pay her a pension of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 9, 1899.*

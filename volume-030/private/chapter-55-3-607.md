# Chapter 607

> An Act Granting a pension to Abigail Wilson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Abigail Wilson, the dependent mother of Alden M. Wilson, late of Company B, Fifty-second Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

# Chapter 528

> An Act To correct the naval record of Martin U. Singhi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to correct the naval record of and grant a discharge to Martin U. Singhi, late a mariner in the United States ships Ohio, Congress, North Carolina, Vermont, and Ottawa: Provided, That said Singhi shall not by the passage of this Act be entitled to any bounty or back pay of any kind.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

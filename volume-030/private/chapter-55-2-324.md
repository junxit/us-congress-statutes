# Chapter 324

> An Act Granting a pension to Hannah C. Pile.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Hannah C. Pile, willow of General William A. Pile, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars a month.


*Approved, May 14, 1898.*

# Chapter 48

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Mrs. Jane L. Fagg, widow of Colonel John A. Fagg.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay Mrs. Jane L. Fagg, widow of Colonel John A. Fagg, who was a soldier in the Mexican war, twelve dollars per month, in lieu of eight dollars per month she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 5, 1898.*

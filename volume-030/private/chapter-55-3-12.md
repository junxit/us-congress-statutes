# Chapter 12

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Theodore W. Cobia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Theodore W. Cobia, late of Colonel Gordon’s regiment of South Carolina Volunteers in the Florida Indian war of eighteen hundred and thirty-six, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, December 20, 1898.*

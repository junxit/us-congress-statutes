# Chapter 321

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to George W. Garrison.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions anti limitations of the pension laws, the name George W. Garrison, late of Company I, Pennsylvania Volunteers, and Company I, One hundred and second Pennsylvania Volunteers, and pay him a pension of thirty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, May 14, 1898.*

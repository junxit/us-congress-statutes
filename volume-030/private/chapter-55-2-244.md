# Chapter 244

> An Act For the relief of Robert Kennish.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to grant an honorable discharge to Robert Kennish, late a mate on board the United States steamer Restless, as of the date of September thirtieth, eighteen hundred and sixty-two.


*Approved, May 5, 1898.*

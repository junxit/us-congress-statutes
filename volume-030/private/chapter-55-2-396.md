# Chapter 396

> An Act To restore a pension to Harriet M. Knowlton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to restore to the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Harriet M. Knowlton, widow of the late Major Knowlton of the Twenty ninth Maine Volunteer Infantry, at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, June 8, 1898.*

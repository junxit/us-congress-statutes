# Chapter 118

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Jordan Thomas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Jordan Thomas, late of Company H, Third Regiment of Kentucky Volunteer Infantry, in the war with Mexico, and pay him a pension at the rate of eighteen dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 4, 1899.*

# Chapter 578

> An Act For the relief of Julius C. Kloenne.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War is hereby authorized and directed to correct the record of Julius C. Kloenne, late captain of Company K, Seventeenth Indiana Infantry Volunteers, whom the record shows was mustered out of service in that regiment on the twenty-third day of December, eighteen hundred and sixty-one, so that the same will show him to have been honorably discharged and mustered out with leave and for the purpose of recruiting and organizing other troops in the State of Indiana, and that said record be so changed and corrected instead of the record now made.

## § 2.

That said Julius C. Kloenne be, and is hereby, declared entitled to pay as captain in said regiment from the date of the last payment made to him up to the date of his discharge as aforesaid.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

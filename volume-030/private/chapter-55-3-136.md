# Chapter 136

> An Act Granting a pension to Charles H. Streeter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Charles H. Streeter, invalid and dependent son of Alonzo Streeter, late of Company E, Thirty-second Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 9, 1899.*

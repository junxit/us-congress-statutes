# Chapter 131

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Elisha M. Luckett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Elisha M. Luckett, second lieutenant Company B, Second Pennsylvania Volunteers, Mexican war, and pay him a pension of twenty-five dollars per month, the same to be in lieu of the pension now drawn by him.


*Approved, April 11, 1898.*

# Chapter 262

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Lucy D. Heady.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Lucy I). Heady, widow of Henry Heady, late Company F, Eighty-ninth New York Volunteers, on the pension roll and pay her a pension at the rate of eighteen dollars per month.


*Approved, February 28, 1899.*

# Chapter 410

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Eliza Miller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Eliza Miller, widow of Abraham Miller, late of Company E, Thirty sixth Ohio Infantry Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month, in lieu of the pension which she is now receiving.


*Approved, June 8, 1898.*

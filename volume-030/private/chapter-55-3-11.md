# Chapter 11

> An Act To increase the pension of Thomas B. Hammond.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Thomas B. Hammond, late of Company If, Twenty-ninth Pennsylvania Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that which he now receives.


*Approved, December 20, 1898.*

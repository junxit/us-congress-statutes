# Chapter 560

> An Act Granting a pension to America Easton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of America Easton, widow of Jacob Easton, who was a private in Company H, One hundred and fourteenth Regiment of United States Colored Infantry, and to pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars a month.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

# Chapter 211

> An Act Granting a pension to Plum P. Miner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Plum P. Miner, late of Company B, Twelfth Indiana Infantry, on the pension roll, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, April 27, 1898.*

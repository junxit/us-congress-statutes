# Chapter 199

> An Act Granting a pension to Eunice Taylor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate anti House of Representatives of the Baited States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Eunice Taylor, a hospital nurse during the civil war, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, April 27, 1898.*

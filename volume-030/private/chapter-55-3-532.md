# Chapter 532

> An Act Granting an honorable discharge to Thomas West.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to cause an honorable discharge, as of September twenty-first, eighteen hundred and sixty-one, to be granted to Thomas West, late a private in Company F, Twelfth New York Infantry Volunteers, and late a private in Company B, One hundred and eighty-fifth New York Infantry Volunteers: Provided, That no pay, bounty, or other emoluments shall become due or payable by virtue of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

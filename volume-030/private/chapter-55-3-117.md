# Chapter 117

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Thomas S. Tefft.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Thomas S. Tefft, late of the First Regiment New York Dragoons, and to pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month in lieu of the pension he now receives.


*Approved, February 4, 1899.*

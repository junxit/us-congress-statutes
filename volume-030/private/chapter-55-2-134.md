# Chapter 134

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Henry F. Rice.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Henry F. Rice, late first lieutenant Company B, Seventeenth United States Infantry, and to pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of the pension be is now receiving.


*Approved, April 11, 1898.*

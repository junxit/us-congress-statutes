# Chapter 114

> An Act For the relief of Joseph P. Patton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to correct the record of the War Department in the ease of Joseph P. Patton, late first lieutenant of Company C, Fifth Regiment of Ohio Volunteer Cavalry, by revoking the order of his dismissal and granting him an honorable discharge, to date September twenty-first, eighteen hundred and sixty-four.


*Approved, April 4, 1898.*

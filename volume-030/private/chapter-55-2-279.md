# Chapter 279

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Elizabeth Connelly.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Elizabeth Connelly, widow of John H. Connelly, late of Company F, Eighty fourth New York Volunteers (Fourteenth New York State Militia), on the pension roll and pay her a pension of twelve dollars per mouth, in lieu of the pension she is now receiving.


*Approved, May 7, 1898.*

# Chapter 26

> An Act Granting a pension to Frances E. Utley Davis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Frances E. Utley Davis, widow of Eugene M. Utley, sergeant, Company F, One hundred and fourteenth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, December 20, 1898.*

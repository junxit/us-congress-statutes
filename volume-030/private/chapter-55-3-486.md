# Chapter 486

> An Act To increase the pension of Aaron B. Page.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Aaron B. Page, late of Company E, Seventy-second Regiment Illinois Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension now received.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

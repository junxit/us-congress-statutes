# Chapter 283

> An Act Granting a pension to Belle Shumard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Belle Shumard, widow of George G. Shumard, late surgeon, United States Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty five dollars per month.


*Approved, February 28, 1899.*

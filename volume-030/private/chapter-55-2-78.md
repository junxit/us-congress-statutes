# Chapter 78

> An Act Granting a pension to Riley W. Pierce.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension roll the name of Riley W. Pierce, a soldier of the Mexican war, at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, March 19, 1898.*

# Chapter 205

> An Act To increase the pension of David N. Thompson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of David N. Thompson, late a private of Company C, Thirty-fourth Regiment Illinois Infantry Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty-five dollars per month in lieu of the pension that he is now receiving.


*Approved, April 27, 1898.*

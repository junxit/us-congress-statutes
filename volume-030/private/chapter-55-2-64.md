# Chapter 64

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Esther Williams.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Esther Williams, widow of Gilbert M. Williams, for services in Captain Stephen’s Company, Georgia Volunteers, Florida war, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of the pension of eight dollars per month she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 14, 1898.*

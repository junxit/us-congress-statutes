# Chapter 546

> An Act Increasing the pension of Howard L. James.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Howard L. James, late sergeant of Company G of the Ninth Indiana Volunteers, on the pension roll and pay him a pension at the rate of eighteen dollars per month, subject to the conditions and limitations of the pension laws and in lieu of the pension he now receives.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

# Chapter 669

> An Act Granting a pension to Corydon G. Crafts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Corydon G. Crafts, dependent and permanently helpless son of Moses Crafts, late of the Tenth Regiment Maine Infantry Volunteers, and to pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month, payable to his duly appointed guardian.


*Approved, July 8, 1898.*

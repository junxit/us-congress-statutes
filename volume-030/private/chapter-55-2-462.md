# Chapter 462

> An Act For the relief of Isaac N. Babb.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to remove the charge of desertion standing against Isaac N. Babb, late a member of the Twenty third Indiana Battery, and issue to him an honorable discharge: Provided, That no pay, bounty, or other emoluments shall become due or payable by virtue of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 16, 1898.*

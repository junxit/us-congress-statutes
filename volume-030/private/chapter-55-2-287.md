# Chapter 287

> An Act To increase the pension of William H. Savage.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William H. Savage, late captain Company A, Seventeenth Maine Volunteers, and to pay him a pension of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that which he is now receiving.


*Approved, May 7, 1898.*

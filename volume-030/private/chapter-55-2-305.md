# Chapter 305

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Lafayette Tibbetts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lafayette Tibbetts, late of Company C, Tenth New Hampshire Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month, in lieu of that he is now receiving, payable to his legally constituted guardian.


*Approved, May 14, 1898.*

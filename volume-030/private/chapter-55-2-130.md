# Chapter 130

> An Act Increasing the pension of William Warner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William Warner, late a private of Company I, Eleventh Regiment Wisconsin Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, April 11, 1898.*

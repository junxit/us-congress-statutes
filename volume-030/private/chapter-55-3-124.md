# Chapter 124

> An Act For the benefit of J. C. Rudd.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay James C. Rudd, of Owensboro, Kentucky, the sum of six hundred dollars, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, being the amount due said Rudd for the rent of a post-office building in the city of Owensboro, Kentucky.


*Approved, February 8, 1899.*

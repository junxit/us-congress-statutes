# Chapter 605

> An Act Granting a pension to Susan I. Barrows

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Susan I. Barrows, idiotic and dependent child of Silas Barrows, deceased, late a member of Company E, Eighth Regiment Maine Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month, said pension to be paid to her duly appointed guardian.


*Approved, July 7, 1898.*

# Chapter 168

> An Act Granting a pension to Emily J. Miller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Emily J. Miller, blind and permanently helpless daughter of Robert B. Miller, late of Company C, One hundred and ninth New York Volunteer Infantry, upon the pension roll and pay her a pension of fifteen dollars per month.


*Approved, April 15, 1898.*

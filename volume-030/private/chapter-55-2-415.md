# Chapter 415

> An Act To increase the pension of Charles Hoffman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Charles Hoffman, a soldier of the Mexican war, Company K, First Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers; also lieutenant Company E, Seventy-eighth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers, in the late war, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, June 8, 1898.*

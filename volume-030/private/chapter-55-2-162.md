# Chapter 162

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Robert P. Wild.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Robert P. Wild, late of Company B, Second North Carolina Mounted Infantry, on the pension roll and pay him a pension of twenty dollars per month, in lieu of ten dollars per month he is now receiving.


*Approved, April 13, 1898.*

# Chapter 223

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Ransom S. Angell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Ransom S. Angell, late private in Company G, First Regiment Wisconsin Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, April 28, 1898.*

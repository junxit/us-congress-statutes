# Chapter 622

> An Act To increase the pension of George W. Detwiler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws, the name of George W. Detwiler, late of Company H, Twelfth Regiment of Pennsylvania Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that which he now receives.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

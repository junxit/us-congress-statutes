# Chapter 149

> An Act To restore Captain Robert W. Dowdy to the active list of the Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized to restore Robert W. Dowdy to the active list of the Army of the United States, with the same rank and relative position he would hold if he had not been retired.


*Approved, February 9, 1899.*

# Chapter 255

> An Act To grant an increase of pension to Nathaniel Haughton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie hereby is, authorized and directed to place the name of Nathaniel Haughton, late colonel of the Twenty-fifth Ohio Volunteer Infantry, upon the pension roll and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month from and after the passage of this Act.


*Approved, May 7, 1898.*

# Chapter 51

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Henry B. Conway.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Henry B. Conway, late of Company F, Second Illinois Volunteers, Mexican war, at the rate of twenty dollars per mouth, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 5, 1898.*

# Chapter 24

> An Act Granting a pension to Joseph Griffith.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Joseph Griffith, dependent father of John II. Griffith, late of Company F, First Eastern Shore Maryland Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, December 20, 1898.*

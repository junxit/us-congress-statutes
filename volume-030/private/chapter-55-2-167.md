# Chapter 167

> An Act Granting a pension to Mrs. Sarah A. Denny.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Mrs. Sarah A. Denny, an army nurse, on the pension roll, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, April 15, 1898.*

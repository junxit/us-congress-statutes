# Chapter 330

> An Act Granting a pension to Emory T. Hippie.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension rolls the name of Emory T. Hippie, permanently helpless child of William n. Hippie, late private Company D, One hundred and forty-ninth Regiment of Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, May 14, 1898.*

# Chapter 221

> An Act Granting a pension to Clarissa E. Hobbs.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Clarissa E. Hobbs, of Louisville, Nebraska, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month for services as nurse in the Twelfth Regiment Iowa Infantry.


*Approved, April 28, 1898.*

# Chapter 337

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Michael H. J. Crouch.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Michael H. J. Crouch, late a private in Company M, Sixth Pennsylvania Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month, in lieu of that which he is now receiving.


*Approved, May 16, 1898.*

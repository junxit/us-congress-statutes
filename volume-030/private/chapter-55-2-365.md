# Chapter 365

> An Act For the relief of George M. Anderson, of the State of Nebraska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That George M. Anderson, of Rock County, Nebraska, be, and he is hereby, permitted and authorized to make a homestead entry of one hundred and sixty acres on any of the public lands of the United States which are now, or may hereafter be, subject to homestead entry, and that in making proof thereof he shall be given credit for any time he may have spent in residing upon and improving homestead entry, by mistake, on the northeast quarter of section twenty-eight, township ninety-five north, of range sixty-nine, in Gregory County, South Dakota.


*Approved, May 27, 1898.*

# Chapter 569

> An Act For the relief of Eudora Hill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Eudora Hill, a member of the Wyandotte tribe, to whom was allotted the east half of the north-west quarter of section nineteen, in township twenty-seven north, of range twenty-four east of the Indian meridian, in the Indian Territory, as a part of her allotment, be, and the same is hereby, authorized to sell, mortgage, transfer, or convey all her right, title, and interest in and to said land to whomsoever she may desire.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

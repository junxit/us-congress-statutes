# Chapter 520

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to George W. Palmer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George W. Palmer, late a private of Company B, Tenth Regiment United States Infantry, at the rate of twenty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, June 28, 1898.*

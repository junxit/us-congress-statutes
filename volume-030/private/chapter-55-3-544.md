# Chapter 544

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Mrs. Susan Stedman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Mrs. Susan Stedman, widow of Kinsley Stedman, late a private in Company H, Eighty-third Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers, from eight dollars, the amount now received by said widow, to fifteen dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

# Chapter 163

> An Act Granting a pension to Caroline A. Slocum.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Caroline A. Slocum, widow of Willard Slocum, late lieutenant-colonel and brevet brigadier-general of the Cue hundred and twentieth Regiment Ohio Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars a month.


*Approved, April 13, 1898.*

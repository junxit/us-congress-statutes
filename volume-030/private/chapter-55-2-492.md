# Chapter 492

> An Act To increase the pension of Edward Starr.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Edward Starr, late a private in Company C, First Regiment Washington Territory Volunteer Infantry, on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that which he is now receiving.


*Approved, June 22, 1898.*

# Chapter 297

> An Act Granting a pension to Justus Townsend.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie hereby is, authorized and directed to place the name of Justus Townsend, late acting assistant surgeon, United States Army, on the pension roll and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 28, 1899.*

# Chapter 107

> An Act Granting increase of pension to John J. Boatwright.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John J. Boatwright, late of Company I, Twenty-fourth Ohio Volunteer Infantry, at the rate of thirty dollars per mouth.


*Approved, March 31, 1898.*

# Chapter 514

> An Act For the relief of Cordell B. Green, Company D, Sixteenth Michigan Infantry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and instructed to remove the charge of desertion from the military record of Cordell B. Green, late of Company D, Sixteenth Michigan Infantry, in the war of the rebellion, and to grant an honorable discharge to said Green: Provided, That no pay or allowances shall become due or payable by reason of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 27, 1898.*

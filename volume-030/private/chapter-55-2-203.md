# Chapter 203

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Martha M. McCall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Martha M. McCall, widow of William Wallace McCall, a soldier of the Mexican war, to the sum of fifteen dollars per mouth.


*Approved, April 27, 1898.*

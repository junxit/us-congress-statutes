# Chapter 309

> An Act Granting a pension to Allen Meeks.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Allen Meeks, late of Company A, Third Arkansas Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 28, 1899.*

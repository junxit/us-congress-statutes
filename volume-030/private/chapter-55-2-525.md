# Chapter 525

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Paul Carr.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Paul Carr, late of the United States steamship Princeton and of the New Ironsides, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars a month in lieu of the pension he is now receiving, the same to be paid to his duly appointed guardian.


*Approved, June 28, 1898.*

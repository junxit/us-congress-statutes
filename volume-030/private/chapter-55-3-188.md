# Chapter 188

> An Act To correct the relative rank of Richard R. Steedman, captain, Eleventh Infantry, United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in order to correct the relative rank Of Richard R. Steedman, captain, Eleventh Infantry, his name shall be placed next below that of Wallis O. Clark, captain. Twelfth Infantry, United States Army.


*Approved, February 24, 1899.*

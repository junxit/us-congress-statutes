# Chapter 105

> An Act Granting a pension to Ellen Wright.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Ellen Wright, late an army nurse, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 4, 1899.*

# Chapter 528

> An Act Granting a pension to Thomas Madden.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Thomas Madden, late of Company H, Thirty-second New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, June 28, 1898.*

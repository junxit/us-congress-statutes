# Chapter 248

> An Act Granting a pension to Zenas T. Haines.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Zenas T. Haines, late corporal of Company D, Forty-fourth Regiment Massachusetts Volunteer Militia, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars a month.


*Approved, February 28, 1899.*

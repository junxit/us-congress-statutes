# Chapter 574

> An Act Granting a pension to John S. Draper.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, directed to place the name of John S. Draper, late a private in the war with Mexico, and subsequently a sergeant in Company E, Third United States Infantry, upon the pension roll, and to pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

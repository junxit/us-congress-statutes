# Chapter 97

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Leah L. Price.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Leah L. Price, widow of William Price, late chaplain of the Second Kentucky Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifteen dollars per month in lieu of the pension she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 4, 1899.*

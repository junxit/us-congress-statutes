# Chapter 132

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to William W. Tumblin, of Bradford County, Florida.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of William W. Tumblin, of Bradford County, Florida, a soldier in the Florida Indian war of the year eighteen hundred and thirty-five, and pay him at the rate of twenty dollars a month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 9, 1899.*

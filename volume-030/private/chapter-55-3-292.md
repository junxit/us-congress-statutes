# Chapter 292

> An Act Granting a pension to Levi M. Truit.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Levi M. Truit, a soldier in the Mexican war, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per mouth.


*Approved, February 28, 1899.*

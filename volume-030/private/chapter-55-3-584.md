# Chapter 584

> An Act To correct the military record of Isaac Alger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to remove the charge of desertion standing against Isaac Alger, late a private in Company L, Tenth Regiment New York Heavy Artillery Volunteers, and to issue to him an honorable discharge as of date November thirtieth, eighteen hundred and sixty-four: Provided, That no pay, bounty, or other allowances shall become due or payable by reason of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

# Chapter 594

> An Act For the relief of Anson W. Gillett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to remove the charge of desertion now standing against Anson W. Gillett, alias Henry W. Edwards, late of Company G, Fifty-ninth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and to give to the said Anson W. Gillett, alias Henry W. Edwards, an honorable discharge from the said company and regiment as of date September sixth, eighteen hundred and sixty-two: Provided, That no pay or allowances shall become due or payable by reason of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

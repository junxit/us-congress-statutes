# Chapter 322

> An Act Increasing the pension of Katherine L. Cushing.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and. House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized anti directed to place on the pension roll the name of Katherine L. Cushing, widow of William B. Cushing, late commander in United States Navy, and pay her a pension of fifty dollars per month in lieu of the pension now paid her.


*Approved, May 14, 1898.*

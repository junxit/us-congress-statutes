# Chapter 112

> An Act To increase the pension of Louis Hirsch.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Louis Hirsch, late second lieutenant United States Volunteers, on the pension roll and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of his present pension.


*Approved, February 4, 1899.*

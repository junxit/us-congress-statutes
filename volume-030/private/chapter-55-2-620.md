# Chapter 620

> An Act Granting a pension to Mary A. Watts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, directed to place the name of Mary A. Watts, widow of Little B. Watts, late of Company G, First Regiment of Alabama Vidette Cavalry, on the pension roll, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, July 7, 1898.*

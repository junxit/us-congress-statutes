# Chapter 657

> An Act Granting a pension to Mary McLaughlin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Mary McLaughlin, widow of James McLaughlin, late captain of Company I, Tenth Regiment Kansas State Militia, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, July 8, 1898.*

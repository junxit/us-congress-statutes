# Chapter 354

> An Act Granting a pension to Mary I. Valentine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Mary I. Valentine, daughter of the late Joseph E. Valentine, of Company A, Ninety-seventh Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, May 23, 1898.*

# Chapter 380

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to John P. Thomas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of John P. Thomas, late a private in Company H, Thirty-sixth Iowa Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension of forty dollars per month in lieu of that be is now receiving.


*Approved, June 4, 1898.*

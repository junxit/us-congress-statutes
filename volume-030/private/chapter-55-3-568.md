# Chapter 568

> An Act Granting a pension to George Brown.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of George Brown, late of Company G, Tenth United States Cavalry, and Company M, Ninth United States Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

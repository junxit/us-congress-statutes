# Chapter 513

> An Act For the relief of James A. Stoddard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to correct the military record of James A. Stoddard, late corporal in Company B, Eighth Wisconsin Volunteer Infantry, by removing the charge of desertion therefrom and granting him an honorable discharge: Provided, That no pay, bounty, or other emoluments shall become due or payable by virtue of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 27, 1898.*

# Chapter 428

> An Act Granting a pension to Mary M. Macauley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Mary M. Macauley, widow of the late Brigadier-General Daniel Macauley, United States Volunteers, upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, June 10, 1898.*

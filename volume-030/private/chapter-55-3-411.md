# Chapter 411

> An Act To authorize Admiral T. O. Selfridge, United States Navy, Captain G. H. Wadleigh, United States Navy, Lieutenant-Commander E. H. Gheen, United States Navy, Lieutenant-Commander Raymond P. Rodgers, United States Navy, Paymaster J. B. Redfield, United States Navy, Lieutenant J. J. Hunker, United States Navy, Surgeon D. N. Bertolette, United States Navy, and Ensign R. L. Russell, United States Navy, to accept medals presented to them by the Russian Government.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Admiral T. O. Selfridge, United States Navy, Captain G. H. Wadleigh, United States Navy, Lieutenant-Commander E. H. Gheen, United States Navy, Lieutenant-Commander Raymond P. Rodgers, United States Navy, Paymaster J. B. Redfield, United States Navy, Lieutenant J. J. Hunker, United States Navy, Surgeon D. N. Bertolette, United States Navy, and Ensign R. L. Russell, United States Navy, be, and they are hereby, authorized to accept medals presented to them by the Russian Government on the occasion of the coronation of the Czar Nicholas the Second, in May, eighteen hundred and ninety-six.


*Approved, March 2, 1899.*

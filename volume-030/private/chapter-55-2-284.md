# Chapter 284

> An Act Granting a pension to Catherine Wiltse.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Catherine Wiltse, late widow of John Cummins, Company B, Fiftieth Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, May 7, 1898.*

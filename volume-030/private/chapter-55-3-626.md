# Chapter 626

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to John E. Gullett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of John E. Gullett, of Wilcox County, Alabama, a lieutenant in the Creek Indian war of eighteen hundred and thirty-six, from eight dollars to fifteen dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

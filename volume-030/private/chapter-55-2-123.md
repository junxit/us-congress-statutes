# Chapter 123

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Enoch G. Adams.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Enoch G. Adams, late captain of Company D, First Regiment United States Infantry, at the rate of fifty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, April 11, 1898.*

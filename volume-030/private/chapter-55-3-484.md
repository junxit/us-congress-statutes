# Chapter 484

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Henry Hatch.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Henry Hatch, late of Battery D, First New York Heavy Artillery, and pay him a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month in lieu of the pension now received by him.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

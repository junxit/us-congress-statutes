# Chapter 46

> An Act For the relief of Charles A. Nazro.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Charles A, Nazro, a lieutenant F and S of the Twenty-sixth Illinois Infantry Volunteers, in-the war of the rebellion, at the rate of fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, March 5, 1898.*

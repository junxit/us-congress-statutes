# Chapter 20

> An Act Granting a pension to A. A. Pinkston.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of A. A. Pinkston, widow of Franklin O. Pinkston, late of Captain Asher’s company of Alabama Volunteers in the Creek Indian war, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollar’s per month.


*Approved, December 20, 1898.*

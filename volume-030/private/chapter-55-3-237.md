# Chapter 237

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to John N. Corgan, of McPherson, Kansas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized, and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John N, Corgan, late of Company G, Fourth United States Infantry, and grant him a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 28, 1899.*

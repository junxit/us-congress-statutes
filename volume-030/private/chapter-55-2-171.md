# Chapter 171

> An Act To grant a pension to James J. Gibbs.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the limitations and conditions of the general pension laws, the name of James J. Gibbs, a private of Captain Gideon White’s company of National Guards of East Tennessee.


*Approved, April 15, 1898.*

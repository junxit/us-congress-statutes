# Chapter 260

> An Act Granting a pension to Sarah E. Daub.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Sarah E. Daub, formerly widow of Nathan Foreman, late a private in Company A, Fifty-first Pennsylvania Infantry; Company B, One hundred and eighty-third Pennsylvania Infantry, and Troop C, Second United States Cavalry, and widow of George W. Daub, late Company F, Fifty-first Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, May 7, 1898.*

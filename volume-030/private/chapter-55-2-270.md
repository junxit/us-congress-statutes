# Chapter 270

> An Act Increasing the pension of William S. Demott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of William S. Demott, late first lieutenant of Company F, One hundred and twenty-first Regiment Indiana Volunteers, and pay him a pension of fifty dollars per month, in lieu of any pension that may now be paid him.


*Approved, May 7, 1898.*

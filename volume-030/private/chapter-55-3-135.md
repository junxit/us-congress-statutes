# Chapter 135

> An Act Granting a pension to John Green.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed, in adjudicating the pension claim of John Green, late of Troop C, Fourth United States Cavalry, to accept disability as having been incurred in line of duty, and thereupon to place the name of said John Green on the pension roll, subject to all other provisions and limitations of the pension laws, such pension, when granted, to date from the approval of this Act.


*Approved, February 9, 1899.*

# Chapter 529

> An Act Granting a pension to Charles E. Mann.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Charles E. Mann, late sergeant of Company H, Second Regiment of Massachusetts Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, June 28, 1898.*

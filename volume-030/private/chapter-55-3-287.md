# Chapter 287

> An Act For the relief of Colonel George G. Pride.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George G. Pride, a volunteer aid, with rank of colonel, on the staff of General U. S. Grant, and pay to him a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, February 28, 1899.*

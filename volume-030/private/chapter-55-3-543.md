# Chapter 543

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Elam Allen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place the name of Elam Allen, late of Company E, Thirty-third Iowa Infantry Volunteers, on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

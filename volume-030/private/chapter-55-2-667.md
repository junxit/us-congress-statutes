# Chapter 667

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Annie J. Bassett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Annie J. Bassett, widow of Lieutenant-Commander Wesley W. Bassett, United States Navy, on the pension roll, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifteen dollars per month in lieu of the pension she is now receiving.


*Approved, July 8, 1898.*

# Chapter 62

> An Act To grant a pension to Emeline C. Sewell, widow of Chie; Engineer George Sewell, United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the limitations of the pension laws, the name pf Emeline C, Sewell, widow of George Sewell, late chief engineer, United States Navy, at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, March 14, 1898.*

# Chapter 271

> An Act Granting a pension to Lovezila L. Patterson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Lovezila L. Patterson, widow of Alexander V. Patterson, late surgeon of the One hundred and second Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, on the pension roll and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, May 7, 1898.*

# Chapter 312

> An Act Granting a pension to Annie Saffell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Annie Saffell, imbecile and dependent daughter of James Saffell, late of Company G, One hundred and seventy-fourth Ohio Volunteer Infantry, on the pension roll, and to pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month, same to be paid to her guardian when appointed.


*Approved, May 14, 1898.*

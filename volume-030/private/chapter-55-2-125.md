# Chapter 125

> An Act Granting a pension to Sarah E. Cotton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Sarah E. Cotton, late a hospital nurse, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month from and after the passage of this bill.


*Approved, April 11, 1898.*

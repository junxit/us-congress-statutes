# Chapter 285

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Sarah A. Luke.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Sarah A, Luke, widow of Captain John W. Luke, late of Company E, Fifteenth Regiment Illinois Infantry Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of the pension she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 28, 1899.*

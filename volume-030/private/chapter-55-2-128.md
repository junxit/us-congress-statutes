# Chapter 128

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Bernard Dunn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Bernard Dunn, late a private in Company A, Second Regiment Iowa Cavalry, and pay him a pension of twenty-four dollars per mouth, in lieu of the pension now received by him.


*Approved, April 11, 1898.*

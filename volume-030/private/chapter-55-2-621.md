# Chapter 621

> An Act Granting a pension to Ellen E. Nash.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Ellen E. Nash, who was an army nurse during the war of the rebellion, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, July 7, 1898.*

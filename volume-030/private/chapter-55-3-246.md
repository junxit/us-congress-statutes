# Chapter 246

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Emma V. Gile.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Emma V. Gile, widow of the late George W. Gile, colonel Eighty-eighth Regiment Pennsylvania Infantry, and brevet brigadier-general volunteers, and colonel, United States Army, retired, at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month, to be paid to her from and after the passage of this Act, in lieu of the pension which she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 28, 1899.*

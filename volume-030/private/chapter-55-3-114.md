# Chapter 114

> An Act Granting a pension to George B. Stone.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of George B. Stone, late of Company C, Fifty-sixth Enrolled Missouri Militia, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 4, 1899.*

# Chapter 179

> An Act Granting a pension to Laura A. Barry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension rolls the name of Laura A. Barry, as widow of Charles W, Barry, late private, Company B, One hundred and eighth New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, April 15, 1898.*

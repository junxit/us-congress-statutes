# Chapter 273

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Bolivar J. Pridgen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and instructed to place, the name of Bolivar J. Pridgen, an invalid Mexican war veteran, on the pension roll at the rate of twenty dollars per month, the same to be in lieu of the pension now drawn by him.

## § 2.

That this Act shall take effect from and after its passage.


*Approved, May 7, 1898.*

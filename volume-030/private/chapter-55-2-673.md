# Chapter 673

> An Act Granting a pension to Addie L. Ballou.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Addie L. Ballou, an army nurse, and to pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, July 8, 1898.*

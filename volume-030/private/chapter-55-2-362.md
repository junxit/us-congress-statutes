# Chapter 362

> An Act For the relief of Samuel McKee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Samuel McKee the sum of one thousand seven hundred and eighteen dollars, for expenses incurred by him in a contest for his seat in the Fortieth Congress.


*Approved, May 24, 1898.*

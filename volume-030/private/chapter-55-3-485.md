# Chapter 485

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Annie E. Ruff.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Annie E. Ruff, widow of Charles F. Ruff, late lieutenant-colonel Third United States Cavalry, and pay to her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension which she now receives.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

# Chapter 491

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Bartlett Corniff.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Bartlett Corniff, late of Company B, Nineteenth Regiment Kentucky Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

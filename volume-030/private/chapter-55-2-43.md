# Chapter 43

> An Act Granting a pension to Mrs. Martha Frank.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the regulations and provisions of law governing the payment of pensions, the name of Mrs. Martha Frank, widow of William Frank, member of the company of Captain John Little in the Indian war of eighteen hundred and eighteen, and allow her a pension rated at eight dollars per month.


*Approved, March 5, 1898.*

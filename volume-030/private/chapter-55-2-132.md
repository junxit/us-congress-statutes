# Chapter 132

> An Act Increasing the pension of Mary E. Wallick.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary E. Wallick, widow of William Wallick, late captain of Company G, Fifty-first Regiment Indiana Volunteer Infantry, and pay to her the sum of twenty dollars per month in lieu of the pension she is now receiving.


*Approved, April 11, 1898.*

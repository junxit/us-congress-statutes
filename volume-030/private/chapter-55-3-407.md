# Chapter 407

> An Act To remove the charge of desertion against William Britton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to remove the charge of desertion now standing against the record of William Britton, of Company A, Fifty-eighth Pennsylvania Volunteers, and to grant him an honorable discharge as of August fourth, eighteen hundred and sixty-four: Provided, That no pay, allowance, or emoluments shall become due by virtue of this Act subsequent to August fourth, eighteen hundred and sixty-four.


*Approved, March 2, 1899.*

# Chapter 300

> An Act Granting a pension to W. L. Faxon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of W. L. Faxon, of Middleboro, Massachusetts, late a surgeon, Thirty-second Regiment Massachusetts Volunteers, and that he receive a pension of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, May 14, 1898.*

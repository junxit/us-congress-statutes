# Chapter 225

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Moses M. Grants.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Moses M. Grants. late a private in Company G, One hundred and seventh Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension of twenty-four dollars he is now receiving, to take effect from and after the passage of this Act.


*Approved, April 28, 1898.*

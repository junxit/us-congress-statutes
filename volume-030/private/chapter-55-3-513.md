# Chapter 513

> An Act To authorize Victor Vifquain, colonel Third Nebraska Volunteer Infantry, to accept the decoration of the “Order of the Double Dragon” from the Emperor of China.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Victor Vifquain, colonel Third Nebraska Volunteer Infantry, be, and he is hereby, authorized to accept from the Emperor of China the decoration of the “Order of the Double Dragon,” in recognition of the valuable services rendered by him to the Chinese residents of the Republic of Colombia during his term of office as consul-general at Panama.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

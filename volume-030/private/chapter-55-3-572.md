# Chapter 572

> An Act Granting a pension to Samuel B. Koontz.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Samuel B. Koontz, and pay him a pension at the rate of fifteen dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

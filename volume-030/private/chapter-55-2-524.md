# Chapter 524

> An Act To pension Mrs. Susan M. Sessford.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Susan M. Sessford, the dependent mother of Thomas J. Martin, late of Company D, Second Battalion District of Columbia Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, June 28, 1898.*

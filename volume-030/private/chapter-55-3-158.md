# Chapter 158

> An Act To remove the charge of desertion standing against Dennis Fitzpatrick.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized to amend the records on file in the Navy Department so as to show that Dennis Fitzpatrick was honorably discharged as a seaman in the United States Navy from the United States steamer Wanderer, and to issue to him an honorable discharge, to date from the seventeenth day of July, eighteen hundred and sixty-three.


*Approved, February 15, 1899.*

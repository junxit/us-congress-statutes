# Chapter 274

> An Act Granting a pension to Elizabeth Holt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and regulations of the general pension laws, the name of Elizabeth Holt, of Topeka, Kansas, the stepmother of L. B. Holt, who was a private in Company L, Fifteenth Pennsylvania Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, May 7, 1898.*

# Chapter 222

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Sarah Brumm.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah Brumm, widow of George W. Brumm, late major Fiftieth Pennsylvania Infantry Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension she is now receiving.


*Approved, April 28, 1898.*

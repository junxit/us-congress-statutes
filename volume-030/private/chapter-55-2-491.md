# Chapter 491

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Catherine Clifford.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Catherine Clifford, widow of James Clifford, Twenty-fifth Battery Indiana Volunteer Light Artillery, on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month in lien of the pension which she is now receiving.


*Approved, June 22, 1898.*

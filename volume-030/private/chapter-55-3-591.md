# Chapter 591

> An Act Removing the charge of desertion from the record of W. H. Sherwood, Company F, Thirteenth Ohio Cavalry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the charge of desertion now standing against W. H. Sherwood on the rolls of Company F, Thirteenth Ohio Cavalry, be, and the same is hereby, removed.

That no pay or allowance shall become due by reason of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

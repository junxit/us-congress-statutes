# Chapter 374

> An Act For the relief of the legal representatives of John Roach, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That to carry out the provisions of the Act making appropriations for the naval service for the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, eighteen hundred and eighty-four (Twenty-second Statutes, page four hundred and seventy-seven), to pay to the legal representatives of John Roach, deceased, the sum of twenty-eight thousand one hundred and sixty dollars and twenty-five cents, for labor and material furnished by the said John Roach in completing the dispatch boat Dolphin, under the advice and assistance of the naval advisory board mentioned in said Act, which amount is hereby appropriated, out of any moneys in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 3, 1898.*

# Chapter 135

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to John N. Bruce.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John N. Bruce, late captain of Company E, Fourteenth Regiment New Hampshire Volunteers, and pay him a pension of fifty dollars per month, in lieu of that be is now receiving.


*Approved, April 11, 1898.*

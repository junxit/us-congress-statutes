# Chapter 302

> An Act To increase the pension of Michael Meehan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Michael Meehan, late private, Company A, One hundred and seventh New York Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lien of the pension be is now receiving.


*Approved, February 28, 1899.*

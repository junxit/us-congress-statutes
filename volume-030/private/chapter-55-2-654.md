# Chapter 654

> An Act Granting a pension to Olive H. South.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Olive H. South, late an army nurse, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, July 8, 1898.*

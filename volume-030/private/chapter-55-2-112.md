# Chapter 112

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Orlando J. Hopkins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Orlando J. Hopkins, late first lieutenant Company A, Fifty-ninth Ohio Infantry, and captain and commissary of subsistence, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month, in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 31, 1898.*

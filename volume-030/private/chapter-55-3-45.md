# Chapter 45

> An Act To remove the charge of desertion against Jacob N. Hamburger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to remove charge of desertion standing against the name of Jacob N. Hamburger, late musician in Company G, First Battalion, Twelfth United States Infantry, and issue him an honorable discharge: Provided, That no pay, bounty, or other emoluments shall become due or payable by virtue of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, January 10, 1899.*

# Chapter 382

> An Act Granting a pension to George Barnes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of George Barnes of Company I, Twenty-fifth Missouri Enrolled Militia, and Company C, Eighty-seventh Missouri Enrolled Militia, on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, and pay him a pension of eighteen dollars per month.


*Approved, June 4, 1898.*

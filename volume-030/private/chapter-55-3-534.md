# Chapter 534

> An Act To increase the pension of Delos M. Kenyon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Delos M. Kenyon, late first lieutenant Company D, One hundred and twenty-first New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

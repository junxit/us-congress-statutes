# Chapter 59

> An Act To remove the charge of desertion standing against the name of Patrick Dougherty, Company A, Thirteenth New York Volunteer Infantry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to remove the charge of desertion standing against the name of Patrick Dougherty, late a private in Company A, Thirteenth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and to issue to him an honorable discharge as of date of twenty-seventh day of April, eighteen hundred and sixty-three: Provided, That no allowances shall become due or payable by reason of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, January 25, 1899.*

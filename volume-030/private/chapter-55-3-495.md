# Chapter 495

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Jehiel J. Stevens.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jehiel J. Stevens, late a private in Company K, Eighteenth Regiment New York Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of forty-five dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

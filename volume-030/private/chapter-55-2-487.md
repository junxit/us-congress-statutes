# Chapter 487

> An Act To increase the pension of Samuel B. Davis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Samuel B. Davis, of Company C, Eighteenth Regiment Indiana Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, June 20, 1898.*

# Chapter 599

> An Act To correct the military record of Sylvester F. Hildebrand.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That extract numbered thirty-three of special order numbered two hundred and sixty-eight, issued by the War Department under paragraph thirteen hundred and seventy-one, referring to restrictions and penalties, be, and the same is hereby, set aside, and that Sylvester F. Hildebrand be given an honorable discharge as of date June seventh, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, by reason of expiration of service as a private in Company E, One hundred and thirty-ninth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers, instead of the discharge issued to him June seventh, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, under said special order, and that he be paid all pay and allowances retained from him on his discharge by reason of the forfeiture specified in paragraph thirteen hundred and seventy-one, Revised Army Regulations of eighteen hundred and sixty-three.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

# Chapter 570

> An Act For the relief of Martha E. Fleschert.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Martha Elizabeth Fleschert, née Stevenson, of Saint Louis, Missouri, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of two hundred and twelve dollars and fifty cents, for services rendered by her as hospital matron in and for One hundred and thirtieth and One hundred and seventeenth regiments of Illinois Volunteers for seventeen months, from October, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, to March, eighteen hundred and sixty-four.


*Approved, July 5, 1898.*

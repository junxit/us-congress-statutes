# Chapter 360

> An Act To increase the pension of Franklin Hull.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Franklin Hull, late sergeant, Company D, First Connecticut Cavalry, and to pay him a pension of thirty dollars per month, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, said pension to be in lieu of that which he now receives.


*Approved, May 23, 1898.*

# Chapter 539

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Mazie V. Sullivan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mazie V. Sullivan, widow of George R. Sullivan, late of Company G, Eleventh Maryland Volunteer Infantry, and to pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month, in lieu of that which she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

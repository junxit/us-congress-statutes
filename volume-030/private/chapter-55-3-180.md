# Chapter 180

> An Act For the relief of the legal representatives of Edwin De Leon, deceased, late consul-general of the United States in Egypt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of four hundred and seventy-nine dollars and eleven cents be paid to the legal representatives of Edwin De Leon, deceased, late consul-general of the United States in Egypt, by the Secretary of the Treasury, out of any moneys not otherwise appropriated, being the balance due him on adjustment of his official accounts by the First Comptroller of the Treasury of the United States of America.


*Approved, February 21, 1899.*

# Chapter 412

> An Act Granting a pension to Bettie Gresham.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Bettie Gresham, widow of Davis C. Gresham, who served in the Georgia Volunteers during the Indian war of eighteen hundred and thirty-six, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, June 8, 1898.*

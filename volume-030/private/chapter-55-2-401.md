# Chapter 401

> An Act To construe an Act approved June third, eighteen hundred and eighty-four, rerating and including the disability of Alonzo B. Chatfield, late of Company B, Thirty-third Regiment of Illinois Volunteer Infantry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act approved June third, eighteen hundred and eighty-four, “rerating and including the disability of Alonzo B. Chatfield, late of Company B, Thirty-third Regiment of Illinois Volunteer Infantry, with that class of pensioners who have lost an arm at or above the elbow,” be construed to be continuous in its provision; and the Commissioner of Pensions is hereby instructed to reenter and continue the name of said pensioner on the roll with the class mentioned in said Act, and at the rate of pension provided for said class, from and after the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 8, 1898.*

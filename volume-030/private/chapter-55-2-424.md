# Chapter 424

> An Act Granting a pension to Amos Webster.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Brevet Lieutenant-Colonel Amos Webster, late of the First Massachusetts Infantry Volunteers, and of the staff of Lieutenant-General U. S. Grant, United States Army, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, June 10, 1898.*

# Chapter 310

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to James H. Kile.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he, is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of James H. Kile, late first lieutenant Battery D, First Tennessee Light Artillery Volunteers, on the pension roll and pay him a pension of twenty-four dollars per month, in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, May 14, 1898.*

# Chapter 218

> An Act To grant a pension to M. Louise Anderson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of M. Louise Anderson, widow of John B. Anderson, late of Company K, Seventeenth Regiment Michigan Infantry, at the rate of twenty-four dollars a month.


*Approved, April 27, 1898.*

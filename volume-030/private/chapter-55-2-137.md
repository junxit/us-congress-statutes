# Chapter 137

> An Act Granting a pension to Mary J. Hart.

*Be it enacted, by the Semite and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Mary J. Hart, widow of Abel Pearson, late member of Company A, Seventy-ninth Regiment Indiana Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, April 11, 1898.*

# Chapter 97

> An Act To correct the naval record of and grant an honorable discharge to Thomas H. Holden, of Pawtucket, Rhode Island.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized to correct the naval record of and grant an honorable discharge to Thomas H. Holden, late a landsman on the United States steamship Sabine and now or late a resident of Pawtucket, Rhode Island.


*Approved, March 23, 1898.*

# Chapter 69

> An Act Granting a pension to Herman Dellit.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, at the rate of eight dollars per month, subject otherwise to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws, the name of Herman Debit, late of Company G, Eighth United States Infantry.


*Approved, January 28, 1899.*

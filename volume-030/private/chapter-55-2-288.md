# Chapter 288

> An Act To repeal chapter one hundred and sixty-four, laws of eighteen hundred and seventy-one, approved March third, eighteen hundred and seventy- one, being an Act entitled “An Act grunting a pension to Hiram R. Rhea.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That chapter one hundred and sixty-four of the laws of eighteen hundred and seventy-one, approved March third, eighteen hundred and seventy-one (Sixteenth United States Statutes at Large), and entitled “An Act granting a pension t Hiram R. Rhea,” be, and the same is hereby, repealed.


*Approved, May 7, 1898.*

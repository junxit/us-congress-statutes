# Chapter 296

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Lemon Holton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and. House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and instructed to place the name of Lemon Holton, late of Company D, Tenth United States Infantry, Mexican war, on the pension roll at the rate of twenty dollars per month, the same to be in lieu of the pension now drawn by him.


*Approved, February 28, 1899.*

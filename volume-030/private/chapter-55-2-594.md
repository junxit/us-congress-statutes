# Chapter 594

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Henry Hinckley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Henry Hinckley, late of Company G, Fifth Volunteer Infantry of Vermont, at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of the pension now received.


*Approved, July 7, 1898.*

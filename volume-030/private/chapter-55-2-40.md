# Chapter 40

> An Act To increase the pension of Esther Brown.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Esther Grown, widow of James Brown, late a captain in Company C, Mormon Battalion Volunteers, Mexican war, at the rate of twelve dollars per month, in lieu of the pension she now receives.


*Approved, March 5, 1898.*

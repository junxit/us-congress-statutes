# Chapter 550

> An Act Increasing the pension of James Webb.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of James Webb, late a private of Company F, Ninth Regiment of Tennessee Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of forty dollars a month in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

# Chapter 551

> An Act To increase the pension of Annie Cusack.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Annie Cusack, widow of Patrick Cusack, late captain, Ninth Cavalry, United States Army, on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that which she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

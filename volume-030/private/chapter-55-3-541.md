# Chapter 541

> An Act To increase the pension of George Alexander.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the general pension laws, the name of George Alexander, late of Company G, First Regiment Michigan Volunteer Cavalry, and pay to him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of the pension now received by him.


*Approved, March 3, 1899.*

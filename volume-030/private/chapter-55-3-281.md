# Chapter 281

> An Act Granting a pension to Rachel J. Comer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Rachel J. Comer, widow of Thomas Comer, late of Company A, Seventy-second Regiment of Indiana Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 28, 1899.*

# Chapter 83

> An Act For the relief of David O. Burleigh.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That David O. Burleigh shall be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a captain of Company I, Fourth New Hampshire Infantry Volunteers, on the fourth day of June, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, and that the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to revoke the order cashiering the said Burleigh, and to issue to him a certificate of honorable discharge as of said date: Provided, That no pay or allowances shall become due and payable by reason of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 21, 1898.*

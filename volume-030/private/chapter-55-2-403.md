# Chapter 403

> An Act Granting a pension to Zolman Tyrrell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Zolman Tyrrell, idiotic and dependent son of Lewis R. Tyrrell, late Company A, First New York Engineers, on the pension roll and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month to be paid to a duly appointed guardian.


*Approved, June 8, 1898.*

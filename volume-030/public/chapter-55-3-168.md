# Chapter 168

> An Act To locate the office of the deputy collector of the port of East Pascagoula, Mississippi, at Scranton, Mississippi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the office of the deputy collector of the port of East Pascagoula, in the Pearl River district of the State of Mississippi, be, and the same is hereby, located at Scranton, Mississippi.


*Approved, February 20, 1899.*

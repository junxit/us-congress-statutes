# Chapter 193

> An Act To amend an Act entitled “An Act to amend an Act to grant to the Gainesville, McAlester and Saint Louis Railway Company a right of way through the Indian Territory.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of section nine of the Act entitled “An Act to grant to the Gainesville, McAlester and Saint Louis Railway Company a right of way through the Indian Territory,” approved March first, eighteen hundred and ninety-three, be, and the same are hereby, extended for a further period of three years from and after the passage of this amendment.


*Approved, February 25, 1899.*

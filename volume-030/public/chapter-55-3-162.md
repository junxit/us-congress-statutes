# Chapter 162

> An Act For a light vessel off Tail of Horse Shoe, Chesapeake Bay.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a light vessel with a light and fog signal is hereby authorized to be placed on or near the shoal known as the Tail of the Horse Shoe, in Chesapeake Bay.


*Approved, February 18, 1899.*

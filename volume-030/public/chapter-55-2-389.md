# Chapter 389

> An Act To remove the disability imposed by section three of the Fourteenth Amendment to the Constitution of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the disability imposed by section three of the Fourteenth Amendment to the Constitution of the United States heretofore incurred is hereby removed.


*Approved, June 6, 1898.*

# Chapter 226

> An Act Extending the time for the completion of bridge across Saint Lawrence River.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section four of the Act approved February ninth, eighteen hundred and ninety-three, authorizing the Saint Lawrence Railway Company, of the State of New York, to build and maintain a bridge across the Saint Lawrence River at some point in Saint Lawrence County, State of New York, as amended by the Act approved February fifteenth, eighteen hundred and ninety-six, be, and is hereby, further amended by extending the time for the completion of said bridge to February ninth, eighteen hundred and ninety-nine.


*Approved, April 29, 1898.*

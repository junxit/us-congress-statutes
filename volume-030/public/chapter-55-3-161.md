# Chapter 161

> An Act For the establishment of a light and fog-signal station on or near Hog Island Shoal, Narragansett Bay, Rhode Island.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be established on or near Hog Island Shoal, Narragansett Bay, Rhode Island, a light and fog-signal station, to take the place of the light vessel now there, at a cost not to exceed thirty-five thousand dollars.


*Approved, February 18, 1899.*

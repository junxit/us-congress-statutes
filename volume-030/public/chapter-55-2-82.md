# Chapter 82

> An Act To change name of port of collection of United States customs from Suspension Bridge to Niagara Falls.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the port for the collection of United States customs in the city of Niagara Falls, county of Niagara and State of New York, now known and designated port of Suspension Bridge, shall hereafter be known and designated port of Niagara Falls.


*Approved, March 21, 1898.*

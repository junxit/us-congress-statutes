# Chapter 154

> An Act To amend section twenty-seven of the Revised Statutes, relative to the apportionment and election of Representatives.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section twenty-seven, of the Revised Statutes of the United States of eighteen hundred and seventy-eight, be amended so as to read as follows:

> “All votes for Representatives in Congress must be by written or printed ballot, or voting machine the use of which has been duly authorized by the State law; and all votes received or recorded contrary to this section shall be of no effect.”


*Approved, February 14, 1899.*

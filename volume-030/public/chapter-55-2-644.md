# Chapter 644

> An Act Fixing pay and allowances of chaplains for volunteer regiments.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all chaplains in the volunteer service shall have the pay and allowances of a captain mounted.


*Approved, July 8, 1898.*

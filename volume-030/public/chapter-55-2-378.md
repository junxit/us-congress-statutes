# Chapter 378

> An Act Granting additional powers to railroad companies operating lines in the Indian Territory.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That it shall and may be lawful for any company operating a line of railroad, either wholly or partially, in the Indian Territory to enter into contracts for the use or lease of the railroad and other property of any railroad company whose line may now or hereafter connect with its line upon such terms as may be agreed upon by the respective companies, and to use and operate such road or roads in accordance with the terms of such contract or lease, but subject to the obligations imposed upon the respective companies by their charters or by the laws of the United States or of the State or Territory in which such leased road may be situate: Provided, That the terms of this Act shall not apply to parallel or competing lines.


*Approved, June 4, 1898.*

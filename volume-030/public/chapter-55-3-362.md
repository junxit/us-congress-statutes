# Chapter 362

> An Act Regulating the postage on letters written by the blind.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all letters written in point print or raised characters used by the blind when unsealed shall be transmitted through the mails as third-class matter. All acts or parts of acts conflicting with this Act are hereby repealed.


*Approved, March 2, 1899.*

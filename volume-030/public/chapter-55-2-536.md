# Chapter 536

> An Act To designate Gladstone, Michigan, a subport of entry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Gladstone, in the State of Michigan, be, and the same hereby is, designated a subport of entry in the customs collection district of Superior, and that the privileges of immediate transportation of dutiable merchandise without appraisement, as defined by the Act of June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, entitled “An Act to amend the statutes in relation to the immediate transportation of dutiable goods, and for other purposes,” be, and the same are hereby, extended to said subport.


*Approved, June 29, 1898.*

# Chapter 642

> An Act To increase the number of post quartermaster-sergeants in the United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the number of post quartermaster sergeants of the Army be increased by the addition of twenty-five post quartermaster-sergeants, to be appointed by the Secretary of War in the manner now provided for by law.


*Approved, July 8, 1898.*

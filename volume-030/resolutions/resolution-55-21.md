# Resolution 21

> Joint Resolution Relative to suspension of part of section three hundred and fifty-five of Revised Statutes, relative to erection of forts, fortifications, and so forth.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in case of emergency when, in the opinion of the President, the immediate erection of any temporary fort or fortification is deemed important and urgent, such temporary fort or fortification maybe constructed upon the written consent of the owner of the hind upon which such work is to be placed; and the requirements of section three hundred and fifty-five of the Revised Statutes shall not be applicable in such cases.


*Approved, April 11, 1898.*

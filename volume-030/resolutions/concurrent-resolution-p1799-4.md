# Concurrent Resolution

> duties on imports.

*Resolved by the House, of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Joint Committee on Printing be, and are hereby, authorized and directed to cause to be printed, indexed, and bound in cloth, in one volume, all of the acts, as they in the United States Statutes, heretofore passed by Congress imposing duties on imports; one thousand copies of said volume to be for the use of the Senate, and two thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives.


*Passed the House of Representatives May 13, 1898. Passed the Senate May 23, 1898.*

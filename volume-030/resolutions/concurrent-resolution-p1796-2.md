# Concurrent Resolution

> national home for disabled volunteer soldiers.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed of the Report of the Board of Managers of the National Home tor Disabled Volunteer Soldiers, in addition to the usual number, five hundred copies of the report proper, five hundred copies of the report of the Assistant Inspector-General on the State Homes, and one hundred and fifty copies of the record of members, for the use of the House.


*Passed by the House of Representatives December 13, 1897. Passed by the Senate December 17, 1897.*

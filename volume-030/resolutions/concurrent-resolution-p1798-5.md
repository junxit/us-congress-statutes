# Concurrent Resolution

> senate election cases.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed and bound in muslin in one volume three thousand copies of the Senate Miscellaneous Document Numbered Sixty seven, Fifty-second Congress, second session, being the compilation of Senate election cases from 1789 to 1893, and the appendix thereto, prepared under the Concurrent Resolution of March 21st, 1894, covering Senate election cases from 1893 to the close of the present session of Congress; one thousand five hundred copies for the use of the Senate and one thousand five hundred copies for the use of the House of Representatives.


*Passed the Senate April 27, 1898. Passed the House of Representatives April 30, 1898.*

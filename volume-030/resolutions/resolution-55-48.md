# Resolution 48

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the President in his discretion to waive the one-year suspension from promotion and to order reexamination of officers of tin Army in certain cases.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That during the existing war the President may, in his discretion, waive the one-year suspension from promotion and forthwith order the reexamination provided in certain cases by the third proviso of section three of the Act approved October first, eighteen hundred and ninety, entitled “An Act to provide for the examination of certain officers of the Army and to regulate pro motions therein.”


*Approved, June 14, 1898.*

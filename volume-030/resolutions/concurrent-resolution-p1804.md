# Concurrent Resolution

> export demand for american horses.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed twenty thousand additional copies of the special report on the export demand for American horses, for the use of the Department of Agriculture.


*Passed by the House of Representatives, January 18, 1899. Passed by the Senate, January 25, 1899.*

# Concurrent Resolution

> alaska.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the commission heretofore appointed pursuant to the Act of June 4th, 1897, to revise and codify the criminal and penal laws of the United States, be, and is hereby, directed to prepare, and through the Attorney-General, submit to Congress at the earliest practicable date a codification of the laws, other than criminal, and a code of procedure thereunder, for the District of Alaska.


*Passed by the Senate March 1, 1898. Agreed to (with an amendment) by the House of Representatives May 31, 1898. (Amendment agreed to by the Senate and incorporated in foregoing.)*

# Concurrent Resolution

> report on production of gold and silver. 1897.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed and bound in cloth, for the use of the Director of the Mint, three thousand extra copies of the Report on the Production of Gold and Silver for the calendar year eighteen hundred and ninety-seven.


*Passed by the House of Representatives, February 25, 1899. Passed the Senate February 27, 1899.*

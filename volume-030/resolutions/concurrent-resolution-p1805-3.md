# Concurrent Resolution

> report superintendent of indian schools.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed one thousand additional copies of the Report of the Superintendent of Indian Schools for eighteen hundred and ninety-eight, the same to be delivered to the Commissioner of Indian Affairs for the use of his office.


*Passed the House of Representatives, February 8, 1899. Passed by the Senate, February 9, 1899.*

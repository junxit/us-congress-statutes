# Concurrent Resolution

> holiday recess, 1898–1899.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Wednesday, the twenty-first day of December, they stand adjourned until twelve o'clock meridian on Wednesday, January fourth, eighteen hundred and ninety-nine.


*Passed by the House of Representatives, December 19, 1898. Passed by the Senate, December 20, 1898.*

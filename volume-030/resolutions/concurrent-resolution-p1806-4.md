# Concurrent Resolution

> statistical abstract, united states, 1898.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed twelve thousand additional copies of the Statistical Abstract of the United States for the year eighteen hundred and ninety-eight, prepared by the Bureau of Statistics, Treasury Department; three thousand copies for the Senate, six thousand for the House of Representatives, and three thousand for the Bureau of Statistics, Treasury Department.


*Passed by the House of Representatives, February 25, 1899. Passed the Senate February 27, 1899.*

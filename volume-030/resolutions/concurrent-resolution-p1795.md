# Concurrent Resolution

> tariff of 1897.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the two Houses twenty thousand copies of H. R. 27, now known as H. R. 379; entitled “A Bill to provide revenue for the Government and to encourage the industries of the United States,” six thousand for the use of the Senate and fourteen thousand for the use of the House.


*Passed the House of Representatives March 15, 1897. Passed the Senate March 16, 1897.*

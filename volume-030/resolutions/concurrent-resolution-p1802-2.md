# Concurrent Resolution

> bankruptcy bill.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed of Public Act Numbered 171, “An Act to establish a uniform system bankruptcy throughout the United States,” seventy-five thousand copies, fifty thousand for the use of the House of Representatives and twenty-five thousand for the use of the Senate. That such prints be of the Act with index, and so forth, as prepared by the Clerk.


*Passed the House of Representatives July 5, 1898. Passed the Senate, with the following amendment, July 6, 1898: Line 9, after “with,” insert: paper corer and Passed the House of Representatives July 7, 1898.*

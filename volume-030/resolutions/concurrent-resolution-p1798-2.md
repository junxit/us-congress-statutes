# Concurrent Resolution

> cuba.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed twenty thousand copies of the Message of the President of April 11, 1898, together with Senate Report 885, being the Report of the Committee on Foreign Relations relative to affairs in Cuba, six thousand five hundred for the use of the Senate and thirteen thousand five hundred for the use of the House of Representatives.


*Passed the Senate April 15, 1898. Passed the House of Representatives April 16, 1898.*

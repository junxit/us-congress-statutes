# Concurrent Resolution

> adjournment, 1898.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President of the Senate and the Speaker of the House of Representatives be authorized to close the present session by adjourning tiieir respective Houses on Friday, July eight, at two o’clock p. in.


*Passed the House of Representatives, July 7, 1898. Passed the Senate July 8, 1898.*

# Resolution 59

> Joint Resolution Relative to electric-lighting wires west of Rock Creek.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioners of the District of Columbia are hereby authorized to issue permits to existing electric light companies in the District of Columbia for the extension of existing overhead electric wires outside the fire limits and west of Rock Creek to be used for lighting purposes only.


*Approved, July 8, 1898.*

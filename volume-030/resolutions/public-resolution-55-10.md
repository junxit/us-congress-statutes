# Public Resolution 10

> Joint Resolution Joint Resolution Providing for the expenses of the Congress of the Universal Postal Union.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of fifty thousand dollars be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any moneys in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to provide for the accommodation and entertainment of the Congress of the Universal Postal Union, to assemble in the City of Washington on the first Wednesday in May, eighteen hundred and ninety-seven, said sum to be expended under the authority of the Postmaster-General.


*Approved, May 7, 1897.*

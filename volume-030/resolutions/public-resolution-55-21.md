# Public Resolution 21

> Joint Resolution Requesting the President to make investigation into the “Regie Contract” system.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is hereby requested to make such investigations as will elicit all the facts in reference to the restrictions put upon the sale of American tobacco in foreign countries under what is known as “regie contracts” and otherwise, and to enter into negotiations with the government of those countries with a view to obtain a modification or removal of such restrictions.


*Approved, July 24, 1897.*

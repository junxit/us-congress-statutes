# Concurrent Resolution

> big sandy river.

*Resolved by the House of Representative (the Senate concurring),*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to prepare and submit more complete plans and estimates for the improvement of the Big Sandy, and the Tug and Louisa forks of same, in Kentucky and West Virginia, with probable cost of same; and also to submit to Congress information as to whether this proposed extended improvement should be made.


*Passed the House of Representatives April 13, 1898. Passed the Senate April 14, 1898.*

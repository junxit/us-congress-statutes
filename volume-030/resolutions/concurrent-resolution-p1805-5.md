# Concurrent Resolution

> return of bill to senate.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That, the President be requested to return to the Senate the bill of the House of Representatives, No. 4838, “granting an increase of pension to Elizabeth V. Litzenbery.”


*Passed the Senate, February 23, 1899. Passed the House of Representatives, February 23, 1899.*

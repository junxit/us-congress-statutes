# Concurrent Resolution

> commercial relations, 1898.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Public Printer be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to print for distribution by the Department of State, five thousand copies of Commercial Relations, eighteen hundred and ninety-eight, and (in separate form) ten thousand copies of the Review of the World’s Commerce, and so forth, being part of said Commercial Relations.


*Passed by the House of Representatives, February 25, 1899. Passed the Senate February 27, 1899.*

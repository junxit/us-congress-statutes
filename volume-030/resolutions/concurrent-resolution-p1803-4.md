# Concurrent Resolution

> cape porpoise, maine.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Secretary of War be directed to submit an estimate of the probable cost of improving the harbor of Cape Porpoise, Maine, in accordance with the recommendations contained in the report of Colonel P. C. Hains, dated October twenty sixth, eighteen hundred and ninety-four, now on file at the War Department.


*Passed by the House of Representatives, December 20, 1898. Passed by the Senate, December 21, 1898.*

# Public Resolution 12

> Joint Resolution To furnish the Congressional Record to the Library of Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Public Printer be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to supply to the Library of Congress six copies of the daily Congressional Record, for use in the following departments: Librarian’s office, reading room, Senators’ reading room, Representatives’ reading room.


*Approved, January 28, 1899.*

# Concurrent Resolution

> proceedings national fishery congress.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed three thousand copies of the Proceedings and Papers of the National Fishery Congress, held at Tampa, Florida, January nineteenth to twenty-fourth, eighteen hundred and ninety-eight, as contained in the Bulletin of the United States Fish Commission, volume seventeen, for eighteen hundred and ninety-seven (Document Num bered five hundred and sixty-one, Fifty-fifth Congress, second session), of which five hundred shall be for the use of the Senate, one thousand for the use of the House of Representatives, and one thousand five hundred for the use of the Fish Commission.


*Passed by the Senate, December 20, 1898. Passed by the House of Representatives, February 7, 1899.*

# Concurrent Resolution

> volunteer soldiers’ home.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed of the Report of the Board of Managers of the National Home for the Disabled Volunteer Soldiers, in addition to the usual number, five hundred copies for the use of the Home.


*Passed by the House of Representatives December 13, 1898. Passed by the Senate March 2, 1899.*

# Concurrent Resolution

> cleveland, ohio.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to prepare and submit estimates of the, cost of dredging Cleveland Harbor in accordance with the recommendations of Colonel Jared A. Smith, local engineer, in his report for the year eighteen hundred and ninety-eight.


*Passed by the House of Representatives, December 20, 1898. Passed by the Senate, December 21, 1898.*

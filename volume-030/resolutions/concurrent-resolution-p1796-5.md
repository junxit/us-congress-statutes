# Concurrent Resolution

> paris exposition.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed five thousand additional copies of the report of the Special Commissioner of the United States to the International Exposition to be held at Paris, of which five hundred copies shall be for the use of the Senate, fifteen hundred copies for the use of the House of Representatives, and three thousand copies for distribution by the Third Assistant Secretary of State.


*Passed the House of Representatives February 9, 1898. Passed the Senate March 7, 1898.*

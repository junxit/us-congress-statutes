# Concurrent Resolution

> steamboat inspection service.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Treasury Department, Steamboat Inspection Service, three hundred copies each of the proceedings of the Board of Supervising Inspectors of Steam Vessels for the years eighteen hundred and ninety-seven and eighteen hundred and ninety eight.


*Passed the House of Representatives May 12, 1898. Passed the Senate May 16, 1898.*

# Concurrent Resolution

> cuba.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed 29,900 copies of the report of the Committee on Foreign Relations of the United States Senate relative to affairs in Cuba, including the Message of the President of the United States, the correspondence, and the testimony, and reports of consuls, (1,509 for the use of the Senate and 13,590 for the use of the House of Representatives.


*Passed the Senate April 19, 1898. Passed the House of Representatives April 21, 1898.*

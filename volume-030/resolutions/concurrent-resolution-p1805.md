# Concurrent Resolution

> return of bill to senate.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate the bill of the Senate numbered 569, “Granting an increase of pension to Clarinda S. Hillman.”


*Passed by the Senate, February 4, 1899. Passed by the House of Representatives, February 6, 1899.*

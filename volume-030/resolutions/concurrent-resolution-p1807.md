# Concurrent Resolution

> return of bill s. 3466 to senate.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate the bill S. 3466, entitled “An Act granting a pension to Sarah Hamilton.”


*Passed by the Senate March 2, 1899. Passed by the House of Representatives March 2, 1899.*

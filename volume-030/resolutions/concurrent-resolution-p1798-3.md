# Concurrent Resolution

> destruction of united states battle ship maine.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed twenty thousand copies of the message of the Presidentof the United States, dated March twenty-eighth, eighteen hundred and ninety-eight, together with the report of the naval court of inquiry upon the destruction of the battle ship Maine in Havana harbor, February fifteenth, eighteen hundred and ninety-eight, and the testimony taken before the court: six thousand five hundred copies for the use of the Senate, and thirteen thousand five hundred for the use of the House of Representatives.


*Passed the Senate April 19, 1898. Passed the House of Representatives April 21, 1898.*

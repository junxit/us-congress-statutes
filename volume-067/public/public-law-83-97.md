# Public Law 97

> AN ACT To amend the Act of January 12, 1951, as amended, to continue in effect the provisions of title II of the First War Powers Act, 1941.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2 of the Act of January 12, 1951 (Public Law 921, Eighty-first Congress) as amended (Public Law 426, Eighty-second Congress), is further amended by deleting the date “June 30, 1953” and inserting in lieu thereof the date “June 30, 1954”.


*Approved June 30, 1953.*

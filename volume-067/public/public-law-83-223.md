# Public Law 223

> AN ACT To amend the National Science Foundation Act of 1950.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (a) of section 16 of the National Science Foundation Act of 1950 is amended by striking out “not to exceed $500,000 for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1951, and not to exceed $15,000,000 for each fiscal year thereafter” and inserting in lieu thereof “such sums as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act”.


*Approved August 8, 1953.*

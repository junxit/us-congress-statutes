# Public Law 20

> AN ACT To extend until July 1, 1955, the period during which personal and household effects brought into the United States under Government orders shall be exempt from duty.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first sentence of section 2 of the Act of June 27, 1942, entitled “An Act to exempt from duty personal and household effects brought into the United States under Government orders” (U. S. C., title 50 App., sec. 802), is hereby amended to it ad as follows: “This Act shall be effective with respect to articles entered for consumption or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption on or after December 8, 1941, and before July 1, 1955.”

## § 2.

Paragraph (18) of subsection (a) of the first section of the Emergency Powers Continuation Act (Public Law 450, Eighty-second Congress) is hereby repealed.


*Approved April 4, 1953.*

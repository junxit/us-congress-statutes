# Public Law 29

> AN ACT To amend the Act entitled “An Act to incorporate the Roosevelt Memorial Association”, approved May 31, 1920, so as to change the name of such Association to “Theodore Roosevelt Association”, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2 of the Act entitled “An Act to incorporate the Roosevelt Memorial Association”, approved May 31, 1920 (41 Stat. 691), is hereby amended by striking out “Roosevelt Memorial Association” and inserting in lieu thereof “Theodore Roosevelt Association”.

## § 2.

Any Saw heretofore enacted by the Congress and now in effect which refers to said Roosevelt Memorial Association shall hereafter be deemed to refer to such Association by its new name, Theodore Roosevelt Association.


*Approved May 21, 1953.*

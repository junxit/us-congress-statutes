# Public Law 151

> AN ACT To extend the duration of the Hospital Survey and Construction Act (title VI of the Public Health Service Act).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first sentence of section 621 of the Public Health Service Act, as amended, is amended by striking out the word “five” and inserting in lieu thereof the word “seven”.


*Approved July 27, 1953.*

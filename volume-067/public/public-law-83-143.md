# Public Law 143

> JOINT RESOLUTION To provide for the appointment of Owen Josephus Roberts as a member of the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian institution.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the vacancy in the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution, of the class other than Members of Congress, caused by the death or Roland S. Morris, be filled by the appointment of Owen Josephus Roberts, a citizen of the State of Pennsylvania, for the statutory term of six years.


*Approved July 23, 1953.*

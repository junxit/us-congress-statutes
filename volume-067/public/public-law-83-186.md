# Public Law 186

> AN ACT To amend the District of Columbia Teachers’ Leave Act of 1949.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 6 of the District of Columbia Teachers’ Leave Act of 1949, approved October 13, 1949 (63 Stat. 842), as amended, is amended by inserting after the word “pay” the words “or on leave without pay”.

## § 2.

This Act shall become effective as of July 1, 1949.


*Approved August 5, 1953.*

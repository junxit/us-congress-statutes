# Public Law 43

> AN ACT To amend the Act which incorporated the Veterans of Foreign Wars of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first section of the Act entitled “An Act to incorporate the Veterans of Foreign Wars of the United States”, approved May 28, 1936 (36 U. S. C., sec. 111), is amended by striking out “and marines” and inserting in lieu thereof “marines, and airmen”.

## § 2.

Section 5 of such Act (36 U. S. C., sec. 115) is amended by striking out “Army, Navy, or Marine Corps” and inserting in lieu thereof “Armed Forces”.


*Approved May 29, 1953.*

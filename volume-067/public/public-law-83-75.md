# Public Law 75

> AN ACT Authorizing the appropriation of funds co provide for the prosecution of projects in the Columbia River Basin for flood control and other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph (b) under the title “Columbia River Basin” in section 204 of the Flood Control Act, approved May 17, 1950, is hereby amended by striking out “$75,000,000” and substituting in lieu thereof “$150,000,000”.


*Approved June 22, 1953.*

# Public Law 237

> AN ACT To amend section 509 of title V of the Agricultural Act of 1949, to extend for two years the period during which agricultural workers may be made available for employment under such title.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 509 of title V of the Agricultural Act of 1949, as amended, is amended by striking out “December 31, 1953” and inserting in lieu thereof “December 31, 1955”.


*Approved August 8, 1953.*

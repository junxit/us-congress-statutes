# Public Law 125

> AN ACT To extend until December 31, 1953, the period with respect to which the excess profits tax shall be effective.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 430 (a), 430 (c), and 432 (b) of the Internal Revenue Code are amended, effective with respect to taxable years ending after June 30, 1953, by striking “June 30, 1953,” and “July 1, 1953, wherever such dates appear, and inserting in lieu thereof “December 31, 1953,” and “January 1, 1954,” respectively.


*Approved July 16, 1953.*

# Public Law 111

> AN ACT To authorize the use of the Sackets Harbor Military Cemetery for the burial of war and peacetime veterans of the Armed Forces of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Sackets Harbor Military Cemetery, which was conveyed to the village of Sackets Harbor by the Secretary of the Army, under authority of the Act entitled “An Act to preserve historic graveyards in abandoned military posts”, approved July 1, 1947, be used for the burial of persons who have served in the Armed Forces of the United States.


*Approved July 13, 1953.*

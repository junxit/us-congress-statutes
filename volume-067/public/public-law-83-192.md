# Public Law 192

> AN ACT To make the provisions of section 1362 of title 18 of the United States Code, relating to injury to or interference with communications systems operated or controlled by the United States, applicable to and within the Canal Zone.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 14 of title 18 of the United States Code is hereby amended by inserting “1362,” after “1301,”.


*Approved August 5, 1953.*

# Public Law 199

> JOINT RESOLUTION To establish the date of the second regular session of the Eighty-third Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second regular session of the Eighty-third Congress shall begin at noon on Wednesday, January 6, 1954.


*Approved August 5, 1953.*

# Public Law 66

> JOINT RESOLUTION To amend the Act of July 1, 1947 (61 Stat. 242), as amended.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first section of the Act authorizing the erection of a memorial to the Marine Corps dead, approved July 1, 1947 (61 Stat. 242), as amended, is amended by inserting after the words “District of Columbia” the words “or immediate vicinity”.


*Approved June 16, 1953.*

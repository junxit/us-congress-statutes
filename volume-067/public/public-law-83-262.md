# Public Law 262

> AN ACT To amend section 9 (h) of the Atomic Energy Act of 1946 relating to the exemption of activities of the Atomic Energy Commission from State and local taxation.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 9 (b) of the Atomic Energy Act of 1946 is amended by striking out the last sentence thereof.

## § 2. This amendment shall be effective only as to tax liabilities which accrue on or after October 1, 1953.


*Approved August 13, 1953.*

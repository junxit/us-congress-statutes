# Public Law 245

> AN ACT To amend the Federal Property and Administrative Services Act of 1949 to extend until June 8, 1954, the period during which the General Services Administration may conduct negotiated sales of surplus property.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 203 (e) of the Federal Property and Administrative Services Act of 1949, us amended (40 U. S. 0., sec. 484 (e)), is amended by striking out “June 30, 1953” and inserting in lieu thereof “June 30, 1954”.


*Approved August 8, 1953.*

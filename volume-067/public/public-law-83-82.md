# Public Law 82

> AN ACT To amend the Act of October 11, 1951, authorizing the President to proclaim regulations for preventing collisions at sea. and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That rule 9 (e) of section 6 of the Act of October 11, 1951 (65 Stat. 406), is amended by striking out the word “traveling” in the first line and inserting in lieu thereof the word “trawling”.

## § 2.

Rule 11 (c) of section 6 of the Act of October II, 1951 (65 Stat. 406), is amended by striking out the word “been” in the second line and inserting in lieu thereof the word “be”.


*Approved June 26, 1953.*

# Public Law 8

> AN ACT To amend the Dependents Assistance Act of 1950 to continue in effect certain of the provisions thereof.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 16 of the Dependents Assistance Act of 1950 (Public Law 771, Eighty-first Congress) is amended by deleting the date “April 30, 1953” and inserting in lieu thereof the date “July 1, 1955”.


*Approved March 23, 1953.*

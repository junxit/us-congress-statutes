# Public Law 26

> AN ACT To abolish the United States Commission for the construction of a Washington-Lincoln Memorial Gettysburg Boulevard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the joint resolution entitled “Joint resolution for the establishment of a commission for the construction of a Washington-Lincoln Memorial Gettysburg Boulevard connecting the present Lincoln Memorial in the city of Washington with the battlefield of Gettysburg in the State of Pennsylvania”, approved May 20, 1935 (49 Stat. 285), is hereby repealed.


*Approved May 13, 1953.*

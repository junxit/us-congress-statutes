# House Concurrent Resolution 1

> INAUGURATION OF PRESIDENT-ELECT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That effective from January 3, 1953, the joint, committee created by Senate Concurrent Resolution 69, of the Eighty-second Congress, to make the necessary arrangements for the inauguration of the President-elect of the United States on the twentieth day of January 1953, is hereby continued and for such purpose shall have the same power and authority as that conferred by such Senate Concurrent Resolution 69, of the Eighty-second Congress.


*Passed January 9, 1953.*

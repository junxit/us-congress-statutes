# Senate Concurrent Resolution 24

> “OUR AMERICAN GOVERNMENT”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Joint Committee on Printing is hereby authorized and directed to revise, by bringing up to date, the pamphlet entitled “Our American Government” as set out in House Document 465, Seventy-ninth Congress.

## § 2.

Such revised pamphlet shall be printed as a Senate Document, and there be printed five hundred thousand additional copies of which thirty thousand copies shall be for the use of the House of Representatives; thirty-one hundred for the Senate Document Room; and six thousand for the House Document Room.


*Agreed to May 18, 1953.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 90

> ADJOURNMENT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Thursday, April 2, 1953, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Monday, April 13, 1953.


*Passed April 1, 1953.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 183

> GENERAL REVENGE REVISION

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed four thousand additional copies of the hearings on general revenue revision held by the Committee on Ways and Means for the use of the said Committee.


*Passed August 3, 1953.*

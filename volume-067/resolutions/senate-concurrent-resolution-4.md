# Senate Concurrent Resolution 4

> REVEREND PETER MARSHALL

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed and bound six thousand additional copies of Senate Document Numbered 86 of the Eighty-first Congress, first session, the Prayers Offered by the Chaplain, the Reverend Peter Marshall, Doctor of Divinity, at the Opening of the Daily Sessions of the Senate of the United States During the Eightieth and Eighty-first Congresses, 1947–1949; of which one thousand nine hundred and fifty copies shall be for the use of the Senate and four thousand and fifty copies for the use of the House of Representatives.


*Agreed to February 25, 1953.*

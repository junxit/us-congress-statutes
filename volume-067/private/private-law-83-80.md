# Private Law 80

> AN ACT For the relief of Keiko Tashiro.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Keiko Tashiro, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Juro and Shizuko Yoshioka, citizens of the United States.


*Approved July 16, 1953.*

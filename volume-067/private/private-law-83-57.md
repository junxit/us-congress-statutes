# Private Law 57

> AN ACT For the relief of Marjorie Goon (Goon Mei Chee).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Marjorie Goon, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Emily Goon, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved June 16, 1953.*

# Private Law 213

> AN ACT For the relief of Philip Jack Sager (Koichi Sasaki).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Philip Jack Sager (Koichi Sasaki) shall he held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Jack Sager, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 13, 1953.*

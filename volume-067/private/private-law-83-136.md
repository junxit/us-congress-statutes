# Private Law 136

> AN ACT For the relief of Richard H. Backus.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to credit the accounts of Richard H. Backus, of Fernow Hall, Cornell University, Ithaca, New York, with the sum of $313.12, representing the value of Government property lost, for which he was held accountable, while in his custody as first lieutenant, Army Air Force.


*Approved August 7, 1953.*

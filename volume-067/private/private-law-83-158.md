# Private Law 158

> AN ACT For the relief of Irene Ezitis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Irene Ezitis shall be held and considered to be the minor child of her mother, Mrs. Ilga Ezitis, a lawful permanent resident of the United States.


*Approved August 12, 1953.*

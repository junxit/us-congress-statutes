# Private Law 159

> AN ACT For the relief of Giuseppe Orsi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Giuseppe Orsi, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Celestino Orsi, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 12, 1953.*

# Private Law 187

> AN ACT For the relief of Rose Maria Gradelone Calicchlo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Rose Maria Gradelone Calicchio, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Michael Calicchio, citizens of the United States.


*Approved August 13, 1953.*

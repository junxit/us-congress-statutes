# Private Law 85

> AN ACT For the relief of Angelo Gurisetti Podesta.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Angelo Gurisetti Podesta, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. John Podesta, citizens of the United States.


*Approved July 16, 1953.*

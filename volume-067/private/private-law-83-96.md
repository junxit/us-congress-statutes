# Private Law 96

> AN ACT For the relief of Marinella Taletti.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Marinella Taletti, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Sergeant and Mrs. Thomas A. Douglas, citizens of the United States.


*Approved July 28, 1953.*

# Private Law 73

> AN ACT For the relief of Harry Block.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of Internal Revenue is hereby authorized and directed to execute a quitclaim deed to Harry Block, covering the premises located at 111 Conselyea Street, Brooklyn, New York.


*Approved June 22, 1953.*

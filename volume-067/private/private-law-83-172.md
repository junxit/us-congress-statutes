# Private Law 172

> AN ACT For the relief of Kim Jung Soo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Kim Jung Soo, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Sergeant and Mrs. Joseph W. Monahan, citizens of the United States.


*Approved August 13, 1953.*

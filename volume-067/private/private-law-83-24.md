# Private Law 24

> AN ACT For the relief of Paula Akiyama.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Paula Akiyama, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Ralph C. Egolf, citizens of the United States.


*Approved May 14, 1953.*

# Private Law 212

> AN ACT For the relief of Rosa Guglielmo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Rosa Guglielmo, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Michael and Minni Guglielmo, citizens of the United States.


*Approved August 13, 1953.*

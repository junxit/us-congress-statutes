# Private Law 47

> AN ACT For the relief of Anna Bosco Lomonaco.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Anna Bosco Lomonaco, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Antonio Lomonaco, citizens of the United States.


*Approved May 29, 1953.*

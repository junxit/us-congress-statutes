# Private Law 22

> AN ACT For the relief of Stephanie Marie Doreey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Stephanie Marie Doreey, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. Carlyle John Doreey and Alberta Tondera Doreey, citizens of the United States.


*Approved May 14, 1953.*

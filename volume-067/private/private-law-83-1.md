# Private Law 1

> AN ACT For the relief of Hose Martin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Rose Martin, shall-be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Colonel and Mrs. L. A. Burbank, citizens of the United States.


*Approved March 27, 1953.*

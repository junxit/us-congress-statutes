# Private Law 17

> AN ACT For the relief of Mary Bouessa Deeb.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mary Bouessa Deeb shall be deemed to have been born in Canada.


*Approved May 14, 1953.*

# Private Law 199

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Lau Hong Shee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 101 (a) (27) (B) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Lau Hong Shee shall be held and considered to be a returning resident alien.


*Approved August 13, 1953.*

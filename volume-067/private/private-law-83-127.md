# Private Law 127

> AN ACT For the relief of Anna Solenniani.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the pur- poses of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Anna Solenniani, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Lieutenant and Mrs. John Howard Birrell, citizens of the United States.


*Approved August 1, 1953.*

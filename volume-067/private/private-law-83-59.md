# Private Law 59

> AN ACT For the relief of Anthony Lynn Neis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Anthony Lynn Neis, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Adolph A. and Lois Marie Neis, citizens of the United States.


*Approved June 16, 1953.*

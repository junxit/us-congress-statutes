# Private Law 149

> AN ACT To authorize the payment of compensation to Clarence A. Beutel, formerly Deputy Administrator of Reconstruction Finance Corporation, for the period from September 10, 1952, through June 1, 1953.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That nothwithstanding the provisions of section 1761 of the Revised Statutes, as amended (5 U. S. C. 56), the Reconstruction Finance Corporation is authorized to pay to Clarence A. Beutel the compensation earned by him as Deputy Administrator of the Reconstruction Finance Corporation from September 10, 1952, through June 1, 1953.


*Approved August 8, 1953.*

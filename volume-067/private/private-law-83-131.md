# Private Law 131

> AN ACT For the relief of Leong Walk Hong.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Leong Walk Hong, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Leong Sik Fo (Robert Leong), a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 5, 1953.*

# Private Law 207

> AN ACT For the relief of Charlotte Witzeling Robinson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That., notwithstanding the provisions of section 212 (a) (9) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Charlotte Witzeling Robinson may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of such Act-


*Approved August 13, 1953.*

# Private Law 145

> AN ACT For the relief of Wera Fazio, a minor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Wera Fazio, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Angelo Fazio, citizens of the United States.


*Approved August 7, 1953.*

# Private Law 61

> AN ACT For the relief of Francesca Servelio.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Francesca Servelio, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Frank and Teresa D. Servelio, citizens of the United States.


*Approved June 18, 1953.*

# Private Law 20

> AN ACT For the relief of Charles Anthony Desotell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the pur- poses of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Charles Anthony Desotell, shall be held and considered to lie the natural-born alien child of Technical Sergeant and Mrs. George G. Desotell, citizens of the United States.


*Approved May 14, 1953.*

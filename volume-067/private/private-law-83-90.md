# Private Law 90

> AN ACT For the relief of Mieko Kristine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Mieko Kristine, shall be held and considered’ to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. James W, Pulver, citizens of the United States.


*Approved July 16, 1953.*

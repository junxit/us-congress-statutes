# Private Law 66

> AN ACT For the relief of Cecile Lorraine Vincent and Michael Calvin Vincent.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor children, Cecile Lorraine Vincent and Michael Calvin Vincent, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien children of Mr. and Mrs. Charles Arthur Vincent, citizens of the United States.


*Approved June 18, 1953.*

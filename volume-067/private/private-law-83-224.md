# Private Law 224

> AN ACT For the relief of George Michael labour.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27J (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, George Michael Jabour shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of George and Fouli Jabour, citizens of the United States.


*Approved August 14, 1953.*

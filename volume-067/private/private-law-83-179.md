# Private Law 179

> AN ACT For the relief of Jekabs Lenbergs.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of section 212 (a) (6) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Jekabs Lenbergs may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if he is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the said Act.


*Approved August 13, 1953.*

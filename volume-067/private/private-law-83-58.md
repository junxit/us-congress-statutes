# Private Law 58

> AN ACT For the relief of Gary Matthew Stevens (Kazuo Omiya).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Gary Matthew Stevens, shall be held and considered to Ire the natural-born alien child of Sergeant First Class and Mrs. George C. Stevens, citizens of the United States.


*Approved June 16, 1953.*

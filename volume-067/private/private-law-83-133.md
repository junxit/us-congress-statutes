# Private Law 133

> AN ACT For the relief of Anneliese Schillings.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provision of section 212 (a) (9) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Anneliese Schillings, the fiancée of Giovannibattista Tudisca, may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act: Provided, That the marriage between Anneliese Schillings and Giovannibattista Tudisca occurs within six months following the enactment of this Act.


*Approved August 5, 1953.*

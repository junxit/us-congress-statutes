# Private Law 211

> AN ACT For the relief of Valda Cimermanis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Valda Cimermanis shall be held and considered to be the minor child of her parents, Milda and Vilis Cimermanis, lawful permanent residents of the United States.


*Approved August 13, 1953.*

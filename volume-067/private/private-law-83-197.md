# Private Law 197

> AN ACT For the relief of Dorothy Sonya Goldschmidt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Naturalizaton Act, Dorothy Sonya Goldschmidt shall be held and considered to have been born in the Dominion of Canada.


*Approved August 13, 1953.*

# Private Law 202

> AN ACT For the relief of Julia S. Criswell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of section 352 (a) (1) of the Immigration and Nationality Act shall not apply to Julia S. Criswell.


*Approved August 13, 1953.*

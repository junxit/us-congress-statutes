# Private Law 84

> AN ACT For the relief of May Ling Ng.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, May Ling Ng, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Lun Foo Ng, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved July 16, 1953.*

# Private Law 95

> AN ACT For the relief of Lee Kwang Nong (George Clifford Roeder).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Lee Kwang Nong (also known as George Clifford Roeder), shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Clifford Roeder, citizen of the United States.


*Approved July 27, 1953.*

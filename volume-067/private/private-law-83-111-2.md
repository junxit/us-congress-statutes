# Private Law 111

> AN ACT For the relief of Nicola, Lucia, and Rocco Fierro.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the children, Nicola, Lucia, and Rocco Fierro, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born minor alien children of Mr. and Mrs. Nicola Fierro, citizens of the United States.


*Approved August 7, 1953.*

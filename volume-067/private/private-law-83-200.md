# Private Law 200

> AN ACT For the relief of Hidenori Utada.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Hidenori Utada, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Itaru Kono and Nancy Mieko Kono, citizens of the United States.


*Approved August 13, 1953.*

# Private Law 94

> AN ACT To release all the right, title, and interest of the United States in and to all fissionable materials in certain land in Marion County, Indiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all the right, title, and interest of the United States in and to all fissionable materials in certain land located in Marion County, Indiana, is hereby released and relinquished to and for the benefit of the lawful owner or owners of such land. Such land, which was acquired by the United States in 1942 and reconveyed to William Ozman on May 16, 1946, subject to a reservation to the United States (pursuant to Executive Order Numbered 9701, dated March 4, 1946) of all fissionable materials therein, contains approximately seven and one-tenths acres and is more particularly described as follows:

Block “A” in the Joe Maloof Addition, sections 1 and 2, an addition to the city of Indianapolis, Marion County, Indiana, as per plat thereof, recorded in plat book 27, page HO, in the office of the Recorder of Marion County, Indiana.


*Approved July 27, 1953.*

# Private Law 112

> AN ACT For the relief of Monika Klein.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Monika Klein, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Doctor and Mrs. Ralph Charles Wood, citizens of the United States.


*Approved July 29, 1953.*

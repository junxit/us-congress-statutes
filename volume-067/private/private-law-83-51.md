# Private Law 51

> AN ACT To terminate restrictions against alienation on land owned by William Lynn Engles and Maureen Edna Engles.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed, upon application of the legal guardian of William Lynn Engles and Maureen Edna Engles, to issue patents in fee to the said William Lynn Engles and Maureen Edna Engles for the fol lowing tracts of land:

To William Lynn Engles: West half section 10, township 29 north, range 52 east, Montana principal meridian, Montana, containing three hundred and twenty acres.

To Maureen Edna Engles: East half section 9, township 29 north, range 52 east, Montana principal meridian, Montana, containing three hundred and twenty acres.


*Approved June 2, 1953.*

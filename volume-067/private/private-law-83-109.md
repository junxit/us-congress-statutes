# Private Law 109

> AN ACT For the relief of Franciszek Jarecki.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Franciszek Jarecki shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act; and that his past membership in the classes defined in section 212 (a) (28) of the Immigration and Nationality Act shall not hereafter be a cause for his exclusion from the United States.


*Approved July 29, 1953.*

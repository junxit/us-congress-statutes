# Private Law 134

> AN ACT For the relief of Akemi Terada.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the pur- poses of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Akemi Terada, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Sergeant Samuel P. Jackson, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 7, 1953.*

# Private Law 223

> AN ACT For the relief of Irene Andrews.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203 (a) (3) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Irene Andrews, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of George Makohon and Mrs. Josephine Makohon, lawful residents of the United States.


*Approved August 14, 1953.*

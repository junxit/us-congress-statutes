# Private Law 7

> AN ACT For the relief of George Riainey Giltner (formerly Joji Wakamiya).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) anti 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Art. the minor child, George Rodney Giltner (formerly.Toji Wakamiya), shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. William H. Giltner, citizens of the United States.


*Approved April 22, 1953.*

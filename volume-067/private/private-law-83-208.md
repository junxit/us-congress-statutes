# Private Law 208

> AN ACT For the relief of Mr. and Mrs. Lucilio Grassi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That pursuant to the provisions set forth in section 402 (j) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Lucilio Grassi and his wife, Alberta Grassi, shall be held to be eligible to take the oath required by section 337 of that Act.


*Approved August 13, 1953.*

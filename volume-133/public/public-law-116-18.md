# Public Law 116–18

> An Act To reauthorize the Bulletproof Vest Partnership Grant Program.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. BULLETPROOF VEST PARTNERSHIP GRANT PROGRAM REAUTHORIZATION.

- **(a)** *In General.—* Section 1001(a)(23) of title I of the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 (34 U.S.C. 10261(a)(23)) is amended by striking “part Y” and all that follows and inserting “part Y, $30,000,000 for fiscal year 2020, and each fiscal year thereafter.”.
- **(b)** *Program Name.—* Part Y of title I of the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968 (34 U.S.C. 10531 et seq.) is amended by inserting before section 2501 the following:
  > ## § 2500. PATRICK LEAHY BULLETPROOF VEST PARTNERSHIP GRANT PROGRAM.
  >
  > “The program under this part shall be known as the ‘Patrick Leahy Bulletproof Vest Partnership Grant Program’.”.


*Approved May 23, 2019.*

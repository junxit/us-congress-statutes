# Public Law 116–15

> An Act To change the address of the postal facility designated in honor of Captain Humayun Khan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. CAPTAIN HUMAYUN KHAN POST OFFICE.

Section 1(a) of Public Law 115–347 (132 Stat. 5054) is amended by striking “180 McCormick Road” and inserting “2150 Wise Street”.


*Approved April 16, 2019.*

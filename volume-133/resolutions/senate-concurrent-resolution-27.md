# Senate Concurrent Resolution 27

> ELIJAH E. CUMMINGS MEMORIAL SERVICES—CATAFALQUE AUTHORIZATION

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Architect of the Capitol is authorized and directed to transfer the catafalque which is situated in the Exhibition Hall of the Capitol Visitor Center to the House wing of the Capitol so that such catafalque may be used in connection with services to be conducted there for the Honorable Elijah E. Cummings, late a Representative from the State of Maryland.


*Agreed to October 21, 2019.*

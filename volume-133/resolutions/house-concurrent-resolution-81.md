# House Concurrent Resolution 81

> ENROLLMENT CORRECTION—H.R. 1158

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That, in the enrollment of the bill H.R. 1158, the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall make the following correction: Amend the long title so as to read: “Making consolidated appropriations for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2020, and for other purposes.”.


*Agreed to December 19, 2019.*

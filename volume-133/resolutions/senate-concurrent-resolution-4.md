# Senate Concurrent Resolution 4

> ENROLLMENT CORRECTION—H.J. RES. 31

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That, in the enrollment of the joint resolution H.J. Res. 31, the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall amend the long title so as to read: “Making consolidated appropriations for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2019, and for other purposes.”.


*Agreed to February 14, 2019.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 93

> PRESIDENTIAL INAUGURATION—CAPITOL ROTUNDA AUTHORIZATION

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

## § 1. USE OF THE ROTUNDA OF THE CAPITOL BY THE JOINT CONGRESSIONAL COMMITTEE ON INAUGURAL CEREMONIES.

The rotunda of the United States Capitol is authorized to be used on January 20, 2005, by the Joint Congressional Committee on Inaugural Ceremonies in connection with the proceedings and ceremonies conducted for the inauguration of the President-elect and the Vice President-elect of the United States.


*Agreed to March 16, 2004.*

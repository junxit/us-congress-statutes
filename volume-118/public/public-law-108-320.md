# Public Law 108–320

> An Act To amend the Stevenson-Wydler Technology Innovation Act of 1980 to permit Malcolm Baldrige National Quality Awards to be made to nonprofit organizations.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. AMENDMENT.

Section 17(c)(1) of the Stevenson-Wydler Technology Innovation Act of 1980 (15 U.S.C. 3711a(c)(1)) is amended by adding at the end the following:

> - **(F)** Nonprofit organizations.”.


*Approved October 5, 2004.*

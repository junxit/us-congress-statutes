# Public Law 108–433

> Joint Resolution Appointing the day for the convening of the first session of the One Hundred Ninth Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first regular session of the One Hundred Ninth Congress shall begin at noon on Tuesday, January 4, 2005.


*Approved December 3, 2004.*

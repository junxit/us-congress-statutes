# Public Law 108–415

> An Act To amend title 31 of the United States Code to increase the public debt limit.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. INCREASE IN PUBLIC DEBT LIMIT.

Subsection (b) of section 3101 of title 31, United States Code, is amended by striking “$7,384,000,000,000” and inserting “$8,184,000,000,000”.


*Approved November 19, 2004.*

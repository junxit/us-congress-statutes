# Public Law 108–299

> An Act To modify certain deadlines pertaining to machine-readable, tamper-resistant entry and exit documents.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. MODIFICATION OF CERTAIN DEADLINES FOR MACHINE-READABLE, TAMPER-RESISTANT ENTRY AND EXIT DOCUMENTS.

Section 303 of the Enhanced Border Security and Visa Entry Reform Act of 2002 (8 U.S.C. 1732) is amended, in each of subsections (b)(2)(A), (c)(1), and (c)(2), by striking “2004,” and inserting “2005,”.


*Approved August 9, 2004.*

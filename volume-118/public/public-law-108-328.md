# Public Law 108–328

> An Act To amend the Safe Drinking Water Act to reauthorize the New York City Watershed Protection Program.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. NEW YORK CITY WATERSHED PROTECTION PROGRAM.

Section 1443(d)(4) of the Safe Drinking Water Act (42 U.S.C. 300j–2(d)(4)) is amended by striking “1997 through 2003” and inserting “2003 through 2010”.


*Approved October 16, 2004.*

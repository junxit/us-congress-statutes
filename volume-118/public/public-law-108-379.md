# Public Law 108–379

> An Act To amend the Agricultural Adjustment Act to remove the requirement that processors be members of an agency administering a marketing order applicable to pears.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. PEAR MARKETING ORDERS.

Section 8c(7)(C) of the Agricultural Adjustment Act (7 U.S.C. 608c(7)(C)), reenacted with amendments by the Agricultural Marketing Agreement Act of 1937, is amended in the last sentence—

- **(1)** by striking “or pears”; and
- **(2)** by striking “: Provided,” and all that follows through “be equal”.


*Approved October 30, 2004.*

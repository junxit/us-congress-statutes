# Public Law 108–272

> Joint Resolution Approving the renewal of import restrictions contained in the Burmese Freedom and Democracy Act of 2003.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Congress approves the renewal of the import restrictions contained in section 3(a)(1) of the Burmese Freedom and Democracy Act of 2003.


*Approved July 7, 2004.*

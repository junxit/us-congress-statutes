# Public Law 97–230

> An Act To amend title 28, United States Code, to modify the bar membership requirements for United States magistrates.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 631(b)(1) of title 28, United States Code, is amended by striking out “He is, and has been for at least five years, a member” and inserting in lieu thereof the following: “He has been for at least five years a member in good standing of the bar of the highest court of a State, the District of Columbia, the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, or the Virgin Islands of the United States, and he is a member”.


*Approved August 6, 1982.*

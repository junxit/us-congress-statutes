# Public Law 97–270

> Joint Resolution To provide for a temporary in crease in the public debt limit.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That during the period beginning on October 1, 1982, and ending on September 30, 1983, the public debt limit set forth in the first sentence of section 21 of the Second Liberty Bond Act (31 U.S.C. 757b) shall be temporarily increased by $890,200,000,000 (and any other provision of law providing for a temporary increase in such limit shall not apply).


*Approved September 30, 1982.*

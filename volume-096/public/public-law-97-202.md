# Public Law 97–202

> An Act Authorizing appropriations to the Secretary of the Interior for services necessary to the nonperforming arts functions of the John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (e) of section 6 of the John F. Kennedy Center Act (Public Law 85–874, as amended; 20 U.S.C. 761) is amended by striking out the period in the last sentence and adding in lieu thereof “, and not to exceed $4,247,000 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1983.”


*Approved June 24, 1982.*

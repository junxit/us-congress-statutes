# Public Law 97–313

> An Act To authorize the use of education block grant funds to teach the principles of citizenship.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 582(1) of the Education Consolidation and Improvement Act of 1981 is amended—

- **(1)** by striking out “and” at the end of clause (H);
- **(2)** by inserting “and” at the end of clause (I); and
- **(3)** by inserting after clause (I) the following new clause:
  > - **(J)** programs to teach the principles of citizenship;”.


*Approved October 14, 1982.*

# Public Law 97–328

> An Act To amend the District of Columbia Self-Government and Governmental Reorganization Act to allow the issuance of revenue bonds to finance college and university programs which provide student educational loans.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first sentence of section 490(a)(1) of the District of Columbia Self-Government and Governmental Reorganization Act (D.C. Code, sec. 47–334) is amended by inserting “college and university programs which provide loans for the payment of educational expenses for or on behalf of students,” after “college and university facilities,”.


*Approved October 15, 1982.*

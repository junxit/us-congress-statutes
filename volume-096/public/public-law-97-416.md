# Public Law 97–416

> An Act To reauthorize the Deep Seabed Hard Mineral Resources Act for fiscal years 1983 and 1984.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 310 of the Deep Seabed Hard Mineral Resources Act (30 U.S.C. 1470) is amended by striking out the period at the end and inserting in lieu thereof a comma and the following: “and $1,469,000 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1983, and $2,150,000 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1984.”.


*Approved January 4, 1983.*

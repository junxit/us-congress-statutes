# Public Law 97–400

> An Act To designate the Lakeview Lake project. Mountain Creek, Texas, as the “Joe Pool Lake”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Lakeview Lake project, Mountain Creek, Texas, authorized by the River and Harbor Act of 1965, shall hereafter be known and designated as “Joe Pool Lake”. Any reference in any law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to such lake shall be held to be a reference to the “Joe Pool Lake”.


*Approved December 31, 1982.*

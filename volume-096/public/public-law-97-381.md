# Public Law 97–381

> An Act To designate the southern Nevada water project the “Robert B. Griffith Water Project’.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the southern Nevada water project, in Clark County, Nevada, shall hereafter be known and designated as the “Robert B. Griffith Water Project”. Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, record or other paper of the United States to that water project shall be held and considered to be a reference to the “Robert B. Griffith Water Project”.


*Approved December 22, 1982.*

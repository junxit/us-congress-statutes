# Public Law 97–207

> An Act To designate the United States Post Office Building in Hartford, Connecticut, as the “William R. Cotter Federal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the building at 135 High Street, Hartford, Connecticut (commonly known as the High Street Post Office), shall hereafter be known, called, and designated as the “William R. Cotter Federal Building”. Any reference in any law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to such building shall be deemed to be a reference to the William R. Cotter Federal Building.


*Approved June 30, 1982.*

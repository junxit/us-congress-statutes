# Public Law 97–387

> An Act To amend the Peace Corps Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** section 6 of the Peace Corps Act is amended by striking out “not to exceed” in the first proviso and by inserting in lieu thereof “not less than”.
- **(b)** This amendment shall be effective as of December 29, 1981.


*Approved December 23, 1982.*

# Public Law 97–445

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the President to proclaim May 13, 1983, as “American Indian Day”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States is authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating May 13, 1983, as “American Indian Day”, and calling upon the people of the United States to observe such day with appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Approved January 12, 1983.*

# Public Law 97–197

> An Act To designate the control tower at Memphis International Airport the Omlie Tower.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the air traffic control tower at the Memphis International Airport is designated and shall hereafter be known as “Omlie Tower”. Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, or other paper of the United States to such control tower shall be held and considered to refer to “Omlie Tower”.


*Approved June 21, 1982.*

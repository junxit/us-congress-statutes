# Public Law 97–236

> Joint Resolution To authorize and request the President to issue a proclamation designating October 17 through October 23, 1982, as “Lupus Awareness Week”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating October 17 through October 23, 1982, as “Lupus Awareness Week”, and calling upon the people of the United States to observe such week with appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Approved August 17, 1982.*

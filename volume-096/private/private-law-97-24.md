# Private Law 97–24

> An Act For the relief of Christina Boltz Sidders.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Christina Boltz Sidders shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence on February 15, 1980, upon payment of the required visa fee.


*Approved June 30, 1982.*

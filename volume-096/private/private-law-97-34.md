# Private Law 97–34

> An Act For the relief of Ok-Boon Kang.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Hquse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Ok-Boon Kang may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(E) of such Act, upon approval of a petition filed in her behalf by Robert J. Cornyn and Linda L. Cornyn, citizens of the United States, except that the natural parents or brothers or sisters of the beneficiary shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under such Act.


*Approved October 15, 1982.*

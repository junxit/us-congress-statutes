# Private Law 97–29

> An Act For the relief of Lourie Ann Eder.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Lourie Ann Eder may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of such Act, upon approval of a petition filed in her behalf by Federico P. Eder and Irinea R. Eder, a lawful permanent resident and a citizen of the United States, respectively, pursuant to section 204 of such Act: Provided, That the natural parents or brothers or sisters of the beneficiary shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved October 1, 1982.*

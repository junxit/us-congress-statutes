# Private Law 97–54

> An Act For the relief of Dana Braford Baretto.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for purposes of section 203(c) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the petition filed on behalf of Dana Braford Baretto by Emily Irene Braford under section 204(a) of such Act shall be held and considered to have been filed on March 29, 1979, the date on which a petition was filed on behalf of Dana Braford Baretto under such section by Ralph Otis Braford.


*Approved January 6, 1983.*

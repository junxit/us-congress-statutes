# Private Law 97–50

> An Act For the relief of Jose Ramon Beltron Aivenda Ostler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Jose Ramon Beltron Aivenda Ostler of Orem, Utah, shall be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(E) of such Act upon approval of a petition filed in his behalf by Mr. and Mrs. M. Ray Ostler of Orem, Utah, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of such Act. No natural parent, brother, or sister, if any, of Jose Ramon Beltron Aivenda Ostler shall, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved January 3, 1983.*

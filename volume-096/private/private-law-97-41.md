# Private Law 97–41

> An Act For the relief of Eun Ok Han.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Eun Ok Han may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of such Act, upon approval of a petition filed in her behalf by Richard E. and Vilma B. Novak, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of such Act. The natural parents or brothers or sisters of Eun Ok Han shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved October 15, 1982.*

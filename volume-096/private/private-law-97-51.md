# Private Law 97–51

> An Act For the relief of Cesar Noel Jump.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Cesar Noel Jump may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(E) of such Act upon approval of a petition filed on his behalf by Mr. and Mrs. Robert I. Jump, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents, brothers, and sisters of the said Cesar Noel Jump shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved January 3, 1983.*

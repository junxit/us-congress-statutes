# Senate Concurrent Resolution 60

> USED MOTOR VEHICLE SALES—DISAPPROVAL OF FTC REGULATION

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Congress disapproves the final rule promulgated by the Federal Trade Commission dealing with the matter of the trade regulation rule relating to the sale of used motor vehicles, which final rule was submitted to the Congress on January 28, 1982.


*Agreed to May 26, 1982.*

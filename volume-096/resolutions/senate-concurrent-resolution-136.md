# Senate Concurrent Resolution 136

> GENERAL EXPLANATION OF THE REVENUE PRO-VISIONS OF THE TAX EQUITY AND FISCAL RESPONSIBILITY ACT OF 1982

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That three thousand additional copies of the General Explanation of the Revenue Provisions of the Tax Equity and Fiscal Responsibility Act of 1982, prepared by the staff of the Joint Committee on Taxation, be printed for the use of the Joint Committee on Taxation.


*Agreed to December 20, 1982.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 435

> CORRECTION IN ENROLLMENT OF H.R. 5447

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That, in the enrollment of the bill (H.R. 5447) to extend the Commodity Exchange Act, and for other purposes, the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall make the following correction:

In section 239, strike out “sections 9,14, and 28” and insert in lieu thereof “sections 207, 212, and 231”.


*Agreed to December 17, 1982.*

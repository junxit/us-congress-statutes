# House Concurrent Resolution 268

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Wednesday, February 10, 1982, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian on Monday, February 22, 1982, and that when the Senate adjourns on Thursday, February 11, 1982, or on Friday, February 12, 1982, pursuant to a motion made by the Majority Leader in accordance with this resolution, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian on Monday, February 22, 1982.


*Agreed to February 10, 1982.*

# Chapter 384

> AN ACT To amend section 203 of the Hawaiian Homes Commission Act, designating certain public lands as available home lands.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 203 of title II of the Hawaiian Homes Commission Act of July 9, 1921 (42 Stat. 109; 48 U. S. C. 697), as amended, designating certain public lands as available home lands, is further amended by adding thereto the following:

> “Wailuku, Maui: That parcel of government land, situate in the District of Wailuku, island and county of Maui, comprising twelve and four hundred and fifty-five one-thousandths acres of the Hi of Kou and being a portion of the land covered by General Lease Numbered 2286 to Wailuku Sugar Company, Limited, notwithstanding the fact that said parcel is cultivated sugarcane land, subject, however, to the terms of said lease.”


*Approved June 3, 1948.*

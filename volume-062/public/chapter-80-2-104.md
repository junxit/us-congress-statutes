# Chapter 104

> AN ACT To authorize the carrying of Civil War battle streamers with regimental colors.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in accordance with such regulations as the Secretary of War may prescribe, each regiment and other units of the Army of the United States is hereby authorized to carry appropriate Civil War battle streamers with its regimental colors or standards, upon verification in the War Department that it is entitled to such honors.


*Approved March 9, 1948.*

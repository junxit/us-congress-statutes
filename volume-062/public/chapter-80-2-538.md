# Chapter 538

> AN ACT To amend the Civil Service Retirement Act of May 29, 1930, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph 5 of section 3A of the Civil Service Retirement Act of May 29, 1930, as amended, is amended by adding at the end thereof the following:

> “Any Member of Congress, who during any war or time of national emergency as proclaimed by the President or declared by the Congress, left or leaves his office to enter the armed forces of the United States shall, for the purpose of this paragraph, be deemed to have continued as a Member of Congress for such period of military service.”


*Approved June 19, 1948.*

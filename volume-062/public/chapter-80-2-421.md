# Chapter 421

> JOINT RESOLUTION To authorize the issuance of a special series of stamps commemorative of the one hundredth anniversary of the founding of Fort Kearney in the State of Nebraska.

Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

That the Postmaster General is authorized and directed to issue, during 1948, a special series of 3-cent postage stamps, of such design as he shall prescribe, in commemoration of the one hundredth anniversary of the founding of Fort Kearney in the State of Nebraska.


*Approved June 4, 1948.*

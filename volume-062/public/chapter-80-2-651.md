# Chapter 651

> JOINT RESOLUTION To increase the sum authorized to be appropriated for the presentation to Eire of a statue of Commodore John Barry.

Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

That, section 2 of the joint resolution approved June 10, 1941, which authorized the President to present to Eire on behalf of the people of the United States a statue of Commodore John Barry, is hereby amended by striking out “$20,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$30,000”.


*Approved June 25, 1948.*

# Chapter 121

> AN ACT Relating to the compensation of commissioners for the Territory of Alaska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions relating to fees earned by commissioners for the Territory of Alaska of section 11 of the Act of June 6, 1900, entitled “An Act making further provision for a civil government for Alaska, and for other purposes”, as amended (U. S. C., 1940 edition, title 48, sec. 116), each such commissioner shall pay to the clerk of the proper division of the court only so much of the aggregate net fees earned during the calendar year by such commissioner as exceeds the sum of $5,000.


*Approved March 15, 1948.*

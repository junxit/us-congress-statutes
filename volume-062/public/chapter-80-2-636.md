# Chapter 636

> AN ACT To amend the Civil Service Retirement Act of May 29, 1930, to provide annuities for certain surviving spouses of annuitants retired prior to April 1, 1948.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 8 of the Civil Service Retirement Act of May 29, 1930, as amended, is amended by inserting after the first sentence thereof a new sentence as follows: “Any such annuitant who died during the period beginning on February 29, 1948, and ending on April 30, 1948, leaving a surviving wife or husband, shall be deemed to have made the election authorized in the foregoing proviso and to have named such wife or husband to receive an annuity as provided in such proviso, but no such annuity shall become due or payable to such wife or husband prior to April 1, 1948.”


*Approved June 25, 1948.*

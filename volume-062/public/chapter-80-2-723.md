# Chapter 723

> AN ACT To amend the Reconstruction Finance Corporation Act, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4 (c) of the Reconstruction Finance Corporation Act, as amended, is hereby amended by striking out “$25,000,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$40,000,000”.


*Approved June 29, 1948.*

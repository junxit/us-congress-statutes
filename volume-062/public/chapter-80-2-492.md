# Chapter 492

> AN ACT Authorizing the Secretary of the Army to have prepared a replica of the Dade Monument for presentation to the State of Florida.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, upon payment by the State of Florida to the Department of the Army of such sum as may be necessary to defray all expenses necessarily incident thereto, the Secretary of the Army is authorized and directed to cause to be prepared an exact replica of the Dade Monument, located on the grounds of the United States military reservation at West Point, New York, and to present such replica to the State of Florida for erection in the Dade State Memorial Park.


*Approved June 17, 1948.*

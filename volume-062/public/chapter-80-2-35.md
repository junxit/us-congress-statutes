# Chapter 35

> AN ACT To amend section 2 of the Act prescribing regulations for the Soldiers’ Home located at Washington, in the District of Columbia, and for other purposes, approved March 3, 1883 (22 Stat. 564).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2 of the Act of March 3, 1883 (22 Stat. 564), is hereby amended to read as follows:

> ## § 2.
>
> The Inspector General of the Army shall designate officers of the Inspector General’s Department under his jurisdiction to inspect thoroughly, once each year, the United States Soldiers’ Home, Washington, District of Columbia, its records, accounts, management, discipline, and sanitary condition, and shall report thereon in writing to the Secretary of the Army, including in his report such suggestions as he desires to make.”


*Approved January 27, 1948.*

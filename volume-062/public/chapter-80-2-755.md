# Chapter 755

> JOINT RESOLUTION Requesting the President to proclaim February 1 as National Freedom Day.

Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

That the President of the United States is authorized to issue a proclamation designating the 1st day of February of each year as National Freedom Day for the purpose of commemorating the signing by President Abraham Lincoln, on February 1, 1865, of the joint resolution adopted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the United States, proposing the thirteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States of America.


*Approved June 30, 1948.*

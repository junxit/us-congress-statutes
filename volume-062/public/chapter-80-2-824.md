# Chapter 824

> JOINT RESOLUTION To authorize the issuance of a special series of stamps commemorative of Juliette Low, founder and organizer of Girl Scouting in the United States of America.

Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

That the Postmaster General is authorized and directed to issue at the earliest practicable date, a special series of 3-cent postage stamps, of such design as he shall prescribe, in commemoration of Juliette Low, the founder and organizer of Girl Scouting in the United States of America.


*Approved July 3, 1948.*

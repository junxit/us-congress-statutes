# Chapter 518

> AN ACT To increase the maximum travel allowance for railway postal clerks and substitute railway postal clerks.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsections (m) and (r) of section 16 of the Act entitled “An Act to reclassify the salaries or postmasters, officers, and employees of the Postal Service; to establish uniform procedures for computing compensation; and for other purposes”, approved July 6, 1945, as amended, are amended by striking out “$4 per day” and inserting in lieu thereof “$6 per day”.


*Approved June 19, 1948.*

# Chapter 787

> AN ACT To authorize an increase in the annual appropriation for the maintenance and operation of the Gorgas Memorial Laboratory.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1 of the Act entitled “An Act to authorize a permanent annual appropriation for the maintenance and operation of the Gorgas Memorial Laboratory”, approved May 7, 1928 (45 Stat. 491), is amended by striking out “$50,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “not to exceed $150,000.”


*Approved July 1, 1948.*

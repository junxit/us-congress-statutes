# Chapter 514

> AN ACT To repeal section 1 of the Act of April 20, 1874, prescribing regulations governing inquiries to be made in connection with disbursements made by disbursing officers of the Army (18 Stat. 33; 10 U. S. C. 174).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1 of the Act of April 20, 1874 (18 Stat. 33; 10 U. S. C. 174), is hereby repealed.


*Approved June 19, 1948.*

# Chapter 143

> AN ACT To provide for selection of superintendents of national cemeteries from meritorious and trustworthy members of the armed forces who have been disabled in line of duty for active field service.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That superintendents of the national cemeteries shall be selected from meritorious and trustworthy members of the armed forces who have been honorably separated from the service of the United States, and who have been disabled in line of duty for active field service.

## § 2.

Section 4874, Revised Statutes (U. S. C., title 24, sec. 275), is hereby repealed.


*Approved March 24, 1948.*

# Chapter 833

> JOINT RESOLUTION To provide for an extension of time within which the Joint Committee on Labor-Management Relations shall make its final report.

Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

That section 403 of title IV of the Labor-Management Relations Act, 1947, is amended by striking out the words “January 2, 1949” and inserting in lieu thereof the words “March 1, 1949”.


*Approved August 10, 1948.*

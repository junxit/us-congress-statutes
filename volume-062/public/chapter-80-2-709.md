# Chapter 709

> AN ACT To authorize the issuance of a special series of stamps in honor and commemoration of Moina Michael, originator of Flanders Field memorial poppy idea.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General is authorized and directed to prepare, for issuance at as early a date as practicable, a special series of 3-cent postage stamps, of such design as he shall prescribe, in honor and commemoration of Moina Michael, originator of Flanders Field memorial poppy idea.


*Approved June 29, 1948.*

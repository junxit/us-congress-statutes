# Chapter 101

> JOINT RESOLUTION To provide for the designation of the Park River dam and reservoir project in Walsh County, North Dakota, as the Homme Reservoir and Dam.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Park River dam and reservoir project in Walsh County, North Dakota, shall be designated on the public records as the Homme Reservoir and Dam.


*Approved March 5, 1948.*

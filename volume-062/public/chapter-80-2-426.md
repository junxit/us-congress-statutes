# Chapter 426

> AN ACT To continue until the close of June 30, 1949, the present suspension of import duties on scrap iron, scrap steel, and nonferrous metal scrap.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of March 13, 1942, entitled “An Act to suspend the effectiveness during the existing national emergency of tariff duties on scrap iron, scrap steel, and nonferrous metal scrap”, as amended (Public Law 497, Seventy-seventh Congress; 56 Stat. 171; Public Law 384, Eightieth Congress), is hereby amended by striking out “June 30, 1948” and inserting in lieu thereof “June 30, 1949”.


*Approved June 8, 1948.*

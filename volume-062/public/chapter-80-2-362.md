# Chapter 362

> AN ACT To transfer lot 1 in block 115, city of Fairbanks, Alaska, to the city of Fairbanks, Alaska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all the right, title, and interest of the United States in and to lot 1 of block 115, Fairbanks, Alaska, be, and the same are hereby, released and relinquished by the United States to the city of Fairbanks, Alaska, for school purposes: Provided, That all mineral rights pertaining to such land are hereby reserved to the United States together with the right to prospect for, mine, and remove the same.


*Approved June 1, 1948.*

# Chapter 633

> AN ACT To continue the authorization for the appointment of two additional Assistant Secretaries of State.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of December 8, 1944 (58 Stat. 798), is hereby amended by deleting the words “not to exceed two years” and inserting in lieu thereof, the words “not to exceed three years”.


*Approved June 24, 1948.*

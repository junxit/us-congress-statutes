# Chapter 798

> AN ACT To extend the time for commencing the construction of a toll bridge across the Rio Grande at or near Rio Grande City, Texas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time for commencing the construction of a toll bridge across the Rio Grande at or near Rio Grande City, Texas, authorized to be built by Gus A. Guerra, his heirs, legal representatives and assigns, by an Act of Congress approved July 31, 1946, is hereby extended until July 31, 1949. Construction of such bridge shall be commenced on or before such date and shall be prosecuted with reasonable diligence until same is completed.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby reserved.


*Approved July 2, 1948.*

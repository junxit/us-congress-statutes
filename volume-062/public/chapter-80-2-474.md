# Chapter 474

> JOINT RESOLUTION To authorize the issuance of a stamp commemorative of William Allen White, whose literary genius made such a great contribution in the field of American literature.

Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

That the Postmaster General is authorized and directed to issue, during 1948, a special 3-centpostage stamp, of such design as he shall prescribe, in commemoration of William Allen White, whose literary genius made such a great contribution in the field of American literature.


*Approved June 16, 1948.*

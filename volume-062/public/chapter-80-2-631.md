# Chapter 631

> AN ACT To amend section 3 of the Standard Time Act of March 19, 1918, as amended, relating to the placing of a certain portion of the State of Idaho in the third time zone.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3 of the Act entitled “An Act to save daylight and to provide standard time for the United States”, approved March 19, 1918, as amended (U. S. C., 1940 edition, title 15, sec. 264), which provides for placing a certain portion of the State of Idaho in the third time zone is hereby amended by striking out the period at the end thereof and inserting a colon and the following: “Provided, That common carriers within such portion of the State of Idaho may conduct their operations on Pacific time.”

## § 2.

This Act shall take effect at 2 o’clock antemeridian of the second Monday following the date of its enactment.


*Approved June 24, 1948.*

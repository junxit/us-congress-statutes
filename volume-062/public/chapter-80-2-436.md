# Chapter 436

> AN ACT To provide for the disposal of surplus sand at Fort Story, Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding any other provision of law the Secretary of the Army is hereby authorized and empowered to dispose of surplus sand on Government owned lands at Fort Story, Virginia, by sale, upon such terms and conditions as are deemed advisable by him.


*Approved June 10, 1948.*

# Chapter 148

> AN ACT To authorize the purchase of a new post-office site at Omaha, Nebraska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Federal Works Administrator is authorized and directed to acquire by purchase, condemnation, or otherwise, a suitable site in Omaha, Nebraska, for the purpose of erecting thereon a building for the use and accommodation of the United States post office at Omaha.


*Approved March 25, 1948.*

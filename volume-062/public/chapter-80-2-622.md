# Chapter 622

> AN ACT To amend the Interstate Commerce Act so as to permit the issuance of free passes to time inspectors of carriers subject to part I of such Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph (7) of section 1 of the Interstate Commerce Act. as amended, is amended by striking out “its officers, surgeons, physicians, and attorneys at law,” and inserting in lieu thereof the following: “its officers, time inspectors, surgeons, physicians, and attorneys at law,”.


*Approved June 24, 1948.*

# Chapter 192

> AN ACT To extend the provisions of the Federal Airport Act to the Virgin Islands.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Federal Airport Act of 1946 is hereby amended by—

- **(1)** Adding after the words “Puerto Rico”, wherever they appear in paragraph 7 of section 2 (a) and in sections 3 (a), 7, and 9 (c) thereof, the phrase “and the Virgin Islands”.
- **(2)** Adding after the word “Alaska” appearing in section 10 (c) the phrase “and the Virgin Islands”.


*Approved April 17, 1948.*

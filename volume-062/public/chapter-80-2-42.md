# Chapter 42

> JOINT RESOLUTION To change the date for filing the report of the Joint Committee on the Economic Report.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 5 (b) (3) of the Employment Act of 1946, as amended, is amended by striking out “February 1” and inserting in lieu thereof “March 1”.


*Approved February 2, 1948.*

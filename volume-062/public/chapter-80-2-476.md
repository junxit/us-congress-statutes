# Chapter 476

> AN ACT To authorize the revision of the boundaries of the Caribou National Forest in the State of Idaho.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the boundaries of the Caribou National Forest are hereby extended to include the following described lands and, subject to any valid existing claim or entry, all lands of the United States within the areas hereafter described are hereby added to and made part of the Caribou National Forest, State of Idaho, to be hereafter administered under the laws and regulations relating to the national forests, and the provisions of the Act approved March 20, 1922 (42 Stat. 465), as amended, are hereby extended and made applicable to all other lands within said described areas:

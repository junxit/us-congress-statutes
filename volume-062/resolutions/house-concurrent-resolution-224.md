# House Concurrent Resolution 224

> adjournment sine die

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Friday, December 31, 1948, and that when they adjourn on said day they stand adjourned sine die.


*Passed December 31, 1948.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 53

> “national aviation policy”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed five thousand additional copies of Senate Report Numbered 949, current session, entitled “National Aviation Policy”, for the use of the Congressional Aviation Policy Board.


*Agreed to May 10, 1948.*

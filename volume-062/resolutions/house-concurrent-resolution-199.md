# House Concurrent Resolution 199

> communist party of the united states

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed six thousand additional copies of the report (House Report Numbered 1920) on the Communist Party of the United States as an Advocate of Overthrow of Government by Force and Violence, of which five thousand copies shall be for the use of the Committee on Un-American Activities of the House of Representatives and one thousand copies shall be for the House document room.


*Passed June 19, 1948.*

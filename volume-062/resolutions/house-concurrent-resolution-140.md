# House Concurrent Resolution 140

> fiftieth anniversary of liberation of cuba

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That House Concurrent Resolution 108, Eightieth Congress, agreed to July 26, 1947, is amended by striking out the last sentence of section 2 (b) and inserting in lieu thereof the following: “The committee shall cease to exist upon the expiration of thirty days after April 19, 1948.”


*Passed February 9, 1948.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 43

> continuation of authority of maritime commission

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Secretary of the Senate, in the enrollment of the joint resolution (S. J. Res. 173) to continue until July 1, 1949, the authority of the Maritime Commission to sell, charter, and operate vessels, and for other purposes, be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to strike out, in the title of said joint resolution, the word “July” and insert “March”, so that as amended the title will read:

> “Joint resolution to continue until March 1, 1949, the authority of the Maritime Commission to sell, charter, and operate vessels, and for other purposes.”


*Agreed to February 25, 1948.*

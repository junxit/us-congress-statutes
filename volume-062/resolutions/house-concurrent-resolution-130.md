# House Concurrent Resolution 130

> joint meeting

*Resolved, by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring) ,*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Wednesday, January 7, 1948, at 1:30 o’clock in the afternoon, for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed January 7, 1948.*

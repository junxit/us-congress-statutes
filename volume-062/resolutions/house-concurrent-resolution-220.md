# House Concurrent Resolution 220

> joint meeting

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Tuesday, July 27, 1948, at 12:30 o’clock in the afternoon, for the purpose of receiving such communication as the President of the United States may be pleased to make to them.


*Passed July 27, 1948.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 42

> revenues and expenditures

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That it is the judgment of the Congress, based upon presently available information, that revenues during the period of the fiscal year 1949 will approximate 47.3 billion dollars and that expenditures during such fiscal year should not exceed 37.2 billion dollars, of which latter amount not more than 26.6 billion dollars would be in consequence of appropriations hereafter made available for obligation in such fiscal year.


*Agreed to February 27, 1948.*

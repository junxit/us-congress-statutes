# Senate Concurrent Resolution 38

> study and investigation of present high prices of consumer goods

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That section 2 of Senate Concurrent Resolution 19, Eightieth Congress, first session, is amended by striking out “February 1” and inserting in lieu thereof “March 1”.


*Passed January 30, 1948.*

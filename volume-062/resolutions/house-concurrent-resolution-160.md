# House Concurrent Resolution 160

> joint meeting

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Wednesday, March 17, 1948, at 12:30 post meridian, for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the Communications from the President. United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed March 16, 1948.*

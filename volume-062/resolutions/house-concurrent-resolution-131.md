# House Concurrent Resolution 131

> reorganization plan no. 1 of 1948

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Congress does not favor the Reorganization Plan Numbered 1 of January 19, 1948, transmitted to Congress by the President on the 19th day of January 1948.


*Passed March 16, 1948.*

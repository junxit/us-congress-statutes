# Senate Concurrent Resolution 52

> investigation of operation of war program

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed seven thousand additional copies of the report (report numbered 440, part 6, current session) of the special committee of the Senate authorized and directed to make a study and investigation of the operation of the war program, of which five thousand copies shall be for the use of the special committee, one thousand for the use of the Senate document room, and one thousand for the use of the House document room.


*Agreed to May 10, 1948.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 154

> “the strategy and tactics of world communism”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed as a House Document, and that there be printed fifteen thousand additional copies each of the report and supplements 1 and 2 of the Subcommittee on National and International Movements of the Committee on Foreign Affairs, entitled “The Strategy and Tactics of World Communism” for the use of the Committee on Foreign Affairs.


*Passed April 14, 1948.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 182

> “housing in america”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed as a House document, and that there be printed fifteen thousand additional copies of the factual analysis on housing prepared for the Joint Committee on Housing, entitled “Housing in America”, for the use of the said joint committee.


*Passed April 28, 1948.*

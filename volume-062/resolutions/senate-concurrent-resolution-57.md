# Senate Concurrent Resolution 57

> shore protective works, nome, alaska

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Secretary of the Senate be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, in the enrollment of the bill (S. 1025) to provide for the construction of shore protective works at the town of Nome, Alaska, to make the following correction, namely: In line 5 of the Senate engrossed bill, strike out the words “Secretary of War” and insert “Secretary of the Army”.


*Agreed to June 8, 1948.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 184

> fiftieth anniversary of liberation of cuba

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the first paragraph of H. Con. Res. 139, Eightieth Congress, is hereby amended to read as follows:

> “That in commemoration of the fiftieth anniversary of the liberation of Cuba, the two Houses of Congress shall assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives at 12 o’clock meridian, on Monday, April 19, 1948.”


*Passed April 14, 1948.*

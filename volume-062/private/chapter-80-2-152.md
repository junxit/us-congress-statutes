# Chapter 152

> AN ACT Authorizing the issuance of a patent in fee to Mabel Townsend Pretty On Top.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, upon application in writing, the Secretary of the Interior is authorized and directed to issue to Mabel Townsend Pretty On Top, of Lodge Grass, Montana, a patent in fee to the following-described lands situated in the State of Montana: The southwest quarter of section 13, township 7 south, range 37 east, Montana principal meridian: Provided, That when the land herein described is offered for sale, the Crow Tribe or any Indian who is a member of said tribe shall have ninety days in which to execute preferential rights to purchase said tract at a price offered to the seller by a prospective buyer willing and able to purchase.


*Approved March 25, 1948.*

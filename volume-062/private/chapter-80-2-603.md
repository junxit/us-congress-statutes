# Chapter 603

> AN ACT For the relief of Hou Chung Chay.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, the alien Hou Shung Chay, of New York, New York, shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted on June 5, 1942, to the United States for permanent residence, upon payment of the required visa fee and head tax. Upon enactment of this Act the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the appropriate quota for the first year in which such quota is available.


*Approved June 22, 1948.*

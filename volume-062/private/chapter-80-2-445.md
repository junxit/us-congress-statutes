# Chapter 445

> AN ACT For the relief of William M. Looney.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That William M. Looney, a substitute clerk in the post office at Canton, Pennsylvania, is hereby relieved of all liability to refund to the United States the amount of the overpayment of salary received by him from the date he returned to duty alter discharge from the armed services and was paid at the rate of $1.49 per hour to the date when it was determined that the rate properly payable to him was $1.19 per hour. In the audit and settlement of the accounts of any postmaster or other transmitting disbursing officer of the Post Office Department or postal service, the Sayment of an hourly rate of $1.49 to the said William M. Looney uring the time specified shall be considered to have been authorized.

## § 2.

Any amount heretofore credited to the said William M. Looney or refunded to the United States by him on account of the overpayment described in the first section of this Act shall be repaid to him out of any money available for the payment of salaries to substitute clerks of first- and second-class post offices.


*Approved June 10, 1948.*

# Chapter 194

> AN ACT For the relief of Hayato Harris Ozawa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions of section 13C of the Immigration Act of May 26, 1924, the Attorney General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to permit Hayato Harris Ozawa, the husband of Shigeko Elizabeth Tamura Ozawa, a citizen of the United States, and the father of two children born in the United States, to remain permanently in the United States, provided he is otherwise admissible under the immigration laws.


*Approved April 17, 1948.*

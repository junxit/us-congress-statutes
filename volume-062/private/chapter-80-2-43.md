# Chapter 43

> AN ACT For the relief of Frankie Stalnaker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Frankie Stalnaker, of Baltimore, Maryland, the sum of $1,500, in full satisfaction of her claim against the United States for reimbursement of medical and hospital expenses incurred by her, and for compensation for personal injuries sustained by her on December 7, 1944, in Baltimore, Maryland, as a result of being struck by a United States Government mail truck.


*Approved February 2, 1948.*

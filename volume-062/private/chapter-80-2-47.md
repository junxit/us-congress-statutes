# Chapter 47

> AN ACT For the relief of John T. Hollandsworth, Junior.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the account of John T. Hollandsworth, Junior, postmaster at Beckley, West Virginia, with the sum of $2,000, on account of a shortage of that amount in his War Savings stamp account.


*Approved February 5, 1948.*

# Chapter 86

> AN ACT Authorizing the issuance of a patent in fee to Charles Ghost Bear, Senior.

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That upon application in writing, the Secretary of the Interior is authorized and directed to issue to Jonah Williams of Sisseton, South Dakota, a patent in fee to the following-described lands allotted to him in the State of South Dakota: The west half of the southeast quarter and the southwest quarter of the northeast quarter of section 32, township 126, range 52 west of the fifth principal meridian.


*Approved February 28, 1948.*

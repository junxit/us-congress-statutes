# Chapter 316

> AN ACT For the relief of George Chan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws the Attorney General is hereby authorized and directed to record the permanent admission to the United States of George Chan as of December 13, 1944. the date on which he was brought to this country by Army airplane. Upon the enactment of this Act the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the quota for persons of the Chinese race of the first year that the said quota is available.


*Approved May 19, 1948.*

# Chapter 60

> AN ACT For the relief of Maria Hedwig Feresz.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, the alien Maria Hedwig Feresz, of Hadley, Massachusetts, a Polish national who entered the United States in 1939 for a temporary stay, shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of January 1, 1939. Upon the enactment of this Act the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the Polish quota for the first year that the same Polish quota is available.


*Approved February 18, 1948.*

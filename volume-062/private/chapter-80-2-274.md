# Chapter 274

> JOINT RESOLUTION To confirm title in fee simple in Joshua Britton to certain lands in Jefferson County, Illinois.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That title in fee simple is hereby confirmed in Joshua Britton who made entry under the Act of 1847 under warrant numbered 52347 on May 21, 1849, at Shawneetown, Illinois, District Land Office, his heirs and assigns, to all rights to and interest in the following-described lands in Jefferson County, Illinois:

South one-half of the southeast quarter of section 23, township 1 south, range 3 east, third principal meridian, Illinois.


*Approved May 11, 1948.*

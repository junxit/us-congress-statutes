# Chapter 743

> AN ACT For the relief of Stefan Magura and Michal Magura.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, Stefan Magura and Michal Magura, of Donora, Pennsylvania, who were admitted into the United States on temporary visas, shall be deemed to have been lawfully admitted into the United States for permanent residence as of April 29, 1948, upon payment of visa fees and head tax.

## § 2.

Upon the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of State is authorized and directed to instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct two numbers from the nonpreference category of the first available immigration quota for Czechoslovakia.


*Approved June 29, 1948.*

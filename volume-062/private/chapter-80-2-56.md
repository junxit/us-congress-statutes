# Chapter 56

> AN ACT For the relief of Ruston Jamsetji Patell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Blouse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws the Attorney General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to record the lawful admission for permanent residence of Ruston Jamsetji Patell as of July 3, 1946, the day after Public Law Numbered 483 of the Seventy-ninth Congress (establishing an annual Indian (racial) quota) was approved. Upon enactment of this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the Indian (racial) quota of the first year that the Indian (racial) quota is hereafter available.


*Approved February 18, 1948.*

# Chapter 361

> An Act For the relief of Leo Hamermann.

*Be it enacted by the Seriate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the Immigration and Naturalization laws the Attorney General is hereby authorized and directed to record the lawful admission for permanent residence of Leo Hamermann. Upon the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the appropriate quota or the first year that such quota is available.


*Approved June 1, 1948.*

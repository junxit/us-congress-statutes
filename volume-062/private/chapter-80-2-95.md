# Chapter 95

> AN ACT Authorizing the issuance of a patent in fee to Tom Eagleman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, upon application in writing, the Secretary of the Interior is authorized and directed to issue to Tom Eagleman, of Pierre, South Dakota, a patent in fee to the southwest quarter of the southeast quarter and the south half of the southwest quarter of section 25 and lot 3 of the southeast quarter of section 26, all in township 108, range 73 west, of the fifth principal meridian, South Dakota: Provided, That when the land herein described is offered for sale, the Crow Creek Sioux and the Lower Brule Sioux Tribes or any Indian who is a member of said tribes shall have ninety days in which to execute preferential rights to purchase said tract at a price offered to the seller by a prospective buyer willing and able to purchase.


*Approved March 3, 1948.*

# Chapter 306

> AN ACT For the relief of Luz Martin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws the Attorney General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to record the lawful admission for permanent residence of Luz Martin as of June 21, 1945, at the port of Norfolk, Virginia. Upon the enactment of this Act the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the quota for the Philippine Islands for the year then current.


*Approved May 19, 1948.*

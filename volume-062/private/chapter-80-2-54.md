# Chapter 54

> AN ACT For the relief of Francesco and Natalia Picchi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 19 (a) of the Immigration Act of February 5, 1917 (39 Stat. 889–890; 54 Stat. 671–673; 56 Stat. 1044; 8 U. S. C. 155), the Attorney General is authorized and directed to permit Francesco and Natalia Picchi of Rockford, Illinois, to remain permanently in the United States.


*Approved February 18, 1948.*

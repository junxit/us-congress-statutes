# Chapter 71

> AN ACT For the relief of Anna Kong Mei.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, the Attorney General is authorized and directed to record Anna Kong Mei as having entered the United States on July 7, 1937, for permanent residence.

## § 2.

The Attorney General is authorized and directed to cancel any warrants of arrest or orders of deportation which may have been issued, and to discontinue any deportation proceedings which may have been commenced, in the case of the said Anna Kong Mei.


*Approved February 25, 1948.*

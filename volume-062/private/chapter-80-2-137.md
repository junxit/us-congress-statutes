# Chapter 137

> AN ACT For the relief of Robert Wilhelm Gerling.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, the Attorney General of the United States shall record the lawful admission for permanent residence of Robert Wilhelm Gerling of New York City, New York, as of August 4, 1939, the date on which he was lawfully temporarily admitted to the United States.


*Approved March 18, 1948.*

# Chapter 268

> AN ACT For the relief of the estate of Carl R. Nall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General is authorized and directed to credit the account of Carl R. Nall, deceased, former postmaster at Sherman, Texas, in such amount as will equal the balance determined by the Comptroller General to be due in such account, as the result of various embezzlements from 1933 to 1945 by one George L. Kolb, former assistant postmaster in the Sherman post office. The estate of the said Carl R. Nall, deceased, and his sureties are hereby relieved of all liability to pay to the United States the whole or any part of the amount credited to the account of the said Carl R. Nall, as provided herein.


*Approved May 10, 1948.*

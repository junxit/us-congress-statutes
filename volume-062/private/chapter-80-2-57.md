# Chapter 57

> AN ACT For the relief of Riyoko Patell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Attorney General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to record the lawful admission for permanent residence of Riyoko Patell, who entered the United States at San Francisco, California, on July 20, 1939, and that the said Riyoko Patell shall, for all purposes under the immigration laws, be deemed to have been lawfully admitted as an immigrant for permanent residence, any provisions of the immigration laws to the contrary notwithstanding. Upon the enactment of this Act the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quotacontrol officer to deduct one number from the quota for persons bom in Japan of the first year that the said quota is available.


*Approved February 18, 1948.*

# Chapter 460

> AN ACT To authorize and direct the Secretary of the Interior to issue to John F. Compton, formerly John Crazy Bull, a patent in fee to certain land.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of theUnited States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, upon application in writing, the Secretary of the Interior is authorized and directedto issue to John F. Compton, formerly John Crazy Bull, RosebudSioux allottee, a patent in fee to the southeast quarter of section 32,township 36 north, range 26 west, sixth principal meridian, SouthDakota, containing one hundred and sixty acres.


*Approved June 12, 1948.*

# Chapter 642

> AN ACT For the relief of Dimitri Petrou.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, the Attorney General is authorized and directed to record the lawful admission for permanent residence of the alien Dimitri Petrou, of New York, New York, as of the 18th of July 1945, the date on which he entered the United States temporarily as a visitor.

Upon the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota control officer to deduct one number from the quota of Greece for the first year such quota is available.


*Approved June 25, 1948.*

# Chapter 318

> AN ACT To legalize the admission to the United States of Sarah Jane Sanford Pansa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Attorney General is hereby directed to record the entry into the United States of Sarah Jane Sanford Pansa at New York, New York, on November 30, 1946, as a lawful admission to the United States for permanent residence for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws. The Secretary of State shall thereupon reduce by one the immigration quota for Italy for the current fiscal year.


*Approved May 19, 1948.*

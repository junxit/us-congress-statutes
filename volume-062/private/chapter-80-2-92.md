# Chapter 92

> AN ACT For the relief of certain officers and employees of the Foreign Service of the United States who, while in the course of their respective duties, suffered losses of personal property by reason of war conditions.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

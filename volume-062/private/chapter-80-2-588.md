# Chapter 588

> AN ACT To authorize the issuance of a land patent to certain public lands, situated in the county of Kauai, Territory of Hawaii, for school purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of Public Lands of the Territory of Hawaii with the consent of the Governor of said Territory of Hawaii be authorized to issue a land patent to the Roman Catholic Bishop of Honolulu, a corporation sole, covering the following described lands: Lots 117, 118, 119, 120, and 122, Kekaha House lots, Waimea, county of Kauai, Territory of Hawaii; said lands to be used for school purposes.


*Approved June 19, 1948.*

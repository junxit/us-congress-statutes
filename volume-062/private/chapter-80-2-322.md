# Chapter 322

> An Act For the relief of Mrs. Maria Smorczewska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws the Attorney General is hereby authorized and directed to record for permanent residence the temporary admission of Mrs. Maria Smorczewska, which occurred at the port of New York on January 9, 1947. Upon the enactment of this Act the Secretary of State shall make a quota deduction of one against the Polish quota for the current year or the first year in which that quota is available.


*Approved May 19, 1948.*

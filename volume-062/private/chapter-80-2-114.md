# Chapter 114

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Gisela Perl (Krausz).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the admin istration of the immigration and naturalization laws the Attorney General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to record the lawful admission for permanent residence of Doctor Gisela Perl (Krausz) as of March 25, 1946, the date she was admitted temporarily to the United States. Upon the enactment of the Act the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the quota for Rumania of the first year that the said quota is available.


*Approved March 11, 1948.*

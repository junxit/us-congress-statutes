# Chapter 201

> AN ACT For the relief of John A. Dilboy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws the Attorney General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to record the lawful admission for permanent residence of John A. Dilboy as of July 2, 1925, the date he was admitted temporarily to the United States. Upon the enactment of this Act the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the Turkish quota of the first year that the said Turkish quota is available.


*Approved April 17, 1948.*

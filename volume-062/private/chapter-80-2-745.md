# Chapter 745

> AN ACT For the relief of Leon Nikolaivich Volkov.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Attorney General is hereby authorized and directed to suspend the deportation of Leon Nikolaivich Volkov for a period of one year from the effective date of this Act.


*Approved June 29, 1948.*

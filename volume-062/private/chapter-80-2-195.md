# Chapter 195

> AN ACT For the relief of Esther Ringel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws the Attorney General is hereby authorized and directed to cancel the outstanding order and warrant of deportation issued pursuant to sections 19 and 20 of the Immigration Act of February 5, 1917 (39 Stat. 889–890; U. S. C., title 8, secs. 155 and 156) and section 14 of the Immigration Act of 1924 (43 Stat. 162; U. S. C., title 8, sec. 214) in the case of Esther Ringel, any previous or existing law to the contrary notwithstanding. From and after the date of the approval of this Act. Esther Ringel should be deemed to be lawfully a resident of the United States.


*Approved April 17, 1948.*

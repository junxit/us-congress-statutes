# Chapter 198

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Keum Nyu Park.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Attorney General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to record Mrs. Keum Nyu Park as having entered the United States for permanent residence on March 9, 1938.


*Approved April 17, 1948.*

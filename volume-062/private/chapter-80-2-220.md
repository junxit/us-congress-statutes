# Chapter 220

> AN ACT For the relief of Martin A. King.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Martin A. King, postmaster at Clarks Summit, Pennsylvania, is relieved of all liability to refund to the United States the sum of $410.53. Such sum represents a shortage in the amount of the said postmaster caused, without fault on his part, by the robbery of post-office funds on the night of July 27–28, 1934, from the post-office vault. The Comptroller General is authorized and directed to credit the account of said postmaster in the sum of $410.53. The sureties of the said postmaster are released from any liability to refund to the United States the whole or any part of such sum of $410.53.


*Approved April 20, 1948.*

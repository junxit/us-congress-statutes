# Chapter 197

> AN ACT To record the lawful admission to the United States for permanent residence of Wilhemina Piper Enz.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Attorney General be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to record the lawful admission for permanent residence of Wilhemina Piper Enz, who entered the United States at New York, New York, on December 1, 1943, and that she shall, for all purposes under the immigration laws, be deemed to have been lawfully admitted as an immigrant for permanent residence.


*Approved April 17, 1948.*

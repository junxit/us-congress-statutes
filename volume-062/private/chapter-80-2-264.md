# Chapter 264

> AN ACT For the relief of James C. Smith, Stephen A. Bodkin, Charles A. Marlin, Andrew J. Perlik, and Albert N. James.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to credit the accounts of James C. Smith, postmaster at Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, Stephen A. Bodkin, former postmaster, and to relieve Charles A. Marlin, Andrew J. Perlik, and Albert N. James, post-office clerks in the Oakland Station post office, Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, from any liability to refund or pay to the United States the sum of $2,578.97, such sum being money-order funds lost in a burglary of the Oakland Station, on March 15, 1944.


*Approved May 4, 1948.*

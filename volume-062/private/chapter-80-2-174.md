# Chapter 174

> AN ACT For the relief of Kazue Oda Takahashi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Attorney General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to record Kazue Oda Takahashi as having entered the United States for permanent residence on July 9, 1937. Upon the enactment of the Act the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the quota for Japan of the first year that the said quota is available.


*Approved April 5, 1948.*

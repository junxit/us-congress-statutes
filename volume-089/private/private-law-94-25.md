# Private Law 94–25

> An Act For the relief of Mrika Mrnacaj.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Mrika Mrnacaj shall be held and considered to have satisfied the requirements of sections 312 and 316 of the Immigration and Nationality Act relating to requirements as to understanding the English language, history, principles, and form of government of the United States and requirements as to periods of residence and physical presence within the United States and, notwithstanding the provisions of section 310(d) of that Act, she may be naturalized at any time after the date of the enactment of this Act if she is otherwise eligible for naturalization under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved December 23, 1975.*

# Private Law 94–21

> An Act For the relief of Dino Mendoza Pascua.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Dino Mendoza Pascua may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1) (F) of such Act upon approval of a petition filed in his behalf by Staff Sergeant and Mrs. Anthony M. Garcia, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of such Act. The natural parents and brothers and sisters of the said Dino Mendoza Pascua shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved December 20, 1975.*

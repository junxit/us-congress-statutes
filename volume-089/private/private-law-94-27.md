# Private Law 94–27

> An Act For the relief of Jana Hlavaty.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Jana Hlavaty shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence on August 26, 1969.


*Approved December 31, 1975.*

# Private Law 94–16

> Joint Resolution To authorize the Secretary of the Senate to pay compensation to Phan Thi Yen.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assemble,*

That notwithstanding any other provision of law, the citizenship or nationality of Phan Thi Yen shall not prohibit the Secretary of the Senate from paying compensation, for a period not to exceed twelve months, to the said Phan Thi Yen while serving as an employee of the Senate.


*Approved July 21, 1975.*

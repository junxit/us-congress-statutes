# Public Law 94–137

> An Act To designate the new Forest Service laboratory at Auburn, Alabama, as the “George W. Andrews Forestry Sciences Laboratory”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Forest Service laboratory being constructed at Auburn, Alabama, shall hereafter be known and designated as the “George W. Andrews Forestry Sciences Laboratory”. Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to such laboratory shall be held to be a reference to the “George W. Andrews Forestry Sciences Laboratory”.


*Approved November 28, 1975.*

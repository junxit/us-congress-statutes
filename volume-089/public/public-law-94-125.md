# Public Law 94–125

> Joint Resolution To extend the authority for the direct purchase of United States obligations by Federal Reserve banks.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

that section 14(b) of the Federal Reserve Act is amended—

- **(1)** by striking out “November 1, 1975” and inserting in lieu thereof “November 1, 1976”; and
- **(2)** by striking out “October 31, 1975” and inserting in lieu thereof “October 31, 1976”.


*Approved November 12, 1975.*

# Public Law 94–113

> An Act To amend the Federal Rules of Evidence, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That rule 801(d) (1) of the Federal Rules of Evidence (88 Stat. 1938) is amended by adding at the end thereof a new clause (C)j as follows: “(C) one of identification of a person made after perceiving him; or”.

## § 2.

This Act shall become effective on the fifteenth day after the date of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved October 16, 1975.*

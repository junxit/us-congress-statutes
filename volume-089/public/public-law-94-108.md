# Public Law 94–108

> An Act To extend until the close of June 30, 1978, the period during which certain dyeing mid tanning materials may be imported free of duty.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** item 907.80 of the Appendix to the Tariff Schedules of the United States (19 U.S.C. 1202) is amended by striking out “9/30/75” and inserting in lieu t hereof “6/30/78”.
- **(b)** The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply with respect to articles entered, or withdrawn from warehouse, for consumption after September 30, 1975.


*Approved October 8, 1975.*

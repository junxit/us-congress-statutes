# Public Law 94–18

> An Act To authorize supplemental appropriations to the Nuclear Regulatory Commission for fiscal year 1975.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is authorized to be appropriated to the Nuclear Regulatory Commission to carry out the provisions of section 261 of the Atomic Energy Act of 1954, as amended, and section 305 of the Energy Reorganization Act of 1974, $50,200,000 for fiscal year 1975.


*Approved April 25, 1975.*

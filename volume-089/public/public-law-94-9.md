# Public Law 94–9

> Joint Resolution To amend the Defense Production Act of 1960, as amended, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsections (h), (i), and (1) of section 720 of the Defense Production Act of 1950, as amended, are amended to read as follows:

> - **(h)** In the first, sentence strike out ‘March 1, 1975’ and insert ‘June 30, 1975’. In the second sentence strike out ‘June 30, 1975’ and insert ‘December 31, 1975’.
> - **(i)**
>
> ## (2)
>
> In the second sentence strike out ‘for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1975’ and insert ‘to remain available until December 31, 1975’.
>
> - **(l)** Strike out ‘for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1975’ and insert ‘to remain available until December 31, 1975’.”


*Approved March 21, 1975.*

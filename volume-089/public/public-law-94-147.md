# Public Law 94–147

> An Act To amend section 218 of title 23, United States Code.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph (1) of subsection (a) of section 218 of title 23, United States Code is amended to read as follows:

> - **(1)** will provide, without participation of funds authorized under this title, all necessary right-of-way for the reconstruction of such highways;”.


*Approved December 12, 1975.*

# Public Law 94–74

> An Act To reserve a site for the use of the Smithsonian Institution.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the portion of the Mall bounded by Third Street, Maryland Avenue, Fourth Street, and Jefferson Drive in the District of Columbia is reserved as a site for the future public uses of the Smithsonian Institution.

## § 2.

The Smithsonian Institution may not make any use of the portion of the Mall described in the first, section of this Act unless such use is first approved by the Congress.


*Approved August 8, 1975.*

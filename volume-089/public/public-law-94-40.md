# Public Law 94–40

> An Act To amend the Forest Pest Control Act of June 25, 1947.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first sentence of section 5 of the Forest Pest Control Act of June 25, 1947 (61 Stat. 177; 16 U.S.C. 594–1 through 594–5), is amended by changing the period at the end thereof to a comma and adding the following: “such Sums appropriated for fiscal year 1975 and thereafter to remain available until expended.”.


*Approved June 20, 1975.*

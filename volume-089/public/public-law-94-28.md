# Public Law 94–28

> An Act To continue the special supplemental food program for women, infants, and children through September 30, 1875.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 17 of the Child Nutrition Act of 1966 (80 Stat. 885, as amended; 42 U.S.C. 1786) is amended—

- **(a)** by inserting after “1975,” in the first sentence of subsection (a) the following: “and for the period July 1, 1975, through September 30, 1975,”; and
- **(b)** by inserting after “1975,” in the third sentence of subsection (b) the following: “and for the period July 1, 1975, through September 30, 1975,”.


*Approved May 28, 1975.*

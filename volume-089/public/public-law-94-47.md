# Public Law 94–47

> An Act To increase the temporary debt limitation until November 15, 1975.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That during the period beginning on the date of the enactment of this Act and ending on November 15, 1975, the public debt limit set forth in the first sentence of section 21 of the Second Liberty Bond Act (31 U.S.C. 757b) shall be temporarily increased by $177,000,000,000.

## § 2.

Effective on the date of the enactment of this Act, the first section of the Act of February 19, 1975, entitled “An Act to increase the temporary debt limitation and to extend such temporary limitation until June 30, 1975” (Public Law 94–3), is hereby repealed.


*Approved June 30, 1975.*

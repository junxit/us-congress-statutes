# Public Law 94–97

> An Act To redesignate November 11 of each year as Veterans Day and to make such day a legal public holiday.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, effective January 1, 1978, section 6103(a) of title 5, United States Code, is amended by striking out—

> “Veterans Day, the fourth Monday in October.”

and inserting in lieu thereof—

> “Veterans Day, November 11.”.


*Approved September 18, 1975.*

# Public Law 94–84

> An Act To designate the John C. Kluczynski Federal Building.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Federal office building at 230 South Dearborn Street, Chicago, Illinois, shall hereafter be known and designated as the “John C. Kluczynski Federal Building”. Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to such building shall be held to be a reference to the John C. Kluczynski Federal Building.


*Approved August 9, 1975.*

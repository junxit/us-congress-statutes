# Public Law 94–77

> An Act To designate the Mountain Park Reservoir, Oklahoma, as the Tom Steed Reservoir.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Mountain Park Reservoir, Oklahoma, authorized to be constructed by the Act of September 21, 1968 (82 Stat. 853), shall be known and designated hereafter as the Tom Steed Reservoir. Any law, regulation, map, document, record, or other paper of the United States in which such reservoir is referred shall be held to refer to such reservoir as the Tom Steed Reservoir.


*Approved August 9, 1975.*

# Public Law 94–46

> An Act To continue for a temporary period the existing suspension of duty on certain istle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** item 903.90 of the Appendix to the Tariff Schedules of the United States (19 U.S.C. 1202) is amended by striking out “9/5/75” and inserting in lieu thereof “6/30/78”.
- **(b)** The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply with respect to articles entered, or withdrawn from warehouse, for consumption after September 5, 1975.

## § 2.

Section 101(f) of Public Law 93–647 is amended by striking “July 1, 1975” and inserting in lieu thereof “August 1, 1975”.


*Approved June 30, 1975.*

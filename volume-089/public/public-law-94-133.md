# Public Law 94–133

> An Act An Acttend the Emergency Petroleum Allocation Act of 1973.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4(g) (1) of the Emergency Petroleum Allocation Act of 1973 is amended by striking out each date specified therein and inserting in lieu thereof in each case “December 15, 1975”.

## § 2.

The amendments made in the first section of this Act to the Emergency Petroleum Allocation Act of 1973 shall take effect at midnight, November 15, 1975.


*Approved November 14, 1975.*

# Public Law 94–159

> Joint Resolution Making further continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 1976, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That clause (c) of section 102 of the joint resolution of June 27, 1975 (Public Law 94–41), is hereby amended by striking out “sine die adjournment of the first session of the Ninety-fourth Congress” and inserting in lieu thereof “March 31, 1976”,


*Approved December 20, 1975.*

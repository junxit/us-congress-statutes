# Public Law 94–109

> An Act To extend the Federal Insecticide, Fungicide, and Rodenticide Act, as amended, for forty-five days.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 27 of the Federal Insecticide, Fungicide, and Rodenticide Act, as amended (7 U.S.C. 136(y)), is amended by adding at the end of such section the following: “There is hereby authorized to be appropriated to carry out the provisions of this Act for the period beginning October 1, 1975, and ending November 15, 1975, the sum of $5,983,500.”.


*Approved October 10, 1975.*

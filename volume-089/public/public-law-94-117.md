# Public Law 94–117

> An Act To provide for the striking of medals in commemoration of the bicentennials of the United States Army, the United States Navy, and the United States Marine Corps.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in commemoration of the Bicentennial of the United States Army on June 14, of the United States Navy on October 13, and of the United States Marine Corps on November 10, 1975, the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to strike three medals, of suitable sizes and metals and with suitable emblems, devices, and inscriptions to be determined by the Secretary of the Army and the Secretary of the Navy, as applicable, subject to the approval of the Secretary of the Treasury.

## § 2.

The Secretary of the Treasury shall furnish the medals to the Secretary of the Army and the Secretary of the, Navy, as applicable, at a price equal to the cost of manufacture.

## § 3.

The Secretary of the Treasury shall also cause such medals to be sold by the Mint, as list medals, under such regulations as he may prescribe, at a price sufficient to cover the cost thereof, including labor, materials, dies, use of machinery, and overhead expenses.


*Approved October 17, 1975.*

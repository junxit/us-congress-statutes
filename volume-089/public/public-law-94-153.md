# Public Law 94–153

> An Act To amend the effective date of the Defense Production Act Amendments of 1975.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That section 9 of the Defense Production Act Amendments of 1975 is amended by striking out “date of enactment of this Act” and inserting in lieu thereof “close of November 30, 1975”.


*Approved December 16, 1975.*

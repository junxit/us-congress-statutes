# Public Law 94–111

> An Act To rescind certain budget authority recommended in the message of the President of July 26, 1975 (H. Doc. 94–225), transmitted pursuant to the Impoundment Control Act of 1974.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and house of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the following rescissions of budget authority contained in the message of the President of July 26, 1975 (H. Doc. 94–225), are made pursuant to the Impoundment Control Act of 1974, namely:

## DEPARTMENT OF INTERIOR BUREAU OF MINES

**Helium Fund**

Contract authority under this head provided by Public Law 87–122 for the fiscal year 1976 is rescinded in the amount of $47,500,000.


*Approved October 13, 1975.*

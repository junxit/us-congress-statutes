# House Concurrent Resolution 27

> “COMPILATION OF THE HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT ACT OF 1974”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be reprinted for the use of the Committee on Banking and Currency two thousand copies of committee print entitled “Compilation of the Housing and Community Development Act of 1974”.


*Passed April 14, 1975.*

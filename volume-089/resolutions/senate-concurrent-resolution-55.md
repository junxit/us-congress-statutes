# Senate Concurrent Resolution 55

> CORRECTION OF ENROLLED BILL S. 555

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (S. 555) to amend the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act, the Secretary of the Senate is hereby authorized and directed, in the enrollment of the said bill, to make the following correction, namely: Insert after the enacting clause the following: “That the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act (the Act) is amended as follows:”.


*Agreed to July 25, 1975.*

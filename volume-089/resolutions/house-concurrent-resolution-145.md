# House Concurrent Resolution 145

> “A COMPILATION OF FEDERAL EDUCATION LAWS”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there is authorized to be printed seven thousand five hundred additional copies of a joint committee print of the Committee on Education and Labor of the House of Representatives and the Committee on Labor and Public Welfare of the Senate entitled “A Compilation of Federal Education Laws”, of which five thousand copies shall be for the use of the Committee on Education and Labor and two thousand five hundred such copies shall be for the use of the Committee on Labor and Public Welfare of the Senate.


*Passed April 14, 1975.*

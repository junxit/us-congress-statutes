# House Concurrent Resolution 405

> CORRECTION OF ENROLLED BILL S. 1247

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Secretary of the Senate is authorized and directed, in the enrollment of the bill (S. 1247) to authorize certain construction at military installations, and for other purposes, to make the following technical corrections:

In section 103, strike out “Section 108 (a),”.

In section 207(b), strike out “section 502” and insert in lieu thereof “section 602”.

In section 307 (c), strike out “$258,874,000” and insert in lieu thereof “$285,874,000”.


*Passed September 29, 1975.*

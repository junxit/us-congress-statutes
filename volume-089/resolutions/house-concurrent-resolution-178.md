# House Concurrent Resolution 178

> “AMNESTY”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed for use of the House Committee on the Judiciary one thousand five hundred copies of the committee hearing, “Amnesty”, Ninety-third Congress.


*Passed June 6, 1975.*

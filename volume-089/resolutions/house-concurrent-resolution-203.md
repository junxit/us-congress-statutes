# House Concurrent Resolution 203

> JOINT MEETING

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Thursday, April 10, 1975, at 9 p.m., for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed April 9, 1975.*

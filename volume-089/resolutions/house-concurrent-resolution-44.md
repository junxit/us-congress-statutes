# House Concurrent Resolution 44

> “OUR AMERICAN GOVERNMENT. WHAT IS IT? HOW DOES IT FUNCTION?”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed, as a House document, a revised edition of the House document “Our American Government. What Is It? How Does It Function?”; and that five hundred and sixty-six thousand additional copies be printed, of which four hundred and forty-two thousand shall be for the use of the House of Representatives and one hundred and three thousand shall be for the use of the Senate, and one thousand shall be for the use of the House Committee on Rules and twenty thousand shall lie for the use of the Joint Committee on Printing.


*Passed May 14, 1975.*

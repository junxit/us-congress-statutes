# Senate Concurrent Resolution 36

> PROPOSED CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENTS TO LIMIT ABORTION-HEARINGS

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on the Judiciary not to exceed two thousand additional copies of each part of its hearings during the second session of the Ninety-third Congress and the first session of the Ninety-fourth Congress on proposed constitutional amendments to limit abortion.


*Agreed to June 26, 1975.*

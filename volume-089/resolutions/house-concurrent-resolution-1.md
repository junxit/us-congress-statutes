# House Concurrent Resolution 1

> JOINT MEETING

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on January 15, 1975, at one o’clock p.m., for the purpose of receiving such communication as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed January 14, 1975.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 17

> “FEDERAL SUBSIDY PROGRAMS”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Joint Economic Committee three thousand additional copies of its joint committee print of the Ninety-third Congress, second session, entitled “Federal Subsidy Programs”.


*Agreed to May 7, 1975.*

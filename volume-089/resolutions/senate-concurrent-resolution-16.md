# Senate Concurrent Resolution 16

> “INCOME SECURITY FOR AMERICANS: RECOMMENDATIONS OF THE PUBLIC WELFARE STUDY”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Joint Economic Committee three thousand additional copies of its joint committee print of the Ninety-third Congress, second session, entitled “Income Security for Americans: Recommendations of the Public Welfare Study”.


*Agreed to May 7, 1975.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 146

> “THE HISTORY AND OPERATION OF THE HOUSE MAJORITY WHIP ORGANIZATION (94TH CONGRESS)”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed as a House document with photographs a revised edition of the booklet entitled “The History and Operation of the House Majority Whip Organization (94th Congress)”; and that ten thousand additional copies shall be printed for the use of the House majority whip.


*Passed May 14, 1975.*

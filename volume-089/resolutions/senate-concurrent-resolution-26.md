# Senate Concurrent Resolution 26

> “CHINA: A QUARTER CENTURY AFTER THE FOUNDING OF THE PEOPLE’S REPUBLIC”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Foreign Relations five thousand copies of the committee print entitled “China: A Quarter Century After the Founding of the People’s Republic.”


*Agreed to June 26, 1975.*

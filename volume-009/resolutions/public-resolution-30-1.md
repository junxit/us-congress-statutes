# Public Resolution 1

> A Resolution authorizing the Erection on the Public Grounds in the City of Washington of a Monument to George Washington.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Washington National Monument Society be, and it is hereby, authorized to erect the proposed monument to the memory of George Washington upon such portion of the public grounds or reservations within the city of Washington, not otherwise occupied, as shall be selected by the President of the United States and the board of managers of said society, as a suitable site on which to erect the said monument, and for the necessary protection thereof.


*Approved, January 31, 1848.*

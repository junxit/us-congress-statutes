# Public Resolution 6

> A Resolution providing for temporary Mail Service in Texas.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster-General be, and he is hereby, authorized to continue the mail service now existing in Texas, under the laws and authority of Texas, or such part thereof as, in his judgment, the public interest may require, from the time that Texas becomes a State in this Union, until contractscan be made, and the mail service put in operation on the past routes in Texas established by Congress at its present session.


*Approved, May 20, 1846.*

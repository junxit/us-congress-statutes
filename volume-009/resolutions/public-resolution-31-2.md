# Public Resolution 2

> A Resolution authorizing the Purchase of the Manuscript Farewell Address of George Washington.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the joint committee on the library be authorized to purchase the manuscript of the Farewell Address to the people of the United States, of George Washington, if the purchase of it can be effected on fair and just terms, in the opinion of the committee.


*Approved, February 12, 1850.*

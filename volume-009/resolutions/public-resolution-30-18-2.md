# Public Resolution 18

> A Resolution to fix the Meaning of the Second Section of an Act for changing the Location of the Land Office in the Chippewa Land District, and establishing an additional Land District in the State of Wisconsin.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the word “Territory,” in the second section of the act entitled “An Act for changing the lo cation of the land office in the Chippewa land district, and establishing an additional land district in the State of Wisconsin,” approved at the present session of Congress, be construed to be and mean “State.”


*Approved, March 3, 1849.*

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

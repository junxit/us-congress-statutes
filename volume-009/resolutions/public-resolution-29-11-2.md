# Public Resolution 11

> A Joint Resolution relative to the Preparation and Presentation of Medals to certain French, British, and Spanish Officers.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States is hereby authorized and requested to cause suitable gold and silver medals to be prepared and presented to the officers and then belonging or attached to the French, British, and Spanish ships-of-war in the harbor of Vera Cruz, who so gallantly, and at the imminent peril of their lives, aided in rescuing from a watery grave many of the officers and crew of the United States brig Somers.


*Approved, March 3, 1847.*

# Public Resolution 10

> A Resolution supplementary to the Resolution of February twentieth, eighteen hundred and forty-five, for distributing the Works of the exploring Expedition.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That of the thirty-seven copies of the narrative and scientific works of the exploring expedition, deposited, and to be deposited, in the library of Congress, one copy shall be presented to the Slate of Florida; and whenever any new State shall be admitted into the Union, one copy of said works shall be presented to such State.


*Approved, July 15, 1846.*

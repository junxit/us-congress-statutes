# Public Resolution 5

> Joint Resolution to supply the Territories of Oregon and Minnesota with the Narrative of the Exploring Expedition.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That of the thirty-seven copies of the Narrative and Scientific works of the Exploring Expedition, deposited and to be deposited in the library of Congress, one copy shall be presented to each of the Territories of Oregon, Minnesota, and such other Territories as may be hereafter organized by act of Congress, to belong to the Territorial libraries of such Territories respectively, and to stand in the place of the copy each of those Territories would be entitled to receive upon being admitted into the Union as a State.


*Approved, February 20, 1850.*

# Public Resolution 15

> Joint Resolution granting to the Secretary of the Treasury further Time to make the Report concerning the Sale and Entry of certain Lands in Cincinnati.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury have until the next session of Congress to make the report required of him by the joint resolution to prohibit the sale at private entry of certain lands in Cincinnati, Ohio, approved March third, one thousand eight hundred and forty-seven.


*Approved, March 3, 1849.*

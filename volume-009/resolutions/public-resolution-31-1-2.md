# Public Resolution 1

> A Resolution for the Appointment of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the vacancies in the board of regents of the Smithsonian Institution of the class “other than members of Congress,” be filled by the reappointment of the late incumbents, viz.: Richard Rush, of Pennsylvania, and Joseph G. Totten, of the city of Washington.(a)


*Approved, December 24, 1850.*

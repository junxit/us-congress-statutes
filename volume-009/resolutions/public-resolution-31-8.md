# Public Resolution 8

> A Resolution expressing the Condolence of Congress for Mrs. Margaret S. Taylor.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be requested to transmit a copy of the proceedings of the two Houses on the tenth instant, in relation to the death of the late President of the United States, to Mrs. Margaret S. Taylor; and to assure her of the profound respect of the two Houses of Congress for her person and character, and of their sincere condolence on the late afflicting dispensation of Providence.


*Approved, July 18, 1850.*

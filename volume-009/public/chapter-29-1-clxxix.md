# Chapter CLXXIX

> An Act authorizing the Payment of certain Claims of the Stale of Alabama.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid to the State of Alabama, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of thirteen thousand four hundred and fifty-five dollars and thirtytwo cents, for moneys paid by the said State for subsistence, supplies, and services, of her local troops, and for provisions and forage furnished the friendly Indians during the Creek and Seminole hostilities, in the years eighteen hundred and thirty-six and eighteen hundred and thirty-seven.


*Approved, August 10, 1846.*

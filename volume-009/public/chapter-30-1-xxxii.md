# Chapter XXXII

> An Act relating to the Collection District of New Orleans, and for other Purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the passage of this act, the city of Lafayette in the State of Louisiana, shall be added to and included in the port of entry of New Orleans; and that the office of surveyor for the city of Lafayette be and is hereby abolished.


*Approved, April 14, 1848.*

# Chapter CXLIV

> An Act to annex the Town of Essex, in the State of Massachusetts, to the Collection District of Gloucester.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the town of Essex, in the State of Massachusetts, now included in the collection district of Newburyport, shall hereafter be included in, and form a part of, the collection district of Gloucester.


*Approved, August 7, 1848.*

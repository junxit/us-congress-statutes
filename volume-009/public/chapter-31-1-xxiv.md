# Chapter XXIV

> An Act authorizing the Legislative Assemblies of Minnesota and Oregon Territories to prolong their next Annual Session to a Period of ninety Days.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the legislative assemblies of Minnesota and Oregon Territories be, and they are hereby, authorized to prolong their next annual session to a period of ninety days, any thing contained in any former act or acts to the contrary notwithstanding.


*Approved, July 18, 1850.*

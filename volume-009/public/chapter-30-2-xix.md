# Chapter XIX

> An Act supplemental to the Act approved the sixth Day of July, eighteen hundred, and forty-two, entitled “An Act confirming certain Land Claims in Louisiana.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a further term of two years, from and after the passage of this act, be, and the same is hereby, conceded to locate the claim named and alluded to in the third and sixth sections of the act to which this act is supplemental, and therein designated as claim number eight.


*Approved, January 19, 1849.*

# Chapter XXXIX

> An Act to amend the Act to change the Time of holding the Circuit and District Courts of the United States for the District of Ohio.(c)

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the term of the Circuit and District Courts of the United States for the district of Ohio now required by law to commence on the third Tuesday of May in each year shall hereafter commence on the third Tuesday of April in each year, and all provisions of law now applicable to the holding of said May term shall apply to the said April term.


*Approved, March 3, 1851.*

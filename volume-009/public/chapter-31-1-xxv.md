# Chapter XXV

> An Act to grant the Franking Privilege to Mrs. Margaret S. Taylor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the franking privilege heretofore accorded to the widows of the deceased Presidents be, and the same is hereby, granted to Mrs. Margaret Smith Taylor, relief of Zachary Taylor, late President of the United States.


*Approved, July 18, 1850.*

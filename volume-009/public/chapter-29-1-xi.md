# Chapter XI

> An Act to authorize the Secretary of the Navy to contract for the Purchase of American water-rolled Hemp for the Use of the Navy.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, That the Secretary of the Navy be and he is hereby authorized to enter into contract, for a period not exceeding three years, for the purchase of American water-rotted hemp for the use of the navy.


*Approved, March 30, 1846.*

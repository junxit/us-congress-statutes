# Chapter CX

> An Act to make Bangor a Port of Entry for Ships or Vessels coming from and beyond the Cape of Good Hope

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Bangor, in the State of Maine, shall be, and hereby is, made a port of entry for ships or vessels coming from or beyond the Cape of Good Hope.


*Approved, July 25, 1848.*

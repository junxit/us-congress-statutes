# Chapter IV

> An Act to continue the Office of the Commissioner of Pensions.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, That the authority given to continue the office of Commissioner of Pensions by the act of the twentieth of January, eighteen hundred and forty-three, entitled “An Act to continue the Office of Commissioner of Pensions,” be extended to the fourth of March, eighteen hundred and forty-nine, and no longer.


*Approved, January 14, 1846.*

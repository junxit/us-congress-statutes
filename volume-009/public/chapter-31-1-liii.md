# Chapter LIII

> An Act to extend the Port of New Orleans.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the port of New Orleans shall be, and is hereby, so extended as to embrace the whole parish of New Orleans on both sides of the Mississippi River.


*Approved, September 16, 1850.*

# Chapter LXX

> An Act to increase the Commissariat of the United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be added to the subsistence department four commissaries of subsistence with the rank of captain, to be taken from the line of the army.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the senior aid-de-camp of the major-general commanding the army may be taken from the captains or majors of the army, and shall be allowed the pay and emoluments of a major of cavalry.


*Approved, September 26, 1850.*

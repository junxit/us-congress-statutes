# Chapter LXIV

> An Act to amend an an Act entitled “An Act to amend ‘An Act to carry into Effect in the States of Alabama and Mississippi tho existing Compacts with those States with Regard to the five per cent. Fund and the School Reservations.’”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of “An Act to amend an Act entitled ‘An Act to carry into Effect in the States of Alabama and Mississippi the existing Compacts with those States with Regard to the five per cent. Fund and the School Reservations,’” approved February twenty-six, eighteen hundred and forty-five, be, and the same are hereby, extended so as to enable the State of Alabama to locate a quantity of land in any of the States or Territories equal to the quantity now due to the inhabitants of the township within the Chickasaw cession within said State: Provided, That they shall be made subject to the restrictions and limitations of the act the title of which has been cited, as far as the same may be applicable.


*Approved, March 3, 1847.*

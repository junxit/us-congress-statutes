# Chapter XXIII

> An Act making Appropriations for the Payment of Navy Pensions for the Year ending the thirtieth of June, one thousand eight hundred and fifty-two.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the following sums be, and the same are hereby appropriated out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the pay of navy pensions, for the year ending thirtieth June, one thousand eight hundred and fifty-two.

For invalid pensions, forty thousand dollars.


*Approved, March 3, 1851.*

# Chapter XXXV

> An Act authorizing a Term of the United States Circuit and District Courts at Chicago, Illinois.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a term of the Circuit and District Courts of the United States for the State of Illinois shall hereafter be held at the city of Chicago, on the first Monday of July in each year.


*Approved, May 9, 1848.*

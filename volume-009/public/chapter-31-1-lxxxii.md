# Chapter LXXXII

> An Act to authorize the Appointment of Indian Agents in California.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be authorized to appoint, with the advice and consent of the Senate, not more than three agents for the Indian tribes within the State of California. Such agents shall perform the duties now prescribed by law to Indian agents, and shall receive an annual compensation of three thousand dollars each.


*Approved, September 28, 1850.*

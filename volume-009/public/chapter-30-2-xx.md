# Chapter XX

> An Act to continue the Office of the Commissioner of Pensions.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the authority Office of Commissioner of Pensions continued till further legislation. 1846, ch. 4. given to continue the office of Commissioner of Pensions by the act of the fourteenth of January, eighteen hundred and forty-six, entitled “An Act to continue the office of Commissioner of Pensions,” be extended until further legislation by Congress; and that said Commis-sioner shall receive the same rate of compensation which was paid to him during the year ending December thirty-first, eighteen hundred and forty-eight.


*Approved, January 19, 1849.*

# Chapter XLI

> An Act for the Relief of Elipkas C. Brown.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of Eliphas C. Brown, a soldier in the service of the United States during the war in Florida, be placed on the roll of invalid pensions, at the rate of eight dollars a month, commencing the first of January, eighteen hundred and forty-six, to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, August 17, 1850.*

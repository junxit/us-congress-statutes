# Private Resolution 6

> Joint Resolution authorising a Settlement of the Accounts of Thomas M. Howe, late Pension Agent at Pittsburg, upon equitable Principles.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the Treasury Department be, and they are hereby, authorized to settle and adjust the accounts of Thomas M. Howe, late pension agent at Pittsburg, in the State of Pennsylvania, according to the principles of equity; and to admit the vouchers without regard to strict legal rules, if to them it shall appear that said vouchers are in all other respects correct.


*Approved, February 22, 1849.*

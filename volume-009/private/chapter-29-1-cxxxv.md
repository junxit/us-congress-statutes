# Chapter CXXXV

> An Act for the Relief of William Pool.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, directed to place the name of William Pool on the pension list of invalid pensioners of the United States; and that said William Pool be entitled to receive a pension at the rate of four dollars per month from the first day of January, eighteen hundred and forty-six.


*Approved, August 8, 1846.*

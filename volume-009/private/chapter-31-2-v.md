# Chapter V

> An Act to amend an Act entitled “An Act for the Relief of Frederic Durrive,” approved August fourteen, eighteen hundred and forty-eight.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proviso contained in the first section of the above-entitled act be, and the same is hereby, repealed.(a)


*Approved, February 14, 1851.*

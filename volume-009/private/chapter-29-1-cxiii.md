# Chapter CXIII

> An Act for the Relief of William Gump.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of Winbe authorized and directed to place the name of William Gump on the roll of invalid pensioners, and that he be paid at the rate of eight dollars per month during his life, to commence on the sixth day of July, eighteen hundred and forty-three.


*Approved, August 8, 1846.*

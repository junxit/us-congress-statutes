# Chapter XL

> An Act for the Relief of Charles Wilson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, directed to place the name of Charles Wilson (late a sergeant of Company D., second regiment of United States artillery) on the roll of invalid pensioners, at the rate of sixteen dollars per month, to commence on the first day of July, eighteen hundred and forty-eight, and continue during his natural life.


*Approved, February 1, 1849.*

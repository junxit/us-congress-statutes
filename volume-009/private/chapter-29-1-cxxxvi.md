# Chapter CXXXVI

> An Act for the Relief of John McAllister.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay, out of any unappropriated money, to John McAllister, of Troup county, Georgia, the sum of fifty dollars, that being the amount still due him as encouragement for his enlistment into the army of the United States, under the provisions of the act of Congress approved twenty-seventh January, eighteen hundred and fourteen.


*Approved, August 8, 1846.*

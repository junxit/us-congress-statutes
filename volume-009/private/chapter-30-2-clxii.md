# Chapter CLXII

> An Act for the Relief of Hervey Jones.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of Hervey Jones, a private in the militia of New York during the last war, be placed upon the list of invalid pensioners, at the rate of eight dollars per month, commencing the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and forty-eight, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, March 3, 1849.*

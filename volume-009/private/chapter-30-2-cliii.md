# Chapter CLIII

> An Act for the Relief of Major Charles Larrabee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War cause to be paid to Major Charles Larrabee a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension now allowed him, to commence on the fourth day of September, eighteen hundred and forty-eight, and continue during his natural life.


*Approved, March 3, 1849.*

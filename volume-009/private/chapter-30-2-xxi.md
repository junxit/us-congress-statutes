# Chapter XXI

> An Act for the Relief of Mrs. Anne W. Angus.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a pension of fifty dollars per month, for the term of five years, to commence on the fourth of March next, be paid to Anne W. Angus, widow of Samuel Angus, late a post captain in the navy, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated: Provided, That, in case of the death of said Anne W. Angus during the said terra, said grant shall enure to the benefit of her children for the remainder thereof; and in case of her ceasing to be a widow, said grant shall cease altogether.


*Approved, January 19, 1849.*

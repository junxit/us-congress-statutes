# Chapter CLXXII

> An Act for the Relief of Gustavus Dorr.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Gustavus Dorr, late captain in the sixth regiment of United States infantry, on the roll of invalid pensioners, and pay to him the sum of twenty dollars per month, during his natural life, commencing on the eighteenth day of November, eighteen hundred and forty-seven.


*Approved, August 12, 1848.*

# Chapter CXLVIII

> An Act for the Relief of Aquilla Goodwin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of Aquilla Goodwin be placed upon the roll of invalid pensioners, and that he be allowed and paid the sum of six dollars per month, for and during his life, to be computed from the first day of January, eighteen hundred and forty-six.


*Approved, August 8, 1846.*

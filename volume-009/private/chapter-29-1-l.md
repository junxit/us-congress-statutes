# Chapter L

> An Act for the Relief of Joseph M. Rhea, of the County of Sullivan, in the State of Tennessee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed and required to place the name of Joseph M. Rhea, of the State of Tennessee, upon the roll of invalid pensioners, and that he pay him at the rate of four dollars per month, during his natural life, commencing on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and forty-six.


*Approved, July 15, 1846.*

# Chapter CXLII

> An Act for the Relief of Patrick Kelly, senior, of the County of Indiana, State of Pennsylvania.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to place the name of Patrick Kelly, senior, on the pension roll, according to the act of seventh June, eighteen hundred and thirty-two, for six months’ service as a private, and that his pension commence the twenty-sixth September, eighteen hundred and forty-five.


*Approved, August 8, 1846.*

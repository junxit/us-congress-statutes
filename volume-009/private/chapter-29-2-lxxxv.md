# Chapter LXXXV

> An Act for the Relief of James Jones, of the City of Brooklyn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of tHe United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of James Jones on the pension roll of invalid pensioners, at the rate of twelve dollars per month, to commence on the second June, one thousand eight hundred and forty-one.


*Approved, March 3, 1847.*

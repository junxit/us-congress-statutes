# Chapter XXVI

> An Act for the Relief of Peter Frost.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be required to place the name of Peter Frost, of Waterboro, in the State of Maine, upon the roll of invalid pensioners, and that he be allowed at the rate of eight dollars per month, to commence the first day of July, eighteen hundred and forty-six.


*Approved, February 25, 1847.*

# Chapter CXXXII

> An Act for the Relief of Captain John Patton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be authorized and directed to place the name of John Patton on the roll of invalid pensioners, and that he be paid at the rate of twenty dollars per mouth during his life, to commence on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and forty-six.


*Approved, August 8, 1846.*

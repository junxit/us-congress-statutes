# Chapter XXVII

> An Act for the Relief of James H. Conley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be, and hereby is, appropriated, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of two hundred and twenty-two dollars and eighty-eight cents, in addition to the amount appropriated by the “Act for the relief of James H. Conley,” approved March three, eighteen hundred and forty-seven, the sum of both of said appropriations to be paid to the said James H. Conley in full payment for his services as acting carpenter on board the United States steam-ship Princeton, from the eighth day of September, eighteen hundred and forty-three, to the seventh day of January, eighteen hundred and forty-six, subject to deduction of such amounts as have already been paid him for his said services, and of his monthly contribution due to the hospital fund.


*Approved, January 26, 1849.*

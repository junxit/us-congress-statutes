# Chapter CLXXXI

> An Act for the Relief of George V. Mitchell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That George V. Mitchell, late postmaster at Bellsvilie, Pennsylvania, be, and he hereby is, released from the payment of a judgment obtained against him in the United States Court for the western district of Pennsylvania, held at Williamsport.


*Approved, August 14, 1848.*

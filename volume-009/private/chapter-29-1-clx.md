# Chapter CLX

> An Act granting a Pension to Abraham Ansman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place the name of Abraham Ansman, of Clermont county, in the State of Ohio, on the roll of invalid pensions, at the rate of six dollars per month, to continue during his natural life, and commence from the first day of January, anno Domini eighteen hundred and forty-six.


*Approved, August 8, 1846.*

# Private Resolution 3

> A Resolution directing the Accounting Officers of the Treasury to adjust the Account of Newton Lane, late Pension Agent at Louisville, Kentucky.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, directed, in adjusting the account of Newton Lane, late pension agent at Louisville, Kentucky, to place to the credit of the said Newton Lane the entire amount paid by him to the heirs of Mary Edwards, deceased, the same having been paid in conformity with the directions of the commissioner of pensions, as conveyed upon the face of a certificate of pension issued by said commissioner to said Mary Edwards on the eleventh day of January, eighteen hundred and forty-nine.


*Approved, February 12, 1850.*

# Chapter LVIII

> An Act for the Relief of Isabella Baldridge, Widow of Captain John Baldridge.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to place the name of Isabella Baldridge, widow of John Baldridge, deceased, an officer in the revolutionary war, on the pension roll, and that she be paid, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, a pension, for the service of her husband as a captain in the revolutionary war, at the rate of four hundred and eighty dollars per annum, commencing on the fourth of September, eighteen hundred and forty-three.


*Approved, July 18, 1846.*

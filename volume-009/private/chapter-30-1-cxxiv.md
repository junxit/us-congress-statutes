# Chapter CXXIV

> An Act for the Relief of John Anderson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of two thousand three hundred and thirty-three dollars and eighty-two and a half cents be paid to John Anderson, of Jefferson county, Missouri, for forty-eight thousand five hundred and seventy-four pounds of lead, received by the agent of the United States as rent for the use of a lead mine which belonged to said Anderson at the time the rent was paid.


*Approved, August 5, 1848.*

# Chapter LXVIII

> An Act for the Relief of Jesse Young.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, required to pay to Jesse Young (whose name is now on the roll of invalid pensions) at the rate of sixteen dollars per month, instead of the pension now received by him, to commence on the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and forty-eight.


*Approved, February 22, 1849.*

## Marginal notes

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

# Chapter XCVIII

> An Act for the Relief of Susan Brum.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Susan Brum, widow of Philip Brum, upon the navy pension list, at the rate of twenty dollars a month, commencing on the first day of June, eighteen hundred and forty-three, and continuing during her natural life.


*Approved, March 3, 1847.*

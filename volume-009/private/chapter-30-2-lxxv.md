# Chapter LXXV

> An Act for the Relief of John Hibbert.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of John Hibbert, of Wiscasset, in the State of Maine, upon the roll of invalid pensions, and pay to the said Hibbert the sum of eight dollars per month, from the first day of January, anno Domini eighteen hundred and forty-six, during his natural life.


*Approved, February 26, 1849.*

# Private Resolution 21

> Joint Resolution for the Relief of John B. Denton and Curtis Humphreys.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That John B. Denton and Curtis Humphreys, and their guarantors, be relieved from any supposed responsibility they may have incurred by reason of the failure of the said John B. Denton and Curtis Humphreys to comply with their contract with the post-office department.


*Approved, August 8, 1846.*

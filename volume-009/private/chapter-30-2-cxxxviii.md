# Chapter CXXXVIII

> An Act for the Relief of Mary G. Leverett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the list of revolutionary pensioners the name of Mary G. Leverett, widow of Thomas Leverett, and that she be paid a pension at the rate of eighty dollars per annum, during her natural life, commencing on the fourth day of September, in the year of our Lord eighteen hundred and forty-seven.


*Approved, March 3, 1849.*

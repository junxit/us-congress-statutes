# Private Resolution 14

> A Resolution in Relation to the naval Pension of John McGarr.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he hereby is, authorized to pay to Catharine McGarr, wife of John McGarr, a naval pensioner, the amount of pension which shall appear on the books to be due to him, and not called for by him or any one duly authorized by him.


*Approved, July 5, 1848.*

# Chapter LXXVII

> An Act for the Relief of the Assignees of the late Bank of Alexandria.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper officers of the Treasury Department, when they shall be satisfied that the principal of the debt owing by the late Bank of Alexandria to the United States has been paid, be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to close the account against the said bank, its trustees and assignees, upon the books of the Treasury Department.


*Approved, March 3, 1847.*

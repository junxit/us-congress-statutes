# Chapter XLV

> An Act for the Relief of William B. Keene.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the entry heretofore made at the land office at Ouachita, in the State of Louisiana, of the east half of the south-east quarter of section twenty-six, in township twenty north, of range twelve east, by certificate number four thousand eight hundred and seventeen, be, and the same is hereby, confirmed, and a patent shall be issued therefor in the same manner as for other lands entered at the same office.


*Approved, March 2, 1847.*

# Chapter CLIV

> An Act for the Relief of Alexander Montgomery, Captain and Assistant Quartermaster of the Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of six thousand dollars be, and is hereby, appropriated, out of any unexpended money in the treasury of the United States, to indemnify Alexander Montgomery, captain and assistant quartermaster, for losses actually sustained as a disbursing officer of the United States in the war with Mexico.


*Approved, March 3, 1849.*

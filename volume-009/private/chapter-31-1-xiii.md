# Chapter XIII

> An Act giving the Assent of Congress to the Leasing of a Portion of the Margin of the Black Warrior River for the Purposes therein mentiotied.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the consent of Congress be, and it is hereby, given to the corporation of the city of Tuscaloosa to lease to the Tuscaloosa Plank Road Company so much of the margin of the Black Warrior River at the termination of said road as may be necessary for a warehouse and landing on said river, any thing contained in the act of Congress of the twenty-sixth May, eighteen hundred and twenty-four, “granting to the corporation of Tuscaloosa certain lots and privileges over the reservations and commons in said town,” to the contrary notwithstanding.


*Approved, May 23, 1850.*

# Chapter XCV

> An Act for the Relief of Zachariah Simmons, of the State of Tennessee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, required to place the name of Zachariah Simmons upon the pension roll; and that he pay him at the rate of six dollars per month during his natural life, commencing upon the first day of January, eighteen hundred and forty-six.


*Approved, March 3, 1847.*

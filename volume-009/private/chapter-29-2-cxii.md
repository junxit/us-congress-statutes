# Chapter CXII

> An Act for the Relief of James H. Conley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be, and is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one thousand and fifty-eight dollars and ninety-six cents, to be paid to James H. Conley, or his legal representative, in full payment for his services, as acting carpenter on board the United States steamship Princeton, from the eighth day of September, eighteen hundred and forty-three, to the seventh day of January, eighteen [hundred] and forty-six.


*Approved, March 3, 1847.*

# Chapter LXV

> An Act to authorize the constituted Authorities of the City of Dubuque, in the State of Iowa, to enter certain Islands between the Landings of said City and the main Channel of the Mississippi River.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the constituted authorities of the city of Dubuque, in the State of Iowa, be, and they are hereby, authorized, to enter the islands in the Mississippi River, opposite the said city, which are fractions within sections nineteen and thirty, in township eighty-nine north, in range three east, and in section twenty-five, township eighty-nine north, range two east, at the minimum price of the public lands: Provided, Said entries shall be made within six months from the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1847.*

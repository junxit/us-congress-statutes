# Chapter LII

> An Act for the Relief of Joseph Watson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be authorized and directed to place the name of Joseph Watson on the roll of invalid pensioners; and that he be paid at the rate of four dollars per month during his life, to commence on the sixth day of December, eighteen hundred and forty-five.


*Approved, July 15, 1846.*

# Chapter CXVI

> An Act granting a Pension to Joseph Dusseau.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to place the name of Joseph Dusseau, of Michigan, upon the list of invalid pensioners, at the rate of six dollars per month, and that he be entitled to receive the same from the first day of January, eighteen hundred and forty-six, for and during his natural life.


*Approved, August 8, 1846.*

# Chapter XXVIII

> An Act for the Relief of Thomas Badger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Thomas Badger, now a resident of the town of Plattsburgh, in the county of Clinton, in the State of New York, on the list of invalid pensioners; and that the said Badger receive a pension at the rate of eight dollars a month, to commence on the fourteenth day of January, eighteen hundred and forty-eight.


*Approved, January 26, 1849.*

# Chapter CLXVII

> An Act for the Relief of Mary Phelps, of the County of Genesee, State of New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to place the name of Mary Pheips on the revolutionary pension roll, at the rate of ninety-six dollars per annum, to commence the seventeenth day of February, eighteen hundred and forty-five, for two years’ service of her husband, Richard Austen.


*Approved, August 8, 1846.*

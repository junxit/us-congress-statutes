# Chapter CLXV

> An Act for the Relief of John Campbell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid to John Campbell, of Penobscot county, Maine, the sum of two hundred and eighty-eight dollars, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, it being for arrears of pension from January, eighteen hundred and forty, to January, eighteen hundred and forty-six.


*Approved, March 3, 1849.*

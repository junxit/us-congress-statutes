# Private Resolution 6

> Joint Resolution for the Settlement of the Accounts of Purser G. R. Barry.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the accounting officers of the treasury be authorized to settle the accounts of the United States ship Boston, Captain Pendergrast, and pass to the credit of the purser, G. R. Barry, the items charged to him in the reconciling statement of his accounts.


*Approved, March 2, 1847.*

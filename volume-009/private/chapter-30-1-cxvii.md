# Chapter CXVII

> An Act for the Relief of the Society for the Reformation of Juvenile Delinquents in the City of New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the judgment rendered in the District Court of the United States for the southern district of New York, in the case of the United States against the managers of the Society for the Reformation of Juvenile Delinquents, so far as the same remains unpaid, be, and the same is hereby, forever released and discharged; and that the proper law officer of the United States in said court cause satisfaction thereof to be entered of record.


*Approved, July 25, 1848.*

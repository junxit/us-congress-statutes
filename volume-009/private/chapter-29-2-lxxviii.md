# Chapter LXXVIII

> An Act to increase the Pension of Roswell Hale.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension of Roswell Hale be increased so that he shall be entitled to receive, from and after the first day of January, eighteen hundred and forty-six, the sum of eight dollars per month during his natural life, in lieu of the pension which he now receives under the act of the twenty-third April, eighteen hundred.


*Approved, March 3, 1847.*

# Chapter LXXXIII

> An Act for the Relief of James Low.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of James Low on the pension roll of invalid pensioners, at the rate of six dollars per month, commencing on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and forty-six.


*Approved, August 4, 1846.*

# Chapter CLII

> An Act for the Relief of James Gee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, authorized and required to pay to James Gee, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, an arrearage of pension at the rate of four dollars per month, from the twenty-second day of September, eighteen hundred and thirty-seven, to the third day of December, eighteen hundred and forty-two.


*Approved, August 8, 1846.*

# Chapter LVII

> An Act for the Relief of Nathaniel Stafford.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, directed to cause to be paid to the legally authorized guardian of Nathaniel Stafford, of Vermont, who became insane by exposure and extreme hardship in the military service of the United States, the amount of arrearage which may be found due to him, at the rate of eight dollars per month from the seventeenth of May, eighteen hundred and fourteen, to the thirtieth day of March, eighteen hundred and forty-six, at which time his pension, already allowed, commenced under existing laws.


*Approved, July 18, 1846.*

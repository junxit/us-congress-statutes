# Chapter LXIX

> An Act for the Relief of W. B. Slaughter, late Secretary of the Territory of Wisconsin.*

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the accounting officers of the Treasury Department be authorized and directed to settle the accounts of W. B. Slaughter, late Secretary of the Treasury of Wisconsin, upon the principles of equity and justice; and that they cause to be paid to him the balance, if any be due him, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated: Provided, That such balance shall not exceed the sum of one thousand seven hundred and thirty-one dollars and fifty-one cents.


*Approved, June 24, 1848.*

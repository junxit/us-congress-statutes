# Chapter LIII

> An Act for the Relief of John Ficklin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be authorized and directed to place the name of John Ficklin on the roll of invalid pensioners; and that he be paid a pension, at the rate of eight dollars per month, during his life, to commence on the twenty-second day of March, eighteen hundred and forty-four.


*Approved, July 15, 1846.*

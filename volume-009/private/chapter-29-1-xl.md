# Chapter XL

> An Act for the Relief of Joseph Kemball.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, directed to pay to Joseph Kemball, or his legal representatives, the sum of one hundred and ninetyeight dollars and forty-seven cents, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, being the balance still due on one of Thomas Tupper’s due bills of twelve hundred and sixty-two dollars and forty-seven cents, after deducting a credit of thirty-nine dollars and forty-seven cents, given by him for work done on Madison barracks, at Detroit, in eighteen hundred and sixteen; one thousand and twenty-three dollars and eighty-six cents of which was allowed to the credit of the assignees under the act for the relief of Jesse Smith and others, passed June fourteenth, eighteen hundred and thirty-six.


*Approved, July 15, 1846.*

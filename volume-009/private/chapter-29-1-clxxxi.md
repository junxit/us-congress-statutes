# Chapter CLXXXI

> An Act for the Relief of the Heirs and Legal Representatives of Richard C. Allen, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the heirs or legal representatives of Richard C. Allen, deceased, late of the Territory (now State) of Florida, be authorized to enter and patent, under such regulations as the Secretary of the Treasury may prescribe, one section of public land, according to legal sub-divisions, subject to sale in the State of Florida, to which there may be no pre-emption right, in full satisfaction of all claims for the services and expenses of said A lien in locating lands reserved for a seminary of learning in said State.


*Approved, August 10, 1846.*

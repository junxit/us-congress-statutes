# Chapter CLXXXVIII

> An Act for the Relief of Alexis Ayot.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to place the name of Alexis Ayot on the list of invalid pensioners, and to pay him at the rate of ten dollars per month, to commence on the twenty-fourth day of July, eighteen hundred and forty-four.


*Approved, August 10, 1846.*

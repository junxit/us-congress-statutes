# Chapter LXXIII

> An Act for the Relief of Harrison Whitson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Harrison Whitson, of Clinton county, and State of Missouri, the sum of two hundred dollars, in full satisfaction of his claim against the United States for services rendered and supplies furnished, while on the march to be mustered into the United States service, the company of Captain Cornelius Gilliam, of the Missouri volunteers, that served in Florida.


*Approved, March 3, 1847.*

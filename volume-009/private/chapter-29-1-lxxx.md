# Chapter LXXX

> An Act for the Relief of John Keith, of New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to place the name of John Keith, of Madison county, New York, on the roll of revolutionary pensioners, under the act of the seventh of June, eighteen hundred and thirty-two, and allow him a pension for six months’ service as a private, in pursuance of said act.


*Approved, August 3, 1846.*

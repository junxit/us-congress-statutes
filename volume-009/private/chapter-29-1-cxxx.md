# Chapter CXXX

> An Act for the Relief of Isaiah Parker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, required and authorized to pay Isaiah Parker the sura of three hundred and twenty-eight dollars, for arrears of pension from March fourth, eighteen hundred and twenty-three, to October thirty-one, eighteen hundred and thirty-six, at the rate of two dollars per month, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, August 8, 1846.*

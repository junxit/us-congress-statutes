# Chapter CLXI

> An Act granting a Pension to Elijah C. Babbit.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War is hereby directed to place the name of Elijah C. Babbit on the list of invalid pensioners of the United States; and that said Babbit be entitled to receive a pension of four dollars per month, from the first day of January, eighteen hundred and forty-six, and to con-tinue during his natural life.


*Approved, August 8, 1846.*

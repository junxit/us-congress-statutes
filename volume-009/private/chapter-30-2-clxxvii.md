# Chapter CLXXVII

> An Act for the Relief of John J. Young, a Commander in the Navy of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy cause the name of John J. Young, a commander in the navy of the United States, to be placed upon the navy pension list, and the same amount paid to said John J. Young which was paid to him prior to the act of Congress of first August, eighteen hundred and forty-one, to wit: twenty-five dollars per month. Said payment to be made according to the rules and regulations now existing, and commencing on the first of August, eighteen hundred and forty-one, the time when the pension of said Young was suspended; which payments shall be made from the navy pension fund, or out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 3, 1849.*

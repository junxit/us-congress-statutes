# Chapter XLII

> An Act for the further Relief of John Mitchell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension allowed to John Mitchell, by the act for his relief, approved the 29th day of May, one thousand eight hundred and forty-eight, be increased, and that he be paid, semi-annually, from and after the fifteenth day of May, one thousand eight hundred and fifty, at the rate of twenty dollars per month, during his life; and the Secretary of War is hereby required to make said payment accordingly.


*Approved, August 17, 1850.*

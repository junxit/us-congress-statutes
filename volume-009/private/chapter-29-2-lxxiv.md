# Chapter LXXIV

> An Act for the Relief of William Causey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of William Causey, of Gilford county, North Carolina, on the roll of invalid pensioners, and to pay him a pension at the rate of four dollars per month, from the ninth day of February, eighteen hundred and forty-six.


*Approved, March 3, 1847.*

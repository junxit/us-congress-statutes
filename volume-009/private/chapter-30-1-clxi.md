# Chapter CLXI

> An Act for the Relief of Alborne Allen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid to Alborne Allen, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of four hundred and thirty-two dollars, for the balance due on a contract for building a boat called the Æolus, on account of the government.


*Approved, August 11, 1848.*

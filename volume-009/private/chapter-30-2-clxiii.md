# Chapter CLXIII

> An Act for the Relief of Eve Boggs.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be authorized and directed to place the name of Eve Boggs, of the county of Davidson, and State of North Carolina, widow of John Boggs, on the revolutionary pension roll, and that she be paid at the rate of eighty dollars per annum, during her life, to commence on the fourth day of September, eighteen hundred and forty-seven.


*Approved, March 3, 1849.*

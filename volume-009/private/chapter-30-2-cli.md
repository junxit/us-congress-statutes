# Chapter CLI

> An Act for the Relief of Captain Alexander McEwen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assetnbled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, required to place the name of Alexander McEwen on the roll of invalid pensions, and that he pay him, semiannually, at the rate of ten dollars per month, for and during his natural life, commencing on the fifteenth day of May, one thousand eight hundred and forty-seven.


*Approved, March 3, 1849.*

# Chapter CXXXI

> An Act for the Relief of Mary Mac Rea, Widow of Lieutenant Colonel William Mac Rea, late of the United States Army, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in considera-tion of the long, arduous, and faithful services of William Mac Rea, late of the United States army, deceased, commencing in seventeen hundred and ninety-one, and continuing through the Indian wars on the north-western frontier of the United States, in which he was distinguished for gallantry and good conduct, and was severely wounded in battle, and also through the late war with Great Britain, in which he rendered valuable and efficient service, as well in battle as in preparation of the means of defence, to the period of his death, while in service, in eighteen hundred and thirty-two, comprising a period of more than forty years’ continuous service, embracing two wars, and of the destitute condition of his widow, there be granted and paid to Mary Mac Rea, widow of the said Lieutenant-Colonel William Mac Rea, annually, for five years, in semiannual payments, a sura equal to one half the pay to which the said William Mac Rea was entitled at the time of his decease, commencing on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and forty-six, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated: Provided, She should live so long.


*Approved, March 3, 1849.*

# Chapter CXXXIII

> An Act for the Relief of Lois Mattison, of the County of Jefferson, State of New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to place the name of Lois Mattison on the pension list, at the rate of forty dollars per annum, to commence the fifteenth day of November, one thousand eight hundred and forty-four.


*Approved, August 8, 1846.*

# Chapter CLXIV

> An Act for the Relief of John Farnham.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of John Farnham, of Woolwich, in the county of Lincoln, and State of Maine, on the roll of invalid pensions, and that he pay to him the sum of five dollars per month, to commence on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and forty-eight, to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, August 11, 1848.*

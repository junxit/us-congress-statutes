# Chapter CII

> An Act for the Relief of William T. Walthall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Fourth Auditor of the Treasury be directed and required to audit and settle the account of William T. Walthall, for his services as acting professor of mathematics, from the twenty-fourth day of May, anno Domini eighteen hundred and forty-three, to the twenty-fifth day of February, anno Domini eighteen hundred and forty-five, inclusive; and that he include also in said settlement, and allow to said Walthall, the amounts allowed and paid by the purser of the frigate Brandywine, on account of his board and medicine and medical attendance at Hong Kong, in China, during the time when, by advice of the naval surgeon, he was left sick ashore at that place in the absence of the East India squadron.


*Approved, March 3, 1847.*

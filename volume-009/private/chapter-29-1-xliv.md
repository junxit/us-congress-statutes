# Chapter XLIV

> An Act for the Relief of Semington Buffenbarger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of fifty dollars, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to be paid to Semington Buffenbarger, of South Charleston, in the county of Clark, in the State of Ohio, it being for money wrongfully received by the receiver of the land office at Waupaukonetta district, in said State of Ohio, for the north-east quarter of the north-east quarter of section four, in township number six south, of range number eight east.


*Approved, July 15, 1846.*

# Chapter XCII

> An Act for the Relief of E. B. Cogswell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to E. B. Cogswell, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of twelve hundred dollars, for services rendered to the Texas Indians, and for coal and iron furnished to the same, as armorer and public blacksmith, from the twentieth May, eighteen hundred and forty-six, to the twentieth May, eighteen hundred and forty-seven.


*Approved, March 2, 1849.*

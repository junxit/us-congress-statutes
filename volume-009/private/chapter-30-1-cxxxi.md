# Chapter CXXXI

> An Act for the Relief of Catharine Fulton, of Washington County, Pennsylvania.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War he, and he is hereby, authorized and required to place the name of Catharine Fulton upon the revolutionary pension roll, under the rates fixed by the acts of the seventh of July, eighteen hundred and thirty-eight, and third of March, eighteen hundred and forty-three., as the widow of Captain Samuel Fulton, for twelve months’ service as a captain in the revolutionary army; and that her pension commence on the first of January, eighteen hundred and forty-five.


*Approved, August 5, 1848.*

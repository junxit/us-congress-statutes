# Chapter XXIX

> An Act for the Relief of Joseph D. Ward and Isaac Walts Griffith.

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of Joseph D. Ward, who was a sergeant in the second regiment of Kentucky volunteers in the late war with Mexico, be placed on the roll of invalid pensions at the rate of twenty dollars a month, commencing on the ninth day of June, eighteen hundred and forty-seven, and to continue during his natural life.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Isaac Walts Griffith, late a sergeant in the army of the United States, on the pension list, at the rate of sixteen dollars per month, to commence on the twenty-eighth day of October, one thousand eight hundred and fortyseven, and to continue during his life, in lieu of the pension to which he is now entitled by law.


*Approved, March 3, 1851.*

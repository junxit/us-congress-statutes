# Chapter XVIII

> An Act for the Relief of Jesse Sutton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Jesse Sutton, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one thousand dollars, for services rendered, and coal, iron, and steel furnished the Texas Indians, as public blacksmith, from the twentieth of May, eighteen hundred and forty-six, to the twentieth of May, eighteen hundred and forty-seven.


*Approved, June 5, 1850.*

# Chapter XXIX

> An Act for the Relief of Andrew Moore.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be authorized and directed to place the name of Andrew Moore on the roil of invalid pensioners, and that he be paid at the rate of twenty dollars per month during his life, to commence on the sixteenth day of December, eighteen hundred and forty-six.


*Approved, February 25, 1847.*

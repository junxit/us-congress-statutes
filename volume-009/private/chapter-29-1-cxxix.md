# Chapter CXXIX

> An Act for the Relief of Margaret Gwinnup, of Hamilton County, Ohio.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Margaret Gwinnup, of Hamilton county, Ohio, on the pension roll, under the act of July seven, eighteen hundred and thirtyeight, and pay her at the rate of one hundred and twenty dollars per annum.


*Approved, August 8, 1846.*

# Chapter XXVII

> An Act for the Relief of Hubert H. Booley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, required to place the name of Hubert II. Booley on the roll of invalid pensions; and that he pay him, semi-annualiy, at the rate of sixteen dollars per month, for and during his life, commencing on the tenth day of October, one thousand eight hundred and forty-seven.


*Approved, March 3, 1851.*

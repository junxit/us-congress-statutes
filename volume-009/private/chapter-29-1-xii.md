# Chapter XII

> An Act for the Relief of David F. Williamson, of Pope County, State of Arkansas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the entry of the north-west quarter of section number twenty-nine, in township number eight north of range number twenty west, in the name of David F. Williamson, made at the Johnson Court-House land office, in the State of Arkansas, per certificate number six hundred and seventeen of that office, be, and the same is hereby, confirmed, and a patent shall issue thereon as in other cases.


*Approved, April 27, 1846.*

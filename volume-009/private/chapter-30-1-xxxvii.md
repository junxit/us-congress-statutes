# Chapter XXXVII

> An Act for the Relief of Christopher Cunningham.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Christopher Cunningham, now resident of New Orleans, Louisiana, on the list of invalid pensioners; and that the said Cunningham receive a pension at the rate of eight dollars a month, to commence from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, May 9, 1848.*

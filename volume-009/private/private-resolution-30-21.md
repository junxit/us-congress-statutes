# Private Resolution 21

> A Resolution respecting the Claims of A. S. & A. W. Benson.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and is hereby, authorized to adjust and settle, upon principles of equity and justice, the claims of A. S. & A. W. Benson, arising out of contracts made with the Navy Department for the transportation of naval stores to the Pacific.


*Approved, March 3, 1849.*

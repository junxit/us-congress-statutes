# Chapter CXLII

> An Act for the Relief of James M. Scantland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War allow and pay to Major James M. Scantland, ofDavidson county, Tennessee, the sum of thirty dollars per month during his natural life, instead of the sum now paid him as an invalid pensioner, to commence from the twenty-first day of July, eighteen hundred and forty-eight.


*Approved, August 7, 1848.*

# Private Resolution 10

> A Resolution in Favor of David Shaw and Solomon T. Corser.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster-General be, and hereby is, authorized and required to examine the claim of David Shaw and Solomon T. Corser, contractors for carrying the mail on route number four, from Portland to Augusta, in Maine, and ascertain whether any sum of money is justly and equitably due them for any more expensive service performed by them on said route than was required or contemplated by their contract; and if it shall be found that any sum of money is due them as aforesaid, then that he ascertain the amount thereof, and pay the same out of the revenues of the Post-Office Department: Provided, That in no event shall a greater sum than three thousand dollars be allowed and paid on the claim aforesaid.


*Approved, June 26, 1848.*

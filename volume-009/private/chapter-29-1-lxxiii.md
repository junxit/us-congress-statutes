# Chapter LXXIII

> An Act for the Relief of Harriet L. Catching.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Harriet L. Catching he, and she is hereby, released from the payment of a judgment obtained against her by the United States in the Circuit Court of the United States for the Southern District of Mississippi, on the sixteenth day of November, eighteen hundred and thirty-nine: Provided, She pay all costs of said suit.


*Approved, July 29, 1846.*

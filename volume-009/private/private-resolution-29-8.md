# Private Resolution 8

> A Resolution to correct a clerical Error in the Act approved June sixth, eighteen hundred and forty-six, “for the Relief of the legal Representatives of George Duval, a Cherokee Indian.”

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the words “calash and horses of the said Duval,” where they occur in the “Act for the Relief of the legal Representatives of George Duval, a Cherokee Indian,” approved June sixth, eighteen hundred and forty-six, shall read and be held to mean, cattle, horses, and hogs, of the said Duval; the word “calash” having been erroneously written for the word cattle, and the word hogs erroneously omitted in said act


*Approved, June 19, 1846.*

# Chapter CLXXXII

> An Act to allow Elijah White Reimbursement of Expenses incurred by him as acting Sub-Agent of Indian Affairs west of the Rocky Mountains.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be authorized and required to allow and pay to Elijah White, and that he be also authorized and required to audit, allow, and pay, the accounts of said White, presented to the war department for payment upon the principles of right and justice, and that the sum of two thousand one hundred and seventy-six dollars and fifty-nine cents be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the purposes aforesaid.


*Approved, August 10, 1846.*

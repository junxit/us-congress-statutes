# Chapter II

> An Act to enable the Trustees of the Methodist Episcopal Church in Georgetown, in the District of Columbia, to hold certain Property for the Purposes therein recited.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the trustees of the Methodist Episcopal Church of Georgetown, in the District of Columbia, be, and they are hereby, enabled to hold the property on Montgomery Street, in said town, now used and occupied by the religious congregation, of which they are the regularly appointed trustees, for the purposes and to the intents expressed in the conveyance from the heirs of Anthony Holmead, deceased, to the said trustees, bearing date the seventh day of November, in the year eighteen hundred and forty-nine, and recorded in Liber J. A. S., number eight, folios four hundred et sequitur, one of the land records for Washington county, in the District of Columbia.


*Approved, February 14, 1850.*

# Public Law 111–41

> An Act To amend title 4, United States Code, to encourage the display of the flag of the United States on National Korean War Veterans Armistice Day.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. SHORT TITLE.

This Act may be cited as the “Korean War Veterans Recognition Act”.

## § 2. DISPLAY OF FLAG ON NATIONAL KOREAN WAR VETERANS ARMISTICE DAY.

Section 6(d) of title 4, United States Code, is amended by inserting “National Korean War Veterans Armistice Day, July 27;” after “July 4;”.


*Approved July 27, 2009.*

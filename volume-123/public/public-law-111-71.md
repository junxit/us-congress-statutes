# Public Law 111–71

> An Act To amend the United States International Broadcasting Act of 1994 to extend by one year the operation of Radio Free Asia, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. ONE YEAR EXTENSION OF OPERATION OF RADIO FREE ASIA.

Section 309(f) of the United States International Broadcasting Act of 1994 (22 U.S.C. 6208(f)) is amended by striking “2009” and inserting “2010”.


*Approved October 9, 2009.*

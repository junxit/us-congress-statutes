# Public Law 111–103

> An Act To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 140 Merriman Road in Garden City, Michigan, as the “John J. Shivnen Post Office Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. JOHN J. SHIVNEN POST OFFICE BUILDING.

- **(a)** *Designation.—* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 140 Merriman Road in Garden City, Michigan, shall be known and designated as the “John J. Shivnen Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References.—* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “John J. Shivnen Post Office Building”.


*Approved November 30, 2009.*

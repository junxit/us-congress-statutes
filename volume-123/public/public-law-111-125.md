# Public Law 111–125

> An Act To extend the commercial space transportation liability regime.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. COMMERCIAL SPACE TRANSPORTATION LIABILITY REGIME EXTENSION.

Section 70113(f) of title 49, United States Code, is amended by striking “December 31, 2009.” and inserting “December 31, 2012.”.

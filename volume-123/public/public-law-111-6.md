# Public Law 111–6

> Joint Resolution Making further continuing appropriations for fiscal year 2009, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Continuing Appropriations Resolution, 2009 (division A of Public Law 110–329) is amended by striking the date specified in section 106(3) and inserting “March 11, 2009”.


*Approved March 6, 2009.*

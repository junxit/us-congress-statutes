# Public Law 111–111

> An Act To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 630 Northeast Killingsworth Avenue in Portland, Oregon, as the “Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr. Post Office”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DR. MARTIN LUTHER KING, JR. POST OFFICE.

- **(a)** *Designation.—* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 630 Northeast Killingsworth Avenue in Portland, Oregon, shall be known and designated as the “Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr. Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References.—* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr. Post Office”.


*Approved November 30, 2009.*

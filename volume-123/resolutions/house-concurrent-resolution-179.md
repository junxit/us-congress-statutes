# House Concurrent Resolution 179

> JOINT SESSION

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Wednesday, September 9, 2009, at 8:00 p.m., for the purpose of receiving such communication as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Agreed to September 8, 2009.*

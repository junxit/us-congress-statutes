# Chapter 1

> AN ACT granting a pension to Abram V. Miller, late a lieutenant in Company E, of the One hundred and eighty-fourth Regiment of Pennsylvania Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Hanse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension- roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Abram V. Miller, late first lieutenant of Company E, One hundred and eighty-fourth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers, to date from and after its passage.


*Approved, December 10, 1878.*

# Chapter 235

> An Act granting a pension to Elizabeth Winters.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Elizabeth Winters, widow of the late Jacob Winters, private Company E, Twenty-eighth Oljio Volunteer Infantry, to take effect from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 15, 1878.*

# Chapter 237

> AN ACT granting a pension to Abigail S. Tilton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior he, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Abigail S. Tilton, widow of Benjamin Stevens, late of Gilmanton, New Hampshire, who was a soldier in the war of the Revolution, served in Captain Samuel McConnel’s company, in General Stark’s brigade.


*Approved, March 3, 1879.*

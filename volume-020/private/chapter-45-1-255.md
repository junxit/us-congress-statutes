# Chapter 255

> An Act granting a pension to Agatha O'Brien, widow of John P. J. O'Brien, brevet major United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension-laws, the name of Agatha O’Brien, widow of Brevet Major John P. J. O’Brien, Fourth Artillery, United States Army.


*Approved, June 15, 1878.*

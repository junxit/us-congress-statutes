# Chapter 350

> An Act to reimburse Richard Joseph for money paid on forged vouchers

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of eleven hundred and sixty seven dollars, lawful money, be, and it hereby is appropriated, out of any moneys in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to reimburse Richard Joseph for losses incurred in his office as disbursing-clerk of the Department of the Interior, without negligence or fault on his part, through the forgeries of Frederick E. Goodridge late a clerk in the United States Patent Office.


*Approved, June 19, 1878.*

# Chapter 281

> An Act granting a pension to Dr. P. F. Reuss, late surgeon Seventh New York Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the peusioulaws, the name of Doctor P. F. Beuss, late snrgeon of the Seventh Begiment New York Volunteers.


*Approved, June 18, 1878.*

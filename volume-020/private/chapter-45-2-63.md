# Chapter 63

> AN ACT granting a pension to H. Louise Gates.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension- roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of II. Louise Gates, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month, instead of the pension now paid her.


*Approved, February 7, 1879.*

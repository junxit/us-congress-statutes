# Chapter 373

> An Act granting a pension to George Denney.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitationsof the pension laws, the name of George Denney


*Approved, June 20, 1878.*

# Chapter 271

> An Act granting a pension to Caroline M. Egbert.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, the name of Caroline M. Egbert, widow of Medical Director Daniel Egbert, United States Navy, to take effect from the date of the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 18, 1878.*

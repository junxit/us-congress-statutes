# Chapter 223

> An Act granting a pension to Mary Martin, mother by adoption of James E. Martin, late a private in the Fifth Regiment Vermont Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Mary Martin, mother by adoption of James R. Martin, late a private in Company I, Fifth Regiment Vermont Volunteers.


*Approved, June 15, 1878.*

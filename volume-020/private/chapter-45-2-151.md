# Chapter 151

> AN ACT granting a pension to Catharine Brennan, widow of John Brennan, late private of Company B, Fifty-eighth Illinois Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is, hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Catharine. Brennan, widow of John Brennan, late private of Company B, Fifty-eighth Illinois Volunteers; but nothing in this act contained shall entitle said Catharine Brennan to arrears of pension.


*Approved, March 1, 1879.*

# Chapter 254

> An Act granting a pension to Charles H. Bugbee, late a private in Company A, Third Vermont Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Charles H. Bugbee, late a private in Company A, Third Regiment Vermont Volunteers, and pay him a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 15, 1878.*

# Chapter 61

> AN ACT granting a pension to Mary Frazee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Mary Frazee, dependent mother of Thomas B. Frazee, deceased, late a private of Company A. Ninetieth Ohio Volunteers.


*Approved, February 7, 1879.*

# Chapter 243

> AN ACT granting a pension to Hiram Howard.

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place mi the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of' Hiram Howard, late private in Company I, Second Regiment. Kansas State Militia.


*Approved, March 3, 1879.*

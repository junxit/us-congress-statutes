# Chapter 118

> An Act restoring the name of Thomas W. Brown to the pension-roll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary be, and he is hereby, directed to restore to the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Thomas W. Brown, late of Company C, Thirty-fourth Illinois Volunteers.


*Approved, May 25, 1878.*

# Chapter 226

> AN ACT granting a pension to Mary Bradley Cross.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Mary Bradley Cross, widow of Colonel Osborn Cross, late a colonel in the United States Army, and pay her a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1879.*

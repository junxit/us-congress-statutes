# Chapter 62

> AN ACT granting an increase of pension to Mrs. Eliza H. Frailey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Uni ted. States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension heretofore granted and paid to Mrs. Eliza H. Frailey, widow of James Madison Frailey, late a commodore in the United States Navy, be, and the same is, increased to the sum of fifty dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 7, 1879.*

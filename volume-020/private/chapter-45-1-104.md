# Chapter 104

> An Act granting a pension to Hannah Streets,

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place ou the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Hannah Streets, widow of John W. Streets, late of Company B,Oue hundred and seventeenth Regiment United States Colored Troops.


*Approved, May 14, 1878.*

# Chapter 113

> An Act for the relief of Dudley A. Fish, of Bucyrus, Ohio.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Dudley A. Fish, late a captain in the One hundred and forty-ninth Regiment of Pennsylvania Volunteers, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws.


*Approved, May 25, 1878.*

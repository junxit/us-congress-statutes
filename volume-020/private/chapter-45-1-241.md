# Chapter 241

> An Act granting a pension to James C. Bates.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That-the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of James C. Bates.


*Approved, June 15, 1878.*

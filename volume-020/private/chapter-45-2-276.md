# Chapter 276

> AN ACT for the relief of Jarvis Jackson, of Laurel County, Kentucky.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, from and after the date of the passage of this act, to pay to Jarvis Jackson, of Laurel County, Kentucky, a pension of sixteen dollars per month, instead of eight dollars, which he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 3, 1879.*

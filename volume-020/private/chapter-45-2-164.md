# Chapter 164

> AN ACT granting a pension to George W. Staplin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of George W. Staplin, late a private in Company C, Fifteenth Illinois Volunteers, and pay him a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 1, 1879.*

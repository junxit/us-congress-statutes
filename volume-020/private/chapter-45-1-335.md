# Chapter 335

> An Act to relieve the political disabilities of E. Kirby Smith of Tennessee

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States on E. Kirby Smith of Tennessee, be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, June 19, 1878.*

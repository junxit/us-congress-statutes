# Chapter 236

> An Act granting a pension to Cynthia A. Mizelle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll,subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Cynthia A. Mizelle, mother of Zedekiah M. Mizelle, late a private Company C, First Regiment North Carolina Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, June 15, 1878.*

# Chapter 346

> An Act to amend an act granting a pension to Ann Hensley approved March 3, 1873.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the act approved March third, eighteen hundred and seventy three, granting a pension to “Ann Heusley, mother of William Hensley, late a sergeant of the First Provisional Regiment of Missouri Volunteers”, shall read: “Ann Hensley, mother of William Heusley, late a sergeant in Company O, First Provisional Regiment Enrolled Missouri Militia.”


*Approved, June 19, 1878.*

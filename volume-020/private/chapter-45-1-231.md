# Chapter 231

> An Act granting a pension to Charles G. Galezio.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Charles G. Galezio, late second lieutenant in Company U, Thirty-fifth Indiana Volunteers.


*Approved, June 15, 1878.*

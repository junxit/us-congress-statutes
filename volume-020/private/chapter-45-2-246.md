# Chapter 246

> AN ACT granting a pension to William Leibig.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of William Leibig, late private Company D, Eighth Regiment Kansas Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, March 3, 1879.*

# Chapter 123

> An Act granting a pension to Benjamin C. Webster.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Benjamin C. Webster, late a private in Company F, Eighth .Regiment Maine Volunteers.


*Approved, May 25, 1878.*

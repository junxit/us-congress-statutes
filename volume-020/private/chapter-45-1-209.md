# Chapter 209

> An Act for the relief of Mrs Amanda Rains, of Illinois.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to pay to Mrs Amanda Rains, widow of Doctor Jonathan Rains, deceased, the sum of one hun- dred and twenty dollars, in full compensation and satisfaction of the claim for the services of said Jonathan Rains as acting assistant surgeon of the Army; at Marshall, Illinois.


*Approved, June 14, 1878.*

# Chapter 265

> AN ACT to remove the political disabilities of Henry H. Lewis of Baltimore Maryland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That Henry H. Lewis, of Baltimore, Maryland, be, and is hereby, relieved of all political disabilities imposed upon hirn by the third section of the fourteenth article of amendments to the Constitution of the United States.


*Approved, March 3, 1879.*

# Chapter 164

> An Act granting a pension to Alfred Richardson, late of Company A, Twelfth Indiana Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Alfred Richardson, late of Company A, Twelfth Indiana Volunteers.


*Approved, June 7, 1878.*

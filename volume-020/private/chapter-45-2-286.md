# Chapter 286

> AN ACT granting a pension to Morris Dwight

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Morris Dwight as dependent father of the late Colonel A. W. Dwight of the One hundred and twenty second Regiment of New York Volunteers.


*Approved, March 3, 1879.*

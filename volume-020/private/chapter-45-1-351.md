# Chapter 351

> An Act for the relief of E. B. Head, postmaster at Harrodsburgh, Kentucky.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be, and is hereby, appropriated, to pay E. B. Head, postmaster at Harrodsburgh, Kentucky, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one hundred and twenty-seven dollars, to reimburse him tor stamps stolen from his office February twenty-second, eighteen hundred and seventy-four; and the proper accounting-officers of the Post-Office Department are hereby directed to credit the said E. B. Head in his account as such postmaster with the same.


*Approved, June 19, 1878.*

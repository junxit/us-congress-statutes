# Chapter 305

> An Act granting a pension to Sarah J. Goss.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-. laws, the name of Sarah J. Goss, widow of Greenberry Goss, late a pilot on the gunboat Covington.


*Approved, June 18, 1878.*

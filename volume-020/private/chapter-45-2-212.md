# Chapter 212

> AN ACT to restore to the pension-roll the name of Michael S. Corl.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to restore to the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Michael S. Corl, late a private in Company K, Fifty-fifth Regiment of Pennsylvania Volunteers.


*Approved, March 3, 1879.*

# Chapter 282

> AN ACT granting a pension to Mrs. Sidney A. Harrison.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, the name of Sidney A. Harrison, widow of Thomas Harrison, who was a soldier and military officer in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve, and to pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1879.*

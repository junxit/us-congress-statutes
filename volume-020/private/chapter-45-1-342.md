# Chapter 342

> An Act granting a pension to Charles Slawson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Charles Slawson, late a soldier in the war of eighteen hnndred and twelve, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws.


*Approved, June 19, 1878.*

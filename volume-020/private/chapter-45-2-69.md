# Chapter 69

> AN ACT to remove the political disabilities of C. H. Kennedy, of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United, States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That C. H. Kennedy, be, and is, hereby relieved of all political disabilities imposed upon him by the third section of the fourteenth article of amendments to the Constitution of the United States.


*Approved, February 14, 1879.*

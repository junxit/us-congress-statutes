# Chapter 345

> An Act to grant a pension to Thomas W. Hewitt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Thomas W. Hewitt, late of Company D, Third Regiment of Michigan Volunteer Infantry, upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, and that he be paid a pension from the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 19, 1878.*

# Chapter 228

> An Act for the relief of Oliver Yake, of Sanilac County, Michigan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of,*

America in Congress assembled, That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Oliver Take on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws.


*Approved, June 15, 1878.*

# Chapter 90

> An Act for the relief of the minor heirs of John H. Evans, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Paymaster General of the Army be, and he is, authorized and directed to pay to the minor heirs of John H. Evans, deceased, late of McNairy County, Tennessee, the pay and bounty due the same, as if he had been enlisted and mustered into the service of the United States on the sixth day of July, eighteen hundred and sixty-three.


*Approved, May 3, 1878.*

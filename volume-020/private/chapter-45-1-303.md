# Chapter 303

> An Act to restore the name of Jesse Stallings, of Butler County, Alabama, to the pension-list.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of Jesse Stallings, as a pensioner on account of service by him in Captain Cyrus White’s company, Georgia militia, in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve, be, and the same is hereby, restored to the pension-roll; and that the entry made against him on the twenty-eighth day of February, eighteen hundred and fifteen, as a deserter, shall in no wise affect the right of said Jesse Stallings to a pension or bounty-land, it appearing that said entry was erroneously made.


*Approved, June 18, 1878.*

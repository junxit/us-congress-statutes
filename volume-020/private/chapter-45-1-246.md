# Chapter 246

> An Act to remove the political disabilities of W. E. Evans, a citizen of Louisiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all political disabilities now imposed by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution upon W. E. Evans, a citizen of Louisiana, be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, June 15, 1878.*

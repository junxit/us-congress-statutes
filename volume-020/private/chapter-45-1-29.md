# Chapter 29

> An Act for the relief of Captain William L. Foulk.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be and he is hereby authorized to appoint William L. Foulk, late captain in the United States Cavalry Tenth Regiment to the position of captain of the same grade and rank held by him, at the time he was dropped from the roll: Provided, That he receive no pay or allowances for the time he was out of the service.


*Approved, March 9, 1878.*

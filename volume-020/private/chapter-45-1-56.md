# Chapter 56

> An Act granting a pension to Fannie E. Records, widow of Albert B. Records, late a private in Company G, Fifteenth Regiment Maine Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Fannie E. Records, widow of Albert B. Records, late a private in Compauj- G, of the Fifteenth Regiment of Maine Volunteers.


*Approved, April 8, 1878.*

# Chapter 117

> An Act granting a pension to Lemuel L, Lawrence, late second lieutenant Company B in the Sixth Regiment Illinois Cavalry Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Lemuel L. Lawrence, late second lieutenant of Company B in the Sixth Regiment of Illinois Cavalry Volunteers, to take effect from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, May 25, 1878.*

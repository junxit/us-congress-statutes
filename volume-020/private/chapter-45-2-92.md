# Chapter 92

> AN ACT for the relief of the estate of Amos Ireland, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the Treasury be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to adjust and settle the account of Amos Ireland, as captain of the light- vessel at Brandt Island Shoals, North Carolina, and allow to the administrator of the estate of Amos Ireland, deceased, the sum of two hundred and eight dollars and thirty-three cents, for bis services from January first, eighteen hundred and sixty-one, until May thirty-first, eighteen hundred and sixty-one, upon producing proper evidence of qualification as administrator of his estate ; and a sufficient sum is hereby appropri- ated for that purpose out of any moneys not otherwise appropriated by law.


*Approved, February 19, 1879.*

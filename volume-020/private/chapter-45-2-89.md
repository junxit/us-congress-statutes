# Chapter 89

> AN ACT granting a pension to Julia Watkins, widow of Thomas H. Watkins, late captain Company B, Purnell Legion, Maryland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension- roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Julia Watkins, widow of Thomas H. Watkins, late captain Company B, Purnell Legion, Maryland, to take effect from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 18, 1879.*

# Chapter 158

> AN ACT granting a pension to Hannah Hallam

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Hannah Hallam, widow of Michael Hallam, late private Company F, Fourteenth West Virginia Volunteers, and pay her a pension of eight dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 1, 1879.*

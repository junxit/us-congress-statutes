# Chapter 60

> AN ACT granting a pension to Lydia A. Morris.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Lydia A. Morris, widow of John K. Morris, late private Company A. Fifth Ohio Volunteer Cavalry.


*Approved, February 7, 1879.*

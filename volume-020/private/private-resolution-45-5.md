# Private Resolution 5

> Joint resolution authorizing Commander Edmund O. Matthews, of the United States Navy, to accept a gilt teapot from the Emperor of Siam

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Commander Edmund O. Matthews, of the United States Navy, be, and is hereby, authorised to accept a gilt teapot, of native manufacture and trifling value, presented by the Emperor of Siam as a souvenir.


*Approved, December 15, 1877.*

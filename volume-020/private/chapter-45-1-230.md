# Chapter 230

> An Act granting a pension to Margaret Kenah, widow of Patrick Kenah, late a private of Company D, First United States Artillery.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Margaret Kenah, widow of Patrick Kenah, late a private of Company D, First Regiment United States Artillery, from the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 15, 1878.*

# Chapter 58

> AN ACT granting a pension to Andrew J. Morrison.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Andrew J, Morrison, and pay him a pension at the rate of the rank of captain of infantry.


*Approved, February 7, 1879.*

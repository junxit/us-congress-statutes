# Chapter 304

> An Act for the relief of Peter G. Mills, his heirs and assigns.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the entry by Peter G. Mills of the west half of the northwest quarter and the northeast quarter of the northwest quarter of section six, township one hundred and two, range forty-three, in the State of Minnesota, is hereby made valid to him, his heirs and assigns.


*Approved, June 18, 1878.*

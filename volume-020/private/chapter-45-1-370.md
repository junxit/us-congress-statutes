# Chapter 370

> An Act granting a pension to Mary McNamara.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Mary McNamara, dependent mother of Lewis Mack, late seaman in the United States Navy.


*Approved, June 20, 1878.*

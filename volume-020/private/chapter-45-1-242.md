# Chapter 242

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Isabell Dunbar, widow of Daniel Dunbar, late first engineer on steamer “Victor, No. 2.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Mrs. Isabel! Dunbar, widow of Daniel Dunbar, late first engineer on steamer “Victor No. 2.”


*Approved, June 15, 1878.*

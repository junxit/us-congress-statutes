# Chapter 139

> AN ACT granting a pension to Mary E. Pauley.

*Be it enacted try the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension- roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Mary E. Pauley, widow of James Pauley, late private in Regiment New York State Volunteer Infantry


*Approved, March 1, 1879.*

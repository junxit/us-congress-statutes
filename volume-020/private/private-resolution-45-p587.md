# Private Resolution

> Joint resolution authorizing Lieutenant T.B.M. Mason, United States Navy, to accept a medal conferred by the King of Italy for extinguishing a fire on a powder-ship.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Lieutenant Theodoras B. M. Mason, of the United States Navy, be, and is hereby, authorized to accept a. silver medal, tendered’ him by the King of Italy, in appreciation of services rendered by him to the Italian bark Delaide, in rescuing said vessel from Are in the harbor of Callao, Peru, June twenty-fifth, eighteen hundred and seventy-four.


*Approved, May 16, 1878.*

# Chapter 166

> An Act for the relief of Nannie Hall

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the claim of Nannie Hall, formerly widow of Lieutenant Frederick J. Denman, of the United States Army, for the proceeds of cotton alleged to have been seized and sold by agents of the United States, be referred to the Court of Claims for its decision and adjudication according to law and the practice of said court, without regard to the statute of limitation.


*Approved, June 7, 1878.*

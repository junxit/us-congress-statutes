# Chapter 352

> An Act to remove the political disabilities of Washington Gwathmay, of Alabama.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all political disabilities imposed upon Washington Gwathmay, of the State of Alabama, by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States by reason of participation in the rebellion be, and the same are hereby removed.


*Approved, June 19, 1878.*

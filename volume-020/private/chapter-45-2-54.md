# Chapter 54

> AN ACT granting a pension to William R. Browne.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension- roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of William R. Browne, late a volunteer lieutenant, commanding the United States ship “Restless”.


*Approved, February 7, 1879.*

# Chapter 277

> AN ACT granting an increase of pension to Josephine Da C. Thomas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be^, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Josephine Da C. Thomas, widow of Evan Thomas, late acting major Fourth United States Artillery, a pension at the rate paid widows of a major of artillery, in lieu of the pension now paid her.


*Approved, March 3, 1879.*

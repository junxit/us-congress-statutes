# Chapter 77

> AN ACT to remove the political disabilities of Henry G. Thomas, of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, {two-thirds of each House concurring therein,),*

That Henry G. Thomas, of Norfolk, Virginia, be, and is hereby, relieved from all political disabilities imposed upon him by virtue of the third section of the fourteenth article of the amendments to the Constitution of the United States.


*Approved, February 14, 1879.*

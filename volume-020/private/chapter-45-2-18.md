# Chapter 18

> AN ACT to remove the political disabilities of James Chesnut of South Carolina.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That James Chesnut be, and is, hereby relieved of all political disabilities imposed upon him by the third section of the fourteenth article of amendment to the Constitution of the United States.


*Approved, January 17, 1879.*

# Chapter 207

> AN ACT granting a pension to Mrs. Maria L. Maxwell, widow of William C. Maxwell, Company D, Twelfth Ohio Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name, Mrs. Maria L. Maxwell, widow of William C. Maxwell, of Company D, Twelfth Ohio Volunteers, pension-claim two hundred and nineteen thousand four hundred and thirty, and that she be paid a pension from the date of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1879.*

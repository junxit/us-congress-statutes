# Chapter 293

> An Act granting a pension to Emily Hughes, alias Burch

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place upon the pension-rolls, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-law, the name of Emily Hughes, alias Burch, the widow of the late Private James Burch, Company G, Thirty-eighth Regiment United States Infantry.


*Approved, June 18, 1878.*

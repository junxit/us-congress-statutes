# Chapter 119

> An Act granting a pension to John Frey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of John Frey, late a private in Company I, Thirtieth Eegiment Illinois Volunteer infantry, and pay him a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, May 25, 1878.*

# Chapter 288

> An Act for the relief of John M. Lord.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to restore John M. Lord, late a second lieutenant in Company A, Third Regiment of Indiana Volunteers, in the war with Mexico, to the pension-roll, to take effect of the date of March fourth, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, subject to the provisions of the pension-laws.


*Approved, June 18, 1878.*

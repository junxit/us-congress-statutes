# Chapter 245

> An Act to remove the political disabilities of William C. Whittle of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That William 0, Whittle be and is hereby relieved of all political disabilities imposed upon him by the third section of the fourteenth article of amendments to the Constitution of the United States.


*Approved, June 15, 1878.*

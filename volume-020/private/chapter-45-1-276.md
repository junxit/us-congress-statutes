# Chapter 276

> An Act for the relief of T. B. Kelly

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum found due by the proper accounting-officers is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to be paid to Second Lieutenant T. B. Kelly, late an officer in the Signal Corps, for services rendered by him from April fourth, eighteen hundred and sixty-three to September fifteenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, during which period be was paid only the pay of a private soldier while performing the duties of an officer and supporting himself: Provided, That no pay in excess of the difference herein stated shall be paid to the said Second Lieutenant T. B. Kelly, or to his heirs.


*Approved, June 18, 1878.*

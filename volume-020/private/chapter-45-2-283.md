# Chapter 283

> AN ACT granting a pension to Ann Cornelia Lanman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Ann Cornelia Lanman, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1879.*

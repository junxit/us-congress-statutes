# Chapter 332

> An Act for the removal of the political disabilities of H. A. Edmundson of Salem, Virginia

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That the political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States upon H. A. Edmundson of the State of Virginia be, and the same are hereby removed.


*Approved, June 19, 1878.*

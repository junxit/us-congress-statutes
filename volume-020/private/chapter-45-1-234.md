# Chapter 234

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Sarah A. Bell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Mrs. Sarah A. Bell, widow of William Beil, of Dundee, Michigan, late private of the Seventh Regiment of Michigan Volunteers, and carry into effect the provisions of existing laws in favor of the minor children.


*Approved, June 15, 1878.*

# Chapter 100

> An Act granting a pension to Melvina A. Maltby.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States to America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Melvina A. Maltby, widow of Jasper A. Maltby, late a brigadier-general of volunteers.


*Approved, May 14, 1878.*

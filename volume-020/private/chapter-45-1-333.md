# Chapter 333

> An Act to remove the political disabilities of William P. Smith

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United, States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States on William P. Smith be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, June 19, 1878.*

# Chapter 52

> AN ACT granting a pension to James B. Treadwell, major of the Eighty-fifth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United, States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, anti he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of James B. Treadwell, late major of the Eighty-fifth Regiment of Pennsylvania Volunteers.


*Approved, February 7, 1879.*

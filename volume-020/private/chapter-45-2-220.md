# Chapter 220

> AN ACT granting a pension to Mrs Mary G. Harris.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, the name of Mary G. Harris, widow of John Harris, late commandant of the United States Marine Coips, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars a month from and after the passage of this act.

Approved, March 3, 1879,

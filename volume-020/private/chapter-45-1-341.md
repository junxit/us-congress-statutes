# Chapter 341

> An Act for the relief of H. G. Boardman, postmaster at Milton, Vermont.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting-officer of the Treasury Department be directed to pay to É. G. Boardman, postmaster at Milton, Vermont, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one hundred and sixteen dollars and thirty-four cents, being the amount of postage-stamps stolen from his office without the fault of said postmaster.


*Approved, June 19, 1878.*

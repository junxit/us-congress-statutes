# Chapter 239

> An Act granting a pension to William Cogswell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of William Cogswell, late captain of Cogswell’s Independent Battery of Light Artillery, Illinois volunteers.


*Approved, June 15, 1878.*

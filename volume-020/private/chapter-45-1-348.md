# Chapter 348

> An Act granting additional pension to Mrs Margaret J. Lovell, widow of the late General Charles S. Lovell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Mrs Margaret J. Lovell, widow of the late Brigadier-General Charles S. Lovell, to fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, June 19, 1878.*

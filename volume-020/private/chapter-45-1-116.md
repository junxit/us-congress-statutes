# Chapter 116

> An Act granting a pension to Rose Miller, widow of Reason F. Miller, deceased, late a private in Company E, One hundred and twenty-third Illinois Infantry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Rose Miller, widow of the late Reason F. Miller, who was a private in Company E, One hundred and twenty-third Illinois Infantry.


*Approved, May 25, 1878.*

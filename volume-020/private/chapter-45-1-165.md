# Chapter 165

> An Act for the relief of Acting Master Robert Platt, United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States is hereby authorized to appoint Acting Muster Robert Platt, United States Navy, for long and meritorious services, a master in the regular Navy of the United States, not in the line of promotion.


*Approved, June 7, 1878.*

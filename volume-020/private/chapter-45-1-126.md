# Chapter 126

> An Act granting a pension to Isabella Cassidy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Isabella Cassidy, dependent mother of George H. Cassidy, late a lieutenant in the Sixteenth Regiment Virginia Cavalry.


*Approved, May 25, 1878.*

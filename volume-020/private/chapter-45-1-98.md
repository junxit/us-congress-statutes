# Chapter 98

> An Act granting a pension to Elizabeth D. Stone.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Elizabeth D. Stone, widow of Briuton Stone, late an acting assistant surgeon in the United States Navy, to take effect from the passage of this act.


*Approved, May 14, 1878.*

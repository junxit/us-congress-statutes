# Chapter 55

> An Act granting a pension to Bridget T. Hopper.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Bridget T. Hoppei, widow of Edward 0. Hopper, late a captain of Company G of the Filth Now Jersey Volunteers


*Approved, April 8, 1878.*

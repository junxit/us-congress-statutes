# Chapter 248

> An Act to relieve the political disabilities of John Green, senior, of Alabama.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States on John Green as a citizen of Alabama be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, June 15, 1878.*

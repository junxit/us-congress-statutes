# Chapter 147

> AN ACT for the relief of Thomas C. Young, late private of Company F, Thirty-ninth Iowa Infantry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Thomas C. Young, late a private in Company F, of the Thirty-ninth Regiment of Iowa Volunteer's, on the pension-roll, and pay him a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 1, 1879.*

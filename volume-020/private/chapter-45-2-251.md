# Chapter 251

> AN ACT granting a pension to Ellen Devlin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Inte rior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name Ellen Devlin, widow of Patrick Devlin, late of Company C, Sixth Regiment of United States Infantry.


*Approved, March 3, 1879.*

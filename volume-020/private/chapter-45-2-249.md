# Chapter 249

> AN ACT granting a pension to Mary E. Parker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place oil the pension- roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Mary E. Parker, widow of Solomon M. Parker, late a private in Company D, Second Regiment New Jersey Cavahy.


*Approved, March 3, 1879.*

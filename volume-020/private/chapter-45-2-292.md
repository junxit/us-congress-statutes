# Chapter 292

> AN ACT for the relief of Peter G. Mills.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to cause the records in the office of the Adjutant-General of the United States Army to be so amended as to discharge Peter G. Mills, late of Company K. Fourth Minnesota Infantry Volunteers from the service of the United States on the seventh day of August, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, and granting him an honorable discharge from said Regiment, and correcting the record as to his muster out of Company K. Tenth Minnesota Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, March 3, 1879.*

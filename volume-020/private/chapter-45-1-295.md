# Chapter 295

> An Act granting a pension to Grace Aikins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Grace Aikins, widow of William R. Aikius, late a private of Company A, Eleventh Iowa Infantry.


*Approved, June 18, 1878.*

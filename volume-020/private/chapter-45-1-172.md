# Chapter 172

> An Act for the relief of Saint Michael's Church, Charleston, South Carolina.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the collector of customs in the city of Charleston, South Carolina, be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to remit the duties on bells recast in England, belonging to the vestry of Saint Michael’s Church, in the city of Charleston, South Carolina.


*Approved, June 8, 1878.*

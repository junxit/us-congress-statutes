# Chapter 71

> AN ACT to remove the political disabilities of C. B. Oliver.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States, upon C. B. Oliver a citizen of the United States be, and the same are hereby removed.


*Approved, February 14, 1879.*

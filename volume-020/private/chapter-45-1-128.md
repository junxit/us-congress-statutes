# Chapter 128

> An Act granting a pension to Joseph L. Young, late a private Company C, Eleventh Regiment Maine Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Joseph L. Young, late a private in Company C, of the Eleventh Regiment of Maine Infantry Volunteers, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions of the pension-laws.


*Approved, May 25, 1878.*

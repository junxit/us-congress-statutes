# Chapter 121

> An Act granting a pension to William H. Shultis, late of Company K, Fourteenth Regiment New York Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of William H. Shultis, late of Company K, Fourteenth Regiment New York Volunteers, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions of the pension-laws.


*Approved, May 25, 1878.*

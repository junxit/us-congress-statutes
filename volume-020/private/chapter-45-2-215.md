# Chapter 215

> AN ACT granting a pension to Henrietta Stringham, widow of Rear Admiral Silas H. Stringham, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Henrietta Stringham widow of Rear Admiral Silas H. Stringham, deceased, late of the United States Navy, and pay her pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1879.*

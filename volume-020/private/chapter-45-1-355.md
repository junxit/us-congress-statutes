# Chapter 355

> An Act granting a pension to Catharine Harris.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Catharine Harris, widow of Matthew Harris late a post-chaplain in the United States Army.


*Approved, June 19, 1878.*

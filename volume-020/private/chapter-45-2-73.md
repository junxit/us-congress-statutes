# Chapter 73

> AN ACT to remove the political disabilities of F. E. Shepperd of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assemblai, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein,),*

That all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States on F. E. Shepperd a citizen of the State of Virginia, be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, February 14, 1879.*

# Chapter 97

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Esther A. George.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Mrs. Esther A. George, widow of George W. George, late a second lieutenant in Company I, Fifth Regiment New Hampshire Volunteer Infantry, war of eighteen hundred and sixty-one, to take effect from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, May 9, 1878.*

# Chapter 226

> An Act to restore the name of Hamilton Ryne to the pension-rolls.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to restore to the pension-rolls the name of Hamilton Ryne, and to pay him a pension at the same rate he was drawing at the time his name was dropped from the rolls.


*Approved, June 15, 1878.*

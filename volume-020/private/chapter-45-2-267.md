# Chapter 267

> AN ACT to remove the political disabilities of S. W. Ferguson, of Mississippi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States upon S. W. Ferguson, a citizen of the State of Mississippi, be, and the same are hereby removed.


*Approved, March 3, 1879.*

# Chapter 222

> AN ACT granting an increase of pension to Catharine H. Gallagher, widow of Captain John Gallagher, late United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Catharine H. Gallagher, widow of Captain John Gallagher, late of the United States Navy, at the rate of fifty dollars per month, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1879.*

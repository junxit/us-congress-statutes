# Chapter 262

> AN ACT granting a pension to Frances McNeil Potter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension- roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Francis McNeil Potter, daughter of the lute General John McNeil, United States Army, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1879.*

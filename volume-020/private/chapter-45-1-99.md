# Chapter 99

> An Act granting a pension to Anna L. Robbins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws the name of Anna L. Robbins, widow of Eliphalet H. Robbins late first lieutenant of Company A, Third Massachusetts Cavalry.


*Approved, May 14, 1878.*

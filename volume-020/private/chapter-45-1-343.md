# Chapter 343

> An Act granting a pension to Daniel Clary.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension- laws, the name of Daniel Clary, a private in Company F, Fourth Illinois Volunteers, in the Mexican war, and to pay him a pension, not exceeding sixteen dollars a month, to take effect from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 19, 1878.*

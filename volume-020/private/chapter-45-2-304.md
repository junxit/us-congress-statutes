# Chapter 304

> AN ACT for the removal of the political disabilities of John McIntosh Kell, of Georgia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That the following named person is hereby relieved from all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States, to wit: John McIntosh Kell, of Georgia.


*Approved, March 3, 1879.*

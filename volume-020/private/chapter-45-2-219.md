# Chapter 219

> AN ACT for the relief of Benjamin Sanders.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and. House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Benjamin Sanders, late of Company A, First Regiment Minnesota In- fan try Volunteers, upon the pension-rolls and pay him a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1879.*

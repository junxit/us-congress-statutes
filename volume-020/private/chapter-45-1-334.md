# Chapter 334

> An Act to remove the political disabilities of William W. Loring

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That the political disabilities of William W. Loring, a citizen of the State of Florida, incurred by the provisions of the Fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States, be, and the same are hereby, removed, and the said William W. Loring is hereby relieved of the same.


*Approved, June 19, 1878.*

# Resolution 11

> Joint resolution declaring that a reduction of the tax on distilled spirits is inexpedient.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a reduction of the tax on distilled spirits is inexpedient.


*Approved, February 18, 1878.*

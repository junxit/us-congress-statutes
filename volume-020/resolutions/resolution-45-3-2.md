# Resolution 3

> Joint resolution extending time for Joint Committee on transfer of Indian Bureau to report.

*Be it Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time, in which the Joint Committee appointed by the Senate and House of Representatives to consider the transfer of the management of Indian Affairs from the Interior to the War Department, may report, be and the same is hereby extended to the first day of February, eighteen hundred and seventy-nine.


*Approved, December 21, 1878.*

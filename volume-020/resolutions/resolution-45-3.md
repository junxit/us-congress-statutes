# Resolution 3

> Joint resolution fixing the date on which the payment of the twenty-one clerks to committees of the House of Representatives, who are paid during the session only, shall begin.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pay of the twenty-one clerks to committees of the House of Representatives, who are paid during the session only, shall begin on the day of the organization of the House, the fifteenth day of October, eighteen hundred and seventy-seven.


*Approved, December 15, 1877.*

# Resolution 5

> Joint resolution for the relief of Bushrod B. Taylor and other naval officers.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy is hereby authorized to organize a Board of three officers not below the grade of rear admiral, who shall examine into the case of Commander Bushrod B. Taylor and such other officers of the Navy as did not have opportunity from any cause whatever to appear before the Board created by virtue of the joint resolution of July first, eighteen hundred and seventy, as may deem themselves unjustly passed over by the promotions made in conformity with the act of Congress approved July twenty- fifth, eighteen hundred and sixty-six, and such officers shall have the right to appear in person and present to such Board their cause of grievance. The Board so organized shall report their conclusions to the Secretary of the Navy who shall report the same to Congress.


*Approved, February 5, 1879.*

# Resolution 31

> Joint resolution to enable the joint commission to carry into effect the act of Congress providing for the completion of the Washington Monument

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the joint commission created by the act of Congress entitled “An act providing for the completion of the Washington Monument”, approved August second, eighteen hundred and seventy-six, be, and they are hereby, authorized to apply a portion of the money appropriated by said act not exceeding thirty-six thousand dollars to give greater stability to the foundation, if they deem it advisable.


*Approved, June 14, 1878.*

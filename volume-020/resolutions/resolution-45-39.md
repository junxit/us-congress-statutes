# Resolution 39

> Joint resolution authorizing the Clerk of the House of Representatives to pay certain clerks out of the contingent fund of the House.

*Be it Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Clerk of the House of Representatives be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay out of the contingent fund to the clerks authorized to be employed by the resolutions adopted by the House March seventh, eighteen hundred and seventy-eight, such compensation as may be fixed by the Commit tee of Accounts upon proper certification by the chairmen of the respective committees named in said resolutions, whether said clerks were sworn or not, at the time they were respectively employed.


*Approved, June 19, 1878.*

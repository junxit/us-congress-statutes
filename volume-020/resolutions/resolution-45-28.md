# Resolution 28

> Joint resolution granting the use of artillery, tents, and so forth, st the soldier’s reunion, to be held at Centreville, Iowa.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized to send from some convenient government arsenal, to be used at the soldier’s reunion at Centreville, Iowa, to be held in August, eighteen hundred and seventy eight, four pieces of artillery, and such tents, muskets, and blank cartridges as can be conveniently spared; said cannon, tents, muskets, and so forth, to be returned after the holding of said reunion meeting in as like good condition as when received: Provided, That all transportation of said articles to and from the place of the reunion to the arsenal shall be without expense to the government.


*Approved, June 14, 1878.*

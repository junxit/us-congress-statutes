# Public Resolution 2

> Joint resolution fixing a site for the equestrian statue of General Greene.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the statue of Major-General Nathanael Greene, ordered by Congress, be erected at the intersection of Maryland and Massachusetts avenues.


*Approved, November 23, 1877.*

# Resolution 16

> Joint resolution authorizing the Secretary of War to send artillery and camp equipage to the soldiers’ reunion at Cambridge, Ohio.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized to send, from some convenient, government arsenal, to be used at the national soldiers’ and sailors’ reunion at Cambridge, Ohio, to be held in August, eighteen hundred and seventy-nine, four pieces of artillery, and such tents, muskets, and blank cartridges as can be conveniently spared; said cannon, tents, muskets, and so forth, to be returned, after the holding of the reunion, in as like good condition as when received.


*Approved, March 3, 1879.*

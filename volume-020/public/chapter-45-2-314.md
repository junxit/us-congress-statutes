# Chapter 314

> AN ACT for the relief of settlers on the public lands.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of an act of Congress, approved March third, eighteen hundred and seventy-seven, entitled “An act for the relief of settlers on the public lands”, are hereby extended to those settlers whose crops were destroyed or seriously injured by the grasshoppers during the year eighteen hundred and seventy-six.


*Approved, June 19, 1878.*

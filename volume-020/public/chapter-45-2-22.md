# Chapter 22

> AN ACT to change the name of the steam-tug C. M. Whittaker to Caroline

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of the steam-tug O. M. Whittaker, of Baltimore, be, and is hereby, changed to Caroline, by which name said steam-tug shall be hereafter licensed and known.


*Approved, March 4, 1878.*

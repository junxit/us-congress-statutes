# Chapter 199

> AN ACT for the relief of Charles O. Allibone

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized to place Charles O. Allibone, lieutenant in the United States Navy, now on the active list in his proper position on the Navy Register next below Lieutenant F. W., Greenleaf, the position to which he was originally entitled.


*Approved, June 14, 1878.*

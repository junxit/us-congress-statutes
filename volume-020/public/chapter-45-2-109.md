# Chapter 109

> AN ACT extending the privileges of sections twenty-nine hundred and ninety to twenty-nine hundred and ninety-seven of the Revised Statutes, inclusive, to the port of Bath, in the State of Maine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the privileges of sections twenty-nine hundred and ninety to twenty-nine hundred and ninety-seven of the Revised Statutes, inclusive, be, and they are hereby, extended to the port of Bath, in the State of Maine.


*Approved, May 25, 1878.*

# Chapter 5

> AN ACT to authorize the Secretary of the Treasury to issue a register and change the name of the schooner Captain Charles Robbins to Minnie.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the owner of the schooner Captain Charles Robbins, of Portland, Maine, be, and he is hereby, authorized to change the name of said schooner to that of Minnie; and the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby authorized and directed to issue a register for the same.


*Approved, November 24, 1877.*

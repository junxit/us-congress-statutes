# Chapter 34

> An act to repeal section twelve hundred and thirty-three of the Revised Statutes relating to company cooks in the Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section twelve hundred and thirty-three of the Revised Statutes be, and the same is hereby, repealed.


*Approved, January 29, 1879.*

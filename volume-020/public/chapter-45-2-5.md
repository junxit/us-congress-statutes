# Chapter 5

> AN ACT authorizing the changing of the name of the ship 'Samuel G. Reed'

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to allow the owner of the ship Samuel G. Reed, a vessel of American ownership and license, to change her name, and be hereafter known as the “Fantee”.


*Approved, December 15, 1877.*

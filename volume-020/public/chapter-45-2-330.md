# Chapter 330

> An act for the relief of Robert C. Walker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America-in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United Slates is hereby authorized to restore Robert C. Walker, late paymaster United States Army, to the Army Register, for the purpose of being placed on the retired list.


*Approved, June 19, 1878.*

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

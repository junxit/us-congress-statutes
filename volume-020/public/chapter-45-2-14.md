# Chapter 14

> AN ACT to change the name of the "Consulate at Omoa and Truxillo."

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of the “Consulate at Omoa and Truxillo” be, and the same is hereby, changed to the “Consulate Ruatan and Truxillo.”


*Approved, February 11, 1878.*

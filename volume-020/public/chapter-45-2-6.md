# Chapter 6

> AN ACT authorizing binding of documents for members of Congress.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Public Printer, be authorized to bind at the Government Printing Office any books, maps, charts, or documents published by authority of Congress, upon application of any member of the Senate or House of Representatives, upon payment of the actual cost of such binding.


*Approved, December 10, 1877.*

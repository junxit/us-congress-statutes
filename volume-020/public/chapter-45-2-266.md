# Chapter 266

> AN ACT for the restoration to market of certain lands in the Territory of Utah.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That so much of the act of Congress approved May fifth, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, and entitled “An act to vacate and sell the present Indian reservation in Utah Territory, and to settle Indians of said Territory in the Uinta Valley”, as directs the Secretary of the Interior to cause to be appraised and offer for sale upon sealed bids the reservations therein referred to, be, and the same is hereby, repealed; and the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to restore the same to the public domain for disposition as other public lands.


*Approved, June 18, 1878.*

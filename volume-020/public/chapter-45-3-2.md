# Chapter 2

> An act establishing the rank of the senior inspector-general.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the passage of this act the rank of the senior inspector-general of the United States army shall be brigadier-general; but no pay or allowances shall be made to said officer other than from the date of appointment under this act: And provided, That nothing herein enacted shall authorize any increase in the number or the rank of the other officers of the Inspector-General’s Department as fixed by the first section of the act of June twenty-third, eighteen hundred and seventy-four.


*Approved, December 12, 1878.*

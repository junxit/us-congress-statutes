# Chapter 78

> AN ACT to provide for the administration of the oath of office to collectors and other officers of the customs in the district of Alaska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That until the formal organization of the Territory ofAlaska, the oath of office required by law to be taken by a collector or other officer of the customs in Alaska may be taken before the judge of any circuit or district court of the United States.


*Approved, May 2, 1878.*

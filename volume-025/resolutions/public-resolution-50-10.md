# Public Resolution 10

> Joint resolution to supply the Department of State with copies of bills and other documents.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Public Printer be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to furnish the Department of State, out of the usual number, with ten copies of each bill and joint resolution, and twenty copies of each executive document, miscellaneous document, and report of committee of either House of Congress.


*Approved, April 5, 1888.*

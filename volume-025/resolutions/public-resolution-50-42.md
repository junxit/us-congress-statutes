# Public Resolution 42

> Joint resolution to correct an error in the act making an appropriation for the Department of Agriculture for the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, eighteen hundred and eighty-nine and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That an error in the act making an appropriation for the Department of Agriculture for the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, eighteen hundred and eighty-nine and for other purposes designating the “Ladies Silk Culture Society of California” as the “California Ladies Silk Culture Association of California” be corrected so that the same shall read “Ladies Silk Culture Society of California.”


*Approved, September 6, 1888.*

# Public Resolution 13

> Joint resolution appointing H. H. Markham, of California, a manager for the National Homes for Disabled Soldiers, to fill the vacancy caused by the death of William Blanding.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That H. H. Markham, of California, be, and he is hereby, appointed a manager of the National Homes for Disabled Soldiers, to fill the vacancy caused by the death of William Blanding.


*Approved, March 2, 1889.*

# Public Resolution 43

> Joint resolution to continue the provisions of a Joint Resolution entitled a “Joint Resolution to continue the provisions of a joint resolution entitled a ‘Joint Resolution to provide temporarily for the expenditures of the Government’.”

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of a joint resolution entitled a “Joint Resolution to continue the provisions of a Joint Resolution entitled a ‘Joint Resolution to provide temporarily for the expenditures of the Government.’” approved September first, eighteen hundred and eighty-eight, be and the same are hereby extended and continued in full force and effect to and including the twenty-fifth day of September, eighteen hundred and eighty-eight.


*Approved, September 15, 1888.*

# Public Resolution 48

> Joint resolution in aid of the sufferers from yellow fever.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of one hundred thousand dollars, or so much thereof as may be necessary, be and the same is hereby appropriated out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to be expended under the direction of the President of the United States whenever, in his opinion, such expenditure will tend to the eradication of the epidemic of yellow fever now prevailing in the United States, or its spread from State to State.


*Approved, October 12, 1888.*

# Public Resolution 41

> Joint resolution to continue the provisions of a joint resolution entitled a “Joint Resolution to provide temporarily for the expenditures of the Government.”

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of a joint resolution entitled a “Joint Resolution to continue the provisions of a Joint resolution approved June thirtieth, eighteen hundred and eighty-eight, entitled a ‘Joint Resolution to provide temporarily for the expenditures of the Government’” approved July thirty-first, eighteen hundred and eighty-eight, be and the same are hereby extended and continued in full force and effect to and including the fifteenth day of September eighteen hundred and eighty-eight.


*Approved, September 1, 1888.*

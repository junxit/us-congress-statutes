# Public Resolution 25

> Joint resolution granting leave of absence to certain persons employed in the service of the United States.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the heads of Departments are hereby authorized to grant to all persons employed in the service of the United States who were present at the battle of Gettysburg, sufficient leave of absence, without loss of pay, to attend the reunion to be held at Gettysburg on the third day of July next, such absence not to be taken from the allowance made by law.


*Approved, July 2, 1888.*

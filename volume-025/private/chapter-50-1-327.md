# Chapter 327

> An Act granting a pension to Stephen Singleton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, directed to place the name of Stephen Singleton, late a private in Company G, Eighty-seventh Indiana Volunteers, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, May 28, 1888.*

# Chapter 580

> An Act granting a pension to Belle M. Baker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Belle M. Baker, widow of Edward Baker, late a private in Company B, Third Regiment of Kentucky Volunteer Cavalry.

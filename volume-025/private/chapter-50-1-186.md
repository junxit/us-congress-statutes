# Chapter 186

> An Act granting a pension to Nancy O. Gray.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Nancy O. Gray, widow of Wilson Gray, late of Company C, Eighteenth New Hampshire Volunteers.


*Approved, April 23, 1888.*

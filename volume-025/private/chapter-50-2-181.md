# Chapter 181

> An act granting a pension to Florence Courtney Cochnower.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Florence Courtney Cochnower, at the rate of twelve dollars per month, for services in the field and as a nurse during the war of the rebellion.


*Approved, February 22, 1889.*

# Chapter 843

> An Act for the relief of John H. Claus.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John H. Claus, late a private in Company K. One hundred and eighth Regiment Ohio Infantry Volunteers, and also late a private in the Ordnance Department of the United States Army.


*Approved, August 9, 1888.*

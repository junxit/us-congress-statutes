# Chapter 452

> An Act for the relief of Hiram M. Goss.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Hiram M. Goss, late of Company A, Ninety-fourth Regiment New York Infantry, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, June 21, 1888.*

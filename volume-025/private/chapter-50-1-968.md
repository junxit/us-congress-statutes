# Chapter 968

> An Act granting a pension to Albert O. Robb.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Albert O. Robb, formerly a private in Company K, Twenty-third Regiment of Kentucky Volunteers.


*Approved, September 6, 1888.*

# Chapter 776

> An Act granting a pension to Eliza Trefren.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Eliza Trefren, widow of James Trefren, late of Company F, Seventeenth Vermont United States Infantry.


*Approved, August 7, 1888.*

# Chapter 633

> An Act granting a pension to Emil Schattle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Emil Schattle, late of Company F, Ninth Iowa Cavalry.


*Approved, July 16, 1888.*

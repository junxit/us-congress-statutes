# Chapter 945

> An Act granting a pension to Hannah L. Irwin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place on. the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Hannah L. Irwin, widow of Cyrus E. Irwin, deceased, late of Company D. Thirteenth Regiment of Pennsylvania Volunteer Cavalry.


*Approved, September 3, 1888.*

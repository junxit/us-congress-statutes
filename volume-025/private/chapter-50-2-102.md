# Chapter 102

> An act granting a pension to Danville A. Ricker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Danville A. Ricker, father of Henry G. Ricker, late private in Company K, Seventh Regiment Maine Volunteers.


*Approved, January 30, 1889.*

# Chapter 297

> An act granting a pension to Charlotte T. Alderman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Charlotte T. Alderman, widow of James Alderman, who was a private in Company E, Forty-second Regiment Illinois Volunteers.


*Approved, February 26, 1889.*

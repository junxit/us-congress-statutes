# Chapter 990

> An Act for the relief of Albert Watson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll the name of Albert Watson, the invalid son of Peter D. Watson, late a private of Company B. Seventy-sixth Regiment Illinois Volunteers, and pay said son a pension of eighteen dollars per month for and during his natural life.


*Approved, September 6, 1888.*

# Chapter 93

> An Act granting a pension to Stephen D. Redfield.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Stephen D. Redfield, late a second lieutenant in Company A. Thirty-seventh Regiment Iowa Volunteers, and to pay him a pension of seventeen dollars a month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, April 13, 1888.*

# Chapter 781

> An Act granting a pension to James McIntyre.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provision and limitations of the pension laws, the name of James McIntyre, late a private in Company G, Tenth Michigan Infantry.


*Approved, August 7, 1888.*

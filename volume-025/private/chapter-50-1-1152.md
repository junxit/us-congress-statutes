# Chapter 1152

> An Act for the relief of Catharine Teegardin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby , authorized and directed to place oil the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Catharine Teegardin, widow of the late Peter Teegardin, who was a member of Company D, One hundred and eighteenth Regiment Ohio Volunteers, and whose pension certificate was numbered three hundred and twenty-eight thousand five hundred and sixty-eight.


*Approved, October 15, 1888.*

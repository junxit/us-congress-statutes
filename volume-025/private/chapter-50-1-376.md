# Chapter 376

> An Act granting a pension to George W. Schell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George W. Schell, late of Company D, Thirty-seventh Regiment of Iowa Volunteers.


*Approved, June 8, 1888.*

# Chapter 14

> An act granting an increase of pension to Caroline M. McDougal.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Caroline M. McDougal, widow of the late Admiral David P. McDougal, United States Navy, at the rate of one hundred dollars per month on account of her total blindness and the necessity of constant personal attendance and assistance.


*Approved, December 24, 1888.*

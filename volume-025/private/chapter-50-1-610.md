# Chapter 610

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Sarah A. Wilcox, now Roberts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to place on the pension-roll, at the rate of twelve dollars per month, the name of Sarah A. Wilcox, now Roberts, who was a nurse in the United States hospitals during the late war. That the said pension of twelve dollars per month shall be in lieu of any pension heretofore granted her.


*Approved, July 9, 1888.*

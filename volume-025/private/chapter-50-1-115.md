# Chapter 115

> An Act for the relief of Mary B. Kirby.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Mary B. Kirby, widow of Reynold Marvin Kirby. Major of the First Regiment of United States Artillery, who died at Fort Sullivan, in the year eighteen hundred and forty-two, while in the line of duty, from disease contracted in the service, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, April 16, 1888.*

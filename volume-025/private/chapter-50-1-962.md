# Chapter 962

> An Act granting a pension to Margaret J. McQuary.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Margaret J. McQuary, daughter of Perry McQuary, late a sergeant of Company B, Ninety-ninth Indiana Volunteers, who was killed in battle before Atlanta, Georgia, July twenty-eight, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, upon the pension-rolls, at the rate of eighteen dollars per month, subject to the rules and regulations of the Pension Department.


*Approved, September 6, 1888.*

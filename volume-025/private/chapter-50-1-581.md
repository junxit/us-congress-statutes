# Chapter 581

> An Act granting a pension to Webster C. Webb.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Webster C. Webb, late second lieutenant of Company H, Seventeenth Regiment of Kentucky Volunteer Cavalry.

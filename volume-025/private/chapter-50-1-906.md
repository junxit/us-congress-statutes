# Chapter 906

> An Act for the relief of Nancy G. Alexander.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the. United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Nancy G. Alexander, of Cumberland County, Kentucky, the sum of two hundred and fifty dollars, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for coal taken from her by officers of the United States Navy and used by the same on gun-boats on the Cumberland River, during the late war of the rebellion.


*Approved, August 21, 1888.*

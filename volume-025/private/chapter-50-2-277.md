# Chapter 277

> An act for the relief of James T. Teeple.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slides of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, directed and authorized to place upon the pension-roll, the name of James T. Teeple, late of Company A, Seventh Michigan Volunteers, at the rate of forty dollars per month.


*Received by the President February 13, 1889.*

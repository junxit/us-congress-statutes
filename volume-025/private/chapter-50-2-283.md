# Chapter 283

> An act granting a pension to Alexander M. Boatright.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slides of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-rolls the name of Alexander M. Boatright, late of Captain Houston’s company. Colonel Sanfords volunteers, Illinois militia, and pay him a pension of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 26, 1889.*

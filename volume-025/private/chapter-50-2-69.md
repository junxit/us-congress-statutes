# Chapter 69

> An act granting a pension to Ann Bryan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ann Bryan, widow of William Bryan, late of Forty-sixth Regiment of Illinois Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, January 17, 1889.*

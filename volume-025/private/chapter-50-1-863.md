# Chapter 863

> An Act granting a pension to David A. Yeaw.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of David A. Yeaw, late a private in Company D, Eleventh Regiment of Rhode Island Volunteers.

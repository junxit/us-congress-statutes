# Chapter 195

> An act granting a pension to Charlotte Ayres.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Charlotte Ayers, the dependent mother of Benjamin Ayres, a private soldier of Company I, Fifth Regiment Kansas Cavalry.


*Approved, February 22, 1889.*

# Chapter 198

> An Act granting a pension to Christopher Wisemiller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Christopher Wisemiller, late private Company B. Third Regiment Wisconsin Cavalry Volunteers.


*Approved, April 24, 1888.*

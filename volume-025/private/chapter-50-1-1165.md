# Chapter 1165

> An Act granting a pension to Nelson J. Crook.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Nelson J. Crook, late a member of Company D. of the Third Missouri Cavalry Volunteers, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, October 16, 1888.*

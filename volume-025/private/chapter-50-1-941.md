# Chapter 941

> An Act granting a pension to Flora Heath.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-rolls the name of Flora Heath, an invalid daughter of Samuel Heath, late a private of Company C, Third Regiment of Indiana Cavalry, in the war of the rebellion, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, September 3, 1888.*

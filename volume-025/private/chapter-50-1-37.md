# Chapter 37

> An Act granting a pension to John Humes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place oil the pension-roll, subject to the pension laws, the name of John Humes, father of George W. Humes, deceased, late a private in Company E, Second Maine Cavalry Volunteers.


*Approved, March 23, 1888.*

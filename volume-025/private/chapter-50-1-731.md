# Chapter 731

> An Act for the relief of the Church of the Ascension in the District of Columbia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That all taxes and assessments, general and special, with the interest and penalties now due and unpaid thereon upon lots one. two, and three in square two hundred and eighty-two, in the city of Washington, District of Columbia, now owned and occupied by the Church of the Ascension, be, and the same hereby are. remitted.


*Approved, August 1, 1888.*

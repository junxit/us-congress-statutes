# Chapter 509

> An act granting a pension to Sarah Jackson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah Jackson, an imbecile daughter of James Jackson, late of Company G. Thirtieth Regiment of Ohio Volunteers, at the rate of eighteen dollars per month, payable to the legally appointed guardian.


*Approved, March 2, 1889.*

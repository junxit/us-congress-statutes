# Chapter 104

> An Act granting a pension to Miss Capitola V. Harsh, daughter of Daniel Harsh.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Miss Capitola V. Harsh, daughter of Daniel Harsh, late of Company H. Fifty-eighth Regiment of Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension of eighteen dollars per month.


*Approved, April 14, 1888.*

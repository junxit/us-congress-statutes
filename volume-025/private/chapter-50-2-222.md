# Chapter 222

> An act granting a pension to Nona J. Tillery, minor child of Wyatte L. Tillery.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Nona J. Tillery, minor child of Wyatte L. Tillery, late a member of Captain J. W. Youngers’ company of Clay Comity Enrolled Missouri Militia.


*Approved, February 23, 1889.*

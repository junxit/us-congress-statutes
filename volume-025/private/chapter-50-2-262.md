# Chapter 262

> An act granting a pension to John Y. Hooper.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slides of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Y. Hooper, late of Company F, First Regiment Nebraska Volunteer Cavalry, and who is now an inmate of the hospital for the insane at Saint Peter, in the State of Minnesota, and that the pension allowed him be paid to his legally constituted guardian.


*Approved, February 25, 1889.*

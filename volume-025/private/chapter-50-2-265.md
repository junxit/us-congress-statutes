# Chapter 265

> An act granting a pension to Margaret M. Nugent.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slides of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Margaret M. Nugent, foster-mother of Patrick J. Hayes, late of Company G, One hundred and fifty-first Regiment New York Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1889.*

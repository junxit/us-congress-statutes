# Chapter 486

> An act for the relief of James A. Stewart.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay James A. Stewart, of Cincinnati, Ohio, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, twenty-five hundred dollars, said sum to be in full payment for services rendered as pilot of Government transports and gun-boat from October sixth, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, to April thirtieth, anno Domini eighteen hundred and sixty-five.


*Approved, March 2, 1889.*

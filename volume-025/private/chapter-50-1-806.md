# Chapter 806

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs Catharine Peterson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs Catharine Peterson, widow of Anthony Peterson late first lieutenant Company F, Thirty-seventh Regiment Ohio Volunteers.


*Approved, August 8, 1888.*

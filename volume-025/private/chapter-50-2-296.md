# Chapter 296

> An act granting a pension to John B. Covert.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slides of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John B. Covert, late a private in Company B, One hundred and forty-seventh Regiment Pennsylvania, Volunteers.


*Approved, February 26, 1889.*

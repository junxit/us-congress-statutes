# Chapter 942

> An Act granting a pension to Gilbert Reed.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Gilbert Reed, and pay him a pension at the rate provided by law for a second lieutenant.


*Approved, September 3, 1888.*

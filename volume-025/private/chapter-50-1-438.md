# Chapter 438

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Mercy Knight.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Mercy Knight, dependent stepmother of Lendall Wright, deceased, late a private in Company D, Fifteenth Regiment of Maine Infantry Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, June 21, 1888.*

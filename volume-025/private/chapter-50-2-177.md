# Chapter 177

> An act to replace Mrs. R. S. Horton upon the pension-rolls.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, empowered and directed to replace Mrs. R. S. Horton, widow of Captain William H. Seaton, late of Company D. Twenty-sixth Ohio Volunteers, upon the pension-rolls, and pay her a pension from the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 21, 1889.*

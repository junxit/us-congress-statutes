# Chapter 286

> An act granting a pension to Charles Schuler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slides of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be and he hereby is, directed to place upon the pension-roll, at eighteen dollars per month, the name of Charles Schuler, late a member of Company K, Fourth Minnesota Infantry, he being mentally and physically incapacitated, subject to the provisions of the pension laws, and pay the same to his lawfully constituted guardian.


*Approved, February 26, 1889.*

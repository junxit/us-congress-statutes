# Chapter 131

> An Act granting a pension to Jane Thomas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Mrs. Jane Thomas, of Dubuque, Iowa, an Army nurse, widow of Lewis A. Thomas, late quartermaster-sergeant of the Forty-sixth Regiment Iowa Infantry Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, April 19, 1888.*

# Chapter 265

> An Act granting a pension to Eliza Shreeve

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place oil the pension-roll, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws, the name of Eliza Shreeve, widow of Alfred T. Shreeve, late of Company A, Sixth Regiment Maryland Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, May 17, 1888.*

# Chapter 405

> An Act to increase the pension of William Clark.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to increase the pension of William Clark, a veteran of the war of eighteen hundred and twelve, from sixteen dollars per month, the amount now allowed him, to thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, June 18, 1888.*

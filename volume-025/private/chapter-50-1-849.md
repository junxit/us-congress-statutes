# Chapter 849

> An Act granting a pension to Martha J. Rushford, widow of John Rushford.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the restrictions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Martha J. Rushford, widow of John Rushford, late of Company F, Sixteenth Wisconsin Volunteers.


*Approved, August 9, 1888.*

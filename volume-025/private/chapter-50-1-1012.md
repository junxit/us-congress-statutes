# Chapter 1012

> An Act for the relief of Thomas Strodder.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is. directed to place the name of Thomas Strodder, late of Company K. Fourth Regiment United States Colored Volunteers, on the pension-rolls, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws.


*Approved, September 11, 1888.*

# Chapter 474

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Therese Guelich.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and is hereby, instructed and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Therese Guelich, a volunteer nurse in the late war and pay her a pension of twelve dollars per month, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 22, 1888.*

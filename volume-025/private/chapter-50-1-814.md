# Chapter 814

> An Act granting a pension to George C. Chase.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George C. Chase, late of Company F, Third Regiment Vermont Volunteers.


*Approved, August 8, 1888.*

# Chapter 852

> An Act granting a pension to Ralph P. Wilborn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House, of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ralph P. Wilborn, late a private in Company F, Twenty-first Regiment of Kentucky Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, August 9, 1888.*

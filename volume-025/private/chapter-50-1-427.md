# Chapter 427

> An Act increasing the pension of Milton Judd.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Milton Judd, a pensioner of the United States, a soldier in the late civil war, in Company B, Thirteenth Regiment Kentucky Volunteer Infantry, be, and he is hereby, allowed a pension at the rate of forty-five dollars per month, in lieu of thirty-six dollars per month as now allowed, and that the Secretary of the Interior be requested and directed to place the name of said Judd on the roll of pensions and paid at above rate.


*Approved, June 20, 1888.*

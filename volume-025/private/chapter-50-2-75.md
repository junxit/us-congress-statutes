# Chapter 75

> An act granting a pension to P. F. Jonte

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of P. F. Jonte, late a member of Company A, Sixtieth Regiment Ohio Volunteers, of Knoxville, Iowa, and pay him a pension, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, January 17, 1889.*

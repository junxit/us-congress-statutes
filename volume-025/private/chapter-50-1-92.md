# Chapter 92

> An Act granting a pension to Ida M. Welton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ida M. Welton, only surviving child of George C. Welton, late a soldier in the Sixth Regiment Vermont Volunteers and pay her a pension at the rate of eighteen dollars per month.


*Approved, April 13, 1888.*

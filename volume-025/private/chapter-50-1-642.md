# Chapter 642

> An Act to increase the pension of Edmund Ashworth.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension of Edmund Ashworth, late a private in Company E. Eighth United States Infantry, in the war with Mexico, be, and the same is increased to twenty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, July 16, 1888.*

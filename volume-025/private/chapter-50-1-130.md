# Chapter 130

> An Act granting a pension to Ida B. Linthicum.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ida B. Linthicum, of Quincy. Illinois, daughter of John D. Linthicum, deceased, late a private in the One hundred and eighteenth Regiment Illinois Volunteers.


*Approved, April 19, 1888.*

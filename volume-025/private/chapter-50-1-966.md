# Chapter 966

> An Act granting a pension to Lucy A. Jordan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Lucy A. Jordan, widow of James W. Jordan, late of Company C. Fifth New Hampshire Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, September 6, 1888.*

# Chapter 281

> An act for the relief of Emily Cross.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slides of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Emily Cross, mother of Robert S. Cross late private of Company F, Thirtieth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, February 26, 1889.*

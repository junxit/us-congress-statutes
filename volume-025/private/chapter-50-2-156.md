# Chapter 156

> An act granting a pension to Catherine M. Lee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Catherine M. Lee, a volunteer nurse in the late war, giving her, during life, the sum of twelve dollars a month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 13, 1889.*

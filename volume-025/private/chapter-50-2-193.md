# Chapter 193

> An act granting a pension to William L. Wilson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll the name of William L. Wilson, who was a private in Captain Samuel Hollingsworth’s company of mounted volunteers in Colonel Thompson’s regiment Illinois Volunteers, in the Black Hawk war, and pay him a pension of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 22, 1889.*

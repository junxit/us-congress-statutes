# Chapter 620

> An Act granting a pension to Martha F. Woodrum, widow of James Woodrum, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, at the rate of fifty dollars per month, the name of Martha F. Woodrum, surviving widow of James Woodrum, deceased, late private of Company C, Ninth Regiment of Kansas Volunteer Cavalry

# Chapter 1009

> An Act for the relief of H. C. Markham.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to remove the charge of desertion resting upon the military record of H. C. Markham, late assistant surgeon Nineteenth Regiment Wisconsin Volunteers. and to accept the resignation of said H. C. Markham tendered on March twenty-seventh, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, as of date April twenty-third, eighteen hundred and sixty-three.


*Approved, September 11, 1888.*

# Chapter 274

> An Act granting a pension to Hannah Varquison.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the limitation of law. the name of Hannah Varquison, widow of John M. Varquison, late private in Company A. One hundred and forty-first Regiment of Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, May 18, 1888.*

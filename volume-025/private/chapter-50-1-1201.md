# Chapter 1201

> An Act granting a pension to Abbie L. Ham.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Abbie L. Ham. widow of John S. P. Ham, late captain of Company C, Thirteenth Maine Volunteers.


*Approved, October 18, 1888.*

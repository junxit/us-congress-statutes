# Chapter 306

> An act granting a pension to W. B. Green.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of W. B. Green, acting assistant surgeon United States Army.


*Received by the President February 16, 1889.*

# Chapter 288

> An Act for the relief of James M. McKeehan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to all the limitations and provisions of the pension laws of the United States, the name of James M. McKeehan, late private in Company G, Seventh Kentucky Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, May 18, 1888.*

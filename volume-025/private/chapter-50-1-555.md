# Chapter 555

> An Act granting a pension to Augustus Axmacher.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Augustus Axmacher, late of Company C, One hundred and Eighty-seventh New York Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month.


*Approved, July 5, 1888.*

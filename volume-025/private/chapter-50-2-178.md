# Chapter 178

> An act to increase the pension of Elijah W. Penny.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Elijah W. Penny, late lieutenant-colonel of the One hundred thirtieth Regiment of Indiana Volunteers by paying him the sum of twelve dollars a month for wound in the side, in addition to the amount to which he is entitled by law for loss of arm at the shoulder.


*Approved, February 21, 1889.*

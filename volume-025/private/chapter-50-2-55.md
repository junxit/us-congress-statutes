# Chapter 55

> An act for the relief of William E Springstein

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is directed to put on the pension roll the name of William E Springstein, of Company G Fourteenth Regiment of Michigan Volunteers, at the rate of forty five dollars per month.


*Approved, January 16, 1889.*

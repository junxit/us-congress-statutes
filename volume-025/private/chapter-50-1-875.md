# Chapter 875

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Margaret Gallagher

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Margaret Gallagher, widow of Edward Gallagher, late private Company K, Forty-second Regiment New York Volunteers.


*Approved, August 13, 1888.*

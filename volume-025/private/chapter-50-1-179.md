# Chapter 179

> An Act to grant a pension to Squire Admire

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Squire Admire, late a private in the Third Regiment Indiana State Legion.


*Approved, April 23, 1888.*

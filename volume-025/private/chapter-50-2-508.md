# Chapter 508

> An act granting a pension to Cathrine. G. Bodfish.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Cathrine G. Bodfish, widow of the late Oliver Bodfish, a soldier of the war of eighteen hundred and twelve.


*Approved, March 2, 1889.*

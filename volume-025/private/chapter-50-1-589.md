# Chapter 589

> An Act to restore J. Rock Williamson to the pension-roll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to restore to the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of J. Rock Williamson, late a private in Company G, One hundred and fifth Illinois Volunteer Infantry.

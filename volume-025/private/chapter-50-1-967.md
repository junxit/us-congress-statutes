# Chapter 967

> An Act granting a pension to Henry Alward, dependent father of Henry M. Alward.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Henry Alward, dependent father of Henry M. Alward. deceased, late a private of Company H. Fortieth Regiment Indiana Volunteer Infantry, according to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, September 6, 1888.*

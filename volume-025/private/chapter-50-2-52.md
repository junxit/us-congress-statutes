# Chapter 52

> An act granting a pension to Carter W. Tiller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Carter W. Tiller, dependent father of G W Tiller, late a private in Company A, Twenty-eighth Kentucky Volunteer Infantry. on the pension-roll subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Received by the President January 4, 1889.*

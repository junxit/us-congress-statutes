# Chapter 156

> An Act for the relief of Julia A. Darrell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Julia A. Darrell, the dependent mother of William Burr Darrell, late of the United States ship Albany, which sailed from New York in June, eighteen hundred and fifty-five, and was never heard from afterwards.


*Approved, April 23, 1888.*

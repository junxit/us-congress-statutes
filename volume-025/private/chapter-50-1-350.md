# Chapter 350

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Andrew Franklin, alias McKee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Andrew Franklin, alias McKee, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving under an act of Congress approved February twenty-eighth, eighteen hundred and eighty-five.


*Approved, June 4, 1888.*

# Chapter 346

> An Act for the relief of John C. Weaver.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to John C. Weaver, of Dennison. Clark County, Illinois, the sum of six hundred dollars, out of any moneys in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated as compensation tor a substitute furnished the Union Army during the war.


*Approved, June 4, 1888.*

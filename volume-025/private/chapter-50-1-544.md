# Chapter 544

> An Act for the relief of Samuel E. Wyman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United, States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Samuel E. Wyman, late of Company G, Fifth Regiment Massachusetts Infantry Volunteers, on the pension-roll, subject to the conditions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, July 5, 1888.*

# Chapter 267

> An Act granting a pension to David W. Seely.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of David W. Seely on the pension-roll, subject to provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, May 17, 1888.*

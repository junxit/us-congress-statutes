# Chapter 643

> An Act to increase the pension of Edward Healy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to increase the pension, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws of Edward Healy, late of the Sixth United States Infantry, and pay him a pension of forty-five dollars per month in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, July 16, 1888.*

# Chapter 1183

> An Act to increase the pension of Charles Hahneman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized to increase the pension of Charles Hahneman, late a private in Company C. Forty-first New York Volunteers, and pay him at the rate of forty-five dollars per month, in lieu of the pension now paid to him.


*Approved, October 16, 1888.*

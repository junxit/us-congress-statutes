# Chapter 434

> An Act to increase the pension of Henry L. Potter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized, and directed to increase the pension of Henry L. Potter, late colonel of the Seventy-first Regiment of New York State Volunteers, to fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, June 20, 1888.*

# Chapter 527

> An Act granting a pension to Mary A. Wells.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Mary A. Wells, a resident of Marshall, Michigan, at the rate of twelve dollars per month, on account of disability resulting from disease contracted while serving as a hospital nurse during the war of the rebellion.


*Approved, July 4, 1888.*

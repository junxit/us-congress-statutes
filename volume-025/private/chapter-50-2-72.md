# Chapter 72

> An act granting a pension to Captain William J. Duley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William J. Duley, chief of scouts, with the rank of captain, in the Army of the Northwest, Major-General John Pope commanding.


*Approved, January 17, 1889.*

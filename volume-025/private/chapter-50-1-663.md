# Chapter 663

> An Act to increase the pension of Manhatton Pickett,

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Manhatton Pickett, late a sergeant of Company B. One hundred and twelfth Regiment New York Volunteers, to forty-five dollars per month, in lieu of the pension now authorized by law.


*Approved, July 16, 1888.*

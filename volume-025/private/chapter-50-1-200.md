# Chapter 200

> An Act for the relief of Mrs. Betsey Winterbottom.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-rolls, subject to the restrictions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Betsey Winterbottom, widow of John Winterbottom, late of Company A, Twenty-seventh Regiment Wisconsin Volunteers.


*Approved, April 25, 1888.*

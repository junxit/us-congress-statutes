# Chapter 579

> An Act granting a pension to Susan L. Watson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Susan L. Watson, widow of Adolphus E. Watson, late pay director, United States Navy.

# Chapter 334

> An act granting a pension to Anna Slater.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Anna Slater, a volunteer nurse in the late war. giving her, during life, the sum of twelve dollars a month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 1, 1889.*

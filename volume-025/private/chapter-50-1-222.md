# Chapter 222

> An Act granting a pension to William F. Randolph.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William F. Randolph, late of Company E. Thirty-fifth Regiment New Jersey Volunteers.


*Approved, May 1, 1888.*

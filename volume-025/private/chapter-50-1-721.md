# Chapter 721

> An Act granting a pension to Emma S. Free, widow of Thomas S. Free, late major of the United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Emma S. Free, widow of Major Thomas S. Free, deceased, late a major of the Tenth Regiment of Volunteers of the United States Army, at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month, the same as was allowed by law to her husband at the time of his death.


*Approved, July 31, 1888.*

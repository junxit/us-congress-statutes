# Chapter 170

> An Act to increase the pension of James Woody.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of James Woody, of Grant’s Pass, Oregon, formerly of the Forty-third Regiment Regular Army, in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve, to thirty dollars per month, in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, April 23, 1888.*

# Chapter 90

> An Act granting a pension to Angerone P. Cole, mother of George H. Cole, private Company B, Twenty-fourth Regiment, Wisconsin Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Angerone P. Cole, mother of George H. Cole, private Company B. Twenty-fourth Regiment Wisconsin Volunteers.


*Approved, April 13, 1888.*

# Chapter 1204

> An Act granting an increase of pension to John N. Bovee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of John N. Bovee, late of Company E. Eighteenth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, from thirty dollars to forty dollars per month.


*Approved, October 18, 1888.*

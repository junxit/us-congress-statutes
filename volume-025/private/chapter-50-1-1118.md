# Chapter 1118

> An Act granting a pension to George Rhody.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of George Rhody, late a private in Company K. Thirty-sixth Regiment Indiana Volunteers to thirty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, October 15, 1888.*

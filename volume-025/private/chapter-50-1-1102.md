# Chapter 1102

> An Act granting a pension to Caroline Motz.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of Pensions be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Caroline Motz, widow of the late John Motz, deceased, who was a private in Company K, Ninety-eighth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers, for pension at the rate and rank of said John Motz.


*Approved, October 12, 1888.*

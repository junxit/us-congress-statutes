# Chapter 957

> An Act granting a pension to Michael Hargain.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Michael Bargain, seaman on the United States schooner Otsego, and so forth, and pay him a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, September 6, 1888.*

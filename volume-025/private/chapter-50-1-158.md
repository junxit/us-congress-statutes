# Chapter 158

> An Act granting a pension to minor children of William Clark.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension law, the minor children of William Clark, late a private in Company C, of the Fortieth Regiment of Indiana.


*Approved, April 23, 1888.*

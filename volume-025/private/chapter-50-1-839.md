# Chapter 839

> An Act for the relief of Eliza Russell, widow of Eldredge Russell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Eliza Russell, widow of Eldridge Russell, of General Harris’ command, war of eighteen hundred and thirty-six, on the pension-roll at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, August 9, 1888.*

# Chapter 9

> An act for the relief of George S. Thwing.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-rolls, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George S. Thwing, formerly of Company E, First Regiment New York Marine Artillery.


*Approved, December 24, 1888.*

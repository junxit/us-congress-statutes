# Chapter 100

> An Act granting a pension to J. Miller Raub

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of J. Miller Raub, late of Company D. One hundred and twenty-second Regiment of Pennsylvania Volunteers.


*Approved, April 14, 1888.*

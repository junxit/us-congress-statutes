# Chapter 498

> An act to restore the pension of Ann G. Barker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the act entitled “An act to restore pensions in certain cases”, approved June ninth, eighteen hundred and eighty, shall be construed so as to include within its provisions Ann G. Barker, widow of Thomas Barker, deceased, late a captain in the United States Army.


*Approved, March 2, 1889.*

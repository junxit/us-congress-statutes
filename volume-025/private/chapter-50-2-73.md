# Chapter 73

> An act granting a pension to John Wallace.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Wallace, father of Robert Wallace, deceased, late of Company C, Ninety-third Regiment of New York Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, January 17, 1889.*

# Chapter 440

> An Act granting a pension to Penelope Morton, widow of Lieutenant Morton

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Penelope Morton, widow of James Morton, late lieutenant Company F, Twenty-second Regiment Kentucky Volunteers.


*Approved, June 21, 1888.*

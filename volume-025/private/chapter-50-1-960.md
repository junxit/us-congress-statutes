# Chapter 960

> An Act granting a pension to William J. Toncray, of Tennessee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to inscribe on the pension-rolls the name of William J. Toncray, late a member of Company F. Second Tennessee Mounted Infantry, at the rate of eight dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, September 6, 1888.*

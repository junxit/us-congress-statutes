# Chapter 1129

> An Act granting a pension to Lydia Ann Wilber.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Lydia Ann Wilber, sister of John M. Wilber, who was a soldier in Company A. of the Seventy-seventh Regiment of Illinois Infantry Volunteers, in the war of the rebellion, and was killed in battle May twenty-second, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, and pay her a pension, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, October 15, 1888.*

# Chapter 335

> An act granting a pension to Mary J. Byrd.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations or the pension laws, the name of Mary J. Byrd, widow of Solomon G. Byrd, late of Company B, Forty-third Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, March 1, 1889.*

# Chapter 247

> An act granting a pension to William Fowler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William Fowler, at the rate of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1889.*

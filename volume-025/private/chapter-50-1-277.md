# Chapter 277

> An Act increasing the pension of Howard S. Abbott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension of Howard S. Abbott, certificate seventy-three thousand five hundred and twenty-two, late adjutant of the Seventy-eighth Ohio Volunteer Infantry, be increased from seventeen dollars to thirty dollars per month, the same to be in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, May 18, 1888.*

# Chapter 36

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs Eliza L. Mace.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the laws regulating pensions, the name of Mrs Eliza L. Mace, widow of Richard E. Mace, late of Company I, Third Maine Volunteers, deceased.


*Approved, March 23, 1888.*

# Chapter 996

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Helen B. Brown.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Helen B. Brown, widow of George H. Brown, deceased, late a private of Company B. First Maine Cavalry Volunteers.

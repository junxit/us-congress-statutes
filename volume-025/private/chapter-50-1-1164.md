# Chapter 1164

> An Act granting a pension to Johanna Geyer, widow of Gustav W. Geyer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Johanna Geyer, widow of Gustav W. Geyer, late a private in Company H, One hundred and eighty-seventh New York Volunteers.


*Approved, October 16, 1888.*

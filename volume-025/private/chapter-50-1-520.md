# Chapter 520

> An Act granting a pension to John Fox.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Fox. late a member of Company H, Seventh Regiment Massachusetts Volunteers.


*Approved, July 4, 1888.*

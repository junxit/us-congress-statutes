# Chapter 453

> An act granting a pension to Mary B. Hunt,

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Mary B. Hunt, widow of Brevet Major-General Henry J. Hunt, deceased, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 2, 1889.*

# Chapter 706

> An Act granting a pension to John Child.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of John Child, late a private in Company D, Sixty-second New York Volunteers, on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, July 24, 1888.*

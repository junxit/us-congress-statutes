# Chapter 1237

> An Act for the removal of the political disabilities of Theodore Lewis, of Louisiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That Theodore Lewis, of the State of Louisiana, is hereby relieved of all political disabilities imposed upon him by the third section of the Fourteenth Amendment to the Constitution of the United States.


*Approved, October 20, 1888.*

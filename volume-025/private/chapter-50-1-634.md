# Chapter 634

> An Act granting a pension to W. A. Hicks.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place oh the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of W. A. Hicks, late of Company M, Fifteenth Regiment Kansas Volunteer Cavalry.


*Approved, July 16, 1888.*

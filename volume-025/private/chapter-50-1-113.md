# Chapter 113

> An Act to increase pension of James A. Buck.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to increase the pension of James A. Buck, late private of Company E. One hundred and fifty-first Regiment of Illinois Volunteers, to forty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, April 16, 1888.*

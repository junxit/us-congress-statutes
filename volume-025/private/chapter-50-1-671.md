# Chapter 671

> An Act granting a pension to Milton Merwin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Milton Merwin, the child of Morris M. Merwin, late a private Company E. Ninetieth New York Volunteers, at the rate of eighteen dollars per month.

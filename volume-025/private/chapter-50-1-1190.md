# Chapter 1190

> An Act granting a pension to Rachel Rogers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Rachel Rogers, dependent mother of Jeremiah T. Rogers, late sergeant of Company H, Fifty-fourth Regiment of Illinois Volunteer Infantry.

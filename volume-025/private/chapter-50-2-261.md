# Chapter 261

> An act granting a pension to George W. Lloyd.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slides of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of George W. Lloyd on the pension-roll, subject to the pension laws of the United States, he being a soldier in the late war, in Company D. Tenth Missouri Cavalry.


*Approved, February 25, 1889.*

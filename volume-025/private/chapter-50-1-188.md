# Chapter 188

> An Act for the relief of Mary E. Hawn, widow of Emanuel R. Hawn, late surgeon of the Forty-ninth Regiment Indiana Volunteers, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to place on the pension-roll the name of Mary E. Hawn, widow of Emanuel R. Hawn, late surgeon of the Forty-ninth Regiment of Indiana Volunteers, subject to the conditions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, April 23, 1888.*

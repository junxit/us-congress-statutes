# Chapter 909

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Avis J. Hockey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the. United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Avis J. Hockey, widow of William Hockey, late a corporal in Company G, Eighteenth Illinois Infantry.


*Approved, August 21, 1888.*

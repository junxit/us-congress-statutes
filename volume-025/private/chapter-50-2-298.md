# Chapter 298

> An act granting a pension to Margaret A. Hillard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to ¡dace on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Margaret A. Hillard, widow of Frank S. Hillard, late a lieutenant in the Seventh United States Colored Troops.


*Approved, February 26, 1889.*

# Chapter 494

> An act granting a pension to Jane Reilly.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Jane Reilly, widow of Alexander M. Reilly, late sergeant of Company M, First New York (Lincoln’s) Volunteer Cavalry, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws.


*Approved, March 2, 1889.*

# Chapter 425

> An Act granting a pension to George Park.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George Park, dependent father of William C. Park. Company H, Fifty-seventh Massachusetts Volunteers.


*Received by the President, June 7, 1888.*

# Chapter 1046

> An Act granting a pension to Henry R. Blakiston.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, at the rate of seventy-two dollars per month, the name of Henry R. Blakiston, late an acting master in the United States Navy.


*Approved, September 26, 1888.*

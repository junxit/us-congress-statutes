# Chapter 80

> An act for the relief of William A. Mathes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, instructed to increase the pension of William A. Mathes, late of Company B, Eighteenth Regiment Indiana Volunteers, to forty-live dollars per month, in lieu of the pension now paid him.


*Approved, January 17, 1889.*

# Chapter 299

> An act granting a pension to Mary P. Myers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary P. Myers, widow of David Myers, late a private in Captain Whitmore’s company, Colonel Goodwin’s regiment of mounted volunteers, in Florida war.


*Approved, February 26, 1889.*

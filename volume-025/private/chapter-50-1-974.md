# Chapter 974

> An Act granting an increase of pension to John D. Jones.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of John D. Jones, late a private in Company G, Seventh Kansas Cavalry, and to pay him a pension of thirty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, September 6, 1888.*

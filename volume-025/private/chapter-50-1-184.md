# Chapter 184

> An Act granting a pension to Elizabeth J. Hascall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elizabeth J. Hascall, widow of Bailey Hascall. late acting assistant paymaster of the United States Navy, during the late war of the rebellion.


*Approved, April 23, 1888.*

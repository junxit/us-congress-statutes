# Chapter 1154

> An Act to place the name of Matilda Spangler on the pension-roll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Matilda Spangler, widow of John E. Spangler, deceased, late a private in Michigan Battery, Captain Lamphier, commanding.


*Approved, October 15, 1888.*

# Chapter 813

> An Act granting a pension to Eliza Mathews.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Eliza Mathews, dependent mother of John Mathews, late a private in Company F, Thirty-second Maine Volunteers.


*Approved, August 8, 1888.*

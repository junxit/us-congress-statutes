# Chapter 223

> An act granting a pension to Thompson D. Hatfield.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Thompson D. Hatfield, at the rate of twenty-five dollars a month, this sum to be paid only to the legally appointed guardian of said Thompson D. Hatfield, an insane person, for the purpose of his support and maintenance.


*Approved, February 23, 1889.*

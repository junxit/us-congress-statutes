# Chapter 653

> An Act granting a pension to Louise F. D. Hoit.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Louise F. D. Hoit, widow of Joseph S. Hoit, late of Company E, Seventh Regiment New Hampshire Volunteers.


*Approved, July 16, 1888.*

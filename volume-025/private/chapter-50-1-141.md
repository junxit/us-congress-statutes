# Chapter 141

> An Act granting a pension to Julia Stokes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Julia Stokes, mother of Thomas J. Stokes, deceased, late of the United States ship Mohawk.


*Approved, April 20, 1888.*

# Chapter 601

> An Act granting a pension to John R. Wheelock.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John R Wheelock, dependent father of William H. H. Wheelock, late of Company E, Seventh Regiment Wisconsin Volunteers.


*Approved, July 9, 1888.*

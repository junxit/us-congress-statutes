# Chapter 109

> An act granting an increase of pension to William H. Marston.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to increase the amount of pension allowed William H. Marston, late of the Seventeenth Michigan Infantry, to the sum of forty-five dollars per month, in lieu of the amount he is now receiving.


*Approved, January 30, 1889.*

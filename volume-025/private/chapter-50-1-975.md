# Chapter 975

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Almeron J. Patchin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to grant an increase of pension to Almeron J. Patchin, late of Company E. Twentieth Regiment Ohio Infantry Volunteers, and to pay to him a pension of forty-five dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, September 6, 1888.*

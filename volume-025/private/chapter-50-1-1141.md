# Chapter 1141

> An Act granting a pension to Henry Mitchell Youngblood.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Henry Mitchell Youngblood, who served in Captain Richard Sloan’s company, First Regiment Drafted Georgia Militia, Indian war, eighteen hundred and thirty-six, under the name of Michael Youngblood, and to pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, October 15, 1888.*

# Chapter 226

> An act granting a pension to Mrs Elvira Parish.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs Elvira Parish, mother of Cyrus O. Parish, late of Company H, Second United States Cavalry.


*Approved, February 23, 1889.*

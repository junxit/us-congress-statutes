# Chapter 992

> An Act for the relief of Samuel Purcell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Samuel Purcell, who was a private in Company A. First Indiana Volunteers, in the Mexican war, and pay him a pension of thirty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he now receives.

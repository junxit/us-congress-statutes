# Chapter 1120

> An Act granting a pension to Joseph Lincoln Young.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations or the pension laws, the name of Joseph Lincoln Young, son of Joseph Young, late a private of Company H, Seventeenth Regiment Maine Volunteers, at the rate of eighteen dollars per month, payable to his legally constituted guardian.


*Approved, October 15, 1888.*

# Chapter 447

> An act for the relief of Jessie Isherwood.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Jessie Isherwood, daughter of James R. Isherwood, deceased, late private Company G, Seventh New York Heavy Artillery, and pay her a pension at the rate of eighteen dollars per month.


*Approved, March 2, 1889.*

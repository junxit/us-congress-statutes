# Chapter 952

> An Act granting a pension to Joseph S. Wilson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Joseph S. Wilson, late a private in Company F, First Pennsylvania Reserve Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, September 6, 1888.*

# Chapter 1187

> An Act increasing the pension of Jesse L. Garrett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Jesse L. Garrett, formerly of the United States Marine Corps, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.

# Chapter 1107

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Jonas Doering.

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, at the rate of forty dollars per month, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jonas Doering, late private of Company A, Eighth Battalion Turner Rifles, District of Columbia, in lieu of pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, October 12, 1888.*

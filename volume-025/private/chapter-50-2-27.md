# Chapter 27

> An act granting a pension to John Lynn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Lynn, late of Company A, Forty-eighth Regiment Kentucky Volunteers.


*Approved, January 15, 1889.*

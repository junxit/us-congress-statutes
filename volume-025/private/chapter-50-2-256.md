# Chapter 256

> An act granting a pension to Elizabeth Myers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slides of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elizabeth Myers, of Appleton City. Saint Clair County, Missouri, widow of John H. Myers, deceased, late private in Company F, of the Sixtieth Regiment of Missouri Enrolled Militia ; and she shall be paid from and after the passage of this act at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1889.*

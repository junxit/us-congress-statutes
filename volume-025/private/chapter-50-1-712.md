# Chapter 712

> An Act granting increase of pension to Marcus D. Raymond.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Marcus D. Raymond, late a corporal of Company I, Twentieth Kentucky Volunteer Infantry, and a private of Company B, Fortieth Kentucky Volunteer Infantry, at the rate of twenty dollars per month, in lieu of the amount per month he is now receiving.


*Approved, July 24, 1888.*

# Chapter 282

> An Act granting a pension to Mary G. Crocker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Mary G. Crocker, widow of George W. Crocker, late of the Seventy-fifth Regiment New York Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, May 18, 1888.*

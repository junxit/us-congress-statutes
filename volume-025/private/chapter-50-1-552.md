# Chapter 552

> An Act granting a pension to Henry P. Alexander.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of Henry P. Alexander, late of Company F. One hundred and sixty-ninth Ohio Volunteers, be placed on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, July 5, 1888.*

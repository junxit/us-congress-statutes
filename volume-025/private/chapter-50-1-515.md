# Chapter 515

> An Act granting a pension to Titus Wilder.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Titus Wilder, who served in the United States Navy as armorer during the war with Mexico in eighteen hundred and forty-six and eighteen hundred and forty-seven, at the rate of seventy-two dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, July 2, 1888.*

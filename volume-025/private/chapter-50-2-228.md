# Chapter 228

> An act granting a pension to Mrs. Maria C. McPherson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs Maria C. McPherson, widow of Daniel McPherson, late a corporal of Company C, Twenty-fourth Michigan Infantry.


*Approved, February 23, 1889.*

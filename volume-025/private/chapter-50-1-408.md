# Chapter 408

> An Act for the relief of Sidney W. Whitelock.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Sidney W. Whitelock, late a private in Company K, Eleventh Regiment Missouri State Militia Cavalry, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, June 18, 1888.*

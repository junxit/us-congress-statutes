# Chapter 227

> An act granting a pension to Enoch B. Vice.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Enoch B. Vice, late private in Company C. Tenth Kentucky Cavalry, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 23, 1889.*

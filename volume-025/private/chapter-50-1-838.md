# Chapter 838

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Rebecca Manlove.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Mrs. Rebecca Manlove, widow of David Manlove, who served in the United States Navy, in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve, from twelve dollars per mouth to thirty dollars per month, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, August 9, 1888.*

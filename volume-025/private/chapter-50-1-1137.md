# Chapter 1137

> An Act granting a pension to Margaret Quinton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Margaret Quinton, an Army nurse during the late war of the rebellion, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, October 15, 1888.*

# Chapter 556

> An Act granting a pension to Martha B. Perry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll the name of Martha B. Perry, foster-mother of Charles H. Perry, late of Company G, Fourth Vermont Volunteers, deceased, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, July 5, 1888.*

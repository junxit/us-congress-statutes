# Chapter 89

> An Act for the relief of Margaret S. Murray.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, ordered to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Margaret S. Murray, of Worcester, widow of Henry Murray and mother of T. Edward Murray, musician in Company C, Third Battalion Massachusetts Rilles, who died from disease contracted in the service, leaving no widow, child, or children, and upon whom said Margaret S. Murray was dependent.


*Approved, April 13, 1888.*

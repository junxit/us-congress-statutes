# Chapter 251

> An act for the relief of Julianna Muller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is. hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Julianna Muller, widow of Frederick Muller, late a member of Company I, Thirty-fifth Regiment New Jersey Volunteers.


*Approved, February 25, 1889.*

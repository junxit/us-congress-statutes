# Chapter 106

> An act granting a pension to Kate C. Van Arnum.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Kate C. Van Arnum, widow of John W. Van Arnum, late a private in Company D, Fourth New York Heavy Artillery.


*Approved, January 30, 1889.*

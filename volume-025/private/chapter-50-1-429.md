# Chapter 429

> An Act to place the name of Smith V. Campbell on the pension-roll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, instructed to place the name of Smith V. Campbell, late assistant surgeon of Twenty third Iowa Volunteer Infantry, on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws of the United States.


*Approved, June 20, 1888.*

# Chapter 153

> An Act granting a pension to Emma R. Johnson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-rolls, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Emma R. Johnson, minor child of William Johnson, late private in Company E, Seventh Regiment Wisconsin Volunteers.


*Approved, April 20, 1888.*

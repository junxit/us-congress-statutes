# Chapter 470

> An Act granting a pension to Emily McClure.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Emily McClure, mother of Thomas J. McClure, late first lieutenant, Company L. Seventh Regiment New York Artillery, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, June 21, 1888.*

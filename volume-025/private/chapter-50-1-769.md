# Chapter 769

> An Act granting a pension to Louise Paul.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Louise Paul, widow of Brigadier-General Gabriel R. Paul, United States Army, and to pay her a pension of one hundred dollars a month, in lieu of the pension she is now receiving.

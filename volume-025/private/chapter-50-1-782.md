# Chapter 782

> An Act granting a pension to George W. Croop.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George W. Croop, late a private of Company A, Thirty-ninth Illinois Infantry.


*Approved, August 7, 1888.*

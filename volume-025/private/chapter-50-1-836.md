# Chapter 836

> An Act granting an increase of pension to A. W. Rose.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-rolls the name of A. W. Rose, and pay him a pension of thirty dollars per month, in lieu of any pension he may now be receiving under the general law, by virtue of certificate numbered eighty thousand five hundred and ninety


*Approved, August 9, 1888.*

# Chapter 240

> An Act for the relief of the Agricultural and Mechanical College of Alabama.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to relieve the Agricultural and Mechanical College at Auburn. Alabama, from all money responsibility for so much of the ordnance and ordnance stores issued to said college under its bond dated February ninth, eighteen hundred and eighty-six, as was destroyed by fire on June twenty-fourth, eighteen hundred and eighty-seven.


*Approved, May 9, 1888.*

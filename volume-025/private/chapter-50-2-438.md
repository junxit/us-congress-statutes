# Chapter 438

> An act for the relief of the trustees of the Protestant Episcopal Theological Seminary and High School, Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be, and is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of twenty thousand dollars, to be paid to the trustees of the Protestant Episcopal Theological Seminary and High School, in Virginia, that amount having been found due them by the Court of Claims.


*Approved, March 2, 1889.*

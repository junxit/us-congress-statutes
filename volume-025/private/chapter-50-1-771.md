# Chapter 771

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Fredericka Hauser.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Fredericka Hauser, widow of John F. Hauser, late of Company H, Sixth Wisconsin Infantry.

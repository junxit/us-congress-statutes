# Chapter 441

> An Act granting a pension to Henry C. Richardson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Henry C. Richardson, late of Company K, Fifth Kentucky Volunteers.


*Approved, June 21, 1888.*

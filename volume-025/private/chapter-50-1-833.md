# Chapter 833

> An Act to place the name of Samuel Massey on the pension-roll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Samuel Massey, of Charleston, Swain County, North Carolina, on the pension-roll, and pay him the pension of a captain, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, August 9, 1888.*

# Chapter 608

> An Act granting a pension to Mary J. McGregor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary J. McGregor, widow of William McGregor, late sergeant of ordnance, United States Army.


*Approved, July 9, 1888.*

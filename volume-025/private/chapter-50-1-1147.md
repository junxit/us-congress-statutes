# Chapter 1147

> An Act for the relief of Phillip Kopplin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Phillip Kopplin, late a private in Company A, First Regiment Missouri Cavalry Volunteers.


*Approved, October 15, 1888.*

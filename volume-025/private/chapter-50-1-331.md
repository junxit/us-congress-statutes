# Chapter 331

> An Act granting a pension to William Lemons.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William Lemons, late a drummer, in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve.


*Approved, May 28, 1888.*

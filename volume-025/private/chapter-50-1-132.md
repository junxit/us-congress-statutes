# Chapter 132

> An Act granting a pension to G. W. Burkhart.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George W. Burkhart, late a sergeant of Company G. Tenth Regiment Ohio Cavalry.


*Approved, April 19, 1888.*

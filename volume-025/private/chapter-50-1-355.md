# Chapter 355

> An Act granting a pension to George W. Peavey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George W. Peavey. late first lieutenant Fifty-seventh United States Colored Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, June 4, 1888.*

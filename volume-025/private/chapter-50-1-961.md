# Chapter 961

> An Act granting a pension to the minor children of Orison S. Baldwin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll the names of the minor children of Orison S. Baldwin, deceased, late of Company G. Fourth Illinois Cavalry, and to pay them a pension at the rate prescribed by law.


*Approved, September 6, 1888.*

# Chapter 985

> An Act for the relief of Elizabeth A. South.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws of the United States, the name of Elizabeth A. South, the widow of John B. Wills, late private in Company B. Twenty-first Kentucky Volunteers in the late civil war, and be paid a pension as though she had not again married.


*Approved, September 6, 1888.*

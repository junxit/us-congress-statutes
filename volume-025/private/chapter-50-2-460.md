# Chapter 460

> An act granting an increase of pension to Jacob S. Shuman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Jacob S. Shuman, late captain of Company H. Eleventh Ohio Volunteer Cavalry, and to pay him a pension of seventy two per month instead of of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 2, 1889.*

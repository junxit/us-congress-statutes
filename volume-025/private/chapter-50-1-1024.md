# Chapter 1024

> An Act to grant a pension to William Thields.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is authorized, and hereby directed, to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William Thields, late a private of Company H, Eighth Regiment Michigan Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, September 14, 1888.*

# Chapter 1167

> An Act granting a pension to Arabella Davis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be and is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-rolls the name of Arabella Davis, widow of Lieutenant David Davis, late quartermaster of the Forty-fifth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, now deceased, subject to the limitations of the general pension laws.


*Approved, October 16, 1888.*

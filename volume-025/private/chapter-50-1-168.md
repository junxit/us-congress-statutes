# Chapter 168

> An Act for the relief of Mary M. Strong.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Mary M. Strong, widow of Thomas J. Strong, late lieutenant-colonel of the Sixteenth Regiment New York Heavy Artillery, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, and to pay her such pension as is provided by law.


*Approved, April 23, 1888.*

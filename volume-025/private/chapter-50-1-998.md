# Chapter 998

> An Act to restore Nathaniel Francis to the pension-roll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to cause to be reinstated on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Nathaniel Francis, late a private in Company D. Forty-eighth Indiana Volunteer Infantry.

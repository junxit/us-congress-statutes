# Chapter 183

> An act granting a pension to Annie May Pifer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Annie May Pifer. invalid daughter of Cornelius Pifer, late of Company F. Second Regiment Illinois Volunteer Cavalry, and pay to her legally constituted guardian, for her use and benefit, a pension of eighteen dollars per month.


*Approved, February 22, 1889.*

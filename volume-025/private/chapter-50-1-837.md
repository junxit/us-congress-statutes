# Chapter 837

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Charles Jewett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws, the name of Charles Jewett, late a private in Company I, Third New Hampshire Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, August 9, 1888.*

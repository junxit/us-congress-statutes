# Chapter 460

> An Act granting a pension to John Glenning.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of John Glenning, late private Company B, Tenth Regiment Tennessee Volunteers, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, June 21, 1888.*

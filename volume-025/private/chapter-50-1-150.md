# Chapter 150

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Delilah Agard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Delilah Agard, widow of George Agard, late a private in Company B, One hundred and fourteenth Regiment New York Infantry Volunteers,


*Approved, April 20, 1888.*

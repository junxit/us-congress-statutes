# Chapter 854

> An Act granting a pension to Myron Teachout.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is. authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Myron Teachout, late of Company G. One hundred and twenty-third Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, subject to the limitations of the general pension laws.


*Approved, August 9, 1888.*

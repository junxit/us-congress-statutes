# Chapter 851

> An Act granting a pension to Sarah Riddle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah Riddle, mother of James H. Riddle, late a private of Company J. First Regiment of Kentucky Volunteer Cavalry.


*Approved, August 9, 1888.*

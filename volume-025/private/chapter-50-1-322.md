# Chapter 322

> An Act to grant an increase of pension to Frederick Beno.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to increase the pension of Frederick Beno, of East Portland, Oregon, to seventy-two dollars per month.


*Approved, May 28, 1888.*

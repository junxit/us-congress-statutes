# Chapter 977

> An Act to increase the pension of W. B. Stokes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of tin United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to in-crease the pension of William B. Stokes, late a colonel of the Fifth Tennessee Cavalry, to fifty dollars per month, commencing from the passage of this act.


*Approved, September 6, 1888.*

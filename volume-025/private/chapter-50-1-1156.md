# Chapter 1156

> An Act to place the name of Cyrus Millins, of Ogden Centre, Michigan, on the pension-roll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place the name of Cyrus Millins, of Ogden Centre. Michigan, late a private of Company B. Fourth Michigan Infantry Volunteers, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws:


*Approved, October 15, 1888.*

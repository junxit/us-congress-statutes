# Chapter 516

> An Act granting a pension to George W. De Motte.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George W. De Motte, late of Company K, Fifty-third Illinois Volunteers.


*Approved, July 2, 1888.*

# Chapter 1175

> An Act granting a pension to Nancy J. Cotner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Nancy J. Cotner, dependent sister of Thomas Cotner, late private Company C, Thirteenth Regiment Indiana. Volunteers, at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, October 16, 1888.*

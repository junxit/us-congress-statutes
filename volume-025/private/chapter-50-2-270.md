# Chapter 270

> An act granting an increase of pension to Stephen L. Kearney.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slides of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Stephen L. Kearney, late a first lieutenant of the Third Regiment of Colored Troops in the service of the United States, on the pension-roll of the United States at the rate of twelve dollars and twenty-five cents per month, in lieu of the pension of eight dollars per month he is now receiving under certificate numbered thirteen thousand and twenty-six as a Mexican veteran under act approved January twenty-ninth, eighteen hundred and eighty-seven. This act shall be in force from its passage.


*Approved, February 25, 1889.*

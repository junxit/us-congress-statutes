# Chapter 259

> An act granting a pension to Mary Gray.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slides of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Mary Gray, mother of Charles R. Gray, deceased, late a private in Company I, Fifty-first Regiment New York Veteran Volunteers, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 25, 1889.*

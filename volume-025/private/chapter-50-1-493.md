# Chapter 493

> An Act to place on the pension-roll the name of Salina A. Sims, widow of William S. Sims.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension-roll subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws the name of Salina A. Sims, widow of William S. Sims, late captain of Company F, Eighty-sixth Regiment Indiana Volunteers.


*Approved, June 25, 1888.*

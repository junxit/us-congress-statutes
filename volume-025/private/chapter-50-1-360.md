# Chapter 360

> An Act granting a pension to Abial G. Chamberlain.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions of the pension laws, the name Abial G. Chamberlain, late Company K. First Massachusetts Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of forty-five dollars per month, in lieu of the pension now received by him.


*Approved, June 4, 1888.*

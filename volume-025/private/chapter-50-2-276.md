# Chapter 276

> An act granting a pension to Christian Sanders.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slides of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of’ Christian Sanders, late a private of Company D, Seventy-ninth Regiment Enrolled Missouri Militia, subject to the limitations and regulations of the pension laws.


*Received by the President February 13, 1889.*

# Chapter 1157

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Daniel Willborg.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Daniel Willborg, late of Company C, Forty-third Illinois Volunteers, from four dollars per month to sixteen dollars per month, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, October 15, 1888.*

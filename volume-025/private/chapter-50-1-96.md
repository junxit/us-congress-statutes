# Chapter 96

> An Act granting a pension to Juliette Stone.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Juliette Stone, and to pay her a pension as though she were the natural mother of Eliphas T. Stone, late of the United States Navy.


*Approved, April 13, 1888.*

# Chapter 179

> An act to increase the pension of Mary A. Bedel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Mary A. Bedel, widow of the late Brigadier-General John Bedel, from thirty to fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 21, 1889.*

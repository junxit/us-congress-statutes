# Chapter 134

> An Act granting a pension to Eliza J. Currier.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Eliza J. Currier, widow of Joseph H. Currier, late a private of Company B, Third Regiment New Hampshire Volunteers, at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, April 19, 1888.*

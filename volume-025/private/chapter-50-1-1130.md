# Chapter 1130

> An Act granting a pension to Ransom Riley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Ransom Riley, late private of Company G, Fifth Regiment Kentucky Volunteer Cavalry, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, October 15, 1888.*

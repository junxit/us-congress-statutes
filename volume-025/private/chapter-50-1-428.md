# Chapter 428

> An Act to place the name of Jacob Behr on the pension-roll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jacob Behr, late of Company B, Second United States Reserve Corps, Missouri.


*Approved, June 20, 1888.*

# Chapter 211

> An act granting a pension to Elias J. Kenaday.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Elias J. Kenaday, in Captain James H. Morgan’s company of Iowa Mounted Volunteers in the war with Mexico, and pay him a pension of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 23, 1889.*

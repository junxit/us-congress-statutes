# Chapter 38

> An act for the relief of Louisa McLain.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations or the pension laws, the name of Louisa McLain, dependent mother of John S. McLain, as stated in application numbered two hundred and sixty thousand one hundred and forty-two in the Pension Office.


*Approved, January 15, 1889.*

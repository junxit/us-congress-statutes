# Chapter 949

> An Act for the removal of the political disabilities of Gustavus W. Smith.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each, House concurring therein),*

That the legal and political disabilities imposed by the Fourteenth Amendment of the Constitution of the United States by reason of participation in the late rebellion, be, and they are, hereby removed from Gustavus W. Smith, now a resident of the State of New York.


*Approved, September 6, 1888.*

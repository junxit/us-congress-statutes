# Chapter 809

> An Act granting a pension to William C. Lord.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and is hereby, directed to place on the pension-rolls the name of William C. Lord, of Portland, Maine, at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, August 8, 1888.*

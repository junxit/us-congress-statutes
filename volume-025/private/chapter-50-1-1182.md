# Chapter 1182

> An Act to increase the pension of Isaac Hurd.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to increase the pension of Isaac Hurd, a veteran of the war of eighteen hundred and twelve, Vermont Militia, from eight dollars per month, the amount now allowed him, to thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, October 16, 1888.*

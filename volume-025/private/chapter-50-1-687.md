# Chapter 687

> An Act granting a pension to Carrie V. Miller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Carrie V. Miller, a volunteer nurse in the late war, and pay her at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, July 19, 1888.*

# Chapter 997

> An Act granting a pension to Joseph Hunter, M. D.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the. United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Joseph Hunter. M. D. late of Company F. One hundred and twenty-sixth Regiment Illinois Volunteers.

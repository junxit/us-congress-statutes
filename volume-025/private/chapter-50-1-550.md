# Chapter 550

> An Act granting a pension to Robert McClean.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Robert McClean, late a private in Company F, Second Illinois Cavalry Regiment.


*Approved, July 5, 1888.*

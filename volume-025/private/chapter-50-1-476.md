# Chapter 476

> An Act granting a pension to James H. Darling.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of James H. Darling, late of Company G Seventy-third Regiment Ohio Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, June 22, 1888.*

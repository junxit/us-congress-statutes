# Chapter 1171

> An Act granting a pension to Jenny Buell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll the name of Jenny Buell, widow of Henry C. Buell, late a private in Company B, Fourteenth Regiment New York State Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, October 16, 1888.*

# Chapter 459

> An act granting an increase of pension to Frank Wolford.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Frank Wolford, late colonel of the First Kentucky Volunteer Cavalry, and grant him a pension of fifty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 2, 1889.*

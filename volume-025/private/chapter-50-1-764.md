# Chapter 764

> An Act granting a pension to Mercy A. Cutts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Mercy A. Cutts, foster mother of Enoch F. Cutts, deceased, late of Company A, Thirty-first Maine Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, August 6, 1888.*

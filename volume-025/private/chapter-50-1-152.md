# Chapter 152

> An Act for the relief of R. S. Stanley, postmaster at Booneville, Mississippi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of eighty-eight dollars be paid to R. S. Stanley, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, on account of money sent from the post-office at Booneville. Mississippi, in a registered-package envelope, to the postmaster at Saint Louis, Missouri, and which was burned en route from Booneville. Mississippi, to Saint Louis, Missouri.


*Approved, April 20, 1888.*

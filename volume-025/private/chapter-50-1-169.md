# Chapter 169

> An Act for the relief of Alonzo Alden.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is. authorized and directed to increase the pension of Alonzo Alden, late colonel of the One hundred and sixty-ninth Regiment New York Volunteers and brevet brigadier-general, from fifty dollars to seventy-two dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, April 23, 1888.*

# Private Resolution 3

> Joint resolution for the removal of all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States upon Abram C. Myers.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all the political disabilities imposed by the third section of the fourteenth article of the Constitution of the United States upon Abram C. Myers, be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, February 8, 1888.*

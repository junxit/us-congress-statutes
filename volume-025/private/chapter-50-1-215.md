# Chapter 215

> An Act granting a pension to Henry B. Very.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Henry B. Very, late a private in Company A. Twelfth Rhode Island Volunteers.


*Approved, May 1, 1888.*

# Chapter 1085

> An Act granting a pension to William E. Taylor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is. authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William E. Taylor, of Missouri, late a member of Company B. Sixtieth Missouri Enrolled Militia.


*Approved, October 9, 1888.*

# Chapter 911

> An Act granting an increase of pension to William Collinsworth.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William Collinsworth, late a private in Company G, One hundred and eleventh Regiment Illinois Infantry Volunteers, and to pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars a month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, August 21, 1888.*

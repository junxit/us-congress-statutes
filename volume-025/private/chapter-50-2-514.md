# Chapter 514

> An act granting a pension to Mrs. Helen Sutherland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby directed to put on the pension-roll the name of Mrs. Helen Sutherland, dependent mother of Andrew Sutherland, late private Company H, First Regiment Michigan Light Artillery.


*Approved, March 2, 1889.*

# Chapter 485

> An Act granting a pension to Jessie M. Barthauer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, to place upon the pension rolls, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jessie M. Barthauer, widow of William C. Barthauer, late private of Company A, One hundredth Regiment New York Volunteers.

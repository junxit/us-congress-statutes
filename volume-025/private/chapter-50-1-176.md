# Chapter 176

> An Act granting a pension to Laura D. Pierce.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Laura D. Pierce, widow of Artemus Pierce of Company E. Fifteenth Vermont Volunteers.


*Approved, April 23, 1888.*

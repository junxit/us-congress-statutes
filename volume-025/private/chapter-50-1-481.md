# Chapter 481

> An Act granting a pension to Warren Ohaver.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Warren Ohaver, late of Company A, Fifty-third Regiment Indiana Infantry.

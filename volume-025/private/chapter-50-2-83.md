# Chapter 83

> An act to increase the pension of Joseph Holmes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll the name of Joseph Holmes, of Company E. Third Regiment Vermont Volunteers, at the rate of forty-five dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, January 17, 1889.*

# Chapter 32

> An act granting a pension to Martha Giddings, formerly Martha Priest.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be authorized and directed to put upon the pension-roll the name of Martha Giddings, formerly Martha Priest, widow of Nathaniel Priest, of Company H, Fourteenth Vermont Volunteers.


*Approved, January 15, 1889.*

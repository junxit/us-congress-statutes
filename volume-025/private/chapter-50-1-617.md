# Chapter 617

> An Act granting a pension to Philomelia L. Dartt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Philomelia L. Dartt, widow of Josiah R. Dartt, late captain of Company A, Fifth Regiment Minnesota Volunteers.

# Chapter 1084

> An Act granting a pension to John B. Ross.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John B. Ross, late of Company F, Thirteenth Regiment Kansas Volunteers.


*Approved, October 9, 1888.*

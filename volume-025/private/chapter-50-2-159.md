# Chapter 159

> An act granting an increase of pension to Eliza J. Alexander.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Eliza J. Alexander, widow of Francis Alexander, formerly lieutenant in United States Navy, from twenty-five dollars to fifty dollars per month, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 13, 1889.*

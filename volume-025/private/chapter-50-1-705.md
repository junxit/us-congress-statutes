# Chapter 705

> An Act granting a pension to Keziah E. Strong.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Keziah E. Strong, widow of the late David Strong, a private of Company F. Fifth Regiment of Maine Volunteers.


*Approved, July 24, 1888.*

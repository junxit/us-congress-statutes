# Chapter 353

> An act to amend an act entitled “An act for the relief of the widow and orphan children of Colonel William R, McKee, late of Lexington, Kentucky.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of the General Land Office, to carry into effect the grant of one-quarter section each to the orphan children of Colonel William R. McKee, made in the second section of said act, be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to issue to the surviving children and grand children of said McKee, or the owners and holders thereof, other certificates for those they now hold, issued by authority of said act, which new certificates they may enter and locate for themselves upon any lands in satisfaction of said grant of the class described in the act to which this is an amendment.


*Approved, March 1, 1889.*

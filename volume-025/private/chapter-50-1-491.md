# Chapter 491

> An Act for the relief of Mrs. Alice Croarkin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Alice Croarkin, widow of James Croarkin, late of Company C, Third Wisconsin Cavalry.


*Approved, June 25, 1888.*

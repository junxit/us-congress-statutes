# Chapter 864

> An Act granting a pension to William A. Humes.

*Be it enacted try the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William A. Humes, late a private of Company I, Thirteenth Regiment of Kentucky Volunteer Cavalry.

# Chapter 267

> An act granting a pension to Ester Gaven.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slides of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll the name of Ester Gaven, mother of Bernard Gaven, late private in Company D. Seventy-first Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 25, 1889.*

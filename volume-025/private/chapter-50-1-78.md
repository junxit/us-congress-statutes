# Chapter 78

> An Act for the relief of Joel J. Goss.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all the right, title, and interest of the United States in and to the following described real estate, to wit: Five lots of land containing forty acres each and known, designated, and numbered two hundred and seventy-eight, two hundred and seventy-nine, two hundred and eighty, two hundred and ninety-eight, and two hundred and ninety-nine, respectively, situate, lying, and being in the eighteenth district of the county of Polk and State of Georgia, be, and the same are hereby, released, quit claim granted, and conveyed to Joel J. Goss, of said county of Polk, his heirs and assigns forever.


*Approved, April 10, 1888.*

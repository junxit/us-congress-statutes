# Chapter 264

> An Act granting a pension to Catharine Black.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Catharine Black, widow of Patrick W. Black, deceased, late a first lieutenant in Company F. Ninth Massachusetts Volunteers, also captain of Company B, Twenty-eighth Massachusetts Volunteers.


*Approved, May 17, 1888.*

# Chapter 684

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Catharine K. Whittlesey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Catharine K. Whittlesey, widow of the late Major J. H. Whittlesey. United States Army, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month in lieu of the pension she is now receiving.


*Approved, July 19, 1888.*

# Chapter 74

> An act granting a pension to Milton Wallen

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of Interior be. and he is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension roll subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Milton Wallen of Clinton County, Kentucky, who was a member of Captain Brooks’s company of Kentucky State troops, and grant him a pension at the rate given other soldiers similarly disabled


*Approved, January 17, 1889.*

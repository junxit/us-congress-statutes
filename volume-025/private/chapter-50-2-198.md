# Chapter 198

> An act to place the name of James H. Tolly on the pension-roll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of James H. Tolly, late private Company L. Twelfth Tennessee Cavalry, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 22, 1889.*

# Chapter 538

> An Act granting a pension to Ruth Ann Porter.

*Be it enacted by the. Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Ruth Ann Porter, dependent sister of George H. Porter, late a private in Company D, Third Regiment of Indiana Volunteer Cavalry, on the pension-roll, and pay tier a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, July 4, 1888.*

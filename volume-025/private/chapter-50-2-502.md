# Chapter 502

> An act granting a pension to Frank M. Martin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws of the United States, the name of Frank M. Martin, ate of Company H. Sixth Regiment Kentucky Cavalry.


*Approved, March 2, 1889.*

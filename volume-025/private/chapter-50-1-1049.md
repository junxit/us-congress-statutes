# Chapter 1049

> An Act granting a pension to Mary Sturgess.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Sturgess, a volunteer nurse in the late war, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, September 26, 1888.*

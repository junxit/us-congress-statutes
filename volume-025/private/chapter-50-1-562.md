# Chapter 562

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Thomas Benson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Thomas Benson, late a private in Company A. Thirty-second Regiment Maine Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty-five dollars per month, in lieu of the amount he is now receiving.


*Approved, July 5, 1888.*

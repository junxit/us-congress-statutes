# Chapter 1104

> An Act granting a pension to Stephen Butler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Stephen Butler, late a private in Company B, Second Nebraska Cavalry.


*Approved, October 12, 1888.*

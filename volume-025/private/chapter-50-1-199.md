# Chapter 199

> An Act to restore to the pension-roll the name of Isaac Dilley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to restore to the pension-roll the name of Isaac Dilley, late of Company H, One hundred and thirtieth Indiana Infantry Volunteers.


*Received by the President, April 12, 1888.*

# Chapter 269

> An act for the relief of Benjamine E. Snyder.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slides of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Benjamine E. Snyder, late of Company L, Fifth Regiment New York Heavy Artillery.


*Approved, February 25, 1889.*

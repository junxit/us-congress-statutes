# Chapter 435

> An Act to increase the pension of Albert E. Magoffin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized to increase the pension of Albert E. Magoffin, late a member of the Eighty-ninth Ohio Veteran Volunteer Infantry, on the pension-roll, so as to include injury to leg, as set forth in original application, said increase of pension to begin from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 20, 1888.*

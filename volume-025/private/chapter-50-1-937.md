# Chapter 937

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Abraham J. Buckles.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, instructed to place on the pension-roll the name of Jane Smallridge, of Fairfield. Iowa, widow of John Smallridge, late a private of Company F, Third Regiment Iowa Cavalry, and pay her a pension of twelve dollars per month, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, September 1, 1888.*

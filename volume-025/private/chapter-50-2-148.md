# Chapter 148

> An act granting a pension to James Miller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of James Miller, of the city of Reading, Pennsylvania, late a private in Company B, of the Two hundred and fifth Regiment of Pennsylvania Volunteers.


*Received by the President January 31, 1889.*

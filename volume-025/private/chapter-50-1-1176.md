# Chapter 1176

> An Act granting a pension to Margaret Gray.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Margaret Gray, mother of Wilson S. Gray, deceased, late of Company G, Eleventh Illinois Cavalry.


*Approved, October 16, 1888.*

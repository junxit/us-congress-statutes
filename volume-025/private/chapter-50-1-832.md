# Chapter 832

> An Act to place the name of John J. Mitchell on the pension-roll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John J. Mitchell, late of Company A. One hundred and tenth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, August 9, 1888.*

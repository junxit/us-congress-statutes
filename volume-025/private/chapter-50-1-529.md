# Chapter 529

> An Act granting a pension to Samson M. David.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Samson M. David, late a private of Company E, Thirty-first Regiment Missouri Enrolled Militia.


*Approved, July 4, 1888.*

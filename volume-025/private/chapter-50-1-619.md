# Chapter 619

> An Act to place the name of Rev. Stephen M. Collis on the muster-roll of the Thirteenth Tennessee Cavalry as chaplain thereof.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Stephen M. Collis on the muster-roll of the Thirteenth Tennessee Cavalry as chaplain thereof, and that he be mustered from the first day of April, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, and mustered out on the first day of September, eighteen hundred and sixty-five.

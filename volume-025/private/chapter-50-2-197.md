# Chapter 197

> An act granting a pension to Rebecca D. Vedder.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Rebecca D. Vedder, stepmother of Edwin H. Vedder. late of Company A, Third Regiment New York Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 22, 1889.*

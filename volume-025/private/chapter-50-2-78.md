# Chapter 78

> An act granting a pension to Mrs Almira J. Towner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Almira J. Towner, dependent mother of Seth S. Towner, late a private in Company A, Second Regiment Illinois Volunteer Cavalry.


*Approved, January 17, 1889.*

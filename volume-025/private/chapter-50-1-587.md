# Chapter 587

> An Act for the relief of William M. Dayton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William M. Dayton, late second lieutenant in Company I, Eighteenth Kentucky Volunteers.

# Chapter 1079

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Eleanor B. Goodfellow.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Eleanor B. Goodfellow, widow of Henry Goodfellow, late major and judge-advocate-general, United States Army, and pay her at the rate of fifty dollars per month, in lieu of that which she is now receiving.


*Approved, October 9, 1888.*

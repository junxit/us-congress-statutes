# Chapter 480

> An Act granting a pension to Linnaeus W. Risley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Linnaeus W. Risley, late of Company D, Eighteenth Indiana Volunteers.

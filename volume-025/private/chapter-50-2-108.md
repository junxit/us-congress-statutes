# Chapter 108

> An act to increase the pension of Seth F. Myers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Seth F. Myers, late surgeon of the Seventy-third Regiment of Indiana Volunteers, from thirty dollars to fifty dollars a month, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, January 30, 1889.*

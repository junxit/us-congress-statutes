# Chapter 865

> An Act for the relief of Thomas W. Lord.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be, and is hereby, authorized to nominate, and by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, to appoint first Lieutenant Thomas W. Lord, United States Army, retired, a captain on the retired-list of the Army, with the retired pay of that grade from the date of such appointment.

# Chapter 246

> An act granting an increase of pension to Thomas Wynne.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Thomas Wynne, late a private of Company C, Second Regiment United States Infantry, and to pay him a pension of thirty-five dollars a month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 25, 1889.*

# Chapter 703

> An Act granting a pension to Sarah F. Jones.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah F. Jones, widow of Albert L. Jones, late of Company B. Sixth Maine Volunteers, deceased.


*Approved, July 24, 1888.*

# Chapter 67

> An Act for the relief of Frank Baker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the Treasury be, and are hereby, authorized and directed to credit Captain Frank Baker, Ordnance Department United States Army, with one hundred and seventy-two dollars and fifty-two cents, being a sum of money stolen from him while acting commissary of subsistence at Fort Fred. Steele, Wyoming Territory.


*Approved, April 5, 1888.*

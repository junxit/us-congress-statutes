# Chapter 330

> An Act granting a pension to Lemuel R. Rea.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lemuel R. Rea, late a private in Company I, Second Illinois Cavalry.


*Approved, May 28, 1888.*

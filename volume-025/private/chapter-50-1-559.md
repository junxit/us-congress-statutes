# Chapter 559

> An Act granting a pension to William P. Gordon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, at the rate of eighteen dollars per month, the name of William P. Gordon, dependent invalid son of William H. Gordon, late a member of Company A, Eighty-fourth Indiana Volunteer Infantry, deceased.


*Approved, July 5, 1888.*

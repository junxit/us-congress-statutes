# Chapter 283

> An Act granting a pension to Noah S. Cramer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Noah S. Cramer, late of the United States Navy.


*Approved, May 18, 1888.*

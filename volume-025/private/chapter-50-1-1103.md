# Chapter 1103

> An Act granting a pension to Martha V. Coleman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Martha V. Coleman, a volunteer nurse in the late war, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars a month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, October 12, 1888.*

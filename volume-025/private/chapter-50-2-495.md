# Chapter 495

> An act granting a pension to Frances H. L. Prescott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Frances H. L. Prescott, daughter of the late Captain George W. Prescott, United States Army, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, March 2, 1889.*

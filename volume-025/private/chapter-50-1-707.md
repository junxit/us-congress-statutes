# Chapter 707

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Mary McGee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Mary McGee, widow of Thomas McGee, late private Company I and Company B, Thirteenth Regiment Connecticut Volunteers.


*Approved, July 24, 1888.*

# Chapter 704

> An Act granting a pension to Mary J. Davis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary J. Davis, dependent mother of the late Lorenzo Crittenden, an enlisted man in the naval service.


*Approved, July 24, 1888.*

# Chapter 291

> An act granting a pension to John Fagan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slides of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Fagan, late of Company H, Seventh Regiment Pennsylvania Reserves.


*Approved, February 26, 1889.*

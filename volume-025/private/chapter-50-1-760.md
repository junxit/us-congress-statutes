# Chapter 760

> An Act granting a pension to Louisa Provost.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Louisa Provost, widow of Peter Provost, late a soldier in Company B, Seventeenth Vermont.


*Approved, August 6, 1888.*

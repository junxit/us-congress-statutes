# Chapter 1174

> An Act granting a pension to Jane E. Knoble.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll the name of Jane E. Knoble. widow of Samuel Knoble, late a private in the Eighty-first Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, October 16, 1888.*

# Chapter 976

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Richard Hogan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-rolls the name of Richard Hogan, a private of Company B, First Regiment of United States Dragoons, in the war with Mexico, and grant him a pension of thirty dollars a month from and after the passage of this act, in lieu of the pension he now receives.


*Approved, September 6, 1888.*

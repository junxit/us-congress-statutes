# Chapter 46

> An act increasing the pension of Miss Sarah Mary Carroll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Miss Sarah Mary Carroll, late an Army nurse, from twelve dollars and fifty cents to twenty-five dollars a month, to date from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, January 15, 1889.*

# Chapter 853

> An Act granting a pension to Moses T. Coffey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Moses T. Coffey, late a private in Company H, One hundred and forty-eighth Regiment Indiana Volunteers.

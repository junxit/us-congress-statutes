# Chapter 337

> An act granting a pension to James M. Frost.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of James M. Frost, late a member of Company A, First Regiment Tennessee Cavalry Volunteers.


*Approved, March 1, 1889.*

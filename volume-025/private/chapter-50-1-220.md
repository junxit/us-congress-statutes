# Chapter 220

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Mrs. Emily M. Wyman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Emily M. Wyman, widow of the late Rear-Admiral Robert H. Wyman. United States Navy, and pay her a pension, from and after the passage of this act, at the rate of fifty dollars per month, in lieu of her present pension.


*Approved, May 1, 1888.*

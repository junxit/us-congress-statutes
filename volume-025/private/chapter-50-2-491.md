# Chapter 491

> An act granting increase of pension to Joseph B. Sellers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Joseph B. Sellers, late of Company I, Twenty-seventh Indiana Volunteers, and pay him at the rate of forty-five dollars per month, in lieu of that which he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 2, 1889.*

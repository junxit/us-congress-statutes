# Chapter 86

> An act granting a pension to Aaron R. Gilkison.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized to place on the pension-rolls the name of Aaron R. Gilkison, late of Company H. Fifteenth Regiment Ohio Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Received by the President January 7, 1889.*

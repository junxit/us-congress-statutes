# Chapter 1168

> An Act granting a pension to Tempy M. Johnston.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Tempy M. Johnston, widow of John Johnston, late sergeant Company B, Eleventh Regiment Kentucky Volunteers.


*Approved, October 16, 1888.*

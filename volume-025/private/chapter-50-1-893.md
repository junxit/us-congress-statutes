# Chapter 893

> An Act to remove the political disabilities of Elliott Johnston.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That the political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States, by reason of participation in the late rebellion, be, and they hereby are, removed from Elliott Johnston, of Accomac county, in the State of Virginia.


*Approved, August 14, 1888.*

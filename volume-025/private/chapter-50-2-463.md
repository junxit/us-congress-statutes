# Chapter 463

> An act to increase the pension of Elizabeth L. Snell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Mrs. Elizabeth L. Snell, dependent mother of the late Alfred T. Snell, a commander in the Navy, to forty dollars per month, instead of twenty-five dollars per month, the amount which she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 2, 1889.*

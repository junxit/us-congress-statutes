# Chapter 146

> An act to relinquish the interest of the United States in certain lands in Kansas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all the interest of the United States in and to the south half of the northeast quarter and the north half of the southeast quarter of section six, township six south, of range eighteen west, of the sixth principal meridian, in Rooks County, Kansas, is hereby relinquished to Elmore S. Stroup.


*Approved, February 12, 1889.*

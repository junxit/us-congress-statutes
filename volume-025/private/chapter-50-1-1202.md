# Chapter 1202

> An Act granting a pension to Martha J. Cole.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Martha J. Cole, mother of Robert E. Horner, late a second lieutenant in the Twenty-seventh Regiment Wisconsin Volunteers.


*Approved, October 18, 1888.*

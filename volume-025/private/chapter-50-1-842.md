# Chapter 842

> An Act for the relief of Coburn D. Outten.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension allowed to Coburn D. Outten be increased to thirty dollars per month, to commence upon the passage of this act.


*Approved, August 9, 1888.*

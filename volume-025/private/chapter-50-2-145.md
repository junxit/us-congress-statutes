# Chapter 145

> An act granting a pension to Reuben Ash.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Reuben Ash. late first lieutenant of Company E, Second Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of forty-five dollars a month, in lieu of the pension lie is now receiving.


*Approved, February 12, 1889.*

# Chapter 190

> An Act for the relief of Cynthia J. Leroy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll the name Cynthia J. Leroy, the invalid daughter of Abraham Leroy, late a private of Company B. Sixth Regiment Illinois Volunteers, and pay said daughter a pension of eighteen dollars per month for and during her natural life.


*Approved, April 23, 1888.*

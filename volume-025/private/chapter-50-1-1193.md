# Chapter 1193

> An Act granting a pension to Mighill H. Patten.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is. authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll the name of Mighill H. Patten, late of Captain Huxford’s company, First Regiment Maine State Militia, and now a resident of Waterford. Minnesota, and pay him a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.

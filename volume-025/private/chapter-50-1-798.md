# Chapter 798

> An Act for the relief of C. L. Wilson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster-General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the account of C. L. Wilson, postmaster at Meilford, Ellis County. Texas, with the sum of fifty dollars, for money lost in transit.


*Approved, August 8, 1888.*

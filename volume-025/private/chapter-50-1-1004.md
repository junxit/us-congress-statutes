# Chapter 1004

> An Act granting a pension to Mary M. Shattuck.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary M. Shattuck, widow of Merritt Shattuck, late a private in Company H, Thirty-fifth Regiment Iowa Infantry.


*Approved, September 10, 1888.*

# Chapter 965

> An Act granting a pension to Susan Singleton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Susan Singleton, dependent mother of Dudley P. Singleton, late a private in Company H. Fifty-ninth Indiana Volunteers.


*Approved, September 6, 1888.*

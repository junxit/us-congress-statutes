# Chapter 955

> An Act granting a pension to Samuel Piercy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to all the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Samuel Piercy, who served in Company E, Ninth Kentucky Volunteer Infantry, in the late civil war.


*Approved, September 6, 1888.*

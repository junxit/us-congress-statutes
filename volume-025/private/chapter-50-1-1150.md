# Chapter 1150

> An Act for the relief of Thomas Shackelford.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, at the rate of eight dollars per month, the name of Thomas Shackelford, late of the Eleventh United States Infantry of the Mexican war.


*Approved, October 15, 1888.*

# Chapter 402

> An Act act for the relief of George E. Oliphant.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of George E. Oliphant, late a private in Company I, Seventeenth Regiment of Indiana Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, June 18, 1888.*

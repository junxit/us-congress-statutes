# Chapter 516

> An act granting a pension to W. W. Wilson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of W. W. Wilson, late in Company H, Eighteenth Regiment Missouri Volunteers, with the same rate of pension to which a private soldier would be entitled for like disabilities.


*Approved, March 2, 1889.*

# Chapter 130

> An act granting a pension to Rebecca H. Lyon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Rebecca H. Lyon, a nurse in the late war, and pay her a pension of twenty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, February 11, 1889.*

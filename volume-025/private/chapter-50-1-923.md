# Chapter 923

> An Act for the relief of Susan E. Alger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of Pensions be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Susan E. Alger, mother of Warren A. Alger, late of Company D, Fifteenth Regiment Massachusetts Volunteers.


*Approved, August 27, 1888.*

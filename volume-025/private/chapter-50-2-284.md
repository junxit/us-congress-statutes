# Chapter 284

> An act granting a pension to Nancy Hamilton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slides of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to restore to the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Nancy Hamilton, widow of David Hamilton, late of Company K, Fourteenth Regiment of Kentucky Volunteers.


*Approved, February 26, 1889.*

# Chapter 427

> An act for the relief of the legal representatives of H. Corths, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the claim of the legal representatives of H. Corths, deceased, and late of Ballard County, State of Kentucky, for quartermaster’s stores and commissary supplies, alleged to have been taken and used by the United States Army during the late war, from the farm of said Corths, in Ballard County, Kentucky, be referred to the Quartermaster-General. who shall investigate the justice and legality of said claim and report the amount and value of said stores and supplies taken and used by the Army of the United States.; and that the quartermaster general also report upon the question of loyalty of said claimants and that he report all the facts and evidence for the consideration of Congress.


*Approved, March 2, 1889.*

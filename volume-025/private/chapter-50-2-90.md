# Chapter 90

> An act granting a pension to Samuel J. Wright.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and lie is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Samuel J. Wright, father of Darwin Wright, deceased, late of Company A, Second Regiment of New York Cavalry Volunteers.


*Received by the President January 7, 1889.*

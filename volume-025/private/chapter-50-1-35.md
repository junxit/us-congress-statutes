# Chapter 35

> An Act increasing the pension of John C. Johnston.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension of John C. Johnston, certificate numbered forty-five thousand three hundred and three, as private Company A. Sixty-first Pennsylvania Infantry Volunteers, be increased to thirty-six dollars per month, the same to be in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 23, 1888.*

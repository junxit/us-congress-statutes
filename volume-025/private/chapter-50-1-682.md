# Chapter 682

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Virginia Grier.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Virginia Grier, widow of William N. Grier, late Brevet Brigadier-General and Colonel of the United States Army; the pension of the said Mrs. Virginia Grier to be at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, July 19, 1888.*

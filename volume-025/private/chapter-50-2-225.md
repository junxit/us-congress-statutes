# Chapter 225

> An act granting a pension to George W. Dickinson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of George W. Dickinson, late a private of Company E, Thirteenth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 23, 1889.*

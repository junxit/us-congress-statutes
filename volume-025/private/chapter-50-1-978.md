# Chapter 978

> An Act to increase the pension of Charles Ritchey

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the invalid pension-roll of the United States the name of Charles Ritchey, late a sergeant of Company K. Thirty-ninth Indiana Infantry Volunteers, at the increased rate of forty dollars per month, for the loss of both lower limbs.


*Approved, September 6, 1888.*

# Chapter 510

> An act granting a pension to John Limeric.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Limeric, late of the Sixth Ohio Battery.


*Approved, March 2, 1889.*

# Chapter 880

> An Act granting a pension to James W. Bowman

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of James W. Bowman, late a corporal in Company C, Seventh Regiment Tennessee Volunteers.


*Approved, August 13, 1888.*

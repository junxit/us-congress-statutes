# Chapter 808

> An Act granting a pension to John Taaffe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Taaffe, late a member of Company F, Sixth Kentucky Infantry, and pay him a pension of eight dollars a month.


*Approved, August 8, 1888.*

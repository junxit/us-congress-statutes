# Chapter 805

> An Act granting a pension to George E. Wells.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of George E. Wells, late a member of the Sixth Ohio Independent Battery, be placed on the pension-roll of the United States, by the Secretary of the Interior, and that he be paid a pension of eight dollars per month from the date of the passage and approval of this act.


*Approved, August 8, 1888.*

# Chapter 576

> An Act granting a pension to Franklin Long.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Franklin Long, of Havana, Kansas, late of Company A, Sixty-first Illinois Volunteers.

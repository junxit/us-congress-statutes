# Chapter 1100

> An Act granting a pension to Samuel Lane.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Samuel Lane, late of Company G, Fifty-fifth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers.


*Approved, October 12, 1888.*

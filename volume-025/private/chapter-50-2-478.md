# Chapter 478

> An act for relief of Joseph Lawless.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Joseph Lawless, late private Company I, Fifth Kentucky Cavalry Volunteers, on the pension-rolls, subject to all the restrictions, limitations, and provisions of the pension laws of the United States.


*Approved, March 2, 1889.*

# Chapter 972

> An Act granting a pension to Philip Neuman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed place on the pension-roll, subject, to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Philip Neuman, late of Company H, Eighth Kentucky Cavalry.


*Approved, September 6, 1888.*

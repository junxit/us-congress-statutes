# Chapter 71

> An act granting a pension to Jane Jackson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Jane Jackson, widow of Nathan Jackson, late a private of Company B, Fifth Regiment of Kentucky Volunteer Cavalry, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, January 17, 1889.*

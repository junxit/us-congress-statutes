# Chapter 229

> An Act for the relief of Horatio R. Maryman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Horatio R. Maryman, of the District of Columbia, father of Richard A. Maryman, late of Company B, Thirteenth Regiment New York Volunteers.


*Approved, May 7, 1888.*

# Chapter 604

> An Act granting a pension to Charles Tidmarsh.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Charles Tidmarsh, late of Company C, First Kentucky Regiment Frankfort Battalion Capital Guards.


*Approved, July 9, 1888.*

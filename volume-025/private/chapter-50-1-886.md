# Chapter 886

> An Act granting a pension to Eliza M. Scandlin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Eliza M. Scandlin, widow of William G. Scandlin, late a chaplain in the Fifteenth Regiment, Massachusetts volunteer Militia, at the rate of twelve dollars a month.


*Approved, August 13, 1888.*

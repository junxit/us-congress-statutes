# Chapter 761

> An Act granting a pension to Gustave E. Peters.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Gustave E. Peters, late of Company K, Twenty-ninth Michigan Volunteers.


*Approved, August 6, 1888.*

# Chapter 964

> An Act granting a pension to Eliza A. Woods.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to inscribe on the pension-rolls the name of Eliza A. Woods, widow of Richard Woods, who was an ensign and lieutenant in Captain Jones’s company, Colonel Anderson’s regiment of United States Infantry, war of eighteen hundred and twelve, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, September 6, 1888.*

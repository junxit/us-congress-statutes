# Chapter 984

> An Act for the relief of John D. Munnerlyn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of one thousand four hundred and ninety-six dollars and twenty-one cents be, and the same is hereby, appropriated for the payment of the salary of John D. Munnerlyn, of the county of Burke. State of Georgia, for services as assistant assessor of internal revenue of the United States from December, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, to September, eighteen hundred and sixty-six, and for which he has never received the pay allowed by law for such services.


*Approved, September 6, 1888.*

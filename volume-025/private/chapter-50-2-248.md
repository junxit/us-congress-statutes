# Chapter 248

> An act granting a pension to John Ebert.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Ebert, late a private in Company A, One hundred and fourteenth Regiment Ohio Volunteers.


*Approved, February 25, 1889.*

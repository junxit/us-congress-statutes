# Chapter 62

> An act granting a pension to Anna Boppell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Anna Boppell, widow of Martin Boppell. fate of Company E, One hundred and fifty-third Regiment New York State Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, January 16, 1889.*

# Chapter 586

> An Act for the relief of Franklin White.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Franklin White, late private, Company K, Fortieth Regiment Kentucky Mounted Infantry Volunteers, on the pension-roll, at the rate prescribed by existing provisions of law.

# Chapter 16

> An act granting a pension to Catherine Tate.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Catherine Tate, widow of John Tate, late of Company C, First Vermont Heavy Artillery.


*Received by the President December 13, 1888.*

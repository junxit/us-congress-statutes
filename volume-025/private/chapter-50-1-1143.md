# Chapter 1143

> An Act granting a pension to Mary E. McQueen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Mary E. McQueen, an imbecile daughter of Anthony McQueen, late a private of Company D, Thirty-first Regiment of Wisconsin Volunteers, and pay to her duly appointed guardian a pension of eighteen dollars per month on and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, October 15, 1888.*

# Chapter 570

> An Act granting a pension to William M. Davis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William M. Davis, late of Company F, Seventh Regiment Iowa Volunteers.

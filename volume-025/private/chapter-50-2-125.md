# Chapter 125

> An act granting a pension to James S. Harden.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of James S. Harden, of West Salem. Illinois, late a private in Company D, One hundred and ninety-third Regiment Ohio Volunteers.


*Approved, February 11, 1889.*

# Chapter 437

> An act for the relief of the widow of Arno Voss.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to audit the account of Colonel Arno Voss’ for six hundred and seven dollars, and pay the same out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 2, 1889.*

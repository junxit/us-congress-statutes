# Chapter 878

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Emeline Anderson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Emeline Anderson, widow of Jeff Anderson, late a private in Company K, First Regiment Minnesota Cavalry.


*Approved, August 13, 1888.*

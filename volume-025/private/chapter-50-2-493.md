# Chapter 493

> An act granting an increase of pension to Maria A. Rousseau.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Maria A. Rousseau, widow of Lovell H. Rousseau, late a brigadier-general in the United States Army, and pay her at the rate of one hundred dollars per month, in lieu of that which she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 2, 1889.*

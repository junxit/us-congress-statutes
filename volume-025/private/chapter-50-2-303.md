# Chapter 303

> An act granting a pension to Irene Rucker Sheridan, widow of General P. H. Sheridan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior he. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Irene Rucker Sheridan, widow of Philip H. Sheridan, late General of the Army of the United States, and pay her at the rate of two thousand five hundred dollars per year, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 27, 1889.*

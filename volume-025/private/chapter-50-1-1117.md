# Chapter 1117

> An Act granting a pension to Thomas Rains.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to cause to be placed upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Thomas Rains, late captain of Company B. Fifth Tennessee Mounted Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, October 15, 1888.*

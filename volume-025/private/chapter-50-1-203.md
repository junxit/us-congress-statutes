# Chapter 203

> An Act granting a pension to William O. Doyel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William O. Doyel. dependent son of Richard G. Doyel. late a private in Company C. Twenty-third Regiment of Kentucky Volunteers, and pay him a pension of eighteen dollars per month.


*Approved, April 25, 1888.*

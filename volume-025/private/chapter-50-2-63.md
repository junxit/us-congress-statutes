# Chapter 63

> An act granting a pension to Jesse Spencer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jesse Spencer, late a private of Company M, Second Regiment of Pennsylvania Cavalry.


*Approved, January 16, 1889.*

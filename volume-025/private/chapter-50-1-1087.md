# Chapter 1087

> An Act granting a pension to George H. Johnson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George H. Johnson, late a seaman in the United States Navy.


*Approved, October 9, 1888.*

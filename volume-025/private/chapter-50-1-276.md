# Chapter 276

> An Act for the relief of Martha Gray.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Martha Gray, widow of Charles Gray, deceased, formerly of Company A, Fourth Illinois Cavalry.


*Approved, May 18, 1888.*

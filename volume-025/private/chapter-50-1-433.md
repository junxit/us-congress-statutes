# Chapter 433

> An Act to place the name of Rebecca E. Shoemaker on the pension-roll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Rebecca E. Shoemaker, widow of David R. P. Shoemaker, deceased, late a private of Company E. Eleventh Ohio Volunteers, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, June 20, 1888.*

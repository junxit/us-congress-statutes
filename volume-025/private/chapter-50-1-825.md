# Chapter 825

> An Act to restore the homestead right of M. F. Vance of Akron, Colorado.

*Be it enacted by the. Senate, and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the homestead right of M. F. Vance, of Akron, Colorado, be restored, and that he be entitled to all the privileges accruing under sections twenty-two hundred and eighty-nine to twenty-three hundred and seventeen, chapter five, title thirty-two of the Revised Statutes of the United States.


*Approved, August 9, 1888.*

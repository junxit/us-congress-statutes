# Chapter 1231

> An Act for the relief of Eliza A Cutler Jones

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

that there be paid Mrs. Eliza A Cutler Jones of Scottsville, Monroe County New York, the sum of five thousand dollars, in five annual installments of one thousand dollars each, out of any money that may hereafter be appropriated for the use and benefit of the Cheyenne Indians.


*Approved, October 19, 1888.*

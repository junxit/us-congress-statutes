# Chapter 1169

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs Susan Bates, widow of Thomas Bates, late private Company A, Twenty-sixth Regiment Michigan Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Susan Bates, widow of Thomas Bates, late private Company A, Twenty-sixth Regiment Michigan Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, October 16, 1888.*

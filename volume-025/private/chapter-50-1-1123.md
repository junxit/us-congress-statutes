# Chapter 1123

> An Act granting a pension to Victor, Gertrude, Margaret, and Helen, minor children of Lieutenant George R. McGuire.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the names of the minor children of George R. McGuire, late a lieutenant of Company I, Thirteenth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers.


*Approved, October 15, 1888.*

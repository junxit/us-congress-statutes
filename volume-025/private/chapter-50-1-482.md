# Chapter 482

> An Act granting a pension to Catherine Tierney.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Catharine Tierney, widow of Mark Tierney, late of Company 1. First Regiment of United States Infantry, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.

# Chapter 1101

> An Act granting a pension to Manon Vangordon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Manon Vangordon. brother of Jacob Vangordon. late of Company K. Eighty-fourth Indiana Volunteer Infantry and pay him a pension of eighteen dollars per month.


*Approved, October 12, 1888.*

# Chapter 1180

> An Act for the relief of Shadrack W. Brown.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Shadrack W. Brown, late of Company D, One hundred and seventy-sixth Regiment New York Volunteers.


*Approved, October 16, 1888.*

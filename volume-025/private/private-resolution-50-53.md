# Private Resolution 53

> Joint resolution authorizing Brigadier-General Absalom Baird, United States Army, to accept from the President of the French Republic a diploma conferring the decoration of Commander of the National Order of the Legion of Honor.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Brigadier-General Absalom Baird, of the United States Army, is hereby authorized to accept from the President of the French Republic a diploma conferring upon him the decoration of Commander of the National Order of the Legion of Honor.


*Approved, October 19, 1888.*

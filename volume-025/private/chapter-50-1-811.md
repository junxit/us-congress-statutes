# Chapter 811

> An Act granting a pension to Susan F. Scott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is. authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Susan F. Scott, mother of John B. Scott, late of Company G, Eighteenth Connecticut Infantry.


*Approved, August 8, 1888.*

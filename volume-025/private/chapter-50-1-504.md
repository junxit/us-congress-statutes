# Chapter 504

> An Act for the relief of Sophia W. Wright.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sophia W. Wright, a volunteer nurse during the war of the rebellion, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, June 29, 1888.*

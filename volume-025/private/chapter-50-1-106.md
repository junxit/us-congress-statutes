# Chapter 106

> An Act granting a pension to Clarissa Harvey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Clarissa Harvey, widow of Henry B. Harvey, late a private in Company C, Tenth Regiment Wisconsin Volunteers.


*Approved, April 14, 1888.*

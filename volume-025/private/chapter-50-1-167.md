# Chapter 167

> An Act granting an increase of pension to John Rowe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of John Rowe, late a private in Company G, Eleventh Wisconsin Volunteers so as to include disability from kidney disease.


*Approved, April 23, 1888.*

# Chapter 513

> An act granting pension to Gertrude Northrop.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Gertrude Northrop, minor child of Mar-shall A. Northrop, late a captain of Company G, Sixth Regiment of Wisconsin Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of eighteen dollars per month hereafter.


*Approved, March 2, 1889.*

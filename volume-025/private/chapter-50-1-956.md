# Chapter 956

> An Act granting a pension to Nancy Baldwin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Nancy Baldwin, widow of Reuben Baldwin, deceased, late of Company B. Fifty-eighth Indiana Volunteers.


*Approved, September 6, 1888.*

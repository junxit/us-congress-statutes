# Chapter 268

> An act for the relief of Catharine Mutz.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slides of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Catharine Mutz, widow of Max Mutz, late of Company H, Sixth Regiment New York Heavy Artillery.


*Approved, February 25, 1889.*

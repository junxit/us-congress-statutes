# Chapter 355

> An act increase the pension of Mrs. Sue B. Johnson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to in-crease the pension of Mrs. Sue B. Johnson, widow of Gilbert M. L. Johnson, late colonel of the Thirteenth Regiment of Indiana Cavalry and brigadier-general by brevet, to the sum of fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, March 1, 1889.*

# Chapter 541

> An Act granting a pension to James W. Harriman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of James W. Harriman, late of Company E, First Regiment Maine Cavalry Volunteers.


*Approved, July 5, 1888.*

# Chapter 223

> An Act granting a pension to Mary Robinson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Robinson, dependent mother of Andrew J. Robinson, late a member of Company C. Eighth New York Cavalry.


*Approved, May 1, 1888.*

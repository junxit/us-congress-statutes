# Chapter 200

> An act granting a pension to Mary K. Allen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Mary K. Allen, dependent mother of Second Lieutenant Jesse K. Allen, late of the Fifth Infantry of the United States Army, and who was killed in action by the Indians August fifteenth, eighteen hundred and fifty-eight.


*Approved, February 22, 1889.*

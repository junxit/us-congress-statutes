# Chapter 87

> An act granting a pension to Charlotte Taylor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Charlotte Taylor, widow of Thomas P Taylor, late a private of Company C, Twelfth Regiment of Kentucky Volunteers Infantry, at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Received by the President January 7, 1889.*

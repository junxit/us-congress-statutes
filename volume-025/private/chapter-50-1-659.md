# Chapter 659

> An Act for the relief of J. H. Corn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of J. H. Corn, late a member of Company A, Fortieth Regiment Illinois Volunteers, and pay him a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 16, 1888.*

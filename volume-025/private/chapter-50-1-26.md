# Chapter 26

> An Act to carry out the findings of the Court of Claims in the case of James H. Ayres.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, directed to pay to James H. Ayres, of Baltimore, Maryland, nine hundred and forty-five dollars, being the amount due him upon a contract with the United States, for expenses incurred in carrying out the same, as found by the Court of Claims.


*Approved, March 5, 1888.*

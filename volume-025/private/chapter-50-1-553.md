# Chapter 553

> An Act granting a pension to Adelaide L. Jessup.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Adelaide L. Jessup, daughter of Robert L. Jessup, late a private in Company K, Seventh Regiment United States Veteran Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of eighteen dollars per month, such payments to be made to her legally constituted guardian.


*Approved, July 5, 1888.*

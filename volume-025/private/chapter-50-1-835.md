# Chapter 835

> An Act granting an increase of pension to John A. Rolf.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to in-crease the pension of John A. Rolf to forty-five dollars per month, in lieu of the pension now received by him under certificate sixty-three thousand and sixty-four.


*Approved, August 9, 1888.*

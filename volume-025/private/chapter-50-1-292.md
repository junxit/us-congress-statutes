# Chapter 292

> An Act granting a pension to Aletta V. Quick.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Aletta V. Quick, dependent mother of Abram Quick, late sergeant-major of Thirtieth Regiment New Jersey Volunteers.


*Approved, May 18, 1888.*

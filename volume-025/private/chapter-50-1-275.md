# Chapter 275

> An Act granting a pension to Henry H. Stutsman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations or the pension laws, the name of Henry H. Stutsman, late of Company F, Thirteenth Regiment Iowa Volunteers.


*Approved, May 18, 1888.*

# Chapter 720

> An Act making an appropriation for the erection of a light-house on the highland (mainland) to the westward of Crooked River, Florida.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of forty thousand dollars be. and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the erection of a light-house on the highland (mainland) to the westward of Crooked River, in Franklin County, Florida, said amount to be expended under the direction of the Secretary of the Treasury.


*Approved, July 31, 1888.*

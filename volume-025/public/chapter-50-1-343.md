# Chapter 343

> An Act to authorize United States marshals to arrest offenders and fugitives from justice in Indian Territory.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That alter the passage of this act any United States marshal is hereby authorized and required, when necessary to execute any process connected with any criminal proceeding issued out of the circuit or district court of the United States for the district of which he is marshal, or by any commissioner of either of said courts, to enter the Indian Territory, and to execute the same therein in the same manner that he is now required by law to execute like processes in his own district.


*Approved, June 4, 1888.*

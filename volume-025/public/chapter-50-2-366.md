# Chapter 366

> An act for the establishment of a light-house and steam fog-signal on the outer breakwater at the harbor of Chicago, Illinois.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a light-house and steam fog-signal be established on the easterly end of the outer breakwater at the harbor of Chicago. Lake Michigan, Illinois, at a cost not to exceed thirty-six thousand dollars: Provided, That this light may be established at any time without regard to the complition of said breakwater.


*Approved, March 2, 1889.*

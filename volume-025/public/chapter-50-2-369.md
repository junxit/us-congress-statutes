# Chapter 369

> An act providing for the construction of a steam-tender for service on the Great Lakes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a steam-tender for light-house duty on the Northwestern Lakes to be constructed at a cost not to exceed eighty-five thousand dollars: Provided, That the construction of said tender shall be let to the lowest responsible bidder after advertisement.


*Approved, March 2, 1889.*

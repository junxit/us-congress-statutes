# Chapter 868

> An act to extend the provisions of “An act to provide for the muster and pay of certain officers and enlisted men of the volunteer forces,” and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of “An act to provide for the muster and pay of certain officers and enlisted men of the volunteer Forces.” approved June fourth, eighteen hundred and eighty-four, as amended by the act approved February third, eighteen hundred and eighty-seven, be, and the same are hereby, revived and extended for a period of five years from the third day of June, eighteen hundred and eighty-seven.

## § 2.

That the limitation heretofore imposed by law on the presentation by officers or soldiers of claims for the loss of horses and equipments in the military services, during the late war is hereby suspended for the period of three years.


*Approved, August 13, 1888.*

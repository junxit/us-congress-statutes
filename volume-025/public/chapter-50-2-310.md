# Chapter 310

> An act to create ports of entry at Tacoma and Seattle, in Washington Territory.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Tacoma. Washington Territory, and Seattle, Washington Territory, be, and they are hereby, constituted ports of entry in the Puget Sound customs collection district, and that the privileges of the first and seventh sections of an act approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, entitled “An act to amend the statutes in relation to immediate transportation of dutiable goods, and for other purposes,” be, and the same are hereby, extended to said ports.


*Approved, March 1, 1889.*

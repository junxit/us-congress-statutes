# Chapter 1219

> An act to facilitate the transportation of life saving and lighthouse supplies at Hog Island Virginia

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be erected at the landing near the Hog Island lighthouse, in Virginia, a wharf, and that a road from the same to the said lighthouse and life saving station be built, to facilitate the transportation of supplies: Provided, That the same shall not cost more than five thousand dollars.


*Approved, October 19, 1888.*

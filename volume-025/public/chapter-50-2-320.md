# Chapter 320

> An act authorizing the President to appoint William English an officer in the Regular Army of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be. and he is hereby, authorized to nominate and by and with the advice and consent of the Senate to appoint William English a second lieutenant in the Regular Army of the United States, and to assign him to any vacancy in said rank existing therein.


*Approved, March 1, 1889.*

# Chapter 244

> An Act for the establishment of a light-house, fog-signal, and day beacon in the vicinity of Goose Rocks. Fox Island Thoroughfare, Maine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be established at or near Goose Rocks, at the entrance to Fox Island Thoroughfare, on the coast of Maine, a light-house and fog-signal, and that there be established at or near Channel Rock, in the vicinity of Goose Rocks, a day beacon, the cost of which shall not exceed the sum of thirty-five thousand dollars, including the cost of the sites.


*Approved, May 14, 1888.*

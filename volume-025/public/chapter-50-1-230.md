# Chapter 230

> An Act for the registry of the barges “Albert M,” “Condor,” and “Adelante.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of Navigation is hereby authorized to admit to registry, as vessels of the United States, the following-named foreign vessels, owned by John Scully, of South Amboy, New Jersey, on satisfactory proof that he is a citizen of the United States, namely: the “Albert I.” the “Condor.” and the “Adelante.”


*Approved, May 9, 1888.*

# Chapter 207

> An Act for establishing a light or lights and other aids to navigation to guide into Charlotte Harbor, Florida.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a light, or lights, and other aids to navigation to guide into Charlotte Harbor, Florida, be established at a cost not to exceed thirty-five thousand dollars.


*Approved, May 1, 1888.*

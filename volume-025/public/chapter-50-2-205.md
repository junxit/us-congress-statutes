# Chapter 205

> An act to change the date for the commencement of the March terms of the district court for the northern district of Georgia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That hereafter the regular terms of the district court for the northern district of Georgia, now held on the first Monday in March, shall commence on the second Monday in March of each year.


*Approved, February 23, 1889.*

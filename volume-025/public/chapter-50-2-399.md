# Chapter 399

> An act for the establishment of a light house with fog-bell on Oyster-Beds Shoal, in the Hudson River. New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a light-house with fog-bell be established at or near Oyster-Beds Shoal, in the Hudson River, opposite Rockland Lake Dock, New York, at a cost not to exceed thirty-five thousand dollars.


*Approved, March 2, 1889.*

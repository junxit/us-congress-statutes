# Chapter 208

> An Act to provide for protecting the navigation of the Illinois River by extending the system of beacon-lights to said river.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Light-House Board be, and it is hereby, directed to establish such number of beacon-lights along the Illinois River as may. in the judgment of said board, be necessary for the proper protection of the navigation of said river: Provided, That the cost of the same shall not exceed seven thousand dollars, which sum is hereby appropriated for that purpose, to be expended under the supervision of the Light-House Board for the purpose herein provided.


*Approved, May 1, 1888.*

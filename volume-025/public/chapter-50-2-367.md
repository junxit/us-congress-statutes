# Chapter 367

> An act providing for the establishment of certain lights on the cost of Mississippi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be established range-lights to guide into the mouth of the Pascagoula River, Mississippi, at a cost not to exceed one thousand dollars.

## § 2.

That there be established a light on the east bank of Pearl River. Mississippi, opposite the draw in the railway bridge, at a cost not to exceed two hundred and fifty dollars.


*Approved, March 2, 1889.*

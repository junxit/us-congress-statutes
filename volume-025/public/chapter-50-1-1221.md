# Chapter 1221

> An act for the establishment of a light house and fog-signal at or near Gull Shoal, Pamlico Sound North Carolina

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a lighthouse and fog-signal be established at or near Gull Shoal Pamlico Sound, North Carolina, at a cost not to exceed thirty thousand dollars.


*Approved, October 19, 1888.*

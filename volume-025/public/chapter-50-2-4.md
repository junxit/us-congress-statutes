# Chapter 4

> An act making an appropriation to supply a deficiency in the appropriation for the contingent expenses of the House of Representatives.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be and hereby is appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury, not otherwise appropriated, the sum of twelve thousand dollars to supply a deficiency in the appropriation for miscellaneous items and expenses of special and select committees of the House of Representativos for the fiscal year eighteen hundred and eighty-nine.


*Approved, December 17, 1888.*

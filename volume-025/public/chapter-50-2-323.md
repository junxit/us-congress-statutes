# Chapter 323

> An act providing for the establishment of a life-saving station at mouth of Coquille River, Oregon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a life Saving Station be and is hereby established at mouth of Coquille River, in Coos County, in the State of Oregon.


*Approved, March 1, 1889.*

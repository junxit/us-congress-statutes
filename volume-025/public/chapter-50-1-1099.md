# Chapter 1099

> An act to include Sapelo Sound, Sapelo River, and Sapelo Island in the Brunswick collection district in the State of Georgia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Sapelo Sound, Sapelo River, and the Island of Sapelo shall henceforth be included in, and be a part of, the second district for the collection of customs, in the State of Georgia, known as the Brunswick district.


*Approved, October 12, 1888.*

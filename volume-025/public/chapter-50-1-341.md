# Chapter 341

> An Act to establish a port of delivery at Grand Rapids, Michigan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Grand Rapids, in the State of Michigan, be. and the same is hereby, constituted a port of delivery; and that the privileges of immediate transportation of dutiable merchandise conferred by the act of June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, entitled “An act to amend the statutes in relation to immediate transportation of dutiable goods, and for other purposes,” be, and the same are hereby, extended to said port.

## § 2.

That there shall be appointed by the President a surveyor of customs for said port, who shall reside at said port, and who shall receive a salary to be determined in amount by the Secretary of the Treasury, not exceeding one thousand two hundred dollars per annum.


*Approved, June 4, 1888.*

# Chapter 363

> An act providing for the reestablishment of the light-house at Point Isabel, Texas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the light-house at Point Isabel, in the State of Texas, be reestablished at a cost not to exceed eight thousand dollars.


*Approved, March 2, 1889.*

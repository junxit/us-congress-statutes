# Chapter 1217

> An act for establishing a light house and fog signal on Roe Island, Suisun Bay, California.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a light house and fog signal be established on Roe Island Suisun Bay, California, at a cost not to exceed ten thousand dollars


*Approved, October 19, 1888.*

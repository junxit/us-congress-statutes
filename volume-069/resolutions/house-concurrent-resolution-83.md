# House Concurrent Resolution 83

> TRADE AGREEMENTS EXTENSION ACT OF 1955

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed two thousand five hundred additional copies of the hearings on H. R. 1, the Trade Agreements Extension Act of 1955, held by the Committee on Ways and Means for the use of the said Committee.


*Passed February 15, 1955.*

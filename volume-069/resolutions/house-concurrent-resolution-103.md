# House Concurrent Resolution 103

> ADJOURNMENT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Monday, April 4, 1955, they stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Wednesday, April 13, 1955.


*Passed March 30, 1955.*

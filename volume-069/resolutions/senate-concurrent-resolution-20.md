# Senate Concurrent Resolution 20

> “OUR CAPITOL”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed three hundred thousand copies of Senate Document Numbered 13, Eighty-fourth Congress, entitled “Our Capitol”, of which one hundred thousand copies shall be for the use of the Senate and two hundred thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives.


*Agreed to August 1, 1955.*

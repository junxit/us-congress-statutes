# Senate Concurrent Resolution 31

> JUVENILE DELINQUENCY

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on the Judiciary not more than three thousand additional copies each of three reports of the Committee on the Judiciary to the Senate on its study of juvenile delinquency in the United States.


*Agreed to June 14, 1955.*

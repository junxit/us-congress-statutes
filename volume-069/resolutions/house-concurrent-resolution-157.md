# House Concurrent Resolution 157

> TENTH ANNIVERSARY OF FOUNDING OF UNITED NATIONS

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

Whereas it is the hope and prayer of the American people that peace will be established among ail the nations of the world, thus avoiding the carnage and destruction of war, making possible the lifting of the burden of arms and thereby freeing the energies of mankind to work more effectively to overcome the ravages of hunger, disease, illiteracy, and poverty: Therefore be it

That the Congress reaffirms the deep desire of the people of the United States for an honorable and lasting peace, and expresses the hope that the people of all the nations of the world join with the people of the United States in a renewed effort for peace.

## § 2.

The President is requested to convey an expression of such reaffirmation and such hope to the representatives of the nations gathered in San Francisco to commemorate the tenth anniversary of the founding of the United Nations.


*Agreed to June 17, 1955.*

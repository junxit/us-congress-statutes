# Senate Concurrent Resolution 9

> “INTERLOCKING SUBVERSION IN GOVERNMENT DEPARTMENTS”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on the Judiciary not to exceed twenty thousand additional copies of parts 21,22,24,25, and 26 of the hearings entitled “Interlocking Subversion in Government Departments”, held before a subcommittee of the above committee during the Eighty-third Congress.


*Agreed to March 16, 1955.*

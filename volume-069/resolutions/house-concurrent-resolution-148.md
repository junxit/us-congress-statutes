# House Concurrent Resolution 148

> “RADIATION STERILIZATION OF FOODS”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Joint Committee on Atomic Energy, twenty-five thousand additional copies of the hearing held by the said joint committee on May 9, 1955, on “Radiation Sterilization of Foods”.


*Passed July 22, 1955.*

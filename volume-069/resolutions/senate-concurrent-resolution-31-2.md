# Senate Concurrent Resolution 31

> REENROLLMENT OF S. J. RES. 60

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President of the United States be, and he is hereby, requested to return to the Senate the enrolled joint resolution (S. J. Res. 6(1) directing a study and report by the Secretary of Agriculture on burley tobacco marketing controls; that if and when returned the action of the Speaker of the House of Representatives and the President pro tempore of the Senate in signing the said joint resolution be, and the same is hereby, rescinded; and that the Secretary of the Senate be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to reenroll the said joint resolution with the following change, namely: In lieu of the date “July 1, 1955” insert “November 1, 1955”.


*Agreed to June 14, 1955.*

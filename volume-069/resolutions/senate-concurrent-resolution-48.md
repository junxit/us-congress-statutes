# Senate Concurrent Resolution 48

> RELIEF OF ALIEN SHEEPHERDERS

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Secretary of the Senate be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, in the enrollment of the bill (S. 633) for the relief of certain alien sheepherders, to make the following change, namely: On page 1, lines 4 and 5, strike out the words “Rufino Merino Jimenez” and the comma.


*Passed July 14, 1955.*

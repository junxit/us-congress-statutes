# Senate Concurrent Resolution 53

> D. C. POLICE AND FIREMEN’S SALARIES

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Secretary of the Senate be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, in .the enrollment of the bill (S. 2428), to increase the salaries of officers and members of the Metropolitan Police force, and the Fire Department of the District of Columbia, the United States Park Police, and the White House Police, and for other purposes, to make the following change, viz: On page 5, line 15, of the engrossed bill, strike out “63f-63k” and in lieu thereof insert “61f-61k”.


*Agreed to July 26, 1955*

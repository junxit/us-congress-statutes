# Senate Concurrent Resolution 57

> SIGNING OF ENROLLED BILLS, ETC.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That, notwithstanding the sine die adjournment of the two Houses, the President of the Senate and the Speaker of the House of Representatives are hereby authorized to sign enrolled bills and joint resolutions duly’ passed by the two Houses and found truly enrolled.


*Agreed to August 2, 1955.*

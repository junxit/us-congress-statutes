# Private Law 483

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Elfrieda Schoeppe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Elfrieda Schoeppe, widow of Sergeant William E. Schoeppe, a deceased former United States citizen who served honorably in the Armed Forces of the United States, shall be deemed to be a nonquota immigrant.


*Approved August 11, 1955.*

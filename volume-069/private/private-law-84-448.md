# Private Law 448

> AN ACT For the relief of Ingrid Liselotte Poch.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the pur-poses of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Ingrid Liselotte Poch, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Corporal George Thomas Murphy, citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 11, 1955.*

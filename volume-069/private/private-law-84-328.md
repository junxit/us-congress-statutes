# Private Law 328

> AN ACT For the relief of Nurith Spier.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203 (a) (3), and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Nurith Spier, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Walter and Edith Spier, lawfully resident aliens of the United States.


*Approved August 5, 1955.*

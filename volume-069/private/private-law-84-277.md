# Private Law 277

> AN ACT For the relief of Carmelo Rodriguez Perez, also known as Carmelo Rodriguez Fenald.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Carmelo Rodriguez Perez, also known as Carmelo Rodriguez Fenald, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Sydney Fenald, citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 1, 1955.*

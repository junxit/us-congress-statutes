# Private Law 488

> AN ACT For the relief of John Axel Arvidson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, John Axel Arvidson shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved August 12, 1955.*

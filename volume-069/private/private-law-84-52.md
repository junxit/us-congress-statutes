# Private Law 52

> AN ACT For the relief of Erika Rambauske.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding section 212 (a) (3) of the Immigration and Nationality Act and for the purposes of that Act, Erika Rambauske, the dependent sister of Werner R. E. Rambauske, who is a German scientist lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence for utilization in the national defense effort, shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fee: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the said Act. Upon the granting of permanent residence to such alien as provided for in this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the appropriate quota for the first year that such quota is available.


*Approved May 25, 1955.*

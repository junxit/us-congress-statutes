# Private Law 419

> AN ACT For the relief of Mary Mancuso.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Mary Mancuso, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Paul Torcasio, citizens of the United States.


*Approved August 9, 1955.*

# Private Law 172

> AN ACT For the relief of Charles (Carlos) Gerlicz.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 212 (a) (1), (4), and (7) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Charles (Carlos) Gerlicz may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if he is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as required by section 213 of that Act.


*Approved July 11, 1955.*

# Private Law 242

> AN ACT For the relief of certain relatives of United States citizens or lawfully resident aliens.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of section 212 (a) (6) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the aliens hereinafter named may be granted visas and be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if they are found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act under such conditions and controls which the Attorney General, after consultation with the Surgeon General of the United States Public Health Service, Department of Health, Education, and Welfare, may deem necessary to impose: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the said Act: Maria Alexandrina Martins Almeida, Theresa Andriopoulos, Rosemarie E.

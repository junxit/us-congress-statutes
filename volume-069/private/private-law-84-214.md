# Private Law 214

> AN ACT To authorize and direct the sale of certain laud in Alaska to Oscar H. Vogel, of Anchorage, Alaska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Oscar H. Vogel, of Anchorage, Alaska, is hereby authorized, for a period of one year from and after the effective date of this Act, to apply for the purchase of. and the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to convey to Oscar H. Vogel, for homestead purposes, the following-described land situated in Alaska: Lot 3, section 25, township 11 north, range 7 west, Seward meridian, Alaska, containing twenty-five and twenty-two one-hundredths acres: Provided, That the purchase price for the land shall be the reasonable value thereof without improvements, as determined by the Secretary of the Interior but not less than $1.25 per acre.


*Approved July 26, 1955.*

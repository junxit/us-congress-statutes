# Private Law 460

> AN ACT For the relief of Mock Jung Shee (Mock Jung Liu).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the pur-poses of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mock Jung Shee (Mock Jung Liu) shall be held and considered to be classifiable as a returning resident as defined by section 101 (a) (27) (B) of that Act.


*Approved August 11, 1955.*

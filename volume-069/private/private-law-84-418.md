# Private Law 418

> AN ACT For the relief of Dalisay Lourdes Cruz.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Dalisay Lourdes Cruz shall be held and considered to be the minor alien child of Mr. Antonio Cruz, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 9, 1955.*

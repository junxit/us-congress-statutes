# Private Law 306

> AN ACT For the relief of Amalia Bertolino Querio.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Amalia Bertolino Querio shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Raymond D. Querio and Comenica Querio, citizens of the United States. Upon the issuance of a visa to the alien as provided for in this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper officer to deduct one number from the allocation of visas provided for issuance under section 5 (a) of the Refugee Relief Act of 1953, as amended.


*Approved August 3, 1955.*

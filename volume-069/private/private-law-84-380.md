# Private Law 380

> AN ACT For the relief of Miss Cecile Patricia Chapman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 101 (b) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Miss Cecile Patricia Chapman shall be held and considered to be within the purview of section 101 (a) (27) (A) of the said Act.


*Approved August 9, 1955.*

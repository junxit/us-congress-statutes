# Private Law 199

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Lee Tai Hung Quan and Quan Ah Sang.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Lee Tai Hung Quan and Quan Ah Sang, wife and minor child of Lloyd Quan, deceased citizen of the United States and World War II veteran, shall be held and considered to be nonquota immigrants.


*Approved July 12, 1955.*

# Private Law 136

> AN ACT For the relief of Carl E. Edwards.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 15 to 20, inclusive, of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes”, approved September 7, 1916, as amended (5 U. S. C. 765–770), are hereby waived in favor of Carl E. Edwards, 3515 Twelfth Street, Des Moines, Iowa, for compensation for a disability allegedly incurred while in the employ of the National Military Establishment, United States naval yard, Brooklyn, New York, and his claim is authorized and directed to be considered and acted upon under the remaining provisions of such Act, as amended, if he files such claim with the Department of Labor (Bureau of Employees’ Compensation) not later than six months after the enactment of this Act: Provided, That no benefits other than hospital and medical expense actually incurred shall accrue prior to the date of enactment of this Act.


*Approved July 1, 1955.*

# Private Law 456

> AN ACT For the relief of Andrew Carrigan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a reentry permit issued pursuant to section 223 of the Immigration and Nationality Act for Andrew Carrigan, born on May 4, 1952, who is the adopted minor child of Mr. and Mrs. James Carrigan, shall be valid until such time as Andrew Carrigan becomes ten years of age, or until such time as his adoptive father, James Carrigan, resumes permanent residence in the United States, whichever date occurs earlier.


*Approved August 11, 1955.*

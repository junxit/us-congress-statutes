# Private Law 429

> AN ACT For the relief of Richard Raffo Hanson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Richard Raffo Hanson, who lost United States citizenship under the provisions of section 349 (a) (3) of the Immigration and Nationality Act of 1952 may be naturalized by taking prior to one year after the effective date of this Act, before any court referred to in subsection (a) of section 310 of the Immigration and Nationality Act or before any diplomatic or consular officer of the United States abroad, the oaths prescribed by section 337 of the said Act. From and after naturalization under this Act, the said Richard Raffo Hanson shall have the same citizenship status as that which existed immediately prior to its loss.


*Approved August 9, 1955.*

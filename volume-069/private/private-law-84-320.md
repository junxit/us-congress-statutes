# Private Law 320

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Uto Ginoza.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Uto Ginoza shall be held to be classifiable as a nonquota immigrant as defined in section 101 (a) (27) (B) of that Act.


*Approved August 3, 1955.*

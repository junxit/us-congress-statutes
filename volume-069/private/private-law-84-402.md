# Private Law 402

> AN ACT For the relief of Iva Druzianich (Iva Druzianic).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the pur-poses of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Iva Druzianich (Iva Druzianic) shall be deemed to be the natural-born minor alien child of John Druzianich, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 9, 1955.*

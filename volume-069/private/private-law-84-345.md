# Private Law 345

> AN ACT For the relief of Orville Ennis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Orville Ennis, shall be held to be eligible for a preferential status under the provisions of section 203 (a) (3) of that Act.


*Approved August 5, 1955.*

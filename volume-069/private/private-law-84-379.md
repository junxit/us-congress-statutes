# Private Law 379

> AN ACT For the relief of Giussepina Cervi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Giussepina Cervi, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Sergeant John Louis Troiano, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 9, 1955.*

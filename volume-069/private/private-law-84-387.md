# Private Law 387

> AN ACT For the relief of Edith Winifred Loch.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Edith Winifred Loch, a British subject who was born in India of British parents, shall be deemed to have been born in Great Britain.


*Approved August 9, 1955.*

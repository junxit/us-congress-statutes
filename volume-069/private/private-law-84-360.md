# Private Law 360

> AN ACT For the relief of Yuji Doi and Mrs. Matsuyo Yamaoka Doi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of section 212 (a) (17) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Yuji Doi and Mrs. Matsuyo Doi may be classified as nonquota aliens under the provisions of section 101 (a) (27) (B) of that Act: Provided, That this exemption shall apply only to a ground for exclusion of which the Department of State or the Department of Justice had knowledge prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved August 5, 1955.*

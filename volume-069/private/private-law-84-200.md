# Private Law 200

> AN ACT For the relief of Virgil Won (also known as Virgilio Jackson).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives or the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, section 202 (b) shall be held not to apply to the ease of Virgil Won (also known as Virgilio Jackson), who was born in Nicaragua.


*Approved July 12, 1955.*

# Private Law 173

> AN ACT For the relief of Stijepo Butch.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Stijepo Buich, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Tom and Anfisa Buich, citizens of the United States.


*Approved July 11, 1955.*

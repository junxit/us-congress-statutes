# Private Law 359

> AN ACT For the relief of Valerie Anne Peterson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Valerie Anne Peterson, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Doctor Freddie N. Peterson, citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 5, 1955.*

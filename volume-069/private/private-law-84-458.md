# Private Law 458

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Koto Nakagawa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Koto Nakagawa shall be held to be classifiable as a nonquota returning resident under the provisions of section 101 (a) (27) (B) of that Act.


*Approved August 11, 1955.*

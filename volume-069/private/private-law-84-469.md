# Private Law 469

> AN ACT For the relief of Edward Naarits.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwith-standing the time limitation provided for in section 330 (a) (2) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Edward Naarits shall be deemed to be within the purview of the said section provided his petition for naturalization is filed within one year from the effective date of this Act.


*Approved August 11, 1955.*

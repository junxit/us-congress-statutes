# Private Law 434

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Lee Shee Yee (also known as Lee Lai Koon).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Lee Shee Yee (also known as Lee Lai Koon) shall be held to be classifiable as a nonquota returning resident under the provisions of section 101 (a) (27) (B) of that Act.


*Approved August 9, 1955.*

# Private Law 447

> AN ACT For the relief of Vasilios Liakopoulos.

*Be it enacted by the. Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Vasilios Liakopoulos, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Gregory and Anna Liakopoulos, citizens of the United States.


*Approved August 11, 1955.*

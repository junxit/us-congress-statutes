# Private Law 437

> AN ACT For the relief of Helene Rapp.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, section 353 (5) (B) shall be held to have been and to be applicable to Helene Rapp.


*Approved August 9, 1955.*

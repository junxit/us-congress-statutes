# Private Law 191

> AN ACT for the Relief of Franciszek Janicki and His Wife Stefania Janicki.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Franciszek Janicki and his wife Stefania Janicki shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fees.


*Approved July 12, 1955.*

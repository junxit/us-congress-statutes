# Private Law 18

> AN ACT For the relief of David Manuel Porter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, David Manuel Porter (Manuel Pacheco), shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Warren R. Porter and Mrs. Betty O’Brian Porter, citizens of the United States.


*Approved May 23, 1955.*

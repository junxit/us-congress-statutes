# Private Law 412

> JOINT RESOLUTION To authorize the President to issue posthumously to the late Seymour Richard Belinky, a flight officer in the United States Army, a commission as second lieutenant, United States Army, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is authorized to issue posthumously to the late Seymour Richard Belinky, late a flight officer, United States Army, a commission as second lieutenant, United States Army, as of December 27, 1944.

## § 2.

The Secretary of the Army is authorized and directed to amend the records of the Department of the Army so as to carry the said Seymour Richard Belinky as a second lieutenant, United States Army, to rank from December 27, 1944.


*Approved August 9, 1955.*

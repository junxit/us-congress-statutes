# Private Law 126

> AN ACT To permit a retired officer of the Navy to be employed in a command status in connection with Antarctic expeditions.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding any other provisions of law, the Secretary of the Navy is authorized to employ Rear Admiral George John Dufek, United States Navy, retired, in a command status in connection with Antarctic expeditions.


*Approved June 29, 1955.*

# Private Law 474

> AN ACT For the relief of Nadia Noland and Samia Ouafa Noland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of paragraphs (9) and (12) of section 212 (a) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Nadia Noland may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence, if she is found to be. otherwise admissible under the provisions of such Act. The provisions of this Act shall apply only to grounds for exclusion under such paragraphs known to the Secretary of State or the Attorney General prior to the date of enactment of this Act.

## § 2.

For the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Samia Ouafa Noland, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of KMC Paul F. Noland, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 11, 1955.*

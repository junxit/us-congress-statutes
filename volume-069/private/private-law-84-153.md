# Private Law 153

> AN ACT For the relief of Frieda Quiring and Tina Quiring.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and (205) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor children, Frieda Quiring and Tina Quiring, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien children of Mr. and Mrs. Cornelius N. Neufeld, citizens of the United States.


*Approved July 7, 1955.*

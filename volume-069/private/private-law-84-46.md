# Private Law 46

> AN ACT For the relief of Andrea Hernandes Montes Rocha.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 212 (a) (9), (16), and (19) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Andrea Hernandes Montes Rocha may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act: Provided, That this exemption shall apply only to a ground for exclusion of which the Department of State or the Department of Justice had knowledge prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved May 25, 1955.*

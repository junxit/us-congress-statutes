# Private Law 405

> AN ACT For the relief of Maria Anna Coone.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of paragraph (9) of section 212 (a) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Maria Anna Coone may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence, if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of such Act. The provisions of this Act shall apply only to grounds for exclusion under such paragraph known to the Secretary of State or the Attorney General prior to the date of enactment of this Act.


*Approved August 9, 1955.*

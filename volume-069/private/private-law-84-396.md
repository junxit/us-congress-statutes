# Private Law 396

> AN ACT For the relief of Gabor Lanyi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of paragraphs (1) and (4) of section 212 (a) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Gabor Lanyi may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if he is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of such Act. The provisions of this Act shall apply only to grounds for exclusion under such paragraphs known to the Secretary of State or the Attorney General prior to the date of enactment of this Act: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the said Act.


*Approved August 9, 1955.*

# Private Law 424

> AN ACT For the relief of Geraldine Gean Hunt and Linda Marie Hunt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Geraldine Gean Hunt and Linda Marie Hunt shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fees.


*Approved August 9, 1955.*

# Private Law 49

> AN ACT For the relief of Janis Arvids Reinfelds.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of sections 212 (a) (1) and 212 (a) (4) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Janis Arvids Reinfelds may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if he is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking approved by the Attorney General be given as prescribed by section 213 of that Act.


*Approved May 25, 1955.*

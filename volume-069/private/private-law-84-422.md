# Private Law 422

> AN ACT For the relief of Sada Zarikian.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Sada Zarikian shall be held and considered to be within the purview of section 354 (5) of that Act.


*Approved August 9, 1955.*

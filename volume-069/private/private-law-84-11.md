# Private Law 11

> AN ACT For the relief of Christine Susan Caiado.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of section 212 (a) (4) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Christine Susan Caiado may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the said Act.


*Approved May 23, 1955.*

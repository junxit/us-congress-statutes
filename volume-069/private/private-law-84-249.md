# Private Law 249

> AN ACT For the relief of Maria Louise Andreis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203 (a) (3) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Maria Louise Andreis shall be held and considered to be the minor alien child of Egidia Bonessi Schaetzler, a lawfully resident alien of the United States.


*Approved August 1, 1955.*

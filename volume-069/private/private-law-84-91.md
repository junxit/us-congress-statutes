# Private Law 91

> AN ACT For the relief of Shirley W. Rothra.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Shirley W. Rothra (Army Service Number 42109864), Mayville, New York, is hereby relieved of all liability to refund to the United States the sum of $150, representing overpayments made to him by the War Department as the result of its failure to make appropriate deductions from his pay for class E allotments during the period beginning October 1, 1944, and ending December 31, 1945. In the audit and settlement of the accounts of any certifying or disbursing officer of the United States, full credit shall be given for any amount for which liability is relieved by this Act.


*Approved June 21, 1955.*

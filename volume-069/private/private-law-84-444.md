# Private Law 444

> AN ACT For the relief of Ernst Fraenkel and his wife, Hanna Fraenkel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Ernst Fraenkel and his wife, Hanna Fraenkel, naturalized citizens of the United States, shall be permitted to reside in Germany until three years following the date of the enactment of this Act without losing their United States citizenship under section 352 (a) of such Act.


*Approved August 11, 1955.*

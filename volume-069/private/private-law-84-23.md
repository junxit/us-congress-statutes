# Private Law 23

> AN ACT For the relief of Vida Kosnik.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 101 (a) (27) (A) and section 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Vida Kosnik shall be held and considered to be the minor alien child of Martin Kosnik, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved May 23, 1955.*

# Private Law 287

> AN ACT For the relief of Jose Domingo Quintanar.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstand-ing the provisions of section 212 (a) (9) and (19) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Jose Domingo Quintanar may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if he is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act: Provided, That these exemptions shall apply only to grounds for exclusion of which the Department of State or the Department of Justice had knowledge prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved August 3, 1955.*

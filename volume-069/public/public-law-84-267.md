# Public Law 267

> AN ACT To amend section 107 of title 28 of the United States Code so as to eliminate separate divisions and reduce the number of places of holding regular terms of the United States District Court for the District of Nebraska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 107 of title 28 of the United States Code is amended to read as follows:

> ## § 107. Nebraska
>
> “Nebraska constitutes one judicial district.
>
> “Court shall be held at Lincoln, North Platte, and Omaha.”

## § 2.

The amendment made by the first section of this Act shall take effect on September 1, 1955.


*Approved August 9, 1955.*

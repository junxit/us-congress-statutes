# Public Law 58

> AN ACT To amend the Act of January 12, 1951, as amended, to continue in effect the provisions of title II of the First War Powers Act, 1941.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2 of the Act of January 12, 1951 (64 Stat. 1257), as amended, is further amended by striking out “1955” and inserting in lien thereof “1957”.


*Approved June 1, 1955.*

# Public Law 122

> AN ACT To continue the effectiveness of the Missing Persons Act. as extended, until July 1, 1956.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 15, Missing Persons Act (56 Stat. 147, 1093), its amended, is further amended by deleting “July 1, 1955” and inserting in lieu thereof “July 1, 1956”.


*Approved June 30, 1955.*

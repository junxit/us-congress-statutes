# Public Law 168

> AN ACT To extend the existing authority for the loan of a small aircraft carrier to the Government of France.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2 of the Act of August 5, 1953 (67 Stat. 363) is hereby amended by striking out the remainder of the sentence after the word “until” and inserting in lieu thereof “June 30, 1958”.


*Approved July 26, 1955.*

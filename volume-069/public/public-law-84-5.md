# Public Law 5

> AN ACT To amend the Act of July 10, 1953, which created the Commission on Intergovernmental Relations.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (c) of section 3 of the Act of July 10, 1953, entitled “An Act to establish a Commission on Intergovernmental Relations”, is hereby amended to read as follows:

> - **(c)** The Commission, not later than June 30, 1955, shall submit to the President for transmittal to the Congress its final report, including recommendations for legislative action; and the Commission may also from time to time make to the President such earlier reports as the President may request or as the Commission deems appropriate.”

## § 2.

Section 6 of such Act of July 10, 1953, is hereby amended to read as follows:

> ## § 6. “termination of the commission
>
> The Commission shall cease to exist at the close of business on June 30, 1955.”


*Approved February 7, 1955.*

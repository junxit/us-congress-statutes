# Public Law 1

> AN ACT To amend section 7237 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1954.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first sentence of subsection (a) of section 7237 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1954 is hereby amended by striking out “subpart C of part I,” and inserting in lieu thereof “part I”. Subsection (b) of such section is hereby amended by striking out “subpart C of”.


*Approved January 20, 1955.*

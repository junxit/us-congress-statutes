# Public Law 325

> AN ACT To provide wage credits under title II of the Social Secure Act for Military service before April 1956, and to permit application for lump-sum benefits under such title to be made within two years after interment or reinterment in the ease of servicemen dying overseas before April 1956.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 217 (e) as the Social Security Act (relating to benefits in case of veterans) inserting in lieu thereof “April 1, 1956”

## § 2.

The last sentence section 2020 (l) of the Social Security Act (relating to lump-sum death payments) is amended by striking out “July 1955” and inserting in lieu thereof “April 1956”.


*Approved August 9, 1955.*

# Public Law 303

> AN ACT To extend the period during which claims for floor stocks refunds may be filed with respect to certain manufacturers’ excise taxes which were reduced by the Excise Tux Reduction Act of 1951.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3416 (a) (2) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1939 (relating to period for tiling claims for certain floor stocks refunds) is hereby amended by striking out “before August 1, 1954” and inserting in lieu thereof “on or before the sixtieth day after the date of the enactment of H. R. 3712, Eighty-fourth Congress”.


*Approved August 9, 1955.*

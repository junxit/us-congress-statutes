# Public Law 209

> AN ACT To extend the provisions of title XII of the Merchant Marine Act, 1936, relating to war risk insurance, for an additional five years.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1214 of title XII of the Merchant Marine Act, 1936, is amended by striking out “5” and inserting in lieu thereof “10”.


*Approved August 3, 1955.*

# Public Law 13

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making an additional appropriation for the Department of Justice for the fiscal year 1955, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not. otherwise appropriated, for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1955, the following sum:

## DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

## Legal Activities and General Administration

## fees and expenses of witnesses

For an additional amount for “Fees and expenses of witnesses”, $710,000.


*Approved March 21, 1955.*

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

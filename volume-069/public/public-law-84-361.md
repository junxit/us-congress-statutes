# Public Law 361

> AN ACT To amend section 313 of the Agricultural Adjustment Act of 1938, with respect to tobacco allotments.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 313 of the Agricultural Adjustment Act of 1938, as amended, is amended by adding at the end thereof the following new subsection:

> - **(j)** The production of tobacco on a farm in 1955 or any subsequent year for which no farm acreage allotment was established shall not make the farm eligible for an allotment as an old farm under subsections (b) and (g) hereof: Provided, however, That by reason of such production the farm need not lie considered as ineligible for a new farm allotment tinder subsections (c) and (g) hereof, but such production shall not be deemed past tobacco experience for any producer on the farm.”


*Approved August 11, 1955.*

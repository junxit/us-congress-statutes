# Public Law 180

> AN ACT To amend the Veterans’ Readjustment Assistance Act of 1952, to extend the time for filing claims for mustering-out payments.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 503 of the Veterans’ Readjustment Assistance Act of 1952 is amended by striking out “within two years after the date of enactment of this title” and inserting in lieu thereof “on or before July 16, 1956”


*Approved July 26, 1955.*

# Public Law 206

> AN ACT To repeal certain laws relating to timber and stone on the public domain.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, subject to valid existing rights and claims, sections 1 to 3, inclusive, of the Act of June 3, 1878 (20 Stat. 89), as amended (43 U. S. C., secs. 311, 312, and 313), are hereby repealed.


*Approved August 1, 1955.*

# Public Law 37

> AN ACT To amend section 402 of the Federal Employees Uniform Allowance Aet, approved September 1, 1954.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 402 of the Federal Employees Uniform Allowance Act, approved September 1, 1954 (68 Stat. 1114), is amended by striking from the first sentence thereof the words “existing on the date of enactment of this Act”.


*Approved May 13, 1955.*

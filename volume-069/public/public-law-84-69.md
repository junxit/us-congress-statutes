# Public Law 69

> JOINT RESOLUTION Extending an invitation to the International Olympic Committee to hold the 1960 Winter Olympic Games at Squaw Valley, California.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That whereas the United States Olympic Association will invite the International Olympic Committee to hold the Winter Olympic Games in the United States at Squaw Valley, California, in 1960, the Government of the United States joins in the invitation of the United States Olympic Association to the International Olympic Committee to hold the 1960 Winter Olympic Games in the United States at Squaw Valley, California; and expresses the sincere hope that the United States will be selected as the site.

## § 2.

The Secretary of State is directed to transmit a copy of this joint resolution to the International Olympic Committee.


*Approved June 13, 1955.*

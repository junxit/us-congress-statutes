# Public Law 145

> AN ACT To repeal the Act of January 19, 1929 (ch. 86, 45 Stat. 1090), entitled “An Act to limit the date of filing claims for retainer pay”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of January 19, 1929 (ch. 86, 45 Stat. 1090), is hereby repealed.


*Approved July 12, 1955.*

# Public Law 45

> AN ACT To amend the Act establishing a Commission of Fine Arts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2 of the Act entitled “An Act establishing a Commission of Fine Arts”, approved May 17, 1910 (40 U. S. C., secs. 104–106), is amended to read as follows:

> ## § 2.
>
> That to meet the expenses made necessary by this Act an expenditure of not exceeding $35,000 a year is hereby authorized.”


*Approved May 25, 1955.*

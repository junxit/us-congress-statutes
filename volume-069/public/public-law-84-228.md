# Public Law 228

> AN ACT To increase the mileage allowance of United States marshals and their deputies from 7 cents per mile to 10 cents per mile.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph (3) of section 553 of title 28, United States Code, is amended by striking out “7 cents” and inserting in lieu thereof “10 cents”.


*Approved August 4, 1955.*

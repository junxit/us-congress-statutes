# Public Law 62

> AN ACT To amend section 2 of the Act of March 2, 1945, pertaining to the Columbia Hi ver at Bonneville, Oregon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2 of the River and Harbor Act approved March 2, 1945, pertaining to the Columbia River at Bonneville, Oregon, is hereby amended by striking out the amount “$50,000” and substituting in lieu thereof “$185,000.”


*Approved June 8, 1955.*

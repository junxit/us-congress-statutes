# Public Law 102

> AN ACT To repeal a service charge of 10 cents per sheet of one hundred words, for making out and authenticating copies of records in the Department of State.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 213 of the Revised Statutes (5 U. S. C. 166) shall be and the same is hereby repealed.


*Approved June 28, 1955.*

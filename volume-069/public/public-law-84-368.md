# Public Law 368

> AN ACT To amend section 303 of the Career Compensation Act of 1949, to authorize travel and transportation allowances, and transportation of dependents and of baggage and household effects to the homes of their selection fur certain members of the uniformed services, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 303 (a) of the Career Compensation Act of 1949 (63 Stat. 813) is amended by inserting the following sentence immediately after the first sentence thereof:

> “Under uniform regulations prescribed by the Secretaries concerned, a member of the uniformed services who— may select his home for the purposes of the travel and transportation allowances payable under this subsection.”
> - **(1)** is retired for physical disability or placed upon the temporary disability retired list; or
> - **(2)** is retired with pay for any other reason, or is discharged with severance pay, immediately following at least eight years of continuous active duty (no single break therein of more than ninety days);

## § 2.

Section 303 (e) of the Career Compensation Act of 1949 (63 Stat. 814) is amended by inserting the following sentences at the end thereof:

> “Under uniform regulations prescribed by the Secretaries concerned, a member of the uniformed services who— is entitled to transportation for bis dependents and for his baggage and household effects to the borne selected for allowance purposes under subsection (a) of this section.”
> - **(1)** is retired for physical disability or placed on the temporary disability retired list; or
> - **(2)** is retired with pay for any other reason, or is discharged with severance pay, immediately following at least eight years of continuous active duty (no single break therein of more than ninety days);

## § 3.

This Act shall be effective from April 1, 1951. No additional amount may be paid to members of the uniformed services as a result of enactment of this amendatory Act unless travel to such selected home be performed on or prior to April 28, 1953, or within one year after such retirement, placement upon the temporary disability retired list, or discharge, whichever is later.


*Approved August 11, 1955.*

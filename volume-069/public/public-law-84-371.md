# Public Law 371

> AN ACT To increase the annuities of certain retired civilian members of the teaching staffs of the United States Naval Academy and the United States Naval Postgraduate School.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of January 16, 1936 (49 Stat. 1092), as amended, is further amended by adding thereto the following new section:

> ## § 7.
>
> - **(a)** The annuities payable under this Act to civilian members of the teaching staffs of the United States Naval Academy and the United States Naval Postgraduate School retired before April 1, 1948, are hereby increased by $300 a year.
> - **(b)** In addition to the increase in annuities authorized by subsection (a), the annuities payable under this Act to all civilian members of the teaching staffs of the United States Naval Academy and the United States Naval Postgraduate School retired before the date of enactment of this amendment shall be increased by $300 a year. No such annuity, however, shall thereby be increased to an amount in excess of $2,160.
> - **(c)** The increase in the annuities of retired members of the teaching staffs of the United States Naval Academy and the United States Naval Postgraduate School authorized by subsection (b) shall not operate to increase the annuities of their survivors.”

## § 2.

Applicable current appropriations shall be available to carry out the provisions of section 1 of this Act.


*Approved August 11, 1955.*

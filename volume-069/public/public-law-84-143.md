# Public Law 143

> AN ACT To repeal two provisions of law requiring that certain military personnel shall be paid monthly.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1268 of the Revised Statutes is repealed.

## § 2.

The last proviso in subtitle “pay” of the Act of August 30, 1890 (26 Stat. 400), is repealed.


*Approved July 11, 1955.*

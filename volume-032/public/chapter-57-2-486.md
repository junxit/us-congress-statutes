# Chapter 486

> An Act To restore to the active list of the Navy the name of John Walton Ross.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United-States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, to appoint John Walton Ross, surgeon, United States Navy, retired, to the active list of the Navy as a medical director (an additional number), not in line of promotion, and to retain his present longevity (from the date of his original commission as surgeon in the Navy), said officer having tendered his services to the United States.


*Approved, February 5, 1903.*

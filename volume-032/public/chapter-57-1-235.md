# Chapter 235

> An Act For the establishment of a light-house at the mouth of Oyster Bayou, near the Louisiana coast, in the Gulf of Mexico.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be established at the mouth of Oyster Bayou, near the Louisiana coast, in the Gulf of Mexico, a light-house, at a cost not to exceed five thousand dollars.


*Approved, March 21, 1902.*

# Chapter 518

> An Act To correct errors in dates of original appointments of Captain James J. Hornbrook and others.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That with a view to correct errors in dates of original appointments, upon their graduation from the United States Military Academy, the President is hereby authorized to cause the names of Captains James J. Hornbrook, William F. Clark, and Samuel G. Jones, of the cavalry, to appear upon the lineal list of captains of cavalry, in the order above named, next below that of Captain Frank M. Caldwell.


*Approved, February 7, 1903.*

# Chapter 1325

> An Act To amend section four of an Act entitled “An Act to provide for a permanent Census Office,” approved March sixth, nineteen hundred and two.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section four of an Act of Congress entitled “An Act to provide for a permanent Census Office,” approved March sixth, nineteen hundred and two, be, and the same is hereby, amended by inserting, after the word “watchmen,” and before the words “and charwomen,” where the same occur in said section four, the words “unskilled laborers.”


*Approved, June 30, 1902.*

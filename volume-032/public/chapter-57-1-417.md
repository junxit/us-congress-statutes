# Chapter 417

> An Act To amend section forty-eight hundred and eighty-three of the Revised Statutes, relating to the signing of letters patent for inventions.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section forty-eight hundred and eighty-three of the Revised Statutes be, and is hereby, amended so as to read as follows:

> ## § 4883.
>
> All patents shall be issued in the name of the United States of America, under the seal of the Patent Office, and shall be signed by the. Commissioner of Patents, and they shall be recorded, together with the specifications, in the Patent Office in books to be kept for that purpose.”


*Approved, April 11, 1902.*

# Chapter 853

> An Act Providing for the interment of the remains of Marie Irene Donaldson and her daughter, Marie Irene Donaldson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the health officer of the District of Columbia be, and he is hereby, authorized to issue a permit for the interment in the District of Columbia of the remains of the late Marie Irene Donaldson and her daughter, Marie Irene Donaldson, formerly residents of the District of Columbia and citizens of the United States, now interred at Puerto Plata, Santo Domingo.


*Approved, February 27, 1903.*

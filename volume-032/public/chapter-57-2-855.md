# Chapter 855

> An Act Authorizing the Secretary of the Interior to issue a patent to the city of Buffalo, Wyoming, for certain tracts of land.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to issue a patentto the city of Buffalo, Wyoming, for lots seven and eight, section three, township fifty north, range eighty-two west of the sixth principal meridian, embraced within the abandoned Fort McKinney Military Reservation, upon the payment by the authorities of said town of the appraised price of said lots.


*Approved, February 27, 1903.*

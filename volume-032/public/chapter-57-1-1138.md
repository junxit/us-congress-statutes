# Chapter 1138

> An Act To fix the fees of jurors in the United States courts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That on and after the passage of this Act the per diem pay of each juror, grand or petit, in any court of the United States, shall be three dollars a day instead of two dollars a day as now provided by law


*Approved, June 21, 1902.*

# Chapter 484

> An Act To authorize the President to appoint Brigadier-General H. C. Merriam to the grade of major-general in the United States Army and place him on the retired list.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, auu he is hereby, authorized to appoint, with the advice and consent of the Senate, Brigadier-General H. C. Merriam to the grade of major-general in the United States Army and place him on the retired list.


*Approved, February 5, 1903.*

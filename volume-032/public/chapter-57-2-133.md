# Chapter 133

> An Act Authorizing the Secretary of the Navy to return to Harvard University certain colors, silver cup, and Nordenfeldt gun.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United, States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy is hereby authorized to give to Harvard University the sixpounder Nordenfeldt gun, the set of colors, including ensign, jack, and pennant, and the silver loving cup presented by Harvard graduates to the auxiliary cruiser Harvard at the opening of the war with Spain: Provided, however, That said gun shall be returned, in like order as when received, to the Government at any time when it may be required for use.


*Approved, January 13, 1903.*

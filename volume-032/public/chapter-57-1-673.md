# Chapter 673

> An Act To amend an Act entitled "An Act granting the right to the Omaha Northern Railway Company to construct a railway across, and establish stations on, the Omaha and Winnebago Reservation, in the State of Nebraska, and for other purposes," by extending the time for the construction of said railway.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time presented by an Act of Congress approved the twenty-sixth day of March, eighteen hundred and ninety-eight, entitled “An Act granting the right to the Omaha Northern Railway Company to construct a railway across, and establish stations on, the. Omaha and Winnebago Reservation, in the State of Nebraska, and for other purposes,’’ for the construction of said railway be, and the same is hereby, extended for a period of three years from the twenty-sixth day of March, nineteen hundred and one.

## § 2.

That all other provisions of said Act are hereby continued in full force and effect.


*Approved, April 30, 1902.*

# Chapter 503

> An Act To provide for the extension of the charters of national banks.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller of the Currency is hereby authorized, in the manner provided by, and under the conditions and limitations of, the Act of July twelfth, eighteen hundred and eighty-two, to extend for a further period of twenty years the charter of any national banking association extended under said Act which shall desire to continue its existence, after the expiration of its charter.


*Approved, April 12, 1902.*

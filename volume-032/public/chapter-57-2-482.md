# Chapter 482

> An Act Authorizing the President to appoint Lieutenant Robert Platt, United States Navy, to the rank of commander.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and is hereby, authorized to appoint Robert Platt, lieutenant of the junior grade, United States Navy, not in the line of promotion, to the rank of commander, United States Navy, and to place him on the retired list of the Navy as such.


*Approved, February 5, 1903.*

# Chapter 539

> An Act To establish a fog bell and lens-lantern light on the southeastern end of Southampton Shoal, San Francisco Bay, California.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be established on the southeastern end of Southampton Shoal, San Francisco Bay, California, a fog bell and lens-lantern light, at a cost not to exceed thirty thousand dollars.


*Approved, February 10, 1903.*

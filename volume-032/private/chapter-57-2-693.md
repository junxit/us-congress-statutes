# Chapter 693

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Julia E. Jones.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized end directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Julia E. Jones, widow of James L. Jones, late second lieutenant Company C, Third Regiment Tennessee Volunteers, war with Mexico, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 18, 1903.*

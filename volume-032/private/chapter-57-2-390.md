# Chapter 390

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Sarah E. Ropes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United State of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the [tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah E. Ropes, widow of James M. Ropes, late captain, Eighth Regiment United States Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty-live dollars per month in lieu or that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 2, 1903.*

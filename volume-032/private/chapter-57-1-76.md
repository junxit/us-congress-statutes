# Chapter 76

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Tempy French.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior he, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name, of Tempy French, widow of John L. French, late of Company E, First Regiment New Hampshire Volunteer Heavy Artillery, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifteen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 27, 1902.*

# Chapter 795

> An Act Granting a pension to Samuel S. Beaver.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary’ of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Samuel S. Beaver, late of Company G, Third Regiment Tennessee Volunteer Mounted Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, May 17, 1902.*

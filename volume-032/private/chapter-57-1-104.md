# Chapter 104

> An Act Granting a pension to Katherine R. A. Ogden.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name or Katherine R. A. Ogden, widow of Charles C. Ogden, late second lieutenant. Thirteenth Regiment United States Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifteen dollars per month.


*Approved, February 27, 1902.*

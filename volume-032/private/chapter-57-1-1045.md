# Chapter 1045

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Charles Edward Price Lance, alias Edward Price.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Charles Edward Price Lance, alias Edward Price, late of Company E, Sixteenth Regiment New York Volunteer Heavy Artillery, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, June 7, 1902.*

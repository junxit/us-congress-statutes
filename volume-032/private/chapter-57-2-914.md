# Chapter 914

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Edmund P. Fox.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject, to the provisions and Invitations of the pension laws, the name of Edmund P. Fox, late of Company H, Sixteenth Regiment New Hampshire Volunteer Infantry, and Company D, Eighteenth Regiment New Hampshire Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 28, 1903.*

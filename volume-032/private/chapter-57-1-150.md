# Chapter 150

> An Act Granting a pension to Nellie Bartlett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Nellie Bartlett, widow of Eli V. Bartlett, junior, late of Company L, Second Regiment Massachusetts Volunteer Heavy Artillery, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month, and two dollars per month additional on account of each of the minor children of the said Eli V. Bartlett, junior, until they reach the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, March 10, 1902.*

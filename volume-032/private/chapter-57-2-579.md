# Chapter 579

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Asa Tarbox.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Asa Tarbox, late of Company F, Eleventh Regiment Massachusetts Volunteer Infantry, and pay hint a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 18, 1903.*

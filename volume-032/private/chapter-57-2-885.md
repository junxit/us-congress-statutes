# Chapter 885

> An Act Granting a pension to Ann M. Green.

*Beit enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of theUnited States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ann M. Green, widow of George H. Green, late of Company A, First Regiment Michigan Volunteer Engineers and Mechanics, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 28, 1903.*

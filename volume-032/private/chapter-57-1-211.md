# Chapter 211

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Thomas W. Robinson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representives United,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Thomas W. Robinson, late of Company G, Sixth Regiment Iowa Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 11, 1902.*

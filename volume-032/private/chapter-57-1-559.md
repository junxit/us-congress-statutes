# Chapter 559

> An Act Granting a pension to Amelia A Russell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roil, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Amelia A. Russell, widow of Michael Russell, late first lieutenant Company I, One hundred and sixty-ninth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month.


*Approved, April 17, 1902.*

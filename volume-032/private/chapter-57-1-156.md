# Chapter 156

> An Act Granting a pension to Rebecca Dobbins.

*Be it enacted by the Sen ate and. House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Rebecca Dobbins, widow of Francis Dobbins, late of Company E, Sixty-eighth Regiment Kentucky Enrolled Militia, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 10, 1902.*

# Chapter 767

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to William Y. Turner.

*Be it enacted bp the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States cf America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William Y. Turner, late of Company F, Fifteenth Regiment Iowa Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 25, 1903.*

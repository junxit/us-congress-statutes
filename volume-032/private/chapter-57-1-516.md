# Chapter 516

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Alfred H. Van Vliet.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Alfred H. Van Vliet, late first lieutenant and adjutant Eleventh Regiment Michigan Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving, such pension to be paid to his duly constituted guardian.


*Approved, April 16, 1902.*

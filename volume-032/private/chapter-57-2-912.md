# Chapter 912

> An Act Granting a pension to Inez McCollum.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Inez McCollum, widow of Stephen A. McCollum, late first lieutenant and adjutant Sixty-fourth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month, and two dollars per month additional on account of the minor child of said Stephen A. McCollum until she reaches the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, February 28, 1903.*

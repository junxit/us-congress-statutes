# Chapter 713

> An Act Granting a pension to Jane Hale.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jane Hale, widow of Samuel Hale, late of Company M,. Fifth Regiment Michigan Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the late of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 19, 1903.*

# Chapter 285

> An Act Granting a pension to Joanna Rogers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Joanna Rogers, widow of Alma Rogers, late of Company C, Seventy-seventh Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, January 23, 1903.*

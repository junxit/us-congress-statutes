# Chapter 1135

> An Act Granting a pension to Mary E. Winterbottom.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary E. Winterbottom, widow of Harrison T. Winterbottom, late of Company A, First Regiment Eastern Shore Mary-land Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1903.*

# Chapter 857

> An Act Granting a pension to Helen F. Lasher.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Helen R Lasher, widow of Oren E. Lasher, late lieutenant. United States Navy, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month and two dollars per month additional on account of each of the minor children of said Oren E. Lasher until they reach the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, May 23, 1902.*

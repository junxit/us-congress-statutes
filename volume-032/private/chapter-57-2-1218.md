# Chapter 1218

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Louis W. Rowe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House erf Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the “provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Louis W. Rowe, late of Company C, Sixteenth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 3, 1903.*

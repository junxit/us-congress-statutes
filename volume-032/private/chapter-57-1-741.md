# Chapter 741

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Jethro M. Getman, alias James M. Getman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives if the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Gethro M. Getman, alias James M. Getman, late first lieutenant Company G, Forty-second Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, May 5, 1902.*

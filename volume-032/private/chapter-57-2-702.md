# Chapter 702

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to John Dinneen, now known as John J. Davidson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Dinneen, now known as John J. Davidson, late of Company G, Thirty-third Regiment Massachusetts Volunteer Infantry, and pity him a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 18, 1903.*

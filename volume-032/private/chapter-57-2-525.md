# Chapter 525

> An Act Granting to Nellie Ett Heen the south half of the northwest quarter and lot four of section two, and lot one of section three, in township one hundred and fifty-four north, of range one hundred and one west, in the State of North Dakota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the following-described land is hereby granted to Nellie Ett Heen, to wit: The south half of the northwest quarter and lot four of section two, and lot one of section three, in township one hundred and fifty-four north, of range one hundred and one west, situate in the Minot United States land district, in the State of North Dakota.


*Approved, February 7, 1903.*

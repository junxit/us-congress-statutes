# Chapter 880

> An Act Granting a pension to Elizabeth McDonald.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elizabeth McDonald, the helpless and dependent daughter of John McDonald, late of Company I, Fifth Regiment Connecticut Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, May 23, 1902.*

# Chapter 763

> An Act Granting a pension to Elise Sigel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elise Sigel, widow of Franz Sigel, late major-general, Jnited States Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of one inndred dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1903.*

# Chapter 234

> An Act For the relief of Edward Kershner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized and empowered to appoint Edward Kershner as medical inspector in the Navy on the retired list.


*Approved, March 20, 1902.*

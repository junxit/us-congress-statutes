# Chapter 174

> An Act Granting a pension to Joseph H. Woodruff.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House, of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Joseph H. Woodruff, late of Company E. Fourth Regiment Iowa Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of fifteen dollars per month.


*Approved, January 13, 1903.*

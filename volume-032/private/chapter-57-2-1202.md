# Chapter 1202

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Charles Strong, alias William Clark.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Haase of Representatives oftheUnited States of America iii Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Charles Strong, alias William Clark, late of Company D, One hundred and fifteenth Regiment, and Company C, Forty-seventh Regiment, New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 3, 1903.*

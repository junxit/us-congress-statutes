# Chapter 843

> An Act Granting a pension to Deborah Edwards.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Deborah Edwards, dependent stepmother of Samuel Edwards, late of the Fourth Independent Company, Ohio Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1903.*

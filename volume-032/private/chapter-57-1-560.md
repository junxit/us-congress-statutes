# Chapter 560

> An Act Granting a pension to Ruth Bartlett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roil, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws. the name of Ruth Bartlett, the dependent and helpless daughter of Sylvanus Bartlett, late first lieutenant Company H, Eighteenth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, April 17, 1902.*

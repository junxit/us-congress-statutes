# Chapter 1055

> An Act Granting a pension to Zebulon A. Shipman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Zebulon A. Shipman, late private, United States Marine Corps, war with Spain.


*Approved, June 7, 1902.*

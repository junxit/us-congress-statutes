# Chapter 448

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Lewis E. Wilcox.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension aws. the name of Lewis E. Wilcox, late principal musician, Twenty- first Regiment Michigan Volunteer Infantry, and to pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 4, 1903.*

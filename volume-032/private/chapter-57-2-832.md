# Chapter 832

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to John Hamilton, second.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the [tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jolin Hamilton, second, late of Battery A, Maryland Junior Volunteer Light Artillerv, and Company E, Sixth Regiment United States Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 25, 1903.*

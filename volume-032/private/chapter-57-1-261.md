# Chapter 261

> An Act Granting a pension to M. Callie Glover.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of M. Callie Glover, widow of Albert D. Glover, late major Third Regiment Missouri Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, March 21, 1902.*

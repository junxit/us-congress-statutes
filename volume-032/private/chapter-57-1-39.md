# Chapter 39

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Eunice P. Detweiler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Eunice P. Detweiler, widow of John S. Detweiler, ate major, Ninth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 27, 1902.*

# Chapter 96

> An Act Granting a pension to Helen M. Evans.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of American Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Helen M. Evans, dependent mother of William F. Evans, late of Company B, Thirty-fourth Regiment Massachusetts Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 27, 1902.*

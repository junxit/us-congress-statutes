# Chapter 362

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Winfield S. Piety.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Winfield S. Piety, late of Company F, Sixth Regiment Indiana Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that lie is now receiving.


*Approved, February 2, 1903.*

# Chapter 797

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Etta Adair Anderson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roil, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Etta Adair Anderson, willow of J. Patton Anderson, late lieutenant-colonel Battalion Mississippi Rifles, war with Mexico, and pay her a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per mouth in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, May 17, 1902.*

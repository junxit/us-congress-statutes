# Chapter 1294

> An Act For the relief of McClure and Willbanks.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to McClure and Willbanks, livery-stable keepers at Toccoa, Georgia, one hundred dollars, the value of a horse hired to internal-revenue officers and killed by illicit distillers in August, nineteen hundred.


*Approved, March 3, 1903.*

# Chapter 1141

> An Act Granting a pension to Arvilla N. Stocker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Arvilla N. Stocker, widow of Henry T. Stocker, ate sailmaker, United States Navy, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1903.*

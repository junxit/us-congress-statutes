# Chapter 900

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Austin H. Patterson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension lairs, the name of Austin H. Patterson, late captain Company A, Fourteenth Regiment New Jersey Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 28, 1903.*

# Chapter 794

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to DeWitt C. Bennett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the 1 pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of DeWitt C. Bennett. lite of Company F, Nineteenth Regiment Michigan Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, May 17, 1902.*

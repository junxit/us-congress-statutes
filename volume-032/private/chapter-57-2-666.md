# Chapter 666

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Jane P. Chester.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jane P. Chester, widow of George F. Chester, late colonel One hundred and first Regiment New York volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 18, 1903.*

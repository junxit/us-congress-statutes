# Chapter 235

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Jane Brosnan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representations of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jane Brosnan, widow of Timothy Brosnan, late of Company I, Thirty-seventh Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, January 22, 1903.*

# Chapter 1133

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Isaac Harris.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representative of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Isaac Harris, late of Company F, Second Regiment New Jersey Volunteer Infantry, and Company A, Seventh Regiment United States Veteran Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 3, 1903.*

# Chapter 1236

> An Act Granting a pension to Mary J. Adams.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary J. Adams, widow of John Adams, late of Company A, First Regiment Maryland Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month, such pension, however, to cease upon proof that said John Adams is still living.


*Approved, June 27, 1902.*

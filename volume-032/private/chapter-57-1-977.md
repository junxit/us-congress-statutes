# Chapter 977

> An Act Granting a pension to Jeremiah Horan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jeremiah Horan, late of Company A, First Regiment United States Infantry, war with Spain, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, May 31, 1902.*

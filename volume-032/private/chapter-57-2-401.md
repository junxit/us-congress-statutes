# Chapter 401

> An Act For the relief of F. M. Vowells.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in auditing and settlement of the claims of F. M. Vowells as an officer in Company H, Sixth Regiment of Kentucky Cavalry Volunteers, for his services he shall be allowed and paid the pay and allowances of a second lieutenant of said company from the first day of August, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, to the fourteenth day of July, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, less any amount paid him as such lieutenant.


*Approved, February 3, 1903.*

# Chapter 722

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Alexander F. McConnell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Alexander F. McConnell, late of Company K, One hundred and Twenty-first Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of forty-five dollars per month in lieu of that be is now receiving.


*Approved, May 5, 1902.*

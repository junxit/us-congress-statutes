# Chapter 1389

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to William S. Hosack.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior he, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the Pension roil, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William S. Hosaek, late of Company G, Seventy-eighth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, July 1, 1902.*

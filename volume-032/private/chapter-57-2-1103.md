# Chapter 1103

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to David K. Wardwell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Rewesentatives of the United States of Anterica in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of David K. Wardwell, late of Company E, First Regiment Massachusetts Volunteer Infantry, war with Mexico, and captain Company F, Fifth Regiment Massachusetts Volunteer Infantry, lieutenant-colonel Thirty-eighth Regiment Massachusetts Volunteer Infantry, and lieutenant-colonel, Veteran Reserve Corps, United States Army, war of the rebellion, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 3, 1903.*

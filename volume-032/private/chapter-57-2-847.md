# Chapter 847

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Grace E. Ash.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Grace E. Ash, widow of Reuben Ash, late first lieutenant Company E, Second Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 25, 1903.*

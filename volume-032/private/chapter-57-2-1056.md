# Chapter 1056

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Eliza Wende.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Eliza Wende, widow of Richard Wende. late of Battery B, Fifth Regiment United States Artillery, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 3, 1903.*

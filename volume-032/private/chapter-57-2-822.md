# Chapter 822

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to George Davis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George Davis, late of Company A, Seventy-eighth Regiment United States Colored Volunteer Infantry, and Company F, Ninth Regiment United States Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 25, 1903.*

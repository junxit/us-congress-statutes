# Chapter 704

> An Act Granting a pension to Shadrack I. Corbett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Shadrack I. Corbett, late of Company D, Third Regiment Tennessee Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month.


*Approved, May 3, 1902.*

# Chapter 1120

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Peter Dugan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Peter Dugan, late of the United States steamship Mound City, United States Navy, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, June 17, 1902.*

# Chapter 26

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Stephen Harris.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Stephen Harris, late of Company I, Ninety-first legiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and Company C, Seventh legiment New York Volunteer Heavy Artillery, and pay him a pension at the rate of fourteen dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, December 27, 1902.*

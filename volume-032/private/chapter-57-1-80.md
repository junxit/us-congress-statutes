# Chapter 80

> An Act granting a pension to Mary Tripp.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Tripp, widow of Charles M. Tripp, late of the frigate Columbus, United States Navy, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 27, 1902.*

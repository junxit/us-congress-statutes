# Chapter 787

> An Act Granting a pension to Rose O. Crummett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Rose O. Crummett, widow of George E. Crummett, late of Company D, Sixteenth Regiment New Hampshire Volunteer Infantry, and second lieutenant Company L, First Regiment New Hampshire Volunteer Heavy Artillery, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1903.*

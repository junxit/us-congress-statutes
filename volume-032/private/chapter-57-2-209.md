# Chapter 209

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Charles S. Ely.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representative of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Charles S. Ely, late of Company B, Forty-third Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, January 22, 1903.*

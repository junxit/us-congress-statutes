# Chapter 656

> An Act Granting a pension to Susan House.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Susan House, widow of Robert C. House, late of Company A, Twenty-ninth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, April 29, 1902.*

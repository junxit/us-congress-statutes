# Chapter 458

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Mary Morgan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Morgan, mother of Algernon Morgan, late of Company C, First Regiment Maine Volunteer Heavy Artillery, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, April 11, 1902.*

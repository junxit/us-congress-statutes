# Chapter 519

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Freeman R. E. Chanaberry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Freeman R. E. Chanaberry, late of Company K, First Regiment Tennessee Volunteer Cavalry, war with Mexico, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that be is now receiving.


*Approved, April 16, 1902.*

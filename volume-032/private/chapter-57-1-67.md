# Chapter 67

> An Act Granting a pension to Jane Taylor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension vs, the name of Jane Taylor, widow of William Taylor, late first lieu-tenant Company G, One hundredth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month.


*Approved, February 27, 1902.*

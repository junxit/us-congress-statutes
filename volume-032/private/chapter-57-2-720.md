# Chapter 720

> An Act Granting a pension to Sabina Lalley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized anti directed to place oh the tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sabina Lailey, dependent mother of Patrick Lailey, late of Company B, Seventeenth Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer: infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 19, 1903.*

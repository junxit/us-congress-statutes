# Chapter 62

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Henry B. Schroeder.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the. pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension Taws, the name of Henry B. Schroeder, late first lieutenant and captain, Third Regiment United States Infantry, war with Mexico, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 27, 1902.*

# Chapter 965

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Elbert H. Dagnall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws. the name of Elbert H. Dagnail, late of Captain Samuel Bone’s company, Georgia Volunteers, Creek Indian war, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 28, 1903.*

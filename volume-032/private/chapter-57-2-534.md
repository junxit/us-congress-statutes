# Chapter 534

> An Act For the relief of L. A. Noyes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one thousand eight hundred and nineteen dollars to L. A. Noyes, for services rendered as acting assistant Treasury agent at the island of Saint George, Alaska, from August first, eighteen hundred and eighty- six, to May thirtieth, eighteen hundred and eighty-seven, inclusive.


*Approved, February 9, 1903.*

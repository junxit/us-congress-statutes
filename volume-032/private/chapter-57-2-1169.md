# Chapter 1169

> An Act Granting a pension to Charles A. Warrick.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Charles A. Warrick, late of Company E, Third Regiment Nebraska Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain.


*Approved, March 3, 1903.*

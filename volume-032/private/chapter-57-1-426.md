# Chapter 426

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to La Myra V. Kendig.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place op the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of La Myra V. Kendig, widow of Harry S. Kendig, late of Company A, One hundred and sixty-fourth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, April 11, 1902.*

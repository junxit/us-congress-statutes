# Chapter 431

> An Act Granting a pension to the minor children of Daniel J. Reedy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the names of Daniel J. Reedy and Annie T. Reedy, minor children of Daniel J. Reedy, late chief machinist. United States Navy, and pay them jointly a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month, and two dollars per month additional on account of each of said children until they reach the age of sixteen years, from which date the pension of Annie T. Reedy, helpless and dependent child of said Daniel J. Reedy, shall continue at the rate of twelve dollars per month during the period of her helplessness.


*Approved, February 4, 1903.*

# Chapter 499

> An Act Granting a pension to Nancy Works.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Nancy Works, widow of Preston Works, late of Company D, Eighteenth Regiment Kentucky Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 5, 1903.*

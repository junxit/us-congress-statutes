# Chapter 1407

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Daniel Thomas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Daniel Toomas, late seaman, United States steam-ships North Carolina, Potomac, and Sabine, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, July 1, 1902.*

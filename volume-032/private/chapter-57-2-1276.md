# Chapter 1276

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to James Flanagan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of James Flanagan, late of Company I, One hundred and twenty-fourth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month’ in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 3, 1903.*

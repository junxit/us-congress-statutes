# Chapter 772

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Thomas J. George.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Thomas J. George, late of Company D, Fourth Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer Infantry, and major. Third Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 25, 1903.*

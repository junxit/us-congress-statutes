# Chapter 697

> An Act Granting a pension to Michael Devine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Michael Devine, late of Company A. Eighteenth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month.


*Approved, February 18, 1903.*

# Chapter 1134

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Alexander H. Taylor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of tins United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Alexander H. Taylor, late of Company E, Twelfth Regiment Connecticut Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 3, 1903.*

# Chapter 491

> An Act Granting a pension to Susan Kennedy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Susan Kennedy, widow of Thomas Kennedy, late of Captain Beauchamp’s company, Alabama Volunteers, Creek Indian war, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 5, 1903.*

# Chapter 841

> An Act Granting a pension to Emma D. Goslin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Emma D. Goslin. widow of Asher Goslin, late surgeon, Forty-eighth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1903.*

# Chapter 555

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Frances E. Scott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Frances E. Scott, widow of Charles H. Scott, late of Company II, Thirteenth Regiment United States Infantry, war with Mexico, and pay her a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, April 17, 1902.*

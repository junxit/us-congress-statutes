# Chapter 1032

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Elias P. Stearns.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elias P. Stearns, late of Company G, Seventy-second Regiment Indiana Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension tit the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 3, 1903.*

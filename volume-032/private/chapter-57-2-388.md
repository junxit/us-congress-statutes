# Chapter 388

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Mary B. Keller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary B. Keller, widow of Charles Keller, late colonel Twenty-third Regiment United States Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving, and two dollars per month additional on account of the minor child of the said Charles Keller until he reaches the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, February 2, 1903.*

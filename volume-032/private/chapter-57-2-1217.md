# Chapter 1217

> An Act Granting a pension to Mary H. Talcott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to thejorovisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary H. Talcott, former widow of George N. Macy, late lieutenant-colonel Twentieth Regiment Massachusetts Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1903.*

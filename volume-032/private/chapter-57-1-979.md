# Chapter 979

> An Act Granting a pension to Samantha B. Van Brocklin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior he, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Samantha B. Van Brocklin, widow of George S. Van Brocklin, late of Company C, Fiftieth Regiment New York Volunteer Engineers, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, May 31, 1902.*

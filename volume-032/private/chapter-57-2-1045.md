# Chapter 1045

> An Act Granting a pension to Catharine A. Sawdy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the ttension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Catharine A. Sawdy, dependent mother of Henry J. Sawdy, late first lieutenant Company I, First Regiment Michigan Volunteer Light Artillery, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1903.*

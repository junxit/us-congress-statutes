# Chapter 1245

> An Act Granting a pension to Ella F. Shandrew.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior he, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of EllaF. Shandrew, widow of John C. Shandrew, late colonel Fifteenth Regiment Minnesota Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1903.*

# Chapter 165

> An Act Granting a pension to Catherine Meade.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Catherine Meade, widow of William Meade, late of the Hospital Corps, United States Army, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month, and two dollars per month additional on account of the minor child of said William Meade until she reaches the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, March 10, 1902.*

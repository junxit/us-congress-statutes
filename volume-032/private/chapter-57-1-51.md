# Chapter 51

> An Act For the relief of John Emerson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to amend the military record of John Emerson, late a private in Company I, Nineteenth Maine Infantry, by removing the charge of desertion from said record and granting him an honorable discharge: Provided, That no pay, bounty, or emolument shall accrue by the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 27, 1902.*

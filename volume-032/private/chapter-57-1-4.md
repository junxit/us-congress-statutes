# Chapter 4

> An Act Providing for the free transportation of all mail matter sent by Mrs. Ida S. McKinley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all mail matter sent by the post by Ida S. McKinley, widow of the late William McKinley, under her written autograph signature, be conveyed free of postage during her natural life.


*Approved, January 22, 1902.*

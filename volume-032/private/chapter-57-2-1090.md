# Chapter 1090

> An Act Granting a pension to Ellen D. Campbell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name, of Ellen D. Campbell, widow of Jerome T. Campbell, late of Company B, One hundred and seventy-first Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1903.*

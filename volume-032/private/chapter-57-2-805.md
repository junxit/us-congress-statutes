# Chapter 805

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Henry R. Bennett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby; authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Henry R. Bennett, late of Company B, Twenty- second Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that lie is now receiving.


*Approved, February 25, 1903.*

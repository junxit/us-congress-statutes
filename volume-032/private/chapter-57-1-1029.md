# Chapter 1029

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Charles T. Crooker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Charles T. Crooker, late of Company H, Tenth Regiment New Hampshire Volunteer Infantry, and Company C, Fourth Regiment New Hampshire Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, June 4, 1902.*

# Chapter 279

> An Act To correct the military record of William T. Pratt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War Ire, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to remove the charge of desertion now standing against the record of William T. Pratt, late a private of Company A, Fifty-first Regiment Indiana Volunteer infantry, and substitute therefor “died November twenty-eighth, eighteen hundred and sixty-four.”


*Approved, March 27, 1902.*

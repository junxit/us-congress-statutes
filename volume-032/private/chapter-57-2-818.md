# Chapter 818

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Oscar H. Prink.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of Arn tv tea in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Oscar H. Prink, late of Company E, First Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 25, 1903.*

# Chapter 35

> An Act Granting a pension to Cappa King.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roil, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Cappa King, helpless and dependent daughter of John W. King, late of Company D, Eighth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, December 27, 1902.*

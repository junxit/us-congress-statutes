# Chapter 478

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Edward Thompson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Edward Thompson, late of Company B, Sixth Regiment United States Infantry, and ordnance sergeant, United States Army, war with Mexico, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, April 11, 1902.*

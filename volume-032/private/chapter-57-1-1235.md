# Chapter 1235

> An Act Granting a pension to Carrie B. Farnham.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension taws, the name of Carrie B. Farnham, widow of Wilson H. Farnham, late of Company F, Ninety-eighth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, June 27, 1902.*

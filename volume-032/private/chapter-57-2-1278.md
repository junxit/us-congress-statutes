# Chapter 1278

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Christopher G. Divers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject, to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Christopher G. Divers, late of Company H, Twenty-seventh Regiment Missouri Volunteer Mounted Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 3, 1903.*

# Chapter 1203

> An Act Granting a pension to Fannie Frost.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Fannie Frost, widow of Robert Frost, late of Company B, Seventh Regiment New York Volunteer Heavy Artillery, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, June 27, 1902.*

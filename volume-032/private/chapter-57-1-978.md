# Chapter 978

> An Act Granting a pension to Hannah T. Knowles.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Hannah T. Knowles, widow of William M. Knowles, late of the United States ship Independence, war with Mexico, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month, such pension to cease upon proof that the soldier is living.


*Approved, May 31, 1902.*

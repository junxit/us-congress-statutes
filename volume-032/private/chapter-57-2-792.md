# Chapter 792

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Ardenia Dillon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws. the name of Ardenia Dillon, widow of William P. Dillon, late captain Company D, Sixth and One hundred and forty-sixth Regiments Indiana Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 25, 1903.*

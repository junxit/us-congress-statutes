# Chapter 573

> An Act To correct the military record of Thomas J. Morman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and is hereby, authorized to correct the military record of Thomas J. Morman, captain E. R. Goulding’s company. Calhoun’s Mounted Battalion Georgia Volunteers, Mexican war, and grant him an honorable discharge, as of the date of June first, eighteen hundred and forty-eight.


*Approved, February 18, 1903.*

# Chapter 744

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Ellen J. Clark.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ellen J. Clark, widow of Henry W. Clark, late of Company K, Third Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving, and two dollars per month additional on account of the minor child of the said Henry W. Clark until he reaches the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, May 5, 1902.*

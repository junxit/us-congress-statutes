# Chapter 94

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Jeremiah Evans.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatively of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jeremiah Evans, late of Company E, Sixty-first Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 27, 1902.*

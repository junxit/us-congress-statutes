# Chapter 726

> An Act Granting a pension to William P. Featherstone.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William P. Featherstone, late of Company F, First Regiment United States Mounted Rifles, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, May 5, 1902.*

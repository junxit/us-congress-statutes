# Chapter 257

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to William Rheuby.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William Rheuby, late of Company A, Thirty-first Regiment Indiana Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at. the rate of twelve dollars per month in lien of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 21, 1902.*

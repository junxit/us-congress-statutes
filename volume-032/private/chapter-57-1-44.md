# Chapter 44

> An Act Granting a pension to Sarah McCord.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah McCord, widow of Marcus D. L. McCord, late of Captain Terry’s company, First Regiment Tennessee Mounted Volunteer Infantry, Cherokee Indian war, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 27, 1902.*

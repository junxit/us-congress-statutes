# Chapter 105

> An Act Granting a pension to Catherine Pflueger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Catherine Pflueger, widow of William Pflueger, late of Company B, One hundred and fifty-fourth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 27, 1902.*

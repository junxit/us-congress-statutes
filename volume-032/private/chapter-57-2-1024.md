# Chapter 1024

> An Act For the relief of George A. Rogers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be appropriated, out of money not otherwise appropriated in the Treasury of the United States, the sum of one thousand nine hundred and fifty-one dollars and one cent, to pay the damages inflicted upon George A. Rogers, a contractor with the. Government, while drilling from the lighter Daylight, in the East River. New York Harbor, said damages being occasioned by the running of the United States torpedo boats at an unwarranted and illegal rate, of speed.


*Approved, March 3, 1903.*

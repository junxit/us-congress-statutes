# Chapter 772

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Clifford Neff Fyffe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Clifford Neff Fyffe, widow of Joseph Fyffe ate rear-admiral, United States Navy, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, May 5, 1902.*

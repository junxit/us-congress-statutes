# Chapter 531

> An Act Granting a pension to Ida S. McKinley.

*Be it enacted by the Seriate and House of Representatives of the United States of America i. Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Ida S. McKinley, widow of William McKinley, late President of the United States, and pay her a pension at the rate of five thousand dollars per year, from and after the passage of this Act.


*Approved, April 17, 1902.*

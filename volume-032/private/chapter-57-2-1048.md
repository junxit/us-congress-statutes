# Chapter 1048

> An Act Granting a pension to Johanna Buse.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and di recited to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Johanna Buse, widow of Louis O. Buse, late recruit, United States Army, war with Mexico, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1903.*

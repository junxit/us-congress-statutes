# Chapter 49

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Daniel C. Knowles.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior he, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the 1 pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Daniel C. Knowles, late captain, Company D, Forty-eighth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 27, 1902.*

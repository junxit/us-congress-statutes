# Chapter 392

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Stephen J. Houston.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Stephen J. Houston, late of Captain Hollman’s company and Captain Arthur Burney’s company, Florida Volunteers, Seminole. Indian war, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 2, 1903.*

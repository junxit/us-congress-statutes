# Chapter 286

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to John Vale.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives if the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension taws, the name of John Vale, late of Company H, Second Regiment Minnesota Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 28, 1902.*

# Chapter 722

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Ada S. Kaempfer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roil subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ada S. Kaempfer, widow of Samuel O. Kaempfer, late assistant surgeon, Two hundred and eighth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month, such pension to cease upon proof that the officer is living.


*Approved, February 19, 1903.*

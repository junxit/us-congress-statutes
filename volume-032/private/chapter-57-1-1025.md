# Chapter 1025

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Hannah A. Van Eaton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name Hannah A. Van Eaton, widow of Flavel H. Van Eaton, late surgeon Eighth Regiment Missouri Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, June 4, 1902.*

# Chapter 1150

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to William King.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William King, late acting third assistant engineer ranking with midshipman, United States Navy, and pay him a pension at the fate of twenty-four dollars per mouth in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 3, 1903.*

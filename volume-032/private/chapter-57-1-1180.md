# Chapter 1180

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Emma F. Shilling.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Emma F. Shilling, widow of John Shilling, late first lieutenant Company H, Third Regiment Delaware Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, June 27, 1902.*

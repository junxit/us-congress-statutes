# Chapter 551

> An Act For the relief of William E. Anderson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to so amend the military record of William E. Anderson, Company H, Third United States Artillery, as to show him honorably discharged to date June eighteenth, eighteen hundred and forty-seven: Provided, That no pay, bounty, or other emoluments shall become due or payable by virtue of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 12, 1903.*

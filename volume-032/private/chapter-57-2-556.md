# Chapter 556

> An Act For the relief of Edward S. Grill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and ho is hereby, authorized to pay to Edward S. Crill. of Florida, the sum of one hundred and seventy-five dollars, for rent of building for post-office in Palatka, Florida, for the third and fourth quarters of the year eighteen hundred and eighty-six.


*Approved, February 16, 1903.*

# Chapter 130

> An Act Granting a pension to Cherstin Mattson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and 1 louse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Cherstin Mattson, widow of Hans Mattson, late colonel Third Regiment Minnesota Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, January 12, 1903.*

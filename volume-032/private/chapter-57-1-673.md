# Chapter 673

> An Act To correct the military record of Calvin A. Rice.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War is hereby authorized and directed to correct the military record of Calvin A. Rice, late captain Company G, Seventy-seventh New York Volunteer Infantry, and issue to him an honorable discharge from said regiment, as of January eighth, eighteen hundred and sixty-three.


*Approved, April 30, 1902.*

# Chapter 137

> An Act To authorize Commander James M. Miller, United States Navy; Surgeon Oliver D. Norton, United States Navy, and Mr. Edwin V. Morgan, formerly secretary of the Samoan Commission and now secretary of the legation of the United States at Seoul, Korea, to accept presents tendered to them by His Majesty the Emperor of Germany.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That Commander James M. Miller, United States Navy: Surgeon Oliver D. Norton, United States Navy, and Mr. Edwin V. Morgan, formerly secretary of the Samoan Commission and now secretary of the legation of the United States at Seoul, Korea, be, and they are hereby, authorized to accept, respectively, a gold watch and chain, a marine glass, and a silver writing table, tendered to them through the Department of State of the United States by His Majesty the Emperor of Germany, King of Prussia; and the department of State is hereby authorized to deliver the testimonials mentioned to them.


*Approved, March 1, 1902.*

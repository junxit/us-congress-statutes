# Chapter 1391

> An Act Granting a pension to Daniel L. Mallicoat.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension law-s, the name of Daniel L. Mallicoat, late captain Company N, Greene County, Missouri, Home Guards, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, July 1, 1902.*

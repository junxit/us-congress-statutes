# Chapter 743

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Enos Turner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Enos Turner, late of Company D, United States Mounted Riflemen, war with Mexico, and Company F, One hundred and fifty-third Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 20, 1903.*

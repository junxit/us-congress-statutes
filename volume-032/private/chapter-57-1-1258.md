# Chapter 1258

> An Act Granting a pension to Margaret Tisdale.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Margaret Tisdale, the former widow of Moses C. Bartlett, late of Company B, Thirty-fifth Regiment Massachusetts Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, June 27, 1902.*

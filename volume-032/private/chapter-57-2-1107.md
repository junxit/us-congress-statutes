# Chapter 1107

> An Act Granting a pension to Bridget Logan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Bridget Logan, dependent mother of William Logan, late of Company K, Second Regiment Massachusetts Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1903.*

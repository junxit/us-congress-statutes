# Chapter 680

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to John Smith.

*Be it enacted by the. Senate and Hanse of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Smith, late of Troop A. Fifth Regiment United States Cavalry, and Company G. One hundred and twenty-fifth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 18, 1903.*

# Chapter 675

> An Act Granting a pension to Bessie Ledyard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Bessie Ledyard, widow of John B. Ledyard, late of Company M, Fifth Regiment United States Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month and two dollars per month additional on account of each of the minor children of said John B. Ledyard until they reach the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, February 18, 1903.*

# Chapter 193

> An Act For the relief of John A. Mason.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the accounts of John A. Mason, collector of internal revenue for the second collection district of New York, with the sum of four hundred and thirty thousand two hundred and forty-nine dollars and eighty-three and one-third cents, being the value of internal-revenue stamps destroyed by fire at. the office of said collector, number one hundred and fourteen Nassau street. New York, New York, on the night of February eleventh, eighteen hundred and ninety-eight.


*Approved, January 19, 1903.*

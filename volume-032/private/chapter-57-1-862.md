# Chapter 862

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Henry E. Williams.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior he. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Henry E. Williams, late, first lieutenant Company B. Thirty-first Regiment Iowa Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month in lieu of that lie is now receiving.


*Approved, May 23, 1902.*

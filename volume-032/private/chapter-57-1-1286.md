# Chapter 1286

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Andrew E. Hicks.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assemble,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he. is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Andrew E. Hicks, late of Company D, Second Regiment Missouri Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, June 27, 1902.*

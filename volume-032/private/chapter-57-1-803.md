# Chapter 803

> An Act Granting a pension to Ada V. Park.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ada V. Park, widow of Andrew G. Park, late of Company A, First Regiment United States Cavalry, war with Spain, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month, and two dollars per month additional on account of the minor child of said Andrew’ G. Park until he reaches the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, May 17, 1902.*

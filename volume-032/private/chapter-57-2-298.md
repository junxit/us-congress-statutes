# Chapter 298

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Catharine A. Carroll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he. is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the, provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Catharine A. Carroll, widow of William Carroll, late of Company F, Seventh Regiment Iowa Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per mouth in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, January 23, 1903.*

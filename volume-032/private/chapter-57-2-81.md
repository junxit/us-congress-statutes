# Chapter 81

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Matthew B. Noel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he. is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the I pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Matthew B. Noel, late of Company C, Thirty-ninth Regiment Iowa Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, January 9, 1903.*

# Chapter 1428

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Nancy J. McArthur.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the tension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Nancy J. McArthur, widow of James R. McArthur, ate captain of Company G, Seventy-first Regiment Indiana Volunteer infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, July 1, 1902.*

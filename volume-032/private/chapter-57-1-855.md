# Chapter 855

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Uriah Garber.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

1 bat the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Uriah Garber, late of Company F, First Regiment Potomac Home Brigade, Maryland Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, May 23, 1902.*

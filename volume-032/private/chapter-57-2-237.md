# Chapter 237

> An Act Granting a pension to Minerva Robinson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Minerva Robinson, widow of James Robinson, late of Companies F and B, First Regiment Missouri Volunteer Engineers, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, January 22, 1903.*

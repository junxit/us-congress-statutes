# Chapter 762

> An Act To refund penalty to the Bank of Colfax, Iowa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to refund to the Bank of Colfax, Iowa, the stun of seventy-five dollars, penalty collected by reason of delay in the receipt of special tax.


*Approved, February 25, 1903.*

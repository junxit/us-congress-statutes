# Chapter 471

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Georgie Josephine Walcott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Georgie Josephine Walcott, widow of William H. Walcott, late captain, Seventeenth Regiment United States Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving: Provided, That in the event of the death of the invalid and dependent son of said Georgie Josephine Walcott the pension of the beneficiary under this bill shall be reduced to the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, April 11, 1902.*

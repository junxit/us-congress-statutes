# Chapter 835

> An Act Granting a pension to Mary Shelton Huston.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is herein, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Marv Shelton Huston, late an army nurse in the Medical Department, United States Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, May 23, 1902.*

# Chapter 1318

> An Act For the relief of S. J. Bayard Schindel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to came to be credited the accounts of Lieutenant S. J. Bayard Schindel, commissary Sixth Regiment United States Infantry, with the sum of seventy-seven dollars and thirteen cents, being for subsistence funds stolen from the commissary storehouse by unknown parties, and for which he was responsible.


*Approved, June 28, 1902.*

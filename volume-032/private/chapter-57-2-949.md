# Chapter 949

> An Act Granting a pension to Lavinia F. Poiron.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lavinia I. Poiron, helpless and dependent daughter of John C. Poiron, late of Company A. Sixth Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 28, 1903.*

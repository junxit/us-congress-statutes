# Chapter 798

> An Act Granting a pension to Rebecca Coppinger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Rebecca Coppinger, widow of William H. Coppinger, late of Company K, First Regiment United States Dragoons, Sabine Indian disturbance, and payner a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, May 17, 1902.*

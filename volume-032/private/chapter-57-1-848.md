# Chapter 848

> An Act Granting a pension to Dewitt Clinton Letts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Dewitt Clinton Letts, late of Company C, One hundred and thirty-ninth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, May 23, 1902.*

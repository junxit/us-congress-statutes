# Chapter 1292

> An Act Granting a pension to Fannie Reardon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Fannie Reardon, widow of Patrick Reardon, alias Patrick Dunn, late of Company B, Second Regiment New York Provisional Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, June 27, 1902.*

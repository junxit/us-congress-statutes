# Senate Concurrent Resolution 24

> irrigation investigations in california.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed three thousand two hundred additional copies of the bulletin entitled “Irrigation Investigations in California, Bulletin Numbered One hundred, United States Department of Agriculture, Office of Experiment Stations, nineteen hundred and one”, of which the thousand copies shall be for the use of the Department of Agriculture and two hundred copies for the use of the Senate.

Passed May 10, 1902.

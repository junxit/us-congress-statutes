# Senate Concurrent Resolution 34

> national sanitarium, hot springs, south dakota.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Committee on Enrolled Bills, in the enrollment of the bill (S. 593) “For the establishment, control, operation, and maintenance of a National Sanitarium of the National Home for Disabled Volunteer Soldiers at Hot Springs, in the State of South Dakota.” are hereby authorized to strike out the words “Branch Home” from line 12, page 1, and insert in lieu thereof the word “Sanitarium”.

Passed May 22, 1902.

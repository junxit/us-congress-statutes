# House Concurrent Resolution 28

> the louisiana purchase.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be published and bound six thousand copies of the State papers, and all correspondence bearing upon the purchase of the territory of Louisiana by the United States, including the treaty of purchase; four thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives and two thousand for the use of the Senate.

Passed May 13, 1902.

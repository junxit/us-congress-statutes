# Senate Concurrent Resolution 15

> interoceanic canals.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed three thousand copies of Senate Report Numbered One, from the Committee on Interoceanic Canals, one thousand for the use of the Senate and two thousand for the use of the House of Representatives.

Passed March 19, 1902.

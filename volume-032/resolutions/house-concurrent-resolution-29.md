# House Concurrent Resolution 29

> report of commission to the five civilized tribes.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed three thousand five hundred additional copies of the annual report of the Commission to the Five Civilized Tribes to the Secretary of the Interior for the fiscal year ended June thirtieth, nine-teen hundred and one, one thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives, five hundred copies for the use of the Senate., and two thousand copies for the use of the Department of the Interior.

Passed May 13, 1902.

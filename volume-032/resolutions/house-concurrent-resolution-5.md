# House Concurrent Resolution 5

> texas oil fields.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed at the Government Printing Office four thousand additional copies of Bulletin Numbered One hundred and eighty-four of the Geological Survey, lately issued, entitled “Oil and Gas Fields of the Western Interior and Northern Texas Coal Measures and of the Upper Cretaceous and Tertiary of the Western Gulf Coast,” by George I. Adams, one thousand for the use of the Senate, two thousand for the use of the House, and one thousand for free distribution by the Geological Survey.

Passed February 7, 1902.

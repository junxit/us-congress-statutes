# House Concurrent Resolution 22

> report of the governor of oklahoma.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Public Printer be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to print five thousand additional copies of the report of the governor of Oklahoma for nineteen hundred and one, and to deliver the same to the Department of the Interior.

Passed April 24, 1902.

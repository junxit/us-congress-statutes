# Senate Concurrent Resolution 16

> promotion of anatomical science, etc.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate the bill (S. 2291) “For the promotion of anatomical science and to prevent the desecration of graves in the District of Columbia.”

Passed April 1, 1902.

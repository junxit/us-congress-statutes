# Senate Concurrent Resolution 20

> central arizona railway company.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate the bill (S. 4363) “Granting the Central Arizona Railway Company a right of way for railroad purposes through the San Francisco Mountains Forest Reserve.”

Passed April 12, 1902.

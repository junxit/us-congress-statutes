# House Concurrent Resolution 48

> biographical congressional directory.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed, and bound in cloth, three thousand copies of a Congressional Directory which embraces the biography of all members of Congress from the Continental Congress to the Fifty-seventh, inclusive, compiled by O. M. Enyart, two thousand copies for the use of the House Representatives and one thousand copies for the use of the Senate.

Passed February 10, 1903.

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 17

> robert s. woodbury.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate the bill (S. 3910) “Granting an increase of pension to Robert S. Woodbury.”

Passed April 4, 1902.

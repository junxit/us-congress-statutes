# Senate Concurrent Resolution 47

> francis s. davidson.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate the bill (S. 1115) “For the relief of Francis S. Davidson, late first lieutenant, Ninth United States Cavalry.”

Passed February 3, 1903.

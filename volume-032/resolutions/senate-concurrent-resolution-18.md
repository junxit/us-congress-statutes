# Senate Concurrent Resolution 18

> abbie george.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate die bill (S. 1872) “ Granting an increase of pension to Abbie George.”

Passed April 4, 1902.

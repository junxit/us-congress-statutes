# Senate Concurrent Resolution 23

> land decisions and pension decisions.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Public Printer be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to print from stereotype plates and to bind two hundred copies each of volume ten and volumes twenty to thirty-one inclusive, Land Decisions, and volumes ten and eleven, Pension Decisions, for distribution and sale by the Secretary of the Interior.

Passed May 10, 1002.

## Marginal notes

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

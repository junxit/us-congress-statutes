# House Concurrent Resolution 6

> urgent deficiencies appropriation bill.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Clerk of the House of Representatives be directed in the enrollment of the bill (H. R. 9315,) “An Act making appropriations to supply urgent deficiencies in the appropriations for the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, nineteen hundred and two, and for prior years, and for other purposes” to insert the word thirteenth on page nine in line nine, in lieu of the word thirtieth.

Passed February 11, 1902.

# House Concurrent Resolution 21

> hannah t. knowles.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the House of Representatives the bill (H. R. 11418) “An Act to increase pension of Hannah T. Knowles.”

Passed April 12, 1902.

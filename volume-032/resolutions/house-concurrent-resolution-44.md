# House Concurrent Resolution 44

> adjournment of congress.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President of the Senate and the Speaker of the House of Representative be authorized to close the present session by adjourning their respective Houses on the first day of July, nineteen hundred and two, at five thirty o’clock post meridian.

Passed July 1, 1902.

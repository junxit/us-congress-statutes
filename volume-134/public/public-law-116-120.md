# Public Law 116–120

> Joint Resolution Approving the request of the Secretary of Veterans Affairs for a waiver under section 1703E(f) of title 38, United States Code.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Congress approves the request for a waiver under section 1703E(f) of title 38, United States Code, as submitted by the Secretary on December 6, 2019.


*Approved March 3, 2020.*

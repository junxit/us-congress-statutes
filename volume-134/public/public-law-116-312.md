# Public Law 116–312

> An Act To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 509 Fairhope Avenue in Fairhope, Alabama, as the “William ‘Jack’ Jackson Edwards III Post Office Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. WILLIAM “JACK” JACKSON EDWARDS III POST OFFICE BUILDING.

- **(a)** *Designation.—* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 509 Fairhope Avenue in Fairhope, Alabama, shall be known and designated as the “William ‘Jack’ Jackson Edwards III Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References.—* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “William ‘Jack’ Jackson Edwards III Post Office Building”.


*Approved January 5, 2021.*

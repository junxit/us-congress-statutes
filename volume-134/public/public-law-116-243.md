# Public Law 116–243

> An Act To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 315 Addicks Howell Road in Houston, Texas, as the “Deputy Sandeep Singh Dhaliwal Post Office Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DEPUTY SANDEEP SINGH DHALIWAL POST OFFICE BUILDING.

- **(a)** *Designation.—* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 315 Addicks Howell Road in Houston, Texas, shall be known and designated as the “Deputy Sandeep Singh Dhaliwal Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References.—* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Deputy Sandeep Singh Dhaliwal Post Office Building”.


*Approved December 21, 2020.*

# Public Law 116–225

> Joint Resolution Making further continuing appropriations for fiscal year 2021, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

This joint resolution may be cited as the “Further Additional Continuing Appropriations Act, 2021”.

That the Continuing Appropriations Act, 2021 (division A of Public Law 116–159) is further amended by striking the date specified in section 106(3) and inserting “December 20, 2020”.


*Approved December 18, 2020.*

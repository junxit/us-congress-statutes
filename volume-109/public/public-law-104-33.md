# Public Law 104–33

> An Act To make the reporting deadlines for studies conducted in Federal court demonstration districts consistent with the deadlines for pilot districts, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. EXTENSION OF CIVIL JUSTICE EXPENSE AND DELAY REDUCTION DEMONSTRATION PROGRAMS.

Section 104 of the Civil Justice Reform Act of 1990 (28 U.S.C. 471 note) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (a)(1) by striking “4-year period” and inserting “5-year period”; and
- **(2)** in subsection (d) by striking “December 31, 1995,” and inserting “December 31, 1996,”.


*Approved October 3, 1995.*

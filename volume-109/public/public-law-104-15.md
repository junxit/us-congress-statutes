# Public Law 104–15

> An Act To reauthorize appropriations for the Navajo-Hopi Relocation Housing Program.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. REAUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS FOR THE NAVAJO-HOPI RELOCATION HOUSING PROGRAM.

Section 25(a)(8) of Public Law 93–531 (25 U.S.C. 640d–24(a)(8)) is amended by striking “1989,” and all that follows through “and 1995.” and inserting “1995, 1996, and 1997.”.


*Approved June 21, 1995.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 19

> ENROLLMENT CORRECTION—H.R. 483

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That, in the enrollment of the bill (H.R. 483) to amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to permit medicare select policies to be offered in all States, and for other purposes, the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall make the following correction: Amend the title so as to read as follows: “An Act to amend the Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act of 1990 to permit medicare select policies to Be offered in all States.”.


*Agreed to June 30, 1995.*

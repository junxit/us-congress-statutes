# House Concurrent Resolution 20

> DAYS OF REMEMBRANCE OF VICTIMS OF THE HOLOCAUST COMMEMORATION CEREMONIES—CAPITOL ROTUNDA AUTHORIZATION

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the rotunda of the Capitol is authorized to be used from 8 o’clock ante meridiem until 3 o’clock post meridiem on April 27, 1995, for ceremonies as part of the commemoration of the days of remembrance of victims of the Holocaust. Physical preparations for the ceremonies shall be carried out in accordance with such conditions as the Architect of the Capitol may prescribe.


*Agreed to March 1, 1995.*

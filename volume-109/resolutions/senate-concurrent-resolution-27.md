# Senate Concurrent Resolution 27

> ENROLLMENT CORRECTION—H.R. 402

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the action of the Speaker of the House of Representatives and the President pro tempore of the Senate in signing the bill (H.R. 402) is rescinded, and the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall, in the reenrollment of the bill, make the following correction:

Strike section 109.


*Agreed to October 17, 1995.*

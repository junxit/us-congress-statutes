# House Concurrent Resolution 28

> adjournment

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Tuesday the 21st day of December 1937, and that when they adjourn on said day they stand adjourned sine die.


*Passed, December 21, 1937.*

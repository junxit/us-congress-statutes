# Chapter 5

> AN ACT To extend the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Tennessee River between Colbert County and Lauderdale County, Alabama.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Tennessee River between Colbert County and Lauderdale County in the State of Alabama, authorized to be built by the State of Alabama, its agent or agencies; Colbert County and Lauderdale County in the State of Alabama; the city of Sheffield, Colbert County, Alabama; the city of Florence, Lauderdale County, Alabama; and the Highway Bridge Commission, Incorporated, of Alabama, or any two of them, or either of them, by an Act of Congress approved June 12, 1934, and extended August 23, 1935, and May 1, 1936, as amended, are hereby further extended one and three years respectively, from the date of the approval of this Act.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, December 22, 1937.*

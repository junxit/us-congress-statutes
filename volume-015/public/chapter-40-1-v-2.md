# Chapter V

> An Act to provide for the Exemption of Cotton from internal Tax.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all cotton grown in the United States after the year eighteen hundred and sixty-seven shall be exempt from internal tax; and cotton imported from foreign countries on and after November first, eighteen hundred and sixty-eight, shall be exempt from duty.


*Approved, February 3, 1868.*

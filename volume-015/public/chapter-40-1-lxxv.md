# Chapter LXXV

> An Act to extend the Boundaries of the Collection District of Philadelphia so as to include the whole consolidated City of Philadelphia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the port of entry and delivery of Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, is hereby extended so as to include within its boundaries the whole consolidated city of Philadelphia.


*Approved, June 25, 1868.*

# Chapter XXI

> An Act for the Relief of Settlers on the late Sioux Indian Reservation in the State of Minnesota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all actual settlers, who have duly filed their declaratory statements under the pre-emption laws with the register of the proper local land office, upon the unsold lands now included within the limits of the late Sioux Indian reservation, in the State of Minnesota, shall be allowed two years from and after the passage of this act within which to make proof and payment for their claims, in accordance with the provisions of the second and third sections of the act approved March third, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, providing for the disposal of said reservation.


*Approved, March 6, 1868.*

# Chapter CCLXXV

> An Act relating to the District Courts of Utah Territory.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the governor of Utah Territory shall assign the district judges of said Territory to their respective districts, and appoint the time and place of holding court in each of said districts, not exceeding two terms in each district in any one year.


*Approved, July 27, 1868.*

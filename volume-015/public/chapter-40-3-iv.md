# Chapter IV

> An Act to amend an Act entitled “An Act imposing Taxes on distilled Spirits and Tobacco, and for other Purposes,” approved July twentieth, eighteen hundred and sixty-eight.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the seventy-eighth section of “An act imposing taxes on distilled spirits and tobacco, and for other purposes,” approved July twentieth, eighteen hundred and sixty-eight, be, and the same is hereby, amended by striking out the words “first day of January,” wherever they occur in said section, and inserting in lieu thereof the words “fifteenth day of February.”


*Approved, December 22, 1868.*

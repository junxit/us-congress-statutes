# Chapter XXIII

> An Act extending to the State of Nebraska the Provisions of an Act relating to Agricultural Colleges.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the grant made by law of the second day of July, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, to each State, of land equal to thirty thousand acres for each of its senators and representatives in Congress, for the purpose of establishing agricultural colleges, is extended to the State of Nebraska in the same manner as if Nebraska had been a State of the Union at the date of the passage of said law.


*Approved, March 30, 1867.*

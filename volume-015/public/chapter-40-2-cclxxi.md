# Chapter CCLXXI

> An Act to disapprove an Act of the Legislative Assembly of Washington Territory redistricting the Territory and reassigning the Judges thereto.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the act of the legislative assembly of the Territory of Washington, approved January twenty-five, eighteen hundred and sixty-eight, entitled “An act defining the several judicial districts of the Territory and assigning the judges thereto,” be, and the same is hereby, disapproved.


*Approved, July 27, 1868.*

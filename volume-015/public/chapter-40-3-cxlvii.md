# Chapter CXLVII

> An Act to regulate Elections in Washington and Idaho Territories.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That elections in the Territories of Washington and Idaho for delegates to the House of Representatives of the forty-second Congress shall be held on the first Monday of June, anno Domini eighteen hundred and seventy, and afterwards biennially on the first Monday of June; and such officers in said Territories as are now elected at the same time with their delegates shall be elected for offices thereafter to be filled at the times herein specified, unless otherwise provided by the laws of said Territory.


*Approved, March 3, 1869.*

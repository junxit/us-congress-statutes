# Chapter CCLXX

> An Act regulating the Times and Places of holding the District and Circuits Courts of the United States for the Northern District of Florida.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times and places of holding the United States district and circuit courts for the northern district of Florida shall hereafter be as follows: At Jacksonville, on the first Monday of December; at Tallahassee, on the first Monday of Feb ruary; and at Pensacola, on the first Monday of March.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the terms of the United States courts heretofore held at Saint Augustine and Appalachicola be hereafter discontinued.


*Approved, July 27, 1868.*

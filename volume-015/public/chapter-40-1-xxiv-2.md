# Chapter XXIV

> An Act for the temporary Relief of destitute People in the District of Columbia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representati0ves of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of fifteen thousand dollars be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the temporary relief of the destitute population in the District of Columbia, to be expended under the supervision and direction of the commissioner of refugees, freedmen, and abandoned lands: Provided, That as far as practical [practicable] said sum shall be expended in the employment of persons upon the public grounds and works in the District of Columbia for which appropriations have been heretofore made by Congress, such laborers to be under the direction of the officer acting as commissioner of public buildings and grounds.


*Approved, March 10, 1868.*

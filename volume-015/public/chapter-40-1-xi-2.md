# Chapter XI

> An Act in Relation to additional Bounty.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, if any person or persons entitled to the bounty provided by sections twelve and thirteen of the act making appropriations for the civil service, approved July twenty-eight, eighteen hundred and sixty-six, shall have died or shall die before receiving said bounty, it shall be paid to the heirs of the soldiers as designated in said act in the order therein named, and to none other.


*Approved, February 21, 1868.*

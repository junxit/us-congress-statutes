# Chapter III

> An Act to repeal a Joint Resolution entitled “A Resolution to provide for the Removal of the Wreck of the Steamship Scotland,” approved January twenty-ninth, eighteen hundred and sixty-seven.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the joint resolution entitled “A resolution to provide for the removal of the wreck of the steamship Scotland,” approved January twenty-ninth, eighteen hundred and sixty-seven, be, and the same is hereby, repealed.


*Approved, March 16, 1867.*

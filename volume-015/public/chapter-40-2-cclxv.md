# Chapter CCLXV

> An Act to pay for indexing the Tax Bill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Clerk of the House of Representatives be, and he is hereby, authorized to pay out of the contingent fund of the House of Representatives, to the Clerk of the Committee of Ways and Means, one hundred dollars for preparing, by order of the House, a full index of the “Act imposing taxes on distilled spirits and tobacco, and for other purposes,” approved July twentieth, eighteen hundred and sixty-eight.


*Approved, July 27, 1868.*

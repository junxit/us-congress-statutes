# Chapter LXXXVII

> An Act increasing the Pension of William J. Patton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William J. Patton, late of the fourth regiment of Arkansas cavalry, and pay him a pension as first lieutenant in lieu of the pension he is now and has been receiving, commencing from his discharge from the service of the United States.


*Approved, March 1, 1869.*

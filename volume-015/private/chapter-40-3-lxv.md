# Chapter LXV

> An Act granting a Pension to Mrs. Naomi Adams.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior cause the name of Mrs. Naomi Adams, mother of G. W. Adams, late a private in company G, thirty-ninth Missouri infantry volunteers, to be placed on the pension rolls at the rate of eight dollars per month, to commence September twenty-seventh, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, and to continue during her widowhood.


*Approved, March 1, 1869.*

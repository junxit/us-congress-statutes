# Chapter CCCXLV

> An Act for the Relief of Nancy Cook, of Tennessee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, directed and authorized to place the name of Nancy Cook, of Johnson County, Tennessee, widow of Alexander Cook, on the pension roll at the rate of eight dollars per month, commencing on the sixth day of August, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, and to continue during her widowhood.


*Approved, July 27, 1868.*

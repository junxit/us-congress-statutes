# Chapter LXXXIV

> An Act granting a Pension to John R. Ray.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John R. Ray, a resident of Caldwell county, Missouri, who was wounded while serving in an organization known as the six months militia of Missouri, and pay him a pension of a private from January first, eighteen hundred and sixty-two.


*Approved, March 1, 1869.*

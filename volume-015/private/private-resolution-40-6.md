# Private Resolution 6

> A Resolution authorizing Rear-Admiral H. K. Thatcher to accept a Decoration from the King of the Hawaiian Islands.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Rear-Admiral H. K. Thatcher, of the navy of the United States, may accept a decoration of the order of Kamahamaha First, which has be[e]n tendered him by the King of the Hawaiian Islands, as an evidence of his appreciation of that officer.


*Approved, January 11, 1868.*

# Chapter I

> An Act to relieve from Disabilities Franklin J. Moses, a Citizen of South Carolina.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each house concurring therein,)*

That all political disabilities imposed on Franklin J. Moses, a citizen of South Carolina, by reason of the third section of the fourteenth article of the amendments to the Constitution of the United States, be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, December 11, 1868.*

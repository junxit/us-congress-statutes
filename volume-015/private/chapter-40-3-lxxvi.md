# Chapter LXXVI

> An Act granting a Pension to Edward W. White.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Edward W. White, late a private in the first troop of Philadelphia city cavalry, and pay him the pension of a private, commencing July thirtieth, eighteen hundred and sixty-three.


*Approved, March 1, 1869.*

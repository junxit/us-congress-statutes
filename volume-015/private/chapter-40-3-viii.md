# Chapter VIII

> An Act to relieve from Disabilities John G. Stokes, a Citizen of Alabama.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two thirds of each house concurring therein,)*

That all political disabilities imposed on John G. Stokes, a citizen of Alabama, by reason of the fourteenth article of the amendments to the Constitution of the United States, be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, January 8, 1869.*

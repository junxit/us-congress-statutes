# Chapter CLXXIV

> An Act granting a Pension to John W. Harris.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place upon the pension rolls the name of John W. Harris, late a pilot in the service of the United States, and to allow and pay to him, from the naval pension fund, a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month, the same to commence on the fifteenth day of April, eighteen hundred and sixty-three.


*Approved, July 13, 1868.*

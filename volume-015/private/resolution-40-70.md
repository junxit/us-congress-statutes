# Resolution 70

> A Resolution for the Restoration of Commander Aaron K. Hughes, United States Navy, to the active List from the retired List.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be authorized to nominate, and by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, to appoint Commander Aaron K. Hughes to the active list of the navy, with the rank to which he may be entitled therein.


*Approved, July 23, 1868.*

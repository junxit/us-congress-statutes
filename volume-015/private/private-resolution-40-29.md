# Private Resolution 29

> A Resolution for the Relief of George W. Doty, a Commander in the United States Navy on the retired List.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of George W. Doty, commander United States navy, be placed upon the navy register as a commander, from the sixteenth day of July, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, the date of his commission.


*Approved, May 19, 1868.*

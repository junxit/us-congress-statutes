# Chapter CCCLIV

> An Act granting a Pension to Hannah K. Cook.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Hannah K. Cook, widow of John M. Cook, late a second lieutenant in the one hundred and nineteenth regiment of Pennsylvania volunteers, commencing July twenty-eighth, eighteen hundred and sixty-five.


*Approved, July 27, 1868.*

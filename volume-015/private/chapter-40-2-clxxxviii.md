# Chapter CLXXXVIII

> An Act granting a Pension to the Widow and Child of John P. Fetty.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the names of Fannie Fetty, the widow, and Ethel May Fetty, child under sixteen years of age of John P. Fetty, late a private in company “I,” fourteenth regiment West Virginia infantry volunteers, and to pay her a pension, commencing October thirty-first, eighteen hundred and sixty-four.


*Approved, July 20, 1868.*

# Chapter CCCL

> An Act granting a Pension to Ruth Barton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ruth Barton, widow of Albert G. Barton, late a hospital steward, United States army, commencing April seventh, eighteen hundred and sixty-five.


*Approved, July 27, 1868.*

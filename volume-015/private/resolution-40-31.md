# Resolution 31

> Joint Resolution for the Relief of Mrs. Ella E. Hobart.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Ella E. Hobart, who was appointed as chaplain to the first regiment of Wisconsin volunteer heavy artillery, shall be entitled to receive the full pay and emoluments of a chaplain in the United States army, for the time during which she faithfully performed the services of a chaplain to said regiment, as if she had been regularly commissioned and mustered into service.


*Approved, March 3, 1869.*

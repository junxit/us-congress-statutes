# Chapter LXXVII

> An Act for the relief of George W. Short.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the paymaster-general is hereby directed to pay to George W. Short, late captain of company D, sixty-third regiment of Illinois volunteers, out of any money appropriated for the pay of the army, three months’ pay proper of a captain of infantry.


*Approved, March 1, 1869.*

# Chapter CXI

> An Act for the Relief of H. A. White.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the paymaster-general is hereby directed to pay to H. A. White, late captain of company C, third regiment of North Carolina mounted infantry, out of any money appropriated for the pay of the army, the full pay and allowances of a captain of infantry, from October fifteenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, to August eighth, eighteen hundred and sixty-five.


*Approved, March 1, 1869.*

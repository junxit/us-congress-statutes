# Chapter LXXXVIII

> An Act granting a Pension to Lorenzo Day.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pensions laws, the name of Lorenzo Day, late a private in company H, of the twenty-seventh regiment of Maine volunteer infantry, and pay him a pension, commencing January first, eighteen hundred and sixty-three.


*Approved, March 1, 1869.*

# Resolution 71

> Joint Resolution for the Relief of Peter M. Carmichael, Surveyor of the Port of Albany.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the Treasury Department be, and they are hereby, authorized, in auditing and adjusting the accounts of Peter M. Carmichael, surveyor of the port of Albany, to admit and allow the charge of one thousand and eight dollars, the same having been paid by him to John Hastings, deputy surveyor and inspector of said port.


*Approved, July 23, 1868.*

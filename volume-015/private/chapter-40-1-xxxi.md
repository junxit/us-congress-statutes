# Chapter XXXI

> An Act for the Relief of Malinda Harmon, of the County of Greene, and State of Tennessee, Widow of Jacob Harmon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act entitled, “An Act for the relief of Matilda Harmon, of the County of Greene, and State of Tennessee, widow of Jacob Harmon,” approved January thirty-one, eighteen hundred and sixty-seven be, and the same is hereby, repealed.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to place the name of Malinda Harmon, of the County of Greene, and State of Tennessee, widow of Jacob Harmon, on the pension roll, at the rate of eight dollars per month, to commence on the seventeenth day of December, eighteen hundred and sixty-one, and to continue during her widowhood.


*Approved, July 19, 1867.*

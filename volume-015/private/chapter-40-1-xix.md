# Chapter XIX

> An Act for the Relief of John Perry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of the act supplementary to the several acts relating to pensions, approved June six, eighteen hundred and sixty-six, shall apply to John Perry, of Illinois, a pensioner, by virtue of a special act approved March third, eighteen hundred and fifty-nine.


*Approved, March 29, 1867.*

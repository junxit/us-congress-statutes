# Chapter XXX

> An Act granting a Pension to Elizabeth J. Miller, Widow of General John Miller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place upon the pension rolls, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elizabeth J. Miller, widow of General John Miller, of Kentucky, and to allow and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month, to commence from the thirtieth day of August, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, and to continue during her widowhood.


*Approved, February 16, 1869.*

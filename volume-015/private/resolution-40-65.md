# Resolution 65

> Joint Resolution for the Relief of Henry B. Ste. Marie.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of State is hereby authorized and directed to pay out of the civil service fund of his department the sum of ten thousand dollars to Henry B. Sainte Marie, for services and information in the arrest of John H. Surratt, in the Kingdom of Italy, charged with the crimes of conspiracy and murder.


*Approved, July 20, 1868.*

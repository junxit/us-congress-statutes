# Chapter CXXII

> An Act to authorize the proper accounting Officers of the Treasury to settle the Accounts of Andrew S. Core.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury are hereby authorized and directed to settle and close the accounts of Andrew S. Core, late collector of internal revenue for the second district of Virginia (now West Virginia), upon principles of justice and equity.


*Approved, July 3, 1868.*

# Private Resolution 35

> Joint Resolution for the Restoration of Captain James F. Armstrong, United States Navy, to the active List from the retired List.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be authorized to nominate, and, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, to appoint Captain James F. Armstrong to the active list of the navy, with the rank to which he may be entitled thereon.


*Approved, June 11, 1868.*

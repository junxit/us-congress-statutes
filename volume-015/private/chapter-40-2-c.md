# Chapter C

> An Act for the Relief of Thomas Crossley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Thomas Crossley have leave to make application to the commissioner of patents for the extension of the letters-patent issued to him for improvements in machines for printing woolen and other goods for the term of fourteen years from the fifth day of April, eighteen hundred and fifty-four, said letters-patent bearing date the twentieth day of June in that year, in the same manner as if the petition for said extension had been filed at least ninety days before the expiration of said patent, and that the commissioner be authorized to consider and determine said application in the same manner as if it had been filed ninety days before the expiration of said patent.


*Approved, June 25, 1868.*

# Chapter X

> An Act for the Relief of Mrs. Emma Wilson, of the State of Indiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Hoitse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be authorized to pay to Mrs. Emma Wilson, of Indiana, one quarter’s salary of her deceased husband, James Wilson, as minister to Venezuela.


*Approved, January 14, 1869.*

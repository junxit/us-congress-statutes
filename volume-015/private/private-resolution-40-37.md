# Private Resolution 37

> Joint Resolution relative to the iron-clad Monitor “Camanche.”

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there shall be, and hereby is, appropriated for the purpose of paying Donahue, Ryan, and Secor, for losses sustained by them in the construction of the monitor “Camanche,” the sum of one hundred and seventy-nine thousand dollars; and the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby directed to pay the same out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 30, 1867.*

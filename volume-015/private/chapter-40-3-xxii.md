# Chapter XXII

> An Act for the Relief of Commander John L. Davis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officer of the treasury pay to commander John L. Davis two hundred and seventy-one dollars and ninety-one cents out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, February 2, 1869.*

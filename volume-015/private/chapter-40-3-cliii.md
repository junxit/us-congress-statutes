# Chapter CLIII

> An Act for the Relief of Captain Charles Hunter, United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be directed to pay to Captain Charles Hunter, United States navy, out of any monies [moneys] in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, pay as commander in the navy, on leave, from the twenty-second day of June, one thousand eight hundred and sixty-three, to the twenty-first day of June, one thousand eight hundred and sixty-six.


*Approved, March 3, 1869.*

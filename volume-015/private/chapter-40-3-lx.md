# Chapter LX

> An Act to confirm an Entry of Land by Moses F. Shinn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the entry by Moses F. Shinn, of the northeast quarter of section sixteen, in township fifteen, north of range thirteen east, in the district of lands subject to sale at Omaha, Nebraska, made on the twenty-second day of August, eighteen hundred and sixty-six, by cash certificate number one thousand nine hundred and thirty-one, be and the same is hereby confirmed.


*Approved, March 1, 1869.*

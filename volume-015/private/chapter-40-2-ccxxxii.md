# Chapter CCXXXII

> An Act granting a Pension to Martha Ann Wallace.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Martha Ann Wallace, widow of the late Brigadier-General W. H. L. Wallace, for pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month, from the tenth day of April, eighteen hundred and sixty-two.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the pension heretofore allowed said Martha A. Wallace under general law be discontinued.


*Approved, July 23, 1868.*

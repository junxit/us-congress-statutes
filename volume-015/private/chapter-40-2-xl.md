# Chapter XL

> An Act for the Relief of William Shunk.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid to William Shunk, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of six hundred and twenty-five dollars, being that amount due from the United States to E. C. Shunk, late postmaster at Cardington, Ohio, for salary as postmaster from January first, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, to March thirty-first, eighteen hundred and sixty-five.


*Approved, March 30, 1868.*

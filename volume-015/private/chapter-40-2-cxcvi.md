# Chapter CXCVI

> An Act for the Relief of Robert Ford.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be, and is hereby, appropriated, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of eight hundred and fourteen dollars to Robert Ford, in full payment for his time and services as a teamster in the quartermaster’s department of the army from May first, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, to August first, eighteen hundred and sixty-four.


*Approved, July 20, 1868.*

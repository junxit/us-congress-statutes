# Chapter CCX

> An Act granting a Pension to W. W. Cunningham.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of W. W. Cunningham, late a sergeant of company C, thirteenth New York cavalry, on the pension rolls, and to allow and pay him a pension at the rate of fifteen dollars per month, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, to commence from the twenty-fifth day of October, one thousand eight hundred and sixty-five.


*Approved, July 20, 1868.*

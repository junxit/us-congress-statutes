# Chapter LXXV

> An Act granting a Pension to Ellen Green.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ellen Green, mother of Philip Green, late a coal-heaver on the United States ship “E. B. Hale,” and pay her a pension, commencing June eleventh, eighteen hundred and sixty-three.


*Approved, March 1, 1869.*

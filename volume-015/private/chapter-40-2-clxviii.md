# Chapter CLXVIII

> An Act for the Relief of Julia M. Molin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to allow and to pay to Julia M. Molin, of Stockholm, Sweden, widow of Charles G. Molin, late a private in the second regiment of Minnesota volunteers, at the rate of eight dollars per month, from the eighteenth day of May, in the year eighteen hundred and sixty-two, the date of the death of her said husband, until the nineteenth day of December, in the year eighteen hundred and sixty-seven, the date of the commencement of the pension heretofore allowed her.


*Approved, July 13, 1868.*

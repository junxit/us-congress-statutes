# Chapter XCII

> An Act granting a Pension to Lucinda A. Wilder.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lucinda A. Wilder, mother of George C. Wilder, late of company I, one hundred and fifteenth New York volunteers, and William E. Wilder, late a private in company H, seventy-seventh New York volunteers, commencing September twentieth, eighteen hundred and sixty-two.


*Approved, March 1, 1869.*

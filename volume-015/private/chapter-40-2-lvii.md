# Chapter LVII

> An Act for the Relief of Thomas McLean.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Thomas McLean be, and he is hereby, authorized to enter and purchase so much of lot number one hundred and twenty-four as has not been disposed of in the Stockbridge reservation, in the county of Calumet, and State of Wisconsin, used and occupied by him, at the price stipulated in the third section of the act of third March, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, providing for the disposal of said reservation, and receive a patent therefor, the said McLean having cultivated and occupied the same for a long series of years.


*Approved, June 11, 1868.*

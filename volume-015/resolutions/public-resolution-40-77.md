# Public Resolution 77

> Joint Resolution relative to printing Specifications of Patents.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That no bills be paid by the treasury for printing specifications of patents above the contract price, except that seventy cents may be added to each thousand words for the additional cost of composition occasioned by change made in the printing by order of the commissioner of patents.


*Approved, July 27, 1868.*

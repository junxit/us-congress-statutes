# Resolution 40

> Joint Resolution authorizing Extensions of the Mail Steamship Service between the United States and China and Japan.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized to extend and improve the mail steamship service to Japan and China, authorized by act of February seventeenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, by establishing regular mail connections with such other seaports in China and Japan as will, in his judgment, promote the usefulness and efficiency of the mail service established by said act: Provided, That such extensions and improvements of the service are made without additional expense to the government.


*Approved, July 20, 1867.*

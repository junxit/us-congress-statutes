# Public Resolution 38

> Joint Resolution authorizing certain distilled Spirits to be turned over to the Surgeon-General for the Use of the Army Hospitals.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to deliver to the surgeon-gen eral of the army all the distilled spirits produced during the experiments made by the late commission for testing meters for the internal-revenue service, to be used for the army hospitals, and to be paid for at a reason able cost out of any moneys appropriated for the purchase of army hos pital stores, the amount received to be applied toward the expenses of said commission.


*Approved, June 25, 1868.*

# Public Resolution 5

> A Resolution for the Appointment of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the vacancies in the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution of the class “other than members of Congress” be filled by the appointment of Theodore D. Woolsey of Connecticut, William B. Astor of New York. John Maclean of New Jersey, and Peter Parker of the city of Washington.


*Approved, January 11, 1868.*

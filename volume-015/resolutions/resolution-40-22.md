# Resolution 22

> Joint Resolution authorizing the Second Auditor to Settle the Accounts of Officers of the Army in certain Cases.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second auditor be, and is hereby, authorized and instructed to audit and settle the accounts of line officers of the army to the extent of their pay for their services as such, due them from the United States, in all cases where such auditor shall be satisfied by affidavit of such line officer or otherwise, of their inability to make their monthly report or returns by reason of their having been prisoners in the hands of the enemy, or any accident or casualty of war, they have been unable to account for property in their possession.


*Approved, March 29, 1867.*

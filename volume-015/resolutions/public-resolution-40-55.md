# Public Resolution 55

> A Resolution in Relation to the Maquoketa River, in the State of Iowa.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the assent of Congress is given to the construction of bridges across the Maquoketa river, in the State of Iowa, with or without draws, as may be provided by the laws of the State of Iowa.

## § 2.

And be it further resolved, That dams and bridges may be constructed across the Iowa river, in the State of Iowa, above the town of Wapello.


*Approved, July 13, 1868.*

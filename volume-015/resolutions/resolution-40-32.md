# Resolution 32

> Joint Resolution providing for the Expenses of carrying into fill Effect an Act entitled “An Act to provide for the more efficient Government of the Rebel States.”

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sufficient money is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to defray the expenses of carrying into full effect in all its parts an act entitled “ An act to provide for the more efficient government of the rebel States,” passed March two, eighteen hundred and sixty-seven, with all its supplementary acts: Provided, That the amount shall not exceed five hundred thousand dollars.


*Approved, March 30, 1867.*

# Public Resolution 54

> A Resolution placing certain Troops of Missouri on an equal Footing with others as to Bounties.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the troops recognized in an act entitled “An act making appropriations for completing the defences of Washington, and for other purposes,” approved February thirteenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, be, and are hereby, considered as placed on an equal footing with the volunteers as to bounties, and that all laws relating to bounties be applicable to them as to other volunteers.


*Approved, July 13, 1868.*

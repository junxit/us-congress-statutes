# Resolution 15

> A Resolution concerning the Uniform of Persons in the Diplomatic Service of the United States.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all persons in the diplomatic service of the United States are prohibited from wearing any uniform or official costume not previously authorized by Congress.


*Approved, March 27, 1867.*

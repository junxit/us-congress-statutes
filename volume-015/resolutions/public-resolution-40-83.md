# Public Resolution 83

> Joint Resolution to aid in relieving from Peonage Women and Children of the Navajo Indians.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Lieutenant-General W. T. Sherman be, and is hereby, authorized and requested to use the most efficient means his judgment will approve to reclaim from peonage the women and children of the Navajo Indians, now held in slavery in the territory adjacent to their homes and the reservation on which the Navajo Indians have been confined.


*Approved, July 27, 1868.*

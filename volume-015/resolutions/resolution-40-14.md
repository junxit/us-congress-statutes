# Resolution 14

> A Resolution to make valid the Laws of New Mexico passed at the Session of the Legislature held at Santa Fé, from the third Day of December, eighteen hundred and sixty-six, to thirty-first day of January, eighteen hundred and sixty-seven.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the laws passed by the legislative assembly of the Territory of New Mexico, at its last session, which began on the third day of December, eighteen hundred and sixty-six, and ending on the thirty-first day of January, eighteen hundred and sixty-seven, and signed by W. F. M. Army, acting secretary and acting governor of said Territory of New Mexico, shall have the same force and effect as though the same had been approved and signed by the governor duly appointed, subject to the future revision and approval of Congress.


*Approved, March 26, 1867.*

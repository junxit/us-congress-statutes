# Public Resolution 68

> Joint Resolution admitting Steam Plows free of Duty for one Year from June thirtieth, eighteen hundred and sixty-eight.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of section two of the joint resolution approved March twenty-six, A. D. eighteen hundred and sixty-seven, respecting the importation of agricultural machinery free of duty, be, and the same is hereby, extended and shall continue in force for the further period of one year from the thirtieth day of June, A. D. eighteen hundred and sixty-eight, and that any such machinery shipped before the said thirtieth day of June, eighteen hundred and sixty-eight, or which may have arrived since that day, be exempt from duty.


*Approved, July 23, 1868.*

# Resolution 30

> A Resolution directing the Secretary of War to furnish certain Arms and Equipments to the State of Tennessee.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to furnish arms and equipments to the State of Tennessee, sufficient for ten thousand militia, to be accounted for by the State of Tennessee to the government of the United States.


*Approved, March 30, 1867.*

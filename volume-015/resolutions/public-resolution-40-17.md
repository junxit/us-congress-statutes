# Public Resolution 17

> A Resolution relating to the Survey of the Northern and Northwestern Lakes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second section of an act entitled “An act making appropriations for the construction, preservation, and repairs of certain fortifications and other works of defence for the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, eighteen hundred and sixty-eight,” ap proved March second, eighteen hundred and sixty-seven, shall not apply to the appropriation for the survey of northern and northwestern lakes.


*Approved, March 2, 1868.*

# Resolution 6

> Joint Resolution in Relation to certain Coin and Bullion on special Deposit in the Treasury.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the one hundred thousand dollars, or thereabout, in coin and bullion, now on special deposit in the treasury of the United States, after said bullion is converted by directions of the treasurer into coin, be paid into the treasury.


*Approved, March 22, 1867.*

# Resolution 22

> A Resolution more efficiently to protect the Fur Seal in Alaska.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the islands of Saint Paul and Saint George in Alaska be, and they are hereby, declared a special reservation for government purposes; and that, until otherwise provided by law, it shall be unlawful for any person to land or remain on either of said islands, except by the authority of the Secretary of the Treasury; and any person found on either of said islands, contrary to the provisions of this resolution, shall be summarily removed; and it shall be the duty of the Secretary of War to carry this resolution immediately into effect.


*Approved, March 3, 1869.*

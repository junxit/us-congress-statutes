# Public Resolution 84

> Joint Resolution relative to the Pay of the chief Clerk in the Office of the Sergeant-at-Arms of the House.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the clerk is directed to pay from the contingent fund of the House to the chief clerk in the office of the sergeant-at-arms the difference between his present pay and the amount voted him by a resolution of the House, passed June twenty-fifth, eighteen hundred and sixty-six, thereby fixing the salary of the clerk in the office of the sergeant-at-arms at twenty-five hundred dollars per annum.


*Approved, July 27, 1868.*

# Resolution 4

> A Resolution for the Relief of Freedmen or Destitute Colored People in the District of Columbia.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That fifteen thousand dollars be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the relief of freedmen or destitute colored people in the District of Columbia, the same to be expended under the direction of the commissioner of the bureau of freedmen and refugees.


*Approved, March 16, 1867.*

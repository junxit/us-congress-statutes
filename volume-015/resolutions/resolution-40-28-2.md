# Resolution 28

> A Resolution reappointing Louis Agassiz a Regent of the Smithsonian Institution.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Louis Agassiz, of Cambridge, Massachusetts, be, and he is hereby, reappointed a regent of the Smithsonian Institution to fill the vacancy occasioned by the expiration of his present term.


*Approved, March 3, 1869.*

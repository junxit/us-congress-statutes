# Resolution 16

> Joint Resolution in Relation to the Meeting of the House of Representatives at the first Session of the Forty-first Congress.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time for the first regular meeting of the House of Representatives of the Forty-first Congress be, and is hereby, postponed from twelve o’clock meridian, on the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and sixty-nine, to the hour of three o’clock in the afternoon of the said day.


*Approved, March 1, 1869.*

# Public Resolution 39

> A Resolution authorizing a Change of Mail Service between Fort Abercrombie and Helena.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster-General is hereby authorized to change the character of the mail service from Fort Abercrombie, Dakota Territory, to Helena, Montana Territory, to post coach service.


*Approved, June 25, 1868.*

# Public Law 110–21

> An Act To amend the Foreign Affairs Reform and Restructuring Act of 1998 to reauthorize the United States Advisory Commission on Public Diplomacy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. REAUTHORIZATION OF UNITED STATES ADVISORY COMMISSION ON PUBLIC DIPLOMACY.

Section 1334 of the Foreign Affairs Reform and Restructuring Act of 1998 (22 U.S.C. 6553) is amended by striking “October 1, 2006” and inserting “October 1, 2009”.


*Approved May 2, 2007.*

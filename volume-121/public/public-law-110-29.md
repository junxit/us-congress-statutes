# Public Law 110–29

> An Act To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 60 Calle McKinley, West in Mayaguez, Puerto Rico, as the “Miguel Angel García Méndez Post Office Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. MIGUEL ANGEL GARCÍA MÉNDEZ POST OFFICE BUILDING.

- **(a)** *Designation.—* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 60 Calle McKinley, West in Mayaguez, Puerto Rico, shall be known and designated as the “Miguel Angel García Méndez Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References.—* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Miguel Angel García Méndez Post Office Building”.


*Approved June 1, 2007.*

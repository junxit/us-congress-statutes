# Public Law 110–9

> An Act To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 1700 Main Street in Little Rock, Arkansas, as the “Scipio A. Jones Post Office Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. SCIPIO A. JONES POST OFFICE BUILDING.

- **(a)** *Designation.—* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 1700 Main Street in Little Rock, Arkansas, shall be known and designated as the “Scipio A. Jones Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References.—* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Scipio A. Jones Post Office Building”.


*Approved March 7, 2007.*

# Public Resolution 6

> Joint resolution explanatory of resolution approved January 31, 1868, entitled “A resolution limiting contracts for stationery and other supplies in the executive departments to one year.”

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the resolution approved January thirty-first,eighteen hundred and sixty-eight, entitled “A resolution limiting contracts for stationery and other supplies in the Executive Departments to one year,” shall not be held, or construed, to apply to, or include, mailbags, mail locks and keys, postal cards, postage stamps, newspaper wrappers, or stamped envelopes.


*Approved, March 24, 1874.*

# Public Resolution 17

> Joint resolution to fill a vacancy in the board of managers of the National Home for Disabled Volunteer Soldiers.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That James S. Negley, of Pennsylvania, be, and is hereby, appointed manager of the National Home for Disabled Volunteer Soldiers, in place of Jay Cooke, of Pennsylvania, resigned.


*Approved, June 23, 1874.*

# Public Resolution 3

> Joint resolution filling existing vacancies in the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the existing vacancies in the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution of the class other than members of Congress, shall be filled by the appointment of Asa Gray, of Massachusetts, in place of Louis Agassiz, deceased; J. D. Dana, of Connecticut, in place of Theodore D. Woolsey; and Henry Coppee, of Pennsylvania, in place of W. B. Astor; and John McLean, and Peter Parker, whose terms have expired, shall be reappointed.


*Approved, January 19, 1874.*

# Chapter 519

> An act to remove the political disabilities of A. S. Taylor, of Maryland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two thirds of each house concurring,),*

That the political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States on A. S. Taylor, a citizen of the State of Maryland, be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, June 23, 1874.*

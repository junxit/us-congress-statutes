# Chapter 71

> An act for the relief of L. S. Campbell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Quartermaster-General be, and he hereby is, directed to pay to L. S. Campbell, out of any money appropriated for the Quartermaster’s Department, the sum of two hundred and twenty-four dollars.


*Approved, March 26, 1874.*

# Chapter 216

> An Act granting a Pension to Davenport Downs—

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the mime of Davenport Downs, late a private in company G, fifth regiment Iowa volunteers, and to pay him a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

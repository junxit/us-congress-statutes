# Chapter 126

> An act to remove the disabilities of Charles H. McBlair, of Maryland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two thirds of each House concurring therein,),*

That all political disabilities imposed upon Charles H. McBIair, of Maryland, by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States, by reason of participation in the rebellion, are hereby removed.


*Approved, April 23, 1874.*

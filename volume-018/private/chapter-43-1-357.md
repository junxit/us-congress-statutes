# Chapter 357

> An act for the relief of Samuel E. Rankin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Paymaster General is hereby authorized and directed to pay to Samuel E. Rankin, late a first lieutenant of the ninth Tennessee, cavalry regiment, the full pay and allowances of a first lieutenant of Cavalry, from the fifth day of October, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, to February thirteenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-six.


*Approved, June 20, 1874.*

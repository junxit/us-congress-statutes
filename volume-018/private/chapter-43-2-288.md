# Chapter 288

> An Act for the relief of Lydia Cruger, executrix of Moses Sheppard, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the claim heretofore reported to Congress by the Court of Claims in favor of Lydia Cruger, executrix of Moses Shepherd, deceased, be referred back to said court, with power to hear and determine the same, as in other cases, and with the right to either party to use the evidence heretofore taken in said cause, so far as the said court shall deem the same pertinent and competent.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

# Chapter 279

> An Act granting a Pension to Margaret Pattison.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Margaret Pattison, mother of William J. Pattison, late second lieutenant of the ninth regiment Michigan Cavalry, and pay her a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

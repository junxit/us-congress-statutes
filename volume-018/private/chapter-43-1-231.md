# Chapter 231

> An act granting a Pension to Susan Bennett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and, House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Susan Bennett, mother of Peter V. Bennett, late a private in company I, one hundred and seventy-seventh regiment New York Volunteers, and pay her a pension as mother of said soldier from the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 6, 1874.*

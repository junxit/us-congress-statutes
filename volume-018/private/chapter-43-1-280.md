# Chapter 280

> An act for the relief of the heirs of Mary B. Belfield, of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one hundred and seventy-three dollars and thirty-three cents to the heirs of Mary B. Belfield, of Virginia.


*Approved, June 12, 1874.*

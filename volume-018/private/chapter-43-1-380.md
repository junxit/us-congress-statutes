# Chapter 380

> An act for the relief of William Walker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to William Walker, of Milton, State of Wisconsin, three hundred and seventy-four dollars and ninety-eight cents, for unpaid salary as vice commercial agent at Gaboon, from October first, eighteen hundred and seventy, to February twelfth, eighteen hundred and seventy-one.


*Approved, June 20, 1874.*

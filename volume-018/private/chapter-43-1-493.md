# Chapter 493

> An act for the relief of Burke and Kunkel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Burke and Kunkel, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, three thousand eight hundred and forty-nine dollars, being the amount of pig iron taken by the agent of the Treasury Department by mistake as Confederate property, July, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, and being the net proceeds which was covered into the Treasury of the United States to the credit of the captured and abandoned property fund.


*Approved, June 23, 1874.*

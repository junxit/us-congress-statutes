# Chapter 352

> An act for the relief of William B. Morgan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of fifty dollars be, and is hereby authorized to be paid to William B. Morgan, late sergeant Company D, one hundred and forty-ninth regiment Pennsylvania volunteers, as additional bounty under the act of Congress approved July twenty-eighth, eighteen hundred and sixty-six, provided he has not already received bounty under said act.


*Approved, June 20, 1874.*

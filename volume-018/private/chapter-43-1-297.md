# Chapter 297

> An act giving the assent of Congress to the acceptance by the officers of the United States ship Monocacy of silver medals presented to them by the King of Siam.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the consent of Congress be, and the same is hereby, given to the acceptance by the officers of the United States ship Monocacy of the silver medals presented to them by the King of Siam on occasion of the recent interchange of civilities between his majesty and the officers of said ship representing the United States.


*Approved, June 17, 1874.*

# Chapter 535

> An act to relieve John Forsyth of Alabama of political disabilities.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two thirds of each house concurring),*

That the political disabilities imposed upon John Forsyth of Alabama by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States, for participation in the rebellion be, and the same are hereby, removed—


*Approved, June 23, 1874.*

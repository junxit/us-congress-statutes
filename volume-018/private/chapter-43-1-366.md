# Chapter 366

> An act granting a pension to Livanna Ingraham.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place the name of Livanna Ingraham, of Lowell, Kent County, Michigan, mother of Douglass Ingraham, late a private in Company F, Third Regiment of Michigan Infantry Volunteers, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, and pay her a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 20, 1874.*

# Chapter 241

> An Act granting a Pension to Sarah S. Cooper.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the. provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah S. Cooper, widow of Major Wickliff Cooper, seventh United States Cavalry, and pay her a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

# Chapter 447

> An act referring the petition and papers in the case of Robert M. and Stephen A. Douglas, in so far as the same relates to cotton seized, to the Court of Claims.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the claim of Robert M. and Stephen A. Douglas, for payment for their cotton seized in March, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, be, and it is hereby, referred to the Court of Claims for its decision, according to law and the practice of that court in such cases and proceedings.


*Approved, June 22, 1874.*

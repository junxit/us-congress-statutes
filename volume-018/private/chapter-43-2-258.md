# Chapter 258

> An Act granting a Pension to Margaret H. Pittenger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Margaret H. Pittenger, mother of James D. Pittenger, late a private in Company I, Sixty-fifth Regiment, New York Volunteers, and pay her a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

# Chapter 514

> An act granting a pension to E. Caroline Webster, widow of Lucius H. Webster.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name, of B. Caroline Webster, widow of Lucius H. Webster, late a private in company H, of the Seventh regiment of Michigan cavalry volunteers, and pay her a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 23, 1874.*

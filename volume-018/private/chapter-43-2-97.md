# Chapter 97

> An Act granting relief to John L. Williams, of New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of one thousand four hundred and sixty dollars be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury of the United States not otherwise appropriated, to John L. Williams of New York, to compensate him for the value of a cargo of hay taken and appropriated by the authorities of the Government in October, eighteen hundred and sixty-one.


> J. G. BLAINE
> Speaker of the House of Representatives.
> HENRY WILSON
> Vice President of the United States, and President of the Senate.

# Chapter 73

> An act to remove the disabilities of William L. Cabell, of Texas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein,),*

That all political disabilities imposed upon William L. Cabell, of Texas, by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States, by reason of his participation in the rebellion, me hereby removed.


*Approved, April 2, 1874.*

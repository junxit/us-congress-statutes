# Chapter 24

> An act to provide for the payment of D. B. Allen and Company for services in carrying the United States mails.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of twenty-one thousand five hundred and forty-three dollars be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any moneys in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the payment of D. B. Allen and Company for carrying the United States mails between New York and San Francisco in eighteen hundred and sixty-four and eighteen hundred and sixty-five, during the suspension of the overland mail service on the overland route, and that the same shall be in full payment for said service.


*Approved, January 22, 1875.*

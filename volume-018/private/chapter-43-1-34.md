# Chapter 34

> An act giving the consent of Congress to the acceptance by James McDonnell, a warrant officer in the United States Navy, of a present from the King of Italy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the consent of Congress be, and hereby is, granted to James McDonnell, a warrant officer in the Navy of the United States, to accept from the King of Italy, a silver medal, for assistance rendered on the twenty-fifth of June, eighteen hundred and seventy-three, in the port of Callao, Peru, to the Italian merchant vessel “Delaide.”


*Approved, February 21, 1874.*

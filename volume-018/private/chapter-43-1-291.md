# Chapter 291

> An act for the relief of Nathaniel McKay, assignee of the builders of the steamers La Portena, Edward Everett, F. W. Lincoln, Azalia, and N. P. Banks.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and hereby is, authorized to refund to Nathaniel McKay assignee of the builders of the steamers La Portena, Edward Everett, F. W. Lincoln, Azalia, and N. P. Banks, a sum not to exceed six thousand five hundred and seventy-four dollars, being the amount of revenue-tax found to have been paid by them to the Government on the bulls and engines of said steamers; said payment to be made out of any funds in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 16, 1874.*

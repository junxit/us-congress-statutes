# Chapter 442

> An act to remove the political disabilities of Henry H. Sibley, a citizen of Fredericksburg, Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring) therein,*

That all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States upon Henry n. Sibley, by reason of his participation in the rebellion, be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, June 22, 1874.*

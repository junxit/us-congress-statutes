# Chapter 263

> An Act granting a Pension to William H. Small

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William H. Small, late a private in Companies E and K of the fifteenth regiment of Pennsylvania cavalry and’ pay him a pension as for the loss of a leg from and after the passage of this act—


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

# Chapter 232

> An act granting a Pension to John Folger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Folger, late a private in company K, one hundred and twenty-second Ohio volunteers, and to pay him a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 6, 1874.*

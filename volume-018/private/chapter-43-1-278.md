# Chapter 278

> An act for the relief of Edward H. Calvert.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, do pay to Edward H. Calvert, of the District of Columbia, the sum of one thousand five hundred dollars, to be in full and complete payment of rent for, and damages done to, the property of Edward H. Calvert, on Good Hope Hill, in the District of Columbia, known during the late war as Fort Wagner, and occupied from eighteen hundred and sixty-two to the end of the war by federal troops.


*Approved, June 12, 1874.*

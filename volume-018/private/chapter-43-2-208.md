# Chapter 208

> An Act to remove the political disabilities of Thomas M. Jones, of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concuring therein),*

That all political disabilities imposed upon Thomas M. Jones, of Virginia, by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States, by reason of his participation in the rebellion, are hereby removed.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

# Chapter 12

> An act to remove the legal and political disabilities of William Steele, of Bexar County, Texas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all legal and political disabilities imposed by virtue of the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States upon William Steele, of Bexar County, Texas, be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, January 20, 1874.*

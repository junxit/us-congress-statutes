# Chapter 44

> An act for the relief of John Brennan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That John Brennan shall be allowed the sum of six hundred and forty-three dollars, for disbursements made by him in compensating assistant janitors in the United States court-house and post office at Indianapolis, Indiana, under authority from the United States marshal.


*Approved, February 11, 1875.*

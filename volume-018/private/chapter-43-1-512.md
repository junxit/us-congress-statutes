# Chapter 512

> An act for the relief of James Coats of Jackson, Mississippi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Sec’y of the Treasury be and he is hereby authorized to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to James Coats, of Jackson. Mississippi the sum of eight hundred dollars, in full for labor and repairs, and materials and furniture, furnished to the United States court-house at Jackson Mississippi


*Approved, June 23, 1874.*

# Chapter 531

> An act to relieve E. L. Winder of his political disabilities.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United,*

States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each house concurring therein,) That all the political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States, on E. L. Winder, of Norfolk, Virginia, late lieutenant in the United States Navy, be, and the same are hereby removed.


*Approved, June 23, 1874.*

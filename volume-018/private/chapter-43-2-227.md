# Chapter 227

> An Act to remove the political disabilities of Thomas T. Fountleroy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all the political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States on Thomas T. Fountleroy, a citizen of Virginia, be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

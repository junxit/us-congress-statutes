# Chapter 255

> An act granting a Pension to Sarah A. Timmons.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah A. Timmons, widow of Stephen D. Timmons, late a private in company G, fourth regiment Tennessee volunteer cavalry, and pay her a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 6, 1874.*

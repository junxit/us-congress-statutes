# Chapter 2

> An act to remove the disabilities of Thomas S. Bocock, E. J. Harvie, and Daniel Trigg, of Virginia; and John H. Reagan, of Texas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein,),*

That all political disabilities imposed upon Thomas S. Bocock, E. J. Harvie, and Daniel Trigg, of Virginia; and John H. Reagan, of Texas, by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States, by reason of their participation in the rebellion, are hereby removed.


*Approved, December 27, 1873.*

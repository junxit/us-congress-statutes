# Chapter 75

> An Act granting relief to Francis Dodge.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of six thousand dollars be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury of the United States not otherwise appropriated, to Francis Dodge, trustee for the owners of the schooner Fairfax, as compensation for the loss and destruction of said schooner, in October, eighteen hundred and sixty-one, by reason of the seizure and appropriation of the same by the naval authorities of the Government.


*Approved, February 13, 1875.*

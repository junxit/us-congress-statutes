# Chapter 81

> An act granting a pension to Mrs. Cynthia McPherson, mother of the late General James B. McPherson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the. pension laws, the name of Mrs. Cynthia McPherson, mother of James B. McPherson, late major general in the United States Army, and pay her a pension at the. rate of fifty dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, April 7, 1874.*

# Chapter 371

> An act granting a pension to John S. Long.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of John S. Long, late of Company D, First Battalion, Twelfth United States Infantry, to take effect from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 20, 1874.*

# Chapter 72

> An Act to remove the political disabilities of Henry Heth, of Virginia

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein,),*

That all political disabilities imposed upon Henry Heth, of Virginia, by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States, by reason of his participation in the rebellion, are hereby removed.


*Approved, February 12, 1875.*

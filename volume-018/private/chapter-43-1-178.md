# Chapter 178

> An act granting a pension to Juliet E. Hall, daughter of William Hall, late colonel of the eleventh regiment of Iowa infantry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Juliet E. Hall, daughter of William Hall, late a colonel of the eleventh regiment of Iowa infantry, commencing November eleventh, eighteen hundred and sixty-nine, the date of the remarriage of the widow.


*Approved, May 15, 1874.*

# Chapter 123

> An Act to relieve C. D. Anderson of political disabilities

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, five-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That O. D. Anderson, of Travis County, Texas, be, and is hereby, relieved of all political disability imposed by third section of fourteenth article of amendments to the Constitution of the United States.


*Approved, March 2, 1875.*

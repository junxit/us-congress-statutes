# Chapter 173

> An act for the relief of Elias C. Boudinot.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the civil proceedings now pending in the name of the United States against the property claimed by the said Elias C. Boudinot, for alleged violations of the internal revenue laws, be discontinued and dismissed.


*Approved, May 14, 1874.*

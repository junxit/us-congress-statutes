# Chapter 508

> An act to remove the disabilities of John Julius Gutherie.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein,),*

That all political disabilities imposed upon John Julius Gutherie, of North Carolina, by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States, by reason of his participation in the rebellion, are hereby removed.


*Approved, June 23, 1874.*

# Chapter 65

> An Act granting a Pension to James Burris.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of James Burris, late a private in thirty-second regiment of colored troops, and that be be paid a pension from the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 11, 1875.*

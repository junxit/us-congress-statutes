# Chapter 134

> An act for the benefit of Uriah Porter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Uriah Porter, of Allen County, Kentucky, be, and is hereby, authorized to operate his distillery in the county and State aforesaid, without being required to procure the written consent of John H. Page, who holds a lieu on the land and distillery of said Porter : Provided, That said Porter, prior to dis tilling, shall execute a bond to the United States, such as is prescribed by section eight of the Internal Revenue act, approved July twenty, eighteen hundred and sixty-eight, as amended, to be taken in lieu of the written consent of the owner of the fee; but nothing herein contained shall be construed as dispensing with any other requirement of the law in this case than that of the procuring of the consent aforesaid.


*Approved, April 28, 1874.*

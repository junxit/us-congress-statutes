# Chapter 524

> An act to remove the political disabilities of George E. Pickett, of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all political disabilities imposed upon George E. Pickett, of Richmond, Virginia, by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States, be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, June 23, 1874.*

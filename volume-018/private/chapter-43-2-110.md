# Chapter 110

> An Act for the relief of Major J. W. Nichols, paymaster United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid to Major J. W. Nichols, paymaster United States Army, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of four thousand five hundred dollars, erroneously charged to and paid by him in the settlement of his accounts.


*Approved, February 27, 1875.*

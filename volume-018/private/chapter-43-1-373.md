# Chapter 373

> An act granting a pension to John W. Truitt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of John W. Truitt, late of Company “E” One hundred and forty-second Regiment, Indiana Volunteers.


*Approved, June 20, 1874.*

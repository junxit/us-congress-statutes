# Chapter 48

> An act for the relief of Thomas Hillhouse, assistant treasurer of the United States in New York city.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to allow Thomas Hillhouse, assistant treasurer of the United States at New York city, in the settlement of his internal-revenue-stamp account, a credit for the sum of one hundred and eighty-five thousand dollars, being the proceeds of sale of internal-revenue stamps embezzled by James I. Johnson, a clerk in his office, without the default or negligence of said assistant treasurer.


*Approved, March 5, 1874.*

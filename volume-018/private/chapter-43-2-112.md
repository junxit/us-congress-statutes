# Chapter 112

> An Act granting a pension to Eunice Wilson, mother of John C. Wilson, late private company D, forty-ninth regiment Illinois Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Eunice Wilson, mother of John C. Wilson, late a private company D, forty-ninth regiment Illinois volunteers, and pay her a pension to commence from the date of the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 27, 1875.*

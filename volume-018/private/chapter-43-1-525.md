# Chapter 525

> An act granting a pension to Ira Douthart.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ira Douthart, late a private in company D, thirteenth regiment Iowa Volunteers, and pay him a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 23, 1874.*

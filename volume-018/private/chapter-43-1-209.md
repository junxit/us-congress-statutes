# Chapter 209

> An act granting a pension to Fanny Newcomb, mother of Irenus Newcomb, late of Company H, Sixth Regiment Vermont Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Fanny Newcomb, mother of Irenus Newcomb, late of Company H of the Sixth Regiment of Vermont Volunteers, and that she be allowed a pension.


*Approved, June 3, 1874.*

# Chapter 152

> An act granting a pension to Eugene Smith.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Eugene Smith, late of company F, First Nebraska Veteran Volunteers.


*Approved, May 7, 1874.*

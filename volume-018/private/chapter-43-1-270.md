# Chapter 270

> An act granting a Pension to John Heddinger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Heddinger, late boatswain’s-mate on the United States steamship “ Eutaw,” and that he be paid a pension to commence from the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 9, 1874.*

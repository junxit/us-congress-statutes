# Chapter 362

> An act for the relief of Joseph C. Breckinridge for services in the Army of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United, States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Paymaster General is hereby directed to pay to Joseph C. Breckinridge the fall pay and allowances of a first lieutenant of cavalry from the thirtieth day of August, eighteen hundred and sixty-one, to the fifth day of June, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, being the period during which he served as aide-de-camp on the staff of General William Nelson and General George H. Thomas prior to his muster into the United States service.


*Approved, June 20, 1874.*

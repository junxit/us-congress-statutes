# Chapter 212

> An Act for the relief of Joseph H. Colton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States is hereby requested to call upon the government of Bolivia to make payment of the money admitted by itself to be due, with interest thereon, according to the decree of the said government of Bolivia, of February first, eighteen hundred and seventy-two, to Joseph H. Colton, for maps engraved for that government under a contract made in eighteen hundred and fifty-eight.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

# Private Resolution 9

> Joint resolution authorizing the acceptance by Captain C. H. Wells, of the United States Navy, of the cross of the legion of honor, conferred upon him by the President of the French Republic

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Captain C. H. Wells, of the United States Navy, be, and he is hereby authorized to accept the cross of an officer of the national order of the Legion of Honor, conferred upon him by the President of the French Republic as evidence of his appreciation of that officer


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

# Chapter 274

> An Act granting a Pension to Emillia O. Black.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Emillia O. Black, widow of Willis H. Black, late deputy provost marshal for the eleventh district of Illinois, and pay her a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

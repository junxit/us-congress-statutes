# Chapter 250

> An Act granting a Pension to Rosalie C. P. Lisle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Rosalie C. P. Lisle, mother of Joseph T. Lisle, late an assistant paymaster in the Navy, and pay her a pension, commencing from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

# Chapter 67

> An Act granting a Pension to Alice Roper.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Alice Roper, widow of Samuel Roper, late captain of company K, Fifty-sixth Illinois, Infantry Volunteers and pay her a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 11, 1875.*

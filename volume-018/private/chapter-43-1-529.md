# Chapter 529

> An act to relieve Joseph Wheeler of Lawrence county, in the State of Alabama, from all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States, by reason of participation in the late rebellion, be and they are hereby removed from Joseph Wheeler of Lawrence County, in the State of Alabama.


*Approved, June 23, 1874.*

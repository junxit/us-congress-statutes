# Chapter 260

> An Act granting a Pension to Mary C. Toy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary C. Toy, widow of John B. Toy, late a private in Company A of the sixteenth regiment of Iowa volunteers, and that she be paid a pension from the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

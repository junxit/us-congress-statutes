# Chapter 233

> An act granting a Pension to Ezra H. Foster.

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ezra H. Foster, late private in company I, first Wisconsin cavalry, and in thirteenth Wisconsin battery.


*Approved, June 6, 1874.*

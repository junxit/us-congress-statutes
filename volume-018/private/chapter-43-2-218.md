# Chapter 218

> An Act to remove the political disabilities of Robert W. Johnson, formerly of Arkansas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That the political disabilities of Robert W. Johnson, of Arkansas, incurred under the provisions of the third section of the fourteenth article of the Constitution of the United States, be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

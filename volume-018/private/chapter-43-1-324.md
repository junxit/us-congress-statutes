# Chapter 324

> An act for the relief of Jonathan D. Hale.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officer of the treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Jonathan D. Hale, the sum of three thousand four hundred and twenty-five dollars, in full for services rendered in the. late war as recruiting officer, scout, and guide for the United States Army, and for two horse l t in said Army.


*Approved, June 19, 1874.*

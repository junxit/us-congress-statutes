# Chapter 518

> An act granting a pension to Franklin Stoner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Franklin Stoner, late a private in company G, of the Eighty-fourth Regiment of Pennsylvania Volunteers, on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, and that he be paid a pension from the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 23, 1874.*

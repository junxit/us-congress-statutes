# Chapter 53

> An act granting a pension to John A. Fisher—

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John A. Fisher, late first lieutenant in company D, twenty-third regiment Missouri volunteers, and pay him such pension as that allowed to the class of pensioners with which his present disability would class him, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 11, 1874.*

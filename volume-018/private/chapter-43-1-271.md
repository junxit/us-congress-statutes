# Chapter 271

> An act to place the name of Mrs. Caroline Duncan on the pension-roll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Caroline Duncan, as the widow of Joseph Duncan, private company I, one hundred and thirty-third Pennsylvania volunteers, killed at Fredericksburgh, Virginia, on the thirteenth December, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, and to pay her a pension from the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 9, 1874.*

# Chapter 523

> An act to release J. W. Bennett from political disabilities.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States, by reason of participation in the late rebellion, be, and they are hereby, removed from J. W. Bennett, of Carroll county, Maryland.


*Approved, June 23, 1874.*

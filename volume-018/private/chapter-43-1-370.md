# Chapter 370

> An act granting a pension to Andrew J. Lasley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Andrew J. Lasley, late a private in Company K, Second United States Infantry, to take effect from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 20, 1874.*

# Chapter 46

> An act granting a Pension to Emily Phillips, widow of Martin Phillips.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Emily Phillips, widow of Martin Phillips, late a corporal of company H, fifty-sixth Ohio volunteer infantry, upon evidence already furnished the Commissioner of Pensions in her claim, number one hundred and ninety-six thousand four hundred and fifty-one.


*Approved, February 11, 1875.*

# Chapter 190

> An act for the relief of P. Hornbrook.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the accounting officers of the Treasury be, and are hereby, authorized and directed to allow, in the accounts of P. Hornbrook, surveyor of customs for the port of Evansville, in the State of Indiana, the sum of eight hundred and seventy- two dollars and thirty-five cents, being amount of payments made to John J. Hays for salary as storekeeper, from December first, eighteen hundred and seventy, to December eleventh, eighteen hundred and seventy-one, and vouchers furnished therefor by said P. Hornbrook, and disallowed in the settlement of his accounts for want of oath of said John J. Hays.


*Approved, May 25, 1874.*

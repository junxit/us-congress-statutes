# Chapter 361

> An act for the relief of Joseph S. Read.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officer of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Joseph S. Read the sum of three hundred and ninety-five dollars and seventy-two cents; the same to be received in full compensation for services of the said Joseph S. Read as an assistant doorkeeper to the House of Representatives, from the first day of August to the tenth day of November, in the year eighteen hundred and sixty-eight.


*Approved, June 20, 1874.*

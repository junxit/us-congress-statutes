# Chapter 268

> An Act removing the political disabilities of O. R. Singleton of Mississippi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all the political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution on Otho R. Singleton, a citizen of the State of Mississippi, be, and the same are hereby removed.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

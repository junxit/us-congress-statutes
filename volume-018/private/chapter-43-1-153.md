# Chapter 153

> An act granting a pension to Mary E. Naylor, widow of Osborn Naylor, late a private in Company C, Second Kansas Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Mary E. Naylor, widow of Osborn Naylor, late a private in Company C, Second Kansas Militia.


*Approved, May 7, 1874.*

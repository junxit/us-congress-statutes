# Chapter 258

> An act for the relief of Pat. O. Hawes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Paymaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay Pat. O. Hawes, late second lieutenant in the Forty-fifth Kentucky regiment of Volunteer Infantry, United States Army, the pay and allowances of a second lieutenant of infantry, from the twenty-fifth day of September, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, until the twenty-third day of January, eighteen hundred and sixty-four.


*Approved, June 8, 1874.*

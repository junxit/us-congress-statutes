# Chapter 63

> An act for the relief of Willard Davis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the Treasury Department be, and they are hereby, directed to pass to the credit of Willard Davis, late collector in the second and fifth collection-districts of Kentucky, the sum of fourteen thousand three hundred and thirty-eight dollars and thirty-three cents.


*Approved, March 23, 1874.*

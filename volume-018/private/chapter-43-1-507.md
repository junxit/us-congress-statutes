# Chapter 507

> An act to remove the political disabilities of Van R. Morgan of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two thirds of each House concurring therein,),*

That all political disabilities imposed upon Van 11. Morgan, of Virginia, by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States, be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, June 23, 1874.*

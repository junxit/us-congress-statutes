# Chapter 3

> An act for the relief of James L. Pugh

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all political disabilities imposed upon James L. Pugh, a citizen of the State of Alabama, by the fourteenth article of the amendments to the Constitution of the United States be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, December 18, 1874.*

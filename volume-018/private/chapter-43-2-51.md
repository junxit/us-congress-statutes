# Chapter 51

> An act granting a Pension to Sarah Summerville.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah Summerville, widow of Alexander S. Summerville, deputy provost-marshal for the eleventh district of Illinois.


*Approved, February 11, 1875.*

# Chapter 291

> An Act for the relief of M. Von Entress Fuersteneck, late second lieutenant of the Sixty-eighth New York Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United, States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one thousand four hundred and fifty-five dollars and twenty cents, to be paid to M. Von Entress Fuersteneck, late second lieutenant of Company A of the Sixty-eighth Regiment of New York Volunteers, to cover a period of service from August thirtieth, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, to December twenty-first, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, inclusive, at which time he was commissioned, and actually performed duty as a second lieutenant, but was not mustered in.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

# Chapter 16

> An act to remove the political disabilities of Dabney H. Maury of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, ( two-thirds of each House concurring therein,),*

That all political disabilities imposed upon Dabney H. Maury, of Virginia, by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States, by reason of his participation in the rebellion, are hereby removed.


*Approved, January 15, 1875.*

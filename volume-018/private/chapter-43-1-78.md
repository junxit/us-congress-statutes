# Chapter 78

> An act granting a pension to Henry B. Ryder

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Henry B. Ryder, late second lieutenant in the Cameron Dragoons, afterward changed to the Fifth Pennsylvania Cavalry, and pay him a pension as second lieutenant from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, April 3, 1874.*

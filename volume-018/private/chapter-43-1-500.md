# Chapter 500

> An act to relieve C. L. Stevenson, of Virginia, of his political disabilities

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two thirds of each House concurring therein,*

That all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States by reason of any participation in the late rebellion be, and they are hereby, removed from C. L. Stevenson, of Virginia.


*Approved, June 23, 1874.*

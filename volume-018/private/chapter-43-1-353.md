# Chapter 353

> An act for the relief of Robert Sutherland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Paymaster General of the Army be, and he is hereby, directed to pay to Robert Sutherland, late second lieutenant Fourth Arkansas Cavalry Volunteers, out of any money appropriated, or that may hereafter be appropriated, for the pay of the Army, the full pay and emoluments of a second lieutenant of cavalry from the thirtieth day of June to the sixth day of September, eighteen hundred and sixty-five.


*Approved, June 20, 1874.*

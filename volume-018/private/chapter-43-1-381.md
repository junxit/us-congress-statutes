# Chapter 381

> An act granting a pension to Charles McCarty.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Charles McCarty, late an engineer on the Mississippi Railroad, and pay him a pension from and after the passage of this act at eight dollars per month.


*Approved, June 20, 1874.*

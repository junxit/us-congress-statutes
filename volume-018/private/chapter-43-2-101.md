# Chapter 101

> An Act granting a pension to William Ira Mayfield.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United, States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of William Ira Mayfield, who was a private in the Ninth Regiment of Oregon Militia Volunteers, be placed upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, to take effect from the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 23, 1875.*

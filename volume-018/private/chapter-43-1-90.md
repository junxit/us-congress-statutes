# Chapter 90

> An act granting a pension to Christiana Bailey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Christiana Bailey, widow of David Bailey, deceased, late a private in Company B, Thirteenth Regiment of West Virginia Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, April 11, 1874.*

# Chapter 109

> An Act for the relief of J. W. Drew, late additional paymaster in the United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting-officers of the Treasury of the United States be, and are hereby, authorized and directed to allow to J. W. Drew, late additional paymaster in the United States Army, in the settlement of his accounts for the months of November and December, eighteen hundred and sixty-eight, the sum of twenty thousand three hundred and nineteen dollars and eighty-eight cents for disbursements made on vouchers lost in transmission: Provided, That said accounting-officers shall be satisfied that said disbursements were made; and in determining the same, secondary evidence may be received.


*Approved, February 27, 1875.*

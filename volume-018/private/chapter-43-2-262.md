# Chapter 262

> An Act removing the political disabilities of John Withers, Joseph F. Minter, and William Kearney.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein,),*

That the political disabilities of John Withers, Joseph F. Minter, and William Kearney, of Bexar county, State of Texas, be and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

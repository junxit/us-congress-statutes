# Chapter 509

> An act for the relief of Marcus Otterbourg, late consul of the United States at the city of Mexico, and Minister to the republic of Mexico.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, directed, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to pay to Marcus Otterbourg, late consul of the United States at the city of Mexico, and having been designated minister plenipotentiary to the republic of Mexico, in full compensation for his services as Minister to Mexico from June twenty-first until September thirtieth, eighteen hundred and sixty-seven, both inclusive.


*Approved, June 23, 1874.*

# Chapter 238

> An act granting a pension to Henry B. Havens, late a private of company K, twelfth regiment Wisconsin volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Henry B. Havens, of Grant county, Wisconsin, late a private of company K, twelfth regiment of Wisconsin volunteers, disabled while in service and line of duty, at the rate of eight dollars per month, subject to the limitations and restrictions provided by law.


*Approved, June 6, 1874.*

# Chapter 58

> An act repealing the act granting a pension to William H. Blair, approved July twenty-seventh, eighteen hundred and sixty-eight.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the act granting a pension to William H. Blair, late a private in company G of the twelfth Regiment of Maine volunteers, approved July twenty-seventh, eighteen hundred and sixty-eight, be, and the same is hereby, repealed.


*Approved, February 11, 1875.*

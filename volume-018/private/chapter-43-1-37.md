# Chapter 37

> An act for the relief of Robert N. McMillan, late collector of customs and superintendent of lights for the district of Teche, in the State of Louisiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the Treasury be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to adjust and settle the account of Robert N. McMillan, late collector of customs and superintendent of lights for the district of Teche, in the State of Louisiana, and allow him the sum of seven hundred and six dollars and fifty-six cents, being the amount paid for salaries of keepers and assistant keepers of light houses for the first quarter of the year eighteen hundred and sixty-one.


*Approved, February 24, 1874.*

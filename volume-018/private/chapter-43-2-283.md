# Chapter 283

> An Act granting a Pension to Mrs. Sarah B. Howe and Mrs. Mary Cranston.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension ‘laws, the name of Sarah B. Howe, widow of Albion Howe, late first lieutenant of the Fourth LT. S. Artillery; and Mrs. Mary Cranston, widow of Arthur Cranston, late first lieutenant in Fourth U. S. Artillery and pay them a pension each, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

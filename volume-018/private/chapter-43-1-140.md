# Chapter 140

> An act for the relief of John B. Weber, late Colonel of the Eighteenth regiment Corps d’Afrique.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Paymaster of the Army be, and he is hereby, directed to allow and pay to John B. Weber, late colonel of the Eighteenth Infantry, Corps d’Afrique, out of the appropriation for the pay of the Army, the pay and allowances as first lieutenant, from the twenty-eighth day of September, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, until the eighth day of November, in the same year, being from the time he received his commission as such officer until the time when he was mustered into the service: Provided, That the said John B. Weber has not been paid for his services for that time.


*Approved, April 30, 1874.*

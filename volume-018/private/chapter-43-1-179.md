# Chapter 179

> An act for the relief of McClintock Young, of the State of Maryland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the extension of the patent of McClintock Young, of the State of Maryland, county of Frederick, and city of Frederick, for an improvement in harvesting-machines, dated June twenty-eighth, eighteen hundred and seventy-three, and numbered twenty-four thousand five hundred and ninety-eight, and signed by the Commissioner of Patents on the twenty-eighth day of June, eighteen hundred and seventy-three, shall have the same binding effect in law as though the said extension of letters-patent were signed by the Commissioner of Patents and bore date the twenty-seventh day of June, eighteen hundred and seventy-three.


*Approved, May 15, 1874.*

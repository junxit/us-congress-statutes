# Chapter 498

> An act for the relief of Oliver P. Mason.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of seven hundred and eighty-seven dollars and fifty cents be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, for the payment of Oliver P. Mason, for services rendered as assistant provost-marshal in the Department of Kansas; and the proper officers of the War and Treasury Departments are hereby authorized to pay the said sum to the said Oliver P. Mason or his legal representatives.


*Approved, June 23, 1874.*

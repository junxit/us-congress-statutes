# Chapter 269

> An act granting a Pension to Mrs. Letitia Carr.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Letitia Carr, mother of William Carr, late a fireman in the United States Navy, and pay her a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 9, 1874.*

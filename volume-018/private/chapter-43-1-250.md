# Chapter 250

> An act granting a Pension to Charles Herbert.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Charles Herbert, late a private in company C, sixty-ninth regiment New York volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars a month from and alter the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 6, 1874.*

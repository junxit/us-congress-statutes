# Chapter 282

> An act for the benefit of the Louisville and Bardstown Turnpike Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Quartermaster-General of the United States Army be authorized and directed to audit, adjust, and pay such sums as he may deem proper and just to the president, directors, and company of the Bardstown and Louisville Turnpike, Road Company, to make compensation for two bridges on the road of said Company, one over Halt river, the other over Floyd’s Fork, constructed for the military service by said Company by advice of the Quartermaster-General, to be paid out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated : Provided, That the whole amount allowed under this act shall not exceed the sum of thirteen thousand six hundred dollars.


*Approved, June 12, 1874.*

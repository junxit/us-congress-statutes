# Chapter 87

> An act granting a pension to Sciotha Brasbears, late of the Seventeenth Regiment Kentucky Cavalry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Sciotha Brasbears, late of the Seventeenth Regiment Kentucky Cavalry, and that he be allowed a pension of eight dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, April 11, 1874.*

# Chapter 282

> An Act granting a Pension to Ansel Thayer, of Braintree, Massachusetts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ansel Thayer, father of George Frederick Thayer, Company F, second regiment Massachusetts Cavalry; Loring W. Thayer, company E, Thirty-second Massachusetts Volunteers; Charles S. Thayer, company K, Third Massachusetts Cavalry ; and Lucien M. Thayer, company I, Forty-second regiment Massachusetts and pay him a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

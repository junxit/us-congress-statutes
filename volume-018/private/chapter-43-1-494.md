# Chapter 494

> An act to amend an act entitled “ An act to incorporate the trustees of the Female Orphan Asylum in Georgetown, and the Washington City Orphan Asylum, in the District of Columbia,” approved May 24, 1828.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section three of an act entitled “An act to incorporate the trustees of the Female Orphan Asylum, in Georgetown, and the Washington City Orphan Asylum, in the District of Columbia,” approved May twenty-fourth, eighteen hundred and twenty-eight, be, and the same is hereby, so amended as to authorize the said corporations or either of them, to increase the annual income of property acquired or to be acquired by either of said corporations to a sum not exceeding twenty-five thousand dollars per annum.


*Approved, June 23, 1874.*

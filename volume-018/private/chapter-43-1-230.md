# Chapter 230

> An act granting a Pension to Guadaloupe Torres.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Guadaloupe Torres, at the rate of eight dollars per month, from the date of the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 6, 1874.*

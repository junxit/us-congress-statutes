# Chapter 243

> An act granting a Pension to Henry Bruckner, late a private of company F, fifty-eighth regiment Illinois volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Henry Bruckner, late a private of company F, fifty-eighth regiment Illinois volunteers.


*Approved, June 6, 1874.*

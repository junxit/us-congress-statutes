# Chapter 68

> An act for the relief of Willard Howe, of Massachusetts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Auditor for the Post-Office Department be directed to credit the account of the said Willard Howe with the sum of four hundred and seventy-four dollars and fifty-four cents, to reimburse the said Howe for the payments so made by him.


*Approved, March 24, 1874.*

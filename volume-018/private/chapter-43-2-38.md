# Chapter 38

> An act to remove the disabilities of James Howard, of Baltimore, Maryland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein,),*

That all political disabilities imposed upon James Howard, of Baltimore, Maryland, by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States, by reason of participation in the rebellion, are hereby removed.


*Approved, February 9, 1875.*

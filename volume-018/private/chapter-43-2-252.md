# Chapter 252

> An Act granting a Pension to Hannah E. Currie.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Hannah E. Currie, mother of Samuel A. Currie late of the Thirty third Regiment of Ohio Volunteer Infantry, based on the evidence on tile in the office of the Commissioner of Pensions as case numbered one hundred and sixty-nine thousand six hundred and fifty two.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

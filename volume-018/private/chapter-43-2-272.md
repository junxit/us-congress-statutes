# Chapter 272

> An Act granting a Pension to Lafayette Briggs.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lafayette Briggs late a private in the First Battery of Wisconsin Artillery, and pay him a pension from and after the passage of this act


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

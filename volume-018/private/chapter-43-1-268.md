# Chapter 268

> An act for the relief of Michael Bannon, of Dayton, Ohio.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Michael Bannon, late a private in company F, ninety-seventh Illinois Infantry ; to take effect from and after the passage of this act


*Approved, June 9, 1874.*

# Chapter 237

> An Act for the relief of William Chester

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officer of the Post Office Department is hereby authorized and directed to credit William Chester, postmaster at Knobnoster, Johnson county, Missouri with the sum of three hundred and twenty-nine dollars and eighty-five cents on his account as postmaster as aforesaid with said Department, said sum being the amount of postage stamps and postal money stolen from said post-office on the eleventh day of May, eighteen hundred and seventy-two, by burglarious entry of the said building in which said office was kept—


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

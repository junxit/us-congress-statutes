# Chapter 256

> An Act granting a Pension to Catharine Lee, widow of Jessee M. Lee, private company B, second regiment Ohio Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Catharine. Lee, widow of Jesse M. Lee, private company B, second regiment Ohio volunteers, and pay her a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

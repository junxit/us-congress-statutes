# Chapter 269

> An Act granting a Pension to Betsey A. Eaton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Betsey A. Eaton, the widow of Willard G. Eaton, late colonel of the thirteenth Michigan Infantry Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

# Chapter 160

> An act granting a pension to Isaac M. Grant.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Isaac M. Grant, late of company K, Thirty-eighth regiment Ohio Volunteers, and pay him a pension from and after passage of this act.


*Approved, May 8, 1874.*

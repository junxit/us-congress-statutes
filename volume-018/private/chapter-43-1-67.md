# Chapter 67

> An act for the relief of Reuel B. Fuller, of Wilton, Maine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General he, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, in the settlement of the accounts of Reuel B. Fuller, postmaster at Wilton, Franklin county, in the State of Maine, to credit the said Reuel B. Fuller with the sum of one hundred and sixty-nine dollars, that being the amount of postage stamps and postal funds stolen from the post-office of Wilton, without fault or negligence on the part of said Reuel B. Fuller.


*Approved, March 24, 1874.*

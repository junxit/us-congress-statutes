# Chapter 161

> An act granting a Pension to James Roach.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of James Roach, late a gunner on the ship “Plymouth,” and pay him a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, May 8, 1874.*

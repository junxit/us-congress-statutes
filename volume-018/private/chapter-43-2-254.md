# Chapter 254

> An Act granting a Pension to William O. Madison.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of W. O. Madison, late a private in company C, Fourth Iowa Infantry, with the right to a pension at the rate of eight dollars a month, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

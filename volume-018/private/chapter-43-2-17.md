# Chapter 17

> An act to remove the political disabilities of Charles M. Fauntleroy of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all political disabilities imposed upon Charles M. Fauntleroy, of Virginia, by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States, by reason of his participation in the rebellion, are hereby removed.


*Approved, January 15, 1875.*

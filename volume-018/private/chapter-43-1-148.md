# Chapter 148

> An act for the relief of James R. Young.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the Treasury are hereby authorized to allow and credit to James R. Young, late postmaster at Lisbon, New Hampshire, the sum of three hundred and nine dollars and eighty-four cents, postage-stamps stolen from his safe and office by burglars on the night of May fifteenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-nine, without any fault or neglect on the part of said postmaster.


*Approved, May 6, 1874.*

# Chapter 375

> An act to relieve Thomas Claiborne, of Tennessee, of political disabilities imposed upon him by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein,),*

That Thomas Claiborne, of Davidson County, Tennessee, be, and he is hereby, relieved of the political disabilities imposed upon him by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States.


*Approved, June 20, 1874.*

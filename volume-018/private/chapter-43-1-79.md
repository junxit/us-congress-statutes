# Chapter 79

> An act to relief from political disabilities Thomas Hardeman, Jr., of Georgia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein,),*

That the following named person is hereby relieved from all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States, to wit, Thomas Hardeman Jr, of Bibb county, Georgia.


*Approved, April 3, 1874.*

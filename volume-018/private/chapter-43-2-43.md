# Chapter 43

> An act granting a Pension to Rosanna Quinn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Rosanna Quinn, mother of Francis P. Quinn, late sergeant-major of the ninety-sixth regiment Illinois volunteers, and to pay her a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 11, 1875.*

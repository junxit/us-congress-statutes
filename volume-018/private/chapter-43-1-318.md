# Chapter 318

> An act granting a pension to Martin V. Jackson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Martin V. Jackson, late a first lieutenant in Captain N. J. Roscoe’s Company, Second Regiment of Kansas State Militia.


*Approved, June 18, 1874.*

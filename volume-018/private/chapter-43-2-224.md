# Chapter 224

> An Act for the relief of Washington Crosland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid to Washington Crosland, of Saint. Louis, Missouri, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of two thousand dollars, in satisfaction of all damages which accrued to him by reason of the construction of a railroad across his two lots of land in the city of Saint Louis by the United States for military purposes.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

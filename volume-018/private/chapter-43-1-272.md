# Chapter 272

> An act granting a Pension to George H. Reynolds.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place, upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George H. Reynolds of company twentieth Regiment of Indiana volunteer infantry.


*Approved, June 9, 1874.*

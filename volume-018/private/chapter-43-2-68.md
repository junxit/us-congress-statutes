# Chapter 68

> An Act granting a Pension to Louisa Thomas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Louisa Thomas, widow of Cyrus Thomas, late private in company E, One hundred and seventy-sixth regiment Ohio Infantry Volunteers, and pay her a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 11, 1875.*

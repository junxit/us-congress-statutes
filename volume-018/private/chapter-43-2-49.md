# Chapter 49

> An act granting a Pension to George Holmes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George Holmes, late a private in the seventh Maine battery, and to pay him a pension of eight dollars a month, dating from the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 11, 1875.*

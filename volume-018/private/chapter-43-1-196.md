# Chapter 196

> An act for the relief of the Foundry Methodist Episcopal Church of Washington City.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the trustees of the Foundry Methodist Episcopal Church of Washington, District of Columbia, be, and they are hereby, authorized to sell, for the benefit of said church, the following described real estate, situate in said city, to wit: The east half of lot numbered nine in square numbered one hundred and fifty-eight.


*Approved, May 28, 1874.*

# Chapter 251

> An act granting a Pension to Nancy Abbott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Nancy Abbott, mother of Amos W. Abbott, a sergeant of company A, in the ninth regiment of Michigan volunteers.


*Approved, June 6, 1874.*

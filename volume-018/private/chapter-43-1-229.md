# Chapter 229

> An act granting a Pension to Mary Storrs.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Storrs, mother of Charles Storrs, late private in company K, seventh regiment Vermont volunteers, and of Gilman D. Storrs, late private in company B, tenth regiment Vermont Volunteers; and to pay her at the rate of eight dollars per mouth, to date from the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 6, 1874.*

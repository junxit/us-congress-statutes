# Chapter 249

> An Act granting a Pension to Mrs Penelope C. Brown, of Tennessee, widow of Stephen C. Brown, late a private of company C, eighth Tennessee cavalry volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he. is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Penelope C. Brown, of Greene county, Tennessee, widow of Stephen C. Brown, late a private of company C, eighth Tennessee volunteer cavalry.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

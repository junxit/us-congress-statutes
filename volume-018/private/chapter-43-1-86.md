# Chapter 86

> An act granting a pension to Elizabeth F. Thompson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elizabeth F. Thompson, mother of Moses Goodwin, late a private in Company I, Ninth Regiment of Maine Volunteers, to take effect from the passage of this act.


*Approved, April 11, 1874.*

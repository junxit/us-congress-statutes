# Chapter 277

> An act relieving the political disabilities of Fitzhugh Lee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two thirds of each House concurring therein,),*

That all the political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States, on Fitzhugh Lee, a citizen of Virginia, be, and the same are hereby removed.


*Approved, June 11, 1874.*

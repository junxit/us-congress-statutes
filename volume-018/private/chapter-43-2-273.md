# Chapter 273

> An Act granting a Pension to Sarah A. Woodworth

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah A. Woodworth, widow of Joseph C. Woodworth, late a private in company II, of the sixty-seventh regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers, and pay her a pension from and after the passage of this act


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

# Chapter 382

> An act granting a pension to Mary J. Blood.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary J. Blood, widow of Emory A. Blood, late private in Company D, Eleventh Regiment Massachusetts Volunteers, pay her a pension of eight dollars a month.


*Approved, June 20, 1874.*

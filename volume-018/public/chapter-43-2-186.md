# Chapter 186

> An act to restore Captain John C. Beaumont of the United States Navy, to his original position on the Navy Register.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be, and he is hereby authorized to restore John C. Beaumont, Captain in the United States Navy, now on the active list, to his original position on the Navy Register, next above Captain Charles H. B. Caldwell.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

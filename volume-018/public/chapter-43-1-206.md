# Chapter 206

> An Act to extend the time to pre-emptors on the public lands in the State of Minnesota, to make final payment.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time at which preemptors on the public lands in the State of Minnesota, including the lands within Fort Ridgely and Sioux Indian reservations, are now required to make final proof and payment, is extended for the period of two years.


*Approved, June 3, 1874.*

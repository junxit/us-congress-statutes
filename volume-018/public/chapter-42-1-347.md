# Chapter 347

> An Act directing the Secretary of the Treasury to report upon the necessity for a public building at Brooklyn, New York, and the cost of the same.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, directed to report to Congress at the beginning of its next session whether the present needs of the Government require the erection of a. public building at at Brooklyn, New York, and the estimated cost of the same, including the site.


*Approved, June 20, 1874.*

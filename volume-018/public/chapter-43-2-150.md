# Chapter 150

> An act to make East Pascagoula, in the State of Mississippi, a port of delivery in the District of Pearl River.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the passage of this act East Pascagoula, in the State of Mississippi, in the district of Pearl River, shall be a port of delivery for said district.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

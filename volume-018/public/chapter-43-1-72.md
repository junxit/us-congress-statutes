# Chapter 72

> An Act to approve an act of the legislative assembly of the District of Columbia relating to parishes of the Protestant Episcopal Church.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the act of the legislative assembly of the District of Columbia entitled “An act for the relief of parishes of the Protestant Episcopal Church in the District of Columbia,” approved June twenty-sixth, eighteen hundred and seventy-three, be, and the same is hereby, approved ; and that all acts now in force in the District of Columbia, inconsistent therewith, be, and the same are hereby repealed.


*Approved, March 28, 1874.*

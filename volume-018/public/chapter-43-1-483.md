# Chapter 483

> An Act to allow the schooner Ocean Wave to take the name of Edith E. Wright, and be registered under that name.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the schooner Ocean Wave, registered in the eastern district of Maryland, may take the name of Edith E. Wright, and be registered under that name.


*Approved, June 23, 1874.*

# Chapter 187

> An act for the relief of Major N. H. McLean, late of Adjutant Generals department, United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and is hereby, authorized to appoint Major Nathaniel H. McLean, late of the United States Army to fill the first vacancy which may occur in the lowest grade of the Adjutant Generals department, or if he shall deem it best, to reinstate and retire him with the rank to which he would have attained in service at the date of the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

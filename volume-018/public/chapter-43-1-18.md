# Chapter 18

> An Act to abolish the office of Deputy Commissioner of Internal Revenue.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the office of Deputy Commissioner of Internal Revenue, made vacant by the death of General B. J. Sweet, be, and the same is hereby, abolished ; and that the Secretary of the Treasury may, upon the recommendation of the Commissioner of Internal Revenue, designate one of the two remaining deputy commissioners as First Deputy Commissioner, who shall perform the duties

and be paid only the salary prescribed for the office of deputy commissioner hereby abolished.


*Approved, January 29, 1874.*

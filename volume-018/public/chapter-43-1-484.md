# Chapter 484

> An Act authorizing and requiring the issuance of a patent for certain lands to the county of Scott, in the State of Missouri.

Whereas, by the act of the Congress of the United States entitled “An act to quiet the title to certain lands in the State of Missouri,” approved December twenty-seventh eighteen hundred and seventy-two, certain lands therein mentioned were granted to the county of Scott, in the State of Missouri, which were not specifically described; and Whereas, no provision for the issuance of a patent for said lauds was made in said act: Therefore, Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

That it shall be the duty of the Commissioner of the General Land Office to cause a patent to be issued to said county of Scott, in the State of Missouri, for all the lands included in that portion of township numbered twenty-seven north, of range twelve east, of the fifth principal meridian, lying east of Little River, as the same appears on the plat of survey on file in the General Land Office: Provided, That nothing in this act shall prejudice the rights of any person claiming any of said lands by virtue of any homestead, preemption, or other entry made under the laws of the United States.


*Approved, June 23, 1874.*

# Chapter 196

> An act for the relief of settlers on lands within railroad limits.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That where any actual settler who shall have paid for any lands situate within the limits of any grant of lands by Congress to aid in the construction of any railroad, the price of such lands being fixed by law at double minimum rates, and such railroad lands having been forfeited to the United States and restored to the public domain for failure to build such railroad, such person or persons shall have the right to locate, on any unoccupied lands, an amount equal to their original entry, without further cost, except such fees as are now provided by law in preemption cases: Provided, That when such location is upon double minimum lands, one-half the amount only shall be taken.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

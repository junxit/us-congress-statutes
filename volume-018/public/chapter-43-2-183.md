# Chapter 183

> An act to provide for the construction of military roads in Arizona.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be, and is hereby appropriated, from any money in the Treasury of the United States not otherwise appropriated, the sum of fifteen thousand dollars to be used under the direction of the Secretary of War, in the construction of military roads in the Territory of Arizona, as follows, namely, From Fort Whipple to Camp McDowell, with a branch to Camp Verde, and from Fort Whipple to Skull Valley direct


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

# Chapter 5

> An Act to establish a post route from the village of L’Anse, in the county of Houghton and State of Michigan, to Huron Bay, in said county and State.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Rouse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a post route from the village of L’Anse, in the county of Houghton and State of Michigan, to Huron Bay, in said county and State, be, and the same is hereby, established.


*Approved, January 5, 1874.*

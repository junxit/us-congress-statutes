# Chapter 402

> An Act to constitute Montgomery, in the State of Alabama, a port of delivery.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Montgomery, in the State of Alabama, shall be, and is hereby, constituted a port of delivery, within the collection-district of Mobile; and there shall be appointed a deputy collector of customs, to reside at said port, who shall receive a salary, to be determined by the Secretary of the Treasury, not exceeding one thousand five hundred dollars per annum.


*Approved, June 22, 1874.*

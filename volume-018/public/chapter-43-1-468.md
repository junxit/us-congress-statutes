# Chapter 468

> An Act to amend the act entitled “An act to amend an act entitled ‘An act to establish a court for the investigation of claims against the United States,’” approved August 6th, 1856.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That any three judges of the Court of Claims shall constitute a quorum; Provided, That the concurrence of three judges shall be necessary to the decision of any case.


*Approved, June 23, 1874.*

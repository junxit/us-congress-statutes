# Chapter 78

> An Act to make an appropriation to the contingent fund of the House of Representatives.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of twenty thousand dollars; the same to be added to the contingent fund of the House of Representatives.


*Approved, February 17, 1875.*

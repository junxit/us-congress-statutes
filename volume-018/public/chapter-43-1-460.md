# Chapter 460

> An Act to provide for the improvement of the Oostenaula River, in the State of Georgia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of ten thousand dollars be, and the same is hereby, appropriated out of the money in the treasury of the United States, for the improvement of the Oostenaula River, in the State of Georgia.


*Approved, June 23, 1874.*

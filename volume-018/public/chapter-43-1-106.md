# Chapter 106

> An Act to amend the act entitled "An act relating to the enrollment and license of certain vessels."

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of the act relating to the enrollment and license of vessels navigating the. western rivers and the waters on the northern, northeastern, and northwestern frontiers of the United States, otherwise than by sea, approved February twenty-eighth, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, are hereby extended to include all vessels of the United States navigating the waters of the United States.


*Approved, April 17, 1874.*

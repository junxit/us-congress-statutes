# Chapter 466

> An Act to extend the time allowed for the redemption of certain lands by the first section of the act entitled “An act to provide for the redemption and sale of lands held by the United States under the several acts levying direct taxes, and for other purposes” approved June eighth, eighteen hundred & seventy-two, and to suspend the operation of the fourth section of said act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time allowed in and by the first section of the act of Congress approved June eighth, eighteen hundred and seventy-two, entitled “An act to provide for the redemption and sale of lands held by the United States under the. several acts levying direct taxes, and for other purposes” be and the same is hereby extended for the term of two years from the first day of June, eighteen hundred and seventy-four.

## § 2.

That the operation of the fourth section of said act be, and the same is hereby, suspended until the expiration of said term of two years from said first day of June.


*Approved, June 23, 1874.*

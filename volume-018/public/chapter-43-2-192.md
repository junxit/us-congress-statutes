# Chapter 192

> An act to donate to the State of Oregon a public building-lot, and material situated at The Dalles, Oregon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the mint-building, material, and lot on which it is located, at The Dalles, Oregon, be, and the same are hereby, donated to the State of Oregon: Provided, That the donation is made on the condition that said building and lot shall be appropriated by the State of Oregon to the use of some educational or charitable institution.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

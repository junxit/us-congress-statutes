# Chapter 187

> An Act repealing the act entitled "An act fixing the time for the election of Representatives from the State of California to the Forty-fourth Congress."

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the act entitled “An act fixing the time for the election of Representatives from the State of California to the Forty-fourth Congress,” approved March third, eighteen hundred and seventy-three, be, and the same is hereby, repealed.


*Approved, May 21, 1874.*

# Chapter 142

> An Act to render available an unexpended balance of appropriation for collection and payment of bounty, &c., for colored soldiers and sailors.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the balance of appropriation, or so much thereof as may be necessary, for collection and payment of bounty, prize-money, and other legitimate claims, of colored soldiers and sailors, unexpended at the expiration of the fiscal year ending the thirtieth of June, eighteen hundred and seventy-three, may be expended under the direction of the Secretary of War for the payment of expenses incurred in the fiscal year ending the thirtieth of June, eighteen hundred and seventy-four, in the collection and payment of bounty, prize-money, and other legitimate claims of colored soldiers and sailors.


*Approved, May 1, 1874.*

# Chapter 199

> An act to authorize the consolidation of the Auburn City National Bank and the First National Bank of Auburn, New York

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Auburn City National Bank of Auburn, New York, is hereby authorized to be merged into and consolidated with the First National Bank of Auburn, New York, whenever the board of directors of each of the said banks, shall accept such merger and consolidation by resolution of the board, confirmed by a vote of three fourths of the stockholders respectively, at meetings called for that purpose, and cause a copy of such action, duly authenticated, to be filed with the Comptroller of the Currency: Provided, That such acceptance be made within six months after the passage of this act, and that all expenses incident to the proposed consolidation including engraving, shall be borne and paid by the First National Bank of Auburn, New York

## § 2.

That all the debts, demands, liabilities, rights, privileges, and, powers, of the Auburn City National Bank, of Auburn, and the First National Bank of Auburn, shall devolve upon and inure to the First National Bank of Auburn, New York, whenever such consolidation is effected


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

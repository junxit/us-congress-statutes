# Chapter 421

> An Act to place on the retired list of the Navy, M. H. Plunkett, late second assistant engineer of the Regular Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and is hereby, authorized to nominate and appoint M. H. Plunkett, late second assistant engineer of the regular naval service, on the retired list of the Navy, with pay to begin from the passage of this act—


*Approved, June 22, 1874.*

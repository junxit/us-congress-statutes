# Chapter 107

> An Act to authorize the employment of certain aliens as engineers and pilots.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That any alien who, in the manner provided for by law, has declared his intention to become a citizen of the United States, and who shall have been a permanent resident of the United States for at least six months immediately prior to the granting of such license, may be licensed, as if already naturalized, to serve as an engineer or pilot upon any steam-vessel subject to inspection under the provisions of the act entitled “An act to provide for the better security of life on board of vessels propelled, in whole or in part, by steam, and for other purposes,” approved February twenty-eighth, eighteen hundred and seventy-one.


*Approved, April 17, 1874.*

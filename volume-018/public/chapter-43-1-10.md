# Chapter 10

> An Act making an appropriation for the legislative expenses of Colorado Territory.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That twenty thousand dollars be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for compensation and mileage of the members of the legislative assembly of Colorado Territory, and for pay of clerks, officers, and contingent expenses thereof.


*Approved, January 14, 1874.*

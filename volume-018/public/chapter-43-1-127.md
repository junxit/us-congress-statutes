# Chapter 127

> An Act to establish the Bismarck land district in the Territory of Dakota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all that portion of Dakota Territory lying north of the seventh standard parallel and west of the ninth guide-meridian be, and the same is hereby, created into a separate land district, to be known as the Bismarck district ; and the land office for said district shall be located at the town of Bismarck, where the North Pacific Railroad intersects the Missouri River.

## § 2.

That a register and a receiver shall be appointed for said district land office, who shall be governed by the same laws and receive the same compensation as prescribed for similar officers in the other land districts of said Territory.


*Approved, April 24, 1874.*

# Chapter 26

> An Act in relation to the monument erected to the memory of the Chevalier de Ternay.

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of eight hundred dollars or so much thereof as may be necessary is hereby appropriated out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to defray the expense of repairing am} protecting from decay the monument erected at Newport, Rhode Island, to the memory of the Chevalier de Ternay, the commander of the French naval forces in aid of the American Revolution in the year seventeen hundred and eighty ; the money hereby appropriated to be expended under the direction of the Secretary of the Navy.


*Approved, February 11, 1874.*

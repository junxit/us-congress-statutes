# Chapter 193

> An act to make an appropriation for public buildings at Covington, Kentucky.

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of one hundred and fifty thousand dollars be, and is hereby, appropriated for the completion of public buildings at Covington, Kentucky.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

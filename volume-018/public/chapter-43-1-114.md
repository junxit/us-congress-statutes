# Chapter 114

> An Act authorizing the changing of the name of the steamer Fannie Lehr.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to allow the owners of the merchant steamer “Fannie Lehr,” a vessel of American ownership and register, to change her name, and be hereafter known as the “ Cockade City.”


*Approved, April 18, 1874.*

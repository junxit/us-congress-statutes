# Chapter 20

> An Act providing for busts of the late Chief Justice Roger Brooke Taney and of Samuel Portland Chase, to be placed in the Supreme Court room of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Joint Committee of the two Houses of Congress on the Library be, and they are hereby authorized to procure-and place in the room of the Supreme Court of the United States, busts of the late Chief Justice Roger Brooke Taney and of the late Salmon Portland Chase.

## § 2.

That for the purpose of carrying this act into effect, the sum of two thousand five hundred dollars, or so much thereof as may be necessary, be, and the same is hereby, appropriated out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, January 29, 1874.*

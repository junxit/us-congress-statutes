# Chapter 264

> An Act to change the name of the schooner "China."

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That authority is hereby granted to the owners of the schooner “China,” built at Trenton, Michigan, in the year eighteen hundred and seventy-three, and registered at the port of Detroit in said State, to change the name of said schooner to that of “Canton,” by which name said schooner shall be known and registered.


*Approved, June 9, 1874.*

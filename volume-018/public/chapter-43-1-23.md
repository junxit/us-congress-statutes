# Chapter 23

> An Act to change the name of the pleasure-yacht "Fearless"

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That authority is hereby granted to the owner of the pleasure-yacht “ Fearless,” of the port of Detroit, State of Michigan, to change the name of said yacht to that of “Lillie” by which name said vessel shall be known and registered.


*Approved, February 7, 1874.*

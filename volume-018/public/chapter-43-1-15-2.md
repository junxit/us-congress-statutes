# Chapter 15

> An Act in relation to the printing of the Biennial Register commonly called the Blue Book.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in lieu of the number of copies of the Biennial Register now authorized by law to be printed, the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to cause to be printed fifteen hundred copies of the said work.


*Approved, January 23, 1874.*

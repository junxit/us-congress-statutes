# Chapter 120

> An Act to declare the true intent and meaning of the twentieth section of an act passed by the legislature of the Territory of Dakota, passed January fourteenth, eighteen hundred and seventy-five, entitled “An act making the conveyance of homesteads not valid unless the wife joins in the conveyance.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the twentieth section of the act named in the title shall not be construed as an absolute repeal of chapter thirty-seven of the laws of Dakota, approved May twelfth, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, but only as repealing so much of said chapter thirty-seven as is inconsistent with the first named act, and no other effect shall be given to said twentieth section.


*Approved, March 2, 1875.*

# Chapter 420

> An Act to change the name of the pleasure-yacht “Blanchette,” to that of “Laxen.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of the pleasure-yacht “Planchette,” owned by Samuel Johnson, of Boston, State of Massachusetts, be, and hereby is, changed to that of “Laxen,” and the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby authorized to grant said vessel proper marine-papers in said name.


*Approved, June 22, 1874.*

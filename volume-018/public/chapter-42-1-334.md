# Chapter 334

> An Act prohibiting the publication of the Revised Statutes of the United States in the newspapers at the expense of the United States

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the revised Statutes of the United States and no part thereof shall be published in the newspapers at the expense of the United States.


*Approved, June 20, 1874.*

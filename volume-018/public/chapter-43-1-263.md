# Chapter 263

> An Act to change the name of the registered steamer Oakes Ames to Champlain.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Staten of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of the steamer Oakes Ames, official number nineteen thousand two hundred and eighty-one, registered in the district of Vermont, be and the same is hereby changed to Champlain, and by that name shall hereafter be registered, licensed and known.


*Approved, June 9, 1874.*

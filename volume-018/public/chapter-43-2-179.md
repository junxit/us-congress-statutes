# Chapter 179

> An act extending the privilege of the Library of Congress to the Regents of the Smithsonian Institution.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Joint Committee of both Houses of Congress on the Library be authorized to extend the use of the books in the Library of Congress to the Regents of the Smithsonian Institution resident in Washington on the same conditions and restrictions as members of Congress are allowed to use the Library.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

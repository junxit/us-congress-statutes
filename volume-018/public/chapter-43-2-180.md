# Chapter 180

> An act to change the name of the pleasure yacht “Dolly Varden” to “Clochette.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the owner of the pleasure yacht “Dolly Varden,” of Boston, Massachusetts, be authorized to change the name of said boat to “Clochette,” and that from the passage of this act she be entitled to registry by that name.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

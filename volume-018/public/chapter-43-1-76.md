# Chapter 76

> An Act to authorize the Secretary of the Treasury to issue an American register to the schooner "Carrie," of Eastport, Maine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United, States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and is hereby, authorized to grant and issue an American register to the schooner “Carrie,” now owned in Eastport, Maine, under such name as the owners may select.


*Approved, April 3, 1874.*

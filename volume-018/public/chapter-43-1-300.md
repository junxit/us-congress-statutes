# Chapter 300

> An Act fixing the time for the election of Representatives from the State of Pennsylvania to the Forty-fourth Congress.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That on the Tuesday next following the first Monday in November, eighteen hundred and seventy-four, there shall be elected in each congressional district in the State of Pennsylvania one Representative to represent said State in the Forty-fourth Congress of the United States.


*Approved, June 18, 1874.*

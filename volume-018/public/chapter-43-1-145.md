# Chapter 145

> An Act to grant an American register to the barque "Azor."

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to issue an American register to the barque “Azor,”.an American built vessel employed as a packet between the island of Fayal and the United States, which had been transferred to a British subject .in eighteen hundred and sixty-three, and re-transferred to the heirs of Charles W. Dabney, late consul of the United States in the island of Fayal.


*Approved, May 6, 1874.*

# Chapter 75

> An Act to improve the mouth of the Mississippi River.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of thirty thousand dollars, to be expended by the Secretary of War at the mouth of the Mississippi River ; said sum to be available from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, April 3, 1874.*

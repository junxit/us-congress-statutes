# Chapter 467

> An Act to authorize the use of gilt letters for the names of vessels.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, section three of the act entitled “An act. concerning the registering and recording of ships and vessels,” approved December thirty-first, seventeen hundred and ninety-two, is hereby so amended as to allow the name of any vessel to be painted upon her stern in yellow or gilt letters.


*Approved, June 23, 1874.*

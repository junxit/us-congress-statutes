# Chapter 202

> An act to provide for the re-publication of the First Volume of the Patent-Office Gazette.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of Patents be, and he is hereby, authorized to have printed, at the government Printing Office, the letterpress of the first year of the “Official Gazette,” for the use of the Patent Office.


*Approved, March 3, 1875.*

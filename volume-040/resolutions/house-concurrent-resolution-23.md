# House Concurrent Resolution 23

> trading with the enemy bill.

*Resolved, by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (H. R. 4960) entitled “An Act to define, regulate, and punish trading with the enemy, and for other purposes,” the Clerk be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to strike out the word “it” on page twenty-one, line twenty, and to insert in lieu thereof the word “him”.


*Passed, September 25, 1917.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 69

> grand army encampment, 1919.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall he printed as a House document one thousand five hundred copies of the journal of the Fifty-third National Encampment of the Grand Army of the Republic for the year nineteen hundred and nineteen, not to exceed $1,700 in cost, with illustrations, one thousand copies of which shall be for the use of the House and five hundred for the use of the Senate.


*Passed, February 27, 1919.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 47

> uniform of friendly nations.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (H. R. 11247) entitled “An Act providing for the protection of the uniform of friendly nations, and for other purposes,” the Clerk be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to strike out the word “persons” in line three, page one, and to insert in lieu thereof the word “person”.


*Passed, July 2, 1918.*

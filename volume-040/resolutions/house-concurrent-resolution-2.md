# House Concurrent Resolution 2

> joint meeting

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Monday, the second day of April, nineteen hundred and seventeen, at eight o’clock and thirty minutes post meridian, for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make them.


*Passed, April 2, 1917.*

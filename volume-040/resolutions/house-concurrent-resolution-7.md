# House Concurrent Resolution 7

> army appropriation bill.

*Resolved, by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (H. R. 13) entitled “An Act making appropriations for the support of the Army for the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, nineteen hundred and eighteen, and for other purposes,” the Clerk be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to omit the figures “$75,000,” on page forty-two, line sixteen, of said bill, as the same were stricken out by the amendment of the Senate numbered seventy-eight, the conference report on said bill and amendments to the contrary notwithstanding.


*Passed, May 8, 1917.*

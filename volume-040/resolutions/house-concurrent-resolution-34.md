# House Concurrent Resolution 34

> choctaw and chickasaw lands.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (H. R. 195) entitled “An Act providing for the sale of the coal and asphalt deposits in the segregatedmineral lands in the Choctaw and Chickasaw Nations, Oklahoma,” the Clerk be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to strike out the word “applied” on page five, line thirteen, and to insert in lieu thereof the word “apply.”


*Passed, January 24, 1918.*

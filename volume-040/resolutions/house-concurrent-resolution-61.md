# House Concurrent Resolution 61

> adjournment.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President of the Senate and the Speaker of the House of Representatives be authorized to close the present session of the Congress by adjourning their respective Houses on the twenty-first day of November, nighteen hundred and eighteen, at five o’clock post meridian.


*Passed, November 21, 1918.*

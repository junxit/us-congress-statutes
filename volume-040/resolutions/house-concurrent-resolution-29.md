# House Concurrent Resolution 29

> holiday recess.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Tuesday, the eighteenth day of December, nineteen hundred and seventeen, they stand adjourned until twelve o’clock, meridian, on Thursday, the third day of January, nineteen hundred and eighteen.


*Passed, December 17, 1917.*

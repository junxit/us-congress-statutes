# Chapter 80

> An Act To amend section four of Chapter V of an Act entitled “An Act making appropriations for the support of the Army for the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, nineteen hundred and nineteen,” approved July ninth, nineteen hundred and eighteen, and to make said amendment retroactive.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section four of Chapter V of an Act entitled “An Act making appropriations for the support of the Army for the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, nineteen hundred and nineteen, ” approved July ninth, nineteen hundred and eighteen, be, and the same hereby is, amended, to be effective as of and from July ninth, nineteen hundred and eighteen, by changing the clause “chief nurses, $120, in addition to the pay of a nurse,” to “chief nurses, $360, in addition to the pay of a nurse.”


*Approved, February 28, 1919.*

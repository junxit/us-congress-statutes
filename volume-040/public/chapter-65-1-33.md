# Chapter 33

> Joint Resolution To correct an error in the sundry civil appropriation Act for the fiscal year nineteen hundred and eighteen.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That that portion of the Act entitled “An Act making appropriations for sundry civil expenses of the Government for the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, nineteen hundred and eighteen, and for other purposes,” which reads: “For maintenance and operation of the Panama Canal, salary of the governor, $100,000,” is amended to read as follows: “For maintenance and operation of the Panama Canal, salary of the governor, $10,000;”.


*Approved, June 21, 1917.*

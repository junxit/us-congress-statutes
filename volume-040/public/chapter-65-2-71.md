# Chapter 71

> An Act Authorizing postage rates on aeroplane mail.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General, in his discretion, may require the payment of postage on mail carried by aeroplane at not exceeding 24 cents per ounce or fraction thereof.


*Approved, May 10, 1918.*

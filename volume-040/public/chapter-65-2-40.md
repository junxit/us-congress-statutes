# Chapter 40

> An Act To authorize the President to reduce temporarily the course of instruction at the United States Naval Academy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and ho is hereby, authorized, until August first, nineteen hundred and twenty-one, to reduce, in his discretion, the course of instruction at the United States Naval Academy from four to three years and to graduate classes which have completed such reduced courses of instruction.


*Approved, April 2, 1918.*

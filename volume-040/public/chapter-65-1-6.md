# Chapter 6

> An Act To provide for the extension of minority enlistments in the naval service.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That hereafter any enlistment for minority in the Navy or Marine Corps may be extended as is provided by law for extending an enlistment for a term of four years, under similar conditions and with like rights, privileges, benefits, and obligations.


*Approved, April 25, 1917.*

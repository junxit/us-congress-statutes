# Chapter 87

> An Act To fix the time of holding court in the Amarillo division of the northern district of Texas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That hereafter the terms of the district court of the United States in the Amarillo division of the northern district of Texas shall be held at Amarillo, Texas, on the third Monday in April and the second Monday in September of each year.


*Approved, March 1, 1919.*

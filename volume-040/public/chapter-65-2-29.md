# Chapter 29

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the assistant to the Secretary of the Interior to sign official papers and documents.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the assistant to the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized to sign such official papers and documents as the Secretary may direct.


*Approved, March 28, 1918.*

# Chapter 109

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the expenditure of money upon lands purchased for use of the Ordnance Department of the Army without reference to section three hundred and fifty-five of the Revised Statutes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section three hundred and fifty-five of the Revised Statutes of the United States shall not apply to the expenditure of appropriations for the Ordnance Department of the Army now available for the purchase of land and for improvements upon such land.


*Approved, October 6, 1917.*

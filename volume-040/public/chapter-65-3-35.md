# Chapter 35

> An Act To extend the time for the construction of a bridge across the Red River of the North, between Traill County, North Dakota, and Polk County, Minnesota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge, authorized by Act of Congress approved August eleven, nineteen hundred and sixteen, to be built across the Red River of the North at or near the village of Bellmont, Traill County, North Dakota, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from the date of approval hereof.

## § 2.

That the right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, February 25, 1919.*

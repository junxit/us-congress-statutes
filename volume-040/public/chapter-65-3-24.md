# Chapter 24

> An Act To amend the public-building Act, approved March fourth, nineteen hundred and thirteen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provision of the public-building Act approved March fourth, nineteen hundred and thirteen (Thirty-seventh Statutes, page eight hundred and seventy-six), which authorizes the acquisition of a suitable site, and so forth, at Eureka, Utah, be, and the same is hereby, amended so as to add the following proviso, namely: “Provided, That the Secretary of the Treasury may, in his discretion, accept a title which reserves or excepts all ores or minerals on the lands with the right of mining the same.”


*Approved, February 25, 1919.*

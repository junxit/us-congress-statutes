# Chapter 10

> An Act To amend section three hundred and thirty-six of the Revised Statutes of the United States relating to the annual report on the statistics of commerce and navigation of the United States with foreign countries.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section three hundred and thirty-six of the Revised Statutes of the United States be, and the same is hereby, amended by striking out the word “fiscal” immediately preceding the word “year” at the end of the first sentence of said section, and by inserting in lieu thereof the word “calendar.”


*Approved, January 25, 1919.*

# Chapter 24

> Joint Resolution Making immediately available moneys appropriated for the investigation of the pneumatic mail-tube service in the Act approved March third, nineteen hundred and seventeen.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the appropriation for the investigation of the pneumatic mail-tube service, under the provisions of the Act approved March third, nineteen hundred and seventeen, be, and the same is hereby, made immediately available.


*Approved, June 1, 1917.*

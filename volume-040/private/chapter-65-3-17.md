# Chapter 17

> An Act For the relief of Ira G. Kilpatrick and Guy D. Dill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the title of Ira G, Kilpatrick and Guy D. Dill, as tenants in common, in and to the west half of the southeast quarter of section three, township thirty-two north, range eight east, in the district of lands subject to sale at Fort Wayne, Indiana, in Whitley County, Indiana, as assignee from Jonathan Pearson, by conveyance be, and the same is hereby, quieted and confirmed, and patent therefor shall issue to the said Ira G. Kilpatrick and Guy D. Dill as tenants in common, upon payment to the United States of $1.25 per acre.


*Approved, February 4, 1919.*

# Chapter 43

> An Act For the relief of David E. Gray.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the accounts of David E. Gray, postmaster at Greeley, Colorado, in the sum of $10,088.09, due to the United States on account of postage stamps and other stamped paper on account of losses resulting from burglary of said post office on July first, nineteen hundred and eleven.


*Approved, February 25, 1919.*

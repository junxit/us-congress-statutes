# Public Resolution 38

> Joint Resolution To continue the provisions of a Joint Resolution approved June twenty-ninth, eighteen hundred and ninety-four, entitled a “Joint Resolution to provide temporarily for the expenditures of the Government.”

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of a Joint Resolution entitled a “Joint Resolution to provide temporarily for the expenditures of the Government,” approved June twenty-ninth, eighteen hundred and ninety-four, be, and the same are hereby, extended and continued in full force and effect to and including the fourteenth day of August, eighteen hundred and ninety-four.


*Approved, July 31, 1894.*

# Concurrent Resolution

> chickamauga park.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives, (the Senate concurring),*

That the invitation of the Honorable Secretary of War be accepted and that a joint special committee of fifteen members is hereby created, nine of whom shall be appointed by the Speaker of the House, and six by the Presiding Officer of the Senate, whose duty it shall be to prepare and report to their respective Houses for consideration a plan for the proper participation of Congress in the dedication of the Chickamauga and Chattanooga National Military Park, on September nineteenth and twentieth next.


*Passed the House of Representatives, February 1, 1895. Passed the Senate, February 9, 1895.*

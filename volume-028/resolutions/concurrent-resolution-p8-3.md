# Concurrent Resolution

> ethnology report.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed at the Government Printing Office 8,000 copies of the Thirteenth Annual Report of the Director of the Bureau of Ethnology, with accompanying papers and illustrations, and uniform with the preceding volumes of the series, of which 1,000 shall be for the use of the Senate, 2,000 for the use of the House of Representatives, and 5,000 for distribution by the Bureau of Ethnology; the illustrations to be obtained by the Public, Printer, under the direction of the Joint Committee on Printing.


*Passed the Senate, January 12, 1894. Passed the House of Representatives, January 18, 1894.*

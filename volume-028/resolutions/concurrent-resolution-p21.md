# Concurrent Resolution

> hon. myron b. wright.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Semite concurring),*

That the eulogies delivered in Congress upon the late Myron B. Wright, late a Representative from the State of Pennsylvania, be printed as required by law.


*Passed the House of Representatives March 2, 1895. Passed the Senate, March 2, 1895.*

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

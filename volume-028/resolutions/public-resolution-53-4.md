# Public Resolution 4

> Joint Resolution To make the provisions of the act of May Fourteenth, Ono Thousand Eight Hundred and Ninety, which provides for townsite entries of lands in a portion of what is known as Oklahoma applicable to the territory known as the “Cherokee Outlet”, and to make the provisions of said act applicable to town-sites in the “Cherokee Outlet.”

*Resolved, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all the provisions of an act of Congress, approved May Fourteenth, One Thousand Eight Hundred and Ninety, which provides for townsite entries of lands in a portion of what is known as “Oklahoma,” be, and the same are hereby, made applicable to the territory known as the “Cherokee Outlet,” and now a part of the Territory of Oklahoma; and that all acts or parts of acts inconsistent with this joint resolution be and the same are hereby repealed.


*Approved, September 1, 1893.*

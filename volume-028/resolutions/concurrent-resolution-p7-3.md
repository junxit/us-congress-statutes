# Concurrent Resolution

> bridge, hudson river.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That line 22 on page 2 of the bill (H. R. No. 3289) entitled “An act to authorize the New York and New Jersey Bridge Companies to construct and maintain a bridge across the Hudson River between New York City and the State of New Jersey,” as enrolled by the Clerk of the House of Representatives, consisting of the following words, namely, “And provided further, That nothing in this act,” which words were included in said bill by error in the conference report, be, and the same are hereby, stricken from said bill, and said bill is directed to be enrolled without said line and words.


*Passed the Senate, January 4, 1894. Passed the House of Representatives, January 8, 1894.*

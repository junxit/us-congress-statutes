# Concurrent Resolution

> production of precious metals, 1893.

*Resolved by the House of Represents fives (the Senate concurring),*

That the report of the Director of the Mint on the production of the precious metals in the United States for the calendar year 1893 be printed: and that 10,000 extra copies be printed, 4,000 copies for the use of the House of Representatives, 2,000 copies for the use of the Senate, and 4,000 copies for the use of the Director of the Mint.


*Passed the House of Representatives, June 20, 1894. Passed the Senate, July 6, 1894.*

# Concurrent Resolution

> weather bureau report, 1893.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed five thousand live hundred copies of the Annual Report of the Chief of the Weather Bureau for the year ended December thirty-first, eighteen hundred and ninety-three, of which one thousand copies shall be for the use of the Senate, two thousand copies for the use of the House, and two thousand five hundred copies for the use of the Bureau.


*Passed the House of Representatives, May 25, 1894. Passed the Senate, May 24, 1894.*

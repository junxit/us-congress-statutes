# Public Resolution 12

> Joint Resolution Making an appropriation of five thousand dollars for clearing the Potomac River of ice.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That five thousand dollars, or so much thereof as may be necessary, payable from any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated and from the revenues of the District of Columbia in equal parts, to be immediately available, is hereby appropriated, to enable the Commissioners of the District of Columbia to meet expenses that may be necessary for the purpose of clearing the Potomac River of ice within the District of Columbia.


*Approved, February 15, 1895.*

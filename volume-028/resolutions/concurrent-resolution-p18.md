# Concurrent Resolution

> pension to richard r. knight.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the House of Representatives the enrolled bill (H. R. 5377) entitled “An Act to pension Richard R. Knight,” for the purpose of correcting an error in the text.


*Passed the House of Representatives, February 2, 1895. Passed the Senate, February 4, 1895.*

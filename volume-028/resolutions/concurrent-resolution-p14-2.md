# Concurrent Resolution

> albert e. redstone.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate the bill (S. 1105) “For the relief of Albert E. Redstone.”


*Passed Senate, July 18, 1894. Passed the House of Representatives, July 18, 1894.*

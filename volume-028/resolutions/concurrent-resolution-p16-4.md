# Concurrent Resolution

> statue of john stark.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the thanks of Congress be given to the people of New Hampshire for the statue of John Stark, illustrious for military services, being especially distinguished at Bunker Hill and as the victorious commander at Bennington.

That the statue be accepted and placed in the National Statuary Hall, and that a copy of these resolutions, signed by the Presiding Officers of the Senate and House of Representatives, be forwarded to His Excellency the Governor of the State of New Hampshire.


*Passed the Senate, December 20, 1894. Passed the House of Representatives, December 20, 1894.*

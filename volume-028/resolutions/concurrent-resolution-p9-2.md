# Concurrent Resolution

> imported merchandise, 1890–1893.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives, (the Senate concurring),*

that there be printed, of the document entitled “Imported Merchandise entered for Consumption in the United States during the years eighteen hundred and ninety to eighteen hundred and ninety-three with the rates and amount of duty collected,” prepared by the Bureau of Statistics, Treasury Department, for the Committee on Ways and Means of the House of Representatives, four thousand copies for the use of the House, two thousand copies for the use of the Senate and two thousand copies for the use of the Bureau of Statistics.


*Passed the House of Representatives, December 11, 1893. Passed the Senate, February 21, 1894.*

# Concurrent Resolution

> statue of daniel webster.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the thanks of Congress be presented to the State of New Hampshire for the statue of Daniel Webster, a citizen of that State, illustrious for historic renown and for distinguished civic service.

That the statue be accepted and placed in the National Statuary Hall in the Capitol, and that a copy of these resolutions, duly authenticated, be transmitted to His Excellency the Governor of New Hampshire.


*Passed the Senate, December 20, 1894. Passed the House of Representatives, December 21, 1894.*

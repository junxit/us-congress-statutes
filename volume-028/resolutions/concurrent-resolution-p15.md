# Concurrent Resolution

> report no. 227. committee on foreign relations.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed six thousand copies of the report of the Committee on Foreign Relations, Numbered two hundred and twenty-seven, with the additional views of members of the committee and the testimony, two thousand copies for the use of the Senate and four thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives.


*Passed the Senate, March 5, 1894. Passed the House of Representatives, July 27, 1894.*

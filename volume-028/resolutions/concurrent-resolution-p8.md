# Concurrent Resolution

> fish commission report.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Report of the Commissioner of Fish and Fisheries, covering the operations of the Commission for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1893, be printed; and that there be printed 8,000 extra copies, of which 2,000 shall be for the use of the Senate, 4,000 for the use of the House of Representatives, and 2,000 for the use of the Commissioner of Fish and Fisheries; the illustrations to be obtained by the Public Printer, under the direction of the Joint Committee on Printing.


*Passed the Senate, January 12, 1894. Passed the House of Representatives, January 15, 1894.*

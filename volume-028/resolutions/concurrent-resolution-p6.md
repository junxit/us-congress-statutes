# Concurrent Resolution

> congressional record.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Public Printer be directed to furnish to each Representative, and Delegate fifteen additional copies of the Congressional Record during the first session of the Fifty-third Congress.


*Passed the House of Representatives, November 3, 1893. Passed the Senate, November 2, 1893.*

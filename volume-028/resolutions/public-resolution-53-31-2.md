# Public Resolution 31

> Joint Resolution To extend time in which Members of the Fifty-third Congress may distribute documents.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time allowed Members of the Fifty-third Congress to distribute public documents now to their credit, or the credit of their respective districts in the Interior or other departments, and to present the names of libraries, public institutions, and individuals to receive such documents, be, and the same is hereby, extended to the meeting of the next Congress.


*Approved, March 2, 1895.*

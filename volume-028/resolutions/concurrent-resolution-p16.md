# Concurrent Resolution

> adjournment, 1894.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives,*

That the President of the Senate and Speaker of the House of Representatives be authorized to close the present session by adjourning their respective Houses on the twenty-eighth day of August, instant, at two o’clock p. m.


*Passed the House of Representatives, August 24, 1894. Passed the Senate, August 24, 1894.*

# Concurrent Resolution

> geological report.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed at, the Government Printing Office, in addition to the number already ordered by law, 7,000 copies of the Fourteenth Annual Report of the Director of the United States Geological Survey, uniform with the preceding volumes of the series, of which number 1,000 shall be for the use of the Senate 2,000 for the use of the House of Representatives, and 4,000 tor distribution by the Geological Survey.


*Passed by the Senate, March 20, 1894. Passed by the House of Representatives, March 23, 1894.*

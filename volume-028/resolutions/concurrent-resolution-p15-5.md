# Concurrent Resolution

> civil service commission report.

*Resolved, by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That twenty-three thousand copies of the Tenth Report of the United States Civil Service Commission be printed, of which one thousand copies shall be for the use of the Semite, two thousand for the use of the House of Representatives, and twenty thousand for distribution by the United States Civil Service Commission.


*Passed the House of Representatives, July 12, 1894. Passed the Senate, August 16, 1894.*

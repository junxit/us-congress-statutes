# Concurrent Resolution

> hon. zebulon b. vance.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the eulogies delivered in Congress upon the Hon. Zebulon B. Vance, late a Senator from the State of North Carolina, be printed as required by law.


*Passed the House of Representatives, March 2, 1895. Passed the Senate, March 2, 1895.*

## Marginal notes

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

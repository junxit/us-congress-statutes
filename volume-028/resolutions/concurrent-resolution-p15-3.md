# Concurrent Resolution

> arredondo grant, florida.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, requested to suspend the opening of the lands in the Arredondo grant of twenty thousand acres in Columbia County, Florida, to entry under the land laws of the United States until the close of the present Congress, unless there is further legislation with reference thereto before that time.


*Passed the Senate, August 3, 1894. Passed the House of Representatives, August 7, 1894.*

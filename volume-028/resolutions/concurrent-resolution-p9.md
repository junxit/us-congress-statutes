# Concurrent Resolution

> smithsonian and national museum reports.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed of the reports of the Smithsonian Institution and the National Museum for the year ending June 3d, 1893, in two octavo volumes, 10,000 extra copies; of which 1,000 copies shall be for the use of the Senate, 2,000 copies for the use of the House of Representatives, 5,000 copies for the use of the Smithsonian Institution, and 2,000 copies for the use of the National Museum.


*Passed the Senate, January 17, 1894. Passed the House of Representatives, January 18, 1894.*

# Public Resolution 53

> Joint Resolution To change the initials of a name in the Indian appropriation bill.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the appropriation to pay “F. G.” Niedringhaus, for beef cows delivered at Fort Peck agency, be corrected to read “W. F.” Niedringhaus.


*Approved, August 28, 1894.*

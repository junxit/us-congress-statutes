# Concurrent Resolution

> minnesota river.

*Be it Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby authorized and instructed to transmit to the House of Representatives any reports that may have been received, since his last annual report pertaining to the improvement on the Minnesota River in the State of Minnesota.


*Passed the House of Representatives, June 28, 1894. Passed the Senate, July 2, 1894.*

# Concurrent Resolution

> statistical abstract, 1893.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there, be printed twelve thousand copies of the Statistical Abstract of the United States for the year eighteen hundred and ninety-three, prepared by the Bureau of Statistics, Treasury Department; three thousand copies for the use of the members of the Senate, six thousand copies for the use of the members of the House of Representatives, and three thousand copies for the use of the Bureau of Statistics, Treasury Department.


*Passed the House of Representatives, March 19, 1894. Passed the Senate, March 21, 1894.*

# Concurrent Resolution

> hon. william lilly.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed of the eulogies delivered in Congress upon the Hon. William Lilly, late a Representative from the State of Pennsylvania, eight thousand copies, of which number two thousand copies shall be delivered to the Senators and Representatives of the State of Pennsylvania which shall include fifty copies to be bound in full morocco, to be delivered to the family of the deceased, and of the remaining, two thousand shall be for the use of the Senate and four thousand for the use of the House of Representatives; and the Secretary of the Treasury is directed to have engraved and printed u portrait of the said William Lilly to accompany the said eulogies.


*Passed the House of Representatives, March 5, 1894. Passed the Senate, March 20, 1894.*

# Concurrent Resolution

> death of hon. isaac p. gray.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Congress of the United States has learned with high gratification of the special honors paid to the memory of the deceased, by the Government of Mexico upon the occasion of the death of the Hon. Isaac P. Gray, late Minister of the United States, near the Republic of Mexico.

That in token of the appreciation of the same, the Secretary of State be requested to forward to the Mexican Government an enrolled copy of this resolution.


*Passed the Senate, February 21, 1895. Passed the House of Representatives, February 22, 1895.*

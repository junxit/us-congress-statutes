# Public Resolution 44

> Joint Resolution To continue the provisions of existing lawn providing temporarily for the expenditures of the Government.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of joint resolutions approved June twenty-ninth and July thirty-first, eighteen hundred and ninety-four, providing temporarily for the expenditures of the Government, be, and the same are hereby, extended and continued in full force and effect to and including the twentieth day of August, eighteen bundled and ninety-four.


*Approved, August 15, 1894.*

# Concurrent Resolution

> health report, district of columbia, 1893.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Public Printer be, and he is hereby, authorized to print and bind in cloth twelve hundred copies of the annual report of the Health Officer of the District of Columbia for eighteen hundred and ninety-three, for the use of said Health Officer of the District.


*Passed the House of Representatives, April 28, 1894. Passed the Senate, April 20, 1894.*

# Public Resolution 45

> Joint Resolution To extend the charter of the Maryland and Washington Railway Company.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time for building and completing the railway provided for in an Act entitled “Au Act to incorporate the Maryland and Washington Railway Company,” approved August first, eighteen hundred and ninety-two, be and the same is hereby, extended six months from and after the first day of August, eighteen hundred and ninety-four.


*Approved, August 23, 1894.*

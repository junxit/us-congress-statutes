# Concurrent Resolution

> hawaiian affairs.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of Congress six thousand (6,000) copies of all papers and messages sent to Congress by the President since January 1, 1893, relating to Hawaiian affairs, and the same shall be arranged, as nearly as may be, in chronological order, and shall be indexed; the arranging and indexing to be done under the direction of the Committee on Foreign Relations; of which number 2,000 shall be for the use of the Senate and 4,000 for the use of the House of Representatives.


*Passed the Senate, February 21, 1894. Passed the House of Representatives, March 23, 1894.*

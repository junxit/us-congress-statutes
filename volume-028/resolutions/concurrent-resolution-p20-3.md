# Concurrent Resolution

> hon. philip sidney post.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the eulogies delivered in Congress upon the Hon. Philip Sidney Post late a Representative from the State of Illinois, be printed as required by law.


*Passed the House of Representatives, March 2, 1895. Passed the Senate, March 2, 1895.*

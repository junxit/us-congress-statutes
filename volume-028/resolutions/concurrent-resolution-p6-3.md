# Concurrent Resolution

> holiday recess, 1893–1894.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Thursday the twenty-first day of December present, they stand adjourned until Wednesday the third day of January, eighteen hundred and ninety-four.


*Passed the House of Representatives, December 20, 1893. Passed the Senate, December 20, 1893.*

# Concurrent Resolution

> hon. francis b. stockbridge.

*Resolved, by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the eulogies delivered in Congress upon the Hon. Francis B. Stockbridge, late a Senator from the State of Michigan, be printed as required by law.


*Passed the House of Representatives, February 28, 1895. Passed the Senate, March 1, 1895.*

## Marginal notes

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

- March 1, 1895 .
- Francis B. Stockbridge. Printing of eulogies ordered.

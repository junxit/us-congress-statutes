# Concurrent Resolution

> hearings before ways and means committee.

*Resolved, by the House of Representatives, (the Senate concurring),*

That the Public Printer be, and is hereby, authorized to print four thousand copies of the Hearings before the Committee on Ways and Means, two thousand copies for the use of the House and two thousand copies for the use of the Senate.


*Passed the House of Representatives, October 12, 1893. Passed the Senate, October 17, 1893.*

# Concurrent Resolution

> cotton report.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed five thousand five hundred copies of the Report of the Committee on Agriculture and Forestry on Cotton, this day submitted, of which five hundred shall be for the use of the said Committee, two hundred for the State Department, and two hundred for the Bureau of Statistics, and one thousand live hundred for the Senate, and three thousand one hundred for the Douse of Representatives.


*Passed the Senate, February 28, 1895. Passed the House of Representatives, March 2, 1895.*

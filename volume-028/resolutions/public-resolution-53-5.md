# Public Resolution 5

> Joint Resolution To make the eighteenth day of September, one thousand eight hundred and ninety-three a holiday within the District of Columbia.

*Resolved, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be added to the days by law declared to be holidays within the District of Columbia the eighteenth day of September one thousand eight hundred and ninety-three, the same being the one hundredth anniversary of the laying of the corner stone of the capitol of the United States.


*Approved, September 9, 1893.*

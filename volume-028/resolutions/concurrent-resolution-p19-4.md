# Concurrent Resolution

> strike report.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed of the Report on the Chicago Strike of June and July, 1894, of the United Suites Strike Commission, appointed by the President, July 26, 1894, ten thousand five hundred extra copies, in paper covers, without appendices, of which number two thousand five hundred shall be for the use of the Senate, live thousand for the use of the House of Representatives, and three thousand for the use of the Department of Labor; also five thousand extra copies of said report, with appendices, in paper covers, of which number one thousand shall be for the use of the Senate, two thousand for the use of the House and two thousand for the use of the Department of Labor.


*Passed the Senate, February 11, 1895. Passed the House of Representatives, March 2, 1895.*

# Public Resolution 12

> Joint Resolution That the acknowledgments of the Government and people of the United States be tendered to various foreign governments of the world who have participated in commemoration of the discovery of America by Christopher Columbus.

*Resolved, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

- **(1)** That it is the sense of Congress that the acknowledgments of the government and people of the United States, be tendered to the various foreign governments of the world, who have so generously and effectively cooperated in the Quadri-centennial Exposition held in Chicago, in commemoration of the discovery of America by Christopher Columbus.
- **(2)** That the President of the United States be requested to communicate to each foreign government that has participated in said Exposition the acknowledgment of Congress for its contribution.

*Approved, October 28, 1893.*

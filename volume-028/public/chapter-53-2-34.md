# Chapter 34

> An Act To establish a port of delivery at Bonners Ferry, Idaho.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Bonners Ferry, Kootenai County, Idaho, be a port of delivery in the customs collection district of Montana and Idaho.


*Approved, March 10, 1894.*

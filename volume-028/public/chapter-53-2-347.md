# Chapter 347

> An Act For the registry or enrollment of the bark Skudesnaes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of Navigation is hereby authorized and directed to admit to registry as a bark of the United States the bark Skudesnaes, a foreign-built vessel, wrecked in American waters, purchased, repaired, and owned by C. C. Pinckney, junior, of Charleston, South Carolina, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved, August 27, 1894.*

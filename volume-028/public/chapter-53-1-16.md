# Chapter 16

> An Act To regulate the fees of the clerk of the United States Court for the Indian Territory.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in all cases where the clerk of the United States court for the Indian Territory is authorized or required to perform duties other than those performed by the clerks of the district and circuit courts of the United States he shall be entitled to receive and retain for his own use and benefit such fees as may be allowed by law for such services.


*Approved, November 3, 1893.*

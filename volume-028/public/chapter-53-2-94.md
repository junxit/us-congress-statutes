# Chapter 94

> An Act To extend mid amend an Act entitled “An Act to authorize the Kansas and Arkansas Valley Railway to construct and operate additional lines of railway through the Indian Territory, and for other purposes,” approved February twenty-fourth, anno Domini eighteen hundred and ninety-one.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of an Act entitled “An Act to authorize the Kansas and Arkansas Valley railway to construct and operate additional lines of railway through the Indian Territory, and for other purposes,” approved February twenty-fourth, eighteen hundred and ninety-one, be, and the same, are hereby, extended for a period of three years from February twenty-fourth, eighteen hundred and ninety-four, so that said Kansas and Arkansas Valley Railway shall have until February twenty-fourth, eighteen hundred and ninety-seven, to build the first one hundred miles of its said additional lines of railway in said Territory.


*Approved, June 6, 1894.*

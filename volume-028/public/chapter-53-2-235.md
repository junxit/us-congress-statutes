# Chapter 235

> An Act Disposing of four condemned cannon of the Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to supply the Grand Army Post of Sac City, Iowa, with four condemned cannon for use in decorating the pedestal of the soldiers’ monument in Sac City, Iowa.


*Approved, August 7, 1894.*

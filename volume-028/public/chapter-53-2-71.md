# Chapter 71

> An Act To extend the limits of the port of New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limits of the port of New York, in the State of New York, be, and the same are hereby, extended so as to include the city of Yonkers, Westchester County, in said State.


*Approved, May 7, 1894.*

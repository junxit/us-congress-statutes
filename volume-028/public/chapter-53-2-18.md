# Chapter 18

> An Act To amend an Act approved September fourth, eighteen hundred and ninety, authorizing the New Orleans, Natchez and Fort Scott Railroad Company to construct two bridges across Boeuf River, in Louisiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act approved September fourth, eighteen hundred and ninety, entitled, “An Act to authorize the construction of two bridges across Boeuf River, Louisiana,” be, and is hereby, amended so that the time within which the actual construction of said bridges may be commenced is hereby extended for the period of one year from the date of the approval of this Act.


*Approved, January 22, 1894.*

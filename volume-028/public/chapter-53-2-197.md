# Chapter 197

> An Act Adding the towns of Manchester and Vernon, in the State of Connecticut, to the customs district of Hartford, Connecticut, and making the city of Rocksville, Connecticut, a port of delivery.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of March third, eighteen hundred and eighty-seven, creating the customs district of Hartford, be amended by adding the towns of Manchester and Vernon, in the State of Connecticut, to the towns already named as comprising said district, and that the town of Vernon, city of Rockville, in said State, of Connecticut, within said district of Hartford, is hereby constituted a port of delivery within the meaning of said Act of March third, eighteen hundred and eighty-seven, and the Act of which said Act of March third, eighteen hundred and eighty-seven, is an amendment.

## § 2.

That the privileges of the seventh section of the Act of June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, entitled “An Act to amend the statutes in relation to immediate transportation of dutiable goods and for other purposes,” be, and they are hereby, extended to the said town of Vernon, city of Rockville, district and State aforesaid.


*Approved, August 3, 1894.*

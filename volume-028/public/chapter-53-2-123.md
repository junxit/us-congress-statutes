# Chapter 123

> An Act To provide that a term of the circuit and district court of the United. States for the district of Vermont may be held at Montpelier.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That hereafter in each year one of the stated terms of the circuit and district court for the district of Vermont may, when adjourned, be adjourned to meet at Montpelier.

## § 2.

That all Acts and parts of Acts in conflict with this Act are hereby repealed.


*Approved, July 3, 1894.*

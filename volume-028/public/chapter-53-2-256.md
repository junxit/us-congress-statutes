# Chapter 256

> An Act To make the city of Oakland, county of Alameda, State of California, a subport of entry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the city of Oakland, county of Alameda, State of California, be, and the same is hereby, made a subport of entry in the district of San Francisco, with the privileges of section seven of an Act entitled “An Act to amend the statutes in relation to immediate transportation of dutiable goods, and for other purposes,” approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty.


*Approved, August 11, 1894.*

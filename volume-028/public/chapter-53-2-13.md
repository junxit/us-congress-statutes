# Chapter 13

> An Act For the establishment of a light and fog-signal station near Butler Flats. New Bedford. Massachusetts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be established, on or near Butler Flats, entrance to the lower harbor of New Bedford, Massachusetts, a light and fog-signal station, at a cost not to exceed forty-five thousand dollars.


*Approved, January 22, 1894.*

# Chapter 31

> An Act To extend the time for building a street railway on the military reservation, Fort Riley, Kansas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of Congress granting right of way to the Junction City and Fort Riley Rapid Transit Street Railway Company into and upon the Fort Riley military reservation, in the State of Kansas, approved February twenty-seventh, eighteen hundred and ninety-one, is hereby amended so that the time for building the said street railway shall be extended eighteen months beyond the date mentioned in the said Act so as to cease and determine on the twenty-seventh day of August, eighteen hundred and ninety-five.


*Approved, March 6, 1894.*

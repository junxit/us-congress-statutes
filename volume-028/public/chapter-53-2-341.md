# Chapter 341

> An Act To amend an Act entitled “An Act to create a new division of the northern judicial district of Georgia,” approved March third, eighteen hundred and ninety-one.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the terms of the circuit and district courts for the western division of the northern judicial District of Georgia, held at Columbus, shall convene on the first Monday in May and the first Monday in December, instead of the first Monday in June and the first Monday in January, as now provided by law. And each of said terms shall continue as long as the presiding judge may deem necessary.

## § 2.

That all laws and parts of laws conflicting with this Act are hereby repealed.


*Approved, August 27, 1894.*

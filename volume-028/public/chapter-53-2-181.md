# Chapter 181

> An Act To provide a register for the schooner barge Astoria.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of Navigation is hereby authorized and directed to cause the ship Astoria, purchased and owned by George Grail and repaired in American ports and converted into a schooner barge, to be registered as a vessel of the United States.


*Approved, August 1, 1894.*

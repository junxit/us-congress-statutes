# Chapter 284

> An Act Relating to lights on fishing vessels.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That article ten of the Act approved March third, eighteen hundred and eighty-five, entitled “An Act to adopt the revised international regulations for preventing collisions at sea,” so far as said article relates to lights for fishing vessels, is hereby’ reenacted and continued in force, anything in the Act approved May’ twenty-eighth, eighteen hundred and ninety-four, entitled “An Act to amend an Act approved August nineteenth, eighteen hundred and ninety, entitled ‘An act to adopt regulations for preventing collisions at sea,’ ” to the contrary’ notwithstanding.


*Approved, August 13, 1894.*

# Chapter 107

> An Act To increase the limit of cost for the erection of a public building at Paterson, New Jersey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the amount heretofore fixed as the limit of cost for the erection of a public building, by the United States Government, at Paterson, New Jersey, be, and the same is hereby, increased to two hundred thousand dollars, and that the same is hereby fixed as the limit of cost for the purchase of a site and the erection thereon of said building.

## § 2.

That the officers of the United States Government having charge of the erection of public buildings are required to be governed by the limitation hereby prescribed in making the plans and contracts for the erection of said building.


*Approved, February 20, 1895.*

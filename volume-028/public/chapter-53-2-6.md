# Chapter 6

> An Act Authorizing the fourth Assistant Postmaster-General to approve postmasters’ bonds.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the passage of this Act the bonds of all postmasters, by the direction of the Postmaster-General, may be approved and accepted and the approval and acceptance signed by the Fourth Assistant Postmaster-General in the name of the Postmaster-General.


*Approved, December 21, 1893.*

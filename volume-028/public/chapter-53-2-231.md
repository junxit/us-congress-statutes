# Chapter 231

> An Act To amend an Act approved January twenty-sixth, eighteen hundred and ninety-three, to authorize the construction of bridges across the Hiwassee, the Tennessee, and Clinch rivers, in the State of Tennessee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act approved January twenty-sixth, eighteen hundred and ninety-three, entitled, “An Act to authorize the construction of bridges across the Hiwassee, the Tennessee, and the Clinch rivers, in the State of Tennessee,” be, and is hereby, amended so that the time, within which the actual construction of said bridges may be commenced is hereby extended for the period of one year from the date of the approval of this Act.


*Approved, August 7, 1894.*

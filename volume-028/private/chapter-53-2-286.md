# Chapter 286

> An Act For the relief of Benjamin Alford.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Benjamin Alford the sum of seventy-nine and twenty one-hundredths dollars, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, which sum is hereby appropriated, and made immediately available therefor.


*Approved, August 13, 1894.*

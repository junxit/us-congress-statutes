# Chapter 266

> An Act Granting a pension to George L. Frymire.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George L. Frymire, a permanently helpless son of William C. Frymire, late a member of Company B, Fifty-sixth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, August 11, 1894.*

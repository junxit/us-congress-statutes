# Chapter 169

> An Act For the relief of the securities of John S. Bradford.

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That John Cassels and Rosalie M. Bradford, securities of John S. Bradford, deceased, United States consul at Antigua, West Indies, from August nineteenth, eighteen hundred and ninety, to August sixth, eighteen hundred and ninety-one, be, and they are, released from the payment of two hundred and thirty-nine dollars and six cents, amount claimed to be due the United States on the official bond of John S. Bradford, and said liability is canceled and his accounts with the Government are deemed adjusted and settled.


*Approved, July 26, 1894.*

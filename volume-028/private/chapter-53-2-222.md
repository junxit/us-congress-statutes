# Chapter 222

> An Act Granting a pension to Celestin P. Hartt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Celestia P. Hartt, widow of Samuel T. Hartt, late naval constructor, and allow her a pension equal to that allowed officers in the Navy of the grade of captain.


*Approved, August 4, 1894.*

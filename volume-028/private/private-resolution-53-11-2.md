# Private Resolution 11

> Joint Resolution Authorizing Major-General Oliver Otis Howard, United States Army, to accept from the President of the French Republic a diploma conferring the decoration of Commander of the National Order of the Legion of Honor.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Major-General Oliver Otis Howard, of the United States Army, is hereby authorized to accept from the President of the French Republic a diploma conferring upon him the decoration of Commander of the National Order of the Legion of Honor.


*Approved, March 9, 1894.*

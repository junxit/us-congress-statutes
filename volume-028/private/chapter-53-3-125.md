# Chapter 125

> An Act To grant a pension to Sarah E. Roebuck.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Sarah E. Roebuck, widow of Lawrence R. Roebuck, late quartermaster’s employee, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 22, 1895.*

# Chapter 118

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Julia Weeks.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Julia Weeks, of Allegan, Michigan, widow of Captain Harrison S. Weeks, Eighth Cavalry, and pay her a pension of thirty dollars per month, the same to be in lieu of the pension now drawn by her.


*Approved, February 21, 1895.*

# Chapter 96

> An Act For the relief of A. P. H. Stewart.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of Internal Revenue be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to reexamine and settle the following claim for tax on bagging and rope wrongfully paid on Government cotton, alleged to have been illegally assessed and collected, and the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the amounts of taxes so found by said Commissioner of Internal Revenue as aforesaid to have been illegally and improperly assessed and collected, to the party hereinafter named: Provided, That the amount paid in any ease shall not exceed the amount hereinafter named:

To A. P. H. Stewart, one hundred and eighty-five dollars and sixty-nine cents.


*Approved, June 6, 1894.*

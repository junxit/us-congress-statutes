# Chapter 236

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Eugenia R. Sweeny.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Eugenia R. Sweeny, widow of Thomas W. Sweeny, late a brigadier-general in the Army of the United States, at the rate of thirty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 2, 1895.*

# Chapter 321

> An Act Granting a pension to Maria Hall, widow of Joseph E. Doak, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Maria Hall, widow of Joseph E. Doak, deceased, late a private in Captain Renshaw’s company of Tennessee militia from December sixth, eighteen hundred and twelve, to April twentieth, eighteen hundred and thirteen, and from September twenty-sixth, eighteen hundred and thirteen, to December tenth, eighteen hundred and thirteen.


*Approved, August 23, 1894.*

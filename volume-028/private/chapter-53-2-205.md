# Chapter 205

> An Act To remove charge of desertion standing against Oliver O’Brien.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy of the United States be, and he is hereby, directed to so amend the record of Oliver O’Brien as to remove the charge of desertion: Provided, That no pay or emolument shall accrue by reason of this Act or pension prior to this date.


*Approved, August 3, 1894.*

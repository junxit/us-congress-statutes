# Chapter 219

> An Act To increase the pension of Major-General Julius H. Stahel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Julius H. Stahel, late major-general of volunteers in the Union Army, and pay same at the rate of fifty dollars per month, in lieu of what he now receives, from and after the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 2, 1895.*

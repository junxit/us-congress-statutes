# Chapter 75

> An Act To pension Nancy Gabrilla Anderson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Nancy Gabrilla Anderson, wholly and permanently helpless daughter of Payton W. Anderson, late of company A, Sixth Regiment Indiana Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the Act approved June twenty-seventh, eighteen hundred and ninety.


*Approved, February 8, 1895.*

# Chapter 198

> An Act For the relief of Maria T. Karge.

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Maria T. Karge, widow of Joseph Karge, colonel Second Regiment New Jersey Cavalry, at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, March 2, 1895.*

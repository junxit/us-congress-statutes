# Chapter 264

> An Act For the relief of Henry James, residing in the original Hot Springs reservation, in the State of Arkansas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United, States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Henry James, who has improved and resides upon lot four, in block one hundred and two, as designated by the survey and map made by the United States commissioners for the Hot Springs reservation, shall have the right to enter and pay for said lot at the land office in Little Rock, Arkansas, within ninety days next after the passage of this Act, at and for the sum of nine hundred dollars.


*Approved, August 11, 1894.*

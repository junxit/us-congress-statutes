# Chapter 325

> An Act Granting a pension to Henry C. Field.

*Be it enacted by the, Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Henry C. Field, dependent father of Daniel Field, late a private of Company H, Twenty-ninth Maine Regiment, and pay him a pension of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, August 23, 1894.*

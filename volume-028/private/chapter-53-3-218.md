# Chapter 218

> An Act Granting a pension to Harrison C. Hobart, brevet brigadier-general of volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, anti he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension rolls the name of Harrison C. Hobart, late brevet brigadier-general of volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, March 2, 1895.*

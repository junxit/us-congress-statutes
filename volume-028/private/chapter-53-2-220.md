# Chapter 220

> An Act Granting a pension to Harriet T. Vosburgh.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Harriet T. Vosburgh, mother of George W. Jarvis, late of Company I, Twenty-first Regiment Massachusetts Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension from the passage of this Act.


*Approved, August 4, 1894.*

# Chapter 76

> An Act Granting a pension to Sarah M. Brown.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah M. Brown, as the foster mother of George Brown, late private Company I, Thirty-sixth Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, February 8, 1895.*

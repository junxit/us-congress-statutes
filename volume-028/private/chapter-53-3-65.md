# Chapter 65

> An Act Granting a pension to Mrs. Katharine Todd Crittenden.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Katharine Todd Crittenden, widow of Thomas L. Crittenden, late a major-general in the United States Army, at the rate of fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 8, 1895.*

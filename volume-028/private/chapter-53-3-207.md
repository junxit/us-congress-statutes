# Chapter 207

> An Act To pension Jane Webster.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Jane Webster, widow of Joel Webster, a former wagon master in the volunteer militia service of the United States during the war of the rebellion, at the rate of twelve dollars per month, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, March 2, 1895.*

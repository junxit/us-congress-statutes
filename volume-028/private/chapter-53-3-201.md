# Chapter 201

> An Act To increase the pension of Mrs. Mary Tassin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to increase to the sum of twenty-five dollars per month the pension of Mrs. Mary Tassin, widow of Augustus G. Tassin, late first lieutenant, Twelfth Regiment United States Infantry; said pension to be in lieu of pension numbered three hundred and ninety-two thousand six hundred and forty-two.


*Approved, March 2, 1895.*

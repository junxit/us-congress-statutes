# Chapter 294

> An Act To correct the naval record, of James Fay and grant him an honorable discharge.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to correct the record of James Fay, late a landsman and fireman on board the United States steamship Shamrock, of the North Atlantic Squadron, and grant him an honorable discharge to date from August sixteenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-five: Provided, That no pension, back pay, bounty, or any other allowance shall be paid to said Fay by virtue of this Act.


*Approved, August 15, 1894.*

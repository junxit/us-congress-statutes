# Chapter 120

> An Act Granting a pension to William Armstrong.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of William Armstrong, late a private in Company A, Gray’s Battalion Arkansas Volunteers, in the war with Mexico, and allow him a pension rated at twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 21, 1895.*

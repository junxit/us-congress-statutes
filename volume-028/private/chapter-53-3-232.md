# Chapter 232

> An Act Granting a pension to Alice K. Potter, widow of General Joseph H. Potter, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Alice K. Potter, widow of the late General Joseph H. Potter, United States Army, subject to the provisions and limitations of existing laws, and pay her a pension of fifty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 2, 1895.*

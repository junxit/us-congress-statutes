# Chapter 203

> An Act For the relief of the trustees of the Presbyterian Church of Bethel Springs, Tennessee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to the trustees of the Presbyterian Church in Bethel Springs, Tennessee, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of four hundred dollars, being for the use, and occupation, of said church by the Army of the United States during the late war.


*Approved, March 2, 1895.*

# Chapter 205

> An Act Granting a pension to Ellen Carney.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the general pension laws, the name of Ellen Carney, dependent mother of Patrick Carney, late a second lieutenant of Company H, Sixty-ninth New York Volunteer Infantry, in the war of the rebellion, and allow the said Ellen Carney a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 2, 1895.*

# Chapter 126

> An Act Granting a pension to Richard R. Knight.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Richard R. Knight, son of Philip T. Knight, alias Patrick Reardon, late of Company B, Thirty-first Regiment Massachusetts Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension subject to the provisions and limitations of the Act approved June twenty-seventh, eighteen hundred and ninety: Provided, That, if any pension be allowed under the general laws on account of the above-named soldier, pension under this Act shall cease.


*Approved, February 22, 1895.*

# Chapter 231

> An Act To provide increase of pension to Hosea Brown, of the war of eighteen hundred and twelve.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Hosea Brown, formerly a member of Captain Edward Burgess’s company of New York militia, and who was in the military service in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve, to fifty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 2, 1895.*

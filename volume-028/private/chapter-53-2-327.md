# Chapter 327

> An Act For the relief of Johanna Gleason.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Johanna Gleason, widow of Thomas Gleason, of Company A, Thirty-fifth New’ York Volunteers, on the pension roll and pay her a pension, subject to the provisions and limitations of the Act approved June twenty-seventh, eighteen hundred and ninety.


*Approved, August 23, 1894.*

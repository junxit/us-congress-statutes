# Chapter 72

> An Act To increase the pension of Pickens T. Reynolds, of Hall County, Georgia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary’ of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Pickens T. Reynolds, of Hall County, Georgia, a soldier of the Indian war of eighteen hundred and thirty-seven and eighteen hundred and thirty-eight, from eight to twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 8, 1895.*

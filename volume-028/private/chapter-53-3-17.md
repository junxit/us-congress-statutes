# Chapter 17

> An Act For the relief of Dollie E. Vedder.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Dollie E. Vedder, widow of the late Captain Simon C. Vedder, of the Nineteenth Regiment, United States Army, on the pension roll, at the rate of twenty dollars per month, in lieu of the amount she now receives.


*Approved, December 29, 1894.*

# Private Resolution 32

> Joint Resolution Authorizing Second Lieutenant F. D. Rockenbach, of the Tenth Cavalry, United States Army, to accept the position of commandant of cadets at the Virginia Military Academy, Lexington, Virginia.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Second Lieutenant F. D. Rockenbach, of the Tenth Cavalry, United States Army, be, and he is hereby, permitted to accept the position of commandant of cadets at the Virginia Military Academy tit Lexington, Virginia, and the emolument pertaining thereto: Provided, That the Secretary of War may, in his discretion, at any time when in his opinion the interests of the service require it, order him upon duty.


*Approved, March 2, 1895.*

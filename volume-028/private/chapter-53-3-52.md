# Chapter 52

> An Act To repeal the Act entitled “An Act granting a pension to Nettie N. Seaver.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act entitled “An Act granting a pension to Nettie N. Seaver,” received by the President August eighth, eighteen hundred and ninety-four, and which became a law without his approval, be, and the same is hereby, repealed.


*Approved, January 28, 1895.*

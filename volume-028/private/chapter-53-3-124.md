# Chapter 124

> An Act Granting a pension to Marrilla Parsons, of Detroit, Michigan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, at the rate of twelve dollars per month, the name of Marilla Parsons, of Detroit, Michigan, dependent stepmother of Daniel P. Parsons, late captain of Company D, Eleventh Missouri Cavalry.


*Approved, February 22, 1895.*

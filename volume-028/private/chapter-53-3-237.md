# Chapter 237

> An Act For the relief of James Curran.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster-General be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to give credit to James Curran, postmaster at Hoboken, New Jersey, in the sum of six thousand five hundred and sixty-six dollars for postage stamps stolen from said office on the third day of December, eighteen hundred and ninety-four, through no fault of the said James Curran.


*Approved, March 2, 1895.*

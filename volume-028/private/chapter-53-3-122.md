# Chapter 122

> An Act To pension Mary R. Williams.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws, the name of Mary R. Williams, widow of Gustavus A. Williams, deceased, late assistant surgeon in the Army of the United States during the Seminole war, at the rate of twelve dollars per month for and during her widowhood.


*Approved, February 21, 1895.*

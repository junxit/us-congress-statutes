# Chapter 89

> An Act Granting a pension to Julia E. Lock, formerly widow of the late General Daniel McCook.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to restore to the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Julia E. Lock, of Sterling, Virginia (formerly widow of the late Daniel McCook, brigadier-general of volunteers), at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 13, 1895.*

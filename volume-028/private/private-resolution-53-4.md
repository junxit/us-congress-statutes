# Private Resolution 4

> Joint Resolution Authorizing Rear-Admiral S. B. Luce, United States Navy, to accept a decoration from the King of Spain.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Rear-Admiral S. B. Luce, of the United States Navy, be, and is hereby, authorized to accept the grand cross of naval merit with the white distinction mark, conferred upon him by the King of Spain in recognition of the services rendered by that officer as a delegate of his Government in the commemoration of the fourth century of the discovery of America, and his active cooperation toward the brilliancy of the acts with which the event has been celebrated.


*Approved, January 16, 1895.*

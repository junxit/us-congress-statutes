# Chapter 340

> An Act To reimburse George C. Tanner, late consul, and so forth, the sum of two hundred dollars, paid by him for rent of rooms.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of two hundred dollars be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, and that the same he paid to George C. Tanner, late consul at Verviers and Liege, for rent of rooms paid by him.

## § 2.

That this Act shall be in force from its passage.


*Approved, August 25, 1894.*

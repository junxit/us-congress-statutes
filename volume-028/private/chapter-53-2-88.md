# Chapter 88

> An Act For the relief of Lennes A. Jackson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of two hundred and seventy dollars be, and the same hereby is, appropriated, out of any moneys in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to refund in full to Lennes A. Jackson the amount paid to James U. Stone, collector internal revenue, Detroit, Michigan, which amount was stolen from the vaults of the Coldwater National Bank, at Coldwater, Michigan, on March second, eighteen hundred and ninety-two.


*Approved, May 30, 1894.*

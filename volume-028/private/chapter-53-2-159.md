# Chapter 159

> An Act Granting a pension to Earnest C. Emerson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Earnest C. Emerson, dependent and imbecile child of Orrin M. Emerson, late a private in Company II, First Rhode Island Cavalry, subject to the limitations and provisions of the Act of June twenty-seventh, eighteen hundred and ninety, at the rate of ten dollars per month, payable to his legally constituted guardian.


*Approved, July 23, 1894.*

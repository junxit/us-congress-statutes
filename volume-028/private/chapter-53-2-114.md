# Chapter 114

> An Act For the relief of Wetmore and Brother, of Saint Louis, Missouri.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby directed to pay to Wetmore and Brother, of Saint Louis, Missouri, the sum of two hundred and twenty dollars, being the amount in part of fees due to them as attorneys in colored bounty cases, retained and covered into the Treasury.


*Approved, June 23, 1894.*

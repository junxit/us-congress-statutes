# Chapter 223

> An Act Granting a pension to Major-General John A. McClernand.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Major-General John A. McClernand, late a major-general in the United States Army, at the rate of one hundred dollars per month.


*Approved, March 2, 1895.*

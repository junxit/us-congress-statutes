# Chapter 41

> An Act To pension Willis Manasco.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Willis Manasco, of Eldridge, Howard County, Arkansas, who served in Captain Philpot’s company of Alabama, Volunteers, Creek Indian war, and pay him a pension of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, January 22, 1895.*

# Chapter 77

> An Act For the relief of Harriet Clayton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Mrs. Harriet Clayton, of Pike County, Missouri, widow of Charles C. Clayton, who served as a soldier in the Mexican war, and pay her a pension of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 8, 1895.*

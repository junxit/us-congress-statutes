# Chapter 650

> AN ACT For the relief of Joseph A. Haddad.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purpose of section 4 (a) and section 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, Joseph A. Haddad, a native and citizen of Lebanon, who is the son of the deceased brother of Mis. Marie Haddad Trucano, a citizen of the United States and a resident of the Territory of Hawaii, shall be considered to be the alien natural-born son of the said Mrs. Marie Haddad Trucano.


*Approved August 8, 1950.*

# Chapter 693

> AN ACT For the relief of Lubomir Mikulik and Viliam Krajcirovic.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration laws, section 4 of the Displaced Persons Act of 1948 (62 Stat. 1011 ; 50 App. U. S. C. 1953), shall be held to be applicable in the cases of Lubomir Mikulik and Viliam Krajcirovic, natives of Czechoslovakia, notwithstanding the fact that they entered the United States illegally after April 1, 1948.


*Approved August 11, 1950.*

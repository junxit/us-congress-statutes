# Chapter 664

> AN ACT For the relief of Therese Hohman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws Therese Hohman, residing in Bethlehem, Pennsylvania, who was admitted into the United States at the port of Boston on a temporary visa, shall be deemed to have been lawfully admitted into the United States for permanent residence as of December 17, 1948; the departure bond shall be canceled upon payment of visa fees and head tax. Upon the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of State is authorized and directed to instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the nonpreference category from the German quota for the first year said quota is available.


*Approved August 9, 1950.*

# Chapter 367

> AN ACT For the relief of Helga Holleb.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of immigration and naturalization laws, the alien Helga Holleb shall be considered to be the natural-born alien daughter of Capta’in and Mrs. Melvyn G. Holleb, of Thomaston, Georgia, citizens of the United States.


*Approved June 26, 1950.*

# Chapter 1095

> AN ACT For the relief of certain Basque aliens.

## § 2.

Upon the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number for each alien named herein from the quota of the appropriate country of the first year that the said quota is available.

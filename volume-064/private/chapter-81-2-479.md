# Chapter 479

> AN ACT For the relief of Nieolae G. Caranfil and his family.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, Nicolae G. Caranfil, his wife, Eufrosina, and their son, Andrei Georges, shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted into the United States for permanent residence as of the date of their last entry into the United States upon payment of the required head taxes and visa fees.

## § 2.

The Secretary of State is authorized and directed to instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct appropriate numbers from the nonpreference category of the proper immigration quota or quotas.


*Approved July 20, 1950.*

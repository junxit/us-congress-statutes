# Chapter 474

> AN ACT For the relief of Ruzina Skalova.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Ruzina Skalova, a native and citizen of Czechoslovakia now residing in Cizice, Czechoslovakia, the fiancee of Lloyd Koehmstedt, a citizen of the United States and an honorably discharged veteran of the armed forces of the United States during World War II, shall be eligible for a visa as a nonimmigrant temporary visitor for a period of three months: Provided, That the administrative authorities find that the said Ruzina Skalova is coming to the United States with the bona fide intention of being married to the said Lloyd Koehmstedt, and that she is found otherwise admissible under the immigration laws. In the event the marriage between the above-named parties does not occur within three months after the entry of the said Ruzina Skalova, she shall be required to depart from the United States, and upon failure to do so shall be deported in accordance with the provisions of sections 19 and 20 of the Immigration Act of February 5, 1917 (U. S. C., title 8, sections 155 and 156). In the event that the marriage between the above-named parties shall occur within three months after the entry of the said Ruzina Skalova, the Attorney General is authorized and directed to record the lawful admission for permanent residence of the said Ruzina Skalova as of the date of her entry into the United States upon the payment of the required head tax and visa fee.


*Approved July 19, 1950.*

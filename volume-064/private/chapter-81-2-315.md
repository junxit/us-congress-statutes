# Chapter 315

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Kiyoko Tanaka Perez.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, which exclude from admission to the United States persons who are ineligible to citizenship, shall not apply to Mrs. Kiyoko Tanaka Perez, a native of Japan, the wife of Joseph M. Perez, a citizen of the United States and an honorably discharged veteran of World War II.


*Approved June 17, 1950.*

# Chapter 865

> AN ACT For the relief of Sirius Proestopoulos.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, the Attorney General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to record the lawful admission for permanent residence of Sirius Proestopoulos who entered the United States on October 6, 1946, at New York, New York, upon the payment of the required visa fee and head taxes.

## § 2.

Upon the enactment of this Act the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the quota for Greece of the first year that such quota number is available.


*Approved September 5, 1950.*

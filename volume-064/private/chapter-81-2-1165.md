# Chapter 1165

> AN ACT For the relief of Lee Yee Yen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Lee Yee Yen, the minor child of Lee Bing Hong, a citizen of the United States, shall be deemed to be eligible for admission into the United States under the provisions of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, if otherwise admissible under the immigration laws.


*Approved December 27, 1950.*

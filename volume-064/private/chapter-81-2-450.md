# Chapter 450

> AN ACT For the relief of Efrosini Abad.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, the provisions of section 4 (a) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, pertaining to the unmarried child under twenty-one years of age of a citizen of the United States, shall be held to be applicable to Efrosini Abad, of Istanbul, Turkey, who is the daughter of a citizen of the United States.


*Approved July 6, 1950.*

# Chapter 156

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Katsuko Nakahara Huntley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, which excludes from admission to the United States persons who are ineligible to citizenship, shall not apply to Mi’s. Katsuko Nakahara Huntley, a native and citizen of Japan, of the Japanese race, the wife of Lawrence Huntley, a United States citizen who is an honorably discharged veteran of the United States armed forces during World War II, and that if otherwise admissible under the immigration laws she shall be granted the status of a nonquota immigrant.


*Approved May 3, 1950.*

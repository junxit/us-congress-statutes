# Chapter 1127

> AN ACT For the relief of Gregory Pirro and Nellie Pirro.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration laws, Gregory Pirro and Nellie Pirro, of Wildrose, North Dakota, who were admitted into the United States on temporary visas, shall be held and considered to have been law-fully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of their hist entry into the United States, upon payment of the required head taxes and visa fees.

## § 2.

The Secretary of State is authorized and directed to instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct two numbers from the non-preference category of the first available immigration quota for nationals of Italy.


*Approved September 30, 1950.*

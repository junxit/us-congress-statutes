# Chapter 1020

> AN ACT For the relief of Susan E. Scott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, shall not apply to Susan E. Scott, alien minor child in the care of Mr. and Mrs. Denny F. Scott, United States citizens, and the said Susan E. Scott shall be held and considered to be the alien natural-born child of the said Mr. and Mrs. Denny F. Scott.


*Approved September 23, 1950.*

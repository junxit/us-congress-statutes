# Chapter 437

> AN ACT For the relief of Marina George Papadopoulos.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, the alien, Marina George Papadopoulos, Las Vegas, Nevada, shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date she last entered the United States, upon payment of the head tax and visa fee. Upon the enactment of this Act the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the appropriate quota for the first year that such quota is available.


*Approved June 30, 1950.*

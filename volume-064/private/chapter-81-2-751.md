# Chapter 751

> AN ACT For the relief of Raief Neahem, Iffef Neahem, and Ihsen Neahem.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws the provisions of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, pertaining to unmarried children under twenty-one years of age of a citizen of the United States, shall be held to be applicable to the aliens Raief Neahem, Iffef Neahem, and Ihsen Neahem, minor grandchildren of Calil Shibley, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 17, 1950.*

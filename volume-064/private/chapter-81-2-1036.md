# Chapter 1036

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Teruko Tominaga Ikeuchi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigi’ation Act of 1924, as amended, which exclude from admission to the United States persons who are ineligible to citizenship shall not apply to Mrs. Teruko Tominaga Ikeuchi, the wife of Harold H. Ikeuchi, a citizen of the United States and an honorably discharged veteran of World War II.


*Approved September 25, 1950.*

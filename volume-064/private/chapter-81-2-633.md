# Chapter 633

> AN ACT For the relief of Yee Balche Yee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, the provisions of section 4 (a) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, pertaining to unmarried children under twenty-one years of age of a citizen of the United States, shall be held to be applicable to the alien Yee Balche Yee, born November 23, 1949, minor child of Yee Kai Teung, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 7, 1950.*

# Chapter 772

> AN ACT Authorizing the Secretary of the Interior to issue a patent in fee to Edgar S. Bigman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives c United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is authorized and directed to issue to Edgar S. Bi a patent in fee to the following-described lands, known as allotment numbered 3064 on the Crow Indian Reservation, Montana: The half of the northwest quarter and the southwest quarter of section 17 the southeast quarter of section 18, and section 20, township 3 ; range 34 east, containing one thousand and forty acres.

## § 2.

- **(a)** The lands herein described shall not be sold after date of enactment of this Act to any purchaser, other than the Tribe or a member thereof, unless (1) at least sixty days prior to sale the Superintendent of the Crow Agency shall have been s with notice of the terms thereof and a copy of such notice, tog with a description of the lands, shall have been posted by the Superintendent in a conspicuous public place at such agency and remained posted for a period of sixty days, and (2) prior t expiration of such sixty days no bona fide oiler in writing to pm such land upon the terms specified in such notice, or upon terms favorable to the owner, shall have been made by the Crow Tribe any member thereof and a copy thereof served upon the Superintendent of the Crow Agency.
- **(b)** A certificate of the Superintendent of the Crow Agency st that notice of the proposed sale was served upon him and was by him for a period of sixty days in accordance with the prov of clause (1) of subsection (a) and that no offer was received in accordance with clause (2) of such subsection, when filed and recorded in the office of the county clerk and recorder of the county in which such lands are situated shall be conclusive evidence of compliance with this section. The superintendent shall furnish the certificate to the purchaser for filing and recording.

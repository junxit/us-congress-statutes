# Chapter 396

> AN ACT For the relief of Koto Kogami Kitsu and Jeannette Akemi Kitsu.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, which exclude from admission to the United States persons who are ineligible to citizenship, shall not apply to Koto Kogand Kitsu and Jeannette Akemi Kitsu, natives of Japan, the wife and daughter of Stanley Y. Kitsu, a citizen of the United States and presently serving in the armed forces of the United States.


*Approved June 28, 1950.*

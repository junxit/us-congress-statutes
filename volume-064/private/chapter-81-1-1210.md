# Chapter 1210

> AN ACT For the relief of Reverend Andrew Chai Kyung Whang.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, which exclude from admission to the United States per-sons who are ineligible to citizenship, Reverend Andrew Chai Kyung Whang, a native of Korea, shall be considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of his last lawful entry into the United States, upon the payment of the required visa fee and head taxes. Upon the enactment of this Act the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the appropriate quota for the first year that such quota is available.


*Approved January 4, 1951.*

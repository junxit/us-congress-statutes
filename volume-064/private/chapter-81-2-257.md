# Chapter 257

> AN ACT For the relief of Ferd H. Gibler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General is authorized and directed to credit the accounts of Feed H. Gibler, postmaster at Freeport, Illinois, in the sum of $9,812.28, representing the balance due the United States on account of the embezzlement or post-office funds by Harry J. Seachrist, former assistant postmaster at the Freeport, Illinois, post office.


*Approved June 15, 1950.*

# Chapter 744

> AN ACT Authorizing the Secretary of the Interior to issue a patent in fee to James Wilbur Watt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is authorized and directed to issue to James Wilbur Watt n patent in fee to the following-described lands, known as allotment 3607 on the Crow Indian Reservation, Montana: The north-east quarter of section 34 ; lots 2, 3, and 6, the west half of the north-east quarter, the northwest quarter, and the northwest quarter of the southeast quarter of section 35, township 8 south, range 38 east, Montana principal meridian, containing five hundred and forty-two acres more or less.


*Approved August 17, 1950.*

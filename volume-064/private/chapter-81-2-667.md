# Chapter 667

> AN ACT For the relief of Alfio Batelli.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the. United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws the Attorney General of the United States shall record the lawful admission for permanent residence of Alfio Batelli as of September 5, 1947, the date of his last entry into the United States, upon payment of the required visa fee and head tax. Upon the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of State shall instinct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the appropriate quota for the first year that such quota is available.


*Approved August 9, 1950.*

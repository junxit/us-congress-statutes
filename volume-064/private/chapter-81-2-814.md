# Chapter 814

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Frederick Daniel McDade.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, Doctor Frederick Daniel McDade, a native of Canada, shall be deemed to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of September 4, 1944, the date on which he was lawfully admitted to the United States temporarily.

## § 2.

Upon the enactment of this Act, Doctor Frederick Daniel McDade may file the petition for naturalization prescribed by law without making the preliminary declaration of intention and, upon satisfactory proof to the court that he is otherwise qualified to become a citizen of the United States, the said Doctor Frederick Daniel McDade may be admitted as a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 28, 1950.*

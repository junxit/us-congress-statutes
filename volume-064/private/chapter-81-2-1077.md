# Chapter 1077

> AN ACT For the relief of Erika Kuebart and her minor son.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, the aliens Erika Kuebart. (the German fiancée of Charles A. Garrard, Junior, a United States citizen and an honorably discharged veteran of World War II), and her minor son, both of 22 Boppard Rhein, Bayerhofgasse 15, Germany, French Zone, shall be eligible for admission to the United States as nonimmigrant temporary visitors for a period of three months if the appropriate administrative authorities find that the said Erika Knebart is coming to the United States with a bona fide intention of being married to the said Charles A. Garrard, Junior, and that she is otherwise admissible under the immigration laws. If the marriage between the above-named parties does not occur within three months after the entry of the said Erika Knebart and her minor son, she and her minor son shall be required to depart from the United States and upon failure to do so shall be deported in accordance with sections 19 and 20 of the Immigration Act of February 5, 1917, as amended (U. S. C., 1946 edition, title 8, secs. 155 and 156). If the above-named parties are married within three months after the entry of the said Erika Knebart and her minor son, the Attorney General is authorized and directed to record the lawful admission for permanent residence of the said Erika Kuebart and her minor son as of the date of their entry into the United States upon payment of the required fees and head taxes.


*Approved September 27, 1950.*

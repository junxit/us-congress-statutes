# Chapter 337

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Rei Yamada Munns and Edward Lee Munns.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration law’s the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, which exclude from admission to the United States aliens who are ineligible to citizenship, shall not hereafter apply to Mrs. Rei Yamada Munns and Edward Lee Munns, the wife and son, respectively, of Ralph W. Munns, a citizen of the United States and an honorably discharged veteran of World War II.


*Approved June 20, 1950.*

# Chapter 678

> AN ACT For the relief of Yuk Onn Won.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws the provisions of section 4 (a of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, pertaining to unmarried children under twenty-one years of age of a citizen of the United States, shall be held to be applicable to the alien Yuk Onn Won; and the said Yuk Onn Won shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Kui Fat Won and Margaret Choy Keau Ching Won, United States citizens and residents of Honolulu, Territory of Hawaii.


*Approved August 10, 1950.*

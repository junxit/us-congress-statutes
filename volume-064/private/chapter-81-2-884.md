# Chapter 884

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Tokie Sato Keating, Terry Yoichi Keating, and Betty Jean Keating.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration laws, the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, excluding from the United States aliens ineligible to citizenship, shall not hereafter apply to Mrs. Tokie Sato Keating, Terry Yoichi Keating, and Betty Jean Keating, the Japanese wife and children, respectively, of Harlow James Keating, a citizen of the United States and an honorably dis-charged veteran of World War II.


*Approved September 5, 1950.*

# Chapter 671

> AN ACT For the relief of Mr. and Mrs. Albert Chandler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That Mr. and Mrs. Albert Chandler, whose son, James Dalton Chandler, died in France on September 19, 1945, while serving in the armed forces, and whose application for benefits under veterans laws was misplaced by the local veterans’ agency to which they had delivered such application on November 13, 1945, shall be entitled to the same benefits under laws administered by the Veterans’ Administration as those to which they would have been entitled if such error had not been made and such application had been received by the Veterans’ Administration within one year after the date of their son’s death.


*Approved August 9, 1950.*

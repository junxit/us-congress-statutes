# Chapter 585

> AN ACT For the relief of Juliana Sosa de Solis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of the eleventh category of section 3 of the Immigration Act of 1917, as amended (8 U. S. C. 136 (e)), Juliana Sosa de Solis, the wife of a citizen of the United States, may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved August 4, 1950.*

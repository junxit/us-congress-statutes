# Chapter 159

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Walter K. Miyamoto (formerly Miyoko Takahashi).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, which exclude from admission to the United States persons who are ineligible to citizenship, shall not hereafter apply to Mrs. Walter K. Miyamoto (formerly Miyoko Takahashi), a native and citizen of Japan, the widow of Technician (Fifth Grade) Walter K. Miyamoto, who died on April 8, 1948, while serving in the United States Army, and, if otherwise admissible under the immigration laws, she shall be granted the status of a nonquota immigrant.


*Approved May 4, 1950.*

# Chapter 608

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor In Sung Kwak.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, Doctor In Sung Kwak, of Norton, Kansas, shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of his last entry into the United States, upon payment of the required visa fee and head tax. Upon enactment of this Act the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the appropriate quota for the first year that such quota is available.


*Approved August 7, 1950.*

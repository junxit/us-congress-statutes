# Chapter 1103

> AN ACT For the relief of Ronald Mow and Angeline Cecilia Mow.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, the provisions of section 4 (a) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, pertaining to unmarried children under twenty-one years of age of a citizen of the United States, shall be held to be applicable to the aliens, Ronald Mow and Angeline Cecilia Mow, minor children of Peter Mow, United States citizen: Provided, That the administrative authorities find that the said Ronald Mow and Angeline Cecelia Mow are the natural-born minor children of the said Peter Mow.


*Approved September 28, 1950.*

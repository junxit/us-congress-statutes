# Chapter 386

> AN ACT For the relief of William Richard Geoffrey Malpas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of the eighth category of section 3 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended (8 U. S. C. 136 (c) ), William Richard Geoffrey Malpas, a native and citizen of Great Britain, may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if he is found otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved June 28, 1950.*

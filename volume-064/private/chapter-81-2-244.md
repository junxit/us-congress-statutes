# Chapter 244

> AN ACT For the relief of Italo Vespa de Chellis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Italo Vespa de Chellis shall be held and considered to be the minor child of his mother, Mrs. Ninetta Vespa de Chellis, lawfully admitted legal resident of the United States.


*Approved June 14, 1950.*

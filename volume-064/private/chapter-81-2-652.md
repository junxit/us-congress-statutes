# Chapter 652

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Miehiko Nogami Cotter and Katsumi Cotter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, the provisions of sections 13 (c) and 28 (n) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, shall not hereafter apply to Michiko Nogami Cotter and Katsumi Cotter, of Yamagata City, Honshu, Japan, the wife and minor daughter, respectively, of Conrad Cotter, a United States citizen and World War II veteran.


*Approved August 8, 1950.*

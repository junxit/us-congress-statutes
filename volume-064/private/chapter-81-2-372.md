# Chapter 372

> AN ACT For the relief of Arthur Chen Shu Jee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of section 2 of the Act of December 17, 1943 (8 U. S. C., 1946 edition, sec. 212a), which requires that Chinese alien children of United States citizens shall be charged to the immigration quota for the Chinese, shall be held and considered not to be applicable to Arthur Chen Shu Jee, the son of Doctor Kenneth Chen Huan Jee.


*Approved June 27, 1950.*

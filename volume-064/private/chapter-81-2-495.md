# Chapter 495

> AN ACT For the relief of Maria Cicerelli.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Maria Cicerelli shall be held and considered to be the minor child of her father, Sam Cicerelli, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved July 26, 1950.*

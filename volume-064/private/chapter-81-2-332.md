# Chapter 332

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Jack B. Meyer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended (U. S. C., title 8, sec. 213 (c)), which excludes from admission to the United States persons who are ineligible to citizenship, shall not hereafter apply to Mrs. Jack B. Meyer, Japanese wife of Sergeant Jack B. Meyer, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved June 20, 1950.*

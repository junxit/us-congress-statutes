# Chapter 416

> AN ACT For the relief of Thomas Pfeiffer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Thomas Pfeiffer, who is presently residing in Montreal, Canada, and who is to be adopted by Mr. and Mrs. Ely Beaumont, of Burbank, California, shall be deemed to be the alien child of said Mr. and Mrs. Ely Beaumont, citizens of the United States.


*Approved June 29, 1950.*

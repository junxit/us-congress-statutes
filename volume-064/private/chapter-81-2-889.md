# Chapter 889

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Yoshiko Ogiso Peterson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, which exclude from admission to the United States aliens who are ineligible to citizenship, shall not hereafter apply to Mrs. Yoshiko Ogiso Peterson, the wife of William Francis Peterson, formerly of the United States Air Force, a citizen of the United States and honorably discharged veteran of World War II.


*Approved September 5, 1950.*

# Chapter 376

> AN ACT For the relief of Hisako Sakata Ikezawa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administrât: ion of the immigration laws, the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, which excludes from admission to the United States aliens who are ineligible to citizenship, shall not apply to Mrs. Hisako Sakata Ikezawa, the wife of a citizen of the United States and an honorably discharged veteran of World War II.


*Approved June 27, 1950.*

# Chapter 422

> AN ACT For the relief of Mindel Malek.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 331 of the Nationality Act of 1940 (8 U. S. C. 731), as amended, Mindel Malek may, if otherwise eligible under all other applicable provisions of the nationality laws, file the petition for naturalization prescribed by law.


*Approved June 30, 1950.*

# Chapter 1170

> AN ACT For the relief of Pamela Bentley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Pamela Bentley shall be held and considered to be the natural born alien minor child of Mr. and Mrs. Robe it Griffin, citizens of the United States.


*Approved December 27, 1950.*

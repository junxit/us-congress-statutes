# Chapter 875

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Michiko Kohga Brooks.

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, excluding persons ineligible to citizenship from admission to the Ünited States, shall not apply to Michiko Kohga Brooks, the Japanese wife of Robert L. Brooks, a citizen of the United States and a member of the Armed Forces of the United States.


*Approved September 5, 1950.*

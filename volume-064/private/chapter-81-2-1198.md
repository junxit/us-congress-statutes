# Chapter 1198

> JOINT RESOLUTION To authorize the President to issue posthumously to the late Walton Harris Walker, lieutenant general, Army of the United States, a commission as General, Army of the United States, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding any other provisions of law, the President is authorized to issue posthumously to the late Walton Harris Walker, late a lieutenant general, Army of the United States, a commission as General, Army of the United States, as of December 20, 1950.

## § 2.

The Secretary of the Army is authorized and directed to amend the records of the Department of the Army so as to carry the late Walton Harris Walker as a General, Army of the United States, to rank from December 20, 1950.


*Approved January 2, 1951.*

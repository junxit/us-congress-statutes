# Chapter 639

> AN ACT For the relief of Yasuko Higuchi Thomson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, which exclude from admission to the United States aliens who are ineligible to citizenship, shall not hereafter apply to Mrs. Yasuko Higuchi Thomson, the wife of Norman Earl Thomson, a citizen of the United States and an honorably discharged veteran of World War II.


*Approved August 7, 1950.*

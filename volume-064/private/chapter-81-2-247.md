# Chapter 247

> AN ACT To amend the Act entitled "An Act to incorporate the trustees of the Presbyterian congregation of Georgetown", and approved March 28, 1806.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proviso in section 2 of the Act entitled “An Act to incorporate the trustees of the Presbyterian congregation of Georgetown”, approved March 28, 1806 (2 Stat. 356), is amended by striking out “three thousand” and inserting in lieu thereof “twenty-five thousand”.

## § 2.

Section 5 of such Act is amended by striking out “held on the first Tuesday of April, in every year hereafter” and inserting in lieu thereof “held at such time as may be prescribed by the bylaws”.


*Approved June 14, 1950.*

# Chapter 258

> AN ACT For the relief of Jacques Yedid, Henriette Yedid, and Ethel Danielle Yedid.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, Jacques Yedid, Henriette Yedid, and Ethel Danielle Yedid shall be considered as having been lawfully admitted for permanent residence as of the date of their last entries into the United States, on payment of the required visa fees and head taxes.

## § 2.

Upon the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of State is authorized and directed to instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct appropriate numbers from the nonpreference category of the first available immigration quota for nationals of Lebanon, Poland, and Egypt.


*Approved June 15, 1950.*

# Chapter 864

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Shikaju Nakashima.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, which excludes from admission into the United States aliens who are ineligible to citizenship, shall not hereafter apply to Mrs. Shikaju Nakashima, Japanese mother of Sumiko Nakashima, a citizen of the United States; and that the said Mrs. Shikaju Nakashima shall be held and considered to be an immigrant previously admitted to the United States returning from a temporary visit abroad under section 4 (b) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended.


*Approved September 5, 1950.*

# Chapter 1096

> AN ACT For the relief of Maria Balsam.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Maria Balsam shall be considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Ben Rose, citizens of the United States.


*Approved September 28, 1950.*

# Chapter 540

> AN ACT For the relief of Yone T. Park.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended (U. S. C., title 8, section 213 (c) ), which excludes from admission to the United States persons who are ineligible to citizenship, shall not hereafter apply to Yone T. Park, of Honshu, Japan, wife of George M. Park, an American citizen, and if otherwise admissible under the immigration laws she shall be deemed to be a nonquota immigrant.


*Approved August 3, 1950.*

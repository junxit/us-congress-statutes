# Chapter 887

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Nobuko Yonashiro Martin and Gerald Philip Martin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, excluding from the United States aliens ineligible to citizenship, shall not hereafter apply to Mrs. Nobuko Yonashiro Martin and Gerald Philip Martin, wife and minor son, respectively, of James L. Martin, a United States citizen and World War II veteran.


*Approved September 5, 1950.*

# Chapter 691

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Ferdinando Schiappa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Ferdinando Schiappa, upon payment of the required head tax and visa fee, be considered, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence on March 14, 1948. Upon the enactment of this Act the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the Italian quota of the first year that the same Italian quota is available.


*Approved August 11, 1950.*

# Chapter 116

> AN ACT For the relief of Janis Shimada.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration laws the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, which exclude from admission to the United States aliens who are ineligible to citizenship, shall not hereafter apply to Mrs. Janis Shimada, the wife of Gordon Leslie Page, a citizen of the United States and an honorably discharged veteran of World War II.


*Approved April 27, 1950.*

# Chapter 914

> AN ACT For the relief of Miriam Rosenblum.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Miriam Rosenblum shall be held and considered to be the natural born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. David Rosenblum, who are citizens of the United States.


*Approved September 7, 1950.*

# Chapter 879

> AN ACT For the relief of Joseph F. Gallagher.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of Américain Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is authorized and directed to credit the account of Joseph F. Gallagher, former postmaster at Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, with the sum of $5,070.83, the amount disallowed in the account of the said Joseph F. Gallagher by the Comptroller General due to the fact that certain trucks were hired to carry mail for the Post Office at Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, during the last quarter of 1947 without advertising or competitive bids.


*Approved September 5, 1950.*

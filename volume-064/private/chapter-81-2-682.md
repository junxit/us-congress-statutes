# Chapter 682

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Masa lyoki.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration laws, the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended (U. S. C., title 8, sec. 213 (c)), which excludes from admission to the United States persons who are ineligible to citizenship, shall not hereafter apply to Mrs. Masa lyoki, a former resident of the United States and mother of an American citizen. If otherwise admissible under the immigration laws, Mrs. Masa lyoki shall be held and considered to be a returning resident under the provisions of section 4 (b) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended.


*Approved August 10, 1950.*

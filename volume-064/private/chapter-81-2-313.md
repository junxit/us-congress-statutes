# Chapter 313

> AN ACT For the relief of Toshiko Ono.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of the immigration laws relating to the exclusion of aliens inadmissible because of race shall not hereafter apply to Toshiko Ono, the Japanese fiancée of John E. Williams, a citizen of the United States and an honorably discharged veteran of World War II: Provided, That in the event the marriage between the above-named parties does not occur within three months after the enactment of this Act, the said Toshiko Ono shall be required to depart from the United States and upon failure to do so shall be deported in accordance with the provisions of sections 19 and 20 of the Immigration Act of February 5, 1917 (U. S. C., title 8, secs. 155 and 156). In the event the marriage between the above-named parties shall occur within three months after the enactment of this Act, the Attorney General is authorized and directed to record the lawful admission for permanent residence of Toshiko Ono as of the date of her entry into the United States, upon the payment by her of the required fees and head tax, if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the immigration laws.


*Approved June 17, 1950.*

# Chapter 1133

> AN ACT For the relief of Colvin Bernard Meik.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, Colvin Bernard Meik, a British army officer who is presently residing in Great Britain, and who was born in India of British parents, shall be deemed to have been born in Great Britain.


*Approved September 30, 1950.*

# Chapter 90

> AN ACT For the relief of Primitive Urcelay-Ruiz.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Attorney General of the United States be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to cancel deportation proceedings in the case of Primitive Urcelay-Ruiz, of central Nevada, legally admitted as a contract laborer, but who has remained in the United States longer than permitted by law and regulations and that this alien shall be considered as having been admitted for permanent entry as of the date of his actual entry on the payment of the visa fee of $10 and a head tax of $8.

Upon the enactment of this Act the Secretary of State shall instinct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the Spanish quota for the first year that the said Spanish quota is available.


*Approved April 19, 1950.*

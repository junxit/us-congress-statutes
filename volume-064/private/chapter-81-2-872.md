# Chapter 872

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Yukiko Yoshii French and her son.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, which exclude from admission to the United States persons who are ineligible to citizenship, shall not apply to Mrs. Yukiko Yoshii French and her son, the wife and child, respectively, of Raymond David French, a citizen of the United States presently serving in the United States armed forces.


*Approved September 5, 1950.*

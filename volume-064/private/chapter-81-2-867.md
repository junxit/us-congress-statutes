# Chapter 867

> AN ACT For the relief of Cornelius VerSluis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, the Attorney General is authorized and directed to record Cornelius VerSluis as having entered the United States on August 15, 1949, for permanent residence, upon the payment of the required visa fee and head tax. Upon the enactment of this Act the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the quota for the Netherlands of the first year that such quota is available.


*Approved September 5, 1950.*

# Chapter 299

> AN ACT For the relief of Anna Helman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration laws, the Attorney General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to record the lawful admission for permanent residence of Anna Helman, of New York City, as of the date of her arrival at Baltimore, Maryland, July 30, 1942, if she be found admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws other than the second category of section 3 of the Act of February 5, 1917 (39 Stat. 875; U. S. C., title 8, section 136).


*Approved June 17, 1950.*

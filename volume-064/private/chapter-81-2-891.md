# Chapter 891

> AN ACT For the relief of Benjamin Paglinaman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of Américain Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, the provisions of section 4 (a) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, pertaining to unmarried children under twenty-one years of age of a citizen of the United States, shall be held to be applicable to the alien Benjamin Paglinaman, minor stepchild of Paul W. Arnold, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved September 5, 1950.*

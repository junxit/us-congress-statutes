# Chapter 309

> AN ACT For the relief of Jodeene Lehrman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, solely for the purpose of section 4(a) and section 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, Jodeene Lehrman shall be considered the alien natural-born child of her adoptive parents, Sergeant and Mrs. Richard L. Lehrman, United States citizens.


*Approved June 17, 1950.*

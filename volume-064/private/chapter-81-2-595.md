# Chapter 595

> AN ACT For the relief of Augusto Segre.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization law’s, Augusto Segre, of Chicago, Illinois, shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted into the United States for permanent residence as of the date of his last entry into the United States, upon payment of the required head tax and visa fee.

## § 2.

The Secretary of State is authorized and directed to instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the nonpreference category of the first available immigration quota for nationals of Italy.


*Approved August 5, 1950.*

# Chapter 1016

> AN ACT For the relief of Rosette Selina Romano, a minor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws. Rosette Selina Romano, native and citizen of Italy, shall be considered to be the alien natural-born child of Mr. and Mrs. Antonino Romano, United States citizens.


*Approved September 23, 1950.*

# Chapter 243

> AN ACT For the relief of Sisters Maria Rita Rossi, Maria Domenica Paone, Rachele Orlando, Assunta Roselli, Rosa Innocenti, and Maria Mancinelli.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws Sisters Maria Rita Rossi, Maria Domeniea Paone, Rachele Orlando, Assunta Roselli, Rosa Innocenti, and Maria Maneinelli, who were admitted to the United States on temporary visas, shall be deemed to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the dates of their last entries, on payment of the required visa fees and head taxes.

## § 2.

Upon the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of State is authorized and directed to deduct six numbers from the nonpreference category of the first available quota for nationals of Italy.


*Approved June 14, 1950.*

# Chapter 1157

> AN ACT For the relief of Gerda Moller Uldall and her son, Mikkel Moller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws Gerda Moller Uldall and her son, Mikkel Moller, shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of their last entry into the United States upon payment of the required head tax and visa fee.

## § 2.

The Secretary of State is authorized and directed to instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct two numbers from the non-preference category of the first available immigration quota for nationals of Denmark.


*Approved December 27, 1950.*

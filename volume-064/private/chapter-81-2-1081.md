# Chapter 1081

> AN ACT For the relief of Regolo Gagliacco and his wife, Gina.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, section 4 (b) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended (8 U. S. C. 204), shall be applicable to Regolo Gagliacco and his wife, Gina Gagliacco, Italian nationals residing in Canada and lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence in 1946.


*Approved September 27, 1950.*

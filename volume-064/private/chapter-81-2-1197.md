# Chapter 1197

> AN ACT For the relief of Linda Leo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Linda Leo, the minor child of Yee Leo, a citizen of the United States, shall be deemed to be eligible for admission into the United States under the provisions of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, if otherwise admissible under the immigration laws.


*Approved January 2, 1951.*

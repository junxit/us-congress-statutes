# Chapter 20

> AN ACT For the relief of Penelope Corolyn Cox.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Penelope Corolyn Cox, who is presently residing in Canada and who was born in India of British parents, shall be deemed to have been born in Great Britain.


*Approved February 14, 1950.*

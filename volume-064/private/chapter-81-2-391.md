# Chapter 391

> AN ACT For the relief of Krikor G. Guiragossian.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, the Attorney General is authorized and directed to record Krikor G. Guiragossian as having entered the United States on August 4, 1946, for permanent residence upon the payment of the head tax and visa fee.

## § 2.

Upon the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the quota for Turkey of the first year that such quota is available.


*Approved June 28, 1950.*

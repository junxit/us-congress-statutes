# Chapter 846

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Martha Reid.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Mrs. Martha Reid, a citizen of Canada, and presently residing in Honolulu, Territory of Hawaii, shall be considered to have been lawfully admitted, on December 27, 1948, to the United States for permanent residence, upon the payment of the required visa fee and head tax.


*Approved September 2, 1950.*

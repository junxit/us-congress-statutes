# Chapter 397

> AN ACT For the relief of Nobuko Maeda.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, relating to the exclusion of aliens inadmissible because of race shall not here-after apply to Nobuko Maeda, the Japanese fiancée of First Lieutenant George N. Asai, a citizen of the United States, and that the said Nobuko Maeda may be eligible for a nonquota immigration visa if she is found otherwise admissible under the immigration laws: Provided, That the administrative authorities find that marriage between the above-named parties occurred within three months immediately succeeding the enactment date of this Act.


*Approved June 28, 1950.*

# Chapter 130

> AN ACT For the relief of Johny Nielsen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Johny Nielsen, a native and citizen of Denmark, shall be considered to be the natural-born son of Mr. and Mrs. Peter Leth-Nissen, United States citizens.


*Approved April 28, 1950.*

# Chapter 831

> AN ACT For the relief of Stamatie Amersonis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Stamatie Amersonis, legally adopted minor son of Nicholas Amersonis and Mrs. Frances Amersonis, United States citizens residing in Chicago, Illinois, shall be held and considered to be the alien natural-born son of the said Nicholas Amersonis and the said Mrs. Frances Amersonis.


*Approved August 31, 1950.*

# Chapter 322

> AN ACT For the relief of Edward A. Seeley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Captain Edward A. Seeley, United States Army, retired, be, and he is hereby, relieved of all liability to refund to the United States any of the compensation paid to him as a civilian employee of the Army during the period from July 1, 1946, to August 7, 1948, both dates inclusive, notwithstanding any other law to the contrary.


*Approved June 20, 1950.*

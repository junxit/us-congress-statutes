# Chapter 611

> AN ACT For the relief of Miju Iseri Tsuda.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration laws, notwithstanding the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, the provisions of section 4 (b) of the said Act shall be applicable to Miju Iseri Tsuda, a returning immigrant who has been previously lawfully admitted into the United States and who is the widowed mother of several American citizens residing in the United States.


*Approved August 7, 1950.*

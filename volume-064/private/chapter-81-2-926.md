# Chapter 926

> AN ACT For the relief of Adelaide Giovanna Summa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, Adelaide Giovanna Summa, of Trenton, New Jersey, shall be held and con- sidered to have been lawfully admitted into the United States for permanent residence as of December 13, 1948, the date of her last entry into the United States, upon payment of the required head tax and visa fee.

## § 2.

The Secretary of State is authorized and directed to instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the non-preference category of the first available immigration quota for nationals of Italy.


*Approved September 8, 1950.*

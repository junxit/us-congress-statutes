# Chapter 131

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Raymond Schaffer, Junior.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, which excludes from the United States persons who are ineligible to citizenship, shall not hereafter apply to Mi’s. Raymond Schaffer, Junior, a native of Japan, who is the wife of Raymond Schaffer, Junior, of York, Pennsylvania, an honorably discharged veteran of World War II and a citizen of the United States. If otherwise admissible under the immigration laws the said Mrs. Raymond Schaffer, Junior, shall be granted the status of a nonquota immigrant.


*Approved April 28, 1950.*

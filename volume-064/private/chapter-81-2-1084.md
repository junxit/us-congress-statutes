# Chapter 1084

> AN ACT For the relief of Fred Hess.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, the alien, Fred Hess, minor adopted son of Captain John E. Murphy and Mrs. Maria Murphy, shall be held and considered to be the alien natural-born son of the said Captain John E. Murphy and the said Mrs. Maria Murphy.


*Approved September 27, 1950.*

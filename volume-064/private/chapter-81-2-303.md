# Chapter 303

> AN ACT For the relief of Deborah Elizabeth Ebel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House, of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding any provision of law-excluding from admission to the United States persons of races ineligible to citizenship, the alien Deborah Elizabeth Ebel, a minor half-Japanese child under the care of Mr. and Mrs. Alexander Ebel, both citizens of the United States residing temporarily in Japan, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born child of the said Mr. and Mrs. Alexander Ebel


*Approved June 17, 1950.*

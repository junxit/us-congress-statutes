# Chapter 1021

> AN ACT For the relief of Leila M. Dodd.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and nationality laws, section 303 of the Nationality Act of 1940 shall not be held applicable to Leila M. Dodd, who was born in French Indochina in 1898, adopted in infancy by an American missionary and his wife, and brought to the United States.


*Approved September 23, 1950.*

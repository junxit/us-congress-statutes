# Chapter 1113

> AN ACT For the relief of Arturo Benetti.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, Arturo Benetti, who was admitted into the United States on a temporary visa, shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of his last entry into the United States, upon payment of the required head tax and visa fee.

## § 2.

The Secretary of State is authorized and directed to instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the non-preference category of the proper immigration quota.


*Approved September 29, 1950.*

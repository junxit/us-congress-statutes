# Chapter 468

> AN ACT For the relief of Low Way Hong.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Low Way Hong, the minor child o? Low Wen Been, a citizen of the United States, shall be considered as eligible for admission to the United States under the provisions of sections 4(a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, if otherwise admissible under the immigration laws.


*Approved July 18, 1950.*

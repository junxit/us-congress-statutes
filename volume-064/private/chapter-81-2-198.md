# Chapter 198

> AN ACT To authorize the Secretary of the Interior to transfer a building in Juneau, Alaska, to the Alaska Native Brotherhood and/or Sisterhood, Juneau Alaska) Camp.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to trans-fer in consideration of 50 per centum of the appraised fair market value thereof to the Alaska Native Brotherhood and/or Sisterhood, Juneau (Alaska) Camp, all the right, title, and interest of the United States in the following-described building in Juneau, Alaska, now owned by the Alaska Native Service:

A one-story two-room frame building, twenty-eight by forty-five feet and twenty-four feet high, presently located on a tract of tide-lands shown as lot 2, block A, on the unofficial survey plat of the Juneau Indian Village.


*Approved May 24, 1950.*

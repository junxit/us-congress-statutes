# Chapter 277

> AN ACT For the relief of Harold L. Lindquist.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, the Attorney General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to record the lawful admission for permanent residence of Harold L. Lindquist, as of January 7, 1941, at New York City from the steamship Ester-Thorden, the date and place he entered the United States, upon payment of the visa fee and head tax. Upon enactment of this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the Finnish quota of the first year, that the Finnish quota is hereafter available.


*Approved June 16, 1950.*

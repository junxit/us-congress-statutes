# Chapter 1035

> AN ACT For the relief of Sumiko Fujita.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of the immigration laws relating to the exclusion of aliens inadmissible because of race shall not hereafter apply to Sumiko Fujita, a native of Japan, the fiancée of Seiyu Steven Shiroma, a citizen of the United States and an honorably discharged veteran of World War II, and that Sumiko Fujita may be eligible for a visa as a non-immigrant temporary visitor for a period of three months: Provided, that the administrative authorities find that the said Sumiko Fujita is coming to the United States with a bona fide intention of being married to said Seiyu Steven Shiroma, and that she is found other-wise admissible under the immigration laws. In the event the marriage between the above-named parties does not occur within three months after the entry of said Sumiko Fujita, she shall be required to depart from the United States and upon failure to do so shall be deported in accordance with the provisions of sections 19 and 20 of the Immigration Act of February 5, 1917 (U. S. C., title 8, secs. 155 and 156). In the event the marriage between the above-named parties shall occur within three months after the entry of said Sumiko Fujita, the Attorney General is authorized and directed to record the lawful admission for permanent residence of said Sumiko Fujita as of the date of her entry into the United States, upon the payment by her of the required fees and head taxes.


*Approved September 25, 1950.*

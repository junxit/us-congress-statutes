# Chapter 858

> AN ACT For the relief of Sergio and Mara Lamberti.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, Sergio and Mara Lamberti, of Latrobe, Pennsylvania, who were admitted into the United States on temporary visas, shall be deemed to have been lawfully admitted into the United States for permanent residence as of July 22, 1948, upon the payment of the required visa fees and head taxes.

## § 2.

Upon enactment of this Act, the Secretary of State is authorized and directed to instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct two numbers from the nonpreference category of the first available quota for Italy.


*Approved September 5, 1950.*

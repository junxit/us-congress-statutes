# Chapter 873

> AN ACT For the relief of Enrica Gianoli.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of immigration and naturalization laws, the alien, Enrica Gianoli, shall be considered to be the natural-born daughter of Mr. and Mrs. James Gianoli, of Rockford, Illinois, citizens of the United States.


*Approved September 5, 1950.*

# Chapter 574

> AN ACT For the relief of John Yee Horn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, section 4 (a) and section 9 of the Immigration Act of May 26, 1924, shall be held applicable to John Yee Horn, the minor unmarried child of Yee Kang Horn, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 4, 1950.*

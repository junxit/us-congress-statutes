# Chapter 82

> AN ACT For the relief of John Joseph McKay.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That in the administration of the immigration laws the Attorney General is authorized and directed to record John Joseph McKay, of Deer Lodge, Montana, as having entered the United States on April 5, 1947, for permanent residence, upon payment by him of the required visa fee and head tax.

## § 2.

Upon the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of State is authorized and directed to instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the quota for Portugal of the first year that such quota number is available.


*Approved April 10, 1950.*

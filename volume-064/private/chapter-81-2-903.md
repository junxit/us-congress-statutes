# Chapter 903

> AN ACT For the relief of Ah-Kim Wong.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, Ah-Kim Wong, also known as Lily Taylor, of New York, New York, shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of April 28, 1947, the date she last entered the United States as a visitor, upon the payment by her of the required visa fee and head tax. Upon the enactment of this Act the Secretary of State is authorized and directed to instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the appropriate immigration quota for the first year that such quota is available.


*Approved September 6, 1950.*

# Chapter 904

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Jaye Kurusu Maddox.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Mrs, Jaye Kurusu Maddox, who lost her citizenship under the operation of section 401 (e) of the Nationality Act of 1940, as amended, may be naturalized by taking prior to one year from the enactment of this Act, before any diplomatic or consular officer of the United States abroad, the oaths prescribed by section 335 of the Nationality Act of 1940, as amended. .

## § 2.

From and after naturalization under this Act, Mrs. Maddox shall have the same citizenship status as that which existed immediately prior to its loss.


*Approved September 6, 1950.*

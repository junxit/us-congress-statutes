# Chapter 1207

> AN ACT For the relief of Archibald Walter Campbell Seymour.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Archibald Walter Campbell Seymour, who was born in India of British parents, shall be held and considered to have been born in Great Britain.


*Approved January 3, 1951.*

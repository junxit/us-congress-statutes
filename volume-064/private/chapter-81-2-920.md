# Chapter 920

> AN ACT For the relief of Tadeusz Herka.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Tadeusz Herka shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted into the United States for permanent residence as of the date of his last entry into the United States, upon the payment of the required visa fee and head tax. Upon the enactment of this Act, the Secretary’ of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the appropriate quota for the first year that such quota is available.


*Approved September 7, 1950.*

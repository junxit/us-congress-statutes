# Chapter 1162

> AN ACT For the relief of Chen Hua Huang.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purpose of the immigration and naturalization laws, Chen Hua Huang shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien minor child of Mr. and Mrs. Chun Ku Huang, citizens of the United States, and shall be deemed to be a nonquota immigrant within the purview of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924.


*Approved December 27, 1950.*

# Chapter 504

> AN ACT For the relief of Thomas Nicholas Epiphaniades and Wanda Julia Epiphaniades.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and, House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Attorney General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to record Thomas Nicholas Epiphaniades as having entered the United States for permanent residence on May 21, 1948, and Wanda Julia Epiphaniades as having entered the United States for permanent residence on May 20, 1948, upon the payment of the required visa fees and head taxes. Upon the enactment of the Act the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the quota for Greece of the first year that the said quota is available, and one number from the quota for the Union of Soviet Socialist Republics of the first year that the said quota is available.


*Approved July 28, 1950.*

# Chapter 1014

> AN ACT For the relief of Daniel Kokal.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, the alien Daniel Kokal, 2 Alfred Street, Pittsburgh 16, Pennsylvania, shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted at New York, New York, on April 27, 1949, to the United States for permanent residence, upon the payment of the required visa fee and head tax. Upon the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the appropriate immigration quota.


*Approved September 23, 1950.*

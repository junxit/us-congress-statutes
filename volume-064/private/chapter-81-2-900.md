# Chapter 900

> AN ACT For the relief of Maria Hoffman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, the alien Maria Hoffman, of Saint Paul, Minnesota, who entered the United States in July 1948 as a temporary visitor, shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of her entry therein, upon the payment of the required visa fee and head tax. Upon the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to make appropriate deduction of one number from the Austrian quota.


*Approved September 6, 1950.*

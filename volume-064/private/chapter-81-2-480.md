# Chapter 480

> AN ACT For the relief of Marco Murolo and his wife Romana Pellis Murolo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws, Marco Murolo and his wife, Romana Pellis Murolo, shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted into the United States for permanent residence as of the date of their lust entry into the United States, upon payment of the required head t axes and visa fees.

## § 2.

The Secretary of State is authorized and directed to instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct two numbers from the appropriate quota for the first year that such numbers are available.


*Approved July 20, 1950.*

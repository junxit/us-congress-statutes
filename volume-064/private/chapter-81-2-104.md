# Chapter 104

> AN ACT For the relief of Charles G. McCormack, captain, Medical Corps, United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Charles G. McCormack, captain, Medical Corps, United States Navy, file numbered 59290, retired December 1, 1947, and recalled to active-duty June 1, 1948, be reinstated to the active list of the Regular Navy.

The Secretary of the Navy shall assign him a position on the lineal list and a running mate which are appropriate to Captain McCormack’s length of active naval service.


*Approved April 26, 1950.*

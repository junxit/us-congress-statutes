# Chapter 625

> AN ACT For the relief of Chiyoko Yano.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, relating to the exclusion of aliens inadmissible because of race shall not hereafter apply to Chiyoko Yano, the Japanese fiancée of Lieutenant Kiyoshi G. Hachiya, a citizen of the United States, and that the said Chiyoko Yano may be eligible for a nonquota immigration visa if she is found otherwise admissible under the immigration laws: Provided, That the administrative authorities find that marriage between the above-named parties occurred within three months immediately succeeding the enactment date of this Act.


*Approved August 7, 1950.*

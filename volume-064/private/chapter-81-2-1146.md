# Chapter 1146

> AN ACT For the relief of Master Stanley (Zaehne) Hiller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Master Stanley (Zachne) Hiller shall be deemed to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Harry Berger, citizens of the United States.


*Approved December 21, 1950.*

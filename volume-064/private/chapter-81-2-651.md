# Chapter 651

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Donald Rafter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Représentatives of the. United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, which exclude from admission to the United States aliens who are ineligible to citizenship, shall not hereafter apply to Mrs. Donald Rafter, the wife of Corporal Donald Rafter, (XXX)—A. S. U. Military Police Detachment, Camp Stoneman, California), a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 8, 1950.*

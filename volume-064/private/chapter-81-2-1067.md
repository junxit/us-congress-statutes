# Chapter 1067

> AN ACT For the relief of Basilio Gorgone.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions of the eleventh category of section 3 of the Immigration Act of 1917, as amended (S U. S. Œ 136 (e) ), Basilio Gorgone, the husband of a citizen of the United States, may be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if he is found otherwise admissible under the provisions of the immigration laws.


*Approved September 27, 1950.*

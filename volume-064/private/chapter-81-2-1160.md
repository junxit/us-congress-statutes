# Chapter 1160

> AN ACT For the relief of Angela Maria Pisano.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purposes of sections 4 (a) and 9 of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, Angela Maria Pisano shall be deemed to be the natural-born alien child of Mrs. Mary Levato, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved December 27, 1950.*

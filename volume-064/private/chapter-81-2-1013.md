# Chapter 1013

> AN ACT For the relief of Fe’R. Dumaguing.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws Fe’R. Dumaguing, of New York City, New York, shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence on the 19th day of September 1946, the date of her actual entry into the United States, upon the payment by her of the required visa fee and head tax.

## § 2.

Upon the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of State is authorized and directed to instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the nonpreference category from the first available Philippine immigration quota.


*Approved September 23, 1950.*

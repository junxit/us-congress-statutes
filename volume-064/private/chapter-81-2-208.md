# Chapter 208

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Georgette Ponsard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of the immigration and naturalization laws which exclude from admission into the United States persons of the Japanese race, shall not be held to apply to Mrs. Georgette Ponsard, the wife of Paul Ponsard, who is residing in Mexico City, Mexico.


*Approved May 25, 1950.*

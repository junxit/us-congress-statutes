# Chapter 478

> AN ACT For the relief of Stefanie Pfister and Hildegarde Weber.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Stefanie Pfister and Hildegarde Weber who are presently residing in Austria, and who have been adopted by Mr. and Mrs. Carl Pfister, of New Britain, Connecticut, shall be deemed to be the natural-born alien children of said Mr. and Mrs. Carl Pfister.


*Approved July 19, 1950.*

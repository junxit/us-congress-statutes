# Chapter 349

> AN ACT For the relief of Veronica Jolly.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the Immigration and Naturalization laws, Veronica Jolly, of Washington, District of Columbia, shall be held and considered to be found lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of her actual entry into the United States upon payment by her of the visa fee of $10 and the head tax of $S.

## § 2.

The Secretary of State is authorized and directed to deduct one number from the quota for Australia.


*Approved June 23, 1950.*

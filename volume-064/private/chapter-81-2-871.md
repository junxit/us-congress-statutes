# Chapter 871

> AN ACT For the relief of Stella Matutina Kazuko Yamazaki.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, the provisions of section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, which excludes from admission to the United States aliens who are ineligible to citizenship, shall not hereafter apply to Stella Matutina Kazuko Yamazaki, the minor Japanese-born child of Teresa Sachiko Yamazaki, a native-born citizen of the United States.


*Approved September 5, 1950.*

# Chapter 297

> AN ACT For the relief of Ann Irene Feikema.

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration laws, relating to the issuance of immigration visas for admission to the United States for permanent residence and relating to admissions at ports of entry of aliens as immigrants for permanent residence in the United States, that provision of section 3 of the Immigration Act of 1917 (39 Stat. 875), as amended (U. S. C., title 8, sec. 136 (e)), which excludes from admission to the United States persons who have been convicted of or admit having committed a felony, or other crime, or misdemeanor involving moral turpitude shall not hereafter be held to apply to Ann Irene Feikema, the wife of a United States citizen and an honorably discharged veteran of World War II.


*Approved June 17, 1950.*

# Chapter 748

> AN ACT Authorizing the Secretary of the Interior to issue a patent in fee to Charlotte Geisdorff Kibby.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is authorized and directed to issue to Charlotte Geisdorff Kibby a patent in fee to the following-described lands on the Crow Indian Reservation, Montana: Lots 9,10,11, and 12 of section 3, lots 9,10,11, and 12 of section 4, the north half and the north half of the north half of the southwest quarter of section 9, and the north half of section 10, township 1 south, range 34 east, Montana principal meridian, containing seven hundred seventy-six and eight one-hundredths acres more or less.


*Approved August 17, 1950.*

# Chapter 302

> AN ACT For the relief of Ng Soo Lip and Ng Yut Chee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws the provisions of section 4 (a) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, pertaining to unmarried children under twenty-one years of age of a citizen of the United States, shall be held to be applicable to the aliens Ng Soo Lip and Ng Yut Chee, minor children of N. Mon Wai, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved June 17, 1950.*

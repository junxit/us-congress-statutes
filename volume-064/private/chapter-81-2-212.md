# Chapter 212

> AN ACT For the relief of A. D. Strenger and his wife Claire Strenger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the Immigration and Naturalization laws the provisions of section 307 (b) of the Nationality Act of 1940 shall not be held to apply to A. D. Strenger and his wife Claire Strenger.


*Approved May 26, 1950.*

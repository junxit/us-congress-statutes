# Chapter 318

> AN ACT For the relief of Miriam Barkle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

’Chat the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Miriam Barkle, Pacific Grove, California, the sum of $5,450.50. Any person violating the provisions of this Act shall be deemed guilty of a misdemeanor and upon conviction thereof shall be fined in any sum not exceeding $1,000.


*Approved June 19, 1950.*

# Chapter 1015

> AN ACT For the relief of Christina Karamanos Demas and Antonia Karamanos Demas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purpose of the immigration and naturalization laws, Christina Karamanos Demas and Antonia Karamanos Demas shall be considered to be the alien natural-born children of Mr. and Mrs. Peter C. Demas, United States citizens.


*Approved September 23, 1950.*

# Chapter 894

> AN ACT For the relief of Mary Rynik Baran.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Mary Rynik Baran, a native and citizen of Czechoslovakia, shall be considered to be the naturalbom alien child of Mr. and Mrs. George Baran, United States citizens.


*Approved September 5, 1950.*

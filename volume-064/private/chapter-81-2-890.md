# Chapter 890

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Eiko Yoshizawa Lendrum and Charles Robert Lendrum, Junior.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, section 13 (c) of the Immigration Act of 1924, as amended, which excludes from the United States persons ineligible to citizenship, shall not hereafter apply to Mrs. Eiko Yoshizawa Lendrum and Charles Robert Lendrum, Junior, Tokyo, Japan, Japanese wife and minor son, respectively, of Charles Robert Lendrum, a United States citizen now serving in Japan with, the Armed Forces of the United States.


*Approved September 5, 1950.*

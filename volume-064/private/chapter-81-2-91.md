# Chapter 91

> AN ACT For the relief of Peter Michael El-Hini.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, the alien, Peter Michael El-Hini, shall be considered to be the natural-born son of his stepfather, John A. Pfau, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved April 19, 1950.*

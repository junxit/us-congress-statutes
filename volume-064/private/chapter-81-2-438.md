# Chapter 438

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Billy J. Knight and Dorothea Knight.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Dorothea Knight, the natural-born daughter of Private First Class Billy J. Knight, deceased, shall be deemed to be a United States citizen by birth.

## § 2.

That, for the purposes of the immigration and naturalization laws, Mrs. Billy J. Knight (formerly Miss Ellinar Pitterich), who would be entitled to nonquota immigration status but for the death of her husband, shall, if otherwise found admissible to the United States, be deemed to be a nonquota immigrant.


*Approved June 30, 1950.*

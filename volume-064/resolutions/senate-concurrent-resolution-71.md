# Senate Concurrent Resolution 71

> NATIONAL HEALTH PROGRAM, 1949

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed one thousand additional copies of the hearings conducted before a subcommittee of the Senate Committee on Labor and Public Welfare on S1106, S1456, S1581, and S1679, Eighty-first Congress, first session, Parts One and Two of the National Health Program, 1949Such additional copies shall be for the use of the Senate Committee on Labor and Public Welfare.


*Agreed to March 23, 1950.*

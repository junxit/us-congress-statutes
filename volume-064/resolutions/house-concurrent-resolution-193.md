# House Concurrent Resolution 193

> ADJOURNMENT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Thursday, April 6, 1950, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian Tuesday, April 18, 1950.


*Passed April 3, 1950.*

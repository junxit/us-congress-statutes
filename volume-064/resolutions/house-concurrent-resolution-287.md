# House Concurrent Resolution 287

> ADJOURNMENT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Saturday, September 23, 1950, they stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian on Monday, November 27, 1950.


*Passed September 22, 1950.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 274

> FEDERAL-AID HIGHWAY ACT OF 1950

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Clerk of the House of Representatives in the enrollment of the bill HR7941, the Federal-Aid Highway Act of 1950, is authorized and directed to make the following corrections : In the provisos of section 5, strike out the words “subsection” wherever they occur and insert the words “section”.


*Passed August 29, 1950.*

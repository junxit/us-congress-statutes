# House Concurrent Resolution 297

> ADJOURNMENT SINE DIE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Tuesday, January 2, 1951, and that when they adjourn on said day they stand adjourned sine die.


*Passed January 2, 1951.*

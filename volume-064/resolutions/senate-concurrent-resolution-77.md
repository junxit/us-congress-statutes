# Senate Concurrent Resolution 77

> EUGENIO MAISTERRENA BARRENECHE

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President of the United States be, and he is hereby, requested to return to the Senate the enrolled bill (S204) for the relief of Eugenio Maisterrena Barreneche; that if and when the said bill is returned by the President, the action of the Presiding Officers of the two Houses in signing the said bill be deemed to be rescinded; and that the Secretary of the Senate be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, in the reenrollment of said bill, to make the following correction, namely, on page 1, line 6, of the Senate engrossed bill, strike out the numerals “1940” and in lieu thereof insert “1946”.


*Agreed to March 2, 1950.*

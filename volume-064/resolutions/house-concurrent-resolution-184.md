# House Concurrent Resolution 184

> BRIGHAM YOUNG STATUE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Brigham Young Statue Commission of Utah is hereby authorized to place temporarily in the rotunda of the Capitol a statue of the late Brigham Young, of Utah, and to hold ceremonies in the rotunda on said occasion; and the Architect of the Capitol is hereby authorized to make the necessary arrangements therefor; and be it further

That said statue shall be permanently located in Statuary Hall.


*Passed May 1, 1950.*

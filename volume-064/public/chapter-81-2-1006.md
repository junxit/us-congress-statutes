# Chapter 1006

> AN ACT Relating to the furnishing of accommodations at Klamath Falls, Oregon, for the United States District Court for the District of Oregon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 142 of title 28 of the United States Code (relating to accommodations at places for holding court) shall not apply to the holding of court at Klamath Falls, Oregon, by the United States District Court for the District of Oregon.


*Approved September 23, 1950.*

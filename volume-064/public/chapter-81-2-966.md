# Chapter 966

> AN ACT To amend section 4 of the Act of March 1, 1911 (36 Stat. L. 962; 16 U. S. C. 513), relating to membership of the National Forest Reservation Commission.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4 of the Act of March 1, 1911 (36 Stat. L. 962; 16 U. S. C. 513), is hereby amended by deleting the comma appearing immediately after the term “Secretary of War” and inserting after the mentioned term the words “or as an alternate, the Chief of Engineers of the Army,”.


*Approved September 21, 1950.*

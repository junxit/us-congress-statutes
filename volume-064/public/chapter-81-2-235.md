# Chapter 235

> AN ACT To provide for the admission of pay patients to the Home for the Aged and Infirm.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That pay patients may be admitted to the Home for the Aged and Infirm for care and treatment at such rates and under such regulations as may be established by the Board of Public Welfare, insofar as such admissions will not interfere with admission of indigent patients: Provided, however, That the rates shall not exceed the estimated per capita cost for the current year.


*Approved June 14, 1950.*

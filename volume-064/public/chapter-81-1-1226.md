# Chapter 1226

> AN ACT To amend section 120 of the Internal Revenue Code.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 120 of the Internal Revenue Code (relating to unlimited deduction for charitable and other contributions) is hereby amended by striking out “in respect of preceding taxable years” and inserting in lieu, thereof “in respect of such year or preceding taxable years”.

## § 2.

The amendment made by this Act shall be applicable to taxable years beginning after December 31, 1942.


*Approved January 11, 1951.*

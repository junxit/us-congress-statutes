# Chapter 537

> AN ACT To suspend restrictions on the authorized personnel strength of the Armed Forces, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That provisions of law imposing restrictions on the authorized personnel strength of any component of the Armed Forces, including section 2 of the Act of April 18, 1946 (60 Stat. 92), and section 2, title I, of the Selective Service Act of 1948 (62 Stat. 605), as amended, and sections 102 and 202 of the Act of July 10, 1950 (Public Law 604, Eighty-first Congress), are hereby suspended until July 31, 1954.


*Approved August 3, 1950.*

# Chapter 1116

> AN ACT To amend the War Claims Act of 1948, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 6 of the War Claims Act of 1948 (Public Law 896, Eightieth Congress; 62 Stat. 1240), as amended, be amended by striking therefrom part 4 of subsection (c) and inserting in lieu thereof: “(4) Parents (in equal shares) if there is no widow, dependent husband, or child.”


*Approved September 30, 1950.*

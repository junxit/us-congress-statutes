# Chapter 351

> JOINT RESOLUTION Extending the period of effectiveness of the Selective Service Act of 1948 for fifteen days.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (b) of section 17 of the Selective Service Act of 1948 is amended by striking out “the second anniversary of the date of enactment of this title” and inserting in lieu thereof “July 9, 1950”.


*Approved June 23, 1950.*

# Chapter 560

> AN ACT Authorizing transfer of land and improvements thereon by the Secretary of the Interior to New Mexico State Fair.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of Interior be, and. he is hereby, authorized to convey, without the payment of any funds, to New Mexico State Fair, a body corporate of the State of New Mexico, the land with improvements thereon which New Mexico State Fair conveyed to United States for use as an Indian exhibit building within the New Mexico State Fair grounds by deed dated July 14, 1938: Provided, however, That in any conveyance made by the Secretary of the Interior he may incorporate therein such terms and conditions with respect to continued use of said premises or any part thereof by the Bureau of Indian Affairs as the Secretary of the Interior may deem necessary or desirable.


*Approved August 4, 1950.*

# Chapter 535

> AN ACT To amend the Tariff Act of 1930 to provide for exemption from duty of certain sound recordings imported by the Department of State, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph 1628 of the Tariff Act of 1930 is hereby amended by striking out the period at the end thereof and by inserting in lieu thereof a semicolon and the following: “sound recordings imported by the Department of State for use in the program authorized by the United States Information and Educational Exchange Act of 1948 (Public Law 402, Eightieth Congress).”


*Approved August 3, 1950.*

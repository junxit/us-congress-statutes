# Chapter 923

> JOINT RESOLUTION To designate the reservoir above the Baldhill Dam in North Dakota as Lake Ashtabula.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the reservoir located above the Baldhill Dam in North Dakota shall hereafter be known as Lake Ashtabula, and any law, regulation, document, or record of the United States in which such reservoir is designated or referred to shall be held to refer to such reservoir under and by the name of Lake Ashtabula.


*Approved September 8, 1950.*

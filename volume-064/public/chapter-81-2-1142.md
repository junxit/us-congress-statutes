# Chapter 1142

> JOINT RESOLUTION To amend and extend the provisions of the District of Columbia Emergency Rent Act, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1 (b) of the District of Columbia Emergency Rent Act, as amended (D. C. Code, 1940 edition, sec. 45–1601 (b)), is hereby amended by striking out “January 31, 1951” and inserting in lieu thereof “March 31, 1951”.


*Approved December 21, 1950.*

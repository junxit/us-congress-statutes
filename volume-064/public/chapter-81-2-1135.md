# Chapter 1135

> AN ACT For the relief of the city of Chester, Illinois.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the city of Chester, Illinois, is hereby relieved of all liability to pay the Department of the Army for the cost of removing the wreckage of the old bridge over the Mississippi River because of financial difficulties and inability to effect such removal.


*Approved December 11, 1950.*

# Chapter 523

> AN ACT To amend the Federal Aid to Wildlife Restoration Act, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 8 (a), added by the Act of August 18, 1941 (55 Stat. 631), to the Act of September 2, 1937 (50 Stat. 917), entitled “An Act to provide that the United States shall aid the States in wildlife-restoration projects, and for other purposes”, is hereby amended by deleting therefrom the words and figures reading “not exceeding $25,000 for Alaska, and $10,000 each for Hawaii, Puerto Rico, and the Virgin Islands”, and inserting in lieu thereof the words and figures reading “not exceeding $75,000 for Alaska, not exceeding $25,000 for Hawaii, and not exceeding $10,000 each for Puerto Rico and the Virgin Islands”.


*Approved August 3, 1950.*

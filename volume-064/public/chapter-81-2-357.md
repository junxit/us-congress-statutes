# Chapter 357

> AN ACT To extend the Rubber Act of 1948 (Public Law 469, Eightieth Congress), and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** subsection (a) of section 9 of the Rubber Act of 1948 (Public Law 469, Eightieth Congress) is amended (1) by striking out “April 1, 1949” and inserting in lieu thereof “April 1, 1951”, and (2) by striking out “January 15, 1950” and inserting in lieu thereof “January 15, 1952”.
- **(b)** Section 20 of such Act is amended by striking out “June 30, 1950” and inserting in lieu thereof “June 30, 1952”.


*Approved June 24, 1950.*

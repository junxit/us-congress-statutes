# Chapter 601

> AN ACT To amend section 104 of title 28 of the United States Code so as to create a Greenville division in the northern district of Mississippi, with terms of court to be held at Greenville.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 104 of title 28 of the United States Code is hereby amended to read as follows:

> ## § 104. Mississippi
>
> “Mississippi is divided into two judicial districts to be known as the northern and southern districts of Mississippi.
>
> - **(a)** *“northern district* The northern district comprises four divisions. “Court for the eastern division shall be held at Aberdeen. “Court for the western division shall be held at Oxford. “Court for the Delta division shall be held at Clarksdale. “Court for the Greenville division shall ne held at Greenville.
>   - **(1)** Eastern division comprises the counties of Alcorn, Attala, Chickasaw, Choctaw, Clay, Itawamba, Lee, Lowndes, Monroe, Oktibbaha, Prentiss, Tishomingo, and Winston.
>   - **(2)** The western division comprises the counties of Benton, Calhoun, Grenada, Lafayette, Marshall, Montgomery, Pontotoc, Tippah, Union, Webster, and Yalobusha.
>   - **(3)** The Delta division comprises the counties of Bolivar, Coahoma, De Soto, Panola, Quitman, Tallahatchie, Tate, and Tunica.
>   - **(4)** The Greenville division comprises the counties of Carroll, Humphreys, Leflore, Sunflower, and Washington.
> - **(b)** *“southern district* The southern district comprises five divisions. “Court for the Jackson division shall be held at Jackson. “Court for the eastern division shall be held at Meridian. “Court for the western division shall be held at Vicksburg. “Court for the southern division shall be held at Biloxi. “Court for the Hattiesburg division shall be held at Hattiesburg.”
>   - **(1)** The Jackson division comprises the counties of Amite, Copiah, Franklin, Hinds, Holmes, Leake, Lincoln, Madison, Pike, Rankin, Scott, Simpson, and Smith.
>   - **(2)** The eastern division comprises the counties of Clarke, Jasper, Kemper, Landerdale, Neshoba, Newton, Noxubee, and Wayne.
>   - **(3)** The western division comprises the counties of Adams, Claiborne, Issaquena, Jefferson, Sharkey, Warren, Wilkinson, and Yazoo.
>   - **(4)** The southern division comprises the counties of George, Hancock, Harrison, Jackson, Pearl River, and Stone.
>   - **(5)** The Hattiesburg division comprises the counties of Covington, Forrest, Greene, Jefferson Davis, Jones, Lamar, Lawrence, Marion, Perry, and Walthall.


*Approved August 7, 1950.*

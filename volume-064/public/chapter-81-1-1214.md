# Chapter 1214

> AN ACT To amend the Federal Airport Act so as to make the United States share of costs for land acquisition the same as for other project costs.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 10 of the Federal Airport Act is amended by striking out all of subsection (d) thereof.


*Approved January 9, 1951.*

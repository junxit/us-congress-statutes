# Chapter 1215

> AN ACT To remove marketing penalties on certain long staple cotton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the marketing penalty provided in section 346 of the Agricultural Adjustment Act of 1938, as amended August 29, 1949, shall not be applied to long staple cotton of the 1950 crop ginned on saw type gins where such action was necessary to conserve the cotton because of frost or weather damage.


*Approved January 9, 1951.*

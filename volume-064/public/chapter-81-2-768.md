# Chapter 768

> AN ACT To amend the Act of March 11, 1948 (62 Stat. 78), relating to the establishment of the De Soto National Memorial, in the State of Florida.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3 of the Act of March 11, 1948 (62 Stat. 78), relating to the establishment of the De Soto National Memorial, Florida, is hereby amended to read as follows:

> ## § 3.
>
> There is hereby authorized to be appropriated such sums, not to exceed $50,000, as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act.”


*Approved August 21, 1950.*

# Chapter 105

> AN ACT To provide an extension of the time for making application for terminal-leave pay.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 5 of the Armed Forces Leave Act of 1946, as amended (37 U. S. C., sec. 34), is hereby amended by striking out “September 1, 1948” wherever it appears in such section and inserting in lieu thereof “June 30, 1951”.


*Approved April 26, 1950.*

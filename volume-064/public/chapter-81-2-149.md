# Chapter 149

> AN ACT To authorize the sale of select base material, at the Fort Benning Military Reservation, to Muscogee County, State of Georgia, for use on county roads.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Secretary of the Army is hereby authorized to sell to the county of Muscogee, State of Georgia, such amounts of select base material as may be available at the Fort Benning Military Reservation, and as may be surplus to the foreseeable needs of the reservation, at the rate of not less than 5 cents per cubic yard, for use on county roads under such terms and conditions, which shall include those terms set out in section 2, as he may deem advisable.

## § 2.

The term “select base material” as used herein shall be construed to mean such clay, sand, gravel, and topsoil as can be excavated by operating a borrow pit at an average depth of three feet.

## § 3.

The purchaser shall assume all responsibility for damage due to contaminations or other military activities and shall not hold the Government of the United States liable for damages of any description.

## § 4.

No select base material shall be removed subsequent to December 31, 1955.

## § 5.

Any contract that may be entered into for the sale of select base material under the authority of this bill shall be revocable at will by the Secretary of the Army.

## § 6.

The purchaser, as excavation in any area is completed, shall be required to level, drain, and fertilize that area to the satisfaction of the commanding general, Fort Benning, and, upon the completion of such leveling, draining, and fertilizing, to plant kudzu crowns on such area at the rate of five hundred to the acre.

## § 7.

The boundaries of the area lying immediately south of the Macon Highway and west of Randall Creek, from which area select base material may be removed, shall be designated by the commanding general, Fort Benning, or by his duly appointed representative.

## § 8.

The proceeds from the sale of select base material shall be covered into the general funds of the United States Treasury.


*Approved May 2, 1950.*

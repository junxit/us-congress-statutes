# Chapter 266

> AN ACT To extend the boundaries of the Toiyabe National Forest in the State of Nevada.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the boundaries of the Toiyabe National Forest are hereby extended to include the following-described lands in Washoe County, Nevada, and, subject to valid and existing claims, all lands of the United States within the described area are hereby made parts of the Toiyabe National Forest and hereafter shall be subject to all laws, rules, and regulations relating thereto:

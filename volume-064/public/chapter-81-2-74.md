# Chapter 74

> AN ACT To authorize the Commissioners of the District of Columbia to provide for the removal of sludge.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioners of the District of Columbia are hereby authorized to provide for the removal of sludge, a byproduct of the District of Columbia sewage-treatment plant, deposited or proposed to be deposited at the District of Columbia Reformatory, Lorton, Virginia, by contract or otherwise, and to enter into contract or contracts for such removal, for periods not exceeding five years.


*Approved March 24, 1950.*

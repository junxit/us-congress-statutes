# Chapter 1191

> AN ACT Authorizing payment to certain States amounts withheld from grazing fees on public lands.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated the sum of $1,351,149.37, for payment by the Secretary of the Treasury to the following States, the following sums: Arizona, $153,829.60; California, $126,880.55; Colorado, $201,641.56; Idaho, $167,473.14; Missouri, $1,805.16; Montana, $130,273.34; Nebraska, $9,473.77; Nevada, $74,851.66; New Mexico, $95,725.13; Oregon, $77,960.80; South Dakota, $14,696.39; Utah, $174,997.45; Washington, $19,085.83; West Virginia, $362.50; Wyoming, $102,092.49. Such sums shall be expended by such States in accordance with the terms of the Acts of May 28, 1908, and March 1, 1911, as amended (16 U. S. C. 500).


*Approved December 29, 1950.*

# Chapter 997

> AN ACT To amend the Soil Conservation and Domestic Allotment Act, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 8 (a), as amended, of the Soil Conservation and Domestic Allotment Act, is amended (a) by striking out “January 1, 1951” wherever it appears therein and inserting in lieu thereof “January 1, 1953”, and (b) by striking out “December 31, 1950” and inserting in lieu thereof “December 31, 1952”.


*Approved September 23, 1950.*

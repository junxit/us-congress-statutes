# Chapter 463

> AN ACT Transferring management of certain public lands from the Agriculture Department to the Fort Sill Indian School in Oklahoma for agriculture uses.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the following-described tract of public land: The north half of the south half of section 19, township 2 north, range 11 west, Indian meridian, Comanche County, Oklahoma, being a part of the Fort Sill Indian School reserve, and formerly transferred to the Department of Agriculture for use as a dry-farming experimental station, is hereby, together with all buildings, improvements, and appurtenances, transferred back to the said Fort Sill Indian School for use in connection with the agriculture training program of such institution.


*Approved July 18, 1950.*

# Chapter 767

> AN ACT To provide for the furnishing of quarters at Newnan, Georgia, for the United States District Court for the Northern District of Georgia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limitations and restrictions contained in section 142, title 28, of the United States Code, shall be waived insofar as pertains to holding court for the Newnan Division of the United States District Court at Newnan, Georgia.


*Approved August 21, 1950.*

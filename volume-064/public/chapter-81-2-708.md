# Chapter 708

> AN ACT To authorize the appointment of two additional district judges for the northern district of Illinois.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President shall appoint, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, two additional district judges for the northern district of Illinois. Accordingly, title 28, United States Code, section 133, is amended to read as follows with respect to said district:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| * | * | * | * | * | * | * |


*Approved August 14, 1950.*

# Public Law 113–171

> An Act To make technical corrections to Public Law 110–229 to reflect the renaming of the Bainbridge Island Japanese American Exclusion Memorial, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. BAINBRIDGE ISLAND JAPANESE AMERICAN EXCLUSION MEMORIAL.

Section 313 of the Consolidated Natural Resources Act of 2008 (Public Law 110–229) is amended as follows:

- **(1)** In the heading of subsection (b), by striking “Japanese American Memorial” and inserting “Japanese American Exclusion Memorial”.
- **(2)** In the heading of subsection (c)(5)(C), by striking “Japanese american memorial” and inserting “Japanese american exclusion memorial”.
- **(3)** In subsection (c)(5)(C), by striking “Japanese American Memorial” and inserting “Japanese American Exclusion Memorial”.


*Approved September 26, 2014.*

# Public Law 113–237

> An Act To make revisions in title 36, United States Code, as necessary to keep the title current and make technical corrections and improvements.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. TABLE OF CONTENTS.

The table of contents for this Act is as follows:

- Sec. 1. Table of contents.
- Sec. 2. Purpose.
- Sec. 3. Technical amendments.

## § 2. PURPOSE.

The purpose of this Act is to make revisions in title 36, United States Code, as necessary to keep the title current and make technical corrections and improvements.

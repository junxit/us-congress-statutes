# Public Law 113–202

> Joint Resolution Making further continuing appropriations for fiscal year 2015, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Continuing Appropriations Resolution, 2015 (Public Law 113–164) is amended by striking the date specified in section 106(3) and inserting “December 13, 2014”.


*Approved December 12, 2014.*

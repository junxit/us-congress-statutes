# Public Law 113–73

> Joint Resolution Making further continuing appropriations for fiscal year 2014, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Continuing Appropriations Act, 2014 (Public Law 113–46) is amended by striking the date specified in section 106(3) and inserting “January 18, 2014”.


*Approved January 15, 2014.*

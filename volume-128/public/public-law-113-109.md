# Public Law 113–109

> An Act To amend the Clean Air Act to remove the requirement for dealer certification of new light-duty motor vehicles.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. REMOVAL OF REQUIREMENT FOR DEALER CERTIFICATION OF NEW LIGHT-DUTY MOTOR VEHICLES.

Section 207(h) of the Clean Air Act (42 U.S.C. 7541(h)) is amended—

- **(1)** by striking paragraph (1); and
- **(2)** by redesignating paragraphs (2) and (3) as paragraphs (1) and (2), respectively.


*Approved June 9, 2014.*

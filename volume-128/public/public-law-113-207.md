# Public Law 113–207

> An Act Designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 25 South Oak Street in London, Ohio, as the “London Fallen Veterans Memorial Post Office”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. LONDON FALLEN VETERANS MEMORIAL POST OFFICE.

- **(a)** *Designation.—* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 25 South Oak Street in London, Ohio, shall be known and designated as the “London Fallen Veterans Memorial Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References.—* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “London Fallen Veterans Memorial Post Office”.


*Approved December 16, 2014.*

# Public Law 115–111

> An Act To facilitate the addition of park administration at the Coltsville National Historical Park, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. AMENDMENT TO COLTSVILLE NATIONAL HISTORICAL PARK DONATION SITE.

Section 3032(b)(2)(B) of Public Law 113–291 (16 U.S.C. 410qqq) is amended by striking “East Armory” and inserting “Colt Armory Complex”.


*Approved January 10, 2018.*

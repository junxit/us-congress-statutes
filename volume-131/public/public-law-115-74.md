# Public Law 115–74

> Joint Resolution Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection relating to “Arbitration Agreements”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection relating to “Arbitration Agreements” (82 Fed. Reg. 33210 (July 19, 2017)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.


*Approved November 1, 2017.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 20

> adjournment of house of representatives

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Thursday, April 22, 1943, it stands adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian Monday, May 3, 1943.


*Passed April 22, 1943.*

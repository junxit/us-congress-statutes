# House Concurrent Resolution 21

> address by prime minister of great britain

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the address delivered before the joint meeting of the Members of the two Houses of Congress by the Prime Minister of Great Britain, the Right Honorable Winston Churchill, on Wednesday, May 19, 1943, be printed as a House document, and that thirty thousand additional copies shall be printed for the House document room.


*Passed May 28, 1943.*

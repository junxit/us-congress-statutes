# Senate Concurrent Resolution 11

> compensation of d. c. employees and white house police force

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President of the United States be, and he is hereby, requested to return to the Senate the enrolled bill (S. 17) to provide for a temporary increase in compensation for certain employees of the District of Columbia Government and the White House Police Force; that if and when the said bill is returned by the President, the action of the Presiding Officers of the two Houses in signing the said bill be deemed to be rescinded; and that the Secretary of the Senate be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, in the reenrollment of the said bill, to make the following corrections, namely: on page 1, line 11, of the engrossed House amendment, after the word “Government”, insert a comma; and on page 2, line 1, of the said engrossed amendment, after the word “purpose”, insert a comma.


*Agreed to March 26, 1943.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 17

> adjournment

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Thursday, July 8, 1943, they shall stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian on Tuesday, September 14, 1943, or until 12 o’clock meridian on the third day after their respective Members are notified to reassemble in accordance with section 2 of this concurrent resolution, whichever event first occurs.

## § 2.

The President of the Senate and the Speaker of the House of Representatives shall notify the Members of the Senate and the House, respectively, to reassemble whenever in their opinion legislative expediency shall warrant it or whenever the majority leader of the Senate and the majority leader of the House, acting jointly, or the minority leader of the Senate and the minority leader of the House, acting jointly, file a written request with the Secretary of the Senate and the Clerk of the House that the Congress reassemble for the consideration of legislation.


*Passed July 8, 1943.*

# Chapter 146

> AN ACT To amend section 32 of the Emergency Farm Mortgage Act of 1933, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the tenth sentence of section 32 of the Emergency Farm Mortgage Act of 1933, as amended, is amended by striking out “July 1, 1943” wherever it appears therein and inserting in lieu thereof “July 1, 1945”.


*Approved June 26, 1943.*

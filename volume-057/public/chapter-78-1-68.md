# Chapter 68

> JOINT RESOLUTION To extend the provisions of the Bituminous Coal Act of 1937 for a period of thirty days.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

-  (a) section 19 of the Bituminous Coal Act of 1937 (relating to termination of the Act) is amended by striking out “April 26, 1943” and inserting in lieu thereof “May 24, 1943”.
- **(b)** Section 3527 of the Internal Revenue Code (relating to termination of the bituminous coal taxes) is amended by striking out “April 25, 1943” and inserting in lieu thereof “May 23, 1943”.


*Approved April 24, 1943.*

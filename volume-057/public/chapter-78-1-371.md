# Chapter 371

> JOINT RESOLUTION To extend the time limit for immunity.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That effective as of December 7, 1943, all statutes, resolutions, laws, articles, and regulations, affecting the possible prosecution of any person or persons, military or civil, connected with the Pearl Harbor catastrophe of December 7, 1941, or involved in any other possible or apparent dereliction of duty, that operate to prevent the court martial or prosecution of any person or persons in military or civil capacity, involved in any matter in connection with the Pearl Harbor catastrophe of December 7, 1941, or involved in any other possible or apparent dereliction of duty, are hereby extended for a period of six months.


*Approved December 20, 1943.*

# Chapter 383

> JOINT RESOLUTION Continuing the Commodity Credit Corporation as an agency of the United States.

*Resolved, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first sentence of section 7 of the Act approved January 31, 1935 (49 Stat. 4), as amended, is hereby amended by striking out “December 31, 1943” and inserting in lieu thereof “February 17, 1944”.


*Approved December 23, 1943.*

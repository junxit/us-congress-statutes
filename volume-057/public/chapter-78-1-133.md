# Chapter 133

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making appropriations for advances to States for certain Federal grants for the first quarter of the fiscal year 1944.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there are hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, such sums as may be necessary for making for the first quarter of the fiscal year 1944 (1) grants to States for old-age assistance, (2) grants to States for aid to dependent children, (3) grants to States for aid to the blind, and (4) grants to States for unemployment compensation administration: Provided, That the obligations incurred and expenditures made for each of such purposes under the authority of this joint resolution shall be charged to the appropriation therefor in the Labor-Federal Security Appropriation Act, 1944.


*Approved June 19, 1943.*

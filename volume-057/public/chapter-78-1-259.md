# Chapter 259

> AN ACT To amend the National Housing Act, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 603 (a) of the National Housing Act, as amended, is hereby amended by (1) striking out “$1,200,000,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$1,600,000,000”; and (2) striking out of the third proviso “July 1, 1944” in each place where it appears and inserting in lieu thereof “July 1, 1945”.

## § 2.

Section 203 (a) of such Act, as amended, is amended by striking out of the third proviso “1944” and inserting in lieu thereof “1946”.

## § 3.

The first sentence of section 2 (a) of such Act, as amended, is amended by striking out “1944” and inserting in lieu thereof “1947”.

## § 4.

The first sentence of section 2 (f) of such Act, as amended, is amended by striking out “three-fourths of”.


*Approved October 15, 1943.*

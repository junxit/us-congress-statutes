# Chapter 15

> AN ACT To extend for one year the provisions of An Act to promote the defense of the United States, approved March 11, 1941.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (c) of section 3 of An Act to promote the defense of the United States, approved March 11, 1941. is amended by striking out “June 30, 1943” wherever it appears therein and inserting in lieu thereof “June 30, 1944”; by striking out “July 1, 1946” and inserting in lieu thereof “July 1, 1947”; and by striking out “July 1, 1943” and inserting in lieu thereof “July 1, 1944”; and subsection (b) of section 6 of such Act is amended by striking out “June 30, 1946” and inserting in lieu thereof “June 30, 1947”.


*Approved March 11, 1943.*

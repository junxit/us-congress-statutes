# Chapter 21

> AN ACT To amend the National Housing Act, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 603 (a) of the National Housing Act, as amended, is hereby amended by (1) striking out “$800,000,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$1,200,000,000”; and (2) striking out of the third proviso “July 1, 1943” in each place where it appears and inserting in lieu thereof “July 1, 1944”.

## § 2.

The first sentence of section 2 (a) of such Act as amended, is amended by striking out “1943” and inserting in lieu thereof “1944”.


*Approved March 23, 1943.*

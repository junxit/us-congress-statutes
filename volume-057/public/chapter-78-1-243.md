# Chapter 243

> AN ACT To designate the Public Library of the District of Columbia a public depository for governmental publications.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Public Library of the District of Columbia is hereby constituted a designated depository of governmental publications, and the Superintendent of Documents shall supply to such library one copy of each such publication, in the same form as supplied to other designated depositories.


*Approved September 28, 1943.*

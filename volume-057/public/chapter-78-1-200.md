# Chapter 200

> AN ACT To authorize the use of part of the United States Capitol Grounds east of the Union Station for the parking of motor vehicles.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Architect of the Capitol is authorized to permit the Washington Terminal Company to use, during the present war and for six months thereafter, for parking space for passenger motor vehicles, that part of the United States Capitol Grounds described as squares S–721 and N–721. Any such use shall be in accordance with such terms and conditions as the Architect of the Capitol may deem necessary and proper.


*Approved July 8, 1943.*

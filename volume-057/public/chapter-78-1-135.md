# Chapter 135

> AN ACT To extend the time within which a suit or suits may be brought under the Act of June 28, 1938 (52 Stat. 1209).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time within which a suit or suits may be brought under the Act entitled “An Act conferring jurisdiction upon the United States Court; of Claims to hear, examine, adjudicate, and render judgment on any and all claims which the Ute Indians or any tribe or band thereof may have against the United States and for other purposes”, approved June 28, 1938 (52 Stat. 1209), be, and the same hereby is, extended until December 31, 1946.


*Approved June 22, 1943.*

# Chapter 28

> JOINT RESOLUTION To permit additional sales of wheat for feed.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limitation contained in the Department of Agriculture Appropriation Act, fiscal year 1943, on the quantity of wheat which Commodity Credit Corporation can sell for feed is hereby increased from one hundred and twenty-five million to two hundred and twenty-five million bushels: Provided, however, That no such wheat shall be sold at a price less than the parity price of corn at the time such sale is made: Provided further, That in making regional adjustments in the sale price of wheat, the minimum price shall not be higher in any area than the United States average parity price of corn.


*Approved March 25, 1943.*

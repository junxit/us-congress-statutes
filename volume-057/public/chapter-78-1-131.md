# Chapter 131

> JOINT RESOLUTION Authorizing the appropriation of such sums as may be necessary to pay the proportionate share of the United States in the annual expenses of the Inter-American Financial and Economic Advisory Committee.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, such sums as may be necessary, but not to exceed $23,000 per annum, for the payment by the United States of its proportionate share in the annual expenses of the Inter-American Financial and Economic Advisory Committee.


*Approved June 19, 1943.*

# Chapter 123

> JOINT RESOLUTION To permit additional sales of wheat for feed.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act approved March 25, 1943 (Public Law 18, Seventy-eighth Congress), be amended by deleting “two hundred and twenty-five million bushels” and inserting in lieu thereof “two hundred and seventy-five million bushels”.


*Approved June 14, 1943.*

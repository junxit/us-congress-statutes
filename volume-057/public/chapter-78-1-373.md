# Chapter 373

> JOINT RESOLUTION Fixing the date of meeting of the second session of the Seventy-eighth Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second session of the Seventy-eighth Congress shall begin at noon on Monday, January 10, 1944.


*Approved December 21, 1943.*

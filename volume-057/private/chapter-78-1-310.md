# Chapter 310

> AN ACT For the relief of Fred A. Flanders.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to cancel the indebtedness of Fred A. Flanders in the amount of $590.55 arising out of the fact that for the period from November 1, 1930, to March 31, 1941, he was paid for services rendered by him as surveyman or inspector in the United States Engineer Office at Clewiston, Florida, and also as cooperative weather observer at Moore Haven, Florida, for the Department of Commerce, the payment of such dual compensation being in contravention of the provisions of section 6 of the Act of May 10, 1916, as amended by the Act of August 29, 1916 (39 Stat. 582, U. S. C., title 5, sec. 58).


*Approved November 22, 1943.*

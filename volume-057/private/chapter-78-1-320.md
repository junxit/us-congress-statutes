# Chapter 320

> AN ACT For the relief of John E. Haas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding any other provision of law John E. Haas, an employee of the Treasury Department retired under the provisions of section 8 (a) of the Act of June 16, 1933, (48 Stat. 305; U. S. C., title 5, sec. 736 (a)), shall not be required to reimburse the United States for any annuity payments received by him during the period of his employment at Hine Junior High School as the director of a boys’ club, under the Community Center Department of the Public Schools of the District of Columbia, from July 1, 1935, to April 1, 1941.


*Approved November 22, 1943.*

# Chapter 209

> AN ACT For the relief of the Morgan Creamery Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to allow the sum of $395.37 in full and final settlement of the claim of the Morgan Creamery Company, Fargo, North Dakota, under contract numbered VA37r–935 entered into on June 25, 1942, with the Veterans’ Administration for furnishing dairy products to the Veterans’ Administration facility, Fargo, North Dakota, during the fiscal year ending June 30, 1943, and the said company is hereby relieved of all liability to the United States on account of excessive cost incurred by reason of its default under the said contract. There is hereby appropriated the sum of $395.37 for payment of the claim.


*Approved April 23, 1946.*

# Chapter 131

> AN ACT For the relief of James F. Desmond.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General and the General Accounting Office are authorized and directed to credit the accounts of James F. Desmond, postmaster at Reading, Massachusetts, in the sum of $7,141.11, representing the net shortage which resulted from embezzlement of funds by the former assistant postmaster at the Reading, Massachusetts, post office.


*Approved April 10, 1946.*

# Chapter 749

> AN ACT To provide for the naturalization of Peter Kim.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That upon compliance with all other provisions of section 701 or 702 of the Nationality Act of 1940, as amended (56 Stat. 182–183; 8 U. S. C. 1001–1002), Peter Kim, Army of the United States, Army serial number 10500015, may be naturalized pursuant to either of said sections as may be applicable, notwithstanding the fact that at the time of his enlistment or induction into the armed forces of the United States, he had not been lawfully admitted to the United States and was not a resident thereof.


*Approved August 2, 1946.*

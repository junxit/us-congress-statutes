# Chapter 415

> AN ACT To confer jurisdiction upon the United States District Court, Southern District of Florida.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That jurisdiction is hereby conferred upon the United States District Court, Southern District of Florida, to hear, determine, and render judgment upon the claims of Mrs. Hortense Arnow, J. M. Rogers, and Mrs. Willie L. Rogers, all of Jacksonville, Florida, for such damages sustained as the result of a collision between the car in which they were riding and a United States Army truck on the highway connecting Jacksonville with Jacksonville Beach, Florida.

## § 2.

Such suit shall be brought in the United States District Court, Southern District of Florida, within one year of the date of approval of this Act.


*Approved June 15, 1946.*

# Chapter 275

> AN ACT To authorize the Secretary of State to transfer certain silver candelabra to May Morgan Beal.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of State is authorized and directed to transfer to May Morgan Beal, of Newton, Massachusetts, all right, title, and interest of the United States in and to the two silver lanterns or candelabra which were originally given to Ambassador Edwin V. Morgan by citizens of Brazil and subsequently presented as a gift to the United States by the said May Morgan Beal in July 1934, for use in the United States Embassy in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil.


*Approved May 28, 1946.*

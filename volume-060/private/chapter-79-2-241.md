# Chapter 241

> AN ACT For the relief of Saunders Wholesale, Incorporated.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Saunders Wholesale, Incorporated, of Key West, Florida, is hereby relieved of liability for the payment of manufacturers’ excise taxes in the amount of $7,834.56 on two million two hundred and forty-two thousand cigarettes and forty-two pounds of manufactured tobacco which were destroyed by fire on February 26, 1945, while stored in a sea stores warehouse operated by the said Saunders Wholesale, Incorporated, after having been lawfully withdrawn from the place of manufacture, without payment of tax, for delivery to vessels for use as sea stores.


*Approved April 27, 1946.*

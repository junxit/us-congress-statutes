# Chapter 405

> AN ACT For the relief of William H. W. Komp.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That William H. W. Komp, of the United States Public Health Service, is hereby relieved of all liability, both as to principal and interest, under the claim of the United States arising by reason of alleged overpayments to him of rental allowances in the amount of $3,543.33, while he was serving at Panama City, Panama, during the period from April 21, 1931, to June 30, 1934; and the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to the said William H. W. Komp, an amount equal to the aggregate of amounts which have been paid by him, or which have been withheld from amounts otherwise due him, in partial satisfaction of such claim. In the settlement of accounts of any disbursing officer of the United States, full credit shall be given for all such alleged overpayments of rental allowances made to the said William H. W. Komp.


*Approved June 14, 1946.*

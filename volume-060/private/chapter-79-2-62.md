# Chapter 62

> AN ACT For the relief of Max Hirsch.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to remove from the records of his office the debt which has been raised therein against Mr. Max Hirsch, county Farm Security Administration supervisor, Anderson, Missouri, in the sum of $289.65, together with interest thereon from date of loss of the public funds for which he is accountable and which were destroyed by fire, without his fault, on November 24, 1943.


*Approved March 6, 1946.*

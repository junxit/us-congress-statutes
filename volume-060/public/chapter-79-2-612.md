# Chapter 612

> AN ACT To include the beads of executive departments and independent agencies within the purview of the Civil Service Retirement Act of May 29, 1930.

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3 (a) of the Civil Service Retirement Act of May 29, 1930, as amended, is amended by striking out the words “and heads of executive departments”.

## § 2.

This Act shall be effective, with respect to any head of a department, at the beginning of his first pay period commencing in the second month following the month in which this Act is enacted. As used in this section the term “department” means any department, independent establishment, or agency (including corporations) in the executive branch of the Government.


*Approved July 24, 1946.*

# Chapter 792

> AN ACT To amend section 121 of the Act entitled “An Act to establish a code of law for the District of Columbia”, approved March 3, 1901, as amended, to authorize the appointment of three additional deputies for the register of wills.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 121 of the Act entitled “An Act to establish a code of law for the District of Columbia”, approved March 3, 1901, as amended by the Act approved June 30, 1902 (title 19, sec. 403, D. C. Code, 1940), is amended by striking out the word “two” preceding the word “deputies” in line 17 thereof, and inserting in lieu thereof the word “five”.


*Approved August 7, 1946.*

# Chapter 283

> AN ACT To repeal section 1548 Revised Statutes (34 U. S. C. 592).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1548, Revised Statutes (34 U. S. C. 592), which provides “The Secretary of the Navy shall cause each commissioned or warrant officer of the Navy, on his entry into the service, be furnished with a copy of the regulations and general orders of the Navy Department then in force, and thereafter with a copy of all such as may be issued,” is hereby repealed.


*Approved June 8, 1946.*

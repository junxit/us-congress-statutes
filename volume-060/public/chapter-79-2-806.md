# Chapter 806

> AN ACT Granting a right-of-way at a revised location to the West Shore Railroad Company, the New York Central Railroad Company, lessee, across a portion of the military reservation at West Point.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress, assembled,*

That the consent of the United States is hereby given to the relocation requested by the Secretary of War, of the railroad of the West Shore Railroad Company, the New York Central Railroad Company being the lessee, across the property belonging to the Government at West Point, in the State of New York, and to the maintenance and operation of said railroad upon such relocated line, subject to such conditions and regulations as may be approved by the Secretary of War.


*Approved August 7, 1946.*

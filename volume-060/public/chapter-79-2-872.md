# Chapter 872

> AN ACT To amend the Act of December 7, 1944, relating to certain overtime compensation of civilian employees of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of December 7, 1944 ( 58 Stat. 796, ch. 520), is amended by making the proviso at the end thereof read as follows: “Provided, That in no case shall there be validated aggregate payment to an employee in excess of six-twelfths of $5,000”.


*Approved August 8, 1946.*

# Chapter 503

> AN ACT To amend the District of Columbia Alley Dwelling Act, approved June 12, 1934, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4 (b) of the Act known as the “District of Columbia Alley Dwelling Act”, approved June 12, 1934, as amended, be further amended to read as follows:

> - **(b)** On and after July 1, 1947, it shall be unlawful to use or occupy any alley building or structure as a dwelling in the District of Columbia.”

## § 2.

That section 6 of such Act, as amended, be further amended by striking “1946” and inserting in lieu thereof “1947”.


*Approved June 26, 1946.*

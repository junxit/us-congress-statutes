# Chapter 494

> AN ACT To fix the salary of the Solicitor of the Department of the Interior.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That hereafter the legal work of the Department of the Interior shall be performed under the supervision and direction of the Solicitor of the Department of the Interior, who shall be appointed by the President with the advice and consent of the Senate and who shall be paid a salary of $10,000 per annum.


*Approved June 26, 1946.*

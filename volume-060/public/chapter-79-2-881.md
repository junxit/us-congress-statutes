# Chapter 881

> AN ACT To amend the Act entitled “An Act to establish a Code of Law for the District of Columbia”, approved March 3, 1901, and the Acts amendatory thereof and supplementary thereto.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United, States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 198 of the Act to establish a Code of Law for the District of Columbia, approved March 3, 1901, and the Acts amendatory thereof and supplementary thereto, constituting the Code of Law for the District of Columbia, as amended, is hereby further amended by striking out therefrom the following sentence: “No person who has served as such Commissioner shall be eligible for reappointment within three years of the date of the expiration of his term of service.”


*Approved August 8, 1946.*

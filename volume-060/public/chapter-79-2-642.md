# Chapter 642

> AN ACT To extend the period within which the Secretary of Agriculture may carry out the purposes of the Soil Conservation and Domestic Allotment Act by making payments to agricultural producers.

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 8 (a), as amended, of the Soil Conservation and Domestic Allotment Act (U. S. C., 1940 edition, Supp. IV, title 16, sec. 590h (a)) is amended (a) by striking out “January 1, 1947” wherever appearing therein and inserting in lieu thereof “January 1, 1949”, and (b) striking out “December 31, 1946” and inserting in lieu thereof “December 31, 1948”.


*Approved July 25, 1946.*

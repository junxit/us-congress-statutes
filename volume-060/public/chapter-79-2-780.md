# Chapter 780

> AN ACT To name the bridge located on New Hampshire Avenue, Washington, District of Columbia, over the Baltimore and Ohio Railroad tracks “The Charles A. Langley Bridge”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the bridge located on New Hampshire Avenue in Washington, District of Columbia, over the Baltimore and Ohio Railroad tracks shall be known and designated as “The Charles A. Langley Bridge”.


*Approved August 7, 1946.*

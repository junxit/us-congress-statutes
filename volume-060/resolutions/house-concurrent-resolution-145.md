# House Concurrent Resolution 145

> adjournment

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Thursday, April 18, 1946, it stands adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian Tuesday, April 30, 1946.


*Passed April 18, 1946.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 69

> pearl harbor attack

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the limit of expenditures authorized by S. Con. Res. 27, Seventy-ninth Congress, for the investigation of the Pearl Harbor attack, as increased by S. Con. Res. 56, be, and the same is hereby, further increased by an additional $25,000, one-half of said amount to be paid from the contingent fund of the Senate and one-half from the contingent fund of the House of Representatives, upon vouchers signed by the chairman of the Joint Committee on the Investigation of the Pearl Harbor Attack.


*Agreed to July 9, 1946.*

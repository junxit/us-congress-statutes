# House Concurrent Resolution 155

> reorganization plan no. 1 of 1946

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Congress does not favor the Reorganization Plan Numbered 1 of May 16, 1946, transmitted to Congress by the President on the 16th of May 1946.


*Passed July 15, 1946.*

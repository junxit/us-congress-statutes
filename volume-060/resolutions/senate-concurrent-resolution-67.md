# Senate Concurrent Resolution 67

> pearl harbor attack

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the time for filing the report of the Joint Committee to Investigate the Pearl Harbor Attack be, and it is hereby, extended to July 10, 1946, and that the powers and functions of the said committee be, and the same are hereby, also extended to said date.


*Agreed to May 31, 1946.*

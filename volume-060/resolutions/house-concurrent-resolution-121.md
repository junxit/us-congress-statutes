# House Concurrent Resolution 121

> report on social security

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That in accordance with paragraph 3 of section 2 of the Printing Act, approved March 1, 1907, the House Committee on Ways and Means be, and is hereby, authorized and empowered to have printed for its use four thousand additional copies of the report to the committee of its technical staff relative to the issues in social security.


*Passed January 25, 1946.*

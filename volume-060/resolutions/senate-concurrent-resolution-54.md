# Senate Concurrent Resolution 54

> pearl harbor attack

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the limit of time required, under the provisions of S. Con. Res. 27, as extended by S. Con. Res. 45, as amended, for the submission to the Senate and House of Representatives of the report of the Joint Committee on the Investigation of the Pearl Harbor Attack, be, and the same is hereby, further extended to June 1, 1946; and said S. Con. Res. 27 is hereby continued in full force and effect until such date.


*Agreed to February 15, 1946.*

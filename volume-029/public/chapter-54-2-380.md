# Chapter 380

> An Act To provide for light-houses and other aids to navigation.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That light-houses and other aids to navigation be established and erected as hereinafter set forth, to wit:

Completing the removal of Cape San Blas light station, Florida, to Blacks Island.

Building a light keeper’s dwelling at Egmont Key light station, Florida.

Reconstructing the front beacon of Apalachicola Bay range-light station, Florida.

Establishing a light station at or near Saint Joseph Point, in Saint Joseph Bay, west coast of Florida.

Establishing range lights to mark the channel over the bar, entrance to Choctawhatchee Bay.

Repairing wharf and buildings of the depot at Key West, Florida.


*Approved, March 3, 1897.*

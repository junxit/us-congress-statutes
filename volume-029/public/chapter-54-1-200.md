# Chapter 200

> An Act To establish the port of Conneaut, in the State of Ohio, as a subport of entry in the district of Cuyahoga, in said State of Ohio.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the port of Conneaut, in the State of Ohio, be, and the same is hereby, declared to be a subport of entry in the district of Cuyahoga, in said State of Ohio, from and after the passage of this Act.


*Approved, May 19, 1896.*

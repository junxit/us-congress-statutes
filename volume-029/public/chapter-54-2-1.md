# Chapter 1

> An Act Making an appropriation for the expanses of a commission to investigate and report on the true divisional line between the Republic of Venezuela and British Guiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of one hundred thousand dollars, or so much thereof as may be necessary, be, and the same is hereby appropriated, for the expenses of a commission to be appointed by the President to investigate and report upon the true divisional line between the Republic of Venezuela and British Guiana.


*Approved, December 21, 1895.*

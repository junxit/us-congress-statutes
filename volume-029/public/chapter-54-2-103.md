# Chapter 103

> An Act Authorizing the construction of a fog signal on the north pier of the entrance to Menominee Harbor, Michigan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

There is hereby authorized to be constructed, at the north pier of the entrance to Menominee Harbor, Michigan, a fog signal, to cost not exceeding five thousand dollars.


*Approved, April 15, 1896.*

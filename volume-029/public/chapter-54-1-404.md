# Chapter 404

> An Act To make the city of Santa Barbara, county of Santa Barbara, State of California, a subport of entry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate awl House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the city of Santa Barbara, State of California, be, and the same is hereby, made a subport of entry in the district of Los Angeles, with the privileges of section seven of an Act entitled “An Act to amend the statutes in relation to immediate transportation of dutiable goods, and for other purposes,” approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty.


*Approved, June 10, 1896*

# Chapter 375

> An Act Declaring the Potomac Flats a public park, under the name of the Potomac Park.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the entire area formerly known as the Potomac Flats and now being reclaimed, together with the tidal reservoirs, be, and the same are hereby, made and declared a public park, under the name of the Potomac Park, and to be forever held and used as a park for the recreation and pleasure of the people.


*Approved, March 3, 1897.*

# Chapter 91

> An Act For the establishment of a light-house and fog-signal station at or near the entrance to Maurice River, New Jersey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That range lights and keeper’s quarters be established at or near the entrance to Maurice River, Delaware Bay, New Jersey: Provided, That the same shall not cost more than tour thousand five hundred dollars.


*Approved, April 6, 1896.*

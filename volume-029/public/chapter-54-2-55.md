# Chapter 55

> An Act Granting to the First Regiment North Carolina State Guard two condemned cannon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

that the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to deliver to the First-Regiment North Carolina State Guard two pieces of condemned cannon: Provided, That the same can be spared without detriment to the service, and that no expense is thereby incurred by the Government.


*Approved, March 13, 1896.*

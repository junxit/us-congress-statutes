# Chapter 52

> An Act To repeal section five hundred and fifty-three of the Revised Statutes of the United States, requiring the district judge for the southern district of Florida to reside at Key West.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section five hundred and fifty-three of the Revised Statutes, requiring the United States district judge for the southern district of Florida to reside at Key West, be, and the same is hereby, repealed.


*Approved, March 13, 1896.*

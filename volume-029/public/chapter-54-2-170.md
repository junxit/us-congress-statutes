# Chapter 170

> An Act To amend an Act entitled “An Act granting to the Eastern Nebraska and Gulf Railway Company right of way through the Omaha and Winnebago Indian reservations, in the State of Nebraska,” by extending the time for the Construction of said railway.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time prescribed by an Act of Congress approved the twenty-seventh day of June, eighteen hundred and ninety-four, entitled “An Act granting to the Eastern Nebraska and Gulf Railway Company right of way through the Omaha and Winnebago Indian reservations, in the State of Nebraska,” for the construction of said railway, be, and the same is hereby, extended for a period of three years from the twenty-seventh day of June, eighteen hundred and ninety seven.

## § 2.

That all other provisions of said Act are hereby continued in full force and effect.


*Approved, February 6, 1897.*

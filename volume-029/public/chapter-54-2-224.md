# Chapter 224

> An Act To provide an American register for the bark E. C. Mowatt, of Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of Navigation is hereby authorized and directed to cause the foreign-built bark E. C. Mowatt, owned by citizens of the United States, to be registered as a vessel of the United States.


*Approved, February 13, 1897.*

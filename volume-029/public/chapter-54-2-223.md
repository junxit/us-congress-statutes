# Chapter 223

> An Act To authorize the establishment of a life-saving station at or near Great Boars Head, on the coast of New Hampshire.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to establish a life-saving station at or near Great Boars Head, on the coast of New Hampshire.


*Approved, February 13, 1897.*

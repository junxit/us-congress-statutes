# Chapter 9

> An Act To amend chapter one hundred and eleven of the Acts of the third session of the Fifty-third Congress.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That chapter one hundred and eleven of the Acts of the third session, Fifty-third Congress, is hereby amended by striking out the first proviso therein and inserting the following: “Provided, That the site shall leave the building unexposed to danger from fire in adjacent buildings by an open space of not less than forty feet, including streets and alleys.”


*Approved, January 9, 1897.*

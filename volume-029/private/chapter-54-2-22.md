# Chapter 22

> An Act To increase the pension of Mary C. Thompson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension of Mary C. Thompson, widow of Doctor Fillmore Thompson, deceased, be increased from the sum of twelve dollars per mouth to twenty dollars per month; and the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place her on the roll at the increased rate of twenty dollars per month, in lieu of the rate of twelve dollars.


*Approved, January 13, 1897.*

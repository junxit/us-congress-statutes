# Chapter 40

> An Act Granting a pension to Sarah Ann Wible.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Sarah Ann Wible, dependent mother of John Wible, deceased, late of Company B, First Indiana Heavy Artillery, at twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, January 16, 1897.*

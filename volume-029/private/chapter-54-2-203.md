# Chapter 203

> An Act Granting a pension to Samuel McKinsey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Samuel McKinsey, of Columbus, Kansas, late private in Company B, Seventeenth Regiment of Indiana Volunteer Infantry, at the rate of seventy-two dollars per month, said pension to be in lieu of pension now being received by said McKinsey under certificate numbered one hundred and nineteen thousand one hundred and ninety-six.


*Received by the President, January 27, 1897.*

# Chapter 379

> An Act Granting a pension to Jerusha H. Brown.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, at the rate of twelve dollars per month, Jerusha H. Brown, of Saint Paul, Minnesota, daughter of Samuel Hayward, a soldier in the American Revolution.


*Approved, June 8, 1896.*

# Chapter 308

> An Act Granting a pension to Ellen C. Ewing, widow of Brevet Major-General Thomas Ewing.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ellen C. Ewing, widow of Brevet Major-General Thomas Ewing, at the rate of seventy-five dollars per month.


*Received by the President, May 21, 1896.*

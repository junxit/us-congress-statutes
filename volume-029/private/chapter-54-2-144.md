# Chapter 144

> An Act Granting a pension to Alice Gard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Alice Garel, of Greenfield, Hancock County, Indiana, at the rate of twenty dollars per month, as the dependent and permanently blind sister of Mahlon B. Gard, late private of Company K, One hundred and twenty-third Regiment Indiana Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, February 3, 1897.*

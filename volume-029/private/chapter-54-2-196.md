# Chapter 196

> An Act Granting a pension to John W. Pogue.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of John W. Pogue, late a private in Company E, Tenth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month.


*Received by the President, January 27, 1897.*

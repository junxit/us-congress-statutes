# Chapter 15

> An Act Granting a pension to Mrs. Eva Davis Cogswell, widow of Brigadier-General William Cogswell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the general pension laws, the name of Mrs. Eva Davis Cogswell, widow of Brigadier-General William Cogswell, at the rate of seventy-live dollars per month.


*Received by the President, January 28, 1896.*

# Chapter 329

> An Act To increase the pension of Elizabeth Wellons.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Elizabeth Wellons, widow of William S. Wellons, a soldier of the war of eighteen hundred and twelve, from twelve dollars per month to twenty dollars per mouth.


*Approved, February 25, 1897.*

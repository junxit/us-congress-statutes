# Chapter 84

> An Act Granting a pension to Thomas Pollock.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Thomas Pollock, late artisan, ordnance department, Benicia Arsenal, California, at such rate as the degree of his alleged disability may entitle him to.


*Approved, January 21, 1897.*

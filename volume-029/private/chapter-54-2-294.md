# Chapter 294

> An Act To grant pension to Jane Cunningham, widow of James Cunningham.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Jane Cunningham, widow of James Cunningham, late of Company B, Seventh Wisconsin Infantry, and to pay her a pension of twelve dollars per month from and after the passage of this Act.


*Received by the President, February 10, 1897.*

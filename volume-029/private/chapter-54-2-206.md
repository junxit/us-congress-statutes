# Chapter 206

> An Act To increase the pension of Ambrose D. Manion.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Ambrose D. Manion, late of Company K, Fifth Kentucky Infantry, from thirty dollars per month to fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 9, 1897.*

# Chapter 131

> An Act For the relief of Pricilla R. Burns.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Pricilla R. Burns, widow of the late Brigadier-General William W. Burns, of the United States Army, on the pension roll, at the rate of fifty dollars per month, in lieu of the amount she now receives.


*Approved, February 2, 1897.*

# Chapter 68

> An Act For the relief of Halvor K. Omlie, of Homen, North Dakota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Halvor K. Omlie, who, on May twelfth, eighteen hundred and ninety-three, made final proof on his preemption claim for the northwest quarter of section eight, township one hundred and sixty-three north, of range fifty-eight west, of the fifth principal meridian in the State of North Dakota, be, and he is hereby, granted a period of one year from the passage of this Act within which to make the necessary payment for said lands.


*Approved, March 23, 1896.*

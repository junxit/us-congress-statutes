# Chapter 184

> An Act Granting a pension to Archibald Hunley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Bouse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Archibald Hunley, late a member of Company H, Thirtieth Regiment Illinois Volunteers, at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Received by the President, January 27, 1897.*

# Chapter 43

> An Act For the relief of M. R. William Grebe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to revoke the order by which M. R. William Grebe was dismissed from the military service of the United States as a captain of the Fourth Missouri Volunteer Cavalry, and to honorably discharge said Grebe, to date from the twelfth day of December, eighteen hundred and sixty-four: Provided, That nothing herein shall entitle said M.R. William Grebe to any back pay or allowances to which he might have been entitled but for the action of such court-martial.


*Received by the President, January 5, 1897.*

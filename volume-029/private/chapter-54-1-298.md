# Chapter 298

> An Act To give increased pension to General James C. Parrott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of James C. Parrott, late colonel of the Seventh Iowa Infantry, and pay him pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, May 30, 1896.*

# Chapter 201

> An Act To restore the name of Ethan A. Sellman to the pension roll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Ethan A. Sellman, late a private in Company E, Fiftieth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and to pay him a pension of thirty dollars per month, without any deduction or retention on account of pension heretofore paid to him.


*Received by the President, January 27, 1897.*

# Chapter 277

> An Act Granting a pension to Samuel Goldwater.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, at the rate of twelve dollars per month, the name of Samuel Goldwater, late of Company A, First Regiment Missouri Volunteer Infantry, National Guard.


*Approved, May 30, 1896.*

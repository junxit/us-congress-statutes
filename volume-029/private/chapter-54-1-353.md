# Chapter 353

> An Act Granting a pension to Mrs. Eleanor Carroll Poe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, at the rate of fifty dollars per month, the name of Eleanor Carroll Poe, widow of the late Colonel Orlando M. Poe, of the United States Army.


*Approved, June 6, 1896.*

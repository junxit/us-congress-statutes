# Chapter 101

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Sarah E. Comly, widow of Major Clifton Comly.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah E. Comly, widow of Major Clifton Comly, and pay her a pension of thirty-five dollars per month, the same to be in lieu of the pension now drawn by her.


*Approved, January 27, 1897.*

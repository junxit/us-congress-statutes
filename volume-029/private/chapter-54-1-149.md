# Chapter 149

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Lewis C. Schilling.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lewis C. Schilling, late a private in Company A, Fourth Ohio Cavalry Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of the amount he is now receiving.


*Approved, May 2, 1896.*

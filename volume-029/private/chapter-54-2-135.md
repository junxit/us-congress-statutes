# Chapter 135

> An Act To confirm the title to certain lands in William Morgan and Harrison Moore, and to require the issue of patents therefor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the entry on March twenty-fourth, eighteen hundred and eighty-two, by William Morgan, of lot numbered twenty-one, of the southwest quarter of section numbered eighteen, in township numbered sixteen, of range numbered eight east, in the State of Kansas, under the Act of March sixteenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, for which full payment has been made and final certificates issued, be, and hereby is, confirmed, and the patent therefor shall be duly issued.

## § 2.

That the entries by Harrison Moore on December thirty-first, eighteen hundred and eighty, under said Act of March sixteenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, of lot numbered fifteen, of the northwest quarter of section numbered nineteen, and lot numbered twenty-two, of the southwest quarter of section numbered eighteen, both in said township numbered sixteen, of range numbered eight east, in said State, and on January fifth, eighteen hundred and eighty-one, under the Act of July fifth, eighteen hundred and seventy-six, of lots numbered thirteen and fourteen of section numbered nineteen, in said township and range, for each of which tracts full payment has been made and final certificates issued, be, and hereby are, confirmed, and the patents therefor shall be issued.


*Received by the President, January 21, 1897.*

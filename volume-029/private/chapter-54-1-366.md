# Chapter 366

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Mrs. Julia A. Jameson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Julia A. Jameson, widow of Brigadier-General Charles D. Jameson, United States Volunteers, at the rate of fifty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension of thirty dollars per month she is now receiving.


*Approved, June 6, 1896.*

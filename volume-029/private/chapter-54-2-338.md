# Chapter 338

> An Act For the relief of Basil Moreland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Basil Moreland the sum of two thousand two hundred and twelve dollars, in full for all claim he may have against the United States for his land and improvements in Blue Earth County, Minnesota, taken by the United States for the Winnebago Indians.


*Approved, February 26, 1897.*

# Chapter 380

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Abraham H. Parker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Abraham H. Parker, late private of Company A, Forty-fifth Regiment Kentucky Mounted Infantry, on the pension roll of the United States at the rate of fifty dollars per month in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, June 8, 1896.*

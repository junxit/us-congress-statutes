# Chapter 128

> An Act For the relief of George T. Stevens, assistant surgeon Seventy-seventh New York Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to revoke the order of November eleventh, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, dismissing George T. Stevens, assistant surgeon Seventy-seventh New York Volunteers.


*Approved, February 1, 1897.*

# Chapter 24

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Orleina J. Clark, of Louisville, Kentucky.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Orleina J. Clark, widow of the late George W. Clark, a soldier in the war with Mexico, from eight dollars per month to twenty dollars per month, to take effect from the passage of this Act.


*Approved, January 13, 1897.*

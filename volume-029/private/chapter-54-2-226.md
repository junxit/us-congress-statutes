# Chapter 226

> An Act Granting a pension to Nancy T. Duncklee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Nancy T. Duncklee, dependent stepmother of Lucian A. Duncklee, late of Company C, Sixteenth Regiment New Hampshire Volunteer Infantry, at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 13, 1897.*

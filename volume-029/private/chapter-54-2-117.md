# Chapter 117

> An Act Granting a pension to Mrs. Jane Stewart Whiting.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, at the rate of fifty dollars per month, the name of Jane Stewart Whiting, widow of the late Commodore William Danforth Whiting, of the United States Navy.


*Approved, January 30, 1897.*

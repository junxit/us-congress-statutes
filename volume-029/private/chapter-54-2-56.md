# Chapter 56

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Isaac H. Whetsel, of Louisville, Kentucky.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Isaac H. Whetsei, late private in Company B, Second Regiment United States Cavalry, to sixteen dollars per month, for gunshot wound of right thigh and callouses of buttocks, in lieu of the pension which he now receives.


*Received by the President, January 5, 1897.*

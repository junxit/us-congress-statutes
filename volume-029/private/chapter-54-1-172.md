# Chapter 172

> An Act Granting an honorable discharge to F. L. Taylor from December second, eighteen hundred and sixty-four.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to correct the military record of Captain F. L. Taylor, late of Thirty-fourth New Jersey Volunteers, and to issue to said officer, a certificate of honorable discharge, as of December second, eighteen hundred and sixty-four.


*Approved, May 11, 1896.*

# Chapter 291

> An Act To reinstate William Waldrup on pension roll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be instructed to reinstate William Waldrup, of Maddox, Tennessee, late of Company F, Sixth Regiment Tennessee Cavalry, on the pension roll of the United States at the rate he was drawing when dropped from the roll.


*Received by the President, February 10, 1897.*

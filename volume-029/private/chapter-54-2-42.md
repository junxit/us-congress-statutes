# Chapter 42

> An Act Granting a pension to Margaret J. Young.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Margaret J. Young, dependent foster mother of John II. Jones, late a private in Company F, Twenty-fifth Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, January 16, 1897.*

# Chapter 160

> An Act For the relief of A. T. Hensley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be, and is hereby, appropriated, out of any moneys in the Treasury of the United States not otherwise appropriated, the sum of four hundred and thirty-two dollars, to be paid by the Secretary of the Treasury to A. T. Hensley, late of Lavaca, Texas, in full compensation for dressed flooring and rough boards furnished to the United States in August, eighteen hundred and sixty-five.


*Approved, May 6, 1896.*

# Chapter 267

> An Act Granting a pension to James Loyd Young, late of Company A, Sixth Regiment Kentucky Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to grant a pension to James Loyd Young, late of Company A, Sixth Regiment Kentucky Volunteers, for injury to right hip, in addition to the pension by him now received, so that he shall receive seventeen dollars per month in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, May 28, 1896.*

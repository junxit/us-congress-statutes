# Chapter 100

> An Act Granting a pension to George M. Brooks.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of George M. Brooks, late private in D Company of the Sixty-ninth Regiment of Indiana Infantry Volunteers, and pay to him a pension of fifteen dollars per month.


*Approved, January 27, 1897.*

# Chapter 412

> An Act Granting a pension to Eliza Craig Heckman, widow of Brigadier-General Charles A. Heckman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Eliza Craig Heckman, widow of Brigadier-General Charles A. Heckman, on the pension roll at the rate of fifty dollars per month.


*Received by the President, February 19, 1897.*

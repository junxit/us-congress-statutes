# Chapter 200

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Patsey E. Broaddus, of Marion, Kansas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase and pay a pension of thirty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension now received by her, to Patsey E. Broaddus, of Marion, State of Kansas, widow of Green B. F. Broaddus, late first lieutenant of Company H, First Kentucky Cavalry, Mexican war, and major Eighth Kentucky Volunteer Infantry in the late war.


*Received by the President, January 27, 1897.*

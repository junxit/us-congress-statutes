# Chapter 345

> An Act For the relief of Mrs. Elizabeth M. Williams, of Monroe County, Tennessee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll .at the rate of eight dollars per month the name of Mrs. Elizabeth M. Williams, widow of William Williams, late a private in Captain Ware’s Company, Tennessee Volunteers, Florida war of eighteen hundred and thirty-six.


*Approved, June 6, 1896.*

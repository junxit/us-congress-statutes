# Chapter 388

> An Act To increase the pension of Mrs. Emily E. Cash.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, directed to increase the pension of Mrs. Emily E. Cash, widow of private Thomas W. Cash, a soldier in the Mexican war, to twenty dollars a month.


*Approved, June 9, 1896.*

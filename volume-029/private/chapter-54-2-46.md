# Chapter 46

> An Act To increase the pension of Byron Cotton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is directed and empowered to place upon the pension roll the name of Byron Cotton, late of Company A, Twenty-fourth Iowa Infantry Volunteers, at the rate of fifty dollars a month in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Received by the President, January 5, 1897.*

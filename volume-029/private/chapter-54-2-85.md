# Chapter 85

> An Act Granting a pension to Anna P. Johnson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll of the United States the name of Anna P. Johnson, widow of Paul Johnson, late a private in Company I, Sixteenth Iowa Volunteers, at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, January 21, 1897.*

# Chapter 59

> An Act To increase the pension of George V. Barnard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension now allowed to George V. Barnard, late a private of Company E, Ninety-fifth Regiment Illinois Infantry Volunteers, so that he shall be allowed and paid fifty dollars per month.


*Received by the President, January 5, 1897.*

# Chapter 144

> An Act To grant an increase of pension to Mary B. Houk.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to pay Mary B. Houk, widow of Colonel Leonidas C. Houk, late colonel of the Third Regiment of Tennessee Volunteer Infantry, a pension of thirty dollars per month in lieu of the pension she is now receiving.


*Received by the President, April 14, 1896.*

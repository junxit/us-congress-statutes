# Chapter 134

> An Act Granting a pension to Frederick Lierman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Frederick Lierman, late private Company B, Johnson County Home Guards, Enrolled Missouri Militia, at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 2, 1897.*

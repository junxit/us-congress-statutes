# Private Resolution 21

> Joint Resolution To authorize Lieutenant Sidney S. Jordan. Fifth United States Artillery, and Captain Edward C. Carter, assistant surgeon, United States Army, to accept medals from the British Government.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Lieutenant Sidney S. Jordan, Fifth United States Artillery, and Captain Edward C. Carter, assistant surgeon, United States Army, be, and they are hereby, authorized to accept from the Government of Great Britain the medals awarded to them, respectively, for their humane services to the shipwrecked crew of the British ship Strathblane.


*Approved, March 7, 1896.*

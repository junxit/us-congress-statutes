# Chapter 199

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Gabriel Widmer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to grant to Gabriel Widmer, late of Company K, Twenty-third Michigan Infantry, an increase of pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Received by the President, January 27, 1897.*

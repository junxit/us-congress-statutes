# Chapter 419

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Charles W. Sentman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Charles W. Sentman, late of Company D, Twentieth Regiment of Indiana Volunteers, and pay him a pension of twenty dollars per month in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Received by the President, February 19, 1897.*

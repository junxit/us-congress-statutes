# Chapter 295

> An Act To increase the pension of Josephine Glover.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Bouse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, directed to increase the pension of Josephine Glover, widow of Captain E. V. Glover, Company A, Thirty-fourth New Jersey Volunteers, and that she be paid a pension of thirty dollars per month.


*Received by the President, February 10, 1897.*

# Chapter 417

> An Act Granting pension to Francis M. Ross.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Francis M. Ross, late a private in Company B, Forty-second Indiana Infantry, and pay him a pension of twelve dollars a month.


*Received by the President, February 19, 1897.*

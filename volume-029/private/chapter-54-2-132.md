# Chapter 132

> An Act Granting a pension to Samuel Holliday.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Samuel Holliday, late sergeant of Company I, Thirty-ninth Iowa Infantry, and pay him a pension of twenty-four dollars per month.


*Approved, February 2, 1897.*

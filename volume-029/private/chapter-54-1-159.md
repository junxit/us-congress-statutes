# Chapter 159

> An Act For the relief of E. R. Shipley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of four hundred and sixty dollars be, and the same hereby is, appropriated, out of any moneys in the Treasury not otherwise, appropriated, for the payment in full to E. R. Shipley for moneys paid, by direction of Post-Office Inspector Edgerton, to parties having money in registered packages stolen from the post-office in Springfield, Missouri, on the twenty-third day of June, eighteen hundred and eighty four.


*Approved, May 4, 1896.*

# Chapter 357

> An Act To increase the pension of Ann Catherine Hull.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, directed to increase the pension of Ann Catherine Hull, widow of Commodore Joseph B. Hull, United States Navy, and that she be paid a pension of fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, June 6, 1896.*

# Chapter 326

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to James H. Jones.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase to the sum of twenty dollars per month the pension of James H. Jones, of Cusseta, Georgia, late of the Georgia Volunteers, Indian war.


*Approved, June 3, 1896.*

# Chapter 133

> An Act Granting a pension to Jacob Brown.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Jacob Brown, late a private of Company A, Fifteenth Iowa Infantry, and pay him a pension of thirty dollars a month.


*Approved, February 2, 1897.*

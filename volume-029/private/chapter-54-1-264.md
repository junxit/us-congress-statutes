# Chapter 264

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to William Brown.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William Brown, late of Company D, Eighth Regiment Michigan Cavalry, at the rate of twenty dollars a month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving, from and after the passage of this Act.


*Approved, May 28, 1896.*

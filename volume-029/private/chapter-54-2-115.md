# Chapter 115

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Celeste A. Bougbton, widow of Brevet Brigadier-General Horace Bougbton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Celeste A. Boughton, widow of Brevet Brigadier-General Horace Boughton, late of the United States Army, retired, and to pay her a pension of fifty dollars per month in lieu of the pension she is now receiving.


*Approved, January 30, 1897.*

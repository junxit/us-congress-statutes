# Chapter 410

> An Act To increase the pension of Joseph M. Donohue.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of Pensions be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Joseph M. Donohue, of Shelby County, Kentucky, late a private in Company A, Fifteenth Kentucky Regiment, on the pension roll as a pensioner at the rate of twelve dollars per month instead of six dollars per month, which he now receives.


*Approved, March 3, 1897.*

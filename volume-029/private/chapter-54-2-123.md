# Chapter 123

> An Act For the relief of Reverend Edwin Warriner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Edwin Warriner on the rolls of the First Battalion Connecticut Cavalry Volunteers as chaplain, and to recognize him as of that grade and organization in the service of the United States from the first day of December, eighteen hundred and sixty-one, the date of his commission, to the twelfth day of March, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, the date of the acceptance of his resignation by the governor of the State.


*Approved, February 1, 1897.*

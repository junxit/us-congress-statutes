# Chapter 219

> An Act To correct the military record of Homer C. McCuskey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the charge of desertion now standing against Homer C. McCuskey, second lieutenant of the Seventh Regiment of United States Colored Troops, on the records of the War Department, be, and the same is, removed, and the Secretary of War is hereby directed to issue to said Homer C. McCuskey an honorable discharge, the same to date from October first, eighteen hundred and sixty-five: Provided, That the said Homer C. McCuskey shall receive no bounty, pay, or emoluments of any kind subsequent to said last-named date.


*Received by the President, February 1, 1897.*

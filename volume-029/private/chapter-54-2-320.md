# Chapter 320

> An Act To increase the pension of Major-General Julius H. Stahel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and, House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Julius H. Stahel, late major-general of volunteers in the Union Army, and pay same at the rate of seventy-five dollars per month, in lieu of what he now receives, from and after the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 25, 1897.*

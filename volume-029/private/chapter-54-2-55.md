# Chapter 55

> An Act To pension William H. Nesbitt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension rolls the name of William H. Nesbitt, late a private in Company F, Second Illinois Cavalry, at the rate of thirty dollars per month, in lieu of pension heretofore granted.


*Received by the President, January 5, 1897.*

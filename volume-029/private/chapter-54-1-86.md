# Chapter 86

> An Act For the relief of Michael Ryan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to revoke the order dishonorably discharging Private Michael Ryan, Company I, Fifth Regiment United States Infantry, on September twentieth, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, and to issue to him an honorable discharge from the military service of the United States as of that date.


*Received by the President, March 19, 1896.*

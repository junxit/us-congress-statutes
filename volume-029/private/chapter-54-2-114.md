# Chapter 114

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to George C. Abbey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of George C. Abbey, late a private of Company M of the First Connecticut Volunteer Heavy Artillery, and to pay him twenty dollars per month from and after the passage of this Act.


*Approved, January 30, 1897.*

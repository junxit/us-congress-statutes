# Chapter 271

> An Act Granting a pension to Miriam V. Kenney, widow of Samuel W. Kenney.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That a pension of twelve dollars per month be, and the same is hereby, granted to Mrs. Miriam V. Kenney, widow of Samuel W. Kenney, during the term of her natural life.


*Approved, February 20, 1897.*

# Chapter 32

> An Act Granting a pension to Emeline Filgate.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Emeline Filgate, widow of James Filgate, late of Company G, Sixth United States Infantry, at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, January 16, 1897.*

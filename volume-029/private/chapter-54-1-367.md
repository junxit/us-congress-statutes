# Chapter 367

> An Act Granting a pension to Elizabeth Marshall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elizabeth Marshall, of Bridgman, Michigan, widow of Henry L. Marshall, a private in Company A, One hundred and eighty-fourth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers, and pay her a pension of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, June 6, 1896.*

# Chapter 104

> An Act Granting a pension to Susan E. Cunningham.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Susan E. Cunningham, totally and permanently blind, daughter of Thomas Cunningham, late private, Company F, Fourth Regiment New York Heavy Artillery, at the rate of twelve dollars a month.


*Approved, January 27, 1897.*

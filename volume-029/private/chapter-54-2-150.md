# Chapter 150

> An Act To increase the pension of Melita E. White.

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject otherwise to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Melita E. White, widow of Carr B. White, late colonel of the Twelfth Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension of forty-two dollars per month.


*Approved, February 4, 1897.*

# Chapter 20

> An Act Granting a pension to Celestia R. Barry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Celestia R. Barry, widow of Claudius C. Barry, deceased, late a private in Captain Connor’s Company, Bell’s Regiment Mexican war volunteers, and to allow the said Celestia R. Barry a pension at the rate of eight dollars a month.


*Approved, January 13, 1897.*

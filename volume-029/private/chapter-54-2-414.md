# Chapter 414

> An Act Granting a pension to Jane H. Vandever.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jane H. Vandever, widow of General William Vandever, late of Ninth Regiment Iowa Infantry, and late brigadier-general United States Volunteers, at the rate of fifty dollars a month.


*Received by the President, February 19, 1897.*

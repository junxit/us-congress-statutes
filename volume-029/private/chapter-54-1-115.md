# Chapter 115

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to A. H. McLaws.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of A. H. McLaws, of Dublin, Georgia, late second lieutenant in Captain Dill’s Company (B), First Georgia Regiment, Mexican war to twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, April 18, 1896.*

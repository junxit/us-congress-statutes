# Chapter 322

> An Act To increase the pension of Julia H. H. Crosby.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Julia H. H. Crosby, widow of Freeman H. Crosby, deceased, late a lieutenant in the United States Navy, commanding the United States steamship McArthur, at the rate of thirty-five dollars per month, in lieu of the pension which she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 25, 1897.*

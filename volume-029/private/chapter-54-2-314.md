# Chapter 314

> An Act To remove the political disabilities of Colonel William E. Simms.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all political disabilities imposed by the third section of the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States be, and the same are hereby, removed from Colonel William E. Simms.


*Approved, February 24, 1897.*

# Chapter 65

> An Act To increase the pension of Caroline S. Baker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, at thirty dollars per month, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Caroline S. Baker, widow of John Howard Baker, late assistant engineer, United States Navy, in lieu of the pension she is now receiving.


*Received by the President, January 7, 1897.*

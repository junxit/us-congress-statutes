# Chapter 296

> An Act Granting a pension to Minnie Parker, widow of Colonel anti Brevet Brigadier-General Ely S. Parker, late of the United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Minnie Parker, widow of Colonel and Brevet Brigadier-General Ely S. Parker, late of the United States Army, and to pay her a pension of thirty dollars per month.


*Received by the President, February 10, 1897.*

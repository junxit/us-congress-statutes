# Chapter 131

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Thomas Corigan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and empowered to place on the invalid pension roll of the United States the name of Thomas Corigan, late a member of Company B of the Eighty-eighth Illinois Volunteer Infantry, at the rate of fifty dollars per month, from and after the passage of this Act.


*Approved, April 24, 1896.*

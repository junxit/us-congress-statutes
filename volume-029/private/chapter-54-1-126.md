# Chapter 126

> An Act To fix the date of the discharge of Thomas Johnson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized to fix the date of the discharge of Thomas Johnson, Company L, Seventh Kentucky Cavalry, at November twentieth, eighteen hundred and sixty-two.


*Approved, April 24, 1896.*

# Chapter 193

> An Act Granting an honorable discharge to Wilson Kale.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to correct the military record of, and grant an honorable discharge to, Wilson Kale, of Truro, Iowa, late a private in Company G, Sixth Regiment of California Infantry Volunteers: Provided, That no payor emoluments shall be clue said soldier by reason of the passage of this Act.


*Received by the President, January 27, 1897.*

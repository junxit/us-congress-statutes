# Chapter 16

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to James H. Osgood.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of James H. Osgood, late a seaman on the United States ship Circassian, and pay him a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month, in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Received by the President, January 28, 1896.*

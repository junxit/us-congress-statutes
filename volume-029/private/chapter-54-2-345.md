# Chapter 345

> An Act Granting a pension to Hiram Santas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place the name of Uiram Santas, late a private of Company G, Nineteenth Wisconsin Volunteer Infantry, upon the pension roll at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 27, 1897.*

# Chapter 23

> An Act To increase the pension of Theresa Peebles, of Jefferson County, Georgia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Theresa Peebles, of Jefferson County, Georgia, a widow of a soldier of the war of eighteen hundred and twelve, from twelve dollars to eighteen dollars per month.


*Approved, January 13, 1897.*

# Chapter 197

> An Act Granting a pension to Thomas J. Thorp.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Thomas J. Thorp, late a private Company K, Third Regiment Ohio Volunteer Cavalry, at the rate of fifty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Received by the President, January 27, 1897.*

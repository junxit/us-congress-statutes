# Chapter 50

> An Act Granting a pension to Catharine Darragh.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Catharine Darragh, widow of John Darragh, late a private in Company H, One hundred and tenth Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension of twelve dollars per month.


*Received by the President, January 5, 1897.*

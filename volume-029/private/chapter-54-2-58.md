# Chapter 58

> An Act For the relief of Henry A. F. Worth.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Henry A. F. Worth, late captain Company K, Sixth Regiment United States Infantry, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, and pay him a pension of seventy-two dollars per month.


*Received by the President, January 5, 1897.*

# Chapter 348

> An Act Granting a pension to Margaret Kirkpatrick.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll of the United States the name of Margaret Kirkpatrick, army nurse, at the rate of twelve dollars per month, subject to the limitations of the general pension laws.


*Approved, February 27, 1897.*

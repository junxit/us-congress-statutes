# Chapter 45

> An Act For the relief of Hiram P. Pauley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Hiram P. Pauley, late a private in the Morgan raid militia of Seventh Indiana Legion, and to pay to him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Received by the President, January 5, 1897.*

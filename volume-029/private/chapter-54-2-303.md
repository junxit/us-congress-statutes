# Chapter 303

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Lewis Keiser.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lewis Keiser, late a private of Company B, Ninety-third Regiment Illinois Infantry Volunteers, at the rate of seventy-two dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Received by the President, February 10, 1897.*

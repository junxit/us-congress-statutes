# Chapter 149

> An Act Granting increase of pension to Thomas D. Walker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Thomas D. Walker, late a private in Company B, Fourth Kentucky Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 4, 1897.*

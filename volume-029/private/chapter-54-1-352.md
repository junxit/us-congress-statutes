# Chapter 352

> An Act Granting a pension to Ada J. Schwatka, widow of the late Lieutenant Frederick Schwatka.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension roll the name of Ada J. Schwatka, widow of the late Frederick Schwatka, late first lieutenant, Third Cavalry, United States Army, at the rate of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, June 6, 1896.*

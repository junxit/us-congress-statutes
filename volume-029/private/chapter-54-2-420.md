# Chapter 420

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Jesse Durnell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jesse Durnell, late second-class pilot on the gunboat Lexington, and pay him a pension of twenty dollars per month.


*Received by the President, February 19, 1897.*

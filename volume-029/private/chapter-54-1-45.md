# Chapter 45

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Mrs. Jane Dulany.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Mrs. Jane Dulany, widow of Colonel William Dulany, from fifty dollars to seventy-two dollars per month.


*Approved, March 4, 1896.*

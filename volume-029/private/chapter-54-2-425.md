# Chapter 425

> An Act For the relief of Harrison Wagner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Harrison Wagner, and pay him a pension of twenty-five dollars per month.


*Received by the President, February 19, 1897.*

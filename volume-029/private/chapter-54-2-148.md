# Chapter 148

> An Act For the relief of John Duncan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to issue an honorable discharge as of date April thirtieth, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, to John Duncan, late of Company D, Twenty-fifth Missouri Infantry, and to correct his military record accordingly as it appears in the archives of the War Department.


*Approved, February 4, 1897.*

# Chapter 410

> An Act Increasing the pension of Daniel D. Jennings, late of Company C, Sixty-fifth Ohio Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and, House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Daniel D. Jennings, a private of Company C, Sixty-fifth Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension of thirty dollars a month in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, June 10, 1896.*

# Chapter 265

> An Act Granting a pension to Elizabeth New, widow of Jethrow New.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject, to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elizabeth New, widow of Jethrow New, late captain of Company D, Twenty-ninth Indiana Volunteer Infantry, of the war of eighteen hundred and sixty-one.


*Approved, May 28, 1896.*

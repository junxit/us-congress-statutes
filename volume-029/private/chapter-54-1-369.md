# Chapter 369

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Michael S. Pettit.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Michael S. Pettit, First Mounted Rifles, regular service, who performed conspicuous service in the war with Mexico, to twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, June 6, 1896.*

# Chapter 64

> An Act For the relief of Patrick Rainey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to remove the charge of desertion from the military record of Patrick Rainey, late of Company K, Fifty-second Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and issue to said Rainey an honorable discharge as of the date of January twenty-third, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, on account of minority.


*Approved, January 18, 1897.*

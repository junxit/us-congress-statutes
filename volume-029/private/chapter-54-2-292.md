# Chapter 292

> An Act Granting a pension to Mellisa Adams, widow of Silas Adams.

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Mellisa Adams, of Liberty, Casey County, Kentucky, widow of Silas Adams, deceased, late colonel of the First Kentucky Cavalry and pay her a pension of thirty dollars per month.


*Received by the President, February 10, 1897.*

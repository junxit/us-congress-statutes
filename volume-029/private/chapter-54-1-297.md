# Chapter 297

> An Act For the relief of Michael H. J. Crouch, late of Company M, Sixth Regiment Pennsylvania Cavalry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Michael H. J. Crouch, late of Company M, Sixth Regiment Pennsylvania Cavalry, on the pension roll at twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, May 30, 1896.*

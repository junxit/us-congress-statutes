# Chapter 38

> An Act Granting a pension to Augustus G. Cary.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Augustus G. Cary, late first lieutenant, United States Revenue-Marine, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, January 16, 1897.*

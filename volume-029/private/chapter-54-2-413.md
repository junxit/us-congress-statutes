# Chapter 413

> An Act Granting a pension to Mrs. Julia Jones Duncan, widow of Brevet Major-General Samuel A. Duncan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the general pension laws, the name of Mrs. Julia Jones Duncan, widow of Brevet Major-General Samuel A. Duncan, at the rate of fifty dollars per month.


*Received by the President, February 19, 1897.*

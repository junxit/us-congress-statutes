# Chapter 184

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Francis Walsh, of Stockham, Nebraska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Francis Walsh, late a member of Company B, Third Regiment, Wisconsin Infantry Volunteers, at the rate of seventy-two dollars per month, in lieu of the pension which he is now receiving.


*Approved, May 15, 1896.*

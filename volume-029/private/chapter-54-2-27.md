# Chapter 27

> An Act Granting a pension to Elvira Bachelder.

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, at the rate of twelve dollars a month, the name of Elvira Bachelder, dependent mother of J. K. P. Bachelder, late a private in Company D, First New Hampshire Heavy Artillery.


*Approved, January 14, 1897.*

# Chapter 109

> An Act Granting a pension to Catherine R. Jardine, widow of Brigadier-General Edward Jardine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Catherine R. Jardine, widow of Edward Jardine, late brigadier-general of the Army of the United States, and pay her at the rate of fifty dollars a month.


*Approved, April 18, 1896.*

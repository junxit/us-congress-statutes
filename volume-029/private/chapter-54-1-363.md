# Chapter 363

> An Act Granting a pension to Thomas Brewer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Thomas Brewer, late of Companies I and F, Thirty-first Regiment Enrolled Missouri Militia, at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, June 6, 1896.*

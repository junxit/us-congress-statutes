# Chapter 186

> An Act Granting increase of pension to Alexander C. Morrison.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Alexander C. Morrison, late a corporal in Company E, Fourth Iowa Veteran Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension of thirty dollars per month.


*Received by the President, January 27, 1897.*

# Chapter 82

> An Act Granting a pension to Samuel D. Gilman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Samuel D. Gilman, late of the Straffard Guards, New Hampshire Volunteers, and pay him a pension of fifty dollars per month.


*Received by the President, January 8, 1897.*

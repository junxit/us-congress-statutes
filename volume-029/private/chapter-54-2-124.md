# Chapter 124

> An Act For the relief of Rufus Betz.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the charge of desertion standing against the name of Rufus Betz, late a private of Company A, Nineteenth Regiment of Ohio Infantry Volunteers, be removed, and he be granted an honorable discharge, to date August ninth, eighteen hundred and sixty-two: Provided, That nothing in this Act shall be construed so as to allow the said Betz any pay, bounty, or other allowances to which he would not have been entitled but for the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 1, 1897.*

# Concurrent Resolution

> venezuela boundary.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That six thousand extra copies of Senate Document No. 226, First Session, 50th Congress, relating to the Venezuela Boundary, and the President’s message of December 17, 1895, and the documents accompanying the same, be printed in paper covers, of which number two thousand shall be for the use of the Senate and four thousand for the use of the House of Representatives.


*Passed the Senate December 20, 1895. Passed the House of Representatives January 6, 1896.*

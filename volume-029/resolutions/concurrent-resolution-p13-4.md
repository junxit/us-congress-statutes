# Concurrent Resolution

> messages of the presidents.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed fifteen thousand additional copies of the Messages and Papers of the Presidents, Richardson’s Compilation, of which there shall be five thousand copies for the Senate and ten thousand copies for the House of Representatives, to be distributed and disposed of, including the fraction in each case, as provided in the House joint resolution numbered one hundred and seventy, approved on the thirtieth day of April, eighteen hundred and ninety six.


*Passed the House of Representatives May 18, 1896. Passed the Senate May 22, 1896.*

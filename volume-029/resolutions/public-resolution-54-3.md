# Public Resolution 3

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the building of a telephone Iine in the District of Columbia.

*Resolved by Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That authority is hereby granted to William J. Browning, Chief Clerk of the House of Representatives, to build a telephone line from the House folding room to the new annex folding room in the McDowell Building, corner of North Capitol street and Massachusetts avenue, using the Government poles now on North Capitol street.


*Approved, January 11, 1897.*

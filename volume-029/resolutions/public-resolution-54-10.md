# Public Resolution 10

> Joint Resolution Providing for the printing of the Consular Regulations of eighteen hundred and ninety-six.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be printed of the Consular Regulations of eighteen hundred and ninety-six, twenty-nine hundred copies, eight hundred copies for the use of the Senate, sixteen hundred copies for the use of the House of Representatives, and live hundred copies to be delivered to the Superintendent of Documents for sale.


*Approved, February 13, 1897.*

# Concurrent Resolution

> repeal of timber-culture laws.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate Senate bill numbered thirty three hundred and twenty eight, entitled “An Act to amend an Act entitled An Act to repeal the timber-culture laws, and for other purposes.’”


*Passed the Senate February 9, 1897. Passed the House of Representatives February 9, 1897.*

# Concurrent Resolution

> tariff laws.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Joint Committee on Printing be, and are hereby, authorized to cause to be printed, indexed, and bound in cloth, in one volume, all of the acts, as they appear in the United States Statutes, heretofore passed by Congress, imposing duties on imports, one thousand copies for the use of the Senate and two thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives.


*Passed the Senate January 13, 1896. Passed the House of Representatives January 16, 1896.*

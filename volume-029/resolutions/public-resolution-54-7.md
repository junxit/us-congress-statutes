# Public Resolution 7

> Joint Resolution To authorize the Secretary of the Interior to use Fort Bidwell for an Indian training school.

*Resolved by Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Fort Bidwell, an abandoned military reservation, in Modoc County, California, together with all the lands, building's, water system, and improvements thereon, having been turned over to the Department of the Interior, the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and empowered to use the same for the purposes of an Indian training school.


*Approved, January 30, 1897.*

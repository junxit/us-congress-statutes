# Concurrent Resolution

> coast and geodetic survey.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate the enrolled Joint Resolution (S. R. 116) “Authorizing the Public Printer to print the Annual Report of the Superintendent of the United States Coast and Geodetic Survey m quarto form and to bind it in one volume,” for the purpose of correcting errors in the text.


*Passed the Senate April 10, 1896. Passed the House of Representatives April 11, 1896.*

## Marginal notes

# Concurrent Resolution

> mercur mining district.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed at the Government Printing Office, at the earliest day practicable, five thousand additional copies in separate form, with paper cover, of the paper entitled “Economic Geology of the Mercur Mining District,” being a part of the Sixteenth Annual Report of the United States Geological Survey, with accompanying illustrations, of which three thousand copies shall be for the use of the House, fifteen hundred copies for the use of the Senate, and five hundred copies for distribution by the Geological Survey.


*Passed the Senate April 2, 1896. Passed the House of Representatives April 15, 1896.*

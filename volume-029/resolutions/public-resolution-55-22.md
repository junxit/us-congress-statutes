# Public Resolution 22

> Joint Resolution Directing the Secretary of War to submit estimates for work upon Newtown Creek, New York.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to submit estimates for work upon Newtown Creek, New York, with a view to secure a depth of eighteen feet and a width of one hundred and twenty-five feet at low water from its mouth to the head of navigation, in accordance with the plans heretofore submitted.


*Approved, March 11, 1896.*

# Concurrent Resolution

> commercial relations, 1894, 1895.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Public Printer be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to print, for distribution by the Department of State, two thousand five hundred copies of Commercial Relations for eighteen hundred and ninety-four and eighteen hundred and ninety five, and (in separate form) ten thousand copies of the “Review of the World’s Commerce,” and so forth, being pages one to two hundred and thirty-five, inclusive, of said Commercial Relations.


*Passed the House of Representatives April 4, 1896. Passed the Senate April 8, 1896.*

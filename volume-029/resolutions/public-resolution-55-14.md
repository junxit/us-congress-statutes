# Public Resolution 14

> Joint Resolution To supply the Department of State with documents.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Public Printer be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to print, in addition to the usual number, and furnish the Department of State with twenty copies of each Senate and House of Representatives document and report.


*Approved, February 7, 1896.*

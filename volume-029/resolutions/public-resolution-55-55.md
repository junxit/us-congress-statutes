# Public Resolution 55

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the Secretary of the Navy to deliver condemned cannon to Chamberlain Post, Grand Army of the Republic, to be posted by the soldiers’ monument at Saint Johnsbury, Vermont.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to deliver to Chamberlain Post, Grand Anny of the Republic, to be posted by the soldiers’ monument Donated for soldiers’ monument, Saint Johnsbury, Vt. in Saint Johnsbury, Vermont, two light pieces of condemned cannon and twenty cannon balls: Provided, That said delivery shall be made without expense to the United States Government and without detriment to the naval service.


*Approved, May 21, 1896.*

# Concurrent Resolution

> board of visitors military academy.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed, in paper covers, six thousand extra copies of the annual report of the Board of Visitors to the United States Military Academy at West Point for the year 1895, of which two thousand shall be for use of the Senate and four thousand for the House of Representatives.


*Passed the Senate December 17, 1895. Passed the House of Representatives December 20, 1895.*

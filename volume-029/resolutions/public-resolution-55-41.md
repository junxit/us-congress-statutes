# Public Resolution 41

> Joint Resolution Directing the Secretary of War to submit estimates of cost of further improvement of Providence River and Narragansett Bay, Rhode Island.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War is hereby directed to prepare and submit estimates for the improvement of Providence River and Narragansett Bay, Rhode Island, with a view of securing a ship channel four hundred feet in width and of a depth of twenty-five feet at mean low water from Sassafras Point, in Providence Harbor, through Providence River and Narragansett Bay by the most direct route practicable to the ocean by way of the “western passage,” so called.


*Approved, April 6, 1896.*

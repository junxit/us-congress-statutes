# Concurrent Resolution

> cotton plant bulletin.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed and bound in cloth seven thousand additional copies of Bulletin numbered thirty-three, United States Department of Agriculture, entitled the Cotton Plant; its History, Botany, Chemistry, Culture, Enemies, and Uses; of which one thousand two hundred copies shall be for the use of the Senate; and two thousand four hundred copies for the use of the House of Representatives, and three thousand four hundred for the use of the Department of Agriculture.


*Passed the Senate February 2, 1897. Passed the House of Representatives February 3, 1897.*

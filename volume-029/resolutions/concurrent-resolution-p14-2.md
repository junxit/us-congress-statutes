# Concurrent Resolution

> steamboat inspection.

*Resolved by the House (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Treasury Department, Steamboat Inspection Service, three hundred copies each of the proceedings of the Board of Supervising Inspectors of Steam Vessels for the years eighteen hundred and ninety five and eighteen hundred and ninety-six.


*Passed the House of Representatives April 4, 1896. Passed the Senate May 29, 1896.*

# Concurrent Resolution

> artesian waters of the dakotas.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed two thousand additional copies of an extract from the Seventeenth Annual Report of the United States Geological Survey, entitled “Preliminary report on artesian waters of a portion of the Dakotas,” for distribution by the Geological Survey.


*Passed the Senate January 8, 1897. Passed the House of Representatives February 16, 1897.*

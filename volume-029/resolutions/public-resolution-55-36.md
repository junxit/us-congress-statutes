# Public Resolution 36

> Joint Resolution Directing the Secretary of War to submit plans and estimates for the improvement of Fairport Harbor.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to report to Congress a re-survey of the Fort of Fairport in the County of Lake and State of Ohio, with an estimate of the cost of such improvements as may be recommended by the Engineers in charge.


*Approved, March 24, 1896.*

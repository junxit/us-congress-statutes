# Concurrent Resolution

> tariff hearings before ways and means committee.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the two Houses of Congress, ten thousand copies of the tariff hearings of the Committee on Ways and Means during the second session of the Fifty-fourth Congress, of which three thousand two hundred fifty copies shall be for the use of the Senate and six thousand seven hundred and fifty copies for the use of the House.


*Passed the House of Representatives February 26, 1897. Passed the Senate February 27, 1897.*

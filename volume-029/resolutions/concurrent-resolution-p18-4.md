# Concurrent Resolution

> foreign relations, 1896.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed and bound in red cloth one thousand copies of Foreign Relations, Eighteen hundred and ninety six, including the last annual message of the President of the United States and the last annual report of the Secretary of State, for the use of the Department of State.


*Passed the Senate March 2, 1897. Passed the House of Representatives March 2, 1897.*

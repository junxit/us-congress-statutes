# Public Resolution 23

> Joint Resolution To print the Annual Report of the General Superintendent of the Life-Saving Service.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section eighty-nine, second proviso, of the Act of January twelfth, eighteen hundred and ninety-five, an Act providing for the public printing and binding, and so forth, be, and is hereby, amended by inserting after the words “tide tables’ coast pilots,” the words “Annual Report of the General Superintendent of the Life-Saving Service,”.


*Approved, March 13, 1896.*

# Concurrent Resolution

> postal laws and regulations.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring)*

that there be printed five thousand additional copies of the Postal Laws and Regulations of 1893, for the use of the Postoffice Department.


*Passed the House of Representatives January 20, 1896. Passed the Senate January 30, 1896.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 204

> PRODUCTS OF THE PEOPLE’S REPUBLIC OF CHINA—NONDISCRIMINATORY TREATMENT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Congress approves the extension of nondiscriminatory treatment with respect to the products of the People’s Republic of China transmitted by the President to the Congress on October 23, 1979.


*Agreed to January 24, 1980.*

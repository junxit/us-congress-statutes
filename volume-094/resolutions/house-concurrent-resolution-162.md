# House Concurrent Resolution 162

> INDIAN CLAIMS COMMISSION—FINAL REPORT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the final report of the Indian Claims Commission be printed as a House document with suitable binding.

## § 2.

In addition to the usual number, there shall be printed five hundred copies of such document for the use of the Committee on Interior and Insular Affairs.


*Agreed to April 15, 1980.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 451

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Friday, November 21, 1980, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian on Monday, December 1, 1980, and that when the Senate recesses on Tuesday, November 25, 1980, it stand in recess until 11 o’clock, ante meridiem on Monday, December 1, 1980.


*Agreed to November 20, 1980.*

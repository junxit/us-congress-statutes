# House Concurrent Resolution 360

> HOUSE REPORT 96–1035

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That, in addition to the usual number of copies, there shall be printed one thousand five hundred copies of House Report Numbered 96–1035, of which seven hundred and fifty shall be distributed to the House of Representatives document room and seven hundred and fifty shall be distributed to the House Committee on Interstate and Foreign Commerce.


*Agreed to June 16, 1980.*

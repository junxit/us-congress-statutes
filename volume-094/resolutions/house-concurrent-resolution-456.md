# House Concurrent Resolution 456

> “FUTURE DIRECTIONS FOR AGING POLICY: A HUMAN SERVICE MODEL”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the House Select Committee on Aging, one thousand copies of the report “Future Directions for Aging Policy: A Human Service Model”, Ninety-sixth Congress.


*Agreed to December 12, 1980.*

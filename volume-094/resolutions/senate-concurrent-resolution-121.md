# Senate Concurrent Resolution 121

> CORRECTIONS IN ENROLLED BILL S. 299

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

The Secretary of the Senate is instructed that in the enrollment of S. 299 the following change shall be made: In section 608(b), in lieu of the word “An” insert the following: “Except as provided in section 605(b), an”.


*Agreed to September 9, 1980.*

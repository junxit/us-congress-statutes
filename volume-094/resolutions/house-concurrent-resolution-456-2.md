# House Concurrent Resolution 456

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Tuesday, December 16, 1980, and that when they adjourn on said day, they stand adjourned sine die.


*Agreed to December 16, 1980.*

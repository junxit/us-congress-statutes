# Senate Concurrent Resolution 126

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That when the Senate recesses on any day beginning with Tuesday, September 30, 1980, but no later than Thursday, October 2, 1980, as determined by the Majority Leader, after consultation with the Minority Leader, and as so moved by the Majority Leader in accordance with this resolution, it stand in recess until 11:00 a.m. on Wednesday, November 12, 1980, and that when the House of Representatives adjourns on Thursday, October 2, 1980, it stand adjourned until 12:00 meridian on Wednesday, November 12, 1980.


*Agreed to October 1, 1980.*

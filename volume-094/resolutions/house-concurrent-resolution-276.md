# House Concurrent Resolution 276

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Wednesday, February 13, 1980, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian on Tuesday, February 19, 1980.


*Agreed to February 7, 1980.*

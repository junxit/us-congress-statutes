# House Concurrent Resolution 413

> “THE CAPITOL”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there will be printed as a House document a revised edition of “The Capitol”, to be published under the direction of the Committee on House Administration.

## § 2.

In addition to the usual number of copies, there shall be printed five hundred and fifty thousand additional copies, of which four hundred and forty-two thousand copies shall be for the use of the House of Representatives, one hundred and three thousand copies shall be for the use of the Senate, and five thousand copies for the use of the Joint Committee on Printing.


*Agreed to October 1, 1980.*

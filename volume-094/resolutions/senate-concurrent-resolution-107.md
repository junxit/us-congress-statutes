# Senate Concurrent Resolution 107

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That when the Senate recesses on Wednesday, July 2, 1980, it stand in recess until 11:00 o’clock a.m. on Monday, July 21, 1980, and that when the House adjourns on Wednesday, July 2, 1980, it stand adjourned until 12:00 o’clock noon on Monday, July 21, 1980.


*Agreed to July 2, 1980.*

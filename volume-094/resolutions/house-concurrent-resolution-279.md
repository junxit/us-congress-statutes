# House Concurrent Resolution 279

> “OUR AMERICAN GOVERNMENT”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed as a House document a revised edition of “Our American Government”, revised under the direction of the Committee on House Administration.

## § 2.

In addition to the usual number of copies, there shall be printed five hundred and fifty-six thousand additional copies, of which four hundred and fifty-three thousand copies shall be for the use of the House of Representatives, and one hundred and three thousand copies shall be for the use of the Senate.


*Agreed to May 9, 1980.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 410

> CORRECTIONS IN ENROLLED BILL S. 2680

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Secretary of the Senate, in the enrollment of S. 2680 (An Act to improve the administration of the Historic Sites, Buildings, and Antiquities Act of 1935 (49 Stat. 666)), shall make the following corrections:

In section 17, change “Relations” to “Organizations” in both the first and second sentences.


*Agreed to August 27, 1980.*

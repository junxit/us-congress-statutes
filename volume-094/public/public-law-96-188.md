# Public Law 96–188

> Joint Resolution To extend by sixty days the expiration date of the Defense Production Act of 1950.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first sentence of section 717(a) of the Defense Production Act of 1950 (50 U.S.C. App. 2166(a)) is amended by striking out “January 28, 1980” and inserting in lieu thereof “March 28, 1980”.


*Approved January 28, 1980.*

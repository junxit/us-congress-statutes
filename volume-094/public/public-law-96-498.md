# Public Law 96–498

> An Act To designate the Jacob K. Javits Federal Building.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the building at 26 Federal Plaza in the Borough of Manhattan, New York City, New York (commonly known as the Federal Building), shall hereafter be known and designated as the “Jacob K. Javits Federal Building”. Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to that building shall be held to be a reference to the Jacob K. Javits Federal Building.

## § 2.

This Act shall take effect January 3, 1981.


*Approved December 4, 1980.*

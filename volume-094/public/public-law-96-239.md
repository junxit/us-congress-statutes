# Public Law 96–239

> An Act To amend title IV of the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 to postpone for two months the date on which the Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation must pay benefits under terminated multiemployer plans.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4082(c) of title IV of the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 (relating to effective dates; special rules) as amended by Public Law 95–214 and Public Law 96–24, is further amended—

- **(1)** by striking “May 1, 1980” in paragraphs (1), (2), and (4) and substituting “July 1, 1980”, in each such paragraph; and
- **(2)** by striking “April 30, 1980” in paragraphs 2(B) and 4(D) and substituting “June 30, 1980” in each such paragraph.


*Approved April 30, 1980.*

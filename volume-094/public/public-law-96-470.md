# Public Law 96–470

> An Act To discontinue or amend certain requirements for agency reports to Congress.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That this Act be cited as the “Congressional Reports Elimination Act of 1980”.

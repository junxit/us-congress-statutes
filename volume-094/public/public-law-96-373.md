# Public Law 96–373

> An Act To amend the Consumer Product Safety Act to modify certain postemployment restrictions applicable to officers and employees of the Consumer Product Safety Commission.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4(g)(2) of the Consumer Product Safety Act (15 U.S.C. 2053(g)(2)) is amended by striking out the last sentence thereof.


*Approved October 3, 1980.*

# Public Law 96–230

> An Act To extend the reorganization authority of the President under chapter 9 of title 5.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 905(b) of title 5, United States Code, is amended by striking out “three years” and inserting in lieu thereof “four years”.


*Approved April 8, 1980.*

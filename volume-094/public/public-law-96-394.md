# Public Law 96–394

> An Act To designate the building known as the Federal Building and United States Courthouse in Amarillo, Texas, as the “J. Marvin Jones Federal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the building known as the Federal Building and United States Courthouse in Amarillo, Texas, shall hereafter be known, called, and designated as the “J. Marvin Jones Federal Building”. Any reference in any law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to such building shall be deemed to be a reference to the “J. Marvin Jones Federal Building”.


*Approved October 7, 1980.*

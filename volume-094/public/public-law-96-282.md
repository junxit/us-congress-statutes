# Public Law 96–282

> Joint Resolution Making additional funds available by transfer for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1980, for the Selective Service System.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the following sum is transferred, out of funds previously appropriated, for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1980, namely:

## Selective Service System

## salaries and expenses

**(By transfer)**

For an additional amount for “Salaries and expenses”, $13,285,000, which shall be derived by transfer from unobligated balances available under the heading “Military Personnel, Air Force”: Provided, That none of the funds made available by this joint resolution shall be available for instituting or taking action to draft any individual for military service.


*Approved June 27, 1980.*

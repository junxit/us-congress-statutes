# Public Law 96–232

> An Act To designate the building known as the Federal Building in Evansville, Indiana, as the “Winfield K. Denton Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the building at 101 Northwest Seventh Street, Evansville, Indiana (commonly known as the Federal Building), shall hereafter be known and designated as the “Winfield K. Denton Building”. Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to that building shall he held to be a reference to the “Winfield K. Denton Building”.


*Approved April 10, 1980.*

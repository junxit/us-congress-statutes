# Public Law 96–293

> An Act To amend title IV of the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 to postpone for one month the date on which the corporation must pay benefits under terminated multiemployer plans.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4082(c) of title IV of the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 (relating to effective dates; special rules) as amended by Public Law 95–214, Public Law 96–24, and Public Law 96–239 is further amended—

- **(1)** by striking “July 1, 1980” in paragraphs (1), (2), and (4) and substituting “August 1, 1980” in each such paragraph; and
- **(2)** by striking “June 30, 1980” in paragraphs 2(B) and 4(D) and substituting “July 31, 1980” in each such paragraph.


*Approved June 30, 1980.*

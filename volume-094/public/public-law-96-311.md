# Public Law 96–311

> An Act To provide an extension of the time frame for nomination of a selection pool under the Cook Inlet land exchange.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of August 14, 1979 (93 Stat. 386), is hereby amended to strike “twelve” and insert in lieu thereof “thirty-six”.


*Approved July 17, 1980.*

# Public Law 96–268

> An Act To amend the Act of October 15, 1966 (80 Stat 953; 20 U.S.C. 65a), relating to the National Museum of the Smithsonian Institution, so as to authorize additional appropriations to the Smithsonian Institution for carrying out the purposes of said Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2(b) of the National Museum Act of 1966 (20 U.S.C. 65a) is amended to read:

> - **(b)** There are hereby authorized to be appropriated to the Smithsonian Institution for the fiscal year 1981, the sum of $803,000, and for the fiscal year 1982, the sum of $1,000,000.”.


*Approved June 13, 1980.*

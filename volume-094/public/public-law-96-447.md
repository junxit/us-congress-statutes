# Public Law 96–447

> An Act To amend section 106 of the Civil Rights Act of 1957 to authorize appropriations for the Commission on Civil Rights for the fiscal year 1981.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That this Act may be cited as the “Civil Rights Commission Authorization Act of 1980”.

## § 2.

Section 106 of the Civil Rights Act of 1957 (42 U.S.C. 1975e) is amended (1) by striking out “$14,000,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$12,600,000”; and (2) by striking out “1980” and inserting in lieu thereof “1981”.


*Approved October 13, 1980.*

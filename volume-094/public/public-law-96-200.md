# Public Law 96–200

> Joint Resolution To provide for the designation of October 3, 1980, as “American Enterprise Day”.

Whereas America’s enterprise system is a cornerstone in our society; and Whereas that system has produced the highest standard of living in the world; and Whereas that system depends on and rewards individual initiative and innovation; and Whereas American productivity is vital to the world’s economy and must be encouraged; and Whereas the continuance and growth of our enterprise system depends in large part on the education of America’s young men and women concerning that system: Now, therefore, be it Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

That the President of the United States is authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating October 3, 1980, as “American Enterprise Day” and encouraging appropriate Government agencies to foster the recognition of the significance of America’s enterprise system on that day.


*Approved March 6, 1980.*

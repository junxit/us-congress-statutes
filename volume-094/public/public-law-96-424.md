# Public Law 96–424

> An Act To repeal a provision of the Refugee Education Assistance Act of 1980.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That effective on the day after the date of enactment of the Refugee Education Assistance Act of 1980, section 501(c)(5) of the Refugee Education Assistance Act of 1980 is repealed.


*Approved October 10, 1980.*

# Public Law 96–271

> An Act To authorize appropriations for the International Natural Rubber Agreement for fiscal year 1981.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in order to meet the obligations of the United States as a member of the International Natural Rubber Organization established by the International Natural Rubber Agreement, there is authorized to be appropriated for the fiscal year 1981 $88,000,000 for the payment of contributions by the United States to the buffer stock account established by the agreement. Funds appropriated under this Act are authorized to remain available during the period in which the International Natural Rubber Agreement remains in effect with respect to the United States.


*Approved June 16, 1980.*

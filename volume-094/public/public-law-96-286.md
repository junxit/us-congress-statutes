# Public Law 96–286

> Joint Resolution To provide for a temporary increase in the public debt limit.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

During the period beginning on the date of the enactment of this Act and ending on February 28, 1981, the public debt limit set forth in the first sentence of section 21 of the Second Liberty Bond Act (31 U.S.C. 757b) shall be temporarily increased by $525,000,000,000.


*Approved June 28, 1980.*

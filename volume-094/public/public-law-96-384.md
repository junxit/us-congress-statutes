# Public Law 96–384

> An Act To change the name of Aubrey Lake, Texas, to Ray Roberts Lake.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Aubrey Lake project, Elm Fork of the Trinity River, Texas, authorized by the River and Harbor Act of 1965 (79 Stat. 1073), shall hereafter be known as Ray Roberts Lake. Any law, regulation, map, document, or record of the United States in which such lake is referred to shall be held and considered to refer to such lake as “Ray Roberts Lake”. This Act shall take effect on January 4, 1981.


*Approved October 6, 1980.*

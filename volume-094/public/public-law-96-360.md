# Public Law 96–360

> An Act To terminate the authority to make grants to the Las Vegas Valley Water District under the Act of August 27, 1954.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That effective on the date of enactment of this Act, the Act of August 27, 1954, entitled “An Act granting to the Las Vegas Valley Water District, a public corporation organized under the laws of the State of Nevada, certain public lands of the United States in the State of Nevada” (68 Stat 864) is hereby repealed.

## § 2.

Nothing in this Act shall in any manner affect any grant made under the authority of the Act of August 27, 1954, prior to the effective date of this Act.


*Approved September 26, 1980.*

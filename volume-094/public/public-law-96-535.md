# Public Law 96–535

> An Act To amend title 32, United States Code, to allow Federal recognition as officers of the National Guard of members of the National Guard of the Virgin Islands in grades above the grade of colonel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (g) of section 307 of title 32, United States Code, is repealed.


*Approved December 16, 1980.*

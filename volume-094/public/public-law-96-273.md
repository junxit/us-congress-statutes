# Public Law 96–273

> An Act To amend the Saccharin Study and Labeling Act to extend to June 30, 1981, the ban on actions by the Secretary of Health, Education, and Welfare respecting saccharin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3 of the Saccharin Study and Labeling Act (21 U.S.C. 348 note) is amended by striking out “During the 18-month period beginning on the date of the enactment of this Act” and inserting in lieu thereof “During the period beginning on the date of the enactment of this Act and ending June 30, 1981”.


*Approved June 17, 1980.*

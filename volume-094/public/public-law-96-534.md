# Public Law 96–534

> An Act To extend the Joint Funding Simplification Act of 1974.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 13 of the Joint Funding Simplification Act of 1974 is amended by striking out “five” and inserting “ten”.


*Approved December 16, 1980.*

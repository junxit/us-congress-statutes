# Private Law 96–101

> An Act To confer United States citizenship posthumously upon Leopoldine Marie Schmid.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Leopoldine Marie Schmid, late of San Mateo, California, and formerly of Zurich, Switzerland, shall be held and considered to have been a citizen of the United States at the time of her death.


*Approved December 22, 1980.*

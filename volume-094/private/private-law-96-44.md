# Private Law 96–44

> An Act For the relief of Rocio Edmondson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Rocio Edmondson shall be held and considered to be a returning resident alien as defined in section 101(a)(27)(A) of that Act, as of the date of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved March 3, 1980.*

# Private Law 96–121

> An Act For the relief of Francisco Pang.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act the provisions of section 312 of that Act shall be inapplicable in the case of Francisco Pang and he may be naturalized upon compliance with all the requirements of the naturalization laws except that no prior residence or specified period of physical presence within the United States or within the jurisdiction of the naturalization court or proof thereof shall be required.


*Approved December 22, 1980.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 44

> holiday recess.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on the legislative day of December 22, 1926, they stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Monday, January 3, 1927.


*Passed, December 22, 1926.*

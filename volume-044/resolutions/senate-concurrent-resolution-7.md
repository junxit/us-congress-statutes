# Senate Concurrent Resolution 7

> statue of crawford w. long.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the statue of Crawford W. Long, presented by the State of Georgia, to be placed in Statuary Hall, is accepted in the name of the United States, and that the thanks of Congress be tendered the State for the contribution of the statue of one of its most eminent citizens, illustrious for his distinguished humanitarian service.

That a copy of these resolutions, suitably engrossed and duly authenticated, be transmitted to the Governor of Georgia.


*Passed, April 29, 1926.*

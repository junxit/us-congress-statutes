# Senate Concurrent Resolution 20

> pecos river.

*Resolved by the Renate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That, the Clerk of the House of Representatives be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, in the enrollment of the bill (H. R. 3862) to provide for the storage of the waters of the Pecos River to correct an error by striking out the language contained in amendment numbered 3 of the Senate engrossed amendments.


*Passed, June 1, 1926.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 21

> arts and artists of capitol.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed and bound as may be directed by the Joint Committee on Printing five thousand additional copies of Senate Document 95, entitled “Art and Artists of the United States Capitol,” of which one thousand copies shall be for the use of the Senate, three thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives, five hundred copies for the use of the Architect of the Capitol, and five hundred copies for the use of the Joint Committee on the Library.


*Passed, July 2, 1926.*

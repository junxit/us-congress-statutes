# House Concurrent Resolution 60

> engrossing, etc., bills.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That during the remainder of the present session of Congress the engrossment and enrolling of bills and joint resolutions by printing, as provided by an Act of Congress, approved March 2, 1895, may be suspended, and said bills and joint resolutions may be engrossed and enrolled by the most expeditious methods consistent with accuracy.


*Passed, March 4, 1927.*

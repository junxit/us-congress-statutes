# House Concurrent Resolution 24

> detroit river bridge.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the report of the Committee of Conference on the disagreeing votes of the two Houses on the amendments of the Senate to the bill (H. R. 8771) entitled “An Act to extend the time for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Detroit River within or near the city limits of Detroit, Michigan,” be recommitted to the Committee of Conference.


*Passed, May 3, 1926.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 22

> bridge mississippi river at minneapolis.

*Resolved by Hie Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President of the United States be, and he is hereby, requested to return to the Senate the enrolled bill (S. 3989) to extend the time for the construction of a bridge by the city of Minneapolis, Minnesota, across the Mississippi River in said city.


*Passed, June 15, 1926.*

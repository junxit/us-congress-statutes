# House Concurrent Resolution 3

> holiday recess.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (five Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn Tuesday, December 22, 1925, they stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Monday, January 4, 1926.


*Passed, December 11, 1925.*

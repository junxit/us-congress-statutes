# Senate Concurrent Resolution 23

> bridge mississippi river at minneapolis.

*Resolved by the Senate (the Rouse of Representatives concurring),*

That the action of the Vice President of the United States and the Speaker of the House of Representatives in signing the enrolled bill (S. 3989) to extend the time for the construction of a bridge by the city of Minneapolis, Minnesota, across the Mississippi River in said city be, and the same is hereby, rescinded, and that the said bill be postponed indefinitely.


*Passed, June 18, 1926.*

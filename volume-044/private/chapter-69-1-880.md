# Chapter 880

> An Act For the relief of Virginia Strickland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration or any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon widows of honorably discharged soldiers, Orrin F. Strickland, late of Company I), One hundred and eleventh Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a member of said company and regiment on the 26th day of December, 1864: Provided, That no back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, July 3, 1926.*

# Chapter 402

> An Act For the relief of Claude T. Winslow.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Post-master General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the accounts of Claude T. Winslow, postmaster at Mayfield, Kentucky, in the sum of $74,628.45, due to the United States on account of money and postage stamps stolen from the safe of the post office at Mayfield, Kentucky, when burglarized on October 10, 1923.


*Approved, March 3, 1927.*

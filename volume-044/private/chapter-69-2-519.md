# Chapter 519

> An Act For the relief of Walter B. Avery and Fred S. Gichner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized to adjust and allow the claims of Walter B. Avery in the amount of $1,493 for elevator penthouse and machinery inclosure erected in the Butler Building occupied by the Coast and Geodetic Survey; and of Fred S. Gichner in the amount of $550 for labor and materials for hoistway for elevator for the Butler Building occupied by the Coast and Geodetic Survey, and the sum of $2,043 is hereby appropriated for said purpose out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 4, 1927.*

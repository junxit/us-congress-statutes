# Chapter 863

> An Act For the relief of Harry Coventry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, or benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Harry Coventry, who served in the World War as a first sergeant in Company I, Three hundred and twenty-sixth Regiment United States Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States on March 3, 1920: Provided, That no back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, July 3, 1926.*

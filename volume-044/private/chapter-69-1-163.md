# Chapter 163

> An Act To remove the charge of desertion from the records of the War Department standing against William J. Dunlap.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws and the laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, William J. Dunlap, formerly a member of Company F, Tenth Regiment United States Infantry, shall be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States on May 20, 1902: Provided, That no back pay, pension, or allowance shall lie held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, April 17, 1926.*

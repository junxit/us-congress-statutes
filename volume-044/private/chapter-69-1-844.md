# Chapter 844

> An Act For the relief of G. C. Alien.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to G. C. Allen, of Hot Springs, Arkansas, the sum of $2,000 for injuries received by a piece of the doorway of the Federal post-office building at Hot Spring, Arkansas, falling upon him, through no fault or negligence of his own.


*Approved, July 3, 1926.*

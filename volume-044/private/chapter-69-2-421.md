# Chapter 421

> An Act For the relief of Charles O. Dunbar.

*Be it enacted by the Senate anti Mouse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the accounting officers of the Treasury be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to credit the account of Charles O. Dunbar, former post-master, Santa Rosa, California, in the sum of $2,049.11, due the United States on account of the loss of postal funds and war savings stamps.


*Approved, March 3, 1927.*

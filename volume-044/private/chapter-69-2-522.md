# Chapter 522

> An Act To grant the right and time for appeal to plaintiffs in suit numbered 33731 in the Court of Claims of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the plaintiffs or complainants in suit numbered 33731 in the Court of Claims of the United States be, and they are hereby, granted one year within which to appeal from any or all orders, judgments, or decrees rendered against them in the trial of said action heretofore had: Provided, That the time within which said appeal may be taken shall begin to run with the date of the approval of this Act.


*Approved, March 4, 1927.*

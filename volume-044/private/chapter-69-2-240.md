# Chapter 240

> An Act For the relief of Roswell H. Bancroft.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Post-master General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the accounts of Roswell H. Bancroft, postmaster at Palisades, Colorado, in the sum of $15,188.91 due to the United States on account of postal funds, postage stamps, and war tax revenue stamps which were lost as a result of burglary on the night of August 2, 1924.


*Approved, February 28, 1927.*

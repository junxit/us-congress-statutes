# Chapter 510

> An Act For the relief of Wade W. Barber.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Wade W. Barber, of Bancroft, Nebraska, who was a member of Company F, Thirty-fifth Infantry, United States Volunteers, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a private of that organization on the third day of January, 1900: Provided., That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 8, 1926.*

# Chapter 233

> An Act For the relief of Patrick C. Wilkes, alias Clebourn P. Wilkes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, or benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers Patrick C. Wilkes, alias Clebourn P. Wilkes, private Battery C, Second Regiment United States Artillery, which subsequently became the Fourteenth Company of Coast Artillery, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as of said organization on the 15th day of March, 1901: Provided, That no back pay, bounty, pension, or other emolument shall accrue prior to the approval of tins Act.


*Approved, February 28, 1927.*

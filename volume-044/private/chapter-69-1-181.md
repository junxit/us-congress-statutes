# Chapter 181

> An Act For the relief of James Madison Brown.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, James Madison Brown, who was a member of Company G, Thirty-fourth Regiment Indiana Volunteer Infantry, Civil War, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a member of that organization on the 4th day of May, 1862: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, April 24, 1926.*

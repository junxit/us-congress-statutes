# Chapter 874

> An Act To correct the military record of John P. Daley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That John P. Daley, late of Battery B, Seventh Artillery, shall hereafter he held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a member oi Battery B, Seventh Artillery, on the 3d day of August, 1900: Provided, That no pay, bounty, pension, or other emolument shall accrue prior to or by reason of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, July 3, 1926.*

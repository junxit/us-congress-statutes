# Chapter 504

> An Act For the relief of John A. Douglas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, John A. Douglas, who was a member of Company G, Twenty-second Regiment Michigan Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a member of that organization on the 18th day of February, 1865: Provided, That no back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 8, 1926.*

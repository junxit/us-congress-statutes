# Chapter 681

> An Act For the relief of Yvonne Therrien.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Howe of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Yvonne Therrien, of Lynn, Massachusetts, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $300 in full settlement for injuries received by being struck by a United States mail truck in the city of Boston, April 29, 1921.


*Approved, June 25, 1926.*

# Chapter 822

> An Act For the relief of John Regan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, John Began, late of Troop A, Fifth Regiment United States Cavalry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States, as a private of that organization on the 10th day of October, 1865: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, July 3, 1926.*

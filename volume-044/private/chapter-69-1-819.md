# Chapter 819

> An Act For the relief of Robert E. A. Landauer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers Robert E. A. Landauer, who was a private of Company I, Twelfth Regiment New York Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a private of that organization on September 17, 1898: Provided, That no back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, July 3, 1926.*

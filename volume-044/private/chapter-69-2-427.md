# Chapter 427

> An Act For the relief of C. G. Duganne and A. N. Ross.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the General Accounting Office of the Treasury Department is hereby authorized and directed to credit in the accounts of C. G. Duganne, formerly disbursing clerk of the Federal Trade Commission, and A. N. Ross, present disbursing clerk of the Federal Trade Commission, both located at Washington, District of Columbia, the amounts of $42.80 and $28.75, respectively, representing amounts paid by them and which were disallowed by the General Accounting Office as having been paid in contravention of the travel regulations of the Federal Trade Commission, or the rulings of the General Accounting Office.


*Approved, March 3, 1927.*

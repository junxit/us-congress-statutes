# Chapter 11

> An Act For the relief of Isaac A. Chandler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Isaac A. Chandler, who was a private in Troop A, Fourth United States Cavalry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a private of said troop and regiment on the 17th day of December, 1875: Provided, That no bounty, pension, pay, or allowances shall be held as accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, December 15, 1926.*

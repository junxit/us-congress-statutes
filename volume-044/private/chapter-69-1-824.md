# Chapter 824

> An Act For the relief of Sam Tilden.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby appropriated $749.50, not otherwise appropriated, to Sam Tilden, or Lapwai, Idaho, compensation in full for expenses incurred by him in necessarily defending himself in the courts for action performed in line of duty while serving as an Indian policeman on May 6, 1914, in endeavoring to prevent the introduction of intoxicating liquor on the Nez Perce Indian Reservation, and the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby authorized and directed to pay the amount herein mentioned to Sani Tilden.


*Approved, July 3, 1926.*

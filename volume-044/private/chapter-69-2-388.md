# Chapter 388

> An Act For the relief of David Barker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws David Barker shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the. United States Marine Corps, August 1, 1900. at Mare Island, California: Provided, That no pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 3, 1927.*

# Chapter 665

> An Act For the relief of James C. Minon.

*Be it enacted try the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in, Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged men of the United States Navy, James C. Minon, formerly a landsman in the United States Navy, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged on the 20th day of November, 1898: Provided, That no back pension, allowance, or other emolument shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 23, 1926.*

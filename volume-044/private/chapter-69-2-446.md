# Chapter 446

> An Act For the relief of Albert H. Hosley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $4,000 to Albert H. Hosley, of Tupper Lake, New York, in payment of house situated on the land conveyed to the United States by deed dated July 26, 1922, as a Government hospital site.


*Approved, March 3, 1927.*

# Chapter 81

> An Act For the relief of Ella Miller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Compress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, and in full settlement against the Government, to Ella Miller, of Brooklyn Borough, New York City, New York, the sum of $1,500 as compensation for personal injuries received and property damage caused by a United States mail truck while crossing a street in Brooklyn, New York.


*Approved, February 8, 1927.*

# Chapter 432

> An Act For the relief of Gagnon and Company, Incorporated.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated out of any moneys in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $3,592 to Gagnon and Company, Incorporated, in full compensation for the erection of a school building at Crow Agency, Montana, in 1915–16.


*Approved, May 29, 1926.*

# Chapter 612

> An Act For the relief of James H. Kelly.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws James H. Kelly, late of Company C, Fifth Regiment West Virginia Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from military service of the United States as a member of said regiment on the 6th day of June, 1864: Provided, That no back pay, bounty, pension, or allowance shall accrue prior to or by reason of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 17, 1926.*

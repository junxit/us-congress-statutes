# Chapter 220

> An Act For the relief of Walter W. Price.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Post-master General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, to credit the accounts of Walter W. Price, late postmaster at Oneida, Tennessee, in the sum of $10,233.27, on account of the loss of postal funds and war-savings stamps resulting from the burglary of the First National Bank building on May 16, 1920.


*Approved, May 1, 1926.*

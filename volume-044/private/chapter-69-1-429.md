# Chapter 429

> An Act For the relief of Mrs. W. H. ReMine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Mrs. W. H. ReMine, widow of First Lieutenant W. H. ReMine, Medical Corps, United States Army, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $2,156.89 for the loss of her furniture, household goods, and personal effects caused by shipment on United States transport Madawaska.


*Approved, May 29, 1926.*

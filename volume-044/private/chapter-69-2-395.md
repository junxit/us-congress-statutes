# Chapter 395

> An Act To correct the military record of Thornton Jackson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws governing Civil War soldiers, Thornton Jackson, now a resident of Indiana, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a private of Company K, One hundred and forty-ninth Regiment Indiana Volunteer Infantry, on the 27th day of September, 1865: Provided, That no back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 3, 1927.*

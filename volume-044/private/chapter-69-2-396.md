# Chapter 396

> An Act For the relief of Lawrence F. Nelson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Lawrence F. Nelson, who was. a member of Troop M, Fourth Regiment United States Cavalry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a private of that organization on the 8th day of December, 1901: Provided,, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 3, 1927.*

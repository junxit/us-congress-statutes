# Chapter 207

> An Act To amend the military record of Leo J. Pourciau.

*Be it enacted By the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Leo J. Pourciau, late of Company B, Fifty-ninth Infantry, United States Army, shall be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a private of that organization on the 20th day of January, 1919: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 25, 1927.*

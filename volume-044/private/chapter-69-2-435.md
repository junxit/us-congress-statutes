# Chapter 435

> An Act For the relief of Garfield Hankins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the account of Garfield Hankins, late postmaster at Musselshell, Montana, in the sum of $633.32, due the United States on account of the loss resulting from the closing of the Musselshell State Bank, of Musselshell, Montana.


*Approved, March 3, 1927.*

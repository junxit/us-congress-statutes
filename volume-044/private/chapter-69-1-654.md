# Chapter 654

> An Act For the relief of certain disbursing officers of the office of Superintendent State, War, and Navy Department Buildings.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is authorized and directed to credit the accounts of Frank W. Hoover and Edward F. Batchelor, disbursing officers, office of the Superintendent State, War, and Navy Department Buildings, in the sum of $24,000, disallowed upon vouchers numbered 350, 224, 182, and 331 during the fiscal year ended June 30, 1923; and vouchers numbered 41, 312, 313, and 487 during the fiscal year ended June 30, 1924.


*Approved, June 22, 1926.*

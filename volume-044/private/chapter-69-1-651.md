# Chapter 651

> An Act For the relief of Alling R. Maish.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Blouse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Alling K. Maish, who was a member of Company M, Seventeenth United States Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a private of that organization on the 4th day of March, 1903: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 22, 1926.*

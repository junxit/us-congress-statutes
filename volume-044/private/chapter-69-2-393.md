# Chapter 393

> An Act To correct the military record of William J. Bodiford.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldier’s, William J. Bodiford, who was a member of Company I, Second Regiment South Carolina Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been dis-charged honorably from the military service of the United States as a private of that organization on September 12, 1898: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 3, 1927.*

# Chapter 619

> An Act To provide for the advancement on the retired list of the Navy of Milton F. Nicholson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Ensign Milton F. Nicholson, United States Navy, retired, shall have the rank and receive the pay and allowances of a lieutenant, junior grade, on the retired list of the United States Navy. Such rank shall take effect on August 7, 1925, and such pay and allowances shall be paid from and after such date.


*Approved, June 17, 1926.*

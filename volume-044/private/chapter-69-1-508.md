# Chapter 508

> An Act For the relief of Joseph A. Choate.

*Be it enacted by the. Senate and. House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Joseph A. Choate, who served under the name of Alexander Choat as a private of Company K, Fourth Regiment Tennessee Volunteer Cavalry, shall hereafter be held anti considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a private of said company and regiment on the 25th day of November, 1864: Provided, That no back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 8, 1926.*

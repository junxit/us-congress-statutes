# Chapter 616

> An Act To correct the naval record of Charles David Guthridge.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Bouse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to review the naval record of Charles David Guthridge, seaman second class, United States Navy, and to remove the charges of desertion against the said Charles David Guthridge and to grant him an honorable discharge.


*Approved, June 17, 1926.*

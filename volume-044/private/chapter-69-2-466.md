# Chapter 466

> An Act For the relief of Charles O. Schmidt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United, States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, and in full settlement against the Government, to Charles O. Schmidt, the sum of $1,317.37, the same being for losses sustained by him resulting from an accident caused by an Army vehicle near Farmingdale, Long Island, New York, on or about November 11, 1919.


*Approved, March 3, 1927.*

# Chapter 159

> An Act For the relief of Mary Moore.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, William M. Moore (now deceased), who was a private in Troop L, Third United States Cavalry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a member of said company and regiment on the 24th day of February, 1899: Provided, That no back pay, pension, or bounty shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 17, 1927.*

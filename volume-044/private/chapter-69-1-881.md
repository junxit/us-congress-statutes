# Chapter 881

> An Act For the relief of Neil Mullane.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Neil Mullane, postal savings clerk, Detroit, Michigan, the sum of $50, being the amount of a wrong payment on postal savings certificate, which was not due to any negligence on his part, and for which his salary has been debited.


*Approved, July 3, 1926.*

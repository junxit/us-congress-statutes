# Chapter 219

> An Act For the relief of the estate of William P. Nisbett, senior, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Post-master General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the legal representatives of the estate of William P. Nisbett, senior, deceased, formerly postmaster at Big Rapids, Mecosta County, Michigan, in the sum of $20,530.52, due to the United States on account of postal stamps, war-saving stamps, and money order funds which were lost as the result of burglary on the night of November 19, 1922.


*Approved, May 1, 1926.*

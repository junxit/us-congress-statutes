# Chapter 172

> An Act For the relief of George Barrett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, or benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, their widows, and dependent relatives George Barrett, Army serial number 1637071, who was a private of Battery F, Twelfth Field Artillery, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a member of said battery and regiment on the 3d day of April, 1919: Provided, That no back pay or allowance of any kind shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, April 22, 1926.*

# Chapter 464

> An Act For the relief of Ira E. King.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Post-master General be, and he is hereby,, authorized and directed to credit Ira E. King, postmaster at Stillwater, Minnesota, in the sum of $17,231.39, due to the United States on account of postal funds, postage stamps, and war tax revenue stamps which were lost as a result of burglary on the night of December 5, 1925.


*Approved, March 3, 1927.*

# Chapter 467

> An Act For the relief of George Boiko and Company (Incorporated).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to George Boiko and Company (Incorporated), out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $5,871.97, in full, final, and complete payment for certain goods taken from the possession of said corporation by the United States.


*Approved, March 3, 1927.*

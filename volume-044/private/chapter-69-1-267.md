# Chapter 267

> An Act For the relief of James W. Laxson.

*Be it enacted Try the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $182 to James W. Laxson for a refund covering timber taken from his homestead entry.


*Approved, May 7, 1926.*

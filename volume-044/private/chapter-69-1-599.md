# Chapter 599

> An Act Authorizing the health officer of the District of Columbia to issue a permit for the removal of the remains of the late Daniel F. Crump within Glenwood Cemetery.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the health officer of the District of Columbia be. and he is hereby, authorized to issue a permit for the removal of the remains of the late Daniel F. Crump from one section of Glenwood Cemetery in the District of Columbia to another location in said cemetery.


*Approved, June 16, 1926.*

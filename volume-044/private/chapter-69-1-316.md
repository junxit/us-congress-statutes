# Chapter 316

> An Act Authorizing the Secretary of the Interior to issue letters patent to George K. Hughes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That upon the payment therefor at the rate of $1.25 per acre, the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to issue letters patent to George K. Hughes, of Monticello, White County, Indiana, for the following-described hinds, to wit: Lot 1, section 28, township 28 north, range 3 west, second principal meridian, Indiana, three and forty-three one-hundredth acres.


*Approved, May 17, 1926.*

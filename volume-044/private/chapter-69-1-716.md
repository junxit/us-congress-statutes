# Chapter 716

> An Act To reinstate William R. F. Bleakney in West Point Military Academy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized to reinstate William R. F. Bleakney as a cadet in the United States Military Academy in the class of 1927: Provided, That this shall not operate to increase the Corps of Cadets at said academy as now authorized by law.


*Approved, June 30, 1926.*

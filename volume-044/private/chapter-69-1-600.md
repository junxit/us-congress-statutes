# Chapter 600

> An Act For the relief of Helen M. Peck.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay Helen M. Peck out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $230.50 in full satisfaction of all claims for damages sustained on January 20, 1921, through the loss of two horses and two pack-saddles while in the use of the National Park Service at Grand Canyon National Park.


*Approved, June 16, 1926.*

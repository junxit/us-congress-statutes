# Chapter 248

> An Act For the relief of Elizabeth W. Kieffer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Post-master General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the accounts of Elizabeth W, Kieffer, postmaster at Fort Russell, Wyoming, in the sum of $568.31, due to the United States on account of loss by theft of post-office funds by Claude E. Davis, on November 14, 1922.


*Approved, March 1, 1927.*

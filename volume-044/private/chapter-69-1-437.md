# Chapter 437

> An Act For the relief of Kate T. Riley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay to Kate T. Riley, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, and in full settlement against the Government, the sum of $481, which said sum represents the amount paid to the United States by the said Kate T, Riley to cover the loss of public funds stolen from the money-order division of the post office at Mobile, Alabama, on January 12, 1924.


*Approved, June 1, 1926.*

# Chapter 827

> An Act For the relief of George A. McKenzie, alias William A. Williams.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the ad-ministration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, George A. McKenzie, alias William A. Williams, who was a member of Company H, Thirteenth Regiment New York Volunteer Cavalry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a private of that organization on the13th day of July, 1865: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to hare accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, July 3, 1926.*

# Chapter 69

> An Act To correct the military record of Daniel C. Darroch.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension and homestead laws and the laws governing the National Home for Disabled Volunteer Soldiers, or any branch thereof, Daniel C. Darroch shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a private of Company B, Fifty-first Indiana Volunteer Infantry, on September 15, 1863: Provided, That no back pay, pension, bounty, or allowances shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 7, 1927.*

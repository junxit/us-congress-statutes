# Chapter 418

> An Act For the relief of Ann Margaret Mann.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be authorized and directed to pay to Ann Margaret Mann, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated and in full settlement against the Government, the sum of $1,596.50, the value of personal property lost by fire on the United States Navy barge numbered 91 on or about October 24, 1922.


*Approved, March 3, 1927.*

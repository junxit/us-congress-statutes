# Chapter 271

> An Act For the relief of Philip Hertz (Philip Herz).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the, United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Philip Hertz (Philip Herz), late of Company H, Sixty-first Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a member of said company and regiment on the 18th day of July, 1864: Provided, That no bounty, pay, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, May 7, 1926.*

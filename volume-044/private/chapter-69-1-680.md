# Chapter 680

> An Act For the relief of Alice M. Durkee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Alice M. Durkee, of Lynn. Massachusetts, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $2,000; in full settlement for injuries received by being struck by a United States mail truck in the city of Boston April 29, 1921.


*Approved, June 25, 1926.*

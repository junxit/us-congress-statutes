# Chapter 419

> An Act For the relief of Charles W. Shumate.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, and in full settlement against the Government, the sum of $2,500 to Charles W. Shumate on account of the death of his daughter, Helen Shumate, who was killed by a United States Army truck on October 11, 1319.


*Approved, March 3, 1927.*

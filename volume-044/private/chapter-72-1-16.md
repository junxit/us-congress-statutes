# Chapter 16

> An Act For the relief of Cyrus S. Andrews.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Cyrus S. Andrews, who was a private in Company H, One hundred and forty-fifth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a private of said company and regiment on the 26th day of June, I860: Provided, That no bounty, pay, or allowances shall be held as accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, December 18, 1926.*

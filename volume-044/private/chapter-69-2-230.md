# Chapter 230

> An Act For the relief of Carlos Tompkins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Carlos Tompkins, who served as a member of Troop H, Second Regiment United States Cavalry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from said service on the 20th day of May, 1865: Provided, That no back pay, pension, bounty, or other emolument shall accrue prior to t he passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 28, 1927.*

# Chapter 656

> An Act For the relief of the New Jersey Shipbuilding and Dredging Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $41,320.25, to reimburse the New Jersey Shipbuilding and Dredging Company for extraordinary work required by the War Department in connection with deepening the channel in East River, New York City, said sum to be in full and final settlement of the claim.


*Approved, June 22, 1926.*

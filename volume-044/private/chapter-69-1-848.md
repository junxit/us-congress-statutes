# Chapter 848

> An Act For the relief of Harold Holst.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws Harold Holst, late of the United States Marine Corps on the United States ship Sabine, United States Navy, shall be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the United States Navy, to date from October 31, 1862: Provided, That no back pay, pension, bounty, or other emolument shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, July 3, 1926.*

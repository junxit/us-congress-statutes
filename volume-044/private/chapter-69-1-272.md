# Chapter 272

> An Act To extend the time for institution of proceedings authorized under Private Law Numbered 81, Sixty-eighth Congress, being an Act for the relief of Henry A. Kessel Company (Incorporated).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That an extension of not more than four months from the date of the passage of this bill, for the time for institution of the proceedings authorized under Private Law 81, Sixty-eighth Congress, being an Act for the relief of Henry A. Kessel Company (Incorporated), be, and is hereby, authorized.


*Approved, May 7, 1926.*

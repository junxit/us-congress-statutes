# Chapter 896

> Joint Resolution For the relief of R. S. Howard Company.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the House of Representatives refer the subject matter of this resolution to the Court of Claims for a rehearing, in view of the decision of the Supreme Court of the United States in the case of A. W. Duckett and Company against the United States, decided by said court on November 17, 1924, subsequent to the statement by the Court of Claims of its conclusion of law on the facts relating to the claim of R. S. Howard Company.


*Approved, July 3, 1926.*

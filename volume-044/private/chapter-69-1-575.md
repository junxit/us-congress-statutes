# Chapter 575

> An Act For the relief of Harry Ross Hubbard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is authorized and directed to credit the accounts of Harry Ross Hubbard, lieutenant (junior grade), United States Navy, with the sum of $942.25, disallowed in item 5 of statement of differences, certificate numbered N–1379–E, dated May 6, 1922.


*Approved, June 12, 1926.*

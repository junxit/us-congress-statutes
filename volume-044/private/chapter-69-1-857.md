# Chapter 857

> An Act For the relief of J. B. Elliott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $470.61, to J. B. Elliott, postmaster at Athens, Tennessee, being the amount paid out by him as the result of services rendered by an expert in burning open the doors of a safe in the post office at Athens, Tennessee, which refused to function on July 10, 1922.


*Approved, July 3, 1926.*

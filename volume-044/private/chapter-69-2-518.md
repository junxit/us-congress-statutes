# Chapter 518

> An Act Authorizing the Secretary of the Interior to issue patent to the County of Del Norte, State of California, to Whaler Island in Crescent City Bay, Del Norte County, California, for purposes of a public wharf.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized to issue patent to the County of De] Norte, State of California, to Whaler Island, containing about three acres, in Crescent City Bay, Del Norte County, California, for purposes of a public wharf.

## § 2.

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby directed to take such action as may be necessary to carry out the purposes of this Act.


*Approved, March 4, 1927.*

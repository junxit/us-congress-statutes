# Chapter 406

> An Act To credit the accounts of Richings J. Shand, United States property and disbursing officer, Illinois National Guard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General is authorized and directed to allow credit in the accounts of Colonel Richings J. Shand, United States property and disbursing officer for Illinois, for any disallowance required or made in his accounts as a result of an inspection made in certain units of the Illinois National Guard by the Inspector General of the Army, dated August 31, 1926, where payments were made in good faith by Colonel Shand.


*Approved, March 3, 1927.*

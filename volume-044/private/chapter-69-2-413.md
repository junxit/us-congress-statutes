# Chapter 413

> An Act For the relief of James C. Baskin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged officers, their widows and dependent relatives, James C. Baskin shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a first lieutenant of Infantry, on the 18th day of June, 1919.


*Approved, March 3, 1927.*

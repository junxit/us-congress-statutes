# Chapter 330

> An Act For the relief of H. R. Butcher.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the General Accounting Office be. and it is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the account of H. K. Butcher, former second lieutenant, Quartermaster Corps, United States Army, disbursing officer of the Army at Camp Funston, Kansas, with $299.78, this sum being an alleged shortage in the accounts of the said H. R. Butcher and a balance due, the United States while acting as disbursing officer at Camp Funston. Kansas, due to the lack of evidence to support certain war-time disbursements.


*Approved, May 18, 1926.*

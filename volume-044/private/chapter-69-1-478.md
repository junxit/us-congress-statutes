# Chapter 478

> An Act For the relief of John P. McLaughlin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to allow credit in the account of Collector of Internal Revalue John P. McLaughlin, first California district, in the sum of $2,204.78 to cover disallowances due to overpayment of subsistence to certain gaugers and storekeeper gaugers, which were incurred in the months of December, 1921, January, February, March, April, May, and June, 1922.


*Approved, June 4, 1926.*

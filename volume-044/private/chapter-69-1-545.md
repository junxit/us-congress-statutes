# Chapter 545

> An Act To authorize Captain F. A. Traut, United States Navy, to accept a decoration from the King of Denmark known as the “Order of Dannebrog.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Captain F. A. Traut, of the United States Navy, be, and he is hereby, authorized to accept from the King of Denmark a decoration known as the “ Order of Dannebrog,” which was bestowed upon him by King Christian when the United States steamship Utah was on a cruise in European waters.


*Approved, June 10, 1926.*

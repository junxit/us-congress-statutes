# Chapter 626

> An Act For the relief of William J. Murphy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representativest of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Post-master General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the accounts of William J. Murphy, late postmaster at Cleveland, Ohio, in the sum of $3,209.33 due the United States on account of the loss of postal funds resulting from larceny and embezzlement.


*Approved, June 18, 1926.*

# Chapter 118

> An Act For the relief of Gordan A. Dennis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of all laws conferring rights, privileges, or benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Gordan A. Dennis, late of the Twentieth Infantry, shall be held to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States on May 5, 1909: Provided, That no back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 12, 1927.*

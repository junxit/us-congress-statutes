# Chapter 407

> An Act For the relief of Charles A. Mayo, T. S. Taylor, and Frank Hickey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of die Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Charles A. Mayo the sum of $1,120, to T. S. Taylor the sum of $90, and to Frank Hickey the sum of $90, on account of property lost by fire at the United States reclamation camp at Rimrock, Washington, May 11, 1920.


*Approved, March 3, 1927.*

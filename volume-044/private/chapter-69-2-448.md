# Chapter 448

> An Act For the relief of Gilbert B. Perkins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Gilbert B. Perkins, the sum of $225.25 as reimbursement for payment of a supervision fee which was collected by the clerk of the United States Circuit Court of Appeals for the Third Circuit.


*Approved, March 3, 1927.*

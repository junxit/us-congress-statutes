# Chapter 882

> An Act To provide payment for services rendered in preparation for the international conference on traffic in habit-forming narcotic drugs.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of State be authorized to pay from the appropriation “International conferences—traffic in habit-forming drugs, 1925,” the sum of $1,950 to Mrs. E. W. Wright for services rendered to the Department of State during the period from December 1, 1923, to June 15, 1924 in preparing and assembling data for the use of the American delegates to the international conferences on the traffic in habit-forming drugs.


*Approved, July 3, 1926.*

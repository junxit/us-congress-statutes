# Chapter 679

> An Act For the relief of A. B. Ewing.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the account of A. B. Ewing, postmaster at El Dorado, Kansas, in the sum of $29,895.06, due to the United States on account of postage stamps, Treasury saving funds, and war-tax revenue stamps which were lost as the result of burglary on December 5, 1923.


*Approved, June 25, 1926.*

# Chapter 359

> An Act Permitting the sale of lot 9, sixteen and sixty-three one-hundredths acres, in section 31, township 2 south, range 17 west, in Bay County, Florida, to P. C. Black.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to sell to P. C. Blade lot 9, sixteen and sixty-three one-hundredths acres, in section 31, township 2 south, range 17 west, Tallahassee meridian, in Bay County, Florida, at the rate of $1.25 per acre.


*Approved, May 21, 1926.*

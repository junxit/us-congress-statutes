# Chapter 468

> An Act For the relief of the Rochester Merchandise Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Souse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, and in full settlement against the Government, to the Rochester Merchandise Company, of Rochester, New York, the sum of $780, being refund of purchase price paid to the War Department for truck bodies sold to the said Rochester Merchandise Company at public auction, but not delivered.


*Approved, June 3, 1926.*

# Chapter 481

> An Act For the relief of Fannie Kravitz.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Con press assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, and in full settlement against the Government, the sum of $3,000 to Fannie Kravitz, of New Haven, Connecticut, in compensation for injuries sustained November 12, 1923, in the city of New Haven, Connecticut, when struck by a United States Navy motor vehicle.


*Approved, March 3, 1927.*

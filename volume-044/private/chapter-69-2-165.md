# Chapter 165

> An Act Authorizing Dominic I. Murphy, consul general of the United States of America, to accept a silver fruit bowl presented to him by the British Government.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Dominic I. Murphy, A consul general of the United States or America, be, and he is hereby, authorized to accept and receive a silver fruit bowl bearing on one side an engraved crest of the British Government and on the other the words “ Presented to Dominic I. Murphy by His Majesty’s Government, November 11, 1918,” the bowl being now in the custody of the Secretary of State.


*Approved, February 21, 1927.*

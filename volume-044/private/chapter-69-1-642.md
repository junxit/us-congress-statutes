# Chapter 642

> An Act For the relief of A. T. Marix.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $1,500 to reimburse A. T, Marix, colonel, retired, United States Marine Corps, for the loss by fire of personal property in Christiania, Norway, while he was serving as naval attaché to the American legations in Norway, Sweden, and Denmark.


*Approved, June 21, 1926.*

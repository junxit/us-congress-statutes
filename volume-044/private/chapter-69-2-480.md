# Chapter 480

> An Act For the relief of Henry F. Downing.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $80.20 to pay Henry F. Downing as bounty due him for services rendered in the United States Navy during the Civil War.


*Approved, March 3, 1927.*

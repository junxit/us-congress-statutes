# Chapter 548

> An Act For the relief of the estate of William Fries, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the account of William Fries, formerly postmaster at Alton, Illinois, in the sum of $35,307.80, due to the United States on account of postage stamps and war revenue stamps which were lost as the result of burglary on May 12, 1924.


*Approved, June 10, 1926.*

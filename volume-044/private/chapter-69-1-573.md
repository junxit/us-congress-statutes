# Chapter 573

> An Act For the relief of Gershon Brothers Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $5,078.11 to Gershon Brothers Company, of Atlanta, Georgia, as just compensation and in full settlement and satisfaction of its damages and loss incurred and suffered by it when the building which it occupied was, on December 3, 1918, under authority of the National Defense Act, commandeered and taken possession of by the United States Government.


*Approved, June 12, 1926.*

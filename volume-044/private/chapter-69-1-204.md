# Chapter 204

> An Act To issue a patent to John H. Bolton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to issue a patent to John H. Bolton, of Cowley County, Kansas, the following described property, to wit: The southeast quarter of the northwest quarter of section 3, township 33 south of range 6 east, upon payment thereof at the rate of $1.25 per acre.


*Approved, April 30, 1926.*

# Chapter 422

> An Act For the relief of Delmore A. Teller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the. United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to allow and credit in the accounts of Delmore A. Teller (formerly captain, Quartermaster Corps, United States Army) the sum of $770, representing public funds for which he was accountable and which were stolen on or about January 31, 1919, and to reimburse him in such amount as he has refunded to the United States to make good the theft of these public funds.


*Approved, March 3, 1927.*

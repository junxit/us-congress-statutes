# Chapter 869

> An Act For the relief of Rachel Thomas, widow of William Thomas, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, William Thomas, deceased, who was a member of Company A, Fifth United States Artillery, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a private of that organization on the 23d day of June, 1865: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, July 3, 1926.*

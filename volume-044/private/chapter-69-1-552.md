# Chapter 552

> An Act For the relief of Cyrus Durey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the General Accounting Office is hereby authorized and directed to allow credit in the account of Collector of Internal Revenue Cyrus Durey, fourteenth New York district, in the sum of $499.25, to cover disallowances due to payments made to Deputy Collector Manning Rested for subsistence expenses and car fare incurred in the months of October, November, and December, 1923, and January, February. March, April, and July, 1924.


*Approved, June 10, 1926.*

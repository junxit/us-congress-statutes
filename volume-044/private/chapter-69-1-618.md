# Chapter 618

> An Act For the relief of Ada Brown-Hopkins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a patent be issued to lots 1, 3, 4, and 9, section 22, township 15 north, range 6 east, Craighead County, Arkansas, to Ada Brown-Hopkins upon her compliance with the homestead law: Provided, That patent to the land may be issued to Ada-Brown-Hopkins upon the payment by her of $1.25 per acre for the land within six months from the date of the enactment of this Act, without further proof.


*Approved, June 17, 1926.*

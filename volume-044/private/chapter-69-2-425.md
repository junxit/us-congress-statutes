# Chapter 425

> An Act For the relief of Olof Nelson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Post-master General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the account of Olof Nelson, postmaster at Yankton, South Dakota, with $20,489.14 on account of loss sustained in the burglary of the post office on October 12, 1926.


*Approved, March 3, 1927.*

# Chapter 287

> An Act For the relief of William W. Green, warrant officer, United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War is authorized and directed to pay to Warrant Officer William W. Green, out of any appropriation now or hereafter available for “pay, and so forth, of the Army,” the sum of $7,897.80, to reimburse him for pay, for additional pay to officers for length of service, for rental allowance, and for subsistence allowance, withheld on account of absence from his post of duty from January 1, 1923, to September 15, 1925, such absence having been caused by his arrest, conviction, and confinement in the State of North Carolina for an offense against such State, for which he has been unconditionally pardoned by the Governor of North Carolina.


*Approved, March 2, 1927.*

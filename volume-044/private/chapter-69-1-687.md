# Chapter 687

> An Act For the relief of Lieutenant (Junior Grade) Thomas J. Ryan, United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $1,221.65, to reimburse Lieutenant (Junior Grade) Thomas J. Ryan, United States Navy, for the loss of uniforms, equipment, clothing, and personal effects of himself, as a result of the earthquake and fire disaster in Japan on September 1, 1923.


*Approved, June 25, 1926.*

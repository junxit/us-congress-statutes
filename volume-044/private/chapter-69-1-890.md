# Chapter 890

> An Act For the relief of Joseph F. MacKnight.

*Be if enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Joseph F. MacKnight, postmaster at Price, Utah, the sum of $311.94, being the amount embezzled by a subordinate in the post office at Price, Utah.


*Approved, July 3, 1926.*

# Chapter 541

> An Act For the relief of Harry McNeil.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $1,000 to Harry McNeil, of San Francisco, California, being a refund on account of the forfeiture of a Liberty bond and which through error on the part of the clerk of the United States District Court of San Francisco was deposited in the Treasury of the United States.


*Approved, June 10, 1926.*

# Chapter 535

> An Act For the relief of L. W. Burford.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General be, and he is hereby, directed to allow credit in the account of L. W. Burford, formerly receiver of public moneys and special disbursing agent at the United States Land Office, Del Norte, Colorado, the sum of $66.35, being the amount disallowed by the Comptroller General under certificate of settlement C–9714–I, April 29, 1924, under the appropriation “Contingent expenses of land offices,” for payment of firewood used in the United States Land Office at Del Norte, Colorado.


*Approved, June 10, 1926.*

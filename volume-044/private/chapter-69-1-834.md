# Chapter 834

> An Act For the relief of Charles D. Baylis, first lieutenant, United States Marine Corps.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is appropriated out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appro-priated, to be paid to Charles D. Baylis, first lieutenant, United States Marine Corps, $1,732.14, to compensate him for loss and damage to household goods, clothing, and other personal effects, while the same were being transported on the United States naval tug Genesee from Olongapo to Cavite, Philippine Islands, during the summer of 1922.


*Approved, July 3, 1926.*

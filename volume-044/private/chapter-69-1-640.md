# Chapter 640

> An Act For the relief of the Andrew Radel Oyster Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Howe of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States Court of Claims be, and it is hereby, authorized and directed to hear and determine the claim of the Andrew Radel Oyster Company for compensation for alleged damage sustained by the said Andrew Radel Oyster Company by reason of the destruction of certain oyster beds located in Raritan Bay, about two miles southwesterly of Old Orchard Light, by a dredge of the United States War Department, on June 19, 1923.


*Approved, June 21, 1926.*

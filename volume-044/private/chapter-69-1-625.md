# Chapter 625

> An Act For the relief of James E. Simpson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Post-master General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the accounts of James E. Simpson, late postmaster at Collinsville, Illinois, in the sum of $13,190.09 due the United States on account of the loss of postal funds resulting from burglary December 18,1920.


*Approved, June 18, 1926.*

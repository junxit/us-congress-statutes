# Chapter 401

> An Act To provide for the appointment of a Commissioner of Reclamation, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Howe of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That under the supervision and direction of the Secretary of the Interior, the reclamation of arid lands, under the Act of June 17, 1902, and Acts amendatory thereof and supplementary thereto, shall be administered by a Commissioner of Reclamation, who shall receive a salary of $10,000 per annum, and who shall be appointed by the President.


*Approved, May 26, 1926.*

# Chapter 692

> An Act To amend the Act of Congress approved March 4, 1913 (Thirty-seventh Statutes at Large, page 876).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in carrying out that provision in the Act of Congress approved March 4, 1913 (Thirty-seventh Statutes at Large, page 876), which authorized the construction of a “ United States post office and land office at Chamberlain, South Dakota,” upon a site to be acquired for that purpose, the Secretary of the Treasury may have said building so constructed as to omit accommodations for the land office.


*Approved, June 26, 1926.*

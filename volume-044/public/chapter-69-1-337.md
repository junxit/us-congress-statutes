# Chapter 337

> An Act Extending the provisions of section 2455 of the United States Revised Statutes to ceded lands of the Fort Hall Indian Reservation.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of section 2455, United States Revised Statutes, as amended by the Act of June 27, 1906 (Thirty-fourth Statutes at Large, page 517), and by the Act of March 28, 1912 (Thirty-seventh Statutes at Large, page 77), are made applicable to the ceded lands on the former Fort Hall Indian Reservation: Provided, That no land shall be sold at less than the price fixed by the law opening the lands to homestead entry.


*Approved, May 19, 1926.*

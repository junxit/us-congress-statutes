# Chapter 370

> An Act Providing an additional wing to the District Jail.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioners of the District of Columbia be, and they are hereby, authorized to construct an additional building at the District Jail and to rearrange the interior construction of the east wing of the present jail building so as to provide accommodations for not less than two hundred additional prisoners at a total cost not exceeding $300,000.


*Approved, May 22, 1926.*

# Chapter 227

> An Act Amending section 3 of the Act approved January 12, 1923, entitled “An Act to distribute the commissioned line and engineer officers of the Coast Guard in grades, and for other purposes.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3 of the Act approved January 12, 1923, entitled “An Act to distribute the commissioned line and engineer officers of the Coast Guard in grades, and for other purposes,” be amended by adding thereto the following proviso:

> “Provided further, That commissioned officers Daniel P. Foley, Francis M. Dunwoody, Howard M. Broadbent, Frank H. Newcomb, and Horace B. West, shall have the rank of commodore on the retired list without any increase of pay by reason of the passage of this Act.”


*Approved, February 28, 1927.*

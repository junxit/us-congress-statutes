# Chapter 154

> An Act Extending the provisions of an Act for the relief of settlers and entrymen on Baca Float Numbered 3, in the State of Arizona.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time within which to make selections and entries under the provisions of the Act of July 5, 1921 (Forty-second Statutes at Large, page 107), entitled “An Act for the relief of settlers and entrymen on Baca Float Numbered 3, in the State of Arizona,” is hereby extended for a period of two years from the approval of this Act.


*Approved, April 17, 1926.*

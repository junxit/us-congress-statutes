# Chapter 587

> An Act Limiting the creation or extension of forest reserves in New Mexico and Arizona.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That hereafter no forest reservation shall be created, nor shall any additions be made to one heretofore created, within the limits of the States of New-Mexico and Arizona except by Act of Congress.


*Approved, June 15, 1926.*

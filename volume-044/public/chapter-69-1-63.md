# Chapter 63

> An Act To provide for the withdrawal of certain lands as a camp ground for the pupils of the Indian school at Phoenix, Arizona.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the east half southwest quarter, and the southeast quarter section 20; the north half northeast quarter section 29, all in township 3 north, range 3 east, Gila and Salt River meridian, Arizona, temporarily withdrawn from settlement, entry, sale, Or other disposal by presidential order dated February 27, 1925, for use as a camp ground for the pupils of the United States Indian school at Phoenix, Arizona, be, and they hereby are, permanently withdrawn for the purpose indicated in said order: Provided, That this withdrawal shall not affect any existing legal right of any person to any of the withdrawn lands.


*Approved, March 22, 1926.*

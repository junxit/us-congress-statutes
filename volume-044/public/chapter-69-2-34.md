# Chapter 34

> Joint Resolution To correct a misnomer contained in the Act to fix the salaries of certain judges of the United States.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of December 13, 1926, entitled “An Act to fix the salaries of certain judges of the United States,” be, and it is hereby, amended by striking out the words “ To each of the members of the Board of General Appraisers, which board ” and inserting in lieu thereof the words “ To the chief justice and associate justices of the United States Customs Court, which court ”.


*Approved, January 14, 1927.*

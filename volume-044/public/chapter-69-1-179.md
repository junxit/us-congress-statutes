# Chapter 179

> An Act Authorizing the Secretary of the Navy, in his discretion, to deliver to the custody of the State of North Dakota the silver service which was presented to the battleship North Dakota by the citizens of that State.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy is authorized, in his discretion, to deliver to the custody of the State of North Dakota, for preservation and exhibition, the silver service which was presented to the Battleship North Dakota by the citizens of that State: Provided, That no expense shall be incurred by the United States for the delivery of such silver service.


*Approved, April 24, 1926.*

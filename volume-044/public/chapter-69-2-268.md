# Chapter 268

> An Act To extend the time for constructing a bridge across the Rainy River, approximately midway between the village of Spooner, in the county of Lake of the Woods, State of Minnesota, and the village of Rainy River, Province of Ontario, Canada.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time for commencing and completing the construction of the bridge authorized by the Act of Congress, approved March 4, 1925, to be built across the Rainy River, between the village of Spooner, Lake of the Woods County, State of Minnesota, and the village of Rainy River, Province of Ontario, Canada, is hereby extended for two years from the date of approval hereof.

## § 2.

That the right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is expressly reserved.


*Approved, March 2, 1927.*

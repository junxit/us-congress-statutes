# Chapter 299

> An Act To repeal section 806 of the Revenue Act of 1926.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That suction 806 of the Revenue Act of 1926 be, and is hereby, repealed.


*Approved, May 14, 1926.*

# Chapter 156

> An Act Authorizing the President to restore Commander George M. Baum, United States Navy, to a place on the list of commanders of the Navy to rank next after Commander David W. Bagley, United States Navy.

*Be it it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and lie is hereby, authorized to restore Commander George M. Baum, United States Navy, to a place on the list of commanders of the Navy to rank next after Commander David W. Bagley, United States Navy: Provided, That the said George M. Baum shall be an additional number in the grade of commander, and to any grade to which he may hereafter be promoted.


*Approved, February 16, 1927.*

# Chapter 765

> An Act To authorize the granting of leave to ex-service men and women employed in the municipal government of the District of Columbia to attend the annual convention of the American Legion in Paris, France, in 1927.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of the Act approved May 20, 1926, entitled “An Act to authorize the granting of leave to ex-service men and women to attend the annual convention of the American Legion in Paris, France, in 1927,” shall include ex-service men and women employed in the municipal government of the District of Columbia.


*Approved, July 3, 1926.*

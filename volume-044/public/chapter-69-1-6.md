# Chapter 6

> An Act To amend an Act entitled “An Act to create a Library of Congress Trust Fund Board, and for other purposes,” approved March 3, 1925.

*Be it enacted By the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first sentence in section 3 of the Act entitled “An Act to create a Library of Congress Trust Fund Board, and for other purposes,” approved March 3, 1925, is amended to read as follows:

> “The board shall have perpetual succession, with all the usual powers and obligations of a trustee, including the power to sell, except as herein limited, in respect of all property, moneys, or securities which shall be conveyed, transferred, assigned, bequeathed, delivered, or paid over to it for the purposes above specified.”

## § 2.

Section 6 of said Act is amended by striking out the comma after the word “undertakings”.


*Approved, January 27, 1926.*

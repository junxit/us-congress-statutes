# Chapter 244

> An Act To amend section 107 of the Judicial Code.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the seventh sentence of section 107 of the Judicial Code is amended to read as follows:

> “Terms of the district court for the western division of said district shall be held at Memphis on the first Mondays in April and October; and for the eastern division, at Jackson, on the fourth Mondays in March and September.”


*Approved, March 1, 1927.*

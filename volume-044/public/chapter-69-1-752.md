# Chapter 752

> An Act To authorize notaries public and other State officers to administer oaths required by the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That in all cases in which, under the laws of the United States, oaths are authorized or required to be administered, they may be administered by notaries public duly appointed in any State, District, or Territory of the United States, by clerks and prothonotaries of courts of record of any such State, District, or Territory, by the deputies of such clerks and prothonotaries, and by ail magistrates authorized by the laws of or pertaining to any such State, District, or Territory to administer oaths.


*Approved, July 3, 1926.*

# Chapter 87

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the Federal Reserve Bank of New York to invest its funds in the purchase of a site and the building now standing thereon for its branch office at Buffalo, New York.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Federal Reserve Bank of New York is hereby authorized to invest in the purchase of land improved by a bank building, already fully constructed. for its branch office at Buffalo, New York, a sum not to exceed $600,000, out of its paid-in capital stock and surplus.


*Approved, March 24, 1926.*

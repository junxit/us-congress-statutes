# Chapter 252

> An Act To amend sections 4 and 5 of the Act entitled “An Act granting the consent of Congress to the Gallia County Ohio River Bridge Company and its successors and assigns, to construct a bridge across the Ohio River at or near Gallipolis, Ohio,” approved May 13, 1926, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 4 and 5 of the Act entitled “An Act granting the consent of Congress to the Gallia County Ohio River Bridge Company and its successors and assigns, to construct a bridge across the Ohio River at or near Gallipolis, Ohio,” approved May 13, 1926, as amended, are amended by striking out the word “twenty” wherever it occurs in such sections and by inserting in lieu thereof the word “twenty-five.”


*Approved, March 2, 1927.*

# Chapter 507

> An Act To authorize the refund of $25,000 to the Columbia Hospital for Women and Lying-in Asylum.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioners of the District of Columbia are authorized and directed to pay to the Columbia Hospital for Women and Lying-in Asylum the sum of $25,000 as a contribution to the maintenance of that hospital.

## § 2.

That there is authorized to be appropriated to carry out the provisions of this Act the sum of $25,000, payable from the revenues of the District of Columbia and in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 4, 1927.*

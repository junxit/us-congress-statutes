# Chapter 763

> An Act To authorize the transfer of surplus books from the Navy Department to the Interior Department.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy is hereby authorized to transfer such books as may not be required for the uses of the Navy Department to the Interior Department as the Secretary of the Interior may consider useful for educational purposes at the Indian school at Santa Fe, New Mexico, and other Indian schools throughout the United States.


*Approved, July 3, 1926.*

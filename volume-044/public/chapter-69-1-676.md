# Chapter 676

> An Act To increase the limit of cost of submarine tender numbered 3, and to authorize repairs and alterations to the United States ship S-48.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limit of cost of submarine tender numbered 3, heretofore authorized, is increased from $4,800,000 to $5,000,000; and repairs and alterations to the United States ship S-48 are hereby authorized to cost not to exceed $1,080,000.


*Approved, June 25, 1926.*

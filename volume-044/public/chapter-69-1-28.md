# Chapter 28

> An Act To extend the time for the construction of a bridge across the Mississippi River at or near the village of Clearwater, Minnesota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge authorized by Act of Congress approved March 4, 1925, to be built by the State of Minnesota and the counties of Sherburne and Wright across the Mississippi River at or near the village of Clearwater in the county of Wright, in the State of Minnesota, are hereby extended to March 4, 1927, and March 4, 1929, respectively.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, February 26, 1926.*

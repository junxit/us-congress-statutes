# Chapter 194

> An Act To authorize the United States Veterans Bureau to accept a title to lands required for a hospital site in Rapides Parish, Louisiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Director of the United States Veterans’ Bureau be, and he is hereby, authorized, in the acquisition of any lands required for a hospital site in Rapides Parish, Louisiana, to accept title to such lands subject to a reservation of the mineral rights of the State of Louisiana.


*Approved, February 25, 1927.*

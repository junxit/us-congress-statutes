# Chapter 135

> An Act Authorizing the Secretary of the Navy, in his discretion, to deliver to the president of the Rotary Club, of Crawsfordsville, Montgomery County, Indiana, a bell of a battleship that is now, or may be, in his custody.

*Be it it enacted by the Senate and House of Representative* of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized, in his discretion, to deliver to the president of the Rotary Club, of Crawfordsville, Montgomery County, Indiana, a bell of a battleship that is now, or may be, in his custody: Provided, That no expense shall be incurred by the United States through the delivery of said bell.


*Approved, February 14, 1927.*

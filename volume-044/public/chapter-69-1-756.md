# Chapter 756

> An Act For the relief of the State of North Carolina.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the State of North Carolina and the United States property and disbursing officer of the National Guard of the State of North Carolina are hereby relieved from accountability for certain property belonging to the United States, of the value of $1,904.39, constituting a part of the property lost, as shown in reports of survey of the War Department, dated September 9, 1925.


*Approved, July 3, 1926.*

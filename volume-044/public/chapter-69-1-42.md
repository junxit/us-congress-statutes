# Chapter 42

> An Act Extending the time for the construction of the bridge across the Mississippi River in Ramsey and Hennepin Counties, Minnesota, by the Chicago, Milwaukee and Saint Paul Railway.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commenting and completing the construction of the bridge authorized by Act of Congress approved February 16, 1924, and amended by Act approved February 7, 1925, to be built by the Chicago, Milwaukee and Saint Paul Railway, its successors and assigns, across the Mississippi River, within or near the city limits of Saint Paul, Ramsey County, and Minneapolis, Hennepin County, Minnesota, are hereby extended two years and four years, respectively, from February 16, 1926.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, March 1, 1926.*

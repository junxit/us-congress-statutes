# Chapter 210

> Joint Resolution To provide for membership of the United States in the Central Bureau of the International Map of the World.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That to enable the United States to become a member of the Central Bureau of the International Map of the World there is hereby authorized to be appropriated the sum of $30 for the payment of a contribution by the United States toward the expenses of the said bureau for the calendar year 1926.


*Approved, May 1, 1926.*

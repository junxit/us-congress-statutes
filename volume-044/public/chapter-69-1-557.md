# Chapter 557

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the Joint Committee on the Library to procure an oil portrait of the late President Warren G. Harding.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Joint Committee on the Library is hereby authorized to procure an oil portrait of the late President Warren G. Harding for the Executive Mansion, at a cost not to exceed $2,500.


*Approved, June 11, 1926.*

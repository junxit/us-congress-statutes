# Chapter 531

> An Act To provide for the construction of ten vessels for the Coast Guard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated not to exceed $9,000,000, to be expended by the Secretary of the Treasury, for the construction and equipment of ten Coast Guard cutters, to be designed and equipped for Coast Guard duties: Provided, That the equipment be bought in open competition.


*Approved, June 10, 1926.*

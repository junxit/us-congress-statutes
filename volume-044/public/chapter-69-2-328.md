# Chapter 328

> An Act To provide a water system for the Indians of the Reno-Sparks Indian Colony, Nevada.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, not to exceed $6,000 to enable the Secretary of the Interior to provide a water system for the Indians of the Reno-Sparks Indian Colony, near Reno, Nevada.


*Approved, March 3, 1927.*

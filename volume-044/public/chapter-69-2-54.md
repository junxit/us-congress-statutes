# Chapter 54

> An Act To provide for the purchase of land for use in connection with Camp Marfa, Texas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a sum not to exceed $27,000 is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the acquisition of the fee title to land in the vicinity of and in connection with the present military reservation at Camp Marfa, Texas, and the Secretary of War is hereby empowered and authorized to acquire said land either by purchase or condemnation.


*Approved, January 22, 1927.*

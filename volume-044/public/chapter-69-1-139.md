# Chapter 139

> An Act Authorizing the payment of tuition of Crow Indian children attending Montana State public schools.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That nothing contained in the provisions of section 16 of the Act of June 4, 1920, Public, Numbered 239, shall be construed to preclude the payment of tuition for Crow Indian children enrolled and educated in Montana State public schools, pursuant to annual or existing appropriations of public money for payment of such tuition.


*Approved, April 14, 1926.*

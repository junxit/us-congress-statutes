# House Concurrent Resolution 320

> “ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY EDUCATION ACT OF 1970”—COMMITTEE HEARINGS

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That six hundred additional copies of the hearings before the Select Subcommittee on Education of the Committee on Education and Labor of the House of Representatives, Ninety-first Congress, second session, on H.R. 14753, entitled “Environmental Quality Education Act of 1970”, be printed for the use of the Select Subcommittee on Education.


*Passed September 24, 1971.*

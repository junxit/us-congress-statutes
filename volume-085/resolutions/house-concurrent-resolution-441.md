# House Concurrent Resolution 441

> “THE JOINT COMMITTEE ON CONGRESSIONAL OPERATIONS: PURPOSE, LEGISLATIVE HISTORY, JURISDICTION, AND RULES”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed as a House document, with illustrations and with a suitable cover approved by the Joint Committee on Printing, a compilation of materials entitled “The Joint Committee on Congressional Operations: Purpose, Legislative History, Jurisdiction, and Rules”; and that there shall be printed two thousand five hundred additional copies of such compilation for the use of the Joint Committee on Congressional Operations.


*Passed December 17, 1971.*

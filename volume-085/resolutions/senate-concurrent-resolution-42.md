# Senate Concurrent Resolution 42

> CORRECTION OF ENROLLED BILL H. R. 4713

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That in the enrollment of H.R. 4713, the Clerk of the House of Representatives be authorized to delete Senate amendment numbered 5, which inserts at page 3, after the second line following line 6, a new section 7.


*Passed September 28, 1971.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 415

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

*Resolved, by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Thursday, October 7, 1971, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Tuesday, October 12, 1971.


*Passed October 7, 1971.*

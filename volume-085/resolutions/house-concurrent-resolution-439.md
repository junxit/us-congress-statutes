# House Concurrent Resolution 439

> “A PRIMER ON MONEY”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That fifty thousand additional copies of the Subcommittee Print of the Subcommittee on Domestic Finance of the Committee on Banking and Currency, of the House of Representatives, Eighty-eighth Congress, second session, entitled “A Primer on Money” be printed for the use of the Committee on Banking and Currency of the House of Representatives.


*Passed December 17, 1971.*

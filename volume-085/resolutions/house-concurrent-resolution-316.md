# House Concurrent Resolution 316

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House of Representatives adjourns on Thursday, May 27, 1971, and when the Senate adjourns on May 26, 1971, they stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Tuesday, June 1, 1971.


*Passed May 25, 1971.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 395

> JOINT MEETING

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Thursday, September 9, 1971, at 12:30 p.m., for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed September 8, 1971.*

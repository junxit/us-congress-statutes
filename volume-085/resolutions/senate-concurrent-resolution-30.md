# Senate Concurrent Resolution 30

> “SOVIET SPACE PROGRAMS, 1966–70”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the study entitled “Soviet Space Programs, 1966–70”, prepared for the use of the Senate Committee on Aeronautical and Space Sciences by the Congressional Research Service with the cooperation of the Law Library, Library of Congress, be printed with illustrations as a Senate document, and that there be printed three thousand additional copies of such document for the use of that committee.


*Passed December 9, 1971.*

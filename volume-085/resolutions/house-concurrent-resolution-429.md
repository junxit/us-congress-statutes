# House Concurrent Resolution 429

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved, by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Thursday, October 21, 1971, the House stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Tuesday, October 26, 1971, and the Senate until 11 antemeridian on the same day.


*Passed October 21, 1971.*

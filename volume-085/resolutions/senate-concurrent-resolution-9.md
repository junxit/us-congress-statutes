# Senate Concurrent Resolution 9

> “INVESTIGATION INTO ELECTRONIC BATTLEFIELD PROGRAM”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on Armed Services two thousand additional copies of the hearings before the Electronic Battlefield Subcommittee of the Preparedness Investigating Subcommittee during the Ninety-first Congress, second session, entitled “Investigation Into Electronic Battlefield Program”.


*Passed June 23, 1971.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 498

> ADJOURNMENT SINE DIE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Friday, December 17, 1971, and that when they adjourn on said day, they stand adjourned sine die.


*Passed December 17, 1971.*

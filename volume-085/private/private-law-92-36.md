# Private Law 92–36

> AN ACT For the relief of Eddie Troy Jaynes, Junior, and Rosa Elena Jaynes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Eddie Troy Jaynes, Junior, and Rosa Elena Jaynes shall lie held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of May 2, 1964, upon payment of the required visa fees.


*Approved November 24, 1971.*

# Private Law 92–37

> AN ACT For the relief of Chen-Pai Miao.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, Chen-Pai Miao, who was lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence on August 30, 1966, shall be held and considered not to be within the classes of persons whose naturalization is prohibited by the provisions of section 313 of the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved November 24, 1971.*

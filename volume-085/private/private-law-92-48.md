# Private Law 92–48

> AN ACT For the relief of Nemesio Gomez-Sanchez.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 101(a)(27)(B) of the Immigration and Nationality Act Nemesio Gomez-Sanchez shall be held and considered to be a returning resident alien.


*Approved December 2, 1971.*

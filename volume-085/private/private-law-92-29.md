# Private Law 92–29

> AN ACT For the relief of Park Jung Ok.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, section 204(c) of that Act, relating to the number of petitions which may be approved in behalf of children, shall be inapplicable in the case of a petition filed in behalf of Park Jung Ok by Mr. and Mrs. Harold David, citizens of the United States. The natural brothers and sisters of the said Park Jung Ok shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved October 14, 1971.*

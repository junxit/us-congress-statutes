# Public Law 92–16

> AN ACT To amend the Small Business Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph (4) of section 4(c) of the Small Business Act is amended by striking out “$2,200,000,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$3,100,000,000”.


*Approved May 18, 1971.*

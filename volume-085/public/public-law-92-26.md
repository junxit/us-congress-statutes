# Public Law 92–26

> JOINT RESOLUTION Designating the last full week in July of 1971 as “National Star Route Mail Carriers Week”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating the last full week in July of 1971 as “National Star Route Mail Carriers Week” and calling upon the Postal Service to observe such week with appropriate recognition to the Nation’s star route mail carriers.


*Approved June 4, 1971.*

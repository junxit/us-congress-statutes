# Public Law 92–135

> AN ACT To amend further the Peace Corps Act (75 Stat. 612), as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3(b) of the Peace Corps Act (22 U.S.C. 2502(b)), which authorizes appropriations to carry out the purposes of that Act, is amended by striking out “1971” and “$98,800,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “1972” and “$77,200,000”, respectively.


*Approved October 8, 1971.*

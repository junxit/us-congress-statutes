# Public Law 92–144

> AN ACT To amend the tobacco marketing quota provisions of the Agricultural Adjustment Act of 1938, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 318(b) of the Agricultural Adjustment Act of 1938, as amended, is amended by inserting in clause (1) following the word “county” the following “: Provided, That in the case of Virginia fire-cured tobacco type 21 and Virginia sun-cured tobacco type 37, any such transfer may be made to a farm in another county in the same State”.


*Approved October 23, 1971.*

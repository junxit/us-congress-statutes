# Public Law 92–118

> AN ACT To authorize appropriations for procurement of vessels and aircraft and construction of shore and offshore establishments for the Coast Guard, and to authorize the annual active duty personnel strength of the Coast Guard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That funds are hereby authorized to be appropriated for fiscal year 1972 for the use of the Coast Guard as follows:

## vessels

For procurement and increasing capability of vessels, $132,446,000.

- A. Procurement:
  - design of vessels.
  - one replacement polar icebreaker.
  - three nigh-endurance cutters.
None of the vessels authorized herein shall be procured from other than shipyards and facilities within the United States.

- B. Increasing capability:
  - increase fuel capacity and improve habitability on high-endurance cutters of the three-hundred-and-twenty-seven-foot class.
  - rehabilitate and improve selected buoy tenders.
  - modernize and improve wind class polar icebreakers.
  - (.4) reweld hull and repair cutter (polar icebreaker) Glacier.
  - increase oceanographic capability of cutter Evergreen.
  - modernize communications capability in selected vessels.
  - replace radio teletypes in selected high-endurance vessels.
  - install water pollution control equipment in vessels.
  - install water pollution monitoring sensors in vessels.

## aircraft

For procurement and extension of service life of aircraft. $41,574,000.

- Procurement:
  - five long-range search aircraft.
  - six medium-range helicopters.
  - one administrative aircraft.
- Extension of service life:
  - repair outer wings on six HC–130 aircraft.
  - replace center wing box beam on three HC–130 aircraft.
  - reactivate six HU–16E aircraft.
  - install water pollution monitoring sensors in aircraft.

## bridge alterations

For payment to bridge owners for the cost of alteration of railroad and public highway bridges to permit free navigation of the navigable waters of the United States, $3,000,000.

## annual active duty personnel strength

For the fiscal year beginning July 1, 1971, and ending June 30, 1972, the average active duty personnel strength of the Coast Guard shall be 38,284. If the Coast Guard Selected Reserve program is not phased out as planned in the budget, the authorized active duty personnel strength is increased 567 men to 38,851; except when the President of the United States determines that the application of these ceilings will seriously jeopardize the national security interests of the United States and informs the Congress of the basis of such determination.


*Approved August 13, 1971.*

# Public Law 92–215

> AN ACT To authorize an additional Assistant Secretary of Defense.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 136(a) of title 10, United States Code, is amended by striking out “eight” and inserting in lieu thereof “nine”.

## § 2.

Section 5315 (13) of title 5, United States Code, is amended to read as follows:

> - **(13)** Assistant Secretaries of Defense (9).”.


*Approved December 22, 1971.*

# Public Law 92–133

> AN ACT To extend the authority for insuring loans under the Consolidated Farmers Home Administration Act of 1961.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 308 of the Consolidated Farmers Home Administration Act of 1961 is amended by striking out “until October 1, 1971, loans” and inserting in lieu thereof “Loans”.


*Approved October 5, 1971.*

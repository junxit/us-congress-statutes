# Public Law 92–120

> AN ACT To add California-grown peaches as a commodity eligible for any form of promotion, including paid advertising, under a marketing order.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 8c(6) (I) of the Agricultural Adjustment Act (as reenacted by the Agricultural Marketing Agreement Act of 1937, and as subsequently amended (7 U.S.C. 608c(6)(I)), is hereby amended by inserting “California-grown peaches,” immediately after “applicable to almonds,”.


*Approved August 13, 1971.*

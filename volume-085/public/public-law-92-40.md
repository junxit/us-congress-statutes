# Public Law 92–40

> AN ACT To amend the Social Security Act in order to continue for two years the temporary assistance program for United States citizens returned from abroad.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1113(d) of the Social Security Act is amended by striking out “1971” and inserting in lieu thereof “1973”.


*Approved July 1, 1971.*

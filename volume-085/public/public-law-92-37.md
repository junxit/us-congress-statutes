# Public Law 92–37

> JOINT RESOLUTION To provide a temporary extension of the authority conferred by the Export Administration Act of 1969.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 14 of the Export Administration Act of 1969 is amended by striking out “June 30, 1971” and inserting “October 31, 1971”.


*Approved June 30, 1971.*

# Public Law 92–79

> AN ACT To amend the maritime Men provisions of the Ship Mortgage Act of 1920.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Ship Mortgage Act, 1920 (46 U.S.C. 911–984) is amended as follows: By striking from subsection R thereof (4G U.S.C. 973) the semicolon, substituting a period therefor and deleting all thereafter.


*Approved August 10, 1971.*

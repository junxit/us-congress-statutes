# Public Law 92–3

> JOINT RESOLUTION Authorizing the President to proclaim the second week of March 1971 as Volunteers of America Week.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating the second week of March 1971 as Volunteers of America Week, and urging the people of the United States, upon the occasion of the seventy-fifth anniversary of the Volunteers of America, to express their gratitude for its untiring and selfless work and to continue their support of its humanitarian activities.


*Approved March 8, 1971.*

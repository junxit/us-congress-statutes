# Public Law 92–71

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making further continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 1972, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That clause (c) of section 102 of the joint resolution of July 1, 1971 (Public Law 92–38), is hereby amended by striking out “August 6, 1971” and inserting in lieu thereof “October 15, 1971”: Provided, That obligations may be incurred for the activities of the Federal Power Commission from July 1, 1971, in anticipation of appropriations for the fiscal year 1972, and are hereby ratified and confirmed if otherwise in accord with the applicable terms of Public Law 92–38, as amended.


*Approved August 9, 1971.*

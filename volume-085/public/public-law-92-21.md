# Public Law 92–21

> AN ACT To amend the Act to authorize appropriations for the fiscal year 1971 for certain maritime programs of the Department of Commerce.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of May 13, 1970 (84 Stat. 207; Public Law 91–247) is amended by striking out of paragraph (b) the figure $193,000,000 and inserting in lieu thereof the figure $273,000,000.


*Approved June 1, 1971.*

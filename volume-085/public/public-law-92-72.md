# Public Law 92–72

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making an appropriation for the Department of Labor for the fiscal year 1972, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the following sum is appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1972, namely:

## DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

## Manpower Administration

## emergency employment assistance

For expenses necessary to carry into effect the Emergency Employment Act of 1971, $1,000,000,000, of which not to exceed $50,000,000 shall be available for program direction and support, administration of the program at the local level, and for agent assistance and statistics, to remain available until June 30, 1973.


*Approved August 9, 1971.*

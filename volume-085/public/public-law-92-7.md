# Public Law 92–7

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making certain further continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 1971, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That clause (c) of section 102 of the joint resolution of June 29, 1970 (Public Law 91–294, as amended by Public Laws 91–370, 91–454, and 91–645), is hereby further amended by striking out “March 30, 1971” and inserting in lieu thereof “June 30, 1971”: Provided,That projects and activities (other than those financed under the appropriation “Civil Supersonic Aircraft Development”) provided for in the Department of Transportation and Related Agencies Appropriation Act, 1971 (H.R. 17755, Ninety-first Congress), may be conducted at a rate for operations, and to the extent and in the manner, provided for in such Act as modified by the House of Representatives on December 15, 1970.

## § 2.

None of the funds provided by this joint resolution shall be available for the execution of a program for commercial production of a civil supersonic aircraft.


*Approved March 30, 1971.*

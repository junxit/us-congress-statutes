# Public Law 92–217

> JOINT RESOLUTION To provide for the beginning of the second session of the Ninety-second Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second regular session of the Ninety-second Congress shall begin at noon on Tuesday, January 18, 1972.


*Approved December 22, 1971.*

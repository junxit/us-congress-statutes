# Public Law 92–69

> AN ACT To amend section 5055 of title 38, United States Code, in order to extend the authority of the Administrator of Veterans Affairs to establish and carry out a program of exchange of medical information.

*Be if enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 5055 of title 38, United States Code, is amended by deleting in the first sentence of subsection (c) (1) “of the first four fiscal years following the fiscal year in which this subchapter is enacted” and inserting in lieu thereof the following: “fiscal year 1968 through 1971, and such sums as may be necessary for each fiscal year 1972 through 1975,”.


*Approved August 6, 1971.*

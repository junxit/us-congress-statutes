# Public Law 92–23

> JOINT RESOLUTION To authorize the President to designate June 1, 1971, as “Medical Library Association Day”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, as a tribute to the important and dedicated work performed by the medical librarians, the President is authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating the day June 1, 1971, as “Medical Library Association Day” to coincide with their annual convention.


*Approved June 2, 1971.*

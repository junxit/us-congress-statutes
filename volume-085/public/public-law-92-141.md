# Public Law 92–141

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making a supplemental appropriation for the Department of Labor for the fiscal year 1972, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the following sum is appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1972, namely:

## DEPARTMENT OF LABOR

## Manpower Administration

## federal unemployment benefits and allowances

For an additional amount for “Federal unemployment benefits and allowances”, $270,500,000.


*Approved October 15, 1971.*

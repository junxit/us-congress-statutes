# Public Law 92–1

> JOINT RESOLUTION To extend the time for the proclamation of marketing quotas for burley tobacco for the three marketing years beginning October 1, 1971.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Secretary of Agriculture may defer any proclamation under section 312 of the Agricultural Adjustment Act of 1938, as amended, with respect to national marketing quotas for burley tobacco for the three marketing years beginning October 1, 1971, until the date he determines is necessary to permit growers to be notified of their farm marketing quotas and the referendum to lie held prior to normal planting time.


*Approved March 1, 1971.*

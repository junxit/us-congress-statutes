# Public Law 92–150

> JOINT RESOLUTION To extend the authority conferred by the Export Administration Act of 1969.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 14 of the Export Administration Act of 1969, as amended (Public Law 92–37; 85 Stat. 89), is amended by striking out “October 31, 1971” and inserting “May 1, 1972”.


*Approved October 30, 1971.*

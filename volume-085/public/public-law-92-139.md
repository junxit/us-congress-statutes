# Public Law 92–139

> JOINT RESOLUTION Milking further continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 1972, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That clause (c) of section 102 of the joint resolution of July 1, 1971 (Public Law 92–38), as amended, is hereby further amended by striking out “October 15, 1971” and inserting in lieu thereof “November 15, 1971”.


*Approved October 15, 1971.*

# Public Law 92–142

> AN ACT To change the name of the “Nebraska National Forest”, Niobrara division, to the “Samuel R. McKelvie National Forest”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the date of enactment of this Act the national forest situated in the State of Nebraska, Cherry County, known and designated as the “Nebraska National Forest”, Niobrara division, shall be known and designated as the “Samuel R. McKelvie National Forest”. All laws, regulations, and public documents and records of the United States in which such national forest is designated or referred to under the name of the “Nebraska National Forest”, Niobrara division, shall be held to refer to such national forest under and by the name of the “Samuel R. McKelvie National Forest”.


*Approved October 15, 1971.*

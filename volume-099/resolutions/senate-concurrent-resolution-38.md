# Senate Concurrent Resolution 38

> ADJOURNMENT—SENATE AND HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That when the Senate adjourns on Wednesday, April 3, 1985, or Thursday, April 4, 1985, pursuant to a motion made by the Majority Leader, or his designee, in accordance with this resolution, and that when the House of Representatives adjourns on Wednesday, April 3, 1985, or Thursday, April 4, 1985, pursuant to a motion made by the Majority Leader, or his designee, in accordance with this resolution, they stand adjourned until 12:00 o’clock noon on Monday, April 15, 1985.


*Agreed to April 4, 1985.*

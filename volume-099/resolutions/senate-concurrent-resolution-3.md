# Senate Concurrent Resolution 3

> ADJOURNMENT—SENATE AND HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That when the Senate adjourns on Monday, January 7, 1985, at the conclusion of the joint session to count the electoral votes, it stand adjourned until 4 o’clock post meridiem on Monday, January 21, 1985, and when the House of Representatives adjourns on January 7, 1985, it stand adjourned until 10 o’clock ante meridiem on Monday, January 21, 1985, or until 12 o’clock meridian on the second day after Members are notified to reassemble pursuant to section 2 of this concurrent resolution.

## § 2.

The Speaker of the House and the Majority Leader of the Senate, acting jointly after consultation with the Minority Leader of the House and the Minority Leader of the Senate, shall notify the Members of the House and the Senate, respectively, to reassemble whenever, in their opinion, the public interest shall warrant it.


*Approved January 3, 1985.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 54

> ADJOURNMENT—SENATE AND HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That when the Senate adjourns on Thursday, June 27, 1985, or Friday, June 28, 1985, pursuant to a motion made by the Majority Leader in accordance with this resolution, and that when the House adjourns on Thursday, June 27, 1985, or Friday, June 28, 1985, pursuant to a motion made by the Majority Leader, or his designee, in accordance with this resolution, they stand adjourned until 12 o’clock noon on Monday, July 8, 1985, or until 12 o’clock noon on the second day after Members are notified to reassemble pursuant to section 2 of this concurrent resolution, whichever occurs first.

## § 2.

The Speaker of the House and the Majority Leader of the Senate, acting jointly after consultation with the Minority Leader of the House and the Minority Leader of the Senate, shall notify the Members of the House and the Senate, respectively, to reassemble whenever, in their opinion, the public interest shall warrant it.


*Agreed to June 27, 1985.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 230

> “1984: CIVIL LIBERTIES AND THE NATIONAL SECURITY STATE”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed, for the use of the Committee on the Judiciary of the House of Representatives, 500 additional copies of the transcript of hearings before the Subcommittee on Courts, Civil Liberties, and the Administration of Justice, entitled “1984: Civil Liberties and the National Security State”.


*Agreed to December 13, 1985.*

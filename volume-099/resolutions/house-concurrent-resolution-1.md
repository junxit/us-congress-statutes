# House Concurrent Resolution 1

> JOINT MEETING

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Wednesday, February 6, 1985, at 9 o’clock post meridiem, for the purpose of receiving such communication as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Agreed to January 22, 1985.*

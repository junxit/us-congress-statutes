# Public Law 99–62

> An Act To provide authorization of appropriations for activities under the Land Remote-Sensing Commercialization Act of 1984.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 609 of the Land Remote-Sensing Commercialization Act of 1984 (15 U.S.C. 4278) is amended by striking “$75,000,000 for fiscal year 1985” and inserting in lieu thereof “$295,000,000 for fiscal years 1985 through 1989, of which not more than $125,000,000 shall be available for fiscal years 1985 and 1986,”.


*Approved July 11, 1985.*

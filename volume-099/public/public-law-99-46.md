# Public Law 99–46

> An Act To amend the Saccharin Study and Labeling Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That section 3 of the Saccharin Study and Labeling Act (21 U.S.C. 348 nt.) is amended by striking out “During the period beginning on the date of enactment of this Act and ending twenty-four months after the date of enactment of the Saccharin Study and Labeling Act Amendment of 1983” and inserting in lieu thereof “During the period ending May 1, 1987”.


*Approved May 24, 1985.*

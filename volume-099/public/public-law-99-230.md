# Public Law 99–230

> An Act To change the date for transmittal of a report.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** section 713(d) of the International Security and Development Cooperation Act of 1985 is amended by striking out “December 31, 1985,” and inserting in lieu thereof “October 1, 1986,”.
- **(b)** The amendment made by subsection (a) shall take effect as of December 30, 1985.


*Approved December 28, 1985.*

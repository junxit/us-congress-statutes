# Public Law 99–69

> An Act To extend the authority to establish and administer flexible and compressed work schedules for Federal Government employees.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That section 5 of the Federal Employees Flexible and Compressed Work Schedules Act of 1982 (96 Stat. 234; 5 U.S.C. 6101 note) is amended by striking out “three years after the date of the enactment of this Act” and inserting in lieu thereof “September 30, 1985”.


*Approved July 22, 1985.*

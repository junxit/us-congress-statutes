# Public Law 99–233

> Joint Resolution Relative to the convening of the second session of the Ninety-ninth Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second regular session of the Ninety-ninth Congress shall begin at 12 o’clock meridian on Tuesday, January 21, 1986.


*Approved December 28, 1985.*

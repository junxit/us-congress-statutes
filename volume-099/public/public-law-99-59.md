# Public Law 99–59

> An Act To extend the provisions of title XII of the Merchant Marine Act, 1936, relating to war risk insurance.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1214 of the Merchant Marine Act, 1936 (46 App. U.S.C. 1294) is amended by striking “September 30, 1984” and inserting in lieu thereof “June 30, 1990”.


*Approved July 3, 1985.*

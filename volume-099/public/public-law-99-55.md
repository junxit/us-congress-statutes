# Public Law 99–55

> An Act To designate the Federal Building and United States Courthouse in Ashland, Kentucky, as the “Carl D. Perkins Federal Building and United States Courthouse”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

,That the Federal Building and United States Courthouse located at Fourteenth Street and Greenup Avenue, Ashland, Kentucky, shall hereafter be known and designated as the “Carl D. Perkins Federal Building and United States Courthouse”. Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to such building shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Carl D. Perkins Federal Building and United States Courthouse”.


*Approved June 26, 1985.*

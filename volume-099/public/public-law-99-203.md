# Public Law 99–203

> Joint Resolution To authorize and request the President to issue a proclamation designating April 20 through April 26, 1986 as “National Organ and Tissue Donor Awareness Week”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating April 20 through April 26, 1986 as “National Organ and Tissue Donor Awareness Week”.


*Approved December 23, 1985.*

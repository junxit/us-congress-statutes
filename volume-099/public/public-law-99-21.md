# Public Law 99–21

> Joint Resolution To authorize and request the President to issue a proclamation designating April 21 through April 27, 1985, as “National Organ Donation Awareness Week”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating April 21 through April 27, 1985, as “National Organ Donation Awareness Week”.


*Approved April 14, 1985.*

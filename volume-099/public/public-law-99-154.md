# Public Law 99–154

> Joint Resolution Making further continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 1986.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 102(c) of the joint resolution of September 30, 1985 (Public Law 99–103) is hereby amended by striking out “November 14, 1985” and inserting in lieu thereof “December 12, 1985”.


*Approved November 14, 1985.*

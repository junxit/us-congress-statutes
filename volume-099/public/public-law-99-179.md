# Public Law 99–179

> Joint Resolution Making further continuing appropriations for fiscal year 1986.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the joint resolution of November 14, 1985 (Public Law 99–154) is hereby amended by striking out “December 12, 1985” and inserting in lieu thereof “six o’clock post meridiem, eastern standard time, December 16, 1985”.


*Approved December 13, 1985.*

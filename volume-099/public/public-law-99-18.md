# Public Law 99–18

> Joint Resolution To approve the obligation and availability of prior year unobligated balances made available for fiscal year 1985 for the procurement of additional operational MX missiles.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Congress approves the obligation and availability of prior year unobligated balances made available for fiscal year 1985 for the procurement of additional operational MX missiles.


*Approved April 4, 1985.*

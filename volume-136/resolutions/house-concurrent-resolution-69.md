# House Concurrent Resolution 69

> JOINT SESSION

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Tuesday, March 1, 2022, at 9:00 p.m., for the purpose of receiving such communication as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Agreed to February 10, 2022.*

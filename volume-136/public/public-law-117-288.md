# Public Law 117–288

> An Act To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 213 William Hilton Parkway in Hilton Head Island, South Carolina, as the “Caesar H. Wright Jr. Post Office Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. CAESAR H. WRIGHT JR. POST OFFICE BUILDING.

- **(a)** *Designation.—* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 213 William Hilton Parkway in Hilton Head Island, South Carolina, shall be known and designated as the “Caesar H. Wright Jr. Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References.—* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Caesar H. Wright Jr. Post Office Building”.


*Approved December 27, 2022.*

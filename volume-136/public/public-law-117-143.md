# Public Law 117–143

> An Act To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 1233 North Cedar Street in Owasso, Oklahoma, as the “Technical Sergeant Marshal Roberts Post Office Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. TECHNICAL SERGEANT MARSHAL ROBERTS POST OFFICE BUILDING.

- **(a)** *Designation.—* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 1233 North Cedar Street in Owasso, Oklahoma, shall be known and designated as the “Technical Sergeant Marshal Roberts Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References.—* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Technical Sergeant Marshal Roberts Post Office Building”.


*Approved June 16, 2022.*

# Public Resolution 5

> Joint Resolution authorizing the Librarian of Congress to exhibit certain documents at the World’s Columbian Exposition.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That that the Librarian of Congress be, and he hereby is, authorized to exhibit at the World’s Columbian Exposition such books, papers, documents, and other articles from the Library of Congress as may relate to Christopher Columbus and the discovery and early history of America.


*Approved, March 24, 1892.*

# Chapter 266

> An act for the establishment of additional aids to navigation in Tampa Bay, Florida.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be established in Tampa Bay, Florida, such additional aids to navigation as maybe found necessary by the Light-House Board, the entire cost of which shall not exceed the sum of six thousand dollars; and the sum of six thousand dollars, or so much thereof as may be necessary, is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the purpose of this act.


*Approved, July 27, 1892.*

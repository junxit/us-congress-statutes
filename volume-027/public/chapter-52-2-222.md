# Chapter 222

> An Act in relation to the pay of Rear-Admiral James E. Jouett, retired.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Rear-Admiral James E. Jouett, of the United States Navy, retired, be paid, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the highest pay and compensation of his grade as a rear-admiral, from and after the date of the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1893.*

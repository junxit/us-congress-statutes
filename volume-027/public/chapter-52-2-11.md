# Chapter 11

> An Act to extend to Duluth, Minnesota, the privileges of the first section of an act entitled “An act to amend the statutes in relation to immediate transportation of dutiable goods, and for other purposes,” approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of the first section of an act entitled “An act to amend the statutes in relation to immediate transportation of dutiable goods, and for other purposes,” approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, be, and the same are hereby, extended to the port of Duluth, Minnesota.


*Approved, December 22, 1892.*

# Chapter 77

> An Act to extend the provisions of an act to provide for the muster and pay of certain officers and enlisted men of the volunteer forces.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of an act to provide for the muster and pay of certain officers and enlisted men of the volunteer forces, approved June third, eighteen hundred and eighty-four, as amended by an act approved February third, eighteen hundred and eighty-seven, be, and the same are hereby, revived and extended for a period of three years from the third day of June, eighteen hundred and ninety two.


*Approved, February 9, 1893.*

# Chapter 85

> An Act making Laredo, Texas, a subport of entry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph three of section twenty-five hundred and seventy-eight of the Revised Statutes be, and the same is hereby, amended so that it shall read:

> - **(3)** The district of Corpus Christi, to comprise all the waters and shores within the counties of Nueces, Zapata, Duval, (Encinao) Encinal, Webb, LaSalle, McMullen, Live Oak, Bee, Refugio, and San Patricio, as bounded on the twenty-eighth day of July, eighteen hundred and sixty-six, in which Corpus Christi shall be the port of entry, Laredo a subport of entry, and Aransas a port of delivery.”


*Approved, June 3, 1892.*

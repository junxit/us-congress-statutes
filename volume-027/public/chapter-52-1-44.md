# Chapter 44

> An Act to extend to Marquette, Michigan, the privilege of immediate transportation of unappraised merchandise.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of an act entitled “An act to amend the statutes in relation to immediate transportation of dutiable goods, and for other purposes,” approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, be, and the same are hereby extended to the port of Marquette, Michigan.


*Approved, April 13, 1892.*

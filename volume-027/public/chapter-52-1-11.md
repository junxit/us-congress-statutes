# Chapter 11

> An Act to constitute Newark, in the State of New Jersey, a port of immediate transportation.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the privileges of the seventh section of the act approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, entitled “An act to amend the statutes in relation to immediate transportation of dutiable goods, and for other purposes,” be, and the same are hereby, extended to the port of Newark, State of New Jersey.


*Approved, March 8, 1892.*

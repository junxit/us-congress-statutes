# Chapter 23

> An Act to provide for the establishment of a port of delivery at Council Bluffs, Iowa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Council Bluffs, in the State of Iowa, be, and hereby is, established as a port of delivery, in the customs collection district of New Orleans, and that a surveyor of customs, shall be appointed for said port.


*Approved, March 26, 1892.*

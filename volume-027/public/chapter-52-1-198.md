# Chapter 198

> An Act to amend section seven of the act approved June twenty-second, eighteen hundred. and eighty-eight, entitled •• An act to authorize the construction of a bridge over the Missouri River at or near the city of Omaha, Nebraska,” and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section seven of the act approved June twenty-second, eighteen hundred and eighty-eight, entitled “An act to authorize the construction of a bridge over the Missouri River at or near the city of Omaha, Nebraska,” is hereby amended so as to require said bridge to be completed within three years from the date of the approval of this act, and all the other provisions of said act are hereby revived and determined to be in full force and effect.


*Approved, July 16, 1892.*

# Chapter 1

> An Act terminating the reduction in the numbers of the Engineer Corps of the Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the reduction in the numbers of the Engineer Corps of the Navy provided for in the act approved August fifth, eighteen hundred and eighty-two, shall be considered as having ceased on the thirtieth day of June, eighteen hundred and ninety-one.

## § 2.

That any and all acts or parts of acts inconsistent with this act are hereby repealed.


*Approved, December 16, 1892.*

# Chapter 15

> An Act to provide for a term of the circuit and district court at Littleton, New Hampshire.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That hereafter there shall be held annually, on the last Tuesday of August, a term of the circuit and district courts of the United States for the district of New Hampshire, in the town of Littleton, in said district.


*Approved, March 10, 1892.*

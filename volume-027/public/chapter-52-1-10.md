# Chapter 10

> An Act to extend the privileges of the transportation of dutiable merchandise without appraisement to the port of Sandusky, Ohio.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the privileges of the seventh section of the act approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, governing the transportation of dutiable merchandise without appraisement, be, and the same are hereby, extended to the port of Sandusky, in the State of Ohio.


*Approved, February 26, 1892.*

# Chapter 151

> An Act to amend an act entitled “An act to provide for the establishment of a port of delivery at Council Bluffs. Iowa.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the privileges of section seven of the act of June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, entitled “An act to amend the statutes in relation to immediate transportation of dutiable goods, and for other purposes,” be, and are hereby, extended to the said port of Council Bluffs, Iowa.


*Approved, February 21, 1893.*

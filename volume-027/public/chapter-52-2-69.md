# Chapter 69

> An Act to make Rockport, Texas, a subport of entry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Aransas (Rockport) in the customs collection district of Corpus Christi, Texas, be, and is hereby, constituted a subport of entry in said district.


*Approved, February 8, 1893.*

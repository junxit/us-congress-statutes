# Chapter 63

> An Act establishing a fog signal at Tibbets Point, Lake Ontario, New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there shall be established a fog signal at Tibbets Point Light, foot Lake Ontario, New York, at a cost not exceeding four thousand three hundred dollars.


*Approved, February 6, 1893.*

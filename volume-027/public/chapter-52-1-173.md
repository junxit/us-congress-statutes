# Chapter 173

> An Act to grant lot numbered one in block numbered seventy-two of the Hot Springs Reservation to the School district of the City of Hot Springs for School purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That lot numbered one in block numbered seventy-two of Hot Springs Reservation be, and the same is hereby granted and Conveyed to the School district of the City of Hot Spring's, Arkansas, for School purposes.


*Approved, July 14, 1892.*

# Chapter 374

> An act changing the date for the dedication of the buildings of the World’s Columbian Exposition.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the date for the dedication of the buildings of the World’s Columbian Exposition is hereby changed from the twelfth day of October, eighteen hundred and ninety-two, to the twenty-first day of October, eighteen hundred and ninety-two.


*Approved, August 4, 1892.*

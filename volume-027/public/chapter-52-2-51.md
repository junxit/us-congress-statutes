# Chapter 51

> An Act relating to post traderships.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That where a vacancy now exists or hereafter occurs in the position of post trader at any military post it shall not be tilled, and the authority to make such appointment is hereby terminated: Provided, That in the event of the death of a post trader his personal representative shall be allowed by the Secretary of War a reasonable time in which to close the business.


*Approved, January 28, 1893.*

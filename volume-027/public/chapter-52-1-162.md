# Chapter 162

> An Act to amend the act approved March first, eighteen hundred and eighty-seven, relating to the Hospital Corps of the Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Section six of the act approved March first, eighteen hundred and eighty-seven, entitled “An act to organize the Hospital Corps of the Army of the United States, to define its duty, and fix its pay,” be, and hereby is, amended to read as follows:

> ## § 6.
>
> That the pay of privates of the Hospital Corps shall be eighteen dollars per month, with the increase on account of length of service as is now or may hereafter be allowed by law to other enlisted men. They shall be entitled to the same allowance as a corporal of the arm of service with which they may be on duty.”


*Approved, July 13, 1892.*

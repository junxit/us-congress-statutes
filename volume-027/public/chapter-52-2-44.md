# Chapter 44

> An Act to amend “An act to promote the construction of a safe deep-water harbor on the coast of Texas,” approved February ninth, eighteen hundred and ninety-one.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section two of said act be so amended as to extend the time of commencing said work two years from the ninth day of February, eighteen hundred and ninety-three.


*Approved, January 23, 1893.*

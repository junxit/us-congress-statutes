# Chapter 156

> An Act to amend an act entitled “An act approving with amendments the funding act of Arizona,” approved June twenty-fifth, eighteen hundred and ninety.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section three of said act be, and is so amended that the interest upon the bonds provided for in said act shall be payable semiannually, on the fifteenth days of January and July in each year, and that no further Territorial legislation shall be necessary to make said Territory liable for the principal and interest of said bonds; and no further advertisement for the sale of one million live hundred thousand dollars of said bonds, at not less than par, shall be necessary.


*Approved, July 13, 1892.*

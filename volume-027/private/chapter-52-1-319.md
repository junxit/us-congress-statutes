# Chapter 319

> An Act to remove the charge of desertion now standing against Michael Keefe, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to remove the charge of desertion now standing against Michael Keefe, deceased, late of Company B. Eighth Regiment Connecticut Volunteers, and to grant an honorable discharge to date September twenty-eight, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, on account of being disabled for further military service, and this act shall not carry with it any right to back pay, bounty, or other emoluments.


*Approved, July 28, 1892.*

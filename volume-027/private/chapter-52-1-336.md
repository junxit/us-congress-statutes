# Chapter 336

> An Act granting a pension to Sarah L. Ronaldson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, required to place the name of Sarah L. Ronaldson, widow of Mathew Ronaldson, late a first lieutenant in Company B, First Iowa Cavalry, and pay her a pension of fifteen dollars per month.


*Approved, July 30, 1892.*

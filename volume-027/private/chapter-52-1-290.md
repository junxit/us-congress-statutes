# Chapter 290

> An Act to pension Nancy Campbell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Nancy Campbell, of Magnolia, Columbia County, Arkansas, widow of Archibald Campbell, late a private of James Blakeney's company, Harllee’s Battalion, South Carolina Militia, Florida war. eighteen hundred and thirty-seven, and pay her a pension of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, July 27, 1892.*

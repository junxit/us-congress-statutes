# Chapter 26

> An Act to remove the political disabilities of John E. F. Tatnall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the political disabilities of John R. F. Tatnall, late an officer of marines in the Marine Corps of the United States Navy, arising under the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States, be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, March 30, 1892.*

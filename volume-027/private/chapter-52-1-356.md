# Chapter 356

> An Act granting a pension to Sarah A. Noble.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah A. Noble, mother of James Dunn, junior, late of Company 1), Twenty sixth Regiment of Iowa Volunteers.


*Approved, August 1, 1892.*

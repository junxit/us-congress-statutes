# Chapter 242

> An Act to pension Ida Cassell

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be and he is hereby authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Ida Cassell, imbecil daughter of Appollas Cassell, late a private in Company G. eighteenth regiment Indiana volunteer infantry, and pay to her legally constituted gardian a pension at the rate of fourteen dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1893.*

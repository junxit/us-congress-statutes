# Chapter 295

> An Act granting a pension to Frances B. Wilson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension rolls, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Frances B. Wilson who was the widow of Ross Wilson, private in Company F. Forty-ninth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry,


*Approved, July 27, 1892.*

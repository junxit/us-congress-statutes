# Chapter 342

> An Act granting a pension to James W. Kirtley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of James W. Kirtley, a soldier of the Black Hawk war, and pay him a pension of twenty dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 30, 1892.*

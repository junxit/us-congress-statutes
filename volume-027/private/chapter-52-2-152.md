# Chapter 152

> An Act for the relief of B. F. Rockafellow.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to pay six hundred and fourteen dollars and eleven cents to B. F. Rockafellow, on his account as postmaster at Canon City, Colorado, or so much of said amount as shall appear to the proper accounting officer of the Government to have been paid by said B. F. Rockafellow for necessary clerk hire in said Canon City post-office.


*Approved, February 21, 1893.*

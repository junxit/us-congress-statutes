# Chapter 239

> An Act to pension Maud Case, of Dodge County, Minnesota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, directed to place upon the pension rolls, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Maud Case, the dependent and totally blind daughter of Hezekiah Case, deceased, late private of Company H, Thirty-third Wisconsin Volunteers, at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1893.*

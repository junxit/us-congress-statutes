# Chapter 210

> An Act granting a pension to Mary E. Law, widow of Captain Richard L. Law, United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary E. Law, widow of Captain Richard L. Law. late of the United States Navy, granting her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month payable out of the naval pension fund.


*Approved, July 20, 1892.*

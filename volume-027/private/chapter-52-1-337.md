# Chapter 337

> An Act granting a pension to Stark Frazier.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Stark Frazier, of Brooks County, Georgia, late a private in Captain Isaac McCreary’s company of cavalry from Sumter County, Georgia, in the war of eighteen hundred and thirty-six, at the rate of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, July 30, 1892.*

# Chapter 243

> An Act granting a pension to Elizabeth Voss.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitation of the pension laws, the name of Elizabeth Voss, widow of Jacob Imsun, late of Company A, Sixteenth Regiment of Iowa Infantry.

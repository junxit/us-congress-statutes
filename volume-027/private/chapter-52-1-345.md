# Chapter 345

> An Act granting a pension to Julia S. Tompkins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Julia S. Tompkins, an army nurse, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, July 30, 1892.*

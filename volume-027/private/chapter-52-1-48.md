# Chapter 48

> An Act for the relief of Edward S. Armstrong

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of one thousand six hundred and seventy-three dollars and fourteen cents be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to pay Edward S. Armstrong, of Ralls County, Missouri, it being the one-eighth part of a judgment rendered by the Court of Claims on the twenty-second of November, eighteen hundred and sixty, against the United States, in favor of the estate of James B. Armstrong, deceased.


*Approved, April 15, 1892.*

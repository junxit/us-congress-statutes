# Chapter 300

> An Act granting a pension to Elizabeth Bowden.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll of the United States the name of Elizabeth Bowden, late a nurse and superintendent of nurses, to be paid a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, July 27, 1892.*

# Chapter 180

> An Act granting a pension to William S. Woodward.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William S. Woodward, of Brooks County, Georgia, late a private in Captain Durant’s company of the battalion of South Carolina Volunteers, commanded by Major Harlee, in the Indian war of eighteen hundred and thirty-six, at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, July 14, 1892.*

# Chapter 248

> An Act granting a pension to Thomas S Kennedy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, the name of Thomas S Kennedy, who served as a soldier in the Mexican war, at the rate of twelve dollars per month from the date of the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1893.*

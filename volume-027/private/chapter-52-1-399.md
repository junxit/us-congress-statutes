# Chapter 399

> An Act granting an increase of pension to George W. Clark.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of George W. Clark, a member of Captain Philip Pryor’s company of light horse cavalry in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per mouth, in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, August 5, 1892.*

# Chapter 142

> An Act for the relief of Thomas A. McLaughlin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to revoke and set aside Special Orders Numbered Two hundred and ninety-eight, War Department, Adjutant-General’s Office, Washington, September eighth, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, dismissing First Lieutenant Thomas A. McLaughlin, One hundred and second Pennsylvania Volunteers, for absence from hospital at Annapolis, Maryland, without proper authority, and conduct unbecoming an officer and gentleman, and to accept the resignation of said McLaughlin as of date August fifteenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, and to issue to him a certificate of honorable discharge of said last-named date.


*Approved, July 1, 1892.*

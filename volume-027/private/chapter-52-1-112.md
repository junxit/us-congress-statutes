# Chapter 112

> An Act to pension Samuel O. Fisher, of Lynn, Massachusetts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Samuel O. Fisher, late of the whale ship Magnolia, Mexican war.


*Approved, June 9, 1892.*

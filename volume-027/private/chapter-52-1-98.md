# Chapter 98

> An Act granting a pension to Sarah L. Henderson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, at the rate of twelve dollars per month, the name of Sarah L. Henderson, a hospital nurse during the civil war.


*Approved, June 7, 1892.*

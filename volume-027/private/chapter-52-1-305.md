# Chapter 305

> An Act granting a pension to Charity W. Clark, mother of Edmond M. Clark, formerly of Company M, Seventeenth Pennsylvania Cavalry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and instructed to place upon the pension rolls of the United States the name of Charity W. Clark, mother of the late Edmond M. Clark, of Company M. Seventeenth Regiment Pennsylvania Cavalry, at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, July 27, 1892.*

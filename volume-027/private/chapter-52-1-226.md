# Chapter 226

> An Act for the relief of Harriett W. Shacklett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to pay to Harriet W. Shacklett, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of five hundred and ninety-seven dollars, which sum shall be taken and accepted and receipted for in full satisfaction of her claim, as found by the Court of Claims.


*Approved, July 21, 1892.*

# Chapter 179

> An Act to remove the disabilities of William F. Robinson, a citizen of the State of Alabama.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all the disabilities imposed upon and incurred by William F. Robinson, of Eufaula, Barbour County, in the State of Alabama, under the provisions of the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States, be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, February 28, 1893.*

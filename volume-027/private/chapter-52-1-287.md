# Chapter 287

> An Act to increase the pension of John D. Prator.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior he, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of John D. Prator, of Magnolia, Arkansas, who served in Captain Allen’s Company from the State of Georgia, Indian war, eighteen hundred and thirty-six, to fifteen dollars per month.


*Approved, July 27, 1892.*

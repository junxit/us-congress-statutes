# Chapter 261

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Ellis P. Phipps, late lieutenant in Company A. Twelfth New Jersey Volunteer Infantry, invalid certificate numbered thirty five thousand six hundred and nineteen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Ellis P. Phipps, late lieutenant Company A, Twelfth Regiment, New Jersey Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month, in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 3, 1893.*

# Chapter 105

> An Act to pension Margaret Turner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the. Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Margaret Turner, of Curtis, Clark County, Arkansas, the widow of Isham Turner, deceased, who served in Captain Stell’s company, Lieutenant-Colonel Wood’s Battalion Georgia Volunteers, Florida war, and pay her the same pension as is allowed by law to the widows of the soldiers of the war of eighteen hundred and twelve.


*Approved, June 7, 1892.*

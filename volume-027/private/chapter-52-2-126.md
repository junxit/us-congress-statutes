# Chapter 126

> An Act to grant a pension to Ida A. Taylor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ida A. Taylor, permanently helpless daughter of Chester Taylor, late private of Company G, Eighty-third Regiment, New York Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 15, 1893.*

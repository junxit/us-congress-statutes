# Chapter 390

> An Act granting an honorable discharge to John H. Randleman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to correct the military record of, and grant an honorable discharge to. John H. Randleman, of De Soto, Iowa, late a member of Company C, Fourth United States Artillery: Provided, That no pay or emoluments shall be due said John H Randleman by virtue of this act.


*Approved, August 5, 1892.*

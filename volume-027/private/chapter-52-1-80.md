# Chapter 80

> An Act authorizing the payment of the pension of Edward S. Smith, accrued at the date of his death, to his mother Catherine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to cause to be paid to Catherine Smith, mother of Edward S. Smith, the pension accrued to said Edward S. Smith, a pensioner under the provisions of certificate numbered four hundred and two thousand and fifty six, to the date of the death of said pensioner, as fully as the same might have been paid to the said Edward S. Smith for the same time were he now living.


*Approved, May 25, 1892.*

# Chapter 194

> An Act for the relief of L. M. Garrett

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

. That the Postmaster-General be, and he is hereby, directed to credit the account of L. M. Garrett, late postmaster at Carthage, Mississippi, with the sum of four hundred and eighty-two dollars, for money order funds remitted by the said Garrett for deposit at New Orleans, Louisiana, December fourteenth, eighteen hundred and eighty-eight, and lost or stolen while in transit without fault or negligence on his part.


*Approved, March 1, 1893.*

# Chapter 95

> An Act granting a pension to Ellen Hewett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ellen Hewett, mother of Frank Hewett, late of Company K, Eleventh Ohio Volunteer Cavalry, at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 11, 1893.*

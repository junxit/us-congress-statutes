# Chapter 186

> An Act granting a pension to Mary Gatlin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Gatlin, widow of John II, Gatlin, a soldier of the war of eighteen hundred and thirty-six, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 14, 1892.*

# Chapter 397

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Esther J. Boone.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

. That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Mrs. Esther J. Boone, of Lincoln, Nebraska, who, by exposure and overexertion, lost her hearing while serving as a hospital nurse and sanitary agent from eighteen hundred and sixty-two till the close of the war of the rebellion, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, August 5, 1892.*

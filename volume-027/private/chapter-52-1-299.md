# Chapter 299

> An Act granting a pension to William Colvill, of Minnesota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of William Colvill, late colonel of the First Regiment of Minnesota Infantry and brevet brigadier-general of volunteers, at the rate of fifty dollars per month, from and after the passage of this act, the same in lien of the pension now allowed to said Colvill.


*Approved, July 27, 1892.*

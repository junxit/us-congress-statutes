# Chapter 334

> An Act granting a pension to Mary J. Corcoran.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Mary J. Corcoran, of Jackson, Michigan, widow of Henry Corcoran, late of Company E, Twelfth Michigan Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 30, 1892.*

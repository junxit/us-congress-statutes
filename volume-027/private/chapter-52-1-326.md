# Chapter 326

> An Act granting a pension to Orinda Leach.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the mime of Orinda Leach, dependent mother of Charles 11. Leach, late of Company E, Seventh Regiment of Vermont Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, July 29, 1892.*

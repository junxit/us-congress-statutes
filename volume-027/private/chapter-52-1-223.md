# Chapter 223

> An Act to pension Edith S. Read.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension rolls, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Edith S. Read, of Bradford, Pennsylvania, widow of Ogden B. Read, late captain of Company F, Eleventh Infantry, United States Army.


*Approved, July 21, 1892.*

# Chapter 304

> An Act granting a pension to Cecilia White.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized to place the name of Cecilia White, of Burlington, Iowa, on the pension roll, and pay her a pension of twelve dollars per month for her services as a nurse during the civil war.


*Approved, July 27, 1892.*

# Chapter 166

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Jennie Vaughan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to restore to the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Jennie Vaughan, late wife of the late Captain William Rowe Baxter, captain of Company H, Ninth Minnesota Volunteers, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 13, 1892.*

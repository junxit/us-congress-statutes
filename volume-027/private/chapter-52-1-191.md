# Chapter 191

> An Act for the relief of Frederick Meredith, late a soldier in the Indian war of eighteen hundred and thirty-two.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension rolls the name of Frederick Meredith, late a private in the company of Captain James Hall, in the Indian war of eighteen hundred and thirty-two, and that he be paid the sum of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, July 14, 1892.*

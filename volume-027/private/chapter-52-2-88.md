# Chapter 88

> An Act to pension Susan S. Murphy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Susan S. Murphy, of Eldorado, Union County, Arkansas, widow of Thomas Murphy, deceased, late a private in Captain Sims’s Company, Alabama Militia, Creek Indian war, and pay her a pension of twelve dollars a month.


*Approved, February 11, 1893.*

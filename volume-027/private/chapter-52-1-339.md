# Chapter 339

> An Act granting a pension to William H. Brewer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to cause to be. placed on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William II. Brewer, late a private in Company C, Seventy-third Regiment Indiana Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, July 30, 1892.*

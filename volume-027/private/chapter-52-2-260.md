# Chapter 260

> An Act to place the name of Sabra A. Wolcott upon the pension rolls.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior hereby is authorized and directed to place the name of Sabra A. Wolcott upon the pension rolls, and that she be granted a pension as the widow of Foster Ames, late of Company E, Seventeenth Michigan Infantry, in accordance with the provisions of law now in force, as though she, the said Sabra A Wolcott, had always remained the widow of the said Foster Ames.


*Approved, March 3, 1893.*

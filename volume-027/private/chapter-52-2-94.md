# Chapter 94

> An Act granting a pension to Alonzo D. Barber.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, of Alonzo D. Barber, late a private in Company I of the Fifty-fifth Regiment Ohio Infantry Volunteers, to thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 11, 1893.*

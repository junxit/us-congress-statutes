# Chapter 344

> An Act granting a pension to Mary A. Sipp.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary A. Sipp, widow of Andrew M. Johnson, late of Company F, Thirty-first. Regiment of Iowa Infantry, and who as such widow was allowed a pension heretofore, and pay her at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, July 30, 1892.*

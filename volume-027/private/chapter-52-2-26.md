# Chapter 26

> An Act granting a pension to Delzell R. Bradford, Twenty-fourth Michigan Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension law of June twenty-seventh, eighteen hundred and ninety, the name of Delzell R. Bradford, late a private in the Twenty fourth Regiment of Michigan Volunteers.


*Approved, January 9, 1893.*

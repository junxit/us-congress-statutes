# Chapter 340

> An Act granting a pension to Ellen Goff.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Ellen Goff, of Battle Creek, Michigan, widow of George H. Piper, late orderly sergeant of Company I, Thirty third Missouri Infantry, and pay her a pension of twelve dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 30, 1892.*

# Chapter 177

> An Act to pension Mrs Adelia S. Ferris.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension rolls the name of Mrs Adelia S Ferris, a noted army nurse during the late war of the rebellion, and pay her a pension of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, July 14, 1892.*

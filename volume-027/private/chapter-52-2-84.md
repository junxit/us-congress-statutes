# Chapter 84

> An Act granting a pension to Amanda Atherton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Amanda Atherton, widow of John Wilson Cook, late of Company F, Twenty-sixth Regiment of Iowa Infantry, and who, as such widow, was allowed a pension heretofore, and pay her at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 11, 1893.*

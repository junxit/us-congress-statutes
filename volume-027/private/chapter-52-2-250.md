# Chapter 250

> An Act granting a pension to Mary Danahay, mother of Daniel Danahay, late a private Company H, Eighteenth New York Cavalry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension rolls the name of Mary Danahay, mother of Daniel Danahay, late a private of Company H, Eighteenth Rigiment New York Cavalry, and pay her a pension, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, March 3, 1893.*

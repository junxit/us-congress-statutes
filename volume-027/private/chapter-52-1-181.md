# Chapter 181

> An Act granting a pension to Bridget Maloy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Bridget Maloy, widow of Arthur Maloy, late of Company E, Forty-third Regiment New York Infantry, and to pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars a month.


*Approved, July 14, 1892.*

# Chapter 384

> An Act granting a pension to Annie Davis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Annie Davis, mother of Samuel Davis, late a member of Company B, of the Sixteenth Regiment of Wisconsin Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, August 5, 1892.*

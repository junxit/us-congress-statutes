# Chapter 51

> An Act for the relief of John R. Blankenship.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the homestead entry of Wallace Mefford, of lot numbered one of the northwest quarter of section numbered thirty, in township numbered thirty-one north, of range numbered nine, west of the fifth principal meridian in Texas County, Missouri, as shown by final receiver’s receipt numbered two thousand four hundred and seventy-six, on application numbered five thousand nine hundred and twelve, issued by George H. Crum, receiver of the United States land office at Ironton, Missouri, on January twenty-second, eighteen hundred and eighty-four, is hereby approved and confirmed, and the President will cause the patent to be issued thereon.


*Approved, April 19, 1892.*

# Chapter 42

> An Act to increase the pension of William Burrough, of Crawford County, Arkansas, Veteran of the War of eighteen hundred and twelve.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension’ of William Burrough, of Crawford County, Arkansas, Veteran of the War of eighteen hundred and twelve, be, and the same is hereby, increased to thirty dollars per month, subject to the conditions and limitations governing pensions.


*Approved, April 11, 1892.*

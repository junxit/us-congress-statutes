# Chapter 107

> An Act granting a pension to William F. C. Nindemann, late of the Jeannette expedition to the Arctic Ocean.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension rolls, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William F. C. Nindemann, late of the United States ship Jeannette in the expedition to the Arctic Ocean.


*Approved, June 7, 1892.*

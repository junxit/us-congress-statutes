# Chapter 286

> An Act to increase the pension of Louis Badger, late of the Fourth Indiana Cavalry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension rolls the name of Louis Badger, late of the Fourth Indiana Cavalry, at the rate of thirty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, July 27, 1892.*

# Chapter 172

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Mary E. Donaldson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Mrs. Mary E. Donaldson, of Santa Ana, California, widow of the said Lieutenant Charles V. Donaldson, and to pay her a pension at the rate of fifteen dollars per month.


*Approved, February 27, 1893.*

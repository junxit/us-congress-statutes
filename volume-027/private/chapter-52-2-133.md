# Chapter 133

> An Act granting a pension to Druke Nettie Barnett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Druke Nettie Barnett, daughter of Edward Barnett, late a member of Company B, Thirty-first Regiment Illinois Volunteers.


*Approved, February 15, 1893.*

# Chapter 367

> An Act granting a pension to Miss Adda Boodger, of Lockport, New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, at the rate of ten dollars per month, the name of Miss Adda L. Boodger, daughter of William Boodger, who served in Company I, of the Ninety-fourth New York Infantry.


*Approved, August 3, 1892.*

# Chapter 387

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Walter Barrett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, at the increased rate of fifty dollars per month, the name of Walter Barrett, late major of the Eighty-fourth Pennsylvania Volunteers.

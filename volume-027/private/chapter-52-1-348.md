# Chapter 348

> An Act restoring the pension of Sallie M. Swigart.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to restore to the pension roll the name of Sallie M. Swigart, widow of Uriah R. Swigart, late of Company L, Second Regiment Iowa Cavalry, to take effect from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 30, 1892.*

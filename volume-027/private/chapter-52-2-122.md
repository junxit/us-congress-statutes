# Chapter 122

> An Act for increase of pension of Frances P. Gardener.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to increase the pension, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, of Frances P. Gardener, widow of Captain George W. Gardener late of the United States Army, and pay her a pension of forty dollars per month in lieu of the pension she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 15, 1893.*

# Chapter 55

> An Act granting a pension to William M. Watson, of Walker County, Georgia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of William M. Watson, of Walker County, Georgia, an invalid soldier of the Mexican War, being a member of Company E. First Alabama Volunteers, eighteen hundred and forty-six, and that in consequence of his disability, arising from a wound received in said service, he be paid at the rate of fifteen dollars per mouth.

# Chapter 232

> An Act for the relief of George M. Henry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to remove the charge of desertion from the name of George M. Henry, late of Company M, Fifth United States Cavalry, charged with desertion from said service, and to amend the military record of said soldier so charged as a deserter: Provided, That the passage of this act shall not serve to entitle him to any pay, bounty, or allowances.


*Approved, March 3, 1893.*

# Chapter 346

> An Act granting a pension to Susanna Davis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Susanna Davis, of the city of Washington, in the District of Columbia, widow of William Davis, late a private in the United States Marine Corps, and who served in the Indian war in Florida, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, July 30, 1892.*

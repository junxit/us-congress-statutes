# Chapter 89

> An Act to increase the pension of Amelia Graham.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Amelia Graham, widow of Francis Graham, late a private in Captain J. Morris’s Company, Pennsylvania Militia, in the war with Great Britain, upon the pension roll at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 11, 1893.*

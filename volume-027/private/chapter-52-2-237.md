# Chapter 237

> An Act to pension John S. Dunham

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension roll the name of John S. Dunham, late of Winter’s company, Grundy County Battery, Missouri Militia, and pay him a pension for injury to breast, ribs, collar bone, and right forefinger, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, March 3, 1893.*

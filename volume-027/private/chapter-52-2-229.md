# Chapter 229

> An Act for the relief of Elizabeth Carpenter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elizabeth Carpenter, widow of Jesse Carpenter, late private in Company A, one hundred and twenty-sixth Regiment West Virginia State Militia, who was killed while in the line of duty in the late war, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month from the approval of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1893.*

# Chapter 27

> An Act to increase the pension of John Malloy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of John Malloy, late a seaman on the sloop-of-war Preble, in the war with Mexico, from eight dollars to fifteen dollars per month.


*Approved, January 9, 1893.*

# Chapter 357

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Addison M. Copen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension of Addison M. Copen, late of Company 1. United States Dragoons. Mexican war, be increased to twenty dollars per month; and the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the general pension laws, the name of said Copen, at the rate of twenty dollars per mouth, the same to be in lieu of all pensions heretofore granted to him.


*Approved, August 1, 1892.*

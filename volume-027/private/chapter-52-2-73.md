# Chapter 73

> An Act for the relief of James A. Finley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be, and is hereby, authorized to nominate and, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, to appoint James A. Finley, late assistant surgeon, United States Army, an assistant surgeon with the same relative grade he had at the time of his retirement, and to place him upon the retired list of the Army.


*Approved, February 8, 1893.*

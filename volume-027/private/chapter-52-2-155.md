# Chapter 155

> An Act to commission David P. Cordray as second lieutenant, to date from June twelfth, eighteen hundred and ninety-two.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and hereby is, authorized to issue a commission as second lieutenant to David P. Cordray, in the infantry, of the date of June twelfth, eighteen hundred and ninety-one, in lieu of the one issued to him August twenty-ninth, eighteen hundred and ninety-one, upon the return by the said David P. Cordray of his commission of the latter date to the President: Provided, That no additional pay shall be taken by said Lieu tenant Cordray by virtue of the passage of this act.

# Chapter 99

> An Act to pension Jane Allen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension rolls, subject to the provisions and regulations of the general pension laws, at the rate of twelve dollars per mouth, the name of Jane Allen, of Ogdensburg, stepmother of Edwin W. Allen of Company I), Eighty-third Regiment New York Volunteers.


*Approved, June 7, 1892.*

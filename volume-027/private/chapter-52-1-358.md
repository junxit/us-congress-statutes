# Chapter 358

> An Act to correct the military record of James Corridon, Fourth United States Infantry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the military record of James Corridon be corrected, that the charge of desert ion be removed, that his name be restored to the status of honorable service, and that he be granted an honorable discharge: Provided, That no bounty, pay, or allowances of any nature accrue from this act.


*Approved, August 1, 1892.*

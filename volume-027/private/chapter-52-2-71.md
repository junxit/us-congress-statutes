# Chapter 71

> An Act to increase the pension of Thomas Enlow.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase to the sum of twenty dollars per month the pension of Thomas Enlow, late of Company E, Fourth Indiana Volunteers, in the war with Mexico.


*Approved, February 8, 1893.*

# Chapter 91

> AN ACT To authorize the Secretary of War to effect exchange of certain rights-of-way in Hawaii.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to convey to the trustees under the will and of the estate of S. M. Damon, deceased, all right, title, and interest in a seventy-foot right-of-way situate on the Island of Oahu, Territory of Hawaii, and described as follows:

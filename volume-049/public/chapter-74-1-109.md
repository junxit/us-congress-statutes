# Chapter 109

> AN ACT To amend the Act of May 19, 1926, entitled “An Act to authorize the President to detail officers and enlisted men of the United States Army, Navy, and Marine Corps to assist the governments of the Latin-American Republics in military and naval matters.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of May 19, 1926 (Public, Numbered 247), be, and the same is hereby, amended by striking out the word “and” preceding the words “Santo Domingo” and inserting after the words “Santo Domingo” the words “and the Commonwealth of the Philippine Islands.”


*Approved, May 14, 1935.*

# Chapter 698

> AN ACT To extend the provisions of veterans’ laws and regulations to persons who served in Russia during the World War, and their dependents.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding any provisions of Public Law Numbered 2, Seventy-third Congress, and the veterans’ regulations issued pursuant thereto, for the purpose of payment of pension for disability not shown to have been incurred in military or naval service, the World War shall be deemed to have ended April 1, 1920, for those persons who served with the United States military forces in Russia.

## § 2.

Veterans who entered active military service subsequent to November 11, 1918, and who served with the United States military forces in Russia prior to April 2, 1920, and their dependents, shall be entitled to the benefits of Public Law Numbered 141, Seventy-third Congress, provided they meet the other requirements thereof.


*Approved, August 26, 1935.*

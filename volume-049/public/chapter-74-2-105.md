# Chapter 105

> JOINT RESOLUTION Postponing the effective date of certain permit and labeling provisions of the Federal Alcohol Administration Act.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3 (c) of the Federal Alcohol Administration Act, approved August 29, 1935, is amended by striking out “March 1, 1936” and inserting in lieu thereof “July 1, 1936”.

## § 2.

Section 5 (e) of such Act is amended by striking out “March 1, 1936” and inserting in lieu thereof “August 15, 1936, in the case of distilled spirits, and December 15, 1936, in the case of wine and malt beverages”.


*Approved, February 29, 1936.*

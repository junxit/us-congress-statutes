# Chapter 445

> AN ACT To extend the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Mississippi River at or near a point between Morgan and Wash Streets in the city of Saint Louis, Missouri, and a point opposite thereto in the city of East Saint Louis, Illinois.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Mississippi River, at or near a point between Morgan and Wash Streets in the city of Saint Louis, Missouri, and a point opposite thereto in the city of East Saint Louis, Illinois, authorized to be built by an Act of Congress approved May 3, 1934, are hereby extended one and three year’s, respectively, from May 3, 1935.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, August 5, 1935.*

# Chapter 738

> JOINT RESOLUTION Amending section 5 of Public Resolution Numbered 6, Seventy-fourth Congress, approved March 4, 1935.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 5 of Public Resolution Numbered 6, Seventy-fourth Congress, approved March 4, 1935, is hereby amended by striking out “December 31, 1936” and inserting in lieu thereof “December 31, 1937.”


*Approved, June 23, 1936.*

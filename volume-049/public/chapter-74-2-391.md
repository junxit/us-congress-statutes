# Chapter 391

> AN ACT To amend an Act entitled “An Act authorizing the Chippewa Indians of Minnesota to submit claims to the Court of Claims”, approved May 14, 1926 (44 Stat. L. 555).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in all suits filed under the Act entitled “An Act authorizing the Chippewa Indians of Minnesota to submit claims to the Court of Claims”, approved May 14, 1926 (44 Stat. L. 555), either party, with the con-sent of the court first had and obtained, shall have the right toamend the pleadings at any time prior to the entry of final judgment so as to include all claims said Indians may have under said Act against the United States and any defense the United States may have thereto.


*Approved, May 15, 1936.*

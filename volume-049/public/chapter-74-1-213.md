# Chapter 213

> AN ACT Authorizing a preliminary examination of Sebewaing River, in Huron County, Michigan, with a view to the controlling of floods.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

-  That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to cause a preliminary examination to be made of the Sebewaing River, in Huron County, Michigan, with a view to the control of floods, in accordance with the provisions of section 3 of the Act entitled “An Act to provide for control of floods of the Mississippi River, and of the Sacramento River, California, and for other purposes”, approved March 1, 1917, the cost thereof to be paid from appropriations heretofore or hereafter made for examinations, surveys, and contingencies of rivers and harbors.

*Approved, June 11, 1935.*

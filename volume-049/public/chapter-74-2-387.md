# Chapter 387

> JOINT RESOLUTION To provide an additional appropriation for folding speeches and pamphlets for the Senate for the fiscal year 1936.

*Resolved by the Senate and Rouse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for folding speeches and pamphlets, for the Senate, at a rate not exceeding $1 per thousand, there is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $4,000 for the fiscal year 1936.


*Approved, May 13, 1936.*

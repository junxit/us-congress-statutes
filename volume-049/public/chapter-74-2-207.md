# Chapter 207

> AN ACT To amend section 2 of the Act entitled “An Act to amend the National Defense Act”, approved May 28, 1928.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2 of the Act entitled “An Act to amend the National Defense Act”, approved May 28, 1928 (45 Stat. 786; U. S. C., title 32, sec. 181b), is hereby amended by inserting the words “Coast Guard” after the words “Marine Corps,” and before the words “National Guard,” in the fourth line of said section.


*Approved, April 11, 1936.*

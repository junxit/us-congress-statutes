# Chapter 332

> AN ACT Authorizing a preliminary examination of Cadron Creek, Arkansas, a tributary of the Arkansas River.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of American in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to cause a preliminary examination to be made of Cadron Creek, Arkansas, a tributary of the Arkansas River, with a view to the control of floods, in accordance with the provisions of section 3 of an Act entitled “An Act to provide for control of floods of the Mississippi River, and of the Sacramento River, California, and for other purposes”, approved March 1, 1917, the cost thereof to be paid from appropriations heretofore or hereafter made for examinations, surveys, and contingencies of rivers and harbors.


*Approved, May 6, 1936.*

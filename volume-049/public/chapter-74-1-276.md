# Chapter 276

> AN ACT To amend an Act entitled “An Act to regulate the manner in which property shall be sold under orders and decrees of any United States courts”, approved March 3, 1893, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United, States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 1, 2, and 3 of the Act entitled “An Act to regulate the manner in which property shall be sold under orders and decrees of any United States courts”, approved March 3, 1893 (ch. 225, 27 Stat. 751, as amended; U. S. C., title 28, secs. 847, 848, and 849), be, and they hereby are, amended by inserting before the period at the end of each of said sections 1, 2, and 3 the following: “or by receivers or conservators of banks, appointed by the Comptroller of the Currency”.


*Approved, June 19, 1935.*

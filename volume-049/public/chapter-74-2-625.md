# Chapter 625

> AN ACT To amend the charter of the National Union Insurance Company of Washington in the District of Columbia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the charter of the National Union Insurance Company of Washington, granted by an Act of Congress approved February 14, 1865, and amended by an Act of Congress approved May 11, 1892, is hereby further amended to permit the said insurance company to insure and reinsure risks in all the various forms authorized by section 3 of an Act of Congress approved March 4, 1922, entitled “An Act to regulate marine insurance in the District of Columbia, and for other purposes.”


*Approved, June 20, 1936.*

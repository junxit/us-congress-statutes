# Chapter 36

> AN ACT Authorizing an appropriation for payment to the Government of Norway in settlement of all claims for reimbursement on account of losses sustained by the owner and crew of the Norwegian steamer Tampen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for payment to the Government of Norway in full and final settlement of all claims for reimbursement on account of losses sustained by the owner and crew of the Norwegian steamer Tampen by reason of the detention of the vessel by the United States Coast Guard during June 1925, the sum of $8,765.


*Approved, January 27, 1936.*

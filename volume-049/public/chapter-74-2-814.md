# Chapter 814

> AN ACT To modify section 20 of the Permanent Appropriation Repeal Act, 1934, with reference to individual Indian money.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 20 of the Permanent Appropriation Repeal Act, approved June 26, 1934 (48 Stat. 1233), shall not be applicable to funds held in trust for individual Indians, associations of individual Indians, or for Indian corporations chartered under the Act of June 18, 1934 (48 Stat. 984).


*Approved, June 25, 1936.*

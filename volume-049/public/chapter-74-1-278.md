# Chapter 278

> JOINT RESOLUTION Requesting the President to proclaim October 9 as Leif Erikson Day.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States is hereby authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating October 9, 1935, as Leif Erikson Day and calling upon officials of the Government to display the flag of the United States on all Government buildings on said date and inviting the people of the United States to observe the day in schools and churches, or other suitable places, with appropriate ceremonies.


*Approved, June 19, 1935.*

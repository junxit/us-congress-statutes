# Chapter 78

> JOINT RESOLUTION To extend the time within which contracts may be modified or canceled under the provisions of section 5 of the Independent Offices Appropriation Act, 1934.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 5 of the Independent Offices Appropriation Act, 1934, is amended by striking out “April 30, 1935” and inserting in lieu thereof “October 31, 1935”,


*Approved, April 24, 1935.*

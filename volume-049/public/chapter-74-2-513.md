# Chapter 513

> AN ACT For the relief of the State of Maine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions of section 3646, as amended, of the Revised Statutes ofthe United States, the Chief Disbursing Officer of the Treasury Department is authorized and directed to issue, without the requirement of an indemnity bond, a duplicate of original check numbered 66562 and a duplicate of original check numbered 66563, drawn February 12, 1935, under his symbol 79088, in favor of “Treasurer, State of Maine (trust fund)” for $7,075 and $11,275, respectively, and lost, stolen, or miscarried in the mails.


*Approved, June 5, 1936.*

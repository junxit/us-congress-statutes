# Chapter 287

> AN ACT Authorizing the construction of buildings for the United States Representative in the Philippine Islands.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated not to exceed $750,000 for the necessary housing for office and residence purposes for the establishment of the United States Representative in the Philippine Islands, including the acquisition of land, the purchase, construction, and reconstruction of buildings, and the procurement of furniture, furnishings, and equipment.


*Approved, June 24, 1935.*

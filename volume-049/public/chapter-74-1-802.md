# Chapter 802

> AN ACT To amend the Inland Waterways Corporation Act, approved June 3, 1924, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subdivision (e) of section 3 of the Act entitled “An Act to create the Inland Waterways Corporation for the purpose of carrying out the mandate and purpose of Congress as expressed in sections 201 and 500 of the Transportation Act, and for other purposes”, approved June 3, 1924, as amended, is amended by striking out the words “ or Snake Rivers ”, and inserting in lieu thereof “ Snake, Sacramento, San Joaquin, or Savannah Rivers ”


*Approved, August 29, 1935.*

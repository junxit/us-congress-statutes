# Chapter 268

> JOINT RESOLUTION Providing for extension of cooperative work of the Geological Survey to Puerto Rico.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of law authorizing the making of topographic and geological surveys and conducting investigations relating to mineral and water resources by the United States Geological Survey in various portions of the United States be, and the same are hereby, extended to authorize such surveys and investigations in Puerto Rico.


*Approved, June 17, 1935.*

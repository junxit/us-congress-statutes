# Chapter 619

> AN ACT To extend the time for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Missouri River at or near Rulo, Nebraska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge authorized by an Act of Congress approved March 4, 1933, to be built by John C. Mullen, John H. Hutchings, and William Shepherd, their heirs, legal representatives, and assigns, across the Missouri River at or near Rulo, Nebraska, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from March 4, 1935.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, August 23, 1935.*

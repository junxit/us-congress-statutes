# Chapter 769

> AN ACT To extend the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Ohio River at or near Owensboro, Kentucky.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Ohio River, at or near Owensboro, Kentucky, authorized to be built by the State Highway Commission of Kentucky, by an Act of Congress approved June 9, 1932, and heretofore extended by an Act of Congress approved June 9, 1933, are hereby further extended one and three years, respectively, from the date of approval hereof.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, August 27, 1935.*

# Chapter 648

> JOINT RESOLUTION Authorizing the State of Arizona to transfer to the town of Benson without cost title to section 16, township 17 south, range 20 east, Gila and Salt River meridian, for school and park purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions of section 28 of the Act of Congress approved June 20, 1910 (36 Stat. 557–572), the State of Arizona is authorized to transfer without cost to the town of Benson title to section 16, township 17 south, range 20 east, Gila and Salt River meridian for park purposes.


*Approved, August 24, 1935.*

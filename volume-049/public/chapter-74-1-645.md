# Chapter 645

> AN ACT Authorizing the Secretary of the Navy to accept without cost to the United States certain lands in Duval County, State of Florida.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized to accept on behalf of the United States, free of all encumbrances, and without cost to the United States, a title in fee simple to the following tract of land in Duval County, in the State of Florida, to wit: Lots 20 to 29, both inclusive, in block 1, central business addition to South Jacksonville, and the foot of Hendricks Avenue (formerly known as “Ferry Street”) from Commerce Street to the Saint Johns River, together with all riparian rights thereunto belonging or in anywise appertaining: Provided, That the cost of maintaining same for the Naval Reserves shall be paid for by the State of Florida.


*Approved, August 24, 1935.*

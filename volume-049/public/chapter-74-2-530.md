# Chapter 530

> AN ACT Declaring Bayou Saint John, in the city of New Orleans, Louisiana, a nonnavigable stream.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Bayou Saint John, in the city of New Orleans, Louisiana, be, and the same is hereby, declared to be not a navigable water of the United States within the meaning of the laws enacted by Congress for the preservation and protection of such waters.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, June 5, 1936.*

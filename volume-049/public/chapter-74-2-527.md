# Chapter 527

> AN ACT To eliminate certain lands from the Craters of the Moon National Monument, Idaho.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the north half and north half of the south half section 16, township 2 north, range 24 east, Boise meridian, Idaho, be, and the same are hereby, eliminated from the Craters of the Moon National Monument.


*Approved, June 5, 1936.*

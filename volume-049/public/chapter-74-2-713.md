# Chapter 713

> JOINT RESOLUTION Fixing the date of meeting of the Seventy-fifth Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Seventy-fifth Congress shall assemble at noon on Tuesday, the 5th day of January 1937.


*Approved, June 22, 1936.*

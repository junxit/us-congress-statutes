# Chapter 282

> AN ACT Transferring certain national-forest lands to the Zuni Indian Reservation, New Mexico.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the lands in townships 8 and 9 north, ranges 16 and 17 west, of the New Mexico principal meridian, New Mexico, comprising the Miller Division of the Cibola National Forest, are hereby eliminated from the Cibola National Forest and withdrawn as an addition to the Zuni Indian Reservation, subject to any valid existing rights of any persons thereto.


*Approved, June 20, 1935.*

# Chapter 43

> AN ACT To change the designation of Leffler Place to Second Place.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the street designated as Leffler Place Northwest, running north from Oglethorpe Street to Peabody Street Northwest, be, and the same is hereby, changed to Second Place, thereby conforming to the general practice in the naming of short streets of this character in the District of Columbia.


*Approved, April 5, 1935.*

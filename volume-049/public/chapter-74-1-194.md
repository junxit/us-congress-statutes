# Chapter 194

> AN ACT To authorize appropriations for the completion of the public high school at Frazer, Montana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized, out of any funds in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $25,000 for the completion of the public high school at Frazer, Montana, and for necessary equipment in connection therewith for manual, laboratory, and other lines of training.


*Approved, June 7, 1935.*

# Chapter 645

> JOINT RESOLUTION To ratify and confirm the appointment of Isaac Gans as a member of the Alcoholic Beverage Control Board of the District of Columbia.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the appointment by the Commissioners of the District of Columbia of Isaac Gans as a member of the Alcoholic Beverage Control Board of the District of Columbia for a term of four years beginning February 4, 1936, is hereby ratified and confirmed.


*Approved, June 20, 1936.*

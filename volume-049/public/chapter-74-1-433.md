# Chapter 433

> AN ACT To amend the Act of June 27, 1930 (ch. 634, 46 Stat. 820).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of June 27, 1930 entitled “An Act to provide for terms of the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania” (ch. 634, 46 Stat. 820) is amended to read as follows:

> “Terms of the United States District Court for the Eastern Judicial District of Pennsylvania shall be held at Easton, Pennsylvania, on the first Tuesdays in June and November of each year: Provided, however, That all writs, precepts, and processes shall be returnable to the terms at Philadelphia and all court papers shall be kept in the clerk’s office at Philadelphia unless otherwise specially ordered by the court, and the terms at Philadelphia shall not be terminated or affected by the terms herein provided for at Easton.”


*Approved, August 3, 1935.*

# Chapter 423

> AN ACT To authorize the acquisition of land on McNeil Island.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Attorney Genera] is hereby authorized to acquire by condemnation proceedings all of that portion of McNeil Island which is not now owned by the United States, Gertrudis Island, and Pitt Island, all in the State of Washington, at a total cost of not to exceed $300,000.


*Approved, August 2, 1935.*

# Chapter 188

> AN ACT To extend the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Ohio River between Rockport, Indiana, and Owensboro, Kentucky.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge across the Ohio River between Rockport, Indiana, and Owensboro, Kentucky, authorized to be built by the Spencer County Bridge Commission, by an Act of Congress approved June 18, 1934, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from June 18, 1936.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, April 10, 1936.*

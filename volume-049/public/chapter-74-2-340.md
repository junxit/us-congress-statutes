# Chapter 340

> JOINT RESOLUTION To amend Public Act Numbered 435, Seventy-second Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and Rouse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the last proviso in that Public Act Numbered 435 of the Seventy-second Congress entitled “An Act to authorize the Secretary of the Interior to modify the terms of existing contracts for the sale of timber on Indian land when it is in the interest of the Indians so to do”, as amended, be, and the same hereby is, amended to read as follows: “And provided further, That the authority granted herein shall terminate on the 4th day of September 1936: Provided further, That all such modified contracts shall have the approval of the tribal general council for tribal lands and of the allottee for allotted lands”.


*Approved, May 6, 1936.*

# Chapter 435

> AN ACT Temporarily to exempt refunding bonds of the Government of Puerto Rico from the limitation of public indebtedness under the Organic Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That any bonds or other obligations of Puerto Rico hereafter issued for the purpose of retiring previously outstanding bonds or obligations shall not be included in computing the public indebtedness of Puerto Rico under section 3 of the Organic Act approved March 2, 1917, as amended, until six months after their issue.


*Approved, August 3, 1935.*

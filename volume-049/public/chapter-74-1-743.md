# Chapter 743

> AN ACT To amend the Filled Milk Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of March 4, 1923, entitled “An Act to prohibit the shipment of filled milk in interstate or foreign commerce” (U. S. C., title 21, ch. 3) be, and the same is hereby, amended by adding at the end thereof the following section:

> ## § 4.
>
> The Secretary of Agriculture is hereby authorized and directed to make and enforce such regulations as may in his judgment be necessary to carry out the purposes of this Act.”


*Approved, August 27, 1935.*

# Chapter 88

> AN ACT To amend Public Law Numbered 249, Seventy-first Congress, entitled “An Act to authorize the Secretary of the Navy to dispose of material no longer needed by the Navy.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Public Law Numbered 249, Seventy-first Congress, approved May 23, 1930, entitled “An Act to authorize the Secretary of the Navy to dispose of material no longer needed by the Navy” is amended by striking out the word “and” before the word “tools” and inserting after the word “tools” a comma following by “ 1 boats and boat equipment”.


*Approved, February 27, 1936.*

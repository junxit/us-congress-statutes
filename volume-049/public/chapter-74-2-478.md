# Chapter 478

> AN ACT Granting the consent of Congress to the Mississippi State Highway Commission to construct, maintain, and operate a free highway bridge across the Pascagoula River at or near Wilkerson’s Ferry, Mississippi.

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the consent of Congress is hereby granted to the Mississippi State Highway Commission to construct, maintain, and operate a free highway bridge and approaches thereto across the Pascagoula River, at a point suitable to the interests of navigation, at or near Wilkerson’s Ferry, Mississippi, in accordance with the provisions of an Act entitled “An Act to regulate the construction of bridges over navigable waters,” approved March 23, 1906.

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, June 2, 1936.*

# Chapter 376

> AN ACT To aid in defraying the expenses of the Sixteenth Triennial Convention of the World’s Woman’s Christian Temperance Union to be held in this country in June 1937.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $10,000 to aid in defraying the expenses of the Sixteenth Triennial Convention of the World’s Woman’s Christian Temperance Union to be held in this country in June 1937, such sum to be expended for such purposes and under such regulations as the Secretary of State shall prescribe and without regard to any other provision of law.


*Approved, May 11, 1936.*

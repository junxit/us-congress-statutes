# Chapter 816

> JOINT RESOLUTION To extend the time within which contracts may be modified or canceled under the provisions of section 5 of the Independent Offices Appropriation Act, 1934.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 5 of the Independent Offices Appropriation Act, 1934, as amended, be amended by striking out “October 31, 1935” and inserting in lieu thereof “March 31, 1936”: Provided, That the right of the United States to annul any fraudulent or illegal contract or to institute suit to recover sums paid thereon is in no manner affected by this joint resolution.


*Approved, August 29, 1935.*

# Chapter 244

> JOINT RESOLUTION Amending paragraph (4) of subsection (n) of section 12B of the Federal Reserve Act, as amended.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph (4) of subsection (n) of section 12B of the Federal Reserve Act, as amended, is amended by striking out “July 1, 1936” and inserting in lieu thereof “July 1, 1938”.


*Approved, April 21, 1936.*

# Chapter 16

> AN ACT To postpone the effective date of certain restrictions respecting air-mail contracts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 15 of the Act entitled “An Act to revise air-mail laws, and to establish a commission to make a report to the Congress recommending an aviation policy approved June 12, 1934, as amended, is amended by striking out “March 1, 1935” and inserting in lieu thereof “April 1, 1936”.


*Approved, February 21, 1935.*

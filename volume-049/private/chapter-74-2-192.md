# Chapter 192

> AN ACT Granting insurance to Lydia C. Spry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay insurance of $8,000, subject to the provisions and limitations of the War Risk Insurance Act, to Lydia C. Spry, widow of James Stewart Spry, late chief machinist’s mate, United States Navy.


*Approved, April 10, 1936.*

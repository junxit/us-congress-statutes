# Chapter 497

> AN ACT For the relief of Thomas F. Olsen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and, House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General is hereby authorized and directed to credit in the accounts of Thomas F. Olsen, postmaster at De Kalb, Illinois, in the sum of $18,687.19. Such sum represents postal funds in the amount of $136.30 and postage-stamp stock in the amount of $18,550.89, which were lost in the burglary of the post office at De Kalb, Illinois, on February 10, 1931, from no fault or negligence on the part of the postmaster.


*Approved, August 8, 1935.*

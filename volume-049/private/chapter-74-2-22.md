# Chapter 22

> AN ACT For the relief of Anna Hathaway.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 17 and 20 of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes”, approved September 7, 1916, as amended, are hereby waived in favor of Anna Hathaway, widow of Frank J. Hathaway, former postmaster at Willard, New Mexico, who is alleged to have sustained disability on or about October 16, 1928, while in the performance of his duties, subsequently resulting in his death: Provided, That compensation, if any, shall commence from and after the date of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, January 21, 1936.*

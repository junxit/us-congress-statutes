# Chapter 654

> AN ACT For the relief of William H. Hildebrand.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission shall be, and it is hereby, authorized and directed to receive and consider the claim of William H. Hildebrand, former employee of the United States naval station at Algiers, port of New Orleans, Louisiana, for injury sustained February 26, 1918, under the provisions of an Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes”, approved September 7, 1916, as amended, except that the time limitations in sections 15 to 20, inclusive, of said Act are hereby waived, any compensation hereunder to commence from and after the passage of this Act.


*Approved, June 20, 1936.*

# Chapter 366

> AN ACT For the relief of George W. Miller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the account of Chief Pay Clerk George W. Miller, a special disbursing agent of the Coast Guard, with the sum of $27.60, such amount representing a sum disbursed by him and disallowed by the General Accounting Office in notice of exception issued July 26, 1934, voucher numbered 462.


*Approved, July 2, 1935.*

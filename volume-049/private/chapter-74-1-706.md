# Chapter 706

> AN ACT For the relief of Edgar Sampson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 15, 17, and 20 of an Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes”, approved September 7, 1916, as amended (U. S. C., title 5, pars. 765, 767, and 770, on p. 79), are hereby waived in favor of Edgar Sampson, who is alleged to have sustained disability as the result of his employment in the United States Post Office, Brooklyn, New York, in December 1927: Provided, That no benefits shall accrue prior to the approval of this Act.


*Approved, August 26, 1935.*

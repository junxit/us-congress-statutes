# Chapter 508

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Ollie Myers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay out of the current appropriation “Pay of the Marine Corps”, to Mrs. Ollie Myers, mother of Earl Eugene Myers, late private, United States Marine Corps, an amount equal to six months’ pay at the rate said Earl Eugene Myers was receiving at the date of his death: Provided, That the said Mrs. Ollie Myers establish to the satisfaction of the Secretary of the Navy that she was actually dependent upon her son, Earl Eugene Myers, at the time of the latter’s death, and the determination of such fact by the Secretary of the Navy shall be final and conclusive upon the accounting officers of the Government.


*Approved, June 4, 1936.*

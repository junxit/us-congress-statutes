# Chapter 483

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Carlysle Von Thomas, Senior.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy is hereby authorized and directed to pay, out of the Naval Hospital fund, to Mrs. Carlysle Von Thomas, Senior, the sum of $168 to reimburse her for money paid by her for nurse services for her son, Carlysle Von Thomas, Junior, yeoman, third- class (F-1), United States Naval Reserve, incident to an operation for appendicitis performed on August 8, 1931, at Saint Luke’s Hospital, Chicago, Illinois, while Von Thomas was on active duty.


*Approved, August 7, 1935.*

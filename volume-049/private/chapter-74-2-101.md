# Chapter 101

> AN ACT For the relief of George Current.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission be, and is hereby, authorized to consider and determine, in the same manner and to the same extent as if application for the benefits of the Employees’ Compensation Act had been made within the one-year period required by sections 17 and 20 thereof, the claim of George Current, on account of disability due to epilepsy alleged to have been proximately caused by an injury received while on duty during his employment in the service of the United States between May 6, 1929, and March 14, 1934: Provided, That no benefits shall accrue prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved, February 28, 1936.*

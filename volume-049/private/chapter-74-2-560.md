# Chapter 560

> AN ACT Providing for the posthumous appointment of Ernest E. Dailey as a warrant radio electrician, United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to appoint, posthumously, Edwin1 E. Dailey, late a radioman, first class, United States Navy, a warrant radios electrician, United States Navy, and to deliver to the widow of said Edwin E. Dailey the warrant of such appointment. Such appointment shall be effective as of February 11, 1935.


*Approved, June 15, 1936.*

# Chapter 467

> AN ACT For the relief of John E. Fondahl.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged marines John E. Fondahl, formerly private in the Forty-fourth Company, Third Regiment, United States Marine Corps, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the naval service of the United States on September 14, 1921: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, August 7, 1935.*

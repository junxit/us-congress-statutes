# Chapter 102

> AN ACT For the relief of David N. Aiken.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers or sailors David N. Aiken, late assigned to the Naval Operating Base of the United States Marine Corps, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the naval service of the United States on the 16th day of March 1929: Provided, That no compensation, retirement pay, back pay, pension, or other benefit shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 28, 1936.*

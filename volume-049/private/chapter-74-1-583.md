# Chapter 583

> AN ACT For the relief of A. Cyril Crilley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to allow credit in the settlement of the accounts of A. Cyril Crilley, assistant trade commissioner and a special disbursing officer of the Bureau of Foreign and Domestic Commerce, in the sum of $113 for amount paid as freight on the shipment of an automobile of Henry B. Pentland, deceased trade commissioner, from Panama to Hollywood, California, upon the specific direction and authority of the Bureau, dated July 8, 1932.


*Approved, August 20, 1935.*

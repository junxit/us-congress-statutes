# Chapter 161

> AN ACT For the relief of John Costigan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Howe of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws and laws conferring rights upon honorably discharged soldiers, their widows, and dependent relatives, John Costigan shall hereafter be held and considered to have been in the military service of the United States as a private in Company D, Fifth Regiment United States Cavalry, from March 27, 1878, and to have been honorably discharged May 31, 1881: Provided, That no back pay, pension, or other back allowance shall accrue by reason of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, May 31, 1935.*

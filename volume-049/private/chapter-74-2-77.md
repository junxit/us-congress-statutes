# Chapter 77

> AN ACT For the relief of Fred Edward Nordstrom.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws or any laws conferring rights, privileges, or benefits upon persons honorably discharged from the United States Army Fred Edward Nordstrom shall be held and considered to have been honorably discharged as a private, Company G, Three Hundred and Fifth Regiment United States Infantry, on April 3, 1920: Provided, That no back pay, compensation, benefit, or allowance shall be held to have accrued by reason of this Act prior to its passage.


*Approved, February 17, 1936.*

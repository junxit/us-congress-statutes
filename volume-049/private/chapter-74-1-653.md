# Chapter 653

> AN ACT For the relief of Sanford Madison Strange.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions and limitations of sections 15 to 20, both inclusive, of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes”, approved September 7, 1916, as amended, the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission is hereby authorized and directed to receive and consider, when filed, the claim of Sanford Madison Strange for disability alleged to have been incurred by him on or about May 1, 1918, and on or about May 27, 1919, while in the employment of the navy yard, Navy Department, at Norfolk, Virginia, and to determine said claim upon its merits under the provisions of said Act: Provided, That no benefits shall accrue prior to the approval of this Act.


*Approved, August 24, 1935.*

# Chapter 564

> AN ACT For the relief of Robert J. Smith, alias William McClocklin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, sailors, and marines Robert J. Smith, alias William McClocklin, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States on October 6, 1899, as a private of Troop I, Third Regiment United States Cavalry: Provided, That no back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, August 19, 1935.*

# Chapter 462

> AN ACT For the relief of Evelyn Jotter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limitation provisions in section 10, and sections 17 and 20 of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes”, approved September 7, 1916, as amended, are hereby waived in favor of Evelyn Jotter, widow of Walter E. Jotter, who is alleged to have died as a result of injuries sustained while in the performance of his duties as associate forester, United States Forest Service, between January 10, 1919, and July 6, 1931: Provided, That no benefits shall accrue prior to the approval of this Act.


*Approved, August 7, 1935.*

# Chapter 630

> AN ACT For the relief of Don C. Fees.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to allow in the accounts of Don C. Fees, former disbursing clerk, Department of Justice, the sum of $416.16 paid by him under authority and direction of said department for the purchase, repair, maintenance, and operation of one motorcycle with side for transportation of freight, which was disallowed by said Comptroller General.


*Approved, August 23, 1935.*

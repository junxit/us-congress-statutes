# Chapter 227

> AN ACT To recognize the service of Brigadier General Edward R. Chrisman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to designate Brigadier General Edward R. Chrisman, retired, as professor of military science and tactics emeritus at the University of Idaho, at Moscow, Idaho, during the remainder of his natural life.


*Approved, April 15, 1936.*

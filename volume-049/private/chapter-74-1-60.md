# Chapter 60

> AN ACT For the relief of Margaret L. Carleton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Margaret L. Carleton, widow of Algar E. Carleton, late American consul at Riga, Latvia, the sum of $6,600, equal to one year’s salary of her deceased husband.


*Approved, April 11, 1935.*

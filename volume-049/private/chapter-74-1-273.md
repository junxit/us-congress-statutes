# Chapter 273

> AN ACT For the relief of John E. Click.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That John E. Click, chief clerk of the administrative office of the Alaska Division, Bureau of Indian Affairs, with headquarters now at Juneau, Alaska, is hereby relieved of reimbursing the United States for credit to the appropriation “Education of natives of Alaska, 1930-1931 ”, the sum of $261.82 paid to the Alaska Steamship Company, of Seattle, Washington, from said appropriation for transporting his personal and household goods from Seattle, Washington, to Juneau, Alaska, upon permanent change of station, under an authorization issued by the Department of the Interior.


*Approved, June 17, 1935.*

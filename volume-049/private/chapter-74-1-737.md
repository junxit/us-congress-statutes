# Chapter 737

> AN ACT For the relief of Cassie M. Lyne.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions and limitations of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes”, approved September 7, 1916, as amended, the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission is authorized and directed to pay, from the date of approval of this Act, to Cassie M. Lyne, in lieu of the retirement pay she is now receiving, the sum of $50 per month during her lifetime as compensation for injuries received while employed as a clerk in the Office of the Quartermaster General, War Department.


*Approved, August 26, 1935.*

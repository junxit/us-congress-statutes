# Chapter 791

> AN ACT For the relief of the estate of Hans Ditmanson, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States, in the settlement and adjustment of accounts and claims for services rendered at third- and fourth-class post offices, be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the account of Hans Ditmanson, deceased, former postmaster at Gulkana, Alaska, with $99.73, being the amount paid from postal receipts to Anna Leak for her voluntary services as acting postmaster at that postoffice from April 18, 1934, to September 30, 1934, inclusive.


*Approved, June 24, 1936.*

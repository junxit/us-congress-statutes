# Chapter 127

> AN ACT For the relief of Charles L. Graves.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he is hereby, directed to allow credit in the accounts of Charles L. Graves, superintendent and special disbursing agent at Jicarilla Agency, New Mexico, for payments aggregating $51,277, made from tribal funds of the Jicarilla Indians to various persons in connection with the purchase of sheep for issue to various members of the tribe, to which payments exception was taken by the General Accounting Office for the reason as claimed that there was no authority of law therefor.


*Approved, May 15, 1935.*

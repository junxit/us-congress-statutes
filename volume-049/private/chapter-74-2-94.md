# Chapter 94

> AN ACT For the relief of Carl A. Butler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers Carl A. Butler, who was a member of the United States Marine Corps, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a member of that organization on the 31st day of March 1919: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 28, 1936.*

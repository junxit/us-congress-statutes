# Chapter 575

> AN ACT For the relief of Ruth Edna Reavis (now Horsley).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the statutory period of entry in the case of Buffalo, Wyoming, 030423, Ruth Edna Reavis (now Horsley), is extended two years to permit the fulfillment of necessary residence and improvement requirements on the land.


*Approved, June 15, 1936.*

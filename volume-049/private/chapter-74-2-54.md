# Chapter 54

> AN ACT For the relief of the Norfolk Southern Railroad Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That jurisdiction is hereby conferred upon the Court of Claims of the United States, notwithstanding any limitations upon the jurisdiction and power of such court, to hear, determine, and render judgment upon the claim of the Norfolk Southern Railroad Company against the United States, arising out of or incident to the removal of an old bridge and the construction of a new drawbridge and fender system over the Albemarle and Chesapeake Canal, now the Virginia cut of the Inland Waterway, near Great Bridge, Virginia, to comply with orders of the War Department.


*Approved, February 11, 1936.*

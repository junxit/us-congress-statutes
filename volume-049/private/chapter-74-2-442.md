# Chapter 442

> AN ACT To authorize the President to bestow the Navy Cross upon Brigadier General Robert H. Dunlap, United States Marine Corps, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States is hereby authorized to bestow the Navy Cross upon Brigadier General Robert H. Dunlap, United States Marine Corps, for distinguishing himself conspicuously by extraordinary courage on May 19, 1931, at LaFariniere, Cinq-Mars-la-Pile, France, where he met his death in a supreme effort to save the life of a French peasant woman, and to deliver said medal to Katherine W. Dunlap, the widow of Brigadier General Dunlap.


*Approved, May 21, 1936.*

# Chapter 673

> AN ACT For the relief of Clara Imbesi and Domenick Imbesi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions and limitations of sections 15 to 20, both inclusive, of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes”, approved September 7, 1916, as amended, the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission be, and the same is hereby, authorized and directed to receive and consider, when filed, the claim of Clara Imbesi and Domenick Imbesi on account of the death of the late Lawrence P. Imbesi on July 16, 1933, as a result of alleged personal injuries sustained while in the performance of his official duties as a temporary carrier at the post office, Ocean City, New Jersey, and to determine said claim upon its merits under the provisions of said Act.


*Approved, June 20, 1936.*

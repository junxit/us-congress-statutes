# Chapter 53

> AN ACT For the relief of Lieutenant Robert A. J. English, United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Lieutenant Robert A. J. English, United States Navy, be, and he is hereby, restored to full-pay and allowances status during such time as he served with the Byrd Antarctic Expedition II, and his services with said expedition shall be counted on his record as “sea duty.”


*Approved, February 11, 1936.*

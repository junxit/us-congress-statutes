# Chapter 313

> AN ACT For the relief of Ward J. Lawton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to credit in the accounts of Ward J. Lawton, special disbursing agent, Lighthouse Service, Department of Commerce, the sum of $204 paid to the Liberty Brush Company, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania (voucher numbered 6660), May 17, 1932, which was later disallowed by the Comptroller General of the United States.


*Approved, June 25, 1935.*

# Chapter 397

> AN ACT For the relief of Robert J. Enochs.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to allow credit in the accounts of Doctor Robert J. Enochs, former superintendent and special disbursing agent of the Choctaw Indian Agency, Philadelphia, Mississippi, for an expenditure of $80.07 made in January 1932 for shoes, and paid from the appropriation “Support of Indians and Administration of Indian Property, 1932.”


*Approved, July 19, 1935.*

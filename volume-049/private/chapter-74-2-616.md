# Chapter 616

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Harold W. Foght.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to allow credit to Harold W. Foght, Superintendent of the Cherokee Indian Agency, North Carolina, for the sum of $377.40, which amount was expended from appropriated funds for the transportation of the household effects of Doctor Foght to his new post of duty at Cherokee, North Carolina.


*Approved, June 19, 1936.*

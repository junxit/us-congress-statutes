# Chapter 98

> AN ACT For the relief of William L. Jenkins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to credit the accounts of William L. Jenkins as American consul, formerly at Trebizond, Turkey, with the sum of $2,000, such sum representing the amount for which he is held personally liable on account of official vouchers lost because of warlike conditions in Turkey during 1916 and 1917.


*Approved, May 8, 1935.*

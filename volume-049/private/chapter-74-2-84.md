# Chapter 84

> AN ACT To authorize the presentation of a Distinguished Flying Cross to Lieutenant Colonel Francis T. Evans, United States Marine Corps.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is hereby authorized to present a Distinguished Flying Cross to Lieutenant Colonel Francis T. Evans, United States Marine Corps, for extraordinary achievement while participating in an aerial flight, prior to April 6, 1917: Provided, That the President shall ascertain that the achievement was of such character as to justify the award.


*Approved, February 25, 1936.*

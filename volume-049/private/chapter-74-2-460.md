# Chapter 460

> AN ACT For the relief of Jesse S. Post.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged enlisted personnel of the United States Army Jesse S. Post, private, Machine Gun Company, One Hundred and Sixteenth Regiment United States Infantry, shall be held to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States on the 7th day of June 1919: Provided, That no pension, back pay, bounty, or allowances shall be held to have accrued prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved, May 26, 1936.*

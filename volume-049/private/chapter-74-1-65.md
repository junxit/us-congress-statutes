# Chapter 65

> AN ACT For the relief of Charles E. Dagenett.

*Be, it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the General Accounting Office is hereby authorized to credit the accounts of Charles E. Dagenett, supervisor of Indian employment and special disbursing officer (retired), in the sum of $125.56, representing funds expended by him in that sum for telephone tolls, lodging, traveling expenses, and so forth, for himself and others in connection with work under his supervision.


*Approved, April 11, 1935.*

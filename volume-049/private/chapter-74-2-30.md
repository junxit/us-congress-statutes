# Chapter 30

> AN ACT Giving jurisdiction to the Court of Claims to hear and determine the claim of the Cherokee Fuel Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Court of Claims of the United States be, and hereby is, given jurisdiction to hear and determine the claim of the Cherokee Fuel Company, Incorporated, Kansas City, Missouri, for damages and for losses resulting from the cancelation by the depot quartermaster at Saint Louis, Missouri, of a contract between the United States Quartermaster Corps and the claimant, dated July 1, 1920, for furnishing coal at Camp Funston, Kansas, to the Quartermaster Corps.


*Approved, January 24, 1936.*

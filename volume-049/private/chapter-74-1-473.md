# Chapter 473

> AN ACT Correcting date of enlistment of Elza Bennett in the United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the laws relating to the Navy and the Naval Reserve Elza Bennett, of the United States Navy, shall for all purposes be held and considered to have reenlisted in the Navy on June 26, 1925: Provided, That no compensation, retirement pay, back pay, pension, or other benefit shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, August 7, 1935.*

# Chapter 52

> AN ACT Granting six months’ pay to Hester Hamilton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of the appropriation “Pay of the Army, 1935 ”, to Hester Hamilton, dependent mother of Milton Hamilton, deceased, late of Company K. Twenty-second Regiment United States Infantry, an amount equal to six months’ pay at the rate said Milton Hamilton was receiving at the date of his death.


*Approved, April 8, 1935.*

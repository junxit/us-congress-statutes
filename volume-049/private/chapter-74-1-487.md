# Chapter 487

> AN ACT For the relief of Captain George W. Steele, Junior, United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to credit the accounts of Captain George W. Steele, Junior, United States Navy, in the sum of $66.45, representing the amount finally disallowed by the Comptroller General in connection with certain payments made by Captain Steele while naval attaché at Paris, France, to Lieutenant Felix L. Johnson, United States Navy.


*Approved, August 7, 1935.*

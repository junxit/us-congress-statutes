# Chapter 876

> AN ACT Authorizing the President to present a gold medal to George M. Cohan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is authorized to present, but not in the name of Congress, a gold medal of appropriate design to George M. Cohan, in recognition of his services during the World War in composing the patriotic song “Over There”, and prior thereto that thrilling song “A Grand Old Flag.”


*Approved, June 29, 1936.*

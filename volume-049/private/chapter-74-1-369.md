# Chapter 369

> AN ACT For the relief of Carrie McIntyre.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Carrie McIntyre, mother of Henry D. McIntyre, who served as a private in Service Company, Fourth Regiment United States Infantry, shall be considered as the duly designated beneficiary and dependent of the late Henry D. McIntyre under the Act approved December 17, 1919 (141 Stat. 367).


*Approved, July 3, 1935.*

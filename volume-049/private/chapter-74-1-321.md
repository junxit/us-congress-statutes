# Chapter 321

> AN ACT For the relief of Cletus F. Hoban.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 15 to 20, inclusive, of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes”, approved September 7, 1916, as amended, are hereby waived in favor of Cletus F. Hoban, on account of disability alleged to have been incurred while a civil employee of the United States in the Internal Revenue Service at Los Angeles, California: Provided, That no benefits shall accrue prior to the approval of this Act.


*Approved, June 27, 1935.*

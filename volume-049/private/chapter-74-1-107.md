# Chapter 107

> AN ACT For the relief of Nellie Oliver.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers Robert Oliver, alias John Lear, deceased husband of Nellie Oliver, who was a member of Company C, Thirteenth Regiment New Hampshire Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held to have been discharged under honorable conditions from the military service of the United States as a member of that organization on the 6th day of July 1864: Provided, That no bounty, back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, May 10, 1935.*

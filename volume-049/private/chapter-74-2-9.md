# Chapter 9

> AN ACT For the relief of R. G. Andis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General is authorized and directed to cancel the charge, in the amount of $68, entered on the accounts of R. G. Andis, former postmaster at Presho, South Dakota, by reason of his deposit of funds of the United States in the First State Bank of Presho, Presho, South Dakota, and the subsequent failure of such bank.


*Approved, January 17, 1936.*

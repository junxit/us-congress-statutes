# Senate Concurrent Resolution 13

> army day

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That Saturday, April 6, 1935, be recognized by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, as Army Day, and that the President of the United States be requested, as Commander in Chief, to order military units throughout the United States to assist civic bodies in appropriate celebration to such extent as he may deem advisable.


*Passed, April 4, 1935.*

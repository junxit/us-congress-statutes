# House Concurrent Resolution 16

> sophie de soto

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the action of the Speaker of the House of Representatives and of the President of the Senate in signing the enrolled bid (H. R. 330) entitled “An Act for the relief of Sophie de Sota”, be rescinded, and that in the reenrollment of the said bill the Clerk of the House of Representatives be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to make the following correction, namely: Strike out the words “de Sota” wherever they appear in said bill and title and insert in lieu thereof the words “de Soto”.


*Passed, March 11, 1935.*

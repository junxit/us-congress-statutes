# House Concurrent Resolution 53

> recess of congress

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That, when the two Houses adjourn on Monday, June 8, 1936, they stand adjourned until 12:00 o’clock meridian Monday, June 15, 1936.


*Passed, June 5, 1936.*

## Marginal notes

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

- June 5, 1936. [H. Con. Res., No. 53.]
- Recess of Congress from June 8 until June 15, 1936.

# House Concurrent Resolution 14

> sophie de sota

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President of the United States be, and he is hereby, requested to return to the House of Representatives the enrolled bill (H. R. 330) entitled “An Act for the relief of Sophie de Sota.”


*Passed, March 6, 1935.*

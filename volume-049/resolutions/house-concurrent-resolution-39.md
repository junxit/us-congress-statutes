# House Concurrent Resolution 39

> enrolled bills and resolutions

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That notwithstanding the adjournment of the first session of the Seventy-fourth Congress, the President of the Senate and the Speaker of the House of Representatives be, and they are hereby, authorized to sign any enrolled bill or joint resolution duly passed by the two Houses and which have been examined by the Committee on Enrolled Bills of each House and found truly enrolled.


*Passed, August 24, 1935.*

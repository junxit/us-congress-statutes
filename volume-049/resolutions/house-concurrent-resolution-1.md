# House Concurrent Resolution 1

> joint meeting

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the hall of the House of Representatives on Friday, the 4th day of January 1935, at 12:30 o’clock in the afternoon, for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed, January 3, 1935.*

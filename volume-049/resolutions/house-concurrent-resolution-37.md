# House Concurrent Resolution 37

> public works on rivers and harbors

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (H. R. 6732) authorizing the construction, repair, and preservation of certain public works on rivers and harbors, and for other purposes, the Clerk of the House of Representatives is authorized and directed to make the following changes:

Page 16, line 11, of the Senate engrossed amendments strike out “4” and insert: “3”.

Page 25, line 12, of the Senate engrossed amendments strike out “5” and insert: “4”.

# House Concurrent Resolution 40

> adjournment

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Monday, the twenty-sixth day of August 1935, and that when they adjourn on said day they stand adjourned sine die.


*Passed, August 26, 1935.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 63

> adjournment

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Saturday, the 20th day of June, 1936, and that when they adjourn on said day they stand adjourned sine die.


*Passed, June 20, 1936.*

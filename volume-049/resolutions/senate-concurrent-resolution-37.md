# Senate Concurrent Resolution 37

> arms and other war munitions

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That forty-four thousand copies of part 3 and subsequent parts of Senate Report Numbered 944, submitted to the Senate pursuant to S. Res. 206 authorizing the appointment of a special committee to make certain investigations concerning the manufacture and sale of arms and other war munitions, be printed for the use of the Senate Special Committee on Investigation of the Munitions Industry.


*Passed, June 3, 1936.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 19

> extension of bank deposit insurance

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Secretary of the Senate be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, in the enrollment of the joint resolution (S. J. Res. 152) to extend for one year the temporary plan for deposit insurance provided for by section 12B of the Federal Reserve Act, as amended, to amend the title thereof by striking out the words “for one year” and inserting in lieu thereof “to August 31, 1935”.


*Passed, June 28, 1935.*

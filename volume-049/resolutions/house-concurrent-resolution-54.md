# House Concurrent Resolution 54

> signing enrolled bills, etc.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That, notwithstanding any recesses of the Senate or House of Representatives or the adjournment of the second session of the Seventyfourth Congress, the President of the Senate and the Speaker of the House of Representatives be, and they are hereby, authorized to sign any enrolled bills or joint resolutions duly passed by the two Houses and which have been examined by the Committee on Enrolled Bills of each House and found truly enrolled.


*Passed, June 5, 1936.*

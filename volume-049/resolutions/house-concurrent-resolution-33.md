# House Concurrent Resolution 33

> second deficiency act of 1935

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That in the enrollment of H. R. 8554, the Second Deficiency Appropriation Act, fiscal year 1935, the Clerk of the House of Representatives is authorized and directed to strike out the word “year” in line 17, page 10, of the engrossed bill, and insert in lieu thereof the following: “years 1935 and”.


*Passed, August 7, 1935.*

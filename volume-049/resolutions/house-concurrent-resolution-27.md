# House Concurrent Resolution 27

> statue of hannibal hamlin

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed with illustrations, and bound, in such form and style as may be directed by the Joint Committee on Printing, the proceedings at the unveiling in Statuary Hall, upon the acceptance of the statue of Hannibal Hamlin, presented by the State of Maine, one thousand copies, of which two hundred shall be for the use of the Senate and five hundred for the use of the House of Representatives, and the remaining three hundred copies shall be for the use and distribution of the Senators and Representatives in Congress from the State of Maine.

## § 2.

The Joint Committee on Printing is hereby authorized to have the copy prepared for the Public Printer, who shall provide suitable illustrations to be bound with these proceedings.


*Passed, July 1, 1935.*

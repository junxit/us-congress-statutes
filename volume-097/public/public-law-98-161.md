# Public Law 98–161

> Joint Resolution Increasing the statutory limit on the public debt.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (b) of increasing section 3101 of title 31, United States Code, is amended by striking out the dollar limitation contained in such subsection and inserting in lieu thereof “$1,389,000,000,000, or $1,490,000,000,000 on and after October 1, 1983,”.


*Approved November 21, 1983.*

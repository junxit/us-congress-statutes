# Public Law 98–14

> An Act To amend title 37, United States Code, to extend certain expiring enlistment and reenlistment bonuses for the Armed Forces.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,,*

That sections 308(g) and 308a(c) of title 37, United States Code, are amended by striking out “March 31, 1983” and inserting in lieu thereof “September 30, 1984”.

## § 2.

Section 308f(c) of title 37, United States Code, is amended by striking out “September 30, 1983” and inserting in lieu thereof “September 30, 1984”.


*Approved March 30, 1983.*

# Public Law 98–105

> An Act To amend title 38, United States Code, to extend for one year the authority of the Veterans’ Administration to provide certain contract medical services in Puerto Rico and the Virgin Islands.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 601(4)(C)(v) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking out “September 30, 1983” and inserting in lieu thereof “September 30, 1984”.


*Approved September 30, 1983.*

# Public Law 98–6

> An Act To repeal section 311 of the Federal Public Transportation Act of 1982.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 311 of the Federal Public Transportation Act of 1982 is repealed.


*Approved March 16, 1983.*

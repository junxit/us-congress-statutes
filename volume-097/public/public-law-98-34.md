# Public Law 98–34

> An Act To increase the permanent public debt limit, and for other purpose.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. INCREASE IN PUBLIC DEBT UMIT.

- **(a)** *General Rule.—* Subsection (b) of section 3101 of title 31, United States Code, is amended by striking out “$400,000,000,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$1,389,000,000,000”.
- **(b)** *Repeal of Temporary Increase.—* Effective on the date of the enactment of this Act, the first section of the Act of September 30, 1982, entitled “Joint resolution to provide for a temporary increase in the public debt limit” (Public Law 97–270) is hereby repealed.

## § 2. INCREASE IN UMIT ON LONG-TERM BONDS.

Subsection (a) of section 8102 of title 31, United States Code, is amended by striking out “$110,000,000,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$150,000,000,000”.


*Approved May 26, 1983.*

# Public Law 98–174

> An Act To designate the Federal Building in Seattle, Washington, as the “Henry M. Jackson Federal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the building located at 915 Second Avenue, Seattle, Washington, known as the Federal Building, shall hereafter be known and designated as the “Henry M. Jackson Federal Building”. Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to that building shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Henry M. Jackson Federal Building”.


*Approved November 29, 1983.*

# Public Law 98–122

> An Act To designate the Federal Building in Las Cruces, New Mexico, as the “Harold L. Runnels Federal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the building located at 200 East Griggs Street, Las Cruces, New Mexico, known as the Federal Building, shall hereafter be known and designated as the “Harold L. Runnels Federal Building”. Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to that building shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Harold L. Runnels Federal Building”.


*Approved October 12, 1983.*

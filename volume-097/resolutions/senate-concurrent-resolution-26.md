# Senate Concurrent Resolution 26

> MX MISSILE PROCUREMENT AND BASING MODE DEVELOPMENT—APPROVAL OF FUNDS

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the House of Representatives and the Senate of the United States approve the obligation and expenditure of funds appropriated in Public Law 97–377 for MX missile procurement and full-scale engineering development of a basing mode for the MX missile.


*Agreed to May 26, 1983.*

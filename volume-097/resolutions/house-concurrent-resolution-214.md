# House Concurrent Resolution 214

> JOHN F. KENNEDY—COMMEMORATION OF HIS DEATH

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Rotunda of the United States Capitol is hereby authorized to be used on November 16, 1983, in such a manner as the Speaker and minority leader of the United States House of Representatives and the majority leader and minority leader of the United States Senate may deem appropriate to commemorate the twentieth anniversary of the death of President John Fitzgerald Kennedy. Physical preparations for the conduct of the ceremony shall be carried out in accordance with such conditions as may be prescribed by the Architect of the Capitol.


*Agreed to November 15, 1983.*

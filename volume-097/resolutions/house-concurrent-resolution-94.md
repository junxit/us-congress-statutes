# House Concurrent Resolution 94

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Thursday, March 24, 1983, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian on Tuesday, April 5, 1983, and that when the Senate adjourns on Thursday, March 24, 1983, or Friday, March 25, 1983, pursuant to a motion made by the Majority Leader in accordance with this resolution, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian on Tuesday, April 5, 1983.


*Agreed to March 24, 1983.*

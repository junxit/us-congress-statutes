# House Concurrent Resolution 102

> CORRECTIONS IN ENROLLMENT OF H.R. 1900

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (H.R. 1900) to assure the solvency of the Social Security Trust Funds, to reform the medicare reimbursement of hospitals, to extend the Federal supplemental compensation program, and for other purposes, the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall make the corrections specified in the succeeding sections of this concurrent resolution.

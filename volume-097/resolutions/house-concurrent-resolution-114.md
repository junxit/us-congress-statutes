# House Concurrent Resolution 114

> JOINT MEETING

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Wednesday, April 27, 1983, at 8 o’clock poet meridiem, for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Agreed to April 21, 1983.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 1

> JOINT MEETING

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Tuesday, January 25, 1983, at 9 o’clock post meridiem for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Agreed to January 25, 1983.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 8

> ADJOURNMENT—SENATE

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring,*

That when the Senate adjourns on Thursday, February 3, 1983, or Friday, February 4, 1983, pursuant to a motion of the Majority Leader or his designee, pursuant to this resolution, it stand adjourned until 12 noon on Monday, February 14, 1983.


*Agreed to February 2, 1983.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 246

- **(1)**
  - On the first page, immediately after line 2, of the engrossed House bill, insert the following:
  - central and southern florida
- **(2)**

  - central and southern florida
- **(3)**
  - On page 2, immediately after line 3, of the engrossed House bill, insert the following:
  - central and southern florida
- **(4)**
  - On page 2, immediately after line 9, of the engrossed House bill, insert the following:
  - central and southern florida
- **(5)**

  - central and southern florida
- **(6)**
  - On page 2, immediately after line 21, of the engrossed House bill, insert the following:
  - central and southern florida
- **(7)**
  - On page 3, immediately after line 2, of the engrossed House bill, insert the following:
  - central and southern florida


*Passed December 18, 1963.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 32

> “SPACE SATELLITE COMMUNICATIONS”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Select Committee on Small Business one thousand additional copies of its hearings of August 2, 3, 4, 9, 10, and 11, 1961, Eighty-seventh Congress, entitled “Space Satellite Communications.”


*Agreed to June 18, 1963.*

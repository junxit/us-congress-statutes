# Senate Concurrent Resolution 36

> CORRECTION OF S. 1035

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Secretary of the Senate is authorized and directed, in the enrollment of the bill (S. 1035) to extend the provisions of section 3 of Public Law 87–346, relating to dual rate contracts, to make the following correction, viz: on line 4, change “76 Stat.” to “75 Stat.”.


*Agreed to April 3, 1963.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 35

> JANUARY 1963 ECONOMIC REPORT OF THE PRESIDENT

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Joint Economic Committee fifteen hundred additional copies of part 1 of its hearings on the January 1963 Economic Report of the President, current Congress.


*Agreed to June 18, 1963.*

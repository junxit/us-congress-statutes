# House Concurrent Resolution 223

> CIVIL RIGHTS HEARINGS

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on the Judiciary, House of Representatives, three thousand additional copies each of the publications entitled “Civil Rights Hearings, Before Subcommittee Numbered 5 of the Committee on the Judiciary, House of Representatives, Parts 1, 2, and 3”, Eighty-eighth Congress, first session.


*Passed November 5, 1963.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 59

> “FEDERAL DISASTER RELIEF MANUAL”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on Government Operations twenty-five thousand additional copies of the revised edition of the committee print entitled “Federal Disaster Relief Manual”, which was issued by that committee on August 30, 1963.


*Agreed to October 16, 1963.*

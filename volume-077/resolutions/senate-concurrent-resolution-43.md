# Senate Concurrent Resolution 43

> “DEVELOPMENT,GROWTH, AND STATE OF THE ATOMIC ENERGY INDUSTRY”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Joint Committee on Atomic Energy one thousand additional copies each of part 1 and part 2 of its 1963 hearings on the “Development, Growth, and State of the Atomic Energy Industry”.


*Agreed to June 18, 1963.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 86

> “OUR FLAG”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the publication entitled “Our Flag”, published by the Office of Armed Services Information and Education, Department of Defense, be printed with illustrations as a House document; and that three hundred thousand additional copies be printed, of which two hundred thousand shall be for the use of the House of Representatives, and one hundred thousand shall be for the use of the Senate.


*Passed April 11, 1963.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 61

> ORGANIZED CRIME AND ILLICIT TRAFFIC IN NARCOTICS

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Government Operations not to exceed four thousand additional copies of all parts of the hearings held by its Permanent Investigating Subcommittee during the current session on organized crime and illicit traffic in narcotics.


*Agreed to October 16, 1963.*

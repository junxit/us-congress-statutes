# Senate Concurrent Resolution 30

> “CONCENTRATION RATIOS IN MANUFACTURING INDUSTRY, 1958”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on the Judiciary two thousand additional copies of part 1 of its committee print of the Eighty-seventh Congress entitled “Concentration Ratios in Manufacturing Industry, 1958”, a report prepared by the Bureau of the Census for the Subcommittee on Antitrust and Monopoly.


*Agreed to June 18, 1963.*

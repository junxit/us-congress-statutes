# Senate Concurrent Resolution 67

> WATER POLLUTION CONTROL

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Public Works not to exceed two thousand additional copies of the hearings on water pollution control, S. 649, and related bills, by the Special Subcommittee on Air and Water Pollution during the current session of Congress.


*Agreed to December 21, 1963.*

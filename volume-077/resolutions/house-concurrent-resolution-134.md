# House Concurrent Resolution 134

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That, when the House adjourns on Thursday, April 11, 1963, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Monday, April 22, 1963.


*Passed April 10, 1963.*

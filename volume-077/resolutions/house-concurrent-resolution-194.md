# House Concurrent Resolution 194

> “PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE TO THE FLAG”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed three hundred and twenty-two thousand five hundred additional copies of House Document Numbered 225, Eighty-fourth Congress, first session, entitled “Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag”, of which two hundred and nineteen thousand five hundred copies shall be for the use of the House of Representatives and one hundred and three thousand copies shall be for the use of the Senate.


*Passed August 15, 1963.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 37

> “STATE DEPARTMENT SECURITY”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on the Judiciary eight thousand additional copies of the report (committee print) of its Internal Security Subcommittee of the Eighty-seventh Congress, second session, entitled “State Department Security”; five thousand copies each of part 1 (the William Wieland case), part 2 (the Office of Security), part 3 (the new passport regulations), part 4 (testimony of Elmer Hipsley, Otto Otepka, John Leahy, Roger Jones, Scott McLeod, Andreas Lowenfeld), and part 5 (testimony of William Wieland), of the subcommittee’s hearings of the Eighty-seventh Congress on State Department security; and five thousand additional copies of part 13 of the subcommittee’s hearings of the Eighty-seventh Congress on Communist threat to the United States through the Caribbean.


*Agreed to June 18, 1963.*

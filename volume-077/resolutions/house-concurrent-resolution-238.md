# House Concurrent Resolution 238

> JOINT MEETING

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Wednesday, the 27th day of November, 1963, at 12:30 o’clock postmeridian, for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed November 26, 1963.*

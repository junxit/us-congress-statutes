# Public Law 88–122

> AN ACT To modify the timid control project for Rend Lake, Illinois.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the flood control project for Rend Lake, Illinois; authorized in section 203 of the Flood Control Act. of 1962, is hereby modified to authorize the Secretary of the Army to credit local interests against their required payment for water supply for any work done by such interests on such project, if he approves such work as being in accordance with the project as authorized.


*Approved September 9, 1963.*

# Public Law 88–97

> AN ACT To change the name of fort Randall Reservoir in the State of South Dakota to Lake Francis Case.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Fort Randall Reservoir hi the State of South Dakota shall be known as Lake Francis Case in honor of the late Senator of South Dakota, who was so very instrumental in the development, of the Missouri River Basin program. Any law, regulation, document, or record of the United States hi which such reservoir is referred to by any other name shall be held and considered to refer to such reservoir by the name of Lake Francis Case.


*Approved August 15, 1963.*

# Public Law 88–83

> AN ACT To provide for the free entry of an orthicon image assembly for the use of the Medical College of Georgia, Augusta, Georgia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to admit free of duty one orthicon image assembly imported for the use of the Medical College of Georgia, Augusta, Georgia.
- **(b)** If the liquidation of the entry of the article described in subsection (a) has become final, such entry shall be reliquidated and the appropriate refund of duty shall be made.


*Approved August 5, 1963.*

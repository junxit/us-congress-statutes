# Public Law 88–63

> AN ACT To improve the active duty promotion opportunity of Air Force officers from the grade of major to the grade of lieutenant colonel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of September 1, 1961, Public Law 87–194 (75 Stat. 424), is amended by striking out the figure “1963” and inserting the figure “1965” in place thereof.


*Approved July 17, 1963.*

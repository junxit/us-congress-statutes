# Public Law 88–42

> AN ACT To amend Public Law 86–272, as amended, with reflect to the reporting date.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 202 of Public Law 86–272 (73 Stat. 556), as amended, is amended to read as follows:

> ## § 202.
>
> The committees shall report to their respective Houses the results of such studies, together with their proposals for legislation on or before March 31, 1964.”


*Approved June 21, 1963.*

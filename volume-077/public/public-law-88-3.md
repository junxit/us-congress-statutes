# Public Law 88–3

> AN ACT To amend section 904, title 38, United States Code, so that burial allowances might be paid In eases where discharges were changed by competent authority after death of the veteran from dishonorable to conditions other than dishonorable.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 904, title 38, United States Code, is amended by inserting, immediately after the first sentence thereof the following new sentence: “If the burial allowance was not payable at the death of the veteran because of the nature of his discharge from the service, but after his death his discharge has been corrected by competent authority so as to reflect a discharge from the service under conditions other than dishonorable, then the burial allowance may be paid if a claim is filed within two years from whichever last occurs, the date of correction of the discharge or the date of enactment of this sentence.”


*Approved April 2, 1963.*

# Public Law 88–203

> AN ACT To amend title V of the Agricultural Act of 1949, as amended, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That section 510 of the Agricultural Act of 1949, as amended, is amended by striking “December 31, 1963” and inserting “December 31, 1964”.


*Approved December 13, 1963.*

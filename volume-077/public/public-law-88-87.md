# Public Law 88–87

> AN ACT To provide fur the free entry of a mass spectrometer for the use of Stanford University, Stanford. California.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to admit free of duty the mass spectrometer (and its accompanying spare parts assortment) imported for the use of Stanford University, Stanford, California, which was entered during October 1962, pursuant to Consumption Entry 1232.
- **(b)** If the liquidation of the entry of the articles described in subsection (a) has become final, such entry shall be reliquidated and the appropriate refund of duty shall be made.


*Approved August 5, 1963.*

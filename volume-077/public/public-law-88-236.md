# Public Law 88–236

> AN ACT To amend sections 510 and 591 of title 10, United States Code, to remove the requirement that an alien must make a declaration of intention to become a citizen of the United: States before he may be enlisted or appointed in a reserve component.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 510(b)(1) and 591(b)(1) of title 10, United States Code, are each amended to read as follows:

> - **(1)** he is a citizen of the United States or has been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence under chapter 12 of title 8; or”.


*Approved December 23, 1963.*

# Public Law 88–109

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 1964, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That clause (c) of section 102 of the joint resolution of June 29, 1963 (Public Law 88–55), is hereby amended by striking out “August. 31, 1963” and inserting in Lieu thereof “October 31, 1963”.


*Approved August 28, 1963.*

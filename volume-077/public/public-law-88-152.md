# Public Law 88–152

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Elizabeth G. Mason.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the award of death compensation which the Veterans’ Administration has held that Mrs. Elizabeth G. Mason, of Houlton, Maine, is entitled to receive as a result, of its finding, because of the death of her late husband, Major Theodore P. Mason, who was killed in combat in Belgium on September 9, 1944, shall be held and considered to be effective as of the day following the said Theodore P. Mason’s death on the basis of her original claim for such death compensation which she filed in March 1945, six months after her husband’s death; and the Administrator of Veterans’ Affairs is hereby authorized and directed to make retroactive payments in accordance with such entitlement.

## § 2.

Section 104(b) of the Civil Rights Act of 1957, as amended (42 U.S.C. 1975c(b)), is amended by striking out “September 30, 1963” and inserting in lieu thereof “September 30, 1964”.


*Approved October 17, 1963.*

# Public Law 88–123

> AN ACT To designate the McGee Bend Dam and Reservoir on the Angelina River, Texas, as the Sam Rayburn Dam and Reservoir.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the McGee Bend Dam and Reservoir on the Angelina River, Texas, hereafter shall be known and designated as the “Sam Rayburn Dam and Reservoir”. Any law, regulation, map, document, record, or other paper of the United States in which McGee Bend Dam and Reservoir is referred to shall be held to refer to such dam and reservoir as the “Sum Rayburn Dam and Reservoir”,


*Approved September 11, 1963.*

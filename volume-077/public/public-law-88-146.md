# Public Law 88–146

> AN ACT To amend section 7 of the Administrative Expenses Act of 1046, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (b) of section 7 of the Administrative Expenses Act of 1946 (60 Stat. 808, as amended; 5 U.S.C. 73b–3(b)) is amended by deleting the word “promoted” wherever it appears and substituting the word “assigned”, and by deleting the word “promotion” wherever it appears and substituting the word “assignment”.


*Approved October 16, 1963.*

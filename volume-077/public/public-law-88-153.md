# Public Law 88–153

> AN ACT Relating to the deductibility of accrued vacation pay.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 97 of the Technical Amendments Act of 1958, as amended (26 U.S.C., sec. 162 note), is amended by striking out “January 1, 1963,” and inserting in lieu thereof “January 1, 1965,”.


*Approved October 17, 1963.*

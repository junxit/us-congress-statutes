# Public Law 88–212

> AN ACT To amend the act of July 24, 1956, granting a franchise to D.C. Transit System, Inc.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That part 1, title I of the Act entitled “An Act to grant a franchise to D.C. Transit System, Inc., and for other purposes”, approved July 24, 1956, is amended by striking therefrom “Public Utilities Commission of the District of Columbia”, and inserting in lieu thereof “Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Commission”.


*Approved December 18, 1963.*

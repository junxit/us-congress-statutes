# Public Law 88–76

> AN ACT To extend for two years the definition of “peanuts” which is now In effect under the Agricultural Adjustment Art of 1938. as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the last paragraph of the Act entitled “An Act to amend the peanut, marketing quota provisions of the Agricultural Adjustment Act of 1938, as amended, and for other purposes”, approved August 13, 1957, as amended (7 U.S.C. 1359 note), is amended by striking out “and 1963” and inserting in lieu thereof “1963, 1964, and 1965”.


*Approved July 25, 1963.*

# Public Law 88–20

> AN ACT To amend title 38 of the United States Code to provide additional compensation for veterans having the service-connected disability of deafness of both ears.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 314(k) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by inserting after “having only light perception,” each place it appears therein the following: “or deafness of both ears, having absence of air and bone conduction,”.

## § 2.

The amendments matte by this Act shall take effect on the first day of the second month which begins after the. date of its enactment.


*Approved May 15, 1963.*

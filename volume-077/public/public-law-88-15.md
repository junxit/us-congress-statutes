# Public Law 88–15

> AN ACT To amend the Act of April 22, 1960, relative to the transfer of certain public lands to the Colorado River Commission of Nevada.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of April 22, 1960 (74 Stat. 74) is amended as follows:

- **(1)** In section 2, strike out “five years” and insert in lien thereof “ten years”; and
- **(2)** In section 3, strike out “five-year period” and insert in lieu thereof “ten-year period”.
- **(3)** In section 4(a), strike out “three years” and insert in lieu thereof “eight years”.


*Approved April 26, 1963.*

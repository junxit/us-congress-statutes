# Public Law 88–142

> AN ACT To change the name of the Memphis lock and dam on the Tombigbee River near Aliceville, Alabama.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Memphis lock and dam near Aliceville, Alabama, on the Tombigbee River shall hereafter be known and designated as the Aliceville lock and dam. Any law, regulation, map, document, record, or other paper of the United States in which such lock and dam are referred to shall be held to refer to such lock and dam as the Aliceville lock and dam.


*Approved October 16, 1963.*

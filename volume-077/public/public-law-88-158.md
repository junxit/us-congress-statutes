# Public Law 88–158

> JOINT RESOLUTION To provide additional housing for the elderly.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 202(a)(4) of the Housing Act of 1959 is amended by striking out “$225,000,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$275,000,000”.


*Approved October 24, 1963.*

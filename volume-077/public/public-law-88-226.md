# Public Law 88–226

> AN ACT To provide that the Commission on the Disposition of Alcatraz Island shall have six months after its formation in which to make its report to Congress.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first sentence of section 4 of the Act entitled “An Act to establish a Federal commission on the disposition of Alcatraz Island”, approved October 16, 1963 (77 Stat, 247), is amended by striking out “December 31, 1963” and inserting “six months after the date on which all the members of the Commission have been appointed”.


*Approved December 23, 1963.*

# Public Law 88–54

> JOINT RESOLUTION Amending section 221 of the National Housing Act to extend for two years the broadened eligibility presently provided for mortgage insurance thereunder.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the fifth sentence of section 221 (f) of the National Housing Act is amended by striking out “1963” and inserting in lieu thereof “1965”.


*Approved June 29, 1963.*

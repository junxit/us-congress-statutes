# Public Law 88–86

> AN ACT To amend the Tariff Act of 1930 to provide that polished sheets and plates of iron or steel shall be subject to the same duty ns unpolished sheets and plates.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph 309 of the Tariff Act of 1930, as amended (19 U.S.C. 1001), be amended by striking out. “sheets and plates of iron or steel, polished, planished, or glanced, by whatever name designated, 1% cents per pound” and also by striking out “other than polished, planished, or glanced, herein provided for,”. ,

## § 2.

This Act shall take effect 30 days after the date of its enactment.


*Approved August 5, 1963.*

# Public Law 88–92

> AN ACT To extend for three years the period during which certain tanning extracts, and extracts of hemlock or eucalyptus suitable for use fur tanning, may be imported free of duty.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Public Law 86–421 (74 Stat. 54), approved April 22, 1960, is amended by striking out “September 30, 1963” and inserting in lieu thereof “September 30, 1966”


*Approved August 8, 1963.*

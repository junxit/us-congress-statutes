# Public Law 88–232

> AN ACT To change the requirements for the annual meeting elate for national banks.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first sentence of section 5145 of the Revised Statutes (12 U.S.C. 71) is amended by striking but all after the semicolon and inserting in lieu 1 hereof “and afterward at meetings to be held on such day of each year as is specified therefor in the bylaws.”

## § 2.

The first sentence of section 5149 of the Revised Statutes (12 U.S.C. 75) is amended by striking out “articles of association” and inserting in lieu thereof “bylaws”.


*Approved December 23, 1963.*

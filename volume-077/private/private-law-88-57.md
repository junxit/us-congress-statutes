# Private Law 88–57

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Mabel Constance Kennedy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the provisions of section 245(c) of that Act shall be inapplicable in the case of Mrs. Mabel Constance Kennedy.


*Approved August 28, 1963.*

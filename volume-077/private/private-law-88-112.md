# Private Law 88–112

> AN ACT For the relief of Generoso Bucci Cammisa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Generoso Bucci Cammisa may be classified as an eligible orphan within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of the Act, upon approval of a petition riled in his behalf by Mr, and Mrs. Joseph Cammisa, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 205 (b) of the Act, subject to all the conditions in that section relating to eligible orphans.


*Approved December 18, 1963.*

# Private Law 88–106

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Pedro B. Montemayor, Junior.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Pedro B. Montemayor, Junior, shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of June 24, 1958.


*Approved November 13, 1963.*

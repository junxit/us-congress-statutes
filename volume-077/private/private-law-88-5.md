# Private Law 88–5

> AN ACT For the relief of certain aliens.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of title III of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Olga Marie Ferrer (XXXXX) shall be held and considered to have been admitted to the United States for permanent residence on May 17, 1947, and to have complied with the residential and physical presence requirements of section 316 of the said Act.

## § 2.

Section 2 of Private Law 87–673 is hereby amended by adding at the end thereof the following: “For the purposes of section 316 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Mehmet Vecihi Kalaycioglu shall be held and considered to have complied with the residential and physical presence requirements of that section of the. said Act.”

## § 3.

For the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Antonio Gutierrez Fernandez shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fee. Upon the granting of permanent residence to such alien as provided for in this section of this Act. the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper quota-control officer to deduct one number from the appropriate quota for the first year that such quota is available.


*Approved June 19, 1963.*

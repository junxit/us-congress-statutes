# Private Law 88–93

> AN ACT For the relief of Tim L. Yen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a)(27)(A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Tim L. Yen shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien minor child of Mr. and Mrs. Don Y. Yen, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents of the said Tim D. Yen shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved October 24, 1963.*

# Private Law 88–71

> AN ACT For the relief of Elizabeth Kolloian Izmirian.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, a petition filed in behalf of Elizabeth Kolloian Izmirian by Mrs. Baizar Izmirian may he approved pursuant to the provisions of section 205(b) of the Act, subject to all the conditions in that section relating to eligible orphans.


*Approved October 11, 1963.*

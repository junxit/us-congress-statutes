# Private Law 88–52

> AN ACT For the relief of Eva Baker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Eva Baker may be classified as sin eligible orphan within the meaning of section 101 (b)(1)(F) of flat Act, upon approval of a petition filed in her behalf by Mr. Robert W. Baker, a citizen of the United States, pursuant to section 205(b) of the Act, subject to all the conditions in that section relating to eligible orphans.


*Approved August 28, 1963.*

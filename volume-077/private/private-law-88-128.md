# Private Law 88–128

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Concetto Foto Napoli, Salvatore Napoli, Antonina Napoli, and Michela Napoli.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration laws, Mrs. Concetta Foto Napoli, Salvatore Napoli, Antonina Napoli, and Michela Napoli shall be deemed to be within the purview of section 1 of the Act of October 24, 1962 (76 Stat. 1247).


*Approved December 19, 1963.*

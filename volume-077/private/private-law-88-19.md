# Private Law 88–19

> AN ACT To amend the Act of June 29, 1960 (Private Law 86–354).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, the Act of June 29, 1960 (Private Law 86–354), be and is hereby amended by inserting in lieu of the words “the date of the enactment of this Act”, the following date: “October 26, 1954”.


*Approved July 17, 1963.*

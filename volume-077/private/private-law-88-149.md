# Private Law 88–149

> AN ACT For the relief of Dulcie Ann Steinhardt Sherlock.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time Dulcie Ann Steinhardt Sherlock, daughter of the late Ambassador Laurence A. Steinhardt and Mrs. Steinhardt, was residing abroad with her parents, during her minority when her father was an Ambassador in the Foreign Service of the United States, shall be held and considered to be compliance with the physical presence requirements of section 301(a)(7) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, as amended.


*Approved December 23, 1963.*

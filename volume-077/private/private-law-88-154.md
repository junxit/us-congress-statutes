# Private Law 88–154

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Susanna Grün (Susanne Roth).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Susanna Grün (Susanne Roth) shall be held and considered to be a returning resident alien within the purview of section 101(a)(27)(B) of that Act.


*Approved December 30, 1963.*

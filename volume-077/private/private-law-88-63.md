# Private Law 88–63

> AN ACT For the relief of Marija Lovsin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Act of October 24, 1962 (Public Law 87–885), Marija Lovsin shall be deemed to be within the purview of section 1 of that Act.


*Approved September 17, 1963.*

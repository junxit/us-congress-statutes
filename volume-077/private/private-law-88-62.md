# Private Law 88–62

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Mohammed Adham.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the periods of time Doctor Mohammed Adham has resided in the United States since September 16, 1954, shall be held and considered to meet the residence and physical presence requirements of section 316 of the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved September 17, 1963.*

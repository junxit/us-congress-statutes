# Private Law 88–103

> AN ACT For the relief of Kazimierz Kurmas and Zdzislaw Kurmas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Kazimierz Kurmaz and Zdzislaw Kurmas may be classified as eligible orphans filed in their behalf by Stanislava Sawicki, a citizen of the United States, may be approved pursuant to section 205(b) of the Act, subject to all the conditions in that section relating to eligible orphans.


*Approved November 13, 1963.*

# Private Law 88–23

> AN ACT For the relief of Peter Hopeton Maylor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a)(27)(A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Peter Ho pet on Maylor shall be held and considered to be the alien child of Wilfred Shirty, a citizen of the United States: Provided, That, the natural mother of the said Peter Hopeton Maylor shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved July 17, 1963.*

# Private Law 88–1

> AN ACT To authorize David H. Forman and Julia Forman to bring suit againts the United States to determine title to certain hinds in Maricopa County, Arizona.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

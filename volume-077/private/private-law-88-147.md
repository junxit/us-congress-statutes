# Private Law 88–147

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Demetrios Flessas and Doctor Eugenia Flessas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Demetrios Flessas and Doctor Eugenia Flessas shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of March 10, 1953, and April 22, 1950, respectively.


*Approved December 23, 1963.*

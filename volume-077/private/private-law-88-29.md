# Private Law 88–29

> AN ACT For the relief of Etsuko Matsuo McClellan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101(a)(27)(A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Etsuko Matsuo McClellan, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Sergeant Curtis O. McClellan mid Jewell McClellan, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents of the said Etsuko Matsuo McClellan shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved August 15, 1963.*

# Private Law 88–38

> AN ACT For the relief of Luigi Giuseppe Luraschi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the provisions of section 352(a)(1) shall be inapplicable in the case of Luigi Giuseppe Luraschi: Provided, That he establishes residence in the United States not later than September 3, 1965.


*Approved August 27, 1963.*

# Private Law 88–80

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Jesse Franklin White.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 15 to 20, inclusive, of the Federal Employees’ Compensation Act are hereby waived in favor of Mrs. Jesse Franklin White, of Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, and her claim for compensation for the death of her husband, Jesse Franklin White, a former employee of the Department of Agriculture, who died on August 28, 1932, shall be acted upon under the remaining provisions of such Act, as amended, if she flies such claim with the Department of Labor (Bureau of Employees’ Compensation) within six months after the enactment of this Act: Provided, That no benefits shall accrue by reason of this Act for any period prior to the date of enactment.


*Approved October 16, 1963.*

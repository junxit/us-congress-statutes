# Private Law 88–101

> AN ACT For the relief of Mercedes Robinson Orr.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mercedes Robinson Orr may be classified as an eligible orphan within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of the Act, upon approval of a petition filed in her behalf by Mr. and Mrs. Eric Orr, a lawfully resident alien and citizen of the United States, respectively, pursuant to section 205(b) of the Act, subject to all the conditions in that section relating to eligible orphans.

Subsection (c) of section 245 of the Immigration and Nationality Act shall be held to be inapplicable in the case of Mercedes Robinson Orr.


*Approved November 13, 1963.*

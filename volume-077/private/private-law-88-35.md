# Private Law 88–35

> AN ACT Authorizing the readmittance of Walter Sowa, Junior, to the United States Naval Academy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding sections 6954 and 6958(a)(1) of title 10, United States Code, the President is authorized to reappoint Walter Sowa, Junior, a former midshipman at the United States Naval Academy, as a midshipman in the appropriate class at such Academy.


*Approved August 20, 1963.*

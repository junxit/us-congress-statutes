# Private Law 88–150

> AN ACT For the relief of Maria Mereghetti (Mother Benedetta) and Annunziata Colombo (Mother Cherubina).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a)(27)(B) and 245 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Maria Mereghetti (Mother Benedetta) and Annunziata Colombo (Mother Cherubina), shall be held and considered to be returning resident aliens.


*Approved December 23, 1963.*

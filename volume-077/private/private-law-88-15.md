# Private Law 88–15

> AN ACT For the relief of Josephine Maria (Bonaccorso) Bowtell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Josephine Maria (Bonaccorso) Bowtell shall be deemed to be within the purview of section 354(5) of that Act.


*Approved July 11, 1963.*

# Private Law 88–12

> AN ACT For the relief of Carmela Calabrese DiVito.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for purposes of sections 101(a)(27)(A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Carmela Calabrese DiVito shall be held and considered to be the minor natural-born alien child of Pasquale and Lucy DiVito, citizens of the United States.


*Approved July 11, 1963.*

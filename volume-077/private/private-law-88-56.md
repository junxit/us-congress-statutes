# Private Law 88–56

> AN ACT For the relief of Shin Sook (Renee) Whang.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, a petition filed in behalf of Sinn Sook (Renee) Whang by Gladys Mary Patricia . Lindsay may be approved pursuant to the provisions of section 205(b) of the Act, subject to all the conditions in that section relating to eligible orphans.


*Approved August 28, 1963.*

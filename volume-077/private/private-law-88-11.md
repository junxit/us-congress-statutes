# Private Law 88–11

> AN ACT For the relief of Miss Ann Super.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Ann Super may be classified as an eligible orphan within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of the Act. upon approval of a petition filed in her behalf by Mr. and Mrs. Steve Super, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 205(b) of the Act, subject to all the conditions in that section relating to eligible orphans.


*Approved July 11, 1963.*

# Private Law 88–158

> AN ACT For the relief of Constantinos A. Grigoras (Gregoras).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of section 4 of the Act of September 22, 1959 (73 Stat. 644), section 42.66(a)(7) of title 22 of the Code of Federal Regulations shall not be applicable in the case of Constantinos A. Grigonis (Gregoras) duly registered as an immigrant on August 11, 1953.


*Approved December 30, 1963.*

# Private Law 88–102

> AN ACT For the relief of Woo You Lyn (also known as Hom You Fong and Lyn Fong Y. Hom).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 244 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, as amended, Woo You Lyn (also known as Hom You Fong and Lyn Fong Y. Hom) shall be held and considered to have been physically present in the United States since July 9, 1951.


*Approved November 13, 1963.*

# Private Law 88–111

> AN ACT For the relief of Eric Voegelin and Luise Betty Onken Voegelin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the provisions of section 352(a)(1) shall be inapplicable in the cases of Eric Voegelin and Luise Betty Onken Voegelin: Provided, That they establish residence in the United States not later than February 9, 1967.


*Approved December 18, 1963.*

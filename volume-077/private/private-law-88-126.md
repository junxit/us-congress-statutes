# Private Law 88–126

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Denise Jeanne Escobar (nee Arnoux).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Denise Jeanne Escobar (nee Arnoux) shall be deemed to be chargeable to the quota for France.


*Approved December 19, 1963.*

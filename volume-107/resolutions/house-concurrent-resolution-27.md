# House Concurrent Resolution 27

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Wednesday, January 27, 1993, it stand adjourned until noon on Tuesday, February 2, 1993.


*Agreed to January 27, 1993.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 13

> DAYS OF REMEMBRANCE OF VICTIMS OF THE HOLOCAUST—CAPITOL ROTUNDA CEREMONIES

Whereas the United States Holocaust Memorial Council has designated April 18 through April 25, 1993, and April 3 through April 10, 1994, as “Days of Remembrance of Victims of the Holocaust”: Now, therefore, be it Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),

That the rotunda of the Capitol is authorized to be used from 8 o’clock ante meridiem until 3 o’clock post meridiem on April 20, 1993, and from 8 o’clock ante meridiem until 3 o’clock post meridiem on April 6, 1994, for ceremonies as part of the commemoration of the days of remembrance of victims of the Holocaust. Physical preparations for the ceremonies shall be carried out in accordance with such conditions as the Architect of the Capitol may prescribe.


*Agreed to March 29, 1993.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 39

> JOINT SESSION

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives today, Wednesday, February 17, 1993, at 9 o’clock post meridiem, for the purpose of receiving such communication as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Agreed to February 17, 1993.*

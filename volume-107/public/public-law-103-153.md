# Public Law 103–153

> Joint Resolution To authorize the President to issue a proclamation designating the week beginning on November 21, 1993, and November 20, 1994, as “National Family Week”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

Whereas the family is the basic strength of any free and orderly society; Whereas it is appropriate to honor the family as a unit essential to the continued well-being of the United States; and Whereas it is fitting that official recognition be given to the importance of family loyalties and ties: Now, therefore, be it

That the President is hereby authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating the week beginning on November 21, 1993, and November 20, 1994 as “National Family Week”.


*Approved November 24, 1993.*

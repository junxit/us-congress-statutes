# Public Law 103–128

> Joint Resolution Making further continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 1994, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 106(c) of Public Law 103–88, as amended by Public Law 103–113, is further amended by striking out “October 28, 1993” and inserting in lieu thereof “November 10, 1993”.


*Approved October 29, 1993.*

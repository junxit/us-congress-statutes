# Public Law 103–151

> An Act To authorise the Beard of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution to plan, design, and construct the West Court of the National Museum of Natural History building.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. PLANNING, DESIGN, AND CONSTRUCTION OF WEST COURT OF NATIONAL MUSEUM OF NATURAL HISTORY BUILDING.

The Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution is authorized to plan, design, and construct the West Court of the National Museum of Natural History building.

## § 2. FUNDING.

No appropriated funds may be used to pay any expense of the planning, design, and construction authorized by section 1.


*Approved November 24, 1993.*

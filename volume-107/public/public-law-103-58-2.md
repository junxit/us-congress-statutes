# Public Law 103–58

> An Act To modify the boundary of Hot Springs National Park.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, ,*

That the boundary of Hot Springs National Park is modified as depicted on the map entitled “Proposed Boundary Map”, numbered 128/80015, and dated August 5, 1985.


*Approved August 2, 1993.*

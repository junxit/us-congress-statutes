# Public Law 103–203

> An Act Making a technical amendment of the Clayton Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. TECHNICAL AMENDMENT OF THE CLAYTON ACT.

Section 8(a)(5) of the Clayton Act (15 U.S.C. 19(a)(5)) is amended by striking “October 30” and inserting “January 31”.


*Approved December 17, 1993.*

# Public Law 103–86

> An Act To extend the current interim exemption under the Marine Mammal Protection Act for commercial fisheries until April 1, 1994.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 114(a)(1) of the Marine Mammal Protection Act of 1972 (16 U.S.C. 1383a(a)(1)), is amended by striking “October 1, 1993,” and inserting in lieu thereof “April 1, 1994,”.


*Approved September 30, 1993.*

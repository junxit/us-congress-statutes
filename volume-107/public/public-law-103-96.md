# Public Law 103–96

> An Act To designate the Federal building in Jacksonville, Florida, as the “Charles E. Bennett Federal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The Federal building at 400 Bay Street in Jacksonville, Florida, is designated as the “Charles E. Bennett Federal Building”. If a new Federal building is built in Jacksonville, Florida, to replace the building at 400 Bay Street, the new Federal building shall be designated as the “Charles E. Bennett Federal Building”.

## § 2. LEGAL REFERENCES.

Any reference in any law, regulation, document, record, map, or other paper of the United States to the Federal building referred to in section 1 is deemed to be a reference to the “Charles E. Bennett Federal Building”.


*Approved October 6, 1993.*

# Public Law 103–166

> An Act To extend authorities under the Middle East Peace Facilitation Act of 1993 by six months.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. EXTENSION OF AUTHORITIES.

Section 3(a) of the Middle East Peace Facilitation Act of 1993 (Public Law 103–125) is amended by striking “January 1” and inserting in lieu thereof “July 1”.


*Approved December 2, 1993.*

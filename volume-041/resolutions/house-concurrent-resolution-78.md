# House Concurrent Resolution 78

> standard time, texas and oklahoma.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (H. R. 14490) entitled “An Act to transfer the Panhandle and Plains section of Texas and Oklahoma to the United States standard central time zone,” the Clerk of the House be authorized and directed to insert on

Page 2, line 13, after “Santa Fe”: Railway Company and other branches of the Santa Fe;, and on

Page 2, line 13, strike out all after “Clovis,” down to and including “Clovis,” in line 14.


*Passed, March 3, 1921.*

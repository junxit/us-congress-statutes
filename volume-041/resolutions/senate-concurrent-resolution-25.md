# Senate Concurrent Resolution 25

> death on the high seas.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (S. 2085) “relating to the maintenance of actions for death on the high seas and other navigable waters,” the Secretary of the Senate be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to strike out, on page 2, line 15, of the engrossed bill, the word “fault” and insert “default” in lieu thereof.


*Passed, March 25, 1920.*

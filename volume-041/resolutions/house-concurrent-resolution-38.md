# House Concurrent Resolution 38

> tercentenary of landing of the pilgrims.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the time within which the Joint Special Committee on the Pilgrim Tercentenary shall report is hereby further extended to January 10, 1920.


*Passed, November 19, 1919.*

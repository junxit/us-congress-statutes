# House Concurrent Resolution 4

> congressional directory.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That an edition of the Congressional Directory for the first session of the Sixty-sixth Congress be prepared and published, under the direction of the Joint Committee on Printing, as provided for in section 73 of the printing act approved January 12, 1895, and that the sum of $800 be paid for preparing, compiling, and indexing said edition, one-half of the said sum to be paid from the contingent fund of the Senate and one-half from the contingent fund of the House of Representatives.


*Passed, December 7, 1927.*

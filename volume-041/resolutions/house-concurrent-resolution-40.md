# House Concurrent Resolution 40

> american expeditionary forces report.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Final Report of General John J. Pershing, Commander-in-Chief American Expeditionary Forces, be printed as a House document, with the accompanying maps and charts, and that fifty thousand additional copies be printed, of which 15,000 copies shall be for the use of the Senate and 35,000 copies for the use of the House of Representatives.


*Passed, January 20, 1920.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 30

> merchant marine bill.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (H. R. 10378) to provide for the promotion and maintenance of the American Merchant Marine, to repeal certain emergency legislation, and provide for the disposition, regulation, and use of property acquired thereunder, and for other purposes, the Clerk be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to number the sections in consecutive order.


*Passed, June 5, 1920.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 8

> tercentenary of landing of the pilgrims.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Joint special committee, consisting of two Senators and four Representatives appointed to confer with officials of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts, or any political subdivision thereof, or with officers of any organization or society or with individuals, relative to the contemplated observance of the three hundredth anniversary of the landing of the Pilgrims, ordered to report its recommendations on or before September 1, 1919, be granted an extension of time for its report and recommendations until December 10, 1919.


*Passed, August 29, 1919.*

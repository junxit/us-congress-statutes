# Senate Concurrent Resolution 4

> german and bolshevik propaganda.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed 2,500 additional copies of Senate Document numbered 62, Sixty-sixth Congress, entitled “Brewing and Liquor Interests and German and Bolshevik Propaganda,” of which 1,000 copies shall be for the use of the Senate, 1,000 copies for the use of the House of Representatives, and 500 copies for the use of the Senate Committee on the Judiciary.


*Passed, September 19, 1919.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 22

> federal reserve act amendments.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Secretary of the Senate be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, to enroll the bill (S. 2472) “To amend an Act approved December 23, 1913, known as the Federal Reserve Act,” as follows:

Insert the matter proposed by House amendment numbered 15 and after “herein” on page 5, line 8, of the engrossed bill, insert: “Nothing contained in this section shall be construed to prohibit the Federal Reserve Board, under its power to prescribe rules and regulations, from limiting the aggregate amount of liabilities of any or all classes incurred by the corporation and outstanding at any one time.”

On page 5, line 24, of the engrossed bill, strike out the word “not.”

On page 5, line 25, of the engrossed bill, after “transacting” insert the word “any.”

On page 5, line 25, of the engrossed bill, after “United States” strike out the comma.

On page 6, line 5, of the engrossed bill, restore the matter proposed to be stricken out by amendment numbered 21 and insert the matter proposed by said amendment.


*Passed, December 18, 1919.*

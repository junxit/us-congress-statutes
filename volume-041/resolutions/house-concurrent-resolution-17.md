# House Concurrent Resolution 17

> holiday recess.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Tuesday, the first day of July, 1919, they stand adjourned until twelve o’clock, meridian, on Tuesday, the eighth day of July, 1919.


*Passed, July 1, 1919.*

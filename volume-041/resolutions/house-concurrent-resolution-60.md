# House Concurrent Resolution 60

> adjournment.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President of the Senate and Speaker of the House of Representatives be authorized to close the present session by adjourning their respective Houses on the 5th day of June, 1920, at 4 o’clock post meridian.


*Passed, June 3, 1920.*

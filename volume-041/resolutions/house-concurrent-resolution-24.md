# House Concurrent Resolution 24

> adjournment of the house.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Saturday, the second day of August, it stand adjourned until twelve o’clock meridian, Tuesday, the ninth day of September.


*Passed, July 28, 1919.*

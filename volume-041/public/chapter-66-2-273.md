# Chapter 273

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the payment of the compensation of session employees of the Senate and House of Representatives for the month of June, 1920, on the 5th day of said month.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Senate and Clerk of the House of Representatives are hereby authorized and directed to pay to the session employees of the Senate and House of Representatives borne on the session roll their respective salaries for the month of June, 1920, on the fifth day of said month.


*Approved, June 5, 1920.*

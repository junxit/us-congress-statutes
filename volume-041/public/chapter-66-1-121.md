# Chapter 121

> Joint Resolution To continue the control of imports of dyes and coal-tar products.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the prior termination of the present war, the provisions of the Trading with the Enemy Act, approved October 6, 1917, and of any proclamation of the President issued in pursuance thereof which prohibit or control the importation into the United States of dyes or other products derived directly or indirectly from coal tar, are continued until January 15, 1920.


*Approved, November 19, 1919.*

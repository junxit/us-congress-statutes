# Chapter 37

> Joint Resolution Extending the time for filing final report of the Joint Commission on Reclassification of Salaries, created by section 9, Public Numbered 314, Sixty-fifth Congress, approved March 1, 1919, to a date not later than March 12, 1920.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time for filing of the final report to Congress of the Joint Commission on Reclassification of Salaries, created by section 9, Public Numbered 314, Sixty-fifth Congress, approved March 1, 1919, be, and the same is hereby, extended to a date not later than March 12, 1920: Provided, That the work of completing the preparation of said final report and the printing thereof shall be accomplished within the limits of the appropriations heretofore made.


*Approved, January 10, 1920.*

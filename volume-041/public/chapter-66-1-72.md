# Chapter 72

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the appointment of an ambassador to Belgium.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized to appoint, as the representative of the United States, an ambassador to the Kingdom of Belgium, who shall receive as compensation the sum of $17,500 per annum.


*Approved, September 29, 1919.*

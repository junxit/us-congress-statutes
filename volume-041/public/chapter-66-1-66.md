# Chapter 66

> An Act To extend the cancellation stamp privilege for the Roosevelt Memorial Association.

*Be it enacted by me Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to permit the employment of special canceling stamps bearing the following words and figures: “Roosevelt Memorial Association, October 20–27,” at such post offices as he may designate and under such rules and regulations as he may prescribe.


*Approved, September 29, 1919.*

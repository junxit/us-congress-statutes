# Chapter 101

> An Act To add certain lands to the Lemhi National Forest, Idaho.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all unappropriated public lands within the following-described areas, found by the Secretary of Agriculture to be chiefly valuable for the production of timber or the protection of stream flow, may, with the approval of the Secretary of the Interior, be included within and made a part of the Lemhi National Forest, in Idaho, by proclamation of the President, said lands to be thereafter subject to all laws affecting national forests:

# Chapter 62

> An Act Declaring Platte River to be a nonnavigable stream.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Platte River in the State of Missouri be, and the same is hereby, declared to be a nonnavigable stream within the meaning of the Constitution and laws of the United States, and jurisdiction over said river is hereby declared to be vested in the State of Missouri.

## § 2.

That the right of Congress to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Received by the President, February 4, 1921.*

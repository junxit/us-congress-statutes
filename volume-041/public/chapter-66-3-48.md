# Chapter 48

> An Act To extend the time for the construction of a bridge across the Susquehanna River at Harrisburg, Pennsylvania.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the reconstruction of a bridge authorized by Act of Congress approved October 19, 1918, to be reconstructed by the Philadelphia, Harrisburg and Pittsburgh Railroad Company, its lessees, successors, and assigns, across the Susquehanna River at or about four thousand two hundred and fifty feet west of Philadelphia, Harrisburg and Pittsburgh Junction, Harrisburg, Penn-sylvania, to a point in the borough of Lemoyne, Cumberland County, State of Pennsylvania, in accordance with Act of Congress approved March 23, 1906, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from the date of approval of this Act.

## § 2.

That the right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, February 15, 1921.*

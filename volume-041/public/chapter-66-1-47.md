# Chapter 47

> An Act Extending the time for the construction of a bridge across Flint River, in the State of Georgia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge authorized by Act of Congress approved April 17, 1916, to be built across the Flint River, Georgia, by Mitchell County, or by Baker County, Georgia, jointly or separately, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from the date hereof.

## § 2.

That the right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, August 15, 1919.*

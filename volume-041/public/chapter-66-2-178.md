# Chapter 178

> An Act For the sale of isolated tracts in the former Fort Berthold Indian Reservation, North Dakota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of section 2455 of the Revised Statutes of the United States as amended by the Act of March 28, 1912 (Thirty-seventh Statutes at Large, page 77),relating to the sale at public auction of isolated tracts of the public domain, be, and the same are hereby, extended and made applicable to lands within the portion of the Fort Berthold Indian Reservation, North Dakota, opened under the Act of June 1, 1910 (Thirty-sixth Statutes at Large, page 455): Provided, That the provisions of this Act shall not apply to lands which are not subject to homestead entry: Provided further, That purchasers of land under this Act shall pay for the lands not less than the price fixed in the law opening such lands to homestead entry.


*Approved, May 10, 1920.*

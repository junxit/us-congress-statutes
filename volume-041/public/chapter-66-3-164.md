# Chapter 164

> An Act Authorizing the Secretary of War to grant a right of way over certain Government lands to the State of Oregon for the Columbia River Highway.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War is hereby authorized to grant to the State of Oregon, for the purpose of constructing, maintaining, and operating the Columbia River High-way, a permanent right of way over and across portions of the lands of the United States acquired and held in connection with the improvement of the Dallas-Celilo section of the Columbia River: Provided, That the exact location and dimensions of the right of way shall be fixed by the Secretary of War: Provided further, That the grant shall be subject to the express condition that the right of way shall be used solely and exclusively for the roadway purpose aforesaid, and any part thereof not so used shall revert to the United States.


*Approved, March 4, 1921.*

# Chapter 32

> An Act For the construction of a bridge across the Susquehanna River at Laceyville, Wyoming County, Pennsylvania.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the consent of Congress is hereby granted to the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania to construct within the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania a bridge, with approaches thereto, across the Susquehanna River at Laceyville, Wyoming County, Pennsylvania, at a point suitable to the interests of navigation, in accordance with the provisions of the Act entitled “An Act to regulate the construction of bridges over navigable waters,” approved March 23, 1906.

## § 2.

That the right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, December 31, 1919.*

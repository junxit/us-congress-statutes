# Chapter 170

> An Act Extending the time for constructing a bridge across the Bayou Bartholomew, in the State of Arkansas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge authorised by Act of Congress approved January 15, 1914, to be built across the Bayou Bartholomew, in the State of Arkansas, by Ashley County, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from the date of the approval hereof.

## § 2.

That the right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, May 7, 1920.*

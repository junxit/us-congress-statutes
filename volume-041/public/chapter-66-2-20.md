# Chapter 20

> Joint Resolution Making immediately available the appropriation for the expenses of regulating further the entry of aliens into the United States.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That so much of the sum of $600,000 appropriated by section 4 of Public Act Numbered 79 of the Sixty-sixth Congress, entitled “An Act to regulate further the entry of aliens into the United States,” as may be necessary is hereby made immediately available for expenses of regulating entry into the United States, in accordance with the provisions of the Act approved May 22, 1918: Provided, That not more than $450,000 of said sum shall be used during the remainder of the fiscal year 1920.


*Approved, December 24, 1919.*

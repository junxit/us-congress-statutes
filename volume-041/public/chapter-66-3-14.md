# Chapter 14

> An Act To authorize an exchange of lands with Henry Blackburn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized to accept title to the southwest quarter of the southeast quarter of section nineteen, township thirty-nine south, range six west, Salt Lake meridian, and to convey in exchange therefor to Henry Blackburn, of Orderville, Utah, title to the northeast quarter of the northeast quarter of section thirty, township thirty-nine south, range six west, Salt Lake meridian, and upon reconveyance the land deeded to the United States shall thereupon become part of the Sevier National Forest and subject to all laws and regulations applicable thereto.


*Approved, January 7, 1921.*

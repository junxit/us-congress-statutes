# Chapter 165

> An Act Act Granting to the city and county of Honolulu, Territory of Hawaii, a right of way over and across the Fort De Russy Military Reservation for the purpose of extending its sewer system.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be, and there hereby is, granted to the city and county of Honolulu, Territory of Hawaii, subject to the conditions named in section 2 of this Act, a right of way over and across the Fort De Russy Military Reservation in said Territory for the purpose of constructing an extension of its sewer system, including a booster station in connection therewith, and of maintaining and operating the same, said right of way to include a strip of land twelve feet in width and approximately one thousand four hundred and twenty feet in length, and in addition thereto a contiguous area of approximately eight hundred square feet for the erection, maintenance, and operation of said booster station.

## § 2.

That the grant made in section 1 of this Act is upon the condition that said sewer and booster station shall be placed underground; that the United States shall have the right at any and all times to connect with said sewer at such place and places as it shall deem desirable and to use the same for purposes of drainage and sewage disposal from said reservation; and that the construction of said sewer and booster station upon the right of way herein granted shall be along such route and upon such site and in accordance with such plans and specifications as shall have been previously approved by the Secretary of War.


*Approved, March 4, 1921.*

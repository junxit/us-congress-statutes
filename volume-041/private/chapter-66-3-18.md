# Chapter 18

> An Act For the relief of John Healy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws John Healy shall be hereafter held and considers I to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a private in Company II, Fourteenth Regiment United States Infantry, on August 15, 1865: Provided, That no back pay, pension, or other emoluments shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, January 7, 1921.*

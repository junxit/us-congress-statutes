# Chapter 21

> An Act For the relief of Kathryn Walker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to issue patent to Kathryn Walker (formerly Kathryn McKnight) for the northeast quarter of section twelve, township twenty-nine south, of range seven west, New Mexico meridian: Provided, That the said Kathryn Walker pay the lawful price of the land within six months after the approval of this Act: Provided further, That the relief granted be made conditional upon the land being free from valid adverse claim at the time payment is made under the terms of the bill.


*Approved, January 9, 1921.*

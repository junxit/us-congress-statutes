# Chapter 84

> An Act Granting a franking privilege to Edith Carow Roosevelt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all mail matter sent by the post by Edith Carow Roosevelt, widow of the late Theodore Roosevelt, under her written autograph signature, be conveyed free of postage during her natural life.


*Approved, October 27, 1919.*

# Chapter 109

> An Act For the relief of Wilson Certain.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Wilson Certain, who was a private in Company A, Thirty-first Regiment Iowa Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a private of said company and regiment on the 27th day of June, 1865: Provided, That no bounty, pay, or allowances shall be held as accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 1, 1921.*

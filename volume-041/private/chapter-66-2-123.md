# Chapter 123

> An Act For the relief of Captain Frederick B. Shaw.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury he, and he is hereby, directed to pay to Frederick B. Shaw, United States Army, the sum of $356.50, which sum, or so much thereof as may be necessary, is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, said sum to be payment in full for all losses of personal property incurred by him by reason of the sinking of the United States transport Meade in the harbor of Ponce, Porto Rico, on or about May 16, 1899: Provided, That the accounting officer of the Treasury shall require a schedule and affidavit from him, such schedule to be approved by the Secretary of War.


*Approved, April 8, 1920.*

# Chapter 79

> An Act For the relief of William E. Johnson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That to reimburse William E. Johnson, former chief special officer and special disbursing agent, for the expenditure made by him under the direction of the Commissioner of Indian Affairs in the defense of Juan Cruz, a noncommissioned officer engaged in the suppression of the liquor traffic among Indians, for the payment of which no appropriation was available, there be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $1,839.95.


*Approved, February 17, 1920.*

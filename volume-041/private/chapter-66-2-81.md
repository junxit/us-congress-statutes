# Chapter 81

> An Act For the relief of Carlow Avellina.

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Carlow Avellina, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sura of $500 as compensation for damages sustained by film when an aeroplane of the Air Mail Service descended on his property in August, 1918.


*Approved, February 18, 1920.*

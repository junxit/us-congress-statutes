# Chapter 97

> An Act For the relief of Susie Currier.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

[Note by the Department of State.—The foregoing act having been presented to the President of the United States for his approval, and not having been returned by him to the house of Congress in which it originated within the time prescribed by the Constitution of the United States, has become a law without his approval.]

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Susie Currier, of Old Town, Maine, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $100, being full compensation for accident and injuries with resulting loss of lime and expense while working as charwoman in the United States post office at Old Town, Maine, December 14, 1914.

Received by the President, October 25, 1919.

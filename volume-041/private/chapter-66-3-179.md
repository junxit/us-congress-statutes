# Chapter 179

> An Act For the relief of Thomas Bayton and Bertha M. Hartt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury of the United States not otherwise appropriated, to Thomas Bayton and Bertha M. Hartt, of Elwha, Clallam County, Washington, the sum of $300, as compensation for the loss of a bull owned by them, which was killed September 15, 1915, by becoming entangled with a telephone wire, the property of the United States, on the Olympic National Forest in the State of Washington.


*Approved, March 4, 1921.*

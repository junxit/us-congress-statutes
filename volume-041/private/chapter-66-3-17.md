# Chapter 17

> An Act For the relief of Matthew McDonald.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representative of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws Matthew McDonald shall be hereafter held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the naval service of the United States Navy October 20, 1863: Provided, That no pension shall be allowed and no back pay and bounty shall be paid because of the enactment of this legislation.


*Approved, January 7, 1921.*

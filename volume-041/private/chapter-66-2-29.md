# Chapter 29

> An Act For the relief of Albert N. Collins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of Am erica in Congress assembled,*

That homestead entry naught thirty-six thousand five hundred and six, Glasgow series, of Albert N. Collins, for lots two, three, and four, and the southeast quarter north-west quarter, section nineteen, township twenty-five north, range thirty-eight east, Montana meridian, be, and the same is hereby, validated, and the Secretary of the Interior is authorized to issue patent thereon upon submission of satisfactory proof of compliance with the law under which said entry was allowed.


*Approved, December 30, 1919.*

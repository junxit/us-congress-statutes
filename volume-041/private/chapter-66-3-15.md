# Chapter 15

> An Act To correct the naval record of Fred C. Konrad.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to review the naval record of Fred C. Konrad, late first-class electrician, United States Navy, and grant him an honorable disability discharge.


*Approved, January 7, 1921.*

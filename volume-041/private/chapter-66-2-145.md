# Chapter 145

> An Act Authorizing the Secretary of the Interior to sell certain lands to school district numbered twenty-one, of Fremont County, Wyoming.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized to sell to school district numbered twenty-one, of Fremont County, Wyoming, the west half, southwest quarter, southwest quarter, southwest quarter, southeast quarter, section four, township one south, range one west of the Wind River meridian, containing one and one-quarter acres, the same being a fart of the Shoshone Agency reserve on the Shoshone or Wind River Indian Reservation.


*Approved, April 15, 1920.*

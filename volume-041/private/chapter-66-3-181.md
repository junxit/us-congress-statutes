# Chapter 181

> Joint Resolution Making an appropriation to pay the widow of Champ Clark.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $7,500 to pay the widow of Champ Clark, late a Representative from the State of Missouri.


*Approved, March 4, 1921.*

# Chapter 181

> An Act To remove the political disabilities of William A Webb of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution on William A. Webb of Virginia be and they are hereby removed.


*Approved, March 3, 1877.*

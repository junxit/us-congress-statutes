# Chapter 201

> An Act Granting a pension to Catharine A. Winslow, widow of the late Rear-Admiral John A. Winslow.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Mrs. Catharine A. Winslow, widow of John A. Winslow, late rear-admiral United States Navy, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars a month from and after the passage of this act, the pension hereby granted to be in lieu of the pension which she now receives.


*Approved, March 3, 1877.*

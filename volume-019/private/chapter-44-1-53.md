# Chapter 53

> An Act To remove the political disabilities of N. H. Van Zandt, of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two thirds of each House concurring therein)*

That all the political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States, upon N. H. Van Zandt, a citizen of the State of Virginia, be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, February 5, 1877.*

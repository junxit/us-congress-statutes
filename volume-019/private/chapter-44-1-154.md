# Chapter 154

> An Act Granting a pension to Marsilvia F. Woodard, mother of George R. Woodard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Marsilvia F. Woodard, mother of George R. Woodard, late private in Company B, Eighth Regiment United States Infantry.


*Approved, March 3, 1877.*

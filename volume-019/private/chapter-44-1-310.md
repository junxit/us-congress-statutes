# Chapter 310

> An Act For the relief of Norman H. Ryan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Norman H. Ryan, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of seven hundred and thirty-six dollars, in full for services as storekeeper of the bonded warehouse of E. W. Dutcher, at Amboy, Illinois, from the eighteenth day of April eighteen hundred and sixty-eight, to the fifteenth day of October eighteen hundred and sixty-eight.


*Approved, August 15, 1876.*

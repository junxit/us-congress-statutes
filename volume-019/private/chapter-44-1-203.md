# Chapter 203

> An Act Granting a pension to Harriet C. Dunham, widow of Charles A. Dunham, late private company A, One hundred and eighteenth regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Harriet C. Dunham, widow of Charles A. Dun ham, late private company A, One hundred and eighteenth regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers.


> MILTON SAYLER,Speaker of the House of Representatives pro tempore
> T. W. FERRYPresident of the Senate pro tempore


*Received by the President July 6, 1876.*

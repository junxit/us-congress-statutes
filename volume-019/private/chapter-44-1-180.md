# Chapter 180

> An Act To remove the political disabilities of Samuel V. Turner of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two thirds of each House thereof concurring therein)*

That all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States by reason of par ticipation in the late rebellion be, and they are hereby, removed from Samuel V. Turner, now resident of the city of Norfolk in the State of Virginia.


*Approved, March 3, 1877.*

# Chapter 309

> An Act For the relief of Robert N. Eddy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Paymaster-General of the United States Army is hereby authorized and directed to pay to Robert N. Eddy, late second lieutenant Company K, One hundred and fourteenth Regiment New York State Volunteer Infantry, the full pay and emoluments of a second lieutenant, from the thirty-first day of December, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, to the twenty-eighth day of August, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, deducting therefrom all pay he may have received from the Government as first sergeant for that period.


*Approved, August 15, 1876.*

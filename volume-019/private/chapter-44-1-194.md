# Chapter 194

> An Act Granting a pension to Clara Brosch, mother of Joseph Brosch, junior, late private Company H, Twenty-fourth Regiment Illinois Infantry Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Clara Brosch, mother of Joseph Brosch, junior, late private Company H, Twenty-fourth Regiment Illinois Infantry Volunteers.


> MILTON SAYLER,Speaker of the House of Representatives pro tempore.
> T. W. FERRYPresident of the Senate pro tempore


*Received by the President July 6, 1876.*

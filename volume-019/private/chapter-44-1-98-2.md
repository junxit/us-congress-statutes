# Chapter 98

> An Act To remove the political disabilities of S. P. Moore, doctor of medicine, a citizen of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein)*

That all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States on S. P. Moore, doctor of medicine, a citizen of the State of Virginia, be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, March 2, 1877.*

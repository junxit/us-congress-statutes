# Chapter 192

> An Act To authorize Louis Petoskey, of Michigan, to enter a certain tract of land which embraces his home and. improvements.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Louis Petoskey, of the State of Michigan, be, and he is hereby, authorized to enter the south half of the northeast quarter of section five, in township thirty-four, north of range five west, in the district of lands subject to sale at Traverse City, Michigan upon payment to the receiver of public moneys of the legal price thereof.


*Approved, March 3, 1877.*

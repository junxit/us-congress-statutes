# Chapter 36

> An Act For the relief of First Lieutenant Henry Jackson, Seventh Cavalry, United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the Treasury Department be, and hereby are, authorized and Jackson. directed to credit First Lieutenant Henry Jackson, Seventh Cavalry, United States Army, property and disbursing officer in the signal-service, • in his account with the United States, with the sum of one thousand two hundred and seventy-one dollars anti thirty four cents; being the amount paid by First Lieutenant Henry Jackson, property and disbursing officer in the signal-service, to Matt France and George M. Brown, citizens of Colorado Springs, Colorado Territory, on false vouchers, and to David H. Sackett, sergeant in the signal-service, United States Army, on false receipts presented by him.


> MICHAEL C. KERRSpeaker of the House of Representatives
> T. W. FERRYPresident of the Senate pro tempore


*Received by the President March 11, 1876.*

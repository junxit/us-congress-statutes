# Chapter 212

> An Act Granting a pension to Harrison H. Dodds.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Harrison H. Dodds, late private in Company C, Sixteenth Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and Company D, Ninth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Cavalry, to take effect from the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1877.*

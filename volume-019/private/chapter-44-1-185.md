# Chapter 185

> An Act Granting a pension to Austin R. Mills.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Austin R. Mills, late a member of the First Regiment of Kansas Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month: Provided, That no payment shall be made for any time for which payment may have already been made on his pension certificate.


*Approved, March 3, 1877.*

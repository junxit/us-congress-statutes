# Chapter 93

> An Act To remove the political disabilities of Wade H. Gibbes, of South Carolina.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein,)*

That all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States, by reason of participation in the rebellion, be, and they are hereby, removed from Wade H. Gibbes, of the State of South Carolina.


*Approved, March 2, 1877.*

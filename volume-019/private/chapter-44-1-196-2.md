# Chapter 196

> An Act Granting a pension to Laura M. Knowlton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Laura M. Knowlton, widow of Thomas J. Knowlton, verterinary surgeon Eighth Iowa Cavalry, to take effect from and after passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1877.*

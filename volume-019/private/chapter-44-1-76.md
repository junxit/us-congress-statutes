# Chapter 76

> An Act For the relief of Hermann Kreismann, United States consul-general at Berlin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officer be, and hereby is, directed to credit Hermann Kreismann, United States consul-general at Berlin, in his account, with the sum of three hundred and ninety-seven dollars and seventy-two cents, being in full for consular funds stolen from the consulate on the thirteenth day of February, eighteen hundred and seventy three, without fault or neglect on the part of said consul.


> MICHAEL C. KERRSpeaker of the House of Representatives
> T. W. FERRYPresident of the Senate pro tempore


*Received by the President April 10, 1876.*

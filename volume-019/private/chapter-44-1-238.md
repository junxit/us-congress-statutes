# Chapter 238

> An Act For the relief of Daniel Wormer, of Albany New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Daniel Wormer, of Albany, New York, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of two thousand dollars, in full compensation for expenses incurred in carrying-out a contract, with the United States to furnish one thousand two hundred cavalry horses.


> MILTON SAYLER,Speaker of the House of Representatives pro tempore.
> T. W. FERRYPresident of the Senate pro tempore


*Received by the President July 17, 1876.*

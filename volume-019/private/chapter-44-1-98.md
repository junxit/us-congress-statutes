# Chapter 98

> An Act Granting a pension to Nancy True.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Nancy True, dependent mother of Samuel F. True, late lieutenant and quartermaster in the Fourteenth Regiment of Illinois Volunteers, to date from September first, eighteen hundred and seventy-five.


> SAMUEL S. COXSpeaker of the House of Representatives pro tempore
> T. W. FERRYPresident of the Senate pro tempore


*Received by the President May 6, 1876.*

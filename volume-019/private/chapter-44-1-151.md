# Chapter 151

> An Act Granting a pension to Thomas F. Wildes, late lieutenant colonel One hundred and sixteenth Regiment Ohio Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Thomas F. Wildes, late lieutenant-colonel of the One hundred and sixteenth Regiment of Ohio Volunteers.


> SAMUEL S. COX,Speaker of the House of Representatives pro tempore
> T. W. FERRYPresident of the Senate pro tempore


*Received by the President June 14, 1876.*

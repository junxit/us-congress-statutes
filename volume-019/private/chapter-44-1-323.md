# Chapter 323

> An Act Granting a pension to Thomas Galloway, late captain of Company C, First Maryland Cavalry Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place ou the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Thomas Galloway, some time captain of Company C, First Maryland Cavalry Volunteers.


*Approved, August 15, 1876.*

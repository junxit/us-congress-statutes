# Chapter 193

> An Act To appropriate one thousand dollars to remove the remains of Honorable E. Rumsey Wing, late minister to Ecuador, from Quito to the cemetery at Owensboro, Kentucky.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of one thousand dollars be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to be used, under the direction of the Secretary of State, in removing the remains of Honorable E. Rumsey Wing, late minister to Ecuador, from Quito to the cemetery at Owensboro, Kentucky.


*Approved, July 18, 1876.*

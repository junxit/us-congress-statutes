# Chapter 162

> An Act Granting a pension to Esther P. Fox.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to place the name of Esther P. Fox, of the city of Buffalo, in the State of New York, widow of Augustus C. Fox, late a second lieutenant in Lieutenant-Colonel Chapin’s regiment New York militia, in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve, on the pension roll, and to pay her a pension of eight dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1877.*

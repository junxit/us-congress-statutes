# Private Resolution 14

> Joint Resolution For the relief of Edward O’M. Condon.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

Whereas, Edward O’M. Condon, a citizen of the United States, is now, and has been for some time closely confined in prison under the sentence of a British court; and whereas an earnest and profound desire, evidenced by resolutions of State legislatures, and petitions numerously signed and addressed to Congresses entertained by a large and respectable portion of the people of the United States that he should be speedily released; therefore,

That the President of the United States be, and he is hereby, requested to take such steps as in his judgment may tend to obtain the pardon or release of the said Edward O’M. Condon from imprisonment.


*Approved, July 7, 1876.*

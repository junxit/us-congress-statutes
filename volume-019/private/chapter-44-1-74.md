# Chapter 74

> An Act Granting a pension to to Philip J. Shaw.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Philip J. Shaw, late private company G. One hundred and twenty-sixth regiment Illinois Infantry Volunteers.


> MICHAEL C. KERRSpeaker of the House of Representatives
> T. W. FERRYPresident of the Senate pro tempore


*Received by the President April 10, 1876.*

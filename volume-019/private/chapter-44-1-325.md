# Chapter 325

> An Act Granting a pension to F. M. Bruner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of F. M. Bruner, late captain of company A, seventh regiment United States colored Infantry.


*Approved, August 15, 1876.*

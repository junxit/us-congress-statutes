# Chapter 209

> An Act To repeal an act granting a pension to Mary H. Bartlett, approved January twenty-eighth, eighteen hundred and seventy-three.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the act granting a pension to Mary H. Bartlett, widow of E. W. Bartlett, late acting steersman on the United States steam-ram Monarch, approved January twenty-eighth, eighteen hundred and seventy-three, be, and the same is hereby, repealed


*Approved, March 3, 1877.*

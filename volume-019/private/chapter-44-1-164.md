# Chapter 164

> An Act Granting a pension to Apoline A. Blair

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Apoline A Blair, widow of Frank P. Blair Jr, a major general during the war of the rebellion.


*Approved, March 3, 1877.*

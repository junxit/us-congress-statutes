# Chapter 204

> An Act To grant a pension to Margaret Hunter Hardie, widow of James A. Hardie Inspector-General in the United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Margaret Hunter Hardie, widow of James A. Hardie, Inspector General in the Army of the United States, and pay her a pen sion at the rate of thirty dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1877.*

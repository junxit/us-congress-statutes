# Chapter 35

> An Act To authorize sundry allowances to James Atkins, late collector of internal revenue for the fourth district of Georgia, in the settlement of his accounts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That James Atkins, late collector of internal revenue for the fourth district of the State of Georgia, be, and he is hereby, allowed, in the settlement of his accounts, the sum of seven thousand seven hundred and fifty dollars and three cents on account of items of assessments and abatements, and the further sum of one thousand four hundred and fifteen dollars and eight cents on account of expenses incurred by him in the performance of the duties of his office.


*Approved, January 25, 1877.*

# Chapter 240

> An Act To remove the political disabilities of George Jackson, a citizen of West Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein)*

That all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States on George Jackson, a citizen of the State of West Virginia, be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, July 29, 1876.*

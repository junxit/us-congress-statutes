# Chapter 22

> An Act Granting a Pension to Elizabeth A. Neibling.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elizabeth A. Neibling, widow of James M. Neibling, late colonel of the Twenty-first regiment Ohio volunteers, and pay her a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 13, 1876.*

# Chapter 7

> An Act To remove political disabilities of George S. Hawkins, of Florida

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That the political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States by reason of participation in the rebellion, be, and they are hereby, removed from George S. Hawkins, of the State of Florida.


*Approved, February 7, 1876.*

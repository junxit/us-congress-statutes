# Chapter 140

> An Act Granting a pension to John McIntire.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John McIntire, private soldier in Company A, Fourteenth Regiment Kentucky Cavalry


> MICHAEL C. KERRSpeaker of the House of Representatives
> T. W. FERRYPresident of the Senate pro tempore


*Received by the President June 8, 1876.*

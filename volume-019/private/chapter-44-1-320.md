# Chapter 320

> An Act Granting a pension to Edward F. Eddy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Edward F. Eddy, who, at the time he was wounded, was second lieutenant company G, Sixteenth regiment Michigan volunteers, and who, when discharged, was lieutenant Colonel Twenty-ninth regiment Michigan infantry


*Approved, August 15, 1876.*

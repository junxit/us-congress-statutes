# Chapter 148

> An Act To remove the political disabilities of W. H. Jenifer, late first lieutenant Second Cavalry United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two thirds of each House concurring therein)*

That all political disabilities imposed upon W. H. Jenifer, late first lieutenant Second Cavalry United States Army, by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States, by reason of participation in the rebellion, are hereby removed.


*Approved, June 26, 1876.*

# Chapter 198

> An Act Granting a pension to T. B. Murdock

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension rolls, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of T. B. Murdock, late a second lieutenant of Company F. Eighteenth United States Colored Infantry.


*Approved, March 3, 1877.*

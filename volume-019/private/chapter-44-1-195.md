# Chapter 195

> An Act Granting a pension to Niram W. Pratt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Niram W. Pratt, late a private in Captain A. J. Millard’s independent Company of Sioux City Iowa Cavalry


> MILTON SAYLER,Speaker of the House of Representatives pro tempore
> T. W. FERRYPresident of the Senate pro tempore


*Received by the President July 6, 1876.*

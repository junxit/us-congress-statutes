# Chapter 190

> An Act For the relief of Maria W. Sanders.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Maria W. Sanders, of Pittsburgh, in the State of Pennsylvania, upon the list of pensioners, as the widow of Brevet Major John Sanders, at the rate of thirty dollars per month, to continue during her widowhood.


> SAMUEL S. COX.Speaker of the House of Representatives pro tempore
> T. W. FERRYPresident of the Senate pro tempore


*Received by the President June 30, 1876.*

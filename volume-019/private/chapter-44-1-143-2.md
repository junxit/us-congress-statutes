# Chapter 143

> An Act Granting a pension to Julia A. Schutt, widow of Martin Schutt, a deceased soldier.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Inte rior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll the name of Julia A. Schutt, widow of Martin Schutt, a private in Company I, in the Fourth regiment of Heavy Artillery, New York Volunteers, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1877.*

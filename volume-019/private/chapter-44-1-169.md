# Chapter 169

> An Act To remove the disabilities of Lawrence S. Baker, of Tarboro, North Carolina.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, two thirds of each House concurring)*

That all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States by reason of participation in the late rebellion, be, and they are hereby removed from Lawrence S. Baker, of Tarboro in the State of North Carolina


*Approved, March 3, 1877.*

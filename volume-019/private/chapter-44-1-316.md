# Chapter 316

> An Act Granting a pension to Mary M. J. Frank.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Inte rior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Mary M. J. Frank, widow of Paul Frank, late colonel Fifty-second Regiment New York Volunteers, to take effect from the passage of this act.


*Approved, August 15, 1876.*

# Chapter 88

> An Act To remove the political disabilities of W. F. Carrington, of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein,)*

That all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States on W. F. Carrington, a citizen of the State of Virginia, be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, March 2, 1877.*

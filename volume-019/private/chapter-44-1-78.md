# Chapter 78

> An Act For the relief of Henry B. Kelly, of Louisiana, from political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two thirds of each House concurring therein,)*

That all political disabilities imposed upon Henry B. Kelly, a citizen of Louisiana by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States by reason of participation in the late rebellion, are hereby removed.


*Approved, February 28, 1877.*

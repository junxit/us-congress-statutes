# Chapter 116

> An Act For the relief of Mrs. Eliza Potter, widow of Lorenzo T. Potter deceased late of Charleston, South Carolina.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be, and hereby is, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of twenty thousand dollars to Mrs Eliza Potter, widow of Lorenzo T. Potter, deceased, late of Charleston, South Carolina.


> SAMUEL S. COX,Speaker of the House of Representatives pro tempore
> T. W. FERRYPresident of the Senate pro tempore


*Received by the President May 20, 1876.*

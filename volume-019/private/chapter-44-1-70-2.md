# Chapter 70

> An Act For the relief of the late Admiral Charles Wilkes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the Treasury Department be, and are hereby, authorized and directed to credit the late Admiral Charles Wilkes, with the sum of three hundred and fifty dollars, being the amount paid by Paymaster Hosford to Paymaster Tolfree on account of mess-bill, and which was repaid to Pay master Hosford, but not taken up in his account.


*Approved, February 27, 1877.*

# Chapter 100

> An Act Granting a pension to James Eli Butts and Malinda Frances Butts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of James Eli Butts and Malinda Frances Butts, minor heirs of Joseph Butts, late a private in Company G, Seventh Regiment Maryland Volunteers.


> SAMUEL S. COXSpeaker of the House of Representatives pro tempore
> T. W. FERRYPresident of the Senate pro tempore


*Received by the President May 6, 1876.*

# Chapter 6

> An Act Directing the Second Auditor to settle the pay and bounty account of John Ammahaie or Ammahe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Second Auditor of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to settle the claim for pay and bounty of John Ammahe or Ammahaie, of Companies E and H of the Forty third New York Volunteers and to issue a certificate for such amount as appears to be due, any charge of desertion upon any rolls of said regiment to the contrary notwithstanding.


*Approved, December 20, 1876.*

# Chapter 206

> An Act Granting a pension to Abigail S. Dawney.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Abigail S. Dawney, dependent mother of Nathan U. Dawney, late a private of Company G of the One hundredth and forty fifth Regiment of Pennsylvania Volunteers, deceased, to take effect from and after the passage of this act.


> MILTON SAYLER,Speaker of the House of Representatives pro tempore
> T. W. FERRYPresident of the Senate pro tempore


*Received by the President July 6, 1876.*

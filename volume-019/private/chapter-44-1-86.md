# Chapter 86

> An Act To remove the political disabilities of Lloyd J. Beall, of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two thirds of each House concurring therein)*

That all the political disabilities imposed by the act of July second eighteen hundred and sixty two, and the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States, upon Lloyd J. Beall, a citizen of the State of Virginia, be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, March 2, 1877.*

# Chapter 194

> An Act To increase the pension of Helen M. Stansbury.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Helen M. Stansbury, widow of Major Howard Stansbury, late major in the Corps of Engineers, United States Army, a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month from and after the passage of this act, said pension to be in lieu of the pension now paid to the said Helen M. Stansbury.


*Approved, March 3, 1877.*

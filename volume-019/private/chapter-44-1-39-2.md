# Chapter 39

> An Act Granting a pension to Margaret C. Bell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Margaret C. Bell, widow of Admiral Henry H. Bell, late of the United States Navy, and that she be paid a pension of fifty dollars per month from and after the passage of this act, which shall be in lieu of the pension now received by her.


> SAM’ J. RANDALLSpeaker of the House of Representatives
> T. W. FERRYPresident of the Senate pro tempore


*Received by the President January 17, 1877.*

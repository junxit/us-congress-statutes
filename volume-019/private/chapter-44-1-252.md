# Chapter 252

> An Act To remove the political disabilities of Samuel Jones, of Virginia

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein)*

That all political disabilities imposed upon Samuel Jones, of Virginia, by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States, by reason of participation in the rebellion, are hereby removed.


*Approved, August 2, 1876.*

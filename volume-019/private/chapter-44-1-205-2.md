# Chapter 205

> An Act Granting a pension to Sarah A. Chamberlain, guardian of the minor heirs of James Eagle, Company F, Second Regiment Kansas State Militia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Inte rior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Sarah A. Chamberlain, guardian of the minor heirs of James Eagle, late private Company F, Second Regiment Kansas State Militia.


*Approved, March 3, 1877.*

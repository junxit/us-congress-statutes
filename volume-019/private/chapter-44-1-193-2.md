# Chapter 193

> An Act Granting a pension to William H. Oliver, of Sweetwater, Tennessee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of William H. Oliver, a private in Company D. of the fifth Regiment of the Tennessee Volunteers, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1877.*

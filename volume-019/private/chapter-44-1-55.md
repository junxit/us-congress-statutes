# Chapter 55

> An Act Granting increased pension to Thomas G Kingsley

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to grant an increase of pension to Thomas G. Kingsley, formerly colonel of the Twenty-sixth Connecticut Volunteers, equal to the special rate provided for the loss of both eves or both feet or hands.


> SAM’ J. RANDALLSpeaker of the House of Representatives
> T. W. FERRYPresident of the Senate pro tempore


*Received by the President January 30, 1877.*

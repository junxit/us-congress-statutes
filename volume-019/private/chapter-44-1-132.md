# Chapter 132

> An Act To remove the political disabilities of Francis T. Nicholls, of Louisiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein,)*

That all the political disabilities imposed upon Francis T. Nicholls, of Louisiana, by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States, by reason of his participation in the rebellion, are hereby removed.


*Approved, June 16, 1876.*

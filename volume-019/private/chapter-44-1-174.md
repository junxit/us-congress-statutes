# Chapter 174

> An Act Granting a pension to Robert Cavanaugh.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Robert Cavanaugh, late a private in Company D, Ninety-eighth regiment Ohio Infantry Volunteers.


> SAMUEL S. COX.Speaker of the House of Representatives
> T. W. FERRYPresident of the Senate pro tempore


*Received by the President June 27, 1876.*

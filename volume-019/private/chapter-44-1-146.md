# Chapter 146

> An Act For the relief of J. B. McCullough.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting-officers of the Treasury be, and they are hereby, directed to pay to J. B. McCullough, of Petersburg, Virginia, the sum of three hundred and twenty-eight dollars and eighty-seven cents; the said sum being for the rent of property belonging to the aforesaid party, and occupied under contract by the United States, and for which vouchers were given to him; and an amount sufficient to pay the same is hereby appropriated from the Treasury.


*Approved, March 3, 1877.*

# Chapter 46

> An Act Granting a pension to George McColly.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George McColly, private in Company E, First Minnesota Volunteers.


> SAM’ J. RANDALLSpeaker of the House of Representatives
> T. W. FERRYPresident of the Senate pro tempore


*Received by the President January 20, 1877.*

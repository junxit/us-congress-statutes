# Chapter 313

> An Act For the relief of William H. French, Jr. U. S. A. late Indian agent at Crow Creek, Dakota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officer of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to adjust and settle the property-accounts of Wm. H. French, Junior, late Indian agent at Crow Creek, Dakota Territory, and if it shall be made clearly to ap pear that John A. Morrow, who was a contractor for furnishing supplies of beef-cattle to the Indian agencies, has delivered to Henry F. Livingston, agent at Crow Creek, beef-cattle in lieu of four hundred and twenty-two thousand one hundred pounds, which he delivered to Wm. H. French, agent, on ninth November, eighteen hundred and seventy, at Crow Creek, and for which said Morrow afterwards got receipts from said Livingston, and collected from the Government on both, then said accounting officer shall be authorized to give said French credit in his settlement accordingly.


*Approved, August 15, 1876.*

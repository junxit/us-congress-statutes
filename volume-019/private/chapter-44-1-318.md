# Chapter 318

> An Act Granting a pension to Levi D. Hayward.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Inte rior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Levi D Hayward, late a private in Company B, Thirty-first Regiment Iowa Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, August 15, 1876.*

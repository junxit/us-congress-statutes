# Chapter 62

> An Act To remove the political disabilities of Reuben Davis of Mississippi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two thirds of each House concurring therein,)*

That all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States by reason of participation in the rebellion, be, and they are hereby, removed from Reuben Davis, a citizen of the State of Mississippi.


*Approved, February 17, 1877.*

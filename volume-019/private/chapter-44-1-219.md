# Chapter 219

> An Act For the relief of Martha J. Coston.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of thirteen thousand dollars be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Martha J. Coston, in full of all claim and demand of the said Martha J. Coston upon the Government of the United States for the use of the Coston signal-light, and the manufacture by her of the same.


> MILTON SAYLER,Speaker of the House of Representatives pro tempore.
> T. W. FERRYPresident of the Senate pro tempore


*Received by the President July 8, 1876.*

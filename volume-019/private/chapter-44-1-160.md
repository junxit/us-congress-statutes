# Chapter 160

> An Act For the relief of James A Jackson and others securities of G R Horton late Post Master at Monticello Arkansas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That James A Jacksen John Hussy Eli Rogers Iverson L Brooks and William F Slemons, securities of George R Horton late Post Master at Monticello Arkansas be, and they are hereby, released from all liability as such securities on account of post office money stamps and money order-funds, stolen from said office on the sixth day of June, eighteen hundred and seventy four amounting to the sum of one thousand and seventy eight dollars and forty eight cents without any fault of said securities or the said Post Master


*Approved, March 3, 1877.*

# Chapter 64

> An Act To remove the political disabilities of Joseph E. Johnston of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two thirds of each House concurring therein,)*

That all the political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States, upon Joseph E. Johnston of Virginia, be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, February 23, 1877.*

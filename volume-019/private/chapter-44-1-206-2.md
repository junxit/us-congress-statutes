# Chapter 206

> An Act Granting a pension to Irena Garrett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Irena Garrett, widow of William H. Garrett, late captain of Company K, One hundred and third Ohio Volunteers.


*Approved, March 3, 1877.*

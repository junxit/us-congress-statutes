# Chapter 243

> An Act Making an appropriation to pay the claim of Butler, Miller and Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of seven thousand two hundred and twenty-four dollars and eighty-eight cents be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Butler, Miller and Company, for cotton taken at Nashville, Tennessee, in eighteen hundred and sixty-three, by the United States Army, and in full satisfaction and discharge of the claim of said Butler, Miller and Company, for such cotton.


> MILTON SAYLER,Speaker of the House of Representatives pro tempore.
> T. W. FERRYPresident of the Senate pro tempore


*Received by the President July 19, 1876.*

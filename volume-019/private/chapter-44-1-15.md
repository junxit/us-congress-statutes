# Chapter 15

> An Act For the relief of Dickson Shinault late assistant keeper of the light vessel at Wolf Trap light-station in the State of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting- officers of the Treasury be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to adjust and settle the account of Dickson Shinault, late assistant keeper of the light-vessel at Wolf Trap light-station in the State of Virginia, and allow him the sum of one hundred and twenty dollars, being the amount due him as salary in full for all wages in eighteen hundred and sixty-one.


> SAM’ J. RANDALLSpeaker of the House of Representatives
> T. W. FERRYPresident of the Senate pro tempore


*Received by the President December 16, 1876.*

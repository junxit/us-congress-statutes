# Chapter 257

> An Act To remove the political disabilities of Thomas H. Williams, of Maryland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein)*

That Thomas H. Williams, of the State of Maryland, be, and is hereby, relieved of all political disabilities imposed by the third section of the fourteenth article of amendments to the Constitution of the United States.


*Approved, August 9, 1876.*

# Public Resolution 1

> Joint resolution Authorizing an allowance to certain clerks of committees

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid out of the contingent funds of the respective houses, the usual per diem allowance to Clerks of Committees not having a yearly salary, for the period of thirty-one days from the first day of August eighteen hundred and seventy-six.


*Approved, December 9, 1876.*

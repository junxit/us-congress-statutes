# Public Resolution 5

> Joint resolution Authorizing the Public Printer to bind in cloth the stitched copies of the House compilation entitled “Counting the Electoral Vote.”

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Public Printer be authorized and required, to bind in cloth at the earliest time practicable for the use of the two houses of Congress the stitched copies of the House compilation entitled “Counting the Electoral Vote,” except such copies as are required for distribution to public libraries.


*Approved, February 5, 1877.*

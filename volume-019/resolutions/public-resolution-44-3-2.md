# Public Resolution 3

> Joint resolution Authorizing the Secretary of War to supply blankets to the Reform School in the District of Columbia.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to deliver, for use of the Reform School of the District of Columbia, two hundred Army blankets, from any in his custody belonging to the United States.


*Approved, January 26, 1877.*

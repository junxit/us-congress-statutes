# Public Resolution 12

> Joint resolution Granting the use of artillery, blankets &c at the National Soldiers Reunion at Caldwell, Ohio.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be and is hereby authorized to Send from some convenient Government Arsenal to be used at the National Soldiers Reunion at Caldwell, Ohio at its next meeting four pieces of artillery and such blankets and muskets and blank cartridges as can be spared said cannon, blankets and muskets to be returned after said reunion meeting.


*Approved, June 8, 1876.*

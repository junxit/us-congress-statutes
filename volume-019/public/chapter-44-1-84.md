# Chapter 84

> An act To correct an error in the Revised Statutes of the United States, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purpose of correcting an error in the act entitled “An act to revise and consolidate the statutes of the United States, in force on the first day of December, anno Domini one thousand eight hundred and seventy-three,” so as to make the same truly express such laws, the following amendment is hereby made therein:

Section two thousand four hundred and three is amended by striking out in the second line the word “seven” and inserting the word one, and all proceedings under said section two thousand four hundred and three shall have the same force and effect as though enacted as herein amended.


*Approved, April 27, 1876.*

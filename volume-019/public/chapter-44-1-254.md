# Chapter 254

> An act To authorize the construction of an inclosure around the United States penitentiary at Boise City in the Territory of Idaho.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be, and is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one thousand five hundred dollars, or so much thereof as may be required, to construct a wooden inclosure around the United States penitentiary at Boise City in the Territory of Idaho, to be expended by the United States marshal for the district of Idaho, under the direction of the Attorney-General of the United States.


*Approved, August 5, 1876.*

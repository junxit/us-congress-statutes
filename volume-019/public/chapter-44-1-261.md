# Chapter 261

> An act To authorize the Secretary of the Treasury to change the name of the steamboat “Hiram Wood.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and is hereby, authorized to change the name of the steamboat “Hiram Wood” to “Dr. Burleigh,” and grant an enrolment license in such other name.


*Approved, August 12, 1876.*

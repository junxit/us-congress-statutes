# Chapter 29

> An act To establish certain post-routes in the State of Texas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the following post-routes be, and they are hereby, established, to wit: A post-route from Paris, Lamar County, Texas, by way of Cotton Plant, in said county, and by way of Cooper, in Delta County, in said State, to Sulphur Springs, in Hopkins County, in said State; also, a post-route from Bonham, in Fannin County, Texas, by way of Ladonia, in said county, and Ben Franklin, in Delta County, Texas, to Cooper in said county.


*Approved, March 16, 1876.*

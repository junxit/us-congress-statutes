# Chapter 19

> An act To change the name of the schooner “Turner and Keller” of Oswego, to that of “Falmouth”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the owners of the schooner “Turner and Keller” of Oswego, New York, have authority to change the mime of said schooner to “Falmouth” by which name said schooner shall hereafter be known and registered.


*Approved, March 3, 1876.*

# Chapter 133

> An act Placing the name of C. G. Freudenberg upon the retired list of the United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper authorities be, and they are hereby, directed to place the name of C. G. Freudenberg upon the retired-list of Army officers, under the rank and grade of lieutenant-colonel, instead of captain, the rank now held by him on said retired-list; and that said promotion take effect from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1877.*

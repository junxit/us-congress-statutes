# Chapter 21

> An act To change the name of the steamship “Whirl-wind” co that of “Arcadia”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to allow the owners of the steamship “Whirl-wind,” a vessel of American ownership and register, to change her name, and be hereafter known as the “Arcadia.”


*Approved, January 13, 1877.*

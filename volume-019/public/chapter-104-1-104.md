# Chapter 104

> An act To extend the time to pre-emptors on the public lands

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That whenever any pre emptor on public lands or Indian reservations shall make satisfactory proof, at the local land office, under rules and regulations to be prescribed by the Secretary of the Interior, that the crops upon the lands occupied by him have been destroyed by grasshoppers within two years prior to the passage of this act, the time within which such pre-emptor is required to make final proof and payment is hereby extended two years.


*Approved, May 23, 1876.*

# Chapter 167

> An act To amend section twelve hundred and twenty-five of the Revised Statutes of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section twelve hundred and twenty-five of the Revised Statutes of the United States be so amended as to read, “But the number of officers so detailed shall not exceed thirty at any time,” instead of twenty, as in said section provided.


*Approved, July 5, 1876.*

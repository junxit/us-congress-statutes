# Chapter 222

> An act Establishing the rank of the Paymaster-General

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the passage of this act, the rank of the Pay master-General of the United States Army shall be brigadier-general; but no pay or allowances shall be made to said officer other than from the date of appointment under this act.


*Approved, July 22, 1876.*

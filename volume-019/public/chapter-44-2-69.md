# Chapter 69

> An act To perfect the revision of the statutes of the United States, and of the statutes relating to the District of Columbia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purpose of correcting errorors and supplying omissions in the act entitled “An act to revise and consolidate the statutes of the United States in force on the first day of December, anno Domini one thousand eight hundred and seventy three,” so as to make the same truly express such laws, the following amendments are hereby made therein:

# Chapter 158

> An act To continue the public printing

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Congressional Printer is hereby authorized to continue the work required by law, in advance of appropriations to be hereafter made; and this act shall continue in force for ten days.


*Approved, June 30, 1876.*

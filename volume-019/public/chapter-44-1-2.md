# Chapter 2

> An act To declare the road leading from Concordia Kentucky to Mooleyville Kentucky a pose rood.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the road leading from Concordia, or Flint Island post office, in Kentucky to Mooleyville Kentucky, be, and the same is hereby declared to be and established as a post road.


*Approved, January 18, 1876.*

# Chapter 34

> An act Making appropriation for the improvement and repair of the military road between Springfield and Fort Randall in the Territory of Dakota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be, and hereby is, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of twenty five hundred dollars, to be expended by and under the direction and authority of the Secretary of War, for the improvement and repair of the military road leading from Sioux City, in the State of Iowa, to Fort Randall, in the Territory of Dakota, the same to be expended between Springfield in said Territory and Fort Randall.


*Approved, January 24, 1877.*

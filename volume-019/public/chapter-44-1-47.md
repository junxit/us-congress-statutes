# Chapter 47

> An act To supply a deficiency in the appropriations for certain Indians.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purpose of supplying the “Sioux Indians of different tribes, including the Santee Sioux of Nebraska,” with necessary subsistence, namely, beef, bacon, flour and corn, and for the necessary transportation thereof, the sum of one hundred and fifty thousand dollars is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, April 6, 1876.*

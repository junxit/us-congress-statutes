# Chapter 100

> An act To repeal the statute forbidding appointments and promotions in the staff of the Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section eleven hundred and ninety-four of the Revised Statutes, now applying only to grades in the Pay Department of the Army above the rank of major, is hereby repealed.


*Approved, March 3, 1877.*

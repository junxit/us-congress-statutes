# Chapter 262

> An act To change the name of the pleasure yacht “Ella” to that of “Myra”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of the pleasure yacht “Ella,” registered in the southern district of New York, be, and the same is hereby, changed to “Myra;” and the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized to grant a register in accordance therewith.


*Approved, August 12, 1876.*

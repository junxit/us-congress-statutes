# Chapter 46

> An act To supply a deficiency in the appropriation for the manufacture of postal-cards for the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, eighteen hundred and seventy-six.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of sixty-two thousand three hundred dollars be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to supply a deficiency in the appropriation for the manufacture of postal-cards tor the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, eighteen hundred and seventy-six.


*Approved, April 6, 1876.*

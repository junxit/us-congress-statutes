# Chapter 132

> An act To change the name of the pleasure yacht Hiram B. to Iola.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Bouse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That authority is hereby granted to the owner of the pleasure yacht Hiram B., of the port of Edgartown, Massachusetts, to change the name of said yacht to that of Iola, by which name said vessel shall be known and registered; and the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby authorized and directed to allow said change of name


*Approved, March 3, 1877.*

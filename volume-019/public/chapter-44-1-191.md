# Chapter 191

> An act To continue the act entitled “An act to continue the public printing”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of an act entitled “An act to continue the public printing” approved June thirtieth, eighteen hundred and seventy-six, be, and the same are hereby extended and continued in full force and effect for a period of ten days from and after the tenth day July, eighteen hundred and seventy six, and no longer.


*Approved, July 18, 1876.*

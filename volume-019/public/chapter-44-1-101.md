# Chapter 101

> An act Appropriating nine thousand dollars to pay the expenses of the Select Committee to investigate the Federal offices in Louisiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of nine thousand dollars, or so much thereof as may be necessary, be, and the same is hereby, appropriated from any moneys in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to defray the expenses of the Select Committee charged with the investigation of the Federal offices in Louisiana; said appropriation to be added to the contingent fund of the House.


*Approved, May 19, 1876.*

# Chapter 86

> An act To protect the public property, turf and grass of the Capitol Grounds from injury.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That it shall be the duty of the Capitol police hereafter to prevent any portion of the Capitol grounds and terraces from being used as play-grounds or otherwise, so far as may be necessary to protect the public property, turf and grass from destruction or injury.


*Approved, April 29, 1876.*

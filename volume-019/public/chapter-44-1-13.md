# Chapter 13

> An act To extend the time for stamping unstamped instruments.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of the act entitled “An act to provide for the stamping of unstamped instruments, documents, or papers,” approved the twenty-third day of June, in the year eighteen hundred and seventy-four, be, and the same are hereby, extended to the first day of January, in the year eighteen hundred and seventy-seven.


*Approved, February 25, 1876.*

# Chapter 53

> An act Donating the military road running from Astoria, Oregon, to Salem, in that State, to the several counties through which it passes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the military road from Astoria to Salem, in the State of Oregon, constructed under the following acts of Congress, to wit, acts approved February seventeenth, eight-een hundred and fifty-five; March second, eighteen hundred and fifty-seven and June fourteenth, eighteen hundred and fifty-eight, be, and the same is hereby, donated to the several couuties in said State through which it runs, to wit, Clatsop, Washington, Yam Hill, and Polk, to each such portion as runs through it; said counties hereafter to own and control the same; and such road is hereby abandoned as a military road.


*Approved, April 11, 1876.*

# Chapter 3

> An act To change the name of the steamboat “Tom Jasper,” of Saint Louis, Missouri.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That authority is hereby given to the owner of the steamboat “Tom Jasper,” of Saint Louis, Missouri, to change the name of said vessel to that of “Centennial,” by which said steamboat shall hereafter be known.


*Approved, January 18, 1876.*

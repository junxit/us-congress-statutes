# Chapter 154

> An act To amend section one thousand nine hundred and eleven of the Revised Statutes of the United States defining the jurisdiction of the courts in Washington Territory.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section one thousand nine hundred and eleven of the Revised Statutes of the United States be amended by inserting the words “and laws” after the word “Constitution” in the latter clause of said section.


*Approved, June 29, 1876.*

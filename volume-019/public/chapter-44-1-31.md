# Chapter 31

> An act To confirm certain school-indemnity selections of public lands by the State of Nebraska

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the selections of school lands made by the State of Nebraska as indemnity for tracts in sections sixteen and thirty-six otherwise disposed of, which are suspended in the General Land Office, for the reason that they are for lands which, under the act of March sixth, eighteen hundred and sixty eight, can only be disposed of under the homestead and preemption laws, and to which no other legal objection exists be, and the same are hereby, confirmed; and title shall be transferred to the State as in other cases of such selections


*Approved, March 23, 1876.*

# Chapter 131

> An act Authorizing the changing of the name of the sloop Addie Parker of New Bedford, Massachusetts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to allow the owner of the sloop Addie Parker, a vessel of American ownership and license, to change her name, and be hereafter known as the Addie.


*Approved, March 3, 1877.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 604

> ADJOURNMENT SINE DIE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Friday, December 15, 1967, and that when they adjourn on said day, they stand adjourned sine die.


*Passed December 15, 1967.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 309

> WORLD COMMUNIST MOVEMENT PUBLICATIONS

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed as a House document with three thousand five hundred additional copies for the use of the Committee on Un-American Activities the publication entitled, “World Communist Movement—Selective Chronology 1818–1957 Prepared by the Legislative Reference Service of the Library of Congress—Volume 4, 1954–1955,” Eighty-Ninth Congress, first session.


*Passed May 19, 1967.*

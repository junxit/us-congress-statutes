# House Concurrent Resolution 1

> JOINT MEETING

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on January 10, 1967, at 9:30 o’clock p.m., for the purpose of receiving such communication as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed January 10, 1967.*

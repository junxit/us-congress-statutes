# Senate Concurrent Resolution 51

> ADJOURNMENT

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the two Houses shall adjourn on Wednesday, November 22, 1967, and that when they adjourn on said day they stand adjourned until 12 o’clock noon on Monday, November 27, 1967.


*Agreed to November 21, 1967.*

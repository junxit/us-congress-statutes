# Senate Concurrent Resolution 49

> FINLAND—ANNIVERSARY OF INDEPENDENCE

Whereas the year 1967 marks the fiftieth anniversary of the independence of Finland: and Whereas these fifty years have been marked by close ties of friendship and association between Finland and the United States: Now, therefore. be it Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),

That the Congress of the United States extends its congratulations and best wishes to the Parliament, of Finland on the occasion of the fiftieth anniversary of the independence of Finland and in affirmation of the affection and friendship of the people of the United States for the people of Finland.


*Agreed to November 27, 1967.*

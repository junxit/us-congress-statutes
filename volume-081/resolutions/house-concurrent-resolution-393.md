# House Concurrent Resolution 393

> ADJOURNMENT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses shall adjourn on Thursday, June 29, 1967, and that when they adjourn on said day they stand adjourned until 12 o’clock noon on Monday, July 10, 1967.


*Passed June 29, 1967.*

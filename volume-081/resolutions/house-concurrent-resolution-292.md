# House Concurrent Resolution 292

> ADJOURNMENT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Thursday, March 23, 1967, they stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Monday, April 3, 1967.


*Passed March 22, 1967.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 346

> “OUR FLAG”

*Revolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the publication entitled “Our Flag”, published by the Directorate for Armed Forces Information and Education, Department of Defense, be printed with illustrations as a House document; and that two hundred and nineteen thousand five hundred additional copies be printed for the use of the House of Representatives.


*Passed July 17, 1967.*

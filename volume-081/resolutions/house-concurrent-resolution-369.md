# House Concurrent Resolution 369

> PUBLICATIONS OF THE SELECT COMMITTEE ON SMALL BUSINESS

*Revolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed for the use of the Select Committee on Small Business of the House of Representatives two thousand additional copies of each of its following publications: hearings entitled “Tax-Exempt Foundations: Their Impact on Small Business”, Eighty- eighth Congress, second session; committee print entitled “Tax- Exempt Foundations and Charitable Trusts: Their Impact on Our Economy”. Eighty-seventh Congress—December 31, 1962; and committee print entitled “Tax-Exempt Foundations and Charitable Trusts: Their Impact on Our Economy-(Third Installment)”—Eighty-eighth Congress—March 20, 1964.


*Passed July 17, 1967.*

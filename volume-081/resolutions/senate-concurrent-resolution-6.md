# Senate Concurrent Resolution 6

> “THE FEDERAL SYSTEM AS SEEN BY FEDERAL AID OFFICIALS”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on Government Operations five thousand additional copies of its committee print of the Eighty-ninth Congress, first session, entitled “The Federal System as Seen by Federal Aid Officials”, a study prepared by the Subcommittee on Intergovernmental Relations.


*Agreed to May 11, 1967.*

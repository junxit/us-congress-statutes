# House Concurrent Resolution 279

> “METROPOLITAN AMERICA: CHALLENGE TO FEDERALISM”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Government Operations, House of Representatives, five thousand additional copies of its committee print entitled “Metropolitan America: Challenge to Federalism.” of the Eighty-ninth Congress, second session.


*Passed May 19, 1967.*

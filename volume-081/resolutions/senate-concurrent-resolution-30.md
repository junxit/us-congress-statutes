# Senate Concurrent Resolution 30

> “MINERAL AND WATER RESOURCES OF ALASKA”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That two thousand copies of the committee print entitled “Mineral and Water Resources of Alaska”, prepared by the United States Geological Survey and the State of Alaska Department of Natural Resources, be reprinted with illustrations for the Senate Committee on Interior and Insular Affairs.


*Agreed to June 27, 1967.*

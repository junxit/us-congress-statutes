# House Concurrent Resolution 519

> HEARINGS OF THE COMMISSION ON POLITICAL ACTIVITY OF GOVERNMENT PERSONNEL

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed for the use of the Committee on House Administration of the House of Representatives, two thousand copies of the hearings of the Commission on Political Activity of Government Personnel established by Public Law 89–617, and two thousand copies of such hearings for the use of the Committee on Rules and Administration of the Senate.


*Passed November 30, 1967.*

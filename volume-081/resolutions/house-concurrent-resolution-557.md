# House Concurrent Resolution 557

> THE CONSTITUTION OF THE UNITED STATES AND THE DECLARATION OF INDEPENDENCE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Constitution of the United States, as amended to February 10, 1967, together with the Declaration of Independence, be printed as a House document, with an index, in such form and style as may be directed by the Joint Committee on Printing, and that one hundred and six thousand six hundred additional copies be printed, of which eighty thousand eight hundred and fifty copies shall be for the use of the House of Representatives and twenty-five thousand seven hundred and fifty copies shall be for the use of the Senate.


*Passed November 30, 1967.*

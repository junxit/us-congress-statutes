# House Concurrent Resolution 394

> SIGNING OF ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That notwithstanding any adjournment of the two Houses until July 10, 1967, the Speaker of the House of Representatives and the President, President, pro tempore, or the Acting President pro tempore of the Senate be, and they are hereby, authorized to sign enrolled bills and joint resolutions duly passed by the two Houses and found truly enrolled.


*Passed June 29, 1967.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 498

> ADJOURNMENT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses shall adjourn on Thursday, August 31, 1967, and that when they adjourn on said day they stand adjourned until 12 o’clock noon on Monday, September 11, 1967.


*Passed August 29, 1967.*

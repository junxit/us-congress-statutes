# Senate Concurrent Resolution 42

> “RETIREMENT AND THE INDIVIDUAL”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Special Committee on Aging five thousand five hundred additional copies each of parts 1 and 2 of its hearings of the Ninetieth Congress, first session, entitled “Retirement and the Individual”.


*Agreed to November 16, 1967.*

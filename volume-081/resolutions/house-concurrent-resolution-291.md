# House Concurrent Resolution 291

> INVASION OF PRIVACY PUBLICATIONS

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Government Operations. House of Representatives, five thousand additional copies of its committee hearings entitled, “Special Inquiry on Invasion of Privacy,” and five thousand additional copies of its committee hearings entitled, “The Computer and Invasion of Privacy,” of the Eighty-ninth Congress, first and second sessions.


*Passed May 19, 1967.*

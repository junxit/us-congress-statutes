# Public Law 90–62

> AN ACT To extend the authority for exemptions from the antitrust laws to assist in safeguarding the balance-of-payments position of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 6 of the Act of September 9, 1965 (79 Stat. 674; 31 U.S.C., Supp. I, 936) is amended by striking out “twenty months after it becomes law” and inserting in lieu thereof “on June 30, 1969”.


*Approved August 9, 1967.*

# Public Law 90–75

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 1968 and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That clause (c) of section 102 of the joint resolution of June 8, 1967 (Public Law 90–38), is hereby amended by striking out “August 31, 1967” and inserting in lieu thereof “September 30, 1967”.


*Approved August 29, 1967.*

# Public Law 90–111

> AN ACT To extend the provisions of the Act of October 23, 1962, relating to relief for occupants of certain unpatented mining claims.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first section of the Act entitled “An Act to provide relief for residential occupants of unpatented mining claims upon which valuable improvements have been placed, and for other purposes”, approved October 23, 1962 (30 U.S.C. 701), is amended by striking out “five years from the date of this Act” and inserting in lieu thereof “the period ending June 30, 1971”.

## § 2.

Section 6(b) of such Act of October 23, 1962 (30 U.S.C. 706(b)), is amended by striking out “five years from the date of its enactment” and inserting in lieu thereof “the period ending June 30, 1971”.


*Approved October 23, 1967.*

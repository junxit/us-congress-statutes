# Public Law 90–217

> AN ACT To provide that the United States District Court for the Eastern District of New York shall be held at Brooklyn, New York, and Mineola, New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second paragraph of section 112(c) of title 28, United States Code, is amended to read as follows:

> “Court for the Eastern District shall be held at Brooklyn and Mineola.”


*Approved December 18, 1967.*

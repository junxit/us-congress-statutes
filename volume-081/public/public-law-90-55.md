# Public Law 90–55

> AN ACT To extend the life of the Commission on Political Activity of Government Personnel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first sentence of section 7(b) of the Act entitled “An Act to create a bi-partisan commission to study Federal laws limiting political activity by officers and employees of Government”, approved October 3, 1966 (80 Stat. 868), is amended to read as follows: “The Commission shall submit a comprehensive report of its activities and the results of its studies to the President and to the Congress on or before December 31, 1967, and upon the filing of the report, the Commission shall cease to exist.”


*Approved July 20, 1967.*

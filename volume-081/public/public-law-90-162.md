# Public Law 90–162

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 1068, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the joint resolution of October 5, 1967 (Public Law 90–102) as amended, is hereby further amended by striking out “November 9, 1967” and inserting in lieu thereof “December 2, 1967”.


*Approved November 28, 1967.*

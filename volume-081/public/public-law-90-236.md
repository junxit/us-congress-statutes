# Public Law 90–236

> AN ACT To amend title 37, United States Code, to authorize the nontemporary storage of household effects of members of a missing status.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 554(b) of title 37, United States Code, is amended by adding the following new flush sentence:

> “When he considers it necessary, the Secretary concerned may, with respect to the household and personal effects of a member who is officially reported as absent for a period of more than twenty-nine days in a missing status, authorize the nontemporary storage or those effects for a period of one year, or longer when justified.”


*Approved January 2, 1968.*

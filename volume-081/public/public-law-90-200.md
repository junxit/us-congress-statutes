# Public Law 90–200

> AN ACT To give the consent of Congress to the State of Ohio to become a party to the agreement relating to bus taxation proration and reciprocity as set forth in title II of the Act of April 14, 1965 (79 Stat. 60), and consented to by Congress in that Act and in the Acts of November 1, 1965 (79 Stat 1157), and November 2, 1966 (80 Stat. 1156).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the consent of Congress is given to the State of Ohio to become a party to the agreement relating to bus taxation proration and reciprocity as set forth in title II of the Act of April 14, 1965 (79 Stat. 60), and consented to by Congress in that Act and in the Acts of November 1, 1965 (79 Stat. 1157) and November 2, 1966 (80 Stat. 1156).

## § 2.

The right to alter, amend or repeal this Act is expressly reserved.


*Approved December 14, 1967.*

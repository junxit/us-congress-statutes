# Public Law 90–48

> AN ACT To continue until the close of September 30, 1967, the existing suspension of duties on certain forms of nickel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** items 911.21 (relating to ferronickel), 911.22 (relating to unwrought nickel), and 911.23 (relating to nickel powders) of the Tariff Schedules of the United States (19 U.S.C. 1202) are each amended by striking out “6/30/67” and inserting in lieu thereof “9/30/67”.
- **(b)** The amendments made by subsection (a) shall apply with respect to articles entered, or withdrawn from warehouse, for consumption, after June 30, 1967.


*Approved July 7, 1967.*

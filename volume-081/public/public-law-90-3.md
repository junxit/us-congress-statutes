# Public Law 90–3

> AN ACT To provide, for the period ending on June 30, 1907, a temporary increase in the public debt limit set forth in section 21 of the Second Liberty Bond Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, during the period beginning on the date of the enactment of this Act and ending on June 30, 1967, the public debt limit set forth in the first sentence of section 21 of the Second Liberty Bond Act (31 U.S.C. 757b) shall lie temporarily increased to $336,000,000,000.


*Approved March 2, 1967, 10:45 p.m.*

# Public Law 90–244

> AN ACT To amend sections 3 and 4 of the Act approved September 22, 1964 (78 Stat. 990), providing for an investigation and study to determine a site for the construction of a sea-level canal connecting the Atlantic and Pacific Oceans.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act approved September 22, 1964 (Public Law 88–609, 78 Stat. 990), as amended, is hereby further amended by striking out “June 30, 1968” in section 3 and inserting in lieu thereof “December 1, 1969”.


*Approved January 2, 1968.*

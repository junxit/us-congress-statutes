# Public Law 90–145

> AN ACT To extend the period during which Secret Service protection may be furnished to a widow and minor children of a former President.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the authority vested in the United States Secret Service by section 3056 of title 18 of the United States Code to protect the person of a widow and minor children of a former President is extended until March 1, 1969, with respect to persons receiving such protection on the date of enactment of this Act.


*Approved November 17, 1967.*

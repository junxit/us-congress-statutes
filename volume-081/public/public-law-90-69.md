# Public Law 90–69

> AN ACT To authorize the appropriation of funds to carry out the activities of the Federal Field Committee for Development Planning in Alaska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

There is authorized to be appropriated not to exceed $300,000 in any one fiscal year ending June 30, 1968, and for each fiscal year thereafter through the fiscal year ending June 30, 1970, for the operating expenses of the Federal Field Committee for Development Planning in Alaska, established by Executive Order 11182, dated October 2, 1964 (F.R. Doc. 64–10178), as authorized by section 5 of the 1964 amendments to the Alaska Omnibus Act (Public Law 88–451, August 19, 1964, 78 Stat. 505), or the successor to such Committee.


*Approved August 21, 1967.*

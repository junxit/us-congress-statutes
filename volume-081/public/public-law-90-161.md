# Public Law 90–161

> AN ACT To amend sections 40e(1) and 52a of the Bankruptcy Act so as to reallocate part of the tiling fee from the clerk’s earnings to the Referees’ Salary and Expense Fund.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph c(1) of section 40 of the Bankruptcy Act (11 U.S.C. 68c(1)) is amended by deleting “$32” and inserting in lieu thereof “$37”.

## § 2.

Paragraph a of section 52 of the Bankruptcy Act (11 U.S.C. 80a) is amended by deleting “$8” and inserting in lieu thereof “$3”.

## § 3.

This amendatory Act shall take effect on the thirtieth day following the date of its approval.


*Approved November 28, 1967.*

# Public Law 90–102

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 1968, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the joint resolution of August 29, 1967 (Public Law 90–75), is hereby amended by striking out “September 30, 1967” and inserting in lieu thereof “October 23, 1967”.


*Approved October 5, 1967.*

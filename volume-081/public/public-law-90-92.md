# Public Law 90–92

> AN ACT To provide for holding terms of the District Court of the United States for the eastern division of the Northern District of Mississippi in Ackerman, Mississippi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the third sentence of section 104(a) (1) of title 28, United States Code, is amended to read as follows:

> “Court for the eastern division shall be held at Aberdeen and Ackerman.”.


*Approved September 27, 1967.*

# Public Law 90–87

> AN ACT To extend for one year the authority for more flexible regulation of maximum rates of interest or dividends, higher reserve requirements, and open market operations in agency issues.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 7 of the Act of September 21, 1966 (80 Stat. 823), is hereby amended by striking “one-year” and inserting in lieu thereof “two-year”.


*Approved September 21, 1967.*

# Public Law 90–182

> AN ACT To provide long-term leasing for the Gila River Indian Reservation.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1 of the Act of August 9, 1955 (69 Stat. 539), as amended, is hereby amended as follows: After the words “Pyramid Lake. Reservation, insert the words “the Gila River Reservation,”.


*Approved December 8, 1967.*

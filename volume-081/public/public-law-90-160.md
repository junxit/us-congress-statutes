# Public Law 90–160

> JOINT RESOLUTION Designating February, 1968 as “American History Month”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That February, 1968 is hereby designated as “American History Month”, and the President of the United States is requested and authorized to issue a proclamation inviting the people of the United States to observe such month in schools and other suitable places with appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Approved November 28, 1967.*

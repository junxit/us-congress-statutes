# Public Law 90–246

> AN ACT To amend section 409 of title 37, United States Code, relating to the transportation of house trailers and mobile dwellings of members of the uniformed services.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second sentence of section 409 of title 37, United States Code, is amended by striking out “51 cents” and inserting in lieu thereof “74 cents”.


*Approved January 2, 1968.*

# Public Law 90–230

> JOINT RESOLUTION Establishing that the second regular session of the Ninetieth Congress convene at noon on Monday, January 15, 1968.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second regular session of the Ninetieth Congress shall begin at noon on Monday, January 15, 1968.


*Approved December 29, 1967.*

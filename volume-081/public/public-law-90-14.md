# Public Law 90–14

> AN ACT To permit duty-free treatment pursuant to the Trade Expansion Act of 1962 of dicyandiamide and of limestone when imported to be used in the manufacture of cement.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for purposes of the Trade Expansion Act of 1962, section 201(b)(1)(relating to limit on decrease in duty), sections 221, 223, and 224 (relating to certain requirements concerning negotiations), and section 253 (relating to staging requirements) of such Act shall not apply with respect to dicyandiamide provided for in item 425.40 of the Tariff Schedules of the United States, and shall not apply with respect to limestone, when imported to be used in the manufacture of cement, provided for in item 513.34 of such Schedules.


*Approved May 5, 1967.*

# Public Law 90–233

> AN ACT To amend section 301 of title III of the Act of August 14, 1946, relating to the establishment by the Secretary of Agriculture of a national advisory committee, to provide for annual meetings of such committee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the fifth sentence of section 301 of title III of the Act to provide for further research into basic laws and principles relating to agriculture and to improve and facilitate the marketing and distribution of agricultural products, approved August 14, 1946 (60 Stat. 1091), is amended to read as follows: “The committee shall meet annually and at such other times as are deemed necessary.”


*Approved December 29, 1967.*

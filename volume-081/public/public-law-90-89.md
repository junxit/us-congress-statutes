# Public Law 90–89

> AN ACT To increase the appropriation authorization for continuing work in the Missouri River Basin by the Secretary of the Interior.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act if July 19, 1966 (80 Stat. 322), is hereby amended by changing “$60,000,000” to “$68,000,000”.


*Approved September 22, 1967.*

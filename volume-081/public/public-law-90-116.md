# Public Law 90–116

> JOINT RESOLUTION To amend the joint resolution of March 25, 1953, to increase the number of electric typewriters which may be furnished to Members by the Clerk of the House.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2 of the joint resolution entitled “Joint resolution to authorize the Clerk of the House of Representatives to furnish certain electrical or mechanical office equipment for the use of Members, officers, and committees of the House of Representatives”, approved March 25, 1953 (2 U.S.C. 112a–1), is amended by striking out “four electric typewriters” and inserting in lieu thereof “five electric typewriters” and by striking out “five electric typewriters” and inserting in lieu thereof “six electric typewriters”.


*Approved October 24, 1967.*

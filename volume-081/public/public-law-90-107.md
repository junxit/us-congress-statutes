# Public Law 90–107

> AN ACT To amend the Act of January 17, 1936 (49 Stat. 1094), reserving certain public domain lands in Nevada and Oregon as a grazing reserve for Indians of Fort McDermitt, Nevada.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of January 17, 1936 (49 Stat. 1094), is amended by inserting a comma after “southwest quarter section 26; west half”.


*Approved October 12, 1967.*

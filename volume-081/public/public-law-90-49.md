# Public Law 90–49

> AN ACT To continue until the close of June 30, 1970, the existing suspension of duties on manganese ore (including ferruginous ore) and related products.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** item 911.07 of the Tariff Schedules of the United States (19 U.S.C. 1202) is amended by striking out “6/30/67” and inserting in lieu thereof “6/30/70”.
- **(b)** The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply with respect to articles entered, or withdrawn from warehouse, for consumption, after June 30, 1967.


*Approved July 7, 1967.*

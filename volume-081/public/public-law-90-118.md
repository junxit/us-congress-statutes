# Public Law 90–118

> JOINT RESOLUTION Extending the time for tiling report of Commission on Urban Problems.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

Section 301(b) of the Housing and Urban Development Act of 1965 is amended by striking “within 18 months” and all that follows and inserting in lieu thereof “not later than December 31, 1968.”


*Approved October 31, 1967.*

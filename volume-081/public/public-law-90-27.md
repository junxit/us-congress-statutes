# Public Law 90–27

> JOINT RESOLUTION To provide for the reappointment of Jerome C. Hunsaker as Citizen Regent of the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the vacancy in the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution, of the class other than Members of Congress, occurring by the expiration of the term of Jerome C. Hunsaker, of Boston, Massachusetts, on March 29, 1967, be tilled by the reappointment of the present incumbent for the statutory term of six years.


*Approved June 16, 1967.*

# Public Law 90–66

> AN ACT To amend section 810 of the Housing Act of 1964 to extend for three years the fellowship program authorized by such section.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 810 of the Housing Act of 1964 is amended by striking out “three-year period” and inserting in lieu thereof “six-year period”.


*Approved August 19, 1967.*

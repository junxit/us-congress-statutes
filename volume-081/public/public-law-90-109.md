# Public Law 90–109

> AN ACT To amend section 6409(b)(1) of title 39, United States Code, which relates to transportation compensation paid by the Postmaster General.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph (1) of section 6409(b) of title 39, United States Code, is amended by striking out “may pay compensation not to exceed eighty cents a pound for letters, post cards and postal cards, and eight cents a pound for other articles” and inserting in lieu thereof “may pay compensation at rates fixed by him”.


*Approved October 21, 1967.*

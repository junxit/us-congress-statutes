# Private Law 90–18

> AN ACT For the relief of William John Masterton and Louis Vincent Nanne.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, William John Masterton and Louis Vincent Nanne shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of September 24, 1959.


*Approved June 26, 1967.*

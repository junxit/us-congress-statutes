# Private Law 90–22

> AN ACT For the relief of Angiolina Condello.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Angiolina Condello may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1) (F) of the Act, upon approval of a petition filed in her behalf by Domenico Condello, a citizen of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of the Act.


*Approved July 7, 1967.*

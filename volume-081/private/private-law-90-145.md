# Private Law 90–145

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor G. F. Valdes-Fauli.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor G. F. Valdes-Fauli shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of October 30, I960.


*Approved November 17, 1967.*

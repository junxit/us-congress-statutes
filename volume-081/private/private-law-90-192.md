# Private Law 90–192

> AN ACT For the relief of Mr. and Mrs. Christos Photinos-Svoronos.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mr. and Mrs. Christos Photinos-Svoronos may be classified as immediate relatives within the meaning of section 201(b) of the Act, upon approval of respective petitions filed in their behalf by their adopted daughter, Mrs. Despina St. George, a citizen of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of the Act.


*Approved December 29, 1967.*

# Private Law 90–204

> AN ACT For the relief of Giuseppe Pacino Biancarosso.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Giuseppe Pacino Biancarosso may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b) (1) (F) of such Act, and a petition may be filed in his behalf by Olga Biancarosso Carmeci, a citizen of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of such Act.


*Approved January 2, 1968.*

# Private Law 90–51

> AN ACT For the relief of Rosa Anna Genovese.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, as amended, Rosa Anna Genovese may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b) (1) (F) of the Act, and a petition may be filed in her behalf by Mr. and Mrs. Seb Sbona, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of the Act.


*Approved July 29, 1967.*

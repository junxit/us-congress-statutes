# Private Law 90–169

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Eduardo Enrique Ramos.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That under the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Eduardo Enrique Ramos shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of October 26, 1960.


*Approved December 15, 1967.*

# Private Law 90–158

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Emanuel Marcus.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the failure of Doctor Emanuel Marcus to meet the licensure requirements of section 4105 of title 38, United States Code, and applicable antecedent statutes, his employment in the Department of Medicine and Surgery of the Veterans’ Administration during the period beginning October 29, 1946, and ending April 15, 1966, shall be held and considered to have been de jure for the purpose of retro-active coverage for all benefits under subchapter III of chapter 83, title 5, United States Code, credit for earned annual and sick leave and payment of final salary.


*Approved November 28, 1967.*

# Private Law 90–117

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Lucio Arsenio Travieso y Perez.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Lucio Arsenio Travieso y Perez shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of June 18, 1961.


*Approved October 31, 1967.*

# Private Law 90–156

> AN ACT For the relief of Elpidio Dimacali Damazo and Natividad Simsuangco Damazo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Elpidio Dimacali Damazo and Natividad Simsuangco Damazo shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of June 27, 1958, and July 11, 1959, respectively.


*Approved November 28, 1967.*

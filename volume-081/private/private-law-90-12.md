# Private Law 90–12

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Anselmo S. Alvarez-Gomez.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Anselmo S. Alvarez-Gomez shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of June 16, 1961.


*Approved June 14, 1967.*

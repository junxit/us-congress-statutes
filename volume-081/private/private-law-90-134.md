# Private Law 90–134

> AN ACT For the relief of Yong Ok Espantoso.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 201 (b) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Yong Ok Espantoso shall be held and considered to be an “immediate relative” and the provisions of section 204 of that Act shall be inapplicable in her case.


*Approved October 31, 1967.*

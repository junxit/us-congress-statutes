# Private Law 90–92

> AN ACT For the relief of the widow of Albert M. Pepoon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Civil Service Retirement Act of May 29, 1930 (as in effect on August 31, 1956), Albert M. Pepoon shall be considered to have retired on August 31, 1956, pursuant to section 6 of such Act, and to have elected at such time, pursuant to section 4(b) of such Act, to receive a reduced annuity and an annuity after death payable to his widow, Loretta C. Pepoon.

## § 2.

No annuity shall be payable by reason of the enactment of this Act for any period prior to the first day of the month in which this Act is enacted.

## § 3.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, benefits payable by reason of the enactment of this Act shall be paid from the civil service retirement and disability fund.


*Approved September 27, 1967.*

# Private Law 90–32

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Alberto Fernandez-Bravo y Amat.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Alberto Fernandez-Bravo y Amat shall be held and considered to have been law-fully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of July 3, 1961.


*Approved July 18, 1967.*

# Private Law 90–59

> AN ACT For the relief of Federico de la Cruz-Munoz.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Federico de la Cruz-Munoz shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of March 28, 1960.


*Approved August 4, 1967.*

# Private Law 90–119

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Francisco Montes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Francisco Montes shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of November 25, 1961.


*Approved October 31, 1967.*

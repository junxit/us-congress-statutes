# Private Law 90–177

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Jose A. Rico Fernandez.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act Doctor Jose A. Rico Fernandez shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the, United States for permanent residence as of August 26, 1961.


*Approved December 15, 1967.*

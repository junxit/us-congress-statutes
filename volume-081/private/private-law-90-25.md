# Private Law 90–25

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Ubaldo Gregorio Catasus-Rodriguez.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Ubaldo Gregorio Catasus-Rodriguez shall lie held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of June 12, 1961.


*Approved July 7, 1967.*

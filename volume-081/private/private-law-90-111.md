# Private Law 90–111

> AN ACT For the relief of Rumiko Samanski.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Kumiko Samanski may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b) (1) (F) of such Act, subject to the proviso to such section, and a petition may be filed in behalf of said Kumiko Samanski by Sergeant and Mrs. Ronald James Samanski, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204(a) of such Act.


*Approved October 31, 1967.*

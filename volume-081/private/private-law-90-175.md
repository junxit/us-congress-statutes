# Private Law 90–175

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Raul Gustavo Fors Docal.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Raul Gustavo Fors Docal shall be. held and considered to be lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of September 10, 1961.


*Approved December 15, 1967.*

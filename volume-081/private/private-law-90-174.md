# Private Law 90–174

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Virgilio A. Ganganelli Valle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Virgilio A. Ganganelli Valle shall be held and considered to be lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of April 9, 1962.


*Approved December 15, 1967.*

# Private Law 90–197

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Jose Rafael Montalvo y Urrutibeascoa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Jose Rafael Montalvo y Urrutibeascoa shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of April 29, 1968.


*Approved December 29, 1967.*

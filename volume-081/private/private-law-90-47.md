# Private Law 90–47

> AN ACT For the relief of Rosa Agostino.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, as amended, Rosa Agostino may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of the Act upon approval of a petition filed in her behalf by Katherine Ferrier, a citizen of the United States pursuant to section 204 of the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved July 27, 1967.*

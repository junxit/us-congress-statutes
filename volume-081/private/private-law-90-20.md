# Private Law 90–20

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Alfredo Reboredo-Newhall

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Alfredo Reboredo-Newhall shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of January 1, 1962.


*Approved July 3, 1967.*

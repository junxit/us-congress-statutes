# Private Law 90–5

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Miguel Alberto Rojas-Machado.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Miguel Alberto Rojas-Machado shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of November 29, 1960.


*Approved June 14, 1967.*

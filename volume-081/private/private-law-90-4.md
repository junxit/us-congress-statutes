# Private Law 90–4

> AN ACT To remove a cloud on the title of certain real property in the State of Oregon owned by John Johnson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purpose of removing a cloud on the title to certain land, the United States hereby quitclaims to John Johnson, of Milo, Oregon, all right, title, or interest in and to the southwest quarter of the northeast quarter of section 7, township 30 south, range 2 west, Willamette meridian, Oregon.


*Approved June 5, 1967.*

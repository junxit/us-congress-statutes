# Private Law 90–114

> AN ACT For the relief of Antonio Luis Navarro.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Antonio Luis Navarro shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of November 22, 1961, and to have complied with the requirements of section 316 of that Act as they relate to residence and physical presence.


*Approved October 31, 1967.*

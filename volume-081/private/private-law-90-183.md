# Private Law 90–183

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Josefina Esther Kouri-Barreto de Pelleya.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Josefina Esther Kouri-Barreto de Pelleya shall lie held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of April 14, 1961.


*Approved December 15, 1967.*

# Private Law 90–137

> AN ACT For the relief of Mary Bernadette Linehan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of paragraph (7) of section 301(a) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, John Guiney Linehan, a citizen of the United States, shall be held and considered to have resided in and to have been physically present in the United States, prior to the birth of his natural child, Mary Bernadette Linehan, for a period of five years after he had attained the age of fourteen years.


*Approved October 31, 1967.*

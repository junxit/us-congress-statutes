# Private Law 90–42

> AN ACT For the relief of Maria Jordan Ferrando.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, as amended, Maria Jordan Ferrando shall be held and considered to be the parent of Mrs. Victoria Trabue, a citizen of the United States, within the meaning of section 201 (b) of the said Act.


*Approved July 20, 1967.*

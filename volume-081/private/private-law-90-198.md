# Private Law 90–198

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Jorge Rolando Guerra-Reyes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Jorge Rolando Guerra-Reyes shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of July 20, 1962.


*Approved December 29, 1967.*

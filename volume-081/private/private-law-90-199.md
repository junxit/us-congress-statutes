# Private Law 90–199

> AN ACT For the relief of Christopher Nicholas Rushton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Christopher Nicholas Rushton shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of July 1, 1947, upon payment of the required head tax.


*Approved December 29, 1967.*

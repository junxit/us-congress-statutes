# Private Law 90–54

> AN ACT For the relief of Giovanni and Francesco Urga-Ferraro.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Giovanni and Francesco Urga-Ferraro may be classified as children within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of the Act, and petitions filed in their behalf by Miss Josephine Bonomo, a citizen of the United States, may be approved pursuant to section 204 of the Act.


*Approved August 4, 1967.*

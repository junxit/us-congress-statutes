# Private Law 90–196

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Octavio Suarez-Murias.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Octavio Suarez-Murias shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of May 29, 1961.


*Approved December 29, 1967.*

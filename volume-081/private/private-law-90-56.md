# Private Law 90–56

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Guillermo Fresco De Jongh.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Guillermo Fresco De Jongh shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of June 18, 1961.


*Approved August 4, 1967.*

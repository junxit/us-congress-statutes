# Private Law 90–149

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Pedro Pina y Gil.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Pedro Pina y Gil shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of March 2, 1962.


*Approved November 18, 1967.*

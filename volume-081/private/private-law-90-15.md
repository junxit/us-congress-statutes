# Private Law 90–15

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Rafael Jacinto Nobo y Pividal (Rafael Nobo).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Rafael Jacinto Nobo y Pividal (Rafael Nobo) shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of June 28, 1961.


*Approved June 14, 1967.*

# Private Law 90–168

> AN ACT For the relief of Isaac Chervony, doctor of medicine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Isaac Chervony, doctor of medicine, shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of September 13, 1962.


*Approved December 15, 1967.*

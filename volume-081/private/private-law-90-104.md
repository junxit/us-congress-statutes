# Private Law 90–104

> AN ACT For the relief of Jan Drobot.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Jan Drobot, who was lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence on June 27, 1960, shall be held and considered not to be within the classes of persons whose naturalization is prohibited by the provisions of section 313 of the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved October 27, 1967.*

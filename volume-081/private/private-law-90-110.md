# Private Law 90–110

> AN ACT For the relief of Sabiene Elizabeth DeVore.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 301(a)(7) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Robert William DeVore, a citizen of the United States, shall be held and considered to have been physically present in the United States, prior to the birth of his daughter, Sabiene Elizabeth DeVore, for a period of five years after he attained the age of fourteen years.


*Approved October 31, 1967.*

# Private Law 90–53

> AN ACT For the relief of John Joseph Shea.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, John Joseph Shea may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101 (b) (1) (F) of the Act, upon approval of a petition filed in his behalf by Mr. and Mrs. John J. Shea, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of the Act. Section 204(c) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, relating to the number of petitions which may be approved, shall be inapplicable in this case.


*Approved August 4, 1967.*

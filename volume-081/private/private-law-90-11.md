# Private Law 90–11

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Ramon Baez Hernandez.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Ramon Baez Hernandez shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of April 30, 1961.


*Approved June 14, 1967.*

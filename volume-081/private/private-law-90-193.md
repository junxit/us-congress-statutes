# Private Law 90–193

> AN ACT To confer United States citizenship posthumously upon Private First Class Alfred Sevenski.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Private First Class Alfred Sevenski, a native of Germany, who served honorably in the United States Army from May 11, 1964, until his death on November 5, 1966, shall be held and considered to have been a citizen of the United States at the time of his death.


*Approved December 29, 1967.*

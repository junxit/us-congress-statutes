# Private Law 90–105

> AN ACT For the relief of Mi Soon Oh.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, section 204(c), relating to the number or petitions which may be approved in behalf of orphans, shall be inapplicable in the case of a petition tiled in behalf of Mi Soon Oh by Mr. and Mrs. Anthony Caucutt, citizens of the United States.


*Approved October 31, 1967.*

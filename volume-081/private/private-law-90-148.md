# Private Law 90–148

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Rafael de la Portilia Lavastida.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Rafael de la Portilia Lavastida shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of August 13, 1960.


*Approved November 17, 1967.*

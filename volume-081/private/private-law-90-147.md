# Private Law 90–147

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Rolando Pozo y Jimenez.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Rolando Pozo y Jimenez shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of June 24, 1959.


*Approved November 17, 1967.*

# Private Law 90–16

> AN ACT For the relief of Doctor Fernando O. Garcia-Hernandez.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Doctor Fernando O. Garcia-Hernandez shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of May 24, 1961.


*Approved June 14, 1967.*

# Private Law 90–26

> AN ACT For the relief of Chang-You Wu, doctor of medicine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Chang-You Wu, doctor of medicine, shall lie held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of February 16, 1956.


*Approved July 7, 1967.*

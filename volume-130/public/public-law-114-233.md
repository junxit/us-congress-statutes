# Public Law 114–233

> An Act To amend the Gullah/Geechee Cultural Heritage Act to extend the authorization for the Gullah/Geechee Cultural Heritage Corridor Commission.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. EXTENSION OF THE AUTHORIZATION FOR THE GULLAH/GEECHEE CULTURAL HERITAGE CORRIDOR COMMISSION.

Section 295D(d) of the Gullah/Geechee Cultural Heritage Act (Public Law 109–338; 120 Stat. 1833; 16 U.S.C. 461 note) is amended by striking “10 years” and inserting “15 years”.


*Approved October 7, 2016.*

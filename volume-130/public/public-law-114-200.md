# Public Law 114–200

> An Act To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 7802 37th Avenue in Jackson Heights, New York, as the “Jeanne and Jules Manford Post Office Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. JEANNE AND JULES MANFORD POST OFFICE BUILDING.

- **(a)** *Designation.—* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 7802 37th Avenue in Jackson Heights, New York, shall be known and designated as the “Jeanne and Jules Manford Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References.—* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Jeanne and Jules Manford Post Office Building”.


*Approved July 29, 2016.*

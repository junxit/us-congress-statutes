# Public Law 114–192

> An Act To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 15 Rochester Street, Bergen, New York, as the Barry G. Miller Post Office.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. BARRY G. MILLER POST OFFICE.

- **(a)** *Designation.—* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 15 Rochester Street, Bergen, New York, shall be known and designated as the “Barry G. Miller Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References.—* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Barry G. Miller Post Office”.


*Approved July 15, 2016.*

# Public Law 114–220

> An Act To designate the Department of Veterans Affairs community-based outpatient clinic in Laughlin, Nevada, as the “Master Chief Petty Officer Jesse Dean VA Clinic”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. MASTER CHIEF PETTY OFFICER JESSE DEAN VA CLINIC.

- **(a)** *Designation.—* The Department of Veterans Affairs community-based outpatient clinic in Laughlin, Nevada, shall after the date of the enactment of this Act be known and designated as the “Master Chief Petty Officer Jesse Dean VA Clinic”.
- **(b)** *References.—* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the community-based outpatient clinic referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Master Chief Petty Officer Jesse Dean VA Clinic”.


*Approved September 23, 2016.*

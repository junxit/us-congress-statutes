# Senate Concurrent Resolution 53

> ENROLLMENT CORRECTION—H.R. 5325

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That, in the enrollment of the bill H.R. 5325, the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall make the following correction to the title so as to read: “Making continuing appropriations for fiscal year 2017, and for other purposes.”.


*Agreed to September 28, 2016.*

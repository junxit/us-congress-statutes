# Chapter 61

> An Act to amend an act entitled “An act to provide for taking the eleventh and subsequent censuses,” approved March first, eighteen hundred and eighty-nine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section eighteen of an act entitled “An act to provide for taking the Eleventh and subsequent censuses,” approved March first, eighteen hundred and eighty-nine, be amended by adding to the last line of said section eighteen, after the words “and actual necessary traveling expenses.” the words “and a per diem allowance in lieu of subsistence of three dollars per day.”


*Approved, April 3, 1890.*

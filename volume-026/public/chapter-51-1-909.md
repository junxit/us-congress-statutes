# Chapter 909

> An act to establish a port of delivery at Sioux City, Iowa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the city of Sioux City, in the State of Iowa, shall be, and is hereby, constituted a port of delivery, annexed to and made a part of the collection district of New Orleans, and shall be subject to the same regulations and restrictions as other ports of delivery in the United States; and the privileges of the first and seventh sections of the act approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, entitled “An act to amend the statutes in relation to immediate transportation of dutiable goods, and for other purposes,” be, and the same are, extended to said port.

## § 2.

That there shall be appointed by the President a surveyor of customs for said port, who shall reside at said port, and receive the same compensation now provided, or which may hereafter be provided, by law for surveyors of the same grade.


*Approved, September 25, 1890.*

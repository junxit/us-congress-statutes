# Chapter 5

> An act to amend chapter one thousand and sixty-five of the acts of the first session of the Fiftieth Congress.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That chapter one thousand and sixty-five of the acts passed at the first session of the Fiftieth Congress be, and the same is hereby, amended as follows, viz: By inserting in line nineteen of said act, between the words “new” and “registering,” the words “or improved.”

## § 2.

That this act fake effect from the date of its passage.


*Approved, December 15, 1890.*

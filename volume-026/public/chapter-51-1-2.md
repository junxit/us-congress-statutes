# Chapter 2

> An Act to amend an act entitled “An act to provide for taking the Eleventh and subsequent censuses,” approved March first, eighteen hundred and eighty-nine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section six of an act entitled “An act to provide for taking the Eleventh and subsequent censuses,” approved March first, eighteen hundred and eighty-nine, be amended by striking out the words “five hundred” and inserting the words “one thousand,” so that the proviso will read: “Provided, That in the aggregate no supervisor snail be paid less than the sum of one thousand dollars.”


*Approved, January 23, 1890.*

# Chapter 211

> An Act to submit the location of the county seat of Shoshone County, Idaho Territory, to a vote of the people of said county.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the location of the county seat of Shoshone County, Idaho Territory, be submitted to a vote of the legal voters of said county at the next general election which shall be held in said county.

## § 2.

That such town as receives a majority of all the votes cast at said election, for the location of the county seat, shall be the county seat of said county until otherwise changed by law.

## § 3.

That said election for the location of the county seat shall be governed in all respects the same as all other general elections in Idaho, are governed


*Approved, May 15, 1890.*

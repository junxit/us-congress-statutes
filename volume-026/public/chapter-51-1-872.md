# Chapter 872

> An act giving the consent of the United States to the State of North Dakota to appropriate for the use of the State Agricultural College, as a site for that institution, section thirty-six township one hundred and forty, range forty-nine west, situate in the county of Cass, in said State.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the consent of the United States is hereby given to the State of North Dakota to appropriate for the use of the State Agricultural College, as a site for that institution, section thirty-six, township one hundred and forty, range forty-nine west, situate in the county of Cass, in said State, being a portion of the lands granted to said State for the purposes of common schools but no indemnity shall be allowed for this section.


*Approved, September 4, 1890.*

# Chapter 62

> An Act to extend to Tampa, Florida, the privilege of immediate transportation of unappraised merchandise.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of the first section of the act entitled “An act to amend the statutes in relation to immediate transportation of dutiable goods, and for other purposes,” approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, be. and the same are hereby, extended to the port of Tampa, Florida.


*Approved, April 3, 1890.*

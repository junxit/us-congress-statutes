# Chapter 195

> An Act to increase the limit of cost of the erection of a public building at Wilmington, Delaware.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the amount heretofore fixed as the limit of cost of site and for the erection of a public building at Wilmington, Delaware, be, and the same is hereby, increased to two hundred and fifty thousand dollars, and that sum is hereby fixed as the limit of cost of site and for the erection of said building.


*Approved, May 5, 1890.*

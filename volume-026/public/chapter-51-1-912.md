# Chapter 912

> An act to amend an act approved March third, eighteen hundred and eighty-seven, entitled “An act to amend sections twenty-five hundred and thirty-three and twenty-five hundred and thirty-four of the Revised Statutes, and making Hartford, in the State of Connecticut, a port of entry, in place of Middletown.”

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section one of the act approved March third, eighteen hundred and eighty-seven, entitled “An act to amend sections twenty-five hundred and thirty-three and twenty-five hundred and thirty-four of the Revised Statutes, and making Hartford, in the State of Connecticut, a port of entry, in place of Middletown.” be amended so as to include within the district of Hartford, Connecticut, the towns of Long Meadow, Agawam, Springfield, and West Springfield, Massachusetts, in which Springfield shall be a port of delivery with the privileges of the seventh section of the act of June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, entitled “An act to amend the statutes in relation to immediate transportation of dutiable goods, and for other purposes;” and there shall be appointed a surveyor of customs for said port, to reside at said port, who shall receive a salary to be determined in amount by the Secretary of the Treasury, not exceeding one thousand dollars per annum.


*Approved, September 25, 1890.*

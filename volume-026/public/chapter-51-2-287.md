# Chapter 287

> An act fixing the salaries of the several judges of the United States district courts at five thousand dollars per annum.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the salaries of the several judges of the district courts of the United States shall hereafter be at the rate of five thousand dollars per annum.


*Approved, February 24, 1891.*

# Chapter 805

> An Act authorizing the city of Charleston, South Carolina, to open Concord street through the grounds of the United States in that city.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the city of Charleston, South Carolina, be, and it is hereby, authorized to open Concord street through the grounds of the United States in that city upon such terms and conditions as the Secretary of the Treasury may prescribe.


*Approved, August 19, 1890.*

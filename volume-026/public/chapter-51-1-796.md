# Chapter 796

> An Act to amend an act approved August sixth, eighteen hundred and eighty-eight, authorizing the construction of bridges by the Houston, Central Arkansas and Northern Railway Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act approved August sixth, eighteen hundred and eighty-eight, entitled “An Act authorizing the Houston, Central Arkansas and Northern Railway Company to construct and maintain bridges across Bayou Bartholomew and across Ouachita, Red, Little and Sabine Rivers in Louisiana,” be and is hereby amended so that the time within which the actual construction of said bridges may be commenced is hereby extended for the period of one year from the date of the approval of this Act.


*Approved, August 18, 1890.*

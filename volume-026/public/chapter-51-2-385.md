# Chapter 385

> An act to prohibit the granting of liquor licenses within one mile of the Soldiers’ Home.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That on and after the passage of this act no license for the sale of intoxicating liquor at any place within one mile of the Soldiers’ Home property in the District of Columbia shall be granted.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

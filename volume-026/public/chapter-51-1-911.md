# Chapter 911

> An act to provide for the establishment of a port of delivery at Rock Island, Illinois.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Rock Island, in the State of Illinois, be, and hereby is, established as a port of delivery, in the customs collection district of New Orleans, and that there shall be appointed at said port a surveyor of customs, with compensation of three hundred and fifty dollars per annum and the usual fees, for the payment of which compensation an appropriation is hereby made out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, September 25, 1890.*

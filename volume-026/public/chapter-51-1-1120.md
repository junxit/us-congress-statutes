# Chapter 1120

> An act to provide an American register for the bark Campanero, of Baltimore, Maryland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and House, of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the commissioner of Navigation is hereby authorized and directed to cause the bark Campanero, owned and rebuilt at the port of Baltimore, Maryland, by John M. Bandel and Sons, citizens of the United States, to be registered as a vessel of the United States.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

# Chapter 217

> An Act to ratify an act entitled “An act to provide for a wagon road between Mount Idaho, in Idaho County, and Little Salmon Meadows, in Washington County,” in Idaho Territory.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the act of the legislative assembly of the Territory of Idaho, passed February second, eighteen hundred and eighty-nine, entitled, “An act to provide for a wagon road between Mount Idaho, in Idaho County, and Little Salmon Meadows, in Washington County,” be, and the same is hereby, ratified and confirmed. The time for the completion of said road shall be extended one year.


*Approved, May 16, 1890.*

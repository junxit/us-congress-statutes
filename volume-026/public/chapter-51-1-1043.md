# Chapter 1043

> An act to authorize the President to restore Tenedor Ten Eyck to his former rank in the Army, and to place him on the retired list of Army officers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and is authorized and empowered to restore to his former rank in the Army and place upon the retired list of Army officers Tenedor Ten Eyck, late a captain in the Eighteenth United States Infantry: Provided, That the said Tenedor Ten Eyck shall be entitled to no pay and allowances as an Army officer for the time he may have been out of the military service, but shall, from the date of his restoration, be entitled to the pay and allowances pertaining to an officer of his rank on the retired list: Provided further, That he shall receive no pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

# Chapter 1245

> An act to authorize the Commissioners of the District of Columbia to annul and cancel the subdivision of part of square one hundred and twelve, known as Cooke Park.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioners of the District of Columbia be, and they are hereby, authorized to annul and cancel the subdivision of part of square numbered one hundred and twelve in Georgetown, known as Cooke Park, made by A. M. Bell September twenty-sixth, eighteen hundred and eighty-five, and recorded in the office of the surveyor of said District in book A. R. S., page one hundred and fifty-seven: Provided, That nil the owners whose property in said subdivision abuts on the avenue shown thereon shall petition therefor.


*Approved, October 1, 1890.*

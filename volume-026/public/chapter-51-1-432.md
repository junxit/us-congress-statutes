# Chapter 432

> An Act to provide for the exportation of fermented liquor in bond without payment of internal-revenue tax.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the first day of January, eighteen hundred and ninety-one, fermented liquor may be removed from the place of manufacture, or storage, for export to a foreign country, without payment of tax, in such packages and under such regulations, and upon the giving of such notices, entries, bonds, and other security, as the Commissioner of Internal Revenue with the approval of the Secretary of the Treasury may from time to time prescribe; and no drawback of tax shall be allowed on fermented liquor exported on and after the first day of January, eighteen hundred and ninety-one, unless entered for exportation prior to such date.


*Approved, June 18, 1890.*

# Chapter 412

> An Act for the registry or enrollment of the barges Herdis and Agostino C.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of Navigation is hereby authorized and directed to admit to registry, as barge of the United States, the following-named foreign-built vessels, wrecked in American waters, purchased, repaired, and owned by C. C. Pinckney, junior, of Charleston, South Carolina, a citizen of the United States, namely, the Herdis and the Agostino C.


*Approved, June 10, 1890.*

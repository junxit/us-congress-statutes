# Chapter 722

> An Act granting certain land to Miles City, Montana, for use as a public park.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all that portion of the Fort Keogh military reservation lying and being east of Tongue River, in the State of Montana, be, and is hereby, withdrawn from military control, and granted to the city of Miles City, Montana, for the perpetual use and benefit of said Miles City and the inhabitants thereof as a public park, subject to the right hereby reserved by the United States to use as much of said granted land as may be necessary in operating a ferry or constructing a bridge across Tongue River and approaches to such ferry or bridge.


*Approved, July 30, 1890.*

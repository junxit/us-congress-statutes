# Chapter 550

> An act to revise the wages of certain employees in the Government Printing Office.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pay of all the employees of the Government Printing Office engaged on night work (between the hours of five o’clock postmeridian and eight o’clock antemeridian) shall be twenty per centum in addition to the amount paid for day labor.

## § 2.

That all acts and parts of acts conflicting herewith are hereby repealed.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

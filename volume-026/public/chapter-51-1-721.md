# Chapter 721

> An Act to amend paragraph three of section forty-four hundred and fourteen of the Revised Statutes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph three of section forty-four hundred and fourteen of the Revised Statutes of the United States be amended as follows: “Strike out the word “Galena” in said paragraph three and insert the word “Dubuque.”


*Approved, July 26, 1890.*

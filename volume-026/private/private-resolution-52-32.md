# Private Resolution 32

> Joint Resolution authorizing J. B. Bernadou, United States Navy, to accept two vases presented to him by the Government of Japan.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Ensign J. B. Bernadou, United States Navy, be, and hereby is, authorized to receive two flower vases presented to him by the Government of Japan in (1325) recognition of services rendered by him to Japanese citizens at an outbreak at Seoul, Korea, in December, eighteen hundred and eighty-four.


*Approved, July 2, 1890.*

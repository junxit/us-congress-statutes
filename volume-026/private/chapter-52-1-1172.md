# Chapter 1172

> An Act granting a pension to John Grace.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House, of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Grace, father of Edward Grace, late a sergeant in Company A, Ninety-first Regiment New York Volunteers.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

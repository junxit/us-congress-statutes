# Chapter 370

> An Act granting a pension to Ira Agan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to put the name of Ira Agan, late a corporal Company C, Second Regiment United States Dragoons, on the pension-rolls, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 27, 1891.*

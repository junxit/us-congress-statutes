# Chapter 720

> An Act granting a pension to Peter William Frederick.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Peter William Frederick, private Company A, Fifth Regiment United States Infantry, for extraordinary services rendered in the years eighteen hundred and fifty seven and eighteen hundred and fifty eight, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

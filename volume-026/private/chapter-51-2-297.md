# Chapter 297

> An Act for the relief of William G. Tidwell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That William G. Tidwell be, and he is hereby, relieved from the charge of desertion from Company E. Seventh Illinois Cavalry Volunteers, and the War Department of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to give said William G. Tidwell an honorable discharge from said company and regiment.


*Approved, February 24, 1891.*

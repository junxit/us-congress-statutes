# Chapter 469

> An Act granting a pension to Ann E. Tew.

*Be it enacted by the, Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Ann E. Tew, widow of George W. Tew, late colonel of the Fifth Rhode Island Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

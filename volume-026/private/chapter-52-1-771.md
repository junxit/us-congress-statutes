# Chapter 771

> An Act to grant a pension to Martin Brachall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Martin Brachall, who was a private in Captain J. M. Gillespie’s company, Illinois Militia, in the Black Hawk war. and pay him a pension of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

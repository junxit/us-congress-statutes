# Chapter 895

> An Act granting a pension to Charles A. Norton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll the name of Charles A. Norton, late of Company K, First Regiment of Maine Cavalry and Company K, Seventh New Hampshire Infantry Volunteers, at the rate of fifty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, September 4, 1890.*

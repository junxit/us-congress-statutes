# Chapter 227

> An Act granting a pension to James T. Irwin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of James T. Irwin, late of Company K, Eleventh Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers.


*Approved, May 19, 1890.*

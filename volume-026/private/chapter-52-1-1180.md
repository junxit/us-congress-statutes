# Chapter 1180

> An Act for the relief of Alexander Callison.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Alexander Callison, who enlisted for the Mexican war and did service on the frontier as a soldier in Company B, Powell’s Battalion, Missouri Mounted Infantry, and that said Alexander Callison be paid a pension of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

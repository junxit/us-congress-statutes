# Chapter 691

> An Act granting a pension to John Bridenback, late private Company L, Fourth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Cavalry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-rolls the name of John Bridenback, late private of Company L, Fourth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Cavalry, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, July 11, 1890.*

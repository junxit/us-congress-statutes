# Chapter 266

> An Act for the relief of John Dehart.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of John Dehart, the invalid son of David Dehart, late a private of Company G, Seventy-ninth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and pay said son a pension of eighteen dollars per month for and during his natural life, said pension to be paid to the conservator or legally appointed guardian of said John Dehart.


*Approved, May 21, 1890.*

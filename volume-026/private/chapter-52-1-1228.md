# Chapter 1228

> An Act granting increase of pension to Katherine W. Howell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, at the rate of fifty dollars per month, in lieu of her present pension of thirty dollars per month, the name of Katherine W. Howell, widow of the. late Brigadier-General Joshua B. Howell, commander of the Third Division, Tenth Corps, United States Army.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

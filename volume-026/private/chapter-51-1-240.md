# Chapter 240

> An Act granting a pension to Clara M. Owen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Clara M. Owen, widow of William H. Owen, late of Company B, Twentieth Maine Volunteers.


*Approved, May 21, 1890.*

# Chapter 206

> An Act granting a pension to George W. Bryant.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of George W. Bryant, late a corporal in Company H, Eighth New Hampshire Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month on and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 14, 1891.*

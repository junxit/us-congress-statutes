# Chapter 582

> An Act granting a pension to Augustus W. Eaton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Augustus W. Eaton, of Orange Park, Florida, late a member of Company H, Fourth Regiment of Maine Volunteers.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

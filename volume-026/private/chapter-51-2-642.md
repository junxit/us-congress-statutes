# Chapter 642

> An Act for the relief of Frank Schader.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to .remove the charge of desertion from the name of Frank Schader, late of Company G, Ninety-first New York Volunteers, and grant him an honorable discharge under date of March twentieth, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, and to pay him all pay, bounty, and allowances as may be due him without reference to the charge of desertion.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

# Chapter 273

> An Act granting a pension to Henry Bloomfield.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to put the name of Henry Bloomfield, late of Company G, Ninety-second New York Infantry, on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws.


*Approved, May 22, 1890.*

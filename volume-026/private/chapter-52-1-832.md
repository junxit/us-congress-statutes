# Chapter 832

> An Act granting a pension to Joseph H. Scoopmire.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House, of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Joseph H. Scoopmire, Company H. Sixth Regiment Indiana Volunteers, at the rate of thirty-six dollars per month.


*Approved, August 29, 1890.*

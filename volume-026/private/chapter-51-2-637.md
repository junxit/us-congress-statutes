# Chapter 637

> An Act for the relief of Charles W. Lambert.

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to amend the military record of Charles W. Lambert so as to show him honorably discharged from Company F, one hundred and fifty-second New York Volunteers.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

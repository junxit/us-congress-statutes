# Chapter 591

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Stewart Herbert.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, at the rate of forty dollars per month, the name of Stewart Herbert, late a private in Company A, Seventh Regiment Maryland Volunteer Infantry, in lieu of the pension now received by him.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

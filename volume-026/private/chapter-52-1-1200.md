# Chapter 1200

> An Act granting a pension to Cynthia A. Gudgell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Cynthia A. Gudgell, at the rate of twelve dollars a month.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

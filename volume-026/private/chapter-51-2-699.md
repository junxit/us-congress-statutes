# Chapter 699

> An Act for the relief of Ellen J. Wharton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the act entitled “An act to restore pensions in certain cases.” approved June ninth, eighteen hundred and eighty, shall be construed so as to include within its provisions Ellen J. Wharton, of Louisville Kentucky.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

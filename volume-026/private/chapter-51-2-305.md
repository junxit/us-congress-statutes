# Chapter 305

> An Act for the relief of Nancy Potter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Nancy Potter, widow of Uriah Potter, late surgeon of the board of enrollment for the eighteenth Congressional district of New York, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 25, 1891.*

# Chapter 282

> An Act granting a pension to Irene D. Swan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll of the United States the name of Irene D. Swan, widow of George W. Swan, late private in Company A, First California Infantry Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, May 22, 1890.*

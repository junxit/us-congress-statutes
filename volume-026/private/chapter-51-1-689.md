# Chapter 689

> An Act granting a pension to Annie E. Dixon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll the name of Annie E. Dixon, widow of Henry T. Dixon, ate a major and assistant paymaster in the volunteer service of the United States, and to pay Tier a pension of twenty-five dollars per month from the passage of this act, to continue during her widow-hood.


*Approved, July 11, 1890.*

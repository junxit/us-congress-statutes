# Chapter 777

> An Act to increase the pension of Joseph Mason, of Dallas City, Illinois.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll the name of Joseph Mason, who was a soldier in Company E, of the Sixth United States Infantry in the war with Mexico, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu or the pension now received by him.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

# Chapter 1087

> An Act for the relief of Allen Morris.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interim be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Allen Morris, late a member of Company E, Second Regiment Missouri Infantry Volunteers in the war with Mexico, from eight to twenty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he now receives.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

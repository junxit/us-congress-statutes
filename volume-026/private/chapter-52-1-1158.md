# Chapter 1158

> An Act granting a pension to Reverend Thomas James.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll of the United States, Reverend Thomas James, of Rochester, New York, late a member of the Military Police attached to the Commissary Department and Freedman’s Bureau in the Department of Kentucky, and pay him at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

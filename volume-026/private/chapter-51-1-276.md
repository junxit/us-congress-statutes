# Chapter 276

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs Fanny W. Mudgett, dependent mother.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs Fanny W. Mudgett, dependent mother of Edward P. Adams, late private Company H, nineteenth Wisconsin Infantry.


*Approved, May 22, 1890.*

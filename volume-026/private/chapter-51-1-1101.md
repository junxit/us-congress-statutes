# Chapter 1101

> An Act granting a pension to Elizabeth Abell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject-to the provisions and limitations or the pension laws, the name of Elizabeth Abell, of Jackson County, Indiana, widow of George W. Abell, of the Twenty-second Regiment Indiana Volunteers.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

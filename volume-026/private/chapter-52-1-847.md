# Chapter 847

> An Act for the relief of Mary B. Le Roy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the passage of this act there be paid, out of the naval pension fund, to Mary B. Le Roy, widow of the late Rear-Admiral William E. Le Roy, United States Navy, the sum of fifty dollars per month during her widowhood, that sum to be in lieu of her present pension.


*Approved, August 30, 1890.*

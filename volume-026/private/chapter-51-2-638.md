# Chapter 638

> An Act for the relief of Luman B. Palmeter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to remove the charge of desertion standing on record against Luman B. Palmeter, lat a private in Company B, Seventy-eighth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and issue to him an honorable discharge as of date February thirteenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-four.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

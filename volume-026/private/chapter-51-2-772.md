# Chapter 772

> An Act to grant pensions to W. B. Cloer late private in Company L. D. Storm’s Arkansas Militia

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House, of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of W. B. Cloer, late private Company L. D. Storm’s company Arkansas Militia, on the pension rolls, subject to the conditions and restrictions of the pension laws of the United States


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

# Chapter 542

> An Act for the relief of Harrison Tryon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Harrison Tryon, late a member of Company F, Twenty-fifth Indiana Volunteers.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

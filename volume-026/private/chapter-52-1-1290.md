# Chapter 1290

> An Act granting a pension to Nancy J. Dorlos.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is authorized and directed to place upon the invalid pension-roll, at twelve dollars a month, the name of Nancy J. Dorios, widow of George P. Dorios, late of Company C, One hundred and seventh Ohio Volunteers.


*Approved, October 1, 1890.*

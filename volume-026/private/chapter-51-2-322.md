# Chapter 322

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs Mary B. Floyd

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby instructed to place on the pension-rolls Mrs. Mary B. Floyd, of Newton County, Georgia, widow of John B. Floyd, late a captain of a company of Georgia troops, in the Creek Indian war of eighteen hundred and thirty-six, and pay her a pension, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 25, 1891.*

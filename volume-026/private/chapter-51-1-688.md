# Chapter 688

> An Act granting a pension to John Bechen, senior

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, at the rate of seventy-two dollars per month, the name of John Bechen, senior, late a private in Company I, Eighth Iowa Volunteers.


*Approved, July 11, 1890.*

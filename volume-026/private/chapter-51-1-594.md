# Chapter 594

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Owen C. Powell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll the name of Owen C. Powell, late a private soldier in company A. commanded by Captain R. A. Gillespie, in the First Regiment of Texas Volunteers in the war with Mexico, with an increase of pension to the amount of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

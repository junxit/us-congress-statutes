# Chapter 143

> An Act granting a pension to Elizalieth Burnett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elizabeth Burnett, of Gallatin County, Illinois, late a nurse in the Fifty-sixth Regiment Illinois Volunteers during the late rebellion, at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 12, 1891.*

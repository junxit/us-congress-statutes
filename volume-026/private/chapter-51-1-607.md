# Chapter 607

> An Act to increase the pension of Andrew Langton, late of Company E, Twenty-seventh Indiana Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension now being paid to Andrew Langton, late of Company E, Twenty-seventh Indiana Volunteer Infantry, be increased to fifty dollars per month, and that the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, instructed to so place the name of said Langton on the pension-rolls


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

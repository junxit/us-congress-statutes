# Chapter 596

> An Act granting an increased pension to Adaline Whelan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, to increase the pension of Adaline Whelan, widow of William Whelan, late Surgeon-General of the Navy of the United States, to fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

# Chapter 97

> An Act for the relief of Amos Gilbert.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place upon the pension-rolls the name of Amos Gilbert, of New Haven, Connecticut, who served as an enlisted seaman in the Navy of the United States, and was discharged on the thirteenth day of July, eighteen hundred and forty-two, and who, while in the service, contracted disabilities from which he has never recovered, and to pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars a month.


*Approved, January 24, 1891.*

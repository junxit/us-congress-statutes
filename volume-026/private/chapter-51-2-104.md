# Chapter 104

> An Act granting a pension to Frederick Slawson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Frederick Slawson, dependent father of Charles B. Slawson, late captain of Company B, Twenty-eighth Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, January 26, 1891.*

# Chapter 697

> An Act granting a pension to Jane Wood, widow of Clayborne Wood, late of Company C, Thirty-third Ohio Infantry Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jane Wood, widow of Clayborne Wood, deceased, Company C, Thirty-third Ohio Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, July 11, 1890.*

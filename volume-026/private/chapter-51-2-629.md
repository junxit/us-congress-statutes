# Chapter 629

> An Act for the relief of Mary B. Clayton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, at the rate of thirty dollars per month, the name of Mary B. Clayton, widow of the late Major Henry Clayton, late paymaster United States Army, and pay her a pension from and after the passage of this act at the rate above named, the same to be in lieu of the pension now paid her.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

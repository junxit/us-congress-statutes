# Chapter 120

> An Act granting a pension to Ann Ford.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to place oil the pension-rolls, the name of Ann Ford, widow of John Ford, late of Company F, Fourteenth Regiment New York Heavy Artillery, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, April 21, 1890.*

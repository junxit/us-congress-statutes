# Chapter 346

> An Act to grant a pension to Lucinda A. Clark.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-rolls, subject to the rules and limitations of the pension laws; the name of Lucinda A. Clark, widow of George M. Clark, lite lieutenant of Company E, Sixteenth Vermont Volunteers.


*Approved, May 24, 1890.*

# Chapter 692

> An Act for the relief of Thomas E. Breckenridge, late Company A, California Battalion, Mexican war.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Thomas E. Breckenridge, late of Company A, California Battalion, under command of Colonel John C. Fremont, in the war with Mexico, from the sum of eight dollars per month to the sum of thirty dollars per month, to take effect from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

# Chapter 393

> An Act granting a pension to William Clawson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William Clawson, late a member of Company I, Fourth Regiment of Iowa Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, June 3, 1890.*

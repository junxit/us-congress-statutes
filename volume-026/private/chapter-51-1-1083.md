# Chapter 1083

> An Act to increase the pension of John N. Harris.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay John N. Harris, of Gurdon Arkansas, late a private in Company H, commanded by Captain Crump, in the First Mississippi Regiment, commanded by Jefferson Davis, in the war with Mexico, a pension at the rate of twenty dollars a month, in lieu of that which he now receives, to take effect from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

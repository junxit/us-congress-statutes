# Chapter 731

> An Act to increase the pension of John Taaffe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembler,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he hereby is, authorized and directed to increase the pension of John Taaffe. late a member of Company F, Sixth Kentucky Infantry, to twenty-five dollars a month.


*Approved, August 13, 1890.*

# Chapter 148

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Mary Hogan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, the name of Mrs. Mary Hogan, widow of Thomas Hogan, late of Battery D, Third United States Artillery, and allow her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 12, 1891.*

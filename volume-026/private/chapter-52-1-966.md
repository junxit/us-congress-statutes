# Chapter 966

> An Act granting a pension to Sallie E. Rickards.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, directed and authorized to place upon the pension-rolls, subject to the restrictions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sallie E. Rickards, widow of Joseph H. Rickards, late Captain Company D, Twelfth Missouri Volunteer Cavalry.


*Approved, September 26, 1890.*

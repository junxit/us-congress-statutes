# Chapter 352

> An Act increasing pension of Sophia Schimmelfennig, widow of Alexander Schimmelfennig, late brigadier-general and major-general by brevet.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension of Sophia Schimmelfennig, widow of Alexander Schimmelfennig, deceased, late brigadier-general of the Army of the United States and major-general by brevet, be, and the same is hereby, increased to fifty dollars a month.


*Approved, May 24, 1890.*

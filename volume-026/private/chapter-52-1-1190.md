# Chapter 1190

> An Act granting a pension to James Malin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of James Malin, late private in Captain Roundtree’s provisional militia of Missouri.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

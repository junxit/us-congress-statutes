# Chapter 193

> An Act to increase the pension of Henry H. Penrod.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to increase the pension now paid to Henry H. Penrod, late a private of Company A, Forty-fifth Regiment of Kentucky Mounted Infantry, on pension certificate numbered two hundred and forty-five thousand one hundred and seventy-five, to twenty-five dollars per month, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, May 3, 1890.*

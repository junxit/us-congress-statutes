# Chapter 531

> An Act for the relief of Mrs. Olive Padgett.

*Be it enacted by the. Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Mrs. Olive Padgett, widow of the late J. William Padgett, of Company D, First Regiment Potomac Home Brigade, Maryland Cavalry, on the pension-roll of the United States, at the rate of twelve dollars per month


*Approved, June 21, 1890.*

# Chapter 783

> An Act granting a pension to Elizabeth M. Riley

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United. States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-rolls, subject to the conditions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elizabeth M. Riley, widow of Larkin M. Riley late lieutenant of Company G, Thirty-first Regiment Illinois Volunteers, at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

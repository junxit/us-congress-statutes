# Chapter 37

> An Act for the relief of James B. Guthrie.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of James B. Guthrie, of Paris. Henry County, Tennessee, late a private of Captain James T. Dunlap’s company of Tennessee troops in the Indian war of eighteen hundred and thirty-six, and pay to him a pension of twenty dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, December 26, 1890.*

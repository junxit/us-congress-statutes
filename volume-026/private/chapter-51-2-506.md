# Chapter 506

> An Act for the relief of Henry L. Morey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to amend the record of Captain Henry L. Morey, Company G, Seventy-fifth Ohio Volunteers, to show him continuously in service as captain of said company from March first, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, date of his muster in, until December twenty-second, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, the date of his muster out.


*Approved, March 2, 1891.*

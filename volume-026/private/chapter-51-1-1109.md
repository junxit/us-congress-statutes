# Chapter 1109

> An Act granting a pension to Mary L. Nash.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay Mary L. Nash, dependent sister of Samuel L. Nash, Company H, Thirty-second Massachusetts Regiment, and Andrew J. Nasif and Francis J. Nash, both of Company B. Thirty-fifth Massachusetts Regiment, a pension of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

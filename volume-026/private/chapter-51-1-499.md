# Chapter 499

> An Act for the relief of Johanna Eckle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Johanna Eckle, widow of Joseph Eckle, late a private Company E, Second Regiment of Maryland Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, June 21, 1890.*

# Chapter 409

> An Act granting a pension to Philip H. Emmert.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Philip H. Emmert, late a private in Company D, Twenty-third Regiment of Kentucky Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

# Chapter 753

> An Act granting a pension to William M, Nourse, of Knoxville, Tennessee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place William M. Nourse on the pension-rolls, subject to the conditions and limitations of the general pension laws; this pension being granted because of gun-shot wound and other injuries received while attached to and in actual service with the Eighteenth Kentucky Infantry, on the seventeenth day of July, eighteen hundred and. sixty-two.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

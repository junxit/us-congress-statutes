# Chapter 1149

> An Act granting an increase of pension to William B. McCreery.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be and he is hereby, directed and authorized to place on the pension-roll the name of William B. McCreery. late Colonel Twenty-first. Michigan Infantry, and pay him a pension of sixty dollars per month, in lieu of the sum he is now receiving.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

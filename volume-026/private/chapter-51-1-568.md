# Chapter 568

> An Act granting a pension to Mary Mayberry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Mary Mayberry, foster mother of George L Brown, late a private in Company E. Eighth Regiment of Maine Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

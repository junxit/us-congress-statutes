# Chapter 789

> An Act to increase the pension of Thomas H. Isbell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior Ire, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to pay to Thomas H. Isbell, a veteran of the Mexican war, who has become totally blind, a monthly pension of thirty dollars, in lieu of the amount now paid him.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

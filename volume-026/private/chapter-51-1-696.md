# Chapter 696

> An Act granting a pension to Eugenia B. Tabler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Eugenia B. Tabler, of Washington, District of Columbia, late a volunteer nurse, in the hospitals of Washing-ton during the war of the rebellion, and pay her a pension of twelve dollars per month from the date of the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 11, 1890.*

# Chapter 279

> An Act granting a pension to Mary B. Stidger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws the name of Mary B. Stidger,widow of William C. Stidger, late first lieutenant and adjutant Fifteenth Regiment of Iowa Volunteers.


*Approved, May 22, 1890.*

# Chapter 792

> An Act to increase the pension of Thomas H. Gohagan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Thomas H. Gohagan, late of Twelfth Kansas Infantry. to forty dollars per month.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

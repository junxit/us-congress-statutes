# Chapter 1199

> An Act granting a pension to John Morgan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Morgan, late a private in Company B, One hundred and seventh Regiment of New York Volunteers.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

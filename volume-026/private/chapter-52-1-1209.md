# Chapter 1209

> An Act granting a pension to Georgiana W. Vogdes.

*Be it enacted, by the. Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Georgiana W. Vogdes, widow of Israel Vogdes. late colonel and brevet brigadier-general, United States Army, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

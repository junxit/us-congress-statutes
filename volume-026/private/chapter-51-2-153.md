# Chapter 153

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Matthew C. Griswold.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Matthew C. Griswold, late a first lieutenant of Company L, Twentieth New York Cavalry, to thirty dollars per month, the same to be in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 12, 1891.*

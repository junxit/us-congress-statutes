# Chapter 901

> An Act granting increase of pension to Hugh Brady.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Hugh Brady, late of Company I, Eighty-fourth Regiment of Illinois Infantry, to thirty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension now received by him under certificate sixty-two thousand eight hundred and forty-eight.


*Approved, September 4, 1890.*

# Chapter 371

> An Act granting a pension to Sarah E. Stewart.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah E. Stewart, widow of William G. Stewart, deceased, late captain Company D, Sixtieth Illinois Volunteers.


*Approved, May 27, 1890.*

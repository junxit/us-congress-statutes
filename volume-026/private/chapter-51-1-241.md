# Chapter 241

> An Act granting a pension to Ann E. Mussman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the tension laws, the name of Ann E. Mussman. widow of William Mussman, deceased, late a member of the Sixth Indiana Light Artillery.


*Approved, May 21, 1890.*

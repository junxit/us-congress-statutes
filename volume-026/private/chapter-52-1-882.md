# Chapter 882

> An Act granting a pension to Zephaniah H. Bones.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Zephaniah H. Bones, late of Company H, Seventh Regiment Iowa Cavalry.


*Approved, September 4, 1890.*

# Chapter 879

> An Act granting a pension to Jeanie Brent Davenport.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Jeanie Brent Davenport, widow of Captain Henry K, Davenport, late of the United States Navy, at the rate of fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, September 4, 1890.*

# Chapter 182

> An Act granting a pension to Ann M. Mosher.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-rolls, subject to the provisions and limitations the pension laws, the name of Ann M. Mosher, mother of Char H. Mosher, late a private of Company A, Eighteenth Regiment Massachusetts Volunteers.


*Approved, February 14, 1891.*

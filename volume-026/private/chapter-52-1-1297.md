# Chapter 1297

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Susan A. Dean.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Susan A. Dean, widow of Mahlon C. Dean, late first lieutenant Company K, Twenty-eighth Iowa Volunteer Infantry, and to grant her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, October 1, 1890.*

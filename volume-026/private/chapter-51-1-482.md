# Chapter 482

> An Act granting a pension to Sarah Cuthbert.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name Sarah Cuthbert, widow of James Cuthbert, late private Company M, Third Regiment Michigan Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, June 21, 1890.*

# Chapter 1298

> An Act granting a pension to Thelbert H. Head.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House, of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he hereby is, empowered and directed to place Thelbert H. Head, late of Company K, Second Iowa Cavalry, on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations and conditions of the general pension laws.


*Approved, October 1, 1890.*

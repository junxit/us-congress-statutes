# Chapter 277

> An Act granting a pension to Nathan C. Moore.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Nathan C. Moore, late a private of Captain Byram’s company (E), of Colonel Benjamin Snodgrass’s regiment, North Alabama Mounted Volunteers, in the Seminole Indian war, and to pay him a pension of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 23, 1891.*

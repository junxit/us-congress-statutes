# Chapter 327

> An Act for the relief of Wesley W. Taylor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name Wesley W. Taylor, late a private in Company D, Seventy-ninth Regiment Illinois Infantry.


*Approved, May 24, 1890.*

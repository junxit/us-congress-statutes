# Chapter 189

> An Act granting a pension to Olina Hanson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Olina Hanson, widow of Austin Hanson, late a private in Company E, Fifteenth Wisconsin Infantry, in the war of the rebellion.


*Approved, May 3, 1890.*

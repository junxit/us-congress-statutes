# Chapter 49

> An Act for the relief of Henry W. Burlingame.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Henry W. Burlingame.


*Approved, January 2, 1891.*

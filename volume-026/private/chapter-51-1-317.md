# Chapter 317

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Andrew J. Ferguson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension-rolls, subject to the limitations of existing laws, the name of Andrew J. Ferguson, late of Company B, Sixty-second Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and allow him a pension of sixteen dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving under certificate numbered three hundred and forty-six thousand one hundred and forty-six.


*Approved, May 24, 1890.*

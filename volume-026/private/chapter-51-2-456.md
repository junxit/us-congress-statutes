# Chapter 456

> An Act increasing the pension of John Britton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay John Britton, late captain of Company F. Eighteenth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Cavalry, forty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

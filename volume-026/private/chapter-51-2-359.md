# Chapter 359

> An Act granting a pension to Mary A. R. Martin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary A. R. Martin, widow of John Martin, a soldier of the war of eighteen hundred and twelve, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 27, 1891.*

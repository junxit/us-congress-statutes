# Chapter 612

> An Act granting a pension to Lizzie E. Haskett, a volunteer army nurse without pay.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-rolls, at the rate of twenty-five dollars a month, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Lizzie E. Haskett, formerly Miss Lizzie E. Denny, who was a volunteer nurse without pay during the late war, and who is now so nearly blind that she has to be led whenever she goes out of the house, and who is now old, poor, and in failing health.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

# Chapter 348

> An Act to increase the pension of Lucian L. Sanborn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase to forty-five dollars a month the pension of Lucian L. San- born, late of'Company D, Sixth Regiment of Vermont Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, May 24, 1890.*

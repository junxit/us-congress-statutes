# Chapter 705

> An Act for the relief of Joseph O. Cotton, dependent father of Gregory H. Cotton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to all the provisions and limitations of the pension laws of the United States, the name of Joseph O. Cotton, dependent father of Gregory H. Cotton, late a private in Company C, Seventy-third Regiment Indiana Volunteers.


*Approved, July 11, 1890.*

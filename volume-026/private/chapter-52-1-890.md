# Chapter 890

> An Act granting a pension to Lloyd H. Snell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lloyd H. Snell, late a private in Company F, First Regiment of Maine Volunteers, and to pay him a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, September 4, 1890.*

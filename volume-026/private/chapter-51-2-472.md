# Chapter 472

> An Act granting a pension to Susan G. Furnald.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Susan G. Furnald, dependent mother of James G. Furnald, late a private of Company A, Third New Hampshire Volunteers.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

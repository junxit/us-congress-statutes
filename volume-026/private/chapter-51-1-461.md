# Chapter 461

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Samuel Chandler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized to place on the pension-roll Samuel Chandler, late a soldier of the war of eighteen hundred and twelve, and pay him a pension of thirty dollars per month, in lieu of eight dollars a month now paid him.


*Approved, June 20, 1890.*

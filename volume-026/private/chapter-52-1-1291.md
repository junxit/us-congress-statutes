# Chapter 1291

> An Act granting a pension to Arthur Connery.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place upon the invalid pension-rolls of the United States the name of Arthur Connery, of Company E, Fifty fifth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers; the rating to be subject to the general pension laws.


*Approved, October 1, 1890.*

# Chapter 1116

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Prentiss M. Fogler.

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House, of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension of Prentiss M. Fogler, of Augusta, Maine, late a captain in Company I. Twentieth Regiment Maine Volunteers, be, and hereby is, increased to fifty dollars a month: Provided, That this act shall not be construed to prevent any further increase by the Pension Office to which the beneficiary may at any time become entitled.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

# Chapter 210

> An Act for the relief of Alexander Sturgeon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the terms and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Alexander Sturgeon, a private in Company E, in the Third Regiment of Indiana Infantry, and pay to him the sum of twenty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension now paid him.


*Approved, February 14, 1891.*

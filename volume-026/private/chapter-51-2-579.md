# Chapter 579

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Sue Ditto.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll the name of Mrs. Sue Ditto, widow of Gideon Ditto, late a lieutenant in Company B. Eighty-first Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension, subject to the rules and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

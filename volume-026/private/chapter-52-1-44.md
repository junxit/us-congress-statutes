# Chapter 44

> An Act granting a pension to Eliza A. Talbott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Eliza A. Talbott, widow of B. W. Talbott, late of Company G, Fourth Regiment Iowa Infantry.


*Approved, March 19, 1890.*

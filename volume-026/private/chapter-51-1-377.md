# Chapter 377

> An Act granting restoration of pension to Sarah A. Woodbridge.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-rolls the name of Sarah A. Woodbridge, dependent mother of Charles Brewer, late a captain in the Nineteenth Regiment Ohio Volunteers.


*Approved, May 27, 1890.*

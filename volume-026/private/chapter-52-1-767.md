# Chapter 767

> An Act granting a pension to Oran M. Collinsworth.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Oran M. Collinsworth, a soldier of the Florida Indian war. and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

# Chapter 782

> An Act to pension Nathaniel Moon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Nathaniel Moon, of Buena Vista, Ouachita County, Arkansas, late a private of Captain Fruits company, Colonel Snodgrass’s regiment Alabama Volunteers. Creek war, and pay him the same pension as is allowed by law for service in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

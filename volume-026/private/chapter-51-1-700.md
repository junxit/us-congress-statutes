# Chapter 700

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Stephen D. Redfield.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United. States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Stephen D. Redfield, late a lieutenant in Company A, Thirty-seventh Regiment Iowa Volunteers, and pay him at the rate of thirty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension now paid to him.


*Approved, July 11, 1890.*

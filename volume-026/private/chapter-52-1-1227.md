# Chapter 1227

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Margaret E. Pierce.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension of Margaret E. Pierce, widow of Captain Henry Hubbard Pierce, late first lieutenant of the Twenty-first United States Infantry, and major of the First Connecticut Heavy Artillery, United States Volunteers, be increased to twenty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

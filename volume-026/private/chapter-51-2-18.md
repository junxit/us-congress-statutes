# Chapter 18

> An Act granting a pension to Mary Jane Blackledge.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Mary Jane Blackledge, late widow of George Goodman, a private in Company F, Twenty-fourth Regiment of Iowa Volunteers.


*Approved, December 15, 1890.*

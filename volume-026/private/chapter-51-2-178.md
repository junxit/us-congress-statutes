# Chapter 178

> An Act granting a pension to Caroline E. Gray.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Caroline E. Gray, an Army nurse during the late war of the rebellion, and pay her a pension of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 14, 1891.*

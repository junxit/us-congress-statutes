# Chapter 321

> An Act granting a pension to Josephine S. Hansel late Wilson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place upon the pension-rolls of the United States the name of Josephine S. Hansel (formerly Wilson), widow of James Wilson, late of Company K, Twenty-eighth Regiment Iowa Infantry Volunteers, at the rate of twelve dollars a month.


*Approved, February 25, 1891.*

# Chapter 446

> An Act granting a pension to Solomon Mayberry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Solomon Mayberry, foster father of George L. Brown, late a private in Company E, Eighth Regiment of Maine Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

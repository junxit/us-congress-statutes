# Chapter 1178

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Thomas G. Boss.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Thomas G. Boss, late private in Company C. Sixteenth Wisconsin Veteran Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he now receives.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

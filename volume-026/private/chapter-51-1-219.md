# Chapter 219

> An Act granting a pension to William Richardson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William Richardson, late a private in company K, First Ohio Artillery.


*Approved, May 16, 1890.*

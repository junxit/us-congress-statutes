# Chapter 988

> An Act increasing the pension of Mrs. Caroline Hanneman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the,pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Caroline Hanneman, widow of the late Julius R. Hanneman, first lieutenant in the Seventh Regiment New York Infantry, at the rate of seventeen dollars per month, instead of eight dollars per month now received.


*Approved, September 26, 1890.*

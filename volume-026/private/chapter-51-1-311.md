# Chapter 311

> An Act granting a pension to Eveline M. Alexander.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of pension laws, the name of Eveline M. Alexander, and rate her pension at fifty dollars per month, which shall be in lieu of the pension she now receives.


*Approved, May 24, 1890.*

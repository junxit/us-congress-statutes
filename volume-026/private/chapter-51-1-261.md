# Chapter 261

> An Act granting a pension to George Dodge.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll the name of George Dodge, late orderly sergeant of Company G of the First United States Dragoons m the Mexican war, and pay him a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, May 21, 1890.*

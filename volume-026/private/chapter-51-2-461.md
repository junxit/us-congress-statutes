# Chapter 461

> An Act to grant a pension to Mary E. Dubridge.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Mary E. Dubridge, widow of Francis Dubridge, late a private of marines, who enlisted in the Marine Corps on the twenty-third of January, eighteen hundred and thirty- six, and was discharged therefrom January twenty-second, eighteen hundred and forty, and served in the Florida war, to be paid a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

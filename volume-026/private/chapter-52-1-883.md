# Chapter 883

> An Act granting a pension to Phillipe Ray.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations or the pension laws, the name of Phillipe Ray, late of Company B, First New Jersey Cavalry in the war of the rebellion.


*Approved, September 4, 1890.*

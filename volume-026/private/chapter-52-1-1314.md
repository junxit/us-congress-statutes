# Chapter 1314

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Edwin Reeder, late a member of Company A, First Tennessee Infantry, in the war with Mexico.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Edwin Reeder, late a member of Company A, of the First Tennessee Infantry, in the war with Mexico, to twenty dollars per month, said amount to be in lieu of the pension of eight dollars per month which he is now drawing under certificate numbered forty-four hundred and sixty-two.


*Approved, October 1, 1890.*

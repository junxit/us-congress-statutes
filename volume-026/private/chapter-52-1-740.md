# Chapter 740

> An Act granting a pension to Gaston Winters.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Gaston Winters, late of Company B, One hundred and Sixteenth Regiment Ohio Volunteers.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

# Chapter 248

> An Act granting a pension to Betsey F. Newhall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Betsey F. Newhall, widow of Timothy Newhall, late a private in the Massachusetts militia in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve.


*Approved, May 21, 1890.*

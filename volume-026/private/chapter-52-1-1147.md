# Chapter 1147

> An Act to pension Silas Beezley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Silas Beezley, of the State of Oregon, be paid twenty-five dollars per month as pension for services in the Black Hawk War, in Captain Jesse B. Brown’s company of Illinois Mounted Rangers, and for disabilities suffered and incurred in said service; and the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized to place the name of said Silas Beezley on the pension-roll.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

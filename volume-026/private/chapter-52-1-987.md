# Chapter 987

> An Act increasing the pension of Smith J. Shafer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, at. the rate of seventy-two dollars per month, in lieu of the pension which he now receives, the name of Smith J. Shafer, late private Company E, Sixty-sixth Ohio Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, September 26, 1890.*

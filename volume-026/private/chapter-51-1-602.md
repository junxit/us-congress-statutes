# Chapter 602

> An Act to grant a pension to James Y. Law.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension-roll the name of James Y. Law, late of Company K, Nineteenth Michigan Infantry Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month, the same to be in lieu of all pension by him now received.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

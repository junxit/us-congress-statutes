# Chapter 363

> An Act granting a pension to Celia Eichele.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the Ïtension laws, the name of Celia Eichele, daughter of Jacob Eichele, ate of Company F, Forty-fifth New York Volunteers, and sister of August Eichele, late Captain Company A, First Regiment Missouri Reserve Corps.


*Approved, February 27, 1891.*

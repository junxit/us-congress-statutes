# Chapter 360

> An Act granting a pension to Robert A. Bride, or McBride.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Robert A. Bride, or McBride, late of Company H, One hundred and eighty-third Regiment of Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, May 27, 1890.*

# Chapter 1107

> An Act granting a pension to William Stover.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William Stover, late of Company I, Twenty-fourth Regiment of Iowa Infantry.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

# Chapter 1174

> An Act granting a pension to Montraville A. Harrington.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws, the name of Montraville A. Harrington, dependent father of Fazelo A. Harrington, deceased, late Colonel of the Twenty-seventh Regiment Illinois Volunteers.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

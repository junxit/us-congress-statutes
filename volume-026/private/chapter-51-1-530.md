# Chapter 530

> An Act granting a pension to Elijah Kilday.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elijah Kilday, late a member of Colonel Fry’s Tennessee Volunteers.


*Approved, June 21, 1890.*

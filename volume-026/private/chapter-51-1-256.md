# Chapter 256

> An Act granting a pension to H. G. Church.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and. House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, required to place on the pension-roll H. G. Church, of Mount Pleasant, Iowa, late a member of Company K, First Regiment of New York Provisional Cavalry, and pay him a pension according to his disability.


*Approved, May 21, 1890.*

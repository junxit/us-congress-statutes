# Chapter 319

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Abram Duryee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension now granted to Abram Duryee, late a brigadier-general of the United States Volunteers, be, and the same is hereby, increased to one hundred dollars per month, on account of wounds received in the line of duty while a volunteer in the war of the rebellion.


*Approved, May 24, 1890.*

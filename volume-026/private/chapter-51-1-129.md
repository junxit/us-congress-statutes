# Chapter 129

> An Act granting a pension to Robert Hill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Robert Hill, a soldier of the Florida Indian war of eighteen hundred and thirty-seven and eighteen hundred and thirty-eight, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month from and after the passage of this act


*Approved, April 21, 1890.*

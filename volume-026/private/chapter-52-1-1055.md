# Chapter 1055

> An Act granting a pension to Nancy Carey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and- House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Nancy Carey, of Charlotte. Michigan, at the rate of twelve dollars per month, on account of disability resulting from disease contracted while serving as a hospital nurse during the war of the rebellion.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

# Chapter 740

> An Act granting a pension to Mary A. Irwin, widow.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior he, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the 1 tension laws, the name of Mrs Mary A. Irwin, widow of William Irwin, late private Company G, First Louisiana Cavalry.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

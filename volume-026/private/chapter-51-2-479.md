# Chapter 479

> An Act granting a pension to Georgiana Smith, an army nurse.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Georgiana Smith upon the pension rolls and pay her a pension, at the rate of twelve dollars per month, on and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

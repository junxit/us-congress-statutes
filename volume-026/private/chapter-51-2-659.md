# Chapter 659

> An Act granting increase of pension to Florida G. Casey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Florida G. Casey, widow of Major-General Silas Casey, of the Regular Army, and pay her a pension at the rate of one hundred dollars per month, in lieu of that which she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

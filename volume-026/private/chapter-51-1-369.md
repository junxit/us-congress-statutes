# Chapter 369

> An Act granting a pension to George W. Padgett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of George W. Padgett, late a private in Company B, Fifteenth Regiment of Illinois Cavalry, on the pension-roll, .subject to the limitations of the pension laws, and pay him a pension of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, May 27, 1890.*

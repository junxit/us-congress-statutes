# Chapter 410

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Frederika B. Jones.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Frederika B. Jones, widow of the late Brigadier-General Roger Jones, on the pension-roll, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

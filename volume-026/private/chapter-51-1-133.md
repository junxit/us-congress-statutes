# Chapter 133

> An Act granting a pension to Jane Reynolds.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, directed to place upon the pension- rolls, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jane Reynolds, widow of Roger Reynolds, late of Company A, Twenty-sixth New York Infantry, and Company C, Fourteenth New York Artillery.


*Approved, April 21, 1890.*

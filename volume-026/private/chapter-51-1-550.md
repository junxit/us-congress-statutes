# Chapter 550

> An Act granting a pension to Kate Woodbridge Michaelis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Kate Woodbridge Michaelis, widow of Otho E. Michaelis, late a major in the Ordnance Department, United States Army.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

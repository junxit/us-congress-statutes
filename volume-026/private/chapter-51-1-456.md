# Chapter 456

> An Act granting a pension to William Crowford.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William Crowford of Spaulding County, Georgia, who was a private soldier in Captain James T. Ellis’ company, in the Creek Indian war of eighteen hundred and thirty-six, and allow him a pension at twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, June 20, 1890.*

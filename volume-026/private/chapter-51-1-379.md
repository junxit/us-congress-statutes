# Chapter 379

> An Act to pension Bartola Thebant, a soldier in the Florida Seminole Indian war of eighteen Hundred and forty-nine and eighteen hundred and fifty.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Bartola Thebant, a soldier in the Florida Seminole Indian war of eighteen hundred and forty-nine and eighteen hundred and fifty.


*Approved, May 27, 1890.*

# Chapter 200

> An Act granting a pension to Diana Dickey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Diana Dickey, late widow of Daniel G, Carl, Company H. One hundred and fifth Regiment of Pennsylvania Volunteers, on the pension rolls at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 14, 1891.*

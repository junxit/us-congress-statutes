# Chapter 511

> An Act granting a pension to Anastasia McGrievy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House, of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Anastasia McGrievy, widow of Henry McGrievy, late of Company K, Forty-third Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, June 21, 1890.*

# Chapter 713

> An Act granting a pension to Kate and Ethel B. Cook.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Kate Cook, widow of George T. Cook, late lieutenant, Company C, Twenty-first New York Volunteer Infantry, lieutenant and acting quartermaster, Veteran Reserve Corps, and lieutenant, Sixth United States Army, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month; and also the name of Ethel B., minor child of the same, and pay her a pension at the rate of two dollars per month, to date from soldier’s death, February fifth, eighteen hundred and ninety-one.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

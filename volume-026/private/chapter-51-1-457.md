# Chapter 457

> An Act granting a pension to Mary A. Lefebvre, widow of Hiram Goodspeed, late of Company A, Fifty-sixth Massachusetts Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-rolls, at the rate of twelve dollars a month, the name of Mary A. Lefebvre, widow of Hiram Goodspeed. late a private in Company A, Fifty-sixth Massachusetts Volunteers,


*Approved, June 20, 1890.*

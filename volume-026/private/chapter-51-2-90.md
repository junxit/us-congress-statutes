# Chapter 90

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. E. J. Baldy, widow of W. H. Baldy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs, E. J. Baldy, widow of W, H. Baldy, of Spaulding County, Georgia, who was a private soldier in the Creek Indian war of eighteen hundred and thirty-six, in Captain William Byne’s company called “Burke Hussars,” and allow her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, January 21, 1891.*

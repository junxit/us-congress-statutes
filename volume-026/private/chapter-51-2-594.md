# Chapter 594

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs Hannah M. Foss.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior he. and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Hannah M. Foss, of Harmony, in the State of Maine, mother of Nathaniel A. Foss, late of Fourth Maine Battery, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

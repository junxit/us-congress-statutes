# Chapter 446

> An Act granting a pension to Richard D. McKinney.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the Pension laws, the name of Richard D. McKinney, late of Company , Forty-eighth Regiment Illinois Volunteers.


*Approved, June 20, 1890.*

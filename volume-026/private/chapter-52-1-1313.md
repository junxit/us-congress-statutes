# Chapter 1313

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Lovey Aldrich.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Lovey Aldrich, widow of Taylor Clark, who served as a soldier in the Light Infantry of New Hampshire in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve, and also widow of Caleb Aldrich, of the Rhode Island Line, war of seventeen hundred and seventy-six, to the sum of thirty dollars per month in lieu of the pension now drawn by said Lovey Aldrich.


*Approved, October 1, 1890.*

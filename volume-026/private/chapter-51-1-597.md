# Chapter 597

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Elnathan Meade, late of Company C, Forty-fourth New York Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elnathan Meade, late of Company C, forty-fourth New York Volunteers, at the rate of seventy two dollars a month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

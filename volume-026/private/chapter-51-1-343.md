# Chapter 343

> An Act to grant a pension to Huldah Burton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, the name of Huldah Burton, widow of Thomas Burton, who was a soldier in Captain Seth Pratt’s company in the Black Hawk war, and pay her a pension of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, May 24, 1890.*

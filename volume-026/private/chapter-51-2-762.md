# Chapter 762

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Squire West.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Squire West, late a private in Company F. Fourth Regiment of Indiana Volunteers, and allow him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month, the same to be in lieu of the amount now paid him.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

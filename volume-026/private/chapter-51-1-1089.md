# Chapter 1089

> An Act for the relief of Olive M. Hechtman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to tile provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Olive M. Hechtman. as volunteer nurse, at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

# Chapter 1302

> An Act granting a pension to Theodore L. Alexander.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Theodore L. Alexander, late a teamster in the quartermaster’s service in the war with Mexico, in consideration of his taking arms and actually participating in the battles resulting from the defense of Fort Puebla against the assaults of the Mexican forces, in obedience to the orders of his commanding officer.


*Approved, October 1, 1890.*

# Chapter 1104

> An Act granting a pension to Margaret M. Clements

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Margaret M. Clements, widow of John Clements, late a lieutenant in Company H. Eighty-third New York Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

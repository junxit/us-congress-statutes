# Chapter 1090

> An Act for the relief of Mary E. Hicks.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That the act entitled “An act to restore pensions in certain cases” approved June ninth, eighteen hundred and eighty, shall be construed so as to include within its provisions Mary E. Hicks, widow of Captain Daniel Hicks of the Adrian Guards, Mexican War.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

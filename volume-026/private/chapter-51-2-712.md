# Chapter 712

> An Act granting a pension to Johanna Carroll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Johanna Car roll, the permanently helpless child of Cornelius Carroll, late a private of Company K, twelfth Illinois Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars a month.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

# Chapter 576

> An Act granting a pension to August Seiter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations or the pension laws, the name of August Seiter, late a private in Captain John Belm’s Company of Minnesota Volunteer Militia.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

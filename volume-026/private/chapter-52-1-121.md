# Chapter 121

> An Act granting a pension to Ellen Shears.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ellen Shears, widow of George A. Shears, ate of the Eighteenth New York Battery Volunteers.


*Approved, April 21, 1890.*

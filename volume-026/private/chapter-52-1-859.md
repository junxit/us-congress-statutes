# Chapter 859

> An Act granting a pension to John L. Russell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John L. Russell, who served in Captain Peter Butler’s company of mounted rangers in the Black Hawk war, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month.


*Approved, September 2, 1890.*

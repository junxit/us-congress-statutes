# Chapter 514

> An Act to grant a pension to Mary C. Hoffman, widow of General William Hoffman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll the name of Mary C. Hoffman, of Rock Island, Illinois, widow of General William Hoffman, who served in the Black Hawk war, the Florida war, the Mexican, the Indian frontier war. and in the late war of the rebellion, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, March 2, 1891.*

# Chapter 111

> An Act granting a pension to Emily P. Collins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations or the pension laws, the name of Emily P. Collins, an army nurse, of Hartford. Connecticut, and grant her a pension of twelve dollars a month.


*Approved, January 28, 1891.*

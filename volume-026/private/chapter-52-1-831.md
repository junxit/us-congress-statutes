# Chapter 831

> An Act granting a pension to Ursula Lucretia Haight.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ursula Lucretia Haight, widow of William H. Haight, late private Company H, Thirty-ninth Regiment Enrolled Militia of Missouri.


*Approved, August 29, 1890.*

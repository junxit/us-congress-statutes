# Chapter 984

> An Act granting a pension to Harriet E. Donaldson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Harriet E. Donaldson, widow of Edward Donaldson, late rear-admiral in the United States Navy, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, September 26, 1890.*

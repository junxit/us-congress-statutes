# Chapter 471

> An Act for the relief of Sarah D. Duke.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah D. Duke, widow of Captain John I. Duke, deceased, late of Maysville, State of Kentucky, formerly aid-de-camp to Brigadier General William H. Nelson, in the war of eighteen hundred and sixty-one


*Approved, June 20, 1890.*

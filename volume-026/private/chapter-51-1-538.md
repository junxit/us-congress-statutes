# Chapter 538

> An Act to grant a pension to Jacob E. Goudy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Jacob E. Goudy, late private Company G, Two hundred and first Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers, on the pension-roll, subject to the restrictions and limitations of the pension laws of the United States.


*Approved, June 21, 1890.*

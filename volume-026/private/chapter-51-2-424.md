# Chapter 424

> An Act granting a pension to Anna S. Shuman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-rolls the name of Anna S. Shuman, late a nurse in the war of the rebellion, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

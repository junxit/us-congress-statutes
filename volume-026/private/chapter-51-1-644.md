# Chapter 644

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Alice A. Cunningham.

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Alice A. Cunningham, widow of James Cunningham, late a private of Company D, Fifty-first Regiment Ohio Volunteers.


*Approved, June 30, 1890.*

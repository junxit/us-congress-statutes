# Chapter 268

> An Act granting a pension to Betsey Johnson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Betsey Johnson, widow of Jehiel Johnson, late of Company A, Twenty-fourth Regiment Connecticut Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, February 23, 1891.*

# Chapter 1053

> An Act granting a pension to Abraham W. Jackson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Abraham W. Jackson, of Concord. Massachusetts, late a Captain in Company C, Eighth Regiment Maine Volunteers.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

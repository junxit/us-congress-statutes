# Chapter 953

> An Act granting a pension to Henry A. Barnum.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Henry A. Barnum, late brigadier-general and brevet major-general of volunteers, and pay him a pension of one hundred dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, September 26, 1890.*

# Chapter 1303

> An Act granting a pension to Louis P. Noros, late of the Jeannette Expedition to the Arctic Ocean.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-rolls, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Louis P. Noros, late of the United States ship Jeannette, in the expedition to the Arctic Ocean.


*Approved, October 1, 1890.*

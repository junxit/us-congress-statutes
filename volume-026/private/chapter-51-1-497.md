# Chapter 497

> An Act granting a pension to Maria Solles.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject, to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Maria Solles, widow of David Solles, Company C, First New York Mounted Rifles.


*Approved, June 21, 1890.*

# Chapter 176

> An Act granting a pension to Hannah R. Langdon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws, the name of Hannah R. Langdon, widow of Henry H. Langdon, late an assistant surgeon in the Seventh Regiment Vermont Volunteers, in the war of the rebellion.


*Approved, April 30, 1890.*

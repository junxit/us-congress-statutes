# Chapter 621

> An Act granting a pension to Alexander G. Davis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-rolls the name of Alexander G. Davis, of Mount Airy, Maryland, late scout and guide with Generals Howard and Augur, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, June 25, 1890.*

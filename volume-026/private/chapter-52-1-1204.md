# Chapter 1204

> An Act granting a pension to Benjamin F. Brown.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Benjamin F. Brown, late second lieutenant of the First New Hampshire Volunteers, at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

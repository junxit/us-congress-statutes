# Chapter 136

> An Act to increase the pension of Mary M. Gibson, widow of Commander William Gibson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on tire pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name'of Mary M. Gibson, widow of Commander William Gibson, United States Navy, and pay her a pension of fifty dollars per month from and after the passage of this act; and this pension shall be in lieu of that which she is now receiving.


*Approved, April 21, 1890.*

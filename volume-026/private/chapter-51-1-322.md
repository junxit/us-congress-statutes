# Chapter 322

> An Act for the relief of John Curran.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension now allowed to John Curran, of Harrison County, Kentucky, be, and is hereby, increased to twenty five dollars a month, and that hereafter said Curran shall be paid twenty-five dollars per month; said Curran was a private in Company A, Third United States Artillery, in the war with Mexico.


*Approved, May 24, 1890.*

# Chapter 774

> An Act to increase the pension of Leah Allen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay Leah Allen, of Andty, Clark County, Arkansas, a pension at the rate of twenty dollars a month, in lieu of the pension of eight dollars per month now received under certificate numbered fifty-nine hundred and twenty-two, Knoxville, Tennessee, agency.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

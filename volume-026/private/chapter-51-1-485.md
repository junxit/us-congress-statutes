# Chapter 485

> An Act granting a pension to Susanna Mitts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Susanna Mitts, widow of James H. Mitts, private in Company F, Sixth Regiment Missouri Infantry Volunteers, in the war of the rebellion.


*Approved, June 21, 1890.*

# Chapter 599

> An Act granting a pension to Agnes C. Junot.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is. authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions of the pension law, the name of Agites C. Junot, widow of Charles Junot, a soldier of the Florida war, and a pensioner under act of Congress approved October sixteenth, anno Domini eighteen hundred and eighty-eight, and pay her at the rate of twelve dollars a month.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

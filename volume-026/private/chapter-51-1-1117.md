# Chapter 1117

> An Act for relief of Lawrence M. Cafflin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lawrence M. Cafflin, late a third assistant engineer on the United States Steamer Republic.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

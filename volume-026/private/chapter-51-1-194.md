# Chapter 194

> An Act to increase the pension of W. H. H. Bailey, of Braintree, Massachusetts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the rate of pension allowed W. H. H. Bailey, late a private of Com- any F, Thirty-eight Regiment of New York Volunteer Infantry, war of eighteen hundred and sixty-one, under certificate numbered thirty-five thousand three hundred and ninety-nine, from the sum of thirty-six dollars per month to the sum of forty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, May 3, 1890.*

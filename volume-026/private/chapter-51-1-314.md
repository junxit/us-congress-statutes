# Chapter 314

> An Act granting a pension to Ida L. Martin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled'.,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Ida L. Martin, a resident of Huntsville, Alabama, at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month, on account of disability resulting from disease contracted while serving as a hospital nurse during the war of the rebellion.


*Approved, May 24, 1890.*

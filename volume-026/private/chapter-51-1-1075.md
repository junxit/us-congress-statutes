# Chapter 1075

> An Act granting a pension to Isadora Ritter, formerly Isadora De Wolf Dimmick.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Isadora Ritter, widow of Judson De Wolf Dimmick, late major Eighty-second Regiment of New York Volunteers.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

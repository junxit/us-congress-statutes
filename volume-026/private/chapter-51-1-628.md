# Chapter 628

> An Act to increase the pension of B. F. Hilliker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of B. F. Hilliker, late Company A, Eighth Regiment Wisconsin Infantry, to seventy-two dollars per month.


*Approved, June 25, 1890.*

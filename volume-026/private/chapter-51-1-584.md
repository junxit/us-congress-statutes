# Chapter 584

> An Act granting a pension to Harriet. E. Cooper.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Harriet E. Cooper, widow of William B. Cooper. late major of the Ninety-eighth Illinois Volunteers.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

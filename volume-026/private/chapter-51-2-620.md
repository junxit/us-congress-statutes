# Chapter 620

> An Act granting a pension to William F. Reed.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll subject to the provisions and limitations of the, pension laws, the name of William F. Reed, late of Company K,. Thirty-fifth Regiment Kentucky Mounted Infantry.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

# Chapter 588

> An Act granting a pension to T. G. Metcalf.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House, of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place, on the pension-roll the name of T. G. Metcalf, late a private in Company B, Thirty-fourth New York Volunteer Infantry, at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

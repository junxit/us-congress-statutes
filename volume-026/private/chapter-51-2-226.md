# Chapter 226

> An Act to pension William A. Todd

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of William A. Todd, who served as a private in Captain John D. Sill’s company of Colonel William Wodd’s battalion, Georgia Creek War Volunteers, eighteen hundred and thirty-six, and pay him the same pension as is allowed by law for service in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve.


*Approved, February 14, 1891.*

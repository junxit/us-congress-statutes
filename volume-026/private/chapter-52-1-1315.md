# Chapter 1315

> An Act to increase the pension of Mrs. Maria B. Judah.

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Mrs. Maria B. Judah, widow of Major Henry M. Judah, United States Army, and pay her at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month, in lieu of the pension now paid her.


*Approved, October 1, 1890.*

# Chapter 739

> An Act granting a pension to Sarah A. Tryon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension law, the name of Sarah A. Tryon mother of Henry Tryon, late of Company B, Fourth Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer Cavalry.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

# Chapter 286

> An Act for the relief of Napoleon B. Mc Kay.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior’be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Napoleon B Me Kay, formerly a member of unassigned company, Thirteenth Regiment Kansas State Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, May 22, 1890.*

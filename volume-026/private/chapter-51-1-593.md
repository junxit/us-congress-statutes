# Chapter 593

> An Act granting an increase of pensions to John Pardy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to re-issue the pension certificate of John Pardy, late a private of Company A, Fifteenth New York Volunteer Cavalry, and allow him a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

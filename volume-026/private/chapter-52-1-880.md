# Chapter 880

> An Act granting a pension to William P. Madden.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William P. Madden, late private Company I, Sixth United States Infantry.


*Approved, September 4, 1890.*

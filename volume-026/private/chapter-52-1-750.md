# Chapter 750

> An Act granting a pension to George W. White.

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George W. White, who served in Florida in the years eighteen hundred and thirty-six, eighteen hundred and thirty-seven, and eighteen hundred and thirty-eight, for disability contracted in such service, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

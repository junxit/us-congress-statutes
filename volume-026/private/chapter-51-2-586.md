# Chapter 586

> An Act granting a pension to Mary E. Graham.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place- on the pension-roll the name of Mary E. Graham, invalid daughter of Barton W. Graham, deceased, late a sergeant in Company H,. Sixty-third Regiment of Indiana Volunteers, and to pay her a pension of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

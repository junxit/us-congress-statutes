# Chapter 156

> An Act for the relief of William Karger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William Karger, a sailor in the United States Navy, and pay him a pension at forty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 12, 1891.*

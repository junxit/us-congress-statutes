# Chapter 694

> An Act for the relief of Harvey E. Maccown.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the passage of this act there be paid, out of the naval pension fund, to Harvey E. Maccown, widow of Robert T. Maccown, late medical director United States Navy, the sum of fifty dollars per month during her widowhood; that sum to be in lieu of her present pension.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

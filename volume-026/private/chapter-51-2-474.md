# Chapter 474

> An Act granting a pension to William D. Calkins, Company A, One hundred and eleventh Pennsylvania Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, at the rate of forty dollars per month, the name of William D. Calkins, late of Company A, One hundred and eleventh Regiment of Pennsylvania Volunteers.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

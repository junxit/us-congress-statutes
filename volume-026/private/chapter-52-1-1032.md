# Chapter 1032

> An Act to grant a pension to James Knetsar.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll the name of James Knetsar, who was a soldier in Captain Daniel Powell’s company, in the First Regiment, commanded by Colonel Hosea Pierce, in the Black Hawk war, and pay him a pension of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, September 27, 1890.*

# Chapter 976

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Elizabeth A. Baker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Elizabeth A. Baker, widow of Jacob S. Baker, private in Company I, Fourth Regiment Indiana Volunteers. in the war of Mexico.


*Approved, September 26, 1890.*

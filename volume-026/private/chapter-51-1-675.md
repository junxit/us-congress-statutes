# Chapter 675

> An Act granting a pension to Annie D. Rundlett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Annie D. Rundlett. widow of Howard M. Rundlett, passed assistant surgeon, United States Navy, and pay her thirty dollars per month


*Approved, July 11, 1890.*

# Chapter 260

> An Act granting a pension to Barbara Schnappinger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, at the rate of twelve dollars per month, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Barbara Schnappinger, widow of Frederick Schnappinger, late a private in Company K, Second Maryland Infantry,


*Approved, May 21, 1890.*

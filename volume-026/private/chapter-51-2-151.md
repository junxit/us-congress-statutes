# Chapter 151

> An Act granting a pension to Thomas J. Wilkins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-rolls the name of Thomas J. Wilkins late a private of Company D, Sixty-sixth Ohio Volunteer Infantry and of Company L, Second Ohio Heavy Artillery, and pay him a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month, he being totally disabled and infirm from wounds and army service.


*Approved, February 12, 1891.*

# Chapter 96

> An Act granting a pension to Nicholas Moy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Nicholas Moy, late Sergeant Companies A and K (consolidated), Thirty-third Regiment Missouri State Mounted Militia.


*Approved, April 17, 1890.*

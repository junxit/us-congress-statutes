# Chapter 305

> An Act granting a pension to Mary E. Tipton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension law, the name of Mary E. Tipton, widow of W. B. Tipton, late a member of Company G, Third Regiment Kentucky Volunteers, Mexican War.


*Approved, May 24, 1890.*

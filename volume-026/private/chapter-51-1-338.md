# Chapter 338

> An Act to pension Eli J. Youngheim.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Eli J. Youngheim. late a private in Company A, Sixth Kansas Cavalry Volunteers.


*Approved, May 24, 1890.*

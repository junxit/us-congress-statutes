# Chapter 611

> An Act for restoration of Abner Morehead to the pension-roll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to restore to the pension-roll the name of Abner Morehead, late of Company K. One hundred and fifteenth Indiana Volunteers, to take effect, from the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

# Chapter 1304

> An Act granting a pension to Samuel S. Humphreys

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Samuel S. Humphreys, of Mitchell County, Georgia, late a private in Captain Abner Williams’ company, Georgia Volunteers, in the Indian war of eighteen hundred and thirty-five and eighteen hundred and thirty-six, at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, October 1, 1890.*

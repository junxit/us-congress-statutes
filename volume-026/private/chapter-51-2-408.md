# Chapter 408

> An Act granting a pension to Emily H. Worthen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Emily H. Worthen, as widow of Isaac P. McMaster, late a sergeant Company E, Sixth Regiment New Hampshire Volunteers.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

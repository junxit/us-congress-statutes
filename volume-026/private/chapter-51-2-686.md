# Chapter 686

> An Act for the relief of Hart County, Kentucky.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of Internal Revenue, with the approval of the Secretary of the Treasury, is hereby authorized and required on appeal to him made to refund and pay back to the County of Hart, State of Kentucky, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, any and all money collected from any and all corporations, or from any other person or party whatsoever, as internal-revenue taxes.on dividends or shares of stock owned by said county in the Louisville and Nashville Railroad Company, to the extent that such taxes were deducted from any dividends due and payable to said county, any statute of limitation to the contrary notwithstanding.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

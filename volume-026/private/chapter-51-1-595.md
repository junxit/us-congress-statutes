# Chapter 595

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Morgan Dimond.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Morgan Dimond, (an insane person), a late private in Company G, Seventy-ninth Regiment New York Infantry, to the sum of forty dollars per month, and pay the same to his legally constituted committee.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

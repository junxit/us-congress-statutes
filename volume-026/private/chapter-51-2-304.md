# Chapter 304

> An Act for the relief of Coplin McKelvey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Coplip McKelvey, late a private in Captain Cheny’s company of Tennessee Volunteers in the Florida war, at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1891.*

# Chapter 1224

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Emily F. Warren.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Emily F. Warren, widow of G. K. Warren, late a major-general in the United States Army, and pay her a pension at the rate of one hundred dollars per month, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

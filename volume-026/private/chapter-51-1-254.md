# Chapter 254

> An Act granting a pension to William A. Merriwether.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-rolls the name of William A. Merriwether, of Louisville, Kentucky, and pay him a pension of thirty dollars a month, for meritorious services m the war with Mexico,


*Approved, May 21, 1890.*

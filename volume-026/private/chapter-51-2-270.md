# Chapter 270

> An Act granting a pen Bion to Andrew J. Elliott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Andrew J. Elliott, Mexican veteran, at the rate of thirty dollars a month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 23, 1891.*

# Chapter 485

> An Act for the benefit of General W J. Landram.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of W. J. Landram, formerly colonel of the Nineteenth Regiment of Kentucky Volunteer Infantry, and afterwards promoted to the rank of brigadier-general by brevet, and that he be granted a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month during his natural life, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

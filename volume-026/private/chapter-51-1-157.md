# Chapter 157

> An Act to increase the pension of William Boone.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and required to place the name of William Boone, of Andrews, Huntington County, Indiana, late a private in Company F, of the Eighty-eighth Regiment Indiana Volunteers, at the rate of fifty dollars per month, in lieu of the rate of pension he now receives.

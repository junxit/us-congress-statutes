# Chapter 236

> An Act granting a pension to Henry A. Hoar.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, the name of Üenry A. Hoar, a member of the Indiana Legion, and pay him a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 14, 1891.*

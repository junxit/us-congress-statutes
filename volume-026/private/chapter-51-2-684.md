# Chapter 684

> An Act to correct the military record of George S. Coyle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to issue a certificate of honorable discharge to G. S. Coyle, lieutenant Second Kentucky Volunteers, from the service of the United States, as of November twenty-second, eighteen hundred and sixty two.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

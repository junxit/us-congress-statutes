# Chapter 532

> An Act for the relief of Elizabeth Earp.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Elizabeth Earp, widow, late an army nurse, at the rate of twelve dollars a month, to date from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 21, 1890.*

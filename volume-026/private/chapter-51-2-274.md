# Chapter 274

> An Act granting a pension to Myra E. Lakin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Myra E. Lakin, widow of John W. Lawrence, late a second lieutenant Company C, Fifth New Hampshire Volunteers.


*Approved, February 23, 1891.*

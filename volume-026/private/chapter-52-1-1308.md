# Chapter 1308

> An Act for the relief of Alexander F. Dutton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Alexander F. Dutton, late a volunteer in the naval service of the United States, upon the pension-roll, subject to the pro-visions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, October 1, 1890.*

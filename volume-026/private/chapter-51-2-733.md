# Chapter 733

> An Act granting a pension to Charles T. Sloat.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws the name of Charles T. Sloat, late of Company B, One hundred and eighth Regiment of Illinois Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

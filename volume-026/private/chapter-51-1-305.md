# Chapter 305

> An Act granting a pension to Harriet F Bowes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Harriet F Bowes, widow of Roland C. Bowes late of Company G, Fifty-first Pennsylvania Volunteers.


*Approved, May 24, 1890.*

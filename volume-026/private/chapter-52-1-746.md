# Chapter 746

> An Act granting pension to Sarah L. Patterson and Jane W. Patterson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he hereby is. directed to place on the pension- Toll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the names of Sarah L. Patterson and Jane W Patterson, sisters of George C. Patterson, deceased, late first lieutenant of Company E, One hundred and fifth Regiment Pennsylvania Infantry, at the rate of eight and one-half dollars each per month.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

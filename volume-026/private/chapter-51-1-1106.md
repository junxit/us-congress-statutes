# Chapter 1106

> An Act granting a pension to Rachael A. Fenstamaker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Rachael A. Fenstamaker, widow of Isaac N Fenstamaker, first lieutenant Company F, Sixty-third Regiment of Pennsylvania Volunteers.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

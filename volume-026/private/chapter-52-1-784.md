# Chapter 784

> An Act to increase the pension of Cornelia R. Chandler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to increase the pension now allowed by law to Cornelia R. Chandler, widow of the late Rear-Admiral Chandler, from thirty to fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

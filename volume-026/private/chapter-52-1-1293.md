# Chapter 1293

> An Act granting a pension to Martha A. Bowling.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of Américain Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Martha A. Bowling, widow of the late Colonel William Bowling, at the rate of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, October 1, 1890.*

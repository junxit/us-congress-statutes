# Chapter 1031

> An Act restoring Rebecca Young to the pension-rolls.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-rolls the name of Rebecca Young, surviving widow of James Herald, late of the United States Navy, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, September 27, 1890.*

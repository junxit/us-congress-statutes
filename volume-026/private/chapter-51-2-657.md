# Chapter 657

> An Act to increase the pension of Colonel Everard Bierer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Colonel Everard Bierer, late colonel of One hundred and seventy-first Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers, to thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

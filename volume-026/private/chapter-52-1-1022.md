# Chapter 1022

> An Act granting a pension to Isaac Riseden.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Isaac Riseden on the pension-rolls and pay him a pension as first lieutenant Company E, Eleventh Regiment Tennessee Cavalry.


*Approved, September 27, 1890.*

# Chapter 399

> An Act granting an increase of pension to John D. Terry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to increase the pension of John D. Terry, formerly a sergeant of Company E, Twenty-third Regiment of Massachusetts Volunteers, and also a first lieutenant and regimental quartermaster Thirty-fifth Regiment United States Colored Troops, to forty-five dollars per month;


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

# Chapter 769

> An Act granting a pension to Mary L. Radford, widow of William Radford, late rear-admiral, United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to cause to be placed on the pension-roll of the United States the name of Mary L. Rad-ford, widow of William Radford, late a rear-admiral in, and for forty-five years in the active service of, the United States Navy, and to pay to the said Mary L. Radford, out of the naval pension fund, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the sum of fifty dollars per month from and after the approval of this act.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

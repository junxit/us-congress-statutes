# Chapter 725

> An Act to increase the pension of Ziba Yarnell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ziba Yarnell, Sixth Battery Ohio Light Artillery Volunteers, at the rate of fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, August 6, 1890.*

# Chapter 783

> An Act to increase the pension of Mrs Henrietta O, Whitaker

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be and lie hereby is, authorized and directed to pay to Mrs. Henrietta O. Whitaker a pension of one hundred dollars a month, in lieu of the amount now paid her, the said Henrietta O. Whitaker being the widow of General Walter C. Whitaker.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

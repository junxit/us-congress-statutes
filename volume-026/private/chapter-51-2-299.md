# Chapter 299

> An Act granting an increase of pension to General W. H. Powell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of W. H. Powell, late brigadier-general and brevet major-general of volunteers, at the rate of seventy-two dollars per month, in lieu of a pension which he now receives.


*Approved, February 24, 1891.*

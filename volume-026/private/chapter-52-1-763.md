# Chapter 763

> An Act granting a pension to George E. Taylor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary . of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension-roll the name of George E. Taylor, late of Company B, Twenty-first Regiment Michigan Infantry Volunteers, and to pay him a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month, on account of total blindness, the same to be in lieu of all other pensions claimed by him.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

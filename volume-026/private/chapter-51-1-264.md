# Chapter 264

> An Act granting a pension to Hannah Ward.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Hannah Ward, widow of David A. Ward, late captain of Company A, Sixth Kentucky Cavalry, at the rate of twenty dollars per month, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, in lieu of the pension now received by her.


*Approved, May 21, 1890.*

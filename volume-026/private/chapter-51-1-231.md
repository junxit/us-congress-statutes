# Chapter 231

> An Act to increase the pension of William Hamill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of William Hamill, late sergeant of Company E, One hundred and seventeenth Regiment Illinois Infantry Volunteers, from eighteen dollars to thirty-six dollars per month.


*Approved, May 19, 1890.*

# Chapter 549

> An Act granting a pension to Sarah Ann Noe.

*Be it enacted by the, Senate, and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the g elision laws, the name of Sarah Ann Noe, widow of Charles L. Cutts, late private in Company A, Tenth Regiment Connecticut Volunteers.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

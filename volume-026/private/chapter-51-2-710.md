# Chapter 710

> An Act granting a pension to Fedolin Buckeviller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Fedolin Buckeviller, and pay him a pension, subject to the rules and restrictions of the pension laws.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

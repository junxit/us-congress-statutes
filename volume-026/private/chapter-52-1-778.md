# Chapter 778

> An Act for the relief of Hayden Sorter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Hayden Sorter, late a private in Company H, Forty-third Regiment Missouri Enlisted Militia, on the invalid pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws in relation to invalid pensions, on account of wounds received in battle during the late war of the rebellion.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

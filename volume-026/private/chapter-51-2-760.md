# Chapter 760

> An Act granting a pension to Caroline Reeble.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior'be, and he is hereby, authorized anti directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Caroline Reeble, mother of Edward feeble, late of Company I, Fourth Regiment Minnesota Volunteers.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

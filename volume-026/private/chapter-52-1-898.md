# Chapter 898

> An Act for the relief of Henrietta E. Boggs.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the passage of this act there be paid, out of the naval pension fund, to Henrietta E. Boggs, widow of the late Rear-Admiral Charles S. Boggs, United States Navy, the sum of fifty dollars per month duringTier widowhood, the same to be in lieu of her present pension.


*Approved, September 4, 1890.*

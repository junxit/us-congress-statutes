# Chapter 366

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Louisa M. Gordon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Mrs. Louisa M. Gordon, formerly widow of Monroe B. Pulliam, first lieutenant and commissary Eleventh Regiment of Kentucky Volunteer Cavalry, United States Army, on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 27, 1891.*

# Chapter 460

> An Act granting an increase of pension to John F. Chase.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to increase the pension of John F. Chase, of Augusta, Maine, late a member of the Fifth Maine Battery and Third Regiment of Maine Volunteers, to seventy-two dollars per month.


*Approved, June 20, 1890.*

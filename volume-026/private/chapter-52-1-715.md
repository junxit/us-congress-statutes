# Chapter 715

> An Act granting increase of pension to Mrs. Adelaide H. Woodall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House, of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie hereby is, authorized and directed to increase the pension to thirty dollars per month of Adelaide H. Woodall, widow of French B. Woodall, late colonel of the One hundred and fifty-first Regiment of Illinois Volunteers.


*Approved, July 22, 1890.*

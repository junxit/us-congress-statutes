# Chapter 159

> An Act increasing the pension of Catherine Doyle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Catherine Doyle, widow of Michael Doyle, late of Company B, First Regiment District of Columbia. Volunteer cavalry, to twenty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension now received by her.


*Approved, February 12, 1891.*

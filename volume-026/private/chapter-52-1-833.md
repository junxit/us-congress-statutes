# Chapter 833

> An Act granting a pension to G. L. Pease.

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws (but at a rate not lower than twenty-five dollars per month), the name of G. L. Pease, a survivor of the Black Hawk war.


*Approved, August 29, 1890.*

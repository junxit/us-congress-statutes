# Chapter 361

> An Act granting a pension to Mary H. Casler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary H. Casier, widow of Marcus R. Casier, late captain of Company ÏI, One hundred and twenty-first . New York Volunteers.


*Approved, May 27, 1890.*

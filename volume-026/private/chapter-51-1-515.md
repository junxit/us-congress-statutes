# Chapter 515

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Mary Shumway.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Shumway, widow of David A. Shumway, late of Company A, First Michigan Engineers and Mechanics’ Volunteers.


*Approved, June 21, 1890.*

# Chapter 246

> An Act granting a pension to Margaret P. Minteer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, instructed to place on the pension-rolls Margaret P. Minteer, of Crawfordsville, Iowa, widow of Alexander H. Minteer, late a corporal of Company I. Twenty-fifth Regiment of Iowa Infantry, and pay her a pension, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws.


*Approved, May 21, 1890.*

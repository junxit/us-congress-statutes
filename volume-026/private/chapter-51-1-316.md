# Chapter 316

> An Act granting a pension to Sarah Sheldon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah Sheldon, widow of Sheppard Sheldon, late of Company G, Thirty-second Regiment Wisconsin Volunteers.


*Approved, May 24, 1890.*

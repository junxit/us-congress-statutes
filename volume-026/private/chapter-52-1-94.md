# Chapter 94

> An Act granting a pension to Thomas Dennis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Thomas Dennis, late private of Company A, One hundred and twelfth Regiment of Illinois Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, April 17, 1890.*

# Chapter 335

> An Act to increase the pension of Mrs. Elizabeth R. Gordon, widow of Major George A. Gordon, Fifth Cavalry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Elizabeth R. Gordon, widow of Major George A. Gordon, Fifth Cavalry, to forty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension she is now receiving, to date from the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 25, 1891.*

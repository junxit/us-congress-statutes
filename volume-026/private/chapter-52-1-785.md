# Chapter 785

> An Act to increase the pension of Arnold Meyer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Arnold Meyer, late private in Company C, Fifteenth Regiment Missouri Volunteer Infantry, and to pay him a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

# Chapter 349

> An Act granting a pension to Henry H. Knopp.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Henry H. Knopp, late a member of the Ohio Militia, called into active service during the rebellion.


*Approved, February 27, 1891.*

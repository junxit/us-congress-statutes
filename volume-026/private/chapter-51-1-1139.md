# Chapter 1139

> An Act increasing the pension of James M. Monroe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension now granted to James M. Monroe, for “loss of left leg and part of right foot,” as a member of Company G. Forty-second Ohio Volunteer Infantry, be increased to forty dollars per month.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

# Chapter 1136

> An Act for the relief of William Clawson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to enter the name of William Clawson as a private of Company I. Fourth Regiment of Iowa Volunteer Infantry, upon the rolls of said company, mustered into the service August second, eighteen hundred and sixty-one. and honorably mustered out August eighth, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, and to issue to him an honorable discharge accordingly; and said Clawson shall be paid all the pay, allowances, and bounties due to a soldier regularly serving in said company between the dates aforesaid.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

# Chapter 732

> An Act increasing the pension of John P. Davis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary o£ the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of John P. Davis, late a sergeant of Company H, Twelfth Regiment New Hampshire Volunteers, from thirty dollars to forty dollars per month, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, August 13, 1890.*

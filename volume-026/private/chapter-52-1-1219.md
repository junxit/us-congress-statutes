# Chapter 1219

> An Act granting a pension to Helen A. Beebe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, at the rate of twelve dollars per month, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Helen A. Beebe, formerly Helen A. Stow, late a volunteer nurse in the Medical Department, United States Army, during the war of the rebellion.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

# Chapter 683

> An Act granting a pension to Mariah L. Pool.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mariah L. Pool, an army nurse during the late war of thé rebellion, and pay her a pension of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, July 11, 1890.*

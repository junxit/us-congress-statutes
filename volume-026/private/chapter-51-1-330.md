# Chapter 330

> An Act for relief of Thomas N. Maxwell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Thomas N. Maxwell, late of Company I, Ninety-eighth Illinois Infantry, at the rate of fifty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, May 24, 1890.*

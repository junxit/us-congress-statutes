# Chapter 1018

> An Act granting a pension to Sophia J. Dimick.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations and provisions oi the pension laws, the name of Sophia J. Dimick, widow of Edwin W. Dimick. late of Company A, One hundred and sixth New York Volunteers.


*Approved, September 27, 1890.*

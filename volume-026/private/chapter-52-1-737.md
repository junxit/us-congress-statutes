# Chapter 737

> An Act granting a pension to Hannah Leo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Hannah Leo, widow of John Leary, late a first sergeant in Battery F, Third Artillery, United States Army, in the war of the rebellion, and pay her at the rate of twelve dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

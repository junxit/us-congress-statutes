# Chapter 862

> An Act granting a pension to Caroline Huddell White.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Caroline Huddell White, widow of George B. White, late captain United States Navy and Chief of Bureau of Yards and Docks, Navy Department, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, September 2, 1890.*

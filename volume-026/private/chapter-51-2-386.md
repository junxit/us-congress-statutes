# Chapter 386

> An Act granting an increase of pension to James McLaughlin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of James McLaughlin, late captain of Company I, Tenth Regiment Kansas State Militia, and pay him a pension of twenty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving under act of Congress approved August twenty-first, eighteen hundred and eighty-eight.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

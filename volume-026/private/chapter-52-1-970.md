# Chapter 970

> An Act granting a pension to Mary A. Hooke.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the. United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior'be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary A. Hooke, as widow of William H. Hooke, late a private in Company A, Eleventh New Hampshire Volunteers.


*Approved, September 26, 1890.*

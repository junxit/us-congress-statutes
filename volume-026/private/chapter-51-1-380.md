# Chapter 380

> An Act to pension Mrs. Theodora M. Piatt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Theodora M. Piatt, widow of Benjamin I. Piatt, late a captain and assistant adjutant-general of United States Volunteers.


*Approved, May 27, 1890.*

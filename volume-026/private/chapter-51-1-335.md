# Chapter 335

> An Act to pension Rebecca Bolerjack.

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Rebecca Bolerjack, mother of Presley F. Bolerjack, late a member of the Twenty-ninth Illinois Regiment,


*Approved, May 24, 1890.*

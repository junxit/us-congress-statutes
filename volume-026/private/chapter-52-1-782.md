# Chapter 782

> An Act to increase the pension of Edward P. Quinn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to increase the pension of Edward P. Quinn, late lieutenant Company D, One Hundred and twenty-third New York Volunteers, to sixty dollars a month.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

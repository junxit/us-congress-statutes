# Chapter 392

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Edwin H. Dill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, of Edwin H. Dill, late a private in Company K, Twenty-third Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, to thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

# Chapter 224

> An Act to pension James Murphy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-rolls the name of James Murphy, late of Captain James Ford’s company, Colonel Dodge’s regiment, in the Black Hawk war. at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, February 14, 1891.*

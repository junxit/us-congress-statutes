# Chapter 624

> An Act granting a pension to Harriet N. Read.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Harriet N. Read, a resident of Dimondale, Michigan, at the rate of twelve dollars per month, on account of disability resulting from disease contracted while serving as a hospital nurse during the war of the rebellion


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

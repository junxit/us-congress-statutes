# Chapter 450

> An Act to place the name of John W. Younger on the pension-roll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John W. Younger, late of Company E, Forty-eight Enrolled Missouri Militia.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

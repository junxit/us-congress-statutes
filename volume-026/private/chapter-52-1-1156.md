# Chapter 1156

> An Act granting a pension to Micager Hancock.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Micager Hancock on the pension-roll, at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month he being a soldier in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

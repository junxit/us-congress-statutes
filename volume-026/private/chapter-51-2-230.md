# Chapter 230

> An Act granting increase of pension to Robert Cullen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Robert Cullen, late captain Company I, Seventy-fourth Ohio Infantry, on the pension rolls, and pay him a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month, in lieu of the sum now paid him.


*Approved, February 14, 1891.*

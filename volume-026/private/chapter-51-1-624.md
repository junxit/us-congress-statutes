# Chapter 624

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Sallie H. Wilson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Sallie H. Wilson, widow of Isaac H. Wilson, late sergeant in Company G, Seventy-seventh Regiment New York State Volunteers.


*Approved, June 25, 1890.*

# Chapter 1105

> An Act granting a pension to Emma G. Clark.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name Emma G. Clark, widow of First Lieutenant William T. Clark, Company E, One hundred and forty-eighth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

# Chapter 333

> An Act to increase the pension of Robert Hall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension now allowed to Robert Hall, of Shelbyville, Missouri, late a soldier in the Mexican war under certificate numbered nine hundred and eighty-two, and pay him the sum of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1891.*

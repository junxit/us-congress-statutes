# Chapter 1102

> An Act granting a pension to Lewis W. Bloom, of Etna, Kansas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lewis W. Bloom, late a member of the Fifth Ohio Light Artillery.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

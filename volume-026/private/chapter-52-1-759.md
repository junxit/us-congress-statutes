# Chapter 759

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Martha E. Grant.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Martha E. Grant, widow of Augustine L. Grant, of the County of Fulton, Georgia. Said Augustine L. Grant, was a private in the company of Captain John A. Urquhart, in the Creek Indian war of eighteen hundred and thirty-six, said company being composed of Georgia troops, and called Columbus Guards.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

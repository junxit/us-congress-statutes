# Chapter 89

> An Act for the relief of Major Joseph W. Wham, paymaster United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers, in settling and adjusting the accounts of Major Joseph W. Wham, paymaster United States Army, are hereby directed to credit said Major Joseph W. Wham, paymaster United States Army, with the sum of twenty-eight thousand three hundred and forty-five dollars and ten cents, Government funds, of which he was robbed in Graham County, Arizona Territory, on the eleventh of May, eighteen hundred and eighty-nine, without his default.


*Approved, January 21, 1891.*

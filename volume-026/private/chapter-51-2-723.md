# Chapter 723

> An Act granting a pension to Carrie F. Ogden and Susan Ogden.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Carrie F. Ogden and Susan Ogden, dependent sisters of Richard P. Ogden, late a sergeant in Company F, Fifth Regiment New Jersey Infantry, at the rate of twelve dollars per month, and in case of the death of either of said sisters the full sum shall be paid the survivor.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

# Chapter 546

> An Act granting a pension to J. N. Jordan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is. authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of J. N. Jordan, late a member of Company H, Fourteenth Illinois Cavalry Volunteers.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

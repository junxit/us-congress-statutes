# Chapter 97

> An Act granting a pension to Catherine Morris.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Catherine Morris, mother of James A. Richards, late private of Company B, Eighteenth Regiment Indiana Infantry.


*Approved, April 17, 1890.*

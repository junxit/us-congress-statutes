# Chapter 692

> An Act granting a pension to Lucy A. Coffield.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior he, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lucy A. Coffield, an army nurse, and pay her a pension of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, July 11, 1890.*

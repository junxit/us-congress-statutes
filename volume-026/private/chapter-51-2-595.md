# Chapter 595

> An Act granting a pension to Drusilla A. Sherwood.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Drusilla A. Sherwood, late a nurse in the United States hospital at Keokuk, Iowa, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

# Chapter 404

> An Act for the relief of Caroline M. McDougal, widow of the late Rear-Admiral David McDougal, United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there shall be paid, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, to Caroline M. McDougal, as the widow of the late Rear-Admiral David McDougal, of the United States Navy, the difference between the pay of a commodore on the retired list and the pay of a rear-admiral on the retired list from and after the twenty-fourth day of August, eighteen hundred and seventy-three, the date of the said McDougal's commission as a rear-admiral, to August twentieth, eighteen hundred and eighty-two, the date of his death.


*Approved, June 9, 1890.*

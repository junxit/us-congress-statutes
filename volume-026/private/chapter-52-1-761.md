# Chapter 761

> An Act granting a pension to Mary Webster.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll the name of Mary Webster, late a hospital nurse, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

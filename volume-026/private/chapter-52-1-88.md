# Chapter 88

> An Act granting an increase of pension to General Horace Boughton

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Horace Boughton, late a brevet brigadier- general in the Army of the United States, at the rate of one hundred dollars per month; said pension to be in lieu of that which he now receives.


*Approved, April 16, 1890.*

# Chapter 384

> An Act granting a pension to Amelia M. McAllister.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Amelia M. McAllister, widow of the late Colonel Julian McAllister, United States Army, at the rate of fifty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension which she now receives.


*Approved, May 28, 1890.*

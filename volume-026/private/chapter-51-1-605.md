# Chapter 605

> An Act to increase the pension of William P. Squire.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William P. Squire, late of Company C, Seventy-fifth Illinois Volunteer Infantry, at the rate of sixty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

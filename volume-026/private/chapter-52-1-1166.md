# Chapter 1166

> An Act granting a pension to Catherine S. Lawrence.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and. House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Catherines. Lawrence, and pay her a pension as an Army nurse of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

# Chapter 572

> An Act granting a pension to Catharine Talkington.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the Eension laws, the name of Catharine Talkington, of White County, llinois, mother of Daniel Bruder, late a member of Company H, Forty-eighth Regiment of Illinois.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

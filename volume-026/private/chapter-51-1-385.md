# Chapter 385

> An Act granting a pension to Mary O. Hall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United, States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior he, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Mary 0. Hall, widow of Augustus J. Hall, late of Company D, Twelfth Maine Volunteers, on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations of the pension law.


*Approved, May 28, 1890.*

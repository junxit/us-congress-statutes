# Chapter 1150

> An Act granting a pension to Ellen Courtney.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United. States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ellen Courtney, mother of Cornelius Courtney, Company M. Fifth Ohio Cavalry.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

# Chapter 964

> An Act granting a pension to Mary White.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary White, widow of Daniel White, late of Captain Dunseomb's Company, of New York Militia, war of eighteen hundred and twelve.


*Approved, September 26, 1890.*

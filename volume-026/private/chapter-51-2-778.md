# Chapter 778

> An Act to increase the pension of Francis A. Gaskill, Company K, Sixth New Jersey Volunteer Infantry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to in-crease the pension of Francis A. Gaskill, late a private in Company K, Sixth Regiment New Jersey Volunteers, at the rate of forty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

# Chapter 699

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Henry G. Healy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Henry G. Healy, late lieutenant- colonel of the Sixty-fifth New York Volunteers, at the increased rate of fifty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws


*Approved, July 11, 1890.*

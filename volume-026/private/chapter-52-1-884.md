# Chapter 884

> An Act granting a pension to Daniel Donovan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Daniel Donovan, dependent father of Jeremiah Donovan, late companies F and G, Ninety-ninth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers.


*Approved, September 4, 1890.*

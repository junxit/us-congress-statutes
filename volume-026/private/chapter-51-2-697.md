# Chapter 697

> An Act for the relief of Margaret Malloy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws, the name of Margaret Malloy, as dependent mother of James Malloy, late of Company D, Third Massachusetts Cavalry.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

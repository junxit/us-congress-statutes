# Chapter 212

> An Act for the relief of Eliza Pyle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed toçlace on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Eliza Pyle, late a nurse in the hospital of the Fortieth Illinois Regiment Volunteers, at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, February 14, 1891.*

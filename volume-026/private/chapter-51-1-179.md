# Chapter 179

> An Act providing a pension for Matilda C. King.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, at the rate of fifty dollars per month, the name of Matilda C. King, widow of the late'Colonel John H. King, brevet major-general of the United States Army.


*Approved, April 30, 1890.*

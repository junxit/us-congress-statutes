# Chapter 651

> An Act for the relief of James A. Rice.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, directed to remove the charge of desertion from the record of James A. Rice, late a private in Company F, Seventh Kentucky Infantry Volunteers, and to issue to him ail honorable discharge as of the seventh day of April, eighteen hundred and sixty-two.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

# Chapter 930

> An Act for the relief of Christian Fredericksen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to issue to Christian Fredericksen, who served under the name of N. T. Wisborg as a private in Company G, Ninth Kansas Volunteers, an honorable discharge from the service of the United States, upon the surrender of the discharge issued Neil P. Wisborg, or of satisfactory proof of its loss, and upon evidence satisfactory to the War Department of the identity of Christian Fredericksen with said Wisborg.


*Approved, September 25, 1890.*

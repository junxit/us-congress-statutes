# Chapter 429

> An Act granting a pension to Walter Scott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension roll of the United States the name of Walter Scott, formerly a private in Captain William Robinson’s company, General Benjamin Patterson’s command, in the Creek war. at the rate of twelve dollars per month, subject to the rules and regulations governing pensions.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

# Chapter 766

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Mrs. S. J. Rayner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to in-crease the pension of Mrs. S. J. Rayner, widow of Reverend James 0. Rayner, late Chaplain United States Army, from twenty dollars per month to thirty dollars per month, to take effect from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

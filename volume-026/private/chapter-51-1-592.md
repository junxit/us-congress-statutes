# Chapter 592

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Charles, H. Moore.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Charles H. Moore, late private Company H. Eleventh Connecticut Volunteers, be, and he is hereby, granted and allowed, from and after the passage of this act, a pension at the rate of twenty five dollars a month; and the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of said Charles H. Moore on the pension-roll at said rate, in lieu of the pension now paid him.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

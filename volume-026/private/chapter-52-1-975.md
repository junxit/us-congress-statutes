# Chapter 975

> An Act granting a pension to Carrie M. Miller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions ami limitations of the pension laws, the name of Carrie M. Miller, widow of Chauncy Miller, late of the Nineteenth Illinois Infantry and Fourteenth Illinois Cavalry.


*Approved, September 26, 1890.*

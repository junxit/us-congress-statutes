# Chapter 1182

> An Act for the relief of Fanny A. Putney.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Fanny A. Putney, widow of Elum Putney, formerly a member of Captain Thaddeus Sheldon’s company of New York Militia, war of eighteen hundred and twelve.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

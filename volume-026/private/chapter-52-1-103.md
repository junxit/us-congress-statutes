# Chapter 103

> An Act for the relief of Eliza Stanton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Eliza Stanton, mother of Lafayette C. Stanton, late of Company A, One hundred and forty-fifth Regiment of Indiana. Volunteers, on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations and provisions of the general pension laws.


*Approved, April 21, 1890.*

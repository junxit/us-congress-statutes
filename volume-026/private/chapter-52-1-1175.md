# Chapter 1175

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Maria L. McCulloh.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs, Maria L. McCulIoh, widow of J. Floyd McCulIoh, late captain Company I, Second Regiment Potomac Home Brigade, Maryland Volunteers.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

# Chapter 738

> An Act granting a pension to George Obergfell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of George Obergfell. late a private of Company E, Fifth Regiment of Pennsylvania Cavalry Volunteers, on the pension-rolls, and to pay him a pension, subject to the rules and regulations of the Pension Bureau.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

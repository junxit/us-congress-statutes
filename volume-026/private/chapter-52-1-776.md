# Chapter 776

> An Act for the relief of Mrs. Angeline Green.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws the name of Mrs. Angeline Greene, dependent mother of Edwin Spurgin. late of Company C, Twenty-seventh Indiana Volunteers; Alexander Greene, of Company , Seventy-third Indiana Volunteers, and Allen Greene, of Company D of the Sixty-seventh Indiana Volunteers, and William Greene, of Company C of the Twenty-seventh Indiana Volunteers, and Alfred Greene, of Company M of Twenty-first Indiana Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

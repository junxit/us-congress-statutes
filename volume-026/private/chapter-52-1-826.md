# Chapter 826

> An Act granting a pension to Nancy A. Thornton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations oi the pension laws, the name of Nancy A. Thornton, widow of John W. Thornton, deceased.


*Approved, August 29, 1890.*

# Chapter 780

> An Act to pension John B. Angell, father of James C. Angell, alias John McShier, late of Company G, Fifth Pennsylvania Cavalry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and instructed to place upon the pension rolls the name of John B. Angell, father of late James C. Angell, alias John McShier, of Company G, Fifth Regiment-Pennsylvania Cavalry, at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

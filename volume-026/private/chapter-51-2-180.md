# Chapter 180

> An Act granting a pension to Alexander Moore.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place, on the pension-rolls, at the rate of twelve dollars per month from the date of approval hereof, the name of Alexander Moore, late nurse of Company H, Twenty-fourth Regiment Indiana Volunteers, war of the rebellion.


*Approved, February 14, 1891.*

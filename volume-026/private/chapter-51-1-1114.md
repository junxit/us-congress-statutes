# Chapter 1114

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Michael Hargain.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Michael Hargain, late a seaman on the United States schooner Otsego, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars a month, in lieu of the amount now paid him.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

# Chapter 800

> An Act to increase the pension of Zo S. Cook, of Wilcox County, Alabama.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension heretofore granted by act of Congress to Zo S. Cook, of Wilcox County, Alabama, be increased to the sum of fifty dollars per month from the date of passage of this act.


*Approved, August 18, 1890.*

# Chapter 618

> An Act granting a pension to Mary W. Smalley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary W. Smalley, step-mother of Jonas Smalley, late a private in Company K, Twenty-first Michigan Volunteers, as if she was the mother of said Jonas Smalley.


*Approved, June 25, 1890.*

# Chapter 483

> An Act granting a pension to Mary Murphy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Murphy, widow of Jeremiah Murphy, late of Company D, One hundred and fifty-fifth New York Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, June 21, 1890.*

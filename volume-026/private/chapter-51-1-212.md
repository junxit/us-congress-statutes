# Chapter 212

> An Act to increase the pension of Samuel Adams.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Samuel Adams, United States naval forces, war with Mexico, from eight dollars per month to thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, May 15, 1890.*

# Chapter 378

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Caroline G. Seyfforth.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-rolls, subject to the provisions of the pension laws, the name of Caroline G. Seyfforth, widow of the late Edmund Seyfforth, a contract surgeon in the United States Army.


*Approved, May 27, 1890.*

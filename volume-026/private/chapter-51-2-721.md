# Chapter 721

> An Act granting a pension to Emily M. Tyler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Emily M. Tyler, widow of the late Erastus B. Tyler, a brevet major-general of the United States Volunteers, in the war for the Union, and pay her a pension from the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

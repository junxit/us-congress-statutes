# Chapter 742

> An Act granting a pension to Braddock F. Stocking.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Braddock F. Stocking, late of Company I. First Regiment Minnesota Mounted Rangers.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

# Chapter 1154

> An Act granting a pension to Anna McCreary.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Anna McCreary, widow of Robert McCreary, late private of Company D, One hundred and eighty-sixth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

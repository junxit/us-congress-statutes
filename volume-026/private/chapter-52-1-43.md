# Chapter 43

> An Act granting a pension to Mary E. Noll, widow of Philip Noll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary E. Noll, widow of Philip Noll, late private Company E, Ninth Regiment Missouri State Militia.


*Approved, March 19, 1890.*

# Chapter 660

> An Act granting an increase of pension to William Martin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and is hereby, authorized to place upon the pension rolls, the name of William Martin, of Fremont, Nebraska, a veteran of the Mexican war, and pay him a pension of fifty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving under certificate numbered forty-nine hundred and eighty-one.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

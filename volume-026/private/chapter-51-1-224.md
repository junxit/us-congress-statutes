# Chapter 224

> An Act granting a pension to Harman Day.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, at the rate of twelve dollars a month, the name of Harman Day, dependent father of Wesley H. Day, late of Company K, Eighth Vermont Volunteers.


*Approved, May 19, 1890.*

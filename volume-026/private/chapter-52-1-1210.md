# Chapter 1210

> An Act granting a pension to John W. Bennett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John W. Bennett, late an acting ensign in the United States Navy.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

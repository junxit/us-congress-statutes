# Chapter 365

> An Act granting a pension to Matilda M. Harriman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Matilda M. Harriman, widow of William Harriman, late of Company F, One hundred and seventieth Regiment of Ohio Volunteers.


*Approved, February 27, 1891.*

# Chapter 226

> An Act granting a pension to Allen Feathers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll of the United States the name of Allen Feathers, late a private in Company F, Third Regiment New York Cavalry, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, May 19, 1890.*

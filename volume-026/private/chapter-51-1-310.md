# Chapter 310

> An Act granting a pension to Esther G. Bryant.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is. directed and authorized to place the name of Esther G. Bryant, widow of John P. Bryant, late of Company B. Ninth Regiment of Vermont Volunteers, on the pension-roll, subject to the rules and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, May 24, 1890.*

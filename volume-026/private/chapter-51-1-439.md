# Chapter 439

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Henry Strawbridge.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Henry Straw-bridge. late a private in Company G. One hundred and thirteenth Regiment Ohio Volunteers, be, and he is hereby, granted and allowed, from and after the passage of this act, a pension at the rate of thirty- five dollars per month, and the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of said Henry Strawbridge on the pension-roll at said rate, in lieu of the pension now paid him.


*Approved, June 20, 1890.*

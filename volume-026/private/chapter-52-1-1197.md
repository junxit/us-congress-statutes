# Chapter 1197

> An Act granting a pension to Wick Morgan.

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Wick Morgan, late major of the Seventy-sixth Regiment of Enrolled Missouri Militia, and' late major of the Fifteenth Regiment of Missouri Cavalry Volunteers.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

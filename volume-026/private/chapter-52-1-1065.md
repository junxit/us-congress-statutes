# Chapter 1065

> An Act granting a pension to Louisa M. Sippell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Louisa M. Sippell, widow of William E. Sippell, of Company H, Thirteenth Regiment New Jersey Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

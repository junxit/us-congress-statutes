# Chapter 1163

> An Act granting a pension to Calvin Gunn

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll. subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Calvin Gunn, late a member of Captain Isaac G. Messer’s company of California Volunteers, in the Humboldt, California, Indian War of eighteen hundred and fifty-eight.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

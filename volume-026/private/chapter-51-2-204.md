# Chapter 204

> An Act granting a pension to Ann Maria Bullock Schram.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Ann Maria Bullock Schram, a resident of Fort Plain, New York, at the rate of twelve dollars per month, on account of disability resulting from disease contracted while serving as a hospital nurse during the war of the rebellion.


*Approved, February 14, 1891.*

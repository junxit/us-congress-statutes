# Chapter 747

> An Act granting a pension to Hannah Bedford.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House, of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Hannah Bedford, widow of James Bedford, late a musician of the Third Rhode Island Heavy Artillery.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

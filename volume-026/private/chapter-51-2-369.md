# Chapter 369

> An Act granting a pension to Millie A, Ritenow.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Millie A. Ritenour, a hospital nurse in the war of the rebellion, on the pension rolls, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 27, 1891.*

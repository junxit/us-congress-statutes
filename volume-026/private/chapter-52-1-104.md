# Chapter 104

> An Act for the relief of Mattie W. House.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the passage of this act there be paid out of the naval pension fund to Mattie W. House, widow of the late Lieutenant Jerome B. House, United States navy, deceased, the sum of forty dollars per month during her widowhood, the same to be in lieu of her present pension.


*Approved, April 21, 1890.*

# Chapter 417

> An Act for the relief of James W. Harvey, as assignee of Joseph Parkins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be, and is hereby, appropriated to James W. Harvey and the legal representative of Samuel Klauber, deceased, sureties upon bond executed by Joseph Parkins to the United States to secure performance of contract entered into by the Government with said Parkins November nineteenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of four thousand nine hundred and twenty-five dollars, the said sum having been expended by said Harvey and Klauber in performing said contract and in furnishing material thereunder, and never having been paid either to the contractor or said sureties.


*Approved, June 11, 1890.*

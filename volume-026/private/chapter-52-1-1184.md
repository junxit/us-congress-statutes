# Chapter 1184

> An Act to remove the charge of desertion against John Milroy, and authorizing his honorable discharge.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be. and he is hereby, authorized to remove the charge of desertion against John Milroy, late a private soldier in Company F, of the One hundred and fifty-fourth Regiment Indiana Volunteers and substitute therefor. Absent without leave since July eighth, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, to the muster out of his company,” and to issue to said soldier a discharge accordingly.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

# Chapter 444

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Jane Potts

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Jane Potts, widow of Noah Potts, late of Company D, Seventh Regiment Illinois Cavalry.


*Approved, June 20, 1890.*

# Chapter 564

> An Act granting a pension to Sarah Connally.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized to place upon the pension-roll the name of Sarah Connally, widow of Patrick Connally, late a member of Company K, Seventieth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension, subject to the restrictions and rules of the pension laws.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

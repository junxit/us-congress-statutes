# Chapter 518

> An Act granting a pension to Elizabeth Bennett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to place on the pension-roll the name of Elizabeth Bennett, mother of John Bennett, late a private in Company H. Seventh New York Heavy Artillery Volunteers, and to pay her a pension.


*Approved, June 21, 1890.*

# Chapter 616

> An Act granting a pension to Alvason Hopkins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior, be, and he is hereby, directed and authorized to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Alvason Hopkins, dependent father of lerman Hopkins, late a private in Company L, Sixth Michigan Cavalry Volunteers.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

# Chapter 767

> An Act pensioning Belinda Jane Phillips

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Belinda Jane Phillips, imbecile daughter of Isaih Phillips, late of Company F. Thirtieth Iowa Volunteer Infantry, and pay to her legally constituted guardian for her use a pension at the rate of eighteen dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

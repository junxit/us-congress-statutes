# Chapter 753

> An Act granting a pension to N. E. Palmer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, empowered and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of N. E. Palmer, late of Company K, One Hundredth Regiment Indiana Volunteers.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

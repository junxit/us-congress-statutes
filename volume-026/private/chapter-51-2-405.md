# Chapter 405

> An Act granting a pension to Lucy Wood.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lucy Wood, of Barre, Vermont, as widow of John Wood, a teamster in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve, and to pay her a pension of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

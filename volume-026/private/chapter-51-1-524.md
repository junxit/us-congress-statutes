# Chapter 524

> An Act granting a pension to Clara Frey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject, to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Clara Frey, widow of George Adam Frey, deceased, late of Company I. Fourth Regiment of Missouri Cavalry.


*Approved, June 21, 1890.*

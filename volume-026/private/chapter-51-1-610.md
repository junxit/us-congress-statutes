# Chapter 610

> An Act increasing the pension of Alonzo L. Page, late of Company B, Third Vermont Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-rolls, at forty-five dollars a month, the name of Alonzo L. Page, late of Company B, Third Vermont Volunteers.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

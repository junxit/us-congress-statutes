# Chapter 93

> An Act granting a pension to Daniel A. Culman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the Emsion laws, tire name of Daniel A, Culman, late of Company B, leventh Regiment New Jersey Volunteers.


*Approved, April 17, 1890.*

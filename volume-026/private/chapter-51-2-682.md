# Chapter 682

> An Act to authorize the Secretary of War to cause to be mustered William P. Atwell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and is hereby, authorized to cause to be mustered William P. Atwell as captain of Company G, Thirty-seventh Wisconsin Volunteer Regiment of Infantry, to date November eighteenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, and that the Secretary of War give to him an honorable discharge as Captain of said Company and Regiment to date November twenty-two, eighteen hundred and sixty-four.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

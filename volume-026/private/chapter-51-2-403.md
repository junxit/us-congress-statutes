# Chapter 403

> An Act granting a pension to Laura A. Burns.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Laura A. Burns, widow of P. Ross Burns, deceased, late captain of Topeka, Battery A, Second Regiment Kansas State Militia Volunteers.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

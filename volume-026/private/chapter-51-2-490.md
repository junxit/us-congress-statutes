# Chapter 490

> An Act granting a pension to Frederick B. Sells.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Frederick B. Sells, late of Company E, hie hundred and forty-eighth Regiment of Illinois Volunteers.

# Chapter 583

> An Act granting a pension to Emma Fulton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, instructed and directed to place upon the pension-rolls, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Emma Fulton, widow of George Fulton, late a member of Company C, Fifth. Michigan Cavalry.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

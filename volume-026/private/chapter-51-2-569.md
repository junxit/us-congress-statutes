# Chapter 569

> An Act granting a pension to Fanny L. Davis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Fanny L. Davis, widow of Rear-Admiral John Lee Davis, late of the United States Navy, at the rate of fifty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

# Chapter 1169

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Emeline Jane Bushnell

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Emetine Jane Bushnell on the pension-roll as a nurse, at the rate of twelve dollars a month.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

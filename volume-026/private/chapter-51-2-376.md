# Chapter 376

> An Act to increase the pension of Mrs. Mary Jane Mallory.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Mrs. Mary Jane Mallory from twelve to twenty-five dollars per month. She is the widow of a soldier of the war of eighteen hundred and twelve she is eighty-two years of age and has been confined to her bed for five years, and is totally blind.


*Approved, February 27, 1891.*

# Chapter 575

> An Act granting a pension to Keziah Randall, Mattapoisett, Massachusetts, widow of Richard Randall, who served in the coast guard, eighteen hundred and twelve to eighteen hundred and fifteen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is. authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Keziah Randall, of Mattapoisett. Massachusetts, widow of Richard Randall, who served as coast guard in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve to eighteen hundred and fifteen, and allow her a pension of twenty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

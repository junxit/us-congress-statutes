# Chapter 1183

> An Act for the relief of Uriah Bryant.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the. name of Uriah Bryant, late a soldier in the Florida war in Captain N. G. Foster’s company and Colonel Cooper’s regiment of Georgia volunteers, and that lie be paid a pension under the provisions of the pension laws.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

# Chapter 375

> An Act granting a pension to Joanna W. Turner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Joanna W. Turner, widow of William D. Turner, late surgeon Ninety-seventh Illinois Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month, from the pas-sage of this act, in lieu of the pension now received by her.


*Approved, May 27, 1890.*

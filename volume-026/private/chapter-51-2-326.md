# Chapter 326

> An Act granting a pension to Mary S. Wheeler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to restore to the pension roll the name of Mary S. Wheeler, widow of Ambrose M. Burleson, late private of Company I. Fourth Regiment Michigan Cavalry, and pay her a pension hereafter at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1891.*

# Chapter 733

> An Act increasing the pension of Thompson N. Statham.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Thompson N. Statham, a soldier of the Florida Indian War of eighteen hundred and thirty-seven and eighteen hundred and thirty-eight, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, August 13, 1890.*

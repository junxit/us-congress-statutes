# Chapter 603

> An Act to grant a pension to John B. Vile

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension-roll the name of John B. Vile, late of Companies C and M, First Michigan Cavalry, and pay him a pension of twenty-five dollars per month, the same to be in lieu of any other pension by virtue of any law of the United States.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

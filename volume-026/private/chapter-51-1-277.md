# Chapter 277

> An Act granting a pension to Eliza Marcy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and lie hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Eliza Marcy, mother of Samuel L. Marcy, ate private in Company G, Eighteenth Connecticut Volunteers.


*Approved, May 22, 1890.*

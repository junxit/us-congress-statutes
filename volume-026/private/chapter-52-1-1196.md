# Chapter 1196

> An Act granting a pension to Betsey A. Mower.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Betsey A. Mower, widow of General Joseph A. Mower, United States Army, and pay her at the rate of one hundred dollars per month.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

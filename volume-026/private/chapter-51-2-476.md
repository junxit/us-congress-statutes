# Chapter 476

> An Act granting a pension to Mary Brooks, army nurse.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the. United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Brooks, late volunteer army nurse, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

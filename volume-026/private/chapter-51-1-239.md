# Chapter 239

> An Act granting a pension to Mary A. Holland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Mary A. Holland, an army nurse, and to pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, May 21, 1890.*

# Chapter 1191

> An Act granting a pension to Frederick H. Macke.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Frederick H. Macke, late of Company A, (Battery) Second Regiment Kansas State Militia.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

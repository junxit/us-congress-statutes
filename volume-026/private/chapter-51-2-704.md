# Chapter 704

> An Act for the relief Martha J. Morrison

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension of Martha J. Morrison, widow of George S. Morrison, deceased, late a private in Pike’s company in the First Regiment of Arkansas Cavalry in the Mexican War, be, and the same is hereby, increased to thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

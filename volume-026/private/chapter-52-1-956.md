# Chapter 956

> An Act granting a pension to Thomas H. Hopkins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and. House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Thomas H. Hopkins, late a private Company K, Second Regiment Illinois Cavalry.


*Approved, September 26, 1890.*

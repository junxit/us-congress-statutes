# Chapter 503

> An Act granting a pension to Benjamin B. Houghkirk.

*Be it enacted by the Senate anti House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Benjamin B. Houghkirk, late of Company E, One hundred and twenty-second Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, fit the rate of fifty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 2, 1891.*

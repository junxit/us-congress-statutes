# Chapter 158

> An Act for the relief of William L. Hurst, of Wolfe County, Kentucky.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed and authorized to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William L. Hurst, of Wolfe County, Kentucky.


*Approved, February 12, 1891.*

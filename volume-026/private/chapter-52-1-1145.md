# Chapter 1145

> An Act to increase the pension of Asenath Turner, a Revolutionary pensioner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Asenath Turner, widow of Samuel Dunham, late a Revolutionary soldier, from twelve dollars per month to thirty dollars per month during her natural life, the same to commence from the taking effect of this act.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

# Chapter 668

> An Act for removal of charge of desertion from the record of Frederick Whitehead

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War he, and hereby is, authorized and directed to remove from the record of Frederick Whitehead, late of Company B. First New York Mounted Rifles, the charge of desertion now standing against him, and substitute that he was discharged by decree of Court on account of being a minor under eighteen years of age, as of date January twenty-first, eighteen hundred and sixty-three


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

# Chapter 1081

> An Act to increase the pension of George H. Brown, Company I, Sixth Vermont Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the. Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-rolls, at the rate of forty-five dollars a month, the name of George H. Brown, late of Company I, Sixth Vermont Volunteers.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

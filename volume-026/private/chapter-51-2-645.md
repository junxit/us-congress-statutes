# Chapter 645

> An Act for the relief of Alfred Crayton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, directed to remove the charge of desertion now standing on the rolls against the name of Alfred Crayton, late of Company B, of Eleventh Illinois Infantry, and substitute therefor : Absented himself without leave on October fifth, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, and re-enlisted on October twenty-third, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, in Company A, One hundred and sixth Illinois Volunteers, and was honorably mustered out.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

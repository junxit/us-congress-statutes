# Chapter 376

> An Act granting a pension to Miss Charlotte Bradford.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Charlotte Bradford, a nurse in the late war of the rebellion, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars a month.


*Approved, May 27, 1890.*

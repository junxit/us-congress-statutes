# Chapter 48

> An Act increasing the pension of Eliza B. Dorrance, widow of the late George W, Dorrance, chaplain United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the passage of this act there be allowed and paid to Mrs. Eliza B. Dorrance, widow of the late George W. Dorrance, chaplain United States Navy, a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month during her widowhood, in lieu of the pension she is now receiving


*Approved, January 2, 1891.*

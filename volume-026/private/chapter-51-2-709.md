# Chapter 709

> An Act granting a pension to Ozro Harrington.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, the name of Ozro Harrington, late a private in Company H, First Regiment Iowa Cavalry Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

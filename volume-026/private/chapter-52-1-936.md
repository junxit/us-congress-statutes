# Chapter 936

> An Act restoring the pension of Mrs. Catharine Sonne.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to restore to the pension-roll the name of Mrs. Catharine Sonne, dependent mother of William H. Sonne, late a private of Company H, Fifth Kentucky Volunteers, to take effect from the passage of this act


*Approved, September 25, 1890.*

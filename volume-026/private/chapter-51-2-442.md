# Chapter 442

> An Act granting a pension to George James.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension rolls the name of George James, of Long Plain, Massachusetts, late cooper on the whale-ship Edward, in the Mexican war, and pay him a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

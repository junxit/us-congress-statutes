# Chapter 587

> An Act granting a pension to Nancy M Gross.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America- in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Nancy M Gross, who was a nurse in the Second and Sixth Maine Regiments, eighteen hundred and sixty-one and eighteen hundred and sixty-two, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

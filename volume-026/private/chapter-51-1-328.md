# Chapter 328

> An Act for relief of William D. Hummer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William D. Hummer late a member of Company B, Fortieth Missouri Infantry.


*Approved, May 24, 1890.*

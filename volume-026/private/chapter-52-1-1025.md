# Chapter 1025

> An Act granting a pension to Clara Reed.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Clara Reed, late widow of George Dustin, a private in Company F, Twenty-third Wisconsin Volunteers.


*Approved, September 27, 1890.*

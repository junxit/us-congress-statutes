# Chapter 251

> An Act for granting a pension to Nelson G. Edwards.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-rolls, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Nelson G. Edwards late Company B, one hundred seventy-ninth New York Volunteers.


*Approved, May 21, 1890.*

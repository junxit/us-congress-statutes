# Chapter 467

> An Act to grant a pension to Nancy F. Glenn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Nancy F. Glenn, widow of Robert A. Glenn, who was a private in Captain William C. Rail’s company in the Fourth Regiment of Illinois Volunteers, which was commanded by Colonel Samuel M. Thompson in the Black Hawk war, and pay her a pension at twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

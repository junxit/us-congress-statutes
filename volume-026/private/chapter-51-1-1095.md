# Chapter 1095

> An Act to pension Susan E. Freeman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and the same is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Susan E. Freeman, of Mount Holly. Union County, Arkansas, widow of Roberson Freeman, late of Captain John Cunton’s company (Monroe Musketeers), Major Mark A. Cooper’s battalion, Georgia volunteers, Florida war. eighteen hundred and thirty-six, and allow her a pension at twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

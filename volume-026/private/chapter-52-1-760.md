# Chapter 760

> An Act granting a pension to William White.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William White, late of the Sixth Iowa Cavalry.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

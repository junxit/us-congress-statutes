# Chapter 707

> An Act for the relief of John F. Mahler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John F. Mahler, late a private of Company C, Fifty-sixth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

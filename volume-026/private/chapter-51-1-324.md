# Chapter 324

> An Act for the relief of Harriet. E. Brown.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior’ be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Harriet. E. Brown, widow of Eleazer H. Brown, late of Battery G, First Regiment Rhode Island Light Artillery Volunteers, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, May 24, 1890.*

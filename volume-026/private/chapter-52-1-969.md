# Chapter 969

> An Act granting a pension to Rebecca McDonald.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Rebecca McDonald, of Elkhart, Indiana, willow of Rufus McDonald, a soldier of the war of eighteen hundred and twelve, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, September 26, 1890.*

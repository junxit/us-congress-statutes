# Chapter 825

> An Act granting a pension to James A. Myers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate awl House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of James A. Myers, late of Company F, One hundred and forty-ninth Regiment of Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, August 29, 1890.*

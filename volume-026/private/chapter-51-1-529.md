# Chapter 529

> An Act granting a pension to Eva T. Blake.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That, the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws, the name of Eva T. Blake, widow of Andrew Blake, late a private of Company I. One hundred and fourth Pennsylvania Infantry; also of Company H, Ninety-eighth Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, June 21, 1890.*

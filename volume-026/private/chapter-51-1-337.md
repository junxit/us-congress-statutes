# Chapter 337

> An Act to pension William G. Hill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension-rolls of the Government the name of William G. Hill, later orderly sergeant of. Captain Jesse Walker’s Company, Oregon Volunteers, for meritorious service, and for wounds received in October, eighteen hundred and fifty-four, while engaged in battle with the Pi-Ute Indians in the Chewaucan Valley in Oregon, then a Territory, and allow him a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, May 24, 1890.*

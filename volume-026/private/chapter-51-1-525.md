# Chapter 525

> An Act granting a pension to John H. McLaughlin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John H. McLaughlin, late second-class fireman on the steamer Ida.


*Approved, June 21, 1890.*

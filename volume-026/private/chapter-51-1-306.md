# Chapter 306

> An Act granting a pension to Agnes Vetter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations or the pension laws, the name of Agnes Vetter, dependent mother of John M. Vetter, captain of Company F, Ninth Kentucky Infantry.


*Approved, May 24, 1890.*

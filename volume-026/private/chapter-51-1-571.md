# Chapter 571

> An Act granting a pension to Sarah A. Harrison

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah A. Harrison, widow of John Harrison, deceased, late second lieutenant of Company G, Seventy-ninth Regiment of Ohio Volunteers.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

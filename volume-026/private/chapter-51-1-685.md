# Chapter 685

> An Act granting a pension to Frederick Kidwiler

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Frederick Kidwiler


*Approved, July 11, 1890.*

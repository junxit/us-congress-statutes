# Chapter 430

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Nancy Springer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, instructed to place on the pension rolls Nancy Springer, of Spalding County, Georgia, widow of James M. Springer, late a private in Captain Mann’s company of Georgia troops, in the Creek Indian war of eighteen hundred and thirty-six, and pay her a pension at twelve dollars per month, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

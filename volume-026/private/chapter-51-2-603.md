# Chapter 603

> An Act granting a pension to John Yost.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Yost, late a member of Company I, Thirtieth Regiment Wisconsin Infantry Volunteers


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

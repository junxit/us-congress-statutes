# Chapter 295

> An Act granting a pension to Gustavus Alonzo Draper.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Gustavus Alonzo Draper, son of the late general Alonzo G. Draper, and pay to his legally constituted guardian a pension at the rate of eighteen dollars per'month.


*Approved, May 24, 1890.*

# Chapter 1024

> An Act granting a pension to Clark Stewart

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed and instructed to place upon the pension-rolls the name of Clark Stewart, late a private in Company D, Fifteenth Illinois Volunteers, at the rate of forty dollars a month, on account of total blindness, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving


*Approved, September 27, 1890.*

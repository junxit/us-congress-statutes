# Chapter 849

> An Act placing the name of Elizabeth Domm on the pension-rolls.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-rolls the name of Elizabeth Domm, widow of John L. Domm, deceased, late member of the Eleventh Ohio Battery, subject to the limitations and restrictions of the pension laws.


*Approved, August 30, 1890.*

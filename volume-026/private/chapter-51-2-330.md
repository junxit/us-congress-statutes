# Chapter 330

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Hugh McHugh.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to grant an increase of pension to Hugh McHugh, late second lieutenant of Company I, Eighty-fifth Regiment Illinois Infantry Volunteers, and to place his name upon the pension-roll, and to pay him thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1891.*

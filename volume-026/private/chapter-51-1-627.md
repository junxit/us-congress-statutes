# Chapter 627

> An Act for the relief of Norman Cleveland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be and he is hereby, authorized to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws the name of Norman Cleveland, late of Company G, First Ohio Light Artillery Volunteers.


*Approved, June 25, 1890.*

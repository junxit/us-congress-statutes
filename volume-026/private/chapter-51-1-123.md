# Chapter 123

> An Act granting a pension to Delia W. Marshall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Delia W. Marshall, widow of George A. Marshall, late a private in Company F. Second Regiment Vermont Volunteers, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, April 21, 1890.*

# Private Resolution 24

> Joint Resolution authorizing the heirs of Rear-Admiral Charles H. Baldwin to receive a snuff-box from the Czar of Russia.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the heirs of the late Rear-Admiral Charles H. Baldwin, United States Navy, be, and hereby are, authorized to receive the snuff-box set in diamonds presented to the rear-admiral by the Czar of Russia while he was special naval representative of the Government at the coronation of the Emperor in eighteen hundred and eighty-three.


*Approved, May 22, 1890.*

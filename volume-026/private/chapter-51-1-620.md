# Chapter 620

> An Act granting a pension to Archibald F. Coon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations of tin. pension laws, the name of Archibald F. Coon, late captain Company E, Fourth Pennsylvania Cavalry.


*Approved, June 25, 1890.*

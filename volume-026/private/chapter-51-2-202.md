# Chapter 202

> An Act granting a pension to Hannah L. Palmer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Hannah L. Palmer, who served as a hospital nurse during the war of the rebellion, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 14, 1891.*

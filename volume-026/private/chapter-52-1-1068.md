# Chapter 1068

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Emma A. Stafford.

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Mrs. Emma A. Stafford, of Des Moines. Iowa, a hospital nurse in the United States Army, and pay her a pension of twelve dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

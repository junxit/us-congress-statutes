# Chapter 362

> An Act granting a pension to John C. Abbott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John C. Abbott, late a private of Company Second Illinois Artillery.


*Approved, May 27, 1890.*

# Chapter 151

> An Act granting a pension to Joseph D Williamson

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Joseph D. Williamson, late private in Company C, One hundred and ninetieth Regiment Pennsylvania Veterans, and allow him a pension from the date of the filing of his claim in the Pension Office.


*Approved, April 22, 1890.*

# Chapter 743

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Zelinda Hill.

*Be it enacted by the. Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Zelinda Hill, mother of George B. Hill, late of Company A, Fifty-ninth Massachusetts Volunteers.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

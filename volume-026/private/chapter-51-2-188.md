# Chapter 188

> An Act granting a pension to Annie M. Kimball, widow of Alvah M. Kimball, Company H, Sixth New Hampshire Regiment Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

granting a pension to Annie M. Kimball, widow of Alvah M. Kimball, Company H, Sixth New Hampshire Regiment Volunteers. That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Annie M Kimball, -widow of Alvah M. Kimball, deceased, late quartermaster Sergeant of the Sixth New Hampshire Volunteers, at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 14, 1891.*

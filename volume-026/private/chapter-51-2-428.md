# Chapter 428

> An Act granting a pension to Caroline J. Craft.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Caroline J. Craft, a sister of David L. Craft, late of Company D, Eighth Pennsylvania Reserves, and Company K. Sixth Regiment United States Infantry, deceased, upon the pension rolls at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

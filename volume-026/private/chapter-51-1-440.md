# Chapter 440

> An Act granting a pension to Mary Ann Schirye.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Ann Schirye, widow of George Schirye, late of Company G, Fifth Regiment Pennsylvania Heavy Artillery.


*Approved, June 20, 1890.*

# Chapter 974

> An Act granting a pension to Mary A. Newcomb.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary A. Newcomb, a volunteer nurse in the late war, giving her during life the sum of twenty-four dollars a month, in lieu of the pension she now receives as the widow of her late husband, Hiram A. W. Newcomb, Company F, Eleventh Illinois Infantry.


*Approved, September 26, 1890.*

# Chapter 367

> An Act granting a pension to Nancy Shotwell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Mrs. Nancy Shotwell, late widow of Arthur Stotts, a soldier in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve, and pay her a pension, at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 27, 1891.*

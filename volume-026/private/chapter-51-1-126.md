# Chapter 126

> An Act granting a pension to Thomas Kelly.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Thomas Kelly, late of Company H, Second Regiment New York Heavy Artillery.


*Approved, April 21, 1890.*

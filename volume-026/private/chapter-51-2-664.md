# Chapter 664

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Rebecca P. Nields.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Rebecca P. Nields, widow of the late Lieutenant Commander Henry C. Nields. United States Navy, from thirty to fifty dollars per month, said increase to take effect from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

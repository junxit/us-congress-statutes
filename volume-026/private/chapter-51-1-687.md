# Chapter 687

> An Act granting a pension to Alice Nichols

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Alice Nichols, mother of Hanson Nichols, late a corporal in Company B, Seventh Regiment United States Colored Troops.


*Approved, July 11, 1890.*

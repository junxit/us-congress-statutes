# Chapter 274

> An Act granting a pension to Catharine Sapp.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Catharine Sapp, widow of Charles Sapp, deceased, late of Company G, Thirty-fifth Regiment of Illinois Volunteers.


*Approved, May 22, 1890.*

# Chapter 490

> An Act granting a pension to Seth M. Walter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension law, the name of Seth M. Walter, late private Company E, twenty-ninth Regiment Michigan Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, June 21, 1890.*

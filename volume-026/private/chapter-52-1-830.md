# Chapter 830

> An Act granting a pension to Anne Rodgers Macomb.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and ho is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Anne Rodgers Macomb, widow of the late John N. Macomb, a colonel in the Engineer Corps of the United States Army, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, August 29, 1890.*

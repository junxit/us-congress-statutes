# Chapter 738

> An Act granting a pension to Gottlieb Hunzicker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to put the name of Gottlieb Hunzicker, of Company A, One hundred and seventy-sixth Regiment New York Volunteers, on the pension-roll subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

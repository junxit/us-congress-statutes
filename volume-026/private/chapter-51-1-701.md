# Chapter 701

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Mrs. Sallie H. Michler, widow of the late Brevet Bregadier-General Nathaniel Michler, United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House, of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to cause the pension of Mrs Sallie H. Michler. widow of the late Brevet Brigadier-General Nathaniel Michler, United States Army, to be increased from thirty dollars per month, the rate she is now receiving, to fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, July 11, 1890.*

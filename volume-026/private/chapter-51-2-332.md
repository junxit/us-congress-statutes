# Chapter 332

> An Act to increase the pension of James A. Underwood.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to increase the pension of James A. Underwood, late of Company B. Eighth Indiana Volunteer Infantry, to fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1891.*

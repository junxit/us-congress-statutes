# Chapter 1119

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. A. W. Ackley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. A. W. Ackley, dependent mother of John T. Ackley, late a private in Company F. Fourth Regiment Michigan Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

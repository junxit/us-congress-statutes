# Chapter 731

> An Act granting a pension to Lettie E. Covell-Buckley, late a nurse in the war of the rebellion.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a pension is hereby granted to Lettie CovellBuckley, at the rate of twelve dollars per month, to commence from the passage of this act, and the Secretary of the Interior is instructed to place her name upon the pension roll in accordance with the provisions of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

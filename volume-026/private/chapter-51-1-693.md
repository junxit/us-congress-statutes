# Chapter 693

> An Act granting a pension to J. M. Stevens.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of J. M. Stevens, late private Company A, Forty-ninth Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, July 11, 1890.*

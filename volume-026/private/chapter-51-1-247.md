# Chapter 247

> An Act granting a pension to John Nagle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Nagle, late of Company C, Ninety-second Regiment of Illinois Infantry.


*Approved, May 21, 1890.*

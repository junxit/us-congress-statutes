# Chapter 799

> An Act to restore to the pension-roll the name of Mrs. Mary E. Boeke.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to restore to the pension-roll the name of Mrs. Mary E. Boeke, widow of Captain Francis Boeke, late captain of Company I, Fourteenth Illinois Cavalry.


*Approved, August 18, 1890.*

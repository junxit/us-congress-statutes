# Chapter 608

> An Act granting a pension to Joseph Dascomb.

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Joseph Dascomb, dependent father of Charles B. Dascomb, late a private in Company D, Fourth New Hampshire Infantry.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

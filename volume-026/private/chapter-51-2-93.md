# Chapter 93

> An Act granting a pension to Charlotte Stenger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Charlotte Stenger, widow of Jacob Stenger, deceased, late of Company C, First Regiment United States Mounted Riflemen.


*Approved, January 24, 1891.*

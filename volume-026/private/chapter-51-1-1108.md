# Chapter 1108

> An Act granting a pension to William Tolle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and required to place on the pension-roll William Tolle, father of Francis H. Tolle, late a member of Merritt’s Missouri Cavalry, and pay him a pension according to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

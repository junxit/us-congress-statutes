# Chapter 190

> An Act granting increase of pension to George L. Warren.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George L. Warren, late a corporal in Company C, Third Delaware Volunteers, and pay him at the rate of eighteen dollars per month in lieu of that which he is now receiving.


*Approved, May 3, 1890.*

# Chapter 585

> An Act granting a pension to Mary P. Thompson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Mary P. Thompson, widow of James B. Thompson, late captain of Company F, One hundred and ninetieth Regiment of Pennsylvania Volunteers.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

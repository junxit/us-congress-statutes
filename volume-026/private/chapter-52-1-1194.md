# Chapter 1194

> An Act granting a pension to Thomas H. Wilkerson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior he, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to tile provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Thomas H. Wilkerson, a private in the company of Captain John W. Biven's company of Arkansas Home Guards, attached to the First Regiment of Arkansas Cavalry.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

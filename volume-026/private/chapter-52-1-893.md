# Chapter 893

> An Act granting a pension to John McLaren.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior he, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John McLaren, late a private in Company K, Third Regiment United States Infantry, and in Captain Fell’s company. Seventh Independent Regiment Veteran Reserve Corps, and a sergeant in Company H, Third Regiment United States Infantry.


*Approved, September 4, 1890.*

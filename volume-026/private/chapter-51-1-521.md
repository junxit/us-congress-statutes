# Chapter 521

> An Act granting a pension to Isaac Moore.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and. House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Harriet McMann. widow of Lieutenant John T. McMann, late lieutenant, Company G. Twenty-first Michigan Infantry.


*Approved, June 21, 1890.*

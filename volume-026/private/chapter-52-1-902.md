# Chapter 902

> An Act granting increase of pension to Daniel H. Kent.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, at the rate of thirty-six dollars per month, in lieu of his present pension of twenty-four dollars per month, the name of Daniel H. Kent, late captain Company F, and brevet major and lieutenant-colonel of the Fourth Regiment Delaware Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, September 4, 1890.*

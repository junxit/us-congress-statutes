# Chapter 311

> An Act granting a pension to Jane Boswell Moore Bristor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll the name of Jane Boswell Moore Bristor, a field hospital nurse during the late war, at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1891.*

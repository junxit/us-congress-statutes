# Chapter 108

> An Act to authorize the Secretary of War to remove the charges of desertion from the records of the War Department as to Dudley Johnson, late ordnance sergeant, United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized to remove the charges of desertion from the records of the War Department as to Dudley Johnson, late ordnance sergeant. United States Army: Provided, That such removal of the charge of desertion shall not entitle the said Johnson to any pay or emoluments.


*Approved, January 26, 1891.*

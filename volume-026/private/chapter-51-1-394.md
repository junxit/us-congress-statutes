# Chapter 394

> An Act granting a pension to Mary Pollock.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations or the late pension laws, the name of Mary Pollock, an army nurse during the war of the rebellion, and pay her a pension of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, June 3, 1890.*

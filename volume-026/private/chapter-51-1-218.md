# Chapter 218

> An Act granting a pension to the widow of the late Commander Samuel H. Baker, United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, at the rate of fifty dollars per month, the name of Margaret T. Baker, widow of the late Commander Samuel H. Baker, United States Navy, the same to be in lieu of any pension, she is now receiving.


*Approved, May 16, 1890.*

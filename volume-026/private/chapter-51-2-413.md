# Chapter 413

> An Act granting a pension to B. Jones.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, at the rate of eight dollars per month, and subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of B. Jones, of South Carolina, late a private of Captain Chestnut’s company of mounted men, South Carolina Volunteers of the Florida war.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

# Chapter 515

> An Act to grant a pension to Nancy Jane Knetsar, of Moline, Illinois.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Nancy Jane Knetsar, widow of James Knetsar, who was a soldier in Captain Daniel Powell’s company in the first regiment, commanded by Colonel Hosea Pierce, in the Black Hawk war, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 2, 1891.*

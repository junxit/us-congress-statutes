# Chapter 888

> An Act granting a pension to Rozalia Junk.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Rozalia Junk, widow of John Junk, alias John Younge, late a private in Company K of the Sixth Regiment of Wisconsin Volunteers.


*Approved, September 4, 1890.*

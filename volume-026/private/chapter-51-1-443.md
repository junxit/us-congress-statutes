# Chapter 443

> An Act granting a pension to Margaret Stewart.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-rolls the name of Margaret Stewart, dependent daughter of William Stewart, deceased, late a private in Company E, One hundred and nineteenth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of eighteen dollars per month.


*Approved, June 20, 1890.*

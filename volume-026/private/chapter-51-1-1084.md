# Chapter 1084

> An Act to increase the pension of Thomas Beaumont.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the allowance of pension now granted to Thomas Beaumont so that said Beaumont shall receive monthly the sum of forty-five dollars.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

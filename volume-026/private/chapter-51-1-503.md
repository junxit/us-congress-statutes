# Chapter 503

> An Act to grant a pension to Barbara Madden.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Barbara Madden, widow of Bartholomew Madden, late private Company D. eighty-eighth New York Infantry, on the pension-roll, subject to the restrictions and limitations of the pension laws of the United States.


*Approved, June 21, 1890.*

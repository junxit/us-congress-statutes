# Chapter 590

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Merritt Lewis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Merritt Lewis, late a private in Company K. Seventh Michigan Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of sixty dollars a month, in lieu of the sum he is now receiving.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

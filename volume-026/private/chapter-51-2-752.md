# Chapter 752

> An Act granting a pension to William P. Witt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William P. Witt, late of Company B, Seventeenth Regiment of Kansas Volunteers.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

# Chapter 469

> An Act to increase the pension of Levi H. Utt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension now paid to Levi H. Utt, late major of Seventh Kansas Cavalry, and pay him the sum of fifty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, June 20, 1890.*

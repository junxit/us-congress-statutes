# Chapter 465

> An Act to pension Mrs. Adeline S. Wilbur.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension rolls, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Adeline S. Wilbur, widow of Charles H. Wilbur, late of the ship Edward, Mexican war, and allow her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

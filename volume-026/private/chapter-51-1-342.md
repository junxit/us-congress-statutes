# Chapter 342

> An Act to pension Thomas K. Edwards for service in the Indian war.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and. House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Thomas K. Ed wards, of Hope, Hemp-stead County, Arkansas, who served in Captain James Love’s company of Georgia volunteers, Indian war, eighteen hundred and thirty- six, and pay him the same pension as is allowed by law for service in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve.


*Approved, May 24, 1890.*

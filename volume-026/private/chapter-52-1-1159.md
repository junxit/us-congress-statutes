# Chapter 1159

> An Act granting a pension to Abigail Hughes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place . on the pension-rolls of the United States the name of Abigail Hughes, widow of Benjamin G. Hughes, late of Company F, Seventy-fourth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, subject to the conditions and limitations of the pension laws of the United States.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

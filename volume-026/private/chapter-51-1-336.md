# Chapter 336

> An Act to pension Christina Edson for meritorious Services rendered the Government during the Indian wars in the Oregon Territory, now the State of Oregon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House, of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension-rolls of the Government the name of Christina Edson, of Ellensburgh, in the state of Oregon, for Services rendered on the frontiers to the Government, and to the settlements in the Oregon Indian wars, and that she be paid the sum of twenty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, May 24, 1890.*

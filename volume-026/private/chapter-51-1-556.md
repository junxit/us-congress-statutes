# Chapter 556

> An Act granting a pension to George F. White.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George F. White, late of Company I, Third Regiment, Wisconsin Cavalry.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

# Chapter 451

> An Act granting a pension to Charles H. Perry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Charles H. Perry, late of the whaleship Edward, Mexican war.


*Approved, June 20, 1890.*

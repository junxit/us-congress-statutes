# Chapter 673

> An Act granting a pension to Ellen G. King.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Ellen G. King, of Boston, Massachusetts, widow of Colonel and Brevet Brigadier-General William S. King, and to pay her at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, July 11, 1890.*

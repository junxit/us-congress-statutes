# Chapter 243

> An Act granting a pension to John McCool.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations of the pension laws, the name of John McCool, late sergeant Company B, Second Regiment of Iowa Infantry Volunteers, and pay him a pension of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, May 21, 1890.*

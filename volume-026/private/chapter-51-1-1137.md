# Chapter 1137

> An Act increasing the pension of Mary Snead, a revolutionary pensioner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Mary Snead, widow of Bowdoin Snead, a private soldier of the Revolutionary war, from twelve dollars per month to thirty dollars per month, the same to commence from the taking effect of this act, by approval or otherwise.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

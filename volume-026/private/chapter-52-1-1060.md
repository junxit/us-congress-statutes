# Chapter 1060

> An Act granting a pension to John L. Lindel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations of the pension laws, the name of John L. Lindel, late a private in Company C, Fiftieth Pennsylvania Infantry.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

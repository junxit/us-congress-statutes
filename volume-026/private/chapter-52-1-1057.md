# Chapter 1057

> An Act granting a pension to Mary H. Curtis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the. United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and instructed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary H. Curtis, dependent mother of Charles S. Curtis, deceased, late a private in Company B, First Vermont Heavy Artillery.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

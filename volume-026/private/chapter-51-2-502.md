# Chapter 502

> An Act granting an increase of pension to John S. Furling.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John S. Furling, late private Company D, Fifth Regiment United States Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving under certificate numbered two hundred and ninety-three thousand one hundred and fifty-eight.


*Approved, March 2, 1891.*

# Chapter 600

> An Act pensioning John George.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be authorized to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name or John George, dependent father of Lafayette George, late of Company H, Fourteenth Illinois Volunteers, now deceased. Residence of father is at Ingalls, Kansas.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

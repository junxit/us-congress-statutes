# Chapter 860

> An Act granting a pension to Jeremiah White.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jeremiah White, late private Captain Hart Fellow’s company, Second Regiment Illinois Cavalry, Black Hawk war.


*Approved, September 2, 1890.*

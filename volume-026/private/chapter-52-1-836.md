# Chapter 836

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Virginia L. M. Ewing.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws the name of Virginia L. M. Ewing, widow of Charles Swing, brigadier-general United States Volunteers, at the rate of fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, August 29, 1890.*

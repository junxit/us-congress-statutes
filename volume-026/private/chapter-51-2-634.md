# Chapter 634

> An Act for the relief of Angus McVigor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place the name of Angus McVigor, late of Company G. Fifteenth Regiment United States Infantry, upon the pension roll at the rate of thirty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension now received by him.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

# Chapter 587

> An Act granting a pension to Catherine Sattle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and instructed to place on the pension-roll the name of Catherine Sattle, widow of Julius Sattle, late a private in Company A, Seventh New York Volunteers, at the rate of twelve dollars per month, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

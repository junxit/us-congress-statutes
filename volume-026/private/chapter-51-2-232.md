# Chapter 232

> An Act increasing the pension of Mrs. Rochie Brien Buell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior of the United States be, and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension-roll of the United States the name of Mrs. Rochie Brien Buell, widow of General George P. Buell, late of the United States Army, at the rate of fifty dollars per month, according to the rules and regulations governing pensions, which shall be in lieu of the pension which the said Mrs. Rochie Brien Buell is now drawing as such widow.


*Approved, February 14, 1891.*

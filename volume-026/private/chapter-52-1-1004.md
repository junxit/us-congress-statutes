# Chapter 1004

> An Act for the relief of Jeremiah Darling.

*Be it enacted by the. Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Jeremiah Darling, of Cincinnati, Ohio, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, three hundred and sixty-two dollars and fifty cents, such payment to be in full of any and all claims the said Jeremiah Darling may have against the United States for carrying the mail on post-route numbered ninety-four-hundred and forty-seven, Cincinnati to Murdock, in the State of Ohio.


*Approved, September 26, 1890.*

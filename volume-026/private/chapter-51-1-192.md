# Chapter 192

> An Act to increase the pension of Zachariah T. Crawford.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Zachariah T. Crawford, late of Company G, Two hundred and thirteenth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, at the rate of fifty dollars.


*Approved, May 3, 1890.*

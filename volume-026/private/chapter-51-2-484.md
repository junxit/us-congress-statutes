# Chapter 484

> An Act pensioning Joseph A. Blair.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Joseph A. Blair, late of Company C, Seventh Kentucky Cavalry, now of Lyons, Kansas.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

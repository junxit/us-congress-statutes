# Chapter 287

> An Act pensioning Harriet B. White.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized to place upon the pension-roil, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Harriet B. White, dependent step-mother of Francis M. White, deceased, late of Company A, Twenty-Second Regiment Pennsylvania Cavalry, and pay her a pension at rate of twelve dollars per month. The number of her application in Pension Department is three hundred and eighty-nine thousand six hundred and eighty-four.


*Approved, May 22, 1890.*

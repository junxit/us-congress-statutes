# Chapter 211

> An Act for the relief of Delila Roe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Delila Roe, widow of George C. Roe, on the pension-roll and pay her a pension of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 14, 1891.*

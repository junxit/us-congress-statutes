# Chapter 1173

> An Act granting a pension to Conrad McClain.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Conrad McClain, late of Company F, One hundred and ninth Regiment of New York Infantry.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

# Chapter 1118

> An Act for the relief of W. G. Triece.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the. United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is. authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of W. G. Triece, late a private of Company H, Tenth Pennsylvania Militia.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

# Chapter 491

> An Act granting an increase of pension to George H. Blodgett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, at the rate of sixteen dollars a month, the name of George H. Blodgett, late a member of Company M, First Regiment Maine Heavy Artillery, in lieu of the pension now received by him.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

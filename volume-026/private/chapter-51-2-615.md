# Chapter 615

> An Act granting a pension to Sarah Knight.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and instructed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah Knight, dependent mother of Horace Knight, deceased, late captain of Company H, Twentieth Regiment Michigan Infantry.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

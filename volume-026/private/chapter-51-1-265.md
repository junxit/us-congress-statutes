# Chapter 265

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Frank Traynor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Frank Traynor, late of Company F, One hundred and second New York Volunteers, a pension at the rate of eighteen dollars per month, in lieu of the pension now paid him, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, May 21, 1890.*

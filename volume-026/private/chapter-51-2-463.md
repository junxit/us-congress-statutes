# Chapter 463

> An Act to grant a pension to Robert Moore, of Kirkwood, Illinois,

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is. authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Robert Moore, late a soldier in Alexander White’s company of Mounted Volunteers in the Black Hawk war, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

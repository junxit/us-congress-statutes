# Chapter 513

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Susannah D. Clark.

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Susannah D. Clark of Jonesboro, Grant County. Indiana, a volunteer army nurse during the late rebellion, and pay her, during life, a pension of twelve dollars per month, from and after the passage of this act


*Approved, June 21, 1890.*

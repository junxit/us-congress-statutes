# Chapter 1167

> An Act granting a pension to John A. Anderson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America' in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of John A. Anderson, late a private in Company I, Fifth United States Artillery, on the pension-roll, subject to the conditions and limitations of the pension laws


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

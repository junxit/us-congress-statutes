# Chapter 191

> An Act granting a pension to Annie B. Pettigrew.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay Annie B. Pettigrew, dependent daughter of Henry J. Pettigrew, Company E, Third New Hampshire Regiment, a pension of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 14, 1891.*

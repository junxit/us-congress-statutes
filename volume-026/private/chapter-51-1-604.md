# Chapter 604

> An Act to increase the pension of Mrs. Antonia B. Lynch.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Mrs. Antonia B. Lynch, widow of Captain Dominick Lynch, United States Navy, deceased, to forty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension now paid to her.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

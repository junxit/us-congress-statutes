# Chapter 352

> An Act granting a pension to Elizabeth Johnson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elizabeth Johnson, stepmother of John Johnson, of late Company H, Seventy-first Pennsylvania Volunteers.


*Approved, February 27, 1891.*

# Chapter 316

> An Act granting a pension to Martha Torrence.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws, the name of Martha Torrence, of Hortonville post-office, Indiana, widow of George W. Camp, late a private in Company H, twelfth Regiment Indiana Volunteers.


*Approved, February 25, 1891.*

# Chapter 744

> An Act granting a pension to Ruth A. Ball.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and is hereby, authorized and directed to restore to the pension-roll, subject to the limitations and regulations of the pension act. the name of Ruth A. Ball, widow of Matthew Holmes, late of Company B, Twenty-eighth Michigan Infantry.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

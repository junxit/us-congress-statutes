# Chapter 300

> An Act granting a pension to Daniel Ullman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Daniel Ullman, late brevet major-general in the Army of the United States, at the rate of seventy-two dollars a month.


*Approved, February 24, 1891.*

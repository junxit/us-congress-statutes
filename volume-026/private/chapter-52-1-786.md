# Chapter 786

> An Act to increase the pension of Bennett S. Shaug.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

, That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Bennett S. Shaug, late of Company K, Fifteenth Ohio Volunteers, in the Mexican war, at the rate of twenty- five dollars per month, in lieu of the pension which he is now receiving.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

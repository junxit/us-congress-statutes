# Chapter 705

> An Act for the relief of John O. McDonald, alias William Barnes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the Pension-roll the name of John 0. McDonald, alias William Barnes, and pay him a pension of sixteen dollars per month,


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

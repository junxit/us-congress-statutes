# Chapter 1161

> An Act granting a pension to Alonzo Hix.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and. House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Alonzo Hix, late of Company D, Fifth Indiana Legion, in the war of the rebellion, the pension to commence from approval of this act.


*Approved, September 30, 1890.*

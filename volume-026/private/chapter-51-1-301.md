# Chapter 301

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Martha E. Jones.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Martha E. Jones, widow of James S. Jones, late a private of Company A, Twelfth Tennessee Cavalry.


*Approved, May 24, 1890.*

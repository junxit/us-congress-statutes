# Chapter 41

> An Act granting a pension to Miss Elizabeth A. Tuttle.

*Be it enacted in the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Miss Elizabeth A. Tuttle, an army nurse uring the late war of the rebellion, and to pay her a pension of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 19, 1890.*

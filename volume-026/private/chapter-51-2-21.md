# Chapter 21

> An Act to pension John D. Bagby.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized to place on the pension rolls, subject to the provisions and restrictions of the pension laws, the name of John D. Bagby, who was a private in Captain Japhet A. Ball’s independent mounted company, Illinois Volunteer Infantry, during the Black Hawk war.


*Approved, December 15, 1890.*

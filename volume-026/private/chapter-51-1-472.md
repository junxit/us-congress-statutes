# Chapter 472

> An Act for the relief of Theodore J. Shandal

*Be it enacted by the, Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll the name of Theodore J. Shandal, late private Company D, Fifth Regiment Connecticut Volunteers (claim numbered four hundred and seventy-five thousand one hundred and forty-three), subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, June 20, 1890.*

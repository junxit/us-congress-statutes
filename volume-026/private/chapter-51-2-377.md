# Chapter 377

> An Act to increase the pension of Edward Jardine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Edward Jardine, late colonel and brevet brigadier-general United States Volunteers, on the pension roll at the rate of one hundred dollars per month, in lieu of his present pension of fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 27, 1891.*

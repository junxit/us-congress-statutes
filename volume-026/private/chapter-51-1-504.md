# Chapter 504

> An Act granting a pension to Ellen Shea.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws, the name of Ellen Shea, mother of Michael Shea, late of Company A, Thirteenth Illinois Volunteers.


*Approved, June 21, 1890.*

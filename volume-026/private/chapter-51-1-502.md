# Chapter 502

> An Act granting a pension to James H. Fleming.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of James H. Fleming, of Company E, One hundred and fifth Ohio Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, June 21, 1890.*

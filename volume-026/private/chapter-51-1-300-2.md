# Chapter 300

> An Act granting a pension to Alvira A. Edwards.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, directed to place upon the pension-rolls, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Alvira A. Edwards, widow of George Edwards, ate of Company K, Fifth New York Heavy Artillery.


*Approved, May 24, 1890.*

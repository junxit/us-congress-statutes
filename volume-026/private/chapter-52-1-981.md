# Chapter 981

> An Act granting a pension to Sarah J. Alexander.

*Be it enacted by the. Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah J. Alexander, an army nurse in the late war. and pay her at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, September 26, 1890.*

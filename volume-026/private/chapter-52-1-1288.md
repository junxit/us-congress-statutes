# Chapter 1288

> An Act granting a pension to Emily Onderdonk.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be’, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Emily Onderdonk, a resilient of Battle Creek. Michigan, at the rate of twelve dollars per month, on account of disability resulting from disease contracted while serving as a hospital nurse during the war of the rebellion.


*Approved, October 1, 1890.*

# Chapter 95

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Aaron H. Le Van.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Aaron H. Le Van, late of Company A, Eighty-second Regiment. Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension commensurate with the degree of disability found to exist upon medical examination.


*Approved, January 24, 1891.*

# Chapter 355

> An Act granting a pension to Christian C. Whistler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place the name of Christian C. Whistler on the pension rolls, subject to the pension laws of the United States, he being the father of William H. Whistler, who was a member of Company F, Ninth Regiment United States Infantry.


*Approved, February 27, 1891.*

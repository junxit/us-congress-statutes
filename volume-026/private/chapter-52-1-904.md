# Chapter 904

> An Act to remove the charge of desertion from the record of William H. Fenton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to cause the records of the War Department to be so amended as to remove the charge of desertion from the service record of William H. Fenton, late a private in Company G. Nineteenth Ohio Infantry Volunteers, and to grant an honorable discharge to the said William H. Fenton as a private of said company as of the date of May twenty-seventh,, eighteen hundred and sixty-four.


*Approved, September 5, 1890.*

# Chapter 702

> An Act granting an increase of pension to John E. Walton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John E. Walton, late of Company C, One hundredth Pennsylvania Volunteers, and pay him at the rate of forty dollars per month in lieu of the pension he is now receiving under certificate numbered one hundred and seventy-six thousand six hundred and forty-one.


*Approved, July 11, 1890.*

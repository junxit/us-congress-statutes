# Chapter 188

> An Act granting a pension to David C. Bullard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of David C. Bullard, late a member of Company G, Forty-ninth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers, afterward transferred to the United States ship Alleghany.


*Approved, May 3, 1890.*

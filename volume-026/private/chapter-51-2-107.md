# Chapter 107

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Mrs. Mary McIntosh.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of seventeen dollars now received by Mrs. Mary McIntosh, widow of First Lieutenant Donald McIntosh, Seventh’ Cavalry, United States Army, to thirty dollars per month during her natural lifetime, to take effect from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, January 26, 1891.*

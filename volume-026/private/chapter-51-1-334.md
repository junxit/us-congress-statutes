# Chapter 334

> An Act to pension Mary Bailey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Mary Bailey, mother of Robert G. Davis, late a private in Company B, Ninety-ninth Pennsylvania Volunteers, subject to the prolusions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, May 24, 1890.*

# Chapter 780

> An Act for the relief of Parker Adams.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, instructed and authorized to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Parker Adams, late a private, under Colonel Thomas, of Illinois, in the First Regiment of Volunteers, in the Black Hawk war, and allow him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

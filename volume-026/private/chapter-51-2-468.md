# Chapter 468

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Richard W. Hubbell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Richard W. Hubbell, late first lieutenant of Company B, First Wisconsin Heavy Artillery, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

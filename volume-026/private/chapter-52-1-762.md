# Chapter 762

> An Act granting a pension to Jane N. Terry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jane N. Terry, widow of William H. Terry, late of Company A, Second Regiment Iowa Cavalry.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

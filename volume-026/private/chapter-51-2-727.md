# Chapter 727

> An Act granting a pension to Elizabeth Dodge.

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll the name of Elizabeth Dodge, an army nurse, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month, subject to the rules and limitations of the pension laws,


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

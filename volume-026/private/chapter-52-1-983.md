# Chapter 983

> An Act granting a pension to George Blum.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the emissions laws, the name of George Blum, late of Company E, Second United States Dragoons.


*Approved, September 26, 1890.*

# Chapter 480

> An Act granting a pension to Henry Allhorn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be directed to put on the pension roll the name of Henry Allhorn, late a private in Company D, First Regiment United States Infantry, in the Florida war, at the rate of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

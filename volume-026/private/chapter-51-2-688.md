# Chapter 688

> An Act To provide for the issue of the commission of Philip C. Johnson as a rear-admiral in the United States Navy.

Whereas Philip C. Johnson, after a long, faithful, and creditable service in the United States Navy, became entitled to promotion to the grade of rear-admiral on the twenty-fifth day of January, eighteen hundred and eighty-seven, but died without being commissioned: Therefore, Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

That the President be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to issue the commission of Philip Johnson as a rear admiral in the United States Navy, to be dated January twenty-fifth, eighteen hundred and eighty-seven, and to deliver the same to the widow of said Philip C. Johnson.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

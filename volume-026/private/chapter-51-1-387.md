# Chapter 387

> An Act granting a pension to John Dean.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to place on the pension-roll the name of John Dean, formerly a private in Company A, Thirteenth New York Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month.

# Chapter 683

> An Act authorizing the President of the United States to revoke the order of dismissal of Chaplain Lorenzo S. Coffin, Thirty-second Iowa Volunteers, and to issue to him a certificate of honorable muster-out of the service.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be, and hereby is, authorized to revoke Special Orders numbered three hundred and thirty-seven. War Department. Adjutant-General’s Office, Washington, July twenty-ninth, eighteen hundred and sixty-three, dismissing Chaplain Lorenzo S. Coffin, Thirty-second Iowa Volunteers, from the service of the United States, for absence without proper authority, so far as it relates to and affects said Lorenzo S. Coffin, and to cause to be issued to him as chaplain of said regiment, a certificate of honorable muster-out of the service as of the date of August tenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-three.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

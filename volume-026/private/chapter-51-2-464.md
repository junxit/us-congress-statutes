# Chapter 464

> An Act to grant a pension to Absalom M. Wolf, of Mier, Indiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is. authorized and required to place upon the pension rolls the name of Absalom M. Wolf, of Mier, Grant County, Indiana, the dependent father of William Wolf, deceased, late a private soldier in Company L. of the Forty-first Regiment of Indiana Volunteers in the war of the rebellion, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars a month


*Approved, February 28, 1891.*

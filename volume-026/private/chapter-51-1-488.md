# Chapter 488

> An Act granting a pension to Mary Personeus.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Personeus. late widow of William . Crisfield, sergeant in Company G, Forty-first Regiment Missouri Infantry Volunteers, in the war of the rebellion, and the wife of Martin Personeus, a hopeless insane person.


*Approved, June 21, 1890.*

# Chapter 848

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Mrs. F. Selina Buchanan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations or the pension laws, the name of Mrs. F. Selina Buchanan, widow of the late McKean Buchanan, of the United States Navy, and pay her a pension of fifty dollars per month, in lieu of the amount she is now receiving.


*Approved, August 30, 1890.*

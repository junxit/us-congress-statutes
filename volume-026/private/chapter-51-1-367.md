# Chapter 367

> An Act placing the name of Bridget White on the pension-rolls.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Bridget White, widow of William White, deceased, late, private Company I, Eleventh Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, on the pension-rolls, subject to the limitations and restrictions of the pension laws.


*Approved, May 27, 1890.*

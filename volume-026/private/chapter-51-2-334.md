# Chapter 334

> An Act to increase the pension of Elias D. Thompson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay Elias D. Thompson, of Company F, First Louisiana Volunteers in the Mexican war, a pension of thirty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 25, 1891.*

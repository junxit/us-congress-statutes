# Chapter 325

> An Act for the relief of Daniel W. Parrish.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized to place the name of Daniel W Parrish, late a private of Company G, One hundred and fifth Indiana State Troops, upon the pension-roll, subject to the conditions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, May 24, 1890.*

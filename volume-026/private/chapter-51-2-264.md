# Chapter 264

> An Act for the relief of Mrs. Elizabeth C. Custer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and hereby is, authorized and directed to pay to Mrs. Elizabeth C. Custer, widow of General George A. Custer, a pension at the rate of one hundred dollars per month, from and after the passage of this act, in lieu of the pension which she now receives


*Approved, February 21, 1891.*

# Chapter 963

> An Act granting a pension to David Drumheller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of David Drumheller, late of Company G, Sixty-fifth Ohio Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, September 26, 1890.*

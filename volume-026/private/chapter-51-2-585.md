# Chapter 585

> An Act granting a pension to Frances Jane McCloud.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations, of the pension laws, the name of Frances Jane McCloud, widow of Alexander McCloud, late private of Company G, Eighteenth Missouri Volunteers.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

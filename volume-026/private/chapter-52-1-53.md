# Chapter 53

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Anna Butterfield.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations or the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Anna Butterfield, dependent mother of James A. B. Butterfield, late a sergeant in the Second Illinois Cavalry.


*Approved, March 19, 1890.*

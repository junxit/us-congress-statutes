# Chapter 306

> An Act for the relief of Montgomery Geiger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Montgomery Geiger, of the county of Lincoln and State of Missouri, a private in Company B. of Powell’s Battalion, Mexican war, and pay him a pension of twenty dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 25, 1891.*

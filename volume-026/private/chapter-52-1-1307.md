# Chapter 1307

> An Act granting a pension to Emily Fry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Emily Fry, widow of Nathaniel Fry, late a soldier in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve.


*Approved, October 1, 1890.*

# Chapter 229

> An Act increasing the pension of Walter P. Harrison.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension of eight dollars per month now received by Walter P. Harrison, late private Company B, First Regiment Louisiana Volunteers, Mexican war, be, and the same is hereby, increased to seventy-two dollars per month, the same to be in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, May 19, 1890.*

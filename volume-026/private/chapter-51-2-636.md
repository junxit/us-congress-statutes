# Chapter 636

> An Act for relief of Major John M. Laing.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and. House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be, and hereby is, authorized to remit the unexecuted portion of the sentence of the Court-martial in the case of John M. Laing, late major of the Fifteenth Regiment of Kansas Cavalry Volunteers, and to cause the said John M. Laing to be honorably mustered out of service as such major as of the date of the sentence of said court-martial: Provided, That the said John M. Laing shall not by virtue of this act be entitled to have or receive any pay or allowance subsequent to the date of said sentence.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

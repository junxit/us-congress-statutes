# Chapter 160

> An Act to grant an increase of pension to Simon J. Fought.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized find directed to grant an increase of pension to Simon J. Fought, formerly a corporal of Company D of the Forty-sixth Regiment of Ohio Volunteers, to fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 12, 1891.*

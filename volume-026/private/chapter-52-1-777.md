# Chapter 777

> An Act for the relief of George W. Hutchison.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George W. Hutchison, a dependent son of John W. Hutchison, late a private of Company G, Twenty-seventh Indiana Volunteers, and pay him, through his legally constituted guardian, a pension at the rate of eighteen dollars per month.


*Approved, August 15, 1890.*

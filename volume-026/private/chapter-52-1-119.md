# Chapter 119

> An Act granting a pension to William H. Brimmer,

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United. States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William H. Brimmer, of Stephentown, New York, late a private in Company K, Twenty-first Regiment of New York Cavalry Volunteers.


*Approved, April 21, 1890.*

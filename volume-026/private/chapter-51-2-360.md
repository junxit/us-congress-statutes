# Chapter 360

> An Act granting a pension to Tunis S. Danford.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the general pension laws, the name of Tunis S. Danford, late a private Company G, Seventh Ohio Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, February 27, 1891.*

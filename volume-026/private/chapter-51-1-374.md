# Chapter 374

> An Act granting a pension to William H. Mays.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name William H. Mays, late of Company C, Fourth Regiment Kentucky Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, May 27, 1890.*

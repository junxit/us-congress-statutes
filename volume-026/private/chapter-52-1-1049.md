# Chapter 1049

> An Act granting a pension to Mary A. Green.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Mary A. Green, widow of William A. Green, late Colonel of the Twenty-ninth Wisconsin Infantry, at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, September 29, 1890.*

# Chapter 609

> An Act to grant an increase of pension to Harvey T. Alcott, late of Company K, One hundred and twenty-sixth New York Infantry Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Harvey T. Alcott. late of Company K, One hundred and twenty-sixth Regiment. New York Volunteers, on the pension-roll and increase his pension to twenty-five dollars a month for gunshot wound and resulting injury to the spine, producing great nervous irritation and incapacitating him for manual or mental labor.


*Approved, June 24, 1890.*

# Chapter 177

> An Act granting a pension to Sarah Phillips

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah Phillips, of Marion County. Illinois. widow of Jonathan Phillips. late a soldier in the Seminole Indian war, at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 14, 1891.*

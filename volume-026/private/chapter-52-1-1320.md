# Chapter 1320

> An Act increasing the pension of Francis Gilman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Blouse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension of Francis Gilman, late a private in the Second Battery Iowa Light Artillery, be, and the same hereby is, increased to thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, October 1, 1890.*

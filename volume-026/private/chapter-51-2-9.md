# Chapter 9

> An Act granting a pension to John Grant.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Grant, alias William McKenzie, private Maddox Battalion, California Volunteers, Mexican war.


*Approved, December 15, 1890.*

# Public Resolution 20

> Joint resolution to print one hundred thousand copies of report on Diseases of the Horse.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be printed one hundred thousand copies of the special report on Diseases of the Horse, prepared under the supervision of the Chief of the Bureau of Animal Industry; twenty thousand copies for the use of members of the Senate; seventy thousand copies for the use of members of the House of Representatives of the Fifty-first Congress; and ten thousand copies for the use of the Secretary of Agriculture, to be paid out of the appropriation hereinafter made.

## § 2.

That the sum of seventy-live thousand dollars, or so much thereof as may be necessary, is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to defray the expenses of printing and binding said report, and for illustrations for the same.


*Approved, March 3, 1891.*

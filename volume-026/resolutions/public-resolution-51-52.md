# Public Resolution 52

> Joint resolution authorizing the transfer of certain appropriations for the Indian Service, on the books of the Treasury.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of Treasury be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to transfer upon the books of the Treasury the sum of one thousand seven hundred and thirty-nine dollars and fifty-six cents, from appropriation “Telegraphing and Purchase of Indian Supplies, eighteen hundred and ninety-one,” to appropriation “Telegraphing and Purchase of Indian Supplies, eighteen hundred and ninety.”


*Approved, September 26, 1890.*

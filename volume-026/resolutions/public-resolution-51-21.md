# Public Resolution 21

> Joint resolution authorizing the use and improvement at Fort Sewall at Marblehead, Massachusetts.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That permission be, and is hereby, granted to the town of Marblehead, in the State of Massachusetts, to improve and beautify Fort Sewall and its appurtenant lands and grounds, situated in said town, and belonging to the United States, with the right to said town to make such excavations and fillings and erect and maintain such structures as the Secretary of War may from time to time approve: Provided, That this resolution shall not be construed to pass any title in said fort lands or grounds, but that the ownership and control of the said grounds shall remain entirely in the United States, and shall be subject to such changes and uses for military or other purposes as the Secretary of War may direct.


*Approved, May 19, 1890.*

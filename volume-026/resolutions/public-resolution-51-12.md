# Public Resolution 12

> Joint resolution providing for taking the census in Alaska.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Superintendent of Census is hereby authorized to pay special agents in Alaska, in addition to their salaries, a per diem allowance to cover all expenses of subsistence and transportation, not to exceed seven dollars per diem.


*Approved, March 19, 1890.*

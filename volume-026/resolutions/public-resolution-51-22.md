# Public Resolution 22

> Joint resolution appropriating the sum of five hundred dollars to complete the Engraving and printing the portrait of James N. Burnes, deceased, late a member of the House of Representatives of the fiftieth Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of five hundred dollars, or so much thereof as may be necessary, is hereby appropriated out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to complete the engraving and printing the portrait of James N. Burnes, deceased, late a member of the House of Representatives in the fiftieth Congress as authorized by the act of Congress approved March first, eighteen hundred and eighty-nine.


*Approved, May 22, 1890.*

# Public Resolution 62

> Joint resolution extending the “Act fixing the rate of interest to be charged on arrearages of general and special taxes now due the District of Columbia, if paid within a time specified” to October thirty-first, eighteen hundred and ninety.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of the act approved May sixth, eighteen hundred and ninety, being “An act fixing the rate of interest to be charged on arrearages of general and special taxes now due the District, of Columbia, if paid within a time specified.” be, and they are hereby, reenacted and extended to the thirty-first day of October, eighteen hundred and ninety.


*Approved, October 1, 1890.*

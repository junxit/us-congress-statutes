# Public Resolution 53

> Joint resolution to correct an error in the act entitled an act making appropriations for sundry civil expenses of the Government for the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, eighteen hundred and ninety-one, and for other purposes, approved August 30, 1890.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section one of the act entitled an act making appropriations for sundry civil expenses of the Government for the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, eighteen hundred and ninety-one, and for other purposes, approved August thirtieth, eighteen hundred and ninety, be, and the same is hereby, amended so that in the clause making appropriation for the purchase of portraits under the Department of State the words “Dalton E. Marchant” shall read “Edward D. Marchant.”


*Approved, September 27, 1890.*

# Public Law 108–14

> An Act To designate the Federal building located at 290 Broadway in New York, New York, as the “Ted Weiss Federal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The Federal building located at 290 Broadway in New York, New York, shall be known and designated as the “Ted Weiss Federal Building”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Federal building referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Ted Weiss Federal Building”.


*Approved April 23, 2003.*

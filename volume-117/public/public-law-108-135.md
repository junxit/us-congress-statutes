# Public Law 108–135

> Joint Resolution Making further continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 2004, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Public Law 108–84 is amended by striking the date specified in section 107(c) and inserting “January 31, 2004”.

## § 2.

Section 8144(b) of the Department of Defense Appropriations Act, 2003 (Public Law 107–248), as amended by Public Law 108–84, is further amended by striking “November 21, 2003” and inserting “January 31, 2004”.


*Approved November 22, 2003.*

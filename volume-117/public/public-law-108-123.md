# Public Law 108–123

> An Act To amend section 5379 of title 5, United States Code, to increase the annual and aggregate limits on student loan repayments by Federal agencies.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. SHORT TITLE.

This Act may be cited as the “Federal Employee Student Loan Assistance Act”.

## § 2. STUDENT LOAN REPAYMENTS.

Section 5379(b)(2) of title 5, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** in subparagraph (A), by striking “$6,000” and inserting “$10,000”; and
- **(2)** in subparagraph (B), by striking “$40,000” and inserting “$60,000”.


*Approved November 11, 2003.*

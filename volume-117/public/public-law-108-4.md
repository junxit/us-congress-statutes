# Public Law 108–4

> Joint Resolution Making further continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 2003, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Public Law 107–229 is further amended by striking the date specified in section 107(c) and inserting in lieu thereof “February 7, 2003”.


*Approved January 31, 2003.*

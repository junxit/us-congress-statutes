# Public Law 108–143

> An Act To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 710 Wicks Lane in Billings, Montana, as the “Ronald Reagan Post Office Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION OF RONALD REAGAN POST OFFICE BUILDING.

- **(a)** *In General.—* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 710 Wicks Lane in Billings, Montana, shall be known and designated as the “Ronald Reagan Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References.—* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the Ronald Reagan Post Office Building.


*Approved December 2, 2003.*

# Public Law 108–103

> An Act To redesignate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 48 South Broadway, Nyack, New York, as the “Edward O’Grady, Waverly Brown, Peter Paige Post Office Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION OF BUILDING.

- **(a)** *Designation.—* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 48 South Broadway, Nyack, New York, and known as the “Nyack Post Office” shall be known as the “Edward O’Grady, Waverly Brown, Peter Paige Post Office Building”.
- **(b)** *References.—* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Edward O’Grady, Waverly Brown, Peter Paige Post Office Building”.


*Approved October 29, 2003.*

# Public Law 108–73

> An Act To extend for six months the period for which chapter 12 of title 11 of the United States Code is reenacted.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. SHORT TITLE.

This Act may be cited as the “Family Farmer Bankruptcy Relief Act of 2003”.

## § 2. SIX-MONTH EXTENSION OF PERIOD FOR WHICH CHAPTER 12 OF TITLE 11, UNITED STATES CODE, IS REENACTED.

- **(a)** *Amendments.—* Section 149 of title I of division C of Public Law 105–277 (11 U.S.C. 1201 note) is amended—
  - **(1)** by striking “July 1, 2003” each place it appears and inserting “January 1, 2004”; and
  - **(2)** in subsection (a)—
    - **(A)** by striking “December 31, 2002” and inserting “June 30, 2003”; and
    - **(B)** by striking “January 1, 2003” and inserting “July 1, 2003”.
- **(b)** *Effective Date.—* The amendments made by subsection (a) take effect on July 1, 2003.


*Approved August 15, 2003.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 20

> JOINT COMMITTEE ON PRINTING—DESIGNATING ADDITIONAL MEMBER

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That effective for the One Hundred Eighth Congress, the Chairman of the Committee on Rules and Administration of the Senate may designate another member of the Committee to serve on the Joint Committee on Printing in place of the Chairman.


*Agreed to March 25, 2003.*

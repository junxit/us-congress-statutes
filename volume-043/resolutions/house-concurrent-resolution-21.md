# House Concurrent Resolution 21

> coast guard.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the action of the Speaker of the House of Representatives and of the President pro tempore of the Senate in signing the enrolled bill (H. R. 6815) entitled “An Act to authorize a temporary increase of the Coast Guard for law enforcement,” be rescinded and that in the reenrollment of the said bill the following amendment be made, viz: On page 2, line 44, after the word “enlisted” insert: , warrant.


*Passed, April 15, 1924.*

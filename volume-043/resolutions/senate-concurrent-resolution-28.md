# Senate Concurrent Resolution 28

> bridges, bayou bartholomew.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the action of the Speaker of the House of Representatives and of the President pro tempore of the Senate in signing the enrolled bill (S. 3G22) granting the consent of Congress to the Louisiana Highway Commission to construct, maintain, and operate a bridge across the Bayou Bartholomew at each of the following named points in Morehouse Parish, La.: Vester Ferry, Ward Ferry, and Zachery Ferry, be rescinded, and that the Secretary of the Senate be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to reenroll the bill with the following amendments:

In line 3 of the enrolled bill strike out “Polish” and insert “Police.”

Amend the title so as to read:

> “An Act granting the consent of Congress to the police jury of Morehouse Parish, Louisiana, or the State Highway Commission of Louisiana to construct, maintain, and operate a bridge across the Bayou Bartholomew at each of the following-named points in Morehouse Parish, Louisiana : Vester Ferry, Ward Ferry, and Zachery Ferry.”


*Passed, February 3, 1925.*

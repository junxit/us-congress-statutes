# Senate Concurrent Resolution 15

> national defense act.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President of the United States be requested to return to the Senate the enrolled bill (S. 2169), entitled “An Act to amend in certain particulars the National Defense Act of June 3, 1916, as amended, and for other purposes.”


*Passed, June 2, 1924.*

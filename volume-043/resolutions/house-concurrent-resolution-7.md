# House Concurrent Resolution 7

> holiday recess.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Thursday, December 20, 1923, they stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian Thursday, January 3, 1924.


*Passed, December 17, 1923.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 4

> joint meeting.

*Resolved, By the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives, on Thursday the sixth day of December, nineteen hundred and twenty-three, at twelve thirty o’clock in the afternoon, for the purpose of receiving such communication as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed, December 6, 1923.*

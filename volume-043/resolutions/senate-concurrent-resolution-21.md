# Senate Concurrent Resolution 21

> choctaw and chickasaw indian claims.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President of the United States be requested to return to the House of Representatives the enrolled bill (H. R. 5325) “conferring jurisdiction upon the Court of Claims to hear, examine, adjudicate, and enter judgment in any claims which the Choctaw and Chickasaw Indians may have against the United States, and for other purposes.”


*Passed, June 5, 1924.*

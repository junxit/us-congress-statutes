# House Concurrent Resolution 6

> coast guard.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President of the United States be requested to return to the House of Representatives the bill (H. R. 6815) entitled “An Act to authorize a temporary increase of the Coast Guard for law enforcement.”


*Passed, April 12, 1924.*

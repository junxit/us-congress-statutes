# Senate Concurrent Resolution 35

> national defense act amendment.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the action of the Speaker of the House of Representatives and of the President pro tempore of the Senate in signing the enrolled bill (S. 3760) to amend in certain particulars the National Defense Act of June 3, 1916, as amended, and for other purposes, be rescinded, and that in the reenrollment of said bill the Secretary of the Senate be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to strike out on page 5, line 4, the word “commissioner” and insert in lieu thereof the word “ commissioned.”


*Passed, February 25, 1925.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 33

> national defense act.

*Resolved by the Senate ( the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President of the United States be, and he is hereby, requested to return to the Senate the bill (S. 3760) to amend in certain particulars the National Defense Act of June 3, 1916, as amended, and for other purposes.


*Passed, February 21, 1925.*

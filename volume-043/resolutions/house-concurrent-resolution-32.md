# House Concurrent Resolution 32

> holiday recess.

*Resolved by the Bouse of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Saturday, December 20, 1924, they stand adjourned until twelve o’clock meridian Monday, December 29, 1924.


*Passed, December 6, 1924.*

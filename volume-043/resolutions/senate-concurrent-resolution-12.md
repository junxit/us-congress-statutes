# Senate Concurrent Resolution 12

> code of district of columbia laws.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the laws relating to the District of Columbia and the laws of former municipal governments in said District, as recompiled, indexed, and annotated in codified form up to and including March 4, 1923, under authority of a Senate Resolution of January 3, 1924, be printed as a Senate document, and that five hundred additional copies be printed and bound for the use of the Senate and one thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives.


*Passed, May 31, 1924.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 37

> war fraud actions.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Secretary of the Senate be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, in the enrollment of the bill (S. 3913) to extend for an additional period of three years the effective period of the Act entitled “ An Act to amend section 51 of chapter 4 of the Judicial Code,” approved September 19, 1922, and an Act entitled “ An Act to amend section 876 of the Revised Statutes,” approved September 19, 1922, to strike out in the first line of the title the words “ three years ” and insert in lieu thereof the words “ one year,” so that the title may conform to the amendment made by the House of Representatives to the text of the bill and agreed to by the Senate.


*Passed, March 2, 1925.*

# Chapter 299

> An Act Regulating the recovery of allotments and allowances heretofore paid to designated beneficiaries.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That so much of section 210 of the War Risk Insurance Act, as amended by the Act of August 9, 1921 (Forty-second Statutes, page 153), as precludes the recovery of an award of allotment, or allowance, or both, paid to, or on behalf of a person designated as beneficiary of an allotment under the War Risk Insurance Act prior to August 9, 1921, shall hereafter be applicable to allotments paid prior to August 9, 1921, to beneficiaries designated under the Army allotment system by any person who served in the Army.


*Approved, February 24, 1925.*

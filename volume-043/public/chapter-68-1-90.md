# Chapter 90

> An Act Providing for the reservation of certain lands in New Mexico for the Indians of the Zia Pueblo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby reserved and withdrawn from settlement, occupancy, or disposal under the laws of the United States and set apart as a reservation for the benefit, use, and occupancy of the Indians of the Zia Pueblo the tract of land in the State of New Mexico particularly described as follows: Lots 4, 5, and 6 and east half southwest quarter section 7; lots 1, 2, 3, and 4 and east half northwest quarter and east half southwest quarter section 18, all in township 15 north, range 2 east, New Mexico principal meridian, New Mexico, containing approximately three hundred and eighty-six and eighty-five one-hundredths acres.


*Approved, April 12, 1924.*

# Chapter 9

> An Act To authorize the National Society United States Daughters of 1812 to place a bronze tablet on the Francis Scott Key Bridge.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the National Society United States Daughters of 1812 is authorized to place, on the Francis Scott Key Bridge across Potomac River, a bronze tablet, inscribed with the insignia of such society and with the last verse of the Star Spangled Banner, after the plans and specifications for such tablet nave been submitted to, and approved by the Commission of Fine Arts on such plans and specifications, the installation of said tablet to be under the direction of the Commissioners of the District of Columbia.

## § 2.

Such tablet shall be erected without expense to the Government of the United States.


*Approved, January 30, 1924.*

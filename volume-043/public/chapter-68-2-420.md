# Chapter 420

> An Act Authorizing the Postmaster General to make monthly payment of rental for post office premises under lease.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Post-master General is hereby authorized to make monthly payment of rental for post office premises under lease.


*Approved, March 3, 1925.*

# Chapter 377

> Joint Resolution In respect of salaries of original appointees to the Board of Tax Appeals.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provisions of section 1761 of the Revised Statutes shall not apply to any person appointed as an original member of the Board of Tax Appeals, established by section 900 of the Revenue Act of 1924, if such appointment is made prior to December 1, 1924.


*Approved, June 7, 1924.*

# Chapter 90

> An Act To extend the time for the construction of a bridge across the White River at or near Batesville, Arkansas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge authorized by Act of Congress, approved July 1, 1922, to be built by the county of Independence, State of Arkansas, across White River at or near Batesville, in such county, are hereby extended one and three years respectively, from the date of approval hereof.

## § 2.

That the right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, January 26, 1925.*

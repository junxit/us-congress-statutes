# Chapter 310

> An Act To legalize a pier and wharf in York River at Gloucester Banks, near Gloucester Point, Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pier and wharf built by Robert H. Talley, trustee, in the York River, State of Virginia, at Gloucester Banks, which is about one mile east of Gloucester Point, Gloucester County, Virginia, and about one-half mile west of Sarah Creek, Virginia, be, and the same is hereby, legalized to the same extent and with like effect as to all existing or future laws and regulations of the United States as if the permit required by the existing laws of the United States in such cases made and provided had been regularly obtained prior to the erection of said pier and wharf: Provided, That any changes in said pier, which the Secretary of War may deem necessary and order in the interest of navigation, shall be promptly made by the owner thereof.

## § 2.

That the right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, February 24, 1925.*

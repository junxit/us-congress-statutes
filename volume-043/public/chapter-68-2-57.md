# Chapter 57

> An Act To authorize the Court of Appeals for the First Circuit to hold sitting at San Juan, Porto Rico.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Court of Appeals for the First Circuit shall, when in its judgment the public interests require, hold a sitting of such court at San Juan, Porto Rico.


*Approved, January 8, 1925.*

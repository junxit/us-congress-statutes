# Chapter 148

> An Act To change the name of Thirty-seventh Street between Chevy Chase Circle and Reno Road.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of the street now known as Thirty-seventh Street between Chevy Chase Circle and Reno Road be, and the same is hereby changed to Chevy Chase Parkway, and the surveyor of the District of Columbia is hereby directed to enter such change on the records of his office.


*Approved, May 3, 1924.*

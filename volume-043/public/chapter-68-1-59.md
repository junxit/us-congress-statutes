# Chapter 59

> Joint Resolution To authorize the National Society United States Daughters of 1812 to place a marble tablet on the Francis Scott Key Bridge.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the National Society United States Daughters of 1812 is authorized to place, on the Francis Scott Key Bridge across Potomac River, a marble tablet, inscribed with the insignia of such society and with the last verse of the Star-Spangled Banner, after the plans and specifications for such tablet have been submitted to, and approved by, the Commission of Fine Arts on such plans and specifications.

## § 2.

Such tablet shall be erected without expense to the Government of the United States.


*Approved, March 17, 1924.*

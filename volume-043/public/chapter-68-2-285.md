# Chapter 285

> An Act To change the name of Third Place northeast to Abbey Place.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of the street not yet cut through, but now on record as Third Place northeast, be, and the same is hereby, changed to Abbey Place, and the surveyor of the District of Columbia is hereby directed to enter such change on the records of his office.


*Approved, February 21, 1925.*

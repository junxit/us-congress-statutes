# Chapter 109

> An Act To complete the construction of the Willow Creek Ranger Station, Montana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of Agriculture is hereby authorized to expend, out of any moneys appropriated for general expenses of the Forest Service, not to exceed the sum of $500 to complete the construction of the Willow Creek ranger station in the Lewis and Clark National Forest, Montana.


*Approved, April 15, 1924.*

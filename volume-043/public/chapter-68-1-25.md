# Chapter 25

> An Act To extend the time for the construction of a bridge across the Pamunkey River, in Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the times for commencing and completing the construction of a bridge authorized by Act of Congress, approved January 30, 1922, to be built by the Pamunkey Ferry Company, across the Pamunkey River, at or near Sweet Hall, in King William County, to a point opposite in New Kent County, in the Commonwealth of Virginia, are hereby extended one and three years, respectively, from the date of approval hereof.

## § 2.

That the right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, February 16, 1924.*

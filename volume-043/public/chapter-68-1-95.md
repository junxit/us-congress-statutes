# Chapter 95

> An Act Amending an Act entitled “An Act for the division of the lands and funds of the Osage Indians in Oklahoma, and for other purposes,” approved June 28, 1906, and Acts amendatory thereof and supplemental thereto.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That any right to or interest in the lands, money, or mineral interests, as provided in the Act of Congress approved June 28, 1906 (Thirty-fourth Statutes at Large, page 539), entitled “An Act for the division of the lands and funds of the Osage Indians in Oklahoma, and for other purposes,” and in Acts amendatory thereof and supplemental thereto, vested in, determined, or adjudged to be the right or property of any person not an Indian by blood, may with the approval of the Secretary of the Interior and not otherwise be sold, assigned, and transferred under such rules and regulations as the Secretary of the Interior may prescribe.


*Approved, April 12, 1924.*

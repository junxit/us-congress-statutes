# Chapter 422

> An Act To provide for terms of the United States district court at Denton, Maryland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House, of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That hereafter and until otherwise provided by law there shall be held annually on the third Monday in January and the first Monday in July terms of the district court of the United States for the district of Maryland, at the town of Denton, in said district, said terms to be in addition to the terms now required to be held in the city of Baltimore and the city of Cumberland in said district: Provided, That suitable accommodations for holding court at Denton are furnished free of expense to the United States.


*Approved, March 3, 1925.*

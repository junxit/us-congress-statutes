# Chapter 149

> An Act To amend section 2 of the Act of August 1, 1888 (Twenty-fifth Statutes at Large, page 357).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2 of the Act of August 1, 1888 (Twenty-fifth Statutes at Large, page 357), be, and the same hereby is, amended to read as follows:

> “The clerks of the several courts of the United States shall prepare and keep in their respective offices complete and convenient indices of all judgment debtors under decrees, judgments, or orders of said courts, and such indices and judgments shall at all times be open to the inspection and examination of the public.”


*Approved, February 7, 1925.*

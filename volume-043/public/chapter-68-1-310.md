# Chapter 310

> An Act Authorizing an appropriation to enable the Secretary of the Interior to purchase a tract of land, with sufficient water right attached, for the use and occupancy of the Temoak Band of homeless Indians, located at Ruby Valley, Nevada.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of $25,000, or so much thereof as may be necessary, is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any moneys in the United States Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to enable the Secretary of the Interior to purchase a tract of land, with sufficient water right attached, for the use and occupancy of the Temoak Band of homeless Indians, located at Ruby Valley, Nevada: Provided, That the title to said land is to be held in the United States for the benefit of said Indians.


*Approved, June 7, 1924.*

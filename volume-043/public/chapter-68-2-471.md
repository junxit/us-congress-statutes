# Chapter 471

> An Act To authorize the creation of a national memorial in the Harney National Forest.

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Mount Harney Memorial Association hereafter to be created by the Legislature of South Dakota is hereby authorized to carve a memorial in heroic figures commemorative of our national history and progress upon a suitable exposure of the Harney Mountain Range, located within the boundaries of the Harney National Forest in the State of South Dakota. Such association is further authorized to select the name, location, and design of such memorial: Provided, That the United States shall be put to no expense in respect of such memorial, and that the Secretary of Agriculture finds that the location of the site will not interfere with the administration of the Harney National Forest.


*Approved, March 3, 1925.*

# Chapter 548

> An Act For the relief of sufferers from cyclone in northwestern Mississippi in March, 1923.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the action of the War Department in directing the issue and the issuance of quartermaster stores out of the reserve stores for the field service of the Army of a value not exceeding $4,582.33 for the relief of sufferers from cyclone in northwestern Mississippi in March, 1923, is approved; and credit for all such supplies so issued shall be allowed in the settlement of the accounts of the officers of the Army.


*Approved, March 4, 1925.*

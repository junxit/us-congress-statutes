# Chapter 20

> An Act To extend the time of the Hudson River Connecting Railroad Corporation for the completion of its bridge across the Hudson River, the State of New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time for the completion of the bridge of the Hudson River Connecting Railroad Corporation, under the provisions of the Act approved February 15, 1921, be extended to the 1st day of January, 1925.

## § 2.

That the right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, February 14, 1924.*

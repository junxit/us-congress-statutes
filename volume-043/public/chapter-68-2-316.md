# Chapter 316

> An Act To authorize the President in certain cases to modify visé fees.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding existing law fixing the fees to be collected for visés of passports of aliens and for executing applications for such visés, the President be, and he is hereby, authorized, to the extent consistent with the public interest, to reduce such fees or to abolish them altogether, in the case of any class of aliens desiring to visit the United States who are not “immigrants ” as defined in the Immigration Act of 1924, and who are citizens or subjects of countries which grant similar privileges to citizens of the United States of a similar class visiting such countries.


*Approved, February 25, 1925.*

# Chapter 10

> Joint Resolution Extending the time for the final report of the joint congressional committee created by the Agricultural Credits Act of 1923.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 506 of the Agricultural Credits Act of 1923 is amended by striking out “ January 31 ” and inserting in lieu thereof “ June 30.”


*Approved, January 31, 1924.*

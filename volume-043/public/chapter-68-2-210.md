# Chapter 210

> An Act To authorize an increase in the limits of cost of certain naval vessels.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the limits of cost for the construction of the United States ships Lexington and Saratoga, the conversion of which vessels into airplane carriers, in accordance with the terms of the treaty providing for the limitation of naval armament, was authorized by the Act of July 1, 1922, is hereby increased to $34,000,000 each.


*Approved, February 11, 1925.*

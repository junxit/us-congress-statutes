# Chapter 307

> An Act To amend the National Defense Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Chief of Finance and the Chief of the Chemical Warfare Service of the Army shall hereafter have the rank, pay, and allowances of a major general.


*Approved, February 24, 1925.*

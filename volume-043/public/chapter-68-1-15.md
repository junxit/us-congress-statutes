# Chapter 15

> An Act For the examination and survey of Dog River, Alabama, from the Louisville and Nashville Railroad Bridge to the mouth of said river including a connection with the Mobile Bay Ship Channel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provision in section 12 of the River and Harbor Act approved September 22, 1922, providing for a preliminary examination and survey of “Channel from the mouth of Dog River, Alabama, to the ship channel in Mobile Bay,” is hereby amended to read as follows: “Dog River, Alabama, from the Louisville and Nashville Railroad Bridge to the mouth of said river, including a channel connection with the Mobile Bay Ship Channel.”


*Approved, February 2, 1924.*

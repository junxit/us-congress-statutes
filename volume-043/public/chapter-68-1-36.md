# Chapter 36

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the erection on public grounds in the city of Washington, District of Columbia, of a memorial to the Navy and marine services, to be known as Navy and Marine Memorial Dedicated to Americans Lost at Sea.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Chief of Engineers, United States Army, be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to grant permission to the honorary national committee and executive committee of the Navy and marine memorial for the erection on public grounds of the United States in the city of Washington, District of Columbia, other than those of the Capitol, the Library of Congress, and the White House, of a memorial to the Navy and marine services, to be known as Navy and Marine Memorial Dedicated to Americans Lost at Sea: Provided, That the site chosen and the design of the memorial shall be approved by the National Commission of Fine Arts, and that the United States shall be put to no expense in or by the erection or maintenance of the said memorial.


*Approved, February 16, 1924.*

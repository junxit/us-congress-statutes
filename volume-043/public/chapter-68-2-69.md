# Chapter 69

> An Act Providing for the holding of the United States district and circuit courts at Poteau, Oklahoma.

*Be, it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a term of the district court of the United States for the eastern district of Oklahoma shall be held in each and every year in the town of Poteau, Oklahoma, beginning on the first Monday in October and continuing till the business is disposed of: Provided, That suitable rooms and accommodations for holding court at Poteau are furnished free of expense to the United States.

## § 2.

That the clerk of the United States district and circuit courts at Muskogee, Oklahoma, shall be the clerk of the United States district and circuit courts at Poteau, Oklahoma, until provision be made by law for the appointment of deputy clerks at the several places of holding United States district and circuit courts in the State of Oklahoma.


*Approved, January 10, 1925.*

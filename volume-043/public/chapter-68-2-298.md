# Chapter 298

> An Act For the purchase of land adjoining Fort Bliss, Texas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a sum not to exceed $366,000 is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the purchase of land in the vicinity of and for use in connection with the present military reservation at Fort Bliss, Texas, and the Secretary of War is hereby authorized to make said purchase.


*Approved, February 24, 1925.*

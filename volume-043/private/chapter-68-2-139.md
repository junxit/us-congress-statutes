# Chapter 139

> An Act For the relief of J. R. King.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated to J. R. King, father and administrator of the late Lawrence B. King, a lieutenant in the Air Service of the Officers’ Reserve Corps, who died on duty at Clover Field, Santa Monica, California, the sum of $478.51, burial and transportation expenses.


*Approved, February 3, 1925.*

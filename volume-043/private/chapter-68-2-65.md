# Chapter 65

> An Act For the relief of F. J. Belcher, junior, trustee for Ed Fletcher.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, directed to pay to F. J. Belcher, junior, trustee for the benefit of Ed Fletcher, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $21,838, in full payment of damages to lands owned by said Ed Fletcher inflicted thereon by the Government while occupying said lands as an Army training camp.


*Approved, January 9, 1925.*

# Chapter 498

> An Act For the relief of William M. Phillipson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws William M. Phillipson shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the United States Navy: Provided, however, That no pension shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 3, 1925.*

# Chapter 185

> An Act For the relief of Clifford W. Seibel and Frank A. Vestal.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the General Accounting Office is hereby authorized to credit the accounts of Clifford W. Seibel, chemist and special disbursing agent for the Bureau of Mines, in the sum of $1,418.89, and Frank A. Vestal, special disbursing agent for the Bureau of Mines, in the sum of $546.42, with payments made by them for the maintenance, repair, and operation of certain motor-propelled, passenger-carrying vehicles.


*Approved, February 9, 1925.*

# Chapter 583

> An Act To correct the military record of Thomas C. Johnson, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, or benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers and their widows, Thomas C. Johnson, who was a private in Company G, Eighteenth Regiment Iowa Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been mustered in as a member of said company or regiment on December 22, 1863, and honorably discharged therefrom on December 1, 1864: Provided, That no bounty, pay, pension, or other emolument shall accrue prior to or by reason of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 4, 1925.*

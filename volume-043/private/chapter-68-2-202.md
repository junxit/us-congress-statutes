# Chapter 202

> An Act For the relief of Reuben R. Hunter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury of the United States not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $3,000 to Reuben R. Hunter, of Deming, New Mexico, in full settlement of all claims against the United States Government on account of injuries received in May, 1904.


*Approved, February 10, 1925.*

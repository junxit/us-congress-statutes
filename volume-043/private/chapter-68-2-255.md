# Chapter 255

> An Act For the relief of Blattmann and Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of $97,804.70 is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the purpose of compensating Blattmann and Company, of Waedenswil, Switzerland, for losses sustained through the wrongful seizure and sale of one million fifty-seven thousand one hundred pounds of devitalized gluten by the Alien Property Custodian of the United States, which belonged to the said Blattmann and Company.


*Approved, February 17, 1925.*

# Chapter 78

> An Act For the relief of Janie Beasley Glisson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Janie Beasley Glisson, $2,500, for damages suffered by her when she was struck and permanently injured by a Government airplane which was in charge of and driven by officers of the United States Army.


*Approved, January 13, 1925.*

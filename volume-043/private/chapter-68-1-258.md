# Chapter 258

> An Act For the relief of Ezra S. Pond.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $552.51 to Ezra S. Pond, a resident of 655 Pinewood Avenue, Toledo, Ohio, administrator of the estate of his only child, the late Harry S. Pond, captain Company K, Fifty-eighth Infantry, for loss of equipment sustained by the said Harry S. Pond in the sinking of the United States transport Moldavia on May 6, 1918.


*Approved, June 4, 1924.*

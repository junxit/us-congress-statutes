# Chapter 408

> An Act For the relief of Emma Zembsch.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Emma Zembsch, widowed mother of Lawrence Zembsch, late chief pharmacist of the United States Navy, is hereby allowed an amount equal to six months’ pay at the rate said Lawrence Zembsch was receiving at the date of his death: Provided, That the said Emma Zembsch establishes to the satisfaction of the Secretary of the Navy the fact that she was actually dependent upon her son, the late Lawrence Zembsch, at the time of his death.

## § 2.

That the payment of the amount of money hereby allowed and authorized to be paid to said Emma Zembsch is authorized to be made from the appropriation of officers who die while on the active list of the United States Navy.


*Approved, March 2, 1925.*

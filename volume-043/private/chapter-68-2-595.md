# Chapter 595

> An Act For the relief of Jesse P. Brown.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws Jesse P. Brown, who served in Company H, Forty-eighth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States on the 15th day of February, 1867: Provided, That no back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 4, 1925.*

# Chapter 390

> An Act For the relief of James B. Porter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $152.73, in full settlement against the Government, to James B. Porter, of Kansas City, Missouri, for damage to his property by a United States mail truck.


*Approved, June 7, 1924.*

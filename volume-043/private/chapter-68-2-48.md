# Chapter 48

> An Act For the relief of Christina Conniff

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Director of the United States Veterans’ Bureau be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to resume and complete the remainder of the payments due to Christina Conniff, widow and beneficiary of Robert E. Conniff, deceased, according to the terms of his Government life insurance policy numbered K-181801.


*Approved, January 7, 1925.*

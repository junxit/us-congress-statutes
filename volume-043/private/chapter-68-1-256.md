# Chapter 256

> An Act For the relief of Frank Vumbaca.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Frank Vumbaca, of Portland, Maine, the sum of $419, to reimburse him for damages to his house, numbered 469 Washington Avenue, Portland, Maine, caused by concussion from blasts.


*Approved, June 4, 1924.*

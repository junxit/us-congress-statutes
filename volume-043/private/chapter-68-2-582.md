# Chapter 582

> An Act For the relief of Palestine Troup.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws and the laws conferring rights and privileges upon honorably discharged soldiers, their widows and dependent relatives, Palestine Troup, formerly a private of Company E, Fortieth Regiment Indiana Volunteer Infantry, shall be held and considered to have been mustered into the military service of the United States as a member of said organization on the 12th day of April, 1864, and to have been honorably discharged on the 20th day of July, 1864: Provided, That no pay, bounty, or other emolument shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 4, 1925.*

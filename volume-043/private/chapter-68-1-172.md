# Chapter 172

> An Act Granting permission to Commander Dorr F. Tozier, United States Coast Guard, retired, to accept a gift from the King of Great Britain.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Commander Dorr F. Tozier, United States Coast Guard, retired, be, and he is hereby, authorized to accept a sword tendered to him by the King of Great Britain through the Department of State of the United States.


*Approved, May 23, 1924.*

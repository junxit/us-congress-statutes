# Chapter 30

> An Act Granting permission to Colonel Harry F. Rethers, Quartermaster Corps, United States Army, to accept the gift of a Sevres statuette entitled “Le Courage Militaire,” tendered by the President of the French Republic.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Colonel Harry F. Rethers, Quartermaster Corps, United States Army, be authorized to accept the gift of a Sevres statuette entitled “Le Courage Militaire,” tendered by His Excellency the President of the French Republic, and that the Department of State be permitted to deliver said statuette to the said Colonel Rethers.


*Approved, January 6, 1925.*

# Chapter 99

> An Act For the relief of William H. Lee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to William H. Lee, lieutenant commander, United States Navy, out of any funds in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $828.29, said sum being the amount of restitution made by him out of his private funds for money stolen from his safe by a man serving under him, for which said officer was held responsible, while stationed as recruiting officer for the United States Navy, in the city of San Francisco, California, on December 30, 1920.


*Approved, April 12, 1924.*

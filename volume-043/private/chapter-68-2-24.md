# Chapter 24

> An Act For the relief of Bertram Gardner, former collector of internal revenue for the first district of New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the account of Bertram Gardner, former collector of internal revenue for the first district of New York, by reason of shortage of one thousand six hundred and twenty-eight distilled spirit export stamps, each stamp valued at 10 cents, a total of $162.80, which stamps were destroyed by fire.


*Approved, January 5, 1925.*

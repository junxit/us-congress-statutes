# Chapter 519

> An Act Permitting the sale of the northeast quarter, section 5, township 6 north, range 15 west, one hundred and sixty acres, in Conway County, Arkansas, to A. R. Bowdre.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That upon the payment therefor at the rate of $1.25 per acre, the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to issue a patent, as hereinafter limited, to A. R. Bowdre, for the following-described land: East half of the northeast quarter of section 5, in township G north, range 15 west, Conway County, Arkansas: Provided, That there shall be reserved to the United States all oil, gas, or other minerals in the land, together with the right to prospect for, mine, and remove the same.


*Approved, March 3, 1925.*

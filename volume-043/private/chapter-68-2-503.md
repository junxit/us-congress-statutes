# Chapter 503

> An Act For the relief of Francis M. Atherton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws Francis M. Atherton shall here-after be held and considered to have been mustered into the service of the United States as a member of the First Battery Vermont Volunteer Light Artillery on the 15th day of March, 1862, and to have been honorably discharged from the same on the 1st day of February, 1863, and to have been mustered into the service of the United States as an unassigned recruit on the 19th day of December, 1863, and to have been honorably discharged from the same on the 9th day of March, 1864: Provided, That no pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 3, 1925.*

# Chapter 567

> An Act For the relief of William Kaup.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the homestead entry of William Kaup on farm unit E, Huntley reclamation project, Montana, under the Act of June 17, 1902, the said entry being known as lot 2, section 31, township 3 north, range 29 east, principal meridian, is hereby validated, subject to further compliance with the law.


*Approved, March 4, 1925.*

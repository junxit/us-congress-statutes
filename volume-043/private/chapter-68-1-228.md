# Chapter 228

> An Act For the relief of Luke Ratigan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Luke Ratigan on the retired list of the Coast Guard as an oiler, first class, retired, at the rate of pay he would be entitled to receive had he held the rating of oiler, first class, when retired.


*Approved, May 31, 1924.*

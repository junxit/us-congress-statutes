# Chapter 141

> An Act For the relief of the William J. Oliver Manufacturing Company and William J. Oliver, of Knoxville, Tennessee

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $170,757.86 to William J. Oliver for damages sustained growing out of the seizure and holding by the Government of the William J. Oliver manufacturing establishment at Knoxville, Tennessee.


*Approved, February 5, 1925.*

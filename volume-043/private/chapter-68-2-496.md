# Chapter 496

> An Act For the relief of James F. Jenkins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to James F. Jenkins, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $21,000, being in payment for six hundred bales of cotton linters taken by the United States on or about July 26, 1918, and the storage thereon up to and including December 14, 1920.


*Approved, March 3, 1925.*

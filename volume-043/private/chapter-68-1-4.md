# Chapter 4

> AN ACT Granting a franking privilege to Florence Kling Harding.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all mail matter sent by the post by Florence Kling Harding, widow of the late Warren Gamaliel Harding, under her written autograph signature, be conveyed free of postage during her natural life.


*Approved, January 25, 1924.*

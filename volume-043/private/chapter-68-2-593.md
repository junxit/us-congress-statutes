# Chapter 593

> An Act For the relief of Matthew Thomas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to remove the charge of desertion now appearing in the military record of Matthew Thomas, of Eaton, Colorado, who served in Company C, One hundred and fifty-fourth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, Civil War, and issue to him an honorable discharge therefrom: Provided, That no back pay, pension, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 4, 1925.*

# Chapter 188

> An Act For the relief of Beatrice J. Kettlewell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $5,000, to Beatrice J. Kettlewell, in compensation for injuries sustained on June 29, 1922, as the result of an accident in the Stanislaus National Forest.


*Approved, February 9, 1925.*

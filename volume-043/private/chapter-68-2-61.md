# Chapter 61

> An Act For the relief of Edward N. McCarty.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $1,200, in full settlement against the Government, to Edward N. McCarty, to compensate him for injuries to his eyes while an employee in the post office at Mattoon, Illinois.


*Approved, January 9, 1925.*

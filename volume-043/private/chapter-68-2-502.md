# Chapter 502

> An Act For the relief of Mrs. M. J. Adams.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Mrs. M. J. Adams, mother of O. L. Adams, deceased, of the county of Tippah and the State of Mississippi, the sum of $3,000, in full compensation for the death of her son, O. L. Adams, who as a civilian in the service of the United States was killed in a fight with Army deserters in Tippah County, Mississippi, in 1918.


*Approved, March 3, 1925.*

# Chapter 379

> An Act For the relief of John F. Malley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit to John F. Malley, former collector of internal revenue of the State of Massachusetts, and his accounts with the sums of $1,200 and $683.34, respectively, being the value of internal revenue stamps charged to him, stolen, lost, or accidentally destroyed at Boston, Massachusetts, in the spring and summer of 1918.


*Approved, February 28, 1925.*

# Chapter 177

> An Act For the relief of Emil L. Flaten.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the, United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the account of Emil L. Flaten, postmaster at Moorhead, Minnesota, in the sum of $16,391.99, due to the United States on account of postage stamps, key-deposit funds, war-savings and thrift-stamp funds, and war-tax revenue stamp funds which were lost as the result of burglary on October 27, 1920.


*Approved, February 9, 1925.*

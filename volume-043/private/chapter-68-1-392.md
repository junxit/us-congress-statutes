# Chapter 392

> An Act For the relief of Hubert Reynolds.

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act entitled “An Act for the relief of Hubert Reynolds,” approved September 21, 1922, be, and the same is hereby, amended by substituting $57,983.20 for the amount $69,300 in line five, in order that the Postmaster General may be authorized to credit the former postmaster at Greeley, Colorado, for the actual value of certain war savings stamps instead of their maturity value as provided by the Act.


*Approved, June 7, 1924.*

# Chapter 569

> An Act For the relief of George E. Harpham.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, George E. Harpham, who was a sergeant of Company D, One hundred and thirty-fourth Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a sergeant of said company and regiment on the 13th day of May, 1918: Provided, That no back pay, pension, or other emolument shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 4, 1925.*

# Chapter 189

> An Act For the relief of Arthur Frost.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated to Arthur Frost, the sum of $960 in full for damages suffered by reason of being negligently shot and seriously injured by a regularly enlisted soldier of the United States while in pursuit of a deserter and in the legal discharge of his duty as a military policeman.


*Approved, May 24, 1924.*

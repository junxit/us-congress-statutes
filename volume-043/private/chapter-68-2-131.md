# Chapter 131

> An Act For the relief of the Pitt River Power Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comp-troller General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized to adjust and settle the claim of the Pitt River Power Company in the amount of $1,767 paid to the United States and deposited with the Treasury, in connection with its application for a water power permit on Pitt River, California, and to certify the same to Congress.


*Approved, February 2, 1925.*

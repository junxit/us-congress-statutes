# Chapter 388

> An Act For the relief of Charles T. Norman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Charles T. Norman, of Richmond, Virginia, the sum of $882.24, the same being a refund of income tax erroneously collected.


*Approved, June 7, 1924.*

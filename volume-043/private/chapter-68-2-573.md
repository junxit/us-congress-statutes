# Chapter 573

> An Act For the relief of Emelus S. Tozier.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged men of the United States Navy, Emelus S. Tozier, formerly a seaman in the United States Navy, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged on the 31st day of January, 1865.


*Approved, March 4, 1925.*

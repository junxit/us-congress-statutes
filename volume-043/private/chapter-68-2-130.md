# Chapter 130

> An Act To allow credit in the accounts of A. W. Smith.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed, in the settlement of the accounts of A. W. Smith, fiscal agent, Forest Service, United States Department of Agriculture, to allow credit in the sum of $111.75 now standing as a disallowance in said accounts on the books of the General Accounting Office, covering expenses incurred during the fiscal year ended June 30, 1917, in the erection of a building at the Bacon ranger station on the Klamath National Forest, California.


*Approved, February 2, 1925.*

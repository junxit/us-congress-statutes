# Chapter 257

> An Act For the relief of Harold Kernan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to credit the account of Harold Kernan, now serving as captain, Twelfth Field Artillery, United States Army, in the sum of $3,426, with which said officer was charged for money stolen from an enlisted man serving under him, for which said officer was held responsible, while stationed at the city of Brest, France, in October, 1919.


*Approved, June 4, 1924.*

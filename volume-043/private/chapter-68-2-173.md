# Chapter 173

> An Act For the relief of Clara T. Black.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the account of Clara T. Black, formerly acting postmaster at Taylorsville, Kentucky, in the sum of $10,578.28, due the United States on account of postal stamps, war-saving certificate stamps, United States Government thrift stamps, and war-tax revenue stamps which were lost as the result of bank burglary on January 13, 1920.


*Approved, February 9, 1925.*

# Chapter 578

> An Act For the relief of Thomas G. Patten.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the account of Thomas G. Patten, former postmaster at New York, New York, with $69,564.80, said sum being a balance due the United States which is chargeable to the embezzlement of postal savings funds by two former employees of Station I, New York. New York.


*Approved, March 4, 1925.*

# Chapter 261

> An Act For the relief of the San Diego Consolidated Gas and Electric Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to the San Diego Consolidated Gas and Electric Company, of San Diego, California, the sum of $2,632.57, in full satisfaction of all claims of such company against the United States on account of injuries to pole tower structures and high tension transmission lines of said company north of San Diego, caused on September 15, 1922, by the collision with such transmission lines of an airplane piloted by Ensign Jack Shafer, deceased, in the course of his regular duties while attached to the Naval Air Station at San Diego, California.


*Approved, February 17, 1925.*

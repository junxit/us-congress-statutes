# Chapter 205

> An Act For the relief of James Moran.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws and the laws conferring rights and privileges upon honorably discharged soldiers, James Moran, late corporal in the One hundred and ninth Company, United States Coast Artillery Corps, shall be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States is a member of Company A, Ninth Regiment United States Infantry: Provided, That no back pay, bounty, or other emoluments shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, May 28, 1924.*

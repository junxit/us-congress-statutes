# Chapter 594

> An Act For the relief of Herman R. Woltman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws Herman R. Woltman, late of Company F, Thirty-second Regiment Michigan Volunteer Infantry, and Company B, Forty-first Regiment United States Volunteer Infantry, shall be considered as having been honorably discharged from Company H, Nineteenth Regiment United States Infantry: Provided, That no back pay, pension, or allowances shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 4, 1925.*

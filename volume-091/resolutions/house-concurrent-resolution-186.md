# House Concurrent Resolution 186

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Wednesday, April 6, 1977, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian on Monday, April 18, 1977, and that when the Senate recesses on Thursday, April 7, 1977, it stand in recess until 12 o’clock meridian on Monday, April 18, 1977.


*Passed April 5, 1977.*

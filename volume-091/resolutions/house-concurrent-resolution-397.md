# House Concurrent Resolution 397

> CORRECTION IN ENROLLED BILL S. 1131

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Secretary of the Senate, in the enrollment of the bill (S. 1131), to authorize appropriations for Nuclear Regulatory Commission for the fiscal year 1978, and for other purposes, shall make the following correction:

In section 4, change “improved systems” to “improved safety systems”.


*Passed November 29, 1977.*

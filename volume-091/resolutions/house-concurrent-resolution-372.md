# House Concurrent Resolution 372

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Thursday, October 6, 1977, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian on Tuesday, October 11, 1977.


*Passed October 6, 1977.*

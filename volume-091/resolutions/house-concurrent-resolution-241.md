# House Concurrent Resolution 241

> “GIVE YOURSELF CREDIT: GUIDE TO CONSUMER CREDIT LAWS”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be reprinted for the use of the Subcommittee on Consumer Affairs of the Committee on Banking, Finance and Urban Affairs one hundred thousand copies of House Document No. 95–167 entitled “Give Yourself Credit: Guide to Consumer Credit Laws”.


*Passed October 31, 1977.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 267

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Thursday, June 30, 1977, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian on Monday, July 11, 1977, and that when the Senate recesses on Friday, July 1, 1977, it stand in recess until 1 o’clock postmeridian on Monday July 11, 1977.


*Passed June 30, 1977.*

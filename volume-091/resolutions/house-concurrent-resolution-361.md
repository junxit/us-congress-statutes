# House Concurrent Resolution 361

> CORRECTIONS IN ENROLLED BILL H.R. 6111

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That, in the enrollment of the bill (H.R. 6111) to amend the Juvenile Justice and Delinquency Prevention Act of 1974, and for other purposes, the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall make the following corrections:

- **(1)** Insert “and” before “section 250” in section 3(a) (3) (B) of the bill.
- **(2)** Strike out “,and any other place it appears therein” in section 3(a) (3) (B) of the bill.


*Passed September 29, 1977.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 53

> “PANAMA CANAL TREATIES”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on Foreign Relations two thousand five hundred additional copies of each part of its hearings entitled “Panama Canal Treaties”.


*Agreed to November 3, 1977.*

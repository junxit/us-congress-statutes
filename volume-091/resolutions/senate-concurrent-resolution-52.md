# Senate Concurrent Resolution 52

> “THE SENATE CHAMBER, 1810-1859”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Commission on Art and Antiquities of the United States Senate fifty thousand additional copies of the booklet entitled “The Senate Chamber, 1810–1859”.


*Agreed to November 3, 1977.*

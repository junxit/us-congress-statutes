# House Concurrent Resolution 330

> ADJOURNMENT—SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the Senate adjourns on Friday, August 5, 1977, or on Saturday, August 6, 1977, it stand adjourned pursuant to the provisions of House Concurrent Resolution 317.


*Passed August 5, 1977.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 442

> ADJOURNMENT SINE DIE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Thursday, December 15, 1977, they shall stand adjourned sine die.

## § 2.

During the adjournment of both Houses of Congress as provided in section 1, the Secretary of the Senate and the Clerk or the House, respectively be, and they hereby are, authorized to receive messages, including veto messages, from the President of the United States.


*Passed December 15, 1977.*

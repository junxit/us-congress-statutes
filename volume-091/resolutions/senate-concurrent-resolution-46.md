# Senate Concurrent Resolution 46

> CORRECTIONS IN ENROLLED BILL H.R. 6415

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Clerk of the House of Representatives, in the enrollment of the bill (H.R. 6415) to extend and amend the Export-Import Bank Act of 1945, shall make the following corrections:

At the beginning of the bill, strike out “That”.

At the end of the bill, strike out “8 of the Export-Import Bank Act of 1945 is amended by striking out ‘June 30’ and inserting in lieu thereof ‘September 30’.”.


*Agreed to October 14, 1977.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 196

> JOINT MEETING

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Wednesday, April 20, 1977, at 9 o’clock post-meridian, for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed April 18, 1977.*

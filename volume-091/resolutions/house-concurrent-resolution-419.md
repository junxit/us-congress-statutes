# House Concurrent Resolution 419

> CORRECTIONS IN ENROLLED BILL H.R. 7345

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (H.R. 7345) to amend title 38 of the United States Code to increase the rates of disability and death pension and to increase the rates of dependency and indemnity compensation for parents, and for other purposes, the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall make the following corrections:

- **(1)** In section 101(1) of the bill, strike out “section (b)(1)” and insert in lieu thereof “subsection (b) (1)”.
- **(2)** In the table in section 415(c) (1) of title 38, United States Code, as proposed in section 201(3) of the bill, strike out “3,700” and insert in lieu thereof “3,770”.


*Passed November 29, 1977.*

# Private Law 95–12

> An Act For the relief of Lee Young Soo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act , Lee Young Soo may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b) (1) (F) of such Act upon approval of a petition filed on her behalf by Lieutenant Colonel and Mrs. Duane L. Ginter, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of such Act. The parents, brothers, and sisters of the said Lee Young Soo shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved November 7, 1977.*

# Private Law 95–23

> An Act For the relief of Patricia R. Tully.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding section 212(a)(23) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1182(a) (23)), Patricia R. Tully may be issued a visa and admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of such Act: Provided, That this exemption shall apply only to a ground for exclusion or which the Department of State or the Department of Justice had knowledge prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved November 23, 1977.*

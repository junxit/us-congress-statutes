# Private Law 95–7

> An Act For the relief of Velzora Carr.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for purposes of determining any entitlement of Velzora Carr of Fort Worth, Texas, to a survivor annuity under section 8341 of title 5, United States Code, her deceased husband, Allen W. Carr, is deemed to have made a timely election, pursuant to section 5(c) of the Act of January 8, 1971 (Public Law 91–658; 84 Stat. 1963), for the purpose of providing a survivor annuity for Velzora Carr.

## § 2.

For purposes of chapter 83 of title 5, United States Code, any entitlement of Velzora Carr to a survivor annuity by reason of the first section of this Act shall be effective as of December 19, 1971, the day after the date of death of Allen W. Carr. The aggregate amount to which Velzora Carr may be so entitled for the period before the date of the enactment of this Act shall be paid in a lump sum.


*Approved August 15, 1977.*

# Private Law 95–6

> An Act For the relief of the Federal Life and Casualty Company of Battle Creek, Michigan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 709 of title 18, United States Code, in the event of any reorganization, merger, or consolidation of the Federal Life and Casualty Company, Battle Creek, Michigan, which was originally incorporated in 1906 in the State of Michigan as the Federal Casualty Company, the surviving or successor entity shall be entitled to continue to use the word “Federal” in its new name.


*Approved August 15, 1977.*

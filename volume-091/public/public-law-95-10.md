# Public Law 95–10

> An Act To rescind certain budget authority recommended in the message of the President of September 22, 1976 (H. Doc. 94–620), transmitted pursuant to the Impoundment Control Act of 1974.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the following rescission of budget authority contained in the message of the President of September 22, 1976 (II. Doc. 94–620), is made pursuant to the Impoundment Control Act of 1974; namely:

## DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

## Bureau of Mines

## helium fund

Contract authority under this head provided by Public Law 87–122 for the fiscal year 1977 is rescinded in the amount of $47,500,000.


*Approved March 10, 1977.*

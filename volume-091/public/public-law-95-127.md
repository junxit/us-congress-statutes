# Public Law 95–127

> An Act To amend the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 to extend the authorization of appropriations contained in such Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 319 of the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 (2 U.S.C. 439c) is amended by striking out “and” after “1976”, and by inserting after “1977” the following: “, and $7,811,500 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1978”.


*Approved October 12, 1977.*

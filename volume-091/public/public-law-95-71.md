# Public Law 95–71

> An Act Granting the consent of Congress to an amendment to the Sabine River Compact entered into by the States of Texas and Louisiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the consent of Congress is given to an amendment to the Sabine River Compact (68 Stat. 690) entered into by the States of Texas and Louisiana, which amendment strikes out the last paragraph of the preamble to such compact.

## § 2.

The right to amend or repeal the first section of this Act is expressly reserved.


*Approved July 23, 1977.*

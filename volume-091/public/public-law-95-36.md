# Public Law 95–36

> An Act To authorize appropriations for the administration of the Deepwater Port Act of 1974.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 25 of the Deepwater Port Act of 1974 (Public Law 93–627) is hereby amended to read as follows:

> ## § 25. “authorization for appropriations
>
> There is authorized to be appropriated for administration of this Act, not to exceed $2,500,000 per fiscal year for the fiscal years ending June 30, 1975, June 30, 1976, September 30, 1977, September 30, 1978, September 30, 1979, and September 30, 1980.”.


*Approved June 1, 1977.*

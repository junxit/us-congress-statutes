# Public Law 95–204

> An Act To amend title 38 of the United States Code to increase the rates of disability and death pension and to increase the rates of dependency and indemnity compensation for parents, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That this Act be cited as the “Veterans and Survivors Pension Adjustment Act of 1977”.

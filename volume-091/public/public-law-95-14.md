# Public Law 95–14

> An Act To amend the Small Business Act and the Small Business Investment Act of 1958.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. business loan and investment fund

Section 4(c) (4) of the Small Business Act is amended by striking out “$6,000,000,000” and by inserting in lieu thereof “$7.400,000.000”.

## § 2. economic opportunity loans

Section 4(c)(4) of the Small Business Act is amended by striking out “$450,000,000” and by inserting in lieu thereof “$525,000,000”.

## § 3. small business investment companies

Section 4(c)(4) of the Small Business Act is amended by striking out “$725,000,000” and by inserting in lieu thereof “$887,500,000”.

## § 4. surety bond guarantees

Section 412 of the Small Business Investment Act of 1958 is amended by striking out “$56,500,000” and by inserting in lieu thereof “$110,000,000”.


*Approved March 24, 1977.*

# Public Law 95–38

> An Act To extend the Defense Production Act of 1950, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 5(g) of the Privacy Act of 1974 (5 U.S.C. 552a note) is amended by striking out the last sentence thereof and inserting in lieu thereof the following: “The Commission shall cease to exist on September 30, 1977.”.


*Approved June 1, 1977.*

# Public Law 95–179

> An Act To amend title 3 of the United States Code to change the name of the Executive Protective Service.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That chapter 3 of title 3 of the United States Code is amended by striking out “Executive Protective Service” each place it appears in such chapter and inserting in lieu thereof at each such place the following: “United States Secret Service Uniformed Division”. Any reference in any other law or in any regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to the Executive Protective Service shall be held to be a reference to the United States Secret Service Uniformed Division.


*Approved November 15, 1977.*

# Public Law 95–7

> Joint Resolution Extending the filing date of the 1977 Joint Economic Report.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions of clause (3), Section 5(b) of the Employment Act of 1946 (15 U.S.C. 1024(b)), the Joint Economic Committee shall file its report on the President’s l977 Economic Report with the Senate and House of Representatives not later than March 30, 1977.


*Approved February 23, 1977.*

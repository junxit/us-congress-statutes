# Public Law 95–20

> An Act To amend the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 to increase the amount authorized to be appropriated for the Securities and Exchange Commission for fiscal year 1977.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 35 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (15 U.S.C. 78a et seq.) is amended by striking the amount “$55,000,000” before the words “for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1977” and inserting in lieu thereof the amount “$56,500,000”.


*Approved April 13, 1977.*

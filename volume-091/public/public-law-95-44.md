# Public Law 95–44

> An Act To authorize appropriations for fiscal year 1978 for carrying out the Comprehensive Employment and Training Act of 1973 as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. short title

This Act may be cited as the “Comprehensive Employment and Training Act Amendments of 1977”.

## § 2. authorization for fiscal year 1978

- **(a)** Section 4(a) of the Comprehensive Employment and Training Act of 1973, as amended, is amended by striking out “three succeeding fiscal years” and inserting in lieu thereof “four succeeding fiscal years”.
- **(b)** Section 601 of such Act is amended by inserting after “fiscal year 1977” the words “and for fiscal year 1978”.
- **(c)** Section 607(a) of such Act is amended by inserting after “fiscal year 1977” the words “and fiscal year 1978”, and by inserting the word “each.” before “such fiscal year” both places such language appears therein.


*Approved June 15, 1977.*

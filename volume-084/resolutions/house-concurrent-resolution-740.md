# House Concurrent Resolution 740

> LEGISLATIVE REORGANIZATION ACT OF 1970—HEARINGS

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the House Committee on Rules three thousand additional copies of its hearings accompanying the Legislative Reorganization Act of 1970.


*Passed October 14, 1970.*

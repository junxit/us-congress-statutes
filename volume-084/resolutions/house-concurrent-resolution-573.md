# House Concurrent Resolution 573

> THE OHIO STATE UNIVERSITY—ONE HUNDREDTH ANNIVERSARY

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Congress sends congratulations and greetings to The Ohio State University on the occasion of the one hundredth anniversary of its founding, and extends the hope of the people of the United States that The Ohio State University will continue to grow and prosper in centuries to come.


*Passed June 26, 1970.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 775

> SIGNING OF ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That notwithstanding any adjournment of the Congress until November 16, 1970, the Speaker of the House of Representatives and the President of the Senate, the President pro tempore, or the Acting President pro tempore, be, and they are hereby, authorized to sign enrolled bills and joint resolutions duly passed by the two Houses and found truly enrolled.


*Passed October 13, 1970.*

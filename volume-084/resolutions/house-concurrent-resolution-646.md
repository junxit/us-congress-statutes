# House Concurrent Resolution 646

> ADJOURNMENT-HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Wednesday, May 27, 1970, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Monday, June 1, 1970.


*Passed May 27, 1970.*

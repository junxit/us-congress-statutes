# House Concurrent Resolution 778

> CORRECTIONS OF ENROLLED BILL H. R. 17654

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (H.R. 17654) to improve the operation of the legislative branch of the Federal Government, and for other purposes, the Clerk of the House of Representatives is authorized and directed—

- **(1)** in paragraph (6) (c) of Rule XXV of the Senate, as amended by section 132(d) of the bill, to strike out “subparagraph (a) ” each place it appeal’s and insert “subparagraph (b) ”; and
- **(2)** in section 601(1) of the bill, to insert “, and section 105 (e) and (f) of the Legislative Branch Appropriation Act, 1968, as amended by section 305 of this Act” after “section 321 of this Act” rather than after “title IV, of this Act” as directed by Senate amendment numbered 52.


*Passed October 13, 1970.*

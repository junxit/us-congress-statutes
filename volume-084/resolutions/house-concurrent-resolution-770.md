# House Concurrent Resolution 770

> “ANATOMY OF A REVOLUTIONARY MOVEMENT: ‘STUDENTS FOR A DEMOCRATIC SOCIETY’ ”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed for the use of the Committee on Internal Security five thousand additional copies of the report entitled “Anatomy of a Revolutionary Movement: ‘Students for a Democratic Society’ ”, Ninety-first Congress, second session.


*Passed October 14, 1970.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 584

> HISTORY OF THE HOUSE COMMITTEE ON AGRICULTURE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed, with illustrations, as a House document a brief history of the House Committee on Agriculture, and materials relating to it, in connection with its one hundred and fiftieth anniversary (1820–1970).

## § 2.

In addition to the usual number, there shall be printed five thousand copies of such document for use of the Committee on Agriculture.

## § 3.

Seventy-five copies shall be bound with a buckram cover and gold lettering for the use of the Committee on Agriculture.


*Passed May 28, 1970.*

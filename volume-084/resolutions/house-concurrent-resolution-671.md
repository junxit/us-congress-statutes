# House Concurrent Resolution 671

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Wednesday, July 1, 1970, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Monday, July 6, 1970.


*Passed June 30, 1970.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 520

> “REPORT OF SPECIAL STUDY MISSION TO SOUTHERN AFRICA”

*Resolved by the House, of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That an additional one thousand copies of House Report 91–610, Ninety-first Congress, first session, entitled “Report of Special Study Mission to Southern Africa” be printed for the use of the Committee on Foreign Affairs of the House of Representatives.


*Passed May 28, 1970.*

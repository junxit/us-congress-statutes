# House Concurrent Resolution 788

> “HEARINGS RELATING TO VARIOUS BILLS TO REPEAL THE EMERGENCY DETENTION ACT OF 1950”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Internal Security three thousand additional copies of the publication entitled “Hearings Relating to Various Bills To Repeal the Emergency Detention Act of 1950,” Ninety-first Congress, second session.


*Passed December 19, 1970.*

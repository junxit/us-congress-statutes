# House Concurrent Resolution 183

> SCHOOL PRAYERS—HEARINGS

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on the Judiciary, House of Representatives, one thousand additional copies each of the publications entitled “Proposed Amendments to the Constitution Relating to Prayers and Bible Reading in the Public Schools, Hearings Before the Committee on the Judiciary, House of Representatives, parts 1, 2, and 3”, Eighty-eighth Congress, second session.


*Passed December 1, 1970,*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 81

> COPYRIGHT LAW REVISION—HEARINGS

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on the Judiciary two thousand additional copies of parts 1, 2, 3, 4, and index of the hearings before its Subcommittee on Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights during the Ninetieth Congress on Copyright Law Revision (S. 597).


*Agreed to October 7, 1970.*

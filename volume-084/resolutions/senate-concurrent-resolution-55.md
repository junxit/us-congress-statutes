# Senate Concurrent Resolution 55

> “ORGANIZED CRIME CONTROL ACT OF 1969”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on the Judiciary one thousand two hundred additional copies of its report of the current Congress entitled “Organized Crime Control Act of 1969” (Senate Report 91–617).


*Agreed to March 25, 1970.*

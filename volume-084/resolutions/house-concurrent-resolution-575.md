# House Concurrent Resolution 575

> OHIO NORTHERN UNIVERSITY—ONE HUNDREDTH ANNIVERSARY

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Congress sends congratulations and greetings to Ohio Northern University on the occasion of the one hundredth anniversary of its founding and’ extends the hope of the people of the United States that Ohio Northern University will continue to grow and prosper in centuries yet to come.


*Passed June 26, 1970.*

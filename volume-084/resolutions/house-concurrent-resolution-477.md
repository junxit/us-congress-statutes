# House Concurrent Resolution 477

> JOINT MEETING

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Thursday, January 22, 1970, at 12:30 p.m., for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed January 19, 1970.*

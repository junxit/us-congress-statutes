# House Concurrent Resolution 771

> “EFFECTS OF POPULATION GROWTH ON NATURAL RESOURCES AND THE ENVIRONMENT”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed for the use of the Committee on Government Operations, House of Representatives, five thousand additional copies of the hearings by the Subcommittee on Conservation and Natural Resources in September 1969 entitled “Effects of Population Growth on Natural Resources and the Environment”.


*Passed December 29, 1970.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 585

> “SUMMARY OF VETERANS LEGISLATION REPORTED, NINETY-FIRST CONGRESS”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed for the use of the Committee on Veterans’ Affairs of the House of Representatives fifty-six thousand one hundred copies of a publication entitled “Summary of Veterans Legislation Reported, Ninety-first Congress”, with an additional forty-three thousand nine hundred copies for the use of Members of the House of Representatives.


*Passed May 28, 1970.*

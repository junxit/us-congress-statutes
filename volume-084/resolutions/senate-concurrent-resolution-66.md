# Senate Concurrent Resolution 66

> SPACE PROGRAM BENEFITS—HEARINGS

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Aeronautical and Space Sciences Committee six thousand additional copies of its hearings held during the Ninety-first Congress, second session, on space program benefits.


*Agreed to June 11, 1970,*

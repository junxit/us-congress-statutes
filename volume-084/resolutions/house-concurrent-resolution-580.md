# House Concurrent Resolution 580

> “MARIHUANA”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed for the use of the Select Committee on Crime of the House of Representatives twenty-five thousand additional copies of House Report Numbered 978 of the Ninety-first Congress, second session, entitled “Marihuana”.


*Passed June 11, 1970.*

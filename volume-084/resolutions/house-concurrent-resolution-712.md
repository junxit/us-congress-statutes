# House Concurrent Resolution 712

> COMMITTEE ON INTERNAL SECURITY—1969 REPORT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Internal Security five thousand additional copies of the committee’s annual report for the year 1969, House Report Numbered 91–983, Ninety-first Congress, second session.


*Passed October 14, 1970.*

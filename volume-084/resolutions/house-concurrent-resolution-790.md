# House Concurrent Resolution 790

> “DISCRIMINATION AGAINST WOMEN”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That five thousand additional copies of parts I and II of the hearings before the Special Subcommittee on Education of the Committee on Education and Labor of the House of Representatives, Ninety-first Congress, second session, on section 805 of H.R. 16098, entitled “Discrimination Against Women”, be printed concurrently for the use of the Special Subcommittee on Education.


*Passed December 29, 1970.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 786

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the Congress adjourns on Wednesday, November 25, 1970, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian. Monday, November 30, 1970.


*Passed November 24, 1970.*

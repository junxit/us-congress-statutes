# House Concurrent Resolution 799

> ADJOURNMENT SINE DIE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Saturday, January 2, 1971, and that when they adjourn on said day, they stand adjourned sine die.


*Passed January 2, 1971.*

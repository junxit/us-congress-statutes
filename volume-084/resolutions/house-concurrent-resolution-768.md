# House Concurrent Resolution 768

> CORRECTION OF ENROLLED BILL H. R. 15124

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (H.R. 15424) to amend the Merchant Marine Act, 1936, the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall add at the end thereof the following new section:

> ## § 44.
>
> This act may be cited as the ‘Merchant Marine Act of 1970’.”


*Passed October 7, 1970.*

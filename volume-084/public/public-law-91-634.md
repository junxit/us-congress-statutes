# Public Law 91–634

> AN ACT To designate the lake formed by the waters impounded by the Butler Valley Dam, California, as “Blue Lake”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the lake formed by the waters impounded by the Butler Valley Dam in the State of California, located on the Mad River, shall hereafter be known as Blue Lake and any law, regulation, document, or record of the United States in which such lake is designated or referred to shall be held to refer to such lake under and by the name of “Blue Lake”.


*Approved December 31, 1970.*

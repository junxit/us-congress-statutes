# Public Law 91–260

> JOINT RESOLUTION To further amend the Elementary and Secondary Education Act.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, effective April 13, 1970, clause (A) in .clause (1) of section 5(c) of the Act of September 23, 1950 (Public Law 815, Eighty-first Congress) is amended by striking out “at least 10 per centum” and inserting in lieu thereof “at least 6 per centum”.


*Approved May 21, 1970.*

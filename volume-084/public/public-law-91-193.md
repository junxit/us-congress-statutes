# Public Law 91–193

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making further continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 1970, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That clause (c) of section 102 of the joint resolution of November 14, 1969 (Public Law 91–117), as amended, is hereby further amended by striking out “January 30, 1970” and inserting in lieu thereof “February 28, 1970”.


*Approved February 9, 1970.*

# Public Law 91–276

> AN ACT To authorize the Public Printer to fix the subscription price of the daily Congressional Record.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the last full paragraph of section 906 of title 44, United States Code, is amended to read as follows:

> “The Public Printer may furnish the daily Record to subscribers at a price determined by the Public Printer based upon the cost of printing and distribution, such price to be payable in advance.”


*Approved June 12, 1970.*

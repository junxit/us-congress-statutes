# Public Law 91–302

> AN ACT To amend section 703(b) of title 10, United States Code, to extend the authority to grant a special thirty-day leave for members of the uniformed services who voluntarily extend their tours of duty in hostile fire areas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 703 (b) of title 10, United States Code, is amended by striking out “June 30, 1970” and inserting in lieu thereof “June 30, 1972”.


*Approved July 2, 1970.*

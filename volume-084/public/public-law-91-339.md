# Public Law 91–339

> AN ACT To amend the Federal Youth Corrections Act (18 U.S.C. 5005 et seq.) to permit examiners to conduct interviews with youth offenders.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 5014 of title 18, United States Code, is amended by inserting “, or an examiner designated by the Division,” after the words “of the Di vision”.

## § 2.

Section 5020 of title 18, United States Code, is amended by deleting the words “or a member thereof” and inserting in lieu thereof “, a member thereof, or an examiner designated by the Division”.


*Approved July 17, 1970.*

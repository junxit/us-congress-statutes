# Public Law 91–625

> AN ACT To designate the lake formed by Die waters impounded by the Libby Dam, Montana, as “Lake Kooeanusa”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the lake formed by the waters impounded by the Libby Dam in the State of Montana shall hereafter be known as Lake Koocanusa and any law, regulation, document, or record of the United States in which such lake is designated or referred to shall be held to refer to such lake under and by the name of “Lake Koocanusa”.


*Approved December 31, 1970.*

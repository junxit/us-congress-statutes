# Public Law 91–396

> AN ACT To provide that a headstone or marker be furnished at Government expense for the unmarked grave of any Medal of Honor recipient.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, the first section of the Act entitled “An Act to provide for the procurement and supply of Government, headstones or markers for unmarked graves of members of the Armed Forces dying in the service or after honorable discharge, therefrom, and other persons, and for other purposes”, approved July 1, 1948 (24 U.S.C. 279a), is amended by inserting immediately after paragraph (5) thereof the following new paragraph:

> - **(6)** Persons awarded the Medal of Honor.”


*Approved September 1, 1970.*

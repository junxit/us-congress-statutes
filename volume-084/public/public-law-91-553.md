# Public Law 91–553

> JOINT RESOLUTION. To amend the joint resolution authorizing appropriations for the payment by the United States of its share of the expenses of the Pan American Railways Congress Association.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Public Law 80–794, Eightieth Congress, approved June 28, 1948, is amended by striking out “$5,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$15,000” in section 2(a).


*Approved December 16, 1970.*

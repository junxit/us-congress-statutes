# Public Law 91–236

> JOINT RESOLUTION To authorize the President to proclaim the lust Friday of April 1970 as “National Arbor Day”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is hereby authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating the last Friday of April 1970 “National Arbor Day” and calling upon the people of the United States to observe such day with appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Approved April 27, 1970.*

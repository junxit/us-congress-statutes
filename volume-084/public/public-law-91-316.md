# Public Law 91–316

> AN ACT To extend the Clean Air Act, as amended, and the Solid Waste Disposal Act, as amended, for a period of sixty days.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That pending extensions by Act of. Congress of the Clean Air Act and Solid Waste Disposal Act, the authorizations contained in sections 104(c) and 309 of the Clean Air Act, as amended, for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1970, and the authorization contained in section 210 of the Solid Waste Disposal Act, as amended, for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1970, shall remain available through August 31, 1970, notwithstanding any provisions of those sections.


*Approved July 10, 1970.*

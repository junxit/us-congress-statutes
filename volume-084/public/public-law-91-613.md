# Public Law 91–613

> AN ACT To amend the definition of “metal bearing ores” in the Tariff Schedules of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That headnote 2(a) of part 1 of schedule 6 of the Tariff Schedules of the United States (19 U.S.C. 1202) is amended by inserting “manganese,” after “copper,”.


*Approved December 31, 1970.*

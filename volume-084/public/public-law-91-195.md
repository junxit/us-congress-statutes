# Public Law 91–195

> JOINT RESOLUTION To authorize the President to designate the period beginning February 13, 1970, and ending February 19, 1970, as “Mineral Industry Week”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is hereby authorized and requested Io issue a proclamation designating the week of February 13 through February 19, 1970, as “Mineral Industry Week”, and calling upon the people of the United States to observe such a week with appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Approved February 11, 1970.*

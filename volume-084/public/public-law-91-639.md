# Public Law 91–639

> AN ACT To change the name of the West Branch Dam and Reservoir, Mahoning River, Ohio, to the Michael J. Kirwan Dani and Reservoir.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the West Branch Dam and Reservoir, Mahoning River, Ohio, authorized by section 203 of the River and Harbor Act of 1958 (72 Stat. 297), shall hereafter be known as the Michael J. Kirwan Dam and Reservoir, and any law, regulation, document, or record of the United States in which such project is designated or referred to shall be held to refer to such project under and by the name of “Michael J. Kirwan Dam and Reservoir”.


*Approved December 31, 1970.*

# Public Law 91–565

> AN ACT To amend section 3(b) of the Securities Act of 1933 to permit the exemption of security issues, not exceeding $500,000 in aggregate amount, from the provisions of such Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3(b) of the Securities Act of 1933 (15 U.S.C. 77c. (b)) is amended by striking out “$300,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$500,000”.


*Approved December 19, 1970.*

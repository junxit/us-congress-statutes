# Public Law 91–406

> AN ACT To amend the National Aeronautics and Space Act of 14158 to provide that the Secretary of Transportation shall he a member of the National Aeronautics and Space Council.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 201 (a) of the National Aeronautics and Space Act of 1958 (42 U.S.C. 2471 (a)) is amended by redesignating paragraphs (4) and (5) as paragraphs (5) and (6), respectively, and by inserting after paragraph (3) the following new paragraph:

> - **(4)** the Secretary of Transportation;”.


*Approved September 23, 1970.*

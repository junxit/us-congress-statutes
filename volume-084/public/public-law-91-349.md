# Public Law 91–349

> AN ACT To amend title II of the Marine Resources and Engineering Development Act of 1966.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 203(b)(1) of the National Sea Grant College and Program Act of 1966 is amended by inserting after “for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1970, not to exceed the sum of $15,000,000,” the following: “for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1971, not to exceed the sum of $20,000,000, for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1972, not to exceed the sum of $25,000,000, and for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1973, not to exceed the sum of $30,000,000,”.


*Approved July 23, 1970.*

# Public Law 91–399

> AN ACT To extend the provisions of title XIII of the Federal Aviation Act of 1938, its amended, relating to war risk Insurance.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United states of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1312 of title XIII of the Federal Aviation Act of 1958, as amended (49 U.S.C. 1542), is amended by striking out the words “September 7, 1970”, and by inserting the words “September 7, 1975” in place thereof.


*Approved September 8, 1970.*

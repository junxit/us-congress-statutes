# Public Law 91–570

> AN ACT Tn amend the Tariff Schedules of the United States so as to prevent the payment of multiple customs duties in the ease of horses temporarily exported for the purpose of racing.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That part 1 of schedule 8 of the Tariff Schedules of the United States (19 U.S.C. 1202) is amended by inserting after item 802.30 the following new item:

> | “ | 802.40 | In the case of horses, use for racing | Free | The column 2 rate applicable in the absence of this item. | ” |
> |---|---|---|---|---|---|


*Approved December 23, 1970.*

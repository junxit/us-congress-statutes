# Public Law 91–192

> JOINT RESOLUTION Designating the week commencing February 1, 1970, as International Clergy Week in the United States, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the week commencing February 1, 1970, is designated as “International Clergy Week in the United States”. The President is authorized and directed to issue a proclamation inviting the people of the United States to observe this week with appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Approved February 4, 1970.*

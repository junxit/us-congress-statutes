# Public Law 91–421

> AN ACT To provide fur the designation of the Veterans’ Administration facility at Bonham, Texas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Veterans’ Administration center at Bonham, Texas, shall hereafter be known and designated as the Sam Rayburn Memorial Veterans Center. Any reference to such center in any law, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States shall be deemed a reference to if as the Sam Rayburn Memorial Veterans Center.


*Approved September 25, 1970.*

# Public Law 91–451

> AN ACT To amend section 3 of the Act of November 2, 1988, to extend for three years the authority to make appropriations to carry out such Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3 of the Act entitled “An Act to provide for the control or elimination of jellyfish and other such pests in the coastal waters of the United States, and for other purposes”, approved November 2, 1966 (16 U.S.C. 1203), is amended by striking out “for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1970” and inserting in lieu thereof “for the period beginning July 1, 1969, and ending June 30, 1973”.


*Approved October 14, 1970.*

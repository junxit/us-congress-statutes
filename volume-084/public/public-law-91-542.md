# Public Law 91–542

> AN ACT To amend the Act of April 24, 1961, authorizing the use of judgment funds of the Nez Perce Tribe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first sentence of section 1 of the Act entitled “An Act to authorize the use of funds arising from a judgment in favor of the Nez Perce Tribe of Indians, and for other purposes,” approved April 24, 1961 (75 Stat. 45), is amended by inserting after “180–A,” the following: “and the funds deposited in the Treasury of the United States to pay the final judgment entered by the Indian Claims Commission on April 29, 1970 in docket 179,”.

## § 2.

The last sentence of section 2 of the aforesaid Act is amended by inserting after “175” a comma and “179”.


*Approved December 11, 1970.*

# Public Law 91–641

> JOINT RESOLUTION To extend the time for the proclamation of marketing quotas for barley tobacco for the three marketing years beginning October 1, 1971.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in. Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Secretary of Agriculture, may defer until March 1, 1971, any proclamation under section 312 of the Agricultural Adjustment Act of 1938, as amended, with respect to national marketing quotas for burley tobacco for the three marketing years beginning October 1, 1971.


*Approved December 31, 1970.*

# Public Law 91–507

> JOINT RESOLUTION To authorize and request the President to issue a proclamation designating January 1971 as “National Blood Donor Mouth”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in recognition of the vital contribution of the voluntary blood donor to medical care, the President is authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating the month of January 1971 as “National Blood Donor Month”, and calling upon the people of the United States and interested groups and organizations to observe such month with appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Approved October 26, 1970.*

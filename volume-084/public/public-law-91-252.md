# Public Law 91–252

> AN ACT To amend the Act of August 7, 1961, providing for the establishment of Cape Cod National Seashore.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 9 of the Act entitled “An Act to provide for the establishment of Cape Cod National Seashore,” approved August 7, 1961 (75 Stat. 284, 293), is amended by striking out the figure $16,000,000” and inserting in lieu thereof the figure “$33,500,01)0”.


*Approved May 14, 1970.*

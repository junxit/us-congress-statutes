# Public Law 91–370

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making further continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 1971, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That clause (c) of section 102 of the joint resolution of June 29, 1970 (Public Law 91–294), is hereby amended by striking out “July 31, 1970” and inserting in lieu thereof “October 15, 1970”.


*Approved August 1, 1970.*

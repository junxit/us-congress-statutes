# Public Law 91–445

> JOINT RESOLUTION Authorizing the President to proclaim National Volunteer Firemen’s Week from October 24, 1970, to October 31, 1970.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is hereby authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating National Volunteer Firemen’s Week from October 24, 1970, to October 31, 1970, and calling upon the people of the United States to observe such week with appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Approved October 14, 1970.*

# Public Law 91–371

> JOINT RESOLUTION To extend the effectiveness of the Defense Production Act of 1950 to August 15, 1970.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1.

Section 717(a) of the Defense Production Act of 1950 is amended by striking out “July 30, 1970” in the first sentence and inserting in lieu thereof “August 15, 1970”.


*Approved August 1, 1970.*

# Public Law 91–410

> JOINT RESOLUTION To amend section 19(e) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934.

*Resolved Ivy the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

Whereas additional time is required for the Securities and Exchange Commission to complete its institutional investors study, and filo a report with respect thereto, pursuant to section 19(e) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934: Now, therefore, be it

That section 19(e) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (15 U.S.C. 78s(e)) is amended by striking out in paragraph (1) “September 1, 1970” and inserting in lieu thereof “December 31, 1970”.


*Approved September 25, 1970.*

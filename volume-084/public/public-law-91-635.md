# Public Law 91–635

> AN ACT To extend until December 31, 1972, the suspension of duty on electrodes for use in producing aluminum.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the matter appearing in the effective period column for item 909.25 of the Tariff Schedules of the United States (19 U.S.C. 1202) is amended by striking out “12/31/70” and inserting in lieu thereof “12/31/72”.

## § 2.

The amendment made by the first section of this Act shall apply with respect to articles entered, or withdrawn from warehouse, for consumption on or after the date of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved December 31, 1970.*

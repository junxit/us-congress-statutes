# Public Law 91–643

> JOINT RESOLUTION Fixing the time of assembly of the Ninety-second Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress Assembled,*

That the Ninety-second Congress shall assemble at noon on January 21, 1971.


*Approved January 1, 1971.*

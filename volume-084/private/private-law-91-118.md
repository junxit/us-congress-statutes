# Private Law 118

> AN ACT For the relief of Rogelio Tabhan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203(a) (2) and 204 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Rogelio Tabhan, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mrs. Aracdi T. Pixler, a lawful permanent resident of the United States: Provided, That the natural mother, brothers, or sisters of the said Rogelio Tabhan shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved July 16, 1970.*

# Private Law 91–90

> AN ACT To confer United States citizenship posthumously upon Specialist Four Aaron Tawil.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Specialist Four Aaron Tawil, a native of Israel, who served honorably in the United States Army from April 11, 1967, until his death on November 17, 1968, shall be held and considered to have been a citizen of the United States at the time of his death.


*Approved May 14, 1970.*

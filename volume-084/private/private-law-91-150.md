# Private Law 150

> AN ACT To confer United States citizenship posthumously upon Jose Guadalupe Esparza-Montoya.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Jose Guadalupe Esparza-Montoya, a native of Mexico, who served honorably in the United States Army from August 21, 1968, until his death on May 20, 1969, shall be held and considered to have been a citizen of the United States at the time of his death.


*Approved August 24, 1970.*

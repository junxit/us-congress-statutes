# Private Law 196

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Maria Zahaniacz (nee Bojkiwska).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Maria Zahaniaez (nee Bojkiwska), the widow of a citizen of the United States, shall be held and considered to be within the purview of section 201(b) of that Act and the provisions of section 204 of such Act shall not be applicable in this case.


*Approved December 8, 1970.*

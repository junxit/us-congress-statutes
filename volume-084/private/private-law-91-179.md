# Private Law 179

> AN ACT For the relief of Kim Julia and Park Tong Op.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, section 204(c) of such Act, relating to the number of petitions which may be approved in behalf of adopted children, shall be inapplicable in the case of petitions filed in behalf of Kim Julia and Park Tong Op by Mr. and Mrs. Lester Gibson, citizens of the United States. The natural brothers or sisters of the said Kim Julia and Park Tong Op shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved October 16, 1970.*

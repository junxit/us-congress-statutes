# Private Law 213

> AN ACT For the relief of Enrico DeMonte.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the determination of the right of Enrico DeMonte, of Niagara Falls, New York, to widower’s insurance benefits under section 202(f) (1) of the Social Security Act, as amended (42 U.S.C. 402(f) (1), as amended), the said Enrico DeMonte shall be held and considered to have been receiving at least one-half of his support from his late wife, Rose DeMonte, at the time of her death on October 15, 1962.


*Approved December 21, 1970.*

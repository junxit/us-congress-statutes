# Private Law 119

> AN ACT To remove a cloud on the titles of certain property located in Malin, Oregon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States hereby releases and quitclaims to the owners of record of the lots hereinafter named those interests reserved pursuant to the Act of August 30, 1890 (26 Stat. 371, 391), relating to the right of the United States to construct ditches and canals upon and through Lots 1,2, 3, 8, 9, and 10 in Block 29, and Lots 1,2, 3,4, 5, and 6 in Block 30 in Malin, Oregon, all as shown on that certain supplemental plat of Malin (Klamath County) Oregon, filed July 5, 1939.


*Approved July 22, 1970.*

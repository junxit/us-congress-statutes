# Private Law 172

> AN ACT For the relief of Kimoko Ann Duke.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Kimoko Ann Duke shall be held and considered to be within the purview of section 323 (c) of such Act.


*Approved October 15, 1970.*

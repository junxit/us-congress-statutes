# Private Law 91–99

> AN ACT For the relief of Michel M. Goutmann.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Art, Michel M. Goutmann shall he held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of September 7, 1956.


*Approved May 28, 1970.*

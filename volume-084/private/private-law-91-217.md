# Private Law 217

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Ruth Brunner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the limitations of section 3010 of title 38 of the United States Code or of any other statute of limitations, the claim filed in 1959 by Ruth Brunner as the widow of Harry Brunner, also known as Henry Brunner (XXXXXXXXXX), shall be held and considered to be a valid and timely claim for such benefits and shall be considered and, if found meritorious, paid in accordance with otherwise applicable law.


*Approved December 23, 1970.*

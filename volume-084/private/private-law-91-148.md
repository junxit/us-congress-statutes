# Private Law 148

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Cumorah Kennington Romney.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 201(g) of the Nationality Act of 1940, Mrs. Cumorah Kennington Romney shall be held and considered to have been residing in the United. States for a period of five years after attaining the age of sixteen years.


*Approved August 18, 1970.*

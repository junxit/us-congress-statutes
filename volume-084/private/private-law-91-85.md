# Private Law 91–85

> AN ACT To authorize tile documentation of the vessel West Wind as a vessel of the United States with coastwise privileges.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding any provision of law to the contrary, the vessel West Wind (official number 514065), owned by George II. Staley, of Seattle, Washington, shall be documented as a vessel of the United States, upon compliance with the usual requirements, with the privilege of engaging in the coastwise trade so long as such vessel is owned by a citizen or the United States.


*Approved May 7, 1970.*

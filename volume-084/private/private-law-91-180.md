# Private Law 180

> AN ACT For the relief of Hyun Joo Lee and Myung Joo Lee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, section 204(c), relating to the number of petitions which may be approved in behalf of adopted children, shall be inapplicable in the case of petitions filed in behalf of Hyun Joo Lee and Myung Joo Lee by Mr. and Mrs. Bruce Bol don, citizens of the United States. The natural brothers and sisters of the said Hyun Joo Lee and Myung Joo Lee shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved October 16, 1970.*

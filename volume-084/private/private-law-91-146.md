# Private Law 146

> AN ACT For the relief of Lillian Biazzo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Lillian Biazzo shall be deemed to be a returning resident alien within the meaning of section 101 (a) (27) (B) of that Act.


*Approved August 18, 1970.*

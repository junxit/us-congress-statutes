# Private Law 192

> AN ACT For the relief of Bruce M. Smith.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the periods of time that Bruce M. Smith has resided in the United States since he was lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence on May 23, 1961, shall be held and considered to meet the residence and physical presence requirements of section 316 of such Act. In this case the petition for naturalization may be filed with any court having naturalization jurisdiction.


*Approved November 25, 1970.*

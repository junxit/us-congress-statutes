# Private Law 210

> AN ACT To require the Foreign Claims Settlement commission to reopen and Redetermine the claim of Julius Deutsch against the Government of Poland, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding any prior decision of the Foreign Claims Settlement Commission, the Commission is authorized and directed to reopen and redetermine, after hearing, the claim of Julius Deutsch against the Government of Poland based upon the claim of said Julius Deutsch of a 50 per centum interest in the Polish corporation, “Lenko” S.A. Any award made by the Commission after said redetermination shall be paid by the Secretary of the Treasury from the Polish Claims Fund to the same extent as if said award had been made prior to March 31, 1966.


*Approved December 19, 1970.*

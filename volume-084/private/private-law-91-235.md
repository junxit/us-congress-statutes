# Private Law 235

> AN ACT For the relief of Irwin Katz.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $800 to Irwin Katz of Brooklyn, New York, in full settlement of his claims against, the United States for losses and expenses and penalties due to the cancellation of a contract for the purchase of a home in New York due to his forced transfer of employment from the Naval Applied Science Laboratory in Brooklyn, New York, to the Naval Weapons Laboratory in Dahlgren, Virginia. No part of the amount appropriated in this Act shall lie paid or delivered to or received by any agent or attorney on account of services rendered in connection with this claim, and the same shall be unlawful, any contract to the contrary notwithstanding. Any person violating the provisions of this Act shall be deemed guilty of a misdemeanor and upon conviction thereof shall be fined in any sum not exceeding $1,000.


*Approved December 31, 1970.*

# Private Law 91–86

> AN ACT For the relief of Barbara Rogerson Marmor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Barbara Rogerson Murmor, the widow of the late Milton Marmor, a citizen of the United States, shall be held and considered to be an alien eligible for immediate relative status under the provisions of section 201 (b) of such Act, and the provisions of section 204 of such Act, shall not be applicable in this case.


*Approved May 7, 1970.*

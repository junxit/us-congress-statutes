# Private Law 91–96

> AN ACT For the relief of Patricia Hiro Williams.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Patricia Hiro Williams may be classified as a child within the meaning of section101(b)(1)(F) of the Act, and a petition filed in her behalf by Technical Sergeant Wayne V. Williams, a citizen of the United States, may be approved pursuant to section 204 of the Act: Provided, That the natural parents or brothers or sister’s of the beneficiary shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved May 14, 1970.*

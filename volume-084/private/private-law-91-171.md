# Private Law 171

> AN ACT For the relief of Ah Mee Locke.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Ah Mee Locke, the widow of the late Loy Hepp Locke, a citizen of the United States, shall be held and considered to be an alien eligible for immediate relative status under the provisions of section 201(b), and the provisions of section 204 of such Act shall not be applicable in this case.


*Approved October 15, 1970.*

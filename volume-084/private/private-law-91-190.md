# Private Law 190

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Anastasia Pertsovitch.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Anastasia Pertsoviteh, the widow of Mr. Dmytro Pertsoviteh, a citizen of the United States, shall be held and considered to be within the purview of section 201(b) of that Act and the previsions of section 204 of said Act shall not be applicable in this case.


*Approved November 25, 1970.*

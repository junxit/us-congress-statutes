# Private Law 141

> AN ACT For the relief of Walter L. Parker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Walter L. Parker of Trenton, New Jersey, is relieved of liability to the United States in the amount of $279.05 representing overpayments of salary paid to him during the period from January 20, 1966, through October 31, 1967, by the Department of the Navy as a result of administrative error and through no fault of his own. In the audit and settlement of the accounts of any certifying or disbursing officer of the United States, credit shall be given for amounts for which liability is relieved by this section.


*Approved August 17, 1970.*

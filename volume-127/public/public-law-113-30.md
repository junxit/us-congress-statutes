# Public Law 113–30

> An Act To amend title 49, United States Code, to modify requirements relating to the availability of pipeline safety regulatory documents, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. AVAILABILITY OF PIPELINE SAFETY REGULATORY DOCUMENTS.

Section 60102(p) of title 49, United States Code, is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “1 year” and inserting “3 years”;
- **(2)** by striking “guidance or”; and
- **(3)** by striking “, on an Internet Web site”.


*Approved August 9, 2013.*

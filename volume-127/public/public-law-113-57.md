# Public Law 113–57

> An Act To extend the Undetectable Firearms Act of 1988 for 10 years.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. EXTENSION OF UNDETECTABLE FIREARMS ACT OF 1988 FOR 10 YEARS.

Section 2(f)(2) of the Undetectable Firearms Act of 1988 (18 U.S.C. 922 note) is amended by striking “25” and inserting “35”.


*Approved December 9, 2013.*

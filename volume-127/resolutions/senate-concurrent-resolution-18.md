# Senate Concurrent Resolution 18

> FRANK R. LAUTENBERG—FUNERAL SERVICES—CATAFALQUE AUTHORIZATION

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Architect of the Capitol is authorized and directed to transfer the catafalque which is situated in the Exhibition Hall of the Capitol Visitor Center to the Senate Chamber so that such catafalque may be used in connection with services to be conducted there for the Honorable Frank R. Lautenberg, late a Senator from the State of New Jersey.


*Agreed to June 4, 2013.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 123

> ADJOURNMENT SINE DIE

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Sunday, August 24, 1958, and that when they adjourn on said day, they stand adjourned sine die.


*Agreed to August 24, 1958.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 102

> STATUE OF DR. FLORENCE RENA SABIN IN STATUARY HALL

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the statue of Doctor Florence Rena Sabin, presented by the State of Colorado, to be placed in the Statuary Hall collection, is accepted in the name of the United States, and that the thanks of the Congress be tendered such State for the contribution of the statue of one of its most eminent citizens, illustrious for her work in the field of medicine ; and be it further

That a copy of these resolutions, suitably engrossed and duly authenticated, be transmitted to the Governor of Colorado.


*Agreed to August 22, 1958.*

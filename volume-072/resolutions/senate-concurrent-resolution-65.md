# Senate Concurrent Resolution 65

> STATUE OF MARIA SANFORD IN CAPITOL ROTUNDA

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Minnesota Statehood Centennial Commission of the State of Minnesota is hereby authorized to place temporarily in the rotunda of the Capitol a statue of the late Maria Sanford, of Minnesota, and to hold ceremonies in the rotunda on said occasion; and the Architect of the Capitol is hereby authorized to make the necessary arrangements therefor.


*Agreed to March 28, 1958.*

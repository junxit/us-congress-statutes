# Senate Concurrent Resolution 111

> FINAL REPORT OF SUBCOMMITTEE ON DISARMAMENT

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the final report of the Subcommittee on Disarmament of the Committee on Foreign Relations, under authority of S. Res. 241, agreed to January 29, 1958, shall be printed as a Senate document, with illustrations, and that two thousand additional copies shall be printed for use of the Committee on Foreign Relations.


*Agreed to August 22, 1958.*

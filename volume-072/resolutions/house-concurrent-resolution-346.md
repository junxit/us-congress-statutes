# House Concurrent Resolution 346

> CENTENNIAL ANNIVERSARY OF THE LINCOLN-DOUGLAS DEBATE

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

Whereas the debate between Abraham Lincoln and Stephen A. Douglas at Freeport, Illinois, in the Illinois senatorial contest of 1858 was one of the great and important events in the history of the United States; and Whereas the centennial anniversary of the Lincoln-Douglas debate is to be appropriately commemorated at Freeport in August of 1958: Now, therefore, be it

That the Congress of the United States joins the city of Freeport in commemorating the centennial anniversary of the Lincoln-Douglas debate which was held in Freeport, Illinois, on August 27, 1858.

## § 2.

A copy of this resolution, suitably engrossed and duly authenticated, snail be transmitted to the Governor of Illinois, and the president of the Lincoln-Douglas Society, Freeport, Illinois.


*Passed July 15, 1958.*

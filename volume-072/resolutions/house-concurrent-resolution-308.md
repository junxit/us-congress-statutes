# House Concurrent Resolution 308

> “TRADE AGREEMENTS EXTENSION ACT OF 1958”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Committee on Ways and Means, House of Representatives, four thousand additional copies of the hearings entitled “Trade Agreements Extension Act of 1958”.


*Passed April 30, 1958.*

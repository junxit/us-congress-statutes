# Senate Concurrent Resolution 109

> UNITED NATIONS

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That it is the sense of the Congress that consideration should immediately be given by the General Assembly of the United Nations to the development, within its permanent structure, of such organization and procedures as will enable the United Nations promptly to employ suitable United Nations forces for such purposes as observation and patrol in situations that threaten international peace and security.


*Agreed to August 22, 1958.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 303

> ADJOURNMENT

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Thursday, April 3, 1958, they stand adjourned until 12 o'clock meridian, Monday, April 14, 1958.


*Passed March 27, 1958.*

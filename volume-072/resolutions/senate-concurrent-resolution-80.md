# Senate Concurrent Resolution 80

> STATUE OF CHARLES MARION RUSSELL IN STATUARY HALL

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the statue of Charles Marion Russell, presented by the State of Montana, to be placed in the Statuary Hall collection, is accepted in the name of the United States, and that the thanks of the Congress be tendered said State for the contribution of the statue of one of its most gifted and colorful citizens, noted for his artistic skill; and be it further

That a copy of these resolutions, suitably engrossed and duly authenticated, be transmitted to the Governor of Montana.


*Agreed to June 25, 1958.*

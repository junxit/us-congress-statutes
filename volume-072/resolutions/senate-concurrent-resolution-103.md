# Senate Concurrent Resolution 103

> STATUE OF DR. FLORENCE RENA SABIN IN CAPITOL ROTUNDA

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the State of Colorado is hereby authorized to place temporarily in the rotunda of the Capitol a statue of the late Doctor Florence Rena Sabin, of Colorado, and to hold ceremonies in the rotunda on such occasion ; and the Architect of the Capitol is hereby authorized to make the necessary arrangements therefor.


*Agreed to August 22, 1958.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 325

> “PHYSICAL RESEARCH PROGRAM AS IT RELATES TO THE FIELD OF ATOMIC ENERGY”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Joint Committee on Atomic Energy lie authorized to have printed for its use ten thousand copies of the public hearings on “Physical research program as it relates to the field of atomic energy”, held by the Subcommittee on Research and Development during the Eighty-fifth Congress, second session ; and be it further

That the Joint Committee be authorized to have printed ten thousand copies of the report on the above hearings; and be it further

That the Joint Committee be authorized to have printed two thousand copies of the index of the above hearings.


*Passed July 24, 1958.*

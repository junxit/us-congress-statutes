# Senate Concurrent Resolution 107

> “INQUIRY INTO SATELLITE AND MISSILE PROGRAMS”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there shall be printed for the use of the committee, one thousand additional copies of parts 1 and 2 of the hearings before the Preparedness Investigating Subcommittee of the Senate Armed Services Committee, entitled “Inquiry Into Satellite and Missile Programs.”


*Agreed to August 6, 1958.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 64

> STATUE OF MARIA SANFORD IN STATUARY HALL

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the statue of Maria Sanford, presented by the State of Minnesota, to be placed in the Statuary Hall collection, is accepted in the name of the United States, and that the thanks of the Congress be tendered said State for the contribution of the statue of one of its most eminent citizens, illustrious for her leadership in education; and be it further

That a copy of these resolutions, suitably engrossed and duly authenticated, be transmitted to the Governor of Minnesota.


*Agreed to March 28, 1958.*

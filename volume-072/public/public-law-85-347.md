# Public Law 85–347

> AN ACT To authorize, in case of the death of a member of the uniformed services, certain transportation expenses for his dependents.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the tenth sentence of the last proviso of section 303 (c) of the Career Compensation Act, as amended (37 U. S. C. 253 (e)), is further amended—

- **(1)** by inserting immediately after the words “who transports” the words “, or in the case of his death his dependents who trans-port,”; and
- **(2)** by inserting immediately after the words “whichever he” the words “or they”.


*Approved March 17, 1958.*

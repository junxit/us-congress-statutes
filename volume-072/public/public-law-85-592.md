# Public Law 85–592

> AN ACT To designate the dam being constructed in connection with the Eagle Gorge Reservoir project on the Green River, Washington, as the “Howard A. Hanson Dam”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the dam to be constructed in connection with the project for the Eagle Gorge Reservoir, on the Green River, Washington, authorized by the Flood Control Act of 1950 (64 Stat. 180; Public Law 516, Eighty-first Congress) shall be known and designated hereafter as the “Howard A. Hanson Dam”. Any law, regulation, map, document, record, or other paper of the United States in which such dam is referred to shall be field to refer to such dam as the “Howard A. Hanson Dam”.


*Approved August 6, 1958.*

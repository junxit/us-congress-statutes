# Public Law 85–763

> AN ACT To amend Reorganization Plan Numbered 1 of 1958 in order to change the name of the office established under such plan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Reorganization Plan Numbered 1 of 1958 is amended by striking out “Office of Defense and Civilian Mobilization” wherever appearing therein and inserting in lieu thereof “Office of Civil and Defense Mobilization”.


*Approved August 26, 1958.*

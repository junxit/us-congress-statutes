# Public Law 85–518

> AN ACT To extend the time for the collection of tolls to amortize the cost, including reasonable interest and financing cost, of the construction of a bridge across the Missouri River at Brownville, Nebraska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first sentence of section 18 (d) of the Act of August 30, 1935 (relating to the construction of certain bridges), as amended by the Act of October 25, 1949, is hereby amended by striking out “thirty years” and inserting in lieu thereof “forty years”.


*Approved July 11, 1958.*

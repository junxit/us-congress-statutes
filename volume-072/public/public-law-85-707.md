# Public Law 85–707

> AN ACT To amend the Act of May 29, 1930. with respect to the stream valley parks in Maryland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph (a) of the first section of the Act of May 29, 1930 (46 Stat. 482), as amended, is amended by striking out the period at the end of the next to the last sentence thereof, and inserting in lieu thereof a colon and the following: “Provided further, That in the discretion of the National Capital Planning Commission, upon agreement duly entered into between that Commission and the Maryland-National Capital Park and Planning Commission, an agency of the State of Maryland, created by chapter 448 of the laws of Maryland of 1927, as amended, such portion of the said $7,500,000 authorized to be appropriated under this paragraph as the said Federal and Maryland agencies may determine may be appropriated for the purposes set forth under paragraph (b) of this section and subject to the conditions imposed by that paragraph.”.


*Approved August 21, 1958.*

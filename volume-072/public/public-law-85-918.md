# Public Law 85–918

> JOINT RESOLUTION To amend the Act of September 7, 1957 (71 Stat. 626), providing for the establishment of a Civil War Centennial Commission.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 9 of the joint resolution of September 7, 1957 (71 Stat. 626), entitled “Joint resolution to establish a commission to commemorate the one hundredth anniversary of the Civil War, and for other purposes”, is hereby amended to read as follows:

> ## § 9.
>
> There is hereby authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this joint resolution, not to exceed $100,000 in any one fiscal year.”


*Approved September 2, 1958.*

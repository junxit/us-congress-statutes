# Public Law 85–572

> JOINT RESOLUTION Amending a Joint resolution making temporary appropriations for the fiscal year 1959, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That clause (c) of section 102 of the joint resolution of June 30, 1958 (Public Law 85–472), is hereby amended by striking out “July 31, 1958” and inserting in lieu thereof “August 31, 1958”.

## § 2.

The amount appropriated by subsection (b) of section 101 of such joint resolution for Mutual Security programs is hereby increased from “$200,000,000” to “$300,000,000”


*Approved July 31, 1958.*

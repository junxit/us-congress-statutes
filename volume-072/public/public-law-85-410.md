# Public Law 85–410

> AN ACT To amend the Tariff Act of 1930 to exempt from duty pistols and revolvers not using fixed ammunition.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph 1723 of the Tariff Act of 1930 (19 U. S. C., sec. 201, par. 1723) is amended to read as follows:

> -  “Par. 1723. Muskets, shotguns, rifles, pistols, and revolvers, all the foregoing not designed to fire or capable of firing a fixed metallic cartridge or fixed shotgun shell, and parts of muskets, shotguns, rifles, pistols, and revolvers provided for in this paragraph.”

## § 2.

The amendment made by the first section of this Act shall apply only with respect to articles entered, or withdrawn from warehouse, for consumption after the date of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved May 16, 1958.*

# Public Law 85–634

> AN ACT To provide for the Board of Trustees of the Postal Savings System to consist of the Postmaster General and the Secretary of the Treasury.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second sentence of the first section of the Act entitled “An Act to establish postal savings depositories for depositing savings at interest with the security of the Government for repayment thereof, and for other purposes”, approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 814), as amended (39 U. S. C. 751), is hereby further amended by striking out “the Secretary of the Treasury, and the Attorney-General”, and inserting in lieu thereof “and the Secretary of the Treasury”.


*Approved August 14, 1958.*

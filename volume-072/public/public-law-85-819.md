# Public Law 85–819

> JOINT RESOLUTION Establishing that the first session of the Eighty-sixth Congress convene at noon on Wednesday, January 7, 1959.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Eighty-sixth Congress shall assemble at noon on Wednesday, January 7, 1959.


*Approved August 28, 1958.*

# Public Law 85–360

> JOINT RESOLUTION Authorizing the President to invite the States of the Union and foreign countries to participate in the Second Annual United States World Trade Fair to be held in New York City, New York, from May 7 to May 17, 1958.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States is authorized, by proclamation or in such other manner as he may deem proper, to invite the States of the Union and foreign countries to participate in the Second Annual United States World Trade Fair to be held at the Coliseum, New York City, New York, from May 7 to May 17, 1958, inclusive, for the purpose of exhibiting merchandise and articles of trade and production; and the promotion of travel, tourism, and transportation; and for the purpose of bringing together buyers and sellers tor the promotion of foreign and domestic trade and commerce.


*Approved March 28, 1958.*

# Public Law 85–723

> AN ACT To amend title 10, United States Code, to provide for a permanent professor of physical education at the United States Military Academy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4331 (a)(5), title 10, United States Code, is amended to read as follows:

> - **(5)** one permanent professor of each of the following subjects—
>   - **(A)** Law;
>   - **(B)** Ordnance; and
>   - **(C)** Physical education;”.


*Approved August 21, 1958.*

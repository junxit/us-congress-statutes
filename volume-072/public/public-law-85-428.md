# Public Law 85–428

> AN ACT To amend the Act of June 5, 1944, relating to the construction, operation, and maintenance of Hungry Horse Dam, Montana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in order to clarify the status of the Hungry Horse project, Montana, section 1 of the Act of June 5, 1944 (58 Stat. 270, 43 U. S. C. 593a), is hereby amended by adding to it a new sentence reading as follows:

> “The Hungry Horse project shall be subject to the Federal reclamation laws (Act of June 17, 1902 (32 Stat. 388), and Acts amendatory thereof or supplementary thereto).”


*Approved May 29, 1958.*

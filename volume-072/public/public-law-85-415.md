# Public Law 85–415

> AN ACT To continue the temporary suspension of duty on certain alumina and bauxite.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That no duty shall be imposed upon—

- **(1)** Alumina, when imported for use in producing aluminum, under such regulations as the Secretary of the Treasury shall prescribe.
- **(2)** Bauxite, crude, not refined or otherwise advanced in condition in any manner.
- **(3)** Calcined bauxite.

## § 2.

This Act shall apply only with respect to articles entered, or withdrawn from warehouse, for consumption after July 15, 1958, and before July 16, 1960.


*Approved May 16, 1958.*

# Public Law 85–714

> AN ACT To amend section 69 of the Hawaiian Organic Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the fourth sentence of section 69 of the Hawaiian Organic Act (31 Stat. 141, 154; 48 U. S. C. 534) is amended to read as follows: “He shall perform such other duties as are prescribed in this Act or as may be required of him by the Legislature of Hawaii.”


*Approved August 21, 1958.*

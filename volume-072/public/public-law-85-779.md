# Public Law 85–779

> AN ACT To amend title V of the Agricultural Act of 1949, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 509 of the Agricultural Act of 1949, as amended, is amended by striking out “June 30, 1959” and inserting “June 30, 1961”.


*Approved August 27, 1958.*

# Public Law 85–808

> AN ACT To amend the Tariff Act of 1930 as it relates to unmanufactured mica and mica films and splittings.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph 208 (a) of the Tariff Act of 1930 is amended by striking out “Mica, unmanufactured: Valued at not above 15 cents per pound, 4 cents per pound; valued at above 15 cents per pound, 4 cents per pound and 25 per centum ad valorem.” and inserting in lieu thereof the following: “Mica, unmanufactured, 4 cents per pound.”.

## § 2.

Paragraph 208 (c) of the Tariff Act of 1930 is hereby repealed.

## § 3.

Subparagraphs (d), (e), (f), (g), and (h) of paragraph 208 of the Tariff Act of 1930 are redesignated as subparagraphs (c), (d), (e), (f), and (g), respectively.

## § 4.

Title II (free list) of the Tariff Act of 1930 is amended by adding at the end thereof a new paragraph as follows:

> - **(1821)** Mica films and splittings, not cut or stamped to dimensions.”.


*Approved August 28, 1958.*

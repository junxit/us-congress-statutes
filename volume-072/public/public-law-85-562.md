# Public Law 85–562

> AN ACT Designating the reservoir located above Heart-Butte Dam in Grant County, North Dakota, as Lake Tschida, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the reservoir located above the Heart-Butte Dam in Grant County, North Dakota, shall hereafter be known as Lake Tschida, and any law, regulation, document, or record of the United States in which such reservoir is designated or referred to shall be held to refer to such reservoir under and by the name of Lake Tschida.


*Approved July 28, 1958.*

# Public Law 85–695

> AN ACT To authorize the Gray Reef Dam and Reservoir as a part of the Glendo unit of the Missouri River Basin project.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Glendo unit of the Missouri River Basin project, as authorized by the joint resolution of July 16, 1954 (68 Stat. 486), is modified to provide for the construction and operation of the small reregulating Gray Reef Dam and Reservoir on the North Platte River downstream from Alcova Dam at an estimated cost of $700,000.

## § 2.

There are authorized to be appropriated such amounts as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act: Provided, That no construction shall proceed until a feasibility report has been approved by the Secretary of the Interior and submitted to the President and the Congress.


*Approved August 20, 1958.*

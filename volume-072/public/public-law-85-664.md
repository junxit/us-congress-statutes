# Public Law 85–664

> AN ACT To amend title VI of the Public Health Service Act to extend for an additional three-year period the Hospital Survey and Construction Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** the first sentence of section 621 of the Public Health Service Act is amended by striking out “nine” and inserting in lieu thereof “fourteen”.
- **(b)** Section 651 of such Act is amended by striking out “four” and inserting in lieu thereof “nine”.


*Approved August 14, 1958.*

# Public Law 85–891

> AN ACT To authorize a program for the conservation, restoration, and management of the rare Hawaiian Nene goose.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That whereas there are less than fifty Nene geese in the wild state in the Territory of Hawaii, and this unique, native species of waterfowl is threatened with imminent extinction, the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to promote a program of research, propagation, and management necessary to effect the restoration of this threatened species in its natural habitat.

## § 2.

The sum of $15,000 per annum is hereby authorized to be appropriated each year for a period of five years to carry out the purposes of this Act.


*Approved September 2, 1958.*

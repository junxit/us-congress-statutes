# Public Law 85–524

> AN ACT To authorize the appointment of one additional Assistant Secretary of State.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1 of the Act of May 26, 1949, as amended (5 U. S. C. 151a), is amended by striking out “ten” and inserting in lieu thereof “eleven”.

## § 2.

Section 106 (a)(17) of the Federal Executive Pay Act of 1956 (70 Stat. 738) is amended by striking out “(10)” and inserting in lieu thereof “(11)”.


*Approved July 18, 1958.*

# Public Law 85–571

> AN ACT To authorize the construction of modern naval vessels.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is authorized to undertake the construction of, or to acquire and convert, not to exceed twenty thousand tons of amphibious warfare vessels and landing craft, as follows:

- One amphibious transport, dock,
- One amphibious assault ship.

## § 2.

There is hereby authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary for the construction, or for the acquisition and conversion of the foregoing vessels.


*Approved July 31, 1958.*

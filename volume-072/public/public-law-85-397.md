# Public Law 85–397

> AN ACT To make permanent the existing privilege of free importation of guar seed.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first section of the Act entitled “An Act to amend the Tariff Act of 1930 to place guar seed on the free list”, approved August 6, 1956 (Public Law 1001, Eighty-fourth Congress; 70 Stat. 1066), is amended by striking out “and prior to the expiration of two years after such date”.


*Approved May 9, 1958.*

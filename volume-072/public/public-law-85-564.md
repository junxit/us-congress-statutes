# Public Law 85–564

> AN ACT To amend the Universal Military Training and Service Act to authorize additional deferments in certain cases.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4 (a) of the Universal Military Training and Service Act (50 U. S. C. App. 454), as amended, is amended by substituting a colon for the period at the end of the third paragraph and adding the following: “And provided further, That except in time of war or national emergency declared by the Congress the standards and requirements fixed by the preceding two provisos may be modified by the President under such rules and regulations as he may prescribe.”


*Approved July 28, 1958.*

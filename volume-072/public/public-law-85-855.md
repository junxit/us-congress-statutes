# Public Law 85–855

> AN ACT To amend Public Law 85–422.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** section 4 (a) of Public Law 85–422 is amended by striking out “and persons with two or less years of service for basic pay purposes who were retired for physical disability or placed on the temporary disability retired list”.
- **(b)** This amendment shall take effect on June 1, 1958.


*Approved August 28, 1958.*

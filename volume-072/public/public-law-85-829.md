# Public Law 85–829

> AN ACT To amend title IV of the Agricultural Act of 1956 to provide that the provisions of such title shall apply in Hawaii.

*Be it enacted by the. Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That title IV of the Agricultural Act of 1956 is amended by adding at the end thereof the following:

> ## § 403. “extension of title to hawaii
>
> As used in this title, the term ‘State’ includes the Territory of Hawaii.”


*Approved August 28, 1958.*

# Public Law 85–865

> AN ACT To amend the Watershed Protection and Flood Prevention Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4 (2) (A) of the Watershed Protection and Flood Prevention Act (Public Law 1018, Eighty-fourth Congress) be amended by inserting immediately after “and disposal of water”, the following: “or for fish and wildlife development.”

## § 2.

The Secretary of Agriculture shall not furnish or agree to furnish financial assistance to local organizations for the institution of works of improvement for fish and wildlife development pursuant to the authority of this Act prior to July 1, 1958.


*Approved September 2, 1958.*

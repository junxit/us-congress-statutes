# Public Law 85–888

> AN ACT To amend the Fish and Wildlife Act of 1956 in order to increase the authorization for the fisheries loan fund established under such Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (c) of section 4 of the Fish and Wildlife Act of 1956 (70 Stat. 1121) is amended by striking out “$10,000,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$20,000,000”.


*Approved September 2, 1958.*

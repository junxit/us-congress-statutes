# Public Law 85–753

> AN ACT To authorize the Secretary of the Interior to convey certain lands in Alaska to the city of Ketchikan, Alaska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States hereby disclaims any right, title, and interest in lot numbered 2, block 13, United States survey 1378, located within the corporate limits of the city of Ketchikan.


*Approved August 25, 1958.*

# Public Law 85–434

> AN ACT To amend the Act of August 25, 1916, to Increase the period for which concessionaire leases may be granted under that Act from twenty years to thirty years.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3 of the Act entitled “An Act to establish a National Park Service, and for other purposes”, approved August 25, 1916, as amended (16 U. S. C. 3), is amended by striking out “twenty years” and inserting in lieu thereof “thirty years”.


*Approved May 29, 1958.*

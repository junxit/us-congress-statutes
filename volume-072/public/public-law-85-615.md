# Public Law 85–615

> AN ACT To amend the law with respect to civil and criminal Jurisdiction over Indian country in Alaska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (a) of section 1162, title 18, United States Code, is amended (i) by inserting after “State” each time the word appears, except in the second column of the list in subsection (a) of section 1162, “or Territory”, (ii) by inserting after “States” each time the word appears “or Territories”, and (iii) by adding at the beginning of the list in subsection (a) of section 1162 the following:

> - “Alaska All Indian country within the Territory”.

## § 2.

Subsection (a) of section 1360, title 28, United States Code, is amended (i) by inserting after “State” each time the word appears, except in the second column of the list in subsection (a) of section 1360, “or Territory”, (ii) by inserting after “States” each time the word appears “or Territories”, and (iii) by adding at the beginning of the list in subsection (a) of section 1360 the following:

> - “Alaska All Indian country within the Territory”.


*Approved August 8, 1958.*

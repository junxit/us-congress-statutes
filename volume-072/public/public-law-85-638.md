# Public Law 85–638

> AN ACT To amend the Veterans’ Readjustment Assistance Act of 1952, to extend the time for filing claims for mustering-out payments.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 503 of the Veterans’ Readjustment Assistance Act of 1952 is amended by striking out “July 16, 1956” and inserting in lieu thereof “July 16, 1959”.


*Approved August 14, 1958.*

# Public Law 85–718

> AN ACT To amend section 73 (1) of the Hawaiian Organic Act, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 73 (1) of the Hawaiian Organic Act (31 Stat. 141, 154) as amended (48 U. S. C. 673), is hereby further amended by increasing the amount of “$5,000” appearing therein to “$15,000”.


*Approved August 21, 1958.*

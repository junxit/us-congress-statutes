# Public Law 85–708

> AN ACT To provide for the periodic transfer to the Hawaiian home-development fund of certain excess funds in the Hawaiian home administration account.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph (3) of subsection (f) of section 213 of the Hawaiian Homes Commission Act, 1920, as amended (48 U. S. C. 707), is amended by striking out “general fund of the treasury of the Territory.” and inserting in lieu thereof “Hawaiian home-development fund,”.


*Approved August 21, 1958.*

# Public Law 85–820

> AN ACT To rescind the authorization for the Waldo Lake Tunnel and regulating works, Willamette River, Oregon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the authorization for the Waldo Lake Tunnel and regulating works, Middle Fork-North Fork, Willamette River. Oregon, contained in the Flood Control Act of 1950 (64 Stat. 163) under the heading “Columbia River Basin”, is hereby rescinded.


*Approved August 28, 1958.*

# Public Law 85–442

> JOINT RESOLUTION To amend section 217 of the National Housing Act.

*Resolved, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 217 of the National Housing Act is amended by striking out “$3,000,000,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$7,000,000,000”


*Approved June 4, 1958.*

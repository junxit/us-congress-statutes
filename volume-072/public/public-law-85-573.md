# Public Law 85–573

> AN ACT To amend section 12 of the Act of May 29, 1884, relating to research on foot-and-mouth disease and other animal diseases.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 12 of the Act of May 29, 1884, as amended (62 Stat. 198, as amended; 21 U. S. C. 113a), is hereby further amended by inserting after the word “tunnel” in the proviso in the first sentence of the section the following clause: “, and except that the Secretary of Agriculture may transport said virus in the original package across the mainland under adequate safeguards”.


*Approved July 31, 1958.*

# Public Law 85–674

> AN ACT To amend sections 802 and 803 of the Veterans’ Benefits Act of 1957 to increase the burial allowance for deceased veterans from $150 to $250.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 802 and 803 of the Veterans’ Benefits Act of 1957 are each amended by striking out “$150” and inserting “$250”.

## § 2.

The amendments made by this Act shall apply only with respect to deaths occurring after the date of enactment of this Act.


*Approved August 18, 1958.*

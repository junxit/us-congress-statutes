# Public Law 85–777

> AN ACT To extend title VII of the Public Health Service Act (relating to health research facilities) for three years, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** section 704 of title VII of the Public Health Service Act is amended by striking out “two” and inserting in lieu thereof “five”.
- **(b)** Subsection (a) of section 705 of title VII of the Public Health Service Act is amended by striking out “1958” and inserting in lieu thereof “1961”.


*Approved August 27, 1958.*

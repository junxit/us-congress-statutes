# Private Law 85–750

> AN ACT For the relief of Boris F. Navratil.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Boris F. Navratil shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of September 16, 1949.


*Approved September 2, 1958.*

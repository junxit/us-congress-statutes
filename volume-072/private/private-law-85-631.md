# Private Law 85–631

> AN ACT For the relief of Mary Louise Shields Wilkinson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 201 (g) of the Nationality Act of 1940, as amended, Mary Louise Shields Wilkinson shall be held and considered to have been physically present in the United States during all the time she was residing abroad with her parents while her father, Leighton Shields, was stationed abroad as an employee of the United States.


*Approved August 23, 1958.*

# Private Law 85–730

> AN ACT For the relief of Erika Margaretha Zintl Pearce.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Erika Margaretha Zintl Pearce shall be held and considered to be within the purview of section 354 (5) of that Act.


*Approved August 28, 1958.*

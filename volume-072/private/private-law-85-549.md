# Private Law 85–549

> AN ACT For the relief of Mr. and Mrs. George Holden.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 5 (a) (3) of the Act of July 28, 1945 (Public Law 161, Seventy-ninth Congress), Mr. and Mrs. George Holden, Poplarville, Mississippi, shall be held and considered to nave been receiving compensation under section 10 (E) of the Federal Employees’ Compensation Act (on account of the death of their daughter Myrtle Holden) on July 28, 1945.

## § 2.

No compensation shall be payable by reason of the enactment of this Act for any period prior to the date of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved August 8, 1958.*

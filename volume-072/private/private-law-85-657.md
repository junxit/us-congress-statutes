# Private Law 85–657

> AN ACT For the relief of Erika Gorenstein Nathanson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Erika Gorenstein Nathanson shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fee: Provided, That the natural parents of Erika Gorenstein Nathanson shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved August 23, 1958.*

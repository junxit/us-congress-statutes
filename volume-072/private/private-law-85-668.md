# Private Law 85–668

> AN ACT For the relief of Lori Biagi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203 (a) (3) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Lori Biagi shall be held and considered to be the minor natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Maurizio Biagi, lawfully resident aliens in the United States.


*Approved August 26, 1958.*

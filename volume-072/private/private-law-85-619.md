# Private Law 85–619

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Margarete Briest, nee Eggers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of section 212 (a) (3) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs, Margarete Briest (nee Eggers) may be issued a visa and admitted to the United States for permanent residence if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the said Act.


*Approved August 21, 1958.*

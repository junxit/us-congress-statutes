# Private Law 85–690

> AN ACT For the relief of Colonel Russell King Alspach.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Colonel Russell King Alspach, a professor at the United States Military Academy, who was appointed to this office prior to August 7, 1947, may, if he retires under section 3886 of title 10, United States Code, elect to receive monthly retired pay computed at the rate of 75 per centum of the basic pay of the grade which he holds at the time of his retirement, or to receive monthly retired pay computed under other provisions of law applicable to him.


*Approved August 26, 1958.*

# Private Law 85–741

> AN ACT For the relief of Homer G. Preston.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Homer G. Preston, of Aurora, Colorado, is hereby relieved of all liability to pay the United States the sum of $927.45. Such sum represents charges made by the Fitzsimons Army Hospital, Denver, Colorado, for outpatient treatment in 1954 and hospitalization in 1955.


*Approved August 28, 1958.*

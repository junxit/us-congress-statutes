# Private Law 85–388

> AN ACT To exempt from taxation certain property of the National Council of Negro Women, Incorporated, in the District of Columbia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the real estate described as lot numbered 55, in square numbered 242, situated in the city of Washington, District of Columbia, owned by the National Council of Negro Women, Incorporated, is hereby exempt from all taxation so long as the same is owned and occupied by the National Council of Negro Women, Incorporated, and is not used for commercial purposes, subject to the provisions of sections 2, 3, and 5 of the Act entitled “An Act to define the real property exempt from taxation in the District of Columbia”, approved December 24, 1942 (56 Stat. 1089).


*Approved April 23, 1958.*

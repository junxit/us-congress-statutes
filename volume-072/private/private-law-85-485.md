# Private Law 85–485

> AN ACT For the relief of Giuseppina Fazio.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203 (a) (3) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Giuseppina Fazio shall be held and considered to be the minor child of Mr. and Mrs. Antonio Fazio, lawful resident aliens of the United States.


*Approved July 18, 1958.*

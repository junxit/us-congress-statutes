# Private Law 85–447

> AN ACT For the relief of Lucy Hedwig Schultz.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203 (a) (3) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Lucy Hedwig Schultz shall be held and considered to be the minor alien child of Artur Schultz, a lawful permanent resident of the United States.


*Approved June 28, 1958.*

# Private Law 85–465

> AN ACT For the relief of Armas Edvin Jansson-Viik.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Armas Edvin Jansson-Viik shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act: Provided, That nothing in this Act shall be construed to waive the provisions of section 315 of the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved July 11, 1958.*

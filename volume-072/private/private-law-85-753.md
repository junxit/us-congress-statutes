# Private Law 85–753

> AN ACT For the relief of Mr. and Mrs. Joseph D. Metzgar.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to relieve Mr. and Mrs. Joseph D. Metzgar of 984 Boulevard Street, Akron, Ohio, of the liability to pay to the United States Army the sum of $885.37. Such sum represents the overpayment of class Q allotments from July 1955 to April 1956 and overpayment of accrued leave pay and allowances at discharge on April 21, 1956.


*Approved September 2, 1958.*

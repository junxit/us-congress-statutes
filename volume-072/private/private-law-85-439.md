# Private Law 85–439

> AN ACT For the relief of Genevieve M. Scott Bell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 301 (a) (7) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (66 Stat. 236), Genevieve M. Scott Bell, daughter of Albert W. Scott, a retired member of the Foreign Service of the United States, and Mrs. Scott, shall be held and considered to have been residing in the United States during all the time she was residing abroad with her parents when her father was serving on active duty in the Foreign Service of the United States.


*Approved June 25, 1958.*

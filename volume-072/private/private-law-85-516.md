# Private Law 85–516

> AN ACT For the relief of Albert Hyrapiet.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Albert Hyrapiet shall be held and considered to be the minor alien child of Mr. and Mrs. George Hyrapiet, citizens of the United States.


*Approved August 6, 1958.*

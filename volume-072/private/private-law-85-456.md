# Private Law 85–456

> AN ACT For the relief of Moo Wah Jung.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Moo Wah Jung shall be held and considered to be the minor alien child of Peter Shu Jung, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved June 28, 1958.*

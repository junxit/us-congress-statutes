# Private Law 85–641

> AN ACT For the relief of Adamantia Papavasiliou.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Adamantia Papavasiliou, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. George (Pappas) Papavasiliou, citizens of the United States: Provided, That no natural parent of the beneficiary, by virtue of such relationship, shall be accorded any right, status, or privilege under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved August 23, 1958.*

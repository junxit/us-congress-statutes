# Private Law 85–645

> AN ACT For the relief of Nessime Kadoch.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provision of section 212 (a) (1) and (4) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Nessime Kadoch may be issued a visa and be admitted to the United States for permanent residence if he is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the said Act: And provided further, That the exemption granted herein shall apply only to a ground for exclusion of which the Department of Justice or the Department of State has knowledge prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved August 23, 1958.*

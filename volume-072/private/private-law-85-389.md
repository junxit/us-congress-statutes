# Private Law 85–389

> AN ACT For the relief of Agapito Jorolan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, on the date of enactment of this Act, Agapito JoroLan, of Orlando, Florida, is relieved of any liability to repay to the United States the remaining unpaid balance of the sum which was erroneously paid him during the period from April 1952 to August 1955 by the Military Sea Transportation Service, Department of the Navy, as the result of the failure of such Service to deduct from his salary (pursuant to sec. 2 (b) of the Civil Service Retirement Act of May 29, 1930, as amended, 5 U. S. C. 715 (b)) an amount equal to the amount he was entitled to receive in civil service retirement annuity payments during such period, the said Agapito Jorolan having been reemployed by such Service subsequent to his retirement on March 31, 1952, under the Civil Service Retirement Act of May 29, 1930, as amended.


*Approved April 24, 1958.*

# Private Law 85–704

> AN ACT To authorize certain retired personnel of the United States Government to accept and wear decorations, presents, and other things tendered them by certain foreign countries.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the following-named retired personnel of the Government of the United States are hereby authorized to accept and wear such decorations, orders, medals, emblems, presents, and other things as have been tendered as of the date of approval of this Act by the foreign government or foreign governments immediately following their names, and that the consent of Congress is hereby expressly granted for this purpose as required under clause 8 of section 9, article I, of the Constitution of the United States:

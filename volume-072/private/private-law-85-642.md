# Private Law 85–642

> AN ACT For the relief of Leopoldo Rodriguez-Meza and Adela Rodriguez Gonzales.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Leopoldo Rodriguez-Meza and Adela Rodriguez Gonzales shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fees.


*Approved August 23, 1958.*

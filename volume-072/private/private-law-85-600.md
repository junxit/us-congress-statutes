# Private Law 85–600

> AN ACT For the relief of Gerald Early.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Gerald Early of Brockton, Massachusetts, is hereby relieved of all liability for payment to the United States of the sum of $1,257, representing overpayments of per diem which he received as an employee of the Department of the Navy while he was assigned to duty at the Golcuk Navy Yard, Ismet, Turkey, such overpayments having been made as a result of administrative error.

## § 2.

The Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to the said Gerald Early, the sum of any amounts received or withheld from him on account of the overpayments referred to in the first section of this Act.


*Approved August 18, 1958.*

# Private Law 85–491

> AN ACT For the relief of Antonios Thomas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of section 101 (a) (27) (A) and section 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Antonios Thomas, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Mitchel Thomas, citizens of the United States: Provided, That no natural parent of the beneficiary, by virtue of such relationship, shall be accorded any right, status, or privilege under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved July 18, 1958.*

# Private Law 85–633

> AN ACT For the relief of Bernabe Miranda and Manuel Miranda.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Bernabe Miranda and Manuel Miranda shall be held and considered to be the minor alien children of Sergeant First Class Elisha Miranda, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 23, 1958.*

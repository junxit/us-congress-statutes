# Private Law 85–677

> AN ACT For the relief of Wong Wing Boa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Wong Wing Boa, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Frank S. Wong, citizens of the United States: Provided, That no natural parent of Wong Wing Boa, by virtue of such relationship, shall be accorded any right, status, or privilege under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved August 26, 1958.*

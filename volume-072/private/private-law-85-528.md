# Private Law 85–528

> AN ACT For the relief of Eva S. Winder.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding section 3774 (b) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1939, the Secretary of the Treasury shall consider, and allow if otherwise allowable, the claims filed on March 9, 1948, by Eva S. Winder, of Deming, New Mexico, for refunds of overpayments of her income taxes for the years 1945 and 1946. No interest shall be allowed on the refunds claimed herein for any period of time.


*Approved August 8, 1958.*

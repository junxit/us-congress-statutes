# Private Law 85–464

> AN ACT For the relief of Claudio Guillen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of sections 101 (a) (19) and 315 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, as amended, and section 3 (a) of the Selective Training and Service Act of 1940, as amended, Claudio Guillen may be naturalized in accordance with the provisions of title III of the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved July 11, 1958.*

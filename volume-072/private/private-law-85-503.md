# Private Law 85–503

> AN ACT For the relief of Lucia (Castaneda) Sayaan and Gloria (Castaneda) Sayaan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor children, Lucia (Castaneda) Sayaan, and Gloria (Castaneda) Sayaan, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien children of Mr. and Mrs. Mathias Sayaan, citizens of the United States.


*Approved July 25, 1958.*

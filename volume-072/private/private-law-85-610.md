# Private Law 85–610

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Louise Nanton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purpose of paragraph (2) of subsection (a) of section 352 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the time (whether before or after the enactment of this Act) during which Mrs. Louise Nanton has resided abroad with her daughter, Evelyn Nanton, while her daughter was an employee of the United States Government, shall not be counted in computing quantum of residence.


*Approved August 20, 1958.*

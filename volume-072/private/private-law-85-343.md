# Private Law 85–343

> AN ACT For the relief of Lieutenant Percy Hamilton Hebert.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That First Lieutenant Percy Hamilton Hebert, Air Force of the United States, retired, serial number AO–888402, is hereby relieved of all liability to pay to the United States the sum of $2,238. Such sum represents certain amounts erroneously paid to the said Percy Hamilton Hebert during the period between November 19, 1944, and June 30, 1950, inclusive, as a result of errors made in the computation of his retired pay.

The Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to the said Percy Hamilton Hebert an amount equal to the aggregate of amounts paid by him, or which have been withheld from sums otherwise due him, in complete or partial satisfaction of such claim of the United States.


*Approved February 19, 1958.*

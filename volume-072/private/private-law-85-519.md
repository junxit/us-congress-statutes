# Private Law 85–519

> AN ACT For the relief of Edward J. Doyle and Mrs. Edward J. (Billie M.) Doyle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to relieve Edward J. Doyle and Mrs. Edward J. (Billie M.) Doyle, of Manistique, Michigan, of all liability to refund the sum of $2,100. Such sum represents the amount of class E allotments which were erroneously paid his wife Billie M. Doyle for the period from September 1, 1942, to November 30, 1945.


*Approved August 6, 1958.*

# Private Law 85–662

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Henry Oscar (Olga McCurdy) Ramsey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, section 352 (a) shall not apply to Mrs. Henry Oscar (Olga McCurdy) Ramsey.


*Approved August 23, 1958.*

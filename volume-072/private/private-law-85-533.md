# Private Law 85–533

> AN ACT For the relief of Sadie Lobe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Sadie Lobe, of Cleveland, Ohio, is hereby relieved of all liability to pay to the United States the sum of $22,348.50. Amounts aggregating such sum were paid to the said Sadie Lobe during the periods from July 1, 1938, to June 30, 1943, and from July 1, 1951, to September 16, 1954, as an employee of the Department of the Treasury in contravention of prohibitions against the payment of compensation to persons not citizens of the United States which were included in annual appropriations Acts for such periods. In the audit and settlement of the accounts of any certifying or disbursing officer of the United States, full credit shall be given for all amounts for which liability is relieved by this Act.


*Approved August 8, 1958.*

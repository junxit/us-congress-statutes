# Private Law 85–733

> AN ACT For the relief of Concettina Iannacchino.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Concettina Iannacchino, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Luigi and Maddalena Iannacchino, citizens of the United States: Provided, That no natural parent of Concettina lannacchino, by virtue of such relationship, shall be accorded any right, status, or privilege under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved August 28, 1958.*

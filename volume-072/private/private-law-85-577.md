# Private Law 85–577

> AN ACT For the relief of Joseph R. Burger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 15 to 20, inclusive, of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes”, approved September 7, 1916, as amended (5 U. S. C. 765–770), are hereby waived in favor of Joseph R. Burger, for compensation for disability allegedly caused by his employment in the Navy Yard, Charleston, South Carolina, on April 14, 1943, and his claim is authorized and directed to be considered and acted upon under the remaining provisions of such Act, as amended, if he files such claim with the Department of Labor (Bureau of Employees’ Compensation) not later than six months after the date of enactment of this Act: Provided, That no benefits except hospital and medical expenses actually incurred shall accrue for any period of time prior to the date of enactment of this Act.


*Approved August 14, 1958.*

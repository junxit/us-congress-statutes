# Private Law 85–727

> AN ACT For the relief of Diana Elaine Greig.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Naturalization Act, the minor child, Diana Elaine Greig, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of J. Steadman and Veta L. Greig, citizens of the United States: Provided, That no natural parent of Diana Elaine Greig, by virtue of such relationship, shall be accorded any right, status, or privilege under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved August 28, 1958.*

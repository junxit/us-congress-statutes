# Private Law 85–616

> AN ACT For the relief of Wolfgang Stresemann.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, section 352 (a) (1) shall be held not applicable in the case of Wolfgang Stresemann: Provided, That he returns to the United States prior to October 20, 1961.


*Approved August 20, 1958.*

# Private Law 85–493

> AN ACT To provide for the advancement of Major General Claire L. Chennault, United States Air Force, retired, to the grade of lieutenant general on the retired list.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Major General Claire L. Chennault, United States Air Force, retired (AO–10090), shall be advanced on the retired list to the grade of lieutenant general, except that no increase in retired pay or benefits shall accrue as a result of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved July 18, 1958.*

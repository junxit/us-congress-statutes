# Private Law 85–490

> AN ACT For the relief of John Demetriou Asteron.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, John Demetriou Asteron, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Arthur Asters, citizens of the United States: Provided, That no natural parent, by virtue of such parentage, shall be accorded any right, status, or privilege under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved July 18, 1958.*

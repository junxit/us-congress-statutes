# Private Law 85–401

> AN ACT For the relief of Michael Romanoff.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Michael Romanoff shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of December 22, 1932, upon payment of the required visa fee.


*Approved May 16, 1958.*

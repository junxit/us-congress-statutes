# Private Law 85–504

> AN ACT For the relief of Maria Alma Dizon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Maria Alma Dizon shall be held and considered to be the minor natural-born alien child of Mr. Jose G. Dizon, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved July 25, 1958.*

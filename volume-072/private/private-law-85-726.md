# Private Law 85–726

> AN ACT For the relief of AlaLu Duncan Dillard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 15 to 20, inclusive, of the Federal Employees’ Compensation Act (5 U. S. C. 765–770), are hereby waived in favor of AlaLu Duncan Dillard as widow of Andrew B. Dillard, and her claim for compensation for the death of said Andrew B. Dillard, on February 13, 1938, which is alleged to have resulted from a personal injury suffered by him on February 10, 1938, while in the performance of his duties as a rural mail carrier at Mathews, Alabama, is authorized to be considered and acted upon under the remaining provisions of such Act, if filed with the Bureau of Employees’ Compensation within six months from the date of the enactment of this Act: Provided, That no benefits except medical expenses shall accrue for any period prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved August 28, 1958.*

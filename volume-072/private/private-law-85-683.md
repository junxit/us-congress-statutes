# Private Law 85–683

> AN ACT For the relief of Marianne (Sachiko) Fuller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Marianne (Sachiko) Fuller, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Jean M. Fuller, a citizen of the United States: Provided, That no natural parent of Marianne (Sachiko) Fuller, by virtue of such relationship, shall be accorded any right, status, or privilege under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved August 26, 1958.*

# Private Law 85–579

> AN ACT To quitclaim interest of the United States to certain land in Smith County, Mississippi, and to terminate restrictions against alienation thereon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all right, title, and interest of the United States in and to the following-described land in Smith County, Mississippi, is hereby quitclaimed to the patentee named in the patent from the United States dated November 10, 1852, Joseph Fisher, his successors, and assigns: Lots 3, 4, 5, and the southwest quarter of section 4, and the southeast quarter of section 5, all township 10 north, range 14 west, St. Stephens meridian, containing 489.56 acres, more or less.

## § 2.

Any restrictions against alienation contained in the patent described in section 1 of this Act are hereby removed, effective as of the date of this Act.


*Approved August 14, 1958.*

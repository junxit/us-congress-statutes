# Private Law 85–559

> JOINT RESOLUTION To facilitate the admission into the United States of certain aliens.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Helen Chronopoulos Vallas shall be held and considered to be the minor natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. George Vallas, citizens of the United States.

## § 2.

For the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Evangelia G. Alexandris, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Georgios Alexandris, citizens of the United States.

## § 3.

For the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Dionysia A. (Pseftis) Vlahos, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. George Annas, citizens of the United States.

## § 4.

For the purposes of sections 203 (a) (3) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Chan Yak Shing, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Peter Chin, also known as Chan Jung Dot, a lawful permanent resident of the United States.

## § 5.

The natural parents of the beneficiaries of this Act shall not, by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved August 14, 1958.*

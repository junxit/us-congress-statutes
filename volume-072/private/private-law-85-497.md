# Private Law 85–497

> AN ACT For the relief of Brigadier General Chester W. Goble.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Brigadier General Chester W. Goble, Army of the United States, retired, shall be advanced on the Army of the United States retired list to the rank of major general effective March 1, 1955, and shall be entitled to retired pay from such date computed on the basis of the rate of basic pay to which he would be entitled if serving on active duty in the grade of major general.


*Approved July 25, 1958.*

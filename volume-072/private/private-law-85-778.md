# Private Law 85–778

> AN ACT For the relief of Mercede Svaldi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 212 (a) (1) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mercede Svaldi may be issued a visa and admitted to the United States if she is found to be otherwise admissible under the provisions of that Act: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of said Act: Provided further, That this exemption shall apply only to grounds for exclusion of which the Department of State or the Department of Justice has knowledge prior to enactment of this Act.


*Approved September 2, 1958.*

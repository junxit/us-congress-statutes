# Private Law 85–729

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Margaret Graham Bonnalie.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, section 352 (a) (1) shall be held not to have been or to be applicable in the case of Mrs. Margaret Graham Bonnalie: Provided, That she returns to the United States for permanent residence within two years following the date of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved August 28, 1958.*

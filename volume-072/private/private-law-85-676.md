# Private Law 85–676

> AN ACT For the relief of Kiyoshi Ueda.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Kiyoshi Ueda shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Willis Edward Fisher, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved August 26, 1958.*

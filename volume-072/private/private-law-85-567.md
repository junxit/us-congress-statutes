# Private Law 85–567

> AN ACT To quiet title and possession with respect to certain real property in the county of San Jacinto, Texas, and authorizing named parties to bring suit for title and possession of same.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That jurisdiction is hereby conferred notwithstanding the lapse of time, laches, or statutes of limitation, on the United States District Court for the Southern District of Texas, Houston Division, to hear, determine, and render judgment on the claims and controversies of the parties hereinafter named in groups 1 through 6, respectively, concerning the title and possession of, and for damages to the land included within the following described tracts of land, numbered 1 through 6, respectively, in corresponding numerical order; and those parties are hereby granted permission and are authorized to bring suit or suits in said courts against the United States of America for the title and possession of, and for damages to the land included within the tracts described herein:

# Private Law 85–391

> AN ACT To clear the title to certain Indian land.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States hereby disclaims on behalf of itself and any Indian allottee, or his heirs or devisees, any interest in land described as: Lot 6, section 17, township 19 south, range 24 east, sixth principal meridian, Miami County, Kansas, and the east half northwest quarter and lots 2 and 3, section 20, township 19 south, range 24 east, sixth principal meridian, Linn County, Kansas, containing 153.10 acres more or less, which lands were conveyed under guardian’s deed to G. Lehr on April 6, 1868, by A. G. McKensie, guardian of So-we-lah-shing or Brown Cabbage, and approved by O. H. Browning, Secretary of the Interior, on November 5, 1868.


*Approved May 9, 1958.*

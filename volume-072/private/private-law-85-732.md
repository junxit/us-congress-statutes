# Private Law 85–732

> AN ACT For the relief of Hing Man Chau.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203 (a) (3) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Hing Man Chau shall be held and considered to be the minor child of King Yat Chau, an alien lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence.


*Approved August 28, 1958.*

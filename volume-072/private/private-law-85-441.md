# Private Law 85–441

> AN ACT For the relief of Eva Lichtfuss.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203 (a) (3) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, and section 5 of Public Law 316, Eighty-fifth Congress, Eva Lichtfuss shall be held and considered to be the minor alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Johann Lichtfuss, lawful permanent residents of the United States.


*Approved June 28, 1958.*

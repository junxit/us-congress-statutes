# Private Law 85–358

> JOINT RESOLUTION To facilitate the admission into the United States of certain aliens.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Florentina Laurente shall be held and considered to be the minor alien child of Anselmo Laurente, a citizen of the United States.

## § 2.

For the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Joon Wong Choi, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Chaplain (Captain) Albert L. Gamble, a citizen of the United States.

## § 3.

In the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, section 202 (c) (1) shall not be applicable in connection with the application for an immigrant visa by Carolyn Foster: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, be deposited as prescribed by section 213 of the said Act.

## § 4.

For the purposes of sections 203 (a) (3) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Elio Rotondo shall be held and considered to be the natural-born minor alien child of Giovanni and Elisa Rotondo, lawfully resident aliens in the United States.

## § 5.

For the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Lee Tai Chon, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Donald Nichols, a citizen of the United States.

## § 6.

For the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Rosario Munez Basante, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. Francisco Basante dos Santos, citizens of the United States.

## § 7.

For the purposes of sections 101 (a) (27) (A) and 205 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the minor child, Slobodan Galeb, shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien child of Mr. and Mrs. B. T. Galeb, citizens of the United States.


*Approved March 6, 1958.*

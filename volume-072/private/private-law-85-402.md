# Private Law 85–402

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Helen Harvey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Mrs. Helen Harvey, of Alexandria, Virginia, is hereby relieved of all liability to refund to the United States the sum of $1,750. Such sum represents the amount of the voluntary allotment payments which were erroneously made by the Department of the Army to the said Mrs. Helen Harvey, as the wife of Warrant Officer (junior grade) Merle L. Harvey, United States Army, during the period beginning April 1, 1953, and ending October 31, 1953, after the said Merle L. Harvey had been discharged from the Army. In the audit and settlement of the accounts of any certifying or disbursing officer of the United States, full credit shall be given for the amount for which liability is relieved by this Act.


*Approved May 29, 1958.*

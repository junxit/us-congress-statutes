# Public Law 103–234

> An Act To redesignate the Federal building located at 380 Trapelo Road in Waltham, Massachusetts, as the “Frederick C. Murphy Federal Center”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. REDESIGNATION.

The Federal building located at 380 Trapelo Road in Waltham, Massachusetts, and known as the Waltham Federal Center, shall be known and designated as the “Frederick C. Murphy Federal Center”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Federal building referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Frederick C. Murphy Federal Center”.


*Approved April 14, 1994.*

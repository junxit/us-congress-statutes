# Public Law 103–288

> An Act To designate the United States courthouse located at 940 Front Street in San Diego, California, and the Federal building attached to the courthouse as the “Edward J. Schwartz Courthouse and Federal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The United States courthouse located at 940 Front Street in San Diego, California, and the Federal building attached to the courthouse shall be known and designated as the “Edward J. Schwartz Courthouse and Federal Building”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the courthouse and Federal building referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Edward J. Schwartz Courthouse and Federal Building”.


*Approved August 1, 1994.*

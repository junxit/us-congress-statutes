# Public Law 103–385

> An Act To redesignate the Post Office building located at 1000 Lamar Street in Wichita Falls, Texas, as the “Graham B. Purcell, Jr. Post Office Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. REDESIGNATION.

The Post Office building located at 1000 Lamar Street in Wichita Falls, Texas, and known as the Main Post Office, shall be known and designated as the “Graham B. Purcell, Jr. Post Office Building”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Post Office building referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the Graham B. Purcell, Jr. Post Office Building.


*Approved October 22, 1994.*

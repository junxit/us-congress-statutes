# Public Law 103–395

> Joint Resolution Providing for the convening of the First Session of the One Hundred Fourth Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first regular session of the One Hundred Fourth Congress shall begin at noon on Wednesday, January 4, 1995.


*Approved October 22, 1994.*

# Public Law 103–241

> An Act To designate the United States courthouse under construction in Denver, Colorado, as the “Byron White United States Courthouse”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The United States courthouse being constructed through renovation of the old post office building in Denver, Colorado, shall be known and designated as the “Byron White United States Court-house”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the courthouse referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Byron White United States Courthouse”.


*Approved May 4, 1994.*

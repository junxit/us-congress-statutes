# Public Law 103–274

> An Act To designate the United States courthouse located in Bridgeport, Connecticut, as the “Brien McMahon Federal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The United States courthouse located at 915 Lafayette Boulevard in Bridgeport, Connecticut, shall be known and designated as the “Brien McMahon Federal Building”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the courthouse referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Brien McMahon Federal Building”.


*Approved July 5, 1994.*

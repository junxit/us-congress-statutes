# Public Law 103–249

> An Act To designate the Federal building located at 711 Washington Street in Boston, Massachusetts, as the “Jean Mayer Human Nutrition Research Center on Aging”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The Federal building located at 711 Washington Street in Boston, Massachusetts, shall be known and designated as the “Jean Mayer Human Nutrition Research Center on Aging”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Federal building referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Jean Mayer Human Nutrition Research Center on Aging”.


*Approved May 16, 1994.*

# Public Law 103–213

> An Act To designate the Federal building located at 525 Griffin Street in Dallas, Texas, as the “A. Maceo Smith Federal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The Federal building located at 525 Griffin Street in Dallas, Texas, is designated as the “A. Maceo Smith Federal Building”.

## § 2. LEGAL REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, regulation, document, record, map, or other paper of the United States to the building referred to in section 1 is deemed to be a reference to the “A Maceo Smith Federal Building”.


*Approved February 16, 1994.*

# Public Law 103–228

> An Act To temporarily extend certain provisions of the Marine Mammal Protection Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 114(a)(1) of the Marine Mammal Protection Act of 1972 (16 U.S.C. 1383a(a)(1)), is amended by striking “April 1, 1994,” and inserting in lieu thereof “May 1, 1994.”.


*Approved March 31, 1994.*

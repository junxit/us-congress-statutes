# Public Law 103–214

> An Act To designate the Federal building located at 100 East Fifth Street in Cincinnati, Ohio, as the “Potter Stewart United States Courthouse”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The Federal building located at 100 East Fifth Street in Cincinnati, Ohio, shall be known and designated as the “Potter Stewart United States Courthouse”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Federal building referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Potter Stewart United States Courthouse”.


*Approved February 16, 1994.*

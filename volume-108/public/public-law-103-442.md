# Public Law 103–442

> An Act To amend title 18, United States Code, with respect to certain crimes relating to Congressional medals of honor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 704(b)(2)(B) of title 18, United States Code, is amended by inserting “, 6241, or 8741” after “3741”.


*Approved November 2, 1994.*

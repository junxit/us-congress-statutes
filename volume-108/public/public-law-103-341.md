# Public Law 103–341

> An Act To designate the United States Post Office building located at 220 South 40th Avenue in Hattiesburg, Mississippi, as the “Roy M. Wheat Post Office”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The United States Post Office building located at 220 South 40th Avenue in Hattiesburg, Mississippi, shall be known and designated as the “Roy M. Wheat Post Office”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the building referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the Roy M. Wheat Post Office.


*Approved October 6, 1994.*

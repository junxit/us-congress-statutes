# Public Law 103–212

> An Act To designate the Federal Building and United States Courthouse located at 402 East State Street in Trenton, New Jersey, as the “Clarkson S. Fisher Federal Building and United States Courthouse”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The Federal Building and United States Courthouse located at 402 East State Street in Trenton, New Jersey, shall be known and designated as the “Clarkson S. Fisher Federal Building and United States Courthouse”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Federal Building and United States Courthouse referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Clarkson S. Fisher Federal Building and United States Courthouse”.


*Approved February 16, 1994.*

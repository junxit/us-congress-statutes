# Public Law 103–459

> An Act To designate the United States Post Office building located at 103–104 Estate Richmond in Saint Croix, Virgin Islands, as the “Wilbert Armstrong Poet Office”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 101. DESIGNATION.

The United States Post Office building located at 103–104 Estate Richmond in Saint Croix, Virgin Islands, shall be known and designated as the “Wilbert Armstrong Post Office”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the building referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the Wilbert Armstrong Post Office.


*Approved November 2, 1994.*

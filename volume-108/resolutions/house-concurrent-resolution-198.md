# House Concurrent Resolution 198

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Wednesday, January 26, 1994, it stand adjourned until noon on Tuesday, February 1, 1994.


*Agreed to January 25, 1994.*

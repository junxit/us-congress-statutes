# House Concurrent Resolution 222

> RAOUL WALLENBERG BUST

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Joint Committee on the Library is authorized to accept a bust of Raoul Wallenberg and to place the bust in an appropriate location in the Capitol, as determined by the Joint Committee on the Library.


*Agreed June 21, 1994.*

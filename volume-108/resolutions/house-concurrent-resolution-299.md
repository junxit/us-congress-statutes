# House Concurrent Resolution 299

> “HISPANIC AMERICANS IN CONGRESS”— HOUSE PRINT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the book entitled “Hispanic Americans in Congress”, prepared under the direction of the Joint Committee on Printing, shall be printed as a House document, with illustrations and suitable binding.

## § 2.

In addition to the usual number, there shall be printed, for the use of the Joint Committee on Printing, the lesser of—

- **(1)** 25,000 copies of the document; or
- **(2)** such number of copies of the document as does not exceed a total production and printing cost of $110,000.


*Agreed to October 7, 1994.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 275

> ADJOURNMENT SINE DIE—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That, notwithstanding the provisions of section 132(a)(1) of the Legislative Reorganization Act of 1946 (2 U.S.C. 198), the House of Representatives and the Senate shall not adjourn for a period in excess of three days, or adjourn sine die, until both Houses of Congress have adopted a concurrent resolution providing either for an adjournment (in excess of three days) to a day certain or for adjournment sine die.


*Agreed to July 29, 1994.*

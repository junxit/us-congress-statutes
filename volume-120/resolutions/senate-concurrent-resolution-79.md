# Senate Concurrent Resolution 79

> PALESTINIAN AUTHORITY—RESTRICTING UNITED STATES ASSISTANCE

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That it is the sense of Congress that no United States assistance should be provided directly to the Palestinian Authority if any representative political party holding a majority of parliamentary seats within the Palestinian Authority maintains a position calling for the destruction of Israel.


*Agreed to February 1, 2006.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 112

> ENROLLMENT CORRECTION—S. 3693

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill, S. 3693, the Secretary of the Senate shall insert “or reentries” after “States, reentry” in section 212(a)(9)(C)(iii)(II) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, as added by section 6(b)(1)(C) of the bill.


*Agreed to July 29, 2006.*

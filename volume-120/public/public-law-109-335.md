# Public Law 109–335

> An Act To designate the Federal building and United States courthouse located at 2 South Main Street in Akron, Ohio, as the “John F. Seiberling Federal Building and United States Courthouse”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION.

The Federal building and United States courthouse located at 2 South Main Street in Akron, Ohio, shall be known and designated as the “John F. Seiberling Federal Building and United States Courthouse”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Federal building and United States courthouse referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “John F. Seiberling Federal Building and United States Courthouse”.


*Approved October 12, 2006.*

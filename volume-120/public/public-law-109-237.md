# Public Law 109–237

> An Act To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 520 Colorado Avenue in Arriba, Colorado, as the “William H. Emery Post Office”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. WILLIAM H. EMERY POST OFFICE.

- **(a)** *Designation.—* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 520 Colorado Avenue in Arriba, Colorado, shall be known and designated as the “William H. Emery Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References.—* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “William H. Emery Post Office”.


*Approved June 23, 2006.*

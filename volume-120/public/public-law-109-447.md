# Public Law 109–447

> Joint Resolution Appointing the day for the convening of the first session of the One Hundred Tenth Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first regular session of the One Hundred Tenth Congress shall begin at noon on Thursday, January 4, 2007.


*Approved December 22, 2006.*

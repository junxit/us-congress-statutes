# Public Law 109–309

> An Act To amend the Ojito Wilderness Act to make a technical correction.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. OJITO WILDERNESS MAP.

Section 2(1) of the Ojito Wilderness Act (16 U.S.C. 1132 note; Public Law 109–94) is amended by striking “October 1, 2004” and inserting “January 24, 2006”.


*Approved October 6, 2006.*

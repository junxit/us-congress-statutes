# Public Law 109–354

> An Act To revise the boundaries of John H. Chafee Coastal Barrier Resources System Jekyll Island Unit GA–06P.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. REPLACEMENT OF CERTAIN JOHN H. CHAFEE COASTAL BARRIER RESOURCES SYSTEM MAP.

- **(a)** *In General.—* The map subtitled “GA–06P”, relating to the John H. Chafee Coastal Barrier Resources System unit designated as Coastal Barrier Resources System Jekyll Island Unit GA–06P, that is included in the set of maps entitled “John H. Chafee Coastal Barrier Resources System” and referred to in section 4(a) of the Coastal Barrier Resources Act (16 U.S.C. 3503(a)), is hereby replaced by another map relating to the unit entitled “John H. Chafee Coastal Barrier Resources System Jekyll Island Unit GA–06P” and dated July 10, 2006.
- **(b)** *Availability.—* The Secretary of the Interior shall keep the replacement map referred to in subsection (a) on file and available for inspection in accordance with the provisions of section 4(b) of the Coastal Barrier Resources Act (16 U.S.C. 3503(b)).


*Approved October 16, 2006.*

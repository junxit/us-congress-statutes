# Public Law 109–278

> An Act To designate the facility of the United States Postal Service located at 1310 Highway 64 NW. in Ramsey, Indiana, as the “Wilfred Edward ‘Cousin Willie’ Sieg, Sr. Post Office”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. WILFRED EDWARD “COUSIN WILLIE” SIEG, SR. POST OFFICE.

- **(a)** *Designation.—* The facility of the United States Postal Service located at 1310 Highway 64 NW. in Ramsey, Indiana, shall be known and designated as the “Wilfred Edward ‘Cousin Willie’ Sieg, Sr. Post Office”.
- **(b)** *References.—* Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the facility referred to in subsection (a) shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Wilfred Edward ‘Cousin Willie’ Sieg, Sr. Post Office”.


*Approved August 17, 2006.*

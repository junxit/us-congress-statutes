# Public Law 109–426

## § 1. REPEAL OF TERMINATION OF AUTHORITY TO USE PENALTY AND FRANKED MAIL TO LOCATE AND RECOVER MISSING CHILDREN.

Public Law 99–87 is amended by striking section 5 (39 U.S.C. 3220 note).

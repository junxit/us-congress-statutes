# Public Law 109–323

> An Act To extend the waiver authority for the Secretary of Education under title IV, section 105, of Public Law 109–148.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representa­tives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. EXTENSION OF AUTHORITY.

Section 105 of title IV of division B of Public Law 109–148 (119 Stat. 2797) is amended—

- **(1)** in subsection (b), by inserting “and, at the discretion of the Secretary, for fiscal year 2007” after “2006”; and
- **(2)** in subsection (c)(2)—
  - **(A)** by inserting “or 2007” after “fiscal year 2006”; and
  - **(B)** by striking “fiscal year 2007” and inserting “for the respective succeeding fiscal year”.


*Approved October 11, 2006.*

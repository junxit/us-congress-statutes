# Public Law 109–369

> Joint Resolution Making further continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 2007, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Continuing Appropriations Resolution, 2007 (Public Law 109–289, division B) is amended by striking the date specified in section 106(3) and inserting “December 8, 2006”.


*Approved November 17, 2006.*

# Public Law 109–325

> An Act To extend relocation expenses test programs for Federal employees.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. EXTENSION OF RELOCATION EXPENSES TEST PROGRAMS.

- **(a)** *In General.—* Section 5739 of title 5, United States Code, is amended—
  - **(1)** in subsection (a)(1), by striking “for a period not to exceed 24 months”; and
  - **(2)** in subsection (e), by striking “7 years” and inserting “11 years”.
- **(b)** *Effective Date.—* The amendments made by this section shall take effect as though enacted as part of the Travel and Transportation Reform Act of 1998 (Public Law 105–264; 112 Stat. 2350).


*Approved October 11, 2006.*

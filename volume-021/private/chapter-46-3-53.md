# Chapter 53

> An Act granting a pension to, Mrs. Elizabeth Upright.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-rolls the name of Mrs. Elizabeth Upright, of Rockford, in the State of Iowa, who sent eleven sous into the Union Army and to pay her a pension from the passage of this act at the rate per month now allowed to parents for the loss of children upon whom such parents were dependent.


*Approved, February 15, 1881.*

# Chapter 166

> An Act granting a pension to Mary Ann McCarrol.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

CHAP. 166.—An act granting a pension to Mary Ann McCarrol.

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Mary Ann McCarrol, of Allegheny County, Pennsylvania, widow of James McCarrol, deceased, late a member of Company B, Forty-third Ohio Volunteers, who died from disease and exposure contracted in the line of duty while in the military service of the United States.


*Approved, March 3, 1881.*

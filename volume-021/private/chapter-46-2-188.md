# Chapter 188

> An Act granting a pension to John S. Corlett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

CHAP. 188.—An act granting a pension to John S. Corlett.

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John S. Corlett late a teamster in the service of the United States, and pay him the pension of a private soldier


*Approved, March 3, 1881.*

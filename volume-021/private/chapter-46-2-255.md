# Chapter 255

> An Act granting a pension to Harvey Burk.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Harvey Burk, late a private in Company E, of the Sixty-seventh Regiment Indiana Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, June 16, 1880.*

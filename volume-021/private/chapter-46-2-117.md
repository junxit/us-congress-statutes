# Chapter 117

> An Act for the relief of Edward Braden and J. W. Angus.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the claim of Edward Braden and J. W. Angus for extra compensation for the construction of supply depot building and shops at San Antonio, Texas, be referred to the Court of Claims to be heard and determined according to law and justice


*Approved, June 1, 1880.*

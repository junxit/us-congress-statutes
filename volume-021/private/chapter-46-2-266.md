# Chapter 266

> An Act authorizing the Secretary of the Interior to place upon the pension-roll the name of Della Benner, widow of the late Lieutenant Hiram H. Benner, of Company "C", Eighteenth Infantry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of American Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be and he is hereby authorized and directed to have placed upon the pension-roll the name of Della Benner, widow of the late Lieutenant Hiram H. Benner, of Company “C”, Eighteenth Infantry.


*Approved, June 16, 1880.*

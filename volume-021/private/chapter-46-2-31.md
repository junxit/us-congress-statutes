# Chapter 31

> An Act for the relief of J. P. Zimmerman and H. P. Snow, of Clinton County, Kentucky.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of ninety- eight dollars to J. P. Zimmerman and H. P. Snow, citizens of Clinton County, Kentucky, being the amount of a forfeited mail-route bond paid by them.


*Approved, February 24, 1880.*

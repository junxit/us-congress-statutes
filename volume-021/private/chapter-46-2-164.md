# Chapter 164

> An Act granting a pension to Wyatt Botts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

CHAP. 164.—An act granting a pension to Wyatt Botts.

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, at the rate of eight dollars per month, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Wyatt Botts, late a private in Company B, Eighty-eighth Regiment Infantry Ohio Volunteers.


*Approved, March 3, 1881.*

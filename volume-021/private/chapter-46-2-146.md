# Chapter 146

> An Act granting a pension to Abner Hoopes

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Abner Hoopes, late a private in Company A, First Regiment of Pennsylvania Reserves, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, and pay him a pension from and after the passage of the act.


*Approved, June 8, 1880.*

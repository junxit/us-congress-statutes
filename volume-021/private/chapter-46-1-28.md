# Chapter 28

> An Act to remove the political disabilities of James F. Harrison, of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States on James F. Harri- 80n, of Virginia, be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, June 18, 1879.*

# Chapter 86

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Mary Allison.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Mary Allison, widow of Robert Allison, a soldier of the war of eighteen hundred and twelve.


*Approved, May 12, 1880.*

# Chapter 218

> An Act to remove the political disabilities of Joseph A. Seawell, of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concur- ring therein),*

That all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States, on Joseph A. Seawell, be, and the same are, hereby removed.


*Approved, June 14, 1880.*

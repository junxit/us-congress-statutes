# Chapter 199

> An Act to remove the political disabilities of William Sharp of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States on William Sharp of Virginia be and the same are hereby removed.


*Approved, June 10, 1880.*

# Chapter 48

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Crafts J. Wright.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Crafts J. Wright, late colonel Thirteenth Regiment Missouri Volunteers, a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that which he now receives, to take effect from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 15, 1881.*

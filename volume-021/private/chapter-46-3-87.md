# Chapter 87

> An Act granting a pension to William Bowman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of William Bowman, Company B, Ninety-first Veteran Pennsylvania Volunteers, at the rate of six dollars per month, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension-laws.


*Approved, February 26, 1881.*

# Chapter 191

> An Act for the relief of Edward T. Brownell

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

CHAP. 191.—An act for the relief of Edward T. Brownell

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to pay Edward T. Brownell the sum at ninety dollars; said sum being the amount of two drafts on the United States depository at Chicago, Illinois, drawn byB. J. Sweet, late pension agent, one draft of thirty dollars payable to Syrena McDade, and one for sixty dollars payable to Almira M. Bacon, both of said drafts being indorsed payable to Edward T. Brownell; and said drafts are still outstanding.


*Approved, March 3, 1881.*

# Chapter 195

> An Act granting a pension to Aaron Hatcher.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Aaron Hatcher, late private Company F of the Twenty-third Regiment Missouri Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, June 10, 1880.*

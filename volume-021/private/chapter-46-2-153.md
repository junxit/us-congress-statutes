# Chapter 153

> An Act for the relief of the heirs of Charles B. Smith, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the Treasury be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed, in the settlement of the accounts of Charles B. Smith, deceased, late first lieutenant of the Fifth Iowa Cavalry Volunteers, to receive and allow, where the proper vouchers cannot be procured, a statement veri- fled, or other satisfactory evidence, of all expenditures or issues made by him for the government, as will be sufiicicnt to close his subsistence and quartermaster’s accounts upon the books of the Treasury.


*Approved, June 8, 1880.*

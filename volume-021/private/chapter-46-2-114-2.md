# Chapter 114

> An Act for the relief of Passed Assistant Engineer Absalom Kirby, of the Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

CHAP. 114.—An act for the relief of Passed Assistant Engineer Absalom Kirby, of the Navy.

That the act of March third, eighteen hundred and seventy-three, restoring Passed Assistant Engineer Absalom Kirby to the active-list of the Navy, be so construed as to allow him the pay of his present grade from the eleventh of October, eighteen hundred and sixty-six, the date from which he takes rank; and a sum sufficient to pay him the amount found to be due is hereby appropriated out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 2, 1881.*

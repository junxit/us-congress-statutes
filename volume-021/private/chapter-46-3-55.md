# Chapter 55

> An Act for the relief of Henry C. Groomes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of Américain Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, to increase the pension of Henry C. Groomes, a private in Company D, Thirteenth Regiment of Indiana Volunteers.


*Approved, February 15, 1881.*

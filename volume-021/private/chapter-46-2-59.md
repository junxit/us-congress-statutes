# Chapter 59

> An Act to remove the political disabilities of Roger A. Pryor, of New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate a/nd House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all political disabilities imposed upon Boger A. Pryor, now a citizen of Now York, by the fourteenth amendment to the constitution of the United States, be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, April 22, 1880.*

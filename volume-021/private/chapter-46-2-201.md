# Chapter 201

> An Act to remove the political disabilities of John M. Brooke, of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all disabilities imposed upon John M. Brooke of Virginia by the third section of the fourteenth article of the amendments to the Constitution of the United States, be, and the same are hereby removed.


*Approved, June 10, 1880.*

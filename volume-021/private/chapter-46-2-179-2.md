# Chapter 179

> An Act granting an increase of pension to James H. Reeve.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

CHAP. 179.—An act granting an increase of pension to James H. Reeve.

That James II. Reeve, late captain and brevet major in the Third Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, be, and he is hereby, granted and allowed, from June twenty-sixth, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, a pension at the rate of twenty- four dollars per month; and the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of said James II. Reeve on the pension-roll at said rate, in lieu of the pension now paid him, deducting therefrom the amounts already paid him.


*Approved, March 3, 1881.*

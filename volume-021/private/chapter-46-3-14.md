# Chapter 14

> An Act granting a pension to Margaret S. Heintzelman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension- roll the name of Margaret S. Heintzelman, widow of Major-General Samuel P. Heintzelman, deceased, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, December 23, 1880.*

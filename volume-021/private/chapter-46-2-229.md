# Chapter 229

> An Act for the relief of Clement C. Clay, of Alabama.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That the disabilities imposed by the third section of the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States are removed from Clement 0. Clay, of Alabama.


*Approved, June 15, 1880.*

# Chapter 99

> An Act to remove the political disabilities of Francis L. Galt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all political disabilities imposed upon Francis L. Galt of Virginia by the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States, be, and the same are hereby, removed.


*Approved, May 22, 1880.*

# Private Resolution 27

> Joint Resolution authorizing tents to be loaned to the Governor of Missour for the use of sufferers by the recent tornado in that State.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to loan to the Governor of the State of Missouri, five hundred tents or so many thereof as may be required to be used for the benefit of the sufferers from the recent tornado in that State, said tents to be returned when no longer required for the purpose herein specified.


*Approved, April 28, 1880.*

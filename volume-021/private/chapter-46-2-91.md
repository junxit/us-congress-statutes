# Chapter 91

> An Act for the relief of George V. Hebb.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the Treasury are hereby authorized and directed to audit the claim of George V. Hebb, late captain and assistant quartermaster in Mexico, for any balance of pay that may be found due him as an officer, and to pay the amount due in full settlement of the same; and a sufficient sum is hereby appropriated out of any money not otherwise appropriated by law, to pay the same, not to exceed the sum of one hundred and thirty- six dollars and fifty cents.


*Approved, May 14, 1880.*

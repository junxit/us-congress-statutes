# Private Resolution 7

> Joint Resolution to pay Patrick Doran for services as messenger, House of Representatives.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Clerk of the House be and he is hereby authorized and directed to pay to Patrick Doran for his services as messenger to the Committee on Expenditures in the Treasury Department during the last session of Forty-fifth Congress the sum of two hundred and ninety-one dollars and sixty cents, in full and final settlement for such services. And the amount to pay the same is hereby appropriated out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 18, 1879.*

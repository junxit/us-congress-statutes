# Chapter 194

> An Act granting a pension to Sally M. Buchanan, widow of General Robert C. Buchanan, United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

CHAP. 194.—An act granting a pension to Sally M. Buchanan, widow of General Robert C. Buchanan, United States Army.

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension- roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sallie M. Buchanan, widow of the late Robert C. Buchanan, colonel and brevet major-general in the United States Army.


*Approved, March 3, 1881.*

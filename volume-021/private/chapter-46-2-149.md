# Chapter 149

> An Act granting a pension to Jacob J. Smith.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Hawse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Hie Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Jacob J. Smith, first lieutenant of Company H, Twelfth Regiment, of Pennsylvania Cavalry, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, June 8, 1880.*

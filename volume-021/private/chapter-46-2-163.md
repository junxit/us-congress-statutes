# Chapter 163

> An Act granting a pension to Jacob Gelwicks.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

CHAP. 163.—An act granting a pension to Jacob Gel wicks.

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jacob Gelwicks, late Company L, Eleventh Regiment Missouri Militia.


*Approved, March 3, 1881.*

# Chapter 167

> An Act for the relief of Calvin Bronson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

CHAP. 167.—An act for the relief of Calvin Bronson.

That the Secretary of the Treasury be authorized and directed to pay to Calvin Bronson eleven thousand two hundred and eleven dollars out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, in full satisfaction of an illegal tax assessed upon certain smoking tobacco and paid by him under protest.


*Approved, March 3, 1881.*

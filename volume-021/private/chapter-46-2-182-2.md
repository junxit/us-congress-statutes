# Chapter 182

> An Act for the relief of Hardie Hogan Helper.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

CHAP. 182.—An act for the relief of Hardie Hogan Helper.

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Hardie Hogan Helper, who was disabled while on special duty under the command of General A. E. Burnside, in the war of the late rebellion, and pay him a pension at the rate of ten dollars per month from the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1881.*

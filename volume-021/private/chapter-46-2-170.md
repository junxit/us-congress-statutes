# Chapter 170

> An Act granting a pension to Edward H. Mitchell

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

CHAP. 170.—An act granting a pension to Edward H. Mitchell.

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension- roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Edward EL Mitchell, of Company I, Seventy-first Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers.


*Approved, March 3, 1881.*

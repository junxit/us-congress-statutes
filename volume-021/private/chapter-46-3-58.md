# Chapter 58

> An Act granting a pension to Jacob Ginder.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior bo, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jacob Ginder father of David H. Ginder, deceased, late a captain Company I, Eighty-first Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer’s.


*Approved, February 15, 1881.*

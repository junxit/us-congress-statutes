# Chapter 181

> An Act to reimburse Charles Dougherty for his expenses to the consulate of Londonderry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and is hereby, authorized and required to pay Charles Dougherty, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one thousand dollars, to reimburse him for his expenses to the consulate of Londonderry.


*Approved, June 9, 1880.*

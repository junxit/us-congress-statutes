# Chapter 113

> An Act for the relief of Philemon B. Hawkins

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Rouse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

CHAP. 113.—An act for the relief of Philemon B. Hawkins

That the Treasurer of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized to pay to Philemon B. Hawkins the sum of seven thousand one hundred and ninety dollars and sixty-three cents for stone furnished for the courthouse and post-office building in the city of Kaleigh, North Carolina.


*Approved, March 2, 1881.*

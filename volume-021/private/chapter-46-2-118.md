# Chapter 118

> An Act for the relief of Ernst Hein.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War is hereby authorized and directed to cause to be issued to Ernst Hein, late corporal of Company C, Thirteenth Regiment Massachusetts Veteran Reserve Corps, an honorable discharge from the service of the United States to date from November thirtieth, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, and to remove the charge of desertion from the military record of said Ernst Hein.


*Approved, June 1, 1880.*

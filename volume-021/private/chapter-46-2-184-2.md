# Chapter 184

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Anna I. Guest.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

CHAP. 184.—An act granting a pension to Mrs. Anna I. Guest.

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Anna I. Guest widow of John Guest, late commodore in the United States Navy, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars from and after th e passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1881.*

# Chapter 267

> An Act to remove the political disabilities of John S. Maury.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two thirds of each House concurring therein},*

That all political disabilities imposed upon John 8. Maury by the third section of the fourteenth article of amendments to the Constitution of the United States be, and the same are hereby removed.


*Approved, June 16, 1880.*

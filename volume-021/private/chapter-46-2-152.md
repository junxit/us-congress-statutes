# Chapter 152

> An Act for the relief of John N. Reed.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of four thousand one hundred and twenty-four dollars and fifty cents be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to pay John N. Reed for material delivered, labor, time, and demurrages, upon the contract of Reed, Pitt and McPherson, approved by the Secretary of War on the third day of September, eighteen hundred and sixty, and assigned to said Reed, and shall be in full discharge of all claim against the United States in any manner arising upon or by reason of the said contract, or for any non-fulfillment thereof.


*Approved, June 8, 1880.*

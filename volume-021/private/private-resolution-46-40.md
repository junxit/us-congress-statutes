# Private Resolution 40

> Joint Resolution authorizing the settlement of the account of Thomas Worthington, late colonel Forty-sixth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, authorized and required to settle and adjust the account of Colonel Thomas Worthington, Forty-sixth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, engaged in the service of the United States during the late war, by crediting and paying him, in addition to what he has already received on account of said services, the sum of nine hundred and sixty-two dollars and forty-nine cents, being the amount of pay to which he would have been entitled as colonel of volunteers from November twenty-first, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, the date of his last payment, to March first, eighteen hundred and sixty-three: Provided, That this compensation shall be accepted and receipted for by said Worthington as a full satisfaction of all claims on his part against the United States on account of said military services.


*Approved, June 1, 1880.*

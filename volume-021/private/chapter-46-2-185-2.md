# Chapter 185

> An Act granting a pension to Luman Case

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

CHAP. 185.—An act granting a pension to Luman Case

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Luman Case.


*Approved, March 3, 1881.*

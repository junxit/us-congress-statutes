# Chapter 111

> An Act granting a pension to Edward U Leib.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension- roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Edward U. Leib, late a brevet lieutenant-colonel, United States Army, captain Fifth United States Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he now receives.


*Approved, May 28, 1880.*

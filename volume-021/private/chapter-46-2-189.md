# Chapter 189

> An Act for the relief of James R. Gordon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

CHAP. 189.—-An act for the relief of James K. Gordon.

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to place the name of James It. Gordon, late a private in Company D, of the Sixteenth Regiment of Pennsylvania Cavalry Volunteers, on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws of the United States.


*Approved, March 3, 1881.*

# Chapter 169

> An Act granting a pension to Henry Mills.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

CHAP. 169.—An act granting a pension to Henry Mills.

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Henry Mills, late a private in Company D, of the Ninety-eighth Regiment Illinois Volunteers.


*Approved, March 3, 1881.*

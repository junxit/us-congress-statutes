# Chapter 126

> An Act to admit free of duty one of the bells of Saint Michael's chimes, Charleston, South Carolina, which has been sent to England to be recast.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

CHAP. 126.—An act to admit free of duty one of the bells of Saint Michael’s chimes, Charleston, South Carolina, which has been sent to England to be recast.

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to admit free of import duty one of the bells composing the chimes of Saint Michael’s Church, Charleston, South Carolina, after it has been recast at the foundery where it was first manufactured in seventeen hundred and sixty-four and been reshipped to America.


*Approved, March 2, 1881.*

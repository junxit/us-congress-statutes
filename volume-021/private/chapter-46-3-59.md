# Chapter 59

> An Act granting a pension to James Forsyth Harrison.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of James Forsyth Harrison, of Cumberland, Maryland, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 15, 1881.*

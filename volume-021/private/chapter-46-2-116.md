# Chapter 116

> An Act for the relief of Joseph Clymer, of Texas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

CHAP. 116.—An act for the relief of Joseph Clymor, of Texas.

That the sum of eighteen thousand three hundred and twenty-five dollars be, and the same hereby is, appropriated, out of any moneys in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to pay Joseph Clymer, of Texas, the amount due him from the United States on a contract for transportation of Army stores made between said Clymer and the United States on the eighteenth day of April, eighteen hundred and fifty-one.


*Approved, March 2, 1881.*

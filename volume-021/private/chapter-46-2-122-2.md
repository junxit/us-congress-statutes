# Chapter 122

> An Act for the relief of George W. Brower.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

CHAP. 122.—An act for the relief of George W. Brower.

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any moneys in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to George W. Brower, late sheriff of Dodge County, Wisconsin, the sum of fourteen hundred and forty-three dollars and five cents; the same to be payment in full for personal services rendered and expenses incurred in connection with the draft of eighteen hundred and sixty-two, in said county of Dodge and State of Wisconsin.


*Approved, March 2, 1881.*

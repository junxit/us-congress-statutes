# Chapter 143

> An Act granting a pension to Rachael J. Reber.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension- roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Rachael J. Reber, widow of Eflenger L. Reber, first lieutenant and adjutant of the Two hundred and fifth Regiment of Pennsylvania Volunteers, and pay her a pension for herself and minor children from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 8, 1880.*

# Chapter 11

> An Act for the relief of Thomas Langton, of Dundee, Michigan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United, States of America in Congress assembled,*

That fifty-two acres of the southeast fractional quarter of the northeast fractional quarter of section twenty-three in township six south, of range six east, in the county of Monroe, State of Michigan, belonging to Thomas Langton lie discharged of and from all lien, claim, or title, on the part of t he United States thereon and thereto, under and by virtue of the levy of a succession tax upon the estate of Alfred Potter, deceased, and the sale of the above described land to the United States for said tax: Provided, That one-fourth of the amount of the tax assessed against said land upon the nonpayment of which said land was sold, and interest thereon at six per centum per annum from the day of the sale, be paid into the Treasury of the United States within ninety days from the passage of this act.


*Approved, January 23, 1880.*

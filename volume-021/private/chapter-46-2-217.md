# Chapter 217

> An Act to remove the political disabilities of John Owins of Portsmouth, Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States on John Owins of Virginia, be and the same are hereby removed.


*Approved, June 14, 1880.*

# Chapter 196

> An Act for the relief of William Redus.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

CHAP. 196.—An act fur the relief of William Rcdus.

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to pay to William Redits the sum of three thousand and six hundred dollars, out of any money belonging to the nation of Osage Indians not otherwise appropriated, in payment in full for one hundred and forty four beeves taken from him by said Indians on the twenty-eighth day of June, eighteen hundred and seventy two: Provided, That said sum shall betaken in full satisfaction of all claims on said Indians on account of the beeves so taken.


*Approved, March 3, 1881.*

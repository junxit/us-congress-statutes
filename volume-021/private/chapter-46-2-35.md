# Chapter 35

> An Act for the relief of the administrator of John D. McGill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of one hundred and two dollars, be, and the same is, hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to pay to the administrator of John D. McGill, for advertising the sale of the property known as the Philadelphia navy-yard, in the Georgetown Courier in November, eighteen hundred and seventy-five.


*Approved, March 5, 1880.*

# Chapter 29

> An Act to remove the political disabilities of Arthur P. Bagby, of Texas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That Arthur P. Bagby, of the State of Texas, be, and he is hereby, relieved of all political disabilities imposed upon him by the third section of the fourteenth article of the amendments to the Constitution of the United States.


*Approved, June 18, 1879.*

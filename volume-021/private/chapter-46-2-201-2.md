# Chapter 201

> An Act restoring the name of Andrew J. Pennel to the pension-roll

*Be it enacted by the Senate and, House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

CHAP. 201.—An act restoring the name of Andrew J. Fennel to the pension-roll

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to restore to the pension-roll the name of Andrew J. Fennel, late of Company F, Fifty seventh Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers; and he shall be paid, on his pension certificate numbered fifty-eight thousand seven hundred and twenty two, the original amount allowed him from the date his name was dropped from said roll.


*Approved, March 3, 1881.*

# Chapter 66

> An Act granting a pension to Jacob H. Eppler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension- roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jacob H. Eppler, late of Company B, Eleventh Regiment Indiana Volunteers.


*Approved, February 21, 1881.*

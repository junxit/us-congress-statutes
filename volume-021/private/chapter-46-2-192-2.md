# Chapter 192

> An Act granting a pension to Peter Grattan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

CHAP. 192.—An act granting a pension to Peter Grattan.

That the Secretary oi the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Peter Grattan, father of John L. Grattan, late second lieutenant of Company G, Sixth Regiment United States Infantry.


*Approved, March 3, 1881.*

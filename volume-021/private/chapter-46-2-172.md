# Chapter 172

> An Act granting a pension to Herman Netterfield.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension- roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Herman Netterfield, lato of Canby’s Scouts, and to pay him a pension as an enlisted private.


*Approved, June 9, 1880.*

# Chapter 104

> An Act for the relief of the estate of J. M. Micou, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

CHAP. 104.—An act for the relief of the estate of J. M. Micou, deceased.

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and hereby is, directed to pay the sum of six hundred and eighty- five dollars and sixty-seven cents, without interest, to the estate of J. M. Micou, deceased, for rent of storehouse in Montgomery, Alabama, from August first, eighteen hundred and sixty-five, to April second, eighteen hundred and sixty-six, used for storing quartermaster’s stores, under contract with said Micou, at a monthly rental of eighty-five dollars per month; and said sum of six hundred and eighty-five dollars and sixty-seven cents is hereby appropriated to pay the same, upon the presentation of proper evidence of the qualification of a legal representative of his estate.


*Approved, March 1, 1881.*

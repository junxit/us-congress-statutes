# Chapter 269

> An Act to reinstate R. W. Barkley as cadet-midshipman in the United States Naval Academy at Annapolis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized to reinstate as cadet-midshipman in the United States Naval Academy at Annapolis, Maryland, R. W. Barkley, of Chillicothe, county of Livingston and State of Missouri, if in his judgment such reinstatement is proper to fill the vacancy now existing in said institution from the tenth Congressional district of the said State of Missouri: And provided, That said Barkley is designated and appointed for such purpose by the Representative in Congress from said district.


*Approved, June 16, 1880.*

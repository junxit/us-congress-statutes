# Chapter 119

> An Act for the relief of Louis P. Di Cesnola, late consul at Cyprus.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Rouse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

CHAP. 119.—An act for the relief of Louis P. Di Cesnola, late consul at Cyprus.

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to pay to Louis P. Di Cesnola the sum of five thousand five hundred dollars, the amount paid by him for the official expenses of his consulate while consul at Cyprus; which said sum is hereby appropriated out of any moneys in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 2, 1881.*

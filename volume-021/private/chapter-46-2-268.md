# Chapter 268

> An Act to correct the military record of Byron Rosecrans.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the military record of Byron Rosecrans, late of Company H, Twenty-ninth Michigan Volunteer Infantry, be corrected by removing the charge of desertion against him, and that he be granted an honorable discharge as of the date of the mustering out of said regiment.


*Approved, June 16, 1880.*

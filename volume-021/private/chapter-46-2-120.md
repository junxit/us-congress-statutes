# Chapter 120

> An Act for the relief of Charles Clinton, of New Orleans, late assistant treasurer at New Orleans.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

CHAP. 120.—An net for the relief of Charles Clinton, of New Orleans, late assistant treasurer at New Orleans.

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Charles Clinton, late assistant United States treasurer at New Orleans, Louisiana, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated the sum of five thousand eight hundred and fifty dollars; being the amount of public money stolen from his office on the first day of May, eighteen hundred and seventy-one, and refunded by him to the Government of the United States.


*Approved, March 2, 1881.*

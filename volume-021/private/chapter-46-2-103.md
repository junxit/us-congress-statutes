# Chapter 103

> An Act granting an increase of pension to William Hamill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

CHAP. 103.—An act grunting an increase of pension to William Hamill,

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension- roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William Hamill, late sergeant Company E, One hundred and seventeenth Regiment Illinois Volunteers, at the rate of eighteen dollars per month from and after the passage of this act, which pension shall be in lieu of that now received by him.


*Approved, March 1, 1881.*

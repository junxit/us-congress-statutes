# Chapter 141

> An Act to remove the charge of desertion against Enoch Davis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the charge of desertion now standing against Enoch Davis, late a private of Company G, Sixth Iowa Volunteer Infantry, on the rolls filed in the office of the Adjutant-General be removed, and that the Adjutant-General aforesaid be, and he is hereby, directed to grant said Davis an honorable discharge.


*Approved, June 8, 1880.*

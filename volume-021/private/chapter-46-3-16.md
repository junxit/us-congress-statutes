# Chapter 16

> An Act for the relief of Samuel I. Gustin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to S. 1. Gustin the sum of one thousand one hundred and twenty-nine dollars for supplies furnished by him under contract made with government officials to the Army of the United States.


*Approved, January 12, 1881.*

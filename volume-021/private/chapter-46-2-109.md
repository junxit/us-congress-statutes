# Chapter 109

> An Act granting a pension to Martha J. Robinson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place, on the pension-roll the name of Martha J. Robinson, widow of James II. Robinson, late private in Company E, Fifth Regiment Indiana Cavalry Volunteers, and to pay per a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, May 28, 1880.*

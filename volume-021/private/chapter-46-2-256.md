# Chapter 256

> An Act granting a pension to Thomas J. Jackson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized to place upon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the, name of Thomas J. Jackson, late lieutenant-colonel of the Eleventh United States Colored Infantry.


*Approved, June 16, 1880.*

# Chapter 186

> An Act for the relief of the sureties of Franklin Travis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

CHAP. 186.—An act for the relief of the sureties of Franklin Travis.

That Joseph W. McCall, Young W. Allen, Wilburn H. Graves, Eli T. McGill, Priestley E. Parker, Henry McCall, and Jasper Ballew be, and they are hereby, released from all liability as sureties of the said Franklin Travis as collector of internal revenue, whether the said liability may have been fixed by judgment or be upon said bond executed by them on the ninth day of May, eighteen hundred and sixty-seven.


*Approved, March 3, 1881.*

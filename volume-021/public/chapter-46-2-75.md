# Chapter 75

> An act to change the name of the steam pleasure-yacht W. J. Gordon to Salmo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of the steam pleasure-yacht W. J. Gordon, of Marquette, Michigan, be, and is hereby, changed to Salino, by which name said steam pleasure-yacht shall be hereafter documented and known.


*Approved, May 3, 1880.*

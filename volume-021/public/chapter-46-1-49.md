# Chapter 49

> An Act changing the time of holding the November term of the United States district court in the district of Connecticut.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the term of the United States district court for the district of Connecticut, at Hartford, now held on the fourth Tuesday in November, shall hereafter be held on the first Tuesday of December.


*Approved, June 30, 1879.*

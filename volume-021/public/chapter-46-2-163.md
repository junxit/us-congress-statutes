# Chapter 163

> An act confirming the title to block numbered fourteen, in Baker City, Oregon, to Baker County.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the title to so much of block fourteen, in Baker City, Oregon, as the United States has title to, be, and the same is hereby, confirmed to the county of Baker, Oregon, and that a patent be issued to the said county therefor, without prejudice to the right of any adverse claimant to any part thereof.


*Approved, June 9, 1880.*

# Chapter 158

> An act to declare the true intent and meaning of “An act to reimburse the State of Kentucky for moneys expended for the United States in enrolling, subsisting, clothing, supplying, arming, equipping, paying and transporting militia forces to aid in suppressing the rebellion,” approved June eighth, eighteen hundred and seventy two.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in adjusting the claim of the State of Kentucky the term “equipment” shall be construed to mean and include the arming of said forces, as an essential part of their equipments, and the word “ supplying ” shall include forage and fuel, and the transportation shall be embraced, as indicated in the title of the act, it being the intention of Congress in the passage of said act to settle and adjust said accounts.


*Approved, March 3, 1881.*

# Chapter 46

> An Act for the relief of Somerville Nicholson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be, and is hereby, authorized to restore Somerville Nicholson, now a captain on the retired list of the Navy, to the active list, to take rank next after Clark II. Welles: Provided, That no claim for arrearages of pay shall accrue to said Nicholson by reason of restoration under the provisions of this act.


*Approved, February 15, 1881.*

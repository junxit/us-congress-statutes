# Chapter 188

> An act to change the name of the schooner “J. H. Dusenberry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of the schooner J. H. Dusenberry” be changed to that of the “Cordorus”.


*Approved, June 10, 1880.*

# Chapter 91

> An Act making the city of Chattanooga, in the State of Tennessee, a port of delivery.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the city of Chattanooga, in the State of Tennessee, is hereby made a port of delivery, with a surveyor at a salary of three hundred and fifty dollars per annum and the customary fees. And the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby directed to carry this act into effect.


*Approved, February 28, 1881.*

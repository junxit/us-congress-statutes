# Chapter 7

> An Act to authorize the employment of three additional assistants in the Library of Congress.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Librarian of Congress be authorized to employ three additional assistants, at a yearly compensation of twelve hundred dollars each; and the sum necessary is hereby appropriated out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, May 14, 1879.*

# Chapter 241

> An Act making an appropriation for the erection of a naval wharf at Key West in tile State of Florida.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of thirty thousand dollars be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to be expended, under the direction of the Secretary of the Navy, for the erection of a naval wharf at Key West in the State of Florida.


*Approved, June 16, 1880.*

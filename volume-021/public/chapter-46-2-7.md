# Chapter 7

> An Act amending section eighteen hundred and fifty-two of the Revised Statutes of the United States

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section eighteen hundred and fifty-two be, and the same hereby is, so amended as to read as follows:

> ## § 1852.
>
> The sessions of the legislative assemblies of the several Territories of the United States shall be limited to sixty days’duration.”


*Approved, December 23, 1880.*

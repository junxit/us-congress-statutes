# Chapter 51

> An Act to amend section one, page two hundred and thirty-four, volume twenty of the United States Statutes at Large, Forty-fifth Congress.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That so much of section one. page two hundred and thirty-four of the twentieth volume of the United States Statutes-at-Large, passed at the Forty-fifth Congress and approved June twentieth, eighteen hundred and seventy-eight, as relates to the steamer “B. P. Cheney” be and the same is hereby repealed.


*Approved, June 30, 1879.*

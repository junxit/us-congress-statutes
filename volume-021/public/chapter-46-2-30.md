# Chapter 30

> An Act authorizing the Secretary of the Treasury to appoint a deputy-collector at Lake Charles, Louisiana.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to appoint a deputy-collector at Lake Charles, Calcasieu Parish, State of Louisiana, who shall have power to enter and clear all vessels coming to that port.


*Approved, February 24, 1880.*

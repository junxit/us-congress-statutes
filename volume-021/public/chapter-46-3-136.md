## § 2.

That the joint resolution approved July twentieth, eighteen hundred and sixty-eight, authorizing the construction of the bridge over the Mississippi River “to connect the island of Rock Island with the cities of Davenport and Rock Island”, is hereby so amended as to require the Chicago, Rock Island and Pacific Railroad Company to charge for any cars carrying freight other than those passing over its own road, and for its own cars when carrying freight for other roads, which it may carry across said bridge, a sum not exceeding three dollars for every loaded car, two-fifths of which sums shall be paid by said railroad Company into the Treasury of the United States each month; and the monthly returns thus made shall be in such form, and with such authentication, as the Secretary of War shall direct.

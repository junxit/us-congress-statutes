# Chapter 141

> An act to amend the act entitled “An act to encourage the establishment of public marine schools”, approved June twentieth, eighteen hundred and seventy-four, so as to extend it to the ports of Wilmington, Charleston, Savannah, Mobile, New Orleans, Baton Rouge, Galveston, and in Narragansett Bay.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the act entitled “An act to encourage the establishment of public marine schools”, approved June twentieth, eighteen hundred and seventy-four, be, and the same is, amended so that it shall extend to the ports of Wilmington, Charleston, Savannah, Mobile, New Orleans, Baton Rouge, Galveston, and in Narragansett Bay.


*Approved, March 3, 1881.*

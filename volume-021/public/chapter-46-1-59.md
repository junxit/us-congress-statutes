# Chapter 59

> An Act to prescribe the times for holding the circuit and district courts of the United States in the district of Kentucky.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the regular terms of the circuit and district courts of the United States in the district of Ken- tucky shall be held at the times and places following, to wit: At Coving-ton, on the second Monday in May and the first Monday in December; at Louisville, on the third Monday in February and the first Monday in October; at Frankfort, on the first Monday in January and the second Monday in June; and at Paducah, on the first Monday in April and the third Monday in November.

## § 2.

The terms of said courts shall not be limited to any particular number of days, nor shall it be necessary to adjourn by reason of the intervention of term elsewhere; but the court intervening maybe adjourned until the business of the court in session is concluded.

## § 3.

Nothing herein contained shall be construed to repeal section five hundred and seventy-eight of the Revised Statutes.


*Approved, July 1, 1879.*

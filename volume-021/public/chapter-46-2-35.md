# Chapter 35

> An Act to provide for the furnishing of certain public documents to Soldiers’ Homes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section forty-eight hundred and thirty-seven of the Revised Statutes of the United States be, and the same is hereby, repealed and reenacted to read as follows: “The Secretary of the Senate and the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall cause to be sent to the National Home for Disabled Volunteer Soldiers at Dayton, in Ohio, and to the branches at Augusta, in Maine, Milwaukee, in Wisconsin, Hampton in Virginia, and the Soldiers’ Home at Knightstown Springs, near Knightstown, in Indiana, each, one copy of each of the following documents: The journals of each House of Congress at each and every session; all laws of Congress; the annual messages of the President, with accompanying documents; the daily Congressional Record, and all other documents or books which may be printed and bound by order of either House of Congress; and the Public Printer is hereby authorized and directed to furnish to the Secretary of the Senate and the Clerk of the House of Representatives the documents referred to in this section.”


*Approved, February 8, 1881.*

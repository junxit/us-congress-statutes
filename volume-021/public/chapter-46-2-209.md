# Chapter 209

> An Act to change the name of the sloop-yacht “Mariah”, of Rochester, New York, to that of “Tourist”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of the sloop-yacht “Mariah”, of Rochester, county of Monroe, and State of New-York, be changed to that of “Tourist”, of Rochester, and State of New York.


*Approved, June 14, 1880.*

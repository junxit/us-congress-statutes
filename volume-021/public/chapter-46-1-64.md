# Chapter 64

> An Act to put salts of quinine and sulphate of quinine on the free list.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the passage of this act the importation of salts of quinine and sulphate of quinine shall be exempt from customs duties; and all laws inconsistent herewith are hereby repealed.


*Approved, July 1, 1879.*

# Chapter 208

> An Act to change the name of the steam-yacht “E. R. Bryant”, of Rochester to “Summerland”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of the steam-yacht “E. R. Bryant”, of Rochester, county of Monroe, and State of Newr York, be changed to that of “Summerland”, of Rochester, and State of New York.


*Approved, June 14, 1880.*

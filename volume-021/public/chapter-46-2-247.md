# Chapter 247

> An Act in relation to the mileage of jurors and witnesses in the State of Colorado.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That jurors and witnesses in the district and circuit courts of the United States in and for the State of Colorado, shall be entitled to receive fifteen cents for each mile actually traveled in coming to or returning from said courts.


*Approved, June 16, 1880.*

# Chapter 47

> An Act to change the name of the ferry-boat James Fisk Jr. to Passaic

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of the Ferryboat James Fisk Jr. of New York, be, and is hereby, changed to Passaic, by which name said Ferryboat shall be hereafter licensed and known.


*Approved, June 30, 1879.*

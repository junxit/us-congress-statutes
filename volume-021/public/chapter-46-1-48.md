# Chapter 48

> An Act to change the name of the steam-propeller “Nuhpa” to “Metropolitan”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of the steam- propeller “Nuhpa” of New York, be, and is hereby, changed to “Metropolitan”, by which name said steam-propeller shall be hereafter licensed and known.


*Approved, June 30, 1879.*

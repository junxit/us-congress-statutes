# Chapter 103

> An act authorizing the changing the name of the schooner Rebecca D.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to allow the owner of the schooner Rebecca D, a vessel of American ownership and license, to change her name, and that said vessel be hereafter known as the Win. H. Barnes.


*Approved, May 26, 1880.*

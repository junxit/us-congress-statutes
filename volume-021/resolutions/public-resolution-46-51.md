# Public Resolution 51

> Joint resolution providing for the printing of the Reports of the Commissioner of Education for the years eighteen hundred and seventy-eight and eighteen hundred and seventy-nine.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That of each of the Reports of the Commissioner of Education for the respective years eighteen hundred and seventy-eight and eighteen hundred and seventy-nine, there be printed four thousand copies for the use of the Senate; eight thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives and thirteen thousand copies for distribution by the Commissioner.


*Approved, June 11, 1880.*

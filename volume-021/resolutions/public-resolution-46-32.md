# Public Resolution 32

> Joint resolution making appropriation for the contingent fund of the Senate.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of fifteen thousand dollars be and the same hereby is, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for contingent expenses of the Senate of the United States, tor the fiscal year ending June the thirtieth, eighteen hundred and eighty, to be credited to “Miscellaneous Items, exclusive of labor”.


*Approved, May 14, 1880.*

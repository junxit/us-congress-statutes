# Public Resolution 30

> Joint resolution relating to salaries of government employees in the District of Columbia.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all employees of the government in the city of Washington, Distinct of Columbia, shall be paid for the fourth day of March (inauguration Day) and the thirtieth day of May (Decoration Day), eighteen hundred and eighty-one, as for other days on which they perform labor.


*Approved, March 3, 1881.*

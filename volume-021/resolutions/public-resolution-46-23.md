# Public Resolution 23

> Joint resolution authorizing the printing and binding of additional copies of the Report of the Chief Signal Officer of the Army.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Public Printer be, and he is hereby, authorized to print and bind, for the use of the Signal Office, five thousand additional copies of the Annual Report of the Chief Signal Officer for the year eighteen hundred and seventy-nine; and the Public Printer is authorized to contract for the illustrations with the person now furnishing the illustrations for the Congressional edition


*Approved, April 22, 1880.*

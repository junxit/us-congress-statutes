# Public Resolution 9

> Joint Resolution accepting from Professor Edward Fontaine, of Louisiana, certain maps, drawings, and explanations of the same.

Whereas Professor Edward Fontaine has exhibited certain maps and drawings, with full explanations of the same, of his improved methods of hydraulic engineering and controlling water-currents, which are believed to lie valuable, which he proposes to present to the Government of the United States, on condition that they be printed: Therefore, Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

That the maps and drawings, with the explanations thereof, of Professor Edward Fontaine’s improved methods of hydraulic engineering and controlling water-currents, which Professor Edward Fontaine proposes to give to the Government of the United States, on condition that they be printed, be, and the same hereby are, accepted.


*Approved, June 19, 1879.*

# Public Resolution 18

> Joint Resolution donating granite blocks to Mower Post Grand Army of the Republic of New Orleans Louisiana.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be and he is hereby authorized and directed to donate to Mower Post Grand Arriy of the Republic at New Orleans certain blocks of granite now lying at Fort Livingston Louisiana, and which are of no use to the government, for the purpose of completing a monument to the union dead at the Chalmette National Cemetery near New Orleans.


*Approved, June 28, 1879.*

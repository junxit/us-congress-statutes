# Public Resolution 14

> Joint Resolution authorizing the appointment of a commission to lease a building for a city postoffice in the city of Washington, District of Columbia.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster-General, the chairman of the Senate and House Committees on Public Buildings and Grounds are hereby constituted a commission with authority to lease such building in the city of Washington, District of Columbia for the purpose of a city post-office, as in their judgment the good of the public service may require: Provided, That said lease shall be for a term of not less than three nor exceeding live years and at an annual rental not to exceed live thousand dollars per annum.


*Approved, June 27, 1879.*

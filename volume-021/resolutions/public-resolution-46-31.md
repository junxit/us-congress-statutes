# Public Resolution 31

> Joint resolution authorizing the Public Printer to print additional copies of bills and other public documents.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Public Printer be and he is hereby directed to furnish to all applicants copies of bills and reports and other public documents hereafter printed by order of Congress and distributed from the Document Rooms of the Senate and House on said applicants paying the cost of such printing with ten per centum added; and giving the notice required by section thirty-eight hundred and nine of Title forty-five of the Revised Statutes.


*Approved, May 8, 1880.*

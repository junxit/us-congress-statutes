# Public Resolution 30

> Joint resolution authorizing the Secretary of War to send rations to the sufferers from the recent cyclone at Macon, Mississippi.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War is hereby authorized and empowered to send four thousand rations to Macon, Mississippi, for the use of the sufferers made destitute by the cyclone of April twenty-fifth.


*Approved, May 4, 1880.*

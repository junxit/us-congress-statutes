# Private Law 92–98

> AN ACT For the relief of John W. Shafer, Junior.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, on such terms as it deems just, the United States Postal Service is authorized to compromise, release, or discharge in whole or in part the joint and several liability of John W. Shafer, Junior, and John F. Schumacher, employees at the Albany, New York, Post Office, for a deficiency in the amount of $3,150 in the stamp stock of John IV. Shafer, Junior.


*Approved June 30, 1972.*

# Private Law 92–69

> AN ACT For the relief of William Lucas (also known as Vanilles Loukatis).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, William Lucas (also known as Vasilios Loukatis) may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of the Act, upon approval of a petition filed in his behalf by Mr. and Mrs. George Lucas, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of the Act: Provided, That the natural parents, or brothers, or sisters of the beneficiary shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved March 6, 1972.*

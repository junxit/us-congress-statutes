# Private Law 92–152

> AN ACT For the relief of Gerald Vincent Bull.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the periods of time Gerald Vincent Bull has resided in the United States shall be held and considered to meet the residence and physical presence requirements of section 316 of the said Act.


*Approved October 21, 1972.*

# Private Law 92–138

> AN ACT For the relief of Sara B. Garner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That on such terms as it deems just, the United States Postal Service is authorized to com-promise, release, or discharge in whole or in part the liability of Sara B. Garner, Postmaster at the Ryland, Alabama, post office, for the loss resulting from a burglary on February 7, 1971, at that post office.


*Approved October 6, 1972.*

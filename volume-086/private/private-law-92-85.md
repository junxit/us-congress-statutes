# Private Law 92–85

> AN ACT For the relief of Antonio Plameras.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Antonio Plame has may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101 (b)(1)(F) of such Act, upon the filing of a petition in his behalf by Dorothy Person, a citizen of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of such Act. The natural brothers or sisters of the said Antonio Plameras shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved May 11, 1972.*

# Private Law 92–91

> AN ACT For the relief of Luz Marin Cruz Aleman Phillips.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Aet, Luz Maria Cruz Aleman Phillips shall be held and considered to have, been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of time date of the enactment of this Act, upon payment of the required visa fee.


*Approved June 30, 1972.*

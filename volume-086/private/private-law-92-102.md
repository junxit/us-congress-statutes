# Private Law 92–102

> AN ACT For the relief of Amparo Coronado Vieuda de Pena and her three minor children: Yolanda Pena, Marfaela Pena, and Lorenzo Pena.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 101(a)(27)(B) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Amparo Coronado Vieuda de Pena, Yolanda Pena, Marisela Pena, and Lorenzo Pena shall be held and considered to be returning resident aliens.


*Approved July 7, 1972.*

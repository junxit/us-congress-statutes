# Private Law 92–156

> AN ACT To provide for the free entry of a four octave carillon for the use of Marquette University, Milwaukee, Wisconsin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to admit free of duty a four octave carillon for the use of Marquette University, Milwaukee, Wisconsin.

## § 2.

If the liquidation of the entry of the article described in the first section of this Act has become final, such entry shall be reliquidated and the appropriate refund of duty shall be made.


*Approved October 27, 1972.*

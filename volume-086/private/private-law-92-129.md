# Private Law 92–129

> AN ACT For the relief of William John West.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of section 101(b)(1) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, William John West shall be deemed to be the natural-born alien child of Staff Sergeant William K. West, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved September 29, 1972.*

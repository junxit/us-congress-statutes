# Private Law 92–147

> AN ACT For the relief of certain postal employees at the Elmhurst, Illinois, Post Office.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, on such terms as he deems just, the Postmaster General is hereby authorized to compromise, release, or discharge in whole or in part, the individual liabilities of Louis H. Linneweh, Howard I). Slavik, Edith J. Fainter, William W. Higgins, Thomas Newett, Koger Olson, and the estate of Joseph J. Holloway, employees at the Elmhurst, Illinois, Post Office, to the United States for the loss resulting from the burglary at the post office on February 21, 1965.


*Approved October 21, 1972.*

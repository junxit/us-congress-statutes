# Private Law 92–83

> AN ACT For the relief of Luis Gnerrero-Chaves Guadalupe Guerrero-Chaves, and Alfredo Guerrero-Chavez.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Luis Guerrero-Chavez, Guadalupe Guerrero-Chavez, and Alfredo Guerrero-Chavez shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of May 20, 1958, and the periods of time each of them has resided in the United States since that date shall be held and considered to meet the residence and physical requirements of section 316 of such Act.


*Approved May 11, 1972.*

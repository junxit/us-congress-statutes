# Private Law 92–68

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Hind Nicholas Chaber, Georgette Hanna Chaber, Jeanette Hanna Chaber. and Violette Hanna Chaber.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Hind Nicholas Chaber, Georgette Hanna Chaber, Jeanette Hanna Chaber, and Violette Hanna Chaber, the widow and children of an alien lawfully admitted for permanent residence, shall be held and considered to be within the purview of section 203(a)(2) of that Act and the provisions of section 204 of such Act shall not be applicable in this case.


*Approved March 6, 1972.*

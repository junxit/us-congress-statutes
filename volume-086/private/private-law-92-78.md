# Private Law 92–78

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Norma McLeish.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Norma McLeish, the widow of a United States citizen, shall be held and considered to be within the purview of section 201 (b) of that Act and the provisions of section 204 of the Act shall be inapplicable in her case.


*Approved March 9, 1972.*

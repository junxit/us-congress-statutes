# Private Law 92–75

> AN ACT For the relief of Lloyd B. Earle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding laches or any statute of limitations, the United States Army Chums Service is authorized and directed to receive, consider, adjudicate, and, if found meritorious, to pay the claim of Lloyd B. Earle, of Chester Depot, Vermont, against the United States arising out of an automobile accident which occurred on or about February 20, 1967 in Cuttingsville, Vermont, involving a vehicle operated by the said Lloyd B. Earle and another vehicle operated by a member of the United States Army.


*Approved March 9, 1972.*

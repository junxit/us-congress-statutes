# Senate Concurrent Resolution 78

> HARRY S TRUMAN—EIGHTY-EIGHTH BIRTHDAY

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Congress of the United States hereby extends to the Honorable Harry S Truman, thirty-third President of the United States, its greetings and warm wishes on the occasion of Ins eighty-eighth birthday on May 8, 1972.

## § 2.

The Congress expresses its admiration and gratitude to President Truman for his many years of distinguished service to the people of the United States and to the world community.

## § 3.

The Congress expresses its appreciation to President Truman for his contributions as Senator, Vice President, President, author, and statesman.

## § 4.

A copy of this concurrent resolution shall be promptly transmitted to the distinguished “Man from Independence”, Harry S Truman.


*Agreed to April 20, 1972.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 554

> CORRECTION OF ENROLLED BILL H. R. 1746

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Clerk of the House of Representatives, in the enrollment of the bill (H.R. 1746) to further promote equal employment opportunities for American workers, is authorized and directed to make the following change: In paragraph (a) of Section 4, strike out “Sec. 705.” and insert in lieu thereof “Sec. 706.”


*Passed March 9, 1972.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 59

> “THE ELECTORAL COMMISSION OF 1877”—PAINTING

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Architect of the Capitol shall, on behalf of the Congress, lend the Cornelia F asset painting, “The Electoral Commission of 1877”, located in the Capitol, to the Smithsonian Institution for the National Portrait Gallery exhibit of political memorabilia relating to elections from 1796 to 1968. Such loan shall be made so that the portrait shall be available for display by the National Portrait Gallery not later than May 2, 1972, and under procedures that will assure its proper preservation, display, and return to the Capitol as soon as practicable after September 5, 1972.


*Agreed to May 1, 1972.*

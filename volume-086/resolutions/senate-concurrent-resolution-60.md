# Senate Concurrent Resolution 60

> “ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ACT OF 1971”—HEARINGS

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be planted for the use of the Senate Committee on Commerce two thousand additional copies of part 1 of the hearings entitled “Environmental Protection Act of 1971” held before the Senate Committee on Commerce.


*Passed June 30, 1972.*

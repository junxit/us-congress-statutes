# House Concurrent Resolution 726

> ADJOURNMENT SINE DIE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Wednesday, October 18, 1972, and that when they adjourn on said day, they stand adjourned sine die.


*Passed October 18, 1972.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 67

> CORRECTION OF ENROLLED BILL 8. 888

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Secretary of the Senate, in the enrollment of the bill (S. 888), providing for the relief of David J. Crumb, is hereby authorized and directed to make the following correction: Strike out “5742(a)” and insert “5724a”.


*Agreed to March 14, 1972.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 88

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Friday, June 30, 1972, they stand adjourned until 12 o’clock noon on Monday, July 17, 1972.


*Agreed to June 30, 1972.*

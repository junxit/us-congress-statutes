# House Concurrent Resolution 600

> J. EDGAR HOOVER—LIE IN STATE IN CAPITOL ROTUNDA

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That it is the sense of Congress that, the body of J, Edgar Hoover should lie in state in the Rotunda of the United States Capitol so that the citizens of the United States may pay their last respects to this great American.


*Passed May 2, 1972.*

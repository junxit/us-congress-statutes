# House Concurrent Resolution 710

> AMERICAN PUBLIC HEALTH ASSOCIATION—ONE HUNDREDTH ANNIVERSARY

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Congress extends its greetings and felicitations to the American Public Health Association on the occasion of the one hundredth anniversary of its founding.


*Passed October 14, 1972.*

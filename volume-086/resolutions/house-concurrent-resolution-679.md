# House Concurrent Resolution 679

> DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA GOVERNMENT ORGANIZATION—HOUSE REPORT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed in addition to the original press run two thousand and five hundred copies each of volume I, and one thousand five hundred copies each of volumes II and III of the House document containing the report of the Commission on the Organization of the Government of the District of Columbia, for the use of the House Committee on the District of Columbia.


*Passed October 6, 1972.*

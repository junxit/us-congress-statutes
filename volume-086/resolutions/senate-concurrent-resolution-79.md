# Senate Concurrent Resolution 79

> AMPHETAMINE LEGISLATION—HEARINGS

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there lie printed for the use of the Senate. Committee on the Judiciary five, thousand additional copies of the hearings of its Subcommittee To Investigate Juvenile Delinquency during the Ninety-second Congress, first session, on S. 674, to amend the Controlled Substances Act to move amphetamines and certain other stimulant substances from schedule III of such Act to schedule II, and for other purposes.


*Agreed to June 30, 1972.*

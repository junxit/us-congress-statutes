# House Concurrent Resolution 681

> “CORRECTIONS”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed one thousand copies each of parts I through VI of serial 15 hearings entitled “Corrections”, Ninety-second Congress, for use of the House Committee on the Judiciary.


*Passed October 6, 1972.*

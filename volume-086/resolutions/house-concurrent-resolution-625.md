# House Concurrent Resolution 625

> JOINT MEETING

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on June 1, 1972, at 9:30 p.m., for the purpose of receiving such communication as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed May 31, 1972.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 70

> CONSUMER PRODUCT SAFETY ACT OF 1971—HEARINGS

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on Commerce two thousand additional copies each of parts 1 and 2 of its hearings during the first session, Ninety-second Congress, on the Consumer Product Safety Act of 1971.


*Passed June 30, 1972.*

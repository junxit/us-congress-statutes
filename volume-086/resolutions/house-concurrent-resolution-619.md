# House Concurrent Resolution 619

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Wednesday, May 24, 1972, and that when the Senate, adjourns on Thursday, May 25, 1972, they stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Tuesday, May 30, 1972.


*Passed May 24, 1972.*

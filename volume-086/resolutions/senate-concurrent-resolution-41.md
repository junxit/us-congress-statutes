# Senate Concurrent Resolution 41

> CONVENTION OF AMERICAN INSTRUCTORS OF THE DEAF

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the report of the proceedings of the forty-fifth biennial meeting of the Convention of American Instructors of the Deaf, held in Little Rock, Arkansas, from June 25, 1971, through July 2, 1971, be printed with illustrations as a Senate document. Five thousand five hundred additional copies of such document shall be printed and bound for the use of the Joint Committee on Printing.


*Agreed to May 2, 1972.*

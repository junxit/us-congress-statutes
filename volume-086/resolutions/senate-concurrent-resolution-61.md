# Senate Concurrent Resolution 61

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That, when the two Houses adjourn on Wednesday, February 9, 1972, the Senate stands adjourned until 12 o’clock noon on Monday, February 14, 1972, and the House of Representatives until 12 o’clock meridian on Wednesday, February 16, 1972.


*Passed February 9, 1972.*

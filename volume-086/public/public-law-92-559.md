# Public Law 92–559

> AN ACT To amend title 10, United States Code, to establish the authorized strength of the Naval Reserve in officers in the Judge Advocate General’s Corps In the grade of rear admiral, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** clause (3) of section 5457(a) of title 10, United States Code, is amended to read as follows:
  > - **(3)** Supply Corps—7.”, .

- **(b)** Section 5457(a) of such title is further amended by redesignating clause (6) as clause (7) and adding after clause (5) a new clause (6) as follows:
  > - **(6)** Judge Advocate General’s Corps—1.”.


*Approved October 25, 1972.*

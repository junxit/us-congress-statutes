# Public Law 92–376

> AN ACT To amend section 122 of title 38 of the United States Code to transfer certain counties of the central division of the judicial district of South Dakota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Rouse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** the first sentence of paragraph (2) of section 122 of title 28 of the United States Code is amended by striking out “Gregory,”.
- **(b)** The first sentence of paragraph (3) of such section 122 is amended to read as follows: “The central division comprises the counties of Buffalo, Dewey, Faulk, Gregory, Haakon, Hand, Hughes, Hyde, Jackson, Jerauld, Jones, Lyman, Mellette, Potter, Stanley, Sully, Todd, Tripp, and Ziebach.”
- **(c)** The first sentence of paragraph (4) of such section 122 is amended by striking out: (1) “Mellette,”: (2) “Todd,”; and (3) “Tripp,”.


*Approved August 10, 1972.*

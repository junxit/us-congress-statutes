# Public Law 92–262

> AN ACT To continue until the close of September 30, 1973, the International Coffee Agreement Act of 1968.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 302 of the International Coffee Agreement Act of 1968 (19 U.S.C. 1356f) is amended by striking out “July 1, 1971” and inserting in lieu thereof “October 1, 1973”.


*Approved March 24, 1972.*

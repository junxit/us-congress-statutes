# Public Law 92–390

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making further continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 11173, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That clause (c) of section 102 of the joint resolution of July 1, 1972 (Public Law 92–334), is hereby amended by striking out “August 18, 1972” and inserting in lieu thereof “September 30, 1972 or the sine die adjournment of the second session of the Ninety-second Congress”.


*Approved August 18, 1972.*

# Public Law 92–594

> AN ACT To amend section 7 of the Fishermen’s Protective Act of 1967.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (e) of section 7 of the Fishermen’s Protective Act of 1967 (22 U.S.C. 1977 (e)), is amended to read as follows:

> - **(e)** The provisions of this section shall be effective until July 1,

## § 2.

Clause (1) of subsection (f) of section 7 of the Fishermen’s Protective Act of 1967 (22 U.S.C. 1977(f) (1)), is amended to read as follows:

> - **(1)** the term ‘Secretary’ means the Secretary of Commerce.”.


*Approved October 27, 1972.*

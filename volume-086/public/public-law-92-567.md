# Public Law 92–567

> AN ACT To amend the Act of August 16, 1971, which established the National Advisory Committee on Oceans and Atmosphere, to increase the appropriation authorization thereunder.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 7 of the Act of August 16, 1971 (Public Law 92–125; 85 Stat 344), is amended to read as follows: “There are hereby authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary of Commerce, for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1973, and for each of the two fiscal years immediately thereafter, such sums, not to exceed $400,000, as may be necessary for expenses incident to the administration of this Act, and for succeeding fiscal years only such sums as may be authorized by law.”.


*Approved October 25, 1972.*

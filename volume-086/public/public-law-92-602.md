# Public Law 92–602

> AN ACT To rename the Mineola Dam and Lake as the Carl L. Estes Dam and Lake.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Mineola Dam and Lake on the Sabine River, Texas, authorized by the Flood Control Act of 1970 shall be known and designated hereafter as the “Carl L. Estes Dam and Lake”. Any law, regulation, map, or record of the United States in which such dam and lake is referred shall be held and considered to refer to such dam and lake by the name of “Carl L. Estes Dam and Lake”.


*Approved October 27, 1972.*

# Public Law 92–232

> JOINT RESOLUTION Extending the date for transmission to the Congress of the report of the Joint Economic Committee.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the joint resolution entitled “Joint resolution extending the dates for transmission of the Economic Report and the report of the Joint Economic Committee”, approved December 22, 1971 (Public Law 92–216; 85 Stat. 778), is amended by striking out “March 10, 1972” and by inserting in lieu thereof “March 28, 1972”.


*Approved February 15, 1972.*

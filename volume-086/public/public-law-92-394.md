# Public Law 92–394

> AN ACT To further amend the United States Information and Educational Exchange Act of 1948.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 703 of the United States Information and Educational Exchange Act of 1948 is hereby amended to read as follows:

> ## § 703.
>
> There are authorized, to be appropriated to the Secretary of State $38,520,000 for fiscal year 1973 to provide grants, under such terms and conditions as the Secretary considers appropriate, to Radio Five Europe and Radio Liberty. Except for funds appropriated pursuant to this section, no funds appropriated after the date of this Act may be made available to or for the use of Radio Free Europe or Radio Liberty in fiscal year 1973.”.


*Approved August 20, 1972.*

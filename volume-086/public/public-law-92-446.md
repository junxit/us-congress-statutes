# Public Law 92–446

> JOINT RESOLUTION Milking further continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 1973, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That clause (c) of section 102 of the joint resolution of July 1, 1972 (Public Law 92–334), as amended, is hereby further amended by striking out “September 30, 1972” and inserting in lieu thereof “October 14, 1972”.


*Approved September 29, 1972.*

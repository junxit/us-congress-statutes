# Public Law 92–542

> AN ACT To authorize appropriations for fiscal year 1973 to carry out the Flammable Fabries Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 13 of the Flammable Fabrics Act (81 Stat. 573) is amended by striking out “1968, and” and inserting “1968,” in lieu thereof, and by inserting immediately after “June 30, 1970,” the following: “and $4,000,000 for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1973,”.


*Approved October 25, 1972.*

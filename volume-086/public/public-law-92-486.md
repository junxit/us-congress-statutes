# Public Law 92–486

> JOINT RESOLUTION Authorizing the President to proclaim October 30, 1972, as “National Sokol Day”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is hereby authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating October 30, 1972, as “National Sokol Day”, and calling upon the people of the United States to observe such day with appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Approved October 13, 1972.*

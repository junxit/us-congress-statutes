# Public Law 92–282

> JOINT RESOLUTION To pay tribute to law enforcement officers of this country on Law Day, May 1, 1972.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the celebration of Law Day, May 1, 1972, special emphasis be given by a grateful people to the law enforcement officers of the United States of America for their unflinching and devoted service in helping to preserve the domestic tranquillity and guaranteeing to the individual his rights under the law.


*Approved April 26, 1972.*

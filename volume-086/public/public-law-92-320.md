# Public Law 92–320

> AN ACT To a mend the Small Business Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph (4) of section 4(e) of the Small Business Act is amended—

- **(1)** by striking out “$3.100,000,(100” and inserting in lieu thereof “$4,300,000.000”;
- **(2)** by striking out “$450.1)00,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$500,000.000”: and
- **(3)** by striking out “$300.000,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$350,000.000”.

## § 2.

Section 402(a) of the Economic Opportunity Act of 1964 (42 U.S.C. 2902(a)) is amended by striking out “$05,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$50,000”.


*Approved June 27, 1972.*

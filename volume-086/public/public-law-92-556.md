# Public Law 92–556

> AN ACT To amend the Act of September 7, 1957, authorizing aircraft loan guarantees, in order to expand the program pursuant to such Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act entitled “An Act to provide for Government guaranty of private loans to certain air carriers for purchase of modern aircraft and equipment, to foster the development and use of modern transport aircraft by such carriers, and for other purposes”, approved September 7, 1957 (49 U.S.C. 1324 note), is amended—

- **(1)** in section 4(d) by striking out “$10,000,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$30,000,000”; and
- **(2)** in section 8 by striking out “fifteen” and inserting in lieu thereof “twenty”.


*Approved October 25, 1972.*

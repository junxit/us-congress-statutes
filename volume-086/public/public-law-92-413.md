# Public Law 92–413

> AN ACT To amend section 2735 of title 10, United States Code, to provide for the finality of settlement effected under section 2733, 2734, 2734a, 2734b, or 2737.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2735 of title 10, United States Code, is amended to read as follows:

> ## § 2735. Settlement: final and conclusive
>
> “Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the settlement of a claim under section 2733, 2734, 2734a, 2734b, or 2737 of this title is final and conclusive.”


*Approved August 29, 1972.*

# Public Law 92–533

> AN ACT To authorize additional funds for acquisition of interests in land within the area known as Piscataway Park In the State of Maryland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4 of the Act of October 4, 1961 (75 Stat. 780, 782), as amended (80 Stat. 319), is further amended by deleting “$4,132,000” and inserting “$5,657,000”.


*Approved October 23, 1972.*

# Public Law 92–250

> AN ACT To provide for a temporary increase in the public debt limit.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That during the period beginning on the date of the enactment of this Act and ending on June 30, 1972, the public debt limit set forth in the first sentence of section 21 of the Second Liberty Bond Act, as temporarily increased by section 2(a) of Public Law 92–5, shall be further temporarily increased by $20,000,000,000.


*Approved March 15, 1972.*

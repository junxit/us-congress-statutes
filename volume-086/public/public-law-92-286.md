# Public Law 92–286

> AN ACT To amend title 39, United States Code, to provide for the renewal of certain star route contracts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 5005 (b)(2) of title 39, United States Code, is amended by striking out “holder” and inserting in lieu thereof “contractor or subcontractor”.


*Approved May 1, 1972.*

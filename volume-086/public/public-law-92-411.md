# Public Law 92–411

> AN ACT To authorize appropriations for the fiscal year 1973 for the Corporation for Public Broadcasting and for making grants for construction of noncommercial educational television or radio broadcasting facilities.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** section 396(k) (1) of the Communications Act. of 1934 is amended to read as follows:
  > - **(k)**
  >   - **(1)** There is authorized to be appropriated for expenses of the Corporation for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1973, the sum of $40.000,000.”

- **(b)** Section 396(k) (2) of such Act is amended by striking out “1972” and inserting in lieu thereof “1973”.

## § 2.

Section 391 of the Communications Act of 1934 is amended to read as follows:

> ## § 391. “authorization of appropriations
>
> There are authorized to be appropriated for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1973, such sums, not to exceed $25,000,000 as may be necessary to carry out the purposes of section 390. Sums appropriated under this section shall remain available for payment of grants for projects for which applications, approved under section 392, have been submitted under such section prior to July 1, 1974.”


*Approved August 29, 1972.*

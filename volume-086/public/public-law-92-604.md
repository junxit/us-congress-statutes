# Public Law 92–604

> AN ACT To authorize appropriations to carry out Jellyfish control programs until the close of fiscal year 1977.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 3 of the Act entitled “An Act to provide for the control or elimination of jellyfish and other such pests in the coastal waters of the United States, and for other purposes”, approved November 2, 1966 (16 U.S.C. 1203), is amended by striking out “and” after “June 30, 1909,”; and by striking out the period at the end thereof and inserting in lieu thereof the following: “, and $400,000 for each of the fiscal years ending June 30, 1974, June 30, 1975. June 30, 1976, and June 30, 1977.”


*Approved October 31, 1972.*

# Public Law 92–292

> JOINT RESOLUTION To provide for the appointment of Robert Francis Goheen as citizen regent of the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Robert Francis Goheen, of Princeton, New Jersey, be appointed a member of the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution, of the class other than Members of Congress, for the statutory term of six years.


*Approved May 11, 1972.*

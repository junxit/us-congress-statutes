# Public Law 92–363

> JOINT RESOLUTION Authorizing the President to proclaim the third Sunday in October of 1972 as “National Shut-In Day”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is hereby authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating the third Sunday in October of 1972 as “National Shut-In Day” and calling upon the people of the United States to observe such day by visiting at least one shut-in person on this special day if possible, and by participating in other appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Approved August 7, 1972.*

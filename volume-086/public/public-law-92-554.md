# Public Law 92–554

> AN ACT To amend the Comprehensive Alcohol Abuse and Alcoholism Prevention, Treatment, anti Rehabilitation Act of 1970 to extend for one year the program of grants for State and local prevention, treatment, and rehabilitation programs for alcohol abuse and alcoholism.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 301 of the Comprehensive Alcohol Abuse and Alcoholism Prevention, Treatment, and Rehabilitation Act of 1970 is amended by striking out “for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1973” and inserting m lieu thereof “for each of the next two fiscal years”.


*Approved October 25, 1972.*

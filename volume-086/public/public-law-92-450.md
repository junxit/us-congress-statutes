# Public Law 92–450

> JOINT RESOLUTION Authorizing the President to proclaim October 1, W72, as “National Heritage Day”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Cong re## assembled,*

That the President is hereby authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating Sunday, October 1, 1972, as “National Heritage Day”, and calling upon the people of the United States, all of us immigrants, to observe such day with appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Approved September 30, 1972.*

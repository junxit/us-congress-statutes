# Public Law 92–452

> JOINT RESOLUTION To authorize the President to issue a proclamation designating the week in November of 1972 which includes Thanksgiving Day as “National Family Week”.

*Reserved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America of Congress assembled,*

That the President is hereby authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating the week beginning on the Sunday preceding the fourth Thursday in November of 1972 as “National Family Week” and inviting the Governors of the several States, the chief officials of local governments, and the people of the United States to observe such day with appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Approved October 2, 1972.*

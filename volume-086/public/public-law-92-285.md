# Public Law 92–285

> JOINT RESOLUTION Asking the President of the United States to declare the fourth Saturday of September 1972 “National Hunting and Fishing Day”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States declare the fourth Saturday of September 1972 as “National Hunting and Fishing Day” to provide that deserved national recognition, to recognize the esthetic, health, and recreational virtues of hunting and fishing, to dramatize the continued need for gun and boat safety, and to rededicate ourselves to the conservation and respectful use of our wildlife and natural resources.


*Approved May 1, 1972.*

# Public Law 107–119

> An Act To amend the Ethics in Government Act of 1978 (5 U.S.C. App.) to extend the authorization of appropriations for the Office of Government Ethics through fiscal year 2006.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. SHORT TITLE.

This Act may be cited as the “Office of Government Ethics Authorization Act of 2001”.

## § 2. EXTENSION OF AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS.

Section 405 of the Ethics in Government Act of 1978 (5 U.S.C. App.) is amended by striking “1997 through 1999” and inserting “2002 through 2006”.


*Approved January 15, 2002.*

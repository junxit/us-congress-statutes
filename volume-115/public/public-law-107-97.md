# Public Law 107–97

> Joint Resolution Making further continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 2002, and for other purposes.

Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

That Public Law 107–44 is further amended by striking the date specified in section 107(c) and inserting in lieu thereof “January 10, 2002”; and by striking the date specified in section 123 and inserting in lieu thereof “January 1, 2002”.


*Approved December 21, 2001.*

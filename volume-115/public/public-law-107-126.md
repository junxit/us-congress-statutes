# Public Law 107–126

> An Act To extend for 4 years, through December 31, 2005, the authority to redact financial disclosure statements of judicial employees and judicial officers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 105(b)(3)(E) of the Ethics in Government Act of 1978 (5 U.S.C. App.) is amended by striking “2001” each place it appears and inserting “2005”.


*Approved January 16, 2002.*

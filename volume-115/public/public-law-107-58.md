# Public Law 107–58

> Joint Resolution Making further continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 2002, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Public Law 107–44 is further amended by striking the date specified in section Ante, p. 269.107(c) and inserting in lieu thereof “November 16, 2001”.

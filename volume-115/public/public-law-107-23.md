# Public Law 107–23

> An Act To designate the Federal building located at 6230 Van Nuys Boulevard in VanNuys, California, as the “James C. Corman Federal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION OF JAMES C. CORMAN FEDERAL BUILDING.

The Federal building located at 6230 Van Nuys Boulevard in Van Nuys, California, shall be known and designated as the “James C. Corman Federal Building”.

## § 2. REFERENCES.

Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Federal building referred to in section 1 shall be deemed to be a reference to the “James C. Corman Federal Building”.


*Approved August 3, 2001.*

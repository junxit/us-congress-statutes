# Public Law 107–17

> An Act To extend for 4 additional months the period for which chapter 12 of title 11 of the United States Code is reenacted.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. AMENDMENTS.

Section 149 of title I of division C of Public Law 105–277, as amended by Public Law 106–5, Public Law 106–70, and Public Law 107–8, is amended—

- **(1)** by striking “June 1, 2001” each place it appears and inserting “October 1, 2001”, and
- **(2)** in subsection (a)—
  - **(A)** by striking “June 30, 2000” and inserting “May 31, 2001”, and
  - **(B)** by striking “July 1, 2000” and inserting “June 1, 2001”.

## § 2. EFFECTIVE DATE.

The amendments made by section 1 shall take effect on June 1, 2001.


*Approved June 26, 2001.*

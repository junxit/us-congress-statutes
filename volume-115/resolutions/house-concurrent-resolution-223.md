# House Concurrent Resolution 223

> PRAYER VIGIL FOR SEPTEMBER 11, 2001, TERRORIST ATTACK VICTIMS—CAPITOL ROTUNDA AUTHORIZATION

Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),

That the Rotunda of the Capitol is authorized to be used at any time on September 12, 2001, for a prayer vigil in memory of those who lost their lives in the events of September 11, 2001. Physical preparations for the vigil shall be carried out in accordance with such conditions as the Architect of the Capitol may prescribe.


*Agreed to September 12, 2001.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 67

> JOINT COMMITTEE ON PRINTING—MEMBERSHIP DESIGNATION

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That effective for the One Hundred Seventh Congress, the Chairman of the Committee on Rules and Administration of the Senate may designate another member of the Committee to serve on the Joint Committee on Printing in place of the Chairman.


*Agreed to October 9, 2001.*

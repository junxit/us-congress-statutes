# House Concurrent Resolution 18

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on the legislative day of Wednesday, January 31, 2001, it stand adjourned until 2 p.m. on Tuesday, February 6, 2001.


*Agreed to January 31, 2001.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 371

> CORRECTION IN ENROLLMENT OF H.R. 2790

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (H.R. 2790), to amend the Colorado River Basin Salinity Control Act to authorize certain additional measures to assure accomplishment of the objectives of title II of such Act, and for other purposes, the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall make the following correction in the first sentence in section 1: change “the Act (15 U.S.C. 1591(b))” to read “the Colorado River Basin Salinity Control Act (43 U.S.C. 1591(b)), hereinafter referred to as the ‘Act’,”.


*Agreed to October 10, 1984.*

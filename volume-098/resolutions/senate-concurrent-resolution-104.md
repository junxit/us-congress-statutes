# Senate Concurrent Resolution 104

> ADJOURNMENT—SENATE AND HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That when the Senate adjourns on Thursday, April 12, 1984, or on Friday, April 13, 1984, or on Saturday, April 14, 1984, and when the House of Representatives adjourns on Thursday, April 12, 1984, or on Friday, April 13, 1984, or on Saturday, April 14, 1984, they stand adjourned pursuant to the provisions of Senate Concurrent Resolution 103.


*Agreed to April 12, 1984.*

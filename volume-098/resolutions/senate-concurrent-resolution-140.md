# Senate Concurrent Resolution 140

> “HANDBOOK FOR SMALL BUSINESS”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That a revised edition of Senate Document Numbered 96–44, entitled “Handbook for Small Business”, explaining programs of Federal departments, agencies, offices, and commissions of benefit to small business and operating pursuant to various statutes enacted by the Congress, shall be printed with illustrations as a Senate document; and that there shall be printed six thousand additional copies for the use of the Senate Committee on Small Business, and three thousand additional copies for the use of the Committee on Small Business of the House of Representatives.


*Agreed to October 5, 1984.*

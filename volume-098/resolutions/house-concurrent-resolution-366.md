# House Concurrent Resolution 366

> GENERAL EXPLANATION OF THE REVENUE PROVISIONS OF THE DEFICIT REDUCTION ACT OF 1984

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That 3,000 additional copies of the General Explanation of the Revenue Provisions of the Deficit Reduction Act of 1984, prepared by the staff of the Joint Committee on Taxation, be printed for the use of the Joint Committee on Taxation.


*Agreed to October 11, 1984.*

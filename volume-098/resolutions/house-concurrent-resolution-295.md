# House Concurrent Resolution 295

> 1984 OLYMPIC TORCH RELAY THROUGH CAPITOL GROUNDS

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That, on May 14, 1984, or on such other day as the President of the Senate and the Speaker of the House of Representatives may jointly authorize, the 1984 Olympic torch relay may be run through the United States Capitol Grounds as part of the journey of the Olympic torch from Olympia, Greece, to the XXIII Olympic games in Los Angeles, California.

## § 1.

The Capitol Police Board shall take such action as may be necessary or appropriate to carry out the first section of this resolution.


*Agreed to May 10, 1984.*

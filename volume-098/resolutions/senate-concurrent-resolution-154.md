# Senate Concurrent Resolution 154

> CORRECTION IN ENROLLMENT OF H.R. 6163

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That, in the enrollment of the bill (H.R. 6163) to amend title 28, United States Code, with respect to the places where court shall be held in certain judicial districts, and for other purposes, the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall make the following correction:

In section 501(4) strike out “(iii)” the first place it appears and insert in lieu thereof “(iv)”.


*Agreed to October 11, 1984.*

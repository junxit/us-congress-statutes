# House Concurrent Resolution 377

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Thursday, October 11, 1984 or on Friday, October 12, 1984, and that when they adjourn on said day, they stand adjourned sine die.


*Agreed to October 11, 1984.*

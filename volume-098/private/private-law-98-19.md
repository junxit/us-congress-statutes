# Private Law 98–19

> An Act For the relief of Marlon Dolon Opelt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the admin-istration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Marlon Dolon Opelt may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F)) of such Act upon approval of a petition filed on his behalf by Mr. and Mrs. Floyd D. Opelt, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of this Act. The parents, brothers, and sisters of the said Marlon Dolon Opelt shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved September 28, 1984.*

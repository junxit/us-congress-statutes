# Private Law 98–34

> An Act Providing for a fifteen-year extension of patent numbered 3,376,198.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of Commerce, acting through the Commissioner of Patents and Trademarks, shall, when patent numbered 3,376,198 (relating to the production of antibodies in milk) expires, extend such patent for fifteen years, with all the rights pertaining thereto.


*Approved October 19, 1984.*

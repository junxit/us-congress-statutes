# Private Law 98–11

> An Act To clear certain impedimenta to the licensing of the yacht Dad’s Pad for employment in the coastwise trade.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding section 27 of the Merchant Marine Act, 1920 (46 App. U.S.C. 883), the Secretary of the department in which the Coast Guard is operating shall cause the vessel Dad’s Pad, official number 549526, owned by John C. Sciacca, to be documented as a vessel of the United States with the privilege of engaging in the coastwise trade, on compliance with all other requirements of law.

## § 2.

Notwithstanding section 27 of the Merchant Marine Act, 1920 (46 App. U.S.C. 883), the Secretary of the department in which the Coast Guard is operating shall cause the vessel Zorba, official number 229763, owned by Howard Costa, to be documented as a vessel of the United States with the privilege of engaging in the coastwise trade, on compliance with all other requirements of law.


*Approved May 3, 1984.*

# Private Law 98–45

> An Act For the relief of Sutu Bungani William Beck.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Sutu Bungani William Beck shall be held to be a child within section 322(c) of the Immigration and Nationality Act relating to naturalization of an adopted child.


*Approved October 19, 1984.*

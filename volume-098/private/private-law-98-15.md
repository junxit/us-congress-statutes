# Private Law 98–15

> An Act For the relief of Jean Willhelm Willrich.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Jean Willhelm Willrich shall be held and considered to have satisfied the residence and physical presence requirements of section 319(a) of the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved July 10, 1984.*

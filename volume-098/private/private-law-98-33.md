# Private Law 98–33

> An Act To confer citizenship posthumously on Corporal Wladyslaw Staniszewski.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Corporal Wladyslaw Staniszewski, a national of Great Britain, who was killed in action on July 7, 1967, in the Republic of Vietnam while serving in the United States Marine Corps shall be held and considered to have been a citizen of the United States at the time of his death: Provided, That no benefits shall thereafter derive to survivors.


*Approved October 15, 1984.*

# Private Law 98–48

> An Act For the relief of the Sisters of Mercy of the Union, Province of Saint Louis, Missouri.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Sisters of Mercy of the Union, Province of Saint Louis, Missouri, the former owner of Mercy Hospital of Brownsville, Texas, is relieved of all liability to the United States under title VI of the Public Health Service Act resulting from the sale of such Mercy Hospital to Medenco, Incorporated, a for-profit organization.


*Approved October 30, 1984.*

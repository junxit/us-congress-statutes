# Public Law 98–483

> An Act To amend the National Historic Preservation Act, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 212(a) of the National Historic Preservation Act (16 U.S.C. 47Ot(aJ) is amended by striking out the second and third sentences and inserting in lieu thereof “To carry out the provisions of this title, there is authorized to be appropriated not more than $2,500,000 for each of the fiscal years 1985 through 1989”.


*Approved October 17, 1984.*

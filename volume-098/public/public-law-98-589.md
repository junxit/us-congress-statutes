# Public Law 98–589

> An Act To designate the United States Courthouse Building in Hato Rey, Puerto Rico, as the “Clemente Ruiz Nazario United States Courthouse”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States Courthouse Building located at Carlos Chardon Street, Hato Rey, Puerto Rico, shall hereafter be known and designated as the “Clemente Ruiz Nazario United States Courthouse”. Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to that courthouse shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Clemente Ruiz Nazario United States Courthouse”.


*Approved October 30, 1984.*

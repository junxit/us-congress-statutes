# Public Law 98–269

> An Act To transfer responsibility for furnishing certified copies of Miller Act payment bonds from the Comptroller General to the officer that awarded the contract for which the bond was given.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first sentence of section 3 of the Act of August 24, 1935 (49 Stat. 794; 40 U.S.C. 270c), as amended by the Act of August 4, 1959 (73 Stat. 279; 40 U.S.C. 270c), is amended by striking out “Comptroller General” and inserting in lieu thereof “department secretary or agency head of the contracting agency”. The second sentence of section 3 of the Act is amended by striking out “Comptroller General” and inserting in lieu thereof “department secretary or agency head of the contracting agency”.


*Approved April 18, 1984.*

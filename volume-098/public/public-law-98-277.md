# Public Law 98–277

> An Act To designate a Federal building in Augusta, Maine, as the “Edmund S. Muskie Federal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Federal building located at 40 Western Avenue, Augusta, Maine, shall hereafter be named and designated as the “Edmund S. Muskie Federal Building”. Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to such building shall be held to be a reference to the “Edmund S. Muskie Federal Building”.


*Approved May 8, 1984.*

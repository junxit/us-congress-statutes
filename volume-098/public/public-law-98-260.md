# Public Law 98–260

> An Act To designate the air traffic control tower at Midway Airport, Chicago, as the “John G. Fary Tower”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the air traffic control tower at Midway Airport, Chicago, Illinois, is designated and shall hereafter be known as the “John G. Fary Tower”. Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, or other paper of the United States to such control tower shall be held and considered to refer to the “John G. Fary Tower”.


*Approved April 13, 1984.*

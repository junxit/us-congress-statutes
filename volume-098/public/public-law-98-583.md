# Public Law 98–583

> An Act To designate the Federal Building and United States Courthouse in Las Vegas, Nevada, as the “Foley Federal Building and United States Courthouse”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Federal Building and United States Courthouse located at 300 Las Vegas Boulevard South, Las Vegas, Nevada, is designated as the “Foley Federal Building and United States Courthouse”. Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to that building shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Foley Federal Building and United States Courthouse”.


*Approved October 30, 1984.*

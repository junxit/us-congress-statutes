# Public Law 98–546

> An Act To designate the lock and dam on the Warrior River in Hale County, Alabama, as the “Armistead I. Selden Lock and Dam”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the lock and dam on the Warrior River in Hale County, Alabama, commonly known as the Warrior Lock and Dam, shall hereafter be known and designated as the “Armistead I. Selden Lock and Dam”. Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, or paper of the United States to such lock and dam shall be held to be a reference to the “Armistead I. Selden Lock and Dam”.


*Approved October 25, 1984.*

# Public Law 98–455

> Joint Resolution Making further continuing appropriations for fiscal year 1985.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the joint resolution enacted by Congress on October 4, 1984 (H.J. Res. 656), is hereby amended by striking out “six o’clock post meridiem, eastern day-light time, October 5, 1984”; and inserting in lieu thereof “October 9, 1984”.


*Approved October 6, 1984.*

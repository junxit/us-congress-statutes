# Public Law 98–308

> Joint Resolution Designating the week of November 18, 1984, through November 24, 1984, as “National Family Week”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is hereby authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating the week of November 18, 1984, through November 24, 1984, as “National Family Week”, and inviting the Governors of the several States, the chief officials of local governments, and the people of the United States to observe such week with appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Approved May 31, 1984.*

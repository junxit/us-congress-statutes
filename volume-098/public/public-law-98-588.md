# Public Law 98–588

> An Act To redesignate the Veterans’ Administration Medical Center located in Poplar Bluff, Missouri, as the “John J. Pershing Veterans’ Administration Medical Center”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Veterans’ Administration Medical Center in Poplar Bluff, Missouri, shall after the date of the enactment of this Act be known and designated as the “John J. Pershing Veterans’ Administration Medical Center”. Any reference to such medical center in any law, regulation, map, document, record, or other paper of the United States shall after such date be deemed to be a reference to the John J. Pershing Veterans’ Administration Medical Center.


*Approved October 30, 1984.*

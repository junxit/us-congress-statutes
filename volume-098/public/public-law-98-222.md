# Public Law 98–222

> An Act To extend the authorities under the Export Administration Act of 1979.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 20 of the Export Administration Act of 1979 (50 U.S.C. App. 2419) is amended by striking out “February 29” and inserting in lieu thereof “March 30”.


*Approved February 29, 1984.*

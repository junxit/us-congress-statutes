# Public Law 98–535

> Joint Resolution Providing for reappointment of Anne Legendre Armstrong as a citizen regent of the Smithsonian Institution.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the vacancy on the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution, in the class other than Members of Congress, occurring by reason of the expiration of the term of Anne Legendre Armstrong of Texas, is filled by reappointment of the incumbent for a term of six years, effective May 10, 1984.


*Approved October 19, 1984.*

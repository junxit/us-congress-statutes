# Public Law 98–593

> An Act To designate the Federal building in Oak Ridge, Tennessee, as the “Joe L. Evins Federal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in recognition of Joe L. Evins, who was a distinguished Member of Congress and served the State of Tennessee for more than 30 years, the Federal Building located on Administration Road in Oak Ridge, Tennessee, is hereby designated as the “Joe L. Evins Federal Building”. Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to that building shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Joe L. Evins Federal Building”.


*Approved October 30, 1984.*

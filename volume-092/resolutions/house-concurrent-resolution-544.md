# House Concurrent Resolution 544

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Wednesday, March 22, 1978, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian on Monday, April 3, 1978, and that when the Senate recesses on Thursday, March 23, 1978, it stand in recess until Wednesday, March 29, 1978 or April 3, 1978, as determined by the Senate on Thursday. March 23, 1978.


*Passed March 22, 1978.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 96

> “INTELLIGENCE ACTIVITIES AND THE RIGHTS OF AMERICANS”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence six hundred copies of book II of the final report of the Senate Select Committee To Study Governmental Operations With Respect to Intelligence Activities, Ninety-fourth Congress, second session, entitled “Intelligence Activities and the Rights of Americans” (S. Rept. 94–755).


*Agreed to September 26, 1978.*

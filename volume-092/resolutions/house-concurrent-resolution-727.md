# House Concurrent Resolution 727

> CORRECTION OF ENROLLED BILL H.R. 8394

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (H.R. 8394), to provide for payments to local governments based upon the acreage of the National Wildlife Refuge System which is within their boundaries, the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall make the following correction: Strike out subparagraph (B) of paragraph (1) of subsection (a) of the first section of the bill and insert in lieu thereof the following:

> - **(B)** by inserting after ‘National Wildlife Refuge System’ the second time it appears National Fish Hatcheries, or other areas,’”.


*Passed October 2, 1978.*

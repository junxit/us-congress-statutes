# Senate Concurrent Resolution 89

> “KOREAN INFLUENCE INQUIRY”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring},*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Select Committee on Ethics two thousand additional copies of volume II of its hearings of the Ninety-fifth Congress, second session, entitled “Korean Influence Inquiry”.


*Agreed to June 7, 1978.*

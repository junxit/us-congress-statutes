# House Concurrent Resolution 441

> “NEW PERSPECTIVES IN HEALTH CARE FOR OLDER AMERICANS”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed for the use of the Select Committee, on Aging 1,500 copies of the report “New Perspectives in Health Care for Older Americans”, Ninety-fourth Congress.


*Passed June 23, 1978.*

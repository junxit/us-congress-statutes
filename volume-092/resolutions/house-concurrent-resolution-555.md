# House Concurrent Resolution 555

> HUNGARIAN PEOPLE’S REPUBLIC—NONDISCR1MINATORY TREATMENT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring ),*

That the Congress approves the extension of nondiscriminatory treatment with respect to the products of the Hungarian People’s Republic transmitted by the President to the Congress on April 7, 1978.


*Passed June 27, 1978.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 106

> EULOGIES TO HUBERT H. HUMPHREY

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there shall be printed five thousand additional bound copies of the eulogies to Hubert H. Humphrey, late a Senator from the State of Minnesota, to be furnished to his successor in office.


*Agreed to September 26, 1978.*

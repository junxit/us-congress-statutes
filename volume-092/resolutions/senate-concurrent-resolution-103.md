# Senate Concurrent Resolution 103

> CORRECTION OF ENROLLED BILL H.R. 6669

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill (H.R. 6669), to establish a national climate program, and for other purposes, the Clerk of the House of Representatives shall make the following correction:

In section 5(b)(1), strike “ (8) ” and insert in lieu thereof “ (9)”


*Agreed to September 6, 1978.*

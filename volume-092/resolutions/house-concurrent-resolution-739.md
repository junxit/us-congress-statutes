# House Concurrent Resolution 739

> CORRECTION OF ENROLLED BILL H.R. 11318

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Clerk of the House is authorized and directed to correct section 203, line 4, by striking “7(i)” and inserting “7(i)” in the enrollment of the bill (H.R. 11318) to amend the Small Business Act and the Small Business Investment Act of 1958.


*Passed October 11, 1978.*

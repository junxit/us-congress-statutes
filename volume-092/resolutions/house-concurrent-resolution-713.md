# House Concurrent Resolution 713

> JOINT MEETING

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Monday, September 18, 1978, at 8 o’clock postmeridian, for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed September 18, 1978.*

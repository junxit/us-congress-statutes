# House Concurrent Resolution 696

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Thursday, August 17, 1978, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian on Wednesday, September 6, 1978, and that when the Senate recesses on Friday, August 25, Saturday, August 26, Monday, August 28, or Tuesday, August 29, 1978, pursuant to a motion made by the Majority Leader m accordance with this resolution, it stand in recess until 10 o’clock a.m. on Wednesday, September 6, 1978.


*Passed August 17, 1978.*

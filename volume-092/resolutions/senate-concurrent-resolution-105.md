# Senate Concurrent Resolution 105

> KOREAN INFLUENCE INQUIRY

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Select Committee on Ethics five thousand additional copies of its final report to the Senate, and two thousand additional copies of volume II of its executive session hearings, on the Korean influence inquiry.


*Agreed to September 19, 1978.*

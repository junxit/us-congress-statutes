# House Concurrent Resolution 654

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Thursday, June 29, 1978, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian on Monday, July 10, 1978, and that when the Senate completes its disposition of H.R. 12426, Calendar No. 883, it stand in recess until 11:00 o’clock a.m. on Monday, July 10, 1978.


*Passed June 29, 1978.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 115

> AUTHORIZATION TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That notwithstanding the sine die adjournment of the two Houses, the Speaker of the House of Representatives and the President of the Senate, and the President pro tempore be, and they are hereby, authorized to sign enrolled bills and joint resolutions duly passed by the two Houses and found truly enrolled.


*Agreed to October 15, 1978.*

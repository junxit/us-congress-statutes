# Senate Concurrent Resolution 90

> “A LEGISLATIVE HISTORY OF THE WATER POLLUTION CONTROL ACT AMENDMENTS OF 1972”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on Environment and Public Works one thousand additional copies each of volumes 1 and 2 of its committee print of the Ninety-third Congress entitled “A Legislative History of the Water Pollution Control Act Amendments of 1972”.


*Agreed to September 26, 1978.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 760

> ADJOURNMENT SINE DIE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives {the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Sunday, October 15, 1978, and that when they adjourn on said day, they stand adjourned sine die.


*Passed October 15, 1978.*

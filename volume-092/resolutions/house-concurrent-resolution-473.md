# House Concurrent Resolution 473

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Thursday, February 9, 1978, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian on Tuesday, February 14, 1978, and that when the Senate recesses on Friday, February 10, 1978, it stand in recess until 10:00 a.m. on Monday, February 20, 1978.


*Passed February 9, 1978.*

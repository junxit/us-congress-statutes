# Private Law 95–151

> An Act For the relief of Elvi Engelsmann Jensen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Elvi Engelsmann Jensen may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b) (1) (E) of such Act upon approval of a petition filed on her behalf by Mr. and Mrs. Donald Jensen, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of such Act. The parents, brothers, and sisters of the said Elvi Engelsmann Jensen shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved October 31, 1978.*

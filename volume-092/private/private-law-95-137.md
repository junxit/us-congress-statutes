# Private Law 95–137

> An Act For the relief of Elisabetta Basso Gallizio.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the Immigration and Naturalization Act the provisions of section 312(1) of that Act shall be inapplicable in the case of Elisabetta Basso Gallizio.


*Approved October 30, 1978.*

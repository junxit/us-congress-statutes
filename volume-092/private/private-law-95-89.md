# Private Law 95–89

> An Act For the relief of Lee So Ryung.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Lee So Ryung may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b) (1)(F) of the Act, and a petition filed in his behalf by Mr. and Mrs. Douglas Dix, citizens of the United States, may be approved pursuant to section 204 of the Act: Provided, That the natural parents or brothers or sisters of the beneficiary shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act. Section 204(c) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, relating to the number of petitions which may be approved, shall be inapplicable in this case.


*Approved October 28, 1978.*

# Private Law 95–86

> An Act For the relief of Anthony Rogers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 203(a) (1) and 204 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Anthony Rogers shall be held and considered to be the natural- born alien son of Mr. Charles Rogers, a citizen of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents or brothers or sisters of the beneficiary shall not by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved October 28, 1978.*

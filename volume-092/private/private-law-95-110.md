# Private Law 95–110

> An Act For the relief of Miriama Jones.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Miriama Jones may be classified as an orphan within the definition of section 101(b) (1) (F) of such Act and a petition filed in her behalf by Mr. and Mrs. David Lyman, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of such Act, shall be approved upon compliance with the remaining requirements of said section 101(b)(1)(F). The natural parents, brothers, and sisters of the beneficiary shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved October 28, 1978.*

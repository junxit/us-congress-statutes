# Private Law 95–164

> An Act For the relief of Veronica Judith Hudson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 101(a) (27) (A) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1101(a)(27)(A)), Veronica Judith Hudson shall be held and considered to be a returning resident alien.


*Approved November 2, 1978.*

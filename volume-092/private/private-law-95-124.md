# Private Law 95–124

> An Act For the relief of Tsutomu Tanaka.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Tsutomu Tanaka shall be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b) (1) (F) of such Act, upon approval of a petition filed on his behalf by Mr. and Mrs. Timothy M. Killian, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of such Act. No natural parent, brother, or sister, if any, of Tsutomu Tanaka shall, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved October 28, 1978.*

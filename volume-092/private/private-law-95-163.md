# Private Law 95–163

> An Act For the relief of Anthony Casamento.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time limitation contained in section 6248(a) of title 10, United States Code, with respect to the awarding of certain medals to persons in the naval service shall not apply with respect to the award of any such medal to Anthony Casamento, of West Islip, New York, for acts of valor performed by the said Anthony Casamento during November 1942 while engaged in combat operations on Guadalcanal Island as a corporal in the United States Marine Corps.


*Approved November 2, 1978.*

# Private Law 95–134

> An Act For the relief of Nora L. Kennedy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Nora L. Kennedy, the widow of a citizen of the United States, shall be deemed to be an immediate relative within the meaning of section 201(b) of such Act, and the provisions of section 204 of that Act shall not be applicable in this case.


*Approved October 30, 1978.*

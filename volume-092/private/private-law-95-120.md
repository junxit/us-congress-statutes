# Private Law 95–120

> An Act For the relief of Susanna Shu-hui Jean.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Susanna Shu-hui Jean may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b) (1) (E) of the Act, upon approval of a petition filed in her behalf by Jong Ho Jean and Fu-mei Freda Jean, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of the Act: Provided, That the natural parents or brothers or sisters of the beneficiary shall not, by virtue of such a relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved October 28, 1978.*

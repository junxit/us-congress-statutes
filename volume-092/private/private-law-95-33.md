# Private Law 95–33

> An Act For the relief of Su-Hwan Choe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purposes of sections 203(a) (1) and 204 of the Immigration and Nationality Act. Su-Hwan Choe shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien son of Thomas and Catherine Jones, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents or brothers or sisters of the beneficiary shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved March 27, 1978.*

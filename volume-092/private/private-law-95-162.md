# Private Law 95–162

> An Act For the relief of Carmen Cecilia Blanquicett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 101(b)(1) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Carmen Cecilia Blanquicett shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien daughter of Cecilia De Miguel, a citizen of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents or brothers or sisters of the beneficiary shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved November 2, 1978.*

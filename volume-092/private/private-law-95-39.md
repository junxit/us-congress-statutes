# Private Law 95–39

> An Act For the relief of Charles I. Abbott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That any debt owed the United States by Charles P. Abbott of Glendora, California, arising from the judgment of the United States District Court for the District of Massachusetts in United States against Abbott (Civil Action No. 60–800-W), entered September 29, 1964, is hereby extinguished.


*Approved June 10, 1978.*

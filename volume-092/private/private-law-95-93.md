# Private Law 95–93

> An Act For the relief of Young Gun Kim.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Young Gun Kim may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b) (I) (F) of the Act, upon approval of a petition filed in his behalf by Mr. and Mrs. Roger D. Taylor, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of the Act: Provided, That the natural parents or brothers or sisters of the beneficiary shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.

Section 204(c) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, relating to the number of petitions which may be approved, shall be inapplicable in this case.


*Approved October 28, 1978.*

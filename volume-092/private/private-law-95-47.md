# Private Law 95–47

> An Act For the relief of Miss Coralla Raposo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purpose of sections 203(a) (1) and 204 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Coralia Maria Pimentel Raposo shall be held and considered to be the, natural born alien daughter of Mr. and Mrs. Richard G. Carnright, citizens of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents or brothers or sisters of the beneficiary of this Act shall not, by virtue of their relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved August 3, 1978.*

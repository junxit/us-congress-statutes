# Private Law 95–167

> An Act For the relief of Sameek Keshary Bay.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, the periods of time Sameek Keshary Ray has resided in the United States since August 10, 1970, shall be held and considered to meet the residence and physical presence requirements of section 316 of the said Act


*Approved November 2, 1978.*

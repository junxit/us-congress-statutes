# Private Law 95–155

> An Act For the relief of Kalnoosh-Fard Bullock and her son, Kami Bullock.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the proviso of section 201(b) and the provisions of section 204 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Kainoosh-Fard Bullock and her son, Kami Bullock, shall be held and considered to be immediate relatives for purposes of such Act.


*Approved October 31, 1978.*

# Private Law 95–165

> An Act For the relief of Andree Marie Helene McGiffin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 101 (a) (27) (A) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Andree Marie Helene McGiffin shall be held and considered to be a returning resident alien.


*Approved November 2, 1978.*

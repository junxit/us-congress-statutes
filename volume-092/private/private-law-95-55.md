# Private Law 95–55

> An Act For the relief of William .1. Elder and the estate of Stephen M. Owens, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That each of the following-named persons is relieved of all liability to repay the United States the amount which appeal’s beside his name:

William J. Elder, $1,143.37.

The estate of Stephen M. Owens, deceased, $231.25.

Such amounts represent overpayments of travel, transportation, and other related expenses made, as a result of an administrative error to the above-named civilian employees of the Department of the Navy during the years 1974 and 1975. In the audit and settlement of the accounts of any certifying or disbursing officer of the United States, credit shall be given for amounts for which liability is relieved by this Act.


*Approved September 30, 1978.*

# Private Law 95–116

> An Act For the relief of Chester Chun Ket Young (also known as Chun-Kit Yeung).

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Chester Chun Ket Young (also known as Chun-Kit Yeung), may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of such Act upon approval of a petition filed on his behalf by Mr. and Mrs. Kwan Loy Young, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of sucn Act. No natural parent, brother or sister, or prior adoptive parent, of Chester Chun Ket Young (also known as Chun-Kit Yeung), shall, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under such Act.


*Approved October 28, 1978.*

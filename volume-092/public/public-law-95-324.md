# Public Law 95–324

> An Act To designate the Federal building and United States courthouse in Hato Rey, Puerto Rico, the “Federico Degetau Federal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Federal building and United States courthouse located on Carlos Chardon Street, Hato Rey, Puerto Rico, is hereby designated as the “Federico Degetau Federal Building”. Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to such building shall be held to be a reference to the “Federico Degetau Federal Building”.


*Approved July 28, 1978.*

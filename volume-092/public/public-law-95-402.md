# Public Law 95–402

> An Act To amend the Agricultural Act of 1949 to ensure that the interest rates on price support loans for upland cotton are not less favorable to producers than the interest rates for such loans on other commodities.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 103(f) of the Agricultural Act of 1949 is amended by striking out the fourth sentence of paragraph (1).


*Approved September 30, 1978.*

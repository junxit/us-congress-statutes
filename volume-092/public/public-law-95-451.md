# Public Law 95–451

> An Act To designate the United States Department of Agriculture’s Pecan Field Station in Brownwood, Texas, as the “W. R. ‘Bob’ Poage Pecan Field Station”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States Department of Agriculture’s Pecan Field Station in Brownwood, Texas, shall hereafter be known and designated as the “W. R. ‘Bob’ Poage Pecan Field Station”. Any reference to such laboratory in any law, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States shall be deemed a reference to it as the W. R. “Bob” Poage Pecan Field Station.


*Approved October 11, 1978.*

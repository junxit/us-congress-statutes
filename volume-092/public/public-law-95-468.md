# Public Law 95–468

> An Act To designate the building known as the Ozark National Forest Headquarters Building in Russellville, Arkansas, as the “Henry R. Koen Forest Service Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the building in Russellville, Arkansas, commonly known as the Ozark National Forest Headquarters Building, shall hereafter be known and designated as the “Henry R. Koen Forest Service Building”. Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to that building shall be held to be a reference to the “Henry R. Koen Forest Service Building”.


*Approved October 17, 1978.*

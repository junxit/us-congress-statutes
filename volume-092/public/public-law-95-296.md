# Public Law 95–296

> An Act To designate the Mike Monroney Aeronautical (tenter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, the Federal Aviation Administration Aeronautical Center, Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, shall hereafter be known and designated as the “Mike Monroney Aeronautical Center”. Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to such center shall be held to be a reference to the Mike Monroney Aeronautical Center.


*Approved June 19, 1978.*

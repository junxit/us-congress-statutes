# Public Law 95–303

> An Act To lower the duty on levulose until the close of June 30, 1980.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subpart B of part 1 of the Appendix to the Tariff Schedules of the United States (19 U.S.C. 1202) is amended by inserting after item 907.80 the following new item:

| 907.90 | Levulose (provided for in item 493.66, part 13B, schedule 4) | 10 percent ad val. | No change | On or before 6/30/80 | ”. |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## § 2.

The amendment made by the first section of this Act shall apply with respect to articles entered, or withdrawn from warehouse, for consumption on or after the date of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved June 29, 1978.*

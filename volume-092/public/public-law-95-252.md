# Public Law 95–252

> An Act To extend the existing temporary debt limit.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first section of the Act of October 4, 1977, entitled “An Act to increase the temporary debt limit, and for other purposes” (Public Law 95–120), is amended by striking out “March 31, 1978” and inserting in lieu thereof “July 31, 1978”.


*Approved March 27, 1978.*

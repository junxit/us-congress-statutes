# Public Law 95–378

> An Act To repeal chapter 27 of title 44, United States Code.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Federal Records Council is hereby abolished.

## § 2.

- **(a)** Chapter 27 of title 44, United States Code, is repealed.
- **(b)** The table of chapters of title 44, United States Code, is amended by striking out the item pertaining to chapter 27.


*Approved September 22, 1978.*

# Public Law 95–285

> An Act To name the lake located behind Lower Monumental Lock and Dam, Washington, “Lake Herbert G. West”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the lake located behind the Lower Monumental Lock and Dam. Washington, a part of the project authorized by the Act of March 2, 1945 (Public Law 14, Seventy-ninth Congress, first session), in accordance with the plan submitted in House Document Numbered 704. Seventy-fifth Congress, third session, shall hereafter be known as Lake Herbert G. West, and any law, regulation, document, or record of the United States in which such lake is designated or referred to as “Lower Monumental Lake” or is referred to by any other name, shall be held to refer to such lake under and by the name of “Lake Herbert G. West”.


*Approved May 25, 1978.*

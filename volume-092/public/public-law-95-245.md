# Public Law 95–245

> An Act To amend the Federal Aviation Act of 1058 to improve cargo air service.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 418(a) of the Federal Aviation Act of 1958 is amended by renumbering paragraph (3) and any reference thereto as paragraph (4) and by adding immediately after paragraph (2) the following new paragraph:

> - **(3)** Any citizen of the United States who has a valid certificate issued under section 401(d)(3) of this title and who provided supplemental air transportation carrying only cargo at any time during the period from January 1, 1977, through the date of enactment of this section may, during the forty-five day period beginning on April 1, 1978, submit an application to the Board for a certificate under this section to provide all-cargo air service. Such application shall contain such information and be in such form as the Board shall by regulation require.”.

## § 2.

Section 418(b)(1)(A) of the Federal Aviation Act of 1958 is amended by deleting “or (2)” and by inserting in lieu thereof“. (2), or (3)”.

## § 3.

Section 418(b)(1)(B) of the Federal Aviation Act of 1958 is amended by deleting “(3)” and inserting in lieu thereof “(4)”.

## § 4.

The provisions of this Act shall become effective on April 1, 1978.


*Approved March 14, 1978.*

# Public Law 95–277

> An Act To authorize the Secretary of the Treasury to designate an Assistant Secretary to serve in his place us a member of the Library of Congress Trust Fund Board.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first paragraph of the first section of the Act entitled “An Act to create a Library of Congress Trust Fund Board, and for other purposes”, approved March 3, 1925 (2 U.S.C. 154), is amended by inserting after “Secretary of the Treasury” the following: “(or an Assistant Secretary designated in writing by the Secretary of the Treasury)”.


*Approved May 12, 1978.*

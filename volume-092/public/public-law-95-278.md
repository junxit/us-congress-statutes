# Public Law 95–278

> An Act To provide for conveyance of certain lands adjacent to the Gund Ranch, Grass Valley, Nevada, to the University of Nevada.

## § 2.

No conveyance shall be made under this Act unless application therefor by the University of Nevada is received by the Secretary of the Interior within one year of the effective date of tills Act.

## § 3.

The land conveyed by this Act shall be used for the establishment and operation of a rangeland research station and for the conduct of associated experimental range management and improvement programs. The patent or other document of conveyance issued pursuant to this Act shall incorporate the limitation set forth in this section and shall provide that title to the land shall revert to the United States if the land is used for any other purpose.

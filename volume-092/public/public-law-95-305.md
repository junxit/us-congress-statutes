# Public Law 95–305

> An Act Authorizing appropriations to the Secretary of the Interior for services necessary to the non performing arts functions of the John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (e) of section 6 of the John F, Kennedy Center Act (72 Stat, 1698), as amended, is amended by adding at the end thereof the following: “There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this subsection not to exceed $4,20(1,000 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1979.”.


*Approved June 29, 1978.*

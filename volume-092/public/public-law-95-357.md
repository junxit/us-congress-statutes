# Public Law 95–357

> An Act To amend chapter 639 of title 10, United States Code, to enable the Secretary of the Navy to change the name of a publication of the Naval Observatory providing data for navigators and astronomers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 7396(a) of title 10, United States Code, is amended by striking the words “in the American Ephemeris and Nautical Almanac”.


*Approved September 8, 1978.*

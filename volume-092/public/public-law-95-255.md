# Public Law 95–255

> Joint Resolution Making an urgent supplemental appropriation for disaster relief for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1978.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the following stint is appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1978, namely:

## FUNDS APPROPRIATED TO THE PRESIDENT

## Federal Disaster Assistance Administration

## disaster relief

For an additional amount for “Disaster relief”, $300,000,000, to remain available until expended: Provided, That not to exceed 3 per centum of the foregoing amount shall be available for administrative expenses.


*Approved April 4, 1978.*

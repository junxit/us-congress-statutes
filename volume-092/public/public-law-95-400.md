# Public Law 95–400

> An Act To amend the pilot project workfare provisions of the Food stamp Act of 1977.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the last sentence of section 17(b)(2) of the Food Stamp Act of 1977 is amended by striking everything after “Act,” and inserting in lieu thereof the following: “shall issue an interim report no later than October 1, 1979, and shall issue a final report describing the results of such pilot projects no later than October 1, 1980.”.


*Approved September 30, 1978.*

# Private Law 94–96

> An Act For the relief of Boulder Daily Camera, Boulder, Colorado.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to settle and adjust the claim of the Boulder Daily Camera, Boulder, Colorado, for the Army Reserve Officer’s Training Corps, University of Colorado, Boulder, Colorado, recruiting advertisement published in September 1972, and to allow in full and final settlement of such claim the sum of $57.12. Such amount shall be payable from the applicable appropriation of the Department of the Army.


*Approved August 19, 1976.*

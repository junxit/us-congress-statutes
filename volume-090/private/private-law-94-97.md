# Private Law 94–97

> An Act For the relief of Mrs. David C. Davis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

that notwithstanding the provisions of an Act entitled “An Act to establish eligibility for burial in national cemeteries, and for other purposes”, approved on May 14, 1948 (62 Stat. 234; 24 U.S.C. 281), Mrs. David C. Davis may be buried next to her husband in Post Cemetery Number 2, Fort Jackson, South Carolina.


*Approved September 10, 1976.*

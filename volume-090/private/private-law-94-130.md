# Private Law 94–130

> An Act For the relief of Barry Ray Leftwich Dibllng.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203(a) (1) and 204 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Barry Ray Leftwich Dibling shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien son of Ray E. Leftwich, a citizen of the United States.


*Approved October 14, 1976.*

# Private Law 94–121

> An Act For the relief of Fernando Alves Macos.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, an immediate relative petition filed by Leopoldina Teixeira Macos in behalf of Fernando Alves Macos may be approved notwithstanding the provisions of sections 204(c) and 212(a) (19) of the Act.


*Approved October 11, 1976.*

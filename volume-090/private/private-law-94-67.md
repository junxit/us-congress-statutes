# Private Law 94–67

> An Act For the relief of Cheryl Lynn V. Camacho.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Cheryl Lynn V. Camacho shall be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of the Act, upon filing a petition in her behalf by Mr. and Mrs. Gregorio Aquino, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of the Act: Provided, That the natural parents or brothers or sisters of the beneficiary shall not by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved August 14, 1976.*

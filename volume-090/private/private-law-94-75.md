# Private Law 94–75

> An Act For the relief of Susan Rosemary Harwood.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 101(a) (27) (B) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Susan Rosemary Harwood shall be held and considered to be a returning resident alien at the time of her admission to the United States on August 21, 1974.


*Approved August 14, 1976.*

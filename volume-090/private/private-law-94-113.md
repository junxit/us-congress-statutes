# Private Law 94–113

> An Act For the relief of Jacinto Vazquez Camacho.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Jacinto Vazquez Camacho may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101 (b) (1) (F) of the Act and a petition filed in his behalf by Michael P. McAndrew, a citizen of the United States, may be approved pursuant to section 204 of the Act: Provided, That the natural parents or brothers or sisters of the beneficiary shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved October 5, 1976.*

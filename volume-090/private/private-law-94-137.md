# Private Law 94–137

> An Act For the relief of Mrs. Helen Wolski, Michael Wolskl, and Steven Wolski.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House. of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, notwithstanding the time limitations contained in section 8193(c) (3) of title 5 of the United States Code, a claim presented to the Secretary of Labor within one year after the date of enactment of this Act by Mrs. Helen Wolski, Michael Wolski, and Steven Wolski for the death of her husband and their father, a Detroit police officer killed in the line of duty, shall be allowed under section 8192(b) to the extent that such claim is otherwise allowable under that section.


*Approved October 17, 1976.*

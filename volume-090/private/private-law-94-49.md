# Private Law 94–49

> An Act For the relief of Paul W. Williams.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions of the Act entitled “An Act providing for the barring of claims against the United States”, approved October 9, 1940 (54 Stat. 1961), the Department of the Navy is authorized and directed (1) to receive and consider any claim filed within six months of the date of enactment of this Act by Paul W. Williams (United States Marine Corps, retired) of Grants Pass, Oregon, for retirement pay he did not receive, due to administrative error, during the period of September 1, 1955, through June 9, 1961, and (2) to award to the said Paul W. Williams any retirement pay to which he would have been entitled for such period.

## § 2.

Any amounts payable by reason of the enactment of this Act shall be paid in a lump sum within sixty days after the determination that such amounts are payable.


*Approved May 29, 1976.*

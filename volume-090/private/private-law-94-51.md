# Private Law 94–51

> An Act For the relief of Miss Rosario Y. Quijano, Walter York Quijano, Ramon York Quijano, Tarcisus York Quijano, Denis York Quijano, and Paul York Quijano.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Rosario Y. Quijano and Walter Y. Quijano shall be held and considered to be entitled to preference status within the purview of section 203(a) (1) of that Act, and Ramon Quijano. Tarcisus Quijano, Denis Quijano, and Paul Quijano shall be held and considered to be immediate relatives within the purview of section 201 (b) of that Act, and the provisions of section 204 of such Act shall not be applicable in these cases.


*Approved June 11, 1976.*

# Private Law 94–50

> An Act For the relief of Angela Garza and her son Manuel Aguilar (aka Manuel Garza).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the I immigration and Nationality Act, Angela Garza, the widow of a citizen of the United States, and her son, Manuel Aguilar (also known as Manuel Garza), shall be held and considered to be within the purview of section 201(b) of that Act and the provisions of section 204 of such Act shall not be applicable in their cases.


*Approved June 10, 1976.*

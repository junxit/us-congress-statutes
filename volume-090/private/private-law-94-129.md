# Private Law 94–129

> An Act For the relief of Ljudevit Previc.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

that in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act the provisions of section 312(1) of that Act shall be inapplicable in the case of Ljudevit Previc.


*Approved October 14, 1976.*

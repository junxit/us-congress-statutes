# Public Law 94–459

> An Act To name the Visitors’ Center at the Sleeping Bear Dunes National Lakeshore the “Philip A. Hart Visitors’ Center”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Visitors’ Center at the Sleeping Bear Dimes National Lakeshore shall hereinafter be known as, and is hereby designated as, the “Philip A. Hart Visitors’ Center”.


*Approved October 8, 1976.*

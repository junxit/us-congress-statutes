# Public Law 94–273

> An Act To provide permanent changes in laws necessary because of the October-September fiscal year.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, this Act may be cited as the “Fiscal Year Adjustment Act”.

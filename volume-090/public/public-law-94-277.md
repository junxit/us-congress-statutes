# Public Law 94–277

> Joint Resolution To extend support under the joint resolution providing for Allen J. Ellender fellowships to disadvantaged secondary school students, and tor other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House, of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2(b)(1) of the joint resolution entitled a “Joint resolution to provide grants for Allen J. Ellender fellowships to disadvantaged secondary school students and their teachers to participate in a Washington public affairs program” approved October 19, 1972, is amended by Striking out “not more than one thousand five hundred”, and by striking out “, in any fiscal year”.

## § 2.

Section 5 of such joint resolution is amended by striking out “two succeeding fiscal years” and inserting in lieu thereof the following: “three succeeding fiscal years, $750,000 each for fiscal year 1977 and fiscal year 1978, and $1,000,000 each for fiscal year 1979 and fiscal year 1980,”.

## § 3.

Section 2(b) of such joint resolution is further amended—

- **(1)** by striking “and” at the end of paragraph (2);
- **(2)** by inserting immediately after such paragraph the following new paragraph:
  > - **(3)** that every effort will be made to achieve participation of students and teachers from rural and small town areas, as well as from urban areas, in the program; and”; and

- **(3)** by redesignating paragraph (3) as paragraph (4).


*Approved April 21, 1976.*

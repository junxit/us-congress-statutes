# Public Law 94–375

> An Act To amend and extend laws relating to housing and community development.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. short title

This Act may be cited as the “Housing Authorization Act of 1976”.

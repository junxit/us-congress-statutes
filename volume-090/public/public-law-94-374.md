# Public Law 94–374

> An Act To amend the Federal Aviation Act of 1868 to extend the authority of the Secretary of Transportation with respect to war risk insurance.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1312 of the Federal Aviation Act of 1958 (49 U.S.C. 1542) is amended by striking out “May 7, 1976”, and inserting in lieu thereof “May 7, 1977”.


*Approved July 31, 1976.*

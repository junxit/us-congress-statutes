# Public Law 94–481

> An Act To amend the Independent Safety Board Act of 1974 to authorize additional appropriations and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 309 of the Independent Safety Board Act of 1974 (49 U.S.C. 1907) is amended by adding at the end thereof the following new sentence: “There are authorized to be appropriated for the purpose of this Act not to exceed $3,800,000 for the transition quarter ending September 30, 1976, $15,200,000 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1977, and $16,400,000 for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1978, such sums to remain available until expended.”.


*Approved October 11, 1976.*

# Public Law 94–560

> An Act To continue until the close of June 30, 1979, the existing suspension of duties on manganese ore (including ferruginous ore) and related products.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** item 911.07 of the Tariff Schedules of the United States (19 U.S.C. 1202) is amended by striking out “6/30/76” and inserting in lieu thereof “June 30, 1979”.
- **(b)** The amendment made by subsection (a) shall apply with respect to articles entered, or withdrawn from warehouse, for consumption after June 30, 1976.


*Approved October 19, 1976.*

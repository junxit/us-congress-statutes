# Public Law 94–516

> An Act To name the Federal office building in Bluefield, West Virginia, the “Elizabeth Kee Federal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America tn Congress assembled,*

That the Federal office building located at 601 Federal Street. Bluefield, West Virginia, is hereby designated as the “Elizabeth Kee Federal Building” Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United Stales to such building shall be held to be a reference to the “Elizabeth Kee Federal Building”.


*Approved October 15, 1976.*

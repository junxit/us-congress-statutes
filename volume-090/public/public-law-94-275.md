# Public Law 94–275

> Joint Resolution To provide for the designation of the week beginning March 13, 1977, as “National Employ the Older Worker Week”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating the week beginning March 13, 1977, as “National Employ the Older Worker Week”, and calling upon employer and employee organizations officially concerned with employment, and upon all the people of the United States to observe such week with appropriate ceremonies, activities, and programs designed to promote employment opportunities for older workers.


*Approved April 21, 1976.*

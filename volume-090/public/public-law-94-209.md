# Public Law 94–209

> An Act To amend the National Portrait Gallery Act to redefine “portraiture”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2(e) of the National Portrait Gallery Act (Public Law 87–443) is amended as follows: “The term ‘portraiture’ includes portraits and reproductions thereof made by any means or process, whether invented or developed heretofore or hereafter.”.


*Approved February 5, 1976.*

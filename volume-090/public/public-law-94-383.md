# Public Law 94–383

> An Act To increase benefits provided to American civilian internees in Southeast Asia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 5 (i) (3) of the War Claims Act of 1948 (50 App. U.S.C. 2004(i)(8)) is amended by striking out “$60” and inserting in lieu thereof “$150”.


*Approved August 12, 1976.*

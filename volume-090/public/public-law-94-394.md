# Public Law 94–394

> An Act To Increase an authorization of appropriations for the Privacy Protection Study Commission, and to remove the flea year expenditure limitation.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provision of law referred to in the note immediately preceding section 553 of title 5, United States Code, is amended to read as follows:

> ## § 9.
>
> There is authorized to be appropriated, without fiscal year limitation only to such extent or in such amounts as are provided in appropriation Acts, the sum of $2,000,600 to carry out the provisions of section 5 of this Act for the period beginning July 1, 1975, and ending on September 30, 1977.”.


*Approved September 3, 1976.*

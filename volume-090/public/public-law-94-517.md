# Public Law 94–517

> An Act To amend the Emergency Livestock Credit Act of 1974.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and. House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 8 of the Emergency Livestock Credit Act of 1974 (88 Stat. 391, as amended; 7 U.S.C. Prec. 1961) is amended to read as follows:

> ## § 8.
>
> The provisions of this Act shall become effective upon enactment, and the authority to make new guarantees shall expire on September 30, 1978.”.


*Approved October 15, 1976.*

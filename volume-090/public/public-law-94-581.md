# Public Law 94–581

> An Act To amend title 38, United States Code, to improve the quality of hospital care, medical services, and nursing home cure in Veterans’ Administration health care facilities; to make certain technical and conforming amendments; and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That this Act may be cited as the “Veterans Omnibus Health Care Act of 1976”.

## TITLE I — GENERAL VETERANS HEALTH CARE AND DEPARTMENT OF MEDICINE AND SURGERY AMENDMENTS

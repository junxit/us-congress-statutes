# Public Law 94–494

> Joint Resolution To provide for the convening of the first session of the Ninety-fifth Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, the first regular session of the Ninety-fifth Congress shall begin at 2 o’clock post meridiem on Tuesday, January 4, 1977.


*Approved October 14, 1976*

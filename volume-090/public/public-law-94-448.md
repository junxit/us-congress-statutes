# Public Law 94–448

> An Act To make the provisions of section 1331(e) of title 10, United States Code, retroactive to November 1, 1953.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purposes of survivor annuities under subchapter I of chapter 73 of title 10, United States Code, and under prior corresponding provisions of law, the provisions of section 1331(e) of such title 10, relating to the date of entitlement to retired pay under chapter 67 of such title 10, shall be effective as of November 1, 1953.

## § 2.

No benefits shall be paid to any person for any period prior to the date of enactment of this Act as a result of the enactment of this Act.


*Approved October 1, 1976.*

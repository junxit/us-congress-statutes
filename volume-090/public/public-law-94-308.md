# Public Law 94–308

> An Act To extend as an emergency measure for one year the District of Columbia Medical and Dental Manpower Act of 1970.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 303(c) of the District of Columbia Medical and Dental Manpower Act of 1970 (D.C. Code. sec. 31–922(c)) is amended by striking out “years ending June 30, 1975, and June 30, 1976,” and inserting in lieu thereof “year ending September 30, 1977,”.


*Approved June 4, 1976.*

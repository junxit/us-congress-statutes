# Public Law 94–274

> An Act To provide tor the orderly transition to the now October 1 to September 30 fiscal year.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That this Act may be cited as the “Fiscal Year Transition Act”.

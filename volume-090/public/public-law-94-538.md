# Public Law 94–538

> An Act To provide that the lake formed by the lock and dam referred to as the “Jones Bluff lock and clam” on the Alabama River, Alabama, shall hereafter be known as the R. E. “Bob” Woodruff Lake.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in honor of late Probate Judge R. E. “Bob” Woodruff, of Lowndes County, Alabama, and in recognition of his long and outstanding service to his county, State, and Nation, the lake formed by the Jones Bluff lock and dam on the Alabama River, Alabama, shall hereafter be known and designated as the R. E. “Bob” Woodruff Lake. Any law, regulation, map, or record of the United States in which such lake is referred to shall be held and considered to refer to such lake by the name of the R. E. “Bob” Woodruff Lake.


*Approved October 18, 1976.*

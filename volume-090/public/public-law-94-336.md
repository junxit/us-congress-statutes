# Public Law 94–336

> An Act To amend the Act of October 15, 1906 (80 Stat. 953: 20 U.S.C. 65a), relating to the National Museum of the Smithsonian Institution, so as to authorize additional appropriations to the Smithsonian Institution for carrying out the purposes of said Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2(b) of the National Museum Act of 1966 (20 U.S.C. 65a) is amended to read:

> - **(b)** There are hereby authorized to be, appropriated to the Smithsonian Institution $1,000,000 each year for fiscal years 1978, 1979, and 1980.”.


*Approved July 1, 1976.*

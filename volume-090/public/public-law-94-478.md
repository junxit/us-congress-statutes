# Public Law 94–478

> An Act To authorize the Secretary of the Department in which the Coast Guard Is operating to lease housing facilities for Coast Guard personnel in a foreign country on a multi-year basis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 475(a) of title 14, United States Code, is amended by inserting, immediately after the first sentence, a new sentence to read as follows: “When any such lease involves housing facilities in a foreign country, the lease may be made on a multi-year basis, for a period not to exceed five years.”.


*Approved October 11, 1976.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 115

> SENATE COMMITTEE ON FOREIGN RELATIONS—ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTIETH ANNIVERSARY

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed with illustrations as a Senate document back-ground information relating to the history of the Senate Committee on Foreign Relations in connection with its one hundred and sixtieth anniversary (1816–1976); and that there be printed for the use of that committee seven thousand five hundred additional copies of such document.


*Agreed to August 30, 1976.*

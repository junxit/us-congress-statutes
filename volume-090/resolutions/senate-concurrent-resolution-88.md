# Senate Concurrent Resolution 88

> SELECT COMMITTEE TO STUDY GOVERNMENTAL OPERATIONS WITH RESPECT TO INTELLIGENCE ACTIVITIES—HEARINGS AND REPORT

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Select Committee To Study Governmental Operations With Respect to Intelligence Activities five thousand additional copies of all parts of its public hearings and of its final report to the Senate.


*Agreed to February 26, 1976.*

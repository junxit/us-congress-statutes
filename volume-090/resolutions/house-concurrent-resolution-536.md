# House Concurrent Resolution 536

> “OUR FLAG”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives {the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed as a House document, with illustrations, a revised edition of the House document “Our Flag”; and that two hundred and seventy-two thousand five hundred additional copies be printed, of which two hundred and twenty-one thousand shall be for the use of the House of Representatives and fifty-one thousand five hundred shall be for the use of the Senate.


*Passed May 10, 1976.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 84

> CONVENTION OF AMERICAN INSTRUCTORS OF THE DEAF

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the report of the proceedings of the forty-seventh biennial meeting of the Convention of American Instructors of the Deaf, held in Greensboro, North Carolina, from June 22, 1975, through June 27, 1975, be printed with illustrations as a Senate document. Four thousand additional copies of such document shall be printed for the use of the Joint Committee on Printing.


*Agreed to April 9, 1976.*

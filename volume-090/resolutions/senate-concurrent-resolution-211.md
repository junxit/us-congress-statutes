# Senate Concurrent Resolution 211

> ADJOURNMENT SINE DIE

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Friday, October 1, 1976, and that when they adjourn on said day, they stand adjourned sine die.


*Agreed to October 1, 1976.*

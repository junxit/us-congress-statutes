# Senate Concurrent Resolution 106

> CORRECTIONS OF ENROLLED BILL H.R. 200

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Clerk of the House of Representatives is directed to make corrections in the enrollment of H.R. 200, a bill to provide for the conservation and management of the fisheries, and for other purposes, as follows:

## § 2.

In section 202(a) (5) of the bill strike “any” and insert in lieu thereof “such”.

## § 3.

In section 205(d) of the bill strike out [Definition.—] and insert Definitions.—

## § 4.

In section 305 of the bill—

- **(1)** strike out [(d) Emergency Actions.—] and insert (e) Emergency Actions.—;
- **(2)** strike out [(e) Annual Report.—] and insert (f) Annual Report.—; and
- **(3)** strike out [(f) Responsibility of the Secretary.—] and insert (g) Responsibility of the Secretary.—

## § 5.

In section 310(d)(1) of the bill, strike “(A)” and “(B)”, the second time they appear therein, and insert in lieu thereof “(i)” and “(ii)”.

## § 6.

In section 311(b)(1)(B) of the bill strike “the Act” and insert in lieu thereof “this Act”.


*Agreed to March 31, 1976.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 537

> CONSTITUTION OF THE UNITED STATES AND DECLARATION OF INDEPENDENCE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Constitution of the United States, as amended to July 1, 1971, together with the Declaration of Independence, be printed as a House document, with an index, in such form and style as may be directed by the Joint Committee on Printing, and that there be printed two hundred and seventy-two thousand five hundred additional copies of such document, of which two hundred and twenty-one thousand shall be for the use of the House of Representatives and fifty-one thousand five hundred shall be for the use of the Senate.


*Passed May 10, 1976.*

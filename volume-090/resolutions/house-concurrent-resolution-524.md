# House Concurrent Resolution 524

> JOINT MEETING

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on January 19, 1976, at 9 o’clock postmeridian, for the purpose of receiving such communication as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Passed January 19, 1976.*

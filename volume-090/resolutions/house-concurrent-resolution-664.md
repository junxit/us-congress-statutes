# House Concurrent Resolution 664

> “WOMEN IN CONGRESS”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That twenty-five thousand copies of a booklet entitled “Women in Congress” be printed for the use of the Joint Committee on Arrangements for the Commemoration of the Bicentennial. Such booklet shall present a history of women who have served as Members of the Congress.

## § 2.

The Joint Committee on Arrangements for the Commemoration of the Bicentennial shall control the arrangement and style of the booklet authorized to be printed by the first section of this concurrent resolution.


*Passed October 1, 1976.*

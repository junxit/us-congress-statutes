# Senate Concurrent Resolution 85

> ADMINISTRATIVE PROCEDURES OF THE INTERNAL REVENUE SERVICE—REPORT

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That a report by a special consultant to the Administrative Conference of the United States on some administrative procedures of the Internal Revenue Service be printed as a Senate document, and that there be printed five hundred additional copies of such document for the use of the Senate Committee on Appropriations.


*Agreed to September 30, 1976.*

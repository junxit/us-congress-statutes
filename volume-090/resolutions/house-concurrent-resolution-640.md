# House Concurrent Resolution 640

> “FEDERAL ELECTION CAMPAIGN LAWS RELATING TO THE UNITED STATES HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed as a House document, “Federal Election Campaign Laws Relating to the United States House of Representatives”, and that fifteen thousand copies be printed, of which five thousand shall be for the use of the Committee on House Administration of the House and the remaining ten thousand for distribution to candidates for the House of Representatives and for political committees supporting them.


*Passed June 10, 1976.*

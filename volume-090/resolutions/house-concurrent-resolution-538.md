# House Concurrent Resolution 538

> CONSTITUTION OF THE UNITED STATES—POCKET-SIZE EDITION

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed as a House document the Constitution of the United States (pocket-size edition), as amended through July 5, 1971, and that there be printed two hundred and twenty-one thousand additional copies of such document for the use of the House of Representatives.


*Passed May 27, 1976.*

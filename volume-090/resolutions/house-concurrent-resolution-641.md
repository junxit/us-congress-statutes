# House Concurrent Resolution 641

> “NEW PERSPECTIVES IN HEALTH CARE FOR OLDER AMERICANS (RECOMMENDATIONS AND POLICY DIRECTIONS OF THE SUBCOMMITTEE ON HEALTH AND LONG-TERM CARE)”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed for the use of the Select Committee on Aging of the House of Representatives one thousand five hundred additional copies of the report of the Subcommittee on Health Care and Long-Term Care entitled “New Perspectives in Health Care for Older Americans (Recommendations and Policy Directions of the Subcommittee on Health and Long-Term Care)”.


*Passed September 9, 1976.*

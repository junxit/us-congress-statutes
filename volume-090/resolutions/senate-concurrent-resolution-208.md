# Senate Concurrent Resolution 208

> “SOVIET ECONOMY IN A NEW PERSPECTIVE”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Joint Economic Committee five thousand additional copies of its joint committee print of the Ninety-fourth Congress, second session, entitled “Soviet Economy in a New Perspective.


*Agreed to October 1, 1976.*

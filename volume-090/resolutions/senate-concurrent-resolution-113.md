# Senate Concurrent Resolution 113

> “SOVIET SPACE PROGRAMS, 1971–1975”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on Aeronautical and Space Sciences one thousand five hundred additional copies each of volumes 1 and 2 of its committee print entitled “Soviet Space Programs, 1971–1975”, Ninety-fourth Congress, second session, prepared by the Congressional Research Service with the cooperation of the Law Library, Library of Congress.


*Agreed to August 30, 1976.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 305

> “COURT PROCEEDINGS AND ACTIONS OF VITAL INTEREST TO THE CONGRESS, FINAL REPORT FOR THE 93d CONGRESS, DECEMBER 1974”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed for the use of the Joint Committee on Congressional Operations, five hundred additional copies of the committee print entitled, “Court Proceedings and Actions of Vital Interest to the Congress, Final Report for the 93d Congress, December 1974”.


*Passed May 24, 1976.*

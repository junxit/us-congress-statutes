# Senate Concurrent Resolution 133

> CORRECTIONS OF ENROLLED BILL H.R. 11670

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Clerk of the House of Representatives is authorized and directed to make corrections in the enrollment of H.R. 11670 as follows:

That in the second section numbered “6” change the section number to “7”.

That in the section numbered “7” change the section number to “8”.


*Agreed to August 30, 1976.*

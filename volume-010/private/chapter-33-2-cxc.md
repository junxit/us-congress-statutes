# Chapter CXC

> An Act for the Relief of Jesse French, of Braintree, Massachusetts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be hereby instructed to place the name of Jesse French on the pension roll, at the rate of four dollars per month, commencing on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and fifty-two, to be continued during his life.


*Approved, March 3, 1855.*

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

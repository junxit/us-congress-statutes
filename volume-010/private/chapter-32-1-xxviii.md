# Chapter XXVIII

> An Act for the Relief of Albra Tripp.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the name of Albra Tripp, a soldier in the service of the United States in the late war with Great Britain, be placed on the roll of invalid pensions, at the rate of four dollars per month, commencing the first day of December, eighteen hundred and fifty-one, to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, May 4, 1852.*

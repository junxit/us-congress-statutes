# Chapter XCII

> An Act for the Relief of Cornelius H. Latham.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place the name of Cornelius H. Latham, of the State of New York, on the invalid pension roll, at the rate of four dollars per month, to commence on the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and fifty, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, July 17, 1854.*

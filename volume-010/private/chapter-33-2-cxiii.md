# Chapter CXIII

> An Act for the Relief of Medford Caffey, of the State of Tennessee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury cause to be paid to Medford Caffey, of the State of Tennessee, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one hundred and six dollars, in full compensation for his horse and equipage lost in the Seminole campaign of eighteen hundred and eighteen.


*Approved, February 17, 1855.*

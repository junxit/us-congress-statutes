# Chapter LXXIII

> An Act for the Relief of the Executors and Heirs of Thomas Fletcher, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That William M. Sudduth and Henry Daniel, surviving executors, and Felix M. Fletcher, David W. Fletcher, John Fletcher, and Margaret Washington (late Margaret Fletcher) heirs of Thomas Fletcher, deceased, be, and hereby are released and forever discharged from a certain penal bond in the sum of twenty thousand dollars, payable to the United States, executed by the said Thomas Fletcher as one of the securities of Cary Nichols, on the twenty-eighth day of May, in the year one thousand eight hundred and seventeen.


*Approved, July 21, 1852.*

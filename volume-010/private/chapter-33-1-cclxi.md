# Chapter CCLXI

> An Act for the Relief of Warren Raymond.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives o f the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Warren Raymond, of the State of New York, upon the roll of invalid pensioners, at the rate of four dollars per month, to commence on the first day of January, Anno Domini, eighteen hundred and fifty-three, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, August 5, 1854.*

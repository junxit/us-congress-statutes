# Chapter CCXXIII

> An Act for the Relief of Henry J. Snow, of Rome, in the State of New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House o f Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Henry J. Snow, of Rome, in the State of New York, upon the list of invalid pensions of the United States, at the rate of eight dollars per month, to commence on the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and fifty-four, and to continue during his life.


*Approved, August 3, 1854.*

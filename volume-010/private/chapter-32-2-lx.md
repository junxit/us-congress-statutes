# Chapter LX

> An Act for the Relief of Barbara Reily.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be required to pay to Barbara Reily, widow of Captain William Reily, an officer of the army of the Revolution, the amount of pension due him from the time he was last paid, to the fourth day of March, one thousand eight hundred and twenty.


*Approved, February 9, 1853.*

# Chapter CXXXIX

> An Act to create and provide a Pension for David Towle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That David Towle, of the County of Oxford and State of Maine, be, and he is hereby, placed on the pension roll, at eight dollars per month from the fifth day of April, eighteen hundred and forty-eight.


*Approved, July 27, 1854.*

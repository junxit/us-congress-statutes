# Chapter LXXXIV

> An Act for the Relief of the Heirs of Semoice, a friendly Creek Indian.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the children and heirs of Semoice, deceased, a Creek Indian, to wit.; Hetty Deas, Vicy Foxy, and Elizabeth Semoice, be and are hereby vested with a title in fee-simple, to fractional section twenty-three, township four, range two east, containing six hundred and two acres and fifty-three hundredths of an acre, being the same land selected and entered by the said Semoice, under and by virtue of an act approved second July, eighteen hundred and thirty-six, entitled “An Act for the relief of Samuel Smith, Lynn Mac Ghee, and Semoice, friendly Creek Indians:” Provided, That this act shall not be construed to defeat or prejudice the legal claim, if there be any, of other persons to the said tract of land.


*Approved, August 16, 1852.*

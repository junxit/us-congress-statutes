# Chapter XXXVIII

> An Act for the Relief of Sylvanus Blodyet.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby required to place the name of Sylvanus Blodget upon the pension roll, at eight dollars per month; commencing on the seventh day of January, A. D. eighteen hundred and forty-six.


*Approved, May 26, 1852.*

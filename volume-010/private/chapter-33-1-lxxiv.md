# Chapter LXXIV

> An Act for the Relief of Zadoc C. Inghram.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster-General be and he is hereby authorized and directed to cause Zadoc C. Inghram, late postmaster at Wapello, Iowa, to be released from a judgment obtained against him by the United States, in March, eighteen hundred and forty-nine for the sum of three hundred and eighteen dollars and eighty seven cents and all interest and costs.


*Approved, June 29, 1854.*

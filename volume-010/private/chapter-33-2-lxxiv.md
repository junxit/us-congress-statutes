# Chapter LXXIV

> An Act for the Relief of Polly Carver, Widow and Executrix of Nathan Carver, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of three hundred and thirty-four dollars and seventy-nine cents be, and the same hereby is, appropriated out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropri- ated, for the payment and full satisfaction of the claims against the government of the said Polly Carver, as widow and executrix of the said Nathan Carver, deceased.


*Approved, February 13, 1855.*

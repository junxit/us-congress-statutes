# Chapter LXXVIII

> An Act for the Relief of Lincoln Bates.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be directed to pay to Lincoln Bates, out of moneys not otherwise appropriated, the sum of fifty-five dollars, in full of the damages sustained by him as a night watchman in the public stores at New York, in July, one thousand eight hundred and forty-nine, in suppressing a fire therein, being the amount of his account, as now on file in the Treasury Department.


*Approved, February 13, 1855.*

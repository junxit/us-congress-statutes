# Private Resolution 15

> Joint Resolution to correct a clerical error in the Act approved June twenty-second, eighteen hundred and fifty-four, “to authorize a Register to be issued to the steamer ‘El Paraguay’ by a new name.”

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the word “Joy” where it occurs in the “Act to authorize a register to be issued to the steamer ‘El Paraguay’ by a new name,” approved June, twenty-second, eighteen hundred and fifty-four, shall read and be held to mean Ivy.


*Approved, July 17, 1854.*

# Chapter LXXXVII

> An Act for the Relief of Edward Box.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Edward Box, of Tennessee, upon the revolutionary pension roll, at the rate of six dollars and sixty-six and two third cents per month, to commence on the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and forty-two, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, March 2, 1853.*

# Chapter XCVII

> An Act to provide a Pension for Sergeant G. W. Torrence.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Sergeant G. W. Torrence, of Captain Fairchild’s company of Louisiana Mounted Volunteers, be placed upon the pension roll, at the rate of twenty dollars per month, to commence on the fourth day of April, eighteen hundred and fifty-four.


*Approved, July 17, 1854.*

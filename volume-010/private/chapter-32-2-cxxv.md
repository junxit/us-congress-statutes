# Chapter CXXV

> An Act for the Relief of George Poindexter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Quarter-master General of the United States, be and he is hereby authorized and directed to cause to be ascertained the amount justly due and owing to George Poindexter for the use and occupation of his property, by the volunteers from the State of Kentucky, mustered into the service of the United States, for the war in Mexico, and that the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to cause the amount so ascertained to be due, to be paid to the said Poindexter, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated: Provided, That the same do not exceed the sum of fifteen hundred dollars, the amount awarded.


*Approved, March 3, 1853.*

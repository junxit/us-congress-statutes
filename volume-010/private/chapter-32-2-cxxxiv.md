# Chapter CXXXIV

> An Act for the Benefit of John Ozias.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Treasurer of the United States is hereby authorized to pay to John Ozias, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated by law, the sum of two hundred dollars, the amount paid by him on the thirty-first October, eighteen hundred and thirty-five, for a quarter section of land which the government had sold to another.


*Approved, March 3, 1853.*

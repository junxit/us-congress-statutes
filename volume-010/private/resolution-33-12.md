# Resolution 12

> Joint Resolution in favor of William Monagan.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to William Monagan thirty-six dollars and fifty cents, it being one fourth of the moneys awarded by the umpire on the claim of Martin, Sherlock, Monagan, and McCartney, reported to the twenty-third Congress at their first session.


*Approved, February 24, 1855.*

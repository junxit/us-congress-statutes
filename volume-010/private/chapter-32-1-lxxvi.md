# Chapter LXXVI

> An Act for the Relief of the legal Representatives of James C. Watson, of Georgia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Treasurer of the United States pay to the legal representatives of General James C. Watson, late of the State of Georgia, deceased, fourteen thousand six hundred dollars, with six per cent, interest per annum from the eighth of May, eighteen hundred and thirty-eight, till paid, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, being the amount paid by him, under the sanction of the Indian agent, to certain Creek warriors for slaves captured by said warriors, while they were in the service of the United States against the Seminole Indians in Florida.


*Approved, July 30, 1852.*

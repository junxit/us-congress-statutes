# Chapter CLXX

> An Act for the Relief of William Darby.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid to William Darby, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of fifteen hundred dollars, in full compensation for his labor and materials furnished in surveying and making a Map of the Territory of Louisiana, in the years eighteen hundred and twelve and eighteen hundred and thirteen.


*Approved, August 1, 1854.*

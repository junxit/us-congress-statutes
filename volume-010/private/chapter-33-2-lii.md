# Chapter LII

> An Act for the Relief of James Wright, Jr., of the State of Tennessee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension of James Wright, jr., of the State of Tennessee, be increased from five dollars and thirty-three cents to eight dollars per month, from the first day of May, in the year eighteen hundred and fifty-four; and that the Secretary of the Interior be directed to cause him to [be] paid the pension of eight dollars per month, from said first day of May, in the year eighteen hundred and fifty-four, during his life, instead and in lieu of his pension of five dollars and thirty-three cents per month, heretofore paid him.


*Approved, January 31, 1855.*

# Chapter LXXXII

> An Act for the Relief of Sergeant Leonard Skinner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior cause to be issued to Leonard Skinner, late sergeant in Captain Newton’s company (B) of the United States regiment of Mounted Riflemen, a military bounty land warrant for one hundred and sixty acres of land; and that the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby directed and required to adjust his accounts and pay the same in the same manner as if he had been of age and regularly discharged by military authority.


*Approved, August 16, 1852.*

# Chapter CXXXIV

> An Act for the Relief of James Dunning.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officers of the Treasury Department be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to pay to James Dunning two hundred and fifty-five dollars and ninety-eight cents, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, in full, for interest due to him from the United States, on a liquidated amount of thirteen thousand four hundred and seventy-two dollars and sixty-two cents, payable by contract, on the eighteenth day of June, eighteen hundred and fifty, and then duly required, but withheld until the twelfth of October following for want of an appropriation therefor.


*Approved, July 27, 1854.*

# Chapter CCXXXV

> An Act for the Relief of David C. Cash, and Giles V. Ellis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay out. of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to David C. Cash, late lieutenant, and Giles U. Ellis, private in the Seminole war in Florida, whatever may be due them on the muster rolls, for military services in said war.


*Approved, August 4, 1854.*

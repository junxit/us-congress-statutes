# Chapter XCIII

> An Act to provide a Pension for Oliver Brown, of Chemung County, New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Oliver Brown, of the county of Chemung, in the State of New York, be, and he is hereby, required to be placed upon the pension roll, at the rate of four dollars per month, from the first day of March, eighteen hundred and fifty-four.


*Approved, February 13, 1855.*

# Chapter CXLIV

> An Act authorizing a Patent to be issued to Peter Poncin for certain Lands therein described.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the entry by Peter Poncin, of the north half of the southeast quarter, and the south half of the northeast quarter of section thirty-six, in township number twenty-nine, of range twenty-three, in the Stillwater Land District, Minnesota, cancelled by the Commissioner of the General Land-Office, be, and the same is hereby, allowed, and reinstated as of the dale of said entry, so that the title to said lands may enure to the benefit of his grantees as far as he may have conveyed the same: Provided, That the money paid for said lands shall not have been withdrawn, or if withdrawn, shall be again paid at said land-offices, and that thereupon a patent shall issue in the name of said Peter Poncin for Said lands.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the Superintendent of Public Schools in said Territory of Minnesota be, and he is hereby, authorized to select other lands in lieu of said section thirty-six, as far as the same has been granted or sold.


*Approved, July 27, 1854.*

# Chapter CLXXXVIII

> An Act for the Relief of Paul S. Ridgway, of Ohio.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby required to place the name of Paul S. Ridgway, of Ohio, upon the pension roll, and cause to be paid to him the sum of eight dollars per month for and during the term of his natural life, commencing June first, one thousand eight hundred and fifty-four.


*Approved, March 3, 1855.*

# Chapter CXXXII

> An Act for the Relief of Joseph Mitchell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper officers of the government be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to examine the claim of Joseph Mitchell, of the State of Maine, a soldier in the late war with Great Britain, for bounty land and for arrearages of pay and bounty, and that they issue to him a warrant for such land, and pay him such arrearages as would be his due if he had received an honorable discharge on the expiration of his term of enlistment at the close of the war.


*Approved, July 27, 1854.*

# Chapter XC

> An Act granting a Pension to John Le Roy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be allowed and paid to John Le Roy, who was an express rider in the late war with Mexico, a pension of twenty dollars per month, to commence on the twenty-second of February, eighteen hundred and forty-seven, and to continue during life; and that the said pension be paid as all other invalid pensions are paid, out of any money in the treasury not Otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, August 25, 1852.*

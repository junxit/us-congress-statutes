# Chapter LIV

> An Act to authorize the issue of a Register to the Barque Colonist, by the name of “The Lily.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be issued, under the direction of the Secretary of the Treasury, a register to the British brig Colonist, by the name of The Lily, now owned by Christian F. Funch, of the city of New York, in the State of New York: Provided, It shall be proved to the satisfaction of said Secretary that the cost of the repairs made in the United States, after the purchase of the said vessel by the present owner, is equal to three fourths of the value of said vessel at the time of said repairs.


*Approved, January 31, 1855.*

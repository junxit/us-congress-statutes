# Chapter CLII

> An Act for the Relief of the Southern Michigan Railroad Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster-General be authorized, and is hereby directed, to pay to the Southern Michigan Railroad Company, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, the sum of nine hundred and seventy-one dollars and forty-three cents, for services in the transportation of the great Northern mail from Tole do to Hillsdale, beyond Detroit, in the State of Michigan.


*Approved, March 3, 1853.*

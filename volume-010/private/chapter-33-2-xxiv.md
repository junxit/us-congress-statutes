# Chapter XXIV

> An Act for the Relief of Herman Chittenden.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, required to place the name of Herman Chittenden upon the pension roll, and cause to be paid to him the sum of eight dollars per month so long as he shall live, commencing March first, eighteen hundred and fifty-four.


*Approved, January 3, 1855.*

## Marginal notes

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

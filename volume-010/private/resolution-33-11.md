# Resolution 11

> A Resolution for the Relief of Rebecca Hinton.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States District Attorney for the district of Ohio, is hereby authorized to settle the suit pending in the circuit court for said district, against the bail of Otho Hinton, and to discharge the bail bond upon the payment of one thousand dollars, and the taxable costs of said suit.


*Approved, February 24, 1855.*

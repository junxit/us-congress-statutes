# Chapter CXV

> An Act to amend an Act entitled “An Act for the Relief of Joseph Johnston,” approved May fourth, eighteen hundred and fifty-two, by correcting a clerical error.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the clerical error in the aforesaid act be corrected by making the name of Joseph Johnston to read Joseph Johnson, which was intended; and that all the benefits of said act shall accrue to, and the same he, and hereby are, extended to said Joseph Johnson.


*Approved, March 3, 1853.*

# Chapter CLVIII

> An Act for the Relief of Samuel H. Hempstead.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of eighteen hun- dred dollars be, and the same is hereby, appropriated out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to pay Samuel H. Hempstead, for extra services rendered by him in defending the title of the United States to certain lands situated in the State of Arkansas.


*Approved, July 27, 1854.*

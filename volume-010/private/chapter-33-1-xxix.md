# Chapter XXIX

> An Act for the Relief of Gray, McMurdo, and Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of five hundred and seventy dollars and seventy cents be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any moneys in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to be paid to Gray, McMurdo, and Company, of New Orleans, in the State of Louisana, in full of their account for interest on moneys advanced by them in one thousand eight hundred and fifty, to the Quartermaster’s Department.


*Approved, March 27, 1854.*

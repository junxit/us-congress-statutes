# Chapter XXXII

> An Act for the Relief of Jane Irwin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, there shall be allowed and paid to Jane Irwin, the only child of Colonel Jared Irwin, who served in the Georgia State troops from the beginning to the close of the Revolutionary war, as an equivalent for services rendered and losses sustained by him, the half-pay of a captain for the period of thirty-five years, without interest.


*Approved, May 6, 1852.*

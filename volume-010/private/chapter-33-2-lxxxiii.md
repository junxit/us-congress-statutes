# Chapter LXXXIII

> An Act for the Relief of William Gove.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, directed to place the name of William Gove, of the State of Maine, upon the pension rolls, and cause him to be paid the sum of eight dollars per month, so long as he shall live, commencing January first, eighteen hundred and fifty-four.


*Approved, February 13, 1855.*

## Marginal notes

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

# Chapter CXXXVII

> An Act for the Relief of Passed Midshipmen George P. Welsh and Clark H. Wells.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officer of the Treasury pay to Passed Midshipman George P. Welsh three hundred and forty-one dollars and sixty-six cents, and to Passed Midshipman Clark H. Wells three hundred and forty-two dollars and ninety-three cents, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, July 27, 1854.*

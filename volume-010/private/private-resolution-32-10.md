# Private Resolution 10

> A Resolution granting the Right of Way to the Fayetteville and Central Plank-road.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the right of way, not exceeding forty feet in width, through the public land of the United States, at Fayetteville, North Carolina, (which is at present not improved,) be, and the same is hereby, granted to the Fayetteville and Central Plank-road: Provided, That in the opinion of the President of the United States, said grant be not injurious to the public interests. And provided further, That if the said road be not completed in two years, or if at any time after its completion the said road be discontinued or abandoned, the grant hereby made shall cease and determine.


*Approved, May 13, 1852.*

# Chapter XCI

> An Act for the Relief of Lieutenant George H. Paige, of the United States Army.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper accounting officer of the United States treasury be, and he is hereby, directed to allow and credit to Lieutenant George H. Paige the sum of sixty-two dollars and twenty-three cents, the amount paid by him to one Philips, a private in the United States army, for services as an auctioneer, in June, eighteen hundred and forty-nine.


*Approved, February 13, 1855.*

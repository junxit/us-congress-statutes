# Chapter CCXXXVII

> An Act for the Relief of Patrick Gass.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper officers of the Government be, and they are hereby, authorized and required to issue to Patrick Gass a warrant for three hundred and twenty acres of land, which warrant may, at the option of the said Gass or his assignee, be located on any public lands of the United States subject to private entry.


*Approved, August 4, 1854.*

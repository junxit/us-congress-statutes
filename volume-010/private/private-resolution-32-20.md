# Private Resolution 20

> A Resolution for the Relief of the Heirs of David Corderey.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proper officers of the Treasury ascertain and pay to the legal representatives of David Corderey, deceased, the value of a certain reservation of six hundred and forty acres as unimproved land at the date of the treaty of eighteen hundred and thirty-five, which reservation was allowed under the eighth article of the treaty of eighteen hundred and seventeen, and thirteenth article of the treaty of eighteen hundred and thirty-five, made with the Cherokee Indians, and that the amount so ascertained, without interest, be paid out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 3, 1853.*

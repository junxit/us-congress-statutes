# Chapter XXXVI

> An Act for the Relief of Frances P. Gardiner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be required to renew the pension heretofore allowed and paid to Frances P. Gardiner, and to continue the same for and during her natural life, commencing on the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and forty.


*Approved, January 27, 1853.*

# Chapter XXVII

> An Act for the Relief of Joseph Johnston.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby required to place the name of Joseph Johnston, of Addison county, Vermont, on the pension roll, at the rate of eight dollars per month; said allowance to commence on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and fifty, and continue during his natural life.


*Approved, May 4, 1852.*

# Chapter LXIV

> An Act for the Relief of John Steene.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be instructed to place the name of John Steene upon the roll of invalid pensions, and to pay him a pension, at the rate of eight dollars per month, during his natural life, commencing on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and fifty-three.


*Approved, February 10, 1855.*

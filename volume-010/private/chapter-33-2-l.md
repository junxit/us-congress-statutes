# Chapter L

> An Act to Repeal an Act for the Relief of the Legal Representatives of Samuel Prioleau, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the act entitled “An act for the relief of the legal representatives of Samuel Prioleau, deceased,” approved July twenty-seventh, eighteen hundred and fifty-four, be, and the same is hereby repealed.


*Approved, January 31, 1855.*

# Chapter CLIX

> An Act for the Relief of William. G. Howison.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That William G. Howison be allowed by the proper accounting officers of the treasury, the sum of one hundred and sixty-seven dollars, in full for all services as a member of the Auxiliary Guard of the city of Washington.


*Approved, March 2, 1855.*

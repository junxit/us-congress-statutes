# Chapter XXX

> An Act for the Payment of Arrears of Pension to the Guardian of Altermas Conant.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the arrears of pension, now due to Artemas Conant, an invalid pensioner, by an act passed July sixth, eighteen hundred and fifteen, be paid to his guardian for the use of said Conant, at the rate of five dollars and thirty-three and one third cents [$5,334] per month, from March fourth, eighteen hundred and thirty, to April eighteen hundred and forty-six.


*Approved, May 4, 1852.*

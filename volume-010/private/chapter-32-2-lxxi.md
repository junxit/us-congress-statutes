# Chapter LXXI

> An Act to change the Name of the Steamboat Forest City.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the owners of the steamboat “Forest City,” be, and they arc hereby authorized to change the name of the said boat to that of “Bay City,” and to make a new registry of said boat in that name, at the port of Detroit, in the State of Michigan.


*Approved, February 14, 1853.*

# Chapter LXXXV

> An Act for the Relief of B. B. Bennett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the passage of this bill, the name of B. B. Bennett shall be placed upon the roll of invalid pensioners, at the rate of eight dollars per month, for and during his life.


*Approved, March 2, 1853.*

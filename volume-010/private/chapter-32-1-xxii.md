# Chapter XXII

> An Act to authorise the issuing of a Register to the Ship Kossuth.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be issued, under the direction of the Secretary of the Treasury, a register to the ship Kossuth, a Guatemalian vessel, lately called the ship Republica de Guatemalia, which has been purchased by J. B. Stouvenel, a citizen of the United States, while said vessel was lying in the port of New York almost a wreck: Provided, That it shall be proved to the satisfaction of the Secretary of the Treasury that the cost of her repairs made in the United States, after the purchase of the said vessel by her present owner, exceeds three fourths of the original cost of building a vessel of the same tonnage in the United States.


*Approved, April 2, 1852.*

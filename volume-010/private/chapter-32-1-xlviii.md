# Chapter XLVIII

> An Act to authorize the issuing a Register to the Schooner Caroline, of Barnstable.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be issued, under the direction of the Secretary of the Treasury, a register to the schooner Caroline, a British vessel which was wrecked on the coast of the United States in January, eighteen hundred and fifty-two, and purchased by Lewis Crosby, a citizen of the United States, and by him repaired: Provided, It shall be proved to the satisfaction of the Secretary of the Treasury, that the cost of the repairs made in the United States, after the purchase of the vessel by the present owner, shall exceed three fourths of the present value of said vessel, after having been so repaired.


*Approved, June 10, 1852.*

# Chapter XCV

> An Act for the Relief of George J. Rallston.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be instructed to place the name of George J. Rallston upon the pension roll, and to pay him at the rate of eight dollars per month, from the first day of January, eighteen hundred and fifty-four, to continue during his life.


*Approved, February 13, 1855.*

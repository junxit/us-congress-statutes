# Chapter CXCI

> An Act to amend an Act entitled “An Act for the Relief of George M. Bentley, of the State of Indiana” approved August five, eighteen hundred and fifty four.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That no payments shall be made to George M. Bentley after the fourth March, eighteen hundred and fifty-five, under an act entitled “An act for the relief of George M. Bentley, of the State of Indiana,” approved August five, eighteen hundred and fifty-four, until the claim and the right to a pension of the said Bentley shall have been presented to the Commissioner of Pensions, supported by evidence, and by said commissioner approved and allowed under the rules and regulations governing the examination and allowance of invalid pension claims for disabilities contracted in the Mexican war.


*Approved, March 3, 1855.*

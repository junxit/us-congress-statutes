# Chapter XLIX

> An Act for the Relief of Joseph Webb.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension of Joseph Webb be increased so that he shall be entitled to receive, from and after the first day of January, eighteen hundred and fifty-two, the sum of eight dollars per month, during his natural life, in lieu of the pension which he now receives under the act of the twenty-fifth June, eighteen hundred and fifty-four [thirty-four.]


*Approved, January 31, 1855.*

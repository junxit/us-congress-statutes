# Chapter LXIII

> An Act for the Relief of Amos Knapp.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Amos Knapp, from and after the passage of this act, shall be placed upon the roll of invalid pensioners, and shall receive eight dollars per month, the pension of a common soldier.


*Approved, July 12, 1852.*

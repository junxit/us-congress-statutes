# Private Resolution 14

> A Resolution giving the consent of Congress to the acceptance by Lieutenant M. F. Maury, of the Navy, of a Gold Medal from His Majesty the King of Sweden.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Lieutenant M. F. Maury, of the United States Navy, be, and he is hereby, authorized to accept a gold medal recently presented to him by His Majesty the King of Sweden.


*Approved, June 29, 1854.*

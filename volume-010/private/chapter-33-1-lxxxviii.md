# Chapter LXXXVIII

> An Act to change the Name of the American-built Brig “Hallowell” to that of “James Rose,” and to grant her a new Register.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Collector of the Customs of the port of Charleston, in the State of South Carolina, be, and he is hereby, authorized under the direction of the Secretary of the Treasury, to admit to register under the name and title of the “James Rose,” the American-built brig now owned by Hall and Company of said Charleston, and known as the “Hallowell.”


*Approved, July 17, 1854.*

# Chapter CCXXXIV

> An Act for the Relief of Jonathan Pearce.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, required to place the name of Jonathan Pearce, of Muskingum County, Ohio, upon the pension rolls, and cause to be paid to him the sum of eight dollars per month so long as he shall live, commencing January first, eighteen hundred and fifty-two.


*Approved, August 4, 1854.*

## Marginal notes

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

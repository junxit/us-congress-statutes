# Chapter CLXXVII

> An Act granting a Pension to Joseph McCormick.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Joseph McCormick, of the State of Virginia, upon the invalid pension roll, at the rate of four dollars per month, to commence on the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and fifty-four, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, March 3, 1855.*

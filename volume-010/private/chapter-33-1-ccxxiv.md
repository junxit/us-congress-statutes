# Chapter CCXXIV

> An Act to provide a Pension for James K. Welch.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That James K. Welch, of Fulton County, New York, who lost his eye-sight while in the service of the United States, in the line of his duty, be placed upon the pension roll, at eight dollars per month from the first day of January, eighteen hundred and fifty four, to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, August 3, 1854.*

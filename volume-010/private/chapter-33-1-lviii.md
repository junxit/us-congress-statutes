# Chapter LVIII

> An Act for the Relief of the Heirs of Anthony G. Willis, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid to the executors or administrators of Anthony G. Willis, deceased, the sum of two hundred and fifty-one dollars as compensation for the. use by the United States of a wagon and team, the property of said Willis, deceased, during the last war with Great Britain.


*Approved, May 10, 1854.*

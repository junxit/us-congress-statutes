# Chapter CCXIV

> An Act for the Relief of John Frink.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States o f America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster-General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to John Frink, out of any money appropriated for mail transportation, the sum of seven hundred and fifty dollars, the amount of two fines,—one in the third and and the other in the fourth quarter of eighteen hundred and thirty-nine,—wrongfully imposed on said Frink, and deducted from his pay, for carrying the mail on route number two thousand eight hundred and eleven, from Joliet to Danville, Illinois, in said year.


*Approved, August 3, 1854.*

# Chapter LII

> An Act for the Relief of William Bedient, late a Sergeant in the Fourth Regiment of Artillery.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to place the name of William Bedient, at present of Newark, in the State of New Jersey, late a sergeant in the fourth regiment of Artillery, on the roll of invalid pensioners, and that he be allowed a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month from the sixteenth day of June, eighteen hundred and forty-nine, to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, February 3, 1853.*

# Resolution 17

> A Resolution explanatory of an Act entitled “An Act for the Relief of Abigail Stafford.”

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in making payment under the act of Congress entitled “An act for the relief of Abigail Stafford,” the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby directed to settle the same in conformity with the resolution passed by Congress on the third day of June, seventeen hundred and eighty-four.


*Approved, March 3, 1855.*

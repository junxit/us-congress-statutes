# Chapter XXV

> An Act to change the Time of holding the United States Distinct Courts in Alabama, and for other Purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the District Courts of the United States for the State of Alabama shall be held in each and every year as follows: At Mobile, on the fourth Monday in April and the second Monday after the fourth Monday in November; at Huntsville, on the second Monday in May and the second Monday in November; and at Montgomery, on the fourth Monday in May and the fourth Monday in November.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the county of Butler shall hereafter form a part of, and be embraced in the middle district of said State.


*Approved, May 4, 1852.*

# Chapter LXXXI

> An Act to provide for the Continuation of the Military Road from Myrtle Creek to Scottsburgh, in Oregon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of twenty thousand dollars be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the extension and completion of the Military Road from Myrtle Creek, in Umpqua Valley, to Scottsburgh, in the Territory of Oregon; the said road to be constructed under the direction of the Secretary of War.


*Approved, July 17, 1854.*

# Chapter CIII

> An Act to amend “An Act making Appropriations for the Improvement of certain Harbors and Rivers,” approved August thirtieth, eighteen hundred and fifty-two.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the section reading as follows: “For reopening a communication between Albemarle Sound, North Carolina, and the Atlantic Ocean, by the construction of a break water across Croatan Sound, fifty thousand dollars,” be amended by striking out the words “by the construction of a breakwater across Croatan Sound.”


*Approved, February 14, 1855.*

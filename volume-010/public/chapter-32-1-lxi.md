# Chapter LXI

> An Act to enable the Legislature of the State of Indiana to dispose of the unsold Saline Lands in said State.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That so much of the act of Congress entitled “An act to authorize the Legislature of the State of Indiana to sell and convey certain lands granted to said State for the use of the people thereof,” approved July third, eighteen hundred and thirty-two, as provides that said lands shall not be sold for a less price than [that] at which the public lands are sold, be, and the same is hereby repealed.


*Approved, July 12, 1852.*

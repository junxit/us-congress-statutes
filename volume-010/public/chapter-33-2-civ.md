# Chapter CIV

> An Act for the Construction of a Military Road in Oregon Territory.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of thirty thousand dollars be, and the same is hereby, appropriated for the construction of a military road from Astoria to Salem, in the Territory of Oregon; the said, road to be constructed under the direction of the Secretary of War, pursuant to contracts to be made by him.


*Approved, February 17, 1855.*

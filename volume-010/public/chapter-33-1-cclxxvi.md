# Chapter CCLXXVI

> An Act to establish a Land District in the State of Florida, to be called the District of Tampa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all that part of the land in the established districts of Neumansville and St. Augustine in the State of Florida, lying south of the line dividing townships nineteen and twenty, south, be, and the same is hereby created a land district, to be called the District of Tampa, the office for which shall be at Tampa.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That this act shall take effect at the expiration of six months from the day of its passage.


*Approved, August 5, 1854.*

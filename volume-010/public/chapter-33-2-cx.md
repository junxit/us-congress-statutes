# Chapter CX

> An Act making Appropriations for improving certain Military Roads in the Territory of Minnesota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the following sums of money be, and they are hereby, appropriated, for improving certain military roads in the Territory of Minnesota, to wit:—

For cutting out the timber on the territorial road from the Falls of St. Anthony to Fort Ridgely, five thousand dollars.

For cutting out the timber on the road from Fort Ripley, via Crow Wing River, to the point where said road intersects the main road leading to the Red River of the north, ten thousand dollars.

The aforesaid sums to be expended under the direction of the Secretary of War.


*Approved, February 17, 1855.*

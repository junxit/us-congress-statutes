# Chapter CXXXVII

> An Act to amend “An Act to establish a Land District in the State of Florida, to be called the District of Tampa.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized to appoint, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, a register and a receiver for the land district called Tampa, in the State of Florida, which district was created by the act approved August fifth, eighteen hundred and fifty-four, whose compensation, duties, responsibilities, and emoluments shall be the same as is or may be pre scribed by law for other land officers in said State.


*Approved, March 2, 1855.*

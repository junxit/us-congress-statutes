# Chapter XXXVI

> An Act to regulate the Mileage of the Delegate from the Territory of Oregon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That so much of the act entitled “An act to establish the Territorial Government of Oregon,” approved August, eighteen hundred and forty-eight, as limits the mileage compensation of the delegate from said Territory, be repealed.


*Approved, May 19, 1852.*

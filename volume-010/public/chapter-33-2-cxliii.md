# Chapter CXLIII

> An Act changing the Times of holding the United States Courts in Tennessee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That instead of the times heretofore fixed by law, the circuit and district courts of the United States for the several districts in Tennessee shall hereafter be held as follows: At Jackson, on the third Mondays in March and September; at Knoxville, on the second Mondays in April and October; and at Nashville, on the first Mondays in May and November. And all writs, pleas, suits, recognizances, indictments, or other proceedings, civil or criminal, issued, commenced, or pending in either of said courts, shall be returnable to, be entered, and have day in court, and be heard and tried according to the times of holding said courts as herein provided.


*Approved, March 2, 1855.*

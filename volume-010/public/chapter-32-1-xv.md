# Chapter XV

> An Act to provide for the Repair of the Congressional Library Room, lately destroyed by Fire.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of seventy-two thousand five hundred dollars be, and the same is hereby appropriated to the repair of the Congressional Library room, whicl was lately destroyed by fire, according to the plan described in the report and drawings which were submitted by the architect to the Secretary of the Interior, and approved by the Committee on Public Buildings of the Senate: Provided, however, That the work shall be executed under the direction of the Secretary of the Interior, and be subject tc such a modification of the details as may be consistent with the general arrangements of the plan, and necessary and proper in the opinion of the President of the United States.


*Approved, March 19, 1852.*

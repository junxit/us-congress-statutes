# Chapter II

> An Act to provide a Room for the Congressional Library.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of twelve hundred dollars be hereby appropriated, to be expended under the direction of the Commissioner of Public Buildings, for the purpose of fitting up the document room and a portion of the adjoining passage to receive temporarily a portion of the books of the Congressional Library.


*Approved, January 23, 1852.*

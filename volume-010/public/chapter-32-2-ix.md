# Chapter IX

> An Act for the Construction of Military Roads in Oregon Territory.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the following sums of money be, and the same are hereby appropriated, for the construction of military roads in the Territory of Oregon, to wit: for the construction of a road from Stilacoomb, on Puget’s Sound to Fort Walla-Walla, twenty thousand dollars; and for the construction of a road from the mouth of Myrtle Creek, on the Umpqua River, to Camp Stuart, in Rogue River Valley, twenty thousand dollars; the said roads to be constructed under the direction of the Secretary of War, pursuant to contracts to be made by him.


*Approved, January 7, 1853.*

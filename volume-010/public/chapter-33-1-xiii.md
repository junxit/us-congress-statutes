# Chapter XIII

> An Act to Extend the Limits of the Port of New Orleans.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the port of New Orleans be so extended as to embrace the right bank of the Mississippi river, for the same distance up said bank as it now extends on the left bank.


*Approved, February 23, 1854.*

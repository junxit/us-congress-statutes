# Resolution 25

> A Joint Resolution making Appropriation for the Payment of those entitled to the Benefits of the Resolution of the House of Representatives of the United States, passed August third, eighteen hundred and fifty-four, voting Extra Compensation to Pages, Folders, and others.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum appropriated by the House of Representatives on this day for the payment of its pages and employees, be paid out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, August 5, 1854.*

# Resolution 10

> A Resolution authorizing the Secretary of the Treasury to pay the Expenses of Codifying and Revising the Revenue Laws.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be, and hereby is, appropriated out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropri-ated, five thousand dollars, or so much thereof as may be necessary to pay for preparing a general revenue law in accordance with a resolution of the Senate passed in January, one thousand eight hundred and fifty-three, and the necessary expenses, and that the Secretary of the Treasury report to Congress at its meeting in December next, the items of such pay and expenditures.


*Approved, April 6, 1854.*

# Resolution 16

> Joint Resolution explanatory of the Second Section of “A Resolution to Establish certain Post Routes,” approved July twelve, one thousand eight hundred and fifty-two.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second section of the joint resolution to establish certain post-routes, approved July twelve, one thousand eight hundred and fifty-two, be so construed as to authorize the Postmaster-General to pay a reasonable compensation to the persons carrying the mail from Valonia Springs, by the way of Ninevah and Coventry, to Oxford, and from Oxford, by the way of Coventryville, to South Bainbridge, in the State of New York, from the time the mail was directed to be carried on said routes, up to the time the first contracts went into operation thereon, exclusive of what has been paid under said resolution, and at the same rate.


*Approved, July 17, 1854.*

# Resolution 13

> Joint Resolution directing the Connection of the Public Surveys in Alabama with the Boundary Line between the States of Alabama and Florida.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the connection of the public surveys in Alabama, with the boundary line between the States of Alabama and Florida, shall be made under the direction of the General Land-Office, and that the same be executed as early as practicable.


*Approved, June 29, 1854.*

# Public Resolution 23

> Joint Resolution making an Appropriation for the Purchase of Territorial Libraries.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That five thousand dollars be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, to be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the purchase of Territorial libraries for each of the Territories of Kansas and Nebraska.


*Approved, March 3, 1855.*

# Resolution 1

> A Resolution of Welcome to Louis Kossuth.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Congress, in the name and behalf of the People of the United States, give to Louis Kossuth a cordial welcome to the Capital and the country; and that a copy of this resolution be transmitted to him by the President of the United States.


*Approved, December 15, 1851.*

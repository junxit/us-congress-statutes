# Resolution 16

> A Joint Resolution providing for the Distribution of the Laws of Congress, and the Debates thereon.

*Be it resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

With a view to the cheap circulation of the Laws of Congress, and the debates contributing to the true interpretation thereof, and to make free the communication between the representative and constituent bodies,

That from and after the present session of Congress, the Congressional Globe and Appendix, which contain the laws and the debates thereon, shall pass free through the mails so long as the same shall be published by order of Congress: Provided, That nothing herein shall be construed to authorize the circulation of the Daily Globe free of postage.


*Approved, August 6, 1852.*

# Public Resolution 14

> A Resolution in Relation to the New Orleans Custom-House.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That authority be given to the Secretary of the Treasury to make such change in the mode, of constructing the New Orleans custom-house as will best, in his judgment, lighten the foundations from the great weight of the superstructure.


*Approved, March 2, 1855.*

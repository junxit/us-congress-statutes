# Resolution 13

> A Joint Resolution accepting from Guiseppe Fagnani, a Portrait of Henry Clay, and ordering it to be placed in the Library of Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the portrait of Henry Clay, presented to the nation by Guiseppe Fagnani, a resident of New York, be placed in the Library of Congress.


*Approved, July 3, 1852.*

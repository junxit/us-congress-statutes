# Public Resolution 24

> A Resolution explanatory of an Act passed August third, eighteen hundred and fifty-four.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That it was the intention of the act of Congress, approved August third, eighteen hundred and fifty-four, and the same shall be construed, to give to Wisconsin in aid of the improvement of the navigation of the Fox and Wisconsin Rivers, a quantity of land, equal mile for mile of its improvement to that granted to Indiana, under the provisions of the Act of Congress approved May the ninth, eighteen hundred and forty-eight.


*Approved, March 3, 1855.*

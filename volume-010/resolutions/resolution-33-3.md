# Resolution 3

> A Resolution authorizing an Increase of the Force in the Office of the Superintendent of the Public Printing.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Superintendent of the Public Printing be, and he is hereby authorized to increase the force in his office by the appointment of two additional clerks, at the same salary per annum now allowed the clerks employed therein.


*Approved, February 10, 1854.*

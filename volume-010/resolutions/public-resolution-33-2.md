# Public Resolution 2

> Joint Resolution to modify or change the original Plan of the Custom-House at New Orleans.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proviso attached to the appropriations for the New Orleans custom-house of March third, eighteen hundred and fifty-one, August thirty-first, eighteen hundred and fifty-two, and March third, eighteen hundred and fifty-three, be so modified as to allow the substitution of wrought-iron beams with light segmental brick arches, instead of the present heavy groined ones in the upper story; and also that, instead of the marble roof contemplated by the original plan, authority be given to substitute such other suitable fireproof roof as shall be found best calculated to relieve the walls from superfluous weight.


*Approved, December 19, 1854.*

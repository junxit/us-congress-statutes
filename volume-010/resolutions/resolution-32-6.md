# Resolution 6

> A Resolution for the Appointment of Regents in the Smithsonian Institution.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the vacancies in the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution of the class “other than members of Congress,” be filled by the appointment of Alexander Dallas Bache, a member of the National Institute, and resident in the City of Washington, and John MacPherson Berrien, of the State of Georgia.


*Approved, January 13, 1853.*

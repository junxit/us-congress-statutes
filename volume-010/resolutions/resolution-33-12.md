# Resolution 12

> A Resolution for Extending an existing Contract for carrying the Mail in Alabama.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster-General be, and he hereby is, authorized to extend the existing contract for carrying the mail upon the route between Montgomery and Mobile, for four years from the time at which said contract would expire by its own limitation, if, in his opinion, the public interest and convenience will be promoted by such extension of said contract.


*Approved, May 3, 1854.*

# Public Law 93–214

> AN ACT To authorize the disposal of copper from the national stockpile and the supplemental stockpile.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, the Administrator of General Services is hereby authorized to dispose of, by negotiation or otherwise, approximately two hundred and fifty-one thousand six hundred short tons of copper now held in the national stockpile established pursuant to the Strategic and Critical Materials Stock Piling Act (50 U.S.C. 98–9811) and the supplemental stockpile established pursuant to section 104(b) of the Agricultural Trade Development and Assistance Act of 1954 (68 Stat. 456, as amended by 73 Stat. 607). Such disposition may be made without regard to the requirements of section 3 of the Strategic and Critical Materials Stock Piling Act: Provided, That the time and method of disposition shall be fixed with due regard to the protection of the United States against avoidable loss and the protection of producers, processors, and consumers against avoidable disruption of their usual markets.


*Approved December 28, 1973.*

# Public Law 93–41

> JOINT RESOLUTION Authorizing the President to proclaim June 17, 1973, as a day of commemoration of the opening of the upper Mississippi River by Jacques Marquette and Louis Jolliet in 1673.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States is authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating June 17, 1973, as a day of commemoration of the opening of the upper Mississippi River by Jacques Marquette and Louis Jolliet, and calling upon the people of the United States to observe the tercentenary with appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Approved June 14, 1973.*

# Public Law 93–202

> AN ACT To postpone the implementation of the Headstart fee schedule.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the last sentence of section 222(a)(1) of the Economic Opportunity Act of 1904 is amended to read as follows: “The Secretary shall defer the implementation of a fee schedule established under this paragraph until July 1, 1975.”.


*Approved December 28, 1973.*

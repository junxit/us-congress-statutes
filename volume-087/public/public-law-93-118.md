# Public Law 93–118

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making further continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 1974, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That clause (c) of section 102 of the joint resolution of July 1, 1973 (Public Law 93–52), is hereby amended by striking out “September 30, 1973” and inserting in lieu thereof “October 11, 1973”.


*Approved October 4, 1973.*

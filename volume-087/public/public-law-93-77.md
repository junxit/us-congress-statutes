# Public Law 93–77

> AN ACT To continue until the close of June 30, 1974, the suspension of duties on certain forms of copper.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** items 911.10 (relating to copper waste and scrap), 911.11 (relating to articles of copper), 911.13 (relating to copper bearing ores and materials), 911.14 (relating to cement copper and copper precipitates), 911.15 (relating to black copper, blister copper, and anode copper), and 911.16 (relating to other unwrought copper) of the Tariff Schedules of the United States (19 U.S.C. 1202) are each amended by striking out “6/30/72” and inserting in lieu thereof “6/30/74”.
- **(b)** Headnote 3 of subpart B of part 1 of the appendix to such schedules is amended by striking out “36” each place it appears therein and inserting in lieu thereof “51”.

## § 2.

The amendments made by the first section of this Act shall apply with respect to articles entered, or withdrawn from warehouse, for consumption on or after July 1, 1973.


*Approved July 30, 1973.*

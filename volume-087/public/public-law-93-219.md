# Public Law 93–219

> AN ACT To authorize the disposal of molybdenum from the national stockpile, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Administrator of General Services is hereby authorized to dispose of, by negotiation or otherwise, approximately thirty-six million five hundred thousand pounds (molybdenum disulphide equivalent) of molybdenum now held in the national stockpile established pursuant to the Strategic and Critical Materials Stock Piling Act (50 U.S.C. 98–9811). Such disposition may be made without regard to the requirements of section 3 of the Strategic and Critical Materials Stock Piling Act; Provided, That the time and method of disposition shall be fixed with due regard to the protection of the United States against avoidable loss and the protection of producers, processors, and consumers against avoidable disruption of their usual markets.


*Approved December 28, 1973.*

# Public Law 93–232

> JOINT RESOLUTION Authorizing the President to proclaim March 20, 1974, as “Vietnam Veterans Day”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is hereby authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating March 29, 1974, as “Vietnam Veterans Day”, and calling upon the people of the United States to observe such day with appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Approved December 29, 1973.*

# Public Law 93–40

> AN ACT To intend Public Law 90–553 authorizing an additional appropriation for gin International Outer for Foreign Chanceries.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first sentence of section 6 of Public Law 90–553 (82 Stat. 958) is hereby amended to read as follows:

> “There is hereby authorized to be appropriated, without fiscal year limitation, not to exceed $2.200,(Hit) to carry out the purposes of section 5 of this Act: Provided, That such sums as may be appropriated hereunder shall be reimbursed to the Treasury from proceeds of the sale or lease of property to foreign governments and international organizations as provided for in the first section of this Act.”


*Approved June 12, 1973.*

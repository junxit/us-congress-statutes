# Public Law 93–128

> AN ACT To amend the Art of August 31, 1965, commemorating certain historical events In the State of Kansas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4 of the Act of August 31, 1965 (79 Stat. 588), is amended to read as follows:

> ## § 4.
>
> In addition to those funds heretofore authorized and appropriated, there are authorized to be appropriated for the purposes of this Act not more than $1,420,000. Such sums, when appropriated, shall be available for planning, site rehabilitation, development, and marking of historic sites pursuant to the provisions of this Act.”


*Approved October 18, 1973.*

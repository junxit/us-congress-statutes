# Public Law 93–57

> AN ACT To amend the Service Contract Act of 1965 to extend its geographical coverage to contracts performed on Canton Island.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 8(d) of the Service Contract Act of 1965 (41 U.S.C. 357(d)) is amended by inserting “and Canton Island,” between “Johnson Island,” and “but”.

## § 2. effective date

The amendment made hereby shall be effective with respect to all contracts entered into at any time after the date of enactment.


*Approved July 6, 1973.*

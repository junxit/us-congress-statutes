# Public Law 93–7

> JOINT RESOLUTION Relating to the date for the submission of the report of the Joint Economic Committee on the President’s Economic Report.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Public Law 1, Ninety-third Congress, is amended by striking out “March 10, 1973” and inserting in lieu thereof “April 1, 1973”.


*Approved February 16, 1973.*

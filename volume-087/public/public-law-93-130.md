# Public Law 93–130

> AN ACT To provide for the filling of vacancies in the Legislature of the Virgin Islands.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 6(h) of the Revised Organic Act of the Virgin Islands (48 U.S.C. 1572(h)) is amended to read as follows:

> - **(h)** The Legislature of the Virgin Islands shall by law provide the procedure for filling any vacancy in the office of member of the legislature.”

## § 2.

The amendment made by the first section of this Act shall apply with respect to vacancies occurring on or niter the date of enactment, of this Act.


*Approved October 19, 1973.*

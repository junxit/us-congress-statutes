# Public Law 93–169

> AN ACT To amend title 10, United States Code, to remove the four-year limitation on additional active duty that a nonregular officer of the Army or Air Force may be required to perform on completion of training at an educational institution.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 4301 (b) and 9301(b) of title 10, United States Code, are each amended by striking out “but not longer than four years” in the sentence.


*Approved November 29, 1973.*

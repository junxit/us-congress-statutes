# Public Law 93–34

> JOINT RESOLUTION To provide a temporary extension of the authorization for the President’s National Commission on Productivity.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4(f) of Public Law 92–210, approved December 22, 1971, is amended by striking out “April 30, 1973” and inserting in lieu thereof “June 30, 1973”.


*Approved May 14, 1973.*

# Public Law 93–49

> AN ACT To authorize additional appropriations to carry out the Peace Corps Act, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first phrase of section 3(b) of the Peace Corps Act (22 U.S.C. 2502(b)), ending with a colon, is amended to read as follows: “There are authorized to be appropriated to the President for the fiscal year 1974 not to exceed $77,001,000 to carry out the purposes of this Act”.

## § 2.

Section 10(d) of the Peace Corps Act (22 U.S.C. 2509(d)) is amended by inserting immediately after “other than”, the following: “section 3709 of the Revised Statutes of the United States, as amended, section 302 of the Federal Property and Administrative Services Act of 1949, and”.


*Approved June 25, 1973.*

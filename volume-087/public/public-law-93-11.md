# Public Law 93–11

> AN ACT To amend the joint resolution establishing the American Revolution Bicentennial Commission, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 7(a) of tire joint resolution to establish the American devolution Bicentennial Commission, and for other purposes, approved July 4, 1966 (80 Stat. 261), as amended, is further amended by striking “until February 15, 1973” and inserting in lieu thereof “between February 16, 1973, and June 30, 1973”.


*Approved March 15, 1973.*

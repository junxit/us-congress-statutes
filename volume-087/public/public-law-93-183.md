# Public Law 93–183

> AN ACT To name the headquarters building in the Geological Survey National Center under construction in Reston, Virginia, as the “John Wesley Powell Federal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the headquarters building in the Department of Interior’s Geological Survey National Center now under construction in Reston, Virginia, shall hereafter be known and designated as the “John Wesley Powell Federal Building”. Any reference in a law, map. regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to such building shall be held to be a reference to the “John Wesley Powell Federal Building”.


*Approved December 15, 1973.*

# Public Law 93–23

> JOINT RESOLUTION Asking the President of the United States to declare the fourth Saturday of September, 1973, “National Hunting and Fishing Day”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States declare the fourth Saturday of September, 1973, its “National Hunting and Fishing Day” to provide that deserved national recognition, to recognize the esthetic, health, and recreational virtues of hunting anti fishing, to dramatize the continued need for gun and boat safety, and to rededicate ourselves to the conservation and respectful use of out wildlife and natural resources.


*Approved April 20, 1973.*

# Public Law 93–63

> JOINT RESOLUTION To provide for an extension of certain laws relating to the payment of interest on time and savings deposits.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 7 of the Act of September 21, 1966 (Public Law 89–597; 80 Stat. 823), is amended by striking out “June 1, 1973” and inserting in lieu thereof “August 1, 1973”.


*Approved July 6, 1973.*

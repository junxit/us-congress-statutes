# Public Law 93–59

> AN ACT To further amend the United States Information and Educational Exchange Act of 1948.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Howe of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 703 of the United States Information and Educational Exchange Act of 1948, as amended, is hereby amended to insert the following sentence between the first and second sentence thereof: “There are further authorized to be appropriated in fiscal year 1973 not to exceed $1,15’0,000 for nondiscretionary costs.”.


*Approved July 6, 1973.*

# Public Law 93–31

> AN ACT To amend section 236 of the Central Intelligence Agency Retirement Act of 1064 for Certain Employees to limit the number of employees that may be retired under such Act during specified periods.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 236 of the Central Intelligence Agency Retirement Act of 1964 for Certain Employees, as amended (78 Stat. 1043; 50 U.S.C. 403 note), is amended by (1) striking out “eight hundred” and inserting in lieu thereof “twenty-one hundred”, and (2) striking out the period at the end of such section and inserting in lieu thereof a comma and the following: “nor a total of fifteen hundred during the period beginning on July 1, 1974, and ending on June 30, 1979.”


*Approved May 8, 1973.*

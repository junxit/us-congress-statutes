# Public Law 93–10

> JOINT RESOLUTION To provide for Die designation of the second full calendar week in March 1973 as “National Employ the Older Worker Week”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is authorized and requested to issue a proclamation designating the second full calendar week in March of 1973 as “National Employ the Older Worker Week”, and calling upon employer and employee organizations, other organizations officially concerned with employment, and upon all the people of the United States to observe such week with appropriate ceremonies, activities, mid programs designed to decrease employment discrimination in employment because of age.


*Approved March 15, 1973.*

# Public Law 93–185

> AN ACT To provide for increasing the amount of interest paid on the permanent fund of tire United States Soldiers’ and Airmen’s Home.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 8 of the Act of March 3, 1883, chapter 130 (24 U.S.C. 46) is amended by striking out “the rate of 3 per centum per annum,” and inserting in place thereof “a rate determined by the Secretary of the Treasury, taking into consideration the current average market yield on outstanding marketable obligations of the United States with remaining periods to maturity comparable to the average maturities of such investments, adjusted to the nearest one-eighth of 1 per centum,”.


*Approved December 15, 1973.*

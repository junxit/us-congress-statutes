# Private Law 93–24

> AN ACT For the relief of Sun Hwa Koo Kim.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United State# of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Sun Hwa Koo Kim shall be held and considered to have been lawfully admitted to the United States for permanent residence as of the date of the enactment of this Act, upon the payment of the required visa fee. Upon the granting of permanent residence to such alien as provided for in this Act, the Secretary of State shall instruct the proper officer to deduct one number from the total number of immigrant visas and conditional entries which are made available to natives of the country of the alien’s birth under paragraphs (1) through (8) of section 203(a) of the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved December 5, 1973.*

# Private Law 93–7

> AN ACT For the relief of Kuay Ten Chang ( Kuay Hong Chang).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Kuay Ten Chang (Kuay Hong Chang) shall be held and considered to be within the purview of section 203(a) (2) of that Act and the provisions of section 204 of that Act shall not be applicable in this case.


*Approved October 18, 1973.*

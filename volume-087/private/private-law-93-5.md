# Private Law 93–5

> AN ACT For the relief of Guido Bellanca.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of section 101(a) (27) (B) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Guido Bellanca shall be held and considered to have been a returning resident alien at the time of his admission to the United States on June 3, 1971


*Approved October 9, 1973.*

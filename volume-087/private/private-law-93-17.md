# Private Law 93–17

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Purita Paningbatan Bohannon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United State,s of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Purita Paningbatan Bohannon, the widow of a United States citizen, shall be determined to be an immediate relative within the meaning of section 201(b) of that Act and the provisions of section 204 shall be inapplicable in her case.


*Approved October 19, 1973.*

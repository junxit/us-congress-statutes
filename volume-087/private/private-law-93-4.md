# Private Law 93–4

> AN ACT For the relief of Slobodan Babic.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203 (a)(1) and 204 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Slobodan Babic shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien son of Petar Babic, a citizen of the United States: Provided, That the natural mother of the beneficiary, shall not. by virtue of such parentage, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved October 1, 1973.*

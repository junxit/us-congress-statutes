# Private Law 93–15

> AN ACT For Tile relief of Jean Al berth a Service Gordon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act. Jean Álbertha Service Gordon may be classified as a child within the meaning of section 101(b) (1) (F) of the Act, and a petition filed in her behalf by Mrs. Laurel Service Gordon, a citizen of the United States, may be approved pursuant to section 204 of the Act and the provisions of section 245(c) of the Act shall be inapplicable in this case: Provided, That the natural parents, or brothers, or sisters, of the beneficiary shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status, under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved October 19, 1973.*

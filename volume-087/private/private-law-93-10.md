# Private Law 93–10

> AN ACT For the relief of Manuela Bonito Martin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, for the purposes of sections 203(a) (1) and 204 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Manuela Bonito Martin shall be held and considered to be the natural-born alien daughter of Richard Allen Martin, a citizen of the United States: Provided, That the natural parents or brothers or sisters of the beneficiary shall not, by virtue of such relationship, be accorded any right, privilege, or status under the Immigration and Nationality Act.


*Approved October 19, 1973.*

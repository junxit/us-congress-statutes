# Private Law 93–16

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Nguyen Thi Le Fintland and Susan Flatland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the. United Styles of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Nguyen Thi Le Fintland and Susan Fintland, the widow and stepdaughter, respectively, of a citizen of the United States, shall be held and considered to be within the purview of section 201(b) of that Act and the provisions of section 204 of such Act shall not be applicable in their cases.


*Approved October 19, 1973.*

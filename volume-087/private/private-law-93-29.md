# Private Law 93–29

> AN ACT For the relief of Mrs. Bruna Turni, Graziella Turni, and Antonello Turni.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and /douse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Mrs. Bruna Turni, Graziella Turni, and Antonello Turni shall be deemed to have a priority date of August 15, 1969, on the fifth preference foreign stale limitation for Italy.


*Approved December 5, 1973.*

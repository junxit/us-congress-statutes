# Private Law 93–37

> AN ACT For the relief of Eugenia C. Lyttle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, on such terms as it deems just, the United States Postal Service is authorized to compromise, release, or discharge in whole or in part the liability of Eugenia C. Lyttle, former postmaster at the Manchester, Kentucky, Post Office, to the United States for the loss resulting from a burglary at the Manchester Post Office on the night of May 11 and 12, 1969.


*Approved December 8, 1973.*

# Private Law 93–38

> AN ACT For the relief of Luther V. Winstead.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, on such terms as it deems just, the United States Postal Service is authorized to compromise, release, or discharge in whole or part the joint and several liability of Luther V. Winstead, postmaster at the Clinton, Maryland, Post Office, for a deficiency in the amount of $17,408.82 in the postal funds and accountable papers resulting from a burglary at the Clinton Post Office in Clinton, Maryland, on May 6, 1967.


*Approved December 8, 1973.*

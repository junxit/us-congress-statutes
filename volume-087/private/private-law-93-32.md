# Private Law 93–32

> AN ACT For the relief of Edgar P. Faulkner and Hay H. New.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, on such terms as it deems just, the United States Postal Service is authorized to compromise, release, or discharge in whole or in part the joint and several liability of Edgar P. Faulkner, as Postmaster and of Ray 11. New, as Assistant Postmaster, at the Tucker, Georgia, Post Office, to the United States for the loss resulting from the burglary at that post office on the night of June 19, 1969.


*Approved December 8, 1973.*

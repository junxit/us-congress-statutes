# Private Law 93–34

> AN ACT For the relief of Joseph C. Leeba.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, on such terms as it deems just, the United States Postal Service is hereby authorized to compromise, release, or discharge in whole or in part the liability of Joseph C. Leeba, superintendent of the West Side Postal Station, Youngstown. Ohio, to the United States for the loss resulting from the burglary at that postal station on February 22, 1969.


*Approved December 8, 1973.*

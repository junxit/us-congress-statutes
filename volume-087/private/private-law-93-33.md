# Private Law 93–33

> AN ACT For tile relief of Hazel IV. Law non and Lloyd C. Johnson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, on such terms as it deems just, the United States Postal Service is authorized to compromise, release, or discharge in whole or in part the joint and several liability of Hazel W. Lawson, postmaster, and of Lloyd C. Johnson, assistant postmaster, of the Avondale Estates Post Office, Avondale, Georgia, to the United States for the loss resulting from a burglary at that post office on July 3, 1967.


*Approved December 8, 1973.*

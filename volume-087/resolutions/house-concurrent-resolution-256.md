# House Concurrent Resolution 256

> “OUR AMERICAN GOVERNMENT. WHAT IS IT? HOW DOES IT WORK?”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed, as a House, document, a revised edition of the House document “Our American Government. What Is It? How Does It Work?”; and that two hundred and seventy-two thousand five hundred additional copies be printed, of which two hundred and twenty-one thousand shall be for the use of the House of Representatives and fifty-one thousand five hundred shall be for the use of the Senate.

## § 2.

Copies of such document shall be prorated to Members of the Senate and the House of Representatives for a period of sixty days, after which the unused balance shall revert to the respective Senate and House document rooms.


*Passed July 20, 1973.*

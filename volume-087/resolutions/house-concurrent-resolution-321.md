# House Concurrent Resolution 321

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

*Resolved by the /louse of Representatives (the Senate, concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Thursday, October 4, 1973, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Tuesday, October 9, 1973.


*Passed October 4, 1973.*

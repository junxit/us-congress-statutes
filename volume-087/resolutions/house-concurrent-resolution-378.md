# House Concurrent Resolution 378

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Thursday, November 15, 1973, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Monday, November 26, 1973, and that when the Senate adjourns on Wednesday, November 21, 1973, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Monday, November 26, 1973.


*Passed November 14, 1973.*

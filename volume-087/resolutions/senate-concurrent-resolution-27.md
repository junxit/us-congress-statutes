# Senate Concurrent Resolution 27

> HONOR AMERICA—FLAG DAY TO INDEPENDENCE DAY

*Resolved by the Senate, (the. House of Representatives concurring),*

That Congress declares the twenty-one days from Flag Day, June 14, 1973, to Independence Day, July 4, 1973, as a period to honor America, and let there be public gatherings and activities at which the people of the United States can celebrate and honor their country in appropriate manner.


*Agreed to June 15, 1973.*

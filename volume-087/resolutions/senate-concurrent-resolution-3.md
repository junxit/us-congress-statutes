# Senate Concurrent Resolution 3

> “SATURDAY NIGHT SPECIAL HANDGUNS, S. 2507”—HEARINGS

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Represent at ices concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Committee on the Judiciary three thousand additional copies of the hearings of its Sub-committee To Investigate Juvenile Delinquency during the Ninety-second Congress, first session, entitled “Saturday Night Special Handguns, S. 2507”.


*Agreed to April 19, 1973,*

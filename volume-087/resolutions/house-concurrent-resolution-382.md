# House Concurrent Resolution 382

> CORRECTION OF ENROLLED BILL H. R. 7446

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Clerk of the House of Representatives, in the enrollment of the bill (H.R. 7446) to establish the American Revolution Bicentennial Administration, and for other purposes, is authorized and directed in the second sentence of section 9(a) to strike out “as he may prescribe” and insert in lieu thereof “as may be presented”.


*Passed November 28, 1973.*

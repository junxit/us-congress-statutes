# House Concurrent Resolution 219

> “THE FEDERAL CIVILIAN EMPLOYEE LOYALTY PROGRAM”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed for the, use of the Committee on Internal Security six hundred additional copies of a report entitled “The, Federal Civilian Employee Loyalty Program”, House Report Numbered 92–1637, Ninety-second Congress, second session.


*Passed July 20, 1973.*

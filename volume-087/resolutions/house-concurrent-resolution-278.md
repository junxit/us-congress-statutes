# House Concurrent Resolution 278

> “SOVIET ECONOMIC PROSPECTS FOR THE SEVENTIES”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there he printed for the use of the Joint Economic Committee live thousand additional copies of its joint committee print of the Ninety-third Congress, first session, entitled “Soviet Economic Prospects for the Seventies”.


*Passed December 19, 1973.*

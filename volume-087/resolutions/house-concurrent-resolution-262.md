# House Concurrent Resolution 262

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Saturday, June 30, 1973, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Tuesday, July 10, 1973, and that when the Senate adjourns on Saturday, June 30, 1973, it stand adjourned until 10 o’clock antemeridian, Monday, July 9, 1973.


*Passed June .30, 1973.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 266

> AGRICULTURE-ENVIRONMENTAL AND CONSUMER PROTECTION APPROPRIATION ACT, 1974—HEARINGS

*Resolved by the House of Representatives {the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed for the use of the House Committee on Appropriations two thousand five hundred copies of part 8 of the hearings on the agriculture-environmental and consumer protection appropriation bill, for the fiscal year 1974.


*Passed August 3, 1973.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 156

> “CORRECTIONS, FEDERAL AND STATE PAROLE SYSTEMS”—HEARINGS

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed one thousand copies each of parts VII–A and V–B of serial 15 hearings entitled “Corrections, Federal and State Parole Systems”, Ninety-second Congress, for use of the House Committee on the Judiciary.


*Passed May 10, 1973.*

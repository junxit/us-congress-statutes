# Senate Concurrent Resolution 13

> “RIGHTS OF CHILDREN, 1972 (EXAMINATION OF THE SUDDEN INFANT DEATH SYNDROME)”—HEARINGS

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there Ire printed two thousand additional copies of part 1 of the hearings held by the Subcommittee on Children and Youth of the Senate Committee on Labor and Public Welfare entitled “Rights of Children, 1972 (Examination of the Sudden Infant Death Syndrome)”, dated January 25, 1972. Such additional copies shall be for the use of the Senate Committee on Labor and Public Welfare.


*Agreed to April 19, 1973.*

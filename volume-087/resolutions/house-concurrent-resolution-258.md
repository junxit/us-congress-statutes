# House Concurrent Resolution 258

> “DRUGS IN OUR SCHOOLS”

*Resolved by the House of Representative (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed sixty-eight thousand two hundred and fifty additional copies of the House report entitled “Drugs in Our Schools”, of which fifty-five thousand two hundred and fifty copies shall be for the use of the House of Representatives, and thirteen thousand copies for the use of the Select Committee on Crime of the House of Representatives.


*Passed July 20, 1973.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 132

> “THE CAPITOL”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed as a House document with illustrations, a revised edition of “The Capitol”, compiled under the direction of the Joint Committee on Printing; and that five hundred and seventy-five thousand additional copies shall be printed, of which four hundred and forty-two thousand copies shall be for the use of the House of Representatives, one hundred and three thousand copies shall be for the use of the Senate, and thirty thousand copies shall be for the use of the Joint Committee on Printing.


*Passed June 28, 1973.*

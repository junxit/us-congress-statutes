# House Concurrent Resolution 381

> CORRECTION OF ENROLLED BILL H. R. 1284

*Resolved by the /louse of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That, in the enrollment of the bill H.R. 1284 entitled “An Act to amend title 5, United States Code, to improve the administration of the leave system for Federal employees”, the Clerk of the House of Representatives is authorized and directed to make the following additional change in the provisions of such bill: On page 3, line 12, of the House engrossed bill, insert “, or under clause. (2) of section 55(i2(i) of this title,” immediately after the word “subsection”.


*Passed November 28, 1973.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 322

> COMMISSION ON THE BANKRUPTCY LAWS OF THE UNITED STATES—REPORT

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That House Document 93–137, part 1, Report of the Commission on the Bankruptcy Laws of the United States, be reprinted as corrected, and that part 2 be printed as corrected, and that one thousand additional copies of each part be printed for the use of the House Committee on the Judiciary.


*Passed October 23, 1973,*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 47

> “EMERGENCY POWERS STATUTES; PROVISIONS OF FEDERAL LAW NOW IN EFFECT DELEGATING TO THE EXECUTIVE EXTRA-ORDINARY AUTHORITY IN TIME OF NATIONAL EMERGENCY”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed for the use of the Senate Special Committee on the Termination of the National Emergency five thousand additional copies of its report to the Senate entitled “Emergency Powers Statutes: Provisions of Federal Law Now in Effect Delegating to the Executive Extraordinary Authority in Time of National Emergency”.


*Agreed to November 10, 1973.*

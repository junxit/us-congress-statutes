# House Concurrent Resolution 275

> “U. S. INTERESTS IN AND POLICY TOWARD THE PERSIAN GULF”—HEARJNGS

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That one thousand additional copies of the hearings before the Subcommittee on the Near East of the Committee on Foreign Affairs of the House of Representatives, Ninety-second Congress, second session, entitled “U.S. Interests In and Policy Toward the Persian Gulf” be printed for the use of the Subcommittee on the Near East.


*Passed October 23, 1973.*

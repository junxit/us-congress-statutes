# Senate Concurrent Resolution 22

> ADJOURNMENT-HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That when the Senate adjourns on Wednesday. April 18, 1973, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Monday. April 30, 1973, and that, when the House adjourns on Thursday. April 19, 1973, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian, Monday, April 30, 1973.


*Agreed to April 18, 1973.*

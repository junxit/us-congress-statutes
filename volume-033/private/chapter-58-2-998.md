# Chapter 998

> An Act Granting a pension to Mary Shiver.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Shiver, widow of Abner B. Shiver, late of Captain Lightner’s company of Alabama Volunteers, Creek Indian war, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, April 8, 1904.*

# Chapter 532

> An Act Granting a pension to Nettie A. Buell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Nettie A. Buell, helpless and dependent daughter of Bennett H. Buell, late of Company I, One hundred and fifty-sixth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, March 11, 1904.*

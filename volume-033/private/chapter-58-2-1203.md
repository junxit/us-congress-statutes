# Chapter 1203

> An Act Granting a pension to Olive Bassett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Olive Bassett, widow of Michael Bassett, late of the First Regiment Michigan Volunteer Infantry, war with Mexico, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, April 11, 1904.*

# Chapter 320

> An Act Granting a pension to Theresa B. Nash.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Theresa B. Nash, widow of Warren C. Nash, late of Companies E and D, Nineteenth Regiment United States Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month, such pension to cease upon proof that the soldier is still living.


*Approved, February 26, 1904.*

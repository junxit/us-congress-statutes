# Chapter 1561

> AN ACT Granting an increase of pension to George R. Hanson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Ho use of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George R. Hanson, late of Company G, Second Regiment Massachusetts Volunteer Heavy Artillery, and pay him a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, April 23, 1904.*

# Chapter 1180

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Julia C. Vanzant.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Julia C. Vanzant, widow of Garrett Vanzant, late of Captains Dell, Haddock, and Chamberlain’s companies, Florida Volunteers, Seminole Indian war, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, April 11, 1904.*

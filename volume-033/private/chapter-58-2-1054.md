# Chapter 1054

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Francis Knapp.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the lpension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Francis Knapp, late of Company B, Tenth Regiment Kansas Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty-six dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, April 8, 1904.*

# Chapter 578

> An Act Granting a pension to Mary Korth.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Korth, widow of Frederick Korth, late teamster in the United States service, who was permanently disabled by gunshot wounds received at the hands of hostile Sioux Indians, at the Lower Sioux agency, during the Sioux outbreak of eighteen hundred and sixty-two in Minnesota, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, March 16, 1904.*

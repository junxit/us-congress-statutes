# Chapter 1295

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Jerome J. Hinds.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jerome J. Hinds, late of Company B, Fourteenth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and second lieutenant and captain Company A, First Regiment Alabama Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, April 13, 1904.*

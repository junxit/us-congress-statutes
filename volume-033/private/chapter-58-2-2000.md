# Chapter 2000

> An Act For the relief of the Bank of North Wilkesboro.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of ninety dollars is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not other-wise appropriated, for the relief of the Bank of North Wilkesboro, at North Wilkesboro, State of North Carolina, for registered letter containing one hundred dollars, addressed to Treasurer of the United States, Redemption Division, Washington, District of Columbia, which was burned in railroad wreck near Charlottesville, Virginia, about March twenty-second, nineteen hundred and two.


*Approved, April 28, 1904.*

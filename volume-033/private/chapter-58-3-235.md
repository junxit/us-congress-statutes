# Chapter 235

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Caroline L. Guild.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Caroline L. Guild, widow of Phineas K. Guild, late assistant surgeon Fifty-second Regiment and surgeon One hundred and twentieth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, January 25, 1905.*

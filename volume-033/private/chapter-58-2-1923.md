# Chapter 1923

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Louisa Weaver.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Louisa Weaver, widow of John D. Weaver, late hospital steward First Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Light Artillery, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, April 28, 1904.*

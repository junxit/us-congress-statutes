# Chapter 249

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Melinda H. Chapman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Melinda H. Chapman, widow of Adolphus J. Chap-man, late of Company C, Semd Regiment Iowa Volunteer Infantry, and first lieutenant and adjutant. Fourteenth Regiment Maine Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 26, 1904.*

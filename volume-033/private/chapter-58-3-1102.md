# Chapter 1102

> An Act Granting a pension to Avery Dalton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Avery Dalton, late of Captain Barns’s company, Illinois Volunteers, Black Hawk Indian war, and pay him a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1905.*

# Chapter 1115

> An Act Granting a pension to Julia A. Allison.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Julia A. Allison, widow of William Allison, late of Captain King’s company, Georgia Volunteers, Cherokee Indian disturbances, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, April 8, 1904.*

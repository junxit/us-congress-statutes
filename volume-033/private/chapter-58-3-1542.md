# Chapter 1542

> An Act For the relief of John Gretzer, junior.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That John Gretzer, junior, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged from the military service of the United States as a private of Company D, First Regiment Nebraska Volunteer Infantry, on the twenty-third day of August, eighteen hundred and ninety-nine, by reason of disability resulting from a wound incurred in said service and in the line of duty.


*Approved, March 3, 1905.*

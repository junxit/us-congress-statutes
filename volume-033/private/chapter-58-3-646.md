# Chapter 646

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Samuel T. Dickson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the lpension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension aws, the name of Samuel T. Dickson, late of Company F, Forty-seventh Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 20, 1905.*

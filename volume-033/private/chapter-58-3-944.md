# Chapter 944

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Israel Roll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Israel Roll, late of Company G, First Regiment California Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that.be is now receiving.


*Approved, February 25, 1905.*

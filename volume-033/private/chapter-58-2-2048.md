# Chapter 2048

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Antoinette I. Sawyer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Antoinette I. Sawyer, widow of Charles F. Sawyer, late sergeant-major and adjutant Fourth Regiment Maine Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, April 28, 1904.*

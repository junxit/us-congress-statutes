# Chapter 1285

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Amanda H. Burrows.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension law, the name of Amanda H. Burrow’s, widow of John J. Burrows, late third lieutenant Company B, First Battalion District of Columbia Militia Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 28, 1905.*

# Chapter 2110

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Leslie C. Armour.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Leslie C. Armour, late of Company B, Second Regiment United States Cavalry, war with Spain, and pay him a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, April 28, 1904.*

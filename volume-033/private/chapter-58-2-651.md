# Chapter 651

> AN ACT Granting an increase of pension to Elizabeth Hatfield.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House ofRepresentatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elizabeth Hatfield, widow of Captain George W. Hatfield, late captain Company A, Eleventh Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 16, 1904.*

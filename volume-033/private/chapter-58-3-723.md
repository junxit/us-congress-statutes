# Chapter 723

> An Act For the relief of Gottlieb C. Rose.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Gottlieb C. Rose, late major of the Fourth Missouri Cavalry, shall be considered and held to have been honorably discharged as such major from the United States service on the twelfth day of December, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, and a certificate of such discharge shall be issued to him: Provided, That this Act shall not entitle the said Rose to any pay, bounty, or allowances.


*Approved, February 21, 1905.*

# Chapter 2142

> An Act Granting a pension to Mary E. Dunford.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary E. Dunford, dependent mother of Jesse M. Dunford, late of Company G, Fifth Regiment United States Infantry, war with Spain, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, April 28, 1904.*

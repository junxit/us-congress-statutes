# Chapter 1092

> An Act Granting a pension to Mary Yaw.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Yaw, widow of Samuel D. Yaw, late of Company K, Fiftieth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollar^ per month.


*Approved, April 8, 1904.*

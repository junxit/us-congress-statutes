# Chapter 843

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Malana W. Brant.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws', the name of Malana W. Brant, widow of William Brant, late of Company A, First Regiment, and Company B, Fourth Regiment, New Jersey Volunteer Infantry, and captain Company B, First Battalion New Jersey Veteran Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 25, 1905.*

# Chapter 36

> An Act Granting a pension to Hannah Kelly.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Hannah Kelly, widow of Joseph J. Kelly, late colonel of One hundred and seventh Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, January 27, 1904.*

# Chapter 509

> An Act For the relief of Joseph M. Simms, captain, United States Revenue-Cutter Service (retired).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Captain Joseph M. Simms, United States Revenue-Cutter Service (retired), having been promoted “for meritorious acts of public service and wounds-received in the United States service, as appears upon the public records of the volunteer service of the Army and Navy and of the Revenue-Cutter Service,” shall hereafter receive the full retired pay of his said rank.


*Approved, March 11, 1904.*

# Chapter 1760

> An Act Granting a pension to Sophronia E. Wilshire.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sophronia E. Wilshire, widow of Ransom S. Wilshire, late of Company H, Eleventh Regiment, and Company D, Twelfth Regiment, Kentucky Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1905.*

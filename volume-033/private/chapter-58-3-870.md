# Chapter 870

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to John Kesler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Kesler, late of Company H, Seventh Regiment Indiana Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving, the same to be paid to his duly constituted guardian.


*Approved, February 25, 1905.*

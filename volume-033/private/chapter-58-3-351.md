# Chapter 351

> AN ACT Granting a pension to Joel W. Nye.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the plension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension aws, the name of Joel W. Nye, late chaplain Eighteenth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 4, 1905.*

# Chapter 241

> An Act Granting a pension to Johniken L. Mynatt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Johniken L. Mynatt, late of Captain Gideon S. White’s company, National Guards of East Tennessee, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 26, 1904.*

# Chapter 46

> An Act To pay J. B. McRae ninety-nine dollars, for services as hospital steward, and so forth.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Treasurer of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to pay, out of any funds in the United States Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of ninety-nine dollars to J. B. McRae, of Jackson, North Carolina, for services rendered as hospital steward of the Second Regiment North Carolina Volunteers, from June sixth, eighteen hundred and ninety-eight, to July thirty-first, eighteen hundred and ninety-eight, in full satisfaction for services rendered.


*Approved, January 18, 1905.*

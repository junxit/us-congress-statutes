# Chapter 1612

> An Act Granting a pension to Henry J. Richardson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Henry J. Richardson, late landsman, United States ship Saratoga, United States Navy, war with Mexico, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1905.*

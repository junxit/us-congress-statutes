# Chapter 1431

> An Act Granting a pension to Hedwig A. Maass.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Hedwig A. Maass, dependent mother of Clara Louise Maass, late hospital nurse, United States Army, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, April 22, 1904.*

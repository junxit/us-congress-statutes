# Chapter 149

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Laura F. Hine.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Laura F. Hine, widow of Howard L. Hine, late commissary sergeant and second lieutenant Company F, Twelfth Regiment Connecticut Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 3, 1904.*

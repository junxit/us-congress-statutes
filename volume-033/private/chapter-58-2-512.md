# Chapter 512

> An Act Granting a pension to Amanda Skinner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Amanda Skinner, widow of John Skinner, late of Company C, First Regiment Florida Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, March 11, 1904.*

# Chapter 54

> An Act For the relief of Miss Lelia G. Cayce.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Miss Lelia G. Cayce the sum of one hundred and seventy-nine dollars and seventy-three cents, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the same being the amount of penalty collected by mistake from the said Lelia G. Cayce at the port of San Francisco, May twenty-third, nineteen hundred and three.


*Approved, January 21, 1905.*

# Chapter 473

> An Act Granting a pension to Eliza Williams.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension aws, the name of Eliza Williams, widow of William Williams, late of Company A, First Battalion District of Columbia Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, March 8, 1904.*

# Chapter 906

> An Act Granting a pension to Isabella Burke.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States af America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name Isabella Burke, widow of Benjamin Burke, alias Johnson, late of Company A, Twenty seventh Regiment United States Colored Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1905.*

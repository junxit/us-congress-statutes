# Chapter 1637

> AN ACT Granting a pension to Clarissa Wolcott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United, States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Clarissa Wolcott, dependent mother of Addison Wolcott, late of Company G, Eighty-sixth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, April 27, 1904.*

# Chapter 294

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Louiza Phillips.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Louiza Phillips, widow of William C. Phillips, late of Captain Boston’s company, Alabama Volunteers Creek Indian war, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 26, 1904.*

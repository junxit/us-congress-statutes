# Chapter 1235

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Ellen Scott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ellen Scott, widow of Henry Scott, late of Company A, Second Regiment Eastern Shore Maryland Volunteer Infantry, and Company K, Eleventh Regiment Maryland Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 28, 1905.*

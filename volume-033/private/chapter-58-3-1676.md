# Chapter 1676

> An Act For the relief of Captain Frank D. Ely.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to relieve Frank D. Ely, captain, Twenty-ninth Infantry, United States Army, from accountability for the sum of five hundred and twenty-six dollars and thirty-three cents, pertaining to the appropriation for subsistence of the Army for the fiscal year nineteen hundred and three.


*Approved, March 3, 1905.*

# Chapter 2160

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Mary Taggart.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Taggart, widow of James C. Taggart, late first lieutenant Company I, Seventy-first Regiment New York Militia Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving: Provided, That in the event of the death of Maggie Taggart, helpless and dependent child of said James C. Taggart, the additional pension herein granted shall cease and determine: And provided further, That in the event of the death of Mary Taggart the name of said Maggie Taggart shall be placed on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, at the rate of twelve dollars per month from and after the date of death of said Mary Taggart.


*Approved, April 28, 1904.*

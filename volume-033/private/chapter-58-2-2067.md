# Chapter 2067

> An Act Granting a pension to Julia A. Henderson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Julia A. Henderson, widow of George W. Doty, late captain, United States Navy, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, April 28, 1904.*

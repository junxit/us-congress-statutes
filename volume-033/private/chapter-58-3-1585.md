# Chapter 1585

> An Act Granting a pension to Sarah Carden.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah Carden, former widow of Jonathan Frazier, late of Company A, First Regiment Alabama Vidette Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1905.*

# Chapter 527

> An Act Granting a pension to Annie P. Erving, formerly Cilla Zerbe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Annie P. Erving, formerly Cilla Zerbe, late nurse, Medical Department, United States Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 11, 1904.*

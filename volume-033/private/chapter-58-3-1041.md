# Chapter 1041

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Isaac Vanatta.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Isaac Vanatta, late of Duncan’s independent company, Ohio Mounted Volunteers, war with Mexico, and pay him a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 25, 1905.*

# Chapter 460

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Margaret Liddle.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Margaret Liddle, widow of Samuel Liddle, late second lieutenant Company E, First Regiment Illinois Volunteer Light Artillery, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifteen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 8, 1904.*

# Chapter 887

> An Act Granting a pension to Paul W. Thomson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Paul W. Thomson, late of Company M, Third Regiment United States Cavalry, war with Spain.


*Approved, February 25, 1905.*

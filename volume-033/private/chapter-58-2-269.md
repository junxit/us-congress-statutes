# Chapter 269

> An Act Granting a pension to Emma H. Higley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Emma H. Higley, widow of Alonzo Higley, late of the Tenth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 26, 1904.*

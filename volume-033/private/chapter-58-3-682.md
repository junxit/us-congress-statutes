# Chapter 682

> An Act Granting a pension to Ursula Bayard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ursula Bayard, helpless and dependent daughter of George A. Bayard, late captain Company H and major One hundred and forty-eighth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 20, 1905.*

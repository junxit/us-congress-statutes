# Chapter 1696

> An Act Granting a pension to Mary Apple.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Apple, widow of Louis Apple, late of Company K, First Regiment United States Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, April 27, 1904.*

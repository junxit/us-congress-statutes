# Chapter 729

> AN ACT Granting an increase of pension to Sylvania S. Cheney.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House ofRepresentatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sylvania S. Cheney, widow of John T. Cheney, late major, First Regiment Illinois Volunteer Light Artillery, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty-five dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 21, 1904.*

# Chapter 735

> An Act Granting a pension to Hannah Small Wiggin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Hannah Small Wiggin, dependent mother of John S. Wiggin, late captain Company I, Third Regiment Maine Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 21, 1904.*

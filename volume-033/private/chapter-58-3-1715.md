# Chapter 1715

> An Act Granting a pension to Maria W. Shaul.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Unite States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Maria W. Shaul, widow of Warren H. Shaul, late of Company A, First Regiment, Company A, Twenty-first Regiment and Company A, Third Regiment, Wisconsin Volunteer Infantry, an pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1905.*

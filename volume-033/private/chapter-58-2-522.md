# Chapter 522

> An Act Granting a pension to Annie G. Norwood.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Annie G. Norwood, widow of Randolph Norwood, late captain, Second Regiment United States Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, March 11, 1904.*

# Chapter 765

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to John C. Besier.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John C. Besier, late first lieutenant Company D, First Regiment Missouri Volunteer Engineers, and pay him a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 23, 1905.*

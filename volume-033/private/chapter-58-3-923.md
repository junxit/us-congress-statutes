# Chapter 923

> An Act Granting a pension to John Crotty.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House ofRepresentatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Crotty, late first lieutenant. Fortieth Regiment United States Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain.


*Approved, February 25, 1905.*

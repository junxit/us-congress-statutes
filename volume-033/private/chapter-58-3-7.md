# Chapter 7

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Henry Wheeler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of me United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Henry Wheeler, late of Company A, Seventh Regiment Indiana Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, December 19, 1904.*

# Chapter 1116

> An Act Granting a pension to Abel N. Button.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Abel N. Button, late of Company A. Sixty-seventh Regiment New York National Guard Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, April 8, 1904.*

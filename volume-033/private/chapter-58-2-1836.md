# Chapter 1836

> An Act Granting a pension to Annie Colt McCook.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Annie Colt McCook, widow of Alexander McDowell McCook, late major-general, United States Army, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month.


*Approved, April 28, 1904.*

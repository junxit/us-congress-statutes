# Chapter 1203

> AN ACT Granting a pension to Susan E. McCarty.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Susan E. McCarty, widow of Patrick McCarty, late of Company K, Eleventh Regiment, and Company B, Twelfth Regiment Kentucky Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 28, 1905.*

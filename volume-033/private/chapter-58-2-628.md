# Chapter 628

> AN ACT Granting a pension to Rosa Glass.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Bepresentatives ofthe United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby,, authorized and directed to place on the lnsion roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Rosa Glass, helpless and dependent daughter, of Peter Glass, late of Company I, First Regiment New York Volunteer Light Artillery, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 16, 1904.*

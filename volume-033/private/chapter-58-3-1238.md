# Chapter 1238

> An Act Granting a pension to Amanda B. Mack.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Amanda B. Mack, widow of John Mack, late of Company A, First Regiment Iowa Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 28, 1905.*

# Chapter 217

> An Act Granting a pension to Sallie H. Hoffecker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sallie H. Hoffecker, widow of Joseph V. Hoffecker, late captain Company A, Sixth Regiment Delaware Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 26, 1904.*

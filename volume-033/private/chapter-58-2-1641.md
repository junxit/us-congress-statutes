# Chapter 1641

> AN ACT Granting an increase of pension to Joseph P. Scott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House ofRepresentatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension aws, the name of Joseph P. Scott, late first lieutenant and battalion adjutant Second Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, April 27, 1904.*

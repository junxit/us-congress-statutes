# Chapter 1443

> An Act Granting a pension to Onslow N. McIntire.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Onslow N. McIntire, helpless and dependent son of Sylvanus McIntire, late third assistant engineer, United States Navy, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, April 22, 1904.*

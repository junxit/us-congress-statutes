# Chapter 182

> An Act Granting a pension to Mary H. Allen.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws,rthe name of Mary H. Allen, former widow of George A. Wiggins, ate of Company B, Second Regiment New Hampshire Volunteer infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 26, 1904.*

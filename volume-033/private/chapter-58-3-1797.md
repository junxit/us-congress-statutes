# Chapter 1797

> An Act To correct the military record of James Houselman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That James Houselman, late second lieutenant of Company II, Sixty-third Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged the service on the seventh day of December, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, and a certificate of such discharge shall be issued to him: Provided, That no pay, bounty, or other allowances shall accrue or become due or payable by virtue of this Act.


*Approved, March 3, 1905.*

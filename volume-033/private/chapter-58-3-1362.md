# Chapter 1362

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Octavia J. Trull.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Octavia J. Trull, widow of George G. Trull, late first lieutenant Second Battery and captain Fourth Battery, Massachusetts Volunteer Light Artillery, and pay her a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 2, 1905.*

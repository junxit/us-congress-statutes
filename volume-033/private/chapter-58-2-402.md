# Chapter 402

> An Act Granting a pension to Lucius E. Polk.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lucius E. Polk, late captain Company D, Forty-third Regiment United States Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, March 5, 1904.*

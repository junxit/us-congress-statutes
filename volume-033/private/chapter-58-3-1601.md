# Chapter 1601

> An Act Granting a pension to Walter Elkan, alias Walter Eckhardt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Walter Elkan, alias Walter Eckhardt, late of Company I, Seventh Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain, and pay him a pension at the rate of six dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1905.*

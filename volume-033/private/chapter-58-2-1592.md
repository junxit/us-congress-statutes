# Chapter 1592

> AN ACT Granting a pension to John F. Burrows.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John F. Burrows, late of the East Tennessee Bridge Burners, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per-month.


*Approved, April 23, 1904.*

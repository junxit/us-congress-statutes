# Chapter 1001

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Alpheus Townsend.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Alpheus Townsend, late of Troop F, First Regiment United States Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that is now receiving.


*Approved, February 25, 1905.*

# Chapter 2089

> An Act Granting a pension to Ida Diamond.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ida Diamond, widow of Constantine Diamond, late of the U. S. S. Monongahela, United States Navy, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, April 28, 1904.*

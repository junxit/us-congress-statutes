# Chapter 490

> An Act Granting a pension to Henry O. Kent.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Henry O. Kent, late colonel Seventeenth Regiment New Hampshire Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, February 6, 1905.*

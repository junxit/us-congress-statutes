# Chapter 663

> An Act Granting a pension to Martha T. Hamlin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, 1the name of Martha T. Hamlin, widow of Decatur Hamlin, late scout, Tenth Regiment Indiana Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 20, 1905.*

# Chapter 63

> An Act Granting a pension to Harriet A. Orr.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, That,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws,tthe name of Harriet A. Orr, dependent mother of Daniel B. Saint John Orr, late of Company K, Fifty-sixth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, January 23, 1905.*

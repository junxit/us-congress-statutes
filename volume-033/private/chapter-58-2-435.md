# Chapter 435

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Blanche L. Chunn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House ofRepresentatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Blanche L. Chunn, widow of Frederick Chunn, late assistant paymaster, United States Navy, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 8, 1904.*

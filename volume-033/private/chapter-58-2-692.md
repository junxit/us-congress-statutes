# Chapter 692

> AN ACT Granting an increase of pension to Sarah J. Shell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the lension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension Laws, the name of Sarah J. Shell, widow of Henry B. Shell, late of Captain James F. Preston’s company, First Regiment Virginia Volunteers, war with Mexico, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 16, 1904.*

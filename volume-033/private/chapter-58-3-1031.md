# Chapter 1031

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Nancy B. Stratton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Nancy B. Stratton, widow of Thomas S. Stratton, late first lieutenant and adjutant, First Regiment Tennessee Volunteer Cavalry, war with Mexico, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 25, 1905.*

# Chapter 750

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Carrie Wages.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Carrie Wages, widow of Shadrack R. Wages, late of Company N, Second Regiment Missouri Mounted Volunteers, war with Mexico, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 22, 1904.*

# Chapter 104

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Agnes B. Hesler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Agnes B. Hesler, widow of Frederick Alexander Hesler, late surgeon United States Navy, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty-five dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving, and two dollars per month additional on account of the minor child of said Frederick Alexander Hesler until she reaches the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, January 23, 1905.*

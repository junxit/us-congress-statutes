# Chapter 1347

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Katharina A. Mueller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Katharina A. Mueller, widow of William Mueller, late of Company I, Thirty-third Regiment New Jersey Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 2, 1905.*

# Chapter 1324

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Amanda L. Mardin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Amanda L. Mardin, widow of Riley H. Mardin, late of Company B, Fourth Regiment Vermont Volunteer Infantry, and second lieutenant Company A, One hundred and twenty-seventh Regiment United States Colored Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, April 16, 1904.*

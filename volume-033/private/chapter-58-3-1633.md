# Chapter 1633

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Andrew T. Welman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Andrew T. Welman, late of Company A, Thirty- third Regiment Indiana Volunteer Infantry, and Company D, One hundred and seventeenth Regiment Indiana Volunteer Infantry, and hay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that e is now receiving.


*Approved, March 3, 1905.*

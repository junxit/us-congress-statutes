# Chapter 10

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Perry Kittredge.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House ofRepresentatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Perry Kittredge, late hospital steward Third Regiment New Hampshire Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving, payable to his legally constituted guardian.


*Approved, January 23, 1904.*

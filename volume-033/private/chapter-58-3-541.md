# Chapter 541

> An Act Granting a pension to Hattie F. Davis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Hattie F. Davis, widow of George E. Davis, late of Company D, Eleventh Regiment New Hampshire Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 6, 1905.*

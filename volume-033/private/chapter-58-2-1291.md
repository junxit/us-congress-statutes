# Chapter 1291

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Grace P. Paddock.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Grace P. Paddock, widow of Richard B. Paddock, late captain, Sixth Regiment United States Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, April 13, 1904.*

# Chapter 1130

> An Act Granting a pension to Ida E. Nerber.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ida E. Nerber, helpless and dependent daughter of Daniel Nerber, late of Company H, One hundred and fifty-first Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, April 8, 1904.*

# Chapter 1634

> An Act To remove the charge of desertion from the record of Henry Beeger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Henry Beeger shall be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the service as a sergeant of Battery D, Second Artillery, as of date of January twentieth, eighteen hundred and fifty-one, and that the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized to issue an honorable discharge in accordance with this Act.


*Approved, March 3, 1905.*

# Chapter 673

> An Act Granting a pension to Mary L. Faunt Le Roy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the lension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension aws, the name of Mary L. Faunt Le Roy, widow of Robert P. Faunt Le Roy, late captain, United States Marine Corps, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month, and two dollars per month additional on account of the minor child of said Robert P. Faunt Le Roy until he reaches the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, February 20, 1905.*

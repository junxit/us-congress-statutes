# Chapter 1506

> An Act Granting a pension to Harriet E. Penrose.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Harriet E. Penrose, widow of William H. Penrose, late colonel Sixteenth Regiment United States Infantry, and brigadier-general, United States Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1905.*

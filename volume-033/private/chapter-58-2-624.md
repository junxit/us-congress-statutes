# Chapter 624

> AN ACT Granting an increase of pension to Henry C. Coffin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House ofBepresardatives ofthe Unitedi States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Henry C. Coffin, late of Company H, Ninety-ninth Regiment Indiana Volunteer Infantryy and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 16, 1904.*

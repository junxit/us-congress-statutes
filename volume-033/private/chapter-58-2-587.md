# Chapter 587

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Martha C. Kuhn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Martha C. Kuhn, widow of Henry H. Kuhn, late second lieutenant Company H, Eighth Regiment United States Veteran Reserve Corps, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 16, 1904.*

# Chapter 2053

> An Act Granting a pension to Harriet J. Woodbury.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Harriet J. Woodbury, widow of Everett T. Woodbury, late of Company E, Fourteenth Regiment Michigan Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, April 28, 1904.*

# Chapter 499

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Nathan M. Gove.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Nathan M. Gove, late musician, Third Regiment New Hampshire Volunteer Infantry, and Second Brigade, Tenth Army Corps, United States Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 8, 1904.*

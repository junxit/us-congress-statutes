# Chapter 1075

> An Act Granting a pension to Edward H. Holden.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Edward H. Holden, late of Company K, First Regiment Vermont Volunteer Infantry, war with Spain.


*Approved, February 25, 1905.*

# Chapter 1325

> An Act Granting a pension to Ella C. Tupper.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ella C. Tupper, w’idow of Francis W. Tupper, late first lieutenant and adjutant, First Regiment Alabama Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month.


*Approved, April 16, 1904.*

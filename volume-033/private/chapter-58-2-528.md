# Chapter 528

> An Act Granting a pension to Shadrach D. Bardin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Shadrach D. Bardin, late of Captain Roberts’s company, Texas Mounted Volunteers, and pay him at pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 11, 1904.*

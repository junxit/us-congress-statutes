# Chapter 1686

> An Act Granting a pension to Grace Miller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Grace Miller, widow of John A. Miller, late of Company A, Second Regiment Kentucky Volunteer Infantry, war with Mexico, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, April 27, 1904.*

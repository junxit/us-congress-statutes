# Chapter 689

> AN ACT Granting a pension to Martha Mullins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House ofRepresentatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Martha Mullins, widow of Joab Mullins, late of Company F, Eighth Regiment Kentucky Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 16, 1904.*

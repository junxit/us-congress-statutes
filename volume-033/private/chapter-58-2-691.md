# Chapter 691

> AN ACT Granting an increase of pension to Margaret E. Keller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House ofRepresentatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the iension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Margaret E. Keller, widow of Augustus R. Keller, late captain and assistant quartermaster, United States Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 16, 1904.*

# Chapter 1652

> An Act Granting a pension to David Kimball.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of David Kimball, late of Company E, First Regiment Missouri Volunteer Engineers of the West, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, April 27, 1904.*

# Chapter 1181

> AN ACT Granting an increase of pension to Emma J. Kanady.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of Amer lea in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Emma J. Kanady, widow of Thomas Kanady, late of Company K, Ninetieth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and captain Company H, First Regiment New Orleans (Louisiana) Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 28, 1905.*

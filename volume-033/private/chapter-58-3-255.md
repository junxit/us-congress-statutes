# Chapter 255

> AN ACT Granting an increase of pension to Dotha J. Whipple.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States ofAmerica in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Dotha J. Whipple, widow of Charles Whipple, late lieutenant-colonel Nineteenth Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, January 25, 1905.*

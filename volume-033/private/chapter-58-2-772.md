# Chapter 772

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Thompson Warren.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Thompson Warren, late of Company B, Fiftieth Regiment New York Volunteer Engineers, and Company F, Second Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 22, 1904.*

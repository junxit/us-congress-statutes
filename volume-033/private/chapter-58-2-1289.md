# Chapter 1289

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Maggie E. Carter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Maggie E. Carter, widow of Samuel B. Carter, late of Company B, Fifty-seventh Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving: Provided, That in the event of the death of Ora Agnes Carter, helpless and dependent daughter of said Samuel B. Carter, the additional pension herein granted shall cease and determine: And provided further, That in the event of the death of Maggie E. Carter the name of said Ora Agnes Carter shall be placed on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, at the rate of twelve dollars per month from and after the date of death of said Maggie E. Carter.


*Approved, April 13, 1904.*

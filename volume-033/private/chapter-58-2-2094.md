# Chapter 2094

> An Act Granting a pension to Delana A. Lynch.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Delana A. Lynch, widow of Thomas J. Lynch, late of Company G, One hundred and twentieth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, April 28, 1904.*

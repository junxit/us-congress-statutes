# Chapter 502

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Sally Dickinson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sally Dickinson, widow of Joseph Dickinson, late lieutenant-colonel and assistant adjutant-general and brevet brigadier-general, United States Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, February 6, 1905.*

# Chapter 687

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Edmund B. Kanada.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Edmund B. Kanada, late of Company D, Tenth Regiment Missouri Volunteer Cavalry, and captain Company G, Second Regiment Missouri Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 20, 1905.*

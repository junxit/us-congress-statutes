# Chapter 1532

> An Act Granting a pension to Louisa E. Satterfield.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Louisa E. Satterfield, widow of Alfred B. Satterfield, late of Company I, Sixth Regiment United States Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1905.*

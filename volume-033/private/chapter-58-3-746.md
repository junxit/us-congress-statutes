# Chapter 746

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to David Phillips.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of David Phillips, late of Company A, Twenty-second Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Cavalry, and Company A, Third Regiment Pennsylvania Provisional Volunteer Cavalry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, February 23, 1905.*

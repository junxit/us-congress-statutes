# Chapter 1688

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Job Throckmorton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Job Throckmorton, late first sergeant, second lieutenant, and captain Company F, Fifteenth Regiment Iowa Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, April 27, 1904.*

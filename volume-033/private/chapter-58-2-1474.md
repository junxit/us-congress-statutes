# Chapter 1474

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to George H. Otis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate a nd House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George H. Otis, late captain Company C, and major Second Regiment New Fork Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, April 22, 1904.*

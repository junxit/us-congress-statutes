# Chapter 678

> AN ACT Granting an increase of pension to Bartlett S. Haggard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Bartlett S. Haggard, late of Captain Williams’s independent company, Kentucky Volunteers, war with Mexico, and pay him a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, March 16, 1904.*

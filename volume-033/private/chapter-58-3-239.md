# Chapter 239

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Jesse L. Sanders.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Statesfof America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name Jesse L. Sanders, late of Captain Sutton’s company, Bell’s regiment, Texas Mounted Volunteers, war with Mexico, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that he is now receiving.


*Approved, January 25, 1905.*

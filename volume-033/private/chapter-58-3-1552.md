# Chapter 1552

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Martha A. Harper.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of American Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Martha A. Harper, widow of the late George W. S. Harper, late of Captain James Rampley’s company of Maryland Militia, war of eighteen hundred and twelve, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 3, 1905.*

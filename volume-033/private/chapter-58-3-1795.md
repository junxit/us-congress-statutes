# Chapter 1795

> An Act Granting a pension to Louise M. Atkins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Louise M. Atkins, widow of Tom Minor Atkins, alias Atkinson, late of the United States steamship Prairie, United States Marine Corps, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1905.*

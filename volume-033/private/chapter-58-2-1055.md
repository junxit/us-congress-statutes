# Chapter 1055

> An Act Granting a pension to Mary E. Springer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary E. Springer, widow of Peter B. Springer, late first engineer ram Lioness, Mississippi Marine Brigade, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, April 8, 1904.*

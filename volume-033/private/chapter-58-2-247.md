# Chapter 247

> An Act Granting a pension to Sarah J. Bellamy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah J. Bellamy, dependent mother of Charles T. Bellamy, late second lieutenant Company K, Twenty-second Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 26, 1904.*

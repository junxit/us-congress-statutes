# Chapter 667

> An Act Granting a pension to Matilda J. Henderson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Matilda J. Henderson, widow of Hiram Henderson, late of the ships Ohio and United States, United States Navy, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, February 20, 1905.*

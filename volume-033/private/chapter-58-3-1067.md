# Chapter 1067

> An Act Granting a pension to Frona J. Wooten.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Frona J. Wooten, widow of Charles N. Wooten, late of Company H, Signal Corps, United States Army, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month and two dollars per month additional on account of each of the minor children of said Charles N. Wooten until they reach the age of sixteen years.


*Approved, February 25, 1905.*

# Chapter 739

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Sarah L. Augur.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House ofRepresentatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah L. Augur, widow of William H. Augur, late first lieutenant and regimental quartermaster Seventh Regiment Connecticut Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of seventeen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, March 21, 1904.*

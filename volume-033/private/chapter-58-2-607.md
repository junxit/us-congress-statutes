# Chapter 607

> An Act Granting a pension to Sarah Ulshafer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah Ulshafer, widow of Jacob Ulshafer, alias Jacob Banks, late of Company A, Fourth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, March 16, 1904.*

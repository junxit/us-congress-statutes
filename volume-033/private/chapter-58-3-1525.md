# Chapter 1525

> An Act Granting an honorable discharge to Eugene H. Ely.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to correct the military record of and grant an honorable discharge to Eugene H. Ely, late first lieutenant of Company G, Third Regiment Indian Home Guards: Provided, That no pay, bounty, or other emoluments shall become due or payable by virtue of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 3, 1905.*

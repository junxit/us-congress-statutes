# Chapter 508

> An Act For the relief of Darwin S. Hall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Darwin S. Hall be, and is hereby, relieved from the obligation to refund the sum of one hundred and eleven dollars, being the difference claimed to be due from him in his account as commissioner, Chippewa Commission in Minnesota, and special disbursing agent from October tenth, eighteen hundred and ninety-seven, to July thirty-first, nineteen hundred.


*Approved, March 11, 1904.*

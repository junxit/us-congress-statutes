# Chapter 1112

> An Act Granting a pension to Alfred D. Burton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roil, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Alfred D. Burton, late of Company C, Seventh Regiment West Virginia Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, April 8, 1904.*

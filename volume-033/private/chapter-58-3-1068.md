# Chapter 1068

> An Act Granting a pension to George W. Cowan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George W. Cowan, late unassigned recruit, First Regiment Virginia Volunteer Infantry, war with Mexico, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 25, 1905.*

# Chapter 216

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Frederick Clark.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Frederick Clark, late of Company F, Twentieth Regiment Maine Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month in lieu of 1,11 at he is now receiving.


*Approved, January 25, 1905.*

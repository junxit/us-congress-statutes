# Chapter 1850

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Minnie H. Eaton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Minnie H. Eaton, widow of Charles A. Eaton, late quartermaster-sergeant, One hundred and twenty-second Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, April 28, 1904.*

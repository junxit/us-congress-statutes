# Chapter 186

> An Act Granting a pension to Arthur W. Post.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Arthur W. Post, helpless and dependent child of John P. Post, late second lieutenant Company C, Fourth Regiment Illinois Volunteers, war with Mexico, and colonel Eighth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, and pay him a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, January 25, 1905.*

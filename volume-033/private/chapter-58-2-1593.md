# Chapter 1593

> AN ACT Granting a pension to Catherine Darr.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House ofRepresentatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Catherine Darr, widow of Joseph Darr, late lieutenant-colonel First Regiment West Virginia Volunteer Cavalry, and pay her a pension at the rate, of thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, April 23, 1904.*

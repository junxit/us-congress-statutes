# Chapter 1507

> An Act For the relief of G. G. Martin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to issue to G. G. Martin, late lieutenant-colonel First United States Colored Troops, an honor-able discharge as of date December thirty-first, eighteen hundred and sixty-three.


*Approved, March 3, 1905.*

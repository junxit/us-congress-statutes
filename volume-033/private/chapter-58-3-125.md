# Chapter 125

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Matilda L. Curkendall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House ofRepresentatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Matilda L. Curkendall, widow of George Curkendall, late first lieutenant, Company D, Third Regiment Iowa Volunteer Cavalry, and lieutenant-colonel One hundred and thirty-eighth Regiment United States Colored Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, January 23, 1905.*

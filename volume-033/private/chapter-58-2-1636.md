# Chapter 1636

> AN ACT Granting an increase of pension to Mary Heaney.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House ofRepresentatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Heaney, widow of Hubert Heaney, alias Feaghey, late of Company D, Fifth Regiment New York Volunteer Heavy Artillery, and pay her a pension at the rate of sixteen dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving.


*Approved, April 27, 1904.*

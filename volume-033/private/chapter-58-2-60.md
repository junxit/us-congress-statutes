# Chapter 60

> An Act Granting a pension to Annie H. Zoll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Ho use ofRepresentatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Annie H. Zoll, helpless and dependent child of Rodolphus B. Zoll, late of Company D, Ninety-eighth Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, January 30, 1904.*

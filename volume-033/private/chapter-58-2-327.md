# Chapter 327

> An Act Granting an increase of pension to Emma M. Johnson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Emma M. Johnson, widow of Arthur M. Johnson, late of Company E, First Regiment Ohio Volunteer Light Artillery, and pay her a pension at the rate of twenty dollars per month in lieu of that she is now receiving: Provided, however, That in the event of the death of Tracy M. Johnson, helpless and dependent child of said Arthur M. Johnson, the additional pension herein granted shall cease and determine.


*Approved, February 26, 1904.*

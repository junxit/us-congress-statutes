# Chapter 1318

> An Act For the relief of Bert E. Barnes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War is authorized to amend the record of Bert E. Barnes so as to show him honorably discharged from Company D, Fifty-first Iowa Infantry, for disability contracted in line of duty.


*Approved, March 2, 1905.*

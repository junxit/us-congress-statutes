# Chapter 34

> An Act Providing for the interment in the District of Columbia of the remains of Rose Dillon Seager.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the health officer of the District of Columbia be, and he is hereby, authorized to issue a permit for the interment in the District of Columbia of the remains of the late Rose Dillon Seager, formerly a resident of the District of Columbia and a citizen of the United States, who died at Panama January second, nineteen hundred and five.


*Approved, January 11, 1905.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 41

> impeachment trial of charles swayne.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed and bound ten thousand copies of the proceedings in the Senate of the United States in the matter of the impeachment of Charles Swayne, judge of the district court of the United States in and for the northern district of Florida, of which four thousand shall be for the use of the Senate and six thousand for the use of the House of Representatives: me :hat the Committee on Printing be, and is hereby, directed to prepare an index thereto


*Passed March 2, 1905.*

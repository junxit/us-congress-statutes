# House Concurrent Resolution 10

> abram claypool.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the House of Representatives the bill (H. R. 9791) “Granting an increase of pension to Abram Claypool.”


*Passed March 12, 1904.*

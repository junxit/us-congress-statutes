# Senate Concurrent Resolution 9

> fort hall indian reservation.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the action of the Speaker of the House of Representatives and of the President pro tempore of the Senate in signing the enrolled bill (S. 2323) “Relating to ceded lands on the Fort Hall Indian Reservation”, be rescinded, and that in the reenrollment of the bill the word “thirty-five” in line sixteen of the enrolled bill be stricken out and the word “thirty-four” be substituted therefor, so as to correctly describe the range, inaccurately stated in the bill.


*Passed March 4, 1904.*

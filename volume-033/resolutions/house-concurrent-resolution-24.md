# House Concurrent Resolution 24

> holiday recess.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Wednesday, December twenty-first, they stand adjourned until twelve o’clock meridian, January fourth, nineteen hundred and five.


*Passed December 13, 1904.*

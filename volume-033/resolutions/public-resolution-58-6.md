# Public Resolution 6

> Joint Resolution To provide for the removal of snow and ice from the streets, cross walks and gutters of the District of Columbia.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of five thousand dollars is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated to he immediately available, for the removal of snow and ice from the streets, cross walks and gutters in the District of Columbia: one-half of said sum to be paid out of the revenues of the District of Columbia and the other half out of the Treasury of the United States,


*Approved, February 5, 1904.*

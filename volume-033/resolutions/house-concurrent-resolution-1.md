# House Concurrent Resolution 1

> holiday recess.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Saturday, December nineteenth, they stand adjourned until twelve o’clock meridian Monday, January fourth, nineteen hundred and four.


*Passed December 16, 1903.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 33

> thanks for statue of frances e. willard.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the statue of Frances E. Willard, presented by the State of Illinois, to be placed in Statuary Hall, be accepted by the United States, and that the thanks of Congress be tendered the State for the statue of one of the most eminent women of the United States.

That a copy of these resolutions, duly authenticated, be transmitted to the Governor of the State of Illinois.


*Passed February 20, 1905.*

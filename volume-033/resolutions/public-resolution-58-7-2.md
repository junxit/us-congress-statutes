# Public Resolution 7

> Joint Resolution Providing for the reappointment of James B. Angell on the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the vacancy on the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution, of the class other than members of Congress, shall be tilled by the reappointment of James B. Angell, of Michigan, whose term of office expires on January twenty-fourth, nineteen hundred and five.


*Approved, January 23, 1905.*

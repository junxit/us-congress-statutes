# House Concurrent Resolution 31

> jacob f. french.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President be requested to return the bill (H. R. 3286) entitled “An Act granting an increase of pension to Jacob F. French.”


*Passed February 4, 1905.*

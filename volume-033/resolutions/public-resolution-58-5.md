# Public Resolution 5

> Joint Resolution To fill vacancies in the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the vacancies in the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution, of the class other than Members of Congress, shall he filled by the reappointment of John B. Henderson and Alexander Graham Bell, residents of the city of Washington, whose terms of office empire on January twenty-fourth, nineteen hundred and four.


*Approved, January 27, 1904.*

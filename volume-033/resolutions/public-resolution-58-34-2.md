# Public Resolution 34

> Joint Resolution For printing the Report on the Mexican Cotton Boll Weevil.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there he printed twenty-five thousand copies of the Report on the Mexican Cotton Boll Weevil, and so forth, of which five thousand copies shall be for the use of the Senate, ten thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives, and ten thousand copies for the Department of Agriculture.


*Approved, March 3, 1905.*

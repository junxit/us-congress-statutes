# Senate Concurrent Resolution 14

> international exchange commission report.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed and bound ten thousand copies of the Report of the Commission on International Exchange and the appendices thereto, being House Document Numbered One hundred and forty-four, Fifty-eighth Congress, second session, two thousand of which shall be for the use of the Senate, four thousand for the use of the House of Representatives, and four thousand for the use of the Commission on International Exchange.


*Passed March 22, 1904.*

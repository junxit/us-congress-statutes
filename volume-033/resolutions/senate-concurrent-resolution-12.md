# Senate Concurrent Resolution 12

> isthmian canal commission report.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed three thousand five hundred copies of the report and appendices of the Isthmian Canal Commission, with illustrations, of which one thousand shall be for the use of the Senate, two thousand for the House of Representatives, and five hundred for the Isthmian Canal Commission.


*Passed March 21, 1904.*

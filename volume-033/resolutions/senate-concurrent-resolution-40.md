# Senate Concurrent Resolution 40

> progress of the beet sugar industry.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed twelve thousand copies of the Report on the Progress of the Beet Sugar Industry in the United States in nineteen hundred and four; one thousand copies for the use of the Senate, three thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives and eight thousand copies for the use of the Department of Agriculture.


*Passed March 2, 1905.*

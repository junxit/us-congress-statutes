# Public Resolution 30

> Joint Resolution Providing for the printing of Senate Document Numbered Two hundred and forty, relating to the beet-sugar industry in the United States.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be printed one hundred and ten thousand copies of Senate Document Numbered Two hundred and forty, Fifty-eighth Congress, second session, being a special report on the beet-sugar industry in the United States, ten thousand copies for the use of the Semite, twenty thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives, and sixty thousand copies for the use of the Department of Agriculture.


*Approved, April 27, 1904.*

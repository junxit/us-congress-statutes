# Senate Concurrent Resolution 4

> louisiana purchase exposition report.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Public Printer be authorized and directed to print and bind in paper covers fifteen thousand additional copies of the statement of the receipts and expenditures of the Louisiana Purchase Exposition, from the date of incorporation to September thirtieth, nineteen hun-dred and three, with the accompanying report submitted by the National Commission of said Exposition, of which five thousand copies shall be for the use of the Senate and ten thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives.


*Passed January 29, 1904.*

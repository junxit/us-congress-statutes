# Public Resolution 22

> Joint Resolution To return to the proper authorities certain Union and Confederate battle flags.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized to deliver to the proper authorities of the respective States in which the regiments which bore these colors were organized certain Union and Confederate battle Hags now in the custody of the War Department, for such final disposition as the aforesaid proper authorities may determine.


*Approved, February 28, 1905.*

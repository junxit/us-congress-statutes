# House Concurrent Resolution 16

> coinage laws.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed, in pamphlet form ten thousand copies of the coinage laws of the United States, five thousand for the use of the Director of the Mint, three thousand five hundred for the House of Representatives, and one thousand five hundred for the Senate.


*Passed March 24, 1904.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 37

> report of commission on international exchange.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed and bound in cloth ten thousand copies of the final report of the commission on International Exchange, together with the appendixes thereto, of which two thousand shall be for the use of the Senate, four thousand for the use of the House of Representatives; and four thousand for the use of the commission.


*Passed March 2, 1905.*

# Public Resolution 24

> Joint Resolution For the acceptance of a statue of General Thaddeus Kosciuszko, to be presented to the United States by the Polish-American citizens.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the offer of a statue of General Thaddeus Kosciuszko, to be erected on one of the corners of Lafayette square, in the city of Washington, District of Columbia, by and at the expense of the Polish-American organizations and of the Polish-American people of the United States generally, as an expression of their loyalty and devotion to their adopted country, for the liberties of which Koseiuszko so nobly fought, which offer has been made through Theodore M. Helinski, president of the central committee of the Polish-American organizations of the United States be, and the same hereby is. accepted: Provided, That the selection of the site on Lafayette square, the approval of the statue offered, and the manner of its erection shall be under the control and direction of a commission, consisting of the Secretary of War and the chairmen of the Committees on the Library of the Senate and House of Representatives of the Fifty-eighth Congress.


*Approved, April 18, 1904.*

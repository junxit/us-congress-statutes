# Public Resolution 14

> Joint Resolution Providing that the bulletins of the Bureau of American Ethnology be printed in octavo.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That hereafter the bulletins issued by the Bureau of American Ethnology shall be in octavo size instead of royal octavo.


*Approved, March 29, 1904.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 11

> fort hall indian reservation.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate the bill of the Senate (S. 2323) “Relating to ceded lands on the Fort Hall Indian Reservation”, that a clerical error appearing therein may be corrected.


*Passed March 15, 1904.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 8

> fort hall indian reservation

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate the bill of the Senate (S. 2323) “Relating to ceded lands on the Fort Hall Indian Reservation”, to correct a clerical error.


*Passed March 1, 1904.*

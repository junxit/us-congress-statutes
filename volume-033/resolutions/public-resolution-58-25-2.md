# Public Resolution 25

> Joint Resolution Providing for the publication of three thousand copies of Bulletin Numbered Twenty-seven of the Bureau of Animal Industry, entitled "Information concerning the Angora goat."

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be printed three thousand copies of Bulletin Numbered Twenty-seven of the Bureau of Animal Industry, entitled “Information concerning the Angora goat,” the same to be first revised under the supervision of the Secretary of Agriculture, one thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives, five hundred for the, use of the Senate, and one thousand five hundred for the use of the Department of Agriculture.


*Approved, March 3, 1905.*

# Public Resolution 16

> Joint Resolution Authorizing the printing of additional copies of Agricultural Bulletin Numbered One hundred and twenty-four, being a report on irrigation in Utah.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there shall be printed from the stereotype plates of the Report of Irrigation Investigation in Utah, tinder the direction of Edward Mead, chief of irrigation investigation, ( Office of Experiment Stations, Department of Agriculture, four thou-sand copies, of which four hundred shall be for the use of the Senate, six hundred for the use of the House of Representatives, and three thousand for the use of the Department of Agriculture.


*Approved, April 1, 1904.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 17

> precious metals and director of the mint reports.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed three thousand additional copies of the Report of the Director of the Mint on the Production of the Precious Metals for the calendar year nineteen hundred and two, bound in cloth and wrapped; for the use of the Director of the Mint.

That there also be printed four thousand additional copies of the Report of the Director of the Mint, Covering the operations of the Mints and Assay Offices of the United States for the fiscal year ended June thirtieth, nineteen hundred and three, to be bound in cloth and wrapped, one thousand for the use of the House of Representatives, five hundred for the use of the Senate, and two thousand five-hundred for the use of the Director of the Mint.


*Passed April 11, 1904.*

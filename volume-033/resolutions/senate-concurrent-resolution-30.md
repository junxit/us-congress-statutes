# Senate Concurrent Resolution 30

> sarah a. rowe.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate the bill (S. 5501) “Granting an increase of pension to Sarah A. Rowe.”


*Passed January 25, 1905.*

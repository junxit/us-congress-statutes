# Senate Concurrent Resolution 27

> thanks for statue of john j. ingalls.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the statue of John J. Ingalls, presented by the State of Kansas to be placed in Statuary Hall, is accepted in the name of the United States, and that the thanks of Congress be tendered the State for the contribution of the statue of one of its most eminent citizens, illustrious for his distinguished civic services.

## Second.

That a copy of these resolutions, suitably engrossed and duly authenticated be transmitted to the Governor of the State of Kansas.


*Passed January 21 1905.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 43

> documents for house library.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Superintendent of Documents be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to issue to the Library of the House, for use in said Library, twenty-five copies each of Volume Numbered 21, and Volume Numbered 27, of the United States Statutes at Large, twenty-five copies of the Revised Statutes of the United States, and twenty-five copies each of Volume Numbered 1. and Volume Numbered 2. of the Supplement to the Revised Statutes of the United States.


*Passed March 3, 1905.*

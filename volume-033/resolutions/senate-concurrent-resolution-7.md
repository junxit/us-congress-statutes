# Senate Concurrent Resolution 7

> j. hudson kibbe.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the Senate Senate bill 167, “Granting an increase of pension to J. Hudson Kibbe.”


*Passed February 26, 1904.*

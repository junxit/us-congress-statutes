# Public Resolution 35

> Joint Resolution Providing for the transfer of certain military rolls and records from the Interior and other Departments to the War Department.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the military rolls and records of the Indian wars or any other wars prior to the civil war, now preserved in the interior or other Departments, be transferred to the War Department, to be preserved in the Record and Pension Office of that Department, and that they shall be properly indexed and arranged for use.


*Approved, April 28, 1904.*

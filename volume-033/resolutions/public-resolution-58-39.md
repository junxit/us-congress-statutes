# Public Resolution 39

> Joint Resolution Providing for the publication of fifty thousand copies of the Special Report on the Diseases of Cattle.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be printed and bound in cloth fifty thousand copies of the Special Report on the Dis-eases of Cattle, with accompanying illustrations, the same to be first, revised and brought to date under the supervision of the Secretary of Agriculture, thirty thousand for the use of the House of Representatives, fifteen thousand for the use of the Senate, and five thousand for the use of the Department of Agriculture.


*Approved, April 28, 1904.*

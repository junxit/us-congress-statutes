# Chapter 1807

> An Act Providing for the removal of the port of entry in the customs collection district in Alaska from Sitka, Alaska, to Juneau, Alaska.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section twenty-five hundred and ninety-two of the Revised Statutes of the United States, second edition, eighteen hundred and seventy-eight, be amended by striking out the word “Sitka” in the last line of the section and inserting in lieu thereof the word “Juneau.” The collector of customs for the customs collection district of Alaska shall reside at Juneau, which is hereby made and constituted the port of entry for said district instead of Sitka.


*Approved, April 28, 1904.*

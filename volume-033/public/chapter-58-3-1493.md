# Chapter 1493

> An Act To transfer Ensign Creighton Churchill from the retired to the active list of the Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President be, and he is hereby, authorized, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, to restore Creighton Churchill, now an ensign on the retired list, to the active list of the Navy: Provided, That the said Churchill shall, upon examination in accordance with regulations to be prescribed by the Secretary of the Navy, before an examining board composed of five members, of whom three shall be line officers his senior in rank and the remaining two medical members, satisfactorily establish his mental, moral, professional, and physical fitness to perform active service, the place to which he shall be restored to be determined by the Secretary of the Navy after recommendation with regard thereto by said board: And provided further, That the said Churchill shall be carried as additional to the number of the grade to which he may be restored or at any time thereafter promoted.


*Approved, March 3, 1905.*

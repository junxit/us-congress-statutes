# Chapter 1830

> An Act To ratify and confirm the present right of way of the Oahu Railway and Land Company through the military reservation of Kahauiki, Territory of Hawaii.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the deeds from the authorities of Hawaii, dated the twenty-ninth day of April, nineteen hundred and three, and the fourth day of February, eighteen hundred and ninety-nine respectively, to the Oahu Railway and Land Company, conveying a right of way forty feet wide for a railway of said company through the military reservation of Kahauiki. district of Kona, Oahu. Hawaiian Islands, he, and the same are hereby, ratified and confirmed.


*Approved, April 28, 1904.*

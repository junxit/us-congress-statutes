# Chapter 4

> An Act Making appropriations for clearing the Potomac River of ice and for the removal of snow and ice in the District of Columbia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and State of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That the following sums are hereby appropriated, payable from any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated and from the revenues of the District of Columbia, in equal parts, namely:

For clearing the Potomac River of ice within the District of Columbia, five thousand dollars.

For cleaning snow and ice from cross walks and gutters, under the Act approved Marell second, eighteen hundred and ninety-five, five thousand dollars.


*Approved, January 15, 1904.*

# Chapter 1611

> An Act To amend an Act entitled “An Act to authorize the Montgomery Bridge Company to construct and maintain a bridge across the Alabama River near the city of Montgomery, Alabama,” approved March first, eighteen hundred and ninety-three.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the said Montgomery Bridge Company shall have authority to construct said bridge mentioned in said Act, across the Alabama River, under and subject to the limitations and restrictions mentioned in said Act, and in the amendment thereto approved March third, eighteen hundred and ninety-seven, if the actual construction of the bridge therein authorized shall be commenced within one year from the approval of this Act and completed within three years from same date.


*Approved, April 27, 1904.*

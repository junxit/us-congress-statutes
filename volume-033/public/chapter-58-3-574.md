# Chapter 574

> An Act Declaring Grand River to be not a navigable stream.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Grand River in the State of Missouri above the city of Brunswick, in the county of Chariton in said State, is hereby declared to be not a navigable stream and shall be so treated by the Secretary of War and by all other authorities.


*Approved, February 15, 1905.*

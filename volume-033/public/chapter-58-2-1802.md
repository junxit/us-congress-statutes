# Chapter 1802

> An Act Establishing a regular term of the United States circuit and district courts at Lewisburg, West Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a regular term of the United States circuit and district courts for the southern district of West Virginia shall be held every year at Lewisburg, West Virginia, on the second Tuesday in February.


*Approved, April 28, 1904.*

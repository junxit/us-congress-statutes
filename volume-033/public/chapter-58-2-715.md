# Chapter 715

> An Act To establish a port of delivery at Salt Lake City, Utah.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Salt Lake City, in the State of Utah, be, and is hereby, constituted a port of delivery, and that the privileges of the seventh section of the Act approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, governing the transportation of dutiable merchandise without appraisement, be, and the same are hereby, extended to said port.

## § 2.

That there shall be appointed a surveyor of customs to reside at said port, whose salary shall be one thousand dollars per annum, in lieu of all fees and commissions of every kind whatsoever.


*Approved, March 18, 1904.*

# Chapter 1466

> An Act Authorizing the President to appoint S. J. Call surgeon in the Revenue-Cutter Service.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States be, and is hereby, authorized to appoint S. J. Call surgeon in the United States Revenue-Cutter Service, with rank, pay, and allowance of first lieutenant in said Service, including longevity pay under provisions of existing law.


*Approved, March 3, 1905.*

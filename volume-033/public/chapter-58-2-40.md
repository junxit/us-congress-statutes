# Chapter 40

> An Act Providing for the appointment of a customs appraiser at Pittsburg, Pennsylvania.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled ,*

That there shall be in the customs collection district of Pittsburg, in the State of Pennsylvania, an appraiser, to he appointed by the President, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, and with compensation at the rate of three thousand dollars per annum.


*Approved, January 30, 1904.*

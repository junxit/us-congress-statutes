# Chapter 21

> An Act Authorizing the Secretary of the Interior to authorize the building of a bridge across Thief River, in the State of Minnesota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and empowered to authorize the proper public municipal authorities to construct a free wagon bridge across thief River, in the State of Minnesota, at such point and subject to such conditions and restrictions as he may designate and require.


*Approved, December 21, 1904.*

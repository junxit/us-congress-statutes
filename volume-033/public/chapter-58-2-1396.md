# Chapter 1396

> An Act Authorizing the recorder of the General Land Office to issue certified copies of patents, records, books, and papers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That copies of any patents, records, books, or papers in the General Land Office authenticated by the seal and certified by the recorder of such Office shall be evidence equally with the originals thereof to the same force and effect as when certified by the Commissioner of said Office.


*Approved, April 19, 1904.*

# Chapter 1297

> An Act To provide an American register for the steam lighter Pioneer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Commissioner of Navigation be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to cause the foreign-built steam lighter Pioneer to be registered as a vessel of the United States.


*Approved, March 1, 1905.*

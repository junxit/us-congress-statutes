# Chapter 941

> An Act Transferring the county of Pierce, in the State of Georgia, from the eastern to the southwestern division of the United States district court for the southern district of Georgia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the county of Pierce, in the State of Georgia, be, and the same is hereby, transferred from the eastern to the southwestern division of the United States district court for the southern district of Georgia.


*Approved, April 7, 1904.*

# Chapter 1300

> An Act To make Gloucester, Massachusetts, a port to which merchandise may be imported without appraisement.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the privileges of section seven of the Act approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, governing the immediate transportation of dutiable merchandise without appraisement, be, and the same are hereby, extended to the port of Gloucester, Massachusetts.


*Approved, March 1, 1905.*

# Chapter 555

> An Act To amend an Act entitled “An Act to authorize W. Denny and company to bridge Dog River, in the State of Mississippi.”

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That W. Denny and Company, a corporation of the State of Mississippi, its successors and assigns, be, and is hereby, authorized to construct and maintain the bridge mentioned in the Act approved April eleventh, nineteen hundred and four, entitled “An Act to authorize W. Denny and Company to bridge Dog River, in the State of Mississippi,” under and subject to the provisions of said Act: Provided, That the actual construction of the bridge therein authorized be commenced within one year and completed within three years from the date of approval of this Act.


*Approved, February 8, 1905.*

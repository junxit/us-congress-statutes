# Chapter 33

> An Act To amend an Act entitled "An Act to authorize Washington and Westmoreland counties, in the State of Pennsylvania, to construct and maintain a bridge across the Monongahela River, in the State of Pennsylvania," approved February twenty-first, nineteen hundred and three.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section seven of an Act entitled “An Act to authorize Washington and Westmoreland counties, in the State, of Pennsylvania, to construct and maintain a bridge across the Monongahela Ri ver. in the State of Pennsylvania.” approved February twenty-first, nineteen hundred and three, be, and is hereby, amended to read as follows:

> ## § 7.
>
> That this Act shall be null and void if actual construction of the bridge herein authorized be. not commenced within one year and completed within three years from February twenty-first, nine-teen hundred and five.”


*Approved, January 11, 1905.*

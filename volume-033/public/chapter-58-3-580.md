# Chapter 580

> An Act For the establishment of subports of entry at Rouses Point and Malone, New York.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Rouses Point, New York, and Malone, New York, be, and are hereby, established as subports of entry in the customs collection district of Champlain, State of New York, and that the privileges of the first section of the Act approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, relating to the transportation of dutiable merchandise without appraisement be, and the same are hereby, extended to said subports.


*Approved, February 17, 1905.*

# Chapter 1162

> An Act To extend the time for the construction of a bridge across Rainy River by the International Bridge and Terminal Company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the International Bridge and Terminal Company, its successors and assigns, shall have the right to commence the construction of a bridge across Rainy River, in Minnesota. subject to the terms and conditions contained in an Act until led “An Act to provide for the construction of a bridge across Rainy River, in Minnesota,” approved February seventh, nineteen hundred and three, within one year, and complete such bridge within three years after the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 28, 1905.*

# Chapter 559

> An Act Making an appropriation for fuel for the public schools of the District of Columbia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby appropriated out of any money in the Treasury of the United States not otherwise appropriated the sum of thirty thousand dollars to supply a deficiency in the appropriation for fuel for public schools in the District of Columbia for the fiscal year nineteen hundred and five, said sum to be paid one half out of the revenues of the District of Columbia and the other half out of the Treasury of the United States.


*Approved, February 8, 1905.*

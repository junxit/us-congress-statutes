# Chapter 1252

> An Act To amend section forty-six hundred and seven of the Revised Statutes, relating to soliciting seamen as lodgers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section forty-six hundred and seven is hereby amended by adding thereto the following:

> “This section shall apply to vessels of the United States engaged in the foreign trade and to foreign vessels.”

## § 2.

That this Act shall take effect one month after its passage.


*Approved, April 13, 1904.*

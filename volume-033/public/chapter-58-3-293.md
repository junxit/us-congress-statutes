# Chapter 293

> An Act Making Norwalk, Connecticut, a subport of entry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Norwalk, in the State of Connecticut, be, and is hereby, constituted a subport of entry in the customs collection district of Fairfield, Connecticut.


*Approved, February 2, 1905.*

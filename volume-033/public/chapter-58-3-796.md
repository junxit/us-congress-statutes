# Chapter 796

> An Act To authorize the promotion of First Lieutenant Thomas Mason, Revenue-Cutter Service.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the Untied States be, and he is hereby, authorized to advance Lieutenant Thomas Mason. Revenue-Cutter Service, one grade, from first lieutenant to that of captain, on the “Permanent waiting orders” list in the Revenue-Cutter Service, for meritorious acts while in the service of (he Navy and of the Revenue-Cutter Service of the United States: Provided however, That no increase in pay or allowance is to be made by the advance in grade hereby authorized.


*Approved, February 25, 1905.*

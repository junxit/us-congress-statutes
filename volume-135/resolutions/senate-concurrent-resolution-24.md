# Senate Concurrent Resolution 24

> ENROLLMENT CORRECTIONS—S. 1605

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That in the enrollment of the bill S. 1605, the Secretary of the Senate shall make the following corrections:

- **(1)** In the table in section 2301(a), in the item relating to Eielson Air Force Base, strike “$44,850,00” in the amount column and insert “$44,850,000”.
- **(2)** In section 4601, in the table relating to Military Construction, Navy, in the item relating to AEGIS Ashore Barracks Planning and Design in Redzikowo, Poland, insert “0” in the Conference Authorized column.


*Agreed to December 20, 2021.*

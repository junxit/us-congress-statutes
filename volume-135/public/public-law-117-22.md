# Public Law 117–22

> Joint Resolution Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission relating to “Update of Commission’s Conciliation Procedures”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Congress disapproves the rule submitted by the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission relating to “Update of Commission’s Conciliation Procedures” (86 Fed. Reg. 2974; published January 14, 2021), and such rule shall have no force or effect.


*Approved June 30, 2021.*

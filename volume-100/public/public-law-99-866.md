# Public Law 99–866

> Joint Resolution To ratify the February 1, 1986, sequestration order of the President for Fiscal Year 1986 issued under section 252 of the Balanced Budget and Emergency Deficit Control Act of 1985.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, effective on and after March 1, 1986, the Congress hereby ratifies and affirms as law the February 1, 1986, sequestration order of the President as issued under section 252(a)(1) of the Balanced Budget and Emergency Deficit Control Act of 1985 and as affected by laws enacted after February 1, 1986, and before the date of adoption of this joint resolution.


*Approved July 31, 1986.*

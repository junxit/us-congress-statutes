# Public Law 99–358

> An Act To permit the use and leasing of certain public lands in Nevada by the University of Nevada.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That

- **(a)** notwithstanding the provisions of the Recreation and Public Purposes Act (43 U.S.C. 869 et seq.), the Secretary of the Interior shall permit the University of Nevada to use (either directly or by lease) the lands described in subsection (b) of this Act as a site for a research and development center.
- **(b)** The lands referred to in subsection (a) are described as follows:
  - **(1)** T. 20 N., R. 19E, Sec. 25: Lots 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 11, SE 1/4 NW 1/4, NE 1/4 SW 1/4, Mount Diablo Meridian, Nevada, containing 309.11 acres; and
  - **(2)** T. 20 N., R. 19E, Sec. 25: Lots 6, 7, SW 1/4 NE 1/4, NW 1/4 SE 1/4, Mount Diablo Meridian, Nevada, containing 158.22 acres.


*Approved July 8, 1986.*

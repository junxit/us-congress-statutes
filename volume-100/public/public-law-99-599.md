# Public Law 99–599

> An Act To designate the building commonly known as the Old Post Office in Worcester, Massachusetts, as the “Harold D. Donohue Federal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the General Services Administration building commonly known as the Old Post Office, located at 595 Main Street, Worcester, Massachusetts, shall hereafter be known and designated as the “Harold D. Donohue Federal Building”. Any reference in any law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to such building shall be deemed to be reference to the “Harold D. Donohue Federal Building”.


*Approved November 5, 1986.*

# Public Law 99–585

> An Act To designate the United States Courthouse at 68 Court Street, Buffalo, New York, as the “Michael J. Dillon Memorial United States Courthouse”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION OF BUILDING.

The United States Courthouse located at 68 Court Street, Buffalo, New York, shall be known and designated as the “Michael J. Dillon Memorial United States Courthouse”.

## § 2. LEGAL REFERENCES.

Each reference in a law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to such building shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Michael J. Dillon Memorial United States Courthouse”.


*Approved October 29, 1986.*

# Public Law 99–439

> An Act Relating to telephone services for Senators.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1205(a) of the Supplemental Appropriations Act, 1984 (2 U.S.C. 58a) is amended by striking out “(except services for which the charge is based on the amount of time the service is used)”.


*Approved October 2, 1986.*

# Public Law 99–491

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the joint resolution of October 11, 1986 (Public Law 99–465) is hereby amended by striking out “October 15, 1986” and inserting in lieu thereof “October 16, 1986”.

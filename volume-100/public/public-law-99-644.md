# Public Law 99–644

> An Act To amend the Alaska National Interest Lands Conservation Act of 1980 to clarify the treatment of submerged lands and ownership by the Alaskan Native Corporation.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (a) of section 901 of the Alaska National Interest Lands Conservation Act (Public Law 96–487), as amended, is hereby amended by striking out the word “six years after the date of execution” each time such words occur in such subsection, and by inserting in lieu thereof in each instance the words “eight years after the date of execution”, and by striking the words “seven years after the date of enactment” each time such words occur in such subsection, and by inserting in lieu thereof in each instance the words “nine years after the date of enactment”.


*Approved November 10, 1986.*

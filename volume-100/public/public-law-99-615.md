# Public Law 99–615

> An Act To change the name of the Loxahatchee National Wildlife Refuge, Florida, to the Arthur R. Marshall Loxahatchee National Wildlife Refuge.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Loxahatchee National Wildlife Refuge, in the State of Florida, shall hereafter be known and designated as the “Arthur R. Marshall Loxahatchee National Wildlife Refuge”. Any reference in any law, regulation, map, document, record, or other paper of the United States to such wildlife refuge, shall be held and considered to be a reference to the “Arthur R. Marshall Loxahatchee National Wildlife Refuge”.


*Approved November 6, 1986.*

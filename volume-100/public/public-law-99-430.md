# Public Law 99–430

> An Act To provide for the extension of certain programs relating to housing and community development, and for other purposes.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That each provision of law amended by Public Law 99–345 is amended by striking out “September 30, 1986” wherever it appears and inserting in lieu thereof “September 30, 1987”.


*Approved September 30, 1986.*

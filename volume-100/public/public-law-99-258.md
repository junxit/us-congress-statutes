# Public Law 99–258

> An Act To amend section 901 of the Alaska National Interest Lands Conservation Act.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (a) of section 901 of the Alaska National Interest Lands Conservation Act (Public Law 96–487) is hereby amended by striking the words “five years after the date of execution” each time such words occur in such subsection, and by inserting in lieu thereof in each instance the words “six years after the date of execution”.


*Approved March 19, 1986.*

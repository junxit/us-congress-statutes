# Public Law 99–593

> An Act To designate the United States Attorney’s Building for the Southern District of New York as the “Silvio James Mollo Federal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the building known as the United States Attorney’s Building for the Southern District of New York, located at One St. Andrew’s Plaza, New York, New York, shall hereafter be known and designated as the “Silvio James Mollo Federal Building”. Any reference in any law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to such building shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Silvio James Mollo Federal Building”.


*Approved October 31, 1986.*

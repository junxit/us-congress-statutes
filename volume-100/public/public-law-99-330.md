# Public Law 99–330

> An Act To settle and adjust the claim of the Tehran American School for $13,333.94.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That this Act may be cited as the “Tehran American School Claim Act of 1985”.

## § 2.

The Comptroller General of the United States be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to settle and adjust the claim of the Tehran American School for $13,333.94, which amount constitutes proceeds from the sale of three motor vehicles arranged by the United States Embassy in Tehran, Iran, in 1979. There is hereby appropriated from any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated such amount as may be necessary for the payment of the claim.


*Approved May 29, 1986.*

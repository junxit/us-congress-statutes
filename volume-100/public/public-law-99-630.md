# Public Law 99–630

> Joint Resolution Calling for a wildlife sanctuary for humpback whales in the West Indies.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President shall, in concert with the International Whaling Commission, seek a treaty or other appropriate international agreement establishing a wildlife sanctuary for humpback whales in the West Indies, in the area encompassing the Turks Islands, Mouchoir Passage, Silver Bank Passage, Navidad Bank, and such additional areas in the West Indies as may be necessary to ensure the protection of the breeding grounds of the humpback whales.


*Approved November 7, 1986.*

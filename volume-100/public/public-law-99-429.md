# Public Law 99–429

> An Act To temporarily delay the repeal of the United States Trustee System.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That effective immediately before September 30, 1986, section 408(c) of the Act of November 6, 1978 (Public Law 95–598; 92 Statute 2687), is amended by striking out “September 30, 1986” and inserting in lieu thereof “November 10, 1986”.


*Approved September 30, 1986.*

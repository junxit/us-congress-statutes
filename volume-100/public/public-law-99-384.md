# Public Law 99–384

> An Act To increase the statutory limit on the public debt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, subsection (b) of section 3101 of title 31, United States Code, is amended by striking out the dollar limitation contained in such subsection and inserting in lieu thereof “$2,111,000,000,000,”.


*Approved August 21, 1986.*

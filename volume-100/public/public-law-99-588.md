# Public Law 99–588

> An Act To designate the United States Courthouse for the Eastern District of Virginia in Alexandria, Virginia, as the “Albert V. Bryan United States Courthouse”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States Courthouse for the Eastern District of Virginia, located on South Washington Street in Alexandria, Virginia, shall hereafter be known and designated as the “Albert V. Bryan United States Courthouse”. Each reference to such building in a law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Albert V. Bryan United States Courthouse”.


*Approved October 29, 1986.*

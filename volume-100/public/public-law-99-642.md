# Public Law 99–642

> An Act To designate the Federal Building at 111 W. Huron Street, Buffalo, New York, as the “Thaddeus J. Dulski Federal Building”.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

## § 1. DESIGNATION OF BUILDING.

The Federal Building located at 111 W. Huron Street, Buffalo, New York, shall be known and designated as the “Thaddeus J. Dulski Federal Building”.

## § 2. LEGAL REFERENCES.

Each reference in a law, map, regulation, document, record, or other paper of the United States to such building shall be deemed to be a reference to the “Thaddeus J. Dulski Federal Building”.


*Approved November 10, 1986.*

# Public Law 99–613

> Joint Resolution Providing for the convening of the first session of the One hundredth Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the first regular session of the One hundredth Congress shall begin at 12 o’clock meridian on Tuesday, January 6, 1987.


*Approved November 6, 1986.*

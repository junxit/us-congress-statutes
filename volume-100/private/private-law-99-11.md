# Private Law 99–11

> An Act For the relief of Mountaha Bou-Assali Saad.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Mountaha Bou-Assali Saad shall be held and considered to have satisfied the requirements of section 312 of the Immigration and Nationality Act relating to knowledge and understanding of the English language and the history, principles, and form of government of the United States and, notwithstanding the provisions of section 310(d) of such Act, may be naturalized within two years after the date of enactment of this Act: Provided, That a suitable and proper bond or undertaking, approved by the Attorney General, in such amount and containing such conditions as he may prescribe, be deposited.


*Approved August 23, 1986.*

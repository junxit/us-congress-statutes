# Private Law 99–16

> An Act For the relief of Cirilo Raagas Costa and Wilma Raagas Costa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Cirilo Raagas Costa shall be held and considered to be a child, and Wilma Raagas Costa shall be held and considered to be a child, within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(E) of such Act upon approval of petitions filed on their behalf by Richard G. Costa and Evangeline M. Costa, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of such Act. No natural parent, brother, or sister of Cirilo Raagas Costa or Wilma Raagas Costa shall, by virtue of such relationship, be ac-corded any right, privilege, or status under such Act.


*Approved September 25, 1986.*

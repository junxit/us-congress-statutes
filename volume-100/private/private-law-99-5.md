# Private Law 99–5

> An Act For the relief of Edwin Marcos Rios (also known as Marcos Edwin Rios) and Geovanna Rios.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, in the administration of the Immigration and Nationality Act, Edwin Marcos Rios (also known as Marcos Edwin Rios) and Geovanna Rios may be classified as children within the meaning of section 101(b)(1)(F) of the Act, upon approval of a petition which shall be eligible to be filed in their behalf in the United States by Robert and Nelly Oliva, citizens of the United States, pursuant to section 204 of the Act, and thereafter the beneficiaries shall be eligible for processing under section 245 of the Act: Provided, That the immigration status authorized under this Act shall only be effective if Robert and Nelly Oliva file a petition within two years after the date of enactment of this Act.


*Approved August 23, 1986.*

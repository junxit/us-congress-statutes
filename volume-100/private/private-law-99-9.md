# Private Law 99–9

> An Act For the relief of John Patrick Brady, Ann M. Brady, and Mark P. Brady.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of section 101(a)(15)(E)(i) of the Immigration and Nationality Act, John Patrick Brady, Ann M. Brady, and Mark P. Brady shall be treated as being aliens described in that section during any period in which John Patrick Brady is employed by Swiss Air Transport Company, Limited.


*Approved August 23, 1986.*

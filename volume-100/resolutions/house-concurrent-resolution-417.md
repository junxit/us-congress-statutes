# House Concurrent Resolution 417

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Friday, October 17, 1986 or on Saturday, October 18, 1986, pursuant to a motion made by the Majority Leader or his designee, and that when the Senate adjourns on Friday, October 17, 1986, or on Saturday, October 18, 1986, pursuant to a motion made by the Majority Leader or his designee, they stand adjourned sine die.


*Agreed to October 18, 1986.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 288

> “U.N. CONFERENCE TO REVIEW AND APPRAISE THE U.N. DECADE FOR WOMEN: REPORT OF CONGRESSIONAL STAFF ADVISORS TO THE NAIROBI CONFERENCE”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed, for the use of the Committee on Foreign Affairs of the House of Representatives, 1,000 additional copies of the committee print entitled “U.N. Conference to Review and Appraise the U.N. Decade for Women: Report of Congressional Staff Advisors to the Nairobi Conference”.


*Agreed to August 15, 1986.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 346

> ENROLLMENT CORRECTIONS—S. 124

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That, in the enrollment of the bill (S. 124), the Clerk of the Senate shall make the following corrections:

- **(1)** In section 101(b), in the amendment adding a new section 1412(b)(8), strike “1451” and insert “1415”.
- **(2)** In section 101(c)(4), strike “1420(e)” and insert “1416(e)”.
- **(3)** In section 301(h), strike “section 6” and insert “section 106”.


*Agreed to June 6, 1986.*

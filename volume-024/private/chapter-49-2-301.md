# Chapter 301

> An Act for the relief of James M. Grigsby.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster-General is hereby authorized to adjust and settle the claim of James M. Grigsby, late postmaster at Montagne, Texas, for money-order funds lost in transit through the mails, notwithstanding the provisions of the act entitled “An act authorizing the Postmaster-General to adjust certain claims of postmasters for loss by burglary, fire, or other unavoidable casualty,” approved March seventeenth, eighteen hundred and eighty-two.


*Approved, February 28, 1887.*

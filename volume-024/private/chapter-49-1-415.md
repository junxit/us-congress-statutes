# Chapter 415

> An Act granting a pension to Elizabeth B. Bell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elizabeth B. Bell, widow of Lieutenant V. Bradford Bell, deceased, late of the Nineteenth Illinois Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, June 1, 1886.*

# Chapter 78

> An act for the relief of Miss Rosa Wallace.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster-General be, and he is hereby, authorized, in his discretion, to credit the accounts of Miss Rosa Wallace, postmaster at Washington, Arkansas, with the sum of three hundred and eighty-one dollars, which sum was lost by a robbery of the mails at or near Hope, Arkansas, on or about November thirtieth, eighteen hundred and eighty-four.


*Approved, May 1, 1886.*

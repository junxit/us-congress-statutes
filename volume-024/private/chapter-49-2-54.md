# Chapter 54

> An Act granting a pension to Caroline Sees.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Caroline Sees, of Harrisburg, Pennsylvania, widow of Major Oliver W. Sees, at the rate now allowed to a widow of a major of volunteers.


*Approved, January 27, 1887.*

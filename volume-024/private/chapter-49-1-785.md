# Chapter 785

> An Act granting a pension to Alexander Harper.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Alexander Harper, late of Company I, First Regiment West Virginia Volunteers.


*Approved, July 26, 1886.*

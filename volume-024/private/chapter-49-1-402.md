# Chapter 402

> An Act granting a pension to John L. Bruce.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll. subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the mime of John L. Bruce, late of Company II, Third Kentucky Volunteer Cavalry.


*Approved, June 1, 1886.*

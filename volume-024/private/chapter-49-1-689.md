# Chapter 689

> An Act for the relief of Nathan Hildabrant.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Nathan Hildabrant, late of Company A, Thirty-first Regiment New Jersey Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

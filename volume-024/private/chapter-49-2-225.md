# Chapter 225

> An Act granting a pension to E. A. McFadden.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of E. A. McFadden, late second lieutenant of Company G, Second Regiment Ohio Volunteers, in the Mexican war, and pay him a pension from and after the passage of this act, subject, to the provisions and limitations of the pension law.


*Approved, February 23, 1887.*

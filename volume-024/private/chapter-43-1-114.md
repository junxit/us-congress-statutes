# Chapter 114

> An act granting a pension to Nathaniel H. Blakely.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

that the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Nathaniel H. Blakely, formerly of Company F, Third Regiment Missouri Mounted Volunteers, in the war with Mexico.


*Approved, May 7, 1886.*

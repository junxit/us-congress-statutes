# Chapter 873

> An act for the relief of Warren L. Rice.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Warren L. Rice, late a private in Company F, Sixth Michigan Heavy Artillery, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, August 3, 1886.*

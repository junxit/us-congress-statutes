# Chapter 813

> An act for the relief of Frances H. Plummer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of one thousand, dollars be paid, out of any money in the Treasury of the United States not otherwise appropriated, to Frances H. Plummer, widow of J. B. Plummer, deceased, in full satisfaction and discharge of any and all claims against the United States for the loss of property by said J. B. Plummer or his said widow at the evacuation of Fort Cobb, Indian Territory, in May, eighteen hundred and sixty-one.


*Approved, July 29, 1886.*

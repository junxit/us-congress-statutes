# Chapter 149

> An act granting a pension to Taylor Voss.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Taylor Voss, late a private in Companies I and G, Eleventh Regiment Kentucky Cavalry, said pension to commence from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, May 7, 1886.*

# Chapter 736

> An Act granting a pension to Frederick Marion.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Frederick Marion, late of Company K, Thirty-first Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

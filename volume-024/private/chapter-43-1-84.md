# Chapter 84

> An act granting a pension to Jennette S. Kent.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jennette S. Kent, dependent mother of James S. Kent, late a corporal in Company G, Second Regiment of United States Sharpshooters.


*Approved, May 3, 1886.*

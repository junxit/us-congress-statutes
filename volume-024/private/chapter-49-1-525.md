# Chapter 525

> An Act granting a pension to Ferdinand Koehler.

*Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assent bled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ferdinand Koehler, late of Company C, Second Regiment Missouri Artillery (pension claim numbered two hundred and seventy-two thousand seven hundred and sixty).


*Approved, June 24, 1886.*

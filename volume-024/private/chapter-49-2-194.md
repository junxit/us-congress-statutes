# Chapter 194

> An Act for the relief of Phila Clark.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Phila Clark, dependent mother of Olin Clark, late of Company C, Eighth New York Heavy Artillery Volunteers.


*Approved, February 22, 1887.*

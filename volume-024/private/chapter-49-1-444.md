# Chapter 444

> An Act for the relief of Mrs. Bridget Smith, mother of Patrick J. Smith.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Bridget Smith, mother of Patrick J. Smith, deceased, late of Company II, Tenth Minnesota Volunteers.


*Approved, June 19, 1886.*

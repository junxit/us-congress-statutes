# Chapter 375

> An Act granting a pension to Harriet M. Rider.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Harriet M. Rider, widow of Van Ranselaer Rider, late a corporal of Company K, Third Regiment Iowa Volunteers.


*Approved, May 24, 1886.*

# Chapter 770

> An Act granting a pension to Ann E. Cooney.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ann E. Cooney, who served as a hospital nurse during the late rebellion, at the rate of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, July 14, 1886.*

# Chapter 239

> An Act for the relief of Lysander H. Carroll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of one hundred and ninety-four dollars and twenty-four cents be, and hereby is, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to reimburse Lysander H. Carroll, late postmaster at Concord, Merrimack County, New Hampshire, for money received for stamps stolen by burglars from the post-office at that place on the night of September fifth, eighteen hundred and eighty-three.


*Approved, February 23, 1887.*

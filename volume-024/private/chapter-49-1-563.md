# Chapter 563

> An Act granting a pension to John G. Nicholas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John G. Nicholas, late of Company F, Ninety third Regiment of Ohio Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, June 28, 1886.*

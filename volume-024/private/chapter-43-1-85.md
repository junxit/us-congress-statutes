# Chapter 85

> An act granting a pension to Isaac N. Minshall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Isaac N. Minshall, late of Company F, Eleventh Ohio Volunteers, and Company G, Seventy-fifth Ohio Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, May 3, 1886.*

# Chapter 522

> An Act granting a pension to Lurena Wages.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lurena Wages, widow of Jackson Wages, late a private in Company E, Seventh Regiment Kentucky Volunteers.


*Approved, June 24, 1886.*

# Chapter 187

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Anna Schier.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the. pension laws, the name of Anna Schier, widow of Nicholas Schier, formerly a member of Company C, One hundred and twelfth Regiment Pennsylvania Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, February 22, 1887.*

# Chapter 137

> An act granting a pension to Asahel Middleton

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Asahel Middleton, late a private in Company F, Sixty-fifth Regiment of Indiana Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, May 7, 1886.*

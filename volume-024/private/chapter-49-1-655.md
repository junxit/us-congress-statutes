# Chapter 655

> An Act granting a pension to C. K. Hughes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of C. K. Hughes, late major of the One hundred and forty-third Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

# Chapter 484

> An Act granting a pension to Henry Bollman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Henry Boll-man, late of Company L, Fourteenth Regiment, Pennsylvania Cavalry, and now a resident of Harwood, Wayne County, Iowa, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, June 24, 1886.*

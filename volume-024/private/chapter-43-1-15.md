# Chapter 15

> An act to remove the disabilities of Edward G. W. Butler, of Missouri.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all disabilities imposed upon and incurred by Edward G. W. Butler, of Missouri, by virtue of the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution, be, and are hereby removed.


*Approved, March 2, 1886.*

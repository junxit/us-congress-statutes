# Chapter 431

> An Act granting a pension to Darius M. Seaman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Darius M. Seaman, late of Company O, One hundred and fiftieth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers.


*Approved, June 19, 1886.*

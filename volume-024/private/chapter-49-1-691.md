# Chapter 691

> An Act granting a pension to William L McCall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, directed to place the name of William L McCall, late a private in Company D, Ninth Tennessee Cavalry, on the pension-roll, subject to the conditions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

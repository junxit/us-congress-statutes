# Chapter 202

> An act granting a pension to Charles E. Bolles.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

[Note by the Department of State.—The foregoing act having been presented to the President of the United States for his approval, and not having been returned by him to the house of Congress in which it originated within the time prescribed by the Constitution of the United States, has become a law without his approval.]

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Charles E. Bolles, of Vineyard Haven, Massachusetts, late a seaman in the United States Navy.

Received by the President, April 27, 1886.

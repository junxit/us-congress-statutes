# Chapter 774

> An Act granting a pension to Samuel F. Garrett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Samuel F. Garrett, late a private in Company I, Thirty-ninth Ohio Volunteers, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, July 16, 1886.*

# Chapter 98

> An act granting a pension to John Defenbaugh.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of John Defenbaugh, late a private in Company K, Eighty-second Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, now a resident of Creston, Iowa, on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations and requirements of the pension laws.


*Approved, May 7, 1886.*

# Chapter 197

> An Act granting a pension to Sarah O'Brien.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Sarah O’Brien, widow of Hugh O’Brien, late a private in Company C, One hundred and seventieth New York Infantry Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 22, 1887.*

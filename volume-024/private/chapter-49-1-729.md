# Chapter 729

> An Act granting a pension to Sarah Ann Murphy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be. and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah Ann Murphy, widow of James Murphy, late a private in Company K, Twentieth Kentucky Volunteers.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

# Chapter 64

> An Act for the relief of Anthon Eitapence.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Anthon Eitapence, of Utica, New York, father of Adam Eitapence, late of Company H, of the One hundred and forty-sixth Regiment New York Volunteers.


*Approved, January 27, 1887.*

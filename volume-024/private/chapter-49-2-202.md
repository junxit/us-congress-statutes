# Chapter 202

> An Act granting a pension to Betsey Cooney.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, at twelve dollars per month, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Betsey Cooney, sister of Stephen Cooney, late of Company C, Third Regiment New Hampshire Volunteers.


*Approved, February 22, 1887.*

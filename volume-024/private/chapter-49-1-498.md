# Chapter 498

> An Act granting a pension to John Butler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Butler, late of Company E, Fourteenth Regiment Pennsylvania Cavalry.


*Approved, June 24, 1886.*

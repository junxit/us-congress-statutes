# Chapter 508

> An Act for the relief of Samuel Shuler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Samuel Shuler, late a private in Company E, Sixty-first Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers, to ten dollars per month.


*Approved, June 24, 1886.*

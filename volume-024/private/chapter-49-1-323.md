# Chapter 323

> An Act for the relief of Harrison W. Moore.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, instructed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Harrison W. Moore, late a private in Company A, First Regiment Wisconsin Cavalry Volunteers.

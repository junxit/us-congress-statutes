# Chapter 650

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Simon J. Fought.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be and he is hereby, authorized and directed to grant an increase of pension to Simon J. Fought, formerly a corporal of Company D of the Forty-sixth Regiment of Ohio Volunteers, to thirty dollars per month.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

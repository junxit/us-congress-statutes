# Chapter 542

> An Act granting a pension to Patrick McKean.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Patrick McKean, formerly of Company K, Eighth Regiment New Hampshire Volunteers.


*Approved, June 24, 1886.*

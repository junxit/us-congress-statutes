# Chapter 367

> An Act granting a pension to Emeline J. Babson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place the name of Emeline J. Babson, widow of Erastus W. Babson, late of Company A, Twelfth Regiment Rhode Island Volunteers, on the pension-roll, subject to the rules and regulations of the Department, the same to begin from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, May 22, 1886.*

# Chapter 653

> An Act granting a pension to Nancy R. Brown.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Nancy R. Brown, widow, mother of Robert M. Brown, late a lieutenant of Company K, One hundred and forty fifth Pennsylvania Volunteers.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

# Chapter 33

> An Act granting a pension to Andrew J. Burrell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Andre J. Burrell, late captain of Company A, Fiftieth Regiment Indiana Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, January 18, 1887.*

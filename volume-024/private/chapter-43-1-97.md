# Chapter 97

> An act granting a pension to James Morgan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of James Morgan, late a private in Company E, Fiftieth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, now a resident of Allerton, Iowa, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, May 7, 1886.*

# Chapter 265

> An Act for the relief of Mrs. Elizabeth Rice.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Elizabeth Rice, of Canandaigua, Michigan.


*Approved, February 25, 1887.*

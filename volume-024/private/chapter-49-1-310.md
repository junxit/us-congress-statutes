# Chapter 310

> An Act granting a pension to Zeba S. Ayer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the pension laws, the name of Zeba S. Ayer, dependent father of Zeba A. Ayer, deceased, late a private in Company A, Twenty-ninth Regiment Maine Infantry Volunteers.

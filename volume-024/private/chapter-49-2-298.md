# Chapter 298

> An Act for the relief of Mary J. Gilbert.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary J. Gilbert, sister of De Witt Gilbert, late of Company G, Eighty-ninth New York Volunteers.


*Approved, February 28, 1887.*

# Chapter 731

> An Act granting a pension to John M. Mattingly

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be,and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John M. Mattingly, late a private in Company A, Tenth Regiment Kentucky Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

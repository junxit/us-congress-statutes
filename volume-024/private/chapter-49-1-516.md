# Chapter 516

> An Act granting a pension to Martin J. Reynolds.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Martin J. Reynolds, late a private in Company K, Third Regiment Iowa Infantry Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, and pay to him a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 24, 1886.*

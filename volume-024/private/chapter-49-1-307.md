# Chapter 307

> An Act to increase the pension of Thomas G. Barton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior he, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Thomas G. Barton, and pay him at the rate of thirty dollars per month.

# Chapter 143

> An act granting a pension to William H. Blake.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William H. Blake, late of Company A, Sixty-sixth Regiment Illinois Volunteers.


*Approved, May 7, 1886.*

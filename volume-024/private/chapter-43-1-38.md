# Chapter 38

> An act granting a pension to Margaret A. Blake.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll of the United States the name of Margaret A. Blake, widow of George A. n. Blake, late Colonel of the First Cavalry and a brevet brigadier-general in the United States Army, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, March 29, 1886.*

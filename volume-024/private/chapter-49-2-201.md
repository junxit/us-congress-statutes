# Chapter 201

> An Act for the relief of William B. Barnes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William B. Barnes, dependent father of Louis W. Barnes, late of Company I, Sixteenth Regiment of Infantry Iowa Volunteers.


*Approved, February 22, 1887.*

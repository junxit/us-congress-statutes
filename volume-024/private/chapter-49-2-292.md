# Chapter 292

> An Act granting a pension to Henry Lafner.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the Dame of Henry Lafner, formerly a private in Company A, One hundred and eighty-first Regiment Ohio Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, February 28, 1887.*

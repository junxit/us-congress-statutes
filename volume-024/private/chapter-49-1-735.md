# Chapter 735

> An Act for the relief of Richard Horrigan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Richard Horrigan, at the rate per month which the medical examination entitles him to, by reason of rupture received while a member of Company A, Sixty-third Regiment New York Volunteers; that the pension granted by this act shall be in addition to the one he is now receiving for another disability.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

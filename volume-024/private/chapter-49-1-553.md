# Chapter 553

> An Act to increase the pension of David M. Rennoe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of David M. Rennoe, late a private in Company H of the Twenty-ninth Regiment Indiana Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension now received.


*Approved, June 24, 1886.*

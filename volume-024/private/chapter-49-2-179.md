# Chapter 179

> An Act granting a pension to John Selby.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Selby, dependent father of George Selby, late a private in Company C, Seventy-third Ohio Volunteers.


*Approved, February 22, 1887.*

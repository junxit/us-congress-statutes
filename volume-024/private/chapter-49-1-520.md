# Chapter 520

> An Act granting a pension to Samuel Robbins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Samuel Bobbins, late of Company A, Twelfth Regiment Missouri State Militia.


*Approved, June 24, 1886.*

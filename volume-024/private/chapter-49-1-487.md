# Chapter 487

> An Act for the relief of Catharine Johnson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Catharine Johnson, widow of Zachariah Johnson, deceased, late a private in Company C in the Sixtieth Regiment of Indiana Volunteers.


*Approved, June 24, 1886.*

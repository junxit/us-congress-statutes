# Chapter 425

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Mrs. Sarah P. McKean, of Marion, Linn County, Iowa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension of Mrs. Sarah 1*. McKean be, and the same.is hereby, increased to fifty dollars per month ; and the Commissioner of Pensions is hereby authorized and directed to place the name of said Mrs. Sarah F. McKean on the pension-roll as a pensioner of the United States for the sum of fifty dollars per month, said fifty dollars per month being in lien of all other pensions heretofore granted.


*Approved, June 19, 1886.*

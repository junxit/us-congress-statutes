# Chapter 793

> An act to pension Martha A. Vorhes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Martha A. Vorhes, widow of John Vorhes, late of Company A, Eighty-fourth Illinois Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, July 26, 1886.*

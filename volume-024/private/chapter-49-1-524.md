# Chapter 524

> An Act granting a pension to John H. Westerhouse, late of Company F, First Regiment Potomac Home Brigade (Thirteenth) Maryland Volunteers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions of the pension laws, the name of John H. Westerhouse, late of Company F, First Regiment Potomac Home Brigade (Thirteenth) Maryland Volunteers.


*Approved, June 24, 1886.*

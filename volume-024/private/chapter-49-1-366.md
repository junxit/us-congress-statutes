# Chapter 366

> An Act granting a pension to Elizabeth Barker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elizabeth Barker, widow of Henry Barker, who served in Captain Paul French’s company of Indiana militia during the early part of the war of eighteen hundred and twelve.


*Approved, May 22, 1886.*

# Chapter 303

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Samuel Baker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension of Samuel Baker, a soldier of the war of eighteen hundred and twelve, be, and the same is hereby, increased to twenty dollars per month, on account of wounds received in the line of duty while a volunteer in said war.

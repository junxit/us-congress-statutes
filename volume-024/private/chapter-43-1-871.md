# Chapter 871

> An act to grant a pension to James Robinson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of James Robinson, late of Company F, Fortieth Regiment United States Infantry.


*Approved, August 3, 1886.*

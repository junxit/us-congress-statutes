# Chapter 651

> An Act granting a pension to James L. McClarran.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of James L. McClarran, late of Company H, Twenty-third Regiment of Ohio Volunteer Infantry, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, and pay him a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

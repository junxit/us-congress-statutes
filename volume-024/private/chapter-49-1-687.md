# Chapter 687

> An Act granting a pension to Ann Little.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ann Little, widow of John Little, late a private in Company A, Eleventh Regiment Massachusetts Volunteers.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

# Chapter 178

> An Act granting a pension to Emily Louisa Spicer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in, Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Emily Louisa Spicer, widow of Commodore William F. Spicer, late of the United States Navy, and pay her at the rate of fifty dollars per month from and after the passage of this act, in lieu of the pension now received by her.


*Approved, February 22, 1887.*

# Chapter 404

> An Act granting a pension to William Bethuren.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and. House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William Bethuren, late of Company I, Fourteenth Regiment Kansas Cavalry.


*Approved, June 1, 1886.*

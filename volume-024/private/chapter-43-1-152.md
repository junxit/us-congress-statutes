# Chapter 152

> An act granting a pension to Minerva T. Thompson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Minerva T. Thompson, widow of Joseph Thompson, deceased, late a private in Company D of the Sixth Regiment of Missouri Cavalry Volunteers.


*Approved, May 7, 1886.*

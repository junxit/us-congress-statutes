# Chapter 106

> An act granting a pension to Edgar Payne.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Edgar Payne, late of Company B, One hundred and ninth Regiment United States Colored Infantry, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, May 7, 1886.*

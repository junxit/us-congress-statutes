# Chapter 106

> An Act granting a pension to Mary E. Hedrick.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary E. Hedrick, widow of Thomas H. Hedrick, late captain of Company K, Fifteenth Iowa Volunteers.


*Approved, February 4, 1887.*

# Chapter 737

> An Act for the relief of Euphemia R. Smith.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to all the limitations of the pension laws, the name of Euphemia R. Smith, widow of Garrett Smith, late of Company A, One hundred and ninth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

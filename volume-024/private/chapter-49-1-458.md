# Chapter 458

> An Act for the relief of Mrs. Sallie Ancrum.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Sallie Ancrum, widow of Aaron Ancrum, late of Company G, Thirty-fourth Regiment United States Infantry.


*Approved, June 19, 1886.*

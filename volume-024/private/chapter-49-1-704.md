# Chapter 704

> An Act granting a pension to John Arthur.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations and restrictions of the pension laws, the name of John Arthur, late of Company K, One hundred and sixtieth Regiment Ohio Volunteers.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

# Chapter 368

> An Act granting a pension to Sarah E. Bassett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah E. Bassett, daughter of Julius Bassett, late captain of Company A, Fifteenth Regiment Connecticut Volunteers, and to pay her a pension of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, May 22, 1886.*

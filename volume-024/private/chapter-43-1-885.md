# Chapter 885

> An act granting a pension to Thomas Walsh.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Thomas Walsh, late of Company F, Seventy-fourth Regiment of New York Volunteers,


*Approved, August 3, 1886.*

# Chapter 435

> An Act granting a pension to George W. Stout.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of George W. Stout, late a private in Company K, Seventieth Ohio Volunteers, on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws, from and after the passage of this act


*Approved, June 19, 1886.*

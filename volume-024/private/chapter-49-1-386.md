# Chapter 386

> An Act granting a pension to Jane Carr.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Jane Carr, widow of Colwell Carr, late a private in Company F, Thirtieth Regiment of Pennsylvania Volunteers, and Company E, Twentieth Regiment of the Veteran Reserve Corps, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, May 27, 1886.*

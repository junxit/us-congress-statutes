# Chapter 248

> An Act for the relief of Frank Shutt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to pay to Frank Shutt, post-master at Litchfield, Illinois, the sum of one hundred and ninety-three dollars and forty-cents, out of any money in the Treasury not other-wise appropriated, to reimburse him for postal funds stolen out of bis safe on the tenth day of November, eighteen hundred and eighty-five.


*Approved, February 23, 1887.*

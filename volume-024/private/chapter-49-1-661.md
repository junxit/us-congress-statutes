# Chapter 661

> An Act granting a pension to Lenford Rose.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, instructed to place on the pension-roll, subjected to the limitations and provisions of the pension law’s, the name of Lenford Rose, son of George W. Rose, late a lieutenant of Company I, Fifth Michigan Infantry, and pay to his legally constituted guardian, for his benefit, a pension of fifteen dollars a month.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

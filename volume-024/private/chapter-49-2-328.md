# Chapter 328

> An Act granting a pension to William B. Eve.

*Be it enacted by. the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be hereby is, directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of William B. Eve, late Second lieutenant of Company IT, Forty-ninth Kentucky Infantry Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, March 2, 1887.*

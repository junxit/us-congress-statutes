# Chapter 707

> An Act to increase the pension of Thomas J. Slayton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Thomas J. Slayton, late a private in Company H, Thirteenth Regiment Vermont Volunteers, from four dollars to fifteen dollars a month, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

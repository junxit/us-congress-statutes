# Chapter 164

> An act granting a pension to Jesse H. Gaines.

*Be it enacted by the Senate find House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and instructed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jesse H. Gaines, late first lieutenant of Company G, Second Regiment Michigan Volunteers.


*Approved, May 7, 1886.*

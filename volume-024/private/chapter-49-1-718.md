# Chapter 718

> An Act granting a pension to David L. McDermott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension- roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of David L. McDermott, late a private in Company K, of the One hundred and seventh Regiment of Pennsylvania Volunteers.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

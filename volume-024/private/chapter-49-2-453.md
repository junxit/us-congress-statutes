# Chapter 453

> An Act granting an increase of pension to H. H. Dodd.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to grant an increase of pension to H. H. Dodd, late of Company C, Sixteenth Regiment Ohio Veteran Volunteers, and to pay him, from and after the passage of this act, forty-five dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1887.*

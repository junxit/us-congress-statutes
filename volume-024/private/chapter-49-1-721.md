# Chapter 721

> An Act granting a pension to Eliza Newman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Eliza Newman, widow of the late Lazarus Newman, late a private in the One hundred and forty-ninth New York Volunteers.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

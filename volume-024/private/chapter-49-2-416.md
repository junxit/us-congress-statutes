# Chapter 416

> An Act granting a pension to Henry P. Reed.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Henry P. Reed, formerly a private in Company D, Fourth Pennsylvania Volunteers.


*Approved, March 3, 1887.*

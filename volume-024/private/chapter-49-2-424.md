# Chapter 424

> An Act to increase the pension of Lewis M. Strong.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lewis M. Strong, late of Company K, Third Regiment Vermont Volunteers, now a pensioner, at the rate of forty dollars per month.


*Approved, March 3, 1887.*

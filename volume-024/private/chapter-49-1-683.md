# Chapter 683

> An Act to increase the pension of John F. Chase

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of John F. Chase, late of the Fifth Maine Battery, to the sum of forty-six dollars per month.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

# Chapter 740

> An Act to increase the pension of Isaac N. Hawkins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension now granted to Isaac N. Hawkins, late a captain in the Seventy-third Regiment Ohio Volunteer Infantry, be increased to thirty-six dollars per month from and after the passage of this act, said pension to be in lieu of the one he is now receiving


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

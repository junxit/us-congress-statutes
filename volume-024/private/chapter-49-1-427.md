# Chapter 427

> An Act granting a pension to Newton Day.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Newton Day, late a private in Company B of the One hundred and fortieth Regiment Indiana Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, June 19, 1886.*

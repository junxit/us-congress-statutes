# Chapter 304

> An Act granting a pension to Isaac Askie.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Cot gross assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll the name of Isaac Askie, late a private in Company C, Thirty-seventh United States Colored Troops, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 28, 1887.*

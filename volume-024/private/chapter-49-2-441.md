# Chapter 441

> An Act granting a pension to James Smithpeter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of James Smithpeter, late of the East Tennessee Union service.


*Approved, March 3, 1887.*

# Chapter 162

> An act to place the name of James B. Wilde on the pension-roll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, instructed to place the name of James B. Wilde, late second lieutenant of Company C, Sixty-first New York Volunteer Infantry, on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws of the United States.


*Approved, May 7, 1886.*

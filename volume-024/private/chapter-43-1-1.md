# Chapter 1

> An act for the relief of Alexander R. Lawton, of the State of Georgia

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That Alexander R. Lawton, of the State of Georgia, is hereby relieved of all political disabilities imposed upon him by the third section of the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States.


*Approved, December 26, 1885.*

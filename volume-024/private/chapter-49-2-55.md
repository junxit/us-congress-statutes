# Chapter 55

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Colonel Charles E. Capehart.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Souse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Charles E. Capehart, late lieutenant-colonel of the First Regiment of West Virginia Cavalry, at the rate of forty-five dollars per month, in lieu of the pension he now receives.


*Approved, January 27, 1887.*

# Chapter 82

> An Act granting a pension to John Wines.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he. is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Wines, father of Rufus II. Wines, late a private in Company E, Twenty-sixth Regiment Michigan Volunteers.


*Approved, January 31, 1887.*

# Chapter 409

> An Act granting a pension to Jacob Teits.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jacob Teits, late of Company A, One hundred and forty-fourth Regiment, Illinois Infantry.


*Approved, March 3, 1887.*

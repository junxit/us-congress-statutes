# Chapter 331

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Mary Lathrop.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations and restrictions of the pension laws, the name of Mary Lathrop, widow of Henry Brooks, late a private in Company F, Twenty-sixth Regiment Connecticut Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, March 2, 1887.*

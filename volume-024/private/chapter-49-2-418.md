# Chapter 418

> An Act for the relief of George Lemon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to amend the records of the War Department in such a manner as to relieve of the charge of desertion the name of George Lemon, formerly a member of Company G. Fourth Regiment United States Infantry.


*Approved, March 3, 1887.*

# Chapter 884

> An act granting a pension to Mrs. Mary M. Gillham.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary M. Gillham, widow of James G. Gillham, late a private in Company I, Seventeenth Regiment of Iowa Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, August 3, 1886.*

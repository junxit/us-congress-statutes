# Chapter 313

> An Act for the relief of Robert Moran.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension paid to Robert Moran, late lieutenant-colonel of the Second Regiment of West Virginia Volunteers, to forty-five dollars per month.

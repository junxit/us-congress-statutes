# Chapter 438

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Charles Sebring.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, ami he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Charles Sebring, late a private in Company F of the Forty- eighth Regiment Indiana Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension now received.


*Approved, June 19, 1886.*

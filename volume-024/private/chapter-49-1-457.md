# Chapter 457

> An Act granting a pension to William M. Swartz.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William M. Swartz, late of Company F, Forty-sixth Regiment of Ohio Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, June 19, 1886.*

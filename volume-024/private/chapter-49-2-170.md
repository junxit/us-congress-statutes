# Chapter 170

> An Act for the relief of Mrs. Sophia Sprain, widow, and two minor children of Louis F. Sprain.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Mrs. Sophia Sprain, widow, and tw*o minor children of Louis F. Sprain, late a private in Company D, Fourteenth Wisconsin Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 21, 1887.*

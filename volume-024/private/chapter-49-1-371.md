# Chapter 371

> An Act for the relief of Rebecca E. Haskin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

Th at the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Rebecca E. Haskin, widow of Lieutenant-Colonel Joseph A. Haskin, United States Army, the pension provided by law for the widow of a lieutenant-colonel in the United States Army, in lieu of the pension she is now receiving.


*Approved, May 22, 1886.*

# Chapter 861

> An act granting an increase of pension to William H. H. Buck.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of William H. H. Buck, late of Company G, First Vermont Cavalry, upon the pension-roll, subject to the limitations of the pension laws, at the rate of twelve dollars per month, instead of six dollars per month, as provided by act of Congress approved March third, eighteen hundred and seventy-nine.


*Approved, August 3, 1886.*

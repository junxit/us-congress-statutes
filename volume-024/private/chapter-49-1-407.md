# Chapter 407

> An Act granting a pension to Henry Shively.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Henry Shively, late first lieutenant of Company D, Twelfth Kansas Volunteers.


*Approved, June 1, 1886.*

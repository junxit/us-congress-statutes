# Chapter 111

> An Act for the relief of the heirs of Malitty Rose.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to James E. Rose, Jane Godfrey, and Francis Patton, the only surviving children of Malitty Rose, deceased, the money due upon reissue certificate numbered thirty- two hundred and fifty-nine, and issued to the said Malitty Rose three weeks prior to her death, as the widow of Elias Rose, late a private in Captain McCoy’s company, United States infantry, of the Florida war.


*Approved, February 4, 1887.*

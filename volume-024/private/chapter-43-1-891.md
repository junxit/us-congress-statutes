# Chapter 891

> An act granting a pension to Martin V. Curry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Martin V. Curry, late a private in Company Q, First Regiment United States Volunteers.


*Approved, August 3, 1886.*

# Chapter 268

> An Act granting a pension to Robert Baxter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Robert Baxter, late a private in Company D, Seventy-seventh Regiment Enrolled Missouri Militia,


*Approved, February 25, 1887.*

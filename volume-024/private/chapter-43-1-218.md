# Chapter 218

> An act increasing the pension of John P. Davis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

[Note by the Department of State.—The foregoing act having been presented to the President of the United States for his approval, and not having been returned by him to the house of Congress in which it originated within the time prescribed by the Constitution of the United States, has become a law without his approval.]

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of John P. Davis, late a sergeant of Company H, Twelfth Regiment New Hampshire Volunteers, from twelve dollars to thirty dollars per month, from and after the passage of this act, said sum to be in lieu of any pension now paid him.

Received by the President, April 27, 1886.

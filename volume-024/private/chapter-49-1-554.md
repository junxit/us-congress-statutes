# Chapter 554

> An Act for the relief of Frances Mosher.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Frances Mosher, widow of Hiram A. Mosher, late a private in Company F, Tenth Minnesota Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, June 24, 1886.*

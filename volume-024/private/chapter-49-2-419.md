# Chapter 419

> An Act granting a pension to Missouri Anderson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Missouri Anderson, widow of Reuben W, Anderson, late of Company A, Tenth Regiment Kentucky Volunteers.


*Approved, March 3, 1887.*

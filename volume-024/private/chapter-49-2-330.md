# Chapter 330

> An Act granting a pension to Mary McCoy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be hereby is, authorized and directed to place the name of Mary McCoy on the pension-roll, and to pay her a pension from and after the passage of this act, at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, March 2, 1887.*

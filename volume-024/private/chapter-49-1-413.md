# Chapter 413

> An Act for the relief of Silas Corzatt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized to place on the pension-roll, the name of Silas Corzatt, late a private in Company K, Second Ohio Cavalry, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, June 1, 1886.*

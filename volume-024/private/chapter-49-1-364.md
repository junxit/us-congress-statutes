# Chapter 364

> An Act granting a pension to William Weightman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William Weightman, dependent father of Albert Weightman, late of Company L, Seventh Regiment Michigan Cavalry.


*Approved, May 22, 1886.*

# Chapter 109

> An Act granting a pension to Rebecca Wiswell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Rebecca Wiswell, an Army nurse, on the pension roll, and pay her a pension of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 4, 1887.*

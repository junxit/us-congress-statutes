# Chapter 638

> An Act for the relief of Elias B. Moore.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of three hundred and twenty-five dollars be, and the same is hereby appropriated, out of any moneys in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the relief of Elias B. Moore, ex-postmaster at Fayetteville, Arkansas, that being the amount of postal stamps taken possession of by the United States Army in eighteen hundred and sixty-two, the value of which has been paid to the Government.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

# Chapter 119

> An act granting a pension to James McAnny.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of James McAnny, late quartermaster-sergeant of Company C, First Regiment New Jersey Cavalry.


*Approved, May 7, 1886.*

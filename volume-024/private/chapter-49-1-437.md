# Chapter 437

> An Act granting a pension to John H. Snyder.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John II. Snyder, late a private in Company C, One hundred and sixth New York Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, June 19, 1886.*

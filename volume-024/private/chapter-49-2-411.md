# Chapter 411

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs Almira Ambler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Mrs Almira Ambler, a volunteer nurse in the late war, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, and pay her the sum of twenty dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1887.*

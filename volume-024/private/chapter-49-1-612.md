# Chapter 612

> An Act granting a pension to Anna Kessinger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Anna Kessinger, widow of Daniel F. Kessinger, late a private in Company A, Sixth Regiment of Ohio Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws. This act to take effect from and after its passage.


*Approved, July 2, 1886.*

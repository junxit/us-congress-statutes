# Chapter 541

> An Act for the relief of Randolph Seaman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Randolph Seaman, late a private in Company U, Sixth Minnesota Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, June 24, 1886.*

# Chapter 107

> An Act granting a pension to Jacob Case.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jacob Case, late of Company D, Thirtieth Regiment New Jersey Volunteers.


*Approved, February 4, 1887.*

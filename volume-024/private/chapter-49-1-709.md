# Chapter 709

> An Act granting a pension to Isaac Fossett.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be directed to place the name of Isaac Fossett, late a private in Company K, First Minnesota Cavalry, on the pension-roll, and pay him a pension, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, from the date of the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

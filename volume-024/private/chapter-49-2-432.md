# Chapter 432

> An Act granting a pension to Moses Williams

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Moses Williams, late a private of the United States Marine Corps.


*Approved, March 3, 1887.*

# Chapter 89

> An Act for the relief of George W. Robaugh.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George W. Robaugh, late of Company C, Twenty-third Regiment Kansas Militia.


*Approved, February 2, 1887.*

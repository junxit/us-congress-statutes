# Chapter 90

> An act granting a pension to Mary A. Tibbets.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary A. Tibbets, mother of Edward I. Tibbets, late a private in Company K, Eighty-second Regiment Indiana Volunteers.


*Approved, May 7, 1886.*

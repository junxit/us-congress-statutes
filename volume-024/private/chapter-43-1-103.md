# Chapter 103

> An act granting a pension to Frederick Bottjer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Frederick Bottjer, late a private in Company I, Eighth Regiment New York Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, May 7, 1886.*

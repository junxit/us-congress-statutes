# Chapter 491

> An Act granting a pension to Mary Merrill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Mary Merrill, widow of Alexander Merrill, late a private in Company L, Twelfth Tennessee Cavalry.


*Approved, June 24, 1886.*

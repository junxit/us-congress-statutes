# Chapter 127

> An act granting a pension to Cornelia W. Ellis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension roll the name of Cornelia W. Ellis, widow of Edmund C. Ellis, late of the United States Navy, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, May 7, 1886.*

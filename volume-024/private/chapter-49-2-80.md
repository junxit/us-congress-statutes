# Chapter 80

> An Act granting a pension to Alonzo Raymond.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Alonzo Raymond, father of Flatt Green Raymond, late a private in Company E, Fifth Regiment Wisconsin Volunteers, and pay him the sum of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, January 31, 1887.*

# Chapter 465

> An Act for the relief of Lydia O. Hutchings.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Lydia O. Hutchings, mother of George T. Pomeroy, late a private in Company C, One hundred and eighty-sixth Regiment New York Volunteers, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, June 22, 1886.*

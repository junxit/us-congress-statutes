# Chapter 161

> An act granting a pension to J Francis Hopper.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of J. Francis Hopper, late captain in Company C, Sixth Missouri Volunteer Cavalry.


*Approved, May 7, 1886.*

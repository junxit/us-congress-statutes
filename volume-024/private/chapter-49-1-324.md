# Chapter 324

> An Act granting a pension to Eliza Humes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Eliza Humes, mother of George W. Humes, deceased, late a private in Company E, Second Maine Cavalry Volunteers.

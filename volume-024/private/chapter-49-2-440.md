# Chapter 440

> An Act for the relief of Miss Eula E. Henry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster-General be, and he is hereby, authorized, in his discretion, to credit the account of Miss Eula E. Henry, late postmaster at Centre Point, Arkansas, with the sum of two hundred and two dollars, lost by a robbery of the mails at or near Hope, Arkansas, on or about November thirtieth, eighteen hundred and eighty-four.


*Approved, March 3, 1887.*

# Chapter 911

> An act for the relief of S. B. Cranston, of Oregon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to S. B. Cranston, of Oregon, the sum of forty dollars, on account of traveling and contingent expenses by him incurred in taking depositions in Oregon in the land case of William Bauerineister, as instructed by the Commissioner of the General Land Office, in eighteen hundred and seventy-seven.


*Approved, August 4, 1886.*

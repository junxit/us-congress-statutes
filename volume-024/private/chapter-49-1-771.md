# Chapter 771

> An Act for the relief of Mrs Maria Walker.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to restore to the pension-roll the name of Mrs Maria Walker, widow of John H. Walker, a soldier of the Revolutiary war, from and after March fourth, eighteen hundred and sixty, and pay her pension therefrom deducting any sums received since that date.


*Approved, July 14, 1886.*

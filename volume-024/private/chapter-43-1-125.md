# Chapter 125

> An act for the relief of Sarah Manz.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah Manz, dependent mother of Christian G. Manz, alias John Stevens, formerly of Company H, Sixty seventh Regiment New York State Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, May 7, 1886.*

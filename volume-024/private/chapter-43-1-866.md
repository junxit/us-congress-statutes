# Chapter 866

> An act granting a pension to Sally A. Stone.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to place on the pension- roll the name of Sally A. Stone, dependent mother of Lieutenant-Colonel Henry M. Stone, deceased, late of the Third New York Volunteer Artillery, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, and to pay her the pension allowed by law to the dependent relatives of deceased soldiers.


*Approved, August 3, 1886.*

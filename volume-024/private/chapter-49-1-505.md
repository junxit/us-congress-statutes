# Chapter 505

> An Act granting a pension to Margaret Mayham.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Margaret Mayham, widow of William Mayham, late lieutenant of Company A, One hundred and forty-fourth Regiment New York Volunteers.


*Approved, June 24, 1886.*

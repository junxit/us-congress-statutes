# Chapter 481

> An Act granting a pension to Mary Manes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Manes, mother of William Manes, late a member of Company D, Fourth Regiment of Wisconsin Cavalry Volunteers.


*Approved, June 24, 1886.*

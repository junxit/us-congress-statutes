# Chapter 513

> An Act granting a pension to George Anna Lippe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George Anna Lippe, widow of Captain Constantine Lippe, late of Company A, One hundred and eighty-eighth Pennsylvania Volunteers.


*Approved, June 24, 1886.*

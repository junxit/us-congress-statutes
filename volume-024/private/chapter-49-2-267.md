# Chapter 267

> An Act for the relief of Maria L. Strong.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That from and after the passage of this act there be paid, out of the Navy pension fund, to Maria L. Strong, widow of the late Rear-Admiral James H. Strong, of the United States Navy, the sum of fifty dollars per month, during her widowhood, the same to be in lieu of her present pension.


*Approved, February 25, 1887.*

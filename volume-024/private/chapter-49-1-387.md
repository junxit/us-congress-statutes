# Chapter 387

> An Act granting a pension to William H. Moore.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William H. Moore, late of Company F, Thirtieth Regiment of Iowa Volunteers.


*Approved, May 27, 1886.*

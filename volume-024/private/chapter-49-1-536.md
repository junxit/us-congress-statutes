# Chapter 536

> An Act for the relief of Bailey Hascall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Bailey Hascall, late an acting assistant paymaster in the United States Navy, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, June 24, 1886.*

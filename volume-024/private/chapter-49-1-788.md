# Chapter 788

> An Act granting a pension to Hiram L. Wait.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Hiram L. Wait, late a private in Company C, Third Regiment of Iowa Infantry.


*Approved, July 26, 1886.*

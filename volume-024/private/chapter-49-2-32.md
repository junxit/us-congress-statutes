# Chapter 32

> An Act granting a pension to Samuel E. Tracy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed,to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Samuel E. Tracy, late of the United States Navy.


*Approved, January 18, 1887.*

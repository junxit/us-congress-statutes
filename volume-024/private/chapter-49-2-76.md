# Chapter 76

> An Act granting-a pension to Joseph Mays.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Joseph Mays, late of Company M, Ninth Regiment Kansas Cavalry Volunteers.


*Approved, January 29, 1887.*

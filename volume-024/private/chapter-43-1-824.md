# Chapter 824

> An act granting an increase of pension to John Ryan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of John Ryan, late First Lieutenant of Company F, Sixty-ninth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers, and pay to him a pension of seventeen dollars per month, in lieu of the pension now paid to him, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 30, 1886.*

# Chapter 100

> An Act granting a pension to John Patton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of John Patton, late of Company C, Tenth Regiment of New York Heavy Artillery.


*Approved, February 3, 1887.*

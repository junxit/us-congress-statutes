# Chapter 224

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Sarah Randles.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be,and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah Randles, widow of William Randles, late captain of Company I, Ninety-third Regiment of New York Volunteers.


*Approved, February 23, 1887.*

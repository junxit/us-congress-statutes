# Chapter 244

> An act for the relief of John W. Robson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United. States of America in Congress assembled,*

[Note by the Department of State.—The foregoing act having been presented to the President of the United States for his approval, and not having been returned by him to the house of Congress in which it originated within the time prescribed by the Constitution of the United States, has become a law without his approval.]

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws, the name of John W. Robson, late a private in the Sixth Wisconsin Battery.

Received by the President, April 27, 1886.

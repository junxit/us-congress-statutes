# Chapter 380

> An Act granting a pension to Eunice E. Clark.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Eunice E. Clark, widow of Daniel B. Clark, late of Company A, First Ohio Cavalry, at the rate of twelve dollars per month subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws.


*Approved, May 26, 1886.*

# Chapter 451

> An Act for the relief of George D. Paul.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in view of the faithful services rendered by George D. Paid as a member of Company A of the Fourth Regiment of Michigan Infantry Volunteers, during the war of the late rebellion, he be regarded in the administration of the pension laws as though no charge of desertion had been made against him upon any records of service prior to that in the said Fourth Regiment of Michigan Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, March 3, 1887.*

# Chapter 215

> An act to restore to the pension-roll the name of Andrew J. Fuller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

[Note by the Department of State.—The foregoing act having been presented to the President of the United States for his approval, and not having been returned by him to the house of Congress in which it originated within the time prescribed by the Constitution of the United States, has become a law without his approval.]

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to restore to the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Andrew J. Fuller, late a member of Company I, First. Missouri State Militia.

Received by the President, April 27, 1886.

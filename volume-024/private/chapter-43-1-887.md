# Chapter 887

> An act to increase the pension of Jonas Schoonover

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to increase, the pension of Jonas Schoonover, late colonel of the Twenty-ninth Regiment Ohio Infantry Volunteers (who is now on the pension-roll under certificate of invalid pensions numbered, at the rate of thirty dollars per month), to forty dollars per month.


*Approved, August 3, 1886.*

# Chapter 838

> An act to place the name of Jacob Madison Pruitt on the pension-roll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jacob Madison Pruitt.

## § 2.

That this act shall be in force from and after its passage.


*Approved, July 31, 1886.*

# Chapter 406

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Joseph W. Rhinehalt.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the amount of pension allowed Joseph W. Rhinehalt, late of Company D, Thirty-fourth Ohio Volunteer Infantry, to the sum of twenty dollars per month, in lieu of the amount he is now receiving.


*Approved, June 1, 1886.*

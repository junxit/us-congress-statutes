# Chapter 97

> An Act granting a pension to Thomas Brown.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Thomas Brown, late a private in Company D, Thirty-first Regiment New Jersey Volunteers.


*Approved, February 3, 1887.*

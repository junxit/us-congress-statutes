# Chapter 167

> An act granting a pension to John G. Burrill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United’ States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of John G. Burrill, late first lieutenant of Company G, Fourteenth Indiana Volunteers, subject to the conditions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, May 7, 1886.*

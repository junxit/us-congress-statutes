# Chapter 665

> An Act for the relief of Samuel C. Fisher.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Samuel C. Fisher, formerly a member of Company H, One hundred and sixteenth Regiment Indiana State Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

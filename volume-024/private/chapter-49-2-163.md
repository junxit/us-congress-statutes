# Chapter 163

> An Act for the relief of Francis H. Shaw.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War is hereby authorized and directed to grant to Francis H. Shaw, late captain of Company C, Fifty-fifth Illinois United States Volunteer Infantry, an honorable discharge and muster-out of the service of the United States.

## § 2.

That nothing in this act contained shall be held to authorize the payment of any money to said Francis H. Shaw on account of the honorable discharge and muster-out as hereinbefore provided.


*Approved, February 21, 1887.*

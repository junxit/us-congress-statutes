# Chapter 875

> An act granting a pension to Ellen J. Welch.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ellen J. Welch, widow of John H. Welch, late of the Third Massachusetts Light Battery Artillery.


*Approved, August 3, 1886.*

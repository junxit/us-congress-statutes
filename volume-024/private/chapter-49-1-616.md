# Chapter 616

> An Act granting a pension to Gilbert A. Philips.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Gilbert A. Philips, of Louisville, Kentucky, who was appointed a quartermaster in the United States Army in eighteen hundred and sixty-one, by order of General William Nelson.


*Approved, July 2, 1886.*

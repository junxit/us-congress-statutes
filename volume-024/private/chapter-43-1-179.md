# Chapter 179

> An act for the relief of Catherine Collins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

[Note by the Department of State.—The foregoing act having been presented to the President of the United States for his approval, and not having been returned by him to the house of Congress in which it originated within the time prescribed by the Constitution of the United States, has become a law without his approval.]

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the regulations and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Catherine Collins, the dependent mother of John Collins, late of Company G, Eighth Veteran Reserve Corps, to commence from and after the passage of this act.

Received by the President, April 27, 1886.

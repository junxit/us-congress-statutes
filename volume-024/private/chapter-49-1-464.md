# Chapter 464

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Abel Comstock.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and Hanse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Abel Comstock, late a private in Company C, Twenty-first Regiment Ohio Veteran Infantry Volunteers, at the rate of thirty dollars per month, in lieu of what he is now receiving.


*Approved, June 22, 1886.*

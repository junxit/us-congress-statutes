# Chapter 53

> An Act granting a pension to Louise Armbrecht

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Louise Armbrecht, the widow of Augustus L. Armbrecht, late a sergeant in the Army of the United States.


*Approved, January 27, 1887.*

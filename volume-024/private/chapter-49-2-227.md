# Chapter 227

> An Act granting a pension to Daniel Schultheis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United: States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Daniel Schultheis, late of Company H, Forty-ninth Illinois- Volunteers.


*Approved, February 23, 1887.*

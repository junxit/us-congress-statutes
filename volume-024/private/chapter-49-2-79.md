# Chapter 79

> An Act granting a pension to William F. Harmon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William F. Harmon, late of Company C, Cue hundred and., forty-eighth Regiment of Ohio Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, January 31, 1887.*

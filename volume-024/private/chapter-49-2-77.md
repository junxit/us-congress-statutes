# Chapter 77

> An Act granting a pension to Eliza Wilkins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of t he Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Eliza Wilkins, widow of Orrin Wilkins, late a private in Company A, First Regiment of Iowa Cavalry.


*Approved, January 29, 1887.*

# Chapter 279

> An act increasing the pension of John P. Brinegar.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled.,*

[Note by the Department of State.—The foregoing act having been presented to the President of the United States for his approval, and not having been returned by him to the house of Congress in which it originated within the time prescribed by the Constitution of the United States, has become a law without his approval.]

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of John P. Brinegar, late a Corporal of Company E, Fourth Regiment of Kentucky Volunteers, to forty dollars per mouth (certificate numbered one hundred and fourteen thousand seven hundred and sixty-seven).

Received by the President, April 27, 1886.

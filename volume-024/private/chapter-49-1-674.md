# Chapter 674

> An Act granting a pension to Henrietta Fisher.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed and authorized to put the name of Henrietta Fisher, widow of Thaddeus S. Fisher, deceased, late of Company B, Fourth United States Colored Troops, on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

# Chapter 107

> An act granting a pension to Daniel T. Ferguson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Daniel T. Ferguson, late of Company K, Fifth Regiment of Kentucky Cavalry, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, May 7, 1886.*

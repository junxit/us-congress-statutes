# Chapter 543

> An Act for the relief of Nancy Franklin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the conditions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Nancy Franklin, of Madison County, North Carolina, dependent mother of Balis Norton and James Norton, late of the Second Regiment of North Carolina Volunteers, United States Army, and Josiah Norton, late of the Third North Carolina Regiment, United States Volunteers, all of whom were killed in fight on Laurel, North Carolina, September, eighteen hundred and sixty-four.


*Approved, June 24, 1886.*

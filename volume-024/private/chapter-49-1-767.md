# Chapter 767

> An Act to amend and correct the act approved June first, eighteen hundred and eighty-six, granting a pension to J. H. Thornburg.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the act approved June first, eighteen hundred and eighty-six, granting a pension to J. H. Thornbury, is hereby amended and corrected so as to read: That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of J. H. Thornburg, late of Company D, Eighth Regiment of Iowa Cavalry Volunteers.

Sec. 2. That the pension shall commence from the date of the approval of the original act.


*Approved, July 14, 1886.*

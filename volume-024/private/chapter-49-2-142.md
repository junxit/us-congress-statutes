# Chapter 142

> An Act for the relief of Richard H. Porter, surviving partner and assignee of the late firm of R. H. and James Porter.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Richard H. Porter surviving partner and assignee of the late firm of R. II. and James Porter the sum of ten thousand one hundred dollars, less the sum of seven hundred and fifty dollars heretofore paid them, in full for all claims for damages or compensation for property impressed by order of Colonel Johnston, in command of the United States troops en route for Utah in eighteen hundred and fifty-seven.

# Chapter 153

> An Act granting a pension to James S. Furniss.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of James S. Fnrniss, late a private in Company E, Seventy-fourth New York Volunteers, who enlisted May fifth, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, and was discharged May thirtieth, eighteen hundred and sixty-five.


*Approved, February 19, 1887.*

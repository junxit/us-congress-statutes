# Chapter 93

> An act granting a pension to George H. Campbell.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the conditions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of George H. Campbell, late lieutenant-colonel of the One hundred and sixth Illinois Infantry Volunteers of the late war, and to pay him a pension as provided in such laws.


*Approved, May 7, 1886.*

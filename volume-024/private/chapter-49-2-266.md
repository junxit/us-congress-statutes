# Chapter 266

> An Act granting a pension to Anna M. Marshall.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of A meriea in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Anna M. Marshall, widow of Captain Patterson 8. Marshall, on the pension-roll as a pensioner, at the rate of twelve dollars a month, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, February 25, 1887.*

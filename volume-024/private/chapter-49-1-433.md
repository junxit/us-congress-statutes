# Chapter 433

> An Act for the relief of Evaline A. White.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, directed to place the name of Evaline A. White, widow of George O. White, deceased, late a second-class fireman in the United States Navy, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, June 19, 1886.*

# Chapter 869

> An act granting a pension to Sallie B. Bent.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sallie B. Bent, dependent mother of David P. Bent, late a private in Company G, Fourth Regiment Vermont Volunteers.


*Approved, August 3, 1886.*

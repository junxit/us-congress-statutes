# Chapter 717

> An Act granting a pension to Amos C. Wertz.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Stales of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to cause to be placed on the pension-roll the name of Amos C. Wertz, late a sergeant of Company L, Second Regiment United States Artillery, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

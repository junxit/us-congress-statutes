# Chapter 109

> An act granting a pension to Lewis A. Thornbury.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Lewis A. Thornbury, late a sergeant in the Thirty-ninth Regiment Kentucky Volunteers.


*Approved, May 7, 1886.*

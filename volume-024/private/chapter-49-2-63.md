# Chapter 63

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Clara B. Davidson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Clara B. Davidson, widow of General John W. David-son, United States Army, and pay her a pension of fifty dollars per month, in lieu of the amount she is now receiving


*Approved, January 27, 1887.*

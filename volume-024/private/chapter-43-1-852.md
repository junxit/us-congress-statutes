# Chapter 852

> An act for the relief of Ellen Sadler, sister of John Sadler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Ellen Sadler, sister of John Sadler, formerly of Company E, Sixty-eighth Regiment of Pennsylvania Volunteers, and pay her a pension at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, August 3, 1886.*

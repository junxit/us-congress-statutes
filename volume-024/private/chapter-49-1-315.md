# Chapter 315

> An Act for the relief of Elizabeth Leffman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elizabeth Lelfman, widow of Lewis Leffman, late an ordnance-sergeant in the United States Army, to take effect from the passage of this act, and that she be paid a pension of twenty-five dollars per month.

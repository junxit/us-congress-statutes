# Chapter 789

> An Act to increase the pension to Samuel Frost.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Samuel Frost, late a lieutenant of Company F, Eighty-fourth Illinois Regiment, and pay him a pension of twenty-four dollars per month, in lieu of the pension now granted him.


*Approved, July 26, 1886.*

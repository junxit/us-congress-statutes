# Chapter 270

> An act to restore to the pension-roll the name of Sarah J. Prater.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

[Note by the Department of State.—The foregoing act having been presented to the President of the United States for his approval, and not having been returned by him to the house of Congress in which it originated within the time prescribed by the Constitution of the United States, has become a law without his approval.]

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to restore to the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah J. Prater, widow of William Prater, deceased, late of Company I, Fourteenth Regiment Kentucky Volunteers, the pension to date from the ninth day of September, eighteen hundred and seventy-eight.

Received by the President, April 27, 1886.

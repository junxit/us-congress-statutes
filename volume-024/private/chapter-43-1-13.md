# Chapter 13

> An act to remove the political disabilities of Thomas L. Rosser.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all disabilities imposed upon and incurred by Thomas L. Bosser, of Virginia, by virtue of the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution, be, and are hereby, removed.


*Approved, March 2, 1886.*

# Chapter 135

> An act for placing the name of Mrs. Ann J. Conwell on the pension-roll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Ann J. Conwell, widow of Matthew Conwell, late a private in Company I, First Indiana Cavalry Volunteers.


*Approved, May 7, 1886.*

# Chapter 727

> An Act granting a pension to Margaret J. Reighter

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to placeon the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Margaret J. Reighter, widow of Andrew J. Reighter, late of Company H, First Regiment Pennsylvania Reserve Volunteers.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

# Chapter 445

> An Act granting a pension to Charlotte Algier, widow of Samuel Algier.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Charlotte Algier, widow of Samuel Algier, late a private in Company G, Thirty-first Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers.


*Approved, June 19, 1886.*

# Chapter 25

> An act for the relief of Sarah B. Jackson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension-laws, the name of Sarah B. Jackson, widow of William B. Jackson, late of Company A, Ninety-first Regiment New York Volunteers.


*Approved, March 19, 1886.*

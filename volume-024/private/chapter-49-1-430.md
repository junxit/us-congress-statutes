# Chapter 430

> An Act granting a pension to Jane W. Devereux.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Jane W. Devereux, mother of George N. Devereux, late of the Tenth Massachusetts Battery, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, June 19, 1886.*

# Chapter 370

> An Act granting a pension to Patrick A. Callanan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That, the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Patrick A. Callanan, late a member of the First Massachusetts Heavy Artillery.


*Approved, May 22, 1886.*

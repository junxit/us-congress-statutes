# Chapter 912

> An act for the relief of Mrs. Henrietta H. Cole.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the patent granted to Henrietta H. Cole, of New York, for improvement in tinting machines, reissue numbered forty-three hundred and forty-nine, on the twenty-fifth day of April, eighteen hundred and seventy-one, be, and the same is hereby, referred to the Commissioner of Patents, who shall determine the same upon testimony; and if, in Ins judgment, the petitioner has not been compensated for her time and ingenuity in perfecting her patent, he shall have authority to extend it for seven years from the twelfth day of June, eighteen hundred and eighty-three.


*Approved, August 4, 1886.*

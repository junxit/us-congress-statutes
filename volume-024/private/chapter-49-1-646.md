# Chapter 646

> An Act granting a pension to Annie Bagley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Annie Bagley, widow of Robert F. Bagley, late a sergeant of Company F, Fifty-first New York Volunteers.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

# Chapter 732

> An Act to increase the pension of Michael McGrayel.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Michael McGrayel, late of Company B, Ninety-eighth Illinois Volunteers, to thirty-six dollars per month, in lieu of the pension now received by him under certificate seventy-six thousand six hundred and twenty-five.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

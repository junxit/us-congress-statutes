# Chapter 847

> An act for the relief of James Cain.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and is hereby, authorized to change the record of James Cain, late a captain in the One hundred and fifty-eighth Regiment of Infantry, New York State Volunteers, and grant to him an honorable discharge, with the rank held by him at the time his connection with the service was severed: Provided, That such change of record does not involve any claim for pay or services from the United States.


*Approved, August 2, 1886.*

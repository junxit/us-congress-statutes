# Chapter 658

> An Act granting a pension to Louis Whorley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Louis Whorley, late of Company F, Fifth Regiment United States Artillery.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

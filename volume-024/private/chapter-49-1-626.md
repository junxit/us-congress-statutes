# Chapter 626

> An Act granting a pension to Lydia Hadlock.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the act of Congress approved March ninth, eighteen hundred and seventy-eight (twentieth Statutes at Large, page twenty-seven), the name of Lydia Hadlock, of Lexington, Massachusetts, widow of Colburn Hadlock, who served three months in the war of eighteen hundred and twelve.


*Approved, July 3, 1886.*

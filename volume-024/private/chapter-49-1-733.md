# Chapter 733

> An Act granting a pension to Levi M. Starne.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Levi M. Starne, late a private in Company B, Eighty-sixth Indiana Volunteers.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

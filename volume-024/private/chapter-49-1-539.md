# Chapter 539

> An Act for the relief of Joseph Guy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the In terior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Joseph Guy, formerly a member of Company C, One hundred and twenty-first Regiment Ohio infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, June 24, 1886.*

# Chapter 308

> An Act granting a pension to John W. Thornton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of John W. Thornton, of Lawrence, Kansas, and pay him a pension for gunshot wounds received in defending the city of Lawrence during the Quantrill raid, subject to the restrictions and limitations of the pension laws.

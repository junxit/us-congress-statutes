# Chapter 614

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Honorah Maloney.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Honorah Maloney, of Dubuque City, Iowa, widow of Daniel Maloney, late a private in Company G, One hundred and eighty third Pennsylvania Infantry.


*Approved, July 2, 1886.*

# Chapter 473

> An Act to restore to the pension-roll the name of Adam Gaston.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to restore to the pension-roll the name of Adam Gaston, late of Company D, One hundred and fortieth Indiana Volunteers, who held certificate one hundred and seven thousand eight hundred and eighty-one.


*Approved, June 24, 1886.*

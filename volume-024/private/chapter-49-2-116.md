# Chapter 116

> An Act to increase the pension of Charles Hahneman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United' States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be hereby is, authorized to increase the pension of Charles Hahneman, late a private in Company C, Forty-first New York Volunteers, and pay him at the rate of twenty-four dollars per month, in lieu of the pension now paid to him.


*Approved, February 4, 1887.*

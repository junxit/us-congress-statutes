# Chapter 414

> An Act for the relief of Mary Hill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

CHAP 414.—An act for the relief of Mary Hill.

That the Secretary of the Interior be directed to place the name of Mary Hill, a nurse in the Army, on the pension-roll, at the rate of twenty-live dollars per month


*Approved, June 1, 1886.*

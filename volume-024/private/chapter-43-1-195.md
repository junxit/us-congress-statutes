# Chapter 195

> An act granting a pension to Aurelia F. Robbins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

[Note by the Department of State.—The foregoing act having been presented to the President of the United States for his approval, and not having been returned by him to the house of Congress in which it originated within the time prescribed by the Constitution of the United States, has become a law without his approval.]

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, at the rate of twelve dollars per month, from and after the passage of this act, the name of Aurelia F. Robbins, dependent daughter of the late Daniel Robbins, a private in Company B, (Second Battery), First Regiment of Maine Light Artillery.

Received by the President, April 27, 1886.

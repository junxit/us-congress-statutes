# Chapter 784

> An Act to remove the political disabilities of William H. F. Lee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That all political disabilities imposed by the fourteenth amendment of the Constitution of the United States be, and the same are hereby, removed from William H. F. Lee, of Virginia.


*Approved, July 26, 1886.*

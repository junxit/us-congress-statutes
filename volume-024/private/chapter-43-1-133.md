# Chapter 133

> An act granting a pension to Sarah A. Gettis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws, the name of Sarah A. Gettis, dependent mother of Lewis J. Gettis, deceased, late a private in Company I, Thirtieth Iowa Infantry Volunteers, who enlisted under the name of Lewis J. McCoy


*Approved, May 7, 1886.*

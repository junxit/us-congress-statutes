# Chapter 221

> An act granting a pension to Margaret Flaherty.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

[Note by the Department of State.—The foregoing act having been presented to the President of the United States for his approval, and not having been returned by him to the house of Congress in which it originated within the time prescribed by the Constitution of the United States, has become a law without his approval.]

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, at the rate of eight dollars per month, the name of Margaret Flaherty, dependent stepmother of Bartlett Flaherty, late a private in Company F, Thirty-first Maine Volunteers, said pension to commence March third, eighteen hundred and eighty-five.

Received by the President, April 27, 1886.

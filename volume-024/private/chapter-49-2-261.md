# Chapter 261

> An Act granting a pension to M. Louise Butler.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of M. Louise Butler, widow of George Butler, late a major of marines in the service of the United States, and pay her a pension at the rate of forty dollars per month; said pension to be in lieu of any she may hereafter receive.


*Approved, February 25, 1887.*

# Chapter 925

> An act for the relief of Beaufort Lee and others.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to pay out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the following specified sums to the persons herein named, for services rendered us laborers in the employ of the House of Representatives of the Forty-fifth Congress, namely:

To Beaufort Lee, the sum of one hundred and eighty dollars.

To Alexander Thomas, the sum of two hundred and sixty dollars.

To James Hall, the sum of two hundred and sixty dollars.

To Charles Carter, the sum of two hundred and sixty dollars.


*Approved, August 4, 1886.*

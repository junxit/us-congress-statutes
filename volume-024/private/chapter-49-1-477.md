# Chapter 477

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Thomas Allcock.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Thomas Allcock, late a private in Company F, Third United States Artillery, during the Florida war, and pay him a pension at the rate of fifty dollars per month, said pension to be in lieu of that he now receives.


*Approved, June 24, 1886.*

# Chapter 31

> An Act granting an increase of pension to General Benjamin F. Kelly.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Benjamin F. Kelly, late a brigadier-general in the Army of the United States, at the rate of one hundred dollars per month; said pension to be in lieu of that which he now receives.


*Approved, January 18, 1887.*

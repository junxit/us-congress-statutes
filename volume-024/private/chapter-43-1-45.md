# Chapter 45

> An act granting a pension to Margaret B. Harwood.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Margaret B. Harwood, widow of Rear-Admiral Andrew A. Harwood, late of the United States Navy, and pay her a pension of fifty dollars a month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, April 5, 1886.*

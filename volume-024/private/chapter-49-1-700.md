# Chapter 700

> An Act granting a pension to Rose A. McManus.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Rose A. McManus, widow of Isaac McManus, who was captain of Company G, One hundred and second Regiment of Illinois Infantry, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

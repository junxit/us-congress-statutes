# Chapter 681

> An Act granting a pension to William B. Jacobs.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary’ of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William B. Jacobs, late a member of the Fifty-ninth Regiment Ohio Volunteers.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

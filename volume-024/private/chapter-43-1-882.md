# Chapter 882

> An act for the relief of Jacob Nix.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Jacob Nix, who served as a captain in the Brown County, Minnesota, militia, during the attack of Indians upon New Uhn, Minnesota, August, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, and grant him the pension as a captain, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, August 3, 1886.*

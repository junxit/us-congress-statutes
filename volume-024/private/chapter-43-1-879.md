# Chapter 879

> An act granting a pension to Erastus W. Kennedy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That 1 he Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Erastus W. Kennedy, late a private in Company K, Ninth Regiment Michigan Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, August 3, 1886.*

# Chapter 507

> An Act granting a pension to Charles Foreman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Charles Foreman, late a private in Company G, Thirty-sixth United States Colored Troops, on the pension-roll, subject to the conditions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, June 24, 1886.*

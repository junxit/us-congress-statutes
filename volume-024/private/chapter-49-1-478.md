# Chapter 478

> An Act for the relief of Mrs. Margaret Colbath.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Margaret Colbath, widow of John D. Colbath, late a private in Company B, Eighth Regiment New York Heavy Artillery Volunteers.


*Approved, June 24, 1886.*

# Chapter 692

> An Act granting a pension to Noah B. Brookshire

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Noah B. Brookshire, late a private in Company B, Third Regiment of North Carolina Mounted Volunteers.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

# Chapter 157

> An act granting a pension to Timothy Driscoll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension list, subject to the laws regulating pensions, the name of Timothy Driscoll, late a private in Company A, First Maine Light Artillery Volunteers.


*Approved, May 7, 1886.*

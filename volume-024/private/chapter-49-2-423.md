# Chapter 423

> An Act granting a pension to Peter P. Hoffman

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension- roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Peter P. Hoffman, late a private in Company H, Second Regiment New Jersey Cavalry Volunteers.


*Approved, March 3, 1887.*

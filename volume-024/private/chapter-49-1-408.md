# Chapter 408

> An Act granting a pension to Sarah E. Henry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah E. Henry, widow of George Henry, late of Company E, Second United States Cavalry, and general service, United States Army.


*Approved, June 1, 1886.*

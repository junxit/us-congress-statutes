# Chapter 890

> An act granting an increase of pension to Captain John F. Morris.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of John F. Morris, late captain of Company B. Eighty-third Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers, to seventy-two dollars per month.


*Approved, August 3, 1886.*

# Chapter 52

> An Act for the relief of Benjamin P. Loyall, of the State of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two-thirds of each House concurring therein},*

That Benjamin P. Loyall, of the State of Virginia, is hereby relieved of all political disabilities imposed upon him by the third section of the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States,


*Approved, January 26, 1887.*

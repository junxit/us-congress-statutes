# Chapter 676

> An Act granting a pension to Charles F. Hildreth.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Charles F. Hildreth, late surgeon of the Fortieth Massachusetts Eegiment.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

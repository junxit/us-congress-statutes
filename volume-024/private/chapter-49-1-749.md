# Chapter 749

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Margaret Hunter Hardie.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pension of Margaret Hunter Hardie, widow of Brevet Major-General James A. Hardie, formerly Inspector-General of the Army, be, and the same is hereby, increased to fifty dollars per mouth.


*Approved, July 8, 1886.*

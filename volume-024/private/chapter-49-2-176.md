# Chapter 176

> An Act to restore the name of Harvey Coburn to the pension-roll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to restore to the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Harvey Coburn, late of Company K, Second Pennsylvania Heavy Artillery (One hundred and twelfth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteers).


*Approved, February 22, 1887.*

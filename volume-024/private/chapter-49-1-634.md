# Chapter 634

> An Act granting a pension to William J. Barker

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William J. Barker, late of Company A, Eleventh Regiment Michigan Volunteers.


*Approved, July 3, 1886.*

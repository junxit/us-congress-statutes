# Chapter 55

> An act to amend and correct the act approved March third, eighteen hundred and eighty-five, granting a pension to Sarah Hague.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the act approved March third, eighteen hundred and eighty-five, granting a pension to Sarah Hague, is hereby amended and corrected so as to read:

> “That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Sarah Hague, dependent mother of William Hague, late of Company L, Sixth New York Heavy Artillery”.

## § 2.

That the pension shall commence from the date of the approval of the original act


*Approved, April 15, 1886.*

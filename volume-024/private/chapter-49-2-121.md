# Chapter 121

> An Act for the relief of Rosella E. Hibbert.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That t he Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Rosella E. Hibbert, daughter of Franklin M. Hibbert, late of Company F, Twenty-seventh Regiment Massachusetts Volunteers, at the rate of twelve dollars a month, to be paid to her legally constituted guardian for her use and benefit.

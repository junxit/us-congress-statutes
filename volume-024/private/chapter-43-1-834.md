# Chapter 834

> An act granting a pension to Mrs. Sarah Young.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he hereby is, authorized to place on the pension-roll, at the rate of twenty dollars per month, the name of Mrs. Sarah Young, of Des Moines, Iowa, for and on account of services rendered as a nurse during the war of the rebellion.


*Approved, July 31, 1886.*

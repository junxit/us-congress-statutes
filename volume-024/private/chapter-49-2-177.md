# Chapter 177

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. Margaret Cashin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, anti he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Margaret Cashiu, widow of Patrick Cashin, late of Company D, Forty-fourth Ohio Volunteers, and Company M, Eighth Ohio Volunteers.


*Approved, February 22, 1887.*

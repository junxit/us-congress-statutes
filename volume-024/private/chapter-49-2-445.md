# Chapter 445

> An Act granting a pension to Mary Renfro.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Mary Renfro, mother of Mark. U, Renfro, deceased, late a private in Company D, Second Regiment Tennessee Volunteers, on the pension-roll of the United States, according to the rules and regulations governing such cases, said pension to continue during the lifetime of said Mary Renfro.


*Approved, March 3, 1887.*

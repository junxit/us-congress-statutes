# Chapter 720

> An Act to increase the pension of Henry Brooks.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension of Henry Brooks, late of Company E, Fifth Vermont Volunteers to eighteen dollars per mouth.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

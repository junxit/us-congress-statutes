# Chapter 815

> An act to remove the political disabilities of Thomas R. Ware, of Virginia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, (two thirds of each House concurring therein),*

That Thomas R. Ware, of the State of Virginia, is hereby relieved of all political disabilities imposed upon him by the third section of the fourteenth amendment to the Constitution of the United States, and the same are hereby removed.


*Approved, July 29, 1886.*

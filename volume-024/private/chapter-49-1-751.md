# Chapter 751

> An Act for the relief of B. Frank Patterson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of B. Frank Patterson, late of Company F, Fourteenth New Jersey Volunteers, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions of the pension laws.


*Approved, July 8, 1886.*

# Chapter 833

> An act for the relief of Levi A. Cronkhite.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Levi A. Cronkhite, late a private in Company E, Eighty-sixth Regiment Indiana Volunteer Infantry, on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, August 3, 1886.*

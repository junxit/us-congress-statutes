# Chapter 725

> An Act granting an increase of pension to Charles F. Ward

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary’ of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Charles F. Ward, formerly of the ship Vermont, United States Navy, a pension of twenty-four dollars a month from and after the passage of this act, in lieu of the pension now received by him.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

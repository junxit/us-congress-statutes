# Chapter 214

> An act for the relief of Pernetta Hendley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to restore to the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Pernetta Hendley, widow of Captain Israel Hendley, who lost his life at the battle of Moroe, in the war with Mexico, in the year eighteen hundred and forty-seven.

## § 2.

[Note by the Department of State.—The foregoing act having been presented to the President of the United States for his approval, and not having been returned by him to the house of Congress in which it originated within the time prescribed by the Constitution of the United States, has become a law without his approval.]

That this act shall take effect and be in force from and after its passage.

Received by the President, April 27, 1886.

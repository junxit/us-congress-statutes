# Chapter 131

> An act granting a pension to Rebecca Marchant.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Rebecca Marchant, mother of Captain Henry A. Marchant, late captain of Company I, Twenty-third Regiment Pennsylvania Infantry


*Approved, May 7, 1886.*

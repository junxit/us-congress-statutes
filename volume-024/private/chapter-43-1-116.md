# Chapter 116

> An act granting a pension to George H. Perkins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of George H. Perkins, late a Private in Company I, Third Regiment Massachusetts Heavy Artillery Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, May 7, 1886.*

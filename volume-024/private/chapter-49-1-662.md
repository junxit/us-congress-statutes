# Chapter 662

> An Act granting a pension to Thomas Tegan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name, of Thomas Tegan, late a private in Company I, Thirty-seventh New York Volunteer Infantry, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

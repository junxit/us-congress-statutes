# Chapter 413

> An Act for the relief of T. J. Payne.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War is hereby directed to remove the charge of desertion made against T. J. Payne, late a private in Company F, Second Tennessee Cavalry, and to place him on the muster-rolls of said company.


*Approved, March 3, 1887.*

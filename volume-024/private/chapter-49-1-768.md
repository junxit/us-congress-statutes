# Chapter 768

> An Act granting a pension to Addie L. Macomber.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Mrs Addie L. Macomber, a volunteer nurse in the late war, giving her the sum of twelve dollars a month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, July 14, 1886.*

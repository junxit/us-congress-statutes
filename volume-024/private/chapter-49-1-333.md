# Chapter 333

> An Act granting a pension to Captain Elihu Jones.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Elihu Jones, late captain of Company G, Eighth Regiment Illinois Infantry Volunteers, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars a month, in lieu of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, May 27, 1886.*

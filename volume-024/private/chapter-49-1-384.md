# Chapter 384

> An Act granting a pension to Michael Daly.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Michael Daly, late of Company B, Eleventh Regiment Wisconsin Volunteers, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, May 27, 1886.*

# Chapter 168

> An act to increase the pension of Leander W. Corey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United’ States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to increase the pension to forty dollars per month of Leander W. Corey, late a private in Company F, Sixteenth Iowa Veteran Volunteers.


*Approved, May 7, 1886.*

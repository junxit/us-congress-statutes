# Chapter 307

> An Act for the relief of Andrew J. Edgerly.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to so amend the military record of Andrew J. Edgerly, late a first lieutenant of the Fourth Regiment New Hampshire Volunteers, as to grant to him an honorable discharge as of March tenth, eighteen hundred and sixty-three.


*Approved, February 28, 1887.*

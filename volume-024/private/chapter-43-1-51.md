# Chapter 51

> An act to increase the pension of Florence Murray.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, to increase the pension of Florence Murray, widow of the late Rear-Admiral Alexander Murray, from thirty to fifty dollars a month, said increase to take effect from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, April 15, 1886.*

# Chapter 184

> An Act granting a pension to Caroline C. McNair.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Caroline C. McNair, of Canisteo, Steuben County, New York, at the rate of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 22, 1887.*

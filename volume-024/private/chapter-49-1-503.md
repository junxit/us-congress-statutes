# Chapter 503

> An Act granting a pension to Henry G. Ballinger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be hereby is, directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Henry G. Ballinger, late Captain of Camden County, Missouri, militia, and pay him a pension from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 24, 1886.*

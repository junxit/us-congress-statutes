# Chapter 203

> An Act granting a pension to Clara M. Tannehill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America, in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Clara M. Tannehill, blind daughter of Martin V. Tannehill, late of Company D, Thirty-fourth Regiment Iowa Volunteers, and pay her a pension of twelve dollars per month from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 22, 1887.*

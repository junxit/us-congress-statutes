# Chapter 412

> An Act granting a pension to Jannet E. B Smith.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Jannet E. B. Smith, the widow of Stephen Barton, late a private in Company C, Twenty-first Regiment of Iowa Infantry.


*Approved, June 1, 1886.*

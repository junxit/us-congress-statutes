# Chapter 881

> An act granting a pension to Lucinda Sawyer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and hereby is, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Lucinda Sawyer, stepmother of John Sawyer, late a private in Company C, Third Regiment Maine Volunteers, subject to the limitations and provisions of the laws governing pensions to dependent parents.


*Approved, August 3, 1886.*

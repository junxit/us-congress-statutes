# Chapter 492

> An Act for the relief of the children of the late Surgeon Alfred M. Owen, and to increase their pensions.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Alfred C. Owen, Christine Q. Owen, Kathleen D. B. Owen, and Albert T. Owen, minor children of he late Surgeon Alfred M. Owen, United States Navy, be, and they are hereby, granted and allowed a pension of fifteen dollars each per month, to continue until each child shall respectively attain the age of sixteen years; and the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the names of the said Alfred C. Owen, Christine Q. Owen, Kathleen D. B. Owen, and Albert T. Owen on the pension-roll at said rates, in lieu of pensions now paid them.


*Approved, June 24, 1886.*

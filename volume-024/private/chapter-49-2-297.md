# Chapter 297

> An Act granting a pension to Adeliza Perry

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Adeliza Perry, of Worcester, Massachusetts, who served as a nurse during the war of the rebellion, and is now incapacitated from earning a livelihood by sickness induced by said service, and pay her a pension of twelve dollars per month.


*Approved, February 28, 1887.*

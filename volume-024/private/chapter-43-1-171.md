# Chapter 171

> An act granting a pension to William Gordon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William Gordon, father of William A. Gordon, deceased, late of Company H, Eleventh Iowa Volunteers.


*Approved, May 7, 1886.*

# Chapter 406

> An Act for the relief of General Speed S. Fry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Speed S. Fry, at the rate of fifty dollars per month, during his natural life.


*Approved, March 3, 1887.*

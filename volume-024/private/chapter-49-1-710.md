# Chapter 710

> An Act granting a pension to Larkin G. Mead.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Larkin G. Mead, late assistant surgeon of the Eighteenth Wisconsin Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

# Chapter 706

> An Act granting a pension to Margaret A. Poland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, the name of Margaret A. Poland, widow of Alexander Poland, late of Leesburg, Loudoun county, State of Virginia, at the rate of twelve dollars per mouth.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

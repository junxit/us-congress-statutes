# Chapter 327

> An Act granting a pension to Rebecca Morris.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll the name of Rebecca Morris, dependent mother of William E. Morris, late a private in Company 0, Fifty-fifth Massachusetts Volunteers, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, March 2, 1887.*

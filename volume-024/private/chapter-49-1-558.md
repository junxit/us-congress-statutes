# Chapter 558

> An Act for the relief of Edwin Stevens.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Edwin Stevens, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of seven hundred and thirty dollars, to indemnify him for certain expenses incurred and paid by him while acting as United States consul at Ningpo, China.


*Approved, June 25, 1886.*

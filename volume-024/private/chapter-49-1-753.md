# Chapter 753

> An Act for increase of pension to Zebulon S. Tompkins.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations and provisions of the pension laws, the name of Zebulon S. Tompkins, late of Company E, Sixth New Jersey Volunteers, at the rate of twelve dollars per mouth, in lieu of the amount now received by him under the law.


*Approved, July 8, 1886.*

# Chapter 561

> An Act granting a franking privilege to Julia D. Grant.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all mail-matter carried by post to Julia D. Grant, widow of the late Ulysses 3. Grant, or sent by her under her written autograph signature, be conveyed free of postage, during her natural life.


*Approved, June 28, 1886.*

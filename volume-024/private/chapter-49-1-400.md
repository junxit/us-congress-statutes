# Chapter 400

> An Act for the relief of E. P. Thompson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Postmaster-General be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to E. P. Thompson, assistant postmaster at Indianapolis, Indiana, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of three hundred and thirty-eight dollars, for postage-stamps and postal cards stolen and destroyed February first, eighteen hundred and eighty-one, for which he was held liable by the postmaster and paid said amount.


*Approved, June 1, 1886.*

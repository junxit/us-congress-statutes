# Chapter 263

> An Act granting a pension to Charles G. Paris.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Charles G. Paris, late of Company H, Seventy-ninth Regiment Ohio Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, February 25, 1887.*

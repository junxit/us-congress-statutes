# Chapter 405

> An Act for the relief of Mary Kaylor.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United Slates of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and lie is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mary Kaylor, widow of John Kaylor, deceased, late of Company C, Fourth Illinois Infantry.


*Approved, March 3, 1887.*

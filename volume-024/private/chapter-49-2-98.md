# Chapter 98

> An Act for the relief of W. D. Havely.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of W. D. Havely, father of Robert M. Havely, late of Company C Fifteenth Regiment of West Virginia Volunteer Infantry.


*Approved, February 3, 1887.*

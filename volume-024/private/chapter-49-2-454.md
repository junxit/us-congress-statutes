# Chapter 454

> An Act placing the name of Mrs. Lydia Burdick on the pension-roll.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension roll, subject to the provisions of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Lydia Burdick, widow of Samuel Burdick, late a private in Company A, Eighteenth Regiment Connecticut Volunteers.


*Approved, March 3, 1887.*

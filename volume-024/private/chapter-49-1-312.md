# Chapter 312

> An Act granting a pension to Emeline Crawford.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Emeline Crawford, widow of Daniel S. Crawford, late a private in Company A, Forty-seventh Regiment of Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry.

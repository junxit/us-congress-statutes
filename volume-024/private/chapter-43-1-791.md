# Chapter 791

> An act granting a pension to Patrick Murphy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and be is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Patrick Murphy, late a seaman in the United States Navy.


*Approved, July 26, 1886.*

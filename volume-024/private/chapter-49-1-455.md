# Chapter 455

> An Act granting a pension to Parmelia Smith.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and, House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Parrnelia Smith, the invalid daughter of the late Joseph R. Smith, a brevet brigadier-general in the United States Army, on the pension-roll, at the rate of twenty dollars per month.


*Approved, June 19, 1886.*

# Chapter 711

> An Act granting a pension to Lieutenant Joseph Prost.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the limitations of the pension laws, the name of Joseph Prost, late a lieutenant of Company E, Fifty-fifth Regiment New York Volunteers.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

# Chapter 679

> An Act granting a pension to Maria Kile.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Maria Kile, mother of Nathaniel Kile, late a member in Company K, Fifty-sixth Regiment New York Volunteers.


*Approved, July 6, 1886.*

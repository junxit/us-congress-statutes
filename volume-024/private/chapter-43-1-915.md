# Chapter 915

> An act granting a pension to Milton P. Shockley.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension Jaws, the name of Milton P. Shockley, late a member of Company 0, Second Regiment of Ohio Volunteers, in the Mexican war.


*Approved, August 4, 1886.*

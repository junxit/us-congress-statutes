# Chapter 99

> An Act granting a pension to James Long.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, required to place on the pension-roll, subject to the restrictions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of James Long, late of Company D, Ninety-third Regiment of Ohio Infantry Volunteers.


*Approved, February 3, 1887.*

# Chapter 145

> An Act granting a pension to Mrs. M. C. Miles.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,That,*

the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. M, C. Miles, of Lansing, Michigan, widow of Marcus H. Miles, late a first lieutenant in the Eleventh Michigan Cavalry.


*Approved, February 19, 1887.*

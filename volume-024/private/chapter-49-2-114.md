# Chapter 114

> An Act granting a pension to Joshua L. Morris.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United' States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Joshua L. Morris, late of Company H, Third Missouri State Militia Cavalry.


*Approved, February 4, 1887.*

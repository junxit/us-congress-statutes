# Chapter 479

> An Act for the relief of Sarah A. Vaughn.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Mrs. Sarah A. Vaughn, dependent mother of Delos A. Graves, late of the Seventeenth Independent Battery Light Artillery, New York Volunteers.


*Approved, June 24, 1886.*

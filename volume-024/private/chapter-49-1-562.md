# Chapter 562

> An Act granting a pension to Reuben Farnum.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of Reuben Farnum, late of Company E, Thirteenth Iowa Drafted Infantry.


*Approved, June 28, 1886.*

# Chapter 851

> An act granting an increase of pension to William P. Squires.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the name of William P. Squires, late of Company C, Seventy-fifth Illinois Volunteer Infantry, at the rate of forty-five dollars per month in lien of the pension he is now receiving.


*Approved, August 3, 1886.*

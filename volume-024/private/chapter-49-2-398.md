# Chapter 398

> An Act for the relief of Mrs. Julia De Quindre

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United, States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-roll, at twenty-five dollars per month, the name of Julia De Quindre, for services as nurse during the war of the rebellion, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws.


*Approved, March 3, 1887.*

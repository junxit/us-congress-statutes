# Chapter 9

> An Act amending sections forty-seven hundred and fifty-six and forty-seven hundred and fifty-seven of the Revised Statutes, relating to pensions to certain disabled persons who have served in the Navy or Marine Corps.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section forty-seven hundred and fifty-six and section forty-seven hundred and fifty-seven of the Revised Statutes of the United States be, and the same are hereby, amended by inserting the words “or as an appointed petty officer, or both,” after the words “as an enlisted person” in the former section, and after the words “as an enlisted man ” in the latter section.


*Approved, December 23, 1886.*

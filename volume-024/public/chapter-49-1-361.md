# Chapter 361

> An act granting the right of way through the Fort Selden Military Reservation, in New Mexico, to the Rio Grande, Mexico and Pacific Railroad company.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the right of way to the extent of one hundred feet, and no more, in width, through the Fort Selden Military Reservation, New Mexico, be, and the same is hereby, confirmed to the Rio Grande, Mexico and Pacific Railroad.


*Approved, May 18, 1886.*

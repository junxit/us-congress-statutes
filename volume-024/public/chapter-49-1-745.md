# Chapter 745

> An Act to amend chapter two hundred and eighteen of the acts of the first session of the Forty-seventh Congress, approved June fifteenth, eighteen hundred and eighty-two.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the county of Attala, in the northern judicial district of the State of Mississippi, is hereby transferred from the western to the eastern division of said district. All crimes and offences heretofore committed within said western division shall be prosecuted tried and determined in the same manner and with the same effect as if this net bad not been passed.


*Approved, July 8, 1886.*

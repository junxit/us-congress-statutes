# Chapter 609

> An Act to construct a road to the national cemetery at Natchez, Mississippi.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of ten thousand dollars be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the purpose of constructing a macadamized or gravel road from the city of Natchez, Mississippi, to the national cemetery near that city: Provided, That the right of way, not less than fifty feet in width, shall first be secured to the United States to the ground over which said road shall run: And provided further, That said road shall be constructed under the supervision of the Secretary of War, upon a contract let to the lowest bidder.


*Approved, July 2, 1886.*

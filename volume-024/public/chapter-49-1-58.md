# Chapter 58

> An act to change the name of Uniontown to Anacostia.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That that portion of the District of Columbia heretofore known and designated as Uniontown, from and after the approval of this act be known and designated as Anacostia.


*Approved, April 22, 1886.*

# Chapter 568

> An act to reduce the fee on domestic money-orders for sums not exceeding live dollars.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the fee for each domestic money-order not exceeding in amount five dollars shall be reduced from eight cents to five cents.

## § 2.

That this act shall take effect within one mouth after the date of its approval by the President.


*Approved, June 29, 1886.*

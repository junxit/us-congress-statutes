# Chapter 61

> An act to establish a light-ship off the entrance to Chesapeake Bay.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be constructed and established a first-class light-ship, with a steam fog-signal, off the entrance to Chesapeake Bay. The cost of which shall not exceed the sum of sixty thousand dollars: Provided, That the construction of said light ship shall be let to the lowest responsible bidder after advertisement, and that said light-ship shall be built in American shipyards.


*Approved, April 22, 1886.*

# Chapter 275

> An Act to make Tampa, Florida, a port of entry.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the port of Tampa, Hills-borough County, Florida, be, and the same is hereby, made a port of entry.


*Approved, February 28, 1887.*

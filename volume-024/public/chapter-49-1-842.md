# Chapter 842

> An Act to provide for the appointment and compensation of a district judge for the southern district of Alabama.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there shall be appointed by the President of the United States, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, a district judge for the southern judicial district of the State of Alabama; and that said judge shall be entitled to receive a yearly salary of three thousand five hundred dollars, payable quarterly.

## § 2.

That the jurisdiction of the present district judge for the several districts of Alabama, and his successors, shall hereafter be confined to the northern and middle districts of said State.


*Approved, August 2, 1886.*

# Chapter 128

> An Act to authorize Frank W. Hunt to erect and maintain a ferry across the Missouri River at the military reservation of Fort Buford, Dakota territory.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Frank W. Hunt, his heirs or assigns, be, and they are hereby, authorized to erect and maintain a ferry either by wire, cable, or steam, across the Missouri River, at the military reservation of Fort Buford, Dakota Territory, for a period of ten years.

## § 2.

That the rates of toll across said ferry shall be fixed or established from year to year by the Secretary of War.

## § 3.

That while this charter is in existence no toll or charge shall be made for crossing the United States mail over said ferry; that the point of crossing the Missouri River to establish this ferry shall be determined upon under the authority of the Secretary of War.

## § 4.

This act may be amended or repealed at any time.


*Approved, February 11, 1887.*

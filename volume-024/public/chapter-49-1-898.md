# Chapter 898

> An Act to relieve the State of Colorado from charges on account of ordnance stores furnished to the State and Territory.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Chief of the Ordnance Bureau of the War Department be, and he is hereby, directed to cause the State of Colorado to be credited on its ordnance account with the amounts now charged against it for arms and ordnance stores issued to the Territory of Colorado, amounting to thirty-three thousand eight hundred and ninety-one dollars.


*Approved, August 4, 1886.*

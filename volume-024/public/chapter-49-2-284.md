# Chapter 284

> An Act granting the right of way to the Prescott and Arizona Centra Railway Company across the Whipple Barracks Military Reservation, in Arizona.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Prescott and Arizona Central Railway Company, a corporation duly organized under the laws of the Territory of Arizona, are hereby granted the right of way, one hundred feet in width, for their said railroad across and through the Whipple Barracks Military Reservation, located in said Territory of Arizona, not to interfere with any buildings or improvements on said reservation, and the location of the line of said right of way to be subject to the approval of the Secretary of War.


*Approved, February 28, 1887.*

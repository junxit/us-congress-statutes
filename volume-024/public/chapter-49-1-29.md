# Chapter 29

> An act to authorize the increase of the capital stock of the First National Bank of Shakopee Minnesota

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller of the Currency be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to allow the First National Bank of Shakopee, Minnesota, to increase its capital stock, in accordance with existing laws, to any sum not exceeding two hundred and fifty thousand dollars.


*Approved, March 29, 1886.*

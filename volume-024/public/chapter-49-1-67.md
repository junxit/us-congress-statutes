# Chapter 67

> An act extending the time for the completion of the records of the clerk of the Court of Commissioners of Alabama Claims, and the transfer of the same to the Department of State.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the powers of the clerk of the late Court of Commissioners of Alabama Claims as extended by section two, of the act of Congress relating thereto, approved June third, eighteen hundred and eighty-four, be and they hereby are extended for an additional period, not to exceed two months from and after the thirtieth day of April, eighteen hundred and eighty-six. for the purpose of closing up the business of his office, and for depositing the records, documents, and all other papers of the court or its officers in the office of the Secretary of State, as provided in the act reestablishing said court, and the act therein referred to; and said clerk is hereby authorized to employ such assistants as he may deem necessary for the purpose of enabling him to carry into effect the provisions of this act, not to exceed in number those heretofore employed, said assistants to be paid monthly, at the same rate of compensation heretofore paid them, on vouchers certified by said clerk, under the direction of the Secretary of State, from said Geneva award fund; and all disbursements shall be made by said clerk, under the direction of the Secretary of State.


*Approved, April 30, 1886.*

# Chapter 580

> An act appropriating money for the completion of the public building at Frankfort, Kentucky.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of fifteen thousand dollars be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the purpose of completing the public building at Frankfort, Kentucky.


*Approved, June 30, 1886.*

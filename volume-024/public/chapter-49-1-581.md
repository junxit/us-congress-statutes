# Chapter 581

> An act to change the time of holding United States circuit and district courts in the southern district of the State of Florida.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the United States circuit and district courts for the southern district of Florida, at Tampa, shall hereafter be held on the second Monday in February of each year, instead of the first Monday in March; and so much of the act entitled “An act to extend the jurisdiction of the district and circuit courts of the United States for the southern district of Florida,” approved February the third, eighteen hundred and seventy-nine, as provides that the said courts shall be held on the first Monday in March, is hereby repealed.


*Approved, June 30, 1886.*

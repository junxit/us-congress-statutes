# Chapter 746

> An Act referring to the Court of Claims the claims for property seized by General Johnston on the Utah expedition for examination and report.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the claims of Joseph C. Irwin and Company and C. A. Perry and Company, freighters, for property claimed to have been taken and impressed into the service, of the United States in the year eighteen hundred and fifty seven, by orders of Colonel Albert Sidney Johnston, in command of the Utah expedition, as well as for property alleged to have been sold to the Government, be, and the same are hereby, referred, with all the papers relating thereto, to the Court of Claims, for adjudication, according to law, on the proofs heretofore presented and such other proofs as may be adduced, and report the same to Congress


*Approved, July 8, 1886.*

# Chapter 12

> An Act to amend section four thousand four hundred and fourteen of the Revised Statutes, relating to inspectors of hulls and boilers.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section forty-four hundred and fourteen of the Revised Statutes is hereby amended by inserting, after the word “Savannah ”, in the second line of the sixth paragraph of said section, the words “Duluth, Minnesota”.


*Approved, January 3, 1887.*

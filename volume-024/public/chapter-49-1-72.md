# Chapter 72

> An act in aid of the national monument at Plymouth, Massachusetts.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

Whereas a national monument to the pilgrim forefathers is in process of construction at Plymouth, Massachusetts, consisting of a central figure of Faith and of four statues representing Morality, Education, Law, and Liberty, of which five the first three have already been erected: Therefore,

That the sum of fifteen thousand dollars be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any moneys in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the purpose of erecting said statue of Liberty and the basrelief accompanying the same, under the direction of the Secretary of State.


*Approved, May 1, 1886.*

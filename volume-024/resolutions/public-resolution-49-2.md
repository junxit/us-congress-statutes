# Public Resolution 2

> Joint resolution filling existing vacancies in the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the existing vacancies in the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution of the class “other than members of Congress” shall be filled by the reappointment of John Maclean, of New Jersey; Asa Gray, of Massachusetts; Henry Coppee, of Pennsylvania; and the appointment of Montgomery C. Meigs, of the city of Washington, vice William T. Sherman, whose term has expired and who is no longer a citizen of Washington.


*Approved, December 26, 1885.*

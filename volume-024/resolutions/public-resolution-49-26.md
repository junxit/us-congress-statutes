# Public Resolution 26

> Joint resolution directing the Public Printer to forward the Congressional Record to our legations abroad.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Public Printer be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to forward, free of charge, through the Department of State, one copy of the daily Congressional Record to each of our legations abroad, commencing at the beginning of the present session.


*Approved, July 28, 1886.*

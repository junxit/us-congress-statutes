# Public Resolution 32

> Joint resolution for printing report of Commissioner of Agriculture.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be printed four hundred thousand copies of the annual report of the Commissioner of Agriculture for the year eighteen hundred and eighty-six; three hundred thousand copies for the use of members and delegates of the House of Representatives, and seventy-five thousand copies for the use of members of the Senate, and twenty-five thousand copies for the use of the Department of Agriculture.

## § 2.

That the sum of two hundred thousand dollars, or so much thereof as may be necessary, is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to defray the cost of the publication of said report.


*Approved, August 4, 1886.*

# Public Resolution 11

> Joint resolution authorizing the printing of Committee reports.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the reports of committees, the evidence and papers submitted therewith, or any part thereof, printed by order of Congress, may be reprinted at the Public Printing Office, at the instance of Senators, Representatives, and Delegates in Congress, upon payment in advance to the Public Printer of the cost thereof with ten per centum added, the same as if originally printed in the Congressional Record.


*Approved, April 15, 1886.*

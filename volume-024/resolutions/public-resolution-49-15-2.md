# Public Resolution 15

> Joint resolution to print the official report of the Investigations of Dr. Edward Shakespeare concerning the nature, and so forth, of Epidemic Cholera.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Official Report of the Investigations of Dr. Edward O. Shakespeare in Europe and Asia, concerning the nature, causes, method of prevention and cure of Epidemic or Asiatic Cholera, made by the direction of the President of the United States, be printed, with the accompanying illustrations, and that there be printed, in addition to the usual number, two thousand copies for the use of the Department of State, one thousand copies for the use of the Senate, and two thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives, said report to be printed under the editorial supervision of Dr. Shakespeare.


*Approved, March 3, 1887.*

# Public Resolution 8

> Joint resolution authorizing the printing of a naval report.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be printed and bound in cloth, at the Government Printing Office, in addition to the “usual number”, four thousand copies of the Report of Naval Constructor Philip Hichborn, United States Navy, on European dockyards, details, fitting and equipment of foreign vessels, torpedo-boats, shipyard appliances, tools, and so forth; of which additional number one thousand copies shall be for the use of the House, five hundred copies for the use of the Senate, and two thousand five hundred copies be delivered to and distributed by the Secretary of the Navy for general information.


*Approved, April 7, 1886.*

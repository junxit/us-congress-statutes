# Public Resolution 22

> Joint resolution for printing five thousand copies of Commander Schley’s report of his search for the Greyly expedition.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be printed and bound in cloth, at the Government Printing Office, and illustrated, five thousand copies of the report of Commander W. S. Schley, United States Navy, commander of the relief expedition of eighteen hundred and eighty-four to rescue Lieutenant A W. Greely and the Lady Franklin Bay expedition; of which number one thousand copies shall be for the use of the House, five hundred copies for the use of the Senate, and three thousand five hundred copies to be distributed by the Secretary of the Navy.


*Approved, March 3, 1887.*

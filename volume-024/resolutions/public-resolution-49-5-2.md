# Public Resolution 5

> Joint resolution appointing James B. Angell a member of the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the existing vacancy in the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution of the class “other than members of Congress,” shall be filled by the appointment of James B. Angell, of the State of Michigan, in place of John Maclean, deceased.


*Approved, January 19, 1887.*

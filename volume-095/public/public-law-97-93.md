# Public Law 97–93

> Joint Resolution To approve the President’s recommendation for a waiver of law pursuant to the Alaska Natural Gas Transportation Act of 1976.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the House of Representatives and Senate approve the waiver of the provision of law (Public Law 95–158, Public Law numbered 688, Seventy-fifth Congress, second session, and Public Law 94–163) as proposed by the President, submitted to the Congress on October 15, 1981.


*Approved December 15, 1981.*

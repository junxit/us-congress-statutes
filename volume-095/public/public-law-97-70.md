# Public Law 97–70

> An Act To make a technical amendment to the International Investment Survey Act of 1976.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 4(b) of the International Investment Survey Act of 1976 (22 U.S.C. 3103(b)) is amended by striking the word “calendar” each time it appears therein.


*Approved October 26, 1981.*

# Public Law 97–5

> An Act To amend the Energy Policy and Conservation Act to extend certain authorities relating to the international energy program.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection (j) of section 252 of the Energy Policy and Conservation Act (42 U.S.C. 6272(j)) is amended by striking out “March 15, 1981” and inserting in lieu thereof “September 30, 1981”.


*Approved March 13, 1981.*

# Public Law 97–17

> Joint Resolution To correct Public Law 97–12 due to an error in the enrollment of H.R. 3512.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in order to correct an error in the enrollment of H.R. 3512, the figure in the second line of the third paragraph on page 42 of Public Law 97–12 is hereby amended as follows:

Strike “$10,455,000” and insert “$410,455,000”.


*Approved June 29, 1981.*

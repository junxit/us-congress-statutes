# Public Law 97–15

> An Act To amend title 38, United States Code, to extend by twelve months the period during which funds appropriated for grants by the Veterans Administration for the establishment and support of new State medical schools may be expended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 5073(a)(2)(G) of title 38, United States Code, is amended by striking out “seventh such period” and inserting in lieu thereof “seventh and eighth such periods”.


*Approved June 17, 1981.*

# Public Law 97–135

> Joint Resolution To provide for the designation of February 7 through 13, 1982, as “National Scleroderma Week”.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President shall issue a proclamation designating February 7 through 13, 1982, as “National Scleroderma Week”, and calling upon the people of the United States to observe such week with appropriate ceremonies and activities.


*Approved December 29, 1981.*

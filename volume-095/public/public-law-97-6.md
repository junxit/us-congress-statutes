# Public Law 97–6

> An Act To amend section 201 of the Agricultural Act of 1949, as amended, to delete the requirement that the support price of milk be adjusted semiannually.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 201 of the Agricultural Act of 1949, as amended (7 U.S.C. 1446), is further amended by deleting subsection (d).


*Approved March 31, 1981.*

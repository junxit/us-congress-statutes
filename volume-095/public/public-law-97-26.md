# Public Law 97–26

> Joint Resolution Making an urgent supplemental appropriation for the Department of Health and Human Services for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1981.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the following sum is appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1981, namely:

## DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

## Health Services Administration

## health services

For an additional amount for “Health Services”, $16,800,000: Provided, That not more than $145,199,000 shall be available under this head for operation of Public Health Service hospitals and clinics.


*Approved July 29, 1981.*

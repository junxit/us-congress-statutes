# Public Law 97–133

> Joint Resolution Providing for the convening of the second session of the Ninety-seventh Congress.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the second regular session of the Ninety-seventh Congress shall begin at 12 o’clock meridian on Monday, January 25, 1982.


*Approved December 29, 1981.*

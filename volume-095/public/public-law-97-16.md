# Public Law 97–16

> An Act To amend title I of the Marine Protection, Research, and Sanctuaries Act, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 111 of the Marine Protection, Research, and Sanctuaries Act, as amended (33 U.S.C. 1420), is amended by striking “and fiscal year 1982,” and inserting in lieu thereof “and not to exceed $4,213,000 for fiscal year 1982,”.


*Approved June 23, 1981.*

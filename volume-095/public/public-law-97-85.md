# Public Law 97–85

> Joint Resolution Making further continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 1982.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That clauses (c) of sections 101 and 102 of the joint resolution of October 1, 1981 (Public Law 97–51), are hereby amended by striking out “November 20, 1981” and inserting in lieu thereof “December 15, 1981”.

The joint resolution of October 1, 1981 (Public Law 97–51), is amended by adding after section 141:

> ## § 142.
>
> Notwithstanding any other provision of this joint resolution, such sums as may be necessary, not in excess of, and under the conditions of the budget request transmitted on November 9, 1981, for operation, improvement, transfer, and closure of Public Health Service hospitals and clinics.”.


*Approved November 23, 1981.*

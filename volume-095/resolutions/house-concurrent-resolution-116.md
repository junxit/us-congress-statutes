# House Concurrent Resolution 116

> JOINT MEETING

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives on Tuesday, April 28, at 9 o’clock post meridiem, for the purpose of receiving such communications as the President of the United States shall be pleased to make to them.


*Agreed to April 27, 1981.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 54

> GENERAL EXPLANATION OF THE ECONOMIC RECOVERY TAX ACT OF 1981

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That three thousand copies of the General Explanation of the Economic Recovery Tax Act of 1981, prepared by the staff of the Joint Committee on Taxation, be printed for the use of the Joint Committee on Taxation.


*Agreed to December 16, 1981.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 58

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the House adjourns on Friday, February 6, 1981, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian on Tuesday, February 17, 1981, and that when the Senate adjourns on Friday, February 6, 1981, it stand adjourned until 12 o’clock meridian on Monday, February 16, 1981.


*Agreed to February 6, 1981.*

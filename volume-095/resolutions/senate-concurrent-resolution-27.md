# Senate Concurrent Resolution 27

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That when the Senate adjourns on Monday, August 3, 1981, it stand adjourned until 12:00 o’clock noon on Wednesday, September 9, 1981, and that when the House adjourns on Tuesday, August 4, 1981, it stand adjourned until 12:00 o’clock noon on Wednesday, September 9, 1981.


*Agreed to August 4, 1981.*

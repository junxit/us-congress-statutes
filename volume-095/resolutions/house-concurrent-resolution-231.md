# House Concurrent Resolution 231

> MARTIN LUTHER KING, JR., COMMEMORATION OF BIRTH—STRUCTURES AND EQUIPMENT ON CAPITOL GROUNDS

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That, in accordance with such conditions as the Architect of the Capitol may impose, a photographic platform, two media towers, a telephone bank, sound amplification devices, a speakers platform, and related structures and equipment may be erected under the auspices of the Stevie Wonder-Martin Luther King Memorial Committee at the west front of the Capitol in connection with a rally to be held on January 15, 1982, in commemoration of the birth of Doctor Martin Luther King, Junior.


*Agreed to December 16, 1981.*

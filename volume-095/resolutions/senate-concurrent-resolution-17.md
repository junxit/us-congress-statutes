# Senate Concurrent Resolution 17

> ADJOURNMENT—HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SENATE

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Friday, April 10, 1981, they stand adjourned until 12 o’clock noon on Monday, April 27, 1981.


*Agreed to April 8, 1981.*

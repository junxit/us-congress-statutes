# Chapter 575

> AN ACT For the relief of Harry K. Snyder.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions and limitations of sections 15 to 20, both inclusive, of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and former purposes”, approved September 7, 1916, as amended, the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to receive and consider, when filed, the claim of Harry K. Snyder, of Wilmington, Delaware, for disability alleged to have been incurred by him on February 16, 1926, while employed as a keeper of the United States Lighthouse Service, Port Penn, Delaware, and to determine said claim upon its merits under provisions of said Act: Provided, That no benefits shall accrue prior to the enactment of this Act: And provided further, That such claim be filed within six months after the passage of this Act.


*Approved, August 7, 1939.*

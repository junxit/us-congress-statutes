# Chapter 467

> AN ACT For the relief of Franklin C. Richardson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions of section 1118 of the Revised Statutes of the United States, the Secretary of War is hereby authorized to reenlist in the United States Army Franklin C. Richardson, private, Hawaiian Casual Section, First Recruit Company of the Overseas Discharge and Replacement Depot, Fort Slocum, New York (serial number XXXX), on such future dates as the said Franklin C. Richardson may make application for reenlistment: Provided, That he meets the other requirements for reenlistment in the Army.


*Approved, August 5, 1939.*

# Chapter 708

> AN ACT For the relief of Thomas J. Smith.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Thomas J. Smith, of Mankato, Kansas, is hereby relieved of the charge against him in the amount of $69, representing the amount paid to him by the United States as an employee of the Soil Conservation Service, Department of Agriculture, for per diem in lieu of subsistence, at the rate of $3 per day for the period March 22 to April 15, 1936, in connection with travel incident to change of duty station from Mankato to Salina, Kansas, and while at Salina.


*Approved, August 11, 1939.*

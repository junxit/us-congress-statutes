# Chapter 333

> AN ACT For the relief of Otis M. Culver, Samuel E. Abbey, Joseph Reger, and August H. Krueger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws or any laws conferring rights, privileges, or benefits upon persons honorably discharged from the United States Army Otis M. Culver, Samuel E. Abbey, Joseph Reger, and August H. Krueger shall be held and considered to have been honorably discharged on December 10, 1898, as privates. Company C, Third Battalion, Fourth Regiment Wisconsin Volunteer Infantry, United States Army: Provided, That no pension, back pay, bounty, or other benefit shall be held to have accrued by reason of this Act prior to its passage.


*Approved, July 19, 1939.*

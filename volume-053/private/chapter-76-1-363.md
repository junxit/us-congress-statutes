# Chapter 363

> AN ACT For the relief of Captain Robert E. Coughlin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to adjust and settle the claim of Captain Robert E. Coughlin, Engineer Corps, United States Army, in the sum of $165 on account of stoppage of pay as the result of alleged neglect of duty while stationed at Fort Worden, Washington, during the year 1922, and to certify the same to Congress for an appropriation.


*Approved, July 25, 1939.*

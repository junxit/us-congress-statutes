# Chapter 602

> AN ACT For the relief of Roscoe B. Huston and Simeon F. Felarca.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to credit the account of Roscoe B. Huston, postmaster at Detroit, Michigan, in the amount of $195.21, representing compensation in part paid, and in part to be paid, to Simeon F. Felarca and earned by the latter while employed as a substitute laborer in the post office at Detroit, disallowance of the amount having been based upon a legal prohibition resulting from lack of proof of citizenship.

## § 2.

The postmaster at Detroit, Michigan, is hereby authorized and directed to pay to Simeon F. Felarca the balance due him of $34.28 for services rendered, such amount being incorporated in the amount stated in section 1 of this Act.


*Approved, August 7, 1939.*

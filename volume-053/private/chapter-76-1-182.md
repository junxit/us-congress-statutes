# Chapter 182

> AN ACT For the relief of R. H. Gray.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That sections 17 and 20 of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes”, approved September 7, 1916, as amended (U. S. C., 1934 edition, title 5, secs. 767 and 770), are hereby waived in favor of R. H. Gray, San Antonio, Texas, who is alleged to have sustained an injury on May 5, 1933, while employed as a quarantine officer in the United States Public Health Service, which resulted in permanent physical disability, and his case is authorized to be considered and acted upon under the remaining provisions of such Act, as amended, if he files a claim for compensation with the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission not later than sixty days after the date of enactment of this Act: Provided, That no benefits shall accrue prior the approval of this Act.


*Approved, June 5, 1939.*

# Chapter 577

> AN ACT For the relief of Guy F. Allen, chief disbursing officer, Division of Disbursement, Treasury Department.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Comptroller General of the United States is hereby authorized and directed to allow credit in the June 1937 and July 1938 accounts of Guy F. Allen, chief disbursing officer, Division of Disbursement, Treasury Department, without charge against the certifying officer of the Department of the Interior, for the following vouchers covering the purchase of tents and canvas: Voucher 13–85798, $65; voucher 13–86963, $73.75; voucher 13–87591, $784.25; voucher 13–87592, $366.02; voucher 13–8340, $40.75.


*Approved, August 7, 1939.*

# Chapter 604

> AN ACT To provide for the presentation of a medal to Howard Hughes in recognition of his achievements in advancing the science of aviation.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in recognition of the achievements of Howard Hughes in advancing the science of aviation and thus bringing great credit to his country throughout the world, the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized and directed to cause to be struck, and the President is authorized to present to the said Howard Hughes, a gold medal of appropriate design with suitable emblems, devices, and inscriptions to be determined by the Secretary of the Treasury.

## § 2.

There is hereby authorized to be appropriated such sum as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act.


*Approved, August 7, 1939.*

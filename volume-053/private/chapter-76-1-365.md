# Chapter 365

> AN ACT To provide a posthumous advancement in grade for the late Ensign Joseph Hester Patterson, United States Navy.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President is hereby authorized to issue posthumously to the late Ensign Joseph Hester Patterson, United States Navy, a commission as a lieutenant (junior grade) of the Navy with the date of rank as of June 4, 1939.


*Approved, July 25, 1939.*

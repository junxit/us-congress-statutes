# Chapter 300

> AN ACT For the relief of Herbert M. Snapp.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions and limitations of sections 15 to 20, both inclusive, of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes”, approved September 7, 1916, as amended and as limited by the Act or February 15, 1934 (48 Stat. 351), the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission be, and the same is hereby, authorized and directed to receive and consider, when filed, the claim of Herbert M. Snapp, of Desha Route, Batesville, Arkansas, for disability to his left eye alleged to have been incurred by him on April 3, 1937, while employed as a foreman at Sylamore Camp F–8, Ozark National Forest, Calico Rock, Arkansas: Provided, That claim hereunder shall be filed within six months after the approval of this Act: Provided further, That no benefits shall accrue prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved, July 15, 1939.*

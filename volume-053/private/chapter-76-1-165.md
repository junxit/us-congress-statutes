# Chapter 165

> AN ACT For the relief of Charles H. Parr.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions and limitations of sections 15 to 20, both inclusive, of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering injuries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes’’, approved September 7, 1916, as amended, the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission is hereby authorized and directed to receive and consider, when filed, the claim of Charles H. Parr for disability alleged to have been incurred by him on or about September 14, 1933, when engaged in authorized activities while an enrollee of the Civilian Conservation Corps at North Vernon, Indiana, and to determine said claim upon its merits under the provisions of said Act: Provided, That said claim shall be filed with the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission not later than sixty days after the approval of this Act: And provided further, That no benefits shall accrue prior to the approval of this Act.


*Approved, May 31, 1939.*

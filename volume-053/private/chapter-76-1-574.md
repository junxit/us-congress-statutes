# Chapter 574

> AN ACT For the relief of Calliope Minaca Pilavakis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the immigration and naturalization laws Calliope Minaca Pilavakis, of Newark, New Jersey, the wife of Xenophon Pilavakis and the mother of two children born in the United States, shall be held and considered to have been admitted to the United States for permanent residence on February 7, 1936: Provided, That the said Calliope Minaca Pilavakis shall never be eligible to become a citizen of the United States.

## § 2.

Any proceedings heretofore or hereafter instituted for the deportation of the said Calliope Minaca Pilavakis on the ground of unlawful residence in the United States shall be null and void.


*Approved, August 7, 1939.*

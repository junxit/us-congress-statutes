# Chapter 293

> AN ACT For the relief of Howard Arthur Beswick.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That notwithstanding the provisions and limitations of sections 15 to 20, both inclusive, of the Act entitled “An Act to provide compensation for employees of the United States suffering in juries while in the performance of their duties, and for other purposes”, approved September 7, 1916, as amended, the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission be, and the same is hereby, authorized and directed to receive and consider, when filed, the claim of Howard Arthur Beswick, of Ludlow, Vermont, for disability alleged to have been incurred by him October 21, 1928, when a plane piloted by him while a Naval Reserve officer on active duty crashed near Des Moines, Iowa: Provided, That claim hereunder shall be filed within six months after the approval of this Act: Provided further, That no benefits shall accrue prior to the enactment of this Act.


*Approved, July 15, 1939.*

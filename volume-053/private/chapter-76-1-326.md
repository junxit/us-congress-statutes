# Chapter 326

> AN ACT To give proper recognition to the distinguished services of Colonel Ernest Graves.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Colonel Ernest Graves, United States Army, retired, now on active duty, shall, after being returned to a retired status, receive the retired pay corresponding to his rank and length of service at the time of said return to said retired status.


*Approved, July 18, 1939.*

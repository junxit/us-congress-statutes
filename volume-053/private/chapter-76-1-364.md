# Chapter 364

> AN ACT For the relief of John Marinis, Nicolaos Elias, Ihoanis or Jean Demetre Votsitsanos, and Michael Votsitsanos.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That John Marinis, Nicolaos Elias, Ihoanis or Jean Demetre Votsitsanos, and Michael Votsitsanos, all residents of Tarpon Springs, Florida, engaged in deep-sea diving for sponges, shall each be permitted to remain in the United States, and shall not be subject to deportation on the grounds of unlawful residence in the United States, so long as engaged in deep-sea diving for sponges.


*Approved, July 25, 1939.*

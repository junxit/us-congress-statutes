# Chapter 718

> AN ACT To amend an Act entitled “An Act to regulate the practice of the healing art to protect the public health in the District of Columbia”, known as the “Healing Arts Practice Act, District of Columbia, 1928”, approved February 27, 1929.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act of Congress entitled “An Act to regulate the practice of the healing art to protect the public health in the District of Columbia”, known as the “Healing Arts Practice Act, District of Columbia, 1928”, approved February 27, 1929, be amended by striking from the first sentence of section 18 thereof the words “beginning on the second Monday in January and July of each year and at such other” and inserting in lieu thereof the words “at such”.


*Approved, August 11, 1939.*

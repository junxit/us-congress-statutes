# Chapter 611

> AN ACT Giving clerks in the Railway Mail Service the benefit of holiday known as Armistice Day.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the third proviso of the first section of the Act entitled “An Act to fix the hours of duty of postal employees, and for other purposes”, approved August 14, 1935, as amended, is amended by striking out the words “three hundred and six days” and inserting in lieu thereof the words “three hundred and five days”.

That this Act shall become effective as of October 1, 1939.


*Approved, August 9, 1939.*

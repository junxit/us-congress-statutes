# Chapter 517

> AN ACT To amend the Act of March 2, 1929 (45 Stat. 536).

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1 (a) (1) of the Act of March 2, 1929, entitled “An Act to supplement the naturalization laws, and for other purposes” (45 Stat., ch. 536, p. 1512), which now reads “(1) Entered the United States prior to June 3, 1921”, is hereby amended, effective as of the date this Act is enacted, so as to read as follows: “(1) Entered the United States prior to July 1, 1924”.


*Approved, August 7, 1939.*

# Chapter 336

> AN ACT To amend the Second Liberty Bond Act, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 21 of the Second Liberty Bond Act (49 Stat. 21, as amended; U. S. C., Supp. IV, title 31, sec. 757b), is amended by striking out the following proviso: “Provided, That the face amount of bonds issued under the authority of this Act shall not exceed in the aggregate $30,000,000,000 outstanding at any one time.”


*Approved, July 20, 1939.*

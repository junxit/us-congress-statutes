# Chapter 660

> AN ACT To amend paragraph (1) of section 96 of title 2 of the Canal Zone Code relating to method of computing annuities.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That paragraph (1) of section 96 of title 2 of the Canal Zone Code is amended to read as follows:

> - **(1)** A sum equal to $37.50 multiplied by the number of years of service, not to exceed thirty years, rendered (a) on the Isthmus of Panama, or (b) in the service of the United States in the Tropics; and”.


*Approved, August 10, 1939.*

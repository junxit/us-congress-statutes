# Chapter 442

> AN ACT To prohibit the issuance and coinage of certain commemorative coins, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsequent to the enactment of this Act no commemorative coins shall be coined or issued pursuant to any Act of Congress, authorizing the coinage and issuance of commemorative coins, enacted prior to March 1, 1939.


*Approved, August 5, 1939.*

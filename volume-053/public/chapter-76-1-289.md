# Chapter 289

> AN ACT To amend section 1223 of the Revised Statutes of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1223 of the Revised Statutes of the United States be amended by adding at the end thereof the following proviso: “Provided, however, That the foregoing provision shall not apply to any officer of the Army on the retired list.”


*Approved, July 15, 1939.*

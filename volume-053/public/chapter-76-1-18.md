# Chapter 18

> AN ACT To repeal section 9 of the Act of March 3, 1875 (18 Stat. L. 450), as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 9 of the Act of March 3, 1875 (18 Stat. L. 450), as amended (U. S. C., title 25, sec. 95), providing for the submission by bidders of certified checks or bonds in the amount of 5 per centum of each proposal in excess of $5,000 for goods, supplies, transportation, and so forth, for and on account of the Indian Service, is hereby repealed.


*Approved, March 27, 1939.*

# Chapter 129

> AN ACT To provide a differential in pay for night work to pneumatic-tube-system employees in the Postal Service.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Act entitled “An Act to provide a differential in pay for night work in the Postal Service”, enacted May 24, 1928 (U. S. C., 1934 edition, title 39, sec. 828), is amended by striking out the words “motor-vehicle service” and inserting in lieu thereof the following: “motor-vehicle and pneumatic-tube services”.


*Approved, May 12, 1939.*

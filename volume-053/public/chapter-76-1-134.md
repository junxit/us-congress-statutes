# Chapter 134

> AN ACT To authorize the mailing of pistols, revolvers, and other firearms capable of being concealed on the person, to officers of the Coast Guard.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 1 of the Act entitled “An Act declaring pistols, revolvers, and other firearms capable of being concealed on the person nonmailable and providing penalty”, approved February 8, 1927 (44 Stat. 1059; U. S. C., title 18, sec. 361), is hereby amended by inserting the words “Coast Guard,” after the word “Navy,” in the first proviso thereof.


*Approved, May 15, 1939.*

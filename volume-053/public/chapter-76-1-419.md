# Chapter 419

> AN ACT To provide that no statute of limitations shall apply to offenses punishable by death.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That an indictment for any offense punishable by death may be found at any time without regard to any statute of limitations.

## § 2.

This Act shall not authorize prosecution, trial, or punishment for any offense now barred by the provisions of existing law.


*Approved, August 4, 1939.*

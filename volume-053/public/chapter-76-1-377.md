# Chapter 377

> JOINT RESOLUTION To provide minimum national allotments for wheat.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the last sentence of section 333 of the Agricultural Adjustment Act of 1938, as amended (relating to the minimum acreage allotment for wheat), is amended to read as follows: “The national acreage allotment for wheat for any year shall be not less than fifty-five million acres.“


*Approved, July 26, 1939.*

# Chapter 157

> AN ACT To amend the Agricultural Marketing Agreement Act of 1937, as amended, to make its provisions applicable to apples produced in the States of Washington, Oregon, and Idaho.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 2 of the Agricultural Marketing Agreement Act of 1937, as amended, is amended by adding at the end thereof the following: “(m)”. Paragraphs (2) and (6) of section 8c are amended by inserting after the word “apples” the words “, other than apples produced in the States of Washington, Oregon, and Idaho,”.


*Approved, May 31, 1939.*

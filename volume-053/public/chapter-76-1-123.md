# Chapter 123

> JOINT RESOLUTION Designating August 19 of each year as National Aviation Day.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the President of the United States is authorized to designate August 19 of each year as National Aviation Day, and to issue a proclamation calling upon officials of the Government to display the flag of the United States on all Government buildings on that day, and inviting the people of the United States to observe the day with appropriate exercises to further and stimulate interest in aviation in the United States.


*Approved, May 11, 1939.*

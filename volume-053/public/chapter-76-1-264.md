# Chapter 264

> AN ACT To provide for the establishment of a Coast Guard station at or near the city of Monterey, California.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to establish a Coast Guard station on the Pacific coast at or in the vicinity of the city of Monterey, California, in such locality as the Commandant of the Coast Guard may recommend.


*Approved, July 12, 1939.*

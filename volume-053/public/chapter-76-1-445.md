# Chapter 445

> AN ACT To make the United States Coast Guard Academy library a public depository for Government publications.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the library of the United States Coast Guard Academy, New London, Connecticut, is hereby constituted a designated depository of Government publications, and the Superintendent of Documents shall supply to such library one copy of each such publication, in the same form as supplied to other designated depositories.


*Approved, August 5, 1939.*

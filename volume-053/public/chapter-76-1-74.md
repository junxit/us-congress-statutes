# Chapter 74

> AN ACT To authorize alterations and repairs to certain naval vessels, and for other purposes.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That for the purpose of modernizing the United States ships Argonaut, Narwhal, and Nautilus, alterations and repairs to such vessels are hereby authorized and expenditures therefor shall not be limited by the provisions of the Act approved July 18, 1935 (49 Stat. 482; 5 U. S. C. 468a): Provided, That the total cost of such alterations and repairs shall not exceed $5,000,000.


*Approved, April 20, 1939.*

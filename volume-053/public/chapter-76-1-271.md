# Chapter 271

> AN ACT To authorize appropriation for the construction of a medical school building at Carlisle Barracks, Pennsylvania.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there is hereby authorized to be appropriated not to exceed $375,000 to be expended for the construction, rehabilitation, and installation at Carlisle Barracks, Pennsylvania, of a medical field service school, and such utilities and appurtenances thereto as may be necessary.


*Approved, July 14, 1939.*

# Chapter 483

> JOINT RESOLUTION To approve the action of the Secretary of the Interior deferring the collection of certain irrigation construction charges against lands under the San Carlos and Flathead Indian irrigation projects.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in accordance with the Act of June 22, 1936, the action of the Secretary of the Interior in deferring such charges under said irrigation projects is hereby approved.


*Approved, August 5, 1939.*

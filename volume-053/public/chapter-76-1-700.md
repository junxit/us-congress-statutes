# Chapter 700

> JOINT RESOLUTION Authorizing the delegation of certain authority within the Department of Agriculture.

Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

That the Secretary of Agriculture may designate in writing the Director of Finance of the Department of Agriculture or, in his absence, the officer acting in his stead, to sign requisitions upon the Secretary of the Treasury for disbursing funds, and such requisitions shall be as valid as if they had been signed by the Secretary of Agriculture.


*Approved, August 11, 1939.*

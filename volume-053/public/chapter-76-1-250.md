# Chapter 250

> AN ACT To continue in effect until June 30, 1942, the Act entitled “An Act to regulate interstate and foreign commerce in petroleum and its products by prohibiting the shipment in such commerce of petroleum and its products produced in violation of State law, and for other purposes”, approved February 22, 1935, as amended.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That section 13 of the Act entitled “An Act to regulate interstate and foreign commerce in petroleum and its products by prohibiting the shipment in such commerce of petroleum and its products produced in violation of State law, and for other purposes”, approved February 22, 1935, as amended by the Act approved June 14, 1937 (50 Stat. 257), is further amended so as to read:

> ## § 13.
>
> This Act shall cease to be in effect on June 30, 1942.”


*Approved, June 29, 1939.*

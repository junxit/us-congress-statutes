# Chapter 716

> AN ACT To amend the Act of August 26, 1937.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That clause (d) of the Act of August 26, 1937, entitled “To provide for the extension of certain prospecting permits, and for other purposes”, is amended to read as follows:

> - **(d)** Under which at least one well shall have been drilled to a depth of not less than two thousand feet subsequent to August 21, 1935, and prior to January 1, 1939.”


*Approved, August 11, 1939.*

# Chapter 104

> JOINT RESOLUTION To amend the joint resolution approved June 16, 1938, entitled “Joint resolution to create a Temporary National Economic Committee.”

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That subsection

- **((a))** of section 6 of the joint resolution entitled “Joint resolution to create a Temporary National Economic Committee”, approved June 16, 1938, is amended by striking out “$500,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$1,100.000”.

## § Sec. 2.

Subsection

- **((b))** of such section is amended by striking out “$100,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$220,000”; and by striking out “$400,000” and inserting in lieu thereof “$880,000”.


*Approved, April 26, 1939.*

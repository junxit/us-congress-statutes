# House Concurrent Resolution 8

> sesquicentennial of first congress under the constitution

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the first paragraph of House Concurrent Resolution 4 of the Seventy-sixth Congress is hereby amended to read as follows: “That in commemoration of the one hundred fiftieth anniversary of the First Congress of the United States under the Constitution, begun and held at the city of New York on Wednesday, the 4th of March 1789, the two Houses of Congress shall assemble in the Hall of the House of Representatives at 12 o’clock meridian, on Saturday, March 4, 1939.”


*Passed, February 16, 1939.*

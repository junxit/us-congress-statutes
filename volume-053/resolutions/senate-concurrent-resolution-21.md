# Senate Concurrent Resolution 21

> statue of will rogers

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the statue of Will Rogers, presented by the State of Oklahoma, now in the Capitol Building, is accepted in the name of the United States, and that the thanks of Congress be tendered to the State for the contribution of the statue of one of its most eminent citizens, illustrious for his distinguished civic services.

That a copy of these resolutions, suitably engrossed and duly authenticated, be transmitted to the Governor of Oklahoma.


*Passed, June 19, 1939.*

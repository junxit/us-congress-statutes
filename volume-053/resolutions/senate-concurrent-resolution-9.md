# Senate Concurrent Resolution 9

> “report of the joint committee of congress appointed to investigate the tennessee valley authority”

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed three thousand additional copies of Senate Document Numbered 56, current session, entitled “Report of the Joint Committee of Congress Appointed to Investigate the Tennessee Valley Authority”, which was submitted to the Congress on March 31, 1939, of which one thousand copies shall be for the use of the Senate document room, and two thousand copies for the use of the House document room.


*Passed, May 9, 1939.*

# Senate Concurrent Resolution 29

> adjournment

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the two Houses of Congress shall adjourn on Saturday, the 5th day of August 1939, and that when they adjourn on said day they stand adjourned sine die.


*Passed, August 5, 1939.*

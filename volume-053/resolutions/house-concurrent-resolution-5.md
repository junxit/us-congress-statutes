# House Concurrent Resolution 5

> “investigation of un-american activities and propaganda”

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed twenty-five thousand additional copies of House Report Numbered 2, current Congress, entitled “Investigation of Un-American Activities and Propaganda”, of which three thousand copies shall be for the use of the Senate document room, and twenty-two thousand copies shall be for the use of the House document room.


*Passed, February 1, 1939.*

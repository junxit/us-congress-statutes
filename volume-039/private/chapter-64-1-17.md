# Chapter 17

> An Act For the relief of William H. Woods.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of $152.21 is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the possession of the United States belonging to the Choctaw and Chickasaw Indian Tribes not otherwise appropriated, to reimburse William H. Woods for expenses incurred by him as assistant tribal attorney of the Chickasaw Indians between the date of December thirty-first, nineteen hundred and thirteen, and May fourth, nineteen hundred and fourteen, inclusive.


*Approved, January 18, 1917.*

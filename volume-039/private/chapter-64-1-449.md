# Chapter 449

> An Act For the relief of John P. Sutton.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws John P. Sutton, who enlisted in Company H, Eighteenth Regiment United States Infantry, on the twenty-seventh day of March, eighteen hundred and sixty-six, under the name of Gilbert Sutton, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a member of said company and regiment under his true name of John P. Sutton.


*Approved, September 6, 1916.*

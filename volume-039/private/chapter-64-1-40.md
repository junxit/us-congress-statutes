# Chapter 40

> An Act For the relief of Sarah E. Elliott.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be, and hereby is, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $1,073, to be paid to Sarah E. Elliott, to compensate her for the death of her husband, James K. P. Elliott, accidentally killed on April tenth, nineteen hundred and one, while in the discharge of his duty in the service of the Government of the United States in the Mare Island Navy Yard.


*Approved, February 8, 1917.*

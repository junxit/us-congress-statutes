# Chapter 37

> An Act For the relief of Allen M. Hiller.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, or benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers Allen M. Hiller, who was a first lieutenant in Company G, One hundred and ninety-ninth Regiment Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as an officer of said company and regiment on the twenty-eighth day of June, eighteen hundred and sixty-five: Provided, That no back pay or pension be allowed prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 8, 1917.*

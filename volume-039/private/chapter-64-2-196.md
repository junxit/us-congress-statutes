# Chapter 196

> An Act For the relief of Marguerite Mathilde Slidell d’Erlanger.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Marguerite Mathilde Slidell d’Erlanger be, and she is hereby, on her own application, unconditionally readmitted to the character and privileges of a citizen of the United States.


*Approved, March 4, 1917.*

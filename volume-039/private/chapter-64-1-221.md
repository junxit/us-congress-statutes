# Chapter 221

> An Act For the relief of Daniel M. Frost.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Daniel M. Frost be authorized to make a homestead entry for an unappropriated quarter section of public land, subject to said entry, as though his former entry numbered sixty-five hundred and ninety-five, Lamed, Kansas, series, had not been made.


*Approved, July 3, 1916.*

# Chapter 405

> An Act For the relief of John L. Moon.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to John L. Moon, of Opelika, Lee County, Alabama, the sum of $200, as a reward for services rendered in the apprehension of criminals in the burglary of the United States post office at Auburn, Alabama.


*Approved, August 24, 1916.*

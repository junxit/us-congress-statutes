# Chapter 381

> An Act For the relief of Hiram P. Geaslin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to credit the accounts of or to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Hiram P. Geaslin, of Hornersville, Missouri, the sum of $81.71, being the value of documentary stamps taken from his custody as postmaster at Hornersville, Missouri, by burglars on the sixth day of April, nineteen hundred and fifteen.


*Approved, August 21, 1916.*

# Chapter 77

> An Act For the relief of Martin V. Parmer.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, or benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Martin V. Parmer, late of One hundred and thirty-eighth Company, Second Battalion, Veteran Reserve Corps, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a member of said company on the fourth day of August, eighteen hundred and sixty-four: Provided, That no pension shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 15, 1917.*

# Chapter 265

> An Act For the relief of Julia R. Goodloe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the title of Juba R. Goodloe in and to fractional section thirty-one, township two, range thirteen, Colbert County, Alabama, be, and the same is hereby, quieted and confirmed, and patent therefor shall issue to the said Julia R. Goodloe.


*Approved, August 1, 1916.*

# Chapter 104

> An Act For the relief of Thomas Smart.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, or benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers Thomas Smart, late of Company B, Eighth Regiment Connecticut Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been present with his company and regiment from the twenty-seventh day of February, eighteen hundred and sixty-four, to May twenty-seventh, eighteen hundred and sixty-four: Provided, That other than as above set forth no pay, bounty, or other emolument shall accrue by reason of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 20, 1917.*

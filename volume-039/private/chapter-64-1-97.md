# Chapter 97

> An Act For the relief of S. Spencer Carr.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, S. Spencer Carr, who was a first lieutenant of Company B, Eighth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States, as a first lieutenant of said company and regiment, on the eighteenth day of September, eighteen hundred and sixty-four: Provided, That no bounty, pay, or allowances shall be held as accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 19, 1917.*

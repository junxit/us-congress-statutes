# Chapter 68

> An Act For the relief of Joseph A. Jennings.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Joseph A. Jennings the sum of $2,500, on account of the amputation of his left arm, incurred while employed in the gun carriage shop of the United States Navy Yard, at Washington, District of Columbia, and a sufficient sum for the payment of the same is hereby appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, February 14, 1917.*

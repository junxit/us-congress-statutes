# Chapter 243

> An Act For the relief of William Foglio.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized to issue patent to William Foglio for the southwest quarter section twenty-three, and the northwest quarter section twenty-six, township twenty-nine north, range thirty east, Montana: Provided, That no adverse right had accrued to said tract prior to January first, nineteen hundred and fifteen.


*Approved, July 11, 1916.*

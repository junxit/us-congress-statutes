# Chapter 52

> An Act For the relief of Stephen J. Simpson.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and. House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers Stephen J. Simpson, late of Company F, Thirty-first Illinois Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a member of said company and regiment on the nineteenth day of July, eighteen hundred and sixty-five: Provided, That no bounty, pay, or allowance shall accrue by virtue of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 12, 1917.*

# Chapter 78

> An Act For the relief of Gardiner L. Eastman.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Gardiner L. Eastman, who was a private of Company H, Thirtieth Regiment Maine Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a member of said company and regiment on or about the sixteenth day of June, eighteen hundred and sixty-five: Provided, That no pay, pension, bounty, nor other emolument shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, February 15, 1917.*

# Chapter 186

> An Act Permitting H. L. Corbin to purchase certain public lands.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That H. L. Corbin be permitted to purchase the west half of the southwest quarter of section thirty-five, township eight south, range seventy west of the sixth principal meridian, containing eighty acres, at $1.25 per acre.


*Approved, June 28, 1916.*

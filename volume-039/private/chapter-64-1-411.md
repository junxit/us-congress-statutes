# Chapter 411

> An Act Authorizing the Secretary of the Interior to issue patent to William H. Ingle for homestead entry in Colorado.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That homestead entry naught fourteen thousand three hundred and sixteen, Sterling series, made by William H. Ingle for the southeast quarter of section five, township one north, range forty-nine west, sixth principal meridian, Colorado, be, and it is hereby, validated and permitted to remain intact, in the same manner as though said Ingle had not, in the year eighteen hundred and seventy-three, made a homestead entry for one hundred and sixty acres and perfected title thereto.


*Approved, August 25, 1916.*

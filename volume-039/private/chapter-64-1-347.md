# Chapter 347

> An Act For the relief of Thomas P. Sorkilmo.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $1,848.38 to Thomas P. Sorkilmo, of Dell Rapids, South'Dakota, for the balance due him for the construction of a brick hospital at the Canton Indian Insane Asylum under his contract of August first, nineteen hundred and thirteen.


*Approved, August 16, 1916.*

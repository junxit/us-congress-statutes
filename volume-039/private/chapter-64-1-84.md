# Chapter 84

> An Act To renew patent numbered twenty-one thousand and fifty-three.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a certain design patent issued by the United States Patent Office of date September twenty-second, eighteen hundred and ninety-one, being patent numbered twenty-one thousand and fifty-three, is hereby renewed and extended for a period of fourteen years from and after the passage of this Act, with all the rights and privileges pertaining to the same as of the original patent, being generally known as the badge of the Daughters of the American Revolution.


*Approved, April 20, 1916.*

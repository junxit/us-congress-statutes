# Chapter 175

> An Act For the relief of Hays Gaskill.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, or benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Hays Gaskill, who was a member of Battery A, Fourth Regiment United States Artillery, under General Order Numbered One hundred and fifty-four, Adjutant General’s Orders, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been honorably discharged from the military service of the United States as a member of said battery on the muster out of the same: Provided, That no back pay, pension, bounty, or other emolument shall accrue prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 3, 1917.*

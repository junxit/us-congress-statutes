# Chapter 277

> An Act For the relief of George W. Trahey.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be paid to George W. Trahey, of Bremerton, Washington, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $762 for damages suffered by him on account of an injury received by him while performing his duties as master shipwright at the navy yard, Puget Sound, on May eighth, nineteen hundred and twelve, said injury resulting from gunshot wounds inflicted upon him by one John N. Haley, an employee of said yard, who at the time of such shooting was insane.


*Approved, August 7, 1916.*

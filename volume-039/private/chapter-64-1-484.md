# Chapter 484

> An Act For the relief of John E. Keys.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of $602 to John E. Keys, for injuries received during the construction of the Corbett Tunnel on the Shoshone Reclamation Project in Wyoming.


*Approved, September 8, 1916.*

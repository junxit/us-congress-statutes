# Chapter 58

> An Act For the relief of the heirs of John Howard Payne, deceased, late United States consul at Tunis.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of $205.92 be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, in payment of the amount found to be due John Howard Payne, late United States consul at Tunis, and that the same be paid to his heirs.


*Approved, April 3, 1916.*

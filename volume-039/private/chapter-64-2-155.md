# Chapter 155

> An Act For the relief of Caleb T. Holland.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of any laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Caleb T. Holland, who was a private of Company E, Sixtieth Regiment Illinois Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a member of said company and regiment on the eighteenth day of April, eighteen hundred and sixty-four: Provided, That no bounty, pay, or allowance shall be held to have accrued prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, March 2, 1917.*

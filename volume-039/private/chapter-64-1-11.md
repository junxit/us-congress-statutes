# Chapter 11

> An Act For the relief of Thomas M. Jones.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the pension laws and laws governing entrance to soldiers’ homes Thomas M. Jones be held and considered to have been mustered into the United States service as a drummer in Independent Company C, Pennsylvania Volunteer Infantry, on September eighth, eighteen hundred and sixty-two, and to have been honorably discharged on April twenty-third, eighteen hundred and sixty-three: Provided, That no bounty, pay, or other allowance shall become due or payable by reason of the passage of this Act.


*Approved, December 30, 1916.*

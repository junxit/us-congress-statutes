# Chapter 204

> An Act Authorizing the Secretary of the Interior to sell certain lands to the city of Lemmon, South Dakota.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Interior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to cause an appraisement to be made of the land, exclusive of all improvements, embraced in the southeast quarter of the northeast quarter of section ten and the southwest quarter of the northwest quarter of section eleven, in township one hundred and twenty-nine north, of range ninety-two west of the fifth principal meridian, in the county of Adams and the State of North Dakota and in the Dickinson, North Dakota, land district, containing eighty acres; and upon the payment of the appraised price by the city of Lemmon, in the State of South Dakota, to convey by patent to said city of Lemmon the said land.


*Approved, June 30, 1916.*

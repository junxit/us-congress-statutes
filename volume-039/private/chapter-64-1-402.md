# Chapter 402

> An Act For the relief of Nathaniel Monroe.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in the administration of the laws conferring rights, privileges, and benefits upon honorably discharged soldiers, Nathaniel Monroe, who was a private of Company B, First Regiment East Tennessee Volunteer Infantry, shall hereafter be held and considered to have been discharged honorably from the military service of the United States as a member of said company and regiment on the first day of May, eighteen hundred and sixty-three: Provided, That no back pay, bounty, or pension shall accrue to him prior to the passage of this Act.


*Approved, August 23, 1916.*

# Chapter 455

> An Act Providing that Indian schools may be maintained without restriction as to annual rate of expenditure per pupil.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all moneys appropriated or available for Indian school purposes may be expended without restriction as to per capita expenditure for the annual support and education of any one pupil in any Indian school: Provided, That in no event shall the per capita cost at any one school exceed the sum of $200 per annum.


*Approved, September 7, 1916.*

# Chapter 140

> AN ACT To amend the public-building Act approved March fourth, nineteen hundred and thirteen, authorizing the acquisition of a suitable site for a public building at Pittston, Pennsylvania.

*Beit enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provision of the public-building Act approved March fourth, nineteen hundred and thirteen (thirty-seventh Statutes, page eight hundred and seventy-six), which authorizes the acquisition of a suitable site, and so forth, at Pittston, Pennsylvania, be, and the same is hereby, amended so as to add the following proviso, namely:

> “Provided, That the Secretary of the Treasury may, in his discretion accept a title which reserves or excepts all ores or minerals on the lands with the right of mining the same.”


*Approved, February 27, 1917.*

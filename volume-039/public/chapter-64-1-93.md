# Chapter 93

> AN ACT To amend paragraphs one hundred and seventy-seven and one hundred and seventy-eight of an Act entitled “An Act to reduce tariff duties and to provide revenue for the Government, and for other purposes,” approved October third, nineteen hundred and thirteen, relating to the duty on sugar, molasses, and other articles.

*Be it enacted by the Senate a nd House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the proviso of paragraph hundred and seventy-seven of the Act entitled “An Act to reduce tariff duties and to provide revenue for the Government, and for other purposes,” approved October third, nineteen hundred and thirteen (Statutes at Large, volume thirty-eight, pages one hundred and fourteen to two hundred and two, inclusive), which proviso reads as follows: “Provided further, That on and after the first day of May, nineteen hundred and sixteen, the articles hereinbefore enumerated in this paragraph shall be admitted free of duty,” be, and the same is hereby, repealed.

## § 2.

That the proviso of paragraph one hundred and seventy-eight of the aforesaid Act, which proviso reads as follows: “Provided, That on and after the first day of May, nineteen hundred and sixteen, the articles hereinbefore enumerated in this paragraph shall be admitted free of duty,” be, and the same is hereby, repealed.


*Approved, April 27, 1916.*

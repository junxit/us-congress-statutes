# Chapter 215

> An Act For the relief of certain homestead entrymen for land within the limits of the Glacier National Park.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the homestead entries heretofore made by Howard E. Jones, Ernest R. Henthorn, Daniel C. Doverspike, Ora Reeves, Louis N. Fournier, Pat Doyle, Walter E. Barricklow, and Frank Kelly for lands within the limits of the Glacier National Park, in the Kalispell, Montana, land district, which entries were allowed under orders issued by the Secretary of the Interior on May twenty-first and twenty-fifth, nineteen hundred and ten, based upon lists approved by the Secretary of Agriculture prior to the passage of the Act of May eleventh, nineteen hundred and ten (Thirty-sixth Statutes at Large, page three hundred and fifty-four), creating the said Glacier National Park, be, and they are hereby, excepted from the force and effect of said Act of May eleventh, nineteen hundred and ten: Provided, That should said entries not be perfected as required by law the lands embraced therein shall revert to and become a part of the said Glacier National Park.


*Approved, July 3, 1916.*

# Chapter 27

> JOINT RESOLUTION Making part of the appropriation, “Construction and machinery, increase of the Navy,” in the naval Act approved March third, nineteen hundred and fifteen, available for the extension of building ways and equipment at the navy yards at New York and Mare Island, California.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That of the money heretofore appropriated under the appropriation “Increase of the Navy, construction and machinery,” in the Act making appropriations for the naval service for the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, nineteen hundred and sixteen, approved March third, nineteen hundred and fifteen, the sums of not exceeding $100,000 for navy yard, New York, and not exceeding $500,000 for navy yard, Mare Island, California, are hereby made available for the extension of building ways and equipment necessary for the construction of battleships or battle cruisers in those yards.


*Approved, February 15, 1916.*

# Chapter 1

> AN ACT Granting the consent of Congress to the Citizens’ Bridge Company to construct a bridge across the Mississippi River at or near Burlington, Iowa.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the consent of Congress is hereby granted to the Citizens’ Bridge Company and its successors and assigns to construct, maintain, and operate a bridge and approaches thereto across the Mississippi River at a point suitable to the interests of navigation at or near Burlington, Iowa, in the County of Des Moines, in the State of Iowa, in accordance with the provisions of the Act entitled “An Act to regulate the construction of bridges over navigable waters,” approved March twenty-third, nineteen hundred and six.

## § 2.

That the right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, December 17, 1915.*

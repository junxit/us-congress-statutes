# Chapter 182

> AN ACT For a public building at Paris, Texas.

*Be it enacted by the Senate, and Souse of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the sum of $170,000, or so much thereof as may be necessary, be, and the same is hereby, authorized to be expended by the Secretary of the Treasury for the purpose of supplying the necessary building for the Federal court, post office, and other Government offices at Paris, Texas.


*Approved, March 4, 1917.*

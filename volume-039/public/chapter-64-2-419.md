# Chapter 419

> An Act To provide for the maintenance of a training camp on the military reservation of Fort Douglas, Utah.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized to maintain upon the military reservation of Fort Douglas, Utah, for such period within the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, nineteen hundred and seventeen, as he may deem advisable, a camp for the military instruction and training of such citizens as may be selected for such instruction and training under such regulations as he may prescribe, and to furnish to said citizens the subsistence, transportation, and uniforms authorized by the Act of Congress approved June third, nineteen hundred and sixteen; and for the purposes of this Act there is hereby appropriated the sum of $30,000, to be paid out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, August 29, 1916.*

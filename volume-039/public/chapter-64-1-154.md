# Chapter 154

> AN ACT To amend an Act entitled “An Act to amend the statutes in relation to immediate transportation of dutiable goods, and for other purposes,” approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the privileges of the first section of the Act approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, entitled “An Act to amend the statutes m relation to immediate transportation of dutiable goods, and for other purposes,” be, and the same are hereby, extended to the port of Jacksonville, Florida.


*Approved, June 16, 1916.*

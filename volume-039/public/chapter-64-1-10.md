# Chapter 10

> AN ACT Granting an extension of time to construct a bridge across Rock River at or near Colona Ferry, in the State of Illinois.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the time for the commencement of the bridge authorized by the Act entitled “An Act to construct a bridge across Rock River at or near Colona Ferry, in the State of Illinois,” by the counties of Henry and Rock Island, in the State of Illinois, approved August nineteenth, nineteen hundred and eleven, is hereby extended to one year from the date of the passage of this Act, and the time for completion of the bridge extended three years from the date of approval of this Act.

## § 2.

That the right to alter, amend, or repeal this Act is hereby expressly reserved.


*Approved, January 14, 1916.*

# Chapter 89

> AN ACT Prohibiting the use of the name of any Member of either House of Congress or of any officer of the Government by any person, firm, or corporation practicing before any department or office of the Government.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That it shall be unlawful for any person, firm, or corporation practicing before any department or office of the Government to use the name of any Member of either House of Congress or of any officer of the Government in advertising the said business.

## § 2.

That this Act shall take effect three months after its date.


*Approved, April 27, 1916.*

# Chapter 30

> JOINT RESOLUTION To appoint Alexander Graham Bell a member of the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution.

*Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the vacancy in the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution of the class “other than Members of Congress” shall be filled by the appointment of Alexander Graham Bell, a citizen of the District of Columbia.


*Approved, February 21, 1916.*

# Chapter 234

> An Act For the establishment of Northport, Chopaka, and Laurier, in the State of Washington, as ports of entry for immediate transportation without appraisement of dutiable merchandise.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the privileges of the first section of the Act approved June tenth, eighteen hundred and eighty, governing the immediate transportation of dutiable merchandise without appraisement, be, and are hereby, extended to the ports of Northport, Chopaka, and Laurier, in the State of Washington.


*Approved, July 8, 1916.*

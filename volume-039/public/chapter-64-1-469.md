# Chapter 469

> An Act To reserve certain lands and make them a part of the Pike National Forest.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That all lands in the State of Colorado described as follows, to wit: Section nineteen and section thirty in township two south, range seventy-two west, sixth principal base and meridian, be, and the same are hereby, reserved, subject to all prior valid adverse rights, and made a part of and included in the Pike National Forest.


*Approved, September 8, 1916.*

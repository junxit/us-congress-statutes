# Chapter 161

> An Act Providing for the establishment of a term of the district court for the middle district of Tennessee at Winchester, Tennessee.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That a term of the district court for the middle district of Tennessee shall be held at Winchester on the first Monday in April and the third Monday in November.


*Approved, June 22, 1916.*

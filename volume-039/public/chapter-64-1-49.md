# Chapter 49

> AN ACT Reserving or excepting all ores or minerals on the lands, with the right of mining the same, on the site of the proposed post-office building at Calumet, Michigan.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the provision of the public building Act approved March fourth, nineteen hundred and thirteen (Thirty-seventh Statutes, page eight hundred and seventy-eight), which authorizes the acquisition of a suitable site for the post office at Calumet, Michigan, be, and the same is hereby, amended as follows:

> “Provided, That the Secretary of the Treasury may, in his discretion, accept a title which reserves or excepts all ores or minerals on the lands, with the right of mining the same.”


*Approved, March 20, 1916.*

# Chapter 181

> An Act To establish a Coast Guard station on the coast of Louisiana, in the vicinity of Barataria Bay.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to establish a Coast Guard station on the coast of Louisiana in the vicinity of Barataria Bay, at such point as he may deem best.


*Approved, June 28, 1916.*

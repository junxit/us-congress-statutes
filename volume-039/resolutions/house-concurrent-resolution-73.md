# House Concurrent Resolution 73

> immigration act.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That ten thousand copies of the Immigration law (Public Numbered Three hundred and one, Sixty-fourth Congress—H. R. 10384) be printed for the use of the Senate and House of Representatives, to be distributed as follows: One thousand five hundred copies for the use of the House Committee on Immigration and Naturalization; one thousand copies for the use of the Senate Committee on Immigration; four thousand five hundred copies to be distributed through the folding room of the House; and three thousand copies through the folding room of the Senate.


*Passed, February 26, 1917.*

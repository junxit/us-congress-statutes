# Senate Concurrent Resolution 26

> federal farm loan act.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed one hundred and fifty thousand additional copies of Senate Document Numbered Five Hundred, Sixty-fourth Congress, entitled “The Federal farm loan Act,” of which fifty thousand copies shall be for the use of the Senate and one hundred thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives.


*Passed, September 7, 1916.*

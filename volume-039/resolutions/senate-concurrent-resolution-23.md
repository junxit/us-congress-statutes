# Senate Concurrent Resolution 23

> omnibus pension bill, s. 4856.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the Secretary of the Senate be authorized in the enrollment of the bill (S. 4856) “granting pensions and increase of pensions to certain soldiers and sailors of the Civil War and certain widows and dependent relatives of such soldiers and sailors,” to change the word “Hentry” to “Henry” where it occurs in line 21, page 7, and to change the word “him” to “her” where it occurs in line 1, page 18.


*Passed, May 27, 1916.*

# House Concurrent Resolution 67

> holiday recess.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn on Friday, December twenty-second, nineteen hundred and sixteen, they stand adjourned until twelve o’clock meridian, on Tuesday, January second, nineteen hundred and seventeen.


*Passed, December 20, 1916.*

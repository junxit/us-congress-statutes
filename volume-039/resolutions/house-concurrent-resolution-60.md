# House Concurrent Resolution 60

> adjournment.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President of the Senate and the Speaker of the House of Representatives be authorized to close the present session of the Congress by adjourning their respective Houses on the eighth day of September, nineteen hundred and sixteen, at ten o’clock antemeridian.


*Passed, September 8, 1916.*

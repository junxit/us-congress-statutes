# Senate Concurrent Resolution 13

> statue of henry mower rice.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That the statue of Henry Mower Rice, presented by the State of Minnesota to be placed in Statuary Hall, is accepted in the name of the United States, and that the thanks of Congress be tendered to the State for the contribution of the statue of one of its most eminent citizens, illustrious for the purity of his life and his distinguished services to the State and Nation.

Second. That a copy of these resolutions, suitably engrossed and duly authenticated, be transmitted to the governor of the State of Minnesota.


*Passed, March 11, 1916.*

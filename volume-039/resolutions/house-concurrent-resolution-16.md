# House Concurrent Resolution 16

> urgent deficiencies appropriation bill.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the Committee of Conference on the disagreeing votes of the two Houses on the amendments of the Senate to the bill H. R. 9416, making appropriations to supply urgent deficiencies in certain appropriations, are authorized to change the text of the paragraph in the bill for payment of judgments in Indian depredation claims so as to correct errors in the certified description of two of the judgments provided for therein.


*Passed, February 12, 1916.*

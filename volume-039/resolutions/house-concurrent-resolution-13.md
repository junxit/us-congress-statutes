# House Concurrent Resolution 13

> diseases of the horse.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there be printed and bound in cloth one hundred thousand copies of the Special Report on the Diseases of the Horse, the same to be first revised and brought to date under the supervision of the Secretary of Agriculture; seventy thousand copies for the use of the House of Representatives and thirty thousand for use of the Senate.


*Passed, February 3, 1916.*

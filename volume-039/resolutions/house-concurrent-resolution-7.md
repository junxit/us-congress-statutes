# House Concurrent Resolution 7

> holiday recess.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That when the two Houses adjourn December seventeenth, nineteen hundred and fifteen, they stand adjourned until twelve o’clock, meridian, on Tuesday, January fourth, nineteen hundred and sixteen.


*Passed, December 17, 1916.*

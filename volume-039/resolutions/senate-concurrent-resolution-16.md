# Senate Concurrent Resolution 16

> statue of henry mower rice.

*Resolved by the Senate (the House of Representatives concurring),*

That there be printed and bound, under the direction of the Joint Committee on Printing, the proceedings in Congress, together with the proceedings at the unveiling in Statuary Hall, upon the acceptance of the statue of Henry Mower Rice presented by the State of Minnesota, sixteen thousand five hundred copies, with suitable illustration, of which five thousand shall be for the use of the Senate and ten thousand for the use of the House of Representatives, and the remaining one thousand five hundred copies shall be for the use and distribution of the Senators and Representatives in Congress from the State of Minnesota.


*Passed, April 8, 1916.*

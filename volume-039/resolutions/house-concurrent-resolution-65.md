# House Concurrent Resolution 65

> grand army encampment, 1917.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That there shall be printed as a House document one thousand five hundred copies of the journal of the fifty-first national encampment of the Grand Army of the Republic, for the year nineteen hundred and seventeen, not to exceed $1,700 in cost, with illustrations, one thousand copies of which shall be for the use of the House and five hundred for the use of the Senate.


*Passed, February 13, 1917.*

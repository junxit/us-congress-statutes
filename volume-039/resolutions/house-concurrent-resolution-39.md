# House Concurrent Resolution 39

> aqueduct bridge, d. c.

*Resolved by the House of Representatives (the Senate concurring),*

That the President be requested to return to the House of Representatives the enrolled bill (H. R. 759) entitled “An Act to provide for the removal of what is now known as the Aqueduct Bridge across the Potomac River, and for the building of a new bridge in place thereof.”


*Passed, May 12, 1916.*

# Chapter CLXXIII

> An Act for the relief of Davis Hunt.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, directed to place upon the list of revolutionary pensioners, Davis Hunt, of New York; and that said Davis Hunt be paid at the rate of forty dollars per annum, commencing on the fourth day of March, anno Do-mini, eighteen hundred and thirty-two.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

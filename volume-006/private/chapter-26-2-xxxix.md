# Chapter XXXIX

> An Act to authorize the enrolment or register of the schooner Amistad.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Spanish schooner Amistad, lately sold at New London, by the marshal, in pursuance of an order of the district court of the United States for the district of Connecticut, may be registered, or enrolled, the same as if said schooner had been built within the United States.


*Approved, March 3, 1841.*

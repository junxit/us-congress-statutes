# Chapter CXXIII

> An Act for the relief of Jacob Slough.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Jacob Slough, formerly a captain in the army of the United States, be, and he is hereby, released from the payment of the sum of one thousand five hundred and twenty-two dollars and fifteen cents, the balance which stands charged against him on the books of the treasury of the United States.


*Approved, May 19, 1824.*

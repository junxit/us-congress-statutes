# Chapter CCXII

> An Act for the relief of the heirs and legal representatives of William Teas, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to the heirs and legal representatives of William Teas, deceased, five years’ full pay as a cornet of horse in the revolutionary war, which five years’ full pay is the commutation of half pay for life.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

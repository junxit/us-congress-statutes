# Chapter CXV

> An Act for the relief of Benjamin F. Hard.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, directed to pay Benjamin F. Hard the sum of one thousand three hundred and twenty-nine dollars and fifty cents, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, August 1, 1842.*

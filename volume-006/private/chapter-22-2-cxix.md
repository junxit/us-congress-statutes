# Chapter CXIX

> An Act for the relief of Lieutenant George D. Ramsay, of the Army of the United States.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the pay department be directed to refund to Lieutenant George D. Ramsay, one hundred and twenty dollars and eighty-one cents; which sum appears to have been charged to said Ramsay’s account by the treasury department, and deducted out of his pay as lieutenant for an alleged omission of duty in turning over to the fifth regiment of infantry certain military convicts, with the papers and orders relating to the same.


*Approved, March 2, 1833.*

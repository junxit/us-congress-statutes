# Chapter CCLX

> An Act for the relief of John Emerson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury, be, and they are hereby, required to settle and adjust the account of John Emerson, and allow him five years’ full pay as a lieutenant of infantry of the revolutionary army in the Virginia line on continental establishment; which five years’ full pay is the commutation of his half pay for life; to be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

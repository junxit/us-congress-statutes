# Chapter XXIV

> An Act for the relief of Joel Abbott, junior.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, directed to pay, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Joel Abbott, junior, the sum of eighty dollars, for a horse lost while in the service of the United States, during the late war.


*Approved, March 3, 1825.*

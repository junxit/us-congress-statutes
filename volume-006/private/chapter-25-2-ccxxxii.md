# Chapter CCXXXII

> An Act to authorize the Commissioner of Pensions tn cause the unpaid pension of Captain Robert Laird, of the stale of Georgia, to be paid to his executor.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Commissioner of Pensions be, and he is hereby, authorized to cause to he paid over to the executor of Captain Robert Laird, deceased, for the use and benefit of the legatees to whom the same was bequeathed, late of Clark county, in the state of Georgia, the amount which was due him, the said Captain Robert Laird, at the time of his death, any law to the contrary notwithstanding.


*Approved, July 7, 1838.*

# Chapter LXII

> An Act for the relief of Edward Wilson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Edward Wilson shall be permitted to withdraw an entry made by James Caldwell, on the fourth day of April, one thousand eight hundred and eleven, at the land office at Steubenville, from the south-west quarter of section number thirteen, in township number nine, of range number seven, in the district of land offered for sale at Steubenville; and the money paid on the said entry shall be placed to the credit of said Wilson on any purchase he may make, or may have made, of public lands in said district.


*Approved, April 20, 1816.*

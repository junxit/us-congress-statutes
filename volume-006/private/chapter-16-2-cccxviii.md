# Chapter CCCXVIII

> An Act for the relief of Martin Prindle.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he hereby is, directed to pay to Martin Prindle, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one hundred and twenty-five dollars, being the value of a horse and sleigh lost while in the service of the United States.


*Approved, July 2, 1836.*

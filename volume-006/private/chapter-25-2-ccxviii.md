# Chapter CCXVIII

> An Act for the relief of the administrator of Wharton Quarles, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury of the United States be authorized to pay, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to the administrator of Wharton Quarles, deceased, late a lieutenant in the second Virginia state regiment in the revolutionary war, the sum of three hundred and forty-six dollars and sixty-six cents, the balance of a judgment obtained by the administrator of said Wharton Quarles in the courts of the state of Virginia, for half pay for revolutionary services; which said judgment was assumed by the United States, by the act of the fifth of July, eighteen hundred and thirty-two.


*Approved, July 7, 1838.*

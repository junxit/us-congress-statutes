# Chapter LIV

> An Act for the relief of James May.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to James May, of Detroit, or his legal representatives, the sum of four hundred and eighty-seven dollars and sixty-two cents, in full for his claim upon the United States for services rendered as Marshal of the Territory of Michigan.


*Approved, March 2, 1827.*

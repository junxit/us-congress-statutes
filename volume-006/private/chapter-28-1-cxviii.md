# Chapter CXVIII

> An Act for the relief if Ephraim D. Dickson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Ephraim D. Dickson, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one hundred and fifty-two dollars for shoeing seventy-six horses in Captain E. D. Dickson’s company of volunteer mounted gunmen, in General Coffee’s brigade of Tennessee volunteers, in September, eighteen hundred and fourteen, at two dollars per horse.


*Approved, June 17, 1844.*

# Chapter LII

> An Act for the relief of the legal representatives of Joseph Rowe, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Navy pay to the legal representatives of Joseph Rowe, a boatswain’s mate, deceased, the sum of one thousand four hundred and twenty-seven dollars and thirteen cents, for his share of prize money arising from the capture of the British fleet on Lake Champlain, during the late war, and reported to be due to him on the books of the treasury; to be paid out of any money not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 18, 1834.*

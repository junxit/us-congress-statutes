# Chapter CXLIX

> An Act for the relief of George Elliot.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, directed to place the name of George Elliot, on the roll of invalid pensioners, and pay to him six dollars a month during his natural life, commencing on the seventh day of December, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-five.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

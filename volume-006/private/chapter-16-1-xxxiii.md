# Chapter XXXIII

> An Act for the relief of Robert Swartwout.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury department be, and they are hereby, authorized to audit and settle the account of Robert Swartwout, and to allow him such sum as may appear equitable and just, in consequence of the loss sustained in the sale of certain treasury notes, for and on account of the United States.


*Approved, April 5, 1820.*

# Chapter LXXVI

> An Act for the relief of Joseph Wheaton.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury department be authorized and directed to settle and adjust the account of Joseph Wheaton, while acting in the quartermaster’s department, during the late war, upon principles of equity and justice.


*Approved, March 3, 1819.*

# Chapter CCCIII

> An Act for the relief of Ebenezer Spaulding.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Ebenezer Spaulding, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, thirty dollars, the sum paid by him into the treasury for a patent, which patent has never been issued.


*Approved, July 2, 1836.*

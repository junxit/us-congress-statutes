# Chapter LXXIV

> An Act for the relief of Edward Evat.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Edward Evat, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, thirteen hundred and thirty-eight dollars and eighty-one cents, being for the value of a house, burnt while occupied by the American troops: Provided, however, That there shall be deducted out of the aforesaid sum of thirteen hundred and thirty-eight dollars and eighty-one cents, whatever amount may have been paid to the said Evat for the hire or use of the said house.


*Approved, March 3, 1823.*

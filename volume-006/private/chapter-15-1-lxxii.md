# Chapter LXXII

> An Act for the relief of Major-General Jacob Brown.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury department be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to settle the claim of Major-General Jacob Brown, on account of a judgment obtained against him in the Supreme Court of the State of New York, at the suit of Henry Utley, together with reasonable costs and charges: Provided, That the sum to be allowed on the settlement aforesaid, shall not exceed the sum of six hundred [and] twenty-one dollars and ninety-two cents.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the aforesaid sum shall be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, April 18, 1818.*

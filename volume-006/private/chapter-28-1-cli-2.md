# Chapter CLI

> An Act for the relief of William R. Davis.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be directed to pay, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to William R. Davis, of Indiana, the sum of seventy-five dollars, for repairing and cleaning arms and accoutrements used in the regiment commanded by Colonel George W. Ewing, at the Pottawatomie Indian payment, in September, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-six, and for furnishing powder, lead, paper, twine, casting balls, and making one thousand cartridges for said expedition.


*Approved, June 17, 1844.*

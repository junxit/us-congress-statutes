# Chapter XXIX

> An Act for the relief of Joseph Bogy.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Joseph Bogy, to whom was confirmed a concession of three hundred and twenty arpens of land in Arkansas, by mistake for eight hundred arpens, be, and he is hereby, empowered to locate four hundred and eighty arpens of land on any land subject to sale at private entry, in the state of Arkansas.


*Approved, March 3, 1841.*

# Chapter CXXIII

> An Act for the relief of Doctor J. M. Foltz.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the same pay be allowed, from the seventh of June, eighteen hundred and thirty-six, to the ninth of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-seven, to J. M. Foltz, that was allowed to surgeon at the headquarters of the United States marine corps who was relieved by him by order of the honorable the Secretary of the Navy.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

# Chapter CCXLVI

> An Act for the relief of George Read.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to George Read, late attorney of the United States for the Delaware district, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one hundred and sixty-two dollars and ninety-eight cents, being in full for the amount of an award and judgment, with interest thereon, in the District Court of the United States in and for the said district, in favor of the said George Read.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

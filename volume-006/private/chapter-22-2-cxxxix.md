# Chapter CXXXIX

> An Act for the relief of Lieutenant Harvey Brown.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the sum of two hundred and fifty-one dollars and twelve cents be paid to Lieutenant Harvey Brown, of the United States’ army, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, as a compensation for quarters not furnished to him in St. Augustine, in east Florida, for sixteen months and twenty-three days.


*Approved, March 2, 1833.*

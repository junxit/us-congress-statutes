# Chapter IX

> An Act for the relief of Samuel Crapin.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to place the name of Samuel Crapin on the pension roll, and pay him a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month, from the twenty-first day of March, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-eight, during his natural life.


*Approved, March 19, 1842.*

# Chapter CXVII

> An Act for the relief of Charles Waldron.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, required and directed to pay to Charles Waldron, or to his legal representatives, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one thousand one hundred and seventy-nine dollars and sixty-one cents, it being for buildings and other property destroyed at Micanopy, Florida, in eighteen hundred and thirty-six, by order of the officer then in command at that post, and to prevent them from falling into possession of the enemy.


*Approved, March 3, 1843.*

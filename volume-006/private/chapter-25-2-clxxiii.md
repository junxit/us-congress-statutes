# Chapter CLXXIII

> An Act for the relief of Allen Rogers.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury Department pay to Allen Rogers, of the state of Maine, or his assigns, owner of the brig L’Orient, the sum of two hundred and eighty-two dollars and five cents, in full for provisions furnished certain invalid seamen returning from Rio Janeiro in said brig, and that said sum be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 3, 1843.*

# Chapter LXIX

> An Act releasing the owners of the ship General Jackson, from the payment of certain duties.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Francis G. Macey, and the other owners of the ship General Jackson, of the port of Nantucket, in the state of Massachusetts, be, and they are hereby, released and discharged from the payment of all duties upon oil, imported into the said port, in the said ship, in the month of July last, and also, from the payment of any extra tonnage duty, charged upon the said ship, in the said month, by reason of the foreign character of John Fisher, the master of the said vessel.


*Approved, May 17, 1824.*

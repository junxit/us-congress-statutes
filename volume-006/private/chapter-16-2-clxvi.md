# Chapter CLXVI

> An Act for the relief of Henry Keefer.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to place the name of Henry Keefer, upon the list of invalid pensioners, at the rate of eight dollars per month, commencing on the first of January, eighteen hundred and thirty-six, and pay him out of any money in the treasury, not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

# Chapter LXIV

> An Act for the relief of Hanson Kelly.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Hanson Kelly, of North Carolina, three thousand five hundred and twenty-seven dollars and forty-six cents; being the balance of pay due him for rations furnished to prisoners of war, at Wilmington, from the twelfth day of May, one thousand eight hundred and fourteen, to the eleventh day of February, one thousand eight hundred and fifteen; and that the said sum be paid out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, May 5, 1824.*

# Chapter XII

> An Act for the relief of Charles Markin.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Charles Markin shall be permitted to withdraw his entry made on the twenty-third day of February, one thousand eight hundred and fifteen, at the land office at Chillicothe, from the north-west quarter of section number nineteen, township number two, of range number sixteen, and the money paid by him on the said entry shall be placed to his credit on any purchase he shall make or may have made of public lands in the same district.


*Approved, February 6, 1816.*

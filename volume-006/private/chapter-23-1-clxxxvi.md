# Chapter CLXXXVI

> An Act for the relief of John C. Naylor.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury cause to be audited and paid to John C. Naylor, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of three hundred and twenty-two dollars and twenty cents, the amount due said Naylor for services rendered as clerk in the surveyor-general’s office south of the state of Tennessee, from the tenth June up to fifth November, one thousand eight hundred and thirty.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

# Chapter CXV

> An Act for the relief of Isaac S. Ketchum, late special Indian agent.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be directed to pay to Isaac S. Ketchum the sum of three hundred and eighty-three dollars and one cent, the balance due him for provisions purchased and delivered by him as special agent for the Government to the Chippewas, Ottawas, and Pottawatomie Indians, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 17, 1844.*

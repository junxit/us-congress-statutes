# Chapter CCLI

> An Act for the relief of the heirs of William B. Bunting.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, pay the full amount of seven years’ half pay of a lieutenant of infantry in the army of the revolution, to such person or persons as may be entitled to the same under the resolution of Congress of the twenty-fourth of August, one thousand seven hundred and eighty, on account of the services of William B. Bunting, late a lieutenant in the army of the revolution, and who died while in the service.


*Approved, July 7, 1838.*

# Chapter XXIX

> An Act for the relief of the executor of Charles Wilkins.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be directed to settle the claim of Benjamin W. Dudley, executor of Charles Wilkins, deceased, against the United States, for interest on a liquidated demand in favor of Jonathan Taylor, James Morrison and the said Charles Wilkins, who were lessees of the United States of the salt works, in the state of Illinois, upon the same principles that the interest upon the said demand was settled with the legal representatives of the said James Morrison, deceased, pursuant to the act of Congress, approved the first of May, eighteen hundred and thirty-four, and that the portion of said interest ascertained to be due to the said Benjamin W. Dudley, executor as aforesaid, be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, February 17, 1836.*

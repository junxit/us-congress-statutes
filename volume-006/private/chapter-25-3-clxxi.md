# Chapter CLXXI

> An Act for the relief of Samuel Mussey and Thomas James, of the county of Crawford, in the state of Missouri.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Samuel Mussey and Thomas James, of the county of Crawford, in the state of Missouri, be, and hereby are, authorized to enter, at the proper land office, as tenants in common or joint partnercy, such lands in township thirty-eight, of the ranges five and six, west of the fifth principal meridian, as they may desire, at the minimum price: Provided, however, That the selection of lands shall be confined to the aforesaid township thirty-eight, of the ranges five and six west of the principal meridian, in the county of Crawford, in the state of Missouri; and this act shall not be construed so as to permit the aforesaid Mussey or James to select, or purchase, or occupy, any lands in said township that may now be in the possession of any actual settler, or under improvement.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

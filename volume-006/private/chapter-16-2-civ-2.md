# Chapter CIV

> An Act for the relief of the widow of Samuel Gibbs.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, directed to allow and pay to the widow of Samuel Gibbs, deceased, late a pensioner on the revolutionary invalid pension roll, the amount which would have become due to the said Samuel as an invalid pensioner, from the fifteenth of May, eighteen hundred and twenty-six, to the time of his death, if he had not taken the benefit of any act of Congress which required him to relinquish such invalid pension as a condition of his being placed on the list of revolutionary pensioners.


*Approved, June 15, 1836.*

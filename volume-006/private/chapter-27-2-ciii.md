# Chapter CIII

> An Act for the relief of George M. Bedinger, of the state of Kentucky.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be authorized and required to place the name of George M. Bedinger on the roll of revolutionary pensioners, and pay him a pension for five months’ service as a sergeant, and fifteen months’ service as a private, according to the provisions of the act of the seventh of June, eighteen hundred and thirty-two; said pension to commence on the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-one.


*Approved, July 27, 1842.*

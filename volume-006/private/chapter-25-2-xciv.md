# Chapter XCIV

> An Act for the relief of William Tharp.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War is hereby authorized and required to examine the claim of William Tharp to the reimbursement of a fee, alleged by the said Tharp to have been paid by him to an attorney to defend a suit against him, instituted about the year eighteen hundred and eight, in the superior court at New Orleans, to recover from him, (the said Tharp,) as endorser, the amount of a draft for eleven hundred and sixty-three dollars and ninety-two cents, drawn by Lieutenant James Reed on the Secretary of War, and protested; and whatever sum the Secretary shall ascertain to have been so paid, he shall cause to be paid to William Tharp out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 7, 1838.*

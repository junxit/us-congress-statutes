# Chapter CXLVI

> An Act for the relief of James Monroe.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to cause to be paid to James Monroe, out of any unappropriated moneys in the treasury, the sum of twenty-nine thousand five hundred and thirteen dollars, in full of all demands whatever, against the United States.


*Approved, May 22, 1826.*

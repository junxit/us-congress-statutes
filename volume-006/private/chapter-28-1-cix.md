# Chapter CIX

> An Act for the relief of Robert Monroe.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and hereby is, directed to place the name of Robert Monroe, of Westmoreland county, in the state of Pennsylvania, on the roll of invalid pensioners, and to pay him four dollars per month during his natural life, from and after the first day of April, anno Domini eighteen hundred and forty-four.


*Approved, June 17, 1844.*

# Chapter LXVII

> An Act for the relief of John Law and Jonathan Elliot, citizens of the city of Washington, in the District of Columbia.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the time allowed to John Law and Jonathan Elliot, (now purchasers and holders of a lot A, part of a reservation numbered ten, in the city of Washington,) for complying with the building regulations of the said city of Washington, be extended for and during the term of one year, from and after the thirtieth day of July, eighteen hundred and twenty; any thing contained in any law of Congress, or regulation made pursuant thereto, in anywise to the contrary notwithstanding.


*Approved, May 4, 1820.*

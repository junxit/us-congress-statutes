# Chapter LI

> An Act for the relief of Davis Floyd.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, authorized and required to allow and pay unto Davis Floyd, one of the commissioners for ascertaining the rights of persons claiming lands in East Florida, the sum of five hundred dollars, as a full compensation for having brought to the seat of government the report of the said commissioners; and the said sum shall be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, May 16, 1826.*

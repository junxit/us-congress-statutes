## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the tract of land described in said plat, as belonging to the church, measuring one arpent, eighteen toises, and one foot, and one-third of a foot, and numbered in the preceding list sixty-five, be, and the same is hereby, confirmed and recognised as valid, to and for the uses and purposes for which the same has been heretofore held and used : Provided, also, That this act shall be only considered a relinquishment on the part of the United States, and not to interfere with the rights of others.


*Approved, June 25, 1832.*

# Chapter CCX

> An Act for the relief of Jasper Parish.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Jasper Parish, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, the sum of four hundred and twenty-seven dollars and fifty cents, the value of his fences necessarily taken and consumed by the troops of the United States, near to, or at the mouth of, Conjockety Creek, in the state of New York, under the command of Brigadier-General Smythe


*Approved, May 29, 1830.*

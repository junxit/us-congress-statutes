# Chapter XCIII

> An Act to authorize the Secretary of State to issue letters patent to James Jones.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of State be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to issue letters patent, in the usual form, to James Jones, for his invention of “certain improvements in the making of rovings, spinning and doubling of cotton, silk, flax, and other fibrous substances,” upon his complying with all the provisions of the existing laws, except so far as they require on the part of aliens a residence of two years in the United States.


*Approved, March 3, 1835.*

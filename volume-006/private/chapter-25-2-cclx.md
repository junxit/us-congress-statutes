# Chapter CCLX

> An Act for the relief of H. Quertier and Albert, of New Orleans.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That A. Quertier and Albert, of New Orleans, are hereby authorized to export, with the benefit of drawback, a package of Spanish playing cards, which was imported into that port in November last, in the ship Alexander Toussin, from Havre, and now remaining in the public stores: Provided, That all the regulations relating to the exportation of merchandise, with the benefit of drawback, shall be complied with.


*Approved, July 7, 1838.*

# Chapter CXXXII

> An Act for the relief of the heirs and, legal representatives of Dr. Samuel J. Axson, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to the heirs and legal representatives of Dr. Samuel J. Axson, five years’ full pay as a surgeon in the revolutionary war; which five years’ full pay is the commutation of half pay for life; together with such interest thereon as would now be due if a certificate for such commutation had been issued at the close of the war, and subscribed under the principles of the funding act, and all dividends thereon were now remaining in the treasury unpaid.


*Approved, June 15, 1832.*

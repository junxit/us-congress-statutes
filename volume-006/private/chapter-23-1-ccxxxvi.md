# Chapter CCXXXVI

> An Act to remunerate Stephen C. Phillips, for the support and transportation of shipwrecked American seamen.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the sum of twenty-two hundred and forty-three dollars and ninety-three cents be paid, out of money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Stephen C. Phillips, of Salem, in the state of Massachusetts, to indemnify him for the expense of maintaining at the Fejee islands, and of transporting from thence to Manilla, twenty-six American seamen, of the officers and crews of two American vessels which had been wrecked and lost at the said Fejee islands in the year one thousand eight hundred and thirty-one.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

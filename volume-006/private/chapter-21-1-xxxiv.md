# Chapter XXXIV

> An Act for the relief of Richard Biddle, administrator of John Wilkins, junior, formerly Quartermaster-General of the army of the United States.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers be, and are hereby, authorized and directed to settle and adjust the accounts of John Wilkins, junior, formerly Quartermaster-General of the army of the United States, upon the principles of equity and justice.


*Approved, March 18, 1830.*

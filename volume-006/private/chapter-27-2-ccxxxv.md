# Chapter CCXXXV

> An Act for the relief of Henry Fry.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to place the name of Henry Fry, of the city of Philadelphia, formerly a purser in the navy, on the list of invalid navy pensioners, and to pay him at the rate of twenty dollars per month, to commence on the first of January, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-eight.


*Approved, August 29, 1842.*

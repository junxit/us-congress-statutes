# Chapter CXLIV

> An Act to amend an act entitled “An act for the relief of Raphael Paine and Elias Arnold,” approved March second, eighteen hundred and thirty-three.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to issue the scrip in said act named to the said Paine and Arnold, or their assignee, in lieu of lot thirty-three, instead of lot thirty-nine, as therein mentioned.


*Approved, June 28, 1838.*

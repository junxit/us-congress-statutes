# Chapter XXXIV

> An Act for the relief of Joseph Forrest.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Joseph Forrest the sum of two thousand one hundred and thirty-six dollars, for demurrage on the schooner William Yeaton, at the port of Laguira, not already paid under the award made at Laguira, in the year eighteen hundred and twelve; and that the said sum be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated: Provided, That, before any payment shall be made, the said Joseph Forrest shall release the United States from any claim in future, on account of said vessel.


*Approved, March 3, 1825.*

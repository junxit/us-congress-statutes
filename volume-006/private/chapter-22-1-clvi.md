# Chapter CLVI

> An Act for the relief of Ephraim Whitaker.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That, in addition to the allowance and payments authorized and required to be made to Ephraim Whitaker by the act, entitled, “An act for the relief of Ephraim Whitaker and John J. Jacobs,” passed on the twenty-ninth day of May, eighteen hundred and thirty, he shall be accounted with, and paid in the same manner, and to the same extent, as is authorized and required by the act, entitled “An act for the relief of certain surviving officers and soldiers of the army of the revolution,” which pay shall begin on the third day of March, eighteen hundred and twenty-six, and continue during his natural life.


*Approved, July 3, 1832.*

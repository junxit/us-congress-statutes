# Chapter CXXXIX

> An Act to revive and extend the provisions of an act passed on the twenty-fourth May, eighteen hundred and twenty-four, entitled “An act for the relief of the representatives of John Donelson, Stephen Heard, and others.”

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That an act entitled “An act for the relief of the representatives of John Donelson, Stephen Heard, and others,” be, and the same is hereby, revived and continued in force for the term of twelve months from and after the passage of this act; and that the said representatives, in addition to the states of Alabama and Mississippi, be, ami they are hereby, authorized to enter said lands at any of the land offices in Louisiana or Arkansas.


*Approved, June 23, 1836.*

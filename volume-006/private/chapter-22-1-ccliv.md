# Chapter CCLIV

> An Act for the relief of Silvia Posner.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the third auditor of the treasury department be, and he is hereby authorized and directed, to adjust and settle the claim of Silvia Posner, for the value of a house, called Richmond Bath, and the adjoining out buildings, and allow her the value thereof; which buildings were burnt while occupied by the American troops during the late war; and the sum so found to be due, shall be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, July 14, 1832.*

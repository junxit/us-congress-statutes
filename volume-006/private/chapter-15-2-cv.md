# Chapter CV

> An Act for the relief of James Orr.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed, to pay to James Orr, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, two hundred and fifty dollars, the value of a certain schooner called the “Telegraph,” taken and sunk for the defence of the harbour of Baltimore, in the year eighteen hundred and fourteen.


*Approved, March 3, 1819.*

# Chapter XII

> An Act to remunerate the captors of the privateer Lydia.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to David Robinson, Ebenezer Robinson, James Robinson, and John Robinson, or to their or either of their heirs or assigns, the sum of one thousand dollars, as a remuneration for the capture of the privateer Lydia, at Robinson’s island, in the year eighteen hundred and thirteen.


*Approved, February 6, 1839.*

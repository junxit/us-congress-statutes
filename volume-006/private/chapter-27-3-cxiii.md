# Chapter CXIII

> An Act for the relief of Samuel Lord.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Samuel Lord, of Charleston, South Carolina, be, and he is hereby, discharged from all further liability upon a judgment obtained and now outstanding against him as surety on a custom-house bond, given to the United States, by Howe and Fitch, in eighteen hundred and eighteen, upon said Lord first paying, or securing to be paid, to the United States the sum of one thousand dollars; and that the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to execute a release to said Lord, for said judgment, upon his paying, or securing to be paid, in a satisfactory manner, the sum of one thousand dollars.


*Approved, March 3, 1843.*

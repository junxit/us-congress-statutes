# Chapter XI

> An Act granting a pension to Margaret Jamison.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Margaret Jamison, widow of Samuel Jamison, deceased, of the state of Pennsylvania, on the revolutionary pension roll, under the act of July fourth, eighteen hundred and thirty-six, for four months’ service of her late husband as a sergeant and two months as a lieutenant.


*Approved, March 19, 1842.*

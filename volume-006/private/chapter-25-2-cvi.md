# Chapter CVI

> An Act for the relief of Motes Van Campen.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury do settle the account of Moses Van Campen, and allow him five years’ full pay as a lieutenant of infantry in the Pennsylvania line on continental establishment; and that the said five years’ full pay be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 12, 1838.*

# Chapter XVIII

> An Act for the relief of the widow and heirs of Felix St. Vrain.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be, and is hereby, granted to the widow and heirs of the late Felix St. Vrain, of Missouri, six hundred and forty acres of land, to be selected by them within the state of Missouri, either in an entire section, or in tracts not less than one quarter section, and entered in the proper Land Office in said state: Provided, said land be subject to entry at private sale, and not settled upon or occupied by any other person.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That on the application of the said widow and heirs or their duly authorized agent, to the Register and Receiver of the proper Land Office, and on payment of one dollar for each tract so entered, a final certificate shall be given for the same, upon which, a patent from the United States to the said widow and heirs of Felix St. Vrain shall be issued.


*Approved, March 24, 1834.*

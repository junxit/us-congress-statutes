# Chapter CCCIII

> An Act for the relief of Grieve Drummond.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to Grieve Drummond, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the amount of a pension granted to him by an act passed March third, one thousand eight hundred and fifteen; which has been withheld in consequence of his omission to comply with the provisions of the act, passed March third, one thousand eight hundred and nineteen.


*Approved, July 16, 1832.*

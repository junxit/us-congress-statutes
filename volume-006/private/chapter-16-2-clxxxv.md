# Chapter CLXXXV

> An Act for the relief of John Lent.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, directed to place John Lent, of the county of Bradford, in the state of Pennsylvania, on the pension roll of the United States, and that there be allowed to the said John Lent, the sum of eighty dollars per annum, during his natural life, to commence on the eighth day of May, eighteen hundred and thirty-three.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

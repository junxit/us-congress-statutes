# Chapter XCVIII

> An Act for the relief of the heirs of Johnston Blakeley.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury department be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to pass to the credit of the account of Johnston Blakeley, late a captain in the United States’ Navy, the sum of three thousand and twenty-eight dollars and sixty-four cents, being the amount of moneys advanced to the said Blakeley, before the first day of May, eighteen hundred and fourteen, for the use of the United States’ Navy.


*Approved, March 3, 1823.*

# Chapter LXI

> An Act for the relief of the representatives of James A. Harper, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay, to the legal representatives of James A. Harper, deceased, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, the sum of two hundred fifty-two dollars and ninety-three cents, for money expended by the said Harper, in the recruiting service of the United States during the late war, and which is now due to said representatives, as it appears by a statement from the treasury department.


*Approved, March 3, 1829.*

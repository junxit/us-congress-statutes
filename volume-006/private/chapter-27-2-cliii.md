# Chapter CLIII

> An Act for the relief of Mary Prettyman.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, directed to cause to be paid, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise, appropriated, the sum of two hundred and forty-seven dollars, to Mary Prettyman, widow of Thomas G. Prettyman, deceased, who was a sergeant in Captain Aisquith’s company, third brigade of Maryland militia, in the service of the United States, and who died of disabilities incurred in said service during the last war with Great Britain.


*Approved, August 11, 1842.*

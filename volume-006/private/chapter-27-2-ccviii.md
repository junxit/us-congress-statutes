# Chapter CCVIII

> An Act for the relief of Charles D. Hammond and Augustus H. Kenan.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That at the next term of the district court of the United States for the district of Georgia, ensuing the passage of this act, Charles D. Hammond and Augustus II. Kenan be permitted to open a judgment obtained against them in the year eighteen hundred and forty, in said court, as the securities of Solomon D. Betton, late postmaster at Milledgeville, Georgia, at the suit of the Postmaster-General of the United States; and that the said Charles D. Hammond and Augustus H. Kenan be permitted to make the same defence to said suit, that they might or could have made had said judgment not been rendered: Provided, however, That the said judgment shall stand as a security for such sum as may be recovered.


*Approved, August 26, 1842.*

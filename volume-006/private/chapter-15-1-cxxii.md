# Chapter CXXII

> An Act for the relief of Benjamin Birdsall and William S. Foster.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the department of war be, and they are hereby, authorized to audit and settle the several accounts of Benjamin Birdsall and William S. Foster, of the army of the United States, in such manner, and upon such terms, as may be reasonable and just.


*Approved, April 20, 1818.*

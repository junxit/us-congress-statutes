# Chapter CLI

> An Act for the benefit of Charles Coffin.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Charles Coffin, of the state of Maine, upon the roll of invalid pensioners, as a pensioner, at the rate of one hundred and twenty dollars per annum; pension to commence from the first day of January, eighteen hundred and thirty-seven; and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, June 28, 1838.*

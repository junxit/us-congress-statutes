# Chapter CLIII

> An Act for the relief of James Mullings.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and is hereby, required to place the name of James Mullings, of Charlotte, Virginia, on the pension roll of revolutionary pensioners, at the rate of thirty dollars per annum, commencing on the fourth day of March, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-one, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

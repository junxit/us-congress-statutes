# Chapter CXIX

> An Act for the relief of James Barnett.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, required to settle the account of James Barnett, a lieutenant of infantry in the Continental line in the revolutionary war, and to allow to him five years’ full pay for his services in said war as a lieutenant as aforesaid, it being the commutation for his half pay for life; and that it be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, May 28, 1830.*

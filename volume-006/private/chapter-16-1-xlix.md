# Chapter XLIX

> An Act for the relief of Elizabeth Braden.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Department of War be, and he is hereby, directed to issue, in the name of Elizabeth Braden, a land warrant for the bounty land to which her son, George Braden, a soldier, who died of wounds received in the late war, would have been entitled had he lived.


*Approved, April 24, 1820.*

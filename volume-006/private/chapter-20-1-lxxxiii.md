# Chapter LXXXIII

> An Act for the benefit of Andrew Wesbrook.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That a patent or patents shall issue to Andrew Wesbrook, formerly of the province of Upper Canada, but now a citizen of the United States, for a quantity of land equal to two sections of land, which may be located according to any of the legal subdivisions of the public lands, on any of the unappropriated lands of the United States, which have been hitherto offered for sale; and which are now subject to entry at private sale.


*Approved, May 23, 1828.*

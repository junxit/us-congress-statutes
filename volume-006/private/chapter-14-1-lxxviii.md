# Chapter LXXVIII

> An Act for the relief of Charles Levaux Trudeau.

*Be it enacted &c.,*

That Charles Levaux Trudeau, or his legal representatives, be authorized to enter with the register of the land office, without payment, any six quarter sections of land in place of other lands confirmed by the board of commissioners west of Pearl river, in the Mississippi territory, provided the same do not exceed one thousand acres, which has been offered at public sale in the Mississippi territory; and on return being made to the commissioner of the general land office, a patent shall issue as in other cases.


*Approved, April 26, 1816.*

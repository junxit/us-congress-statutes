# Chapter VIII

> An Act for the relief of Loudon Case.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to Loudon Case, or his legal representative, out of any money in the treasury, not otherwise appropriated, the sum of ninety-two dollars, the value of a yoke of oxen, impressed into the service of the United States, in the fall of the year one thousand eight hundred and thirteen.


*Approved, January 7, 1824.*

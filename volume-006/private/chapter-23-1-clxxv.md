# Chapter CLXXV

> An Act for the relief of Henry Whitney.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Henry Whitney, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, the sum of four hundred and fifty-seven dollars and fifty cents, for services rendered on board the Guerriere frigate, as acting secretary of the squadron, in the years eighteen hundred and twenty-nine, and eighteen hundred and thirty, in the Pacific.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

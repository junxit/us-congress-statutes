# Chapter CXLIV

> An Act for the relief of William Biggs.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That William Biggs, of the state of Illinois, be, and he is hereby, authorized to enter three sections of land, in quarter sections, of the lands subject to entry at private sale in the Sangamon district, in Illinois, in consideration of his services as lieutenant in the regiment of the late General George Rogers Clark, which marched against, and subdued, the posts of Kaskaskias and Vincennes.


*Approved, May 22, 1826.*

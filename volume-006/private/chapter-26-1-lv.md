# Chapter LV

> An Act for the relief of Ichabod Beardsley, of New York.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to place the name of Ichabod Beardsley on the roll of Revolutionary Pensioners, under the act of Congress passed the seventh day of June, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-two, and that he pay to the said Ichabod Beardsley, the pension of a private for the service of eighteen months under said law, from the fourth day of March, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-one, during his natural life.


*Approved, July 20, 1840.*

# Chapter LXXXVII

> An Act for the relief of Gervis Foote.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, directed to pay to Gervis Foote, of the state of New York, or his heirs, the sum of eleven hundred and fifty dollars, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, being the amount agreed to be paid to him by the agent of the United States, under his contract for the delivery of a certain quantity of stone on the bank of Lake Erie, at Barcelona, in eighteen hundred and thirty-eight, and eighteen hundred and thirty-nine.


*Approved, June 15, 1844.*

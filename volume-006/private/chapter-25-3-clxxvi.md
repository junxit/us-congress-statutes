# Chapter CLXXVI

> An Act for the relief of Joseph Safford and other heirs at law of Joseph Sufford, late of the New Hampshire line in the revolutionary war, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That a duplicate warrant for two hundred acres of land be issued by the proper officer to Joseph Safford and other heirs of Joseph Safford, late a lieutenant in the New Hampshire line in the revolutionary war, deceased, in lieu of warrant number one thousand six hundred and eighty-three, which heretofore issued to the same persons, and which has been, as they allege, lost by time or accident.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

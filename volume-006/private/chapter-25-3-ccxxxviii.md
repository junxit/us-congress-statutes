# Chapter CCXXXVIII

> An Act for the relief of the legal representatives of the late Doctor James H. Cheears.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to the legal representatives of Doctor James H. Cheears, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the compensation of assistant surgeon in the army of the United States, from the ninth of July, eighteen hundred and thirty-seven, to the twenty-second of October, eighteen hundred and thirty-seven, the said Doctor Cheears having discharged the duties of that office in General Armstrong’s brigade of mounted Tennessee volunteers, in their campaign in Florida.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

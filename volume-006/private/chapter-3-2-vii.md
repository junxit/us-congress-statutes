# Chapter VII

> An Act for the relief of Peter Covenhoven.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be allowed and paid to Peter Covenhoven, late a serjeant in the militia, the sum of four hundred and eight dollars and twenty-six cents, in full compensation of certain costs and expenditures attending the cure of a wound he received in an action near Fort Schuyler, during the late war.


*Approved, January 1, 1795.*

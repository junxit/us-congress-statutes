# Chapter CCXC

> An Act for the relief of Elizabeth Scott, only surviving child, and heir at law, of Captain William Blackwell, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized, directed, and required to issue to Elizabeth Scott, a land warrant for three hundred acres of military bounty lands, as and for the land to which her father, Captain William Blackwell, was entitled, on account of his military services in the war of the revolution; and that the same may be located on any vacant or unlocated lands heretofore appropriated by Congress for that purpose.


*Approved, July 14, 1832.*

# Chapter XIII

> An Act for the relief of John Steele.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers liquidate and settle the account of John Steele, for his services as Secretary of the Mississippi Territory, from the seventh day of May, eighteen hundred and two, to the second day of March, eighteen hundred and three, inclusively; and that there be paid to him for his salary and official expenditures, such compensation as is by law allowed to persons acting in that capacity.


*Approved, January 31, 1805.*

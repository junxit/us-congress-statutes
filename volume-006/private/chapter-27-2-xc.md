# Chapter XC

> An Act for the relief of Isaac Fessenden.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and hereby is, authorized to pay to Isaac Fessenden fifty dollars, the amount of a fine imposed upon and paid by him in October, eighteen hundred and thirty-six, as master of the schooner Pearl, by the collector of the customs at Pensacola, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, July 27, 1842.*

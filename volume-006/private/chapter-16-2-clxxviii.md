# Chapter CLXXVIII

> An Act granting a pension to Peter L. Allen.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Peter L. Allen on the invalid pension roll, and that he be paid at the rate of thirteen dollars and thirty-three cents and one-third per month, being the one-third of a captain’s pay, to commence on the first of January, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-six.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

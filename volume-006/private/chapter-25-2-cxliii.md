# Chapter CXLIII

> An Act for the relief if Leonard Loomis.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War lie directed to place the name of Leonard Loomis on the list of invalid pensioners; and that there be paid to said Loomis a pension at the rate of six dollars a month, commencing on the first day of January, anno Domini eighteen hundred and thirty-seven.


*Approved, June 28, 1838.*

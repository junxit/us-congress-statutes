# Chapter CCXXXI

> An Act for the relief of John Jones and Charles Souder and Robinson Carr and. Company.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, to John Jones and Charles Souder, one hundred and eight dollars fifteen cents, and to Robinson Carr and Company, one hundred and nineteen dollars twenty cents, for expenses incurred by them, respectively, in unloading stone at Peapatch Island, which was to have been incurred by the United States.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

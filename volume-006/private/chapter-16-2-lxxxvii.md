# Chapter LXXXVII

> An Act for the relief of Richard T. Archer.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Richard T. Archer, of the state of Mississippi, be, and he is hereby, authorized to become the purchaser of the south half of section thirty-three, of township twenty, of range two east, of lands in the north-western district of lands in the late Choctaw purchase, in the state of Mississippi, at the price of one dollar and twenty-five cents per acre, the said half section of land having been illegally reserved from the location of the said Archer, under an assignment of a grant from the trustees of Jefferson college, in the said state, at the public sale of lands at Chocehuma, in the mouth of October, in the year eighteen hundred and thirty-three.


*Approved, March 3, 1835.*

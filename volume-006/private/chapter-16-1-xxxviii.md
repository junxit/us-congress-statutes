# Chapter XXXVIII

> An Act authorizing payment to be made for certain Muskets impressed into the Service of the United States.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to David Henley, agent of Edward Fox, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of two hundred and twenty-eight dollars, it being the value of nineteen muskets, impressed into the service of the United States: Provided, That the said David Henley shall first give security to indemnify the United States against the claim of any other individual, for the value of said muskets.


*Approved, April 7, 1820.*

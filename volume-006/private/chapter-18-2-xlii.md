# Chapter XLII

> An Act for the relief of Sarah Shillito.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Sarah Shillito, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of three hundred and fifty dollars for a house destroyed in the summer of one thousand eight hundred and thirteen, while in the occupation of the United States’ troops; deducting, however, from the above sum, the amount that has been paid for the rent of said house, by any officer of the army of the United States.


*Approved, March 3, 1825.*

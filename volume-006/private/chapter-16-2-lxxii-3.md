# Chapter LXXII

> An Act for the relief of Philip F. Voorhees.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Philip F. Voorhees, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of six hundred dollars, in full for expenses incurred by him while commanding the ship John Adams, in the Mediterranean, during the years eighteen hundred and thirty-one, and eighteen hundred and thirty-two, in conveying our consuls to and from their consulates, and the Tripolitan Ambassador from Malta to Tripoli, by orders from Commodores Biddle and Patterson.


*Approved, March 3, 1837.*

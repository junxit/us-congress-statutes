# Chapter LXXXIV

> An Act for the relief of James Young.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Commissioner of Public Buildings be, and he is hereby, required to convey, in fee simple, to James Young, or his assigns, lot number sixteen, square six hundred and eighty-six, in the city of Washington, upon the payment by said Young, or his assigns, for said Jot, at the rate of one cent per square foot; Provided, however, That said Young, or his assigns, shall pay for said lot, at the rate aforesaid, within six months after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1835.*

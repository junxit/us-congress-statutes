# Chapter LXXIII

> An Act granting a pension to Benjamin Leslie.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be authorized and directed to place the name of Benjamin Leslie on the roll of invalid pensioners, at the rate of eight dollars per month to commence on the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-four.


*Approved, March 3, 1835.*

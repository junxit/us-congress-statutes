# Chapter XCII

> An Act for the relief of James H. Clark.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the accounting officers of the treasury department be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to allow James H. Clark the sum of eight hundred and sixteen dollars, in the settlement of his accounts; the same being the amount of money of which he was robbed in Marseilles, on the third day of December, eighteen hundred and fifteen.


*Approved, March 3, 1823.*

# Chapter CXCVIII

> An Act for the relief of Elizabeth Gibbs.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Elizabeth Gibbs, of Tompkins county, New York, on the pension roll, and that she be paid during her natural life, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, for six months service of her first husband, Abram Smith, as a lieutenant, and for six months service as a private in the revolutionary war, according to the provisions of the act of July fourth, eighteen hundred and thirty-six, and the explanatory act of March third, eighteen hundred and thirty-seven.


*Approved, August 23, 1842.*

# Chapter LII

> An Act granting a pension to John Bryant.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of John Bryant, of the county of Strafford’ in the state of New Hampshire, on the roll of invalid pensioners, and pay to him the sum of eight dollars per month during his natural life, commencing on the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-five.


*Approved, March 3, 1835.*

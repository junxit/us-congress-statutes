# Chapter CCXLIX

> An Act for the relief of John Chandler and William Johnson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Postmaster-General be authorized and directed to pay to John Chandler and William Johnson, sureties of J. B. Griffin, formerly postmaster at Selma, in the state of Alabama, the sum of three hundred and ninety-seven dollars, collected from them.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

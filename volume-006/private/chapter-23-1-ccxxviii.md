# Chapter CCXXVIII

> An Act for the relief of John Shackford.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to John Shackford, out of any money in the treasury, not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one hundred and forty-four dollars, in full, for his claim for rent of a store-house in the town of Galena, state of Illinois, owned by him, and used by the United States for the storage of lead, subsequent to the spring of the year one thousand eight hundred and twenty-nine.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

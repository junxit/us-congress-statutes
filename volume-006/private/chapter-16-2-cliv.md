# Chapter CLIV

> An Act for the relief if Stephen Talmage.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, directed, to place Stephen Talmage, of the county of Suffolk, in the State of New York, on the pension roll of the United States, and that there be allowed to the said Talmage, the sum of thirty-six dollars and seventy-five cents, per annum, during his natural life, to commence on the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-one.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

# Chapter LXVII

> An Act for the relief of Isaac Richer.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury department be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Isaac Ricker, or his legal representatives, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one hundred dollars, being the value of a horse, saddle, and bridle, impressed into the public service by Colonel Miller, in the year eighteen hundred and twelve, and captured by the British, at the surrender of Detroit by General Hull.


*Approved, March 2, 1827.*

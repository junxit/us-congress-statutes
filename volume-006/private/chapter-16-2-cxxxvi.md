# Chapter CXXXVI

> An Act for the relief of Archibald Small.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Archibald Small be, and he is hereby, permitted and allowed to enter the west half of the south-east quarter of section number thirty-three, in township number thirteen, north of range five east, in the district of lands offered for sale at Indianopolis, in the state of Indiana, for which he shall be entitled to a patent, on his relinquishing to the United States the west half of the south-west quarter of section number thirty-three, in township number thirteen, north of range five east, in the district aforesaid, and which was entered by said Archibald Small through mistake, and not detected, by reason of the said Archibald Small not being able to read.


*Approved, June 23, 1836.*

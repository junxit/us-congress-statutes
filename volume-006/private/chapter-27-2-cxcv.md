# Chapter CXCV

> An Act for the relief of Jehosaphat Briggs.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, required to place the name of Jehosaphat Briggs, of Jefferson county, New York, on the list of United States’ pensioners, and to pay him the sum of five dollars and thirty-three and one-third cents per month, from the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and forty, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, August 23, 1842.*

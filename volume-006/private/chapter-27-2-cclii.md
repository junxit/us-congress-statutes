# Chapter CCLII

> An Act for the relief of Jacob Greaves.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, required to place the name of Jacob Greaves, of the city of Washington, on the invalid pension roll, and to pay him at the rate of eight dollars a month during his natural life, commencing on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and forty.


*Approved, August 29, 1842.*

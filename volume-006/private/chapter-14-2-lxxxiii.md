# Chapter LXXXIII

> An Act for the relief of Caleb Nicholls.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the department of war be, and they are hereby, authorized to adjust and settle the claim of Caleb Nicholls, for the damage done to his house and store, under the orders of General Macomb, in the month of September, eighteen hundred and fourteen, at Plattsburg, in the state of New York.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the amount of such damage, when ascertained, shall be paid to the said Caleb Nicholls, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 3, 1817.*

# Chapter CLXXXIII

> An Act for the relief of Ephraim F. Gilbert.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, authorized, empowered, and directed to place the name of Ephraim F. Gilbert, late a captain in the army of the United States, on the roll of invalid pensioners of the United States, at and after the rate of fifteen dollars per month, commencing on the first of January, eighteen hundred and thirty-three, and to pay him from that time, at that rate, during his natural life.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

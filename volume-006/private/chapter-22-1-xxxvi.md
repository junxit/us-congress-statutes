# Chapter XXXVI

> An Act for the relief of Eber Hubbard.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Eber Hubbard, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, the sum of seven hundred dollars, the value of a boat captured by the enemy during the late war, on the return of said boat from Ogdensburg to Sackett’s Harbor, having been employed to transport a cargo of provisions and munitions of war from the latter to the former place, by the order of General Dodge.


*Approved, March 7, 1832.*

# Chapter CCXXXVIII

> An Act for the benefit of the widow of Noah Chittendon.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to the widow of Noah Chittendon, the sum of ninety-six dollars.


*Approved, July 7, 1838.*

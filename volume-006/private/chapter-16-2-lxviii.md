# Chapter LXVIII

> An Act for the relief [of] Benjamin Holland.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Benjamin Holland on the invalid pension roll of the United States, at the rate of eight dollars per month, to commence on the first of January, eighteen hundred and thirty-five, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, March 3, 1835.*

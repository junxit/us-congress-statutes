# Chapter CXXIII

> An Act for the relief of Joshua Cannon, Reuben Hickman, and Fielding Hickman.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper officers of the government be, and they are hereby, authorized and required to issue to Joshua Cannon, Reuben Hickman, and to Fielding Hickman, soldiers in the late war with Great Britain, patents for their bounty lands, of one hundred and sixty acres each, and also to pay to each of them whatever sum may be due for the balance of their pay or bounty.


*Approved, May 8, 1822.*

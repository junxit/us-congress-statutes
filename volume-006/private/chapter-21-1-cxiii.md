# Chapter CXIII

> An Act for the relief of Lucy M. Lipscomb.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the sum of one hundred dollars, out of any money in the treasury, not otherwise appropriated, be, and the same is hereby, appropriated, to the payment of that sum to Lucy M. Lipscomb, of Missouri, for so much money, erroneously deposited by her agent to the credit of the Treasurer of the United States; to be paid to the said Lucy, or to her order on demand.


*Approved, May 26, 1830.*

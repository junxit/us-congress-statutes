# Chapter LVIII

> An Act for the relief of the Louisville Savings Institution.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury revise the account of interest charged against said institution, under the act of June twenty-third, eighteen hundred and thirty-six, and deduct therefrom such amount as has been charged on public money deposited in said institution, while under actual orders to be transferred elsewhere.


*Approved, March 2, 1839.*

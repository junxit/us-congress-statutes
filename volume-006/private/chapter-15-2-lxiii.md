# Chapter LXIII

> An Act for the relief of Harold Smyth.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury department be, and they are hereby, authorized to audit and settle the accounts of Harold Smyth, late a captain by brevet in the army of the United States, and quartermaster at Fort McHenry, upon the principles of equity and justice.


*Approved, March 3, 1819.*

# Chapter CXL

> An Act for the relief of James McFarland.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to pay to James McFarland, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, an arrearage of pension at the rate now received by him; to commence the first of January, eighteen hundred and twenty-three, and to terminate at the time he was placed on the pension roll.


*Approved, June 28, 1838.*

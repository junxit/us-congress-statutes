# Chapter CXLIII

> An Act for the relief of Simeon Dennin.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War place the name of Simeon Dennin on the invalid pension roll, and that he pay him at the rate of four dollars per month during his natural life, commencing from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 17, 1844.*

# Chapter CCCVII

> An Act for the relief of the legal representatives of Joseph Hazlet, and Jemima Monro, surviving children of the late Colonel John Hazlet, of the state of Delaware, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers ascertain what will be the amount of seven years’ half pay due to the children of the said Colonel John Hazlet, deceased.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That said accounting officers do pay whatever sum maybe so found to be due to the legal representatives of Joseph Hazlet and of Jemima Monro, the two surviving children of the said Colonel John Hazlet, deceased, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, July 2, 1836.*

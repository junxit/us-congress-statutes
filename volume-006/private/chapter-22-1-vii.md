# Chapter VII

> An Act for the relief of Charles Cassedy.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Charles Cassedy, four hundred and ten dollars, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, for his services as Secretary and Clerk to a Commission appointed by the United States to ascertain the boundary lines of cession made by the Creek Indians to the United States.


*Approved, January 19, 1832.*

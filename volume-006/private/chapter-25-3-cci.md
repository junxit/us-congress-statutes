# Chapter CCI

> An Act for the relief of the representatives of Ann Levacher De Van Brun.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the Treasury be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to settle the account of Ann Levacher De Van Brun, and to allow her seven years’ half pay, to which she is entitled on account of the services of her husband, John Levacher De Van Brun, deceased, who was a lieutenant in the revolutionary war, in the Maryland continental line, and died before the close of the war; and that the sum found to be due be paid out of any motley in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to the representatives of Ann Levacher De Van Brun.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

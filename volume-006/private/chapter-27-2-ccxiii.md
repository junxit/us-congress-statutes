# Chapter CCXIII

> An Act for the relief of Phillis Patton.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War is hereby authorized and required to place Phillis Tatton, of Lebanon, in the county of New London, state of Connecticut, widow of Cuff Wells, on the roll of revolutionary pensioners of the United States, and allow to her a pension for two years’ military service of her said husband, to be paid to her agreeably to the act of the seventh of June [July], eighteen hundred and thirty-eight, allowing pensions to certain widows; to be paid as other pensions are paid.


*Approved, August 26, 1842.*

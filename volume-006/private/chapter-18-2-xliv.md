# Chapter XLIV

> An Act for the relief of John McLure.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to settle, upon the principles of equity, the accounts of John McLure, of Virginia, lately engaged as a contractor, in making a road from Cumberland, in Maryland, to the state of Ohio; and that the balance which may be found due to the said John McLure, be paid to him out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 3, 1825.*

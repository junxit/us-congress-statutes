# Chapter CLIII

> An Act for the relief of Seth Knowles.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the collector of the port of Boston and Charlestown be, and he is hereby, authorized to allow to Seth Knowles the debentures upon one hundred hogsheads of rum, shipped on board the Florida, for Hamburg, in the year one thousand eight hundred and nineteen, in the same manner as if the entries had been sworn to within the time prescribed bylaw: Provided, That the said collector shall be satisfied that all the other requirements of the law, to entitle the said Knowles to the debentures, have been complied with.


*Approved, May 26, 1828.*

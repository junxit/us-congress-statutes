# Chapter CXXXVII

> An Act for the relief of Mary Reynolds.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the sum of one thousand three hundred and twenty-three dollars and eighty-eight cents, be paid to Mary Reynolds, executrix of the last will and testament of John Reynolds, deceased, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, which sum appears to have been due to the said John Reynolds, in his lifetime, as a commissary in the clothing department for the state of Rhode Island, during the revolutionary war.


*Approved, May 24, 1828.*

# Chapter LXX

> An Act for the relief of the legal representatives of Balthazar Kramar, and the legal representatives of Captain Richard Taylor.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be authorized, and he is hereby directed, to pay to the legal representatives of Balthazar Kramar, at the rate of eight dollars per month, from the twenty-eighth of November, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-two, until the time of his decease, the same being due to him as an invalid pensioner of the United States.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the Secretary of War be authorized and directed to pay to the legal representatives of Captain Richard Taylor, an invalid pensioner, the amount of pension due the said Richard Taylor, at the time of his decease, in the same manner as if his biennial examination had taken place.


*Approved, March 2, 1827.*

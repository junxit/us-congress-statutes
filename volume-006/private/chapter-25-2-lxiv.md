# Chapter LXIV

> An Act for the relief of James Callan.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to James Callan out of any money not otherwise appropriated, for his services as draughtsman in the General Land Office for the month of May, eighteen hundred and thirty-four, such compensation as he received for the same services in the month of June following.


*Approved, April 20, 1838.*

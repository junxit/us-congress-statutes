# Chapter CXLII

> An Act for the relief of John Javins.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of John Javins, of the state of Pennsylvania, on the roll of invalid pensioners, and that he pay him at the rate of eight dollars per month during his natural life; to commence on the first day of December, eighteen hundred and forty-one.


*Approved, March 3, 1843.*

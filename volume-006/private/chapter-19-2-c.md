# Chapter C

> An Act for the relief of the legal representatives of Joseph Jeans, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury department pay unto the legal representatives of Joseph Jeans, deceased, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one hundred and seventy-five dollars, it being for two horses, taken by impressment into the public service, during the late war, and lost while in said service: Provided, however, That whatever sum may have been paid to said Jeans, for the use of the said horses, shall be deducted from the sum above-mentioned.


*Approved, March 3, 1827.*

# Chapter XCV

> An Act for the relief of William Blagrove.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That William Blagrove be, and he is hereby, released and discharged from the payment of a judgment recovered against him, at the suit of the United States, in the southern district of New York, in the month of December, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-two, amounting to four hundred and thirty-nine dollars and sixty-four cents; which release and discharge shall be in full for all claims in favor of the said William Blagrove, against the United States.


*Approved, May 18, 1824.*

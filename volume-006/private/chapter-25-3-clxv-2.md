# Chapter CLXV

> An Act granting a pension to John Clark.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of John Clark on the invalid pension roll, and that he be paid at the rate of six dollars per month, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated; to commence on the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-seven.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

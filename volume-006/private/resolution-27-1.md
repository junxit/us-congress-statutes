# Resolution 1

> Joint Resolution to authorize the settlement of the accounts of George Whitman.

*Be it resolved, &c.,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, directed to examine the accounts of George Whitman, late a contractor on the mail route from Mobile to New Orleans, and to make such allowances to the said George Whitman, as he would have been entitled to, on a full hearing of all the circumstances of his case, upon an original examination thereof.


*Approved, April 14, 1842.*

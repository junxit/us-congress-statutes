# Chapter CLXXII

> An Act for the relief of John Jorden.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to place the name of John Jorden, of Tompkins county, in the state of New York, on the invalid pension roll, and that he be paid at the rate of eight dollars per month during his natural life, commencing on the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and forty.


*Approved, August 16, 1842.*

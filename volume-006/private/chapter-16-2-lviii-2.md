# Chapter LVIII

> An Act granting a pension to Justus Cobb.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to place the name of Justus Cobb, of the county of Addison, in the state of Vermont, on the roll of invalid pensioners, and to pay him six dollars per month, during his natural life, commencing on the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-five.


*Approved, March 3, 1835.*

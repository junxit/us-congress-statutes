# Chapter LIX

> An Act for the relief of Charles Gwynn, of Baltimore.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, directed to pay to Charles Gwynn, of Baltimore, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of nine hundred and forty-four dollars sixteen cents and a half, being so much paid by him into the treasury of the United States, on a judgment obtained against him, as surety for the faithful administration of the estate of William Greetham, deceased, more than the assets of that estate had produced.


*Approved, May 5, 1824.*

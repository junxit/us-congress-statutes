# Chapter CLI

> An Act granting a pension to Samuel Cole.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War he directed to place the name of Samuel Cole, now of Bradford county, Pennsylvania, on the invalid pension roll, and that he be paid at the rate of eight dollars per month, commencing on the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-five.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

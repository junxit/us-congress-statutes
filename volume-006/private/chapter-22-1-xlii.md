# Chapter XLII

> An Act for the relief of Amariah Squirrel, administrator of Jacob Squirrel, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Third Auditor of the Treasury, adjust the amount due to Jacob Squirrel, late of the sixteenth regiment of infantry, and report the same to the Secretary of the Treasury, who is directed to pay the amount so reported to Amariah Squirrel, administrator of said Jacob Squirrel, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 15, 1832.*

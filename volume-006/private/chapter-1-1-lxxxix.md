# Chapter LXXXIX

> An Act allowing further time to the Trustees of Centre College to dispose of the land heretofore granted to the Deaf and Dumb Asylum, in the slate of Kentucky.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the further time of two years, from and after the fifth day of April, one thousand eight hundred and forty, be, and the same is hereby, allowed to the trustees of Centre College, at Danville, in the state oi Kentucky, to dispose of the lands heretofore granted to the Deaf and Dumb Asylum, in said state of Kentucky.


*Approved, July 20, 1840.*

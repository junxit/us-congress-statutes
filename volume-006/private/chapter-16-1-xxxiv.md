# Chapter XXXIV

> An Act for the relief of John Harding, Giles Harding, John Shute, and John Nichols.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they hereby are, authorized and directed to audit and settle the claim of John Harding, Giles Harding, John Shute, and John Nichols, for fifteen boats belonging to them, and detained, at Baton Rouge, by Major-General Thomas, in the year eighteen hundred and fifteen; and to allow them any sum not exceeding, in the whole, nine hundred dollars; which allowance, when made, shall be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, April 5, 1820.*

# Chapter XXVII

> An Act for the relief of Avery, Saltmarsh, and Company.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to pay, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Avery, Saltmarsh, and Company, the contractors for carrying the mail on the route from Columbus, Georgia, to Montgomery, Alabama, the sum of nine thousand seven hundred and seventy-nine dollars, it being for property employed by them in transportation of the mail captured and destroyed by the Creek Indians in Alay, eighteen hundred and thirty-six.


*Approved, March 3, 1841.*

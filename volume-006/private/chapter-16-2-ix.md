# Chapter IX

> An Act for the relief of Margaret Perry.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Department of War be, and he is hereby, directed to issue to Margaret Perry, a land warrant for the bounty land to which her son, William Valiant, a soldier, who died in the service of the United States, would have been entitled had he lived.


*Approved, January 17, 1821.*

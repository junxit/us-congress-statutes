# Chapter CVIII

> An Act for the relief of Reuben Ewing and others.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Agent of the Treasury be empowered and directed to cause that the judgment recovered in the state of Kentucky against Reuben Ewing and others, the security of William Whitsell, late Regimental Paymaster, be endorsed satisfied, on the payment of sixty dollars, the interest on this sum, and the costs of suit.


*Approved, March 3, 1825.*

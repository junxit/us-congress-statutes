# Chapter LXXXVII

> An Act for the relief of James Porter and Tunstall Quarles.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That James Porter and Tunstall Quarles, of the state of Kentucky, be, and they are hereby, released from a judgment obtained against them by the post office department, as the sureties of William J. Salee: Provided, That said sureties shall pay the legal costs which have accrued in the prosecution of said demand.


*Approved, March 3, 1825.*

# Chapter CXXVIII

> An Act for the relief of Amos Thompson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to place the name of Amos Thompson, of Mansfield, in the state of Connecticut, on the roll of revolutionary pensioners, and to pay him the sum of twenty dollars per annum, during his natural life, commencing on the fourth day of March, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-one.


*Approved, June 28, 1838.*

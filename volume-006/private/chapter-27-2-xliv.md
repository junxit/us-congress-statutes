# Chapter XLIV

> An Act for the relief of Francis G. McCauly.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Francis G. McCauly, a purser in the navy of the United States, the sum of seven hundred and forty-two dollars and nine cents, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated; that being the amount found to be his due, on settlement of his accounts with the treasury department, in July, A. D. one thousand eight hundred and thirty-two.


*Approved, June 22, 1842.*

# Chapter CCXCVI

> An Act for the relief of William Hoffman, a Canadian volunteer.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War cause to be issued to William Hoffman, of the county of Erie, in the state of New York, a warrant for one hundred and sixty acres of bounty land, to which he was entitled under the act of the fifth of March, one thousand eight hundred and sixteen, entitled “An act granting bounties in land and extra pay to certain Canadian volunteers,” in addition to one hundred and sixty acres heretofore received pursuant to said act; to be located in legal subdivisions, on any of the public lands of the United States in the Territory of Michigan, which have been offered at public sale, and are now subject to entry at private sale.


*Approved, July 14, 1832.*

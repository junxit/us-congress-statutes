# Chapter XLIII

> An Act for the relief of Elizabeth Mays.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That a pension of one hundred and fourteen dollars per annum, for the term of five years from the third day of September, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-nine, be allowed and paid, in half-yearly payments, out of the Navy Pension Fund, to Elizabeth Mays, the mother of Wilson Mays, late a Carpenter’s Mate in the Navy of the United States: Provided, however, That such pension shall be deemed to continue only during the life and widowhood of the said Elizabeth Mays.


*Approved, March 25, 1830.*

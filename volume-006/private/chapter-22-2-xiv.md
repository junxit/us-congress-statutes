# Chapter XIV

> An Act for the relief of William A. Tennille, of Georgia.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That William A. Tennille, of Georgia, be placed on the roll of invalid pensioners, and be paid at the rate of eight dollars per month, commencing on the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and fourteen.


*Approved, January 30, 1833.*

# Chapter LXXIX

> An Act for the relief of David Cooper.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to David Cooper, or his legal representative, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of eighty dollars, the value of an unfinished house pulled down and converted into United States’ barracks, in the month of December, one thousand eight hundred and fourteen.


*Approved, May 17, 1824.*

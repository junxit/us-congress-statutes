# Chapter CCVII

> An Act for the relief of the legal representatives of Doctor William Johonnot, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby directed and required to allow the legal representatives of Doctor William Johonnot the five years’ full pay of a hospital surgeon, being the commutation of half pay for life of said Johonnot, as assistant apothecary of the hospital department of the revolutionary army; and that the same be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, July 7, 1838.*

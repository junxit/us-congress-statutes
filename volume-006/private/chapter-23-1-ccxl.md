# Chapter CCXL

> An Act for the relief of the representatives of Hugh McGennis, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to John B. Orton, administrator of Hugh McGennis, deceased, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of six hundred and eighty-five dollars, forty-eight cents, for extra work done, and materials furnished by the said McGennis on the Cumberland road west of the town of Zanesville, in the state of Ohio.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

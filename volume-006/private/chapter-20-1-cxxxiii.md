# Chapter CXXXIII

> An Act for the relief of Allan B. M’Alhany.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be authorized, and he is hereby required, to cause to be issued, in favor of Allan B. M’Alhany, who served as a private in the seventh regiment United States infantry, during the late war, a military bounty land warrant.


*Approved, May 24, 1828.*

# Chapter XXIII

> An Act for the relief of Joseph Russ and Stephen J. Roach.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Quartermaster-General settle the account of Joseph Russ and Stephen J. Roach, for labor bestowed and money expended in repairing or constructing a road leading from Pensacola to Tallahassee, in Florida, under a contract made on the twenty-sixth of November, eighteen hundred and thirty-two, of which they are the holders, and to allow them therefor, such sum, so far as the labor be-stowed and the money expended were advantageously applied, taking as the data for making the allowance, the price to have been paid for making or repairing the entire road, on completing it according to the contract.


*Approved, February 17, 1836.*

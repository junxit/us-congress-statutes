# Chapter CXXXV

> An Act for the relief of John Randolph Clay, late secretary of legation of the United States at the court of Vienna.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to cause to be paid to John Randolph Clay, late secretary of legation of the United States at the Court of Austria, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of three thousand seven hundred and fifty dollars, in full of all demands for services of a diplomatic character rendered by the said John Randolph Clay in the interval between the eighteenth of September, eighteen hundred and forty, the date of the recall of Mr. Muhlenburg, late minister plenipotentiary to Austria, and the recognition of his successor (Mr. Jenifer) on the eighteenth of March, eighteen hundred and forty-two.


*Approved, March 3, 1843.*

# Chapter CCIII

> An Act the relief of Job Daniels.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place Job Daniels, of the state of New Jersey, upon the roll of revolutionary pensioners of the United States, at the rate of forty-five dollars per annum, commencing on the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-one, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

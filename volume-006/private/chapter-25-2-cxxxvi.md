# Chapter CXXXVI

> An Act for the relief of Daniel Rardon.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to pay to Daniel Rardon, pensioner, an increase of pension, at the rate of two dollars per month, commencing on the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-seven, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 28, 1838.*

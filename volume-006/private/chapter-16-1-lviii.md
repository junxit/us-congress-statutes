# Chapter LVIII

> An Act for the relief of Fielding Jones.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to Fielding Jones, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, eighty dollars, in full compensation for a horse impressed into the public service in the late war with Great Britain.


*Approved, May 2, 1820.*

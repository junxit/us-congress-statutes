# Chapter XXXIX

> An Act granting pensions to James Barker and Zebulon Pike.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place James Barker on the list of revolutionary pensioners of the United States, at the rate of eight dollars per month, commencing on the fourth day of December, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-four; and that said Barker shall be entitled to receive said pension in the manner provided by law.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place Colonel Zebulon Pike upon the list of revolutionary pensioners of the United States, and cause to be paid to him at the rate of twenty dollars per month, commencing on the day the said Zebulon Pike was stricken from the pension roll, up to the sixth of November, eighteen hundred and twenty-four, from which time his pension shall continue.


*Approved, March 3, 1825.*

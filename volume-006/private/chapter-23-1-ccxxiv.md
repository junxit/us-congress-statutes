# Chapter CCXXIV

> An Act for the relief of James Fife, a Creek Indian.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to cause to be ascertained the fair value of one horse, stolen from James Fife, a Creek Indian, by white men, in the spring of the year one thousand eight hundred and thirty-three; and that the said value, when so ascertained, shall be paid to the said James Fife, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

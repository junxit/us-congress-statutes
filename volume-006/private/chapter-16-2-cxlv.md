# Chapter CXLV

> An Act for the relief of Peter Dimick, of the state of New York.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War, be directed to place the name of Peter Dimick, of the state of New York, on the roll of revolutionary pensioners, and to pay him twenty dollars per year, during his natural life, commencing on the fourth day of March, in the year one thousand eight hundred and thirty-one.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

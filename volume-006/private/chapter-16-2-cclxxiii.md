# Chapter CCLXXIII

> An Act for the relief of George Dawson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to settle upon principles of equity the accounts of George Dawson, late a contractor for mason work on the Cumberland road, and that the balance found due to him be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, July 2, 1836.*

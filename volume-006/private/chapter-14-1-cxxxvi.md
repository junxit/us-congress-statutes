# Chapter CXXXVI

> An Act for the relief of Menassah Miner and Isaac Denison.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the Treasury Department be, and they are hereby authorized and directed, to pay to Menassah Miner the sum of three hundred and three dollars and seventy-four cents, and to Isaac Denison the sum of two hundred and fifty-five dollars and ninety-two cents, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, for services rendered and expenses incurred by said Menassah in providing for his son John Miner, and for like services and expenditures rendered and incurred by Isaac Denison, in providing for his son Frederick Denison, while they were diseased by wounds received by them in the service of the United States, in defence of Stonington Point, in the year one thousand eight hundred and fourteen.


*Approved, April 29, 1816.*

# Chapter CXIII

> An Act for the relief of the legal representatives of Patrick McGibbony.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury shall adjust and settle the account of Patrick McGibbony, deceased, late of the state of North Carolina, allowing him five years’ full pay as a lieutenant in the continental service during the revolutionary war; and pay the amount thereof to his legal representatives, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, in full satisfaction of his half pay for life.


*Approved, June 13, 1838.*

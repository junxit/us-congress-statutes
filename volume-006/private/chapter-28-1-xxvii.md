# Chapter XXVII

> An Act for the relief of Joseph Bonnell.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Joseph Bonnell on the pension roll; and that he be paid, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, a pension, for eighteen months’ service as a private soldier in the revolutionary war, under the act of Congress of the seventh June, eighteen hundred and thirty-two.


*Approved, May 23, 1844.*

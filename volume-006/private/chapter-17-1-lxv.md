# Chapter LXV

> An Act for the relief of William Henderson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officer of the treasury department cause to be paid to William Henderson, or to his legal representatives, the sum of two thousand seven hundred and sixty-five dollars, for the value of his property destroyed by the enemy during the late war, at Monday’s Point, in Virginia; and that the same be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, May 7, 1822.*

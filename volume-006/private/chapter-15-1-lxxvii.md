# Chapter LXXVII

> An Act for the relief of Mary Sullivan.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury department be, and they are hereby authorized and directed to liquidate and settle the claim of Mary Sullivan, founded upon a certificate dated the tenth of March, one thousand seven hundred and eighty-one, for ten thousand weight of tobacco, or money equivalent, signed by Richard Young, A. D. Q. M., according to the principles of equity and justice, and to allow to her such sum of money as may appear to be justly due; and the same shall be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, April 18, 1818.*

# Chapter LVI

> An Act for the relief of the legal representatives of Firman Le Sieur.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the legal representatives of Firman Le Sieur, late of the state of Missouri, be, and they are hereby, authorized to enter one section of land, in any of the land offices in the state of Missouri, the sale of which is authorized by law, in full satisfaction for a like quantity, patented to them by the United States, on the third day of July, one thousand eight hundred and twenty: Provided, said representatives of Firman Le Sieur shall first release to the United States all claim by virtue of the said patent, and surrender the same to the Commissioner of the General Land Office; and shall confine such entry to lands which have been heretofore offered for public sale, and conformably to the lines of the public surveys.


*Approved, May 5, 1824.*

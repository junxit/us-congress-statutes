# Chapter CCI

> An Act for the relief of William Price.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, required to settle the account of William Price, and to allow him five years’ full pay as a Lieutenant incite revolutionary war, which five years’ full pay is the commutation of his half pay for life; to be paid out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, May 29, 1830.*

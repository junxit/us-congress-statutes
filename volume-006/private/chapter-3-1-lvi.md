# Chapter LVI

> An Act to make provision for the window and orphan children of Robert Forsyth.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the sum of two thousand dollars be allowed to the widow of Robert Forsyth, late marshal of the district of Georgia, for the use of herself and the children of the said Robert Forsyth, to be paid in equal proportions, out of any moneys in the treasury of the United States not heretofore appropriated.


*Approved, June 7, 1794.*

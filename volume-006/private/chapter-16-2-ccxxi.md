# Chapter CCXXI

> An Act for the relief of Henry Maggart.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to place the name of Henry Maggart, of Sullivan county, Tennessee, upon the list of invalid pensions, and to pay him eight dollars per month, from the first of January, in the year one thousand eight hundred and thirty-three, during his natural life, out of any moneys in the treasury, not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

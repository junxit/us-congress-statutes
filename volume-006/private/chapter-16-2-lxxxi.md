# Chapter LXXXI

> An Act granting pensions to William Baden and James Harrington.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and is hereby, directed to place on the invalid pension roll of the United States the names of the following persons, whereupon they and each of them shall be entitled to receive the pensions severally set against their names respectively, during life, that is to say:

William Baden, at the rate of eight dollars per month, commencing on the first day of April, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-four.

James Harrington, at the rate of eight dollars per month, commencing on the first day of April one thousand eight hundred and thirty-four.


*Approved, March 3, 1835.*

# Chapter XIII

> An Act for the relief of the legal representatives of Edward Moore, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Navy pay to the legal representatives of Edward Moore, deceased, the sum of one hundred and twenty dollars and forty-two cents, reported on the books of the Fourth Auditor of the Treasury to the said Edward Moore, for his share of the prize money for the British vessels captured on Lake Erie, during the late war; to be paid out of any money hot otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, January 27, 1831.*

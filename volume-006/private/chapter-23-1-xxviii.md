# Chapter XXVIII

> An Act for the relief of Samuel Thompson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Samuel Thompson one hundred and fifty-eight dollars and sixty-five cents, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, for that value of blankets furnished to different detachments of militia, after General Hall’s surrender, in the service of the United States.


*Approved, April 15, 1834.*

# Chapter LXXXIII

> An Act refund the moiety of theforfeiture upon the schooner Volant.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be repaid to John Burton, Dunbar Henderson, E. Hale, Charles Holmenhouser, Benjamin Burton, and Thomas Burton, the amount paid by them into the Treasury of the United States, as a moiety of the forfeiture decreed against the schooner Volant; and that the same be paid out of any money in the treasury, not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, April 24, 1830.*

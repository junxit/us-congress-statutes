# Chapter LXIV

> An Act for the relief of Joshua Foltz.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, required to settle and adjust the account of Joshua Foltz, and to allow him for ten months’ services, as a wagoner in the revolutionary war; to be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 3, 1829.*

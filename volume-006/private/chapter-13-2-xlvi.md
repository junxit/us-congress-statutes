# Chapter XLVI

> An Act for the relief of William H. Savage.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury department be, and they are hereby authorized and required to settle and adjust the claim of William H. Savage, late agent of American seamen and commerce at Jamaica, and to allow him such compensation for office rent as may appear reasonable, for the period he may have acted as the agent of the United States.


*Approved, April 9, 1814.*

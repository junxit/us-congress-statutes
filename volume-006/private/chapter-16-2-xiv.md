# Chapter XIV

> An Act for the relief of Samuel Bragdon, David Chase, and the crew of the schooner Halcyon.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Collector of the Customs in Newburyport, state of Massachusetts, is hereby authorized to pay to Samuel Bragdon, David Chase, and the officers and crew of the schooner Halcyon, which was lost at sea, to be distributed according to law, the same sum said vessel would have been entitled to receive as a bounty or drawback if she had safely arrived in port; having complied with all the requirements of law necessary to receive such bounty or drawback for having been engaged four months or more in the cod-fishery.


*Approved, January 27, 1835.*

# Chapter CCXVI

> An Act for the relief of Balaam Bowers, of Virginia.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, required to place upon the roll of revolutionary pensioners, Balaam Bowers, of Virginia, at the rate of sixty dollars per annum, the said pension to commence and take effect from the fourth of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-one.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

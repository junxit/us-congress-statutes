# Chapter LVII

> An Act in favor of Ellen Turney.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War place the name of Ellen Turney, of Fairfield, Connecticut, on the roll of revolutionary pensions, and pay her a pension of at the rate of four hundred and eighty dollars a year, for and during the term of five years from the fourth day of Alarch, eighteen hundred and thirty-six.


*Approved, July 9, 1842.*

# Chapter LIII

> An Act for the relief of Findley Kellock.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to place Findley Kellock, of the state of Maine, on the roll of revolutionary pensioners, and to cause to be paid to the said Kellock, during his natural life, the sum of twenty-nine dollars per annum, the payment thereof to commence on the fourth day of March, anno Domini, eighteen hundred and thirty-one.”


*Approved, March 3, 1837.*

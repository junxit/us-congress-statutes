# Chapter CLXIII

> An Act for the relief of Abraham Forbes, a spy in the late war.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Abraham Forbes be, and is hereby, allowed to enter three hundred and twenty acres of land, on any of the public lands subject to entry at private sale, for services rendered by him as a Canadian volunteer.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

# Chapter CXVII

> An Act for the relief of George B. Dameron and William Howze, of Mississippi.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to make a reasonable allowance to the Register and Receiver of the land office at Jackson Court House, Mississippi, for extra services performed by them under the third section of the act of the third of March, eighteen hundred and twenty-seven.


*Approved, March 3, 1831.*

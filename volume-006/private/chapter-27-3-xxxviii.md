# Chapter XXXVIII

> An Act for the relief of Asahel Brainard.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, required to pay, out of any moneys of the treasury not otherwise appropriated, a pension, at the rate of twenty dollars a month, to Asahel Brainard, in lieu of the pension now received by him, commencing from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, February 24, 1843.*

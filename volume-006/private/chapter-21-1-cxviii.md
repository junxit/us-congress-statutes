# Chapter CXVIII

> An Act for the relief of Henry Williams.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Henry Williams, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one hundred and twenty dollars, the value of two horses which died in the service of the United States, in the year eighteen hundred and thirteen, for the want of forage, in the campaign against the Creek Indians, the property of said Williams.


*Approved, May 28, 1830.*

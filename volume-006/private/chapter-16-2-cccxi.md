# Chapter CCCXI

> An Act for the relief of Seaborn Jones and Joel Crawford.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That, at the next term of the district court of the United States for the district of Georgia, Seaborn Jones and Joel Crawford be permitted to open a judgment lately obtained against them in said court as the securities of Thomas F. Green, late postmaster at Milledgeville, Georgia, at the suit of the Postmaster-General of the United States. And that the said Seaborn Jones and Joel Crawford be permitted to make the same defence to said suit that they might or could have made had said judgment not have been rendered.


*Approved, July 2, 1836.*

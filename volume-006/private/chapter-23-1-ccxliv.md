# Chapter CCXLIV

> An Act for the relief of the heirs of Thomas Wallace, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, required to settle the account of Thomas Wallace, deceased, and allow to his heirs five years’ full pay, as a lieutenant in the eighth Virginia regiment on continental establishment; which five years’ full pay is the commutation of his half pay for life; to be paid to the said heirs of Thomas Wallace, deceased, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

# Chapter CCXX

> An Act for the relief of Joseph Prescott.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, required to adjust and settle the account of Doctor Joseph Prescott, late a surgeon of the army of the United States on continental establishment, and allow him five years’ full pay of a surgeon of the army, in commutation of the half pay for life promised by the resolutions of Congress; and that the same be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, July 7, 1838.*

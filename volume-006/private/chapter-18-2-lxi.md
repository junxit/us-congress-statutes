# Chapter LXI

> An Act for the relief of Thomas R. Broome.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Thomas R. Broome be, and he hereby is, released from the payment of the sum of two hundred and sixty-two dollars, being part of a judgment obtained against him by the United States, in the Circuit Court of the United States, for the fourth circuit in and for the District of Maryland, at the December term thereof, in the year one thousand eight hundred and twenty-four.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the proper accounting officers of the treasury department be, and they are hereby, authorized to settle the account for forage of the said Thomas R. Broome, on principles of equity, by allowing him a credit on the judgment obtained against him in the District Court of the United States, for the sum actually paid by him for forage.


*Approved, March 3, 1825.*

# Chapter IX

> An Act for the relief of John Castille of the city of New Orleans.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby authorized and required to discharge from his imprisonment John Castille, at present confined in the prison at Orleans, in consequence of a judgment obtained against him at the suit of the United States: Provided however, That nothing contained in this act shall exonerate any property (which the said John Castille now has or may hereafter acquire) from the judgment obtained against him by the United States.


*Approved, December 1, 1814.*

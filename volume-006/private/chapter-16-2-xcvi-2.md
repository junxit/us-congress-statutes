# Chapter XCVI

> An Act for the relief of Samuel W. Ruff.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, authorized to audit and settle the account of Samuel W. Ruff, a surgeon in the navy of the United States, and to allow him the pay and emoluments of a surgeon, in lieu of the pay and emoluments of an assistant surgeon, from the fourth day of April, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-one, up to the time of his appointment as surgeon in the year one thousand eight hundred and thirty-four; and that the amount due to the said Samuel W. Ruff, upon such settlement and allowance, be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 14, 1836.*

# Chapter CCXXII

> An Act granting a pension to Johan Jost Dietz, of New York.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to place the name of Johan Jost Dietz, of Albany county, New York, on the pension roll, and pay him forty dollars per annum, for and during his natural life-time, the same to commence and take effect at and from the time his name was dropped from the pension roll.


*Approved, July 7, 1838.*

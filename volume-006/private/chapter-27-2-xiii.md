# Chapter XIII

> An Act for the relief of Margaret Barnes, widow of Elijah Barnes.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War cause the name of Margaret Barnes, widow of Elijah Barnes, a soldier of the revolution, to be entered on the pension roll, in conformity to the act of Congress of July fourth, eighteen hundred and thirty-six, and pay her at the rate of twenty dollars per annum, commencing on the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-one, as is required in other cases by the provisions of the aforesaid act.


*Approved, March 19, 1842.*

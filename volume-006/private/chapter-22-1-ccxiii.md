# Chapter CCXIII

> An Act for the relief of Walter Cockburn.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the President of the United States be, and he is hereby, authorized to issue patents to Walter Cockburn, for lots numbered twenty-nine, thirty-two, sixty-seven, and thirty-three, as designated in the plan of a town laid off at York Bluff, in the state of Alabama, and purchased by said Cockburn at the public sale of said four lots.


*Approved, July 13, 1832.*

# Chapter XV

> An Act for the relief of Peter Lionberger.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he hereby is, authorized to pay to Peter Lionberger, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one hundred dollars, for the value of a horse of the said Peter Lionberger, which died while in the service of the United States in eighteen hundred and thirteen, for want of sufficient forage.


*Approved, January 20, 1843.*

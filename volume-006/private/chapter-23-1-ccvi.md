# Chapter CCVI

> An Act for the relief of Thomas Minor.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Thomas Minor, five years’ full pay for the services of the said Minor as a captain in the war of the revolution.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

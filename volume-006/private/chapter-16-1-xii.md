# Chapter XII

> An Act for the relief of the heirs of Anthony Burk.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the heirs of Anthony Burk be authorized to enter, within twelve months from the passage of this act, with the Register of the Land Office at Chillicothe, without payment, two quarter sections, within the boundaries of the district of Chillicothe.


*Approved, February 18, 1820.*

# Chapter CXV

> An Act for the relief of Richard S. Hackley.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to Richard S. Hackley, or to his agent or attorney, duly authorized, out of any money in the treasury, not otherwise appropriated, the sum of six thousand two hundred and thirteen dollars and seventy-four cents; being the amount of expenses incurred and paid by him, in and about the detention of the ship Vigilant, seized by him at Cadiz, in the year one thousand eight hundred and nine, under the orders of George W. Erwing, Esquire, then Minister of the United States in Spain.


*Approved, May 15, 1820.*

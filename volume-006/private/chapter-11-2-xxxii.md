# Chapter XXXII

> An Act concerning invalid pensioners.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

## § 3.

And be it further enacted, That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby directed to place Andrew Pinkerton on the pension list of invalid pensioners of the United States, and to pay him at the rate of three dollars and thirty-fthree] cents and one third of a cent per month, commencing on the twenty-fifth of August, eighteen hundred and nine.

## § 4.

And be it further enacted, That the several sums specifically appropriated by this act, shall be paid out of any moneys in the treasury, not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, April 27, 1810.*

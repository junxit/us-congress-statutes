# Chapter CLXXXIV

> An Act for the relief of Godfrey Vought.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, directed to place Godfrey Vought, of the county of Bradford, in the state of Pennsylvania, on the pension roll of the United States, and that there be allowed to the said Godfrey Vought, the sum of seventy dollars per annum, during his natural life, to commence on the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-one.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

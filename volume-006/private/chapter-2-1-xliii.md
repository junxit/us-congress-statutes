# Chapter XLIII

> An Act to compensate the services of the late Colonel George Gibson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be allowed and paid to the executors or administrators of the late Colonel George Gibson, or to their attorney or attorneys legally appointed and authorized to receive the same for the use and benefit of his heirs, the sum of one thousand dollars for extraordinary services by him rendered to the United States during the year one thousand seven hundred and seventy-six.


*Approved, May 8, 1792.*

# Chapter III

> An Act for the relief of George Chinn.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury make such further allowance to George Chinn, for his rations as lieutenant on board of the revenue cutter Search, from the first day of July, one thousand eight hundred and eighteen, to the twelfth day of March, one thousand eight hundred and nineteen, as will, with what has heretofore been paid to him, make an amount equal to what other lieutenants were entitled to, who were duly commissioned; and that the amount so found his due, be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, January 24, 1834.*

# Chapter CLXXI

> An Act for the relief of Isaiah Townsend, Peter Dox, and Gerrit Le Grange, sureties of Gerrit L. Dox.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Postmaster-General be, and he is hereby, authorized to allow the sum of one thousand five hundred and seventy-eight dollars and fifty cents, together with any interest which may have been charged upon that sum, from the first day of April, one thousand eight hundred and sixteen, as a credit upon any judgment which may have been recovered against Isaiah Townsend, Peter Dox, and Gerrit Le Grange, as the sureties of Gerrit L. Dox, late a Postmaster at Albany, in the state of New York, that amount of postage having been paid by him, and for which no credit has ever been given.


*Approved, May 29, 1830.*

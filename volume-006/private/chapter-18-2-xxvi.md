# Chapter XXVI

> An Act for the relief of Peter Yandez.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury department be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Peter Yandez, one hundred and twenty dollars, the value of a horse lost by said Yandez while engaged in the service of the United States, by towing a boat to Ogdensburg during the late war; and that the same be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 3, 1825.*

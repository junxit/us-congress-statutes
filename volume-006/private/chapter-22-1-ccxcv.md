# Chapter CCXCV

> An Act for the relief of Alexander Oswald Brodie, of New York.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Collector for the port of New York, is hereby authorized and required to deduct from a bond given by Alexander Oswald Brodie, for duties on books imported in the month of January, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-six, in the brig Nancy, from Hamburg, such amount of duty as may have been levied on any Greek and Latin books and Polyglott Bibles, over and above four cents on each volume: Provided, That the said Brodie shall satisfy the collector aforesaid, that all the books were printed before the year one thousand seven hundred and seventy-five.


*Approved, July 14, 1832.*

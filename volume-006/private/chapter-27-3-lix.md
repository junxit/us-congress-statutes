# Chapter LIX

> An Act for the relief of John Core.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, directed to pay to John Core the sum of eighty dollars, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, for a horse which was killed while in the public service.


*Approved, March 1, 1843.*

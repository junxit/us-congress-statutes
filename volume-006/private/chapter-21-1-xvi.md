# Chapter XVI

> An Act for the relief of Nancy Dolan.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to Nancy Dolan, of Louisiana, out of any money in the treasury, not otherwise appropriated, the sum of twelve hundred dollars, which shall be in full satisfaction of the claim of said Nancy, for remuneration from the United States for the loss of a lot of ground at Baton Rouge, in said state.


*Approved, February 11, 1830.*

# Chapter CVIII

> An Act for the relief of Elijah Lincoln.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to issue to Elijah Lincoln a military land warrant for one hundred acres of land, for revolutionary services, in lieu of a warrant number one thousand and ninety-two, alleged to have been issued to Elijah Lincoln in the year one thousand eight hundred, and to have been lost.


*Approved, June 28, 1834.*

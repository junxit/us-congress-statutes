# Chapter CCLXXII

> An Act for the relief of the legal representatives of Captain James Craine.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, directed, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated by law, to pay to Edwin C. Brown and Maria Brown, the latter being the only child and heir of Captain James Craine, deceased, the amount of five years’ full pay of a captain of infantry in the revolutionary army of the United States.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

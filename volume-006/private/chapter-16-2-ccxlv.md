# Chapter CCXLV

> An Act for the relief of Scioto Evans.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, directed to pay to Scioto Evans, of Indiana, the sum of fifty-five dollars, for a horse, saddle, bridle, and blanket, furnished Robert Robertson, for the use of the United States, in an expedition against the hostile Indians in eighteen hundred and thirty-two: Provided, The said Evans shall file his bond, with sufficient security, to indemnify the United States against the claim of any other person or persons to the said property.


*Approved, July 1, 1836.*

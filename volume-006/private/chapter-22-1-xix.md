# Chapter XIX

> An Act for the relief of James Lucius Sawyer.

*Be it enacted &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to James Lucius Sawyer, thirty dollars for his services as Recorder or Judge Advocate on the trial of Doctor Pendergrast, before a court-martial ordered by General Wilkinson, during the late war.


*Approved, February 18, 1832.*

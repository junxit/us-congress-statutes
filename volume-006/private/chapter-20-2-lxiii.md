# Chapter LXIII

> An Act for the relief of William R. Maddox.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers pay unto William R. Maddox, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of three hundred and four dollars and fifty-three cents, being in full for the balance of the price of sixty-seven thousand eight hundred and eighty-three bricks, delivered at the Capitol for the use of the United States, in the year one thousand eight hundred and twenty.


*Approved, March 3, 1829.*

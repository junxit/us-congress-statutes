# Chapter XVII

> An Act for the relief of Thomas B. Parish.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury cause to be paid unto Thomas B. Farish, the sum of one hundred and forty-nine dollars and sixty cents, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, February 4, 1819.*

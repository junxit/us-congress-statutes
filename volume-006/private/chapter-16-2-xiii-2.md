# Chapter XIII

> An Act for the relief of the heirs of Thomas F. Townsley.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay, to the heirs of Thomas F. Townsley, seven hundred and ninety-nine dollars eighty-one cents, for damages paid by the said Thomas F. Townsley, on drafts drawn by Lieutenant Graham, in favor of said Townsley, on George Gibson, Commissary General of Subsistence, which were pro-tested, and afterwards paid; said drafts were for supplies furnished at New Orleans, and necessary for the troops of the United States.


*Approved, February 17, 1836.*

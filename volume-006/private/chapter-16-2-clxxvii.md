# Chapter CLXXVII

> An Act granting a pension to William Lawrence, late a colonel in the army.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, authorized and required to pay to William Lawrence, late a colonel in the army, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, a pension at the rate of thirty dollars a month, to commence at the date of his resignation and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

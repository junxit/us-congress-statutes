# Chapter CLXXXVI

> An Act for the relief of John Hayner.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to John Hayner, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one hundred dollars, the value of a horse he lost at the attack on Baltimore during the late war, by reason of being dismounted and separated from him, the said John Hayner serving as a mounted riflleman in cavalry, in a company commanded by H. St. G. Tucker.


*Approved, May 29, 1830.*

# Chapter LIII

> An Act for the relief of William Townsend.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to pay to William Townsend, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of nine hundred twenty-six dollars and fourteen cents, in full, for an anchor, taken from the navy-yard, in New York, during the late war, and applied to the use of the government.


*Approved, March 3, 1825.*

# Chapter CL

> An Act for the relief of Asa Davis.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Asa Davis on the invalid pension roll; and that he pay to him at the rate of six dollars per month, from and after the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and forty-two, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 17, 1844.*

# Chapter LXVI

> An Act for the relief f Mary Williams, widow of Jacob Williams, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Mary Williams, of East Hartford, in the state of Connecticut, widow of Jacob Williams, a revolutionary soldier, deceased, be placed on the pension roll, under the act of July fourth, eighteen hundred and thirty-six, and that she be paid such annual pension while she remains a widow as her said husband would have been entitled to had he been living on the seventh of June, eighteen hundred and thirty-two.


*Approved, March 1, 1843.*

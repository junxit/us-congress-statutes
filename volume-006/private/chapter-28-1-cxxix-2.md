# Chapter CXXIX

> An Act for the relief of Daniel W. Church.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, required to place the name of Daniel W. Church on the invalid pension roll; and there shall be paid out of the treasury of the United States, to the said Daniel W. Church, a pension at the rate of twelve dollars and seventy-five cents each month, from and after the fourth day of March, one thousand eight hundred and forty-four, in the manner, and at the times, other invalid pensions are payable.


*Approved, June 17, 1844.*

# Chapter II

> An Act for the relief of Samuel Clarkson and Alexander Elmslie.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That there be refunded and paid to Samuel Clarkson and Alexander Elmslie, severally, the following sums of money: to Samuel Clarkson, four hundred and fifteen dollars and thirty-five cents, and to Alexander Elmslie, four hundred and eighty-six dollars and thirty-three cents; which said several sums have been paid by the persons abovementioned to the collector of Philadelphia, on the importation into the United States of sundry copper bottoms, and bolts, or bars, the same not being by law subject to the payment of duties.


*Approved, January 11, 1822.*

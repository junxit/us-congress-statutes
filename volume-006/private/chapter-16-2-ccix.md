# Chapter CCIX

> An Act for the relief of George Fields.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the pension granted by an act, approved March second, eighteen hundred and thirty-three, to George Fields, be, and the same is hereby, raised to the sum of ten dollars per month, the said Fields having been a captain in the service of the United States; and the Secretary of War is hereby authorized to pay said Fields the arrears of his pension, computed at that rate from the ninth day of November, one thousand eight hundred and thirteen, the day on which he was wounded, deducting from the sum now due to him, the amount he has already received; said sum to be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

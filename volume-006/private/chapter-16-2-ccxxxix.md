# Chapter CCXXXIX

> An Act for the relief of Andrew Hoover,of Indiana.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper officer of the treasury pay to Andrew Hoover, of Marion county, Indiana, ninety dollars in full, for a horse lost in the service of the United States, in June, eighteen hundred and thirty-two, which horse was lost while in the use of John Steale as a mounted volunteer: Provided, The said Hoover shall file his bond with sufficient security to indemnify the United States against the claim of any other person or persons for said property.


*Approved, July 1, 1836.*

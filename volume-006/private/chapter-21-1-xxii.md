# Chapter XXII

> An Act for the relief of John Long.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury department, cause to be paid to John Long, of the state of Kentucky, or to his legal representative, the sum of one hundred and ten dollars, out of any money in the treasury, not otherwise appropriated, in full, for a horse lost by him, while in the service of the United States, during the late war with Great Britain.


*Approved, February 11, 1830.*

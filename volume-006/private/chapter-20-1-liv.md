# Chapter LIV

> An Act for the relief of Willoughby Barton.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to adjust and settle the contract of Willoughby Barton with the Commissioners of the Navy for live oak, entered into on the eighth day of June, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-five, upon the same terms on which a like contract, entered into between Robert Newell and the Commissioners of the Navy, on the twenty-second day of September, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-five, was settled.


*Approved, May 15, 1828.*

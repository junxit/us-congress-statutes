# Chapter XVI

> An Act for the relief of Erastus Fairbanks and Thaddeus Fairbanks.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to Erastus Fairbanks and Thaddeus Fairbanks, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one hundred and five dollars, being the amount of an over-payment made by them into the treasury, for the reissue at the patent office of seven patents.


*Approved, February 6, 1839.*

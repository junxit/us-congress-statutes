# Chapter CLVI

> An Act for the relief of Francis F. Saint Cyr.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, required to place the name of Francis F. Saint Cyr, of the county of Saint Charles, in the State of Missouri, upon the roll of invalid pensioners, and to pay him at the rate of eight dollars a month, during his natural life, commencing on the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-four.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

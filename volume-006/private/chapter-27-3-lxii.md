# Chapter LXII

> An Act for the relief of Thomas D. Gilson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to pay, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, unto Thomas D. Gilson, late sheriff of Clinton county, New York, the sum of one hundred and fifty-one dollars and eighty-five cents, in full for his official services in assisting to preserve our neutral relations on the Champlain frontier.


*Approved, March 1, 1843.*

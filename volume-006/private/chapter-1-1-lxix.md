# Chapter LXIX

> An Act for the relief of Jared Winslow.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place upon the roll of invalid pensioners, the name of Jared Winslow, of China, in the county of Genesee and state of New York, and pay him at the rate of eight dollars a month; commencing on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and thirty-eight; to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, July 20, 1840.*

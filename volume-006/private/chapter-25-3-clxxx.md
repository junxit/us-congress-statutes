# Chapter CLXXX

> An Act for the relief of Ashbel Mason.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War is hereby authorized and required to cause to be issued to Ashbel Mason, or his legal representatives, a bounty land warrant for one hundred acres of land, to which he is entitled for his services during the revolutionary war.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

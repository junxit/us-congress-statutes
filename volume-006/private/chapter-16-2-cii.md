# Chapter CII

> An Act for the relief of Theodore Owens.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, required to pay to Theodore Owens, of Key West, Florida, the sum of three hundred dollars, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, for additional compensation for taking the census in the southern district of Florida.


*Approved, March 3, 1835.*

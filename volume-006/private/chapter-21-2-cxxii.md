# Chapter CXXII

> An Act for the relief of Joseph S. Cannon.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized and required toplace the name of Joseph S. Cannon on the Navy Pension List, at the rate of ten dollars per month, payable from the first day of January, eighteen hundred and twenty-nine.


*Approved, March 3, 1831.*

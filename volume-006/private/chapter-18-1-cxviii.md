# Chapter CXVIII

> An Act for the relief of Mary H. Hawkins.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be allowed to Mary H. Hawkins a credit, without interest, for five years from the first day of June next, upon the balance due from her, on account of the purchase money of the real estate of William Hawkins, deceased, sold at the suit of the United States, and purchased by the said Mary.


*Approved, May 19, 1824.*

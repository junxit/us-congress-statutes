# Chapter CCXXXIX

> An Act for the relief of Enoch Hidden.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be authorized and directed to pay to Enoch Hidden the sum of two hundred dollars out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, in renumeration for the use, heretofore and in the future, of his improvement in the elevating screw-box and cap for carronades.


*Approved, August 29, 1842.*

# Chapter LXXXVII

> An Act granting a section of land for the use of schools in St. Clair county, state of Illinois.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That one section of the public lands subject to private entry and sale in the state of Illinois, be located for the use and benefit of schools for the inhabitants of township one north, range ten west of the third principal meridian, in said state, in lieu of the sixteenth section, which has been appropriated by the Government for the use of private claims.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That any person appointed by the county commissioners’ court of the county of St. Clair, in the state of Illinois, be, and he is hereby, authorized to locate the said section named in this act for the purposes above-named.


*Approved, July 20, 1840.*

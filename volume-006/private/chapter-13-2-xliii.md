# Chapter XLIII

> An Act for the relief of George Walkington.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That George Walkington, who is now confined in the district of New York, at the suit of the United States, on judgment obtained on a bond conditioned for the observance of the embargo acts, be, and the same is hereby discharged from his confinement.


*Approved, March 31, 1814.*

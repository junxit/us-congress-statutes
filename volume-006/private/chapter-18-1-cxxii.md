# Chapter CXXII

> An Act for the relief of Hugh M’Culloch.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to pay to Hugh M’CulIoch, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of three hundred dollars, the amount paid by the said Hugh for the purchase of certain property in North Carolina, sold by the Marshal for the District of North Carolina, at the suit of the United States, which sale was subsequently reversed by reason of irregularity in the execution.


*Approved, May 19, 1824.*

# Chapter LXXIX

> An Act for the relief of James Barron.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to James Barron, a captain in the navy of the United States, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of seventy dollars and twenty cents, being the amount of duties paid by the said Barron to the collector of New York, for certain models for spinning rope yarns, imported by the said Barron into the port of New York, in August, one thousand eight hundred and fifteen.


*Approved, May 7, 1822.*

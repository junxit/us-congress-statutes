# Chapter LXXVI

> An Act for the relief of James McMahan.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to James McMahon three hundred and twenty-one dollars and fifteen cents, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, in full payment of his advances for costs and expenses of several suits arising out of the arrest and prosecution of Charles B. Rouse, formerly a postmaster at Mayville, New


*Approved, April 20, 1838.*

# Chapter LVIII

> An Act for the relief of Iluldah Farlow.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War place the name of Huldah Farlow, of Newtown, Connecticut, on the roll of revolutionary pensioners, and pay her a pension, at the rate of eighty dollars a year, for and during the term of five years from the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-six.


*Approved, July 9, 1842.*

# Chapter CLIV

> An Act for the relief of the heirs if Francis Jarvis, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Commissioner of the Land Office be, and he is hereby, directed to issue to the heirs of Francis Jarvis, deceased, a scrip certificate for one hundred acres, in consequence of military land warrant number six thousand three hundred and forty-five being located upon lands previously entered, and which could not be holden by said heirs.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

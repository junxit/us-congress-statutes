# Chapter CXXXI

> An Act for the relief of Return B. Brown.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the War Department be, and they are hereby, authorized to inquire into and settle the claims of Return B. Brown, late a captain in the army, and, upon proof, to allow him such credit as may be equitable and just, notwithstanding the judgment which the United States have recovered against him; and that such sums as may thus [be] found due to him shall be applied to the amount thereof in satisfaction of said judgment


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

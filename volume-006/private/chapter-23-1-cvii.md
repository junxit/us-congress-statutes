# Chapter CVII

> An Act for the relief of William Weedon.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That William Weedon be, and he is hereby, authorized to enter any other quarter section of unappropriated land within the Arkansas military district of bounty lands, instead of the north-east quarter of section twenty-two, of township five north, and range twelve west; for which a patent issued to said Weedon on the twenty-seventh day of November, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-one, but was not received by him until said quarter section was sold for the taxes due thereon: Provided, the said Weedon shall first surrender said patent, and file his relinquishment of all claim to said quarter section in the General Land Office.


*Approved, June 28, 1834.*

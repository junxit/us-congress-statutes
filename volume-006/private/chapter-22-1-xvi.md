# Chapter XVI

> An Act to authorize the Secretary of the Treasury to compromise the claim of the United States on the Commercial Bank of Lake Erie.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he hereby is, authorized to compromise and finally settle the claim of the United States on the Commercial Bank of Lake Erie, on such terms as he may deem most conducive to the best interests of the United States.


*Approved, February 10, 1832.*

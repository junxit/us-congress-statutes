# Chapter LV

> An Act to amend the act entitled “An act for the relief of Chauncey Calhoun” passed March third, eighteen hundred and thirty-nine.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the.Secretary of the Treasury pay to Chauncey Calhoun, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum which shall be found due him, upon auditing and settling his account, according to the provisions of an act entitled “ An act for the relief of Chauncey Calhoun,” passed the third of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-nine.


*Approved, July 9, 1842.*

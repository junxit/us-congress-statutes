# Chapter XV

> An Act for the relief of William M’ Donald, administrator of James M’ Donald, deceased, late captain in the army of the United States.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officer of the treasury department be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed, in the adjustment and settlement of the accounts of the late Captain James M’Donald, with his brother and administrator, William M’Donald, to make such further allowance for bounties and premiums paid, and for money due him, as an officer for recruiting, and for contingencies, as may appear equitable and just: Provided, however, That the sum so to be allowed shall not exceed the amount charged to the account of the late James M’Donald aforesaid on the books in the office of the third auditor of the treasury department.


*Approved, February 28, 1820.*

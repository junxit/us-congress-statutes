# Chapter CLV

> An Act for the relief of the legal representatives of General Moses Hazen, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to the legal representatives of the said Moses Hazen, the amount due for the loss of half pay, as a lieutenant on the British establishment, which he sustained in consequence of entering into the military service of the United States, agreeably to a resolution of Congress of the twenty-second day of January, Anno Domini seventeen hundred and seventy-six: Provided, That no interest shall be allowed on the amount due for loss of half pay, as aforesaid.


*Approved, May 26, 1828.*

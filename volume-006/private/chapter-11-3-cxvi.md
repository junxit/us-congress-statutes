# Chapter CXVI

> An Act for the relief of Anna Young, heiress and representative of Colonel John Durkee, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the accounting officers of the Department of War be, and they are hereby, authorized and required to settle the account of Colonel John Durkee, deceased, and to allow Anna Young, his sole heiress and representative, his seven years’ half pay, and interest thereon, to be paid out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, July 1, 1812.*

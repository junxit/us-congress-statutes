# Chapter CXLI

> An Act for the relief of Bannister Stone.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Bannister Stone be, and he is hereby, released from a judgment recovered against him by the United States, in the Circuit Court for the District of South Carolina, as one of the securities of Adam Carruth, late of South Carolina, in a contract entered into by said Carruth with the ordnance department of the United States, for the manufacture and delivery of ten thousand stand of arms to the United States: Provided, That nothing in this act contained shall be construed to release any other person or persons than the said Bannister Stone; nor shall it be so construed as to release him from the payment of the costs of recovering said judgment.


*Approved, May 24, 1828.*

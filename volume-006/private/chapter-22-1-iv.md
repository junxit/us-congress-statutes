# Chapter IV

> An Act for the relief of Robertson and Barnwell.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he hereby is, authorized to pay, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to the firm of Robertson and Barnwell, of the city of Mobile, eight hundred and sixty-four dollars, being the amount of duties paid by them upon certain barrels of Spanish playing cards, by them imported into the port of Mobile, and thence re-exported to Mexico.


*Approved, January 19, 1832.*

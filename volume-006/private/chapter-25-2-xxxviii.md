# Chapter XXXVIII

> An Act for the relief of Robert Peebles and John Graham.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay Robert Peebles and John Graham, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, one thousand and ten dollars and fifty-seven cents, which sum was incorrectly debited on their account, and deducted from money due them as army contractors, in the year one thousand eight hundred and thirty-five.


*Approved, March 19, 1838.*

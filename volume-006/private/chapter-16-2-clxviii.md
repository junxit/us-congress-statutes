# Chapter CLXVIII

> An Act for the relief of Bernard Pompilly.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the name of Bernard Pompilly be placed upon the list of invalid pensioners, at the rate of eight dollars per month, to commence on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and thirty-six.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

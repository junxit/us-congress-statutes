# Chapter CIII

> An Act for the relief of Orris Gay.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, directed to place the name of Orris Gay on the list of invalid pension-ers, and to cause him to be paid, as other pensioners on said list, at the rate of eight dollars per month, from and after the eleventh day of May, eighteen hundred and thirty-three.


*Approved, June 15, 1836.*

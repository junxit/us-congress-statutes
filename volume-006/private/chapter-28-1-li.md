# Chapter LI

> An Act for the relief of Harvey and Slagg.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, required to pay to Harvey and Slagg, of the city of New York, the drawback of duties upon ten cases of merchandise, duly entered for exportation at the New York custom-house on the fourth day of November, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-nine, examined by the proper officer, found correct, and sent alongside of the ship Patrick Henry, in charge of the proper officer of the customs, and which were actually exported on board said vessel, and landed in Liverpool; the payment of which drawback of duties was withheld by reason of informality in lading the said ten cases of merchandise on board said ship: Provided, The Secretary aforesaid shall be satisfied that every other requirement of law shall have been fully complied with.


*Approved, March 3, 1845.*

# Chapter LVII

> An Act for the relief of Philip Sehwartztrawbcr of Ohio.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized to pay to Philip Schwartztrawber, for his services in taking care of the booms left on Red river by Captains Tyson and Shreve, the surn of two hundred and forty dollars, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 3, 1845.*

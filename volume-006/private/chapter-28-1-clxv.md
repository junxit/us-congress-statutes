# Chapter CLXV

> An Act for the relief of Robert Layton’s children.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War pay to the widow of Captain Robert Layton, if any widow there be, or, if no widow, to Richard Layton, Elizabeth Gordon, John Layton, and Charles Layton, the surviving children of Captain Robert Layton, deceased, late a pensioner of the United States, the sum of one thousand one hundred and twenty-four dollars, it being the amount of pension, improperly suspended, which fell due to said Captain Robert Layton between the fourth March, eighteen hundred and thirty-five, and the eighth day of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-eight, the day of the said Captain Layton’s death.


*Approved, March 3, 1843.*

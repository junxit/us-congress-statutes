# Chapter XXV

> An Act for the relief of Reuben Attwater.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the sum of five hundred dollars be paid out of any moneys in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Reuben Attwater, as an additional allowance for his services whilst acting as secretary of the Michigan territory, and commissioner of land claims, and as a full compensation for all his ex-officio services.


*Approved, February 24, 1813.*

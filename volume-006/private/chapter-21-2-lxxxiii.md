# Chapter LXXXIII

> An Act for the relief of Henry Becker.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be authorized and directed to place Henry Becker on the list of invalid pensioners, at the rate of eight dollars per month, to commence on the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-six.


*Approved, March 2, 1831.*

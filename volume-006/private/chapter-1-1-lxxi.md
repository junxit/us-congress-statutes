# Chapter LXXI

> An Act granting a pension to Chauncey Rice.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be required to place the name of Chauncey Rice, of Northampton county, Pennsylvania, on the pension-roll, and pay him eighty dollars per annum during life; to commence and be computed from the fourth of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-one; to be paid out of any moneys not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, July 20, 1840.*

# Chapter CLXXV

> An Act for the relief of Jeremiah Kimball.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War is hereby directed to restore the name of Jeremiah Kimball, of Watertown, in the state of New York, to the roll of invalid pensioners, and to pay him, during his natural life, a pension of five dollars and thirty-three and a third cents per month; commencing on the second day of August, in the year eighteen hundred and thirty-nine.


*Approved, March 3, 1843.*

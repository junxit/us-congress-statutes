# Chapter CCXXXI

> An Act for the relief of Isaac Finney.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, directed to restore the name of Isaac Pinney, to the roll of revolutionary pensioners, and to cause him to be paid at the rate of eight dollars per month, from and after the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-eight.


*Approved, May 31, 1830.*

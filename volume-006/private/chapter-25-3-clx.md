# Chapter CLX

> An Act granting a pension to Frances Jones, widow of John Jones, of the State of Virginia.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, directed to cause the name of Frances Jones, widow of John Jones, late of the county of Kanawha, in the state of Virginia, to be placed on the pension roll; and that there be paid to her forty dollars per annum during her natural life; commencing at the time of the last payment made to her late husband, John Jones.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

# Chapter LXXXIII

> An Act for the relief of Jervis Cutler.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers be, and they are hereby authorized to settle and adjust the account of Jervis Cutler, late a captain in the army of the United States, and to allow him the pay and emolument of a captain, from the third of March until the fifteenth of June, one thousand eight hundred and nine.


*Approved, April 18, 1814.*

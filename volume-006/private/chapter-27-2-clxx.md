# Chapter CLXX

> An Act for the relief of George Sheffler.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to cause to be placed on the roll of invalid pensioners the name of George Sheffler, and that the said Sheffler be paid at the rate of four dollars per month, from and after the first day of January, eighteen hundred and forty-two.


*Approved, August 16, 1842.*

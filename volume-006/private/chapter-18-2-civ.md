# Chapter CIV

> An Act for the relief of William P. Yonge.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to William P. Yonge, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of three hundred and thirty-eight dollars, being the amount of extra tonnage duty and light money, illegally exacted by the Collector of St. Mary’s of the owners of the British brig Henry, in the year eighteen hundred and eighteen.


*Approved, March 3, 1825.*

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

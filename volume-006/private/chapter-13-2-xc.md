# Chapter XC

> An Act for the relief of John D. Hay.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That John D. Hay, post-master at Vincennes, be, and he is hereby released and discharged from the payment of three hundred and twenty-five dollars received by him as post-master for the general post-office department, which said three hundred and twenty-five dollars were, together with other property belonging to the said John D. Hay, destroyed by fire.


*Approved, April 18, 1814.*

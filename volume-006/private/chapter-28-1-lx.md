# Chapter LX

> An Act for the relief of March Farrington.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be authorized and directed to place the name of March Farrington on the roll of invalid pensioners, and that he be paid a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month, to commence on the twenty-third of December, eighteen hundred and forty-three, and to continue during life.


*Approved, March 3, 1845.*

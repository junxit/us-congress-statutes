# Chapter CX

> An Act for the relief of Adam Caplinger.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Adam Caplinger, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one hundred and two dollars forty-three and three-fourth cents, for his services as artificer on a tour of militia duty, performed in eighteen hundred and twelve and eighteen hundred and thirteen.


*Approved, March 2, 1833.*

# Chapter LI

> An Act to extend the time for selling the land granted to the incorporated Kentucky asylum, for teaching the deaf and dumb.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the further time of two years, from and after the fifth day of April, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-six, be and the same is hereby allowed the trustees of the Centre college of Kentucky, who are also trustees of the Kentucky asylum for teaching the deaf and dumb, to sell the land granted to said trustees for the use and benefit of said asylum, by an act entitled “An act for the benefit of the incorporated Kentucky asylum for teaching the deaf and dumb,” approved the fifth day of April, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-six.


*Approved, April 11, 1836.*

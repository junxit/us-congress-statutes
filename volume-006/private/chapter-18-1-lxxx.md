# Chapter LXXX

> An Act for the relief of John Thomas and Company.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to John Thomas and Company, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of four hundred and forty-one dollars, being the amount of transportation, and price of twenty-one barrels of flour, taken in the year one thousand eight hundred and fourteen, from said Thomas and Company, by the officers commanding at Forts Jennings and Winchester, and issued to the troops of the United States.


*Approved, May 17, 1824.*

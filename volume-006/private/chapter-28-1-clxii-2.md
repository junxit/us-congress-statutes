# Chapter CLXII

> An Act for the relief of John Atchison.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, directed to cause satisfaction to be entered on a judgment obtained by the United States against John Atchison in the United States court for the district of Illinois, it appearing that the sum agreed to be received by the proper department, in satisfaction of the same, has been paid by the said John Atchison.


*Approved, June 17, 1844.*

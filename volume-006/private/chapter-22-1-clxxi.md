# Chapter CLXXI

> An Act for the relief of William P. Gibbs, executor of Benjamin Gibbs, of Kentucky, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, directed and required to pay to William P. Gibbs, executor of Benjamin Gibbs, of Kentucky, deceased, the sum of twenty-five dollars and sixty-two ninetieths, that being the true amount of a final settlement certificate held by said Gibbs, originally drawn in favor of one David Johnson, dated the twentieth of January, one thousand seven hundred and eighty-four; together with interest at six per cent. on the said sum, from the first day of January, seventeen hundred and eighty-three, up to the passage of this act; to be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, July 4, 1832.*

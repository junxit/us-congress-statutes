# Chapter CXXIII

> An Act for the relief of Henry Stoddard.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury cause to be issued to Henry Stoddard, assignee of Nicholas Smith, who is assignee of Francis Dochoquet, a patent for three hundred and twenty acres of land, in accordance to a grant of the chiefs of the Shawnee nation of Indians, in the late Wapaghkonetta reservation, in the state of Ohio; which tract of [and is situated on the north side of the Auglaise river at a place called Cotoseka, embracing parts of sections numbers thirty and thirty-one, in township five south, of range six east, according to the plat and survey thereof: Provided, nevertheless, That the patent directed to be issued, as aforesaid, shall only operate as a relinquishment, on the part of the United States, and not against the rights of third persons.


*Approved, June 23, 1836.*

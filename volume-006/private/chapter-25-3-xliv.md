# Chapter XLIV

> An Act for the relief of Levi Chadwick.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War cause to be issued to Levi Chadwick, a private in the New Jersey line in the revolutionary war, a duplicate of military bounty land warrant number seven hundred and sixteen, for one hundred acres of land, which issued to Levi Chadwick, alias Shadwick, the fourth day of December, eighteen hundred and eighteen, and which has been lost; and the said Levi Chadwick shall have the said duplicate located and proceeded upon in the same manner as it it were an original warrant; and the said original warrant is hereby declared null and void.


*Approved, March 2, 1839.*

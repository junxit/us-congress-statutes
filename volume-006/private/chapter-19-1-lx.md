# Chapter LX

> An Act relinquishing the right of the United States in a certain tract of land, to William Hollinger.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That all right, title, and interest, which the United States possess, in the reservation made to William Hollinger, of lands within the tract of country ceded to the United States, by the treaty of Fort Jackson, on the ninth day of August, eighteen hundred and fourteen, with the Creek nation of Indians, be, and the same is hereby, vested in the said William Hollinger: Provided, That only six hundred and forty acres shall be granted in virtue of this act.


*Approved, May 16, 1826.*

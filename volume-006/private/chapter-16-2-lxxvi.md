# Chapter LXXVI

> An Act for the relief of John P. Becker.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be authorized and directed to place the name of John P. Becker on the pension roll of the United States, and that he pay the said Becker the sum of twenty dollars per annum, to commence the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-one, and to continue for and during his natural life.


*Approved, March 3, 1837.*

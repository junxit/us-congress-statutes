# Chapter XXXVII

> An Act for the relief of David Gilmore.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That a judgment, recovered by the United States against David Gilmore and others, at the District Court of the United States, holden at Portland, in the District of Maine, on the first Tuesday of June, in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and twenty-four, for the sum of eighty-four dollars and fifteen cents, damages and costs, be, and the same is hereby, released and discharged. And the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby authorized and directed to pay to the said David Gilmore, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of seventy dollars, being the amount of cost expended by the said Gilmore in defending a suit wrongfully commenced against him.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That if the said David Gilmore, and others, have paid or shall hereafter, and before they can avail themselves of the benefit of this act, pay the amount of said judgment, the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby authorized and directed to pay to the said David Gilmore, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the amount of said judgment, together with the amount of the marshal’s fees taken thereon.


*Approved, March 3, 1825.*

# Chapter XXVII

> An Act for the relief of Gustavus Loomis.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the war department be, and they are hereby authorized and directed, to audit and settle the claim of Gustavus Loomis, assistant deputy quarter-master general, and to allow him, in the settlement thereof, such credits as may be equitable and just.


*Approved, March 19, 1816.*

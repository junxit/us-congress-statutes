# Chapter LIV

> An Act for the relief of Esther Parrott.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized to place the name of Esther Parrott, of Bridgeport, Connecticut, on the roll of revolutionary pensioners, and to pay her the sum of eighty dollars a year, for the term of five years, from the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-six.


*Approved, July 9, 1842.*

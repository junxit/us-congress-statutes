# Chapter CXXV

> An Act for the relief of John Topp.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to John Topp, of Davidson county, Tennessee, the sum of three hundred dollars, the value of a quantity of wood necessarily taken by the United States’ troops, from the lands of the said Topp, near Nashville, in the state of Tennessee, during the last war.


*Approved, May 19, 1824.*

# Chapter XL

> An Act for the relief of Benjamin B. Ferguson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, required to place the name of Benjamin B. Ferguson, of the state of Pennsylvania, on the roll of invalid pensioners, at the rate of five dollars and fifty cents per month; said pension to commence on the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-eight, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, June 4, 1844.*

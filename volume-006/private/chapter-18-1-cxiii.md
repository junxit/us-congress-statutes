# Chapter CXIII

> An Act for the relief of Robert S. Foreman.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War cause to be issued to Robert S. Foreman, late a private of the thirteenth regiment of United States’ infantry, a warrant for one hundred and sixty acres of land; which may be located as other warrants are, which have been issued in favor of soldiers of the late war.


*Approved, May 19, 1824.*

# Chapter CLXVII

> An Act for the relief of David Brooks.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the allowance required to be made to the said David Brooks by the act, entitled “An act for the relief of David Brooks,” approved May the twenty-ninth, one thousand eight hundred and thirty, shall commence and be computed from the third day of March, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-six, instead of the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and thirty.


*Approved, July 4, 1832.*

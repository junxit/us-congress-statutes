# Chapter LXXXII

> An Act for the relief of John Edmonson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of John Edmonson, of Fulton county, in the state of Illinois, upon the roll of revolutionary pensioners, and that he pay him at the rate prescribed in the act of seventh of June, eighteen hundred and thirty-two, for two years’ service as a private in the revolutionary war; and that bis pension commence according to the provisions of the said act, and be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 15, 1844.*

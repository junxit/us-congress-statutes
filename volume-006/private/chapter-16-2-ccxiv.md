# Chapter CCXIV

> An Act for the relief of John Scott.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of John Scott, on the invalid pension roll of the United States, at the rate of eight dollars per month, from and after the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-six, and pay to said Scott, the said sum of eight dollars per month, during bis natural life.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

# Chapter LXXXII

> An Act for the relief of Thomas Taylor, junior.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and is hereby, authorized and directed to pay Thomas Taylor, junior, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, one thousand and eighty-one dollars and sixty cents, being in full for filling up and gravelling the carriage way on the north side of the President’s house, and for work done on New York avenue, between Seventeenth street west, and the President’s wall.


*Approved, March 3, 1825.*

# Chapter V

> An Act for the relief of James H. Brewer.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to James H. Brewer, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, the sum of two hundred and four dollars, for his service and loss of time in bringing to condign punishment two pirates, in the year one thousand eight hundred and thirty-one.


*Approved, February 5, 1834.*

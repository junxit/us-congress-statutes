# Chapter CXXXIV

> An Act granting a pension to Thomas West, of Jilabania.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be required to place the name of Thomas West, of Limestone county, Alabama, upon the roll of revolutionary pensioners of the United States, and pay him forty dollars per annum during his natural life; to commence and take effect on the fourth of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-one: Provided, That the said Thomas does not already receive a pension from the United States.


*Approved, June 28, 1838.*

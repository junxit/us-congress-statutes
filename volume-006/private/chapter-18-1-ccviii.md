# Chapter CCVIII

> An Act for the relief of Joseph M. White and William Davidson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, authorized to settle the accounts of Joseph M. White and William Davidson, of Florida, for services by them rendered, in the capacity of district attorney, and marshal, of the district of West Florida, under the appointment of the district judge, in the same manner, and upon the same principles, as if the said officers had been legally and regularly appointed by the President of the United States.


*Approved, May 26, 1824.*

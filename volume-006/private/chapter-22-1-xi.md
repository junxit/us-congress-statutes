# Chapter XI

> An Act for the relief of Robert A. Forsythe.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Robert A. Forsythe, out of any money, not otherwise appropriated, the sum of six hundred and twenty-five dollars, for his services and expenses for one hundred and fifty-three days in collecting and accompanying the Patawatamie tribe of Indians to meet the commissioners of the United States, in council, at Greenville, in the state of Ohio, in the year one thousand eight hundred and fourteen.


*Approved, January 23, 1832.*

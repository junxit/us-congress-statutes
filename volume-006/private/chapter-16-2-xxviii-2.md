# Chapter XXVIII

> An Act for the relief of Jacob B. Gilbert.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Jacob B. Gilbert, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of six hundred dollars, being the value of a house destroyed by the enemy during the late war, on the Niagara frontier, which belonged to the said Jacob B. Gilbert, and at the time of its destruction, was used as a place of military deposit.


*Approved, February 17, 1836.*

# Chapter LII

> An Act for the relief of Thomas Wilson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the accounting officers of the Department of War be, and they are hereby authorized and required to audit and settle the account of Thomas Wilson, for extra compensation for rations furnished the detachments of the troops of the United States, at new posts in Louisiana, and that they allow him for any unforeseen and unavoidable expenses which he shall adduce evidence to prove he incurred in furnishing the said rations.


*Approved, April 8, 1812.*

# Chapter LXII

> An Act granting a pension to John Gerodelle.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to pay to John Gerodelle, late a soldier in the corps of artillery, a pension at the rate of six dollars a month, commencing on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and thirty-four, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, March 3, 1835.*

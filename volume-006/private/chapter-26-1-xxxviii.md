# Chapter XXXVIII

> An Act for the relief of General Duncan L. Clinch.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, required to adjust all claims of General Duncan L. Clinch for the corn and sugar cane consumed or destroyed by the troops of the United States commanded by General Call, in the year eighteen hundred and thirty-six, during the period said troops were in possession of his plantation; and that the amount, when ascertained, shall be paid by the Secretary of the Treasury out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 19, 1840.*

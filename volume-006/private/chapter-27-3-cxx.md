# Chapter CXX

> An Act for the relief of James Gray.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to James Gray, of Washington county, Pennsylvania, at the rate of eight dollars per month, from and after the first day of January, eighteen hundred and forty-three.


*Approved, March 3, 1843.*

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

# Chapter LVII

> An Act for the relief of Marigny D’Auterive.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Marigny D’Auterive, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of eight hundred and fifty-five dollars, for ninetyfive cords of wood, taken by the troops, during the late war, under the command of General Carroll, near New Orleans, and for the use of a cart, horse, and driver, from the seventeenth of December, one thousand eight hundred and fourteen, to the twenty-fourth of January, one thousand eight hundred and fifteen.


*Approved, April 2, 1830.*

# Chapter CCXCI

> An Act for the relief of Sarah Easton and Dorothy Storer.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they hereby are, directed, in adjusting and settling the account of Sarah Easton and Dorothy Storer for the commutation granted them as children and heirs at law of the late Colonel Robert Hanson Harrison, to allow and pay to them such sum, as interest, as would have accrued on such commutation according to the regulations prescribed for funding and paying the domestic debt, had a certificate for such commutation been issued at the close of the war, and been in due time subscribed to said fund, and certificates of stock for the same regularly issued therefor, and all dividends thereon were now remaining in the treasury unpaid, to be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, July 14, 1832.*

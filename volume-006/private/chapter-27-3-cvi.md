# Chapter CVI

> An Act for the relief if Solomon Emerson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Solomon Emerson, of the state of Massachusetts, on the invalid pension roll, and to pay him at the rate of four dollars per month, from and after the fifth day of February, eighteen hundred and forty-two.


*Approved, March 3, 1843.*

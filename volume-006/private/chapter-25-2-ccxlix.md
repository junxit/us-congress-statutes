# Chapter CCXLIX

> An Act for the relief of the legal representatives of Timothy Feely, deceased, late a lieutenant in the army of Ute revolution.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, required to adjust and settle the account of Timothy Feely, late a lieutenant of the Virginia continental line of the army of the revolution, and, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to pay to his legal representatives the amount of five years’ full pay of a lieutenant of infantry.


*Approved, July 7, 1838.*

# Chapter LXXXV

> An Act for the relief of William P. Gould.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to William P. Gould, of Alabama, out of any unappropriated money in the treasury of the United States, the sum of one hundred and sixty-eight dollars and forty cents, as a compensation for the services of the said William P. Gould, in the land office at Tuscaloosa, in the state of Alabama, from the sixteenth day of October, eighteen hundred and twenty-three, to the fourteenth day of January, eighteen hundred and twenty-four.


*Approved, May 20, 1826.*

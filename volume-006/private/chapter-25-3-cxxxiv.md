# Chapter CXXXIV

> An Act for the relief of Zebulon Sheets.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Zebidon Sheets, or his legal representatives, be, and are hereby, authorized, upon surrendering to the United States, free of encumbrance, forty-seven acres and two-hundredths of an acre of land, entered by the said Sheets at the land office at Crawfordsville, state of Indiana, in section six, township twenty-six, range four west, to enter the same amount anywhere in said state, upon any lands of the United States subject to private entry and unoccupied.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

# Chapter CVIII

> An Act for the relief of the legal representatives of Henry Morfit, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the Treasury Department be, and they are hereby, directed to settle the account of Henry Morfit, deceased, and allow to his legal representatives five years’ full pay as a lieutenant in the Pennsylvania line on continental establishment, without interest; which five years’ full pay is the commutation of his half pay for life; to be paid to the said legal representatives of Henry Morfit, deceased, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 12, 1838.*

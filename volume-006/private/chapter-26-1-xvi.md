# Chapter XVI

> An Act for the relief of Meigs D. Benjamin and Company.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be allowed and paid, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Meigs D. Benjamin and Company, of the city of New York, two hundred dollars and forty-six cents, for duties paid by them on ten cases of leather gloves imported by them in the month of January, eighteen hundred and thirty-nine.


*Approved, May 2, 1840.*

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

# Chapter LXXV

> An Act for the relief of Simeon Meachum, of the state of New York.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War reinstate the name of Simeon Meachum of the state of New York, on the roll of revolutionary pensioners, as on the twelfth day of July, one thousand eight hundred and twenty, and that he, the said Simeon Meachum, be paid, as arrears of his pension, at the rate of eight dollars a month from the day aforesaid, until the ninth day of July, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-three.


*Approved, March 3, 1835.*

# Chapter CVIII

> An Act for the relief of David Kincaid.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That David Kincaid be confirmed in his title to five hundred arpens of land, situate in the forks of the river Chorette, district of St. Charles, being that lot of land claimed by the said David Kincaid, under a special permission to settle a concession from Charles Dehault Delassus, lieutenant-governor, &,c., dated fourteenth January, eighteen hundred and three; and that a patent issue in the usual form for the same: Provided, That this act shall only operate as a relinquishment, on the part of the United States, of all their right and claim to the above-described lot of ground, and shall not be considered as interfering with the rights of third persons.


*Approved, March 3, 1835.*

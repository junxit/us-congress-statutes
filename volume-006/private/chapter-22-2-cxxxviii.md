# Chapter CXXXVIII

> An Act in aid of an act, entitled “An Act for the relief of James Barnett.”

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he hereby is, authorized and required to pay to James Barnett, such interest on his commutation, provided for in an “Act for the relief of said James Barnett,” as he would have been entitled to receive if said commutation had been subscribed to the loan provided for by the several acts of Congress establishing the funding system, and the interest and principal had been paid under the several provisions of the said acts; and that the same be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 2, 1833.*

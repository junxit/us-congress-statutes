# Resolution 9

> Joint Resolution for the relief of Ferdinand Pettrich.

*Be it resolved, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized to pay over to Ferdinand Pettrich the unexpended balance of the appropriation for the finishing of the statue of Washington.


*Approved, August 29, 1842.*

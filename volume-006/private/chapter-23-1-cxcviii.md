# Chapter CXCVIII

> An Act for the relief of Robert Reynolds.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, required to place the name of Robert Reynolds on the list of revolutionary pensioners, and to pay him a pension at the rate of eight dollars a month, to commence and be computed from the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and thirty.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

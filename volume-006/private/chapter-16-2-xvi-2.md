# Chapter XVI

> An Act for the relief of David Bartlett.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to David Bartlett, of the state of Rhode Island, the sum of twelve hundred and thirty dollars and thirty-two cents, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, being in full of the balance due for money paid and expended by him for the public service, as a captain in the army of the United States, during the late war.


*Approved, February 17, 1836.*

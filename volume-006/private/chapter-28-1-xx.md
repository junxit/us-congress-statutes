# Chapter XX

> An Act for the relief of Violet Calhoun, widow of John Calhoun.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be authorized and required to place Violet Calhoun, widow of John Calhoun, late a captain in the army of the Revolution, on the roil of revolutionary pensioners of the United States: and that she be allowed annually the full pay of a captain, commencing on the seventh day of July, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-eight.


*Approved, May 23, 1844.*

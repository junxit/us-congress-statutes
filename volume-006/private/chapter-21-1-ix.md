# Chapter IX

> An Act for the relief of Theophilus Cooksey.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Theophilus Cooksey, out of any money in the treasury, not otherwise appropriated, the sum of sixty dollars, the value of a horse that died in the military service of the United States, in one thousand eight hundred and thirteen, for want of forage.


*Approved, January 30, 1830.*

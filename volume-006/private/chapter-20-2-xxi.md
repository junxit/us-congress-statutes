# Chapter XXI

> An Act for the relief of Jacob Rentleman.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Jacob Rentleman, of the state of Illinois, who entered by mistake, in the office of the Register of the Land Office for the district of Kaskaskia, the east half of section thirty-five, in township twelve, south of range three, west, and who obtained a patent therefor, be, and he is hereby, authorized to enter with the said register any other half section of land, within the said district, which is subject to private sale, upon his relinquishing to the United States the half section described as aforesaid.


*Approved, February 24, 1829.*

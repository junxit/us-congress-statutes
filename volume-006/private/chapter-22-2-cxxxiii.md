# Chapter CXXXIII

> An Act for the relief of Newton Berryman.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he hereby is, authorized and required to pay to Newton Berryman, the sum of sixty-six dollars, being for services rendered as a clerk employed in the navy department, from the fifth day of March to the sixth day of April, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-nine.


*Approved, March 2, 1833.*

# Chapter CCV

> An Act for the relief of the representatives of Colonel Anthony Walton White.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury cause to be paid, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated by law, to the representatives of Anthony White, late a colonel of the first regiment of light dragoons, of the Virginia continental line of the army of the revolution, the sum of three thousand seven hundred and fifty dollars, being the specie value, at the rate of forty for one, of one hundred and fifty thousand dollars, continental currency, advanced by Colonel White, on the fourth day of July, seventeen hundred and eighty, for the use of his regiment.


*Approved, July 7, 1838.*

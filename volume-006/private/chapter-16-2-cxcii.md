# Chapter CXCII

> An Act for the relief of Joseph Danforth.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, instructed to place the name of Joseph Danforth, of New Hampshire, on the roll of revolutionary pensioners, and that he cause to be paid to said Danforth the sum of twenty dollars per annum during his natural life, to commence on the fourth day of March, anno Domini one thousand eight hundred and thirty-one.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

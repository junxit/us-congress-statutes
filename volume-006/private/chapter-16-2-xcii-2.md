# Chapter XCII

> An Act for the relief of Henry Darling.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Henry Darling, of Bucksport, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of two hundred and forty-eight dollars and seventy-five cents, being the moiety of a forfeiture on a license bond recovered from said Darling as a surety of John Phillips, and paid into the treasury of the Uni-ted States.


*Approved, June 14, 1836.*

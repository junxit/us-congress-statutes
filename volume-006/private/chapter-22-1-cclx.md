# Chapter CCLX

> An Act for the relief of Gates Hoit.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of three hundred dollars to Gates Hoit, in full of all claims of said Gates Hoi on the United States for secret services rendered by him during the late war.


*Approved, July 14, 1832.*

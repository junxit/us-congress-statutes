# Chapter XXVI

> An Act for the relief of Jacob Butler.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury of the United States pay, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Jacob Butler, the sum of one hundred seventy-two dollars forty-four cents, the balance of the value of two horses, belonging to the said Jacob Butler, which died in the service of the United States for the want of forage.


*Approved, March 2, 1827.*

# Chapter CCXX

> An Act for the relief of John H. Harrison.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That John H. Harrison be, and he is hereby, released from a judgment recovered against him by the United States, in the circuit court for the district of South Carolina, as one of the securities of Francis Adams, late of South Carolina, on a bond given by the said Francis Adams, as a collector of the internal revenue of the United States for the fifth collection district of South Carolina: Provided, That nothing in this act contained shall be construed to release any other person or persons than the said John H. Harrison, nor shall it be so construed as to release him from the payment of the costs of recovering said judgment.


*Approved, July 13, 1832.*

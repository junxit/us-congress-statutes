# Chapter XXXVIII

> An Act for the relief of Percia Tupper, executrix of Samuel Tupper, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Percia Tupper, executrix of the last will and testament of Samuel Tupper, deceased, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, four hundred and fifty dollars, for a barn of the said Samuel Tupper, on his farm near the village of Buffalo, occupied for military purposes of the United States, and destroyed by the enemy, during the late war.


*Approved, March 7, 1832.*

# Chapter LXXVII

> An Act for the relief of Elias Johns.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Elias Johns seventy-five dollars, the value of a mare lost by him for want of forage, in eighteen hundred and fifteen, while engaged in the service of the United States, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, April 20, 1838.*

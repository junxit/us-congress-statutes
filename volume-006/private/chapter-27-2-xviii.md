# Chapter XVIII

> An Act for the relief of John 8. Billings.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the pension allowed to John S. Billings, an invalid pensioner, be increased to eight dollars per month, from the last day of June, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-seven, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, April 14, 1842.*

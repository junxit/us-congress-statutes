# Chapter CCXXV

> An Act for the relief of Daniel Cobb.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the name of Daniel Cobb, of the state of Maine, be restored to the roll of revolutionary pensioners, and that he be paid at the rate of eighty dollars per annum, during his natural life; the said payment to commence from the time he was last paid his said pension.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

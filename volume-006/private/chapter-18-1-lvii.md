# Chapter LVII

> An Act for the relief of Ichabod Lord Skinner.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to settle the accounts of Ichabod Lord Skinner, lately engaged as a sub-contractor in “making a road from Cumberland, in the state of Maryland, to the state of Ohio,” on principles of equity; and that the balance which may be found due to the said Ichabod Lord Skinner be paid to him out of any money in the treasury unappropriated; Provided, That the proceedings of the said Secretary be first approved by the President of the United States.


*Approved, May 5, 1824.*

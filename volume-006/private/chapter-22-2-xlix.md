# Chapter XLIX

> An Act for the relief of John S. Devlin.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, directed to settle the accounts of John S. Devlin, Quartermaster Sergeant in the United States’ marine corps, upon principles of equity and justice.


*Approved, February 27, 1833.*

# Chapter CXLVIII

> An Act for the relief of Winslow Lewis.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to pay to Winslow Lewis, out of the sum appropriated for rebuilding the light-house at Cape Florida, one thousand seven hundred and eighty-one dollars and sixty-eight cents, for his expenses actually incurred in chartering a vessel, employing hands, &c. for the purpose of rebuilding the light-house at Cape Florida; which he was prevented from doing by the hostility of the Seminole Indians, and the suggestion of the superintending agent of the Government.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

# Chapter CL

> An Act for the relief of John Bennet.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, directed to place the name of John Bennet on the roll of invalid pensioners, and to pay him seventeen dollars a month during his natural life, commencing on the seventh day of December, in the year of our Lord eighteen hundred and thirty-five.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

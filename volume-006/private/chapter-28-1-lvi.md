# Chapter LVI

> An Act for the relief of the legal representatives of Alexander Mitchell.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the legal representatives of Alexander Mitchell, deceased, and the real and personal property of the estate, and widow and heirs of the said Alexander Mitchell, he, and they hereby are, released and relieved from four several judgments, and all liens and encumbrances of said judgments, in favor of the United States, obtained in the United States district court for the western district of Virginia, against the said Alexander Mitchell, in his life-time, as security for Peter Yarnall and Samuel Mitchell: Provided, That nothing herein contained shall operate to release the said Peter Yarnall or Samuel Mitchell from their liability on said judgments.


*Approved, March 3, 1845.*

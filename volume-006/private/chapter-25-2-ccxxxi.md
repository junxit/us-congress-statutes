# Chapter CCXXXI

> An Act for the relief of Henry Barton.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be required to place the name of Henry Barton on the roll of invalid pensioners, and pay him at the rate of two dollars and sixty-six cents per month, to commence on the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-eight, in addition to the pension he now receives.


*Approved, July 7, 1838.*

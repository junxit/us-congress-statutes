# Chapter CCXI

> An Act for the relief of the heirs and legal representatives of John Taylor, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to the heirs and legal representatives of John Taylor, deceased, five years’ full pay as a lieutenant of infantry in the revolutionary war, which five years’ full pay is the commutation of half pay for life.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

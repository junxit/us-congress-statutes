# Chapter XXXV

> An Act for the relief of the legal representative of James Morrison, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be authorized to settle a suit in favor of the United States against the legal representative of James Morrison, deceased, and the claim against the United States in favor of said representative, for interest on a liquidated demand, on the principles of justice and equity; and, on a compliance with his decision by the said representative, the said Secretary is authorized to discharge a suit against said representative, on a bond purporting to have been signed by said Morrison, as the surety of Ashton Garrett.


*Approved, May 1, 1834.*

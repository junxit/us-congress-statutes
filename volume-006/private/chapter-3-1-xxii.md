# Chapter XXII

> An Act allowing Lieutenant-Colonel Tousard an equivalent for his pension for life.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be allowed to Lieutenant-Colonel Tousard three thousand six hundred dollars, in full discharge of his pension of three hundred and sixty dollars for life.


*Approved, April 30, 1794.*

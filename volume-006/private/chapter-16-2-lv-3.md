# Chapter LV

> An Act for the relief of David Kilbourn.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be and he hereby is, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money not otherwise appropriated to David Kilbourn, of the county of Oswego in the state of New York, the sum of two thousand five hundred dollars for loss of property he sustained in consequence of services rendered by him to the United States on the northern frontier during the late war with Great Britain.


*Approved, March 3, 1837.*

# Chapter LXXIX

> An Act for the relief of Patrick Callan.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officer of the treasury department be, and he is hereby, authorized to audit and settle the accounts of Patrick Callan, late a lieutenant in the second regiment of light dragoons, on principles of justice and equity.


*Approved, March 3, 1819.*

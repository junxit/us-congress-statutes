# Chapter XVII

> An Act for the relief of Albion T. Crow.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Albion T. Crow, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, three hundred and fifteen dollars twenty cents, the value of a stable occupied by the mounted Illinois volunteers while in the military service of the United States, in July, eighteen hundred and thirty-two, by order of Colonel Strode, and accidentally burnt while in said occupation, and in consequence thereof.


*Approved, March 2, 1838.*

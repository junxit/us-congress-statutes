# Chapter CCLXXXI

> An Act for the relief of the sureties of George Brown, deceased, late collector of internal duties and direct tax for the first district in the state of Maryland.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be authorized, and they are hereby directed, in the settlement of the amount of the claim of the United States against George Brown, late collector in the first district for the state of Maryland, to allow to his sureties the same commissions on the sums heretofore paid, or hereafter to be paid, by them, or either of them, as the said collector would have been entitled to if the money had been paid by said Brown, and regularly accounted for by him, under the several laws regulating the collection of the internal duties and direct tax.


*Approved, July 14, 1832.*

# Chapter XXXI

> An Act to authorize the issuing of a register to the brig American, of Savannah.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be issued, under the direction of the Secretary of the Treasury, a register to the brig American, a French vessel, which vessel was stranded on the coast of the United States, and purchased by James Taylor, of Savannah, Georgia, and by him repaired: Provided, It shall be proved to the satisfaction of the Secretary of the Treasury, that the cost of the repairs made in the United States, after the purchase of the said vessel, by the present owner, exceeds three-fourths of the original cost of building a vessel of the same tonnage in the United States.


*Approved, May 1, 1834.*

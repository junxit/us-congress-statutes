# Chapter XXXVI

> An Act for the relief of William W. Street.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby authorized to pay to William W. Street six hundred and twenty-five dollars, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, for five days’ use of his steamboat in the transportation of Florida militia, in October, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-seven.


*Approved, February 18, 1843.*

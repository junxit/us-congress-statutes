# Chapter CIII

> An Act for the relief of John M’Causland.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury cause to be paid unto John M’Causland, or his legal representative, the sum of ninety-six dollars; being the amount of a militia fine improperly assessed on him in the year one thousand eight hundred and fourteen; and that the same be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 3, 1819.*

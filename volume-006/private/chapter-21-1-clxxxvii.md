# Chapter CLXXXVII

> An Act for the relief of William Morrison.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to William Morrison, out of any money in the treasury, not otherwise appropriated, the sum of three thousand seven hundred and fifty-eight dollars seventytwo cents, the balance due him for supplies furnished the troops of the United States, under his contract with the Secretary of War, dated the third day of April, one thousand eight hundred and sixteen.


*Approved, May 29, 1830.*

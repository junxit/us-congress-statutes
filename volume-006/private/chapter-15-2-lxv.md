# Chapter LXV

> An Act for the relief of B. and P. Jourdan, brothers.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury department be, and they are hereby, authorized to settle and adjust the accounts of B. and P. Jourdan, brothers, of the state of Louisiana, not exceeding in amount the sum of twenty-eight thousand dollars; and that the same be paid out of any unappropriated money in the treasury.


*Approved, March 3, 1819.*

# Chapter LXXIX

> An Act the relief of John Cullins, a soldier of the revolutionary war.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, directed to place the name of John Cullins, of the state of Ohio, on the invalid pension roll, at the rate of eight dollars per month, to commence on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and thirty-four, and to continue during his said Cuilins’s natural life.


*Approved, March 3, 1835.*

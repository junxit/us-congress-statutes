# Chapter XLVI

> An Act for the relief of John Smith T. and Wilson P. Hunt.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury department be, and they hereby are, authorized and instructed to audit and settle the accounts of Alpha Kingsley, late a paymaster in the army of the United States, on principles of equity and justice; and to extend the benefit of such settlement, if any, to the judgment obtained against the securities of said Kingsley, in the state of Missouri, so far as the same may operate, or lessen, or discharge the said judgment, but not to the payment of any balance from the United States.


*Approved, March 2, 1829.*

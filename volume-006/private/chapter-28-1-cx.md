# Chapter CX

> An Act for the relief of Sengstack.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, directed to pay to C. P. Sengstack the sum of six hundred and one dollars and thirty-five cents, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, being the amount of his bill of glass, purchased for public use and delivered to the new post office building by order of the architect of the public buildings, and now subject to his control.


*Approved, June 17, 1844.*

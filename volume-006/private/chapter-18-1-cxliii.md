# Chapter CXLIII

> An Act for the relief of J. Ottramare.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury shall cause to be paid to J. Ottramare, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of three hundred and sixty dollars, being the amount received into the treasury, arising from the sales of four packages of jewelry, libelled and condemned in the District Court for the District of Louisiana.


*Approved, May 24, 1824.*

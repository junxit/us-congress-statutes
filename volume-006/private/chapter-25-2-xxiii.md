# Chapter XXIII

> An Act for the relief of Frances Gardiner.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War ascertain what property of the late Captain Gardiner was in the military occupation of the United Slates at Fort Brook, and destroyed in consequence of such occupation, and its value; also, if not destroyed, the damage the same sustained; also, what was destroyed by order of an officer, for military purposes, and its value; also, what was detained by order of an officer, and prohibited from being removed, and was destroyed in consequence thereof, and its value; and the respective sums so found due shall be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Frances Gardiner.


*Approved, March 7, 1838.*

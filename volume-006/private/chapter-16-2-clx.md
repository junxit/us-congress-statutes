# Chapter CLX

> An Act for the relief of Robert McJimsey, surviving partner of the firm of Jackson and McJimsey.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby authorized to pay, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Robert McJimsey, surviving partner of the firm of Jackson and McJimsey, of New York, the amount which may be found due the said firm for the drawback upon one hundred and fifty boxes of sugar exported at New York in the month of July, one thousand eight hundred and thirty, in the ship Elizabeth Frith, for Leghorn.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

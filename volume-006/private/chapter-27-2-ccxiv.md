# Chapter CCXIV

> An Act for the relief of Mary Hand.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War is hereby directed to place the name of Mary Rand, widow of Walter Rand of North Carolina, on the revolutionary pension roll, and pay her a pension, at the rate of eighty dollars per annum, for five years; to commence on the fourth day of March, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-six.


*Approved, August 26, 1842.*

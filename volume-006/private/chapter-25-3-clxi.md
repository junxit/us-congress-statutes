# Chapter CLXI

> An Act for the relief of David Rollins.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of David Rollins on the invalid pension list, and pay him at the rate of six dollars per month; to commence from the first of January, in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and thirty-eight.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

# Chapter CCVI

> An Act for the relief of Alexander Scott, late Collector of Pensacola.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to pay, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Alexander Scott, late collector of Pensacola, a salary of five hundred dollars per annum, from the day on which his salary of one thousand dollars per annum ceased, until his duties, as collector of Pensacola, were discontinued.


*Approved, May 26, 1824.*

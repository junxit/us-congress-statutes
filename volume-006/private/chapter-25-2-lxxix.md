# Chapter LXXIX

> An Act for the relief of Curtis Grubb.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Curtis Grubb, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, five hundred dollars, for the use and occupation of the farm at Gray’s Ferry, on the Schuylkill, during the late war, for military purposes, and for the crops and fences destroyed, and for the expense of levelling a redoubt constructed thereon.


*Approved, April 20, 1838.*

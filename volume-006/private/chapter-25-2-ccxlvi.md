# Chapter CCXLVI

> An Act granting a pension to James M. Edwards.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, required to place the name of James M. Edwards, of Henderson county, Kentucky, upon the roll of invalid pensioners, and to pay him at the rate of six dollars per month, during his natural life, commencing on the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-seven.


*Approved, July 7, 1838.*

# Chapter CXXXV

> An Act for the relief of John and Samuel Rowe, heirs and legal representatives of Ludwick Rowe, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to issue duplicate warrant number four hundred and four, for one hundred acres of land, issued the fourth of April, eighteen hundred and eight, in the names of John Rowe and Samuel Rowe, legal heirs and representatives of Ludwick Rowe, who was a private in the Pennsylvania line in the revolution; the original warrant having been lost.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

# Chapter V

> An Act for the relief of Jonathan B. Eastman.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the war department be, and they are hereby authorized to audit and settle the accounts of Jonathan B. Eastman, district pay-master of the lakes, and to allow him, in the settlement thereof, such credits as may appear equitable and just.


*Approved, January 17, 1816.*

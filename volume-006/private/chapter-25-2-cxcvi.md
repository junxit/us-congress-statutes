# Chapter CXCVI

> An Act for the relief of Miller Francis.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War he directed to place the name of Miller Francis, of Nashville, in the state of Tennessee, on the roll of invalid pensioners, and pay him at the rate of fifteen dollars per month, to commence from and after the first day of January, in the year of our Lord 1837, during his natural life.


*Approved, July 7, 1838.*

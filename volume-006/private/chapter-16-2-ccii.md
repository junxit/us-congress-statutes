# Chapter CCII

> An Act for the relief of Isaac Ruland.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, required to pay Isaac Ruland, of the state of Missouri, the sum of eight dollars per month, from the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-six, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

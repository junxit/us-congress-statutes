# Chapter CXXVII

> An Act for the relief of Samuel Goode.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Samuel Goode, eighty dollars, it being the amount of money paid by him in transporting certain sick soldiers from Greenville, Mississippi, to Nashville, Tennessee, in the year one thousand eight hundred and thirteen.


*Approved, March 2, 1833.*

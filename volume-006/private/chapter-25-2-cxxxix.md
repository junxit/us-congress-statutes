# Chapter CXXXIX

> An Act for the relief if Primus Hall, alias Trask.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War cause the name of Primus Hall, alias Trask, of Massachusetts, a free coloured man, to be placed on the roll of revolutionary pensioners, and that he be paid at the rate of sixty dollars per annum during his natural life; the first payment to commence on the fourth day of March, anno Domini eighteen hundred and thirty-one.


*Approved, June 28, 1838.*

# Chapter XXXVIII

> An Act for the relief of William Mendenhall.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay unto William Mendenhall, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of eighty-five dollars and fifty cents, the balance due him for two horses, lost in the service of the United States for want of forage.


*Approved, March 2, 1827.*

# Chapter CCXXXV

> An Act for the relief of the widow and heirs of Captain William Royall, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War shall cause to be paid to Anne Royall, the widow, and to the heirs of the late William Royall, five years’ full pay, as a captain from the state of Virginia, on the continental establishment during the revolutionary war, in lieu of half pay for the life of said William.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the said amount of five years’ full pay shall be paid to the said widow and heirs, in such proportions as will conform to the laws of Virginia for the distribution of the personal estate of persons dying intestate; and that said payments be made out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

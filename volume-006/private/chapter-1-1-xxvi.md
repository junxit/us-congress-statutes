# Chapter XXVI

> An Act to allow the Baron de Glaubeck the pay of a Captain in the Army of the United States.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the pay of a captain in the army of the United States be allowed to the Baron de Glaubeck, from the ninth day of March, one thousand seven hundred and eighty-one, to the twenty-fourth day of August, one thousand seven hundred and eighty-two, to be paid in the same manner as other foreign officers in the service of the United States have been paid.


*Approved, September 29, 1789.*

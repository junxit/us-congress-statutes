# Chapter CLXXV

> An Act for the relief of James Taylor.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to James Taylor, of Massachusetts, a revolutionary soldier, a pension from the eighteenth day of March, one thousand eight hundred and thirty, at the rate of eight dollars a month, to the fourth day of January, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-two.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

# Chapter L

> An Act for the relief of Elisha Morrell, administrator cf Joseph Icard, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to settle with, and pay to Elisha Morrell, administrator of Joseph Icard, deceased, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, one third of whatever sum may have been retained in the treasury of the United States on account of an award made by the commissioners under the convention with France of the fourth of July, eighteen hundred and forty-one, in behalf of Julien S. Roulet, on account of the loss of the cargo on board of the vessel called the Cadoz, after deducting any commissions or charges which may have been allowed for attending to said claim.


*Approved, March 3, 1845.*

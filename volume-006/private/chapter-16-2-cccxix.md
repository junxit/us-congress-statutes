# Chapter CCCXIX

> An Act for the relief of the legal, representatives of the late Theodore W. Maurice.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he hereby is, authorized to settle the accounts of the late Theodore W. Maurice, upon the principles of equity and justice; and that any balance which may be found due, be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, July 2, 1836.*

# Chapter CCCIX

> An Act for the relief of Isaac Means and others, late owners of the schooner Elizabeth.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper officers of the treasury pay to the said Means and others, late owners of the schooner Elizabeth, which was condemned and sold by a decree of the district court for the district of Massachusetts, at a term thereof held at Boston in September, A. D. eighteen hundred and thirty-two, the sum of three hundred dollars and twenty-one cents, being that portion of the avails of the sale which was paid into the treasury of the United States; it appearing that the condemnation of said vessel was without fault on the part of said owners.


*Approved, July 2, 1836.*

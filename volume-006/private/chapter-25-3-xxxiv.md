# Chapter XXXIV

> An Act supplemental to the “Act granting certain city lots to the corporation of the Columbian College for the purposes therein mentioned,” approved the fourteenth day of July, eighteen hundred and thirty-two.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the corporation of the Columbian College be, and hereby is, authorized to sell so many of the city lots, granted to said corporation by the act to which this is supplemental, as shall be sufficient to raise the sum of seven thousand dollars, and to apply the proceeds of such sale to the payment of debts due from said corporation, any thing in the act to which this is supplemental to the contrary notwithstanding.


*Approved, February 28, 1839.*

# Chapter LXV

> An Act for the relief of Thomas Shiverick.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby authorized to pay to Thomas Shiverick, of Dennis, in Massachusetts, out of any money in the treasury, not otherwise appropriated, the sum of fifty dollars, being the amount of a penalty incurred and paid by him for not renewing, before the expiration of the legal term, the coasting license of the schooner Adeline.


*Approved, April 7, 1830.*

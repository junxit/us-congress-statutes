# Chapter CIV

> An Act for the relief of Riddle, Becktle, and Headington, and their representatives.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Riddle, Beck-tie, and Headington, and their representatives, the sum of two hundred and fifty dollars and sixty-two cents, to carry into full effect the act passed for their relief at the last Congress.


*Approved, March 3, 1835.*

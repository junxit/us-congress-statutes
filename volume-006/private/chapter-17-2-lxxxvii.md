# Chapter LXXXVII

> An Act for the relief of Captain Richard Hightower.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers settle and adjust the account of Captain Richard Hightower, on the principles of justice and equity, and admit to his credit such sums, not exceeding seven hundred dollars, as shall be proved, to their satisfaction, to have been disbursed in the recruiting service, in the year one thousand eight hundred and twelve, by said Hightower, and the subaltern officers of his company.


*Approved, March 3, 1823.*

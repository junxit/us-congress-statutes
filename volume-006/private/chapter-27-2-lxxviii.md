# Chapter LXXVIII

> An Act for the relief of George Mayfield.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to place the name of George Mayfield, of Tennessee, on the list of invalid pensioners, and pay him at the rate of six dollars per month, during his natural life, commencing on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and forty.


*Approved, July 27, 1842.*

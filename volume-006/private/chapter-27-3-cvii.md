# Chapter CVII

> An Act for the relief of George J. Winslow.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, required to pay to George A. Winslow the sum of one hundred and forty-four dollars, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated; it being the amount due him for premium money in enlisting recruits during the last war with Great Britain.


*Approved, March 3, 1843.*

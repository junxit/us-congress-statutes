# Chapter CXL

> An Act for the relief of Lund Washington.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, directed to pay to Lund Washington the sum of two hundred dollars, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, being the amount due for the services of his minor son, William T. Washington, as a clerk in the war department during the first quarter of the year eighteen hundred and eighteen.


*Approved, June 17, 1844.*

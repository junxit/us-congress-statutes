# Chapter CLIX

> An Act for the relief of Thomas Dixon and Company of New York.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby authorized and directed to remit the penalty incurred by Thomas Dixon and Company, of New York, on two invoices of woollens, imported by the ships Nestor and James Monroe, in the month of July, eighteen hundred and twenty-one, from Liverpool, for the want of consular certificates, which were subsequently furnished, and to pay the same out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated: Provided, That no greater amount shall be paid than was received into the treasury.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

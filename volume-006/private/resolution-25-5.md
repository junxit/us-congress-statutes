# Resolution 5

> Resolution for the relief of Abraham Wright, of New York.

*Be it resolved, c.,*

That the Secretary of War cause to be issued to Abraham Wright, of Washington county, New York, as assignee of Ezra St. John, jr., who was assignee of Theodore Treat, a duplicate land warrant, number four hundred and eighty-six, the original having issued the twenty-second of February, eighteen hundred and ten, to said Ezra St. John, jr., assignee of Theodore Treat; and the said duplicate shall be located and proceeded upon in the same manner as if [it] were the original warrant; and said original warrant is hereby declared void.


*Approved, March 2, 1839.*

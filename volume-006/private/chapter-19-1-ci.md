# Chapter CI

> An Act for the relief of Jacob Hampton.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Commissioner of the General Land Office be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to make out and deliver, or cause to be delivered, to Jacob Hampton, of Wayne county, in the state of Indiana, a patent, in due form, for the fractional section number seven, in township number fourteen, and range one west, of the lands directed to be sold at Cincinnati, agreeably to the final receipt given to the said Jacob Hampton for the said fractional section, when the same shall be demanded.


*Approved, May 20, 1826.*

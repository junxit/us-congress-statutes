# Chapter XXXVIII

> An Act for the relief of Cata Bunnell.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Cata Bunnell, the mother of Abden Turrill, late a soldier in the army of the United States, and who died in said service, be entitled to receive the bounty in land to which the heirs of said Abden would have been entitled had he left any heirs; and that the proper officers cause a warrant and patent to issue accordingly.


*Approved, April 4, 1818.*

# Chapter LXXXVI

> An Act for the relief of Thomas Bennett.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Thomas Bennett, of the state of New York, be placed on the pension roll at the rate of thirty dollars per annum during his natural life; to commence on the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-one.


*Approved, July 20, 1840.*

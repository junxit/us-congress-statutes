# Chapter XXXII

> An Act for the relief of Simeon Thayer.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Simeon Thayer, late a major in the army of the United States, who was disabled at the battle of Monmouth, be placed on the pension-list of the United States, and that he be allowed the half pay of a major, from the first day of January one thousand seven hundred and eighty-one: Provided he return into the treasury-office a sum equivalent to the whole of his commutation of half pay.


*Approved, March 2, 1793.*

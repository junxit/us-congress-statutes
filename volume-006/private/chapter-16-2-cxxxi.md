# Chapter CXXXI

> An Act for the relief of the citizens of the reserved township in Monroe county, in the state of Indiana.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the register and receiver of the land office at Indianapolis be, and they are hereby, authorized to select within said district out of any of the public lands, a quantity of land for the use of schools within the reserved township in Monroe county, equivalent in value, and in lieu of the sixteenth section in said township, which was granted by Congress to the state of Indiana for the use and benefit of a state college.


*Approved, June 23, 1836.*

# Chapter XXIV

> An Act for the relief of Peleg Spencer.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Peleg Spencer, the sum of two hundred and fifty-three dollars, in satisfaction of his claim against the United States, for the loss of two horses, and the injury done to his wagon and harness while in the military service of the United States, on the frontier of Illinois, in the year eighteen hundred and thirty-two.


*Approved, April 15, 1834.*

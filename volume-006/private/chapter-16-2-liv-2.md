# Chapter LIV

> An Act for the relief of Alexander Gibson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War cause the name of Alexander Gibson, of Kentucky, to be restored to the roll of revolutionary pensioners, and that he be paid all arrears due him for his pension, since he was last paid, and that he be paid for and during his natural life a pension at the rate of eighty dollars per annum.


*Approved, March 3, 1837.*

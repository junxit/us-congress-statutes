# Chapter XV

> An Act for the remission of certain duties to the Pennsylvania Academy of the Fine Arts.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That all duties due and payable to the United States on a large box of paintings and engravings, owned by the Pennsylvania Academy of the Fine Arts, imported from Halifax to Boston in the year eighteen hundred and twelve, on board the cartel ship Agnes, be, and the same are hereby remitted.


*Approved, July 22, 1813.*

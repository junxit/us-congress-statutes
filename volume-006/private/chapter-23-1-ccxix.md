# Chapter CCXIX

> An Act for the relief of William D. Acken.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officer of the treasury department cause to be paid, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of eighty-two dollars and twenty-seven cents, to William D. Acken, late agent for the preservation of timber on the public lands in Florida, in addition to the allowances already made by the Navy Department, and in full of all demands for contingent expenses on account of said agency.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

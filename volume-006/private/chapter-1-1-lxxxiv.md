# Chapter LXXXIV

> An Act for the relief of certain settlers on the salt-lick reservation in the western district of Tennessee.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the provisions of an act of Congress entitled “An act for the relief of certain settlers living on what is called the salt-lick reservation in the western district of Tennessee,” approved on the third day of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-nine, be, and the same are hereby, extended to the first day of June, eighteen hundred and thirty-nine.


*Approved, July 20, 1840.*

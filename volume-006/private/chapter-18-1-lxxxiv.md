# Chapter LXXXIV

> An Act for the relief of Stephen Brace.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury department be, and they are hereby, authorized to settle the account of Stephen Brace, deducting therefrom any amount he may have received, either for his services as a boatman, or for subsistence while in captivity, and that the same be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, May 17, 1824.*

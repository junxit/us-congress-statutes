# Chapter XXII

> An Act for the relief of Joab Seely.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, directed to refund and pay to Joab Seely, the sum of twenty dollars, being the amount of an overpayment made by him into the treasury of the United States for a patent for a new invention.


*Approved, March 2, 1841.*

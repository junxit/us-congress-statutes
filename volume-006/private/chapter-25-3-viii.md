# Chapter VIII

> An Act for the relief of William East.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to William East, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of fifty-six dollars, for one hundred and twelve pairs of horseshoes furnished Captain Hodge’s command of mounted volunteer gunmen, in General Coffee’s brigade of Tennessee volunteers, in the month of February, eighteen hundred and fifteen.


*Approved, February 6, 1839.*

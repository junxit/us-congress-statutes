# Chapter CXX

> An Act for the relief of the legal representatives of John Louderman.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be authorized, and he is hereby directed, to pay to the legal representatives of John Louderman, late a soldier of the United States’ army, a sum at the rate of six dollars and forty cents per month, commencing from the thirtieth day of June, one thousand eight hundred and fifteen, up to the thirtieth day of April, one thousand eight hundred and sixteen.


*Approved, May 19, 1824.*

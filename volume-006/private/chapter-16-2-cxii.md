# Chapter CXII

> An Act for the relief of Gaius Hitchcock.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, directed to enter the name of Gaius Hitchcock, on the roll of Revolutionary pensioners, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty-eight dollars a year, during his natural life, commencing on the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-one.


*Approved, June 15, 1836.*

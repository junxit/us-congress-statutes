# Chapter LXXIII

> An Act for the relief of William Andrews, of the slate of New York.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of William Andrews, of the state of New York, on the pension roll, and to pay him sixty dollars per annum during life, instead of the pension now allowed him, and to commence at the time his pension was reduced.


*Approved, July 20, 1840.*

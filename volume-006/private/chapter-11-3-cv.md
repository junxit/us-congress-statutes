# Chapter CV

> An Act for the relief of Lieutenant-Colonel William D. Beall.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the accounting officers of the Department of War, in the settlement of the account of Lieutenant-Colonel William D. Beall, allow him the pay and emoluments of adjutant and inspector of the army, at and near New Orleans, from the seventeenth of June, until the twenty-fourth of November, in the year one thousand eight hundred and nine, to be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 24, 1812.*

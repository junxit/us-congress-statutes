# Chapter LXX

> An Act for the relief of Joseph Gilbert.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be authorized to pay to Joseph Gilbert, of the state of Virginia, who is now a pensioner under the act entitled “An act to provide for certain persons engaged in the land and naval service of the United States in the revolutionary war,” at the rate of eight dollars per month from the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and nineteen, when his name was dropped from the roll, up to the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and twenty; and that he also pay him the like sum per month from the said fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and twenty, to the second day of November, eighteen hundred and thirty, when his pension was re-stored: Provided, That the Secretary of War be satisfied, from the usual proof, that the said Gilbert was embraced by the provisions of the act of May one, eighteen hundred and twenty.


*Approved, March 3, 1835.*

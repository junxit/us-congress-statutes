# Chapter VI

> An Act for the relief of Lewis Schrack.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Lewis Schrack, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one hundred and five dollars, the value of four hundred and twenty rifle stocks, delivered by the said Lewis to the military storekeeper at Philadelphia, under a contract with the United States.


*Approved, January 30, 1830.*

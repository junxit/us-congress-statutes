# Chapter CLXXXVI

> An Act for the relief of Patrick McEwen.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and hereby is, required to pay to Patrick McEwen eleven hundred and twenty-nine dollars and fourteen cents, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated; the same being in full for pension due him, the said Patrick McEwen, a revolutionary pensioner of the United States.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

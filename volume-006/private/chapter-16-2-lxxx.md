# Chapter LXXX

> An Act for the relief of Job Barton.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Job Barton, a soldier of the late war with the rank of sergeant, a pension at the rate of four dollars per month, commencing on the fourth day of March, in the year one thou-sand eight hundred and twenty-eight, and to continue during his natural life, to be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 3, 1835.*

# Chapter CLXII

> An Act for the relief of Weight Hurlbert.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Weight Hurlbert on the pension list of invalid pensioners of the United Slates; and that said Weight Hurlbert be entitled to receive a pension at the rate of six dollars a month, to commence on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and thirty-eight.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

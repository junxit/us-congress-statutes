# Chapter LVIII

> An Act for the relief of Charles A. Burnett.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Charles A. Burnett, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of fifty-nine dollars sixty-one and a half cents, the balance due him for work done, and articles furnished, for the President’s House, during the administration of James Monroe.


*Approved, March 3, 1829.*

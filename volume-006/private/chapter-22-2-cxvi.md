# Chapter CXVI

> An Act for the relief of Archibald W. Hamilton.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Archibald W. Hamilton, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of three hundred and thirty-six dollars and forty-seven cents; being for property purchased for the Quartermaster-General’s Department while he was in the service of the United States, and which he has not been repaid.


*Approved, March 2, 1833.*

# Chapter XLIV

> An Act for the relief of William Clower.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Postmaster-General be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to settle the claim of William Clower, for carrying the mail between Greenville and Fayette, Mississippi, upon principles of equity and justice.


*Approved, March 2, 1831.*

# Chapter XCIV

> An Act explanatory of an Act for the relief of sundry citizens of Baltimore.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the act, entitled “An act for the relief of sundry citizens of Baltimore,” shall be so construed as to refer to a copy of estimates verified at Baltimore by Thorndike Chase and John Snyder, on the twenty-eighth day of February, one thousand eight hundred and twenty, instead of the twentieth day of February aforesaid.


*Approved, May 7, 1822.*

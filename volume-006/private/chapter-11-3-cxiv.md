# Chapter CXIV

> An Act for the relief of James Wilkinson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That from the sixteenth day of March, one thousand eight hundred and two, Brigadier-General Wilkinson be and he hereby is allowed, for the transportation of his baggage, and for fuel, the same emolument and compensation as has been and now is allowed to the other brigadier-generals in the service of the United States.


*Approved, July 1, 1812.*

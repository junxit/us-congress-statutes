# Chapter CLXIII

> An Act for the relief of Daniel Penhallow.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Daniel Penhallow upon the roll of invalid pensioners, and cause to be paid to him at the rate of four dollars per month, from and after the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and forty-two, out of any moneys not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 3, 1843.*

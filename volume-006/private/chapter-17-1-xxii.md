# Chapter XXII

> An Act for the relief of John Anderson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to John Anderson, of the Michigan territory, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of thirteen hundred dollars, in full, for the loss of a house by fire, in the month of December, one thousand eight hundred and thirteen; and while, without the consent of the owner, it was occupied by the troops of the United States.


*Approved, April 17, 1822.*

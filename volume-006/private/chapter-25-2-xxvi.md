# Chapter XXVI

> An Act for the relief of Allen R. Moore.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay, out of any unappropriated money, to Allen R. Moore, twenty-eight dollars, in full for the use of his store in the village of Champlain, in the state of New York, by the troops of the United States, as barracks, in March, one thousand eight hundred and fourteen, and for damage done to said store during such occupancy.


*Approved, March 7, 1838.*

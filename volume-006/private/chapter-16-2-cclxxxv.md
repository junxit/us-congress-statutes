# Chapter CCLXXXV

> An Act for the relief of the widow of Peter Smith.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to pay to the widow of Peter Smith, of Walpole in the state of Massachusetts, the amount of pension which would have accrued to her husband, if he had been placed on the pension roll, at the rate of twenty dollars a year, from the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-one, to the time of his death.


*Approved, July 2, 1836.*

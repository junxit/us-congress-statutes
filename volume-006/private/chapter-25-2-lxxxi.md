# Chapter LXXXI

> An Act to extend the time for selling the land granted to the Kentucky asylum for leaching the deaf and dumb.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the further time of two years, from and after the fifth day of April, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-eight, be, and the same is hereby allowed the trustees of the Kentucky asylum for teaching the deaf and dumb, to sell the land heretofore granted by Congress to said institution.


*Approved, April 20, 1838.*

# Chapter LXXVIII

> An Act for the relief of Isaac Minis and others.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury department be, and they are hereby, authorized to audit and settle, on just and equitable principles, the accounts of Minis and Henry, merchants, of Savannah, Theodore A. Schoedde, Stephen Hall, Asahel Howe, William T. Williams, Dunham and Reed, Meigs and Reed, and Elias Reed, for payments by them made to the troops of the regular army, and the militia employed in the service of the United States, during the late war with Great Britain.


*Approved, March 3, 1819.*

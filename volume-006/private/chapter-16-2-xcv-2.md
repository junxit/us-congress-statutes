# Chapter XCV

> An Act for the relief of William Woodward and William Mitchell.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, thirty dollars to William Wood-ward and William Mitchell, being money paid for a patent which they do not wish should issue.


*Approved, June 14, 1836.*

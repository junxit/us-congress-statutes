# Chapter CXXIX

> An Act for the relief of John Hicks.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of John Hicks on the invalid pension roll, and that he be entitled to receive at the rate of eight dollars per month during his natural life, commencing the first day of April, one thousand eight hundred and forty.


*Approved, March 3, 1843.*

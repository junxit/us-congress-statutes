# Chapter CXLI

> An Act for the relief of Richard Hendley.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be authorized and directed to pay to Richard Hendley, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, ninety-three dollars, as compensation for furnishing a room, fire, and candles to the guards round the old post office thirty-one days.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

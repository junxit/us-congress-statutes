# Chapter CIII

> An Act granting a pension to Michael McCray.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Michael McCray, of the county of Venango, state of Pennsylvania, on the roll of invalid pensions, and pay to him the sum of two dollars and sixty-six cents per month, during his natural life, commencing on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and thirty-eight.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

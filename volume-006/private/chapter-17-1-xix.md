# Chapter XIX

> An Act for the relief of Jonathan N. Bailey.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to Jonathan N. Bailey, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one hundred and thirty-three dollars and seventy-two cents, being for so much overpaid by him to the collector of the district of Niagara, on goods imported by him from Queenstown, in Upper Canada.


*Approved, April 17, 1822.*

# Chapter CCIV

> An Act for the relief of Daniel Trabue.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary for the War Department be, and he is hereby, directed to place Daniel Trabue, of the state of Kentucky, upon the roll of the revolutionary pensioners of the United States, at the rate of twenty dollars per annum, commencing on the fourth day of March, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-one, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

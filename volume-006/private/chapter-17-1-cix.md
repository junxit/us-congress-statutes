# Chapter CIX

> An Act for the relief of Samuel Ewings.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury cause to be issued to Samuel Ewings, or his legal representatives, upon application, a patent for five hundred acres of land, situated on the Miami of the Lake, it being the same tract which was confirmed to him under an act, entitled “An act regulating the grants of land in the territory of Michigan,” passed the third of March, one thousand eight hundred and seven: And for which he holds the Register’s certificate, numbered five hundred and seventy-eight.


*Approved, May 7, 1822.*

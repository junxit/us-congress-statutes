# Chapter V

> An Act for the relief of Peggy Mellen.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be authorized to issue, in the name of Peggy Mellen, a land warrant for the bounty land to which Alford Stebbins, late a soldier in the army of the United States, deceased, would have been entitled, had he lived.


*Approved, February 4, 1822.*

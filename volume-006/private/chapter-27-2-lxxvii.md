# Chapter LXXVII

> An Act for the relief of Betsey Breeland.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to place the name of Betsey Vreeland, of the county of Essex, state of New Jersey, widow of Daniel Vreeland, a soldier of the revolutionary war, on the list of pensioners of the United States, at the rate of eighty dollars per annum, for the term of five years, to be computed from the fourth day of March, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-six.


*Approved, July 27, 1842.*

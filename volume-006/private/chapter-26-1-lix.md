# Chapter LIX

> An Act for the relief of Medad Cook.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to place Medad Cook, of Jefferson county, in the state of New York, on the pension-list, and to pay him at the rate of four dollars per month, commencing on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and thirty-nine, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, July 20, 1840.*

# Chapter X

> An Act for the relief of John B. Perkins.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to John B. Perkins ninety-five dollars compensation for a horse lost for want of forage, on the fourth of May, eighteen hundred and eighteen, while engaged in the service of the United States, in the Seminole campaign, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, February 22, 1838.*

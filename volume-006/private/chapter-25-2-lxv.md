# Chapter LXV

> An Act for the relief of Ann W. Johnston.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Ann W. Johnston out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, one hundred and fifty dollars, the value of a horse killed in an engagement with the enemy, during the late war, at St. David’s in Upper Canada, the property of her late husband, a lieutenant in a company of light dragoons in the service of the United States.


*Approved, April 20, 1838.*

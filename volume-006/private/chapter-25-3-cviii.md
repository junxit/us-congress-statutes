# Chapter CVIII

> An Act for the relief of Nicholas Phelan, heir at law of John Phelan, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War cause to be issued to Nicholas Phelan, heir at law of John Phelan, deceased, late of the city of Baltimore, a warrant for two hundred acres of land, for the service of said John Phelan, as a lieutenant or captain in the war of the Revolution.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

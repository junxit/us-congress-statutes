# Chapter LXXXII

> An Act for the relief of Lemuel Tanner, assignee of Pierre Dufresne.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Lemuel Tanner, assignee of Pierre Dufresne, be, and he is hereby, authorized to locate, within twelve months after the passage of this act, under the direction of the Surveyor-General of Louisiana, on any unlocated lands in said state, a tract not exceeding six hundred and forty acres of land; which said tract shall be granted to the said Lemuel Tanner, in lieu of an equal quantity confirmed to him by the Register and Receiver, under a certificate number five hundred and eighty-eight; and that the proper officers of the Government be authorized and directed to issue a patent accordingly.


*Approved, March 3, 1835.*

# Chapter CXVI

> An Act for the relief of Horace Wetherall.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Horace Wetherall, an invalid pensioner, be paid, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, at the rate of eight dollars per month from the first day of August, in the year eighteen hundred and thirty-nine, to the thirtieth day of October, in the year eighteen hundred and forty.


*Approved, March 3, 1843.*

# Chapter LXXXIX

> An Act for the relief of John H. Thomas, claiming under Antoine Patin.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the legal representatives of Antoine Patin, claiming a tract of land in the county of Attakapas, in the state of Louisiana, situated on the east side of Bayou Teche, and numbered one thousand one hundred and fourteen in the report of the Commissioners for the adjustment of land titles in the western district of Louisiana, be, and the same are hereby, confirmed in them to the quantity of land embraced in said claim: Provided, That this act shall only be construed, as a relinquishment of the Government of all claim to said tract of land to said claimant: And provided, also, That this act shall not operate against the claim of any third person to said tract of land.


*Approved, May 19, 1832.*

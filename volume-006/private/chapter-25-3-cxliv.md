# Chapter CXLIV

> An Act for the relief of Enoch Matson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Enoch Matson be, and he is hereby, authorized to withdraw his location of warrant number one hundred and fifty-five, for six hundred and forty-acres, and to locate the same upon any of the unappropriated lands of the United States, in the state of Missouri, now liable to entry at private sale.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

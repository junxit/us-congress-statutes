# Chapter LXIII

> An Act granting a pension to William Wilges.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be and he is hereby, required to place the name of William Wilges, of Harford county, in the state of Maryland, upon the roll of invalid pensioners, and to pay him at the rate of eight dollars a month during his natural life, commencing on the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-four.


*Approved, March 3, 1835.*

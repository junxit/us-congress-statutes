# Chapter LXIII

> An Act for the reliefof Joseph Shomo, late a Captain in the United States’ Army.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officer be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to settle and adjust the accounts of Joseph Shomo, late a captain in the United States’ army, upon principles of equity and justice, so far as relates to the enlistment of ten soldiers.


*Approved, May 16, 1826.*

# Chapter LVIII

> An Act for the relief of John Webster.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury department be, and they are hereby, authorized and required to pay to John Webster, late collector of the internal revenue for the county of Somerset, in the district of Pennsylvania, the sum of two hundred and sixty-four dollars; which sum shall be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 3, 1821.*

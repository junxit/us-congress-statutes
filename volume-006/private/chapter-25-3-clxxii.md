# Chapter CLXXII

> An Act for the relief of the legal representatives of James Maxwell, of Pennsylvania.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War cause to be issued to the legal representatives of James Maxwell, of Pennsylvania, for his services during the war of the revolution, a bounty land warrant for one hundred acres of land.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

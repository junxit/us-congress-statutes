# Chapter XXXV

> An Act for the relief of Edward Lee.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Edward Lee, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, three hundred and fifty dollars, the value of five thousand pounds of hard bread, taken from him by the soldiers in the service of the United States, at Sackett’s Harbor, in the year one thousand eight hundred and thirteen, the same being necessary for their subsistence.


*Approved, March 7, 1832.*

# Chapter XIII

> An Act for the relief of the legal representatives of the late General William Hull.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury cause to be paid to the legal representatives of General William Hull, deceased, or to their authorized agent, the sum of one thousand three hundred and eighty dollars, in full for his claims against the United States; and that the same be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 10, 1828.*

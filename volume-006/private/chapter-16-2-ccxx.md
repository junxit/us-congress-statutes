# Chapter CCXX

> An Act granting a pension to Micajah Ricketts.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Micajah Ricketts on the invalid pension roll, and that he be paid at the rate of four dollars per month, during his natural life, commencing on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and thirty-six, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

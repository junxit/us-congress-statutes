# Chapter XLII

> An Act for the relief of Edwin T. Satterwhite, late a purser of the Hornet.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the secretary of the navy be, and he is hereby authorized and required to adjust and settle the accounts of Edwin T. Satterwhite, late a purser of the United States’ sloop of war Hornet, upon principles of equity and justice, and to make him such allowances as under the circumstances of his case shall appear reasonable.


*Approved, March 31, 1814.*

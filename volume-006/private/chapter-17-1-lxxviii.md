# Chapter LXXVIII

> An Act for the relief of Peter Cadwell and James Britten.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the sum of two hundred and twenty-two dollars and twenty-two cents be paid to Peter Cadwell and James Britten, or their legal representatives, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, being one-half the amount of the net proceeds of their property imported in the ship Northern Liberties, which was seized and sold in the port of New York, for a breach of the law.


*Approved, May 7, 1822.*

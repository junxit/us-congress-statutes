# Chapter CXIII

> An Act for the relief of Daniel Davis.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to issue to Daniel Davis, a soldier of the revolutionary army, a land warrant for one hundred acres of land, which warrant, when issued, shall be located on any unlocated land in the manner and within the time provided by law for other military warrants issued for services in the revolutionary army, and a patent shall be granted thereon, as in other cases.


*Approved, May 20, 1826.*

# Chapter XV

> An Act for the relief of Charles Gordon and the crew of the schooner two Sons.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Collector of the Customs for the district of Belfast, in the state of Maine, is hereby authorized to pay to Charles Gordon, owner of the fishing schooner Two Sons, of seventy-five tons burthen, and to the persons composing her late crew, such allowance, to be distributed according to law, as they would have been entitled to receive had she completed her fishing term: the said schooner having been driven on shore in a gale, by which she was prevented from accomplishing the full term required by law to entitle her. to the bounty.


*Approved, January 27, 1835.*

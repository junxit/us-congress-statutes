# Chapter CXV

> An Act for the relief of Jonathan Britton.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to place the name of Jonathan Britton, of Otisfield, Cumberland county, and state of Maine, on the pension roll, and pay him a pension, at the rate of eighty dollars per annum, from the fourth day of March, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-one, during his natural life.


*Approved, March 3, 1843.*

# Chapter XCV

> An Act for the relief of George J. Knight.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the sum of two thousand dollars be paid to George J. Knight, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, for a schooner owned by him, and which was pressed into the service of the United States, and captured and destroyed by the enemy during the last war.


*Approved, May 25, 1832.*

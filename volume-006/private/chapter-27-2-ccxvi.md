# Chapter CCXVI

> An Act for the relief of David Freelove.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be hereby authorized and required to place the name of David Freelove on the pension roll, under the act of June, eighteen hundred and thirty-two, and that he cause him to be paid the sum of eighty dollars per annum during his natural life.


*Approved, August 26, 1842.*

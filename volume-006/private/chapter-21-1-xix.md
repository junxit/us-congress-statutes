# Chapter XIX

> An Act for the relief of Orson Sparks, and John Watson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Orson Sparks, the sum of forty-five dollars, and to John Watson the sum of sixty dollars, out of any money in the treasury, not otherwise appropriated; the value of two horses lost by them during the late war, while in the service of the United States.


*Approved, February 11, 1830.*

# Chapter CXCII

> An Act for the relief of William Clark.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to issue a land warrant for one hundred and sixty acres of land to William Clark, a Canadian volunteer, to be located on any of the unappropriated lands which have been offered for sale, and are subject to private entry in the Territory of Wisconsin: and also, that the Secretary of the Treasury do pay to said William Clark, the sum of twenty-four dollars, for three months extra pay, all which shall be in full compensation for the services of said Clark in the late war with Great Britain.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

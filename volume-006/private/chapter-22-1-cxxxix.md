# Chapter CXXXIX

> An Act for the relief of Hopkins Rice.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Hopkins Rice be hereby authorized to enter and locate a half quarter section of land of the public land in the state of Alabama, which may be subject to entry at private sale, in lieu of the west half of north-east quarter of section numbered two, township numbered twenty-two, range numbered one west, containing seventy-six and twenty-eight hundredths acres of land, entered by him by mistake, on the twenty-seventh day of December, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-eight, for which half quarter section when entered, a patent shall issue as in other cases: Provided, The said Hopkins Rice file in the proper land office a relinquishment to the United States of the said half quarter section of land entered by mistake as aforesaid.


*Approved, June 15, 1832.*

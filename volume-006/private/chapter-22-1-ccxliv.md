# Chapter CCXLIV

> An Act for the relief of the officers and soldiers of Fort Delaware.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the second auditor of the Treasury be, and he hereby is, authorized and required to ascertain the amount of property lost by each officer and soldier in the conflagration which occurred at Fort Delaware, on the night of the eighth of February, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-one; and the amount so ascertained, shall be paid out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, July 14, 1832.*

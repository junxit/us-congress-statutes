# Chapter CCLXXI

> An Act for the relief of John Wilson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That to correct an error in the proceedings of the late Board of Commissioners appointed to examine and adjust private land claims in Missouri, John Wilson be, and he is hereby, confirmed in his title to six hundred and twenty-two acres of land, situate in township number forty-two north, of range five east, of the fifth principal meridian, instead of six hundred and twenty-two arpens, adjudged to him by the Board of Commissioners aforesaid.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

# Chapter CXXVIII

> An Act for the relief of James Caulfield.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That James Caulfield be authorized to enter with the proper land office in Alabama, at one dollar and twenty-five cents per acre, so much land as is known to constitute the reservation of Peggy Bailey, a half breed Creek Indian woman, under the treaty of Fort Jackson; being that portion of section seven, in township five, range five, on the east of the Alabama river, and so much of the fraction seven of said township and range, on the west side of the Alabama river, as will make, in the whole, three hundred and twenty acres of land.


*Approved, June 23, 1836.*

# Chapter CLXXXIX

> An Act for the relief of John McCarroll, junior.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury cause to be issued to John McCarroll, junior, land scrip on a Virginia military land warrant, number two thousand four hundred and twenty-nine, which issued to James Wilder, for one hundred acres of land, on the ninth day of February, one thousand seven hundred and eighty-four, upon said John McCarroll, junior, giving bond and satisfactory security to refund the value thereof, should the heirs or other claimants under said James Wilder show a better claim thereto than said John McCarroll.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

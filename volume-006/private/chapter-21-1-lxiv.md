# Chapter LXIV

> An Act for the relief of John Rodriguez.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to John Rodriguez, or to his legal representatives, out of any money in the treasury, not otherwise appropriated, the sum of eight hundred and eighty-three dollars, for damages done to his plantation by digging and using earth for works of defence during the late war, and for materials and labor to repair said plantation.


*Approved, April 7, 1830.*

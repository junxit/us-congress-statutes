# Chapter LXIV

> An Act for the relief of William Fabre.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to William Fabre, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one hundred and twenty dollars and forty-two cents, being for prize money due to said William Fabre, when a sailor on board the Saratoga, on Lake Champlain.


*Approved, March 1, 1843.*

# Chapter XLIX

> An Act for the relief of John Daly, late of Canada.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he hereby is, directed to pay to John Daly, late of New Ark, in the province of Upper Canada, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of five thousand dollars, being for supplies furnished and services rendered to the army of the United States in Canada, and for losses of property sustained by him on the Niagara frontier during the late war.


*Approved, March 2, 1831.*

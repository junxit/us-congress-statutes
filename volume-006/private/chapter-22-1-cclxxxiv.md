# Chapter CCLXXXIV

> An Act for the relief of Randall Allis, Timothy Twichell, and John Lee Williams.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, required to pay to Randall Allis the sum of four hundred dollars, to Timothy Twichell the sum of four hundred dollars, and to John Lee Williams the sum of two hundred dollars, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, for certain lands relinquished to the United States under a contract with H. M. Breckenridge as agent of the navy department.


*Approved, July 14, 1832.*

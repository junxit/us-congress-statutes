# Chapter CXXVI

> An Act for the relief of Absalom Boles.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Absalom Boles, of the county of Marion, and state of Alabama, be, and he is hereby authorized, to surrender to the Register and Receiver of the Land Office at Huntsville, the certificate heretofore issued to him for the west half of the north-east quarter of section number one, township thirteen, range number fifteen west of the meridian of Huntsville, which was purchased by him through mistake; and the said Absalom Boles, on filing his relinquishment to all right and title thereto, shall be entitled to a certificate from the Register and Receiver, stating the amount which has been by him so paid by mistake, which shall be received in payment for any land of the United States which may be liable to private entry, or which may hereafter be sold.


*Approved, March 2, 1833.*

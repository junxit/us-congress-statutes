# Chapter LIV

> An Act to increase the pension of Charles Larrabee.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Charles Larrabee, an invalid pensioner, be, and he is hereby, entitled to receive twenty-five dollars per month, in lieu of the pension to which he is now entitled.


*Approved, April 2, 1830.*

## Marginal notes

Printed in the margin of the volume beside the text above, not in it.

- April 2, 1830.
- $25 per month to be paid him.

# Chapter LX

> An Act for the relief of Daniel Page.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Daniel Page, a revolutionary pensioner of the United States, the sum of eight dollars per month, from the fourth day of September, eighteen hundred and twenty-three, to the fourteenth day of August, eighteen hundred and thirty-two.


*Approved, March 3, 1835.*

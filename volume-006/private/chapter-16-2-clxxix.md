# Chapter CLXXIX

> An Act for the relief of John Logan.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to place the name of John Logan, of Ohio, on tile roll of revolutionary pensioners; and that said Logan be paid the sum of thirty dollars per annum, commencing on the fourth day of March, anno Domini, eighteen hundred and thirty-one.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

# Chapter LXXI

> An Act for the relief of Henry J. Defrees and Stephen Jenny.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Henry J. Defrees and Stephen Jenny, the only surviving sureties in a bond executed by them, together with sun-dry other persons, in the year eighteen hundred and seventeen, on be-half of John B. Fanning, then a purser in the United States navy, and to secure the faithful performance of his duty as such, be, and they are hereby, released and discharged from said bond.


*Approved, July 27, 1842.*

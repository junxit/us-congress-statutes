# Chapter CCIX

> An Act for the relief of the heirs at law of Joshua Fanning, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, required to pay to the heirs at law of Joshua Fanning, an officer in the navy, killed in the service of the United States during the revolutionary war, the aggregate amount of . the half pay of a first lieutenant of a frigate, for seven years, to be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, July 7, 1838.*

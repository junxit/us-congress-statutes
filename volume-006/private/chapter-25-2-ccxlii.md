# Chapter CCXLII

> An Act granting a pension to Sarah Barney, widow of Samuel Barney, late if Connecticut.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be required to place the name of Sarah Barney, widow of Samuel Barney, late of New Haven, Connecticut, deceased, on the pension roll, and pay her forty dollars per annum, during her natural life-time, the payment thereof to commence and take effect on the fourth of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-one.


*Approved, July 7, 1838.*

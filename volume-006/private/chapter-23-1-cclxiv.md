# Chapter CCLXIV

> An Act for the relief of Alwater and Daggett and others, owners of the brig Hannah.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to Atwater and Daggett, Samuel J. Clarke, and Elihu Sanford, owners of the brig Hannah, of New Haven, in the state of Connecticut, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of seventy-one dollars and fifty cents, to reimburse them for tonnage duties paid to the collector of said port, on the twenty-second June eighteen hundred and thirty-one.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

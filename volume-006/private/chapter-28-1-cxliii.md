# Chapter CXLIII

> An Act for the relief of John F. Wiley.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to place the name of John F. Wiley on the roll of invalid pensions, and that he receive four dollars per month from the first day of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-four.


*Approved, March 3, 1843.*

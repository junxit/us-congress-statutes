# Chapter CXLIII

> An Act for the relief of John Heard, junior, surviving assignee of Amasa Davis, junior.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to John Heard, junior, as surviving assignee of Amasa Davis, junior, deceased, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, one thousand dollars, in full for the repayment of the moiety of a penalty erroneously collected from the said Davis by the Marshal of the District of Massachusetts, and by him paid into the treasury of the United States.


*Approved, June 25, 1832.*

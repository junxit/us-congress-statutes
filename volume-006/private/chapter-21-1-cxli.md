# Chapter CXLI

> An Act for the relief of Jacob Wilderman.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury department cause to be paid to Jacob Wilderman, of the state of Illinois, the sum of two hundred and forty dollars, out of any money in the treasury, not otherwise appropriated, in full for the balance of his pay as a mounted ranger, in the company of Captain Short, from the sixteenth day of May, one thousand eight hundred and fourteen, to the fifteenth of May, one thousand eight hundred and fifteen.


*Approved, May 28, 1830.*

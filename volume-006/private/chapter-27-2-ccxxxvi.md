# Chapter CCXXXVI

> An Act for the relief of John Compton, assignee and representative of Garrigues Flaujac.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That a patent shall be issued in the usual form to John Compton, assignee and representative of Garrigues Flaujac, for the land entered by him on the twenty-fourth day of May, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-six, at the Opelousas land office, as a back pre-emption to the tract on Red river, located under the act for the relief of Garrigues Flaujac, in lieu of land on Bayou Gros Tete, confirmed to said Flaujac by act of Congress of the twenty-eighth of February, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-three.


*Approved, August 29, 1842.*

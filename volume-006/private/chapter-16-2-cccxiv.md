# Chapter CCCXIV

> An Act to confirm the title of the heirs of M. de la Carera to a tract of land.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That a gratuitous grant of seven hundred and ninety-eight arpens of land, situated on the Escambia river, in West Florida, made by the Intendant, General Morales, on the fifth of May, one thousand eight hundred and nine, to Milan de la Carera, be, and the same is hereby, confirmed to the heirs or legal representatives of said Carera: Provided, That this confirmation shall only operate as a relinquishment of the title of the United States.


*Approved, July 2, 1836.*

# Chapter X

> An Act for the relief of William Bartlett and John Stearns, owners of the schooner Angler, and Nathaniel Carver, owner of the schooner Harmony, and others.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Collector for the District of Plymouth, in the state of Massachusetts, be, and he is hereby, directed to pay to William Bartlett and John Stearns, owners of a fishing schooner called Angler, of seventy-two tons and fifty-one ninety-fifths of a ton; and to Nathaniel Carver, owner of a fishing vessel called the Harmony, of seventy- nine tons; and to the persons composing the crews of the said vessels, the amount of the allowance to which said vessels would have been entitled, had they returned into port, after completing their fishing terms, to be distributed according to law.


*Approved, January 19, 1824.*

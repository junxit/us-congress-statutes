# Chapter XLIX

> An Act for the relief of Lewis Chacherie.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper officers of the Treasury Department be, and they are hereby authorized and required to pay to Lewis Chacherie the additional sum of fourteen hundred dollars, in consideration of his services as translator of the French and Spanish languages, to the board of commissioners for the western land district of the territory of Orleans, now state of Louisiana, which shall be paid out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 3, 1813.*

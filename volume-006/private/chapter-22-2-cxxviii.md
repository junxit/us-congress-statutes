# Chapter CXXVIII

> An Act for the relief of William Tharp.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to William Tharp, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of nine hundred twenty dollars and eleven cents, the balance of a judgment recovered against him as endorser of a protested draft drawn by Lieutenant Reed, including interest and taxable cost.


*Approved, March 2, 1833.*

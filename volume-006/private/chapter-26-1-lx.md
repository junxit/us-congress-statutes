# Chapter LX

> An Act for the relief of Lyman Bristol.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War is hereby directed to place Lyman Bristol, of the county of Columbia, state of New York, on the pension list, and to pay him a pension of eight dollars per month, commencing on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and thirty-nine, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, July 20, 1840.*

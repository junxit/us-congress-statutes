# Chapter CLXXI

> An Act for the relief of Thaddeus Tuttle.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and required toplace the name of Thaddeus Tuttle upon the list of revolutionary pensioners, and to pay him at the rate of twenty dollars per annum, commencing on the fourth of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-one.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

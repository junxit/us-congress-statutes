# Chapter XCVI

> An Act for the relief of William Garrard.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper officers of the treasury department be, and they are hereby, authorized and required to pay to William Garrard the additional sum of one thousand five hundred dollars, in consideration of his services as a land commissioner in the territory of Orleans; which shall be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 10, 1812.*

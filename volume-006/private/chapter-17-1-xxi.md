# Chapter XXI

> An Act for the relief of Solomon Porter, junior.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to Solomon Porter, junior, the sum of five hundred and eighty-one dollars and forty cents, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, being for the same amount paid by him to the collector of the Fourth Collection District of the state of Connecticut, for duty on two thousand nine hundred and seven gallons of low wines, which the Supreme Court of the United States has decided not to be subject to duty.


*Approved, April 17, 1822.*

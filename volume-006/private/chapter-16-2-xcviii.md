# Chapter XCVIII

> An Act for the relief of William Haskell and others.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the collector of the customs for the port of Marblehead, in the state of Massachusetts, is hereby authorized to pay to William Haskell, owners, master, and crew, of the fishing schooner Friendship, to be distributed according to law, the same said vessel would have been entitled to receive as a bounty or drawback, if she had been actually at sea during the whole time required by law to en-title her to said bounty, she having been lost before she had accomplished the full term required by law.


*Approved, March 3, 1835.*

# Chapter CCXVII

> An Act for the relief of the children and heirs at law of John Chilton, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, directed and required to pay to the children and heirs at law of John Chilton, deceased, who was a captain in the Virginia line on continental establishment, during the war of the Revolution, and who was slain in the battle at Brandywine, in September, seventeen hundred and seventy-seven, the aggregate amount of the half pay of a captain of infantry for seven years, to be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, July 7, 1838.*

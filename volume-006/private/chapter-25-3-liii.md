# Chapter LIII

> An Act for the relief of John E. .Alexander.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to John E. Alexander, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriaated, the sum of one hundred and seventy dollars, for thirty-four days’ services, by direction of the Secretary of State, in pursuing after and arresting persons engaged in counterfeiting the coin of the United States, and bills, and putting them in circulation.


*Approved, March 2, 1839.*

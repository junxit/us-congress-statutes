# Chapter XCI

> An Act for the relief of Martha Flood.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there shall be paid, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Martha Flood, administratrix of William Flood, late of Buckingham county, in the state of Virginia, the sum of one hundred and twenty-two dollars and four cents, being the amount paid by the said William Flood for the duty charged on a boiler used for the boiling of water only, which boiler was not legally subject to duty.


*Approved, May 11, 1820.*

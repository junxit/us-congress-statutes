# Chapter VIII

> An Act for the relief of John G. Camp.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the war department be, and they are hereby authorized, to audit and settle the accounts of John G. Camp, assistant deputy quarter-master general, in such manner, and upon such terms, as may be equitable and just.


*Approved, February 1, 1816.*

# Chapter CCL

> An Act for the relief of Hannah Carrier.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Hannah Carrier, of the county of Oneida, and state of New York, widow of Jeremiah Carrier, and former widow of Guy Dodd, a soldier of the Revolution, be placed upon the pension roll for five years, commencing on the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-six, at the rate of sixty-four dollars and eighty-six cents per annum: Provided, That, in the event of the marriage of said widow, said annuity or pension shall be discontinued.


*Approved, August 29, 1842.*

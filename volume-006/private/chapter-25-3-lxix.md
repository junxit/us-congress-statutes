# Chapter LXIX

> An Act for the relief of John H. Peaxe.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to John II. Pease, of Nantucket, Massachusetts, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of twenty-five dollars; being one-half the amount of a forfeiture incurred by him as master of. a coasting vessel between said Nantucket and New Bedford, in the month of April, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-six, which has been paid into the treasury.


*Approved, March 2, 1839.*

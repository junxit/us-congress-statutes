# Chapter CCLXXVI

> An Act for the relief of Nathaniel Platt.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Nathaniel Platt, out of any money in the treasury, not otherwise appropriated, the sum of two hundred and eighty-three dollars and fifty cents, in full compensation of his claim, for rent, forage, and provisions, furnished the troops under command of General Mooers, in eighteen hundred and fourteen, and for all damages done to the premises of the said Platt, in consequence of the occupancy thereof by the troops aforesaid.


*Approved, July 2, 1836.*

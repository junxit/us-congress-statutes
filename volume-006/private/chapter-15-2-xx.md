# Chapter XX

> An Act for the relief of Samuel F. Hooker.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper officers of the treasury department be, and they are hereby, authorized and required to pay to Samuel F. Hooker the sum of seven hundred and eighty-five dollars; to be paid out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, February 15, 1819.*

# Chapter CLXVIII

> An Act for the relief of Daniel Kleiss.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Daniel Kleiss out of any money not otherwise appropriated, the sum of eighty- three dollars and fifty-two cents, as compensation for his service in the gang of armorers at Key West, from the third of June, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-four, to the thirtieth of April, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-six.


*Approved, August 11, 1842.*

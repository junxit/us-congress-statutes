# Chapter XXXIX

> An Act for the relief of Kenzie and Forsyth.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the accounting officers of the treasury department be authorized and directed to settle the accounts of Kenzie and Forsyth, for three mules and ten horses, which were lost in the public service at the evacuation of Chicago during the late war; and that the sum found due to said Kenzie and Forsyth, be paid to them out of any unappropriated money in the treasury.


*Approved, February 24, 1819.*

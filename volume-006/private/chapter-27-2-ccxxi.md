# Chapter CCXXI

> An Act for the relief of Dorothy Bowman.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to pay to Dorothy Bowman, of Marshall county, Virginia, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, at the rate of eight dollars per month, from the time her late husband, Samuel Bowman, a revolutionary soldier, was stricken from the pension roll, in eighteen hundred and thirty-four, to the twentieth day of February, A. D. eighteen hundred and forty, the time of his death.


*Approved, August 26, 1842.*

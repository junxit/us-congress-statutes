# Chapter XXXVI

> An Act for the relief of Caleb Eddy.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to Caleb Eddy, of Warren, in the state of Rhode Island, out of any money in the treasury not other-wise appropriated, the sum of two hundred and eighty-five dollars and eighty-seven cents, for the amount of drawback of duties on thirty-nine bags of coffee, shipped at the port of Boston in the vessel called the Rasselas, in the month of May in the year one thousand eight hundred and twenty-one.


*Approved, June 4, 1842.*

# Chapter XVIII

> An Act for the relief of Daniel Johnson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the sum of one hundred and ninety-four dollars, be paid to Daniel Johnson, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, as a compensation to him for that amount, which is equitably due to him by the United States.


*Approved, February 5, 1833.*

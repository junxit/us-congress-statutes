# Chapter XLIV

> An Act restoring to the ship Diana the privileges of a sea-letter vessel.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the ship Diana, belonging to John C. and Thomas Vowel, merchants, of Alexandria, be, and she is hereby, restored to all the rights and privileges to which she was entitled, as a sea-letter vessel, prior to the first of March, one thousand eight hundred and seventeen; any thing in the act concerning the navigation of the United States, passed the third [first] of March, one thousand eight hundred and seventeen, to the contrary notwithstanding.


*Approved, May 1, 1822.*

# Chapter CCLVIII

> An Act for the relief of Christopher Brooks.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the sum of one hundred dollars be paid to Christopher Brooks, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the apprehension and prosecution of a negro concerned in robbing the mail, at or near Doak’s stand, in the state of Mississippi, in the month of September, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-seven.


*Approved, July 14, 1832.*

# Chapter CLXX

> An Act for the relief of George Waddle.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to place the name of George Waddle, of Kentucky, on the roll of invalid pensioners, and pay him four dollars per month, to commence on the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and forty.


*Approved, March 3, 1843.*

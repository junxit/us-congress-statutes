# Chapter XXXIV

> An Act for the relief of James Kiser and Barnett Foley, of Missouri.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That James Kiser and Barnett Foley, of Missouri, or their legal representatives, be, and they are hereby, authorized to enter at the land office, at St. Louis, Missouri, at the price of one dollar and twenty-five cents, per acre, the west half of the north-east quarter of section twenty-six, township thirty-nine, range seven, lying in said district; and upon such entry, and the payment of the purchase money, a patent shall issue, as in other cases: Provided, Said entry be made, and the purchase money paid, within six months from the date of this act: And provided, further, That said land has not been sold by the United States before the passage of this act.


*Approved, June 4, 1842.*

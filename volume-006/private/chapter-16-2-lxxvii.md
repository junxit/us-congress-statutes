# Chapter LXXVII

> An Act granting a pension to Isaac Janvier.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, required to place the name of Isaac Janvier of the county of Newcastle, and state of Delaware, upon the roll of invalid pensioners, and pay him the sum of eight dollars per month during his natural life, commencing on the first day of January, in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and thirty-four.


*Approved, March 3, 1835.*

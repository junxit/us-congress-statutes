# Chapter CXXI

> An Act for the relief of John Work.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to John Work, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of two hundred and fifty-nine dollars and forty-nine cents, in full for provisions and forage furnished for several corps of Kentucky militia, and others who volunteered their services and marched for the protection of the Pigeon Roost, and other frontier settlements of Indiana, in September, eighteen hundred and twelve.


*Approved, April 20, 1818.*

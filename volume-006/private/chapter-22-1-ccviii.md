# Chapter CCVIII

> An Act for the relief of George E. Tingle.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the sum of eight hundred and eighty-three dollars and twenty cents be paid by the Secretary of the Treasury to George E. Tingle, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, in full satisfaction for services rendered by him as a clerk in the Executive office of Florida.


*Approved, July 13, 1832.*

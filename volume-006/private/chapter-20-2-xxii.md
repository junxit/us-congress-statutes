# Chapter XXII

> An Act for the relief of Augustus Aspinwall.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to allow the drawback on forty-one boxes of white sugar, laden at Boston, on board the brig Cherub, bound for Gibraltar, which boxes of sugar had been entered by Augustus Aspinwall, at the Custom-House in Boston, for debenture, on the nineteenth day of April, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-eight.


*Approved, February 24, 1829.*

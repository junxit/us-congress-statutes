# Chapter XXXV

> An Act for the relief of Jennings O’Bannon.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury department be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed equitably to settle the account of expenses incurred by Jennings O’Bannon, in a suit erroneously instituted against him on behalf of the United States, on the exhibtion of proper vouchers; and the sum which shall be found due shall be paid to the said Jennings O’Bannon, out of any moneys in the treasury, not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, April 5, 1820.*

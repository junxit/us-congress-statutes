# Chapter CCXXXII

> An Act for the relief of Jamison and Williamson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Postmaster-General be, and he is hereby, authorized to settle the account of Jamison and Williamson, and pay them such sum, out of the funds of the post office department, as in equity and justice they ought to receive for the increased quantity of mail matter thrown upon their mail route in consequence of hostilities with the Creek Indians; and the sum so ascertained to be due is hereby appropriated for the object aforesaid.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

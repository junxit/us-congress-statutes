# Chapter CXXI

> An Act for the relief of Richard Sneed.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to make any arrangement or compromise with Richard Sneed, in the settlement of a judgment had against him, as one of the executors of Stephen Sneed, in the circuit court of the United States for the district oi North Carolina, rendered at May term, one thousand eight hundred and forty-two, upon such terms as he may deem most conducive to the best interest of the United States.


*Approved, June 17, 1844.*

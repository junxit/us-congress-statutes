# Chapter LXVI

> An Act for the relief of Hubert La Croix.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the sum of eleven hundred and fifty dollars be paid, out of the treasury, out of any moneys not otherwise appropriated, to Hubert La Croix, of the Territory of Michigan, in full satisfaction for the destruction of a dwelling-house, the property of said La Croix, by the British and Indians, at Frenchtown, on the river Raisin, on the twenty-third of January, eighteen hundred and thirteen, and, at the time of its destruction, in the military occupation of the United States.


*Approved, April 7, 1830.*

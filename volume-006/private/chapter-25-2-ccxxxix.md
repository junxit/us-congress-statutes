# Chapter CCXXXIX

> An Act for the benefit, of Tandy and Walker.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Tandy Walker on the list of invalid pensioners, at the rate of eight dollars per month, to commence on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and twenty-eight, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, July 7, 1838.*

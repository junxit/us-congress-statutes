# Chapter CXIII

> An Act to extend the patent of Samuel Browning for a further period of fourteen years.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be, and hereby is, granted unto Samuel Browning, a citizen of the United States, his heirs, administrators and assigns, for the term of fourteen years from the twenty-fourth day of November, eighteen hundred and twenty-eight, the full and exclusive right and liberty of making, constructing, using, and vending to others to be used, his improvement called a “magnetic separating machine,” a description of which is given in a schedule annexed to letters patent granted to the said Samuel Browning for the same, on the twenty-fifth day of November, eighteen hundred and fourteen.


*Approved, March 3, 1831.*

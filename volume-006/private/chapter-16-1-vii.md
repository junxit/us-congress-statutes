# Chapter VII

> An Act for the relief of Matthew Barrow.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to Matthew Barrow, out of any unappropriated money in the treasury of the United States, the sum of three hundred and nine dollars and nineteen cents; which sum was expended by the said Barrow in defence of a prosecution at the suit of Absalom Page, for property impressed by him, in and for the service of the United States, during the late war with Great Britain.


*Approved, January 28, 1820.*

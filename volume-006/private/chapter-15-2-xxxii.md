# Chapter XXXII

> An Act for the relief of Henry Davis.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury cause to be paid unto Henry Davis, of Rockingham county, state of North Carolina, the sum of forty-seven dollars and seventy cents, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, February 20, 1819.*

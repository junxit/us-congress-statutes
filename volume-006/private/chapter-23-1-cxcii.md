# Chapter CXCII

> An Act for the relief of William Enos, of Arkansas.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the provisions of an act of Congress approved on the twenty-second day of May, eighteen hundred and twenty-six, entitled “An act authorizing certain soldiers of the late war to surrender the bounty lands drawn by them, and to locate others in lieu thereof,” be, and the same are hereby, extended to William Enos, of Arkansas, a soldier of the late war; and that the first proviso of said act shall be considered inoperative in his case.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

# Chapter LVI

> An Act for the relief of Mary Crawford.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Mary Crawford, widow of David Ross Crawford, late of the United States navy, be placed on the roll of pensioners, and that there be paid to her such sum, as an annual pension, as she would have been entitled to receive had her late husband been a lieutenant in the service at the time of his death.


*Approved, March 1, 1843.*

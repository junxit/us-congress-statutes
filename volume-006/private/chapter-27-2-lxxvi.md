# Chapter LXXVI

> An Act for the relief of Sarah Besly, widow of William Besly, deceased, and, previous thereto, widow of Doctor Henry Adams.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Sarah Besly, widow of William Besly, and, previous thereto, of Doctor Henry Adams, a surgeon in the army of the revolution, be placed on the pension roll, and that she be allowed the benefit of the act of July seventh, eighteen hundred and thirty-eight, in the same manner, form, and extent, as if she had never married the second time, and had been the widow of said Doctor Henry Adams on the passage of said act.


*Approved, July 27, 1842.*

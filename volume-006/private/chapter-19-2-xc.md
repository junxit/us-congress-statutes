# Chapter XC

> An Act for the relief of J. Balastier and Company, Peter Harmony, and W. W. Russel.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Collector of the port of New York be, and he is hereby, authorized to permit J. Balastier and Company, Peter Harmony, and William W. Russel, merchants of New York, to export, with benefit of drawback, certain cases or packages of Spanish playing cards, by them severally imported into that port, in the year eighteen hundred and twenty-five; the said J. Balastier and Company, Peter Harmony, and William W. Russel, complying with the forms and requisites of the existing acts, allowing the benefit of drawback.


*Approved, March 3, 1827.*

# Chapter CCLII

> An Act for the relief of Lieutenant James L. Dawson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to Lieutenant James L. Dawson, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one hundred dollars and eleven cents, the balance due to him by the United States “for surveying and making a road from Little Rock to Cantonment Gibson.”


*Approved, July 14, 1832.*

# Chapter CLXII

> An Act to revive an act entitled “ An act for the relief of John Davlin.”.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the act entitled “ An act for the relief of John Davlin,” approved on the. second day of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-nine, be, and the same is hereby, revived in favor of the said John Davlin, or his legal representatives, and shall continue in force twelve months from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, March 3, 1843.*

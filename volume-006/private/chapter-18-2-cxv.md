# Chapter CXV

> An Act for the relief of Moses Shepherd.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to Moses Shepherd, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of thirtythree thousand six hundred and seventy-four dollars and thirty-six cents, in full for the balance due him.


*Approved, March 3, 1825.*

# Chapter LVII

> An Act for the relief of William Allen.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Navy place the name of William Allen, of Portland, in the state of Maine, on the roll of invalid pensioners, at the rate of five dollars per month, to commence on the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-nine, and to be continued so long as said Allen’s disability shall continue to be total.


*Approved, March 1, 1843.*

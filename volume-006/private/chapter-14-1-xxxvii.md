# Chapter XXXVII

> An Act placing certain persons on the list of Navy Pensioners.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to place on the list of navy pensioners those persons who were wounded at Dartmoor prison, in England, in the month of April, one thousand eight hundred and fifteen; also the widows and children of such as were killed, or who died in consequence of wounds received there; and that, in the allowance of pensions to the persons aforesaid, the regulations established by law, in relation to the placing persons on the list of navy pensioners, be observed.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That this act shall be construed to take effect from the sixth day of April, in the year one thousand eight hundred and fifteen.


*Approved, April 2, 1816.*

# Chapter CCXLIV

> An Act granting a pension to Amaziah Goodwin.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Navy cause the name of Amaziah Goodwin, of the state of Maine, to be placed upon the roll of invalid pensioners, and pay to him six dollars per month during his natural life, commencing on the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and forty.


*Approved, August 29, 1842.*

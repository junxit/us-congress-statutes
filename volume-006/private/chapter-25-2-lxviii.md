# Chapter LXVIII

> An Act for the relief of Melancthon T. Woolsey.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the accounting officers of the Navy Department settle the accounts of Melancthon T. Woolsey, on the principle of justice and equity, subject to the supervision of the Secretary of the Navy.


*Approved, April 20, 1838.*

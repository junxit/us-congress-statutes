# Chapter LXII

> An Act for the relief of Michael Jones.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there shall be paid to Michael Jones, of the Illinois territory, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one thousand five hundred dollars, in full compensation for extra services performed, and clerk hire expended, by him while acting as register of the land office and commissioner of claims to land in the district of Kaskaskia.


*Approved, April 16, 1818.*

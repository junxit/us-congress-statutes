# Chapter XIV

> An Act for the relief of William Cloyd.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, directed to pay, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to William Cloyd, the sum of ninety-six dollars, the amount of a fine improperly assessed and paid by him, for not performing a tour of duty in the military service of the United States during the late war.


*Approved, March 10, 1828.*

# Chapter XIII

> An Act for the relief of Robert Haile.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and is hereby, authorized to issue to Robert Haile a land warrant for three hundred and twenty acres of land in lieu of warrant number sixteen, issued on the sixth March eighteen hundred and seven, in favor of Thomas F. Howard under the act of third March, eighteen hundred and seven, entitled “An act making compensation to Messrs. Lewis and Clark and their companions,” which warrant was afterwards assigned to Robert Haile, and appears to have been lost.


*Approved, January 27, 1835.*

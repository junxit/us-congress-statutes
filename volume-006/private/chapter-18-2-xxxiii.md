# Chapter XXXIII

> An Act granting certain rights to David Tate, Josiah Fletcher, and John Weatherford.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That all right, title, and interest, which the United States possess, in each of the reservations severally made to David Tate, Josiah Fletcher, and John Weatherford, of lands within the tract of country ceded to the United States by the treaty of Fort Jackson, of the ninth day of August, eighteen hundred and fourteen, with the Creek Nation of Indians, be, and the same is hereby, vested in each occupant and claimant of the tract so reserved and occupied and claimed by him: Provided, That no one claim shall exceed the quantity of six hundred and forty acres.


*Approved, March 3, 1825.*

# Chapter VIII

> An Act for the relief of William B. Lewis.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury department be, and they are hereby, authorized to audit and settle the account of William B. Lewis, assistant deputy quarter-master general to the Tennessee militia and volunteers under the command of Major-General Jackson, in such manner, and upon such terms, as may appear reasonable and just.


*Approved, January 8, 1819.*

# Chapter CCI

> An Act for the relief of the legal representatives of James Brown.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized to pay to the legal representatives of James Brown the sum of four thousand one hundred and forty-nine dollars sixty-six and two-third cents, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

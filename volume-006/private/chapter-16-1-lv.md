# Chapter LV

> An Act for the relief of John Steele.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That, for the discharge of a judgment obtained against John Steele, collector of Philadelphia, on account of his refusal to grant a clearance for the Spanish brig Los Dos Amigos, in which refusal the said collector acted under an instruction from the department of state, a sum, not exceeding three thousand three hundred dollars, be, and the same is hereby appropriated; to be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, May 1, 1820.*

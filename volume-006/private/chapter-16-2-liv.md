# Chapter LIV

> An Act granting a pension to Thomas Morton.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Thomas Morton, of the county of Gibson, in the state of Tennessee, on the roll of invalid pensioners, and to pay him eight dollars per month during his natural life, commencing on the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-four.


*Approved, March 3, 1835.*

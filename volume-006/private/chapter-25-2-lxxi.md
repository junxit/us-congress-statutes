# Chapter LXXI

> An Act for the relief of Jonathan Eliot.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay, out of any unappropriated money, to Jonathan Eliot, the sum of four hundred and forty-four dollars and forty cents, in full, for hay, grain, and other articles furnished by him to the American troops under General Hampton, in Canada, in the month of October, eighteen hundred and thirteen.


*Approved, April 20, 1838.*

# Chapter IX

> An Act for the relief of Jesse E. Dow.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Jesse E. Dow, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, one hundred dollars, being one moiety of the money paid to the United States, by reason of criminal proceedings against A. Baker, alias William A. Chelsey, in the United States district court in the district of Massachusetts, on the complaint and prosecution of the said Jesse E. Dow, for violating the act of April thirtieth, seventeen hundred and ninety, entitled “An act for the punishment of certain crimes against the United States.”


*Approved, February 22, 1838.*

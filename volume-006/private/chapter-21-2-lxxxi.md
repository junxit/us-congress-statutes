# Chapter LXXXI

> An Act to authorize the executor of Stephen Tippett to locate a tract of land in the state of Louisiana.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the legal representatives of Stephen Tippett be, and they are hereby, authorized to locate and enter a tract of land of fifteen arpens front, by the ordinary depth of forty arpens, within the counties of Attakapas and Opelousas, in the state of Louisiana, under a grant of Baron de Carondelet of the twenty-first of September, one thousand seven hundred and ninety-six, Provided, however, That the location authorized to be made under this act shall not be made so as to interfere with the claims of others.


*Approved, March 2, 1831.*

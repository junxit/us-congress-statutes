# Chapter LXXX

> An Act for the relief of Henry Welk.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Henry Wells, of the state of New York, on the roll of invalid pensioners, and pay him four dollars per month, commencing on the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-eight, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, July 27, 1842.*

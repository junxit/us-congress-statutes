# Chapter CXLII

> An Act for the relief of Samuel Hunt.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, directed to place the name of Samuel Hunt, of Oakham, in the county of Worcester, in the state of Massachusetts, on the roll of invalid pensioners, and pay to him eight dollars per month, during his natural life, commencing on the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-four.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

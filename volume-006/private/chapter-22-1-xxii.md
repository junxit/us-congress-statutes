# Chapter XXII

> An Act for the relief of Ariel Ensign.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay Ariel Ensign, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of two hundred and two dollars, in full satisfaction of all improvements made by the said Ariel Ensign, on a certain lot in the city of Hartford, and state of Connecticut, heretofore belonging to the United States.


*Approved, February 18, 1832.*

# Chapter LIX

> An Act placing Captain Cole, a Seneca Indian chief, on the pension roll.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be ordered to place the name of Captain Cole, a warrior of the Onondaga tribe of Indians, on the pension roll, at the rate of eight dollars per month, to commence on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and thirty-five.


*Approved, March 3, 1835.*

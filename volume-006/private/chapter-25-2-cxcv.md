# Chapter CXCV

> An Act for the relief of Isaac Gale.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Isaac Gale on the pension list of invalid pensioners of the United States; and that the said Isaac Gale be entitled to receive a pension, at the rate of six dollars a month, from the first of January, eighteen hundred and thirty-eight.


*Approved, July 7, 1838.*

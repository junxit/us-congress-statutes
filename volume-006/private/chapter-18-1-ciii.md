# Chapter CIII

> An Act for the relief of the legal representatives of Charles Bradford.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be authorized, and he is hereby directed, to pay to the legal representatives of Charles Bradford, late an invalid pensioner of the United States, the pension allowed him from the fourth of March, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-one, the time he was last paid, up to the first September, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-two, the time he died.


*Approved, May 19, 1824.*

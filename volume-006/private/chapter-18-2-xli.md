# Chapter XLI

> An Act for the relief of Joseph Decrits.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, directed to settle and adjust the accounts of Joseph Decrits, who served as a volunteer artillerist in the army of the United States, during the fall of the year one thousand eight hundred and thirteen; and that they allow him the pay of a sergeant for the time he may prove he was in the service.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the amount so found due be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 3, 1825.*

# Chapter LXXVII

> An Act for the relief of the sureties of Joseph Pettipool.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury department be, and they are hereby, authorized to audit and settle the accounts of Joseph Pettipool, late paymaster of the twentieth regiment of United States infantry, so far as respects the items of his account which were suspended at his last settlement, according to the principles of equity.


*Approved, May 7, 1822.*

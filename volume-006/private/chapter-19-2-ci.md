# Chapter CI

> An Act for the relief of Thomas Collins.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Thomas Collins be exonerated from the payment of two hundred dollars sixty-seven cents, a balance charged against him by the accounting officers of the treasury department, in the settlement of his accounts as captain in the service of the United States, in the late war, and that a judgment recovered against him, for the same, in the District Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania, be released.


*Approved, March 3, 1827.*

# Chapter XXIII

> An Act granting a pension to Lemuel White.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War place the name of Lemuel White on the roll of revolutionary pensioners, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars a year, from the fourth day of March, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-one, during his life.


*Approved, March 2, 1841.*

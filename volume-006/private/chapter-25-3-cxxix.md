# Chapter CXXIX

> An Act for the relief of John Plane.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Commissioner of Patents be, and he is hereby, empowered to issue to John Blanc, a native of France, but now a citizen of the United States, a new patent for a machine, the invention of said Blanc, called a “Marsh Drainer,” (said Blanc having obtained a patent therefor on the second day of July, anno Domini eighteen hundred and thirty-six, which patent was destroyed by fire on the eighth day of May, anno Domini eighteen hundred and thirty-seven,) in the same manner and under the same regulations as provided by law had said patent been destroyed prior to the fifteenth day of December, eighteen hundred and thirty-six.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

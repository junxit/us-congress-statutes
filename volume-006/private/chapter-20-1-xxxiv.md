# Chapter XXXIV

> An Act for the relief of John Shirkey.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place John Shirkey on the pension list of military pensioners of the United States, who shall be entitled to, and shall receive, a pension, at the rate of five dollars thirty-three and one-third cents per month, to commence on the nineteenth day of February, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-seven.


*Approved, April 17, 1828.*

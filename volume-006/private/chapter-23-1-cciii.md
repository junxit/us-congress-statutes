# Chapter CCIII

> An Act for the relief of Ephraim Whitaker.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Ephraim Whitaker, five years’ full pay as a captain in the revolutionary war; which five years’ full pay is the commutation of half pay for life.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

# Chapter LXVI

> An Act for the relief of Joseph Summers and John Allen.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Joseph Summers and John Allen be, and they are hereby, authorized to withdraw their respective erroneous entries made in the district of Vincennes; and the moneys paid by them on their said entries shall be placed to their credit on any purchase of public land they may have made or shall make.


*Approved, March 3, 1817.*

# Chapter XCV

> An Act for the relief of Davis Floyd.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury department be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to settle the accounts of Davis Floyd, upon the principles of justice and equity, and allow him such credits as shall be found due him, not exceeding the amount with which he stands charged on the books, exclusive of his pay as Deputy Quartermaster-General.


*Approved, May 20, 1826.*

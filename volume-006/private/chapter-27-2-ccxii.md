# Chapter CCXII

> An Act for the relief of John King.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to John King, or his legal representatives, the sum of nine hundred dollars, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the same being for a dwelling-house which was burnt by the British, in Richmond county, Virginia, during the late war.


*Approved, August 26, 1842.*

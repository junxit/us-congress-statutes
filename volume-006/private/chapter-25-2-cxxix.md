# Chapter CXXIX

> An Act for the relief of Solomon Ketcham.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of .War cause the name of Solomon Ketcham to be restored to the roll of revolutionary pensioners, and that he be paid all arrears due him for his pension since he was last paid , and that he be paid, for and during his natural life, pension at the rate of twenty-four dollars per annum.


*Approved, June 28, 1838.*

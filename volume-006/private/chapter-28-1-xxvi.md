# Chapter XXVI

> An Act for the relief of Levi Colmus.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be authorized, empowered, and directed, and he is hereby authorized, empowered, and directed, to place on the invalid pension roll of the United States the name of Levi Colmus; and to pay to him, from and after the passage of this act, four dollars per month, for and during his natural life.


*Approved, May 23, 1844.*

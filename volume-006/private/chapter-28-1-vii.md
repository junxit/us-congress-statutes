# Chapter VII

> An Act for the relief of Marie Simpson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be authorized and directed to place the name of Mark Simpson on the roll of invalid pensioners, and that he be paid a pension, at the rate of six dollars per month, during his life, to commence on the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and forty-four.


*Approved, February 13, 1845.*

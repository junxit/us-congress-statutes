# Chapter CXX

> An Act for the relief of Joseph Shaw.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, directed to cause Joseph Shaw, a revolutionary pensioner of the United States, to be paid at the rate of eight dollars per month, from the tenth day of April, one thousand eight hundred and eighteen, (the date of his first declaration under the act, entitled “An act to provide for certain persons engaged in the land and naval service of the United States in the revolutionary war,” approved the eighteenth of March, one thousand eight hundred and eighteen,) up to the eleventh day of October, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-seven, the day on which his pension was allowed to commence under the regulations of the department of war.


*Approved, May 28, 1830.*

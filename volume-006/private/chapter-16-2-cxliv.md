# Chapter CXLIV

> An Act for the relief of Richard Martin.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, required to place upon the roll of revolutionary pensioners, the name of Richard Martin, at the rate of eighty dollars per annum, to commence on the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-one.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

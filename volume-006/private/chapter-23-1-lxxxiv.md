# Chapter LXXXIV

> An Act granting further relief to Joel Byington.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay, out of any moneys therein not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one hundred and forty-eight dollars and one cent to Joel Byington, of Chazy, in the state of New York, in full of his demand for damages done to his property by the occupation thereof by troops of the United States during the late war.


*Approved, June 26, 1834.*

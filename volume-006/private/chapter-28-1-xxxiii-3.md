# Chapter XXXIII

> An Act for the relief of Samuel Neely.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Samuel Neely, of the state of Pennsylvania, on the pension roll, and to pay him at the rate of six dollars per month, from and after the fourth day of September last.


*Approved, March 1, 1845.*

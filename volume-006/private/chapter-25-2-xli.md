# Chapter XLI

> An Act for the relief of Samuel Sanderson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Samuel Sanderson, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, the sum of three thousand and twenty-five dollars, in full compensation for all the costs, services, and charges of taking one hundred and twenty-one Africans from the Spanish brig Guerrero, on the Florida reef, and delivering them to the collector at Key West, in the month of December, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-seven.


*Approved, March 28, 1838.*

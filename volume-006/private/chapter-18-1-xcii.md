# Chapter XCII

> An Act for the relief of Mareen Duval.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby authorized and directed to pay Mareen Duval, or his representative, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of fifty-seven dollars eighteen cents, in full for provisions purchased by said Duval, of Joseph Harris, in the year one thousand eight hundred and twelve, and furnished to a regiment of Ohio militia, at their rendezvous, at Steubenville, commanded by said Duval.


*Approved, May 18, 1824.*

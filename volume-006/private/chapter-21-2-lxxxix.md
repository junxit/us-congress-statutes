# Chapter LXXXIX

> An Act for the relief of Samuel Nowell.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War cause to be paid to Samuel Nowell, of New Hampshire, a pension of eight dollars per month during his natural life, commmencing on the fourth day of March, one thousand eight hundred and thirty.


*Approved, March 2, 1831.*

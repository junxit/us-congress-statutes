# Chapter CXCVII

> An Act for the relief of Nathaniel Mitchell.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Nathaniel Mitchel] the sum of two hundred and seventy-five dollars out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to correct an error in the amount appropriated in a bill passed the present session for the relief of said Mitchell.


*Approved, August 23, 1842.*

# Chapter CCV

> An Act for the relief of Benjamin Coit.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be authorized and required to place the name of Benjamin Coit on the roll of revolutionary pensioners, and that he be paid the sum of fifty-three dollars and thirty-three cents per annum, commencing on the fourth day of March, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-one, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

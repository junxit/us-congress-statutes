# Chapter CXX

> An Act for the relief of the owners and crew of the schooner “ Privado.”.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the collector of the district of Portsmouth, in the state of New Hampshire, is hereby authorized to pay to the owners and crew of the schooner “ Privado,” for the fishing season of the year one thousand eight hundred and thirty-nine, to be distributed according to law, the same sum of money they would have been entitled to, as bounty or allowance, if she had not foundered on her second fare, in June, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-nine, but had complied with all the requirements of law to secure said bounty; said vessel being of the burden of fifty-five and fifty-eight ninety-fifths tons, and having sailed last from Portsmouth on the seventeenth day of said June.


*Approved, June 17, 1844.*

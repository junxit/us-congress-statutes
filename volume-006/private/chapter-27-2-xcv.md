# Chapter XCV

> An Act for the relief of Sarah Moore, of the state of Maryland.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place upon the pension roll the name of Sarah Moore, of the city of Baltimore, in the state of Maryland, widow of the late Nicholas Ruxton Moore, a captain of cavalry in the army of the revolution; and that there be allowed to the said Sarah Moore five years full pay of a captain, to be paid according to the provisions of the act of seventh July, eighteen hundred and thirty-eight, allowing pensions to certain widows.


*Approved, July 27, 1842.*

# Chapter X

> An Act for the relief of Francis Duchouquet.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to Francis Duchouquet, out of any moneys in the treasury, not otherwise appropriated, the sum of two hundred and ninety-one dollars, and eighty-four cents, in full compensation for moneys by him advanced to redeem certain American citizens captured by the Indians.


*Approved, March 16, 1802.*

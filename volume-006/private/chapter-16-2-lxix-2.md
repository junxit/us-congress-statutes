# Chapter LXIX

> An Act for the relief [of] Israel Ingalls.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, required to place the name of Israel Ingalls, of Hamilton county, Ohio, on the roll of revolutionary pensioners; and to pay him a pension, at the rate of twenty dollars per annum, to commence, and be computed, from the fourth day of March, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-one.


*Approved, May 14, 1836.*

# Chapter CXCIII

> An Act for the relief of Jacob Wallace.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, instructed to place the name of Jacob Wallace, of New York, upon the pension roll of revolutionary soldiers: and that he cause to be paid to said Jacob Wallace the sum of twenty-five dollars per annum during his natural life, commencing on the fourth day of March, anno Domini one thousand eight hundred and thirty-one.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

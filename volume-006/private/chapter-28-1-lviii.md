# Chapter LVIII

> An Act for the relief of Thomas Bronaugh.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be directed and required to place the name of Thomas Bronough, of the state of Indiana, on the roll of invalid pensioners, at the rate of four dollars per month, from the twelfth day of April, eighteen hundred and forty-four, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, March 3, 1845.*

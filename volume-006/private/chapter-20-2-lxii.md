# Chapter LXII

> An Act for the relief of Thomas Hunt.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers pay unto Thomas Hunt, or his legal representatives, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of five hundred and five dollars and sixty-seven cents, in full for his pay and subsistence, as an adjutant, with the pay and rank of a lieutenant, from the twenty-first of July, one thousand eight hundred and twelve, to thirtieth April, one thousand eight hundred and thirteen.


*Approved, March 3, 1829.*

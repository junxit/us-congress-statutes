# Chapter LXI

> An Act for the relief of Robert M’Calla and Matthew H. Jouett.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury department be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to settle the accounts of Robert M’Calla, late a major in the service of the United States, and Matthew H. Jouett, late paymaster to the twenty-eighth regiment of infantry, upon the principles of equity and justice: Provided, That it shall appear that the said Robert M’Calla and Matthew H. Jouett have not been guilty of any misconduct or default in failing to render their accounts for settlement.


*Approved, March 3, 1819.*

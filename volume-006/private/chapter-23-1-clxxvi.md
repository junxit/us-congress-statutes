# Chapter CLXXVI

> An Act to amend, an act entitled “An act for the relief of William Burris, of Mississippi,” approved February nineteen, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-one.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That William Burris be authorized to relinquish to the United States his title to the east half of the north-west quarter of section twenty-one, township three, range six east, instead of the east half of the south-west quarter of section twenty-one, township three, range six east, as authorized by the act to which this is an amendment.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

# Chapter CCLXXXVII

> An Act for the relief of Bartholomew Shaumburgh.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to Bartholomew Shaumburgh, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one thousand three hundred and seventeen dollars and twenty-eight cents; being the amount of advances made by him for the pay of soldiers of the army of the United States.


*Approved, July 14, 1832.*

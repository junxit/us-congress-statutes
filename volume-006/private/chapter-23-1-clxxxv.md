# Chapter CLXXXV

> An Act for the relief of Samuel D. Walker.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby authorized to pay to Samuel D. Walker, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of sixty-three dollars and six cents, being the amount of drawback on forty seroons Spanish tobacco, exported per ship Guinare to Valparaiso, and payable to said Samuel D. Walker.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

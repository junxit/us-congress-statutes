# Chapter XCVII

> An Act for the settlement of the accounts of Edmund Roberts, late diplomatic agent of the United. Stales to Cochin China, Muscat and Siam.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be authorized to allow to the legal representatives of the late Edmund Roberts the salary of a chargé d’affaires, without the outfit, during the time he was employed as diplomatic agent of the United States, under his appointment of January twenty-seventh, eighteen hundred and thirty- two, deducting the per diem allowance already paid, and that the same be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, which shall be in full of all demands.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

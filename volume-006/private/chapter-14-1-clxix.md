# Chapter CLXIX

> An Act for the relief of Peter Audrain.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to Peter Audrain, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one thousand dollars, upon his executing to the United States a receipt in full discharge for his claim for services as clerk and interpreter to the board of commissioners at Detroit, from the first of July, one thousand eight hundred and nine, to the first of July, one thousand eight hundred and eleven.


*Approved, April 30, 1816.*

# Chapter CCXLVIII

> An Act for the relief of F. B. De Bellevue.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to place the name of F. B. De Bellevue on the roll of invalid pensioners and pay to the said De Bellevue a pension at the rate of ten dollars per month, for disability consequent upon a wound in the Hand while said De Bellevue was in the discharge of his duty as second lieutenant of marines.


*Approved, August 29, 1842.*

# Chapter XXXVIII

> An Act for the relief of Messrs. Smith anti Town.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the collector of the customs for the district of New York is authorized to allow and pay the drawback on twenty-one hundred and eight bags of sugar, exported by Messrs. Smith and Town, on the eighth day of July, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-six, in the Prussian brig Die Oder, from New York to Antwerp, notwithstanding the omission of the inspector’s return, as required by law.


*Approved, March 2, 1839.*

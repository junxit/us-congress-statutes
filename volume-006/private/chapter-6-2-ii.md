# Chapter II

> An Act for the relief of Solomon Boston.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That Solomon Boston, now confined in the public gaol of Kent county in the State of Delaware, be released and discharged, and he hereby is released and discharged from all claim and demand of the United States, to a certain judgment, for a penalty of one thousand dollars, recovered at their suit in the District Court of the District of Delaware; saving and reserving, nevertheless, the right and interest which belongs to any third person, if any such exist.


*Approved, January 30, 1801.*

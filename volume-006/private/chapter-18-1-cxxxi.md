# Chapter CXXXI

> An Act for the relief of John S. Moffitt.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, authorized to settle and adjust the accounts of John S. Maffitt, late a collector of the third collection district of the state of Maryland, and to allow him commissions on all sums on which commissions had not been allowed.


*Approved, May 21, 1824.*

# Chapter CXIII

> An Act authorizing a patent to be issued to Bartholomew Pellerin, his heirs or assigns.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Commissioner of the General Land Office be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to issue a patent to Bartholomew Pellerin, or to his heirs and assigns, in right of widow Masmer or Asmard, for his claim to a tract of land situate at the bay of St. Louis, in the state of Mississippi, containing seventeen thousand and eighty-four superficial arpens, according to a survey of the same approved by Vincente Sebastien Pintado, on the thirtieth of January, eighteen hundred and ten, the same having been confirmed by the act of the third of March, eighteen hundred and nineteen: Provided, That the same shall only operate as a relinquishment of the right of the United States, and shall not affect the right of third persons.


*Approved, August 1, 1842.*

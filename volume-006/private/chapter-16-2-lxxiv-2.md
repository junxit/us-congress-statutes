# Chapter LXXIV

> An Act for the relief of Peter Doxtator and Jacob Weaver.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be authorized and required to place the names of Peter Doxtator and Jacob Weaver, on the list of revolutionary pensioners, and pay to each of them the sum of eighty dollars, per annum, commencing on the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-one, and to continue during their natural lives.


*Approved, May 14, 1836.*

# Chapter CX

> An Act for the relief of Jonas Duncan.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to Jonas Duncan, or to his legal representative, out of any money in the treasury, not otherwise appropriated, the sum of sixty dollars, in full compensation for the use of a house owned by the said Duncan, and occupied by the officers of the United States during the late war with Great Britain.


*Approved, May 19, 1824.*

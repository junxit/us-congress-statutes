# Chapter LXXXVI

> An Act to extend the patent of Robert Eastman for a further period of seven years.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be, and hereby is, granted unto Robert Eastman, a citizen of the United States, his heirs, administrators, and assigns, for the term of seven years from the fifteenth day of March, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-four, the full and exclusive right and liberty of making, constructing, using, and vending to others to be used, an invention called a “Circular Saw Clap-board Machine,” a description of which is given in a schedule annexed to letters patent granted to the said Robert Eastman and Josiah Jaquith for the same, on the sixteenth day of March, one thousand eight hundred and twenty.


*Approved, March 3, 1835.*

# Chapter XXIX

> An Act for the relief of James C. Hallock.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, directed to place the name of James C. Hallock, of’Dutchess county’ New York, on the pension list of invalid pensioners of the United States; and that the said James C. Hallock be entitled to receive a pension, at the rate of eight dollars per month, from the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-six, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, May 23, 1844.*

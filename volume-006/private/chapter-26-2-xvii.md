# Chapter XVII

> An Act granting a pension to Hannah Leighton.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War place the name of Hannah Leighton on the roll of revolutionary pensioners, and pay her a pension at the rate of eighty dollars a year, from the fourth day of March, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-one, during her life.


*Approved, March 2, 1841.*

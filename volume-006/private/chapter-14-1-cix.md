# Chapter CIX

> An Act concerning Pharaoh Farrow and others.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the judge of the Albemarle district court in the state of North Carolina be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to distribute to Pharaoh Farrow, and his associates, the proceeds arising from the sale of two armed boats belonging to the British privateer Mars; which boats, with their tackle and apparel, were captured by the said Pharaoh Farrow, and his associates, on the nineteenth day of October, in the year one thousand eight hundred and thirteen, in New-Inlet, in the said state; and which, with their tackle and apparel, have been condemned and sold by a decree of the court, the proceeds of which sale are held to the benefit of the United States; and that the said distribution be made agreeably to the laws of the United States, relating to captures made by private armed ships.


*Approved, April 27, 1816.*

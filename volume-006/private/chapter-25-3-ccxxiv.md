# Chapter CCXXIV

> An Act for the relief of William Traverse.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to William Traverse, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, two hundred and twenty-eight dollars, for ten head of cattle killed, the property of said Traverse, in the month of February or March, eighteen hundred and thirty-six, by order of Major Mark A. Cooper, or with his knowledge and approbation, for the subsistence of a battalion of Georgia volunteers under his command, in the service of the United States, then deficient in provisions, near the Saint John’s, in the Territory of Florida.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

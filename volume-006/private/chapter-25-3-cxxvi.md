# Chapter CXXVI

> An Act for the relief of Philip Catner.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Philip Catner be, and he is hereby, authorized to relinquish to the United States, in such form as the Commissioner or the General Land Office may prescribe, the north-east quarter of the south-east quarter of section number twenty-one, in township number eleven south, of range number three west, in the district of land offered for sale at Kaskaskia, Illinois; and upon such relinquishment being made, as aforesaid, the said Philip Catner shall be, and he is hereby, authorized to enter any other quarter-quarter section, containing not more than forty acres, in the district of lands subject to sale at Kaskaskia, in the state of Illinois, subject to private entry.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

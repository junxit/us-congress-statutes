# Chapter XII

> An Act for the relief of Henry G. Rice.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Collector of the district of Boston and Charlestown be, and he is hereby, authorized to allow Henry G. Rice the amount of debentures on a quantity of colored cottons, exported by him, in the fall of the year one thousand eight hundred and nineteen, in the brig Franklin, for Leghorn, in the same manner as if the export oath had been taken within the time then required by law: Provided, That said collector shall be satisfied that all the other requirements of the law were complied with by said Rice, so as to entitle him to the drawback on the cottons aforesaid.


*Approved, March 10, 1828.*

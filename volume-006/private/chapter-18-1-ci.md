# Chapter CI

> An Act for the relief of Joshua Bennett.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury department be directed to pay to Joshua Bennett, of Kentucky, the sum of sixty dollars, in full for the value of a horse lost by said Bennett on the north-western frontier, during the late war, and that the same be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, May 19, 1824.*

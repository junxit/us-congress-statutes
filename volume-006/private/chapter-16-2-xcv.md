# Chapter XCV

> An Act for the relief of Henry Awkward.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Henry Awkward, of the city of Washington, the sum of four hundred dollars, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 3, 1835.*

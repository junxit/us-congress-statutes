# Chapter CXLI

> An Act for the relief of Jesse F. Royston, of Missouri.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to pay to Jesse F. Royston, of Missouri, the sum of eighty dollars, being the value of a horse lost by said Royston, while in the service of the United States, during the late war, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, May 20, 1826.*

# Chapter CCXXII

> An Act for the relief of William Marcus, of Arkansas.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That William Marcus be, and he is hereby, authorized to enter one quarter section of land, by legal subdivisions, of any of the vacant unappropriated and unoccupied public lands in the state of Arkansas, at the price of one dollar and twenty-five cents per acre, in the proper land office.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

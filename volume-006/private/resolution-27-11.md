# Resolution 11

> Joint Resolution to authorize the Postmaster General to settle the accounts of Patton Pitcher and Company.

*Be it resolved, &c.,*

That the Postmaster General be, and he is hereby, directed to allow Patton Pilcher and Company, contractors on mail route number three thousand seven hundred and eighteen, from Mobile to New Orleans, the amount which, in his discretion, he may deem just and equitable, not exceeding the amount they would have received if they had performed the service, the said contractors having been prevented from carrying the mail for twenty-three days by the interference of the postmaster at New Orleans.


*Approved, August 31, 1842.*

# Chapter LXXXIX

> An Act for the relief of Daniel Homans.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to pay to Daniel Homans two hundred dollars, being a balance due to him on his contract for building a marine hospital at Charleston, South Carolina, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, July 27, 1842.*

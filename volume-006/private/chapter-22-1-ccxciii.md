# Chapter CCXCIII

> An Act for the relief of Henry Waller.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Third Auditor of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to ascertain the value of the buildings of Henry Waller, which were destroyed by fire by the enemy during the late war, on the eastern shore of Maryland; and that the amount thereof, when so ascertained, be paid to the said petitioner out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, July 14, 1832.*

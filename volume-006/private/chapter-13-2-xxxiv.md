# Chapter XXXIV

> An Act for the relief of Mary Philip Le Due.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the sum of six hundred dollars be paid out of any moneys in the treasury, not otherwise appropriated, to Mary Philip Le Due, as an additional allowance for his services as translator to the board of land commissioners at St. Louis, and as a full compensation therefor.


*Approved, March 28, 1814.*

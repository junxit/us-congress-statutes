# Chapter CCXVII

> An Act for the relief of John Flood.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to place the name of John Flood upon the list of invalid pensioners, at the rate of four dollars per month, commencing on the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and forty, and pay him out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, August 26, 1842.*

# Chapter CXXXIX

> An Act for the relief of Isaac Conly.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, directed to place the name of Isaac Conly, of Tennessee, upon the pen sion roll, and pay him the sum of six dollars per month during life; commencing on the first day of January, anno Domini eighteen hundred and thirty-eight.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

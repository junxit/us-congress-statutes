# Chapter XXXIX

> An Act for the relief of Hyacinth Bernard.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Hyacinth Bernard be, and he is hereby, confirmed in his claim to thirty-three arpents of land, by forty arpents in depth, on both sides of the Bayou Teche, in the state of Louisiana, to be surveyed and taken according to the plot of survey made by James L. Johnson, on the twenty-first of November, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-three, and as recommended for confirmation by the Commissioner of the Land Office, to whom it was presented: Provided, That this act shall only be construed to a relinquishment on the part of the United States, and shall not interfere with the rights of third persons.


*Approved, March 23, 1830.*

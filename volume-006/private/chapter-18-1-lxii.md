# Chapter LXII

> An Act for the relief of Thaddeus Mayhew.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, directed to pay to Thaddeus Mayhew, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of seven hundred and fifteen dollars, for property taken for the use of the American troops at New Orleans.


*Approved, May 5, 1824.*

# Chapter XXXV

> An Act for the relief of Abner Stilson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, directed to pay to Abner Stilson, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of sixty dollars, for carrying an express from the settlers of Elkhart, in the state of Indiana, to Governor Noble, and for carrying his reply back to the said settlers, during the Indian disturbances, in the year eighteen hundred and thirty-two.


*Approved, February 17, 1836.*

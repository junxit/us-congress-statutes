# Chapter CXIV

> An Act for the relief of John Edgar, of Illinois.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officer of the treasury department be, and he is hereby, authorized to pay to General John Edgar,, heretofore an acting Captain in the Navy, during the revolutionary war, the same sum, in gross, and the same pay during his life, which other captains have received, in virtue of the provisions of the act of Congress, entitled “An act for the relief of certain surviving officers and soldiers of the army of the revolution,” approved fifteenth May, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-eight.


*Approved, May 26, 1830.*

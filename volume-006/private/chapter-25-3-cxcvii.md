# Chapter CXCVII

> An Act for the relief of James H. Grant, Moses TV. Simpson, and Preston Going.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, directed to pay James H. Grant, Moses W. Simpson, and Preston Going, the same amount that was paid to other privates in Captain Ayers Maupin’s company of volunteers, under an act of Congress passed the first day of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-seven, concerning volunteers raised in the summer of eighteen hundred and thirty-six.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

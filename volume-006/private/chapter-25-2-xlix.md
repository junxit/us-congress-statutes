# Chapter XLIX

> An Act for the relief of William Eadus.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay, out of any unappropriated money, to William Eadus, two thousand dollars, in full, for his house burnt by the British at Sodus, New York, in June, eighteen hundred and thirteen.


*Approved, April 6, 1838.*

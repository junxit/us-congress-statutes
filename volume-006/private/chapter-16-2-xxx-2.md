# Chapter XXX

> An Act for the relief of Henry Knowles, senior.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Third Auditor of the Treasury ascertain the value of a horse, the property of said Knowles, impressed into the service of the United States in the year eighteen hundred and four-teen, by order of Captain Humphreys of Virginia, to transport the baggage of his company to Baltimore, and not returned; and that the Secretary of the Treasury pay to the said Henry Knowles, senior, the value, when so ascertained, out of any money in the treasury not other-wise appropriated.


*Approved, February 17, 1836.*

# Chapter CXCIX

> An Act granting a pension to Ebenezer Dewey.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Ebenezer Dewey, of Royalton, in the county of Windsor, and state of Vermont, on the roll of revolutionary pensions, and to pay him at the rate of forty dollars per annum during his natural life, to commence on the fourth day of March, anno Domini, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-one.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

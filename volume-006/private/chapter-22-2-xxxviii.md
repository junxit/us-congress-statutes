# Chapter XXXVIII

> An Act for the relief of William P. Zantzinger.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury credit on the account of William P. Zantzinger, as purser of the United States’ ship Hornet, as of the date of said account, so as to stop accruing interest, the sum of three thousand three hundred and six dollars and forty-one cents, the amount of loss sustained by him on the sale of thirty-five thousand eight hundred dollars of treasury notes to pay the officers and crew of said ship.


*Approved, February 19, 1833.*

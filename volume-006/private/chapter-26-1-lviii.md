# Chapter LVIII

> An Act granting a pension to Phillip Hartman, of Virginia.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be required to place the name of Phillip Hartman, of Rockingham county, Virginia, on the roll of revolutionary pensioners, and pay him the sum of forty dollars per annum, commencing at the time that his name was stricken from the pension list.


*Approved, July 20, 1840.*

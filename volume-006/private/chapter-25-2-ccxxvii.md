# Chapter CCXXVII

> An Act for the relief of Letitia Crane.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and be is hereby, authorized to place the name of Letitia Crane, on the roll of revolutionary pensioners, and pay her the full pension to which her deceased husband would be entitled for his services as a lieutenant in the army of the Revolution, to commence and be computed from the fourth of July, eighteen hundred and thirty-six.


*Approved, July 7, 1838.*

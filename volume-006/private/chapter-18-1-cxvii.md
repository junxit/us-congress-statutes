# Chapter CXVII

> An Act for the relief of William Hall, an invalid soldier of the revolutionary army.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be authorized, and he is hereby directed, to place William Hall on the pension list of invalid pensioners of the United States, at the rate of eight dollars per month, commencing on the fourth of March, eighteen hundred and twenty-four, in lieu of the pension he now receives.


*Approved, May 19, 1824.*

# Chapter CCLII

> An Act for the relief of the legal representatives of Sawney York, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War pay to the legal representatives of Sawney York, late a pensioner of the United States now deceased, the sum of one hundred and eighty-four dollars being the amount due the said York at the time of his death, at the rate of eight dollars per month.


*Approved, July 7, 1838.*

# Chapter CII

> An Act for the relief of the legal representations of John Miller, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, authorized, to audit the accounts of John Miller, late clerk of the superior and district courts for the Territory of West Florida, upon the following principles, namely: they are to allow the legal representative of said Miller, a per diem allowance for attendance on the several courts, a reasonable compensation for office rent, fuel, and stationery, on the same terms as such allowances have hitherto been made to the clerks of the circuit and district courts of the several states and territories.


*Approved, March 2, 1833.*

# Chapter XCIV

> An Act for the relief of Lemuel Arms.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to Lemuel Arms, the sum of one hundred and fifty dollars, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, deducting therefrom any money which may have been received by him for rent, in full for the loss by him sustained, in the destruction of a building, in the village of Sackett’s Harbor, by the American troops, in the month of January, one thousand eight hundred and fifteen.


*Approved, May 18, 1824.*

# Chapter XXVIII

> An Act for the relief of James Reid.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of James Reid, of the state of Illinois, on the invalid pension roll, and to pay him at the rate of eight dollars per month, from and after the fourth day of February, eighteen hundred and forty-two.


*Approved, May 23, 1844.*

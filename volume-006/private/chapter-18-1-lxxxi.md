# Chapter LXXXI

> An Act for the relief of Elliott Rucker.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Postmaster General shall be, and he is hereby, authorized, upon the payment of two hundred and eighty dollars and eighty-two cents, by Elliott Rucker, one of the securities of Edmond F. Vawter, late Postmaster at Versailles, to relinquish to the said Rucker, the residue of the judgment obtained against him, as one of the securities of said Vawter, at the May term, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-three, of the Circuit Court for the district of Kentucky.


*Approved, May 17, 1824.*

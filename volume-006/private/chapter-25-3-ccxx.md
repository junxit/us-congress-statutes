# Chapter CCXX

> An Act for the relief of John Balch, junior.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to pay, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to John Balch, junior, the sum of three hundred and forty-two dollars and seventy-five cents, being a compensation for two cable chains and two anchors, sold by the marshal for the district of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts under a decree of the district court of said district, on or about the twenty-eighth of July, eighteen hundred and thirty-six.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

# Chapter XLI

> An Act for the relief of William Owens.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to William Owens, out of any money in the treasury, not otherwise appropriated, one hundred and ten dollars and seventy-eight cents, that sum having been due from the United States to James Shirley, a soldier in the service of the United States, at the time of his death, and to which money Elizabeth Owens, wife of William Owens, is justly entitled, as sole heir at law of said James Shirley.


*Approved, March 15, 1832.*

# Chapter CCCLXVII

> An Act to authorize the corporation of Georgetown to close certain streets in said town.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the corporation of Georgetown, in the District of Columbia, be, and is hereby, authorized to sell, in fee simple, or otherwise dispose of, and convey, all that portion of Fayette street, and Lingan street, in said town, lying south of the canal: Provided, That nothing herein contained shall affect the right of any individual.


*Approved, July 4, 1836.*

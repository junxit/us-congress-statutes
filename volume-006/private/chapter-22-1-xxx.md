# Chapter XXX

> An Act for the relief of the representatives of Doctor Hanson Catlett.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers pay to the legal representatives of Doctor Hanson Catlett, late of the United States’ army, out of any money in the treasury, not otherwise appropriated, the sum of six hundred and forty-five dollars, being the amount claimed by said Doctor Catlett, in his life-time, for pay and rations advanced to a hospital steward at the arsenal near Pittsburg, from the ninth day of June, one thousand eight hundred and sixteen, to the ninth day of January, one thousand eight hundred and twenty.


*Approved, February 24, 1832.*

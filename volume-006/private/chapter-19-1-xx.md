# Chapter XX

> An Act for the relief of Elijah Buckley.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That any forfeiture which may have accrued to the United States, from the non-payment of the purchase money for the east half of the south-east quarter of section numbered two, in township numbered nineteen, of range nine, in the district of lands offered for sale at Cahawba, be, and the same is hereby, relinquished; and that Elijah Buckley shall be entitled to receive a patent for the same, on making payment to the receiver of public moneys for the land office at Cahawba, of the sum of one dollar and one cent.


*Approved, March 31, 1826.*

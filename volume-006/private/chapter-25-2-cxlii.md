# Chapter CXLII

> An Act for the relief of Eli Eastman.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to pay to Eli Eastman, of Vermont, the sum of sixty-seven dollars per annum, during his natural life, as a pension for his services as a revolutionary soldier; the said payment to commence on the fourth day of September, anno Domini eighteen hundred and thirty-four, at which time the pension previously granted to said Eastman, as a revolutionary soldier, was suspended.


*Approved, June 28, 1838.*

# Chapter XXVIII

> An Act for the relief of Joseph McNiel.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to Joseph NcNiel, or his legal representative, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appro- priated, the sum of seven hundred dollars, in full for his claim for injuries sustained during the invasion of New Orleans by the British, in the years eighteen hundred and fourteen—fifteen.


*Approved, March 2, 1821.*

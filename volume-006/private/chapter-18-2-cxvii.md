# Chapter CXVII

> An Act concerning Charles D. Brodie.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to Charles D. Brodie, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one thousand dollars, for his invention and services in stopping a leak in the bottom of the Delaware seventy-four.


*Approved, March 3, 1825.*

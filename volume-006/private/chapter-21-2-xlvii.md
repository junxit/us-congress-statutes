# Chapter XLVII

> An Act for the relief of the legal representatives of Daniel McIntire, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to pay to the legal representatives of Daniel McIntire, late an invalid pensioner, deceased, the sum due said pensioner at his death.


*Approved, March 2, 1831.*

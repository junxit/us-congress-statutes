# Chapter LXXXIII

> An Act for the relief of Pearson Freeman.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to place the name of Pearson Freeman on the list of revolutionary pensioners, and to pay him the sum of eighty dollars per annum during his natural life, commencing on the fourth day of March, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-one.


*Approved, June 26, 1834.*

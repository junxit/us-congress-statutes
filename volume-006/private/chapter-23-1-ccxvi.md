# Chapter CCXVI

> An Act for the relief of Margaret Riker.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and required to pay to Margaret Riker, the widow of Abraham Riker, a captain in the army of the revolution, who died in service on the seventh day of May, seventeen hundred and seventy-eight, seven years’ half pay of a captain of the second regiment of New York, on continental establishment.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

# Chapter CXVII

> An Act for the relief of Thomas Wheatley.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Thomas Wheatley, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of forty-five dollars, the value of a horse that died in the military service of the United States, in one thousand eight hundred and twelve, for the want of forage.


*Approved, May 28, 1830.*

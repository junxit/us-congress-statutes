# Chapter XCIV

> An Act for the relief of William Harper.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War is authorized, and hereby required, to place William Harper, of Anderson district, South Carolina, on the roll of revolutionary pensioners, and allow him a pension for seven months and five days’ service as a private soldier in the war of the revolution, to be paid to him, together with the arrears of pension from the fourth of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-one, as other pensioners are paid, under the act of May, eighteen hundred and thirty-two, allowing pensions to the surviving officers and soldiers of the revolution.


*Approved, July 27, 1842.*

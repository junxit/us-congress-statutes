# Resolution 8

> Joint Resolution explanatory of “An act for the relief of Thomas King.”

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That it shall be the duty of the Secretary of War, instead of the Commissioner of Pensions, to carry into execution the act of the present session of Congress entitled “An act for the relief of Thomas King,” approved the twenty-fourth day of February, eighteen hundred and forty-three.


*Approved, March 3, 1843.*

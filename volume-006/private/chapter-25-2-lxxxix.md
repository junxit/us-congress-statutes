# Chapter LXXXIX

> An Act granting a pension to David Gilmore.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War place the name of David Gilmore on the roll of revolutionary pensioners, and pay him a pension at the rate of twenty dollars a year, from the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-one, during his life.


*Approved, May 31, 1838.*

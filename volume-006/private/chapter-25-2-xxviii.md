# Chapter XXVIII

> An Act for the relief of Abraham Lansing.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be authorized to examine the account of Abraham Lansing for services performed as assistant in the naval recruiting service at Boston, and for services as master’s mate at the same station; and, if the said Lansing performed said services, the same shall be paid, as is allowed for like services, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, notwithstanding the certificate of Commodore Bainbridge to said account was not given before his decease.


*Approved, March 7, 1838.*

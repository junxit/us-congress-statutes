# Chapter CXXII

> An Act for the relief of Joseph W. Torrey.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Joseph W. Torrey be allowed the sum of one hundred dollars, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, for his services as an attorney in defending three Indians indicted for murder in the Circuit Court of Green Bay.


*Approved, May 31, 1832.*

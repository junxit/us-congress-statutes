# Chapter XL

> An Act making provision for the claim of M. Poirey.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, authorized to liquidate, settle, and allow, the claim of M. Poirey, as secretary and aid-de-camp to Major-General La Fayette, during the time of his service, in those capacities, in the time of the revolutionary war between the United States of America and Great Britain.


*Approved, February 24, 1819.*

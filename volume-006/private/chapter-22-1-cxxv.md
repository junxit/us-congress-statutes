# Chapter CXXV

> An Act for the benefit of Doctor Eliakim Crosby.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That a patent or patents shall be issued to Doctor Eliakim Crosby, formerly of the province of Upper Canada, now a citizen of the United States, for a quantity of land equal to two sections of land; which may be located on any of the unappropriated lands of the United States which have been hitherto offered for sale, and which are now subject to entry.


*Approved, June 4, 1832.*

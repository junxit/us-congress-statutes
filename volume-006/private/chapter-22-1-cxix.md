# Chapter CXIX

> An Act for the relief of Captain John Burnham.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the sum of two hundred and sixty-seven dollars and fifty-seven cents be allowed and paid to Captain John Burnham, or his legal representatives, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, May 31, 1832.*

# Chapter CX

> An Act for the relief of Frederick Raymer.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Frederick Raymer, of Dryden, in the county of Tompkins, and state of New York, the sum of two hundred and fifty dollars, in full satisfaction of his claim against the United States for services rendered and supplies furnished in the revolutionary war.


*Approved, June 28, 1834.*

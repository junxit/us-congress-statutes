# Resolution 3

> Resolution for the relief of sundry owners of vessels sunk for the defence of Baltimore.

*Be it resolved, &c.,*

That the memorial of John S. Stiles, and the memorial of the other owners of vessels, taken and sunk for the defence of Baltimore during the late war, with the papers and documents referred to the Committee on Claims of the House of Representatives in the cases aforesaid, be referred to the Third Auditor for his decision, under the act of May twenty-nine, eighteen hundred and thirty, “for the relief of sundry owners of vessels sunk for the defence of Baltimore;” which decision shall be subject to the supervision of the Secretary of the Navy.


*Approved, March 2, 1833.*

# Chapter CX

> An Act for the relief of John Shirring.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby authorized to pay to John Skirving three thousand two hundred and eighty-seven dollars and twenty-five cents, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, for building furnaces for warming the Treasury Department.


*Approved, March 3, 1843.*

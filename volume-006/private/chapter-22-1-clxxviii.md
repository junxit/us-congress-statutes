# Chapter CLXXVIII

> An Act granting a pension to William Scott.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, directed to place on the pension roll of the United States, the name of William Scott, of the county of Knox, and state of Tennessee; and that there be allowed to said Scott the sum of eight dollars per month, during his natural life; to commence on the fourth day of December, in the year one thousand eight hundred and thirty.


*Approved, July 9, 1832.*

# Chapter LXXXVII

> An Act for the relief of Thomas Ap Catesby Jones.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the navy department be, and they are hereby authorized and directed, to audit and settle the accounts of Thomas Ap Catesby Jones, a lieutenant of the navy of the United States, and to allow him, in the settlemen. thereof, such credits as may be equitable and just.


*Approved, April 26, 1816.*

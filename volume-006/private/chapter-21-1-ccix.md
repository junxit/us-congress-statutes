# Chapter CCIX

> An Act for the relief of Elizabeth Williams.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, to Elizabeth Williams, widow of the late Thomas Williams, of the District of Columbia, the sum of two hundred eighty-five dollars, the value of the labor performed by the said Thomas Williams, on the wall around the jail of the county of Washington, in said district.


*Approved, May 29, 1830.*

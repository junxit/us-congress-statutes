# Chapter LXXXI

> An Act for the relief of William Pemberton.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to William Pemberton the sum of fifty-five dollars, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, in full compensation for the loss of a horse during the late war.


*Approved, March 3, 1825.*

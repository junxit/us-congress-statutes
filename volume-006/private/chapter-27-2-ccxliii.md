# Chapter CCXLIII

> An Act for the relief of Dennis Dygert.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to place Dennis Dygert, of Herkimer county, in the state of New York, on the list of invalid pensioners, at the rate of ten dollars per month, during his natural life; said pension to commence on the first day of January, in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and forty.


*Approved, August 29, 1842.*

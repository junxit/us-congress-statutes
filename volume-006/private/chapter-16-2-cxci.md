# Chapter CXCI

> An Act for the relief of Mathias Traverse.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, required to place the name of Mathias Traverse, of Dorchester county, Maryland, on the roll of revolutionary pensioners; and to pay him a pension at the rate of one hundred and twenty dollars per annum, to commence and be computed from the fourth day of March, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-one, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

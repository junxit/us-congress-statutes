# Chapter XIX

> An Act for the relief of Edwin T. Satterwhite.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the accountant of the Navy Department be, and he is hereby authorized and required to assume the day of the departure of the United States’ brig Vixen from a port in the United States on her last cruize, as the day on which the account of said Edwin T. Satterwhite, the purser of said brig, shall be settled and balanced. And that the accountant of the Navy Department be authorized to pay to the said Edwin T. Satterwhite such sum as may be due for pay and commissions, previous to the said day of the sailing of the brig Vixen from a port in the United States, if in his judgment any such sum may be due.


*Approved, July 22, 1813.*

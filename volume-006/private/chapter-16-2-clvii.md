# Chapter CLVII

> An Act for the relief of Luke Voorhise.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Luke Voorhise, of the State of Ohio, a pension at the rate of eight dollars a month, commencing on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and thirty-two, and to continue during his natural life; to be paid out of any moneys in the treasury, not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

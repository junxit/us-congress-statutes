# Chapter CXX

> An Act for the benefit of Eliphalet Spofford.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Eliphalet Spaftbrd on the list of invalid pensioners, at the rate of four dollars a month; to commence the first day of January, eighteen hundred and thirty-eight, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

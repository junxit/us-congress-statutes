# Chapter CCXXII

> An Act for the relief of Joseph Parker.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, directed to place the name of Joseph Parker, of Windsor, in the state of Vermont, on the list of revolutionary pensioners of the United States, and to pay him the sum of sixty dollars per annum from the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-one, during his natural life.


*Approved, August 26, 1842.*

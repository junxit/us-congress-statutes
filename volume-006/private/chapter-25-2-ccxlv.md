# Chapter CCXLV

> An Act granting a pension to Thomas Frazer.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Thomas Frazer, of the county of Oxford, state of Maine, on the roll of invalid pensioners, and pay to him the sum of two dollars and sixty cents per month, during his natural life, commencing on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and thirty-eight.


*Approved, July 7, 1838.*

# Chapter XLII

> An Act making provision for the claim of M. de Vienne.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the accounting officers of the war department be, and they are hereby, authorized to liquidate, settle, and allow, the claim of M. de Vienne, for the pay, appointments, and emoluments, of lieutenant-colonel, for seven months’ service during the revolutionary war between the United States and Great Britain.


*Approved, February 24, 1819.*

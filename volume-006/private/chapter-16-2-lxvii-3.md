# Chapter LXVII

> An Act for the relief of Peter Harmony, of New York.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the collector of the port of New York be, and he is hereby, authorized to permit Peter Harmony, of New York, to export, with the benefit of drawback, certain cases or packages of Spanish playing cards, imported by him into that port, upon his com-plying with the forms and requisitions required by existing laws relating to the exportation of merchandise with the benefit of drawback.


*Approved, March 3, 1837.*

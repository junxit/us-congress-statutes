# Chapter XLVII

> An Act for the relief of Thomas Buford.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the sum of six hundred dollars be paid to Thomas Buford, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, for his services as a Deputy Quartermaster-General, from the twenty-ninth day of June, one thousand eight hundred and twelve, to the third day of July, one thousand eight hundred and thirteen.


*Approved, March 25, 1830.*

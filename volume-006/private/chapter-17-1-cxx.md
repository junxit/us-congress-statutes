# Chapter CXX

> An Act for the relief of John Holmes.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to John Holmes, of the state of Alabama, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of sixty dollars, being in full for a horse killed in the action with the Creek Indians, at Emuckfaw, on the twenty-second day of January, one thousand eight hundred and fourteen.


*Approved, May 7, 1822.*

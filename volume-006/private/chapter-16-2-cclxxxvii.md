# Chapter CCLXXXVII

> An Act for the relief of Ephraim Page.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, directed to place the name of Ephraim Page, a soldier in the late war, upon the list of invalid pensioners, and to pay him at the rate of eight dollars a month, during his natural life, commencing on the first day of May, in the year of our Lord one thousand eight hundred and thirty-five.


*Approved, July 2, 1836.*

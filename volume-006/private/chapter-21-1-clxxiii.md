# Chapter CLXXIII

> An Act for the relief of Roger Enos.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to pay to Roger Enos, late Collector of the district of Memphreymagog, in Vermont, out of any moneys in the trea- sury, not otherwise appropriated, one hundred and three dollars, being the moiety of the proceeds of a penalty recovered by the said Roger Enos, in the name of the United States, against a certain Josiah Parmelee, in the year one thousand eight hundred and eighteen.


*Approved, May 29, 1830.*

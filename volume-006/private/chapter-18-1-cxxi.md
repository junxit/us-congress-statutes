# Chapter CXXI

> An Act for the relief of J. M. C. Montgomery.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there shall be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to J. M. C. Montgomery, or his legal representative, the sum of sixty-one dollars, that sum being the amount paid by said Montgomery, to the master boat-builder at Floydsville, in the state of Georgia, above the sum allowed by law.


*Approved, May 19, 1824.*

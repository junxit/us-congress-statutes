# Chapter XXXIV

> An Act for the relief of Joshua Cullumber.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be directed to pay to Joshua Cullumber one thousand dollars, the value of the schooner Two Brothers, owned by him, and forcibly impressed into the service of the United States, by order of an officer, and destroyed by the British, in eighteen hundred and fourteen, in St. Leonard’s creek, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, February 17, 1836.*

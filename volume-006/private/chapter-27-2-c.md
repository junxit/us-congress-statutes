# Chapter C

> An Act for the relief of Nathaniel Mitchell.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Treasurer of the United States pay to Nathaniel Mitchell the sum of nine hundred and thirty-one dollars and sixty cents, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, out of the fund appropriated for the service of the Post Office Department; being the amount paid by said Mitchell in defending a suit brought against him by William Merriam, for acts done by said Mitchell under orders from the Postmaster-General, in arresting the said Merriam on a charge for violating the Post Office laws of the United States.


*Approved, July 27, 1842.*

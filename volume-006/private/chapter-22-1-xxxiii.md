# Chapter XXXIII

> An Act for the relief of Peter Peck.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Peter Peck, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, the sum of fifty dollars, the value of a horse lost by him at Portage river, in the state of Ohio, during the last war, by reason of his having been dismounted, and ordered into Canada in the expedition commanded by Governor Shelby.


*Approved, February 24, 1832.*

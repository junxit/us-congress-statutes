# Chapter LXI

> An Act for the relief if Daniel Homans.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, directed to pay to Daniel Homans the sum of three thousand two hundred and fifty-seven dollars and thirty-six cents, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, being the amount due him for damages sustained by lightning, money retained, and extra work done on the marine hospital built by him for the United States in eighteen hundred and thirty-one, eighteen hundred and thirty-two, and eighteen hundred and thirty-three.


*Approved, March 3, 1845.*

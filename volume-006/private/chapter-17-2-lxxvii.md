# Chapter LXXVII

> An Act for the relief of Robert Purdy.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to cause to be paid to Robert Purdy, late lieutenant- colonel in the army of the United States, the sum of eight hundred and sixteen dollars and seventy cents, the amount of judgment, fine, and cost, paid by said Purdy for the imprisonment of William Luty; and that the same be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 3, 1823.*

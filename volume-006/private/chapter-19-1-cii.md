# Chapter CII

> An Act for the relief of Jarvis Jackson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Jarvis Jackson, late deputy Postmaster at Hazelpatch, in Kentucky, be, and he is hereby, released from all demands which the Post office department may have against him, in consequence of moneys received by him whilst in that office.


*Approved, May 20, 1826.*

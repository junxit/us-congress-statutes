# Chapter CCXCVII

> An Act for the relief of Henry H. Hall.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he hereby is, authorized and required to issue scrip to the amount of one hundred dollars, to and in favor of Henry H. Hail, in lieu of that amount of scrip by him paid into the land office at Springfield, Illinois for eighty acres of land; which said land was afterwards sold by the United States to James J. Mosely, by virtue of a preemption right thereto. Said scrip, when so issued, shall be receivable in payment for any lands within the state of Illinois, which are subject to entry at private sale.


*Approved, July 2, 1836.*

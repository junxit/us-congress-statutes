# Chapter LII

> An Act for the relief of James Gibson, of Vincennes, Indiana, and James Kay, of Kentucky.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place James Gibson on the list of invalid pensioners, who served in Captain Begg’s company of light dragoons, in the battle with the Indians at Tippecanoe, at the rate of eight dollars per month, commencing on the fourth of September, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-five.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place James Kay, of Kentucky, upon the list of invalid pensioners, who was wounded in the battle of Brandywine, during the revolutionary war, at the rate of eight dollars per month, to commence upon the fourth day of September, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-five.


*Approved, May 16, 1826.*

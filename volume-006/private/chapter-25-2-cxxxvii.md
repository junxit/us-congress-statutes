# Chapter CXXXVII

> An Act granting a pension to Seth Whitney, of Delaware county, New York.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be required to place the name of Seth Whitney, of the town of Delhi, in the county of Delaware and state of New York, upon the roll of revolutionary pensions, and pay him twenty-six dollars and sixty-seven cents per annum during life, to commence and take effect from and after the fourth of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-one.


*Approved, June 28, 1838.*

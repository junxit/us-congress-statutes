# Chapter CXIII

> An Act for the relief of John A. Rodgers.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of John A. Rodgers on the invalid pension list, at the rate of fifteen dollars a month, to commence on the thirty-first day of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-five, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, June 15, 1836.*

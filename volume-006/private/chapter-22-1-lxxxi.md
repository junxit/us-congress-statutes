# Chapter LXXXI

> An Act to revive and continue inforce “An actfor the relief of the representatives of John Donelson, Stephen Heard, and others.”

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That an act, entitled “An act for the relief of the representatives of John Donelson, Stephen Heard, and others,” passed the twenty-fourth of May, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-four, be, and the same is hereby, revived and continued in force for the term of twelve months, from and after the passage of this act.


*Approved, May 19, 1832.*

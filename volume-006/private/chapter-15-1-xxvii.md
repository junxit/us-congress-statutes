# Chapter XXVII

> An Act for the relief of John Small.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That John Small be, and he hereby is, confirmed his title to a tract of land, containing two hundred and fifty-five acres, situated on Embarass Creek, in the Illinois territory, as the assignee of Nicholas Bayarjon; which tract shall be limited to the quantity aforesaid, and to the boundaries as claimed by said Bayarjon.


*Approved, April 3, 1818.*

# Chapter CLXX

> An Act for the relief of Isaac D. Saunders.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War cause to be issued to Isaac D. Saunders, as assignee of Edward Bevin, a bounty land certificate for one hundred and sixty acres of land; to be located on some part of the public lands set apart for that purpose.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

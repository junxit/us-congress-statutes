# Chapter CCXXXVIII

> An Act for the relief of Jonathan M. Blaisdell.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to pay, to Jonathan M. Blaisdell, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of three hundred and sixty-eight dollars, being one moiety of a judgment recovered by mistake against him, by the United States, and the interest thereon.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

# Chapter CLX

> An Act for the relief of Robert Ramsay.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be, and he is hereby, required to place the name of Robert Ramsay on the roll of navy pensioners, and to pay him a pension, at the rate of five dollars per month; to commence and be computed from the thirtieth day of December, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-seven.


*Approved, March 3, 1843.*

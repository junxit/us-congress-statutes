# Chapter XXXI

> An Act for the relief of John Adams and John Adams, junior.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to John Adams and John Adams, junior, of Massachusetts, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of five hundred dollars, the amount by them paid in satisfaction of a fine imposed by Mr. Charles, the revenue officer of Elizabeth city, North Carolina, for an alleged violation of the revenue laws of the United States.


*Approved, February 27, 1845.*

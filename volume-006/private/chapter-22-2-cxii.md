# Chapter CXII

> An Act for the relief of Robert Kane.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Robert Kane, a mounted volunteer of the New York militia, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of sixty-five dollars, for equipments captured by the enemy at the Battle of the Beaver Dams, during the late war with Great Britain.


*Approved, March 2, 1833.*

# Chapter CCLIII

> An Act for the relief of Amey Babbitt.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, directed to place the name of Amey Babbitt, of Rhode Island, on the pension roll, under the act of the fourth of July, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-six, and to pay to her ten dollars a month, from the fourth of March, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-one.


*Approved, August 29, 1842.*

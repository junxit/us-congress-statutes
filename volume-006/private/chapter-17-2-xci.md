# Chapter XCI

> An Act for the relief of Amos Nichols.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Amos Nichols, of the state of Maine, four hundred and sixty-six dollars and sixty-six cents, being the amount of pay due him for seven months’ service as clerk in the navy department, during the years eighteen hundred and eighteen and nineteen.


*Approved, March 3, 1823.*

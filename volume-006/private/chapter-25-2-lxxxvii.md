# Chapter LXXXVII

> An Act for the relief of James Withered.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury do settle the account of James Witherell, and allow him five years’ full pay as an ensign of infantry of the revolutionary army, in the Massachusetts line on continental establishment, and that the said sum of money be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, May 25, 1838.*

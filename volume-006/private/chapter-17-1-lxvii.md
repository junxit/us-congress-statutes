# Chapter LXVII

> An Act for the relief of Charles Campbell.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the accounting officers of the treasury department be, and they hereby are, authorized and required to settle the accounts of Charles Campbell, formerly Brigade Inspector, or County Lieutenant, of the county of Westmoreland, in the state of Pennsylvania, upon just and equitable principles; and that, in the mean time, all proceedings upon the judgment against the said Charles Campbell be suspended.


*Approved, May 7, 1822.*

# Chapter XIV

> An Act for the relief of Sampson S. King.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury department be, and they are hereby, authorized to settle the account of Major Sampson S. King, on equitable principles, and give such credits as shall seem just and reasonable, from the best evidence the nature of the case will admit: Provided, That it shall appear that the said Sampson S. King has not been guilty of any misconduct or default in failing to render his accounts for settlement.


*Approved, February 4, 1819.*

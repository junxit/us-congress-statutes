# Chapter CCCI

> An Act for the relief of Nancy Leamaster, widow of James Leamaster.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Nancy Leamaster, widow of James Leamaster, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, one hundred dollars, for provisions and forage taken from the farm of James Leamaster, in the year eighteen hundred and twelve, by mounted volunteers in the service of the United States.


*Approved, July 2, 1836.*

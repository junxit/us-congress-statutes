# Chapter CXXII

> An Act for the relief of Isaac Miller.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Isaac Miller, of Union county, Illinois, be, and he is hereby, authorized to relinquish to the United States, in such form as the Commissioner of the General Land Office may require, cer- tificate number five hundred and twenty, for the south-west quarter of the north-west quarter of section twenty-two, in township eleven south of range three west, in the district of lands subject to entry at Kaskaskia, Illinois; and upon such relinquishment being made, as aforesaid, the said Isaac Miller shall be, and he is hereby, authorized to enter any other forty acres in the Kaskaskia land district, in the state of Illinois.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

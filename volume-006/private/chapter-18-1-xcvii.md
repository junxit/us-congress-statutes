# Chapter XCVII

> An Act for the relief of Thomas Williams.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and required, on application, to cause to be issued to Thomas Williams, who was a soldier in Captain Ransom’s company of Colonel Dunker’s regiment of the American army, in the revolutionary war, a warrant for one hundred acres of land, which may be located on any vacant part of the fifty quarter townships, and fractional townships, reserved by law for the holders of military warrants, and a patent issue, as in other cases.


*Approved, May 18, 1824.*

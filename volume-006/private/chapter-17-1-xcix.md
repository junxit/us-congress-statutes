# Chapter XCIX

> An Act for the relief of William Gwynn.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury department be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to pay to William Gwynn, of the state of Alabama, forty-seven dollars and fifty cents, for a horse killed in battle on the twenty-second day of January, one thousand eight hundred and fourteen, at Oak Fusky; and that the same be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, May 7, 1822.*

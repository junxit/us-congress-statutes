# Chapter CLXVII

> An Act for the relief of Francis Bashford.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to place the name of Francis Bashford, upon the list of invalid pensioners, at the rate of eight dollars per month, the payment to commence on the twelfth of April, in the year one thousand eight hundred and thirty-three, and pay him out of any money in the treasury, not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

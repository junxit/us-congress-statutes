# Chapter LXXXIX

> An Act for the relief of Christian Jacob Burcle.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Collector of the port of Philadelphia be, and he is hereby, authorized to permit Christian Jacob Burckle to export, with the benefit of drawback, two packages of Spanish playing cards, which were imported into that port in the brig Hibernia, from Hamburg, the said Christian Jacob Burckle complying with all the forms and requisites of the existing acts allowing the benefit of drawback in other cases.


*Approved, March 3, 1825.*

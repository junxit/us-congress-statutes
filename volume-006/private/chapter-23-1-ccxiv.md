# Chapter CCXIV

> An Act for the relief of the legal representatives of George Hurlbut, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury do settle the account of Captain George Hurlbut, a captain of the revolutionary army, in Colonel Sheldon’s regiment of dragoons, of the Connecticut line, and allow him five years’ full pay of a captain of dragoons, as a commutation for the half pay for life which, by the resolve of seventeen hundred and eighty, Congress engaged to pay to certain officers of the revolutionary army, who should serve to the end of the war.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the sum so found due be paid to the legal representatives of the said George Hurlbut out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

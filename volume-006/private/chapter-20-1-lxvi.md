# Chapter LXVI

> An Act for the relief of William McClure.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to pay to William McClure, or to his legal representatives, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of seven thousand thirty-seven dollars and three cents, in full satisfaction for his services as a Commissioner under the Convention of the thirtieth of April, one thousand eight hundred and three, between France and the United States.


*Approved, May 19, 1828.*

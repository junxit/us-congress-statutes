# Chapter LXXXIV

> An Act for the relief of the Miami Exporting Company.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to the President and Directors of the Miami Exporting Company, out of any money in the treasury, not otherwise appropriated, the sum of eight thousand five hundred and eighty-five dollars, in full satisfaction on advances made by the said Company for and on account of the United States, from the first day of September, one thousand eight hundred and twelve, to the tenth of March, one thousand eight hundred and thirteen.


*Approved, May 19, 1832.*

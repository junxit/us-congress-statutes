# Chapter III

> An Act for the relief of Isaac Finch.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officer of the treasury department be, and he is hereby, authorized to audit and settle the accounts of Isaac Finch, late a major of the twenty-sixth regiment of infantry, on principles of justice and equity.


*Approved, February 4, 1822.*

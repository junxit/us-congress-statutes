# Chapter CXXIV

> An Act explanatory of an act entitled “ An act for the relief of Clark Woodrooff,” passed May tenth, eighteen hundred and forty-two.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the. Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to refund the balance of the amount paid for lands purchased from the United States, at the land office of the St. Helena district, in the state of Louisiana, on the tenth and twenty-third days of February, of the year eighteen hundred and thirty-seven, in the name of Clark Woodrooff, either to said Woodrooff, or to any other person or persons jointly interested with him in said purchase, or to his or their heirs, assigns, or legal representatives, on his or their complying with the conditions of said act.


*Approved, August 4, 1842.*

# Chapter CCLXXVII

> An Act for the relief of the Lexington and Ohio Railroad Company.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to refund to the Lexington and Ohio Railroad Company, the sum of nineteen hundred and eighty-four dollars, paid by said company into the treasury of the United States, as duties on railroad iron imported into the United States, in the years eighteen hundred and thirty-two and eighteen hundred and thirty-three, for making of a rail-road from Lexington, in Kentucky, to the Ohio river, whenever it shall be made appear to the satisfaction of the Secretary of the Treasury that said iron has been applied to the uses for which it was imported.


*Approved, July 2, 1836.*

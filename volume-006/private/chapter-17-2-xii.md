# Chapter XII

> An Act to provide for the settlement of the accounts of Daniel D. Tompkins, late Governor of the State of New York.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and they are hereby, authorized to adjust and settle the accounts and claims of Daniel D. Tompkins, late Governor of the state of New York, on principles of equity and justice, subject to the revision and final decision of the President of the United States.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That nothing contained in the second section of the act, entitled “An act making appropriations for the support of government for the year one thousand eight hundred and twenty-two, and for other purposes,” passed April twentieth [thirtieth,] one thousand eight hundred and twenty-two, shall be construed to extend or apply to the said Daniel D. Tompkins.


*Approved, February 21, 1823.*

# Chapter CCXI

> An Act granting a pension to John Miles.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, authorized and required to pay to John Miles, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, a pension at the rate of four dollars a month, to commence on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and thirty-five, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*

# Chapter CXXXI

> An Act for the relief of Josiah Dillon, late assistant quartermaster of the army.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War pay to Josiah Dillon, late assistant quartermaster-general, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one hundred and thirty-eight dollars and fifty cents, being the balance found to be due to said Dillon, on vouchers suspended for the want of form in the settlement of his accounts.


*Approved, June 17, 1844.*

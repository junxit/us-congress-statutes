# Chapter XII

> An Act to authorize James Alexander to relinquish certain land, and to locate other land in lieu thereof.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That James Alexander be, and he is hereby, authorized to relinquish to the United States the east half of the south-east quarter of section number three, in township number nine north, in range fourteen west of the second principal meridian, in the Palestine land district, state of Illinois; and that he be permitted to enter, in lieu thereof, a like quantity of land within the limits of said district subject to private entry.


*Approved, May 2, 1840.*

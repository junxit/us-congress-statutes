# Chapter LXXV

> An Act for the relief of Eleanor Lawrence.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to Eleanor Lawrence, out of any money in treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one thousand and seventy dollars, being in full for damages, occasioned by the military occupation of her farm on Long Island, in the state of New York, from the year one thousand eight hundred and thirteen, to the year one thousand eight hundred and twenty-one.


*Approved, March 3, 1823.*

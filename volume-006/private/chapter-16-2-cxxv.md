# Chapter CXXV

> An Act for the relief of Francis R. Theobald.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the collector of the port of Bath, in the State of Maine, be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to Francis R. Theobald, master of the brig Euphrates, of Gardner, in said State, the sum of fifty dollars, the amount of a fine paid by him to the collector of New Orleans, for departing from said port of Bath, in said brig Euphrates, in November, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-three, without having on board a manifest of his cargo; it being in evidence that the omission was altogether a mistake, and not the result of any design to defraud the revenue, or violate any law of the United States.


*Approved, June 23, 1836.*

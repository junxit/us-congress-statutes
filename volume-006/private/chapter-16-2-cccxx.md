# Chapter CCCXX

> An Act for the relief of Henry Wainwright.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be refunded to Henry Wainright, of Boston, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of thirteen hundred and twenty-eight dollars and fifty-eight cents, being the amount of an over-payment made by him, on the importation of certain quantities of lead, in March, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-four.


*Approved, July 2, 1836.*

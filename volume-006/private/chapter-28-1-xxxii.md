# Chapter XXXII

> An Act to alter the corporate limits of Georgetown.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the present corporate limits of the town of Georgetown, in the District of Columbia, be, and they are hereby, so altered and amended as to exclude therefrom so much of the territory within said present limits as lies west of the west line of Fayette street, and between the north line of Third street and a line drawn west and parallel therewith from a point on the said west line of Fayette street at the end of sixty feet north of the north line of Sixth street, saving 118 4D excepting and reserving however out of said territory so to be excluded the several lots of ground lying in Threlkeld’s Addition of Georgetown, and respectively being lots numbers one hundred and fifty-three, one hundred and sixty-one, one hundred and sixty-two, one hundred and sixty-three, one hundred and seventy-two, one hundred and seventy-four, one hundred and seventy-six, and one hundred and seventy-eight, and the south half of lot number one hundred and seventy-one.


*Approved, February 27, 1845.*

# Chapter XXVIII

> An Act for the relief of Ezra Thurbur, and the legal representatives of Gideon King.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the sum of one thousand two hundred dollars be paid out of any money in the treasury, not otherwise appropriated, to Ezra Thurbur, and the legal representatives of Gideon King, in full satisfaction of the claim against the United States, for the capture and detention of the sloop Essex, by the British, on Lake Champlain, during the late war.


*Approved, February 27, 1830.*

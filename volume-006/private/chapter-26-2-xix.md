# Chapter XIX

> An Act for the relief of Charles M. Keller and Henry Stone.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Charles M. Keller and Henry Stone, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, the sum of two hundred and twenty-seven dollars and fifty cents, for property destroyed by fire while in the public service.


*Approved, March 2, 1841.*

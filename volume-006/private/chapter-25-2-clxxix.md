# Chapter CLXXIX

> An Act for the relief of John McGinnis, a soldier in the late war.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War issue to John McGinnis a land warrant for military bounty land, for his services during the late war.


*Approved, March 3, 1843.*

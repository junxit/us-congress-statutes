# Chapter XXIII

> An Act for the relief of Philip Hickey.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Third Auditor of the Treasury ascertain the value of the timber (as it was when taken) taken from a tract of land owned by Philip Hickey, after the seventeenth of June, one thousand eight hundred and ten, and before the year one thousand eight hundred and twenty-five, by the troops of the United States, for the public works at Baton Rouge, and for fuel; and that the amount so ascertained be paid to the said Philip Hickey by the Secretary of the Treasury, out of any money not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 24, 1834.*

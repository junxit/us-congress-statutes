# Chapter LXIII

> An Act for the relief of Augustus M’Kinney and Layzel Bancroft.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the secretary of the treasury be, and he is hereby authorized and required to remit the fines, penalties, and forfeitures incurred by Augustus M’Kinney and Layzel Bancroft, who imported, in an open and public manner, into the district of Whitehall, on the twenty-seventh day of June, one thousand eight hundred and twelve, from Montreal, nine barrels of nitre.


*Approved, April 18, 1814.*

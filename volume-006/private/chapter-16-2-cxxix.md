# Chapter CXXIX

> An Act for the relief of Daniel Smith.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Commissioner of the General Land Office be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to issue a patent to Daniel Smith, of Morgan county, Indiana, for the south-east quarter of section twenty, township thirteen north, range one west, in the district of lands offered for sale at Crawfordsville: Provided, That this act shall not be construed to affect the rights of any third party.


*Approved, June 23, 1836.*

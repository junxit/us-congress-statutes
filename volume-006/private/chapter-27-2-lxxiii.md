# Chapter LXXIII

> An Act for the relief of Joseph J. Martin.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, requested to place the name of Joseph J. Martin, of the state of Tennessee, upon the roll of invalid pensioners, and to pay him at the rate of eight dollars per month, commencing on the first of July, one thou-sand eight hundred and forty-one.


*Approved, July 27, 1842.*

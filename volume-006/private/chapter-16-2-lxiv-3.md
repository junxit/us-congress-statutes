# Chapter LXIV

> An Act for the relief of Charles W. Pickering.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officer of the treasury be authorized to adjust and settle the account of Charles W. Pickering, a midshipman in the navy of the United States, and to allow him the difference between the pay and emoluments of a lieutenant over that of a midshipman, for the period during which he performed the duties of a lieutenant on board the United States ship Falmouth, by order of her commander, Francis II. Gregory, in the Pacific Ocean, in the year eighteen hundred and thirty-three; and the amount so found due to him shall be paid out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 3, 1837.*

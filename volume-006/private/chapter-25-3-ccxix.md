# Chapter CCXIX

> An Act for the relief of Patrick Green.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Patrick Green, who was wounded on the night of the eighth of July, eighteen hundred and twenty-three, whilst protecting and defending the mail between Baltimore and Havre de Grace, be, and he is hereby, allowed the sum of one thousand dollars; to be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

# Chapter CXLVII

> An Act for the benefit of John Perham.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, required to place the name of John Perham, of the state of Maine, upon the roll of invalid pensioners, at the rate of seventy-two dollars per annum; said pension to commence on the first day of January, eighteen hundred and thirty-seven, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, June 17, 1844.*

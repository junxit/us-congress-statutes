# Chapter XIV

> An Act for the relief of Benjamin Sherfey.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Benjamin Sherfey, ninety-six dollars, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, being for a military fine improperly assessed by a regimental court martial, and collected by the deputy marshal, and deposited by the marshal in the Bank of Knoxville, Tennessee, of which notice was given to the Treasurer of the United States.


*Approved, February 26, 1834.*

# Chapter CCXLII

> An Act for the relief of Charles J. Hand.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Charles J. Hand, of Indiana, the sum of one hundred and seventy-five dollars, for reclaiming fourteen horses lost in the Indian country in August, eighteen hundred and thirty-two, by the detachments of Indiana and Illinois militia under the commands of Colonel Russel and Colonel Moore; which service he was requested to perform by the Governor of Indiana.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

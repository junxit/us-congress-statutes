# Chapter CCXLVII

> An Act for the relief of Joshua Pitcher.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized to allow to Joshua Pitcher, the sum of one hundred dollars in the settlement of his accounts as Indian agent, in addition to three hundred dollars authorized by law to be paid to an interpreter.


*Approved, July 1, 1836.*

# Chapter IV

> An Act granting a certain sum of money to the widow and children of John de Neufville, deceased.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That in consideration of particular services rendered the United States, during the war of their revolution, by the late John de Neufville, of the United Netherlands, the President of the United States be, and he is hereby authorized to cause to be paid, out of any moneys which may be in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one thousand dollars to Anna de Neufville, widow of the said John de Neufville; a like sum for the use of Leonard de Neufville, his son; and a like sum for the use of Anna de Neufville, his infant daughter.


*Approved, March 2, 1797.*

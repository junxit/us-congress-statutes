# Chapter LXV

> An Act for the relief of Jerah Fenner.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to Jerah Fenner, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, his pay as a soldier in the service of the United States, from the thirtieth of June eighteen hundred and fourteen, to the eighteenth of October, eighteen hundred and fourteen, inclusive.


*Approved, March 3, 1837.*

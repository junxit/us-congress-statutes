# Chapter LXIX

> An Act granting a pension to Isaac Eckright.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to place the name of Isaac Eckright, of the county of Indiana and state of Pennsylvania, on the roll of invalid pensioners, and to pay to him four dollars per month during his natural life, commencing on the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-five.


*Approved, March 3, 1835.*

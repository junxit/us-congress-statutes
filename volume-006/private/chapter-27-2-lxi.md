# Chapter LXI

> An Act for the relief of Clarissa Turney.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be authorized and required to place Clarissa Turney on the roll of revolutionary pensions, under the act of eighteen hundred and thirty-six, allowing pensions to certain widows; and that she be paid at the rate of eighty dollars a year during her life, or remaining a widow, to commence on the fourth of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-one.


*Approved, July 9, 1842.*

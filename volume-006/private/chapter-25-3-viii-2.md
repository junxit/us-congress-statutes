# Chapter VIII

> An Act for the relief of John H. Jacocks.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to John H. Jacocks, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of four hundred and thirty dollars, for services rendered as inspector of the customs at the port of New Haven, in the state of Connecticut, from the first of April, eighteen hundred and thirty-four, to the fifteenth of February, eighteen hundred and thirty-five.


*Approved, April 10, 1840.*

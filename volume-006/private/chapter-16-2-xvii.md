# Chapter XVII

> An Act for the relief of William Baker, of the State of New York.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be directed to pay, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to William Baker, the sum of two hundred and twenty-five dollars and thirty cents, being for the use and for damages done to the property of the said William Baker, at Sackett’s Harbor, by the troops of the United States, while in possession thereof, under a military order, in the year one thousand eight hundred and thirteen.


*Approved, February 17, 1836.*

# Chapter CCXLIX

> An Act giving Catharine Lehman the benefit of the act of the seventh July, eighteen hundred and thirty-eight.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Catharine Lehman, widow of Anthony Lehman, late a revolutionary pensioner, of York county, Pennsylvania, on the pension roll, and that she be allowed such pension, under the act of the seventh July, eighteen hundred and thirty-eight, as her said husband would have been entitled to had he been living on the seventh June, eighteen hundred and thirty-two.


*Approved, August 29, 1842.*

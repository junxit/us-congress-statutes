# Chapter CXXXI

> An Act granting a pension to Frederick Hill.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Frederick Hill, of the county of Bedford, in the state of Pennsylvania, on the roll of revolutionary pensioners, and to pay to him at the rate of thirty-six dollars per annum, commencing on the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-one, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, June 28, 1838.*

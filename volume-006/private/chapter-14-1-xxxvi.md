# Chapter XXXVI

> An Act for the relief of John T. Wirt.

*Be it enacted &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the war department be, and they are hereby, authorized and directed to settle the claim of John T. Wirt, assistant deputy quartermaster-general, and to allow him, in the settlement thereof, such credits as may be equitable and just.


*Approved, April 2, 1816.*

# Chapter LXV

> An Act for the relief of the widow and children of George Ludlum, deceased.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to issue a warrant to the widow and children of George Ludlum, deceased, for the military bounty land of the said George, who, during the late war, enlisted as a private in the twenty-ninth United States infantry, for and during the war, and who continued in service until the close thereof.


*Approved, June 19, 1834.*

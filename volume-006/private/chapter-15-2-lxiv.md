# Chapter LXIV

> An Act for the relief of Daniel Moss.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to issue to Daniel Moss, of the city of New York, a military warrant, for one hundred acres of land, for revolutionary services, in lieu of a warrant alleged to have been issued to said Moss in one thousand eight hundred and seven, and to have been lost.


*Approved, March 3, 1819.*

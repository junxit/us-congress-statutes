# Chapter CV

> An Act for the relief of Madame Poidevin.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and empowered to pay to Madame Poidevin, the sum of one thousand nine hundred and thirty-four dollars and seventy-nine cents, the same being the amount of her claim against the consulate at l’Orient.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the aforesaid sum shall be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, April 20, 1818.*

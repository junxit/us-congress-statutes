# Chapter XVII

> An Act for the relief of the administrator of John Jackson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he hereby is, authorized to enter credits of one hundred dollars and six hundred and fifty dollars to the account upon the books of the Treasury Department, against Captain John Jackson, deceased, so as to stop interest upon one hundred dollars of any balance that may stand against him on said books, from April twenty-seventh, eighteen hundred and thirty-six, and upon six hundred and fifty dollars of said balance, from May nineteenth, eighteen hundred and thirty-six.


*Approved, January 28, 1843.*

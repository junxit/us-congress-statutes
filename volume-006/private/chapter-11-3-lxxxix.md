# Chapter LXXXIX

> An Act for the relief of John N. Stout.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the accounting officers of the treasury department be, and they are hereby, directed to adjust and settle the account of John N. Stout, of Kentucky, and allow him the sum of eighty-seven dollars fifteen cents, for his fees and compensation as a jailor, for committing, subsisting, and releasing, Archibald Hamilton, a prisoner of the United States.


*Approved, May 20, 1812.*

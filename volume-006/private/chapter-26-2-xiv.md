# Chapter XIV

> An Act for the relief of Lieutenant John E. Bispham.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to Lieutenant John E. Bispham, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of twenty-nine dollars and sixteen cents, the amount awarded to him by Congress, as one of the captors of a piratical felucca, taken in the West Indies, in eighteen hundred and twenty-three, and transferred to the treasury by reason of his non-claim within the time limited by law.


*Approved, March 2, 1841.*

# Chapter CCXCVIII

> An Act for the relief of the owners, officers, and crews of the private armed vessels Neptune and fox.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to the owners, officers, and crews of the private armed vessels Neptune and Fox, or their legal representatives, by the proper officers of the treasury, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, the sum of twenty-five dollars for each of sixty-nine prisoners captured by said Neptune and Fox on the St. Lawrence river, on the nineteenth day of July, eighteen hundred and thirteen, and delivered to the authorized agent of the United States at Sackett’s Harbor.


*Approved, July 2, 1836.*

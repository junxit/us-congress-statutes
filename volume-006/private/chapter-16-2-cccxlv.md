# Chapter CCCXLV

> An Act for the relief of James Bradford, of Louisiana.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That James Bradford, of Louisiana, shall be, and he hereby is, confirmed in his right to four hundred and twenty-seven arpens of land, situated in the parish of West Feliciana, in the state of Louisiana, fronting on the Mississippi river, three quarters of a mile below the mouth of Bayou Sarah, bounded on the upper side by lands granted to William Williams; below by those of Augustin Allain; and back by those of the aforesaid Williams and others; and the register of the land office in the proper district, on a presentation of the survey and Spanish patent for the said tract, shall cause an entry thereof to be made, and grant him a certificate therefor, whereupon a patent shall issue for the same in due form: Provided, however, That this act shall operate only as a relinquishment, on the part of the United States, of their right and claim to the said tract of land, but shall not interfere with the claim or claims of other persons.


*Approved, July 2, 1836.*

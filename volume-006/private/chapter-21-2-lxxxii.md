# Chapter LXXXII

> An Act for the relief of Hugh Barnes.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he hereby is, directed to cause to be paid to Hugh Barnes, an invalid pensioner of the United States, an arrearage of pension withheld from him, in consequence of a mistake made by the examining surgeon in September, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-one, being in addition to what he has received, at the rate of ten dollars per month, from the fourth day of September, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-one, to the twenty-second day of September, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-four, when his pension of twenty dollars per month was restored to him.


*Approved, March 2, 1831.*

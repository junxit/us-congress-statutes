# Chapter CXLIX

> An Act for the relief of John Skirving.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby authorized and directed to pay to John Skirving the sum of six hundred dollars, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, it being for services by him performed upon the public buildings, in the years eighteen hundred and thirty-nine, eighteen hundred arid forty, and eighteen hundred and forty-one.


*Approved, March 3, 1843.*

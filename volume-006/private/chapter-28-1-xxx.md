# Chapter XXX

> An Act for the relief of Walker, Kinkle, and Caruthers.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Auditor of the Treasury for the Post Office Department state the account of Walker, Kinkle, and Caruthers, for their service in transporting the mails from the first of February, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-five, to the sixth of August, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-six, allowing them at the rate of two-thirds of the excess paid M. A. Price and Company, who became the contractors, and who, on the said sixth day of August, took possession of the routes, over and above the amount allowed to Walker, Kinkle and Caruthers, under their contract for the same routes; and that the said Auditor deduct from said account any sum which may have been paid to said Walker, Kinkle, and Caruthers, on account of the service so rendered between the above stated periods; and that the Postmaster-General pay, or cause to be paid to them, the balance, if any.


*Approved, February 27, 1845.*

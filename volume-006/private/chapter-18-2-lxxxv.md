# Chapter LXXXV

> An Act for the relief of George Love.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay to George Love, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of sixty-two dollars eighty cents, the amount paid as paymaster of the New York militia, to the representatives of deceased officers and soldiers, not heretofore allowed.


*Approved, March 3, 1825.*

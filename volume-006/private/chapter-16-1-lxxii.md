# Chapter LXXII

> An Act for the relief of Thomas C. Withers.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to Thomas C. Withers the sum of three hundred and seventy dollars; the same being due for four horses, a wagon, and gear, impressed into the service of the United States during the late war; and that the aforesaid sum be paid out of any money in the treasury, not otherwise appropriated: Provided, however, That there shall be deducted from the aforesaid sum, whatever amount the claimant may have received for the use of his wagon, team, and gear, as aforesaid.


*Approved, May 8, 1820.*

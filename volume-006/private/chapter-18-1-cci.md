# Chapter CCI

> An Act for the relief of John Holliday.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to John Holliday, or his legal representative, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of three hundred and fifty-five dollars, the value of a wagon and team, captured by the hostile Indians, while in the service of the United States, during the late war.


*Approved, May 26, 1824.*

# Chapter CLVI

> An Act for the relief of Alexander Scott.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the sum of one thousand four hundred and seventy-one dollars and ninety-seven cents be paid, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Alexander Scott, on account of his services as a political agent of the Government of the United States in Venezuela.


*Approved, May 29, 1830.*

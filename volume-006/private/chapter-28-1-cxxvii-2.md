# Chapter CXXVII

> An Act for the relief of Lathrop Foster.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to place the name of Lathrop Foster on the roll of invalid pensioners, at the rate of one-half the pay of a private, from the first of January, one thousand eight hundred and forty-four.


*Approved, June 17, 1844.*

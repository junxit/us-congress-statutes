# Chapter XLVI

> An Act for the relief of John W. Monette.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the President of the United States cause patents to be issued to John Wesley Monette, for lots numbered one, three, and four, in townships sixteen, of range twelve, east, in the district north of Red River, in the state of Louisiana, said lands having been duly entered and paid for by said John Wesley Monette.


*Approved, July 4, 1840.*

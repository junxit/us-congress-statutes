# Chapter XXXII

> An Act for the relief of Joel Sartain.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be directed to pay, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of fifty-five dollars to Joel Sartain, the value of his horse lost in the military service of the United States.


*Approved, February 17, 1836.*

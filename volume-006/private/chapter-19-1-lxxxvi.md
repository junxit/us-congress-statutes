# Chapter LXXXVI

> An Act for the relief of David Tate.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to pay David Tate two hundred and eighteen dollars, for forage impressed by the officers of the United States, in the year eighteen hundred and fourteen, for the use of the army, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, May 20, 1826.*

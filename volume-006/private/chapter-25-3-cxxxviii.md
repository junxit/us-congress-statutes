# Chapter CXXXVIII

> An Act for the relief of Milley Yales.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, required to cause to be located to Milley Yates, a Choctaw woman, wife of Allen Yates, two sections of land, on any of the public lands within the country acquired by the treaty concluded with the Choctaw Indians at Dancing Rabbit creek, on the twenty-seventh of September, eighteen hundred and thirty, not previously located to any Choctaw reservee, nor subject to any preemption claim, in one or more tracts, not less in any case than one quarter section: Provided, The said Milley Yates shall release to the United States the two sections heretofore located in her name, by Colonel George W. Martin, and since disposed of at public sale at St. Stephens.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That when such location shall have been made and confirmed, the said Milley Yates shall be entitled to a grant for the same from the United States.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

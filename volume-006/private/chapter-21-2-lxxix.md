# Chapter LXXIX

> An Act for the relief of Peter Cleer, of Maryland.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be authorized and directed to place the name of Peter Cleer on the roll of revolutionary pensions, and to cause him to be paid at the rate of eight dollars per month, to commence on the nineteenth day of December one thousand eight hundred and twenty-five.


*Approved, March 2, 1831.*

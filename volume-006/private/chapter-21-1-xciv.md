# Chapter XCIV

> An Act for the relief of James Abbott.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to James Abbott, out of any money in the Treasury, not otherwise appropriated, the sum of seventy dollars for his fences necessarily taken by the soldiers of the United States, stationed at Detroit, and burnt for fuel, in the winter of eighteen hundred and thirteen.


*Approved, May 10, 1830.*

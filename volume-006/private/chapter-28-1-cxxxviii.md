# Chapter CXXXVIII

> An Act for the relief of Johnson Patrick.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he hereby is, authorized and directed to pay to Johnson Patrick, or to his legal representatives, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of thirteen hundred and fifty-one dollars and seventy-six cents, for boarding certain Pottawatomie chiefs, while holding a council at Prairie road, in Kalamazoo county, Michigan, in the summer of eighteen hundred and thirty-two.


*Approved, March 3, 1843.*

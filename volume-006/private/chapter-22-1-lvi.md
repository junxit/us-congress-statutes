# Chapter LVI

> An Act for the relief of Captain Thomas Paine.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officers of the treasury be, and are hereby, authorized to pay to Thomas Paine, the sum of five hundred dollars, in full for two months and a half provisions lost in the revenue cutter Crawford, on the sixteenth of March, one thousand eight hundred and twenty-nine.


*Approved, March 22, 1832.*

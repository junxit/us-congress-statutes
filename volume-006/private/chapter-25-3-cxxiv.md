# Chapter CXXIV

> An Act for the relief of Frederick Richmond.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, required to pay to Frederick Richmond out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated the sum of ninety-eight dollars and forty cents, for his pay and subsistence as a lieutenant in Captain McClure’s company, in Colonel Dubois’s regiment of New York militia, intermediate the twenty-second day of October, eighteen hundred and twelve, and the first day of January, eighteen hundred and thirteen, he having been wounded and taken a prisoner at the battle of Queenstown, in eighteen hundred and twelve.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

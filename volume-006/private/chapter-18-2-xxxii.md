# Chapter XXXII

> An Act for the relief of Elias Glen.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he is hereby, authorized to pay to Elias Glen, the sum of one hundred dol- lars, out of any moneys in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, in full for his services in taking depositions, at the instance and request of the late Secretary of the Navy.


*Approved, March 3, 1825.*

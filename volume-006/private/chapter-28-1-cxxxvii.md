# Chapter CXXXVII

> An Act for the relief of John Wolfenden.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be paid to John Wolfenden, out of the treasury, from any moneys not otherwise appropriated, a sum of money, annually, equal to the amount paid at the naval asylum, at Philadelphia, for the support of a seaman in the service of the United States, but not exceeding one hundred and eight dollars; said payment to be made semi-yearly, and to continue during the life of said John Wolfenden.


*Approved, March 3, 1843.*

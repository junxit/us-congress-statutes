# Chapter CCLXXXIII

> An Act for the relief of John and Benjamin Welles.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the collector of the customs for the district of Boston and Charlestown be, and he is hereby, authorized to issue certificates of debenture to John and Benjamin Welles of Boston, for the amount of the drawback of duties on two cases of indigo, shipped at Boston, on or about the sixteenth day of September, in the year one thousand eight hundred and eighteen, on board the schooner Governor Carver, for Havre, in France: Provided, That the said collector shall be satisfied that all the requirements of the law have been complied with, except that of taking the oath, and giving the export bond, within twenty days from the time when said merchandise was exported.


*Approved, July 14, 1832.*

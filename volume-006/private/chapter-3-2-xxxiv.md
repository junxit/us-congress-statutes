# Chapter XXXIV

> An Act act for the relief of Angus M’Lean.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the officers of the treasury be, and they hereby are authorized and required to issue and deliver to the said Angus M’Lean, or his attorney duly authorized, a certificate or certificates for the amount of two hundred and fifty-four dollars and forty-three cents, bearing interest at six per cent, from the first day of July, one thousand seven hundred and eighty-three, being for his services in the corps of sappers and miners during the late war.


*Approved, February 27, 1795.*

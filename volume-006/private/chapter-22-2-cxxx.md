# Chapter CXXX

> An Act for the relief of Joseph Gaston, of South Carolina.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to place the name of Joseph Gaston, of Chester District, in the state of South Carolina, on the roll of invalid pensioners; and pay to him eight dollars per month during his natural life, commencing on the first of January, eighteen hundred and thirty-three.


*Approved, March 2, 1833.*

# Chapter CLXI

> An Act for the relief of William McPherson.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Navy be directed to place the name of William McPherson on the roll of invalid naval pensioners, and that there be paid to the said William McPherson a pension at the rate of eight dollars per month during his natural life, to commence on the first day of January, one thousand eight hundred and forty-three.


*Approved, June 17, 1844.*

# Chapter II

> An Act to remit the duty on a painting presented to the Pennsylvania Hospital.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,*

That the duty secured to the United States on a painting lately presented by Benjamin West, president of the royal academy, London, to the Pennsylvania hospital, be, and the same is hereby, remitted.


*Approved, January 14, 1818.*

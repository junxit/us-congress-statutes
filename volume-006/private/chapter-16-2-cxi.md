# Chapter CXI

> An Act for the relief of Stephen Bohannan.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of Stephen Bohannan, of Bristol, in New Hampshire, on the invalid pension list, and to pay him at the rate of four dollars a month, during his natural life, commencing on the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-six.


*Approved, June 15, 1836.*

# Chapter CCII

> An Act for the relief of Gratia Ray.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That a pension of two hundred and sixteen dollars, for the term oi five years, from the sixth day of January, in the [year] one thousand eight hundred and thirty-four, be allowed, and paid in half-yearly payments, out of the navy pension fund, to Gratia Ray, widow of William Ray, late a quartermaster in the navy of the United States: Provided, however, That such pension shall be deemed to continue only during the life and widowhood of the said Gratia Ray.


*Approved, July 7, 1838.*

# Chapter LXVIII

> An Act for the relief of the representatives of John B. Dash.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That there be refunded and paid to the legal representative or representatives of John B. Dash, deceased, the sum of two thousand five hundred and sixty-seven dollars and sixty-six cents; which said sum of money was paid by the said John B. Dash, in his lifetime, to the collector of New York, on the importation into the United States of sundry copper bottoms, and bars or bolts, the said articles not being subject by law to the payment of duties.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the said sum shall be paid out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, May 7, 1822.*

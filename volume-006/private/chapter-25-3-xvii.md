# Chapter XVII

> An Act for the relief of Elisha Town.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he hereby is, directed to pay to Elisha Town, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of thirty dollars, being the amount of an over-payment, made by him into the treasury, for a patent for his improved crane stove.


*Approved, February 6, 1839.*

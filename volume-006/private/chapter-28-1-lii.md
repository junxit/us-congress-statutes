# Chapter LII

> An Act granting a pension to John E. Wright.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to place the name of John E. Wright on the roll of invalid pensioners, and to pay him a pension at the rate of six dollars per month, to commence from the tenth day of May, eighteen hundred and forty-four, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, March 3, 1845.*

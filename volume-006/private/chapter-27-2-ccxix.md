# Chapter CCXIX

> An Act for the relief of Randolph Carter.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place the name of Randolph Carter, of the state of Tennessee, on the roll of invalid pensioners; who shall be enti- tied to, and receive a pension, at the rate of eight dollars a month, from the first day of January, eighteen hundred and thirty-three, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, August 26, 1842.*

# Chapter C

> An Act for the relief of certain officers of the Florida militia.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War cause the officers and non-commissioned officers of the second regiment of the second brigade of Florida militia, ordered into the service of the United States by General J. M. Hernandez, in the year eighteen hundred and thirty-five, to be paid according to their respective ranks, for the time they were in the service, deducting such payments as may heretofore have been made to any of them on account of services rendered at that time or under the order by which they were originally called out.


*Approved, March 3, 1839.*

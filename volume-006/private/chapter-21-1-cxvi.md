# Chapter CXVI

> An Act for the relief of James Smith.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay to James Smith, out of any money not otherwise appropriated, the sum of eighty dollars, for the loss of a horse which Captain Robert Brackenridge impressed into the service of the United States, in the year one thousand eight hundred and fourteen, the property of said Smith.


*Approved, May 28, 1830.*

# Chapter CCLXXXVI

> An Act for the relief of Larned Swallow.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be directed to place the name of Larned Swallow on the invalid pension list, and cause him to be paid at the rate of twenty-four dollars a month, during life, to commence on the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-six, in lieu of the pension to which he is now entitled by law.


*Approved, July 2, 1836.*

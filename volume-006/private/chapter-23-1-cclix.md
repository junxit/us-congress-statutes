# Chapter CCLIX

> An Act for the relief of the Roman Catholic Church at St. Louis, Missouri.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the duties on a set of five bells, with their necessary appendages of brass, iron, and wood, (being donations by individuals residing in Europe, to the Roman Catholic Church at St. Louis, Missouri,) imported from Havre, in France, in the year eighteen hundred and thirty-four, into the port of New Orleans, by the Roman Catholic bishop of St. Louis, be, and the same are hereby, remitted; and the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby authorized and required to repay to the said bishop, or to his successor in office, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the amount of such sum or sums of money as may have been paid for or on account of said duties.


*Approved, June 30, 1834.*

# Chapter CXLIV

> An Act for the relief of James Sweetman.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, directed to place the name of James Sweetman, of the state of New York, on the pension roll, and to pay him at the rate of eight dollars per month, from and after the twenty-first day of February” one thousand eight hundred and forty-two.


*Approved, March 3, 1843.*

# Chapter CI

> An Act for the relief of John Hughes.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury refund to the Reverend John Hughes, of Philadelphia, three hundred and sixty-three dollars, being the amount of duties paid by him on one case of church vestments and ornaments, imported into the port of New York in November, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-one, in the ship Sully, from Havre, and one case of church plate, imported into the port of Philadelphia, in the brig Caroline, from Havre, fifth September, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-one; the said articles being donations for the use of the church whereof the said John Hughes is pastor.


*Approved, May 25, 1832.*

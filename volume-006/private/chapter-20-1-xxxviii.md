# Chapter XXXVIII

> An Act for the relief of Asa Herring.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That Asa Herring be, and he is hereby, released from the payment of that portion of the debt due to the general post office department by William Myers, formerly Post Master at Mechanicsburg, in Pennsylvania, which accrued subsequently to the seventeenth of June, eighteen hundred and twenty-two.


*Approved, April 28, 1828.*

# Chapter XV

> An Act for the relief of George Willis.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be authorized and required to pay to George Willis the sum of eighty dollars, for the loss of a pilot-boat while, piloting a revenue cutter over Ocracoke bar, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated.


*Approved, May 2, 1840.*

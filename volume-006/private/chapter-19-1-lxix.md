# Chapter LXIX

> An Act for the relief of James Cook.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury pay unto James Cook, out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of one hundred and forty-eight dollars and forty cents, the balance due him as assignee of James McLaughlin, for supplies furnished the troops of the United States, in the years one thousand eight hundred and thirteen, and one thousand eight hundred and fourteen, under a contract made with Lieutenant Fuller.


*Approved, May 18, 1826.*

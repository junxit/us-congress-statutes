# Chapter CXXV

> An Act for the relief of Judah Alden.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to issue a duplicate military bounty land warrant to Judah Alden, a Captain in the second Massachusetts regiment, in the army of the revolution, for three hundred acres of land; the original warrant, number twelve, having been lost or mislaid.


*Approved, May 28, 1830.*

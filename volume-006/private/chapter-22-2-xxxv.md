# Chapter XXXV

> An Act for the relief of John D. Sloat.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the proper accounting officer of the treasury department be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to settle the account of John D. Sloat, a master commandant of the navy, by allowing him a credit for the sum of four hundred and seventy-eight dollars and seventy-nine cents; being the amount of certain discounts on treasury notes, which has heretofore been suspended in consequence of the vouchers, once furnished by the said John D. Sloat, being now mislaid or lost.


*Approved, February 19, 1833.*

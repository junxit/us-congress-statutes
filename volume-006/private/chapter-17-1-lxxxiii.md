# Chapter LXXXIII

> An Act for the relief of James Brisban and Jonah Lewis.

*Beit enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War cause to be issued to James Brisban, a duplicate of land warrant number thirty-two, originally issued to James Hoyt, a Canadian volunteer, and by him subsequently assigned to the said James Brisban, by whom the same has been lost; which said duplicate may be located, and a patent issued thereon, in the manner provided by the act, entitled “An act to regulate the location of land warrants and the issuing of patents in certain cases,” approved March third, eighteen hundred and twenty-one.

## § 2.

And be it further enacted, That the Secretary of War cause to be issued to Jonah Lewis, a duplicate of land warrant number thirty-four, which issued to him as a Canadian volunteer; which duplicate may be located, and a patent issued, in the manner provided by the act under which the original issued, and the act amendatory thereto, passed the third of March, eighteen hundred and seventeen.


*Approved, May 7, 1822.*

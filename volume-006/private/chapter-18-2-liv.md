# Chapter LIV

> An Act for the relief of Gregory Ennis and William R. Maddox.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of the Treasury be, and he hereby is, directed to pay out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated, to Gregory Ennis and William R. Maddox, the sum of three thousand and eighteen dollars and fifty-five cents, in full for a balance due them by the Corporation of Washington, for the graduation and improvement of the circular road around the Capitol Square.


*Approved, March 3, 1825.*

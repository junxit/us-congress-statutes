# Chapter CXCIV

> An Act for the relief of Jonathan Dow.

*Be it enacted, &c.,*

That the Secretary of War be authorized and directed to place the name of Jonathan Dow on the pension roll of the United States, and that he pay the said Dow the sum of twenty dollars per annum, to commence the fourth day of March, eighteen hundred and thirty-one, and to continue during his natural life.


*Approved, June 28, 1836.*
